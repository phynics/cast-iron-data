The flamesinger finds inspiration in the mesmerizing beauty of fire. While the flamesinger's
tradition originated among the varied peoples of the Padishah Empire of Kelesh, it long ago spread
to Qadira and from there throughout the Inner Sea region.

**Fire Music**

**At 1st Level:** A flamesinger gains [Fire Music](ref/feat/fire-music) as a bonus feat (without
needing the meet the normal prerequisites).

**At 4th Level:** When she gains access to 2nd-level spells (4th level), she adds
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1) and [summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2)
to her bard spells known; every 3 levels thereafter (7th, 10th, 13th, 16th) she gains the next level
of _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster),_ until she adds
[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6) to her spells known at 16th level.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge, lore master

**Special:** The flamesinger must apply the Fire Music feat's effect when casting these spells.

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** A flamesinger gains the following bardic performance.

**Blazing Blades (Su)**

**Ability:** The flamesinger's song causes allies' weapons to burst into flames. To be affected, an
ally must be able to hear the bard perform and be within 30 feet. An affected creature's successful
attacks with manufactured or natural weapons deal 1d4 additional points of fire damage. These flames
do not harm the weapon or its wielder. This damage stacks with other sources of fire damage, such as
the [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) special weapon ability.

**At 11th Level:** The bonus damage becomes 3d4.

**At 17th Level:** The bonus damage becomes 4d4.

**At 5th Level:** The bonus damage becomes 2d4.

**Replaces:** Inspire courage

**Special:** This is a fire effect that relies on audible components.

**Wildfire (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Like a raging wildfire, a flamesinger moves with blistering speed.

**At 10th Level:** The bonus increases to +15 feet.

**At 14th Level:** The bonus increases to +20 feet.

**At 18th Level:** The bonus increases to +25 feet.

**At 2nd Level:** The flamesinger gains a +5-foot enhancement bonus to her base speed.

**At 6th Level:** The bonus increases to +10 feet.

**Replaces:** Versatile performance
