**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 40\
By tapping into the world's natural music, specialized bards known as filidhs are able to see not
only the tapestry of life but divine portents of the future from the rhythm of all life's song.

**Armor and Weapon Proficiency**

**Alters:** Armor and weapon proficiencies

**Info:** A filidh is proficient with light armor and is prohibited from wearing metal armor. A
filidh can wear wooden armor that has been altered by the [ironwood](ref/spell/ironwood) spell.
Filidhs are proficient with shields (except tower shields) but can use only those made of wood.

**Special:** A filidh who wears prohibited armor or uses a prohibited shield is unable to cast bard
spells or use any of his supernatural or spell-like class abilities while doing so and for 24 hours
thereafter.

**Natural Magic**

**Alters:** Spellcasting

**Info:** A filidh casts spells as a bard, but the spells are divine, not arcane, and therefore not
subject to arcane spell failure. A filidh must use a holy symbol or a musical instrument as a divine
focus when a spell includes such a component. He still uses the bard spell list.

**Nature's Song**

**Ability:** Filidhs can hear the resonant song generated by all living creatures - a primal music
that stretches back to the beginning of time and ahead to the unwritten future. By tapping into this
cosmic melody, a filidh can steal glimpses of the future.

**Swift:** A filidh can sacrifice a spell slot to gain an additional number of rounds of bardic
performance for that day equal to the spell's level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per bard level

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** A filidh gains the following types of bardic performances.

**Echoes of Nature's Song (Su)**

**Ability:** A filidh can use his bardic performance to imbue his allies with subconscious knowledge
of their own futures, improving their reflexes and ability to avoid danger. An affected ally
receives a +1 insight bonus on Reflex saving throws and to AC.

**Replaces:** Inspire courage

**Special:** This performance is a mind-affecting ability and relies on audible components.

At 7th level: This bonus +2.

At 13th level: This bonus +3.

At 19th level: This bonus +4.

**Divinatory Song (Sp)**

**Ability:** A filidh can use his performance to create an effect equivalent to
[divination](ref/spell/divination), using the filidh's level as the caster level. The filidh and all
allies who can hear his performance receive the information provided by the divination as flashes of
inspiration and knowledge.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Suggestion

**Special:** Divinatory song takes 10 minutes and uses 6 rounds of bardic performance. Divinatory
song relies on audible components.

**Voices of Life (Su)**

**Ability:** A filidh can grant himself and all allies who can hear his performance the ability to
speak with animals and plants as if affected by [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)
and [speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants) so long as he maintains this performance.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Dirge of doom

**Unity of Life (Su)**

**Ability:** A filidh can use his performance to interlink the life force of two allies who can hear
his performance as though they were affected by [shield other](ref/spell/shield-other), using the
filidh's bard level as his caster level. The filidh designates which ally is the warded target and
which ally receives half the warded creature's damage.

**Free:** The filidh can switch the targets of this ability (and the effect of the performance the
targets receive) once per round at the start of each turn that he maintains the performance.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Inspire heroics

**Song of the Cycle (Su)**

**Ability:** A filidh can grant awe-inspiring glimpses into the future with his magic. All allies
who can see and hear the filidh are affected as though by the personal version of
[foresight](ref/spell/foresight) for the duration of the performance.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Deadly performance
