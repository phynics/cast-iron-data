**Sources:** Champions of Balance pg 18\
Bards are experts at manipulating the emotions and perceptions of others. Negotiators are those
bards who take the skills of a performer and apply them to the realms of business and law.

**Hard Bargainer**

**Passive:** A negotiator adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his class level (minimum 1)
on Bluff, Diplomacy, Intimidate, Knowledge (local), and Sense Motive checks.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** A negotiator gains the following types of bardic performance.

**Counterargument (Su)**

**Ability:** This performance acts as [countersong](ref/performance/countersong), except a
negotiator can use only Perform (act, comedy, oratory, or sing) to perform a counterargument.

**Fast Talk (Su)**

**Ability:** A negotiator can use performance to affect the perceptions of any number of targets
that can hear him. While the negotiator performs, these targets take a -1 penalty on saving throws
against enchantment (charm) and illusion (figment, glamer, or shadow) effects, as well as a penalty
on Appraise checks equal to half the negotiator's level (minimum -1). A target that fails its
Appraise check believes that the object in question is worth 10% more or less (negotiator's choice)
than its actual value.

**At 11th Level:** These become -3 and 30%.

**At 17th Level:** These become -4 and 40%.

**At 5th Level:** The penalty on saving throws becomes -2 and the percentage becomes 20%.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Inspire courage

**Special:** Fast talk is a language-dependent, mind-affecting ability that uses audible components.

**Binding Contract (Sp)**

**Ability:** The negotiator can bind another creature's promise to the negotiator with a geas. This
acts as [lesser geas](ref/spell/geas-lesser), except the creature must have Hit Dice equal to or
less than the negotiator's level. The target can negate this effect with a successful Will save (DC
= 10 + 1/2 the negotiator's level + the negotiator's Charisma modifier). The geas is discharged if
the negotiator breaks his end of the bargain. Using this ability requires 3 rounds of continuous
performance, and the target must be able to see and hear the bard throughout the performance.

**At 18th Level:** This effect acts as [geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest) (no HD limit, and the
target cannot attempt a saving throw).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Inspire greatness

**Special:** Binding contract is an enchantment (compulsion), mind-affecting, language-dependent
ability that relies on audible components.

**Rogue Talents**

**At 10th Level:** The negotiator gains another talent.

**At 14th Level:** The negotiator gains another talent.

**At 18th Level:** The negotiator gains another talent.

**At 2nd Level:** A negotiator gains a rogue talent, as the rogue class feature of the same name.

**At 6th Level:** The negotiator gains another talent.

**Replaces:** Versatile performance

**Special:** A negotiator cannot select a rogue talent that modifies the sneak attack ability.

**Master of Rhetoric (Ex)**

**Ability:** The negotiator can take 10 on any Bluff, Diplomacy, Intimidate, Knowledge (local), or
Sense Motive check if he has ranks in that skill. A negotiator can choose not to take 10 and can
instead roll normally. In addition, once per day, the negotiator can take 20 on one of those skill
checks (without spending any additional time).

**At 11th Level:** He can take 20 twice a day.

**At 17th Level:** He can take 20 three times a day.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Lore master

**Advanced Talents**

**At 10th Level:** A negotiator can now select an advanced rogue talent in place of a rogue talent.
This also applies to the talents gained at 14th and 18th levels.

**Special:** A negotiator cannot select an advanced talent that modifies the sneak attack ability.
