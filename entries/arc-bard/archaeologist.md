No stodgy researcher, this archaeologist meets his research head-on in the field. Archaeologists
sacrifice the bard's inspirational performance for a smattering of rogue talents. This archetype
also fits rogue-like characters that focus more on learning than on stabbing foes in the back.

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** Archaeologists do not gain the _bardic performance_ ability or any of its performance
types.

**Archaeologist's Luck (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Fortune favors the archaeologist.

**Special:** Archaeologist's luck is treated as bardic performance for the purposes of feats,
abilities, effects, and the like that affect bardic performance. Like bardic performance, it cannot
be maintained at the same time as other performance abilities.

**Swift:** An archaeologist can call on fortune's favor, giving him a +1 luck bonus on attack rolls,
saving throws, skill checks, and weapon damage rolls. Maintaining this bonus is a **free action**,
but it ends immediately if the archaeologist is killed, paralyzed, stunned, knocked unconscious, or
otherwise prevented from taking a free action to maintain it each round.

**Usage:** Cha4 modifier rounds/day

At 7th level: The bonus +2.

At 13th level: The bonus +3.

At 19th level: The bonus +4.

**Clever Explorer (Ex)**

**At 6th Level:** An archaeologist can take 10 on Disable Device checks, even if distracted or
endangered, and can disarm magical traps.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** An archaeologist gains a bonus equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his
class level on Disable Device and Perception checks. He can disable intricate and complex devices in
half the normal amount of time (minimum 1 round) and open a lock as a **standard action**.

**Replaces:** Versatile performance

**Uncanny Dodge (Ex)**

**Ability:** An archaeologist gains [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge), as the rogue class
feature of the same name.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Well-versed

**Trap Sense (Ex)**

**Ability:** An archaeologist gains [trap sense](ref/ability/trap-sense), as the rogue class feature
of the same name. The bonus granted by _trap sense_ is equal to one-third of your archaeologist
level.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Rogue Talents**

**Ability:** An archaeologist gains a rogue talent. He gains an additional rogue talent for every
four levels of archaeologist gained after 4th level. Otherwise, this works as the rogue's rogue
talent ability.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Evasion (Ex)**

**Ability:** An archaeologist gains [evasion](ref/ability/evasion), as the rogue ability of the same
name.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Advanced Talent**

**Ability:** An archaeologist can now choose an advanced rogue talent instead of a rogue talent.

**Gained:** At 12th Level
