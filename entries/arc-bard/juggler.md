**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 7\
Jugglers are masters of manipulating objects, most famously by keeping multiple objects in the air
simultaneously. They often incorporate this talent into acting, comedy, or dance. Adventuring
jugglers find their abilities translate well into the use of thrown weapons.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Alters:** Weapon and armor proficiencies

**Info:** A juggler is proficient with all simple weapons, plus martial thrown weapons. A juggler is
not proficient with shields.

**Fast Reactions (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** A juggler gains [Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows) as a bonus feat.

**At 11th Level:** The juggler can use the ability granted by these feats an additional time each
round (with a -5 penalty on his second attack roll).

**At 17th Level:** The juggler gains another use of these feats (with a -10 penalty on his third
attack roll).

**At 5th Level:** He gains [Snatch Arrows](ref/feat/snatch-arrows) as a bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge, lore master

**Special:** The juggler doesn't need to meet the prerequisites for these feats.

**Combat Juggling (Ex)**

**Ability:** A juggler can hold and wield (in other words, &quot&juggle&quot&) up to three items or
weapons in his hands. The juggler must be able to hold and wield an object in one hand in order to
juggle it. This ability doesn't grant the juggler additional attacks, though it does allow him to
use different weapons as part of a [full attack](ref/rule/full-attack). As long as he is juggling
fewer than three objects, the juggler is considered to have a free hand (for the purposes of drawing
a weapon, using somatic components, using Deflect Arrows, and so on).

**At 10th Level:** The juggler can juggle five items or weapons at once.

**At 14th Level:** The juggler can juggle six items or weapons at once.

**At 18th Level:** The juggler can juggle seven items or weapons at once.

**At 6th Level:** The juggler can juggle four items or weapons at once.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Versatile performance

**Special:** If the juggler is affected by any action or condition that would require a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check while he is juggling, he must attempt a Sleight of
Hand check to continue juggling and avoid losing concentration. The check's DC is the same as a
concentration check, treating the spell level as twice the number of objects being juggled. If the
juggler fails his Sleight of Hand check, he drops all juggled objects but one, determined randomly.

**Evasion (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** He gains [improved evasion](ref/talent/improved-evasion), as the advanced rogue
talent.

**At 2nd Level:** A juggler gains [evasion](ref/ability/evasion), as the [rogue](ref/class/rogue)
class feature of the same name.

**Replaces:** Well-versed, soothing performance
