**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 80\
A master of the martial applications of steel and spell, the arcane duelist blends both into a
lethal combination.

**Arcane Strike**

**Info:** Arcane duelists gain [Arcane Strike](ref/feat/arcane-strike) as a bonus feat at 1st level.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge

**Bardic Performance**

**Ability:** An arcane duelist gains the following types of bardic performance.

**Rallying Cry (Su)**

**Ability:** Each round he makes an Intimidate check. Any ally (including the bard) within 30 feet
may use this check in place of his own saving throw against fear and despair effects. Those already
under a fear or despair effect can attempt a new save each round using the bard's Intimidate check.

**Flavor:** An arcane duelist can use performance to rally dispirited allies.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Countersong

**Special:** Rallying cry does not work on effects that don't allow saves. This is a mind-affecting
ability that uses audible components.

**Bladethirst (Su)**

**Ability:** An arcane duelist may use performance to grant one weapon, one natural weapon, one end
of a double weapon, or 50 items of ammunition of the same type within 30 feet a +1 enhancement
bonus. This enhancement bonus increases by +1 for every three levels after 6th (maximum +5 at 18th
level).

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Suggestion

**Special:** These bonuses stack with existing bonuses and may be used to increase the item's
enhancement bonus up to +5 or to add any of the following weapon properties:
[defending](ref/magic-enh/defending), [distance](ref/magic-enh/distance),
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon), [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen),
[mighty cleaving](ref/magic-enh/mighty-cleaving), [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning),
[shock](ref/magic-enh/shock), [shocking burst](ref/magic-enh/shocking-burst),
[seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking), [speed](ref/magic-enh/speed), or
[wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding). If the weapon is not magical, at least a +1 enhancement bonus
must be added before adding special abilities.

**Mass Bladethirst (Su)**

**Ability:** An arcane duelist can use his _bladethirst_ performance to enhance the weapons of as
many allies as desired within 30 feet. The bonus provided by this power is +4 if conferred on two
allies, +3 for three allies, +2 for four allies, and +1 for five or more allies. The power granted
to each weapon must be identical.

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Replaces:** Mass suggestion

**Bonus Feats**

**Flavor:** An arcane duelist weaves might and magic in his combat style.

**At 10th Level:** Gain [Spellbreaker](ref/feat/spellbreaker) as a bonus feat

**At 14th Level:** Gain [Penetrating Strike](ref/feat/penetrating-strike) as a bonus feat

**At 18th Level:** Gain [Greater Penetrating Strike](ref/feat/greater-penetrating-strike) as a bonus
feat

**At 2nd Level:** Gain [Combat Casting](ref/feat/combat-casting) as a bonus feat

**At 6th Level:** Gain [Disruptive](ref/feat/disruptive) as a bonus feat

**Replaces:** Versatile performance, well-versed

**Arcane Bond (Ex)**

**Ability:** An arcane duelist gains the arcane bond ability as a [wizard](ref/class/wizard), using
a weapon as his bonded item, allowing him to cast any one additional spell that he knows once per
day.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Lore master

**Special:** He may use the hand holding his bonded weapon for somatic components.

**Bonded Object**

**Action:** Ability

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the wizard ability.

You begin play with a bonded object at no cost. This object is always masterwork quality. Weapons
acquired at 1st level are not made of any special material. Weapons must be wielded to have an
effect. If you attempt to cast a spell without your bonded object in hand, you must make a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check or lose the spell. The DC for this check is equal to
20 + the spell's level.

A bonded object can be used once per day to cast any one spell that you have in your spellbook and
are capable of casting, even if the spell is not prepared. This spell is treated like any other
spell cast by you, including casting time, duration, and other effects dependent on your level. This
spell cannot be modified by metamagic feats or other abilities.

You can add additional magic abilities to your bonded object as if you have the required item
creation feats and if you meet the level prerequisites of the feat. For example, a wizard with a
bonded dagger must be at least 5th level to add magic abilities to the dagger (see
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor)). The magic properties of a bonded
object, including any magic abilities added to the object, only function for the wizard who owns it.
If a bonded object's owner dies, or the item is replaced, the object reverts to being an ordinary
masterwork item of the appropriate type.

If a bonded object is damaged, it is restored to full hit points the next time you prepare your
spells. If the object of an arcane bond is lost or destroyed, it can be replaced after 1 week in a
special ritual that costs 200 gp per wizard level plus the cost of the masterwork item. This ritual
takes 8 hours to complete. Items replaced in this way do not possess any of the additional
enchantments of the previous bonded item. You can designate an existing magic item as your bonded
item. This functions in the same way as replacing a lost or destroyed item except that the new magic
item retains its abilities while gaining the benefits and drawbacks of becoming a bonded item.

**Arcane Armor (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** An arcane duelist gains Medium Armor Proficiency and can cast bard spells in
medium armor with no chance of arcane spell failure.

**At 16th Level:** He gains Heavy Armor Proficiency and can cast bard spells in heavy armor with no
arcane spell failure chance.

**Replaces:** Jack of all trades
