**Sources:** Healer's Handbook pg 19\
Faith singers show their faith in their deity with songs or other appropriate performances.

**Faithful**

**Info:** Like a cleric, a faith singer must worship a deity, and his alignment must be within one
step of his deity's alignment.

**Devout Spell Knowledge (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** He can similarly cast the 3rd-level domain spell while giving a bardic
performance.

**At 14th Level:** He can similarly cast the 4th-level domain spell while giving a bardic
performance.

**At 18th Level:** He can similarly cast the 5th-level domain spell while giving a bardic
performance.

**At 2nd Level:** A faith singer can select one [domain](ref/ability/domains) from among those
belonging to his deity. Once per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) using
his bard level as his caster level, he can cast the 1st-level domain spell while giving a bardic
performance.

**At 6th Level:** He can similarly cast the 2nd-level domain spell while giving a bardic
performance.

**Replaces:** Versatile performance

**Special:** He can use each [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) only once per day.
