## Plant Speaker (Vine Leshy only)

Plant speakers build upon their racial _plantspeech_ ability and their storytelling tendencies to
create an art form full of deep metaphors and allegory rather than specific details and facts.

**Prerequisites:** Vine leshy

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** Plant speakers gain the following bardic performance.

**Leshy Speaker (Su)**

**At 9th Level:** The plant speaker can speak to its disembodied brethren, the spirits of nature
that become leshys. He performs for 10 minutes and spends 7 rounds of bardic performance to gain the
effects of [commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature).

**Replaces:** Inspire greatness

**Plant Speech**

**At 2nd Level:** A plant speaker's racial plantspeech allows him to communicate with all plants,
not just plants that match his alternate form.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A plant speaker's mind-affecting bard spells and class abilities also affect plants,
ignoring plants' immunity to mind-affecting effects.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge, well-versed

**Mystical Allegory (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** He can perform for 10 minutes and spend 7 rounds of bardic performance to gain
the effects of [divination](ref/spell/divination).

**At 17th Level:** He can perform for 1 hour and spend 10 rounds of bardic performance to gain the
effects of [legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore), but the information is always vague and incomplete.

**At 5th Level:** The plant speaker can tell a story that matches some of the themes of the current
situation in order to gain insight, gaining the effects of [augury](ref/spell/augury) by performing
for 1 minute and spending 4 rounds of bardic performance.

**Replaces:** Lore master
