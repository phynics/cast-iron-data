**Sources:** Healer's Handbook pg 18\
Solacers are experts in the healing arts as well as creators of performances that console the
distraught, rally the stricken, raise spirits, and vanquish sorrow.

**Gained class skills:** heal

**Lost class skills:** app

**Learned Physician (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** A solacer adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his class level (minimum 1)
on Heal checks and can attempt Knowledge checks untrained.

**At 11th Level:** He can Take 20 twice a day.

**At 17th Level:** He can Take 20 three times a day.

**At 5th Level:** A solacer can take 10 on checks with any Knowledge skill he has ranks in. The
solacer can choose not to take 10 and can instead roll normally. In addition, once per day, the
solacer can take 20 on any Heal check, though this does not affect the time it takes to perform the
task attempted.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge, lore master

**Inspire Tenacity (Su)**

**Ability:** A solacer can use his bardic performances to bolster his allies' mental endurance when
they need it most. All allies within 30 feet automatically stabilize if they are
[dying](ref/misc/dying) and gain a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects,
poisons, and disease.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Countersong

**Special:** This performance relies on audible components.

**Creative Treatment (Su)**

**Ability:** When a solacer attempts a Heal check and fails, he can immediately reroll the check and
take the better result.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Versatile performance

**Special:** Additionally, when acting as a confidante (or any other type of advisor) to
[reduce sanity damage with rest](ref/rule/reducing-sanity-damage), the solacer can attempt a
Charisma check instead of an Intelligence or Wisdom check and add his Charisma modifier to the
amount of sanity damage removed instead of his Intelligence or Wisdom modifier. When acting as a
confidante (or any other type of advisor) to reduce a [madness's](ref/rule/madness) DC with rest,
the solacer can attempt a Charisma check instead of an Intelligence or Wisdom check and reduce the
madness's DC by 1/2 his Charisma modifier instead of 1/2 his Intelligence or Wisdom modifier.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 bard levels beyond 2nd

**Invigorating Artistry (Su)**

**Ability:** The solacer can perform for 1 hour (this requires no skill check) and grant several
benefits for 24 hours to the creatures that can hear or see his performance, or both, depending on
whether the performance has audible or visual components. Affected creatures gain a +3 bonus on
saving throws against curses, possession spells and effects (such as
[magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar)), and domination and mind control spells and effects (such as
[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person)). If the target is suffering any or multiple such
effects, it immediately can attempt a new saving throw against each; the effect ends if the saving
throw is successful. Only one such extra saving throw can be granted against each condition in any
24-hour period. If a creature benefits from the daily performance between saving throws against a
[corruption](ref/rule/corruption), it gains a +3 bonus on the next saving throw against that
corruption.

**Flavor:** A solacer can use his performances to protect and heal a target's soul.

**At 16th Level:** This bonus increases to +4.

**At 19th Level:** This bonus increases to +5.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** Jack-of-all-trades
