Not merely an entertainer in a disguise, the masked performer takes on the persona represented by
the likeness she wears. Her alter ego might be a theatrical archetype, a legendary hero, or a
revered spirit. Masked performers are often the stars of religious plays and festivals, and
aficionados easily recognize them and associate them with their most famous role.

**Dual Identity (Ex)**

**Ability:** This ability acts as the [vigilante](ref/class/vigilante) ability of the same name,
except as follows. The masked performer's identities are referred to as social and masked, rather
than social and vigilante. Unlike a vigilante, most people who know of the masked performer actually
know that the masked performer's identities are one and the same. Nonetheless, the dual identity
ability is valuable to the masked performer due to offering her two perspectives on any problem and
two different alignments.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** A masked performer gains the following types of bardic performance.

**Seamless Guise (Ex)**

**Ability:** A masked performer learns to behave in a way that appears perfectly proper and normal
for her current role, on stage or off. She can maintain this performance for 1 hour for every 3
rounds of bardic performance she spends. While this performance is active, she receives a +10 bonus
on Disguise and Perform (act) checks to remain in character after she has already established her
character. For instance, this bonus does not apply against the initial Perception check when meeting
a creature paying attention to her but does apply on the automatic additional Perception check each
hour of interacting.

**Replaces:** Countersong

**Exaggerated Pose (Su)**

**Ability:** A masked performer can enhance her own physical and social abilities by simply altering
her posture. When she starts this performance, the masked performer chooses a Strength-, Dexterity-,
or Charisma-based skill and gains a +2 competence bonus on checks with that skill as long as she
maintains this performance.

**At 11th Level:** The bonus becomes +4.

**At 15th Level:** This bonus becomes +5.

**At 19th Level:** The bonus becomes +6.

**At 7th Level:** The bonus becomes +3.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Inspire competence

**Special:** _Exaggerated pose_ is an emotion effect and relies on visual components.

**Swift:** The masked performer can change the chosen skill.

**Stage Combat (Su)**

**Ability:** A masked performer gains the benefit of a combat feat she doesn't have as long as she
maintains this performance. The masked performer chooses the feat when she starts this performance.
The masked performer must meet all the feat's prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Suggestion

**Special:** As usual when temporarily gaining a feat, if the feat she chooses has any limitations
on uses, such as [Stunning Fist's](ref/feat/stunning-fist) daily uses, all uses of that combat feat
while maintaining _stage combat_ count toward that feat's limit. In the case of Stunning Fist, if
the masked performer gained that feat again later the same day, the expended uses would remain
expended. _Stage combat_ relies on visual components.

**Multiplicity of Masks (Su)**

**Ability:** A masked performer can cause any number of her allies to appear as herself or as
another ally within 60 feet. To be affected, an ally must be within 60 feet of the masker performer
and able to see her. As long as the masked performer maintains this performance, enemies perceive
all affected allies as the chosen character. If an affected ally is a different size or creature
type than the chosen character, enemies can attempt a Will save (DC = 10 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) the masked performer's bard level + her Charisma modifier)
to disbelieve. Otherwise, there is no saving throw, but a successful attack against a disguised ally
automatically reveals the ally's true form.

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Replaces:** Mass suggestion

**Special:** If an ally moves out of range, that ally reverts to his original form, and the entire
effect ends if the ally providing his appearance to the others moves out of range. _Multiplicity of
masks_ is an illusion (glamer) effect that relies on visual components.

**Social Grace (Ex)**

**Ability:** A masked performer gains the [social grace](ref/soctalent/social-grace) vigilante
social talent.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Versatile performance

**Special:** She gains an additional skill with _social grace_ 1 level later than a vigilante (at
6th level, 10th level, 14th level, and 18th level).

**Masked Talents**

**At 17th Level:** A masked performer can choose any of the above talents or an advanced rogue
talent from the following list: [defensive roll](ref/talent/defensive-roll),
[fast tumble](ref/talent/fast-tumble), [hide in plain sight](ref/talent/hide-in-plain-sight), and
[skill mastery](ref/talent/skill-mastery).

**At 5th Level:** A masked performer gains a rogue talent from the following list:
[expert leaper](ref/talent/expert-leaper), [fast stealth](ref/talent/fast-stealth),
[ledge walker](ref/talent/ledge-walker), [rogue crawl](ref/talent/rogue-crawl),
[rope master](ref/talent/rope-master), [stand up](ref/talent/stand-up), and
[wall scramble](ref/talent/wall-scramble).

**Replaces:** Lore master

**Special:** This ability does not qualify her for the
[Extra Rogue Talent](ref/feat/extra-rogue-talent) feat.
