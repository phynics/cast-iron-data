As quick at wordplay as at swordplay, daredevils are dashing heroes, inspiring their allies to match
their clever repartee and acrobatic feats. Daredevils often lean toward comedy, dance, oratory, and
singing.

**Agile (Ex)**

**Passive:** A daredevil adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her class level (minimum 1) on
Acrobatics, Bluff, Climb, and Escape Artist checks.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** A daredevil gains the following type of bardic performance.

**Derring-do (Su)**

**Ability:** The daredevil and any allies who see her receive a +1 morale bonus on Reflex saving
throws and a +2 competence bonus on Dexterity-based skill checks. Allies who move at least 10 feet
during their turn gain a +1 dodge bonus to their Armor Class until the start of their next turn.

**Flavor:** A daredevil can use her performance to bring out grace in the clumsiest allies.

**At 11th Level:** The skill bonus becomes +6, and the other bonuses become +3.

**At 17th Level:** The skill bonus becomes +8, and the other bonuses become +4.

**At 5th Level:** The skill bonus becomes +4, and the other bonuses become +2.

**Replaces:** Inspire courage

**Special:** This is a mind-affecting ability that uses visual components.

**Canny Foe (Ex)**

**Choice:** A daredevil can choose one type of [combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers).

**At 10th Level:** The daredevil chooses a third combat maneuver to gain a +2 bonus.

**At 14th Level:** The daredevil chooses a fourth combat maneuver to gain a +2 bonus.

**At 18th Level:** The daredevil chooses a fifth combat maneuver to gain a +2 bonus.

**At 6th Level:** The daredevil gains a +2 bonus for a second type of combat maneuver.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** She gains a +2 bonus on her combat maneuver checks to attempt the chosen maneuver and
to her CMD to resist that maneuver.

**Replaces:** Versatile performance

**Special:** She may not choose the same maneuver twice.

**Dauntless (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A daredevil gains a +1 morale bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects,
including all fear effects.

**Replaces:** Well-versed

At 6th level: This bonus +2.

At 10th level: This bonus +3.

At 14th level: This bonus +4.

At 18th level: This bonus +5.

**Scoundrel's Fortune (Ex)**

**Ability:** A daredevil can choose to roll two dice instead of one for any skill check, keeping the
best result.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Lore master

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 3 bard levels beyond 5th
