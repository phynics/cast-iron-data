Kobolds' yammering songs distract opponents in combat, hindering their ability to attack.

**Prerequisites:** Kobold

**Bardic Performance**

**Info:** A dragon yapper gains the following types of bardic performance.

**Yapping Song (Su)**

**Ability:** A dragon yapper can use performance to annoy those that hear it, causing them to take a
-1 penalty on attack and damage rolls (minimum 1) and a -1 penalty on saves against fear effects and
charm effects as long as the dragon yapper continues performing.

**At 11th Level:** These penalties become -3.

**At 17th Level:** These penalties become -4.

**At 5th Level:** These penalties become -2.

**Replaces:** Fascinate

**Special:** Yapping song is a mind-affecting ability that uses audible components, but is not
language-dependent.

**Frightful Song (Su)**

**Ability:** A dragon yapper can amplify his voice to sound like an actual dragon. Enemies within 30
feet become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) unless they succeed at a Will save (DC 10
+[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) the dragon yapper's level + the dragon yapper's Charisma
modifier). A successful save renders targets immune to this ability for 24 hours.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Dirge of doom

**Special:** Frightful song is a mind-affecting ability, and a fear effect that uses audible
components.

**Versatile Performance (Ex)**

**At 2nd Level:** A dragon yapper must choose sing at 2nd level.
