The paladin's lay on hands also acts as [calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions), but only for the
purpose of suppressing morale bonuses (such as from the [rage](ref/spell/rage) spell) and emotion
effects that aren't fear effects. Use the paladin's level as the caster level.
