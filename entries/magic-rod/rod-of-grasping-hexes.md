**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This rod is crafted from a gnarled branch covered in sharp thorns. Three times per day when a
wielder of this rod uses a hex (but not an advanced hex or grand hex), she can use this rod's power
to double the range of the hex, so long as the hex has a range measured in feet.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Reach Spell](ref/feat/reach-spell), creator must
possess the hex class feature; **Cost** 5,500 gp
