## Rod of Metamagic, Concussive

**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 32,500 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 73,000 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

Up to three times per day the wielder can cast a spell with the sonic descriptor that causes those
who take damage from that spell to take a -2 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks,
and ability checks for a number of rounds equal to the actual spell level of the spell cast as
though using the Concussive Spell feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Concussive Spell](ref/feat/concussive-spell);
**Cost** 4,500 gp (lesser), 16,250 gp (normal), 36,500 gp (greater)
