## Rod That Should Not Be (Lesser)

**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This rod is a fusion of magic and the limb of a dangerous, nearly mindless aberration. This rod
features a single writhing tentacle at one end. The rod contains the mind of a living creature, but
it does not have the ability to communicate via speech, empathy, or telepathy. If allowed, the rod
attempts to attack creatures within reach, and must be stowed in a bag or backpack when the wielder
is sleeping to keep it from grasping at the wielder or other creatures.

On command, the tentacle attacks an adjacent creature as a melee touch attack (attack bonus +8). On
a successful hit, the tentacle deals 2d8 points of acid damage and the target must succeed at a DC
14 Will saving throw or be [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for 1 round. This paralysis is a
mind-affecting fear effect.

The greater version of this rod has four tentacles, which attack the same target simultaneously on
command (attack bonus +12). The tentacles collectively deal 4d8 points of acid damage and the target
must succeed at a DC 18 Will saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 round. This paralysis is a
mind-affecting fear effect. The greater rods have a
[dedicated purpose](ref/rule/special-purpose-items): serve a bloodthirsty master.

### Construction

**Requirements** [summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) (Lesser),
[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5) (Greater); **Price** 6,000 gp (Lesser), 20,000 gp
(Greater)

### Statistics

**Alignment** chaotic evil; **Ego** 3\
**Senses** 30 ft.\
**Int** 5, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 8\
**Communication** none (none)
