**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 29\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 45,700 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This flanged iron rod is much heavier than it looks, and grants the wielder a portion of the skill
of a master coldwarper.^[1](ref/magic-rod/coldwarp-key#footnote-c)^ The wielder gains a +5
competence bonus on Craft checks involving metal.

A coldwarp key has three charges, which are renewed at midnight every night and can be expended to
create a variety of effects. Expending one charge from a coldwarp key allows the wielder to use
_[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate)_ (as the spell) to affect metal only. If the wielder is in a
natural underground environment, she can expend two charges from a coldwarp key to draw raw metal
ore from the surrounding stone and shape it to her liking. This ability acts as the
_[major creation](ref/spell/major-creation)_ spell, except the wielder can create only base metals
and the duration is instantaneous.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate),
[major creation](ref/spell/major-creation); **Cost** 22,850 gp

###### ^1^ {#footnote-c}

Coldwarping is a Drow magical tradition that forges metal without need of fire.
