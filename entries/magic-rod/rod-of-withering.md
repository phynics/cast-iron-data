**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 185, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 485\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

A rod of withering acts as a +1 [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace) that deals no hit point
damage. Instead, the wielder deals 1d4 points of [Strength damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain)
and 1d4 points of Constitution damage to any creature she touches with the rod (by making a melee
touch attack). If she scores a critical hit, the damage from that hit is permanent ability drain. In
either case, the defender negates the effect with a DC 17 Fortitude save.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [contagion](ref/spell/contagion); **Cost** 12,500 gp
