**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 14,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 54,000 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 121,500 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that can be cast an additional time that day as if
using the Echoing Spell feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Echoing Spell](ref/feat/echoing-spell); **Cost**
7,000 gp (lesser), 27,000 gp (normal), 60,750 gp (greater)
