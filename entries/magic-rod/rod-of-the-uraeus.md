**Sources:** Pathfinder \#81: Shifting Sands pg 59, Osirion, Land of the Pharaohs pg 25\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** rod; **Price** 14,400 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This rod is fashioned in the form of a uraeus, the legendary two-headed winged cobra that is the
symbol of the Ancient Osirian deity [Wadjet](ref/faith/wadjet), patron of Osirion and the goddess of
good serpents, the River Sphinx, and wisdom. The priesthood of Wadjet is credited with creating the
first rods of the uraeus, but others have since replicated the feat.

A rod of the uraeus can be wielded as a +1 [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace). When holding the
rod, the wielder gains a +2 resistance bonus on Reflex saves and a +5 competence bonus on Sense
Motive checks. The wielder can fire a 30-foot line of stinging poison from the rod up to twice per
day (once for each head of the uraeus). A creature in the area of effect can avoid the poison with a
successful DC 13 Reflex save. On a failed save, a creature is [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) and
[paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for 8 rounds. Each round on its turn, an affected creature may
attempt a DC 13 Fortitude save to end the blindness and paralysis effects. A rod of the uraeus
functions only if its possessor is lawful.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [poison](ref/spell/poison), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance),
creator must have 5 ranks in Sense Motive; **Cost** 7,200 gp
