**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 5,500 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 12,250 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that alter how the spell raises or lowers the
[illumination level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in their affected areas as though using the Eclipsed
Spell feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Eclipsed Spell](ref/feat/eclipsed-spell);
**Cost** 750 gp (lesser), 2,750 gp (normal), 6,125 gp (greater)
