**Aura** n/a (see source); **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 65,000 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This ornate, [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) rod appears to be a cross between a
[halberd](ref/eq-weapon/halberd) and a strange scepter of alien design. Despite its rigid
appearance, the rod is surprisingly flexible and can extend in combat - the weapon functions as a +3
[keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) adamantine halberd that has [reach](ref/misc/reach) but can still
function normally against adjacent foes. As a full-round action up to five times per day, the rod
can be used to fire razors. Treat these as +3 keen adamantine arrows fired from a
[longbow](ref/eq-weapon/longbow) (any feats possessed by the user that function with longbows work
with these flechettes).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [blade barrier](ref/spell/blade-barrier),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), [shield](ref/spell/shield),
[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon); **Cost** 33,435 gp
