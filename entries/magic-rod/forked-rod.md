**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 51\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 50,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This bifurcated mithral rod resembles an oversized tuning fork. The forked rod acts as a focus for
_[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift),_ but the plane to which it is attuned to can be adjusted as a
standard action - a forked rod can be attuned to any plane that a common tuning fork (see
_[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_) can be attuned to. Once per day, a forked rod can be used to
cast _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ to the plane it is currently attuned to. Three times per
day as a standard action, the user can attack a creature or object with a forked rod as if it were a
+1 [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace). On a hit, the forked rod deals no damage, but affects the
target with _[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ for 9 minutes. As long as a target
is affected in this way, the forked rod is inert and cannot be used to cast
_[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift),_ as a focus for _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift),_ or to
affect another target with _[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod),
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor), [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Cost**
25,000 gp
