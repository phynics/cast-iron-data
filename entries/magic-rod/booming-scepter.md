**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 30\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 34,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This bejeweled scepter constantly emits a low hum that amplifies the wielder's voice. A booming
scepter acts as a +1 [thundering](ref/magic-enh/thundering) [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace)
that grants the wielder a +5 competence bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and the
ability to use _[shout](ref/spell/shout),_ as per the spell, once per day. When a booming scepter is
held in both hands, the wielder's voice carries far enough to be heard by all creatures within 1
mile, regardless of ambient noise or intervening obstacles, although this feature cannot penetrate
the area of a [silence](ref/spell/silence) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [shout](ref/spell/shout), [ventriloquism](ref/spell/ventriloquism);
**Cost** 17,000 gp
