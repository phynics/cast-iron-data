**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 14\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This rod roughly resembles a torch with a small brass cage at the end. The wielder can cast any
spell of 3rd level or lower into the rod. The rod can contain one spell at a time; casting a new
spell into it replaces the older one. As long as a spell is contained within the rod, the brass cage
glows as brightly as a [candle](ref/eq-misc/candle). The wielder of a rod of perilous pits can use
the rod when casting a pit spell (_[create pit](ref/spell/create-pit),_
[hungry pit](ref/spell/hungry-pit), and similar spells with "pit" in their names). Doing so changes
that spell's casting time to a full-round action (or adds 1 round, if the casting time is already at
least 1 full round). After successfully casting the _pit_ spell, the rod's wielder can choose to
manifest the contained spell inside the summoned pit. The contained spell manifests at the bottom of
the pit, but does so after creatures have attempted their initial saves to avoid falling in. This
allows the caster to center spells with a duration of instantaneous (such as
[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)) or spells with longer durations (such as
[stinking cloud](ref/spell/stinking-cloud)) within the pit.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [create pit](ref/spell/create-pit), creator must
be a caster of at least 12th level; **Cost** 12,500 gp
