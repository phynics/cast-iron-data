**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 30\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This short metal rod is etched with the outlines of various weapons and is usually owned by
strong-armed rulers who keep a tight rein on their nation's military. A chastising baton adds 1 to
the saving throw DC of any compulsion spell cast by the wielder and adds the
[pain](ref/spelldef/pain) descriptor to the spell. Creatures that succeed at a Will save against a
compulsion spell cast by the wielder are racked with pain, taking 1d6 points of nonlethal damage and
becoming [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [pain strike](ref/spell/pain-strike); **Cost**
2,500 gp
