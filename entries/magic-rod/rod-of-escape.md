**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Wrought from [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron), this rod allows its wielder to escape from even
the tightest of bonds or grasps. If an opponent [grapples](ref/rule/grapple) the wielder of this
rod, the ends of the rod extend, flailing about in an attempt to free the user. The rod's wielder
gains a +5 bonus to CMD when attempting to evade or break out of a grapple; additionally, the holder
cannot be pinned while wielding the rod.

The rod's wielder gains a +5 competence bonus on [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) checks
when restrained by nonmagical and inanimate means, such as ropes or manacles. The rod's owner does
not need to be holding the rod to gains this bonus; it must merely be on the owner's person.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), [shatter](ref/spell/shatter); **Cost** 7,500 gp
