**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 17,312 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This iron rod is crowned with a metallic sphere and functions as a +1
[heavy mace](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-mace). In the hands of a divine spellcaster, the rod also counts as
a holy or unholy symbol and divine focus. If the wielder of a scepter of divine providence has the
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature, she can increase the radius of her
channeled energy [burst](ref/misc/burst) by 10 feet, at the cost of one additional use of her
channel energy ability. The wielder can expend additional uses of her channel energy ability to
further expand the burst radius, to a maximum of 30 additional feet.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Widen Spell](ref/feat/widen-spell), creator must
have the channel energy class feature; **Cost** 8,812 gp
