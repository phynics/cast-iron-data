## Rod of Metamagic, Quicken

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 190, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 485\
**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 35,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 75,500 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 170,000 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that are quickened as though using the Quicken Spell
feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Quicken Spell](ref/feat/quicken-spell); **Cost**
17,500 gp (lesser), 37,750 gp (normal), 85,000 gp (greater)
