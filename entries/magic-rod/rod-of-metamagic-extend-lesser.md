## Rod of Metamagic, Extend

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 189, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 485\
**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 11,000 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 24,500 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that are extended as though using the Extend Spell
feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell); **Cost**
1,500 gp (lesser), 5,500 gp (normal), 12,250 gp (greater)
