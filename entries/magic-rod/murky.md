**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 63\
**Aura** (no school); **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 5,500 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 12,250 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** -

The wielder of this rod can transform mist and fog spells into murky sediment underwater up to three
times per day as though using the Murky Spell feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement); **Cost** 750 gp (lesser), 2,750 gp (normal),
6,125 gp (greater)
