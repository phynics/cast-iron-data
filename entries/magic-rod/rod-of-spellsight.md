**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 14\
**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This beautiful ivory rod is embedded with countless sapphires and covered with intricate designs
inlaid in silver. The vision of a rod of spellsight's wielder is unaffected by any magical cloud,
fog, mist, smoke, or vapor effect. This allows the wielder to see through the effects of
[fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud), [obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist), and even
[incendiary cloud](ref/spell/incendiary-cloud). The rod does not protect the wearer against blinding
effects such as [blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness) or the blinding effect of
[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 6,000 gp
