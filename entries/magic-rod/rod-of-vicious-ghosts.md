**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 26,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Foreboding runes flow like shadows across the surface of this [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral)
rod. Up to three times per day while on the Ethereal Plane, the wielder of this short rod can affect
creatures on the Material Plane with spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
with the force descriptor. After using this ability, the wielder glows faintly and can be seen by
creatures on the Material Plane and the Ethereal Plane. This glowing effect lasts for 1 minute and
is otherwise identical to [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt);
**Cost** 13,000 gp
