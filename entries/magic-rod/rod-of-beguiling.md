**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 180\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This eye-catching rod is bedecked with glittering spangles. The wielder gains a +5 competence bonus
on Bluff and [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks, as well as on opposed Charisma
checks to give commands to charmed creatures.

A rod of beguiling allows a bard's [fascinate](ref/performance/fascinate),
[suggestion](ref/performance/suggestion), and [mass suggestion](ref/performance/mass-suggestion)
bardic performance to take effect as if she were 5 levels higher, and grants a +5 bonus on a bard's
Perform checks when using [distraction](ref/performance/distraction).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism); **Cost** 9,000
gp
