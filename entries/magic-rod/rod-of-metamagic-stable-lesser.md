## Rod of Metamagic, Stable

**Sources:** Wardens of the Reborn Forge pg 49\
**Aura** strong universal; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 11,000 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 24,500 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

Three times per day, the wielder can lower the chance of a spell he casts triggering a
[primal magic](ref/rule/primal-magic) event, as though using the Stable Spell feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Stable Spell](ref/feat/stable-spell); **Cost**
1,500 gp (lesser), 5,500 gp (normal), 12,250 gp (greater)
