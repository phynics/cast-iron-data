**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

An overflowing rod amplifies and focuses the side effects of a [kineticist's](ref/class/kineticist)
burn. While holding the rod, the kineticist counts as 3 levels higher for the purpose of determining
the effects of her elemental overflow ability. If used by a character without elemental overflow,
the rod grants its wielder the benefit of elemental overflow as a 3rd-level kineticist (which is
generally helpful only to low-level kineticists or kineticists with archetypes that replace
elemental overflow with another class feature).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), creator must be a 10th-level kineticist; **Cost**
12,500 gp
