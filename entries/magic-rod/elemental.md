**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 188, Advanced Player's Guide pg 294\
**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 11,000 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 24,500 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

Each elemental metamagic rod is built with the power to control and transform a specific energy type
(acid, cold, electricity, or fire). The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that deal damage
of the rod's energy type instead of the spell's normal damage type, as though using the Elemental
Spell feat. For example, an elemental metamagic rod (cold) always makes spells deal cold damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Elemental Spell](ref/feat/elemental-spell);
**Cost** 1,500 gp (lesser), 5,500 gp (normal), 12,250 gp (greater)
