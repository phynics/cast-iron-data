**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 32,500 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 73,000 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that sicken affected creatures as though using the
Sickening Spell feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Sickening Spell](ref/feat/sickening-spell);
**Cost** 4,500 gp (lesser), 16,250 gp (normal), 36,500 gp (greater)
