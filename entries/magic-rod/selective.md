**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 190, Advanced Player's Guide pg 296\
**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,000 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 11,000 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 24,500 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day as though using the Selective Spell feat. For each
spell, he can select up to four creatures of his choice within the area that are unaffected.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Selective Spell](ref/feat/selective-spell);
**Cost** 1,500 gp (lesser), 5,500 gp (normal), 12,250 gp (greater)
