**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 64\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This 3-foot-long iron rod has a flanged, conical head and strikes as a +1
[light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace). On command when held in hand, the rod points in the
direction of the closest grave, tomb, or cairn within 500 feet. If the wielder concentrates on a
specific type or subtype of creature, the rod identifies only gravesites that hold the remains of
that type of creature. The rod can search for the remains of a specific individual if the wielder
carries an item that the individual owned in life.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [locate object](ref/spell/locate-object); **Cost**
4,250 gp
