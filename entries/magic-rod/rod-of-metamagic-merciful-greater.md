## Rod of Metamagic, Merciful

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 190, Advanced Player's Guide pg 295\
**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp (lesser \[up to 3rd-level spells\]), 5,500 gp (normal \[up to
6th-level spells\]), 12,250 gp (greater \[all spells\]); **Weight** 5 lbs.

The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that deal nonlethal damage as though using the
Merciful Spell feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [Merciful Spell](ref/feat/merciful-spell);
**Cost** 750 gp (lesser), 2,750 gp (normal), 6,125 gp (greater)
