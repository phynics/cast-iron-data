**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This rod is valued by treasure hunters and miners alike, for it pulses and hums in the wielder's
hand in the proximity of metal. As the wearer aims the rod, the pulsations grow more noticeable as
it points to the largest mass of metal within 30 feet. However, the wielder can also concentrate on
a specific metal or mineral. If the specific mineral is within 30 feet, the rod points to any places
it is located, and the rod wielder knows the approximate quantity as well. If more than one deposit
of the specified metal or mineral is within range, the rod points to the largest cache first. Each
operation requires a full-round action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [locate object](ref/spell/locate-object); **Cost**
5,250 gp
