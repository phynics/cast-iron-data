**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 184, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 485\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 19,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This rod strikes as a +2 [heavy mace](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-mace). Once per day, upon the wielder's
command, the head of the rod becomes that of an actual serpent for 10 minutes. During this period,
any successful strike with the rod deals its usual damage and also poisons the creature hit. This
poison deals 1d3 points of [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per round for 6
rounds. Poisoned creatures can make a DC 16 Fortitude save each round to negate the damage and end
the affliction. Multiple hits extend the duration by 3 rounds and increase the DC by 2 for each hit.
The rod only functions if its possessor is evil.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod),
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), [poison](ref/spell/poison),
creator must be evil; **Cost** 9,500 gp
