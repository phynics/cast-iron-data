**Sources:** People of the Sands pg 29\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This unusual type of [immovable rod](ref/magic-rod/immovable-rod) has a button near each end, and it
can be activated only by two separate creatures, each one pressing a button while speaking a
different command word. Once set in place, it can only be moved by the same method. A creature using
a Strength check to move the inviolate marker risks being affected by a
_[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ effect (-6 Str; Will DC 16 negates and grants the creature
immunity to the curse effect for 24 hours).

An inviolate marker projects an illusory banner or crest from each side, each chosen by the creature
that activated it, and is used to demarcate territorial boundaries. If an inviolate marker would be
buried by a change in the landscape or environment, the marker teleports to the surface of the newly
formed terrain in order to maintain its position relative to the new terrain.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse),
[levitate](ref/spell/levitate), [silent image](ref/spell/silent-image); **Cost** 5,000 gp
