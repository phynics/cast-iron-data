**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 44,200 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This copper rod is made up of constantly grinding gear-like parts. It is believed that these rods
may be some alternate or lesser form of [harbinger archons](ref/monster/archon-harbinger-archon).
The rod can take may useful forms, and after 24 hours in the possession of a lawful good creature,
the wielder can command it to take the form of any tool, including a
[chime of opening](ref/magic-wondrous/chime-of-opening). Very intelligent and witty, these rods
communicate with their wielders in truespeech (see [archon subtype](ref/subtype/archon)) or
Celestial. Furthermore, the rod has the following powers.

- Three times per day, on command, the rod's wielder can use
  _[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door),_ as the spell.
- Once per day, on command, the rod can emit a blast of energy that deals 2d6 points of force damage
  to creatures adjacent to the wielder. A successful DC 12 Reflex save halves the damage. The
  wielder can choose to omit any target from this blast that it desires, damaging only the foes it
  chooses.

The rod's [purpose](ref/rule/special-purpose-items) is to destroy or foil the plans of chaotic evil
creatures.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door),
[knock](ref/spell/knock), [explosive runes](ref/spell/explosive-runes), creator must be lawful good;
**Price** 22,100 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** lawful good; **Ego** 10\
**Senses** 30 ft.\
**Int** 10, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 12\
**Communication** telepathy (Celestial; truespeech)
