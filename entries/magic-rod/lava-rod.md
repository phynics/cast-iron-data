**Aura** strong evocation \[fire\]; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 80,000 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This irregularly shaped iron rod is studded with orange gems. It can be wielded as a +2
[club](ref/eq-weapon/club). For 10 rounds per day, the rod can be wielded as a +2
[flaming burst](ref/magic-enh/flaming-burst) club. These rounds need not be consecutive.
Additionally, three times per day as a standard action, a creature wielding this rod can use it to
spray a 30-foot cone of lava droplets that deals 13d6 points of fire damage (Reflex DC 20 half).
Once per day as a standard action, the wielder can instead release a cone of harmless pebbles that
fall to the ground and cover the area. These pebbles transform into tiny blobs of lava up to 5
rounds later. The user determines the round upon which the pebbles transform into lava when
initially activating the rod for this effect. Any creature in the area of the pebbles when they
transform takes 13d6 points of fire damage (Reflex DC 20 half).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod),
[delayed blast fireball](ref/spell/delayed-blast-fireball); **Cost** 40,000 gp
