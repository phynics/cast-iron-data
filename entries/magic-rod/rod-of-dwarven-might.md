**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 181, Advanced Race Guide pg 19\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 19th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 80,000 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This dwarven version of the more famous [rod of lordly might](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-lordly-might) has
no spell-like powers; however, when wielded by a [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), it increases all AC,
attack roll, CMD, CMB, and saving throw bonuses from a dwarf's racial traits by +1. The rod shares
the mundane powers of a rod of lordly might, but its magical weapon forms are tailored to dwarven
preferences.

- In its normal form, the rod can be used as a +1 [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning)
  [light hammer](ref/eq-weapon/light-hammer).
- When button 1 is pushed, the rod becomes a +3
  [dwarven longhammer](ref/eq-weapon/longhammer-dwarven).
- When button 2 is pushed, the rod becomes a +4 [dwarven waraxe](ref/eq-weapon/dwarven-waraxe).
- When button 3 is pushed, the rod becomes a +2 [light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) or +2
  [heavy crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-crossbow).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Cost** 40,000 gp
