**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 183, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 485\
**Aura** strong varies; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 37,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This device negates the spell or spell-like function or functions of magic items. The wielder points
the rod at the magic item, and a pale gray beam shoots forth to touch the target device, attacking
as a ray (a ranged touch attack). The ray functions as a
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater) spell, except it only affects magic items. To
negate instantaneous effects from an item, the rod's wielder needs to have a
[readied action](ref/rule/ready). The dispel check uses the rod's caster level (15th). The target
item gets no saving throw, although the rod can't negate [artifacts](ref/rule/artifacts) (even minor
artifacts). A rod of negation can function three times per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) or [miracle](ref/spell/miracle); **Cost** 18,500 gp
