**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 20\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This lengthy rod looks like a walking cane with a stylized crystal skull at its top. As a standard
action up to three times per day, the wielder can slam the base of the rod into the ground while
designating a target within 60 feet. Grasping hands of ectoplasm manifest from the ground
surrounding the target in a 5-foot radius. Creatures within the radius must succeed at a DC 14
Reflex save or they are [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) by the grasping hands and unable to move
more than 10 feet from the affected area (as if snared by a [net](ref/eq-weapon/net) with a trailing
rope). Creatures moving through the ectoplasm must also succeed at a Reflex save or be entangled. As
a standard action, a creature entangled by the ectoplasm can attempt an additional DC 14 Reflex save
to attempt to escape and end the effect. The summoned ectoplasm lasts for 10 rounds before
disappearing.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), [ectoplasmic snare](ref/spell/ectoplasmic-snare);
**Cost** 9,000 gp
