**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 254\
**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 121,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Slightly smaller and shorter than [vril staves](ref/magic-staff/vril-staff), hollow rods are hollow
silver tubes covered in intricate glyphs and small, flute-like keys. Three times per day, a
[kineticist](ref/class/kineticist) can utilize the hollow rod in conjunction with a blast wild
talent or the [kinetic healer](ref/kinetic/kinetic-healer) wild talent to augment that talent's
destructive or healing capabilities, treating the wild talent as
[maximized](ref/feat/maximize-spell). A hollow rod can't be used on a wild talent affected by
metakinesis.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Rod](ref/feat/craft-rod), creator must be a 10th-level kineticist; **Cost**
60,750 gp
