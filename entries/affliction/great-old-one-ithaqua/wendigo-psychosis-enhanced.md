**Type:** Curse (Curse-unspeakable presence or wind walk)

**Save:** Will DC 36

**Frequency:** 1/hour

**Effect:** 1d6 [Wis drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) (minimum Wis 1). When a victim's Wisdom
reaches 1, it seeks an individual of its race to kill and devour. After completing this act, the
victim takes off at a run, and in 1d4 rounds sprints up into the sky at such a speed that its feet
burn away into jagged stumps. The victim transforms into a wendigo over the next 1d4 minutes. Once
the transformation is complete, the victim is effectively dead, replaced by a wendigo.
[Miracle](ref/spell/miracle), [true resurrection](ref/spell/true-resurrection), or
[wish](ref/spell/wish) can restore such a victim to life, yet doing so does not harm the new
wendigo.

The save DC is Charisma-based.
