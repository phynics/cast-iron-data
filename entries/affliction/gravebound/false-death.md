**Type:** Disease (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Charisma damage 1d4

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

The save DC is Charisma-based. A creature whose Charisma score is reduced to 0 by false death falls
into a deep coma and appears to be dead to anyone who doesn't succeed at a DC 25 Heal check. After
one week, the affected creature wakes up with all of its Charisma restored.
