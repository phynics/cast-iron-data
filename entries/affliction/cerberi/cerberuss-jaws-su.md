**Type:** Curse (bite)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Effect:** A creature affected by this curse cannot utilize extradimensional travel such as
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport), as if it were under the effect of a
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor) spell.

The save DC is Con-based.
