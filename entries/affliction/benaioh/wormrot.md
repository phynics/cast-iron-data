**Type:** Disease (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 20

**Onset:** 1 minute

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d3 and [sickened](ref/misc/sickened)

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

The save DC is Constitution-based. A creature that dies of wormrot transforms into a benaioh over
the course of the next 24 hours.
