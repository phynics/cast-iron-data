**Type:** Disease (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 19

**Onset:** 1 round

**Frequency:** 12 hours

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d2 and this damage cannot be healed while the creature is infected

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Any creature that dies while infected rises as a plague zombie in 2d6 hours. The save DC is
Constitution-based.
