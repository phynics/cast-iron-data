**Type:** Disease (Slam-contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d2, Constitution damage 1, and [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued)

**Cure:** 1 save

Any creature that touches a slime mold with an unarmed strike or a natural attack is also exposed to
this foul disease. The save DC is Constitution-based.
