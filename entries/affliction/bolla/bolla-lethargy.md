**Type:** Curse (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 23

**Frequency:** 1/round

**Effect:** 3d10 nonlethal damage and [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued)

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

A creature that falls unconscious while subject to this curse stops taking nonlethal damage, falls
asleep, and cannot be woken until the curse is removed. The victim ages normally, but does not
require any nourishment. The save DC is Charisma-based.
