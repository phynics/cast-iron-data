**Type:** Disease (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 22

**Onset:** immediate

**Effect:** Dexterity damage 1d4 and Constitution damage 1d3

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

The save DC is Charisma-based. A humanoid that succumbs to Leng ghoul fever becomes a normal ghoul
unless in life it had 12 or more Hit Dice, in which case it rises from death as a Leng ghoul.
