**Type:** Curse (injury)

**Save:** Will DC 14

**Effect:** The creature takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, ability checks, and
skill checks. A creature can be affected by a curse of ill luck multiple times (up to a maximum
cumulative penalty of -4).

Chickcharneys are immune to this curse. The save DC is Charisma-based.
