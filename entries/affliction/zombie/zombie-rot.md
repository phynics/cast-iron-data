**Type:** Disease (Injury-slam)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the zombie's HD + its Cha modifier

**Onset:** 1d4 days

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d2 and this damage cannot be healed while the creature is infected

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Anyone who dies while infected rises as a plague zombie in 2d6 hours.
