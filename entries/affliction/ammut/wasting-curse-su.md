**Type:** Curse (Swallow whole-contact)

**Save:** Will DC 29

**Frequency:** 1/hour

**Effect:** Charisma drain 1d4

A creature whose Charisma score is reduced to 0 by this wasting curse dies; its body is destroyed
and it is subject to the ammut's devour soul ability as if it had died while within the ammut's gut.
The save DC is Charisma-based.
