**Type:** Disease (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the Mana Wastes mutant's HD + its Con modifier

**Onset:** 1d4 minutes

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d2 and Charisma drain 1d2

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

If the base creature has 8 HD or more, the Con damage and Cha drain both increase to 1d3.
