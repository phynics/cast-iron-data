**Type:** Disease (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** 1d3 days

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** 1d3 Str and 1d3 Dex damage, target must succeed at a second Fortitude save or 1 point of
each type of ability damage is drain instead

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves - the secondary save to keep damage from becoming drain does not count
toward this requirement.

The save DC is Constitution-based.
