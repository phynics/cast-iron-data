**Type:** Disease (Iron feather or wing-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 23

**Onset:** immediate

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** 1d4 [Dex damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) and 1 Con drain. Each time a victim
takes Dexterity damage from scarlet tetanus, there is a 50% chance its jaw muscles and joints
stiffen, preventing speech and the use of spells with verbal components for 24 hours

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

The save DC is Constitution-based.
