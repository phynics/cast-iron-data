**Type:** Disease (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 13

**Onset:** 1d3 days

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d2 and Wisdom damage 1d2

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

A creature afflicted by ubashki fever develops unsightly splotches and sores all over its body that
persist until the disease is cured. The save DC is Charisma-based.
