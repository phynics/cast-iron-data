A character with this ability can no longer be flanked. This defense denies rogues (or other classes
with the [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) ability) the ability to sneak attack them by
flanking them, unless the attacker has at least four more rogue levels (or levels in the class
granting sneak attack) than the target has levels in the class that grants improved uncanny dodge.

This ability is gained at 5th level by the [bloodrager](ref/class/bloodrager),
[barbarian](ref/class/barbarian), and [unchained barbarian](ref/class/unchained-barbarian), and at
8th level by the [rogue](ref/class/rogue), [unchained rogue](ref/class/unchained-rogue),
[ninja](ref/class/ninja), and [skald](ref/class/skald).

If a character already has uncanny dodge from another class, the levels from the classes that grant
uncanny dodge stack to determine the minimum sneak attack level required to flank the character.
