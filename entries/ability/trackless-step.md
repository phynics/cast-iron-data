**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook\
Starting at 3rd level, a [druid](ref/class/druid) leaves no trail in natural surroundings and cannot
be tracked. She may choose to leave a trail if so desired.

A [shifter](ref/class/shifter) gains this feature at 5th level.
