A kineticist can choose from among the following elements. Infusion wild talents are marked with a
dagger (†); all other wild talents listed below from 1st-9th are utility wild talents.

### Aether

Kineticists who focus on the element of aether - a rare substance formed when elemental energy
affects the Ethereal Plane - are called telekineticists. Telekineticists use strands of aether to
move objects with their minds.
