**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook\
This is an improved version of [evasion](ref/ability/evasion). The character still takes no damage
on a successful Reflex saving throw against attacks while wearing light armor or no armor, but
henceforth they take only half damage on a failed save. A [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) character
does not gain the benefit of improved evasion.

This ability is gained by [familiars](ref/sidekick/familiar) at 1st level, and at 9th level by the
[monk](ref/class/monk) and [unchained monk](ref/class/unchained-monk). Rogues and unchained rogues
can access it via the [improved evasion](ref/talent/improved-evasion) advanced rogue talent. An
[eidolon](ref/sidekick/eidolon) or [unchained eidolon](ref/sidekick/unchained-eidolon) gains this
ability at 14th level, and an [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) gains it at 15th
level. The [ranger](ref/class/ranger) gains it at 16th level, and it applies when wearing medium
armor, too.
