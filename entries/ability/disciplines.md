The following disciplines represent those most commonly chosen by psychics. Followers of a
discipline might come to it in a variety of different ways, and though some might join together in
orders, others follow personal, singular interpretations of the discipline.

|Discipline|Pool Stat|Description|
| --- | --- | --- |
|[Abomination](ref/discipline/abomination)|Charisma|Your mind is impure, tainted by outside forces.|
|[Bleaching](ref/discipline/bleaching)|Wisdom|You draw power from a void in your emotions, a yawning and empty chasm where your hopes and dreams and fears should be.|
|[Dream](ref/discipline/dream)|Charisma|You discover deeper and more powerful corners of your mind through journeys you make in your dreams.|
|[Enlightenment](ref/discipline/enlightenment)|Wisdom|Your quest for enlightenment has opened your eyes to new concepts and heights of spiritual awareness.|
|[Faith](ref/discipline/faith)|Wisdom|Your belief in a higher power fuels your psychic abilities.|
|[Ferocity](ref/discipline/ferocity)|Wisdom|You are in touch with the most primal part of your subconscious.|
|[Hag-Called](ref/discipline/hag-called)|Charisma|A hag's call echoes through your mind like a siren's song, enticing you to surrender and become something horrible.|
|[Haunted](ref/discipline/haunted)|Charisma|You attract the attention of spirits as naturally as a lodestone attracts iron filings.|
|[Lore](ref/discipline/lore)|Wisdom|By collecting expert information and reading all sorts of treatises and chapbooks on occult subjects, you learn proper techniques and rituals to empower your mental abilities.|
|[Mindtech](ref/discipline/mindtech)|Wisdom|You are innately tied to and can communicate with electronic machinery and similar technological objects, through which you can funnel your psychic power like flowing water.|
|[Pageantry](ref/discipline/pageantry)|Charisma|The act of ritual, no matter how ostentatious, can be a route to mental strength.|
|[Pain](ref/discipline/pain)|Charisma|Mental blocks prevent your immense inborn psychic energies from flowing freely.|
|[Psychedelia](ref/discipline/psychedelia)|Wisdom|You ingest hallucinogens to expand your mind.|
|[Rapport](ref/discipline/rapport)|Charisma|Forging a close bond with other people brings out the best in you, making it easier for you to link your mind with theirs.|
|[Rebirth](ref/discipline/rebirth)|Charisma|Your mind is forged from the most powerful memories of a multitude of lifetimes.|
|[Rivethun](ref/discipline/rivethun)|Charisma|You have embraced those aspects of your body that cause you agony, respecting the perspective they grant you if not necessarily the roles they've condemned you to in life.|
|[Self-Perfection](ref/discipline/self-perfection)|Wisdom|You take a holistic view of psychic power, believing that you must hone both your mental and physical attributes to remain in balance.|
|[Shadow](ref/discipline/shadow)|Wisdom|You perceive the space between light and darkness, and draw power from it.|
|[Sorrow](ref/discipline/sorrow)|Charisma|You draw strength from deep feelings of abandonment, loneliness, and sadness, turning these emotions into a powerful weapon or a barrier that protects you.|
|[Superiority](ref/discipline/superiority)|Charisma|You know that you are far better than average creatures of your kind.|
|[Symbiosis](ref/discipline/symbiosis)|Charisma|Like pollen on the wind or the roots of plants in the ground, your thoughts permeate your surroundings, allowing you to study and manipulate the world around you.|
|[Tranquility](ref/discipline/tranquility)|Wisdom|Psychic magic flows through you when you attain a peaceful mental state.|
|[Warp](ref/discipline/warp)|Charisma|Ratfolk mythology claims they were appointed by the gods to sniff out the ragged edges of creation.|
