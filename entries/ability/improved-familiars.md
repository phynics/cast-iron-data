**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook\
The [Improved Familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar) feat allows you to acquire a powerful familiar,
but only when you could normally acquire a new familiar.

When choosing a familiar, the creatures listed below are also available to you. You may choose a
familiar with an alignment up to one step away on each alignment axis (lawful through chaotic, good
through evil).

Improved familiars otherwise use the rules for regular [familiars](ref/sidekick/familiar), with two
exceptions: if the creature's type is something other than animal, its type does not change; and
improved familiars do not gain the ability to speak with other creatures of their kind (although
many of them already have the ability to communicate).

The familiars below are organized according to the level the master must be, and the alignment the
master must be within one step of.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-t}

This may be replaced with any other animal from the standard [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) list,
with the appropriate template ([celestial](ref/template/celestial),
[entropic](ref/template/entropic), [fey-touched](ref/template/fey-touched-creature),
[fiendish](ref/template/fiendish), or [resolute](ref/template/resolute)) applied to it.

###### ^5^ {#footnote-h}

The master must first create the homunculus.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-p}

The pyrausta's heart spark glows slightly less brightly, but more steadily. This removes its
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) ability and heart of flame special abilities.

###### ^3^ {#footnote-e}

The spellcaster's alignment **must** match this eactly, instead of being within one step of the
familiar's alignment.

###### ^6^ {#footnote-n}

The spellcaster's alignment **must** be True Neutral, or the spellcaster must be a
[duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker) with the [Personal Chronicler](ref/feat/personal-chronicler) feat.
