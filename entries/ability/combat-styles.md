At 2nd level, a ranger must select a combat style to pursue. The ranger's expertise manifests in the
form of bonus feats at 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th level. He can choose feats from his selected
combat style, even if he does not have the normal prerequisites.

**Archery**

**At 1st Level:** [Far Shot](ref/feat/far-shot), [Focused Shot](ref/feat/focused-shot),
[Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot), [Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot), and
[Rapid Shot](ref/feat/rapid-shot).^1^

**At 10th Level:** Add [Pinpoint Targeting](ref/feat/pinpoint-targeting) and
[Shot on the Run](ref/feat/shot-on-the-run) to the list.

**At 6th Level:** Add [Crossbow Mastery](ref/feat/crossbow-mastery),
[Improved Precise Shot](ref/feat/improved-precise-shot), [Manyshot](ref/feat/manyshot), and
[Point-Blank Master](ref/feat/point-blank-master) ^2^ to the list.
