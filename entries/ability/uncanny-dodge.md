**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook\
A character with this ability can react to danger before their senses would normally allow them to
do so. They cannot be caught [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed), nor do they lose their Dexterity
bonus to AC if the attacker is invisible. They still loses their Dexterity bonus to AC if
immobilized, or if an opponent successfully uses the [feint](ref/rule/feint) action against them.

This ability is gained at 2nd level by the [bloodrager](ref/class/bloodrager),
[barbarian](ref/class/barbarian), and [unchained barbarian](ref/class/unchained-barbarian). It is
gained at 4th level by the [rogue](ref/class/rogue), [unchained rogue](ref/class/unchained-rogue),
[ninja](ref/class/ninja), and [skald](ref/class/skald).

If a member of these classes already has uncanny dodge from a different class, they automatically
gain [improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) instead.
