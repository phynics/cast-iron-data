**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook\
A [rogue](ref/class/rogue) or [investigator](ref/class/investigator) adds 1/2 her level to
Perception skill checks made to locate traps and to Disable Device skill checks (minimum +1). A
rogue or investigator can use Disable Device to disarm magic traps.
