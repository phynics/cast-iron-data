Starting at 2nd level, a [druid](ref/class/druid) may move through any sort of undergrowth (such as
natural thorns, briars, overgrown areas, and similar terrain) at her normal speed and without taking
damage or suffering any other impairment. Thorns, briars, and overgrown areas that have been
magically manipulated to impede motion, however, still affect her.

[Shifters](ref/class/shifter) gain this feature at 3rd level. A [hunter](ref/class/hunter) and their
animal companion gain it at 5th level, while [rangers](ref/class/ranger) gain it at 7th level.
