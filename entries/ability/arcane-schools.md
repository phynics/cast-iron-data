The following descriptions detail each arcane school and its corresponding powers.

### Focused Arcane Schools

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 143\
Wizards that specialize in one of the schools of magic can instead chose to focus their studies
still further. Wizards who choose a focused arcane school retain the bonus spells from their school
and must select two prohibited schools as normal, but the focused school changes one or more of the
powers granted by their arcane school. A wizard must take all of the replacement powers associated
with his focused arcane school. Once a focused arcane school is chosen, it cannot be changed.

### Thassilonian Magic

**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 63, Inner Sea Magic pg 17\
While the concept of wizard specializations along the classically recognized schools of magic
certainly dates back to the time of Azlant, and perhaps beyond into the dim recesses of time before
humanity rose to prominence, it was the runelords of Thassilon who explored school specialization to
its full extreme.

Focusing their research on the discovery that each school of magic was opposed by two specific
opposition schools, the runelords developed methods of further enhancing their mastery over their
chosen arcane specialties. Essentially, they defined these seven schools as much by what they
couldn't do as by what they could. By excising "impurities" introduced by fixed oppositional
schools, they traded arcane versatility for greater strength in their chosen fields. The seven
schools of [Thassilonian Magic](ref/arcaneschool/thassilonian-magic) are presented here.

### Elemental Arcane Schools

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 142, Elemental Master's Handbook pg 21\
Instead of specializing in one of the standard eight schools of magic, a wizard can choose to
specialize in one of the four elemental schools of magic. Like a normal arcane school, an elemental
school grants a number of school powers and one bonus spell slot of each level the wizard can cast,
from 1st on up. This bonus spell slot must be used to prepare a spell from the elemental school's
spell list. Unlike a normal arcane school, each elemental school requires the wizard to select his
opposed element as his opposition school (air opposes earth, fire opposes water). He does not need
to select a second opposition school. He must expend two spell slots to prepare a spell from his
opposed elemental school as normal.

Wizards who specialize in an elemental arcane school can further refine their studies by choosing a
**focused school.** Wizards who choose a focused school keep the bonus spells from their elemental
school and must select an opposition elemental school as normal, but a focused school replaces some
of the powers granted by their elemental school. Once a focused school is chosen, it can't be
changed.

#### Five Elements Variant

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 88\
In some lands, scholars of magic insist that material things consist of five elements, not four:
fire, earth, metal, water, and wood. Rather than directly opposing each other, these five elements
counter and generate each other in a wheel-like formation: wood overcomes earth, earth overcomes
water, water overcomes fire, fire overcomes metal, and metal overcomes wood. Similar to the four
types of elemental wizard schools, some wizards specialize in the schools of magic based on metal or
wood. Like a normal arcane school, an elemental school grants a number of school powers and one
bonus spell slot of each level the wizard can cast, from 1st on up. This bonus spell slot must be
used to prepare a spell from the elemental school's spell list. Unlike a normal arcane school, each
elemental school requires the wizard to select his opposed element as his opposition school - in the
case of the five-element system, he must choose the element that overcomes his element as his
opposition element (so a metal elementalist must select fire as his opposition school because fire
overcomes metal). He does not need to select a second opposition school. He must expend two spell
slots to prepare a spell from his opposed elemental school as normal.

|Elemental School|Focused Schools|Opposition Element|
| --- | --- | --- |
|[Aether](ref/arcaneschool/aether)|—|Any one|
|[Air](ref/arcaneschool/air)|Ice, Smoke|Earth|
|[Earth](ref/arcaneschool/earth)|Magma, Mud|Air (classical) or Wood (five elements)|
|[Fire](ref/arcaneschool/fire)|Magma, Smoke|Water|
|[Metal](ref/arcaneschool/metal)|—|Fire|
|[Void](ref/arcaneschool/void)|—|Any one|
|[Water](ref/arcaneschool/water)|Ice, Mud|Fire (classical) or Earth (five elements)|
|[Wood](ref/arcaneschool/wood)|—|Metal|

_Dragon Empires Primer_ later added the _void_ element, while _Elemental Master's Handbook_ added
the _aether_ element. Both elements do not have a defined opposition element; a wizard that
specializes in these elements chooses another element as their opposition school. He does not need
to select a second opposition school.
