**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Keleshite

**Flavor:** You grew up racing along the plains of Paresh, hoping to one day compete in the
Histaqen.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks, and a +1
trait bonus on [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks. This trait applies only to horses.
