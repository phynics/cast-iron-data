**Prerequisite:** Member of the Grand Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You resist attempts to dissuade you from obeying the Decemvirate's will. You gain a +1 trait bonus
on saving throws against enchantment spells and spell-like abilities.
