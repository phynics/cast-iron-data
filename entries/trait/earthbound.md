**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Benefit:** Your druid spells use a handful of earth or sand as a divine focus in addition to the
normal components. While you are touching the ground, you add a +2 trait bonus to saving throw DCs
and on caster level checks to overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) for all spells
you cast against creatures with the [air](ref/subtype/air) subtype.
