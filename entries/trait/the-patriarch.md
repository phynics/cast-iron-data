**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 21 Sarenith and 20 Erastus

**Flavor:** Like the father of the Cosmic Caravan, you have an innate sense of direction.

**Benefit:** Three times per day, you can use [know direction](ref/spell/know-direction) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), treating your character level as your caster
level.
