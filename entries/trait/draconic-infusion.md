**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You were exposed to an artifact or other powerful magic that infused you with the power
of dragons.

**Benefit:** Choose the acid, cold, electricity, or fire spell descriptor when you take this trait.
Once per day for every 2 caster levels you have (minimum once per day), when you cast a spell that
has your chosen elemental descriptor, you can deal an additional 1d4 points of damage of that energy
type to one target of that spell.
