**Religion:** [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria)

**Flavor:** You are well versed in the arts of misdirection, plotting, and intrigue, and you know
how to recognize these tactics when they're used against you.

**Benefit:** You receive a +1 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks, and
Sense Motive is a class skill for you.
