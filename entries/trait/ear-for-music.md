**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn)

**Flavor:** You spent countless hours of your youth in one of Shelyn's temples, listening to
wonderful musicians and singers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on one category of [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) checks
and a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks that deal with the
local art or music scene.
