**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** Memories of the cruel abuse and taunts you suffered as a child cause you to strike back
with great fury at anyone who slanders you.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls made against creatures that have insulted you
until you actually succeed in dealing damage.
