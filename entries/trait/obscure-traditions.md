**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf); Snowcaster

**Flavor:** You confound those who think they know about elves.

**Benefit:** Any creature attempting to make a [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) check about your
people, a [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) check to determine whether you are lying, or a
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) check to track you takes a -2 penalty on the check.

Additionally, any character or creature that normally gains a bonus on attack or damage rolls
against elves (such as a ranger's [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) ability) has such a
bonus reduced by 1 (minimum 0).
