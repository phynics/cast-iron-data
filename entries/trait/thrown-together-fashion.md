**Type:** Equipment

**Flavor:** You can modify old clothing to make it effective in new circumstances.

**Benefit:** With a basic sewing kit or [disguise kit](ref/eq-misc/disguise-kit), you can make a
[Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) check to change one outfit into another outfit. The DC of this check
is equal to 10 + the gp difference between costs of the outfits, as found in the outfits' entries.
The alteration takes 10 minutes per 1-gp difference in the cost of the outfits.
