**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma)

**Flavor:** Instructed at a young age in the tenets of the faith of Pharasma, you view the undead as
abominations that must be destroyed so their souls can journey beyond to be judged.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on weapon damage rolls against undead.
