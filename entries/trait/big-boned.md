**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Hungerseed

**Flavor:** Your ancestors' great size has gifted you with an exceptionally sturdy frame.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on combat maneuver checks made to [overrun](ref/rule/overrun)
opponents, as well as a +1 trait bonus to your CMD against [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempts.
