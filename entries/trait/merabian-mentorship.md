**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Suli](ref/race/suli)

**Region:** Thuvia

**Flavor:** You were apprenticed to a renowned alchemist of Merab, and she taught you how to
recognize elemental reagents by taste.

**Benefit:** Upon imbibing a potion containing a spell on your formula list of a spell level you are
capable of casting, you can attempt a [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) check (DC 15 + extract
level). On a success, you can immediately add the spell to your formula book.
