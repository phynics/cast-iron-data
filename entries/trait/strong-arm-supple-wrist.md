**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You have been trained in the javelin and the spear, and you know how to make the best
use of your momentum.

**Benefit:** When you move at least 10 feet before making an attack with a thrown weapon, you can
add 10 feet to the range increment of the weapon thrown. You can gain this benefit only once per
round.
