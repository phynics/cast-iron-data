**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You are often struck with epiphanies about alchemical processes and substances.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **free action**, you may gain a trait bonus equal to your Charisma
modifier (minimum 0) on a [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (alchemy) check you make. You can apply this
bonus after you roll the check, possibly turning a failure into a success.
