**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Region:** Galt

**Flavor:** Your time spent amid angry mobs has taught you exactly how to use crowds of people to
distract your opponents.

**Benefit:** If you have at least three allies threatening your opponent, you gain a +2 bonus on
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to feint him and a +2 bonus on
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks against him.
