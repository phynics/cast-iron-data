**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You are skilled at subduing your enemies.

**Benefit:** Whenever you attack one of your [favored enemies](ref/ability/favored-enemy) with a
weapon that deals lethal damage and choose to deal nonlethal damage, you take only a -2 penalty on
the attack roll instead of the normal -4.
