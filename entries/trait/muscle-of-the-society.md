**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your intense training with the Swords has taught you how to infiltrate otherwise
inaccessible ruins and extract their treasures without too much exertion.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Strength checks made to break doors and lift portcullises,
and you treat your Strength score as 2 higher for the purpose of determining your carrying capacity.
