**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker)

**Flavor:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against curses.

**Benefit:** You treat your caster level as 1 higher when casting spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with the curse descriptor.
