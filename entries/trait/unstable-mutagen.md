**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You discovered or were given a secret to make your mutagens more unstable - but also
more potent.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can create an _unstable mutagen._ It is like a normal mutagen in most
ways, but also gains a single benefit or hindrance due to its instability. Roll 1d6 to determine the
result of the instability.

|1d6|Instability|
| --- | --- |
|1|The _unstable mutagen_ lasts for 5 minutes per alchemist level.|
|2|The _unstable mutagen_ lasts for 20 minutes per alchemist level.|
|3|The _unstable mutagen_ does not grant a natural armor bonus.|
|4|The natural armor bonus granted by the mutagen increases by 2.|
|5|The penalty that the mutagen applies to the corresponding mental ability score increases by 2.|
|6|The _unstable mutagen_ does not apply a penalty to the corresponding mental ability score.|
