**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae)

**Flavor:** Iomedae's valor fills you with divine energy, allowing you to stave off even the worst
plague.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can reroll a saving throw against a disease or poison. You must take
the second result, even if it is worse.
