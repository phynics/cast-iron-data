**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Oread](ref/race/oread)

**Flavor:** You are skilled at blending in with the desert terrain and attacking creatures as they
pass by.

**Benefit:** When [prone](ref/rule/prone) in desert terrain, you gain a +2 trait bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks and take only a -2 penalty on attack rolls (rather than -4) when
attacking from prone.
