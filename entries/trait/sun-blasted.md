**Type:** Regional

**Region:** desert

**Flavor:** Desert orcs have learnt to survive with practically nothing in the desert.

**Benefit:** You receive a +2 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made in a desert,
and resolve all checks at becoming lost in deserts as though you had proper navigational tools (a +4
bonus). The presence of such tools does not increase your ability, however.
