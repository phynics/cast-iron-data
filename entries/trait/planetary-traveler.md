**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Flavor:** You take every opportunity to learn about or visit far-off planets.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography),
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local), and [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature)
checks regarding other planets.
