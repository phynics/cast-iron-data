**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Changeling](ref/race/changeling)

**Flavor:** You possess natural magical ability.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following spells: [bleed](ref/spell/bleed),
[create water](ref/spell/create-water), [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights),
[detect good](ref/spell/detect-good), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[putrefy food and drink](ref/spell/putrefy-food-and-drink). You can cast this spell three times per
day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).
