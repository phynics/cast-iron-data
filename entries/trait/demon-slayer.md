**Type:** Combat

**Prerequisite:** Mendev Crusaders

**Flavor:** By studying a demonic opponent, you can sense and target its weak spots.

**Benefit:** You can attempt a [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) check to find a
demon's weak spot. (DC 10 + the demon's CR). If you succeed, you gain a +3 trait bonus on rolls to
confirm critical hits against that [demon](ref/family/demon) until the end of the battle. This
effect only works on demons.
