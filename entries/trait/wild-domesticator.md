**Prerequisite:** [Half-elf](ref/race/half-elf)

**Benefit:** [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) is always a class skill for you. You gain a +2
trait bonus on Handle Animal checks to train an animal, and can teach a trained animal one
additional trick beyond its normal maximum.
