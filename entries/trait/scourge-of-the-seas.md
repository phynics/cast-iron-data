**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** You have long appreciated the flamboyance and versatility of the whip, both as a weapon
and a tool for enforcing discipline.

**Benefit:** When you wield a whip or scourge, you gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. Additionally, once per day when you use a
[whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) or _scourge_ to perform a successful [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) or
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver against an opponent, you regain 1 point of grit or panache.

There are no official stats for a scourge, but the [Duergar Slaver](ref/npc/duergar-slaver)
describes a scourge as "a [cat-o'-nine-tails](ref/eq-weapon/cat-o-nine-tails) that deals lethal
damage."
