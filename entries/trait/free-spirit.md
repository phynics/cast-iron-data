**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You respect the freedom of others to speak as they will. Your rough-and-tumble youth
taught you that everyone deserves a chance to say her piece. After all, you can always simply choose
to ignore the speaker.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against language-dependent and effects with
the sonic descriptor. In addition, the DC to [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) you increases by 2.

**Suggested Characters:** Andorens, Desnans, Erastil worshipers.
