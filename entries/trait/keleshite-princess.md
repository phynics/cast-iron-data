**Type:** Social

**Prerequisite:** Keleshite; female

**Region:** Qadira

**Flavor:** You have been raised to expect obedience from those around you, regardless of their
station, and are not shy about demanding what you want.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class
skill for you.
