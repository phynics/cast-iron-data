**Type:** Combat

**Prerequisite:** Associated with the [Ulfen Guard](ref/rule/f-imperial)

**Flavor:** Your determination allows you to hold onto a shred of your rational mind at all times.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following skills: [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist), [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal), or
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth). You may use this skill normally while raging.
