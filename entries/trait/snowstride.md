**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Darklands

**Benefit:** The wide stance and sure footing you developed while traipsing over ice-covered rock
and frozen earth provides you with a +1 trait bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks
made on slippery surfaces and enables you to [trip](ref/rule/trip) or
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) opponents two size categories larger than you instead of just one.
