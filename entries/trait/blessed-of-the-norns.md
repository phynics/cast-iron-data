**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Lands of the Linnorm Kings

**Flavor:** You or one of your parents belonged to a coven that honored the norns - powerful fey
seers - and you still bear their favor in the form of a subtle sense for danger.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to notice traps
and ambushes, and a +1 trait bonus to your AC against traps and during any surprise round in which
you are caught unaware.
