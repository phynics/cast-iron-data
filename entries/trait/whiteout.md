**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Undine](ref/race/undine)

**Flavor:** When the air is full of water, your elemental blood allows you to blend with your
surroundings and become one with the precipitation.

**Benefit:** In areas of nonmagical fog, rain, snow, or similar conditions (such as the spray of a
waterfall), you gain the effects of [concealment](ref/rule/concealment), but with a miss chance of
10%. If the precipitation would already grant you concealment, the miss chances stack.
