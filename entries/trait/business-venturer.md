**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk)

**Flavor:** Your mercantile pursuits have sent you across Golarion, making you better traveled than
most ratfolk.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise),
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local), and [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any)
(merchant) checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is a class skill for you.
