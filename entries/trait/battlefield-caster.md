**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Angradd](ref/faith/angradd), [Gorum](ref/faith/gorum), [Torag](ref/faith/torag), or
[Trudd](ref/faith/trudd)

**Flavor:** Your faith protects you even as you unleash spells in the thick of battle.

**Benefit:** You receive a +1 trait bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks to cast
defensively and a +1 trait bonus to AC against attacks provoked by or readied against spellcasting.
