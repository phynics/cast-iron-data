**Type:** Magic

**Region:** Cassomir

**Benefit:** Once per day after 10 minutes of foraging, you can sprinkle nutritious herbs into a
magical consumable item (such as a potion of [cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds)). If
the item is consumed within 1 minute, it provides nourishment as if it were a normal meal to the
creature that partakes of it.
