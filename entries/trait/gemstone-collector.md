**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Yuelral](ref/faith/yuelral)

**Flavor:** You have long found focus and inspiration in the natural beauty of rare gemstones.

**Benefit:** As long as you carry bejeweled objects worth a total combined value of 200 gp &times&
your character level, you gain a +1 trait bonus on all Will saving throws and a +2 trait bonus on a
single Charisma-based skill check of your choice.
