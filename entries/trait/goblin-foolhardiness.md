**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Flavor:** You have a tendency toward gross overconfidence in combat.

**Benefit:** When facing an enemy that's larger than you are, if you have no allies in any adjacent
squares, your posturing, bravado, and cussing grant you a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls with
non-reach melee weapons.

**Note:** This trait is also printed as _Goblin Bravery_ in _We Be Goblins!_ See
[Goblin Bravery](ref/trait/goblin-bravery).
