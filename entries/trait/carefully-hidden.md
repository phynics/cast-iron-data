**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Keleshite

**Flavor:** Modern Taldor is not kind to Keleshites. Your life as a member of an unpopular ethnic
group has given you an uncanny knack for avoiding detection.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to Will saves and a +2 trait bonus to saving throws versus
divination effects.
