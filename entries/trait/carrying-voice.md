**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Sylph](ref/race/sylph)

**Flavor:** You grew up amid powerful storms and mimicked the rolling thunder with your voice.

**Benefit:** Treat your caster level as 2 higher to determine the range of sonic spells and effects.
Others do not suffer a penalty on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to hear your voice due
to wind.
