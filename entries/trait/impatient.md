**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You love leaping into battle at the earliest opportunity, and it frustrates you to wait
for others to act.

**Benefit:** You can't [delay](ref/rule/delay) or [ready](ref/rule/ready) actions, and if you are
the last of your allies to act in a round of combat, you take a -1 penalty on ability checks, attack
rolls, saving throws, and skill checks.
