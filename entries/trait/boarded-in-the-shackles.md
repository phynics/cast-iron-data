**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Serpent's Skull

**Flavor:** Your last home was in the Shackles, a dangerous land of pirates and treachery, but also
of fantastic opportunity. A life of weal and woe on the sea and amid the pirate controlled lands has
toughened you to a variety of hardships.

**Benefit:** Pick one of the three categories of saving throws - you gain a +1 trait bonus on all
saving throws of that type.
