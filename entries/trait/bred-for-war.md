**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Shoanti

**Flavor:** You tower above most other humans and possess a physique of hard, corded muscle.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and a +1 trait
bonus on your CMB because of your great size. You must be at least 6 feet tall.
