**Type:** Mount

**Prerequisite:** Have a mount

**Benefit:** This mount can learn four tricks per point of Intelligence, instead of the typical
three. Commanding the mount with the Handle an Animal use of the
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) skill requires a successful DC 15 skill check instead of
the usual DC 10, however, as it is willful and easily bored.
