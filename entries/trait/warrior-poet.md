**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Lands of the Linnorm Kings

**Flavor:** You were trained by the skalds, keepers of the oral tradition of Kalsgard.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) checks related to
dwarves (dwarven history, dwarven construction, and so on) and a +1 trait bonus on Fortitude saves
in any round in which you use a bardic performance ability.
