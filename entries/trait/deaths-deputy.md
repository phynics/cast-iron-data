**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Grimspawn

**Flavor:** When you send opponents to death's door, you often hurl their corpses right across its
threshold.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on the damage dealt for any attack that would already reduce
your target to negative hit points without this trait.
