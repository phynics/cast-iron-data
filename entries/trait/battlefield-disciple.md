**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You are skilled at supporting your allies in the heat of battle.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Heal checks made to stabilize a [dying](ref/misc/dying)
creature. In addition, when you successfully use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action in
combat to grant an ally a bonus on her next attack roll, the bonus you grant increases by 1.
