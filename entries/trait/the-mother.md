**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 17 Rova and 30 Lamashan

**Flavor:** Granting warmth and comfort against the forbidding cold of the wilderness, the Mother is
the heart of the Caravan.

**Benefit:** Allies within 10 feet of you gain a +1 morale bonus on saving throws to resist fear
effects. This trait functions only while you are conscious.
