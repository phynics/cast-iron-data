**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Giantslayer

**Flavor:** You've always been fascinated by the giant races, and have devoted a considerable amount
of time to studying their history and societies, gaining insight into the way they think and use
their abilities.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks against creatures
with the giant subtype and a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks
regarding creatures with the giant subtype, and one of these skills (your choice) becomes a class
skill for you. In addition, you know the Giant language (this does not count toward your number of
languages).
