**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Tian

**Flavor:** You take pride in recording the genealogy of your family, tracing your heritage on large
scrolls dating back hundreds, if not thousands of years.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history), and
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) checks, and one of these skills (your choice)
is always a class skill for you.
