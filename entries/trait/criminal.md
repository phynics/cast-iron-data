**Flavor:** You spent your early life robbing and stealing to get by.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following skills: [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device),
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), or [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand). You gain a +1
trait bonus on that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
