**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [Orc](ref/race/orc)

**Flavor:** You have an innate ability to weave stories about yourself that extol your might and
prowess.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on all [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), and [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (oratory) checks made
against orcs.
