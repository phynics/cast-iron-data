**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber)

**Benefit:** At any point after taking this trait, you may choose one
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill. You gain a permanent +2 trait bonus on checks with that
skill, and it is a class skill for you. Once this skill is chosen, it cannot be changed.
