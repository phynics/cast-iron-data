**Type:** Mount

**Prerequisite:** Have a Qadiran dromedary as a mount

**Benefit:** [Qadiran dromedaries](ref/eq-misc/camel) heal nonlethal damage at a rate of 2 hit
points per hour per Hit Die. This trait does not stack with other effects that increase a creature's
rate of healing nonlethal damage.
