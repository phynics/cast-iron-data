**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You were inducted into a secret magical tradition such as the Esoteric Order of the
Palatine Eye.

**Benefit:** You can cast [read magic](ref/spell/read-magic) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) checks to activate scrolls you cannot normally use.
