**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You were raised in a community that included numerous individuals who spoke with
creatures of the wild.

**Benefit:** You gain one of the following languages of your choice as a bonus language: Aklo,
Aquan, Auran, Giant, Ignan, or Sylvan. With your GM's permission, you can instead select Druidic,
but druids are protective of their language - you are likely to find yourself hunted by druids
wishing to eliminate you as a threat to their sole ownership of their secrets.
