**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Geb

**Flavor:** As a resident of the undead lands of Geb, you know well that the walking dead often
carry deadly poisons and diseases within them.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on saving throws against disease and poison abilities and
effects inflicted by creatures of the undead type (such as [ghoul fever](ref/disease/ghoul-fever) or
a [vargouille's](ref/monster/vargouille) poison).
