**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You are touched by the supernatural essence of nature, marking you since birth as
something other than purely mortal.

**Benefit:** Choose a single 0-level druid spell. You can cast this spell once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to your character
level.
