**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Kellid

**Flavor:** You pray to whatever local deity will answer your prayers - and if they won't, to hell
with them.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion)
checks and can make Knowledge (religion) checks as if trained. Once per day, you can cast
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance) on yourself as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) cast at your highest caster level (CL 1st if you
have no caster level).
