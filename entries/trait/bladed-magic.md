**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You have an innate talent for using magical weaponry and those weapons capable of
becoming magical.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) checks made to craft magic or
[masterwork weapons](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons). In addition, when you use your _arcane pool_
class ability to grant a weapon an enhancement bonus, that bonus lasts for 2 minutes instead of 1.
