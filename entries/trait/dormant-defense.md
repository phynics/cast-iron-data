**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your body never lets down its defenses, even when you rest.

**Benefit:** The DC for [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks you attempt while asleep increases
only by 7 (instead of the normal +10 increase). While you are [dazed](ref/misc/dazed),
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned), or [helpless](ref/misc/helpless), each critical hit or sneak attack
scored against you has a 25% chance of being negated; when a critical hit or sneak attack is
negated, roll its damage normally.

This doesn't stack with the [fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification) armor special ability and
similar effects.
