**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** Cannot be an elf

**Region:** Ironbound Archipelago

**Flavor:** Your interactions with the elves of the Mordant Spire have taught you much about Azlanti
artifacts, and you now share the Mordant Spire elves' fascination with these relics.

**Benefit:** You gain Azlanti as a bonus language, and you gain a +2 trait bonus on
[Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) and [Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) checks when
attempting to identify or use authentic Azlanti artifacts.
