**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); The Motherless

**Flavor:** You have learned how to exploit the natural disgust you provoke when in close proximity
to others.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on combat maneuver checks to perform a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver for the purpose of making your opponent
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened).
