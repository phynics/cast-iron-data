**Type:** Regional

**Region:** urban

**Flavor:** Growing up among acolytes or monks, you were exposed to their supernatural power.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against the supernatural effects of
[channeled energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) or abilities powered by ki, and regain 1 additional
hit point when healed by supernatural effects.
