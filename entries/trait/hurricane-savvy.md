**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** You are used to sailing in the terrible winds that roll out from the Eye of Abendego,
and can apply the skills you've learned to any storm.

**Benefit:** You treat [wind](ref/rule/winds) effects as one wind force category less than they
actually are, both for any personal checks you must make and for any you make to captain or steer a
ship.
