**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mediogalti

**Flavor:** After spending a month in the Mediogaltian jungle, you developed a knack for dealing
with dinosaurs.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks concerning dinosaurs. You also gain a +1 trait bonus on
[wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks made to affect dinosaurs.
