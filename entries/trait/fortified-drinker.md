**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Cayden Cailean](ref/faith/cayden-cailean)

**Flavor:** Cayden Cailean's holy brews invigorate your mind, making you less susceptible to mental
attacks.

**Benefit:** Whenever you imbibe any alcoholic beverage, you gain a +2 trait bonus on saves against
mind-affecting effects for 1 hour.
