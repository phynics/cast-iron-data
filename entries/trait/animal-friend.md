**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** You've long been a friend to animals, and feel safer when animals are nearby.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Will saving throws as long as an animal (Tiny or larger,
must be at least indifferent toward you) is within 30 feet, and
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) is always a class skill for you.
