**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You specialize in delivering or procuring shipments where those commodities are not
allowed or where your identity would elicit negative bias.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) or
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks, and that skill becomes a class skill for you.
In addition, choose one humanoid species; you ignore the -2 penalty on Disguise checks to disguise
yourself as a member of that species.
