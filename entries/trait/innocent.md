**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar)

**Flavor:** No one can believe someone as pure as you could be deceptive in any way. You have an air
of innocence about you, and people have a hard time believing you could ever do anyone harm.

**Benefit:** When you make a [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check to tell a lie, your target always wants
to believe you, granting you the standard +5 bonus on your Bluff check. This bonus only applies if
the lie you tell is either believable or unlikely.
