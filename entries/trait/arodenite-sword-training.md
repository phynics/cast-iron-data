**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your family has passed down training in [Aroden's](ref/faith/aroden) favored weapon,
even though the god no longer answers their prayers.

**Benefit:** You are proficient with the [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword). If your class grants
longsword proficiency as well, you gain a +1 trait bonus on attacks of opportunity with longswords.
