**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** Must have a religion

**Flavor:** A shared religious fervor makes your healing magic more effective.

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a spell belonging to the healing subschool on someone of your
religion, increase the hit points healed by +1.
