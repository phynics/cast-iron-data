**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Werecrocodile-kin

**Flavor:** Once badly bitten by a stronger werecrocodile-kin, you learned the importance of showing
your strength.

**Benefit:** Once per day, when you hit a [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) foe with a melee
attack, you can make an [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to demoralize that foe as a **swift
action**. Intimidate becomes a class skill for you.
