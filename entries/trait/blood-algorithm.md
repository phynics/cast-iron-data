**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Android](ref/race/android)

**Flavor:** Your inhumanity extends to your very core.

**Benefit:** Pain receptors were omitted during your construction, so you gain a +2 trait bonus on
saving throws against spells with the pain descriptor. Additionally, once per day when you are
healed by a spell that targets constructs (such as a [make whole](ref/spell/make-whole) spell), you
heal 1 extra hit point per level of the spell.
