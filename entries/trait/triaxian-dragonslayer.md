**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Triaxian](ref/race/triaxian)

**Benefit:** You receive a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against the spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of dragons and a +1 trait bonus on caster level
checks to overcome dragons' [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance).
