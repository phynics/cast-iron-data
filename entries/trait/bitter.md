**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You have been hurt repeatedly by those you trusted, and it has become difficult for you
to accept help.

**Benefit:** When you receive healing from an ally's class feature, spell, or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), reduce the amount of that healing by 1 hit
point.
