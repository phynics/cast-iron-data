**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Triaxus

**Flavor:** Your experience with one of the long Triaxian seasons has taught you much about the
dangers of a long summer or winter.

**Benefit:** Choose disease or cold. You gain a +2 trait bonus on saves against spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with the chosen descriptor. If you choose
disease, you gain the bonus on Fortitude saves against nonmagical diseases as well.
