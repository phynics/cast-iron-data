**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Rahadoum

**Flavor:** You're a secret member of a forbidden faith in Rahadoum. You have learned to hide your
beliefs and how to identify others of your faith.

**Benefit:** You gain a +5 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to receive secret
messages, and a +5 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to intercept secret
messages from others of your faith.
