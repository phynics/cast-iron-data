**Type:** Regional

**Region:** swamp or river

**Flavor:** You learned to swim right after you learned to walk. When you were a youth, a gang of
river pirates put you to work swimming in nighttime rivers and canals with a dagger between your
teeth so you could sever the anchor ropes of merchant vessels.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on damage rolls with a dagger and a +1 trait bonus on
[Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks. Swim is always a class skill for you.
