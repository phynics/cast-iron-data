**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** You grew up hearing stories of the lost Sky Citadels, and have sworn to find and reclaim
one.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) and
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks. One of these skills (your choice) is
always a class skill for you.
