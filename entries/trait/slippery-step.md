**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Undine](ref/race/undine)

**Flavor:** You are used to walking on wet and slippery surfaces.

**Benefit:** You are always considered to have firm footing when
[fighting underwater](ref/rule/fighting-underwater), and you gain a +2 trait bonus on
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks and [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks on wet surfaces or in
wet terrain, including bogs, standing or running water, or a storm-tossed ship.
