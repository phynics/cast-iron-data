**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Region:** Taldor

**Flavor:** Though others think you merely waste time, you know that those extra flourishes and
ceremonial touches actually make your work just that much better.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics),
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal),
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any), and [Ride](ref/skill/ride)
checks with class skills if you spend twice as long as it normally takes to make that check.
