**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Plane of Fire

**Flavor:** You are well acquainted with the many ways words can be twisted.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks.
Furthermore, if you succeed at a DC 25 Sense Motive check prior to making any [wish](ref/spell/wish)
granted by an outsider, you become aware of your _wish's_ potential pitfalls. If you succeed at this
check by 5 or more, you figure out how to word your _[wish](ref/spell/wish)_ in such a way that your
words are not twisted.
