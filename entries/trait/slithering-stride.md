**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Green-scaled

**Flavor:** You were raised as a forest trapper, and are adept at moving quickly through underbrush.

**Benefit:** For 3 rounds per day, you can move through
[light or heavy undergrowth](ref/rule/forest-terrain) at full speed. This ability doesn't work in
terrain that has been magically manipulated to impede motion.
