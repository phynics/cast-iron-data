**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Dahak](ref/faith/dahak)

**Flavor:** You have been taught to identify the subtle and not-so-subtle signs of a dragon's
passage.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to track dragons,
and you can track a dragon with Survival untrained if the check's DC is 15 or lower.
