**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** All your life, the sky has beckoned to you, and you have dreamed of reveling in the
freedom and bliss of flight.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Fly](ref/skill/fly) checks, as well as
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks attempted while flying. Fly is always a class skill for
you.
