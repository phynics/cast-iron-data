**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your childhood was ended by a great disaster or atrocity that you witnessed. To cope
with the horror, you have learned to reflexively suppress all emotion.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Will saving throws against emotion and fear effects.

**Suggested Characters:** Galtans, [Hellknights](ref/rule/aff-lawknights), Nidalese.
