**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You have spent so long hiding your true identity to escape political enemies that you
have lost much of your sense of self.

**Benefit:** You take a -1 penalty on Will saving throws against compulsions. This penalty increases
to -2 against foes who know your true identity.
