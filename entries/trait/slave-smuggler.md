**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Flavor:** You know how to get contraband across borders.

**Benefit:** When hiding a Medium or smaller creature from an opponent actively searching you or
your cargo, both you and the creature to be smuggled can attempt [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks
and select the higher of the two results for your smuggled ally to use while hiding.
