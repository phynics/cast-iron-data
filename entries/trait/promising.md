**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your superiors have identified you as an [Eagle Knights](ref/rule/aff-vangard)
candidate, and their praise encourages you to strive to meet their expectations.

**Benefit:** Each day after getting a full night's sleep, you gain a +1 trait bonus to your AC when
wearing armor or carrying a shield. This bonus lasts until you are first hit by an attack within the
next 24 hours.
