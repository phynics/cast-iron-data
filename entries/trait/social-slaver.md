**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Cheliax

**Flavor:** Your upbringing has accustomed you to commanding those you deem inferior.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks against
creatures one or more size categories smaller than yourself. The DC of compulsion spells cast by you
against smaller creatures increases by 1.
