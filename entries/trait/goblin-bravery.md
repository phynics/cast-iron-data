**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Flavor:** You have a worrying propensity for overconfidence in combat.

**Benefit:** When facing an enemy that's larger than you, if you have no allies in any adjacent
squares, your posturing, bravado, and cussing grant a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls with non-reach
melee weapons.

This trait was printed as "Goblin Foolhardiness" in _Goblins of Golarion._
