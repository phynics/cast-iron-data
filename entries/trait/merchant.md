**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You lived your life as a merchant, buying and selling goods.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on any [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) and
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks you attempt while bargaining for the price of goods.
Appraise is always a class skill for you.
