**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Amanandar

**Flavor:** Having matured in the melting pot of New Oppara, you know the customs of the Tian-Shus
as well as those of the Taldans, and you utilize this knowledge to create peace between rival
groups.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks, and Diplomacy
becomes a class skill for you.
