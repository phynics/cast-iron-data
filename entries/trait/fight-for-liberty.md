**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** You inspire slaves to fight for freedom with a rousing battle cry.

**Benefit:** As a **standard action**, you may make a rallying cry with a DC 15
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (oratory) check. On a successful check, any unarmed ally within 30
feet who is able to hear you gains a +2 trait bonus on attack rolls with
[improvised weapons](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) and unarmed strikes. This bonus lasts for 1 round
per level.
