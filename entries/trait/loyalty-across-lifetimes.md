**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** You and your eidolon share a link that seems to span multiple lifetimes.

**Benefit:** Your eidolon treats its Constitution score as if it were 2 points higher for the
purposes of determining when its negative hit point total sends it back to its home plane. In
addition, your eidolon gains a +1 trait bonus on Will saves against enchantment spells and effects.
