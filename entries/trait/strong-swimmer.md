**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh)

**Flavor:** Your faith in Gozreh's grace gives you the confidence to swim without fear.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks, and can
[hold your breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for an extra 2 rounds when underwater.
