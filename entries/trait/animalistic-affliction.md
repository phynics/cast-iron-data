**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Ulfen

**Flavor:** You grew up with an affinity for predatory animals, and whispers amid your clan suggest
somewhere in the past, lycanthropy afflicted an ancestor. Whether or not you share this affliction,
you often have animalistic urges.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) and
[Wild Empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks.
