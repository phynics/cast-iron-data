**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** You can read subtle clues in the body language of fiends, allowing you to react just a
bit faster than normal when dealing with such beings.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on opposed Dexterity-based skill checks against outsiders
with the evil subtype. In addition, when your initiative is tied with such creatures, you always act
first regardless of which of you has a higher initiative modifier.
