**Type:** Regional

**Region:** River Kingdoms

**Flavor:** In the River Kingdoms' Daggermark, odds are good that any given blade is coated in some
kind of poison, and people there are used to both poisoning and being poisoned.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Fortitude saving throws against injury
[poisons](ref/rule/poisons). Additionally, you are immune to one specific nonmagical injury poison.
You must choose this poison when you choose this trait and cannot change it.
