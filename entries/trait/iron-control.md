**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc); human-raised

**Flavor:** You learned the hard way that expressing the rage you inherited from your orc blood
exacerbates humans' fearful and hostile reactions to you; after years of effort, you've mastered
self-control and buried your anger deep.

**Benefit:** Whenever you're [confused](ref/misc/confused) and roll to determine behavior in a given
round, subtract 10 from the result.
