**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** Vampire; Vetala

**Flavor:** Wisdom stolen from an enlightened mortal has opened your own mind. Knowing yourself
helps you to understand others.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on all [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks, and
Sense Motive becomes a class skill for you.
