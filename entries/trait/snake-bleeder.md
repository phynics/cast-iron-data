**Region:** Nagajor

**Flavor:** You were a nagaji vassal of knowledgeable nagas, and they taught you much regarding how
they concoct their various poisons.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (alchemy) checks when brewing
poisons, and Craft (alchemy) becomes a class skill for you.
