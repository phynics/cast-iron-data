**Region:** River Kingdoms

**Flavor:** You've grown up on the tributaries of the Sellen River system, and are as comfortable on
boats as on land.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks and
any skill checks involving rope use.
