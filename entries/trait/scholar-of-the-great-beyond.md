**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Your greatest interests as a child did not lie with current events or the mundane; you
have always felt out of place, as if you were born in the wrong era. You take to philosophical
discussions of the Great Beyond and of historical events with ease.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) and
[Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is
always a class skill for you.
