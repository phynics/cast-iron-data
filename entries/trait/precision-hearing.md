**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Werebat-kin

**Flavor:** You have long relied on your enhanced hearing to warn you of those who would persecute
you for your nature.

**Benefit:** Whenever you succeed at a [Perception](ref/skill/perception) check to hear a creature
you can't see and your check exceeds the DC by 10 or more, you can pinpoint the exact square where
the sound originated.
