**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** Living under constant threat of death or worse from your oppressive government, your
nerves have frayed terribly.

**Benefit:** Whenever you [take 10](ref/rule/taking-10-and-taking-20) on an ability check or skill
check that imparts any penalty for failure (aside from wasted time), you treat your check as if you
had rolled an 8 instead of a 10, even if you are able to take your time.
