**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You are skilled at slipping away from crowds.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can attempt a [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check to create a diversion to
hide with a +2 trait bonus on the check, and if successful, you take only a -5 penalty on your
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) check to get to an unobserved place while observers are distracted
(instead of the normal -10 penalty).
