**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Curse of the Crimson Throne

**Flavor:** Your family is particularly hearty, and has even passed down tales of a Varisian
ancestor who sought to cure (or, some loose-lipped relatives say, create) diseases.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws made to resist diseases. Additionally, you
are immune to the diseases [Vorel's phage](ref/disease/vorels-phage) and
[blood veil](ref/disease/blood-veil).

You do not need to be of the Varisian ethnicity to take this campaign trait, but if you're not, your
background should explain how you have a Varisian ancestor.
