**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Echo Wood or Thornkeep

**Benefit:** You trained with the [Hellknights](ref/rule/aff-lawknights) at Fort Inevitable or the
paladins of [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae) at Fort Riverwatch. If you trained with the Hellknights,
you gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against chaotic spells and the
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of chaotic outsiders. If you trained with the
Iomedaean Paladins, you gain this bonus against evil spells and evil outsiders instead.
