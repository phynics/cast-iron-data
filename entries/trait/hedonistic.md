**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You are a creature of pleasure and comfort.

**Benefit:** Whenever you spend a day without gaining reward or treasure (at least 10 or more gold
pieces) or spending an hour on entertainment or pleasure, attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save at the end
of that day. If you fail, you begin the next day [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued). This fatigue lasts 4
hours, or until you receive a reward or sufficient entertainment or pleasure.
