**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Gralton

**Flavor:** Though forced to live in the River Kingdoms, you never forget you're a Galtan noble.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks while interacting
with aristocrats and nobles, and a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks while
interacting with commoners.
