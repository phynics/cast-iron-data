**Prerequisite:** Member of the Cheliax faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your parents signed a pact with a fiend of Hell before you were born, and as a result you can summon
fire to your aid. Once per day, as a swift action, you can summon fire, imbuing a single weapon you
hold with a nimbus of fire that deals an extra 1 point of fire damage for a number of rounds equal
to your Charisma bonus. This fire gives off light equivalent to a torch. This is a supernatural
ability.
