**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Hunter](ref/class/hunter) or have access to the
_[animal aspect](ref/spell/animal-aspect)_ class feature; Shoanti

**Flavor:** You call upon your quah's totem to grant you power. You gain a unique totem aspect that
replaces one of the _animal aspects_ granted to you by the _animal focus_ feature.

**Benefit:** You must choose the Shoanti quah to which you belong. You gain access to the totem
aspect listed for that clan, and you lose access to the animal aspect listed. Once made, this choice
cannot be changed.

**Frog Aspect:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics)
checks to avoid falling and on Constitution checks to avoid drowning. This bonus increases to +4 at
8th level and +6 at 15th level.

**Bear Aspect:** The creature gains fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5. This increases to fire
resistance 10 at 8th level and fire resistance 15 at 15th level.

**Owl Aspect:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks. This
bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.

**Falcon Aspect:** The creature gains [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) and a +2
competence bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th
level and +6 at 15th level.

**Snake Aspect:** The creature gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against cold dangers, disease, heat
dangers, starvation, and thirst. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 15th level.

**Mouse Aspect:** The creature gains a +2 enhancement bonus to Charisma. This bonus increases to +4
at 8th level and +6 at 15th level.

**Wolf Aspect:** The creature gains +2 bonus on saving throws against fear effects and
mind-affecting effects. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th level.
