**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Mwangi

**Benefit:** Your study of Magaambyan magic traditions allows you to add one spell with the good
descriptor from the druid or cleric spell list to the spell list of an arcane spellcasting class of
your choice, at the same spell level as it appears on the cleric or druid list. Pick the spell when
you choose this trait.
