**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You are a master of calligraphy, an art once highly valued in Lung Wa and still held in
high esteem in Lingshen.

**Benefit:** The DC to detect forgeries you create increases by 2. If you attempt to persuade
someone via a written message, you gain a +2 bonus on any [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), or [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check attempted for that
communication. [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) is always a class skill for you.
