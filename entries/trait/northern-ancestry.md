**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Reign of Winter

**Flavor:** One of your parents came from the North, and the tales of the frozen lands at the top of
the world that you grew up listening to excited your imagination. Alternatively, maybe one of your
ancestors passed on the blood of some frost-rimed creature. You feel most alive during the chill of
winter, and as a child, you spent hours playing in the snow. You rarely feel the cold, and you've
always had a restless longing to travel north.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Fortitude saves, as well as cold
[resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 2; this resistance does not stack with cold resistance gained from
any other source.
