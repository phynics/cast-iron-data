**Prerequisite:** Member of the Exchange faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Whether you're carrying illegal contraband or simply regulated products, you have a knack for
sneaking goods past watchful eyes. You gain a +3 trait bonus on Sleight of Hand checks to hide an
object, and Sleight of Hand is a class skill for you.
