**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Numeria

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Fortitude saves against the negative effects of
[skymetals](ref/eq-material/skymetal), as well as a +2 trait bonus on [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any)
checks made to create weapons or armor out of skymetals.
