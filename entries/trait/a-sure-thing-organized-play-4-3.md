**Prerequisite:** Member of the Silver Crusade faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Once per day, you gain a +2 bonus on a single attack roll against an evil-aligned creature. If the
creature is not evil-aligned, this ability is wasted with no benefit.
