**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil)

**Flavor:** You know that all things pass in time, and are used to having to repeat arguments
multiple times to convince stubborn believers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to influence
hostile or unfriendly creatures, and if you fail at such an attempt, you may retry it once.
