**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Flavor:** You are a member of the [Lantern Bearers](ref/rule/aff-lights) or an agent of the Winter
Council, tasked with ensuring that knowledge of the elves' secret shame - the drow - never reaches
the surface.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) checks pertaining to
drow and drow settlements.
