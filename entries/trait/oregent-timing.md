**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Oregent

**Flavor:** After years of soul-numbing obedience to the schedules of others, you find synchronizing
your actions with an ally second nature.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you take a [readied action](ref/rule/ready) in combat, you gain a +3
trait bonus on the d20 roll for that action if it was triggered by an ally's action.
