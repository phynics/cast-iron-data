**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Riddleport

**Flavor:** You're a frequent customer at the Gold Goblin Gambling Hall and enjoy some popularity
within its walls.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (gambling) checks
and a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to gather information when in
Riddleport.
