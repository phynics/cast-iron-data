**Prerequisite:** Member of the Andoran faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your parents had you blessed by the god Erastil as a youth, and you are a prodigy with a bow. You do
not take any penalties for the second range increment when using a longbow or shortbow, and you are
always considered proficient with one of these weapons (your choice).
