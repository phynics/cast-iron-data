**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Angradd](ref/faith/angradd)

**Flavor:** Angradd teaches that a good offense can be the best defense.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls made as part of a
[readied action](ref/rule/ready), and a +1 trait bonus on caster level checks when casting a spell
as a readied action.
