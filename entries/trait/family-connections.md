**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** Your dark ancestry gives you a special insight into how to motivate or placate fiends
and their kin.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made against outsiders belonging to the evil subtype.
