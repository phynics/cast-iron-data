**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Sevenarches

**Flavor:** You've long heard rumors about the mysterious archways of Sevenarches and have spent
much of your life trying to unravel the mysteries behind the portals.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks
and [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to notice secret doors, and Knowledge (history)
becomes a class skill for you.
