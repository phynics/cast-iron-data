**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You have difficulty controlling your temper after living in inhuman conditions in
Cheliax, Geb, Irrisen, Katapesh, or Qadira.

**Benefit:** Whenever a foe provokes an attack of opportunity from you, you must take it unless you
succeed at a Will save against a DC equal to 10 + your level. You take a -1 penalty on Will saving
throws against spells with the emotion descriptor that do not also have the fear descriptor.
