**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 21 Abadius and 16 Calistril

**Flavor:** You are bathed in the light of the Lantern Bearer, who guides the Caravan through the
darkest nights.

**Benefit:** You can cast [light](ref/spell/light) three times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), treating your character level as your caster
level.
