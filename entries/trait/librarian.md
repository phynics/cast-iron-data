**Prerequisite:** Member of the Dark Archive faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You are trained to manage and maintain books, and you find it easy to obtain information from tomes.
You gain a +1 bonus on Linguistics and Profession (librarian) checks, and one of these skills (your
choice) is a class skill for you. Once per day when you would gain a bonus on a skill check from
reading a book, you can increase that bonus by 1.
