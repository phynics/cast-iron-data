**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Stories of the depredations present in the Darklands drive you to explore its shadowy
depths and purge the corruption from within.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against fear while in the Darklands and a +1
trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks against creatures native to the Darklands.
