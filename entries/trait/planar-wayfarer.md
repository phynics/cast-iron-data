**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Onos](ref/faith/onos)

**Flavor:** The countless natural hazards of the planes cannot deter you from witnessing their
grandeur.

**Benefit:** Once per day you can meditate on the nature of the planes for 1 hour to gain
[energy resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 2 against acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage.
This resistance lasts 24 hours or until you perform this meditation again and choose a new
resistance.
