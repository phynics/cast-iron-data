**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** The faith of Holomog focuses on finding the good in unusual places and appreciating the
nuances of virtue in the world.

**Benefit:** You may treat [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) as if he were a lawful neutral deity for
the purposes of determining your own alignment as a cleric, inquisitor, or other divine spellcaster.
You may not select the evil domain unless your own alignment also contains an evil aspect.
