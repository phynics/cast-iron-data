**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Flavor:** Your training as a maid or valet has left you with unconscious mannerisms that seem to
put members of the upper class at ease.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to influence
anyone with at least one level of [Aristocrat](ref/npcclass/aristocrat).
