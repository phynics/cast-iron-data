**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Black-scaled

**Flavor:** The murky tunnels of your tribal home were infested with voracious oozes, which were a
constant threat to your tribe. To protect yourselves, you and your tribespeople never strayed far
without anti-ooze acid.

**Benefit:** You begin play with three [alkali flasks](ref/eq-weapon/alkali-flask). These don't
count against your starting character wealth.
