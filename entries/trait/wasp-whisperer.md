**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Religion:** [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria)

**Flavor:** Your association with Calistria's church has toughened your flesh against venom and
blessed you with the ability to influence wasps and similar insectile vermin.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Fortitude saving throws against poison. You may make
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks (or [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks if you
are a druid or ranger) to improve a Hostile or Unfriendly insectile vermin's attitude toward
Indifferent.
