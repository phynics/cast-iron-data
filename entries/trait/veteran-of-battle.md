**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Gorum](ref/faith/gorum)

**Flavor:** You have fought in several battles, and each time felt the presence of Gorum guiding
your sword-arm, making you ready to act at a moment's notice.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on initiative checks, and if you are able to act during a
surprise round, you may draw a weapon (but not a potion or magic item) as a **free action** during
that round.
