**Type:** Campaign

**Prerequisite:** This is a special trait that can only be gained by progressing through the Hell's
Vengeance Adventure Path. It is not available for normal character creation.

**Campaign:** Hell's Vengeance

**Flavor:** You have sworn your body to Queen Abrogail and House Thrune in a ritual ceremony, and
are willing to lay down your life in service to Thrune.

**Benefit:** Once per week, when reduced to fewer than 0 hit points, you automatically gain
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1 for 1 minute. Once this ability has been used, you instead
gain a +4 trait bonus on Constitution checks to stabilize when reduced to negative hit points.

In addition, you receive an invisible [arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark) of the encircled cross
emblem of Cheliax as a symbol of your loyalty. This mark does not fade and is visible only to other
creatures with this trait, or creatures with the ability to perceive the invisible (via
[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility) or [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing), for
example). You can make this mark visible to other creatures for 1 round as a **swift action**. If
the mark is dispelled or removed (such as with [erase](ref/spell/erase)), it can be replaced with
another casting of _[arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark),_ which thereafter functions as detailed in
this trait.
