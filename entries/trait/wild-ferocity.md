**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc)

**Benefit:** Select one terrain type from the ranger's
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) list. While in this terrain type, taking a **standard
action** while [disabled](ref/misc/disabled) does not cause you to take the normal 1 point of
damage. You can use this trait when acting as if disabled despite having a negative hit point total
as a result of _orc ferocity_ or the [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard) feat.
