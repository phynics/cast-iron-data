**Prerequisite:** Member of the Shadow Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your experience caring for wounded Pathfinder agents has granted you a +2 trait bonus on Heal checks
made to treat diseases and poisons. Additionally, your caster level is considered one higher when
casting spells of the healing subschool with remove in the spell's name.
