**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have an uncanny ability to spot a mutiny brewing.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to detect
when an underling is trying to hide something from you, and can attempt such checks instead of
Perception checks to notice and react to a subordinate's surprise attack against you.
