**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk)

**Flavor:** You are able to learn information by bombarding others with questions that come across
as conversational.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks. In addition, you
can use Diplomacy to gather information in 1d2 hours instead of 1d4.
