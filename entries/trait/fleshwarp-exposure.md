**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Drow](ref/race/drow)

**Flavor:** Your body responds strongly to transmutations because you were exposed to fleshwarping
magic.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against transmutation spells and effects.
Transmutations you willingly accept affect you as though they were cast at 1 caster level higher for
the purpose of determining duration.
