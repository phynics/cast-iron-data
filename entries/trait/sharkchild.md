**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Wereshark-kin

**Flavor:** In your youth, you swam into the ocean as deep as you could, and had it not been for
your wereshark ancestry you would have surely drowned.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks. As long as you have a swim
speed (such as that granted by your bestial form), you can use the run action to move up to five
times your swim speed while swimming straight down from or straight up toward the surface of the
water.
