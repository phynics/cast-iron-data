**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** At your birth, your parents dedicated your soul to [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae) to mold
into a sword of her light. The goddess blessed you, granting you a gift of light to brighten your
path through darkness and a fine sword with which to spread her will.

**Benefit:** You may cast [light](ref/spell/light) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (caster level 1st), and you begin play with a
[masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword). In addition,
whenever _[light](ref/spell/light)_ is cast upon this sword, the radius of light and its duration is
doubled.
