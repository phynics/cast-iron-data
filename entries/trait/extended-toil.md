**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Droskar](ref/faith/droskar)

**Flavor:** Your faith teaches you to push magical servants to the limit.

**Benefit:** When you cast a spell that creates a loyal servant to fulfill a specific or mindless
task (such as [unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant), [mount](ref/spell/mount), or
[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon), but not [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)
spells), you may choose to increase its duration by 50%. This does not stack with the
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) feat. The effect weakens the spell, however; dispel checks
against spells you cast in this manner gain a +4 bonus.
