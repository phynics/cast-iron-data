**Flavor:** Struggle as they might, ne'er-do-wells and vagrants are hard pressed to evade the law
once in your clutches.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) checks to
lawfully and nonlethally restrain a creature you've witnessed commit a crime.
