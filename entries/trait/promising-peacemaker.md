**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc)

**Flavor:** You were raised by a mix of human and orc family members, or by accommodating half-orc
parents, and many people expected you to act as a flawless peacemaker. Whether these hopes saw
fruition or met with frustration, you learned a great deal about both cultures.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks
involving orcs, half-orcs, and humans, and a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy)
checks against members of those races.
