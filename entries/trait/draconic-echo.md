**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Blue-scaled

**Flavor:** Like the blue dragons your tribe reveres, you have a touch of illusory magic.

**Benefit:** You are able to cast [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound) twice per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this effect is equal to
your character level. This spell-like ability's save DC is Intelligence-based.
