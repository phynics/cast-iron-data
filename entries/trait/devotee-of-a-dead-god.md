**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Aroden](ref/faith/aroden)

**Flavor:** You simply cannot and will not accept that Aroden is dead. Your faith in his teachings
and religion are stronger than ever.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks and
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks. One of these skills (your choice) is
always a class skill for you.
