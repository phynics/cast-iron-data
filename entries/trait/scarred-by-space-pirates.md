**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You were attacked by Vercite space pirates in your youth, inspiring you to find ways to
deal with the alien interlopers should they return.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls against humanoids from beyond Golarion,
including androids, kasathas, Triaxians, and Vercites, among others.
