**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Isger, River Kingdoms, or Varisia

**Flavor:** You're a member of the Greenskin Stalkers. Due to your affiliation with this brotherhood
of goblin slayers, you've learned to think and act like a goblin, as disturbing as that sounds.

**Benefit:** You receive a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all), [Perception](ref/skill/perception),
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks against or
concerning goblinoids. You can also attempt Knowledge checks concerning goblinoids untrained.
