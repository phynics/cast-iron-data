**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Divine powers smile upon you each time you grievously wound a favored enemy.

**Benefit:** When you confirm a critical hit against a [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy),
you may extend the duration of any divine spell affecting you by 1 round. The benefits from multiple
critical hits stack. Divine spells with an instantaneous duration can't be extended in this manner.
