**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You have laboriously studied the training rites of various
[Hellknight](ref/rule/aff-lawknights) orders with the intent of joining one yourself.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on damage rolls against outsiders summoned through
conjuration (calling) and conjuration (summoning) spells and effects.
