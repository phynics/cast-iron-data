**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You have become so accustomed to binging on the finer things in life that you find going
without such excess particularly strenuous.

**Benefit:** Goods and services cost you 10% more (and can't be paid for by allies), and you need
twice as much food and liquid as normal for the purposes of preventing
[starvation and thirst](ref/rule/starvation-and-thirst).
