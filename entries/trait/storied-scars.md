**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Kellid

**Flavor:** You proudly bear the numerous scars of your life on your body, and each one tells a tale
of your experiences.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to interact
with other Kellids, and a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks when dealing
with non-Kellid peoples.
