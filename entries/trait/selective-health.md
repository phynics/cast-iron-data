**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar)

**Flavor:** You are resistant to most illnesses that strike those around you, but vulnerable to the
contagions of evil.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Fortitude saves against diseases. However, whenever you
must make a Fortitude saving throw against a disease inflicted by an undead creature or evil
outsider (such as [mummy rot](ref/curse/mummy-rot) or a [vrock's](ref/monster/demon-vrock) spores),
you do not gain this bonus, and instead take a -2 penalty on your Fortitude save.
