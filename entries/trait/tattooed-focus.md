**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Varisian

**Flavor:** You possess an intricate tattoo that proclaims your skill at your chosen vocation.

**Benefit:** Choose a [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any), [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any), or
[Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) skill. You gain a +2 trait bonus on checks with that skill,
and it is always a class skill for you.
