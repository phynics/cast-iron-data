**Type:** Magic

**Benefit:** When a nonevil humanoid attempts to read your mind via a magical effect, the reader
must attempt a Will save opposed by your Wisdom or Charisma check (your choice). If the reader fails
this save, she is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round. If she fails by 10 or more, she is instead
frightened for 1 round, then shaken for 1 round.
