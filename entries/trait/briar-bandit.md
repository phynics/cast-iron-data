**Type:** Regional

**Region:** forest

**Flavor:** The time you've spent crawling through thorny brambles and other thick underbrush during
raids and ambushes taught you how to move without disturbing the dense flora around you.

**Benefit:** When in overgrown areas, you gain a +2 trait bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
checks and a +1 trait bonus on damage rolls with sneak attacks.
