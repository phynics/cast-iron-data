**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Forest of Spirits

**Flavor:** You have traversed the Forest of Spirits numerous times, and have learned how best to
communicate with its otherworldly inhabitants.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks made against outsiders that have the native subtype.
