**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc)

**Flavor:** You have enough human features that it's easy for you to pass for a pure-blooded human.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks to pass as human,
and Disguise is always a class skill for you.
