**Type:** Regional

**Region:** wasteland

**Flavor:** You have built up a resistance to the diseases that ash giants and their vermin
guardians carry.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Fortitude saving throws to resist the disease abilities of
[ash giants](ref/monster/giant-ash-giant) and creatures with the vermin type. In addition, you gain
a +1 trait bonus on melee attack rolls against vermin.
