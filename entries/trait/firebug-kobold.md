**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Red-scaled

**Flavor:** Like the red dragons that inspire your tribe, you're blessed with a small spark of
incendiary magic.

**Benefit:** You are able to cast [spark](ref/spell/spark) three times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level is equal to your character
level. This spell-like ability's save DC is Intelligence-based.
