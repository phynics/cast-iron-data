**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Wayang](ref/race/wayang)

**Flavor:** You grew up in a secretive community by the water, perhaps among the rivers of Shenmen
or the islands of Minata.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks and a +2 bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks on watercraft and when at least half-submerged in water.
