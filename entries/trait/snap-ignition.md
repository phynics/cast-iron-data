**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Ifrit](ref/race/ifrit)

**Flavor:** You are a pyromaniac by nature and love nothing more than setting fires.

**Benefit:** As a **full-round action**, you can produce a small flame at the end of your finger
that ignites combustible materials as a [tindertwig](ref/eq-misc/tindertwig). Alternatively, you can
maintain a flame equivalent to a [candle](ref/eq-misc/candle) by spending a **standard action** each
round to concentrate on it.
