**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Ulfen

**Flavor:** As soon as you were old enough to hold it, you were trained to defend yourself with
traditional Ulfen weapons.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on damage rolls with the following weapons:
[bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword), [battleaxe](ref/eq-weapon/battleaxe),
[greataxe](ref/eq-weapon/greataxe), [greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword),
[handaxe](ref/eq-weapon/handaxe), [light hammer](ref/eq-weapon/light-hammer),
[longbow](ref/eq-weapon/longbow), [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword),
[shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow), [short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword),
[throwing axe](ref/eq-weapon/throwing-axe), and [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer).
