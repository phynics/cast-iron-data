**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Varisian

**Flavor:** You grew up around the mysterious fortune-tellers known throughout Ustalav and Varisia.

**Benefit:** You start play with a [harrow deck](ref/eq-misc/harrow-deck) passed down from a
relative. Because of your skill with fortune-telling, you gain a +1 trait bonus on initiative
checks.
