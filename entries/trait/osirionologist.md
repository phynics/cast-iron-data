**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Osirion

**Flavor:** You have studied the history of ancient Osirion, and may have even ventured inside one
of the great pyramids.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering)
and [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks, and one of them is always a class
skill for you. In addition, you may choose Ancient Osiriani as one of your bonus languages.
