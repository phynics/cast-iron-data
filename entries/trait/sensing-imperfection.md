**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Religion:** [Irori](ref/faith/irori)

**Flavor:** Your belief in Irori has honed your ability to recognize when someone lies or
dissembles.

**Benefit:** [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) is a class skill for you, and you gain a +1
trait bonus on Sense Motive checks.
