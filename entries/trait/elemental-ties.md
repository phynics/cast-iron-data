**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Osirion

**Flavor:** Interactions with elementals are commonplace for you.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when dealing with
creatures of the elemental type, and you begin play knowing one of the following languages as an
additional bonus language: Aquan, Auran, Ignan, or Terran.
