**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You might face dangerous situations with bravado, but you are constantly afraid.

**Benefit:** Your base speed when [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) and fleeing increases by 5 feet,
and the penalties you take from having the [cowering](ref/misc/cowering), frightened,
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked), or [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) conditions increase by 1.

If you would normally be immune to fear, you do not take these penalties but instead lose your
immunity to fear (regardless of its source).
