**Type:** Regional

**Region:** hills or mountains

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in rugged badlands or hills, and you've become something of an
expert at evading the predators, monsters, and worse that haunt the highlands.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks, and Stealth is always
a class skill for you. This trait bonus increases to +2 in hilly or rocky areas.
