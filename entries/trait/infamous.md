**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You were publicly linked, truthfully or not, to a crime of significant infamy. You draw
attention wherever you go and risk imprisonment or worse in the jurisdiction where the crime was
committed.

**Benefit:** You and your apparent allies take a -4 penalty on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy)
checks to interact with law-abiding citizens (except enemies of the authority that accused you).
