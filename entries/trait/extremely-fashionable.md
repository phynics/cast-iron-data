**Type:** Equipment

**Flavor:** You really know how to make a good impression when you're dressed well.

**Benefit:** Whenever you are wearing clothing and/or jewelry worth at least 150 gp (and not
otherwise covered in gore, sewage, or other things that mar your overall look), you gain a +1 trait
bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. One of these skills (your choice) is a class skill for
you.
