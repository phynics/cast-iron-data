**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** You're familiar with the treacherous coastlines, currents, and tides of the Shackles,
and can use that knowledge anywhere.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) and
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) checks while on the ocean. You also gain a +1
trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks, and Swim is always a class skill for you.
