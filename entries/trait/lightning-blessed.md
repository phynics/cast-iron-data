**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Blue-scaled

**Flavor:** You're favored by the lightning shaman of your tribe, and he gifted you several sacred
items as a reminder of your origins.

**Benefit:** You begin play with three vials of
[bottled lightning](ref/eq-weapon/bottled-lightning). These don't count against your starting
character wealth.
