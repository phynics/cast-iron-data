**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You have an unquenchable thirst for knowledge about the world and desire to obtain it
firsthand.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on all
[Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering) and
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks, and you may treat your caster level as 1
level higher for all conjuration spells of the teleportation subschool.
