**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** You are helpful and hardworking.

**Benefit:** When you successfully use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action to aid an
ally's Craft or Profession check, that character gains an additional +1 trait bonus on the check (in
addition to the +2 bonus normally granted for successfully aiding another). If you have a racial
bonus on that skill, your ally gains a +2 trait bonus instead.
