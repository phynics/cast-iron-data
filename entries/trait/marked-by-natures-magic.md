**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker)

**Flavor:** You hail from a community - perhaps in Arcadia or the Lands of the Linnorm Kings - that
encouraged you to interact with fey.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) checks
involving fey and on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to influence fey. Knowledge (nature) is
always a class skill for you.
