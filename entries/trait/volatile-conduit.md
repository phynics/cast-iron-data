**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You discovered a secret that enhances the energy of some spells.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **free action**, when you cast a spell that deals acid, cold,
electricity, or fire damage, you can enhance that spell with volatile energy. When you do, it deals
1d4 points of extra damage of the same energy type.
