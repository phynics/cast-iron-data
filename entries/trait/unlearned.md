**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You have always had difficulty learning and retaining information, and know very little
about the world beyond the obvious and what pertains to your profession.

**Benefit:** Choose one [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill. You cannot attempt untrained
Knowledge checks with any other Knowledge skills, even if the check's DC is 10 or lower. If you have
access to a library that covers a specific Knowledge skill, you may attempt an untrained Knowledge
check with a -2 penalty.
