**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Shanguang

**Flavor:** You belong to one of the nomadic Mutabi-qi tribes that wander the wastes of Shaguang,
and you are adept at navigating the seemingly endless desert wastes.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography)
checks and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made in the desert.
