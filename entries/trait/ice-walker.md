**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Kellid

**Flavor:** You spent years roaming the frozen tundra of the frigid north, and have adapted to its
numbing effects.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against attacks that deal cold damage.
Furthermore, you may ignore the [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) penalty for ice and can move
across ice at normal speed.
