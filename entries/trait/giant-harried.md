**Type:** Regional

**Region:** wasteland

**Flavor:** You were robbed by giants or nearly killed by a giant's prank, and the experience gave
you a keen instinct for outmaneuvering bigger creatures.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on combat maneuver checks against creatures larger than you
and a +1 trait bonus to CMD against such creatures.

**Likely Regions:** Geb, Mana Wastes, Nex, Nidal, Numeria.

**Likely giants:** Ash, Hill, River, Shadow
