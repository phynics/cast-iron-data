**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You have learned much from tinkering with ancient technology from the Jistka Imperium.

**Benefit:** Choose one of the following skills: [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device),
[Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering), or
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device). Once per day, you can reroll a check with that skill
before the outcome of the check is revealed. You must take the second result, even if it is worse.
