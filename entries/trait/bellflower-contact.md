**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Flavor:** Before you were old enough to be a [Bellflower Network](ref/rule/aff-antislavery)
member, you kept an ear out for information useful to slave liberators.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks,
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to understand secret messages, and
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to send secret messages.
