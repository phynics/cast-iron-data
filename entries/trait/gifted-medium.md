**Religion:** [Magrim](ref/faith/magrim)

**Flavor:** Your understanding of Magrim's teachings makes you a perfect conduit for mystical
forces.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on your caster level when using divinations to reach out to
other entities, such as [commune](ref/spell/commune) or
[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead).
