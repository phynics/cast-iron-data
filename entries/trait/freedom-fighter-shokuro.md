**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Shokuro

**Flavor:** You come from a family of farmers, but are determined to fight for your rights.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attacks made with
[improvised weapons](ref/misc/improvised-weapons). If you do not possess the
[Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard) or [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything) feats, you
still take penalties on these attack rolls as normal.
