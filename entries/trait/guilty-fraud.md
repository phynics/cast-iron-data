**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You received something through trickery that you did not deserve, and your guilt for the
misdeed distracts you from dangers around you.

**Benefit:** You take a -4 penalty on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks against creatures with an
attitude toward you of indifferent or better.
