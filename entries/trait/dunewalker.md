**Prerequisite:** Member of the Osirion faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your people have long roamed the shifting and treacherous sands of Osirion, and you do not fear the
harsh wrath of the unforgiving desert. You gain a +4 trait bonus on Fortitude saves to resist
nonlethal damage from hot conditions, and you can always move through nonmagical sand as if it were
normal terrain.
