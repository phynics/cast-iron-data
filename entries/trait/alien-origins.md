**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Silver Mount

**Flavor:** You are in some way tied to the mysterious alien monument known as Silver Mount in
Numeria, but you have forgotten an important piece of your past. Perhaps you were a metallurgist's
experiment gone awry, a rediscovered automaton that escaped the mountain's confines, or the result
of some equally enigmatic phenomenon. Regardless, your unusual origins mark you as somehow different
from others, and you know the gaps in your memory hold the key to your past.

**Benefit:** You start play with a unique [compass](ref/eq-misc/compass) that doesn't point north
and is riddled with indecipherable alien markings - the only object you have that you know to be
tied to your home. In addition, your inscrutable mannerisms give you a +2 trait bonus on opposed
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks.

**Suggestion:** Consider being a member of the [android race](ref/race/android).
