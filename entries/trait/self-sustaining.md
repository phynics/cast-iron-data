**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** See text

**Flavor:** As a follower of Razmir, the [Prophecies of Kalistrade](ref/rule/f-profits), or some
other tradition that preaches self-reliance, you make a point of not seeking divine aid for minor
matters.

**Benefit:** When you gain this trait, choose one of the following 0-level spells:
[create water](ref/spell/create-water), [mending](ref/spell/mending),
[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink), or [stabilize](ref/spell/stabilize). You
can cast this spell once per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using your
character level as your caster level. Once chosen, this selection can't change.
