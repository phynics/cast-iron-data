**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Mummy's Mask

**Flavor:** You're not a native Osirian, but the opportunity to explore the tombs of Ancient
Osirion - and &quot&liberate&quot& the treasures they hold - is too good to pass up. Whether or not
you're interested in the history of this land, you're definitely interested in the wealth that's
lain hidden in dusty crypts for millennia: such as the tombs in the newly opened necropolis of Wati.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks, and that skill is
always a class skill for you. In addition, your contacts in the antiquities markets allow you to
sell relics of Ancient Osirion for 60% of their listed price, rather than the normal 50% value. What
is considered a relic of Ancient Osirion is left to the GM's discretion.
