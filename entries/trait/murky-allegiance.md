**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Fetchling](ref/race/fetchling)

**Flavor:** You came of age in a place where fetchlings serve dark forces.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to send secret messages
and to imply you serve a powerful sinister conspiracy, evil authority figure, or shadow government.
You also gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when interacting with such
entities.
