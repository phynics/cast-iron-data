**Prerequisite:** Member of the Osirion faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your ancestors paid the proper obeisance to Nethys, who granted their heirs special divinatory
gifts. Once per day, you may gain a +2 trait bonus on any single Knowledge check. Additionally,
choose one Knowledge skill—this skill becomes a class skill for you.
