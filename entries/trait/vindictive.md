**Prerequisite:** Member of the Taldor faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your family is prone to endless feuds, and you were raised by people who simply do not forgive a
slight easily. Once per day, you can declare a creature that has dealt you hit point damage to be
your vindictive target as an immediate action when the damage is dealt. You receive a +1 trait bonus
on damage rolls against this target for 1 minute.
