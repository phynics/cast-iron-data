**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Your family has long operated outside the law.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to influence
criminals and on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks to influence law-abiding citizens, but
take a -2 penalty on Diplomacy checks to influence law-abiding citizens. Diplomacy or Intimidate
(your choice) is a class skill for you.
