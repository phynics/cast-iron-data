**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Ruins of Azlant

**Flavor:** You have always had a knack for physical activity. You either grew up in a rural area
and had an active childhood where you were always climbing trees, swimming in lakes and rivers, and
clambering up rocky hillsides, or you competed in and excelled at contests or sports while growing
up in a larger settlement. Due to this background, little can slow you down or inhibit your
movements. When the Bountiful Venture Company interviewed you, they noticed your stature and
physicality, and they chose you for the colonial expedition knowing that a strong back is always
helpful in a growing colony.

**Benefit:** You reduce your armor check penalty by a number equal to one-third your character level
(minimum 1) for purposes of [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics), [Climb](ref/skill/climb), and
[Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks, and you gain a +1 trait bonus to one of these three skills, chosen
when you take this trait.
