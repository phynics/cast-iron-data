**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Suli](ref/race/suli)

**Flavor:** You traveled constantly in your youth, and were forced to practice making a good first
impression until you could do so effortlessly.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made against NPCs
whom you have known for less than 1 hour.
