**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** You are determined to ward off the Bleaching, and get a certain thrill out of being
forced to experience negative emotions.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you fail a saving throw against a spell with the emotion descriptor,
you can gain 1d6 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points). These temporary hit points
last for 1 minute.
