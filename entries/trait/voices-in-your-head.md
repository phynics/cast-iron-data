**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Through pacts with eldritch beings or a simple quirk of circumstance, your voice has an
uncanny rapport with those swept up in madness.

**Benefit:** Once per day as an **immediate action**, you can issue a command to a
[confused](ref/misc/confused) creature within 30 feet. This allows the creature to immediately
reroll on the [confused condition table](ref/misc/confused), and you can pick which of the two
actions - the action of its initial roll or that of the reroll - the creature takes. Confused
creatures do not need to understand you for this trait to take effect.
