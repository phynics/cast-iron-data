**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gillman](ref/race/gillman)

**Flavor:** You have prominent Azlanti features, which you can use to inspire confidence in your
allies.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to aid an adjacent
ally, increase the bonus you grant your ally by 1. This is a trait bonus.
