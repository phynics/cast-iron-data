**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You catch others off-guard when a confrontation escalates.

**Benefit:** As long as you are standing in or adjacent to water, or holding at least a pint of
liquid (including insoluble liquids like [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil) or
[alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire)), you can perform a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver to blind a foe within reach without provoking an
attack of opportunity.

If you select the [Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick) feat, you gain a +2 trait
bonus on dirty trick combat maneuvers to blind foes as long as you are standing in or adjacent to
water or holding a pint of liquid.
