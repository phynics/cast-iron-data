**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 11 Arodus and 16 Rova

**Flavor:** You are blessed by the beasts that trail the caravan, which represent protection in
numbers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks to teach
animals tricks, and Handle Animal becomes a class skill for you.
