**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Kurgess](ref/faith/kurgess)

**Flavor:** You are practiced at using physical might to escape your bonds.

**Benefit:** You can use Strength instead of Dexterity as your base ability for
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) skill checks to free yourself from bondage. Additionally,
you gain a +1 trait bonus on such Escape Artist checks.
