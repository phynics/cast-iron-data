**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Thuvia

**Flavor:** You were employed briefly by the blue dragon Deyrubrujuan's alchemist cabal to harvest
sun orchids deep in the Thuvian desert. Though you never worked your way deeper into the cabal, you
retain the knowledge gained during your employment.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made in desert environments.
