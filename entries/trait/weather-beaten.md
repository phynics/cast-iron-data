**Type:** Mount

**Prerequisite:** Have an Erutaki husky as a mount

**Benefit:** [Erutaki huskies](ref/eq-misc/dog-riding) gain a +4 trait bonus on Fortitude saves to
avoid nonlethal damage from cold environments, and only take a -2 penalty on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks made in inclement weather such as rain or snow (instead of
the usual -4 penalty).
