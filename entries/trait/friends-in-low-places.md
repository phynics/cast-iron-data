**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Outcasts know their own, and that knowledge opens doors and loosens tongues.

**Benefit:** [Gathering information](ref/skill/diplomacy) in lower quarters, such as vice dens and
poorer districts, takes you 1d2 hours (instead of 1d4 hours). In addition, the attitudes of any
destitute or impoverished NPCs you interact with begin one step closer to helpful.
