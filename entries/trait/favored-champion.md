**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Shoanti

**Flavor:** Omens from the spirits indicated you would be a great champion against your tribe's
enemies.

**Benefit:** Once per day as an **immediate action** when you fail a combat maneuver check, you can
reroll it. You must take the second result even if it is worse.
