**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 21 Neth and 29 Neth

**Flavor:** Like the lone traveler that walks alongside the caravan for but a brief while, you have
a way with strangers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks to gather information. Additionally, either
Diplomacy or Knowledge (local) becomes a class skill for you.
