**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Your faith in the natural world or one of the gods of nature makes it easy for you to
pick up on related concepts.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) and
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is
always a class skill for you.
