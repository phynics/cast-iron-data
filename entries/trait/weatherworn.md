**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** Vampire; Moroi

**Flavor:** Lairing in desolate mountains or on coastal cliff tops has taught you to deal with the
effects of strong winds.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus against the effects of spells that create wind or move air,
such as [gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind). You treat natural [weather](ref/rule/weather)
conditions as one step weaker. These abilities also apply when you are in gaseous form.
