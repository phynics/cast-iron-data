**Prerequisite:** Member of the Grand Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

It helps to pay attention to your surroundings and the people you meet. Choose either Perception or
Sense Motive. You gain a +1 trait bonus on all checks with the chosen skill, and the chosen skill is
a class skill for you.
