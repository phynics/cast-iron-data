**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Garundi

**Flavor:** You have spent many years traveling across Garund, and have met many different people on
your journeys.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on two of the following skills:
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local), and
[Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics).
