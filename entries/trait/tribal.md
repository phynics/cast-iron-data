**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [Orc](ref/race/orc)

**Benefit:** You are especially devoted to your tribe, and within 60 feet of a tribal standard, you
gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls and Will saves against fear effects, as if you were the
recipient of a [bless](ref/spell/bless) spell.
