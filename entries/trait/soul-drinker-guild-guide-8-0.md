**Prerequisite:** Member of the Dark Archive faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

There is a dark hunger in you that rejoices when you or an ally slays a foe. Once per day when an
enemy creature is killed, as an immediate action, you can gain a number of temporary hit points
equal to the slain foe's Hit Dice. These temporary hit points last for 1 minute. This is a
supernatural ability.
