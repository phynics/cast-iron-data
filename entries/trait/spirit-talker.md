**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** Mwangi shamanistic faith

**Flavor:** You are a devotee of the shamanistic practices of the Mwangi, having little use for the
deities of the colonials.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 racial bonus on Will saves against divine spells and spell-like effects
employed against you by clerics or paladins of Aroden, Abadar, Iomedae, Gozreh, and Shelyn - but you
suffer a -1 penalty on Will saves against divine spells cast by devotees of Mwangi faiths.
