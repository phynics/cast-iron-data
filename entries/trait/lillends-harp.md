**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar); Musetouched

**Flavor:** Your ancestor was a [lillend](ref/monster/azata-lillend), a being of exceptional grace
and singular musical talent.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (string) checks. This
bonus increases to +2 when you make a Perform check as part of a bardic performance.
