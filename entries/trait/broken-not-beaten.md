**Type:** Combat

**Benefit:** You automatically [stabilize](ref/misc/stable) when reduced below 0 hit points. Once
per day when brought below 0 hit points, you can accept 1 point of damage to regain consciousness
for 1 round, though you remain staggered and only able to take a single move or standard action.
