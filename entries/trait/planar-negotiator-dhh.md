**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Abyss

**Flavor:** You are used to dealing with outsiders that would fluster a less experienced negotiator.

**Benefit:** When dealing with an outsider within one step of your alignment, you gain a +1 trait
bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks.
Increase these bonuses to +2 if you and the outsider worship the same deity.
