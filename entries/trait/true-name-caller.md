**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Abyss

**Benefit:** You are skilled at discovering the true names of outsiders. Choose a plane other than
the Material Plane. When attempting to discover the true name of an outsider from the chosen plane,
you halve the amount of research time required and gain a +4 trait bonus on the
[Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) check made to learn the outsider's name.
