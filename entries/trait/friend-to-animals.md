**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human)

**Flavor:** You grew up in close proximity to animals and relate with them intuitively.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) and
[Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks, and one of those skills (your choice) is always a class skill for
you.
