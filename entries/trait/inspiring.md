**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** As a child, you learned the power of a simple expression of faith in another when you
gave a hero the inspiration to fight on in the face of terrible odds and save you from danger.

**Benefit:** As a **standard action**, you can speak words of encouragement to grant a +1 morale
bonus to an ally within 30 feet who can see and hear you. The ally can use this bonus on any d20
roll of her choice before the start of your next turn. You cannot use this trait to grant a bonus to
yourself or to an ally who has already benefited from this trait within the last 24 hours.

**Suggested Characters:** [Eagle Knights](ref/rule/aff-vangard), [Iomedaeans](ref/faith/iomedae),
Mendevian crusaders.
