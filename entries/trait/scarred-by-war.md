**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Dalenydra](ref/faith/dalenydra)

**Flavor:** You have experienced firsthand the horrors of war, which have left you physically or
mentally scarred. Now you have committed yourself to the teachings of the Blessed Attendant, seeking
to heal those wounded in combat and to prevent battles from occurring whenever you can.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks outside of combat.
In addition, when you heal creatures other than yourself using class abilities, feats, spells, and
spell completion or spell trigger magic items, you restore 1 additional hit point.
