**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You've learned how to infuse your healing magic with lingering traces of light.

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a spell from the conjuration (healing) school that has a casting time
of a standard action, you can extend its casting time to a **full-round action** to imbue the target
with light, as per [light](ref/spell/light), with a duration of 1 round per caster level.
