**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil)

**Flavor:** You are skilled in passing along knowledge of rural matters.

**Benefit:** If you have 4 or more ranks in [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) or
[Survival](ref/skill/survival), when you [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) with these skills, you
grant a +4 bonus on the check you are aiding instead of the normal +2.
