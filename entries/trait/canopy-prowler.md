**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk)

**Flavor:** Growing up in the Mwangi Expanse, you learned to hunt from above, where your prey seldom
searches for danger.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks, and Climb is always a
class skill for you. If you possess the _climber_ racial trait, you also gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks while climbing.
