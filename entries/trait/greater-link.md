**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** The link between you and your eidolon is stronger than most.

**Benefit:** Your eidolon's current and maximum hit point totals are not reduced by 50% until you
are separated by 110 feet or more. Your eidolon's current and maximum hit point totals are not
reduced by 75% until you are separated by 1,100 feet or more. All other aspects of your life link
class feature are unaffected.
