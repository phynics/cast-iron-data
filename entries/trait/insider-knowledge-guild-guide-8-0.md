**Prerequisite:** Member of the Grand Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Venture-Captain Valsin likes to keep abreast of situations within the Pathfinder Society, and you do
your best to emulate him. Choose either Diplomacy or Knowledge (local). You gain a +1 trait bonus on
all checks with the chosen skill, and the chosen skill is a class skill for you.
