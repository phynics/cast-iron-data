**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Shattered Star

**Flavor:** As a child in Magnimar, the city's ancient monuments inspired you with wonder. Your
fascination with these relics has only grown in the years since, encouraging you to join the ranks
of the [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars) so you can further quench your thirst for
knowledge. Though you know little about the actual lore of the monuments, you've studied these
colossal works extensively, developing an in-depth knowledge of some of the city's most famous
landmarks.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks
made in Varisia due to your familiarity with its historical remnants. This bonus increases to +2 if
the check relates to the city of Magnimar. Additionally, you gain a +2 trait bonus on any skill
check made to receive a boon from one of Magnimar's magically imbued monuments.
