**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** One of your ancestors was a conjurer who specialized in binding outsiders of a
particular type. You have inherited some of his skills, but also some of his enemies. Choose a
single outsider subtype.

**Benefit:** The DC of your spells increases by 1 against outsiders of the chosen subtype. Outsiders
of the chosen subtype gain a +1 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls made against you, and their
starting attitude toward you is reduced by one step.
