**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Your frequent handling of magic items, artifacts, and other enchanted goods has made you
an expert at determining when something has magical properties.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks. In addition, you
need only exceed the DC of an Appraise check by 2 in order to determine whether the item in question
has magic properties (although success still does not grant knowledge of the magic item's
abilities).

**Suggested Characters:** Drumans, dwarves, Torag worshipers.
