**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Triaxian](ref/race/triaxian)

**Flavor:** As a Transitional Triaxian, you are attuned to the weather no matter what world you
stand upon.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you attempt a [Survival](ref/skill/survival) check to predict the
weather, you can add a trait bonus equal to 3 + 1/2 your character level. In addition, you gain a +1
trait bonus on Fortitude saves against the effects of cold and hot weather.
