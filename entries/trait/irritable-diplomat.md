**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc)

**Flavor:** Most people expect half-orcs to be savage and violent, and you're used to working a
little harder to overcome their assumptions. Unfortunately, you're still working on keeping patient
when they ignore your efforts.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you may choose to gain a +5 trait bonus on any
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check, but if it fails, you take a -2 penalty on all Charisma-based
checks for the next 10 minutes.
