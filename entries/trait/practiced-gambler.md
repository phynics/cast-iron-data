**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Varisian

**Flavor:** Your upbringing around the Sczarni has exposed you to games with cards and dice.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks; for the purpose of gambling, these bonuses
increase by 1.
