**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 30 Neth and 17 Kuthona

**Flavor:** You are born under the sign of the dark figure that trails the Caravan - the specter of
death itself.

**Benefit:** You receive a +2 bonus on saving throws against death effects. All allies within 10
feet of you take a -1 penalty on saving throws against death effects.
