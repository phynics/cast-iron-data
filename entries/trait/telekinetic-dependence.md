**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** Vampire; Nosferatu

**Flavor:** Your body is so ancient you fear damaging it with exertion.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to your caster level when casting (or using as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)) [fly](ref/spell/fly),
[levitate](ref/spell/levitate), [mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand),
[telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis), and similar spells that move objects through sheer will.
