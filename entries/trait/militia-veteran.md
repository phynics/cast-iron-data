**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Taldor or any town or village

**Flavor:** Your first job was serving in a civilian militia in your hometown. The skills you
learned through daily drilling and protecting your fellow townsfolk gave you special insight into
military life.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following skills: [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (soldier),
[Ride](ref/skill/ride), or [Survival](ref/skill/survival). You gain a +1 trait bonus on checks with
that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
