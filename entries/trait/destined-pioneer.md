**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Taldan

**Flavor:** Your forebears served in an Army of Exploration, and you retain that expansionist
spirit.

**Benefit:** Select a terrain from the [ranger's](ref/class/ranger) list of
[favored terrains](ref/ability/favored-terrain). Once selected, this choice cannot be changed. Once
per day while in the selected terrain, you may move across nonmagical
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) as though it were not difficult terrain until
the end of your turn.
