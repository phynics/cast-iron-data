**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Tian

**Flavor:** You have mastered the deadly fighting arts of your people, and when you wield a
[masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) version of any sword, you become one with its blade.

**Benefit:** If you act before your opponent during the surprise round, you gain a +2 trait bonus on
weapon damage rolls for the surprise round only. This additional damage is precision damage.
