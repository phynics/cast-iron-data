**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Jinin

**Flavor:** You were one of the mithral miners of Jinin, and learned how to extract the delicate ore
as well as determine its value.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks, and a +1 trait
bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (miner) checks. In addition, Profession (miner)
becomes a class skill for you.
