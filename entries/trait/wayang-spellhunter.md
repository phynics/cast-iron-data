**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Minata

**Flavor:** You grew up on one of the wayang-populated islands of Minata, and your use of magic
while hunting has been a boon to you.

**Benefit:** Select a spell of 3rd level or below. When you use this spell with a
[metamagic feat](ref/rule/feats-basic), it uses up a spell slot one level lower than it normally
would.
