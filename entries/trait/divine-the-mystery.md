**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Kellid

**Flavor:** Your time spent exploring the ruins of Silver Mount has taught you much about the
strange ways of the metal creatures from the stars.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering)
checks to identify creatures with the robot subtype and relics of Numerian technology. When using
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) to feint against an intelligent construct, you do not take a penalty for
feinting against a non-humanoid.
