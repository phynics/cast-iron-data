**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You draw strength from adhering to the Ichimeiyo honor code in combat.

**Benefit:** Once per day, if an effect would compel you to harm an ally, abandon an ally, or break
your word, you can attempt a Will save at the usual DC to end the effect. If the effect normally
allows a Will save at this point, you can roll twice and take the better result.
