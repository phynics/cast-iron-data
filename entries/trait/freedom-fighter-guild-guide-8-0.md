**Prerequisite:** Member of the Liberty's Edge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your family has long waged war against tyranny, and you learned a great deal about guerilla warfare
in your youth. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Stealth checks and a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls
during surprise rounds.
