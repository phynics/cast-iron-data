**Prerequisite:** Member of the Grand Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your time training at the Grand Lodge of Absalom has served you well. Choose either Knowledge
(geography) or Knowledge (history). You gain a +1 trait bonus on all checks with the chosen skill,
and the chosen skill is a class skill for you.
