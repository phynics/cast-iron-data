**Religion:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar)

**Flavor:** Your deity has granted you an uncanny ability to find the true worth of items.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks, you don't suffer
the effects of failing such checks by 5 or more, and Appraise is a class skill for you.
