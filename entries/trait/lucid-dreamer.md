**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** Varisian wanderers or Wonderseekers

**Flavor:** Your dreams are a special place of power that only you get to enjoy; driving away
threats to your subconscious is as easy as slamming a door.

**Benefit:** You receive a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) checks
and a +2 trait bonus on Will saves against effects on your dreaming mind or subconscious, such as a
night hag's dream haunting ability, the [nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare) spell, and illusions of the
[phantasm](ref/spelldef/phantasm) subschool.
