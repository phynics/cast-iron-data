**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Plane of Earth

**Flavor:** Perhaps you worship the Elemental Lord of Earth, or perhaps you have gleaned your fated
powers via some other means of interacting with the Eternal Delve. Your divine powers are infused
with the elemental powers of the Plane of Earth.

**Benefit:** Whenever you [channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) to heal living
creatures, affected targets gain a +2 profane bonus to CMD to resist
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [reposition](ref/rule/reposition), and [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat
maneuvers. Alternately, if you _channel negative energy_ to harm living creatures, affected targets
take a -2 penalty to CMD against such combat maneuvers. This effect lasts 1 round.
