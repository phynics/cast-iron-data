**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Hermea

**Flavor:** You are the product of generations of selective breeding, organized by the gold dragon
Mengkare. Your education is top-notch, and you have a broad base of knowledge. Unfortunately, the
isolation of Hermea has also made you slightly naive.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) and
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks but take a -1 penalty on all
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks.
