**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Zyphus](ref/faith/zyphus)

**Flavor:** Your patron grants you expertise at creating or disabling traps.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (traps) checks, as well as a
+1 trait bonus on [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks to disable traps.
