**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** For a time during your childhood, you were left in the care of a juju oracle or wendifa,
who saw your potential and taught you simple juju magic to influence the weakminded for brief
periods of time.

**Benefit:** You can use [daze](ref/spell/daze) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day, using your character level as your
caster level (minimum 1). The save DC of this effect is Charisma-based. The dazed individual looks
ashen and deathly for the duration of the effect.
