**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Ustalav

**Flavor:** You have survived and thrived in the dark forests of Ustalav by knowing how to make
deals with terrible things.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against fear effects and a +1 trait bonus on
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) skills checks against evil fey,
monstrous humanoids, and undead.
