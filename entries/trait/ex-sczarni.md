**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Shattered Star

**Flavor:** Though you were raised as a member of one of Magnimar's various Sczarni gangs, you never
fit in with the rest of your settled, metropolitan brethren. The
[Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars) in particular recognized your wanderlust and potential
as an agent, and on more than one occasion you impressed or bamboozled their agents with your street
smarts and finesse. Over the years, you fell out of favor with the Sczarni, and eventually left to
pursue interests outside of simple banditry, though you still remember the lessons your criminal
relations taught you.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to pass hidden messages.
Also, anytime you deliver a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless), you deal an additional 1d6 points of
damage.
