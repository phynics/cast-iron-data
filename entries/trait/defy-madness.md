**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Old Cults](ref/rule/f-old-cults)

**Flavor:** Your direct involvement with supernatural and alien forces has left you less vulnerable
to the insanity they cause.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on any saving throws against confusion, insanity, madness,
and [Wisdom damage and drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).
