**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Bas-o

**Flavor:** You have lived among the Bas-o and know Sargava well.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill
for you.
