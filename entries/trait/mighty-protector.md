**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Trudd](ref/faith/trudd)

**Flavor:** When you were too young to fight, you witnessed a great tragedy befall an innocent and
you swore to Trudd you'd become strong enough to prevent something like that from happening again.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you hit a foe with a melee attack, you can attempt an
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action as a **free action** to increase an adjacent ally's AC
against that foe. If you succeed, you grant the ally a +4 bonus to AC instead of a +2 bonus for that
round.
