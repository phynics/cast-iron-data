**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Realm of the Mammoth Lords

**Flavor:** You have experience trapping and training large creatures such as mammoths, mastodons,
woolly rhinoceroses, and other megafauna.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks related to
these creatures.
