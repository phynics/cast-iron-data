**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Undine](ref/race/undine)

**Flavor:** Your marid ancestry is particularly strong, and magic runs thick through your veins.

**Benefit:** When you use your [hydraulic push](ref/spell/hydraulic-push)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), it can extinguish magical fires as well as
nonmagical fires as long as you succeed at an opposed caster level check (the DC is equal to 11 +
the magical fire's caster level).
