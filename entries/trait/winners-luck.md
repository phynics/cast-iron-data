**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Riddleport

**Flavor:** Winning at the tables in Riddleport's gambling houses can be as devastating as losing.
Years of dodging the jealous blows of card table patrons have given you a sixth sense for when your
luck is about to run out.

**Benefit:** Whenever you succeed at a Reflex saving throw, you may draw a weapon as an **immediate
action**, or a hidden weapon as a **move action**.
