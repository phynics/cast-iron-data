**Type:** Combat

**Region:** Sargava

**Flavor:** You are used to fighting in [hilly environments](ref/rule/hills-terrain).

**Benefit:** You may [run](ref/rule/run) or [charge](ref/rule/charge) downhill on a steep slope
(moving up to your base speed) without making an [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) check to avoid
stumbling - but if you travel farther than this distance, the normal rules for steep slopes apply.
