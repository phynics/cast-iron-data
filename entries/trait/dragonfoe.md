**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Giantslayer

**Flavor:** You've always hated dragons. Perhaps one of your ancestors was a well-known
dragonslayer, or a rampaging dragon killed your family or friends. Maybe the stories you grew up
hearing about knights rescuing captive princes and princesses from the clutches of evil dragons
inspired you to slay dragons. Whatever the reason for your obsession, it has driven you to study how
to fight dragons and defend yourself against their fearsome attacks.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC against creatures with the dragon type and a +2 trait
bonus on Reflex saves against breath weapon attacks.
