**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Egorian

**Flavor:** You studied under one of the masters of the Egorian School style of architecture.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on [Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering)
checks and a +2 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to find secret doors or
traps in buildings built in the Egorian School style.
