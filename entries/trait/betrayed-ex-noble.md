**Type:** Regional

**Region:** City of Brass

**Flavor:** You were born a noble in the City of Brass, but were unjustly stripped of your title or
exiled. Now, your ire prevents you from brooking any further insult unto your already disgraced
name.

**Benefit:** Whenever an enemy attempts to [feint](ref/rule/feint) against you in combat, the DC to
do so is equal to 10 + your base attack bonus + your Wisdom modifier, or 10 + your Intimidate bonus,
whichever is higher.
