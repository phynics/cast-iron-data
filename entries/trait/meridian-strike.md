**Prerequisite:** Member of the Lantern Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Energy flows through channels in the body, like rivers through the land. Once per day when you roll
damage on a successful critical hit, reroll any 1s and take the new roll (even if it is another 1).
