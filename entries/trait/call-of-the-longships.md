**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Lands of the Linnorm Kings

**Flavor:** Your blood sings with longing to relive the adventures of one of your ancestors, and you
are inexorably drawn to the sea.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks and
a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls made onboard ships.
