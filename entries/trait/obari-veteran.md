**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You've spent years aboard ships traveling the lucrative Obari Crossing, transporting
Garundi and Vudrani goods.

**Benefit:** You gain +1 bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks, and
Profession (sailor) is a class skill for you. You also gain a +1 trait bonus on weapon damage rolls
against creatures with the aquatic subtype.
