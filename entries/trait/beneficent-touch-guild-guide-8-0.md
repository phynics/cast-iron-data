**Prerequisite:** Member of the Silver Crusade faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Once per day, when you cast a spell or use a class ability that heals hit point damage, reroll any
1s that appear on the dice. You must accept the new rolls, even if they result in 1s.
