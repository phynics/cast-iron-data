**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-elf](ref/race/half-elf)

**Flavor:** One of your parents was a member of a wild elven tribe, and you've inherited a portion
of your elven parent's quick reflexes.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on initiative checks.

This was printed in _Legacy of Fire Player's Guide_ as "Ekujae Reflexes" and specifies that "one of
your parents was a member of the Ekujae tribe of the Mwangi Expanse."
