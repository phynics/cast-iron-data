**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Being self-taught has made it necessary for you to scour all documentation you can get
your hands on.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks to decipher
unfamiliar languages, and Linguistics is always a class skill for you. In addition, you gain a +1
trait bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks made to decipher the writing on a scroll.
