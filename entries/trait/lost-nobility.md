**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Your family was prominent before the rise of Thrune, but that legacy is now forgotten by
all but you.

**Benefit:** You hold every Chelish government official (including
[Hellknights](ref/rule/aff-lawknights)) personally responsible for your lost glory. You gain a +1
trait bonus on attack and damage rolls against these officials.
