**Prerequisite:** Member of the Sovereign Court faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Whether it is a result of your dedication to your work or just pure guts, nothing seems to shake
you. You gain a +1 trait bonus on saves against fear, and the DCs of Intimidate checks to demoralize
you increase by 3.
