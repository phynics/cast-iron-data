**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Flavor:** Born and raised a slave with no hope of advancement, you are adept at avoiding your
overseer's ire and attention, the better to spend time on your own interests.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks and a +3 trait bonus
on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to avoid punishment by
lawful authority.
