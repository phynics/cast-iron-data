**Type:** Combat

**Benefit:** Choose one type of medium or heavy armor (such as [chainmail](ref/eq-armor/chainmail)
or [half-plate](ref/eq-armor/half-plate)). If you have no allies within 60 feet when you are wearing
that type of armor, you gain DR 1/-. This stacks with any one other source of damage resistance you
have.
