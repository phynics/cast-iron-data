**Type:** Social

**Prerequisite:** [Kitharodian Academy](ref/rule/f-bards)

**Flavor:** From a very young age, the ability to master performance types has come quite naturally
to you.

**Benefit:** Choose a performance type. You gain a +2 trait bonus on all
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) checks made using that performance type. Perform is always a class
skill for you.
