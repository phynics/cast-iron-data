**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Ganzi](ref/race/ganzi)

**Flavor:** Straightforward answers bore you, for they only encourage people to depend on others
rather than find out for themselves. You have grown adept over the years at convincing people of
falsehoods and couching your honest answers in so much doublespeak they may as well be lies.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks, which increases to +5
when attempting to convince someone an honest answer is a falsehood.
