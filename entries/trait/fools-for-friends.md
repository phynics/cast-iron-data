**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Second Darkness

**Flavor:** You don't think of yourself as a gambler. In fact, you rather detest the whole thing.
Unfortunately, one or more of your friends (pick one or more of the other players' characters)
doesn't think so, and you've recently learned that friend - or friends - have decided to go to the
Gold Goblin's &quot&Cheat the Devil and Take his Gold&quot& tournament. Which pretty much means you
have to go as well, since if no one's there to watch out for them, they'll lose all their money and
respect. Again. Sometimes it's hard being the responsible one. Your devotion to your friendships
(even when said friends seem, at times, to be trying to test that devotion) is a point of pride to
you.

**Benefit:** Whenever you take the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action to help an ally, or
whenever an ally [aids you](ref/rule/aid-another-2) in this manner, a successful check grants an
additional +1 trait bonus to the check for which aid was being rendered. Additionally, as long as
one of your friends is within 30 feet, you gain a +1 trait bonus on all saving throws against charm
and compulsion effects.
