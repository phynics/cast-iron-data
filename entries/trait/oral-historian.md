**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Shoanti

**Flavor:** You learned numerous mnemonic devices to more easily recall information imparted by your
quah's master storyteller.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) and
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (oratory) checks, and one of those skills (your choice) is always a
class skill for you.
