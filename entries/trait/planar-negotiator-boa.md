**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar)

**Flavor:** You are skilled at convincing outsiders that you require their aid.

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast [lesser planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-lesser),
[planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally), or [greater planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-greater), you
receive a 10% discount on the monetary cost required by the summoned outsiders. This bonus has no
effect on services the outsiders may demand in order to strike a bargain.
