**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You grew up among thieves and scoundrels, and their unusual speech patterns and turns of
phrase don't faze you in the slightest.

**Benefit:** Anyone who attempts to use [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) to deliver a secret message to you
gains a +5 bonus on her Bluff check. When you attempt to intercept a secret message using
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), you gain a +5 trait bonus on the attempt.
