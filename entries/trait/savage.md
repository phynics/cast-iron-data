**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in untamed lands far from civilization. You learned to survive
in the elements among brutal humanoids and beasts.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) checks
and a +1 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to get along in the wild. Knowledge
(nature) is always a class skill for you.
