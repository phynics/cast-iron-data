**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dhampir](ref/race/dhampir); Ajibachana

**Flavor:** The unnatural symmetry of your face gives you an attractiveness that is at once
captivating but hard to quantify.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks and a +1 trait bonus
on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to influence those who would be attracted to you.
