**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Religion:** [Zogmugot](ref/faith/zogmugot)

**Flavor:** You are adept at swimming and holding your breath.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks, and can
[hold your breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for a number of rounds equal to three times your
Constitution score before you risk drowning.
