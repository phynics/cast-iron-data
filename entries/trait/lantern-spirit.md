**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar); Lawbringer

**Flavor:** Your celestial ancestor was a [lantern archon](ref/monster/archon-lantern-archon) for
centuries before ascending to a higher rank.

**Benefit:** You feel a strange sense of nostalgia whenever you use your
[continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame) [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and
can use it as a **move action** instead of a standard action.
