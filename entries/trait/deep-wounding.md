**Religion:** [Achaekek](ref/faith/achaekek)

**Flavor:** You have learned the bloody secret of striking true and deep.

**Benefit:** When you deal [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage with a melee weapon, a successful DC 20
[Heal](ref/skill/heal) check is required to stop the bleed damage. Magical healing stops the damage
as normal.
