**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** When deprived of magic, you fight harder.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on weapon attack and damage rolls while within an area of
antimagic such as that created by an [antimagic field](ref/spell/antimagic-field). You also gain
this bonus for 1 round after you are subject to an area or targeted
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) spell, regardless of the effect's success or failure in
dispelling your spells and magical effects.
