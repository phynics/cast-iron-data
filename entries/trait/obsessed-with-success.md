**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Chelaxian

**Flavor:** A Chelaxian without a visible degree of success is marked as pathetic, and so you grew
up careful not to let failure stick.

**Benefit:** Once per day, when you re-attempt a skill check you have previously failed within the
last minute, you can add a +4 trait bonus to that check.
