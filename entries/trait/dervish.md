**Prerequisite:** Member of the Qadira faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You trained at a secret fighting school in Katheer, where you learned the art of rapid movement in
combat. You gain a +1 dodge bonus to Armor Class against attacks of opportunity caused when you move
out of or within a threatened area.
