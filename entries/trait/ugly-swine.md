**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Flavor:** You are a repulsive little freak whose origins are unclear. Your head is small for a
goblin and your mouth oddly narrow, allowing you to pass in urban environments as an unidentifiable
mongrel, providing you keep a low profile. However, if examined closely, your goblin origins are
revealed.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks, and Disguise is a
class skill for you.
