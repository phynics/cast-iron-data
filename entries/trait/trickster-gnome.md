**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** You are particularly adept with your racial
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and as child you quickly learned how
[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights) and [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound) could be used to
amuse your friends and fool your elders. This natural talent for illusion continued as you grew
older, and before long you were being offered training in more advanced figments and glamers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to your caster level when determining the duration, range,
and area of all spells from the illusion school.
