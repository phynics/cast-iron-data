**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Whether you were on your way to becoming a drow slave or slated for the ghoul chattel
pits, you very narrowly avoided a life of miserable captivity.

**Benefit:** You make a habit of avoiding restraints or tight spaces, gaining a +1 trait bonus on
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) checks and a +2 trait bonus to CMD to avoid being pinned.
