**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** You are able to tap into the living energy of the world to shatter lesser magic.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on caster level checks while touching the ground or unworked
stone. This includes [dispel](ref/spell/dispel-magic) checks and checks to overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance).
