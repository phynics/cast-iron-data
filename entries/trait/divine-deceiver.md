**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Razmiran

**Flavor:** You are trained in the methods of Razmir's priesthood, and can use your arcana to fuel
minor healing powers.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a full-round action, you can sacrifice a prepared arcane spell or spell
slot to manifest healing magic. This acts as [cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds),
except you heal 1d6 points of damage per level of the spell or spell slot sacrificed (maximum 5d6)
and the hit points healed are [temporary](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) (lasting 1 hour).
