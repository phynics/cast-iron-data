**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Rahadoum

**Flavor:** As a Rahadoumi who rejects covenants with gods, your belief is strong enough to repel
divine spells.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against divine spells, but you must make a
saving throw even when that magic is beneficial to you.
