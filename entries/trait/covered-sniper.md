**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kasatha](ref/race/kasatha)

**Flavor:** You were attacked by strangers that threatened to expose your existence to dangerous
forces and you defended yourself using cover.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on ranged weapon damage rolls as long as you have
[cover](ref/rule/cover) from your opponent.
