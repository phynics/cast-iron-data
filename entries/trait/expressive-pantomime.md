**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have learned to express yourself without having to depend on words.

**Benefit:** When attempting a [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check to deliver a secret message, you can
take a -5 penalty on the check to communicate a simple message even to creatures that do not share a
language with you. You cannot communicate with mindless creatures.
