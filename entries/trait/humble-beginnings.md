**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Varisia

**Flavor:** You didn't have any intention of becoming an adventurer, but a single, unexpected
confrontation changed everything.

**Benefit:** Choose one of the following: boot, bucket, frying pan, mug, rolling pin, spade, or
stool. You are treated as having the [Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard) feat when wielding
the chosen item. (These items should all be considered improvised melee weapons that deal 1d4 points
of bludgeoning damage.)
