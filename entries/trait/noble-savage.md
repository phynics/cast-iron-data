**Type:** Social

**Prerequisite:** Associated with the [Ulfen Guard](ref/rule/f-imperial)

**Flavor:** You hail from the fringes of civilization or the bottommost strata of society, but
something in your character makes that seem endearing.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) checks when dealing with people of high social standing.
