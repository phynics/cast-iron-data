**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Skull and Shackles

**Flavor:** You are a student of the ancient history of Golarion, and you've come to the Shackles to
explore the crumbling and vine-choked ruins of the cyclops empire of Ghol-Gan, which have lain
abandoned among the isles of the Shackles for thousands of years.

You went to a tavern called the Formidably Maid in Port Peril last night to meet a contact who
supposedly had an old map of lost cyclops ruins to sell, but the drink was stronger than you had
expected and you passed out before making the deal.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) and
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks, and one of these skills is a class skill for
you. In addition, you gain Cyclops or Polyglot as a bonus language.
