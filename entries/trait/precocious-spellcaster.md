**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Taldor

**Flavor:** Before you even began your training as a spellcaster, you spent a great deal of time
studying cantrips and simple spells on your own. Because of this, you developed some innate magical
abilities without any outside guidance.

**Benefit:** Select one cantrip and one 1st-level spell; when you cast these spells, they function
at one caster level higher than your actual caster level.
