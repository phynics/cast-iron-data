**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Thuvia

**Flavor:** You are one of the famed performers of Lamasara, and your skill is known throughout
Thuvia.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you start a bardic performance, you can increase the DC of the saving
throw to resist your performance's effects by 1. This effect lasts until you switch performances or
end your performance.
