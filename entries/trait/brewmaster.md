**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** Your family brought the secrets of dwarven brewing to non-dwarven lands. Though this has
given you skill in the brewer's craft, it's also earned you distrust among dwarven purists.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (brewer) and
[Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (alchemy) checks, but you take a -1 penalty on
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to change the attitude of dwarves who know that your
family has shared brewing secrets with non-dwarves.
