**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk)

**Flavor:** In desperate times, your adrenaline kicks in to help you avoid danger.

**Benefit:** During the first round of combat in which you can act, you ignore
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) and gain an additional 5 feet of movement. This
benefit does not apply when you are carrying a medium or heavier load.
