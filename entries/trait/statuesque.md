**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Oread](ref/race/oread)

**Flavor:** You inherited the statuesque physical features of your shaitan parent.

**Benefit:** [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) becomes a class skill for you, and you gain a +2 trait
bonus on Disguise checks to disguise yourself as a statue made of a specific type of stone, such as
marble, sandstone, or alabaster. Furthermore, you take none of the penalties normally associated
with disguising yourself as a different type of creature.

You must choose the type of stone you resemble when you take this trait; once chosen, it cannot be
changed.
