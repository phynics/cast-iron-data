**Prerequisite:** Member of the Silver Crusade faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your good-aligned spells are especially powerful, and they function as if your caster level were 1
higher. This trait makes your aura more powerful (one step higher), as outlined in detect evil.
