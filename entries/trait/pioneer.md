**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Kingmaker

**Flavor:** You have long lived along the southern border of Brevoy, in the shadow of wilderness
known as the Stolen Lands. Life has been hard, but through hunting, trapping, trading, and coaxing
crops from the freezing earth, you've learned how to survive on the rugged frontier. With the
wilderness ever at your door, you've also learned much about its denizens and the wild creatures
that lurk in that unwholesome land. Your family might even claim holdings in the Stolen Lands, with
elders telling stories of being driven from or robbed of a lost ancestral homestead, fertile
farmlands, bountiful orchards, or a hidden mining claim. Whether because of your personal expertise
and familiarity with the borderlands or in order to reclaim your family's land, you've joined the
expedition into the Stolen Lands.

**Benefit:** You begin play with a [horse](ref/eq-misc/horse). Also, choose one of the following
skills: [Climb](ref/skill/climb), [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal),
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature), [Perception](ref/skill/perception),
[Ride](ref/skill/ride), [Survival](ref/skill/survival), or [Swim](ref/skill/swim) - you gain a +1
trait bonus on this skill.
