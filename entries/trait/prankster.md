**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** You always have a great idea for a prank.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver
checks. You also gain a +1 trait bonus to your CMD when an opponent attempts a dirty trick combat
maneuver check against you. If you succeed at a dirty trick combat maneuver check against a
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) opponent, increase the duration of the condition caused by 1
round.
