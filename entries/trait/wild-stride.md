**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Benefit:** Choose a favored terrain from those listed in the ranger
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) class ability. You can move through naturally
occurring difficult terrain without any penalty while within your favored terrain. Terrain created
or modified by magic or traps affects you normally.
