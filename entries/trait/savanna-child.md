**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Katapesh, plains

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in central Katapesh, among the rolling savannahs that dominate
the nation's interior. You spent many of your youth exploring these vast reaches, and know much of
the savannah's secrets.

**Benefit:** Pick one of the following skills: [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal),
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature), or [Ride](ref/skill/ride). You gain a +1 trait
bonus on that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
