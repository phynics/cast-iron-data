**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Jadwiga

**Flavor:** You come from a family that commands respect.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks, and Intimidate
is always a class skill for you. In addition, you gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) checks related to the Irrisen aristocracy.
