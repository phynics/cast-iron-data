**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Taldor

**Flavor:** You attack life with the ferocity of a charging lion and inspire others with your
audacious deeds.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you make a [charge](ref/rule/charge) attack, you can inspire allies
within 30 feet. They gain an additional +2 trait bonus on attack and damage rolls when they make a
charge attack before the beginning of your next turn.
