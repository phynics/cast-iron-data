**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Rise of the Runelords

**Flavor:** As a faithful adherent of Abadar, Desna, Erastil, Gozreh, Sarenrae, or Shelyn, you've
come to the Swallowtail Festival to celebrate the consecration of Sandpoint Cathedral.

**Benefit:** You gain a [silver holy symbol](ref/eq-misc/holy-symbol-silver) of your chosen deity,
and so long as you worship that deity and openly wear his or her symbol, you regain +1 additional
hit point every time you receive magical healing.
