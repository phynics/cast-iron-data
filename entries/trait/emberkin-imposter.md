**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Qadira

**Flavor:** You have found it is easier to be mistaken for someone with celestial blood than to
reveal your true nature.

**Benefit:** You gain a +5 trait bonus on Disguise checks when pretending to be an aasimar, and a +2
trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks against those you have convinced of your
supposed celestial nature.
