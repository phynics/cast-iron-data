**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **standard action**, you can force a particularly odious belch at a
single opponent within 5 feet. The character affected must make a DC 12 Fortitude save or be
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1d6 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based.
