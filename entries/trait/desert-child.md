**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Thuvia, desert

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in rocky deserts, and are accustomed to high temperatures.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on saving throws made to resist the effects of being in
[hot conditions](ref/rule/heat-dangers) and a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against fire effects.
