**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Brigh](ref/faith/brigh)

**Flavor:** You hear Brigh's whisper in the workings of technology, granting you better
understanding of and insight into constructs.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on caster level checks to overcome the
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) of constructs.
