**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** You know what it takes to get your goods to market and will stop at nothing to protect
your products. Years of fending off thieves, cutthroats, and brigands have given you a sixth sense
when it comes to danger.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks made to
determine surprise, and Perception is always a class skill for you.
