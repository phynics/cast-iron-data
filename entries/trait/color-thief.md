**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Benefit:** Your skin is an odd tint that somehow absorbs background colors. Maybe because your
mother drank a lot of potions when you were still inside her belly. Maybe because you've been
blessed by the [barghests](ref/monster/barghest). Maybe because you're just better than the other
boring goblins. In any case, you gain a +2 trait bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks,
providing you wear only light armor or no armor.
