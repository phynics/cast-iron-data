**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have long since forgotten to care what people think about you - though your
interactions are always memorable, at the very least.

**Benefit:** Choose [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (act), Perform (comedy), Perform (oratory), or
Perform (sing); you gain a +1 trait bonus on checks with the chosen skill and gain it as a class
skill.
