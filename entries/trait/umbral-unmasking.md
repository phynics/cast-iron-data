**Type:** Drawback

**Benefit:** You cast no shadow whatsoever, or the shadow you do have is monstrous. Under normal
lighted conditions, this is not hard to observe - but uncommon to notice. Creatures that succeed at
a DC 15 Wisdom check notice it plainly (an additional [Perception](ref/skill/perception) check may
be required based on environmental conditions). This telltale sign of wickedness cannot be concealed
by [misdirection](ref/spell/misdirection), [nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection), or illusions,
except those that also affect shadows (such as [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility)).
