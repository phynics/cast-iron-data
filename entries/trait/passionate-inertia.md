**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Your passions drive your thinking so irresistibly that even magic struggles to change
your mind.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Will saves to resist having your mind changed about
something, but you take a -1 penalty on Will saves to resist temptation and compulsions you might
reasonably be already inclined toward.
