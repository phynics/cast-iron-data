**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** You were exposed to a potent source of positive energy as a child, perhaps by being born
under the right cosmic sign, or maybe because one of your parents was a gifted healer.

**Benefit:** As a **standard action**, you may automatically [stabilize](ref/spell/stabilize) a
[dying](ref/misc/dying) creature merely by touching it.
