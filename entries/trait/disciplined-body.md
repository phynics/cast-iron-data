**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Vudrani

**Flavor:** You respect your mind's mastery over your body's full potential.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can use your Wisdom modifier instead of either your Strength or
Dexterity modifier on an attack roll, a combat maneuver check, a Strength or Dexterity-based skill
check, a Strength check, a Dexterity check, or a Reflex save. You must choose to use this trait
before rolling.
