**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You're familiar with Nar-Voth's trade routes and negotiation involving the denizens of
the Darklands.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks relating to
subterranean trade and on [Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering) checks
relating to subterranean creatures. Select one of these skills to be a class skill for you.
