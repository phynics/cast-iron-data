**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar)

**Flavor:** You attract followers who agree with your philosophical outlook on life. Your followers
and cohorts are more willing to serve you because of their confidence in your strong moral position.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to your [Leadership](ref/feat/leadership) score if all your
cohorts and followers have an alignment within one step of your alignment.
