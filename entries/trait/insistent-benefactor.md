**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Pathfinders of different backgrounds don't always realize what's best for them, and you
have perfected the art of forcing your magical boons on your allies.

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a harmless spell, you gain a +2 trait bonus on caster level checks to
overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance), and the saving throw DC of the spell (if
any) is increased by 2.
