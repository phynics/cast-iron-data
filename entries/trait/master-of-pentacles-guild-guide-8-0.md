**Prerequisite:** Member of the Dark Archive faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your many years spent studying the art of summoning have given you a unique knowledge of this subtle
and highly complicated discipline. Once per day, when casting a spell of the conjuration school,
treat your caster level as 2 higher when determining the spell's duration.
