**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Galt

**Flavor:** You know how best to appear supportive of the current regime despite the vagaries of a
shifting government.

**Benefit:** The [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) DC needed for a city guard, a politician, or
other governmental functionary in a large town or larger settlement to gain a hunch about your
trustworthiness is 25 (instead of 20). On a failed check, you're seen as trustworthy regardless of
the situation.
