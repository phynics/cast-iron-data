**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** Your greatest joy is being in the thick of battle, and smiting your enemies for a
righteous or even dastardly cause.

**Benefit:** Whenever you have a morale bonus to weapon attack rolls, you also receive a +1 trait
bonus on weapon damage rolls.
