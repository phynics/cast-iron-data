**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Osirion

**Benefit:** Whenever a caster level check is required to treat an affliction with magic (such as
when casting [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) on a cursed creature or object), you gain a +2
trait bonus on your caster level check. Additionally, you gain a +1 trait bonus on your saving
throws against curses.
