**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Serpent's Skull

**Flavor:** Traveling from lands far to the north, you likely have little experience with the
jungle, its denizens, or its strange forces. Just as the lands of Garund are wild and exotic to you,
so are you to them. This unusualness manifests in one of two ways.

**Benefit:** Choose to gain either a +2 trait bonus on all [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks made
against inhabitants of the jungle, or treat your spells with the mind-affecting descriptor as being
+1 caster level higher when cast against creatures and natives of the jungle.
