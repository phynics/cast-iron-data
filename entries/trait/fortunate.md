**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You are blessed by fate.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you use a spell or magic item with a randomized effect (such as
[confusion](ref/spell/confusion), [mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image),
[prismatic spray](ref/spell/prismatic-spray), or a
[bag of tricks](ref/magic-wondrous/bag-of-tricks-tan)), before you roll to determine the result, you
may choose to roll twice and pick either result.

**Suggested Characters:** Calistrians, harrow deck readers, Vudrani.
