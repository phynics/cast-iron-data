**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold)

**Flavor:** Your distinctive golden scales strongly reflect the light, making other kobolds mistrust
you, but aboveground races favor you.

**Benefit:** You take a -1 penalty on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when dealing with other kobolds, but a +2 bonus on Bluff
checks and Diplomacy checks when dealing with non-reptilian humanoids.
