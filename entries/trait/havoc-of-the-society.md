**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** Member of The [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars)

**Flavor:** Through countless hours studying vast libraries of arcane power, you have learned how to
cause greater damage to your foes.

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals damage, you gain a +1 trait bonus to the total
damage dealt. This damage is considered force damage regardless of the type of damage dealt by the
spell.
