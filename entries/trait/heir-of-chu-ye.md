**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Tian Xia

**Benefit:** If you would take a penalty for using the [Heal](ref/skill/heal) skill to treat deadly
wounds without one or more uses of a [healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit), that penalty is
reduced by 2 (minimum no penalty). Additionally, a creature can benefit from you treating its deadly
wounds if you do so within 48 hours of its injury instead of within 24 hours.
