**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** At some point your family served a dragon, and acted as its emissary to others. Some
members of your family have benefited from this arrangement, gaining some small fraction of that
dragon's noble bearing.

**Benefit:** Choose one category of [true dragon](ref/family/dragon-true) (chromatic, metallic,
primal, etc.). You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks against that
category of dragon and a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks against all
creatures of the dragon type that are not of the same category of true dragon.
