**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Osirion

**Flavor:** You are descended from one of Ancient Osirion's pharaohs, and a sliver of her
otherworldly power has been passed onto you.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Will saves against fear effects. Any time you become
[confused](ref/misc/confused), reduce the duration of the effect by a number of rounds equal to your
Wisdom modifier (to a minimum of 1 round of confusion).
