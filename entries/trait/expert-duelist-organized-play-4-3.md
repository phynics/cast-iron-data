**Prerequisite:** Member of the Taldor faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

In your youth, you spent countless hours perfecting the art of the duel, focusing your feints on
defeating a single foe. You gain a +1 trait bonus to your Armor Class so long as you are adjacent to
a single foe. This trait bonus is not applied to your Armor Class for touch attacks or when you are
denied your Dexterity bonus.
