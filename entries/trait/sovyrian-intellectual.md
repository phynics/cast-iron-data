**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf); Must be a 1st-level character

**Flavor:** Having grown up in one of the intellectual centers of Castrovel, you speak precisely and
persuasively, especially among elves.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks. This bonus
increases to +3 against creatures with the elf subtype.
