**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Some disagree with your choice to wield a firearm, and their scorn has only hardened
your resolve in battle.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on all saving throws against charm and compulsion effects.
Also, once per day when you succeed at a saving throw against such an effect, you regain 1 grit
point.
