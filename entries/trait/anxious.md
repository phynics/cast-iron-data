**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** After suffering terribly for not being tightlipped enough as a child, such as when you
accidentally exposed your family to enemy inquisitors, you developed a habit of being overly
cautious with your words.

**Benefit:** You take a -2 penalty on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks and must speak slowly
due to the concentration required. Unless stated otherwise, you are assumed to not be speaking at a
volume above a whisper.
