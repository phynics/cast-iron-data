**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Chaldira Zuzaristan](ref/faith/chaldira-zuzaristan)

**Flavor:** Your studies of Chaldira Zuzaristan's exploits have given you a knack for avoiding
trouble.

**Benefit:** Once per day before the result of a saving throw is known, you can reroll that saving
throw. You must take the second result even if it is worse.
