**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Razmiran

**Flavor:** You are an adherent of the Razmiri faith, or once were and have since renounced it.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) and
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks, and one of these skills is always a
class skill for you. This bonus increases to +2 when dealing with specifics of the Razmiri faith.
