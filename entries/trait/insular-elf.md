**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Flavor:** After living among non-elves for a time, you developed a dismissive attitude toward
rash, shorter-lived races.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Will saving throws against charm, compulsion, and fear
effects created by humanoids other than elves.
