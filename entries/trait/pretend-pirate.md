**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** You enjoy luring slave ships to you. Sometimes you run a pirate flag to lull criminals
into a false sense of security; other times you disguise yourself as a merchant ship.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks made to disguise yourself
as a pirate or merchant sailor. In addition, you can take 5 minutes to alter your ship's appearance
to add +2 to the DC of any check made to identify your ship's allegiance or purpose.
