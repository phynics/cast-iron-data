**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mwangi Expanse

**Flavor:** The Mwangi Expanse is a land of dozens of languages, and you grew up speaking most of
them.

**Benefit:** You gain Polyglot as a bonus language. In addition, you gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks when trying to communicate with someone who does not
share a common language with you.
