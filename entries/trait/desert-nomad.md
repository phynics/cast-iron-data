**Region:** Thuvia

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in the burning sands of Thuvia's desert interior.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on any saving throws made to resist the effects of being in
hot conditions, and a +1 trait bonus on all saving throws against fire effects.
