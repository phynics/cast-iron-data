**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Magnimar

**Benefit:** You were raised in the Shadow district of Magnimar beneath the Irespan, a veiled place
the light of the sun touches for only 3 hours per day. Your well-founded suspicions grant you a +2
trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks in [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light),
and Perception is always a class skill for you.
