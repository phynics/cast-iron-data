**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You've always found yourself in positions where others look up to you as a leader, and
you can distinctly remember an event from your early childhood where you led several other children
to accomplish a goal that each of you individually could not.

**Benefit:** All cohorts, followers, or summoned creatures under your leadership gain a +1 morale
bonus on Will saves to avoid mind-affecting effects. If you ever take the
[Leadership](ref/feat/leadership) feat, you gain a +1 trait bonus to your Leadership score.
