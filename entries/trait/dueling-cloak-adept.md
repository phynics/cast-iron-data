**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You are trained in using your cloak to disguise your body and deceive your enemies.

**Benefit:** While wearing a cloak and using an
[Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword), you gain a +2 trait bonus on
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks made to feint. Additionally, every time you successfully feint while
wearing a cloak, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to your Armor Class until your next turn.
