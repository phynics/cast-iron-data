**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [Orc](ref/race/orc)

**Benefit:** Huge, sharp tusks bulge from your mouth, and you receive a
[bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d4 damage for Medium characters). If used as part of a full
attack action, the bite attack is made at your full base attack bonus -5.
