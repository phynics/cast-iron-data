**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Your eidolon - and your connection to it - makes others ill at ease.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks, and Intimidate
is always a class skill for you. In addition, if your eidolon is summoned and within 30 feet, and
its size exceeds your own, use its size modifier on any Intimidate checks you make.
