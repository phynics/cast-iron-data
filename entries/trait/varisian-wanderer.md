**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Varisia

**Flavor:** You were raised among or have spent time with a group of Varisian nomads, whether
travelers, Sczarni criminals, or entertainers, and have learned their ways.

**Benefit:** Choose one of the following skills: [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (choose one type),
[Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (fortune-teller), or
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand). You gain a +1 trait bonus on this skill, and it is
always a class skill for you.
