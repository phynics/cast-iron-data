**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You know how to take advantage of enemies who are unprepared for your assault.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on initiative checks. In addition, once per day when you make
an attack of opportunity, you gain a +2 trait bonus on the attack roll.
