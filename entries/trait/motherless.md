**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** Your birth killed your mother, and you learned, even before words, how to manipulate
others into looking after you.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks made for the purpose of making you seem injured, sickly, or
weak in some other way.
