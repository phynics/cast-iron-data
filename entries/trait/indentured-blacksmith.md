**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Kaoling

**Flavor:** As a former slave of the hobgoblins, you were forced to make many of the weapons and
shields that your captors used in war.

**Benefit:** Choose either [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (armor) or Craft (weapons). You gain a +1
trait bonus on the chosen skill, and it becomes a class skill for you.
