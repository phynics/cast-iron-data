**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Werebear-kin

**Flavor:** When you first demonstrated your werebear ancestry, you slept for a week and awoke
feeling more refreshed than ever before.

**Benefit:** Whenever you undergo complete [bed rest](ref/rule/healing) for 24 hours, you recover an
amount of hit points equal to three times your character level.
