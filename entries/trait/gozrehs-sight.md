**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh)

**Flavor:** Your faith in the Wind and the Waves helps you navigate through all manner of mist or
haze.

**Benefit:** You can see normally up to 5 feet away in areas of nonmagical [fog](ref/rule/fog),
mist, [rain](ref/rule/rain-snow-sleet-and-hail), or similar conditions (such as the spray of a
waterfall). Your sight is still obscured beyond 5 feet as normal in these conditions.
