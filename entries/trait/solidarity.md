**Type:** Regional

**Region:** City of Brass

**Flavor:** While enslaved in the City of Brass, you watched too many of your friends fall victim to
efreet tempers. Now, you help others avoid thorny situations whenever you are able.

**Benefit:** Any time you successfully use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action to help an
ally succeed at a [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) or [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) check, you
add a +3 bonus to this check (rather than a +2 bonus).
