**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Apostae

**Flavor:** Although you were born and lived on Golarion, you recently awakened in a strange vault
with no memory of how you got there, surrounded by sleeping creatures of every shape and size in
smooth glass eggs, all watched over by uncommunicative crab-like automatons.

**Benefit:** As a side effect of your stasis, you gain all the benefits of 8 hours of sleep in only
2 hours. This allows a spellcaster who must rest to prepare spells to do so after only 2 hours, but
does not allow a spellcaster to prepare spells more than once per day.
