**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Flavor:** Your bones, flesh, and skin are a bit more elastic than those of most goblins - when you
fall, you tend to bounce a little better as a result.

**Benefit:** Whenever you take [falling damage](ref/rule/falling), the first 1d6 points of lethal
damage suffered in the fall are automatically converted to nonlethal damage. You also gain a +2
trait bonus on all Reflex saves made to avoid unexpected falls.
