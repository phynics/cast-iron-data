**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Chelaxian

**Flavor:** You grew up in wonder of Chelish theater, and spent countless hours in the opera houses
across Cheliax and even more time perfecting your voice.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (sing) checks.
Furthermore you have memorized the librettos of many works sung in the ancient Azlanti tongue,
granting you a +2 trait bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks when trying to decipher
the ancient Azlanti language.
