**Type:** Regional

**Region:** River Kingdoms

**Flavor:** Terrible things prowl the moors of your homeland, and you are all too familiar with
hiding or fleeing from them.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks when you are in bog,
marsh, or swamp terrain, and you treat shallow bogs and swamp undergrowth as normal terrain for the
purposes of movement.
