**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your family has strong ties to a [Hellknight](ref/rule/aff-lawknights) order, and you
harness this belief as a weapon.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **swift action**, you can empower the next single melee attack you
make to be treated as lawful-aligned for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
