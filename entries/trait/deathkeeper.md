**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf); Pahmet

**Flavor:** You have lived for a time deep inside the necropolises of the pharaohs. Your exposure to
undead creatures and their necromantic energies has strengthened your ability to resist the powers
of the unliving.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against the supernatural abilities of undead
creatures.
