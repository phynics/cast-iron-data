**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Varisian

**Flavor:** You spent many years wandering the roads of Varisia and beyond, and during clear nights,
you took to tracking the celestial bodies that adorn Golarion's skies.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks.
