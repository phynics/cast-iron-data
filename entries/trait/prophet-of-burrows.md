**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk)

**Region:** Druma

**Flavor:** While you might not be a practicing follower of the
[Prophecies of Kalistrade](ref/rule/f-profits), you value human coin more than most ratfolk do and
have taken the mercantile practices of the Prophecies to heart.

**Benefit:** Whenever you roll a skill check to earn money, increase the amount of money that you
earn by 5%.
