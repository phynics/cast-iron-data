**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Vildeis](ref/faith/vildeis)

**Flavor:** You interposed yourself between an innocent and a dangerous evil monster or force, lost
a great deal of blood as a result, and barely survived. Now, your blood seems to replenish with
blessed speed, a sure sign of Vildeis's favor.

**Benefit:** You [recover twice as many hit points](ref/rule/healing) and points of ability damage
as normal when you rest, or three times as many when you take total bed rest.
