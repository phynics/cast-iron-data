**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** Dwarven Deities

**Flavor:** Your knowledge of the dwarven pantheon tells you precisely which gods have jurisdiction
over which aspects of your life, and you can call upon the appropriate deity for help even if that
deity is not your patron.

**Benefit:** At the start of each day, pick one of the following skills:
[Appraise](ref/skill/appraise), [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (pick one
craft skill), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), or
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local). You gain a +2 trait bonus on that skill until the
start of the next day.
