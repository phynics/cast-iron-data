**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You've been raised with tales of the great linnorms, and taught that the death curses of
these vile dragons cannot stop the truly mighty.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saves to resist curses and spells with the curse
descriptor and a +3 trait bonus on saves to resist curses originating from a
[linnorm](ref/family/linnorm).
