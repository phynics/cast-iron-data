**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Blue-scaled

**Flavor:** The magic-users in your tribe pride themselves on their ability to control the forces of
water. Integral to this practice is the ability to study and communicate with visitors from the
Elemental Plane of Water.

**Benefit:** In addition to speaking Draconic, you begin play with the ability to speak Aquan.
