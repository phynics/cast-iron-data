**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** People tend to assume you either are, or are willing to become, a minor cog in some evil
cabal or conspiracy.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to either join or imply that you already belong to an
evil organization.
