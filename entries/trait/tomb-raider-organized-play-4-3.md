**Prerequisite:** Member of the Osirion faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You've spent most of your life exploring the ancient tombs and catacombs of Osirion. You gain a +1
bonus on Perception and Knowledge (dungeoneering) checks, and one of these skills (your choice)
becomes a class skill for you.
