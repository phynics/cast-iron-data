**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn)

**Benefit:** Once per day when you manifest your faith in your goddess, you gain a +4 trait bonus on
a single [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy),
or [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) skill check. You must choose to use this trait immediately after
rolling the skill check but before the result is revealed by the GM.
