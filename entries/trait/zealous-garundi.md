**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Garundi

**Flavor:** You felt lost and aimless in your youth until a patron's emotional intervention showed
you the power of throwing yourself completely into what you do.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can add a +2 trait bonus on any attack roll, saving throw, or skill
check. You must choose to apply this bonus before rolling the die. When you use this ability, you
take a -2 penalty to your AC and on saving throws until the end of your next turn.
