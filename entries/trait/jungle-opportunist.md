**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk)

**Flavor:** You trained with your clan to hunt large prey such as the dinosaurs of the Valashmai
Jungle.

**Benefit:** When you grant an ally a bonus on an attack roll against a foe of Large or larger size
due to [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) or the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action, that ally
gains a +1 trait bonus on weapon damage rolls against that creature as well.
