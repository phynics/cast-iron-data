**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Ghlaunder](ref/faith/ghlaunder)

**Flavor:** Your work with infections has infused your spirit with disease.

**Benefit:** Any creature that performs a [blood drain](ref/umr/blood-drain) or
[swallow whole](ref/umr/swallow-whole) attack on you must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC =
10 + your character level + your Constitution modifier) or become infected with
[filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever). This is a poison effect.
