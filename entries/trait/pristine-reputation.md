**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Shizuru](ref/faith/shizuru)

**Flavor:** You come from an especially esteemed family, religious order, school, or other group
that worships Shizuru, and draw upon that tradition to maintain your identity.

**Benefit:** The first time each day that you fail a saving throw against a charm or compulsion
effect, you can reroll that saving throw. If you do so, you gain a +2 trait bonus on this roll and
use this result as the actual result for that saving throw.
