**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Tian

**Flavor:** After a close call with [snow blindness](ref/rule/reflective-snow) on the Crown of the
World or the scouring winds of Hongal, you honed your instincts to protect your eyes.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws to avoid being [blinded](ref/misc/blinded)
or [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled).
