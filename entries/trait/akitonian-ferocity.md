**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Akiton

**Flavor:** You were raised on Akiton, where you were hardened against the ravages of battle.

**Benefit:** Once per day, the first time you are brought below 0 hit points but not killed, you can
fight on for 1 more round as if [disabled](ref/misc/disabled). At the end of your next turn, unless
brought to above 0 hit points, you immediately fall unconscious and begin [dying](ref/misc/dying).
