**Prerequisite:** Member of the Scarab Sages faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You were raised to believe that undead are nothing to fear—they are simply the unliving remnants of
your honored ancestors. Once per day, you can surround yourself with an aura of unlife.
Unintelligent undead ignore you unless you take an action against them, as per hide from undead. The
protection lasts 1 round for every 2 character levels you have (minimum of 1 round). If you take any
offensive action against any undead, this effect immediately ends. This is a supernatural ability.
