**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Thuvia

**Flavor:** Alchemy is a point of national pride in Thuvia, home of the famous
[sun orchid elixir](ref/magic-artifact/sun-orchid-elixir). But resources are often hard to come by
in the blasted desert landscape, so you have found creative ways to create many of the tools you
need to survive.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (alchemy) skill checks. This
bonus increases to +3 when you are using Craft (alchemy) to craft an alchemical item.
