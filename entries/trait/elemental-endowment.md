**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Osirion

**Flavor:** You see the interactions between elementals as the epitome of balance; you've studied
the traditional elemental bindings of Ancient Osirion and can draw upon their power.

**Benefit:** As an **immediate action**, once per day when you cast a spell with the air, earth,
fire, or water descriptor, you can gain a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to the spell's level. This effect lasts
for 1 minute.
