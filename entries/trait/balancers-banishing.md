**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** The interference of the Outer Planes in mortal affairs angers you to no end and
invigorates the potency of your magic.

**Benefit:** As long as you remain neutral, whenever you cast a spell that would send an outsider
with the chaotic, evil, good, or lawful subtype back to its home plane, the spell's saving throw DC
increases by 1.
