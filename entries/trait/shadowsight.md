**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Nidal

**Flavor:** Your people's ancient compact with Zon-Kuthon manifests strongly in you. While the
darkness remains fearsome, you can at least see what lurks there.

**Benefit:** As a **swift action** once per day, your eyes can pierce the surrounding darkness - you
gain the benefit of [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) for a number of rounds equal to
your level.
