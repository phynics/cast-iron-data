**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** Characters from Qadira who have had family members, neighbors, or friends affected
by Taldan aggression or who have fallen victim to this hatred themselves.

**Region:** Qadira

**Flavor:** You have spent your adult life waiting for Taldor to give you an excuse to fight.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on initiative checks, and if you are able to act during the
surprise round of an encounter, you can draw a weapon (but not a potion or magic item) as a **free
action** during that round.
