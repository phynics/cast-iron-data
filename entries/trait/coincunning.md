**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Janderhoff

**Flavor:** Whether from working with dwarven merchants or being one yourself, you've developed a
distinctly dwarven business sense.

**Benefit:** You receive a +2 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to notice
coins, gems, jewelry, and valuable trade goods. You gain a chance to notice such goods whenever you
pass within 10 feet of them, whether or not you are actively looking.
