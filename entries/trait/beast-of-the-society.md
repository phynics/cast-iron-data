**Type:** Magic

**Prerequisite:** Member of The [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars)

**Flavor:** A druid at the Grand Lodge revealed to you secrets changing your form into that of an
animal.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use your _wild shape_ class ability to turn yourself into a Small or
Medium animal, the effect lasts for 2 hours per druid level instead of 1 hour per druid level.
