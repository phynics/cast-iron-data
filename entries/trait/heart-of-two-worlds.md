**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Born into a world split in twain, you are particularly skilled at understanding and
empathizing with those around you.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks involving creatures that belong to the same race (or,
if human, ethnicity) as your mother or father.
