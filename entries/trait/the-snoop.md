**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Tyrant's Grasp

**Flavor:** You know a little bit about most everything and everyone in Roslar's Coffer. It may be
your profession, as you could be an archivist, crier, or librarian - or you simply make a hobby of
gossip. Either way, you have a gift for recall and reading others.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) and
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks, and these both become class skills for you.
Once each day you can reroll a single [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) or
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) check against a creature you have known for at least 24
hours, using the better result.
