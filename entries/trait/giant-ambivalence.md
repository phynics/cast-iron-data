**Type:** Regional

**Region:** temperate highlands

**Flavor:** You witnessed or were otherwise strongly influenced by two separate acts by giants, one
of great beneficence and one of great violence. You reflexively gauge the intentions of powerful
creatures in case they turn to violence.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on initiative checks. You also gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks, and a +2 trait bonus on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to not be surprised in a surprise round of combat.

**Likely Regions:** Isger, Molthune, Numeria, Realm of the Mammoth Lords, Varisia.

**Likely giants:** Cloud, Hill, River, Stone
