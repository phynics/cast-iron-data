**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk)

**Region:** Osirion

**Religion:** [Bastet](ref/faith/bastet)

**Flavor:** Although Bastet has been all but forgotten by the native Osirians, you either sojourned
with or were raised in a cult dedicated to the cat-headed goddess of Ancient Osirion.

**Benefit:** You treat the [tekko-kagi](ref/eq-weapon/tekko-kagi) as a martial weapon instead of an
exotic weapon. You also treat the tekko-kagi as if it were a set of
[claw blades](ref/eq-weapon/claw-blades) when determining how it interacts with other effects.
