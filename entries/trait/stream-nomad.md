**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Darklands

**Flavor:** Digging tunnels near a body of rushing water has taught you how to use water as a means
of travel and escape. Others whisper that you must have been born with webbed feet.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks, and Swim is always a class
skill for you. You increase the speed of any
[raft, barge, keelboat, or rowboat](ref/rule/water-vehicles) you pilot by 4 miles per day (1/2 mile
per hour).
