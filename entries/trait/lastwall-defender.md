**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your military service in Lastwall taught you how to identify undead creatures, changing
them in your mind from mysterious terrors to concrete foes you know how to deal with.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack and damage rolls against undead creatures that you
have personally identified using the [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) skill.
