**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You studied at Anuli's center for druidic magic long enough to begin bonding with a
familiar.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks. You may
substitute this trait for [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will) as the prerequisite for the
[Familiar Bond](ref/feat/familiar-bond) feat.
