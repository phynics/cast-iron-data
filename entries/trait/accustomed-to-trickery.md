**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Darkmoon Wood

**Flavor:** You have been exposed to fey magic and know tricks to avoid it.

**Benefit:** Once per day, when you fail a saving throw against a fey's spell-like or supernatural
ability, you may reroll the save. You must take the second result, even if it is worse.
