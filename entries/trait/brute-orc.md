**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [Orc](ref/race/orc)

**Flavor:** You are an especially large and hulking individual, and when you strike, you aim to
deliver the maximum amount of pain.

**Benefit:** Whenever you score a critical threat, you add (but not multiply, in the case of a
confirmed critical hit) an amount of damage equal to your weapon's critical modifier.
