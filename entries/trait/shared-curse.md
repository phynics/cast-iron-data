**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Werewolf-kin

**Flavor:** A portion of your ancestor's fell curse still courses through your blood.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **standard action**, you can share a portion of your accursed
heritage with a willing ally by dealing 1 point of damage to that ally with a natural weapon. The
ally gains a natural bite attack, as if from your skinwalker ability, for 2 rounds.
