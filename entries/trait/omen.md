**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** You are the harbinger of some future event. Whether this event bodes good or ill, you
exude an ominous presence.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks, and Intimidate
is always a class skill for you. Once per day, you may attempt to demoralize an opponent as a
**swift action**.
