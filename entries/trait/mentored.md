**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** A tutor or private instructor guided you in learning your art, profession, or trade, and
through your education, you became capable of teaching and guiding others.

**Benefit:** Choose a single [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any), [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any), or
[Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) skill. You gain a +1 trait bonus on checks with that skill.
You also gain a +1 trait bonus when you [aid another's](ref/rule/aid-another) skill check with any
skill.
