**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Werewolf-kin

**Flavor:** When your lycanthropic heritage awakened, you thrilled at any excuse for violence.
Although you might have learned to focus or suppress this urge, it still gives you energy in a
fight.

**Benefit:** Once per day, when you deal damage to a foe with a natural attack, you may gain 1d6
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) as an **immediate action**. These temporary
hit points last for 1 minute.
