**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Five Kings Mountains

**Flavor:** Your time in the Five Kings Mountains has taught you that any obstacle can be endured
and overcome.

**Benefit:** When you have more than one Will saving throw to overcome an effect (such as from
[greater command](ref/spell/command-greater) or [hold person](ref/spell/hold-person), or by using
the _good fortune_ ability of the [Luck](ref/domain/luck) domain), you receive a +3 trait bonus on
the extra Will saves against that effect.
