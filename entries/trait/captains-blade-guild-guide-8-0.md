**Prerequisite:** Member of the Liberty's Edge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You were born aboard a ship and learned to fight beside the sailing men and women of Andoran's Gray
Corsairs. While on board any vessel afloat on water, you gain a +1 trait bonus on Acrobatics and
Climb checks. One of these skills (your choice) is a class skill for you.
