**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Raised in the company of skilled healers, you were always encouraged to devote your time
and energy to the welfare of others.

**Benefit:** Whenever you take 20 on a [Heal](ref/skill/heal) check to treat deadly wounds, you
restore an additional 1d4 hit points to those you aid.

**Suggested Characters:** Battle clerics, [Iomedaeans](ref/faith/iomedae), Kellids.
