**Region:** River Kingdoms

**Flavor:** Since you were young, you've been a member of one River Kingdoms bandit gang or another.

[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), or [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth). You gain a +1 trait bonus on
that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
