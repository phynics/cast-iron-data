**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Milani](ref/faith/milani)

**Benefit:** When you are the target of an attack by an opponent that is
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking) you, as an **immediate action** once per day you can foil that
opponent's attack. For that attack, the opponent does not gain any of the bonuses or effects that
are associated with flanking.
