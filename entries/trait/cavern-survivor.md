**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Nar-Voth

**Flavor:** You've regularly traversed Nar-Voth and know how to subsist in that lightless realm
better than most surface dwellers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to survive in a
subterranean wilderness, and Survival is a class skill for you.
