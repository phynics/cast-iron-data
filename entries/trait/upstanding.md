**Prerequisite:** Member of the Exchange faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your preferred means of doing business involves giving your clients a fair deal and winning their
continued business and good will. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Diplomacy and Sense Motive checks,
and one of these skills (your choice) is a class skill for you.
