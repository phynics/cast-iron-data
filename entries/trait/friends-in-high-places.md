**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have often seen lawbreakers go free when they demonstrate they have friends in high
places, and so you put special effort into befriending powerful people yourself.

**Benefit:** Whenever you are in an area in which you have used the [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy)
skill to gather information, you gain a +1 trait bonus on Diplomacy and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. This bonus increases to +2 when dealing with government
officials.

**Suggested Characters:** Absalom natives, [Aspis Consortium](ref/rule/aff-profiteers) agents,
Taldans.
