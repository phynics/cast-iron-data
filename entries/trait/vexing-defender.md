**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Giantslayer

**Flavor:** You're trained in fighting opponents that are larger than you, and you are skilled at
keeping them on their toes, pestering them from all directions.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks, and Acrobatics
becomes a class skill for you. In addition, you gain a +4 trait bonus on Acrobatics checks to move
through an enemy's space without provoking an attack of opportunity, provided that enemy is larger
than you.
