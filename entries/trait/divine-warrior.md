**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae)

**Flavor:** From an early age, you were trained by a militaristic order of clerics. You are devoted
to the teachings of Iomedae and to spreading those teachings by force.

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a divine spell that affects any melee weapons, choose one of those
weapons; it gains a +1 trait bonus on damage rolls for the duration of the spell.
