**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Bekyar or Shoanti

**Flavor:** The spirit voices of your ancestors sing to you of the world around you.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can act in the surprise round even if you aren't aware of your
attackers; you can use this action only to start a bardic song or
[raging song](ref/ability/raging-song), and you're still considered
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) until you act in the first normal round of combat.
