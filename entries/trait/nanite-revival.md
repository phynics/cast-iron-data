**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Android](ref/race/android)

**Flavor:** You can instinctively activate your nanites to save your life.

**Benefit:** Once per day as an **immediate action**, you can command your nanites to stabilize you
while you are [dying](ref/misc/dying), even while unconscious or helpless. This does not count as a
use of your _nanite surge_ ability.
