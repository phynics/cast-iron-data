**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Chaldira](ref/faith/chaldira-zuzaristan)

**Flavor:** You consider mischief a sacred rite in humbling enemies.

**Benefit:** You do not provoke attacks of opportunity when performing a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver while your _smite evil_ bonus applies to combat
maneuver checks. If you already do not provoke such attacks of opportunity (such as by having
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick)), you instead gain a +2 trait bonus on dirty
trick combat maneuver checks against creatures that are the targets of your _smite._
