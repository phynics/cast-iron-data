**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc)

**Flavor:** You have worked for a crime lord, either as a low-level enforcer or as a guard, and are
adept at frightening away people.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks, and Intimidate
is always a class skill for you.
