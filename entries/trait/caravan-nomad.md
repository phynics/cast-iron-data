**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Kitsune](ref/race/kitsune)

**Region:** Varisia

**Flavor:** Your family has lived on the open roads of Varisia for untold generations, impersonating
nomadic Varisians.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) checks, and one of these skills is a class skill for you.
