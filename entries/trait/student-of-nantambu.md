**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Mwangi

**Flavor:** You have spent time at [Magaambya](ref/rule/aff-i-school), the magical academy of
Nantambu, absorbing the ancient knowledge taught there.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) and
[Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks, and you may make these checks untrained.
