**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Keleshite

**Flavor:** One of your ancestors was genie-kind.

**Benefit:** Select an element (air, earth, fire, or water). You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving
throws against attacks that utilize that element (cold for air, acid for earth, fire for fire, and
electricity for water),&dagger& and gain a +1 trait bonus on Bluff, Diplomacy, Intimidate, and Sense
Motive checks made against creatures of that subtype.

> &dagger& This is different from the usual associations, where cold is water and electricity is
> air. Consult your GM if you want to use the normal associations.
