**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Somewhere in your family line, the mystic power of red dragons was infused into one of
your relatives.

**Benefit:** If you ever take a class that requires you to select a draconic heritage, you must
select a red dragon as your type. After successfully completing a [charge](ref/rule/charge) attack,
you gain a +2 trait bonus on Will saves for a full round.
