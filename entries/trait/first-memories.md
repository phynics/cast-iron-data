**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome) with the _gnome magic_ racial trait

**Flavor:** For your whole life, you've felt there was a world just beyond the fabric of the
material objects around you, and sometimes you can hear the north wind calling you or smell the
dread taint of polluted water before you taste it.

**Benefit:** You do not gain the normal list of [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
for the _gnome magic_ racial trait. Instead, if you have a Wisdom of 11 of higher, you gain the
following spell-like abilities: 1/day-[detect poison](ref/spell/detect-poison),
[know direction](ref/spell/know-direction), [stabilize](ref/spell/stabilize), and
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals). The caster level for these effects is equal to
your character level. The DC for these spells is equal to 10 + the spell's level + your Wisdom
modifier.
