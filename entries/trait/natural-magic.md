**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Yuelral](ref/faith/yuelral)

**Flavor:** Yuelral's teachings revealed to you that all magic can ultimately be traced back to
nature.

**Benefit:** Choose two 0-level druid spells. You treat each spell as being on your class spell list
and as a spell known for all spellcasting classes you have.
