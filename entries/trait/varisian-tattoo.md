**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Varisian

**Flavor:** You bear the elaborate tattoos of your people, marking you as a free son or daughter of
the road.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against charm and compulsion effects.
Additionally, you are proficient with [bladed scarves](ref/eq-weapon/bladed-scarf) and
[starknives](ref/eq-weapon/starknife).
