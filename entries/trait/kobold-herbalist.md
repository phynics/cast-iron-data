**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Green-scaled

**Flavor:** You are familiar with the flora of your native forest and have rudimentary skill as an
herbalist.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can attempt a DC 20 [Survival](ref/skill/survival) check instead of a
[Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (alchemy) check to create a single dose of
[scent cloak](ref/eq-misc/scent-cloak) without paying any cost for raw materials. Any scent cloak
created using this method must be used within 24 hours, or it becomes inert.
