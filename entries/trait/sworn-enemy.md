**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You were raised with defiance, fear, or hatred of a particular sort of foe. To make you
ready to resist such foes, you were trained for long hours in the proper ways to defeat them.

**Benefit:** Choose a foe from the ranger's list of [favored enemies](ref/ability/favored-enemy).
You gain a +1 trait bonus on attacks of opportunity against the chosen foe.

**Suggested Characters:** Dwarves, Isgeri, Mendevian crusaders.
