**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Your connection with the river and its tributaries has blended with your reverence for
the gods of the river passages so much so that you seem divinely blessed while plying river waters.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks in rivers or streams, and a
+2 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to forage for food in or near a river or
stream.
