**Type:** Regional

**Region:** tundra or other cold regions

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in the cold wastes of the far north or far south, and are
accustomed to low temperatures.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on saving throws made to resist the effects of being in
[cold conditions](ref/rule/cold-dangers) and a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against cold effects.
