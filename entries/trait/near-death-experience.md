**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Belkzen

**Flavor:** You had a close brush with death that you survived only thanks to one of the witch
doctors of Belkzen, and your body has learned to cling to life in dire situations.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Constitution checks to [stabilize](ref/misc/stable), and
you don't die until your hit points drop to a negative amount equal to 2 + your Constitution score.
