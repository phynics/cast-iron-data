**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 17 Calistril and 11 Pharast

**Flavor:** Your sign is of two lovers, intertwined as one.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to influence those who
might be romantically attracted to you, and Diplomacy becomes a class skill for you.
