**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You are far too easily convinced of the friendly intentions of others.

**Benefit:** You take a -2 penalty on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks and on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to see through disguises, and on saving throws against
charm spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).
