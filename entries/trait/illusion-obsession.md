**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** After realizing the great potential of illusions - particularly figments - for use in
pranks, you became obsessed with studying their properties and better methods to cast them.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to your caster level when casting illusions of the figment
subschool.
