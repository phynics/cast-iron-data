**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have a passion for history and news concerning monastic disciplines.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on checks with your choice of either
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) or
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history), and the one you choose is always a class skill
for you.

In addition, you gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to gather
information about any person with levels in monk.
