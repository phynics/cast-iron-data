**Type:** Race

**Flavor:** Long ago, your ancestors' blood mixed with that of dragons.

**Benefit:** Choose one of the following: gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks, gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), or
gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against effects that cause sleep or paralysis.
