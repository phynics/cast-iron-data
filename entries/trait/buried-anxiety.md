**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Spitespawn

**Benefit:** You laugh at what makes others tremble, but are secretly disturbed by a particular sort
of mundane item. Pick a specific sort of object, color, sound, or similar relatively common
phenomena. You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws made to resist fear effects except when you
can see, hear, smell, or taste that which unnerves you.
