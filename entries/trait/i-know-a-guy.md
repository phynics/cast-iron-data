**Prerequisite:** Member of the Sczarni faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

The Sczarni are often related to each other, and no matter how distant the kinship, you know that
you can rely upon your extended family for information. You gain a +2 trait bonus on Diplomacy
checks made to gather information and a +1 trait bonus on Knowledge (local) checks.
