**Prerequisite:** [Changeling](ref/race/changeling)

**Flavor:** Your strange bearing unsettles others.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and Intimidate
is a class skill for you. You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when
interacting with [hags](ref/family/hag).
