**Type:** Regional

**Region:** settlement near a famous dungeon

**Benefit:** As a youth, you marveled at the crusaders, spelunkers, and lore seekers who came
through your town on their way to the nearby dungeon, tomb, or ruins. One such intrepid role model
in particular was of great import to you. On the last day you ever saw your hero, the adventurer
gave you a unique dungeon guide worth no more than 750 gp and with an accuracy modifier of no
greater than +2.

**Suggestion:** With the help of your GM and using the example dungeon guides, construct a unique
dungeon guide tied to the dungeon near where you grew up.
