**Prerequisite:** Member of the Taldor faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

As a youth, you were involved with the avant garde theater scene of Oppara. Your performance skills
have been known to bring even the toughest crowd to their feet. Choose one type of Perform skill.
You gain a +1 trait bonus on Perform checks of the chosen type and this skill becomes a class skill
for you.
