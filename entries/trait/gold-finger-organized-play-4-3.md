**Prerequisite:** Member of the Qadira faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your family comes from a long, proud tradition of housebreaking and thievery. You are a strong part
of that tradition. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Disable Device and Sleight of Hand checks, and one
of these skills (your choice) becomes a class skill for you.
