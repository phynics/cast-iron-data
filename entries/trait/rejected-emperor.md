**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** The Eternal Emperor

**Flavor:** When you were young, your parents presented you to the oracles of Po Li, claiming a
divine sign showed you were the reincarnation of Lung Wa's Eternal Emperor. The oracles did not
support the claim, but you are not untouched by celestial influence.

**Benefit:** You may use [Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) or
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) in place of
[Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) to identify outsiders, and nonevil outsiders you
encounter begin with an [attitude](ref/skill/diplomacy) that is one step better than normal.
