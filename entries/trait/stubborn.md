**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** Vampire; Vetala

**Flavor:** The angry child inside bolsters your will, and you often prove utterly defiant.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against attempts to control you, such as
[command undead](ref/spell/command-undead), [halt undead](ref/spell/halt-undead), or
[channeled energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) to turn or control undead.
