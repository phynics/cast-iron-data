**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Serpent's Skull

**Flavor:** You boarded the Jenivere in the Mwangi Expanse, where you've lived or recently traveled
through. You harbor no illusions regarding the deadliness of the jungles, the creatures that inhabit
it, or the peoples that make their home therein.

**Benefit:** You gain Polyglot as a bonus language and receive a +1 trait bonus on all
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) checks regarding the jungle.
