**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Nethys](ref/faith/nethys)

**Flavor:** You have a need to bring old knowledge into the light, no matter what language it's
written in.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks, and
Linguistics is a class skill for you.
