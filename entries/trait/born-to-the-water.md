**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Ijo

**Flavor:** You were born on the shore, and you easily live on and under the water.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks and only need to make Swim checks
once every 2 hours to avoid fatigue, instead of once each hour.
