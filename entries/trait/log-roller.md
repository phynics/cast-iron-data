**Type:** Regional

**Region:** forest

**Flavor:** The time you spent leaping between slippery logs as they whirled down the river to
market taught you how to keep your footing.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks and a +1 trait
bonus to your CMB when attempting to resist [trip](ref/rule/trip) attacks.
