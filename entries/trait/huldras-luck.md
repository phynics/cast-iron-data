**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Lands of the Linnorm Kings

**Flavor:** At some point in your life, you were touched by a fey creature called a
[huldra](ref/monster/huldra) and inherited a bit of its luck.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you may choose to gain a +1 luck bonus on a single saving throw, attack
roll, or skill check.
