**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** You recall the dangers the dwarves faced during the Quest for Sky.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering) checks and a +2 trait bonus on
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks pertaining to the Quest for Sky. Select
one of these skills to be a class skill for you.
