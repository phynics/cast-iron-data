**Type:** Social

**Benefit:** Long exposure to the endless variety of the [Nightstalls](ref/rule/nightstalls) grants
you a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to gather information and on
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks to recall information about criminal
activities. One of these (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
