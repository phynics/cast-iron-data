**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Blessed with good looks, you've come to depend on the fact that others find you
attractive.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus when you use [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) or
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) on a character that is (or could be) sexually attracted to you, and
a +1 trait bonus to the save DC of any language-dependent spell you cast on such characters or
creatures.
