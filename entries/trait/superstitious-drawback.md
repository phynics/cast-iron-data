**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** Raised around those who regarded most magic as innately dangerous, no matter the source,
you cannot bring yourself to submit to magic willingly even for your own good.

**Benefit:** Whenever you are affected with a harmless spell, there is a 50% chance that you must
roll a saving throw to prevent that effect, even if it would benefit you. This does not apply to
harmless spells cast by you upon yourself.
