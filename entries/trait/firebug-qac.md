**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You were the child of a gunsmith or alchemist and always enjoyed experimenting with
fiery alchemical items and minor explosives.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls made with
[thrown splash weapons](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) and [alchemist](ref/class/alchemist) bombs.

**Suggested Characters:** Alkenstar natives, Katapeshi, Thuvians.
