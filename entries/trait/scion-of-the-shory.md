**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mwangi Expanse

**Flavor:** The blood of a Shory aeromancer flows through your veins.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks when casting
flight spells such as [levitate](ref/spell/levitate), [fly](ref/spell/fly), or
[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight). Once per day, you can use [message](ref/spell/message)
as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).
