**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mwangi Expanse

**Flavor:** You're a child of the wild, living on your own without need of others.

**Benefit:** Each time you attempt a [Survival](ref/skill/survival) check to get along in the wild
or resist severe weather, you can choose to gain a +4 trait bonus on that check. If you do so, you
can't use that check to provide assistance to others, even if your result exceeds the DC.
