**Prerequisite:** Member of the Shadow Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

As a member of the Shadow Lodge, you are trained to detect lies and doublespeak. You gain a +1 trait
bonus on Sense Motive checks, and Sense Motive becomes a class skill for you.
