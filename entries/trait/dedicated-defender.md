**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** When a companion is down, you fight harder.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls and on checks made to overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) while you are adjacent to a [dying](ref/misc/dying) or
[disabled](ref/misc/disabled) ally, animal companion, eidolon, familiar, or mount.
