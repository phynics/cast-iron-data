**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Growing up amid a large number of children, you learned quickly how to get others'
attention and keep it.

**Benefit:** When you modify a character's attitude with the [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) or
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) skill, the attitude change lasts 1-1/2 times longer than it
otherwise would. Whenever you create a fear or mind-affecting effect that imposes a penalty or bonus
with a duration of at least 2 rounds, it lasts 1 additional round after it would normally end. For
example, if you demoralize a foe with an Intimidate check and beat the DC by 5, the victim is
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 3 rounds instead of the usual 2 rounds.

**Suggested Characters:** Free Captains, gnomes, Keleshites.
