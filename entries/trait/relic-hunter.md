**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Garundi

**Flavor:** You have made a study of the ancient lands and ruins of Garund and are familiar with the
relics and artifacts their inhabitants left behind.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) and
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks made to examine ruined buildings more than
1,000 years old as well as any ruined objects found in them. If such an item is magical, you gain a
+1 trait bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks to identify its properties and
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) checks to activate it.
