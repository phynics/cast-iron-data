**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Realm of the Mammoth Lords

**Flavor:** The rocky foothills of your homeland are ideal for staging leaping ambushes that leave
your victims battered and routed.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on melee damage rolls when you make a
[charge](ref/rule/charge) attack from higher ground. Once per day, when you make a charge attack
from higher ground, you can use the [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) skill to demoralize your
target as a **swift action**.
