**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Region:** Kyonin

**Flavor:** You helped maintain the [elf gates](ref/magic-artifact/aiudara) and are familiar with
the operation of teleportation magic.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks to identify
teleportation magic and a +1 trait bonus to caster level checks when casting teleportation spells.
You know the proper commands to operate a particular elf gate (most likely an often-used elf gate,
but at the GM's discretion it may be a less popular or out-of-the-way elf gate).
