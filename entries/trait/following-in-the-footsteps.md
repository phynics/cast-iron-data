**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Ruins of Azlant

**Flavor:** One of your dear friends or family members was part of the first wave of colonists to be
selected by the Bountiful Venture Company to establish Talmandor's Bounty. You applied for the first
wave as well, but weren't selected. Now that you've made your way into the second wave, you can't
wait to be reunited with your family member. Your devotion to your family or friends is important to
you, and it shows not only in how you feel about that friend or family member you are soon to
reunite with, but also in how you treat other people with respect.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action to aid an adjacent
ally, increase the bonus you grant your ally by 1. In addition, due to your positive and helpful
nature, you gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks.
