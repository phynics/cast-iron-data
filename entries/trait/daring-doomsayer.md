**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Second Darkness

**Flavor:** The end times are coming, and the shadow in the sky over Riddleport is just the first
piece of proof. Convinced that the end of the world is nigh, you've decided to take part in the
Golden Goblin's gambling tournament - after all, what do you have to lose?

**Benefit:** Whenever you roll a 1 on an attack roll, you gain a +1 trait bonus on your next attack
roll so long as it is made within 1 minute. You also begin play with a placard bearing an
apocalyptic slogan of your choice; this wooden sign can be used as a [club](ref/eq-weapon/club).
