**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Garundi

**Flavor:** Your parents were scholars of history, whether genealogists of your own family tree,
sages on the subject of ancient empires, or simply hobbyists with a deep and abiding love for the
past.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks
and _bardic knowledge_ checks, and Knowledge (history) is always a class skill for you.
