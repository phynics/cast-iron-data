**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Often forced to go without food or stimulation for days at a time, you have learned to
use prolonged sleep as a means of escape and a way to heal your wounds.

**Benefit:** You heal temporary ability damage at a rate of 3 points per day of complete
[bed rest](ref/rule/healing). A normal night of rest (8 hours) still heals you at a rate of only 1
point of temporary ability damage per night of rest.
