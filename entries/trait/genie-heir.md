**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** Geniekin

**Flavor:** You once met one of your genie ancestors, who taught you the ways of her race and their
allies.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Charisma checks and Charisma-based skill checks to
influence outsiders from the home plane of your genie ancestor.
