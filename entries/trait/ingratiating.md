**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Chelaxian

**Flavor:** Someone close to you was removed from her position and replaced after losing the
government's favor, and you had to quickly prove your loyalty to her replacement or face your own
disgrace.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when interacting with people in positions of authority over
you.
