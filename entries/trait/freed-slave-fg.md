**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling); Member of The
[Bellflower Network](ref/rule/aff-antislavery)

**Flavor:** You grew up as a slave in Cheliax and know the ins and outs of Chelish nobility better
than most.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) checks and Knowledge (nobility) is a class
skill for you.
