**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** You've dared uncharted tunnels in pursuit of the gladdringgar (the chiseling of
name-runes in subterranean depths).

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks, and a +1 trait
bonus on saving throws against fear effects.
