**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Anuli

**Flavor:** Your family helped to rebuild the ruined city of Anuli, and that tradition runs in your
blood.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to notice unusual
stonework, traps, and hidden doors in worked stone and artificial constructions.
[Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering) is always a class skill for you.
