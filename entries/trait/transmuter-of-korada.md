**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You learned the secrets of transmutation from a follower of the empyreal lord
[Korada](ref/faith/korada).

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a spell from the transmutation school, its effects manifest at +1
caster level.

Additionally, select one of the following transmutation spells:
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), [eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor),
[fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning), or [owl's wisdom](ref/spell/owls-wisdom). Once per day when
you cast that spell, its duration is doubled. A spell affected by this trait cannot be modified
further by the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) metamagic feat or similar abilities.
