**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Sarkoris or The Worldwound

**Flavor:** Having grown up near the Worldwound, you have a rough understanding of the demons'
guttural babble.

**Benefit:** You add Abyssal to your list of languages known, though you take a -2 penalty on
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), and [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff)
checks made while speaking Abyssal, as well as a -2 penalty on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics)
checks made to read Abyssal text.

If you gain Abyssal as a language again at a later time (such as by taking a rank in Linguistics and
selecting Abyssal as a learned language), these skill check penalties become +2 trait bonuses
instead.
