**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Valashmai Jungle

**Flavor:** You have traveled to the Valashmai Jungle on numerous occasions, and your prowess in
traversing the jungle wilderness makes you a formidable guide and explorer.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks, and a +1 trait
bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks in jungle terrain. One of these skills becomes a
class skill for you.
