**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar)

**Flavor:** Your religious training involved serving in the city watch of a large city, the primary
duty of which was standing sentinel on a city wall.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks, and Perception
is always a class skill for you.
