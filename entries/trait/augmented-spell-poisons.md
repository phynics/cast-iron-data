**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Vishkanya](ref/race/vishkanya)

**Flavor:** You can use your natural poison to augment the spells you cast.

**Benefit:** You can spend a use of your _toxic_ racial trait and use a bit of your toxic saliva or
blood as an additional material component for a spell with the poison descriptor to increase the
spell's save DC by 1.
