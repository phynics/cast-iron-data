**Type:** Cosmic

**Prerequisite:** Born between 19 Gozran and 13 Desnus

**Flavor:** You are filled with the light-hearted joy of the Daughter, who dances her way into
springtime.

**Benefit:** Every creature within 10 feet of you adds +2 moral bonuses to the DCs of checks to
[demoralize](ref/skill/intimidate) them.
