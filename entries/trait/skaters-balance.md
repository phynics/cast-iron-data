**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); White-scaled

**Flavor:** You grew up stalking across the frozen pools of water that dot your tribe's Darklands
home.

**Benefit:** You don't need to spend extra movement to enter a square of an
[ice sheet](ref/rule/cold-dangers), and the DCs of your [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks on
ice sheets don't increase by 5.
