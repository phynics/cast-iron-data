**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** War for the Crown

**Flavor:** All too often, great minds must suffer for their art, but you're one of the lucky ones.
Your skills attracted the attention of a noble patron who pays for your food, housing, and tools. As
your patron introduces you to other cultural elites, you're slowly learning to navigate the
treacherous waters of high society, but you still remember the lean times when each meal was a
hard-won treasure.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks, as well as checks using a single [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any)
or [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) skill of your choice; one of these skills is always a class
skill for you.

Once each day, you can invoke your patron's reputation or name one of your own prominent works to
gain a +2 circumstance bonus on a single [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy),
or [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check.

You begin the game with a [masterwork musical instrument](ref/eq-misc/musical-instrument-masterwork)
or [tool](ref/eq-misc/masterwork-tool) appropriate to the Craft or Perform skill you selected, a
gift from your patron.
