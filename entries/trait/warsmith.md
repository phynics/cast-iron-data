**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)

**Flavor:** You have an intuitive grasp of the weak points of metal and stone.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to damage rolls against creatures and objects made primarily
of clay, crystal, earth, metal, or stone. [Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering)
is a class skill for you.
