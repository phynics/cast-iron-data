**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Your study of monsters keeps you well informed.

**Benefit:** Choose either [Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana),
[Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering), or
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature). Whenever you succeed at a Knowledge check to
identify a creature and its special powers - or vulnerabilities - with the chosen skill, you gain
one additional piece of useful information (as if your skill check result had been 5 higher).
