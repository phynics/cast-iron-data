**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Andoletta](ref/faith/andoletta)

**Flavor:** You were once faced with a grave injustice committed by an ignorant or delusional
person, and righteous indignation moved you to correct the mistaken party.

**Benefit:** Once per day, when you confirm a critical hit against, successfully use a _smite_
attack on, or use [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) on a creature that is affected by a charm
or compulsion effect, you can invoke Andoletta to open the target's eyes. If the effect originally
allowed a saving throw to resist it, the target can attempt a new saving throw against the charm or
compulsion; if she's successful, the effect ends immediately.
