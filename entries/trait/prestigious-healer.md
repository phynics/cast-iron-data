**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Cheliax

**Benefit:** Once per day as an **immediate action** when you cast a spell of the healing subschool,
you can attempt a [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check to improve the attitude of your target or
anyone within 30 feet who can see you before the end of your next turn. You gain a +2 trait bonus on
this check.
