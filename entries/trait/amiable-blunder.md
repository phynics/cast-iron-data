**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Your easy-going demeanor allows you to sometimes recover from potentially awkward social
situations or even condemnatory faux pas.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you fail a [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check by 5 or more and
would cause an NPC's attitude toward you to worsen, you can immediately attempt another Diplomacy
check against the same DC as a **free action**. If you succeed at this second Diplomacy check, the
character's attitude doesn't change, as though you had failed the original check by 4 or less.
