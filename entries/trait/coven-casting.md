**Type:** Magic

**Prerequisite:** Belong to a coven or magical cabal

**Flavor:** You have belonged to a coven or magical cabal and know how to coordinate your
spellcasting with others.

**Benefit:** When within 30 feet of an allied spellcaster, you both gain a +2 trait bonus on
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks.
