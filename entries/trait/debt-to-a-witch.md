**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Irrisen

**Flavor:** Your life was spared in youth by the magic of a White Witch healer who was using your
survival to perpetuate her agenda. Her magic was painful, but it made your body stronger.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against spells with the cold descriptor and
against the dangers of [cold weather](ref/rule/cold-dangers).
