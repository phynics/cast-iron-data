**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** Great Old Ones

**Flavor:** Whenever you are affected by a transmutation spell of the polymorph subschool, your new
form is always in some way horrific, grotesque, or otherwise marked by the corruption of the Great
Old Ones.

**Benefit:** While polymorphed, you gain a +2 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)
checks and increase the DC of spells you cast with the fear descriptor by 1. However, you also take
a -2 penalty on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks while polymorphed.
