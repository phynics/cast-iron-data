**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Five Kings Mountains

**Flavor:** You are skilled at getting along in the mountains, enabling you to find hidden trails
and secret entrances into the caverns beneath mountains.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks when in mountainous areas, and Knowledge (geography) is always
a class skill for you.
