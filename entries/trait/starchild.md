**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Desna](ref/faith/desna)

**Flavor:** Desna sensed your love of travel and promised you would always be able to find your way
home.

**Benefit:** You can automatically determine where true north is. You gain a +4 trait bonus on
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to avoid becoming lost.
