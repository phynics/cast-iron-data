**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You have been trained to hunt and fight on the open plains since birth.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attacks of opportunity attack rolls made with a
[shortspear](ref/eq-weapon/shortspear), as well as a +1 racial bonus on Constitution checks to
continue [running](ref/rule/run) and avoid nonlethal damage from a
[forced march](ref/rule/overland-movement).
