**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mendev

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) and
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks. In addition, you gain a +2 trait bonus
on both [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) and [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks while attempting to
impersonate a knight.
