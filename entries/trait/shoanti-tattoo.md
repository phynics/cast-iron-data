**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Shoanti

**Flavor:** Upon reaching adulthood, you received a tribal tattoo, marking you with the symbols of
your quah and totem spirits.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against fear effects. Additionally, you are
proficient with [earth breakers](ref/eq-weapon/earth-breaker), [klars](ref/eq-armor/klar), and
[Shoanti bolas](ref/eq-weapon/shoanti-bolas).
