**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar)

**Flavor:** You have a strong belief in the righteousness of law and justice.

**Benefit:** You receive a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against charm and compulsion effects, and
may attempt a new saving throw with an additional +1 bonus (for a total of +2) if you are directed
to break the law.
