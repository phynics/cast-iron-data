**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Ustalav

**Flavor:** Leaving Graidmere Swamp is forbidden, and to flee the mire is to court the black dragon
Seryzilian's wrath. However, you are one of the few to successfully make it out of the swamp alive.
Now, you're resolved to liberate your kinfolk.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to find fresh water, and
magical liquids in your possession gain a +2 bonus on Will saves to avoid being corrupted or
otherwise spoiled.
