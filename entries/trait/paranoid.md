**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You believe that someone or something is always out to get you, so you have a hard time
truly trusting anyone.

**Benefit:** Anyone who attempts an [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action of any type to
assist you must succeed at a DC 15 check instead of the normal DC 10 check.
