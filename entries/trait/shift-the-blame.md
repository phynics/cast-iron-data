**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Spitespawn

**Flavor:** You have learned many tricks to keep yourself from facing the consequences of your
misdeeds.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks made to convince someone that another person is actually at
fault for your actions.
