**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You are a magically adept merchant who has learned tricks to protect your own goods, or
you serve a city as an impartial auditor who guards against arcane trickery in the bazaars.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks and Will saves
to identify an item's true properties, such as against spells like
[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura).
