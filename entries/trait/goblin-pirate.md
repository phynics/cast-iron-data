**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** Although you might prefer life on board a ship, you often end up in the water for some
odd reason.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) and
[Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks, and Swim is always a class skill for you. You might also have a peg
leg or a hook for a hand, but that's not so helpful.
