**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Aroden](ref/faith/aroden)

**Flavor:** You worship a dead god, and are skilled at making that sound reasonable.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks; this bonus
increases to +3 if you are dealing with a follower of Aroden or [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae).
