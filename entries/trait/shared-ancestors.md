**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Myr](ref/faith/myr)

**Flavor:** Through your detailed study of your own lineage, you can better understand and honor
even your most distant relatives.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy),
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history), [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local),
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive)
checks associated with any creature who shares your type and at least one of your subtypes. One of
these skills becomes a class skill.
