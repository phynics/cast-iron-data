**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Nidal

**Flavor:** Numerous expeditions to accursed regions like Nidal's Finder's Gulch have reinforced
your already steadfast faith.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you may reroll a Will saving throw made against an attack or effect that
originates from an undead creature. You must decide to use this trait after the first saving throw
is rolled, but before the results are revealed. You must take the result of the second roll, even if
it is lower.
