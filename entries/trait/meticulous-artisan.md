**Prerequisite:** Member of the Lantern Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

A well-carved statuette, the smooth stitching on a fine coat, the keen edge on a blade—you know how
to craft masterpieces. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Day Job checks using the Craft skill.
