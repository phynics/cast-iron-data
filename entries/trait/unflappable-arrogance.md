**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Ifrit](ref/race/ifrit)

**Flavor:** You laugh in the face of your aggressors.

**Benefit:** The DC to demoralize you with the [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) skill increases
by 5. If an opponent's Intimidate check would be high enough to cause you to become demoralized for
more than 1 round, you are instead demoralized for only 1 round.
