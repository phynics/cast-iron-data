**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Minata

**Flavor:** The hidden coves and bays of the island of Belem have been your home throughout most of
your career as a pirate, and the winding waterways of Minata have trained you well in the art of
sailing.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography)
checks and [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks, and one of these skills becomes a
class skill for you.
