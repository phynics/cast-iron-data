**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** For everything there is a price, and you are a negotiator at heart.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy),
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks made while
negotiating payment for a quest or a service that you provide.
