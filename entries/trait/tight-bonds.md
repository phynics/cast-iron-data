**Type:** Family

**Flavor:** Whether siblings, spouses, or dedicated members of the same clan, characters who take
this trait are an inseparable pair committed to each other.

**Benefit:** Your partner or partners must also have this trait. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Will
saves against charm and compulsion effects if a partner is within 60 feet, increasing to +3 against
a command to compromise your relationship or harm a partner.
