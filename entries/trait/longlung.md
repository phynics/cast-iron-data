**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Monkey goblin](ref/race/monkey-goblin)

**Flavor:** You have spent long hours scouring shipwrecks and shoreline tide pools during low tide
for shiny treasure.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks while underwater
and can [hold your breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for a number of rounds equal to three times
your Constitution score before you risk drowning.
