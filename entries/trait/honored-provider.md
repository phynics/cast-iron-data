**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Ghoran](ref/race/ghoran)

**Flavor:** Nex depends upon the food produced by ghorans, and you inspire gratitude in creatures
you feed.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to interact with
a person to whom you have given needed food in the past 24 hours.
