**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Plane of Air

**Flavor:** Your years of piloting airships on the Plane of Air, dodging angry mephits and
protecting your passengers from the depredations of capricious elementals, have taught you how to
expertly command your vessel, as well as how to arrest yourself even in the midst of the most
turbulent planar storms.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Wisdom checks to move in a plane with subjective
directional gravity (see the [planar rules](ref/rule/planar-traits)) and a +2 trait bonus on driving
checks (see the [vehicle rules](ref/rule/full-vehicle-rules)).
