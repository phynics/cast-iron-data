**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Faultspawn

**Flavor:** You have an instinctive flair for causing such pain to the faithful that they
temporarily lose their connection with their god.

**Benefit:** If a divine spellcaster makes a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check to avoid
losing a spell because of an injury you caused, increase the DC of that check by +2.
