**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Numeria

**Flavor:** Exposure to the corruption that seeps through every drop of water and grain of dirt in
Numeria has altered your body.

**Benefit:** Horrific growths beneath your skin provide you a +1 natural armor bonus to AC, but your
body does not work as a normal creature's would. You become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1
round anytime you receive magical healing.
