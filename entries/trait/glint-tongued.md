**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Ulfen

**Flavor:** You have a gift for poetic turns of phrase and vicious streams of insults.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Charisma-based skill checks made to impress or persuade
other Ulfen (or, at the GM's option, other poetically-minded people), and a +2 trait bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks.
