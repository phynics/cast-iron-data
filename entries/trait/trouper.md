**Prerequisite:** Member of the Sczarni faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You cling to the Varisian traveling performer traditions and can recognize the magic hidden in song
and dance. You gain a +1 trait bonus on checks using a Perform skill of your choice and a +1 trait
bonus on saving throws against abilities that rely upon the Perform skill to function, such as the
bardic performance class ability.
