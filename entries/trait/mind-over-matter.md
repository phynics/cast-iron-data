**Prerequisite:** Member of the Lantern Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Whether from the pages of a dusty scroll or at the feet of a master, you have learned to set your
mind to adapt to the physical realm. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Will saving throws.
