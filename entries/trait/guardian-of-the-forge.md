**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Torag](ref/faith/torag)

**Flavor:** Torag's sacred duties are to protect the faithful, take lessons from the great craftsmen
and strategists of the past, and prepare against dark times.

**Benefit:** You receive a +1 trait bonus on
[Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering) and
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks. One of these skills (your choice) is a
class skill for you.
