**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Svirfneblin](ref/race/svirfneblin)

**Flavor:** You trained from birth to never reveal secrets and never betray anyone's trust.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against divinations or enchantments that
would compel you to reveal a secret or allow anyone to read your mind.
