**Prerequisite:** Associated with the court of the Black Dome

**Region:** Osirion

**Flavor:** You have gained favor with the government of Sothis.

**Benefit:** Once per week, you can attempt a DC 20 [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) or
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to gain access to an exclusive site or event, such as a
government building or an aristocrat's ball.
