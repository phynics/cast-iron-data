**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Taldor

**Flavor:** You spent your childhood in the infamous Narrows district of Oppara. Years of living in
violent squalor have sharpened your senses and given you an ardent distrust of humanity.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to Initiative and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive)
checks. Sense Motive is always a class skill for you.
