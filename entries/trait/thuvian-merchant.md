**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Thuvia

**Flavor:** You have traveled the great circular trade route around Thuvia, following the sale of
the [sun orchid elixir](ref/magic-artifact/sun-orchid-elixir) from city to city, and are used to
haggling with foreigners.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise), [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks made for business deals and transactions.
