**Region:** Katapesh

**Flavor:** You excel at maximizing mercantile contacts and convincing merchants to dig just a bit
deeper into their pockets for you.

**Benefit:** Whenever you buy from or sell to a merchant, you treat a settlement's
[Base Value and Purchase Limit](ref/rule/the-settlement-stat-block) as 10% higher.
