**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You had a traumatic experience with a spirit at a young age that colors your reactions
to such creatures even to this day.

**Benefit:** Whenever you perceive a fey, outsider, or undead from within 60 feet, you become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d4 rounds unless you succeed at a Will save with a DC equal to 15 +
the creature's Charisma modifier. Immunity to fear effects does not allow you to forgo this saving
throw; even if you are immune to fear, you must roll the Will saving throw for this drawback when
applicable, albeit with a +4 bonus.
