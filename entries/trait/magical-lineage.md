**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** One of your parents was a gifted spellcaster who not only used metamagic often, but also
developed many magical items and perhaps even a new spell or two - and you have inherited a fragment
of this greatness.

**Benefit:** Pick one spell when you choose this trait. When you apply
[metamagic feats](ref/rule/feats-basic) to this spell that add at least 1 level to the spell, treat
its actual level as 1 lower for determining the spell's final adjusted level.
