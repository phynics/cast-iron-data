**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Flavor:** Your head is particularly wide and large, even for a goblin.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks, and Perception
is always a class skill for you. Any [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) checks that requires
you to squeeze your head through a tight space take a -8 penalty.
