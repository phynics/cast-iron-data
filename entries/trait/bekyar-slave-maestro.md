**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Bekyar or Shoanti

**Flavor:** You spent years using your magic to bend the will of captured slaves to suit your whims.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks against anyone you have held captive for at least 24
hours.
