**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** You've spent your entire life thumbing your nose at the establishment and take pride in
your run-ins with the law. Somehow, despite all your mischievous behavior, you've never been
caught - you always manage to stay one step ahead of your pursuers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) checks and a +1
trait bonus on initiative checks.
