**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Andoletta](ref/faith/andoletta)

**Flavor:** You had a close encounter with a servant or worshiper of Andoletta who provided you with
a memorable, if unconventional, consolation after a loss.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against emotion and fear effects. If you are
immune to fear, you instead gain a +4 trait bonus on saves against emotion effects.
