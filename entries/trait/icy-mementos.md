**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); White-scaled

**Flavor:** As a parting gift, the shaman of your tribe gave you an alchemical concoction as a
reminder of your icy home.

**Benefit:** You begin play with three bottles of [liquid ice](ref/eq-weapon/liquid-ice). These
don't count against your starting character wealth.
