**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Serpent's Skull

**Flavor:** While you aren't necessarily a native of Cheliax, you boarded the Jenivere at one of
that country's port cities. As the people of Cheliax generally know the Mwangi Expanse as a near
legendary land home to strange beasts and exotic treasures, your views of the region have a
distinctly romantic tinge, and the desire for fame, wealth, and adventure likely motivate your
travels.

**Benefit:** You begin the campaign with a detailed map of the Mwangi Expanse (this grants +2
competence bonus on all [Knowledge \[geography\]](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) checks made
regarding this region) and 200 gp worth of mundane equipment to aid your exploration of the jungle.
