**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold)

**Flavor:** Your outlandish purple scales made you an outcast from your tribe and forced you to
survive in isolation at the edges of kobold society. While wandering alone through the Darklands,
you contacted several other underground races and expanded your linguistic repertoire.

**Benefit:** In addition to speaking Draconic, you begin play with the ability to speak one of the
following: Aklo, Dwarven, Goblin, or Undercommon.
