**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Mwangi

**Flavor:** You have an almost instinctive ability to sense danger and peril in ruined structures.

**Benefit:** Whenever you're in ruins, you gain a +2 trait bonus on Initiative checks and a +1 trait
bonus on saving throws against traps and natural hazards.
