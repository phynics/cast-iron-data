**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Grimspawn

**Flavor:** The act of murder allows you to draw vitality from fleeing souls.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) action to kill a creature (either
by damage or through a failed Fortitude save), you gain a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to half your character level (minimum 1)
for 1 minute. These temporary hit points do not stack with multiple _coups de grace._
