**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** You have a fondness for a particular port, and the people who live and do business there
return the sentiment.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to gather
information in your chosen home port.
