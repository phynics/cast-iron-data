**Type:** Social

**Prerequisite:** Member of The [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars)

**Benefit:** The skills of Golarion's greatest musicians are at your fingertips, thanks to the vast
treasure trove of musical knowledge in the vaults beneath the Grand Lodge in Absalom. Studying this
knowledge gives you the ability to use your bardic performance an additional 3 rounds per day.
