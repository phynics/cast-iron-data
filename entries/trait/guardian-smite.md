**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Trudd](ref/faith/trudd)

**Flavor:** Paladins of Trudd taught you to be a blessed, living barrier against enemies who
threaten those you hold dear.

**Benefit:** Once per day, when you strike a foe with _smite evil,_ you can halve the total damage
dealt to the foe to reduce that foe's speed to 5 feet for 1 round.
