**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold)

**Flavor:** You are one of the diehard kobold faithful, respected in your kobold community.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when dealing with
other kobolds. Additionally, if your deity grants the Artifice, [Earth](ref/domain/earth), or
Trickery domain, then your kobold faith unlocks a corresponding subdomain: Trap, Radiation, or
Ambush.
