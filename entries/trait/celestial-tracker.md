**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar); Angelkin

**Flavor:** You share the same talent for tracking evildoers that your angelic ancestor possessed.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made to follow
tracks. In addition, you treat trails as being up to 24 hours fresher than they actually are when
using the Survival skill to follow tracks.
