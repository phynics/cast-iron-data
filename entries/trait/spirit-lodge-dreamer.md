**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Erutaki

**Flavor:** You have experienced visions of the unseen spirit world, and bear the marks of the
spirits upon your flesh.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saves against illusions, and reduce any miss chance you
would take from [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) by 10%.
