**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Bellis

**Flavor:** Your frequent exposure to bees - and their stings - makes all such creatures seem more
like nuisances than actual threats.

**Benefit:** You gain DR 3/- against damage dealt by swarms and a +1 trait bonus on saving throws
against _[distraction](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/distraction)_ and poison
from swarms.
