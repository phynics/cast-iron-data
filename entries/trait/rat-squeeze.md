**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Wererat-kin

**Flavor:** You first discovered your wererat-related abilities while escaping someone, whether the
authorities or a rival, and ever since you have honed your ability to squeeze out of tight
situations.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use your skinwalker ability to change shape into your bestial form, you
can [squeeze](ref/misc/squeezing) through narrow spaces at least half as wide as your normal space
without slowing your movement; each move into or through a narrow space counts as 1 square, though
you still take all the normal penalties associated with squeezing.
