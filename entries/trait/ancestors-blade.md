**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Shizuru](ref/faith/shizuru)

**Flavor:** You were trained by your family with a weapon passed down from a venerable ancestor.

**Benefit:** You begin play with an heirloom [masterwork weapon](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) worth
up to 400 gp. You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls with weapons of this type (not just your
initial heirloom weapon) when you _smite evil._
