**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** At least one of your parents was a [Hellknight](ref/rule/aff-lawknights), and you see
the orders as the embodiment of civic virtue.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to influence
Hellknights and a +2 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks to influence enemies
of the Hellknights.
