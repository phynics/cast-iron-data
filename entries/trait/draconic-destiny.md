**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Whether you are aware of it or not, the moment of your birth was but one part of a
far-reaching machination in a dragon's grand design, and the influence of that mysterious plan makes
you a little harder to kill.

**Benefit:** When [dying](ref/misc/dying), you gain a +2 trait bonus on Constitution checks to
become stable. In addition, you die from hit point loss only once your hit points equal or exceed a
negative amount equal to your Constitution score + 2.
