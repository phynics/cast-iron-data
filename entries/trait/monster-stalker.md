**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You've been raised to hunt certain beasts.

**Benefit:** Choose either aberrations, animals, dragons, fey, magical beasts, oozes, or vermin. You
gain a limited version of the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability (15 feet normal, 30 feet upwind, 5 feet
downwind) that detects only your chosen creature type.
