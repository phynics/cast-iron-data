**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Witchmarket

**Flavor:** You traveled with the Witchmarket for a time, either willingly or as a result of a
bargain made by some other buyer. In your time in the Witchmarket, you learned to listen to the
whispers of items as though they were living creatures, giving you clues as to their function.

**Benefit:** Choose [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise), [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any),
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device), or [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft). You apply your
Charisma modifier on checks with that skill instead of the skill's normal ability score. That skill
is a class skill for you.
