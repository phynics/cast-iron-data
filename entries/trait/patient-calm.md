**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** As a child, you struggled to suppress a sharp temper, but the care and patient respect
of a trusted community leader rubbed off on you. As you grew older, you developed a remarkable
ability to keep calm and collected when performing a task you are familiar with.

**Benefit:** Choose one [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) or [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any)
skill. Whenever you take 10 with that skill, determine the result as if you had rolled a 12 instead
of a 10.

**Suggested Characters:** [Asmodeans](ref/faith/asmodeus), Drumans, [Irorans](ref/faith/irori).
