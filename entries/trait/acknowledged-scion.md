**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dhampir](ref/race/dhampir)

**Flavor:** You grew up in Nidal, Geb, or another place where your undead lineage was considered a
mark of power.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) and
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks. One of these skills (your choice) is a
class skill for you.
