**Flavor:** You are trained in the destruction of blights.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus to confirm critical hits against a creature that has a
template affiliated with some kind of communicable condition, such as a
[blighted fey](ref/template/blighted-fey) or a [fungal creature](ref/template/fungal-creature).
