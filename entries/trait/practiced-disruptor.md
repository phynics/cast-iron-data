**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You are experienced at facing off against spellcasters.

**Benefit:** You can attempt [Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) checks untrained; if
you have ranks in that skill, you instead gain a +1 trait bonus on those checks. The concentration
DC to [cast a spell defensively](ref/rule/concentration) in your threatened area increases by 1.
