**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dhampir](ref/race/dhampir); Ancient-born

**Flavor:** Your fragile flesh shows signs of years not yet lived.

**Benefit:** Any creature trying to discern your true age must make a
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) or [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) check opposed by your
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check; you gain a +5 trait bonus on this check. You gain a +2 trait bonus
on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks if your disguise makes you look at least one
[age category](ref/rule/age) older, and ignore the check penalty for disguising yourself as a
different age category while doing so.
