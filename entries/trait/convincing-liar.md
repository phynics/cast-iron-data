**Type:** Social

**Prerequisite:** Member of The [Bellflower Network](ref/rule/aff-antislavery)

**Flavor:** You've spent your life perfecting the art of half-truths in order to accomplish what you
need to.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) or
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and one of these skills (your choice) is a class skill for
you.
