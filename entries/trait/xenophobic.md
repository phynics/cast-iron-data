**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You have a hard time understanding and trusting those with unfamiliar ways and
appearances.

**Benefit:** You take a -2 penalty on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks made against creatures of a different race or from a
different culture.
