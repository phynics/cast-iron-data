**Prerequisite:** Member of the Qadira faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Ever since you were a young child, you have studied the ancient arcane traditions of several Tian
societies, and those studies have made your spellcasting ability difficult to resist. Once per day,
you may select one single spell that you are casting that allows for a saving throw. You may
increase the DC of that spell by +2.
