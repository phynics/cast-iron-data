**Prerequisite:** Any

**Flavor:** The blood of fiends taints your line, manifesting physically, though it may be barely
noticeable.

**Benefit:** Choose one of the following skills: [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), or [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes). You gain a
+1 trait bonus on checks with that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
