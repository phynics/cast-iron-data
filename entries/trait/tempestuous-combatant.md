**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Keleshite

**Flavor:** You move confidently in the face of danger.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **swift action**, you can choose one opponent. You and your mount (if
any) do not provoke attacks of opportunity from that opponent for moving within or exiting his
threatened area.
