**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Curse of the Crimson Throne

**Flavor:** No crime is too despicable for Gaedren Lamm and his thugs - a gang of lowlife murderers,
kidnappers, and drug dealers that operate in Korvosa's Midland district. You've lost someone to Lamm
and now you're out for revenge.

**Benefit:** While Lamm remains alive and free, you are not slain until your hit point total reaches
a negative number equal to your Constitution score + 3. Once Lamm is imprisoned or killed, you lose
the former benefit but gain 3 permanent hit points.
