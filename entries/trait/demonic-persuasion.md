**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** Demon Lords

**Flavor:** Your lord's favor aids you in your interactions with demonic creatures.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks when dealing with creatures of the
[demon](ref/subtype/demon) subtype.
