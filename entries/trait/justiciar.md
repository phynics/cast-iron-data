**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Rahadoum

**Flavor:** You are experienced in interpreting and applying legal doctrine, notably the Laws of
Man, independent of any divine mandates.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks to settle disputes or arbitrate laws.

Once per day if you succeed at an [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to demoralize a foe who
has repudiated or ignored a pronounced judgment you've made, you may choose to cause the creature to
be [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) instead of [shaken](ref/misc/shaken). The duration of the effect
remains the same.
