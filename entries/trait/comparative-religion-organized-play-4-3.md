**Prerequisite:** Member of the Silver Crusade faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your allies within the Silver Crusade have taught you a lot about Golarion's deities and their
followers. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Knowledge (religion) checks, and Knowledge (religion)
becomes a class skill for you.
