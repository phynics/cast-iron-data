**Type:** Magic

**Benefit:** Once per day when you cast a divination spell, you can cast that spell as if your
caster level were 1 level higher in the appropriate class. You also gain a +1 trait bonus on any
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) checks associated with your sorcerer bloodline, if any.
