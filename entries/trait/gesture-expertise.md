**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You are adept at nonverbal communication.

**Benefit:** You can use a sign language or gestural system to send complex messages quickly in
combat, and with a successful DC 15 [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) check, you can express
yourself clearly enough to communicate basic messages to people who do not understand your gestures.
You gain a +2 trait bonus to send secret messages via gestures using the [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff)
skill, and to understand gestures with the Linguistics skill.
