**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Orc](ref/race/orc)

**Flavor:** You frequently helped capture and wrangle dangerous animals and megafauna for your
tribe, and you had to control them well enough to avoid being killed.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can add your Strength modifier in addition to your Charisma modifier
when attempting a [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) check.
