**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Whispering Way](ref/rule/f-gravepath)

**Flavor:** You have learned the secrets of _[sotto voce](ref/spell/sotto-voce),_ the eerie, dry
whispering voice used to pass on cult secrets.

**Benefit:** You may cast [sotto voce](ref/spell/sotto-voce) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) three times per day as a spell-like ability. This
spell-like ability is cast at your highest caster level; if you have no caster level, it functions
at CL 1st.
