**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** You grew up in wilderness where kobolds make their homes. You learned early on how to
find traps before they find you, how to disable them, and when you should just walk away.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus to [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to discover
traps and a +1 trait bonus to [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks.
