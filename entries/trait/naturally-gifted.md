**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome) with the _gnome magic_ racial trait

**Flavor:** For unknown reasons, you have deeper reserves of magical energy than those around you.
Perhaps you are descended from a family of powerful arcane casters, or maybe you believe this trait
marks you as destined for magical greatness.

**Benefit:** You gain an additional use of one of your _gnome magic_
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) each day. This does not always have to be the
same spell-like ability - one day you might use [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights) twice,
only to use the additional casting for [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) the next
day.
