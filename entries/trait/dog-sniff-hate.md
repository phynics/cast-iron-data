**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Benefit:** You gain the [scent](ref/umr/scent) special quality but only against canine creatures,
like dogs or wolves, including monstrous hounds such as [hell hounds](ref/monster/hell-hound),
[yeth hounds](ref/monster/yeth-hound), and even [werewolves](ref/template/lycanthrope). You gain a
+2 trait bonus on attack rolls against dogs you can smell.
