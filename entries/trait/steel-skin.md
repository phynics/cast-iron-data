**Type:** Combat

**Benefit:** You gain the ability to [don or remove](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor) heavy
armor in half the normal time.

Additionally, you begin play with a great helm bearing the iconography of your family (this helm
grants no special defensive benefits). While wearing this helm with a suit of heavy armor, you gain
a +2 trait bonus on Intimidate checks.
