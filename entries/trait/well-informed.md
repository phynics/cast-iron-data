**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Benefit:** You make it a point to know everyone and to be connected to everything around you. You
frequent the best taverns, attend all of the right events, and graciously help anyone who needs it.
Because of this, you gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to gather
information and [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks. One of these skills (your
choice) is always a class skill for you.
