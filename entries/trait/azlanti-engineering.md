**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Azlanti

**Flavor:** You were trained in the style of Azlanti architecture and craft.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering)
checks. In addition, any masterwork item you craft has its [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) and break DC
increased by 1. This increase is an enhancement bonus.
