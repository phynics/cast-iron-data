**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** [Tengu](ref/race/tengu)

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** Your presence dampens curses and other ill effects.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saves against curses, including the hex class ability of
shamans and witches. Once per day, you can grant this bonus to all allies within 30 feet for 1 round
as an **immediate action**.
