**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Grundinnar](ref/faith/grundinnar)

**Flavor:** Your tact soothes bristling tempers and cools heated blood.

**Benefit:** You receive a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks. In addition,
you receive a +1 trait bonus to the DC of any charm or compulsion that does not provide ongoing
control and results in peaceful acts, such as [calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions),
[sleep](ref/spell/sleep), or a [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion) to lay down arms.
