**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Bachuan

**Flavor:** As a member of the Tiger Brigade, you are ready to guide the people of Bachuan and other
nations.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks made against
anyone whose [age](ref/rule/age) is one or more aging steps above yours, and Intimidate becomes a
class skill for you.
