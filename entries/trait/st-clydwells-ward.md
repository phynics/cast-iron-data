**Type:** Faith

**Prerequisite:** Mendev Crusaders

**Flavor:** You have learned the secrets of an ancient prayer that wards off the attacks of demons.

**Benefit:** As a **standard action**, you can recite a holy chant that affects
[demons](ref/family/demon) as if you were under the effect of a [sanctuary](ref/spell/sanctuary)
spell (caster level equal to 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier).
