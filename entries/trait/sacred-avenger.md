**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Religion:** [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria)

**Flavor:** You have a special connection between you and Calistria that enhances your ability to
achieve vengeance.

**Benefit:** When a creature damages you with a weapon, on your next turn you gain a +1 trait bonus
on weapon damage against that creature. If this creature is your _ilduliel_ (a personal, mutual
nemesis), this bonus increases to +2.
