**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Mwangi

**Flavor:** Your ability to endure in the darkest jungles has earned you a reputation as a novice
guide.

**Benefit:** While traversing any wilderness, you gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks, and gain a +1 trait bonus on Fortitude saving throws against
diseases.
