**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You witnessed or were party to the rescue of a halfling from Chelish slavery by the
[Bellflower Network](ref/rule/aff-antislavery) or [Eagle Knights](ref/rule/aff-vangard).

**Benefit:** Once per day when attempting to directly free a slave, you gain a +2 trait bonus on
your choice of an [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) check, an
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) check, a [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) check, or a skill
check attempted as part of a [heist](ref/rule/heists) or [pursuit](ref/rule/pursuit)
