**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Taldor

**Flavor:** You study magic at one of Taldor's many social clubs, wowing your friends with your
expertise in the simplest of magical exploits.

**Benefit:** Select two non-harmful arcane cantrips. You can cast these two cantrips once per day
each (caster level 1st). If you have levels in a class that can cast these cantrips, your caster
level for these cantrips is equal to that class level.
