**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Taldan

**Flavor:** You spent countless hours of your youth wandering the open campuses of the
[Kitharodian Academy](ref/rule/f-bards) and the Rhapsodic College, listening to wonderful musicians
and singers and daydreaming of your life as a bard.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to one category of [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) checks
and a +2 trait bonus to any [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks that deal with the
Oppara music scene.
