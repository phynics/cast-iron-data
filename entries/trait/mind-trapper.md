**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dhampir](ref/race/dhampir); Svetocher

**Flavor:** Your honeyed words are nearly impossible for your enchanted subjects to ignore.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Charisma checks to convince an ally enchanted by one of
your charm spells (such as [charm person](ref/spell/charm-person) or
[charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster)) to do things it wouldn't normally do.
