**Type:** Combat

**Prerequisite:** Member of The [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars)

**Flavor:** Thanks to your time as a Pathfinder, you've mastered the art of tracking.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on initiative checks and a +2 trait bonus on Survival checks
made while traveling through any of your favored terrains. This is in addition to any bonuses you
receive from your [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) class ability.
