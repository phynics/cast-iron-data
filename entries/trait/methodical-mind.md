**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Strange Aeons

**Flavor:** When you awake with nothing but hazy memories, you begin carefully cataloging your
thoughts to determine the reasons for your condition and where you might be. This comforts you, and
you know deep down that you have always relied on your ordered mind and pragmatic approach to face
challenges. You use this focus and sensibility to your advantage as you investigate your current
dire situation.

**Benefit:** Choose three [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skills. You gain a +1 trait bonus on
Knowledge checks of these kinds and on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks. In addition,
choose one Knowledge skill. When [researching](ref/rule/research), you are considered to have
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) for that Knowledge skill when determining what die you roll to
reduce a library's knowledge points (kp).
