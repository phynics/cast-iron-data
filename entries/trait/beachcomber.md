**Type:** Regional

**Region:** coastal or river

**Flavor:** You have a knack for finding what you need as it washes ashore.

**Benefit:** Once per week, if you spend 8 hours in a single day searching along a shoreline, you
can attempt a [Survival](ref/skill/survival) check. You find any combination of mundane objects
whose value in gold pieces is less than or equal to the result of the check, in a usable (if
somewhat waterlogged) condition.
