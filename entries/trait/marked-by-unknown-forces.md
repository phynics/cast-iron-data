**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** That which has marked you for power has also given you proof to show others.

**Benefit:** You may cast [light](ref/spell/light) three times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), but this ability only targets the palm of your
hand, and it manifests as a glowing sigil representing your ominous birth - a holy or unholy symbol,
a demonic symbol, or perhaps some other distinctive marking established by you and your GM. Those
who recognize this symbol have a [starting attitude](ref/skill/diplomacy) toward you of one step
closer to friendly (if they are followers of or scholars studying the sign) or one step closer to
hostile (if they are opposed to the sign or its followers).
