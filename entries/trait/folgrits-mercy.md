**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Folgrit](ref/faith/folgrit)

**Flavor:** You were called upon to defend your home or family at a vulnerable time and led with a
clarity of mind you did not know you had.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you use [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands), you also cure the
target of 1d4 points of Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma damage. You choose which ability score
gains the healing effect.
