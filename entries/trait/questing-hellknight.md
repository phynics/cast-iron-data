**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You are a quick study when it comes to local laws.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can attempt a [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) check
untrained to recall information about local laws, rulers, and popular locations. Additionally, you
gain a +1 trait bonus on Knowledge (local) checks, and Knowledge (local) is a class skill for you.
