**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Alkenstar

**Flavor:** Growing up bordering the Mana Wastes and living within the Grand Duchy of Alkenstar has
made you familiar with tools and practical objects. You rely on mechanics to solve problems and are
familiar with basic engineering concepts.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following skills: [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (any),
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device), or
[Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering). You gain a +1 trait bonus on checks with
that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
