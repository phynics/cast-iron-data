**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Benefit:** Select one terrain type from the ranger's
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) list. You can attempt [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
checks in this terrain in normal light without having cover or being invisible. You still can't
attempt Stealth checks in areas of bright light without invisibility or cover.
