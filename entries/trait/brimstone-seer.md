**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Orc](ref/race/orc)

**Benefit:** You were exposed to vision-granting caustic vapors by the oracles of the Brimstone
Haruspex, whether because you had a pressing question or because a parent or chieftain considered
you an expendable proxy. The vapors left scars that impose a -1 penalty on Charisma-based checks,
but once per day you gain a flash of insight that allows you to roll twice on a
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill check or Intelligence check and take the better result.
You must choose to use this trait before rolling.
