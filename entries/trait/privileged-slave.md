**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Kaoling

**Flavor:** You were enslaved by the hobgoblins of Kaoling, and obeyed your masters dutifully,
turning your back on comrades in order to find favor with your slavers - all the while plotting your
escape.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks, and Bluff becomes a class
skill for you.
