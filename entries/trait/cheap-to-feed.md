**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Region:** Osirion or Varisia

**Flavor:** Life in a big city taught the consequences of pride. Now you have no qualms about
pointing out how little of a burden your upkeep would prove to potential employers, captors,
innkeepers, or anyone else able to provide you with the necessities of life.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks made in order to gain
food, water, or shelter.
