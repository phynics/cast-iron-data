**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Nethys](ref/faith/nethys)

**Flavor:** You have studied the great masters of spellcraft, and your knowledge is exceptional.

**Benefit:** You gain a bonus of either +1 on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks or +2 on
[Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) checks. You must choose which bonus you receive
when you take the trait; once chosen, it cannot be changed.
