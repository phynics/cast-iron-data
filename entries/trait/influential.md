**Prerequisite:** Member of the Sovereign Court faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

When you make a suggestion, strangers often assume that it was their idea to begin with. You gain a
+3 bonus on Diplomacy checks to make requests of a creature. Once per day, you can select one single
spell you are casting with the language-dependent descriptor; you increase the saving throw DC of
that spell by 1.
