**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Religion:** [Venkelvore](ref/faith/venkelvore)

**Benefit:** If you have a piece of food (or something a goblin might consider food), you can
quickly eat it as part of a [charge](ref/rule/charge) attack once per day. Doing so increases your
speed by 10 feet for 1 round.
