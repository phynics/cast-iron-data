**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** Elven Deities

**Benefit:** Your knowledge of the elven gods has given you a broad perspective, thus granting you a
+1 trait bonus on all [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) skill checks, and
Knowledge (religion) is always a class skill for you.

This trait was published as "Elven Pantheist" in _Faiths and Purity_.
