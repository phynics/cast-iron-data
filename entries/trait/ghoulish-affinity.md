**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Darklands

**Flavor:** Ever since you recovered from [ghoul fever](ref/disease/ghoul-fever), the scars on your
arms and hands itch whenever one of these vile creatures is nearby.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to notice undead
disguised as living creatures, and a +2 trait bonus on Fortitude saves against diseases and poisons
inflicted by undead.
