**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Lands of the Linnorm Kings

**Flavor:** Long nights in the frigid winters of the north have inured you to cold.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on any savings throws made to resist the effects of
[cold environments](ref/rule/cold-dangers), as well as a +1 trait bonus on all saving throws against
cold effects.
