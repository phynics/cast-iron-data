**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Kyonin

**Flavor:** You have learned how to keep your flesh out of the way of the toxic thorns and vines
that are often prevalent in verdant lands.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on saving throws against poison and disease abilities and
effects inflicted by creatures of the plant type (such as a [basidirond's](ref/monster/basidirond)
spores).
