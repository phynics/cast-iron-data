**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Syrinx](ref/race/syrinx)

**Flavor:** You learned from your syrinx elders that your race is better than non-winged races and
you take care to keep above them physically as well.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Fly](ref/skill/fly) checks. This bonus increases to +4 on
checks to avoid losing altitude when damaged.
