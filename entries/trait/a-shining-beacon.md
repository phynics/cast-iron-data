**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae)

**Flavor:** You carry a burning hatred in your heart for all things demonic, and have studied their
weaknesses carefully.

**Benefit:** You deal an amount of additional damage equal to your weapon's critical hit modifier
when you score a successful critical hit with a weapon against a [demon](ref/family/demon). This
damage is added only after the damage is totaled, and is not multiplied by the critical hit
multiplier.
