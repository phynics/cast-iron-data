**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar)

**Flavor:** You carry the blood of a self-sacrificing celestial, and strive to live up to your
potential for heroism.

**Benefit:** As long as your current hit point total is less than half of your maximum hit points
possible, you gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls against evil foes.
