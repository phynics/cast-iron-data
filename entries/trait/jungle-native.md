**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Grippli](ref/race/grippli)

**Region:** Mwangi Expanse

**Flavor:** Your people have called the jungles home for generations, and they are extremely
proficient in moving about in that terrain.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against diseases and poisons, a +1 trait
bonus on initiative checks attempted in jungle terrain, and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) is a
class skill for you.
