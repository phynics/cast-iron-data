**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** You are used to crewing on many of the different types of ships that sail the Shackles.
As a result, you have a knack for quickly determining the weaknesses and strengths of any ship, even
ones you've never set foot on before.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks,
and Profession (sailor) becomes a class skill for you.
