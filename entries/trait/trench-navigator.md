**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Xidao

**Flavor:** You've sailed the treacherous waters of the Xidao Gulf in your past, and you can
navigate their dangers adroitly.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography)
checks, and a +1 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks. One of these
skills becomes a class skill for you.
