**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** You were one of only a few that survived the destruction of the merchant vessel Vantage.
Your crew was more than able to hold off the blundering pirate attackers, but their red dragon ally
proved too much for your defenses. As you watched the sharks and eels feast on the bodies of your
fallen comrades, you vowed to see Aashaq slain.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Reflex saves against spells and effects with the fire
descriptor.
