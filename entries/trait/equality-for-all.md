**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** Even when facing foes much larger than yourself, you still embody the Andoran ethos of
liberty.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus to your CMB and your CMD when facing opponents who are at
least two sizes larger than you.
