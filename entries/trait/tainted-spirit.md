**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** When you were a child, a parent or other person with authority over you made a minor
pact with a fiend on your behalf, stealing some of your vitality in exchange for a minor boon.

**Benefit:** At the end of any combat, you must attempt a Fortitude saving throw. The DC is 10 + the
number of rounds you acted in combat. If you fail this save, you become
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for 10 minutes per round you acted during that encounter.
