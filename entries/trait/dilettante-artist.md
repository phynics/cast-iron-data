**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Flavor:** Art for you is a social gateway and you use it to influence and penetrate high society.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to one category of [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) checks
and a +1 trait bonus to [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks. One of these skills (your choice)
is always a class skill for you.
