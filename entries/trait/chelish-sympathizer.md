**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You feed intelligence to the Thrice-Damned House of Thrune for your own gain or
ideological reasons and are given valuable clues gathered by other Chelish spies in exchange.

**Benefit:** Once per month while in an Inner Sea region settlement of at least 2,000 people, you
can call upon your connections to gain an edge in a [verbal duel](ref/rule/verbal-duels) or gain a
+2 bonus on one [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), or
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check.
