**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae)

**Flavor:** You know that within the heart of even the most hateful and cruel living creature exists
a sliver of shame and hope for redemption. You have trained long on martial techniques to use bladed
weapons not to kill, but to subdue.

**Benefit:** When striking to inflict nonlethal damage with any slashing weapon, you do not take the
normal -4 penalty on your attack roll, and gain a +1 trait bonus to any nonlethal damage you inflict
with a slashing weapon.
