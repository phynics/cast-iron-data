**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** Deep, compulsive greed gnaws at you. Whenever monetary treasure is divided, you must end
up with a greater share of that treasure than your companions or you're wracked with feelings of
jealousy and ill will.

**Benefit:** When treasure is divided, if you do not end up with at least 10% more treasure than any
other individual companion does, you have a hard time being helpful to your allies. You become
irritable, and can't take the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action for the next week.
