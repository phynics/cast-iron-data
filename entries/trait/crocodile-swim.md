**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Werecrocodile-kin

**Flavor:** You grew up near crocodiles, learning to move as they do.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) and [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
checks while moving at half speed or less and mostly or completely underwater. One of these skills
(your choice) becomes a class skill for you.
