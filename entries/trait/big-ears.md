**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Benefit:** Your massive ears are your pride and joy, and other goblins claim you can hear a flea
scream as it falls off a goblin dog. While this might not quite be the case, you gain a +2 bonus on
all [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks made to hear noises. Additionally, you can hear noises
that normally only dogs or other animals can hear, like the sound of a dog whistle.
