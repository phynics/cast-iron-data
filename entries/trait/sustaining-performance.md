**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Flavor:** Abandoned by a traditional healer at a critical time, you improvised a tale or jokes to
keep up the spirits of someone in dire health.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **standard action**, you can attempt a DC 15
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) check with an audible component (such as comedy, oratory, sing, or
with any instrument) to stabilize a [dying](ref/misc/dying) creature that can hear you within 30
feet. You can also attempt Perform checks instead of [Heal](ref/skill/heal) checks to provide
long-term care.
