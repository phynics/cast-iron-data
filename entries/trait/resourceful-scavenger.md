**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Numeria

**Flavor:** Living in Numeria required you to become an expert scavenger, searching through ruins
and crash sites for valuables and sustenance. Years spent in this pursuit have given you a keen eye
for finding valuables and potentially useful resources in the unlikeliest places.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks when you search
a location for valuables or resources. [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) is always a class skill for
you.
