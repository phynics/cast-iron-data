**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** In gathering ingredients necessary for mind-altering drugs and other concoctions, you
have become intimately familiar with the desert's many-legged pests.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on
[Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering) checks to identify vermin and on
saving throws against vermin's extraordinary abilities.
