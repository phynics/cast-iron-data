**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You learned from the [Forthright Men](ref/rule/red-silk-route), the Coins' peace-keeping
thieves' guild, to use candor to avoid trouble.

**Benefit:** You gain a +5 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to convince others
to overlook offensive or illegal acts. Diplomacy is always a class skill for you.
