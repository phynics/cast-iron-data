**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar); Angelkin

**Flavor:** Ever since you were a child, you've experienced vivid dreams of cutting down hordes of
shrieking, twisted demons.

**Benefit:** Whenever you succeed at a critical hit with a melee weapon against an evil outsider,
you ignore an amount of the outsider's [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) (if any) equal
to the critical multiplier of your weapon (this cannot reduce a creature's damage reduction to below
0).
