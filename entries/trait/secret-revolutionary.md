**Region:** Cheliax

**Flavor:** You seek to return Cheliax to its heyday before the rise of the House of the Thrune. You
have trained yourself to resist any questioning or torture should you ever be caught.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Will saves against mind-affecting effects, and on
