**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar); Plumekith

**Flavor:** You've always loathed serpents and other slithering monsters, ever since you can
remember, and have studied them extensively.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on
[Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering) checks and Knowledge (dungeoneering)
is always a class skill for you.
