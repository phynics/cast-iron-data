**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You are able to divorce your knowledge of the facts from your beliefs about the truth,
and thus from your facial expressions and body language.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to lie, provided the
person you're lying to has never known you to lie to him. Failing this check counts as being caught
in a lie by the target, negating any future use of this bonus against that person.
