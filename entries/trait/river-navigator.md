**Type:** Regional

**Region:** River Kingdoms

**Flavor:** You grew up in the River Kingdoms and are familiar with the many winding streams that
cross the land.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks or
[Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks dealing with lakes, rivers, and their
immediate surroundings, and one of these skills becomes a class skill for you.
