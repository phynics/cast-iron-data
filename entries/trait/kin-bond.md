**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** The bond between you and a close sibling is nearly mystical.

**Benefit:** Choose a single sibling with whom to share this bond. Once per day when you fail a
saving throw while your sibling is within 30 feet, you may reroll that saving throw using your
sibling's saving throw modifier. If you and the sibling are twins or otherwise part of a multiple
birth, you gain a +2 trait bonus on the rerolled saving throw. Your sibling must be willing to grant
you the reroll. If you fail the saving throw, you and your sibling are [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1
round.
