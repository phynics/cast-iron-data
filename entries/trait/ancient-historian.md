**Prerequisite:** Member of the Scarab Sages faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You are well acquainted with the lore of fallen empires. Choose either Knowledge (history) or
Linguistics. That skill is a class skill for you, and you begin play able to speak and read one of
the following languages: Ancient Osiriani, Azlanti, Cyclops, Jistka, Tekritanin, or Thassilonian.
