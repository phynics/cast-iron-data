**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Oread](ref/race/oread)

**Flavor:** Feeling harried by flighty non-oreads, you chose to embrace the life of a hermit with
few contacts beyond your rocky home.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Heal](ref/skill/heal) and [Survival](ref/skill/survival)
checks. Your bonus on Survival checks increases by 1 in underground or mountain environments.
