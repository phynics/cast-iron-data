**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Dranngvit](ref/faith/dranngvit)

**Flavor:** Once wronged, your vengeance can be swift and brutal.

**Benefit:** Once per day as an **immediate action**, you gain a +1 trait bonus on a single attack
roll. In addition, if the target has damaged you within the last round, you deal 1 additional point
of damage on a successful hit. You must choose to use this ability before making the attack roll.
