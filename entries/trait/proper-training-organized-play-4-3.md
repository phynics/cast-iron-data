**Prerequisite:** Member of the Grand Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your time at the Grand Lodge of Absalom has served you well. Choose either Knowledge (geography) or
Knowledge (history). You gain a +1 trait bonus on all checks for the chosen skill, and the chosen
skill becomes a class skill for you.
