**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Qadira

**Flavor:** You are a descendant of a long line of skilled dwarven weapon makers or have apprenticed
under such artisans. Either way, you learned their long-held secrets relating to the production of
magical arms and armor.

**Benefit:** You treat your caster level as 1 higher for purposes of creating magical weapons, and
gain a +1 trait bonus on [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) checks to create magical armor, shields, or
weapons.
