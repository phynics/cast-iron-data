**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** You have an instinctive talent for unleashing the vilest spells, letting the taint in
your blood empower your malicious magic.

**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a spell with the evil descriptor, you act as if you were one level
higher for the purpose of determining that spell's effects.
