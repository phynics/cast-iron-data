**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You have a vicious streak, and nothing satisfies you more than warm blood on your hands
and blade.

**Benefit:** Whenever you make an attack that reduces a foe to 0 hit points or fewer or you confirm
a critical hit, your attack deals 1 additional point of damage. The additional damage is a trait
bonus, and is multiplied by your weapon's critical hit multiplier.
