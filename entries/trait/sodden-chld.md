**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Sodden Lands

**Flavor:** You spent most of your life moving through murky bogs and have grown acclimated to the
terrain.

**Benefit:** You move normally through shallow bogs (as long as the terrain hasn't been magically
manipulated) but are still affected by [deep bogs](ref/rule/marsh-terrain) as normal.
