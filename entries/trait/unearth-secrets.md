**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Hellspawn

**Flavor:** You have a gift for recognizing secret desires.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks when trying
to get a hunch that might reveal a target's hidden vice, dark impulse, or any other craving it would
not want publicly revealed.
