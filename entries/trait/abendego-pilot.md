**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Sylph](ref/race/sylph)

**Region:** Eye of Abendego

**Flavor:** The howling gales of the Eye of Abendego were your lullaby as a child, and you quickly
learned to anticipate the winds' danger as well as parse their song.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks,
and you treat the wind force of environmental [wind](ref/rule/winds) effects as one step less
powerful for the purposes of determining any penalties on ranged attacks and [Fly](ref/skill/fly)
checks as well as whether you would be checked or blown away.
