**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You were bullied often as a child, and you are now constantly ready to defend yourself
with your fists when an enemy comes near.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack of opportunity attack rolls made with unarmed
strikes.

Note that this trait does not grant the ability to make attacks of opportunity with your unarmed
strikes - you must have a level in monk, the
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) feat, or some other similar power to
gain the use of this character trait. However, that doesn't prevent you from selecting this trait.
You simply cannot make use of it until a later point if you do.
