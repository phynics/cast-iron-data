**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling); Shackleborn

**Flavor:** Your fiendish ancestry has granted you unnatural skill with the chain, and your enemies
know to stay away when you wield it as a weapon.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on combat maneuver checks made to [trip](ref/rule/trip)
opponents with a [spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain) or [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip).
