**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-elf](ref/race/half-elf); human-raised

**Flavor:** You picked up not only the basics of your human parent's profession, but also the lingo
typical of the craft.

**Benefit:** Choose a [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) or [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) skill.
You gain a +1 trait bonus on checks with the chosen skill, and a +1 trait bonus on
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks when dealing with people
in the context of that craft or profession.
