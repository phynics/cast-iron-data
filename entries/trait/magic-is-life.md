**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Nethys](ref/faith/nethys)

**Flavor:** Your faith in magic allows you to reflexively use the energy of any spell effect
currently on you to save you from death.

**Benefit:** As long as you are under the effects of a spell, you gain a +2 trait bonus on saving
throws against death effects. If you are reduced to negative hit points while you are under the
effects of any spell, you automatically confirm stabilization checks to stop bleeding.
