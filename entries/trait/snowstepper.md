**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** As a native of the North, you have acquired the ability to tread lightly on snow.

**Benefit:** You ignore difficult terrain caused by snow, and it costs you only 2 squares of
movement to enter a square covered in [heavy snow](ref/rule/rain-snow-sleet-and-hail), rather than 4
squares of movement. This benefit only applies when you are not carrying a medium or heavier load.
