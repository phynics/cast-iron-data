**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** You're an expert at rescuing slaves. You know that a few slaves freed from their chains
can help turn the tide during a boarding action.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks made to
open locks, Strength checks made to break chains or bars in order to free slaves, and combat
maneuver checks made to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) chains or bars in order to free slaves.
