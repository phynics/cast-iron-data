**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Grundinnar](ref/faith/grundinnar)

**Flavor:** You are at your best when you serve others.

**Benefit:** When you cast spells that offer protection to an ally at your expense (such as
[shield other](ref/spell/shield-other)) or spells whose duration is divided when shared among many
(such as [water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing) or any of the _communal_ spells), you do so at
+1 caster level.
