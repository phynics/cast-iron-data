**Type:** Social

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on skill checks to haggle prices or negotiate payment; this
bonus increases to +4 if you are in a position of military or economic power over your target. You
also gain a +1 trait bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks and Appraise is always a class
skill for you.
