**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** You have spent many years studying the tenets of your faith, and now believe that the
dominant factions within the church have deviated from its true meaning and purpose. Rather than
breaking with your misguided church, you've decided to stand up and fight to reform the organization
you love. You have fervently studied scripture and steeled your will to combat the divergent beliefs
of your peers.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion)
checks that pertain to your own religion or spiritual practice, and you gain a +2 trait bonus on
saving throws against spells cast by others who worship your god or adhere to your philosophy.
