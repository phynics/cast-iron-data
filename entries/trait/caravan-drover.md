**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc)

**Flavor:** With your hand on the lash, your caravans always arrive on time.

**Benefit:** You treat [whips](ref/eq-weapon/whip) as martial weapons, and up to 10 animals and
humanoids traveling with you can [hustle](ref/rule/overland-movement) or make a forced march for 1
hour longer than normal before taking nonlethal damage and becoming fatigued.
