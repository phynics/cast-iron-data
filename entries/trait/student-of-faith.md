**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Rise of the Runelords

**Flavor:** While you have personally dedicated your life to a single deity, you study all religions
and mortal faiths. Upon hearing that the town of Sandpoint recently completed a cathedral dedicated
to the six deities most popular in the area, you had to see the place for yourself, and have arrived
in time for the consecration of this holy edifice.

**Benefit:** Because of your strong faith and broad range of study, you cast all cure spells at +1
caster level, and whenever you [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), you gain a +1 trait
bonus to the save DC of your channeled energy.
