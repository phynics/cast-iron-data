**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus)

**Flavor:** Raised in the church (whether or not you are currently a follower), you've focused your
indoctrinated fervor primarily on the elimination of demons.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) checks
about [demons](ref/family/demon) and a +2 trait bonus on Will saves against mind-affecting spells
and effects from demons.
