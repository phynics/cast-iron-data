**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Religion:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil)

**Flavor:** Few things exalt your faith more than feeding the hungry with your own honest sweat.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on any d20 roll to acquire food or water for others. Though
this bonus most often applies to [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks, it could also apply to
attacking monsters that are safe and nutritious to eat (mainly animals and plants). However, Erastil
takes a dim view of waste and of those who scorn his favor, and if you abuse this gift (for example,
by using the bonus to defeat a bear, then only eating one bite of it), he punishes you with a -2
penalty on attacks, checks, and saves for 24 hours.
