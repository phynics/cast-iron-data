**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Tales of the Knights of Ozem have inspired you since childhood to lash out reflexively
against the undead.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls when making attacks of
opportunity against undead. While you wield a shield, your shield bonus to AC improves by 1 against
attacks of opportunity made by undead.
