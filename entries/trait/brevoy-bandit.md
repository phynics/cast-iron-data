**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mivon

**Flavor:** You fled the persecution of Brevoy authorities and wound up in Mivon. Whether or not you
are guilty of your alleged crimes, you seek to build a new life amid new neighbors.

**Benefit:** Choose one [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) skill and one ability score other
than Wisdom. You add that ability score's modifier in addition to your Wisdom modifier on checks
with the chosen Profession skill.
