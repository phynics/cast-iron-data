**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human)

**Flavor:** You survived an encounter with disease in a slum or contagion-prone area.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Fortitude saving throws against diseases and on
Constitution checks to stabilize while [dying](ref/misc/dying).
