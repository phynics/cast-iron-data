**Prerequisite:** Member of the Dark Archive faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You bear the stain of a higher fiend upon you, and any evil creature that sees it may think twice
before crossing you. You gain a +2 trait bonus on all Bluff, Diplomacy, Intimidate, and Sense Motive
checks when dealing with outsiders of the evil subtype.
