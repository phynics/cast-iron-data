**Type:** Mount

**Prerequisite:** Have a Chernasardo riding elk as a mount

**Benefit:** While riding a Chernasardo riding [elk](ref/monster/herd-animal-elk), you gain a +4
trait bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal), [Ride](ref/skill/ride), and
[wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks to resist another creature's unnatural aura.
