**Type:** Regional

**Region:** urban

**Flavor:** You grew up among the outcasts and outlaws of your society, learning to forage and
survive in an urban environment.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following skills: [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device),
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist), or [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand). You gain
a +1 trait bonus on checks with that skill, and it is always a class skill for you.
