**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Tian Xia

**Flavor:** For as long as you can remember, the spirits have always whispered into your ears and
guided you - provided that you appease them.

**Benefit:** At the start of each day, you can choose one [medium spirit](ref/ability/spirits) and
accept one of its _taboos_ for 24 hours. As long as you don't break that _taboo,_ once during the
next 24 hours, you can add a +1 trait bonus to any one roll, check, or saving throw (but not to AC)
listed under your chosen spirit's _spirit bonus._ Using this ability is a **free action** that you
can use even if it isn't your turn.

If you are a [medium](ref/class/medium), the spirit you choose with this trait must be the same as
the spirit you are channeling and vice versa
