**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Shackles

**Benefit:** You have sailed on some of the roughest seas on Golarion, and have skirted the Eye of
Abendego. You're accustomed to the pitch and yaw of sailing vessels, even in a storm, and gain a +2
trait bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to keep your footing on uneven ground or
unsteady surfaces.
