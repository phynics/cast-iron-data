**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Varisia

**Benefit:** When you had your deity's holy symbol tattooed on your body by a Sczarni tattooist
years ago, you never imagined it would save your life. Your holy symbol tattoo functions in all ways
as a holy symbol. When you use your holy symbol tattoo to keep a [vampire](ref/template/vampire) at
bay, the DC for the vampire to overcome its revulsion is 28 (rather than the normal 25).
