**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Taldan

**Flavor:** Your reliability is a core part of your self-image.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can gain a +1 trait bonus on an attack roll, saving throw, or skill
check if success on that check is directly relevant to fulfilling a verbal or written agreement you
have made. You must choose to use this trait before rolling, and this ability does not work if you
initiated the agreement for the purpose of activating this ability.
