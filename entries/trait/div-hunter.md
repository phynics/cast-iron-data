**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Thuvia

**Flavor:** Divs have tormented you and your loved ones for as long as you can remember. You've
improved your skills to better oppose the creatures.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Knowledge checks to identify [divs](ref/family/div) (and
their special powers and weaknesses) and a +1 trait bonus on weapon damage rolls against divs.
