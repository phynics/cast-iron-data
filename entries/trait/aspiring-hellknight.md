**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Cheliax

**Flavor:** Your family has a long tradition of service in the
[Hellknights](ref/rule/aff-lawknights), and your strict upbringing and training have given you a
forceful aura of command.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks, and Intimidate
is always a class skill for you.
