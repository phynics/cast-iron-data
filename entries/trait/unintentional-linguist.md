**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You can speak with outsiders.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on all [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks, and you
begin play knowing one of the following languages (in addition to those granted by your race and
Intelligence modifier): Abyssal, Aquan, Celestial, Ignan, Infernal, Protean, or Terran.
