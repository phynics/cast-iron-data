**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Mummy's Mask

**Flavor:** Osirion has a history stretching back over 8,000 years, and the deities worshiped today
in modern Osirion are not the same ones revered in Ancient Osirion's heyday - deities with names
like Anubis, Osiris, Ra, and Set, among others. Your family never lost the faith of your ancestors,
however, and your devotion to one of the deities of Ancient Osirion has helped keep the memory of
Osirion's past alive - a past that still lingers on in the untouched necropolis of Wati.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) and
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks, and one of those skills is always a
class skill for you. In addition, your faith in the old gods of Osirion grants you a +1 trait bonus
on saving throws against divine magic.
