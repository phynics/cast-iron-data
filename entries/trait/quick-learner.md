**Type:** Equipment

**Flavor:** You're skilled at learning the feel of a particular weapon after a few swings in combat.

**Benefit:** After your first attack with a weapon in which you are not proficient, the
non-proficiency penalty decreases by 2 (to a -2 penalty). This effect lasts only while you have the
weapon in hand; you must &quot&relearn&quot& the weapon each time you pick it up.
