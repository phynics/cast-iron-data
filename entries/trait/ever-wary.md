**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** Constant fear that your fiendish nature might provoke a sudden attack ensures that you
never completely let down your guard.

**Benefit:** During the surprise round and before your first action in combat, you can apply half
your Dexterity bonus (if any) to your AC. You still count as [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) for
the purposes of attacks and effects.
