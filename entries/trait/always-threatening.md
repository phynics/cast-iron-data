**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Thamir Gixx](ref/faith/thamir-gixx)

**Flavor:** You are fast on the draw.

**Benefit:** As a **move action**, you can draw a light, concealed weapon for which you have
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus). If you have the [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw) feat,
drawing this weapon is a **free action**.
