**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Fetchling](ref/race/fetchling)

**Flavor:** A life spent avoiding racial mistrust has taught you to humor others effectively when it
is useful to do so.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks and on attack rolls
against opponents who are denied their Dexterity bonus to AC against you.
