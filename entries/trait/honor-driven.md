**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Ulfen

**Flavor:** Your honor is such a part of you that you can sense it (or its lack) in others.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks, and Sense
Motive is always a class skill for you.
