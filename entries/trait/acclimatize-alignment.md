**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Like a climber ascending a mountain, you gradually grow acclimatized to an opposing
planar alignment.

**Benefit:** For each consecutive day you remain on a
[mildly aligned plane](ref/rule/alignment-traits) that opposes your alignment, reduce your penalty
on Charisma-based checks by 1, to a minimum of 0. This does not affect the penalty from a strongly
aligned plane.
