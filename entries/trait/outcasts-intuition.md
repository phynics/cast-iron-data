**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You are able to sense the motives of others and use that sense to bolster your magic
against dispelling.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks, and Sense
Motive is always a class skill for you. In addition, your caster level is treated as 1 level higher
for the purposes of any attempts to dispel your magic.
