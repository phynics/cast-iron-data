**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Undine](ref/race/undine)

**Region:** River Kingdoms

**Flavor:** Raised amid or near the transitive aquatic kingdom known as Outsea, your predilection
for watery environs has been strong since birth and you know the types of creatures who call the
ocean home.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) checks to identify
aquatic monsters. Anytime you are looking through water and would take a penalty on vision-based
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks due to murkiness or other natural circumstances, reduce
the penalty by 2.
