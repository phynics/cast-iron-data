**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker)

**Flavor:** Your first transformation was in a public place, observed by loved ones who later
shunned you or townsfolk who chased you away. You have become good at hiding your nature.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks, and Bluff becomes a class
skill for you.
