**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Aballon

**Flavor:** You spent at least part of your youth in Horsethroat and are acclimated to the frigid
cold of Aballon's Ice Wells.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on saves to avoid nonlethal damage from
[cold environments](ref/rule/cold-dangers). In addition, you begin play with a
[cold-weather outfit](ref/eq-misc/cold-weather-outfit) at no cost.
