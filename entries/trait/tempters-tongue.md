**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** The blood of crafty, bargain-making fiends runs in your veins, and you have a talent for
brokering deals of your own.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks and on opposed
Charisma checks to influence creatures you have charmed (such as with
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)).
