**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Inspired by stories of mortals ascending to godhood, you're certain that you can do the
same, and do all you can to hone your talents and reach your utmost potential.

**Benefit:** Once per day, when you take 20 on a roll or check, you can complete the action in only
five times the normal amount of time for that action, rather than 20 times the normal amount.
