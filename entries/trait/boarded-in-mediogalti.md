**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Serpent's Skull

**Flavor:** Your travels have brought you to the Jenivere through the dangerous port of Ilizmagorti,
home to both pirates and assassins. Perhaps you're fleeing a price on your head, perhaps you've
jumped ship from another vessel, or perhaps you're merely looking for a new start in a land where
the dangers make themselves more obvious.

**Benefit:** Your familiarity with subtle slayings and toxins grants you a +2 trait bonus on all
saves against poison. In addition, you can pick one type of poison listed below to be immune to,
having undergone a long and painful process of building up an immunity to its effects:
[blue whinnis](ref/eq-poison/blue-whinnis), [giant wasp poison](ref/eq-poison/giant-wasp-poison),
[medium spider venom](ref/eq-poison/medium-spider-venom),
[small centipede poison](ref/eq-poison/small-centipede-poison), or _spider vine poison_ (a poison
derived from a toxic plant native to the Mwangi Expanse).
