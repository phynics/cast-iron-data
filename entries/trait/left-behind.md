**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Region:** Mwangi Expanse or Varisia

**Flavor:** Having been born to those who chose to remain on Golarion during the Age of Darkness in
the Mwangi Expanse, you understand the people of this world better than most elves.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when interacting
with Forlorn elves and non-elf races.
