**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Elion](ref/faith/elion)

**Flavor:** You have traveled far with only your compass and faith as your guides.

**Benefit:** You begin play with a [compass](ref/eq-misc/compass), and any compass or
[wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder) in your possession also functions as a holy symbol of
Elion. Either [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) or
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) becomes a class skill for you.

Once per day you can consult your compass or wayfinder as a **full-round action** to gain a +2 trait
bonus on one Knowledge (geography) or Survival check.
