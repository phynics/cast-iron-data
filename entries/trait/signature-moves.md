**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You're known for some unique item that has become your trademark.

**Benefit:** Your starting equipment includes a single masterwork item worth less than 900 gp, and
you gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks
while wielding this item in one or both hands.
