**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Benefit:** You can channel your evil ancestry to take advantage of any effects for which such an
identity would matter (such as [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)). You detect as an evil outsider
with the same number of HD as you possess. You can begin or end this effect as a **swift action**.
You can maintain this effect for a number of rounds per day equal to your HD.
