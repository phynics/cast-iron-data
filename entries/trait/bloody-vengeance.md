**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Vildeis](ref/faith/vildeis)

**Flavor:** You were slain by evil forces as a child, but a follower of Vildeis restored you to
life. Now you live on in service to the Cardinal Martyr, battling the forces of evil so no other
child will know the icy grip of death.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on attack rolls to confirm critical hits against any creature
that has at least a faint evil aura (see [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)).
