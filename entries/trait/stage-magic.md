**Type:** Equipment

**Flavor:** You have put in many hours practicing with flashy alchemical items that enhance your
Perform skill.

**Benefit:** When you use an attention-getting alchemical item such as a flask of
[alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick),
[thunderstone](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone), or another appropriate alchemical item (GM's discretion)
as part of a performance, you gain a +2 trait bonus on your [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) check
for the duration of that performance.

The item is expended without its normal effects and only serves to make your performance more
impressive; for example, you could pretend to ignite yourself with alchemist's fire as part of a
Perform (comedy) check or make a dramatic appearance with a smokestick as part of a Perform (act)
check, but you would not actually take fire damage or gain [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from
smoke.
