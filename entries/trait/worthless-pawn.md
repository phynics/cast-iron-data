**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** Great Old Ones

**Flavor:** Your existence is so meaningless to your chosen patron that it cares nothing for how you
wield its power.

**Benefit:** You may prepare and cast spells whose alignment is in opposition to your patron's, but
doing so causes you to take 1 point of [Wisdom damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per spell
level when the spell is cast. This does not permit you to use
[spell trigger](ref/misc/spell-trigger) or [spell completion](ref/misc/spell-completion) items of
opposing alignments.
