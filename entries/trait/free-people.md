**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** Kellid

**Region:** Realm of the Mammoth Lords

**Flavor:** The independent tribes from the Realm of the Mammoth Lords hold little regard for the
control others wield over free people. Their willful, independent streak makes them suspicious of
any attempt to guide their behavior.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks and on Will
saves against mind-affecting effects.
