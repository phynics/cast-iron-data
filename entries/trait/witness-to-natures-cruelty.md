**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Kellid

**Flavor:** You saw firsthand the terrible toll of starvation and the elements, and refuse to allow
even your worst enemy to suffer that way.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Heal](ref/skill/heal) and [Survival](ref/skill/survival)
checks made on behalf of others. One of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
