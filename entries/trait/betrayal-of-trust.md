**Type:** Family

**Special:** It is possible to regain the Tight Bonds trait, for either the same or a different
relationship, but it requires substantial in-character work. The GM decides when this trait reverts
back to the original one.

**Flavor:** If you have the trait [Tight Bonds](ref/trait/tight-bonds), you swap it for this trait
at the moment you decide to betray the relationship. (You need not wait until you have actually
committed an act of betrayal.)

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks, increasing to +4 on
checks specifically made to conceal or deny your infidelity.
