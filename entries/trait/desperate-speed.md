**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Your eidolon excels at a movement type you do not possess.

**Benefit:** Your eidolon receives a +5-foot enhancement bonus to one type of speed it has that you
do not, such as a climb or swim speed. If your eidolon doesn't already possess such a mode of
movement, you may apply this trait bonus later if it gains an evolution that grants it such a mode.
Once this bonus is applied to a mode of movement, it can't be switched to another mode.
