**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Nirmathas

**Flavor:** You have served in one of the militia bands of Nirmathas, and have learned valuable
skills.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following skills: [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (soldier),
[Ride](ref/skill/ride), or [Survival](ref/skill/survival). You gain a +1 trait bonus on that skill,
and it is always a class skill for you.
