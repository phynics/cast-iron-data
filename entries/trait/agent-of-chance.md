**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Nivi Rhombodazzle](ref/faith/nivi-rhombodazzle)

**Flavor:** You can change the fortunes of others, typically for the better.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can allow an adjacent ally to reroll a skill check as an **immediate
action** before the result is revealed. The ally must take the second roll, even if it is worse.
