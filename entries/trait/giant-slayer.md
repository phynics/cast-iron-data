**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Rise of the Runelords

**Flavor:** Your family's village was plundered by giants in the wilds of Varisia, leaving nothing
but a smoldering ruin. After the destruction of your village, your family trained for combat against
giants to prevent such a tragedy from ever happening again. Since hearing of giants mobilizing
throughout the countryside, you ventured to Sandpoint to help the town prepare for a possible
incursion.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Perception](ref/skill/perception), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks and +1 trait
bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls against creatures of the giant subtype.
