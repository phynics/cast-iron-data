**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Varisian wanderers

**Flavor:** A childhood spent at your mystically gifted grandmother's side has taught you the basics
of real magic.

**Benefit:** You may cast [prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation) three times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This spell-like ability is cast at your highest
caster level; if you have no caster level, it functions at CL 1st.
