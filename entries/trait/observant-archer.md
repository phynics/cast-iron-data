**Type:** Regional

**Region:** aquatic

**Flavor:** Growing up in an aquatic environment, where three-dimensional movement and perception
were necessary, has given you keen observational skills and the ability to notice and target unusual
positions with ranged attacks.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on ranged attack rolls when your target is at least 10 feet
above or below you.
