**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** You've developed a reputation as a privateer who fights for freedom.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks. This bonus
becomes a -2 penalty when dealing with slavers or those who support the slave trade. Diplomacy
becomes a class skill for you.
