**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Jade Regent

**Flavor:** Intrigued by the travels of one of your long-time acquaintances, you've convinced her to
teach you one of the exotic languages she picked up in her journeys.

**Benefit:** Choose one of the following characters. You may select one of that character's
languages as a bonus language. Additionally, you gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff)
checks made to pass secret messages to the chosen character (see the description of the Bluff
skill).

- **Ameiko:** Tien, Varisian
- **Koya or Sandru:** Varisian
- **Shalelu:** Elven, Goblin
