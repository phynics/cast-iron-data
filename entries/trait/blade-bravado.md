**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Taldan

**Flavor:** You grew up fighting in one of the many Taldan dueling academies and have honed your
skills with one-handed weapons.

**Benefit:** When wielding a one-handed weapon and nothing in your off hand, you gain a +1 trait
bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) and [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks.
