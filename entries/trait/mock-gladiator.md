**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have learned how to work a crowd in Magnimar's mock-gladiator fights.

**Benefit:** Choose one weapon. You take no penalty on attack rolls when dealing nonlethal damage
with that weapon, and once per day if you score a critical hit with the weapon, you can immediately
attempt an [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to demoralize your target.
