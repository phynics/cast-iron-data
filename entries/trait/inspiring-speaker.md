**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Galt

**Flavor:** You have learned the arts of oratory, and know how to speak to crowds to rile them up or
to calm them down.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when addressing crowds of 10 people or more.
