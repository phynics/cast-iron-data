**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Nagaji](ref/race/nagaji)

**Region:** Jalmeray

**Flavor:** You were groomed from childhood to act as an emissary to Nagajor's allies in Jalmeray,
and you understand much about the Jalmeri people as a result.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks, and Diplomacy is
a class skill for you. In addition, you gain Vudrani as a bonus language.
