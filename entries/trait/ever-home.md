**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Garundi

**Flavor:** You have lived in many cities dotting the northern coastline of Garundi, and after you
have departed from each, you have taken a small token to remember it by.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) and
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks.
