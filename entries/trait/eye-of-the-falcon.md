**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** You have a way of seeing right to the heart of things.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against illusion (phantasm) spells and a +1
trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to know when you are being lied to, so
long as you are face to face with the speaker in question.
