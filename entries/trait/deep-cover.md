**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have lived a double life since your youth, perhaps to avoid persecution for your
true identity or in service to an enemy of the people or group associated with your cover identity.

**Benefit:** You can always take 10 on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise)
checks to assume and maintain your cover identity. Bluff or Disguise (your choice) is a class skill
for you.
