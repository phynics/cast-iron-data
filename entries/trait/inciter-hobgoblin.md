**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Hobgoblin](ref/race/hobgoblin)

**Flavor:** You witnessed or were part of a feud that turned close friends into hateful enemies.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to convince others that
their allies have wronged them and on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to persuade others to
betray their allies' trust.
