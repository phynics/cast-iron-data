**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** An outsider visited you at a young age, and you have had a significant affinity with
that type of outsider ever since.

**Benefit:** Choose a subtype of outsider, such as [angel](ref/subtype/angel) or
[elemental](ref/subtype/elemental). When you summon an outsider of that subtype via
[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally),
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), or similar spells and abilities, the summoned creature
gains a number of temporary hit points equal to the level of spell used to summon it.
