**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Mummy's Mask

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in the city of Wati, and you know its streets and secrets well.
Although it's frowned upon by the city's authorities, you have sneaked into the necropolis on
multiple occasions to wander its dusty, abandoned streets. Out of respect for the deceased, you've
never actually entered one of the necropolis's silent tombs, but you have no fear of what might lie
inside.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saves against fear effects. In addition, your knowledge of
the city grants you a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) checks, and
that skill is always a class skill for you.
