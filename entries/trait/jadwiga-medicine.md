**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Irrisen

**Benefit:** Once per day as an **immediate action** when you cast a spell of the healing subschool
or a hex that produces the effect of such a spell, you can attempt an
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to demoralize your target or anyone who can see you within
30 feet. You gain a +2 trait bonus on this check.
