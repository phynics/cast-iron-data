**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome)

**Flavor:** You were once delighted to discover an object that could be the crown jewel of your
collection, only to be thwarted by its weight. Thereafter, you trained yourself to pack items in the
most efficient ways possible.

**Benefit:** Treat your Strength score as 2 points higher when calculating your
[carrying capacity](ref/rule/carrying-capacity).
