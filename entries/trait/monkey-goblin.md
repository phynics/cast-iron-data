**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Region:** Mediogalti

**Flavor:** You are adept at arboreal activity, and are more at home in the branches of a tree than
you are on the ground.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) and
[Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks while climbing trees.
