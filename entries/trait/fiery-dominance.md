**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Plane of Fire

**Flavor:** Raised among the efreet of the Plane of Fire, you developed a similarly assured sense of
superiority over people from other backgrounds.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks against
humanoids of a subtype different from your own (or all non-human humanoids, if you are not a
humanoid), and the DC for such creatures to demoralize you with Intimidate is 2 higher than usual.
