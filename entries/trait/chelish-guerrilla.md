**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Cheliax

**Flavor:** In overthrowing House Thrune, you've spent many years training to face the diabolist

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) checks to identify
[devils](ref/family/devil) (and their special powers and weaknesses) and a +1 trait bonus on weapon
damage rolls against devils.
