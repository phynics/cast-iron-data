**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Bolka](ref/faith/bolka)

**Flavor:** You have the art of turning a passing glance into love.

**Benefit:** You receive a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks with those who
find you attractive. You can use [daze](ref/spell/daze) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), with a caster level equal to your character
level.
