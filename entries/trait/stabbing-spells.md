**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Aroden wrote much about ways to use weapons to enhance magic's effectiveness, and you
have taken to heart the insights gleaned from his words.

**Benefit:** Whenever you strike a foe with a weapon, you gain a +2 trait bonus on caster level
checks to overcome that foe's [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) until the end of your
next turn.
