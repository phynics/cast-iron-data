**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Qadira

**Flavor:** You have studied at the College of Medicaments and Chirurgery, and know by sight the
signs of ailments and toxins you might encounter.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus to [Heal](ref/skill/heal) checks to treat diseases or
poisons, and Heal is always a class skill for you.
