**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Religion:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar)

**Flavor:** Your ability to notice even the slightest change, and the discomfort it causes, made
service to Abadar a logical choice.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on any [Perception](ref/skill/perception) check made to
identify any changes to a location that have occurred since you last visited it.
