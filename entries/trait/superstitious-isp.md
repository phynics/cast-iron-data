**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Realm of the Mammoth Lords

**Flavor:** You are distrustful of any magic that goes beyond your native shamanic ways.

**Benefit:** As long as you do not possess any levels in a class that grants arcane spellcasting
power, you gain a +1 trait bonus on all saving throws against arcane spells.
