**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Sarkoris or The Worldwound

**Flavor:** You are used to the maddening energy fluctuations emanating from the Worldwound, and
have steeled your mind against their influence.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on saving throws against the mind-affecting effects of evil
outsiders.
