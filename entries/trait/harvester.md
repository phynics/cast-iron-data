**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You were trained to harvest all parts of an animal with care and precision.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (tanner) or
Profession (trapper) checks, and you may make these checks as if you were trained in the skill even
if you have no ranks. Additionally, you do not risk poisoning yourself whenever you handle or apply
poison taken from a venomous creature.
