**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Kyonin

**Flavor:** The elven druids of Kyonin taught you to commune with the natural healing powers of
animals.

**Benefit:** Once per day when you cast a spell that restores hit points, you restore 1 additional
hit point for every die rolled as long as a creature of the animal type is within 50 feet. You must
decide to use this ability before rolling to determine the number of hit points that your spell
restores.
