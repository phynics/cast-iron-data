**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [Orc](ref/race/orc)

**Flavor:** You have a knack for breaking animals to your will, though you know little of civilized
training methods.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks, but should you ever be
forcibly dismounted in combat, your mount attacks you to the best of its ability.
