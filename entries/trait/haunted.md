**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** Something from your past - or a dark secret you presently hold - makes it difficult for
you to ever be at peace, and your chronic worry that you might fall to evil influence has become a
self-fulfilling prophecy.

**Benefit:** You take a -2 penalty on saves against spells with the evil descriptor.
