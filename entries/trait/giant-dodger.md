**Type:** Regional

**Region:** desert

**Flavor:** In your youth, you were chased by giants for trespassing - or for sport - and you have
learned to evade the reach of your opponents.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to avoid provoking an
attack of opportunity when moving out of a threatened space.

**Likely Regions:** Katapesh, Osirion, Qadira, Rahadoum, Thuvia.

**Likely giants:** Cliff, Desert, River
