**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mediogalti

**Flavor:** You've rubbed elbows with pirates of all stripes in Ilizmagorti's taverns and convinced
a few to show off their swordfighting skills.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on attacks of opportunity made with
[daggers](ref/eq-weapon/dagger), [short swords](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword),
[cutlasses](ref/eq-weapon/cutlass), [rapiers](ref/eq-weapon/rapier),
[scimitars](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar), or [hook hands](ref/eq-weapon/hook-hand).
