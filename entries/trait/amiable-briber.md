**Type:** Social

**Region:** Absalom

**Flavor:** Your attempts to bribe others usually come off as playful or as a gag.

**Benefit:** The first time someone refuses a bribe you offer, that person's attitude toward you
doesn't worsen, even if the offer would normally offend the person (as though you had failed a
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check by 5 or more).
