**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You have spent considerable time in arid wastelands where girtablilus and other venomous
creatures are common, and you have built up a resistance to poison.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on Fortitude saving throws against poison, and once per day
when attempting a saving throw to resist [girtablilu](ref/monster/girtablilu) poison, you can roll
twice and take the higher result.
