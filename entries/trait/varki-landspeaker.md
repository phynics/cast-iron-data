**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Varki

**Flavor:** You are deeply attuned to the lands your people call home and the spirits that live
there.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks in arctic or cold
terrain. In addition, while in such terrain, you may query the spirits of the land once per day to
gain a +5 trait bonus on a single skill check to follow tracks, forage, or find shelter.
