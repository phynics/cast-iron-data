**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Diaspora

**Flavor:** You were raised in the Diaspora and are accustomed to being isolated from most other
life in the vast void of space.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saves against fear; this bonus increases to +2 whenever
you are 30 feet or farther from your nearest ally.
