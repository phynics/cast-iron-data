**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Garundi

**Flavor:** You are inured to the bright glare of the hot desert sun, and its dazzling rays pose
little danger to you.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against effects that would cause you to
become [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) or [blinded](ref/misc/blinded), and once per day when you would
be dazzled or blinded by an effect, you can reduce the duration of that effect by half (minimum 1
round). You cannot use this ability if you are already dazzled or blinded or if you have the
[light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity) or [light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness) special
quality.
