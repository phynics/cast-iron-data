**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You were born in a place with a plethora of strangely interacting magic, such as Geb,
the Mana Wastes, or Nex, and magic is dangerously eager to surge into action around you.

**Benefit:** You roll twice and take the lower result on
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) checks to activate items blindly and on Reflex saving
throws against effects produced by magic items.
