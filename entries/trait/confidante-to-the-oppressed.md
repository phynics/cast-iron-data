**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Andoran

**Flavor:** Others recognize that you are filled with the Andoren spirit of liberty and equality.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to improve the
attitude of oppressed, enslaved, or imprisoned subjects, and unless they have reason to distrust
you, their starting attitude toward you is at least indifferent, if not better.
