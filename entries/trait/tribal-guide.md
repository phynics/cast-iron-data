**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold)

**Flavor:** You were trained to guide the movements of larger creatures, perhaps travelers from
Ilizmagorti or transports under Absalom.

**Benefit:** You provide food and water or a bonus against severe weather for two additional
creatures whenever you succeed at the appropriate [Survival](ref/skill/survival) check, and Survival
is a class skill for you.
