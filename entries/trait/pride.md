**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** You can't abide challenges to your dignity, authority, or honor.

**Benefit:** When someone threatens, accuses, or challenges you, you take a -2 penalty on
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks involving
that creature until the creature apologizes to you.
