**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Triaxian](ref/race/triaxian)

**Flavor:** You know how dragons move and how they perceive body language.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus to your CMD against dragons, and whenever you encounter a
particular dragon for the first time, you can make a [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check to
improve its attitude toward you by a maximum of one step as a **full-round action** as long as you
have not acted in a hostile manner toward it.
