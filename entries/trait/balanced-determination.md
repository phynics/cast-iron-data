**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You've come to appreciate the balanced approach taken by sovereign dragons.

**Benefit:** Once per day, before attempting a saving throw against a spell ability originating from
another creature, you can invoke the determination of
[sovereign dragons](ref/family/dragon-imperial-sovereign). You take 10 on the saving throw (treat
the d20 die result as if it were a 10).
