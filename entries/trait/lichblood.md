**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Orc](ref/race/orc), [undead](ref/blrgbloodline/undead) bloodrager bloodline

**Region:** Belkzen

**Flavor:** You are among the fabled lichblood orcs, and so you command respect among the orcs of
Belkzen.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks made against orcs of Belkzen.
