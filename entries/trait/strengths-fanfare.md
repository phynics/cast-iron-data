**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Kurgess](ref/faith/kurgess)

**Flavor:** Songs of your glory and prowess bolster your strength.

**Benefit:** When you are the subject of any sonic effect that grants a morale bonus on attack or
damage rolls, you also gain a +1 trait bonus on Strength-based skill checks; on combat maneuver
checks to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [grapple](ref/rule/grapple), and
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition); and to your CMD against those combat maneuvers.
