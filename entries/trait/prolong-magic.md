**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** Constant drills and preparation allow you to get more out of your innate magic.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) gained through
your tiefling heritage, it automatically acts as if affected by the
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) metamagic feat.
