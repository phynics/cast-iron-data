**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You have endured many hardships throughout your life, and this has hardened you to the
suffering of others to the point that you are not easily swayed by a sad story.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks that oppose
the [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks of creatures that attempt to deceive you by winning your
sympathy.
