**Prerequisite:** Member of the Cheliax faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your many years spent studying the art of summoning have given you a unique knowledge of this subtle
and complicated discipline. Once per day, when casting a spell of the Conjuration school, treat your
caster level as two higher when determining the duration of the spell.
