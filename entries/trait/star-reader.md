**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Sodden Lands

**Flavor:** You are one of the few remaining descendants of the lost civilization of Lirgen. Growing
up learning of your people's history has granted you a fine understanding of celestial divination as
well as a deep-seated hatred of the demon-worshiping monsters that have come to rule your ancestral
lands.

**Benefit:** While under starlight, the caster level of all your divination spells is increased
by 1. If the target of your spell is a [demon](ref/family/demon), the caster level is increased
by 2.
