**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** Your exposure to teaching from the [Houses of Perfection](ref/rule/aff-monasteries)
makes your attacks with monk weapons even more deadly.

**Benefit:** Select one [monk](ref/class/monk) weapon with which you are proficient (unarmed attacks
do not qualify for this purpose). You gain a +1 trait bonus on damage rolls with this weapon.
