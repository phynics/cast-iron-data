**Exemplar:** The Traveling Hearts, Amiriel and Pai Sy

You have learned the ways of many lands. Choose any two skills; each of those skills becomes a class
skill for you. For every other regional trait you have, select an additional skill to treat as a
class skill.

**Exemplar trait:** A character can have only one exemplar trait, and selecting an exemplar trait
takes the place of two regular traits.
