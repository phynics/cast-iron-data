**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Lastwall

**Flavor:** You have studied at Vigil's Crusader War College, and are skilled in both heraldry and
siege tactics.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering)
and [Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) checks, and one of these skills is always a
class skill for you.
