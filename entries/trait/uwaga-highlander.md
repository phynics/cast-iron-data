**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Katapesh

**Flavor:** You were born and raised in western Katapesh, in the rugged badlands and hills of the
Uwaga Highlands. Even if you were born in a city like Solku, you often had to travel the dangerous
trails with your family. Predators, gnolls, and worse haunt the Uwaga Highlands, and you've become
something of an expert at evading them.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. This trait bonus
increases to +2 in hilly or rocky areas.
