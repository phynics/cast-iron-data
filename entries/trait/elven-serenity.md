**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Half-elf](ref/race/half-elf); elf-raised

**Flavor:** You embody the peace of the elven way of life, and apply this philosophy in your
interactions with others.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks to calm other creatures. You need not speak the same
language to attempt a Diplomacy check in this manner.
