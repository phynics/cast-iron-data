**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Ragathiel](ref/faith/ragathiel)

**Flavor:** You were born into a family, nation, or other group that tried to impress evil ideals
upon you, but you were inspired by Ragathiel to live a moral life despite their influence.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to see
through an evil creature's [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) attempts.
