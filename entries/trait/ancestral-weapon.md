**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Numeria

**Benefit:** You have inherited a sacred tribal weapon wielded by your forebears since the days
before the Rain of Stars, and you were trained in its use from a young age. Select either
[cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) or [silver](ref/eq-material/alchemical-silver). You begin
play with a [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) melee weapon made of the material of your
choice. You must be proficient with this weapon, and its combined cost cannot exceed 500 gp. You
gain a +1 trait bonus on attack rolls with weapons made of the selected material.
