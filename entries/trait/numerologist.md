**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Osirion

**Flavor:** Having studied the sciences and other formal academics in your formative years, you
swiftly became interested in the esoteric numerological practices of the ancient Osirian
mathematicians, and know how to apply this science to dungeons.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can attempt a [Perception](ref/skill/perception) check while studying
a trap (a **standard action**). The DC of this check is equal to the
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) DC of the trap - 5. If you succeed, you identify the DC
necessary to disable the trap, as well as the DC of the Reflex save (if any) to avoid its effects.
