**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae)

**Flavor:** You strive to spread the Dawnflower's message of kindness and compassion throughout the
land, no matter how dangerous the road may be.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), _fervor,_ or
[lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) to heal a living creature, you can reroll any healing die
roll that results in a natural 1 (you must use the new result).
