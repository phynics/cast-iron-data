**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Razmiran

**Flavor:** Priests of Razmiran often make subtle use of potions to hide the fact that they do not
receive spells from their deity, and they have become quite adept at imbibing potions
surreptitiously. You have mastered these techniques.

**Benefit:** Whenever you consume a potion, you can attempt a
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check with a +2 trait bonus opposed by onlookers'
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to hide the fact that you are drinking a potion (as if you
were using Sleight of Hand to hide an object). The observer's success doesn't prevent you from
drinking the potion, just from doing so unnoticed. This does not hide any visible effects that the
potion might have on you, only the physical act of drinking the potion.
