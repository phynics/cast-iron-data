**Type:** Regional

**Region:** urban

**Flavor:** You are disconnected from your heritage and do what you can to learn more about where
your family is or where your people came from.

**Benefit:** [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) is always a class skill for you, and you
can always take 10 on Knowledge (local) checks, even when stress or distractions would normally
prevent you from doing so.
