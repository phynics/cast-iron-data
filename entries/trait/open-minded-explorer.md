**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Averaka

**Flavor:** You grew up constantly interacting with people who speak unfamiliar languages and have
diverse customs; your manners may not be polished, but your acceptance of others is genuine.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks. This bonus
increases to +2 when you're speaking the racial or ethnic language of the target creature.
