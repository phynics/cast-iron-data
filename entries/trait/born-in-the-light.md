**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Dhampir](ref/race/dhampir)

**Flavor:** The circumstance of your birth and upbringing left little room to accommodate your
weaknesses, and many hours toiling in broad daylight have decreased the severity of your innate
hindrances.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on checks and saves affected by the [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance)
feat (this bonus does not stack with the bonus granted by the Endurance feat). When your
_[light sensitivity](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/light-sensitivity)_ causes
you to become [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled), you do not take the -1 penalty on sight-based
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks.
