**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold)

**Flavor:** Though your light pink scales made you the subject of ridicule by your tribe, you can
adapt to more environments than they can.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks, and Survival is always a
class skill for you. Most kobolds with carnation scales also have the _day raider_ alternate racial
trait.
