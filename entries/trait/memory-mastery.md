**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Gillman](ref/race/gillman)

**Flavor:** You can suppress your own memories to help tell more convincing lies.

**Benefit:** Once per day as an **immediate action**, you can cause yourself to forget a single
event or fact for a period of 10 minutes. Enemies reading your mind cannot learn the information.
Until the memory returns, you gain a +10 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to convince
others that you do not know the information.
