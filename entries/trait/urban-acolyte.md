**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** Growing up in a city has broadened your philosophy, and helped you interpret your
patron's divine will in a novel way.

**Benefit:** You gain [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) as a class skill. Additionally,
you can select one of the following subdomains using the normal rules for subdomains so long as your
deity grants access to its associated domain: Competition, [Duels](ref/domain/war), Espionage,
[Fortifications](ref/domain/protection), Industry, Legislation, Plague, or Riot.
