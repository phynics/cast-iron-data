**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Suli](ref/race/suli)

**Flavor:** You inherited your janni parent's knack for reversing a tense situation.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can treat an [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) roll to improve a
creature's attitude toward you as if you had used [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) instead. You must
use this ability after making the check but before the GM has revealed the result. If you continue
threatening the target after making the check, the check automatically fails.
