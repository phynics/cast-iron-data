**Type:** Campaign

**Campaign:** Serpent's Skull

**Flavor:** This trip to Eleder is just one of many in a long career as a sailor aboard the Jenivere
and other cargo vessels. Whether you joined the crew with the hope of one day captaining your own
ship, as the means of paying your way to a foreign port with sweat and blood, or as a slave or
indentured servant, you now call the ship home.

Characters with this trait have likely been on the Jenivere since it departed Magnimar.

**Benefit:** Pick one of the following skills: [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics),
[Climb](ref/skill/climb), [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature),
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography), [Swim](ref/skill/swim), or
[Survival](ref/skill/survival). You gain a +1 trait bonus on that skill, and that skill is always
treated as a class skill for you.
