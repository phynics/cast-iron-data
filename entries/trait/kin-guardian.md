**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You are dedicated to defending members of your family.

**Benefit:** When you use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to give a member of your
family a bonus to AC, increase the bonus by 2. This increase is a trait bonus (and therefore doesn't
stack with increases granted by other family members using this trait). This trait has no effect
when using the aid another action to increase a family member's next attack roll.
