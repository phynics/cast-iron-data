**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa)

**Benefit:** Your experience with undead creatures gives you a +2 trait bonus on
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when dealing with undead, as well as a +2 trait bonus on
Charisma checks to influence undead (such as with the [command undead](ref/spell/command-undead)
spell).
