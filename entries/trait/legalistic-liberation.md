**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Region:** Molthune or Osirion

**Flavor:** You followed - or some might say exploited - the rules and earned your freedom by
finding the loopholes within your homeland's labyrinthine codes regulating slavery and indentured
servitude.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on all [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) checks to find a
legitimate way to avoid, negate, or escape an existing obligation.
