**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); White-scaled

**Flavor:** The icy breath of a white dragon fills your lungs.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can quickly exhale to produce a frigid cloud of dense fog that
quickly sinks to the ground. This freezing mist covers a 10-foot-square area with slippery ice
that's as difficult to traverse as an [ice sheet](ref/rule/cold-dangers).
