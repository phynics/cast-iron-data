**Prerequisite:** Member of the Taldor faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You spent your formative years as a young blade in Oppara and learned the ins and outs of using
fashion to improve your relations with others. So long as you are wearing clothing and jewelry worth
more than 80 gp, you gain a +1 trait bonus on Bluff, Diplomacy, and Sense Motive checks. One of
these skills (your choice) becomes a class skill for you.
