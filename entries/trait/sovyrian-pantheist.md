**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf)

**Religion:** Elven Deities

**Flavor:** Strongly influenced by the elves' mysterious other realm, you embrace obscure elven
deities that have little influence on Golarion.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks
to recognize or evaluate art,
[Knowledge (architecture and engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering) checks,
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to hunt game, Constitution checks to [run](ref/rule/run), and
[Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks involving crystals or gems.
