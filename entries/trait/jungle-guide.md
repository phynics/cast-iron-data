**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Sargava

**Flavor:** You've made your living outfitting and guiding expeditions deep into the Mwangi interior
in search of ancient ruins and lost cities.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks, and a +1
trait bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks in jungle terrain. One of these skills is a
class skill for you.
