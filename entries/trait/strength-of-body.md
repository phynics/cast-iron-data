**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Wereboar-kin

**Flavor:** You took longer than most to learn control over your transforming abilities, but in that
time you learned a trick to make the most of your strength.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can use your Strength modifier in place of your Constitution modifier
on a single Fortitude saving throw. You must announce that you are using this ability before you
roll the saving throw.
