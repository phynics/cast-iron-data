**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** Kelish

**Region:** Qadira

**Flavor:** Qadira is a land of poetry, and you have grown up immersed in the words and language of
that culture. Your facility with the language is evident to all when you speak.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks when speaking with anyone fluent in Kelish.
