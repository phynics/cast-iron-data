**Prerequisite:** Member of the Grand Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You know the consequences of failure and strive never to make the same mistake twice. Once per
scenario, when you roll a natural 1 on any saving throw, you gain a +1 trait bonus on your next
saving throw, which must be used before the end of the scenario.
