**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Trox](ref/race/trox)

**Flavor:** You fought off devious magic in order to escape duergar captors.

**Benefit:** Choose two of Fortitude, Reflex, or Will saves. You gain a +1 trait bonus on saves of
the types you chose against spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and you
take a -1 penalty on all saves of the remaining type.
