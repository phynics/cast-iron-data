**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mediogalti

**Flavor:** You've spent a great deal of time hunting down and following treasure maps - most false,
but a few with legitimate information.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) or
[Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) (sailor) checks made to navigate using a map or chart, and
one of them becomes a class skill for you.
