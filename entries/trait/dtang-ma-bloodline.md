**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Dtang Ma

**Flavor:** As you were born in Dtang Ma and possess one of the favored bloodlines, your sorcerous
powers are particularly potent.

**Benefit:** Select one of the following sorcerer bloodlines: [fey](ref/sorcbloodline/fey),
[maestro](ref/sorcbloodline/maestro), [starsoul](ref/sorcbloodline/starsoul), or
[stormborn](ref/sorcbloodline/stormborn). Once per day, you may use one of your bloodline powers as
if your sorcerer level were 1 higher than normal. This trait does not give you access to bloodline
powers whose level requirements you do not yet meet.
