**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar)

**Flavor:** Having been raised among other aasimars, you have a stronger connection to your planar
heritage than most of your kind, and have more thoroughly explored your magical talent.

**Benefit:** You may expend your once-per-day [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) to
spontaneously cast [cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds) (CL equal to your character
level), but doing so is draining and you take an amount of nonlethal damage equal to half the number
of hit points you heal.
