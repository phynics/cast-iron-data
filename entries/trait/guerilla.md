**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Dtang Ma

**Flavor:** You have spent much time in the rainforests of Dtang Ma, and know its secrets.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) checks as
well as [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made in the rainforest. One of these skills becomes a
class skill for you.
