**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Rahadoum

**Flavor:** You have spent time among the famed Pure Legion and understand some of their techniques
for spotting signs of religion and the faithful.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to see through
disguises and mundane attempts to conceal religious iconography or dress. You also gain a +2 trait
bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to see through lies about a target's
religious beliefs and to understand secret messages relating to the gods.
