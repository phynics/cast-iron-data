**Prerequisite:** Member of the Lantern Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your long journeys between the Inner Sea and Tian Xia have exposed you to a wide variety of campfire
tales. Once per scenario, you can make a Knowledge check with a trait bonus equal to your
Intelligence modifier plus 3 (minimum +1). You can make this check even if you do not have ranks in
the required Knowledge skill.
