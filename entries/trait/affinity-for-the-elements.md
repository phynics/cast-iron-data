**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** Elemental Lords

**Benefit:** Add 1 to the DC of saving throws made to resist the effects of spells cast by you that
have either the air, earth, fire, or water descriptor, depending on which elemental lord you
worship. You must choose a single elemental lord when you take this trait; once chosen, it cannot be
changed.
