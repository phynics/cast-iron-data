**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Your birth came at a time when the sun was auspiciously aligned with your birth sign. As
a result, you have a natural affinity for light and life.

**Benefit:** Whenever you're affected by a healing effect that would heal more hit points than your
maximum hit point total, you gain the excess healing as
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points). You can gain up to a number of temporary hit
points per day equal to your character level in this way. These temporary hit points last for 1
minute.
