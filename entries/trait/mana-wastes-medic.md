**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mana Wastes

**Flavor:** After witnessing a terrible disaster result from an attempt at magical healing in the
Mana Wastes, you are careful and methodical when crafting your own healing tinctures.

**Benefit:** The amount of hit points restored by your [Heal](ref/skill/heal) checks to treat deadly
wounds and your extracts of the healing subschool is increased by 1.
