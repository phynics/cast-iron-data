**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** You venerate nature, either by paying homage to your Shoanti totem spirit or by
appeasing the [kami](ref/family/kami) around you in accordance with Tamashigo. In return, the
spirits bless your path.

**Benefit:** Once per week, you can build a small shrine of natural materials and pay your respects
in a ritual that takes 15 minutes. Spellcasters can do this as part of their daily spell
preparation. You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks for 24 hours.
