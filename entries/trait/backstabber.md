**Type:** Religion

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Religion:** [Thamir Gixx](ref/faith/thamir-gixx)

**Flavor:** Pledging your faith to [Norgorber's](ref/faith/norgorber) lethal shadow gives you the
skill to make a brutal attack against an opponent who cannot fully fend you off.

**Benefit:** When you hit a foe you are [flanking](ref/rule/flanking), you deal an additional 1
point of damage (this damage is added to your base damage, and is multiplied on a critical hit).
This additional damage is a trait bonus.
