**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Carpenden

**Flavor:** Your regular participation in the moonmelon festival has made you wickedly effective at
hurling harmless objects.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on attack rolls when throwing items that do not deal normal
hit point damage (such as weapons that deal nonlethal damage or items such as
[tanglefoot bags](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag) and [thunderstones](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone)).
