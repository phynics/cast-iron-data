**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You came of age in a place with harsh winters amid a culture that rewarded you for
testing your limits in the cold.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against cold effects and a +2 trait bonus on
Fortitude saves against the effects of cold weather.

**Suggested Characters:** Irriseni, Kellids, Ulfen.
