**Type:** Regional

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Region:** Cheliax or Katapesh

**Flavor:** As a former slave, you learned how to conceal your malice toward your owner behind a
facade of clumsiness.

**Benefit:** The first time you attack someone with an attack that deals nonlethal damage, your
target must make a [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) opposed by your [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff)
check minus the damage dealt to realize you actually intended to harm him; failure means he believe
the injury was an accident on your part instead (though he may still want to punish you for it).
