**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mediogalti

**Flavor:** Your parents were both [Red Mantis assassins](ref/pclass/red-mantis-assassin) and your
family has a long tradition of service in the [Red Mantis](ref/rule/aff-assassins) cult. Your
strict, fanatical upbringing and familiarity with murder have bestowed upon you an air of confidence
and menace.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. If you act
during the surprise round, as a **free action** during that round you can draw a light weapon (or
two if you have the [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) feat).
