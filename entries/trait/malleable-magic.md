**Type:** Magic

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **swift action**, you can lose a single 1st-, 2nd- or 3rd-level magus
spell to regain 1 _arcane pool_ point. You can't exceed the maximum number of _arcane pool_ points
in your _arcane pool_ by using this trait.
