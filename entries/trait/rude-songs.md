**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)

**Benefit:** Providing an opponent can hear and understand you, the lyrics of your songs are so
personally insulting that you gain a +2 trait bonus on all [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (sing)
checks. Perform is always a class skill for you.
