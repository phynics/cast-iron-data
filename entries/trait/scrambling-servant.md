**Type:** Regional

**Region:** mountains

**Flavor:** You were hired or captured by giants and found yourself frequently scrambling up and
down giant-sized furniture or slave pit walls. As a result, you are an agile climber.

**Benefit:** You retain your Dexterity bonus to Armor Class while climbing and gain a +1 trait bonus
on [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks.

**Likely Regions:** Osirion, Rahadoum, the Shackles, Thuvia, Varisia.

**Likely giants:** Cave, Fire, River, Slag, Storm
