**Type:** Regional

**Region:** The Worldwound

**Flavor:** You have studied techniques designed to pierce magical protections, with the goal of
assisting your Abyssal masters in bringing down the wardstones that guard the borders of Mendev.

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can attempt a melee touch attack against an opponent that you believe
has a deflection bonus to AC. If your attack is successful, your foe's deflection bonus to AC is
halved (to a minimum of +0) for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier.

You can use this ability twice per day at 10th level and three times per day at 20th level.
