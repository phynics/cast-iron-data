**Prerequisite:** Member of the Scarab Sages faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You've spent most of your life exploring the ancient tombs and catacombs of Scarab Sages. You gain a
+1 bonus on Perception and Knowledge (dungeoneering) checks, and one of these skills (your choice)
is a class skill for you.
