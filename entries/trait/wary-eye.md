**Type:** Combat

**Flavor:** You know that you could be exposed at any time. Even your closest friends could someday
become enemies, and so you have trained yourself to never be surprised by a betrayal.

**Benefit:** At the beginning of combat, before initiative checks are revealed, you can select one
opponent you are aware of to be the subject of this trait. If you succeed at a Wisdom check opposed
by the subject's initiative check, you are not considered [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed)
against this subject for this combat, even if you normally would be.

You can use this trait in a surprise round, even if you cannot otherwise act that round.
