**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Vildeis](ref/faith/vildeis)

**Flavor:** You are blind but trained by followers of Vildeis to sense your surroundings by sound
and touch. You need not attempt a skill check to move at full speed.

**Benefit:** You take a -4 penalty from [blindness](ref/misc/blinded) on only
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics), [Ride](ref/skill/ride),
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand), and [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. You gain
[Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) as a bonus feat. You lose the benefits of this trait whenever
you can see, but regain them if you once again become blind.
