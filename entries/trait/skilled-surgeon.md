**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Qadira or Ustalav

**Flavor:** Whenever you perform surgery or otherwise must deal damage to remove a condition or
affliction, you roll the damage but use the minimum result unless you rolled the maximum result.

**Benefit:** The amount of hit points or points of ability damage restored each day when you provide
long-term care is increased by 1.
