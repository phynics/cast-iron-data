**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Katapesh

**Flavor:** You spent several years addicted to [pesh](ref/drug/pesh), a habit that you may or may
not have kicked.

**Benefit:** You start with only half of the normal starting cash, but your knowledge of the pesh
lifestyle grants you a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks.
One of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
