**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Werebat-kin

**Flavor:** You have always been most comfortable in a group. Since awakening to your werebat
powers, this feeling has only grown.

**Benefit:** As long as you are within 10 feet of at least two allies, you gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and attack rolls to make attack of opportunity.
