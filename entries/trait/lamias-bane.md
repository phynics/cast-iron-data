**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** You have been exposed to the magical energies of lamias in your past, or you have heard
innumerable stories about your family's dealings with lamias, and you have built up a resistance to
their charms.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against enchantment effects and a +2 trait
bonus on saving throws against the spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
of [lamias](ref/monster/lamia).
