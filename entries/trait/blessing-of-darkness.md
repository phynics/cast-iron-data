**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** Your innate connection with the powers of darkness serves you well when evil zealots
pray on your behalf.

**Benefit:** Whenever a spellcaster capable of channeling negative energy casts a beneficial spell
on you, she acts as if she were one level higher for the purpose of determining that spell's
effects.
