**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Geb

**Flavor:** As a child, you somehow got your hands on a lurid, forbidden text written by a
now-forgotten Blood Lord of ages past. You studied the details within intently, learning much about
extraplanar undead from beyond this reality, and you developed a strange, almost macabre way of
thinking because of this book's influence.

**Benefit:** Anytime you would roll a [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) check, you
may roll a [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) or
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) check instead.
