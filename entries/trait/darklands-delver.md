**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Darklands beneath Tian Xia

**Flavor:** You are familiar with the byways and passages of Tian Xia's underworld.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on
[Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering) checks, and a +1 trait bonus on
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made in the Darklands. One of these skills becomes class skill
for you.
