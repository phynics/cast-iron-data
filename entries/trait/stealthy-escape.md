**Type:** Equipment

**Flavor:** You are adept at escaping from confinement in an unobtrusive manner.

**Benefit:** When trying to escape manacles, rope, or other similar restraints (but not grapples or
similar attacks), you may make a [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check instead of your
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) modifier. If you free yourself, you gain a +2 trait bonus
on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to pretend that you are still bound.
