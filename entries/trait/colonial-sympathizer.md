**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Mwangi

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks involving Sargavan colonials and others with this trait.
