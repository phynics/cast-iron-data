**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Oread](ref/race/oread)

**Flavor:** You were inexplicably transformed in the womb by elemental forces, making your
connection to the earth exceptionally strong.

**Benefit:** As a **swift action**, you can gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) to a range of 60
feet until the beginning of your next turn. You can use this ability once per day at 1st level, plus
one additional time per day at 5th level and every 5 levels thereafter, to a maximum of five times
per day at 20th level.
