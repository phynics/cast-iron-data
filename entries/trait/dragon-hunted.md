**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** You've survived being hunted by the draconic nightmares on the Shackles islands of
Dahak's Fang, Dahak's Horn, or Dahak's Tooth.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Will saves against fear effects, and against any saving
throw from a spell or effect created by a dragon.
