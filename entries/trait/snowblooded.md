**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Elf](ref/race/elf); Snowcaster

**Flavor:** Your family has a long tradition of producing excellent sentries and
[twilight speakers](ref/arc-skald/twilight-speaker).

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on Fortitude saving throws made to avoid nonlethal damage
from cold environments, and gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against spells with the cold
descriptor.
