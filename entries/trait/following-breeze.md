**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Sylph](ref/race/sylph)

**Flavor:** You always seem to be chased by drafts of moving air, and know exactly the right moment
and method to lift yourself upon the breezes.

**Benefit:** As long as you get a running start, you gain a +2 trait bonus on
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks made to jump. Your speed increases by 5 feet when you use
the [run](ref/rule/run) action.
