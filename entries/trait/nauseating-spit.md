**Type:** Mount

**Prerequisite:** Have a [camel](ref/eq-misc/camel) as a mount

**Benefit:** Once per hour, a Keleshite trade camel can regurgitate the contents of its stomach,
spitting the foul material at a single target within 10 feet as a ranged touch attack. The target
must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half the camel's Hit Dice + the camel's Constitution
modifier) or be [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round.
