**Type:** Faith

**Flavor:** You were inspired with the reverence offered by worshipers to priests and other
spiritual leaders, so you assisted them.

**Benefit:** You gain a +3 trait bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to get
hunches about those who discuss matters of faith, mythology, morality, religion, or the Outer Planes
with you. Sense Motive is a class skill for you.
