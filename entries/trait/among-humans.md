**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kitsune](ref/race/kitsune)

**Flavor:** You have spent your entire life living among humans and are an expert at impersonating
them.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks to appear human
while assuming your specific human form with the
_[change shape](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/change-shape)_ racial trait, and
Disguise is always a class skill for you.
