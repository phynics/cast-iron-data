**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Blue-scaled

**Flavor:** You're blessed with a devious mind, and the more elaborate your traps are, the more
effective they become.

**Benefit:** When creating a trap, if your [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (trap) roll exceeds the DC
of the check by 5 or more, that trap's Disable Device DC increases by 2.
