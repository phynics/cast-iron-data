**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Garundi

**Flavor:** You naturally observe others, and move to complement them.

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **move action**, you can select an adjacent ally. For the next
minute, you can take 10 on attack rolls and skill checks to use the
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to aid that ally, even if you are distracted.
