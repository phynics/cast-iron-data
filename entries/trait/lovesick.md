**Type:** Drawback

**Flavor:** Your love for another occupies your every thought, and you become despondent when you
are away from that person.

**Benefit:** The person is chosen by your GM (with consideration to your roll on the Romantic
Relationships table if you used the [background generator](ref/rule/background-generator)). At the
start of each day, if you are away from this person you take a -2 penalty on initiative checks and
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks. If you ever lose or fall out of love with this person,
exchange this drawback for the [Doubt](ref/trait/doubt) drawback.
