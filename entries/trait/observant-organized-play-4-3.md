**Prerequisite:** Member of the Grand Lodge faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Sometimes it helps to pay attention to your surroundings and the people you meet. Choose either
Perception or Sense Motive. You gain a +1 trait bonus on all checks for the chosen skill, and the
chosen skill becomes a class skill for you.
