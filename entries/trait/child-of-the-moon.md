**Type:** Magic

**Flavor:** Your lunar gift becomes much more powerful when the moon is full.

**Benefit:** Choose either [Climb](ref/skill/climb), [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth), or
[Swim](ref/skill/swim). You gain a +1 trait bonus on checks made with the chosen skill. On dates
during and after the first quarter of a lunar phase but before end of the last quarter (the half of
the lunar cycle where the moon is fuller), this bonus increases to +2. During the full moon, this
bonus increases to +4.
