**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Kols](ref/faith/kols)

**Flavor:** Your respect for the letter of the law bleeds into your spellcasting.

**Benefit:** Any spells you cast that prescribe certain behaviors with a consequence for breaking
these directives (such as [mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice) or
[geas](ref/spell/geas-quest)) have their save DC increased by 1.
