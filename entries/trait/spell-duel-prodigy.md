**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Erages

**Flavor:** You've participated in many spell duels during your time at Erages Academy, and You've
developed a keen talent for identifying magical arts.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 trait bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks to identify a
spell as it is being cast and on dispel checks to [counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) a spell.
