**Prerequisite:** Member of the Sovereign Court faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Throughout your youth, you spent countless hours perfecting the art of the duel, focusing your
efforts on defeating a single foe. You gain a +1 trait bonus to your Armor Class so long as you are
adjacent to a single foe. This trait bonus is not applied to your Armor Class against touch attacks
or when you are denied your Dexterity bonus.
