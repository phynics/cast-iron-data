**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Red-scaled

**Flavor:** You've spent so much of your life around fires that smoke no longer affects you the way
it does other creatures.

**Benefit:** Your vision isn't impaired by nonmagical smoke, and you gain a +5 trait bonus on
Fortitude saves to avoid coughing and choking because of [smoke inhalation](ref/rule/forest-fires).
This trait confers no benefits against magically created smoke, such as that produced by
[pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics).
