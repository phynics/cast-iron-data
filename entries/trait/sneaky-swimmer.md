**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold); Black-scaled

**Flavor:** Swimming comes naturally to you, as does hunting marsh vipers and other aquatic
creatures.

**Benefit:** Increase the number of rounds you can [hold your breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath)
underwater by an amount equal to your Constitution score. You gain a +2 bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks in swamps and in underwater environments.
