**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar); Emberkin

**Flavor:** You're especially adept at casting flame-based spells.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on damage rolls for any spell you cast with the fire
descriptor. Spells that do not deal damage do not benefit from this trait.
