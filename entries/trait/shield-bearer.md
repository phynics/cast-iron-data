**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Human](ref/race/human); Ulfen

**Flavor:** You have survived many battles thanks to your skill with your shield.

**Benefit:** When performing a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash), you deal 1 additional point of
damage. Also, once per day on your turn as a **free action**, you may provide one adjacent ally a +2
trait bonus to his AC. This bonus lasts for 1 round, so long as you and the target remain adjacent
to one another. You can only use this ability if you are using a shield. You retain your shield
bonus to your armor class when using this ability.
