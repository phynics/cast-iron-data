**Prerequisite:** Member of the Dark Archive faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

You have spent years handling magic items and know how to test their functions while avoiding
catastrophic results. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Use Magic Device checks, and this skill is a
class skill for you.
