**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Mwangi Expanse

**Flavor:** You are trained in a Mwangi tradition of herbalism dating back to the Age of Darkness
and therefore have great insight into how to use the natural ingredients at your disposal to nurse
an ailing body.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 trait bonus on [Heal](ref/skill/heal) checks when providing long-term
care, and Heal is a class skill for you.
