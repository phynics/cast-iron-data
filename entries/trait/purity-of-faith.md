**Type:** Religion

**Religion:** [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae)

**Flavor:** Your soul is free from impurity, and you are deeply committed to fulfilling your duties
to the church.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Will saving throws and a +1 trait bonus on saving throws
against spells and effects originating from an outsider with the evil subtype.
