**Type:** Regional

**Region:** Goka

**Flavor:** Your exposure to the many peoples intersecting in Goka and the tongues spoken there has
given you a particular facility for speech.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks, and
Linguistics becomes a class skill for you.
