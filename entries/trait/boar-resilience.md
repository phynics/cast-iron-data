**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker); Wereboar-kin

**Flavor:** You were victimized by bullies or even abusive adults. Although the harassment got worse
when your wereboar features became more obvious, those features also gave you fortitude that helped
you resist it.

**Benefit:** You are unimpaired by being at exactly 0 hit points. In addition, you can ignore the
[Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) prerequisite for the [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard) feat.
