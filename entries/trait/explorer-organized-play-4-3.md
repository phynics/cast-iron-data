**Prerequisite:** Member of the Andoran faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your family heritage is filled with proud and renowned explorers, and you are a natural trailblazer.
You gain a +1 trait bonus on Survival checks, and Survival becomes a class skill for you.
