**Type:** Social

**Flavor:** Your experiments - both your successes and failures - have inured you to the effects of
stored magic.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on saving throws against effects produced by
[spell completion](ref/misc/spell-completion) and [spell trigger](ref/misc/spell-trigger) items.
