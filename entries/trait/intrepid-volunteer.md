**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling)

**Flavor:** Like many free halflings, you volunteered readily for military service or an adventuring
career and met other halflings who showed you how to make up for your small size.

**Benefit:** Choose one Strength-based skill or one combat maneuver. You can use your Dexterity
modifier in place of your Strength modifier to calculate your total modifier for that skill check or
combat maneuver check.
