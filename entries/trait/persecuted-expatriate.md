**Type:** Race

**Prerequisite:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling)

**Flavor:** You were born in a nation that persecuted you for your heritage. Although you were under
terrible duress, you escaped either with the aid of family or by your own cunning.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on one skill depending on your fiendish heritage: asura
([Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local)), daemon
([Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand)), demodand ([Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)), demon
([Perception](ref/skill/perception)), devil ([Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive)), div
([Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy)), kyton ([Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist)), oni
([Disguise](ref/skill/disguise)), qlippoth ([Survival](ref/skill/survival)), or rakshasa (Sense
Motive). That skill is always a class skill for you.
