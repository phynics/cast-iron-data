**Prerequisite:** Member of the Sovereign Court faction

**Campaign:** Pathfinder Society

Your grandiose posturing often makes it difficult for anyone to concentrate around you. Once per day
as a full-round action, you can attempt to distract adjacent foes with a lengthy display of your
martial prowess. All adjacent foes must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your level + your
Charisma modifier) or gain the shaken condition. This condition persists for 1 round.
