The earth elementalist draws power from the stone around him, shaping it, shattering it, and bending
it to his will. He can use it to defend himself or cause it to rise up and crush his foes.

**Earth Supremacy (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** Earth and stone do not block the line of effect of your spells, although they do
still block your line of sight.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You gain an enhancement bonus to your CMD to resist [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush),
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition), [trip](ref/rule/trip), and [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) attempts
as long as you are touching the ground. This bonus is equal to 2 + one-fifth of your wizard level.
In addition, you gain a +1 insight bonus on melee attack and damage rolls whenever both you and your
foe are touching the ground.

**Acid Cloud (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can create a 5-foot-radius cloud of acid vapor within 30 feet that lasts for 1
round. This cloud deals an amount of acid damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your wizard level to all creatures in the cloud and
[sickens](ref/misc/sickened) them for 1 round. A successful Fortitude save negates the sickened
effect and halves the damage. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 your wizard level + your
Intelligence modifier. Creatures that begin their turn inside the cloud can move out that turn
without penalty, but those that enter the cloud are affected.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Earth Glide (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to move through earth, dirt, and stone. You cannot move through
worked earth or stone; only natural substances can be traversed. If your total duration expires
before you exit the earth, you are flung back to the point where you entered the stone, take 4d6
points of damage, and are [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round. Your burrowing does not leave a
hole, nor does it give any sign of your presence (although you can be detected by creatures with
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense)).

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per wizard level

### Magma School

**Sources:** Elemental Master's Handbook pg 21\
**Associated School:** Earth or Fire.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school powers replace the _acid cloud_ and
_[earth glide](ref/spell/earth-glide)_ powers of the earth school.

**Magma Furrow (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can send forth a 20-foot line of magma. Any creature in this line takes an amount
of fire damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your wizard level. A
successful Reflex save (DC = 10 + half your wizard level + your Intelligence modifier) halves this
damage. In addition, the ground in this area remains molten for another 1d4 rounds. Creatures that
begin their turns in the area can move out that turn without penalty, but those that enter the area
each take 1d6 points of fire damage.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Lava Shield (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can create a barrier of lava to protect you for 1 round. Choose one edge of your
space. That edge is treated as a solid wall for attacks targeting you only. You gain total
[cover](ref/rule/cover) from attacks that pass through this edge, and you gain the benefits of
[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield) (warm shield) while adjacent to the lava shield. You can
dismiss the lava shield as a free action, and it disappears immediately if you leave your space.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per 2 wizard levels

### Mud School

**Sources:** Elemental Master's Handbook pg 21\
**Associated School:** Earth or Water.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school powers replace the _acid cloud_ and
_[earth glide](ref/spell/earth-glide)_ powers of the earth school.

**Hindering Muck (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can create a 15-foot cone of sticky mud. Any creature in the cone must succeed at
a Reflex save (DC = 10 + half your wizard level + your Intelligence modifier) or become
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) for 1 round for every wizard level you have. An affected creature
can take a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity to wipe off enough of the mud to
remove the entangled condition. Jumping into a large body of water or being subject to an effect
that creates a substantial amount of water (such as a casting of
[create water](ref/spell/create-water)) removes all the mud on a creature.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Mud Missile (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can fling a ball of hardened mud at a foe within 30 feet. If you succeed at a
ranged touch attack, the mud slams into the target's face, dealing 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage
for every 2 wizard levels you have and [blinding](ref/misc/blinded) the target for 1d4 rounds. A
creature can remove the blinding mud from itself or another creature with a successful Strength
check (DC = 10 + half your wizard level) as a standard action.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 wizard levels
