Transmuters use magic to change the world around them.

**Physical Enhancement (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** This bonus now applies to two physical ability scores of your choice.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You gain an enhancement bonus to one physical ability score (Strength, Dexterity, or
Constitution). This enhancement bonus is equal to 1 + [one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding)
of your wizard level. You can change this bonus to a new ability score when you prepare spells.

**Telekinetic Fist (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can strike with a telekinetic fist, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged
touch attack. The telekinetic fist deals an amount of bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your wizard level.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Change Shape (Sp)**

**At 12th Level:** This ability functions like [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3) or
[elemental body II](ref/spell/elemental-body-2).

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can change your shape, functioning like [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)
or [elemental body I](ref/spell/elemental-body-1).

**Usage:** 1 time/day per wizard level

At 5th, 10th, 15th, and 20th levels, a transmuter may learn an [annointing](ref/ability/annointings)
instead of gaining a wizard bonus feat.

### Enhancement School

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 145\
**Associated School:** Transmutation.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school powers replace the _telekinetic fist_ and
_[change shape](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/change-shape)_ powers of the
transmutation school.

**Augment (Sp)**

**At 10th Level:** The enhancement bonus to one ability score increases to +4.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can touch a creature and grant it either a +2 enhancement bonus to a single
ability score of your choice or a bonus to natural armor that stacks with any natural armor the
creature might possess; this natural armor bonus is equal to 1 +
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your wizard level. This augmentation lasts a number
of rounds equal to 1/2 your wizard level (minimum 1 round).

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Perfection of Self (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Swift:** You can grant yourself an enhancement bonus to a single ability score equal to 1/2 your
wizard level (maximum +10) for one round.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per wizard level

### Shapechange School

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 147\
**Associated School:** Transmutation.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school power replaces the _telekinetic fist_ power of the
transmutation school.

**Battleshaping (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** You can shape two natural weapons.

**At 4th Level:** Your natural weapon gains an enhancement bonus on attack and damage rolls equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your wizard level.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Swift:** You grow a single [natural weapon](ref/umr/natural-attacks). The natural weapon lasts for
1 round. You can grow a claw, a bite, or a gore attack. These attacks deal the normal damage for a
creature of your size. You may not grow additional limbs or a tail with this ability.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day
