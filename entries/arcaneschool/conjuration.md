**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 80\
The conjurer focuses on the study of summoning monsters and magic alike to bend to his will.

**Summoner's Charm (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** You can change the duration of all _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_
spells to permanent. You can have no more than one [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell
made permanent in this way at one time. If you designate another
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell as permanent, the previous spell immediately
ends. This increase is not doubled by [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a conjuration (summoning) spell, increase the duration by a number of
rounds equal to 1/2 your wizard level (minimum 1).

**Acid Dart (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash an acid dart targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. The acid dart deals an amount of acid damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your wizard level.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Dimensional Steps (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Standard:** You can use this ability to teleport from one place to another. You can bring other
willing creatures with you, but you must expend an equal amount of distance for each additional
creature brought with you.

**Usage:** You can travel up to 30 feet per wizard level per day. This teleportation must be used in
5-foot increments.

**Usage:** You can travel up to 30 feet per wizard level per day. This teleportation must be used in
5-foot increments.

### Cheliax: Egorian Academy Infernal Binder School

**Sources:** Inner Sea Primer pg 29\
The prestigious Egorian Academy of the Magical Arts in Cheliax's capital city teaches a full
spectrum of magic, both specialized and universalist, but the college is best known for its Infernal
Binding program in the school of conjuration. An infernal binder learns the Names of
[Hell](ref/rule/hell), and can use those powers to summon and bind [devils](ref/family/devil) to do
her bidding. If she makes a single mistake in the binding, she opens herself to an eternity of
torment. Egorian Academy wizards are recognized across Avistan as premier practitioners of the
arcane arts. Many hold high positions in magical colleges across Golarion, but most seek to return
one day to teach at their alma mater in Egorian.

**Suggested Opposition Schools:** Divination, illusion, or transmutation.

**Alignment:** Lawful Evil, Lawful Neutral, Neutral Evil, or Neutral.

**Requirements:** An infernal binder must choose a familiar as her arcane bond at 1st level.

**Associated School:** Conjuration.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school powers replace the _acid dart_ and _dimensional steps_
powers of the conjuration school.

**Planar Knowledge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** As an infernal binder, you gain a +3 bonus on Knowledge (planes) checks.

**Assume Control (Su)**

**At 9th Level:** You can use this ability to bargain with called creatures as if you were the
caster who conjured them.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can attempt to gain control over a summoned creature by disrupting the bond
between it and the caster who summoned it. You must make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level)
equal to 10 + the summoning caster's level. If you know the summoned creature's name, you receive a
+2 circumstance bonus on the check. If the check is successful, you can control the summoned
creature as if you had summoned it for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your wizard level (minimum 1
round). This does not increase the duration of the original summoning. The original summoning caster
can attempt to regain control of the summoned creature as a standard action by making a caster level
check against your caster level + 10. When your control ends, the creature reverts to the control of
its summoner.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Imp Familiar (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** You gain [Improved Familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar) as a bonus feat, and must take
an [imp](ref/monster/devil-imp) as a familiar. The imp replaces your current familiar.

### Creation School

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 144\
**Associated School:** Conjuration.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school powers replace the _acid dart_ power and the
_dimensional steps_ power of the conjuration school.

**Create Gear (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can create any object that weighs no more than 1 pound per wizard level you
possess. The item remains for 1 minute before fading away, although it disappears after one round if
it leaves your possession. Creating an item to an exact specification might require a
[Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) skill check, subject to GM discretion. The object must be made of
simple materials, such as wood, stone, glass, or metal, and cannot contain any moving parts. You
could use this ability to create a [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger), but not a vial of
[alchemist's fire.](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire)

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day

**Creator's Will (Sp)**

**At 12th Level:** This ability improves to [major creation](ref/spell/major-creation).

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can cast [minor creation](ref/spell/minor-creation) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). You can have no more than one minor creation
active at a time. If you cast the spell again, the previous casting immediately ends.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 wizard levels

### Extradimension School

**Sources:** Black Markets pg 26\
**Associated School:** Conjuration.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school power replaces the _summoner's charm_ power of the
conjuration school.

**Reshape Space (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** You can make one spell that creates an extradimensional space or demiplane
permanent. You can have no more than one such spell made permanent in this way at a time. If you
designate another spell as permanent, the previous permanent spell ends.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell that creates an extradimensional space or demiplane, increase
the duration by a number of units (rounds, minutes, hours, etc.) equal to 1/2 your wizard level
(minimum 1).

### Teleportation School

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 147\
**Associated School:** Conjuration.

**Replacement Powers:** The following school power replaces the _acid dart_ power of the conjuration
school.

**Shift (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Swift:** You can teleport to a nearby space as if using
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door). You must be able to see the space that you are moving
into. You cannot take other creatures with you when you use this ability (except for familiars). You
can move 5 feet for every two wizard levels you possess (minimum 5 feet).

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day
