**Sources:** Pathfinder \#39: The City of Seven Spears pg 71, Occult Adventures pg 258\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 36,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

### Description

A greater ganji doll looks similar to and functions as the
[normal version](ref/magic-fetish/ganji-doll), except that it can be used 5 times per day, imposes a
-4 penalty on the target's saving throws against its effects, and has the following altered or
additional special abilities in addition to all the abilities of a ganji doll.

Once the doll has been created, three times per day, any bearer can employ the doll to create any
one of the following effects. These effects only target the creature bound to the doll. The target
takes a -2 penalty on all saving throws against these effects.

**Damage:** Damaging the doll causes the target to take 6d6 points of damage (DC 16 Fortitude save
for half). This damage ignores all resistances and immunities.

**Locate Creature:** The bearer of a ganji doll can use it to locate its bound target, as per the
spell [locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature).

**Suggestion:** The user whispers in the doll's ear, creating the effect of a
_[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ spell. The target must make a DC 16 Will save to resist the
effect. Unlike the spell, the target need not comprehend the user's language.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse), [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion), and a piece of the body
of the humanoid to be affected; **Cost** 18,000 gp
