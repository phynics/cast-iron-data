**Sources:** Pathfinder \#39: The City of Seven Spears pg 71\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 1,800 gp; **Weight** —

### Description

This small glass ornament is usually molded into the shape of an eye or drop. Once per day the
wearer can activate the tear, gaining the ability to
_[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility)_ with a range of 10 feet for 5 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility); **Cost** 900 gp
