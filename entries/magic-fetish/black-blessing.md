**Sources:** Pathfinder \#39: The City of Seven Spears pg 71\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 500 gp; **Weight** —

### Description

This small black pouch typically bears a symbol of a heart, bird's head, or fish scribed in blood.
It grants the wearer a +1 resistance bonus on Fortitude and Reflex saving throws. However, the
wearer also takes a -2 penalty on all Will saves against spells cast by the item's creator.
