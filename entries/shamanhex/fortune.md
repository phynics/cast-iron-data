**&L&Fortune (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** The shaman grants a creature within 30 feet a bit of good luck for 1 round. The target
can call upon this good luck once per round, allowing it to reroll any ability check, attack roll,
saving throw, or skill check, taking the better result. The target creature must to decide to use
this ability before the first roll is made.

**Special:** Once a creature has benefited from the _fortune_ hex, it cannot benefit from it again
for 24 hours.

At 8th level: The duration of this hex 2 rounds.

At 16th level: The duration of this hex 3 rounds.
