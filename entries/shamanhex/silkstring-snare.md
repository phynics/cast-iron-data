**Silkstring Snare (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes ribbons of spider silk to erupt from the ground beneath a single
creature. The target must succeed at a Reflex save or be [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) and
anchored in place. The foe can escape the silk as a **standard action** with a successful
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) or Strength check at the same DC, or by dealing an amount
of damage to the silk equal to double the shaman's class level (the silk has
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 0).

This hex lasts a number of rounds equal to 3 + the shaman's Wisdom modifier.

**Special:** A creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.
