The shaman understands any spoken language for a number of minutes per day equal to her level, as
_[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages)_. This duration does not need to be
consecutive, but it must be spent in 1-minute increments. At 5th level, a shaman can use this
ability to speak any language, as _[tongues](ref/spell/tongues)_.
