A shaman incites a creature within 30 feet into a primal fury. The target receives a +2 morale bonus
on attack rolls and a +2 resistance bonus on saving throws against fear for a number of rounds equal
to the shaman's Wisdom modifier. At 8th and 16th levels, these bonuses increase by 1. Once a
creature has benefited from the fury hex, it cannot benefit from it again for 24 hours.
