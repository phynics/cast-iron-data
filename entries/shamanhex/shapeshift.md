The shaman transforms herself into another form for a number of minutes per day equal to her level,
as _[alter self](ref/spell/alter-self)_. This duration does not need to be consecutive, but must be
spent in 1-minute increments. Changing form (including changing back) is a standard action that
doesn't provoke an attack of opportunity. At 8th level, this ability works as
_[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1)_. At 12th level, this ability works as
_[beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)_. At 16th level, this ability works as
_[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)_. At 20th level, this ability works as
_[beast shape IV](ref/spell/beast-shape-4)_.
