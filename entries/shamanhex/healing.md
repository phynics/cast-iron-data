A shaman soothes the wounds of those she touches. This acts as
_[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds)_, using the shaman's caster level. Once a creature
has benefited from the healing hex, it cannot benefit from it again for 24 hours. At 5th level, this
acts as _[cure moderate wounds](ref/spell/cure-moderate-wounds)_.
