**Kitumu**

**Sub:** Mother of Fireflies

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 316

### Details

**Alignment** CE\
**Pantheon** Other Deities\
**Areas of Concern** Fireflies, hibernation, swarms\
**Domains** [Animal](ref/domain/animal), Chaos, Evil, [War](ref/domain/war)\
**Subdomains** [Blood](ref/domain/war), Demon (Chaos), Demon (Evil), Fear, [Fur](ref/domain/animal),
[Insect](ref/domain/animal)^[1](ref/faith/kitumu#footnote-a)^\
**Favored Weapon** [Dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger)\
**Symbol** Four legged firefly\
**Sacred Animal(s)** Firefly\
**Sacred Color(s)** Black, yellow

## Description

Kitumu is shaped like a firefly, its beetle wings spanning hundreds of feet. She sends plagues of
carnivorous fireflies as she awakens, and only a human sacrifice will calm her again.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-a}

Requires the [Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait.
