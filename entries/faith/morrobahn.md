## Description

The Parasite Seed knows that redundancy is the only insurance. Across countless expanses, it has
cultivated perfect beings - perfect singers, perfect reproducers, perfect killers. Now it seeks to
propagate them to new homes, assuring that no one calamity can wipe out said perfection.
