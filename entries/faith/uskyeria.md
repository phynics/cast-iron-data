## Description

**Sources:** Chronicle of the Righteous pg 55\
Uskyeria's natural form is that of a giant bear with gold-streaked red fur. The Saintly Slumberer
and her agents teach mortals how to hunt while respecting the animals they kill and preserving the
areas in which they hunt. Insomniacs also pray to Uskyeria to bless them with deep and restful
sleep.
