## Description

Ioramvol suffered countless deaths via falls, premature burial, and rockslides before finally being
reborn as Ioramvol with the Mouth Full of Boulders. He appears as a lumbering, tattooed colossus
pierced with shards of rock, and he is capable of spitting jagged rocks from his toothless maw.
