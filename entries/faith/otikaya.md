## Description

A mighty hawk-headed rakshasa, Otikaya the Spirit Archer can supposedly split his spirit into a
thousand pieces and use them to fire on his enemies simultaneously. Some say his true body is
trapped somewhere in the Worldwound and the form he presents is merely a projected image.
