## Description

A cruel and capricious demigoddess, she builds monuments of dust and ice and destroys settlements as
befits her whim. Due to her natural invisibility, few can speak to her true form. Hshurha's schemes
are often so convoluted that they seem senseless, but she is, after all, an ancient entity whose
very nature is as mercurial as the wind.
