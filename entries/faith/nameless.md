## Description

Nameless manifests as a lingering thought in the back of one's mind, a misheard sound, or a vague
sense of movement out of the corner of one's eye. Upon an Empty Throne seeks to crush those who
aspire to power, and doesn't bother to discriminate between the righteous and the wicked.
