## Description

Vibhishah the Seeker spent a thousand lifetimes searching for a single whisker that had fallen from
his face, and when he finally found it, he ascended to immortality. That whisker supposedly holds
captive the soul of one of the wisest shamans to have ever lived, though none recall her name.
