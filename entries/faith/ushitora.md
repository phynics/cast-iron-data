## Description

The Keeper of the Oni Gate dwells in the depths of a remote rift in northern Kaoling, where she
guards the mysterious Oni Gate. Only Ushitora knows where the keys to the Oni Gate lie hidden, or
what dread doom will unfold upon the world when the gate is opened. She appears as a four-armed
[void yai](ref/monster/oni-void-yai) who wields two immense jade-and-gold tetsubos.
