## Description

Lao Shu Po, or Old Rat Woman, was not always a goddess. Once, long ago, she was simply a rat,
skulking in the shadows. When [Fumeiyoshi](ref/faith/fumeiyoshi) killed his brother
[Tsukiyo](ref/faith/tsukiyo), Lao Shu Po crept into the moon god's grave to feed on Tsukiyo's
corpse. She absorbed some of Tsukiyo's divine power, such that when Tsukiyo was returned to life and
Fumeiyoshi was banished from heaven, she was able to steal the portfolio of night from Fumeiyoshi,
becoming the goddess of night, thieves, and rats. Lao Shu Po is the patron of thieves and of many
wayangs, who pray to her to hide their activities in the dark of night. Lao Shu Po appears as an old
hag or as an immense, six-legged rat.
