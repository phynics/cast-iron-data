## Description

Chavazvug appears as a towering tangle of fire-drooling digestive organs that stumbles and crawls on
impossibly long, thin legs. He is unusual among the qlippoth in that his hatred is not of mortal
life but of demonic life. He often invades demonic realms with armies of minions to wage war,
content in the knowledge that if slain, he can grow a new body from one of thousands of boiling
lakes of bile in his hidden lair.
