## Description

To the Red King, there is no perfume sweeter than smoke. He appears as a towering, handsome
[fire yai](ref/monster/oni-fire-yai) with two mouths and four eyes, and he dwells in a scorched
valley that lies in the mountainous border region between Dtang Ma and Xa Hoi.
