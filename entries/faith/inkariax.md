## Description

Stillness, solitude, and stasis: these are the true perfections. The White Death knows the
fleetingness of perfection and seeks to preserve it. Whether in lands gripped by ice, ornate vaults,
or deepest space, frozen-fingered Inkariax hoards and protects all manner of beautiful treasures,
singular notes, and perfect souls against the ravages of entropy.
