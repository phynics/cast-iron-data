## Description

One of the most darkly charming of Hell's many infernal dukes, the Knight of the Laurels commands a
fortress that straddles a cataract of lava in Avernus. An immaculate host, Furcas is also a
pyromaniac herbalist obsessed with creation and destruction and a fiend of immense anger when
roused.
