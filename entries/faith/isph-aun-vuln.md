## Description

One of the most intelligent and subversive of the qlippoth lords, Isph-Aun-Vuln is a monstrous
parasite of the soul who, on the rare occasions she manifests outside of a hideously mutated host
body, appears as a twisting mass of mist-shrouded, dripping tendrils tangled around a fanged
anemone's maw. Isph-Aun-Vuln is particularly fond of using humanoid hosts to lead nations to
suicidal ends through either war or decadence.
