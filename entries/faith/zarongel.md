## Description

Zarongel boasts a magnificent pelt of living fire that was a gift from
[Lamashtu](ref/faith/lamashtu) when he became the first of the barghests to pledge his loyalty to
her. The other barghests remain jealous of Zarongel's gift; [Hadregash](ref/faith/hadregash) in
particular often schemes to steal the fiery pelt for himself. Zarongel taught goblins how to kill
dogs and tame goblin dogs, which is the source of his title, Bark Breaker.
