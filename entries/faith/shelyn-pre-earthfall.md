## Description

As all great art changes throughout time, so has Shelyn. In the early days of Azlant, Shelyn was a
minor goddess of art, beauty, and music. After the death of her mother, a goddess of love, Shelyn
took on her area of concern. She also shared the space in Azlanti religion with her brother
[Dou-Bral](ref/faith/zon-kuthon), and the two were worshiped as one in many cases. They were
inseparable for ages, and ancient depictions show the siblings creating brilliant masterpieces and
singing duets that could bring even the most surly curmudgeon to tears. However, one day the two had
a terrible fight and Dou-Bral departed for spaces unknown. Shelyn was utterly devastated about
losing her brother and feared the worst for his fate. From that point forward, up until the end of
the empire, depictions of Shelyn showed her wearing a veil and dark clothing to mourn the loss of
her sibling. Art in the empire took on a more somber tone in those years. Painters produced works
using dull shades, and musicians played slow introspective songs in somber keys.

Though in the Age of Darkness the Song of the Prism would wrest her brother Dou-Bral's glaive from
him to serve as her favored weapon, in the age of ancient Azlant she favored using a dagger.
