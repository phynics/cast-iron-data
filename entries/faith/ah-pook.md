## Description

Ah Pook is a vile god who preys on the fears of mortals, twisting their doubt and insecurity until
they ultimately self-destruct. This self-destruction can be physical, psychological, or social, with
many victims ruining the lives of others around them alongside their own. This process usually
results in victims losing their loved ones, leaving them even more isolated and miserable, a result
Ah Pook revels in. Ah Pook has many followers throughout Arcadia, each committed to corrupting the
thoughts of others on their god's behalf. Many [sahkils](ref/family/sahkil) also serve the
Destroyer, as it allows them greater opportunities to further the fears of mortals. Arcadians are
well aware of Ah Pook's machinations, and even less destructive fears are seen as the god's work.
Common anxieties and worries are usually countered by small prayers to ward off Ah Pook, in hopes of
relieving these thoughts. The commonality of such fears has earned Ah Pook several names throughout
Arcadia, with those living near Xopatl and Innazpa referring to him as Ohachtsik.
