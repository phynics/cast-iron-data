## Description

Following [Geryon's](ref/faith/geryon) betrayal, Aroggus was one of the elder kytons who led his
people from Hell into the darkness of the Plane of Shadow. There he raised the Abbey of Nevers, a
half-physical kaleidoscope of impossibilities where the kytons could hide forever if necessary. In
the ages since, the Abbey Maker has become one with his refuge, indulging his victims, his
followers, and himself in inescapable delusions of retribution, ever perfecting the one true
revenge.
