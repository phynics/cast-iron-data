## Description

One of the wittiest and most proficient blasphemers in Hell, Sabletongue delights in bringing souls
to his side of their own accord, after he's convinced them to excise all beliefs they held in life.
Fiendish lore holds that Ruzel's tongue is so sharp he can turn living creatures into undead with a
single well-aimed jest.
