## Description

Iggeret claims she was once a mortal who faked her own kidnapping just to see how it would affect
her family and village. After her parents killed themselves out of despair, She Who Was Lost
entombed herself with their bodies to join them in death. Iggeret appears as a young, drowned girl
with a skeletal face.
