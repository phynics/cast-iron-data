## Description

Bohga looted hundreds of abandoned monasteries and temples in the aftermath of the rampage of the
spawn of [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug) known as Kothogaz, but after a millennium meditating upon the
stolen goods, she attained perfection and came to be known as the Treasurer. She appears as a
beautiful but faceless woman made of gold and gems.
