## Description

The feline Beast Who Waits in Smoke, Zurapadyn revels in any act of fiery purification performed for
misguided reasons. He occasionally leaves the immense hellfire volcano he calls home in order to
leap out from pyres and set fire to temples run by self-important acolytes.
