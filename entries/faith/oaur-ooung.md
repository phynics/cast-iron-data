## Description

Pallid and poisonous, Oaur-Ooung is an aquatic fungoid horror of eyes and gasping throats whose
floating roots dangle miles below her like the twitching tendrils of the largest jellyfish
imaginable. [Cyth-V'sug](ref/faith/cyth-vsug) is believed to have been spawned from one of her
blisterwombs. A new blisterwomb is said to be growing upon Oaur-Ooung's flank - her biggest and most
disgusting yet.
