## Description

Andak the Dismembered is a grisly sight - a tortured soul whose limbs have been hewn into multiple
pieces and reattached awkwardly and randomly. His priests practice self-mutilation and seek to
improve upon the physical form via surgery and magical but hideous regeneration.
