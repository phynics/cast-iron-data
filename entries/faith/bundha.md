## Description

The Singing Butcher is an obscenely rotund, boar-headed rakshasa immortal with six tusks and four
ears. Every day he eats nearly his weight in the raw meat of his enemies, preferring to butcher his
victims alive while singing in his rich, unnerving baritone.
