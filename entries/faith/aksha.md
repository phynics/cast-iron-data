## Description

Tales tell that the tiger-headed rakshasa immortal Aksha of the Second Breath was originally a
reincarnation of a sorceress who traded her soul to [Mephistopheles](ref/faith/mephistopheles) for
the ability to breathe hellfire upon her enemies at will. Aksha has two mouths - one through which
she can speak in sibilant, silken tones, and another through which she can breathe terrible cones of
caustic fire.
