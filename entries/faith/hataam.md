## Description

Monstrous creatures who prey on the weak and humanoid cultists who dwell in dreadful mires and fetid
swamps venerate Hataam. They ritually drown or dehydrate those they sacrifice to the River Eater.
Hataam appears as a tangle of flotsam and decayed limbs.
