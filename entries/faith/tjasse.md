## Description

**Sources:** Giants Revisited pg 54\
Proud Tjasse is lord of [rocs](ref/monster/roc), wind, and high mountain peaks. He is attributed
with taming the fearsome rocs to serve [storm giants](ref/monster/giant-storm-giant) and is thought
to be a talented shapeshifter. His arrogance is legendary as one who looks down upon all others,
literally and figuratively. The only love that overcomes his pride is for his beautiful daughter,
[Skode](ref/faith/skode) the huntress. This jealous paternal affection manifests as a wild and
savage protectiveness for person, possessions, and reputation alike, with Tjasse embodying the angry
destructive power of the tempest as the tangible manifestation of brutal vengeance against those who
would dare to cross a storm giant.
