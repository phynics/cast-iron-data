## Description

As one might read a tree's years in the exposed rings of its stump, followers of the Seer in Skin
divine all a mortal's days by scrutinizing the whorls and layers within its flesh. Some even teach
that, if a destined bond exists, a killer's life is reflected in a victim's fateful meat.
