## Description

**Sources:** Chronicle of the Righteous pg 54\
Neshen's followers are those who desire to turn their lives around and embrace the path of light.
The Knight of the Steel Lash may require them to undergo physical duress and difficult obedience, in
a process that can take years, but those who come to him eventually reach their coveted atonement.
