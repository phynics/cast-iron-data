**Sicva**

**Sub:** Mistress of All

### Details

**Alignment** LE\
**Pantheon** Azlanti Pantheon\
**Areas of Concern** Domination, invasive species, and oppression\
**Domains** [Animal](ref/domain/animal), Evil, Law, Nobility, Strength\
**Subdomains** Devil (Evil), Devil (Law), Ferocity, Leadership, Loyalty, Tyranny\
**Favored Weapon** [Lasso](ref/eq-weapon/lasso)\
**Symbol** Virus

## Description

Despite her parents' best efforts to raise her, Sicva ended up a terrible child. Resented her
father, [Elion](ref/faith/elion), for his capricious nature and her mother, [Myr](ref/faith/myr),
for her dedication to charity, she took on the worst aspects of both parents, twisting normally
righteous ideals into instruments of hate and oppression. The Mistress of All represents the parts
of good ideals that can be corrupted, fester, and grow into oppressive dogma.

Sicva represents the boot of oppression and the domination that eventually breaks even the strongest
of wills. She also champions invasive species and charges her followers to spread plants and animals
to areas where they can wreak havoc on local flora and fauna.

Sicva finds worshipers in heavy-handed people who delight in controlling others through brute force
or mental or social subjugation. Priests of Sicva wear authoritative clothing in various shades of
black, gray, and white; these outfits resemble military uniforms more than the cassocks and silk
robes worn by clergy of other faiths.

Worship of Sicva was popular among Azlant's political tyrants and rural despots alike, but the vast
majority of her worshipers were ordinary people from all walks of life who were swept up in the
pageantry of her services and the constant promise that fortune comes to those who go out and take
it. These days, very few people are aware of Sicva and her faith, but tyrants and despots would
certainly find her worship to be to their liking. In the modern age, only a few small cults of Sicva
remain, scattered across Golarion.
