## Description

> **Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 93, Book of the Damned - Volume 2: Lords of Chaos pg 25\
> Sifkesh is unusual among the demons of the Abyss in that her motives seem much more diabolical
> than those of most of her kin. She doesn't seek to destroy the body and ruin the environment, but
> instead works to twist the mind away from purity, to seduce men and women of faith into betraying
> their religions with blasphemies that cause lingering damage to their faith's reputation in
> society. Her greatest pleasure is to linger near a fallen priest when he or she realizes what has
> been done and dies by suicide, for then Sifkesh can snatch away the heretic's soul and consume it.

Sifkesh appears as a human woman with snow-white bird wings and stringy black hair that drips blood.
Her lips and eyes are stitched shut with rusty wire, and her body is cut into sections at the
joints. Usually, these amputations appear at the hips and shoulders, but at times they can appear
elsewhere. Each portion floats independently, not quite moving in sync with the rest. Many believe
Sifkesh was originally a powerful [erinyes](ref/monster/devil-erinyes) devil who became one of
Hell's first heretics. Sifkesh embodies the roles from all three major fiendish races - she corrupts
like a [devil](ref/family/devil), feeds like a [daemon](ref/family/daemon), but is in fact a
[demon](ref/family/demon), a conundrum that has long vexed scholars seeking to impose order upon
their understanding of the demonic.
