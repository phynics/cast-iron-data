**Aerekostes**

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#144: Midwives to Death pg 74

### Details

**Alignment** N\
**Pantheon** Iblydan Hero-Gods\
**Areas of Concern** Contingencies, intelligent items\
**Domains** Artifice, [Healing](ref/domain/healing), Knowledge, Magic\
**Subdomains** Arcane, Construct, Memory, [Resurrection](ref/domain/healing)\
**Favored Weapon** none

### On Golarion

**Nationality** Iblydan

## Other Rules

- Aerekostes can grant 9th-level spells

## Description

Believed to be either the hero-god Ximakter trapped in his own weapon or a blade that spontaneously
manifested sapience and mythic power, this agender [falcata](ref/eq-weapon/falcata) and hero-god of
contingencies and intelligent items resides in its own temple and occasionally lends itself to
aspiring heroes.
