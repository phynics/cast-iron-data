**Bergelmir**

**Domains** Chaos, Community, Good, Knowledge\

**Symbol** Three bolts of lightning\
**Sacred Animal(s)** Sea turtle\
**Sacred Color(s)** Gray, white

## Description

**Sources:** Giants Revisited pg 54\
Matronly Bergelmir is the venerable and staid goddess of wisdom, history, family, and elders, while
sly [Skrymir](ref/faith/skrymir) is a witty wanderer, god of riddles, trickery, wanderlust,
cleverness, and magic; the two are usually portrayed as siblings, cousins, or mother and son.
Together, they represent storm giants' urge for discovery and wandering, yet also their innate roots
to their families and the hearthstone.
