## Description

**Sources:** Chronicle of the Righteous pg 54\
The Lady of the Martyred Womb is the empyreal lord of childlessness. Her mortal followers take vows
to remain childless, believing that the sacrifice of their childbearing ability adds to their
righteousness and proves their devotion. Reymenda rewards her followers with divine powers and often
provides agents to embark on particularly dangerous missions, knowing they will leave no dependents
behind.
