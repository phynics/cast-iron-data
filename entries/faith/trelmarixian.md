## Description

The youngest and most ambitious of the Four Horsemen, Trelmarixian the Black obsesses over the
systematic exploration of soul consumption. In his words, "We are born of souls and destined to
destroy mortality. But we are destined for even greater things thereafter."

Often depicted as a thin, starving man with three jackal heads, crystalline teeth, and black, oddly
fluid or membranous flesh, Trelmarixian in fact has no true form. The Horseman of Famine's physical
body exists as a protoplasmic slurry of bile, blood, and mucus, like a corpse that has decayed and
been reduced to a liquefied, pulpy muck.
