## Description

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 182\
[Bolka's](ref/faith/bolka) brother encourages hard work and harmony, and brings together friends
separated by distance or misunderstanding. Dwarves who form bonds of friendship with members of
other races sometimes present them with gifts of small ornamental hammers in Grundinnar's honor.

**Sources:** Dwarves of Golarion pg 29\
This glib deity is well practiced in breaking up fights over his sister Bolka. He mends fences,
encourages dwarves to be neighborly, and makes sure that dwarves do not forget friends they haven't
seen in decades.
