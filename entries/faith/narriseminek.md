## Description

Narriseminek, sometimes called Intercessor of the Speakers, often manifests as a
[naunet](ref/monster/protean-naunet), [imentesh](ref/monster/protean-imentesh), or
[keketar](ref/monster/protean-keketar) protean, with a circular scar or burn upon its head as if it
had been mutilated by a molten crown. Their rarely observed true form is that of a golden,
iridescent protean with empty eye sockets, crowned with a drifting halo of dozens of burning eyes.
They rarely deal with non-proteans, as they are largely occupied with exalting lesser proteans into
keketars or [izfiitars](ref/monster/protean-izfiitar) as well as granting philosophy-altering
revelations to entire choruses at a time.

The clergy of the Crownless focus on prophecy and transformation, spending their time divining the
future and using transformative magic to alter their bodies and those of any other willing
creatures - an act which the Maker of Kings supports. Members of Narriseminek's clergy have even
been known to physically embody their own patron.

The domain of Narriseminek, whose name is sung and largely incomprehensible to non-proteans, is more
commonly known as the Drifting Mountain of Narriseminek the Crownless. A massive, miles-wide
concretion of stone and crystal with myriad constellation islands, it pulses with a subtle heartbeat
and contains a great subterranean temple of the proteans' dualistic divinity. At the heart of this
domain is a mysterious artifact known as the _Singing-Luminous Eye of the Speakers._
