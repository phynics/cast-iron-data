## Description

Gavidya the Numberless is a cloaked figure who has no fewer than six faces at all times, each
forming from folds on his scalp before gravitating toward the center of his head and competing to be
eaten by his smiling mouth. His cult establishes false prophets who undermine religions with
apostasy and internecine conflict before spreading like viruses to other faiths.
