## Description

Daikitsu is widely worshiped in Tian Xia, as she is the goddess of rice, a staple food in those
lands, as well as of agriculture and craftsmanship. Farmers pray to Daikitsu for good harvests,
smiths and craftsmen seek her blessing for their creations, and families ask for her protection for
their homes and families. Known as the Lady of Foxes, Daikitsu usually appears as beautiful
[kitsune](ref/race/kitsune) woman with snow-white fur and nine tails. She is also the patron of
kitsune, who venerate her as the mother of their race.
