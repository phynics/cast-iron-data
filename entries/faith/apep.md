## Description

Born in the waters of primeval chaos before creation, the malevolent serpent Apep stands in sharp
contrast to the more benevolent serpent goddess Wadjet. Apep is the personification of chaos and
darkness, the hidden dangers that lurk just beneath the surface, the deadly whorls and eddies that
drown and swallow, and the hungry river snakes that prey on those who venture too near the water's
edge. Apep is the raging rapids, the boundless flood that inundates and sweeps away all that mortals
seek to build. He is the darkness in the water's depths that swallows light and life, devouring the
sun and plunging the world into eternal night.

Said to dwell in the Tenth Region of Night, Apep is the great enemy of the sun god
[Ra](ref/faith/ra), and ancient Osirians believed each sunrise was but a temporary victory, for the
Devourer of the Dawn always waits to seize and consume the sun again and again, until one day the
sun shall rise no more and all shall be ended. Apep is a great golden serpent miles long, and his
crushing coils encircle the world. He also appears as a fearsome dragon with night-black scales, and
evil dragons of Osirion venerate him in this form.
