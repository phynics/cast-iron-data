## Description

The most powerful of the oni daimyo of Tian Xia is Inma, the Empress of the World. She dwells in a
fortress nestled in a hidden valley under the highest peak in the oni-ruled nation of Chu Ye. She
appears as dark-skinned, three-eyed, four-armed [void yai](ref/monster/oni-void-yai).
