## Description

This secretive deity shuns contact with most other divine beings, focusing his attention on events
in the mortal world. He constantly urges his followers to war on other races, taking slaves and
resources so they can improve their own equipment and conduct more effective raids. He discourages
fighting between [fire giant](ref/monster/giant-fire-fire-giant) clans and has been known to
relocate entire settlements to prevent such conflicts. Several fire giant "kings" are
[half-fiends](ref/template/half-fiend), usually born of fire giant queens (and apparently the male
giants do not object to this cuckolding). Sculptures of Zursvaater depict a mighty, armored fire
giant with a closed-faced helm, sometimes holding a heavy chain in one hand.
