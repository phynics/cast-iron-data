## Description

The Hunter of Blood is a cunning stalker of prey, master of ambushes, and skilled trapper. He stalks
the vast expanse of the Forest of Spirits, appearing as a flensed
[ogre mage](ref/monster/oni-ogre-mage) whose arrow-perforated body constantly weeps blood.
