## Description

Virtually every Osirian tomb contains images of the jackal-headed god Anubis, the god of
mummification and protector of tombs. He presides over funerals and embalming, and guides souls to
[Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma) to await their judgment in the afterlife, punishing tomb robbers and
defending the dead on their journeys to the Boneyard.

Anubis is the son of [Osiris](ref/faith/osiris) and [Nephthys](ref/faith/nephthys), and assisted
[Isis](ref/faith/isis) with Osiris's mummification. Like his father, Anubis frequently comes into
conflict with [Set](ref/faith/set), particularly regarding that god's association with undeath. As a
guardian of the dead and their tombs, Anubis frequently works with Isis, [Neith](ref/faith/neith),
Nephthys, and [Selket](ref/faith/selket), who protect the canopic jars containing the organs of the
deceased. Anubis usually appears as a man with jet-black skin and the head of a jackal, though he
sometimes takes the entire form of a jackal. Clerics and paladins of Anubis are dedicated to
destroying undead creatures wherever they find them. Anubis is the patron of embalmers, and priests
and embalmers typically wear jackal masks that cover their entire heads in honor of their god.
