## Description

Said to inhabit a realm that constantly teleports to new corners of Stygia, the infernal duke known
as the Wandering carries all manner of hellish terrors under a black cloak formed from undulating
night. A single glance at one of these terrors can supposedly tear the life from a mortal's flesh.
