## Description

A joiner of souls and unifier of skins, Kaikyton seeks to merge parts into greater wholes. The
Stitchweaver left her mark upon Golarion during the Age of Darkness when she created the canyon city
of Kagalko. Ever since, the Choir Vales of Southern Garund have been marred by the miles-long march
of razor-sharp ridges known as Kyton Lace.
