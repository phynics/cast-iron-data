## Description

The Awakener of Wrath is born in the dreaming minds of those whose anger has no outlet, particularly
those who have been imprisoned or oppressed to the point of losing all hope. He appears as a
headless, four-armed man; countless screaming faces swirl in the roiling cloud of black smoke
spewing from the ragged stump of his neck.
