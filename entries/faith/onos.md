## Description

Onos promotes travel through the planes, especially the Inner Planes, as a way of discovery and a
means of witnessing the raw elements that form reality. Onos is difficult for many mortals to
understand, as it is more of an animated force than a being with a distinct personality. When it
speaks to its followers, it is always direct and to the point, not bothering with cryptic parables
or mysterious symbolism.

Onos is the god of embassies, thus many shrines and temples to this deity were found in Azlanti
outposts throughout Golarion, but most places dedicated to the worship of Onos exist elsewhere on
the Inner Sphere, such as the Elemental Planes. The largest and most extravagant temples of Onos are
in the City of Brass and similar planar outposts, serving as safe havens and meeting places for
planar travelers in inhospitable locations.

Priests of Onos are for the most part polite and welcoming, especially to strangers. They bear a
calm temperament, and they and are rarely roused to anger. Though Onos doesn't count law among its
areas of concern, many of its priests serve as mediators, a role that suits them well, for studying
the balance between primal forces is paramount to their faith and understanding of reality.

While Onos was an obscure deity even at the height of the Azlanti Empire, the God of Many Doors
still has many worshipers, though few of them reside on Golarion now. Planar travelers whisper
prayers to Onos each time they call forth a gate or shift between planes of existence.
