> While blindfolded, turn your back to the setting sun and concentrate on what the world might be

## Description

The Dark Mother appears as a lithe [ogre mage](ref/monster/oni-ogre-mage) with gray skin. Rumor
holds that she dwells in an underground complex somewhere below Minkai's capital of Kasai.
