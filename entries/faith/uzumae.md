## Description

Frozen Uzumae dwells in a fortress carved from the icy heart of a glacier in the northernmost
mountains of Tian Xia's Wall of Heaven. It is said that the sound of the wind blowing across the
spires of his realm lures lost travelers to their doom. The Daimyo of the North appears as a
[kuwa oni](ref/monster/oni-kuwa) with frostbitten skin and icicles for teeth and fingernails.
