## Description

There is no beauty in meat. The Voice in Screams seeks to defy physicality and become a being of
breath, song, and thought. She has stripped away nearly all of her physical being, retaining only
what she needs to whisper her gospel. Her most devout adherents seek the mysteries of music and
transform their bodies into vessels of the sublime.
