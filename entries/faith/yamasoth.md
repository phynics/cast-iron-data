## Description

Yamasoth, the Polymorph Plague, is a strange beast indeed. His stony body evokes the image of a
nightmarish octopus with too many tentacles, each partially connected by a leathery web. Each
tentacle ends in a different method of inflicting pain, be it blade, claw, pincer, or serpent's
head. Yamasoth's "face" (if it can be called such) consists of a single huge maw filled with teeth
and stinging tongues. Several eyes surround this mouth, but one hidden deep in his throat remains
closed except in combat, when the lord can open it to use a terrible [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) weapon
that transforms enemies into feral monsters. Yamasoth rules a vast kingdom in the cavern realm of
Sekatar-Seraktis - a kingdom populated entirely by the hideous results of his strange and vile
experiments into the secrets of flesh and the heresies of life.
