>

> All nascent demon lords are chaotic evil and range in power from CR 21 to CR 25. The boons they
> grant are less complex than those granted by demon lords, manifesting as

## Description

Cadaverous Menxyr, known to many as the Coffin Groom, stands nearly 12 feet tall but weighs barely
100 pounds. Despite his emaciated and seemingly fragile frame, which he can fold up to appear like
little more than a tangled mess of old bones, he has great strength. He can pull forth bones from
living creatures, animate the dead to serve his foul lusts, and even climb inside the bodies of the
freshly dead to animate them and seduce those who mourn the loss of a loved one.
