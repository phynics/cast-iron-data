## Description

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 182\
Torag's half-sister Dranngvit is seen by many dwarves as an unfortunate necessity in life. The Debt
Minder oversees the righting of wrongs, but her doctrine can be read as valuing revenge over
justice.

**Sources:** Dwarves of Golarion pg 29\
This bitter goddess is Torag's half-sister; some legends say she was to have been his bride but he
chose Folgrit instead, and to earn her forgiveness he made her a goddess. The dwarves consider
Dranngvit a necessary evil, a goddess to propitiate when they have wronged someone; only those
consumed by hatred and bitterness choose her as a patron. She may be Kols's mother, though Folgrit
treats him like her own son.
