Quick wits and deceptive techniques can often succeed where brute force might not. A lore warden is
the consummate warrior-scholar of the [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars), outsmarting her
competition even when locking blades with powerful foes. Outside of combat, a lore warden's
extensive education also helps her document the past and survive great danger.

**Scholastic (Ex)**

**Info:** A lore warden gains 2 additional skill ranks each level. These ranks must be spent on
Intelligence-based skills. All [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (CHA) and
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) (INT) skills are class skills for lore wardens, as are
[Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) (INT) and [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) (INT).

**Replaces:** Proficiency with medium armor, heavy armor, and shields

**Skill Over Strength (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** She can treat her base attack bonus as though it were 2 higher for the purpose of
calculating the effects of Combat Expertise.

**At 6th Level:** Se gains Combat Expertise as a bonus feat, even if she would not normally qualify
for the feat. If she already has Combat Expertise, she instead gains any one combat feat that
includes Combat Expertise as a prerequisite (and for which she otherwise qualifies).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A lore warden qualifies for feats and other abilities as though she had the
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) feat.

**Replaces:** Bravery and the fighter bonus feat gained at 2nd level

**Swords Secret (Ex)**

**Choice:** A lore warden gains one swords secret. Except where noted, a lore warden cannot select
the same swords secret more than once.

**Flavor:** A lore warden learns specialized techniques that help her to quickly analyze and defeat
her foes.

**At 11th Level:** She chooses a third swords secret.

**At 15th Level:** She chooses a fourth swords secret.

**At 19th Level:** She chooses a fifth swords secret.

**At 7th Level:** She chooses a second swords secret.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Exploit Weakness (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** Whenever she confirms a critical hit, her weapon attacks ignore the first 5
points of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) or [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) the target
has until the end of her next turn.

**At 19th Level:** The lore warden can automatically confirm a critical hit once per round when she
threatens a critical hit.

**Passive:** The lore warden adds [one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her class level on
attack rolls to confirm critical hits.

**Hair's Breadth (Ex)**

**Immediate:** Once per day when subject to a critical hit, the lore warden can attempt an
Acrobatics check to reduce the damage. If the result of this Acrobatics check is greater than the
opponent's confirmation roll, she negates the critical hit; the attack still hits and deals normal
damage.

**At 15th Level:** She can use this secret one additional time per day.

**Prereq:** 11th-level lore warden

**Know Thy Enemy (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** She also gains a +2 bonus to her AC against the creature when using this ability.

**At 19th Level:** The insight bonus increases to +3.

**Standard:** When the lore warden succeeds at a Knowledge check to identify a creature's abilities
and weaknesses, she can also use a **standard action** to grant herself a +2 insight bonus on all
attack and weapon damage rolls made against that enemy. This bonus lasts for a number of rounds
equal to half her class level (minimum 2 rounds), or until the lore warden uses this ability against
a different creature.

**Swift Assessment (Ex)**

**Ability:** The lore warden can now use her _know thy enemy_ swords secret as a **move action**.

**At 15th Level:** She can use this ability as a **swift action**.

**Prereq:** Know Thy Enemy

**Maneuver Training (Ex)**

**Ability:** The lore warden gains a brawler's _maneuver training_ class feature, treating her
fighter level as her [brawler](ref/class/brawler) level.

**Replaces:** Armor training, armor mastery
