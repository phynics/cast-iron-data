Opportunists believe every battle is one of wits rather than arms. The ratfolk enforcers who protect
Goka's infamous Deepmarket embrace this philosophy, unleashing a disorienting mix of martial skill
and alchemical weaponry.

**Prerequisites:** Ratfolk

**Duplicitous (Ex)**

**Alters:** Class skills

**Info:** An opportunist adds [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) (CHA), [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive)
(WIS), [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) (DEX), and [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) (DEX) to
his list of class skills. He gains 2 bonus skill ranks at each level, which must be allocated among
these skills.

**Replaces:** Bravery

**Underhanded (Ex)**

**Ability:** An opportunist gains [Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick) as a bonus
feat even if he does not meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** The bonus feat gained at 1st level

**Cunning Edge (Ex)**

**Choice:** An opportunist can select an edge from those listed below.

**At 12th Level:** He can select a third edge.

**At 16th Level:** He can select a fourth edge.

**At 20th Level:** He can select a fifth edge.

**At 8th Level:** He can select a second edge.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 20th levels

**Alchemical Admixture (Ex)**

**Fullround:** The opportunist can combine two alchemical weapons into a single vial. When thrown as
a splash weapon, the mixture has the effects of both component substances and targets are affected
as if hit by both. The mixture becomes inert after 1 hour. Combining identical alchemical weapons
has no effect.

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** Yes

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 4 fighter levels

**Alchemical Refinement (Ex)**

**Ability:** The opportunist increases the save DC of any alchemical item he crafts by an amount
equal to [one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his fighter level. Doing so increases the
raw material cost to craft that item by 10 gp for every +1 increase. This bonus applies only when
the opportunist uses the crafted alchemical item himself; it functions as a normal item of its type
for all other users.

**Bombs (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

The opportunist can make a number of bombs per day equal to his Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).
These bombs act as [alchemist's](ref/class/alchemist) bombs and deal 1d6 points of fire damage, plus
an additional 1d6 points for every 3 fighter levels the opportunist has.

> _Text from the alchemist class:_
>
> Drawing the components of, creating, and throwing a bomb is a single **standard action**. Thrown
> bombs have a range of 20 feet and use the
> [Throw Splash Weapon special attack](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon). Splash damage from your bomb
> is always equal to the bomb's minimum damage. Those caught in the splash damage can attempt a
> Reflex save for half damage, with a DC equal to 10 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your
> alchemist level + your Intelligence modifier.
>
> Bombs are considered weapons and can be selected using feats such as
> [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot) and [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus). Your
> bombs become inert if used or carried by anyone else.

**Clouded Shift (Ex)**

**Ability:** As part of the standard action to ignite a [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick) or
[smoke pellet](ref/eq-misc/smoke-pellet), the opportunist can take a 5-foot step, even if he has
already moved this round. He can use this ability only if he has not already taken a 5-foot step
this round, and only if the smoke from his alchemical tool conceals his starting location.

**Poison Use (Ex)**

**Ability:** The opportunist no longer risks poisoning himself when applying poison to a weapon.

**Rogue Talent (Ex)**

**Choice:** The opportunist can select one of the following rogue talents for which he qualifies,
treating his fighter level as his rogue level: [deft palm](ref/talent/deft-palm),
[fast fingers](ref/talent/fast-fingers), [fast stealth](ref/talent/fast-stealth),
[honeyed words](ref/talent/honeyed-words), [lasting poison](ref/talent/lasting-poison),
[minor magic](ref/talent/minor-magic), or [quick trapsmith](ref/talent/quick-trapsmith).

**Special:** The opportunist can select this edge multiple times.

**Alchemical Onslaught (Ex)**

**Free:** Once per round, he can draw an alchemical weapon.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** An opportunist gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls with alchemical weapons and bombs and
can add his Intelligence modifier to damage rolls for splash weapons.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1
