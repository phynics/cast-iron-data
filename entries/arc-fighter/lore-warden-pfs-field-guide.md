Quick thinking and deception can often carry the day where raw force might not. Lore wardens are the
bodyguards, guardians, and soldiers of the [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars). Not only are
they often called upon to protect and watch over important repositories of lore, but they themselves
are impressive keepers of all manner of knowledge. Lore wardens are fighters who benefit from
learning to outsmart and outmaneuver their foes rather than just overpower them. Even out of combat,
lore wardens have a quick wit and a surprising breadth of knowledge that can often grant them
significant advantages over enemies even before a battle begins. Indeed, for many lore wardens, a
fight that can be won without a single weapon being drawn is the best kind of victory. A lore warden
has the following class features.

**Scholastic (Ex)**

**Info:** Lore wardens gain 2 additional skill ranks each level. These ranks must be spent on
Intelligence-based skills. All Intelligence-based skills are class skills for lore wardens.

**Replaces:** Proficiency with medium armor, heavy armor, and shields

**Expertise (Ex)**

**Ability:** A lore warden gains [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) as a bonus feat, even
if he would not normally qualify for this feat.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Bravery 1

**Maneuver Mastery (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A lore warden gains a +2 bonus on all CMB checks and to his CMD.

**Replaces:** Armor training 1

**Know Thy Enemy (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 2

**Special:** Note that this bonus on attack and damage rolls applies only to that specific creature.

**Standard:** A lore warden can study a specific target in sight. He must make a Knowledge check to
determine the target's abilities and weaknesses as part of this standard action. If successful, the
lore warden not only notes the appropriate abilities and weaknesses, as detailed under the Knowledge
skill, but also gains a +2 competence bonus on all attack rolls and weapon damage rolls made against
that enemy. This bonus lasts for the duration of an encounter, or until the lore warden attempts a
new Knowledge check to use this ability on a different target.

**Hair's Breadth (Ex)**

**Immediate:** A lore warden can attempt an Acrobatics check to negate a confirmation role for a
critical hit. The critical hit confirmation is negated (though the attack still hits and deals
normal damage) if the Acrobatics check is greater than the opponent's confirmation roll.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Swift Lore (Ex)**

**Ability:** A lore warden can use his _Know Thy Enemy_ ability as a **swift action**.

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 4

**Know Weakness (Ex)**

**Ability:** A lore warden can automatically confirm a critical hit once per round when he threatens
a critical hit.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Armor mastery
