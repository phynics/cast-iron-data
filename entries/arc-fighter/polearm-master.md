**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 106\
The polearm master is schooled in the ancient wisdom that enemies are best faced at the end of long
striking pole, lashing like a serpent before clumsy swords and axes can even be brought to bear.

**Pole Fighting (Ex)**

**Immediate:** A polearm master can shorten the grip on his spear or polearm with reach and use it
against adjacent targets. This action results in a -4 penalty on attack rolls with that weapon until
he spends another immediate action to return to the normal grip.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: The penalty on attack rolls -3.

At 10th level: The penalty on attack rolls -4.

At 14th level: The penalty on attack rolls -5.

At 18th level: The penalty on attack rolls -6.

**Steadfast Pike (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A polearm master gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls with [readied](ref/rule/ready)
attacks and attacks of opportunity made with a spear or polearm.

**Replaces:** Armor training 1, 2, 3, and 4

At 7th level: The bonus +2.

At 11th level: The bonus +3.

At 15th level: The bonus +4.

At 19th level: The bonus +5.

**Polearm Training (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A polearm master gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with spears and polearms.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

At 9th level: The bonus +2.

At 13th level: The bonus +3.

At 17th level: The bonus +4.

**Flexible Flanker (Ex)**

**Ability:** A polearm master may choose any square adjacent to him and treat that square as his
location for determining who he is [flanking](ref/rule/flanking), even if that square is occupied by
a creature, object, or solid barrier.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Sweeping Fend (Ex)**

**Ability:** A polearm master can use any spear or polearm to make [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush)
maneuvers, though he takes a -4 penalty on combat maneuver checks when making such attempts. When
using a spear or polearm to make a [trip](ref/rule/trip) maneuver, he treats these weapons as if
they had the [trip](ref/misc/trip) weapon feature. Weapons with the trip property do not incur this
penalty on trip maneuvers.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Step Aside (Ex)**

**Immediate:** When a creature threatened by a polearm master takes
[a 5-foot step](ref/rule/take-5-foot-step) into a square adjacent to him, he can take a 5-foot step.
This 5-foot step must be subtracted from his movement on the next turn. He also gains a +2 dodge
bonus to his AC against that opponent until the end of his next turn.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Polearm Parry (Ex)**

**Immediate:** When an opponent threatened by a polearm master makes a melee attack against an ally,
he may grant his ally a +2 shield bonus to AC and DR 5/- against that attack.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Armor mastery

**Special:** He may use this ability to protect himself, but only if the attacking creature is not
adjacent to him.

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Alters:** Weapon mastery

**Info:** A polearm master must choose a spear or polearm for _weapon mastery._
