Molthuni defenders occupy and protect contested territory in their war against Nirmathas. These
heavily armored soldiers don't train for maneuverability, but rather for immovability. Taking
advantage of their weighty armor, Molthuni defenders can hold back an onslaught of enemies intent on
breaking through their line. The majority of Molthuni defenders were laborers who joined the
military to gain citizenship, though a noteworthy minority are from stouter non-human races.

**Armored Defense (Ex)**

**Action:** Passive Ability

**At 11th Level:** The bonus becomes +3 in medium armor and +5 in heavy armor. He can choose a
second combat maneuver to add half his _armor training_ bonus to.

**At 15th Level:** The bonus becomes +4 in medium armor and +6 in heavy armor. He can choose a third
combat maneuver to add half his _armor training_ bonus to.

**At 7th Level:** The bonus becomes +2 in medium armor and +4 in heavy armor. He can choose an
additional combat maneuver to add half his _armor training_ bonus to.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 1 through 4

**Special:** This counts as _armor training_ for the purposes of qualifying for feats and prestige
classes. It does not count as _advanced armor training._

A Molthuni defender gains a bonus to his CMD against bull rush, dirty trick, drag, grapple, overrun,
reposition, and trip combat maneuver checks. This bonus also applies to the DC of foes' Acrobatics
checks to move through squares he threatens. This bonus is +1 when the Molthuni defender is wearing
medium armor, and +3 when he is wearing heavy armor.

In addition to this, the Molthuni defender is able to choose one of the following combat maneuvers:
bull rush, dirty trick, drag, grapple, overrun, reposition, or trip. When he is wearing medium or
heavy armor, the Molthuni defender adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his _armor training_
bonus on combat maneuver checks for the selected maneuver.
