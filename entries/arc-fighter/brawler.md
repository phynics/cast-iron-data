> _**Note:** This is a fighter archetype, and has no relation to the [brawler](ref/class/brawler)
> hybrid class._

All melee is up close and personal, but some warriors bring it as close as they can get. Brawlers
can be found anywhere, among all races and societies. A brawler could be hired muscle in a tavern, a
local crime syndicate enforcer, or a hotheaded recruit among the ranks of a baron's guard. Often
brawlers' in-your-face attitudes are as powerful as their tactics.

**Close Control (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A brawler becomes skilled at forcefully moving his opponent around the battlefield.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** The brawler gains a +1 bonus on [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [drag](ref/rule/drag),
and [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuver checks. The brawler also gains a +1 bonus to
CMD when attacked with the bull rush, drag, and reposition maneuvers.

**Replaces:** Armor training 1

At 6th level: p!These bonuses +2.

At 10th level: p!These bonuses +3.

At 14th level: p!These bonuses +4.

At 18th level: p!These bonuses +5.

**Close Combatant (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** The bonuses become +3 and +5.

**At 15th Level:** These bonuses increase to +4 and +6.

**At 19th Level:** The bonuses become +5 on attack rolls and +7 on damage rolls.

**At 7th Level:** These bonuses increase to +2 and +4, respectively.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A brawler gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls and a +3 bonus on damage rolls with weapons
in the close [weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups).

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1 and 2

**Menacing Stance (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A brawler constantly harries and distracts his enemies.

**At 11th Level:** These penalties increase to -2 and -5, respectively.

**At 15th Level:** The penalties become -3 and -6.

**At 19th Level:** These penalties increase to -4 on attack rolls and -7 on concentration checks.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** While adjacent to the brawler, enemies take a -1 penalty on attack rolls and a -4
penalty on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks.

**Replaces:** Armor training 2, 3, and 4, armor mastery

**Special:** Creatures do not take these penalties if the brawler is dazed, helpless, staggered,
stunned, or unconscious.

**No Escape (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Taking [a 5-foot step](ref/rule/take-5-foot-step) out of the area of a brawler's
_menacing stance_ or moving out of the area of a brawler's _menacing stance_ with a
[withdraw](ref/rule/withdraw) action provokes an attack of opportunity from the brawler.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3 and 4

**Stand Still**

**Ability:** A brawler gains [Stand Still](ref/feat/stand-still) as a bonus feat, even if he does
not have the [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes) feat. If the brawler already has the Stand
Still feat, he can take any other combat feat instead.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** He gains a bonus equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his fighter level on
combat maneuver checks when using the Stand Still feat.

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Alters:** Weapon mastery

**Choice:** A brawler must select a close weapon for this ability.
