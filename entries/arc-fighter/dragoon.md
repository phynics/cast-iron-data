**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 46\
These gallant lancers serve in the vanguard of many armies or as knights-errant. They are born
leaders and masters of the mounted charge.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A dragoon is not proficient with tower shields.

**Skilled Rider (Ex)**

**Ability:** A dragoon gains both the [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat) and
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Ride) feats as bonus feats.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** The 1st-level fighter bonus combat feat

**Spear Training (Ex)**

**Alters:** Weapon training

**Choice:** A dragoon must select _weapon training_ with the
[spear group](ref/ability/weapon-groups).

**At 13th Level:** The dragoon's _weapon training_ bonus with spears improves by another +1 on
attack rolls and another +2 on damage rolls.

**At 17th Level:** The dragoon's _weapon training_ bonus with spears improves by yet another +1 on
attack rolls and +2 on damage rolls.

**At 9th Level:** The dragoon's _weapon training_ bonus with spears improves by +1 on attack rolls
and +2 on damage rolls.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Special:** The dragoon does not gain _weapon training_ in any other groups as he increases in
level.

**Spinning Lance (Ex)**

**Ability:** A dragoon may alternate attacks with the piercing head of his
[lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance) with reach, or with the butt end (treat as a
[club](ref/eq-weapon/club)) against adjacent targets.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 2

**Special:** Unlike a double weapon, the masterwork quality and magical special abilities apply to
both ends of the lance, except for those weapon special abilities that apply only to edged weapons.

**Banner (Ex)**

**Ability:** A dragoon may attach a banner to his lance. This is identical to the
[cavalier](ref/class/cavalier) class feature.

**At 14th Level:** The bonuses provided by the dragoon's banner increase by +1.

**At 19th Level:** The bonuses provided by the dragoon's banner increase by +2.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2, 3, and 4

**Special:** Cavalier levels stack with his dragoon level for determining the effect of his banner,
and he can take the better banner progression.

**Piercing Lance (Ex)**

**Ability:** As a **standard action** or as part of a [charge](ref/rule/charge), a dragoon attacking
a mounted opponent can make two attacks, one against the mount and the other against the rider,
using his highest base attack bonus. Furthermore, if the mount is hit and its rider attempts to
negate the hit with the Mounted Combat feat, the dragoon's attack roll is considered 4 higher when
calculating the DC of the Ride check to negate the hit.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Leaping Lance (Ex)**

**Ability:** When charging, a dragoon may jump from his mount toward his target. If he jumps 10
feet, his charge modifiers on attack rolls and to AC are doubled and he is still considered mounted
for lance damage, mounted combat feats, and so on.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** A dragoon and his mount suffer no armor check penalty on Acrobatics checks while
mounted.

**Replaces:** Armor training 4

**Special:** This ability qualifies you for the [Rebounding Leap](ref/feat/rebounding-leap) feat.

**Weapon Mastery**

**Alters:** Weapon mastery

**Choice:** The dragoon must select the lance for _weapon mastery._
