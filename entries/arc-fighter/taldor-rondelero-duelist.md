**Sources:** Inner Sea Primer pg 25\
Taldor's warriors have perfected the art of fighting with the [falcata](ref/eq-weapon/falcata) and
[buckler](ref/eq-armor/buckler) in an art they call rondelero. They use their bucklers both to
defend themselves and to launch unexpected strikes while chopping and hewing at their foes'
unprotected bodies and heads with their falcatas. Rondeleros wear any type of armor, but favor
bucklers, and are comfortable using both hands for offense and defense.

**Buckler Bash (Ex)**

**Ability:** A rondelero can perform a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) with a buckler (use the
same damage and critical modifier as for a light shield).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Bravery

**Buckler Catch (Ex)**

**Ability:** A rondelero can catch his opponent's weapon between his buckler and his forearm,
effectively wedging the hafts of polearms and hammers or the flats of blades. This functions as a
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) combat maneuver, and the rondelero gains a +4 bonus on the roll. If the
rondelero's attack fails by 10 or more, he suffers a -2 penalty to his AC until the start of his
next turn.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 1

**Strong Swing (Ex)**

**Ability:** With a [full-attack](ref/rule/full-attack) action, a rondelero may alternate between
using his falcata or his buckler for each attack. This does not grant additional attacks or incur
penalties as two-weapon fighting does.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A rondelero gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls when wielding a falcata and
buckler that applies to attacks made by either hand.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

At 9th level: p!These bonuses +2.

At 13th level: p!These bonuses +3.

At 17th level: p!These bonuses +4.

**Armor Training (Ex)**

**Ability:** A rondelero gains _armor training 1,_ and can move at his normal speed in medium armor.

**At 15th Level:** The rondelero gains _armor training 2,_ and can move at his normal speed in heavy
armor.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 2

**Chopping Blow (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Standard:** A rondelero can make a single melee attack with a falcata. If the attack hits, he may
make a [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver against the target of his attack as a **free
action** that does not provoke an attack of opportunity.
