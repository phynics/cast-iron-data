Advances in technology have made archaic armors obsolete by the twentieth century, and modern
soldiers concentrate training on firearms and swift feet.

**Trench Warfare (Ex)**

**Choice:** A trench fighter can select one specific type of firearm (such as a machine gun,
revolver, or rifle). He gains a bonus equal to his Dexterity modifier on damage rolls when firing
that type of firearm.

**At 11th Level:** The trench fighter picks up a third type of firearm, gaining these bonuses for
those types as well.

**At 15th Level:** The trench fighter picks up a fourth type of firearm, gaining these bonuses for
those types as well.

**At 7th Level:** The trench fighter picks up another type of firearm, gaining these bonuses for
those types as well.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** When behind partial, normal, or improved [cover](ref/rule/cover), a trench fighter
gains an additional +2 AC bonus from the cover.

**Replaces:** Armor training 1, 2, 3, and 4
