In combat, most fighters have some sort of code of honor. Some believe that one should not kick
enemies when they are down, or should limit use of other such dirty tricks to the most dire of
circumstances. The cad places no such limitations on himself. Battles are for winning, and anything
that gives the cad the upper hand against his enemies is a legitimate tactic. No trick is too
treacherous if it leads to victory.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A cad is not proficient with medium armor, heavy armor, or tower shields.

**Gained class skills:** acro, bluff, ea, soh, stl

**Dirty Maneuvers (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A cad becomes skilled at deceiving and discomfiting his opponents.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** The cad gains a +1 bonus on [disarm](ref/rule/disarm),
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick), and [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver checks. The cad
also gains a +1 bonus to CMD when attacked with the disarm, dirty trick, and steal combat maneuvers.

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: p!These bonuses +2.

At 10th level: p!These bonuses +3.

At 14th level: p!These bonuses +4.

At 18th level: p!These bonuses +5.

**Catch Off-Guard**

**Ability:** The cad gains the [Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard) feat.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 1

**Payback (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A cad gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls against any creature that has
attacked the cad since the beginning of his last turn.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1, 3, and 4

At 9th level: This bonus +2.

At 13th level: This bonus +3.

At 17th level: This bonus +4.

**Deadly Surprise (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a cad hits an opponent that is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against him with a
weapon or unarmed attack, he may attempt a dirty trick combat maneuver as an **immediate action** as
part of the attack.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 2

**Razor-Sharp Chair Leg (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** The cad has a critical threat range of 19-20/&times&2 with any improvised melee weapon.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Swift:** A cad may alter the type of damage dealt by an
[improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) to bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage.

**Craven Combatant (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** When [fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or using
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) or [total defense](ref/misc/total-defense), a cad
cannot be [flanked](ref/rule/flanking) except by a rogue or ninja whose level is four or more higher
than the cad's fighter level.

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Sweeping Prank (Ex)**

**At 17th Level:** The cad can use this ability as a **full-round action** to attempt a dirty trick
maneuver against a number of enemies equal to 2 + his Dexterity bonus (if any).

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3 and 4

**Standard:** A cad can use a dirty trick maneuver against any two adjacent opponents that he can
reach, making a separate combat maneuver check against each opponent. He must use the same dirty
trick maneuver against each opponent.

**Treacherous Blow (Ex)**

**Immediate:** When a cad confirms a critical hit, he can attempt a dirty trick combat maneuver as
part of that attack.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 4

**Ultimate Payback (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Any critical threats a cad makes against an opponent that has attacked him since the
beginning of his last turn are automatically confirmed.

**Replaces:** Weapon mastery
