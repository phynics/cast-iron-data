**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 14\
While the axe is the most famous dwarven weapon, the hammer is at the heart of dwarves' heritage as
forgemasters and warriors alike.

**Prerequisites:** Dwarf

**Sledgehammer (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A foehammer wielding a hammer gains a +2 circumstance bonus on combat maneuver checks
made to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun), [sunder](ref/rule/sunder), or
[trip](ref/rule/trip).

**Replaces:** Armor training 1

**Weapon Training (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A foehammer must select hammers and does not gain weapon training with
[other groups](ref/ability/weapon-groups), though his weapon training bonus improves by +1 every
four levels after 5th.

**Hammer to the Ground (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a foehammer succeeds at a bull rush combat maneuver, he can make a trip combat
maneuver at the end of the bull rush. If he does not move with the target, the force of his blow may
still trip his foe, but he takes a -5 penalty on the combat maneuver check to trip.

**At 15th Level:** Any creature a foehammer successfully bull rushes is automatically knocked
[prone](ref/rule/prone) at the end of the bull rush.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 2 and 4

**Rhythmic Blows (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Each time that a foehammer hits a target, he gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls against
that target. This bonus stacks with each hit against that target, but lasts only until the end of
the foehammer's turn.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Piledriver (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Standard:** A foehammer may make a single melee attack with a weapon from the hammer weapon
training group. If the attack hits, he may make a bull rush or trip combat maneuver against the
target of his attack as a **free action** that does not provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Ground Breaker (Ex)**

**Fullround:** A foehammer may strike the ground with his hammer. If the attack deals more damage
than the floor's [hardness](ref/umr/hardness), the space he occupies and all adjacent squares become
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). Creatures in those squares, except for the
foehammer, are knocked prone (DC 15 Reflex negates).

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Hammer Master (Ex)**

**Ability:** Any combat feats a foehammer has learned with any weapon from the hammer weapon
training group (e.g., [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus)) apply to all weapons from that group.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Devastating Blow (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Armor mastery

**Standard:** A foehammer may make a single melee attack with a weapon from the hammer weapon
training group at a -5 penalty. If the attack hits, it is treated as a critical threat. Weapon
special abilities that only activate on a critical hit do not activate if this critical hit is
confirmed.

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Alters:** Weapon mastery

**Info:** A foehammer must choose a weapon from the hammer group.
