**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 5\
The venomblade has learned to take full advantage of his toxic spittle, blinding his targets and
then rushing in to cut them down where they stand.

**Prerequisites:** Nagaji

**Venom Projection (Ex)**

**Ability:** The venomblade must take the [Spit Venom](ref/feat/spit-venom) nagaji racial feat as
his 1st-level fighter bonus feat if he does not already have that ability.

**Alters:** Bonus feat gained at 1st level

**At 12th Level:** He can use it as a **swift action**.

**At 2nd Level:** He does not provoke an attack of opportunity for using the ability granted by the
Spit Venom feat.

**At 4th Level:** He can use the ability granted by the Spit Venom feat as a **standard action**.

**At 8th Level:** He can use it as a **move action**.

**Replaces:** Bravery

**Sneak Attack (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A venomblade deals 1d6 additional points of damage to targets within 30 feet that he
flanks or that are denied their Dexterity bonus to AC against him.

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 6th level

At 10th level: This additional damage 2d6.

At 14th level: This additional damage 3d6.

At 18th level: This additional damage 4d6.

**Viper Strike (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 12th level

**Swift:** Upon making a successful attack with a slashing or piercing weapon, the venomblade can
spend a use of the ability granted by the Spit Venom feat to spray his toxin into the wound. This
imposes a -4 penalty on the victim's saving throw against the poison and causes the target to take
1d6 additional points of damage from all successful attacks for 1 round. This is a poison effect.
