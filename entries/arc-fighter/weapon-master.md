**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 109\
Devoted to the perfection of a single weapon, the weapon master's meditations upon his favored
weapon border on the obsessive, but none can deny his consummate skill.

**Chosen Weapon**

**Choice:** The weapon master must select a single type of weapon (such as longsword or shortbow).
All of his abilities apply to that weapon type.

**Weapon Guard (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A weapon master gains a +1 bonus to CMD against [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) and
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) attempts while wielding his chosen weapon. This bonus also applies on
saves against any effect that targets his chosen weapon (for example, [grease](ref/spell/grease),
[heat metal](ref/spell/heat-metal), [shatter](ref/spell/shatter), [warp wood](ref/spell/warp-wood)).

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: The bonus +2.

At 10th level: The bonus +3.

At 14th level: The bonus +4.

At 18th level: The bonus +5.

**Weapon Training (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A weapon master gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with his chosen weapon.

**Replaces:** Armor training 1, 2, 3, and 4

At 7th level: The bonus +2.

At 11th level: The bonus +3.

At 15th level: The bonus +4.

At 19th level: The bonus +5.

**Reliable Strike (Ex)**

**Immediate:** A weapon master may reroll an attack roll, critical hit confirmation roll, miss
chance check, or damage roll. He must accept the second roll even if it is worse.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 5 fighter levels

**Mirror Move (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** A weapon master gains his weapon training bonus as an insight bonus to AC when attacked
by his chosen weapon.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Deadly Critical (Ex)**

**Immediate:** When a weapon master confirms a critical hit with his chosen weapon, he can increase
the weapon's damage multiplier by +1.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 3 fighter levels beyond 13th

**Critical Specialist (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** The save DCs of any effects caused by a critical hit with a weapon master's chosen
weapon increase by +4.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Unstoppable Strike (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Armor mastery

**Standard:** A weapon master can make one attack with his chosen weapon as a touch attack that
ignores [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) (or [hardness](ref/umr/hardness), if attacking
an object).
