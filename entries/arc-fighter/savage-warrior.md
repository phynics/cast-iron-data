**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 107\
Warriors' might is not measured only by their skill with steel, but also by their ability to inflict
death with fang and claw, horn and hoof, and every exotic appendage the natural and unnatural world
has to offer.

**Spark of Life (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A savage warrior gains a +1 bonus on saving throws made against energy drain and death
effects.

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: The bonus +2.

At 10th level: The bonus +3.

At 14th level: The bonus +4.

At 18th level: The bonus +5.

**Natural Savagery (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A savage warrior gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with natural weapons. This
bonus also applies to CMB and CMD for [grappling](ref/rule/grapple).

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

At 9th level: This bonus +2.

At 13th level: This bonus +3.

At 17th level: This bonus +4.

**Savage Charge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** When a savage warrior attacks with a natural weapon at the end of a
[charge](ref/rule/charge), he gains a bonus on his natural weapon attack rolls equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his fighter level, while suffering a penalty to his AC equal
to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his fighter level. These replace the normal attack roll
bonus and AC penalty for charging. This bonus also applies to his CMB for a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuvers made when charging.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Careful Claw (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** When using a natural weapon to attack a creature using
[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield) or a similar effect that damages creatures attacking it (such
as a [barbed devil's](ref/monster/devil-barbed-devil-hamatula) barbed defense), a savage warrior
reduces the damage from such effects by an amount equal to 1/2 his fighter level.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Greater Savage Charge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** When using _savage charge,_ the AC penalty is reduced to 1/4 his fighter level instead
of 1/2 his fighter level. In addition, a savage warrior can charge through friendly creatures and
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles).

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Natural Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Info:** A savage warrior must choose one natural weapon.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon mastery
