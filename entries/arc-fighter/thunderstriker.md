The thunderstriker adopts an unusual fighting style, gripping a heavy weapon with both hands and
switching to a defensive posture with weapon and buckler, lashing out with the shield with
surprising speed and power.

**Strapped Shield (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A thunderstriker takes no penalty on attack rolls when using a weapon in two hands
while wearing a [buckler](ref/eq-armor/buckler).

**Replaces:** Armor training 1

**Hardbuckler (Ex)**

**Ability:** A thunderstriker may make [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) attacks with a buckler as
if it were a light shield.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 2

**Knockback Smash (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** When a thunderstriker uses his buckler to attack, he gains a bonus equal to the
enhancement bonus of the buckler on both attack and damage rolls.

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Hammer and Anvil (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** A thunderstriker suffers only half the normal penalties for two-weapon fighting when
using a buckler as his off-hand weapon.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Buckler Defense (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** A thunderstriker retains partial use of his buckler even when using a weapon in both
hands or in each hand (rather than losing his shield bonus until the beginning of his next turn). He
gains a +1 shield bonus to AC and may apply the benefits of any feats he knows that require the use
of a shield, but does not benefit from any magical properties his shield may possess.

**Replaces:** Armor training 4

**Balanced Bashing (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** A thunderstriker no longer suffers two-weapon fighting penalties when using a buckler
as his off-hand weapon.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Improved Buckler Defense (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Passive:** A thunderstriker does not forfeit his shield bonus to AC from a buckler when fighting
two-handed.

**Replaces:** Armor mastery
