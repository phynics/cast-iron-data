**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 104\
Perfectly at home in the darkness, the cavern sniper capitalizes on stealth and ranged attacks
imbued with his [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) to harass his opponents. The
cavern sniper focuses on surprise, his innate magical abilities, and poison to take down unwary
foes. The cavern sniper has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Drow

**Gained class skills:** stl

**Lost class skills:** intm

**Imbued Shot (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** The 1st-level fighter bonus feat

**Special:** The arrow must be fired during the round it was imbued, or the
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) is wasted. If the arrow or bolt misses, the use
of the spell-like ability is wasted.

**Swift:** The cavern sniper gains the ability to imbue his arrows or bolts with the effect of one
of his drow [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire), [darkness](ref/spell/darkness), or
[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
(provided he has access to the ability). When such an arrow or bolt is fired, the spell's area is
centered where the arrow or bolt lands. If the target of the attack has a space larger than 5 feet,
the cavern sniper can choose which square of the creature's space is the center of the spell-like
ability's effect, as long as that square is within line of sight of the cavern sniper. The cavern
sniper can instead choose to target a single square within line of sight with an imbued arrow or
bolt, and uses that square as the center of the spell-like ability's area of effect on a hit (AC 5).

**Silent Shooter (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A cavern sniper gains a +2 bonus on Stealth checks made when loading a bow or crossbow,
poisoning ammunition, and making sniping attempts.

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: This bonus +4.

At 10th level: This bonus +6.

At 14th level: This bonus +8.

At 18th level: This bonus +10.

**Quick and Deadly (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavern sniper can move at full speed while using Stealth at no penalty.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** The 4th-level fighter bonus feat

**Swift:** He can apply poison to a single arrow or crossbow bolt.

**Sniper Training (Ex)**

**Choice:** The cavern sniper chooses the bow or crossbow [weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)
and gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1, 3, and 4

At 9th level: This bonus +2.

At 13th level: This bonus +3.

At 17th level: This bonus +4.

**Greater Imbued Shot (Su)**

**Ability:** The cavern sniper gains two extra uses of both his
_[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire)_ and _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), but can only use these extra uses to imbue
arrows and bolts with the _imbued shot_ class feature.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Alters:** Weapon mastery

**Choice:** When the cavern sniper gains _weapon mastery,_ he must choose a weapon in either the bow
or crossbow weapon group.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
