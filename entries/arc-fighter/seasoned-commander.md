The seasoned commander excels at leading troops through inspiration and the use of unit tactics.

**Strategic Training (Ex)**

**Alters:** Skill ranks per level, class skills

**Info:** A seasoned commander gains 4 skill ranks + a number of skill ranks equal to his
Intelligence modifier at each level instead of the normal 2 skill ranks + Intelligence modifier at
each level. A seasoned commander adds [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) (CHA),
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) (INT),
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) (INT), [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics)
(INT), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) (WIS) to his list of class skills.

**Replaces:** Bonus fighter combat feat gained at 1st level, proficiency with heavy armor and tower
shields

**Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** A seasoned commander gains the [cavalier's](ref/class/cavalier) _tactician_ class
feature, treating his fighter level as his cavalier level for the purposes of this ability.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 1

**Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** You receive a teamwork feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the prerequisites for this
feat.

**Flavor:** Text from the cavalier class

**Standard:** You can grant this feat to all allies within 30 feet who can see and hear you. Allies
retain the use of this bonus feat for a number of rounds equal to 3 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cavalier level. Allies do not need to meet the
prerequisites of these bonus feats.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 cavalier levels

**Inspiring Speech (Ex)**

**At 13th Level:** He can perform the speech as a **swift action**.

**At 15th Level:** He can use this ability three times a day.

**At 7th Level:** He can perform the speech as a **move action**.

**At 9th Level:** He can use this ability twice a day.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1 and 3

**Standard:** A seasoned commander can give an inspiring speech once per day. If he does, all allies
who can hear his speech gain the benefits of [inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage),
granting bonuses 1 lower than those granted by a bard of the seasoned commander's fighter level,
lasting 1 round per fighter level + his Charisma modifier (abilities that affect a bardic
performance, such as [Lingering Performance](ref/feat/lingering-performance), have no effect on this
ability).

**Inspire Greatness (Ex)**

**Ability:** A seasoned commander can also choose to use his _inspiring speech_ to grant the effects
of [inspire greatness](ref/performance/inspire-greatness) to one ally (instead of _inspire
courage_).

**At 11th Level:** He can target two allies while using this ability.

**At 13th Level:** He can target up to three allies with this ability.

**At 15th Level:** He can now target up to four allies.

**At 17th Level:** He can target up to five allies.

**At 19th Level:** He can target up to six allies.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Special:** This ability functions in all other respects like the seasoned commander's _inspire
courage_ ability. Whichever _inspiring speech_ he chooses to use, if the seasoned commander uses an
_inspiring speech_ before the duration of his previous _inspiring speech_ ends, the new _inspiring
speech_ replaces the old _speech._

**Greater Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** The seasoned commander gains the [cavalier's](ref/class/cavalier) _greater tactician_
class feature, treating his fighter level as his cavalier level for the purposes of this ability.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Greater Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** You receive an additional teamwork feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the
prerequisites for this feat. You can grant this feat to your allies using the _tactician_ ability.

**Flavor:** Text from the cavalier class

**Swift:** Using the _tactician_ ability becomes a **swift action**.

**Inspire Heroics (Ex)**

**Ability:** A seasoned commander can choose to use his _inspiring speech_ to grant the effects of
[inspire heroics](ref/performance/inspire-heroics) to one ally (instead of _inspire courage_ or
_inspire greatness_).

**At 17th Level:** He can target up to two allies with this ability.

**At 19th Level:** He can target up to three allies with this ability.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 4

**Special:** This ability functions in all other respects like the seasoned commander's _inspire
courage_ ability.

**Master Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** The seasoned commander gains the cavalier's _master tactician_ class feature, treating
his fighter level as his cavalier level for the purposes of this ability.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Master Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** You receive an additional teamwork feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the
prerequisites for this feat. You can grant this feat to your allies using the _tactician_ ability.

**Flavor:** Text from the cavalier ability

**Special:** Whenever you use the _tactician_ ability, you grant any two teamwork feats that you
know. You can select from **any** of your teamwork feats, not just your bonus feats.
