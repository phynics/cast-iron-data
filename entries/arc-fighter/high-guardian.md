As the shield that protects his lord from the myriad dangers around every corner, the high guardian
pledges his life to keep his charge safe. High guardians epitomize personal devotion, making them
especially popular among Taldan nobles envious of the emperor's [Ulfen Guard](ref/rule/f-imperial),
as well as in the courts of the Lands of the Linnorm Kings, the war councils of Molthune and
Lastwall, and the refined chambers of Osirion.

**Gained class skills:** kl, kno

**Lost class skills:** kd, ke

**Obligation (Ex)**

**Ability:** A high guardian can spend 1 minute of focused concentration each day to select a single
ally as his _obligation,_ vowing to keep that person alive for that day. Once he has chosen, he
can't change his _obligation_ until the following day. If his _obligation_ dies, the high guardian
must atone for 1 week before he can select another _obligation._

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Right Hand (Ex)**

**Immediate:** A high guardian can take [a 5-foot step](ref/rule/take-5-foot-step), as long as he
ends this movement adjacent to his _obligation._ If he takes this step, he cannot take a 5-foot step
during his next turn and his total movement is reduced by 5 feet during his next turn.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** The bonus feat gained at 1st level

**Defender's Reflexes (Ex)**

**Ability:** A high guardian gains [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes) as a bonus feat, and
he can use his Strength modifier instead of his Dexterity modifier to determine the number of
additional attacks of opportunity he can make per round. If he already has Combat Reflexes, he
instead gains [Stand Still](ref/feat/stand-still) as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** The bonus feat gained at 2nd level

**Unassailable Allegiance (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A high guardian gains a +1 bonus on Will saves against compulsion spells and effects.

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: This bonus +2.

At 10th level: This bonus +3.

At 14th level: This bonus +4.

At 18th level: This bonus +5.

**Royal Protector (Ex)**

**Ability:** A high guardian gains [Bodyguard](ref/feat/bodyguard) and
[In Harm's Way](ref/feat/in-harms-way) as bonus feats, though he can use them only to improve his
_obligation's_ AC or intercept a successful attack against his _obligation._

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** The bonus feat gained at 4th level
