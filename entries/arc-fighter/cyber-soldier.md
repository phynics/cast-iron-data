**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 12\
Cyber-soldiers replace fallible flesh with precision-crafted machinery. Some seek to exceed the
limitations of humanoid flesh, while others become cyber-soldiers through drastic repairs
necessitated by horrific injuries. A cyber-soldier has the following class features.

**Cybernetic Combat (Ex)**

**Ability:** A cyber-soldier gains a slam attack for each _cybernetic arm._ These attacks deal 1d6
points of bludgeoning damage for a Medium cyber-soldier and 1d4 points of bludgeoning damage for a
Small cyber-soldier.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A cyber-soldier gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls with
[implanted weapons](ref/tech-cyber/implanted-weaponry),
[cybernetic arms](ref/tech-cyber/cybernetic-arm), and melee or thrown weapons wielded with a
_cybernetic arm._ Weapons wielded with two _cybernetic arms_ receive double the bonus, except when
such weapons are thrown. This attack bonus does not stack with the attack bonus for weapon training.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

At 9th level: The damage bonus +2.

At 13th level: The damage bonus +3.

At 17th level: The damage bonus +4.

**Improved Implantation (Ex)**

**At 15th Level:** Her body slot can accommodate two more pieces of cybertech than normal.

**Info:** A cyber-soldier can exceed the normal limitations on [cybertech](ref/rule/cybertech)
implantation. Her brain and body slots each accommodate one more piece of cybertech than normal. In
addition, her limit on implantation points increases by [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her
cyber-soldier level. She can allocate these points between her Intelligence and Constitution scores
as she chooses.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 2 and 4

**Resilience (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Passive:** A cyber-soldier has a reservoir of 5
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) for each piece of implanted cybertech in her
body. Lost temporary hit points are recovered at the rate of 10 points per hour.

**Replaces:** Armor mastery
