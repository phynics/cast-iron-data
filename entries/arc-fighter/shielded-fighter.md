**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 108\
A shielded fighter focuses on both offense and defense, blending weapon and shield in perfect
balance to impede his enemies while delivering deadly blows, and even turning the shield itself into
a formidable weapon. These fighting school benefits apply when wielding a weapon and a shield
simultaneously.

**Active Defense (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A shielded fighter gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC when wielding a shield and
[fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively), using
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), or using [total defense](ref/misc/total-defense).

**Replaces:** Armor training 1, 2, 3, and 4

**Swift:** He may share this bonus with one adjacent ally, or
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of the bonus (minimum +0) with all adjacent allies, until
the beginning of his next turn.

At 7th level: The dodge bonus +2.

At 11th level: The dodge bonus +3.

At 15th level: The dodge bonus +4.

At 19th level: The dodge bonus +5.

**Shield Fighter (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A shielded fighter gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls when making a
[shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash).

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

**Special:** With a [full attack](ref/rule/full-attack) action, a shielded fighter may alternate
between using his weapon or his shield for each attack. This action does not grant additional
attacks or incur penalties as two-weapon fighting does.

At 9th level: The bonus +2.

At 13th level: The bonus +3.

At 17th level: The bonus +4.

**Shield Buffet (Ex)**

**At 13th Level:** A shielded fighter may use this ability as a **swift action**.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Move:** A shielded fighter may make a combat maneuver check to use his shield to impede an
adjacent enemy. If successful, the target suffers a -2 penalty on its attack rolls against the
shielded fighter and a -2 penalty to AC on attacks made by the shielded fighter until the beginning
of his next turn.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2 and 3

**Shield Guard (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Swift:** A shielded fighter may designate one square adjacent to him. He may designate two squares
if using a heavy shield or three squares if using a tower shield, but these squares must be
contiguous. Enemies in these squares cannot [flank](ref/rule/flanking) the shielded fighter and do
not count for flanking with other creatures. This effect lasts until he moves from his position or
uses another swift action to change the affected squares.

**Shield Mastery (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Passive:** A shielded fighter gains DR 5/- when wielding a shield.

**Replaces:** Armor mastery

**Shield Ward (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** A shielded fighter gains [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) (as a [rogue](ref/class/rogue))
while wielding a shield, and adds his shield bonus to his AC (not including enhancement bonuses) on
Reflex saves and to his touch AC. In addition, his shield cannot be [disarmed](ref/rule/disarm) or
[sundered](ref/rule/sunder).

**Replaces:** Weapon mastery
