**Sources:** Blood of the Ancients pg 27\
As staunch believers in the value of higher ground, the Shory developed special fighting styles that
capitalize on fighting from above. While many dismiss such techniques as a niche tactic, aerial
assaulters leap to great heights and create higher ground where there is none.

**Gained class skills:** acro, fly

**Lost class skills:** kd, ride, swim

**Aerial Expertise (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** An aerial assaulter gains a +2 bonus on Fly checks. Additionally, whenever he is
affected by a spell or ability that grants him a fly speed, he treats that spell or ability as
though its caster level were 2 higher for the purposes of determining its effects.

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: The bonus on Fly checks +4.

At 10th level: The bonus on Fly checks +6.

At 14th level: The bonus on Fly checks +8.

At 18th level: The bonus on Fly checks +10.

**Take the High Ground (Ex)**

**Flavor:** An aerial assaulter can leap above his foes to gain an advantage in combat.

**At 12th Level:** An aerial assaulter can use this ability as a **swift action**.

**At 4th Level:** An aerial assaulter increases his bonus from higher ground by an amount equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his fighter level.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Move:** He can attempt an Acrobatics check with the DC equal to the CMD of an adjacent enemy. If
he succeeds, the aerial assaulter is treated as if he had [higher ground](ref/rule/combat-modifiers)
against that enemy until the beginning of his next turn.

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 2nd and 12th level

**Aerial Dodge (Ex)**

**Flavor:** An aerial assaulter can perform an aerial dodge that allows him to evade attacks while
flying.

**Immediate:** When an opponent attempts an attack against the aerial assaulter, he can attempt a
Fly check with a DC equal to the result of the attacker's attack roll. If he succeeds, he can take a
5-foot step and gain a dodge bonus to his AC equal to his Dexterity modifier. This movement doesn't
negate the attack if the aerial assaulter remains in range of the attacking enemy's reach at the end
of the 5-foot step. If the Fly check fails, the aerial assaulter does not move, does not gain an AC
bonus, and provokes an attack of opportunity from each creature that threatens him, including the
initial attacker.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Armor mastery

**Special:** The aerial assaulter must be flying to use this ability.

**High Ground Mastery (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever an aerial assaulter threatens a critical hit with an attack made from higher
ground, he automatically confirms the critical hit, and the weapon's damage multiplier is increased
by 1.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** All bull rush, dirty trick, drag, grapple, reposition, and trip combat maneuver checks
against the aerial assaulter fail when attempted by opponents on lower ground than he is.

**Replaces:** Weapon mastery
