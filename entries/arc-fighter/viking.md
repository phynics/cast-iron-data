**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 51, People of the North pg 24\
Vikings seek to raid "softer societies" and return with their longships filled with plunder. A
viking strikes fear into the heart of her foes, and in battle she can fly into a terrible rage. Many
vikings wear the pelts of bears or wolves, drawing on the strength and ferocity of these beasts in
battle. Vikings do not wear heavy armor, but they are skilled at fighting with an axe or spear in
conjunction with a shield.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Weapon and armor proficiencies

**Info:** A viking is not proficient with heavy armor or tower shields.

**Fearsome (Ex)**

**At 18th Level:** She can attempt to demoralize a foe as a **free action** once per round.

**At 10th Level:** She can do so as a **swift action**.

**At 2nd Level:** A viking can attempt an Intimidate check to demoralize an opponent as a **move
action**.

**Replaces:** Bravery

**Shield Defense (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A viking learns the art of fighting with a shield.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** Whenever she is wearing medium, light, or no armor and using a shield, the viking's
shield bonus to AC increases by 1.

**Replaces:** Armor training

**Berserker (Ex)**

**Ability:** A viking gains the _rage_ ability as per the [barbarian](ref/class/barbarian) class
feature, and she uses her fighter level as her barbarian level for the purposes of determining her
rounds of rage, qualifying for rage powers, and determining the effects of rage powers.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training

**Rage Powers (Ex)**

**Alters:** The fighter's bonus feats

**Choice:** Whenever a viking gains a fighter bonus feat, she can instead gain a single rage power,
as the barbarian class feature, in place of the bonus feat. Once selected, these rage powers cannot
be changed.

**Gained:** At 6th Level
