**Sources:** Knights of the Inner Sea pg 21\
With myriad weapons, armor, and useful gear always on hand, the weapon bearer squire ensures her
knight is equipped for any task.

**Weapon Rack (Ex)**

**Immediate:** A weapon bearer squire can pick up items dropped by her knight.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Move:** Once per round, a knight can retrieve an item carried by his weapon bearer squire.

**Replaces:** The 1st-level fighter bonus combat feat

**Special:** These abilities do not provoke attacks of opportunity. The knight and the weapon bearer
squire must be adjacent to each other and must both be able to move and communicate to use these
abilities.

**Swift Sharpening (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A weapon bearer squire can quickly hone her lord's weapons to make them more effective
in combat.

**Fullround:** A weapon bearer squire can use a [whetstone](ref/eq-misc/whetstone) to sharpen a
weapon she is holding, granting whoever wields the weapon a +1 circumstance bonus on his next attack
roll with that weapon.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** The 2nd-level fighter bonus combat feat

**Standard:** She can apply a [weapon blanch](ref/eq-misc/weapon-blanch-adamantine) to a weapon she
is holding. (This is normally a full-round action.)

**Combat Repairs (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A weapon bearer squire can ensure her liege never goes unarmed, even if his weapon has
all but shattered.

**Fullround:** A weapon bearer squire can make rapid repairs to a weapon she is holding and is
proficient with by succeeding at a DC 20 Craft (weapons) check. The item does not regain any hit
points, but loses the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition for a number of rounds equal to the weapon
bearer squire's class level.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 1
