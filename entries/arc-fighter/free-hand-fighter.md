The free hand fighter specializes in the delicate art of handling a single weapon in one hand while
using his free hand to balance, block, tip, and distract his opponents. While not a brawler, his
open hand is as much a weapon as a bow or blade. His fighting school benefits only apply when he is
using a one-handed weapon and carrying nothing in his other hand.

**Deceptive Strike (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A free hand fighter gains a +1 bonus to CMB and CMD on [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) checks
and on Bluff checks to feint or create a diversion to hide.

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: This bonus +2.

At 10th level: This bonus +3.

At 14th level: This bonus +4.

At 18th level: This bonus +5.

**Elusive (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A free hand fighter gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC.

**Replaces:** Armor training 1, 2, 3, and 4

**Special:** This bonus does not apply when wearing medium or heavy armor or carrying a medium or
heavier load.

At 6th level: This bonus +2.

At 10th level: This bonus +3.

At 14th level: This bonus +4.

At 18th level: This bonus +5.

**Singleton (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A free hand fighter gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls when wielding a melee
weapon in one hand and leaving his other hand free.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1 and 4

At 11th level: This bonus +2.

At 17th level: This bonus +3.

**Timely Tip (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Move:** A free hand fighter can make a disarm combat maneuver against a target he threatens to
push aside the target's shield. If successful, the target loses its shield bonus to AC against the
free hand fighter's next attack.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Interference (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Move:** A free hand fighter can make a disarm or [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver against a
target he threatens to push his opponent off balance. If successful, the target becomes
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed). This condition lasts until the target takes damage from a melee
or ranged attack or until the beginning of the free hand fighter's next turn, whichever comes first.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Reversal (Ex)**

**Immediate:** A free hand fighter can make a disarm combat maneuver against a creature he threatens
when he is the target of a melee attack from another creature. If successful, the attack changes to
target the target of the free hand fighter's maneuver instead of the free hand fighter himself.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Armor mastery
