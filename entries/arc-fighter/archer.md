The archer is dedicated to the careful mastery of the bow, perfecting his skills with years of
practice honed day after day on ranges and hunting for game, or else on the battlefield, raining
destruction down on the enemy lines.

**Hawkeye (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** An archer gains a bonus on Perception checks equal to 1 +
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his fighter level. The range increment for any bow he
uses increases by 5 feet, plus an additional 5 feet for every four fighter levels he has.

**Replaces:** Bravery

**Trick Shot (Ex)**

**Choice:** An archer can choose one of the following combat maneuvers or actions:
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm), [feint](ref/rule/feint), or [sunder](ref/rule/sunder). He can perform
this action with a bow against any target within 30 feet, with a -4 penalty to his CMB.

**At 11th Level:** He can choose a third trick shot option. Those options expand to include
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [grapple](ref/rule/grapple), and [trip](ref/rule/trip). A target
grappled by an arrow can break free by destroying the archer's arrow ([hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
5, hit points 1, break DC 13) or with an Escape Artist or CMB check (against the archer's CMD -4).

**At 15th Level:** He can choose a fourth trick shot.

**At 19th Level:** He can choose a fifth trick shot.

**At 7th Level:** He can choose a second option to receive this same benefit.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 1, 2, 3, and 4

**Special:** These maneuvers use up arrows as normal.

**Expert Archer (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** An archer gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with bows.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

At 9th level: This bonus +2.

At 13th level: This bonus +3.

At 17th level: This bonus +4.

**Safe Shot (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** An archer does not provoke attacks of opportunity when making ranged attacks with a
bow.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Evasive Archer (Ex)**

**At 17th Level:** This bonus becomes +4.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** An archer gains a +2 dodge bonus to AC against ranged attacks.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Volley (Ex)**

**Fullround:** An archer can make a single bow attack at his highest base attack bonus against any
number of creatures in a 15-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst), making separate attack and damage
rolls for each creature.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Ranged Defense (Ex)**

**Immediate:** He can catch an arrow fired at him and shoot it any target he chooses, as if he had
the [Snatch Arrows](ref/feat/snatch-arrows) feat.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Passive:** An archer gains DR 5/- against ranged attacks.

**Replaces:** Armor mastery

**Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Alters:** Weapon mastery

**Info:** An archer must choose a type of bow.
