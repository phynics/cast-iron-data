**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 11\
Drill sergeants excel at training other combatants in fighting techniques.

**Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** A drill sergeant gains the [cavalier's](ref/class/cavalier) _tactician_ class feature,
treating his fighter level as his cavalier level for the purposes of this ability.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Bravery

**Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** You receive a teamwork feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the prerequisites for this
feat.

**Flavor:** Text from the cavalier class

**Standard:** You can grant this feat to all allies within 30 feet who can see and hear you. Allies
retain the use of this bonus feat for a number of rounds equal to 3 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cavalier level. Allies do not need to meet the
prerequisites of these bonus feats.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 cavalier levels

**Weapon Training (Ex)**

**Ability:** A drill sergeant gains _weapon training_ with one
[group of weapons](ref/ability/weapon-groups). He doesn't gain _weapon training_ with other groups
at later levels, though his _weapon training_ bonus improves by 1 for every 4 levels after 5th
level.

**Alters:** Weapon training 1

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Greater Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** The drill sergeant gains the cavalier's _greater tactician_ class feature.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 2

**Greater Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** You receive an additional teamwork feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the
prerequisites for this feat. You can grant this feat to your allies using the _tactician_ ability.

**Flavor:** Text from the cavalier class

**Swift:** Using the _tactician_ ability becomes a **swift action**.

**Weapon Trainer (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** When a drill sergeant wields a weapon he has _weapon training_ in, all allies within 30
feet who can see and hear the drill sergeant gain [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his
_weapon training_ bonus when they wield any weapon from the same weapon group. This bonus doesn't
stack with any _weapon training_ bonus an ally already possesses.

**Replaces:** Weapon training 3

**Master Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** The drill sergeant gains the cavalier's _master tactician_ class feature.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 4

**Master Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** You receive an additional teamwork feat as a bonus feat. You must meet the
prerequisites for this feat. You can grant this feat to your allies using the _tactician_ ability.

**Flavor:** Text from the cavalier ability

**Special:** Whenever you use the _tactician_ ability, you grant any two teamwork feats that you
know. You can select from **any** of your teamwork feats, not just your bonus feats.
