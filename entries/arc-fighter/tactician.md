While many fighters focus on the fundamentals of melee and ranged combat, there are those who are
trained to view the bigger picture on the battlefield. These fighters use their training and
tactical acumen to overcome challenges that would overwhelm mere brute strength and skill at arms.

**Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Armor proficiencies

**Info:** A tactician is not proficient with heavy armor or tower shields.

**Strategic Training (Ex)**

**Info:** A tactician gains 4 skill points + a number of skill points equal to his Intelligence
modifier at each level, instead of the normal 2 skill points + Intelligence modifier at each level.
Furthermore, [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) (Cha),
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) (Int),
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) (Int), [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics)
(Int), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) (Wis) are all class skills for the tactician.

**Replaces:** The bonus fighter combat feat gained at 1st level

**Bonus Feats**

**Info:** A tactician may choose [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) or any teamwork feat, in
addition to combat feats, as bonus feats.

**Tactical Awareness (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A tactician gains a +1 bonus on initiative checks.

**Replaces:** Bravery

At 6th level: This bonus +2.

At 10th level: This bonus +3.

At 14th level: This bonus +4.

At 18th level: This bonus +5.

**Tactician (Ex)**

**Ability:** A tactician gains this ability as the [cavalier](ref/class/cavalier) class feature.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Weapon training 1

**Special:** If the tactician also has cavalier levels, these levels stack for determining the
number of uses per day, and he can take the better progression.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per fighter level

**Cooperative Combatant (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a tactician uses the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) special attack, he may
affect one additional ally per point of Intelligence bonus. For each ally that a tactician aids, he
can pick whether to grant that ally the +2 bonus on its next attack against the opponent or the +2
bonus to AC against the opponent's next attack on that ally, and can grant different allies
different bonuses.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 3

**Battle Insight (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Armor training 4

**Swift:** A tactician can grant his Intelligence modifier as an insight bonus on the attack rolls
made by a single ally within line of sight that can both see and hear the tactician. That ally gains
the bonus until the end of the tactician's next turn.

**Usage:** Int3 modifier times/day
