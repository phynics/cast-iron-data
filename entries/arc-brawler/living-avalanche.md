When a living avalanche is on the move, no one can stand in her way.

**Earth Discipline (Ex)**

**At 2nd Level:** A living avalanche gains [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun) as a bonus
feat, even if she doesn't meet the prerequisites.

**At 5th Level:** She gains [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush) as a bonus feat, even
if she doesn't meet the prerequisites.

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 2nd and 5th levels

**Landslide (Ex)**

**Alters:** Maneuver training

**At 11th Level:** If she succeeds at an overrun combat maneuver as part of a
[charge](ref/rule/charge), she automatically knocks her target [prone](ref/rule/prone) (no matter
the result of her check). In addition, if the living avalanche succeeds at a bull rush combat
maneuver as part of a charge, she reduces the penalty combat maneuver checks to bull rush each
creature beyond the first by 2.

**At 15th Level:** The living avalanche can attempt bull rush and overrun combat maneuvers against
creatures two sizes larger than herself, but only as part of a charge.

**At 19th Level:** If the living avalanche succeeds at a bull rush combat maneuver as part of a
charge, she reduces the penalty on combat maneuver checks to bull rush each creature beyond the
first by an additional 2. When attempting an overrun combat maneuver as part of a charge, she also
ignores any increase to a creature's CMD from having more than two legs.

**At 3rd Level:** A living avalanche must choose [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) as her first combat
maneuver for maneuver training.

**At 7th Level:** She must choose [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) as her second combat maneuver for
maneuver training. She doesn't gain training in any new combat maneuvers at later levels, but her
bonuses on combat maneuver checks and to CMD for bull rush and overrun combat maneuvers increase as
normal.

**Unyielding (Ex)**

**At 13th Level:** This becomes DR 3/adamantine.

**At 18th Level:** This becomes DR 4/adamantine.

**At 4th Level:** When a living avalanche wears light armor or no armor, she gains DR 1/adamantine.

**At 9th Level:** This becomes DR 2/adamantine.

**Replaces:** The brawler's AC bonus

**Avalanche (Ex)**

**Fullround:** A living avalanche can attempt to overrun multiple creatures. The living avalanche
moves up to twice her speed in a straight line and attempts an overrun combat maneuver against each
creature in her path, as long as that creature is no more than two size categories larger than
herself. Each creature she succeeds against is knocked prone and takes damage as if the living
avalanche hit it with a close weapon she is wielding or an unarmed strike. If she fails her combat
maneuver check against a creature, she stops her movement in the space directly in front of the
creature.

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Replaces:** Awesome blow

**Improved Avalanche (Ex)**

**Ability:** If a living avalanche rolls a natural 20 on an overrun combat maneuver check as part of
the avalanche ability, she can immediately attempt to confirm the critical hit by attempting another
combat maneuver check with the same modifiers as the previous one; if the critical hit is confirmed,
the attack deals double damage.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Improved awesome blow
