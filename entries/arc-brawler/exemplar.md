**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 86\
A versatile soldier who inspires her companions with her fighting prowess, an exemplar is at home on
the front lines of battles anywhere.

**Call to Arms (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** She can use it as a **free action**.

**At 12th Level:** She can use it as an **immediate action**.

**At 6th Level:** The exemplar can use it as a **swift action** instead.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Move:** An exemplar can expend a use of martial flexibility to rouse her allies into action. All
allies within 30 feet are no longer [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed), even if they are surprised.

**Replaces:** Unarmed strike, close weapon mastery

**Inspiring Prowess (Ex)**

**Ability:** An exemplar gains the ability to use certain bardic performances. The exemplar's
effective bard level for this ability is equal to her brawler level - 2.

**At 11th Level:** The exemplar can use [inspire greatness](ref/performance/inspire-greatness).

**At 15th Level:** The exemplar can use [inspire heroics](ref/performance/inspire-heroics).

**At 3rd Level:** The exemplar can use [inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Maneuver training, AC bonus

**Special:** Instead of the Perform skill, she activates this ability with impressive flourishes and
displays of martial talent (this uses visual components). This ability otherwise functions as bardic
performance; feats and other effects that affect bardic performance (such as the
[Extra Performance](ref/feat/extra-performance) feat) apply to it.

**Usage:** brawler level + Charisma2 modifier rounds/day

**Field Instruction (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** She can use this three times a day.

**At 17th Level:** She can use this four times a day.

**At 9th Level:** She can use this twice a day.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Brawler's strike

**Standard:** An exemplar can grant a teamwork feat to all allies within 30 feet who can see and
hear her. This teamwork feat must be one the exemplar knows or has gained with the martial
flexibility ability. Allies retain the use of this teamwork feat for a number of rounds equal to 3 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her brawler level. If the granted teamwork feat is one
gained from martial flexibility, this duration ends immediately if the exemplar loses access to that
feat. Allies don't need to meet the prerequisites of this teamwork feat. This ability otherwise
counts as the cavalier's _tactician_ class feature; feats and other effects which affect _tactician_
(such as [Practiced Tactician](ref/feat/practiced-tactician)) apply to it.

**Usage:** Once a day
