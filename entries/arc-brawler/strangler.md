**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 88\
A strangler is trained to choke the life out of her victims with her vise-like grip. Some stranglers
are self-taught and are little more than brutish murderers, unhinged sociopaths, or opportunistic
alley-bashers. Others are members of murder cults or specialized schools of assassination, trained
since the cradle to kill with their bare hands.

**Gained class skills:** stl

**Strangle (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** A strangler deals +1d6 [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) damage whenever
she succeeds at a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) check to damage or pin an opponent. The strangler is
always considered flanking her target for the purpose of using this ability.

**At 15th Level:** The damage becomes +4d6.

**At 2nd Level:** The damage becomes +2d6.

**At 8th Level:** The damage becomes +3d6.

**Replaces:** Unarmed strike, brawler's flurry

**Practiced Strangler (Ex)**

**At 2nd Level:** When a strangler has the [grappled](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) condition, she
does not take a -4 penalty to Dexterity and does not lose her Dexterity bonus to AC.

**At 9th Level:** A grappling or grappled strangler still threatens an area and is still able to
make attacks of opportunity while grappling or grappled; when pinned, she is not denied her
Dexterity bonus and does not take a -4 penalty to Armor Class.

**Replaces:** AC bonus

**Sleeper Hold (Ex)**

**Ability:** A strangler can instantly render a pinned opponent unconscious. This works like the
_knockout_ ability, but requires a successful grapple combat maneuver check against the pinned
opponent and does not have to damage the opponent.

**Alters:** Knockout

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Special:** Creatures that do not need to breathe, are immune to critical hits, or are immune to
nonlethal damage are immune to this ability.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 6 brawler levels beyond 4th

**Neckbreaker (Ex)**

**At 20th Level:** The strangler can attempt this as a **move action**.

**Replaces:** Awesome blow, improved awesome blow

**Special:** Creatures that are immune to critical hits are immune to this ability.

**Standard:** A strangler can attempt to instantly kill a pinned opponent as a standard action. This
works like the _sleeper hold_ ability, but imposes a -5 penalty on her grapple combat maneuver
check. If the opponent succeeds at its Fortitude save, the strangler deals damage as if she had
attempted the grapple check to damage her opponent; if the opponent fails its Fortitude save, it
dies.
