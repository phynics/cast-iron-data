## Plane of Earth Domain

**Sources:** Planes of Power pg 6

**Granted Powers:** You can navigate the untold wonders of the
[Plane of Earth](ref/rule/plane-of-earth) with ease.

**Wild Empathy**

**Info:** You can use [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) to influence a creature with the
earth subtype if its Intelligence score is 1 or 2.

**Spelunker (Su)**

**Flavor:** You ably clamber through underground areas.

**Free:** You can ignore [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) from stony surfaces,
you take no penalties for squeezing through rocky areas, and you gain
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 5/- against rocky hazards such as
[spike growth](ref/spell/spike-growth). This effect lasts for 1 round.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**One with the Stone (Su)**

**Choice:** You can choose between one of two options.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Earth Glide**

**Swift:** You gain the [earth glide](ref/umr/earth-glide) universal monster ability with a speed
equal to your base speed. You can breathe normally while using this ability.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per druid level

**Familiar**

**Ability:** You can choose to gain a [Small earth elemental](ref/monster/earth-elemental) as a
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) as if you possessed the
[Improved Familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar) feat. Your effective wizard level for this ability
is equal to your druid level and stacks with other classes that grant a
[familiar](ref/ability/familiars).

**Domain Spells**

1. stone fist
1. elemental speech
1. burrow
1. planar adaptation
1. passwall
1. mass planar adaptation
1. stone to flesh
1. repel metal or stone
1. clashing rocks
