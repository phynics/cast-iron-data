## Plane of Fire Domain

**Sources:** Planes of Power pg 7

**Granted Powers:** You embrace the destructive flames and ever-roaring volcanoes of the
[Plane of Fire](ref/rule/plane-of-fire), having proven yourself impervious to its heat.

**Wild Empathy**

**Info:** You can use [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) to influence a creature with the fire
subtype if its Intelligence score is 1 or 2.

**Fire Hardened (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can touch a willing creature to grant these benefits for 1 hour.

**Passive:** You ignore fire damage from the fire-dominant planar trait, and you gain fire
resistance 5. If you have natural fire resistance, it increases by 5 instead, and if you are
naturally immune to fire, you heal hit points and [ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) at
twice the normal rate when resting on a fire-dominant plane.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**All-Consuming Flames (Su)**

**Choice:** You can choose between one of two options.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Flaming Benefits**

**Standard:** You can channel the extraplanar heat into your allies' weapons within 30 feet. For 1
minute, the affected weapons gain the cold-outsider-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane), fire-outsider-bane,
or [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon special abilities. You must grant each weapon the same
ability, and this ability affects no more than two of any ally's weapons.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 druid levels beyond 8th

**Familiar**

**Ability:** You can choose to gain a [Small fire elemental](ref/monster/fire-elemental) as a
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar). as if you possessed the
[Improved Familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar) feat. Your effective wizard level for this ability
is equal to your druid level and stacks with other classes that grant a
[familiar](ref/ability/familiars).

**Domain Spells**

1. obscuring mist (creates smoke)
1. elemental speech
1. ash storm
1. planar adaptation
1. scorching ash form
1. mass planar adaptation
1. delayed blast fireball
1. destruction
1. fiery body
