## Mountain Domain

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 35

**Granted Powers:** You have mastered the mighty powers of the great mountains that pierce the sky
and stand aloof above the lowlands.

**Foothold (Su)**

**Standard:** You can cause an adjacent stone surface up to 10 feet square to mold itself into
ridges and creases. A foothold that is created on a horizontal surface is treated as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles), and a Medium or smaller creature standing in
the area or moving through it takes a -2 penalty on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks and to
CMD due to poor footing. A foothold that is created on a vertical surface grants a +10 bonus on
[Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks to climb it. The stone remains altered for 1 hour.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Thin Air (Su)**

**Free:** You can surround yourself with a 5-foot aura of supernaturally thin air that draws the
breath from creatures adjacent to you. Creatures beginning their turn in the aura are
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) (Fortitude negates). A fatigued creature that begins its turn in the
aura must save or suffer from [altitude sickness](ref/rule/mountain-travel), taking 1 point of
ability damage to all ability scores.

**At 16th Level:** The aura increases to 10 feet.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Special:** Creatures that do not breathe are immune to this aura.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per druid level
