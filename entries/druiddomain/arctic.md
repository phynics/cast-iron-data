## Arctic Domain

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 33

**Granted Powers:** You master the might of the endless, snow-covered wastes beneath the midnight
sun, where icy polar winds blow.

**Call Cold (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) (as a cleric of your druid level),
but only to heal creatures with the cold subtype or to command them (similar to using the
[Command Undead](ref/feat/command-undead) feat against undead). You can take other feats that add to
this ability, such as [Extra Channel](ref/feat/extra-channel) and
[Improved Channel](ref/feat/improved-channel), but not feats that alter this ability, such as
[Elemental Channel](ref/feat/elemental-channel) and [Alignment Channel](ref/feat/alignment-channel).
The DC to save against this ability is equal to 10 + 1/2 your druid level + your Charisma modifier.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Banish Flame (Su)**

**Ability:** You may use your _call cold_ ability to damage creatures with the fire subtype (as a
cleric channeling negative energy) or to cause them to flee from you (as the
[Turn Undead](ref/feat/turn-undead) feat). Alternatively, you may use this ability to negate magical
fire effects as if using a targeted [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic).

**At 12th Level:** This functions as targeted or area
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater) instead of
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)._

**Gained:** At 6th Level
