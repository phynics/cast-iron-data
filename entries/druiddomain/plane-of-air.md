## Plane of Air Domain

**Sources:** Planes of Power pg 6

**Granted Powers:** You draw your power from the
[boundless expanse of air and clouds](ref/rule/plane-of-air).

**Fly**

**Info:** [Fly](ref/skill/fly) is a class skill for you, and you can take ranks in the skill even
without first having a reliable means to fly or glide.

**Wild Empathy**

**Info:** You can use [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) to influence a creature with the air
subtype if its Intelligence score is 1 or 2.

**Aerial Agility (Ex)**

**Flavor:** You can navigate the air unhindered.

**Free:** You automatically succeed at your Wisdom check to change your direction in an area of
subjective gravity, and you can increase or decrease your falling speed by 10 feet per druid level
during the first round after you change the direction of gravity.

When you are in areas of light, normal, or heavy gravity, activating this ability instead grants you
a bonus equal to your druid level on Fly checks and [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to
jump for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Wind Savant (Su)**

**Choice:** You can choose between one of two options.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Wind Benefits**

**Passive:** You treat the penalties from natural or magical [wind](ref/rule/winds) effects as two
steps less severe. In addition, you are surrounded by a cushion of air that grants you a bonus equal
to 1/2 your druid level on saving throws against gases, gaseous breath weapons, inhaled poisons, and
similar effects.

**Familiar**

**Ability:** You can choose to gain a [Small air elemental](ref/monster/air-elemental) as a
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) as if you possessed the
[Improved Familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar) feat. Your effective wizard level for this ability
is equal to your druid level and stacks with other classes that grant a
[familiar](ref/ability/familiars).
