## Plains Domain

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 35

**Granted Powers:** The wide open spaces of the prairie, savanna, and steppe are yours to master.

**Migrating Herd (Su)**

**Ability:** When you summon an [aurochs, bison](ref/monster/herd-animal-aurochs),
[elephant, mastodon](ref/monster/elephant), [horse](ref/monster/horse),
[pony](ref/monster/horse-pony), or similar quadruped herbivore with a summoning spell, the duration
of the spell is increased by 100% (this does not stack with [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell)).
The creature's land speed is increased by 10 feet.

**Pounce (Ex)**

**Ability:** You may use the [pounce](ref/umr/pounce) special attack once per day.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

At 9th level: You can use this ability 2 times a day.

At 12th level: You can use this ability 3 times a day.

At 15th level: You can use this ability 4 times a day.

At 18th level: You can use this ability 5 times a day.
