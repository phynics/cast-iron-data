## Monkey Domain

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 34

**Granted Powers:** The balanced wisdom, skill, agility, and playfulness of the monkey are the model
for your spiritual discipline.

**Familiar**

**Ability:** You gain a [monkey](ref/monster/familiar-monkey) [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar).
Your effective wizard level for this ability is equal to your druid level. Your druid level stacks
with levels from other classes that grant [familiars](ref/ability/familiars) when determining the
powers of your familiar.

**Monkey Athletics (Su)**

**Free:** You may add a competence bonus equal to half your druid level (minimum of +1) on one
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics), [Climb](ref/skill/climb),
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device), or [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Ranged Legerdemain (Su)**

**Ability:** You can use the Disable Device and Sleight of Hand skills at a range of 30 feet.
Working at a distance increases the normal skill check DC by 5, and you cannot take 10 on this
check. Any object to be manipulated must weigh 5 pounds or less. You can only use this ability if
you have at least 1 rank in the skill being used.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Domain Spells**

1. beguiling gift
1. spider climb
1. cacophonous call
1. freedom of movement
1. summon nature's ally V (dire ape or girallon only)
1. confusion
1. animal shapes (apes and monkeys only)
1. clenched fist
1. foresight
