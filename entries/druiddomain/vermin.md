## Vermin Domain

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 46, Inner Sea Monster Codex pg 35

**Granted Powers:** You see the perfection of nature in the spindly limbs and strange forms of all
vermin, great and small alike.

**Vermin Whisperer (Su)**

**Ability:** You can use your [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) ability on vermin. When you
do so, you impart a modicum of implanted intelligence on the vermin, allowing you to interact with
vermin as if they were animals. _Vermin whisperer_ functions only on vermin that are mindless or
have an Intelligence score of 2 or lower.

**Sudden Sting (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Swift:** You can inflict a lingering, painful sting. This requires a successful melee touch
attack. This sting deals an amount of piercing damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your druid level, and the target must succeed at a
Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half your druid level + your Constitution modifier) or become
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. This is a pain effect.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day
