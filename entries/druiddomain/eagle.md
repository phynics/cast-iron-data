## Eagle Domain

**Granted Powers:** Your spirit soars with the mightiest and noblest of all winged creatures.

**Familiar**

**Ability:** You gain a [hawk](ref/monster/familiar-hawk) [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar). Your
effective wizard level for this ability is equal to your druid level. Your druid level stacks with
levels from other classes that grant familiars when determining the powers of your familiar.

**Hawkeye (Su)**

**Swift:** You may add a bonus equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your druid level
(minimum +1) on one ranged attack or on one [Perception](ref/skill/perception) check.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Aerial evasion (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** You gain [improved evasion](ref/talent/improved-evasion) while flying.

**At 6th Level:** You gain the [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) ability (as a rogue) when you are
flying.
