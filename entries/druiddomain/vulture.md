## Vulture Domain

**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 25

**Granted Powers:** Yours is the power of death and rebirth. Your spirit soars above all and moves
with the flow of unseen currents.

**Death's Companion (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Your totem grants you protection from the harbingers of death.

**Immediate:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against disease, death spells, and death effects
that lasts a number of rounds equal to your druid level.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

At 6th level: This bonus +4.

At 11th level: This bonus +6.

At 16th level: This bonus +8.

**Agent of Rebirth (Sp)**

**Ability:** You may expend a quantity of special oils worth 1,000 gp to cast
[reincarnate](ref/spell/reincarnate) as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) usable
once per day.

Additionally, when using this ability or casting _[reincarnate](ref/spell/reincarnate)_ as a
prepared spell, you have some influence over the physical form that the reincarnated spirit will
take. When rolling against the spell's incarnation table, you may roll twice and choose between the
two results.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Domain Spells**

1. deathwatch
1. glide
1. death knell
1. death ward
1. breath of life
1. circle of death
1. resurrection
1. finger of death
