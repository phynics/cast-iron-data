## Cave Domain

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 33

**Granted Powers:** The wonders and dark secrets of the deepest caves give you strength.

**Cavesight (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can grant [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet to a willing creature you
touch. This effect lasts 1 minute, or 1 hour if used on yourself.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Tremorsense (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 60 feet.

**At 6th Level:** You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 30 feet.

**Domain Spells**

1. detect aberration
1. stone call
1. deeper darkness
1. echolocation
1. wall of stone
1. conjure black pudding
1. statue (looking like a stalagmite or stalactite)
1. earthquake
1. imprisonment
