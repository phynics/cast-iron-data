## Swamp Domain

**Granted Powers:** The sour-sweet depths of bogs, marshes, and swamps are your places of power.

**Natural Healing (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) (as a cleric of your druid level),
but only to heal animals, plants, and vermin. You may reduce the number of dice healed to cure
[ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) (your choice) to all affected creatures, curing 1
point of ability damage for each d6 that the channel energy is reduced.

**Special:** You can take other feats to add to this ability, such as
[Extra Channel](ref/feat/extra-channel), but not feats that alter this ability, such as
[Elemental Channel](ref/feat/elemental-channel) and [Alignment Channel](ref/feat/alignment-channel).

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Reed Hunter (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** This improves to [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) 30 feet with respect to these
conditions.

**At 6th Level:** You gain [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) 30 feet with respect to
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) and [cover](ref/rule/cover) from fog, vegetation, or water.
