**Sources:** Arcane Anthology pg 13

The scholars of the [Magaambya](ref/rule/aff-i-school) claim that Old-Mage Jatembe developed this
spell to help eradicate the lost city of Ird, although such claims have never been confirmed. This
spell causes tall grass, weeds, and other plants to swell into Gargantuan vines and tendrils that
erupt from the ground, reaching for any structure or any creature of evil alignment, functioning
like black tentacles except as noted. When you are determining the tendrils' CMB, the tendrils use
your caster level as their base attack bonus and have a +13 bonus due to their Strength. Any tendril
that succeeds in grappling a foe deals 4d6+13 points of damage to the grappled creature.
Additionally, the tendrils created by this spell can detect evil auras, as if they had spent 3
rounds observing all creatures and objects within the spell's area using
[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil), and attack and impede the movement of only evil creatures,
though they also attack any structures in their area. Each structure suffers a single attack each
round; your caster level acts as the base attack bonus for these attacks, and the tendrils deal
4d6+13 points of damage on each successful attack. Creatures that lack evil auras are not attacked
or impeded by this spell.
