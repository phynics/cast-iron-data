**Sources:** Bestiary 6 pg 250\
**School** necromancy \[evil\]; **Level** 9\
**Casting Time** 9 hours (only during the new moon)\
**Components** V, S, F (ring of standing stones empowered with necromancy that cost 25,000 gp to
create via [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item)), sacrifice (18 living intelligent
beings of the same type as the caster, sacrificed every 30 minutes during the ritual's casting time)\
**Skill Checks** Intimidate DC 31, 2 successes; Knowledge (nature) DC 31, 3 successes; Knowledge
(religion) DC 31, 4 successes\
**Range** touch\
**Target** caster\
**Effect** NUL\
**Duration** instantaneous\
**Saving Throw** none; **SR** no\
**Backlash** The caster is reduced to -1 hp\
**Failure** The caster is immediately slain.

### Effect

After sacrificing 18 lives (traditionally, these lives are those of unwilling druids who would have
tried to stand against the blasphemy the caster is attempting), the caster sacrifices himself and
immediately rises from death as a [siabrae](ref/monster/siabrae).
