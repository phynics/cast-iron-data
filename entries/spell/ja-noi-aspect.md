title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 3: Alchemist, Arcanist, Bloodrager, Druid, Hunter, Investigator, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, DF
Range: personal
Duration: 1 round/level (d; see below)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: yes
Target: you
Subschool: Polymorph
:::

You gain some of the appearance and qualities of the ja noi, oni who take hobgoblin form. Your skin
turns a fiery red color and you gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 3, although this ability
cannot repair damage caused by acid or fire. You can dismiss this spell as an immediate action when
you fail a Will saving throw; if you do, you can reroll your saving throw, but you must take the
second result.
