::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: evocation \[acid, cold, electricity, or fire\]
Level: 4: Alchemist, Arcanist, Bloodrager, Investigator, Magus, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a dragon scale)
Range: 30 ft. or 60 ft.
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: Reflex half
Spell resistance: yes
Area: cone-shaped burst or line
:::

You breathe out a blast of energy. Creatures in the affected area take 1d6 points of energy damage
per caster level (maximum of 12d6). A successful Reflex save results in half damage. The spell's
effect and energy type depend on the type of dragon scale used:

> _Black dragon:_ 60-foot line of acid.
>
> _Blue or bronze dragon:_ 60-foot line of electricity.
>
> _Green dragon:_ 30-foot cone of acid.
>
> _Gold or red dragon:_ 30-foot cone of fire.
>
> _Silver or white dragon:_ 30-foot cone of cold.
>
> _Brass dragon:_ 60-foot line of fire.
>
> _Copper dragon:_ 60-foot line of acid.
