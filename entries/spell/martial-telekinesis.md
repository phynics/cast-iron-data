> _The following spell is available to the indicated race or ethnicity: Vudrani_

You mingle the power of your mind and body to exert force beyond your reach. This spell functions as
the combat maneuver version of [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis), except as noted above.
Alternatively, you can inflict a telekinetic unarmed strike with this spell, using your own unarmed
strike damage and applying your primary spellcasting ability score modifier to the damage in place
of your Strength modifier.
