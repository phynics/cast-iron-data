> _[Aasimars](ref/race/aasimar) have access to the following spell._

You surround yourself with a veil of positive energy, making it harder for evil outsiders to harm
you. For the duration of this spell, you gain a +2 sacred bonus to AC and on saves. Both of these
bonuses apply only against attacks or effects created by outsiders with the evil subtype. You can
dismiss this spell as a swift action to deal 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per paladin level to all
such outsiders within 5 feet. A Will save halves this damage.
