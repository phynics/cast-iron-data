title = "Spell statistics"
School: abjuration
Level: 1: Cleric, Inquisitor, Oracle, Shaman, Warpriest, Witch
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M/DF (a small knot of twin)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 round/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

You mimic tugging at the Green Mother's legendary Tangled Knot to subtly alter your fate. The next
single attack made against you takes a -10 penalty. If that attack is a critical hit, it is instead
treated as a normal hit.
