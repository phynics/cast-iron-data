Saving throw: Will negates (see text)

Target: one willing creature

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 228

You project your soul out of your body and into the body of a willing creature. This possession is
blocked by [protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) or a similar ward. The target's
soul shares its body with you; it is helpless but can still use its senses. You and the target's
soul can communicate telepathically as if using a common language.

You keep your Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, level, class, base attack bonus, base save bonuses,
alignment, and mental abilities. The body retains its Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, hit points,
natural abilities, and natural attacks (such as a bite or sting). A body with extra limbs does not
allow you to make more attacks (or more advantageous two-weapon attacks) than normal. You can't
choose to activate the body's extraordinary, spell-like, or supernatural abilities.

As a standard action, you can return to your own body, ending the spell. While your soul is
possessing the target, your body is helpless. If the host body is slain, you return to your own body
if it is within range, and the life force of the host departs (it is slain). If the host body is
slain beyond the range of the spell, both you and the host die. Any life force with nowhere to go is
treated as slain.
