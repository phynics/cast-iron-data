**Sources:** Arcane Anthology pg 8

You inspire the target with the power to seize a grand destiny. The target gains a +4 competence
bonus that it can choose to apply before rolling any attack roll, caster level check, saving throw,
or skill check. The spell ends once the bonus has been applied to two rolls. At 12th level and 15th
level, the bonus can be applied an additional time before the spell is discharged. A subject can be
under the effect of only one grand destiny spell at a time.
