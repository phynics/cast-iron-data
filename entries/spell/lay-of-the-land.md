Target: you

**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 29

In a flash of recognition, you learn about the geography of your surroundings within a radius of 1
mile per 2 caster levels (minimum 1 mile). This instant familiarity grants you an insight bonus
equal to your caster level (maximum +5) on Knowledge (geography) checks and Survival checks to avoid
getting lost so long as you remain in the affected area.

Additionally, for the duration of the spell you can make Knowledge (geography) checks regarding the
affected area as though you were trained in that skill.
