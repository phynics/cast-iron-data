::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: abjuration
Level: 3: Bard, Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Medium, Oracle, Shaman, Skald, Spiritualist, Warpriest, Witch
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

This spell allows you to channel the essence of Cayden Cailean's philosophy, giving you the benefits
of a freedom of movement spell and granting you a +4 bonus on saving throws against fear effects.
The spell also makes you drunk. For the duration of the spell, you take a -2 penalty on attack
rolls, ability checks, skill checks, and [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks. If you
somehow remove or avoid these penalties, the spell's duration ends.
