title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 3: Arcanist, Cleric, Inquisitor, Oracle, Sorcerer, Warpriest, Wizard
Casting time: 1 minute
Components: V, S
Range: personal
Duration: 24 hours or until discharged
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

Prized among those who seek to monitor the attention paid to them, this spell is said to be used by
[Arazni](ref/faith/arazni) to identify those followers who dare refer to her as "the Harlot Queen"
so she might summarily dismiss them. When you cast this spell, designate a word, phrase, or name. If
a creature within 1 mile of you uses that word, phrase, or name to refer to you, you immediately
learn that creature's name, appearance, and location. This discharges the spell.
