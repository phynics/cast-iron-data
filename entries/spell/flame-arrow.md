::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 3: Arcanist, Bloodrager, Magus, Occultist, Red Mantis Assassin, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a drop of oil and a small piece of flint)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 10 minutes/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: fifty projectiles, all of which must be together at the time of casting
Descriptors: fire
:::

This spell allows you to turn ammunition (such as arrows, crossbow bolts, shuriken, and sling
stones) into fiery projectiles. Each piece of ammunition deals an extra 1d6 points of fire damage to
any target it hits. A flaming projectile can easily ignite a flammable object or structure, but it
won't ignite a creature it strikes.
