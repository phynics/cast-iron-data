::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: enchantment
Level: 2: Alchemist, Arcanist, Bard, Investigator, Medium, Mesmerist, Occultist, Psychic, Skald, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: personal
Duration: 10 minutes/level or until discharged
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
Subschool: Compulsion
Descriptors: mind-affecting
:::

Your mind is able to correlate information effectively. When attempting an Appraise, Knowledge,
Linguistics, or Spellcraft check, you can roll twice and take the higher result. If you have an
ability that allows you to roll twice, you cannot use that ability and this benefit on the same
roll.

The spell is discharged once you have used its benefit a number of times equal to your caster level.
