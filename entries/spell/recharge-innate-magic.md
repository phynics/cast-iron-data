> _[Gnomes](ref/race/gnome) have access to the following spell._

You channel magic energy into your own aura, recharging your innate magic abilities. You regain one
use of all 0-level and 1st-level [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) you can use as
a result of a racial trait.
