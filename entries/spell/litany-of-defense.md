Level: 2: Antipaladin, Inquisitor, Paladin

Components: V, S, DF
Range: personal
Duration: 1 round
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: yes
Target: you

Invoking this litany strengthens your defenses. Any enhancement bonus your armor has is doubled and
you are immune to fear.

While subject to this spell, the target cannot be the target of another spell that has the word
"litany" in the title.
