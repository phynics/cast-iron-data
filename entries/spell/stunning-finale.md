::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: enchantment
Level: 5: Bard, Skald
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 1 round
Saving throw: Fortitude partial, see text
Spell resistance: yes
Targets: up to 3 creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
Subschool: Compulsion
Descriptors: mind-affecting
:::

You must have a bardic performance in effect to cast this spell. With a flourish, you immediately
end your bardic performance, assaulting the senses of the targets with your finale. Each target is
stunned for 1 round. On a successful saving throw, a target is staggered for 1 round.
