> _[Dwarven](ref/race/dwarf) spellcasters are renowned for shaping the ground beneath them, molding
> metal, and using magic to aid their craft. This spell one of several samples of such magics._

You can cast this spell as part of the action to begin an inspire competence bardic performance. The
benefit of inspire competence persists for as long as is necessary to complete the target's next
skill check using the chosen skill (up to a maximum of 1 hour per caster level), even if you cease
your bardic performance.
