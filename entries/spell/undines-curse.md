> _[Undines](ref/race/undine) have access to the following spell._

The target loses its body's natural ability to breathe automatically. As long as it remains
conscious and is able to take physical actions, it keeps breathing and is able to function normally.
If it is ever unconscious (including sleeping) or unable to take physical actions, it stops
breathing and stops breathing, must hold its breath, and might begin to suffocate. Creatures that do
not have to breathe are immune to this spell.
