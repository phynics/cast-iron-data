::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 2: Arcanist, Magus, Occultist, Psychic, Red Mantis Assassin, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 minute
Components: V, S, F (a miniature of the target siege engine, costing 10 gp)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: one disassembled siege engine
:::

You assemble a siege engine, using up to one fewer worker per two caster levels you possess. You can
do without one additional worker if you have any ranks in Knowledge (engineering), plus one
additional worker per 5 ranks you have in that skill. If your caster level combined with Knowledge
(engineering) fails to eliminate the need for workers, this spell fails.
