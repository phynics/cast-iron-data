::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 4: Alchemist, Investigator\
6: Arcanist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: S, M (a mixture of oil and water)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
Descriptors: water
:::

When you cast this spell, your body takes on a slick, oily appearance. For the duration of this
spell, your form can stretch and shift with ease and becomes slightly transparent, as if you were
composed of liquid. This transparency is not enough to grant concealment. You gain DR 10/slashing
and your reach increases by 10 feet. In addition, you can pass through small holes or narrow
openings, even mere cracks, with anything you were carrying at the time the spell was cast (except
other creatures). Finally, you can move through water with a swim speed of 60 feet and can breathe
both water and air for the duration of this effect. You are treated as if you had the water subtype
while this spell is in effect.
