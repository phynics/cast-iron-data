::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: illusion
Level: 2: Arcanist, Bard, Mesmerist, Psychic, Red Mantis Assassin, Skald, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 5 minute/level (maximum 1 hour)
Saving throw: Will negates
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one humanoid creature
Subschool: Phantasm
:::

This spell induces a hallucinogenic reality in the mind of your target. Surfaces seem to swim, and
movement constantly distracts the eye. The target takes a -2 penalty on Will saving throws, caster
level checks, Intelligence-based skill checks, and Wisdom-based skill checks.
