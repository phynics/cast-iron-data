::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 6: Alchemist, Investigator, Magus, Summoner\
7: Arcanist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a wren's egg)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 round/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
Subschool: Teleportation
:::

When under the effects of this spell, you can teleport up to 30 feet as a move action. You must end
this movement in an unoccupied space that you can stand on within line of sight. Alternatively, you
can spend a move action to teleport to a standing position from lying prone. Teleporting does not
provoke attacks of opportunity.
