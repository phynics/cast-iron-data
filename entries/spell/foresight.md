**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 285

This spell grants you a powerful sixth sense in relation to yourself or another. Once foresight is
cast, you receive instantaneous warnings of impending danger or harm to the subject of the spell.
You are never surprised or flat-footed. In addition, the spell gives you a general idea of what
action you might take to best protect yourself and gives you a +2 insight bonus to AC and on Reflex
saves. This insight bonus is lost whenever you would lose a Dexterity bonus to AC.

When another creature is the subject of the spell, you receive warnings about that creature. You
must communicate what you learn to the other creature for the warning to be useful, and the creature
can be caught unprepared in the absence of such a warning. Shouting a warning, yanking a person
back, and even telepathically communicating (via an appropriate spell) can all be accomplished
before some danger befalls the subject, provided you act on the warning without delay. The subject,
however, does not gain the insight bonus to AC and Reflex saves.
