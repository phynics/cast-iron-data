::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: abjuration
Level: 2: Arcanist, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Medium, Mesmerist, Oracle, Ranger, Skald, Sorcerer, Summoner, Unchained Summoner, Warpriest, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: personal
Duration: 10 minutes/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no (harmless)
Effect: 20-foot-radius burst of stabilizing magic centered on you
:::

This spell creates an area of stabilizing magical energy that emanates from you. Any creature within
this area that fails a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check to cast a spell normally in an
area of wild magic rolls twice to determine the wild magic effect and choose its preferred result.
