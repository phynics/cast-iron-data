Spell resistance: yes
Target: one creature or object/level touched

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 234

With a gesture, this spell allows you to dismiss even the foulest or most distinctive scents. When
cast, the targeted creatures or objects lose all natural and unnatural odors. A creature under the
effect of negate aroma cannot be tracked, located, or pinpointed by the scent special quality. In
addition, this spell prevents the target creature from using the stench special ability and similar
odor-based abilities (such as those possessed by troglodytes).

Negate aroma does not prevent the target from acquiring outside smells or odors. Dowsing the
creature with a pungent substance effectively negates the benefits of the spell until the substance
is neutralized or washed away.
