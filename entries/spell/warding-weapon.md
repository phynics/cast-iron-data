::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: abjuration
Level: 1: Bloodrager, Magus, Occultist\
2: Arcanist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Summoner, Unchained Summoner, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, F (one melee weapon you are proficient in and you are holding)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 round/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

The focus of this spell flies upward above your head and takes a defensive position within your
space. It lunges at opponents, as if guided by a martially trained hand, parrying and turning back
melee attacks aimed at you, but does not strike back at any opponent nor does it damage them. The
weapon serves only as a defense. While it protects you, you can cast spells without provoking
attacks of opportunity, without the need to cast them defensively.

A creature with the [Disruptive](ref/feat/disruptive) feat can easily bypass this spell's defenses.
You provoke attacks of opportunity for casting spells against these creatures even when subject to
this spell, though you can still choose to cast defensively.
