::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 4: Summoner, Unchained Summoner
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a stick of incense)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
Subschool: Healing
:::

Your eidolon is restored to full health when you summon it. Upon casting this spell, you must
immediately begin the ritual to summon your eidolon. Upon completion of the ritual, your eidolon
appears at full hit points and without any damage or penalties to its ability scores, regardless of
its previous condition. In addition, any temporary negative conditions affecting your eidolon are
immediately removed. Permanent conditions and ability drain are not affected by this spell.
