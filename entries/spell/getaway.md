::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 6: Arcanist, Bard, Mesmerist, Occultist, Psychic, Skald, Sorcerer, Spiritualist, Wizard
Casting time: 1 minute
Components: V, S, M (a brass doorknob)
Range: unlimited
Duration: 1 hour/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Targets: you and one willing creature/2 levels, all of which must be within 30 feet of you
Subschool: Teleportation
:::

Getaway teleports you and allies you designate at the time of casting to a predetermined location.
You must initially cast the spell at that location-all creatures affected by the spell must be
present at that time and within 30 feet of you. At any time before the spell's duration expires, you
may trigger the spell as a swift action. At this time, all affected creatures (or their remains and
gear) within 30 feet of you are teleported to the location where you first cast the spell. Creatures
more than 30 feet away from you are left behind. Selected creatures within 30 feet of you who do not
wish to travel with you at that time can simply choose not to go. You can be transported any
distance within a plane but cannot travel between planes.
