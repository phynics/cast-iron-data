::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 2: Arcanist, Magus, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: 0 ft.
Duration: 1 minute/level (d) or until discharged (see text)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Effect: whip of water
Subschool: Creation
Descriptors: water
:::

You create a coil of flowing fresh water, functioning a whip appropriate for your size, except you
make a melee touch attack instead of a regular attack. Anything you strike with the whip takes
damage and is doused with 1 pint of water. A creature with the fire subtype takes an additional 1d6
points of damage. If the target is on fire, it gains a +2 bonus on its next saving throw to
extinguish the flames.

After you have successfully used the whip to hit a creature a number of times equal to your caster
level, the water is expended and the spell is discharged. The water created by this spell is
otherwise identical to that produced by create water.
