Level: 1: Arcanist, Bard, Cleric, Mesmerist, Occultist, Oracle, Paladin, Psychic, Skald, Sorcerer, Warpriest, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: personal
Duration: 24 hours
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

While protected by fastidiousness, your person and your equipment stay clean and dry. Dust and
moisture slough off immediately; even when you are completely soiled, your appearance reverts to a
pristine state over the course of 1 minute. While this spell offers no protection against most
attacks, staying constantly sanitary grants you a +2 circumstance bonus on Fortitude saving throws
against inhaled and ingested diseases.

Members of the Prophet of Kalistrade prestige class can cast this spell, even though it is not a
divination or enchantment.
