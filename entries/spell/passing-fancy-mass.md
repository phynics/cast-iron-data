## Passing Fancy

**Sources:** Spymaster's Handbook pg 29

Taldor's infamous Lion Blades imported this spell from the ninja clans of Minkai, and both groups
use it to great effect when maneuvering among the shallow upper classes. When you cast the spell,
you dictate one specific topic or recent event and instill a magical obsession with that subject
within the target. An affected creature becomes fascinated by the topic, and becomes completely
focused on discussing the event with anyone nearby, possibly wandering off or distracting unaffected
creatures. Passing fancy has no effect on a creature with an Intelligence of 2 or less.

Because the target talks and interacts with its usual cadence, onlookers take a -10 penalty on Sense
Motive checks to notice that anything is amiss with the target's behavior.

### Mass Passing Fancy

**Sources:** Spymaster's Handbook pg 29

This spell functions as per passing fancy except as noted above.
