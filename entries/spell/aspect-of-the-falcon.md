**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 203

You take on an aspect of a falcon. Your eyes become wide and raptor-like, and you grow feathers on
the sides of your head. You gain a +3 competence bonus on Perception checks, a +1 competence bonus
on ranged attacks, and the critical multiplier for your bows and crossbows becomes 19-20/×3. This
effect does not stack with any other effect that expands the threat range of a weapon, such as the
[Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) feat or a keen weapon.
