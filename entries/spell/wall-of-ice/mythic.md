**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 111\
The wall's hit points increase to 5 hit points per inch of thickness. Add your tier to the Strength
check DC to break through the wall. The damage dealt to those that step through the wall increases
to 1d8 points of cold damage + 1 point per caster level. Any creature that passes through the wall
must succeed at a Fortitude save or be staggered for 1d4 rounds; this is a cold effect.

_Augmented (5th):_ If you expend two uses of mythic power, you cover the wall in sharp, protruding
icicles. Any creature that touches, strikes, or breaks through the wall takes 1d6 points of cold
damage, 1d6 points of piercing damage, and 1 point of bleed damage.
