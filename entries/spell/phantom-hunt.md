::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 4: Druid, Hunter, Ranger
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, DF
Range: personal (see text)
Duration: 1 hour/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you and one animal companion within 30 ft.
:::

You affect yourself and a single animal companion within 30 feet of you. Both you and the animal
companion gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to 100 feet, [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) to
20 feet, and a 40-foot enhancement bonus to movement. Furthermore, you can track while moving at
full speed without taking any penalty. If you already have the ability to track at full speed, you
gain a +4 bonus on Survival checks while tracking instead.

The duration of this spell immediately ends if you or the animal companion attack another creature
or are attacked by another creature.
