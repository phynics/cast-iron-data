> _The following spell is available to the indicated race or ethnicity: Mwangi_

You weave golden masks shaped like animals around the targets' faces, imbuing each target with an
animal spirit that matches her strength of character. Each target gains one animal special ability
of your choice granted by beast shape I. You can choose a different special ability for each target.
