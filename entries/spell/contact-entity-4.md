### Contact Entity IV

This spell functions like _[contact entity III](ref/spell/contact-entity-3),_ except that you can
contact creatures from the _contact entity IV_ list, and as noted above.

|Contact Entity IV|Material Component|
| --- | --- |
|[Bhole](ref/monster/bhole)|Jar of slime created by a bhole's breath weapon|
|[Bodythief](ref/monster/bodythief)|Chunks of brain from a lobotomy subject \[evil\]|
|[Neothelid](ref/monster/neothelid)|Ruby worth 3,000 gp \[chaotic, evil\]|
|[Shoggoth](ref/monster/shoggoth)|Art object worth 3,000 gp recovered from the bottom of a sea \[chaotic\]|
