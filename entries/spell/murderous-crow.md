**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 221

> _The following spell is available to the indicated race or ethnicity: [Orc](ref/race/orc)_

You conjure a supernaturally vicious and clever crow to watch over you on the battlefield. Choose
one of the following templates; the crow has that template and this spell gains the descriptor
listed in parentheses: [celestial](ref/template/celestial) (good), [entropic](ref/template/entropic)
(chaotic), [fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) (evil), or [resolute](ref/template/resolute) (lawful).
In addition to its normal abilities, the crow gains the [Improved Steal](ref/feat/improved-steal)
feat as a bonus feat and gains an eye rake attack. If both of its claw attacks hit the same living
foe in a single round, that foe must succeed at a DC 13 Reflex save or be
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) as the bird scratches and tears at the foe's eyes. The blindness lasts
for 1d4 days or until healed with [remove blindness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness).

If the focus leaves your possession, the crow vanishes and the spell ends.
