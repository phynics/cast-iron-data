School: abjuration
Level: 6: Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Oracle, Psychic, Shaman, Warpriest
Casting time: 1 round
Components: V, S, DF
Range: 10 ft.
Duration: 1 minute/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: yes
Area: 10-ft.-radius emanation, centered on you
:::

You bring into being a mobile, hemispherical energy field that prevents the entrance of most types
of living creatures.

The effect hedges out animals, aberrations, dragons, fey, giants, humanoids, magical beasts,
monstrous humanoids, oozes, plants, and vermin, but not constructs, elementals, outsiders, or
undead.

This spell may be used only defensively, not aggressively. Forcing an abjuration barrier against
creatures that the spell keeps at bay collapses the barrier.
