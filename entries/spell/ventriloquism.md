::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: illusion
Level: 1: Arcanist, Bard, Medium, Mesmerist, Psychic, Red Mantis Assassin, Skald, Sorcerer, Spiritualist, Summoner, Unchained Summoner, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, F (parchment rolled into cone)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 1 minute/level (D)
Saving throw: Will disbelief (if interacted with)
Spell resistance: no
Effect: intelligible sound, usually speech
Subschool: Figment
:::

You can make your voice (or any sound that you can normally make vocally) seem to issue from
someplace else. You can speak in any language you know. With respect to such voices and sounds,
anyone who hears the sound and rolls a successful save recognizes it as illusory (but still hears
it).
