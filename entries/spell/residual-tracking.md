::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 1: Hunter, Ranger\
2: Occultist
Casting time: 1 minute
Components: V, S, M (a bit of plaster)
Range: touch
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: footprint touched
:::

By touching a footprint, you receive a clear mental image of the person or creature that made it.
The image has the exact same appearance as the creature that made the footprint at the moment it
made the imprint, including any telling features or expressions, any gear or equipment, or anything
else the creature was carrying at the time. For example, the footprint of a horse would reveal both
the horse and any creature riding it when it made the footprint.
