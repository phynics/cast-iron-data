Effect: one spectral dog

**Sources:** Occult Mysteries pg 58, Osirion, Land of the Pharaohs pg 27

This spell creates a spectral saluqi, an undead canine resembling an oversized hound with black fur,
a gray ruff and tail, and milky gray eyes. The hound shares your alignment and can converse with you
telepathically. It can see and attack ethereal creatures, and otherwise has the same statistics as a
yeth hound. It usually leaves no tracks because it prefers to fly an inch above the ground. You are
immune to the hound's bay ability. The hound's bite is considered aligned to any single alignment
you possess for the purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
