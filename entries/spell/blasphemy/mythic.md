When determining the spell's effect on non-mythic creatures, add your tier to your caster level.
Nonevil creatures that fail the save take a -4 penalty on attack rolls and saving throws, and their
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) decreases by 5 for as long as the spell's other
effects last.
