::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: illusion
Level: 3: Antipaladin, Medium\
4: Bard, Inquisitor, Mesmerist, Psychic, Skald\
5: Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 10 minutes/level (D)
Saving throw: Will negates
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one creature
Subschool: Glamer
Descriptors: glamer
:::

You subtly alter both the target's aura and subtle cues in its body language, tone of voice, and
word choice, which makes everything that the target says seem to be a lie. Every statement that the
target makes appears to be a lie under both magical scrutiny (such as discern lies) and mundane
scrutiny (such as using the Sense Motive skill). Someone who closely scrutinizes the target can
determine when it is actually telling the truth with a successful Sense Motive check (DC = 15 + your
caster level).

As long as you are within close range of the target, as a standard action you can suppress or resume
the effects of this spell, allowing you to let the target seem to be telling the truth at some times
and still seem to be lying at others.
