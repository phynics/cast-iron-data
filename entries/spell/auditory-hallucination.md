::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: illusion
Level: 1: Arcanist, Bard, Magus, Medium, Mesmerist, Occultist, Psychic, Skald, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: S
Range: long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
Duration: concentration
Saving throw: Will disbelief
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one creature/level, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart
Subschool: Phantasm
Descriptors: mind-affecting
:::

You cause the targets to believe they hear any sound you imagine. The sound can include intelligible
speech. Instead of precisely imagining a sound, you can identify a sound the subjects know and they
imagine it doing what you describe as you cast the spell. For example, you could cast this spell on
orc warriors and have them imagine the sound of their chieftain calling for help, even if you've
never heard their chieftain and even if the chieftain speaks in a language you don't understand. All
targets hear the same hallucination. You can change the sound as part of concentrating on the spell.
