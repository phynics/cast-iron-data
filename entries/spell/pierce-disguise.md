::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 3: Arcanist, Bard, Medium, Occultist, Psychic, Shaman, Skald, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

You see through magical disguises (whether illusions or polymorph effects) of the same spell level
or lower. This allows you to see the true forms of creatures magically disguised by such methods.
The true form appears as a ghostly overlay on the creature's disguised appearance.
