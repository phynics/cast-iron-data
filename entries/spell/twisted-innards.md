::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 2: Alchemist, Investigator\
3: Magus, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a cocoon tied with string)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

For the duration of this spell, your vital organs writhe, shift, and move about, making it difficult
to strike you in a vulnerable area. While this spell is in effect, critical hits and sneak attacks
against you have a 25% chance of failing to inflict any additional damage - though you still take
the normal damage from the attack. At 7th level, the chance to ignore additional damage increases to
50%, while at 13th level the chance increases to 75%.
