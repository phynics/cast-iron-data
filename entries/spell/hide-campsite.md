::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: illusion
Level: 2: Hunter, Ranger\
3: Druid
Casting time: 10 minutes
Components: V, S, M (a sprig of mistletoe, and a vial of quicksilver)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 2 hours/level (D)
Saving throw: Will disbelief (if interacted with)
Spell resistance: no
Area: one 20-ft. cube
Subschool: Glamer
Descriptors: glamer
:::

You make the area around a campsite appear to be a thicket of untouched and forbidding foliage, or
some other unwelcoming feature matching the surrounding terrain. Creatures outside the area cannot
sense any activity going on inside the area - they cannot smell campfires or cooking food, they
cannot hear conversation, loud noises, or spells being cast, and they cannot even feel heat or a
rush of wind coming from the area. Those inside the area can see out normally. Once a creature steps
into the area of the spell, it can see everything in and around the area normally.
