The subject is freed from spells and effects that restrict movement, including binding, entangle,
grappling, imprisonment, maze, paralysis, petrification, pinning, sleep, slow, stunning, temporal
stasis, and web. To free a creature from imprisonment or maze, you must know its name and
background, and you must cast this spell at the spot where it was entombed or banished into the
maze.
