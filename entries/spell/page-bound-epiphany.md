::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 2: Arcanist, Bard, Cleric, Magus, Oracle, Skald, Sorcerer, Warpriest, Wizard
Casting time: 1 round
Components: V, S, F (a book with blank pages)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 round/level (see text)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

You magically scour the world's libraries for information that might refresh your memory about a
topic. Upon casting this spell, the focus book's pages fill with snippets and selections from
countless books. You can spend up to 1 round per caster level (maximum 10) reading these notes. You
may cease reading at any time, and when you do you can immediately attempt one Knowledge check with
a +1 circumstance bonus for each round you spent studying the book (maximum +10). The writing
disappears when the spell ends, and if you fail to succeed at a Knowledge check on the round you
stop reading the notes, you don't gain the benefits of this spell.
