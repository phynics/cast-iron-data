::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 2: Arcanist, Druid, Hunter, Magus, Mesmerist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (rare mushrooms worth 5 gp)
Range: medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
Duration: 1 round/level
Saving throw: Fortitude negates (see text)
Spell resistance: no
Effect: fog spreads in 20-ft. radius, 20 ft. high
Subschool: Creation
Descriptors: poison
:::

You create a bank of fog similar to that created by fog cloud except its vapors are intoxicating.
Living creatures in the cloud become fascinated. This condition lasts as long as a creature is in
the cloud and for 1d4+1 rounds after it leaves. Any creature that succeeds at its save but remains
in the cloud must continue to save each round on your turn.
