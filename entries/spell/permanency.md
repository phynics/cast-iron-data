title = "Spell statistics"
School: universal
Level: 5: Arcanist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 2 rounds
Components: V, S, M (see tables below)
Range: see text
Duration: permanent; see text
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: see text
:::

This spell makes the duration of certain other spells permanent. You first cast the desired spell
and then follow it with the permanency spell.

Depending on the spell, you must be of a minimum caster level and must expend a specific gp value of
diamond dust as a material component.

You can make the following spells permanent in regard to yourself.

| --- | --- | --- |
|[Arcane sight](ref/spell/arcane-sight)|11th|7,500 gp|
|[Aura sight](ref/spell/aura-sight)|11th|7,500 gp|
|[Comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages)|9th|2,500 gp|

|[Tongues](ref/spell/tongues)|11th|7,500 gp|

You cannot cast these spells on other creatures. This application of permanency can be dispelled
only by a caster of higher level than you were when you cast the spell.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-a}

No level requirement or cost was provided in the original source of
_[alter river](ref/spell/alter-river)._ Consider using _[curse terrain](ref/spell/curse-terrain)_ as
a benchmark.

###### ^6^ {#footnote-c}

No level was given in the source text, but the price is consistent with
_[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item),_ which requires an 11th-level caster.

###### ^3^ {#footnote-d}

No level was provided for the _[create demiplane](ref/spell/create-demiplane)_ spells, but their
costs line up with other spells that require 15th (lesser), 16th (normal), and 17th-level (greater)
casters; also note that the text of _[quintessence mastery](ref/spell/quintessence-mastery)_ says
its _permanency_ requirements should copy _[create demiplane](ref/spell/create-demiplane)._

###### ^4^ {#footnote-e}

Permanency is mentioned in the _[light](ref/spell/light)_ spell's description, but no costs or
requirements are provided. If you wish to include this as a possibility, consider using
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ (or even [continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame))
as a benchmark.

###### ^7^ {#footnote-f}

No level was given for _[teleport trap](ref/spell/teleport-trap),_ and the cost given is multiplied
by the number of _teleport traps_ that lead to the same destination. Consider using _teleporation
circle_ as a benchmark.

###### ^5^ {#footnote-l}

No level was given for _[lost passage](ref/spell/lost-passage)_ and
_[lost locale](ref/spell/lost-locale)._ The former's cost is roughly consistent with a 12th-level
caster, but the latter is more than twice the cost of _[yellow sign](ref/spell/yellow-sign),_ the
next-costliest spell.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-u}

The cost must be paid in diamond dust.
