## Thought Shield I

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 189

Sensing an intrusion, you throw up a defense to protect your mind from attack or analysis. This
grants you a +4 circumstance bonus on Will saving throws against mind-affecting effects. As long as
this spell lasts, spells and effects that allow a creature to read your thoughts receive no
information from you.

### Thought Shield II

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 189

This functions as thought shield I, but the circumstance bonus on Will saves to resist
mind-affecting effects increases to +6. This spell can be undercast.

### Thought Shield III

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 190

This functions as thought shield I, but the circumstance bonus on Will saves to resist
mind-affecting effects increases to +8. In addition, any creature that tries to read your thoughts
while this spell lasts must succeed at a Will save or be stunned for 1 round. This spell can be
undercast.

### Thought Shield IV

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 190

This functions as thought shield III, but any creature that tries to read your thoughts while this
spell lasts must succeed at a Will save or be stunned for 1d4 rounds. Additionally, if you succeed
at your save against a psychic crush spell, you take no damage. This spell can be undercast.

### Thought Shield V

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 190

This functions as thought shield IV, except as noted above. This spell immediately ends if you fail
a Will saving throw against a mind-affecting effect. This spell can be undercast.
