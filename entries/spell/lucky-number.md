::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 1: Arcanist, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Inquisitor, Oracle, Paladin, Red Mantis Assassin, Skald, Sorcerer, Summoner, Unchained Summoner, Warpriest, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: touch
Duration: 24 hours or until discharged
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: one willing creature
:::

You are able to tweak tiny variables affecting a creature's immediate future in order to grant the
target a bit of luck at the right time. Roll a d20; once during the duration of lucky number, when
the target creature rolls that result (regardless of what type of dice the target rolls), the
creature has the option to either reroll the result or add a +2 luck bonus to the result. The
creature must decide to use this ability before the success or failure of the original roll is
known. A creature can have only one lucky number at a time. If lucky number is cast on a creature
already affected by that spell, the new number replaces the previous one.
