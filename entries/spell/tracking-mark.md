**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 243, Pathfinder \#32: Rivers Run Red pg 71

You gain a supernatural ability to detect tracks and other clues left behind by the target. You
treat the DCs of all Survival checks made to track the target as if they were 5 lower than normal,
and you gain a +5 bonus on Perception checks made to notice the target if it is using Stealth or
recognize it if it is using Disguise.

Some spells are more common among the worshipers of a god. Worshipers of a spell's associated deity
always treat the spell as common, and need not research it in order to prepare or learn it. This
spell is available to members of other faiths, though some temples or religious organizations may
proscribe the use of specific spells.
