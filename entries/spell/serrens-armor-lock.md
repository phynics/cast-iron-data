::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 2: Magus\
3: Arcanist, Red Mantis Assassin, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a pinch of rust)
Range: medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
Duration: 1 round/level (D)
Saving throw: Reflex partial, see text
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one armored creature
:::

Upon pointing at an armored foe, you cause all of the joints of the target's armor to stiffen as
otherworldly chains wrap around the target. On a failed Reflex save, a target in heavy metal armor
becomes staggered. A target in light or medium metal armor instead becomes entangled. Targets
wearing no armor or nonmetallic armor are unaffected. If the target is wearing heavy metal armor and
succeeds at its Reflex save, it becomes entangled instead of staggered; a target in light or medium
metal armor that succeeds at its saving throw is unaffected by the spell.
