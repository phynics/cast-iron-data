title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 0: Adept, Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Inquisitor, Medium, Occultist, Oracle, Shaman, Spiritualist, Summoner, Unchained Summoner, Warpriest, Witch
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: touch
Duration: 1 minute or until discharged
Saving throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes
Target: creature touched
:::

This spell imbues the subject with a touch of divine guidance. The creature gets a +1 competence
bonus on a single attack roll, saving throw, or skill check. It must choose to use the bonus before
making the roll to which it applies.
