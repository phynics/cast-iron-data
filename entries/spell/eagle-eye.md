::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 2: Druid, Hunter, Psychic, Ranger, Shaman
Casting time: 1 minute
Components: V, S, DF
Range: long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
Duration: 1 minute/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Effect: magical sensor
:::

Eagle eye creates a magical sensor directly above you. The sensor can appear anywhere above you, to
a maximum height equal to the spell's range. You can see from this vantage as if you were actually
there, rotating your viewpoint 360 degrees. You perceive with your normal visual senses. A caster
using eagle eye can easily see for a considerable distance. The spell does not penetrate any solid
surface, although it is unaffected by foliage and the like.
