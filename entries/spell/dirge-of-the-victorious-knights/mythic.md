The damage increases to 1d10 points of damage per caster level (maximum 20d10). Any creature that
fails its Reflex saving throw is knocked prone, and must succeed at a Fortitude save or be stunned
for 1 round.

_Augmented (7th):_ If you expend two uses of mythic power, the area increases to a 240-foot line 20
feet wide. All cold damage dealt by the spell ignores cold resistance and cold immunity.
