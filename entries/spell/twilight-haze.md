::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: illusion
Level: 2: Arcanist, Mesmerist, Psychic, Red Mantis Assassin, Sorcerer, Spiritualist, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 1 round/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Effect: fog spreads in 20-ft. radius
Subschool: Shadow
Descriptors: darkness, shadow
:::

You create a gray haze that obscures sight (as fog cloud) and causes the illumination level within
it to drop one step (as darkness). Since this spell produces an illusion rather than a true physical
fog, it is unaffected by wind and can be cast underwater.
