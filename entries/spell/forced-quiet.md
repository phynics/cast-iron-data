::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 1: Arcanist, Bard, Inquisitor, Mesmerist, Psychic, Red Mantis Assassin, Skald, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: S
Range: medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
Duration: 1 round/level
Saving throw: Will negates
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one creature
Descriptors: sonic
:::

With a gesture, you muffle sound around the target, making it unable to yell or otherwise make loud
noises. This does not affect spellcasting by the target. The target can still use sonic effects, but
the DC of these effects decreases by 2. The target gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against sonic
effects. The target gains a +4 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks.
