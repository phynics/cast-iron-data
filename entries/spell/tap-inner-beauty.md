::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 1: Bard, Cleric, Oracle, Skald, Warpriest, Witch
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, M (a tiny mirror)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 243, Faiths of Purity pg 29

This spell allows you to tap into the natural beauty of your soul and let it shine from your eyes
and your speech. While the spell is active, you gain a +2 insight bonus on all Charisma ability
checks and Charisma-based skill checks.

Some spells are more common among the worshipers of a god. Worshipers of a spell's associated deity
always treat the spell as common, and need not research it in order to prepare or learn it. This
spell is available to members of other faiths, though some temples or religious organizations may
proscribe the use of specific spells.
