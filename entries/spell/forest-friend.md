::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: abjuration
Level: 2: Druid, Hunter, Ranger
Casting time: 1 round
Components: V, S, M (a pinch of manure)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Targets: one willing living creature per three levels, no two of which may be more than 30 ft. apart.
:::

While subject to this spell, the targets find that forested areas become helpful instead of
hindering. The targets suffer no hindrances to movement and suffer no penalties on Acrobatics and
Stealth checks from undergrowth in forested terrain. Furthermore, forested overgrowth imposes a miss
chance 10% lower than normal.

When moving in and among trees, those subject to the spell gain a +5 circumstance bonus on
Acrobatics checks and Climb checks. Rangers in their [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain)
can also add their favored terrain bonus on such Acrobatics and Climb checks.
