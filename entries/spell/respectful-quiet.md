**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 222

> _The following spell is available to the indicated race or ethnicity: Chelaxian_

All targets are rendered silent, as per silence. The silence affects the entirety of each creature
but does not extend beyond. However, the silence is one-way-sounds from outside the creatures'
spaces can still enter. Quieted creatures gain a +10 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks.
