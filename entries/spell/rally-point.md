title = "Spell statistics"
School: enchantment
Level: 1: Paladin
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, DF
Range: 5 ft.
Duration: 1 minute/level (D)
Saving throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes (harmless)
Area: one 5-ft. square
Subschool: Compulsion
Descriptors: emotion, good, mind-affecting
:::

You create a spot that has the power to briefly hearten any good creature who comes into contact
with it. A good creature who enters this square (even if simply as part of its normal move) gains a
+2 morale bonus on attacks, saving throws, and 2 temporary hit points per caster level for 1 round.
Nongood creatures gain no benefit from this spell. A creature cannot benefit more than once from the
same casting of this spell.
