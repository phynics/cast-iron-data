::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 3: Occultist, Spiritualist, Summoner, Unchained Summoner\
4: Adept, Arcanist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 minute
Components: V, S, M (a tiny piece of matter of the same sort of item you plan to create with minor creation)
Range: 0 ft.
Duration: 1 hour/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Effect: unattended, nonmagical object of nonliving plant matter, up to 1 cu. ft./level
Subschool: Creation
:::

You create a nonmagical, unattended object of nonliving vegetable matter. The volume of the item
created cannot exceed 1 cubic foot per caster level. You must succeed on an appropriate Craft skill
check to make a complex item.

Attempting to use any created object as a material component causes the spell to fail.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-r}

Includes adamantine, alchemical silver, and mithral. You can't use major creation to create a cold
iron item.
