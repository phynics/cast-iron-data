> _The following spell is available to the indicated race or ethnicity:
> [Half-Elf](ref/race/half-elf)_

You gain a glimpse of a potential reality similar to this one but that never was. If the target
fails her Will save, you can ask about one topic and attempt the appropriate Knowledge check with a
+5 competence bonus; track Knowledge checks attempted with this spell separately for the purpose of
disallowing retries on Knowledge checks. You learn information on that topic from a reality in which
you were an acquaintance of the target, although the differences in that reality might cause the
answer to be subtly different than in your own. Once you have probed a target's history,
successfully or not, you cannot target the same creature's history or search for the same
information in another creature's history.
