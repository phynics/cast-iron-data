::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 2: Arcanist, Bard, Hunter, Medium, Occultist, Psychic, Ranger, Skald, Sorcerer, Wizard\
3: Druid
Casting time: 1 hour
Components: V, S, M (powdered metal and rare inks worth 100 gp)
Range: touch
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: one dead creature
:::

You can take a piece from a dead creature's body and use it to create a map that reveals the
locations of any valuables that creature knew about while still alive. You must cut this piece off
yourself and the creature cannot have died more than 24 hours prior to the casting of this spell.
You cannot cast this spell on a creature without a physical body, and the portion you remove must
have a fairly level surface such as a piece of hide, carapace, or skin. The map you create is
accurate to the best knowledge the creature had at the time of its death. It reveals the location of
one source of treasure for every three caster levels you possess. Any inaccuracies or faulty
information held by the creature are represented on the map as well. Similarly, the map cannot
account for any changes that occur after the creature's death. You must choose the scale of the map
when creating it, opting between nearby (e.g., one or two dungeon levels), local (e.g., a valley or
community), or broad (e.g., a country or larger). The map reveals the locations of what the creature
deemed most valuable within its area. Depending on the creature, the map might reveal a source of
tasty food, suitable mates, or even your own treasure vault.
