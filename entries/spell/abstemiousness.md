::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 1: Bard, Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Oracle, Skald, Warpriest, Witch
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V
Range: touch
Duration: 1 hour
Saving throw: Fortitude negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes (harmless)
Target: a handful of berries, grains, nuts, or rice
:::

Sometimes Irori smiles on his worshipers, granting them a reprieve from physical hunger. This spell
magically enhances a handful of simple food, imbuing it with enough nutrition to satisfy a Medium or
smaller creature for a full day. The spell does not create food, and thus will not alone prevent
someone from starving, but it can extend even limited reserves for lengthy periods.

Some spells are more common among the worshipers of a god. Worshipers of a spell's associated deity
always treat the spell as common, and need not research it in order to prepare or learn it. This
spell is available to members of other faiths, though some temples or religious organizations may
proscribe the use of specific spells.
