::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 2: Hunter, Ranger\
3: Druid, Inquisitor, Shaman
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
Duration: 1 hour/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Area: circle centered on you, with a radius of 100 feet + 10 feet per level
Descriptors: water
:::

You use divination magic to enhance the underwater trails in the area around you, allowing you to
track creatures through the water as if across soft ground as long as they passed through the area
less than 1 week ago (this lets you notice trails that are much older than normal underwater
trails). The spell's area moves with you, so you can follow the trail through the water over long
distances. Other creatures can also follow the trail as long as they move with you. Because of the
plethora of sea creatures in the higher zones, it's possible that the trail that most interests you
will be covered by the trails of other aquatic creatures, making this spell even more useful than
normal in the Abyssal and Abaddonian zones.
