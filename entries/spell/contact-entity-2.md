### Contact Entity II

This spell functions like _[contact entity I](ref/spell/contact-entity-1),_ except that you can
contact creatures from the _contact entity II_ list, and as noted above.

|Contact Entity II|Material Component|
| --- | --- |
|[Colour out of space](ref/monster/colour-out-of-space)|Shard of a meteorite|
|[Denizen of Leng](ref/monster/denizen-of-leng)|Painting of a memory from a dream worth 1,000 gp|
|[Elder thing](ref/monster/elder-thing)|Magic item or piece of technology worth 2,000 gp|
|[Hound of Tindalos](ref/monster/hound-of-tindalos)|Exotic, trance-inducing elixir worth 500 gp|
|[Leng ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul-leng-ghoul)|Skull of a dead scholar|
|[Mi-go](ref/monster/mi-go)|Fresh brains of an intelligent creature \[evil\]|
|[Neh-thalggu](ref/monster/neh-thalggu)|Vial of cerebrospinal fluid from a creature that still lives \[chaotic, evil\]|
|[Rhu-chalik](ref/monster/rhu-chalik)|Brain of a creature that had 16 Int or higher \[evil\]|
|[Shantak](ref/monster/shantak)|A shard of rock harvested from another world \[chaotic\]|
|[Yangethe](ref/monster/yangethe)|Scroll of an emotion spell of 4th level or higher|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-l}

determine the spell gains the chaotic and evil descriptors.
