> _[Drow](ref/race/drow) have access to the following spell._

You launch a ball of webbing at a target, which must make a save or be affected as if by a web spell
occupying only the creature's space. If the creature saves or breaks free of the webbing, the
remaining webs dissolve and the square is not considered difficult terrain. The spell has no effect
if the target is not on or adjacent to a solid surface that can support the webbing.
