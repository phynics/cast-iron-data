Area: 20-foot-radius spread

The ground attempts to pull creatures beneath its surface as if hungry for the flesh of mortals.

Immediately, and at the beginning of each of your turns, every creature touching the ground within
the area of the spell is the target of a grapple combat maneuver. Creatures that enter the area of
effect are also automatically attacked. The ground does not provoke attacks of opportunity. The
earth's CMB is equal to 5 + your caster level for the purpose of this combat maneuver check. Attempt
the combat maneuver check only once each round and apply the result to all creatures in the area of
effect.

Each time the ground succeeds at a combat maneuver check to grapple a foe, it drags the creature
farther down, eventually forcing the creature below its surface. With the first successful check,
the target gains the grappled condition. Grappled opponents can't move without first breaking the
grapple (doing so requires a successful DC 20 combat maneuver or Escape Artist check). The ground
receives a +5 bonus on combat maneuver checks to grapple opponents it is already grappling. After
the second successful grapple combat maneuver check, the grappled creature is pulled to the ground
and becomes prone. On the third successful grapple combat maneuver check, the creature is fully
buried and must hold its breath or begin suffocating.

A buried creature can't attempt to escape unless the effect ends or it breaks the grapple. The DC to
escape the grapple increases to 25 for a creature that has been pulled beneath the earth.
