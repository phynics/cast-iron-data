Saving throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes (harmless)
Target: one living creature
Subschool: Healing
Descriptors: emotion
:::

With this prayer you provide long-lasting succor to a wounded creature. For as long as the effect
lasts, the target receives a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against fear and death effects. At any
time while the spell is in effect, the target can choose to end the spell as a swift action for a
burst of healing energy. The target loses the saving throw bonus, but is healed of 1d8 points of
damage +1 point per caster level (maximum +10).
