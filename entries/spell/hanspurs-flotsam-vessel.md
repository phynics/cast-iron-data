**Sources:** People of the River pg 26

This spell assembles a sturdy raft and oars from driftwood, reeds, and other river detritus. The
smallest raft created by the spell is roughly 5 feet square, increasing by an additional 5 feet
square for every 2 caster levels you possess. Each 5-foot section of the raft can carry two Medium
passengers or 300 pounds of cargo. The vessel functions as a normal raft, except it is not slowed or
damaged by nonmagical river hazards such as rapids or shallows, and it is always considered to be
traveling downstream for the purpose of calculating travel speed, regardless of its actual
direction.
