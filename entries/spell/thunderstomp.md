::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: evocation
Level: 1: Arcanist, Bloodrager, Druid, Hunter, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one creature
Descriptors: earth
:::

You stomp your foot or strike your weapon against the ground or floor, creating a ripple of power
that you can use to trip a creature. Attempt the combat maneuver check to trip the target, but
instead of your base attack bonus you can use your caster level, and instead of your Strength
modifier you can use your spellcasting ability score modifier (Intelligence for magi and wizards;
Wisdom for druids and rangers; Charisma for bloodragers and sorcerers). This does not provoke an
attack of opportunity. This spell has no effect if you cannot reach the ground or floor, or if your
target is not in contact with the ground or floor.
