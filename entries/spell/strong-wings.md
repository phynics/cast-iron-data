> _[Strix](ref/race/strix) have access to the following spell._

The target's wings grow more powerful, causing its fly speed to increase by +10 feet and its
maneuverability to improve by one category (to a maximum of good). This increase counts as an
enhancement bonus. This spell has no effect on wingless creatures or winged creatures that cannot
fly.
