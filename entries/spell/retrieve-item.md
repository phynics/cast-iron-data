::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 2: Arcanist, Bard, Skald, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 full-round action
Components: V, S
Range: long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
Duration: permanent until discharged
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: an unattended object weighing 1 lb. or less whose longest dimension is 6 inches or less
Subschool: Calling
:::

You call a specific nonliving item directly to your hand from a nearby location. First, you must
cast the spell on the item while holding it. Thereafter, you can summon the item by speaking a
special word (set by you when the spell is cast) and snapping your fingers. The item appears
instantly in your hand.

If the item is in the possession of another creature, the spell fails.
