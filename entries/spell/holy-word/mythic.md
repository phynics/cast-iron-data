**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 98\
When determining the spell's effect on non-mythic creatures, add your tier to your caster level.
Nongood creatures that fail their saves also take a -4 penalty on attack rolls and saving throws,
and their [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) decreases by 5 for as long as the spell's
other effects last.
