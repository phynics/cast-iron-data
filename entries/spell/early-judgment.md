title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 1: Witch\
2: Cleric, Inquisitor, Oracle, Warpriest
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, DF
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 1d4 rounds
Saving throw: Will negates
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one humanoid creature
:::

You show one creature the effect of its life so far and what it might expect when it passes under
Pharasma's impartial gaze at the end of its life. Depending on the creature's alignment and its
adherence to its ethos, you can provide it a brief glimpse of the reward or punishment that waits
for it when it dies by showing it a mental image of its destined plane in the Great Beyond. If the
target is good-aligned, it is fascinated for 1d4 rounds on a failed save. If the target is
neutral-aligned, it is confused for 1d4 rounds on a failed save. If the target is evil-aligned, it
is shaken for 1d4 rounds on a failed save.

Some spells are more common among the worshipers of a god. Worshipers of a spell's associated deity
always treat the spell as common, and need not research it in order to prepare or learn it. This
spell is available to members of other faiths, though some temples or religious organizations may
proscribe the use of specific spells.
