> _[Undines](ref/race/undine) have access to the following spell._

You radiate the unearthly grace of a nereid. If you're not wearing armor, you gain a deflection
bonus to your AC and CMD equal to your Charisma bonus (maximum +3). The maximum increases by 1 for
every 6 levels you possess (maximum +6 at 18th level).
