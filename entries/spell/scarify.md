**Sources:** Inner Sea Temples pg 13

Your touch causes a single creature's skin to produce fibrous tissue that sutures the creature's
wounds, rapidly closing them and leaving jagged scars where the wounds were. The process is painful,
and converts 2d6 points of damage + 1 point per caster level (maximum +5) into nonlethal damage. If
the target creature is immune to nonlethal damage, the spell fails. Damage resistance against
nonlethal damage reduces the amount of damage converted. This effect counts as healing for the
purpose of stopping bleed damage. Kuthites are especially fond of this spell, as it gives them
permanent physical reminders of the all too brief pain they endured.
