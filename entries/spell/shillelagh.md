::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 1: Druid, Hunter
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, DF
Range: touch
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: Will negates (object)
Spell resistance: yes (object)
Target: one touched nonmagical oak club or quarterstaff
:::

Your own nonmagical club or quarterstaff becomes a weapon with a +1 enhancement bonus on attack and
damage rolls. A quarterstaff gains this enhancement for both ends of the weapon. It deals damage as
if it were two size categories larger (a Small club or quarterstaff so transmuted deals 1d8 points
of damage, a Medium 2d6, and a Large 3d6), +1 for its enhancement bonus. These effects only occur
when the weapon is wielded by you. If you do not wield it, the weapon behaves as if unaffected by
this spell.
