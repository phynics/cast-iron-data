::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 6: Druid, Hunter
Casting time: 10 minutes
Components: V, S, F (the staff that stores the spell)
Range: touch
Duration: permanent until discharged (D)
Saving throw: Will negates (object)
Spell resistance: yes (object)
Target: wooden quarterstaff touched
:::

You store one spell that you can normally cast in a wooden quarterstaff. Only one such spell can be
stored in a staff at a given time, and you cannot have more than one spellstaff at any given time.
You can cast a spell stored within a staff just as though it were among those you had prepared, but
it does not count against your normal allotment for a given day. You use up any applicable material
components required to cast the spell when you store it in the spellstaff.
