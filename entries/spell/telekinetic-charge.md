::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: evocation
Level: 4: Arcanist, Bloodrager, Psychic, Sorcerer, Spiritualist, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes (harmless)
Target: one willing creature
Descriptors: force
:::

You telekinetically launch an ally across the battlefield to anywhere within this spell's range.
While moving, your ally is flying just above the ground unless you wish otherwise. Movement from
this spell provokes attacks of opportunity as normal, although you can lift your ally over objects
or out of enemy reach, as long as your ally remains within this spell's range. If your ally lands
adjacent to an opponent, he can spend an immediate action to make a melee attack against that
opponent with a +2 bonus on the attack roll.
