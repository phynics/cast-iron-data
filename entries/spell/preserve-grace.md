**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 115

The [Lantern Bearers](ref/rule/aff-lights) must often make difficult decisions during their
missions, such as determining what to do with captured enemies-should they be executed, imprisoned,
set free, or given a chance to mend their ways? With this spell, you grant a creature the ability to
foresee moral consequences, as if the creature were wearing a phylactery of faithfulness. The
spell's guidance focuses on the protection of beauty and encourages peaceful resolution and mercy.
