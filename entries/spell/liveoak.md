title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 6: Druid, Hunter\
7: Shaman
Casting time: 10 minutes
Components: V, S
Range: touch
Duration: 1 day/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: tree touched
:::

This spell turns an oak tree into a protector or guardian. The spell can only be cast on a single
tree at a time; while liveoak is in effect, you can't cast it again on another tree. Liveoak must be
cast on a healthy, Huge oak. A triggering phrase of up to one word per caster level is placed on the
targeted oak. The liveoak spell triggers the tree into animating as a treant.

If liveoak is dispelled, the tree takes root immediately wherever it happens to be. If released by
you, the tree tries to return to its original location before taking root.
