This spell summons a single [accuser devil](ref/monster/devil-accuser-devil-zebub) from Hell to do
the spellcaster's bidding, similar to how summon monster spells function. Accuser devils, known to
diabolists as zebubs, are hideous flying creatures with the torso of a plump human infant and the
body and wings of an enormous fly. They make excellent spies, for they have the ability to grant
visions of what they have witnessed to others - the Queen's Hands sometimes use summoned zebubs to
observe suspects from afar.
