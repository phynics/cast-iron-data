::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: necromancy
Level: 2: Arcanist, Bloodrager, Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Oracle, Psychic, Shaman, Sorcerer, Warpriest, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (the knucklebone of a dire animal)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Targets: 1 creature/level, no two of which can be more than 30 feet apart
:::

The bones of your targets' joints grow thick and sharp, protruding painfully through the skin at the
knuckles, elbows, shoulders, spine, and knees. The targets each gain a +1 bonus to natural armor and
a +2 bonus on damage rolls with natural weapons, and they are treated as having armor spikes, with
which they are proficient.
