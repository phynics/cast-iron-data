::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 1: Alchemist, Arcanist, Cleric, Inquisitor, Investigator, Oracle, Sorcerer, Warpriest, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M/DF (a miniature magnifying glass or spectacles)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Effect: see text
:::

Your understanding of the written word becomes analytical and discerning. You gain an insight bonus
equal to your caster level (maximum +10) on Appraise checks to determine the value of books and
scrolls and on Linguistics checks to detect forgeries, and you can attempt a Linguistics check to
detect a forged document at a glance, rather than taking the normal 1 round of examination per page.
In addition, you immediately detect whether written works within 30 feet and within your line of
sight have a magical aura and the strength of any such auras, as though you had concentrated on each
written work for 1 round using [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic).
