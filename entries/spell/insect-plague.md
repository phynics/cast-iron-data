**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 301

You summon a number of swarms of wasps (one per three levels, to a maximum of six swarms at 18th
level). The swarms must be summoned so that each one is adjacent to at least one other swarm (that
is, the swarms must fill one contiguous area). You may summon the wasp swarms so that they share the
area of other creatures. Each swarm attacks any creatures occupying its area. The swarms are
stationary after being summoned, and won't pursue creatures that flee.
