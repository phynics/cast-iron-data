title = "Spell statistics"
School: necromancy

2: Arcanist, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a locust)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: yes
Target or Area: see text
:::

You hurl a tiny ball of negative energy, destroying plant life either in a line 60 feet long or a
10-foot-radius spread. This effect removes the cover and concealment provided by trees and
undergrowth, eliminates the movement penalties associated with undergrowth, and so forth.

You may also target a single plant creature with this spell. You must succeed on a ranged touch
attack to hit your target. An affected plant creature takes 2d8 points of damage.
