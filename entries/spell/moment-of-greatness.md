::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: enchantment
Level: 1: Arcanist, Bard, Cleric, Oracle, Psychic, Skald, Sorcerer, Warpriest, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M/DF (rabbit fur)
Range: 50 ft.
Duration: 1 minute/level or until discharged
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: yes
Target: The caster and allies within a 50-ft. burst centered on the caster
Subschool: Compulsion
Descriptors: mind-affecting
:::

Each creature affected by this spell is given the potential for greater success and glory. If the
affected creature is benefiting from a morale bonus of any type, it can double that morale bonus on
one roll or check, before making the roll. Once an affected creature uses this spell's effect, the
spell is discharged for that subject.
