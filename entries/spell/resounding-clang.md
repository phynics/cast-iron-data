::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 2: Bard, Skald
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: S, F (bell, chimes, gong, or cymbals)
Range: 30 ft.
Duration: 1 round/level
Saving throw: Reflex negates
Spell resistance: yes (object)
Area: 30-ft.-radius burst, centered on you
:::

You create a loud, resonating clangor that causes metallic objects to vibrate wildly. Affected
creatures wielding metal weapons take a -1 penalty on attack rolls, while creatures using metallic
tools take a -1 penalty on associated skill checks. Creatures wearing metal armor increase the armor
check penalty of their armor by 1. These penalties increase by 1 at 8th caster level and every 4
caster levels thereafter (to a maximum of -4 at 16th level).

To be affected by the spell, an item must be composed primarily of metal. Nonmetallic objects with
metal components (such as studded leather armor or padded armor with armor spikes) are not affected.
Creatures made of metal take the penalties described above on attack rolls, damage rolls, skill
checks, and Reflex saves.
