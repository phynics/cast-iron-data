::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 7: Druid, Shaman
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: Reflex negates
Spell resistance: no
Effect: 10-ft.-by-80-ft. fissure in the earth, 40 ft. deep
Descriptors: earth
:::

With a thrust of tectonic energy, you split the earth. Each creature standing in the area where the
fissure opens must succeed at a Reflex saving throw to avoid falling into the rift. Those standing
adjacent to the fissure when it is created must succeed at a Reflex save or be knocked prone by
tremors. This spell is effective only when cast on an area of dirt, earth, sand, or stone.
