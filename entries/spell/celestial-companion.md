::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 1: Paladin, Summoner, Unchained Summoner\
2: Arcanist, Druid, Hunter, Ranger, Shaman, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M/DF
Range: touch
Duration: 1 minute/level (D)
Saving throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes (harmless)
Target: animal companion, eidolon, familiar, or mount
:::

You bestow divine protection on your companion. The target gains
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 5 plus half your caster level against spells
and spell-like effects produced by evil creatures. The target also gains a +1 sacred bonus on saving
throws and DR 5/evil or silver. Creatures under the effects of this spell often take on a celestial
appearance, such as golden feathers or a radiant aura, but they are not truly celestial creatures.
