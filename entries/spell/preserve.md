Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M/DF (a pinch of salt)
Range: touch
Duration: 1 week
Saving throw: Will negates (object)
Spell resistance: yes (object)
Target: one or more objects, up to 1 lb./level
:::

**Sources:** Inner Sea Temples pg 43

You imbue one or more objects with magic that dramatically slows down natural processes that lead to
decay and spoilage. For the duration of the spell, food, water, plants, and other perishable objects
affected by the spell remain as fresh as they were when the spell was cast. The spell also protects
these objects against spells of the same level or lower that cause spoilage, such as
[putrefy food and drink](ref/spell/putrefy-food-and-drink). When the duration of preserve expires,
the objects resume aging at their normal rate. The spell also works on severed body parts and the
bodies of small creatures as per [gentle repose](ref/spell/gentle-repose), provided that the weight
of the creature or part does not exceed the spell's limit.

Preserve can be made permanent with a [permanency](ref/spell/permanency) spell by a caster of 9th
level or higher for 500 gp.
