::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 2: Bard, Skald\
3: Arcanist, Cleric, Oracle, Sorcerer, Warpriest, Wizard
Casting time: 1 round
Components: V, S
Range: personal
Duration: 1 round/level; see text
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

You gain a brief understanding of the workings of a single puzzle, device, or trap, gaining an
insight bonus equal to your caster level (maximum +10) on checks to disable, manipulate or solve the
object in question. Your insight is ineffable; you are unable to communicate your understanding to
others. At the GM's option, this spell may instead give you a clue or other piece of useful
information. In this case you may communicate the clue to others and retain knowledge of it after
the spell's duration has expired.
