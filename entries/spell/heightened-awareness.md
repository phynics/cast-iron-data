::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 1: Alchemist, Arcanist, Bard, Druid, Hunter, Inquisitor, Investigator, Medium, Mesmerist, Occultist, Psychic, Ranger, Shaman, Skald, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, M/DF (a coffee bean)
Range: personal
Duration: 10 minutes/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

You enter a heightened state of awareness that allows you to notice more about your surroundings and
recall information effortlessly. You gain a +2 competence bonus on Perception checks and on all
Knowledge checks that you are trained in.

If this spell is active when you have to make an initiative check, you can instantly dismiss this
spell and gain a +4 bonus on that check.
