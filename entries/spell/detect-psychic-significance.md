::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: divination
Level: 0: Medium, Mesmerist, Occultist, Psychic, Spiritualist
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S
Range: 40 ft.
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Area: 40-ft.-radius burst, centered on you
:::

You detect the presence of psychically significant items in your vicinity. Such items are those that
might have significant psychic imprints or histories that can be read by the
[psychometry](ref/skill/appraise) unlock, or items under the effects of the
[charge object](ref/spell/charge-object) or [implant false reading](ref/spell/implant-false-reading)
spells. Items within range that contain significant psychic energy spark a recognition in your
mind's eye, but no other information is imparted. This spell doesn't automatically detect magic
items or strongly aligned items, though such items often have storied histories and might have had
previous owners who possessed psychic abilities.
