**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 103\
The damage healed increases to 3d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level (maximum +20). The
damage dealt to undead increases to 1d8 points of damage per caster level (maximum 10d8), or 2d6
points of damage per caster level for undead vulnerable to bright light (maximum 20d6). If a dead
creature is brought into the pillar's square and that creature could be revived by breath of life,
the pillar of life ends and the creature returns to life as per breath of life.
