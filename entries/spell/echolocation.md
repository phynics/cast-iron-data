::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 4: Alchemist, Bard, Druid, Hunter, Investigator, Occultist, Skald\
5: Arcanist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V
Range: personal
Duration: 10 minutes/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
Descriptors: sonic
:::

You can perceive the world by creating high-pitched noises and listening to their echoes. This gives
you [blindsight](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) to a range of 40 feet. The echo-producing
noises are too high-pitched to be heard by most creatures, and can only be detected by dragons,
other creatures with this ability (such as bats), and creatures with hearing-based blindsense or
blindsight. You cannot use this ability if you are deaf, and cannot detect anything in an area of
silence.
