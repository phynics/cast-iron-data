::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: enchantment
Level: 3: Bard, Mesmerist, Skald\
5: Arcanist, Psychic, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a tiny shard of a mirror)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 10 minutes/level (D)
Saving throw: Will negates
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one creature
Subschool: Compulsion
Descriptors: emotion, mind-affecting
:::

You cause your target to become overwhelmed by its own importance, talents, and attractiveness. The
target cannot help but look at itself in every reflective surface at every possible opportunity. In
social situations, the target always tries to comment about how attractive it is or how ugly someone
else is by comparison. The target remains constantly distracted, always looking for a reflective
surface to gaze upon (such as a mirror, pool of water, a polished shield, and so on). The
distraction gives the target a -2 penalty on all skill checks. In combat, the target worries about
enemies damaging its appearance, and focuses on defense rather than offense (casting defensive
spells rather than offensive spells, using the fight defensively or total defense action, and so
on).
