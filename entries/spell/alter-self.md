::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 2: Alchemist, Arcanist, Bard, Investigator, Magus, Medium, Mesmerist, Psychic, Red Mantis Assassin, Shaman, Skald, Sorcerer, Summoner, Unchained Summoner, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a piece of the creature whose form you plan to assume)
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
Subschool: Polymorph
:::

When you cast this spell, you can assume the form of any Small or Medium creature of the humanoid
type. If the form you assume has any of the following abilities, you gain the listed ability:
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), scent, and
swim 30 feet.

_Small creature:_ If the form you take is that of a Small humanoid, you gain a +2 size bonus to your
Dexterity.

_Medium creature:_ If the form you take is that of a Medium humanoid, you gain a +2 size bonus to
your Strength.
