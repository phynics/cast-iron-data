::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: conjuration
Level: 4: Antipaladin, Hunter, Paladin, Ranger\
5: Arcanist, Druid, Shaman, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, F (a feather, tuft of fur, or similar momento from your target)
Range: long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
Duration: instantaneous
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: yourself and one animal companion, familiar, or spirit animal within range
Subschool: Teleportation
:::

You trade places with the other target, teleporting as if you were both affected by dimension door.
Both you and the other target arrive in a square you choose in the other's former space. If the
targets are different sizes, they must appear in locations that cover previously occupied squares.
As with dimension door, after casting this spell, you can't take any other actions until your next
turn, and the other target is staggered until the end of its next turn due to the transposition.
