::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 5: Alchemist, Investigator
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: S
Range: personal
Duration: 1 day/level (d) or until discharged
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

When you consume this extract, you quickly consume another extract of your choice-this second
extract's effects do not come into effect until a later point. You must consume this second,
companion extract on the round following delayed consumption or waste the extract. The companion
extract can be no higher than 4th level, and you must pay any costs associated with the companion
extract when you consume it.

At any point during the duration of this extract, you can cause the companion extract to take effect
as an immediate action. You can only have one delayed consumption in effect at one time. If a second
is consumed, the first is dispelled without any effect.
