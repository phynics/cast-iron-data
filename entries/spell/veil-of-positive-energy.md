::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: abjuration
Level: 1: Paladin
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, DF
Range: personal or 5 ft.; see text
Duration: 10 minutes/level (D)
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you or all creatures within 5 ft.; see text
Descriptors: good
:::

You surround yourself with a veil of positive energy, making it harder for undead creatures to harm
you. When under the effect of this spell, you gain a +2 sacred bonus to AC and a +2 sacred bonus on
saves. Both of these bonuses apply only against attacks or effects created by undead creatures. You
can dismiss this spell before its normal duration as a swift action on your turn to deal a number of
points of positive energy damage equal to your level to all undead creatures within 5 feet of you.
