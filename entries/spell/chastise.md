::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: transmutation
Level: 1: Arcanist, Bard, Red Mantis Assassin, Skald, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V
Range: personal
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving throw: none
Spell resistance: no
Target: you
:::

You gain a +5 bonus on Bluff, Diplomacy, and Intimidate checks to convince a listener that they will
get in trouble with their superiors or with the law if they don't do what you ask.

This spell is primarily used by the [Eagle Knights](ref/rule/aff-vangard), though they did not
create it; [Hellknights](ref/rule/aff-lawknights) also use it extensively to gather information and
foster cooperation.
