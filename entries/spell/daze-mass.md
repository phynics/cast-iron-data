## Daze

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 264

This spell clouds the mind of a humanoid creature with 4 or fewer Hit Dice so that it takes no
actions. Humanoids of 5 or more HD are not affected. A dazed subject is not stunned, so attackers
get no special advantage against it. After a creature has been dazed by this spell, it is immune to
the effects of this spell for 1 minute.

### Mass Daze

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 216

This spell functions as daze, except as noted above.
