::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: abjuration
Level: 4: Alchemist, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Hunter, Inquisitor, Investigator, Medium, Mesmerist, Occultist, Oracle, Psychic, Ranger, Skald, Spiritualist, Warpriest
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, DF, M (a leather strip bound to the target)
Range: personal or touch
Duration: 10 minutes/level
Saving throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes (harmless)
Target: you or creature touched
:::

This spell enables you or a creature you touch to move and attack normally for the duration of the
spell, even under the influence of magic that usually impedes movement, such as paralysis,
[solid fog](ref/spell/solid-fog), [slow](ref/spell/slow), and [web](ref/spell/web). All combat
maneuver checks made to grapple the target automatically fail. The subject automatically succeeds on
any combat maneuver checks and Escape Artist checks made to escape a grapple or a pin.

The spell also allows the subject to move and attack normally while underwater, even with slashing
weapons such as axes and swords or with bludgeoning weapons such as flails, hammers, and maces,
provided that the weapon is wielded in the hand rather than hurled. The freedom of movement spell
does not, however, grant water breathing.
