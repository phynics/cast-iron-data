**Sources:** Heroes of the Wild pg 31

You can cast this spell only if it is the first action you take on your turn. In a burst of shadowy
feathers, you turn into a Tiny blurred shape reminiscent of a black raven until the beginning of
your next turn. You gain a fly speed of 50 feet with good maneuverability, and apply appropriate
size modifiers (though your ability scores don't change). Until the beginning of your next turn, you
can take only the 5-foot step, move, run, or withdraw actions. If the spell is dispelled while you
are still aloft, the power of flight dissipates slowly; you float downward 60 feet on your next
turn, then fall any remaining distance.
