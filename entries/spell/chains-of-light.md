A creature targeted by this spell is held immobile by glowing golden chains composed of pure light.
The creature is paralyzed and held in place, but may attempt a new saving throw each round to end
the effect. While held by the golden chains, a creature cannot use any sort of extradimensional
travel, such as [astral projection](ref/spell/astral-projection), [blink](ref/spell/blink),
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door), [ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt),
[etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness), [gate](ref/spell/gate), [maze](ref/spell/maze),
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift), [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk),
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport), and similar spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The spell does not affect creatures that are
already in ethereal or astral form when the spell is cast.
