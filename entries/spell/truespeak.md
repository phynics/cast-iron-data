> _[Aasimars](ref/race/aasimar) have access to the following spell._

You can communicate with any creature that is not mindless. As long as you can be heard, your speech
is understandable to all creatures, each of which hears you as though you were conversing in its
language or other natural mode of communication, and you hear their responses as though in your own
native language. You may ask questions and receive answers, though this spell does not make
creatures more friendly or cooperative than normal, and non-sentient creatures may give limited
responses. While using truespeak, your language-dependent effects can affect any creature that is
not mindless.
