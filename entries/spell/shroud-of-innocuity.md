Descriptors: glamer

**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 10

All affected creatures are veiled with a commonplace appearance. This appearance varies based on the
viewer, but is always an ordinary member of that viewer's race, ethnicity, or nationality. This
familiarity grants a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks attempted by affected targets against those not
affected by the spell.

Creatures with an attitude of unfriendly or hostile toward an affected target can attempt a Will
save to disbelieve the effect the first time they encounter that target.
