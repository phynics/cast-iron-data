::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: necromancy
Level: 2: Arcanist, Druid, Hunter, Ranger, Sorcerer, Witch, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M/DF (a drop of eidolon ichor)
Range: touch
Duration: 1 minute/level (see below)
Saving throw: Fortitude negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes (harmless)
Target: one of your animal companions or your familiar
:::

You create a life link between yourself and the target. You sense whenever the target is wounded and
are made immediately aware if the target is slain. If you are wounded or slain, the target is made
aware. As a free action, you can call out to the target, causing it to return to you (if able and
willing); this ends the spell.
