**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 186

You focus emotional energy and weave it into a shroud of hardened ectoplasm around the weapon you
touch, infusing it with a ghostly glow and great power. The weapon becomes a
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon, and gains one of the following additional
benefits, depending on the type of emotion you infuse into the weapon. If a special ability wouldn't
apply to the chosen weapon (such as vicious on a ranged weapon), the weapon doesn't gain that
benefit.

> **Anger:** The weapon also gains the [vicious](ref/magic-enh/vicious) special ability.
>
> **Dedication:** The weapon also gains the [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) special ability.
>
> **Despair:** The weapon also gains the [cruel](ref/magic-enh/cruel) special ability.
>
> **Fear:** The weapon also gains the [menacing](ref/magic-enh/menacing) special ability.
>
> **Hatred:** The weapon also gains the [cunning](ref/magic-enh/cunning) special ability.
>
> **Jealousy:** The weapon also gains the [mimetic](ref/magic-enh/mimetic) special ability.
>
> **Kindness:** The weapon also gains the [allying](ref/magic-enh/allying) special ability. —
> Psychic Anthology pg. 4
>
> **Remorse:** The weapon also gains the [heartseeker](ref/magic-enh/heartseeker) special ability. —
> Occult Origins pg. 19
>
> **Whimsey:** The weapon also gains the [merciful](ref/magic-enh/merciful) special ability. —
> Heroes from the Fringe pg. 17
>
> **Zeal:** The weapon also gains the [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) special ability.

If you are a spiritualist and the type of emotional energy you choose matches the emotional focus of
your phantom, the weapon grants its wielder a +2 bonus on skill checks with both skills you gain
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in from your phantom's emotional focus.
