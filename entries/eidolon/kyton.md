Scholars of the exquisiteness of agony and explorers of the limitations of the flesh,
[kytons](ref/family/kyton) understand the mortal form is a blank canvas aching for transformation.
They appear as humanoids, save that they are covered in piercings, draped with chains, and bear
grisly body modifications. Kyton eidolons gain access to the [tentacle](ref/unchevolution/tentacle)
and [web](ref/unchevolution/web) evolutions, but kyton eidolon tentacles and webs always appear as
animated chains.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Lawful evil.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Biped ([improved natural armor](ref/unchevolution/improved-natural-armor),
[limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\], limbs \[legs\]).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Kyton eidolons gain the [resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance) (cold) and
skilled (Heal) evolutions. They also gain proficiency with the
[spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain).

**At 12th Level:** A kyton eidolon gains DR 5/good. The effects of a kyton eidolon's _unnerving
gaze_ now persist for 1d3 rounds, and as a **free action**, a kytons can target up to two creatures
per round with its _[gaze](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/gaze)_ on its turns.

**At 16th Level:** A kyton eidolon loses the _resistance_ (cold) evolution, and gains the
[immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) (cold) evolution in its place. The effects of a kyton
eidolon's _unnerving gaze_ now [stagger](ref/misc/staggered) the target for 1 round, and as a **free
action**, a kyton eidolon can target up to three creatures per round with its
_[gaze](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/gaze)._

**At 20th Level:** A kyton eidolon gains [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) 5 (good weapons, good
spells). It is still banished to the Shadow Plane as normal for an eidolon if it takes enough
damage.

**At 4th Level:** A kyton eidolon adds 1 point to its evolution pool.

**At 8th Level:** A kyton eidolon gains an _unnerving gaze_ that manipulates the perceptions of
those who look upon it. Unlike typical [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) weapons, a kyton eidolon's _unnerving
gaze_ can affect only one creature per round when the kyton directs its
_[gaze](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/gaze)_ at the target on its turn as a
**free action**. An _unnerving gaze_ has a range of 30 feet, and can be negated by a successful Will
save.

Any creature that fails the save becomes [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) by the kyton's grisly
modifications for 1 round. A kyton eidolon is immune to the _unnerving gazes_ of other kytons, and
the kyton's summoner is immune to its _unnerving gaze._ This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The
save DC is Charisma-based.
