Created to monitor a specific duality, an aeon eidolon often proves frustratingly headstrong in
enforcing its own interpretation of balance.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Biped ([claws](ref/unchevolution/claws), [limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\],
limbs \[legs\]) or serpentine (limbs \[arms\], [tail](ref/unchevolution/tail),
[slam](ref/unchevolution/slam)).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Aeon eidolons gain the _envisaging_ ability of other [aeons](ref/family/aeon).
Aeon eidolons halve the armor and natural armor bonuses they gain per level, but gain an equal
deflection bonus to their AC. They also gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against poison.

**At 12th Level:** Aeon eidolons gain the ability to influence either emotions or time as a
**standard action**, as chosen by the summoner. Whenever the summoner gains a level, he can change
which ability the eidolon influences.

When affecting emotions, an aeon eidolon affects up to five targets within 30 feet as
[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair). A target can negate this effect with a successful
Will save (DC = 14 + the eidolon's Charisma modifier). For each target that fails its save, the
eidolon can affect one other creature within 30 feet as [good hope](ref/spell/good-hope). The caster
level equals the eidolon's Hit Dice, and the save DC is equal to 10 + half the eidolon's Hit Dice +
the eidolon's Charisma modifier.

When affecting time, the ability functions in the same way, but the eidolon affects primary targets
as [slow](ref/spell/slow) and secondary targets as [haste](ref/spell/haste).

The eidolon can use this ability once per day for every 5 Hit Dice it has.

**At 16th Level:** Aeon eidolons become immune to critical hits, poison, and sneak attacks.

**At 20th Level:** Aeon eidolons gain the ability to cast
[moment of prescience](ref/spell/moment-of-prescience) (CL 20th) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) three times per day.

**At 4th Level:** Aeon eidolons gain the benefits of the
[moderate fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-moderate) armor special ability and cannot be
tripped.

**At 8th Level:** Aeon eidolons gain the [flight](ref/unchevolution/flight) evolution (using magic)
with a speed equal to their base speed.
