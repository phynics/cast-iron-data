Plant eidolons consist of vegetable matter infused with raw natural spiritual power.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Any. Plant eidolons don't have alignment subtypes.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Cactus ([limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\], limbs \[legs\],
[sting](ref/unchevolution/sting)), conifer ([claws](ref/unchevolution/claws), limbs \[arms\], limbs
\[legs\]), fungus ([bite](ref/unchevolution/bite), limbs \[arms\], limbs \[legs\]), leaf (limbs
\[arms\], limbs \[legs\], [slam](ref/unchevolution/slam)), seaweed (limbs \[arms\], slam,
[swim](ref/unchevolution/swim) \[2\]).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Plant eidolons gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) as well as the
[resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance) (electricity) and _resistance_ (sonic) evolutions.

**At 12th Level:** Plant eidolons gain immunity to paralysis, poison, sleep, and stun.

**At 16th Level:** Plant eidolons gain the ability to use
[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature) once per day and can use their
_[speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants)_ ability at will.

**At 20th Level:** Plant eidolons gain a +8 resistance bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting
effects. They lose their _resistance_ (electricity) and _resistance_ (sonic) evolutions and instead
gain the [immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) (electricity) and
_[immunity](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/immunity)_ (sonic) evolutions.

**At 4th Level:** Plant eidolons gain constant [pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace)
and they can change shape into plants of the size category corresponding to their base form with
results similar to [tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape).

**At 8th Level:** Plant eidolons gain the ability to use
[speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants) for 1 minute per Hit Die per day with any plants
related to the eidolon's base form. These minutes need not be consecutive.
