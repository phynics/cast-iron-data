[Kami](ref/family/kami) eidolons are spirits of nature bound to an unchained summoner. They seek to
protect their wards, but because of their summoner's bond, they have more flexibility choosing their
wards than most of their kind.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Any non-evil. Kami eidolons do not have an alignment subtype, instead having the
[native](ref/subtype/native) subtype.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Biped ([limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\], limbs \[legs\],
[slam](ref/unchevolution/slam)).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** A kami eidolon gains the [resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance) (fire)
evolution and the 4-point [weapon training](ref/unchevolution/weapon-training) evolution
(proficiency in martial weapons).

**At 12th Level:** A kami eidolon gains the 4-point version of the
[fast healing](ref/unchevolution/fast-healing) evolution and the _merge with ward_ ability (see the
[kami subtype](ref/subtype/kami). A kami eidolon automatically emerges from its _ward_ if they are
merged when that object or creature ceases to be the eidolon's _ward._

**At 16th Level:** Kami eidolons are immune to bleed, mind-affecting, petrification, and polymorph
effects.

**At 20th Level:** A kami eidolon increases the _[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing)_ from its
_[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing)_ evolution by 2 points whenever it is within 30 feet of its
_ward;_ while the kami is merged with its _ward,_ the _ward_ gains the same amount of
_[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing)._

**At 4th Level:** A kami eidolon gains acid _resistance_ 10 and cold _resistance_ 10.

**At 8th Level:** A kami eidolon can declare a _ward_ once per day as a **standard action**. Its
_ward_ must be an object, a plant, or a creature with an Intelligence score of 2 or less, and the
_ward_ maintains the designation for 1 hour. So long as a kami eidolon is within 60 feet of its
_ward,_ it and its _ward_ gain a +2 sacred bonus on saving throws.
