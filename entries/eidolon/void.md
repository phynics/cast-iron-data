Like the sharp-angled [sceaduinars](ref/monster/sceaduinar) they resemble, void eidolons exult in
entropy and resent anything bearing even a hint of life - including their summoners.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral or neutral evil.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Biped ([bite](ref/unchevolution/bite), [limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[legs\],
[skilled](ref/unchevolution/skilled) \[Stealth\], [wing buffet](ref/unchevolution/wing-buffet)).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Void eidolons have [negative energy affinity](ref/umr/negative-energy-affinity)
and are immune to death effects, disease, energy drain, and poison.

**At 12th Level:** Void eidolons gain the [lifesense](ref/umr/lifesense) ability. They also gain the
[energy drain](ref/umr/energy-drain) ability (1 negative level) with their bite attacks, though they
can drain only a total number of levels per day equal to half their Hit Dice (rounded down).

**At 16th Level:** Void eidolons lose the _resistance_ (cold) evolution and instead gain the
[immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) (cold) evolution. They also gain DR 5/adamantine.

**At 20th Level:** Void eidolons can bestow up to 2 negative levels with a successful bite attack,
and the number of negative levels they can bestow per day increases by their Constitution modifier.
Their [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) increases to DR 10/adamantine.

**At 4th Level:** Void eidolons gain the [resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance) (cold)
evolution. Their natural attacks can strike incorporeal targets as though they were
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapons.

**At 8th Level:** Void eidolons can use their wings to fly, gaining the
[flight](ref/unchevolution/flight) evolution. When selecting the
[energy attacks](ref/unchevolution/energy-attacks) evolution, void eidolons can instead choose to
deal 1d6 points of negative energy damage on a successful hit; this damage does not heal creatures.
