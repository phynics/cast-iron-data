Patient and enlightened liaisons of the good-aligned Outer Planes, [agathion](ref/family/agathion)
eidolons seek to vanquish evil and protect that which is good. Agathion eidolons always have aspects
of a single animal or creature, rather than a hodgepodge of several. Though they have patience for
summoners with unorthodox methods and even those who stray from the path of good, they will not
brook their powers being used for evil ends. An agathion eidolon seeks to bring its summoner closer
to its own enlightenment.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral good.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Biped ([claws](ref/unchevolution/claws), [limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\],
limbs \[legs\]) or quadruped (limbs \[legs, 2\], [bite](ref/unchevolution/bite)).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Agathion eidolons gain the [resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance)
(electricity) evolution. They also gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against poison and
petrification.

**At 12th Level:** Agathion eidolons gain DR 5/evil. They also gain immunity to petrification and
the _truespeech_ ability (see the [agathion creature subtype](ref/subtype/agathion)).

**At 16th Level:** Agathion eidolons lose the _resistance_ (electricity) evolution, and instead gain
the [immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) (electricity) evolution. They also gain the agathion's
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ ability (see the agathion creature subtype).

**At 20th Level:** Agathion eidolons gain [detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) at will and also increase their
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) to DR 10/evil.

**At 4th Level:** Agathion eidolons gain cold _resistance_ 10 and sonic _resistance_ 10.

**At 8th Level:** Agathion eidolons gain [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) as paladins with
levels equal to their Hit Dice.
