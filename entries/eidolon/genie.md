Genies are elemental beings that take humanoid forms, and genie eidolons often vacillate between
humble kowtowing and biting observations about the summoner's foibles that make the eidolon look
superior.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Chaotic good, chaotic neutral, lawful evil, lawful neutral, or neutral.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Biped ([limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\], limbs \[legs\],
[slam](ref/unchevolution/slam)).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Genie eidolons gain the 4-point
[weapon training](ref/unchevolution/weapon-training) evolution as well as the
[resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance) evolution for any one energy type.

**At 12th Level:** Genie eidolons lose the resistance evolution gained at 1st level and instead gain
the [immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) evolution to the selected energy type. They can also cast
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift) as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once
per day (CL 12th), but only to transport willing targets to the Astral Plane, Elemental Planes, or
Material Plane.

**At 16th Level:** Once per day as a **standard action**, genie eidolons can cast
[cleanse](ref/spell/cleanse) or [greater evolution surge](ref/spell/evolution-surge-greater) (CL
16th) on themselves as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), but only in response to
their masters' spoken wish (such as &quot&I wish you were stronger&quot&). The master can make such
a wish as a **free action**.

**At 20th Level:** Genie eidolons gain the 20th-level base evolutions of an
[elemental](ref/eidolon/elemental) eidolon of any one element.

**At 4th Level:** Genie eidolons gain the ability to grow in size twice per day as a **full-round
action**. This grants them the [large](ref/unchevolution/large) evolution for 1 round per Hit Die
they have, increases the size of their equipment for the duration, and does not stack with other
size-changing effects. Genie eidolons who later purchase the _large_ evolution lose this ability,
but they add 1 point to their evolution pools.

**At 8th Level:** Genie eidolons gain either the [flight](ref/unchevolution/flight) evolution (using
magic) with a speed equal to their base speed; the [burrow](ref/unchevolution/burrow) evolution; or
the [gills](ref/unchevolution/gills) evolution, plus the [swim](ref/unchevolution/swim) evolution
twice, gaining a swim speed equal to their base speed + 20 feet.
