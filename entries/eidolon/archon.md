Summoned from heaven, [archon](ref/family/archon) eidolons are the embodiments of righteousness.
They often take on powerful, graceful forms, rippling with muscles but refined in their movements.
Archon eidolons are totally dedicated to the cause of good, and have little patience for those who
violate their oaths and ideals - including the summoner who called them. Archons will do whatever it
takes to stop the advance of evil and protect the innocent.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Lawful good.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Biped ([limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\], limbs \[legs\],
[slam](ref/unchevolution/slam)).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Archon eidolons gain the [resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance) (electricity)
evolution and the [skilled](ref/unchevolution/skilled) (Intimidate) evolution. They also gain a +4
bonus on saving throws against poison.

**At 12th Level:** Archon eidolons gain DR 5/evil. They also gain immunity to petrification and the
_truespeech_ ability (see the [archon creature subtype](ref/subtype/archon)).

**At 16th Level:** Archon eidolons lose the _resistance_ (electricity) evolution and skilled
(Intimidate) evolution, and instead gain the [immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) (electricity)
evolution. In addition, they gain the _aura of menace_ ability (see the archon creature subtype).

**At 20th Level:** Archon eidolons gain the ability to use
[greater teleport](ref/spell/teleport-greater) at will, as the spell (caster level 14th), except
they can teleport only themselves and up to 50 pounds of carried objects.

**At 4th Level:** Archon eidolons add 1 point to their evolution pools.

**At 8th Level:** Archon eidolons gain the [ability increase](ref/unchevolution/ability-increase)
evolution, applied to an ability score the summoner chooses.
