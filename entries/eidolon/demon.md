Raw destruction given material substance, [demon](ref/family/demon) eidolons form out of the Abyss's
stew of soul energy, leading some scholars to speculate that the summoner's arts are related to the
magical tampering that gave rise to the first demons. Demon eidolons revel in causing destruction
and inflicting suffering, and they will do so for their summoners without question, taking pleasure
in whatever havoc they can create. For a demon eidolon, the means justify the ends.&FN&

**Alignment**

**Details:** Chaotic evil.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Aberrant ([bite](ref/unchevolution/bite),
[tentacle mass](ref/unchevolution/tentacle-mass)), Biped ([claws](ref/unchevolution/claws),
[limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\], limbs \[legs\]), quadruped (limbs \[legs, 2\], bite), or
serpentine (bite, [improved damage](ref/unchevolution/improved-damage) \[bite\],
[reach](ref/unchevolution/reach) \[bite\], [tail](ref/unchevolution/tail),
[tail slap](ref/unchevolution/tail-slap)).

**Base Evolutions**

**At 1st Level:** Demon eidolons gain the [resistance](ref/unchevolution/resistance) (electricity)
and _resistance_ (fire) evolutions as well as a +4 bonus on saving throws against poison.

**At 12th Level:** Demon eidolons gain DR 5/good. They also gain the
[ability increase](ref/unchevolution/ability-increase) evolution in an ability score of the
summoner's choice.

**At 16th Level:** Demon eidolons lose the _resistance_ (electricity) evolution, and instead gain
the [immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) (electricity) evolution. They also gain
[telepathy](ref/umr/telepathy).

**At 20th Level:** Demon eidolons gain [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) as a constant
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 4th Level:** Demon eidolons gain acid _resistance_ 10 and cold _resistance_ 10.

**At 8th Level:** Demon eidolons lose the +4 bonus on saving throws against poison and gain immunity
to poison. They also add 1 point to their evolution pools.
