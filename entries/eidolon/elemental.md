Pulled in from one of the four elemental planes, these eidolons are linked to one of the four
elements: air, earth, fire, or water. Generally, an [elemental](ref/family/elemental) eidolon
appears as a creature made entirely of one element, but there is some variation. Elemental eidolons
are decidedly moderate in their views and actions. They tend to avoid the conflicts of others when
they can and seek to maintain balance. The only exception is when facing off against emissaries of
their opposing elements, which they hate utterly.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral.

**Base Form**

**Details:** Aberrant ([bite](ref/unchevolution/bite),
[tentacle mass](ref/unchevolution/tentacle-mass)), Biped ([limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) \[arms\],
limbs \[legs\], [slam](ref/unchevolution/slam)), quadruped (limbs \[legs, 2\], bite), or serpentine
(bite, [improved natural armor](ref/unchevolution/improved-natural-armor),
[reach](ref/unchevolution/reach) \[bite\], [tail](ref/unchevolution/tail),
[tail slap](ref/unchevolution/tail-slap)).

**Base Evolutions**

**Choice:** The base evolutions of an elemental eidolon depend entirely on its chosen element. When
first summoning an elemental eidolon, the summoner must select air, earth, fire, or water. Once
made, this choice cannot be changed. The eidolon gains the following evolutions and abilities, which
can vary depending on its element.

**At 1st Level:** All elemental eidolons gain immunity to paralysis and sleep. In addition, air
elemental eidolons gain the [immunity](ref/unchevolution/immunity) (electricity) evolution. Earth
elemental eidolons gain the
_[immunity](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/immunity)_ (acid) evolution. Fire
elemental eidolons gain the
_[immunity](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/immunity)_ (fire) evolution. Water
elemental eidolons gain the
_[immunity](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/immunity)_ (cold) evolution.

**At 12th Level:** All elemental eidolons gain immunity to bleed, poison, and stun. In addition,
they can no longer be [flanked](ref/rule/flanking).

**At 16th Level:** All elemental eidolons gain immunity to critical hits and do not take additional
damage from precision-based attacks, such as sneak attack.

**At 20th Level:** An air elemental eidolon gains the [whirlwind](ref/umr/whirlwind) ability, with a
maximum height of 15 feet (30 feet for a Large air elemental) and dealing 1d6 points of damage (1d8
for a Large air elemental). Earth elemental eidolons gain the _earth mastery_ ability of an
[earth elemental](ref/monster/earth-elemental) and DR 5/-. Fire elemental eidolons gain the
[energy attacks](ref/unchevolution/energy-attacks) (fire) evolution and the [burn](ref/umr/burn)
ability. Water elemental eidolons gain the _drench_ and _[vortex](ref/spell/vortex)_ abilities of a
[water elemental](ref/monster/water-elemental). The _[vortex](ref/spell/vortex)_ ability works as
the air elemental eidolon's _[whirlwind](ref/spell/whirlwind)_ ability (except as noted in the
_[vortex](ref/spell/vortex)_ ability's description).

**At 4th Level:** All elemental eidolons add 1 point to their evolution pools.

**At 8th Level:** Air elemental eidolons gain the [flight](ref/unchevolution/flight) evolution
(using magic) with a speed equal to their base speed. Earth elemental eidolons gain the
[burrow](ref/unchevolution/burrow) evolution. Fire elemental eidolons increase their base speed by
20 feet. Water elemental eidolons gain the [swim](ref/unchevolution/swim) evolution twice, gaining a
swim speed equal to their base speed + 20 feet. They also gain the [gills](ref/unchevolution/gills)
evolution, which allows them to breathe underwater.
