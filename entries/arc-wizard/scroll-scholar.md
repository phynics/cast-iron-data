A scroll scholar values knowledge and learning as much as her other abilities, trading a portion of
her potential in her chosen class for greater breadth of skill at deciphering old texts, piecing
together strange fragments of esoteric lore, and deciphering the hidden qualities of strange and
unusual magical items. The scroll scholar archetype can be taken by either clerics or wizards - they
give up [different class abilities](ref/arc-cleric/scroll-scholar) for the new abilities granted by
this archetype. A wizard must either be a [universalist](ref/arcaneschool/universalist) wizard or a
[diviner](ref/arcaneschool/divination) wizard in order to take this archetype - other types of
school specializations are not suited to this archetype's focus. A scroll scholar has the following
class features.

**Diligent Student (Ex)**

**Choice:** A scroll scholar chooses one [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill. This becomes a
class skill if it is not already one.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** The scroll scholar adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her class level to all
Knowledge checks of this type (minimum 1).

**Repeat:** A scroll scholar chooses 1 additional Knowledge skill to receive this bonus., 5, 5

**Replaces:** Diviner's fortune (if a diviner) or hand of the apprentice (if a universalist)

**Secrets Revealed (Sp)**

**At 11th Level:** One of these [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (scholar's
choice) becomes a constant spell-like ability.

**At 13th Level:** The other spell becomes a constant
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 5th Level:** A scroll scholar gains the ability to cast
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages) and [identify](ref/spell/identify) as
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Her caster level is equal to her wizard class
level. She can cast these spells once per day.

**At 7th Level:** She can cast them twice per day each.

**At 9th Level:** She can cast them three times per day each.

**Replaces:** 5th-level bonus feat

**Flash of Insight (Su)**

**Immediate:** Once per day, a scroll scholar can gain a +5 bonus on a single attack roll, caster
level check, or saving throw. This decision must be made before the results of the roll are known,
but can be made after the die is rolled.

**At 15th Level:** This ability can be used twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** This ability can be used 3 times per day.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** The normal addition of a new 4th-level spell slot; from this point on, the wizard has
one fewer 4th-level spell slot than other wizards
