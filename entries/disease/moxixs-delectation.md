**Type:** Disease (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d3 and Wisdom damage 1d3

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

A humanoid afflicted with Moxix's delectation must attempt a new Will save each day. If the humanoid
fails, it attacks and attempts to eat the weakest humanoid nearby. If the save is successful, it
resists. A humanoid who dies or is killed while afflicted rises as a [ghast](ref/monster/ghoul) at
the next midnight. The save DC is Charisma-based.
