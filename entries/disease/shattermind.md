**Type:** Disease (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 17

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** 1d4 Wis damage (cannot reduce Wisdom below 1); on any day the victim fails her saving
throw, she suffers the effects of a type of insanity. Unlike normal insanity, on any day she makes a
successful saving throw (using the DC for shattermind, not her form of insanity), she regains her
sanity for that day. A given variety of shattermind spreads the same form of insanity to all of its
victims.

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Shattermind refers to a group of related infectious mental patterns spread by skin-to-skin contact
between infected victims. Most forms of shattermind affect only humanoids and monstrous humanoids.
Each form of shattermind spreads a different form of insanity to its victims.
[Restoration](ref/spell/restoration) allows a new saving throw but does not cure the insanity.
