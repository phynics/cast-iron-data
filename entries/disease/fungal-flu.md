**Sources:** Pathfinder \#13: Shadow in the Sky pg 81

**Type:** Disease (Inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 12

**Onset:** 3d6 days

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d4

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Rolling a 1 on any Fortitude save to resist taking damage from fungal flu results in blindness. The
blindness persists - even resisting spells like remove blindness/deafness - until the disease is
cured.
