**Type:** Disease (Contact or injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Onset:** 1d4 days

**Frequency:** 1/2 days

**Effect:** Dexterity damage 1d2 and Charisma damage 1d2

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Reptiles and creatures with the reptilian subtype receive a -2 penalty on saving throws and require
3 consecutive saves to recover.
