**Sources:** Pathfinder \#1: Burnt Offerings pg 43, Bestiary 4 pg 132

**Type:** Disease (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Onset:** 1d6 days

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Dexterity damage 1d4

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Each time someone takes Dexterity damage from tetanus, there's a 50% chance his jaw muscles stiffen,
preventing speech and the use of spells with verbal components for the next 24 hours.
