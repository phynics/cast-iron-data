**Type:** Disease (Injury or ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d3, Wisdom damage 1d6, and victim is fatigued if it takes any
ability damage from the disease

**Cure:** 3 consecutive saves

Of the afflictions wreaked upon Sarkoris by the Worldwound and Deskari's host, the demonplague is
perhaps the most insidious. This vile sickness spreads through water, affecting plant and animal
life alike. In higher creatures, the demonplague causes madness and then death, but in animals and
other unintelligent beasts, it continues beyond death, animating them as undead horrors called
[plagued beasts](ref/template/plagued-beast). The plague also twists and corrupts plant life - many
hazardous plants are the results of normal plants becoming corrupted by the demonplague.
