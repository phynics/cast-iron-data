**Type:** Disease (Contact or injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d4 and Charisma damage 1d4

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

The first time ethnically Varisian humanoids, including human, half-elves, and half-orcs with
Varisian parentage, are exposed to blood veil, each such character has a flat 5% chance that she is
immune to the disease.

Blood veil takes its name from its most apparent symptoms: a rash and mask of blisters that cover
the face. In its initial stages, the disease is characterized by headache, fatigue, coughing, and
the aforementioned rash. As it progresses, the cough becomes more obtrusive; the rash spreads to the
neck, face, and limbs and develops into pox-like blisters; and the lymph glands swell into painful
buboes. At its most advanced stage, the blisters grow to the size of grapes or larger, internal
bleeding creates black patches on the skin, and the victim expectorates blood. Ultimately, a bloody,
wheezing death occurs. If left untreated, blood veil kills the average human in 7 days.
