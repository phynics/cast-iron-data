**Sources:** Pathfinder \#51: The Hungry Storm pg 67

**Type:** Disease (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 20

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** 1d4 Str damage and 1d4 Con damage, target must make a second Fort save or 1 point of the
damage is drain instead, and a creature that has suffered ability drain glows blue as if limned with
faerie fire

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves
