**Type:** Disease (Contact or injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Dexterity damage 1d6 and target is exhausted, its speed is reduced by half, and it is
unable to speak

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Known to take root in places long buried by supernatural cold, this affliction infects ruins and
deep forests throughout Irrisen. It slowly freezes creatures from the inside out without dealing
cold damage, so sufferers simply feel the weariness and fatigue of exerting themselves in a cold
environment. The affliction reduces an affected creature's range of movement and acts in many ways
like very gradual petrification. A sufferer is unable to speak, is severely slowed, and takes
Dexterity damage; if the victim's Dexterity score drops below 5, it is unable to move at all.
Creatures suffering from this plague don't die from it, but it often strands them out in the cold
where they die from exposure.
