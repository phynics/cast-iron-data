**Sources:** Undead Unleashed pg 15, Mythic Realms pg 21

**Type:** Disease (Contact (see text))

**Save:** Fortitude DC 22

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d6 and Charisma damage 1d6

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Contact is automatic when a creature comes within a 60-foot radius of blightburn, and can be blocked
only by lead sheeting, 1 foot of stone, or a force effect
