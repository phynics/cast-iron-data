**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 140\
Ancient guardians revere nature and draw power from its divine energies, but also protect the
customs, histories, and lore of their tribe and race. They act as ambassadors and protectors of the
old ways, nurturing their tribe's growth and holding back civilization's corruptive influence.

**Ancient Arms**

**Alters:** Weapon proficiencies

**Info:** An ancient guardian can't wield weapons made primarily of metal. She is proficient with
only the [club](ref/eq-weapon/club), [dart](ref/eq-weapon/dart),
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), [shortspear](ref/eq-weapon/shortspear),
[sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling), and [spear](ref/eq-weapon/spear). She is also proficient with all
natural attacks (claw, bite, and so forth) of any form she assumes with _wild shape._ Using a
prohibited weapon renders her unable to cast druid spells or use any of her druid supernatural or
spell-like class abilities while wielding the weapon and for 24 hours after ceasing to wield it.

**Community Bond (Ex)**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** An ancient guardian must choose the domain nature bond ability and select from the
following domains: Community, [Healing](ref/domain/healing), Knowledge,
[Protection](ref/domain/protection), or Repose.

**Patience of Nature (Ex)**

**At 2nd Level:** An ancient guardian adds [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) (CHA),
[Perform (oratory)](ref/skill/perform-any) (CHA), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) (WIS)
to her list of class skills and gains an insight bonus equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her druid level on such checks.

**Replaces:** Wild empathy

**Ancient Ways (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** An ancient guardian increases any climb, fly, or swim speeds she has or gains via her
_wild shape_ ability by 10 feet.

**Replaces:** Trackless step, woodland stride

**Dispel Hostility (Su)**

**Ability:** An ancient guardian can channel stored spell energy into a spell capable of dispelling
hostile thoughts and strong emotions. She can &quot&lose&quot& a prepared spell in order to cast
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions). Instead of the normal Will saving throw for that spell,
the DC of a _[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions)_ spell cast with this ability is 10 + 1/2 the
ancient guardian's druid level + her Wisdom modifier.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Spontaneous casting

**Unimpeachable (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** An ancient guardian gains a +4 bonus on saves against enchantments.

**Replaces:** Resist nature's lure

**Undo Artifice (Sp)**

**Ability:** An ancient guardian gains the ability to reduce a nonliving item to its component raw
materials. This acts as the spell [polymorph any object](ref/spell/polymorph-any-object), but works
on magical or nonmagical nonliving objects. The duration is always permanent. Unlike
_[polymorph any object](ref/spell/polymorph-any-object),_ this ability can't be used to mimic the
effects of other spells. Items subject to this effect receive a Fortitude saving throw to negate the
effect (DC = 10 + 1/2 the ancient guardian's druid level + her Wisdom modifier). Magic items receive
a circumstance bonus on this save equal to +1 for every 5,000 gp of the item's value. An ancient
guardian can use _undo artifice_ a number of times per day equal to her Wisdom modifier.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** A thousand faces
