Supernaturalists wholly embrace paranormal phenomena as extensions of nature.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Weapon and armor proficiencies

**Info:** A supernaturalist is not proficient with any type of armor.

**Eldritch Botanist (Ex)**

**Ability:** A supernaturalist can affect plants with mind-affecting effects she creates and with
spells that normally affect animals. Any such spell that allows a Will save can instead be negated
by the target plant with a successful Fortitude save at the same DC.

**Paranormal Scholar (Ex)**

**Ability:** A supernaturalist gains [Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) as a bonus
feat and adds [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) (CHA),
[Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) (INT), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive)
(WIS) as class skills.

**Replaces:** Nature sense, trackless step, resist nature's lure

**Special:** At 3rd level and every 3 levels thereafter, she can select a spell from the psychic
class's spell list and add it to her druid spell list, and is able to prepare and cast it as a druid
spell.

**Animal Spirit (Su)**

**Ability:** A supernaturalist can perform a seance to call an animal spirit of any animal with
which she is familiar to serve as her companion and guide. The supernaturalist gains _spirit, spirit
bonus, spirit surge,_ and _taboo_ abilities as a [medium](ref/class/medium) 3 levels lower than her
druid level. The supernaturalist's allies cannot participate in this seance.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Special:** The spirit abilities of the animal spirit are given below.

**Spirit Bonus**

**Info:** Your spirit bonus applies on attack and damage rolls with natural weapons and on skill
checks relating to animals and plants.

**Seance Boon**

**Passive:** Your natural armor bonus to AC increases by 1.

**Favored Location**

**Info:** Native terrain of the animal type being invoked.

**Influence Penalty**

**Passive:** You become wild and savage, taking a penalty equal to your spirit bonus on caster level
checks, Charisma- and Intelligence-based ability checks, and skill checks.

**Taboos**

**Action:** Choice

Choose one:

- You must not speak (except with animals and plants)
- You must not wield manufactured weapons
- You must not eat anything you did not kill or harvest yourself

**Totem Transformation (Lesser, Su)**

**Action:** Ability

You gain any of the following abilities possessed by the animal you select as your animal spirit:

- [Bite](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d6 for a Medium druid)
- 2 claws (1d4 for a Medium druid)
- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)
- Improved land movement speed (up to a +20-foot enhancement bonus to the druid's base land movement
  speed)
- [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision)
- [Scent](ref/umr/scent)
- Swim (up to 30 feet).

**Pack Leader (Intermediate, Su)**

**Passive:** When you summon animals that match your animal spirit, they gain a +2 bonus on attack
rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws.

**Wildstrike (Greater, Su)**

**Swift:** You can allow your animal spirit to gain 1 point of influence over you to allow an animal
or plant (including your companion) within 30 feet that can see or hear you to immediately take an
additional standard action.

**Wild Heart (Supreme, Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast any [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)
spell you can cast spontaneously as a **standard action** without expending a spell slot.

**Passive:** You gain a +4 bonus on saves against enchantment and mind-affecting effects, and
immunity to effects that affect only humanoids.

### Optional Abilities

A supernaturalist can choose _plant bond_ and _green empathy_ in place of _nature bond_ and _wild
empathy._ This choice must be made when the druid takes this archetype.

**Plant Bond (Ex)**

**Choice:** A supernaturalist forms a mystic bond with plant life. This bond can take one of two
forms.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Nature bond

**Domain**

**Ability:** This grants the supernaturalist one of the following domains:
[Plant](ref/domain/plant), [Growth](ref/domain/plant), [Jungle](ref/druiddomain/jungle),
[Swamp](ref/druiddomain/swamp). When determining the powers and bonus spells granted by this domain,
the supernaturalist's effective cleric level is equal to her druid level. A supernaturalist who
selects this option also receives additional domain spell slots, just like a cleric. She must
prepare the spell from her domain in this slot, and this spell cannot be used to cast a spell
spontaneously.

**Plant companion**

**Ability:** A supernaturalist can form a close bond with a plant companion. A supernaturalist may
begin play with any of the plants listed under **Plant Companions** in the treesinger archetype.
This plant is a loyal companion that accompanies the supernaturalist on her adventures. Except for
the companion being a creature of the plant type, drawn from the list of plant companions, this
ability otherwise works like the standard druid's animal companion ability.

**Green Empathy (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Wild empathy

A supernaturalist can improve the attitude of a plant creature. This ability functions just like a
Diplomacy check made to improve the attitude of a person. The supernaturalist rolls 1d20 and adds
her druid level and her Charisma modifier to determine the _green empathy_ check result. The typical
wild plant creature has a starting attitude of indifferent.

To use _green empathy,_ the supernaturalist and the plant creature must be within 30 feet of one
another under normal conditions. Generally, influencing a plant creature in this way takes 1 minute
but, as with influencing people, it might take more or less time.

A supernaturalist can also use this ability to influence an animal, but she takes a -4 penalty on
the check.
