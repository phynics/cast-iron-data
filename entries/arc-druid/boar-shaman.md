A boar shaman chooses the stolid and ferocious boar as her totem. Content to be left alone, she
becomes one of the most dangerous creatures of the wild when provoked.

**Nature Bond**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** A boar shaman who chooses an animal companion must select a [boar](ref/companion/boar).
If choosing a domain, the boar shaman must choose from the [Animal](ref/domain/animal),
[Destruction](ref/domain/destruction) (Rage), [Protection](ref/domain/protection), and Strength
domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A boar shaman can use _wild empathy_ with porcine creatures as a **full-round action**
with a +4 bonus.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Totem Transformation (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 12th Level:** This becomes a **swift action**.

**At 7th Level:** This becomes a **move action**.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This is a polymorph effect and cannot be used while the druid is using another
polymorph effect, such as _wild shape._

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per druid level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

A boar shaman may adopt an aspect of the boar while retaining her normal form. The druid may select
from the following sets of bonuses and abilities:

- **Movement** (+10 enhancement bonus to land speed)
- **Senses** ([low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent))
- **[Natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks)** (gore \[1d8 for a Medium druid\], +2 on combat
  maneuver checks to overrun)
- **Toughness** (+2 natural armor bonus to AC, [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) feat).

While using _totem transformation,_ the boar shaman may speak normally and can cast
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) (boars and related animals only) at will.

**Totemic Summons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Standard:** A boar shaman may cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) to summon
[boars](ref/monster/boar) or other porcine creatures, and these summoned creatures gain
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to her druid level. She can apply the
[young](ref/template/young) template to any porcine summon to reduce the level of the summoning
spell required by one. She can also increase the level of summoning required by one in order to
apply either the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) or the [giant](ref/template/giant) template, or
increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant templates.

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A boar shaman gains _wild shape,_ but the ability functions at her druid level - 2. If
she takes on the form of a boar, she instead uses her druid level +2.

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** A boar shaman gains one of the following bonus feats:
[Bleeding Critical](ref/feat/bleeding-critical), [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight),
[Diehard](ref/feat/diehard), or [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun).

**At 13th Level:** The druid gains another feat from the list above.

**At 17th Level:** The druid gains a third bonus feat from the list above.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats.
