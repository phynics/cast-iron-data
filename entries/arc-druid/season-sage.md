**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 11\
Season sages wander as the seasons change, turning the leaves and transforming nature as they pass
and taking pleasure in the transition between seasons.

**Prerequisites:** Gathlain

**Season's Touch (Su)**

**Ability:** A season sage can infuse living things with the essence of a season with a touch,
causing them to manifest the changes associated with that season - for example, a touch of winter
can make some animals grow a thick winter coat, while a touch of summer can make them shed. A touch
of autumn would make a deciduous tree's leaves change colors, a touch of winter would make its
leaves fall, and a touch of spring would make it put forth new leaves.

**Info:** In addition, a season sage can use the following abilities (in any combination) a total
number of times per day equal to 1 + his Wisdom modifier.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Vernal Growth**

**Ability:** A touched living creature gains the effects of
[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person) (even if it is not a humanoid) for 1 minute per druid
level. A touched non-creature plant likewise doubles in all dimensions for the same duration.

**Summer Might**

**Ability:** A touched creature gains a +4 enhancement bonus to Constitution for 1 minute per druid
level.

**Autumn Rot**

**Ability:** A touched creature or object made of plant matter takes 1d6 points of acid damage per 2
druid levels.

**Winter Frost**

**Ability:** A touched creature or object takes 1d6 points of cold damage per 2 druid levels.

**Season's Veil (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** The radius increases to 40 feet.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Standard:** A season sage can radiate an aura of seasonal power. This affects a 20-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) centered on the season sage and lasts in that area for 10 minutes,
regardless of where the season sage moves afterward.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 2 druid levels beyond 6th

**Vernal Bloom**

**Ability:** The area surges with life. Plants form vibrant,
[heavy undergrowth](ref/rule/forest-terrain) while in the aura. If the area can support plants, a
circle of minor plants lingers afterward.

**At 8th Level:** The season sage can affect the area with [plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth) as
a **swift action** at will, which can linger after the aura passes.

**Summer Heat**

**Ability:** The area of effect is treated as if it were under the effects of
[extreme heat](ref/rule/heat-dangers).

**At 8th Level:** As a **swift action** the season sage can have the area deal 1d6 points of fire
damage each round to other creatures in the area.

**Autumn Squall**

**Ability:** The area is filled with fog, rain, and blown leaves. All creatures in the area gain
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (abilities to specifically see through fog or rain don't apply).

**At 8th Level:** The season sage can affect the area with
[diminish plants](ref/spell/diminish-plants) as a **swift action** at will, which also lingers after
the aura passes.

**Winter Chill**

**Ability:** The area of effect is treated as if it were under the effects of
[extreme cold](ref/rule/cold-dangers).

**At 8th Level:** As a **swift action** the season sage can have the area deal 1d6 points of cold
damage each round to other creatures in the area.

**Season Mastery (Sp)**

**Ability:** A season sage can cast [control weather](ref/spell/control-weather) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day; his choice of effects is not
limited by the season.

**Gained:** At 12th Level
