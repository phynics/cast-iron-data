A nature fang is a druid who stalks and slays those who despoil nature, kill scarce animals, or
introduce diseases to unprotected habitats. She gives up a close empathic connection with the
natural world to become its deadly champion and avenger.

**Studied Target (Ex)**

**Ability:** A nature fang gains the [slayer's](ref/class/slayer) _studied target_ class feature.
Unlike a slayer, a nature fang does not gain the ability to maintain more than one studied target at
the same time.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Nature sense, wild empathy, woodland stride

**Studied Target (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Text from the slayer class

**Free:** You can discard this connection to a studied target, allowing you to study another target
in its place.

**Immediate:** If you deal sneak attack damage to a target, you can study that target immediately,
allowing you to apply your studied target bonuses against that target (including to the normal
weapon damage roll).

**Move:** You can study an opponent you can see. You then gain a bonus on Bluff, Knowledge,
Perception, Sense Motive, and Survival checks attempted against that opponent, and a bonus on weapon
attack and damage rolls against it; these bonuses are equal to 1 +
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your slayer level. The DCs of slayer class abilities
against that opponent increase by 1 + one-fifth of your slayer level. These bonuses remain in effect
until either the opponent is dead or you study a new target.

**Slayer Talent**

**Choice:** A nature fang selects a slayer talent. At every even-numbered level (6th, 8th, 10th, and
so on), she gains a new slayer talent. She uses her druid level as her slayer level to determine
what talents she can select.

**At 12th Level:** She can select an advanced slayer talent in place of a slayer talent.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Special:** The following slayer talents complement the nature fang archetype:
[foil scrutiny](ref/slayertalent/foil-scrutiny), [poison use](ref/slayertalent/poison-use),
[ranger combat style](ref/slayertalent/ranger-combat-style). Additionally, the following rogue
talents, which are also available to slayers, complement the archetype:
[bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack), [camouflage](ref/talent/camouflage),
[fast stealth](ref/talent/fast-stealth), [finesse rogue](ref/talent/finesse-rogue),
[lasting poison](ref/talent/lasting-poison), [rogue crawl](ref/talent/rogue-crawl),
[slow reactions](ref/talent/slow-reactions), [surprise attack](ref/talent/surprise-attack),
[swift poison](ref/talent/swift-poison), [terrain mastery](ref/talent/terrain-mastery). The
following advanced talents complement the nature fang archetype:
[assassinate](ref/slayertalent/assassinate),
[slayer camouflage](ref/slayertalent/slayer-camouflage),
[woodland stride](ref/slayertalent/woodland-stride). The advanced rogue talent
[master of disguise](ref/talent/master-of-disguise) also fits the archetype.

**Sneak Attack (Ex)**

**Ability:** A nature fang gains [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +1d6. This functions as
the [rogue](ref/class/rogue) sneak attack ability. If the nature fang gets a sneak attack bonus from
another source, the bonuses on damage stack.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Resist nature's lure

**Swift Studied Target**

**Ability:** A nature fang can study an opponent as a **move** or **swift action**.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity
