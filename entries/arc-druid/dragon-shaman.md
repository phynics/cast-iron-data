Your totem is the legendary dragon, fearsome and deadly yet cunning and wise, a creature born of
pure magic and raw elemental fury, bound within a shell of fangs, claws, and scales that few dare to
challenge. Though your initial focus is on dragons' mundane cousins, as your powers grow you become
attuned to actual dragons.

**Nature Bond**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** A dragon shaman who chooses an animal companion must select a
[crocodile](ref/companion/crocodile) or [monitor lizard](ref/companion/lizard-monitor-lizard). If
choosing a domain, the dragon shaman must choose from the [Air](ref/domain/air),
[Animal](ref/domain/animal), [Destruction](ref/domain/destruction), [Earth](ref/domain/earth),
[Fire](ref/domain/fire), [War](ref/domain/war), and [Water](ref/domain/water) domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A dragon shaman can use her _wild empathy_ ability with lizards as a **full-round
action** with a +4 bonus.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Totem Transformation (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 12th Level:** This becomes a **swift action**.

**At 5th Level:** The druid may select a new option: **Movement** (fly speed 30 ft. \[average\])

**At 7th Level:** This becomes a **move action**.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This is a polymorph effect and cannot be used while the druid is using another
polymorph effect, such as _wild shape._

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per druid level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

A dragon shaman may adopt an aspect of the dragon while retaining her normal form. She gains one of
the following bonuses:

- **Senses** ([low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), +4 racial bonus to Perception)
- **Toughness** (+2 natural armor bonus to AC, [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) feat)
- **[Natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks)** (bite \[1d6\] and 2 claws \[1d4\] for a Medium
  shaman, +2 bonus on CMB on grapple checks).

While using _totem transformation,_ the dragon shaman may speak normally and can cast
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) (lizards only) at will.

**Totemic Summons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** A thousand faces

**Standard:** A dragon shaman may cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) to
summon lizards, and summoned lizards gain [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points)
equal to her druid level. She can apply the [young](ref/template/young) to any lizard to reduce the
level of the summoning spell required by one. She can also increase the level of summoning required
by one in order to apply either the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) or the
[giant](ref/template/giant) template, or increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant
templates.

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A dragon shaman gains _wild shape,_ but the ability functions at her druid level - 4.
If she takes on the form of a lizard, she instead uses her unmodified druid level.

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Dragon Bite (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** A dragon shaman's bite attack (whether using _totem transformation_ or _wild shape_ to
take the form of a lizard) deals +1d6 points of energy damage (acid, cold, electricity, or fire).
She chooses what kind of energy damage that she deals each time she bites.

**Replaces:** The additional use of _wild shape_ at 8th level

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** A dragon shaman gains one of the following bonus feats:
[Combat Casting](ref/feat/combat-casting), [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display),
[Magical Aptitude](ref/feat/magical-aptitude), [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Knowledge
\[arcana\]), and [Spell Penetration](ref/feat/spell-penetration).

**At 13th Level:** The druid gains another feat from the list above.

**At 17th Level:** The druid gains a third bonus feat from the list above.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats.
