Shepherds of the lakes and seas, aquatic druids guard ecosystems ranging from shallows streams to
deep ocean trenches, ministering to their residents and communing with the tides.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** An aquatic druid's _wild empathy_ functions only on creatures that have a swim speed or
the aquatic or water subtype; however, she can improve the attitude of any such creature with
Intelligence 2 or less regardless of type, including mindless creatures.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Aquatic Adaptation (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** An aquatic druid gains an insight bonus on Initiative checks and Knowledge (geography),
Perception, Stealth, Survival, and Swim checks equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her
druid level in aquatic terrain, and she cannot be tracked such environments.

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Natural Swimmer (Ex)**

**Ability:** An aquatic druid gains a swim speed equal to half her land speed.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trackless step

**Resist Ocean's Fury (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** An aquatic druid gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against spells of the water type or
the exceptional or supernatural abilities of creatures with the aquatic or water subtype.

**Replaces:** Resist nature's lure

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Ability:** An aquatic druid gains _wild shape,_ except that her effective druid level for the
ability is equal to her druid level - 2.

**Alters:** Wild shape

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Seaborn (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** An aquatic druid gains the [aquatic](ref/subtype/aquatic) subtype, the
[amphibious](ref/umr/amphibious) trait, and a swim speed equal to her land speed. She also can
endure cold climate effects as if using [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements).

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Deep Diver (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** A aquatic druid gains DR/slashing or piercing equal to 1/2 her level. This
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) also applies against spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) that inflict damage by grappling or crushing
(e.g., [black tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles), [crushing hand](ref/spell/crushing-hand)). She
never takes [pressure damage](ref/rule/water-dangers) from deep water.

**Replaces:** A thousand faces
