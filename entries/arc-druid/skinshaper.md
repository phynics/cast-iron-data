Using the same primal energy with which other druids assume animal forms, skinshapers can imitate
some of the most dangerous beasts of all: humans and other humanoids. Rather than merely copying
humanoids' shapes and abilities, skinshapers unlock brutal energies that push their borrowed bodies
far beyond the capabilities of normal humanoid creatures. Their connection to humanoids bring
skinshapers into settlements more often than typical druids, and their penchant for disguise allows
them to excel at intrigue.

**Gained class skills:** dis

**Lost class skills:** ride

**Skinshaping (Su)**

**Ability:** A skinshaper gains the ability to turn herself into any Small or Medium humanoid and
back again once per day. This ability functions as per [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self), except as
noted here. The effect lasts for 1 hour per druid level, or until the skinshaper changes back.
Changing form is a **standard action** and doesn't provoke an attack of opportunity. The skinshaper
must be familiar with the form she has chosen.

**At 20th Level:** She can use _skinshaping_ at will.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Special:** She also gains other benefits while _skinshaping,_ as described below.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 2 druid levels beyond 4th

**Unarmed Strike**

**At 4th Level:** The skinshaper learns to intuitively use her body as a weapon. While
_skinshaping,_ she gains the benefit of the
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) feat, and her unarmed strike deals
damage as if she were a [monk](ref/class/monk) with a monk level equal to her druid level - 3.

**Unarmed Strike**

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the monk class ability.

**At 12th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d10 (Small), 2d6 (Medium), or 3d6 (Large) damage.

**At 16th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d6 (Small), 2d8 (Medium), or 3d8 (Large) damage.

**At 4th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium), or 2d6 (Large) damage.

**At 8th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d8 (Small), 1d10 (Medium), or 2d8 (Large) damage.

**At 1st Level:** Your unarmed strike deals 1d4 (Small), 1d6 (Medium), or 1d8 (Large) damage.

**Enhancement Bonus**

**At 10th Level:** She gains an additional +2 enhancement bonus to one ability score, or can stack
them as a +4 bonus to one score.

**At 14th Level:** She gains a third +2 enhancement bonus to one ability score. She can distribute
these bonuses as she wishes in increments of +2, but the enhancement bonus on any single ability
score cannot exceed +4.

**At 18th Level:** She gains a fourth +2 enhancement bonus to one ability score, with the same
restrictions as above.

**At 6th Level:** A skinshaper gains the ability to channel the versatile nature of humanoids to
enhance her body and mind. Whenever she uses _skinshaping_ to assume a humanoid shape other than her
own, she gains a +2 enhancement bonus to any one ability score. The bonus persists as long as she
remains in that form.

**Advanced Form**

**At 8th Level:** A skinshaper can imitate humanoid creatures even more accurately. She gains any of
the following abilities that the form she assumes has: [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 90 feet,
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent), climb 60 feet, fly 60 feet
(good maneuverability), swim 60 feet, [ferocity](ref/umr/ferocity),
[orc ferocity](ref/race/half-orc), and [hold breath](ref/umr/hold-breath). If the form she assumes
has the [aquatic](ref/subtype/aquatic) subtype, she gains the aquatic subtype and the
[amphibious](ref/umr/amphibious) special quality.

**Racial Memory**

**At 12th Level:** A skinshaper can access the racial memory of the humanoid she is imitating. If
the form she assumes has any of the following racial abilities, she gains those abilities: defensive
training, hatred, [poison use](ref/ability/poison-use), [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack)
+2d6, stability, and weapon familiarity. Additionally, she can speak any racial languages of the
chosen form.

**Skill Bonuses**

**At 16th Level:** A skinshaper gains the racial skill bonuses of the imitated humanoid, to a
maximum of a +4 bonus on a given skill. The skinshaper cannot benefit from any variable racial skill
bonuses associated with the chosen form (such as gnomes' racial bonus on any one Craft or Profession
skill).

**Flashmorph (Su)**

**Info:** A skinshaper learns to change her shape extremely quickly. She can use skinshaping as a
**swift action**.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** A thousand faces
