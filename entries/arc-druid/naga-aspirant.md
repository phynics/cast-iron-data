**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 196\
The naga aspirant follows the ancient beliefs and engages in the rituals of a druidic sect dedicated
to the transcendence of her nagaji form through absolute devotion to nagas and naga gods. Through
acting as a herald to the naga deities, the aspirant is rewarded with the ability to unlock her
ultimate spirit form and become a true naga. A naga aspirant has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Nagaji

**Aspirant's Bond (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A naga aspirant gains a spiritual connection to the serpentine deities worshiped by the
nagas.

**Passive:** At 1st level, and **each time she gains a druid level**, she may add one of the
following spells to her druid spell list.

**Replaces:** Spontaneous casting

**Cantrips:** Acid splash, bleed, daze, mage hand, open/close, ray of frost

**Spells:** Charm person, divine favor, expeditious retreat, mage armor, magic missile, ray of
enfeeblement, shield, shield of faith, silent image, true strike

**Spells:** Acid arrow, detect thoughts, invisibility, mirror image, scorching ray, see invisibility

**Spells:** Dispel magic, displacement, fireball, lightning bolt, suggestion

**Spells:** Divine power, greater invisibility

**Aspirant's Enlightenment (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** A naga aspirant gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against the
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), supernatural abilities, and poison of nagas.

**Replaces:** Resist nature's lure

**Naga Shape (Su)**

**At 4th Level:** The naga aspirant gains the _wild shape_ ability, as a normal druid.

**At 6th Level:** The naga aspirant can use her _wild shape_ ability to assume the form of a true
naga. This effect functions in a similar manner to a [shapechange](ref/spell/shapechange) spell with
the following exception. The druid's true naga form is unique, representing her personal evolution.
When taking naga form, the nagaji's body transforms into that of a large serpent, though she keeps
her own head. The naga aspirant loses her limbs and her size increases by one category, granting her
a +4 size bonus to Strength and Constitution, a -2 penalty to Dexterity, and a +2 enhancement bonus
to her natural armor bonus. She gains a +10 enhancement bonus to land speed and a
[bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals 1d6 points of damage. She can cast verbal spells
in this form, but cannot cast spells with other components without metamagic or feats such as
[Natural Spell](ref/feat/natural-spell). This otherwise works like and replaces _wild shape._

**Replaces:** Wild Shape

**Augmented Form (Su)**

**At 13th Level:** The aspirant gains another ability from the list below.

**At 17th Level:** The aspirant gains a third ability from the list below.

**At 9th Level:** A naga aspirant can choose one of the following abilities to enhance her naga
form. Once chosen, this augmentation cannot be changed and always applies to her naga form. The
caster level for these abilities is equal to her druid level, and unless otherwise stated, the DC is
equal to 10 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) the druid's class level + the druid's Charisma
bonus.

**Replaces:** Venom immunity, a thousand faces, timeless body

**Charming Gaze (Sp)**

**Ability:** The druid gains a [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack that affects creatures within 30 feet as
a [charm person](ref/spell/charm-person) spell.

**Darkvision (Su)**

**Passive:** The druid gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet.

**Detect Thoughts (Su)**

**Ability:** The druid can use _[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts)_ at will.

**Guarded Thoughts (Ex)**

**Passive:** The druid gains a +2 racial bonus on saves against charm effects and immunity to any
form of mind reading (such as _[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts)_).

**Poison Immunity (Ex)**

**Passive:** The druid gains immunity to all poisons.

**Prereq:** The druid's naga form must have at least one poison-based natural attack.

**Poisonous Sting (Ex)**

**Action:** Passive Ability

**Prereq:** The naga form's tail must have a _sting._

The druid's stinger becomes venomous.

**Type:** Poison (Sting-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the druid's level + her Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1 round

**Effect:** sleep for 2d4 minutes

**Cure:** 1 save

**Spit Venom (Ex)**

**Prereq:** The naga form must have a _[venomous bite](ref/spell/venomous-bite)._

**Standard:** The naga form can spit her venom up to 30 feet. This is a ranged touch attack with no
range increment. Any opponent hit by this attack must make a successful save (see Venemous Bite
poison below) to avoid the effect.

**Sting (Ex)**

**Passive:** The naga form grows a stinger on the end of her tail, granting her a sting natural
attack that deals 1d6 points of piercing damage.

**Swim (Ex)**

**Passive:** The naga form gains a swim speed equal to her base speed.

**Tough Scales (Ex)**

**Passive:** The druid's enhancement bonus to her natural armor increases by +2.

**Special:** The druid may select this ability more than once. Its effects stack.

**Venomous Bite (Ex)**

**Action:** Passive Ability

The naga form's bite attack becomes poisonous.

**Type:** Poison (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the druid's level + her Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

**True Naga (Su)**

**Ability:** A naga aspirant metamorphoses into a unique naga. Her _wild shape_ form becomes her
natural form, though she can transform into her original nagaji shape at will. Her creature type
permanently changes to aberration.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Wildshape (at will)
