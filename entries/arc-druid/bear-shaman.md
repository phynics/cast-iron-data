**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 102\
A shaman with this focus calls upon the mighty bear, titan of the woodlands and mountains, a paragon
of strength and ferocity, and yet also a quiet protector rich in wisdom.

**Nature Bond**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** A bear shaman who chooses an animal companion must select a [bear](ref/companion/bear).
If choosing a domain, the bear shaman must choose from the [Animal](ref/domain/animal),
[Earth](ref/domain/earth), [Protection](ref/domain/protection), and Strength domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bear shaman can use _wild empathy_ with bears and wolverines as a **full-round
action** with a +4 bonus.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Totem Transformation (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 12th Level:** This becomes a **swift action**.

**At 7th Level:** This becomes a **move action**.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This is a polymorph effect and cannot be used while the druid is using another
polymorph effect, such as _wild shape._

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per druid level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

A bear shaman may adopt an aspect of the bear while retaining her normal form. She gains one of the
following bonuses:

- **Movement** (+10 enhancement bonus to land speed, +4 racial bonus on Swim checks)
- **Senses** ([low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent))
- **Toughness** (+2 natural armor bonus to AC, [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) feat)
- **[Natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks)** (bite \[1d6\] and 2 claws \[1d4\] for a Medium
  shaman, +2 to CMB on grapple checks).

While using _totem transformation,_ the bear shaman may speak normally and can cast
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) (mammals only) at will.

**Totemic Summons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** A thousand faces

**Standard:** A bear shaman may cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) to summon
bears, and summoned bears gain [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to her
druid level. She can apply the [young](ref/template/young) template to any bear to reduce the level
of the summoning spell required by one. She can also increase the level of summoning required by one
in order to apply either the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) or the [giant](ref/template/giant)
template, or increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant templates.

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Ability:** A bear shaman gains _wild shape,_ but the ability functions at her druid level - 2. If
she takes on the form of a bear, she instead uses her druid level + 2.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** A bear shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard),
Endurance, [Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude),
[Improved Great Fortitude](ref/feat/improved-great-fortitude), [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness).

**At 13th Level:** The druid gains another feat from the list above.

**At 17th Level:** The druid gains a third bonus feat from the list above.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats.
