**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 39\
A shaman with this focus calls upon the primeval dinosaur, the archaic terror that lingers as a
hungering, atavistic stranger at the fringes of the ecosystem, a destroyer and despoiler whose
coming other animals dread.

**Nature Bond**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** A saurian shaman who chooses an animal companion must select a dinosaur. If choosing a
domain, a saurian shaman must choose from the [Animal](ref/domain/animal),
[Destruction](ref/domain/destruction), Strength, and [War](ref/domain/war) domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A saurian shaman can use _wild empathy_ with dinosaurs and reptiles as a **full-round
action** with a +4 bonus.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Totem Transformation (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 12th Level:** This becomes a **swift action**.

**At 7th Level:** This becomes a **move action**.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This is a polymorph effect and cannot be used while the druid is using another
polymorph effect, such as _wild shape._

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per druid level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

A saurian shaman may adopt an aspect of the saurian while retaining her normal form. The druid may
select from the following bonuses:

- **Movement** (+10 enhancement bonus to land speed)
- **Scales** (+2 natural armor bonus to AC)
- **Senses** ([low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent))
- **[Natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks)** (bite \[1d6\], 2 claws \[1d4\] for a Medium druid,
  rake, +2 CMB to grapple)

While using _totem transformation,_ a saurian shaman may speak normally and can cast
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) (reptiles and dinosaurs only) at will.

**Totemic Summons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Standard:** A saurian shaman may cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) to
summon reptiles and dinosaurs, and these summoned creatures gain
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to her druid level. She can apply the
[young](ref/template/young) template to such summons to reduce the level of the summoning spell
required by one. She can also increase the level of summoning required by one in order to apply
either the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) or the [giant](ref/template/giant) template, or
increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant templates.

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Ability:** A saurian shaman gains _wild shape,_ but the ability functions at her druid level - 2.
If she takes on the form of a reptile or a dinosaur, she instead uses her druid level +2.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** A saurian shaman gains one of the following bonus feats:
[Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun), [Nimble Moves](ref/feat/nimble-moves),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Intimidate), or
[Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike).

**At 13th Level:** The druid gains another feat from the list above.

**At 17th Level:** The druid gains a third bonus feat from the list above.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats.
