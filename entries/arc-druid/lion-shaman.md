**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 103\
A shaman with this totem calls upon the proud lion, imposing and majestic, the mighty leader of
deadly hunters.

**Nature Bond**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** A lion shaman who chooses an animal companion must select a
[lion](ref/companion/cat-big). If choosing a domain, the lion shaman must choose from the
[Animal](ref/domain/animal), Glory, Nobility, and [Sun](ref/domain/sun) domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A lion shaman can use _wild empathy_ with felines as a **full-round action** with a +4
bonus.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Totem Transformation (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 12th Level:** This becomes a **swift action**.

**At 7th Level:** This becomes a **move action**.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This is a polymorph effect and cannot be used while the druid is using another
polymorph effect, such as _wild shape._

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per druid level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

A lion shaman may adopt an aspect of the lion while retaining her normal form. The druid may select
from the following bonuses:

- **Movement** (+20 enhancement bonus to land speed)
- **Senses** ([low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent))
- **[Natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks)** (bite \[1d4\], 2 claws \[1d4\] for a Medium druid,
  rake, +2 CMB to grapple)

While using _totem transformation,_ the lion shaman may speak normally and can cast
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) (felines only) at will.

**Totemic Summons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Standard:** A lion shaman may cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) to summon
felines, and these summoned creatures gain [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points)
equal to her druid level. She can apply the [young](ref/template/young) template to any feline to
reduce the level of the summoning spell required by one. She can also increase the level of
summoning required by one in order to apply either the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) or the
[giant](ref/template/giant) template, or increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant
templates.

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A lion shaman gains _wild shape,_ but the ability functions at her druid level - 2. If
she takes on the form of a feline, she instead uses her druid level + 2.

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** A lion shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Lunge](ref/feat/lunge), [Improved Iron Will](ref/feat/improved-iron-will),
[Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will), or [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Acrobatics).

**At 13th Level:** The druid gains another feat from the list above.

**At 17th Level:** The druid gains a third bonus feat from the list above.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats.
