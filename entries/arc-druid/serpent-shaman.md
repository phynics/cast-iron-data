**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 103\
A shaman with this totem calls upon the cunning serpent, the stealthy deceiver who draws the weak
minded in and strikes while they are unaware. Some hate its treacherous nature, while others praise
its thoughtful pragmatism.

**Nature Bond**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** A serpent shaman who chooses an animal companion must select a snake. If choosing a
domain, the serpent shaman must choose from the [Animal](ref/domain/animal),
[Charm](ref/domain/charm), Trickery, and [Water](ref/domain/water) domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A serpent shaman can use _wild empathy_ with reptiles as a **full-round action** with a
+4 bonus.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Totem Transformation (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 12th Level:** This becomes a **swift action**.

**At 7th Level:** This becomes a **move action**.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This is a polymorph effect and cannot be used while the druid is using another
polymorph effect, such as _wild shape._

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per druid level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

A serpent shaman may adopt an aspect of the snake while retaining her normal form. The druid may
select from the following bonuses:

- **Movement** (climb speed 20 feet, swim speed 20 feet)
- **Scales** (+2 natural armor bonus to AC)
- **Senses** ([low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent))
- **[Natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks)** (bite \[1d4\] and poison for a Medium druid, +2 CMB
  to grapple)

**Serpent Shaman Venom**

**Type:** Poison

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the druid's level + her Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

While using _totem transformation,_ the serpent shaman may speak normally and can cast
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) (reptiles only) at will.

**Totemic Summons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Standard:** A serpent shaman may cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) to
summon snakes, and these summoned creatures gain
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to her druid level. She can apply the
[young](ref/template/young) template to any snake to reduce the level of the summoning spell
required by one. She can also increase the level of summoning required by one in order to apply
either the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) or the [giant](ref/template/giant) template, or
increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant templates.

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A serpent shaman gains _wild shape,_ but the ability functions at her druid level - 2.
If she takes on the form of a snake, she instead uses her druid level + 2.

**Replaces:** Wild shape

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** A serpent shaman gains one of the following bonus feats:
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), [Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint),
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Bluff), [Stealthy](ref/feat/stealthy), or
[Strike Back](ref/feat/strike-back).

**At 13th Level:** The druid gains another feat from the list above.

**At 17th Level:** The druid gains a third bonus feat from the list above.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats.
