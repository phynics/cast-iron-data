A shaman with this totem calls upon the mighty ape, a peaceful but powerful simian whose strength is
beyond compare. An ape shaman is a friendly protector of the forest, but will crush those enemies
who rouse her anger.

**Nature Bond**

**Alters:** Nature bond

**Choice:** An ape shaman who chooses an animal companion must select an [ape](ref/companion/ape) or
related primate. If choosing a domain, the ape shaman must choose from the
[Animal](ref/domain/animal), Community (Family subdomain), [Destruction](ref/domain/destruction)
(Rage subdomain), and Strength domains.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** An ape shaman can use _wild empathy_ with apes and other primates as a **full-round
action** with a +4 bonus.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Totem Transformation (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 12th Level:** This becomes a **swift action**.

**At 7th Level:** This becomes a **move action**.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This is a polymorph effect and cannot be used while the druid is using another
polymorph effect, such as _wild shape._

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per druid level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

An ape shaman may adopt an aspect of the ape while retaining her normal form. The druid gains one of
the following sets of bonuses and abilities:

- **Movement** (climb speed 20 ft., +4 racial bonus on Climb checks)
- **Senses** ([low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent))
- **[Natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks)** (2 slams \[1d6 for a Medium shaman\], +2 on combat
  maneuver checks to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple))
- **Toughness** (+2 natural armor bonus to AC, [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) feat)

While using _totem transformation,_ the ape shaman may speak normally and can cast
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) (primates only) at will.

**Totemic Summons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** A thousand faces

**Standard:** An ape shaman may cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) to summon
primates, and these summoned creatures gain [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points)
equal to her druid level. She can apply the [young](ref/template/young) template to any primate to
reduce the level of the summoning spell required by one. She can also increase the level of
summoning required by one in order to apply either the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) or the
[giant](ref/template/giant) template, or increase it by two to apply both the advanced and giant
templates.

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Ability:** An ape shaman gains _wild shape,_ but the ability functions at her druid level - 2. If
she takes on the form of an ape, she instead uses her druid level + 2.

**Alters:** Wild shape

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** An ape shaman gains one of the following bonus feats: [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard),
[Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), [Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude),
[Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), and [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness).

**At 13th Level:** The ape shaman gains another bonus feat from the list above.

**At 17th Level:** The ape shaman gains a third bonus feat from the list above.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Venom immunity

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites for these bonus feats.
