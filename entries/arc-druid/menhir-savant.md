**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 38\
Some druids study the paths of nature's power through the nodes and ley lines that connect standing
stones and megalithic circles, learning to tap into their energies.

**Spirit Sense (Sp)**

**Ability:** A menhir savant can detect the presence of undead; fey; outsiders; and astral,
ethereal, or incorporeal creatures. This ability functions like
[detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead), and the druid detects all of these creatures rather than
trying to detect one kind.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Nature sense, wild empathy

**Place Magic (Su)**

**Flavor:** A menhir savant learns to identify and tap into ley lines in different types of terrain.

**Free:** She can tap into the magic of a nearby ley line and increase her caster level by +1 for 1
round.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride, trackless step

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Walk the Lines (Su)**

**Ability:** A menhir savant can use her connection to ley lines to cast
[transport via plants](ref/spell/transport-via-plants).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Usage:** Wis modifier times/day

**Empty Body (Su)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** A thousand faces

**Standard:** A menhir savant can become ethereal, as if using
[ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt).

**Usage:** 1 round/day per druid level
