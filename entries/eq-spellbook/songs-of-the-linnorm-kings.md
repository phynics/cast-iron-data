### Level 5 Skald

_This book's cover is made of scales that are warm to the touch._

**Sources:** Arcane Anthology pg 5

**Value** 180 gp (440 gp with the preparation ritual)

#### Spells

- 2nd-[marching chant](ref/spell/marching-chant), [path of glory](ref/spell/path-of-glory),
  [scare](ref/spell/scare)
- 1st-[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds),
  [heightened awareness](ref/spell/heightened-awareness), [remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear)
- 0 (at will)-_dancing lights, ghost sound, lullaby, mage hand, mending, resistance_

#### Ritual

**Storied Spell Lore (Su)** When you use this meditation ritual, choose a spell of 2nd level or
lower in this meditation book. Whenever you cast a bard or skald conjuration spell, you can spend
this boon as a swift action to add the chosen spell to your list of bard or skald spells known for 1
round.
