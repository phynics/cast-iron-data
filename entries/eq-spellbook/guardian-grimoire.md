### Level 15 Abjurer

_These three well-made books are covered with [runes of warding](ref/spell/rune-of-warding) against
outsiders and undead. The pages inside show water damage along the edges._

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 125

**Opposition** Illusion, necromancy\
**Protection** Explosive runes (Reflex DC 16) and fire trap (Reflex DC 17)\
**Value** 10,055 gp (13,055 gp with the preparation ritual)

#### Ritual

**Guardian Trick (Su)** With a quick phrase of power, you attempt to foil an attack. Spend this
boon's effect as an immediate action when an undead or outsider hits you with an attack. The
attacking creature must reroll the attack, taking the second roll even if it is lower.

A spell from the spellbook creator's specialty school.
