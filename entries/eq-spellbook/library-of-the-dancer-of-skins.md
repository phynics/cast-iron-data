### Level 19 Universalist

_This quartet of books is held together by a shining silver strand wound around the four tomes and
tied in an intricate knot. Inside, the writing is expansive and sloppy, filled with marginalia; the
author's notes indicate clear excitement about her discoveries, and while the thinker is obviously
brilliant, she is also apparently undisciplined._

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 126

**Value** 16,435 gp (20,710 gp with the preparation ritual)

#### Ritual

**Defensive Boon (Su)** You throw up a quick burst of arcane energy that deflects an incoming spell
attack. As an immediate action when targeted with a touch spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), you can spend this boon's effect to gain a +4
deflection bonus to AC against the triggering attack.
