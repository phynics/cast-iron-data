dozen tiny dials arranged in a semi-circle around the locking mechanism._

#### Ritual

**Enduring Alteration (Su)** Your spells resist reality's attempts to restore the things you change.
You can spend this boon to apply the benefits of the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) feat to a
transmutation spell as you cast it. This also increases the DC to dispel that spell (via
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ or similar abilities) by 4. This boon does not increase the
spell level or casting time of your spell.
