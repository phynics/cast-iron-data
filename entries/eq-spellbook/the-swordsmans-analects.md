### Level 3 Universalist

_This book's worn leather cover is emblazoned with a winged sword with an eye at its hilt._

**Sources:** Arcane Anthology pg 6

**Value** 250 gp (400 gp with the preparation ritual)

#### Spells

- 2nd-[force sword](ref/spell/force-sword), [human potential](ref/spell/human-potential)
- 1st-[expeditious construction](ref/spell/expeditious-construction),
  [identify](ref/spell/identify), [magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon),
  [mirror strike](ref/spell/mirror-strike), [protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil),
  [unerring weapon](ref/spell/unerring-weapon)

#### Ritual

**Shielding Weapon (Su)** Your spells infuse a weapon with protective magic. Spend this boon as an
immediate action whenever you cast an abjuration spell. When you do, a weapon you wield grants you a
shield bonus to your AC equal to 1 + 1/2 the spell's level for 1 round.
