#### Ritual

**Glorious Blade (Su)** When you attack with a sword (including sword-like effects created by spells
such as [spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon), as long as they require attack rolls), you
can expend a prepared spell or spell slot as a free action to spend this boon. If you do, you gain
an insight bonus on the attack roll equal to the level of the prepared spell or spell slot expended.
