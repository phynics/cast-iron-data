### Level 16 Illusionist

_Each volume of this three-book set is written in gold ink on jetblack paper. Illusory script hides
descriptions of the geography and denizens of the Shadow Plane._

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 125

**Opposition** Divination, evocation\
**Protection** illusory script (Will DC 16; on failure the suggestion is "Forget the existence of
the descriptions and notes"), phantom trap (opening any of the books seems to set off a trap)\
**Value** 11,865 gp (15,065 gp with the preparation ritual)

#### Ritual

**Shadow Knowledge (Su)** The knowledge in this book clings to your consciousness like the Shadow
Plane itself. Unlike other boons, you do not spend this one. Until you prepare spells again, you are
considered trained in all Knowledge skills as long as the check pertains to the geography and
denizens of the Shadow Plane. If you are already trained in the appropriate Knowledge skill, you
gain a +2 circumstance bonus on these checks instead.
