**Conjured Armor (Su)**

**Ability:** By spending 1 point from her phrenic pool, the psychic grants any creature she conjures
or summons with the linked spell a +2 deflection bonus to AC. This bonus lasts for 1 round per
caster level or until the creature disappears, whichever comes first. This amplification can be
linked only to conjuration (calling) or conjuration (summoning) spells.

At 8th level: The bonus +3.

At 15th level: The bonus +4.
