**Deflection Field (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever the psychic casts a linked spell that grants her a deflection bonus to AC, she
can spend 2 points from her phrenic pool to automatically reflect the first ranged attack that
misses her (as long as the linked spell remains active) back at the attacker. This reflected attack
uses the original attack roll plus a circumstance bonus equal to the psychic's caster level.
