**Will of the Dead (Su)**

**Ability:** The psychic can spend 2 points from her phrenic pool to overcome an undead creature's
immunity to mind-affecting effects for the purposes of the linked spell. This ability functions even
on mindless undead, but has no effect on creatures that aren't undead.

**Flavor:** Even undead creatures can be affected by the psychic's mind-affecting spells.

**Special:** This amplification can be linked only to spells that have the mind-affecting
descriptor.
