Even undead creatures can be affected by the psychic's mind-affecting spells. The psychic can spend
2 points from her phrenic pool to overcome an undead creature's immunity to mind-affecting effects
for the purposes of the linked spell. This ability functions even on mindless undead, but has no
effect on creatures that aren't undead. This amplification can be linked only to spells that have
the mind-affecting descriptor.
