The psychic can warp space with her mind, teleporting herself as she casts her linked spell. She
teleports herself 10 feet per point she spends from her phrenic pool (as
_[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_). The maximum number of points she can spend in this
way is equal to the linked spell's level.
