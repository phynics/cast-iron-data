**&L&Telempathic Restoration (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** When the psychic casts [telepathic bond](ref/spell/telepathic-bond), she can spend 1
point from her phrenic pool to remove the [dazed](ref/misc/dazed),
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened), [panicked](ref/misc/panicked), [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), or
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) condition from one target of the bond. Doing so removes the status from
the target of the linked spell, but applies it immediately (with any remaining duration) to the
psychic.
