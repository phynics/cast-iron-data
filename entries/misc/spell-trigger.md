## Spell Trigger

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 458\
Spell trigger activation is similar to [spell completion](ref/misc/spell-completion), but it's even
simpler. No gestures or spell finishing is needed, just a special knowledge of spellcasting that an
appropriate character would know, and a single word that must be spoken. Spell trigger items can be
used by anyone whose class can cast the corresponding spell. This is the case even for a character
who can't actually cast spells, such as a 3rd-level paladin. The user must still determine what
spell is stored in the item before she can activate it. Activating a spell trigger item is a
standard action and does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
