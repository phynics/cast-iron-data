**Lavatory**

**Benefit:** Bonus on Fortitude saves against contracting disease

**Create:** 3 Goods, 3 Labor (120 gp)

**Time:** 4 days

**Size:** 1-4 squares

**Upgrade from:** Shack

A Lavatory includes up to four 5-foot-by-5-foot private rooms for dealing with biological functions.
If a building doesn't have a Lavatory, people in it must go elsewhere for this sort of activity.
Depending on the building and settlement, a Lavatory might be an outhouse, a closet with a chamber
pot, or a stool connected to an external system such as a cesspit or pig trough. If the building has
a Sewer Access, you can automatically connect all Lavatories in the building to the settlement's
sewer system. The sanitation improvement from having a Lavatory mean residents, guests, employees,
and others who frequent the building gain a +2 bonus on Fortitude saves to resist contracting a
disease while in the settlement.
