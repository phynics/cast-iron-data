**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 106

**Robbers**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Influence +4

**Create:** 1 Goods, 4 Influence, 3 Labor (200 gp)

**Time:** 3 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade from:** Cutpurses

Robbers are expert thieves who commit burglary, robbery, and other violent crimes for profit. A
typical robber is a 3rd-level expert or rogue with 3 ranks each in Climb, Disable Device,
Intimidate, Perception, and Stealth.

> _This can also be upgraded into [Skirmishers](ref/rule/wilderness-teams)._
