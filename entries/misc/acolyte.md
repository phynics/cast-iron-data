**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 104

**Acolyte**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Magic +4

**Create:** 2 Goods, 2 Influence, 2 Labor, 3 Magic (440 gp)

**Time:** 3 days

**Size:** 1 person

**Upgrade to:** Priest, Sage

An Acolyte is a newly trained divine spellcaster, sufficient for tending to the sick or advising on
religious matters. An Acolyte is typically a 1st-level adept, cleric, druid, or oracle, with light
armor (or no armor if an adept) and a weapon appropriate to her faith.
