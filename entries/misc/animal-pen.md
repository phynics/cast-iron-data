**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 94

**Animal Pen**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Labor +8

**Create:** 6 Goods, 1 Influence, 5 Labor (250 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 4-16 squares

**Upgrade to:** Habitat

An Animal Pen houses animals that need more attention than horses and cattle. It could be used to
house animals for food (like chickens or pigs), display (like song birds or reptiles), or protection
(like dogs or large cats). One animal pen can support 1 Large, 2 Medium, 4 Small, or 8 Tiny or
smaller creatures, providing them with water and shelter. Food is not provided.
