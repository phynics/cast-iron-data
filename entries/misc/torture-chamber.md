**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 103

**Torture Chamber**

**Benefit:** Bonus on Intimidate checks

**Create:** 7 Goods, 3 Influence, 5 Labor (330 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 6-16 squares

This grim room is used for interrogation as well as torture and other morally questionable acts. It
contains structures to bind humanoids and various implements designed to inflict pain and
discomfort. Interrogators in this room gain a +3 bonus on Intimidate checks to influence captives.
