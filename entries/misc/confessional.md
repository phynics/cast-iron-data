**Confessional**

**Benefit:** Bonus on Stealth checks

**Create:** 2 Goods, 3 Labor (100 gp)

**Time:** 4 days

**Size:** 2-4 squares

This pair of tiny, linked rooms allows for private conversations. Alternatively, you may construct a
hidden space that allows you to watch another room without being observed, such as through a
peephole in a tapestry or mosaic. One side of this room provides a +4 bonus on Stealth checks to
hide from creatures in the adjoining room. A Confessional can be constructed in a way that allows
this bonus to apply to creatures in both sides or just in one.
