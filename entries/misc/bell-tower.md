**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 95

**Bell Tower**

**Earnings:** Capital +1 (of a type the building already generates)

**Create:** 11 Goods, 3 Influence, 7 Labor (450 gp)

**Time:** 28 days

**Size:** 9-25 squares

This two-story room contains one or more bells suitable for warnings or music, along with bell pulls
for operating the instruments from below. The bells can be heard up to 1 mile away.
