**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 102

**Sports Field**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +10

**Create:** 17 Goods, 3 Influence, 18 Labor (790 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 40-100 squares

**Upgrade from:** Battle Ring

This outdoor area is used for jousting, athletics, war games, and other sports. A Sports Field
contains a playing area, seats for spectators and equipment for one type of game.
