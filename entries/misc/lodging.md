**Lodging**

**Earnings:** gp +12

**Create:** 10 Goods, 1 Influence, 10 Labor (430 gp)

**Time:** 30 days

**Size:** 20-35 squares

**Upgrade from:** Bunks

This area is subdivided into smaller chambers and provides private housing and limited storage for
up to 10 people. Each chamber typically includes one or two small beds, linens, a chamber pot, and a
small table and chair. The door to the chamber is a simple wooden door with a simple lock. You may
upgrade individual locks by paying the price difference between a simple lock and the desired lock.
