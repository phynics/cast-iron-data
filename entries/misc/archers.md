**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 104

**Archers**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Labor +6

**Create:** 4 Goods, 3 Influence, 7 Labor (310 gp)

**Time:** 4 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade:** from Soldiers to Elite Archers

Archers are soldiers trained to use projectile weapons. They are typically 1st-level warriors, each
with scale mail, a longbow, a buckler, and a longsword.

> _This can also be upgraded into [Skirmishers](ref/rule/wilderness-teams)._
