**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 103

**Vault**

**Create:** 8 Goods, 7 Labor (300 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 4-8 squares

**Upgrade:** from Reliquary, Storage to Secret Room

This is a secure room designed to keep out intruders. The access to this space is controlled by an
iron door with a good lock. If you upgrade this room to a Secret Room, the door retains its material
and lock and also becomes a secret door.
