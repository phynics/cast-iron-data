**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 568\
A shaken character takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability
checks. Shaken is a less severe state of fear than [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) or
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked).
