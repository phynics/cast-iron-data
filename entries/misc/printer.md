**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 100

**Printer**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, Influence, or Labor +8

**Benefit:** Counts as [masterwork artisan's tools](ref/eq-misc/artisans-tools-masterwork) for
writing and printing skills

**Create:** 9 Goods, 2 Influence, 7 Labor (380 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 5-16 squares

**Upgrade from:** Scriptorium

This specialized workshop contains a printing press, storage for paper, and drying racks for
finished books and pamphlets. Up to three people can use the room at a time.
