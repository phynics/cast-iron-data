**Mill Room**

**Earnings:** gp or Goods +8

**Create:** 8 Goods, 7 Labor (300 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 20-30 squares

A Mill Room contains a mechanism to process grain, foodstuffs, and other raw materials. Most simple
mills are powered by hand, but those powered by horses or other beasts of burden require a stable,
those powered by water require running water for the water wheel, and those powered by wind require
a tower.
