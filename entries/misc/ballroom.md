**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 94

**Ballroom**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +10

**Benefit:** Bonus on Perform checks

**Create:** 19 Goods, 19 Labor (760 gp)

**Time:** 40 days

**Size:** 40-60 squares

**Upgrade:** from Common Room to Auditorium

This large open room is intended for dances, receptions, and other elaborate events. The superior
acoustics and decor grant a +2 bonus on all Perform checks made in this room.
