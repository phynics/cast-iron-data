**Bar**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +10

**Benefit:** Bonus on Diplomacy checks to gather information

**Create:** 6 Goods, 1 Influence, 5 Labor (250 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 10-20 squares

A Bar stores a selection of drinks and includes a counter for preparing them. After spending an hour
with local people in this room, for the next 24 hours you gain a +1 bonus on Diplomacy checks you
make to gather information in the settlement.
