**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 99

**Infirmary**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +8

**Benefit:** Counts as a [healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit) (see below)

**Create:** 6 Goods, 1 Influence, 6 Labor, 1 Magic (370 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 4-12 squares

An Infirmary is used for treating injured and sick people. It contains beds or cots, a wash basin,
and medical supplies. This counts as having a healer's kit for up to two healers at a time. As long
as the building doesn't have the broken condition, you don't need to track individual uses of these
healer's kits.
