**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 106

**Sailors**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Labor +2

**Create:** 1 Goods, 1 Influence, 2 Labor (90 gp)

**Time:** 0 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade from:** Laborers

Sailors know how to sail a ship, navigate while at sea, and defend the vessel against pirates and
other hostile boarders. A typical sailor is a [2nd-level expert](ref/npc/old-sailor) with 2 ranks
each in Acrobatics, Climb, Perception, Profession (sailor), Survival, and Swim.
