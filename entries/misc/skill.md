**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 12

A skill represents a creature's ability to perform an ordinary task, such as climb a wall, sneak
down a hallway, or spot an intruder. The number of ranks possessed by a
[creature](ref/misc/creature) in a given skill represents its proficiency in that skill. As a
creature gains [Hit Dice](ref/misc/hit-dice), it also gains additional skill ranks that can be added
to its skills.
