**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 102

**Statue**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +1

**Create:** 1 Goods, 2 Labor (60 gp)

**Time:** 2 days

**Size:** 1-9 squares

This area contains a statue, fountain, or other large decoration. If it has religious significance,
it might serve as a shrine. The listed cost and time are only to install a completed wood, bronze,
or stone feature - they don't include the cost and time to create the feature in the first place,
but it must be installed to produce Earnings.
