**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 98

**Greenhouse**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Influence +12

**Create:** 8 Goods, 7 Labor (300 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 10-20 squares

**Upgrade from:** Garden

This garden is protected by glass and has its interior climate deliberately regulated. Delicate or
exotic plants raised in this area grow larger and healthier than in a normal garden. Certain types
of rare plants can be raised only in a Greenhouse.

> _This room can upgrade into the Mystic Greenhouse in
> [Wilderness Rooms](ref/rule/wilderness-rooms)._
