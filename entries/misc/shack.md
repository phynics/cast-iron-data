**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 101

**Shack**

**Create:** 3 Goods, 2 Labor (100 gp)

**Time:** 3 days

**Size:** 2-4 squares

**Upgrade to:** Lavatory, Storage

This no-frills wooden shelter contains a simple table, pallet bed, and stool. One person can build a
shack with simple tools and basic materials. For an additional 1 point of Goods and 2 points of
Labor, you can construct a brick or stone hut instead of a wooden shack.
