**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 185

The **total defense** action lets you defend yourself as a
[standard action](ref/rule/standard-actions). You get a +4 dodge bonus to your AC for 1 round. Your
AC improves at the start of this action. You can't combine total defense with
[fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or with the benefit of the
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) feat. You can't make attacks of opportunity while
using total defense.
