**Elite Archers**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Labor +7

**Create:** 5 Goods, 4 Influence, 8 Labor (380 gp)

**Time:** 6 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade:** from Archers, Elite Soldiers to Cavalry Archers

An Elite Archers team consists of 3rd-level warriors, each with scale mail, a longbow, a buckler,
and a longsword.
