**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 568\
A staggered creature may take a single move action or standard action each round (but not both, nor
can he take full-round actions). A staggered creature can still take free, swift, and immediate
actions. A creature with nonlethal damage exactly equal to its current hit points gains the
staggered condition.
