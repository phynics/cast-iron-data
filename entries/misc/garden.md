**Garden**

**Earnings:** gp or Goods +8

**Create:** 5 Goods, 4 Labor (180 gp)

**Time:** 12 days

**Size:** 10-20 squares

**Upgrade to:** Greenhouse

This area of carefully tended soil is fit for growing plants that require greater attention than
crops, though you can use it to grow food crops if you choose.
