**Mage**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Magic +7

**Create:** 3 Goods, 2 Influence, 2 Labor, 8 Magic (960 gp)

**Time:** 7 days

**Size:** 1 person

**Upgrade from:** Apprentice

A Mage is a skilled but not especially remarkable arcane spellcaster, often a 3rd-level bard,
sorcerer, summoner, witch, or wizard.
