**Kitchen**

**Earnings:** gp or Goods +4

**Create:** 4 Goods, 4 Labor (160 gp)

**Time:** 12 days

**Size:** 2-6 squares

A Kitchen is used to prepare food. It contains a stove, sink, and small pantry with basic cooking
tools and supplies. A Kitchen for a business that serves food, such as an Inn, probably also has
Storage just for foodstuffs.
