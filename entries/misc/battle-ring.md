**Battle Ring**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +15

**Benefit:** Contestant gains a bonus on Intimidate and
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) checks

**Create:** 18 Goods, 4 Influence, 16 Labor (800 gp)

**Time:** 40 days

**Size:** 40-100 squares

**Upgrade to:** Sports Field

This enclosed field is used for some form of dangerous contest, from nonlethal sports like wrestling
or boxing to lethal blood sports such as animal fights or gladiatorial combat. It includes seating
for spectators, appropriate flooring (padded or sandy), and often some manner of barrier between the
audience and combatants. Each day, the person in charge of the Battle Ring can grant one combatant a
+2 bonus on Intimidate and performance combat checks. This benefit applies only within the
settlement.
