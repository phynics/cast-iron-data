**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 102

**Storage**

**Earnings:** gp +2

**Create:** 3 Goods, 3 Labor (120 gp)

**Time:** 8 days

**Size:** 4-8 squares

**Upgrade:** from False Front, Shack to Office, Vault

Storage is any room used to store objects, keeping them out of the way for later use. Most
Warehouses are just multiple Storage rooms built into a single building. A low-cost shop may allow
its customers to browse items in the Storage area. A door to a Storage room includes an average
lock.
