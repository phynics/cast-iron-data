**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 100

**Observation Dome**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Magic +5

**Benefit:** Bonus on Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (nature), and Knowledge (planes) checks

**Create:** 8 Goods, 9 Labor, 1 Magic (440 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 10-20 squares

This elevated room is open to the sky, has a skylight, or has a retractable roof to allow you to
observe the passing of celestial bodies. An Observation Dome includes shelves containing records and
notes, a telescope, and other devices dedicated to celestial study. If someone spends 1 hour
researching in the Observation Dome, she gains a +2 bonus on Knowledge (geography), Knowledge
(nature), and Knowledge (planes) checks to answer a question about the heavens.
