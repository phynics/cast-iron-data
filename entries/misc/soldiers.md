**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 106

**Soldiers**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Labor +5

**Create:** 3 Goods, 2 Influence, 5 Labor (220 gp)

**Time:** 2 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade:** from Guards to Archers, Bureaucrats, Cavalry, Elite Soldiers

Soldiers are trained in combat and have the means and will to kill your enemies. Unlike Guards,
Soldiers actively engage in fighting on behest of a leader (although you can make Soldiers act as
Guards). Depending on the nature of your organization, they might be enforcers rather than
military-style soldiers. They are typically 1st-level warriors, each with scale mail, a longsword, a
heavy wooden shield, and javelins.

> _This can also be upgraded into [Skirmishers](ref/rule/wilderness-teams)._
