**Stall**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Labor +8

**Create:** 6 Goods, 1 Influence, 5 Labor (250 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 6-16 squares

**Upgrade to:** Habitat

A Stall is a place to keep 1-2 horses or other Large domestic animals. It contains gates, feed
troughs, feed, and straw.
