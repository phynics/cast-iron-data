**Labyrinth**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +5

**Create:** 15 Goods, 15 Labor (370 gp)

**Time:** 30 days

**Size:** 40-100 squares

A Labyrinth is a walled maze, hedge maze, or simple tiled pattern on the ground that those in need
of tranquility can walk for quiet meditation.
