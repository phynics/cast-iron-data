**Elite Guards**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Labor +4

**Create:** 3 Goods, 1 Influence, 4 Labor (170 gp)

**Time:** 2 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade:** from Guards to Elite Soldiers

Like Guards, this team defends a location but doesn't take on an active role. An Elite Guards team
consists of 3rd-level warriors, each wearing a chain shirt or banded mail and using a heavy steel
shield and shortspear, a guisarme, or a halberd.
