**Clockwork Shop**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Labor +10

**Benefit:** Counts as [masterwork artisan's tools](ref/eq-misc/artisans-tools-masterwork) for Craft
(clockwork)

**Create:** 9 Goods, 9 Labor (360 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 8-16 squares

This workshop provides all of the precision tools and workstations required for creating delicate
clockwork goods. Up to three people can use the room at a time.
