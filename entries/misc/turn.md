In a [round](ref/misc/round), a [creature](ref/misc/creature) receives one turn, during which it can
perform a wide variety of [actions](ref/misc/action). Generally in the course of one turn, a
character can perform one standard action, one move action, one swift action, and a number of free
actions. Less-common combinations of actions are permissible as well, see
[Actions in Combat](ref/rule/actions-in-combat) for more details.
