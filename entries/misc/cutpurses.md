**Cutpurses**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Labor +3

**Create:** 3 Influence, 1 Labor (110 gp)

**Time:** 1 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade to:** Robbers

Cutpurses are minor thieves (often youths) who earn money from petty crimes such as pickpocketing or
stealing items from market stalls. They are typically 1st-level commoners, experts, or rogues with 1
rank in Perception, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth.
