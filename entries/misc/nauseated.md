**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 568\
Creatures with the nauseated condition experience stomach distress. Nauseated creatures are unable
to attack, cast spells, concentrate on spells, or do anything else requiring attention. The only
action such a character can take is a single move action per turn.
