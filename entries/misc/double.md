Some weapons have special features in addition to those noted in their descriptions.

**Double:** You can use a double weapon to fight as if fighting with
[two weapons](ref/rule/two-weapon-fighting), but if you do, you incur all the normal attack
penalties associated with fighting with two weapons, just as if you were using a one-handed weapon
and a light weapon. You can choose to wield one end of a double weapon two-handed, but it cannot be
used as a double weapon when wielded in this way - only one end of the weapon can be used in any
given round.
