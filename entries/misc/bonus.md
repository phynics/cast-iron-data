**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 11

Bonuses are numerical values that are added to [checks](ref/misc/check) and statistical scores. Most
bonuses have a type, and as a general rule, bonuses of the same type are not cumulative (do not
["stack"](ref/misc/stacking)) - only the greater bonus granted applies.
