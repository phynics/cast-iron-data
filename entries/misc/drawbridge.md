**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 97

**Drawbridge**

**Create:** 8 Goods, 2 Influence, 5 Labor (320 gp)

**Time:** 6 days

**Size:** 4-8 squares

This retractable bridge spans a pit, moat, or similar danger, allowing you to control access to an
area. You can raise or lower the bridge by spending a full-round action to operate the mechanisms
constructed on either side of the span. When raised, the bridge creates a wooden barrier
([hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 5, 40 hit points). If the Drawbridge is destroyed, it can be rebuilt
in the same place for half the initial construction price.
