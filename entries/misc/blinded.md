**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 565\
The creature cannot see. It takes a -2 penalty to Armor Class, loses its Dexterity bonus to AC (if
any), and takes a -4 penalty on most Strength- and Dexterity-based skill checks and on opposed
Perception skill checks. All checks and activities that rely on vision (such as reading and
Perception checks based on sight) automatically fail. All opponents are considered to have total
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (50% miss chance) against the blinded character. Blind creatures
must make a DC 10 Acrobatics skill check to move faster than half speed. Creatures that fail this
check fall prone. Characters who remain blinded for a long time grow accustomed to these drawbacks
and can overcome some of them. Also see: [Darkness](ref/rule/darkness).
