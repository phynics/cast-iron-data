**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 11

Alignment represents a creature's basic moral and ethical attitude. Alignment has two components:
one describing whether a [creature](ref/misc/creature) is lawful, neutral, or chaotic, followed by
another that describes whether a character is good, neutral, or evil. Alignments are usually
abbreviated using the first letter of each alignment component, such as LN for lawful neutral or CE
for chaotic evil. Creatures that are neutral in both components are denoted by a single "N."
