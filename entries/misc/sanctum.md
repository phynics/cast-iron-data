**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 101

**Sanctum**

**Benefit:** Bonus on one Will save

**Create:** 2 Goods, 1 Influence, 1 Labor, 1 Magic (190 gp)

**Time:** 6 days

**Size:** 1-4 squares

This is a basic room with simple and pleasing decorations, clean lines, and a calming environment
perfect for meditation, prayer, and solitude. A person who spends at least 4 hours in a Sanctum
doing nothing other than praying or meditating gains a +1 bonus on Will saves. This bonus ends once
the person leaves the settlement or after the first time she attempts a Will save.
