**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 95

**Brewery**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +10

**Create:** 9 Goods, 2 Influence, 7 Labor (380 gp)

**Time:** 24 days

**Size:** 12-24 squares

A Brewery allows you to ferment or distill ingredients such as fruits and grain to create potent
beverages.
