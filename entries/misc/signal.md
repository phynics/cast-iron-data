**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 43\
Some items, like [chipfinders](ref/tech-misc/chipfinder-brown), [commsets](ref/tech-misc/commset),
and [detonators](ref/tech-misc/detonator), can remotely interact with other objects via **signals**.
Signals have a maximum range set by the device. Furthermore, a signal cannot penetrate solid
barriers well. A signal is blocked by 1 foot of metal, 5 feet of stone, or 20 feet of organic
matter. [Force fields](ref/tech-misc/force-field-brown) do not block signals at all.
[Signal boosters](ref/tech-misc/signal-booster) and [signal jammers](ref/tech-misc/signal-jammer)
can also affect signal range.
