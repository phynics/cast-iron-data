Many times, the rules will instruct you to increase or decrease the damage die of an attack. Below
are the three main sources that list damage dice by steps.

**Sources:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary pg 315

Damage dice increase as follows: 1d2, 1d3, 1d4, 1d6, 1d8, 2d6, 3d6, 4d6, 6d6, 8d6, 12d6.

A weapon or attack that deals 1d10 points of damage increases as follows: 1d10, 2d8, 3d8, 4d8, 6d8,
8d8, 12d8.

> _— From [Improved Natural Attack](ref/feat/improved-natural-attack)_

**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 296
