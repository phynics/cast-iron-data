**Pit**

**Earnings:** gp or Labor +1

**Create:** 1 Goods, 1 Labor (40 gp)

**Time:** 2 days

**Size:** 1-5 squares

This is a place to dump things no longer needed. It can be used to contain refuse, dangerous waste,
and piles of junk, or as a mass graveyard, communal latrine, or crude surface well. A typical pit is
5-15 feet deep with steep sides.
