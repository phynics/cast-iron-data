**Dock**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, Influence, or Labor +12

**Create:** 7 Goods, 2 Influence, 6 Labor (320 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 10-30 squares

This is a series of walkways and sturdy posts used to safely moor a water vessel such as a boat or
ship. If attached to Storage, it allows you to easily move cargo to and from the water.
