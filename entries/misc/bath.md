**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 95

**Bath**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +3

**Benefit:** Bonus on Fortitude saves against disease

**Create:** 3 Goods, 1 Influence, 2 Labor (130 gp)

**Time:** 8 days

**Size:** 3-6 squares

**Upgrade from:** Sauna

A Bath contains a single large bathtub or multiple smaller basins, along with a stove for heating
water. After spending 1 hour in this room, you gain a +2 bonus on your next ongoing Fortitude save
against disease.
