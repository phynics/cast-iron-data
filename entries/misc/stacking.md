Stacking refers to the act of adding together bonuses or penalties that apply to one particular
check or statistic. Generally speaking, most [bonuses](ref/misc/bonus) of the same type do not
stack. Instead, only the highest bonus applies. Most [penalties](ref/misc/penalty) do stack, meaning
that their values are added together. Penalties and bonuses generally stack with one another,
meaning that the penalties might negate or exceed part or all of the bonuses, and vice versa.
