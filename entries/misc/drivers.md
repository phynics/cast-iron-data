**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 105

**Drivers**

**Earnings:** gp, Goods, or Labor +2

**Create:** 2 Goods, 1 Influence, 1 Labor (90 gp)

**Time:** 0 days

**Size:** 1 person

**Upgrade:** from Laborers to Lackeys

Drivers are trained to handle and move common animals' managing ox-drawn carts, herding cattle, or
training riding horses. A Driver is typically 1st-level expert with ranks in Handle Animal,
Knowledge (geography), Profession (driver), Ride, and Survival.
