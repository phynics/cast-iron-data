**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 105

**Elite Soldiers**

**Earnings:** gp, Influence, or Labor +6

**Create:** 5 Goods, 3 Influence, 7 Labor (330 gp)

**Time:** 5 days

**Size:** 5 people

**Upgrade:** from Elite Guards, Soldiers to Cavalry, Elite Archers

Like Soldiers, this team will take aggressive action and seek out your enemies to kill them. An
Elite Soldiers team consists of 3rd-level warriors, each wearing a chain shirt or banded mail and
using a guisarme, a halberd, or a heavy steel shield and a shortspear.
