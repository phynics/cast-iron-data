**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 103

**Tollbooth**

**Earnings:** gp or Goods +4

**Create:** 2 Goods, 1 Influence, 2 Labor (110 gp)

**Time:** 6 days

**Size:** 1-5 squares

**Upgrade to:** Guard Post

A Tollbooth is a small shelter designed to restrict movement on a road or bridge so the owner can
collect fees from travelers. If this room is built near a settlement, it requires 1 point of
Influence per day to maintain - or might be illegal, depending on the settlement.
