The distinction between "minor", "medium" and "major" magic items is blurry, and never outright
stated in any core book. But there are inferences we can make from the tables given in these books.
In the [Magic Items](ref/rule/magic-items) section of the _PRPG Core Rulebook,_ the tables overlap
heavily, making it possible to roll a "minor" item that's worth much more than a "medium" item; for
example, it's possible to roll for a minor [Magic Armor](ref/rule/magic-armor) and end up with an
_improved slick, improved shadow elven chain_ worth 35,150 gp! _Ultimate Equipment_ was published
later and included a [Treasure Generator](ref/rule/treasure-generator) that ironed out most of these
outliers, and it is what informs most of the information below.

## Minor Items

- **Armor and shields:** No more than a +2 bonus (including +1 special abilities), maxing out at
  about 5,000 gp.
- **Weapons:** No more than a +2 bonus (including +1 special abilities), maxing out at about 5,000
  gp.
- **Potions:** Spell level 1 or 2, caster level 1.
- **Rings:** No more than 7,000 gp.
- **Rods:** Rods are **never** minor.
- **Scrolls:** Spell level 1-3, caster level 1-5.
- **Staves:** **No** staff is consider minor.
- **Wands:** Spell level 0-2, caster level 1-3.
- **Wondrous Items:** No more than 7,900 gp; includes items that give up to a +2 stat or armor
  bonus, pearls of power up to 2nd level, most bags of holding, and necklaces of fireballs up to
  Type V.

## Medium Items

- **Armor and shields:** No more than a +4 bonus (including +1 or +2 special abilities), maxing out
  at about 16,000 gp.
- **Weapons:** No more than a +4 bonus (including +1 or +2 special abilities), maxing out at about
  23,000 gp.
- **Potions:** Spell level 1-3, caster level 1-3.
- **Rings:** No more than 27,000 gp.
- **Rods:** No more than 19,000 gp, including _merciful_ metamagic rods, lesser metamagic rods up to
  +3, and normal metmagic rods up to +2.
- **Scrolls:** Spell level 2-5, caster level 3-9.
- **Staves:** No more than 28,000 gp.
- **Wands:** Spell level 2 or 3, caster level 3-5.
- **Wondrous Items:** Between 8,000 gp and 27,500 gp; includes items that give a stat or armor bonus
  of +3, +4 or +5, items that give a +2 or +3 natural armor bonus, +1 manuals, brooms and small
  carpets of flying, type IV bags of holding, portable holes, pearls of power up to 5th level, and
  most ioun stones.

## Major Items

- **Armor and shields:** At least a +3 bonus, minimum 9,000 gp.
- **Weapons:** At least a +3 bonus, minimum 8,000 gp.
- **Potions:** Spell level 2 or 3, caster level 3-5.
- **Rings:** Minimum 28,000 gp.
- **Rods:** Minimum 20,000 gp, including all greater metamagic rods, normal metamagic rods +2 and
  up, and lesser rods +4 and up.
- **Scrolls:** Spell level 4 or more, caster level at least 7th.
- **Staves:** Minimum 28,000 gp.
- **Wands:** Spell level 3 or 4, caster level 5-7.
- **Wondrous Items:** Minimum 28,000 gp; includes items that give +4 and higher natural armor
  bonuses, items that give +6 and higher stat or armor bonuses, pearls of power 6th level and up,
  manuals of +2 and higher, all crystal balls, large flying carpets, and golem manuals.
