**Farmland**

**Earnings:** gp or Goods +10

**Create:** 15 Goods, 15 Labor (600 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 60-100 squares

This large swath of fertile land is used for farming or fodder for livestock. The price for this
room includes clearing the land, fertilizing the soil, and so on. At the GM's discretion, you might
discover a plot of available land that automatically counts as a Farmland at no cost.
