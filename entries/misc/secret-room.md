**Secret Room**

**Create:** 5 Goods, 6 Labor (220 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 6-10 squares

**Upgrade:** from Vault to Escape Route

This is either a room or a passage connecting two rooms in the building. The access to this space is
controlled by a [secret door](ref/rule/doors) (DC 20). A passage can have secret doors at both ends
or a normal door at one end and a secret door at the other. If it's a room, it is typically used to
hide someone or something you don't want discovered. If it's a passage, it's typically used for
clandestine travel within the building, often for the purpose of smuggling or spying. For every 500
extra gp you spend, you can improve one secret door in the building to a well-hidden secret door (DC
30).
