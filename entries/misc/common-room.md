**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 96

**Common Room**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +7

**Create:** 7 Goods, 8 Labor (300 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 10-30 squares

**Upgrade to:** Ballroom, Ceremonial Room, Throne Room

This versatile open area has enough space for many people to use at once. A Common Room is typically
furnished with benches, chairs, cushions, mats, pews, or stools, and might have tables.
