**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 12

Monsters are [creatures](ref/misc/creature) that rely on racial [Hit Dice](ref/misc/hit-dice)
instead of class [levels](ref/misc/level) for their powers and abilities (although some possess
class levels as well). PCs are usually not monsters.
