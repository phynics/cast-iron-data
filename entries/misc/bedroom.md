**Bedroom**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +3

**Create:** 8 Goods, 7 Labor (300 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 4-8 squares

A Bedroom provides comfort and privacy for one to two people, and typically features one large bed
or two smaller beds. Many also have furnishings or features, such as chairs, wardrobes, chests,
tables, or small fireplaces. A Bedroom might be the sleeping place of a building's owner or a
comfortable room for rent.
