**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 20

Some weapons have special features in addition to those noted in their descriptions.

**Semi-Automatic:** A semi-automatic weapon normally fires one shot as an attack. However, the user
can take a full-attack action to fire twice, as if using the [Rapid Shot](ref/feat/rapid-shot) feat
(including taking a -2 penalty on all attacks). If the wielder has the Rapid Shot feat, she can use
the additional shot from that as well, but the penalty for all shots fired in that round increases
to -6.
