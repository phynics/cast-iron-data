**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 106

**Sage**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +5

**Create:** 5 Goods, 2 Influence, 2 Labor (200 gp)

**Time:** 4 days

**Size:** 1 person

**Upgrade from:** Acolyte, Apprentice

A sage is a person with extensive knowledge in one or more related areas who can advise you about
his areas of expertise. A typical sage is a 3rd-level expert with 3 ranks each in skills such as
Appraise, Knowledge, Linguistics, Profession, and Spellcraft.
