**Nursery**

**Earnings:** gp or Influence +6

**Create:** 6 Goods, 1 Influence, 5 Labor (250 gp)

**Time:** 14 days

**Size:** 8-16 squares

A Nursery is used to care for infants and children. It contains cribs and beds for children, toys
for their entertainment, a table for changing, and cabinets for supplies.
