**Office**

**Create:** 3 Goods, 3 Labor (120 gp)

**Time:** 8 days

**Size:** 2-5 squares

**Upgrade from:** Storage

This simple room includes a door with a simple lock, a chair, and a large desk that has two drawers
with simple locks. An Office affords its user privacy and a refuge from other activity in the
building.
