**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 11

Each [creature](ref/misc/creature) has six ability scores: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution,
Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. These scores represent a creature's most basic attributes. The
higher the score, the more raw potential and talent your character possesses.
