**Forge**

**Earnings:** gp or Goods +10

**Benefit:** Counts as [masterwork artisan's tools](ref/eq-misc/artisans-tools-masterwork) for
smithing skills

**Create:** 9 Goods, 1 Influence, 8 Labor (370 gp)

**Time:** 20 days

**Size:** 8-16 squares

A Forge includes a hearth, an anvil, a slack tub, metalworking tools, and other appropriate
materials for shaping iron and other metals. A Forge counts as artisan's tools for up to three
people working on metalworking skills such as Craft (armor) and Craft (weapons).
