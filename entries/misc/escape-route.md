**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 97

**Escape Route**

**Create:** 9 Goods, 9 Labor (360 gp)

**Time:** 16 days

**Size:** 6-12 squares

**Upgrade from:** Secret Room

This is a hallway or tunnel leading to a hidden exit from the building. The exit door is typically a
simple wooden door with an average lock (Disable Device DC 20 to open). Either or both ends of the
hallway can be secret doors (Perception DC 20 to notice).
