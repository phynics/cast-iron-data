## Body Piercings

**Aura** faint transmutation CL 3rd\
**Slot** body; **Price** 1,800 gp (Minor), 2,400 gp (Major), 3,200 gp (Greater); **Weight** —

These piercings come in the form of grotesque spikes and needles that cover every inch of the
wearer's skin.

**Minor:** The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus to CMD against [grappling](ref/rule/grapple)
attempts.

**Major:** Any creature attacking the wearer with a natural or unarmed attack must succeed at a DC
15 Reflex save or take 1d4 points of damage.

**Greater:** The wearer is considered to have +1 [armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes) when
not wearing armor or bulky clothing, and is considered proficient with armor spikes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Shadow Piercing](ref/feat/craft-shadow-piercing),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple), [magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon); **Cost** 900
gp (Minor), 1,200 gp (Major), 1,600 gp (Greater)
