## Chest Piercings

**Sources:** Champions of Corruption pg 28

**Aura** faint transmutation CL 3rd\
**Slot** body; **Price** 2,200 gp (Minor), 3,750 gp (Major), 5,750 gp (Greater); **Weight** —

These excruciating body ornaments come in the form of sharp barbs and serrated rings inserted in the
nipples, abdomen, or along the ribs. The pain they cause sharpens the mind and blocks out
distractions.

**Minor:** The wearer gains a +2 profane bonus on saves against effects that cause the
[dazed](ref/misc/dazed), [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated), and [sickened](ref/misc/sickened)
conditions.

**Major:** The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks.

**Greater:** The wearer gains a +4 profane bonus on saving throws against effects that deal
[ability damage or drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Shadow Piercing](ref/feat/craft-shadow-piercing),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance) (minor and greater),
[fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning) (major); **Cost** 1,100 gp (Minor), 1,875 gp (Major), 2,875
gp (Greater)
