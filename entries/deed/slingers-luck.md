**Slinger's Luck (Ex)**

**Ability:** The gunslinger can spend grit to reroll a saving throw or a skill check. It costs 2
grit points to reroll a saving throw, and 1 grit point to reroll a skill check. The gunslinger must
take the result of the second roll, even if it is lower.

**Prereq:** 15th-level gunslinger

**Special:** The deed's cost cannot be reduced by the _true grit_ class ability, the
[Signature Deed](ref/feat/signature-deed) feat, or any other effect that reduces the amount of grit
a deed costs.
