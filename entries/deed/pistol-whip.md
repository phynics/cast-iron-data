**&L&Pistol-Whip (Ex)&FN&**

**Prereq:** 3rd-level gunslinger

**Special:** If the attack hits, the gunslinger can make a
[combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) check to knock the target [prone](ref/rule/prone) as a
**free action**. Performing this deed costs 1 grit point.

**Standard:** The gunslinger can make a surprise melee attack with the butt or handle of her
firearm. When she does, she is considered to be proficient with the firearm as a melee weapon and
gains a bonus on the attack and damage rolls equal to the enhancement bonus of the firearm.

The damage dealt by the pistol-whip is of the bludgeoning type, and is determined by the size of the
firearm. One-handed firearms deal 1d6 points of damage (1d4 if wielded by Small creatures) and
two-handed firearms deal 1d10 points of damage (1d8 if wielded by Small creatures). Regardless of
the gunslinger's size, the critical multiplier of this attack is 20/&times&2.
