**Deadeye (Ex)**

**Ability:** The gunslinger can resolve an attack against touch AC instead of normal AC when firing
beyond her firearm's first range increment. Performing this deed costs 1 grit point per range
increment beyond the first. The gunslinger still takes the -2 penalty on attack rolls for each range
increment beyond the first when she performs this deed.

**Prereq:** 1st-level gunslinger
