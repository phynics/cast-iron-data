**Cheat Death (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever the gunslinger is reduced to 0 or fewer hit points, she can spend all of her
remaining grit points (minimum 1) to instead be reduced to 1 hit point.

**Prereq:** 19th-level gunslinger
