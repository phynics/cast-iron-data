**&L&Lightning Reload (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** As long as the gunslinger has at least 1 grit point, she can reload a single barrel of
a one-handed or two-handed firearm as a **swift action** once per round. If she has the
[Rapid Reload](ref/feat/rapid-reload) feat or is using an alchemical cartridge (or both), she can
reload a single barrel of the weapon as a **free action** each round instead.

**Prereq:** 11th-level gunslinger

**Special:** Using this deed does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
