**Quick Clear (Ex)**

**Prereq:** 1st-level gunslinger

**Special:** Alternatively, if the gunslinger spends 1 grit point to perform this deed, she can
perform quick clear as a **move-equivalent action** instead of a standard action.

**Standard:** The gunslinger can remove the broken condition from a single firearm she is currently
wielding, as long as that condition was gained by a firearm misfire. The gunslinger must have at
least 1 grit point to perform this deed.
