## Hotchkiss 6-Pounder

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#71: Rasputin Must Die! pg 64

The Hotchkiss 6 pounder gun is a shortened version of the Hotchkiss 6 pounder naval gun, designed
for use in the sponsons of World War I-era tanks such as the Mark IV. The Hotchkiss 6 pounder is a
direct-fire advanced siege firearm that must be mounted in place on a vehicle to be used. It fires
high explosive (HE) shells that deal 8d6 points of bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage in a
30-foot radius around the point of impact (Reflex DC 20 for half). A Hotchkiss 6 pounder is a Medium
weapon and has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10 and 70 hit points. Hotchkiss HE shells cost 25 gp
each and weigh 6 pounds each.

**Cost** 2,000 gp **Weight** Special\
**Damage** 8d6; **Critical** ×3; **Range** 300 ft.; **Type** B, P, and S\
**Misfire** 1-2; **Crew** 2; **Aim** 0; **Load** 2\
**Category** Siege; **Proficiency** Exotic
