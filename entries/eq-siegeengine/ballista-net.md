**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 27

This ballista bolt is specially equipped with a net which unfurls as the bolt flies toward its
target. Unlike a standard ballista bolt, a ballista net requires only a touch attack to hit. If a
ballista net hits, it deals no damage, but causes the target to become
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled). An entangled creature takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls and a -4
penalty to Dexterity, can move at only half speed, and cannot charge or run.

**Cost** 75 gp **Weight** 15 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition
