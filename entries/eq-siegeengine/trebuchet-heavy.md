## Trebuchet

Trebuchets are similar in form to catapults, with the payload placed into a basket, cup, or sling at
the end of a long lever, and a counterweight (often with crew or animals pulling attached ropes)
close to the fulcrum. The leverage imparted by a trebuchet allows it to hurl massive missiles that
scatter to a number of squares around the target square based on the size of the trebuchet.
Trebuchets are too bulky to move on wheels or vehicles and must be assembled on the battlefield.
Trebuchets have a minimum range of 150 feet.

**Light:** These Large siege engines typically hurl large stones indirectly at a target (targeting
DC 15). A light trebuchet's ammunition scatters on contact, dealing full damage to the target square
and half its damage to creatures and objects within 5 feet of the target square (creatures can make
a DC 15 Reflex save to halve the damage again). Light trebuchets have a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
of 5 and 50 hit points. One use of light trebuchet stones costs 15 gp and weighs 60 pounds.\
