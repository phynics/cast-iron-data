This barbed ballista bolt is equipped with a heavy weight at the end of a long chain. The bolt's
extra weight makes the bolt harder to fire accurately, and imposes a -2 penalty on attack rolls. In
addition to the normal damage dealt by a ballista bolt of its size, on a successful hit the weighted
bolt lodges itself into the target's body, leaving the weighted chain hanging loosely, imparting a
-5 penalty on all Fly checks made by the target. These penalties stack if a target is hit with
multiple weighted bolts. A weighted bolt can be removed with a successful DC 25 Heal check or a DC
20 Strength check.

**Cost** 50 gp **Weight** 40 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition
