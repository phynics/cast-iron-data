**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 162

Cannons are crafted of metal - some are cast in one piece, others welded with iron bands - and
mounted either in the ground or on wooden frames. Cannons use black powder to propel their
projectiles with great force. A cannon has a misfire range of 1 (20 feet). Cannons have a
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 10 and 70 hit points. Cannon balls cost 30 gp each and weigh 25
pounds.

**Cost** 6,000 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 6d6; **Critical** ×4; **Range** 100 ft.; **Type** B and P\
**Crew** 2; **Aim** 1; **Load** 3; **Speed** 10 ft.\
**Category** Direct-Fire (Large); **Proficiency** Siege Engine
