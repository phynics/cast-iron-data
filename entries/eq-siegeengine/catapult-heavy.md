## Catapult

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 162, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 435

Catapults are stone-throwing siege engines powered by winched arms that run through torsion skeins,
and hold their payload in a cup that swings up and over the weapon when released. Catapults can hurl
a variety of different types of ammunition (the damage given is for stone projectiles; other types
of ammunition may deal different damage). They are indirect-fire siege engines.

**Light:** These catapults are Large and often mounted on wheels. The targeting DC of a light
catapult is 15. Light catapults have a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 5 and 50 hit points. Light
catapult stones cost 10 gp and weigh 50 pounds each.\
**Cost** 550 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 4d6; **Critical** ×2; **Range** 150 ft. (50 ft. min.); **Type** B\
**Crew** 2; **Aim** 2; **Load** 3; **Speed** 10 ft.\
**Category** Indirect-Fire (Large); **Proficiency** Siege Engine

**Standard:** These Huge siege engines are too large to be transported to the battlefield in one
piece, and require assembly. They typically hurl large stones indirectly at a target (targeting DC
20). Standard catapults have a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 5 and 100 hit points. Standard
catapult stones cost 15 gp and weigh 75 pounds each.\
**Cost** 800 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 6d6; **Critical** ×2; **Range** 200 ft. (100 ft. min.); **Type** B\
**Crew** 3; **Aim** 2; **Load** 3; **Speed** 0 ft.\
**Category** Indirect-Fire (Huge); **Proficiency** Siege Engine

**Heavy:** These Gargantuan siege engines are too large to be transported to the battlefield in one
piece, and require assembly. They typically hurl large stones indirectly at a target (targeting DC
25). Heavy catapults have a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 5 and 200 hit points. Heavy catapult
stones cost 25 gp and weigh 90 pounds each.\
**Cost** 1,000 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 8d6; **Critical** ×2; **Range** 300 ft. (100 ft. min.); **Type** B\
**Crew** 4; **Aim** 3; **Load** 3; **Speed** 0 ft.\
**Category** Indirect-Fire (Gargantuan); **Proficiency** Siege Engine
