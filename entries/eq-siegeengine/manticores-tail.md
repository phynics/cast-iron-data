**Sources:** Belkzen, Hold of the Orc Hordes pg 39

This massive ballista fires a dozen or more bolts at once in a devastating rain of steel that deals
3d8 points of damage. The crew leader makes a separate attack roll with a -2 penalty against each
creature and object within a 60-foot cone, ignoring [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). Confirm any
critical threats separately. This weapon has a mishap only if the first attack roll is a mishap. A
manticore's tail has a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 5 and 100 hit points. Ammunition for one use
of the manticore's tail costs 120 gp and weighs 120 pounds.
