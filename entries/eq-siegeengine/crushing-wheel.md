**Sources:** Belkzen, Hold of the Orc Hordes pg 39

The bulk of this weapon is a steel roller nearly 10 feet wide. Handles or a harness project from the
axle, allowing four orcs or one Large beast to push the crushing wheel from behind. As a full-round
action, its crew can roll the crushing wheel over creatures in front of it. This functions as the
[trample](ref/umr/trample) monster ability except as noted here. Creatures that are overrun fall
[prone](ref/rule/prone) and take 4d6 points of damage. Targets that succeed at a DC 15 Reflex saving
throw take only half damage and avoid falling prone. A crushing wheel has a
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 10 and 100 hit points.

A crushing wheel is a Large siege engine.
