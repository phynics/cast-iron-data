This siege engine firearm ammunition can be loaded in a cannon or a fiend's mouth cannon. It is
especially good at tearing through sails and dirigibles (see the
[Vehicle rules](ref/rule/full-vehicle-rules)), dealing double its normal damage to those forms of
propulsion. When fired at a creature, on a hit, the creature must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude
saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Cost** 50 gp **Weight** 30 lbs\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Siege Engine
