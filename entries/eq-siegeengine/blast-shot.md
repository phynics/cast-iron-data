Instead of a single hard ball, this ammunition is a bundle of large pellets, balls, or pieces of
scrap metal, propelled a short distance by black powder and attacking all creatures and objects
within an area. Both cannons and fiend's mouth cannons can fire this kind of ammunition. When such a
siege engine fires this ammunition, it hits every creature and object within a 30-foot-cone
[burst](ref/misc/burst). The siege engine makes attack rolls against each creature and unattended
object in the burst. It must miss every creature or target to misfire, and a misfire generates the
normal effect. It deals its normal damage on a hit, but does not ignore the
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of objects.

**Cost** 30 gp **Weight** 25 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Siege Engine
