**Sources:** Belkzen, Hold of the Orc Hordes pg 39

Usable in catapults and trebuchets, this ammunition consists of a wooden crate packed with wax
globes, from which a faint buzzing sound emanates. Visible as a dark shape at the center of each
globe is a rat-sized hornet known as a rockwasp. The alchemical wax keeps the wasps immobilized,
while tiny air holes allow them to breath. This ammunition deals only half damage, but releases a
[wasp swarm](ref/monster/wasp-wasp-swarm) - two wasp swarms if used for a heavy catapult or heavy
trebuchet. The wasps attack for 1 minute before dispersing. On a siege engine mishap, the wasps
escape before the ammunition is launched.

**Cost** 2,000 gp **Weight** 100 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Siege Engine
