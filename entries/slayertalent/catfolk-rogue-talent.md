**Catfolk Rogue Talent**

**Ability:** A catfolk slayer can select one of the following catfolk rogue talents in place of a
slayer talent: [deadly scratch](ref/talent/deadly-scratch),
[graceful faller](ref/talent/graceful-faller), [nimble climber](ref/talent/nimble-climber), or
[vicious claws](ref/talent/vicious-claws). Any talent effects based on rogue level use the slayer's
class level. The slayer must fulfill any prerequisites.

**Prereq:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk)
