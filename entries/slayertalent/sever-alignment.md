**Sever Alignment (Su)**

**Ability:** When making a sneak attack against an opponent with an alignment subtype, a slayer with
this talent can forgo her sneak attack damage to scramble the creature's metaphysical nature. If the
attack succeeds, the slayer deals weapon damage as normal, and the target must succeed at a
Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half the slayer's level + the slayer's Intelligence modifier) or lose all
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) overcome by aligned weapons (such as DR 10/good), lose
all [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) overcome by aligned sources (such as regeneration 10
\[good\]).

**Prereq:** [Aligned sneak attack](ref/slayertalent/aligned-sneak-attack)
