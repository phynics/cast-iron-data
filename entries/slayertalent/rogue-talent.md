**Rogue Talent**

**Ability:** A slayer can select one of the following rogue talents in place of a slayer talent:
[bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack)\*, [camouflage](ref/talent/camouflage),
[cloying shades](ref/talent/cloying-shades), [combat trick](ref/talent/combat-trick),
[demand attention](ref/talent/demand-attention),
[emboldening strike](ref/talent/emboldening-strike),
[extinguishing strike](ref/talent/extinguishing-strike), [fast stealth](ref/talent/fast-stealth),
[feint from shadows](ref/talent/feint-from-shadows), [finesse rogue](ref/talent/finesse-rogue),
[firearm training](ref/talent/firearm-training), [focusing attack](ref/talent/focusing-attack),
[gloom magic](ref/talent/gloom-magic), [greater gloom magic](ref/talent/greater-gloom-magic),
[grit](ref/talent/grit), [hard to fool](ref/talent/hard-to-fool),
[lasting poison](ref/talent/lasting-poison), [mien of despair](ref/talent/mien-of-despair),
[obscuring blow](ref/talent/obscuring-blow), [poison use](ref/talent/poison-use),
[powerful sneak](ref/talent/powerful-sneak), [rogue crawl](ref/talent/rogue-crawl),
[shadow duplicate](ref/talent/shadow-duplicate), [shadow's chill](ref/talent/shadows-chill),
[slow reactions](ref/talent/slow-reactions)\*, [snap shot](ref/talent/snap-shot),
[sniper's eye](ref/talent/snipers-eye), [surprise attack](ref/talent/surprise-attack),
[swift poison](ref/talent/swift-poison), [terrain mastery](ref/talent/terrain-mastery),
[trap spotter](ref/talent/trap-spotter), [umbral gear](ref/talent/umbral-gear),
[underhanded trick](ref/talent/underhanded-trick), [unwitting ally](ref/talent/unwitting-ally), or
[weapon training](ref/talent/weapon-training).

Any talent effects based on rogue level use the slayer's class level. If the rogue talent has a
prerequisite, the slayer must meet the prerequisite before taking that rogue talent.

If the talent is marked with an asterisk (\*), the talent is considered a Primary Sneak Attack
Talent (only one Primary Sneak Attack Talent can be applied to an individual attack, and the
decision must be made before the attack roll is made).

**Special:** This talent can be selected multiple times; each time, it grants the slayer a new rogue
talent.
