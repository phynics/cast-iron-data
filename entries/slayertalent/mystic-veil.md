**&L&Mystic Veil (Sp)&FN&**

**Ability:** The slayer can cast [silent image](ref/spell/silent-image) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using his slayer level as his caster level. The
slayer uses his Intelligence modifier on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks when using
this ability, and the DC to disbelieve the illusion is equal to 11 + the slayer's Intelligence
modifier.

**Flavor:** Samsaran slayers are adept at obscuring the passes of their mountain homes from
intruders.

**Prereq:** [Samsaran](ref/race/samsaran), Intelligence 11+

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 slayer levels
