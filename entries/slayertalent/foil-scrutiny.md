A slayer with this talent is able to obscure his thoughts and intentions. He gains a +2 bonus on all
Bluff and Disguise checks, as well as on Will saving throws to resist mind-reading spells and
effects (such as _[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts)_ and
_[discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies)_).
