**Reaping Stalker (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Some slayers prefer weapons with grim symbolism of lives cut short and can wield such
weapons with unparalleled lethality.

**Passive:** A slayer with this talent treats any [sickle](ref/eq-weapon/sickle) or
[scythe](ref/eq-weapon/scythe) he wields as though it were
[one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) for the purpose of determining its
damage dice. In addition, the slayer increases the critical threat range of any sickle or scythe he
wields by 1; this does not stack with other effects that alter a weapon's threat range.
