**Focused Poison (Ex)**

**Ability:** When the slayer studies a target, he can immediately reduce the number of additional
_studied targets_ he can maintain. Reducing this number does not require an action. For each target
reduced in this way, the DC of poisons used against his _studied target_ increases by 1.

**Flavor:** The slayer can focus his poison delivery to be especially deadly to his _studied
target._

**Prereq:** 6th-level slayer, [poison use](ref/slayertalent/poison-use)
