**Sources:** Black Markets pg 13\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot**; **Price** 1,200 gp; **Weight** —

These Shadow Plane spores inhibit a victim's ability to absorb positive energy.

**Type** poison, inhaled; **Save** Fortitude DC 16\
**Onset** 10 minutes; **Frequency** 1/hour for 6 hours\
**Effect** 1d2 [Dex damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), and until the Dex damage is healed, the
victim regains only half the normal amount of hit points from
[channeled energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) and conjuration (healing) spells; **Cure** 2
consecutive saves

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[undead anatomy I](ref/spell/undead-anatomy-1); **Cost** 225 gp
