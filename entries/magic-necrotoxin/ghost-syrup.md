**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 23\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot**; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** —

This necrotoxin causes a creature to lose corporeal substance.

**Type** ingested; **Save** Fortitude DC 20\
**Onset** 1 minute; **Frequency** 1/round for 6 rounds\
**Effect** 1d3 [Str damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), and each round a victim takes damage
from this poison, she has a 50% chance of dropping one held item. If a victim's Strength is reduced
to 0 by ghost syrup, the Strength damage becomes Strength drain, and the victim becomes permanently
incorporeal. Carried and worn equipment is unaffected and remains corporeal, and the victim can be
restored only through a [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [miracle](ref/spell/miracle),
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), or [wish](ref/spell/wish) spell; **Cure** 2 consecutive
saves

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt), creator must have 13 ranks in Craft (alchemy); **Cost**
3,000 gp
