**Sources:** Black Markets pg 13\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot**; **Price** 5,400 gp; **Weight** —

These dark gray flakes induce ravenous hunger.

**Type** poison, ingested; **Save** Fortitude DC 18\
**Onset** 10 minutes; **Frequency** 1/minute for 8 minutes\
**Effect** 1d2 [Con damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), and a humanoid who dies while poisoned
rises as an uncontrolled [ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul) at the next midnight, retaining none of the
abilities it possessed in life; **Cure** 2 consecutive saves

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[create undead](ref/spell/create-undead); **Cost** 2,700 gp
