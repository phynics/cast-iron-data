**Intermediate Psychic Magic**

**Ability:** The eidolon gains 2 points of psychic energy that it can spend each day.

**Choice:** Select one spell from the following list:
[compel hostility](ref/spell/compel-hostility), [lesser confusion](ref/spell/confusion-lesser),
[hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism), [mind thrust I](ref/spell/mind-thrust-1), or
[vanish](ref/spell/vanish). The eidolon can use that spell thereafter as a psychic spell by spending
1 point of psychic energy. The caster level and save DC are the same as for _basic psychic magic._

**Prereq:** 5th-level unchained summoner, [aberrant](ref/eidolon/aberrant) subtype,
[basic psychic magic](ref/unchevolution/basic-psychic-magic), Charisma 11+

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once, selecting a different spell each time.
The eidolon's amount of psychic energy available to spend each day does not stack - it uses only the
highest number of points granted by its most powerful _[psychic magic](ref/umr/psychic-magic)_
evolution.
