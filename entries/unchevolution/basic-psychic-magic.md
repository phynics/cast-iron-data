**Basic Psychic Magic**

**Ability:** The eidolon can cast the chosen spell as a psychic spell at will. The spell requires
thought and emotion components as normal for [psychic magic](ref/rule/psychic-magic). The eidolon's
caster level is equal to the eidolon's Hit Dice - 2 (minimum CL 1st). The save DC equals 10 + 1/2
the eidolon's HD + the eidolon's Charisma modifier.

**Choice:** Select one spell from the following list: [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights),
[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[grave words](ref/spell/grave-words), [know direction](ref/spell/know-direction),
[lullaby](ref/spell/lullaby), [mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand), [open/close](ref/spell/open-close),
or [telekinetic projectile](ref/spell/telekinetic-projectile).

**Prereq:** [Aberrant](ref/eidolon/aberrant) subtype, Charisma 10

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once, selecting a different spell each time.
