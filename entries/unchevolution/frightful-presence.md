**Frightful Presence (Ex)**

**Ability:** The eidolon becomes unsettling to its foes, gaining the
[frightful presence](ref/umr/frightful-presence) ability. The eidolon can activate this ability as
part of an offensive action, such as a [charge](ref/rule/charge) or attack.

Opponents within 30 feet of the eidolon must succeed at a Will save or become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 3d6 rounds. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's Hit
Dice + the eidolon's Charisma modifier.

If the eidolon has at least 4 more Hit Dice than an opponent that fails this save, that opponent
becomes [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) instead.

**Prereq:** 11th-level unchained summoner

**Special:** Foes with more Hit Dice than the eidolon are immune to this effect.
