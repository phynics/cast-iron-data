**&L&Immunity (Su)&FN&**

**Flavor:** The eidolon's body becomes extremely resilient to one energy type.

**Passive:** Select one energy type: acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic. The eidolon gains
immunity to that energy type.

**Prereq:** 7th-level unchained summoner

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once. Its effects do not stack. Each time it
applies to a different energy type.
