**Web (Ex)**

**Ability:** The eidolon gains a pair of spinnerets, giving it the ability to spin webs. The eidolon
can use these webs to support itself plus one creature of up to the same size.

**Ability2:** It can throw webbing as a ranged touch attack up to eight times per day, entangling a
creature up to one size larger than the eidolon. The webbing has a range of 50 feet and a 10-foot
range increment.

Creatures [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) by the web can escape with a successful Escape Artist
check, or with a Strength check at a -4 penalty. The DC of these checks is equal to 10 + 1/2 the
eidolon's Hit Dice + the eidolon's Constitution modifier. The webs have a
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 0 and a number of hits points equal to the eidolon's total Hit Dice.

**Prereq:** 7th-level unchained summoner; [daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon), [demon](ref/eidolon/demon),
or [protean](ref/eidolon/protean) subtype; [climb](ref/unchevolution/climb)

**Special:** The eidolon can climb its own webs at its climb speed and can pinpoint the location of
any creature touching its webs.
