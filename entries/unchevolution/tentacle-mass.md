**&L&Tentacle Mass (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** The eidolon grows a thick mass of tentacles that can be used as a primary
[natural weapon](ref/umr/natural-attacks). The tentacles deal 1d8 points of damage if the eidolon is
Medium.

**Prereq:** [Aberrant](ref/eidolon/aberrant) subtype eidolon

**Special:** Eidolons with the [grab](ref/unchevolution/grab) evolution that is linked to a
_tentacle mass_ can use that ability to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) foes of up to the eidolon's
size, and they can also use this evolution in place of the serpentine base form to qualify for the
[constrict](ref/unchevolution/constrict) evolution.
