The eidolon's form takes on a resiliency to one particular energy type, which is usually reflected
in its physical body (ashen hide for fire, icy breath for cold, and so on). Select one energy type
(acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic). The eidolon gains [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5
against that energy type. This resistance increases by 5 for every 5 levels the summoner possesses,
to a maximum of 15 at 10th level. This evolution can be selected more than once. Its effects do not
stack. Each time the eidolon selects this evolution, it applies to a different energy type.
