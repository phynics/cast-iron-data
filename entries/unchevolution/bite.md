**Bite (Ex)**

**Ability:** The eidolon's maw is full of razor-sharp teeth, giving it a
[bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks). This attack is a primary attack. The bite deals 1d6 points
of damage (1d8 if Large, 2d6 if Huge).

If the eidolon already has a bite attack, this evolution allows it to deal 1-1/2 times its Strength
modifier on damage rolls made with its bite.

**Prereq:** [Agathion](ref/eidolon/agathion), [daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon),
[demon](ref/eidolon/demon), [devil](ref/eidolon/devil), [div](ref/eidolon/div),
[elemental](ref/eidolon/elemental), [protean](ref/eidolon/protean), or
[psychopomp](ref/eidolon/psychopomp) subtype
