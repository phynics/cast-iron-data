**&L&Skilled (Ex)&FN&**

**Passive:** The eidolon becomes especially adept at a specific skill, gaining a +8 racial bonus on
that skill.

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once. Its effects do not stack. Each time the
eidolon selects this evolution, it applies to a different skill.
