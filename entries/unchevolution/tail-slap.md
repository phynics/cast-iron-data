**Tail Slap (Ex)**

**Ability:** The eidolon can use its tail to bash nearby foes, granting it a
[tail slap attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks). This attack is a secondary attack. The tail slap deals
1d6 points of damage (1d8 if Large, 2d6 if Huge).

**Prereq:** [Agathion](ref/eidolon/agathion), [daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon),
[demon](ref/eidolon/demon), [devil](ref/eidolon/devil), [div](ref/eidolon/div),
[elemental](ref/eidolon/elemental), [protean](ref/eidolon/protean), or
[psychopomp](ref/eidolon/psychopomp) subtype; [tail](ref/unchevolution/tail) evolution

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once, up to the number of _tail_ evolutions
the eidolon possesses.
