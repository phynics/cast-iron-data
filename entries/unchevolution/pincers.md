**Pincers (Ex)**

**Ability:** An eidolon grows large pincers at the ends of one pair of its limbs, giving it two
[pincer attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks). These attacks are secondary attacks. The pincers deal 1d6
points of damage (1d8 if Large, 2d6 if Huge).

Eidolons with the [grab](ref/unchevolution/grab) evolution linked to their pincers gain a +2 bonus
on combat maneuver checks to grapple.

**Prereq:** [Agathion](ref/eidolon/agathion), [daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon),
[demon](ref/eidolon/demon), [devil](ref/eidolon/devil), [div](ref/eidolon/div),
[elemental](ref/eidolon/elemental), [protean](ref/eidolon/protean), or
[psychopomp](ref/eidolon/psychopomp) subtype; [limbs](ref/unchevolution/limbs) (arms) evolution, or
its base form has [claws](ref/unchevolution/claws) (this evolution replaces the claws)

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once, up to the number of _limbs (arms)_
evolutions the eidolon possesses.
