**Tentacle (Ex)**

**Ability:** The eidolon possesses a long, sinuous tentacle, granting it a
[tentacle attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks). This attack is a secondary attack. The tentacle attack
deals 1d4 points of damage (1d6 if Large, 1d8 if Huge).

**Prereq:** [Daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon), [demon](ref/eidolon/demon), or
[protean](ref/eidolon/protean) subtype

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once.
