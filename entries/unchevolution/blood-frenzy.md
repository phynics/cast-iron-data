**&L&Blood Frenzy (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** When the eidolon is dealt damage, it enters a blood frenzy. It gains a +2 morale bonus
on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls and attacks the nearest creature each round, friend or foe,
although it ceases attacking its summoner after hitting once. The frenzy lasts for 5 rounds or until
the eidolon can perceive no creatures.

**Prereq:** 7th-level unchained summoner; [aberrant](ref/eidolon/aberrant),
[daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon), or [demon](ref/eidolon/demon) subtype

**Special:** At the end of that time, the eidolon is [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for 1 minute. It
cannot enter a blood frenzy while fatigued.
