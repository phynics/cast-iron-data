**Disease**

**Ability:** One of the eidolon's natural weapons carries a disease chosen from the following list:
[bubonic plague](ref/disease/bubonic-plague), [filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever),
[leprosy](ref/disease/leprosy), [red ache](ref/disease/red-ache), or [shakes](ref/disease/shakes).
Each hit forces a saving throw against the disease's normal effects. The disease has no onset,
however. The save DC equals 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's Hit Dice + the eidolon's Constitution modifier.
The eidolon can expose a creature to its disease no more than once per round.

**Prereq:** 7th-level unchained summoner; [aberrant](ref/eidolon/aberrant),
[daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon), [demon](ref/eidolon/demon), or [devil](ref/eidolon/devil) subtype

**Special:** By spending 2 additional evolution points, you can instead choose the following
additional diseases: [demon fever](ref/disease/demon-fever) (only if the eidolon has the _demon_
subtype), [devil chills](ref/disease/devil-chills) (only if the eidolon has the _devil_ subtype), or
[slimy doom](ref/disease/slimy-doom).
