**Trip (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever the eidolon makes a successful bite attack, it can attempt a free combat
maneuver check. If the eidolon succeeds at this check, the target is knocked
[prone](ref/rule/prone). If the eidolon fails, it is not tripped in return.

**Flavor:** The eidolon becomes adept at knocking foes to the ground with its bite, granting it a
[trip](ref/umr/trip) attack.

**Prereq:** [Daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon), [demon](ref/eidolon/demon), [devil](ref/eidolon/devil),
[elemental](ref/eidolon/elemental), or [protean](ref/eidolon/protean) subtype;
[bite](ref/unchevolution/bite)

**Special:** This ability works only on creatures of a size category equal to or smaller than the
eidolon.
