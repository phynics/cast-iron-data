The eidolon becomes adept at knocking foes to the ground with its [bite](ref/unchevolution/bite),
granting it a [trip](ref/umr/trip) attack. Whenever the eidolon makes a successful bite attack, it
can attempt a free combat maneuver check. If the eidolon succeeds at this check, the target is
knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone). If the eidolon fails, it is not tripped in return. This ability
works only on creatures of a size category equal to or smaller than the eidolon. _Requirements_:
[Daemon](ref/eidolon/daemon), [demon](ref/eidolon/demon), [devil](ref/eidolon/devil),
[elemental](ref/eidolon/elemental), or [protean](ref/eidolon/protean) subtype; bite evolution.
