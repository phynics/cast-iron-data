**Sources:** Bestiary 6 pg 23\
**Aura** overwhelming all; **CL** 30th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 20 lbs.

The Eclipsing Eye is a +4 [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine)
[axiomatic](ref/magic-enh/axiomatic) [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy)
[wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding) [heavy mace](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-mace); it is a token of esteem
and power granted by [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) himself to the governor of Hell's greatest city.
It allows the wielder to command allegiance as per a
[rod of rulership](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-rulership) with an unlimited duration when activated in
[Hell](ref/rule/hell). Outside of Hell, it remains limited to 500 total minutes of this effect, but
does not crumble to dust if these minutes are used up. Every hour it spends in Hell restores 1
minute of expended duration. When used as a weapon, The Eclipsing Eye has a critical multiplier of
×3. The Eclipsing Eye automatically casts _blindness_ (as per
[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness) heightened to 9th level, save DC 23) on the first
creature it strikes each round. The Eclipsing Eye is [Dispater's](ref/faith/dispater) rod of office,
and he can summon it to his hand as a free action from any distance, even across planar boundaries.
As long as he wields it, all lawful evil, neutral evil, and lawful neutral creatures treat him as if
he were under a [sanctuary](ref/spell/sanctuary) spell (CL 30th, save DC 23 as if heightened to a
9th level spell). Three times per day, The Eclipsing Eye can create any of the following spell
effects, heightened to 9th level as per the [Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell) feat:
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions), [demand](ref/spell/demand),
[discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies), and [discern location](ref/spell/discern-location).

### Destruction

Asmodeus can will the destruction of The Eclipsing Eye as a free action, but otherwise it can be
destroyed only by immersing it in the headwaters of the River Styx for a century, allowing the
artifact to rust away.
