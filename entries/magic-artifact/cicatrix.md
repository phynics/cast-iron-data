**Sources:** Bestiary 4 pg 93\
**Aura** overwhelming necromancy; **CL** 30th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 2 lbs.

When [Vildeis](ref/monster/empyreal-lord-vildeis) reached for a tool to permanently put an end to
her sight so she no longer had to bear witness to the evils of the multiverse, her hand fell upon
Cicatrix. Although even she doesn't know who or what placed the black blade within her grasp, in the
ages since it put out its mistress's eyes, the blade has rarely left Vildeis's hand. Cicatrix is a
+5 [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) [vicious](ref/magic-enh/vicious)
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) that counts as both cold iron and silver. Any time Cicatrix deals
damage, the creature struck must attempt a DC 25 Will save. If it fails, the wielder instantly knows
that creature's alignment and can listen to its surface thoughts, as
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts). If the wielder chooses, she can then force the
creature to attempt a DC 25 Will save; if the creature fails its saving throw, the wielder may blind
the target (as [blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness)) or inscribe a martyr's mark upon
the target. Inscribing a martyr's mark means the wielder takes the damage from the attack instead of
the target; the martyr's mark is triggered like a [mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice), but
activates a [geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest) of the wielder's choosing instead of
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse). A martyr's mark can be removed as if it were a
_[mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice)._

### Destruction

Cicatrix is destroyed if an evil outsider of CR 20 or higher uses the blade to sacrifice itself to
save a good outsider's life.
