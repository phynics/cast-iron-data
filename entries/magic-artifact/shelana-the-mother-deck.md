**Sources:** Classic Treasures Revisited pg 21\
**Aura** strong (all schools); **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** -

Shelana the Mother Deck operates as an intelligent
[deck of many things](ref/magic-artifact/deck-of-many-things), save that all her benefits and
penalties are doubled (for example, the Star card grants a +4 bonus to ability stats instead of +2,
the Skull card summons two dread wraiths at a time, and so on). Shelana can cast
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport) once per day, transporting herself and her puzzle box only.

### Destruction

Shelana can only be destroyed by using her own Moon card to wish for her destruction.

### Statistics

**Alignment** CN; **Ego** 21\
**Senses** 30 ft. senses\
**Int** 19, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 20\
**Communication** speech, telepathy
