**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 164\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 1 lb.

This +6 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [speed](ref/magic-enh/speed)
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) was constructed in defiance
of the gods and their chosen mortal champions, for Legendsbane was created to slay mythic creatures.
Legendsbane bypasses all types of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), and deals an
additional 3d6 points of damage to mythic creatures and to non-mythic creatures that have epic DR.
This additional damage is multiplied for critical hits.

Non-mythic characters can wield Legendsbane normally. However, when a mythic character first
attempts to wield the dagger, she must succeed at a DC 25 Fortitude save or suffer the effects of a
[destruction](ref/spell/destruction) spell. If the mythic character survives, she can wield the
dagger normally.

Legendsbane imparts upon its wielder the ability to track down mythic creatures she has hunted
before. At will, the wielder can cast [locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature) to find a
specific mythic creature known to the wielder.

### Destruction

Legendsbane can be destroyed if it's anointed in the blood of a slain 10th-rank mythic creature and
then struck three times by a [vorpal](ref/magic-enh/vorpal) weapon. This act also destroys the
vorpal weapon.
