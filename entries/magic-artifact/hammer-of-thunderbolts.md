**Sources:** Pathfinder \#95: Anvil of Fire pg 53, Advanced Player's Guide pg 314, Ultimate
Equipment pg 330\
**Aura** strong evocation, necromancy, and transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +3 Large [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer) deals 2d6
points of damage on a successful hit. In the hands of a wielder who wears a
[belt of giant strength](ref/magic-wondrous/belt-of-giant-strength) and knows the weapon is a hammer
of thunderbolts (not just the +3 warhammer it appears to be), it becomes a +5 Large
giant-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) returning [thundering](ref/magic-enh/thundering) warhammer with the
following two properties.

First, when the bearer makes a successful thrown attack with the hammer, the hammer emits a powerful
clap of thunder, causing all creatures within 90 feet to be [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round
(Fortitude DC 15 negates); the wielder is not immune to this effect. Second, the hammer's range
increment increases to 30 feet.

### Destruction

A hammer of thunderbolts is destroyed if heated in a
[fire giant's](ref/monster/giant-fire-fire-giant) forge and quenched in the blood of a good-aligned
humanoid, which causes the iron of the hammer to become so brittle it shatters the next time it is
used to strike. Only bathing it in a fire giant's blood can reverse the hammer's brittleness; once
the hammer is shattered, though, not even this can repair it. If it is bathed in the blood of a fire
giant king or queen, it temporarily gains two additional powers for the next 9 days: the bearer
gains resistance 30 against fire and sonic damage, and gains immunity to the stunning effect of the
hammer's clap of thunder.
