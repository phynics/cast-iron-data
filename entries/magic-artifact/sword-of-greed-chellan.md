**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 16th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 25 lbs.

Of the Seven Blades of Conviction wielded by the runelords' champions, weapons known as the
Alara-hai to the Thassilonians, the sword of greed was the most extravagant - for it was made of
magically hardened gold, tempered to the strength of adamantine yet retaining its luster. Although
made of gold, Chellan functions as an [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) weapon for the
purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and bypassing
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness). Chellan is a +5 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen)
[scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar), and its density allows it to damage foes as if it were
[one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) than its actual size. It can be properly
wielded only by characters with a Strength of 18 or higher - all other characters must either wield
it as a two-handed weapon or take a -2 penalty on attack rolls with the weapon.

Whenever Chellan strikes a foe, the target must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or be slowed as
per the spell [slow](ref/spell/slow) (CL 16th). If the sword scores a critical hit, the target must
succeed at a DC 25 Fortitude save or be turned into a crystalline statue, as if by
[flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone). Characters turned to crystal by Chellan appear to be
made of ruby, diamond, or some other valuable gem, but are in fact merely colored quartz of little
value to looters.

Fanatical in the extreme, Chellan seeks to force its wielder into serving Karzoug and furthering the
runelord's goals. To enforce this purpose of their existence, it can use its intelligence and
special abilities to aid and manipulate those who think to wield it.

### Destruction

Karzoug can destroy the sword of greed merely by commanding its destruction with his voice (a free
action for the runelord).
