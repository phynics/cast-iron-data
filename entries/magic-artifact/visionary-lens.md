**Sources:** Artifacts and Legends pg 63, Realm of the Fellnight Queen pg 29\
**Aura** strong divination, enchantment, and illusion; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This ornate mirror measures 4 feet in diameter, framed in gold and embellished with silver. The
frame depicts dozens of stylized eyes from fey creatures that alternately open and close in
wakefulness or slumber. During the day, these eyes influence the mirror's surface to act as a
[crystal ball with telepathy](ref/magic-wondrous/crystal-ball-telepathy). At night, the eyes can
cast [scrying](ref/spell/scrying) (looking into the dreams of subjects) and
[nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare) instead. Lastly, during the twilight hours between day and night,
the eyes can be commanded to bestow one of the following effects upon any single person gazing into
the mirror:

- [geas](ref/spell/geas-quest) (DC 21)
- [maze](ref/spell/maze) (DC 22)
- [vision](ref/spell/vision)

The mirror does not function outside of [the First World](ref/rule/the-first-world) or demiplanes
that share characteristics of the First World (such as the Fellnight Realm).

### Destruction

The visionary lens shatters if exposed to a [wail of the banshee](ref/spell/wail-of-the-banshee)
cast by someone wearing a
[medallion of thought projection](ref/magic-wondrous/medallion-of-thought-projection).
