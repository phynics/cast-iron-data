**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 337

**Aura** overwhelming necromancy; **CL** 22nd\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 80 lbs.

This armor looks like a more massive and dangerous suit of
[demon armor](ref/magic-armor/demon-armor). It is a suit of +5
[heavy fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-heavy) [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) that
allows its wearer to make claw attacks that deal 2d10 points of damage, and strike as +3 weapons
that are treated as chaotic and evil for the purposes of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). Additionally, those struck by the claws must succeed
at a DC 20 Fortitude save or be affected as per the spell [contagion](ref/spell/contagion). The
armor's claws are built into the armor so these weapons cannot be disarmed. While wearing the armor,
the wearer benefits from the following demonic traits.

- The wearer gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft. If it already has darkvision, the range
  of its darkvision is increased by 60 ft.
- The wearer does not need to breathe or eat.
- The wearer is immune to electricity and poison.
- The wearer gains resistance to acid 10, cold 10 and fire 10.
- The wearer gains telepathy and speaks Abyssal, Celestial, and Draconic.

### Destruction

The Demon Prince Armor dissolves if submerged in the tears of a demon lord. If that foul concoction
is then drank by an angel, the armor is destroyed.
