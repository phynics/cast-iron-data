**Sources:** Rise of the Runelords Anniversary Edition pg 426, Pathfinder \#6: Spires of Xin-Shalast
pg 65\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 16th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 5 lbs.

The first Sihedron Tomes were said to have been crafted by a dragon in the service of Emperor Xin.
Each of these books has a unique appearance, and hold an infinite number of pages for spellcasters
to inscribe spells upon. Any wizard who prepares spells from a Sihedron Tome, which potentially
holds libraries worth of arcane knowledge, may prepare [bonus spells](ref/rule/determine-bonuses) as
if her Intelligence were 6 points higher. This is not an actual bonus to Intelligence and grants the
user no additional benefit. Karzoug's Sihedron Tome contains all sorcerer/wizard spells (save for
those of the schools of Enchantment and Illusion) found in the Core Rulebook, plus the spells
[blood money](ref/spell/blood-money), [covetous aura](ref/spell/covetous-aura),
[deathwine](ref/spell/deathwine), [raiment of command](ref/spell/raiment-of-command),
[sign of wrath](ref/spell/sign-of-wrath), [swipe](ref/spell/swipe), and
[unconscious agenda](ref/spell/unconscious-agenda)); at the GM's discretion, it can certainly hold
more spells.

### Destruction

A special 9th-level spell must be designed that exists only to destroy the specific and particular
Sihedron Tome into which it is inscribed. This spell, which has numerous expensive and rare material
components, must then be cast on the Sihedron Tome by its owner.
