**Aura** overwhelming all; **CL** 26th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 4 lbs.

[Barbatos](ref/faith/barbatos) wields a potent weapon named Eyjatas, a +4
[speed](ref/magic-enh/speed) [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy)
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff) embedded with innumerable inhuman eyeballs. Three times
per day when striking a creature with Eyjatas, its wielder can rip the target's eyes out,
permanently [blinding](ref/misc/blinded) it and dealing 2d6 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed)
damage. A creature can resist this effect with a successful DC 30 Fortitude save, but no save is
allowed if the attack was a confirmed critical hit. Once Eyjatas rips out a creature's eyes, it
functions as a [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon against that target.

Three times per day, the wielder of Eyjatas can cast [prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes). In
addition, the wielder can use [arcane eye](ref/spell/arcane-eye) at will, even on other planes
viewed through [scrying](ref/spell/scrying), and can use [eyebite](ref/spell/eyebite) (DC 20) at
will within 30 feet of the _[arcane eye](ref/spell/arcane-eye)._

### Destruction

To destroy Eyjatas, the quarterstaff's dozens of eyes must be blinded by the purifying light of an
unwavering truth spoken by an immortal that has only ever spoken lies in its life. Once the eyes are
blinded in this way, the staff must be hurled into a magical portal that has no entrance and that
leads nowhere.
