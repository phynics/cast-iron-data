**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 335, Artifacts and Legends pg 11, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 547

**Aura** strong conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 12 lbs.

This is a +6 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [throwing](ref/magic-enh/throwing)
goblinoid-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [dwarven waraxe](ref/eq-weapon/dwarven-waraxe). Any dwarf who
holds it doubles the range of his or her [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision). Any non-dwarf who grasps
the Axe takes 4 points of temporary [Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain); these points
cannot be healed or restored in any way while the Axe is held. The current owner of the Axe gains a
+10 bonus on Craft (armor, jewelry, stonemasonry, traps, and weapons) checks. The wielder of the Axe
can summon an [elder earth elemental](ref/monster/earth-elemental) (as
[summon monster IX](ref/spell/summon-monster-9); duration 20 rounds) once per week.

### Destruction

The Axe of the Dwarvish Lords rusts away to nothing if it is ever used by a goblin to behead a
dwarven king.
