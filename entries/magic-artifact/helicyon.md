**Sources:** Heaven Unleashed pg 18\
**Aura** overwhelming varied; **CL** 28\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 4

Helicyon is a +5 [anchoring](ref/magic-enh/anchoring)
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy)
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff). Helicyon can also appear as a walking stick and can
assume its true form as a free action. Any time Helicyon deals damage, the creature struck must
attempt a DC 25 Will saving throw. If it fails, the target becomes overwrought with remorse for
events that took place in the course of its existence, even if it is denied, never had, or lost the
capacity to feel emotion, as per [overwhelming grief](ref/spell/overwhelming-grief). This effect
affects only creatures with Intelligence 3 or greater, even if they are normally immune to
mind-affecting effects. Once affected or after a successful saving throw, the target becomes immune
to this ability for 24 hours. As a standard action, [Andoletta](ref/faith/andoletta) can summon
Helicyon to her hand from any distance, even across planar boundaries.

### Destruction

Helicyon must be broken in the bare hands of an [archon](ref/family/archon) whose CR is 20 or
greater, while that being renounces [Heaven](ref/rule/heaven) and swears allegiance to an evil
master or cause.
