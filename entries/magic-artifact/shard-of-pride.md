**Sources:** Pathfinder \#61: Shards of Sin pg 69\
**Aura** overwhelming illusion; **CL** 25th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 1 lb.

The Shard of Pride is made of coppery [horacalcum](ref/eq-material/horacalcum). As long as the Shard
of Pride is carried, its owner can use [major image](ref/spell/major-image) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day, gains a +2 insight bonus on saves
versus illusion spells, and gains a +2 insight bonus on all skill checks.

> _Curse:_ The owner suffers delusions of grandeur, and is [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) whenever
> she must serve another creature as a subordinate of any sort for as long as that arrangement
> persists. She cannot gain the benefit of the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action, nor can
> she take the aid another action.

### Destruction

A shard of the [Sihedron](ref/magic-artifact/sihedron) cannot be destroyed individually. Only by
recombining them into the Sihedron and then using that artifact's method of destruction can one
destroy the shards.
