**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 3 lbs.

The perfect golden lute is said to be a flawless instrument; its tone and clarity are unrivaled. It
is an instrument so fine it practically performs itself, granting anyone who plays it a +10
competency bonus on [Perform (string)](ref/skill/perform-any) checks with it. A character with ranks
in Perform (string) can play the lute to activate [countersong](ref/performance/countersong),
[distraction](ref/performance/distraction), or [fascinate](ref/performance/fascinate) (as the bardic
performance abilities) as if he were a 1st-level bard, performing a number of rounds per day equal
to his Charisma modifier. If the user of the perfect golden lute is a bard, he adds +2 to the DC of
all bardic performance abilities using the lute, and can use bardic performance with the lute for an
additional 6 rounds per day.

### Destruction

The perfect golden lute shatters into splinters if a day passes in which no music is played within
1,000 miles of it, or if its bearer is killed by a bard spell or bardic performance.
