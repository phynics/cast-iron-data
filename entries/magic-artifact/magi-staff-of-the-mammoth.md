**Aura** strong (all schools); **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Seven feet of twisted, gnarled spruce carved with primitive runes and icons depicting mammoths,
tigers, and hunters form this regal staff. The staff has all the powers of a
[staff of the magi](ref/magic-artifact/staff-of-the-magi) with the following changes:

- [sleet storm](ref/spell/sleet-storm) (instead of _[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)_)
- [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud) (instead of _[pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics)_)
- [wall of ice](ref/spell/wall-of-ice) (instead of _[wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire)_)

The staff can also cast [cone of cold](ref/spell/cone-of-cold) (15d6 damage, Reflex DC 19 half) for
a cost of 2 charges. When casting [summon monster IX](ref/spell/summon-monster-9), the staff of the
mammoth always summons 1d4+1 [celestial](ref/template/celestial) or
[fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) [mastodons](ref/monster/elephant-mastodon).

### Destruction

This item can be destroyed in the same manner as a normal _staff of the magi._
