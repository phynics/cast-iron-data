**Sources:** Curse of the Crimson Throne pg 432, Pathfinder \#12: Crown of Fangs pg 64\
**Aura** strong enchantment, illusion, and necromancy; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** head; **Price** -; **Weight** 4 lbs.

The eponymous fangs set into this jeweled golden circlet are those of the dragon Kazavon. The crown
grants its wearer a +6 enhancement bonus to her Constitution and Charisma scores, as well as
[regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) 20. Only a blade capable of destroying the crown, like
[Serithtial](ref/magic-artifact/serithtial), can bypass this regeneration. The wearer gains the
personal benefits of [foresight](ref/spell/foresight), while the crown itself can cast each of the
following spells three times per day: [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self),
[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person) (DC 17), [major image](ref/spell/major-image) (DC 17),
[mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image), and [mislead](ref/spell/mislead) (DC 19).

Queen Ileosa and this crown are bound together, and she retains its bonuses to her ability scores
and regeneration even if she isn't wearing the crown, regardless of the distance between the two.
She prefers to wear the crown because that allows it to use its spells in her defense - and because
of her vanity and pride.

### Destruction

The Crown of Fangs is destroyed if struck (while unattended) by a [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) sword
forged by a once-mortal god - the blade Serithtial is one such blade.
