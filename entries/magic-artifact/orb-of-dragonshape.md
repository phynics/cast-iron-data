**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 19th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 4 lbs.

Once per day, the bearer of one of these scintillating, 1-foot-diameter orbs can transform into a
Huge dragon of a specific variety. This effect is similar to that created by
[form of the dragon III](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-3), but the bearer also gains
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) 21 and the following abilities linked the orb's dragon
type, usable three times during the transformation. The transformation lasts for 10 minutes and
cannot be ended prematurely.

- [Black Dragon](ref/family/dragon-black) Orb: [Darkness](ref/spell/darkness)
- [Blue Dragon](ref/family/dragon-blue) Orb: [Ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound) (DC 10),
  [minor image](ref/spell/minor-image) (DC 13), [ventriloquism](ref/spell/ventriloquism) (DC 11)
- [Brass Dragon](ref/family/dragon-brass) Orb: [Endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
  [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals), [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion) (DC 14)
- [Bronze Dragon](ref/family/dragon-bronze) Orb:
  [Create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water), [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud),
  _[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_
- [Copper Dragon](ref/family/dragon-copper) Orb: [Grease](ref/spell/grease) (DC 11),
  [hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter) (DC 13), [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape)
- [Gold Dragon](ref/family/dragon-gold) Orb: [Bless](ref/spell/bless),
  [daylight](ref/spell/daylight), [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)
- [Green Dragon](ref/family/dragon-green) Orb: [Charm person](ref/spell/charm-person) (DC 11),
  [entangle](ref/spell/entangle) (DC 11), _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ (DC 14)
- [Red Dragon](ref/family/dragon-red) Orb: [Detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic),
  [pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics) (DC 13), _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ (DC 14)
- [Silver Dragon](ref/family/dragon-silver) Orb: _[Detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil),_
  [feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), _[fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud)_
- [White Dragon](ref/family/dragon-white) Orb: _[Fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud),_
  [gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind) (DC 13)

### Destruction

To destroy an Orb of Dragonshape that transforms the user into a good-aligned dragon, an evil dragon
must use the orb to transform and sacrifice itself to save another. To destroy an orb tied to evil
dragons, a good dragon must use it to transform and go on a rampage resulting in the death of more
than 100 innocents.
