**Aura** strong conjuration, illusion, and transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** head; **Price** -; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) cap includes prominent guards for the eyes and nose. The
helmet was forged under duress by a dwarven smith to further the ambitions of his mad brother. The
Tarnhelm has the following powers.

- The wearer gains the [shapechanger](ref/subtype/shapechanger) subtype.
- The wearer gains immunity to polymorph effects, except ones he chooses to allow to affect him.
- At will, the wearer can use [greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater) as a
  [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) for as long a duration as desired.
- At will, the wearer can change his appearance in the same fashion as a
  [hat of disguise](ref/magic-wondrous/hat-of-disguise), with a DC 25 Will save required to
  disbelieve the illusion. If the Tarnhelm is used to create a disguise, the wearer receives a +25
  bonus on the Disguise check.
- Three times per day, the wearer can use [greater teleport](ref/spell/teleport-greater), exactly as
  if he had cast the spell of the same name. The wearer can teleport additional times by expending
  one use of mythic power per teleport.
- Three times per day, the wearer can assume the form of an animal as though he had cast
  [beast shape IV](ref/spell/beast-shape-4), except the duration lasts until the effect is
  [dismissed](ref/misc/dismissible) or dispelled. The wearer can change shape additional times by
  expending one use of mythic power per transformation.
- By expending two uses of mythic power, the wearer can assume the form of Gargantuan chromatic or
  metallic [dragon](ref/family/dragon-true) dragon. The effect lasts until dispelled or dismissed.
  He gains a +14 size bonus to Strength, a +12 size bonus to Constitution, a +10 natural armor
  bonus, a fly speed of 150 feet (poor), [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) 90 feet,
  [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 180 feet, a breath weapon, DR 15/magic,
  [frightful presence](ref/umr/frightful-presence) (DC 23), one bite (4d6), two claws (2d8), two
  wing attacks (2d6), and one tail slap (2d8). All his breath weapons deal 16d8 points of damage and
  allow a Reflex save (DC 10 plus his tier plus his Constitution modifier) for half damage. This
  ability otherwise functions as [form of the dragon III](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-3).

### Destruction

The Tarnhelm can be destroyed by being struck by a succession by hammer blows from a dwarven smith
wielding an [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) hammer, a human smith with a steel hammer, and
an elven smith using a hammer of spun glass. Each must succeed at a DC 30 Craft (armor) check when
landing the blow. Failure destroys the hammer, disrupts the attempt to destroy the Tarnhelm, and
deals 12d6 points of damage to the smith.
