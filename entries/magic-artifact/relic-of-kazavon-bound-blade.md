**Sources:** Artifacts and Legends pg 36, Pathfinder \#12: Crown of Fangs pg 64\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 6 lbs.

The Bound Blade is a +3 [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine)
[bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword), forged from the claws of Kazavon. For worshipers of
[Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), the Bound Blade is heresy made manifest. The sword grants the user a +6
enhancement bonus to Strength. As a free action, upon using the sword to reduce a sentient living
creature to 0 or fewer hit points, the wielder can cast [soul bind](ref/spell/soul-bind), forcing
that creature to make a successful DC 25 Will save or have its soul trapped within the sword. While
there is a soul trapped within the sword, the Bound Blade emanates the effects of the spell
[desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate) in a 50-foot-radius aura. This aura lasts for a number of days
equal to the number of Hit Dice the slain creature possessed. After that period passes, the soul
within the sword is destroyed and cannot be restored by any method short of divine intervention. The
sword wielder can release a trapped soul by using the blade's _[soul bind](ref/spell/soul-bind)_
ability on another creature.

### Destruction

The Bound Blade shatters if used by a [paladin](ref/class/paladin) of Abadar to slay a fallen
paladin dedicated to [Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon).
