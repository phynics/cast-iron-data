**Aura** overwhelming all; **CL** 30th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 4 lbs.

[Pazuzu's](ref/monster/demon-lord-pazuzu) weapon is the Scepter of Shibaxet, a staff of black metal
with a large hook at its tip. The scepter functions as a
[rod of enemy detection](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-enemy-detection) that can be used any number of times
per day. If Pazuzu presents the scepter as a standard action, all creatures within 30 feet who view
it become [panicked](ref/misc/panicked) for 1d6 rounds unless they succeed at a DC 23 Will save
(this is a mind-affecting fear effect). Once per day, Pazuzu can use the Scepter of Shibaxet as a
[rod of cancellation](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-cancellation) (this does not deplete or harm the
scepter). As a standard action, he can call the scepter to his hand from any distance, even across
planar boundaries. As an immediate action, Pazuzu can transform the scepter into a +5
[anarchic](ref/magic-enh/anarchic) [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy)
[longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) or return it to its normal shape. None of its other powers can
be used in sword form.

### Destruction

The Scepter of Shibaxet can be broken by a redeemed demon lord if the regenerating towers of
Shibaxet are already destroyed.
