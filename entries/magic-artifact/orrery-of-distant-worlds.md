**Aura** strong (all schools); **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This portable [orrery](ref/eq-misc/orrery) models Golarion's solar system, and is collapsible down
to a 1-foot-diameter globe - expanding it out to its full size (about 4 feet across) or collapsing
it takes 2d6 minutes.

When expanded, the orrery of distant worlds becomes affixed to whatever surface it sits upon,
functioning as if anchored to that surface via an [immovable rod](ref/magic-rod/immovable-rod). If
consulted, it grants a +10 bonus on all [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography)
checks made to navigate or to answer questions about outer space. Once per month, it may be used to
contact alien minds on far-flung worlds - this works identically to the spell
[contact other plane](ref/spell/contact-other-plane) (use the demigod line to determine the effects
of the spell or to determine ability score decreases). At your discretion, use of the orrery can
have unanticipated results, as it can accidentally release stored information when activated.

The 11 planetary spheres and the sun sphere can be detached and carried separately. As long as even
one of these spheres is missing, the orrery cannot be collapsed or moved, nor can it be used to
contact alien minds. Once detached, a planetary sphere carries 4 charges that can be used to
activate a spell effect. The effects of the 12 spheres and the number of charges required to use a
sphere's spell are as follows:

- Sun-[searing light](ref/spell/searing-light) (1 charge)
- Aballon-[haste](ref/spell/haste) (2 charges)
- Castrovel-[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts) (1 charge)
- Golarion-[resilient sphere](ref/spell/resilient-sphere) (4 charges)
- Akiton-[rage](ref/spell/rage) (1 charge)
- Verces-[align weapon](ref/spell/align-weapon) (1 charge)
- Eox-[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward) (2 charges)
- Triaxus-[teleport](ref/spell/teleport) (4 charges)
- Liavara-[dream](ref/spell/dream) (4 charges)
- Bretheda-[restoration](ref/spell/restoration) (2 charges)
- Apostae-[fly](ref/spell/fly) (2 charges)
- Aucturn-[confusion](ref/spell/confusion) (2 charges)

A sphere can be recharged by replacing it on the orrery, at which point it may be recharged by an
arcane spellcaster in the same method by which a [staff](ref/rule/staves) is recharged.

### Destruction

Although it is a minor artifact, the orrery of distant worlds may be destroyed by regular damage. It
has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 20 and 300 hit points, but has already taken 180 points of damage
from the crash. Charges held within spheres remain usable even after the orrery is destroyed, but
they can no longer be recharged.
