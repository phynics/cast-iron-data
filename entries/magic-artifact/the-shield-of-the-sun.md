**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 339, Advanced Player's Guide pg 317

**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This +5 Large shield, emblazoned with the symbol of the sun, allows the wielder to cast spells as if
she were a 20th-level [paladin](ref/class/paladin) with a Charisma of 20. The spells gained are
cumulative with any existing spells per day the character might have, even from paladin class
levels. The Shield of the Sun also grants its wielder
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) 15. It absorbs the first 10 points of damage from any
energy attack (fire, cold, acid, electricity, or sonic). In return for all this, once per year the
shield's owner must undertake a [quest](ref/spell/geas-quest) at the behest of a lawful good deity
(no saving throw to avoid).

A chaotic or evil character gains four [negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels)
when wielding the Shield of the Sun. The negative levels persist as long as the shield is in hand
and cannot be overcome in any way (including restoration spells). The negative levels disappear when
the shield is stowed or leaves the wearer's possession.

### Destruction

The Shield of the Sun may be destroyed if a good-aligned character casts it into the Well of Eternal
Night, its light fading into the inky depths before it finally disappears. If a nongood character
does the deed, the shield is not destroyed, merely lost, and a quest to the Plane of Shadow may
recover it.
