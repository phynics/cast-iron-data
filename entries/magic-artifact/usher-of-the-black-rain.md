**Sources:** Bestiary 6 pg 161\
**Aura** overwhelming all; **CL** 29th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 12 lbs.

[Apollyon's](ref/faith/apollyon) weapon is the Usher of the Black Rain. He can cause this weapon to
shift from a [scythe](ref/eq-weapon/scythe) to a
[composite longbow](ref/eq-weapon/composite-longbow) (Str +16) as a move action, but in both forms
the Usher is a +5 [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding) weapon. As a
longbow, it creates ammunition automatically as it is fired. Against foes who are currently
suffering from a disease, the Usher of the Black Rain deals an additional 6 points of profane damage
with each hit.

### Destruction

The Usher of the Black Rain burns to ash if Apollyon's cloak (which was stitched from the flesh of
the angels whose souls empower his bow) is brought to [Nirvana](ref/rule/nirvana), washed clean
there, and then buried while intoning each dead angel's long-forgotten name.
