**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 337, Advanced Player's Guide pg 317

**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 12 lbs.

This +5 [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding)
[unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) [ranseur](ref/eq-weapon/ranseur) was once the weapon of a powerful
archdevil who they say controlled an entire layer of [Hell](ref/rule/hell). When he attempted to
defy the Prince of Darkness, he was overthrown and banished into some cold and remote prison at the
edge of the multiverse. Still bearing some infernal vestige of the forgotten fiend's power, the fork
grants the bearer a +10 bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks and the power to use
[scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray) three times per day.

Any nonevil creature who touches the Fork of the Forgotten One is given a choice: become lawful evil
immediately, or die. Those who choose death are instantly slain (Fortitude DC 30 negates). Those who
choose to become lawful evil change alignment immediately (no saving throw) and are subject to a
[demand](ref/spell/demand) spell (Will DC 25) requiring that they find the Forgotten One and free
him from his icy prison - presumably thereafter he will press them into service for his plans of
revenge against those who imprisoned him.

### Destruction

The Fork of the Forgotten One is destroyed when a [paladin](ref/class/paladin) of 19th level or
higher touches it and chooses death rather than becoming lawful evil. This sacrifice still kills the
paladin, though she can be raised from the dead.
