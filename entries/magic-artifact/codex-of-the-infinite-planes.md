**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 336, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 547, Artifacts and Legends pg 18

**Aura** overwhelming transmutation; **CL** 30th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 300 lbs.

The Codex is enormous - supposedly, it requires two strong men to lift it. No matter how many pages
are turned, another always remains. Anyone opening the Codex for the first time is utterly
annihilated, as with a [destruction](ref/spell/destruction) spell (Fortitude DC 30). Those who
survive can peruse its pages and learn its powers, though not without risk. Each day spent studying
the Codex allows the reader to make a [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) check (DC 50) to learn one
of its powers (choose the power learned randomly; add a +1 circumstance bonus on the check per
additional day spent reading until a power is learned). However, each day of study also forces the
reader to make a Will save (DC 30 + 1 per day of study) to avoid being driven insane (as the
[insanity](ref/spell/insanity) spell). The powers of the Codex of the Infinite Planes are as
follows: [astral projection](ref/spell/astral-projection), [banishment](ref/spell/banishment),
[elemental swarm](ref/spell/elemental-swarm), [gate](ref/spell/gate),
[greater planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-greater),
[greater planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding-greater), [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift),
and [soul bind](ref/spell/soul-bind). Each of these
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) are usable at will by the owner of the Codex
(assuming that he or she has learned how to access the power). The Codex of the Infinite Planes has
a caster level of 30th for the purposes of all powers and catastrophes, and all saving throw DCs are
20 + spell level. Activating any power requires a Spellcraft check (DC 40 + twice the spell level of
the power; the character can't take 10 on this check). Any failure on either check indicates that a
catastrophe befalls the user (roll on the table below for the effect). A character can incur only
one catastrophe per power use.

|d%|Catastrophe|
| --- | --- |
|01-25|**Natural Fury:** An [earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake) spell centered on the reader strikes every round for 1 minute, and an [intensified](ref/feat/intensified-spell) [storm of vengeance](ref/spell/storm-of-vengeance) spell is centered and targeted on the reader.|
|26-50|**Fiendish Vengeance:** A _[gate](ref/spell/gate)_ opens and 1d3+1 [balors](ref/monster/demon-balor), [pit fiends](ref/monster/devil-pit-fiend), or similar evil outsiders step through and attempt to destroy the owner of the Codex.|
|51-75|**Ultimate Imprisonment:** Reader's soul is captured (as [trap the soul](ref/spell/trap-the-soul); no save allowed) in a random gem somewhere on the plane while his or her body is entombed beneath the earth (as [imprisonment](ref/spell/imprisonment)).|
|76-100|**Death:** The reader utters a [wail of the banshee](ref/spell/wail-of-the-banshee) and then is subject to a _[destruction](ref/spell/destruction)_ spell. This repeats every round for 10 rounds until the reader is dead.|

### Destruction

The Codex of the Infinite Planes is destroyed if one page is torn out and left on each plane in
existence. Note that tearing out a page immediately triggers a catastrophe.
