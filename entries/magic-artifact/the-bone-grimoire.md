**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Anyone who reads the entire hundred pages of blood-scripted text bound within the Bone Grimoire (a
task that requires 1 week of study and a successful DC 25 Linguistics check by someone who can read
Thassilonian) gains a +2 inherent bonus to Intelligence as well as a permanent +4 insight bonus on
Knowledge (arcana) and Spellcraft checks associated with necromancy. In addition, the pages contain
information identical to that contained in a
[flesh golem manual](ref/magic-wondrous/golem-manual-flesh) - these pages restore themselves and can
be used once per year.

A character who reads the Bone Grimoire becomes somewhat separated from the living. His flesh takes
on a pallid cast, and he is now affected by positive and negative energy as if he were undead. If
the reader previously had the ability to [channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), he
now channels negative energy. A [miracle](ref/spell/miracle) or [wish](ref/spell/wish) can remove
the effects of the Bone Grimoire, both beneficial and otherwise.

### Destruction

If the Bone Grimoire is subjected to the runewaking ritual, it is destroyed.
