**Sources:** Pathfinder \#107: Scourge of the Godclaw pg 16\
**Aura** strong abjuration, evocation, and transmutation; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** -; **Weight** 1 lb. each

Each Godclaw gauntlet is a +2 [axiomatic](ref/magic-enh/axiomatic)
[spiked gauntlet](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-gauntlet). Once per day, the left Godclaw gauntlet can be
used to cast a [quickened](ref/feat/quicken-spell) [order's wrath](ref/spell/orders-wrath) spell,
while the right Godclaw gauntlet can be used to cast a quickened [dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal)
spell.

If a character wears both Godclaw gauntlets, he gains the benefit of
[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) while attacking with the gauntlets (but not with
other weapons he might wield). In addition, once per day, the wearer can request the aid of one of
five deities and cast a specific spell, as detailed below. Note that calling upon the aid of a deity
of a different alignment than your own or the aid of a deity other than the one you worship (if you
are a divine spellcaster who worships a specific deity) may have alignment- or class-based
repercussions.

> **Abadar:** The wearer gains the benefit of [discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies).
>
> **Asmodeus:** The wearer creates a [wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire).
>
> **Iomedae:** The wearer gains the benefit of [divine power](ref/spell/divine-power).
>
> **Irori:** The wearer gains the benefit of [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement).
>
> **Torag:** The wearer gains the benefit of [stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin).

### Destruction

A Godclaw gauntlet must be brought to the Worldwound or the Abyss by a
[Hellknight](ref/rule/aff-lawknights) [of the Godclaw](ref/hkorder/order-of-the-godclaw), who must
then allow a demon of at least CR 15 or higher to bite off and consume both gauntlet and hand alike.
