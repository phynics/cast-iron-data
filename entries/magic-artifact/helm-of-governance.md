**Sources:** Pathfinder \#126: Beyond the Veiled Past pg 55\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** head; **Price** -; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This helm masks the wearer's face and functions as a
[helm of telepathy](ref/magic-wondrous/helm-of-telepathy) combined with an
[amulet of proof against detection and location](ref/magic-wondrous/amulet-of-proof-against-detection-and-location).
The wearer can cast [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self) at will and benefits from
[discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies) and [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) at all times. The
helm provides complete protection from all [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attacks and all forms of mental
control. The helm also interfaces with illusory effects, allowing the wearer to see and hear through
certain illusions as though those illusions acted as scrying sensors. The helm is currently attuned
to numerous projection pads depicting Jazradan's programmed image, scattered throughout the ruins of
Azlant.

Once per day as a swift action, the wearer of the helm of governance can utter the Azlanti word for
"stop," which causes a [time stop](ref/spell/time-stop) effect. Similarly, up to three times per day
as a swift action, the wearer can say "insufficient" in Azlanti, which targets a creature within 300
feet with [greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater).

### Destruction

The helm is forever tied to its wearer, even unto death. Only by killing the helm's owner, Grand
Arcanist Jazradan, can this artifact be destroyed.
