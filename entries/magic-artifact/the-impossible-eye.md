**Sources:** Pathfinder \#23: The Impossible Eye pg 51\
**Aura** overwhelming divination; **CL** 21st\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 3 lbs.

The Impossible Eye grants a number of potent powers of divination. In order to activate any of these
powers, the user must look into the mirror and make a DC 20 Will save; failure indicates that the
user becomes [fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) by his reflection until an outside force breaks his
gaze. Left alone, a fascinated viewer doesn't eat, drink, move, or sleep, and eventually dies of
thirst. If the Will save is successful, the viewer can activate any of the mirror's powers as a
standard action.

**5/day:** [vision](ref/spell/vision) 3/day: [greater prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes-greater),
[greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater) 1/day:
[contact other plane](ref/spell/contact-other-plane),
[discern location](ref/spell/discern-location), [foresight](ref/spell/foresight)

The Impossible Eye can also be used to read any nonmagical language - any written message reflected
in the mirror appears in unmirrored text written in the holder's native tongue. The mirror can be
used to safely peruse the text of the Codex of Infinite Planes in this manner.

### Destruction

The Impossible Eye was crafted in a secret foundry somewhere in the Plane of Fire - it may only be
destroyed by returning it to this foundry and smashing it against the oven in which its glass was
created.
