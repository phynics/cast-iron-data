**Sources:** Mythic Realms pg 52\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 12 lbs.

Carved long ago from the ice at the heart of an ancient glacier and infused with a cold and
unstoppable drive, this +5 [icy burst](ref/magic-enh/icy-burst) [quenching](ref/magic-enh/quenching)
[bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword) changes size to match its wielder.
[Spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) used by Winter's Heart receive the benefits of
[Greater Spell Focus](ref/feat/greater-spell-focus) (evocation).

As a standard action, the wielder of Winter's Heart can dispel fire effects as a
[frost brand](ref/magic-weapon/frost-brand). Alternatively, as a standard action, the wielder can
quench a Huge or smaller nonmagical fire by touching it with the sword.

The sword commands the following spell-like abilities: 3/day-[heroism](ref/spell/heroism) (on its
wielder only) 1/day-[empowered](ref/feat/empower-spell) [cone of cold](ref/spell/cone-of-cold) (DC
21), [maximized](ref/feat/maximize-spell) [widened](ref/feat/widen-spell)
[detonate](ref/spell/detonate) (cold only, DC 20), [word of recall](ref/spell/word-of-recall)

The DC for _[detonate](ref/spell/detonate)_ is determined as if it were an 8th-level spell. If
_[detonate](ref/spell/detonate)_ is cast on a creature with the cold subtype, it also acts as
[heal](ref/spell/heal); if cast on a creature with the fire subtype, it also acts as
[harm](ref/spell/harm).

Winter's Heart has the special purpose of uniting the lands of the north under one iron rule. When
pursuing this purpose, it surrounds its wielder in icy armor that combines the protection of
[stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin) and [fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield) (cold flames). If the
protection of the stoneskin is exhausted, both protections are lost and the sword can't reestablish
them for 10 minutes.

### Destruction

Winter's Heart can be destroyed only if it's hurled into the center of a cataclysmic volcanic
eruption. Even a normal volcano's heat is insufficient to melt the blade, and Immersing it in magma
merely solidifies the magma into rock.
