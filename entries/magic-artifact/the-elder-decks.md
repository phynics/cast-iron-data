**Sources:** Classic Treasures Revisited pg 19\
**Aura** strong (all schools); **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** -

These ancient artifacts function as [decks of many things](ref/magic-artifact/deck-of-many-things),
save that they have only 13 cards: Euryale, Flames, Jester, Key, Knight, Moon, Rogue, Ruin, Skull,
Star, Sun, Throne, and The Void.

### Destruction

An elder deck can be destroyed by losing it in a wager with a deity of law. The deity must be
unaware of the nature of the deck.
