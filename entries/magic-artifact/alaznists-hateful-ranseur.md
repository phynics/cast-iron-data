**Sources:** Pathfinder \#138: Rise of New Thassilon pg 70\
**Aura** strong evocation \[evil\]; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 12 lbs.

Alaznist's Hateful Ranseur is a +3 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen)
[spell-storing](ref/magic-enh/spell-storing) [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding)
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) [ranseur](ref/eq-weapon/ranseur) that bears a charred human
skull - a representation of Runelord Alderpash - impaled upon its blade, though this does not impede
its use as a weapon. The ranseur grants the wielder a +2 luck bonus on attacks, saves, and ability
and skill checks. On a critical hit, Alaznist's Hateful Ranseur deals 1d4 points of damage to the
target's Charisma, Intelligence, or Wisdom - whichever score is the highest. The weapon tirelessly
threatens destruction and vengeance upon any wielder who is not a rightful ruler of Bakrakhan, given
its purpose of defeating all others than its wielder. The ranseur can cast
[cure serious wounds](ref/spell/cure-serious-wounds) and
[protection from energy](ref/spell/protection-from-energy) on its wielder each three times per day,
and it can cast [lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt) three times per day.

### Destruction

Alaznist's Hateful Ranseur can be destroyed if it is hurled under the crushing feet of the
[Oliphaunt of Jandelay](ref/monster/oliphaunt-of-jandelay), but only if there is no current Runelord
of Wrath living in the world.

### Statistics

**Alignment** CE; **Ego** 24\
**Senses** [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 120 ft., hearing\
**Int** 17, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 17\
**Communication** read languages, speech, telepathy (Abyssal, Azlanti, Thassilonian)
