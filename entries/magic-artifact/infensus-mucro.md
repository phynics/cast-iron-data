**Sources:** Tomb of the Iron Medusa pg 13\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** -

This elegantly wrought +3 [speed](ref/magic-enh/speed) [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) remains
pristine despite having been sheathed in a submerged corpse's chest cavity for over 200 years. Given
to Bartolomae by an agent of [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) in return for the warlord's (initially)
secret allegiance to the arch fiend, this weapon carries within it the cruel soul of a long-dead
high priest of Asmodeus. The weapon begins in a state of dormancy, at which point all of its
intelligent weapon powers are unavailable, and it functions as nothing more than a +3 speed
longsword. See the description of area L4 for details on how the sword can be awakened.

### Destruction

To destroy the weapon, you must immerse it in a bath of Asmodeus's tears.

### Statistics

**Alignment** LE; **Ego** 33\
**Int** 18, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 20\
**Communication** empathy\
**Powers** [bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse) 3/day (DC 19),
[spell immunity](ref/spell/spell-immunity) 1/day (CL 17th)\
**Dedicated Powers** wielder gains fire immunity and the ability to cast
[repulsion](ref/spell/repulsion) 1/day (CL 17th)\
**[Special Purpose](ref/rule/special-purpose-items)** defend the servants and interests of Asmodeus
