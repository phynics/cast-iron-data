**Sources:** Bestiary 6 pg 29\
**Aura** overwhelming all; **CL** 30th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 1 lb.

This [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) quill pen is the greatest weapon
[Mephistopheles](ref/faith/mephistopheles) has in the corruption of souls. As a full-round action
that does not provoke attacks of opportunity, he can use it to inscribe a
[magic circle against good](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-good) (or chaos), place a
[mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice), or pen an
[infernal contract](ref/rule/infernal-contracts). These contracts can grant any number of boons, but
typically do so in the form of a [wish](ref/spell/wish) spell or the
[devilbound](ref/template/devilbound) template.

Visineir can be wielded as a +1 adamantine [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen)
[wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger), but Mephistopheles rarely does
so, preferring his [trident](ref/eq-weapon/trident) in battle. The quill can store a sample of blood
from every creature it strikes, storing up to 33 blood samples at a time. The quill gains the
[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) special ability against any creature whose blood it stores, and it marks
the target as if Mephistopheles had used a witch's [scar](ref/hex/scar) hex on it, allowing him to
use his [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) on that creature with a range of 1 mile
(or with unlimited range, if the creature is in Caina). Mephistopheles can expend one stored blood
sample as a full-round action to inscribe a symbol spell of any type he wishes, or to call the
creature to him as if he had cast [greater planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding-greater)
(regardless of the creature's actual type - this is not limited to calling outsiders).

### Destruction

If Visineir is used to pen a contract that results in [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) having to void
the creation of Mephistopheles, the quill crumbles away to ashes, as does Mephistopheles himself.
