**Sources:** Pathfinder \#40: Vaults of Madness pg 34\
**Aura** strong conjuration (healing); **CL** 18th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 2,000 lbs.

This beautifully carved stone fountain is decorated with figures of dancing fey creatures and holds
pure, shimmering water that glows with silvery light. The moon-goddess [Acavna](ref/faith/acavna)
gave the Argental Font to the city of Saventh-Yhi at its founding as a gift in gratitude for the
heroine Savith's valor. Anyone drinking from the fountain enjoys the combined benefits of
[greater restoration](ref/spell/restoration-greater) and [heal](ref/spell/heal), and is immediately
cured of all afflictions, including [ability damage or drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain),
[negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels), and insanities. The waters cannot
return the dead to life, though an undead creature immersed in the fountain is immediately destroyed
(no save). The fountain never runs dry, no matter how many times it is used or how much water is
taken from it, but the water retains its potency for only 1 round after being removed from the
fountain, becoming normal water thereafter. The font can only be moved from its location with a
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle) or [wish](ref/spell/wish) spell. An individual creature can only
benefit from the effects of the Argental Font once per year.

### Destruction

If a good celestial creature of at least 10 Hit Dice ever willingly commits suicide while in the
font's waters, the fountain ceases to flow and its waters lose all special properties.
