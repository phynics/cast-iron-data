**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 5 lbs.

The Morrowfall is one-half of the Aohl, an artifact gifted to the ancient Jaytirian Society
following a sacred truce between their two feuding gods of millennia past. The Morrowfall is the
remaining manifestation of the power of [Easivra](ref/faith/easivra), a potent sun god now all but
forgotten (it's a DC 30 Knowledge \[religion\] check to learn Easivra's name, but any good-aligned
creature that touches the Morrowfall immediately knows the name and how to use the artifact's
powers). The Morrowfall constantly emits [daylight](ref/spell/daylight), as per the spell. In
addition, by presenting it strongly and uttering Easivra's name (a standard action), the Morrowfall
can be used to cast the following spells.

> _At will_ - [detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead), [disrupt undead](ref/spell/disrupt-undead),
> [daze monster](ref/spell/daze-monster) (DC 13)\
> _3/day_ - [blindness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness) (DC 13), _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight),_
> [searing light](ref/spell/searing-light)\
> _1/day_ - [sunbeam](ref/spell/sunbeam) (DC 20), [sunburst](ref/spell/sunburst) (DC 22)

### Destruction

When the Morrowfall is joined with its counterpart, [the Totemrix](ref/magic-artifact/the-totemrix),
its powers (as well as those of the Totemrix) are suppressed. In this state, both it and the
Totemrix can be destroyed by anything capable of destroying a magic metal object of its size.
