**Sources:** The House on Hook Street pg 30\
**Aura** strong (all schools); **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 6,000 lbs.

Mog-Lathar is a well-preserved fossil of a gigantic [Leng spider](ref/monster/leng-spider) that has
achieved sentience. Since its discovery in the Mindspin Mountains and subsequent arrival in
Bridgefront, the petrified [idol's](ref/rule/idols) cult has grown to over 300 supplicants. The
idol's holy days fall on the vernal and autumnal equinoxes, as well as on the northern solstice.
Sacrifices are typically in the form of fine goods and deeply intoxicated, lucidly dreaming
humanoids.

Despite its imposing petrified bulk, the fossil is relatively brittle for a stone object of its
size, with [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 8, 78 hp, and break DC 35.

The Huge idol can animate at will, as the spell [animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), and
can grant divine spells to worshipers up to 5th level with access to the Chaos, Evil, and Trickery
domains as a result of selecting the _divine source_ [idol ability](ref/rule/idol-abilities) twice.
Its _immortal promise_ ability grants a limited immortality to up to 20 favored worshipers. They do
not age; do not need to eat, drink, or breathe; and do not suffer ill effects from extreme heat or
cold.

### Destruction

Mog-Lathar must be physically transported to the [Dimension of Dreams](ref/rule/dimension-of-dreams)
and encased in the webbing of its Leng spider brethren. Once cocooned, a single blow from a silver
hammer will turn the idol to dust.
