**Sources:** Pathfinder \#78: City of Locusts pg 62\
**Aura** strong conjuration and necromancy (evil); **CL** 20th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** -; **Weight** 2 lbs.

A gift to Areelu from [Deskari](ref/faith/deskari) himself, Deskari's Tooth is a +6
[anarchic](ref/magic-enh/anarchic) [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger)
that automatically casts [soul bind](ref/spell/soul-bind) whenever it is used to slay a foe,
provided no soul is currently stored in the blade. A soul of any power can be stored in the blade in
this manner, but only one soul at a time. If the dagger's wielder is slain while a soul is contained
in Deskari's Tooth, the soul is instantly consumed and the effect or attack that would normally have
slain the wielder is instead negated. If Deskari's Tooth is used to perform a
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) on a lawful good creature, it can be used to cast
[lesser planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding-lesser). If the lawful good creature has more than
11 Hit Dice, the dagger can instead be used to cast [planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), and
if the creature has more than 17 Hit Dice, it can instead be used to cast
[greater planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding-greater).

### Destruction

Deskari's Tooth must be used by a [paladin](ref/class/paladin) to commit suicide, so that the
paladin's soul becomes bound to the blade. If that soul is later used by a [demon](ref/family/demon)
to prevent death, the dagger shatters upon preventing that death.
