**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** -

A radiant blue sphere ioun stone grants a +4 enhancement bonus to Wisdom while active.

### Destruction

This stone can be destroyed like any other [ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone), except it
has AC 26, 50 hit points, and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10.
