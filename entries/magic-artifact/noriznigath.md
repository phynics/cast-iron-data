**Aura** strong evocation (evil); **CL** 20th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** -; **Weight** 10 lbs.

The favored blade of the Storm King, Noriznigath is named for the first nascent demon lord it was
used to behead, and is a Large +6 [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy)
[longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) - as with all slashing weapons, Noriznigath becomes a
[vorpal](ref/magic-enh/vorpal) weapon in the hands of a [balor](ref/monster/demon-balor). The body
of a creature decapitated by Noriznigath is disintegrated if the decapitation results in death, and
heals the wielder of 100 points of damage.

### Destruction

Noriznigath melts into blood and mist if its namesake, the long-dead nascent demon lord Noriznigath,
is restored to life.
