**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 339, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 548, Artifacts and Legends pg 46

**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 1 lb.

This artifact was crafted ages ago, weaving together wispy strands of shadow into a twisted black
staff. The Shadowstaff makes the wielder slightly shadowy and incorporeal, granting him a +4 bonus
to AC and on Reflex saves (which stacks with any other bonuses). However, in bright light (such as
that of the sun, but not a torch) or in absolute darkness, the wielder takes a -2 penalty on all
attack rolls, saves, and checks. The Shadowstaff also has these powers.

- **Summon Shadows:** Three times per day the staff may summon 2d4 [shadows](ref/monster/shadow).
  Immune to turning, they serve the wielder as if called by a
  [summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5) spell cast at 20th level.
- **Summon Nightshade:** Once per month, the staff can summon an [advanced](ref/template/advanced)
  [shadow demon](ref/monster/demon-shadow-demon) that serves the wielder as if called by a
  [summon monster IX](ref/spell/summon-monster-9) spell cast at 20th level.
- **Shadow Form:** Three times per day the wielder can become a living shadow, with all the movement
  powers granted by [gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form).
- **Shadow Bolt:** Three times per day the staff can project a ray attack that deals 10d6 points of
  cold damage to a single target. The shadow bolt has a range of 100 feet.

### Destruction

The Shadowstaff fades away to nothingness if it is exposed to true sunlight for a continuous 24 hour
period.
