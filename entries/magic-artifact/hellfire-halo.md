**Sources:** Pathfinder \#107: Scourge of the Godclaw pg 36\
**Aura** strong abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 16th\
**Slot** head; **Price** -; **Weight** 1 lb.

This tarnished ring looks like a fiery halo set ablaze in evil-looking fire. The flames are harmless
and shed light as an [everburning torch](ref/eq-misc/everburning-torch); the wearer can suppress or
activate these flames as a swift action. A hellfire halo functions as a
[headband of mental superiority +2](ref/magic-wondrous/headband-of-mental-superiority-2) that grants
ranks in [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate). If the flames of the halo are burning, the wearer
becomes immune to the effects of an [archon's](ref/family/archon) aura of menace and ignores the
protective aura of any [angel](ref/family/angel) he attacks. In addition, up to three times per day
as he strikes any creature under the effects of a [holy aura](ref/spell/holy-aura), the wearer can
attempt to dispel that effect as if casting a [quickened](ref/feat/quicken-spell)
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) (CL 16th). The first time each day that the wearer is
attacked by a creature using smite good, that smite effect immediately ends and is wasted, and the
creature making the attack must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered)
for 1d4 rounds.

### Destruction

A hellfire halo can be destroyed by immersing it in [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water) and then
casting [dispel evil](ref/spell/dispel-evil) on it.
