**Sources:** Classic Treasures Revisited pg 44\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** -

This sphere acts as a normal [sphere of annihilation](ref/magic-artifact/sphere-of-annihilation) in
all respects, save that any use of a [gate](ref/spell/gate) spell to destroy it results in an
automatic planar rift to a plane of the GM's choosing. The sphere remains behind at its original
location, intact and unaffected.

### Destruction

A rift-born sphere can only be destroyed by using a
[rod of cancellation](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-cancellation) or fusion with another, normal _sphere_
(which destroys both spheres in a 180-foot blast dealing 5d6 × 10 points of damage).
