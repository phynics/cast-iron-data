**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This stone tablet bears a carving in the shape of a twig with five branches. There are only a
limited number of Elder Signs in existence, so each time one of these artifacts is destroyed, the
universe loses one of the few things that can reliably keep cosmic horrors at bay - and inevitable
doom draws closer.

A creature holding an Elder Sign can cast [banishment](ref/spell/banishment),
[forbiddance](ref/spell/forbiddance), and [greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater) at
will by reciting occult mantras, though all three effects work only against creatures and magic
related to the Elder Mythos, including any of the following creatures (and other cosmic
horror-related creatures, at the GM's discretion): [bhole](ref/monster/bhole),
[colour out of space](ref/monster/colour-out-of-space), [deep one](ref/monster/deep-one),
[elder deep one](ref/monster/deep-one-elder-deep-one),
[denizen of Leng](ref/monster/denizen-of-leng), [elder thing](ref/monster/elder-thing),
[flying polyp](ref/monster/flying-polyp), [gug](ref/monster/gug),
[hound of Tindalos](ref/monster/hound-of-tindalos), [Leng ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul-leng-ghoul),
[Leng spider](ref/monster/leng-spider), [mi-go](ref/monster/mi-go),
[nightgaunt](ref/monster/nightgaunt), [ratling](ref/monster/ratling),
[shantak](ref/monster/shantak), [shoggoth](ref/monster/shoggoth),
[spawn of Yog-Sothoth](ref/monster/spawn-of-yog-sothoth),
[star-spawn of Cthulhu](ref/monster/star-spawn-of-cthulhu), [voonith](ref/monster/voonith),
[wendigo](ref/monster/wendigo), and [yithian](ref/monster/yithian). Casting
_[forbiddance](ref/spell/forbiddance)_ this way doesn't require a costly material component, but if
the same Elder Sign is used to cast _[forbiddance](ref/spell/forbiddance)_ again, the artifact's
connection to a previous location warded by the spell is severed, ending the first spell's effects
immediately. Casting _[banishment](ref/spell/banishment)_ on one of the listed creatures works even
if the creature isn't extraplanar; in that case, it sends the creature to the place on the Material
Plane from which it originated.

An Elder Sign can also banish a [Great Old One](ref/family/great-old-one) (no save), but doing so
causes the artifact to be consumed in a bright flame.

### Destruction

Elder Signs are immune to damage, and the only certain way to destroy one of these artifacts is to
banish a Great Old One with it.
