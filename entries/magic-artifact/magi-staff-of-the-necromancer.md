**Sources:** Classic Treasures Revisited pg 49\
**Aura** strong (all schools); **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This long staff of smooth, pale bone is bent in a swaying shape, like a crone's back, and is carved
with ancient symbols of death and spirits, with the writing standing out in stark black from a thick
paste of human ash rubbed into it. Any good creature who holds or carries the staff gains two
permanent [negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) (similar to holding an
[unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) weapon). The staff's powers (and the equivalent
[staff of the magi](ref/magic-artifact/staff-of-the-magi) powers they replace) are:

- [detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead) (_[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic),_ 0 charges)
- [disrupt undead](ref/spell/disrupt-undead) (_[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person),_ 0
  charges)
- [spectral hand](ref/spell/spectral-hand) (_[knock](ref/spell/knock),_ 1 charge)
- [vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch) (_[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt),_ 1
  charge)
- [animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead) (_[wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire),_ 1 charge)
- [finger of death](ref/spell/finger-of-death) (_[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift),_ 2 charges)
- [energy drain](ref/spell/energy-drain) (_[summon monster IX](ref/spell/summon-monster-9),_ 2
  charges)

The staff otherwise has the normal powers and abilities of a _staff of the magi._ If broken in a
retributive strike, the bearer has a 25% chance to be transported to the Negative Energy Plane, a
50% chance to be transported to a random plane, and a 25% chance to be utterly destroyed. Rumor has
it that the staff contains a hollow space to hold a [lich's](ref/template/lich) phylactery, though
arcane scholars point out that no sane lich would keep his life essence in a magic item that
explodes when broken.

### Destruction

This item can be destroyed in the same manner as a normal _staff of the magi._ It is also destroyed
immediately if a good cleric casts heal on the staff while on the Positive Energy Plane.
