**Sources:** Artifacts and Legends pg 58, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 51\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** head; **Price** -; **Weight** 3 lbs.

The members of the [Pathfinder Society's](ref/rule/aff-scholars) administrative body, the
Decemvirate, retain their anonymity by wearing closefaced, magically empowered helmets while
fulfilling their official roles. The helms not only mask the wearers' faces, but also function as
[helms of telepathy](ref/magic-wondrous/helm-of-telepathy) combined with
[amulets of proof against detection and location](ref/magic-wondrous/amulet-of-proof-against-detection-and-location).
The wearer of a Decemvirate helm can use [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self) at will and
benefits from [discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies) and [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) at all
times. The helm provides 100% protection from all [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attacks and all forms of
magical control. Specific Decemvirate helms may have additional powers and all are said to be unique
in physical appearance.

### Destruction

Whatever their original purpose, Decemvirate helms seem designed to function as mantles of office.
If one of these helms is worn by five or more individuals in one 24-hour period, it ceases to
function for 1 day. If it is again worn by five or more individuals within 24 hours of regaining its
properties, the helm permanently loses its magic properties.
