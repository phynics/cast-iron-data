**Sources:** Hell Unleashed pg 39\
**Aura** overwhelming varied; **CL** 28th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 24 lbs.

[Furcas's](ref/faith/furcas) weapon is a +4 [axiomatic](ref/magic-enh/axiomatic)
[flaming burst](ref/magic-enh/flaming-burst) [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy)
[trident](ref/eq-weapon/trident). The weapon's flaming burst special ability wreathes the trident in
hellfire - half the damage its flames deal is fire damage, while the other half results directly
from infernal power and is therefore not reduced by fire resistance. As a standard action, Furcas
can slam the trident's haft against the ground, causing all creatures within 30 feet to be affected
by [dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor). Any affected creature that attempts to
teleport or travel to another plane (even by a permanent [gate](ref/spell/gate)) instead appears in
a square adjacent to Furcas. As a standard action, Furcas can call the trident to his hand from any
distance, even across planar boundaries.

### Destruction

The Avernus Claw must be sunk to the bottom of the deepest sea of Elysium for 1 year, then dealt a
blow by the weapon of a chaotic good deity.
