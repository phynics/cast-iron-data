**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 333, Advanced Player's Guide pg 314

**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** -

The spindle of perfect knowledge is large, bright, and flawless marquise-cut diamond
[ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone) the size of a halfling's fist. The gemstone would be a
treasure in itself if it lacked magic, but it is also a powerful ioun stone. The spindle of perfect
knowledge functions like a
[headband of mental superiority +4](ref/magic-wondrous/headband-of-mental-superiority-4) and grants
5 ranks in two random Knowledge skills. The wearer can communicate and understand languages as if
using [comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages), [tongues](ref/spell/tongues), and
[telepathy](ref/spell/telepathy) with a range of 100 feet.

### Destruction

The spindle of perfect knowledge is destroyed if worn by a mindless creature for an entire year.
