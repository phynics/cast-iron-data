**Sources:** Bestiary 6 pg 25\
**Aura** overwhelming all; **CL** 30th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This horn can be sounded three times per day as a swift or move action to function as a
[greater horn of blasting](ref/magic-wondrous/horn-of-blasting-greater) with no chance of exploding
and an effect duration of 1 minute. Creatures [deafened](ref/misc/deafened) by the horn hear
whispers and falsehoods echoing in their minds, twisting their perceptions; they treat all other
creatures as if they had [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) and take a -4 penalty on saves against
mind-affecting effects as long as they remain deaf. In addition, any competence or insight bonuses
are treated as penalties instead until they regain their hearing.

The horn can be sounded three additional times per day to grant allies within 60 feet a +4 bonus on
saves against divine spells, while enemies within 60 feet become unable to tell the truth (Will DC
25 negates). This includes verbal, written, and any other form of communication, but does not
prevent saying things that are neither true nor untrue, such as commands, questions, and verbal
components of spells. While compelled to falsehood, [Geryon's](ref/faith/geryon) enemies also become
paranoid and treat no one as their ally; thus, they are unable to provide or benefit from
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking), teamwork feats, or the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action,
and always attempt to save against harmless effects. Affected creatures are unable to cast divine
spells or activate domain powers unless they worship an archdevil. This effect lasts 1 minute. This
is a sonic mind-affecting curse effect.

### Destruction

If the Horn of Lies is sounded by a chaotic good divine spellcaster within the Silent Cairns of
[Elysium](ref/rule/elysium), it shatters into 13 fragments, each of which transforms into a
serpentine monster. If these 13 serpents are slain within 24 hours, the artifact is destroyed - but
if even one serpent remains alive, the Horn of Lies is restored and returned to
[Asmodeus's](ref/faith/asmodeus) keeping in [Hell](ref/rule/hell).
