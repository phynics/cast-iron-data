**Sources:** Artifacts and Legends pg 17\
**Aura** strong conjuration, necromancy, transmutation; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 6 lbs.

The blade called Briar is an intelligent +5 [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron)
[vorpal](ref/magic-enh/vorpal) [bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword) whose special purpose is
to defeat denizens of [the First World](ref/rule/the-first-world).

### Destruction

If Briar is claimed by the nymph Nyrissa - of whom it is fundamentally a part - the blade gradually
begins rejoining with the fey queen. If left in Nyrissa's possession, Briar fades out of existence
over the course of a week. At the end of this time, the weapon is destroyed and Nyrissa regains her
ability to love. Only one of the fey lords known as the [Eldest](ref/rule/the-eldest) can restore
Briar once Nyrissa has reclaimed it.

### Statistics

**Alignment** CN; **Ego** 23\
**Senses** 120 ft., [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)\
**Int** 10, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 16\
**Communication** empathy, speech, telepathy\
**Languages** Aklo, Common, Sylvan\
**Powers** 3/day - [barkskin](ref/spell/barkskin) (wielder only), [blight](ref/spell/blight) (as
swift action upon a creature it strikes in combat)\
1/month - [true resurrection](ref/spell/true-resurrection) (wielder only)
