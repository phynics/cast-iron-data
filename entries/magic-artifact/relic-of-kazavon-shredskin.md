**Aura** strong divination and illusion; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** -; **Weight** 20 lbs.

Kazavon's shattered ribs were twisted into a mantle of bone fragments known as the Shredskin. The
mantle grants its wearer a +6 enhancement bonus to Wisdom, a +5 resistance bonus on all saving
throws, and the ability to hide in plain sight as per the [shadowdancer](ref/pclass/shadowdancer)
class feature. The wearer can speak with any shadow, in a way similar to the effects of the spell
[stone tell](ref/spell/stone-tell). Shadows willingly tell the wearer what dangers they obscure and
what creatures have come in contact with them in the past 24 hours. The shadows can only relate
information about creatures they've enshrouded. Creatures bearing a light source are not usually
touched by shadows, though shadows can tell when they've been disrupted by passing light.

### Destruction

The Shredskin must be taken to the Positive Energy Plane or [Sarenrae's](ref/faith/sarenrae) domain
on [Nirvana](ref/rule/nirvana) and exposed to direct light for 1 month.
