**Aura** strong divination and enchantment; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +4 [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding) [throwing](ref/magic-enh/throwing)
[longspear](ref/eq-weapon/longspear) has a 2-foot-long iron spearhead covered with over 30 cruel
flesh barbs. On a critical hit, the barbs lodge in the victim's flesh and can be removed only by
tearing the blade free. Yanking the blade free is a full-round action that requires a successful
Strength check with a DC equal to the attack roll that struck the critical hit, and deals 3d6 points
of damage to the victim.

In the hands of a mythic creature, the spear gains the [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) special
ability, and when thrown seemingly comes alive, screaming wickedly as it flies. When it hits a
creature, it radiates [fear](ref/spell/fear) (as the spell) in a 60-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) surrounding the creature struck.

Each use of mythic power the wielder expends when throwing the spear increases the spear's throwing
range by 100 feet.

### Destruction

A screaming spear of the sun disintegrates if thrown into the flaming heart of a sun.
