**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 268\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 1 lb.

Its origin lost in the infinite expanse of time, a primordial symbol is a polished stone bearing a
mystic sign that resembles a lopsided, two-barred cross. When held forth and invoked by a bearer who
is able to cast psychic spells or has the [Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) feat,
a primordial symbol flares with an invisible flash in a 100-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst),
which has the following effects on those in range.

- Creatures with 40 Hit Dice or fewer with the extraplanar subtype are returned to their home planes
  as though by the [banishment](ref/spell/banishment) spell, regardless of
  [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) and with no save permitted. This includes
  extraplanar entities possessing other creatures.
- Any non-extraplanar entities possessing other creatures or extraplanar entities with more than 40
  Hit Dice possessing other creatures are driven out of their hosts.
- All eidolons, medium spirits, and phantoms are [dismissed](ref/spell/dismissal).
- All psychic spells in the area are dispelled as though by
  [greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater), with no caster level check required.
- All abjuration effects and [magic circles](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-evil) in the area are
  similarly dispelled.
- All creatures are [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round (Reflex DC 22 negates).

Other creatures and objects within the area are otherwise unharmed. When the symbol is activated,
its wielder takes 2d6 points of
[Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), rolled separately for
each ability. The primordial symbol then collapses in on itself and vanishes, only to reappear
unharmed in a distant land or world.

### Destruction

Though seemingly destroyed by activation, the eldritch potential bound into a primordial symbol
persists and recreates the symbol elsewhere in the multiverse. To permanently destroy the artifact,
the primordial symbol must be brought to a realm of pure chaos at the unformed edges of the Outer
Planes and melted in the primal energy of creation.
