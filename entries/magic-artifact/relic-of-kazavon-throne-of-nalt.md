**Sources:** Artifacts and Legends pg 38, Pathfinder \#12: Crown of Fangs pg 66\
**Aura** strong divination and enchantment; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 3,475 lbs.

Great horns of gold, a massive sea serpent's skull, and the wings of Kazavon form the ornate Throne
of Nalt. The throne grants whoever is seated upon it a +6 enhancement bonus to Charisma and Wisdom.
Once per day, the user can cast [geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest). Any creature with the evil
subtype takes a -6 penalty on saving throws to resist this effect.

### Destruction

The Throne of Nalt crumbles to dust if a person who wants nothing sits upon it and bequeaths all he
has to another.
