**Sources:** Classic Treasures Revisited pg 38\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Price** -; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This horn operates as a normal [horn of Valhalla](ref/magic-wondrous/horn-of-valhalla), save that it
can be blown more than once per day, with the warriors from previous soundings remaining until their
1 hour has passed. When the wielder blows the horn for the third time, he becomes destined to die
before the next dawn, with chance-based events turning against him in more and more improbable ways
until he's slain. The manner of this curse and any potential methods of defeating it are left to the
GM.

### Destruction

Linnorm's Lament shatters if someone blows it a third time and survives for 1 year afterward.
