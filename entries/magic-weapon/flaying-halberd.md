**Sources:** Pathfinder \#64: Beyond the Doomsday Door pg 61\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 24,310 gp; **Weight** 12 lbs.

A flaying halberd is a +1 [halberd](ref/eq-weapon/halberd) that can be used to slice off large
swaths of a creature's skin. In combat, a character can denote a maximum of one attack per round to
be a flaying strike. This attack deals normal damage, but the resulting wound is particularly
painful as skin is torn away from the target's body. This causes an additional 1d6 points of
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage, and as long as a victim suffers from this bleed damage, it is
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered). A successful DC 15 Fortitude save made at the time the flaying
strike occurs negates the bleed damage and staggered condition. The bleed effect can be stopped by a
successful DC 15 [Heal](ref/skill/heal) check or any spell that cures hit point damage. A flaying
strike that results in a critical hit also deals 1d4 points of Charisma damage (no save) to the
victim. Creatures immune to [ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) and critical hits are
immune to flaying strikes.
