**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 254\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,970 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This wicked-looking +1 [greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword) is engraved with glowing Ulfen runes
that spell out a Gorumite battle song. When wielded, the blade sheds light as a
[candle](ref/eq-misc/candle). Once per day, the wielder can use _rage_ (self only). The runes shine
with a murderous red light, equivalent to a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch), whenever the _rage_ ability
is active and for 1 round whenever the weapon is used to deal damage to a living creature.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[rage](ref/spell/rage); **Price** 2,160 gp
