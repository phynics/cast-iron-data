**Sources:** Pathfinder \#34: Blood for Blood pg 66\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 50,302 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This +2 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) [spear](ref/eq-weapon/spear)
is adorned with feathers and fur, as well as with a bright steel chain that trails down from the top
of its haft.

On a critical hit, Chada's harpoon lodges into the target's flesh or armor, and the wielder can
choose to _[pull](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/pull)_ the target toward him,
reeling the victim in by use of the magically extended chain. This functions as the
[pull](ref/umr/pull) universal monster ability with the following exceptions. Compare the initial
attack roll (instead of a new CMB check) to the target's CMD to determine success. Success pulls the
target 20 feet closer to the wielder. The wielder can make additional attack rolls each round
(instead of new CMB checks) to pull the victim closer. Unlike the normal pull ability, Chada's
harpoon can pull creatures of any size. Once the target is pulled adjacent to the wielder, the bull
rush ends.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Price** 25,302 gp
