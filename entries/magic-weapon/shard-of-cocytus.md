**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 49\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** 16,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Appearing as a bloodstained icicle with a worn leather handle, this +1 [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost)
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) is impervious to both cold and fire. Once per day, the dagger can
blast forth a ray of hellfrost at any target within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack, dealing 4d6
points of damage. This hellfrost deals half cold damage and half damage from unholy energy. Evil
creatures and creatures with the evil subtype take no damage from this unholy energy; good creatures
and those with the good subtype take double the usual damage.
[Protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) and similar effects protect against the
damage from the ray's unholy energy. If the shard of Cocytus is used to deliver a
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) against a good creature, it recovers its ray of hellfrost ability
for the day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[unholy ice](ref/spell/unholy-ice); **Price** 8,302 gp
