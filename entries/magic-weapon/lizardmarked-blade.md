**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 15\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,715 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

The pommel of this +1 [shock](ref/magic-enh/shock) [gladius](ref/eq-weapon/gladius) is shaped like
the head of a lightning-breathing [linnorm](ref/family/linnorm). The wielder automatically detects
any electrical discharges within 100 feet, and as a move action can concentrate on a creature within
60 feet to determine whether it has a resistance or immunity to electricity damage.

Once per day, the wielder can speak the lizardmarked blade's command word to cause the weapon to
deal damage as if it's [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) than it actually
is (effectively increasing the gladius's damage die from 1d6 to 1d8). This lasts for 10 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[lead blades](ref/spell/lead-blades), [lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt); **Price** 8,015 gp
