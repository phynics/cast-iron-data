**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 57\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,350 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This +1 [greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword) is often found among the robot-hunters of Numeria.
Three times per day as a standard action, the wielder can envelop the blade with a razor-edged field
of force for 1 minute. Thanks to this field, the sword ignores the
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) of creatures with DR 5 or less (other than DR/epic),
and when it's used to [attack an object](ref/rule/breaking-things) or [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) a
weapon, the sword treats the item as having a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 5 less than the item's
actual hardness.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[wall of force](ref/spell/wall-of-force); **Price** 4,350 gp
