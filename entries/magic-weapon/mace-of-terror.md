**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 157, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 471\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 38,552 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This weapon usually appears to be a particularly frightening-looking iron or steel mace. On command,
this +2 [heavy mace](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-mace) causes the wielder's clothes and appearance to
transform into an illusion of darkest horror, such that living creatures in a 30-foot cone become
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked) as if by a _fear_ spell (Will DC 16 partial). Those who fail this save
take a -2 morale penalty on saving throws, and they flee from the wielder. The wielder may use this
ability up to three times per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[fear](ref/spell/fear); **Price** 19,432 gp
