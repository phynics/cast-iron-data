**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This bow appears to be a +2 [distance](ref/magic-enh/distance) [shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow),
but its curse is that it suffers a -4 cumulative penalty for each range increment beyond the first.
Furthermore, it takes an additional -4 penalty on critical confirmation rolls when firing beyond the
first range increment. This bow must be used as its wielder's primary ranged weapon and can only be
discarded after the owner is subject to a [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell or similar
effect.
