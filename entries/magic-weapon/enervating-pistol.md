**Aura** strong necromancy \[death\]; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 51,300 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

Kerdak Bonefist had this +1 [reliable](ref/magic-enh/reliable) [distance](ref/magic-enh/distance)
[pistol](ref/eq-weapon/pistol) specially crafted for him to take advantage of his unique skeletal
hand. The pistol shows signs of wear and exposure to the elements, but its walnut stock,
nickel-plated barrel, and gold accents are of fine craftsmanship, and its mechanisms still move with
precision. Ammunition fired from an enervating pistol deals an extra 1d6 points of negative energy
damage on a successful hit. On a successful critical hit, an enervating pistol bestows a
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on its target. One day after being
struck, subjects must make a DC 16 Fortitude save for each negative level or they become permanent.
An enervating pistol bestows one permanent negative level on any creature wielding it that is not
undead or does not possess an undead hand. The negative level remains as long as the weapon is in
hand and disappears when the weapon is no longer wielded. This negative level cannot be overcome in
any way (including [restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while the weapon is wielded.
