**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,925 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

A 2-foot-long blade extends from the heavy crossbar of this +1
[boar spear](ref/eq-weapon/boar-spear). Designed to catch and hold humanoid prey, a spear of
manhunting automatically resizes to match the size of its wielder when grasped. Once per day on a
successful critical hit, the wielder of a spear of manhunting can cast
_[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person)_ as an immediate action on the target hit by the attack (Will
DC 13 negates). The _[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person)_ effect immediately ends if the spear is
dropped or withdrawn, such as by making additional attacks with the weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person), [shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** 2,115 gp
