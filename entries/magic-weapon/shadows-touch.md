**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 145\
**Aura** strong illusion; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 102,962 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

Amazingly light, this ebon +2 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) fades into
near intangibility at its tip. It ignores bonuses to AC provided by deflection and force effects,
and deals 2 points of [Strength damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) on a confirmed critical hit.
By expending one use of mythic power while attacking, the wielder can strike a force effect and
disintegrate it as a touch attack. This effect also automatically dispels force effects that can be
dispelled whether or not they're subject to disintegrate.

As a standard action, the wielder can expend a use of mythic power while slashing at the air to open
up a rift to the Shadow Plane. This functions as _[shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk),_ except that
a maximum number of creatures equal to double the wielder's tier can pass through before the rift
closes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk),
[shadow weapon](ref/spell/shadow-weapon); **Price** 51,632 gp
