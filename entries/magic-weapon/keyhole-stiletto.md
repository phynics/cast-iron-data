**Sources:** Down the Blighted Path pg 62\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 23,002 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This sturdy +1 [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon)
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) can be used as a punch to
destroy the workings of a lock or other mechanical device, granting a +2 bonus to
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks for that purpose, but leaving obvious signs of
tampering. Its true usefulness becomes evident when wielded against force magic. The wielder of a
keyhole stiletto ignores armor bonuses granted by a force effect (such as the
[mage armor](ref/spell/mage-armor) spell or
[bracers of armor](ref/magic-wondrous/bracers-of-armor)). Once per day, the user may whisper a
command word to allow the stiletto to cut a single small hole in a force effect such as a
[wall of force](ref/spell/wall-of-force) or [resilient sphere](ref/spell/resilient-sphere). The hole
can be any shape, but the blade cannot cut a line longer than 3 feet or carve a circle wider than 1
foot.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate), [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Price** 13,002 gp
