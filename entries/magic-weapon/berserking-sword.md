**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This sword appears to be a +2 [greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword). However, whenever it is used
in battle, its wielder goes berserk (gaining all the benefits and drawbacks of the
[barbarian's](ref/class/barbarian) rage ability). He attacks the nearest creature and continues to
fight until unconscious or dead or until no living thing remains within 30 feet. Although many see
this sword as a cursed object, others see it as a boon.

**Requirements** +2 greatsword, any magic weapon; **Cost** -
