**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 227\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 50,340 gp; **Weight** 14 lbs.

The following weapon is generally available only to members of the indicated race or ethnicity.

The hematite head of this +1 [earth breaker](ref/eq-weapon/earth-breaker) crackles with electricity,
dealing an additional 1d6 points of electricity and 1d6 points of sonic damage on each hit. Three
times per day, after a confirmed critical hit, the wielder can trigger a great thunderclap as an
immediate action, [stunning](ref/misc/stunned) the target of the critical hit for 1 round unless
that target succeeds at a DC 16 Fortitude save.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt), [sound burst](ref/spell/sound-burst), creator must be a
Shoanti; **Price** 25,340 gp
