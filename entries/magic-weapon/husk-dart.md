**Aura** moderate conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 19,301 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This thick metal +2 [wushu dart](ref/eq-weapon/wushu-dart) has a ring at its base tied with a short
ribbon of green silk. If the wielder confirms a critical hit against a living creature with the husk
dart, the creature is stricken with an insatiable hunger, as
_[feast of ashes](ref/spell/feast-of-ashes)_ (Fortitude DC 16 negates). If the creature fails its
saving throw, the husk dart's wielder gains 2d6
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) that last up to 24 hours and is nourished as
though he had just eaten a normal meal; a wielder with the ki pool class feature also regains 1 ki
point.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[feast of ashes](ref/spell/feast-of-ashes), [lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser);
**Price** 9,801 gp
