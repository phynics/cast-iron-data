**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 30\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,805 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +1 [battle aspergillum](ref/eq-weapon/battle-aspergillum) has a dappled surface and a hollow
interior, and it's made from a corrosion-resistant alloy that allows it to contain certain
alchemical weapons. If the aspergillum is filled with one of these alchemical weapons, treat the
contents as [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), except that it deals the corresponding energy
damage: acid ([acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid) or [alkali flask](ref/eq-weapon/alkali-flask)), cold
([liquid ice](ref/eq-weapon/liquid-ice)), electricity
([bottled lightning](ref/eq-weapon/bottled-lightning)), fire
([alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire)), or sonic
([burst jar](ref/eq-weapon/burst-jar)). If the wielder is an alchemist, the aspergillum can function
in this way an additional number of attacks equal to his Intelligence bonus before it needs to be
refilled.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy); **Price** 1,402 gp
