**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 13,308 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This +1 dragon [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [guisarme](ref/eq-weapon/guisarme) is has a shaft wound
tightly with dragonhide. A dragoncatch guisarme can be used to make a special [trip](ref/rule/trip)
maneuver against creatures using wings to fly. If the maneuver succeeds, the target's wings are
fouled and the creature is knocked off balance, [falling](ref/rule/falling) to the ground and
gaining the [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) condition. A creature tripped in this way can attempt a
DC 15 [Fly](ref/skill/fly) check as a move action to remove the entangled condition.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[black tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1);
**Price** 6,808 gp
