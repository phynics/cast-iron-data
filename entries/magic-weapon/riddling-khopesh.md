**Aura** moderate conjuration, evocation, and transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,320 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This +1 [khopesh](ref/eq-weapon/khopesh) has a handle in the shape of an elongated
[sphinx](ref/family/sphinx), with the sphinx's serene face as the pommel of the sword. Once per day,
the wielder can attempt to answer a series of riddles posed telepathically by the khopesh over 10
minutes. At the end of that time, if the wielder succeeds at a DC 15 Intelligence check (the wielder
can't take 10 or 20 on this check), the sword's enhancement bonus increases by 1 for the next 24
hours as long as it is wielded by the creature who correctly answered its riddles. If the result of
the Intelligence check is 20 or more, the wielder can also add the [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming),
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon), or [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) special weapon
ability to the weapon for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[commune](ref/spell/commune); **Price** 7,820 gp
