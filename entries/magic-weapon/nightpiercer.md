**Sources:** Inner Sea Temples pg 42\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 12,700 gp; **Weight** 12 lbs.

This +1 [repeating heavy crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/repeating-heavy-crossbow) is carved in the shape of
a stylized whippoorwill; the stirrup resembles the head, the limbs look like the wings, and the
stock is shaped like the bird's tail. Nightpiercer applies the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon special ability to all bolts fired from it,
and the bolts shriek like whippoorwills as they fly through the air. Whenever a bolt fired from
Nightpiercer deals damage to an incorporeal undead creature, the bolt remains embedded in the
creature, reducing all of the target's speeds by half (to a minimum of 5 feet) for 1 round.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
