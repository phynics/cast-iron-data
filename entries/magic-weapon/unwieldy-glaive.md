**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 351

**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This weapon appears and acts as a +2 [glaive](ref/eq-weapon/glaive), but cannot be used to make
attacks of opportunity, and hinders its wielder whenever such an attack is attempted. When its
wielder attempts an attack of opportunity, the glaive flails around madly, causing its wielder to
become unbalanced. The wielder takes a -2 penalty to AC and on attack rolls until the end of its
next turn.

### Construction

**Requirements** +2 glaive; **Cost** -
