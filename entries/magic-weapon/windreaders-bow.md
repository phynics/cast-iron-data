**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 54,400 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Whenever an arrow fired from this +1 [adaptive](ref/magic-enh/adaptive)
[seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking) [composite longbow](ref/eq-weapon/composite-longbow) damages the
wielder's [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) or studied target (as per the ranger and
[slayer](ref/class/slayer) class features, respectively), in addition to its normal effects, it also
deals 2 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage. As long as the bleed effect continues, the wielder
can sense that foe's approximate direction and distance twice per day by smelling the wind as a
standard action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature),
[warp wood](ref/spell/warp-wood); **Price** 27,400 gp
