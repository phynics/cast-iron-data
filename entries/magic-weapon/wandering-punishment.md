**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,320 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

The [oni](ref/family/oni) of Chu Ye grant these +3 [tetsubos](ref/eq-weapon/tetsubo) to loyal human
servants, who use them to enforce the oni's will throughout the nation in spite of the Steel Dictate
limiting their involvement. The wielder of a wandering punishment can, as a standard action, assume
the appearance of an oni of her size as though under the effects of
[alter self](ref/spell/alter-self). This transformation does not affect her ability scores and lasts
as long as the weapon is held in one or both hands. Once per day, the wielder can also grow in
stature as per [enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person), taking on the form of a larger oni in the
process. This growth lasts 10 minutes or until the wielder drops the wandering punishment.

Once per day while enlarged, the wielder can deliver an awesome blow. As a standard action, the
wielder can attempt a free [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) combat maneuver check as part of a
standard attack with a wandering punishment. She can't move with her target if this combat maneuver
succeeds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[giant form I](ref/spell/giant-form-1); **Price** 15,160 gp
