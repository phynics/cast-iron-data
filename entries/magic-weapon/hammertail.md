**Sources:** Pathfinder \#39: The City of Seven Spears pg 55\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,305 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This +1 [bolas](ref/eq-weapon/bolas), crafted from strands of jungle vine woven into strong cord and
weighted with rocks, is favored by the [charau-ka](ref/monster/charau-ka). Ranged
[trip](ref/rule/trip) attacks with a hammertail are made with a +4 enhancement bonus on the CMB
check. In addition, if an attack with a hammertail misses its target, the weapon returns as if it
had the [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) property.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[entangle](ref/spell/entangle), [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Price** 5,305 gp
