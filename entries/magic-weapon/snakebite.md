**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 27\
**Aura** moderate necromancy and transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 57,320 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This +2 [injecting](ref/magic-enh/injecting) [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding)
[rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier) is prized among the cults of [Ydersius](ref/faith/ydersius). This
sword increases the save DC of any poison applied to its blade by 1. If the wielder worships
Ydersius, she can utter a silent prayer to the god as a swift action up to three times per day. Each
prayer creates 1 dose of [purple worm poison](ref/eq-poison/purple-worm-poison) in the sword's
pommel reservoir.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [pernicious poison](ref/spell/pernicious-poison); **Price** 28,660 gp
