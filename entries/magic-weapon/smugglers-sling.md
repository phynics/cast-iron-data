**Sources:** Giant Hunter's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,300 gp; **Weight** -

Smuggler's slings are favored in Chu Ye, where they are employed by freedom fighters sneaking
contraband weapons past [oni](ref/family/oni) slave masters. The wielder of this +1
[sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling) gains a +5 competence bonus on
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks to conceal the sling. Up to five times per day,
the wearer can load the sling with a conjured bullet as a swift action. A conjured bullet counts as
magical for the purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and attacking
incorporeal creatures, but is otherwise a standard [sling bullet](ref/eq-weapon/sling-bullets). This
bullet must be fired by the end of the round or it vanishes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[reloading hands](ref/spell/reloading-hands); **Price** 2,650 gp
