**Sources:** Giant Hunter's Handbook pg 28\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 11,330 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

Born from dwarven forges, these +1 giant-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane)
[dwarven waraxes](ref/eq-weapon/dwarven-waraxe) are crafted in the likeness of the favored weapon of
King Harral, the last king of the Pious Kingdom of Saggorak. According to legend, before he and his
comrades starved to death during the orc siege of Saggorak, King Harral challenged and defeated an
[ettin](ref/monster/ettin) in single combat by hacking its legs from beneath its body with his axe.
An axe of felling possesses the [trip](ref/misc/trip) weapon feature and can be used to trip
opponents up to two size categories larger than its wielder.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1);
**Price** 5,830 gp
