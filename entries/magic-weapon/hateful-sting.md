**Sources:** Inner Sea Temples pg 62\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 60,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This +1 [dancing](ref/magic-enh/dancing) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) can attack foes up to 60
feet from the activating character, and takes the form of a rat-sized metal wasp when the dancing
special ability is in use. It can store up to 4 doses of poison in its hilt. While dancing, if it
does not currently have poison applied to it, it automatically applies 1 dose of stored poison (if
any is currently stored) to itself before the first attack it makes each round. The hateful sting
can envenom itself only when dancing. Removing the poison from the hilt to apply to the blade
manually takes a full-round action.
