**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 24\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 496 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This +1 [sling bullet](ref/eq-weapon/sling-bullets) makes a faint rattling noise when shaken. When
it strikes a creature, the creature must succeed at a DC 13 Fortitude save or become
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[sound burst](ref/spell/sound-burst); **Price** 251 gp
