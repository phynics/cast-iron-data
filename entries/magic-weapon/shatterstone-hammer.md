**Sources:** Heroes of the Wild pg 29\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 26,712 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

The head of this +1 [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer) is made from magically treated stone that
is as hard as metal. A shatterstone hammer deals double damage against unattended earthen and stone
objects, and gains the benefit of being a [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon when used against
creatures of the earth subtype and constructs made of clay or stone. Once per day on a successful
attack made with the hammer, the wielder can cause the target to be slowed (as the
_[slow](ref/spell/slow)_ spell). A DC 14 Will save negates this effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shatter](ref/spell/shatter), [slow](ref/spell/slow); **Price** 13,512 gp
