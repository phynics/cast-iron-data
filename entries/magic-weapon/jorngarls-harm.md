**Aura** strong necromancy \[evil\] and transmutation; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 150,320 gp; **Weight** 24 lbs.

This [Large](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) +2 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen)
[vorpal](ref/magic-enh/vorpal) [greataxe](ref/eq-weapon/greataxe) has an
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) haft and a chipped, pitted guillotine blade instead of a
curved axehead. Obviously made for a giant's hands, the axe laughs evilly whenever it kills. Like
the final blades of Galt, any creature killed by it (whether from decapitation or simple damage)
finds its soul trapped within the blade, and it cannot be raised from the dead while so trapped.
Presumably the [Gray Gardeners](ref/pclass/gray-gardener) would know how to release a soul from the
blade.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[circle of death](ref/spell/circle-of-death), [enervation](ref/spell/enervation),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Price** 75,320 gp
