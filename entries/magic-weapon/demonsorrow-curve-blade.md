**Aura** strong evocation and necromancy; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 90,469 gp; **Weight** 7 lbs.

This blade's hilt is shaped as a beautiful angel with elven features. Elaborately detailed feathered
wings spread from the hilt to form the guard, with the angel's face at the base of the blade. This
+3 [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy)
demon-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [elven curve blade](ref/eq-weapon/elven-curve-blade) hinders those
it was created to destroy. It bars all creatures with the demon subtype that are within 60 feet of
the wielder from teleporting, as if they were under the effect of a
_[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ spell (no saving throw).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor), [holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite),
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Price** 46,460 gp
