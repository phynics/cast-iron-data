**Sources:** Pathfinder \#72: The Witch Queen's Revenge pg 63\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 98,320 gp; **Weight** 36 lbs.

This [Huge](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments), double-bitted stone axe, or labrys, is carved entirely
of hematite, with a wooden shaft covered in fur. It functions as a +3 [stone](ref/eq-material/stone)
[impact](ref/magic-enh/impact) [greataxe](ref/eq-weapon/greataxe) that automatically resizes to
match the size of its wielder, from Small to Huge size. The hematite that composes the axe is as
strong as iron, and doesn't have the [fragile](ref/misc/fragile) quality. Once per day, the labrys
of the stone idol can be struck against the ground to create a powerful tremor, as the
_[earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake)_ spell. When used in this way, the labrys creates a shield
around its wielder, protecting him from the immediate effects of the earthquake, including fissures,
falling rubble, and landslides. This shield disappears after 1 round, when the earthquake's effects
end.
