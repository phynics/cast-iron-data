**Sources:** Champions of Purity pg 31\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 37,550 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

The gold-washed barrel of this elegant +2
[double-barreled pistol](ref/eq-weapon/double-barreled-pistol) always catches the light, and its
maple grip feels pleasantly warm to the touch. When its wielder successfully bypasses an evil
creature's [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) with a single attack, the creature's skin
cracks into fissures of golden light. This light negates the creature's DR for the gunslinger and
her allies, fading away after 1 minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[versatile weapon](ref/spell/versatile-weapon); **Price** 19,800 gp
