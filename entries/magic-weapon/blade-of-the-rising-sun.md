**Aura** strong evocation and necromancy; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 51,850 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This golden-bladed +1 [glorious](ref/magic-enh/glorious) undead-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane)
[katana](ref/eq-weapon/katana) shines like the first rays of the morning sun. Whenever the wielder
of a blade of the rising sun threatens a critical threat (regardless of whether the critical is
confirmed or whether the target is subject to critical hits), the target is surrounded by a halo of
golden sunlight equivalent to [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire) until the beginning of the
wielder's next turn. Undead surrounded by this glow take 1d6 points of damage at the beginning of
their turn.

The wielder of a blade of the rising sun may declare a sacrificial strike (similar to the
retributive strike of a [staff of power](ref/magic-staff/staff-of-power)) as a standard action, or
as an immediate action before losing consciousness upon gaining the [dying](ref/misc/dying) or
[dead](ref/misc/dead) condition. A sacrificial strike converts the wielder's body and spirit into
raw energy equivalent to a _[sunburst](ref/spell/sunburst)_ (DC 22). Allies of the wielder within
the _[sunburst](ref/spell/sunburst)_ are not harmed, and the wielder may choose to affect all allies
within the _[sunburst](ref/spell/sunburst)_ as
[mass cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds-mass) or all allies within 10 feet as
[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life). The caster level for the effects of the sacrificial
strike is equal to the sword's caster level plus 1/2 the wielder's level (maximum 25th level).

A sacrificial strike destroys the wielder's body (as the spell
[disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate)), but she has a 50% chance of being reincarnated (as the
[reincarnate](ref/spell/reincarnate) spell) 24 hours after performing a sacrificial strike in a
random safe location within 1 mile. The wielder's gear is left behind unharmed after the sacrificial
strike; however, the blade of the rising sun is teleported to a random location at least 100 miles
distant.
