**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 22\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 180,375 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This +5 [second chance](ref/magic-enh/second-chance) [longbow](ref/eq-weapon/longbow) is made of
supple willow that forever looks freshly carved. When the wielder hits a foe multiple times with the
bow, the arrows land in the same spot, driving their way deeper. A creature struck by an arrow fired
from an arrow splitter takes an additional 1d6 points of precision damage for each arrow fired from
an arrow splitter that has struck the creature within the same round. For example, if a creature is
struck by three arrows fired from an arrow splitter in the same round, the first arrow deals damage
as normal, the second arrow deals an additional 1d6 points of precision damage, and the third arrow
deals an additional 2d6 points of precision damage.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[ricochet shot](ref/spell/ricochet-shot), [sonic thrust](ref/spell/sonic-thrust); **Price** 90,375
gp
