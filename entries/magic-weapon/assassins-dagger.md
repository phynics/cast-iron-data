**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 150, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 471\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This wicked-looking, curved +2 [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) provides a +1 bonus to the DC of a
Fortitude save forced by the death attack of an [assassin](ref/pclass/assassin).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[slay living](ref/spell/slay-living); **Price** 5,302 gp
