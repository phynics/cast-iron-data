**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,310 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This +2 [lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance) is made out of white spruce wood with a handle carved from
mammoth tusk ivory. Its shaft is decorated with images of charging mammoths or running wolves.
Traditionally, the owner of a mammoth lance adorns the weapon with small trophies attached via short
leather strips or carves notches in the shaft to represent triumphs. When wielded while mounted on a
creature with the animal type, a mammoth lance uses the mount's Strength modifier on damage rolls
rather than the wielder's Strength modifier.

When its wielder is not mounted, or is mounted on a creature without the animal type, a mammoth
lance functions as a normal +2 lance.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[animal growth](ref/spell/animal-growth), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Price**
16,310 gp
