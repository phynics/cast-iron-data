**Aura** faint abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,060 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This simple but finely crafted +1 [rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier) has a hilt in the shape of a
tankard, open on one end for the wielder's hand. Once per day, the wielder can use
_[remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear);_ if the wielder ever becomes
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked) and this ability has not already
been expended for the day, the weapon automatically activates this ability on the wielder's next
turn. Once per day, the wielder can turn up to 8 gallons of water (fresh, salt, or contaminated)
into weak beer that is safe to drink.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink), [remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear);
**Price** 1,690 gp
