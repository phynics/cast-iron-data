**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 153\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A wielder with a free hand can split this +1 [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) into two identical +1
[daggers](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) as a swift action, or a free action if she has the
[Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw) feat. The doubled daggers can't be split again. If either dagger
is thrown while doubled, the hurled dagger vanishes after resolving the attack and the remaining
dagger can be split again. If the wielder drops a doubled dagger or it otherwise leaves her person,
it vanishes. If both daggers leave the wielder's hands at the same time, determine randomly which
dagger vanishes.

Spells or effects placed on a dagger of doubling don't duplicate when the dagger is split. Any
active effects on a dagger end when it vanishes. Destroying one of the doubled daggers just causes
the duplicate to disappear, but any damage to a single dagger of doubling remains on both daggers
when it doubles.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shadow weapon](ref/spell/shadow-weapon); **Price** 5,302 gp
