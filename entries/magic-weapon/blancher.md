**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 63,312 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

This Large +4 [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) [heavy pick](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-pick) has a
haft of petrified bone and a head of crudely formed adamantine. Blancher is unusually heavy and must
be wielded with two hands, but it deals damage as if it were a
[Huge](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) weapon rather than a Large weapon. On a successful critical
hit, it deals 1d12 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage (this bleed damage does not stack with
multiple hits) and 1d3 points of [Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) as the victim's
flesh, hair, and eyes are blanched and whitened.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse), [bleed](ref/spell/bleed); **Price** 33,312 gp
