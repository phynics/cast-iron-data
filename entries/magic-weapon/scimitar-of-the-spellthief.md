**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 159\
**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 75,815 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This curved blade of golden metal, a +1 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen)
[heartseeker](ref/magic-enh/heartseeker) [spellstealing](ref/magic-enh/spellstealing)
[scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar), is the bane of spellcasters. Inscribed with flowing magical
script that purports to reveal the inner secrets of magic, the scimitar of the spellthief grants the
wielder a +2 bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) and
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) checks. The scimitar also reveals magical auras by
touch; this functions as [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) but affects only a single object or
creature at a time, which must be touched with the blade while the wielder concentrates on
detection, and the magical auras revealed are clearly visible to all.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), [greater arcane sight](ref/spell/arcane-sight-greater),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish); **Price** 37,815 gp
