**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 156, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 471\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 120,630 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +2 [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) becomes a +5
[holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) cold iron longsword in the hands of a [paladin](ref/class/paladin).

When wielded by a paladin, this sacred weapon provides [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance)
of 5 + the paladin's class level to the wielder and anyone adjacent to her. It also enables the
paladin to use [greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater) (once per round as a standard
action) at the class level of the paladin. Only the area dispel is possible, not the targeted dispel
or counterspell versions of _[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[holy aura](ref/spell/holy-aura), creator must be good; **Price** 60,630 gp
