**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 22\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** 14,012 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This bladed scarf is made of fine, silvery blue silk. Members of the mystery cult of
[Ashava](ref/faith/ashava) - the True Spark and a deified [azata](ref/family/azata) - first created
channeler's scarves, but spiritually inclined members of many other faiths also prize them.

A channeler's scarf is a +1 [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral)
[bladed scarf](ref/eq-weapon/bladed-scarf). When one is wielded by a
[spiritualist](ref/class/spiritualist) whose phantom is currently harbored in her consciousness,
some of the phantom's emotional energy is channeled into the scarf. As long as the wielder's phantom
is harbored in her consciousness, once per day she can activate the scarf as a standard action to
affect it with a _[greater magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon-greater)_ spell, using the wielder's
spiritualist level as the spell's caster level. This effect's duration is equal to 1 minute per the
wielder's spiritualist level. This duration need not be used all at once, but must be used in
1-minute increments.

In addition, while the scarf is under this _[greater magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon-greater)_
effect, it gains a single weapon special ability based on the emotional focus of the wielder's
phantom, as though affected by a _[spirit-bound blade](ref/spell/spirit-bound-blade)_ spell. If the
phantom's emotional focus does not have an entry for spirit-bound blade, the weapon gains the
[heartseeker](ref/magic-enh/heartseeker) special ability. This effect does not grant the scarf any
other benefits of the _[spirit-bound blade](ref/spell/spirit-bound-blade)_ spell.
