**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 325\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 80,318 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

The black blade of this +2 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy)
[scythe](ref/eq-weapon/scythe) reflects faint images of the souls of those it has claimed. Whenever
the scythe is used to drop a creature to -1 hit points or less, the creature must immediately make a
DC 14 Will save or be instantly slain. If the reaver's scythe kills a creature, either through
damage or because the creature failed its Will save, the wielder immediately receives 1
[hero point](ref/rule/hero-points). This hero point must be used within 1 hour or it is lost. The
wielder cannot gain another hero point from the scythe until the first is used or lost.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), [malediction](ref/spell/malediction),
[unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight); **Price** 40,318 gp
