**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 343

**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This vicious-looking +2 [battleaxe](ref/eq-weapon/battleaxe) grants its wielder the
[Great Cleave](ref/feat/great-cleave) feat. But when using this feat, the wielder makes an
additional attack against a random adjacent creature instead of choosing which creature to attack.
When determining which creature is attacked, the wielder always counts himself as a possible target,
even if he is not adjacent to the target. The wielder of a biting battleaxe must always uses it as
his primary weapon and must use Great Cleave at every opportunity until subject to a
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell or similar effect.
