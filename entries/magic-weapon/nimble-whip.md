**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 25\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,005 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

On a successful critical hit with this +1 [scorpion whip](ref/eq-weapon/scorpion-whip), the wielder
can attempt a [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver against the target as a swift action as if she
had the [Improved Steal](ref/feat/improved-steal) feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand); **Price** 2,655 gp
