**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 11,400 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This +1 [adaptive](ref/magic-enh/adaptive) [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
[composite longbow](ref/eq-weapon/composite-longbow) is made of a single piece of petrified wood
that appears to have been burnt black but is magically sturdy. When the wielder pulls the bowstring
while within 5 feet of a flame at least as large as a lit torch, the bow generates and nocks an
arrow of rock-hard ash. Attacks made with an ash arrow deal damage as a normal bow but ignore
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) miss chances from smoke, ash, dust, fog, and other airborne
particles (though the wielder still risks targeting the wrong square when trying to shoot at foes
with total concealment). The ash arrow crumbles to nothing 1 round after leaving the bow.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[fireball](ref/spell/fireball) or [flame blade](ref/spell/flame-blade),
[warp wood](ref/spell/warp-wood); **Price** 5,900 gp
