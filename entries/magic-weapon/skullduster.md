**Aura** strong abjuration and conjuration; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 104,508 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This +2 [disruption](ref/magic-enh/disruption) [morningstar's](ref/eq-weapon/morningstar) metal head
resembles the face of a half-orc. The weapon can cast
_[remove paralysis](ref/spell/remove-paralysis)_ three times per day. Skullduster rarely speaks, and
then usually only to utter grim predictions. The weapon craves the destruction of undead creatures,
and it uses _[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward)_ at will so long as its owner diligently pursues
this goal. If Skullduster's owner ever parleys with an undead creature, the weapon withholds any
further aid, grumbles constantly in Orc about treachery, and seeks a new owner.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward), [heal](ref/spell/heal),
[remove paralysis](ref/spell/remove-paralysis); **Price** 52,408 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** neutral; **Ego** 13\
**Senses** 60 ft., [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)\
**Int** 12, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 10\
**Communication** speech (Common, Orc, Skald)
