**Aura** moderate necromancy and transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,320 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

An artful rapier with a lotus petal guard, this magnificent weapon dedicated to
[Arazni's](ref/monster/arazni) former incarnation is useful to warriors and artists alike.

The tip of this narrow +3 [rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier) can be used as a mundane writing
instrument, producing any color of ink the wielder desires. Once per day as a free action when the
wielder confirms a critical hit against a creature with the Red Crusader's quill, she can inscribe
an _[arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark)_ on the creature. Once per month, the wielder can instead
inscribe a _[mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice);_ this mark ends immediately if the wielder
inscribes another _[mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice)_ with the Red Crusader's quill.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark), [mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice), creator must
worship [Arazni](ref/faith/arazni); **Price** 12,820 gp
