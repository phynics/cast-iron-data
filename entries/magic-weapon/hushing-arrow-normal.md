## Hushing Arrow

**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** normal (547 gp), greater (1,047 gp); **Weight** 1/20 lb.

This +1 [arrow](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) makes no sound on release or as it flies through the air. A
creature struck by a hushing arrow must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be affected by
_[silence](ref/spell/silence)_ (as the spell) for 5 rounds. The _[silence](ref/spell/silence)_
effect is not triggered by arrows that miss or those that strike an inanimate object.

A greater hushing arrow functions just like a normal hushing arrow, except the target must succeed
at a DC 16 Will save to avoid being silenced. Hushing [bolts](ref/eq-weapon/crossbow-bolts) and
greater hushing bolts can also be made; they share the same statistics as hushing arrows and greater
hushing arrows, except they weigh 1/10 of a pound per bolt.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell) (greater hushing arrow only),
[silence](ref/spell/silence); **Price** 277 gp (normal), 577 gp (greater)
