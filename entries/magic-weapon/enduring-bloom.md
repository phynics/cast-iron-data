**Aura** faint conjuration and enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,848 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This +1 [morningstar](ref/eq-weapon/morningstar) has a rose design on the handle and the spikes on
its head resemble thorns. Once per day, the wielder can use _[bless](ref/spell/bless)._
Additionally, once per day when the wielder is reduced to 0 hit points or fewer, the weapon
automatically casts _[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds)_ on the wielder at the start
of her next turn, so long as it remains in the same square as the wielder.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Quicken Spell](ref/feat/quicken-spell), [bless](ref/spell/bless),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds); **Price** 2,078 gp
