**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 215\
**Aura** moderate conjuration \[evil\]; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,870 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This wide machete is darkly stained with blood. It is treated as a +1 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen)
[short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword) that also has a vicious cutting edge, allowing it to deal
slashing damage in addition to piercing damage.

The murderer's machete grants its wielder the ability to move with unnatural grace and speed while
stalking potential victims. The wielder gains a +2 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
checks and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to track. In addition, once per day, the wielder
can lick the bloodstains on the blade as a standard action to gain the benefits of cruel jaunt.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cruel jaunt](ref/spell/cruel-jaunt), [keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), creator must sacrifice a
sentient living creature; **Price** 16,590 gp
