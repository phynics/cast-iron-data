**Sources:** Giant Hunter's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,810 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This +1 [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine)
[swordbreaker dagger](ref/eq-weapon/swordbreaker-dagger) has a jagged ebon blade inscribed with
ancient Thassilonian runes that translate to "cut down to size." Three times per day, when the
wielder successfully [sunders](ref/rule/sunder) a weapon using a shrivelblade, he can permanently
reduce the size of the sundered weapon by one size category, thus reducing its damage and its
intended wielder size by one size category and its weight by half. Using this ability is a swift
action. If the sundered weapon is magical, it can attempt a DC 14 Will save to negate this effect.
Multiple uses of this ability don't stack. A [make whole](ref/spell/make-whole) spell can return a
weapon shrunken by a shrivelblade to its proper size.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** 5,060 gp
