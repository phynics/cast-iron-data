**Sources:** Cheliax, Empire of Devils pg 21\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** 20,350 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

Bestowed as rewards for unwavering loyalty to [Hellknight](ref/rule/aff-lawknights) paravicars of
great renown, these filigreed +2 [greatswords](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword) pulse with a sickening
carmine light in rhythm with the heartbeats of their bearers. Prized trophies, these weapons are
brandished eagerly by staunch champions of Cheliax.

Once per day as a standard action, you may strike the blade against the ground to create a one-way
rift from Phlegethon (the fieriest layer of [Hell](ref/rule/hell)) centered where the sword hits.
The rift spews magical flames upward in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder. All creatures in
this area (including you) take 9d6 points of damage (Reflex half DC 17). Half the damage is fire,
but the other half results directly from divine power and is therefore not subject to fire
resistance or immunity.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike), caster level 6th; **Price** 10,350 gp
