**Sources:** Andoran, Spirit of Liberty pg 26\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** (19,515 gp); **Weight** 6 lbs.

Weapons like this [alchemical silver](ref/eq-material/alchemical-silver) +2
[defending](ref/magic-enh/defending) [bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword) are favored blades
of the [Eagle Knights](ref/rule/aff-vangard). Easily recognized by its notched blades and golden
quillons crafted like upswept eagle wings, its defending ability also protects the wielder's mount.
Wealthier Eagle Knights who frequently skirmish on the western border against Chelish fiends
sometimes have their swords crafted with the ability to cast
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor) on the target as a free action upon a successful
hit; these modified blades are called [talonstrike swords](ref/magic-weapon/talonstrike-sword).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shield](ref/spell/shield) or [shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith),
[shield other](ref/spell/shield-other); **Price** 10,015 gp
