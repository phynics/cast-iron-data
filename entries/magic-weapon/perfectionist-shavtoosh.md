**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 256\
**Aura** faint conjuration and divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,461 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This long piece of cloth is so fine that it can pass through a finger ring. Delicately woven with
multiple colors to spell out [Iroran](ref/faith/irori) prayers, it is usually worn as a shawl or
belt, or wrapped around the chest and shoulders - it does not use a magic item body slot. If wrapped
around at least one limb, it grants the wearer the
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) feat. It may also be wielded as a +1
[whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip). The wearer can meditate on the shawl's sacred mantras to use
_[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds)_ and _[know the enemy](ref/spell/know-the-enemy)_
each once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), [know the enemy](ref/spell/know-the-enemy);
**Price** 2,381 gp
