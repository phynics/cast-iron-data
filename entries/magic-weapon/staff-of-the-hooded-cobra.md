**Aura** moderate abjuration and necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 42,400 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Carved from a single piece of darkwood and topped by the head of a hooded cobra, this staff
functions as a +3 [defending](ref/magic-enh/defending) [darkwood](ref/eq-material/darkwood)
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff). Additionally, once per day when the wielder hits a
creature with the staff, she can cause the carved cobra head to bite her target. The attack deals an
additional 1d6 points of damage and injects the target with a virulent poison.

**Type:** Poison (injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 20

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d2 and Constitution damage 1d2

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[poison](ref/spell/poison), [shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith); **Price** 21,360 gp
