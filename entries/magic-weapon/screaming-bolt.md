**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 159, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 471\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 267 gp; **Weight** 1/10 lb.

This +2 [bolt](ref/eq-weapon/crossbow-bolts) screams when fired, forcing all enemies of the shooter
within 20 feet of the path of the bolt to make a successful DC 14 Will save or become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[doom](ref/spell/doom); **Price** 137 gp
