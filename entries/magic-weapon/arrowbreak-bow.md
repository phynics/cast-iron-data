**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 342

**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This shortbow is beautifully made and inlaid with iridescent designs, appearing to be a +2
[shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow). When it fires its arrows, though, it does it with so much force
that the arrows are always broken on either a hit or a miss. This bow must be used as its owner's
primary ranged weapon and can only be discarded after the owner is subject to a
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell or similar effect.

**Requirements** +2 shortbow; **Cost** -
