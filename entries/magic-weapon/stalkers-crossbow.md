**Sources:** Pathfinder \#76: The Midnight Isles pg 61\
**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 54,735 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This crimson-and-black +3 [seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking)
[light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) is strung with what appears to be a length of
twisted, smoking shadows. Once per day, the wielder of a stalker's crossbow may cast
_[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature),_ targeting the last creature that was damaged by a
bolt fired from the weapon. As long as the _[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature)_ effect
continues, the stalker's crossbow gains the [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon special ability
against that target.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Price** 27,535 gp
