**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 90,335 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This +2 [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) [bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword) becomes
a +2 [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) cold iron bastard sword in the hands of an
[antipaladin](ref/class/antipaladin) sworn to the demon lord [Nocticula](ref/faith/nocticula). In
the hands of a good creature, Iron Lash bestows one permanent
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) as though it were unholy while granting
none of the associated benefits of the unholy special ability.

Once per day, the wielder of this sword can cast _[finger of death](ref/spell/finger-of-death)_ with
a range of 60 feet, dealing 150 points of damage on a successful hit. If the target succeeds at a DC
20 Fortitude saving throw, the attack instead deals 3d6+15 points of damage. This is a death effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[finger of death](ref/spell/finger-of-death), [unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight), creator must
be chaotic evil; **Price** 45,335 gp
