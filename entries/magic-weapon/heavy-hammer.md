**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 346\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 20 lbs.

This massive [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer) is even heavier than its size would normally
warrant. A creature wielding the hammer takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls and a +4 bonus on damage
rolls, as well as a -10 penalty to speed. Once picked up, this weapon must be used as the creature's
primary weapon until subject to a successful [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell.
