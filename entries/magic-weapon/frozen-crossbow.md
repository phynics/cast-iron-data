**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 22\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,700 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

When this +2 [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost)
[repeating heavy crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/repeating-heavy-crossbow) is empty and underwater, pulling
the reloading lever as a free action creates and loads a bolt of solid ice. The icicle bolt and its
wielder ignore all penalties for using a ranged weapon [underwater](ref/rule/underwater-combat). The
icicle bolt deals an amount of cold damage equal to that of a normal bolt and melts 1 round after
leaving the crossbow.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement), [icicle dagger](ref/spell/icicle-dagger);
**Price** 13,200 gp
