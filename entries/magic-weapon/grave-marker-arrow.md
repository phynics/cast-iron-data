**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 307 gp; **Weight** 1/10 lb.

This +1 [arrow](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) is crafted from human bone by agents of the
[Whispering Way](ref/rule/f-gravepath). When it strikes a target, all mindless undead within 300
feet are compelled to attack the creature struck. This effect ignores the immunity to mind-affecting
effects normally granted by the undead creature type. Undead under the control of another creature
can be forced to ignore this command if their master succeeds at a DC 11 caster level check.
Sentient undead are unaffected. The compulsion lasts for 1 minute, after which the undead revert to
their normal behavior.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[command undead](ref/spell/command-undead); **Price** 157 gp
