**Sources:** Pathfinder \#116: Fangs of War pg 29\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 13,400 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Etchings of small, hairy folk adorn this knotty +1 [thundering](ref/magic-enh/thundering)
[club](ref/eq-weapon/club). Once per day as a standard action, a wielder can animate his own hair to
wield the club without his hands for 4 rounds, as if the korrigan club had the
[dancing](ref/magic-enh/dancing) weapon special ability. A wielder's head hair or beard hair must be
at least 2 feet long to activate this ability. [Sundering](ref/rule/sunder) the wielder's hair (hp
2) immediately causes the korrigan club to fall to the ground and renders the wielder unable to
activate this special ability until the lost length regrows.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness);
**Price** 6,700 gp
