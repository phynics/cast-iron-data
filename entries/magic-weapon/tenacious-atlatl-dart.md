**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 25\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 29,101 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This +1 [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) [atlatl dart](ref/eq-weapon/atlatl-dart) is not
destroyed when used as ammunition for an [atlatl](ref/eq-weapon/atlatl). Its returning special
ability functions even if it is fired as ammunition. Like all atlatl darts, a tenacious atlatl dart
can be thrown; if it is thrown, it is treated in all ways as a [javelin](ref/eq-weapon/javelin). Two
times per day, when the dart successfully strikes a foe who is benefiting from an abjuration spell
or ability, the atlatl automatically attempts a dispel check against that effect, as
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),_ before returning to the wielder as normal.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Price** 14,701 gp
