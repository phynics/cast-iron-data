**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 227\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,310 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The following weapon is generally available only to members of the indicated race or ethnicity.

This +1 [short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword) parts shadows in its wake, allowing its wielder to
ignore the miss chance associated with [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) due to darkness or
shadows, and to deal precision damage, such as [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack), against
targets benefiting from concealment. This ability applies only on strikes made with this weapon, and
does not affect the miss chance resulting from total concealment or any other source of concealment
(such as the [blur](ref/spell/blur) spell).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness), [darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision), creator must be an elf;
**Price** 4,310 gp
