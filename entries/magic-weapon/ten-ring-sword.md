**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 14,315 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This finely crafted +2 [nine-ring broadsword](ref/eq-weapon/nine-ring-broadsword) has an extra gap
at the base of the blade's unsharpened edge, closest to the hilt, seemingly to fit an extra ring.
The wielder of a ten-ring sword can use a full-round action to place a magic ring into the empty
slot, where the ring then rests until removed (another full-round action). Once a ring is fitted to
the tenth slot, the sword's wielder can employ that ring as if he were wearing it, even if he is
currently wearing two magic rings.

Alternatively, the sword's wielder can fit a ring made of a specific metal like
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) or [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) into the tenth
slot. Doing so allows the sword to penetrate [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) as if it
were made of the same metal as the ring. Using this property does not grant the weapon any
additional properties of that metal (such as bypassing [hardness](ref/umr/hardness), etc.).
Typically, such metal rings cost one-tenth the cost of a nine-ring broadsword made from that
material. In the event that a magic ring made from a specific metal, like silver, is affixed to the
sword's tenth slot, the sword's wielder decides which of the weapon's two properties will be in
effect when the ring is placed. However, the sword's wielder can use a move action to switch between
properties.
