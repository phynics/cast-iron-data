**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 27\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,302 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

The blade of this +1 [rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier) is imbued with the ability to change form
slightly when used to [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) foes, allowing the wielder to hit
multiple targets.

Three times per day when the wielder makes a sneak attack against a foe that is adjacent to another
creature, she may choose to attack both opponents with a single strike as the blade unwinds,
lengthens, and bends to meet their flesh. The wielder uses the same attack roll result to hit both
creatures. If successful, the attack deals weapon damage to any creature it hits, though only the
first creature takes sneak attack damage.
