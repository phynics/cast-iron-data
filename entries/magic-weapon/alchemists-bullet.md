**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 330 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This +1 [sling bullet](ref/eq-weapon/sling-bullets) contains a magical reservoir that can store a
single vial of any alchemical substance capable of being used as a splash weapon. If the alchemist
bullet hits, the splash weapon also strikes the target and causes splash damage as normal. If the
alchemist's bullet misses but is not destroyed, the alchemical substance remains stored within, and
the bullet does not explode and can be reused. If it misses and is destroyed, the bullet is
destroyed as well without effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** 180 gp
