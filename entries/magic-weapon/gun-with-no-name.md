**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 145\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 98,300 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Though the green, glowing runes on this firearm denote it as magical, otherwise this +2 mythic
[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [greater lucky](ref/magic-enh/lucky-greater)
[pepperbox](ref/eq-weapon/pepperbox) appears beat up and nearly useless. This weapon can be reloaded
normally, but if its wielder expends either one use of mythic power or 1 grit point as a swift
action, each of the firearm's empty barrels magically reloads with either black powder and a bullet
or with a [flare alchemical cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge-flare) (the wielder
chooses, and she can mix and match the ammunition type with each barrel). While carrying this
firearm, the wielder is immune to [scrying](ref/spelldef/scrying) and any form of divination that
attempts to ascertain her location, thoughts, or alignment. The firearm itself is immune to
[locate object](ref/spell/locate-object) and similar effects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor),
[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection); **Price** 50,800 gp
