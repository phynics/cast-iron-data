**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 81\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,301 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

A red flame motif stands out along the length of this +1 [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
[whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip). It deals additional damage whenever it is used to perform a non-damaging
combat maneuver, such as [trip](ref/rule/trip) or [drag](ref/rule/drag). Once the combat maneuver is
resolved, the target takes 2d6 points of fire damage. Objects moved by the whip (through a
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) combat maneuver, for example) take this same damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Whip Mastery](ref/feat/whip-mastery), [fireball](ref/spell/fireball); **Price** 5,301 gp
