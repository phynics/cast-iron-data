**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 152\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 75,350 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This exquisitely forged katana is the result of painstaking attention and craftsmanship by one who
is dedicated to mastering swordplay in its highest form.

A blade of the sword-saint is a +3 [ki intensifying](ref/magic-enh/ki-intensifying)
[katana](ref/eq-weapon/katana). In the hands of a [monk](ref/class/monk), it becomes an instrument
of deadly utility. In addition to its more common properties, this weapon can be employed as though
the wielder were unarmed: the wielder benefits from feats such as
[Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows) or [Snatch Arrows](ref/feat/snatch-arrows) (although he
can redirect attacks but not catch them if he has no hands free), and a monk treats the blade as a
monk weapon for use in his flurry of blows.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), creator must be
a monk; **Price** 37,850 gp
