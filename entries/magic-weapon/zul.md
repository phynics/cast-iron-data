**Sources:** Pathfinder \#56: Raiders of the Fever Sea pg 57\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,395 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

Zul is a +1 human-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [trident](ref/eq-weapon/trident) that has been passed
down from chieftain to chieftain in Krelloort's tribe for generations. Zul's three-pronged head is
crafted of magically strengthened seashells, and its haft is composed of rough, twisted coral that
deals 1 point of damage each time it is grasped, unless the wielder has a natural armor bonus or is
wearing [gauntlets](ref/eq-weapon/gauntlet).

As an immediate action after a successful melee attack with the trident, Zul's wielder can transmute
the substance of the target creature into rigid coral. This attack deals 1d4 points of
[Dexterity damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) and [slows](ref/spell/slow) the target (as the
spell) for 1 round as its flesh calcifies. A DC 16 Fortitude save negates the slow effect but not
the ability damage. A target reduced to 0 Dexterity is permanently petrified, transformed into
inert, mindless coral. [Break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment) or
[restoration](ref/spell/restoration) reverses all of the effects of this calcification. This ability
can be used up to 7 times per day.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[calcific touch](ref/spell/calcific-touch), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1);
