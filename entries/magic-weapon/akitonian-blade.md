**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,311 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This delicate-looking +1 [bill](ref/eq-weapon/bill) imparts to its wielder the grace and agility of
one born in the low gravity of the red planet Akiton. When holding the blade, the wielder triples
the result of any [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to determine how far she can jump. In
addition, a successful DC 15 Acrobatics check allows the wielder to negate any damage from the first
30 feet of [falling](ref/rule/falling) damage and converts any damage from the second 30 feet to
nonlethal damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider); **Price** 4,311 gp
