**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 31\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,302 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

When the wielder of this glimmering +1 [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon)
[spear](ref/eq-weapon/spear) enters a rage, she can drum the spear against a shield or the ground as
a swift action. If she does, colorful lights dance around the spear and the wielder can choose to
gain the benefit of _[guidance](ref/spell/guidance),_ grant the touched shield the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-armor) armor special ability, or grant the spear the
[returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) special ability. The chosen benefit lasts for the duration of
the rage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance), [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire),
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Price** 7,802 gp
