**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 216\
**Aura** moderate evocation \[evil\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,315 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +2 [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) contains the hideous captured screams of a torture
victim. The blade howls these blasphemies and profanities whenever it is drawn. This discordant
noise grants the wielder a +5 competence bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks, but any
creature can hear the howls with a Perception DC of -10, regardless of the wielder's Stealth.

The disturbing noise makes it difficult for nearby creatures to concentrate. Any creature other than
the wielder within 60 feet attempting to cast a spell must succeed at a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC = 15 + spell level) or the spell fails. Creatures
within 60 feet take a -2 penalty on skill checks that require concentration and are unable to take
10 (unless they have an ability that enables them to take 10 while distracted or threatened). If the
wielder is evil and has the bardic performance ability, she can use the screams as a masterwork
instrument for either [Perform (sing)](ref/skill/perform-any) or Perform (wind) and the DCs of her
bardic performances that are fear effects increase by 1.

[Silence](ref/spell/silence) and similar effects suppress all the blade's magical abilities except
its enhancement bonus.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[sound burst](ref/spell/sound-burst), creator must be evil and torture a sentient creature during
creation; **Price** 12,815 gp
