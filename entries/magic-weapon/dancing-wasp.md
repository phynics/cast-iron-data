**Sources:** Pathfinder \#49: The Brinewall Legacy pg 60\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,392 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This +1 [kusarigama](ref/eq-weapon/kusarigama) makes a shrill whistling sound whenever it is used in
combat. Once per day as a standard action, the wielder of Dancing Wasp can whirl the weapon above
his head as a full-round action to summon a [giant wasp](ref/monster/wasp-giant-wasp). The wasp
appears in the air above the wielder's head and follows the wielder's mental commands to the best of
its ability as long as the user continues to twirl the weapon (requiring a standard action each
round), to a maximum of 5 rounds, after which the wasp vanishes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) or
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4); **Price** 6,352 gp
