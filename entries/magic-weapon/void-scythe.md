**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 95,318 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

Instead of a blade, a swathe of absolute darkness inimical to life tips this scythe. It functions as
a +1 [brilliant energy](ref/magic-enh/brilliant-energy) [scythe](ref/eq-weapon/scythe), except the
blade is formed of negative energy and gives off no light whatsoever. The wielder can
[channel negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) directly into the scythe to charge it with
life-destroying energy for 1 round per die of channeling. For example, channeling negative energy
for 5d6 points of damage fuels the scythe for 5 rounds. While so charged, the void scythe bestows
one [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) with each hit. One day after being
struck, subjects must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save for each negative level or the negative
levels become permanent.

Black energy utterly consumes any creature that is slain by the void scythe, leaving no body behind
but leaving the creature's equipment unharmed. A victim of the void scythe can be restored only by
[true resurrection](ref/spell/true-resurrection) or carefully worded [miracle](ref/spell/miracle) or
[wish](ref/spell/wish).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate), [enervation](ref/spell/enervation); **Price** 47,815 gp
