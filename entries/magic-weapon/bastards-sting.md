**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 150\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 123,035 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This +2 [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) [bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword)
becomes a +5 [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) adamantine bastard sword in the hands of an antipaladin.

While wielded by an [antipaladin](ref/class/antipaladin), a bastard's sting deals 2d6 points of
negative energy damage to all living creatures that start their turn adjacent to the wielder. The
antipaladin also gains [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 5 as long as at least one living
creature takes damage from this negative energy. This negative energy has no effect on undead. The
sword also enables the antipaladin to use _[unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight)_ at will on
command at the class level of the antipaladin. Paladins take a -2 saving throw penalty against this
effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[unholy aura](ref/spell/unholy-aura), [unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight), creator must be
evil; **Price** 63,035 gp
