**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 25\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These +1 [gauntlets](ref/eq-weapon/gauntlet) are made of snakeskin and grant a +1 enhancement bonus
on [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) checks.

Once per day as a standard action, the wielder of a pair of constrictor's gauntlets can target a
single creature wearing medium or heavy armor within 30 feet. The target must succeed at a DC 16
Will saving throw or its armor shrinks and constricts, dealing 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage to
the target if it's wearing medium armor or 1d8 points of bludgeoning damage if it's wearing heavy
armor. The armor deals this damage each round for 3 rounds, and then returns to its normal size. The
armor's armor bonus, maximum Dexterity bonus, armor check penalty, and other attributes do not
change while it is shrunken.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** 6,302 gp
