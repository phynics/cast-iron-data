**Sources:** Rise of the Runelords Anniversary Edition pg 421, Pathfinder \#2: The Skinsaw Murders
pg 51\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 9 lbs.

The impaler of thorns is an ancient weapon dating back to Thassilonian times, when it was often used
by city guards to aid in breaking up civil unrest. An impaler's shaft is made of
[darkwood](ref/eq-material/darkwood), and its head is a thorn-like, wide-bladed barb. When an
impaler of thorns is used in combat, its successful critical hits are accompanied by an unsettling
screech, as of some wild beast in anger.

An impaler of thorns is a +1 [longspear](ref/eq-weapon/longspear). Once per day as it strikes a foe,
the wielder can cause it (as a free action) to unleash a 30-foot-radius burst of despair that
affects all creatures not wielding an impaler of thorns. Creatures affected must succeed at DC 16
Will saves or become overwhelmed with sadness and despair, taking -2 penalties on attack rolls,
saving throws, ability checks, skill checks, and weapon damage rolls for 6 minutes. The target
struck when this effect is triggered must also succeed at a second DC 16 Will save to resist
becoming [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) with despair for 1 round.
