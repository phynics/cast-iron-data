**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 255\
**Aura** strong evocation and illusion; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 11,035 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This vicious-looking +1 [corrosive](ref/magic-enh/corrosive)
[sawtooth sabre](ref/eq-weapon/sawtooth-sabre) has a crimson blade and a hilt that appears to be
beautifully carved from dark red stone. On command, a corrosive acid with a deep blood-red hue drips
from the sabre. Once per day, the wielder of a fuming blood sabre can use
_[vanish](ref/spell/vanish)_ - typically to approach an unaware victim or flee undetected from the
scene of an assassination.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[acid arrow](ref/spell/acid-arrow), [vanish](ref/spell/vanish); **Price** 5,685 gp
