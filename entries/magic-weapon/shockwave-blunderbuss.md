**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 23\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 87,300 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

Whenever the wielder fires pellets from this +2 [sonic boom](ref/magic-enh/sonic-boom)
[blunderbuss](ref/eq-weapon/blunderbuss), she attempts a free combat maneuver check to
[trip](ref/rule/trip) each creature struck by the pellets. The wielder uses the shockwave
blunderbuss's Combat Maneuver Bonus of +20 for these trip attempts. The wielder doesn't provoke
attacks of opportunity for these trip attempts and doesn't risk knocking herself prone.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[reverse gravity](ref/spell/reverse-gravity), [shout](ref/spell/shout); **Price** 44,800 gp
