**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 97, Advanced Class Origins pg 29\
**Aura** faint conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,305 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This +1 [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace) is forged of black iron and topped with what appears
to be a clenched fist with spikes protruding from between the fingers. As a move action, the wielder
can expend 1 point from his arcane pool (as per a [magus](ref/class/magus)), 1 point from his arcane
reservoir (as per an [arcanist](ref/class/arcanist)), or one use of fervor (as per a
[warpriest](ref/class/warpriest)) to make a single melee attack with the signifer's fist. Characters
without any of these options can instead activate a signifer's fist in this way as a standard action
once per day. If the attack deals damage to a creature, the signifer's fist gains the
[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon special ability against that creature's type (or subtype if the
creature is a humanoid or outsider) for 1 minute. This benefit doesn't apply if the target is immune
to [bleed](ref/rule/bleed), though the attack still deals damage normally.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[haste](ref/spell/haste), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Price** 5,305 gp
