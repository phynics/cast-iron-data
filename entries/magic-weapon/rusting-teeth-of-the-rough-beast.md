**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 257\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,320 gp; **Weight** 12 lbs.

This +1 [greataxe](ref/eq-weapon/greataxe) has a corroded edge that constantly sheds metal shards
and rusty powder, though the blade never gets any smaller and this doesn't weaken the weapon in any
way. Whenever the axe damages a creature, the target must succeed at a DC 14 Reflex save or a shard
from the weapon lodges in the creature's flesh; the target is [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) until
the shard is removed. Removing a shard deals 1d4+1 points of damage to the target, unless whoever is
removing the shard succeeds at a DC 15 [Heal](ref/skill/heal) check as a full-round action. For
every 5 by which the check exceeds the DC, an additional shard is removed. A
[rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp) spell immediately destroys all shards embedded in the
target.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [ray of sickening](ref/spell/ray-of-sickening); **Price** 4,320 gp
