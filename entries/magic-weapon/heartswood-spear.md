**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 156\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 50,302 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This humble weapon appears to be little more than a crudely sharpened stake or makeshift spear, but
in truth it is a far more formidable weapon. In addition to being a +2
undead-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [spear](ref/eq-weapon/spear), a heartswood spear deals bludgeoning
damage when it is swung as a [staff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff). When employed in such a manner, it
also possesses the [disruption](ref/magic-enh/disruption) property.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[heal](ref/spell/heal), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Price** 25,302 gp
