**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 154\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,805 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

The [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water) in this +1
[battle aspergillum](ref/eq-weapon/battle-aspergillum) never runs dry. However, the water vanishes 1
round after leaving the aspergillum, so it cannot be collected. As a full-round action, the wielder
can swing the weapon to dispense a vial's worth of holy water as a ranged touch attack with a
maximum range of 10 feet and no range penalty. An everflowing aspergillum counts as a good-aligned
weapon for purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bless water](ref/spell/bless-water), [create water](ref/spell/create-water); **Price** 3,905 gp
