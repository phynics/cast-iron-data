**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,301 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This item is crafted from tightly braided rawhide from a water buffalo skin, and is usually awarded
to a prominent member of a tribe. The mokele-mbembe tail whip functions as a +2
[whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip), but deals an additional 1d6 points of sonic damage on a successful hit.
In addition, anyone hit by a critical hit with the whip must succeed at a DC 21 Fortitude save or be
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round. A successful critical hit with the whip does not multiply
the weapon's sonic damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shout](ref/spell/shout); **Price** 16,301 gp
