**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 257\
**Aura** moderate evocation, necromancy, and transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,875 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This +1 [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) [falchion](ref/eq-weapon/falchion) is engraved with
leering, long-tongued demonic faces. As a standard action, the bearer can will the weapon to
transform into a +1 flaming [kukri](ref/eq-weapon/kukri) or back to its larger form. The weapon has
an affinity for humanoid blood and remains slick and wet with it long after it would normally dry.
If the wielder kills a humanoid with Redlust's Daughter, she can lick clean this blood as a move
action to heal 1d4 hit points. [Lamashtu's](ref/faith/lamashtu) church also creates a similar weapon
called Chillheart's Daughter, which is a +1 [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) falchion that otherwise has
the same properties as this weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), [flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** 5,125 gp
