**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 215\
**Aura** faint necromancy \[evil\]; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The serrated blade of this +1 [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) is set in a hilt made of human bone
carved in the shape of a scorpion's tail. Once per day when the wielder delivers a
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) with the sacrificial dagger and the target dies as a result of
the attack, the dagger traps the dead creature's life energy for 10 minutes per Hit Die of the
creature. A dark mist visibly seeps from the dagger during this time, and anyone wielding the dagger
gains the benefits of _[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell)._ Determine the number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) granted when the target dies. The temporary
hit points stem from the dagger, so if another creature wields the dagger, that creature gains the
remaining temporary hit points (if any) and the previous wielder loses them.

If, while the _[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell)_ effect is active, the wielder casts a spell
that requires sacrificing a creature, she can expend the life energy stored in the dagger. For the
purpose of fulfilling the spell's requirements, the life energy counts as the creature did when it
was alive (including Hit Dice and whether or not it was sentient). Expending the trapped life energy
ends the _[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell)_ effect immediately, before the casting of the
sacrificial spell is complete, so the _[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell)_ effect doesn't increase
the wielder's caster level for that spell. Sacrificing another creature dissipates the life energy
of any previous sacrifice already held in the dagger.
