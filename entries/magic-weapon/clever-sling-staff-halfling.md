**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 226\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 60,320 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

The following weapon is generally available only to members of the indicated race or ethnicity.

The enhancement bonus of this Small +3 [seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking)
[halfling sling staff](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-sling-staff) applies to both ranged sling attacks and
melee club attacks. A [halfling](ref/race/halfling) wielder with the warslinger racial trait can
reload a clever sling staff as if it were a sling.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[haste](ref/spell/haste), [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing), creator must be a halfling;
**Price** 30,320 gp
