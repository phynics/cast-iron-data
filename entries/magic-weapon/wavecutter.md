**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,320 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This unusual +1 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [terbutje](ref/eq-weapon/terbutje) is made of sharp
chunks of quartz embedded along a length of steel. A wavecutter may be
[used underwater](ref/rule/fighting-underwater) without suffering a slashing weapon's normal -2
penalty on attack rolls.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement), [keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Price**
5,320 gp
