**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,310 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

An unnaturally cold, razor-thin blade of magically-hardened ice juts from the pommel of this +1
[frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword). On
a confirmed critical hit, an ice shard breaks off into the wound and inflicts numbing cold. For the
next 5 rounds, the victim takes 2 points of cold damage and is [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for
that round if he takes this damage. This is a [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) effect. The shard can be
removed as a standard action with a successful DC 15 [Heal](ref/skill/heal) check or with the
application of any magical healing, and is destroyed instantly if the victim takes at least 5 points
of fire damage from any attack. The shard lasts for only 1 round when inflicted on a creature with
the fire subtype. A blade of the ice stalker can implant an ice shard once per minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [chill metal](ref/spell/chill-metal), [keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge);
**Price** 16,310 gp
