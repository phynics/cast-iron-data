**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 206 gp; **Weight** -

This +1 [featherweight dart](ref/eq-weapon/featherweight-dart) is designed to be fired from a small
wrist launcher. As with a normal featherweight dart, it deals no damage; it instead administers 1
dose of an applied poison as long as the target doesn't have
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), [hardness](ref/umr/hardness), or the like. Once the
dart has been launched, whether it hits or misses, the creature that fired it can use a swift action
to recall the dart to a convenient location, such as the palm of a hand or a pocket, removing the
dart from the scene of the attack. The magical recovery of the dart does not prevent it from being
damaged, so as with other magical ammunition, it can't be reused.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[apport object](ref/spell/apport-object); **Price** 106 gp
