**Sources:** Pathfinder \#82: Secrets of the Sphinx pg 63\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 27,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This bronze +2 [scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar) bears an eagle motif on its pommel, and allows the
user to soar through the air to deliver his attacks. As a free action, the wielder can fly without
having to maintain concentration, as if affected by the _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_ spell for up to 20
rounds each day, with a +5 bonus on [Fly](ref/skill/fly) checks. These rounds need not be
consecutive. In addition, as long as the scimitar is in hand, the wielder can use
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ at will.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [fly](ref/spell/fly); **Price** 13,500 gp
