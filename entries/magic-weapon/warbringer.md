**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 163\
**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 61,375 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This massive, wickedly curved red-bladed sword is a +3 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen)
[vicious](ref/magic-enh/vicious) [falchion](ref/eq-weapon/falchion) that murmurs a low, rumbling
battle chant when drawn. Whenever the wielder confirms a critical hit with a warbringer, the wielder
gains the benefits of _rage_ for 1 round (if a barbarian, the wielder gains 1 additional round of
rage for that day), while the creature struck is compelled to move toward and attack its nearest
ally with a melee weapon, natural weapon, or unarmed strike on its next turn (DC 14 Will negates),
as the _[murderous command](ref/spell/murderous-command)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), [murderous command](ref/spell/murderous-command),
[rage](ref/spell/rage); **Price** 30,875 gp
