**Sources:** Pathfinder \#71: Rasputin Must Die! pg 61\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 33,800 gp; **Weight** 140 lbs.

The bullets fired by this +2 [Maxim M1910 machine gun](ref/eq-weapon/maxim-m1910-machine-gun) are
enhanced to pierce protective cover, allowing the wielder to ignore the AC bonus granted to targets
by anything less than total cover. A Maxim of suppressive fire can make attacks against targets with
total cover, but the wielder must still guess the correct square of its target's location, and the
target still benefits from total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (50% miss chance).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[phase door](ref/spell/phase-door); **Price** 17,800 gp
