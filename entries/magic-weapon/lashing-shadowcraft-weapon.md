**Sources:** Blood of Shadows pg 22\
**Aura** moderate illusion and transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 14,500 gp; **Weight** varies

This two-handed melee [shadowcraft weapon](ref/magic-weapon/shadowcraft-weapon) is extremely
ductile, and it easily bends and stretches without hampering its ability to harm opponents. On
command, a lashing shadowcraft weapon can be extended as a swift action, increasing the weapon's
reach by 5 feet for 1 round. While extended, the wielder does not threaten adjacent creatures, or
creatures up to 10 feet away if the weapon already has [reach](ref/misc/reach).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[long arm](ref/spell/long-arm), [major creation](ref/spell/major-creation),
[shadow weapon](ref/spell/shadow-weapon); **Price** 7,250 gp
