**Sources:** Pathfinder \#53: Tide of Honor pg 60\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 29,902 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The bars of this +2 [nunchaku](ref/eq-weapon/nunchaku) are solid iron, wrapped in leather decorated
with an imperial dragon motif, and connected by a chain. Once per day, when the wielder of a
dragonmaw nunchaku confirms a critical hit against an opponent with the nunchaku, he can cast
_[confusion](ref/spell/confusion)_ on that creature as a free action. The
_[confusion](ref/spell/confusion)_ effect lasts for 8 rounds, and a DC 16 Will save negates the
effect.

In addition, in the round following a successful [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) attack with the dragonmaw
nunchaku, the wielder can spin the nunchaku as a standard action, creating a curtain of whirling
force replicas of the disarmed weapon around himself for 1 round. This functions as a ringed
_[blade barrier](ref/spell/blade-barrier)_ with a radius of 10 feet around the wielder (Reflex DC
19). The disarmed weapon determines the damage dice of the
_[blade barrier](ref/spell/blade-barrier)_ (i.e., 12d6 for a wakizashi, 12d8 for a katana, and so
on). The disarmed weapon is unaffected, and drops to the ground as normal. The wielder can create
the _[blade barrier](ref/spell/blade-barrier)_ once per day, and only in the round following a
successful disarm attack.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[blade barrier](ref/spell/blade-barrier), [confusion](ref/spell/confusion); **Price** 15,102 gp
