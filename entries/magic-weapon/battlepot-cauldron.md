**Sources:** Blood of the Coven pg 30\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,312 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This small cast-iron [cauldron](ref/eq-misc/cauldron) has ring handles and three latches that can
secure the lid in place. While the pot can be used for cooking and brewing potions, when gripped by
the handles, it can also be wielded as a +1 [heavy mace](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-mace) without imposing
an [improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) penalty. As a standard action that provokes an
attack of opportunity, a potion can be sealed inside the cauldron, destroying the potion and
infusing the iron with its magic. As a free action when he successfully strikes a creature with the
battlepot, the wielder can inject the stored potion into the target, treating the struck creature as
if it had just consumed the potion. Up to five potions can be stored in a battlepot at a time, but
only one potion can be expended per successful hit; the wielder decides which potion is discharged
with each blow.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor);
[cauldron](ref/hex/cauldron) hex or [touch injection](ref/spell/touch-injection); creator must have
8 ranks in Craft (alchemy); **Price** 4,656 gp
