**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 155\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,380 gp; **Weight** -

This +1 [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) [sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling) is crafted of blue-dyed leather.
In lieu of adding cold damage to normal ammunition, three times per day wielder may create and hurl
a magical snowball with the sling instead. This ranged touch attack has a maximum range of 40 feet,
deals 1d6+6 points of nonlethal cold damage, and leaves the target [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued).
The fatigued condition ends when the target recovers from the nonlethal damage. This effect cannot
make the target [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) even if it is already fatigued.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[frostbite](ref/spell/frostbite); **Price** 4,840 gp
