**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 255\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,655 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This human-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) has a
gold-colored metal lath and a keyhole-shaped slot for the bolt to rest in. The wielder gains a
+10-foot enhancement bonus to his base speed. The church of [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar) typically
commissions fugitive finders to aid officials in chasing down and capturing escaped criminals, but
Abadarans also craft variants that have the [merciful](ref/magic-enh/merciful) special ability
instead of human-bane, giving the wielder a better opportunity to capture a fleeing target alive.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Price**
6,495 gp
