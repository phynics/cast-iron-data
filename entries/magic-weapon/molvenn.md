## Molvënn

**Aura** strong necromancy and transmutation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 63,312 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This heavy warhammer is capped with steel spikes on both ends of its massive head, and a leather
thong prevents it from falling from the wielder's hand. Molvënn is a +3
[warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer) that deals both piercing and bludgeoning damage. Yet although
it is already a formidable weapon, its true potential is only realized in the hands of a barbarian.

Wielded by a barbarian, Molvënn becomes a +5 [thundering](ref/magic-enh/thundering) warhammer. The
barbarian gains an additional 1 hit point per barbarian level when raging and can add twice her
Strength modifier (instead of half again her Strength modifier) to damage rolls while wielding
Molvënn two-handed. Additionally, while Molvënn's wielder is raging, she cannot be
[disarmed](ref/rule/disarm), nor can Molvënn be [sundered](ref/rule/sunder).

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
