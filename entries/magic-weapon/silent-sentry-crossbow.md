**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 241\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 38,335 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs

Popular with [assassins](ref/pclass/assassin) and [vigilantes](ref/class/vigilante) due to its ease
of concealment and lack of need for ammunition, this durable +2
[endless ammunition](ref/magic-enh/endless-ammunition)
[light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) is crafted from wood blackened over an alchemically
treated fire. When used in conjunction with the [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) skill, the silent
sentry crossbow reduces the penalty imposed by sniping by 5.

Three times per day, the wielder can fire two invisible magical bolts that deal no damage. These
bolts must each be fired at a solid surface, into which they embed themselves. As long as both bolts
have an unobstructed line between them, no longer than 180 feet, they create an invisible
1-inch-wide line between themselves. The wearer is mentally alerted anytime a visible Tiny or larger
creature passes through this line, and gains a vision of the creature's appearance. Creatures that
can see invisible objects can see the line, and if they recognize it for what it is, they can duck
under it or leap over it, thus avoiding detection. The bolts last for 6 hours before they dissipate.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[alarm](ref/spell/alarm), [minor creation](ref/spell/minor-creation), [silence](ref/spell/silence);
**Price** 19,335 gp
