**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 916 gp; **Weight** 1/20 lb.

When this translucent +2 [arrow](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) is fired, it becomes ethereal until it enters
the target's space. A phase arrow ignores [cover](ref/rule/cover) of all kinds, including total
cover, and can be fired at a target even if the wielder doesn't have line of effect. However, if the
wielder cannot see or otherwise pinpoint the target, she risks attacking the wrong space, and even
if she attacks the right space, the target still has total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment)
against the attack (granting a 50% miss chance).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[blink](ref/spell/blink); **Price** 461 gp
