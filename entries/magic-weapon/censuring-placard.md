**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 14,300 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Most often appearing as a simple flattened board inscribed with a portion of
[Cayden Cailean's](ref/faith/cayden-cailean) holy text, a censuring placard is used to inflict just
punishment on offenders of church guidelines or to knock allies roughly back to their senses. A
censuring placard functions as a +1 [club](ref/eq-weapon/club). As a swift action with a successful
melee attack, the wielder can use the placard to cast
_[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment)_ on that target. Whether this effort is successful
or not, the _[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment)_ ability cannot be used again for a
week.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment); **Price** 7,300 gp
