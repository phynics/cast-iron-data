**Sources:** Tears at Bitter Manor pg 18\
**Aura** moderate evocation \[good\]; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 21,015 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

Clarity is a +1 [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) [scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar) crafted to vanquish
the evil creatures of the night. Though she can't speak, her empathic communications clearly
indicate a protective mindset. Clarity enjoys giving warm, encouraging energy to her wielder as long
as that wielder opposes evil and gives his best effort. To preserve her wielder's valor in fights,
she can cast _[remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear)_ up to three times per day, affecting only her
wielder.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite), [remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear); **Price** 10,665 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** neutral good; **Ego** 7\
**Senses** 30 ft.\
**Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 14\
**Communication** empathy
