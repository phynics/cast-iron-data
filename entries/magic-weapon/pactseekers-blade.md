**Sources:** Blood of the Coven pg 31\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 55,330 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +2 [cruel](ref/magic-enh/cruel) [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) features layers of
[cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) and steel folded over one another repeatedly. The blade
counts as cold iron for the purpose of overcoming a creature's
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). Whenever a pactseeker's blade successfully deals at
least 1 point of damage (after damage reduction) to a target, the weapon also deals damage equal to
the weapon's enhancement bonus to each ally that shares an ongoing spell (such as
[haste](ref/spell/haste)) with or is otherwise magically bound to the target (such as other members
of a [hag](ref/family/hag) coven). This damage disregards the range between the weapon's target and
such bound creatures, and the damage bypasses damage reduction.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear), [death knell](ref/spell/death-knell),
[symbol of pain](ref/spell/symbol-of-pain); **Price** 27,830 gp
