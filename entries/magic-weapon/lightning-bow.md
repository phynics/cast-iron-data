**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 358

**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 54,300 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This intelligent item is called a lightning bow by mortals, a name is seen as a slight to the
[lightning elemental](ref/monster/lightning-elemental) trapped within it: a creature honor-bound to
serve in the bow for no less than 100 years. Each of these +3 [adaptive](ref/magic-enh/adaptive)
[composite longbows](ref/eq-weapon/composite-longbow) is a haughty and often highly intelligent
member of its race, who chides those who don't know its true name: often a complicated series of
high pitched squeaks, like live wood burning on a pyre. The elemental in a lightning bow only speaks
Auran. Lightning bows will not fire on other outsiders with the elemental subtype. Some are also
bound not to attack other individuals, based on their history. For instance, one of these bows might
be foresworn not to attack the Sultan of Fire, fire giants, or red dragons. Additionally, lightning
bows have the following powers.

- A lightning bow needs no ammunition. With the pull of a string, it creates a +3
  [shock](ref/magic-enh/shock) arrow or a +3 [brilliant energy](ref/magic-enh/brilliant-energy)
  arrow (the wielder's choice with each shot).
- Three times per day, the wielder can cast [true strike](ref/spell/true-strike) as a swift action.
  The _[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike)_ can only be used with attacks made with the lightning
  bow.
- Three times per day, the wielder can use [lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt) as a
  [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[call lightning](ref/spell/call-lightning), the willing service of one Huge lighting elemental for
at least 100 years; **Price** 27,300 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** neutral; **Ego** 9\
**Senses** 120 ft.\
**Int** 6, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 11\
**Communication** speech (Auran)
