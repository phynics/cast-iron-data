**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 11,700 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

Crafted in the forges of Alkenstar, this +1 [merciful](ref/magic-enh/merciful)
[pistol](ref/eq-weapon/pistol) is ornamented with filigree of angelic creatures along the barrel,
and houses intricate brass gears. Whenever the wielder scores a critical hit while using this weapon
to deal nonlethal damage, the target must succeed at a DC 12 Will saving throw or be
[dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round. As a swift action, a wielder with the grit class feature can
spend 1 grit point to increase the DC of this save by 4.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), [daze monster](ref/spell/daze-monster); **Price**
6,500 gp
