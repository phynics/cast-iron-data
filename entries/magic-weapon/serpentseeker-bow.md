**Sources:** Mystery Monsters Revisited pg 49\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and divination; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,330 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This +1 [shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow) is crafted from one of the long spines that bristle along
the body of a sea serpent. When used against a magical beast with the aquatic subtype, a
serpentseeker bow gains the benefit of the [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon special ability. If
such a creature is hit by an arrow fired from this weapon, anyone who touches the bow senses the
location of that creature as though by _[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature)_ as long as the
arrow remains imbedded in the creature. If the creature is protected by
[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection) or a similar effect, anyone holding the bow gains a +10
competence bonus on the caster level check to overcome it, but a failed check means the arrow's
magic burns out. The locating magic on an arrow fired from this bow automatically ends after 1d6
days.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1);
**Price** 7,830 gp
