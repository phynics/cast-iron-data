**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 22\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,335 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +2 [light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) is made of silver that never needs to be
polished. A mage's crossbow is especially effective against creatures with
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). Against such creatures, the weapon's effective
enhancement bonus increases to +3, and it deals an additional 1d6 points of damage. Whenever the
wielder casts a spell that requires him to make a ranged touch attack (such as a ray) while he has
the crossbow in hand, he gains a bonus on his ranged attack roll equal to the crossbow's enhancement
bonus against the target creature. If the spell hits, the caster also applies this bonus to the
caster level check to overcome the target's spell resistance (if any) and on the spell's damage roll
(if any).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Spell Penetration](ref/feat/spell-penetration), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Price** 9,835 gp
