**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 34,800 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This wooden pole is plain, save that green dragon scales adorn to its length. Treated as a +2
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), it is a favorite of Irori's wandering followers. In the
hands of a worshiper of [Irori](ref/faith/irori), a quarterstaff of contemplation gains the
[ki intensifying](ref/magic-enh/ki-intensifying) weapon special ability.

If the wielder is a follower of Irori and possesses the wholeness of body class feature, he can
spend a full-round action to meditate using the staff as a focus. Treat this as using wholeness of
body at the cost of 1 ki point instead of 2.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), creator must be
a 7th-level monk; **Price** 17,550 gp
