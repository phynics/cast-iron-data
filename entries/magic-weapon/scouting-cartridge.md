**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 566 gp; **Weight** -

This +1 [paper alchemical cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge) is contains a bullet and is
marked with an open eye. When fired from a firearm, a scouting cartridge allows the wielder to
temporarily transfer his sense of sight to the location where the bullet lands. Until he dismisses
the effect by rubbing his eyes as a move action, the wielder can see as _clairvoyance_ from the
square in which the bullet landed, regardless of whether it hit its mark.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance); **Price** 286 gp
