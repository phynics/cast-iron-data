**Aura** moderate abjuration and evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,830 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This weapon consists of a flexible blade attached to a handle by a fine coil of steel, and is
favored by [tieflings](ref/race/tiefling) and other outcasts in Jalmeray. This +1
[mighty cleaving](ref/magic-enh/mighty-cleaving) [urumi](ref/eq-weapon/urumi) grants its wielder a
deflection bonus to AC equal to the number of enemies within her reach with the urumi (maximum +4).
The wielder also gains a +5 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to move
through a threatened area.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith); **Price** 9,580 gp
