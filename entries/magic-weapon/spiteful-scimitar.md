**Sources:** Pathfinder \#119: Prisoners of the Blight pg 49\
**Aura** moderate abjuration, transmutation, and necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 75,315 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +2 [cruel](ref/magic-enh/cruel) [furyborn](ref/magic-enh/furyborn)
[scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar) was created accidentally by [Treerazer](ref/monster/treerazer)
when the demon lord plunged the glaistig Hephloma's scimitar through her own chest. Images of
shriveled vines and wilted flowers decorate its blade. Three times per day as an immediate action
after dealing damage to a creature, the wielder can cause the blighted plant life depicted on the
blade to animate, embedding thorny vines in the creature's wounds. The creeping, twisting vines
cause the creature to become [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) for 1d4 rounds, and it is
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) by the pain while the entangled condition persists. A successful DC
22 Fortitude saving throw negates the nauseated condition.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear), [death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), [rage](ref/spell/rage),
[sickening entanglement](ref/spell/sickening-entanglement); **Price** 37,815 gp
