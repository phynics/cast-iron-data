**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 254\
**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,248 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

Three colorful silk ribbons tied at the base of this elegant, ornately chased +1
[glaive](ref/eq-weapon/glaive) flutter gracefully with every movement and never seem to get dirty.
The glaive grants a +4 sacred bonus on one [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) skill (chosen when the
weapon is created). The wielder retains this bonus so long as the weapon remains within 10 feet,
which allows two-handed instruments to be played. Additionally, once per day the wielder may use
_[color spray](ref/spell/color-spray)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[color spray](ref/spell/color-spray), [eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor); **Price** 2,778
gp
