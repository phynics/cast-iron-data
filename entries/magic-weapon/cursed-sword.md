**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This longsword performs well against targets in practice, but when used in combat its wielder takes
a -2 penalty on attack rolls.

All damage dealt is also reduced by 2 points, but never below a minimum of 1 point of damage on any
successful hit. The sword always forces that character to employ it rather than another weapon. The
sword's owner automatically draws it and fights with it even when she meant to draw or ready some
other weapon.
