**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 155\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 35,312 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This massive +1 [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon)
[disruption](ref/magic-enh/disruption) [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer) is crafted from a single
piece of metal. It surface has no blemishes or distinguishing marks, and its haft is wrapped in dry,
leathery, ancient skin. Three times per day as a free action, when used to strike an undead
creature, that creature must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or be knocked
[prone](ref/rule/prone).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[heal](ref/spell/heal), [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Price** 17,812 gp
