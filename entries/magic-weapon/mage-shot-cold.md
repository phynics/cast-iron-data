## Mage Shot

**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 178 gp; **Weight** -

When propelled from a firearm, this +1
[alchemical paper cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge) changes the damage, depending on
the type of mage shot.

**Acid:** The damage changes to acid damage. If the target takes acid damage from the cartridge, it
takes an additional 2d4 points of acid damage for the next 2 rounds or until the acid is
neutralized. This damage doesn't stack with multiple shots or with acid arrow.

**Cold:** The damage changes to cold damage. If the target takes cold damage from the cartridge, it
must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude saving throw or be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d4 rounds.

**Electricity:** The damage changes to electricity damage. If the target takes electricity damage
from the cartridge, it must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude saving throw or be [dazed](ref/misc/dazed)
for 1 round.

**Fire:** The damage changes to fire damage. If the target takes fire damage from the cartridge, it
must succeed at a DC 14 Reflex saving throw or [catch on fire](ref/rule/catching-on-fire), taking an
additional 1d6 points of fire damage on that round and on subsequent rounds, until the flames are
extinguished.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor) and
[acid arrow](ref/spell/acid-arrow) (acid), [cone of cold](ref/spell/cone-of-cold) (cold),
[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt) (electricity), or
[scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray) (fire); **Price** 98 gp
