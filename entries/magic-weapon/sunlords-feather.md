**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,100 gp; **Weight** -

Named after [Sarenrae's](ref/faith/sarenrae) herald,
[Sunlord Thalachos](ref/monster/herald-sunlord-thalachos), this +1 [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
[holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) [arrow](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) has a platinum arrowhead and brilliant white
fletching. When the arrow successfully strikes an evil-aligned creature, it explodes in a shower of
golden sparks that summons a swarm of celestial doves (treat as a
[celestial](ref/template/celestial) [bat swarm](ref/monster/bat-bat-swarm)) that continues to attack
the arrow's target for 10 rounds or until destroyed or dismissed. If the target is slain before the
duration expires, the remaining doves fly off into the horizon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike), [holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite),
[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm), creator must be good; **Cost** 550 gp
