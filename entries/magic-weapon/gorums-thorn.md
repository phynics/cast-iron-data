**Sources:** Pathfinder \#92: The Hill Giant's Pledge pg 33\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,350 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This forge-blackened +1 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword) is
festooned with religious iconography depicting glorious images of the Lord in Iron. If
[Gorum](ref/faith/gorum) is the wielder's patron, Gorum's Thorn gains an additional ability: when
the wielder confirms a critical hit against any creature with the giant subtype, he can attempt to
[trip](ref/rule/trip) that target as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity and
ignoring any size restrictions related to the target. If the trip attempt fails by 10 or more, the
wielder is not knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), [righteous might](ref/spell/righteous-might); **Price** 6,350 gp
