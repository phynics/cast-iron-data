**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 21\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 80 gp; **Weight** -

This +1 [arrow](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) is as black as the darkest night and is fletched with sleek
raven feathers. If the arrow strikes a foe, magical darkness infuses the creature's sight. The
creature counts the light level as _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_ for 1 round, regardless of the
actual light level, and if the creature has [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), it must succeed at a
DC 13 Fortitude saving throw or lose its darkvision for 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness); **Price** 40 gp
