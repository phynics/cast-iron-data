**Aura** faint enchantment and evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,815 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

The head of this +1 [heavy flail](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-flail) looks like a human skull with wicked
spikes growing out of it. At will, the wielder can affect a creature struck by the obliviating flail
with _[lesser confusion](ref/spell/confusion-lesser)_ as a free action (a successful DC 11 Will
saving throw negates this effect). If the struck opponent fails its save against the
_[lesser confusion](ref/spell/confusion-lesser)_ effect, the wielder of the obliviating flail is
automatically affected by this effect as well (no saving throw). If the wielder uses the flail to
perform a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) and successfully kills her target, she gains 1d4
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points), which last for 1 minute per HD of the target
slain.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), [lesser confusion](ref/spell/confusion-lesser); **Price**
2,565 gp
