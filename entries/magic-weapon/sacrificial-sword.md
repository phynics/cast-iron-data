**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 11\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,235 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This finely crafted +2 [bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword) has three blood-red gems
embedded in its pommel. When the wielder kills a living creature (even herself) with the sacrificial
sword, she can transfer the life force of that creature into the body of a dead or dying ally up to
50 feet away as a swift action. The transferred life force acts as a reach
_[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life)_ spell. Each time this ability is activated, all color
drains from one of the embedded gems. When all three gems are clear, the sacrificial sword can no
longer transfer life energy, but it can still be used as a +2 bastard sword.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Reach Spell](ref/feat/reach-spell), [breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life); **Price** 14,215 gp
