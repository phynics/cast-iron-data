## Leng Flail

**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 25\
**Aura** moderate transmutation (lesser) or moderate transmutation and enchantment (greater); **CL**
6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** lesser (8,315 gp), greater (50,315 gp); **Weight** 10 lbs.

The immense and inscrutable [Leng spiders](ref/monster/leng-spider) are known to construct makeshift
flails by linking nearby debris, such as rocks or skulls, with their own extruded web fibers.
Although this organic webbing typically degrades over time, skillful alchemy can preserve one of
these weapons indefinitely.

A lesser Leng flail is a +1 [heavy flail](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-flail) that allows its wielder to use
_[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb)_ at will. Much less common are greater Leng flails, which
are +2 [throwing](ref/magic-enh/throwing) heavy flails that have the same
_[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb)_ ability of the lesser flail. In addition, when the wielder
of a greater Leng flail confirms a critical hit against a creature, the target suffers horrific
hallucinations for 1d4 rounds, becoming [confused](ref/misc/confused) (Will DC 19 negates). This is
a mind-affecting effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor);
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb) (lesser) or [confusion](ref/spell/confusion),
[magic stone](ref/spell/magic-stone), _[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb)_ (greater); **Price**
4,315 gp (lesser), 25,315 gp (greater)
