**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 201\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,315 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

The head of this weapon resembles a knot of entwined serpents. In this form, it can be used as a
[masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-armor) [heavy mace](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-mace). Upon command, the
head separates into three individual snakes joined to the weapon's handle by their tails, and the
weapon acts as a +1 [speed](ref/magic-enh/speed) [heavy flail](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-flail). Speaking
the command word again makes the weapon revert to its mace form.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[haste](ref/spell/haste); **Price** 16,165 gp
