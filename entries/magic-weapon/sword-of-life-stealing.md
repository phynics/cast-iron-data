**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 162, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 471\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,715 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This black iron +2 [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) bestows a
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) when it deals a critical hit. The sword
wielder gains 1d6 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) each time a negative level
is bestowed on another. These temporary hit points last for 24 hours. One day after being struck,
subjects must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save for each negative level gained or any such levels
become permanent.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[enervation](ref/spell/enervation); **Price** 13,015 gp
