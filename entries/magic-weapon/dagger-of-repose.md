**Sources:** Feast of Dust pg 7\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,902 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This short, curved dagger shimmers with folded layers of iron and steel. It acts as a +1
undead-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger). Once per day when the dagger of
repose is used to perform a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) to slay a living creature, the
wielder can bless the victim's corpse, preventing the victim from rising as an undead because of
ongoing diseases or curse effects. This protection also prevents the victim's corpse from being
animated by necromancy magic such as [animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead) or
[create undead](ref/spell/create-undead) for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[sanctify corpse](ref/spell/sanctify-corpse), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1);
**Price** 4,602 gp
