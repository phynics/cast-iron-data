**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 306 gp; **Weight** -

This animated wood and metal snake squirms and hisses when handled, attempting to bite its holder
just as a real snake might. To use the snakebiter arrow as a projectile, the bearer must first
succeed at a DC 10 [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) or
[wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) check to get the snake to shape itself into a +1
[seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking) [arrow](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) or a +1 seeking
[bolt](ref/eq-weapon/crossbow-bolts) (this is considered a trick known by the snake) for 1 minute.
Failure means the snake attacks the bearer for 1 round, using the statistics of a Tiny
[viper](ref/monster/familiar-viper) familiar. A target struck by the projectile must succeed at a
saving throw or be affected by a powerful variant
[black adder venom](ref/eq-poison/black-adder-venom) (Fortitude DC 20). Normally, these items are
carried in a pouch rather than a quiver to prevent them from getting loose.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [poison](ref/spell/poison),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Price** 156 gp
