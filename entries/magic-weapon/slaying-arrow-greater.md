## Slaying Arrow

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 160, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 471\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** normal (2,282 gp), greater (4,057 gp); **Weight** 1/10 lb.

This +1 [arrow](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) is keyed to a particular type or subtype of creature. If it
strikes such a creature, the target must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or take 50 points of
damage. Note that even creatures normally exempt from Fortitude saves (undead and constructs) are
subject to this attack. When keyed to a living creature, this is a death effect (and thus
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward) protects a target). To determine the type or subtype of creature
the arrow is keyed to, roll on the table below.

A greater slaying arrow functions just like a normal slaying arrow, but the DC to avoid the death
effect is 23 and the arrow deals 100 points of damage if the saving throw is failed.
