**Sources:** Paths of the Righteous pg 21\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,320 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

When the command word is spoken as a move action, this +1 [morningstar](ref/eq-weapon/morningstar)
transforms into a pendant in the shape of a single red rose that does not radiate magic. Only
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) or similar magic reveals the true nature of this weapon.

Once per day as part of an initiative check, as long as she holds the everbloom thorn in its pendant
shape in one hand, the wielder can cause the pendant to instantaneously transform back into its +1
morningstar form. When she does so, the wielder of the everbloom thorn adds her Charisma bonus
(minimum of +1) to her initiative check.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura), [shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** 3,160 gp
