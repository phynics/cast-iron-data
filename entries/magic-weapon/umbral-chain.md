**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 257\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,825 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This bloodstained spiked chain has a large handle-ring that glows a pale violet color when wielded
in normal light. An umbral chain grows more potent in darkness. It acts as a +1
[spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain) when in [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light). In
darkness, it acts as a +2 spiked chain. When surrounded by supernatural darkness (such as in an area
of _[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness)_), it acts as a +2 spiked chain and grants the
wielder the [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) feat. In daylight or bright illumination, the weapon
temporarily loses all its magical enhancement bonuses and is effectively a
[masterwork weapon](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons), though it resumes its magical functions once it's
no longer in bright light. The violet glow from the weapon dims in response to ambient light,
vanishing completely in darkness or supernatural darkness.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness); **Price** 4,575 gp
