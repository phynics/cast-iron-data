**Sources:** Undead Slayer's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,002 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The black-lacquered handle of this slender blade is inlaid with the image of a broken skull. A
master's woe stiletto is a +1 [living steel](ref/eq-material/living-steel)
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) that allows its wielder to weaken a would-be necromancer's control
over the undead. Three times per day when the wielder successfully strikes a target with the
stiletto, the wielder can activate the stiletto as a free action to force the target to attempt a DC
13 Will save. On a failure, the number of undead creatures the target can control through spells
like _[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead)_ or feats like
[Command Undead](ref/feat/command-undead) is reduced by 6 Hit Dice (to a minimum of 1 HD). Any
excess undead become uncontrolled, but their former controller may choose which creatures are
released.

This effect lasts for 3 hours and cannot be [dispelled](ref/spell/dispel-magic), though it can be
removed by [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), [break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment),
or similar effects. Any uncontrolled undead remain uncontrolled when the effect ends.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[necromantic burden](ref/spell/necromantic-burden); **Price** 5,402 gp
