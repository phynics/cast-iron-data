**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 226\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 35,005 gp; **Weight** 16 lbs.

The following weapon is generally available only to members of the indicated race or ethnicity.

This +2 [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding) [harpoon](ref/eq-weapon/harpoon) has a head carved from
whalebone, and has been fitted with a grip akin to that of a calvary lance. It deals double damage
to and ignores the [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of wooden objects, including most ships. These
weapons frequently appear among Absalom's Sea Cavalry, and are responsible for many of the wrecks in
the Flotsam Graveyard.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [warp wood](ref/spell/warp-wood), creator must be a gillman; **Price**
17,655 gp
