**Sources:** Mystery Monsters Revisited pg 12\
**Aura** moderate evocation and transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 24,304 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

A curved yellow fang juts from the wooden haft of this light pick in place of a metal tip. Though
the fang seems fragile, the enchantments on the weapon render it as hard as steel, and the fang
evokes some of the powers of the beast from which it came. A chupar pick functions as a +1
[light pick](ref/eq-weapon/light-pick) of [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding). When the chupar pick
scores a critical hit, its wielder is affected by a _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_ effect for 7 rounds
as if targeted by a _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_ spell. Subsequent critical hits made while the
wielder is affected extend the duration of the _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_ effect but have no
additional effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [haste](ref/spell/haste), one [chupacabra](ref/monster/chupacabra) fang;
**Price** 12,304 gp
