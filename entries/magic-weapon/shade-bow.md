**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 41,535 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This +2 [distance](ref/magic-enh/distance) [light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) shrouds
its wielder in shadows, granting her a +4 competence bonus on Stealth checks. Three times per day,
the wielder can also become invisible, as per the _invisibility_ spell, for up to 6 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), creator must have 4 ranks in Stealth; **Price** 20,935 gp
