**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 27\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and enchantment \[mind-affecting, sonic\]; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 11,901 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This leathern lash is dyed with special unguents that give it a disturbing crimson hue. A tamer's
whip is a +1 animal-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) that deals lethal damage
to animals and magical beasts regardless of their natural armor bonus. Cracking a tamer's whip as a
move action grants the wielder the following benefits until the end of her turn: a +4 competence
bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks made against animals and magical beasts, a +4
competence bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks made to push them, and a +2
bonus on any [combat maneuvers](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) against animals or magical beasts.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[animal trance](ref/spell/animal-trance), [snare](ref/spell/snare),
[summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1); **Price** 6,101 gp
