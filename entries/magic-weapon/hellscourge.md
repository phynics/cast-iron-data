**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 156\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 39,305 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This +1 [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) [scorpion whip](ref/eq-weapon/scorpion-whip) is fashioned
from skin flayed from a [barbed devil's](ref/monster/devil-barbed-devil-hamatula) back. It grants
its wielder a +5 competence bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. A
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) creature struck by the hellscourge must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or
become [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for 1 round. A frightened or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked)
creature struck must instead save or [cower](ref/misc/cowering) for 1d4 rounds. Both of these are
mind-affecting fear effects.

If wielded by a person who wears [hamatula hide](ref/magic-armor/hamatula-hide) armor, the
hellscourge functions as a +1 [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) unholy [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip).
Such a wielder can lash the whip once per day to hurl flames as a
_[scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray)_ (creating two rays); doing so extinguishes the whip's
flames until the next day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[fear](ref/spell/fear), [scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray),
[unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight); **Price** 19,805 gp
