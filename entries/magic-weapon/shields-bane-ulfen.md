**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 227\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 20,310 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

The following weapon is generally available only to members of the indicated race or ethnicity.

Ulfen raiders traditionally fight carrying a shield alongside a battleaxe or sword, leading to an
arms race between armorers who seek to produce unstoppable weapons and those who seek to make
impenetrable shields. Shields' bane is a long-bearded +1 [battleaxe](ref/eq-weapon/battleaxe) that
ignores all shield bonuses to the target's AC. Additionally, the wielder gains a +4 competence bonus
on combat maneuver checks to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) or [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) a foe of
equipment that grants a shield bonus.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shatter](ref/spell/shatter), [true strike](ref/spell/true-strike), creator must be an Ulfen;
**Price** 10,310 gp
