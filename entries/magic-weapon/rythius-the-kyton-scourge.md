**Sources:** Champions of Purity pg 31\
**Aura** strong abjuration and evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 53,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Once possessed by a [brijidine azata](ref/monster/azata-brijidine) dedicated to ending the
depredations of [kytons](ref/family/kyton), this potent magic weapon now holds a portion of her
essence. This +1 [shock](ref/magic-enh/shock) [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) is woven from green leather
and crackles with electricity, seemingly writhing of its own volition. Three times per day, when the
wielder uses Rythius to successfully [trip](ref/rule/trip) a creature, the target of the trip attack
is subject to a _[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ effect. The wielder can choose
to suppress this ability as a swift action.

Furthermore, in the hands of a wielder with a good alignment, the weapon becomes a +1
kyton-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) shock whip. Rythius is infused with good, and as a result it
bestows 1 [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on any nongood creature
wielding it. This negative level persists as long as the weapon is wielded and disappears when the
item is put away. The negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including by means of
[restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while it is wielded.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor), [holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite), creator must
be good; **Price** 26,650 gp
