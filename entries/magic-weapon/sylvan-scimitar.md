**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 162, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 471\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 47,315 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

When used outdoors in a temperate climate, this +3 [scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar) grants its
wielder the use of the [Cleave](ref/feat/cleave) feat and deals an extra 1d6 points of damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[divine power](ref/spell/divine-power) or creator must be a 7th-level druid; **Price** 23,815 gp
