**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 59,700 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This +2 [lesser designating](ref/magic-enh/designating-lesser)
[composite longbow](ref/eq-weapon/composite-longbow) (+3 Str) has a bowstring that resembles a
thorny vine. Once per day as a standard action, the wielder can prick her finger on the vine and
fire an arrow at a creature or square to cause the arrow to become a
_[wall of thorns](ref/spell/wall-of-thorns)_ centered on the struck creature or object. When the
wielder is targeting a square, treat that as a ranged attack against AC 5. The wielder can choose
how the _[wall of thorns](ref/spell/wall-of-thorns)_ is oriented.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[instant enemy](ref/spell/instant-enemy), [wall of thorns](ref/spell/wall-of-thorns); **Price**
30,200 gp
