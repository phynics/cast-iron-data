**Sources:** Pathfinder \#91: Battle of Bloodmarch Hills pg 35\
**Aura** moderate transmutation \[good\]; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,052 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The fire-blackened blade of this hopeknife bears the inscription "my love" in stark silver and glows
with light equivalent to a [light](ref/spell/light) spell when drawn. Brinya's Love is a +1
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) infused with the soul of the fallen hero Rodrik Grath and the tears
of his fiancee Brinya Kelver. Once per day on command, the wielder can grant the weapon the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon special ability for 1 minute. During this
time, Brinya's Love sheds light equivalent to a _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ spell and the
wielder gains a +3 sacred bonus to his CMD against [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) and
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) attempts directed at the hopeknife.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[blade of bright victory](ref/spell/blade-of-bright-victory), [daylight](ref/spell/daylight);
**Price** 2,677 gp
