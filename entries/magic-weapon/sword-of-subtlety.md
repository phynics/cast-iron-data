**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 22,310 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

A +1 [short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword) with a thin, dull gray blade, this weapon provides a
+4 bonus on its wielder's attack and damage rolls when she makes a
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) with it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[blur](ref/spell/blur); **Price** 11,310 gp
