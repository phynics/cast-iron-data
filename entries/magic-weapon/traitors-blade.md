**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 25, Lost Treasures pg 59\
**Aura** strong necromancy and enchantment; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 50,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This +2 [wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding) [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) allows the wielder to use
_[charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster)_ (DC 18) three times per day. When the wielder hits a
creature affected by the dagger's charm effect, the dagger deals an additional 2d6 points of damage
to that creature, even if the creature is no longer charmed because of the hostile action. All other
creatures charmed by the blade that see this act of betrayal become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened)
for 1d4 rounds, but are immediately freed from the charm effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster),
[inflict moderate wounds](ref/spell/inflict-moderate-wounds); **Price** 25,302 gp
