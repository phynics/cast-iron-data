**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 160, Advanced Player's Guide pg 291\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 21,324 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

A faint trail of sparks marks the passage of this +1 [shock](ref/magic-enh/shock)
[starknife](ref/eq-weapon/starknife) whenever it is thrown. Once per day, its wielder may throw it
so it transforms into an 8th-level _[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt)_ (Reflex DC 14 half),
returning to its physical form at the end of the bolt's path.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt); **Price** 10,824 gp
