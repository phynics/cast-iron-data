**Sources:** Pathfinder \#74: Sword of Valor pg 63\
**Aura** strong conjuration \[evil\]; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 45,508 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

Forged by Staunton Vhane with the aid of several Templars of the Ivory Labyrinth, this intelligent
+2 [transformative](ref/magic-enh/transformative) [glaive](ref/eq-weapon/glaive) is infused with a
[babau's](ref/monster/demon-babau) sentience and eagerness for murder. Soulshear is capable of
hiding its evil nature with [misdirection](ref/spell/misdirection), and if its wielder's alignment
isn't chaotic evil, it delights in infiltrating enemy forces to create personality conflicts with
its wielder, manipulating his downfall through acts of conspiracy, betrayal, and murder. Even
without dominating those who carry it, Soulshear can occasionally summon a babau to clandestinely
carry out its will. These summonings often result in unexplained murders, or even the death of
Soulshear's owner so the glaive can pass to further victims or be returned to the devoted cultists
of [Baphomet](ref/faith/baphomet).

Soulshear can use _[misdirection](ref/spell/misdirection)_ on itself three times per day, typically
selecting an unaligned magic item carried by its wielder to hide its evil. Once per day, it can cast
[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5), but only to summon a babau demon.
