**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 30\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,301 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This steel tankard functions as a +1 [light hammer](ref/eq-weapon/light-hammer). Worshipers of
[Cayden Cailean](ref/faith/cayden-cailean) are automatically proficient with it. The interior of the
tankard contains six identical extradimensional spaces. Each space can hold 1 dose of a different
potable liquid of up to 1/2 cubic foot in volume (including potions, but not poisons). Liquid never
sloshes out of the these spaces accidentally (though it often appears ready to do so), and when the
wielder drinks from the tankard, it provides the drink from the desired extradimensional pocket (or
a random pocket, if the wielder has no preference).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [secret chest](ref/spell/secret-chest);
**Price** 5,301 gp
