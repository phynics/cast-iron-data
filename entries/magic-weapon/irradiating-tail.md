**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 30\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,315 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The end of this +1 [viridium](ref/eq-material/viridium)
[long lash](ref/carried/advanced-race-guide/races/kobolds/new-racial-rules/kobold-equipment/kobold-tail-attachments)
tail attachment is a shard of toxic green rock. As with any viridium weapon, a successful hit with
an irradiating tail causes the target to contract [leprosy](ref/disease/leprosy) - and a critical
hit can poison the target with [greenblood oil](ref/eq-poison/greenblood-oil). However, unlike with
most viridium weapons, the wielder of an irradiating tail is magically immune to the weapon's toxic
effects. The viridium has been strengthened, so the weapon isn't fragile.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease); **Price** 2,815 gp
