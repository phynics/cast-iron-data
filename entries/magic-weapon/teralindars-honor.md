**Aura** strong conjuration and divination; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 48,535 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This +2 aberration-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [bastard sword](ref/eq-weapon/bastard-sword) is made
of magically hardened [bronze](ref/eq-material/bronze) and has the special purpose of killing
aberrations and creatures intrinsically tied to [Xhamen-Dor](ref/faith/xhamen-dor) (including seeded
undead and the dormant Star Seed). Upianshe's spirit can sense such foes within 60 feet, her bane
enchantment applies to them, and against them her critical hit multiplier increases to ×3. Finally,
she can attempt [Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering) checks as though she
had 5 ranks in the skill (total +13 bonus).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[detect aberration](ref/spell/detect-aberration), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1);
**Price** 24,435 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** chaotic neutral; **Ego** 17\
**Senses** 60 ft., [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), read magic\
**Int** 20, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 14\
**Communication** not listed (Aklo, Azlanti, Common, Draconic, Kelish, Vudrani)
