**Sources:** Heaven Unleashed pg 55\
**Aura** moderate conjuration, strong evil; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

When this curved +1 [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) is used to perform a successful
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) attack on a human, it gains the human-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane)
weapon special ability for the next 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Price** 4,302 gp
