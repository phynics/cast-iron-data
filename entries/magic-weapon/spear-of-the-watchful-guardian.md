**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** 8,462 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This magically strengthened bronze-tipped +1 [spear](ref/eq-weapon/spear) enables its wielder to
better face a variety of foes. Originally created by priests of [Nethys](ref/faith/nethys) for use
by their temple guards, a spear of the watchful guardian also grants its wielder arcane aid to
strike true. Once per day as a standard action, the wielder of a spear of the watchful guardian can
alter the physical properties of the weapon, allowing it to bypass
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) of one of the following types: bludgeoning, cold iron,
silver, or slashing. This change lasts for 5 minutes.

In addition, if the wielder of a spear of the watchful guardian fails an attack roll, as an
immediate action once per day, she can retroactively add a +1 competence bonus on that roll. If this
bonus is enough to make the failure a success, the attack roll succeeds.

Finally, a spear of the watchful guardian sharpens the senses of its wielder, granting her a +2
insight bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[timely inspiration](ref/spell/timely-inspiration), [versatile weapon](ref/spell/versatile-weapon);
**Price** 4,382 gp
