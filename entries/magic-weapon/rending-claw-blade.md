**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,305 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This set of +1 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [claw blades](ref/eq-weapon/claw-blades) is most effective
when the wearer has multiple claw attacks per round. When making a full attack with claws, if the
wielder hits the same target with this weapon and a different claw attack or set of claw blades, he
deals an extra 1d4 damage plus 1-1/2 times his Strength bonus. The wearer can deal this extra damage
no more than once per round. This counts as a [rend](ref/umr/rend) attack and does not stack with
other abilities that grant rend attacks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Price** 5,305 gp
