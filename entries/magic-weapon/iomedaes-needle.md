**Aura** strong divination and necromancy; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** 8,016 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This brilliant [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) ballista bolt radiates a blinding sheen. Its
fletching is made of white and gold feathers.

This +1 [light ballista bolt](ref/eq-siegeengine/ballista-light) functions as a
[greater undead slaying arrow](ref/magic-weapon/slaying-arrow-greater), except its enchantment is so
potent that it deals 50 points of damage even if the saving throw is successful. If the wielder
utters the name of his target as the bolt is fired, he gains the benefits of
_[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike)_ on the attack roll.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike), [undeath to death](ref/spell/undeath-to-death); **Price**
4,016 gp
