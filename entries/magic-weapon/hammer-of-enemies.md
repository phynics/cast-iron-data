**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 54,212 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This special +3 [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer) seeks only to crush
the enemy of its wielder in battle. The hammer can speak Common, constantly mutters, curses when it
is not engaged in battle, and sings deeply of its love of carnage when it is being used in a fight.
The hammer of enemies is always eager to point out potential enemies within its vision. Because of
its bloodlust it does not bestow [negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on
creatures that do not share its alignment - it is willing to fight for anyone. Additionally, three
times per day, the hammer of enemies can change the creature type of its bane ability. A creature
type remains until a new one is chosen.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor);
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4); **Price** 27,262 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** chaotic neutral; **Ego** 14\
**Senses** 30 ft., [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)\
**Int** 10, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 14\
**Communication** speech (Common)
