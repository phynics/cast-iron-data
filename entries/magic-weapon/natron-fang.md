**Sources:** Pathfinder \#80: Empty Graves pg 63\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon; **Price** 7,280 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

Carved from a single chunk of magically strengthened mineral salts, a natron fang is a +1
[khopesh](ref/eq-weapon/khopesh) forged to combat the undead, specifically mummies and incorporeal
undead. A natron fang overcomes the [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) of
[mummies](ref/monster/mummy), mummified creatures, and other mummy-like creatures at the GM's
discretion. Once per day on a successful strike against an incorporeal undead creature, a natron
fang also deals 1d6 points of [Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) (Will DC 13 half) in
addition to the weapon's normal damage.
