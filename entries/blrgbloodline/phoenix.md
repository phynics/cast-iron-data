## Phoenix Bloodline

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#144: Midwives to Death pg 82

One of your ancestors may have witnessed the fiery resurrection of a [phoenix](ref/monster/phoenix)
or been healed by the grace of this legendary bird. Whatever the case, the flames of the phoenix
burn brightly within your soul, filling you with an inextinguishable vitality that can withstand the
most harrowing of assaults. When you _bloodrage,_ vibrant energy boils forth from beneath your skin,
granting you both the soothing warmth to heal a friend's wounds and the brutal power to burn flesh
from bone. Your rage is an awesome and terrible thing to behold, as the raw power of your untamed
life force can allow you to pull yourself back from the grasp of death itself.

### Bloodline Powers

When you _bloodrage,_ you channel the molten fires of the [phoenix](ref/monster/phoenix) to sear
your foes and fill your allies with an unquenchable vigor.

**Dispelling Strikes (Su)**

**Ability:** When you confirm a critical hit against a target, you can also attempt to dispel the
target as if you had cast [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) as a bloodrager spell and used the
targeted dispel function. You can dispel only a single magical effect per use of this ability.

**At 20th Level:** You can attempt to dispel all magical effects on your target when using this
ability.

**At 8th Level:** You gain a +2 bonus on your dispel check when using this ability.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** No limits on usage, except a creature can only be targeted once per day.

**Usage:** No limits on usage, except a creature can only be targeted once per day.

**Heart of Fire (Su)**

**At 8th Level:** Your fire resistance increases to 10, and you regain 2 additional hit points per
die rolled when you are healed by a _cure_ spell.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5. Whenever you are subjected to a
magical healing effect from a cure spell, you regain 1 additional hit point per die rolled.

**Blazing Vitality (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** When tensions run high and your emotions flare, you let forth waves of restorative
energy from within. You emit a 10-foot-radius aura of energizing fire while _bloodraging._ Any ally
that ends their turn within this aura gains a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to your Constitution modifier. These
temporary hit points last for 1 minute.

**Molten Wings (Su)**

**Ability:** When entering a _bloodrage_ you can choose to have wings of flame sprout from your
back, giving you a fly speed of 60 feet with average maneuverability.

**At 16th Level:** Your fly speed increases to 80 feet with good maneuverability.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Self-Resurrection (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** This instead functions as a [heal](ref/spell/heal) spell, except that the healing
can return you to life as per _[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life),_ using your bloodrager
level as your caster level.

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** From the ashes of your body springs forth new life. Once per day when you are reduced
below 0 hit points while you are _bloodraging,_ you can call upon the power in your blood to pull
yourself back from death. This functions as a [breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life) spell cast
upon yourself. Using this ability does not take an action. This ability does not function if your
body is completely destroyed by an effect such as [disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate).

**Phoenix Fire (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** The power of the phoenix brings righteous destruction to any who oppose it. While
_bloodraging,_ you gain the following effects: your melee attacks deal an additional 2d6 points of
fire damage, any enemies within 20 feet of you must succeed at a Reflex save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your
bloodrager level + your Constitution modifier) or take 4d6 points of fire damage at the start of
their turn, and any creature that attacks you with a natural or non-reach weapon takes 1d6 points of
fire damage (no save) with each successful hit.
