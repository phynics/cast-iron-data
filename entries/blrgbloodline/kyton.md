### Bloodline Powers

The sadistic power of the kytons flows through you, twisting you into a creature of pain and
darkness when you _bloodrage._

**Painful Strike (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** When you confirm a critical hit, the target is [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for a
number of rounds equal to 1/2 your bloodrager level (minimum 1). During this time, the creature must
succeed at a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC = 15 + the spell's level) in order to
cast a spell.

**Grasping Chains (Su)**

**At 9th Level:** The bonus increases to +8 and you gain a Climb speed equal to 1/2 your base speed.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** Chains cover your body and extend from your arms, grasping at the air around you and
granting you a +4 bonus on Climb skill checks.

**Armor of Chains (Su)**

**At 16th Level:** The resistance increases to 10 and the armor bonus increases to +8.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 to cold and the chains covering your body
provide a +4 armor bonus to AC with no armor check penalty, maximum Dexterity bonus, or additional
weight.

**Agony's Embrace (Su)**

**At 16th Level:** The bonus to Strength instead increases by 4.

**At 20th Level:** The bonus to Strength instead increases by +6.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** Whenever you're hit by a critical hit, your morale bonus to Strength from your
_bloodrage_ increases by 2 for 1d6 rounds or until your _rage_ ends.

**Unnerving Gaze (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** Your foes begin to see flashes of the faces of dead loved ones when they look at your
face. You gain a [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack with a range of 30 feet. All foes who fail a Will save
(DC = 10 + 1/2 your bloodrager level + your Charisma modifier) become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for
1d3 rounds.

**Kyton Immunities (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to cold and DR 10/good and silver. You have this
benefit constantly, even when not _bloodraging._
