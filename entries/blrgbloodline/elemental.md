## Elemental Bloodline

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 20

The power of the elements resides in you, and at times you can hardly control its fury. This
influence comes either from an elemental outsider in your family history or from a moment when you
or your ancestors were exposed to a powerful elemental force or cataclysm.

### Bloodline Powers

One of the four elements infuses your being, and you can draw upon its power while _bloodraging._ At
first level, you must select an element: air, earth, fire, or water. Once chosen, this cannot be
changed. A number of your abilities deal damage and grant resistances based on your element, as
noted below.

**Element:** Choose one.

| --- | --- | --- |
|Air|Electricity|Fly 60 feet (good)|

**Elemental Strikes (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** All of your melee attacks deal this damage, and this ability no longer requires a
swift action to activate.

**At 8th Level:** You can now use this five times a day.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Swift:** You can imbue your melee attacks with elemental energy. For 1 round, your melee attacks
deal 1d6 points of additional damage of your energy type.

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain energy [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 against your energy type.

**Elemental Movement (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You gain a special movement type or bonus based on your chosen element, as indicated on
the above table.

**Power of the Elements (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** The energy damage done by your _elemental strikes_ bloodline ability bypasses
resistance to that energy type, and still deals half damage to creatures with
[immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to energy of that type.

**Elemental Form (Su)**

**Ability:** When entering a _bloodrage_ you can take an elemental form as
[elemental body IV](ref/spell/elemental-body-4).

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Usage:** Once a day.

**Usage:** Once a day.

**Elemental Body (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Elemental power surges through your body. You gain immunity to
[sneak attacks](ref/ability/sneak-attack), critical hits, and damage from your energy type. You have
this benefit constantly, even while not _bloodraging._
