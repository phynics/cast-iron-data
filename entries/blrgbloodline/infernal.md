The Pit lives in your blood. Maybe one of your ancestors was seduced by the powers of
[Hell](ref/rule/hell) or made a deal with a [devil](ref/family/devil). Either way, its corruption

### Bloodline Powers

When you _bloodrage,_ hellfire infuses your blood with terrifying effects.

**Hellfire Strike (Su)**

**At 12th Level:** You can use this ability five times per day, and the effect is treated as
[flaming burst](ref/magic-enh/flaming-burst).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Swift:** You can infuse your attacks with hellfire. When you do, your melee attacks gain the
[flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon special ability for 1 round.

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Infernal Resistance (Ex)**

**At 8th Level:** Your fire resistance increases to 10, and the bonus on saving throws against
poison increases to +4.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5, as well as a +2 bonus on saving
throws against poison.

**Diabolical Arrogance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against enchantment and fear effects.

**Dark Wings (Su)**

**Ability:** When entering a _bloodrage_ you can choose to have bat-like wings grow from your back,
giving you a fly speed of 60 feet with average maneuverability.

**At 16th Level:** Your fly speed increases to 80 feet with good maneuverability.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Hellfire Charge (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** When you [charge](ref/rule/charge) the attack you make at the end of the charge gains
the benefit of your _hellfire strike_ without expending a use of it. If you already have _hellfire
strike_ active, the fire damage ignores fire resistance of 10 or lower.

**Fiend of the Pit (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to fire and poison. You also gain resistance 10
to acid and cold, and gain the [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) ability. You have these
benefits constantly, even while not _bloodraging._
