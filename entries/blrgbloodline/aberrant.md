### Bloodline Powers

While _bloodraging,_ you gain the abilities and immunities of some
[aberrations](ref/type/aberration), but show signs of your tainted heritage.

**Staggering Strike (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** When you confirm a critical hit the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or
be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 your
bloodrager level + your Constitution modifier. These effects stack with the
[Staggering Critical](ref/feat/staggering-critical) feat; the target must save against each effect
individually.

**Abnormal Reach (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** Your limbs elongate; your reach increases by 5 feet.

**Aberrant Fortitude (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You become immune to the [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) and
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) conditions.

**Unusual Anatomy (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** Your internal anatomy shifts and changes, giving you a 50% chance to negate any
critical hit or [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) that hits you. The damage is instead rolled
normally.

**Aberrant Resistance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** You are immune to disease, exhaustion, fatigue, and poison, and to the staggered
condition.

**Aberrant Form (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Your body becomes truly unnatural. You are immune to critical hits and sneak attacks.
In addition, you gain [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) with a range of 60 feet and your bloodrager
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) increases by 1. You have these benefits constantly,
even while not _bloodraging._
