## Medusa Bloodline

**Sources:** Heroes of Golarion pg 16

No magical creature is more iconic in Iblydan lore than the [medusa](ref/monster/medusa). Perhaps
your lineage traces back to a medusa, or an ancestor survived a medusa's petrifying gaze and was
forever changed by the experience. However the medusa's influence entered your bloodline, when you
_bloodrage,_ you gain fearsome and ancient powers.

### Bloodline Powers

The cold fury of your cursed rage can freeze others in their tracks.

**Gaze (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can harness the power of the medusa through your gaze. Choose a creature within
your line of sight who is within 30 feet of you. The creature must succeed at a Fortitude saving
throw or have its speed halved for a number of rounds equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1
round). The DC of this save is equal to 10 + half your bloodrager level + your Constitution
modifier.

**Gift of the Ancients (Su)**

**At 8th Level:** These bonuses increase to +4.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +2 resistance bonus on saving throws against [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attacks
and to resist poison. You also gain a +2 competence bonus on Perception checks.

**Staggering Gaze (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** When a creature is affected by your
_[gaze](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/gaze)_ bloodline power, it is
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) in addition to being slowed.

**Viper's Touch (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** You grow two sets of venomous claws resembling the teeth of a serpent. These claws are
primary [natural attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deal 1d8 points of damage (1d6 if you are
Small). The claws also gain the following poison ability.

**Viper's Venom**

**Type:** Poison (Claw-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your bloodrager level + your Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d3

**Cure:** 1 save

**Stone Resistance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** You gain acid [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 and are immune to disease, poison,
and the [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) and staggered conditions. You also cannot be
[flanked](ref/rule/flanking).

**True Petrification (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Standard:** You can use your _[gaze](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/gaze)_
bloodline ability to permanently turn a creature to stone, as
[flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone). You can still use the less powerful versions of your
_[gaze](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/gaze)_ if you do not wish to turn the
target to stone, but you must declare this at the time of the attack.
