**Sources:** Advanced Class Origins pg 8

Contact with the [black blood of Orv](ref/magic-artifact/black-blood-of-orv) - by you or one of your
ancestors - transformed your bloodline. This necromantic taint in your blood mutates you into
something peculiar.

### Bloodline Powers

The insidious magic of black blood grants you profane powers and hideous resistances.

**Black Blood-Immunity (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Your blood runs black with the blood of Orv. You are immune to the effects (both
beneficial and destructive) of black blood. You have this benefit constantly, even while not
_bloodraging._

**Black Blood-Cold (Su)**

**Immediate:** When you take damage from a slashing or piercing attack while _bloodraging,_ you can
grant your melee attacks the [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) weapon special ability for 2 rounds.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** 3 times/day

**Usage:** 3 times/day

**Abnormal Reach (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** The black blood pumping within you twists and elongates your limbs. Your reach
increases by 5 feet.

**Black Blood Resistance (Su)**

**At 16th Level:** This resistance increases to 10 and the saving throw bonus increases to +4.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 to cold and a +2 bonus on saving throws
against [ability drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), death effects, disease,
[energy drain](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels), paralysis, and poison.

**Retributive Spray (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** Whenever you're hit by an attack or spell that deals slashing or piercing damage, your
black blood sprays up to 10 feet toward the opponent who attacked you, striking the first creature
along its path. That creature takes an amount of cold damage equal to 1d8 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your bloodrager level. A successful Reflex save (DC =
10 + 1/2 your bloodrager level + your Constitution modifier) halves the damage.

**Black Blood Transfusion (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** When you confirm a critical hit, some of your black blood seeps into the target's
wounds. The next time the target would be healed by positive energy, the healing burns away the
black blood and ends this effect, providing no other benefit. This ability is not effective against
creatures that have no blood. The black blood transfusion persists until it negates one positive
energy healing effect, but it can be removed sooner with a successful DC 25 Heal check.

**Black Blood Immunity (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to cold, nonlethal damage, critical hits, and
[sneak attacks](ref/ability/sneak-attack). You have this benefit constantly, even when not
_bloodraging._
