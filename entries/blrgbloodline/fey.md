## Fey Bloodline

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 21

One of your ancestors was [fey](ref/type/fey), or the fey realm somehow intermixed with your
bloodline. It affects your _bloodrage_ in tricky and surprising ways.

### Bloodline Powers

The power of the natural world saturates your being, and manifests itself when you _bloodrage._

**Confusing Critical (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Fey power courses through your attacks. Each time you confirm a critical hit, the
target must succeed at a Will saving throw or be [confused](ref/misc/confused) for 1 round. The DC
of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 your bloodrager level + your Constitution modifier. This is a
mind-affecting compulsion effect.

**Leaping Charger (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You are able to leap over overgrowth and similar impediments with ease while charging.
When you [charge](ref/rule/charge), you ignore [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles)
(but not other creatures). You can move through it at normal speed, and it does not impede your
charge.

**Blurring Movement (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You become a blur of motion when you move. As long as you move at least 10 feet, you
gain the effects of [blur](ref/spell/blur) for 1 round.

**Quickling Bloodrage (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** While _bloodraging_ you're treated as if you are under the effects of
_[haste](ref/spell/haste)._

**One with Nature (Su)**

**Action:** Move-Equivalent Action

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Usage:** Three times a day.

**Usage:** Three times a day.

You can transport yourself from one tree to another tree, as [tree stride](ref/spell/tree-stride)
with half the transport range.

Creatures of the animal or plant types don't attack you unless compelled to do so by magic or you
attack them first.

You gain these abilities even while not _bloodraging._

**Fury of the Fey (Su)**

**Ability:** When entering a _bloodrage_ you can choose one type of creature (and subtype for
humanoids or outsiders) that can be affected by the [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon special
ability. All of your melee attacks are considered to have _[bane](ref/spell/bane)_ against that
type. This ability doesn't stack with other forms of _[bane](ref/spell/bane)._

**Gained:** At 20th Level
