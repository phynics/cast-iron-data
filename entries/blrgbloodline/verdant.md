## Verdant Bloodline

Your body is suffused with raw plant life. When you _bloodrage,_ your plant-like characteristics
bolster your durability and grant you some control over vegetation.

### Bloodline Powers

While _bloodraging,_ you gain plant-like abilities and immunities as you become overgrown with
jagged branches, rugged bark, and thick vines.

**Verdant Growth (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** You instead gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 4.

**At 13th Level:** You instead gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 5.

**At 16th Level:** You instead gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 6.

**At 19th Level:** You instead gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 7.

**At 4th Level:** You instead gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2.

**At 7th Level:** You instead gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 3.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1 while _bloodraging._ If you have a
bloodrager class feature that also grants you fast healing (such as the
[spelleater's](ref/arc-bloodrager/spelleater) blood of life class feature), you can stack up to half
of your fast healing from such abilities with the fast healing gained from this ability, but you
cannot do so by an amount that exceeds your Charisma bonus (minimum 0).

**Oaken Skin (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** The bonus becomes +4.

**At 16th Level:** The bonus becomes +5.

**At 20th Level:** The bonus becomes +6.

**At 8th Level:** The bonus becomes +3.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** When entering a _bloodrage,_ your skin thickens like bark and you gain a +2 enhancement
bonus to your natural armor.

**Botanical Plasticity (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Swift:** You can bend and twist the plant life within your body to elongate your limbs. Your reach
increases by 5 feet until the end of your turn.

**Verdant Call (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Swift:** When entering a _bloodrage,_ you can animate nearby plants to aid you in combat,
functioning as [wilderness soldiers](ref/spell/wilderness-soldiers), using your Charisma modifier
instead of your Wisdom modifier. This effect lasts for as long as you continue _bloodraging,_
regardless of the spell's normal duration.

**Nature's Thorns (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** Creatures that hit you in melee (other than those using manufactured reach weapons)
take 4d6 points of piercing damage. A creature takes this damage only on its first successful attack
against you in a combat round, and not on subsequent attacks in that round.

**Verdant Apotheosis (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Verdant power surges throughout your being. While _bloodraging,_ you gain
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) out to 30 feet. In addition, you gain immunity to paralysis,
poison, sleep, and stunning effects, even while you are not _bloodraging._
