## Sphinx Bloodline

**Sources:** Heroes of Golarion pg 23

Those who successfully traded riddles with sphinxes sometimes find themselves or their descendants
exhibiting both arcane power and violent fury. These bloodragers find being outwitted or
outmaneuvered especially infuriating; though they are usually scholarly and erudite, their fits of
frenzied rage are all the more terrifying in comparison.

### Bloodline Powers

You gain the ruthless claws and implacable voice of the [sphinx](ref/family/sphinx) while
_bloodraging._

**Claws (Su)**

**At 12th Level:** The critical threat range for your claws increases to 19-20.

**At 4th Level:** Your claws are considered magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 8th Level:** The base damage your claws deal increases to 1d8 points (1d6 if you are small).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You grow claws while _bloodraging._ These claws are treated as
[natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks), allowing you to make two claw attacks as a full attack,
using your full base attack bonus. These attacks deal 1d6 points of damage each (1d4 if you are
Small) plus your Strength modifier.

**Roar (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** The bloodrager can instead cause targets who fail their save to be
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked) or [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) (bloodrager's choice) and
[deafened](ref/misc/deafened) for the duration.

**At 16th Level:** The bloodrager can instead cause targets who fail their save to be
[paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for 1 round.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Standard:** The bloodrager learns the secrets of the sphinx's roar. He can roar as a standard
action a number of times per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier. Enemies within 60 feet of the
bloodrager that can hear the roar must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half the bloodrager's
character level + his Charisma modifier) or become [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for 1d6 rounds.
Whether or not the save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of the bloodrager's roar
again for 24 hours.

**Usage:** Cha3 modifier times/day

**Desert Fortitude (Su)**

**At 6th Level:** Your resistance to electricity and fire increases to 10.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 to electricity and fire. In addition, you
gain [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) as a constant
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) and a +2 competence bonus on saves against arcane
spells.

**Rending Rage (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a [rend](ref/umr/rend) attack, as the universal monster rule, that deals extra
damage equal to 2d4 + 1-1/2 times your Strength modifier.

**At 18th Level:** This damage increases to 2d6 + 1-1/2 times your Strength modifier.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Master of Mysteries (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Standard:** You can cast either [maze](ref/spell/maze) or
[symbol of insanity](ref/spell/symbol-of-insanity) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), with a caster level equal to your character
level. You also gain [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 11 + your level.

**Final Riddle (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to fire and electricity and ignore environmental
penalties due to temperature. Your competence bonus on saving throws against arcane spells increases
to +6. You have these benefits constantly, even while not _raging._
