### Bloodline Powers

**Underwater Attacks (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Your bludgeoning and slashing melee attacks underwater ignore the usual underwater
penalties.

**Aquatic Adaptation (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** Your swim speed increases to 60 feet, and you have the 4th-level benefits of
aquatic adaptation constantly, even when not in _bloodrage_ (but not the 8th- and 12th-level
benefits).

**At 8th Level:** You gain cold [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain a swim speed of 30 feet and the ability to breathe both underwater and in air.

**Watersense (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** You instead gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) with a range of 60 feet when
in water.

**At 8th Level:** You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) with a range of 30 feet in water.

**Wavedarter (Sp, Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** Your _bloodrage_ directs the flow of water to assist you in battle. While underwater,
your reach increases by 5 feet and you gain the effects of [haste](ref/spell/haste).

**Currentcaller (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Swift:** When underwater, at the start of your turn you can call up a
[current](ref/rule/currents-and-running-water) with a speed up to 20 feet in the direction of your
choice in your space (moving with you) that lasts until the end of your turn.

**Usage:** 3 times/day

**Usage:** 3 times/day

**Deep Fury (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 120 feet in water, as well as
[evasion](ref/ability/evasion) and [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to cold and
[pressure damage](ref/rule/water-dangers). You have these benefits constantly, even when not in
_bloodrage._
