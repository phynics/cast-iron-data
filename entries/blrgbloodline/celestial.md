## Celestial Bloodline

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 19

By way of a celestial ancestor or divine intervention, the blood of angels fills your body with a
holy potency, granting you a majestic visage and angelic powers when you enter your _bloodrage._

### Bloodline Powers

Your bloodline grants a number of resistances and changes your form to something angelic and
terrible to behold when you _bloodrage._

**Angelic Attacks (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Your melee attacks are considered good-aligned weapons for the purpose of bypassing
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). Furthermore, when you deal damage with a melee attack
to an evil outsider, you deal an additional 1d6 points of damage. This additional damage stacks with
effects such as [align weapon](ref/spell/align-weapon) and those granted by a weapon with the
[holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) weapon special ability.

**Celestial Resistances (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** These resistances increase to 10.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 to acid and cold.

**Conviction (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per _bloodrage_ you can reroll one ability check, skill check, or saving throw you
just made. You must decide to use this ability after the die is rolled, but before the GM reveals
the results. You must take the second result, even if it's worse.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Wings of Heaven (Su)**

**Ability:** You can choose to sprout feathery wings and fly with a speed of 60 feet and good
maneuverability.

**At 20th Level:** Your fly speed increases to 80 feet.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Angelic Protection (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +4 deflection bonus to AC and a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws against
attacks made or effects created by evil creatures. In addition, you are affected as if subject to a
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) spell. This effect cannot be dispelled.

**Ascension (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You become infused with the power of the heavens. You gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity)
to acid, cold, and petrification. You also gain resistance 10 to electricity and fire, as well as a
+4 racial bonus on saving throws against poison. You have these benefits constantly, even while not
_bloodraging._
