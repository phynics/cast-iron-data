### Bloodline Powers

Your future greatness grants you the might to strike your enemies with awe.

**Destined Strike (Su)**

**Free:** You can grant yourself an insight bonus equal to 1/2 your bloodrager level (minimum 1) on
one melee attack.

**At 12th Level:** You can use this five times a day.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Fated Bloodrager (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain a luck bonus to AC and on saving throws. This bonus is equal to one-fourth of
your bloodrager level.

**Certain Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** You may reroll an attack roll once during a _bloodrage._ You must decide to use this
ability after the die is rolled, but before the GM reveals the results. You must take the second
result, even if it's worse.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Defy Death (Su)**

**Ability:** When an attack or spell that deals damage would result in your death, you can attempt a
DC 20 Fortitude save. If you succeed, you are instead reduced to 1 hit point; if you succeed and
already have less than 1 hit point, you instead take no damage.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Usage:** Once a day

**Usage:** Once a day

**Unstoppable (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Passive:** Any critical threats you score are automatically confirmed. Any critical threats made
against you confirm only if the second roll results in a natural 20 (or is automatically confirmed).

**Victory or Death (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You are immune to paralysis and petrification, as well as to the stunned, dazed, and
staggered conditions. You have these benefits constantly, even while not _bloodraging._
