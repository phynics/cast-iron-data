## Hag Bloodline

[Hags](ref/family/hag) interbreed with other races frequently, and those children who escape their
grasp may pass their heritage on to later generations before it reemerges. The potent humors that
accompany hag blood are better suited to a bloodrager's ferocity than to traditional spellcasting.

### Bloodline Powers

While _bloodraging,_ you manifest the physical power and arcane corruption of your hag ancestor.

**Evil Eye (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can curse an opponent with a wicked glance, afflicting one target within 30 feet
that you can see, causing it to take a -2 penalty to AC and on attack rolls for 1 round per
bloodrager level or until the target hits you with an attack. The target can negate this effect with
a successful Will saving throw (DC = 10 + half your bloodrager level + your Charisma modifier).

**Usage:** Cha3 modifier times/day

**Hag Fortitude (Su)**

**At 8th Level:** You also become immune to poison.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You become immune to mundane and magical diseases.

**Hag Transformation (Su)**

**At 16th Level:** You apply your morale bonus on Will saving throws while _bloodraging_ to
Fortitude and Reflex saving throws against spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** Your form grows more twisted and powerful while _bloodraging,_ but clumsy, blind
aggression clouds your mind. Your morale bonus to Strength increases by 2, and you gain
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) and [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) (60 feet), but
you take a -2 penalty to both Dexterity and Wisdom for the duration of your _bloodrage._

**Covenguard (Su)**

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Standard:** You can cast _[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). You count as a hag for the purposes of joining a
coven; the coven must contain at least one hag.

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Usage:** 3 times/day.

**Curse Conduit (Su)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 20th Level

You gain immunity to negative energy damage and all spells with the curse descriptor.

Whenever you confirm a critical hit with a melee weapon, you can immediately target your opponent
with a _[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as a free action.
