|20 gp|1/2 lb.|Smokestick (misc)|

These items work best with spells that create clouds or smoke.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (20 gp)|_fog cloud, obscuring mist, pyrotechnics_|Uses are listed below.|

[Fog Cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud) **(M):** Increase the radius of the cloud by 5 feet.

[Obscuring Mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist) **(M):** The spell creates a smoky haze instead of mist.
This haze cannot be dispersed by fire spells and dissipates naturally after 1 minute.

[Pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics) **(M):** Increase the radius of the cloud by 5 feet and the
duration by 2 rounds.
