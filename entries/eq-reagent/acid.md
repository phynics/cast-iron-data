A common flask of acid has many uses as a power component, particularly with attack spells.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (10 gp)|_acid arrow, acid fog, acid splash, dispel magic, grease, wall of ice_|Uses are listed below.|

[Acid Arrow](ref/spell/acid-arrow) **(M):** The spell's acid lasts 1 round longer than normal.

[Acid Fog](ref/spell/acid-fog) **(M):** The fog's radius and height increase by 5 feet.

[Acid Splash](ref/spell/acid-splash) **(F):** The spell deals +1 point of damage.

[Acid Splash](ref/spell/acid-splash) **(M):** The spell lasts 1 round longer than normal.

[Dispel Magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) **(M):** Using acid as a material component while attempting
to dispel or counterspell a spell or effect with the earth subtype gives you a +2 bonus on your
dispel check. This bonus applies whether you are using _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),_
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater), or some other spell that you can use for
counterspelling (for example, if you have the
[Improved Counterspell](ref/feat/improved-counterspell) feat).

[Grease](ref/spell/grease) **(M):** The grease is acidic and deals 1 point of acid damage per round
to any creature in the area or holding the greased object.

[Wall of Ice](ref/spell/wall-of-ice) **(M):** For each flask of acid used as a power component, you
may designate one 10-foot square of ice wall that, if broken through, deals 1d6 acid damage in
addition to the normal cold damage.
