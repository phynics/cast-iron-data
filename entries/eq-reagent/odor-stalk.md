## Odor stalk

**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 27

|Price|Weight|Equipment|
| --- | --- | --- |
|70 gp|1/10 lb.|Odor Stalk (misc)|

These alchemically treated [incense](ref/eq-misc/incense) sticks contain pheromones that certain
creatures find repellent. Each odor stalk is created to irritate a specific creature type.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (70 gp)|_ghoul touch, repel vermin, stinking cloud_|Uses are listed below.|

[Ghoul touch:](ref/spell/ghoul-touch) The stench only affects creatures of the odor stalk's
associated creature type.

[Repel vermin:](ref/spell/repel-vermin) The spell functions as written, but applies to the odor
stalk's associated creature type instead of to vermin. The Will save DC is always 12 when using this
component.

[Stinking cloud:](ref/spell/stinking-cloud) The Fortitude save DC increases by 1 for creatures of
the odor stalk's prescribed type, but decreases by 1 for all other creature types.

> _Note: The original source doesn't state whether the spells use it as a material or focus cost.
> Consult your GM._
