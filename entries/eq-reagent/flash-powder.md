|50 gp|-|Flash Powder (weapon)|

Useful as a distraction, flash powder makes light-burst spells more dangerous.

### \[Power Component\](rule/alchemical\_power\_components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (50 gp)|_flare, pyrotechnics_|Uses are listed below.|

[Flare](ref/spell/flare) **(M):** Increase the saving throw DC of the spell by 2.

[Pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics) **(M):** When used to create fireworks, increase the DC by 1
and the blindness duration by 1 round.
