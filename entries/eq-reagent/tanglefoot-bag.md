|50 gp|4 lbs.|Tanglefoot Bag (weapon)|

These sticky pouches can make a troublesome spell even worse.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (50 gp)|_black tentacles, slow, web_|Uses are listed below.|

[Black Tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles) **(M):** For each tanglefoot bag used as a power
component, you may reroll the tentacles' grapple check against one creature of your choice.

[Slow](ref/spell/slow) **(M):** For each tanglefoot bag used as a power component, you may designate
one slowed creature as being affected by a tanglefoot bag.

[Web](ref/spell/web) **(M):** Increase the DC of breaking free by making a combat maneuver or Escape
Artist check by 1.
