This small, reddish crystal shard flickers with a brilliant, dancing flame - a representation of the
[Plane of Fire](ref/rule/plane-of-fire).

### \[Power Component\](rule/alchemical\_power\_components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (350 gp)|fire spells (M or F)|When you use a fire fragment as a material component or focus for a spell that deals fire damage, that spell is affected as per the [Flaring Spell](ref/feat/flaring-spell) metamagic feat (though this does not increase the spell slot that the spell uses), and you treat your caster level as 1 higher for the purpose of determining the spell's damage. (You cannot use this as a material component and a focus for the same spell.)|
