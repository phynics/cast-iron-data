The shimmering, multicolored whimsy star captures the mutability of
[the First World](ref/rule/the-first-world).

### \[Power Component\](rule/alchemical\_power\_components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (200 gp)|any (M or F)|When you use a whimsy star as a material component or focus for a spell, roll 1d6 and consult the table below. You can't use magic or other abilities to affect the result of this roll. If a result cannot apply to your spell, then the spell resolves normally. Regardless of the result, the spell slot required to cast the spell doesn't change. (You cannot use this as a material component and a focus for the same spell.)|

|d6|Result|
| --- | --- |
|1|The spell fails and you are [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1d4 rounds.|
|2|The spell resolves normally.|
|3|The spell is affected as per the [Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell) metamagic feat.|
|4|The spell is affected as per the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) metamagic feat.|
|5|The spell is affected as per the [Widen Spell](ref/feat/widen-spell) metamagic feat.|
|6|The spell is affected as per the [Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell) metamagic feat, increasing the spell's level by 1.|
