This substance augments cold magic.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (40 gp)|_cone of cold, gentle repose, ray of frost, protection from energy, resist energy_|Uses are listed below.|

[Cone of Cold](ref/spell/cone-of-cold) **(M):** The spell deals +1 point of damage per caster level.

[Gentle Repose](ref/spell/gentle-repose) **(M):** The spell's duration increases to 2 days per
level.

[Ray of Frost](ref/spell/ray-of-frost) **(F):** The spell deals +1 point of damage.

[Ray of Frost](ref/spell/ray-of-frost) **(M):** The spell creates an icicle of frozen water vapor
that strikes the target and deals 1d3 points of piercing damage and 1 point of cold damage.

[Protection from Energy](ref/spell/protection-from-energy) **(M):** If cast to ward against fire,
increase the amount of fire damage absorbed by 5.

[Resist Energy](ref/spell/resist-energy) **(M):** If cast to ward against fire, increase the fire
resistance to 12. At caster level 7th, you may use two jars of liquid ice as a power component to
increase the fire resistance to 24. At caster level 11th, you may use three jars as a power
component to increase the fire resistance to 36.
