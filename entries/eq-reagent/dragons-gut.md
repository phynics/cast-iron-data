A dragon's stomach contains highly corrosive acid that can be crafted into an alchemical item called
dragon's gut.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (50 gp)|_acid arrow, acid fog, acidic spray_|Uses are listed below.|

[Acid arrow](ref/spell/acid-arrow) **(M):** The spell lasts 1d3 rounds longer than usual.

[Acid fog](ref/spell/acid-fog) **(M):** The fog takes on a sticky quality and clings to creatures
within it. Any creature damaged by the acid fog takes an additional 2d6 points of acid damage on the
round after leaving the fog's area of effect. The creature can negate this additional damage by
spending a standard action to wipe the clinging acid from its body.

[Acidic spray](ref/spell/acidic-spray) **(M):** The spell deals 1d8 points of acid damage per caster
level (maximum 15d8) instead of the normal 1d6 per caster level on the round of its casting (Reflex
half). It still deals 1d6 points of damage per two caster levels on the following round as normal.

> _Note: The original source doesn't state whether the spells use it as a material or focus cost.
> The first two have clear parallels with the_ acid _entry, but the third is a guess. Consult your
> GM._
