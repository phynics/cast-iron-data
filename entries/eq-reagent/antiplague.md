Certain healing spells have greater effects when used with this substance.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (50 gp)|_heroes' feast, remove disease_|Uses are listed below.|

[Heroes' Feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast) **(M):** For each vial of antiplague used as a power
component, one creature eating the feast gains the benefits of antiplague for 12 hours. Antiplague's
normally foul taste does not change the taste of the feast.

[Remove Disease](ref/spell/remove-disease) **(M):** Add +2 on your caster level check to cure
diseases on the target.
