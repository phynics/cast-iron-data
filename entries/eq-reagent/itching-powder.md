|Price|Weight|Equipment|
| --- | --- | --- |
|60 gp|2 lbs.|Itching Powder (weapon)|

This powder can make an otherwise innocuous spell especially irritating.

### \[Power Component\](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components)

|Doses|Spells|Effects|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1 (60 gp)|_glitterdust, repel vermin, summon swarm_|Uses are listed below.|

[Glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust) **(M):** For each packet of itching powder used as a power
component, you may designate one creature in the area to be affected by itching powder (DC 12).

[Repel Vermin](ref/spell/repel-vermin) **(M):** The first vermin that enters the emanation is
subject to the effects of itching powder (DC 15). Each packet of itching powder beyond the first
means the spell affects another vermin that enters the emanation. A swarm of vermin counts as one
vermin for the purpose of this effect.

[Summon Swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm) **(M):** Increase the swarm's distraction DC by 2.
