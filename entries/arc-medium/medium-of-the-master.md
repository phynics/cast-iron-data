**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 5\
A medium of the master forgoes a connection with most spirits to form a bond with the spirits of
great martial artists.

**Gained class skills:** acro

**Lost class skills:** umd

**Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Armor proficiency

**Info:** A medium of the master is proficient with only light armor.

**Unarmed Strike**

**Ability:** A medium of the master gains
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as a bonus feat.

**Dedicated Spirit (Su)**

**Ability:** A medium of the master can channel only a [champion](ref/spirit/champion) spirit. A
medium of the master can channel that spirit in places of quiet introspection, such as a Darklands
grotto, peaceful glade, or windswept peak, in addition to a champion spirit's favored locations.

**Alters:** Spirit

**Dedicated Surge (Su)**

**Ability:** A medium of the master can use her _spirit surge_ ability twice per day without
incurring any influence (this stacks with any additional uses she gains from accepting a _taboo,_
starting at 2nd level). In addition, when she uses _spirit surge,_ the die she rolls is one step
larger (1d8 at 1st level, 1d10 at 10th level, and 2d6 at 20th level).

**Alters:** Spirit surge

**Spirit Call (Su)**

**Flavor:** The medium of the master's unique bond allows her to briefly call on a champion spirit
without performing a _seance._

**Standard:** She can allow a spirit to gain 1 point of influence over her to channel it for a
number of minutes equal to her level. This influence remains for 24 hours, stacking with any
influence the medium gains by using this ability multiple times or by channeling a spirit in a
_seance_ during that time.

**Master's Power (Su)**

**Ability:** When a medium of the master channels a champion spirit, she treats her unarmed strike
damage as that of a [monk](ref/class/monk) of her level. For the purpose of determining damage,
these levels stack with levels from any other class that grants increased damage to unarmed strikes.

**Alters:** Spirit power (lesser)

**Unarmed Strike**

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the monk class ability.

**At 12th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d10 (Small), 2d6 (Medium), or 3d6 (Large) damage.

**At 16th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d6 (Small), 2d8 (Medium), or 3d8 (Large) damage.

**At 20th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d8 (Small), 2d10 (Medium), or 4d8 (Large) damage.

**At 4th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium), or 2d6 (Large) damage.

**At 8th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d8 (Small), 1d10 (Medium), or 2d8 (Large) damage.

**At 1st Level:** Your unarmed strike deals 1d4 (Small), 1d6 (Medium), or 1d8 (Large) damage.

**Shared Prowess (Su)**

**Flavor:** A medium of the master's spirit endows her with greater martial expertise.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** She gains a dodge bonus to AC equal to her spirit bonus as long as she is wearing light
or no armor.

**Replaces:** Shared seance

**Master's Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** When a medium of the master gains the intermediate power granted by her champion
spirit, she also learns one type of style strike. Whenever she gains an additional attack from
_sudden attack,_ she can designate one of the blows as a style strike as long as she makes it with
an unarmed strike. She can select a different style strike each time she channels a champion spirit.

**Alters:** Spirit power (intermediate)

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Ki Contact (Su)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Trance of three

**Swift:** A medium of the master can allow her spirit to gain 1 point of influence over her in
order to gain a ki power and a number of ki points equal to her Charisma modifier (minimum 1) until
the spirit leaves her body. She uses her medium levels as monk levels for the purposes of meeting
the ki power's prerequisites and any level-dependent benefits of the power. Multiple uses of this
ability stack, allowing her to select one power to serve as a prerequisite for another.

**Master's Style (Su)**

**Ability:** When a medium of the master channels a champion spirit, she gains a bonus style feat
and the two feats in that style's feat path. She does not have to meet the prerequisites for these
feats.

**Alters:** Spirit power (supreme)

**Gained:** At 17th Level
