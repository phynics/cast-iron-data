Rather than assume aspects taken from the traits of natural animals, a leafshifter transforms into
plant-animal hybrids that resemble [living topiaries](ref/monster/living-topiary). This affords the
leafshifter the savage power of the animal kingdom while also granting her the strength and
timelessness of plant life.

**Prerequisites:** Ghoran

**Shifter's Slam (Ex)**

**Ability:** This ability functions as the _shifter claws_ class feature, except the leafshifter
gains two slam attacks instead of two claw attacks. These slam attacks are made with her arms, which
are covered in jagged bark and thorns, so these attacks deal bludgeoning and piercing damage.

**Alters:** Shifter claws

**Leafshifter's Aspect (Su)**

**Ability:** This ability functions as the _shifter aspect_ ability of the standard shifter, except
a leafshifter doesn't gain the minor forms of her aspects. Instead, whenever she gains a new aspect,
she chooses one of the plant focus abilities from the [plant master](ref/arc-hunter/plant-master)
hunter archetype (listed below) to associate with her new aspect. This choice cannot be changed once
made.

**Alters:** Shifter aspect

**Assassin Vine**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple).

At 8th level: This bonus +3.

At 15th level: This bonus +4.

**Brambles**

**At 15th Level:** This becomes 3 points.

**At 8th Level:** This increases to 2 points.

**Passive:** When the creature is hit by an unarmed strike or natural attack, the attacker takes 1
point of piercing damage.

**Creeping Vine**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on Climb checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

**Giant Flytrap**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on Stealth checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

**Mushroom**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +4 enhancement bonus on saves against poison.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

**Oak**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +2 enhancement bonus to CMD.

At 8th level: This bonus +4.

At 15th level: This bonus +6.

**Shrieker**

**At 15th Level:** The creature also gains [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) with a range of 10 feet.

**At 8th Level:** The range increases by 30 feet.

**Passive:** The creature gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet.

**Spore**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on Fly checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

**Water Lily**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on Swim checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.
