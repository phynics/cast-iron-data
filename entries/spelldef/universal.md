## Universal (school of magic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 209

A small number of spells ([arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [permanency](ref/spell/permanency),
[prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation), and [wish](ref/spell/wish)) are universal, belonging
to no school.
