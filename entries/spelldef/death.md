## Death (spell discriptor)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 137

Spells with the death descriptor directly attack a creature's life force to cause immediate death,
or to draw on the power of a dead or dying creature. The [death ward](ref/spell/death-ward) spell
protects against death effects, and some creature types are immune to death effects.
