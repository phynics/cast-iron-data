## Pain (spell discriptor)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 138

Pain effects cause unpleasant sensations without any permanent physical damage (though a sensitive
target may suffer mental repercussions from lengthy exposure to pain). Creatures that are immune to
effects that require a Fortitude save (such as constructs and undead) are immune to pain effects.
