## Summoning (subschool of magic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 210

A summoning spell instantly brings a creature or object to a place you designate. When the spell
ends or is dispelled, a summoned creature is instantly sent back to where it came from, but a
summoned object is not sent back unless the spell description specifically indicates this. A
summoned creature also goes away if it is killed or if its hit points drop to 0 or lower, but it is
not really dead. It takes 24 hours for the creature to reform, during which time it can't be
summoned again.

When the spell that summoned a creature ends and the creature disappears, all the spells it has cast
expire. A summoned creature cannot use any innate summoning abilities it may have.

Summoning is a subschool of [Conjuration](ref/spelldef/conjuration).
