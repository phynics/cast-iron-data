## Phantasm (subschool of magic)

A phantasm spell creates a mental image that usually only the caster and the subject (or subjects)
of the spell can perceive. This impression is totally in the minds of the subjects. It is a
personalized mental impression, all in their heads and not a fake picture or something that they
actually see. Third parties viewing or studying the scene don't notice the phantasm. All phantasms
are [mind-affecting](ref/spelldef/mind-affecting) spells.

Phantasm is a subschool of [Illusion](ref/spelldef/illusion).
