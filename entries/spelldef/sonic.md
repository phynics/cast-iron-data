## Sonic (spell discriptor)

Sonic effects transmit energy to the target through frequent oscillations of pressure through the
air, water, or ground. Sounds that are too high or too low for the humanoid ear to detect can still
transmit enough energy to cause harm, which means that these effects can even affect deafened
creatures. Sound effects can cause hit point damage, deafness, dizziness, nausea, pain, shortness of
breath, and temporary blindness, and can detect creatures using bat-like echolocation.
