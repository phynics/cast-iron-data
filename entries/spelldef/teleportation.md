## Teleportation (subschool of magic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 210

A teleportation spell transports one or more creatures or objects a great distance. The most
powerful of these spells can cross planar boundaries. Unlike [summoning](ref/spelldef/summoning)
spells, the transportation is (unless otherwise noted) one-way and not dispellable.

Teleportation is instantaneous travel through the [Astral Plane](ref/rule/astral-plane). Anything
that blocks astral travel also blocks teleportation.

Teleportation is a subschool of [Conjuration](ref/spelldef/conjuration).
