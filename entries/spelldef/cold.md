## Cold (spell discriptor)

Cold effects deal damage by making the target colder, typically by blasting it with supernaturally
cooled matter or energy. Cold effects also include those that create ice, sleet, or snow out of
nothing. They can cause frostbite, numbness, coordination problems, slowed movement and reactions,
stupor, and death.
