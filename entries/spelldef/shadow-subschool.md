## Shadow (subschool of magic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 211

A shadow spell creates something that is partially real from extradimensional energy. Such illusions
can have real effects. Damage dealt by a shadow illusion is real.

Shadow is a subschool of [Illusion](ref/spelldef/illusion).
