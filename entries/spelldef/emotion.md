## Emotion (spell discriptor)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 137

Spells with this descriptor create emotions or manipulate the target's existing emotions. Most
emotion spells are [enchantments](ref/spelldef/enchantment), except for [fear](ref/spelldef/fear)
spells, which are usually [necromancy](ref/spelldef/necromancy).
