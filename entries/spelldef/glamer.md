## Glamer (subschool of magic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 210

A glamer spell changes a subject's sensory qualities, making it look, feel, taste, smell, or sound
like something else, or even seem to disappear.

Glamer is a subschool of [Illusion](ref/spelldef/illusion).
