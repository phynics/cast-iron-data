## Pattern (subschool of magic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 210

Like a [figment](ref/spelldef/figment), a pattern spell creates an image that others can see, but a
pattern also affects the minds of those who see it or are caught in it. All patterns are
[mind-affecting](ref/spelldef/mind-affecting) spells.

Pattern is a subschool of [Illusion](ref/spelldef/illusion).
