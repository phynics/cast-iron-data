## Evocation (school of magic)

Evocation spells manipulate magical energy or tap an unseen source of power to produce a desired
end. In effect, an evocation draws upon magic to create something out of nothing. Many of these
spells produce spectacular effects, and evocation spells can deal large amounts of damage.
