## Darkness (spell discriptor)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 137

Spells that create darkness or reduce the amount of light should have the darkness descriptor.
Giving a spell the darkness descriptor indicates whether a spell like [daylight](ref/spell/daylight)
is high enough level to counter or dispel it.
