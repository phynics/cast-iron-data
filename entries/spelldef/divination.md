## Divination (school of magic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 210

Divination spells enable you to learn secrets long forgotten, predict the future, find hidden
things, and foil deceptive spells.

Many divination spells have [cone-shaped](ref/rule/aiming-a-spell) areas. These move with you and
extend in the direction you choose. The cone defines the area that you can sweep each round. If you
study the same area for multiple rounds, you can often gain additional information, as noted in the
descriptive text for the spell.

[Scrying](ref/spelldef/scrying) is a subschool of Divination.
