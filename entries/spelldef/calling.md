## Calling (subschool of magic)

A calling spell transports a creature from another plane to the plane you are on. The spell grants
the creature the one-time ability to return to its plane of origin, although the spell may limit the
circumstances under which this is possible. Creatures who are called actually die when they are
killed; they do not disappear and reform, as do those brought by a
[summoning](ref/spelldef/summoning) spell. The duration of a calling spell is instantaneous, which
means that the called creature can't be dispelled.

Calling is a subschool of [Conjuration](ref/spelldef/conjuration).
