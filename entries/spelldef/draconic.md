## Draconic (spell discriptor)

Spells with the draconic descriptor were created by [dragons](ref/family/dragon-true) in ages long
past, and still resonate within the blood of true dragons to this day. Creatures of the
[dragon type](ref/type/dragon) with 5 or more racial hit dice can select a draconic spell as a spell
known regardless of the class spell list it's on. Each time such a creature gains an additional
racial hit die, it can select a draconic spell in place of an existing spell known of the same or
higher spell level.
