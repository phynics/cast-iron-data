**Ability:** The vigilante gains [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack) as a bonus feat, ignoring
its prerequisites.

**Prereq:** 6th-level vigilante

**Repeat:** The vigilante can designate one additional creature when he uses Spring Attack; the
vigilante's movement this round does not provoke attacks of opportunity from any of the designated
secondary creatures., 10, 4
