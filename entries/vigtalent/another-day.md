**&L&Another Day (Ex, Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** When the vigilante is dropped unconscious due to hit point damage, he automatically
[stabilizes](ref/misc/stable), but he can also appear to be dead, unless an examining creature
succeeds at a Heal check (DC = 20 + the vigilante's class level) or, if using magic such as
[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch), a caster level check with the same DC (the ability to affect
spells is supernatural; the rest of the ability is extraordinary).

**Special:** The vigilante counts as having received aid and automatically succeeds at the
Constitution check to become conscious after 1 hour (unless he has actually died in the meantime).
