When the vigilante is dropped unconscious due to hit point damage, he automatically
[stabilizes](ref/misc/stable), but he can also appear to be dead, unless an examining creature
succeeds at a Heal check (DC = 20 + the vigilante's class level) or, if using magic such as
_[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch),_ a caster level check with the same DC (the ability to affect
spells is supernatural; the rest of the ability is extraordinary). The vigilante counts as having
received aid and automatically succeeds at the Constitution check to become conscious after 1 hour
(unless he has actually died in the meantime).
