**Shackle Smash (Ex)**

**Ability:** Perhaps trained to remove or destroy restraints from slaves and prisoners by the
Twilight Talons or [Bellflower Network](ref/rule/aff-antislavery), a vigilante with this talent can
choose to make no more noise than people talking when he attempts a [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat
maneuver check or attempts a Strength check to break an object. He ignores half an object's
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) when attempting sunder combat maneuver checks and when attacking
nonmagical objects.
