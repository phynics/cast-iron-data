Whenever the vigilante [charges](ref/rule/charge), he can make a full attack. Whenever he uses this
ability, he takes a -4 penalty to his AC until the start of his next turn (in addition to the AC
penalty for charging). Only an [avenger vigilante](ref/class/vigilante#avenger) of at least 12th
level can select this talent.
