**Turnabout (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a foe provokes an attack of opportunity from the vigilante by attacking or
casting a spell, the vigilante can attempt a [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver
check in place of the attack of opportunity. If he succeeds at the check, in addition to the usual
options, the vigilante can redirect the attack or spell to a new target or area the foe could have
chosen.

**Flavor:** A vigilante with this talent can capitalize on any opportunities in combat.
