The vigilante gains [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) as a bonus feat. He can also deal hidden
strike damage against targets with total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). At 10th level, he
gains [Improved Blind-Fight](ref/feat/improved-blind-fight) as a bonus feat and can deal all types
of precision damage against targets with concealment or total concealment. At 16th level, he gains
[Greater Blind-Fight](ref/feat/greater-blind-fight) as a bonus feat. The vigilante does not have to
meet the prerequisites for either Improved Blind-Fight or Greater Blind-Fight to gain these feats
with this talent.
