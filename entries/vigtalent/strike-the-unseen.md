**Strike the Unseen (Ex)**

**Ability:** The vigilante gains [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) as a bonus feat. He can also
deal _hidden strike_ damage against targets with total concealment.

**At 10th Level:** He gains [Improved Blind-Fight](ref/feat/improved-blind-fight) as a bonus feat
and can deal all types of precision damage against targets with [concealment](ref/rule/concealment)
or total concealment.

**At 16th Level:** He gains [Greater Blind-Fight](ref/feat/greater-blind-fight) as a bonus feat.

**Special:** The vigilante does not have to meet the prerequisites for either Improved Blind-Fight
or Greater Blind-Fight to gain these feats with this talent.
