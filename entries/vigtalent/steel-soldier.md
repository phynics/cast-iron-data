**&L&Steel Soldier (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** The vigilante can spend 1 day and 100 gp per class level modifying a set of armor with
which he is proficient. The vigilante can conceal one set of unworn modified armor on his person as
though it were an extraordinarily small object, as per [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand),
and can [don modified armor](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor) without aid.

**Flavor:** Some vigilantes ceaselessly tinker with their armor until the two seem inseparable;
[masked maidens](ref/arc-vigilante/masked-maiden) are particularly prone to developing this
obsession.

**At 8th Level:** The vigilante treats modified armor's enhancement bonus (if any) as a weapon
enhancement bonus when making attacks with the armor's gauntlets, if the armor has any.

**Special:** The [armor skin](ref/vigtalent/armor-skin) talent also applies to armor with this
ability.
