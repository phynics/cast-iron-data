**Foe Collision (Ex)**

**Ability:** When the vigilante deals _hidden strike_ damage to a corporeal foe with a melee attack,
he can also deal an amount of nonlethal damage equal to his reduced _hidden strike_ damage to an
opponent adjacent to the first (even if the second opponent is out of his reach). The damage is
bludgeoning damage, and if the colliding foe has any natural attacks, the damage is also considered
magic, evil, or any other type of damage the natural attack deals.

**Flavor:** The vigilante can shove one of his foes into another.

**Special:** Unlike normal vigilante talents that affect _hidden strikes,_ this vigilante talent
applies in all cases when the target is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC.
