A stalker vigilante can apply only one talent marked with an asterisk (\*) to a given hidden strike, and only when that hidden strike is dealt against a foe that is unaware of the stalker vigilante's presence (or who considers him an ally), unless otherwise noted.

The vigilante can shove one of his foes into another. When the vigilante deals hidden strike damage
to a corporeal foe with a melee attack, he can also deal an amount of nonlethal damage equal to his
reduced hidden strike damage to an opponent adjacent to the first (even if the second opponent is
out of his reach). The damage is bludgeoning damage, and if the colliding foe has any natural
attacks, the damage is also considered magic, evil, or any other type of damage the natural attack
deals. Unlike normal vigilante talents that affect hidden strikes, this vigilante talent applies in
all cases when the target is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC. Only a
[stalker vigilante](ref/class/vigilante#stalker) can select this talent.
