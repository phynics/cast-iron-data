**&L&Leap and Bound (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** A vigilante with this talent adds his Strength bonus on
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks in addition to his Dexterity modifier. He is always
treated as having a running start when jumping, and his high jumps are treated as long jumps when
determining the DC.

When the vigilante jumps, he does not fall until the end of his turn, allowing him to attack or
perform other actions in midair.

If the vigilante grapples a creature capable of bearing his weight, he does not fall, instead
remaining adjacent to the creature as it moves.

**Prereq:** 10th-level vigilante
