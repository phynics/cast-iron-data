**Steely Resolve (Ex)**

**Immediate:** Three times per day, when the vigilante fails a Will saving throw against a
mind-affecting effect, the vigilante can delay the effect's onset for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his vigilante level, after which the vigilante is affected
as normal by the entire effect. Rounds spent delaying the effect do not count toward the effect's
duration.
