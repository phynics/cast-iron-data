**Magical Familiarity (Sp)**

**Ability:** He selects a 0-level spell from the sorcerer/wizard spell list. The vigilante can cast
the chosen spell three times per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using
his vigilante level as his caster level.

**Flavor:** The vigilante taps into a past life's arcane secrets.

**At 12th Level:** In addition to these 0-level spells, he chooses a 1st-level spell that he can
cast once per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 18th Level:** He can cast his 1st-level spell three times per day and his 0-level spells at
will.

**At 6th Level:** He chooses a second 0-level spell that he can also use three times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Prereq:** Intelligence 10+
