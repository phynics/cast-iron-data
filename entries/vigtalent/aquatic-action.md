**Aquatic Action (Ex)**

**Ability:** The vigilante uses weapons, natural weapons, and unarmed strikes in aquatic
environments as though under the effect of [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement),
including when making melee attacks against targets in the water while the vigilante is on land. The
vigilante ignores limitations and penalties on ranged attacks, including thrown weapons, imposed by
being underwater for the first 15 feet. (See [Underwater Combat](ref/rule/underwater-combat) for
more information.)

**Prereq:** 8th-level vigilante
