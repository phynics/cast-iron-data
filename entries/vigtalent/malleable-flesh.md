**Malleable Flesh (Su)**

**Ability:** A vigilante with this talent gains the [shapechanger](ref/subtype/shapechanger) subtype
and the [compression](ref/umr/compression) universal monster rule. In addition, the vigilante can
alter his appearance as [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self), except that the changes are
physical rather than illusory. However, aspects of the vigilante's aesthetic persist in all forms,
halving the bonus on Disguise checks gained from this effect and from _seamless guise._

**Flavor:** Whether through mutation or alchemy, some vigilantes have flesh that is as moldable as
clay and as fluid as ink.

**At 12th Level:** The vigilante can pass through narrow openings, even mere cracks, along with any
item he wears or carries (to a maximum of his light load).
