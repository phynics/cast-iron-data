**Mighty Ambush (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per round, when the vigilante succeeds at a _hidden strike,_ he can instantly drop
the damaged enemy [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious) for 1d4 rounds. A successful Fortitude saving
throw negates this effect (DC = 10 + 1/2 the vigilante's class level + the higher of the vigilante's
Strength and Dexterity modifiers).

**Prereq:** 10th-level stalker

**Special:** Whether or not a creature's saving throw is successful, it can't be affected by that
vigilante's _mighty ambush_ again for 24 hours.
