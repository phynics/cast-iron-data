**Instant Plan (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, the vigilante can state a plan of action. All allies within 30 feet that
can hear him gain a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against fear effects and are immune to being
demoralized. They also gain a +1 morale bonus on skill checks or combat maneuver checks of the
vigilante's choice. The benefits last for 1 round per vigilante level.

**Flavor:** The vigilante can quickly manage a situation that seems to be spiraling out of control.
