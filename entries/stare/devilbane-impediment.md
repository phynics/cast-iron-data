**&L&Devilbane Impediment&FN&**

**Info:** The target of your _hypnotic stare_ must succeed at a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC = 15 + the level of the spell) to activate any of
its [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (but not standard spellcasting). If the
target fails the check, the spell-like ability doesn't function but is still expended.
