**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 164\
The shigenjo walks the path of enlightenment and transcendence by seeking oneness with the celestial
spirits. In doing so, she unlocks the martial potential of her own spiritual power. A shigenjo has
the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Tengu; Alignment must have a neutral component

**Gained class skills:** sur

**Lost class skills:** diplo

**Recommended Mysteries:** [ancestor](ref/mystery/ancestor), [battle](ref/mystery/battle),
[flame](ref/mystery/flame), [heavens](ref/mystery/heavens), [lore](ref/mystery/lore),
[metal](ref/mystery/metal), [nature](ref/mystery/nature), [stone](ref/mystery/stone),
[time](ref/mystery/time), [waves](ref/mystery/waves), [wood](ref/mystery/wood).

**Bonus Spells**

**Info:** The oracle gains these bonus spells at the listed levels.

**Replaces:** The bonus spells gained at 2nd, 4th, 8th, 12th, 14th, and 16th levels from the
oracle's mystery

**Spells:** Magic jar

**Spells:** Ki shout

**Spells:** Moment of prescience

**Spells:** True strike

**Spells:** Alter self

**Spells:** Divine power

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** A shigenjo gains a pool of ki points, supernatural energy she can use to accomplish
amazing feats. The number of points in the shigenjo's _ki pool_ is equal to
[one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her oracle level + her Charisma modifier. The _ki
pool_ is replenished each morning after 8 hours of rest or meditation; these hours do not need to be
consecutive.

**Info:** If the shigenjo possesses levels in another class that grants points to a _ki pool,_ ki
points gained from the shigenjo class stack with those gained from the other class to determine the
total number of ki points in the combined _pool,_ but only one ability score modifier is added to
the total. The choice of which score to use is made when the second class ability is gained, and
once made, the choice is permanent.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** 7th-level revelation

**Special:** The shigenjo can use ki points from this _pool_ to power the abilities of every class
she possesses that grants a _ki pool._

**Ki Abilities**

**Ability:** As long as she has at least 1 point in her _ki pool,_ a shigenjo can make a _ki strike_
as an [unchained monk](ref/class/unchained-monk) whose level is equal to her oracle level - 3.

**Swift:** She may spend 1 point of ki to gain one of the following benefits.

**Ki Magic**

**Passive:** Add +1 to the DC of the next spell she casts on her turn.

**Ki Curse**

**Passive:** Treat her oracle level as 5 higher for the purpose of determining the effects of her
_[curse](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/curse)_ for the next round.

**Ki Insight**

**Passive:** Gain a +4 insight bonus on Spellcraft checks for 1 round.

**Quivering Palm (Su)**

**Ability:** A shigenjo may learn quivering palm as the [monk](ref/class/monk) ability of the same
name in place of a revelation. She treats her oracle level as her monk level for this ability, and
the DC is based on her Charisma bonus instead of her Wisdom bonus.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to speak with any creature that uses a language. For a number of
days equal to your Wisdom score, you can ignore the negative effects of extreme weather, starvation,
thirst, and exhaustion. If you die, your powerful connection to the celestial realm allows you to be
reborn 3 days later (as [reincarnate](ref/spell/reincarnate)).

**Flavor:** You achieve true enlightenment and becomes one with the celestial spirits.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Final revelation
