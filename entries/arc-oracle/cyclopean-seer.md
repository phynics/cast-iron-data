**Sources:** Inner Sea Monster Codex pg 17\
A cyclopean seer draws power from the mysterious prophetic abilities of ancient giants.

**Recommended Mysteries:** [Ancestor](ref/mystery/ancestor), [lore](ref/mystery/lore),
[nature](ref/mystery/nature), [occult](ref/mystery/occult), [time](ref/mystery/time).

**Replaces:** Bonus skills from the oracle's mystery

**Gained class skills:** ka, kd, ke, kg, kl, kna, kno, ling

**Oracle's Curse**

**Info:** A cyclopean seer must choose the [haunted](ref/oraclecurse/haunted),
[hunger](ref/oraclecurse/hunger), [powerless prophecy](ref/oraclecurse/powerless-prophecy), or
[tongues](ref/oraclecurse/tongues) curse.

**Bonus Spells**

**Ability:** Each time a cyclopean seer would gain a bonus spell from her mystery, she instead
selects a divination spell from any class's spell list. If the spell is not on the cleric/oracle
spell list, it must be at least 1 level lower than the highest-level oracle spell the cyclopean seer
can cast.

**Alters:** Bonus spells granted by the oracle's mystery

**Assume Fate (Su)**

**Immediate:** When targeted by an effect that requires a saving throw, you can choose another
creature within line of sight that is attempting a save against the same effect. The target takes a
penalty on its saving throw equal to 1d4 + 1 for every 4 levels you possess. You gain the value of
their penalty as a luck bonus on your saving throw.

**Replaces:** Revelation gained at 1st level

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Brutal Trance (Su)**

**Replaces:** Revelation gained at 7th level

**Standard:** You can enter a trance that lasts 1 round per oracle level you possess. While in this
trance, you are [confused](ref/misc/confused), but each round that you can act normally, you can ask
a single question as if using [commune](ref/spell/commune).

**Revelations**

**Alters:** Revelations

**Info:** All cyclopean seers have access to the following revelations, regardless of their chosen
mysteries.

**Doomsaying (Su)**

**Special:** This is a curse effect.

**Standard:** You can pronounce doom on a creature within 30 feet. The target takes a penalty equal
to your oracle level on either all skill checks, all savings throws, or all attack rolls (your
choice) for 1 minute or until he fails one such roll.

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Flash of Insight (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the [cyclops](ref/monster/cyclops) racial ability of the same name. If you
already possess this ability, you gain an additional daily use.

**Immediate:** _Text from the_ cyclops _entry:_ Once per day, a cyclops can peer into an occluded
visual spectrum of possible futures, gaining insight that allows it to select the exact result of
one die roll before the roll is made. This effect can alter an action taken by the cyclops only, and
cannot be applied to the rolls of others.

**Prescience (Ex)**

**Passive:** You gain a bonus to your Armor Class against attacks of opportunity and a bonus on
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks. These bonuses are equal to 2 + one-fifth of your
oracle level.

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** Upon reaching 20th level, you embody ancient cyclopes' insight. You can use each of the
following once per day as a spell-like ability: [discern location](ref/spell/discern-location),
[prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes), and [stone tell](ref/spell/stone-tell).

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Final revelation

**Special:** In addition, using _flash of insight_ to select a d20 roll result equal to or lower
than your Charisma modifier doesn't expend that ability.
