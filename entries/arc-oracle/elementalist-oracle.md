**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 68, People of the Sands pg 15\
An elementalist oracle shares an affinity with the elements and elemental creatures. She can
communicate with elemental creatures and can eventually become an elemental herself. She sees
perfection in manifestations of elemental fury in the natural world, be they the thunderous wrath of
the storm, the churning waters of the ocean's tides or a flooding river, the terrain-shaping power
of a landslide or earthquake, or the apocalyptic fury of a volcanic eruption.

**Recommended Mysteries:** [Flame](ref/mystery/flame), [stone](ref/mystery/stone),
[waves](ref/mystery/waves), [wind](ref/mystery/wind).

**Revelations**

**Info:** An elementalist oracle must take the following revelations at the listed levels.

**Elemental Linguist (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a bonus language of your choice from the following list: Aquan, Auran, Ignan,
or Terran.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Repeat At:** You gain another language chosen from this list., 5, 10, 15

**Elemental Form (Su)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 11th Level

You gain the [air](ref/subtype/air), [earth](ref/subtype/earth), [fire](ref/subtype/fire), or
[water](ref/subtype/water) subtype and an associated ability:

- **Air:** Fly speed of 30 feet with perfect maneuverability.
- **Earth:** [Tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) with a range of 30 feet.
- **Fire:** Immunity to fire and vulnerability to cold.
- **Water:** Swim speed of 30 feet and the ability to breathe underwater.

**Elemental Revelation (Su)**

**Ability:** You fully harness the power of the elements. You can apply the
[Elemental Spell](ref/feat/elemental-spell) metamagic feat to spells you cast without increasing the
level or casting time. You can choose acid, cold, electricity, or fire each time you use this
ability.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Whenever you score a critical hit with an attack that deals energy damage, you ignore
the target's energy resistance (if any). If you score such a critical hit against a creature that
has immunity to the energy type you are attacking with, the creature takes half damage from the
attack.

**Replaces:** Final revelation
