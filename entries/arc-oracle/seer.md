**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 59\
While all oracles possess some ability at divination, the seer is a true prophet, able to see things
as they really are, or will be.

**Recommended Mysteries:** [lore](ref/mystery/lore), [nature](ref/mystery/nature),
[time](ref/mystery/time).

**Revelations**

**Info:** A seer must take the following revelations at the listed levels.

**Natural Divination (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can read the entrails of a freshly killed animal or humanoid to gain an insight
bonus equal to your Charisma modifier on one saving throw.

**Ability2:** Alternatively, by observing and interpreting the flights of birds, you may apply a +10
competence bonus on any one skill check.

**Ability3:** Finally, by charting marks in dirt or stone, or observing the behavior of sand when
thrown into the wind, you gain a +4 insight bonus on one initiative check.

**Info:** This is the same as the [nature](ref/mystery/nature) mystery revelation of the same name.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** These bonuses must be used during the next 24 hours and you must declare you are using
the bonus before the check or save is made.

**Usage:** Making a natural divination takes 10 minutes. You may use natural divination (in any
combination) once per day plus a number of additional times per day equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level.

**Usage:** Making a natural divination takes 10 minutes. You may use natural divination (in any
combination) once per day plus a number of additional times per day equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level.

**Gift of Prophecy (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can enter a trance to gain a glimpse of the future. This trance lasts
for 1 minute, which must be uninterrupted and during which you can take no other actions. You gain
the benefits of an [augury](ref/spell/augury) spell with 90% effectiveness.

**At 5th Level:** You gain the benefits of a [divination](ref/spell/divination) spell with 90%
effectiveness.

**At 9th Level:** The knowledge you gain is equivalent to a [commune](ref/spell/commune) spell.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Special:** None of these spell effects require material components.
