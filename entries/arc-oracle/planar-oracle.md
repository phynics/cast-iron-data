A planar oracle has an affinity with one of the Outer Planes. She is at home on the multiple planes
of the Great Beyond, and can eventually become an outsider herself.

**Recommended Mysteries:** [dark tapestry](ref/mystery/dark-tapestry), [flame](ref/mystery/flame),
[heavens](ref/mystery/heavens), [stone](ref/mystery/stone), [waves](ref/mystery/waves),
[wind](ref/mystery/wind).

**Revelations**

**At 1st Level:** You must choose one Outer Plane with which you have an affinity. This choice
cannot be changed.

**At 3rd Level:** A planar oracle must take the following revelation.

**Replaces:** 3rd-level revelation

**Planar Resistance (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain energy resistance 10 against one energy type associated with your chosen
plane.

**At 11th Level:** Your energy resistance increases to 20.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You become an extraplanar creature. Choose one outer plane, such as Heaven or the
Abyss. You gain superficial physical characteristics as appropriate to natives of your chosen outer
plane. For example, if your chosen plane is Heaven, you gain angelic features such as a halo and
metallic skin. This change does not alter your Hit Dice, hit points, saving throws, skill points,
class skills, or proficiencies. Your type changes to [outsider](ref/type/outsider)
([extraplanar](ref/subtype/extraplanar)), except on your associated plane, where your type is
outsider (native).

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** In addition, you gain immunity to your associated energy type and gain
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 10/magic. Unlike other outsiders, you can still be
brought back from the dead as if you were a member of your previous creature type.

**Replaces:** Final revelation
