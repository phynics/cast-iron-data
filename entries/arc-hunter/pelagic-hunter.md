Pelagic hunters form strong bonds that extend deep beneath the sea, reaching out to whatever heeds
their call, whether it be mundane animals or something far more sinister.

**Pelagic Companion**

**Alters:** Animal companion

**Choice:** A pelagic hunter must select an aquatic animal companion.

**Pelagic Focus (Su)**

**Ability:** Unlike with a normal hunter's [animal focus](ref/ability/animal-foci), pelagic focus
allows the hunter to contact magical deep sea creatures instead of animals, using the options below
instead of the normal hunter options.

**Alters:** Animal focus

**Aboleth**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +1 morale bonus on saves against divine spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

At 8th level: This bonus +2.

At 15th level: This bonus +3.

**Cetus**

**At 12th Level:** Instead of gaining Lunge, the creature increases its reach by 5 feet.

**Passive:** The creature gains [Lunge](ref/feat/lunge) as a bonus feat.

**Charybdis**

**At 15th Level:** They now ignore 10 points of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

**At 8th Level:** This improves to ignore the first 7 points of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

**Passive:** The creature's attacks ignore the first 5 points of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

**Deep One**

**At 12th Level:** The creature is immune to damage from changing pressure.

**Passive:** The creature reduces [pressure damage](ref/rule/water-dangers) it takes by 10.

**Hippocampus**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on Swim checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

**Kraken**

**At 15th Level:** If it has a swim speed, the jet's speed increases to seven times its swim speed.

**At 8th Level:** If it has a swim speed, the jet ability's speed increases to five times its swim
speed.

**Passive:** The creature gains the [jet](ref/umr/jet) ability with a speed equal to its unmodified
base land speed or four times its swim speed (if it has one), whichever is higher.

**Reefclaw**

**At 12th Level:** Instead of gaining Diehard, the creature gains the
[ferocity ability](ref/umr/ferocity).

**Passive:** The creature gains [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard) as a bonus feat.

**Scylla**

**At 15th Level:** It increases to 1d6.

**At 8th Level:** The bleed damage increases to 1d3.

**Passive:** The creature's attacks deal 1 point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage in addition to
their normal damage.

**Sea Serpent**

**Passive:** The creature is considered to be under a [nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection) spell
it cast on itself, with a caster level equal to the pelagic hunter's class level.

**Selkie**

**Passive:** The creature gains a +4 competence bonus on Bluff checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

**Shoggoth**

**At 12th Level:** The creature can't be flanked.

**Passive:** The creature gains a +2 competence bonus on Perception checks and reduces the
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking) bonuses of creatures flanking it by 1.

At 8th level: This perception bonus +3.

At 15th level: This perception bonus +4.

**Siyokoy**

**At 15th Level:** It increases to 1d6.

**At 8th Level:** The electricity damage increases to 1d3.

**Passive:** The creature's attacks deal 1 additional point of electricity damage.

**Blood to Water (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** When a pelagic hunter or her animal companion bleeds underwater into the surrounding
water (whether from piercing or slashing damage, a bleed effect, or another effect that causes blood
to spill), the blood transmutes into ordinary water within 1 round.

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** This prevents sharks and other predators with [keen scent](ref/umr/keen-scent) from
being drawn to the pelagic hunter's position by the blood and makes it harder for such creatures to
track her.
