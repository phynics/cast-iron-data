Well adapted to their drenched surroundings, flood flourishers coordinate deadly ambushes with their
loyal animal companions by capitalizing on their mastery of the soggy terrain.

**Twin Hunters (Ex)**

**Passive:** Each time a flood flourisher gains a benefit from this archetype, she also grants the
benefit to her animal companion.

**Replaces:** Hunter tactics

**Skilled Ambusher (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher gains either [Athletic](ref/feat/athletic) or
[Stealthy](ref/feat/stealthy) as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** The teamwork feat gained at 3rd level

**Watery Stride (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher and her animal companion gain a swim speed equal to their base land
speed (maximum 30 feet). A flood flourisher or animal companion that already has a swim speed
instead gains a +10 bonus on Acrobatics checks to avoid provoking attacks of opportunity from
swimming out of a threatened square.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Water Striker (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher gains the benefits of either
[Shot on the Run](ref/feat/shot-on-the-run) or [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack), even if she
does not meet either of the feats' prerequisites. She gains the benefits of this feat only while
using her swim speed.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** The teamwork feat gained at 6th level

**Submerged Stealth (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher learns how to ambush targets from beneath the muck, becoming a
silent threat. While in a deep bog, the flood flourisher can use cover from the water to attempt
Stealth checks with no penalties from the natural environment.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** While in a deep bog, as long as she moves no more than 5 feet in a round, she has
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) out to a range of 30 feet against anything that moves within the
same body of water. While underwater in a deep bog, she can
[hold her breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for a number of minutes equal to 6 times her
Constitution score before she risks drowning.

**Replaces:** Swift tracker

**Aquatic Action (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher gains the benefits of the
[aquatic action](ref/vigtalent/aquatic-action) vigilante talent.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** The teamwork feat gained at 9th level

**Sudden Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher can better take advantage of the element of surprise. She can take a
full round's worth of actions during the surprise round, rather than a single standard action or
move action.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** The teamwork feat gained at 12th level

**Fast Submerged Stealth (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher can move up to her full swim speed with no penalty while using
Stealth in a deep bog, and she can maintain her [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) from her
submerged stealth while doing so.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** The teamwork feat gained at 15th level

**Fast Swimmer (Ex)**

**Ability:** A flood flourisher increases her swim speed by 20 feet.

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Replaces:** The teamwork feat gained at 18th level
