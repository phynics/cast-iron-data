**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 21

Most samurai swear themselves to the code of the warrior, which emphasizes duty, honor, loyalty, and
obedience. This code extends not only to the samurai's lord and master, but also to his comrades and
family. Samurai who follow the order of the warrior are among the most trusted and feared warriors
in the land.

**Edicts**

**Details:** The [samurai](ref/class/samurai) must protect the life and lands of his lord with his
life. He must be truthful and courageous, respectful to his elders and his masters, and loyal to his
friends and liege. He must conduct himself with honor and dignity.

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever an order of the warrior samurai declares a _challenge,_ he receives DR/-
against attacks made by the target of his _challenge._ This DR is equal to 1 + one-fourth of his
samurai level.

**Skills**

**Details:** An order of the warrior samurai adds [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history)
and [Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) to his list of class skills.\~\~\~ An order
of the warrior samurai can make Knowledge (nobility) checks untrained. If he has ranks in the skill,
he receives a bonus on the check equal to 1/2 his samurai level (minimum +1) as long as the check
involves the nobles or politics of his land.

### Order Abilities

A samurai who belongs to the order of the warrior gains the following abilities as he increases in
level.

**Honor in All Things (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The order of the warrior samurai draws strength from his sense of honor.

**Free:** Whenever the samurai makes a skill check or saving throw, he can call upon his honor to
grant him a +4 morale bonus on the roll.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cavalier levels beyond 2nd

**Way of the Samurai (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The order of the warrior samurai's sense of honor and loyalty drives him to accomplish
seemingly impossible tasks.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Special:** Using this ability expends one daily use of his _resolve._

**Standard:** The samurai can focus his mind and will. Once during the next minute, he can choose to
roll an attack roll, skill check, or saving throw three times and take the best result. He must
decide to use this ability before the roll is made.

**Strike True (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The order of the warrior samurai can use his years of training and focus to make the
perfect strike.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Standard:** When he uses this ability, the samurai makes an attack as normal. If the attack hits,
it is a critical threat. The samurai must roll to confirm the critical as normal.

The attack deals the maximum amount of damage, although additional dice from weapon qualities,
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack), and additional dice from a critical hit are rolled
normally. The damage from this attack ignores any [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) the
target might have and also causes the target to become [blinded](ref/misc/blinded),
[deafened](ref/misc/deafened), [sickened](ref/misc/sickened), or [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for
1d4 rounds (the samurai's choice).

**Usage:** Once per day

**Usage:** Once per day
