**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 34

A cavalier who belongs to this order serves only himself, working to further his own aims and
increase his own prestige. Cavaliers of this order tend to be selfish and concerned only with
personal goals and objectives.

**Edicts**

**Details:** The cavalier must keep his own interests and aims above those of all others. He must
always accept payment when it is due, rewards when earned, and an even (or greater) share of loot.
The cavalier must take every opportunity to increase his own stature, prestige, and power.

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever an order of the cockatrice cavalier issues a _challenge,_ he receives a morale
bonus on all melee damage rolls made against the target of his _challenge_ as long as he is the only
creature threatening the target (not counting his mount). This morale bonus is equal to 1 +
one-fourth of his cavalier level.

**Skills**

**Details:** An order of the cockatrice cavalier adds [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) and
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) to his list of class skills.\~\~\~ In addition, an order of the
cockatrice cavalier adds his Charisma modifier to the DC on another creature's attempt to demoralize
him through [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) (in addition to his Wisdom modifier, as normal).

### Order Abilities

A cavalier that belongs to the order of the cockatrice gains the following abilities as he increases
in level.

**Braggart (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier can spend a **standard action** to extol his own accomplishments and
battle prowess.^1^ He receives [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display) as a bonus feat. He
does not need a weapon in hand to use this ability. The cavalier receives a +2 morale bonus on melee
attack rolls made against demoralized targets.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Steal Glory (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a creature other than the cavalier scores a critical hit against a target that
the cavalier is threatening, he can make an attack of opportunity against the same target.

**Flavor:** The cavalier can steal the glory from another creature's successful strike.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Moment of Triumph (Ex)**

**Free:** The cavalier can declare a moment of triumph. For 1 round, the cavalier receives a
competence bonus equal to his Charisma modifier on all ability checks, attack rolls, damage rolls,
saving throws, and skill checks. This bonus is also added to his AC. In addition, any critical
threats he makes are automatically confirmed.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Usage:** Once per day.

**Usage:** Once per day.

^1^ This ability is confusingly worded. The general consensus is that the morale bonus is constant
and you get Dazzling Display as a bonus feat (if you meet its prerequisites), but you can use it as
a standard action instead of a full-round action, and you can use it without a weapon in hand. As
always, consult your GM.
