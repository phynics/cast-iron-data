**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 90

A cavalier who belongs to this order serves nature in all its forms. These cavaliers believe that
animals make the most loyal companions, and keep close to woodland creatures.

**Edicts**

**Details:** An order of the of the beast cavalier must strive to protect nature - including animals
and magical beasts' from the encroachment of civilization and from those that seek to harm it. He
can slay enraged animals and magical beasts only when attempts to calm or pacify them fail.

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever an order of the of the beast cavalier issues a _challenge,_ his mount gains a
circumstance bonus on melee attack rolls against the target of his _challenge_ whenever the cavalier
is threatening the target. This circumstance bonus is equal to 1 + one-fourth of his cavalier level.

**Skills**

**Details:** An order of the beast cavalier adds [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature)
and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) to his list of class skills.\~\~\~ When the cavalier uses
Survival to follow tracks, he gains a bonus on the check equal to 1/2 his cavalier level (minimum
+1).

### Order Abilities

An order of the beast cavalier gains the following abilities as he increases in level.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier can improve the attitude of an animal. This ability functions as the druid
ability of the [same name](ref/ability/wild-empathy). The cavalier uses his level as his effective
druid level when using this ability.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Wild Mount Shape (Su)**

**Ability:** An order of the of the beast cavalier gains the ability to turn his mount into a
different Medium or Large animal and back again once per day. His options for new forms include all
creatures with the animal type.

This ability functions as [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2), except as noted here. The
effect lasts for 1 hour per cavalier level, or until the cavalier chooses to change his mount back.
Changing the mount's form (to a different animal or back to normal) requires the cavalier to touch
his mount as a **standard action** that does not provoke an attack of opportunity. The form chosen
must be that of an animal the cavalier is familiar with. The mount does not change size, but does
receive the bonuses (not the penalty) presented in _[beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)_ for
the Large animal.

**At 15th Level:** The cavalier can use _wild mount shape_ to change his mount into a Medium or
Large dragon. When taking this form, the cavalier's _wild mount shape_ now functions as
[form of the dragon I](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-1), except as above.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cavalier levels beyond 6th

**Ferocious Charge (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The cavalier is the embodiment of nature's fury.

**Free:** When the cavalier [charges](ref/rule/charge) a creature, he can attempt an
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to demoralize his enemy. If he succeeds, the target is
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened).

**Gained:** At 15th Level
