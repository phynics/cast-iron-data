**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 20

A cavalier who belongs to this order has pledged himself to cruelty and malice, serving only those
who grant him free rein to cause suffering. Cavaliers of this order inflict pain upon those they
engage and capture those who submit.

**Edicts**

**Details:** The cavalier must seek out those weaker than himself to punish and torture. He must
force others to obey his commands without question. He must strive to cause pain and suffering
wherever possible. Since these edicts are evil, he must be of an evil alignment.

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever an order of the whip cavalier issues a _challenge,_ he receives a morale bonus
on all melee damage rolls against the target of his _challenge_ as long as he damaged the creature
in the previous round. This morale bonus is equal to 1 + one-fourth of his cavalier level, maximum
+5.

**Skills**

**Details:** An order of the whip cavalier adds [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) and
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) to his list of class skills.\~\~\~ Whenever an
order of the whip cavalier uses [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) to force another creature to do
what he says, he receives a bonus on the check equal to 1/2 his cavalier level (minimum +1).

### Order Abilities

A cavalier who belongs to the order of the whip gains the following abilities as he increases in
level.

**Whip Crack (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier becomes proficient with a [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) and gains
[Whip Mastery](ref/feat/whip-mastery) as a bonus feat.

**Ability2:** When a creature whose number of Hit Dice is less than or equal to 1/2 the cavalier's
level takes nonlethal damage from the cavalier's whip, it is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round.
This does not stack with other fear effects.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Inspiring Pain (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The cavalier can inspire brutal malice among his allies.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Swift:** The cavalier can allow allies within 30 feet to deal nonlethal damage with their weapons
without taking the usual -4 penalty (the weapon must still be capable of dealing nonlethal damage).
Affected allies gain a +2 bonus on nonlethal weapon damage rolls. This effect lasts for 1 round and
can be used once every 5 minutes.

**Assert Authority (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever an enemy makes a melee attack against the cavalier or one of his adjacent
slaves or servants, that enemy provokes an attack of opportunity from the cavalier, and the cavalier
gains a +2 morale bonus on this attack of opportunity.

If the enemy's attack was a confirmed critical hit, the cavalier counts the enemy as if it were the
target of his _challenge_ for this attack of opportunity.

**Flavor:** The cavalier demonstrates his ruthlessness against those who strike the cavalier or his
slaves.

**Gained:** At 15th Level
