**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 34

Cavaliers belonging to the order of the asp belong to a cutthroat group where ruthless pragmatism
and underhanded dealing are the keys to moving up the chain of command. They tend to be brutal,
self-centered leaders who reap confidence from their subordinates' misery.

**Edicts**

**Details:** An order of the asp cavalier must endeavor to enhance her prestige, wealth, and power,
as well as that of her patrons. She must endeavor to maximize her profits and efficiency, directing
hirelings and even sacrificing them when doing so would further her goals and make her appear
exceptional by comparison.

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever an order of the asp cavalier issues a _challenge,_ she receives a morale bonus
on attack and damage rolls made against the target of the _challenge_ so long as it is entangled,
exhausted, fatigued, flanked, nauseated, prone, shaken, staggered, or denied its Dexterity bonus to
AC. This morale bonus is equal to 1 + one-fourth of her cavalier level.

**Skills**

**Details:** An order of the asp cavalier adds [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) and
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) to her list of class skills.\~\~\~ The cavalier gains a
bonus on Knowledge (local) checks equal to half her cavalier level (minimum +1) as long as the check
involves her patrons or organization. She also reduces her armor check penalty when using Sleight of
Hand by an amount equal to half her cavalier level (to a minimum of 0).

### Order Abilities

An order of the asp cavalier gains the following abilities.

**Indiscriminate (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier can perform a [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver against
creatures with the shaken condition without provoking an attack of opportunity.

**Free:** Whenever the order of the asp cavalier uses [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) to
demoralize one or more creatures, she can attempt to demoralize an equal number of allies within 30
feet.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** While within 30 feet of an ally with the [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) condition, the
cavalier gains a morale bonus on saving throws. This bonus is equal to 1 + one-sixth of his cavalier
level.

**Command the Meek (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** The cavalier treats all of her allies with the shaken condition as though they had the
same teamwork feats as the cavalier for the purpose of determining whether the cavalier receives a
bonus from her teamwork feats. Her allies do not receive any bonuses from these feats unless they
actually have the feats themselves. The allies' positioning and actions must still meet the
prerequisites listed in the teamwork feat for the cavalier to receive the listed bonus.

**Special:** When the cavalier uses _tactician, greater tactician,_ or _mighty tactician,_ the
duration doubles for any creatures that have no more than half the cavalier's Hit Dice or that have
the shaken condition when she activates the ability.

**Better You Than Me (Ex)**

**Immediate:** The cavalier can interpose an adjacent ally in the way of an incoming attack, gaining
[cover](ref/rule/cover) against the attack. The attack must target only the cavalier, and she must
choose to use this ability before seeing the result of the attack roll. The ally must have the
shaken condition or have no more than half the Hit Dice of the cavalier, and the ally gains a Reflex
save to negate this ability's effect entirely (DC = 10 + half the cavalier's level + the higher of
her Strength modifier or Charisma modifier).

If the attack hits, the damage is split evenly between the cavalier and the ally. If the ally's save
was a natural 1, the ally takes all of the damage, including any associated effects (bleed, poison,
etc.). An ally can purposefully fail this saving throw, but cannot purposefully roll a natural 1.

**Gained:** At 15th Level
