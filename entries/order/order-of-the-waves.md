**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 53

Cavaliers of the order of the waves explore the secrets under the sea.

**Edicts**

**Details:** The cavalier must perpetually seek out and explore new places and secrets under the
sea, stopping only to fight to protect his newfound treasures and his people from undersea threats
(typically evil undersea races like [sahuagin](ref/monster/sahuagin), but alternatively goodly or
neutral enemy races for cavaliers from evil races).

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever a cavalier of the order of the waves issues a _challenge,_ he receives a
morale bonus on his saving throws as long as he is underwater. This morale bonus is equal to 1 +
one-fourth of his cavalier level.

**Skills**

**Details:** A cavalier belonging to the order of the waves adds
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) and [Perception](ref/skill/perception) to his
class skills.\~\~\~ He can attempt Knowledge (geography) checks untrained, but if he has ranks in
the skill, he adds half his cavalier level (minimum 1) to all Knowledge (geography) checks that
involve finding an underwater location.

### Order Abilities

A cavalier belonging to this order gains the following abilities as he increases in level.

**Waverider (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier gains a swim speed equal to his unmodified base land speed. If he already
has a swim speed, his swim speed increases by 10 feet, and if he and his mount both have a swim
speed, the mount's swim speed also increases by 10 feet.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** He gains a +1 morale bonus on damage rolls when underwater.

**Current's Rush (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** The cavalier gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls when [charging](ref/rule/charge) in the
same direction as a current that's currently pushing him, as well as a +1 bonus on damage rolls for
every 10 feet of the current's speed (maximum +6 points of damage).

**Explore the Seas (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier gains [Pressure Adept](ref/feat/pressure-adept) as a bonus feat and adds
two oceanic zones to his native range instead of one.

**Ability2:** When exploring a new location underwater, he gains a +2 morale bonus on initiative
checks and Perception checks, and can always act in the surprise round.

**Gained:** At 15th Level
