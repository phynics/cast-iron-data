**Sources:** People of the River pg 10

Often affiliated with the church of Calistria, those ex-Galtan cavaliers who subscribe to the order
of vengeance seek not only to reclaim their rightful homeland, but to punish those who betrayed
them.

**Edicts**

**Details:** The cavalier must seek retaliation (legal or otherwise) for any unjustified harm to her
person or property. She may not forgive even the slightest insult.

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever a cavalier of the order of vengeance issues a _challenge_ against a creature
whose kind she has encountered in the past 24 hours, the cavalier gains a morale bonus on attack
rolls made against the target of her _challenge._ This morale bonus is equal to 1 + one-fourth of
her cavalier level.

**Skills**

**Details:** A cavalier of the order of vengeance adds
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) and
[Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) to her list of class skills.\~\~\~ Whenever the
cavalier uses [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) to gather information, she receives a bonus on the
check equal to 1/2 her cavalier level (minimum +1).

### Order Abilities

A cavalier that belongs to the order of vengeance gains the following abilities as she increases in
level.

**Air Grievances (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** When the cavalier attempts to demoralize an opponent using
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), the cavalier gains a bonus on her Intimidate check equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her cavalier level.

At 8th level: The penalties a cavalier's target takes for being demoralized increase by 1.

At 14th level: The penalties a cavalier's target takes for being demoralized increase by 2.

At 20th level: The penalties a cavalier's target takes for being demoralized increase by 3.

**Eye for an Eye (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier gains [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus) as a bonus feat. The
cavalier can grant a critical feat (in place of a teamwork feat) to her allies using the _tactician_
ability.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Retribution (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever an enemy makes a successful melee attack against the cavalier or an adjacent
ally devoted to the same faith as the cavalier, the enemy provokes an attack of opportunity from the
cavalier. The cavalier receives a +2 morale bonus on the attack of opportunity.

If the attack made by the enemy was a critical hit, the cavalier may treat the enemy as the target
of his _challenge_ for the attack of opportunity.

The cavalier can use this ability once per round.

**Flavor:** This is the same as the [order of the star](ref/order/order-of-the-star) ability of the
same name: The cavalier can take retribution on those who dare to strike an agent of his faith.

**Gained:** At 15th Level
