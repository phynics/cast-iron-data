**Sources:** Knights of the Inner Sea pg 24

There are some cavaliers who dedicate themselves to the land and the people who inhabit the rural
areas between urban centers. These cavaliers often lead untrained militias of revolutionaries in
backwoods towns, fighting against oppressive governments or the savage elements that threaten
citizens in their daily lives. Self-reliance and the ability to make do with sparse resources are
hallmarks of a cavalier who follows the order of the land.

**Edicts**

**Details:** The cavalier must always strive to protect the common folk from the depredations of
oppressive regimes. He must not decline any duty or mission because he is not properly equipped for
it.

**Challenge**

**Details:** Whenever an order of the land cavalier issues a _challenge,_ he receives a morale bonus
on ranged attack rolls against the target of his _challenge._ This morale bonus is equal to 1 +
one-fourth of his cavalier level.

**Skills**

**Details:** An order of the land cavalier adds [Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) to his list of class skills.\~\~\~ In addition, whenever an order of
the land cavalier makes a Survival check to avoid getting lost, he receives a bonus on the check
equal to 1/2 his cavalier level (minimum +1).

### Order Abilities

A cavalier belonging to the order of the land gains the following abilities as he increases in
level.

**Adaptive Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier receives [Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard) as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Swift:** He can cause an armed opponent to become flat-footed against any attacks he makes during
that round with an improvised weapon. This ability can be used once per combat.

**Terrain Training (Ex)**

**Choice:** The cavalier may select a terrain type from the ranger
[Favored Terrains](ref/ability/favored-terrain) table.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Repeat:** The cavalier may select a new terrain type that this ability can affect., 12, 4

**Standard:** He can shout orders that grant all allies within 60 feet a competence bonus equal to
his Charisma modifier on initiative checks and
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography), [Perception](ref/skill/perception), and
Survival checks while they are in the chosen terrain for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 his
cavalier level.

**Wild Charge (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cavalier and his mount can ignore
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) for the purpose of making a
[charge](ref/rule/charge) attack. In addition, if the charge attack is successful and both he and
his opponent are occupying squares with difficult terrain, the cavalier gains a +2 competence bonus
when using his _mighty charge_ ability to make a bull rush, disarm, sunder, or trip
[combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers).

**Gained:** At 15th Level
