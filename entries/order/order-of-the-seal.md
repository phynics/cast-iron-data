**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 34

Cavaliers of the order of the seal are a secretive order, each charged with the protection of a
specific object (such as a dangerous magic item), place (a lost temple), or secret (that a certain
peasant is actually the last descendant of the royal line). Such cavaliers are the defenders of
ancient traditions or the servants of rulers with important secrets to hide, sometimes having to
search out such secrets in order to properly protect and preserve them.

**Edicts**

**Details:** The cavalier must guard his sworn charge with all he has: his health, his honor, and
his very life. If his charge is a place, he must keep intruders out. If it is a thing, he must keep
it safe from thieves - and restore the item to its rightful place if taken.

**Challenge**

**Details:** An order of the seal cavalier can make a free bull rush or trip combat maneuver anytime
he takes the full-attack action against the target of his _challenge._ This free combat maneuver
does not provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Skills**

**Details:** An order of the seal cavalier adds [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) and
[Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) to his list of class skills.\~\~\~ Whenever the cavalier uses
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) to conceal information about his sworn charge, he receives a competence
bonus equal to 1/2 his cavalier level (minimum +1).

### Order Abilities

A cavalier belonging to the order of the seal gains the following abilities as he increases in
level.

**Keeper (Ex)**

**Choice:** Once per day the cavalier must select a location, or a secret that he has sworn to
protect.

If he chooses a location, he gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls when directly defending his
charge.

If he chooses a secret, he gains a +2 morale bonus on saving throws and opposed checks to resist
revealing information about his charge.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Special:** He gains these bonuses until he picks a new object, location, or secret to protect.

**I Shall Not Be Moved (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The cavalier can draw on his dedication to overcome physical weakness.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** Whenever the cavalier does not move more than a 5-foot step, he receives a +2 dodge
bonus to his CMD to resist bull rush, overrun, reposition, and trip combat maneuvers until his next
turn.

**Staggering Assault (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The cavalier can drive opponents back without giving ground.

**Fullround:** The cavalier can make a single attack at his highest attack bonus. If the attack
hits, the attack does damage as normal, and the cavalier may attempt a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) against the target. The cavalier gains a bonus on the bull rush
combat maneuver check equal to half the amount of damage dealt on the initial attack.

**Gained:** At 15th Level
