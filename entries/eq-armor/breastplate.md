**Cost** 200 gp **Weight** 30 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +6; **Max Dex Bonus** +3; **Armor Check Penalty** -4\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 25%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Medium armor

A breastplate protects a wearer's torso with a single piece of sculpted metal, similar to the core
piece of a suit of full plate. Despite its sturdiness, its inflexibility and open back make it
inferior to complete suits of metal armor, but still an improvement over most non-metal armors.
