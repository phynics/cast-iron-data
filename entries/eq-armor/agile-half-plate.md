**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 9, Advanced Player's Guide pg 179\
**Cost** 850 gp **Weight** 55 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +8; **Max Dex Bonus** +0; **Armor Check Penalty** -7\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 40%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Heavy armor

This style of [half-plate](ref/eq-armor/half-plate) is specially crafted in a manner that allows
extra maneuverability for some physical activities. The armor check penalty for
[Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks and [jump](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks is only -4
([masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-armor) and [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) versions of this
armor reduce this penalty as well as the normal penalty). In addition, unlike with most heavy
armors, the wearer can still [run](ref/rule/run) at quadruple speed instead of triple speed.
