**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 96, Path of the Hellknight pg 63\
**Cost** 850 gp **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +8; **Max Dex Bonus** +0; **Armor Check Penalty** -7\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 40%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Heavy armor

The armor worn by a [Hellknight signifer](ref/pclass/hellknight-signifer) is a vestment of armored
plates, chain, and dark fabric. It functions as [half-plate](ref/eq-armor/half-plate), but it can be
donned and removed as though it were medium armor. Any spellcaster who wears this armor is treated
as if he were wearing Hellknight plate for the purposes of any feats and effects that require one to
be wearing Hellknight plate. This armor includes a [signifer mask](ref/eq-misc/signifer-mask),
though signifer masks can be purchased separately.
