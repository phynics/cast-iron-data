**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 9, Advanced Player's Guide pg 179\
**Cost** 53 gp **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Shield Bonus** +1; **Max Dex Bonus** —; **Armor Check Penalty** -2\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 5%

A wooden quickdraw shield is essentially the same as a
[steel quickdraw shield](ref/eq-armor/light-steel-quickdraw-shield), except it responds differently
to some spells and effects (such as [rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp)). A druid can use a
light wooden quickdraw shield, but not a steel quickdraw shield.

**Shield Bash Attacks:** You can bash an opponent with a light shield. Used this way, a light shield
is a martial bludgeoning weapon. For the purpose of penalties on attack rolls, treat a light shield
as a light weapon. If you use your shield as a weapon, you lose its AC bonus until your next turn.
An enhancement bonus on a shield does not improve the effectiveness of a shield bash made with it,
but the shield can be made into a magic weapon in its own right.

**Damage** 1d2 (small), 1d3 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special** -\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Close; Tribal
