**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 9, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 151\
**Cost** 1,500 gp **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +9; **Max Dex Bonus** +1; **Armor Check Penalty** -6\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 35%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Heavy armor

This metal suit comprises multiple pieces of interconnected and overlaying metal plates,
incorporating the benefits of numerous types of lesser armor. A complete suit of full plate (or
platemail, as it is often called) includes gauntlets, heavy leather boots, a visored helmet, and a
thick layer of padding that is worn underneath the armor. Each suit of full plate must be
individually fitted to its owner by a master armorsmith, although a captured suit can be resized to
fit a new owner at a cost of 200 to 800 (2d4 × 100) gold pieces.
