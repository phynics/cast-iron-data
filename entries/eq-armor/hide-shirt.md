**Cost** 20 gp **Weight** 18 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +3; **Max Dex Bonus** +4; **Armor Check Penalty** -1\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 15%; **Speed** 30 ft./20 ft.\
**Category** Light armor

Made from animal hide and giant lizard scales over a shirt of interwoven cords, these chest and
shoulder coverings protect Shoanti riders without restricting their mobility. The wearer of a hide
shirt can make a DC 15 Strength check as a standard action. If he succeeds, the armor gains the
[broken](ref/misc/broken) condition and drops to the ground rather than requiring the usual 1 minute
it would take to remove it. The armor must be repaired as though it had taken 8 points of damage
before it can be used again; a broken hide shirt grants no bonus to Armor Class.
