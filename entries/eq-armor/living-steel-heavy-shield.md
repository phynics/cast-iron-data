**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 132\
**Cost** 30 gp **Weight** 15 lbs.\
**Shield Bonus** +2; **Max Dex Bonus** —; **Armor Check Penalty** -2\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 15%

This nonmagical [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) is made out of
[living steel](ref/eq-material/living-steel). It has no enhancement bonus, but its material and
construction can damage metal weapons that strike it. Whenever the wielder of a metal weapon (other
than one made of adamantine) rolls a natural 1 on an attack against a creature wielding this shield,
the item must make a DC 20 Fortitude save or gain the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition. If the
weapon already has the broken condition, it is instead destroyed.
