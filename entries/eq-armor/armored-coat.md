**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 9, Advanced Player's Guide pg 179\
**Cost** 50 gp **Weight** 20 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +4; **Max Dex Bonus** +3; **Armor Check Penalty** -2\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 20%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Medium armor

This sturdy leather coat is reinforced with metal plates sewn into the lining. An armored coat is
more cumbersome than light armor but less effective than most medium armors. The advantage of it is
that a person can don it or remove it as a move action (there is no "don hastily" option for an
armored coat). If worn over other armor, use the better AC bonus and worse value in all other
categories; an armored coat has no effect if worn with heavy armor. The only magic effects that
apply are those of armor, clothing, or items worn on top.
