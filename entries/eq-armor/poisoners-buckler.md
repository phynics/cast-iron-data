**Cost** 1,505 gp **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Shield Bonus** +1; **Max Dex Bonus** —; **Armor Check Penalty** —\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** —%

Half-drow make these bucklers to allow for quick application of exactly the right poison in the
middle of combat. This [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) [buckler](ref/eq-armor/buckler) is painted
black with three deep grooves running over the surface, each fitted with a slot that can hold a vial
of poison. As a move action, the user can swipe a weapon over the surface of the buckler, coating it
with a single dose of poison. The buckler can hold 3 doses of poison. If the wearer does not have
the [poison use](ref/ability/poison-use) ability, she faces the standard risk of poisoning herself.
