**Cost** 1,800 gp **Weight** 75 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +9; **Max Dex Bonus** +1; **Armor Check Penalty** -6\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 35%; **Speed** 15 ft./10 ft.\
**Category** Heavy armor

This armor is crafted by dwarven stonesmiths from alchemically strengthened plates of shale or
basalt. Stoneplate is heavy and unwieldy, but offers incredible protection to its wearer. It is
primarily used by dwarven druids who cannot wear metal armor.
