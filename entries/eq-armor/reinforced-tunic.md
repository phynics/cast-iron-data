**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 6\
**Cost** 1 gp **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +1; **Max Dex Bonus** +5; **Armor Check Penalty** 0\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 5%; **Speed** 30 ft./20 ft.\
**Category** Light armor

Sometimes called a leine, this belted tunic has thick cords woven through it that cover vital areas.
A reinforced tunic's armor bonus is increased by 2 against attack rolls made to confirm critical
hits against the wearer.
