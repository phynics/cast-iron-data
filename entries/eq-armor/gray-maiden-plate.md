**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 84\
**Cost** 1,500 gp **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +9; **Max Dex Bonus** +1; **Armor Check Penalty** -6\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 35%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Heavy armor

The [Gray Maidens'](ref/rule/aff-scarred-ladies) distinctive armor is one of their most iconic
identifiers. Gray Maiden plate is mechanically identical to [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate),
and any effect that functions with full plate functions with Gray Maiden plate. Certain options for
Gray Maidens function only when they wear Gray Maiden plate.
