**Cost** 3,300 gp **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +9; **Max Dex Bonus** +1; **Armor Check Penalty** -5\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 35%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Heavy armor

This suit of [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) is made of
[dragonhide](ref/eq-material/dragonhide), rather than metal, so druids can wear it. It is otherwise
identical to [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-armor) full plate.

The wearer of dragonhide plate tends to draw the ire of dragons of the same type as that used in the
armor's creation.
