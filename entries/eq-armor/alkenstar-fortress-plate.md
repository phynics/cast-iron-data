**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 6\
**Cost** 2,100 gp **Weight** 75 lbs.\
**Armor Bonus** +8; **Max Dex Bonus** +0; **Armor Check Penalty** -7\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 40%; **Speed** 20 ft./15 ft.\
**Category** Heavy armor

This [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf)-forged armor features layers of overlapping plates molded to deflect
projectiles - especially those from firearms. Whenever you are the target of a ranged weapon attack
that would ignore your armor bonus to AC, you add half the fortress plate's armor bonus (including
enhancement bonuses) to your AC against that attack. This benefit does not apply to energy attacks
or magical touch attacks such as rays. Because the armor is designed with dwarves in mind, If you
have the dwarf's weapon familiarity racial trait, you increase this projectile-only bonus by 1.
Fortress plate is incredibly bulky, and its armor category can't be reduced by any effect (including
by special materials, such as [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral)). Fortress plate includes gauntlets
and a helm.
