**Sources:** Pathfinder \#110: The Thrushmoor Terror pg 14

**Type:** ingested

**Addiction:** minor

**Fortitude DC:** 14

**Price:** 15 gp

**Effect:** 3 hours; +1d6 alchemical bonus on a single Knowledge (any one), Perception, or Sense
Motive check once per minute for the duration of the effect Onset 30 minutes

**Damage:** Charisma 1d4

These small mushrooms, grown in dark forests, have red caps dotted with white spots. Able to induce
strong hallucinogenic states, they have long been used by shamans and other spiritual leaders to
enter trances to seek flashes of insight.
