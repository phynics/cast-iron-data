**Sources:** Numeria, Land of Fallen Stars pg 23

**Type:** ingested

**Addiction:** minor

**Fortitude DC:** 14

**Price:** 25 gp

**Effect:** 2 hours; +2 on Knowledge (arcana, planes, or religion) and Spellcraft checks, victim is
distracted for the purposes of [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks

**Damage:** Wisdom 1d2

Refined from the thorns of [bloodbrush](ref/monster/bloodbrush), this extract numbs the senses and
evokes vivid hallucinations, heightened awareness, and altered thought processes. Although repeated
doses can be toxic, bloodbrush extract is not widely used as a poison but rather as a recreational
drug.
