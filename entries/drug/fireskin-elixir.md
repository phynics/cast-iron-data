**Sources:** Blood of the Elements pg 27

**Type:** ingested

**Addiction:** severe

**Fortitude DC:** 20

**Price:** 100 gp

**Effect:** 1 day; +10 alchemical bonus on Disguise checks to appear as an efreeti, -1 penalty on
all saves

**Damage:** Wisdom 1d2

This elixir turns its imbiber fiery red, making it easier to pass as an
[efreeti](ref/monster/genie-efreeti). Unscrupulous efreet can sell
[fireskin elixir](ref/eq-misc/fireskin-elixir) to guests who have been barred from citizen-only
districts and must rely on disguises. Fireskin is highly addictive. Many once-savvy merchants have
fallen into destitution as they scrambled to maintain efreet favor and secure a steady supply of the
drug.
