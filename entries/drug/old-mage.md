**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 27

**Type:** injury

**Addiction:** severe

**Fortitude DC:** 18

**Price:** 1500 gp

**Effect:** 12 hours; the user gains the ability to activate scrolls as if she had succeeded at a
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) check to activate a given scroll. Each time the user
activates a scroll this way, she takes 1d4 points of
[Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) and an amount of nonlethal damage equal to the
scroll's caster level. Charisma damage taken this way is counted as ability damage caused by the
drug for the purpose of calculating the DC of saving throws to resist addiction to the drug. Neither
the Charisma damage nor the nonlethal damage can be healed until the user takes a full 8 hours of
rest.

**Damage:** see Effects

In higher circles of Avistani nobility, some petty nobles pretend to be mighty wizards. Typically
created in and smuggled north from Sargava, old-mage offers a temporary taste of eldritch power at
an exorbitant cost - both financial and physical.
