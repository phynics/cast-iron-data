**Type:** ingested

**Addiction:** moderate

**Fortitude DC:** 18

**Price:** 60 gp

**Effect:** 1 hour; +1d4 Cha and +1 Dex damage

**Damage:** Intelligence 1d2

These amber-colored lozenges instill within the user a slightly increased agility and enhance both
physical beauty and speech. So named for the fact that prostitutes often abuse the drug, harlot
sweets are gaining popularity among certain circles in the aristocracy as well.
