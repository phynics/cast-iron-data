**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 27

**Type:** ingested

**Addiction:** moderate

**Fortitude DC:** 20

**Price:** 15 gp

**Effect:** 1d3 hours; +10 alchemical bonus on Perception checks

**Secondary effect:** 4 hours; user is denied his Dexterity bonus to AC

**Damage:** Constitution 1d4

Scholars and students are often willing to accept the clumsiness and slow reactions in exchange for
the benefits gained in research and study from this bitter white powder.
