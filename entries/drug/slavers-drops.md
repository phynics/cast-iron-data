**Type:** ingested

**Addiction:** minor

**Fortitude DC:** 13

**Price:** 20 gp

**Effect:** 3 hours; -3 on Will saves, +3 on Perception checks

**Damage:** Strength 1d2

Dropped into the eyes, this clear liquid awakens the senses but makes the user mentally pliant and
open to suggestion. This drug is often used during interrogations or by slavers who seek to force
compliance via enchantment effects or similar methods of mind-control.
