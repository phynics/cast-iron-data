**Sources:** Bastards of Golarion pg 21

**Type:** inhaled

**Addiction:** minor

**Fortitude DC:** 13

**Price:** 20 gp

**Effect:** 1 hour; +1d2 alchemical bonus to Charisma, -2 penalty on saves against illusions

**Secondary effect:** after 1 hour; 75% chance to become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1d4 hours

**Damage:** Wisdom 1d2

The gnome prima donnas of Wispil in Taldor are thought to be the inventors of this mild, dizzying
powder, but its use has spread throughout much of southern Avistan. Once taken, this sparkling,
honeydew-colored inhalant gives the user a euphoric sensation that can quickly become overwhelming
and even sickening. While the long-term effects are relatively minor, few except the hardy care to
risk stomaching this toxin.
