**Type:** Ingested or inhaled

**Addiction:** severe

**Fortitude DC:** 15

**Price:** 300 gp

**Effect:** 1 hour; gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet, the ability to
see invisible creatures (as [see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility)), and vulnerability to
sonic damage

**Damage:** Constitution 1d2, 1 Wisdom drain

A resilient, iridescent mold that blooms with a peridot-colored encrustation before releasing
invisible spores, starspore grows on [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) and other
[skymetals](ref/eq-material/skymetal) as well as their alloys. It requires trace toxins found in the
rains and water of Numeria to grow. Ingesting or inhaling starspore opens the mind, enhances the
senses, and bestows a feeling of invulnerability upon the user, but these sensations come at a
price.
