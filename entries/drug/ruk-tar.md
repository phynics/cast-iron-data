**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 27

**Type:** injury

**Addiction:** moderate

**Fortitude DC:** 25

**Price:** 55 gp

**Effect:** 1 hour; +2 alchemical bonus to Strength and immunity to pain effects

**Secondary effect:** after 1 hour; the user gains the [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) condition

**Damage:** Intelligence 1d4

Common among desperate adventurers, gladiatorial combatants, and orc tribes, ruk tar is often
applied after the first blood of a fight is spilled, using the wound as an opportunity to enhance
the user's abilities to ensure victory.
