**Type:** inhaled

**Addiction:** severe

**Fortitude DC:** 24

**Price:** 600 gp

**Effect:** 24 hours; the user gains the benefits of the
[Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) feat but takes a -4 penalty on Will saves and
automatically fails Will saves against possession attempts

**Damage:** Constitution 1d4, Wisdom 1d4

Legends tell of esoterum dens in Tian Xia and Vudra where dream chasers open their minds to the
spirit world, gaining deep insights and abilities beyond mortal ken. Such tales usually continue to
describe the destructive rampages that follow when malevolent spirits take advantage of an opened
mind and possess the unfortunate individual.
