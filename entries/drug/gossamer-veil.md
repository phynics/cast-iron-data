**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 26

**Type:** ingested

**Addiction:** minor

**Fortitude DC:** 18

**Price:** 20 gp

**Effect:** 1 day; +4 alchemical bonus on saving throws against disease effects

**Secondary effect:** after 1 day; -4 penalty on saving throws against disease effects for 7 days

**Damage:** Wisdom 1d4

Cultists of [Ghlaunder](ref/faith/ghlaunder) and similar manipulators wander the streets of
impoverished neighborhoods, administering this "vaccine" to prevent diseases common in squalid
conditions, often with an admonition that it remains effective only as long as the recipient
maintains faith in the priest's deity.
