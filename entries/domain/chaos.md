**Granted Powers:** Your touch infuses life and weapons with chaos, and you revel in all things
anarchic.

**Touch of Chaos (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can imbue a target with chaos as a melee touch attack. For the next round, anytime
the target rolls a d20, he must roll twice and take the less favorable result.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Chaos Blade (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can give a weapon touched the [anarchic](ref/magic-enh/anarchic) special weapon
quality for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Domain Spells**

1. protection from law
1. align weapon (chaos only)
1. magic circle against law
1. chaos hammer
1. dispel law
1. animate objects
1. word of chaos
1. cloak of chaos
1. summon monster IX (chaos spell only)

### Azata Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 87

**Associated Domains:** Chaos, Good

**Associated Deities:** Ashava, Black Butterfly, Calistria, Cayden Cailean, Cernunnos, Chadali,
Chucaro, Desna, Elion, Findeladlara, Hathor, Hembad, Immonhiel, Jalaijatali, Keltheald, Ketephys,
Kofusachi, Lalaci, Marishi, Mazludeh, Milani, Picoperi, Pulura, Reymenda, Selket, Sinashakti, Skode,
Skrymir, Thisamet, Tolc, Valani, Yuelral

[Azatas](ref/family/azata) are a race of chaotic good outsiders.

**Elysium's Call (Su)**

**Standard:** With a touch, you can imbue creatures with the spirit of Elysium, lifting their
spirits and freeing them from bonds. The creatures touched can immediately reroll any failed saving
throws against spells and spell-like abilities of the enchantment (charm) and enchantment
(compulsion) subschools. In addition, targets receive a +2 sacred bonus on such saving throws and a
+2 sacred bonus on CMB checks to escape a grapple. Finally, targets can ignore up to 5 feet of
difficult terrain each round, as if they had the [Nimble Moves](ref/feat/nimble-moves) feat. These
bonuses last for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1), although the saving
throw reroll only applies when the creature is touched.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier rounds/day

**Replaces:** The touch of chaos power of the Chaos domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** expeditious retreat

**3rd:** fly

**6th:** planar ally (azata only)

### Demodand Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Chaos, Evil

**Associated Deities:** Rovagug, Yaezhing

**Faith-Stealing Strike (Su)**

**Standard:** You can make a single melee attack using your highest base attack bonus against a
creature capable of casting divine spells. If you damage the creature, it must succeed at a Will
save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier) or be unable to cast any divine spells
for 1 round. If you do not damage your target with the attack, this ability is expended with no
effect.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of chaos ability of the Chaos domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** align weapon (chaos or evil only)

**6th:** corrosive consumption

**Fury of the Abyss (Su)**

**Swift:** As a swift action, you can give yourself an enhancement bonus equal to 1/2 your cleric
level (minimum +1) on melee attacks, melee damage rolls, and combat maneuver checks. This bonus
lasts for 1 round. During this round, you take a -2 penalty to AC.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of chaos power of the Chaos domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** doom

**3rd:** rage

**6th:** planar binding (demons only)

### Protean Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Bastet, Besmara, Gorum, Hei Feng, Nephthys, Rovagug, Sekhmet, Sivanah,
Sobek, Speakers of the Depths, Sun Wukong

[Proteans](ref/family/protean) are a race of chaotic neutral outsiders.

**Aura of Chaos (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can surround yourself with a field of wild energies. These energies manifest as a
30-foot aura of chaos for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. All enemies within
this aura must declare one type of action at the start of their turn (attack, cast a spell, move,
use an item, or activate a special ability) and make a Will save. Creatures that fail the Will save
must take an action other than their declared action. If they succeed, they must take the declared
action. Creatures cannot select actions that they cannot perform.

**Replaces:** The chaos blade power of the Chaos domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** lesser confusion

**3rd:** displacement

**6th:** planar binding (proteans only)

### Riot Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Lamashtu, Milani

**Aura of Bedlam (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot-radius aura of chaos and strife. Enemies in the aura take a
penalty on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks and are treated as experiencing vigorous
motion (concentration DC = 10 + spell level) when attempting to cast spells. The penalty is equal to
[one-half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level. This is a mind-affecting effect.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The chaos blade power of the Chaos domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** confusion

**7th:** song of discord

**9th:** symbol of strife

### Whimsy Subdomain

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 227, Blood of Angels pg 27

**Associated Deities:** Chucaro, Findeladlara, Lalaci, Picoperi, Skrymir, Thamir Gixx, Thisamet,
Tsathoggua

**Unexpected Whimsy (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You dance, act like a buffoon, or commit some other act of whimsy. When you do,
enemies within 30 feet of you that can see and hear you must succeed a Will saving throw (DC = 10 +
1/2 your cleric level + half your Wisdom modifier) or they collapse into gales of manic laughter,
falling prone. Those who fail their saving throws can take no actions other than laughing for 1
round, but are not considered [helpless](ref/misc/helpless).

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The chaos blade of the Chaos domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** hideous laughter

**4th:** confusion

**6th:** cloak of dreams

### Variant Domain Powers

A cleric (or any other character capable of selecting a domain) who worships the deity in question
can choose to gain the variant domain power upon first gaining the domain in question, but the
choice to replace the domain power must be made immediately upon gaining access to that domain and
cannot be changed thereafter.

#### Cayden Cailean

Cayden Cailean stands opposed to any force that denies free will. Servants of Cayden make unique
exorcists, as their methods radically differ from those of other churches, despite often using
similar spells and items. Spirited consumption of alcohol usually precedes any formal activity, and
while it may seem to onlookers that Caydenites are feckless drunks, the devoted use this time to
elicit truthful statements about the possessed from those with whom they drink. In this way, the
servants of Cayden ensure they have all the proper information before moving against a supernatural
threat, which they undoubtedly do with a (mostly) sober head.

**Drunken Autonomy (Su)**

**Swift:** Whenever under the effects of mind-affecting magic, possession, or similar external
control, you can channel the unrestricted nature of your god. Instead of acting under the
compulsion, you instead act as though you had the [confused](ref/misc/confused) condition for 1
round. Producing the "act normally" result allows you to act free of mental compulsion for that
round.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of chaos domain power of the Chaos domain
