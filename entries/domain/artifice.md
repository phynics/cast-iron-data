**Granted Powers:** You can repair damage to objects, animate objects with life, and create objects
from nothing.

**Artificer's Touch (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can cast [mending](ref/spell/mending) at will, using your cleric level as the
caster level to repair damaged objects. In addition, you can cause damage to objects and construct
creatures by striking them with a melee touch attack. Objects and constructs take an amount of
damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level. This attack
bypasses an amount of damage reduction and hardness equal to your cleric level.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Dancing Weapons (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can give a weapon touched the [dancing](ref/magic-enh/dancing) special weapon
quality for 4 rounds.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Domain Spells**

1. animate rope
1. wood shape
1. stone shape
1. minor creation
1. fabricate
1. major creation
1. wall of iron
1. statue
1. prismatic sphere

### Alchemy Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 22

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Domains:** Artifice, Magic

**Associated Deities:** Brigh, Haagenti, Norgorber, Orgesh, Qi Zhong

**Divine Alchemy (Su)**

**Ability:** You can perform a 1-minute ritual that infuses a flask of water with one of your
prepared spells, creating an improvised potion that lasts until consumed or the next time you
prepare spells. You can use this ability only with spells that target one or more creatures, and the
maximum spell level you can infuse in this way is equal to 1 +
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level. These potions are treated as
[alchemist](ref/class/alchemist) extracts for the purpose of your domain spells.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The artificer's touch power of the Artifice domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** touch injection

**4th:** amplify elixir

**6th:** delayed consumption

### Construct Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Aerekostes, Aesocar, Amaznen, Apsu, Atlach-Nacha, Bharnarol, Brigh, Cixyron,
Daikitsu, Droskar, Eldas, Findeladlara, Haagenti, Malthus, Mammon, Minderhal, Neith, Ptah, Sabnach,
Soralyon, Speakers of the Depths, Torag, Yaezhing, Yamasoth, Yuelral

**Animate Servant (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can give life to inanimate objects. This ability functions as
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects) using your cleric level as the caster level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The dancing weapon power of the Artifice domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**7th:** limited wish

**8th:** polymorph any object

### Industry Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Brigh, Daikitsu, Torag

**Aura of Efficiency (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot-radius aura that grants your allies a +4 bonus on all saving
throws against effects that inflict the [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) or
[exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) condition. Any equipment wielded by your allies within this area
gets a bonus to hardness equal to 1/2 your cleric level, and your allies' attacks against targets
within this area ignore an equal amount of hardness.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The dancing weapon power of the Artifice domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** crafter's fortune

**7th:** mage's magnificent mansion

**9th:** wooden phalanx

### Toil Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Apsu, Atlach-Nacha, Bifrons, Chimon, Cixyron, Droskar, Eldas, Haagenti,
Khepri, Malthus, Mammon, Minderhal, Nameless, Omrataji, Ose, Sabnach, Speakers of the Depths,
Stygidvod, Torag, Yaezhing, Yamasoth

**Aura of Repetition (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of repetition. All enemies within this aura must make a
Will save each round or repeat their action from the previous round (if possible). Creatures that
attacked on the previous round attack again on the following round, although they may change their
target. Creatures that moved the previous round must take the same move action again, although they
may change their route. Creatures that drank a potion must do so again, even they can only drink
from an empty bottle. Actions that cannot be repeated are wasted.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The dancing weapon power of the Artifice domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** command

**5th:** waves of fatigue

**7th:** waves of exhaustion

### Trap Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** N/A

**Replacement Power:** Note: Only creatures with the [Kobold Faith](ref/trait/kobold-faith) trait
can take this subdomain.

**Supernatural Trap (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Ability:** When you gain this ability, select a supernatural ranger trap (_Ultimate Magic_ 64).
You can use this trap a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom bonus. The DCs for Perception
checks, Disable Device checks, and saving throws against the trap are equal to 10 + 1/2 your
character level + your Wisdom bonus. The trap lasts for 1 hour or until triggered.

**Usage:** Wisdom bonus times/day

**Replaces:** The dancing weapons power of the Artifice domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** snare

**3rd:** improve trap

**7th:** teleport trap

### Anointings

Clerics with the artifice domain can select an [anointing](ref/ability/annointings) in place of
their 8th-level domain ability. In each of these cases, the character treats levels in the class
used to select the [anointing](ref/ability/annointings) as his alchemist level for all relevant
anointing calculations.
