**Granted Powers:** Your touch staves off pain and death, and your healing magic is particularly
vital and potent.

**Rebuke Death (Sp)**

**Id:** rebuke

**Standard:** You can touch a living creature, healing it for an amount of damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level. You can only use this ability on a
creature that is below 0 hit points.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Healer's Blessing (Su)**

**Id:** bless

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** All of your cure spells are treated as if they were empowered, increasing the amount of
damage healed by half (+50%). This does not apply to damage dealt to undead with a cure spell. This
does not stack with the [Empower Spell](ref/feat/empower-spell) metamagic feat.

**Domain Spells**

1. cure light wounds
1. cure moderate wounds
1. cure serious wounds
1. cure critical wounds
1. breath of life
1. heal
1. regenerate
1. mass cure critical wounds
1. mass heal

**Save:** domain

### Medicine Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 24

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Dalenydra, Immonhiel, Irori, Korada, Qi Zhong, Sarenrae

**Blessed Surgery (Su)**

**Ability:** You can use this ability when using the Heal skill in order to roll the check twice and
take the higher result. When you're using this ability, any use of the Heal skill requiring 1 hour
instead takes at most 1 minute.

**Flavor:** Your divine patron guides your healing hands, allowing you to perform minor miracles
with mundane cures.

**Replaces:** The rebuke death power of the Healing domain

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** diagnose disease

**2nd:** placebo effect

**From:** domain

### Restoration Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 95

**Associated Deities:** Aesocar, Barravoclair, Bolka, Cihua Couatl, Dalenydra, Immonhiel, Irori,
Jiraviddain, Korada, Lythertida, Marishi, Milani, Osiris, Qi Zhong, Raetorgash, Reymenda, Sarenrae,
Sekhmet, Selket

**Restorative Touch (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can touch a creature, letting the healing power of your deity flow through you to
relieve the creature of a minor condition.

**Replaces:** The rebuke death power of the Healing domain

**Standard:** Your touch can remove the [dazed](ref/misc/dazed), [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued),
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken), [sickened](ref/misc/sickened), or [staggered](ref/misc/staggered)
condition. You choose which condition is removed.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** remove disease

**4th:** neutralize poison

**5th:** break enchantment

**From:** domain

### Resurrection Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 96

**Associated Deities:** Aerekostes, Aesocar, Barravoclair, Dalenydra, Immonhiel, Isis, Lorcan,
Osiris, Osolmyr, Pharasma, Qi Zhong, Sarenrae, Shei, Vildeis

**Gift of Life (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The healer's blessing power of the Healing domain

**Standard:** You can touch a creature that has died within the past minute to grant it a few
moments of life. The dead creature returns to life for a number of rounds equal to your cleric
level. Creatures returned to life in this way have a number of hit points equal to half your cleric
level, and continue to be affected by any still-active spells, conditions, or afflictions present at
the time of their death. At the end of this time, the creature dies again. The creature is free to
act as it sees fit during this time. You are granted no control over it.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**5th:** raise dead

**7th:** resurrection

**9th:** true resurrection

**From:** domain

### Variant Domain Powers

**Sources:** Haunted Heroes Handbook\
A cleric (or any other character capable of selecting a domain) who worships the deity in question
can choose to gain the variant domain power upon first gaining the domain in question, but the
choice to replace the domain power must be made immediately upon gaining access to that domain and
cannot be changed thereafter.

#### Irori

**Sources:** Haunted Heroes Handbook pg 8

Through [Irori's](ref/faith/irori) teachings, impurities in oneself and in the world are overcome.
Irori's followers are some of the most self-disciplined of Golarion's faithful, believing that
knowledge is the best weapon one can wield against the shackles of possession. They rarely directly
involve themselves in battling those intruding forces, instead hoping the afflicted can overcome
their situations through their own force of will. Haunts are the one exception to this mantra of
non-intervention. As spirits beyond the mortal cycle, haunts are often targeted by the servants of
Irori as being stains on the delicate fabric of the world.

**Exorcise Haunt (Sp)**

**Replaces:** The rebuke death domain power of the Healing domain

**Standard:** You can emit a [burst](ref/misc/burst) of positive energy. Any haunt within 15 feet of
you takes damage equal to 1d8 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day
