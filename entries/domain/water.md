**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 48

**Granted Powers:** You can manipulate water and mist and ice, conjure creatures of water, and
resist cold.

**Icicle (Sp)**

**Id:** icicle

**Standard:** You can fire an icicle from your finger, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged
touch attack. The icicle deals an amount of cold damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Cold Resistance (Ex)**

**Id:** res

**At 12th Level:** The resistance increases to 20.

**At 20th Level:** You gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to cold.

**At 6th Level:** Gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) cold 10.

**Domain Spells**

1. obscuring mist
1. fog cloud
1. water breathing
1. control water
1. ice storm
1. cone of cold
1. elemental body IV (water only)
1. horrid wilting
1. elemental swarm (water spell only)

**Save:** domain

### Flotsam Subdomain

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 180, Goblins of Golarion pg 27, Inner Sea Gods pg 225

**Associated Deities:** Jalaijatali, Jubilex, Tresmalvos, Zogmugot

**Sift (Su)**

**At 12th Level:** The objects that you retrieve are not broken.

**At 20th Level:** You can retrieve objects worth up to 5,000 gp.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** The cold resistance power of the Water domain

**Standard:** Once per day you can reach into water and pull out an object with a maximum gp value
of up to 50 gp &times& your class level. This object always has the [broken](ref/misc/broken)
condition upon being withdrawn from the water. The water you reach into must be at least deep enough
to fully immerse yourself in. If you're retrieving an object that would not normally float, assume
it rests on top of or is entwined with debris that does float. Objects retrieved in this manner
disappear after 24 hours if they're not already used up or otherwise destroyed before then.

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** make whole

**5th:** major creation

**6th:** animate objects

**From:** domain

### Flowing Subdomain

**Sources:** Champions of Balance pg 17

**Associated Deities:** Gozreh, Pharasma, Yamatsumi

**Go with the Flow (Su)**

**Replaces:** The \# ability of the Water domain

**Standard:** You can touch a creature to suppress its strong emotions. The target is affected as
the [calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions) spell, except it can still take violent actions and
violent actions against the target do not end the effect. This effect lasts a number of rounds equal
to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** cat's grace

**4th:** freedom of movement

**6th:** fluid form

**8th:** mind blank

**From:** domain

### Ice Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 92

**Associated Deities:** Charon, Inkariax, Ithaqua, Kostchtchie, Orgesh, Pharasma, Sithhud, Thremyr,
Tolc, Uzumae, Yamatsumi

**Body of Ice (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The cold resistance power of the Water domain

**Standard:** You can transmute your body and equipment to ice for a period of time. You can end the
transmutation with a **free action** on your turn. When you take on the form of ice, you are
[immune](ref/umr/immunity) to cold and have DR 5/-, but you take twice the normal amount of damage
from fire.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**7th:** freezing sphere

**9th:** polar ray

**From:** domain

### Oceans Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 94

**Associated Deities:** Aegirran, Besmara, Bokrug, Crocell, Dagon, Gaap, Geryon, Ghatanothoa,
Gozreh, Guyuku, Hei Feng, Kelizandri, Neith, Oaur-Ooung, Onos, Orgesh, Ovonovo, Pharimia, Ragadahn,
Roqorolos, Speakers of the Depths, Ylimancha, Zogmugot

**Surge (Su)**

**Replaces:** The \# power of the Water domain

**Standard:** You can cause a mighty wave to appear that pushes or pulls a single creature. Make a
combat maneuver check against the target, using your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier as your
CMB. If successful, you may pull or push the creature as if using the
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or [drag](ref/rule/drag) combat maneuver.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** slipstream

**3rd:** water walk

**9th:** tsunami

**From:** domain

### Rivers Subdomain

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 181, Inner Sea Gods pg 226, Pathfinder \#80: Empty Graves pg 65

**Associated Deities:** Crocell, Gaap, Gogunta, Hanspur, Hataam, Jalaijatali, Sobek, Wadjet

**Current Flow (Su)**

**Free:** You can increase either your base speed or your swim speed by 10 feet. While swimming, you
gain a bonus on Swim checks equal to half your class level (minimum 1). These effects last for a
number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1).

**Replaces:** The \# power of the Water domain

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** hydraulic push

**2nd:** slipstream

**5th:** elemental body II

**6th:** fluid form

**From:** domain
