**Granted Powers:** You follow a strict and ordered code of laws, and in so doing, achieve
enlightenment.

**Touch of Law (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a willing creature, infusing it with the power of divine order and
allowing it to treat all attack rolls, skill checks, ability checks, and saving throws for 1 round
as if the natural d20 roll resulted in an 11.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Staff of Order (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can give a weapon touched the [axiomatic](ref/magic-enh/axiomatic) special weapon
quality for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Domain Spells**

1. protection from chaos
1. align weapon (law only)
1. magic circle against chaos
1. order's wrath
1. dispel chaos
1. hold monster
1. dictum
1. shield of law
1. summon monster IX (law spell only)

### Archon Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 86

**Associated Domains:** Good, Law

**Associated Deities:** Aesocar, Andoletta, Angradd, Apsu, Arqueros, Damerrich, Easivra, Eldas,
Erastil, Falayna, Folgrit, Ghenshau, Grundinnar, Iomedae, Kelinahat, Kroina, Lymnieris, Mazludeh,
Myr, Neshen, Olheon, Osiris, Ragathiel, Shizuru, Smiad, Svarozic, Tanagaar, Torag, Tsukiyo, Vildeis,
Wadjet, Winlas, Zohls

[Archons](ref/family/archon) are a race of lawful good outsiders.

**Aura of Menace (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of menace. Enemies in this aura take a -2 penalty to AC
and on attacks and saves as long as they remain inside the aura.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The staff of order power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** divine favor

**3rd:** prayer

**6th:** planar ally (archon only)

**Associated Deities:** Achaekek, Alichino, Alocer, Ardad Lili, Asmodeus, Baalzebul, Barbariccia,
Barbatos, Belial, Bifrons, Cagnazzo, Calcabrina, Circiatto, Crocell, Deumus, Dispater, Doloras,

[Devils](ref/family/devil) are a race of lawful evil outsiders.

**Hell's Corruption (Su)**

**Ability:** You can cause a creature to become more susceptible to corruption as a melee touch
attack. Creatures touched take a -2 penalty on all saving throws and must roll all opposed skill
checks twice, taking the worse result. This effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your
cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of law power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** command

**3rd:** suggestion

**6th:** planar binding (devils only)

### Inevitable Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Abadar, Alseta, Amaznen, Anubis, Dranngvit, Horus, Irori, Kols, Maat,
Magrim, Ra, Thoth

[Inevitables](ref/family/inevitable) are a race of lawful neutral outsiders.

**Command (Su)**

**Standard:** You can give a creature an emotionless yet undeniable order, as per the spell
[command](ref/spell/command). A Will save negates this effect. You cannot target a creature more
than once per day with this ability.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of law power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**3rd:** command undead

**5th:** greater command

**6th:** planar binding (inevitables only)

### Judgment Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Damerrich, Maat, Neshen, Ragathiel, Zohls

**Chastisement (Su)**

**Standard:** You can cast a strengthened spell against a creature that damaged you since your last
turn. This spell must target the creature that damaged you, and is cast at +1 caster level. Area of
effect spells cannot be used in conjunction with this ability, but other spells that target multiple
creatures may be.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of law ability of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** castigate

**4th:** rebuke

**5th:** mark of justice

### Kyton Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Evil, Law

**Associated Deities:** Zon-Kuthon

**Pain Is Power (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Immediate:** After taking damage from any source, you may attempt a DC 25
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check. If you succeed, you gain the benefits of a
[blessing of fervor](ref/spell/blessing-of-fervor) spell for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your
cleric level. If you fail, this ability is expended with no effect.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The staff of order ability of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** delay pain

**2nd:** instrument of agony

**3rd:** agonize

**5th:** symbol of pain

### Legislation Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Law, Rune

**Associated Deities:** Abadar, Asmodeus, Dispater, Lissala, Winlas

**Prohibition (Su)**

**Standard:** You can command a creature within 30 feet to refrain from committing a particular
action (including any referenced in the spell _[forbid action](ref/spell/forbid-action)_). If the
creature performs the named action before the beginning of your next turn, it takes an amount of
damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level and the effect
ends. This is a language-dependent effect.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of law power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** forbid action

**5th:** greater forbid action

**9th:** mass hold monster

### Loyalty Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Aesocar, Ghenshau, Grundinnar, Kols, Matravash, Sicva, Winlas

**Touch of Loyalty (Su)**

**Standard:** You can touch a willing creature, granting it a +4 sacred bonus on saving throws to
resist charm, compulsion, and fear effects for 1 hour.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of law power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** remove fear

**5th:** greater command

### Slavery Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Circiatto, Furcas, Gavidya, Hadregash, Inkariax, Jacarkas, Kalma, Lanishra,
Losarkur, Vevelor, Zursvaater

**Master's Yoke (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can attempt to assert your dominance over any visible creature within 30 feet by
declaring the target your property. The target can resist this effect with a successful Will save
(DC = 10 + half your class level + your Wisdom modifier). If the target fails the save, it is
affected as if by [dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster) for a number of rounds equal to
half your class level. The target can choose to ignore any order you give and instead take 2 points
of Constitution damage and become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. This is a
language-based, mind-affecting effect.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The staff of order power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** charm person

**5th:** dominate person

**8th:** binding

### Sovereignty Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Asmodeus, Dispater, Iomedae, Lissala, Zon-Kuthon

**Demand Subjugation (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Ability:** You can force a defeated creature to accept a binding pact as a condition of its
surrender, as if you are using lesser geas, except the creature must have a number of Hit Dice equal
to or less than your cleric level.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**At 16th level,** the ability functions as per [geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest).

**Replaces:** The staff of order power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** castigate

**5th:** mass castigate

**9th:** overwhelming presence

### Tyranny Subdomain

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 182, Blood of Fiends pg 15, Inner Sea Gods pg 227

**Associated Deities:** Aksha, Aroggus, Bohga, Bundha, Caera, Dispater, Dradjit, Eligos, Hudima,
Inma, Jyotah, Kaikyton, Kunkarna, Lanishra, Malacoda, Mursha, Otikaya, Prihasta, Ravana, Sicva,
Surpa, Uruskreil, Vibhishah, Wylgart, Zabha, Zursvaater

**Tyrannical Strike (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Immediate:** Upon successfully landing a melee attack on an opponent, you can choose to forgo the
damage (but not any other effects of the attack) to instead affect the creature you hit as per a
greater command spell, with a caster level equal to your class level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The staff of order power of the Law domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** command

**3rd:** bestow curse

**7th:** symbol of persuasion
