**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 41

**Granted Powers:** You can speak with and befriend animals with ease.

**Class Skill (Ex)**

**Passive:** You treat Knowledge (nature) as a class skill.

**Speak with Animals (Sp)**

**Id:** speak

**Standard:** You can [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals), as per the spell.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric3 level

**Animal Companion (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain the service of an [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion). Your
effective druid level for this animal companion is equal to your cleric level - 3. (Druids who take
this ability through their nature bond class feature use their druid level - 3 to determine the
abilities of their animal companions).

**Domain Spells**

1. calm animals
1. hold animal
1. dominate animal
1. summon nature's ally IV (animals only)
1. beast shape III (animals only)
1. antilife shell
1. animal shapes
1. summon nature's ally VIII (animals only)
1. shapechange

**Save:** domain

### Feather Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 90

**Associated Deities:** Aldinach, Cernunnos, Daclau-Sar, Easivra, Erastil, Horus, Ketephys,
Losarkur, Morrobahn, Neith, Rasvocel, Shub-Niggurath, Tanagaar, Tjasse, Uskyeria, Ylimancha

**Replacement Power:** Add Fly to your list of class skills. In addition, whenever you cast a spell
that grants you a fly speed, your maneuverability increases by one step (up to perfect).

**Eyes of the Hawk (Ex)**

**Passive:** You gain a racial bonus on Perception checks equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum
+1). In addition, if you can act during a surprise round, you receive a +2 racial bonus on your
Initiative check.

**Replaces:** The speak with animals power of the Animal domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** feather fall

**3rd:** fly

**6th:** mass fly

**From:** domain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 91

**Associated Deities:** Aksha, Aldinach, Alocer, Angazhan, Anogetz, Baphomet, Barbariccia, Bastet,
Bundha, Caera, Camazotz, Cernunnos, Daclau-Sar, Daikitsu, Dradjit, Ealdeez, Erastil, Ghlaunder,

Tresmalvos, Urazra, Uskyeria, Vibhishah, Zabha, Zarongel

**Predator's Grace (Su)**

**Replaces:** The speak with animals power of the Animal domain

**Swift:** Grant yourself a +10-foot bonus to your base speed for 1 round. This bonus increases by 5
feet for every 5 cleric levels you possess. In addition, you gain
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) for 1 round. If you already possess low-light vision,
the range of your sight becomes three times that of a human in dim light for 1 round.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** magic fang

**3rd:** beast shape I (animals only)

**From:** domain

### Insect Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 23

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Achaekek, Aldinach, Deskari, Ghlaunder, Kitumu, Mazmezz

**Replacement Power:** The domain spells provided by the Insect domain affect vermin rather than
animals, treating them as if they were not immune to spells with the mind-affecting descriptor. Any
such spell that allows a Will save can instead be negated by the target vermin with a successful
Fortitude save at the same DC.

**Exoskeleton (Su)**

**Replaces:** The speak with animals power of the Animal domain

**Swift:** Grow an exoskeleton that grants you an enhancement bonus to your natural armor and an
amount of [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points). The enhancement bonus is equal to
1 + [one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level, and the tempoerary hit points
are equal to 1d4 + half of your cleric level. The exoskeleton retracts after 1 round, ending its
benefits.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** giant vermin

**5th:** vermin shape II

**8th:** summon nature's ally VIII (1d3 goliath stag beetles)

**From:** domain
