**Granted Powers:** You have mastery over earth, metal, and stone, can fire darts of acid, and
command earth creatures.

**Acid Dart (Sp)**

**Id:** dart

**Standard:** As a standard action, you can unleash an acid dart targeting any foe within 30 feet as
a ranged touch attack. This acid dart deals an amount of acid damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Acid Resistance (Ex)**

**Id:** res

**At 12th Level:** Your acid resistance increases to 20.

**At 20th Level:** You gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to acid.

**At 6th Level:** You gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) acid 10.

**Domain Spells**

1. magic stone
1. soften earth and stone
1. stone shape
1. spike stones
1. wall of stone
1. stoneskin
1. elemental body IV (earth only)
1. earthquake
1. elemental swarm (earth spell only)

**Save:** domain

### Caves Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 87

**Associated Deities:** Abhoth, Ashukharma, Ayrzul, Benorus, Bes, Cyth-V'sug, Draghignazzo,
Fandarra, Ioramvol, Mhar, Murnath, Nalmungder, Nivi Rhombodazzle, Orgesh, Soralyon, The Green
Mother, Torag, Xoveron, Yamasoth, Yamatsumi, Yuelral

**Tunnel Runner (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The acid resistance power of the Earth domain

**Standard:** You can choose to move through tunnels and caves with ease. While this ability is
active, you can move across any stone surface as if under the effects of
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb). You can also see very well in darkness, gaining
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) out to a range of 60 feet. If you already possess darkvision,
extend the range by 60 feet. While underground, you also gain an insight bonus equal to your cleric
level on Stealth skill checks and an insight bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier on initiative
checks.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per cleric level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** create pit

**3rd:** spiked pit

**6th:** hungry pit

**From:** domain

### Metal Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 94

**Associated Deities:** Abadar, Anubis, Ayrzul, Benorus, Brigh, Cixyron, Eldas, Imbrex, Kaikyton,
Mammon, Minderhal, Murnath, Omrataji, Onos, Orgesh, Ptah, Quindiovatos, Torag, Ushitora, Varg,
Wylgart

**Metal Fist (Su)**

**Replaces:** The acid dart power of the Earth domain

**Swift:** You can turn your fists into metal for 1 round, allowing you to make unarmed strikes that
deal 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage plus your Strength modifier. These unarmed strikes do not
provoke attacks of opportunity, but attacking with both uses the two-weapon fighting rules as
normal. In addition, these unarmed strikes ignore the [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of items with a
hardness of 10 or less.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** heat metal

**6th:** wall of iron

**8th:** iron body

**From:** domain

### Petrification Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 25

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Ayrzul, Minderhal, Soralyon, Xoveron

**Fossil Form (Su)**

**Replaces:** The acid dart power of the Earth domain

**Swift:** At the beginning of your turn, you can infuse your flesh with minerals, granting you
cumbersome security. Until the beginning of your next turn, you are [staggered](ref/misc/staggered),
gain [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 2, and gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against petrification.
This hardness increases by 2 and your saving throw bonus increases by 1 for every 4 cleric levels
you have.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** calcific touch

**5th:** stoneskin

**6th:** flesh to stone

**8th:** statue

**From:** domain

### Radiation Subdomain

**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 13

**Associated Deities:** N/A

**Replacement Power:** Note: Only creatures with the [Kobold Faith](ref/trait/kobold-faith) trait
can take this subdomain.

**Radiating Touch (Su)**

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** Yes

**Replaces:** The acid dart power of the Earth domain

**Standard:** You can irradiate any unattended object for a number of rounds equal to your cleric
level. While an object is irradiated, it emits a faint aura of transmutation. Any creature that
passes within 5 feet of the aura must succeed at a Will save (DC equal to 10 + 1/2 your cleric
level + your Wisdom modifier) or become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened). You're immune to this
object's effect.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** defoliate

**4th:** blight

**8th:** horrid wilting

**From:** domain
