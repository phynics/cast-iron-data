**Granted Powers:** You are infused with luck, and your mere presence can spread good fortune.

**Bit of Luck (Sp)**

**Id:** bit

**Standard:** You can touch a willing creature, giving it a bit of luck. For the next round, any
time the target rolls a d20, he may roll twice and take the more favorable result.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Good Fortune (Ex)**

**Id:** good

**Immediate:** You can reroll any one d20 roll that you have just made before the results of the
roll are revealed. You must take the result of the reroll, even if it's worse than the original
roll.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 6 cleric levels

**Domain Spells**

1. true strike
1. aid
1. protection from energy
1. freedom of movement
1. break enchantment
1. mislead
1. spell turning
1. moment of prescience
1. miracle

**Save:** domain

### Curse Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 88

**Associated Deities:** Andirifkhu, Calistria, Chinostes (Evil Aspect), Chinostes (Good Aspect),

**Malign Eye (Su)**

**Replaces:** The bit of luck power of the Luck domain

**Standard:** You can afflict one target within 30 feet with your malign eye, causing it to take a
-2 penalty on all saving throws against your spells. The effect lasts for 1 minute or until the
target hits you with an attack.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** bane

**3rd:** bestow curse

**6th:** eyebite

**From:** domain

### Fate Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 90

**Associated Deities:** Chadali, Chaldira, Desna, Hembad, Irez, Kofusachi, Kurgess, Lalaci, Magdh,
Nivi Rhombodazzle, Raetorgash, Shelyn, Shelyn (pre-Earthfall), Sinashakti, Uaransaph

**Tugging Strands (Su)**

**Ability:** You can force a creature within line of sight to reroll any one roll that it has just
made before the result of the roll is revealed. The result of the reroll must be taken, even if it
is worse than the original roll.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The good fortune power of the Luck domain

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 6 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** augury

**3rd:** borrow fortune

**From:** domain

### Imagination Subdomain

**Sources:** Champions of Purity pg 21

**Associated Deities:** Bes

**Haze of Daydreams (Su)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** The good fortune ability of the Luck domain

**Standard:** You can emit a 15-foot-radius haze of daydreams. Creatures within the haze gain the
[fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) condition unless they succeed at a Will save (DC equal to 10 + 1/2
your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier). You may designate a number of creatures equal to your
cleric level that are not affected by the haze. The fascinated condition ends immediately when the
creatures leave the area or the aura expires.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** silent image

**2nd:** minor image

**3rd:** major image

**5th:** dream

**From:** domain
