**Granted Powers:** You can manipulate lightning, mist, and wind, traffic with air creatures, and
are resistant to electricity damage.

**Lightning Arc (Sp)**

**Id:** arc

**Standard:** Target any foe within 30 ft. with a ranged touch attack, dealing an amount of
electricity damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Electricity Resistance (Ex)**

**Id:** res

**At 12th Level:** Resistance increases to 20

**At 20th Level:** [Immune](ref/umr/immunity) to electricity

**At 6th Level:** Gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) electricity 10

**Domain Spells**

1. obscuring mist
1. wind wall
1. gaseous form
1. air walk
1. control winds
1. chain lightning
1. elemental body IV (air only)
1. whirlwind
1. elemental swarm (air spell only)

**Save:** domain

### Cloud Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 88

**Associated Deities:** Chupurvagasti, Findeladlara, Ghlaunder, Gozreh, Hathor, Hei Feng, Ithaqua,
Nyarlathotep (Faceless Sphinx), Pazuzu, Pulura, Rull, Shelyn, Shelyn (pre-Earthfall), Tjasse

**Thundercloud (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The electricity resistance power of the Air domain

**Standard:** Summon a storm cloud. This power functions as [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud) except
that creatures inside the cloud are [deafened](ref/misc/deafened) and take 2d6 points of electricity
damage each round from the flashes of thunder and lightning. Once created, you can concentrate on
the cloud to move it up to 30 feet each round.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** solid fog

**9th:** storm of vengeance

**From:** domain

### Lightning Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 24

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Gozreh, Hei Feng, Rovagug, Rull

**Lightning Rod (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The electricity resistance power of the Air domain

**Swift:** When you cast a spell with the electricity descriptor, you can designate one creature
within line of sight. The spell's damage against that creature increases by 50%, as if affected by
the [Empower Spell](ref/feat/empower-spell) feat. This additional damage results from divine power
that is not subject to being reduced by electricity resistance, and you take an equal amount of
electricity damage immediately after you cast the spell. The spell can deal this additional damage
only once, even if it could affect the target multiple times.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** shocking grasp

**2nd:** flame blade (deals electricity damage and gains the electricity descriptor instead of fire)

**3rd:** lightning bolt

**From:** domain

### Wind Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 97

**Associated Deities:** Areshkagal, Baalzebul, Chupurvagasti, Eligos, Geon, Ghlaunder, Gozreh, Hei
Feng, Horus, Hshurha, Isph-Aun-Vuln, Ithaqua, Libicocco, Llamolaek, Nyarlathotep (Faceless Sphinx),
Onos, Pazuzu, Rull, Seramaydiel, Sugroz, Tjasse, Uaransaph, Xiquiripat, Ylimancha

**Wind Blast (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The lightning arc power of the Air domain

**Standard:** Unleash a blast of air in a 30-foot line. Make a combat maneuver check against each
creature in the line, using your caster level as your base attack bonus and your Wisdom modifier in
place of your Strength modifier. Treat the results as a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) attempt.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** whispering wind

**6th:** wind walk

**9th:** winds of vengeance

**From:** domain
