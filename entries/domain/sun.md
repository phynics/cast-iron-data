**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 47

**Granted Powers:** You see truth in the pure and burning light of the sun, and can call upon its
blessing or wrath to work great deeds.

**Sun's Blessing (Su)**

**Id:** sun

**Passive:** Whenever you [channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) to harm undead
creatures, add your cleric level to the damage dealt. Undead do not add their
[channel resistance](ref/umr/channel-resistance) to their saves when you channel positive energy.

**Nimbus of Light (Su)**

**Id:** light

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot nimbus of light. This acts as a [daylight](ref/spell/daylight)
spell. In addition, undead within this radius take an amount of damage equal to your cleric level
each round that they remain inside the nimbus. Spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with the darkness descriptor are automatically
dispelled if brought inside this nimbus.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

**Domain Spells**

1. endure elements
1. heat metal
1. searing light
1. fire shield
1. flame strike
1. fire seeds
1. sunbeam
1. sunburst
1. prismatic sphere

**Save:** domain

### Day Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 88

**Associated Deities:** Easivra, Horus, Iaozrael, Iomedae, Jaidi, Keltheald, Khepri, Muronna,
Nurgal, Ra, Sarenrae, Shizuru, Skode

**Day's Resurgence (Su)**

**Ability:** You can restore a single creature as if it had just completed 8 hours of rest. It takes
10 minutes to use this power. If the use of this power is disrupted, it must be restarted, but it is
not lost.

At the end of the 10 minutes, a single willing creature that you have touched regains hit points as
if it had rested for the night and may make new saving throws against effects that require a save
once per day. The target does not suffer any negative effects if such saving throws are failed, but
success counts toward removing the affliction (if possible).

This ability does not allow a target to prepare spells an additional time per day.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The nimbus of light power of the Sun domain

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 cleric levels

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** continual flame

**3rd:** daylight

**From:** domain

### Light Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 93

**Associated Deities:** Aldinach, Easivra, Horus, Iaozrael, Iomedae, Jaidi, Keltheald, Lalaci,
Pharimia, Ra, Sarenrae, Shizuru, Skode

**Blinding Flash (Su)**

**Replaces:** The sun's blessing power of the Sun domain

**Standard:** You can emit a flash of light from your holy symbol or divine focus. The most powerful
light emanates out 20 feet from you. Creatures with fewer Hit Dice than your cleric level within
this area are [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4 rounds unless they succeed at a Fortitude save.
All creatures in this area are [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** faerie fire

**3rd:** daylight

**From:** domain

### Revelation Subdomain

**Sources:** Champions of Purity pg 21

**Associated Deities:** Iomedae, Sarenrae, Shizuru

**Guided Eyes (Su)**

**Passive:** [Perception](ref/skill/perception) (WIS) is always a class skill for you. In addition,
whenever you make a skill check to see through a disguise or find something that is hidden or
concealed, you gain a +4 sacred bonus on the check.

**Replaces:** The sun's blessing ability of the Sun domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** detect secret doors

**2nd:** see invisibility

**3rd:** banish seeming

**5th:** true seeing

**From:** domain

### Thirst Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 26

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Aldinach, Lalaci, Nurgal, Sarenrae

**Wilting Glare (Su)**

**Replaces:** The sun's blessing power of the Sun domain

**Standard:** You can leach moisture and vitality from a living, corporeal creature you can see
within 30 feet. The target takes an amount of nonlethal damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level and becomes
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) until the beginning of your next turn (Fortitude negates). Plant
creatures and those with the aquatic or water subtype take lethal damage from this ability, and they
are fatigued for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Withering Nimbus (Su)**

**Alters:** The nimbus of light power of the Sun domain

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot nimbus of light. This acts as a [daylight](ref/spell/daylight)
spell. In addition, creatures with the aquatic or water subtype within this radius take an amount of
damage equal to your cleric level each round that they remain inside the nimbus. Spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with the darkness descriptor are automatically
dispelled if brought inside this nimbus.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**3rd:** cup of dust

**9th:** horrid wilting

**From:** domain
