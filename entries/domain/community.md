**Granted Powers:** Your touch can heal wounds, and your presence instills unity and strengthens
emotional bonds.

**Calming Touch (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a creature as a standard action to heal it of 1d6 points of nonlethal
damage + 1 point per cleric level. This touch also removes the [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued),
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken), and [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) conditions (but has no effect on more
severe conditions).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Unity (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Immediate:** Whenever a spell or effect targets you and one or more allies within 30 feet, you can
use this ability to allow your allies to use your saving throw against the effect in place of their
own. Each ally must decide individually before the rolls are made.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Domain Spells**

1. bless
1. shield other
1. prayer
1. imbue with spell ability
1. telepathic bond
1. heroes' feast
1. refuge
1. mass cure critical wounds
1. miracle

### Cooperation Subdomain

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 224, Champions of Purity pg 20

**Associated Deities:** Arqueros, Bharnarol, Elion, Hembad, Matravash, Mazludeh, Nephthys, Svarozic,
Ulon

**Synergistic Touch (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a creature to confer upon it the benefits of any one teamwork feat that
you possess. This effect persists for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The calming touch ability of the Community domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** borrow skill

**2nd:** share language

**3rd:** coordinated effort

### Education Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Community, Knowledge

**Associated Deities:** Abraxas, Andoletta, Bergelmir, Bharnarol, Elion, Erastil, Eritrice, Irori,
Nethys, Shei, Svarozic

**Teaching Moment (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Immediate:** When you or an ally within 30 feet rolls a natural 1 or a natural 20 on an attack
roll, an ability check, a skill check, or a saving throw, you can grant all allies within 30 feet
special insights that help them overcome similar challenges. Once during the next minute, each
affected creature can choose to roll twice and take the better result before attempting an attack
roll, ability check, skill check, or saving throw.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The unity power of the Community domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** know the enemy

**2nd:** fox's cunning

**6th:** battlemind link

**8th:** circle of clarity

### Family Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Bergelmir, Bes, Bolka, Daikitsu, Dretha, Erastil, Fandarra, Folgrit,
Gavidya, Iapholi, Imbrex, Inma, Isis, Jiraviddain, Kaikyton, Kurgess, Lorris, Lythertida, Mazludeh,
Shei, Svarozic, Thisamet

**Binding Ties (Su)**

**Standard:** You can touch an ally and remove one condition affecting the ally by transferring it
to yourself. This transfer lasts a number of rounds equal to your cleric level, but you can end it
as a free action on your turn. At the end of this effect, the condition reverts to the original
creature, unless it has ended or is removed by another effect. While this power is in use, the
target is immune to the transferred condition.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The calming touch power of the Community domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** calm emotions

**3rd:** create food and water

### Home Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Alseta, Bergelmir, Bes, Daikitsu, Dretha, Elion, Erastil, Fandarra,
Findeladlara, Folgrit, Ghenshau, Imbrex, Inma, Jiraviddain, Kurgess

**Guarded Hearth (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Ability:** You can create a ward that protects a specified area. Creating this ward takes 10
minutes of uninterrupted work. This ward has a maximum radius of 5 feet per 2 cleric levels you
possess. When the ward is completed, you designate any number of creatures inside its area. Should
any other creature enter the warded area, all of the selected creatures are immediately alerted (and
awoken if they were asleep). The designated creatures also receive a sacred bonus equal to your
Wisdom modifier on all saving throws and attack rolls while inside the warded area. This ward
immediately ends if you leave the area. The ward lasts for 1 hour per cleric level.

**Usage:** Once a day

**Replaces:** The unity power of the Community domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** alarm

**3rd:** glyph of warding

**7th:** guards and wards
