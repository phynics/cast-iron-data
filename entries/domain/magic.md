**Granted Powers:** You are a true student of all things mystical, and see divinity in the purity of
magic.

**Hand of the Acolyte (Su)**

**Standard:** You can cause your melee weapon to fly from your grasp and strike a foe before
instantly returning. You can make a single attack using a melee weapon at a range of 30 feet. This
attack is treated as a ranged attack with a thrown weapon, except that you add your Wisdom modifier
to the attack roll instead of your Dexterity modifier (damage still relies on Strength). This
ability cannot be used to perform a combat maneuver.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Dispelling Touch (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can use a targeted [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) effect as a melee touch
attack.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Domain Spells**

1. identify
1. magic mouth
1. dispel magic
1. imbue with spell ability
1. spell resistance
1. antimagic field
1. spell turning
1. protection from spells
1. mage's disjunction

### Alchemy Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 22

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Domains:** Artifice, Magic

**Associated Deities:** Brigh, Haagenti, Norgorber, Orgesh, Qi Zhong

**Divine Alchemy (Su)**

**Ability:** You can perform a 1-minute ritual that infuses a flask of water with one of your
prepared spells, creating an improvised potion that lasts until consumed or the next time you
prepare spells. You can use this ability only with spells that target one or more creatures, and the
maximum spell level you can infuse in this way is equal to 1 +
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level. These potions are treated as
[alchemist](ref/class/alchemist) extracts for the purpose of your domain spells.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The hand of the acolyte power of the Magic domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** touch injection

**4th:** amplify elixir

**6th:** delayed consumption

### Arcane Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Abraxas, Aerekostes, Aesdurath, Alseta, Amaznen, Asmodeus, Barbatos, Isis,
Lorthact, Nalinivati, Nethys, Nyarlathotep (Black Pharaoh), Nyarlathotep (Faceless Sphinx),
Nyarlathotep (Haunter of the Dark), Shiggarreb, Shyka, Thoth, Tsathoggua, Yuelral

**Arcane Beacon (Su)**

**Standard:** You can become a beacon of arcane energy until the end of your next turn. The aura
emanates 15 feet from you. All arcane spells cast within the aura either gain a +1 bonus to their
caster level or increase their saving throw DC by +1. The caster chooses the benefit when she casts
the spell.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The hand of the acolyte power of the Magic domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** magic aura

**4th:** arcane eye

**6th:** analyze dweomer

### Divine Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Aesdurath, Amaznen, Asmodeus, Barbatos, Isis, Korada, Kroina, Nalinivati,
Nethys, Nyarlathotep (Black Pharaoh), Nyarlathotep (Faceless Sphinx), Nyarlathotep (Haunter of the
Dark), Orcus, Qi Zhong, Sezelrian, Shyka, Skrymir, Tsathoggua, Urgathoa, Yuelral

**Divine Vessel (Su)**

**Swift:** Whenever you are the target of a divine spell, you can grant each ally within 15 feet of
you a divine boon. This boon grants a +2 bonus on the next attack roll, skill check, or ability
check made before the end of their next turn.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The hand of the acolyte power of the Magic domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** bless water

**5th:** cleanse

**7th:** resurrection

**9th:** miracle

### Rites Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 26

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Abraxas, Asmodeus, Nalinivati, Nethys, Nyarlathotep (Black Pharaoh),
Nyarlathotep (Faceless Sphinx), Nyarlathotep (Haunter of the Dark), Sivanah

**Replacement Power:** When casting a Rites domain spell with a costly material component - or
casting _[permanency](ref/spell/permanency)_ on any domain spell - reduce the cost of the necessary
components by 20%.

**Enduring Ritual (Su)**

**Swift:** When you cast a spell with a duration of at least 1 minute per caster level, you can
increase the spell's effective caster level by 1 for the purpose of determining its duration and
preventing attempts to dispel it.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**At 10th level,** the effective caster level increases by 2.

**At 20th level,** the effective caster level increases by 3.

**Replaces:** The hand of the acolyte power of the Magic domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** sanctify corpse

**5th:** permanency

**8th:** binding
