**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 182, Gods and Magic pg 53, Horror Adventures pg 49, Bestiary 4 pg
134, Inner Sea World Guide pg 217

**Granted Powers:** You can call upon the cold darkness between the stars to gain flight, travel to
other worlds, or summon monsters from beyond to do your bidding.

**Guarded Mind (Ex)**

**Id:** mind

**Passive:** You gain a +2 insight bonus on saving throws against all mind-affecting effects.

**Part the Veil (Su)**

**Action:** Swift Action

**Flavor:** You can lace spells you cast with the raw madness that waits in the outer darkness.

**Id:** part

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 cleric levels

Activating this ability must be done as you cast a spell that targets a single creature and that
allows a Will saving throw to negate or reduce the spell's primary effect. If the target fails to
resist the spell, the target is also [confused](ref/misc/confused) for a number of rounds equal to
the spell's level as visions of the void cause temporary insanity.

The victim must succeed at a new saving throw each round to end the effect; these additional saving
throws apply only to the additional confusion effect and not to the original spell effect. Part the
veil is a mind-affecting effect.

**Domain Spells**

1. feather fall
1. levitate
1. fly
1. lesser planar binding
1. overland flight
1. planar binding
1. reverse gravity
1. greater planar binding
1. interplanetary teleport

**Save:** domain

### Dark Tapestry Subdomain

**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 49, People of the Stars pg 23, Inner Sea Gods pg 224, Pathfinder
\#46: Wake of the Watcher pg 62, Paizo Blog - Golarion Day Subdomains pg 1, Bestiary 4 pg 135

**Associated Deities:** Azathoth, Cthulhu, Hastur, Nhimbaloth, Rhan-Tegoth, Shub-Niggurath,
Yog-Sothoth

**It Came from Beyond (Su)**

**Passive:** Once per day when you cast a summoning spell, any one creature you summon is more
powerful than normal. The creature gains the [advanced creature](ref/template/advanced) simple
template. If you summon more than one creature with a spell, only one of the summoned creatures
gains the advanced creature simple template.

A summoned creature that gains the advanced creature simple template in this manner looks unusually
deformed or hideous. This ability only works on spells you cast as a cleric - it does not work on
spellcasting abilities gained from any other spellcasting classes you might have.

**Replaces:** The guarded mind power of the Void domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** summon monster II

**5th:** summon monster V

**7th:** insanity

**From:** domain

### Isolation Subdomain

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 181, Horror Adventures pg 49, Inner Sea Gods pg 225

**Associated Deities:** Ah Pook, Atlach-Nacha, Iggeret, Inkariax, Maeha, Mneoc, Nhimbaloth,
Nocticula (Redeemed), Roqorolos, Shawnari, Uaransaph

**Aura of Isolation (Su)**

**Replaces:** The part the veil power of the Void domain

**Standard:** You can create a 20-foot aura that causes enemies within to be overcome with feelings
of isolation and loss. Your enemies treat the aura as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles), as they become sluggish within its area of
effect. Furthermore, while within the aura, your enemies can't [flank](ref/rule/flanking) with one
another, nor can they use or benefit from the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action. This is
a mind-affecting emotion effect.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier rounds/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** crushing despair

**6th:** wall of force

**8th:** prismatic wall

**From:** domain

### Stars Subdomain

**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 49, Bestiary 4 pg 135, Paizo Blog - Golarion Day Subdomains pg 1,
Pathfinder \#46: Wake of the Watcher pg 62, Inner Sea Gods pg 227, People of the Stars pg 23

**Associated Deities:** Azathoth, Black Butterfly, Cthulhu, Groetus, Hastur, Maat, Maeha, Pulura,
Rhan-Tegoth, Shub-Niggurath, Yog-Sothoth

**The Stars Are Right (Su)**

**Passive:** If you prepare your cleric spells while the stars are visible to you, you may
spontaneously cast any of your Stars subdomain domain spells by swapping out a spell of an equal
spell level. Any Stars subdomain spells that you cast while the stars are visible to you heal you of
damage equal to the spell's level as you cast the spell.

**Replaces:** The part the veil power of the Void domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** hypnotic pattern

**7th:** sunbeam

**9th:** meteor swarm

**From:** domain
