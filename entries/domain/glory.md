**Granted Powers:** You are infused with the glory of the divine, and are a true foe of the undead.

**Channel Boost (Su)**

**Passive:** When you channel positive energy to harm undead creatures, the save DC to halve the
damage is increased by 2.

**Touch of Glory (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can cause your hand to shimmer with divine radiance, allowing you to touch a
creature and give it a bonus equal to your cleric level on a single Charisma-based skill check or
Charisma ability check. This ability lasts for 1 hour or until the creature touched elects to apply
the bonus to a roll.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Divine Presence (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of divine presence. All allies within this aura are
treated as if under the effects of a [sanctuary](ref/spell/sanctuary) spell with a DC equal to 10 +
1/2 your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier. If an ally leaves the area or makes an attack, the
effect ends for that ally. If you make an attack, the effect ends for you and your allies.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Domain Spells**

1. shield of faith
1. bless weapon
1. searing light
1. holy smite
1. righteous might
1. undeath to death
1. holy sword
1. holy aura
1. gate

### Chivalry Subdomain

**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 21

**Associated Deities:** Iomedae, Shizuru

**Bolstering Touch (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a willing creature, bracing it against frightening situations and
allowing it to remain calm. The first fear effect that would affect the subject within the next hour
is suppressed for the first 1d4 rounds of its effect. If you touch a creature currently suffering
from an ongoing fear effect, this ability instead suppresses the ongoing fear effect for 1 round.
The rounds during which the subject ignores the triggering fear effect still count against the
effect's duration. If the fear effect is suppressed for longer than it lasts, the subject isn't
affected by the fear effect at all.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of glory power of the Glory domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** remove fear

**3rd:** heroism

**6th:** greater heroism

### Heroism Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Damerrich, General Susumu, Iomedae, Myr, Osolmyr, Ra, Sarenrae, Shizuru,
Skode

**Aura of Heroism (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Swift:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of heroism. Allies in the area are treated as if they were
under the effects of [heroism](ref/spell/heroism).

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The divine presence power of the Glory domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**3rd:** heroism

**6th:** greater heroism

### Honor Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Akuma, Damerrich, General Susumu, Iomedae, Jaidi, Myr, Quindiovatos, Ra,
Rubicante, Shizuru, Smiad, Uruskreil

**Honor Bound (Su)**

**Standard:** With a touch, you can remind a creature of its duties and responsibilities, granting
it a new saving throw against each enchantment (charm) or enchantment (compulsion) effect that
currently affects it. If the saving throw is successful, the enchantment effect is ended. This power
only affects effects that allow a save. If you fail a save against such an effect, you can use this
ability as an immediate action to grant yourself an additional save. Once the target (either you or
a touched creature) has made one additional save per effect, this ability has no further effect on
that particular enchantment effect.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of glory power of the Glory domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** zone of truth

**6th:** geas/quest

### Hubris Subdomain

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Domains:** Glory, Nobility

**Associated Deities:** Dispater, General Susumu, Iomedae, Jaidz, Lissala, Olheon, Ydersius

**Class Skill**

**Passive:** You gain [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) (CHA) as a class skill.

**Replaces:** The channel boost ability of the Glory domain

**Divine Demand (Su)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Swift:** You can petition your divine patron for far greater power than you deserve. This ability
can be used only as you cast a spell that has a reduced (but not negated) effect on a successful
save. The saving throw DC of the spell increases by 2, and you gain a +2 bonus on caster level
checks to overcome spell resistance with the spell. Any creature that succeeds at the saving throw
instead avoids the effect entirely. If half or more of the targets are unaffected, you become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for a number of rounds equal to the spell's level. If all of the targets
are unaffected, you instead lose the ability to cast divine spells, channel energy, and use domain
powers for 1d4+1 rounds; you can end this loss by loudly apologizing to your patron deity as a
full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 6th

**Replaces:** The divine presence ability of the Glory domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** hollow heroism

**7th:** greater hollow heroism

**9th:** overwhelming presence

If you are evil, replace the domain spells _[bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon)_ and
_[holy aura](ref/spell/holy-aura)_ with [aid](ref/spell/aid) and
[unholy aura](ref/spell/unholy-aura) respectively.

### Legend Subdomain

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** General Susumu, Gorum, Shizuru

**Bonus Feat**

**Passive:** In campaigns that use the optional [hero points](ref/rule/hero-points) system, you gain
[Hero's Fortune](ref/feat/heros-fortune) as a bonus feat. Otherwise, you gain
[Marked for Glory](ref/feat/marked-for-glory) as a bonus feat, even if you do not meet its
prerequisites.

**Replaces:** The channel boost ability of the Glory domain

**Witness the Legend (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Move:** You can extol the virtues of yourself or an ally within 60 feet that you can see. This
causes the target to shed light like a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) for 1 minute, and while the effect
lasts, you can expend a daily use of your _touch of glory_ domain ability as an immediate action to
add 1d6 to an attack, saving throw, skill check, or ability check attempted by the target; for a
roll to confirm a critical hit or save against a fear effect, you instead add 1d12.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The divine presence ability of the Glory domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** aid

**3rd:** contagious zeal

**4th:** righteous vigor

**6th:** unshakeable zeal

If you are evil, replace the domain spells _holy smite, holy sword,_ and
_[holy aura](ref/spell/holy-aura)_ with [unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight),
[unholy sword](ref/spell/unholy-sword), and [unholy aura](ref/spell/unholy-aura) respectively.
