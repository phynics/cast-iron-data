**Granted Power:** You manipulate shadows and darkness.

**Bonus Feat (Ex)**

**Passive:** You receive [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) as a bonus feat.

**Touch of Darkness (Sp)**

**Ability:** As a melee touch attack, you can cause a creature's vision to be fraught with shadows
and darkness. The creature touched treats all other creatures as if they had
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment), suffering a 20% miss chance on all attack rolls. This effect
lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Eyes of Darkness (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Ability:** Your vision is not impaired by lighting conditions, even in absolute darkness and magic
darkness.

**Usage:** 1/2 cleric level rounds/day; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Domain Spells**

1. obscuring mist
1. blindness/deafness (only to cause blindness)
1. deeper darkness
1. shadow conjuration
1. summon monster V (summons 1d3 shadows)
1. shadow walk
1. power word blind
1. greater shadow evocation
1. shades

### Loss Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 93

**Associated Deities:** Ah Pook, Ahriman, Alazhra, Apollyon, Count Ranalc, Diceid, Droskar, Groetus,
Iggeret, Lao Shu Po, Maeha, Mordiggian, Nalmungder, Nightripper, Nocticula, Nyarlathotep (Haunter of
the Dark), Rahu, Reymenda, Set, Shawnari, Shivaska, Sifkesh, Slandrais, Tresmalvos, Zon-Kuthon

**Aura of Forgetfulness (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of forgetfulness. Creatures you target in this area must
make a Will save or have no memory of any time spent inside the area. In addition, spellcasters in
the area lose one prepared spell or available spell slot per round spent in the area, starting with
1st-level spells and going up through higher-level spells. Spellcasters are allowed a save each
round to negate this loss (this save is separate from the memory loss save).

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The eyes of darkness power of the Darkness domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**5th:** enervation

**6th:** modify memory

**9th:** energy drain

### Moon Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Acavna, Ah Pook, Ashava, Camazotz, Haggakal, Jezelda, Kelinahat, Ketephys,
Nocticula (Redeemed), Tanagaar, Thoth, Tsukiyo, Yan-gant-y-tan

**Moonfire (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can shoot a blast of divine moonlight from your eyes as a ranged touch attack
against a single target within 30 feet. Moonfire deals 1d8 points of damage per 2 class levels you
have, and the target is [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 round per class level you have. Moonfire
deals 1d10 points of damage per class level you have against lycanthropes.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The eyes of darkness power of the Darkness domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** faerie fire

**4th:** moonstruck

**6th:** dream

### Night Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Abhoth, Acavna, Alazhra, Apep, Apollyon, Camazotz, Count Ranalc, Deumus,
Groetus, Iggeret, Kelinahat, Lao Shu Po, Maeha, Mordiggian, Muronna, Nephthys, Nocticula, Nocticula
(Redeemed), Nyarlathotep (Haunter of the Dark), Rahu, Tanagaar, Tsukiyo, Yan-gant-y-tan,
Yog-Sothoth, Zon-Kuthon

**Night Hunter (Su)**

**Standard:** You can blend into the shadows of the night, becoming nearly invisible. As long as you
are in an area of dim light or darkness, you are invisible (as per
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility)) to creatures without darkvision. This ability lasts for a
number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of darkness power of the Darkness domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** sleep

**6th:** nightmare

### Shadow Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Darkness, Death

**Associated Deities:** Nocticula, Norgorber, Urgathoa, Zon-Kuthon

**Whispering Shadows (Su)**

**Immediate:** You can darken the shadows around you and cause them to hiss, confounding and
distracting your enemies. You force a target within 30 feet to reroll a miss chance due to
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or a saving throw against an illusion or necromancy spell. The
target of this ability must keep the result of the second roll, even if it is worse than the
original roll.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of darkness ability of the Darkness domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** ray of enfeeblement

**4th:** phantasmal killer

**8th:** orb of the void
