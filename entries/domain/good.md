**Granted Powers:** You have pledged your life and soul to goodness and purity.

**Touch of Good (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a creature, granting a sacred bonus on attack rolls, skill checks,
ability checks, and saving throws equal to half your cleric level (minimum 1) for 1 round.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Holy Lance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can give a weapon you touch the [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) special weapon quality
for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Domain Spells**

1. protection from evil
1. align weapon (good only)
1. magic circle against evil
1. holy smite
1. dispel evil
1. blade barrier
1. holy word
1. holy aura
1. summon monster IX (good spell only)

### Agathion Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 86

**Associated Deities:** Arshea, Benorus, Bes, Bharnarol, Chaldira, Cihua Couatl, Dalenydra,
Eritrice, Halcamora, Irez, Isis, Jaidi, Jaidz, Khepri, Korada, Kurgess, Lorris, Lythertida,
Mazludeh, Neith, Ondisso, Qi Zhong, Rowdrosh, Sarenrae, Seramaydiel, Shei, Shelyn, Shelyn
(pre-Earthfall), Trudd, Uskyeria, Ylimancha

[Agathions](ref/family/agathion) are a race of neutral good outsiders.

**Protective Aura (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot protective aura. Allies in this aura receive a +2 deflection
bonus to AC and a +2 resistance bonus on all saving throws. In addition, allies in the area gain the
benefits of [protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) (although the AC bonus and saving
throw bonus do not stack with those granted by this effect).

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The holy lance power of the Good domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** shield of faith

**3rd:** tongues

**6th:** planar ally (agathions only)

**Associated Deities:** Aesocar, Andoletta, Angradd, Apsu, Arqueros, Damerrich, Easivra, Eldas,
Erastil, Falayna, Folgrit, Ghenshau, Grundinnar, Iomedae, Kelinahat, Kroina, Lymnieris, Mazludeh,

[Archons](ref/family/archon) are a race of lawful good outsiders.

**Aura of Menace (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of menace. Enemies in this aura take a -2 penalty to AC
and on attacks and saves as long as they remain inside the aura.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The holy lance power of the Good domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** divine favor

**3rd:** prayer

**6th:** planar ally (archon only)

### Azata Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Chaos, Good

**Associated Deities:** Ashava, Black Butterfly, Calistria, Cayden Cailean, Cernunnos, Chadali,
Chucaro, Desna, Elion, Findeladlara, Hathor, Hembad, Immonhiel, Jalaijatali, Keltheald, Ketephys,
Kofusachi, Lalaci, Marishi, Mazludeh, Milani, Picoperi, Pulura, Reymenda, Selket, Sinashakti, Skode,
Skrymir, Thisamet, Tolc, Valani, Yuelral

[Azatas](ref/family/azata) are a race of chaotic good outsiders.

**Elysium's Call (Su)**

**Standard:** With a touch, you can imbue creatures with the spirit of Elysium, lifting their
spirits and freeing them from bonds. The creatures touched can immediately reroll any failed saving
throws against spells and spell-like abilities of the enchantment (charm) and enchantment
(compulsion) subschools. In addition, targets receive a +2 sacred bonus on such saving throws and a
+2 sacred bonus on CMB checks to escape a grapple. Finally, targets can ignore up to 5 feet of
difficult terrain each round, as if they had the [Nimble Moves](ref/feat/nimble-moves) feat. These
bonuses last for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1), although the saving
throw reroll only applies when the creature is touched.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier rounds/day

**Replaces:** The touch of good power of the Good domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** expeditious retreat

**3rd:** fly

**6th:** planar ally (azata only)

### Friendship Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Chaldira, Elion, Findeladlara, Grundinnar, Lymnieris, Lythertida, Omrataji,
Seramaydiel, Sinashakti, Upion and Warrik

**Powerful Bond (Su)**

**Free:** You can open a link to communicate telepathically with a single ally within 60 feet. The
telepathic link is two-way and lasts for 1 minute. Unlike normal telepathy, this ability requires a
shared language.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of good power of the Good domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** shield other

**5th:** telepathic bond

### Redemption Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Iomedae, Sarenrae

**Aura of Sanctification (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Immediate:** You can emit a 30-foot-radius aura of sanctification. Within this aura, effects that
are specifically harmful to good-aligned creatures are inverted so that they no longer harm good
creatures and instead harm evil creatures. Similarly, effects that are specifically beneficial to
evil creatures instead become beneficial only to good creatures.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The holy lance ability of the Good domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** qualm

**4th:** forced repentance

**5th:** atonement
