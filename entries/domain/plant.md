**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 46

**Granted Powers:** You find solace in the green, can grow defensive thorns, and can communicate
with plants.

**Wooden Fist (Su)**

**Free:** Your hands can become as hard as wood, covered in tiny thorns. While you have wooden
fists, your unarmed strikes do not provoke attacks of opportunity, deal lethal damage, and gain a
bonus on damage rolls equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1).

**Id:** fist

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier rounds/day

**Bramble Armor (Su)**

**Free:** You can cause a host of wooden thorns to burst from your skin. While bramble armor is in
effect, any foe striking you with an unarmed strike or a melee weapon without reach takes an amount
of piercing damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level.

**Id:** armor

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

**Domain Spells**

1. entangle
1. barkskin
1. plant growth
1. command plants
1. wall of thorns
1. repel wood
1. animate plants
1. control plants
1. shambler

**Save:** domain

### Decay Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 88

**Associated Deities:** Angazhan, Cyth-V'sug, Gozreh, Lady Nanbyo, Nhimbaloth, Oaur-Ooung,
Shub-Niggurath, Tamede, The Green Mother, Treerazer, Trelmarixian, Vermilion Mother, Vorasha,
Xhamen-Dor, Zyphus

**Aura of Decay (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The bramble armor power of the Plant domain

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of decay. Living creatures in this aura (except you) take
1d6 points of damage per round as their flesh rots. They also take a cumulative -1 penalty to
Strength each round they remain in the aura. Once outside the aura, the penalty fades at the rate of
-1 per round, but it begins building again if they reenter the aura. Plant creatures take 2d6 points
of damage per round.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**3rd:** contagion

**4th:** poison

**6th:** harm

**From:** domain

### Leshy Subdomain

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 23

**Associated Deities:** Gozreh, Green Faith

**Replacement Powers:** The following granted powers replace t.

**Leshy Caller (Sp)**

**Passive:** You can spontaneously swap out any of your domain spells for a
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell of the same level or lower, to a
maximum of [summon nature's ally VII](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-7). If you do, you must use the
spell to summon one or more [leshys](ref/family/leshy). Add
[leaf leshy](ref/monster/leshy-leaf-leshy), [gourd leshy](ref/monster/leshy-gourd-leshy),
[fungus leshy](ref/monster/leshy-fungus-leshy), [seaweed leshy](ref/monster/leshy-seaweed-leshy),
and [lotus leshy](ref/monster/leshy-lotus-leshy) to your _summon nature's ally I, II, III, IV,_ and
_V_ lists, respectively.

**Replaces:** The wooden fist power of the Plant domain

**Special:** You count as a plant creature for the purpose of creating leshys.

**Standard:** You can grant a single leshy within 30 feet a +4 sacred bonus on its next attack roll
before the start of your next turn.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** tree shape

**5th:** commune with nature

**From:** domain

### Thorns Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 26

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Shub-Niggurath, The Green Mother, Zyphus

**Cruel Thicket (Su)**

**Replaces:** The wooden fist power of the Plant domain

**Standard:** You can cause the ground in a 5-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) centered on you to
sprout twisting, thorny vines. Any creature moving through it must travel at half speed or take
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage equal to half your cleric level; if you activate this ability in an
area with numerous plants (grass, weeds, trees, etc.), the area also becomes
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). These effects last for 1 minute, after which
the vines crumble to dust. Creatures able to move through natural undergrowth unhindered ignore the
effects of this ability.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** thorn javelin

**3rd:** thorny entanglement

**4th:** thorn body

**From:** domain
