**Granted Powers:** You are sinister and cruel, and have wholly pledged your soul to the cause of
evil.

**Touch of Evil (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can cause a creature to become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) as a melee touch
attack. Creatures sickened by your touch count as good for the purposes of spells with the evil
descriptor. This ability lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Scythe of Evil (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can give a weapon touched the [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) special weapon
quality for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Domain Spells**

1. protection from good
1. align weapon (evil only)
1. magic circle against good
1. unholy blight
1. dispel good
1. create undead
1. blasphemy
1. unholy aura
1. summon monster IX (evil spell only)

### Cannibalism Subdomain

**Sources:** Champions of Corruption pg 18

**Associated Deities:** Lamashtu, Rovagug, Urgathoa

**Consume the Enemy (Su)**

**Full-round:** As an [evil](ref/spelldef/evil) act, you can bite a helpless living or freshly
killed creature, dealing 1d3 points of damage. By consuming the creature's flesh, you gain a profane
bonus on saving throw DCs for all spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities you use
against creatures of the same type (and subtype, if humanoid or outsider) as the cannibalized
creature. The profane bonus is equal to 1 + [one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
cleric level. This effect lasts for a number of minutes equal to 1/2 your cleric level or until you
use this ability against a different creature.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of evil ability of the Evil domain

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** Yes

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** magic fang

**2nd:** enemy's heart

**3rd:** greater magic fang

### Corruption Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Asmodeus, Dahak, General Susumu, Gyronna, Lamashtu, Rovagug, Urgathoa,
Velgaas, Zipacna

**Spark Malfeasance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Ability:** You can awaken the sinful desires of a target within 30 feet with a ranged touch
attack. The target suffers extreme guilt and is [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for a number of rounds
equal to 1/2 your cleric level. To end the effect earlier, the target can willingly commit an evil
act (if it is of good alignment) or attempt a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your cleric level + your
Wisdom modifier) at the beginning of its turn each round. Creatures that succeed at their saving
throws are immune to this ability for 24 hours.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The scythe of evil ability of the Evil domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** suggestion

**6th:** greater bestow curse

**Whispering Evil (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can whisper a hypnotizing litany of empty promises. Each enemy within a 30-foot
[emanation](ref/misc/emanation) that can hear you must succeed on a Will saving throw or become
[fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) for as long as you continue the litany.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Replaces:** The scythe of evil power of the Evil domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** cause fear

**3rd:** vampiric touch

**6th:** planar binding (daemons only)

### Demodand Subdomain

**Sources:** Champions of Corruption pg 19

**Associated Domains:** Chaos, Evil

**Associated Deities:** Rovagug, Yaezhing

**Faith-Stealing Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** You can make a single melee attack using your highest base attack bonus against a
creature capable of casting divine spells. If you damage the creature, it must succeed at a Will
save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier) or be unable to cast any divine spells
for 1 round. If you do not damage your target with the attack, this ability is expended with no
effect.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of evil ability of the Evil domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** align weapon (chaos or evil only)

**6th:** corrosive consumption

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

### Plague Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Death, Evil

**Associated Deities:** Apollyon, Cyth-V'sug, Ghlaunder, Urgathoa

**Touch of Virulence (Su)**

**Standard:** You can touch a diseased creature and exacerbate its condition. If it fails a
Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier), the creature takes damage
as though it had failed its Fortitude save against the disease and any remaining onset time for the
disease ends.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 6 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The touch of evil power of the Evil domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** ray of sickening

**3rd:** contagion

**6th:** plague storm

**8th:** horrid wilting

### Sahkil Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Ah Pook, Ananshea, Chamiaholom, Charg, Dachzerul, Hataam, Iggeret, Nameless,
Ozranvial, Shawnari, Velgaas, Vermilion Mother, Xiquiripat, Zipacna

**Touch of Paranoia (Su)**

**Ability:** You can imbue a target with great suspicion and doubt as a melee touch attack. A
creature affected by your touch does not have allies and is not considered to be an ally to any
other creature, including other creatures affected by this effect. The affected creature cannot move
freely through its allies' spaces, [flank](ref/rule/flanking) creatures with them, cooperate with
them using teamwork feats, or give or receive benefits from the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another)
action or any spells or effect that affects only allies. This ability lasts for a number of rounds
equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The touch of evil power of the Evil domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** haunting mists

**4th:** they know

**6th:** phobia
