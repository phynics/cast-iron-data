**Granted Powers:** You can cause the living to bleed at a touch, and find comfort in the presence
of the dead.

**Bleeding Touch (Sp)**

**Ability:** As a melee touch attack, you can cause a living creature to take 1d6 points of damage
per round. This effect persists for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum 1) or
until stopped with a DC 15 Heal check or any spell or effect that heals damage.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Death's Embrace (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You heal damage instead of taking damage from channeled negative energy. If the
channeled negative energy targets undead, you heal hit points just like undead in the area.

**Death's Kiss (Su)**

**Ability:** You can cause a creature to take on some of the traits of the undead with a melee touch
attack. Touched creatures are treated as undead for the purposes of effects that heal or cause
damage based on positive and negative energy. This effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2
your cleric level (minimum 1). It does not apply to the [Turn Undead](ref/feat/turn-undead) or
[Command Undead](ref/feat/command-undead) feats.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The bleeding touch power of the Death domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** ghoul touch

**4th:** enervation

**9th:** energy drain

### Variant Domain Powers

A cleric (or any other character capable of selecting a domain) who worships the deity in question
can choose to gain the variant domain power upon first gaining the domain in question, but the
choice to replace the domain power must be made immediately upon gaining access to that domain and
cannot be changed thereafter.

#### Urgathoa

A risen spirit is akin to a plant that must be tended. Such are the teachings of Urgathoa, the
goddess who is believed to be the first undead creature. Unlike many of her deific counterparts,
Urgathoa champions the shepherding and protection of haunts and possessing spirits, even to the
danger and detriment of her own followers. The faithful of the Pallid Princess believe that by
prolonging the torment and tortured existence of a spirit, they create something akin to a beautiful
piece of artwork. In a similar manner, servants of Urgathoa view the possession of a mortal body by
a deceased spirit as a blessing.

**Necrotic Tending (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch an undead creature or area affected by a haunt, restoring an amount of
hit points equal to 1d8 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level. This ability
has no effect on other creatures.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The bleeding touch domain power of the Death domain
