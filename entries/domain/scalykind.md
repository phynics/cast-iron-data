**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 181, Inner Sea World Guide pg 216, Pathfinder \#37: Souls for
Smuggler's Shiv pg 69, Gods and Magic pg 51

**Granted Powers:** You are a true lord of reptiles, and your gaze can drive weak creatures into
unconsciousness.

**Venomous Stare (Sp)**

**Id:** stare

**Standard:** You can activate a [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack with a 30-foot range that can target a
single creature within range. The target must make a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your cleric level +
your Wisdom modifier). Those that fail take an amount of nonlethal damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level and are
[fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) until the beginning of your next turn. This is a mind-affecting
effect.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Serpent Companion (Ex)**

**Id:** comp

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain the service of an [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion). Your
effective druid level for this animal companion is equal to your cleric level - 2. You may choose
either a [viper](ref/companion/snake-viper) or a
[constrictor snake](ref/companion/snake-constrictor) as your companion.

**Domain Spells**

1. magic fang
1. animal trance
1. greater magic fang
1. poison
1. animal growth (reptiles only)
1. eyebite
1. creeping doom (takes the form of Diminutive-sized reptiles)
1. animal shapes (reptiles only)
1. shapechange

**Save:** domain

### Dragon Subdomain

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 180, Inner Sea Gods pg 224, Paizo Blog - Golarion Day Subdomains
pg 1, Champions of Purity pg 20

**Associated Deities:** Apep, Apsu, Ardad Lili, Dahak, Draghignazzo, Ragadahn, Yig

**Dragonbreath (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** The serpent companion ability of the Scalykind domain

**Standard:** You can use a breath weapon once per day. When you gain this ability, choose what kind
of damage your breath weapon deals: acid, cold, electricity, or fire. Once you make this choice, you
can't change it later. Your breath weapon fills a 15-foot cone and deals **X**d6 points of damage,
where **X** is equal to 1 + [one-half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your cleric level. A
creature hit by your dragonbreath attack can attempt a Reflex save (DC = 10 + half your class
level + your Wisdom modifier) to take half damage.

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**3rd:** draconic reservoir

**4th:** dragon's breath

**6th:** form of the dragon I

**From:** domain

### Saurian Subdomain

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 181, Paizo Blog - Golarion Day Subdomains pg 1, Inner Sea Gods pg
226

**Associated Deities:** Sobek, Ydersius, Zevgavizeb

**Dinosaur Companion (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain the service of a loyal animal companion. Your effective druid level for the
purpose of this animal companion is equal to your class level - 2. You must choose a dinosaur as
your companion.

**Replaces:** The serpent companion ability of the Scalykind domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** summon nature's ally IV ([deinonychus](ref/monster/dinosaur-deinonychus) or
[pteranodon](ref/monster/dinosaur-pteranodon) only)

**5th:** beast shape III

**7th:** summon nature's ally VII ([brachiosaurus](ref/monster/dinosaur-brachiosaurus) or
[tyrannosaurus](ref/monster/dinosaur-tyrannosaurus) only)

**From:** domain

### Venom Subdomain

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 182, Inner Sea Gods pg 227

**Associated Deities:** Isph-Aun-Vuln, Mazmezz, Nergal, Ragadahn, Rytara, Scarmiglione, Vois,
Vorasha, Yig

**Venomous Saliva (Su)**

**Replaces:** The venomous stare power of the Scalykind domain

**Swift:** Just as certain serpents can spray poison from their fangs, you can spit a gout of
life-stealing venom onto a weapon you are wielding. The poison (see below) lasts for 1 minute or
until you make an attack with that weapon that injures a foe.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Life-Stealing Venom**

**Type:** Poison (injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your class level + your Wis modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 3 rounds

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** pernicious poison

**6th:** cloudkill

**From:** domain
