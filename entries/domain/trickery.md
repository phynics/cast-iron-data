**Granted Powers:** You are a master of illusions and deceptions.

**Class Skills (Ex)**

**Passive:** Bluff, Disguise, and Stealth are class skills.

**Copycat (Sp)**

**Move:** You can create an illusory double of yourself. This double functions as a single
[mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image) and lasts for a number of rounds equal to your cleric level,
or until the illusory duplicate is dispelled or destroyed. You can have no more than one copycat at
a time. This ability does not stack with the _[mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image)_ spell.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Master's Illusion (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can create an illusion that hides the appearance of yourself and any number of
allies within 30 feet for 1 round per cleric level. This ability otherwise functions like the spell
[veil](ref/spell/veil). The save DC to disbelieve this effect is equal to 10 + 1/2 your cleric
level + your Wisdom modifier. The rounds do not need to be consecutive.

**Domain Spells**

1. disguise self
1. invisibility
1. nondetection
1. confusion
1. false vision
1. mislead
1. screen
1. mass invisibility
1. time stop

### Ambush Subdomain

**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 13

**Associated Deities:** Kro'akoth

**Prerequisites:** Only creatures with the [Kobold Faith](ref/trait/kobold-faith) trait can take
this subdomain.

**Chameleon Aspect (Su)**

**Swift:** You can subtly alter the color of your scales, blending them with the surrounding
environment. While under the effect of this power, you gain [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from
creatures more than 10 feet away (attacks have a 20% miss chance), and total concealment from
creatures more than 40 feet away (attacks have a 50% miss chance).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier rounds/day

**Replaces:** The copycat power of the Trickery domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** alarm

**5th:** illusory wall

**6th:** veil

**Flavor:** In the blink of an eye, you can appear somewhere else.

**Immediate:** After you are missed by a melee attack, you can teleport up to 10 feet to a space
that you can see. This space must be inside the reach of the creature that attacked you.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The copycat power of the Trickery domain

### Espionage Subdomain

**Associated Domains:** Knowledge, Trickery

**Associated Deities:** Droskar, Erecura, Kelinahat, Norgorber

**Deep Cover (Su)**

**Ability:** When you prepare your spells, you can also choose one cover identity - whether that of
a real person or a fictitious one. You can assume the disguise of your cover identity in half the
normal amount of time.

**Immediate:** When targeted by a spell or effect that would compromise your chosen disguise (such
as [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil), [detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts), or
[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth)), you can force the creature using the ability to attempt a
Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier). If the creature fails, it gains
false information in keeping with your cover identity. You can use this false information ability a
number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Replaces:** The copycat power of the Trickery domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** anonymous interaction

**4th:** zone of silence

**8th:** mind blank

### Greed Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Asmodeus, Dahak, Lao Shu Po, Norgorber

**More for Me (Su)**

**Immediate:** When another character within 30 feet casts a spell with a range greater than touch,
you can attempt a caster level check (DC = 15 + the spell's level). If you succeed at the check, you
receive the spell's benefit instead of one of the spell's intended targets (chosen by you). If you
are already a target of the spell, you are affected as though you were targeted by the same spell
twice (which may or may not benefit you; remember that bonuses from the same source do not stack).
This ability does not grant you knowledge of the spell being cast.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The copycat ability of the Trickery domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** masterwork transformation

**6th:** guards and wards

**8th:** create demiplane

### Variant Domain Powers

A cleric (or any other character capable of selecting a domain) who worships the deity in question
can choose to gain the variant domain power upon first gaining the domain in question, but the
choice to replace the domain power must be made immediately upon gaining access to that domain and
cannot be changed thereafter.

#### Asmodeus

While the church of Asmodeus periodically endorses the use of possession for certain reasons, it is
usually vehemently against unsanctioned possession and the creation of uncontrolled undead forces.
Asmodeans have a particular hatred for celestials who partake in acts of possession. Such outsiders
are hunted down, and the Church of Asmodeus may offer its exorcising agents' services pro bono for
the chance to strike at a particularly hated celestial. The eventual consequence of such
arrangements - often left unsaid - is that Asmodeans track those they have helped in this way, and
often return to force those individuals to host a [devil](ref/family/devil) for a period of
predetermined length as recompense for their prior service.

**Deceptive Reprieve (Sp)**

**Standard:** When you touch a creature under direct mental control (such as that granted via
[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person), but not via [charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)) or
the victim of [possession](ref/rule/possession), that creature can attempt a new Will save against
the control or possession effect with a +2 bonus. If the creature succeeds, the effect or possession
is temporarily halted, as per [protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil), for a number
of rounds equal to 1/2 your character level. While this ability is active, the target takes a -2
penalty on saves against new mind-affecting effects and new attempts at possession.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The copycat power of the Trickery domain
