**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 46

**Granted Powers:** Your faith is your greatest source of protection, and you can use that faith to
defend others.

**Resistance**

**Id:** res

**Passive:** You receive a resistance bonus on saving throws equal to 1 +
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level.

**Resistant Touch (Sp)**

**Id:** touch

**Standard:** You can touch an ally to grant him your resistance bonus for 1 minute. When you use
this ability, you lose your resistance bonus granted by the Protection domain for 1 minute.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Aura of Protection (Su)**

**Id:** aura-def-protect

**At 14th Level:** The elemental resistance increases to 10.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of protection. You and your allies within this aura gain a
+1 deflection bonus to AC and [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 against all elements (acid, cold,
electricity, fire, and sonic).

**Usage:** 1 time/day per cleric level

At 12th level: The deflection bonus +2.

At 16th level: The deflection bonus +3.

At 20th level: The deflection bonus +4.

**Domain Spells**

1. sanctuary
1. shield other
1. protection from energy
1. spell immunity
1. [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance)
1. antimagic field
1. repulsion
1. mind blank
1. prismatic sphere

**Save:** domain

**Deflection Aura (Su)**

**Replaces:** The resistant touch power of the Protection domain

**Standard:** Once each day, you can emit a 20-foot aura for a number of rounds equal to your cleric
level. Allies within the aura gain a +2 deflection bonus to AC and combat maneuver defense.

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** shield

**2nd:** barkskin

**7th:** deflection

**From:** domain

### Fortifications Subdomain

**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 18

**Associated Deities:** Abadar, Arqueros, Torag

**Sheltering Walls (Sp)**

**Replaces:** The resistant touch power of the Protection domain

**Standard:** You can touch an ally to summon a floating blockade of stone, granting the ally the
benefits of partial [cover](ref/rule/cover) for a number of rounds equal to 1 +
[one-sixth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your cleric level. This ability does not grant the
ally enough cover to attempt a Stealth check or avoid provoking attacks of opportunity. If the ally
benefits from any other form of cover against an attack, the bonuses to AC and on Reflex saving
throws increase by 1.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** hold portal

**5th:** wall of stone

**9th:** prismatic wall

**From:** domain

### Purity Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 94

**Associated Deities:** Aesocar, Andoletta, Anubis, Chadali, Jaidz, Lymnieris, Maat, Milani,
Nephthys, Ondisso, Qi Zhong, Selket, Shelyn, Shelyn (pre-Earthfall), Soralyon, Wadjet, Winlas

**Purifying Touch (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The aura of protection power of the Protection domain

**Standard:** You can touch a willing creature with divine power, giving it a saving throw against
each effect currently affecting it, using the original saving throw DC of the effect. Each
successful saving throw ends the related effect. The creature can choose not to make a save against
an effect.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 6 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** protection from chaos/evil/good/law

**3rd:** remove blindness/deafness

**5th:** atonement

**From:** domain

### Solitude Subdomain

**Sources:** Champions of Balance pg 17

**Associated Deities:** Nethys, Yamatsumi

**Taboo (Su)**

**Immediate:** When a creature touches you or strikes you with a melee attack, you can activate this
power. That creature takes a -1 penalty on saving throws for 1 minute. This penalty increases by 1
for every 5 levels you possess. When you use this ability, you lose your resistance bonus granted by
the Protection domain for 1 minute.

**Replaces:** The resistant touch ability of the Protection domain

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** silence

**4th:** detect scrying

**5th:** mirage arcana

**9th:** greater create demiplane

**From:** domain
