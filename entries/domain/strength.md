**Granted Powers:** In strength and brawn there is truth - your faith gives you incredible might and
power.

**Strength Surge (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a creature to give it great strength. For 1 round, the target gains an
enhancement bonus equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1) to melee attacks, combat maneuver
checks that rely on Strength, Strength-based skills, and Strength checks.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Might of the Gods (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can add your cleric level as an enhancement bonus to your Strength score. This
bonus only applies on Strength checks and Strength-based skill checks.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Domain Spells**

1. enlarge person
1. bull's strength
1. magic vestment
1. spell immunity
1. righteous might
1. stoneskin
1. grasping hand
1. clenched fist
1. crushing hand

### Competition Subdomain

**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 18

**Associated Deities:** Cayden Cailean, Irori, Kurgess, Marishi, Trudd

**Athletic Exploit (Su)**

**Swift:** You gain the ability to overcome physical obstacles with ease. Until the beginning of
your next turn, you halve your armor check penalty when attempting Acrobatics, Climb, Escape Artist,
and Swim skill checks, and you gain a bonus equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1) on such
skill checks.

**Replaces:** The strength surge power of the Strength domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** jump

**3rd:** greater animal aspect

**6th:** greater heroism

**Ferocious Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you make a melee attack, you can designate that attack as a _ferocious
strike._ If the attack hits, it deals additional damage equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replaces:** The strength surge power of the Strength domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**3rd:** rage

**6th:** mass bull's strength

### Fist Subdomain

**Associated Deities:** Gorum, Irori, Yamatsumi

**Wooden Fist (Su)**

**Free:** Your hands can become as hard as wood, covered in tiny thorns. While you have wooden
fists, your unarmed strikes do not provoke attacks of opportunity, deal lethal damage, and gain a
bonus on damage rolls equal to 1/2 your cleric level (minimum +1).

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier rounds/day

**Replaces:** The strength surge ability of the Strength domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** true strike

**3rd:** greater magic fang

**4th:** force punch

**Bestow Resolve (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can bless creatures with the boldness of your deity. You can bestow a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to your level + your Wisdom modifier to
all allies within 20 feet. The temporary hit points remain for 1 minute.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replaces:** The might of the gods power of the Strength domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** bless

**6th:** heroes' feast

### Self-Realization Subdomain

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Domains:** Liberation, Strength

**Associated Deities:** Arshea, Falayna, Haagenti, Irori, Kurgess, Scal, Shei, Urgathoa

**Perfected Form (Su)**

**Flavor:** You maintain an unshakable sense of who you are, and no force can compel you to be
anything else.

**Passive:** You gain a +1 sacred (if your patron is good or neutral) or profane (if your patron is
evil) bonus on saving throws against polymorph, petrification, and transmutation effects. This bonus
increases by 1 for every 5 cleric levels you have (maximum +5).

**Immediate:** Once per day when you succeed at a saving throw against such an effect, you can gain
a surge of self-confidence that grants you a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to your cleric level and a +2 morale
bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws; both effects last for 1 minute.

**Replaces:** The strength surge ability of the Strength domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** paragon surge (always matches your actual race)

**6th:** primal scream

**7th:** transformation
