**Granted Powers:** You are an explorer and find enlightenment in the simple joy of travel, be it by
foot or conveyance or magic.

**Speedy (Ex)**

**Id:** speed

**Passive:** Increase your base speed by 10 feet.

**Agile Feet (Su)**

**Free:** You can gain increased mobility for 1 round. For the next round, you ignore all
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) and do not take any penalties for moving through
it.

**Id:** feet

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Dimensional Hop (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Move:** You can teleport. You must have line of sight to your destination to use this ability. You
can bring other willing creatures with you, but you must expend an equal amount of distance for each
creature brought.

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Usage:** You can travel up to 10 feet per cleric level per day. This teleportation must be used in
5-foot increments.

**Usage:** You can travel up to 10 feet per cleric level per day. This teleportation must be used in
5-foot increments.

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 90

**Associated Deities:** Aegirran, Apsu, Cayden Cailean, Chimon, Count Ranalc, Desna, Dranngvit,
Eligos, Elion, Folca, Hanspur, Hathor, Jaidz, Kelizandri, Keltheald, Kofusachi, Morrobahn, Ng,

Wadjet, Yog-Sothoth, Zohls

**Door Sight (Su)**

**Ability:** You can lay your hand upon any surface and see what is on the other side, as if using
[clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance). Using this power takes 1 minute, during which
time you must be touching the surface you want to see through. You can keep looking for as long as
10 minutes with each use of this power, but must touch the surface and take no other action the
entire time. The surface cannot be thicker than 6 inches plus 1 inch per cleric level you possess.

**Replaces:** The agile feet power of the Travel domain

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** expeditious retreat

**4th:** locate creature

**9th:** world wave

**From:** domain

### Portal Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 25

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Deities:** Abadar, Alseta, Barbatos, Onos, Yog-Sothoth

**Sacred Threshold (Su)**

**Replaces:** The base speed increase power of the Travel domain

**Standard:** You can bless a door, a window, or another portal with a touch. For 1 minute, you can
increase or decrease the DC of any check to unlock or force open the portal by an amount equal to
half your cleric level. In addition, your first ally to pass through the portal during this time
gains an amount of [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to 1d4 + 1 for every
2 cleric levels you have. These temporary hit points last for 1 minute.

**Usage:** Wis3 modifier times/day

**Travel as One (Su)**

**Passive:** When you cast a spell with the teleportation descriptor, treat your caster level as 3
higher for the purpose of determining how many additional creatures you can bring with you.

In addition, you and any creatures you teleport gain a number of temporary hit points equal to your
level that disappear after you reach your destination; these offset any damage you might take from
teleportation mishaps or attempting to teleport into a solid body.

**Replaces:** The agile feet power of the Travel domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** open and shut

**2nd:** knock

**3rd:** urban step

**9th:** teleportation circle

**From:** domain
