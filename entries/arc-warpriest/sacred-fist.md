**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 130\
Unlike many warpriests, sacred fists leave behind armor and shield and instead rely on their fists
and whatever protection their deity bestows on them.

**Gained class skills:** acro, ea, kh, per, stl

**Lost class skills:** ha, ke, sur

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** Sacred fists are proficient with the [club](ref/eq-weapon/club),
[light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow), [heavy crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-crossbow),
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger), [handaxe](ref/eq-weapon/handaxe), [javelin](ref/eq-weapon/javelin),
[kama](ref/eq-weapon/kama), [nunchaku](ref/eq-weapon/nunchaku),
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), [sai](ref/eq-weapon/sai),
[shortspear](ref/eq-weapon/shortspear), [short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword),
[shuriken](ref/eq-weapon/shuriken), [siangham](ref/eq-weapon/siangham),
[sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling), and [spear](ref/eq-weapon/spear). Sacred fists are not proficient with
any armor or shields.

**Replaces:** Weapon and armor proficiency

**Special:** When wearing armor, using a shield, or carrying a medium or heavy load, a sacred fist
loses his _AC bonus_ and _flurry of blows._

**AC Bonus (Su)**

**Flavor:** A deity protects her sacred fist as long as he is unarmored and unencumbered.

**At 4th Level:** A sacred fist gains a dodge bonus to AC and CMD. This bonus is equal to one-fourth
of his warpriest level.

**Passive:** A sacred fist adds his Wisdom modifier (minimum 0) to his AC and his CMD.

**Special:** These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks or when the sacred fist is
flat-footed. He loses these bonuses when he is immobilized or helpless, when he wears any armor,
when he carries a shield, or when he carries a medium or heavy load.

This counts as the [monk](ref/class/monk) ability of the same name, and the sacred fist's warpriest
levels stack with monk levels for determining the benefits.

**Flurry of Blows (Ex)**

**Ability:** A sacred fist can make a flurry of blows attack as a full-attack action. This ability
works like the monk ability of the same name, except the sacred fist's attack bonus from warpriest
levels does not count as his warpriest level.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Sacred weapon

**Flurry of Blows (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Flavor:** Text from the monk class ability.

**At 15th Level:** You can make three additional attacks when you use flurry of blows, as if using
[Greater Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/greater-two-weapon-fighting) (even if you do not meet the
prerequisites for the feat).

**At 8th Level:** You can make two additional attacks when you use flurry of blows, as if using
[Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/improved-two-weapon-fighting) (even if you do not meet the
prerequisites for the feat).

You can make a flurry of blows as a full-attack action. When doing so you may make one additional
attack using any combination of unarmed strikes or attacks with a special monk weapon (kama,
nunchaku, quarterstaff, sai, shuriken, and siangham) as if using the
[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) feat (even if you do not meet the prerequisites
for the feat). For the purpose of these attacks, your base attack bonus from your monk class levels
is equal to your monk level. For all other purposes, such as qualifying for a feat or a prestige
class, you use your normal base attack bonus.

You apply your full Strength bonus to your damage rolls for all successful attacks made with flurry
of blows, whether the attacks are made with an off-hand or with a weapon wielded in both hands. You
may substitute [disarm](ref/rule/disarm), [sunder](ref/rule/sunder), and [trip](ref/rule/trip)
combat maneuvers for unarmed attacks as part of a flurry of blows. You cannot use any weapon other
than an unarmed strike or a special monk weapon as part of a flurry of blows. You cannot use natural
weapons (if you have any) as part of a flurry of blows, nor can you make natural attacks in addition
to your flurry of blows attacks.

**Unarmed Strike**

**Ability:** A sacred fist gains [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as a
bonus feat. He uses his warpriest levels as monk levels for determining the amount of damage dealt
with an unarmed strike.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Focus weapon

**Unarmed Strike**

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the monk class.

**At 1st Level:** Your unarmed strike deals 1d4 (Small), 1d6 (Medium), or 1d8 (Large) damage.

**At 12th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d10 (Small), 2d6 (Medium), or 3d6 (Large) damage.

**At 16th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d6 (Small), 2d8 (Medium), or 3d8 (Large) damage.

**At 20th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d8 (Small), 2d10 (Medium), or 4d8 (Large) damage.

**At 4th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium), or 2d6 (Large) damage.

**At 8th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d8 (Small), 1d10 (Medium), or 2d8 (Large) damage.

**Info:** The amount of damage you deal depends on your size.

**Blessed Fortitude (Su)**

**Flavor:** A sacred fist can avoid even magical and unusual attacks with help from his deity.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** If he succeeds at a Fortitude saving throw against an attack that has a reduced effect
on a successful save, he instead avoids the effect entirely.

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 3rd level

**Special:** A [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) sacred fist does not gain the benefit of the blessed
fortitude ability.

**Bonus Style Feat**

**Ability:** The sacred fist gains a style feat as a bonus feat. The sacred fist must meet the style
feat's prerequisites. He uses his warpriest levels as monk levels for the purposes of meeting the
feat's prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Repeat:** A sacred fist gains either another style feat or a feat that requires a style feat as a
prerequisite., 12, 6

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 6th, 12th, and 18th levels

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** The sacred fist gains a ki pool. This functions as the monk class feature, using the
sacred fist's level - 3 as his monk level when determining the number of points in his pool and
bonuses granted to his unarmed strike.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Sacred armor

**Swift:** Additionally, the sacred fist can spend 1 point from his ki pool to grant himself a +1
insight bonus to his AC for 1 minute. (This is in addition the normal ki ability to gain a dodge
bonus to AC.) This insight bonus increases by 1 for every 3 levels above 7th (to a maximum of +5 at
19th level).

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a pool of ki points. The number of points in your ki pool is equal to 1/2 your
monk level + your Wisdom modifier.

**Flavor:** Text from the monk class ability.

**At 10th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are also treated as lawful weapons for the purpose of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 16th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are treated as [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) weapons
for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and bypassing
[hardness](ref/rule/breaking-things).

**At 7th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are also treated as cold iron and silver for the purpose of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Passive:** As long as you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, you can make a _ki strike,_ which
allows your unarmed attacks to be treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Special:** The ki pool is replenished each morning after 8 hours of rest or meditation; these
hours do not need to be consecutive.

**Swift:** By spending 1 point from your ki pool, you can make one additional unarmed strike at your
highest attack bonus when making a flurry of blows attack. In addition, you can spend 1 point to
increase your speed by 20 feet for 1 round. Finally, your can spend 1 point from your _ki_ pool to
give yourself a +4 dodge bonus to AC for 1 round.

**Miraculous Fortitude (Su)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** The sacred fist's _blessed fortitude_ ability improves. He still takes no damage or
negative effect when he succeeds at a Fortitude save, but now when he fails a Fortitude saving throw
against a spell or effect that deals damage (including
[ability damage and drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain)), he takes only half the amount of damage.

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 9th level

**Special:** A [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) sacred fist does not gain the benefit of _miraculous
fortitude._
