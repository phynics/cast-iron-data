While many warpriests focus on threats to the body, the disenchanter focuses on dangers to the mind
and the soul. Using the power of his patron, the disenchanter seeks to keep the powers of magic in
check.

**Bonus Feats (Ex)**

**Alters:** Bonus feats

**At 12th Level:** He adds the following feats to this list:
[Destructive Dispel](ref/feat/destructive-dispel),
[Dispelling Critical](ref/feat/dispelling-critical), [Parry Spell](ref/feat/parry-spell), and
[Ray Shield](ref/feat/ray-shield).

**At 6th Level:** He adds the following feats to this list:
[Dispel Synergy](ref/feat/dispel-synergy),
[Improved Great Fortitude](ref/feat/improved-great-fortitude),
[Improved Iron Will](ref/feat/improved-iron-will), and
[Improved Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/improved-lightning-reflexes).

**Info:** Whenever a disenchanter gains a bonus feat, he must choose from the following list:
[Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude),
[Improved Counterspell](ref/feat/improved-counterspell), [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will),
[Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/lightning-reflexes), [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Spellcraft),
or [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (abjuration).

**Special:** He need not meet the prerequisites for these feats.

**Mystic Interference (Su)**

**Ability:** A disenchanter can channel a [burst](ref/misc/burst) of pure abjuration magic to grant
protection to himself and all allies with 30 feet. Affected creatures receive a bonus on saving
throws against spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) for a number of
rounds equal to his warpriest level. The bonus is equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his warpriest level. This is a sacred bonus if the
warpriest is good-aligned or able to spontaneously cast _cure_ spells, and a profane bonus if the
warpriest is evil-aligned or able to spontaneously cast _inflict_ spells. Using this ability
consumes two uses of his _fervor_ ability.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Channel energy

**Banish Enchantment (Su)**

**Flavor:** A disenchanter learns to focus his mystic interference.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 6th level

**Standard:** He can consume two uses of his _fervor_ ability to target a single creature within 30
feet with a targeted [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic).
