**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 97\
When a warpriest devotes himself to a god of the natural world, he is sometimes blessed with
supernatural powers that allow him to evoke animalistic power and fury.

**Feral Blessing**

**Alters:** Blessings

**Info:** A feral champion must take the [Animal](ref/blessing/animal) blessing and does not gain a
second blessing.

**Sacred Claws (Su)**

**Ability:** Rather than empowering a physical weapon, a feral champion grows claws as primary
[natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks) on each hand. These claws deal damage as a warpriest's
_sacred weapon_ and can be enhanced as such.

**Alters:** Sacred weapon

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Ability:** A feral champion gains _wild shape,_ as the [druid](ref/class/druid) ability of the
same name, and treats his warpriest level - 3 as his effective druid level for the purposes of this
ability. However, a feral champion does not gain the ability to take on elemental or plant forms
with wild shape.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Sacred armor

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 warpriest levels beyond 7th

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**Flavor:** Text from the druid class ability.

**At 6th Level:** You can _wild shape_ as [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2), and you can now
change into a Large or Tiny animal.

**At 8th Level:** You can _wild shape_ as [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3), and you can
now change into a Huge or Diminutive animal.

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Special:** You lose your ability to speak while in animal form because you are limited to the
sounds that a normal, untrained animal can make, but you can communicate normally with other animals
of the same general grouping as your new form. (The normal sound a wild parrot makes is a squawk, so
changing to this form does not permit speech.)

**Standard:** You gain the ability to turn yourself into any Small or Medium animal and back again.
Your options for new forms include all creatures with the animal type. This ability functions like
the [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1) spell, except as noted here. The effect lasts for 1
hour per druid level, or until you change back. The form chosen must be that of an animal with which
you are familiar.
