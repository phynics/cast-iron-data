Divine champions are unswervingly devoted to their causes, and specialize in bringing battle to the
enemies of their faiths. They are similar to [divine paragons](ref/arc-cleric/divine-paragon) in
many ways, but tend to focus more on their deity's combat prowess rather than seeking to become a
physical manifestation of the deity's will.

**Devotion**

**Info:** A divine champion's power comes from his close connection to his deity. He must worship a
deity, and must maintain the same alignment as that deity.

**Obedient Champion**

**Ability:** The divine champion gains [Deific Obedience](ref/feat/deific-obedience) as a bonus
feat, even if he doesn't meet the feat's prerequisites. When a divine champion gains boons from this
feat, he gains the appropriate **sentinel** boon offered by his deity, rather than gaining the
**exalted** boon.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 3rd level

**Know the Infidel (Ex)**

**Ability:** The divine champion gains a +2 bonus on Bluff, Intimidate, Knowledge, Perception, and
Sense Motive checks against followers of the enemy faith, as well as a +2 bonus on weapon attack and
damage rolls made against them. Additionally, he can attempt Knowledge skill checks relating to the
deity and its church untrained, even if the DC is higher than 10.

**Choice:** He must choose a deity whose alignment is opposed to his own deity's alignment in at
least one way (good versus evil or law versus chaos); preferably, he should choose a deity whose
alignment diametrically opposes that of his own deity. If the divine champion worships a deity whose
alignment is neutral, he must instead select a deity whose alignment is chaotic evil, chaotic good,
lawful evil, or lawful good.

The choice of this enemy deity is subject to GM approval. At the GM's discretion, the divine
champion may be allowed to choose a philosophy or religious organization that's not affiliated with
a deity, such as the [Green Faith](ref/faith/green-faith), the
[Pure Legion](ref/trait/pure-legion-recruit), or the [Whispering Way](ref/rule/f-gravepath), but
only if that philosophy or religious organization is an enemy of the divine champion's deity.

**Flavor:** The divine champion becomes an expert at combating enemies of his faith.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Repeat:** The divine champion can select an additional deity to oppose. In addition, the bonus
against the followers of any one deity he selected previously (including the one just selected, if
so desired) increases by +2., 12, 6

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 6th, 12th, and 18th levels

**Fervent Boon (Sp)**

**Ability:** By spending _fervor,_ he can cast one of the
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) included in his deity's first **sentinel**
boon.

By expending one use of _fervor,_ he can cast the [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
that the boon normally grants three uses of per day; by expending two uses of _fervor_ he can cast
the spell-like ability that the boon normally grants twice per day; and by expending three uses of
_fervor,_ he can cast the spell-like ability that the boon normally grants once per day. These uses
don't count against any uses granted by the boon.

**Flavor:** A divine champion can call upon his deity for minor boons.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 9th level
