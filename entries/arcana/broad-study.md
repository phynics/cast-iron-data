**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 11

**Broad Study (Ex):**

**Ability:** The magus can use his _spellstrike_ and _spell combat_ abilities while casting or using
spells from the spell list of that class.

**Choice:** The magus selects another one of his spellcasting classes.

**Prereq:** 6th-level magus, levels in at least one other spellcasting class

**Special:** This does not allow him to cast arcane spells from that class's spell list without
suffering the normal chances of arcane spell failure, unless the spell lacks somatic components.
