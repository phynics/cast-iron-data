**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 12

**Silent Magic (Su):**

**Ability:** The magus can cast one spell per day as if it were modified by the
[Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell) feat. This does not increase the casting time or the level of
the spell.
