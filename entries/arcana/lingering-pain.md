**Lingering Pain (Su):**

**Immediate:** The magus can expend 1 point from his arcane pool after hitting a target with a
weapon attack. All damage from that attack (including damage from a spell cast using the spellstrike
ability) is considered continuous damage for the purposes of any
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks made by the target prior to the beginning of the
magus's next turn.
