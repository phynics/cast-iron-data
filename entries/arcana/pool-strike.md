**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 12

**Pool Strike (Su):**

**Special:** He can use this ability with the _spellstrike_ class feature. If he misses with this
attack, he can hold the charge for up to 1 minute before it dissipates.

**Standard:** The magus can expend 1 point from his arcane pool to charge his free hand with energy.
He can make a melee touch attack with that hand as a **free action** as part of activating this
ability. If the touch attack hits, it releases the charge and deals 2d6 points of energy damage
(acid, cold, electricity, or fire, chosen when he spends the arcane pool point to activate this
ability).

At 6th level: The damage 3d6.

At 9th level: The damage 4d6.

At 12th level: The damage 5d6.

At 15th level: The damage 6d6.

At 18th level: The damage 7d6.
