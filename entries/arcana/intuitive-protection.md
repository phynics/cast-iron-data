**Sources:** Monster Summoner's Handbook pg 9

**Intuitive Protection (Sp):**

**Ability:** When a conjuration (summoning) spell is cast by an opponent and the magus successfully
identifies the spell with a [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) check, he can spend 1 point form his
arcane pool as an **immediate action** to cast
[protection from chaos, evil, good, or law](ref/spell/protection-from-chaos-evil-good-law) (chosen
when using this ability) on himself; this lasts a number of rounds equal to his magus level.

**At 7th Level:** He can instead cast
[magic circle against chaos, evil, good, or law](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-chaos-evil-good-law)
when using this ability.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level magus
