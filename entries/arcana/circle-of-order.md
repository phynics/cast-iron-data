**Sources:** Disciple's Doctrine pg 9

**Circle of Order (Su):**

**Prereq:** 9th-level magus

**Swift:** The magus can spend 1 point from his arcane pool to fortify his defenses against chaotic
attacks. This grants him a dodge bonus to his AC equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding)
his magus level against chaotic-aligned attacks and effects and outsiders with the chaotic subtype
until the beginning of his next turn. (For example, the magus would gain the dodge bonus against
attacks from an [anarchic](ref/magic-enh/anarchic) weapon or a
[glabrezu's](ref/monster/demon-glabrezu) pincers.)
