**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 21

**Arcane Scent (Ex):**

**Ability:** The magus can sniff out other spellcasters. By spending 1 point from his arcane pool,
the magus gains the [scent](ref/umr/scent) special quality for 1 hour per magus level. This scent
can only detect creatures capable of casting spells or using
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Move:** Additionally, while this effect persists, the magus can attempt a Spellcraft check to
determine the highest level spell a detected creature is capable of casting (DC = 10 + the
creature's caster level). The magus can only attempt this Spellcraft check once per creature per 24
hours.
