**Throwing Magus:**

**Ability:** Whenever the magus enhances his weapon using his arcane pool, he can spend 1 additional
point from his arcane pool to add the [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) and
[throwing](ref/magic-enh/throwing) abilities to the list of available weapon special abilities.

When the magus throws a weapon enhanced by his arcane pool and hits a foe, he regains 1 arcane pool
point. The magus can regain a number of arcane pool points per day equal to his Intelligence
modifier in this way.

**Special:** If he throws his held weapon and draws another in the same round, he can enhance the
newly drawn weapon with his arcane pool as a **free action** instead of a swift action.
