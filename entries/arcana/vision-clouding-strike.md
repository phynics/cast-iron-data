**Vision-Clouding Strike:**

**Prereq:** 9th-level magus

**Swift:** The magus can expend 1 or more points from his arcane pool to empower his weapon with
shadowy energy that dissipates within 1 minute. A creature struck with the weapon must succeed at a
Will save (DC = 1/2 the magus's level + his Intelligence modifier) or treat the magus as being in an
area of [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) for 1d4 rounds, plus 1 additional round for each
arcane pool point expended. [True seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) penetrates this effect, but
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) and similar darkness-penetrating effects do not.
