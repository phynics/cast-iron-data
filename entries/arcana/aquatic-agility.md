**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 21

**Aquatic Agility (Su):**

**Immediate:** The magus can spend 1 point from his arcane pool to gain the benefits of
[water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing) for 1 round per level. During this time, he can ignore
the effects of [rough water](ref/skill/swim) and [underwater combat](ref/rule/underwater-combat) on
his attacks and movement.

**Prereq:** 6th-level magus
