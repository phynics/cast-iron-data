The magus can expend 1 point from his arcane pool as an immediate action after hitting a target with
a slashing or piercing weapon in order to deal an amount of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage equal to
his Intelligence modifier (minimum 0). The magus must be at least 9th level before he selects this
arcana.
