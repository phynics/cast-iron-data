**Sources:** Monster Summoner's Handbook pg 9

**Planar Hunter (Su):**

**Ability:** Whenever the magus enhances his weapon using his arcane pool, he can spend 1 additional
point from his arcane pool to add the [planar](ref/magic-enh/planar) special ability to his weapon,
or 2 additional points to add the [phase locking](ref/magic-enh/phase-locking) special ability.

**Prereq:** 9th-level magus
