**Sources:** The Harrow Handbook pg 25

**Rakshasa's Fortune (Su):**

**Free:** Whenever the magus casts a spell of the polymorph subschool on himself, he can draw a card
from a complete [harrow deck](ref/eq-misc/harrow-deck) he owns. If the card's alignment and the
magus's alignment are a true match, the spell's duration is doubled and the magus can select two
additional monster abilities granted by the polymorph spell (such as [scent](ref/umr/scent) or
[grab](ref/umr/grab) for [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)), even if the form assumed by the
magus doesn't normally grant that ability.

On a partial match, the magus can select an additional ability granted by the polymorph spell, even
if the form assumed by the magus doesn't normally grant that ability.

On an opposite match, the duration of the spell is halved and the magus must forgo one of the
abilities granted by his form.

**Prereq:** 9th-level magus
