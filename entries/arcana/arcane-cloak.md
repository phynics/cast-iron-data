**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 54

**Arcane Cloak (Su):**

**Ability:** The magus can expend 1 point from his arcane pool to add his Intelligence bonus to
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks and [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to create a diversion in
order to hide. This bonus lasts for 1 minute.
