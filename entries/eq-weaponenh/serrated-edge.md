**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 10\
**Cost** +2,000 gp **Weight** -\
**Category** Modification; **Proficiency** Modification

The weapon's blade inflicts wounds that bleed profusely. Only piercing or slashing melee weapons can
have a serrated edge. This modification can be added to a
[sawtooth sabre](ref/eq-weapon/sawtooth-sabre) or [rhoka sword](ref/eq-weapon/rhoka-sword) without
increasing its weapon proficiency category, but doing so increases the cost of modification by 2,000
gp. When you make an attack with a weapon with a serrated edge, you can take a -2 penalty on the
attack roll. If you do and the attack deals damage, the weapon also inflicts 1 point of
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage. This bleed stacks with itself (to a maximum equal to the weapon's
critical multiplier), but does not stack with bleed from other sources.
