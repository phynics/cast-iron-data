**Cost** +1,000 gp **Weight** -\
**Category** Modification; **Proficiency** Modification

The weapon's edge is honed to incredible sharpness. The weapon's edge dulls after a number of daily
uses, but the bonus can be reset by spending 15 minutes [whetting](ref/eq-misc/whetstone) the blade
after a night's rest. Only piercing or slashing weapons can have the razor-sharp modification.
Projectile weapons can't be razor-sharp, but their ammunition can. A razor-sharp weapon gains a +1
bonus on weapon damage rolls for a number of attacks each day equal to 3 + your base attack bonus.
