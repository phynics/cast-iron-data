**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 10\
**Cost** +500 gp **Weight** -\
**Category** Modification; **Proficiency** Modification

A weapon with the versatile design modification is easier to wield for those skilled with other
weapon groups. When versatile design is added to a weapon, choose a
[fighter weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups). The modified weapon is considered to be a weapon
of that weapon group (such as for the [fighter's](ref/class/fighter) weapon training class feature).
A melee weapon cannot be considered part of a weapon group for ranged weapons, and vice versa.
