**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 24\
**Price** +150 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Channel Foci\
**Deity** Any

Consecrated weapons may be of any type - swords, axes, masterwork,
[mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral), and even magical - and function appropriately. When activated,
the cleric charges the weapon with positive or negative energy (as appropriate) so it deals extra
damage when it hits a target. This functions like the [Channel Smite](ref/feat/channel-smite) feat,
except the additional damage is equal to half the cleric's
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) damage (the target makes a Will save as normal, based
on the cleric's channel energy DC). The weapon remains charged with this energy until it
successfully strikes a creature or 24 hours pass, whichever comes first. Creatures other than the
cleric are able to use the charged weapon and this ability.
