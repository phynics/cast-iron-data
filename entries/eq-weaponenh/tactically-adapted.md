**Cost** +2,000 gp **Weight** -\
**Category** Modification; **Proficiency** Modification

A tactically adapted weapon is designed for specialized tactics rather than just swinging and
stabbing. Only melee weapons can be tactically adapted. When this quality is added to a weapon,
choose a weapon quality from the following list: [blocking](ref/misc/blocking),
[brace](ref/misc/brace), [disarm](ref/misc/disarm), [distracting](ref/misc/distracting),
[nonlethal](ref/misc/nonlethal), [performance](ref/misc/performance), or [trip](ref/misc/trip). The
modified weapon gains that weapon quality.
