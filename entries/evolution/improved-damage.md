**Improved Damage (Ex)**

**Ability:** Select one natural attack form and
[increase the damage die](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) type by one step.

**Flavor:** One of the eidolon's natural attacks is particularly deadly.

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once. Its effects do not stack. Each time an
eidolon selects this evolution, it applies to a different natural attack.
