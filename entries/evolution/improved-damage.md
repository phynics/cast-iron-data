1-Point Evolutions: The following evolutions cost 1 point from the eidolon's evolution pool.

One of the eidolon's natural attacks is particularly deadly. Select one natural attack form and
[increase the damage die](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) type by one step. This evolution can be
selected more than once. Its effects do not stack. Each time an eidolon selects this evolution, it
applies to a different natural attack.
