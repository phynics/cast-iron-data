**Rake (Ex)**

**Ability:** An eidolon grows dangerous claws on its feet, allowing it to make 2
[rake](ref/umr/rake) attacks on foes it is grappling. These attacks are
[primary attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks). The eidolon receives these additional attacks each time
it succeeds on a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) check against the target. These rake attacks deal 1d4
points of damage (1d6 if Large, 1d8 if Huge).

**Prereq:** 4th-level summoner, eidolon has quadruped base form

**Special:** This evolution counts as one natural attack toward the eidolon's maximum.
