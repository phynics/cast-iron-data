An eidolon's attacks become charged with energy. Pick one energy type: acid, cold, electricity, or
fire. All of the eidolon's natural attacks deal 1d6 points of energy damage of the chosen type on a
successful hit. The summoner must be at least 5th level before selecting this evolution.
