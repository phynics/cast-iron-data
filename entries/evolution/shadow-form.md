**Shadow Form (Su)**

**Ability:** This shadow form grants the eidolon constant [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20%
miss chance), and its melee attacks affect incorporeal creatures as if it had the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon property. The eidolon's melee attacks deal
only half damage to corporeal creatures.

**Flavor:** The eidolon's body becomes shadowy and more indistinct.
