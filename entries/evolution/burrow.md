An eidolon grows thick and gnarled claws, allowing it to move through the earth. The eidolon gains a
burrow speed equal to 1/2 its base speed. It can use this speed to move through dirt, clay, sand,
and earth. It does not leave a hole behind, nor is its passage marked on the surface. The summoner
must be at least 9th level before selecting this evolution.
