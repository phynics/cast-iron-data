1-Point Evolutions: The following evolutions cost 1 point from the eidolon's evolution pool.

An eidolon has a pair of sharp hooves at the end of its [limbs](ref/evolution/limbs), giving it two
[hoof attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks). These attacks are secondary attacks. The hooves deal 1d4
points of damage (1d6 if Large, 1d8 if Huge). The eidolon must have the [limbs](ref/evolution/limbs)
evolution to take this evolution. This evolution can only be applied to the limbs (legs) evolution
once. Alternatively, the eidolon can replace the [claws](ref/evolution/claws) evolution from its
base form with these hoof attacks (this still costs 1 evolution point). This evolution can be
selected more than once, but the eidolon must possess an equal number of
[limbs](ref/evolution/limbs) evolutions.
