**Hooves (Ex)**

**Ability:** An eidolon has a pair of sharp hooves at the end of its limbs, giving it two
[hoof attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks). These attacks are secondary attacks. The hooves deal 1d4
points of damage (1d6 if Large, 1d8 if Huge).

**Prereq:** [Limbs](ref/evolution/limbs) evolution, or its base form has
[claws](ref/evolution/claws) (this evolution replaces the claws)

**Special:** This evolution can only be applied to the _limbs (legs)_ evolution once. This evolution
can be selected more than once, but the eidolon must possess an equal number of _limbs_ evolutions.
