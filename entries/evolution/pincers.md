**Pincers (Ex)**

**Ability:** An eidolon grows a large pincers at the end of one pair of its limbs, giving it two
pincer attacks. These attacks are secondary attacks. The pincers deal 1d6 points of damage (1d8 if
Large, 2d6 if Huge). Eidolons with the [grab](ref/evolution/grab) evolution linked to pincers gain a
+2 bonus on CMB checks made to grapple.

**Prereq:** [Limbs](ref/evolution/limbs) (arms), or its base form has [claws](ref/evolution/claws)
(this evolution replaces the claws)

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once, but the eidolon must possess an equal
number of the _limbs_ evolution.
