**&L&Blindsense (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** An eidolon's senses become incredibly acute, giving it [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense)
out to a range of 30 feet. This ability allows the eidolon to pinpoint the location of creatures
that it cannot see without having to make a Perception check, but such creatures still have total
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from the eidolon.

**Prereq:** 9th-level summoner

**Special:** Visibility still affects the eidolon's movement and it is still denied its Dexterity
bonus to Armor Class against attacks from creatures it cannot see.
