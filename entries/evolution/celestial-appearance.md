**Celestial Appearance (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The eidolon appears as a celestial creature and manifests some of the abilities of a
celestial.

**At 12th Level:** By spending 2 additional evolution points, this protection is increased to
immunity against these attacks and the [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) is increased to
11 + its HD (the summoner must pay for the 7th-level upgrade before paying for this 12th-level
upgrade).

**At 7th Level:** By spending 2 additional evolution points, this bonus on saves is increased to +4
and the [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) is extended to affect any spells and effects
from evil creatures.

**Passive:** Spells and effects that target creatures with the good subtype or have specific effects
against such creatures affect the eidolon as if it were a celestial. The eidolon gains a +2 bonus on
saves against disease, petrification, poison, and electricity spells and effects. It also gains
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 5 + its HD against spells with the evil
descriptor.

**Prereq:** The summoner must be good-aligned
