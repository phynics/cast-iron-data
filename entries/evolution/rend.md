**&L&Rend (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** An eidolon learns to rip and tear the flesh of those it attacks with its claws, gaining
the [rend](ref/umr/rend) ability. Whenever the eidolon makes two successful claw attacks against the
same target in 1 round, its claws latch onto the flesh and deal extra damage. This damage is equal
to the damage dealt by one claw attack plus 1-1/2 times the eidolon's Strength modifier.

**Prereq:** 6th-level summoner, [claws](ref/evolution/claws)
