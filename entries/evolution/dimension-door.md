4-Point Evolutions: The following evolutions cost 4 points from the eidolon's evolution pool.

An eidolon learns to cast _[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day. The caster level for this evolution
is equal to the eidolon's Hit Dice. The save DC for this spell is 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's HD + the
eidolon's Charisma modifier. The eidolon must have a Charisma score of at least 14 to take this
evolution. The summoner must be at least 13th level before selecting this evolution.
