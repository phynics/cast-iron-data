**Dimension Door (Sp)**

**Ability:** An eidolon learns to cast [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day. The caster level for this evolution
is equal to the eidolon's Hit Dice. The save DC for this spell is 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's HD + the
eidolon's Charisma modifier.

**Prereq:** 13th-level summoner, eidolon has charisma 14+
