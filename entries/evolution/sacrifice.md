**Sacrifice (Su)**

**Flavor:** An eidolon can sacrifice its own hit points to heal another creature.

**Standard:** The eidolon can sacrifice up to 2 hit points per Hit Die and then touch a target
creature, thereby healing the creature for half the amount sacrificed.
