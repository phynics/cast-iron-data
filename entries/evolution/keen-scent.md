2-Point Evolutions: The following evolutions each cost 2 points from the eidolon's evolution pool.

An eidolon's sense of smell becomes even more acute. The eidolon can notice other creatures by
[scent](ref/evolution/scent) in a 180-foot radius underwater and can detect blood in the water at
ranges of up to a mile. The eidolon must possess the [gills](ref/evolution/gills) and
[scent](ref/evolution/scent) evolutions to take this evolution.
