**Blindsight (Ex)**

**Ability:** An eidolon's senses sharpen even further, granting it [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight)
out to a range of 30 feet. The eidolon can maneuver and attack as normal, ignoring darkness,
invisibility, and most forms of [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) as long as it has line of effect
to the target.

**Prereq:** 11th-level summoner, [blindsense](ref/evolution/blindsense)
