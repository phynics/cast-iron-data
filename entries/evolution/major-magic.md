**Major Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** This spell can be cast once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this evolution is equal to
the eidolon's Hit Dice - 2. The save DC for this spell is 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's HD + the eidolon's
Charisma modifier.

**Choice:** Select one spell from the following list: [acid arrow](ref/spell/acid-arrow),
[cure moderate wounds](ref/spell/cure-moderate-wounds), [darkness](ref/spell/darkness),
[daze monster](ref/spell/daze-monster), [glide](ref/spell/glide),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility) (self only),
[lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser), [levitate](ref/spell/levitate),
[minor image](ref/spell/minor-image), [scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray),
[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility), or [spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb).

**Flavor:** An eidolon learns to cast a major spell as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 10th Level:** This spell can be cast three times per day by spending 2 additional evolution
points.

**Prereq:** 7th-level summoner, eidolon has charisma 12+, [minor magic](ref/evolution/minor-magic)

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once. Each time an eidolon selects this
evolution, it applies to a new spell from the above list.
