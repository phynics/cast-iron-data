**&L&Grab (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** An eidolon becomes adept at grappling foes, gaining the [grab](ref/umr/grab) ability.
Pick bite, claw, pincers, slam, tail slap, or tentacle attacks. Whenever the eidolon makes a
successful attack of the selected type, it can attempt a free combat maneuver check. If successful,
the eidolon [grapples](ref/rule/grapple) the target.

**Prereq:** Must have a bite, claw, pincers, slam, tail slap, or tentacle attack

**Special:** This ability only works on creatures of a size one category smaller than the eidolon or
smaller. Eidolons with this evolution receive a +4 bonus on CMB checks made to grapple.
