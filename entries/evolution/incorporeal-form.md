4-Point Evolutions: The following evolutions cost 4 points from the eidolon's evolution pool.

Once per day, an eidolon can become [incorporeal](ref/subtype/incorporeal) for 1 round per summoner
level. While in this form, the eidolon gains the [incorporeal](ref/umr/incorporeal) subtype and
incorporeal quality. It only takes half damage from corporeal sources as long as they are magic (it
takes no damage from nonmagical weapons and objects). Likewise, its spells or
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) deal only half damage to corporeal creatures.
Spells and other effects that do not deal damage function normally. The summoner must be at least
15th level before selecting this evolution.
