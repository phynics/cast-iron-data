**Resistance (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** The resistance becomes 15.

**At 5th Level:** The resistance becomes 10.

**Passive:** An eidolon's form takes on a resiliency to one particular energy type, which is usually
reflected in its physical body (ashen hide for fire, icy breath for cold, and so on).

Pick one energy type (acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic). The eidolon gains
[resist](ref/umr/resistance) 5 against that energy type.

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once. Its effects do not stack. Each time an
eidolon selects this evolution, it applies to a different energy type.
