**Ultimate Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** The chosen spell can be cast once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this evolution is equal to
the eidolon's Hit Dice - 2. The save DC for this spell is 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's HD + the eidolon's
Charisma modifier.

**Choice:** Select one spell from the following list: [arcane sight](ref/spell/arcane-sight),
[create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water),
[cure serious wounds](ref/spell/cure-serious-wounds), [daylight](ref/spell/daylight),
[fireball](ref/spell/fireball), [fly](ref/spell/fly), [gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form),
[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt), [major image](ref/spell/major-image),
[stinking cloud](ref/spell/stinking-cloud), [tongues](ref/spell/tongues), or
[water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing).

**Flavor:** An eidolon learns to cast a powerful spell as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Prereq:** 11th-level summoner, eidolon has Charisma 13+, [major magic](ref/evolution/major-magic)

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once. Each time an eidolon selects this
evolution, it applies to a new spell from the above list.
