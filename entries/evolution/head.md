**Head (Ex)**

**Ability:** An eidolon grows an additional head. The eidolon does not gain any additional natural
attacks for the additional head, but the additional head does allow the eidolon to take other
evolutions that add an additional attack to a head (such as a [bite](ref/evolution/bite),
[gore](ref/evolution/gore), or [breath weapon](ref/umr/breath-weapon)).

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once.
