2-Point Evolutions: The following evolutions each cost 2 points from the eidolon's evolution pool.

An eidolon grows an additional head. The eidolon does not gain any additional natural attacks for
the additional head, but the additional head does allow the eidolon to take other evolutions that
add an additional attack to a head (such as a [bite](ref/evolution/bite),
[gore](ref/evolution/gore), or [breath weapon](ref/umr/breath-weapon)). This evolution can be
selected more than once.
