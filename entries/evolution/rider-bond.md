**Rider Bond (Ex)**

**Flavor:** You and your eidolon share an empathic connection while you ride it.

**Passive:** You gain a bonus on Ride checks equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your
summoner level and you also gain [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat) as a bonus feat when
mounted on your eidolon.

**Prereq:** [Mount](ref/evolution/mount)
