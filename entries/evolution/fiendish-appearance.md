**Fiendish Appearance (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The eidolon appears as a fiendish creature and manifests some of the abilities of a
fiend.

**At 12th Level:** By spending 2 additional evolution points, the eidolon gains immunity to acid,
disease, fire, and poison. Its [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) increases to an amount
of equal to 11 + its HD. (The summoner must pay for the 7th-level upgrade before paying for this
12th-level upgrade.)

**At 7th Level:** By spending 2 additional evolution points, the summoner increases the bonus on
saving throws to +4 and extends the [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) to affect any
spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) cast by good creatures.

**Passive:** Spells and effects that target creatures with the evil subtype or have specific effects
against such creatures affect the eidolon as if it had that subtype. The eidolon gains a +2 bonus on
saving throws against acid, disease, fire, and poison spells and effects. It also gains an amount of
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 5 + its HD against spells with the good
descriptor.

**Prereq:** The summoner must be evil-aligned
