**Flight (Ex or Su)**

**Ability:** An eidolon grows large wings, like those of a bat, bird, insect, or dragon, gaining the
ability to fly. The eidolon gains a fly speed equal to its base speed.

The eidolon's maneuverability depends on it size. Medium or smaller eidolons have good
maneuverability. Large eidolons have average maneuverability, while Huge eidolons have poor
maneuverability.

For 2 additional evolution points, the eidolon flies by means of magic. It loses its wings, but its
maneuverability increases to perfect. Flying via magic means makes this a supernatural ability.

**Prereq:** 5th-level summoner

**Special:** The eidolon's fly speed can be increased by spending additional evolution points,
gaining a 20-foot increase to fly speed for each additional point spent.
