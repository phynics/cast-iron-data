**&L&Slam (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** An eidolon can deliver a devastating [slam attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks). This
attack is a primary attack. The slam deals 1d8 points of damage (2d6 if Large, 2d8 if Huge).

**Prereq:** [Limbs](ref/evolution/limbs) (arms) evolution, or its base form has
[claws](ref/evolution/claws) (this evolution replaces the claws)

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once, but the eidolon must possess an equal
number of the _limbs_ evolution.
