1-Point Evolutions: The following evolutions cost 1 point from the eidolon's evolution pool.

An eidolon gains webbed hands, feet, or powerful flippers, giving it a swim speed equal to its base
speed. This evolution does not give the eidolon the ability to breathe underwater. This evolution
can be selected more than once. Each additional time it is selected, increase the eidolon's swim
speed by 20 feet.
