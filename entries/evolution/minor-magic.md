**Minor Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** The chosen spell can be cast once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this evolution is equal to
the eidolon's Hit Dice - 2. The save DC for this spell is 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's HD + the eidolon's
Charisma modifier.

**Choice:** Select one spell from the following list: [burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands),
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds),
[detect chaos/evil/good/law](ref/spell/detect-evil), [magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile),
[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist), [silent image](ref/spell/silent-image),
[vanish](ref/spell/vanish) (self only), or [ventriloquism](ref/spell/ventriloquism).

**Flavor:** An eidolon learns to cast a minor spell as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 7th Level:** This spell can be cast three times per day by spending 2 additional evolution
points.

**Prereq:** 4th-level summoner, eidolon has Charisma 11+, [basic magic](ref/evolution/basic-magic)

**Special:** This evolution can be selected more than once. Each time an eidolon selects this
evolution, it applies to a new spell from the above list.
