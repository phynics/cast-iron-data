**&L&Poison (Ex)&FN&**

**Action:** Ability

**Prereq:** 7th-level summoner, eidolon has a bite or sting attack

An eidolon secretes toxic venom, gaining a poison attack. Pick one bite or sting attack. Whenever
the selected attack hits, the target is poisoned.

**Eidolon poison**

**Type:** Poison (Natural attack-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the eidolon's HD + its Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d4

**Cure:** 1 save

For 2 additional evolution points, this poison deals Constitution damage instead. This poison can be
used no more than once per round.
