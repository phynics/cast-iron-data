**Shadow Blend (Su)**

**Passive:** In any condition of [illumination](ref/rule/vision-and-light) other than bright light,
the eidolon disappears into the shadows, giving it [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss
chance). If it has the [shadow form](ref/evolution/shadow-form) evolution, it instead gains total
concealment (50% miss chance). The eidolon can suspend or resume this ability as a **free action**.
