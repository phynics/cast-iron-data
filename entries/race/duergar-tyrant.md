**Sources:** Occult Bestiary pg 23, Advanced Race Guide pg 186\
Duergar tyrants are defined by their class levels - they do not have racial Hit Dice. Duergar
tyrants have all of the racial traits of [duergar](ref/race/duergar) characters except
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), in addition to the following.

### Duergar Racial Traits

**+2 Strength, +4 Constitution, +2 Wisdom, -2 Charisma:** Duergar tyrants are strong, hardy, and
willful.

**Medium:** Duergar Tyrants are Medium creatures and have no bonuses or penalties due to their size.

**Humanoid:** Duergar Tyrants are humanoids with the dwarf subtype.

**Slow and Steady:** Duergar have a base speed of 20 feet, but their speed is never modified by
armor or encumbrance.

**Superior Darkvision:** Duergar Tyrants can see in the dark up to 120 feet.

**Duergar Immunities:** Duergar are immune to paralysis, phantasms, and poison. They gain a +2
racial bonus on saves against spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Psychic Resilience (Ex):** Duergar tyrants' racial bonus against spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) increases from +2 to +4 on saving throws
against psychic spells and spell-like abilities.

**Stability:** Duergar receive a +4 racial bonus to their Combat Maneuver Defense against bull rush
or trip attempts while standing on the ground.

**Light Sensitivity:** Duergar Tyrants are dazzled in areas of bright light.

**Languages:** Duergar Tyrants begin play speaking Common, Dwarven, and Undercommon. Duergar Tyrants
with high Intelligence scores can choose from the following bonus languages: Aklo, Draconic, Giant,
Goblin, Orc, and Terran.

**Telekinetic Adept:** A duergar tyrant has access to the
[basic telekinesis](ref/kinetic/basic-telekinesis), [kinetic form](ref/kinetic/kinetic-form),
[telekinetic finesse](ref/kinetic/telekinetic-finesse),
[telekinetic haul](ref/kinetic/telekinetic-haul), and
[telekinetic invisibility](ref/kinetic/telekinetic-invisibility) kineticist wild talents with an
effective [kineticist](ref/class/kineticist) level equal to her total character level.

**Psychic Magic:** Duergar tyrants can cast [ironskin](ref/spell/ironskin) (2 PE) and
[mind thrust I](ref/spell/mind-thrust-1) (1 PE) as per the [psychic magic](ref/umr/psychic-magic)
universal monster rule. The caster level is equal to the duergar tyrant's total character level.
Duergar tyrants have a reservoir of 5 PE per day.

**Spell Resistance:** A duergar tyrant has [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to
11 + her character level.
