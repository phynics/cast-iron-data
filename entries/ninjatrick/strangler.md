**Strangler (Ex)**

**Ability:** A ninja with this trick knows how to use a scarf, usually with a coin or large knot, as
a lethal [garrote](ref/eq-weapon/garrote). The ninja takes no penalties when using a scarf as an
improvised garrote.

**Free:** In addition, when attempting a grapple combat maneuver check, the ninja can spend 1 ki
point to gain the [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) special attack on that maneuver with damage equal
to half the ninja's sneak attack dice (minimum 1d6).
