**&L&Arcane Backfire (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** When a ninja with this trick successfully attacks a creature that is holding a magic
item that has a number of charges or uses per day, she can attempt a
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) check (DC = 20 + the item's CL) to cause the item to
lose either one daily use or a number of charges equal to the number of the ninja's sneak attack
dice.
