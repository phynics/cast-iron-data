**Choking Bomb (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a ninja throws a _smoke bomb,_ all living creatures in the resulting cloud
must make a Fortitude save or become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) by the choking black smoke for
1d4 rounds. The DC of this saving throw is equal to 10 + 1/2 the ninja's level + the ninja's
Charisma modifier. The ninja can decide to create an ordinary _smoke bomb_ or add poison to this
_smoke bomb_ as normal.

**Prereq:** [Poison bomb](ref/ninjatrick/poison-bomb)
