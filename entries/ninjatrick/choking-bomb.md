Whenever a ninja throws a smoke bomb, all living creatures in the resulting cloud must make a
Fortitude save or become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) by the choking black smoke for 1d4 rounds.
The DC of this saving throw is equal to 10 + 1/2 the ninja's level + the ninja's Charisma modifier.
The ninja can decide to create an ordinary smoke bomb or add poison to this smoke bomb as normal.
The ninja must have the [poison bomb](ref/ninjatrick/poison-bomb) ninja trick before selecting this
trick.
