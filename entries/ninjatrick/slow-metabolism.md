**Slow Metabolism (Ex)**

**Ability:** She can [hold her breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for twice as long as normal before
needing to make Constitution checks (four times her Constitution score).

**Flavor:** The ninja has a slower-than-normal metabolism.

**Passive:** In addition, whenever the ninja is poisoned, the time between saving throws (the
poison's frequency) is doubled. Thus, a poison that has a frequency of _1/round for 6 rounds_ would
instead be _1/2 rounds for 12 rounds._
