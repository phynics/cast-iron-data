Whenever a ninja uses the [vanishing trick](ref/ninjatrick/vanishing-trick) ninja trick, she is
treated as if she were under the effects of
_[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater)_. The ninja must have the
[vanishing trick](ref/ninjatrick/vanishing-trick) ninja trick before selecting this ninja trick.
