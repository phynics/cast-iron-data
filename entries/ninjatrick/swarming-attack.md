**Swarming Attack**

**Ability:** While using the swarming racial trait to share a space with an ally, a ninja with this
talent gains a bonus on damage roles equal to the number of sneak attack dice her ally has. For
example, a ninja would gain +3 damage if her ally has sneak attack 3d6.

**Flavor:** Ratfolk ninjas use their large numbers to overwhelm their enemies with countless
attacks.

**Prereq:** [Ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk), _swarming_ racial trait
