Whenever the ninja throws a [smoke bomb](ref/ninjatrick/smoke-bomb), all living creatures in the
cloud must make a Fortitude save or be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) by the black smoke for 1d4
rounds. The DC of this saving throw is equal to 10 + 1/2 the ninja's level + the ninja's Charisma
modifier. This trick cannot be combined with a [choking bomb](ref/ninjatrick/choking-bomb), but the
ninja can combine a blinding bomb with a [poison bomb](ref/ninjatrick/poison-bomb). The ninja must
have the [choking bomb](ref/ninjatrick/choking-bomb) ninja trick before selecting this trick.
