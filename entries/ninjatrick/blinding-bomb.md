**Blinding Bomb (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever the ninja throws a [smoke bomb](ref/ninjatrick/smoke-bomb), all living
creatures in the cloud must make a Fortitude save or be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) by the black
smoke for 1d4 rounds. The DC of this saving throw is equal to 10 + 1/2 the ninja's level + the
ninja's Charisma modifier.

**Prereq:** [Choking bomb](ref/ninjatrick/choking-bomb)

**Special:** This trick cannot be combined with a _choking bomb,_ but the ninja can combine a
_blinding bomb_ with a [poison bomb](ref/ninjatrick/poison-bomb).
