**Smoke Bomb (Ex)**

**Special:** Each use of this ability uses up 1 ki point.

**Standard:** This ability allows a ninja to throw a _smoke bomb_ that creates a cloud of smoke with
a 15-foot radius. This acts like the smoke from a [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick). The ninja
can center this smoke on herself, or throw the bomb as a ranged touch attack with a range of 20
feet.
