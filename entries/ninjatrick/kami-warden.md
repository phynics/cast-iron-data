**&L&Kami Warden (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** A ninja with this trick gains [Bodyguard](ref/feat/bodyguard) and
[In Harm's Way](ref/feat/in-harms-way) as bonus feats, ignoring their prerequisites.

**Flavor:** Ninjas can act as bodyguards as well as assassins or spies; some mystically emulate the
relationship between [kami](ref/family/kami) and their wards when protecting their charges.

**Special:** When using In Harm's Way to intercept an attack, the ninja can spend 1 ki point to
gains DR 10/cold iron and resistance 10 to acid, electricity, and fire for that attack.
