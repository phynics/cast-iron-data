**Advanced Talent**

**Ability:** The ninja can select a rogue talent from the list of Advanced Talents in place of a
ninja trick. The ninja cannot select a rogue talent that has the same name as a ninja trick.

**Special:** The ninja cannot select [improved evasion](ref/talent/improved-evasion) unless she has
[evasion](ref/ability/evasion) (either as a class feature or as a
[ninja master trick](ref/ninjatrick/evasion)).
