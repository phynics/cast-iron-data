**Spiritual Companion (Ex)**

**Ability:** A ninja with this trick gains an [improved familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar),
treating her ninja level as her wizard level. The ninja can select any of the following as her
familiar: [calligraphy wyrm](ref/monster/calligraphy-wyrm), [pipefox](ref/monster/pipefox),
[shikigami kami](ref/monster/kami-shikigami), or [spirit oni](ref/monster/oni-spirit-oni). The ninja
must have an alignment compatible with the chosen familiar.

**Flavor:** While ninjas are accustomed to solitude, some form pacts with magical beings to seek aid
and advice.
