**Ghost Step (Su)**

**Flavor:** A ninja with this trick can pass through walls as if she were a ghost.

**Special:** Each use of this ability uses up 1 ki point.

**Swift:** Until the end of her turn, she can pass through a wall or other surface that is up to 5
feet thick per level as if she were incorporeal. She must exit the solid material by the end of her
turn.
