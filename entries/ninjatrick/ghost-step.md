As a swift action, a ninja with this trick can pass through walls as if she were a ghost. Until the
end of her turn, she can pass through a wall or other surface that is up to 5 feet thick per level
as if she were incorporeal. She must exit the solid material by the end of her turn. Each use of
this ability uses up 1 ki point.
