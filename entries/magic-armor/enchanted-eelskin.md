**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 126\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 11,160 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [slick](ref/magic-enh/slick) [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) armor is made of shining,
slippery eelskin that allows the wearer to move with deadly grace and sudden swiftness, especially
underwater. When immersed in water, enchanted eelskin gains the [shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow)
property and grants the wearer a swim speed of 30 feet and the ability to breathe water. In
addition, during a surprise round the wearer can act as if she had a full round of actions, rather
than a single standard action, as long as those actions do not require leaving the water. If she
does leave the water, her surprise round turn immediately ends.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[grease](ref/spell/grease), [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility),
[touch of the sea](ref/spell/touch-of-the-sea); **Price** 5,660 gp
