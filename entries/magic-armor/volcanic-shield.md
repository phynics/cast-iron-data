**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 133\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 14,170 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This +1 [bashing](ref/magic-enh/bashing) [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) burns
with an internal fire upon its boss, gaining the [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon special
ability when used to make [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) attacks. On command, the wielder can
make the shield belch forth smoke as a [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick), but this smoke does not
impair the shield user's vision. Once per day, the wielder may cause the volcanic shield to erupt in
a cascade of brimstone as the _[burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands),
[pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics); **Price** 7,170 gp
