**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 251\
**Aura** moderate divination, evocation, and necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 5,380 gp; **Weight** 40 lbs.

This blue-white +1 [deathless](ref/magic-enh/deathless) [chainmail](ref/eq-armor/chainmail) is made
of an intricate pattern of light and dark links that form the shape of
[Pharasma's](ref/faith/pharasma) spiral on the armor's chest. The wearer can use _detect undead,
disrupt undead,_ and _[light](ref/spell/light)_ each once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward), [detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead),
[disrupt undead](ref/spell/disrupt-undead), [light](ref/spell/light); **Price** 2,840 gp
