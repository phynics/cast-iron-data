**Sources:** Inner Sea Monster Codex pg 29\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

This flat, gray +1 [breastplate](ref/eq-armor/breastplate) is embossed with the iron fin symbol of
the Wave Riders (Absalom's sea cavalry), and is enchanted to make it lighter in water. The armor
imposes no armor check penalty on Swim checks, but still contributes to the wearer's
[encumbrance](ref/rule/carrying-capacity) and applies all other armor check penalties as normal.
Once per day, the wearer can speak a command word even if submerged to cause the armor - and the
wearer if the armor is worn - to float as per the _[buoyancy](ref/spell/buoyancy)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[buoyancy](ref/spell/buoyancy), [water walk](ref/spell/water-walk); **Price** 2,500 gp
