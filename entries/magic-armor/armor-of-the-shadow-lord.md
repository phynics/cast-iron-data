**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 117,160 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This suit of +4 [greater shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow-greater)
[greater slick](ref/magic-enh/slick-greater) [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) armor fits as snugly as
a tight suit of silk against its wearer's skin and harbors an amoral and bloodthirsty intelligence.
Via the telepathy it shares with its wielder, the armor gives constant advice on how to be stealthy,
reach out of the way spots, and get the drop on foes. It also grants an extra +1d6 points of
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) damage, but the wearer must already have the sneak attack
class feature to gain this bonus. Furthermore the armor has a maximum Dexterity bonus of +10 and no
arcane spell failure.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[grease](ref/spell/grease), [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [silence](ref/spell/silence),
creator must have the sneak attack class feature; **Price** 58,660 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** neutral; **Ego** 14\
**Senses** 120 ft., [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)\
**Int** 14, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 14\
**Communication** telepathy (Aklo, Common, and Elven)
