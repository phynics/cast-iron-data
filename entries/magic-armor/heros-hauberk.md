**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 211\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 16,600 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

The [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) of this dull gray +1
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) [chain shirt](ref/eq-armor/chain-shirt) stacks with any
other damage reduction the wearer possesses. For example, a wearer with DR 5/magic would reduce
damage from nonmagical attacks by 6, and magical attacks by 1. While the wearer rages or maintains a
[raging song](ref/ability/raging-song), the armor emits a low droning sound and grants the benefits
of [light fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-light).

While the wearer maintains a bardic performance, the armor's links jangle in harmony with his song
or movements, aiding his focus. The wearer receives a +1 luck bonus on all ability checks and skill
checks he attempts during such a performance. In addition, any penalties applied to his attack
rolls, ability checks, skill checks, and saving throws are reduced by 1. If a die roll has multiple
penalties, the total of the penalties is reduced by 1, not each individual penalty.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) or [miracle](ref/spell/miracle), creator must have the raging
song class feature; **Price** 11,850 gp
