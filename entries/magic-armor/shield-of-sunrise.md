**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 7,659 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This +1 [bashing](ref/magic-enh/bashing) [light steel shield](ref/eq-armor/light-steel-shield)
features a brilliant sun motif, and always seems comfortably warm to the touch. Once per day when
the shield's bearer successfully hits a creature with a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) attack,
he may activate the shield as a swift action to mark the target with radiant energy. This radiant
energy lasts for 1d4 rounds. During this time, the marked creature shines like a
[sunrod](ref/eq-misc/sunrod), and the wielder's allies gain a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls
against the marked creature as long as they are within 30 feet of it. If the marked creature is
vulnerable to light, this insight bonus increases to +3.

When a marked creature is hit by a melee or ranged attack, the radiant energy discharges in a burst
of light, dealing 1d6 points of damage to the marked creature and ending the marking effect. If the
creature is vulnerable to light, it is also [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round.
