**Sources:** Pathfinder \#58: Island of Empty Eyes pg 60\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 33,810 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This red +2 [slick](ref/magic-enh/slick) [electricity resistance](ref/magic-enh/energy-resistance)
[leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) armor is crafted from the skin of the
[crimson hagfish](ref/monster/fish-varisian-hagfish), more commonly known as the Shackles slime eel.
The armor exudes a slippery slime that grants its wearer a +5 competence bonus on Escape Artist and
Swim checks. In addition, as a standard action once per day, the wearer can form an aura of
electricity around himself that persists for 5 rounds. Creatures adjacent to the wearer when this
power is activated and at the start of the wearer's turn take 2d6 points of electricity damage and
are [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. A successful DC 14 Reflex save halves the damage
and negates the staggering effect. The aura persists for 5 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[elemental aura](ref/spell/elemental-aura), [grease](ref/spell/grease),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy); **Price** 16,985 gp
