**Sources:** Misfit Monsters Redeemed pg 30\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 36,000 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

This breastplate made from the shell of a flail snail functions as a +1
[reflecting](ref/magic-enh/reflecting) [breastplate](ref/eq-armor/breastplate) (even though that
shield property cannot normally be added to armor). Once per day, the wearer can cause a spell
targeted at him to reflect back at the caster, exactly like
_[spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning)._ At the GM's discretion, the wearer can instead expend
this one use per day to roll on the flail snail's chart, randomly warping the spell in question.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning), one [flail snail](ref/monster/flail-snail) shell;
**Price** 18,000 gp
