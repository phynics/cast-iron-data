**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 132\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 46,030 gp; **Weight** 22 lbs.

This +1 [arrow deflection](ref/magic-enh/arrow-deflection)
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-armor) [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral)
[tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) glows with a perpetual bluish radiance. Infused with
magical force, a force tower renders its wielder immune to magic missiles and also allows its
wielder to use its arrow deflection special ability to deflect magical rays and ranged touch
attacks. The Reflex save DC to deflect such attacks is equal to 20 + the level of the spell (or 1/2
the class level or Hit Dice of the creature that created the effect, if a supernatural effect). If a
spell or effect creates multiple rays or attacks, the force tower deflects only one of them per
round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness), [resilient sphere](ref/spell/resilient-sphere),
[shield](ref/spell/shield); **Price** 23,530 gp
