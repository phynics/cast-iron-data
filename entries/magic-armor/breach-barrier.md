**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,370 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) is often stylized by its dwarven
crafters to look like a door or archway. Once per day after uttering a command word, the wielder can
press this shield against a portal to secure it as per the _[hold portal](ref/spell/hold-portal)_
spell. The shield remains adhered to the portal for 3 minutes, after which the shield comes free.
The wielder can remove the shield from her arm as a move action, leaving the shield attached to the
portal for the remainder of the duration. Before the end of the duration, the wielder can pull the
shield from the portal, automatically ending the effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[hold portal](ref/spell/hold-portal); **Price** 1,270 gp
