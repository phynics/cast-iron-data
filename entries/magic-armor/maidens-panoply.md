**Aura** moderate abjuration and divination; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 30,650 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This set of +3 [Gray Maiden plate](ref/eq-armor/gray-maiden-plate) creates a mental link between the
wearer and her allies. The wearer can telepathically communicate with allies within 30 feet
(although this communication is one-way, unless an ally also has telepathy). If an ally within 30
feet is aware of a threat, the wearer of this armor is aware as well, and the wearer is never
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) unless all allies within 30 feet are also flat-footed. In
addition, if the wearer of a maiden's panoply also wields a
[maiden's aegis](ref/magic-armor/maidens-aegis), activating that shield's ability does not reduce
the maiden's aegis's enhancement bonus, and the bonus it grants increases to +2.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts), [telepathy](ref/spell/telepathy); **Price** 16,150 gp
