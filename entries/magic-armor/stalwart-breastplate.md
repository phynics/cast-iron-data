**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 143\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 22,350 gp; **Weight** 40 lbs.

The burden of this weighty +1 [light fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-light)
[breastplate](ref/eq-armor/breastplate) gives it an armor check penalty of -5. As an immediate
action, the wearer can negate the effects of a successful combat maneuver made to
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun), or [trip](ref/rule/trip) her. As a
swift action, the wearer can expend one use of mythic power to gain immunity to
[ability damage, ability drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), critical hits, and
[sneak attacks](ref/ability/sneak-attack). This effect lasts for 1 round per mythic tier the wearer
possesses.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [iron body](ref/spell/iron-body), either
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) or [miracle](ref/spell/miracle); **Price** 11,350 gp
