**Aura** strong abjuration \[evil\]; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 141,240 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This jet-black +3 [unrighteous](ref/magic-enh/unrighteous) [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) is
made in the image of the armor worn by [thanatotic titans](ref/monster/titan-thanatotic) and grants
the wearer a fragment of their immense power. The wearer of thanatotic plate gains SR 21 against
divine spells. In addition, the wearer can speak a command word to gain the benefits of _air walk,
break enchantment,_ or _[spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning)_ once per day each. Once per week,
the wearer can use _[greater planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-greater)_ to call upon a
[tarry demodand](ref/monster/demodand-tarry-demodand).

A newly created set of thanatotic plate always includes a
[thanatotic visage](ref/magic-armor/thanatotic-visage); this is included in the price and weight. A
set of thanatotic plate missing its visage has its price reduced by that of the visage.

A nonevil character who dons thanatotic plate immediately gains 4
[negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels); these negative levels can't be removed
or ignored, but they disappear when the armor is removed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk), [break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment),
[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life), [greater planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-greater),
[heal](ref/spell/heal), [spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning),
[unholy aura](ref/spell/unholy-aura); **Price** 71,445 gp
