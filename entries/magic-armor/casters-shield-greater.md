**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 131\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 10,153 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This +3 [light wooden shield](ref/eq-armor/light-wooden-shield) acts in all ways like a
[caster's shield](ref/magic-armor/casters-shield), but the strip can accommodate spells of up to 5th
level.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Scribe Scroll](ref/feat/scribe-scroll); **Price** 5,153 gp
