**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 130\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 19,170 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

The front of this +1 [bashing](ref/magic-enh/bashing)
[heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) looks as though it is crafted from hundreds of
bits of rubble and larger stones. When used to make a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) attack,
stones fall from shield and cling to the target of the shield bash. The target must succeed at a DC
14 Fortitude saving throw or be [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) for 1d4 rounds. Once per day on
command, the target's wielder can choose to petrify the target of a shield bash instead. The target
must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude saving throw or become [petrified](ref/rule/petrified) for 1
minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape),
[flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone); **Price** 9,670 gp
