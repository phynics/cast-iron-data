**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 13,400 gp; **Weight** 35 lbs.

This gilded suit of +2 [banded mail](ref/eq-armor/banded-mail) is decorated with images of celestial
beings holding their weapons high. Legion armor is typically worn by high-ranking
[angels](ref/family/angel) and [archons](ref/family/archon) directly combating the forces of evil on
the Material Plane.

Once per day as a standard action, the wearer of a suit of legion armor can cast the spell
_[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally)_ with a duration of 1 minute. This ally typically takes
on the appearance of an imposing angelic warrior. At any time while the armor's
_[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally)_ is conjured, the wearer of a suit of legion armor can
dismiss the effect as a standard action, causing the _[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally)_ to
explode with a thunderous choir of voices and blaring trumpets. This effect causes each evil-aligned
creatures within 100 feet to become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) unless it succeeds at a DC 20 Will
save.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear), [spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally); **Price** 6,900 gp
