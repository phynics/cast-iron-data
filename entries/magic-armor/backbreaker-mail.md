**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 50\
**Aura** moderate illusion and transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 17,650 gp; **Weight** 45 lbs.

This +2 [spiked](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes) [splint mail](ref/eq-armor/splint-mail) is fashioned
from hacked-off chunks of tanned hide and held together by humanoid spines. Used by orcs of the
Broken Spine clan in Belkzen, these sets of armor are seen as a status symbol, and are adorned with
trophies from slain foes, such as [wayfinders](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder). This armor grants its
wearer the [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) ability, dealing an amount of damage equal to 1d6 + the
wearer's Strength modifier.

The vertebrae of the spines adorning the backbreaker mail are sharpened, acting as +1
human-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) armor spikes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1), [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self),
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Price** 9,025 gp
