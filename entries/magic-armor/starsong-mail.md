**Aura** faint conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 3,460 gp; **Weight** 40 lbs.

This +1 [chainmail](ref/eq-armor/chainmail) is usually a metallic midnight blue and may sport a
badge in the shape of a butterfly, moth, or [starknife](ref/eq-weapon/starknife). Once per day, the
wearer can use _[expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat)._ If the armor's wearer sleeps
in or next to the armor, the armor automatically casts _[dream feast](ref/spell/dream-feast)_ once
per night on the wearer or the last creature who wore it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dream feast](ref/spell/dream-feast), [expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat);
**Price** 1,880 gp
