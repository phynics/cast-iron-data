**Aura** faint conjuration and enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 3,910 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This regal-looking +1 [half-plate](ref/eq-armor/half-plate) is a status symbol in the church of
[Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus), and is decorated with red infernal symbols and an inverted
pentagram. The wearer can use _[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)_ and
_[infernal healing](ref/spell/infernal-healing)_ each once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person), [infernal healing](ref/spell/infernal-healing); **Price**
2,330 gp
