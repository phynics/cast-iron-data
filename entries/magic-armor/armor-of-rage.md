**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 16th\
**Slot** armor; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This armor is similar in appearance to armor of command and functions as a suit of +1
[full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate). However, when it is worn, the armor causes the character to
take a -4 penalty to Charisma. All unfriendly creatures within 300 feet gain a +1 morale bonus on
attack rolls against her. The effect is not noticeable to the wearer or those affected. In other
words, the wearer does not immediately notice that donning the armor is the cause of her problems,
nor do foes understand the reason for the depth of their enmity.

### Construction

**Requirements** [breastplate of command](ref/magic-armor/breastplate-of-command), +1 full plate;
