**Sources:** Chronicle of the Righteous pg 52

**Aura** strong evocation \[fire\]; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 100,050 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

Legend tells that the first suits of empyreal armor were forged from the battered, cast-off
breastplates worn by [solars](ref/monster/angel-solar) in battle. These suits were gifted to
powerful mortal heroes and have since changed hands many times.

Empyreal armor is made of steel forged so that it reflects a golden sheen in the sunlight. This
armor functions as a suit of +3 [moderate fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-moderate)
[full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate). In addition, three times per day, its wearer can activate a
_[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield)_ (warm shield only) that lasts for 13 rounds. Any creature
attacking the wearer with its body or a handheld weapon while the
_[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield)_ effect is active deals normal damage but takes 1d6+13 points
of damage in return. Half this damage is fire damage, but the other half results directly from
divine power and is therefore not subject to energy resistance.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield), [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) or
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle); **Price** 50,850 gp
