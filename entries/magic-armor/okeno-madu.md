**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 52\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 5,590 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

A common sight among the more deplorable slavers of Katapesh, this +1
[leather madu](ref/eq-armor/madu-shield-leather) is adorned with body parts - ranging from fingers
and toes to severed heads - each one crudely affixed to the shield's spikes. The wielder of the
shield gains a +4 competence bonus on Intimidate checks while the shield is visible. The wearer and
any associated companions take a -4 penalty on all Diplomacy checks made against good-aligned
creatures while the shield is visible.

Once per day as a standard action, the wielder of an Okeno madu can shake the shield, causing the
fetishes to rattle uncontrollably for 3 rounds. When he shakes the shield, the wielder can choose
one creature within 35 feet to suffer the effects of the spell _[castigate](ref/spell/castigate)_ as
long as the fetishes continue shaking.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[castigate](ref/spell/castigate); **Price** 2,890 gp
