**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 12,405 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This silk +1 [shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow) [padded](ref/eq-armor/padded) armor is as dark as night
and stitched with thread the color of dried blood. When worn by a rogue with the
[bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack) talent, it increases the bleed damage of the rogue's
sneak attacks by +1.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [silence](ref/spell/silence);
**Price** 6,280 gp
