**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 212\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 11,165 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

This +1 [hide](ref/eq-armor/hide) armor is always made from the pelt of a migratory herd animal.
Anyone wearing this armor can maintain a [hustle](ref/rule/overland-movement) for a full 8 hours
without growing fatigued, doubling her normal daily overland movement rate. After that, the wearer
begins to take nonlethal damage each hour, per the hustle rules. This armor does not increase
tactical movement during combat. The benefits of this armor aren't replenished until the character
has had a full night's rest.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance); **Price** 5,665 gp
