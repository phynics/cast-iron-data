**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 25,180 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +2 [_spell resistance_ (13)](ref/magic-enh/spell-resistance-13)
[tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) grants a +4 resistance bonus on Reflex saving throws
against spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Once per day, when the
wearer succeeds at a saving throw against a spell or spell-like ability that allows a Reflex saving
throw for half damage, she may spend an immediate action to instead take no damage from that spell
or spell-like ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), [spell resistance](ref/spell/spell-resistance); **Price**
12,680 gp
