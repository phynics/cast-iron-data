**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 120,830 gp; **Weight** 23 lbs.

Shined to a mirror finish and fitted with supple leather straps, this +1
[mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) [tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) displays no decoration
or markings on its highly reflective surface. Once per day, the bearer of this shield can issue a
challenge to a foe and ring the shield like a gong, resulting in a resonating blast that negates all
magic in a 10-foot radius, as per _[antimagic field](ref/spell/antimagic-field)._ This effect lasts
for 1 minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[antimagic field](ref/spell/antimagic-field); **Price** 60,930 gp
