**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 132, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 467\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 9,170 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +2 [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) is fashioned to appear to be a roaring
lion's head. Three times per day as a free action, the lion's head can be commanded to attack
(independently of the shield wearer), biting with the wielder's base attack bonus (including
multiple attacks, if the wielder has them) and dealing 2d6 points of damage. This attack is in
addition to any actions performed by the wielder.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4); **Price** 4,670 gp
