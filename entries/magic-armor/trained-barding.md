**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 27\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 8,350 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

This suit of Large +2 [scale mail](ref/eq-armor/scale-mail) [barding](ref/eq-armor/barding) is
equipped with leather straps and loops suitable for attaching harnesses and leashes. It can fit any
Large animal or magical beast, though not creatures of any other type. When worn by an animal, this
armor grants the animal two additional [tricks](ref/ability/animal-tricks) or one general purpose
(as defined by the [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) skill), determined at the time the armor
is created.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[awaken](ref/spell/awaken); **Price** 4,350 gp
