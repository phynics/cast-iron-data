**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 43,300 gp; **Weight** 40 lbs.

> _The following suit of armor is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
> ethnicity._

This armor functions as [hallowed chain](ref/magic-armor/hallowed-chain-varisian), except that it
protects as a suit of +3 [martyring](ref/magic-enh/martyring) [chainmail](ref/eq-armor/chainmail)
and can absorb up to 10 points of positive energy.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[mass cure moderate wounds](ref/spell/cure-moderate-wounds-mass), creator must be a Varisian;
**Price** 21,800 gp
