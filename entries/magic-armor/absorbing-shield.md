**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 130, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 467\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 50,170 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) is made of metal, but its color is a
flat black that seems to absorb light. Once every 2 days, on command, it can
_[disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate)_ an object that it touches, as the spell but requiring a
melee touch attack. This effect only functions as an attack - it can't be activated to target a
creature or weapon as it strikes the shield.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate); **Price** 25,170 gp
