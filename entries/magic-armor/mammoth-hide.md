**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 14,665 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

Made of tattered mammoth hide and bone, this +3 [hide](ref/eq-armor/hide) armor is similar to
[rhino hide](ref/magic-armor/rhino-hide), but is fashioned in the harsh lands of the Realm of the
Mammoth Lords using beasts common to that region. In addition to granting a +3 enhancement bonus to
AC, it has a -1 armor check penalty and grants the wearer 4d6 additional points of damage on the
first successful [charge](ref/rule/charge) attack she makes in a round, including a mounted charge.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Price** 7,415 gp
