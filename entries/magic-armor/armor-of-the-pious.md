**Sources:** Pathfinder \#74: Sword of Valor pg 62\
**Aura** moderate abjuration \[good\]; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 23,300 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

This gold-plated +1 [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) armor
has images of [Iomedae's](ref/faith/iomedae) symbol etched into its immaculately gleaming surface.
The armor requires 24 hours to attune itself to the wearer and her patron deity, after which point
the images on the armor shift to match the symbol of the wearer's patron deity (or remain associated
with Iomedae if the wearer doesn't worship a deity). Thereafter, once per day the armor's wearer can
offer a _[prayer](ref/spell/prayer)_ to her deity (or Iomedae) as a swift action to increase the
armor's enhancement bonus to +2 for 10 rounds. For this duration, the armor also grants a +2 sacred
bonus on saving throws. A paladin wearing armor of the pious can use her
[lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) ability one additional time per day; a mythic paladin
wearing armor of the pious also gains one additional use of mythic power per day. An evil character
who wears armor of the pious gains 2 [negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels).
These negative levels remain as long as the armor is worn and disappear when the armor is removed -
they never become permanent but can't be overcome in any way as long as the armor is worn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[prayer](ref/spell/prayer), [sanctify armor](ref/spell/sanctify-armor); **Price** 16,900 gp
