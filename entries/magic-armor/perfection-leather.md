**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 253\
**Aura** faint divination, necromancy, and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 3,860 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) armor is usually inscribed with [Irori's](ref/faith/irori)
open-hand symbol at the neck, and with meditative mantras along the sleeves. The wearer can use
_guidance, stone fist,_ and _[touch of fatigue](ref/spell/touch-of-fatigue)_ each once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance), [stone fist](ref/spell/stone-fist),
[touch of fatigue](ref/spell/touch-of-fatigue); **Price** 2,010 gp
