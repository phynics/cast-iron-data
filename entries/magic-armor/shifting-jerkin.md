**Aura** faint illusion and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 5,965 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This +1 [lamellar cuirass](ref/eq-armor/lamellar-cuirass) grants its wearer shapeshifting. On
command, the wearer of a shifting jerkin can change her appearance at will as per
_[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)._ If the wearer has a talents class feature (investigator
talents, ninja tricks, rogue talents, or slayer talents), once per day she can spend 1 hour to swap
out one talent for another for which she qualifies. She can't swap a talent that is a prerequisite
for other talents or feats she has.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self), [fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning); **Price** 3,065
gp
