**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 251\
**Aura** faint divination and illusion; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 5,425 gp; **Weight** 20 lbs.

This mottled green-and-brown +1 [shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow)
[studded leather](ref/eq-armor/studded-leather) has the symbol of [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil)
burned into the shoulder. Once per day as a standard action, the wearer can prepare a perfectly
aimed shot; as long as the wearer's next attack roll is made before the end of the next round, this
attack ignores the target's miss chance and AC bonus from [cover](ref/rule/cover) and
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (but not total concealment).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [silence](ref/spell/silence),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Price** 2,800 gp
