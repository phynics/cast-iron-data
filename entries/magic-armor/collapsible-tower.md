**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 131\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 8,170 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

Three times per day, this +2 [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) can be commanded
to transform into a +2 [tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield). It can stay in tower shield form
for 1 minute or until it is commanded to take its normal form again (a swift action). A collapsible
tower's weight does not change when it takes tower shield form.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** 4,170 gp
