**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 51\
**Aura** faint abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 3,900 gp; **Weight** 20 lbs.

Made of densely compacted jungle leaves by the Ekujae jungle elves of the Mwangi, this +1
[leaf armor](ref/eq-armor/leaf-armor) has been delicately inscribed with elven script. Prized by
druids who venture out of the jungle, this armor counts as a plant for the purposes of an
_[entangle](ref/spell/entangle)_ spell, allowing the spell to originate from the position of the
wearer.

Ekujae jungle armor is uniquely suited to jungle terrain, keeping out the elements and granting the
wearer a +5 competence bonus on Stealth checks in such terrain. This armor also allows the wearer to
ignore the effects of a hot climate as if under the effects of
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [entangle](ref/spell/entangle), creator must be a
druid; **Price** 2,275 gp
