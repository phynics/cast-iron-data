**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 137,650 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

Invincible armor shifts between three different appearances, but always weighs the same and protects
as +2 [impervious](ref/magic-enh/impervious-armor)
[moderate fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-moderate)
[full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate). As an immediate action, the wearer can change the armor into
heavy plate that blocks slashing attacks (DR 10/bludgeoning or piercing), thickly layered chainmail
that blocks piercing attacks (DR 10/bludgeoning or slashing), or overlapping metal scales that block
bludgeoning attacks (DR 10/piercing or slashing). In addition, invincible armor provides the wearer
with 5 points of resistance to acid, cold, electricity, fire, and sonic, and is itself unharmed by
damage from any of these energy types.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate), [make whole](ref/spell/make-whole),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), and [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) or
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle); **Price** 69,650 gp
