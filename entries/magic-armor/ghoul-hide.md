**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 51\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 2,865 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

Typically crafted in the nation of Geb, this morbid +1 [hide](ref/eq-armor/hide) armor is stitched
from the flesh torn from the bodies of [ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul) servants as a punishment for
failing their masters in some manner. The wearer of this armor is immune to the paralytic touch of
ghouls, ghasts, and lacedons.

If the wearer is the subject of a bite attack or is swallowed whole, the attacker must succeed at a
DC 13 Fortitude save or contract [ghoul fever](ref/disease/ghoul-fever).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[ghoul touch](ref/spell/ghoul-touch); **Price** 1,515 gp
