**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 41,300 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This specialized suit of full plate is made from the tanned hide of a linnorm and acts as +4
[dragonhide plate](ref/eq-armor/dragonhide-plate). If a creature is slain while wearing such armor,
its killer is subject to a curse. The cursed creature must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or gain
vulnerability to fire until the curse is removed. This curse can be removed only by a
[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment), [miracle](ref/spell/miracle), or
[wish](ref/spell/wish) spell. This particular suit of armor is made from the hide of a
[crag linnorm](ref/monster/linnorm-crag-linnorm), though others exist, each with a different curse
effect similar to the curse of the associated linnorm.

Once per day as a swift action, the wear of this armor can summon forth the uncanny movement powers
of a linnorm, gaining the benefits of _[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement)_ for 10
minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[form of the dragon I](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-1),
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement), [major curse](ref/spell/curse-major);
**Price** 22,300 gp
