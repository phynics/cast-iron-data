**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 252\
**Aura** faint enchantment and evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 4,840 gp; **Weight** 40 lbs.

This +1 [chainmail](ref/eq-armor/chainmail) is decorated with a row of small metal disks, each
stamped with a tankard symbol. The wearer gains a +1 luck bonus on all saving throws and can use
_[moment of greatness](ref/spell/moment-of-greatness)_ once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor), [moment of greatness](ref/spell/moment-of-greatness);
**Price** 2,570 gp
