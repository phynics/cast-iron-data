**Aura** strong illusion and transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 25,160 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +2 [improved shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow-improved) [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) armor is
attractive, yet eerie. It grants its wearer the ability to use _[vanish](ref/spell/vanish)_ as a
[quickened](ref/feat/quicken-spell) [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) five times
per day. If a mythic wearer uses the _[vanish](ref/spell/vanish)_ ability, he can expend one use of
mythic power to also make himself incorporeal for 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [vanish](ref/spell/vanish),
[ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt); **Price** 12,660 gp
