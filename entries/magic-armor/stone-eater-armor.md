**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and evocation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 8,950 gp; **Weight** 75 lbs.

This suit of +1 [stoneplate](ref/eq-armor/stoneplate) bears a horned [gorgon's](ref/monster/gorgon)
head on each shoulder and the screaming face of a [medusa](ref/monster/medusa) on its breastplate.
The first suits of stone-eater armor found in the Inner Sea region were brought by explorers from
faraway Casmaron, along with wild tales of a lost city destroyed by a powerful medusa.

Whenever the wearer of stone-eater armor is subject to a petrifying effect, such as that caused by a
[flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone) spell, the effect is negated and the stone-eater armor's
enhancement bonus increases by 1 for 1 minute as the armor grows thicker and bulkier, covering more
of the wearer's body without hindering her movement.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[stone to flesh](ref/spell/stone-to-flesh); **Price** 5,450 gp
