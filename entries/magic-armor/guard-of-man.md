**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 51\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 17,180 gp; **Weight** 45 lbs.

This massive, imposing +2 [spell resistance (13)](ref/magic-enh/spell-resistance-13)
[tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) is a metal bulwark engraved with the main edicts of the
Laws of Man. The [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) special ability of this shield works
only against divine spells and does not ward the bearer against arcane magic. While held, the shield
grants a +4 resistance bonus on Fortitude saving throws against divine spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

Three times per day as a swift action, when the wielder of a guard of man is in the area of
[channeled energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) (regardless of whether she is excluded by an effect
such as [Selective Channeling](ref/feat/selective-channeling)), she can rebuke the god powering the
ability. Using this ability causes the text on the guard of man to glow and decreases the amount of
channeled energy by half (minimum 1). This reduction affects the channeled energy itself, and thus
reduces the effect on all targets within its area. The power of the channeled energy can be further
reduced with a successful Will saving throw, as normal.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[spell resistance](ref/spell/spell-resistance), creator must be an atheist; **Price** 8,680 gp
