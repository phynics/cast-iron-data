**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 36,155 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

Once per round as an immediate action, this +3 [arrow deflection](ref/magic-enh/arrow-deflection)
[buckler](ref/eq-armor/buckler) can be used to block an incoming melee attack. When used in this
way, the buckler's enhancement bonus increases by +2 against that attack. The buckler's user must
decide to use this ability before the attack roll is made. When the shield's user blocks a melee
attack in this way, it cannot use its arrow deflection special ability for 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shield](ref/spell/shield); **Price** 18,155 gp
