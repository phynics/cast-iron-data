**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 53\
**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 6,950 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Worn by enforcers and acolytes of the living god Razmir, this +1
[chain shirt](ref/eq-armor/chain-shirt) appears as a fine hooded robe made of silk of a color
appropriate to the wearer's rank within the church as if it had the
[glamered](ref/magic-enh/glamered) special ability (it appears white when worn by anyone who doesn't
follow Razmir). Used primarily as a means of appearing to be protected by the false god's power, the
armor is treated as a +3 [breastplate](ref/eq-armor/breastplate) against attacks made by any enemy
who has not seen through the illusion. Unlike with standard glamered armor, any creature that
successfully hits the target with a melee weapon is considered to have interacted with the illusion,
and can see the armor's true form with a successful DC 16 Will save.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self); **Price** 3,600 gp
