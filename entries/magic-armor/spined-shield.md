**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 133, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 467\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 5,580 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) is covered in spines. It acts as a
normal [spiked shield](ref/eq-armorenh/shield-spikes). On command up to three times per day, the
shield's wearer can fire one of the shield's spines. A fired spine has a +1 enhancement bonus, a
range increment of 120 feet, and deals 1d10 points of damage (19-20/×2). Fired spines regenerate
each day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile); **Price** 2,875 gp
