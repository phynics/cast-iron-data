**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 127\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 61,300 gp; **Weight** 40 lbs.

Mail of malevolence hangs in rags and tatters and acts as nonmagical chainmail to wearers who lack
at least a moderate evil aura. When worn by a sufficiently evil character, it instead functions as
+2 [unrighteous](ref/magic-enh/unrighteous) [chainmail](ref/eq-armor/chainmail). Mail of malevolence
does not bestow a negative level on good wearers.

The wearer of mail of malevolence gains SR 21, but only against spells with the good descriptor. In
addition, whenever the wearer [channels negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) to harm living
creatures, she heals a like amount of damage herself regardless of whether she is living or undead.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[righteous might](ref/spell/righteous-might), [spell resistance](ref/spell/spell-resistance);
**Price** 30,800 gp
