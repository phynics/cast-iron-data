**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 11,462 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

> _The following suit of armor is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
> ethnicity._

The Shoanti craft this +1 [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) [klar](ref/eq-armor/klar) from the skull of a
horned spirestalker. Its enhancement bonus applies to attack rolls and damage rolls for attacks made
with the blade, as well as to its shield bonus to AC. If the wielder confirms a critical hit against
a creature that is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC, the target takes an additional 1d6 points of
[bleed](ref/umr/bleed) damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed), [keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), creator must be a Shoanti; **Price**
5,962 gp
