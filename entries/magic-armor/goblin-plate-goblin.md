**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 224\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 23,650 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

> _The following suit of armor is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
> ethnicity._

Normally found sized for Small humanoids, this battered and rusted suit of +1
[fire resistance](ref/magic-enh/energy-resistance) [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) resists any
attempts to polish out its blemishes. When a critical hit is confirmed against the wearer with a
manufactured weapon, natural weapon, or unarmed strike, there is a 50% chance that the armor negates
the critical hit. Such a blow knocks the armor out of alignment, giving it the
[broken](ref/misc/broken) condition until the wearer spends 1d4+1 minutes refitting the pieces (or 1
minute if another person assists in the process). This does not otherwise harm the armor. While out
of alignment, the goblin plate cannot negate critical hits. Broken armor provides only half the
usual AC bonus (rounded down), and any armor check penalty is doubled.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), [shatter](ref/spell/shatter), creator must be a goblin;
**Price** 12,650 gp
