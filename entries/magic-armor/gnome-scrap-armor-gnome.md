**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 224\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 11,175 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

> _The following suit of armor is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
> ethnicity._

A suit of gnome scrap armor protects its wearer as +2
[studded leather](ref/eq-armor/studded-leather) armor, but myriad metal trinkets festoon it in place
of studs. A number of these badges bear a special dweomer; each such badge can be removed as a move
action, at which point it turns into a full-sized version of the trinket. The wearer automatically
recognizes which badges have this property, and only the wearer can detach them.

A fully stocked suit of gnome scrap armor holds 20 badges representing the following gear: a flask
of [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid), two flasks of [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), a
[block and tackle](ref/eq-misc/block-and-tackle), a buckler, a [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar), a
[heavy pick](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-pick), a folded [net](ref/eq-weapon/net), a potion of
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), a potion of
[lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser), a [shovel](ref/eq-misc/shovel-common), two lit
[sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod), two [tanglefoot bags](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag), a
[throwing axe](ref/eq-weapon/throwing-axe), a [thunderstone](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone), a
[warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer), a [bottle of fine wine](ref/eq-misc/wine-fine-bottle), and a
[winter blanket](ref/eq-misc/blanket). When the armor has 10 or fewer badges remaining, it is
reduced to +1 studded leather armor until sufficient badges are returned. If all the patches are
removed, the armor becomes nonmagical [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-armor) studded leather armor.

While the armor retains any magic, the wearer can return badges to the armor by casting
_[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item)_ on the appropriate object while wearing the armor and
touching the object to the armor. This can even convert appropriate potions into badges, despite
_[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item)_ not normally working on magic items. Only items exactly
corresponding to missing badges can be merged with the armor. Merging a potion reduces the potion's
caster level to the minimum caster level for that spell. Items produced by gnome scrap armor match
the size of the armor, so the typical [gnome](ref/race/gnome)-sized version of the suit produces
items sized for Small creatures.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item), creator must be a gnome; **Price** 5,675 gp
