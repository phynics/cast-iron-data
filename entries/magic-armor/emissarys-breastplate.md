**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 11,350 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

When not being worn, this simple steel +1 [breastplate](ref/eq-armor/breastplate) appears to be
typical magic armor. When worn by a [cavalier](ref/class/cavalier) with the banner class feature, an
emissary's breastplate automatically displays the cavalier's heraldry and counts as his banner. As
long as the cavalier is not in disguise, he is considered to be displaying his banner while wearing
the armor. Additionally, the wearer can expend a use of his challenge ability to speak on behalf of
himself, his lord, or other agent who has given him the authority to do so. This allows him to use
_[tongues](ref/spell/tongues)_ (as per the spell) and issue a single _[command](ref/spell/command)_
(as per the spell).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark), [command](ref/spell/command), [tongues](ref/spell/tongues),
creator must have 5 ranks in Knowledge (nobility); **Price** 5,850 gp
