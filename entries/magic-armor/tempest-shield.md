**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 15,170 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

The face of this +1 [bashing](ref/magic-enh/bashing)
[heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) swirls with the image of swirling clouds,
driving rain, or falling snow. When used to make a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) attack, the
shield's wielder can make a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) maneuver as a free action against her
target without provoking an attack of opportunity from the creature being bull rushed. Once per day
on command, the wielder can cause the tempest shield to emit a stream of high-powered air as the
_[gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind); **Price** 8,170
gp
