**Sources:** Cheliax, Empire of Devils pg 20\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 17,175 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

Devised by Her Infernal Majestrix, Abrogail I, this +3
[studded leather](ref/eq-armor/studded-leather) armor is lavishly adorned with a bound
[swarm of hellwasps](ref/monster/hellwasp-swarm). Once ranking among the most spectacular pieces of
clothing in Cheliax, the discomfort caused by during the creation of this corset is as legendary as
the protection it offers.

The wasps bound to the corset prevent the first 20 points of damage you take each day (resetting at
midnight). Once the wasps have prevented 20 points of damage for the day, they angrily animate and
swarm around you, and you must make a DC 18 Fortitude save or be [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for
1 round. After 1 round of activity, the swarm then returns to the corset and goes dormant, and this
ability to prevent damage does not function until it recovers at midnight.

The swarm cannot be targeted separately from the armor and cannot be killed unless the armor is
destroyed (not just broken).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[lesser planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-lesser), [summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm), caster
level 9th; **Price** 8,675 gp
