**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 115\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 16,150 gp; **Weight** 20 lbs.

This set of +2 [elven chain](ref/eq-armor/elven-chain) grants its wearer a +4 _resistance_ bonus on
saving throws against spells, [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and other
effects created by worshipers and minions of a specific demon lord, chosen when the armor is
created. Against such foes, the armor's enhancement bonus increases to +4. The first suit of this
armor was created quite recently by Perelir for her close friend Malindil as part of the ranger's
vengeful crusade against [Abraxas](ref/faith/abraxas); since then, many other suits focused against
other powerful demons (particularly the nascent demon lord [Treerazer](ref/monster/treerazer)) have
been crafted.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance); **Price** 10,650 gp
