**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 23\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 9,480 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This elaborate +2 [silken ceremonial](ref/eq-armor/silken-ceremonial) armor is made of layers of
silken braids and is often worn by diplomats and royalty when more drab or utilitarian armor is
socially inappropriate. The wearer of writhing armor can cause it to unravel long braids of silk
that twist and lash out of their own accord. The wearer can use one such braid to make attacks as a
+1 [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip). The constant writhing of the braids also grants the wearer a +4 dodge
bonus to AC for 1 round when taking the [withdraw](ref/rule/withdraw) action as a full-round action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[animate rope](ref/spell/animate-rope); **Price** 4,980 gp
