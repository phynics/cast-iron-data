**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 252\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 2,780 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) armor usually looks like heavy protective work clothing,
such as the garb of a professional blacksmith, butcher, or woodcutter. Once per day when an adjacent
ally falls unconscious, the wearer can use _[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds)_ on
that ally as a swift action; if the wearer falls unconscious and this ability has not already been
expended for the day, it automatically triggers on the wearer.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds); **Price** 1,470 gp
