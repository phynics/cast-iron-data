**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 18,300 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

The plates of this suit of +1 [steel lamellar](ref/eq-armor/lamellar-steel) armor are etched with
the complete Laws of Man from the secular nation of Rahadoum. Suits of armor of the Pure Legion are
created for high-ranking members in the eponymous organization and grant protection against the two
most common scourges of Rahadoum: divine magic and desert heat. Such armor grants its wearer
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) against divine spells equal to 5 + the wearer's base
attack bonus. It also protects the wearer from [heat dangers](ref/rule/heat-dangers) as if she were
affected by a continuous _[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ spell. Additionally, once
per day when the wearer of a suit of armor of the Pure Legion fails a Will save against a
mind-affecting spell cast by a divine spellcaster, she can attempt another save as a free action on
the following round to end the spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [mind blank](ref/spell/mind-blank); **Price** 9,300 gp
