**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 131\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 7,170 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +2 [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) has a distinct curve to its shape and
often bears the device of a valiant knight. The wearer of this shield gains a +2 bonus on Reflex
saves against effects that originate or [burst](ref/misc/burst) from a point outside his own square,
as though he had [cover](ref/rule/cover). It does not protect against [spread](ref/misc/spread)
effects. This bonus does not stack with that granted by actual cover or give cover to any creature
other than the wearer. In addition, the dragonslayer's shield grants its wielder a +2 morale bonus
on Will saves against the [frightful presence](ref/umr/frightful-presence) ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear), [shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith); **Price** 3,670
gp
