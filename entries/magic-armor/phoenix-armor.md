**Aura** moderate evocation and transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 36,130 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This brilliant golden +2 [fire resistance](ref/magic-enh/energy-resistance)
[full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) is decorated with an ornate feather motif curling across its
surface and seems to radiate warmth. Once per day, the wearer can invoke the name of the
[phoenix](ref/monster/phoenix) that blessed the armor and bring the might of that phoenix against
the wearer's foe. Activating the armor causes it to ignite, wreathing the wearer in red-gold flames
as if under the effects of _[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield)._ In addition, great golden wings
unfold out of the back of the armor, allowing the wearer to _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_ as per the spell.
Both of these effects last for 9 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield), [fly](ref/spell/fly); **Price** 18,890 gp
