**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 47\
**Aura** strong abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 22,165 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

This grotesque suit of +1 [hide](ref/eq-armor/hide) armor is stitched together from the hides of
[gugs](ref/monster/gug) from the underworld of the [Dreamlands](ref/rule/dimension-of-dreams) and
the nightmare realm of [Leng](ref/rule/leng). The wearer of a suit of gug hide gains the benefits of
Leng's [basic infusion](ref/feat/planar-infusion) when in an underground environment. Once per day
as a standard action, the wearer can cause his arms to each split into two arms at the elbow, each
bearing a furred hand like that of a horrid subterranean gug. The wearer must designate one hand as
his primary hand; all others are considered off hands. He can use any of his hands for other
purposes that require free hands. A user who has [Two Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting)
is treated as if he had [Multiweapon Fighting](ref/feat/multiweapon-fighting) while these extra arms
are activated. This ability lasts for 10 minutes and has no effect if the wearer already has more
than two arms.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[alter self](ref/spell/alter-self), [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish); **Price** 11,165 gp
