**Aura** moderate abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 54,115 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

This +3 [hide](ref/eq-armor/hide) armor is made from tiger skin. It is magically treated to make it
easier to move in, reducing its armor check penalty to 0, reducing its arcane spell failure chance
to 10%, and increasing its maximum Dexterity bonus to +6. It grants DR 5/- against natural weapon
attacks. In addition, the wearer can use the [pounce](ref/umr/pounce) special attack, but only
during the first round she can act in any combat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3); **Price** 29,115 gp
