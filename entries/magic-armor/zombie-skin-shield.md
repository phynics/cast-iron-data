**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 133\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 2,159 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [light steel shield](ref/eq-armor/light-steel-shield) is wrapped in a layer of gray skin.
Once per day, the wearer can animate the skin as a human [zombie](ref/template/zombie) under her
control, as if she had created it with _[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead),_ for a period of 5
minutes. When the time expires, the zombie returns to the shield. If the zombie is destroyed or
cannot reach the shield by the time it expires, placing the shield on a Medium humanoid corpse for 1
hour recreates the shield's skin.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead); **Price** 1,159 gp
