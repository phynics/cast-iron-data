**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 6,160 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This form-fitting black +1 [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) features eight ridged support ribs along
the back. On command, the arachnid harness sprouts eight enormous spidery legs from these ribs.
These legs allow the armor's wearer to attempt Climb checks at full speed without needing to use her
hands or feet. Additionally, while the legs are deployed, the armor's wearer doesn't lose her
Dexterity bonus to AC while climbing, and need not succeed at a Climb check to avoid falling if she
takes damage while climbing.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb); **Price** 3,160 gp
