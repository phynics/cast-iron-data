**Sources:** Lost Treasures pg 26\
**Aura** moderate enchantment and necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 60,500 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This suit of +4 [bolstering](ref/magic-enh/bolstering) [grinding](ref/magic-enh/grinding)
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) bristles with long
thorns and barbs along the edges of its heavy plates. These are gained from the grinding special
ability, and also act as +1 [cruel](ref/magic-enh/cruel)
[armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes). As a swift action whenever Ebon Thorn's spikes damage
an opponent, its wearer can attempt a Intimidate check to demoralize that opponent. The wearer
doesn't take the normal -4 penalty on Intimidate checks made against larger opponents.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear), [death knell](ref/spell/death-knell),
[heroism](ref/spell/heroism), [keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Price** 38,500 gp
