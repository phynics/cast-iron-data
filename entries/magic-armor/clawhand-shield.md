**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 211\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 8,158 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This shield is made of bone and ivory, but has been imbued with magic to function as a +2
[mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) (though it
is not considered to be made of metal). The shield has stylized animal claws encircling its outer
rim. If the wearer is [grappled, pinned](ref/rule/grapple-conditions), or
[swallowed whole](ref/umr/swallow-whole), as a standard action he can cause the claws to animate and
make a single melee attack dealing 1d8 points of damage + the wielder's Strength modifier (1d6 + his
Strength modifier for a Small wielder) without requiring a successful combat maneuver check to do
so. This is considered a weapon attack with a one-handed weapon the wielder is proficient with, not
a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash).

Additionally, the claws can animate to perform the somatic components of a spell as long as the arm
holding the shield would be able to provide these components if it weren't carrying a shield. As a
result, the shield has no arcane spell failure chance.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1); **Price** 4,158 gp
