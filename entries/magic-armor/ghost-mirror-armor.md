**Sources:** Pathfinder \#51: The Hungry Storm pg 62\
**Aura** strong abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 22,675 gp; **Weight** 45 lbs.

Crafted by Uqtaal spirit-shamans, this ancient +1 [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-armor)
[four-mirror armor](ref/eq-armor/four-mirror) consists of four polished steel plates harnessed
together with leather shoulder straps. Two round plates protect the wearer's front and back, while
two smaller rectangular plates cover the sides of the torso. The wearer of ghost mirror armor can
cast _[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch)_ (caster level 1st) three times per day. In addition, when
the wearer is attacked by an incorporeal undead creature, the armor automatically casts
_[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil)_ on the wearer as an immediate action up to
once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch), [etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness),
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil); **Price** 11,435 gp
