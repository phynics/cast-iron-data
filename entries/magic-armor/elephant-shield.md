**Aura** moderate evocation and transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 21,340 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This +2 [leather madu](ref/eq-armor/madu-shield-leather) is designed in the form of a bull
elephant's head, complete with imposing ivory tusks. The shield provides a +3 circumstance bonus on
combat maneuver checks made to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) opponents. If used as a weapon, the
elephant shield grants a +1 enhancement bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls and deals damage as
if it were one size category larger than it is. Once per day, the bearer of the elephant shield can
have the trunk of the shield lash out, allowing the wielder to attempt a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple)
combat maneuver check against a foe as a swift action. This combat maneuver doesn't provoke attacks
of opportunity.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1), [force punch](ref/spell/force-punch); **Price** 10,840 gp
