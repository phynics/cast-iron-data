**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 7\
**Aura** moderate transmutation (see text); **CL** 7th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 19,650 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

This dull black suit of +2 [scale mail](ref/eq-armor/scale-mail) does not impose an armor check
penalty on the wearer's Stealth checks, though other armor check penalties apply as normal.
Additionally, infiltrator's mail reacts to [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) as though it were
nonmagical and, when worn, extends this effect to all other magic items worn or held by the wearer
unless the viewer uses [identify](ref/spell/identify) or a similar effect while examining the items.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), [magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura); **Price** 13,650 gp
