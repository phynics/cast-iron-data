**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 47\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 63,150 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

Originally crafted by [jyoti](ref/monster/jyoti) armorsmiths of the Positive Energy Plane, this
gleaming suit of +1 [radiant](ref/magic-enh/radiant) [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) has a
mirror-like sheen that reflects even tiny sparks of light into brilliant ribbons of blazing color.
Its ability to shine as brightly as a torch cannot be suppressed unless the wearer is in magical
darkness. Once per day as an immediate action while charging, the wearer can transform her body into
brilliant light. The wearer becomes incorporeal until she arrives at the end of her charge and makes
her physical attacks, and she can charge through any creatures and obstacles that do not hinder
incorporeal creatures (although she must have line of sight to her target when she begins her
[charge](ref/rule/charge), as normal).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[sunburst](ref/spell/sunburst); **Price** 32,400 gp
