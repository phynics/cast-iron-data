**Sources:** Sargava, the Lost Colony pg 29\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 19,393 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This large wood-and-hide shield is similar to other Mwangi shields, but the totem animal painted on
the shield's face seems to move when the shield is carried. Crafted by Mwangi shamans to grant its
wielder the power of tribal totem spirits, a Mwangi spirit shield functions as a +1
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-armor)
[light wooden shield](ref/eq-armor/light-wooden-shield). Once per day on command, the shield can
grant its bearer _[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength)_ for 1 minute.

Other versions of the Mwangi spirit shield exist; some are known to grant
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace) or [bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance) to their
wielders instead of _[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness); **Price** 9,733
gp
