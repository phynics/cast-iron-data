**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 38,312 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This metal +1 [klar](ref/eq-armor/klar) is shaped like the head of a storm [roc](ref/monster/roc),
whose sharp feathers serve as the klar's spikes, and it hums and crackles like a distant stormy sky.
When used to make a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) attack, a Storval's fang deals an additional
1d6 points of sonic damage. In addition, the wielder gains electricity resistance 5. When the
wielder confirms a critical hit with a Storval's fang, it unleashes a thunderclap,
[deafening](ref/misc/deafened) the target of the critical hit for 1 minute unless that target
succeeds at a DC 16 Fortitude saving throw.

If the wielder of a Storval's fang also wields a Storval's thunder and uses the
[Thunder and Fang](ref/feat/thunder-and-fang) feat, each weapon deals an additional 1d6 points of
electricity damage, the DCs to negate their critical hit effects increase by 2, and a creature that
fails such a saving throw is also pushed back 10 feet and knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt), [resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy),
[sound burst](ref/spell/sound-burst), creator must be of Shoanti descent; **Price** 19,312 gp
