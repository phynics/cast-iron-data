**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 131, Advanced Race Guide pg 88\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 13,170 gp; **Weight** 7 lbs.

This gold-edged +2 [blinding](ref/magic-enh/blinding)
[heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) is impossibly light and handy despite its
size. It has no armor check penalty or arcane spell failure chance, and it allows the wielder to use
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ on himself once per day. A creature wearing
[celestial armor](ref/magic-armor/celestial-armor) while wielding a celestial shield may command the
armor to provide _[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight)_ rather than [fly](ref/spell/fly)
once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight); **Price**
6,670 gp
