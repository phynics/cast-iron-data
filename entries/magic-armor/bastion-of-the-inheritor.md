**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 142\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 22,170 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [rallying](ref/magic-enh/rallying) [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield)
prominently displays the sword emblem of the god [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), and functions as a
holy symbol for her worshipers. When using this shield, the wearer reduces the extra damage she
takes from [anarchic](ref/magic-enh/anarchic) weapons, smite good, smite law, and
[unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) weapons by her tier, to a minimum of 0. When its wearer uses the
smite evil class feature, she can expend one use of mythic power to extend the deflection bonus to
AC she gains against her target to all allies in a 10-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) centered
on her. This bonus lasts for 1 minute or until the smite evil ends, whichever comes first.
