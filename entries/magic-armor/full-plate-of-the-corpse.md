**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 211\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 35,650 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This suit of +2 [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) has been etched and stained in such a way that
it resembles the form and shape of a decomposed body that is encased in a suit of armor. The wearer
is effectively hidden from non-intelligent undead, which can't see, hear, or smell him; even
extraordinary or supernatural senses capabilities can't penetrate the magical shroud. An intelligent
undead creature can attempt a single Will saving throw (DC 14) to penetrate the ward. All affected
undead behave as if the wearer simply isn't there.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead); **Price** 18,650 gp
