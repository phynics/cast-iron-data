**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 2,625 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This finely crafted +1 [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) [parade](ref/eq-armor/parade) armor from
Kyonin is as light and comfortable as regular clothing. An Iadaran dress uniform counts as a
[courtier's outfit](ref/eq-misc/courtiers-outfit) (including appropriate jewelry) when the wearer is
interacting with nobles or courtiers, in addition to providing a +2 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy
and Intimidate checks to influence people from the elven home nation. Further, the wearer also gains
a +2 competence bonus on Perception and Sense Motive checks to intuit that a creature is concealing
its true beliefs, feelings, or motives.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[owl's wisdom](ref/spell/owls-wisdom), creator must be an [elf](ref/race/elf); **Price** 2,265 gp
