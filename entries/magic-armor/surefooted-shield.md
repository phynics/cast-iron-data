**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** 14,200 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

The face of this +2 [heavy steel shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-steel-shield) is lined with sheets of
riveted steel to absorb more impact. A surefooted shield grants its wielder a +2 bonus on his CMD
versus awesome blow, [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), and [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat
maneuvers and on Reflex saves against [trample](ref/umr/trample) attacks.

Additionally, up to three times per day, whenever an enemy's melee attack misses the wielder, an
enemy fails a combat maneuver against the wielder, or the wielder succeeds at a Reflex save against
a trample attack, the wielder can move 5 feet as an immediate action; this movement does not provoke
attacks of opportunity and does not count as a 5-foot step.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[abjuring step](ref/spell/abjuring-step); **Price** 7,185 gp
