**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 47\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 24,175 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

This tattered suit of +1 [deathless](ref/magic-enh/deathless)
[studded leather](ref/eq-armor/studded-leather) has a black leather hood attached to the neck.
Although the face of the hood is open and doesn't impair the wearer's senses, the wearer's face is
always hidden in deep shadow regardless of the ambient light level. Once per day as an immediate
action, when the wearer reduces an undead creature to 0 or fewer hit points, the wearer can draw
upon the departed creature's essence to gain treat her level as 2 higher spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with the death descriptor and spells and
spell-like abilities from the Death and Repose domains. This effect lasts for 1 hour.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward), [undeath to death](ref/spell/undeath-to-death); **Price** 12,175
gp
