**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 224\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 15,350 gp; **Weight** 20 lbs.

> _The following suit of armor is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
> ethnicity._

Overlapping scallop shells form this suit of +2 [scale mail](ref/eq-armor/scale-mail). While the
wearer is swimming in water, the coat of shells restricts her movement speed as light armor instead
of as medium armor. Whether worn in or out of water, a coat of shells requires medium armor
proficiency.

When out of the water, a coat of shells keeps its wearer's skin wet and cool. This provides the
wearer resist fire 5 and the benefits of the _[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ spell
against hot temperatures only, and it doubles the amount of time a wearer with the water dependent
racial trait or [water dependency](ref/umr/water-dependency) can spend out of water without harm.
None of these benefits apply in water.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[create water](ref/spell/create-water), [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement), creator must be either a gillman or a merfolk;
**Price** 7,850 gp
