**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 250\
**Aura** faint conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 5,240 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 [bitter](ref/magic-enh/bitter) [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather) armor looks much like a
blacksmith's apron or an alchemist's smock, and has many small pockets suitable for holding
extracts, potions, or poisons. Once per day, the wearer can use
_[negate aroma](ref/spell/negate-aroma)_ (lasting 1 hour). This effect suspends the armor's bitter
special ability if used on the wearer. Once per day, the wearer can pull a
[tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag) from one of the apron's pockets, although the bag
crumbles into dust if it is not used within 1 minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[negate aroma](ref/spell/negate-aroma), [stinking cloud](ref/spell/stinking-cloud),
[web](ref/spell/web); **Price** 2,700 gp
