**Sources:** Black Markets pg 15\
**Aura** strong necromancy and transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot**; **Price** 6,400 gp; **Weight** —

A single necrograft leg allows its host to [travel overland](ref/rule/overland-movement) for 1-1/2
times longer than normal before needing to attempt Constitution checks to continue running or to
avoid nonlethal damage from a forced march. A humanoid with two necrograft legs can travel overland
twice as longer than normal before needing to attempt these same checks. In addition, each
necrograft leg grants its host a +2 bonus to CMD against [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) and
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) attempts and provides a 50% chance to ignore
[caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead), [regenerate](ref/spell/regenerate), creator must have 8
ranks in Heal; **Cost** 3200 gp
