**Aura** strong necromancy and transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot**; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** —

An arm of undead flesh allows its host to lift up to 1-1/2 times his
[maximum load](ref/rule/carrying-capacity) over his head. A humanoid with two necrograft arms can
lift up to twice his maximum load over his head. Each necrograft arm also provides its host with a
[natural slam attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (replacing any natural attacks that limb may have
once had) that deals 1d4 points of bludgeoning damage (1d3 for Small creatures) as a secondary
attack. If a host attacks with only slam attacks in a round, the slams are instead considered
primary attacks. A necrograft arm is clumsy, imposing a cumulative -2 circumstance penalty on all
Craft, Disable Device, and Sleight of Hand checks per limb replaced, as well as on relevant
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) checks (such as for string and wind).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead), [regenerate](ref/spell/regenerate), creator must have 8
ranks in Heal; **Cost** 6000 gp
