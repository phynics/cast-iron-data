**&L&Conflicted Identity (Ex)&FN&**

**Flavor:** The vigilante's mind is in a constant state of turmoil, which makes him even more
ethically and morally fluid than other vigilantes.

**Passive:** When targeted by a harmful spell or effect that would affect the vigilante in his
current identity but not his other identity (or when he would suffer a lesser effect in his other
identity), he has a 50% chance of being affected as though he were in his other identity.

**Special:** This chance is rolled when the spell or effect is cast or triggered and before any
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) roll, if applicable. For example, if a vigilante has a
lawful-neutral social identity and a neutral vigilante identity, he has a 50% chance of taking only
half damage from a [chaos hammer](ref/spell/chaos-hammer) spell while in his social identity, since
he would always take only half damage from a _[chaos hammer](ref/spell/chaos-hammer)_ spell when in
his vigilante identity.
