**&L&Hidden Magic (Ex)&FN&**

**At 11th Level:** The vigilante and any magic items he is carrying appear to be nonmagical (as if
he had cast [greater magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura-greater)).

**Passive:** The auras of magic items the vigilante is carrying are hidden (as per
[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura)). The vigilante can suppress or reactivate this effect as a
**standard action**.

**Prereq:** 5th-level vigilante
