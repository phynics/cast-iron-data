**Transformation Sequence (Su)**

**Ability:** The vigilante can complete the switch between his identities in 5 rounds, improving to
a **standard action** if he has the [quick change](ref/soctalent/quick-change) social talent, and to
a **swift action** if he has the [immediate change](ref/soctalent/immediate-change) social talent.
However, the transformation causes quite a spectacle, involving loud sounds or music, brilliant
colorful energies, and swift motions.

**Flavor:** The vigilante's transformation between identities is assisted by magic. This makes it
faster than usual, but also more noticeable.

**Prereq:** Able to cast spells or [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)

**Special:** A vigilante with the [magical child](ref/arc-vigilante/magical-child) archetype
effectively gets this social talent for free at 1st level.
