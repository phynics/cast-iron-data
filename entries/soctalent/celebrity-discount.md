The vigilante's social identity is popular enough to receive discounts in his area of
[renown](ref/soctalent/renown). Whenever he buys an item in his area of renown that costs 500 gp or
less, he can buy it at 90% of the market price, rather than the full price. If he has the
[great renown](ref/soctalent/great-renown) social talent, the gp limit increases to 2,000 gp, and if
he possesses the [incredible renown](ref/soctalent/incredible-renown) social talent, the gp limit
increases to 8,000 gp. A [vigilante](ref/class/vigilante) must be at least 3rd level and have the
[renown](ref/soctalent/renown) social talent to select this talent.
