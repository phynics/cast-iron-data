**Companion to the Lonely (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, the vigilante can spend at least 1 hour engaged in acts of physical
pleasure with a willing partner to gain a pool of morale points equal to his Charisma bonus or his
partner's Charisma bonus, whichever is higher. For the next 24 hours, the vigilante can spend a
morale point as an **immediate action** to roll a Charisma-based skill check or a Will saving throw
again after rolling the die but before learning the consequences; he must take the second result
even if it is lower.

**Flavor:** Whether religiously motivated, as are followers of [Arshea](ref/faith/arshea),
[Calistria](ref/faith/calistria), or [Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn), or for purely carnal reasons,
physical intimacy helps the vigilante cope with the loneliness of his double life.
