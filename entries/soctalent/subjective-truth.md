**&L&Subjective Truth (Ex)&FN&**

**Flavor:** The vigilante's disparate identities allow him to defeat magic that detects lies.

**Passive:** As long as what he says is true from the point of view of his current identity, it
detects as true to effects such as [discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies), and he can say it in
effects such as [zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth) that force him to speak the truth.

**Prereq:** 9th-level vigilante, [feign innocence](ref/soctalent/feign-innocence)
