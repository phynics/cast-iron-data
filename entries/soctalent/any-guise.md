**Any Guise (Su)**

**Ability:** A vigilante can use his _everyman_ social talent to disguise himself as any specific
person, even a king or high priest. Furthermore, if the actual individual wouldn't normally be able
to be found via magic (such as by being dead or protected from divinations designed to locate the
individual), such divinations always find the vigilante instead.

**Prereq:** 17th-level vigilante, [everyman](ref/soctalent/everyman)
