A vigilante can use his [everyman](ref/soctalent/everyman) social talent to disguise himself as any
specific person, even a king or high priest. Furthermore, if the actual individual wouldn't normally
be able to be found via magic (such as by being dead or protected from divinations designed to
locate the individual), such divinations always find the vigilante instead. A
[vigilante](ref/class/vigilante) must be at least 17th level and have the
[everyman](ref/soctalent/everyman) social talent to select this talent.
