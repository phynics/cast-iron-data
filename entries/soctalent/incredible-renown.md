**Incredible Renown (Ex)**

**Ability:** He can gain _renown_ in a single community of up to 25,000 individuals (a
[large city](ref/rule/the-shape-of-civilization)) or up to two smaller cities of no more than 10,000
individuals each (two small cities). The bonus while he is in his social identity remains unchanged,
but the circumstance bonus to [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) from his _great renown_ social
talent while in he is in his vigilante identity increases to +8.

**Flavor:** The vigilante is incredibly famous in both identities.

**Prereq:** 11th-level vigilante, [great renown](ref/soctalent/great-renown)
