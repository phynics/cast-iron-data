The vigilante is incredibly famous in both identities. He can gain renown in a single community of
up to 25,000 individuals (a [large city](ref/rule/the-shape-of-civilization)) or up to two smaller
cities of no more than 10,000 individuals each (two small cities). The bonus while he is in his
social identity remains unchanged, but the circumstance bonus to [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)
from his [great renown](ref/soctalent/great-renown) social talent while in he is in his vigilante
identity increases to +8. A [vigilante](ref/class/vigilante) must be at least 11th level and have
the [great renown](ref/soctalent/great-renown) social talent to select this talent.
