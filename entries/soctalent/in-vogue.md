The vigilante's crafting or professional business is always at the height of the local trends,
allowing the vigilante to gain more profits than usual. Goods he crafts with a
[Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) skill he chose with [social grace](ref/soctalent/social-grace) are
worth 1/3 more gp than normal due to his celebrity, without increasing the cost to create. Whenever
he uses a [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) skill he chose with social grace to make money, he
makes twice as much money. A [vigilante](ref/class/vigilante) must be at least 5th level and have
both the [double time](ref/soctalent/double-time) and social grace social talents to take this
talent.
