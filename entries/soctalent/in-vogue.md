**&L&In Vogue (Ex)&FN&**

**Flavor:** The vigilante's crafting or professional business is always at the height of the local
trends, allowing the vigilante to gain more profits than usual.

**Passive:** Goods he crafts with a [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) skill he chose with _social grace_
are worth 1/3 more gp than normal due to his celebrity, without increasing the cost to create.
Whenever he uses a [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any) skill he chose with _social grace_ to make
money, he makes twice as much money.

**Prereq:** 5th-level vigilante, [double time](ref/soctalent/double-time),
[social grace](ref/soctalent/social-grace)
