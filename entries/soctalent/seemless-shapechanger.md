**Seemless Shapechanger (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The vigilante seamlessly adopts any persona he assumes with magic.

**Passive:** The vigilante adds his _seamless guise_ bonus to the bonus on
[Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks that he gains to assume the shape of another creature with a
[polymorph](ref/spelldef/polymorph) spell or effect.

**Prereq:** Must have the [shapechanger](ref/subtype/shapechanger) subtype
