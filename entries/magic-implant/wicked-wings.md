**Sources:** Pathfinder \#74: Sword of Valor pg 74\
**Aura** moderate transmutation \[evil\]; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 55,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

### Description

These ragged wings may be bird-like with moldy feathers, or tattered and membranous like the wings
of a bat. The recipient's shoulder blades and collarbones must be broken in order to attach the
wicked wings - breaking them deals 2d4 points of Strength and
[Dexterity damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to the recipient and renders both arms useless
until the ability damage heals. The recipient gains a fly speed equal to its normal land speed with
average maneuverability. In addition, when not flying a creature with wicked wings is considered to
have [Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows) as a bonus feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1); **Cost** 27,500 gp
