**Aura** strong conjuration \[evil\]; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lbs.

### Description

This implant appears as a demonic eye, tongue, finger, ear, or nose floating in a small jar of
transparent fluid. In order to gain the benefit of this implant, the contents of the jar - thick
fluid, body part, and all - must be consumed as a full-round action. This immediately grants a
permanent +8 racial bonus on all Perception checks (note that as a racial bonus, this does not stack
with any other racial bonuses a creature might already possess). After gaining this bonus, the part
of the creature's body corresponding to the body part eaten changes to match the eaten part - eyes
might become red, ears might grow long and pointed, a tongue might become forked, and so on.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [regenerate](ref/spell/regenerate); **Cost** 10,000 gp
