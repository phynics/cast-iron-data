**Sources:** Book of the Damned - Volume 2: Lords of Chaos pg 44\
**Aura** faint evocation \[evil\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 800 gp; **Weight** 1 lbs.

### Description

A character who drinks a dose of this black, bubbling ichor becomes [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated)
for 1d6 rounds if he fails a DC 15 Fortitude save. He also gains an enhanced capacity to affect
[demons](ref/family/demon) with his magical attacks for 1 hour - during this time, demons suffer a
-2 penalty on their saving throws against the user's spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and the user gains a +2 bonus on caster level
checks made to penetrate a demon's [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). Demon blood is
somewhat addictive, and every day for a week after a user stops daily doses of the ichor, he must
make a DC 15 Fortitude save to avoid taking 1 point of
[Wisdom damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) and being [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for the
entire day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate); **Cost** 400 gp
