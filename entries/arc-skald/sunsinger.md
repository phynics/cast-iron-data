**Sources:** Qadira, Jewel of the East pg 33\
Qadiran sunsingers are particularly religious skalds of Sarenrae who call down their goddess's glory
to fill soldiers with fire.

**Prerequisites:** Must be lawful good, neutral good, or neutral, and must worship
[Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae)

**Pillar of Light (Su)**

**Ability:** A great beam of sunlight shines down upon the skald, casting
[bright light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in a 30-foot radius, and allows the skald's _raging song_
to function as the [fascinate](ref/performance/fascinate) bardic performance.

**Flavor:** A sunsinger skald can use her _raging song_ to call upon her goddess to imbue her with
glory and make all who see it pay heed.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Song of marching

**Channel Solar Energy (Su)**

**Ability:** A sunsinger skald can [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) as a cleric once per
day to heal wounds or harm undead like a good-aligned cleric. When she does so, she fills the area
affected by the channeled energy with light, and can outline creatures in the area of effect as per
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire). The sunsinger uses her skald level as her effective cleric
level when channeling positive energy.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Spell kenning

**Special:** Undead who are particularly harmed by sunlight take a -2 penalty on saving throws
against this channeled energy, and take 1 additional point of damage per die of damage dealt by the
effect.
