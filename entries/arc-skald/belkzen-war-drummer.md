These fierce drummers are equally adept at tapping out a driving rhythm and rapping enemies upside
the head with the same massive clubs they use to beat the crude hidecovered drums they carry into
battle.

**Weapon Proficiency**

**Info:** A war drummer is proficient with all simple weapons and the
[greatclub](ref/eq-weapon/greatclub).

**Fearsome Mien (Ex)**

**Passive:** A war drummer adds 1/2 his class level (minimum 1) to all Intimidate and Bluff skill
checks.

**Replaces:** Bardic knowledge

**Deadly Rhythm (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** During his _raging song,_ a Belkzen war drummer can draw
[clubs](ref/eq-weapon/club) and greatclubs as a **free action** as if he had the
[Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw) feat.

**At 3rd Level:** The war drummer gains a +1 bonus on damage rolls for attacks made with clubs or
greatclubs.

**At 6th Level:** The war drummer gains [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) with the
club or the greatclub as a bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Scribe Scroll

At 7th level: This bonus +2.

At 11th level: This bonus +3.

At 15th level: This bonus +4.

At 19th level: This bonus +5.

**Weapon Master (Ex)**

**Ability:** A war drummer gains [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor)
as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Lore master

**Raging Song (Su)**

**Info:** A war drummer gains access to the following _raging song._

**Siege Drums (Su)**

**Ability:** The Belkzen war drummer can shatter massive walls, doors, and other inanimate obstacles
using his war drums. This ability works as the [shatter](ref/spell/shatter) spell, except the
drummer can increase the amount of damage and size of the object damaged by playing for longer
periods. The siege drummer can affect objects weighing up to 10 pounds per level, plus an additional
5 pounds per level for each additional round he plays. The damage dealt increases by 2d6 per round
(to a maximum of 20d6 after 10 rounds). The damage is dealt only after the drummer ceases playing.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** 7th-level versatile performance gained benefit
