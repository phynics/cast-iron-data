**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 48\
The bold schemer combines wrath, wits, and daring to win battles and wars in unconventional ways,
often through infiltration and deception. If raw force of arms is insufficient, she improvises ways
to pull victory from the jaws of defeat.

**Gained class skills:** dd, soh, stl

**Lost class skills:** ka, kd, kp

**Bardic knowledge**

**Alters:** Bardic knowledge

**Info:** A bold schemer's _bardic knowledge_ does not apply to Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge
(dungeoneering), or Knowledge (planes).

**Skald of Twists and Turns (Su)**

**Action:** Ability

**Flavor:** A bold schemer becomes increasingly adept at infiltration and devising cunning
strategies to overcome enemy fortifications.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge, improved uncanny dodge, [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)

At 8th level: The insight bonus +4.

At 12th level: The insight bonus +6.

At 16th level: The insight bonus +8.

At 20th level: The insight bonus +10.

A bold schemer can spend 8 hours observing a physical location (such as a fortress, a walled city,
or perhaps even a section of an underground dungeon or cavern complex), contemplating methods to
infiltrate the location and engage its defenders in battle in ways that subvert their defensive
advantages.

At the end of the 8 hours, the bold schemer gains a +2 insight bonus on her Bluff, Disable Device,
Disguise, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth checks while in that same physical location. The bonus lasts
until the bold schemer observes a new location. While observing a location, the bold schemer must
fully focus her attention; any distractions, such as engaging in combat, immediately ends the
observation and the bold schemer must start a new period of observing a physical location to receive
the bonus.

**Bold Strategy (Su)**

**Flavor:** A bold schemer can tailor her plans and observations so they are able to assist her
allies as well.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** As long as they can see or hear the bold schemer while inside the observed location,
all of her allies gain half the insight bonus she gains from the _skald of twists and turns_
ability.

**Replaces:** Dirge of doom
