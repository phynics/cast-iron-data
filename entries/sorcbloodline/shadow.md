Spirits from the shadow plane dally at times in the world of light, and such as these lay with your
ancestors once upon a time, imparting the mystery of shadow-stuff into your lineage. You are often
sullen and withdrawn, preferring to skulk at the fringes of social circles and keep to yourself,
cultivating an air of mystery and majesty that is all your own.

**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell with the darkness descriptor or the shadow subschool, you
gain a circumstance bonus on Stealth checks equal to the spell's level for 1d4 rounds.

### Bloodline Powers

Your benighted heritage infuses your mastery of magic with the essence of shadowstuff.

**Shadowstrike (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** Creatures with [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) or
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) are not dazzled by this ability.

**Standard:** You can make a melee touch attack that inflicts nonlethal damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level. In addition, the target is
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 minute.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Nighteye (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 30 feet.

**At 9th Level:** You gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Special:** If you already possess [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), its range is increased by
these amounts.

**Shadow Well (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can use the Stealth skill even while being observed and without cover
or concealment, as long as you are within 10 feet of a shadow other than your own.

**At 13th Level:** You can instead switch the positions of two willing allies, each of whom must be
within 60 feet of you.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times a day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Special:** Unless otherwise noted, the swapping ability is identical to
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door).

**Standard:** When within an area of darkness or [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light), you may
choose to switch places with a willing ally within 60 feet, who must also be in darkness or dim
light.

**Enveloping Darkness (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once a day, you may create an area of _[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness)_
that you can see through without penalty. All creatures except you are
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) within this darkness unless using
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) or a similar effect.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Shadow Master (Su)**

**Ability:** You can see perfectly in natural or magical darkness.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** When you use _[shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration)_ or
_[shadow evocation](ref/spell/shadow-evocation)_ spells, your creations are 20% more real, and any
creatures you create gain the benefits of the [Augment Summoning](ref/feat/augment-summoning) feat.

### Umbral (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 73\
Your nature is to gather the darkness into yourself.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell in an area of dim light or darkness, your effective caster
level is increased by 1.

### Bloodline Powers

Your shadow magic is more defensive than offensive.

**Cloak of Shadows (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Shadowstrike

**Standard:** You can grant one target a _[cloak of shadows](ref/spell/cloak-of-shadows)._ This
_cloak_ gives the target a bonus on Stealth checks made in areas of dim or no light equal to half
your sorcerer level for a number of rounds equal to half your sorcerer level (minimum +1 bonus for 1
round).

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day
