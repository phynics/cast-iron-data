### Bloodline Powers

The solar power that infuses your being alters the way you interact with the world, searing through
your spells.

**Sunsight (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) and cannot be
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** If you already have [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), you instead gain a
+4 bonus on saving throws against blindness effects.

**Friend of Fire (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** You gain immunity to fire.

**At 5th Level:** When in contact with flame or a burning object (including a
[flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon, lantern, or torch), add 1 per die to any healing effect of
which you are the target.

**At 9th Level:** Your fire resistance increases to 20.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10.

**Cleansing Flame (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can use fire to restore the health of yourself or your allies.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Standard:** Twice per day, you can wreathe your hand in a halo of flame and touch yourself or
another creature. The touch heals 2d8 + your character level points of damage. You can also remove
one of the following conditions affecting the target: 1d6 points of ability damage, blinded,
confused, dazzled, deafened, diseased, exhausted, fatigued, nauseated, poisoned, or sickened.

**Healing Fire (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) twice per day as a cleric of half
your level. Instead of using this ability to damage undead, you can convert the positive energy to
flame and deal an equivalent amount of fire damage instead.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Solar Ascension (Su)**

**Fullround:** You can become an incorporeal being of light. While in this form, you gain the
[incorporeal](ref/subtype/incorporeal) subtype and take half the normal damage from corporeal magic
attacks (you take no damage from nonmagical weapons and objects). Your spells deal half damage to
corporeal creatures, but spells and abilities that do not deal damage function normally.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Special:** While in this form, any creature you move through (as the [overrun](ref/rule/overrun)
combat maneuver) takes 2d6 points of fire damage.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per sorcerer level
