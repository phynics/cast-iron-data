### Bloodline Powers

The microscopic nanites in your blood grant you incredible powers and alien magic.

**Nanite Strike (Ex)**

**Action:** Free Action

**At 11th Level:** The cure becomes two successful saves.

**At 5th Level:** Weapons you use with this ability are treated as magical for the purposes of
overcoming DR, and the poison also deals 1 point of
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).

**At 7th Level:** The damage increases to 1d2 points of Constitution damage and 1d2 points of
Strength damage.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier rounds/day

You can cause nanites to flow from your body and coat the striking end of any manufactured melee
weapon you wield. When you hit with a melee attack using this weapon, some of the nanites infect the
blood of the victim.

**Nanite Strike**

**Type:** Poison (Poison-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

**Nanite Surge (Ex)**

**Immediate:** Once per day, you can cause your nanites to surge, granting you a bonus equal to 3 +
your sorcerer level on any one d20 roll; this ability must be activated before the roll is made.

If you already possess the _nanite surge_ ability from another source (such as from being an
[android](ref/race/android)), you can use your _nanite surge_ an additional time per day.

**At 9th Level:** You can use this ability an additional time per day.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Nanite Resurgence (Ex)**

**Immediate:** Once per day upon being reduced to one-quarter your maximum hit points, you can force
the mechanical swarm living inside you to keep you alive. This ability functions like a
[resurgent transformation](ref/spell/resurgent-transformation) spell using your sorcerer level as
the caster level, except it is an extraordinary ability and cannot be dispelled.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day (though using this ability twice in one
24-hour period entails a chance of system shock and death; see the spell's description for more
details).

**At 20th Level:** You never die of system shock from using this ability.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Distributed Body (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** Any time you are struck by a critical hit or a sneak attack, there is a 25% chance that
your nanites scatter and reincorporate around the attack, negating the extra damage and causing the
attack to deal damage as normal instead.

**Living Swarm (Su)**

**Ability:** At will, you can break down your body and move in a shimmering stream of flying
nanites, as if affected by _[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)._

**Flavor:** You and the machines within you become one.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Your nanites consume and replicate your body, allowing swift and radical physical
alterations with little more than a thought. You become immune to bleed effects, diseases, and
poisons, and gain DR 5/-.
