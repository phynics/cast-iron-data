### Bloodline Powers

Your salamander blood gives you great skill at crafting, even as it reshapes your form.

**Ember (Su)**

**Ability:** When making a [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) check involving metal, you can expend any
number of uses of this ability (up to half your sorcerer level, minimum one use) to gain a
circumstance bonus on the check equal to twice the number of uses expended.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Forge and Fire (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor) as a bonus
feat. In addition, whenever you cast a non-permanent, non-instantaneous spell that improves or
enhances a manufactured weapon, you can also give that weapon the [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
special ability for the spell's duration. (This has no effect if the weapon already has the
_flaming_ special ability.)

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Serpent's Tail (Su)**

**Free:** You can transform your legs into a slithering serpentine tail. Your speed is reduced by 10
feet (to a minimum of 5 feet), and you can't be tripped. Your footwear melds into your body and is
non-functional unless it provides a constant bonus and does not need to be activated. You also gain
a [tail slap](ref/umr/natural-attacks) natural attack that deals 1d8 points of damage (1d6 if you
are Small) + your Strength modifier.

**At 15th Level:** Your reach with your tail slap increases by 5 feet.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per sorcerer level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Searing Heat (Su)**

**Ability:** You deal 1d6 additional points of fire damage with natural weapons and melee attacks
made with metal weapons. In addition, you deal 2d6 points of fire damage per round to any creature
you [grapple](ref/rule/grapple). This heat does not damage your equipment.

**Flavor:** Your body becomes as hot as a salamander's.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Special:** You can activate or deactivate this effect as a **free action**.

**Reforged Flesh (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You become immune to fire damage and gain DR 10/adamantine and magic.
