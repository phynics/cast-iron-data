**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you apply a metamagic feat to a spell that increases the slot used by at least
one level, increase the spell's DC by +1. This bonus does not stack with itself and does not apply
to spells modified by the [Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell) feat.

### Bloodline Powers

Magic comes naturally to you, but as you gain levels you must take care to prevent the power from
overwhelming you.

**Arcane Bond (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain an _arcane bond,_ as a [wizard](ref/class/wizard) equal to your sorcerer
level. Your sorcerer levels stack with any wizard levels you possess when determining the powers of
your familiar or bonded object. This ability does not allow you to have both a familiar and a bonded
item.

**Ability2:** Once per day, your _bonded item_ allows you to cast any one of your spells known
(unlike a wizard's bonded item, which allows him to cast any one spell in his spellbook).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Metamagic Adept (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can apply any one metamagic feat you know to a spell you are about to cast without
increasing the casting time. You must still expend a higher-level spell slot to cast this spell.

**At 20th Level:** This ability is replaced by _arcane apotheosis_ (see below).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 sorcerer levels beyond 3rd

**New Arcana (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can add any one spell from the sorcerer/wizard spell list to your list of spells
known. This spell must be of a level that you are capable of casting.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Repeat:** You can add one additional spell., 13, 4

**School Power (Ex)**

**Ability:** Pick one school of magic. The DC for any spells you cast from that school increases by
+2. This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus).

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Arcane Apotheosis (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can add any metamagic feats that you know to your spells without increasing their
casting time, although you must still expend higher-level spell slots.

**Ability2:** Whenever you use magic items that require charges, you can instead expend spell slots
to power the item. For every three levels of spell slots that you expend, you consume one less
charge when using a magic item that expends charges.

**Flavor:** Your body surges with arcane power.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

### Sage (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 72\
Scholars of the arcane are always creating new means to use magic.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** You use your Intelligence, rather than your Charisma, to determine all class features
and effects relating to your sorcerer class, such as bonus spells per day, the maximum spell level
you can cast, the save DCs of your spells, and the number of daily uses of your bloodline powers.

**Flavor:** Unlike most sorcerers, whose innate magic is powered by force of personality, you use
your intellect to understand and master your mystic powers.

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on all Knowledge (arcana) and Spellcraft checks.

### Bloodline Powers

Your preferred focus is a bolt of mind-spun magic.

**Arcane Bolt (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Arcane bond

**Standard:** You can unleash a ray of magic force, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged
touch attack. This ray deals an amount of damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level. This damage is treated as a spell of
a level equal to half your sorcerer level, and is a force effect.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day
