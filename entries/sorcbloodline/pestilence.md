### Bloodline Powers

You awaken and quicken the lurking pestilence in your own body or the surrounding world to wreak
hideous malice, or to command and commune with agents of such plagues.

**Plague's Caress (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can make a melee touch attack that causes a living creature's flesh to break out
into rancid-smelling pustules and sores for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level (minimum 1 round). These sores cause
the victim to become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for the duration of the effect; this is a disease
effect.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Accustomed to Awfulness (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** You become immune to the [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) condition and to the
debilitating effects of disease (but you can still be a carrier of diseases).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You become immune to the sickened condition and gain a +4 bonus on all saving throws
against effects that cause nausea or disease.

**Shroud of Vermin (Su)**

**Flavor:** Swarms no longer see you as prey. You can walk among swarms without fear of being harmed
by them at all.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Even when you aren't standing amid a swarm, your body crawls with vermin, and their
chitinous bodies increase your natural armor bonus by +1.

**Standard:** You can mentally command a swarm in which you stand. You can direct that swarm's
attacks and movements as long as you have more Hit Dice than the swarm.

At 11th level: This bonus +2.

At 17th level: This bonus +3.

**Pestilential Breath (Su)**

**Flavor:** The sickness within your body finally becomes so potent that your very breath is deadly.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times per day.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Standard:** Once per day, you can exhale a cloud of pestilence in a 30-foot cone. Those caught in
the area of this miasmic cloud receive a single Fortitude save to avoid suffering the effects of two
different diseases. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma
modifier.

You can choose what two diseases you inflict on each target that succumbs to your plague breath, but
they must be two different diseases chosen from the following list:
[blinding sickness](ref/disease/blinding-sickness), [bubonic plague](ref/disease/bubonic-plague),
[cackle fever](ref/disease/cackle-fever), [filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever),
[leprosy](ref/disease/leprosy), [mindfire](ref/disease/mindfire), [red ache](ref/disease/red-ache),
[shakes](ref/disease/shakes), or [slimy doom](ref/disease/slimy-doom).

The victim suffers the initial effects of these two diseases immediately.

**Plague Carrier (Su)**

**Ability:** Your touch inflicts [mummy rot](ref/curse/mummy-rot) on those you strike. You can
choose to suppress this ability for 1 round as a **swift action**. You can make a touch attack to
inflict this disease on a target, or transfer it as part of an attack with any melee weapon or
touch-based spell. The creature touched can resist contracting _mummy rot_ by making a Fortitude
save - the DC is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
