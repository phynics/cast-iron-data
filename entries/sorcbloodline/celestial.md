**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell of the [summoning](ref/spelldef/summoning) subschool, the
creatures summoned gain DR/evil equal to half your sorcerer level (minimum 1). This does not stack
with any DR the creature might have.

### Bloodline Powers

Your celestial heritage grants you a great many powers, but they come at a price. The lords of the
higher planes are watching you and your actions closely.

**Heavenly Fire (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash a ray of heavenly fire, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged
touch attack. Against evil creatures, this ray deals an amount of damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level. This damage is divine and not
subject to energy resistance or immunity.

This ray heals good creatures of an amount of damage equal to 1d4 + half of your sorcerer level. A
good creature cannot benefit from your heavenly fire more than once per day.

Neutral creatures are neither harmed nor healed by this effect.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Celestial Resistances (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** Your resistances increase to 10.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) acid 5 and resist cold 5.

**Wings of Heaven (Su)**

**Ability:** You can sprout feathery wings and fly with a speed of 60 feet and good maneuverability.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per sorcerer level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Conviction (Su)**

**Ability:** You can reroll any one ability check, attack roll, skill check, or saving throw you
just made. You must decide to use this ability after the die is rolled, but before the results are
revealed by the GM. You must take the second result, even if it is worse. You can use this ability
once per day.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Ascension (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain unlimited use of the _wings of heaven_ ability. You also gain the ability to
speak with any creature that has a language (as per the [tongues](ref/spell/tongues) spell).

**Flavor:** You become infused with the power of the heavens.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to acid, cold, and petrification. You also gain resist electricity
10, resist fire 10, and a +4 racial bonus on saves against poison.

### Empyreal (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 71\
Your heavenly power derives from insight rather than force of personality.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Flavor:** Unlike most sorcerers whose innate magic is powered by force of personality, you use
pure willpower to master and fuel your magic.

**Info:** You use your Wisdom, rather than your Charisma, to determine all class features and
effects relating to your sorcerer class, such as bonus spells per day, maximum spell level you can
cast, and the save DCs of your spells.

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on all Heal and Knowledge (religion) checks.

### Bloodline Powers

Your quasi-divine nature gives you strange powers.

**Sacred Cistern (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) once per day as a cleric of your
sorcerer level - 4.

**Flavor:** Your bloodline makes you a natural receptacle of divine energy.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Wings of heaven
