**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Info:** Your sorcerer spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) count as
[psychic](ref/rule/psychic-magic) instead of arcane. You use thought and emotion components instead
of verbal and somatic components when casting your spells.

### Bloodline Powers

Your psychic powers make you a master mentalist, but your soul struggles with the urge to transcend
into a pure thought-form.

**Psychic Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** You can target one creature within 30 feet that you can see; that creature must succeed
at a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level +
your Charisma modifier) or it takes an amount of damage equal to 1d6 + half of your sorcerer level
and becomes [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round.

**Flavor:** You can overwhelm the minds of those nearby.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Mental Resistance (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Your mind blocks attempts to assail it.

**At 9th Level:** This bonus increases to +4.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects.

**Undercasting Prodigy (Sp)**

**Ability:** Whenever you gain a new level of spells, you automatically replace any psychic
bloodline spells that can be undercast with the highest-level version you can cast in your list of
spells known. For example, at 9th level, you would replace _mind thrust I, id insinuation I,_ and
_[ego whip I](ref/spell/ego-whip-1)_ with [mind thrust IV](ref/spell/mind-thrust-4),
[id insinuation III](ref/spell/id-insinuation-3), and [ego whip II](ref/spell/ego-whip-2),
respectively, as spells known.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Thoughtsense (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can detect the thoughts of others around you, as a constant
[thoughtsense](ref/umr/thoughtsense) effect with a range of 30 feet instead of 60 feet.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**True Thought-Form (Sp)**

**Flavor:** You can shed the restrictions of the flesh and become a thought-form - a being that
exists independently of its substance.

**Immediate:** Whenever a creature would kill you, you can attempt to use
[mind swap](ref/spell/mind-swap) against that creature just before dying.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
