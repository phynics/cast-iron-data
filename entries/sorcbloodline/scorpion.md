**Ability:** You are trained in the use of poison and cannot accidentally poison yourself when

### Bloodline Powers

Wherever your lineage hails from now, the desert is in your blood. Your ancestors may have worshiped
the power of the scorpion or learned to channel it to survive.

**Progenitor's Sting (Sp)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**At 11th Level:** Whenever you apply your _progenitor's sting_ to a weapon, you can magically apply
it onto all willing allies' weapons within 20 feet as well. This counts as one use of your bloodline
ability.

**At 5th Level:** The ability damage of your poison increases to 1d3.

**At 7th Level:** Each time you apply your _progenitor's sting,_ you can choose two ability scores
for your bloodline poison to affect.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

You can magically apply your toxic essence onto your weapon or onto the weapon of a willing ally
within 30 feet.

**Toxic Essence**

**Type:** Poison (Poison-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Cha modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** 1 Str, Dex, or Con damage (chosen when the ability is used)

**Cure:** 1 save

**Modify Onset (Su)**

**Ability:** You can increase the onset time of poisons you use by up to 1 hour per level.

**At 9th Level:** You can alter a poison so that it does not have an onset time, but you can trigger
it as a **swift action** to take effect at any time, regardless of distance. If you do not trigger
this poison within 1 week of poisoning a target, the poison dissipates harmlessly. The poison can be
detected and removed before it is triggered using any normal methods of detecting and removing
poison.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Sudden Sting (Ex, Sp)**

**Ability:** When you are able to act in the surprise round, you can take a full round's worth of
actions rather than just a single standard action.

**Ability2:** You can also cast [accelerate poison](ref/spell/accelerate-poison) and
[delay poison](ref/spell/delay-poison) as [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) a
total of three times per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Sandwalker (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain you gain a burrow speed of 30 feet and [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) with
a range of 60 feet.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**It Is My Nature (Su)**

**Flavor:** Your poisoned blood protects you, reflexively conjuring an ethereal stinger to lash out
at foes who come too close.

**Immediate:** Three times per day when an enemy moves into a square adjacent to you, you can make a
melee touch attack against the creature. On a hit, the creature must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC
= 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier) or die. A creature that succeeds at this
Fortitude save is immune to this effect for 24 hours.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Special:** This is a poison and death effect. You can use this ability even when you are surprised
or flat-footed.
