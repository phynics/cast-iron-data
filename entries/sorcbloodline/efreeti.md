**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals energy damage, you can change the type of damage
to fire. This also changes the spell's descriptors to match this energy type.

### Bloodline Powers

Like an efreeti, you have both natural power over air and several other genie-based abilities.

**Fire Ray (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash an elemental ray, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. This ray deals fire damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
sorcerer level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** Your fire resistance increases to 20.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10.

**Efreeti Form (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to assume the form of an efreeti once per day. This acts like the
spell [giant form I](ref/spell/giant-form-1), except you only use it to become an efreeti and its
duration is 1 round per level. While in this form, you also gain an efreeti's
_[heat](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/heat)_ ability.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Heat**

**Flavor:** Text from the efreeti monster entry.

**Passive:** An efreeti's body deals 1d6 points of fire damage whenever it hits in melee, or in each
round it [grapples](ref/rule/grapple).

**Elemental Movement (Su)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** Your base speed increases by +30 feet.

**Power of the Efreet (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Such wishes must begin with the words &quot&I
wish,&quot& and cannot duplicate a wish you have granted within the past 24 hours. If you use this
ability to duplicate a spell with a costly material component, you must provide that component.

**Ability2:** You can use _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ once per day to travel to or from
the Plane of Fire.

**Flavor:** You gain the power of genies to grant wishes.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You also become immune to fire damage.
