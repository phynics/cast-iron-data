Your family is a long line of dreamers, who dream not as ordinary mortals do but rather as those who
reach through and touch the supernal realm of dreams and the farthest shores of night. Whether it is
a gift or curse is not always clear, but your visions of the past and future call you ineluctably to
a life of adventure.

**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you target a single creature with a spell, you gain an insight bonus equal to
half the spell's level (minimum +1) for 1 round to your AC and saving throws against any spell or
attack made by that creature.

### Bloodline Powers

You reach out into and through the world of dreams to touch the minds and destinies of those around
you.

**Lullaby (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use [lullaby](ref/spell/lullaby) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) a number of times per day equal to 3 + your
Charisma modifier. This effect lasts for 1 minute and does not require concentration. The penalty on
saves versus sleep effects increases to -4.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Combat Precognition (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a +1 insight bonus on initiative checks.

**Flavor:** Your insight into the future grants you an advantage in combat.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

At 7th level: This bonus +2.

At 11th level: This bonus +3.

At 15th level: This bonus +4.

At 19th level: This bonus +5.

**Dreamshaper (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can manipulate the dreamscape of others, drawing forth or tampering
with their subconscious minds. This power allows you to tamper with the target's memories as if
using [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory), or you may ask questions as if using
[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead) upon a corpse. A successful Will save negates the
effect. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier, with
further modifiers as the [nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare) spell.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times a day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Eye of Somnus (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can project your consciousness as if using [arcane eye](ref/spell/arcane-eye). In
addition, at any point you can cause the _[arcane eye](ref/spell/arcane-eye)_ to become visible. The
eye can no longer be moved, but it acts as a [symbol of sleep](ref/spell/symbol-of-sleep) to all who
see it. You may use this power once per day.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Solipsism (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can become incorporeal. You gain the [incorporeal](ref/subtype/incorporeal) subtype
and take only half damage from corporeal magical attacks (you take no damage from nonmagical weapons
and objects). Your spells deal only half damage to corporeal creatures, but spells and abilities
that do not deal damage function normally.

**Flavor:** You can drift into the dream world, fading from the world around you.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per sorcerer level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

### Visionary (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 73\
Your dream-magic strongly influences the waking world.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Though you can still only regain spells once per day, you need only a single hour of
sleep prior to doing so. (You still risk fatigue as normal if you don't receive enough rest.)

### Bloodline Powers

Your dreams hint at the future.

**Visions (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day when sleeping, you may gain information about actions taken in the next
week as if you had cast a _[divination](ref/spell/divination)_ spell. You can gain information about
a single question.

**Flavor:** When you sleep, you can gain information through prophetic visions.

**At 17th Level:** You can have two questions answered while you sleep.

**At 20th Level:** You can have three questions answered.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Dreamshaper
