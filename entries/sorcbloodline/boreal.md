**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
Replace Power Attack with [Powerful Maneuvers](ref/rule/powerful-maneuvers).

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell with the cold descriptor, increase the spell's save DC by 1.

### Bloodline Powers

Your connection to primeval winters grants you a mastery of cold magic that few can match, and a
savagery that fewer still can withstand.

**Cold Steel (Sp)**

**At 9th Level:** You can confer the [icy burst](ref/magic-enh/icy-burst) property instead, but the
duration of the power is halved.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can touch a weapon or up to 50 pieces of ammunition, giving it the
[frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) property for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your sorcerer level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Icewalker (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) cold 5 and can move across snow and icy surfaces
without penalty and without leaving tracks.

**At 9th Level:** You gain resist cold 10 and can climb icy surfaces as if using
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Snow Shroud (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can surround yourself with a cloak of swirling snow for a number of
rounds per day equal to your sorcerer level. This power acts and deals damage as a
[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield) (chill shield) that sheds no light. It provides a 20% miss
chance on attacks made against you and grants a bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks equal
to half your sorcerer level in snowy or icy areas.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** You ignore [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) and [Perception](ref/skill/perception)
penalties in natural or magical snow, ice, fog, and similar weather conditions.

**Blizzard (Sp)**

**Ability:** This power acts as [control winds](ref/spell/control-winds), but in addition the entire
area (not including the &quot&eye&quot& at the center of the storm) is affected as a
[sleet storm](ref/spell/sleet-storm) and all in the area are exposed to extreme cold.

**Flavor:** You can create a savage winter storm centered on you.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Usage:** Once per day

**Usage:** Once per day

**Child of Ancient Winters (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain the [cold](ref/subtype/cold) subtype and you become immune to fatigue and
exhaustion. In addition, you gain immunity to sneak attacks and critical hits. This power causes you
to gain vulnerability to fire.

### Rime-Blooded (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 72\
The wintry origin of your magic flows like ice water in your veins.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell with the cold descriptor, you may select one target of the
spell to be [slowed](ref/spell/slow) (as the spell) for 1 round. A Fortitude save (DC 10 + the level
of cold spell + your Charisma modifier) negates the effect.

### Bloodline Powers

Sometimes you must bring the cold to your enemies.

**Freezing Bolt (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cause the air to erupt in freezing sleet. This 10-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) does 1d6 points of cold damage per sorcerer level (Reflex half). The DC of
this save is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier. This power has a range
of 60 feet.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Snow shroud
