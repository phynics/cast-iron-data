**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 67\
The spiritual power of art and song runs strongly through your family line. This may be the result
of ancient pacts made with [azata](ref/family/azata) patrons, or even an inheritance from a lillend
or trumpet [archon](ref/family/archon) ancestor. It could also be the lingering taint of a less
savory heritage, such as from a [harpy](ref/monster/harpy) or a forefather driven to madness by the
disquieting gibbering of a [shoggoth](ref/monster/shoggoth). However it came to be, your family's
artistic bent shows itself strongly in your magic.

**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell with a verbal component and no somatic or material component,
you treat your caster level as if it were one higher.

### Bloodline Powers

You hear music whenever you cast spells, and can use the songs in your head to create magic effects.

**Beguiling Voice (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can use the sound of your voice to lull a target creature into taking no action.
This ability acts as the [daze](ref/spell/daze) spell, except it is language-dependent, has a
duration of 1 round, and affects a living creature whose Hit Dice do not exceed your sorcerer level.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Fascinate (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to use a Perform skill to cause one or more creatures to become
fascinated with you. This acts as the [fascinate](ref/performance/fascinate) bardic perform ability,
except the save DC is 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma bonus, and it lasts 1
round/level. You may use this ability once per day.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

At 8th level: You may use this ability 2 times per day.

At 13th level: You may use this ability 3 times per day.

At 18th level: You may use this ability 4 times per day.

**Perfect Voice (Su)**

**Ability:** You understand all efforts to communicate through sound, and can make yourself
understood to any creature able to understand language. You can speak to, and understand the speech
of, any creature that understands at least one spoken language.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** The save DCs of language-dependent spells you cast increase by +1.

**Inspire (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use [greater heroism](ref/spell/heroism-greater) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day.

**Flavor:** Your artistic magic can inspire your allies to great accomplishments.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

At 17th level: You may use this ability 2 times per day.

At 19th level: You may use this ability 3 times per day.

**Grand Maestro (Su)**

**Ability:** You cast any spell with a verbal component as if it had the
[Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell) metamagic feat applied to it, with no increase to the casting
time or spell slot required to cast it.

**Flavor:** The power of pure magic music flows through you.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to sonic damage and language-dependent spells.
