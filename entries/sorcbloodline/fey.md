Replace Mobility with [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack).

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell of the [compulsion](ref/spelldef/compulsion) subschool,
increase the spell's DC by +2.

### Bloodline Powers

You have always had a tie to the natural world, and as your power increases, so does the influence
of the fey over your magic.

**Laughing Touch (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can cause a creature to burst out laughing for 1 round as a melee touch attack. A
laughing creature can only take a **move action** but can defend itself normally. Once a creature
has been affected by _laughing touch,_ it is immune to its effects for 24 hours.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Woodland Stride (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can move through any sort of undergrowth (such as natural thorns, briars, overgrown
areas, and similar terrain) at your normal speed and without taking damage or suffering any other
impairment. Thorns, briars, and overgrown areas that have been magically manipulated to impede
motion, however, still affect you.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Fleeting Glance (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can turn invisible. This ability functions as
[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per sorcerer level

**Fey Magic (Su)**

**Ability:** You may reroll any caster level check made to overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). You must decide to use this ability before the
results are revealed by the GM. You must take the second result, even if it is worse. You can use
this ability at will.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Soul of the Fey (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) using your sorcerer level as your caster level.

**Flavor:** Your soul becomes one with the world of the fey.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to poison and DR 10/cold iron. Creatures of the animal type do not
attack you unless compelled to do so through magic.

### Dark Fey (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** People of the River pg 11\
Your powers were born of malevolent trickery and shadowy First World magic.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell with the curse subtype, increase the spell's DC by 2.

### Bloodline Powers

In your presence, senses are deceived.

**Fey Fascination (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Laughing touch

**Standard:** You can force one target within 30 feet to make a Will save (DC = 10 + half your
sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier) or be [fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) for a number of
rounds equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level (minimum 1). This is
a mind-affecting, illusion (pattern) effect.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Sylvan (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 72\
Your ties to nature have more to do with creatures than with capriciousness.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Info:** See bloodline powers.

### Bloodline Powers

Your magic shows a kinship to that of the beast-talkers and shapechanger fey.

**Animal Companion (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain an [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion). Your effective druid
level for this ability is equal to your sorcerer level - 3 (minimum 1st).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Laughing touch, bloodline arcana

**Special:** This bloodline power counts as your bloodline arcana.

**Fey Wings (Su)**

**Ability:** You can grow insect-like wings from your back and become one size category smaller (as
if you had used [reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person)), gaining a fly speed of 60 feet with
average maneuverability.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Fey magic

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per sorcerer level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments
