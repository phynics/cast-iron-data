### Bloodline Powers

Your skyward gaze and communion with the heavens focus and enhance your magical talents.

**Minute Meteors (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can summon a rain of tiny meteorites to fall in a 5-foot column, 30 feet high,
with a range of 30 feet. The meteors inflict an amount of fire damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level. A Reflex save negates this damage.
The save DC is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Voidwalker (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) and [resist](ref/umr/resistance)
cold and fire 5.

**At 9th Level:** You no longer need to breathe, as if wearing a
[necklace of adaptation](ref/magic-wondrous/necklace-of-adaptation).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Aurora Borealis (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can create a sheet of cascading colors. This power acts as a
[wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire), but it inflicts cold damage and does not radiate heat.
However, one side of the aurora designated by you [fascinates](ref/misc/fascinated) creatures within
10 feet, up to a maximum of 2 HD of creatures per sorcerer level. A Will save negates this fascinate
effect. The save DC is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per sorcerer level

**Breaching the Gulf (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day you can teleport a single creature within 30 feet into the void of space
if it fails a Will save. The save DC is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma
modifier. The target can attempt a new saving throw as a full-round action each round to return.
While trapped in the airless void, the target takes 6d6 points of cold damage per round and must
[hold its breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) or begin to suffocate.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** Your caster level is increased by 3 when casting spells of the teleportation subschool.

**Starborn (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain immunity to cold and [blindness](ref/misc/blinded), and you can see perfectly
in natural or magical darkness. In addition, you gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1 when
you are outdoors at night.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

### Void-Touched (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 73\
The darkness between the stars calls to you.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast an evocation spell, you may select one affected target that fails its
save to suffer the choking airlessness of the void, silencing it (as [silence](ref/spell/silence),
but affecting only the target) for 1 round. This is a supernatural ability.

### Bloodline Powers

Your magic is blacker than any night.

**Black Motes (Sp)**

**Ability:** This ability works exactly like _minute meteors,_ except it deals cold damage.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Minute Meteors

**Voidfield (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can create an area influenced by the black void. This ability acts as an
[ice storm](ref/spell/ice-storm), except the area is also subject to
[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) for a number of rounds equal to one-fourth of your
sorcerer level.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Aurora Borealis

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 3 sorcerer levels
