**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a mind-affecting or language-dependent spell, it affects animals,
magical beasts, and monstrous humanoids as if they were humanoids who understood your language.

**Flavor:** Your powers of compulsion can affect even bestial creatures.

### Bloodline Powers

The treacherous and yet hypnotic serpent's blood that flows through you taints your magic with a
sinuous and seductive grace.

**Serpent's Fang (Ex)**

**Action:** Free Action=

**At 11th Level:** Your poison damage increases to 1d4 Con.

**At 5th Level:** These fangs are considered magical for the purpose of overcoming DR and the poison
damage increases to 1d2 Con.

**At 7th Level:** Your poison requires 2 successful saves to cure.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier rounds/day

You can grow fangs. These fangs are treated as a [natural weapon](ref/umr/natural-attacks)
inflicting 1d4 points of damage plus your Strength modifier (1d3 if you are Small) plus poison.

**Serpent's Fang Poison**

**Type:** Poison (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

**Serpentfriend (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can use [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) at will with reptilian
animals (including various forms of dinosaurs, lizards, and other cold-blooded creatures).

**Ability2:** You gain a [viper](ref/monster/familiar-viper) [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) using
your sorcerer level - 2 as your effective wizard level.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Snakeskin (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +1 bonus to natural armor, a +2 racial bonus on saves against poison, and a
+2 bonus on Escape Artist checks.

**Den of Vipers (Sp)**

**Ability:** You may summon a host of writhing serpents. This power acts as
[creeping doom](ref/spell/creeping-doom), but the swarms' poison inflicts Constitution damage and
any creature other than you sharing a space with a swarm is [entangled](ref/rule/entangled).

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Scaled Soul (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the [shapechanger](ref/subtype/shapechanger) subtype, and you can assume the
form of a reptilian humanoid (as [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self)) or snake of Diminutive to Huge
size (as [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)) at will. You retain the power of speech and the
ability to use somatic spell components when transformed.

**Ability2:** You may use _serpent's fang_ as often as desired, and you may choose to inflict damage
to any ability score.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You also become immune to poison and paralysis.

### Envenomed (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 71\
The ophidian source of your bloodline has more to do with stealth and assassination than it does
with leadership and alliances.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on Acrobatics, Climb, and Stealth checks.

### Bloodline Powers

The stink of poison taints your magic.

**Envenom (Su)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Serpentfriend

**Special:** The poison cannot be removed or stored, and the weapon loses the benefit of the poison
after the first successful attack or 1 hour has passed.

**Swift:** You can lick or bite a melee weapon to imbue it with 1 dose of
[black adder venom](ref/eq-poison/black-adder-venom). The poison's DC is equal to 10 + half your
sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 3 sorcerer levels
