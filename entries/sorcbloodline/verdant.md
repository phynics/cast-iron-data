**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell with a range of personal, your skin toughens, granting you a
natural armor bonus equal to the spell's level for 1d4 rounds. This bonus does not stack with any
other natural armor bonuses you might have.

### Bloodline Powers

The power of living things infuses every aspect of your magic, as you draw power from nature and its
life energies flow through you.

**Tanglevine (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can create a 15-foot-long, animated vine that springs from your hand. This vine
lasts for 1 round and can be used to make a single [disarm](ref/rule/disarm),
[steal](ref/rule/steal), or [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver, using your sorcerer level plus
your Charisma modifier in place of your normal CMB.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Photosynthesis (Ex)**

**Flavor:** You feed upon nature's raw essence.

**At 9th Level:** These bonuses increase to +4.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** Your need to eat and sleep is reduced as if wearing a
[ring of sustenance](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-sustenance), and you gain a +2 racial bonus on saving
throws made against poison and sleep effects.

**Massmorph (Sp)**

**Fullround:** You can alter the size and health of plant life, as if using
[plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth) or [diminish plants](ref/spell/diminish-plants).

Alternatively, you can transform one willing non-plant creature per sorcerer level (no two of which
can be more than 30 feet apart) as [tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape).

**At 15th Level:** You may transform creatures as [plant shape I](ref/spell/plant-shape-1).

**At 20th Level:** You can transform creatures as [plant shape II](ref/spell/plant-shape-2).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Special:** You can transform non-plant creatures using this power once per day.

**Rooting (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Move:** You can extend roots into the ground. Your speed is reduced to 5 feet, but you gain a +4
bonus to natural armor and a +10 bonus to CMD against [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush),
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun), [reposition](ref/rule/reposition), and trip maneuvers. You also gain
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 30 feet and [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per sorcerer level

**Shepherd of the Trees (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 30 feet even when not rooted.

**Flavor:** Your verdant heritage fully manifests.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +4 natural armor bonus. You gain immunity to paralysis, poison, polymorph,
sleep, and stunning.

### Groveborn (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 71\
Some plant magic is more green, while some is more brown, like hardwood.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Flavor:** Your powers of compulsion can affect even plant creatures.

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a mind-affecting or language-dependent spell, it affects creatures of
the plant type as if they were humanoids that understood your language.

### Bloodline Powers

Your nature powers create tough, resilient minions.

**Lush Summoning (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you summon creatures with a conjuration (summoning) spell, you can decide that
they appear green and leafy. The natural armor bonus of such creatures is increased by +2, and they
gain a +4 bonus on saves against paralysis, poison, polymorph, sleep, and stunning.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Photosynthesis
