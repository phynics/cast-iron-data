**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a harmful spell, you gain a bonus equal to the spell's level on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks made against any creature adversely effected by that spell
until the end of your next turn. Adversely effected typically means damage, but it can also mean
debilitating effects or conditions.

### Bloodline Powers

You draw upon ancestors both legendary and forgotten.

**Student of Humanity (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Gained class skills:** diplo, kh, kl, kno, ling

**Passive Ability:** In addition, when using these skills to learn, study, or gather information
about humans, you add an insight bonus equal to your Charisma bonus on such checks.

**Heroic Echo (Su)**

**Immediate:** If you receive a morale effect that affects an area or multiple targets, you can
share your increased bonus with all other recipients. This increase to other participants lasts a
number of rounds equal to your Charisma bonus.

**At 9th Level:** These abilities also apply to competence bonuses.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** When you receive a morale bonus from any spell,
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or magic item, including those you cast on
yourself, that bonus increases by +1.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 3 sorcerer levels

**Take Your Best Shot (Su)**

**Immediate:** If you are targeted by a harmful spell,
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or supernatural ability and suffer no harm from
it, whether because of a successful saving throw, [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance), the
attack missing, or some other protection, you can make an Intimidate check to demoralize the
creature that produced the effect, but only if the creature that used the harmful effect is within
30 feet and can clearly see and hear you.

**At 13th Level:** You can use this ability after succeeding at a saving throw to reduce the effects
of an attack (if no save is allowed, you cannot use this ability).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Heroic Legends (Su)**

**Ability:** You may [inspire greatness](ref/performance/inspire-greatness) or
[inspire heroics](ref/performance/inspire-heroics) as a [bard](ref/class/bard) of your sorcerer
level by sacrificing a spell slot as a **swift** or **move action**. The effect lasts a number of
rounds equal to the sacrificed spell's level; this duration is doubled for human recipients.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Immortal Legend (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You cease aging; no longer need to eat, drink, or sleep; and gain immunity to death
effects and energy drain.
