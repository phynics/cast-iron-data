### Bloodline Powers

You can draw upon the ancient magic of the nagas to ensnare the minds of lesser creatures and
destroy those who would defy your will.

**Vanishing (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can cloak yourself in illusory magic, making you invisible (as per _invisibility_)
for a number of rounds per day equal to your sorcerer level + your Charisma bonus (if any). These
rounds need not be used consecutively.

**Naga Resistances (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** This bonus increases to +4.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects and poison effects.

**Ensnaring Eyes (Su)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Add 2 to the save DC of any spell, [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities),
or supernatural ability you use that causes a target to become [fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) or
that belongs to the [charm subschool](ref/spelldef/charm).

**Cast without Hands (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast an arcane spell with a somatic component, you can do so even with
your hands full, by twisting and gyrating your body. This doubles the arcane spell failure chance
from your armor (if any), and you can't use this ability if you are pinned or immobile.

**Flavor:** You learn to eschew the use of your hands when casting spells, as a naga would.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Power of the Naga (Su)**

**Ability:** In addition, you can also assume the shape of a naga at will as if using
[naga shape III](ref/spell/naga-shape-3).

**Flavor:** Your form becomes enhanced by the power of the naga.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to charm effects, mind-reading effects, and poison.
