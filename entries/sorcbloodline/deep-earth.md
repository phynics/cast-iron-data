**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you and the target of your spell are both underground, increase the spell's
save DC by +1.

### Bloodline Powers

The powers of the living rock are within you, and your communion with the strength of stone is
refined and enhanced as your power grows.

**Tremor (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can cause the ground to shake beneath a single creature within 30 feet,
functioning as a [trip](ref/rule/trip) maneuver using your sorcerer level plus your Charisma
modifier in place of your CMB.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Rockseer (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the stonecunning trait as a [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf); if already a dwarf, your
bonus improves to +4.

**At 15th Level:** You can see through solid objects as if using a
[ring of x-ray vision](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-x-ray-vision) for a number of rounds per day equal to
your sorcerer level. These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

**At 9th Level:** You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 30 feet.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Crystal Shard (Sp)**

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times a day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Standard:** Once per day, you can touch a metal or stone weapon (or up to 50 pieces of
ammunition), giving it the [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) property against any creature with the earth
subtype, oozes, or constructs made of stone or metal, for 1 minute.

**Earth Glide (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can glide through any sort of natural earth or stone as easily as a fish swims
through water, with a burrow speed equal to half your normal speed. You do not leave a tunnel or
trace of your passage.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per sorcerer level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Strength of Stone (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Your flesh becomes as hard as stone, giving you DR 10/adamantine, and you become immune
to petrification. You suffer no penalties while squeezing through tight spaces and are immune to
bull rush, drag, grapple, reposition, and trip maneuvers, and to [push](ref/umr/push) and
[pull](ref/umr/pull) effects as long as you are standing on the ground.

### Bedrock (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 70\
Your earth magic is more defensive than offensive.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell of the summoning subschool, the creatures summoned gain
DR/adamantine equal to half your sorcerer level (minimum 1). This does not stack with any DR the
creature might have.

### Bloodline Powers

Rock is timeless; it endures.

**Iron Hide (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Crystal shard

**Swift:** You can grant yourself DR 10/adamantine.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per sorcerer level
