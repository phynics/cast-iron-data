**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals energy damage, you can change the type of damage
to cold. This also changes the spell's descriptors to match this energy type.

### Bloodline Powers

Like a marid, you have both natural power over water and several other genie-based abilities.

**Frost Ray (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash an elemental ray, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. This ray deals cold damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
sorcerer level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** Your cold resistance increases to 20.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain cold resistance 10.

**Water's Fury (Su)**

**Flavor:** You gain the ability to summon a rushing jet of water from the elemental plane of water
and direct it against your foes.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Standard:** You can create a jet of water in a 60-foot line that deals 1d6 points of damage per
two sorcerer levels you possess, and [blinds](ref/misc/blinded) the target that was struck for 1d6
rounds. A Reflex save (DC 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma bonus) reduces the damage by
half and negates the blinding effect.

**Elemental Movement (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a swim speed of 60 feet.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Power of the Marid (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Such wishes must begin with the words &quot&I
wish,&quot& and cannot duplicate a wish you have granted within the past 24 hours. If you use this
ability to duplicate a spell with a costly material component, you must provide that component.

**Ability2:** You can use _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ once per day to travel to or from
the Plane of Water.

**Flavor:** You gain the power of genies to grant wishes.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You also become immune to cold damage.

### Shahzada (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Blood of the Elements pg 15\
You are descended from the ranks of noble marids, making your blood royal as well as magical.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell with the water descriptor, you gain a swim speed of 30 feet
for a number of rounds equal to double the level of the spell cast.

### Bloodline Powers

Your mastery over water is coupled with the power to alter it.

**Sweeping Waves (Su)**

**Ability:** You can summon a wave of water from within, which pours from your mouth and violently
sweeps creatures away from you on all sides. Creatures within 5 feet of you must succeed at a Reflex
save (the DC is equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier) or take a number of
points of damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level.

Additionally, the waves of water [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) each affected creature, using your
caster level as your base attack bonus and your Charisma modifier as your Strength modifier to
determine the CMB of the bull rush.

A successful save halves the damage and negates the bull rush.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Elemental Movement
