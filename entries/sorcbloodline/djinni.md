**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals energy damage, you can change the type of damage
to electricity. This also changes the spell's descriptors to match this energy type.

### Bloodline Powers

Like a djinni, you have both natural power over air and several other genie-based abilities.

**Electricity Ray (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash an elemental ray, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. This ray deals electricity damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of
your sorcerer level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** Your electricity resistance increases to 20.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain electricity [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10.

**Whirlwind (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to turn into a 10-foot-high whirlwind once per day for 1 round per
sorcerer level.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Elemental Movement (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a fly speed of 60 feet with average maneuverability.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Power of the Djinn (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Such wishes must begin with the words &quot&I
wish,&quot& and cannot duplicate a wish you have granted within the past 24 hours. If you use this
ability to duplicate a spell with a costly material component, you must provide that component.

**Ability2:** You can use _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ once per day to travel to or from
the Plane of Air.

**Flavor:** You gain the power of genies to grant wishes.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You also become immune to electricity damage.
