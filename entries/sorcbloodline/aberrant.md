**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell of the [polymorph](ref/spelldef/polymorph) subschool,
increase the duration of the spell by 50% (minimum 1 round). This bonus does not stack with the
increase granted by the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) feat.

### Bloodline Powers

Aberrant sorcerers show increasing signs of their tainted heritage as they increase in level,
although they are only visible when used.

**Acidic Ray (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can fire an acidic ray, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack.
The acidic ray deals acid damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
sorcerer level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Long Limbs (Ex)**

**Ability:** Your reach increases by 5 feet whenever you are making a melee touch attack. This
ability does not otherwise increase your threatened area.

**At 11th Level:** This bonus to your reach increases to 10 feet.

**At 17th Level:** This bonus to your reach increases to 15 feet.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Unusual Anatomy (Ex)**

**At 13th Level:** This chance increases to 50%.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Your anatomy changes, giving you a 25% chance to ignore any critical hit or sneak
attack scored against you.

**Alien Resistance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** You gain [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to your sorcerer
level + 10.

**Aberrant Form (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Your body becomes truly unnatural.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You are immune to critical hits and sneak attacks. In addition, you gain
[blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) with a range of 60 feet and
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 5/-.

### Warped (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 73\
The alien taint in your blood causes malformations and mutations in others.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell of the polymorph subschool, one target of your choice may
receive one random effect from the Warped Polymorph Benefits table (see below). This bonus lasts as
long as the polymorph effect on the target.

### Bloodline Powers

Your strange blood brings out the worst in people.

**Warp Touch (Sp)**

**Ability:** You create brief, disorienting changes in a creature's physical form. This ability
affects one creature within 30 feet, which is [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round (Fortitude
negates; DC 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Acidic ray

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day
