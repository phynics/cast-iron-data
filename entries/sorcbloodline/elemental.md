**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals energy damage, you can change the type of damage
to match the type of your bloodline. This also changes the spell's type to match the type of your
bloodline.

### Bloodline Powers

One of the four elements infuses your being, and you can draw upon its power in times of need. At
first level, you must select one of the four elements: air, earth, fire, or water. This choice
cannot be changed. A number of your abilities grant resistances and deal damage based on your
element, as noted below.

|Element|Energy Type|Elemental Movement|
| --- | --- | --- |
|Air|Electricity|Fly 60 feet (average)|
|Earth|Acid|Burrow 30 feet|
|Fire|Fire|+30 feet base speed|
|Water|Cold|Swim 60 feet|

**Elemental Ray (Sp):**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash an elemental ray, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. This ray deals an amount of damage of your energy type equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** Your energy resistance increases to 20.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain energy [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 against your energy type.

**Elemental Blast (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can unleash a blast of elemental power once per day. This 20-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) does 1d6 points of damage of your energy type per sorcerer level. Those
caught in the area of your blast receive a Reflex save for half damage. Creatures that fail their
saves gain vulnerability to your energy type until the end of your next turn. The DC of this save is
equal to 10 + half your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier. This power has a range of 60 feet.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Elemental Movement (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a special movement type or bonus. This ability is based on your chosen
element, as indicated on the above chart.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Elemental Body (Su)**

**Flavor:** Elemental power surges through your body.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to sneak attacks, critical hits, and damage from your energy type.

### Primal (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 72\
Your powers are attuned to the concentrated core of the elemental plane.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell with an energy descriptor that matches your elemental
bloodline's energy type, that spell deals +1 point of damage per die rolled.

### Bloodline Powers

You can infuse raw energy into your summoned minions.

**Elementalist Summoning (Su)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Whenever you summon a creature, it gains energy [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10
against the energy type that matches your elemental bloodline (if it already has such resistance,
its resistance increases by +5), and its natural attacks deal an additional 1d6 points of damage of
the same energy type.

**Replaces:** Elemental blast

### Lifewater (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Blood of the Elements pg 15\
Your soul is infused with the restorative and life-giving powers of elemental water.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Anytime you cast a spell with the cold or water descriptor, you gain a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to the level of the spell cast. You can
grant up to half of these temporary hit points to an adjacent ally, in which case you gain the
remaining temporary hit points. These temporary hit points last 1 minute.

### Bloodline Powers

Your powers are revitalizing rather than destructive.

**Lifewater Stream (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can unleash a surge of restorative elemental water that aids your
allies. This ability affects creatures in a 30-foot line extending from your palm, and you can
select one of the following conditions to remove from the affected creatures: fatigued, shaken, or
sickened. Creatures affected by a more extreme version of the chosen condition (such as exhausted
for fatigued, frightened for shaken, and nauseated for sickened) have their condition reduced to the
less extreme version.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day, and you can choose from the following
additional conditions: blinded, deafened, dazed, and staggered.

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability three times per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Elemental blast
