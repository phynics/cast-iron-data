**Title:** Bonus Feats

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell with a range of &quot&personal,&quot& you gain a luck bonus
equal to the spell's level on all your saving throws for 1 round.

### Bloodline Powers

You are destined for great things, and the powers that you gain serve to protect you.

**Touch of Destiny (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can touch a creature, giving it an insight bonus on attack rolls, skill checks,
ability checks, and saving throws equal to half your sorcerer level (minimum 1) for 1 round.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Fated (Su)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain a +1 luck bonus on all of your saving throws and to your AC during surprise
rounds and when you are otherwise unaware of an attack.

At 7th level: This bonus +2.

At 11th level: This bonus +3.

At 15th level: This bonus +4.

At 19th level: This bonus +5.

**It Was Meant To Be (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you may reroll any one attack roll, critical hit confirmation roll, or
level check made to overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). You must decide to use
this ability after the first roll is made but before the results are revealed by the GM. You must
take the second result, even if it is worse.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Within Reach (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, when an attack or spell that causes damage would result in your death,
you may attempt a DC 20 Will save. If successful, you are instead reduced to -1 hit points and are
automatically [stabilized](ref/misc/stable). The bonus from your _fated_ ability applies to this
save.

**Flavor:** Your ultimate destiny is drawing near.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Destiny Realized (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can automatically succeed at one caster level check made to overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). You must use this ability before making the roll.

**Flavor:** Your moment of destiny is at hand.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Any critical threats made against you only confirm if the second roll results in a
natural 20 on the die. Any critical threats you score with a spell are automatically confirmed.

### Karmic (Wildblooded Mutation)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 71\
The universe punishes those who interfere with destiny.

**Replacement Bloodline Arcana**

**Passive:** If you are casting defensively because a creature is threatening you and you fail your
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check, one of the creatures that is threatening you provokes
an attack of opportunity from you or from one ally who is adjacent to the enemy. You decide which
creature provokes this attack and which of its adjacent opponents may make the attack.

### Bloodline Powers

Your blood responds angrily when you are attacked.

**Fate's Retribution (Su)**

**Immediate:** When you are hit by a melee attack you can curse the creature that struck you. The
target takes a -2 penalty on all attack and damage rolls for 1d4 rounds. A Will save (DC 10 + half
your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier) negates this effect.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Touch of destiny

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day
