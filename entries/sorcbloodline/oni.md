### Bloodline Powers

While some would call you cursed, you have learned to unlock the powers of your oni heritage. Your
emergent cruelty becomes more dominant as your power increases.

**Touch of Agony (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can make a melee touch attack that wracks a living creature with agonizing pain.
The target takes 1d4 points of nonlethal damage each round for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level (minimum 1).

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Altered Form (Sp)**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to change shape (as the spell [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self))
for a number of minutes per day equal to your sorcerer level. These rounds need not be consecutive.

**At 17th Level:** The duration of this effect is no longer limited, and you may remain in your
chosen form as long as you want.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Windborne (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can turn gaseous (as the spell [gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)) for a number
of rounds per day equal to your sorcerer level. These rounds need not be consecutive.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

At 11th level: Your speed while gaseous increases by +10 feet.

At 13th level: Your speed while gaseous increases by +20 feet.

At 15th level: Your speed while gaseous increases by +30 feet.

At 17th level: Your speed while gaseous increases by +40 feet.

At 19th level: Your speed while gaseous increases by +50 feet.

**Oni Healing (Su)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** If your hit points drop below 0, you automatically stabilize and begin regenerating 2
hit points per round for a number of rounds per day equal to your sorcerer level.

While regenerating in this way, you are not affected by spells or effects that would cause you to
continue dying, such as [bleed](ref/spell/bleed), but if you take any acid or fire damage, you stop
regenerating for the rest of the day (but remain stabilized) and you are affected by such effects as
normal.

**Special:** You still die if your hit points reach a negative number equal to your Constitution
score, regardless of whether you are regenerating. This ability only works once per day - if you
drop below 0 hit points a second time, you do not benefit from this ability.

**Hedonistic Master (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to change your shape between your natural form and that of any one
Large humanoid creature of the giant subtype at will (as the spell
[giant form I](ref/spell/giant-form-1)). You must choose which creature you wish to be able to
change into when you reach 20th level; once you make this decision, you cannot change it. You can
switch between your forms at will, and can assume your giant form for as long as you want.

**Flavor:** You fully embrace your oni heritage to unlock its maximum potential.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** In addition, you gain SR equal to 6 + your sorcerer level, and whenever you cast a
spell of the [charm](ref/spelldef/charm) or [compulsion](ref/spelldef/compulsion) subschool, the
spell's DC increases by +2.
