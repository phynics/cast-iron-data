**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** When casting any spell that deals fire damage, you can instead heal your targets. The
spell deals no damage, and living creatures affected by the spell instead regain a number of hit
points equal to half the fire damage the spell would normally deal.

### Bloodline Powers

The phoenix is an elemental creature of sky and primal fire, and its blood within you sings with a
similar power.

**The Unseen World (Su)**

**Flavor:** The phoenix's blood drives you to find and save lost knowledge and magical items.

**At 5th Level:** As a **swift action**, you can automatically identify the properties of a
non-cursed magic item you hold; you must still identify a cursed item as normal to correctly
identify it as cursed. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier
per day.

**Info:** You gain [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) and [read magic](ref/spell/read-magic) as
spells known.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Immolation (Su)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Swift:** You gain the ability to surround yourself in fire. This fire burns for a number of rounds
per day equal to your character level plus your Charisma bonus. These rounds do not have to be
consecutive.

Any unarmed attacks you make while affected by immolation deal an additional 1d6 points of fire
damage, and any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you while you're affected by _immolation_
also takes 1d6 points of fire damage.

**Vermilion Wings (Su)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Standard:** You gain the ability to grow a pair of phoenix wings from your back. The wings grant
you a fly speed of 60 feet with good maneuverability. You can dismiss the wings as a **free
action**.

**Restoring Flames (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can cast [greater restoration](ref/spell/restoration-greater) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Rebirth (Su)**

**Ability:** When you die, you are brought back to life, as
[true resurrection](ref/spell/true-resurrection), after 1 minute. This ability can be used only once
every 24 hours, and if you are slain again within this period, your death is permanent.

**Flavor:** The full power of a phoenix erupts from within you if you perish.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
