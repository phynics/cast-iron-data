**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals energy damage, you can change the type of damage
to acid. This effect also changes the spell's descriptors to match this energy type.

### Bloodline Powers

Like a shaitan, you have both natural power over earth, and several other genie-based abilities.

**Acid Ray (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash an elemental ray, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. This ray deals acid damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
sorcerer level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** Your acid resistance increases to 20.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain acid [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10.

**Avalanche (Su)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Special:** If the target is in contact with earth, stone, or rock, you gain a +4 bonus on your CMB
check.

**Swift:** Whenever you hit a single target with a spell that deals damage, you may make a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) check. Your CMB for this maneuver is equal to your sorcerer caster
level + your Charisma bonus. You can make this maneuver even if the target is not in melee range,
and you do not provoke an attack of opportunity for making this maneuver.

**Elemental Movement (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a burrow speed of 30 feet.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Power of the Shaitan (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Such wishes must begin with the words &quot&I
wish,&quot& and cannot duplicate a wish you have granted within the past 24 hours. If you use this
ability to duplicate a spell with a costly material component, you must provide that component.

**Ability2:** You can use _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ once per day to travel to or from
the Plane of Earth.

**Flavor:** You gain the power of genies to grant wishes.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You also become immune to acid damage.
