### Bloodline Powers

The dark energies that fuel your existence and undying hunger form the basis of your power.

**Ghoulish Claws (Su)**

**Free:** You can grow claws. These claws are treated as [natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks),
allowing you to make two claw attacks as a full attack using your full base attack bonus. Each of
these attacks deals 1d4 points of damage + your Strength modifier (1d3 if you are Small).

**At 5th Level:** Creatures damaged with your claw attack are [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for 1
round, as the ghoul ability (Fortitude negates).

**At 7th Level:** The duration of the paralysis increases to 1d4+1 rounds, and the claws are
considered magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming DR.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** Per the [paralysis](ref/umr/paralysis) ability, the DC is 10 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your level (or Hit Dice) + your Constitution modifier. Per
the [ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul) entry, elves are immune to this effect.

If you're a ghoul with this bloodline, instead of gaining this ability, your levels in sorcerer
stack with your racial Hit Dice when determining the DC of your _[paralysis](ref/rule/paralysis)_
and _[disease](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/disease)_ special abilities.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier rounds/day

**Leathery Skin (Ex)**

**At 15th Level:** Your natural armor bonus increases to +4.

**At 9th Level:** Your cold resistance increases to 10 and natural armor bonus increases to +2.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain cold [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 and a +1 natural armor bonus to AC.

**Ravenous Frenzy (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain the benefits of [haste](ref/spell/haste). Each time you hit a creature with
two or more claw attacks during a full attack while affected by this ability, that creature takes
1d4 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per sorcerer level

**Earth Crawler (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a burrow speed of 30 feet. Additionally, as long as you are surrounded by at
least 5 feet of dirt or natural stone on all sides, you gain
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 10. You can heal a number of hit points per day equal to 10
&times& your sorcerer level with this ability.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Ghoulish Aspect (Su)**

**Flavor:** You take on aspects of the nature and appearance of ghouls.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You no longer feel the need to eat and are immune to hunger (though you are still able
to consume flesh if you choose to). You gain immunity to cold, nonlethal damage, paralysis, and
sleep. You also gain DR 5/-.

Additionally, you gain the [stench](ref/umr/stench) ability with a radius of 10 feet. Creatures that
fail their Fortitude saving throws are [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1d6+4 minutes. Use half
your sorcerer level instead of half your racial Hit Dice to determine the saving throw DC.
