## Subtype: Good

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary, Bestiary 2, Bestiary 3, Bestiary 4, Bestiary 5

This subtype is usually applied to outsiders native to the good-aligned Outer Planes. Most creatures
that have this subtype also have good alignments; however, if their alignments change, they still
retain the subtype. Any effect that depends on alignment affects a creature with this subtype as if
the creature had a good alignment, no matter what its alignment actually is. The creature also
suffers effects according to its actual alignment. A creature with the good subtype overcomes damage
reduction as if its natural weapons and any weapons it wields were good-aligned (see
[Damage Reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)).
