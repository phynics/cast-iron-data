## Subtype: Kyton

**Sources:** Bestiary 4, Bestiary 3

Kytons are a race of lawful evil outsiders native to the Shadow Plane who feed on fear and pain.
Kytons possess the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry).

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet.
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to cold.
- **Regeneration (Ex):** The extent of a kyton's [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) varies
  according to type, and can be neutralized by good weapons, good spells, and silver weapons.
- **Unnerving Gaze (Su):** All kytons have a gaze attack that manipulates the perceptions of those
  who look upon them. An unnerving gaze has a range of 30 feet, and can be negated by a Will save -
  the exact effects caused by a particular kyton's unnerving gaze depend on the type of kyton. All
  kytons are immune to the unnerving gazes of other kytons. Unnerving gaze is always a
  mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.
