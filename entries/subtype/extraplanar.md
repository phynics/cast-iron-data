## Subtype: Extraplanar

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary, Bestiary 2, Bestiary 3, Bestiary 4, Bestiary 5

This subtype is applied to any creature when it is on a plane other than its native plane. A
creature that travels the planes can gain or lose this subtype as it goes from plane to plane.
Monster entries assume that encounters with creatures take place on the Material Plane, and every
creature whose native plane is not the Material Plane has the extraplanar subtype (but would not
have it when on its home plane). Creatures native to the Material Plane gain the extraplanar subtype
if they leave the Material Plane. No creature has the extraplanar subtype when it is on a Transitive
Plane (the Astral Plane, the Ethereal Plane, or the Plane of Shadow).
