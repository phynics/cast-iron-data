## Subtype: Oni

An [oni](ref/family/oni) is an evil spirit who takes humanoid form to become a
[native](ref/subtype/native) [outsider](ref/type/outsider). All oni have the following traits,
unless otherwise noted in a specific creature's entry.

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
- **Change Shape (Su):** All oni are shapechangers with the [shapechanger](ref/subtype/shapechanger)
  subtype, but an oni takes only other shapes similar to its normal humanoid form.
- **Humanoid Shape:** As evil spirits clad in humanoid flesh, all oni possess a humanoid subtype.
- **Regeneration (Ex):** The rate of regeneration and damage type that suspends it depends on the
  type of oni, but is typically acid or fire.
