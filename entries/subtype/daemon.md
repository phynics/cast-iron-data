## Subtype: Daemon

[Daemons](ref/family/daemon) are neutral evil outsiders that eat souls and thrive on disaster and
ruin. They have the following traits unless otherwise noted.

- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to acid, death effects, disease, and poison.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to cold 10, electricity 10, and fire 10.
- **Summon (Sp):** Daemons share the ability to [summon](ref/umr/summon) others of their kind,
  typically another of their type or a small number of less powerful daemons.
- [Telepathy](ref/umr/telepathy).
- Except where otherwise noted, daemons speak Abyssal, Draconic, and Infernal.
