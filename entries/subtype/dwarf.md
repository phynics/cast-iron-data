## Subtype: Dwarf

**Sources:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary

This [humanoid](ref/type/humanoid) subtype is applied to [dwarves](ref/race/dwarf) and creatures
related to dwarves. Creatures with the dwarf subtype have [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet.
