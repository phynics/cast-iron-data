## Subtype: Aquatic

These creatures always have swim speeds and can move in water without making [Swim](ref/skill/swim)
checks. An aquatic creature can breathe water. It cannot breathe air unless it has the
[amphibious](ref/umr/amphibious) special quality. Aquatic creatures always treat Swim as a class
skill.
