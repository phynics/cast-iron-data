## Subtype: Goblinoid

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary, Bestiary 4

Goblinoids are stealthy [humanoids](ref/type/humanoid) who live by hunting and raiding. All
goblinoids speak Goblin and treat [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) as a class skill.
