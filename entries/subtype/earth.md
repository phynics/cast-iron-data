## Subtype: Earth

This subtype is usually used for outsiders with a connection to the Elemental
[Plane of Earth](ref/rule/plane-of-earth). Earth creatures usually have burrow speeds, and most
earth creatures can burrow through solid rock. Earth creatures with a burrow speed have
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense).
