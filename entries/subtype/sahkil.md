## Subtype: Sahkil

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Bestiary 5

[Sahkils](ref/family/sahkil) are neutral evil [outsiders](ref/type/outsider) native to the
[Ethereal Plane](ref/rule/ethereal-plane). They have the following traits (unless otherwise noted in
a creature's entry).

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to death effects, disease, fear effects, and poison.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to cold 10, electricity 10, and sonic 10.
- Most sahkils are resistant to magic. Such a sahkil has SR equal to 11 + its CR.
- Except where otherwise noted, sahkils speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Infernal.
- [Telepathy](ref/umr/telepathy).
- **Easy to Call (Ex):** Sahkils count as having 4 fewer Hit Dice (minimum 2) for purposes of spells
  or effects that call outsiders, such as [planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding). However, they
  receive a racial bonus equal to 1/2 their Hit Dice on Charisma checks to break free of
  _[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding)_ spells and similar effects, and their
  [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) counts as if it were 5 higher for the purpose of
  breaking free of _[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding)_ spells and similar effects.
- **Emotional Focus (Ex):** Whenever a sahkil casts a spell or uses a
  [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) or effect with the emotion or fear descriptors
  that allows a save, the DC is increased by 2.
- **Look of Fear (Su):** All sahkils have a [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack with a range of 30 feet
  (though when a sahkil is on the Ethereal Plane, it functions against creatures on the Material
  Plane that can see ethereal creatures), and can be negated by a Will save. The exact effects
  caused by a particular sahkil's look of fear depend on the type of sahkil. All sahkils are immune
  to their own look of fear and that of other sahkils. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The
  save DC is Charisma-based and includes the +2 bonus from the sahkil's emotional focus ability.
- **Skip Between (Su):** Many sahkils can shift between the Ethereal Plane and the Material Plane as
  a move action. This ability is otherwise identical to [ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt)
  (CL 15th).
- **Spirit Touch (Ex):** A sahkil's natural weapons, as well as any weapon it wields, are treated as
  though they had the [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon special ability.
