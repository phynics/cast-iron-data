## Subtype: Skinwalker

**Sources:** Bestiary 5

This subtype is applied to the [humanoid](ref/type/humanoid) race known as
[skinwalkers](ref/race/skinwalker), who are similar to [lycanthropes](ref/template/lycanthrope), but
aren't afflicted with the same curse.
