## Subtype: Agathion

[Agathions](ref/family/agathion) are beast-aspect outsiders native to [Nirvana](ref/rule/nirvana).
They have the following traits.

- [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision)
- +4 racial bonus on saving throws against poison.
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to electricity and petrification.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to cold 10 and sonic 10.
- Except where otherwise noted, agathions speak Celestial, Infernal, and Draconic.
- [Lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) as a paladin whose level equals the agathion's Hit Dice.
- **Speak with Animals (Sp):** An agathion gains [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)
  as a constant [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (caster level equal to the
  agathion's Hit Dice), except using it is a free action that does not require sound.
- **Truespeech (Su):** All agathions can speak with any creature that has a language, as though
  using a [tongues](ref/spell/tongues) spell (caster level equal to the agathion's Hit Dice). This
  ability is always active.
