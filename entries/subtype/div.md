## Subtype: Div

**Sources:** Bestiary 3

[Divs](ref/family/div) are neutral evil outsiders that sow misfortune and ruin. They have the
following traits unless otherwise noted.

- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to fire and poison.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to acid 10 and electricity 10.
- **See in Darkness (Su):** Some divs can see perfectly in darkness of any kind, even that created
  by a [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) spell.
- **Summon (Sp):** Some divs share the ability to [summon](ref/umr/summon) others of their kind.
  Each entry describes the success chance and type of divs summoned.
- [Telepathy](ref/umr/telepathy).
- Except where otherwise noted, divs speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Infernal.
