## Subtype: Devil

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary, Bestiary 2, Bestiary 3, Bestiary 4, Bestiary 5

[Devils](ref/family/devil) are lawful evil outsiders that hail from the plane of
[Hell](ref/rule/hell). Devils have a particular suite of traits (unless otherwise noted in a
creature's entry).

- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to fire and poison.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to acid 10 and cold 10.
- A devil's natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as lawful and evil for
  the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
- Except where otherwise noted, devils speak Celestial, Draconic, and Infernal.
- [Telepathy](ref/umr/telepathy).
- **See in Darkness (Su):** Some devils can [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) of any kind,
  even that created by a [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) spell.
- **Summon (Sp):** Devils share the ability to [summon](ref/umr/summon) others of their kind,
  typically another of their type or a small number of less powerful devils.
