## Subtype: Demon

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary, Bestiary 2, Bestiary 3, Bestiary 4, Bestiary 5

[Demons](ref/family/demon) are chaotic evil outsiders that call [the Abyss](ref/rule/the-abyss)
their home. Demons have a particular suite of traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry),
as summarized here.

- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to electricity and poison.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to acid 10, cold 10, and fire 10.
- A demon's natural weapons, as well as any weapon it wields, are treated as chaotic and evil for
  the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
- Except where otherwise noted, demons speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Draconic.
- [Telepathy](ref/umr/telepathy).
- **Summon (Sp):** Demons share the ability to [summon](ref/umr/summon) others of their kind,
  typically another of their type or a small number of less powerful demons.
