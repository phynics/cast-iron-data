## Subtype: Air

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary, Bestiary 2, Bestiary 3, Bestiary 4, Bestiary 5

This subtype is usually used for outsiders that have a connection to the Elemental
[Plane of Air](ref/rule/plane-of-air). Air creatures always have fly speeds and usually have perfect
maneuverability. Air creatures treat [Fly](ref/skill/fly) as a class skill.
