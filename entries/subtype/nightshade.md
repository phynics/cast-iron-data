## Subtype: Nightshade

**Sources:** Bestiary 2 pg 308

Nightshades are monstrous [undead](ref/type/undead) composed of shadow and evil. They have the
following traits.

- [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
- **Desecrating Aura (Su):** All nightshades have a 30-foot-radius emanation equivalent to a
  [desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate) spell centered on a shrine of evil power. Undead within this
  radius (including the nightshade) gain a +2 profane bonus on attack and damage rolls and saving
  throws, as well as +2 hit points per die, and the save DC of channeled negative energy is
  increased by +6 (these adjustments are included for the nightshades in their entries). This aura
  can be negated by [dispel evil](ref/spell/dispel-evil), but a nightshade can reactivate it on its
  turn as a free action. A desecrating aura suppresses and is suppressed by
  [consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate) or [hallow](ref/spell/hallow); both effects are negated within
  any overlapping area of effect.
- **Channel Energy (Su):** A nightshade can [channel negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) as
  cleric of a level equal to its base CR. It can use this ability a number of times per day equal to
  3 + its Charisma modifier.
- **Darksense (Ex):** Nightshades gain [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) in dim light and
  darkness. Regardless of light conditions, they can detect living creatures and their health within
  60 feet, as [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) with [deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch) continuously
  active. [Mind blank](ref/spell/mind-blank) and [nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection) prevent the
  latter effect but not the nightshade's _[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing)._
- **Light Aversion (Ex):** A nightshade in bright light becomes [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) - the
  penalties from this condition are doubled when the nightshade is in natural sunlight.
- **Summon (Sp):** Nightshades can [summon](ref/umr/summon) undead creatures. They can be summoned
  only within areas of darkness, and summoned undead cannot create spawn. The exact type and number
  of undead they can summon vary according to the nightshade in question, as detailed in each
  nightshade's entry.
