## Subtype: Gnome

**Sources:** Bestiary 2, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary

This [humanoid](ref/type/humanoid) subtype is applied to [gnomes](ref/race/gnome) and creatures
related to gnomes. Creatures with the gnome subtype have
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
