## Subtype: Inevitable

**Sources:** Bestiary 2

Inevitables are construct-like outsiders built by the [axiomites](ref/monster/axiomite) to enforce
law. They have the following traits.

- [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
- **Constructed (Ex):** Although inevitables are living outsiders, their bodies are constructed of
  physical components and in many ways they function as constructs. For the purposes of effects
  targeting creatures by type (such as a ranger's [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) and
  [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapons), inevitables count as both outsiders and constructs. They are
  [immune](ref/umr/immunity) to death effects, disease, mind-affecting effects, necromancy effects,
  paralysis, poison, sleep, stun, and any effect that requires a Fortitude save (unless the effect
  also works on objects, or is harmless). Inevitables are not subject to nonlethal damage, ability
  damage, ability drain, fatigue, exhaustion, or energy drain. They are not at risk of death from
  massive damage. They have bonus hit points as [constructs](ref/type/construct) of their size.
- **Saves:** An inevitable's good saving throws are Fortitude and Will.
- **Skills:** In addition to the class skills all [outsiders](ref/type/outsider) have, inevitables
  have [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy),
  [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) as class skills.
- **Regeneration (Ex):** Inevitables have [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) (chaotic). The
  regeneration amount varies by the type of inevitable.
- **Truespeech (Su):** An inevitable can speak with any creature that has a language, as if using a
  [tongues](ref/spell/tongues) spell (caster level 14th). This ability is always active.
