## Subtype: Demodand

[Demodands](ref/family/demodand) are chaotic evil outsiders who stalk
[the Abyss](ref/rule/the-abyss). Unless otherwise noted in the creature's entry, a demodand has the
following traits.

- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to acid and poison.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to fire 10 and cold 10.
- A demodand's natural weapons, as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as chaotic and evil
  for the purpose of resolving [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
- Except when otherwise noted, demodands speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Common.
- **Faith-Stealing Strike (Su):** When a demodand's natural attack or melee weapon damages a
  creature capable of casting divine spells, that creature must succeed at a Will saving throw or be
  unable to cast any divine spells for 1 round. Once a creature succeeds at this save, it is immune
  to further faith-stealing strikes from that particular demodand for 24 hours. The save DC is
  Charisma-based.
- **Heretical Soul (Ex):** All demodands gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against divine spells. In
  addition, any attempts to scry on a demodand using divine magic automatically fail. The caster can
  see the scried area normally, but the demodand simply does not appear.
- **Summon (Sp):** Demodands share the ability to [summon](ref/umr/summon) others of their kind,
  typically another of their type or a small number of less powerful demodands.
