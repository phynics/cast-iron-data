## Subtype: Azata

[Azatas](ref/family/azata) are a race of celestials, or good outsiders, native to chaotic
good-aligned Outer Planes, such as [Elysium](ref/rule/elysium). An azata has the following traits
(unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry).

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to electricity and petrification.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to cold 10 and fire 10.
- **Truespeech (Su):** All azatas can speak with any creature that has a language, as though using a
  [tongues](ref/spell/tongues) spell (caster level 14th). This ability is always active.
