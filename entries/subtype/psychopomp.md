## Subtype: Psychopomp

[Psychopomps](ref/family/psychopomp) are neutral [outsiders](ref/type/outsider) native to the
Boneyard. Psychopomps have the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry).

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
- DR 5/adamantine.
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to death effects, disease, and poison.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to cold 10 and electricity 10.
- Psychopomps speak Abyssal, Celestial, and Infernal.
- **Spirit Touch (Ex):** A psychopomp's natural weapons, as well as any weapon it wields, are
  treated as though they had the [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon special
  ability.
- **Spiritsense (Su):** A psychopomp notices, locates, and can distinguish between living and undead
  creatures within 60 feet, just as if it had the [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) ability. This
  sense does not allow it to detect objects, but it does allow it to notice living things that are
  not creatures.
