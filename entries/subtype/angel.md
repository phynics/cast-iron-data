## Subtype: Angel

**Sources:** Bestiary 6, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary, Bestiary 2, Bestiary 4, Bestiary 5

[Angels](ref/family/angel) are a race of good outsiders native to the good-aligned Outer Planes. An
angel has the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry).

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
- +4 racial bonus on saves against poison.
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to acid, cold, and petrification.
- [Resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to electricity 10 and fire 10.
- **Protective Aura (Su):** Against attacks from or effects created by evil creatures, this ability
  provides a +4 deflection bonus to AC and a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws to anyone within
  20 feet of the angel. Otherwise, it functions as a
  [magic circle against evil](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-evil) effect and a
  [lesser globe of invulnerability](ref/spell/globe-of-invulnerability-lesser), both with a radius
  of 20 feet (caster level equals the angel's HD). The defensive benefits from the circle are not
  included in an angel's statistics.
- **Truespeech (Su):** All angels can speak with any creature that has a language, as though using a
  [tongues](ref/spell/tongues) spell (caster level equal to the angel's Hit Dice). This ability is
  always active.
