**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 250\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,000 gp; **Weight** 500 lbs.

This large tree bears both vibrant red and dull yellow apples. The imbiber of a red apple gains a +5
insight bonus on her next Knowledge check. Consuming a yellow apple grants a single use of
_[augury](ref/spell/augury)._ The effects of an apple wear off after 24 hours, whether or not its
abilities have been used.

A tree of knowledge produces one red apple per day and one yellow apple per week during its growing
season, which lasts for 13 weeks in the spring. The tree has no more than seven red apples and one
yellow apple at any given time.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Cultivate Magic Plants](ref/feat/cultivate-magic-plants),
[augury](ref/spell/augury), cultivator must have 1 rank in Knowledge (nature); **Cost** 1,500 gp
