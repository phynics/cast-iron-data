**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 16\
**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

A creature who consumes an ambrosial lotus must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or be overtaken by such
a state of total bliss so as to be almost oblivious to the world around him. The creature's
Intelligence and Charisma scores drop to 1, and the creature can no longer cast spells, understand
language, or communicate. These effects are permanent unless removed with a [heal](ref/spell/heal),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [miracle](ref/spell/miracle), or [wish](ref/spell/wish)
spell.

An ambrosial lotus stalk grows one flower per day during its 13-week growing period in spring.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Cultivate Magic Plants](ref/feat/cultivate-magic-plants),
[feeblemind](ref/spell/feeblemind), cultivator must have 10 ranks in Knowledge (nature); **Cost**
12,500 gp
