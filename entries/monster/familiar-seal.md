### Description

Sleek-bodied aquatic mammals, seals spend the majority of their lives in the water, whether in the
harbors of tropical ports or amid wandering icebergs. They prefer rocky beaches, upon which they
rest and raise their young, and from which they dive to hunt fish, sea birds, squid, and other small
aquatic creatures. They are well known for their vocal communications consisting of barks, grunts,
and flipper slaps - a trait that causes many humanoids to remark on their intelligence and the ease
with which they can be trained.
