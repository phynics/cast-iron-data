### Special Abilities

**Spit (Ex)** Once per hour, a camel can regurgitate the contents of its stomach, spitting the foul
material at a single target within 10 feet. The target must make a DC 13 Fortitude save or be
sickened for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based.

### Description

Camels are large, desert-dwelling herd animals noted for their stamina and ill tempers. A typical
camel stands about 6 feet at the shoulder and 7 feet at the hump.
