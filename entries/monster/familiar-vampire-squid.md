### Special Abilities

**Photophores (Ex)** Once per day as a standard action while underwater, the vampire squid can
release a 15-foot cone of disorienting bioluminescent mucus from its photophores, illuminating the
area with [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light). A creature within this shimmering cloud of blue
light must succeed at a DC 11 Fortitude save or be [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for as long as it
remains in the cloud and for 1 round thereafter. The cloud persists for 1d4+1 rounds. The save DC is
Constitution-based.

**Pressure Adaptation (Ex)** A vampire squid takes no [pressure damage](ref/rule/water-dangers) from
changes in water depth.

**Tentacles (Ex)** A vampire squid's tentacles all strike as a single primary attack but only to
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple).

### Description

The vampire squid - so called for its red eyes and cloak-like webbing - is a cephalopod from the
lightless depths of the oceans. This eerie mollusk is covered in photophores, which it can use to
create a defensive barrage of light when agitated.

A vampire squid can reach a total body length of up to 1 foot and weighs 1 pound.
