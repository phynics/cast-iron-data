### Special Abilities

**Slime Cloud (Ex)** While underwater, as a free action, a Varisian hagfish can secrete slime that
transforms a 5-foot cube of water into a transparent cloud of viscous slime. This slime is the
aquatic equivalent of [rough terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles), and creatures swimming
through it expend 2 squares of movement. The slime cloud remains for 10 rounds before dissipating.
Hagfish are unaffected by these slime clouds. The slime also grants the hagfish a +4 bonus on
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) checks.

### Description

These cousins to normal hagfish most commonly appear in the Varisian Gulf. They can reach lengths of
4 feet and weigh up to 15 pounds.
