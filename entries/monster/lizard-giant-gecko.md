### Description

These lizards are mostly docile and shy away from attacking creatures larger than a cat or dog.
Despite their shyness, they are ultimately curious creatures, and often approach bigger creatures
simply to investigate. Of course, a giant gecko is more than capable of defending itself, biting
creatures that react violently to the lizard when fleeing is not an option. Some humanoids train
giant geckos as bestial guardians.

Smaller humanoids (in particular, goblins) can even use the creatures as mounts, taking advantage of
their superior climbing skills to attack from seemingly impossible positions and angles. A giant
gecko measures 8 feet from nose to tail and weighs 120 pounds. Coloration among giant geckos can
vary wildly, though most are some shade of green.
