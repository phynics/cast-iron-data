### Description

In areas where large creatures like megafauna or dinosaurs dwell, the giant tick behaves much like
its smaller kin - it lies in wait in the undergrowth, and when a suitable host passes by, it drops
onto the body to feed. In other areas, these vermin are much more aggressive, and actively hunt
smaller prey like dogs, livestock, or even humanoids. A giant tick drains blood quickly, but once it
has caused 6 points of Constitution damage, it drops off, sated, to crawl away to digest. A giant
tick is 3 feet long and weighs 50 pounds.
