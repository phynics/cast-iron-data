### Description

Of the multitude of slithering predators that infest jungles, forests, and swamps, few inspire such
terror as the giant anaconda. Incredibly strong, fearless hunters, these creatures are capable of
taking down and eating elephants, dinosaurs, and giants. Their olive green and black mottled scales
afford them exceptional camouflage in the murky waters they call home. A giant anaconda can grow to
a length of 60 feet.
