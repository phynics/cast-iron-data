### Special Abilities

**Devour Soul (Su)** As a standard action, an astradaemon that begins its turn with a
[grappled](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) opponent can attempt to draw out and consume the soul of its
victim, killing it instantly. This ability only works on living creatures, which may resist with a
DC 25 Fortitude saving throw. The save is Constitution-based. For every 5 HD of the slain creature,
the daemon gains a +1 profane bonus on attacks, saving throws, and checks for 24 hours. This ability
does not consume all of the soul, and pieces of it still exist after the daemon completes its feast
(enough to be able to resurrect the slain victim normally).

**Soul Siphon (Su)** If a Small or larger living creature dies within 10 feet of an astradaemon, the
daemon gains 1d8 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) and a +2 bonus to Strength
for 10 minutes. These bonuses stack with themselves. Incorporeal undead and living spirits traveling
outside the body (such as a person using [astral projection](ref/spell/astral-projection) or
[magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar)) take 1d8 points of damage each round within the daemon's aura.

### Description

Believed to be creations of the Four Horsemen, astradaemons live out their existence in search of
souls to harvest. These deadly creatures are ravening planar predators, openly hunting throughout
the void for souls on which to feed. These voracious creatures are the personifications of death
resulting from negative energy or level drain. Their vile touch drains life force from their
enemies, and even perishing near them sates their thirst for life and souls.
