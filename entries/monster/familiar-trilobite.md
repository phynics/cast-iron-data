### Special Abilities

**Curl (Ex)** As a standard action, a trilobite can curl into a ball, increasing its natural armor
bonus by 2 but preventing it from taking any move actions. Uncurling is a standard action.

### Description

Ancient arthropods adapted to scavenging the ocean floor, trilobites have both resilient exteriors
and keen senses honed for use in the lightless depths of the sea, and are known for curling into
balls to protect themselves.
