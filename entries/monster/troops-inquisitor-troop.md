### Special Abilities

**Cunning Initiative (Ex)** An inquisitor troop adds its Wisdom bonus to its initiative checks.

**Judgment of Protection (Su)** An inquisitor troop gains a +1 profane bonus to its armor class.

**Fast Healing (Sp)** An inquisitor troop's [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) is a reflection of
the fact that each round, some of the inquisitors in the troop cast cure light wounds spells on
wounded members. Not all inquisitors in a troop use this ability simultaneously, and they can only
grant fast healing to the troop for up to 10 rounds per day. Activating this ability is a free
action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Protect Ally (Ex)** See dottari troop above.

### Description

House Thrune and the Church of Asmodeus can mobilize special groups of dottari and inquisitors known
as troops. Typically, a troop is placed under the command of a captain or priest who does not
function as part of the troop.
