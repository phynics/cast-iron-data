### Description

These large land animals, majestically wandering the plains in tightly knit family herds, are
symbols of wisdom and strength. Having few natural predators, elephants are sometimes hunted for
their ivory tusks. These tusks often become jewelry, statuettes, and trinkets for the wealthy.
