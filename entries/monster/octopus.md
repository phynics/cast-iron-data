### Special Abilities

**Ink Cloud (Ex)** An octopus can emit a 10-foot-radius sphere of ink once per minute as a free
action. The ink provides total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) in water, and persists for 1
minute.

**Jet (Ex)** An octopus can jet backward once per round as a full-round action, at a speed of 200
feet. It must move in a straight line while jetting, and does not provoke attacks of opportunity
when it does so.

**Poison (Ex)**

**Type:** Poison (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 13

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

### Description

The octopus is a cunning animal capable of using complex tactics to get food.
