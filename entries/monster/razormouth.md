### Special Abilities

**Savage Bite (Ex)** A razormouth's bite threatens a critical hit on a 19-20, and on a confirmed
critical hit deals ×3 damage and 2d6 points of bleed damage.

### Description

Razormouths most often reside in mountainous areas or large canyons with steep cliffs. While most
creatures would find such areas difficult to traverse, razormouths are especially mobile, being
choice climbers that are able to leap from cliff faces to outcrops with singular alacrity. This
exceptional mobility, along with a devastating pair of long, serrated fangs, make the fearsome
razormouths unparalleled hunters, and few survive getting too close to their fangs.
