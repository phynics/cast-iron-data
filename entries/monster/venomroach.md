### Description

Like their smaller and more mundane cousins, venomroaches lurk along the fringes of humanoid
civilization, skittering through the dark corners of the world. Dangerous denizens of sewers and
alleyways, venomroaches have a pair of traits that set them apart from most of their kin. As their
name would suggest, these vermin generate a potent toxin that withers tissue and poisons the blood.
Unlike their smaller cousins, venomroaches also have enlarged mouthparts that give them a potent
bite attack.

Venomroaches are 8 feet long and weigh 120 pounds.
