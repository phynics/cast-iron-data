### Description

Lording over the darkest swamps and marshes, black dragons are the undisputed masters of their
domain, ruling through cruelty and intimidation. Those who dwell within a black dragon's reach live
in fear. Black dragons tend to make their lairs in remote parts of the swamp, preferably in caves at
the bottom of dark and fetid pools. Inside, they pile up their filthy treasure and sleep amid the
roots and muck. Black dragons prefer their food a bit rotten and will often allow a meal to sit in a
pool for days before consuming it. Black dragons prefer treasures that do not rot or decay, making
their hoard, full of coins, gemstones, jewelry, and other objects made from stone or metal.
