### Description

Larger, more aggressive, and far more deadly than its lesser cousins, the dire bear (known to
barbarian tribes variously as the cave bear or the short-faced bear) is a primeval menace indeed.
This behemoth avoids more civilized reaches of the world, preferring to dwell in remote wilderness
locations. A dire bear is a rare and terrifying sight - especially to those not used to normal
bears.

Unlike most bears, a dire bear is short-tempered and swift to anger when confronted with intruders
in its territory. Like a [grizzly](ref/monster/bear-grizzly-bear), a dire bear attempts to subdue or
kill with its claws. However, a dire bear is far more likely (and willing) to begin biting a grabbed
foe.
