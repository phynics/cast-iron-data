### Special Abilities

**Construction Points (Ex)** [Animated objects](ref/rule/animated-objects) have a number of
Construction Points (CP) used to purchase abilities and defenses in addition to those presented
above. A medium animated object has 2 CP; differently sized objects have CP totals as detailed on
the size chart below. If an animated object spends more CP than its size category would allow, its
CR increases by 1 (minimum of +1) for every 2 additional CP spent.

- **Additional Attack (Ex, 1 CP):** Gains an additional slam attack.
  ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Additional Movement (Ex, 1 CP):** Gains a new mode of movement (burrow, climb, fly \[clumsy\],
  or swim) at a speed equal to its base speed. ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Additional Natural Attack (Ex, 1 CP):** The object gains an additional natural attack. The
  object must have an appropriate appendage or part for the natural attack (e.g., an animated
  warthog head trophy would be able to take the gore attack, but an animated vase would not).
  ^[2](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-s)^
- **Amalgam (Ex, 2 CP):** The object is made up of smaller but similar objects. Its ability to
  constantly shift its form grants it immunity to critical hits and [flanking](ref/rule/flanking).
  It also does not take additional damage from precision-based attacks, like
  [sneak attacks](ref/ability/sneak-attack). The object can move through spaces half its size
  without [squeezing](ref/misc/squeezing). The object also receives a +10 racial bonus on Stealth
  checks. ^[3](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-t)^
- **Augmented Critical (Ex, 1 CP):** Increase the threat range for the animated object's melee
  attacks by 1 or the threat multiplier by 1. This cannot combine with itself or with the piercing
  attack or slashing attack object abilities. ^[4](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-u)^
- **Burn (Ex, 1 CP):** The object gains [burn](ref/umr/burn) (1d6) with its slam attacks. This can
  be applied multiple times. Its effects stack. ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Constrict (Ex, 1 CP):** Gains [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) with its slam attacks (the object
  must have grab before it can take this ability). ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Exceptional Reach (Ex, 1 CP):** The object gains +5 feet of [reach](ref/misc/reach) with one
  melee attack. Increase reach on all attacks for an additional +1 CP.
  ^[4](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-u)^
- **Faster (Ex, 1 CP):** One of the object's movement modes increases by +10 ft.
  ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Grab (Ex, 1 CP):** Gains [grab](ref/umr/grab) special attack with slam attacks.
  ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Guardian (Ex, 1 CP):** The object is granted the ability to follow basic orders and watch for
  enemies, changing its Wisdom to 10 and granting it a +2 racial bonus on Perception checks.
  Additional CP can be spent on this ability, each increasing the object's Wisdom by an additional
  +2 and improving its racial bonus to Perception by +2.
  ^[6](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-d)^
- **Improved Attack (Ex, 1 CP):** All the animated object's melee or ranged attacks do damage as
  though it were [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments). A crafter must
  purchase Improved Attack separately for melee and ranged attacks.
  ^[4](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-u)^
- **Metal (Ex, 2 CP):** The object is made of common metal. Its [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
  increases to 10, and it gains a +2 increase to its natural armor bonus.
  [Mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) objects cost 4 CP, and gain hardness 15 plus a +4 increase to
  natural armor. [Adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) objects cost 6 CP, gain hardness 20, and
  receive a +6 increase to natural armor. ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Piercing Attack (Ex, 1 CP):** Replace one melee attack with an attack that does the same amount
  of piercing damage and has a ×3 multiplier. Replace all melee attacks for an additional +1 CP.
  Object abilities that specify slam attacks do not work on piercing attacks.
  ^[4](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-u)^
- **Pull (Ex, 1 CP):** The object gains a [pull](ref/umr/pull) (5 feet) special attack with slam
  attacks (the object must have _[grab](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/grab)_
  before it can take this ability). ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Ranged Attack (Ex, 2 CP):** Replace one slam attack with a ranged attack. It does the same
  amount of damage, and has a range of 20 feet. Replace all attacks for an additional +2 CP. Object
  abilities that specify slam attacks do not work on ranged attacks.
  ^[4](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-u)^
- **Resistance (Ex, 2CP):** The object gains resistance 5 to one type of damage (acid, cold,
  electricity, or fire) chosen at the time of the object's creation. This effect can be applied
  multiple times. Its effects do not stack; each time it is applied, it applies to a different type
  of damage. ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Slashing Attack (Ex, 1 CP):** Replace one slam attack with an attack that does slashing damage
  and has either a 19-20 threat range (for blade-like attacks) or a ×3 threat multiplier (for axe-
  or scythe-like attacks). Replace all melee attacks for an additional +1 CP. Object abilities that
  specify slam attacks do not work on slashing attacks.
  ^[4](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-u)^
- **Stone (Ex, 1 CP):** The object is made of stone or crystal. Its [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
  increases to 8 and it gains a +1 increase to its natural armor bonus.
  ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Trample (Ex, 2 CP):** The object gains the [trample](ref/umr/trample) special attack.
  ^[1](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-b)^
- **Trip (Ex, 2 CP):** The object gains the [trip](ref/misc/trip) special ability with one of its
  slam attacks. ^[4](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-u)^

**Flaws** Animated objects can gain more CP by applying flaws, which hamper the object but provide
additional CP to spend on beneficial abilities. If the CP gained in this way is not spent on
beneficial abilities, its CR decreases by 1 for every 2 CP conserved.

- **Brittle (Ex, +1 CP):** The object gains [vulnerability](ref/umr/vulnerabilities) to cold.
  ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Cloth (Ex, +1 CP):** The object is made of thick cloth. Its [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
  decreases to 0. ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Clunky (Ex, +1 CP):** Treat the object as though it had the [staggered](ref/misc/staggered)
  special quality. ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Flammable (Ex, +1 CP):** The object gains [vulnerability](ref/umr/vulnerabilities) to fire.
  ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Haunted (Ex, +1 CP):** The object is haunted by a malevolent spirit. It takes damage from
  positive energy as if it were an undead creature and can be detected by
  [detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead). ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^
- **Slower (Ex, +1 CP):** One of the object's movement modes decreases by –10 ft.
  ^[5](ref/monster/animated-object#footnote-h)^

### Description

An animated object is not simply one monster, but a whole category. The stats presented here are for
a Medium animated object (with 2 CP that have not been spent to gain additional abilities), but any
object can become animated, most commonly via the spell
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects). Permanent animated objects can be built using the
[Craft Construct](ref/feat/craft-construct) feat. Unless an animated object uses a Construction
Point to be made of another material, all animated objects are made of wood or material of
equivalent [hardness](ref/umr/hardness). Creating an animated object of a different size than Medium
can be done simply by adjusting the object's size (and thus adjusting its Strength, Dexterity,
natural armor bonus, and size modifier to attack and AC as detailed
[here](ref/rule/adding-racial-hit-dice)) and Hit Dice.
