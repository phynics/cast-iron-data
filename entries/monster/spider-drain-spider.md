### Description

Already a tiny creature, the drain spider has the ability to compress its legs and body even
further, allowing it to lurk in pipes, niches, and (its favorite hideout) sink or tub drains with
ease. The sewers below Korvosa are infested with drain spiders in certain places. In the wild, drain
spiders prefer to hunt in swamps or along river banks, but they have adapted readily to urban life
in Korvosa and now infest numerous nooks and crannies in the city's lower-class quarters,
particularly along the waterfronts.

A variant of the drain spider, known as the shingle spider, exists in Korvosa as well. Shingle
spiders dwell in the upper reaches of the city, particularly enjoying the chimneys and gutters of
abandoned buildings as a place to make their nests. Shingle spiders retain the drain spider's water
skating ability, although they rarely have the opportunity to use it.
