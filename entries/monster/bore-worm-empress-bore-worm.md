### Special Abilities

**Corrosive Wake (Ex)** This ability functions as the
[bore worm monarch's](ref/monster/bore-worm-bore-worm-monarch) ability of the same name, except it
leaves a 10-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.

**Painful Bite (Ex)** Living creatures damaged by a bore worm are racked with panic-inducing pain,
gaining the [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) condition (Fortitude DC 18 negates) for 1d6 rounds.
The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Viviparous Birth (Ex)** In an effort to preserve the next generation, an empress bore worm
violently expunges any young it may be carrying when it dies; these young function as 1d6
[bore worm swarms](ref/monster/bore-worm-bore-worm-swarm). In addition, the empress bore worm
explodes in a burst of caustic blood and ichor, dealing 12d6 points of acid damage to all creatures
in a 30-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) (Reflex DC 22 half). The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Vulnerable to Water (Ex)** Water is lethal to bore worms, which shrivel when exposed to either
fresh or salt water. Bore worms take half again as much damage (+50%) from spells and abilities with
the water descriptor, and take a -2 penalty on saving throws against spells and abilities with the
water descriptor that do not deal hit point damage.

### Description

A single, finger-length bore worm has never been much of a threat to any one creature, but isolated
bore worms are found only in laboratories. Bore worm swarms, as they are more commonly encountered,
consume organic material with a voracious appetite, tunneling through the earth like water as they
reach progressively larger sizes.
