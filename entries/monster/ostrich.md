### Special Abilities

**Crouch (Ex)** An ostrich can hide in plain sight as a bush or shrub by crouching down and
concealing its head and legs. It makes a Stealth check as normal, but anyone who observes it from
within 30 feet automatically sees through the ruse.

### Description

Adult female ostriches average 6 feet tall and weigh about 220 pounds, with males averaging 7 to 9
feet and 250 pounds. Although they are likely to flee from predators when they can, ostriches are
more than willing to stand and fight when trapped or defending their chicks; they're known to kick
even lions to death in defense of their young.

A trained ostrich has a market price of 80 gp, whereas an egg is worth 25 gp. Because it's bipedal,
an ostrich requires an exotic saddle.
