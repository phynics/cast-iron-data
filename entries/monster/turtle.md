### Special Abilities

**Shell Retreat (Ex)** A turtle can retreat within its shell as a swift action, gaining a +2
enhancement bonus to its existing natural armor. While in its shell, a turtle cannot take any action
except to end the retreat. The turtle can end its retreat with a free action on its turn.

### Description

Turtles are slow-moving reptiles with hard shells that they can retreat into when threatened. Many
turtles have flippers and are better suited for swimming than walking. These statistics can also be
used for tortoises, which have feet instead of flippers and live in arid regions.
