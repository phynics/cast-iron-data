### Tactics

**During Combat** A serpentfolk illusionist seeks to deceive opponents with spells until it can
debilitate them with blindness/deafness, color spray, or a venomous bite enhanced with pernicious
poison. Otherwise, it attacks with its wand or uses suggestion and dominate person to turn enemies
into allies.

### Description

intensive arcane study. On the surface world, it is often the leader of a group of serpentfolk or a
