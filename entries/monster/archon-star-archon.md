### Special Abilities

**Explosive Rebirth (Su)** When killed, a star archon explodes in a blinding flash of energy that
deals 50 points of damage (half fire, half holy damage) to anything within 100 feet (Reflex DC 29
half). The save DC is Constitution-based. The slain archon reincarnates 1d4 rounds later as an
[advanced](ref/template/advanced) [shield archon](ref/monster/archon-shield-archon).

**Spells (Sp)** Star archons cast divine spells as 19th-level [clerics](ref/class/cleric). They do
not gain access to domains or other cleric abilities.

### Description

Star archons are the tacticians and strategists of [Heaven](ref/rule/heaven). Gifted with insight
and powerful magic, they spend much of their time steering long-term plans for Heaven's armies and
good folk in the world.
