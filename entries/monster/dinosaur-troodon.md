### Special Abilities

**Easily Trained (Ex)** A troodon is unusually quick to pick up tricks and animal training. All
Handle Animal checks made to train a troodon gain a +2 bonus. A troodon can learn an additional 2
[tricks](ref/ability/animal-tricks) beyond what an animal of its intelligence can normally be
taught.

### Description

The swift, curious troodons are unusually quick to learn, and some can even mimic human speech in
the manner of a parrot.

A troodon is 3 feet tall and just over 6 feet long from nose to tail. It weighs 50 pounds.
