### Special Abilities

**Clockwork Repair (Ex)** The toy golem has [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2 when in an area
with a high concentration of clockwork parts, such as Mutalib's workshop or the area created by a
similar golem's discorporate ability.

**Disease (Ex)** [Tetanus](ref/disease/tetanus), but DC is 13.

### Description

A toy golem is 6 feet tall and weighs about 450 pounds; each component toy is only 1 foot tall and
weighs 1-1/2 pounds. The components that make up the golem are capable of winding one another up if
they can reach each other.

**Family:** [Clockwork](ref/family/clockwork)

### Construction

A toy golem requires approximately 300 tiny clockwork components worth a total of 1,000 gp.
