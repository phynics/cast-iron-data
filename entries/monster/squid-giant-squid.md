### Description

The giant squid is a legendary beast capable of feeding on humans with ease. Hunger has been known
to drive these normally deep-dwelling creatures up to the ocean surface where anything they
encounter is potential prey. A giant squid is 45 feet long and weighs 1,500 pounds.
