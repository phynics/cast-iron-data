### Description

A rat swarm typically consists of a biting, roiling mass of hundreds of disease-ridden rats driven
to uncharacteristic heights of aggression by fantastic and overwhelming hunger. In such numbers,
they become voracious hunters, capable of killing a full-grown human with hundreds of bites. Rat
swarms are often found in the sewers of large human settlements.

Rat swarms surround and attack any warm-blooded prey in their path.
