### Special Abilities

**Glide (Ex)** A flying squirrel cannot use its fly speed to hover. When flying, a flying squirrel
must end its movement at least 5 feet lower in elevation than where it started.

### Description

Although not capable of actual flight, a flying squirrel possesses a furry membrane stretching
between its wrists and ankles, allowing it to glide for long distances.
