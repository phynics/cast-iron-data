### Special Abilities

**Sprint (Ex)** Once per hour, a cheetah can move at 10 times its normal speed (500 feet) when it
makes a charge.

### Description

The cheetah is a swift and deadly predator capable of moving with incredible speed, allowing it to
run down unsuspecting foes hundreds of feet away. The hunting cat avoids areas of dense and tangled
undergrowth, but has great skill at lying in wait in tall grass.

An adult cheetah is 4-1/2 feet long and weighs 140 pounds.
