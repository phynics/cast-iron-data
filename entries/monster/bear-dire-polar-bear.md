### Description

Dire polar bears, kin to [cave bears](ref/monster/bear-dire-bear-cave-bear), are larger and more
aggressive than cave bears and normal [polar bears](ref/monster/bear-polar-bear) alike. These
short-tempered animals tend to avoid settled regions, but sometimes venture dangerously close to
them while hunting, potentially resulting in bloody massacres.
