### Special Abilities

**Ink Cloud (Ex)** While within water, an octopus can emit a 5-foot-radius sphere of ink once per
minute as a swift action. This ink provides total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) and persists
for 1 minute.

**Jet (Ex)** The octopus can jet 60 feet in a straight line as a full-round action. This does not
provoke attacks of opportunity.

**Poison (Ex)**

**Type:** Poison (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 10

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

### Description

Blue-ringed octopuses are aquatic animals capable of a surprising amount of cunning. They cannot
survive out of water.
