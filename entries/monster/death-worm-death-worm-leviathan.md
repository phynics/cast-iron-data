### Description

Sometimes a pregnant death worm lays an especially promising egg among her brood, about twice as
large as the others and with a nigh-impenetrable, brown-speckled shell. This egg requires even more
warmth than the others in its clutch, and upon emerging the worm would rather devour its brethren
than the meal left by its parent. Such rare specimens grow up to become death worm leviathans, and
those few who have heard of the fell beasts know to fear them as well as to hold them in awe. Death
worm leviathans possess powers nearly identical to those of their smaller kin, albeit at a much
deadlier scale. In addition, their massive girth makes it even easier for them to consume prey both
large and small, and a single leviathan might consume an entire desert village before its terrible
hunger is sated.
