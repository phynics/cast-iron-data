### Special Abilities

**Tremorsense (Ex)** A water strider's [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) functions only when the
creature is in contact with the water's surface.

**Water Skating (Ex)** A water strider can move on the surface of water as if it were on land. A
water strider swimming at the water's surface can pull itself onto the water with a successful Swim
check.

### Description

A giant water strider is a long-legged insect with the ability to walk on water. It feeds on fish
and birds. Water striders use their weak flight only to escape predators or for seasonal migrations
to new breeding areas. They are sometimes kept as mounts by coast-dwelling humanoids. A giant water
strider lives 1-2 years.

Carrying more than a light load (200 pounds) prevents a giant water strider from using its water
skating ability.
