### Description

Much like their tiny cousins, giant flies feed upon carrion. Wholly monstrous, these disgusting
creatures have been known to sometimes attack still-living foes, particularly when they are hungry
or living creatures disturb their meals. Some species of giant fly bear their larva live, ejecting
piles of undulating giant maggots from their engorged abdomens rather than laying eggs in decaying
corpses.
