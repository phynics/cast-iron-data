### Special Abilities

**Blood Rain (Su):** Black-blooded creatures constantly leak and spray bursts of freezing
[black blood](ref/magic-artifact/black-blood-of-orv). Any creature within 15 feet of the moldering
emperor takes 9 points of cold damage. This damage is Constitution-based.

**Mind Thrust (Su)** As a standard action up to three times a day, the moldering emperor can deliver
a massive blast of mental energy at any one target within 60 feet, inflicting 15d10 points of
damage. A successful DC 26 Will save negates the effect. This effect can only harm creatures with
Intelligence scores. This is a mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Psychic Crush (Su)** As a standard action up to three times a day, the moldering emperor can
attempt to crush the mind of a single creature within 60 feet. The target must make a DC 26 Will
save or collapse, becoming unconscious and dying at -1 hit points. If the target succeeds on the
save, it takes 6d6 points of damage and is [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round. This is a
mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Tainted Life (Ex):** The black blood is antithetical to all life and consumes all but the
heartiest hosts. The moldering emperor must make a DC 15 Fortitude save every day or take 1d4 points
of [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).

**Trace Teleport (Ex)** The moldering emperor telepathically and reflexively learns the mental
coordinates of the destination, of all creatures that teleport within 60 feet of it, gaining an
awareness of the location equivalent to "seen casually." This knowledge fades and is lost after 1
minute. This power does not grant any environmental information about the conditions of the
destination.

**Skills** The Moldering Emperor has a +8 racial bonus on any Swim check to perform some special
action or avoid a hazard. It can always choose to take 10 on a Swim check, even if distracted or
endangered.

### Description

Lurking amid the depths of the Caltherium at the heart of the Land of Black Blood writhes one of the
vault's deadliest hunters, the fearsome Moldering Emperor. Burdened with mutations, out-of-control
tendrils, and wing-like fins, this hunter of the depths claims every black-blood-soaked region of
the vast cavern as its personal domain, and it doesn't take kindly to trespassers.

> stats differ slightly from the original.
