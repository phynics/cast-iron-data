Much like their smaller kin, giant cockroaches are extremely adaptive and exist in any place they
can find a ready source of food. Giant cockroaches are not normally very violent, but readily attack
if threatened or if food becomes scarce.

A number of species of giant cockroach exist, as summarized on the table above. Most of these
variants have additional abilities, such as poison stings.
