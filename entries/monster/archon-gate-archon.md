### Special Abilities

**Graven Wings (Ex, Sp)** A gate archon can inscribe a
_[glyph of warding](ref/spell/glyph-of-warding)_ onto its wings; this glyph can be triggered only
when the archon is using its _[statue](ref/spell/statue)_ form.

**Stoneblade (Ex, Su)** A gate archon can create a +3 conductive greatsword as a full-round action.
A stoneblade crumbles to dust if the archon dies or crafts a new sword.

### Description
