### Description

The behemoth hippopotamus is a true monster. Standing taller than an elephant, this animal has few
enemies in the natural world - even dinosaurs and dire crocodiles avoid fights with them. Making
them even more dangerous is the fact that they are practically carnivores - while not above eating
plant matter, they devour carrion or foolish creatures that come too close.
