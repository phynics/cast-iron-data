### Special Abilities

**Clinging Flames (Ex)** A creature that takes damage from a mythic hell hound's breath weapon also
catches on fire (using the save DC for its burn ability).

### Description

A mythic hell hound is a prince among the wolves of Hell, feral but still subservient to the
archdevils. Allowed to run wild, they are the original creatures from which the "tamer" common hell
hounds were made.
