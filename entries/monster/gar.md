### Description

Gars are predatory fish that live in deep lakes and rivers. Sleek and swift, they regard anything
their own size or smaller as food. A typical gar is 7 feet long and weighs 200 pounds. One species
known as the alligator gar can grow to lengths of 10 feet. An alligator gar is a gar with the
advanced creature template.
