### Description

Rot grubs are foul, nauseating parasites that feed on flesh and use corpses as nests in which to
grow. While a rot grub can derive nourishment from dead flesh, its true hunger is for the flesh of
the living. Thankfully, rot grub swarms occur only rarely, as they require the infested carcass of a
Huge or larger creature and many weeks to build up the numbers necessary to constitute a swarm.

In some cases, a rot grub continues to feed and grow, eventually reaching enormous size as a giant
rot grub.
