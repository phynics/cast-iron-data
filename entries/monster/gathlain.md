### Description

According to some fey ballads, [gathlains](ref/race/gathlain) were one of the first peoples awakened
in the primal world of fey. They were created from the seeds of an enormous magical tree, with the
tree's mistletoe grown into their flesh forming their strange wings.

Mischievous and capricious, these creatures have discordant temperaments. They act purely to
entertain themselves and sate their immense curiosity about the world around them. That very
curiosity has caused many to migrate to the Material Plane and adventure there. These gathlains seek
out and attempt to mingle with gnomes. However, gnomes often find gathlains too undisciplined,
random, and foolish for their tastes.
