### Description

Infamous among travelers for its deadly poisonous bite, the emperor cobra is a foul-tempered snake
typically encountered in bogs. The snake's scales are generally a dark green, with a pale green or
even ivory underbelly. Its eyes are bright red and lack the distinctive serpentine slit most snake
eyes possess. Emperor cobras are often trained to serve as guardians in temples. They are 16 feet
long and weigh 200 pounds.
