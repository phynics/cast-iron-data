### Description

The giant sea snake is one of the biggest reasons many sailors of the Shackles have a downright
visceral and phobic response to the thought of falling overboard. These patient predators are known
to follow sailing ships, swimming just below the surface of the water while they wait for the
opportunity to strike at any unfortunate crew members who might fall over the side. Particularly
hungry giant sea snakes have been known to climb the side of a vessel and snatch unwary sailors
right from the deck.

Giant sea snakes typically reach up to 30 feet in length and weigh 500 pounds.
