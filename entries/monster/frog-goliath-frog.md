### Special Abilities

**Tongue (Ex)** A goliath frog's tongue is a primary attack with three times the reach of its bite.
Its tongue deals no damage but can be used to grab. The frog does not gain the grappled condition
when grappling with its tongue.

### Description

These hulking frogs haunt warm marshlands and river shallows where thick undergrowth can conceal
them. They are dangerous, aggressive predators that gorge themselves on smaller creatures or team up
to bring down larger prey. They often climb to the low branches of ancient, mossy trees, picking off
prey from the wetland's floor before their existence is even suspected.
