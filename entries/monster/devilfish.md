### Special Abilities

**Poison (Ex)**

**Type:** Poison (Savage bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d2

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Savage Bite (Ex)** A devilfish can attack with its savage bite whenever it makes a successful
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) check. This attack is in place of any other action made with a
successful grapple check. The bite threatens a critical hit on a roll of 18-20, and injects the
target with poison as well.

**Unholy Blood (Su)** A devilfish's blood is infused with fiendish magic. Once per day, as a swift
action, a devilfish can emit a night-black cloud of this foul liquid, filling a 20-foot-radius cloud
if underwater, or a 20-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) on land. In water, the blood provides
total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) for everything but a devilfish (which can see through the
blood with ease); on land the slippery blood coats the ground, making the area
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). The blood persists for 1 minute before fading.
Anyone who enters a cloud of the blood in the water or who is within the area of a burst of blood on
land must make a DC 15 Fortitude save or be [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1d4 rounds; this
save need be made only once per cloud. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Water Dependency (Ex)** A devilfish can survive out of the water for 1 hour, after which it
becomes [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued). After 2 hours, the devilfish becomes
[exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) and begins to [suffocate](ref/rule/suffocation).

### Description

Although the devilfish superficially resembles a seven-armed octopus, it is an altogether different
creature. Possessing a rudimentary intellect, a devilfish can understand and even speak a few words
and phrases in various languages, although when it speaks, it has a tendency to mix languages
together, making it somewhat difficult to understand for anyone who doesn't speak all the languages
known by the devilfish.

More than those of a mere animal, the devilfish's intelligence and several of its abilities are
gifts from a fiendish legacy - most sages believe that the original devilfish were once outsiders
from the Abyss, and that over the course of thousands of years they became true natives of the
Material Plane's oceans. Rumors of far more intelligent devilfish dwelling in the deepest ocean
trenches persist, although if these rumors are true, these deep-dwelling devilfish do not often come
to the surface.

A devilfish is 10 feet long and weighs 500 pounds.
