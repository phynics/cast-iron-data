### Description

Infused with the power of waves and storms, sea dragons - or jiaolungs, as they are known in many
lands - are draconic protectors of oceans and their creatures. Possessing tempestuous natures, sea
dragons wander widely, sometimes claiming thousands of miles of ocean and coastlines as their
protectorates.
