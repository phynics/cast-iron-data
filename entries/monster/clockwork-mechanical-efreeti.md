### Description

Mechanical genies are best used as stationary guards rather than scouts, and are often placed in
pairs so they can wind each other up. They stand 2 feet tall and weigh around 8 pounds.

The most common mechanical genie is that presented above. A mechanical efreeti has a face sculpted
into a permanent scowl and an inner boiler to heat its large fists.

**Family:** [Clockwork](ref/family/clockwork)

### Construction

A mechanical efreeti requires intricate gears and other components worth 500 gp.

**CL** 7th; **Price** 3,500 gp\
**Requirements** [Craft Construct](ref/feat/craft-construct),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [fireball](ref/spell/fireball),
[lesser geas](ref/spell/geas-lesser), creator must be at least caster level 7th; **Skill** Craft
(clockwork) DC 15; **Cost** 2,000 gp

### Variant Mechanical Genies

Other mechanical genies exist, each with variations based on the associated genie.

**Mechanical Djinni:** This construct flies by means of cunning propellers mounted under its torso
in place of legs. A mechanical djinni loses the burn ability but gains a fly speed of 20 feet with
clumsy maneuverability. When [charging](ref/rule/charge) while flying, a mechanical djinni doesn't
provoke attacks of opportunity when entering its opponent's square, and it gains a +2 bonus on
damage rolls. Construction of a mechanical djinni requires [fly](ref/spell/fly) in place of
_[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)._

**Mechanical Janni:** A mechanical janni loses the burn ability, but its slam attack deals slashing
damage with a critical threat range of 18-20 and an additional 1d4 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed)
damage. Construction of a mechanical janni requires [keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge) in place of
_[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)._

**Mechanical Marid:** A mechanical marid is quick but tends to be erratic. It loses the burn
ability, but gains a swim speed of 30 feet and Swim skill of +6. Its base speed increases to 40
feet, and it gains a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls against creatures in or touching water. Its
construction requires [haste](ref/spell/haste) in place of _[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)._

**Mechanical Shaitan:** A mechanical shaitan loses the burn ability, but gains an additional slam
attack and a +4 bonus to Strength. Creation of a mechanical shaitan requires
[stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape) in place of _[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)._
