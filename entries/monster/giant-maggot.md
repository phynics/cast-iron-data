### Special Abilities

**Regurgitate (Ex)** Once per day, a giant maggot can empty its putrid stomach upon one creature
within 5 feet. The target must make a DC 13 Fortitude save or be sickened for 1 minute (or until the
target spends a full-round action with at least a gallon of water to wash off the filth). The save
DC is Constitution-based.

### Description

Voracious scavengers, giant maggots feed constantly, gorging themselves on the dead in preparation
for their transformation into giant flies. Their pallid, corpulent bodies are the size of human
children, and their rasping teeth are capable of eating even the bones of a corpse. Giant maggots
have no legs, and move with a disturbing undulation as they crawl over their meals. Giant maggots
feed for 2 weeks (often moving from one corpse to another) before entering a pupal stage, after
which they emerge as giant flies.
