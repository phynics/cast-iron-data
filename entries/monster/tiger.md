### Description

Tigers stand more than 3 feet tall at the shoulder and are about 9 feet long. They weigh from 400 to
600 pounds.

Tigers are usually the top animal predators in their territories, and have been known to kill bears,
crocodiles, giant snakes, wolves, and even other great cats. Even humanoids are far from safe,
especially in cases where a tiger has developed a taste for humanoid flesh. Tigers prefer terrain
with plenty of cover and proximity to water as their hunting grounds.

While the tiger itself is a fearsome predator, its strength and ferocity pales in comparison to that
of the larger dire tiger. Known to many scholars as the smilodon and to tribal societies as the
saber-toothed tiger, the dire tiger is invariably one of the region's top predators. Its defining
feature is a pair of huge incisors that hang down like fearsome knives from the upper jaw,
protruding menacingly even when the creature's mouth is shut.

These immense hunting cats grow to be over 12 feet long and can weigh up to 6,000 pounds.
