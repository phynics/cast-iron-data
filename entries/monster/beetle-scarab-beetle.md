### Special Abilities

**Gnaw (Ex)** A scarab beetle deals 1 point of [Strength damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) on a
successful bite. This damage is doubled against creatures with no armor or natural armor bonus to
AC, but creatures in heavy armor or with a natural armor bonus of +10 or greater are immune, as are
creatures immune to critical hits or without flesh.

### Description

In Osirion, these massive beetles are a threat to ancient tombs as they chew their way through the
interred. Some say scarab beetles' connection with the dead gives them powers greater than those of
normal beetles, but any evidence of this has yet to been proven true. Merchants claim powdered
scarab is an essential element in magic that protects against death effects.
