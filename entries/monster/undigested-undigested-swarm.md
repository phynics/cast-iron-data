### Special Abilities

**Digest (Ex)** A target that takes swarm damage from an undigested swarm is covered in acidic slime
and must succeed at a DC 16 Reflex save or take 2d6 points of acid damage for 1d4 rounds at the
start of its turn. An affected creature can make a new save as a full-round action. Washing off the
acid grants a +4 bonus on this save. The save DC is Constitution-based.

### Description

Whereas individual undigested are more of a nuisance than a threat to adventurers, a swarm of such
creatures can be dangerous to even experienced adventurers. An undigested swarm comprises hundreds
of individual undigested. Driven by insatiable hunger, they fall upon and consume any living
creatures crossing their path, surrounding the unfortunate victims and drenching them in acidic
slime, digesting their still-living prey even before the screaming ends.
