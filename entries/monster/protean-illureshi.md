### Special Abilities

**Prehensile Tail (Ex)** An illureshi can't wield weapons with its tail, but can use its tail to
grab unattended items within 10 feet or stowed objects carried on its person as a swift action. It
can hold such objects with its tail. The only items it can manipulate with its tail are wands, which
it can activate as a move action.

**Spellwarp Aura (Su)** Once per round, if a creature casts a spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) within the illureshi's aura, the protean can
attempt an opposed caster level check to redirect the effect to another legal target within range.

**Spellcasting (Sp)** An illureshi casts spells as a 6th-level sorcerer. It does not gain access to
other sorcerer abilities.

### Description

Illureshis embody chaos through sorcery, traveling the planes as chatty and whimsical masters of
arcane magic.
