### Special Abilities

**Alternate Form (Ex)** As a full-round action, an iron colossus can transform its legs into a
massive, destructive track. Its speed becomes 80 ft., it can't be tripped, and it gains a trample
attack that deals 4d8+30 damage. It loses its stomp attack. It can resume its base form as a
full-round action.

**Deadly Fumes (Ex)** An iron colossus emits a 30-foot-radius cloud of poisonous gas, exposing
creatures that start their turn in this area.

- [Affliction: Deadly Fumes](ref/affliction/colossus-iron-colossus-mythic/deadly-fumes)

**Fire Absorption (Ex)** Not only is a flesh colossus immune to fire, but when targeted or hit by a
spell or attack that would deal fire damage, it heals half the fire damage it would be dealt.

**Selective Antimagic Aura (Su)** Spells with the fire descriptor and fire effects are unaffected by
this field.

**Siege Tower (Ex)** An iron colossus's ballistae don't provoke attacks of opportunity, and they
reload themselves at the start of the colossus's turn.
