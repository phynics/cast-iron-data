### Description

Strapped to her giant vulture companion, the goblin vulture pilot is a peerless scout and saboteur.
Enemies find goblin vulture pilots tough to pin down as they lob explosive bombs from the air.
Vulture pilots are braver, more disciplined, and more patient than most goblins, as they have to
sneak into giant vulture nests to steal eggs, resist the temptation to eat those eggs, and raise the
hatchlings to be loyal mounts. Many pilots die in flying accidents; the lucky ones often discover
how to craft extracts of bouncy body, levitate, or similar effects to help them survive the
inevitable crashes.

Goblin vulture pilots take great pride in their abilities, and often wear their mounts' feathers as
headdresses or paint themselves with guano as marks of status.
