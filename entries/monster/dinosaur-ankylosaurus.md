### Special Abilities

**Stun (Ex)** The ankylosaurus's tail can deliver a powerful, stunning blow. A creature struck by
this attack must make a DC 23 save or be [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round. If the strike is a
critical hit and the target fails its save, it is instead [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1d4
rounds. The save DC is Strength-based.

### Description

The ankylosaurus is a powerful, squat dinosaur more than capable of defending itself against
enemies. Its back is heavily armored with thick bony plates and spikes. A solid blow from an
ankylosaurus's tail can leave most creatures stunned long enough for the armored dinosaur to make
good an escape, although once confronted, most ankylosauruses are too ill-tempered and stubborn to
flee. Instead, they stand their ground and use their tails to great effect in battle. An
ankylosaurus is 30 feet long and weighs 6,000 pounds.
