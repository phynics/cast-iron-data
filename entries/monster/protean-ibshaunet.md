### Special Abilities

**Corrosive Aura (Su)** A creature that enters an ibshaunet's aura must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude
save or take 3d6 points of acid damage. Once a creature is affected by this aura, it is not affected
again unless it leaves and reenters the aura. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Warpwave Breath (Su)** An ibshaunet can exhale a 50-foot cone of entropic energy every 1d4 rounds,
dealing 10d6 points of acid damage to creatures in the area of effect (Reflex DC 22 half). A
creature damaged by the breath must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save or be subject to a
[warpwave](ref/subtype/protean). The save DC is Constitution-based.

### Description

Ibshaunet proteans are rarely seen engines of destruction.
