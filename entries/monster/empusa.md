### Special Abilities

**Lash (Su)** As a move action, an empusa can manifest a whip-like weapon that appears to be a
coiling serpent-once manifested, the lash exists as long as the empusa carries it. A lash vanishes
if it is disarmed or the empusa drops it or is slain. An empusa's lash has a reach of 10 feet and
deals 1d6 points of slashing damage on a hit. In addition, a creature damaged by the lash is
affected by its toxic venom.

- [Affliction: Lash Venom](ref/affliction/empusa/lash-venom)

**Phantasmal Vengeance (Sp)** This [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) functions as
phantasmal killer, save that the image it creates is of a creature or person who the viewer has
wronged in their past (or of the empusa herself if there are no other appropriate options). If the
target of a phantasmal vengeance fails his Fortitude save, the empusa can opt to have the victim
become paralyzed for 1 hour rather than die from fear.

### Description

The empusas are Calistria's handmaidens and elite agents. Effortlessly passing for elves, empusas
move secretly through society, manipulating mortals and influencing events to achieve their missions
of vengeance.

An empusa stands 6 feet tall and weighs 120 pounds.
