### Description

Winter wolves are larger, smarter, more ferocious variants of worgs with white fur and a deadly
breath weapon.

A typical winter wolf is 8 feet long, stands 4-1/2 feet tall at the shoulder, and weighs about 450
pounds. Winter wolves will often ally themselves with frost giants, hill giants, and other large
humanoids, serving entire tribes of giants as both scouts and guards.
