### Description

The hookfang is primarily an ambush predator; it swims quickly, but only in brief bursts. When
hunting, it covers most of its body under the sands of the ocean floor and remains perfectly still
until potential prey swims nearby. When it lunges, its bite is so quick and powerful that its
hook-teeth can cut targeted creatures in half. A smaller (but still sizable) variant of the hookfang
is the vampire worm, which drains blood from its victims.
