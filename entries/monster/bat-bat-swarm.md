### Special Abilities

**Wounding (Ex)** Any living creature damaged by a bat swarm continues to [bleed](ref/rule/bleed),
losing 1 hit point per round thereafter. Multiple wounds do not result in cumulative bleeding loss.
The bleeding can be stopped by a DC 10 Heal check or the application of a _cure_ spell or some other
healing magic.

### Description

Bat swarms dwell in large caves, ruins, or even city sewers - anywhere they can find darkness to
hide in during the day and a supply of food to feast upon at night. They are only encountered
outside in a group at dawn or dusk, or when they have been startled and forced to flee their lairs.
