### Description

The leaders of small orc warbands aren't much smarter or more cunning than the rank-and-file orc
warriors they lead - but just a small advantage in strength is enough to let them take the best
shares of treasure and slaves and lord their dominance over their fellow orcs. Orc commanders
enforce discipline through intimidation and are not afraid to lead underlings to their deaths.
