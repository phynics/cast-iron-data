### Description

In their philosophy on life's futility, urdefhans see being raised as an undead as a suitable
punishment for enemies since it wrenches their souls from the sweet oblivion that awaits them beyond
and forces their continued suffering on the Material Plane. Necromancers flourish among urdefhans,
serving as jailers, mercenaries, and labor contractors.
