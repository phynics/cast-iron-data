### Special Abilities

**Armored Stomach (Ex)** A giant snapping turtle's body is difficult to cut through-its stomach
gains a +4 bonus to its AC and has double the normal hit points when determining the success of a
creature attempting to cut its way free.

### Description

Giant snapping turtles typically grow to diameters of about 35 feet and weigh 20,000 pounds.
