### Special Abilities

**Ink Cloud (Ex)** A squid can emit a 5-foot-radius cloud of ink once per minute as a free action
while underwater. This cloud provides total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). The ink persists
for 1 minute.

**Jet (Ex)** A squid can jet in a straight line as a full-round action. It does not provoke attacks
of opportunity while jetting.

### Description

Squids are aggressive predators that fear little and are more than willing to attack prey larger
than themselves.
