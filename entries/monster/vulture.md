### Description

Though related to raptors and other birds of prey, vultures mainly feed on carrion they spot or
scent from on high. Vultures have weak claws compared to predatory birds, but their sharp and
wickedly hooked beaks tear living flesh as readily as the sharpest blade.

Despite dining on carrion, vultures prefer their corpses newly dead rather than rotten. Some even
hunt smaller prey when food is scarce.
