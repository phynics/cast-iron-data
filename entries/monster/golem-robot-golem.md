### Special Abilities

**Immunity to Magic (Ex)** A robot golem is immune to spells or
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) that allow
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). In addition, certain spells and effects function
differently against it, as noted below.

- A magical attack that deals electricity damage deals half damage to the golem. Additionally, for
  1d4+1 rounds the golem gains the benefits of [haste](ref/spell/haste) and goes berserk. The
  uncontrolled golem goes on a rampage, attacking the nearest living creature or smashing an object
  smaller than itself if no creature is within reach; it then moves on to spread more destruction.
  The golem comes back under the control of its creator when the duration ends.
- A [transmute metal to wood](ref/spell/transmute-metal-to-wood) spell reduces the robot golem's
  [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) by 5 and its natural armor bonus by 9 for 1d4
  rounds.
- A robot golem is affected normally by rust attacks, such as those of a
  [rust monster](ref/monster/rust-monster) or a [rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp) spell.

**Rend Construct (Ex):** This functions as the rend special ability, except the robot golem can rend
only other constructs.

**Shockwave (Ex)** A robot golem can release a pulse of electrical energy held within its
non-functioning batteries as a standard action once every 1d4 rounds. This pulse is a 30-foot-radius
spread that deals 8d6 points of electricity damage to all creatures in its area of effect (Reflex DC
19 half). The save DC is Constitution-based and includes a +2 racial bonus.

### Description

When robots are damaged beyond the abilities of Numerian technologists to repair, salvages sometimes
animate their frames via magic rather than science. What results is more of a walking mechanical
corpse that lacks the intelligence and vulnerabilities of a true robot. The arcanists of the Technic
League often employ these magically animated constructs to combat rogue robots or those under the
control of their enemies.

**Family:** [Golem](ref/family/golem)

### Construction

A robot golem's body used to be a Large robot, but is now non-functional. Any major damage on the
intended body must be repaired prior to animation.
