### Special Abilities

**Barbed Hairs (Ex)** A tarantula can throw barbed hairs from its back at a creature as a ranged
touch attack (range increment 20 feet). A creature struck by these hairs must make a DC 22 Fort save
or be nauseated for 1d6 rounds. A creature that attacks a giant tarantula with a non-reach melee
weapon must make a DC 22 Reflex save to avoid being struck by these hairs. The save DC is Con-based.
