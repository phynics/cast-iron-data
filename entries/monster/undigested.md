### Description

Undigested are the animate slurry of the indigestible parts of a humanoid creature. They come into
being when a giant beast that swallowed its prey alive is slain by unspeakable necromantic arts. A
primal shard of the beast's sentience is ripped from it during the agonizing moments of its death,
animating the gelatinous humanoid remains within its stomach into an ooze-like undead creature which
hungers to inflict its digestive fate upon others. If the beast was digesting multiple creatures,
this phenomenon results in undigested swarms instead. Undigested-implacable ambush predators-slop,
ooze, and undulate through the dark places of the world in search of sustenance.
