### Description

Common in a variety of environments and climates, dire weasels are larger relatives of normal
weasels. They're relentless predators known for their fearless nature and killing frenzies, and are
dangerous to farmsteads and settlements - they not only prey on cattle and horses, but also target
humanoids. Those that do often become obsessed with the taste of that flesh and hunt such prey
exclusively.

Sometimes, dire weasels are trained to be mounts by evil humanoids - usually by bugbears or
hobgoblins.

Measuring 12 feet long, a dire weasel stands 4 feet tall at the shoulder and weighs around 900
pounds.
