### Description

Temperamental and prone to violent outbursts, magma dragons are regarded by most other dragons as
dangerously insane - an assumption that, more often than not, proves correct. One can rarely predict
a magma dragon's state of mind until it either attacks or attempts to engage in conversation. For
their part, magma dragons can justify all of their actions - they just rarely feel the need to do
so.
