### Description

Beheaded are floating skulls or severed heads whose bodies have long since abandoned them, either in
the moment of death or long after. Reanimated via dark magic, these horrors are usually created as
mindless sentinels for dungeons or lairs. Beheaded silently hover at about eye-level, often making
them the last thing a casual wanderer or careless villager in an infested area ever sees.

See the [beheaded](ref/monster/beheaded) entry for variants and methods of creation.
