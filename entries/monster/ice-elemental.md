### Special Abilities

**Ice Glide (Su)** A burrowing ice elemental can pass through nonmagical ice and snow as easily as a
fish swims through water. Its burrowing leaves behind no tunnel or hole, nor does it create any
ripple or other sign of its presence. A [control water](ref/spell/control-water) spell cast on an
area containing a burrowing ice elemental flings the elemental back 30 feet,
[stunning](ref/misc/stunned) the creature for 1 round unless it succeeds on a DC 15 Fortitude save.

**Icewalking (Ex)** This ability works like the [spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb) spell, but
the surfaces the elemental climbs must be icy. The elemental can move across icy surfaces without
penalty and does not need to make [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to run or charge on ice.

**Numbing Cold (Su)** When an ice elemental deals cold damage to a creature, that creature must
succeed on a Fortitude save or be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. The save DC is listed
in the elemental's stat block and is Constitution-based.

**Snow Vision (Ex)** An ice elemental can see perfectly well in snowy conditions and does not take
any penalties on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks while in snow.

### Description

Ice elementals are creatures made of animated snow and ice. They form in especially cold parts of
the [Plane of Water](ref/rule/plane-of-water) and along its border with the
[Plane of Air](ref/rule/plane-of-air), where giant icebergs careen off of world-high waterfalls into
the open sky. Ice elementals vary in their exact appearance.
