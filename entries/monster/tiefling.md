### Description

Tieflings are humans with demonic, devilish, or other evil outsider blood in their ancestry. Often
persecuted for their strange appearance and unnatural mannerisms, most tieflings disguise their
nature or are forced to live on the fringes or underbelly of civilized society. Though they are not
born evil, it is an easy path for them to find, especially as most suffer at the hands of "normal"
folks while growing up. Tieflings look mostly human except for some physical traits that reveal
their strange heritage.
