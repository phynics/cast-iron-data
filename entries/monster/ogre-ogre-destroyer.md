### Description

An ogre destroyer is profane, disgusting, and filled with rage; nothing is safe from his murderous
and hateful urges. He indulges every whim, revels in the powers granted by his dark patrons, and
lives utterly free of all civilized restraint. He represents what people think of when they hear the
word "antipaladin."
