### Description

Titanoboas are deadly ambush predators, striking quickly from beneath the surface of the swamps and
jungle waters they call home. Titanoboas vary in their coloration to camouflage effectively in their
homes, typically patterned gray-brown and green to match the coloration of local foliage, water, and
ground.

A titanoboa is 45 feet long and weighs 2,500 pounds.

**Family:** Megafauna
