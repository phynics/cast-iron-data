### Description

The great white shark is one of the largest predatory fish in the ocean, and preys on seals, small
whales, seabirds, dolphins, and turtles. Its strength and speed even allow it to leap out of the
water to reach prey on land, rafts, or boats.

Though fierce and ruthless, great white sharks are often careful when they encounter a new type of
prey. They make many biting passes to evaluate the threat their quarry poses.

An adult great white shark is 20 feet long and weighs 5,000 pounds.
