### Description

A mythic black dragon eyes can pierce fog to find prey. Its very flesh and blood is infused with
deadly acid, and like a snake it can gulp down an entire meal without chewing. A mythic black dragon
enjoys regurgitating a swallowed opponent to let it rot for a few days before eating it as an actual
meal, or repeats this cycle on a living creature as cruel torture.
