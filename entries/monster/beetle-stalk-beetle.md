### Special Abilities

**Sawtooth (Ex)** The serrated mandibles of a stalk beetle allow it to ignore
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 5 or less when attacking objects. In addition, a stalk beetle deals
1 point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage upon a successful critical hit with its bite attack.

### Description

Stalk beetles are a plague in Rahadoum, scouring the land like
[giant locusts](ref/monster/locust-giant-locust), and eating any and all vegetation they encounter.
