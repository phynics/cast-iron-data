### Description

The lashunta are the primary race of Castrovel, called "the Green Planet" for its steaming jungles.
Though the women look like idealized elves or humans - save for the twitching antennae sprouting
from their foreheads - their men are squat, hairy, and broad-shouldered, with a fierce and
confrontational demeanor. Both genders excel at scholarship, and the race boasts a large number of
telepaths and telekinetics, in addition to conventional magic-users. This ability to create mental
or empathic bonds is particularly prevalent among female warriors, who form close connections with
their saurian mounts.

Female lashunta are typically 6 feet tall and weigh 140 pounds. The males of the race are usually 5
feet tall and weigh 200 pounds.
