### Special Abilities

**Protect Ally (Ex)** The troop provides soft cover to allies sharing its space. Such allies treat
squares occupied by the troop as difficult terrain, must succeed at a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC = 15 + spell level) to cast spells while so
protected, and don't take troop damage from being within the troop's space

### Description

While individually not much of a threat, these low-level fighters turn into a serious menace to
travelers on remote roads when they work together as a troop.
