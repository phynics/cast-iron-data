### Description

Skeletal mages are minor spellcasters (typically 5th-level or less) who have retained both their
intelligence and their spellcasting abilities. These variant
[skeletal champions](ref/template/skeletal-champion) gain Silent Spell as a bonus feat. Like
skeletal champions, skeletal mages cannot be created with animate dead - they only arise under rare
conditions or through ancient, esoteric rituals.
