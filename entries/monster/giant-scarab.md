### Special Abilities

**Disease (Ex)** [Filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever), but the save DC is 17.

**Gnaw (Ex)** Scarabs deal 1d4+6 additional points of damage and 1 point of Strength damage on a
successful bite. This is doubled against creatures with no armor or natural armor, but creatures in
heavy armor or with a natural armor bonus of +10 or greater are immune, as are fleshless creatures
and those immune to critical hits.

### Description

Scarabs are small, six-legged insects with sharp protrusions on their front legs that they use for
burrowing. They are primarily coprophagous, eating dung for sustenance. An individual scarab often
spends days rolling a single ball of dung across the desert until it can find the perfect place to
bury it and deposit eggs. Because they burrow underground to create life, scarab beetles are
associated with burial rites. Some say scarab beetles' connection with the dead gives them powers
greater than those of normal beetles, but as of yet there is no evidence this is true.
