### Tactics

**Before Combat** The priest casts _[shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith)_ on herself. She
prefers to let her minions do the fighting, and has them stand between her and any approaching foes.

**During Combat** The priest [channels negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) at groups of
foes and uses single-target spells against her most dangerous opponent (typically starting with
_[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person)_ so her allies can surround the hapless target).

**Base Statistics** Without _[shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith),_ the drow's statistics
are AC 22, touch 11, flat-footed 21.

### Description

Having manipulated and assassinated her way into the middle ranks of her church, the drow priest
uses her powers to eliminate rivals as she continues her climb. The drow religious orders are just
as cutthroat as every other aspect of [drow](ref/monster/drow) society. Their priests see the
ability to out-scheme one's foes as a sign of the unholy favor of their demonic patrons.
