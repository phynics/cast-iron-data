### Description

Unlike its smaller cousins, the giant jellyfish is an active predator that seeks out prey. Capable
of slithering through narrow cracks, a giant jellyfish is a horrifying beast to encounter lurking in
the hold of a flooded or sunken ship. Other species of these vermin exist, as summarized on the
following table - these variants often have different types of poison or other abilities like
translucency or constriction.
