### Special Abilities

**Musk (Ex)** Up to twice per day, a skunk can spray a stream of noxious musk at a single target
within 10 feet as a standard action. With a successful ranged touch attack, the creature struck by
this spray must make a DC 11 Fortitude save or be nauseated for 1d4 rounds and then sickened for 1d4
minutes by the horrific stench. A successful save reduces the effect to only 1d4 rounds of being
sickened. A creature cannot use the scent ability as long as it is affected by this musk. The save
DC is Constitution-based, and includes a +2 racial bonus.

### Description

These small mammals are well known to humanoids and forest creatures alike, with their distinctive
black-and-white-striped markings and infamous scent glands. Creatures like big cats or wolves that
normally would make a meal of such a small creature give skunks plenty of space, for they have
learned well that the combination of black and white on a skunk's back equates to a hideously
overpowering stench.
