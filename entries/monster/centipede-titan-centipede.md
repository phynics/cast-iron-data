### Description

The largest of their kind, titan centipedes are thankfully also the rarest. They attack other living
creatures fearlessly, and require constant sustenance to nourish their vast bulk. Humanoids are in
most danger from titan centipedes when mounted rather than afoot - a good-sized horse makes a much
more satisfying meal to the centipede than a few bites of human. Titan centipedes are solitary by
nature and because of the demands of their appetite, though when food is plentiful, multiple
centipedes sometimes lurk in the same area.
