### Description

There are certain evil fungal creatures (such as [fungus queens](ref/monster/fungus-queen), but also
rare fungal growths or extraplanar blights upon the wild) that can infest vermin with spores that
have been infused with sinister power and negative energy. These foul spores grow quickly in the
body of a dead vermin, eventually bursting from its head to form disturbing, antler-like growths. At
the same time, the spores animate the vermin as an intelligent undead creature.

These are then known as [spore zombies](ref/template/spore-zombie).
