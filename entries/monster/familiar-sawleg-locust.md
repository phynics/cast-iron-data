### Special Abilities

**Leap (Ex)** A sawleg locust can take 10 on Acrobatics checks to jump even if distracted or in
danger.

**Voracious (Ex)** A sawleg locust's bite attack deals double damage to creatures with the plant
subtype and to objects made of paper, wood, or other plant materials.

### Description

A sawleg locust is a larger variety of grasshopper that grows as large as 2 feet in length and feeds
on a variety of plants. Periods of drought sometimes cause these locusts to gather in large groups,
either to migrate to better feeding grounds or rarely to overpower and consume small animals.
