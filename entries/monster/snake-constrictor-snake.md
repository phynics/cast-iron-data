### Description

Be they jungle-dwelling pythons or swamp-dwelling boas like the anaconda, constrictor snakes are
among the most deadly predatory animals of the tropical wilds. Fortunately, these large snakes are
relatively passive, save for when they are preparing to shed their skins or are particularly hungry.
Nevertheless, the sinuous creatures are held in fear by many societies who often ascribe fiendish
qualities to them.

The constrictor snake presented here is a relatively small one. You can create stats for a larger
man-eater like an anaconda by applying the advanced and giant simple templates, or by advancing the
stats above to a 7 HD Large snake (CR 5), or even a 14 HD Huge snake (CR 10).
