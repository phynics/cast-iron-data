### Description

Moles are small rodent mammals that live primarily in burrows underground. While many varieties of
moles exist, most share common features that include subterranean habitats, poor eyesight, and the
ability to dig long distances with their powerful forelimbs. The typical mole is 6 inches long and
weighs less than a pound. These familiars are popular choices with [ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk).
