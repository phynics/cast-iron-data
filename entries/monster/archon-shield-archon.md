### Special Abilities

**Spear and Shield (Su)** At will as a free action, a shield archon can transform his hands into a
+1 [tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) and a +3 [shortspear](ref/eq-weapon/shortspear), or
either individually, or back to hands again. He cannot transform both hands into shields or both
into shortspears. A shield archon never takes the typical -2 penalty on attack rolls while wielding
a tower shield. A shield archon's weapons cannot be disarmed, but they can be sundered. If a shield
archon loses his spear or shield, he can manifest a new one as a full-round action. When a shield
archon is slain, these two items fade away - they cannot be looted or wielded by any other creature.

**Stability (Ex)** Shield archons receive a +4 racial bonus to CMD when resisting a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempt.

**Transpose Ally (Su)** Once per day as a standard action, a shield archon can teleport to the
location of a willing (or unconscious) ally and immediately teleport that ally to the archon's
previous position, in effect switching places with the ally. The archon must have line of effect to
the target.

### Description

Shield archons are the mighty rocks of celestial armies, withstanding waves of
[demons](ref/family/demon) and [devils](ref/family/devil) without complaint. Though more than
capable of tearing apart lesser demons and devils, their true strength lies in their ability to
shrug off deadly attacks from superior opponents, giving their offense-oriented allies time to flank
and overwhelm their mutual foes. Shield archons are 9 feet tall and weigh 800 pounds.
