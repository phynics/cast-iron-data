### Special Abilities

**Phantom Trench (Su)** Three times per day as a full-round action, a forsaken defender can create
an extradimensional trench, as create pit (Reflex DC 14 avoids), except the pit must be in a
contiguous shape made of up to four 5-foot squares. The pit is 10 feet deep and persists for 4
rounds. The save DC is Intelligence-based.

**Phantom Wall (Su)** Three times per day as a standard action, a forsaken defender can create a
wall from loose rocks and bricks, as wall of stone, except the maximum area of the wall is 20 feet
and the wall is always 10 feet high and 1 inch thick. The wall persists for 4 rounds.

### Description

Long ago, these defense specialists planned and supervised the construction of field fortifications
for the Jistka Imperium. Now powered by unholy energies, they can create impromptu defenses in the
blink of an eye.
