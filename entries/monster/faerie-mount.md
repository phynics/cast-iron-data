### Special Abilities

**Stability (Ex)** While standing on the ground, faerie mounts gain a +4 racial bonus to their
Combat Maneuver Defense against [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) and [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat
maneuvers.

**Sure-Footed (Ex)** As long as it is carrying no more than a light load, a faerie mount gains
Nimble Moves as a bonus feat.

### Description

Also called the fey cur and the dwarf dog, the faerie mount is a cousin to the dog and the wolf that
carries Tiny and Small riders well. The creatures' sturdy legs, low center of gravity, and muscular
torsos are especially well-suited to navigating the twisting, hilly, greenerychoked landscape of the
First World, making them the preferred mounts for scouts, messengers, and even knights small enough
to ride the legendarily loyal creatures. While usually found in fey stables, bedecked in saddles and
barding, small packs of fey curs sometimes form symbiotic relationships with herds of wild deer and
goats, protecting the herbivores in exchange for milk and the first opportunity to scavenge fallen
animals.

Faerie mounts grow up to 5 feet long and 3 feet tall, and weigh around 120 pounds.

Tales of the animals' legendary bravery and loyalty inspired Taldan dog breeders to create a breed
of similar proportions. These canines make excellent herding dogs, and are found in the central
highlands of Taldor, Isger, and Molthune. To reflect the Taldan corgi, use the statistics for a
Small [dog](ref/monster/dog), remove its racial Acrobatics bonus to jump, and add the faerie mount's
stability special ability.
