### Special Abilities

**Crippling Bite (Ex)** A slicer beetle's bite attack threatens a critical hit on a roll of 19-20.
If a slicer beetle scores a critical hit on a target, its mandibles cut deep, resulting in a wound
that causes 1d6 [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) and leaving its foe [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d3
rounds from the tremendous pain dealt.

### Description

The slicer beetle sports specialized mouthparts that scissor apart flesh for easier consumption.
Each side of its horizontally closing mandibles fits into the other with barely a hair's breadth to
spare. These creatures prefer to dwell in compact caves or warrens. A typical slicer beetle measures
10 feet long and weighs 800 pounds.
