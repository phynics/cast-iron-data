### Description

Poisonous frogs are a bright green color, with additional bright yellow, red, or blue markings. They
are just over a foot long and weigh 10 pounds. The poison of poisonous frogs is often gathered by
primitive tribes and used to coat darts and arrows for hunting.

You can make a larger poison frog either by applying the giant simple template or by giving a normal
giant frog a poison frog's venomous bite.
