### Description

The enormous deadfall scorpion is disturbingly stealthy for a creature of its size. So named for the
creatures' habit of making their lairs amid the hollows created by deadfalls of old trees, deadfall
scorpions adorn their already camouflaged carapaces with moss and old branches, then lie in wait to
ambush prey when it draws near. They prefer to drag their prey back to their hidden deadfall lairs
to eat, and thus incidental treasure can often be found within such a den. A deadfall scorpion is 24
feet long and weighs 10,000 pounds.
