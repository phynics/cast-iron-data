### Description

The duergar clergy of Droskar generally avoid direct combat, preferring instead to command hordes of
mercenary forces in battle on their behalf. Almara Kazaar is one of the few priests of the Dark
Smith who prefers to wade into the thick of battle herself, meting out the dark fury of her god with
the head of her warhammer. In combat, she is cold and calculating, and prefers to first use her
numerous spells to improve her physical abilities and then unleash other debilitating effects at
range. She wields her warhammer in close quarters, not hesitating to use her racial
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) to increase her size and strike at as many foes
as she can.

The leader of Droskar's faithful in Mabbryn, Almara spends much of her time in the Forge Quencher,
the massive fortress that protects Mabbryn's water supply. Much of Almara's daily life is fraught
with political squabbles with Mabbryn's military leader, Margrave Daguda Urgadan. Nicknamed the
"Would-Be Queen" by members of Droskar's faithful, Almara maintains a tense relationship with
Daguda, but hopes to replace the other political leader of the city one day.
