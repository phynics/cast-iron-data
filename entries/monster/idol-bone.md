### Special Abilities

**Feverish Fortitude (Su)** All creatures within 30 feet of a bone idol gain a +2 bonus on saving
throws against disease and poison. The effect lasts as long as a creature remains within the bone
idol's aura.

**Mad Rage (Su)** Any creature bitten by a bone idol must make a DC 16 Will save or fly into an
uncontrollable, violent rage. Those affected by this rage are treated as being under the effects of
both the spells [rage](ref/spell/rage) and [confusion](ref/spell/confusion) for 1 minute. However,
whenever the affected creature rolls a confusion result that would lead it to flee or attack the
bone idol, the idol instead chooses a target for it to attack. This is a mind-affecting effect. The
save DC is Charisma-based.

**Zombie Fetish (Su)** Any zombie created within 30 feet of a bone idol rises as a
[fast zombie](ref/template/zombie).

**Share Abilities (Su)** An idol can share any [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
that would normally only affect itself (as the caster) with any ally within 5 feet. The idol's
creator or allies familiar with its abilities may request the idol grant them specific powers on its
turn. Spell-like abilities usable in this way are marked with a superscript S.

### Description

Carved from human and animal bones and stitched together with hair, bone idols serve as grisly
fetishes and totems for cannibalistic tribes. Although a number of small, nameless tribes carve bone
idols in honor of obscure gods of disease and beastliness, most bone idols are fashioned to resemble
the fearsome, simian appearance of [Angazhan](ref/faith/angazhan), the demon lord of apes and
jungles.
