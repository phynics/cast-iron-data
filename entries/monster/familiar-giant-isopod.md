### Special Abilities

**Curl (Ex)** As a standard action, a giant isopod can curl into a ball, increasing its natural
armor bonus by +2 but preventing it from taking any move actions. Uncurling is also a standard
action.

### Description

Giant isopods are carnivorous scavengers that scour the ocean's floor. They vaguely resemble the
common wood louse in appearance, though they can grow to be up to 2-1/2 feet long and weigh nearly 4
pounds. Their unusual size and insectile appearance cause many humanoids to be wary of them, despite
their relative harmlessness.
