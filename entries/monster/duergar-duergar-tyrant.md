### Description

One in a hundred births brings forth a duergar bestowed with powerful psychic gifts. When such
children grow into their powers, they often become authoritarian members of their clans known as
tyrants.

[Duergar tyrants](ref/race/duergar-tyrant) are defined by their class levels - they do not have
racial Hit Dice. A duergar tyrant's Challenge Rating is equal to her class level.
