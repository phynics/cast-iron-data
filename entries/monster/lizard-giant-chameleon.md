### Special Abilities

**Tongue (Ex)** A giant chameleon can grab a foe with its tongue and draw the victim to its mouth.
This tongue attack has a reach of 15 feet. The attack does no damage, but allows the creature to
grab. A giant chameleon does not gain the grappled condition while using its tongue in this manner.

### Description

These large lizards have the ability to shift the pigments in their skin to match their
surroundings. A giant chameleon is typically 11 feet long and weighs 160 pounds.
