### Description

True masters of the wild, deer gracefully glide through meadows and among the thickets of the
deepest forests. Found in all climates and most terrains, deer form tightly knit herds for
protection. These creatures fall victim to predators, and many humanoids hunt the animals for food
and hides. They're not picky eaters, and consume all manner of plant material. In areas where
humanoids live, deer can become pests as they graze in fields and destroy gardens in search of food.

The stat block shown above represents a male deer (a stag). Unlike caribou and other similar
animals, female deer only rarely grow antlers, thus they lack gore attacks. Stags are approximately
6 feet long from nose to tail and weigh up to 350 pounds.
