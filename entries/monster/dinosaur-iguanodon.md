damage on a successful critical hit.

### Description

Iguanodons are large, herbivorous dinosaurs that inhabit swamps and forests where they can take
advantage of abundant vegetation. The iguanodon is capable of moving on two feet or on four, quickly
switching from one stance to the other, depending on whether it needs to move through dense foliage
or to reach delectable morsels hanging up in the canopy.

Although iguanodons are herbivores, they are notoriously quick to anger. Their thumb spikes make
their claws particularly devastating weapons - a well-placed blow from one of these claws can turn a
hungry predator into a cowering beast with one swift strike. Iguanodons are 30 feet long and weigh
6,000 pounds.
