### Special Abilities

**Stampede (Ex)** A stampede occurs if three or more creatures with stampede make a trample attack
while remaining adjacent to each other. While stampeding, the creatures can trample foes of their
size or smaller, and the trample's save DC increases by +2.

**Docile (Ex)** Unless specifically trained for combat (see the
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) skill), an ox or yak's gore attacks are treated as
secondary attacks.

### Description

Oxen (singular ox) are castrated cattle.
