### Description

These constructs are the pinnacle of clockwork inventions. An ingenious combination of mechanical
devices and magical conduits allows a clockwork mage to channel the power of a wand into a variety
of arcane powers. They often serve powerful arcane spellcasters, or as unflinching and unquestioning
casters to those who wants the benefit of spells without ego or free thought.

A humanoid figure of [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) and steel, a clockwork mage has large
crystal ball filled with shifting vapors for a head. It stands 7 feet tall.

**Family:** [Clockwork](ref/family/clockwork)
