### Description

The dire bat is usually found lairing in desolate areas, resting in caves or other secluded areas
during the day and taking to the skies in search of prey at night. This immense creature has an
average wingspan of 15 feet and weighs roughly 200 pounds.

The dire bat generally doesn't shelter in groups larger than eight, often living a solitary life. A
dire bat prefers feeding on livestock and herd animals.
