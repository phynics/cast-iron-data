### Description

The woolly rhinoceros is an herbivore, though unlike its non-woolly cousin it grazes for food rather
than browsing on larger plants. The woolly rhino is legendary for its foul temper, and its size and
huge horn give it a ferocious bravery. Any threat (real or perceived) to the rhino or its herd is
met with loud bellows and the stomping of feet. Often, those who unwittingly anger a woolly rhino
have no time to amend the intrusion before the beast attacks.
