### Tactics

**During Combat** A firestarter avoids melee, instead making sneak attacks with its bow and using
alchemist's fire or flaming arrows to start fires, cause panic, and trap opponents. It saves its
goblin skull bomb for a weak opponent, hoping to kill that foe and craft a new goblin skull bomb
from the corpse.

### Description

Goblins adore theft, and are adept at slinking about undetected. Goblin sneaks are master ambushers
and spectacular hiders. The most talented also incorporate their racial love of fire into their
tactics.
