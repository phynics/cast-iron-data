### Description

Tanaq Mammoth-Eater is a savage shaman among his clan of taiga giants, and his prowess in both the
arctic cold and the towering heights of the Tusks proves the title was rightfully earned. A century
ago, he led the tribe from their previous home in Red Rune Canyon in the Realm of the Mammoth Lords
when their preferred mammoth-hunting grounds started to reek foully of demonic taint. He wields his
father's bloodstone impaler, one of the first of its kind crafted by his people, and his hatred for
the Kellid raiders who years ago slew his family still burns fiercely within him.
