### Special Abilities

**Righteous Bite (Su)** A cayhound's bite is treated as magical for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Thunderous Bark (Su)** Once every 1d6 rounds, a cayhound can bark with the concussive force of a
thunderbolt. Every creature within a 15-foot cone-shaped [burst](ref/misc/burst) must succeed at a
DC 14 Fortitude saving throw or take 3d8 points of sonic damage and be knocked
[prone](ref/rule/prone). A successful saving throw reduces this damage by half and negates being
knocked prone. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Unbound (Su)** Cayhounds move as if under a continuous
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) spell. They are immune to
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor), but are affected by
[dimensional lock](ref/spell/dimensional-lock) normally.

### Description

Cayhounds, also called leap dogs, are good-aligned outsiders who exemplify strength and freedom.
They are descendants of Thunder, the pet mastiff of the Accidental God,
[Cayden Cailean](ref/faith/cayden-cailean), who uplifted his faithful companion upon achieving his
own apotheosis. Thunder sired the entire cayhound race (including his famous son, Little Thunder) on
the golden fields of Elysium. Cayhounds are filled with wild and turbulent forces barely contained
within their canine shapes. They are intelligent and feel true lust for and joy in life, including
righteous battle, good food, and strong drink. These fiery red hounds stand head to chest with most
humans and weigh approximately 250 pounds.

Wild as they are, the cayhounds are still more good-aligned than they are chaotic. Like their true
master, they are great champions of freedom, and they channel his strength and courage (and a bit of
luck) in opposing nefarious schemes wherever they find them. Some ally themselves with
[azatas](ref/family/azata), supporting good crusades against the Outer Planes. A pack of cayhounds
might seek to disrupt a night hag and her steed's theft of good souls traveling to their intended
destinations in the Great Beyond. Single cayhounds may be summoned via
[lesser planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-lesser) by priests of Cayden Cailean to come to the aid
of the faithful.
