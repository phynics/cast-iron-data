### Special Abilities

**Electricity (Ex)** An electric eel can produce a powerful jolt of electricity from its tail,
delivering the jolt with a successful touch attack. On a critical hit, the creature struck must make
a DC 15 Fortitude save or be [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is
Constitution-based.

### Description

The electric eel is a curious fish that breathes air instead of water, yet certainly its most
unusual characteristic is its ability to generate powerful jolts of electricity. An electric eel is
6 feet long and weighs 45 pounds.
