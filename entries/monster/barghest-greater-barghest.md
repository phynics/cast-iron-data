### Description

Said to be fiendish relations of all goblinoid races, the hateful [barghests](ref/monster/barghest)
come to the Material Plane to feed. As they consume the bodies of innocents, they grow increasingly
powerful. A barghest eventually sheds its skin to transform into a greater barghest, at which point
it often seeks out a tribe of [goblins](ref/monster/goblin) or other creatures to rule.
