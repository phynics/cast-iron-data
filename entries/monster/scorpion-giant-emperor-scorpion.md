### Special Abilities

**Ambush Pit (Ex)** A giant emperor scorpion can spend 1 minute digging itself into the soil or
other ground debris. It then waits for its [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) to detect prey. When a
creature comes within range, it can erupt from the ground as a free action as part of a charge,
allowing it to use its pounce ability. When dug in, the scorpion has improved cover granting it a
+10 bonus on Stealth checks.

**Poison (Ex)**

**Type:** Poison (Sting-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 22

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d4 and Dexterity damage 1d4

**Cure:** 1 save

The save DC is Constitution-based.

### Description

A giant emperor scorpion is an ambush predator, burying itself in soil or sand until prey
approaches.
