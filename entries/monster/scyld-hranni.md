### Description

Scyld Hranni is a revered storm giant warrior, her family originally hailing from a small islet just
west of the island of Thuryan in Cheliax. The Hranni family gave up their holdings not long after
Cheliax bound their fate to the devils of Hell, and since their disbanding, Scyld has searched for
purpose amid the vast waters of the Arcadian Ocean, traveling along the coast of southern Avistan
and northern Garund. Other storm giants regard her violet hair as a sign of being blessed by the
gods, a notion she encourages through her devoted worship of Aegirran. Clad in sleek mithral armor
and a barnacle-encrusted shield of driftwood and jagged coral, Scyld trains the myriad sea creatures
of the Arcadian Ocean to help her in her search for purpose and her ambitions of eliminating evil
throughout the region.
