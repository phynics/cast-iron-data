### Tactics

**Before Combat** The monarch casts _false life, mage armor,_ and
_[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection)_ on herself.

**During Combat** A ghoul monarch avoids direct confrontation unless she can fight using
_[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person)_ or _[magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar)._ The ghoul
monarch starts combat by casting quickened _[grease](ref/spell/grease)_ and
_[hungry earth](ref/spell/hungry-earth),_ trapping as many spellcasters as she can in the effect. If
the monarch has minions, she focuses on disabling and keeping enemies off guard rather than using
direct damage spells. She targets powerful spellcasters with _[suffocation](ref/spell/suffocation)_
or _[black tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles)._

**Morale** A ghoul monarch flees to a safe haven or attempts to flee by burrowing underground at the
first sign she is in serious danger.

**Base Statistics** Without _false life, mage armor,_ and _[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection),_
the monarch's statistics are AC 19, touch 17, flat-footed 13; hp 161; Defensive Abilities
[channel resistance](ref/rule/channel-resistance) +2.
