### Special Abilities

**Cling (Ex)** If a creature leaves an army ant swarm's square, the swarm suffers 1d6 points of
damage to reflect the loss of its numbers as several of the crawling pests continue to cling
tenaciously to the victim. A creature with army ants clinging to him takes 3d6 points of damage at
the end of his turn each round. As a full-round action, he can remove the ants with a DC 17 Reflex
save. High wind or any amount of damage from an area effect destroys all clinging ants. The save DC
is Dexterity-based.

**Consume (Ex)** An army ant swarm can rapidly consume any creature it swarms over. Against
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless) or [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) targets, an army ant swarm attack
deals 6d6 points of damage.

### Description

The notoriety of the army ant swarm is well earned. Constantly on the march, a hive of army ants is
capable of eating anything that gets in the way of its unabating swath of destruction and ruin.
