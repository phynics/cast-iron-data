### Special Abilities

**Bitter Cold (Su)** All creatures within 20 feet of a glacier toad take 1d6 points of cold damage
each round on the toad's turn.

### Description

Glacier toads are strange, magical cousins of the more common giant toads. None can say for certain
whether they were bred, or evolved from exposure to elemental energies. Glacier toads have no
practical use for most treasure, but have a fondness for shiny objects like gems and glowing items.
