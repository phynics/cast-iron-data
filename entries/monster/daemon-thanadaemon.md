### Special Abilities

**Draining Weapon (Su)** A thanadaemon's energy drain attack functions through any melee weapon it
wields.

**Fear [Gaze](ref/umr/gaze) (Su)** [Cower](ref/misc/cowering) in fear for 1d6 rounds, 30 feet, Will
DC 21 negates. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Soul Crush (Su)** A thanadaemon can crush a _soul gem_ (see
[cacodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-cacodaemon)) as a standard action to gain
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 15 for 15 rounds (this is a standard action). This action
condemns the crushed soul to Abaddon - resurrecting this victim requires a DC 28 caster level check.

### Description

While all daemons represent death in some fashion, thanadaemons, the Deacons of Death, represent the
inevitable death through old age. Thanadaemons effortlessly work eerie skiffs along every pus- and
bile-choked river in Abaddon, including the legendary River Styx. For the right price (typically 50
pp or 2 gems worth at least 300 gp each), a thanadaemon will even carry passengers on its skiff, yet
those who travel with these fiends should beware - they frequently renegotiate the terms once
they've got their passengers in dangerous realms.
