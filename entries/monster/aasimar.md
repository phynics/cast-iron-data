### Description

[Aasimars](ref/race/aasimar) are humans with a significant amount of celestial or other good
outsider blood in their ancestry. Aasimars are not always good, but it is a natural tendency for
them, and they gravitate to good faiths or organizations associated with celestials. Aasimar
heritage can hide for generations, only to appear suddenly in the child of two apparently human
parents. Most societies interpret aasimar births as good omens. Aasimars look mostly human except
for some minor physical trait that reveals their unusual heritage. Typical aasimar features are hair
that shines like metal, unusual eye or skin color, or even glowing golden halos.
