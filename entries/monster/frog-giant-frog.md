### Special Abilities

**Tongue (Ex)** A giant frog's tongue is a primary attack with reach equal to three times the frog's
normal reach (15 feet for a Medium giant frog). A giant frog's tongue deals no damage on a hit, but
can be used to grab. A giant frog does not gain the grappled condition while using its tongue in
this manner.

### Description

Giant frogs have razor-sharp teeth lining their mouths. They are 6 feet long and weigh 200 pounds.
