### Special Abilities

**Aura of Courage (Su)** The Hand of the Inheritor radiates an aura of courage identical to that of
an 18th-level paladin.

**Divine Trigger (Su)** The Hand of the Inheritor can activate any paladin spell from a
[spell trigger](ref/misc/spell-trigger) item as if he were an 18th-level paladin.

### Description

The Hand of the Inheritor is a warrior angel, always ready to answer Iomedae's call whenever she
must directly intervene on Golarion. With his angelic speed and might, he leads the charge when the
Inheritor's celestial armies must go to war. A veteran of countless sorties into the Abyss, the Hand
fights with a song of battle on his lips and unbreakable courage in his heart. The Hand stands 9
feet tall and weighs 900 pounds.
