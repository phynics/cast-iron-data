**Sources:** Black Markets pg 13\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot**; **Price** 150 gp; **Weight** —

Brewed from [belladonna](ref/eq-poison/belladonna) and popular among the backstabbing nobility of
Taldor and Cheliax, burning heart causes victims to burst into flames at the most embarrassing
times.

**Type** poison, ingested; **Save** Fortitude DC 14\
**Onset** 10 minutes; **Frequency** 1/minute for 6 minutes\
**Effect** 1d2 [Str damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), target can attempt one save to cure a
[lycanthropy](ref/rule/lycanthropy) affliction contracted in the past hour; **Cure** 1 save\
**Spell Effect** touch of combustion (Reflex DC 11)

**Requirements** [Brew Potion](ref/feat/brew-potion), [Infuse Poison](ref/feat/infuse-poison),
[touch of combustion](ref/spell/touch-of-combustion); **Cost** 125 gp
