**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 24,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you the ability to use a 1st-level spell from the cleric, druid, or
sorcerer/wizard spell list as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) three times per
day, using your character level as your caster level. The spell keyed to the ioun stone is selected
by the stone's creator. In order for you to use this spell-like ability, you must have a Charisma
score of 11 or higher. The save DCs of the chosen spell are Charisma-based, if any.

#### Resonance

All [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) granted by race are treated as 1 caster
level higher.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[imbue with spell ability](ref/spell/imbue-with-spell-ability), creator must be 12th level; **Cost**
12,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 16,000 gp\
This stone functions as a normal silver spindle ioun stone, but it deals 1d3
[Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to you each time you use the
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 12,000 gp\
This stone functions as a normal silver spindle ioun stone, but instead grants a 0-level spell.
