**Sources:** Pathfinder \#122: Into the Shattered Continent pg 20, Seekers of Secrets pg. 45-47\
**Aura** strong divination and evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you a +5 competence bonus on checks with one [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all)
skill (chosen by the stone's creator).

#### Resonance

This stone grants a +1 competence bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 2,500 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 1,600 gp\
This stone grants a +5 competence bonus on checks with one Knowledge skill (chosen by the stone's
creator) and imparts a -2 penalty to Constitution.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 200 gp\
This stone grants a +1 competence bonus on checks with one Knowledge skill (chosen by the stone's
creator).
