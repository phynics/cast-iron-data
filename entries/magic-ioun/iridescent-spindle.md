**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 149, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 521, Ultimate Equipment pg 305\
**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

Sustains creature without air.

#### Resonance

The wearer gains the constant effects of [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) and takes only
half damage from exposure to vacuum and underwater pressure.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 9,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 12,000 gp\
This stone sustains the wearer without air, but he is always [staggered](ref/misc/staggered).

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 500 gp\
This stone grants a +4 competence bonus on Constitution checks to
[hold your breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath).
