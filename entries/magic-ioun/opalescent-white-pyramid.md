**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

Each stone of this type is keyed to a particular kind of weapon
([heavy flail](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-flail), [light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow),
[short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword), and so on) and grants you proficiency with that weapon.

#### Resonance

[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the stone's keyed weapon.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[transformation](ref/spell/transformation) or proficiency in the appropriate weapon, creator must be
12th level; **Cost** 5,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\
**Price** 3,000 gp\
This stone grants proficiency with the keyed weapon and gives the wearer a -2 penalty to Dexterity.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 1,500 gp\
The stone grants the wearer weapon familiarity with the keyed weapon (it is treated as a martial
weapon for him).
