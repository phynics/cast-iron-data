**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you a +5 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) and
[Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks.

#### Resonance

Breathe water for up to 10 minutes per day, useable in 1-minute increments.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), creator must be
12th level; **Cost** 5,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\
**Price** 8,000 gp\
This stone grants a +5 competence bonus on Acrobatics and Swim checks and a -2 penalty to
Constitution.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 400 gp\
