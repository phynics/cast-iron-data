**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

Sustains creature without food or water.

#### Resonance

The wearer can cast
[protection from chaos/evil/good/law](ref/spell/protection-from-chaos-evil-good-law) on himself once
per day (CL 12th). He can activate this ability as an immediate action, but doing so drains the ioun
stone of its magic, turning it into a [dull gray](ref/magic-ioun/dull-gray-stone) ioun stone.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 2,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 2,000 gp\
This stone sustains the wearer without food but he must consume twice the normal amount of water.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 46\
**Price** 1,000 gp\
This stone sustains the wearer on half the normal amount of food.
