**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

+1 caster level.

#### Resonance

Every time the bearer casts a spell, he rolls 1d4-2 and adds the result (as well as the +1 caster
level granted by the normal power of the ioun stone) to his caster level.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 15,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\
**Price** 25,000 gp\
This stone grants a +1 caster level and a -2 penalty to the wearer's primary casting ability score
(Wisdom for clerics, Intelligence for wizards, and so on).

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 1,000 gp\
Wearer adds one cantrip or orison (determined when the stone is created) to his list of spells known
or spells prepared.
