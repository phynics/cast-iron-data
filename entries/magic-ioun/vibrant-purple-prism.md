**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 149, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 521, Ultimate Equipment pg 305\
**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 36,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

Stores three levels of spells, as a
[ring of spell storing (minor)](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-spell-storing-minor). Spells must be placed
by a spellcaster, but can be used by anyone.

#### Resonance

As a standard action, the bearer can expend a stored spell to refresh a cast spell of the same level
as if using a [pearl of power](ref/magic-wondrous/pearl-of-power). The bearer can refresh up to 3
spell levels per day using this ability.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 18,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\
**Price** 36,000 gp\
This stone stores three levels of spells, as a _ring of spell storing (minor),_ except casting
spells out of the stone requires at least a full-round action.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 2,000 gp\
This stone stores one spell level, as a _ring of spell storing (minor)._
