**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This deep brown ioun stone is streaked with amber and looks much like a tiger's eye gem save for its
perfectly spherical shape. While using this stone, you know at all times exactly how many feet you
are under or above sea level.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[discern location](ref/spell/discern-location), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 5,000 gp
