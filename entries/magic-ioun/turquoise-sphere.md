**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 45\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you a +5 competence bonus on [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks. Any horse or pony you
ride gains the benefits of the [Fleet](ref/feat/fleet) feat.

#### Resonance

+2 competence bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal), [longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider), creator must be 12th
level; **Cost** 3,000 gp
