**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 15\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This brilliant, deep violet crystal is perfectly cut in the shape of a five-pointed star. The gem
grants its bearer a swim speed of 30 feet. When inserted into a wayfinder, it allows the bearer to
breathe water for up to 10 minutes per day (in 1-minute increments).

#### Resonance

Can breathe water for 10 minutes a day (in 1-minute increments)

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[touch of the sea](ref/spell/touch-of-the-sea), creator must be at least 12th level; **Cost** 4,000
gp
