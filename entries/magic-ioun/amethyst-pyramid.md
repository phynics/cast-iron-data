**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you the ability to pass undetected before undead as if under the effects of a
_[hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead)_ spell. Unintelligent undead automatically can't see
you, and intelligent undead receive a single DC 12 Will save to notice you despite the stone's
effects. If you attempt to [channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), turn or command
undead, touch an undead creature, or attack any creature (even with a spell), the stone's effects
are negated for 1 hour.

#### Resonance

+2 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks against undead.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 4,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 6,000 gp\
This stone functions as a normal amethyst pyramid ioun stone, but against undead you take a -1
penalty to AC and on saves.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 150 gp\
This stone grants a +2 competence bonus on [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion)
checks to identify undead and their special abilities.
