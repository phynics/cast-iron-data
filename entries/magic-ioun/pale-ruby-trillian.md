**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 45\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you a +5 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks.

#### Resonance

Increase by 10 feet the distance the bearer may fall safely (stacks with other effects that increase
this distance, such as making [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks or falling into water).

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 2,500 gp
