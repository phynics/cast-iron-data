**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 149, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 521, Ultimate Equipment pg 305\
**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

+1 competence bonus on attack rolls, saves, skill checks, and ability checks.

#### Resonance

Twice per day as a free action, the bearer can remove the [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) condition
from himself (or reduce the [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) condition to fatigued).

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 15,000 gp
