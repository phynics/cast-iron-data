**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

[Alertness](ref/feat/alertness).

#### Resonance

The wearer no longer takes penalties on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks for being
distracted or asleep; while asleep, the wearer can remain open-eyed and fully aware of his
environment, though he is still helpless against undetected threats until he actually wakes up.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 5,000 gp

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 46\
**Price** 400 gp\
This stone grants a +1 competence bonus on Perception and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive)
checks.

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 300 gp\
This stone grants a +2 competence bonus on Perception checks and a -1 penalty to initiative checks.
