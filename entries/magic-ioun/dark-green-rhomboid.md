**Sources:** Dungeoneer's Handbook pg 28\
**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This ioun stone is forest green with flecks of yellow. At all times, it allows you to immediately
notice any poison within 5 feet of you. This effect is otherwise identical to the
_[detect poison](ref/spell/detect-poison)_ spell.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect poison](ref/spell/detect-poison), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 4,000 gp
