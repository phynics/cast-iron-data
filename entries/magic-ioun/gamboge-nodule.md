**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg. 44, 47\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 54,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you immunity to poison, as if wearing a
[periapt of proof against poison](ref/magic-wondrous/periapt-of-proof-against-poison).

#### Resonance

+2 resistance bonus on saving throws against disease.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 27,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 40,000 gp\
This stone grants immunity to poison and a -4 penalty on saving throws against curses and diseases.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 1,500 gp\
This stone grants immunity to one kind of poison, such as [arsenic](ref/eq-poison/arsenic) or
[scorpion venom](ref/eq-poison/large-scorpion-venom).
