**Aura** faint universal; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

These are ioun stones that have been burned out or otherwise rendered all but powerless. They retain
the ability to float and orbit, and are useful as the target of spells such as
[continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame), [daylight](ref/spell/daylight), and
[silence](ref/spell/silence), allowing you to keep your hands free. They may be any shape (cabochon,
disk, ellipsoid, and so on).

#### Resonance

[Read magic](ref/spell/read-magic) 1/day for 10 minutes.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 12 gp, 5 sp
