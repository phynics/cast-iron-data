**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you the ability to comprehend, speak, read, and write a single language (chosen by
the ioun stone's creator).

#### Resonance

+1 bonus on saves against language-dependent effects.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [tongues](ref/spell/tongues),
creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 2,500 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 1,000 gp\
This stone grants you the ability to comprehend and read, but not speak or write, a single language
(chosen by the ioun stone's creator).

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 150 gp\
This stone grants a +1 competence bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks.
