**Sources:** Pathfinder Society Primer pg. 22-23\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 200,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone absorbs your soul as the _[magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar)_ spell immediately prior to
your death. You can't transfer your soul from the ioun stone receptacle to another creature's body,
and are, for all intents and purposes, dead. While your soul is in the ioun stone, you can sense any
life forces within 120 feet as though you were placed in a _[magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar)._ At
any point within 12 hours of your soul's transfer into as long as it is within 120 feet of the ioun
stone. Upon returning to your body, you are [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious) and
[dying](ref/misc/dying) with a number of negative hit points equal to your Constitution score
minus 1. If you fail to stabilize and aren't healed after 1 turn, you die and your soul is not
absorbed by the ioun stone. Your soul can be absorbed into a pale orange rhombus ioun stone only
once per 24-hour period.

#### Resonance

A soul held within the stone can remain there indefinitely as long as the wayfinder remains on the
soul's original body. If the wayfinder is removed from the body, the ioun stone's resonant power is
suppressed until the wayfinder is replaced.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 100,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 12,000 gp\
This stone functions as a normal pale orange rhomboid, but only once, after which it burns out and
turns [dull gray](ref/magic-ioun/dull-gray-stone).

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 1,800 gp\
If you die while using this ioun stone, the stone can be used as an additional material component in
any spell to return you to life, reducing the spell's total material component cost by 20%.
