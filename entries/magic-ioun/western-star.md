**Sources:** Occult Mysteries pg 29\
**Aura** strong illusion; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This blue crystal is cut in the shape of a five-pointed star. As a standard action, its user can
alter his appearance as with a _[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)_ spell. When so disguised,
the user can render the western star and other ioun stones in his possession invisible.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self); **Cost** 2,000 gp
