**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 45\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you a +5 competence bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks.

#### Resonance

+2 competence bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 5,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\

This stone grants a +5 competence bonus on Bluff and Diplomacy checks and a -2 penalty on Will
saving throws.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 400 gp\
