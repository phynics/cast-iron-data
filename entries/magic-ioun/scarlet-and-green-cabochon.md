**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 45\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you the [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) feat.

#### Resonance

+5 bonus on Constitution checks made to stabilize.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), creator must be
12th level; **Cost** 5,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\
**Price** 8,000 gp\
This stone grants the wearer the [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) feat. Anything that would make the
wearer [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) instead makes him [sickened](ref/misc/sickened). Anything that
would make the wearer [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) instead makes him
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated).

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 1,400 gp\
This stone grants a +4 bonus on one type of check affected by the Endurance feat.
