**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 149, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 521, Ultimate Equipment pg 305\
**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

Regenerate 1 point of damage per 10 minutes, as a
[ring of regeneration](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-regeneration). It only cures damage taken while the
user is using the stone.

#### Resonance

+2 sacred bonus on saves against death effects and attacks that rely on negative energy.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 10,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
