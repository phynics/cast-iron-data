**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 15\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This pearlescent crystal is perfectly cut in the shape of a five-pointed star. It grants the bearer
a +5 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) and [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks.
When inserted into a wayfinder, it grants the bearer a +4 bonus on Reflex saves to avoid falling.

#### Resonance

+4 bonus on Reflex Saves to avoid falling

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [jump](ref/spell/jump),
creator must be at least 12th level; **Cost** 5,000 gp
