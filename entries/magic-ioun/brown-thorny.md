## Thorny

**Aura** strong varies; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

Used by warriors of ancient Azlant, these strangely shaped ioun stones are popular with combat-savvy
members of the [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars). These variant ioun stones resemble a
thorny sphere - each possesses a circular shape with dozens of needle-like points jutting out. Each
type of stone is a different color and grants its wearer a +2 competence bonus on checks for a
specific [combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers). All varieties of thorny ioun stone have the
same price and cost.

> **Brown Thorny:** +2 competence bonus on [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempts.
>
> **Crimson Thorny:** +2 competence bonus on [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) attempts.
>
> **Orange Thorny:** +2 competence bonus on [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) attempts.
>
> **Silver Thorny:** +2 competence bonus on [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) attempts.
>
> **Teal Thorny:** +2 competence bonus on [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) attempts.
>
> **Violet Thorny:** +2 competence bonus on [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) attempts.

#### Resonance

Grants the bearer the benefit of either the [Acrobatic](ref/feat/acrobatic) or the
[Athletic](ref/feat/athletic) feat. The feat varies by individual ioun stone, and each is equally
likely (50% chance).

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be 12th level;
**Cost** 4,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 6,900 gp\
The flawed version of a thorny ioun stone of any type functions as the normal version, but the
wearer also takes a -2 penalty to CMD against the corresponding type of combat maneuver check.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 3,200 gp\
A cracked thorny ioun stone provides a +1 competence bonus on its corresponding type of combat
maneuver check.
