**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 24,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you a +2 enhancement bonus to Constitution. The enhancement bonuses from multiple
onyx rhomboids stack (up to a maximum of a +6 enhancement bonus to Constitution).

#### Resonance

[Virtue](ref/spell/virtue) at will (self only).

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 12,000 gp

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 48\
**Price** 16,000 gp\
This stone grants a +2 enhancement bonus to Constitution per stone, stacking up to +4 total. These
stones do not stack with non-flawed onyx rhomboid ioun stones.

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 47\
**Price** 12,000 gp\
Individually has no effect; when used in pairs, each pair grants a +2 enhancement bonus to
Constitution. The bonuses from multiple stones (cracked or otherwise) stack, up to a maximum of a +6
bonus.
