**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** —

#### Description

This stone grants you a benefit when you're [dying](ref/misc/dying). When determining the number of
negative hit points you must accrue to die, treat your Constitution score as if it were 2 higher.
This doesn't provide a bonus to Constitution, so it doesn't grant a bonus on checks to stabilize,
but it does provide you 2 additional rounds in which you can attempt such checks. You can benefit
from only one tourmaline sphere ioun stone at a time.

#### Resonance

This stone grants a +1 bonus on Constitution checks to stabilize when dying.

#### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance), creator must be 12th level; **Cost** 500 gp

### Cracked Ioun Stone

**Price** 800 gp\
This stone grants a +1 insight bonus on saving throws against death effects.

### Flawed Ioun Stone

**Price** 600 gp\
This stone functions as a normal tourmaline sphere ioun stone, but you take a -1 penalty on
Constitution checks to stabilize when dying.
