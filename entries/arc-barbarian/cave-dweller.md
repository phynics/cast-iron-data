**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 37\
Unused to the light of the sun, cave dwellers use their subterranean expertise to protect their
clans from the manifold dangers of deep caverns and tunnels. The typical cave dweller is perfectly
content to live his entire life underground, and he may even view the light of the sun as
particularly abhorrent or taboo. While this lifestyle doesn't in and of itself imply evil or
sinister intent, many outlanders ignorantly assume that cave dwellers avoid the light of the sun for
malicious reasons. In truth, cave dwellers are as capable of goodness and kindness as any other -
they just see little reason to venture out of their preferred homelands. When a cave dweller joins
an adventuring party, she may retain an inherent nervousness about sleeping under the open sky and
may long for the familiar confines of a comforting cavern, but this rarely translates directly into
a notable disadvantage. Indeed, many adventuring groups come to value the specialized talents and
skills the cave dweller brings to the party.

**Gained class skills:** stl

**Lost class skills:** ride

**Tight Tunnels (Ex)**

**Passive:** Cave dwellers pursue prey through the winding tunnels of the deep, which rarely
accommodate open sprints. A cave dweller can make a single turn up to 90 degrees while
[running](ref/rule/run) (though not while [charging](ref/rule/charge)). Additionally, a cave dweller
takes no penalties to movement speed while [squeezing](ref/misc/squeezing).

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Tunnel Vision (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A cave dweller gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to a range of 60 feet and a
bonus on Perception checks to detect ambushes, movement, and sound while in darkness. This bonus is
equal to one-third of her barbarian level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Sun Walker (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** The bonus becomes +2 and the reduction is now 2.

**At 13th Level:** The bonus becomes +3 and the reduction is now 3.

**At 16th Level:** The bonus becomes +4 and the reduction is now 4.

**At 19th Level:** The bonus becomes +5 and the reduction is now 5.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** A cave dweller gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC and on saving throws against effects with
the light descriptor or that produce bright or sudden light. Any penalties on attack rolls, saving
throws, or skill checks because of bright light are reduced by 1.

**Replaces:** [Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)
