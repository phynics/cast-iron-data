Some barbarians are truly savage, having little training in modern arms. These savage barbarians
learn to avoid blows and toughen up their skin. A savage barbarian has the following class features.

**Naked Courage (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** The savage barbarian gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC and a +1 morale bonus on saving
throws against fear when wearing no armor (shields are allowed).

**Replaces:** Trap sense

At 9th level: p!The bonuses +2.

At 15th level: p!The bonuses +3.

**Natural Toughness (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** The savage barbarian gains a +1 natural armor bonus to AC when wearing no armor
(shields are allowed).

**Replaces:** [Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)

At 10th level: The bonus +2.

At 13th level: The bonus +3.

At 16th level: The bonus +4.

At 19th level: The bonus +5.

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the savage barbarian archetype:
[flesh wound](ref/ragepower/flesh-wound), [guarded stance](ref/ragepower/guarded-stance),
[increased damage reduction](ref/ragepower/increased-damage-reduction),
[intimidating glare](ref/ragepower/intimidating-glare),
[rolling dodge](ref/ragepower/rolling-dodge), [superstition](ref/ragepower/superstition),
[terrifying howl](ref/ragepower/terrifying-howl).
