**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 7\
Though named for the wild savages who fight alongside rabid dogs, mad dogs employ all manner of
beasts as their battle brethren.

**War Beast (Ex)**

**Ability:** A mad dog gains the service of an animal companion, using the barbarian's level as her
effective druid level.

**Replaces:** 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th level rage powers

**Pack Tactics (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A mad dog and her _war beast_ gain a +4 bonus on attack rolls while flanking the same
opponent (instead of the normal +2 bonus).

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Rage (Ex)**

**Ability:** A mad dog gains this ability at 4th level, except that her effective barbarian level
for the ability is equal to her barbarian level - 3.

**Alters:** Rage

**Ferocious Fetch (Ex)**

**Ability:** A mad dog's _war beast_ gains [Improved Drag](ref/feat/improved-drag) as a bonus feat.
As a swift action, a mad dog can command her _war beast_ to move toward an opponent within 30 feet
and attempt a [drag](ref/rule/drag) combat maneuver to pull the target back toward the mad dog. The
_war beast_ can move before and after performing the maneuver, but this movement counts toward the
_war beast's_ total movement during its turn.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Damage Reduction (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** A mad dog gains [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) DR 1/-. A mad dog's _war
beast_ gains the same damage reduction as the mad dog.

**Special:** At 13th level and every 3 barbarian levels thereafter, this
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) rises by 1, to a maximum of DR 4/- at 19th level.

**Throat Cutter (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever her _war beast_ succeeds at a bull rush, drag, grapple, overrun, or trip
combat maneuver against an opponent threatened by the mad dog, a mad dog can take an attack of
opportunity against that opponent.

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Replaces:** Indomitable will
