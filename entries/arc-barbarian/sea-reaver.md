**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 29\
Not all barbarians hunt forests, plains, and mountains. Some are raiding terrors on the sea and
coasts, pillaging those who hoard treasure and pursuing monsters of the deep. Some sea reavers are
no more than hunters of the open sea, while others are raiders striking fear into coastal
settlements within reach of the sea reavers' longships.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A sea reaver is not proficient with medium armor.

**Marine Terror (Ex)**

**Passive:** A sea reaver can [hold her breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for a number of rounds
equal to four times her Constitution score. In addition, a sea reaver can move normally though
squares of standing water or bog that is 1 foot deep. It does not cost her extra movement to
traverse these terrains. Lastly, a sea reaver ignores the normal [cover](ref/rule/cover) bonus to AC
when attacking creatures that are partially immersed in water.

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Eyes of the Storm (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A sea reaver ignores any [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) provided by fog, rain,
sleet, mist, wind, or other weather effects that is less than total concealment, and any penalties
weather applies on Perception checks are halved.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Savage Sailor (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A sea reaver gains a bonus on Acrobatics, Climb, Profession (sailor), Survival, and
Swim checks made in aquatic terrain, including aboard a ship or along shorelines. This bonus is
equal to one-third of her barbarian level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Sure-Footed (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A sea reaver takes no penalties when moving across slick surfaces, whether natural or
magical (e.g., [grease](ref/spell/grease), [ice storm](ref/spell/ice-storm), and
[sleet storm](ref/spell/sleet-storm)). She is not at risk of falling, is not denied her Dexterity
bonus when moving across such areas, and does not treat them as difficult terrain.

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge
