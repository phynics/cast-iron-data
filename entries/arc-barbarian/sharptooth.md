**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 39\
Coastal hunters and agents of the ocean's rage, sharptooth barbarians emulate the greatest predators
of the sea. Sharks, in particular, are respected and admired by many such tribes, if not as
manifestations of divine power, then merely as scions of the power and fury of the natural world.

**Swim Like a Fish (Ex)**

**Ability:** A sharptooth gains a swim speed of 10 feet.

**At 10th Level:** The swim speed becomes 20 feet.

**At 15th Level:** The swim speed becomes 25 feet.

**At 20th Level:** The swim speed becomes 30 feet.

**At 5th Level:** The swim speed becomes 15 feet.

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Scent of Blood (Ex)**

**Ability:** A sharptooth gains [scent](ref/umr/scent).

**At 5th Level:** She also gains [keen scent](ref/umr/keen-scent).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge, improved uncanny dodge

**Ocean Breath (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A sharptooth gains a bonus equal to [one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her
barbarian level. She adds this number to her Constitution score for the purposes of
[holding her breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath). She also adds it as a bonus on Constitution checks
to continue holding her breath.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Blood in the Water (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A sharptooth can tear the flesh from living creatures.

**At 12th Level:** This bleed damage increases to equal her Constitution modifier if it's higher.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** Her attacks add 3 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage while she is _raging._

**Replaces:** 6th- and 12th-level rage powers

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the sharptooth archetype:
[animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury), [bleeding blow](ref/ragepower/bleeding-blow),
[contagious rage](ref/ragepower/contagious-rage), [pack rage](ref/ragepower/pack-rage),
[powerful blow](ref/ragepower/powerful-blow), [primal scent](ref/ragepower/primal-scent),
[raging swimmer](ref/ragepower/raging-swimmer), [scent](ref/ragepower/scent).
