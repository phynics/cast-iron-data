**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 78\
Barbarians are known for their ability to consume potent drink, but drunken brutes turn drinking
into a combat tactic, using the potent liquor to fuel their rage and grant them additional powers. A
drunken brute has the following class feature.

**Raging Drunk (Ex)**

**Move:** While _raging,_ the drunken brute can drink a potion, or a tankard of ale or similar
quantity of alcohol, as a move action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. A potion has its
normal effect, while an alcoholic drink allows the barbarian to maintain her _rage_ that round
without expending a round of _rage_ for the day (instead of the alcohol's normal effects).

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Special:** For each alcoholic drink consumed while _raging,_ the barbarian is
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round when her _rage_ expires, in addition to the normal
fatigue that follows a _rage. Tireless rage_ does not negate this nauseated condition but the
_internal fortitude_ rage power does.

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the drunken brute archetype:
[boasting taunt](ref/ragepower/boasting-taunt),
[good for what ails you](ref/ragepower/good-for-what-ails-you),
[internal fortitude](ref/ragepower/internal-fortitude),
[liquid courage](ref/ragepower/liquid-courage),
[moment of clarity](ref/ragepower/moment-of-clarity), [roaring drunk](ref/ragepower/roaring-drunk),
[staggering drunk](ref/ragepower/staggering-drunk).
