**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 114\
A flesheater eats flesh to create a spiritual bond between herself and the consumed creature,
allowing her to take on aspects of the creature that served as the meal.

**Rage**

**Alters:** Rage

**Passive:** In additional to the standard bonuses and penalties of _rage,_ a flesheater takes a -2
penalty to Intelligence while _raging._ The additional benefits and penalties from a flesheater's
abilities last only as long as her _rage_ does, and she cannot use these abilities if they would
bring the total penalty to Intelligence to an amount equal to or greater than her Intelligence
score.

**One Flesh (Su)**

**Ability:** When entering a _rage,_ a flesheater can spend an additional round of _rage_ and take
an additional -2 penalty to Intelligence to take on a single ability of the valid consumed creature
whose flesh she ate most recently. For the purpose of this ability, a valid consumed creature is any
dead animal, dragon, humanoid, or magical beast that died and was consumed by the flesheater within
the past 24 hours. She can choose any ability listed under [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1),
as long as the creature possessed that ability.

**At 5th Level:** The flesheater can take on any number of the creature's abilities that are listed
under _[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1)._

**At 8th Level:** The flesheater can instead take on one of the creature's abilities listed under
[beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2). The flesheater's appearance alters to resemble the
consumed creature, but she does not change shape. This is a polymorph effect.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge, improved uncanny dodge, the 2nd- and 8th-level rage powers

**Unbound Rage (Su)**

**Ability:** _Unbound rage_ affects her as [enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person), except she
receives a +4 size bonus to Strength and an additional -2 penalty to Intelligence.

**Flavor:** A flesheater can draw in mass from the Ethereal Plane to enlarge herself when she enters
a _rage,_ as long as the creature whose flesh she last consumed was at least one size category
larger than her base size.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Greater rage

**Feast (Su)**

**Ability:** A flesheater can use her _one flesh_ and _unbound rage_ abilities to emulate any one
valid consumed creature of her choice, rather than only the one she consumed most recently.

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Replaces:** The 14th-level rage power

**Unbound Form (Su)**

**Ability:** A flesheater can use her _one flesh_ ability to assume the shape of a valid consumed
creature. This ability functions as [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self),
[beast shape IV](ref/spell/beast-shape-4), [form of the dragon III](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-3),
or [giant form II](ref/spell/giant-form-2), as appropriate. This is a polymorph effect.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Mighty rage

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the flesheater archetype:
[animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury), [beast totem](ref/ragepower/beast-totem),
[inspire ferocity](ref/ragepower/inspire-ferocity),
[internal fortitude](ref/ragepower/internal-fortitude), and
[terrifying howl](ref/ragepower/terrifying-howl).
