Some barbarians are born or bred outside the reach of civilization. These wildborn fight and survive
without ever seeing a forge or worked stone, and only dare a trip into the city when no other option
is available.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Ability:** A wildborn gains [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) and
[Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard) as bonus feats.

**Alters:** The barbarian's normal weapon and armor proficiencies

**Passive:** A wildborn is proficient with leather and hide armors (but not shields) and all simple
weapons that can be crafted from bone, stone, or wood. If she uses any other kind of weapon or
armor, she loses her abilities from this archetype (except illiteracy) for the next 24 hours.

**Illiteracy**

**Info:** A wildborn cannot read or write. She can learn to read or write a language only if she
takes a level in another class, and even then only by taking 1 or more ranks in Linguistics.

**Live Off the Land (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A wildborn adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her barbarian level as a bonus
on Survival checks to hunt or gather food and gains a bonus on Fortitude saves to resist the effects
of extreme temperatures or other environmental effects; the Fortitude save bonus is equal to
one-third of her barbarian level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Bonus Feats**

**Alters:** Rage powers

**Special:** At 4th level, 10th level and 16th level, a wildborn can choose a bonus feat from the
following rather than select a rage power: [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Animal Affinity](ref/feat/animal-affinity), [Athletic](ref/feat/athletic),
[Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude),
[Improvised Weapon Mastery](ref/feat/improvised-weapon-mastery), [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will),
[Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/lightning-reflexes), [Self-Sufficient](ref/feat/self-sufficient),
[Stealthy](ref/feat/stealthy), or [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything).

**Inexhaustible (Ex)**

**Alters:** [Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** A wildborn recovers nonlethal damage for each minute of rest as if an hour had passed.
She applies twice her [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) gained from barbarian levels
against nonlethal damage.

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the wildborn archetype:
[animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury), [beast totem](ref/ragepower/beast-totem),
[bestial climber](ref/ragepower/bestial-climber), [bestial swimmer](ref/ragepower/bestial-swimmer),
[greater beast totem](ref/ragepower/greater-beast-totem),
[lesser beast totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-beast-totem),
[low-light vision](ref/ragepower/low-light-vision), [raging climber](ref/ragepower/raging-climber),
[raging leaper](ref/ragepower/raging-leaper), [raging swimmer](ref/ragepower/raging-swimmer),
[swift foot](ref/ragepower/swift-foot).
