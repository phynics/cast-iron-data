A raging barbarian is frightening enough in melee, but some become skilled at throwing objects at
their foes before closing in for the kill. A hurler has the following class feature.

**Skilled Thrower (Ex)**

**Passive:** The hurler is skilled at throwing objects in combat. Increase the range increment of
any thrown weapon or object by 10 feet.

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the hurler archetype:
[hurling](ref/ragepower/hurling), [greater hurling](ref/ragepower/greater-hurling),
[hurling charge](ref/ragepower/hurling-charge), [lesser hurling](ref/ragepower/lesser-hurling),
[strength surge](ref/ragepower/strength-surge),
[surprise accuracy](ref/ragepower/surprise-accuracy).
