**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 38, Champions of Corruption pg 27\
While savagery is not inherently evil, some barbaric cultures thrive on depravity and welcome the
act of feeding on their own kind. The raging cannibal is a barbarian who feasts upon her fallen
opponents not out of hunger, or even a taste for flesh, but rather because she believes consuming
her foes and defiling their lost lives demonstrates her strength. This penchant for feasting on the
flesh of her kin makes the raging cannibal an awkward companion to travel with for many groups, with
rumors revolving around the raging cannibal's culinary exploits often causing tense situations that
other party members might be called upon to resolve via diplomacy before the raging cannibal grows
frustrated and decides to take matters into her own hands!

**Animal Fury**

**Ability:** A raging cannibal must select [animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury) as her first
rage power.

**Alters:** The barbarian's 2nd-level rage power

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Consume Vigor (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a raging cannibal reduces a creature of the same creature type as herself to 0 or
fewer hit points with her bite attack during combat while _raging,_ she can consume a chunk of its
body to gain a portion of its power. If the creature's CR equals or exceeds her barbarian level, the
raging cannibal gains 1 additional round of _rage._ She can never consume the same creature's vigor
more than once.

**At 5th Level:** The raging cannibal now gains 2 additional rounds of _rage_ each time she uses
this ability.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

At 8th level: She now gains 3 rounds of _rage._

At 11th level: She now gains 4 rounds of _rage._

At 14th level: She now gains 5 rounds of _rage._

At 17th level: She now gains 6 rounds of _rage._

At 20th level: She now gains 7 rounds of _rage._

**Intimidating Gouge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** When a raging cannibal confirms a critical hit against a creature of the same creature
type as herself with her bite attack while _raging,_ she gains a bonus on Intimidate checks equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her barbarian level for the duration of the _rage._

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Feed from Fury (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A raging cannibal is empowered by eating her enemies during combat.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** When a raging cannibal confirms a critical hit against a creature of the same creature
type as herself with her bite attack while _raging,_ she gains a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to her barbarian level. These temporary
hit points stack with other temporary hit points gained from this ability but not with those from
other sources. These temporary hit points fade after a period of time equal to 10 minutes per
barbarian level.

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Razor-Toothed Fury (Ex)**

**Ability:** When using her bite attack, the raging cannibal can take a -1 penalty on her attack
roll to add 2 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage to the damage dealt.

**At 10th Level:** The penalty becomes -2 and the bleed damage becomes 4.

**At 14th Level:** The penalty becomes -3 and the bleed damage becomes 6.

**At 18th Level:** The penalty becomes -4 and the bleed damage becomes 8.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** [Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the raging cannibal archetype:
[internal fortitude](ref/ragepower/internal-fortitude),
[intimidating glare](ref/ragepower/intimidating-glare), [primal scent](ref/ragepower/primal-scent),
[scent](ref/ragepower/scent), [terrifying howl](ref/ragepower/terrifying-howl).
