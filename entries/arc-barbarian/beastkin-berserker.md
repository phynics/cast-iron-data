While some barbarians take on bestial aspects in their rages, the beastkin berserker descends so
deeply into primal fury that she actually transforms into an animal. Berserkers of the surface world
often associate with predators such as bears or wolves, and are sometimes mistaken for lycanthropes.
The barbarians in Deep Tolguth shapechange into rampaging dinosaurs or megafauna, crafting their
armor out of the hide and bones of their favored animals.

**Savage Rapport (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** The beastkin berserker can select a third kind of animal to which these bonuses
apply.

**At 15th Level:** The beastkin berserker can select a fourth kind of animal to which these bonuses
apply.

**At 20th Level:** The beastkin berserker can select a fifth kind of animal to which these bonuses
apply.

**At 5th Level:** The beastkin berserker can select a second kind of animal to which these bonuses
apply.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A beastkin berserker gains a spiritual connection to a specific kind of creature with
the animal type (such as a [badger](ref/monster/badger) or a
[triceratops](ref/monster/dinosaur-triceratops)). She adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding)
her level (minimum 1) on Handle Animal, Intimidate, and Sense Motive checks involving the chosen
type of animal.

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Feral Transformation (Su)**

**Ability:** When entering a _rage,_ a beastkin berserker can take the form of a Small or Medium
animal. This functions as [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1), except the duration is for as
long as the beastkin berserker rages and the animal must be one she has chosen with her _savage
rapport._

**At 12th Level:** A beastkin berserker is able to use _feral transformation_ to take the form of a
Huge or Diminutive animal that she has chosen with her savage rapport. _Feral transformation_ now
functions as [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3).

**At 8th Level:** The beastkin berserker can use _feral transformation_ to take the form of a Large
or Tiny animal that she has chosen with her savage rapport. _Feral transformation_ now acts as
[beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2).

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** 4th, 8th, and 12th level rage powers
