More "civilized" folk sometimes scoff at the people of the Mwangi Expanse, dismissing them as
primitives with inferior technology. Although some tribes do rely on wood and bone over metal, their
greatest advantage comes from mastery of the terrain. A jungle rager can disappear from sight,
strike from hiding, and use the environment to shield herself from harm.

**Home Ground Advantage (Ex)**

**Choice:** She chooses a specific terrain type from the ranger list of
[favored terrains](ref/ability/favored-terrain).

**Flavor:** A jungle rager learns how to make optimal use of the natural cover of her homeland.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** When in her chosen terrain, she increases her bonus to AC and on Reflex saving throws
gained from [cover](ref/rule/cover), partial cover, and improved cover (but not soft cover) by 1. In
this terrain, she also increases the miss chance caused by [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) for
attacks against her by 5%.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Jungle Endurance (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A jungle rager gains a bonus on Fortitude saves to resist the effects of
[hot weather](ref/rule/heat-dangers) and diseases. This bonus is equal to one-third of her barbarian
level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Home Ground Supremacy (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A jungle rager becomes even more capable when navigating and fighting in her preferred
terrain. She gains the [woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride) ranger ability, though this
ability works only in the terrain she selected for the home ground advantage ability. In this
terrain, she gains an additional +1 bonus to AC when benefiting from cover or improved cover (but
not soft cover).

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Damage Reduction (Ex)**

**Alters:** [Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)

**Special:** A jungle rager gains this ability at 8th level, except her effective barbarian level
for the ability is equal to her barbarian level - 1. In addition, this
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) is doubled against damage dealt by creatures with the
swarm subtype. This ability otherwise functions as the barbarian ability of the same name.

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the jungle rager archetype:
[animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury), [bestial climber](ref/ragepower/bestial-climber),
[hive totem](ref/ragepower/hive-totem), [internal fortitude](ref/ragepower/internal-fortitude),
[low-light vision](ref/ragepower/low-light-vision), [night vision](ref/ragepower/night-vision),
[raging climber](ref/ragepower/raging-climber), [scent](ref/ragepower/scent),
[sharpened accuracy](ref/ragepower/sharpened-accuracy),
[surprise accuracy](ref/ragepower/surprise-accuracy).
