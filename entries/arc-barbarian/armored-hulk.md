**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 28\
Some barbarians disdain the hides and leather used as armor by most of their kin. Instead they
master the heaviest of armors, even those created by more civilized people, to gain greater
protection and stability in battle.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Ability:** An armored hulk gains proficiency in heavy armor.

**Indomitable Stance (Ex)**

**Passive:** An armored hulk gains a +1 bonus on combat maneuver checks and to CMD for overrun
combat maneuvers, and on Reflex saves against [trample](ref/umr/trample) attacks. She also gains a
+1 bonus to her AC against charge attacks and on attack and damage rolls against charging creatures.

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Armored Swiftness (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** When wearing medium or heavy armor, an armored hulk can move 5 feet faster than normal,
to a maximum of her speed.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Resilience of Steel (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** While wearing heavy armor, she gains a bonus to AC that applies only on critical hit
confirmation rolls. This bonus is equal to one-third of her barbarian level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Improved Armored Swiftness (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** An armored hulk's land speed is faster than the norm for her race by +10 feet.

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Special:** This benefit applies when she is wearing any armor, including heavy armor, but not
while carrying a heavy load. Apply this bonus before modifying the armored hulk's speed because of
any load carried or armor worn. This bonus stacks with any other bonuses to the barbarian's land
speed.
