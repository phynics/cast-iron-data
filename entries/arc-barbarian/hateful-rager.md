From a young age, many half-orcs are treated cruelly, bullied, ridiculed, and made outcasts. While
some hide their shame, others foster a deep, burning hatred that they channel into a raw fury and
unleash against their enemies. These half-orcs are called hateful ragers. A hateful rager has the
following class features.

**Reduced Rage (Ex)**

**Info:** At 2nd level and every level thereafter, a hateful rager only gains 1 additional round of
_rage_ per day instead of the normal 2 additional rounds of _rage_ per day.

**Favored Enemy (Ex)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the hateful rager makes every effort to fight a favored enemy rather
than other opponents. If aware of the presence of a favored enemy, the hateful rager must make a DC
20 Will save each round to attack another creature; failure means the barbarian must attack the
favored enemy or move closer to that enemy. She may freely attack creatures preventing her from
reaching that favored enemy (regardless of whether they are actively trying to prevent her attacks
or merely in the way). She can avoid harmful obstacles normally in her attempts to reach the target
and is not forced to take the shortest route.

**Choice:** A hateful rager selects a [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy). This ability works
identically to the [ranger](ref/class/ranger) ability of the same name.

**At 14th Level:** The hateful rager selects a third favored enemy and increases her bonus against
one favored enemy type.

**At 20th Level:** The hateful rager selects a fourth favored enemy and increases her bonus against
one favored enemy type.

**At 8th Level:** The hateful rager selects a second favored enemy and increases her bonus against
one favored enemy type, as described in the ranger class.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** 2nd, 8th, 14th, and 20th level rage powers

**Feed the Rage (Ex)**

**Ability:** A hateful rager gains 1 additional round of _rage_ for each favored enemy she knocks
unconscious or kills in combat. These current rounds of _rage_ can only be used to add to the
duration of her _rage,_ and disappear when the _rage_ ends.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Amplified by Hate (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** A hateful rager adds [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her favored enemy bonus to
the DC of any rage power she uses against a favored enemy.

**Replaces:** Trap sense +3

**Rage Powers:** The following rage powers complement the hateful rager archetype:
[intimidating glare](ref/ragepower/intimidating-glare), [roused anger](ref/ragepower/roused-anger),
[terrifying howl](ref/ragepower/terrifying-howl), [come and get me](ref/ragepower/come-and-get-me),
[inspire ferocity](ref/ragepower/inspire-ferocity),
[overbearing advance](ref/ragepower/overbearing-advance),
[reckless abandon](ref/ragepower/reckless-abandon).
