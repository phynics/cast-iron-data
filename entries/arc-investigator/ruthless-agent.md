**Sources:** Antihero's Handbook pg 27\
Ruthless agents are often called upon for their skills at extracting information by any means
necessary. Though some view their methods as extreme, ruthless agents prize getting the job done at
all costs. Ruthless agents can be found in the employ of nearly any organization - even goodly
groups whose leaders either don't know the extent of ruthless agents' zeal or are willing to look
the other way to take advantage of these investigators' incredible efficiency.

**Prerequisites:** Alignment must be nongood.

**Inspiration (Ex)**

**Ability:** She can use _inspiration_ on Intimidate, Knowledge, and Sense Motive checks without
expending a use of _inspiration,_ provided she's trained in the skill. A ruthless agent can't spend
_inspiration_ on attack rolls or saving throws.

**Alters:** Inspiration

**Flavor:** A ruthless agent focuses on reading motivations and using interrogation tactics.

**Enhanced Intimidation (Ex)**

**Ability:** Additionally, the ruthless agent is an expert in blending coercion and fear. The first
time the ruthless agent attempts to use Intimidate to influence an opponent's attitude and fails,
she can retry it against that same opponent with no increase to the DC. Subsequent attempts to
influence the same opponent are subject to increased DCs as normal.

**Passive:** A ruthless agent adds half her investigator level (minimum +1) as a bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. When the ruthless agent uses Intimidate to influence an
opponent's attitude, the duration of the attitude shift is 1d6&times&10 minutes per investigator
level (rather than the normal 1d6&times&10 minutes).

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Interrogate (Su)**

**Fullround:** A ruthless agent can ask a target a number of questions equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her investigator level. The target is not compelled to
answer truthfully, but the ruthless agent's implicit or explicit threats and looming manner leads
the target to believe the ruthless agent will cause it serious harm if it lies. This imposes a -2
penalty on the target's Bluff checks to lie when answering the specific questions the ruthless agent
asked.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Special:** This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

**Usage:** Intelligence3 modifier times/day

At 6th level: The penalty -4.

At 9th level: The penalty -6.

At 12th level: The penalty -8.

At 15th level: The penalty -10.

At 18th level: The penalty -12.

**Agonizing Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** When she deals damage using her _studied strike,_ she can make this damage nonlethal
(regardless of whether her weapon normally deals lethal or nonlethal damage), forcing the target to
succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + half the ruthless agent's level + her Intelligence
modifier) or be [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round and take a -2 penalty on Will saving
throws for 1 minute.

**Flavor:** A ruthless agent has learned how to finesse her attacks to discomfort a foe.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Swift alchemy

**Special:** Multiple agonizing strikes do not worsen the condition or impose an additional penalty
on Will saves, but they do increase the duration of both effects.

**Concoction of Truth (Su)**

**Info:** A ruthless agent automatically adds [discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies) to her list of
formulae known. She prepares this as a 3rd-level extract, and she can prepare one
_[discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies)_ extract each day that does not count against her daily
allotment of extracts.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Investigator talent gained at 7th level

**Compel Obedience (Sp)**

**Fullround:** A ruthless agent can issue a binding command to a creature once per day. This command
acts like [geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest) with the creature as the target, using the ruthless
agent's investigator level as the caster level. The ruthless agent can compel obedience from only
one creature at a time (as soon as the _[geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest)_ is fulfilled, however,
the ruthless agent can use this ability again, as long as she has a use of this ability available).

**At 17th Level:** The ruthless agent can compel obedience from two creatures at once, and she is
always aware of the targets as if they were under the effects of a [status](ref/spell/status) spell
(caster level = the ruthless agent's investigator level).

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Investigator talents gained at 11th and 17th levels
