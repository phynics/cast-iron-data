**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 17\
Lamplighters are investigators tasked with keeping the night streets safe and lit, and securing
darkened areas against whatever dangers lurk in the shadows. They also investigate those who seek to
hide their crimes from the light of day.

**Lost class skills:** perf, prof

**Lamplighter (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Move:** A lamplighter can create a small flame that immediately ignites a lamp, torch, or similar
flammable item he's holding.

**Alchemical Illumination (Sp)**

**At 11th Level:** The lamplighter can cast [judgment light](ref/spell/judgment-light). The
lamplighter need not have an active _judgment_ to cast this spell, can choose what color of judgment
light is created, can use his alignment for golden light, and can select any element for copper
light.

**At 5th Level:** The lamplighter can cast [daylight](ref/spell/daylight) and
[discovery torch](ref/spell/discovery-torch).

**At 8th Level:** The lamplighter can cast [searing light](ref/spell/searing-light).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Poison lore, poison resistance, poison immunity

**Standard:** A lamplighter can cast certain [light](ref/spelldef/light) spells by burning a magic
extract, infusion, or potion of a spell of the same or greater level than the light spell in a lit
lantern or torch. If the spell has a range of touch, the lantern or torch counts as the object
touched. The spell's DC, if any, equals 10 + spell level + the lamplighter's Intelligence modifier.
Starting out, the lamplighter can cast [light](ref/spell/light) and
[continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame) (24 hour duration).

**Ready for the Revelation (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** The lamplighter adds his Intelligence bonus as well as his Dexterity bonus to
initiative checks.

**At 15th Level:** The lamplighter can act last during the surprise round even if he is otherwise
unaware combat has started.

**At 18th Level:** The lamplighter is not limited to just a move or standard action during the
surprise round: he can act normally.

**At 3rd Level:** A lamplighter can use _inspiration_ on initiative checks without spending a use of
_inspiration._ He can use his Intelligence modifier in place of his Dexterity modifier on initiative
checks.

**At 6th Level:** If the lamplighter has [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw), he can draw a weapon as
part of his initiative check.

**At 9th Level:** The lamplighter isn't [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) before he acts in the
first round of combat.

**Replaces:** Keen recollection, trap sense
