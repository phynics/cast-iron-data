A majordomo is a skilled individual able to keep the complex operations of a noble's holdings
running on a day-to-day basis. In a world of intrigue where the loyalties of staff can be bought and
sold, sometimes it takes the skills of an investigator to follow the byzantine paper trails and
prevent embezzling and espionage within the estates of the elite.

**Paper Trail (Ex)**

**Ability:** A majordomo who analyzes the handwriting of a document and succeeds at a Linguistics
check against a DC equal to 20 + the writer's Bluff bonus gets a hunch about whether the document's
writer was lying or otherwise nervous. This ability doesn't work if the document was dictated to
someone who thought the contents were legitimate.

**Passive:** A majordomo is trained to uncover forgeries and trace down discrepancies in paperwork
in order to ferret out intrigue. She adds half her investigator level (minimum 1) to Linguistics and
Profession checks to spot a forgery, deal with paperwork, and notice discrepancies in paperwork.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Delegate (Ex)**

**Ability:** A majordomo gains a bonus teamwork feat.

**Alters:** Alchemy, swift alchemy

**Flavor:** In order to perform all the necessary tasks of her household, a majordomo quickly learns
how to delegate.

**At 10th Level:** She gains a fourth teamwork feat and can use the _delegate_ ability four times a
day. She can also use this ability as a **swift action**.

**At 13th Level:** She gains a fifth teamwork feat, and can use the _delegate_ ability five times a
day. She can grant up to three teamwork feats to her allies when she uses _delegate._

**At 16th Level:** She gains a sixth teamwork feat, and can use the _delegate_ ability six times a
day. In addition, she can spend 1 minute and use this ability to grant a single teamwork feat to all
of her allies until the next time she recovers uses of her _delegate_ ability. As usual for multiple
uses of the same ability, if she later uses _delegate_ to grant three teamwork feats, the two uses
don't stack to grant four bonus teamwork feats, but the longer-duration teamwork feat returns after
the three shorter-duration feats expire.

**At 19th Level:** She gains a seventh teamwork feat and can use her _delegate_ ability seven times
a day.

**At 4th Level:** She gains a second bonus teamwork feat. She can use her _delegate_ ability to
grant any bonus teamwork feat to her allies twice a day. In addition, a majordomo can use this
ability and designate a single noncombat task that could make use of the teamwork feat (for
instance, designating the [Cooperative Crafting](ref/feat/cooperative-crafting) feat and a session
of crafting). As long as the allies pursue the task uninterrupted, instead of experiencing the
benefits for a number of rounds equal to 3 + the majordomo's investigator level, her allies retain
the benefits until the task is complete, to a maximum of 8 hours. Additionally, the majordomo can
use her delegate ability as a **move action** instead of a standard action.

**At 7th Level:** She gains a third bonus feat, and can use the _delegate_ ability three times a
day. In addition, a majordomo can use it to grant two teamwork feats to her allies, instead of one
feat.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** A majordomo can't take the [alchemist discovery](ref/invtalent/alchemist-discovery)
investigator talent or any discovery that affects or involves alchemy

**Standard:** Once per day, she can grant all allies within 30 feet who can see or hear her this
teamwork feat (even if they don't meet the prerequisites) for a number of rounds equal to 3 + her
investigator level.

**Inspired Manager (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Flavor:** A majordomo knows how to make just about any work task more efficient, from cleaning a
great hall after a big party to crafting a magic item.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** All instances of trap sense

At 6th level: Her effective Intelligence score now increases by 4.

At 9th level: Her effective Intelligence score now increases by 6.

At 12th level: Her effective Intelligence score now increases by 8.

At 15th level: Her effective Intelligence score now increases by 10.

At 18th level: Her effective Intelligence score now increases by 12.

Whenever an ally begins a workday task such as Craft, Profession, magic item creation,
[spell research](ref/rule/adding-spells-to-a-wizards-spellbook), managing a kingdom, using the
[downtime](ref/rule/downtime) rules, or other similar tasks, the majordomo can spend one use of
_inspiration_ and 1 minute of planning to create a plan for that ally. If the ally chooses to follow
the majordomo's plan and attempts the task on the same day, if the task would normally take at least
4 hours, it instead takes 30 minutes less time, and if the task would normally take at least 8
hours, it instead takes 1 hour less time.

If using the [kingdom-building rules](ref/rule/kingdom-building), the majordomo counts her
Intelligence score as 2 higher when determining the bonus she provides in a kingdom leadership role.
