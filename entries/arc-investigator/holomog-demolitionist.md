Holomog is an ancient nation built on even more ancient ruins. Its renowned civil engineers develop
skills that allow them to take full advantage of their environments.

**Structural Knowledge (Ex)**

**Ability:** A Holomog demolitionist gains [Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder) as a bonus
feat, even if he does not meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Poison lore

**Structural Insight (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** He can attempt a sunder combat maneuver check against a wall no more than 5 feet
thick. If the damage from the sunder combat maneuver exceeds the wall's
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness), the Holomog demolitionist creates a 5-foot opening in the wall,
through which a Medium creature can easily pass.

**At 2nd Level:** A Holomog demolitionist gains an insight bonus equal to his investigator level on
Acrobatics checks to move through [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles).

**At 5th Level:** When attempting to damage objects and structures, he ignores the first 5 points of
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

**At 8th Level:** The Holomog demolitionist gains a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls when attempting
to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) an item.

**Replaces:** Poison resistance

**Ricochet (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** The damage increases to 4d6, and the investigator can create this effect with a
ranged weapon by targeting a wall or object within 30 feet.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Investigator talents gained at 3rd and 9th levels

**Standard:** The Holomog demolitionist can attack an adjacent wall or object with a melee weapon.
On a hit, he creates a 15-foot-cone of rock shards or splinters that deals 2d6 points of damage to
targets in the area of effect. A target can attempt a Reflex save (DC = 10 + half the Holomog
demolitionist's level + his Intelligence modifier) to reduce this damage by half.

**Studied Strike (Ex)**

**Alters:** Studied strike

**Info:** A Holomog demolitionist's _studied strike_ damage increases every 4 levels, rather than
every 2 (to 2d6 at 8th, 3d6 at 12th, 4d6 at 16th, and 5d6 at 20th level).

**Battlefield Preparation (Ex)**

**Ability:** The investigator can create 10 feet of
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) by spending a **full-round action** to analyze
and manipulate his surroundings. This terrain can be in any shape designated by the investigator,
but at least 1 square must be adjacent to his position. This is a physical alteration that remains
in place until the surface is altered again (the road is repaved, the floor repaired, etc.), and the
Holomog demolitionist ignores the effects of any difficult terrain he creates in this way.

**At 10th Level:** The Holomog demolitionist can use this ability to create clouds of dust and
rubble that create [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) rather than difficult terrain in the same
area. This concealment lasts 1d4 rounds plus 1 round for every 4 investigator levels, and the
Holomog demolitionist is affected by the concealment as normal.

**At 14th Level:** The difficult terrain the Holomog demolitionist creates provides
[cover](ref/rule/cover) for himself and his allies.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

At 10th level: He can now create 20 feet of difficult terrain.

At 14th level: He can now create 30 feet of difficult terrain.

At 18th level: He can now create 40 feet of difficult terrain.
