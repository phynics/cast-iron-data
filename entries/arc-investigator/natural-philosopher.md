**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 56\
Natural philosophers are scholars, explorers, and survivalists who feel at home in nature, seldom
longing for the comforts of the city. They eschew the fancy tools and weaponry of gentlefolk
detectives and instead employ weapons that double as tools, materials available in nature, and
methods better suited for untamed wilderness.

**Gained class skills:** sur

**Lost class skills:** perf

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A natural philosopher is proficient with simple weapons, plus the
[handaxe](ref/eq-weapon/handaxe), [kukri](ref/eq-weapon/kukri), [shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow),
[throwing axe](ref/eq-weapon/throwing-axe), and [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip). A natural philosopher is
proficient with light armor but not with shields.

**Replaces:** Weapon and armor proficiency

**Natural Philosopher's Inspiration (Ex)**

**Ability:** A natural philosopher can use _inspiration_ on Heal and Survival checks without
expending a use of _inspiration,_ provided he is trained in the skill, but cannot do so for
Linguistics or Spellcraft checks.

**Alters:** Inspiration

**Flavor:** Natural philosophers employ a skill set suited to studying the natural world.

**Special:** He can later trade an **investigator talent** for the ability to use _inspiration_ on
Linguistics and Spellcraft checks without expending a use of _inspiration,_ provided he is trained
in the skill.

**Track (Ex)**

**Passive:** A natural philosopher adds half his level (minimum 1) on Survival checks to follow
tracks.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Herbalism (Su)**

**Ability:** He gains the [infusion](ref/discovery/infusion) discovery and can use Knowledge
(nature) in place of Craft (alchemy) and Profession (herbalist) checks. He also gains a competence
bonus equal to his class level on Craft (alchemy) checks to create alchemical items, and he gains
half that bonus on [Profession (herbalist)](ref/skill/profession-any) checks to find herbs.

**Alters:** Alchemy

**Flavor:** A natural philosopher augments his knowledge of alchemy with herbal lore.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense, investigator talent gained at 3rd level
