> _**Note:** This is an investigator archetype, and has no relation to the
> [spiritualist](ref/class/spiritualist) occult class._

While most investigators look to the physical world to gain their knowledge, there are those who
seek out knowledge beyond the pale. Those who think that the dead tell no tales are quickly proven
wrong by the spiritualist. Instead of toying with chemicals and reagents to find clues, he talks
directly to the spirit world to uncover the ways and means of skullduggery and the desperate acts
committed in the heat of dark passions.

**Commune with Spirits (Sp)**

**Ability:** A spiritualist gains the ability to use
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages),
[detect secret doors](ref/spell/detect-secret-doors), and [identify](ref/spell/identify), but each
has a casting time of 1 minute, as the spiritualist needs to consult with the spirits of the dead
that dwell in his area or that are somehow attached to him in some way.

**Flavor:** Instead of relying on alchemical research to find clues, a spiritualist relies on
communion with the world beyond death.

**At 5th Level:** He can use [augury](ref/spell/augury).

**At 7th Level:** He can use [speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead) and
[locate object](ref/spell/locate-object).

**At 9th Level:** He can use [legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore).

**Info:** As he goes up in level, the spiritualist gains the ability to use other spells with the
casting time as above. Each of these spells requires one use of this ability.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Alchemy

**Special:** A spiritualist cannot take the [alchemist discovery](ref/invtalent/alchemist-discovery)
talent or any investigator talent that affects alchemy.

**Usage:** A number of times per day equal to his investigator level + his Wisdom modifier.

**Usage:** A number of times per day equal to his investigator level + his Wisdom modifier.

**Spirit Sense (Su)**

**Ability:** When a spiritualist attempts a saving throw against an ability or an effect delivered
by an incorporeal creature, he can expend one use of his _inspiration_ instead of two to augment
that saving throw. If the spiritualist has the
[combat inspiration](ref/invtalent/combat-inspiration) investigator talent, he need not spend any
_inspiration_ to augment such saving throws.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Poison lore

**Strong Life (Ex)**

**Flavor:** His dealings with the other side and knowledge of what lies beyond strengthens the
spiritualist's will.

**At 5th Level:** This bonus increases to +4.

**At 8th Level:** This bonus becomes +6.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** He gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against death effects and negative energy damage.

**Replaces:** Poison resistance

**Sixth Sense (Su)**

**Ability:** A spiritualist can spend one of his uses of the _commune with spirits_ ability to
reroll a single saving throw that he has failed. He must take the results of the reroll, even if it
is lower.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Whispering Spirits (Su)**

**Ability:** He can expend one use of his _commune with spirits_ ability in order to gain an insight
bonus to both AC and saving throws equal to his Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) for 1 minute.

**Flavor:** The spiritualist can open a conduit with the spirit world that can aid him in combat for
a short time.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Swift alchemy

**Touched by the Beyond (Su)**

**Flavor:** The spiritualist's ability to touch the beyond grants him further protection against the
dangers of death and negative energy.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** The spiritualist becomes immune to death effects, and he takes half damage from
negative energy.

**Replaces:** Poison immunity
