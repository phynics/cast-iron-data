**Superior Feint (Ex)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level swashbuckler

**Standard:** A swashbuckler with at least 1 panache point can purposefully miss a creature she
could make a melee attack against with a wielded light or one-handed piercing weapon. When she does,
the creature is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC until the start of the swashbuckler's next turn.
