At 15th level, while the swashbuckler has at least 1 panache point, she can take 10 on any
Acrobatics, Climb, Escape Artist, Fly, Ride, or Swim check, even while distracted or in immediate
danger. She can use this ability in conjunction with the [derring-do](ref/swashdeed/derring-do)
deed.
