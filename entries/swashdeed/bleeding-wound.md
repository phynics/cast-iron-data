At 11th level, when the swashbuckler hits a living creature with a light or one-handed piercing
melee weapon attack, as a free action she can spend 1 panache point to have that attack deal
additional [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage. The amount of bleed damage dealt is equal to the
swashbuckler's Dexterity modifier (minimum 1). Alternatively, the swashbuckler can spend 2 panache
points to deal 1 point of
[Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution bleed damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) instead
(swashbuckler's choice). Creatures that are immune to sneak attacks are also immune to these types
of bleed damage.
