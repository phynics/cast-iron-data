**Evasive (Ex)**

**Passive:** While a swashbuckler has at least 1 panache point, she gains the benefits of the
[evasion](ref/ability/evasion), [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge), and
[improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) rogue class features. She uses her
swashbuckler level as her rogue level for _improved uncanny dodge._

**Prereq:** 11th-level swashbuckler
