At 19th level, when a swashbuckler hits a creature with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon,
she can spend 2 panache points to stun the creature for 1 round. The creature must succeed at a
Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the swashbuckler's level + the swashbuckler's Dexterity
modifier) or be [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round. Creatures that are immune to critical hits
are also immune to this effect.
