**Stunning Stab (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a swashbuckler hits a creature with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon,
she can spend 2 panache points. The creature must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2
the swashbuckler's level + the swashbuckler's Dexterity modifier) or be [stunned](ref/misc/stunned)
for 1 round.

**Prereq:** 19th-level swashbuckler

**Special:** Creatures that are immune to critical hits are also immune to this effect.
