::: paragraph statline
The wearer of this light, short cloak gains a bonus on all saving throws, as with a _cloak of
resistance_, but the resistance bonus on Reflex saves is one greater. Furthermore, if the wearer
fails a Reflex saving throw, she can expend one use of mythic power as an immediate action to reroll
that saving throw. If the wearer fails the saving throw on the reroll, she can't use this ability
again for 24 hours.
:::
