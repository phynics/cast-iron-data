A type of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), DR/epic can be overcome only by a weapon
with an enhancement bonus of +6 or greater. Weapons with special abilities also count as epic for
the purposes of overcoming damage reduction if the total bonus value of all of their abilities
(including the enhancement bonus) is +6 or greater.
