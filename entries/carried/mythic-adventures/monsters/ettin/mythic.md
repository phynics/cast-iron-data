## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Two Brains {#two-brains}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Anytime a mythic ettin attempts a Will saving throw, it rolls twice and uses the better result.

A mythic ettin is descended from a sordid lineage of mythic hill giants, mythic stone giants, and at
least one mythic orc. Angrier, filthier, and stronger than other ettins, it is a leader of its kind,
and is better at keeping a band or colony together for months or years.
