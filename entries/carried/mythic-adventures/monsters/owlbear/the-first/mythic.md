_This owlbear seems lean and especially mean, and moves with a spookily silent grace._

Though created long ago, the very first owlbear still lives. Fecund, nomadic and cantankerous, the
First Owlbear's offspring are always owlbears, whether the mother is a bear, a giant owl, or an
owlbear. He brings his "harem" of mates with him when he hunts, driving them off when he tires of
their company or food becomes scarce.
