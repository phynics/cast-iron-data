## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Darkness Aura {#darkness-aura}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

As a free action, a mythic worg can activate its darkness aura, which reduces the light level within
30 feet of it by one step. This never reduces the light level to supernatural darkness. Multiple
worgs within range can reduce the light level multiple steps. Because a mythic worg has
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) and [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), this ability
never interferes with its own vision. It can end this ability as a free action.

A mythic worg is a creature of fear and foul darkness, usually born under an ominous new moon or by
the intervention of a deity of shadows and terror. Many serve vampires, protecting their masters
from hunters and hazardous daylight.
