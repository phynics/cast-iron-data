_You're so familiar with certain spells you can prepare them with the greatest of ease._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Spell Mastery._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You can prepare spells you have selected with Spell Mastery as a full-round action. You must spend
the appropriate amount of time preparing other spells you have not mastered. As a full-round action,
you can expend one use of mythic power to prepare all your spells that you have selected with Spell
Mastery.
:::
