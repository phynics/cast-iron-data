_Your summoning spells bring not mere ordinary beasts, but instead mythic creatures._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Augment Summoning._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Any creature you summon using a summon spell is considered mythic for the purpose of interacting
with other mythic creatures. It doesn't gain any mythic abilities or power, but is affected by
mythic spells and abilities as if it were a 1st-tier mythic creature. Additionally, if the creature
summoned has [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), its damage reduction becomes DR/epic.
:::
