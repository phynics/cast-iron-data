Your summoning spells bring not mere ordinary beasts, but instead mythic creatures.\
**Prerequisites:** Augment Summoning.\
**Benefit:** Any creature you summon using a summon spell is considered mythic for the purpose of
interacting with other mythic creatures. It doesn't gain any mythic abilities or power, but is
affected by mythic spells and abilities as if it were a 1st-tier mythic creature. Additionally, if
the creature summoned has [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), its damage reduction
becomes DR/epic.
