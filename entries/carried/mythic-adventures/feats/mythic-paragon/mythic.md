_Your mythic power is even more potent than that of most other mythic beings._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Your tier is considered 2 higher for determining the potency of mythic abilities, feats, and spells.
This doesn't grant you access to mythic abilities or greater versions of mythic spells at a lower
tier than you would normally need to be to get them, nor does it grant you additional uses of mythic
power or adjust the dice you roll for your surge.
:::
