_Your skill as a rider allows you to take cover and shoot with amazing accuracy._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Mounted Archery._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When using the Ride skill to use your mount as cover, you can still make a single ranged weapon
attack as a standard action. While your mount is moving, you can expend one use of mythic power to
negate the penalties for making ranged weapon attacks while mounted until the beginning of your next
turn.
:::
