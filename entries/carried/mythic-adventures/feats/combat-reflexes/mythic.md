_You strike viciously whenever your foe gives you an opening._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Combat Reflexes._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You can make any number of additional attacks of opportunity per round. As a swift action, you can
expend one use of mythic power to, until the start of your next turn, make attacks of opportunity
against foes you've already made attacks of opportunity against this round if they provoke attacks
of opportunity from you by moving.
:::
