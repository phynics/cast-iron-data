_The blood of your sorcerous ancestor mingles with your mythic power to great effect._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Eldritch Heritage._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You gain sorcerer bloodline powers of the bloodline tied to Eldritch Heritage as if your sorcerer
level were your character level – 2. For that bloodline's 1st-level bloodline power, use your full
character level to determine its effect; for all other bloodline powers, treat your sorcerer level
as your character level – 2.
:::
