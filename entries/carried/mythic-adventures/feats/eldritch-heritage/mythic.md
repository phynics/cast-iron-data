The blood of your sorcerous ancestor mingles with your mythic power to great effect.\
**Prerequisites:** Eldritch Heritage.\
**Benefit:** You gain sorcerer bloodline powers of the bloodline tied to Eldritch Heritage as if
your sorcerer level were your character level – 2. For that bloodline's 1st-level bloodline power,
use your full character level to determine its effect; for all other bloodline powers, treat your
sorcerer level as your character level – 2.
