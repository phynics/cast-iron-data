_Your illusions are astonishingly realistic._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Spell Focus (illusion)._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Non-mythic creatures can't identify your illusion spells with Spellcraft, including checks attempted
as part of _[arcane sight](ref/spell/arcane-sight)_, _[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic)_, or
similar effects. When mythic creatures attempt to identify your illusions, add your tier to the DC
of those Spellcraft checks. Mythic creatures informed by their allies that one of your illusions
isn't real gain only a +2 bonus on their Will saves to disbelieve. Non-mythic creatures gain no
bonus to disbelieve.
:::
