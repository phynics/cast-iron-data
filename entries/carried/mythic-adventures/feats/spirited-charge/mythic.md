_Your mounted charge knocks the wind and wits from those foolish enough to stand against you._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Spirited Charge._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When you hit an opponent with a mounted charge, that opponent must succeed at a Fortitude saving
throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Str modifier) or be staggered for a number of rounds
equal to your tier. If you use a lance with this attack, increase the saving throw DC by 2. As a
free action before you make the attack, you can expend one use of mythic power to daze your opponent
on a failed saving throw instead of staggering it.
:::
