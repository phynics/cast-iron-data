Your mounted charge knocks the wind and wits from those foolish enough to stand against you.\
**Prerequisites:** Spirited Charge.\
**Benefit:** When you hit an opponent with a mounted charge, that opponent must succeed at a
Fortitude saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Str modifier) or be staggered for a
number of rounds equal to your tier. If you use a lance with this attack, increase the saving throw
DC by 2. As a free action before you make the attack, you can expend one use of mythic power to daze
your opponent on a failed saving throw instead of staggering it.
