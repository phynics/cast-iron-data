## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Whenever you use Combat Expertise, you gain an additional +2 dodge bonus to your Armor Class. You
can expend one use of mythic power to negate the penalties on melee attack rolls and combat maneuver
checks caused by using Combat Expertise for 1 minute.
