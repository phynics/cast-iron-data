You can dart out of the way of attacks with skill and defiance.\
**Prerequisites:** Combat Expertise.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use Combat Expertise, you gain an additional +2 dodge bonus to your Armor
Class. You can expend one use of mythic power to negate the penalties on melee attack rolls and
combat maneuver checks caused by using Combat Expertise for 1 minute.
