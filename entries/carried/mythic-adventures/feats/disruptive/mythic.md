_You're a master at disrupting nearby spellcasters._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Disruptive._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The DC to cast spells defensively within your threatened area increases by half your tier. This
stacks with the increase granted by Disruptive. In addition, even if you can't make attacks of
opportunity, non-mythic spellcasters are still wary of you and continue to suffer from the effects
of Disruptive.
:::
