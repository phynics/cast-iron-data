_Your staff is valuable for attack and defensive balance._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Tripping Staff._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You add half your tier to CMD against trip maneuvers and on combat maneuver checks to trip with a
quarterstaff. When you're wielding a quarterstaff and an opponent you threaten attempts to trip you
and fails, it provokes an attack of opportunity from you. You must use your staff for this attack of
opportunity.
:::
