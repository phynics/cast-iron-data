_You can retrieve any item quickly, not just weapons._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Quick Draw._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You can use Quick Draw to draw items of any kind, not just weapons, provided they are stored or
concealed on your person. As a move action, you can expend one use of mythic power to retrieve up to
two hidden items. You must have two hands free to do so.
:::
