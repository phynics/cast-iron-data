_Your attacks create a rhythmic barrage that doesn't sacrifice precision for force._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Furious Focus._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When you are using Furious Focus, you don't take Power Attack's penalty on attack rolls that are
made as attacks of opportunity. As a free action, you can expend one use of mythic power to negate
Power Attack's penalty on all melee attacks you make for 1 round while using this feat.
:::
