_The power of your faith harms all creatures that follow the alignment you abhor._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Alignment Channel._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Your channeled energy affects any creatures with the alignment chosen when you took Alignment
Channel (not just outsiders, and not just those with the alignment subtype), but grants only half
healing or deals only half damage to these targets. Alternatively, you can expend two uses of mythic
power when using Alignment Channel to affect all targets of the chosen alignment as if they had that
alignment subtype.
:::
