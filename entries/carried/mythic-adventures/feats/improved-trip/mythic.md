_You're expert at knocking your foes down._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Improved Trip._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a bonus equal to half your tier on checks to trip, and to your CMD when an opponent tries
to trip you. These bonuses stack with those granted by Improved Trip.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Furthermore, you can make an attack of opportunity against any creature that attempts to trip you,
unless it also has this feat.
:::
