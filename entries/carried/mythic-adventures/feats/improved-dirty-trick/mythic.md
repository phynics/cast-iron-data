_You're a paragon of down-and-dirty fighting._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Improved Dirty Trick._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a bonus equal to half your tier on checks to attempt a dirty trick, and to your CMD when an
opponent attempts to perform a dirty trick on you. These bonuses stack with those granted by
Improved Dirty Trick.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Furthermore, you can make an attack of opportunity against any creature that attempts a dirty trick
maneuver against you, unless it also has this feat.
:::
