::: statblock
Type: Mythic
:::

_Your blood burns with savage fury, giving you bestial powers and abilities._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Aspect of the Beast._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Your bestial nature manifests itself in one of the following ways. You choose the manifestation when
you choose the feat, and you can't change it.
:::

### Mythic Senses {#mythic-senses}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

::: paragraph indented
If you have normal vision, you gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 30 feet. If you have
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), you gain darkvision 60 feet. If you have darkvision,
the range of your darkvision increases by 30 feet plus 10 feet per tier.
:::

### Mythic Claws {#mythic-claws}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

::: paragraph indented
You grow a pair of powerful claws. These claws are primary attacks that deal 1d4 points of damage
(1d3 if you're Small). If you already have claws, the damage they deal increases by one die type
(d4s become d6s, d3s become d4s, and so on). You can expend one use of mythic power when you hit
with both claws during the same round to rend with those claws, dealing extra damage equal to one
claw's damage plus 1-1/2 times your Strength bonus.
:::

### Mythic Leap {#mythic-leap}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

::: paragraph indented
You can make a running jump without needing to run 10 feet beforehand. In addition, you gain a +5
bonus on your Acrobatics checks to determine the success of that jump and the distance covered. If
you already have the predator's leap manifestation of Aspect of the Beast, the bonus on your
Acrobatics checks increases to +10.
:::

### Mythic Instinct {#mythic-instinct}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

::: paragraph indented
You gain a +2 bonus on initiative checks and a +2 bonus on Survival checks. These bonuses stack with
those gained from the wild instinct manifestation of Aspect of the Beast.
:::
