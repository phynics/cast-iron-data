You know just how to strike to bypass even the most heavily armored foe's defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** Penetrating Strike.\
**Benefit:** When using Penetrating Strike or Greater Penetrating Strike, you can ignore an
additional point of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) for every 3 tiers you posses.
Additionally, the effects apply to damage reduction without a type (such as DR 10/—).
