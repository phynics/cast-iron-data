::: statblock
Type: Mythic
:::

_The power of your divine energy can not only cause undead to flee but also destroy them where they
stand._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Turn Undead._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When you use Turn Undead, the range increases by 10 feet per tier, and non-mythic undead take a
penalty on their saving throws equal to your tier.
:::

::: paragraph statline
You can expend one use of mythic power when using Turn Undead to destroy undead. Instead of fleeing,
undead that fail their saves might be destroyed. You destroy 2d4 Hit Dice worth of undead per tier.
Undead with the fewest HD are affected first; among undead with equal HD, those that are closest to
the burst's point of origin are affected first. No undead of 9 or more HD can be affected, and HD
that aren't sufficient to affect an undead are wasted.
:::
