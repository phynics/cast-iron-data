_You apply metamagic to your spells with greater ease than do other spontaneous casters._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Spontaneous Metafocus._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You can change which spell your Spontaneous Metafocus feat applies to each morning when you restore
your expended spell slots. The spell it applies to can be of any level, but it must be a legal
selection for the Spontaneous Metafocus feat.
:::

## Special {#special}

::: paragraph statline
You can gain this feat multiple times. The effects do not stack. Each time you take this feat, it
applies to a different non-mythic Spontaneous Metafocus feat.
:::
