_Your control of the undead is nearly absolute._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Command Undead._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When you command undead, the DC of the Will save increases by half your tier, and intelligent undead
get no additional saves beyond their first to resist your commands. Any attempts by non-mythic
creatures to control undead you command automatically fail, and you gain a +4 bonus on Charisma
checks to prevent mythic creatures from stealing control of your undead.
:::
