Your precise counterattacks degrade your foes' advantages in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Strike Back.\
**Benefit:** When you make a melee attack as a readied action against a foe outside your melee
reach, you can take a 5-foot step toward that foe. If you expend one use of mythic power, you can
move your speed toward the foe instead. Neither of these counts toward the movement made during your
turn.
