_Your precise counterattacks degrade your foes' advantages in combat._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Strike Back._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When you make a melee attack as a readied action against a foe outside your melee reach, you can
take a 5-foot step toward that foe. If you expend one use of mythic power, you can move your speed
toward the foe instead. Neither of these counts toward the movement made during your turn.
:::
