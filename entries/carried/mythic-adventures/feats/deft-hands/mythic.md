## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The bonus on Disable Device and Sleight of Hand skill checks from Deft Hands increases by 2. In
addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Disable Device or Sleight of Hand check
as if you had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
