_Persuasive._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate skill checks granted by Persuasive increases by 2. In
addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Diplomacy or Intimidate check as if you
had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
