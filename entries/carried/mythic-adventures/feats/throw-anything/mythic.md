_You're so skilled at throwing weapons that it hardly matters what you throw._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Throw Anything._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls made using an improvised thrown weapon or
splash weapon. This bonus stacks with the bonus from the non-mythic Throw Anything feat. If you miss
with a thrown splash weapon, it always lands adjacent to its target regardless of how many range
increments it was thrown.
:::
