_You can quickly deter others with your martial mastery._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Dazzling Display._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
While wielding the weapon with which you have Weapon Focus, you can use Dazzling Display as a
standard action with a –5 penalty on your Intimidate check, as a move action with a –10 penalty, or
as a swift action with a –20 penalty. You can expend one use of mythic power when you make a
Dazzling Display to make those affected by the display frightened rather than shaken.
:::
