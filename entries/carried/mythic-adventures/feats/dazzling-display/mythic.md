You can quickly deter others with your martial mastery.\
**Prerequisites:** Dazzling Display.\
**Benefit:** While wielding the weapon with which you have Weapon Focus, you can use Dazzling
Display as a standard action with a –5 penalty on your Intimidate check, as a move action with a –10
penalty, or as a swift action with a –20 penalty. You can expend one use of mythic power when you
make a Dazzling Display to make those affected by the display frightened rather than shaken.
