_Your reaching strikes aid your defenses against those you hit._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Lunge._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Whenever you use Lunge and hit a creature with the melee attack, you no longer take a –2 penalty to
AC against that creature. You can expend one use of mythic power when you use Lunge to negate the –2
penalty to AC whether you hit or miss, and gain a +2 bonus on attacks of opportunity you make while
Lunge is in effect.
:::
