_Your barrage of unarmed attacks builds to a single, staggering strike._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Medusa's Wrath._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You may forgo the two additional unarmed strikes of Medusa's Wrath to instead make a single unarmed
strike at your highest base attack bonus. If you successfully hit your opponent, it must succeed at
a Fortitude saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be staggered
for 1 round. You can expend one use of mythic power when you stagger a target with Medusa's Wrath to
extend the duration of the staggered condition by a number of rounds equal to half your tier.
:::
