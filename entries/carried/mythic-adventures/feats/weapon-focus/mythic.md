Type: Mythic

_You're a master of one type of weapon._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Weapon Focus._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Double the bonuses on your attack rolls from Weapon Focus and Greater Weapon Focus. As a swift
action, you can expend one use of mythic power to gain a bonus on attack rolls with your selected
weapon equal to half your tier until the end of your turn.
:::

## Special {#special}

::: paragraph statline
If you have taken non-mythic Weapon Focus multiple times, you must choose which of those feats this
enhances. You can take this feat multiple times. The effects do not stack. Each time you take this
feat, it applies to a different non-mythic Weapon Focus feat you have.
:::
