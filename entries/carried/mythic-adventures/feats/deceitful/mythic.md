## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The bonus on Bluff and Disguise skill checks from Deceitful increases by 2. In addition, you can
expend one use of mythic power to treat a Bluff or Disguise check as if you had rolled a natural 20.
You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
