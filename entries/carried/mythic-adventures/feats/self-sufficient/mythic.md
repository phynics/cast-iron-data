## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The bonus on Heal and Survival skill checks from Self-Sufficient increases by 2. In addition, you
can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Heal or Survival check as if you had rolled a
natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
