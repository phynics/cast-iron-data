_Your kills are truly frightening to behold._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Dreadful Carnage._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When you use Dreadful Carnage, you affect non-mythic enemies within 60 feet in addition to all
enemies within 30 feet. You can expend one use of mythic power before making the Intimidate check to
cause enemies demoralized by Dreadful Carnage to become frightened instead of shaken, for the same
duration as the normal shaken effect. While frightened by this ability, an enemy takes a penalty
equal to your tier on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and ability checks.
:::
