_You put your foes right where you want them._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Improved Reposition._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a bonus equal to half your tier on checks to attempt a reposition combat maneuver, and to
your CMD when an opponent attempts to perform a reposition combat maneuver against you. These
bonuses stack with those granted by Improved Reposition.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Furthermore, you can make an attack of opportunity against any creature that attempts to reposition
you, unless it also has this feat.
:::
