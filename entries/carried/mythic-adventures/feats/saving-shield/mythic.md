_Shielding a comrade from harm gives you a chance to strike back._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Saving Shield._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The shield bonus you grant an ally with Saving Shield increases to +3. If you use Saving Shield to
successfully negate an attack against an adjacent ally, you can immediately make an attack of
opportunity against the attacker so long as it is within your melee reach.
:::
