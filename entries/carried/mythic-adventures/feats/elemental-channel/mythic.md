_Your power over elemental beings is nearly absolute._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Elemental Channel._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Your Elemental Channel can affect any elemental subtype, not just the one you chose when you took
Elemental Channel. Each time you use Elemental Channel, you must pick one elemental subtype before
using the feat. You can expend one use of mythic power when you use Elemental Channel to choose a
second elemental subtype to affect.
:::
