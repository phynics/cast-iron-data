_Your witch knife is as much an extension of your body as it is an extension of your power._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Witch Knife._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You can use your witch knife as an additional focus component for all your witch spells, not just
your patron spells, adding 1 to their DC. This bonus stacks with the bonus to patron spells granted
by Witch Knife, granting your patron spells a total +2 bonus to their DC. Additionally, the hand
holding the witch knife is still considered free for the purpose of casting spells and delivering
touch attacks.
:::
