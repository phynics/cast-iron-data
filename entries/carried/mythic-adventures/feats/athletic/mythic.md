_You excel far beyond others at acts of physical prowess._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Athletic._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The bonus on Climb and Swim skill checks from Athletic increases by 2. In addition, you can expend
one use of mythic power to treat a Climb or Swim check as if you had rolled a natural 20. You must
decide to use this ability before making the roll.
:::
