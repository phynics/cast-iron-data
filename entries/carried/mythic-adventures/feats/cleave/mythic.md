_You can cleave any foe within your reach._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Cleave._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Whenever you use Cleave or Great Cleave, one of your attacks can be made against a foe that is
within your reach, but not adjacent to the foe you attacked. You can expend one use of mythic power
when you use Cleave or Great Cleave to continue making attacks against foes within your reach,
regardless of their position, as long as you continue to hit each foe. You can't use this ability to
attack a foe more than once per round.
:::
