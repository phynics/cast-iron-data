You can cleave any foe within your reach.\
**Prerequisites:** Cleave.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use Cleave or Great Cleave, one of your attacks can be made against a foe
that is within your reach, but not adjacent to the foe you attacked. You can expend one use of
mythic power when you use Cleave or Great Cleave to continue making attacks against foes within your
reach, regardless of their position, as long as you continue to hit each foe. You can't use this
ability to attack a foe more than once per round.
