_The damage you deal with your chosen weapon is truly awesome to behold._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Weapon Specialization._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When using your chosen weapon, you gain a bonus equal to half your tier on damage rolls. This stacks
with the bonus from Weapon Specialization and Greater Weapon Specialization.
:::

## Special {#special}

::: paragraph statline
If you have taken non-mythic Weapon Specialization multiple times, you must choose which of those
feats this enhances. You can take this feat multiple times. The effects do not stack. Each time you
take this feat, it applies to a different non-mythic Weapon Specialization feat you have.
:::
