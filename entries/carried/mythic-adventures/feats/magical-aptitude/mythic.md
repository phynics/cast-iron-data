## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Magical Aptitude._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
The bonus on Spellcraft and Use Magic Device skill checks from Magical Aptitude increases by 2. In
addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Spellcraft or Use Magic Device check as
if you had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
:::
