_Your unarmed strikes are more powerful than others._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Improved Unarmed Strike._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You can add half your tier to your damage with unarmed strikes. As a swift action, you can expend
one use of mythic power to overcome the [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of objects with your unarmed
strikes. This effect last for a number of rounds equal to your tier. If an object's hardness is
greater than 15, you don't ignore any of its hardness.
:::
