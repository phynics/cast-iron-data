_Your precognitive powers aid you further._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Prophetic Visionary._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
You can use Prophetic Visionary's _[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ ability at will, spending 10 minutes
in a deep trance each time. Your chance of a successful _[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ increases by 1%
per tier. A successful _[augury](ref/spell/augury) _made by using this feat also grants you the
benefits of _[guidance](ref/spell/guidance)_, but the bonus is equal to your tier and is an insight
bonus instead of a competence bonus.
:::
