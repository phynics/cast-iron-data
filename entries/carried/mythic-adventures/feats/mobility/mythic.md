_You have perfected your ability to weave through the battlefield, using your momentum to propel
yourself far._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Mobility._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Whenever you use Mobility, you gain a +6 dodge bonus to AC instead of the normal +4. In addition,
once per round when an attack of opportunity provoked by your movement misses you, you may move 5
feet as a free action. This movement doesn't count against your total overall movement for the
round, but it does provoke attacks of opportunity.
:::
