## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Monastic Legacy._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
Add half the levels you have in classes other than monk to your monk level to determine your
effective monk level for your AC bonus. As a free action, you can expend one use of mythic power to
add half your tier to your effective monk level to determine your base unarmed strike damage and AC
bonus until the start of your next turn.
:::
