_You knock your opponents down from their mounts with frightening concussive force._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Unseat._

## Benefits {#benefits}

::: paragraph statline
When you successfully bull rush an opponent off his mount with Unseat, your opponent takes 1d6
points of falling damage per 2 tiers you possess. Items or abilities that reduce falling damage,
such as a successful Acrobatics check, can reduce the damage dealt by the impact.
:::
