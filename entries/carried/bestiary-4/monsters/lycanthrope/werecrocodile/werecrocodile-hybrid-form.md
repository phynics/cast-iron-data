## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Death Roll {#death-roll}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

When grappling a foe of its size or smaller, a werecrocodile can perform a death roll upon making a
successful grapple check. As it clings to its foe, the werecrocodile deals its bite damage, knocks
the creature prone, and maintains the grapple.

### Sprint {#sprint}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Once per minute, a werecrocodile can increase its land speed to 60 feet for 1 round.

Werecrocodiles merge the ruthless determination of a crocodile with the intelligence and
adaptability of a humanoid. This fusion creates a maliciously cunning creature with no doubt of its
own martial superiority.
