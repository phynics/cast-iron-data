## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Master Grappler {#master-grappler}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A magaav can wield a weapon and still attempt grapple checks. While not wielding a weapon, a magaav
gains a +4 bonus on grapple checks.

### Noxious Breath {#noxious-breath}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Three times per day, as a standard action a magaav can exhale a breath that reeks of pure corruption
upon a creature within 5 feet. The target must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save or be sickened for
1d4 rounds. Creatures that successfully save cannot be affected by the same magaav's noxious breath
for 24 hours. This is a poison effect. The save DC is Constitution-based.

### Shared Senses {#shared-senses}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

All magaavs within 100 feet of one another share the same senses. Thus, if one individual perceives
something (for example, with a successful Perception check), all others within range are immediately
aware of it. Senses are instantly relayed from one magaav to the next, allowing for the senses of a
single devil to potentially spread through and inform an entire swarm instantly. It is still
possible for a magaav to be flat-footed for other reasons even if other magaavs nearby are not.

Hunters of souls, host devils retrieve Hell's most elusive property. Whether souls that have long
evaded capture upon the plains of Avernus, damned beings who have somehow managed to escape Hell, or
creatures that have reneged upon infernal contracts, vast flocks of these winged fiends fly from the
Pit to recover their prey. Rarely seen alone, host devils travel in great swarms that often number
in the thousands. These four-winged mockeries of the angelic form swarm in enormous columns, moving
in tandem as though they were one colossal, infernal beast controlled by a single brain.

Magaavs stand 5-1/2 feet tall and weigh 150 pounds, with wingspans reaching 10 feet across. Their
fetid breath draws flies that swarm over their bodies.
