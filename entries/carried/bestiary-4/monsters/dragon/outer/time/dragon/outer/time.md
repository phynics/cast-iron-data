## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Immortal {#immortal}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Time dragons age, but don't die from old age.

### Read the Threads {#read-the-threads}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A time dragon gains a racial bonus on initiative checks equal to its age category. In addition, a
time dragon is immune to the staggered condition.

### Alien Presence {#alien-presence}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A creature that fails its saving throw against a time dragon's alien presence is staggered for 5d6
rounds (or stunned if it has 4 Hit Dice or fewer).

### Shifting Breath {#shifting-breath}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Instead of a cone of electricity, an old or older time dragon can use its breath weapon to displace
creatures in time. Each creature in the cone must succeed at a Will save or be shifted forward in
time a number of rounds equal to 1/2 the dragon's age category (DC equal to breath weapon). For an
affected creature, it's as if no time passed. Creatures that succeed at their saves are staggered
for 1 round.

### Second Chance {#second-chance}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Three times per day as an immediate action, an ancient or older time dragon can force a creature
(including itself) to reroll any d20 roll. The target must use the result of the second roll.

### Time Travel {#time-travel}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Up to three times in its life, a great wyrm time dragon can travel to any point in time, taking with
it a number of willing creatures equal to its Charisma modifier.
