_A hairy, stout animal with opposable thumbs, this creature has a pronounced muzzle and bright red
buttocks._

Baboons are known for their aggressive nature and distinctive build, including a canine-like maw
filled with sharp teeth, strong jaw muscles, a short tail, and prominent calluses on their brightly
colored and protruding buttocks. Though they are primarily vegetarians, baboons are known to eat
fish, insects, shellfish, and other small creatures. Ferociously territorial, baboons are quick to
rise and defend their homes from any sort of intruders.

A baboon is 3 feet tall and weighs 70 pounds.
