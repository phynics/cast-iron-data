## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Coordinated Swarm {#coordinated-swarm}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A monkey swarm coordinates its attacks more than a typical swarm, and deals swarm damage one step
higher than a swarm of its HD would normally cause.

Monkeys sometimes travel in huge colonies of hundreds of individuals. In such quantities, these
primates can become quite dangerous, capable of overwhelming many foes by their sheer numbers.

Unlike most swarms, monkey swarms work well together. A swarm of monkeys does not possess a true
hive mind, but it is capable of working in tandem with other swarms to make basic tactical decisions
in combat.
