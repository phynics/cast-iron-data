_This armored creature has a bony, turtle-like shell from which protrude short limbs, a blunt head,
and a short spiky tail._

The gylptodon is often hunted for the value of its outer armor, made up of bony plates in turn
composed of many smaller, knobby “scales.” The creature is quite capable of defending itself with
swipes from its deadly claws, as its ill-tempered nature often urges it to do.
