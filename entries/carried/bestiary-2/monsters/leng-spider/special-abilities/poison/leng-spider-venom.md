::: statblock
Type: poison
Contracted: injury
Save: Fortitude DC 25.
Frequency: 1/round for 6 rounds
Effect: 1d4 Constitution plus confusion for 1 round
Cure: 2 consecutive saves
:::

A Leng spider's venom causes flesh to blister and rot away and the mind to experience vivid and
horrific hallucinations—these visions cause the poisoned creature to react in an unpredictable
manner, as if confused. The hallucination element of this poison is mind-affecting.
