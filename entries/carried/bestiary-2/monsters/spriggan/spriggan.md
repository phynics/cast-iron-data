## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Size Alteration {#size-alteration}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At will as a standard action, a spriggan can change his size between Small and Large. Weapons,
armor, and other objects on the spriggan's person grow proportionally when he changes size (objects
revert to normal size 1 round after a spriggan releases them). When a spriggan becomes Large, his
speed increases to 30 feet, he gains +12 Strength, –2 Dexterity, and +6 Constitution, and he takes a
–2 size penalty to his AC. While Large, a spriggan cannot use his sneak attack or his racial
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (although if he possesses either from class
levels or templates, he retains their use in both sizes).

### Spriggan Magic {#spriggan-magic}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A spriggan gains a +1 racial bonus on concentration checks and to save DCs for all of its racial
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

### Spriggan Skills {#spriggan-skills}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Climb, Disable Device, Perception, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth are class skills for spriggans.
