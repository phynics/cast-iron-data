Despite boggards' generalized training, natural divergences in ability mean adventurers traveling
through marshes and neighboring environments might encounter groups of boggards with wildly
different compositions.

## Exiles {#exiles}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 7
:::

Those boggards the tribe doesn't accept are devoured by their betters or cast out. Frequently,
exiles are those with strange powers or markings whom their superstitious kin stubbornly refused to
allow in the village, but considered too powerful to challenge directly.
