A common practice among depraved ogre hunters who catch humanoid prey is to sever the victim's hand,
foot, or entire limb, cauterize the wound, and then force the creature to watch while the ogre or
his hungry wolf companion devours the amputated body part; when the captive has no limbs left, it's
kicked around like a toy and finally roasted on a spit.
