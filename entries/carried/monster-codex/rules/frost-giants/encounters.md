## War Band {#war-band}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 17
:::

This war band could be called together by a local jarl to solve a specific, difficult problem—such
as evicting monsters from a disused fortress, or stopping a party of adventurers who have been
disrupting the tribe's attempts to terrorize nearby settlements. The frost giants' usual forces are
bolstered by svathurims recruited to strike thunderous fear into foes.
