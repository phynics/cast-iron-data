Ratfolk travel far and wide in search of new discoveries and economic opportunities. As a result,
adventurers might encounter them in a wide variety of circumstances and group compositions.

## Ratfolk Advance Scouts {#ratfolk-advance-scouts}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 6
:::

Traveling groups of ratfolk often send a small team of scouts several miles ahead to survey the lay
of the land and check for dangerous creatures before risking the entire caravan.

## Ratfolk Caravan {#ratfolk-caravan}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 9
:::

Ratfolk tend to form large caravans when traveling from one trading hub to another. A typical
caravan consists of a dozen ratfolk tradesmen driving wagons drawn by riding rats, led and guarded
by experienced explorers.

## Colonists {#colonists}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 10
:::

usually leads this band, bringing with him any useful texts and knowledge.
