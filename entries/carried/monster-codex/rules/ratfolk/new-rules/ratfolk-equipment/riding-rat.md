Horses, ponies, and other draft animals are ill-suited to living underground for long stretches, so
the ratfolk bred a particularly strong type of dire rat to assist in pulling their wagons and
serving as mounts for caravan scouts. As rodents, riding rats respond favorably to ratfolk's
commands, and the animals' hardiness and omnivorous diet make them very easy to care for even on
long journeys through inhospitable terrain. Use the statistics for a dire rat with the giant simple
template. A ratfolk with the animal companion, mount, or similar class feature can select a riding
rat as her mount.
