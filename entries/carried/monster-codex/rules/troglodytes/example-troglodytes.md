## Troglodyte Sneaks {#troglodyte-sneaks}

Using their natural stealth or magical invisibility, these troglodytes sneak up on enemies, then
either slip away to report what they've seen to the tribe or attack enemies that look weak.

## Troglodyte Holy Ones {#troglodyte-holy-ones}

Troglodyte divine spellcasters act as spiritual advisors for their tribes. Troglodyte clerics
usually worship demon lords, particularly those associated with caverns and reptiles.

## Troglodyte Chieftain {#troglodyte-chieftain}

One chieftain usually leads each troglodyte tribe, ruling over the lesser members of the tribe with
a strong and vengeful hand.

## Troglodyte Warren Guard {#troglodyte-warren-guard}

The largest and most aggressive troglodytes are chosen to supervise the tribes' egg clutches and
keep them safe from intruders.

## Troglodyte Tyrant {#troglodyte-tyrant}

Troglodyte tyrants are sovereigns of advanced troglodyte societies found in the lowest depths of the
world's subterranean wilderness.

## Slaugrak {#slaugrak}

This hulking reptilian humanoid's torso bristles with twitching vestigial limbs, milk-white eyes,
and drooling half-formed mouths. The creature's oversized maw is filled with curved teeth
reminiscent of sickle blades.
