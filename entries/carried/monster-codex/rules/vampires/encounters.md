Encounters with vampires can be as varied as a wandering group of hunting vampires or an established
family that has been in residence for decades.

## False Family {#false-family}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 5
:::

Many times, a true vampire sends mortal and undead minions to scout out an area, with a spawn
pretending to be full vampire. If the group prospers, the vampire might visit. If the group is
destroyed, the vampire knows it should proceed with caution. A vampire might also use this sort of
team to claim a newly risen spawn from its coffin or crypt.

## Celebration {#celebration}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 8
:::

When a vampire seducer throws a party, it is the talk of the town, and everyone hopes to be invited.
The exotic pleasures are unforgettable. The vampire's herd works the party, ushering individuals
away from the crowd to spend a few quiet moments with the vampire. Those partygoers return to the
party feeling light headed, while the vampire feeds well that night.

## Hunger {#hunger}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 12
:::

When several vampires are on the prowl for blood, they collectively call themselves a hunger of
vampires. This group constantly tries to creatively one up each other in the ways they seduce and
kill their victims. At least one vampire brings along a butler to ensure any other desires are met.

## Family {#family}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 16
:::

A mockery of a mortal family, a vampire "family" consists of a powerful vampire lord along with
several lesser vampires, spawn, and mortal minions. This family, all of whom are either dominated or
enslaved by the vampire lord, follow their leader's directives without fail.
