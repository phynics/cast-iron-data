The following section details new orc equipment, feats favored by orcs, and new magic items used by
orcs.

## Orc Feats {#orc-feats}

The following feats are common among orcs, but they can be taken by other races as well.

### Horn Rider {#horn-rider}

::: statblock
Abbrev: Combat
:::

You can defend yourself using the horn or tusk of a creature you're lashed to.

#### Prerequisite {#prerequisite}

::: paragraph statline
Base attack bonus +1.
:::

#### Benefit {#benefit}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a +1 shield bonus to AC when lashed into a horn harness.
:::

#### Special {#special}

::: paragraph statline
A creature with this feat can take the Mounted Archery feat without possessing 1 rank in the Ride
skill or the Mounted Combat feat.
:::

### Horn Rider's Charge {#horn-riders-charge}

::: statblock
Abbrev: Combat
:::

When the animal you're lashed to charges, you use its momentum in your own attack.

#### Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

::: paragraph statline
Horn Rider, base attack bonus +1.
:::

#### Benefit {#benefit-2}

::: paragraph statline
Whenever you're lashed into a horn harness and you ready an action to make a melee attack against
the target of a charge made by the creature you're lashed to, you gain the attack bonus for charging
and your attack deals double damage (or triple damage with a lance). This benefit does not stack
with the benefit of Spirited Charge.
:::

### Mutual Hatred {#mutual-hatred}

::: statblock
Abbrev: Combat
:::

Others hate you, and their hatred feeds your own.

#### Benefit {#benefit-3}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls against creatures whose hatred racial trait or
favored enemy class feature applies to you. For instance, an orc would gain this bonus against all
dwarves and against rangers who chose humanoid (orc) as a favored enemy.
:::

### Scarred Legion {#scarred-legion}

::: statblock
Abbrev: Teamwork
:::

Your ritual scars make you more fearsome, and allies with similar scars grow bolder when they're
near you.

#### Benefit {#benefit-4}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a +2 bonus on Intimidate checks. You also gain a +2 morale bonus on Will saving throws
while adjacent to an ally with this feat. When you charge, if you started the charge adjacent to an
ally with this feat, you retain the bonus on Will saves until the start of your next turn.
:::
