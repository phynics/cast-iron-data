## Inner Circle {#inner-circle}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 15
:::

core of a bugbear clan or another monstrous infestation. While these individuals likely squabble
among themselves, and prefer to let their underlings do all the work, when faced with an opponent
that actually threatens their rule, they operate as a team to viciously put down the interloper.
