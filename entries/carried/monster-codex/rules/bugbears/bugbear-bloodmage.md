The few bugbears who manifest a glimmer of arcane magical ability rarely pursue their gift. Such
things get in the way of what is important: stalking, terrifying, and murdering prey. But those who
pursue their gifts—and live to reach their full potential—become horrifying vessels of bloody doom.
