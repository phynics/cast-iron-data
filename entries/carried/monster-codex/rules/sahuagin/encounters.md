Despite their penchant for brutal competitions, sahuagin are communal creatures that live and hunt
in groups for their mutual good; these groups range from small raiding parties to massive underwater
cities. Here are some example encounters using the various types of sahuagin presented in this
section.

## Boarding Party {#boarding-party}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 5
:::

In the dead of night, sahuagin clamber up the sides of ships to spread fear and bring back a bounty
for their lord.

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 11

they pursue might be a dangerous beast as well, and could be encountered before the hunting party is
