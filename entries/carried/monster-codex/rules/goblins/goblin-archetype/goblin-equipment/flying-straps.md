::: paragraph statline
These crude straps are used to secure a goblin or other small creature to the underside of a dire
bat or giant vulture . These straps count as an exotic riding saddle. If the mount fails a Fly
check, there's a 50% chance the straps break and the rider falls. Masterwork flying straps count as
an exotic military saddle, and don't have a chance to break if the mount fails a Fly check.
:::
