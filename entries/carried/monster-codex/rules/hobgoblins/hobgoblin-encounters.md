## Elite Strike Force {#elite-strike-force}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 11
:::

Typically sent on the most sensitive missions, elite forces of hobgoblin soldiers can
single-handedly turn the tides of deadlocked conflicts. Their military precision is renowned, and
their ability to succeed at assassinations, sabotage, and reconnaissance deep behind enemy lines
makes them as important as entire battalions of foot soldiers.
