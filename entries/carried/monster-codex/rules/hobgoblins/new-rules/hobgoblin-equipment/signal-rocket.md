::: paragraph statline
You can use this rocket can deliver simple auditory and visual signals to an army from a distance.
When used during mass combat, a signal rocket allows a commander to change strategy track with a
higher degree of success than normal. A commander can launch a signal rocket while changing a
strategy track by more than 1 step to grant his army a +5 circumstance bonus on its Morale check to
implement his orders. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 15 Craft (alchemy) check.
:::
