## Hobgoblin Battle Priest {#hobgoblin-battle-priest}

Serving as spiritual leaders and sources of inspiration to the corps of a hobgoblin army, battle
priests factor prominently in the hobgoblin arts of war. They tend to devote less effort toward
healing and curing their allies' ailments than they do to imparting powerful effects to soldiers in
preparation for battle.

::: entry-link
target = "ref/monster/hobgoblin-hobgoblin-battle-priest"
title = "Hobgoblin Battle Priest"
:::

## Hobgoblin Paragon {#hobgoblin-paragon}

Though hobgoblins are innately drawn to combat and conflict, not all find a place as faceless
members among the ranks of a military unit. Some seek to perfect their own bodies to become a
one-person squadron, the pinnacle of martial mastery, and a temple to perfected technique and
dedicated skill. These hobgoblins are austere, cruel, and completely confident in their ability.

::: entry-link

title = "Hobgoblin Paragon"
:::
