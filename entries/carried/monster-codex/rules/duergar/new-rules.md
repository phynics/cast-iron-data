When the duergar made deals with their dark god to turn the tide against their hated enemies, they
were fundamentally changed, both physically and psychologically. After a period of adjustment to
their new circumstances, their artisans developed innovations to better cope with their harsh
environment, and their spellcasters mastered magical secrets gifted to them by the dark powers that
granted them succor during the race's time of direst need.

## Alternate Racial Traits {#alternate-racial-traits}

Since the calamity that drove the duergar from their original homes under the earth, the duergar
have faced many adversaries. While the duergar may be fiercely traditional and as inflexible as
adamantine, their trials, the strange rites of their priests, and the bizarre energies of the remote
chambers buried beneath the world have changed them in essential ways. Some duergar have the
following racial traits.

### Ironskinned {#ironskinned}

The soul of the earth infuses some duergar, giving them the ability to harden their skin. Duergar
with this racial trait can use _[ironskin](ref/spell/ironskin)_ once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using their character level as their caster
level. This racial trait replaces the _[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_ spell-like
ability.

### Twilight-Touched {#twilight-touched}

While most duergar have the power to become invisible, a few are instead able to extinguish the
light around them. Duergar with this racial trait can use
_[dust of twilight](ref/spell/dust-of-twilight)_ once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using their character level as their caster
level. This racial trait replaces the _invisibility_ spell-like ability.

## Duergar Feats {#duergar-feats}

Duergar have access to the following feats.

### Gray Dwarf Magic {#gray-dwarf-magic}

You gain a new [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) from the list of duergar racial
traits.

#### Prerequisite {#prerequisite}

::: paragraph statline
Duergar.
:::

#### Benefit {#benefit}

::: paragraph statline
Choose one [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) that is usable once per day and is
granted by a duergar racial trait you don't have. You can use that spell-like ability once per day,
with a caster level equal to your character level.
:::

#### Special {#special}

::: paragraph statline
You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, you must select a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) that's usable once per day from a different
duergar racial trait.
:::

### Innate Flexibility {#innate-flexibility}

Your innate magic is flexible.

#### Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

::: paragraph statline
Duergar, two racial [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) usable once per day.
:::

#### Benefit {#benefit-2}

::: paragraph statline
You gain a number of additional uses of your once per day racial
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) equal to the number of such abilities you have.
You can expend these additional uses in any combination. Normal duergar spell-like abilities and
those granted by duergar racial traits both count for the purpose of this feat.
:::

::: paragraph statline
For example, a normal duergar has two once per day
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (_[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_
and _invisibility_ ), so taking this feat would grant her two additional uses of her once per day
spell-like abilities; each day she can use either two additional uses of
_[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_, two additional uses of _invisibility_, or one
additional use of each.
:::

#### Special {#special-2}

::: paragraph statline
You can take this feat multiple times. Its effects stack.
:::

### Tough as Iron {#tough-as-iron}

Effects that grant natural armor work even better for you.

#### Prerequisites {#prerequisites-2}

::: paragraph statline
Toughness, duergar.
:::

#### Benefit {#benefit-3}

::: paragraph statline
When you gain an enhancement bonus to your natural armor, that bonus increases by 2. This has no
effect if your natural armor bonus is +0 or you do not have an enhancement bonus increasing your
natural armor bonus.
:::
