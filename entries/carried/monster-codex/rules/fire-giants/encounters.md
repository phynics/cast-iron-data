## Royal Accompaniment {#royal-accompaniment}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR 23
:::

Some fire giant nobles deem it necessary to descend from their thrones to dally among the common
laborers and soldiers of the clan, or to survey their newly conquered holdings. They're escorted by
a small group of loyal lieutenants to ensure their safety from would-be assassins. When not
traveling, these lieutenants serve as royal guards in fire giant throne rooms.
