::: statblock
Type: insanity
Save: Will DC 14
Onset: 1 day
Effect: target is sickened (if manic) or shaken (if phobic) as long as the source of the mania or phobia is obvious; chance of becoming fascinated or frightened (see below)
:::

## Description {#description}

A mania is an irrational obsession with a (usually inappropriate) particular object or situation,
while a phobia is an irrational fear of a (usually commonplace) object or situation. Additionally,
if a manic or phobic character is directly confronted by his obsession (requiring a standard
action), he must make a Will save against the insanity or become fascinated (if manic) or frightened
(if phobic) by the object for 1d6 rounds.
