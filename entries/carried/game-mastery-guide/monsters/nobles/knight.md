Knights are noble warriors, proud of bearing and lineage and yet humble in service to their liege.
Though merciful and generous of spirit, a true knight is always ready to level lance or bare steel
in pursuit of justice and to protect the innocent. Knights may also serve as local lord-stewards,
judges, or fortress commanders. Knights are usually found singly or accompanied by a squire,
escorting a pair of pilgrims (CR 8), guarding two nobles (CR 11), or leading a lance of four cavalry
(CR 10).
