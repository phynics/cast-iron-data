A sage is an academic of the first order. Both a polyglot and polymath, a sage can be consulted to
answer any number of questions by anyone wealthy enough to afford her considerable fees. A sage is
often accompanied by a merchant prince who procures all the research materials she desires (CR 12),
and together they may sit on the small council of a queen (CR 13) or king (CR 15).
