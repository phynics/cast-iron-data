A monster hunter is a clever, experienced hunter, riding the forest trails with her faithful hawk
serving as her eyes above. Whether seeking a reward or bounty, an impressive trophy, or simply an
epic fireside tale of the hunt, a monster hunter seldom rests or tarries long when she hears
whispered tales of wild things on the prowl. Monster hunters can be outriders and protectors of the
wilderness or skilled hunters. Different types of monster hunters can be easily created by changing
the monster hunter's favored enemy, favored terrain, or animal companion.

A merchant prince often has a pair of monster hunters on payroll (CR 10) to dispose of threats to
business. Patrols of four monster hunters (CR 9) might serve as wilderness border guards A monster
hunter may also adventure with a battle mage, a medium or minstrel, and a tomb raider (CR 9).
