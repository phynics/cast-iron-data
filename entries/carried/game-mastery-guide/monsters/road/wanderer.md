A wanderer is a member of a band of traveling folk who ply the back roads and alleys of more settled
lands, often chased by rumors of thievery or kidnapping, mending pots and knives and giving exotic
performances that arouse the ire of the staid.

Wanderers are often excellent entertainers, whether musicians, dancers, or actors, but can also
serve as exotic cutpurses and charlatans.

Wanderers often travel in troupes of four entertainers (CR 6), or in larger groups of a dozen or
more (CR 9+). A lone wanderer might accompany a vagabond (CR 4) or hedge wizard (CR 5), while a
troupe of five or six might work with a fortune teller (CR 8) or highwayman (CR 9).
