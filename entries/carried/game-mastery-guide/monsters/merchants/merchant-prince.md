Merchant princes are the captains of commerce, canny mercantilists who deal with nation-spanning
trade contracts in bulk commodities as well as backroom deals over baubles of great price. They are
clever negotiators and can serve as highly skilled spies, diplomats, or charlatans emulating true
magicians. A merchant prince often retains a sellsword as a bodyguard (CR 10).
