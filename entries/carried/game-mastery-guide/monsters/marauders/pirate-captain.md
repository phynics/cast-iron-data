Pirate captains are the deadly mistresses of pirate bands, villainous cutthroats who have cursed,
looted, ravaged, betrayed, and carved their way to the top. These steel-handed stingrays lead their
crews by sheer force of will. Pirate captains make excellent champion pit fighters and deadly
assassins. A pirate captain is usually found with a first mate and a dozen shipmates (CR 12).
