Bounty hunters rarely hunt animals or beasts. Instead, they make not only sport but a lucrative
business of capturing humanoid targets at the behest of wealthy patrons. Bounty hunters often work
alone, but may lead a press gang of a sellsword, torturer, and two slavers (CR 12).
