_This fiendish figure, complete with bat wings, claws, a fanged bull's head, and cloven feet,
bellows a roar of challenge._

Half-fiends are creatures heavily tainted with demonic, infernal, or other evil power.

## Creating a Half-Fiend {#creating-a-half-fiend}

### Half-Fiend {#half-fiend}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR varies
:::

“Half-fiend” is an inherited or acquired template that can be added to a living, corporeal creature
with an Int score of 4 or more. A half-fiend uses all the base creature's statistics and special
abilities except as noted here.

#### Challenge Rating {#challenge-rating}

HD 4 or less, as base creature + 1; HD 5 to 10, as base creature + 2; HD 11 or more, as base
creature + 3.

#### Alignment {#alignment}

Any evil.

#### Type {#type}

The creature's type changes to outsider (native). Do not recalculate HD, BAB, or saves.

#### Armor Class {#armor-class}

Natural armor improves by +1.

#### Defenses/Qualities {#defenses-qualities}

Gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet; immunity to poison; acid, cold, electricity, and
fire resistance 10; DR 5/magic (if HD 11 or less) or 10/magic (if HD 12 or more); and SR equal to
creature's CR + 11 (maximum 35).

#### Speed {#speed}

Unless the base creature flies better, the half-fiend flies at twice the base creature's land speed
(good).

#### Melee {#melee}

A half-fiend gains two claw attacks and a bite attack. Damage depends on its size (see pages
301–302).

#### Special Attacks {#special-attacks}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A half-fiend gains the following.

::: paragraph indented
_Smite Good_ Once per day as a swift action the half-fiend can smite good as the smite evil ability
of a paladin of the same level as the half-fiend's Hit Dice, except affecting a good target. The
smite persists until the target is dead of the half-fiend rests.
:::

::: paragraph indented
_Spell-Like Abilities_: A half-fiend with an Int or Wis score of 8 or higher has a cumulative number
of [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) set by its HD. Unless otherwise noted, an
ability is usable 1/day. CL equals the creature's HD (or the CL of the base creature's spell-like
abilities, whichever is higher).
:::

#### Abilities {#abilities}

A half-fiend gains a +4 bonus on three ability scores of its choice and a +2 bonus on the other
three.

#### Skills {#skills}

A half-fiend with racial HD has skill points per racial HD equal to 6 + Int mod. Racial class skills
are unchanged, and class level skill ranks are unaffected.
