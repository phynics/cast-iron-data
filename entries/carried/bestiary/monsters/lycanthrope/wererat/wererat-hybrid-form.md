Natural wererats tend to be short and wiry, with constantly darting eyes and frequent nervous
twitches. Males often have thin, ragged moustaches.

Wererats prefer cities where they can blend in with the humanoid and rat population. Their abilities
make them especially good at thieving and spying, and in many cities the thieves' guild employs
numerous wererat members.
