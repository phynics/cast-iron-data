in its eye sockets._

Some skeletons retain their intelligence and cunning, making them formidable warriors. These undead
are far more powerful than their mindless kin, and many gain class levels.

## Creating a Skeletal Champion {#creating-a-skeletal-champion}

### Skeletal Champion {#skeletal-champion}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR +1 to Skeleton
:::

“Skeletal Champion” is an acquired template that can be added to any corporeal creature (other than
an undead) that has a skeletal system (referred to hereafter as the base creature) and a minimum
Intelligence of 3.

#### Challenge Rating {#challenge-rating}

A skeletal champion's CR is +1 higher than a normal skeleton with the same HD.

#### Type {#type}

The creature's type becomes undead. It keeps subtypes save for alignment subtypes and subtypes that
indicate kind.

#### Alignment {#alignment}

Any evil.

#### Armor Class {#armor-class}

Natural armor as per skeleton.

#### Hit Dice {#hit-dice}

Change all of the creature's racial HD to d8s, then add 2 racial Hit Dice to this total (creatures
without racial HD gain 2). HD from class levels are unchanged.

#### Defensive Abilities {#defensive-abilities}

A skeletal champion gains DR 5/bludgeoning, [channel resistance](ref/rule/channel-resistance) +4,
and immunity to cold. It also gains all of the standard undead traits.

#### Speed {#speed}

As standard skeleton.

#### Attacks {#attacks}

As standard skeleton.

#### Abilities {#abilities}

Str +2, Dex +2. As undead, it has no Constitution score.

#### BAB {#bab}

Its BAB for racial HD equals 3/4 of its HD.

#### Skills {#skills}

Gains skill ranks per racial Hit Die equal to 4 + its Int modifier. Class skills for racial HD are
Climb, Disguise, Fly, Intimidate, Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (religion), Perception, Sense
Motive, Spellcraft, and Stealth. Skills gained from class levels remain unchanged.

#### Feats {#feats}

A skeletal champion gains Improved Initiative as a bonus feat.

#### Saves {#saves}

Base save bonuses for racial Hit Dice are Fort +1/3 HD, Ref +1/3 HD, and Will +1/2 HD + 2.
