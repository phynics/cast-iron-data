Although most consider it to be the weakest and most feral of the chromatic dragons, the white
dragon makes up for its lack of cunning with sheer ferocity. White dragons dwell on remote, frozen
mountaintops and in arctic lowlands, making their home in glittering caves full of ice and snow.
They prefer their meals completely frozen.
