_A winged unicorn rises into the air on ivory pinions, a paragon of grace and beauty._

Most half-celestials are born of a mortal who loved a good outsider, but powerful holy magic can
also create one.

## Creating a Half-Celestial {#creating-a-half-celestial}

### Half-Celestial {#half-celestial}

::: statblock
Abbrev: CR varies
:::

“Half-celestial” is an inherited or acquired template that can be added to any living, corporeal
creature with an Intelligence score of 4 or more. A half-celestial creature retains the base
creature's statistics and special abilities except as noted here.

#### Challenge Rating {#challenge-rating}

HD 5 or less, as base creature + 1; HD 6–10, as base creature + 2; HD 11 or more, as base
creature + 3.

#### Alignment {#alignment}

Any good.

#### Type {#type}

The creature's type changes to outsider (native). Do not recalculate HD, BAB, or saves.

#### Armor Class {#armor-class}

Natural armor improves by +1.

#### Defenses/Qualities {#defenses-qualities}

It gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet; immunity to disease; +4 racial bonus on saves
vs. poison; acid, cold, and electricity resist 10; DR 5/magic (if HD 11 or less) or 10/magic (if HD
12 or more); and SR equal to CR + 11 (maximum 35).

#### Speed {#speed}

Unless the base creature flies better, the half-celestial flies at twice the base creature's land
speed (good maneuverability).

#### Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A half-celestial gains the following.

_Smite Evil_ Once per day as a swift action the half-celestial can smite evil as a paladin of the
same level as its Hit Dice. The smite persists until the target is dead of the half-celestial rests.

_Spell-Like Abilities_: A half-celestial with an Int or Wis score of 8 or higher has a cumulative
number of [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) depending on its Hit Dice. Unless
otherwise noted, an ability is usable once per day. Caster level equals the creature's HD (or the
caster evel of the base creature's spell-like abilities, whichever is higher).

#### Abilities {#abilities}

A half-celestial gains a +4 bonus on three ability scores of its choice and a +2 bonus on the other
three.

#### Skills {#skills}

A half-celestial with racial Hit Dice has skill points per racial Hit Die equal to 6 + its
Intelligence modifier. Racial class skills are unchanged from the base creature's. Skill ranks from
class levels are unaffected.
