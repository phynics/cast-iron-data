::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

This creature is an accomplished rock thrower and has a +1 racial bonus on attack rolls with thrown
rocks. The creature can hurl rocks up to two categories smaller than its size; for example, a Large
hill giant can hurl Small rocks. A “rock” is any large, bulky, and relatively regularly shaped
object made of any material with a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of at least 5. The creature can hurl
the rock up to five range increments. The size of the range increment varies with the creature.
Damage from a thrown rock is generally twice the creature's base slam damage plus 1-1/2 times its
Strength bonus.

## Format {#format}

rock throwing (120 ft.); _Location_: Special Attacks (damage is listed in Ranged attack).
