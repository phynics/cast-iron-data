::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

The creature can swim backward as a full-round action at the listed speed. It must move in a
straight line while jetting, and does not provoke attacks of opportunity when it does so.

## Format {#format}

jet (200 ft.); _Location_: Speed.
