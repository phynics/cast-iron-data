::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A creature with this special attack gains extra natural attacks under certain conditions, typically
when it grapples its foe. In addition to the options available to all grapplers, a monster with the
rake ability gains two free claw attacks that it can use only against a grappled foe. The bonus and
damage caused by these attacks is included in the creature's description. A monster with the rake
ability must begin its turn already grappling to use its rake—it can't begin a grapple and rake in
the same turn.

## Format {#format}

rake (2 claws +8, 1d4+2); _Location_: Special Attacks.
