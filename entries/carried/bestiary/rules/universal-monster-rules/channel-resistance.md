::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A creature with this special quality (usually an undead) is less easily affected by channeled
negative or positive energy. The creature adds the listed bonus on saves made to resist the effects
of channel energy, including effects that rely on the use of channel energy (such as the Command
Undead feat).

## Format {#format}

channel resistance +4; _Location_: Defensive Abilities.
