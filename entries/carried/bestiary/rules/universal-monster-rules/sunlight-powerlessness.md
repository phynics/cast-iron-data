::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

If the creature is in sunlight (but not in an area of _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ or similar
spells), it cannot attack and is staggered.

## Format {#format}

sunlight powerlessness; _Location_: Weaknesses.
