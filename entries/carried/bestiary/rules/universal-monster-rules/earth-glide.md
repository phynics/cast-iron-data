::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

When the creature burrows, it can pass through stone, dirt, or almost any other sort of earth except
metal as easily as a fish swims through water. If protected against fire damage, it can even glide
through lava. Its burrowing leaves behind no tunnel or hole, nor does it create any ripple or other
sign of its presence. A _[move earth](ref/spell/move-earth)_ spell cast on an area containing the
burrowing creature flings it back 30 feet, stunning it for 1 round unless it succeeds at a DC 15
Fortitude save.

## Format {#format}

earth glide; _Location_: Speed.
