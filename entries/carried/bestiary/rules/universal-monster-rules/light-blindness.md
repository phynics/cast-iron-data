::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Creatures with light blindness are blinded for 1 round if exposed to bright light, such as sunlight
or the _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ spell. Such creatures are dazzled as long as they remain in
areas of bright light.

## Format {#format}

light blindness; _Location_: Weaknesses.
