::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A creature with immunities takes no damage from listed sources. Immunities can also apply to
afflictions, conditions, spells (based on school, level, or save type), and other effects. A
creature that is immune does not suffer from these effects, or any secondary effects that are
triggered due to an immune effect.

## Format {#format}

:** Immune** acid, fire, paralysis; _Location_: Defensive Abilities.
