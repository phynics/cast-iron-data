::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

The creature automatically latches onto its target when it successfully makes the listed attack. The
creature is considered grappling, but the target is not. The target can attack or grapple the
creature as normal, or break the attach with a successful grapple or Escape Artist check. Most
creatures with this ability have a racial bonus to maintain a grapple (listed in its CMB entry).

## Format {#format}

attach; _Location_: individual attacks.
