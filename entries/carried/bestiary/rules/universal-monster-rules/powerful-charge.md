::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

When a creature with this special attack makes a charge, its attack deals extra damage in addition
to the normal benefits and hazards of a charge. The attack and amount of damage from the attack is
given in the creature's description.

## Format {#format}

powerful charge (gore, 4d8+24); _Location_: Special Attacks.
