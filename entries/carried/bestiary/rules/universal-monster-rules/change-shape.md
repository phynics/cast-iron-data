::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A creature with this special quality has the ability to assume the appearance of a specific creature
or type of creature (usually a humanoid), but retains most of its own physical qualities. The
creature cannot change shape to a form more than one size category smaller or larger than its
original form. This ability functions as a polymorph spell, the type of which is listed in the
creature's description, but the creature does not adjust its ability scores (although it gains any
other abilities of the creature it mimics). Unless otherwise stated, it can remain in an alternate
form indefinitely. Some creatures, such as lycanthropes, can transform into unique forms with
special modifiers and abilities. These creatures do adjust their ability scores, as noted in their
descriptions.

## Format {#format}

change shape (wolf, _beast form I_); _Location_: SQ, and in Special Abilities for creatures with a
unique listing.
