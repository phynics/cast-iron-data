::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Creatures with light sensitivity are dazzled in areas of bright sunlight or within the radius of a
_[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ spell.

## Format {#format}

:** Weaknesses** light sensitivity; _Location_: Weaknesses.
