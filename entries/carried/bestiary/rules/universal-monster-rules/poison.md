::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A creature with this ability can poison those it attacks. The effects of the poison, including its
save, frequency, and cure, are included in the creature's description. The saving throw to resist a
poison is usually a Fort save (DC 10 + 1/2 the poisoning creature's racial HD + the creature's Con
modifier; the exact DC is given in the creature's descriptive text). Poisons can be removed through
_[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison)_ and similar effects.

## Format {#format}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

:** Poison Name **Sting—injury; _save_ Fort DC 22, _frequency_ 1/round for 6 rounds, _effect_ 1d4
Con, _cure_ 2 consecutive saves; _Location_: Special Attacks and individual attacks.
