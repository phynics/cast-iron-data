::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A creature with this special quality ignores some damage of the indicated type each time it takes
damage of that kind (commonly acid, cold, electricity, or fire). The entry indicates the amount and
type of damage ignored.

## Format {#format}

:** Resist** acid 10; _Location_: Defensive Abilities.
