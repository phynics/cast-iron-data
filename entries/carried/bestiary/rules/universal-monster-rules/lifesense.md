::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

The creature notices and locates living creatures within 60 feet, just as if it possessed the
[blindsight](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) ability.

## Format {#format}

lifesense; _Location_: Senses.
