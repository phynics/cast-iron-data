::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A creature with this ability can cease or resume flight as a free action. If the creature has wings,
flight is an extraordinary ability. Otherwise, it is spell-like or supernatural, and it is
ineffective in an antimagic field; the creature loses its ability to fly for as long as the
antimagic effect persists.

## Format {#format}

fly 30 ft. (average); _Location_: Speed.
