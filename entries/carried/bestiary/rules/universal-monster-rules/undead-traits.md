::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Undead are immune to death effects, disease, mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale
effects, phantasms, and patterns), paralysis, poison, sleep, stun, and any effect that requires a
Fortitude save (unless the effect also works on objects or is harmless). Undead are not subject to
ability drain, energy drain, or nonlethal damage. Undead are immune to damage or penalties to their
physical ability scores (Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution), as well as to fatigue and
exhaustion effects. Undead are not at risk of death from massive damage.

## Format {#format}

undead traits; _Location_: Immune.
