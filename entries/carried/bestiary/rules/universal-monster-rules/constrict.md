::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A creature with this special attack can crush an opponent, dealing bludgeoning damage, when it makes
a successful grapple check (in addition to any other effects caused by a successful check, including
additional damage). The amount of damage is given in the creature's entry and is typically equal to
the amount of damage caused by the creature's melee attack.

## Format {#format}

constrict (1d8+6); _Location_: Special Attacks.
