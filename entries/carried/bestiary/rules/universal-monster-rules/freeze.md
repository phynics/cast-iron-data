::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

The creature can hold itself so still it appears to be an inanimate object of the appropriate shape
(a statue, patch of fungus, and so on). The creature can take 20 on its Stealth check to hide in
plain sight as this kind of inanimate object.
