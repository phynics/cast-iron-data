::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Animals do not willingly approach the creature unless the animal's master makes a DC 25 Handle
Animal, Ride, or wild empathy check.

## Format {#format}

unnatural aura (30 ft.); _Location_: Aura.
