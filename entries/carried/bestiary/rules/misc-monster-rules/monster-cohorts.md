The Leadership feat allows a character to gain a loyal cohort. With the GM's approval, this cohort
can be a similarly aligned monster rather than a member of the standard player races. Monsters on
the following list all work well as cohorts (be they assassins, bodyguards, mounts, etc.)—their
effective cohort “level” corresponds to the level available to the PC as afforded by his Leadership
score.

A monster cohort gains experience points as if it were a character of its cohort level, and when it
gains enough XP to advance a level, it should generally gain a key class level (fighter is often the
best choice for most cohorts). You can use the monsters on the following table as guidelines when
determining effective cohort levels for monsters not on this list.
