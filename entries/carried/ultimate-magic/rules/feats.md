Feats represent special tricks or abilities a character has acquired through training, luck, or as a
quirk of his or her birth. They grant characters abilities that others lack, giving them an edge in
the right situation. While some feats specifically interact with a PC's race or class, most can be
taken by anyone who meets the prerequisites.
