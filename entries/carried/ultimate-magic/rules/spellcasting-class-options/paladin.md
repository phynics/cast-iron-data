## Paladin {#paladin}

All paladins are holy champions of law and good, yet some devote themselves to specific causes with
exceptional zeal, swearing great oaths to solve specific problems or give their lives trying. This
section introduces a new paladin archetype, the oathbound paladin, and several sample oaths for
paladins with that archetype.
