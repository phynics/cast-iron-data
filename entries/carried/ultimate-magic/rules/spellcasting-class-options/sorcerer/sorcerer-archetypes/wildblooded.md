A wildblooded sorcerer has a mutated version of a more common bloodline, with one arcana and at
least one bloodline power that are different from those of an unmutated bloodline. When creating a
wildblooded sorcerer, select an existing bloodline, then select one of the following mutated
bloodlines associated with that bloodline. Use the normal bloodline's class skill, bonus spells, and
bonus feats, and the mutated bloodline's bloodline arcana. Use the normal bloodline's bloodline
powers, except when the mutated bloodline replaces one of those powers.

## Anarchic {#anarchic}

Your magical power taps into pure anarchy.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline}

Protean.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana}

Whenever you fail a concentration check to cast a spell, a cantrip effect is created. Randomly
determine one of your cantrips known (for example, if you know 6 cantrips, roll 1d6). There is a 50%
chance this cantrip affects a target of your choice within 60 feet; otherwise it affects you.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers}

Raw magical energy lashes out when you destroy or negate hostile spells.

#### Wild Feedback {#wild-feedback}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 3rd level, when you successfully dispel or counterspell an opponent's spell, the caster (if it is
within 100 feet) takes 1d6 points of damage +1 per level of the spell affected. This bloodline power
replaces protean resistances.

## Aerial {#aerial}

Your magic is more focused on the power of air and rain than on lightning and thunder.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-2}

Stormborn.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-2}

Whenever outdoors during any form of precipitation, your effective caster level is increased by 2.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-2}

The greatest storms bring ruin through wind, not electricity.

#### Windcaller {#windcaller}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

At 9th level, you can call the winds to obey your commands for 1 minute per level. This functions
like _[control winds](ref/spell/control-winds)_, except that you may choose to be immune to any
increased wind effects you create. The ability's duration does not need to be consecutive, but it
must be used in 1-minute increments. This bloodline power replaces thunderbolt.

## Bedrock {#bedrock}

Your earth magic is more defensive than offensive.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-3}

Deep Earth.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-3}

Whenever you cast a spell of the summoning subschool, the creatures summoned gain DR/adamantine
equal to 1/2 your sorcerer level (minimum 1). This does not stack with any DR the creature might
have.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-3}

Rock is timeless; it endures.

#### Iron Hide {#iron-hide}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

At 9th level, as a swift action, you can grant yourself DR 10/adamantine for a number of rounds per
day equal to your sorcerer level. The rounds do not need to be used consecutively. This bloodline
power replaces crystal shard.

## Brutal {#brutal}

The powers of the Abyss can vary radically, even for similar fiends.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-4}

Abyssal.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-4}

Whenever you cast a spell that deals hit point damage, one target of your choice affected by the
spell takes 2 additional hit points of damage. This ability has no effect on spells that do not deal
hit point damage (such as ability score damage).

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-4}

Flight is often the key to survival.

#### Wings of the Abyss {#wings-of-the-abyss}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 9th level, you can sprout leathery wings and fly for a number of minutes per day equal to your
sorcerer level, with a speed of 60 feet and good maneuverability. This duration does not need to be
consecutive, but it must be used in 1-minute increments. This bloodline power replaces strength of
the abyss.

## Empyreal {#empyreal}

Your heavenly power derives from insight rather than force of personality.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-5}

Celestial.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-5}

Unlike most sorcerers whose innate magic is powered by force of personality, you use pure willpower
to master and fuel your magic. You use your Wisdom, rather than your Charisma, to determine all
class features and effects relating to your sorcerer class, such as bonus spells per day, maximum
spell level you can cast, and the save DCs of your spells. You gain a +2 bonus on all Heal and
Knowledge (religion) checks.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-5}

Your quasi-divine nature gives you strange powers.

#### Sacred Cistern {#sacred-cistern}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 9th level, your bloodline makes you a natural receptacle of divine energy. You can channel energy
once per day as a cleric of your sorcerer level – 4. This bloodline power replaces wings of heaven.

## Envenomed {#envenomed}

The ophidian source of your bloodline has more to do with stealth and assassination than it does
with leadership and alliances.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-6}

Serpentine.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-6}

You gain a +2 bonus on Acrobatics, Climb, and Stealth checks.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-6}

The stink of poison taints your magic.

#### Envenom {#envenom}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 3rd level, as a swift action, you can lick or bite a melee weapon to imbue it with 1 dose of
black adder venom. The poison's DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Charisma
modifier. You may use this ability once per day at 3rd level, and one additional time per day for
every three additional levels. The poison cannot be removed or stored, and the weapon loses the
benefit of the poison after the first successful attack or 1 hour has passed. The bloodline power
replaces serpentfriend.

## Groveborn {#groveborn}

Some plant magic is more green, while some is more brown, like hardwood.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-7}

Verdant.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-7}

Your powers of compulsion can affect even plant creatures. Whenever you cast a mind-affecting or
language-dependent spell, it affects creatures of the plant type as if they were humanoids that
understood your language.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-7}

Your nature powers create tough, resilient minions.

#### Lush Summoning {#lush-summoning}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 3rd level, whenever you summon creatures with a conjuration (summoning) spell, you can decide
that they appear green and leafy. The natural armor bonus of such creatures is increased by +2, and
they gain a +4 bonus on saves against paralysis, poison, polymorph, sleep, and stunning. This
bloodline power replaces photosynthesis.

## Karmic {#karmic}

The universe punishes those who interfere with destiny.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-8}

Destined.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-8}

If you are casting defensively because a creature is threatening you and you fail your concentration
check, one of the creatures that is threatening you provokes an attack of opportunity from you or
from one ally who is adjacent to the enemy. You decide which creature provokes this attack and which
of its adjacent opponents may make the attack.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-8}

Your blood responds angrily when you are attacked.

#### Fate's Retribution {#fates-retribution}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Starting at 1st level, when you are hit by a melee attack, as an immediate action, you can curse the
creature that struck you. The target takes a –2 penalty on all attack and damage rolls for 1d4
rounds. A Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier) negates this effect.
You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This
bloodline power replaces touch of destiny.

## Linnorm {#linnorm}

Your draconic heritage stems from a mighty, primordial linnorm rather than from the more common,
somewhat civilized dragons.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-9}

Draconic.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-9}

Whenever you cast a spell with an energy descriptor that matches your linnorm bloodline's energy
type, you gain a natural armor bonus equal to the spell's level for 1d4 rounds.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-9}

True dragons blast their foes with rays of energy.

#### Elemental Spit {#elemental-spit}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Starting at 1st level, you can fire an elemental ray that matches your linnorm bloodline's energy
type as a standard action, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. The ray deals
1d6 points of damage + 1 for every two sorcerer levels you possess. You can use this ability a
number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces claws.

## Pit-Touched {#pit-touched}

Your diabolic corruption is from a source in the deepest parts of Hell.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-10}

Infernal.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-10}

Whenever you cast a spell, you gain a bonus on Intimidate checks equal to the spell's level for 1
round.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-10}

The crushing weight of evil has given you the strength to endure.

#### Tough as Hell {#tough-as-hell}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

At 9th level, you gain a +2 inherent bonus to your Constitution. This bonus increases to +4 at 13th
level, and to +6 at 17th level. This bloodline power replaces hellfire.

## Primal {#primal}

Your powers are attuned to the concentrated core of the elemental plane.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-11}

Elemental.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-11}

Whenever you cast a spell with an energy descriptor that matches your elemental bloodline's energy
type, that spell deals +1 point of damage per die rolled.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-11}

You can infuse raw energy into your summoned minions.

#### Elementalist Summoning {#elementalist-summoning}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 9th level, whenever you summon a creature, it gains energy resistance 10 against the energy type
that matches your elemental bloodline (if it already has such resistance, its resistance increases
by +5), and its natural attacks deal an additional 1d6 points of damage of the same energy type.
This bloodline power replaces elemental blast.

## Rime-Blooded {#rime-blooded}

The wintry origin of your magic flows like ice water in your veins.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-12}

Boreal.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-12}

Whenever you cast a spell with the cold descriptor, you may select one target of the spell to be
slowed (as the spell) for 1 round. A Fortitude save (DC 10 + the level of cold spell + your Charisma
modifier) negates the effect.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-12}

Sometimes you must bring the cold to your enemies.

#### Freezing Bolt {#freezing-bolt}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

At 9th level, you can cause the air to erupt in freezing sleet. This 10-foot-radius burst does 1d6
points of cold damage per sorcerer level (Reflex half). The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2
your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier. At 9th level, you can use this ability once per day.
At 17th level, you can use this ability twice per day. At 20th level, you can use this ability three
times per day. This power has a range of 60 feet. This bloodline power replaces snow shroud.

## Sage {#sage}

Scholars of the arcane are always creating new means to use magic.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-13}

Arcane.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-13}

Unlike most sorcerers, whose innate magic is powered by force of personality, you use your intellect
to understand and master your mystic powers. You use your Intelligence, rather than your Charisma,
to determine all class features and effects relating to your sorcerer class, such as bonus spells
per day, the maximum spell level you can cast, the save DCs of your spells, and the number of daily
uses of your bloodline powers. You gain a +2 bonus on all Knowledge (arcana) and Spellcraft checks.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-13}

Your preferred focus is a bolt of mind-spun magic.

#### Arcane Bolt {#arcane-bolt}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

Starting at 1st level, you can unleash a ray of magic force as a standard action, targeting any foe
within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. This ray deals 1d4 points of damage + 1 for every two
sorcerer levels you possess. This damage is treated as a spell of a level equal to half your
sorcerer level, and is a force effect. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to
3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces arcane bond.

## Sanguine {#sanguine}

The power of undeath can derive from cold blood as well as hard bone.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-14}

Undead.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-14}

Whenever you cast a spell of the necromancy school, your effective caster level is increased by 1.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-14}

Your magic emulates that of the hungry dead.

#### The Blood Is the Life {#the-blood-is-the-life}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 1st level, you can gain sustenance from the blood of the recently dead. As a standard action, you
can drink the blood of a creature that died within the past minute. The creature must be corporeal,
must be at least the same size as you, and must have blood. This ability heals you 1d6 hit points
and nourishes you as if you'd had a full meal. You may use this ability a number of times per day
equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces grave touch.

## Seaborn {#seaborn}

Your powers rise like the tides.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-15}

Aquatic.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-15}

When you are in a body of water large enough to float in, your effective caster level is increased
by 1.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-15}

You prefer to attack with water rather than to leech it away.

#### Water Blast {#water-blast}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

As a standard action, you can fire a bolt of water at a foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack.
The foe is knocked prone, and at your option may be pushed 5 feet directly away from you. A reflex
save (DC 10 +1/2 your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier) negates this effect. You can use this
ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces
dehydrating touch.

## Sylvan {#sylvan}

Your ties to nature have more to do with creatures than with capriciousness.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-16}

Fey.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-16}

See bloodline powers.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-16}

Your magic shows a kinship to that of the beast-talkers and shapechanger fey.

#### Animal Companion {#animal-companion}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

You gain an animal companion. Your effective druid level for this ability is equal to your sorcerer
level – 3 (minimum 1st). This bloodline power counts as your bloodline arcana and also replaces
laughing touch.

#### Fey Wings {#fey-wings}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

At 15th level, you can grow insectlike wings from your back and become one size category smaller (as
if you had used _[reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person)_), gaining a fly speed of 60 feet with
average maneuverability. You can maintain this form for 1 minute per level. This duration does not
need to be consecutive, but it must be used in 1-minute increments. This bloodline power replaces
fey magic.

## Umbral {#umbral}

Your nature is to gather the darkness into yourself.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-17}

Shadow.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-17}

Whenever you cast a spell in an area of dim light or darkness, your effective caster level is
increased by 1.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-17}

Your shadow magic is more defensive than offensive.

#### Cloak of Shadows {#cloak-of-shadows}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

At 1st level, as a standard action, you can grant one target a cloak of shadows. This cloak gives
the target a bonus on Stealth checks made in areas of dim or no light equal to 1/2 your sorcerer
level for 1 round per 2 sorcerer levels you possess (minimum +1 bonus for 1 round). You may use this
ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces
shadowstrike.

## Visionary {#visionary}

Your dream-magic strongly influences the waking world.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-18}

Dreamspun.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-18}

Though you can still only regain spells once per day, you need only a single hour of sleep prior to
doing so. (You still risk fatigue as normal if you don't receive enough rest.)

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-18}

Your dreams hint at the future.

#### Visions {#visions}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

At 9th level, when you sleep, you can gain information through prophetic visions. Once per day when
sleeping, you may gain information about actions taken in the next week as if you had cast a
_[divination](ref/spell/divination)_ spell. You can gain information about a single question at 9th
level. At 17th level, you can have two questions answered while you sleep, and at 20th level, three
questions. This bloodline power replaces dreamshaper.

## Void-Touched {#void-touched}

The darkness between the stars calls to you.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-19}

Starsoul.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-19}

Whenever you cast an evocation spell, you may select one affected target that fails its save to
suffer the choking airlessness of the void, silencing it (as _[silence](ref/spell/silence)_, but
affecting only the target) for 1 round. This is a supernatural ability.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-19}

Your magic is blacker than any night.

#### Black Motes {#black-motes}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

This ability works exactly like (and replaces) minute meteors, except it deals cold damage.

#### Voidfield {#voidfield}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

At 9th level, you can create an area influenced by the black void. This ability acts as an
_[ice storm](ref/spell/ice-storm)_, except the area is also subject to
_[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness)_ for 1 round per four sorcerer levels. You may use
this ability once per day at 3rd level, and one additional time per day for every three additional
levels. The bloodline power replaces aurora borealis.

## Warped {#warped}

The alien taint in your blood causes malformations and mutations in others.

### Associated Bloodline {#associated-bloodline-20}

Aberrant.

### Bloodline Arcana {#bloodline-arcana-20}

Whenever you cast a spell of the polymorph subschool, one target of your choice may receive one
random effect from the Warped Polymorph Benefits table (see below). This bonus lasts as long as the
polymorph effect on the target.

### Bloodline Powers {#bloodline-powers-20}

Your strange blood brings out the worst in people.

#### Warp Touch {#warp-touch}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Spell-Like
:::

Starting at 1st level, you create brief, disorienting changes in a creature's physical form. This
ability affects one creature within 30 feet, which is dazed for 1 round (Fortitude negates; DC 10 +
1/2 your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier). You can use this ability a number of times per
day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. This bloodline power replaces acidic ray.
