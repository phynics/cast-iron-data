## Cleric {#cleric}

Clerics are messengers and servitors of the gods, channeling divine magic and pure positive or
negative energy. Though the typical adventuring cleric stands ready to smite enemies of the faith
with spell and weapon, many cloister themselves in remote libraries and honor the gods with
scholarly works, while others espouse heretical beliefs that embarrass mainstream members of the
clergy. They use their divine channeling to heal or harm, and some have developed new ways to blast
their opponents or bolster allies.

This section introduces alternative effects for channeling positive and negative energy, and is
followed by four new cleric archetypes.

## Variant Channeling {#variant-channeling}

An iconic ability of clerics is their power to channel positive or negative energy, whether for
healing, for damage, or to turn or command the undead. However, given the great variety of deities
and their divine portfolios, it naturally follows that some deities would endow their mortal
servants with the power to channel energies in other ways that more closely mirror their particular
focus. The following categories are examples of alternative channeling abilities based on the nature
of a deity's power; for example, a fire deity's negative energy channeling may deal fire damage as
part of or instead of the damage from channeling.

When you create a cleric character, decide whether she uses the standard form of channel energy or a
variant presented here based on one aspect of her deity's portfolio. Once this choice is made, it
cannot be altered. Variant channeling has the same area of effect, save DCs, uses per day, and other
rules relating to channeling energy. Feats and abilities that modify or present alternative uses for
channeled energy (such as Command Undead and Turn Undead) work normally with these variant
channeling abilities.

A variant channeling either modifies positive channeled energy when used to heal or modifies
negative energy when used to harm. When using positive energy to heal, affected creatures gain only
half the normal amount of healing but also receive a specific beneficial effect. When channeling
negative energy to harm, affected creatures take only half the normal damage but take an additional
penalty or harmful effect; a successful saving throw negates the additional penalty or effect but
does not reduce the damage any further. Creatures that would normally ignore the effect of a
particular channel (such as undead with respect to a positive energy channel used to heal) ignore
the variant effect of that channel.

Some variant channeling abilities are enhanced when used on particular creature types. Such
channeling increases the normal healing or damage from channeled energy by 50% for that creature
type, rather than the default half healing or damage for the alternative channeling. For example, a
7th-level cleric normally heals 4d6 points of damage with channeled positive energy; with the Nature
alternative channeling, that cleric instead heals only half that amount (2d6) when channeling, but
heals animals and fey an additional +50% over the unhalved value (4d6 + 50%).

Unless otherwise stated, bonuses granted by a variant channeling are sacred bonuses if you channel
positive energy or profane bonuses if you channel negative energy. If an alternative channeled
energy provides a “channel bonus” on rolls or statistics, the bonus is +1, increasing to +2 at
cleric level 5 and for every 5 cleric levels thereafter (to a maximum of +5). Likewise, a “channel
penalty” is initially –1, increasing to –2 at cleric level 5 and every 5 cleric levels thereafter
(to a maximum of –5).

A character who has the channel energy ability from a class other than cleric may use these variant
channeling rules if the class's abilities are tied to serving a deity. For example, paladins can
select alternative channeling abilities if they serve a deity, as can oracles with the Life mystery
(as they serve many deities), but necromancer wizards cannot.

The following is a list of example portfolios and the variant channeling abilities available to
each.

### Air/Sky/Wind {#air-sky-wind}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Acrobatics and Fly checks, saving throws
against wind effects, and effects with the air descriptor until the end of your next turn.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are buffeted with wind until the end of your next turn; this wind
gives them a channel penalty on ranged attacks, and their movement is halved if they move toward
you.

### Ale/Wine {#ale-wine}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures ignore the nauseated and sickened conditions, as well as ability
damage and drain from poison, until the end of your next turn. This does not bring back to life
creatures killed by Constitution damage. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are nauseated for 1
round.

### Art/Music {#art-music}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Perform checks and on saving throws
against illusions, sonic effects, and language-dependent effects for 1 minute.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures take a channel penalty on saving throws against illusions, sonic
effects, and language-dependent effects for 1 minute.

### Battle/Wrath {#battle-wrath}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on weapon damage and critical hit
confirmation rolls until the end of your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures take a
channel penalty on weapon damage and critical hit confirmation rolls until the end of your next
turn.

### Beauty/Love/Lust {#beauty-love-lust}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on saving throws against charm effects and
abilities based on fascination or physical attractiveness (such as a nymph's blinding beauty and
stunning glance abilities) for 1 minute. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty
on saving throws against charm effects and effects that fascinate for 1 minute.

### Bravery/Valor {#bravery-valor}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures affected by fear may attempt another saving throw and receive a
channel bonus on the roll. A creature unaffected by fear gains a channel bonus to its Armor Class
until the end of your next turn and on its attack roll if it makes a charge attack before your next
turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—This works like a standard channel (not halved).

### Cities {#cities}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on attack rolls when flanking and to Armor
Class when flanked until the end of your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—All creatures' squares
and threatened areas are treated as difficult terrain until the start of your next turn (these areas
move with the creatures rather than being fixed in place at the time of the channeling). Until the
end of your next turn, creatures gain a channel penalty on Acrobatics checks and overrun attempts to
move through these squares.

### Contracts/Oaths {#contracts-oaths}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on saves against compulsion effects until
the end of your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on saving
throws against compulsions until the end of your next turn.

### Darkness {#darkness}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) until the end
of your next turn. At cleric level 5, they gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 30 feet as well,
increasing this range by 30 feet for every 5 cleric levels thereafter. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—The
illumination level in the area drops by 1 step, as _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_, for 1 minute.
At cleric level 10, the light level drops by 2 steps. At cleric level 15, it drops by 3 steps, and
areas of dim light or darkness become supernaturally dark (even creatures with darkvision cannot see
within it).

### Death {#death}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on stabilization checks when dying and on
saves against death effects for 1 minute. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty
on stabilization checks when dying for 1 minute, and subtract the channel penalty from all healing
magic, [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing), and regeneration.

### Destruction {#destruction}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on attack and damage rolls against objects,
CMB for sunder attempts, and Strength checks to break objects until the end of your next turn.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Unattended objects take full channel damage (not half).

### Disease {#disease}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures heal a number of points of ability damage to one ability score
(your choice) equal to your channel bonus. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are sickened until the
end of your next turn, plus a number of rounds equal to your channel penalty. At cleric level 10,
you may select one creature as the target of a _[contagion](ref/spell/contagion)_ in addition to the
effects of the channeled energy. At cleric level 20, all creatures in the area are subject to
_[contagion](ref/spell/contagion)_ in addition to the effects of the channeled energy.

### Dreams {#dreams}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures affected by sleep effects may attempt another saving throw and
apply the channel bonus on the roll. Willingly sleeping creatures gain a channel bonus on saving
throws and Perception checks for up to 8 hours (this bonus ends if the creature awakens).
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on Perception checks and saving throws
against exhaustion, fatigue, and sleep effects for 1 minute.

### Earth {#earth}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a DR 1/adamantine until the end of your next turn. This DR
improves by 1 at 5th level and every 5 levels thereafter. This does not allow recipients to overcome
DR/adamantine with their own attacks. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—All squares in the area are treated
as difficult terrain for 1 minute.

### Envy {#envy}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on disarm and steal combat maneuvers, Bluff
checks, and Sleight of Hand checks for 1 minute. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures must attempt a
steal combat maneuver on their next turn against an adjacent enemy. This is a compulsion effect.

### Farming {#farming}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures ignore fatigue (but not exhaustion) for 1 minute. The healing
effect is enhanced for plant creatures. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—The damage effect is enhanced for
plant creatures. Creatures are fatigued, as if experiencing starvation.

### Fate {#fate}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on one d20 roll made during their next
readied action as long as it is taken before the end of your next turn.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on ability checks and skill checks for 1
minute.

### Fire {#fire}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—The healing effect is enhanced for creatures with the fire subtype.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—The damage effect is enhanced for creatures with the cold subtype. Affected
creatures who fail their saves catch on fire.

### Forge {#forge}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures in metal armor gain a channel bonus to Armor Class until the end
of your next turn. Alternatively, you may repair damage to metal objects and metal constructs as if
they were creatures, and this healing is enhanced. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—The damage effect is
enhanced against metal constructs and unattended metal objects.

### Freedom {#freedom}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Escape Artist checks, CMB checks to
escape a grapple, and saving throws against becoming entangled, paralyzed, or slowed.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are slowed (as _[slow](ref/spell/slow)_) until the end of your
next turn.

### Hunting {#hunting}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Survival checks and ranged attack rolls
until the end of your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a –5 penalty on Stealth
checks for 1 minute and temporarily lose effects or special abilities that reduce or negate tracks
for that duration.

### Justice/Law {#justice-law}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Lawful creatures gain a channel bonus on Perception and Sense Motive
checks, attack rolls, and saving throws until the end of your next turn.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are affected by _[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth)_ until
the end of your next turn.

### Knowledge {#knowledge}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Knowledge and Perception checks until the
end of your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Sentient creatures (Intelligence 3+) who fail their
saves take 1d2 points of Intelligence damage. A successful save negates this Intelligence damage.

### Luck {#luck}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus or a luck bonus (creature's choice) on one
roll (attack roll, CMB check, saving throw, or skill check) made before the end of your next turn.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures take a channel penalty on all d20 rolls until the end of your
next turn.

### Madness {#madness}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on saving throws against confusion,
insanity, rage, and similar effects. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are _confused_ until the end
of your next turn.

### Magic {#magic}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on caster level checks and concentration
checks until the end of your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—All creatures must make
concentration checks (DC = channel energy DC plus spell level) to use spells or
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) until the end of your next turn.

### Monsters {#monsters}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—The healing effect is enhanced for aberrations, dragons, magical beasts,
and monstrous humanoids. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on attack and
damage rolls against summoned and called creatures.

### Murder {#murder}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures that critically hit before the end of your next turn add a bleed
effect equal to your channel bonus. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a bleed effect equal to
your channel penalty.

### Nature {#nature}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—The healing effect is enhanced for animals and fey. Creatures gain a
channel bonus on Handle Animal and wild empathy checks for 1 minute. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—The
harmful effect is enhanced for animals and fey.

### Nightmares {#nightmares}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on charm and compulsion saves for 1 minute.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on concentration checks and saves against
fear and phantasms.

### Pain {#pain}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on pain effect saves for 1 minute.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are sickened until the end of your next turn, plus a number of
rounds equal to your channel penalty. At cleric level 10, you may select one creature to be
nauseated in addition to the effects of the channeled energy.

### Poison {#poison}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on poison saves for 1 minute.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures take 1 point of Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution damage (your
choice) as a poison effect.

### Protection {#protection}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus to Armor Class until the end of your next
turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty to Armor Class until the end of your
next turn.

### Revenge/Vengeance {#revenge-vengeance}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—This works like a standard channel (not halved).
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on attack rolls until the end of your next
turn.

### Rulership {#rulership}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Diplomacy checks and to the DC of their
language-dependent and charm effects until the end of your next turn.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are dazed for 1 round.

### Ocean/Sea/Water {#ocean-sea-water}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Climb and Swim checks and Constitution
checks to hold their breath; this bonus lasts for 1 minute. Creatures ignore fatigue from thirst and
pressure damage from deep water for 1 minute. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—The harming effect is
enhanced for creatures with the aquatic or water subtypes.

### Secrets {#secrets}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Sense Motive checks and caster level
checks and to save DCs of their divination spells until the end of your next turn.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on saving throws against divinations for 1
minute. Creatures using Sense Motive against affected creatures for the next minute gain a channel
bonus on Sense Motive checks.

### Self-Perfection {#self-perfection}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures may ignore one temporary condition of their choice until the end
of your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on attempts to dispel,
remove, or make additional saving throws against all ongoing conditions for 1 minute (this does not
apply to any initial saving throw allowed against such an effect but does apply to effects that
begin during this duration).

### Slavery/Tyranny {#slavery-tyranny}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures ignore fatigue and exhaustion for 1 minute.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on saves against compulsions, pain, and
stun for 1 minute.

### Strategy {#strategy}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Until your next turn, creatures using aid another add your channel bonus to
the aided creature's roll in addition to the normal aid another bonus.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures may not take attacks of opportunity until the end of your next
turn.

### Strength {#strength}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on all Strength-based attacks, combat
maneuver checks that rely on Strength, Strength-based skills, and Strength checks until the end of
your next turn. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on all Strength-based
rolls and to Strength-based game statistics (such as CMD) until the end of your next turn.

### Sun {#sun}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—The illumination level in the area increases by one step, and creatures
gain a channel bonus on saves against blindness and light-based effects.
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures are dazzled for 1 minute; creatures with light blindness or light
sensitivity are blinded instead of dazzled.

### Trickery {#trickery}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on Bluff, Disguise, Sleight of Hand, and
Stealth checks for 1 minute. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel penalty on Perception
and Sense Motive checks for 1 minute.

### Undeath {#undeath}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—This works like a standard channel (not halved).
_[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—The healing effect is enhanced for undead creatures and those with negative
energy affinity.

### Weapons {#weapons}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on attack rolls until the end of your next
turn when wielding your deity's favored weapon. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Creatures gain a channel
penalty on attack and damage rolls until the end of your next turn when wielding manufactured
weapons.

### Weather {#weather}

_[Heal](ref/spell/heal)_—Creatures gain a channel bonus on saving throws against electricity, sonic,
and wind effects. _[Harm](ref/spell/harm)_—Each time you channel energy, you may change half the
channeled energy damage to electricity or sonic damage.
