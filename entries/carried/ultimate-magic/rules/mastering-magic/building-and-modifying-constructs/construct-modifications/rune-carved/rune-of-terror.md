## Trigger and Effect {#trigger-and-effect}

This rune is triggered the first time a creature moves adjacent to the modified construct. The rune
creates 20-foot-radius spread of necromantic energy. Creatures in the area must succeed on a DC 20
Will saving throw or become panicked for 1d4 rounds. If the Will save succeeds, the creature is
shaken for 1 round.
