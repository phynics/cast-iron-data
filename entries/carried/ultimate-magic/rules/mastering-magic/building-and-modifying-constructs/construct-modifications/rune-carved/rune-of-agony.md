## Trigger and Effect {#trigger-and-effect}

This modification is triggered the first time the modified construct takes damage. Creatures within
a 20-foot-radius spread must succeed on a DC 17 Fortitude save or suffer from wracking pains that
impose a –4 penalty on attack rolls, skill checks, and ability checks for 1 minute. On a successful
saving throw, the creature takes the penalties imposed by the rune for 1 round.
