## Trigger and Effect {#trigger-and-effect}

This rune is triggered the first time the modified construct is attacked with a melee attack, ranged
attack, or _[magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile)_. The construct gains a +4 shield bonus to its
Armor Class for 3 minutes. This is a force effect and applies against incorporeal touch attacks.
