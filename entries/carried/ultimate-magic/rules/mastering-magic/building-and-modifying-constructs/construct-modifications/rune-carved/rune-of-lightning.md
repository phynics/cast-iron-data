## Trigger and Effect {#trigger-and-effect}

This rune is triggered when the modified construct is hit by a melee attack. The rune releases a
5-foot-radius burst of electricity dealing 3d8 points of electrical damage to all creatures within
the burst. A successful DC 12 Reflex saving throw halves the damage.
