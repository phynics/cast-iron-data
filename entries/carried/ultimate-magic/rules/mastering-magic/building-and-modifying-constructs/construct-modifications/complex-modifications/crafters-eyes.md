::: statblock
Crafter's Eyes\: CR Increase: : none
Crafter's Eyes\: Cost: : 8,000 gp
Crafter's Eyes\: Requirements: : Craft Construct, scrying
:::

The crafter gives her construct crystalline eyes that permit her to use a
_[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ or _[greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater)_ spell to see from
the perspective of her creation. This modification requires the crafter also to prepare a special
crystalline orb magically bonded to the eyes, which serves as her focus for the
_[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ or _[greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater)_ spell. The cost of
creating that focus is included in the cost of modifying the construct.

Unlike normal _[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ or_ [greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater)_,
there is no chance of failure for viewing creatures though the sensor; the creator simply sees what
the construct sees for the duration of the spell. Spells cast through the sensor still have the
normal chance of operating through the construct's eyes.
