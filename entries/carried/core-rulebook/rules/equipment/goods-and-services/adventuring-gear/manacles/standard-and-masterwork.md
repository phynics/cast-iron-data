Weight: 2 lbs.
Gear Type: Adventuring Gear
Hit Points: 10
Hardness: 10
Masterwork\: Break: DC 28 Strength Check
Masterwork\: Slip Free: DC 35 Escape Artist Check
Masterwork\: Price: 50 gp
Standard\: Break: DC 26 Strength Check
Standard\: Slip Free: DC 30 Escape Artist Check
Standard\: Price: 15 gp
:::

Manacles can bind a Medium creature. A manacled creature can use the Escape Artist skill to slip
free (DC 30, or DC 35 for masterwork manacles). Breaking the manacles requires a Strength check (DC
26, or DC 28 for masterwork manacles). Manacles have [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10 and 10 hit
points.

Most manacles have locks; add the cost of the lock you want to the cost of the manacles.

For the same cost, you can buy manacles for a Small creature. For a Large creature, manacles cost 10
times the indicated amount, and for a Huge creature, 100 times the indicated amount. Gargantuan,
Colossal, Tiny, Diminutive, and Fine creatures can be held only by specially made manacles, which
cost at least 100 times the indicated amount.
