::: statblock
Price: 1 sp
Weight: 1 lb.
Gear Type: Adventuring Gear
:::

A lamp illuminates a small area, providing normal light in a 15-foot radius and increasing the light
level by one step for an additional 15 feet beyond that area (darkness becomes dim light and dim
light becomes normal light). A lamp does not increase the light level in normal light or bright
light. A lamp burns for 6 hours on one pint of oil. You can carry a lamp in one hand.
