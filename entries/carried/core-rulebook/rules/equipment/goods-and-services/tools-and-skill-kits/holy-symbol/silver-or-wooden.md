A holy symbol focuses positive energy and is used by good clerics and paladins (or by neutral
clerics who want to cast good spells or channel positive energy). Each religion has its own holy
symbol.

## Unholy Symbols {#unholy-symbols}

An unholy symbol is like a holy symbol except that it focuses negative energy and is used by evil
clerics (or by neutral clerics who want to cast evil spells or channel negative energy).
