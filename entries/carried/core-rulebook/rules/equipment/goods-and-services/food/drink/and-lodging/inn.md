Poor\: Price: 2 sp/per day
Gear Type: Food, Drink, and Lodging
Weight: —
Good\: Price: 2 gp/per day
Common\: Price: 5 sp/per day
:::

Poor accommodations at an inn amount to a place on the floor near the hearth. Common accommodations
consist of a place on a raised, heated floor and the use of a blanket and a pillow. Good
accommodations consist of a small, private room with one bed, some amenities, and a covered chamber
pot in the corner.
