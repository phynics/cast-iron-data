A horse is suitable as a mount for a human, dwarf, elf, half-elf, or half-orc. A pony is smaller
than a horse and is a suitable mount for a gnome or halfling.

A war-trained horse can be ridden into combat without danger. See the Handle Animal skill for a list
of tricks known by horses and ponies with combat training.
