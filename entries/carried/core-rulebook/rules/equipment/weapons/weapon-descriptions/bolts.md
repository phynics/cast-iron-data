A crossbow bolt used as a melee weapon is treated as a light improvised weapon (–4 penalty on attack
rolls) and deals damage as a dagger of its size (crit ×2). Bolts come in a case or quiver that holds
10 bolts (or 5, for a repeating crossbow).
