## DESCRIPTION {#description}

This _+1 arrow_ is keyed to a particular type or subtype of creature. If it strikes such a creature,
the target must make a DC 20 Fortitude save or take 50 points of damage. Note that even creatures
normally exempt from Fortitude saves (undead and constructs) are subject to this attack. When keyed
to a living creature, this is a death effect (and thus _[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward)_ protects
a target). To determine the type or subtype of creature the arrow is keyed to, roll on the table
below.

A _greater slaying arrow_ functions just like a normal _slaying arrow_, but the DC to avoid the
death effect is 23 and the arrow deals 100 points of damage if the saving throw is failed.
