## DESCRIPTION {#description}

This _+3 adamantine heavy mace_ has a +5 enhancement bonus against constructs, and a successful
critical hit dealt to a construct completely destroys the construct (no saving throw). A critical
hit dealt to an outsider deals ×4 damage rather than ×2.
