School: abjuration
Level: 6: Cleric, Inquisitor, Oracle, Warpriest
Casting Time: 10 minutes
Components: V, S, M (powdered diamond worth 200 gp)
Range: touch
Target or Area: object touched or up to 5 sq. ft./level
Duration: permanent until discharged (D)
Saving Throw: see text
Spell Resistance: no (object) and yes; see text
:::

_As glyph of warding, but up to 10d8 damage or 6th-level spell._

This spell functions like _[glyph of warding](ref/spell/glyph-of-warding)_, except that a _greater
blast glyph_ deals up to 10d8 points of damage, and a _greater spell glyph_ can store a spell of 6th
level or lower.

_[Read magic](ref/spell/read-magic)_ allows you to identify a
_[greater glyph of warding](ref/spell/glyph-of-warding-greater)_ with a DC 16 Spellcraft check.

_Material Component_: You trace the glyph with incense, which must first be sprinkled with powdered
diamond worth at least 400 gp.
