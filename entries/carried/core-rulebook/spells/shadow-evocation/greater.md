_As shadow evocation, but up to 7th level and 60% real._

This spell functions like _[shadow evocation](ref/spell/shadow-evocation)_, except that it enables
you to create partially real, illusory versions of sorcerer or wizard evocation spells of 7th level
or lower. If recognized as a _[greater shadow evocation](ref/spell/shadow-evocation-greater)_, a
damaging spell deals only three-fifths (60%) damage.
