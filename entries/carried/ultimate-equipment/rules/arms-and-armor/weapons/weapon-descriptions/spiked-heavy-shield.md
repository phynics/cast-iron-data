::: paragraph statline
Spiked shields are intimidating weapons, and can have a single protruding central spike, razored
shield edges, or a whole forest of deadly protrusions. You can bash with a spiked heavy shield
instead of using it for defense. A spiked heavy shield can't be disarmed. See the armor spikes entry
on page 10 for details.
