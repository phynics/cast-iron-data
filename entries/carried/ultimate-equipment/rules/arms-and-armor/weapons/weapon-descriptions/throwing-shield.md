::: paragraph statline
This shield is designed for throwing and has specially designed straps that allow you to unclasp and
throw it as a free action. Tower shields cannot be throwing shields. Neither a shield's enhancement
bonus to AC nor its shield spikes apply on your attack or damage rolls. A throwing shield can't be
disarmed.
:::
