::: paragraph statline
Alternating diamonds and rubies stud this band of white gold. A _minor ring of fortitude_ reduces
ability damage or temporary ability penalties the wearer takes by 2 points and ability drain by 1
point. If an effect targets multiple ability scores (for example, exhaustion, fatigue, or
_[touch of idiocy](ref/spell/touch-of-idiocy)_), the ring reduces the damage, drain, or penalty for
all ability scores.
:::

::: paragraph statline
A _major ring of inner fortitude_ reduces ability damage or temporary penalties by 4 points and
reduces ability drain by 2 points.
:::

::: paragraph statline
A _greater ring of inner fortitude_ reduces ability damage or temporary penalties by 6 points and
reduces ability drain by 3 points.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Wearing a _ring of inner fortitude _ring does not make the wearer immune to conditions such as
fatigued or exhausted even if the wearer ignores all ability damage, drain, or penalties from the
condition.
:::
