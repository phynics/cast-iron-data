::: paragraph statline
Up to three times per day the wielder can cast a spell with the sonic descriptor that causes those
who take damage from that spell to take a –2 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks,
and ability checks for a number of rounds equal to the actual spell level of the spell cast as
though using the Concussive Spell feat.
:::
