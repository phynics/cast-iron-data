::: paragraph statline
This _+1 firearm bullet_ deals normal damage, but when it hits a living creature, it burrows into
the creature's flesh, causing wracking pain until removed or until the bullet burrows its way out of
the creature. While these bullets burrow, the creature is staggered. This effect lasts for 1d3
rounds or until the bullet is removed with a DC 15 Heal check made as a standard action. _Greater
burrowing bullets_ take longer to pass though the bodies of living creatures (the staggered effect
lasts 1d3+2 rounds) and are harder to remove (DC 20 Heal check as a standard action).
:::
