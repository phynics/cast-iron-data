## Specific Magic Armor {#specific-magic-armor}

The magic armor that intrepid heroes don in the face of danger is often the only thing that stands
between a brave adventurer and a blade to the chest. The following section includes armors with
unusual powers and magical abilities. Magic armors may have abilities that need to be activated to
function or powers that work passively and constantly.
