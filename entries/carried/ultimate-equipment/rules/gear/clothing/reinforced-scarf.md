One side of this 8-foot-long scarf is reinforced with chain links and metal plates. While not enough
to provide a benefit to Armor Class, these versatile scarves can be used like a length of chain to
climb short distances or bind an enemy. A reinforced scarf has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10 and 4
hit points. It can be burst with a successful DC 24 Strength check.
