::: paragraph statline
When lit, this foot-long wooden tube launches a flaming pyrotechnic “candle” every round for 4
rounds. The projectiles deal 1 point of nonlethal damage and 1 point of fire damage if they hit; on
a critical hit, the target is also blinded for 1 round. The projectiles shed light as candles for 1
round and have a range increment of 5 feet. Attacking with a star candle is a ranged touch attack
and always has a –4 nonproficiency penalty. Crafting this item is a DC 20 Craft (alchemy) check.
:::
