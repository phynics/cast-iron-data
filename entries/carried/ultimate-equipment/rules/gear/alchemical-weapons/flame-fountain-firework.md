::: paragraph statline
This hollow metal or wooden tube contains slow-burning powder. When you light the fuse (a move
action), it creates a 3-foot-long, blazing fountain of red-hot fiery sparks. You wield this fountain
of sparks as if it were a heavy mace. Attacks with the fountain are melee touch attacks. The
fountain deals 1d8 points of fire damage (your Strength modifier does not apply to the damage).
:::

::: paragraph statline
The fountain sheds light as a torch and can ignite combustible materials such as parchment, straw,
dry sticks, and cloth. Once ignited, it lasts for 1 minute. If extinguished before this time, the
remaining duration is wasted. Crafting this item is a DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.
:::
