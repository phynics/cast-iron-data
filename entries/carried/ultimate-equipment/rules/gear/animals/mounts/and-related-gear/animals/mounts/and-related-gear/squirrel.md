and vegetation. Squirrels have the same statistics as rats.
:::

## Flying squirrel {#flying-squirrel}

::: paragraph statline
These Tiny squirrels use a membrane of skin between their front and hind legs to glide. They
sometimes serve spellcasters as familiars.
:::
