## Animals, Mounts, and Related Gear {#animals-mounts-and-related-gear}

The animals, mounts, and related gear in this section can be found in most large cities. Based on
its location, a settlement might not have all of these animals or related gear available at a given
time (as the GM deems fit).

The tables for this section are split up into five categories: Pets and Familiars, Guard and Hunting
Animals, Farm and Work Animals, Mounts, and Animal-Related Gear. Animals that fit into more than one
category appear on multiple lists.
