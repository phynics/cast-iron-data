## Type {#type}

|Type|Price|Weight|
| --- | --- | --- |
|Common|15 gp|2 lbs.|
|Masterwork|50 gp|2 lbs.|
|Mithral|1,000 gp|2 lbs.|

::: paragraph statline
Manacles can bind a Medium creature. A manacled creature can use the Escape Artist skill to slip
free (DC 30, or DC 35 for masterwork manacles). Breaking the manacles requires a Strength check (DC
26, or DC 28 for masterwork manacles). Manacles have [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10 and 10 hit
points.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Most manacles have locks; add the cost of the lock you want to the price of the manacles.
:::

::: paragraph statline
For the same price, you can buy manacles for a Small creature. For a Large creature, manacles cost
10 times the indicated amount, and for a Huge creature, 100 times the indicated amount. Gargantuan,
Colossal, Tiny, Diminutive, and Fine creatures can be held only by specially made manacles, which
cost at least 100 times the indicated amount.
:::

## Mithral Manacles {#mithral-manacles}

::: paragraph statline
These bindings are more difficult to break than standard manacles and are particularly useful
against lycanthropes. They have [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 15, 30 hit points, and a break DC
of 30. They are otherwise treated as masterwork manacles
:::
