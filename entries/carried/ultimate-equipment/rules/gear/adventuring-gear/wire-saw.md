::: paragraph statline
This flexible saw consists of an abrasive wire hooked to two metal rings. By feeding the wire around
a deadbolt or small door bar, you can use the rings to quietly saw through the obstruction. The saw
takes 10 minutes to cut through a 2-inch piece of wood or a 1-inch iron bar. A wire saw can cut
through items with [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10 or less. After five uses, the abrasive wire is
worn smooth and must be replaced (costing 25 gp).
:::

## Adamantine Wire Saw {#adamantine-wire-saw}

::: paragraph statline
This works twice as fast as a normal saw when cutting materials with [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10
or less, or at normal speed when cutting materials with hardness 11–20. Replacing an adamantine wire
saw's wire costs 145 gp.
:::
