These clothes and outfits can be useful to PCs and NPCs who want to dress for special occasions,
such as braving an arctic wasteland, attending a gala, sleuthing around an urban setting, or any
other situation where specific garb might be beneficial or fitting.

All characters begin play with one outfit valued at 10 gp or less. Additional outfits can be
purchased normally.
