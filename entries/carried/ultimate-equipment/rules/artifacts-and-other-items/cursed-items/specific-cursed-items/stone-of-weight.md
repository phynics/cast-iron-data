::: paragraph statline
This dark, polished stone reduces the possessor's base land speed by half. Once picked up, the stone
cannot be disposed of by any nonmagical means—if it is thrown away or smashed, it reappears
somewhere on the possessor's person. If a _[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse)_ spell is cast
upon a _loadstone, _the item may be discarded normally and no longer haunts the individual.
:::
