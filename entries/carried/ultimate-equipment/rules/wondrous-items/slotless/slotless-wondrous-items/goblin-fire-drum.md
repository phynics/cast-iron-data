::: paragraph statline
This small crude-looking drum is attached to a strap long enough to loop around the wearer's neck. A
successful DC 12 Perform (percussion) check activates the drum, which enhances any nearby natural or
magical fire as long as the bearer spends a move action each round to continue playing. All fires
(including fire spells and effects) within 30 feet of the bearer deal an additional +1 point of fire
damage per die (maximum +10).
:::

::: paragraph statline
All unattended flammable alchemical objects such as tindertwigs, flasks of oil or alchemist's fire,
and fireworks within this range have a 10% chance of spontaneously igniting or exploding (as
appropriate to the item) when the drum activates. This chance increases by 5% each round and is
checked at the start of the performer's turn as long as the playing continues.
:::

::: paragraph statline
A _greater goblin fire drum_ has all the above abilities and can _create pyrotechnics_ once per day.
:::
