## Headband Slot Wondrous Items {#headband-slot-wondrous-items}

The headband slot consists of bands, headbands, phylacteries, and other non-head-slot items that can
be worn on the head.

Anyone can use a head slot wondrous item unless specified otherwise by its description. These
wondrous items are usually use-activated or triggered by a command word, but details vary from item
to item.
