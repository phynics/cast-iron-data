## Belt Slot Wondrous Items {#belt-slot-wondrous-items}

The belt category includes belts, scabbards and other items worn on or around the waist.

Anyone can use a belt slot wondrous item unless specified otherwise by its description. These
wondrous items are usually use-activated or triggered by a command word, but details vary from item
to item.
