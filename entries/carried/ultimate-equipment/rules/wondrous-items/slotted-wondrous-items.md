When a character wears a slotted wondrous item he cannot gain the benefit from a wondrous items of
the same slot until the first item is removed. Of course, a character may carry or possess any
number of slotted items of the same type, but additional items have no effect until they are worn.
Sometimes a slotted wondrous item must be worn for a period of time (typically 24 hours) before the
item's full effect manifests. If a creature is missing the body part where a wondrous item is worn,
it cannot use that type of slotted item. If a creature has multiple body parts that correspond to a
single wondrous item slot, it can still only gain the benefit of one item of that slot.

## Belt {#belt}

This slot consists of belts and other items that can be worn around the waist.

## Body {#body}

This slot consists of body wraps, cassocks, corsets, dusters, harnesses, robes, vestments and any
other article of clothing that can be worn on the body.

## Chest {#chest}

This slot consists of jackets, mantels, shirts, vests and other items that can be worn around the
torso or chest.

## Eyes {#eyes}

This slot consists of goggles, lenses, monocles, spectacles, and other items that can be worn over
the eyes.

## Feet {#feet}

This slot consists of boots, horseshoes, sandals, shoes, slippers, and other items that can be worn
on the feet.

## Hands {#hands}

This slot consists of gauntlets, gloves, and other items that can worn on the hands.

## Head {#head}

This slot consists of circlets, crowns, hats, helms, hoods, masks, and other items that can be worn
on the head.

## Headband {#headband}

This slot consists of bands, headbands, laurels, phylacteries, and other non-head slot items that
can be worn around the forehead.

## Neck {#neck}

This slot consists of amulets, brooches, medallions, necklaces, periapts, scarabs, and other items
that can be worn around the neck or fastened to a cloak.

## Shoulders {#shoulders}

This slot consists of capes, cloaks, cords, mantels, pauldrons, shawls, stoles, wings, and other
items that can be worn on the shoulders.

## Wrists {#wrists}

This slot consists of armbands, bracelets, bracers, gauntlets, manacles, shackles, vambraces, and
other items that can worn over the wrists.
