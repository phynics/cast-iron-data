Wondrous items not worn in one of the magic item slots are called “slotless” wondrous items.
Sometimes these items take the form of trinkets, like the _figurines of wondrous power._ other times
they are larger items, such as the _carpet of flying_. Typically the possession of such an item is
enough to gain its benefit, but sometimes one must manipulate and activate the item. Slotless
wondrous items are the largest category and most diverse of the wondrous items.
