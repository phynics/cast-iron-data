## Feet Slot Wondrous Items {#feet-slot-wondrous-items}

The feet slot consists of boots, horseshoes, sandals, shoes, slippers, and other items that can be
worn on the feet.

Anyone can use a feet slot wondrous item unless specified otherwise by its description. These
wondrous items are usually activated by use or by a command word, but details vary from item to
item.
