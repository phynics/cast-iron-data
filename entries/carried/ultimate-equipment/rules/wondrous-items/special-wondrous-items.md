Some wondrous items are intelligent, while others provide some clue as to their function. Roll d%. A
roll of 01 indicates the wondrous item is intelligent, 02–31 indicates that something (a design,
inscription, or the like) provides a clue to its function (or if the item is command word-activated,
that it has the command word or words written on it), and 32–100 indicates no special qualities.
Intelligent items have extra abilities and sometimes extraordinary powers and special purposes (see
Intelligent Items). Wondrous items with charges can never be intelligent.
