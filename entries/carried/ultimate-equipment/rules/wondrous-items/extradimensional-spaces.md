A number of spells and magic items utilize extradimensional spaces, such as
_[rope trick](ref/spell/rope-trick)_, _bags of holding_, _handy haversacks_, and _portable holes_.
These spells and magic items create a tiny pocket space that does not exist in any dimension. Such
items do not function, however, inside another extradimensional space. If placed inside such a
space, they cease to function until removed from the extradimensional space. For example, if a _bag
of holding_ is brought into a _[rope trick](ref/spell/rope-trick)_, the contents of the _bag of
holding_ become inaccessible until the _bag of holding_ is taken outside the
_[rope trick](ref/spell/rope-trick)_. the only exception to this is when a _bag of holding_ and a
_portable hole_ interact, forming a rift to the astral plane, as noted in their descriptions.
