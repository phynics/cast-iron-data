## Body Slot Wondrous Items {#body-slot-wondrous-items}

The body slot consists of robes, vestments, and items that can be worn on or around the body.

Anyone can use a body slot wondrous item unless specified otherwise by its description. These
wondrous items are usually use-activated or triggered by a command word, but details vary from item
to item.
