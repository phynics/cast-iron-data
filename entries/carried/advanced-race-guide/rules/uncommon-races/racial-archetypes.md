This section presents a single racial archetype for each of the uncommon races. Typically, only
members of the section's race can take the listed archetype. An archetype usually features a
thematic link to the race, granting it class features that complement the abilities and the
background of the race. Because adventurers are often societal outliers, sometimes these archetypes
feature a theme that is the exception to the norm for racial tendencies. At the GM's discretion, a
member of another race can take most of these archetypes either because they fit a character concept
or because the character was raised or trained by a member of the race that can typically select the
archetype. Such exceptions should be rare for the archetypes detailed in this chapter, however,
since these races are not common in most campaigns. The following is a list of all the archetypes
featured in this chapter, listed by race. The class for each archetype is listed in parentheses.
