Each race's entry features a sidebar listing the race's standard racial traits. This information
includes the race's type, size, vision, and base speed, as well as a number of other traits common
to most members of the race. With your GM's permission, you will also have the option to exchange
these standard racial traits for a number of alternate racial traits, the rules for which are
provided in the section below.
