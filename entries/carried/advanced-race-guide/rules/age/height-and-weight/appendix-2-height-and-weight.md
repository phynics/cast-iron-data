To determine a character's height, roll the modifier dice indicated on the appropriate Random Height
& Weight table and add the result, in inches, to the base height for your character's race and
gender. To determine a character's weight, multiply the result of the modifier dice by the weight
multiplier and add the result to the base weight for your character's race and gender.
