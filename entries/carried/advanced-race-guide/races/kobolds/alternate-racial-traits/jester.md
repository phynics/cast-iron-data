Some kobolds swallow their pride and survive by groveling, placating, and amusing the powerful.
Kobolds with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Diplomacy and Perform checks. Diplomacy and
Perform are always class skills for them. This racial trait replaces crafty.
