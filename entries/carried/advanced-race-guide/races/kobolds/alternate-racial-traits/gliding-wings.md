Some kobolds are born with wings that, while too weak for actual flying, do allow them to fall at a
very slow and safe pace. A kobold with wings can use them to glide. It can make a DC 15 Fly check to
fall safely from any height without taking falling damage, as if using
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_. When falling safely, it may make an additional DC 15 Fly
check to glide, moving 5 feet laterally for every 20 feet it falls. This racial trait replaces
crafty.
