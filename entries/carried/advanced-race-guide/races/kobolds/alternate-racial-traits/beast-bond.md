Some kobolds have a talent for training animals and beasts to help them both on and off the
battlefield. Kobolds with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Handle Animal and Ride checks.
Handle Animal and Ride are always class skills for them. This racial trait replaces crafty.
