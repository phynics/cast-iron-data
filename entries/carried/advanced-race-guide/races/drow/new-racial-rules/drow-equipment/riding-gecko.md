Larger than even what is commonly referred to as a giant gecko, these mammoth lizards have been
specifically bred to be used as mounts for the drow. Prized for their ability to run along cave
ceilings and sheer walls, these creatures require exotic saddles to ride. Use the statistics for a
giant gecko with the giant template.
