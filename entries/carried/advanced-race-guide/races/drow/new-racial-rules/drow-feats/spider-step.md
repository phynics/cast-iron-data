::: statblock
Type: General
Prerequisites: Character level 3rd, drow.
:::

You tread where only arachnids dare.

## Benefit {#benefit}

::: paragraph statline
You can cast _[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb)_ once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using your character level as the caster level.
Furthermore, you gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against the web special attacks of spiders and the
effects of _web_ and other similar spells (such as the _[web cloud](ref/spell/web-cloud)_ spell).
:::
