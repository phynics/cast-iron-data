Some drow dwell close to the surface lands, either because they serve drow causes or they were
exiled. Drow with this racial trait gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), allowing
them to see twice as far as humans in conditions of dim light. This racial trait replaces the
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) and light blindness racial traits.
