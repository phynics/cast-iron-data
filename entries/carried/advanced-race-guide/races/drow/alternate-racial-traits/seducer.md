Certain drow possess an innate understanding of the darkest desires that lurk in every heart. Drow
with this racial trait add +1 to the saving throw DCs for spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of the enchantment school. In addition, drow
with a Wisdom score of 15 or higher may use _[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)_ once per day as
a spell-like ability (caster level equal to the drow's character level). This racial trait replaces
drow immunities.
