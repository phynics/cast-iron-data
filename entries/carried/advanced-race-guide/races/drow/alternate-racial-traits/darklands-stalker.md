The lands outside of drow cities, from rough-hewn tunnels to rocky caverns, are treacherous to
navigate. Drow with this racial trait may move through difficult terrain without penalty while
underground. In addition, drow with a Dexterity of 13 or higher gain Nimble Moves as a bonus feat.
This racial trait replaces the [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
