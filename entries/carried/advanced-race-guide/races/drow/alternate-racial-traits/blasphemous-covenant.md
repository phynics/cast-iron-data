Since their twisted beginnings, the drow have consorted with demons. Some drow have strong ties with
these creatures and may call upon ancient and obscene associations to sway demonic cooperation. Drow
with this racial trait gain a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks made against unbound creatures with the
demon subtype. Furthermore, demons conjured with any
_[summon](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/summon)_ spell gain +2 hit points per
Hit Die. Lastly, the cost of bribes or offerings for any _[planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally)_
spell cast by these drow to summon a demon is reduced by 20%. This racial trait replaces keen senses
and poison use.
