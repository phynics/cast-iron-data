Hobgoblin slavers excel at tracking down runaway slaves and surviving in filthy conditions.
Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Survival checks and a +2 racial bonus on
Fortitude saves against disease. This racial trait replaces sneaky.
