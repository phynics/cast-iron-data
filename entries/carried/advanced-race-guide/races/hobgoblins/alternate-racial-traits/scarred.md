A hobgoblin can scar himself with both blade and fire to toughen his hide into a mass of horny
scars. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain a +1 natural armor bonus to Armor Class. However, the
repeated exposure to fire permanently damages their eyes. This racial trait replaces the
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) racial trait.
