Hobgoblins hate and fear arcane casters. A magehunter gains a +2 racial bonus on Spellcraft checks
made to identify a spell being cast and a +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against arcane
spellcasters. He only gains this bonus against creatures that use spells, and not against those that
only use [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This racial trait replaces sneaky.
