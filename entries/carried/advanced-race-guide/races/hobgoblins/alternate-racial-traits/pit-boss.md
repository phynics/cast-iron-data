Slave blood fuels the hobgoblin war machine. Pit bosses extract the last breath of labor from their
charges with a liberal touch of the lash. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain proficiency with
whips and a +1 racial bonus on combat maneuver checks made to disarm or trip with a whip. This
racial trait replaces sneaky.
