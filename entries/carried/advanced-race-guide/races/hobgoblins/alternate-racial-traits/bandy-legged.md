Bandy-legged hobgoblins have an even more exaggerated stature than other hobgoblins, with bowed legs
and massive shoulders. Hobgoblins with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Climb and Ride
checks, and a +2 racial bonus to their CMD against bull rush or trip attempts while on solid ground.
The base speed of bandy-legged hobgoblins is reduced to 20 feet.
