Hobgoblins who failed to secure a position in the military hold the lowest status in hobgoblin
society, and quickly learn the value of currying favor with their betters. Hobgoblins with this
racial trait gain proficiency in a single martial weapon and +1 racial bonus on Bluff and Diplomacy
checks. This racial trait replaces sneaky.
