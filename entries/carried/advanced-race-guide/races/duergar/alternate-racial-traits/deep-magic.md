Duergar spellcasters labor long to overcome the inborn [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance)
held by so many of their underground foes. Duergar with this racial trait receive a +2 racial bonus
on caster level checks made to overcome spell resistance and a +2 racial bonus on dispel checks.
This racial trait replaces the _[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_ and _invisibility_
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).
