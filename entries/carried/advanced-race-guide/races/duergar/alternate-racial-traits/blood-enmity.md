Duergar have long warred against their dwarven cousins and the hated drow. Duergar with this racial
trait receive a +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against humanoid creatures of the dwarf or elf
subtypes. This racial trait replaces the _invisibility_
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).
