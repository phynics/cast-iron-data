Raised with little or no proper food, many goblins have learned to survive by eating whatever they
happen across and can digest nearly anything without getting sick. Goblins with this trait gain a +4
on Survival checks to forage for food and a +4 racial bonus on saves versus effects that cause the
nauseated or sickened conditions. This racial trait replaces skilled.
