Goblins' traditional weapons are the dogslicer and the horsechopper, weapons designed specifically
to bring down their most hated foes. Goblins with this trait are proficient with the dogslicer and
the horsechopper, and treat any weapon with the word "goblin" in it as martial weapons. This racial
trait replaces skilled.
