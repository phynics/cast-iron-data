Goblins are known for their balloonlike heads and enormous maws, but some have even more
exaggeratedly large heads filled with razor-sharp teeth. Goblins with this trait gain a bite attack
as a primary natural attack that deals 1d4 points of damage. This racial trait replaces skilled.
