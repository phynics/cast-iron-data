Used most often by gnome sheriffs and rangers, a sheriff's whistle gives off a piercing shriek that
can easily be heard up to 1,000 feet away under ordinary circumstances. However, the frequency of
the shriek is such that it can only be heard by badgers, dogs, and gnomes.
