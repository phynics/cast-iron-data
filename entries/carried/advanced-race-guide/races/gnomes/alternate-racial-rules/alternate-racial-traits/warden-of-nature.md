Gnomes must often protect their homes against unnatural or pestilential infestations. Gnomes with
this racial trait gain a +2 dodge bonus to AC against aberrations, oozes, and vermin, and a +1 bonus
on attack rolls against them because of their special training. This racial trait replaces defensive
training and hatred.
