Gnomes experiment with all manner of mechanical devices. Gnomes with this racial trait gain a +1
bonus on Disable Device and Knowledge (engineering) checks. They are treated as proficient with any
weapon they have personally crafted. This racial trait replaces defensive training and hatred.
