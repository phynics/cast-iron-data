Some gnomes have strong ties to specific kinds of terrain, as a holdover from their fey origins.
These gnomes gain a +2 dodge bonus to AC when in a specific terrain type selected from the ranger
list of favored terrains. This choice is made at character creation, and cannot be changed. This
racial trait replaces defensive training and hatred.
