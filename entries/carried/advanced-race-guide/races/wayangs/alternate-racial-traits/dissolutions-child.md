Once per day, you may change your appearance to look as if you were little more than a 4-foot-tall
area of shadow. Your physical form still exists and you are not incorporeal—only your appearance
changes. This works like _invisibility_, except it only lasts 1 round per level (maximum 5 rounds).
This is a supernatural ability. This racial trait replaces shadow magic.
