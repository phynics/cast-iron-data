Some tengus can use their feathered arms and legs to glide. Tengus with this racial trait can make a
DC 15 Fly check to fall safely from any height without taking falling damage, as if using
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_. When falling safely, a tengu may make an additional DC 15
Fly check to glide, moving 5 feet laterally for every 20 feet he falls. This racial trait replaces
gifted linguist.
