Tengus with this racial trait have learned to use their claws as natural weapons. They gain two claw
attacks as primary natural attacks that deal 1d3 points of damage, and are treated as having the
Improved Unarmed Strike feat for the purpose of qualifying for other feats. This racial trait
replaces swordtrained.
