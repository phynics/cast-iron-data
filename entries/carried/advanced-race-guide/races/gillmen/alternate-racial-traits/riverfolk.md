Some gillmen groups live in colonies along vast riverways, and have adapted to living on land for
much longer periods. Gillmen with this trait have a thin coating of natural oil that keeps their
skin from cracking even without water. However, this natural oil also makes such gillmen
particularly susceptible to flames, and they gain vulnerability to fire. This racial trait replaces
water dependent.
