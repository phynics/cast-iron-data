Some gillmen are throwbacks to their land-dwelling human ancestors. Gillmen with this racial trait
do not have the amphibious trait, have the human subtype instead of the aquatic subtype, have no
swim speed or bonuses to the Swim skill, cannot breathe water, and do not have the water dependent
racial trait.
