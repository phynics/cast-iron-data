Gillmen with this trait are from lineages that have fought against aboleths since the aberrations
rescued their human ancestors. They receive a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against aboleth
spells, [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural abilities. This
racial trait replaces enchantment resistance.
