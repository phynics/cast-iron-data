A few half-elves with drow ancestry exhibit the innate magic of that race. Half-elves with this
trait have drow blood somewhere in their background, and can cast
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_, _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_, and
_[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire)_ each once per day, using the half-elf's character level as
the caster level for these [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This racial trait
replaces the adaptability and multitalented racial traits.
