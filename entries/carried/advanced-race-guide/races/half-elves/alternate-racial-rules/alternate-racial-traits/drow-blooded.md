Some half-elves born of drow parents exhibit more drow traits than others—particularly many of the
physical features of the drow—and have [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and light
blindness. This racial trait replaces the [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) racial
trait.
