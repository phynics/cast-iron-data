The eyes of some undines are especially adapted to the lightless depths of the oceans, but not to
air-filled environments. An undine with this racial trait has [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 120
feet when underwater, but otherwise has no darkvision at all. This racial trait replaces darkvision.
