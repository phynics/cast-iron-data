Some undines can change their coloration to blend in with underwater terrain, mixing browns, grays,
and greens to resemble kelp or other natural water plants. As a standard action, an undine with this
racial trait can change her coloration, gaining a +4 bonus on Stealth checks in underwater
environments. She can return to normal as a free action. This racial trait replaces energy
resistance.
