Some undines can trace their ancestry to nereids as well as to outsiders. Once per day as a standard
action, such an undine can create a 20-foot-radius aura that causes humanoids within the aura's
range to become fascinated with her for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 the undine's character level
(minimum 1). Targets may resist with a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 the undine's level + the undine's
Charisma modifier). This is a supernatural ability. This racial trait replaces the
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
