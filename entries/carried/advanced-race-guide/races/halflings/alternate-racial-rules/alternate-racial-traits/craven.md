While most halflings are fearless, some are skittish, making them particularly alert. Halflings with
this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on initiative checks and a +1 bonus on attack rolls when flanking.
They take a –2 penalty on saves against fear effects and gain no benefit from morale bonuses on such
saves. When affected by a fear effect, their base speed increases by 10 feet and they gain a +1
dodge bonus to Armor Class. This racial trait replaces fearless and halfling luck.
