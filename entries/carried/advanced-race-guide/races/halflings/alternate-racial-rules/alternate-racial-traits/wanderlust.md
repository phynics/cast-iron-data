Halflings love travel and maps. Halflings with this racial trait receive a +2 bonus on Knowledge
(geography) and Survival checks. When casting spells or using abilities that provide or enhance
movement, halflings treat their caster level as +1 higher than normal. This racial trait replaces
fearless and halfling luck.
