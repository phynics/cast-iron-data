Halflings have a reputation for larceny and guile—and sometimes it's well deserved. Halflings with
this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Bluff and Sleight of Hand checks, and Sleight of Hand is
always a class skill for them. This racial trait replaces sure-footed.
