Some halflings, especially those who spend a lot of time traveling, develop a talent for learning
new languages. These halflings gain a +2 racial bonus on Linguistics checks, and it is always a
class skill for them. Halflings with this racial trait also begin play with the ability to speak
Common, Halfling, and any one other language of their choice (except for secret languages, such as
Druidic) in addition to bonus languages due to high Intelligence. They still gain the normal list of
halfling bonus languages. This racial trait replaces keen senses and alters the halfling language
racial trait.
