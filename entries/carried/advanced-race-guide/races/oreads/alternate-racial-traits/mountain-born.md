Oreads are drawn to mountains and other high places, and after many generations they've grown well
suited to their environment. Oreads with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Acrobatics
checks made to cross narrow ledges and on saves against altitude fatigue and sickness. This racial
trait replaces the [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
