Oread spellcasters sometimes find that their elemental heritage makes creatures of earth more
willing to serve them. _[Summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ and
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spells that such oreads cast last 2 rounds
longer than normal when used to summon creatures with the earth subtype. This racial trait replaces
earth affinity.
