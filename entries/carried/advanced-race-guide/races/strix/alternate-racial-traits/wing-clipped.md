The flight of wing-clipped strix is weaker than normal, whether from deformity or injury. Their fly
speed is 20 feet (poor) instead of the normal fly speed, and they must make a DC 30 Fly check to fly
upward. Ostracized by their tribes and forced to deal with other races, these strix compensate for
their weakness by gaining a +2 racial bonus on Bluff, Climb, and Diplomacy checks.
