Elves who dwell in the most extreme environments, from arctic wastelands to volcanic plains, develop
natural resistance to the dangers of their homelands over the course of a few generations. Elves
with this racial trait gain elemental resistance 5 to acid, cold, electricity, or fire. This choice
is made at character creation, and once made it cannot be changed. This racial trait replaces elven
immunities.
