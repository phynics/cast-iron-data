While all elves are naturally lithe and agile, some also are naturally speedy and have a strong
desire to rush into situations rather than worrying about looking ahead. Elves with this racial
trait receive Run as a bonus feat and a +2 racial bonus on initiative checks. This racial trait
replaces keen senses and weapon familiarity.
