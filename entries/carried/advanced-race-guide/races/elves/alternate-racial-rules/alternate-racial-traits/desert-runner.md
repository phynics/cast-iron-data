Some elves thrive in the deepest deserts, forever roaming across burned and parched lands. Elves
with this racial trait receive a +4 racial bonus on Constitution checks and Fortitude saves to avoid
fatigue, exhaustion, or ill effects from running, forced marches, starvation, thirst, or hot or cold
environments. This racial trait replaces elven magic.
