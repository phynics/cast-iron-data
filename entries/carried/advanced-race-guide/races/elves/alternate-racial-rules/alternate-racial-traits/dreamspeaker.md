A few elves have the ability to tap into the power of sleep, dreams, and prescient reverie. Elves
with this racial trait add +1 to the saving throw DCs of spells of the divination school and sleep
effects they cast. In addition, elves with Charisma scores of 15 or higher may use
_[dream](ref/spell/dream)_ once per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
(caster level is equal to the elf's character level). This racial trait replaces elven immunities.
