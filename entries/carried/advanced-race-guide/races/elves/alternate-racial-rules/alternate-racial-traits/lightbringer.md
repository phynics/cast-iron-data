Many elves revere the sun, moon, and stars, but some are literally infused with the radiant power of
the heavens. Elves with this racial trait are immune to light-based blindness and dazzle effects,
and are treated as one level higher when determining the effects of any light-based spell or effect
they cast (including spell-like and supernatural abilities). Elves with Intelligence scores of 10 or
higher may use _[light](ref/spell/light)_ at will as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This racial trait replaces the elven immunities
and elven magic racial traits.
