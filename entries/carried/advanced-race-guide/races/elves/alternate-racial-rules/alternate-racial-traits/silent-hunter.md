Elves are renowned for their subtlety and skill. Elves with this racial trait reduce the penalty for
using Stealth while moving by 5 and can make Stealth checks while running at a –20 penalty (this
number includes the penalty reduction from this racial trait). This racial trait replaces elven
magic.
