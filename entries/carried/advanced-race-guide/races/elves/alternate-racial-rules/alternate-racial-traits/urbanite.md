Elves who live in cities for more than a century can grow to know the ebb and flow of social
situations just as their forest-dwelling cousins know the rules of the wild. Elves with this racial
trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Diplomacy checks made to gather information and Sense Motive checks
made to get a hunch about a social situation. This racial trait replaces keen senses.
