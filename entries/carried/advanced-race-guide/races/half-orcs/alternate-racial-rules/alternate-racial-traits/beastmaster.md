Some half-orcs have a spiritual kinship with fantastical beasts, capturing them for sport or living
and hunting with them. A half-orc with this trait treats whips and nets as martial weapons and gains
a +2 racial bonus on Handle Animal checks. This racial trait replaces orc ferocity.
