Some half-orcs have escaped from slavery and reforged the chains of their imprisonment into deadly
weapons. Half-orcs with this racial trait are proficient with flails and heavy flails, and treat
dire flails and spiked chains as martial weapons. This racial trait replaces weapon familiarity.
