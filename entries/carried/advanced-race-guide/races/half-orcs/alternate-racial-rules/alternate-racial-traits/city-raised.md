Half-orcs with this trait know little of their orc ancestry and were raised among humans and other
half-orcs in a large city. City-raised half-orcs are proficient with whips and longswords, and
receive a +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (local) checks. This racial trait replaces weapon
familiarity.
