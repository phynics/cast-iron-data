On occasion, a dhampir may inherit his father's lengthy canines. Whenever the dhampir makes a
grapple combat maneuver check to damage an opponent, he can choose to bite his opponent, dealing 1d3
points of damage as if using a natural bite attack. As a standard action, the dhampir can bite a
creature that is bound, helpless, paralyzed, or similarly unable to defend itself. This racial trait
replaces the [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
