A few fortunate dhampirs were born during the day under the blessings of priests, and their blood
has weaker ties to their undead bloodline than others of their kind. Such dhampirs are unhindered by
daylight and lose the light sensitivity racial trait. This racial trait replaces the
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
