Some catfolk have an amazing sense of balance and keen knowledge of their own center of gravity.
Catfolk with this trait land on their feet even when they take lethal damage from a fall.
Furthermore, catfolk with this trait gain a +1 bonus to their CMD against trip maneuvers. This
racial trait replaces sprinter.
