Catfolk's generally friendly disposition doesn't preclude craftiness. Some of them see social
obstacles as games to be played and won. These catfolk receive a +2 racial bonus on Bluff,
Diplomacy, and Sense Motive checks. This racial trait replaces natural hunter.
