Catfolk are naturally inquisitive about the world around them, though some are more curious than
others. Such catfolk gain a +4 bonus on Diplomacy checks to gather information, and Knowledge
(history) and Knowledge (local) are always class skills for them. If they choose a class that has
either of these Knowledge skills as class skills, they gain a +2 racial bonus on those skills
instead. This racial trait replaces natural hunter.
