Some catfolk have stronger and more developed claws than other members of their race, and can use
them to make attacks. Catfolk with this racial trait have a pair of claws they can use as natural
weapons. These claws are primary attacks that deal 1d4 points of damage. This racial trait replaces
natural hunter.
