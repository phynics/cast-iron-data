Orcs refuse to yield to any foe, including the sun. Some spend hour upon hour glaring at the sun
until their ruined eyes acclimatize to bright light. Orcs with this racial trait take a –2 penalty
on all ranged attack rolls. This racial trait replaces light sensitivity.
