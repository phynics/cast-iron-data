Some tieflings have feet that are more bestial than human. Whether their feet resemble those of a
clawed predator or are the cloven hooves common to many of their kind, tieflings with this trait
gain a 10-foot racial bonus to their speed when using the charge, run, or withdraw actions. This
racial trait replaces skilled.
