Rare tieflings have a peculiar sight that allows them to see the state of a creature's soul. They
can use _[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch)_ at will as
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This racial trait replaces the spell-like
ability and fiendish sorcery racial traits.
