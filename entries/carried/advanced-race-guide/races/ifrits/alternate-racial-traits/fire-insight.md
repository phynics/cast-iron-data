Ifrit spellcasters sometimes find that their elemental heritage makes creatures of fire more willing
to serve them. _[Summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ and
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spells that the ifrit casts last 2 rounds
longer than normal when used to summon creatures with the fire subtype. This racial trait replaces
fire affinity.
