Ifrits thrive in the deserts of the world, where their keen instincts and resistance to heat give
them a huge edge over their competitors. Those with this trait gain a +2 racial bonus on Stealth
checks in desert environments and on saves to resist starvation and thirst. This racial trait
replaces fire affinity.
