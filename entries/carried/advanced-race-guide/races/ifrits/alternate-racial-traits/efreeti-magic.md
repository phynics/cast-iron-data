Some ifrits inherit an efreeti ancestor's ability to magically change a creature's size. They can
cast either _[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_ or
_[reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person)_ (the ifrit chooses when using this ability) once per day
as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (caster level equals the ifrit's level). The
ifrit can use this ability to affect other ifrits as though they were humanoid creatures. This
racial trait replaces the spell-like ability racial trait.
