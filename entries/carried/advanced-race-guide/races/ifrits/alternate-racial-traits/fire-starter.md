Ifrits with this racial trait derive sadistic satisfaction from watching others burn. Anytime the
ifrit causes a creature to catch fire, he gains a +1 morale bonus on the next single attack roll,
saving throw, skill check, or ability check that he makes in the next round. The ifrit only gains
this bonus the first time he causes a particular creature to catch fire; subsequent times the
creature catches fire provide no bonus. This racial trait replaces fire affinity.
