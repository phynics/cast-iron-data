Not all ifrits are descended from efreet—some instead descend from azers or even salamanders. Such
ifrits gain a +2 racial bonus on Craft (armor and weapons) checks and saves to resist fatigue and
exhaustion. This racial trait replaces the [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial
trait.
