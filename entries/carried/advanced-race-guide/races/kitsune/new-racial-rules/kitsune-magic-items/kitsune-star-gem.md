## Description {#description}

This magical jewel glows like _[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire)_, shedding light as a candle. It
acts as _pearl of power_, except it only allows a kitsune to recover a spent use of a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of the appropriate spell level.
