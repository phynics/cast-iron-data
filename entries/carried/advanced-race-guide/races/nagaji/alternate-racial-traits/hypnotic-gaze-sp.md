The nagaji's gaze is so intense it stops others in their tracks. Once per day, it can attempt to
hypnotize a single target, as per the spell _[hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism)_ (caster level equal
to the nagaji's Hit Dice). The DC of this effect is equal to 11 + the nagaji's Charisma modifier.
The effects of the hypnotic gaze only last a single round. This racial trait replaces serpent's
sense.
