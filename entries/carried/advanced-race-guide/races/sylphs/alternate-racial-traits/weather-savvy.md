Some sylphs are so in tune with the air and sky that they can sense the slightest change in
atmospheric conditions. Sylphs with this trait can spend a full-round action to predict the weather
in an area for the next 24 hours. The sylph's prediction is always accurate, but cannot account for
spells or supernatural effects that might alter the forecast. This racial trait replaces the sylph's
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
