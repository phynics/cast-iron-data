Some sylphs are especially thin and wispy, as though they were made more of air than flesh. Sylphs
with this racial trait gain a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks. This racial trait replaces the
sylph's [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
