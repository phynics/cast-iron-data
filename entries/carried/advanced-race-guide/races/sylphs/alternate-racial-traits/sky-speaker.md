Sylphs with this racial trait feel kinship toward the creatures of the air, and can use
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ once per day to speak to birds or other flying
animals. Her caster level for these effects is equal to her level. This racial trait replaces the
sylph's [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial trait.
