Fetchlings who spend their time studying the subtle magic of their adopted plane gain arcane
insights on the use of shadow spells. These fetchlings gain a +1 racial bonus to the DC of any
illusion (shadow) spells they cast. This racial trait replaces the skilled racial trait.
