Rather than taking on the forms of others, some fetchling are adept at destroying the memories of
other creatures. Instead of gaining _[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), such fetchlings can use
_[memory lapse](ref/spell/memory-lapse)_ once per day as a spell-like ability. For this ability, a
fetchling's caster level is equal to his total Hit Dice. This racial trait modifies the spell-like
ability racial trait.
