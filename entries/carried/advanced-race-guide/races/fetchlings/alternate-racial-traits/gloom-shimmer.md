Some fetchlings can manipulate shadowy energy in order to displace their location instead of
transporting between shadows. Upon reaching 9th level, instead of gaining
_[shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk)_ as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities),
these fetchlings gain _[displacement](ref/spell/displacement)_ as a spell-like ability usable twice
per day. For this ability, a fetchling's caster level is equal to his total Hit Dice. This racial
trait modifies the spell-like ability racial trait.
