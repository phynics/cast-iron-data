Fetchlings who have spent most of their lives on the Material Plane can become more acclimated to
their new environments. Instead of gaining a +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (planes) checks, these
fetchlings gain a +1 racial bonus on Knowledge (nature) and Knowledge (local) checks. This racial
trait modifies the skilled racial trait.
