The wayangs are a race of small supernatural humanoids who trace their ancestry to the Plane of
Shadows. They are extremely gaunt, with pixielike stature and skin the color of deep shadow. Deeply
spiritual, they follow a philosophy known as "The Dissolution," which teaches that in passing they
may again merge into the shadow. They readily express their beliefs through ritual scarification and
skin bleaching, marking their bodies with raised white dots in ornate spirals and geometric
patterns. Shy and elusive, they live in small, interdependent tribes. Wayangs rarely associate with
outsiders.

## Wayang Racial Traits {#wayang-racial-traits}

### +2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, -2 Wisdom {#2-dexterity-2-intelligence-2-wisdom}

Wayang are nimble and cagey, but their perception of the world is clouded by shadows.

### Wayang {#wayang}

Wayangs are humanoids with the wayang subtype.

### Small {#small}

Wayangs are Small creatures and gain a +1 size bonus to their AC, a +1 size bonus on attack rolls, a
–1 penalty on their CMB and to CMD, and a +4 size bonus on Stealth checks.

### Slow Speed {#slow-speed}

Wayangs have a base speed of 20 feet.

### Darkvision {#darkvision}

Wayangs can see in the dark up to 60 feet.

### Light and Dark (Su) {#light-and-dark-su}

Once per day as an immediate action, a wayang can treat positive and negative energy effects as if
she were an undead creature, taking damage from positive energy and healing damage from negative
energy. This ability lasts for 1 minute once activated.

### Lurker {#lurker}

Wayangs gain a +2 racial bonus on Perception and Stealth checks.

### Shadow Magic {#shadow-magic}

Wayangs add +1 to the DC of any saving throws against spells of the shadow subschool that they cast.
Wayangs with a Charisma score of 11 or higher also gain the following spell-like abilities:
1/day—_[ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound)_, _[pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace)_,
and _[ventriloquism](ref/spell/ventriloquism)_. The caster level for these effects is equal to the
wayang's level. The DC for these spells is equal to 10 + the spell's level + the wayang's Charisma
modifier.

### Shadow Resistance {#shadow-resistance}

Wayangs get a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against spells of the shadow subschool.

### Languages {#languages}

Wayangs begin play speaking Common and Wayang. Wayangs with high Intelligence scores can choose from
the following: any human language, Abyssal, Aklo, Draconic, Goblin, Infernal, Nagaji, Samsaran, and
Tengu.

## Favored Class Options {#favored-class-options}

The following options are available to all wayangs who have the listed favored class, and unless
otherwise stated, the bonus applies each time you select the favored class reward.

### Bard {#bard}

Add one spell known from the wizard's illusion school spell list. This spell must be at least one
level below the highest spell level the bard can cast. The spell is treated as being one level
higher, unless it is also on the bard spell list.

### Oracle {#oracle}

Add one spell known from the wizard's illusion school spell list. This spell must be at least one
level below the highest spell level the oracle can cast. That spell is treated as one level higher
unless it is also on the oracle spell list.

### Sorcerer {#sorcerer}

Add +1/2 point of damage to any illusion spells of the shadow subschool cast by the sorcerer.

### Summoner {#summoner}

Add +1 skill rank to the summoner's eidolon.

## Racial Archetypes {#racial-archetypes}

The following racial archetype is available to wayangs.

### Shadow Puppeteer (Bard) {#shadow-puppeteer-bard}

A shadow puppeteer invokes amazing and terrifying shadow puppet shows, producing supernatural
effects by creating and manipulating shadow. A shadow puppeteer has the following class feature.

#### Bardic Performance {#bardic-performance}

A shadow puppeteer gains the following types of bardic performance. The character must be able to
perform shadow puppetry in order to activate any of these abilities. Shadow puppetry uses Perform
(act), and requires a light source. These abilities replace all levels of inspire courage and
inspire competence.

##### Shadow Servant (Sp) {#shadow-servant-sp}

At 1st level, the puppeteer can create a _shadow servant_ to perform simple tasks. The shadow
servant is identical to an _[unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant)_ (caster level equal to the
shadow puppeteer's bard level), except it appears as a formless shadow. Shadow servant relies on
visual components.

##### Shadow Puppets (Sp) {#shadow-puppets-sp}

At 1st level, a shadow puppeteer can use bardic performance to create one quasi-real shadowy
creature resembling a monster from the _[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1)_ list. These
shadowy creatures otherwise work like _[shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration)_, and
targets interacting with them get a Will saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your bard level + your Charisma
bonus) to treat them as only 20% real. At 4th level and every three levels thereafter, this ability
acts like the next higher version of _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_
(_[summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2)_ at 4th level and so on).
