Humans are sometimes orphaned and adopted by other races. Choose one humanoid race without the human
subtype. You start play with that race's languages and gain that race's weapon familiarity racial
trait (if any). If the race does not have weapon familiarity, you gain either Skill Focus or Weapon
Focus as a bonus feat that is appropriate for that race instead. This racial trait replaces the
bonus feat trait.
