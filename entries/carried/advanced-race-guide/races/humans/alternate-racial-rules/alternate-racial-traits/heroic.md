Some humans are born heroes. In campaigns that use the optional hero point system, each time these
humans gain a level, they gain 2 hero points instead of 1. If they take the Blood of Heroes feat,
they gain 3 hero points each level instead of 2. This racial trait replaces the bonus feat trait.
