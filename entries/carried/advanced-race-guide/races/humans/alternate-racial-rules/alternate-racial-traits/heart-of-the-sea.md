Humans born near the sea are always drawn to it. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Profession (sailor)
and Swim checks, and these are always class skills for them. They can hold their breath twice as
long as normal, and spellcasters gain a +4 racial bonus on concentration checks when attempting to
cast spells underwater. This racial trait replaces skilled.
