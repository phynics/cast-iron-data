Humans raised in the wild learn the hard way that only the strong survive. They gain a racial bonus
equal to half their character level on Survival checks. They also gain a +5 racial bonus on
Constitution checks to stabilize when dying and add half their character level to their Constitution
score when determining the negative hit point total necessary to kill them. This racial trait
replaces skilled.
