Humans born in chilly climes treat cold climates as one category less severe. They gain a +2 racial
bonus on Fortitude saving throws against the effects of cold climates, on any check or saving throw
to avoid slipping and falling, and to CMD against trip combat maneuvers. This bonus applies on
Acrobatics and Climb checks made in slippery conditions. This racial trait replaces skilled.
