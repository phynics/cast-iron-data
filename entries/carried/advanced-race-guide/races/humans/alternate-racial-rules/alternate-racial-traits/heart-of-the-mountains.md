Humans born in the mountains are skilled at negotiating heights and precipices. They gain a +2
racial bonus on Climb checks and Acrobatics checks to move on narrow surfaces and uneven ground.
Furthermore, they are considered acclimated to the effects of high altitude. This racial trait
replaces skilled.
