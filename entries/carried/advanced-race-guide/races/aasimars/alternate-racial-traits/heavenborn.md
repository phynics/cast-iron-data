Born in the celestial realms, aasimars with this racial trait gain a +2 bonus on Knowledge (planes)
checks and they cast spells with the good or light descriptor at +1 caster level. This racial trait
replaces the skilled and [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) racial traits.
