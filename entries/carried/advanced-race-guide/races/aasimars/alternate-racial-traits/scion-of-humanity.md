Some aasimars' heavenly ancestry is extremely distant. An aasimar with this racial trait counts as
an outsider (native) and a humanoid (human) for any effect related to race, including feat
prerequisites and spells that affect humanoids. She can pass for human without using the Disguise
skill. This racial trait replaces the Celestial language and alters the native subtype.
