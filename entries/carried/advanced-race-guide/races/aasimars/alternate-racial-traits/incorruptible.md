Occasionally, aasimars arise with the ability to further ward away evil. Aasimars with this racial
trait can cast _[corruption resistance](ref/spell/corruption-resistance)_ against evil once per day
as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). If an aasimar uses this ability on herself,
the duration increases to 1 hour per level. This racial trait replaces the spell-like ability racial
trait.
