Some aasimars possess the ability to manifest halos. An aasimar with this racial trait can create
_[light](ref/spell/light)_ centered on her head at will as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). When using her halo, she gains a +2 circumstance
bonus on Intimidate checks against evil creatures and on saving throws against becoming blinded or
dazzled. This racial trait replaces the [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) racial trait.
