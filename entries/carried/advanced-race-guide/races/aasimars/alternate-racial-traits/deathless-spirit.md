Particularly strong-willed aasimars possess celestial spirits capable of resisting the powers of
death. They gain resistance 5 against negative energy damage. They do not lose hit points when they
gain a negative level, and they gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against death effects,
energy drain, negative energy, and spells or [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of
the necromancy school. This racial trait replaces celestial resistance.
