Some aasimars follow their destiny to war against the powers of ultimate evil. These individuals
gain a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls and to AC against evil outsiders and a +2 racial bonus on
Knowledge (planes) and Spellcraft checks to identify evil outsiders or items or effects created by
evil outsiders; they may use these skills untrained for this purpose. This racial trait replaces
celestial resistance and skilled.
