Aasimars with this racial trait defy the powers of death. They gain a +2 bonus on Knowledge
(history) checks and saving throws against death effects and can use
_[lesser age resistance](ref/spell/age-resistance-lesser)_ once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This racial trait replaces the skilled and
spell-like ability racial traits.
