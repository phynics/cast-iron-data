A tailblade is a small, sharp knife designed to be strapped to the tip of a wielder's tail. It takes
a full-round action to strap on or remove a tailblade. The wearer can loosely attach the tailblade
(without strapping it securely in place) as a move action, but using a loosely attached tailblade
gives the wielder a –4 penalty on all attack rolls made with the weapon, and other creatures get a
+4 bonus on disarm combat maneuver checks to disarm the tailblade. A ratfolk wielding a tailblade
can make a tail attack, adding its Strength modifier to the tailblade's damage. Ratfolk are
considered proficient with such attacks and can apply feats or effects appropriate to natural
attacks to tail attacks made with a tailblade. If used as part of a full attack action, attacks with
a tailblade are considered secondary attacks.
