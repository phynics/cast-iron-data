Some ratfolk can blend easily into their environments, and move with surprising grace. Ratfolk gain
a +2 racial bonus on Stealth checks, and take only a –5 penalty on Stealth checks made to hide from
creatures they have distracted with a Bluff check (rather than the normal –10 penalty). This racial
trait replaces tinker.
