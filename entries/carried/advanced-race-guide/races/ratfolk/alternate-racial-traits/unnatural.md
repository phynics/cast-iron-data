Some ratfolk unnerve normal animals, and train to defend themselves against the inevitable attacks
from such creatures. These ratfolk take a –4 penalty on all Charisma-based skill checks to affect
creatures of the animal type, and receive a +2 dodge bonus to AC against animals. An animal's
starting attitude toward ratfolk is one step worse than normal. This racial trait replaces rodent
empathy.
