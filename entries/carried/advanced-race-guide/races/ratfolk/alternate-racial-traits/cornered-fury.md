Ratfolk can fight viciously when cut off from friends and allies. Whenever a ratfolk with this
racial trait is reduced to half or fewer of his hit points, and has no conscious ally within 30
feet, he gains a +2 racial bonus on melee attack rolls and to Armor Class. This racial trait
replaces swarming.
