Dwarves with this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls against humanoids with the giant
subtype. Furthermore, they gain a +2 bonus on Survival checks to find and follow tracks made by
humanoids with the giant subtype. This racial trait replaces the hatred racial trait.
