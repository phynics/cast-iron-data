Dwarves with this racial trait grew up facing the abominations that live deep beneath the surface.
They receive a +2 dodge bonus to AC against monsters of the aberration type and a +2 racial bonus on
combat maneuver checks made to grapple such creatures (or to continue a grapple). This racial trait
replaces defensive training.
