Dwarves with this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and a +2 dodge bonus to AC and on
saving throws against the exceptional, supernatural, and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of dragons. They also gain a +2 racial bonus on
Knowledge (arcana) checks to identify dragons and can make such checks untrained. This racial trait
replaces defensive training, hatred, and stonecunning.
