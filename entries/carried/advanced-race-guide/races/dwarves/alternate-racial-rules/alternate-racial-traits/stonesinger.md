Some dwarves' affinity for the earth grants them greater powers. Dwarves with this racial trait are
treated as one level higher when casting spells with the earth descriptor or using granted powers of
the Earth domain, the bloodline powers of the earth elemental bloodline, and revelations of the
oracle's stone mystery. This ability does not give them early access to level-based powers; it only
affects the powers they could use without this ability. This racial trait replaces stonecunning.
