Some dwarves have dwelt so long aboveground they have lost their ability to see at night. However,
their adaptation to extreme environments allows them to treat wind conditions (when determining
whether or not they are checked or blown away) and either hot or cold climates (choose one) as one
step less severe. This racial trait replaces [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision).
