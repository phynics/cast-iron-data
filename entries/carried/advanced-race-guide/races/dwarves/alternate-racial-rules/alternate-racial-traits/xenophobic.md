Isolationist dwarves despise non-dwarven humanoids. They speak only Dwarven and do not gain any
bonus languages from possessing a high Intelligence score. In addition, they learn only one language
per 2 ranks of Linguistics they possess. However, their untrusting nature gives them a +1 bonus
against mind-affecting effects, except for fear affects. This racial trait replaces a dwarf's normal
languages.
