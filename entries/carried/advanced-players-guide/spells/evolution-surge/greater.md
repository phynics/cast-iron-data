::: statblock
School: transmutation
Level: 4: Summoner
Casting Time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a chameleon scale)
Range: touch
Target: your eidolon
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving Throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell Resistance: no
:::

_Grants eidolon two evolutions with a total of 6 evolution points._

This spell functions as _[lesser evolution surge](ref/spell/evolution-surge-lesser)_, except that
you can grant any two evolutions whose total cost does not exceed 6 evolution points.
