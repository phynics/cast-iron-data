School: transmutation
Level: 7: Arcanist, Elementalist Wizard, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, F (a wing feather)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)

Duration: 10 minutes/level
Saving Throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell Resistance: yes (harmless)

_One creature/level gains ability to fly._

This spell functions as _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_ (_Core Rulebook_ 284), save that it can target
numerous creatures and lasts longer.
