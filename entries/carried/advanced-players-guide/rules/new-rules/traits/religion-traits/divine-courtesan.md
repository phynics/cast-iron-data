::: statblock
Abbrev: CN
:::

You worked in one of your goddess's temples as a sacred courtesan, and you know how to flatter,
please, and (most of all) listen. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Sense Motive checks and Diplomacy
checks to gather information, and one of these skills (your choice) is always a class skill for you.
