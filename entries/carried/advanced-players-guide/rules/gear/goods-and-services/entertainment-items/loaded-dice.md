Most loaded dice are weighted—a heavier substance is included opposite the desired number. You can
spot marks from tampering with a die in this fashion with a DC 15 Appraise or Perception check.
Higher-quality dice (such as wooden dice carved around a naturally heavy inclusion) have DCs ranging
from 20 to 30.
