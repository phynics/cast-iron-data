::: statblock
Price: 10 gp
Weight: 2 lbs.
Gear Type: Tools and Skill Kits
:::

This small kit contains a simple slate with a grid carved into its surface and a number of different
colored pieces of chalk. Anyone using this kit to draw a map as they travel receives a +2
circumstance bonus on Survival skill checks to avoid becoming lost.
