::: statblock
Price: 50 gp
Weight: 4 lbs.1
Gear Type: Adventuring Gear
:::

This backpack has numerous pockets for storing various items that might be needed while adventuring.
Hooks are included for attaching items such as canteens, pouches, or even a rolled-up blanket. It
has padded bands that strap across the chest and the waist to distribute its weight more evenly.
Like a common backpack, it can hold about 2 cubic feet of material in its main container. When
wearing a masterwork backpack, treat your Strength score as +1 higher than normal when calculating
your carrying capacity.
