::: statblock
Gear Type: Special Substances and Items
Weight: 1/2 lb.
Adamantine\: Price: 100 gp
Adamantine\: Craft DC: 25
Silver\: Price: 5 gp
Silver\: Craft DC: 20
Cold Iron\: Price: 20 gp
Cold Iron\: Craft DC: 20
:::

These alchemical powders have a gritty consistency. When poured on a weapon and placed over a hot
flame for a full round, they melt and form a temporary coating on the weapon. The blanching gives
the weapon the ability to bypass one kind of material-based
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), such as adamantine, cold iron, or silver. The
blanching remains effective until the weapon makes a successful attack. Each dose of blanching can
coat one weapon or up to 10 pieces of ammunition. Only one kind of weapon blanch can be on a weapon
at one time, though a weapon made of one special material (such as adamantine) can have a different
material blanch (such as silver), and counts as both materials for the first successful hit.
