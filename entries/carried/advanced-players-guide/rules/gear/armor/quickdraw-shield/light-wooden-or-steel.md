This light shield is specially crafted with a series of straps to allow a character proficient in
shields to ready or stow it on his or her back quickly and easily. If you have a base attack bonus
of +1 or higher, you may don or put away a quickdraw shield as a swift action combined with a
regular move. If you have the Two-Weapon Fighting feat, you can draw a light or one-handed weapon
with one hand and a quickdraw shield with the other in the time it would normally take you to draw
one weapon. If you have the Quick Draw feat, you may don or put away a quickdraw shield as a free
action.
