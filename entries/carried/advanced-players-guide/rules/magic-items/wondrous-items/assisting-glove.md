## Description {#description}

The wearer of this simple glove can speak a command word to transform it into a glowing disembodied
hand. As a swift action, the wearer can have the glove assist him with a task as if using the aid
another action; the glove uses the wearer's base attack bonus or skill ranks when making the check
to see if aid another is successful (it does not gain the benefit of the wearer's ability scores or
other bonuses). The hand remains until it has attempted one action or until 1 minute passes,
whichever comes first, at which time it becomes a nonmagical lambskin glove.
