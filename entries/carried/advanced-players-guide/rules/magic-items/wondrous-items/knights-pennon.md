## Description {#description}

This narrow cloth flag is made to attach to the end a knight's lance, though it can be flown from a
spear, polearm, or even a staff. It has no effect if not mounted appropriately. A _knight's pennon_
grants different benefits depending on its color and design.

### Battle {#battle}

The red _knight's pennon of battle_ allows its bearer to benefit from _[heroism](ref/spell/heroism)_
once per day.

### Honor {#honor}

The gold _knight's pennon of honor_ bestows _[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil)_
upon the bearer once per day.

### Parley {#parley}

The white _knight's pennon of parley_, when displayed approaching a negotiation, grants the bearer a
+4 enhancement bonus on Bluff, Diplomacy, Intimidate, and Sense Motive checks for 1 hour, so long as
the bearer does not initiate any hostile action.
