## Description {#description}

Each _elemental metamagic rod_ is built with the power to control and transform a specific energy
type (acid, cold, electricity, or fire). The wielder can cast up to three spells per day that deal
damage of the rod's energy type instead of the spell's normal damage type, as though using the
Elemental Spell feat. For example, an _elemental metamagic rod (cold)_ always makes spells deal cold
damage.
