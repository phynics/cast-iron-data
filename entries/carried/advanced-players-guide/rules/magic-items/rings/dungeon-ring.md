## Description {#description}

A _jailer's dungeon ring_ is worked gold, set with a carnelian. The ring is magically attuned to one
or more iron _prisoner's dungeon rings_. When the wearer of a _jailer's ring_ places a _prisoner's
ring_ on a subject, the prisoner cannot remove the ring without the use of a
_[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse)_, _[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish)_,
_[miracle](ref/spell/miracle)_, or _[wish](ref/spell/wish)_ spell. The wearer of the _jailer's ring_
can also remove a linked _prisoner's ring_ at any time.

The jailer is aware of any wearer of a linked _prisoner's ring_ like a constant
_[status](ref/spell/status)_ spell. Additionally, all wearers of linked _prisoner's rings_ count as
familiar to the jailer for purposes of spells such as _[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ and
_[teleport](ref/spell/teleport)_.
