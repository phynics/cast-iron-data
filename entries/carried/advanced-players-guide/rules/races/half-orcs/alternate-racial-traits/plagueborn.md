Half-orcs are sometimes forced to live on the rancid and unsanitary margins of society, becoming
inured to all manner of sickness. Half-orcs with this racial trait gain a +2 racial bonus on saving
throws against disease, ingested poisons, and becoming nauseated or sickened. This racial trait
replaces the intimidating and orc ferocity racial traits.
