Some half-orcs have a spiritual kinship with fantastical beasts, capturing them for sport or living
and hunting with them. A half-orc with this trait treats whip and net as martial weapons and gains a
+2 bonus on Handle Animal checks. This racial trait replaces the orc ferocity racial trait.
