Dwarves are skilled at pushing their way through a battlefield, tossing aside lesser foes with ease.
Dwarves with this racial trait receive a +2 bonus on Combat Maneuver checks made to bull rush or
overrun an opponent. This bonus only applies while both the dwarf and his opponent are standing on
the ground. This racial trait replaces the stability racial trait.
