Half-elves are skilled at charming others and recovering from faux pas. If half-elves with this
racial trait attempt to change a creature's attitude with a Diplomacy check and fail by 5 or more,
they can try to influence the creature a second time even if 24 hours have not passed. This racial
trait replaces the adaptability racial trait.
