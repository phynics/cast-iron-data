Some half-elves are born of elves adapted to life on or near the water. These half-elves gain a +4
racial bonus on Swim checks, can always take 10 while swimming, and may choose Aquan as a bonus
language. This racial trait replaces the half-elf's adaptability and multitalented racial traits.
