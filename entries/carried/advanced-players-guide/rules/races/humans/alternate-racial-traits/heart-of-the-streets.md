Humans from bustling cities are skilled with crowds. They gain a +1 bonus on Reflex saves and a +1
dodge bonus to Armor Class when adjacent to at least two other allies. Crowds do not count as
difficult terrain for them. This racial trait replaces the skilled racial trait.
