Humans have great intuition for hidden potential. They gain a +2 bonus on Sense Motive checks. In
addition, when they acquire an animal companion, bonded mount, cohort, or familiar, that creature
gains a +2 bonus to one ability score of the character's choice. This racial trait replaces the
bonus feat racial trait.
