Elves know the deep secrets of the wild like no others, especially those of the forests. Elves with
this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on Knowledge (nature) and Survival checks. In forest terrain,
these bonuses improve to +2. This racial trait replaces the elven magic racial trait.
