Gnomes love languages and learning about those they meet. Gnomes with this racial trait gain a +1
bonus on Bluff and Diplomacy checks, and they learn one additional language every time they put a
rank in the Linguistics skill. This racial trait replaces the defensive training and hatred racial
traits.
