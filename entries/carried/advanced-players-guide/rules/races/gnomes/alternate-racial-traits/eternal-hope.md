Gnomes rarely lose hope and are always confident that even hopeless situations will work out. Gnomes
with this racial trait receive a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against fear and despair effects.
Once per day, after rolling a 1 on a d20, the gnome may reroll and use the second result. This
racial trait replaces the defensive training and hatred racial traits.
