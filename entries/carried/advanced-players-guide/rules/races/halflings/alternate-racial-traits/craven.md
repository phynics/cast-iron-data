While most halflings are fearless, some are skittish, making them particularly alert. Halflings with
this racial trait gain a +1 bonus on initiative checks and a +1 bonus on attack rolls when flanking.
They suffer a –2 penalty on fear saves and gain no benefit from morale bonuses on fear saves. When
affected by a fear effect, their base speed increases by 10 feet and they gain a +1 dodge bonus to
Armor Class. This racial trait replaces the fearless and halfling luck racial traits.
