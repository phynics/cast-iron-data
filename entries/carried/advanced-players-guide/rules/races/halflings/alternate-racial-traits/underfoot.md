Halflings must train hard to effectively fight bigger opponents. Halflings with this racial trait
gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC against foes larger than themselves and a +1 bonus on Reflex saving
throws to avoid trample attacks. This racial trait replaces the halfling luck racial trait.
