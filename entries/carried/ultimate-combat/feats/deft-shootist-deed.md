_You keep an eye out while focusing on your weapon, allowing you to avoid attacks while shooting and
reloading firearms._

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

_Grit class feature or Amateur Gunslinger feat, Dodge, Mobility._

## Benefits {#benefits}

As long as you have at least 1 grit point, you do not provoke attacks of opportunity when shooting
or reloading a firearm.
