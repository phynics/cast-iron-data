::: statblock
School: transmutation (polymorph)
Level: 3: Alchemist, Bloodrager, Druid, Hunter, Hunter, Hunter, Investigator, Magus, Ranger\
4: Arcanist, Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting Time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M/DF (a part of the animal)
Range: personal
Target: you
Duration: 1 minute/level (D)
Saving Throw: none
Spell Resistance: yes (harmless)
:::

_As animal aspect, but you gain two animal qualities._

This spell functions like _[animal aspect](ref/spell/animal-aspect)_, except you can either gain two
aspects at once or one aspect that adds effects to the aspects that
_[animal aspect](ref/spell/animal-aspect)_ grants, as described below.

::: paragraph indented
_Frog:_ You gain a Swim speed equal to half your normal speed, and all the benefits of having a swim
speed.
:::

::: paragraph indented
_Gorilla:_ You gain a +2 competence bonus on Perception checks, and a +1 competence bonus on
Fortitude saves.
:::

::: paragraph indented
_Monkey:_ You gain a +4 competence bonus on Acrobatics checks.
:::

::: paragraph indented
_Otter_: Your swim speed increases to your full normal speed, and you need not make concentration
checks to cast spells underwater.
:::

::: paragraph indented
_Raccoon_: Your competence bonuses on Disable Device, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth checks increase
to +4.
:::

::: paragraph indented
_Raptor_: Your enhancement bonus to speed increases to +30 feet, and you gain the benefit of the Run
feat.
:::

::: paragraph indented
_Tree Lizard_: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Stealth checks.
:::
