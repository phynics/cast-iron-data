Feats represent special tricks or abilities a character has acquired through training, luck, or as a
quirk of his birth. They grant characters abilities that others lack, giving them an edge in the
right situation. While some feats specifically interact with a PC's race or class, most are
available to anyone who meets the prerequisites. This section introduces two new types of feats—grit
feats and style feats. Grit feats expand a gunslinger's ability to use grit and deeds, while style
feats each grant a style of martial arts that can be used by monks and other characters who learn
the style.
