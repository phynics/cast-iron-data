The madu is a round, light shield with two animal horns—usually from an antelope—extending from
opposite sides of the shield. If you are proficient with the madu, you may use it to fight
defensively with a –2 penalty instead of the normal –4, and your attack penalty for using Combat
Expertise improves by +1 (minimum –1 penalty). You cannot hold anything else in the hand that bears
a madu. If you are not proficient with the madu, treat it as a light spiked shield.
