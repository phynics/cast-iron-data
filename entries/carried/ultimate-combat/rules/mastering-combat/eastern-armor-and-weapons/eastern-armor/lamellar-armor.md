Lamellar is a type of armor in which small plates of various types of materials are strung together
in parallel rows using fine cord. Lamellar plates can be constructed from lacquered leather, horn,
or even stone, though steel and heavier iron are most common. Lamellar armor can be crafted into
various shapes, including partial pieces such as breastplates, greaves, or even entire coats. The
properties of specific suits and pieces of lamellar armor are determined by their material.
