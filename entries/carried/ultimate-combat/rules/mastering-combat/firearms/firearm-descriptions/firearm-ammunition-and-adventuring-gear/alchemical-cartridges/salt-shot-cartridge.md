::: paragraph indented
This mix of black powder and rock salt can only be loaded into a blunderbuss, a dragon pistol, or
other scatter weapon. It deals nonlethal instead of lethal damage, and increases the misfire value
by 1. You can only use it with a scatter weapon's cone attack.
:::
