::: paragraph indented
This mix of black powder and an alchemically treated resin strong enough to survive the shot can
only be loaded into a blunderbuss, a dragon pistol, or other scatter weapon. It deals half damage to
those hit by a cone attack made with this weapon, but any creature hit by the shot must succeed at a
DC 15 Reflex saving throw or become entangled for 2d4 rounds. An entangling shot cartridge increases
the firearm's misfire value by 2.
:::
