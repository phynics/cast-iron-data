::: statblock
Price: 3 gp
Weight: 1 lb.
Gear Type: Item
:::

Typically crafted from animal horn, but increasingly crafted from metal in a wide variety of shapes,
a powder horn can hold up to 10 doses of black powder. A powder horn protects black powder stored
within in it from exposure to fire, electricity, firearm misfires, and water.
