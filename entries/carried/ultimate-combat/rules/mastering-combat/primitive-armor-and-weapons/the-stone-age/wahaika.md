This short and broad club is made of hardened wood or bone. It has a notch on one side that is used
for catching weapons. If you are proficient, you use the notch in the wahaika to disarm your foes.
Otherwise, treat this weapon as a club. Feats and abilities that affect clubs apply to the wahaika.
