A taiaha is a long, heavy stick, club-shaped at one end and tipped with a wooden or metal spear
point at the other. You wield it with a combination of solid strikes with the club and fending
motions with the spear. A taiaha can be wielded as a martial weapon that deals 1d8/×2 bludgeoning
damage.
