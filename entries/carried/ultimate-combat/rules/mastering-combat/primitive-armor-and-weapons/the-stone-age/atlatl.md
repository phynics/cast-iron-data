An atlatl is a thin piece of wood or antler used as a lever to hurl a specially fitted dart. An
atlatl gives much greater range to a dart, but must be loaded like a projectile weapon. Your
Strength modifier applies to damage rolls when you use an atlatl, just as it does for thrown
weapons. You can fire—but not load—an atlatl with one hand. Loading an atlatl is a move action that
requires two hands and provokes attacks of opportunity. The Rapid Reload feat can be taken for
atlatls, allowing you to load a dart as a free action.
