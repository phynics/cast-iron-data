The kestros is an oddly shaped sling used for launching darts. Your Strength modifier applies on
damage rolls when you use a kestros, just as it does for thrown weapons. You can fire—but not load—a
kestros with one hand. Loading a kestros is a move action that requires two hands and provokes
attacks of opportunity. Halflings treat the kestros as a martial weapon.
