This dart, meant to be thrown using a kestros, consists of a heavy metal point about 9 inches long
attached to a shaft of wood about 12 inches long, fletched with feathers for stability. If used as a
melee weapon, it is a light improvised weapon (–4 penalty on attack rolls) and deals damage as a
dagger of its size (critical multiplier ×2). Kestros darts come in a quiver that holds 10 darts.
