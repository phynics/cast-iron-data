The first spell lists presented are the lists of spells separated by class and level. These include
the spell name and a brief and incomplete description of the spell. Immediately after those lists is
a complete list of all spells with full descriptions of those spells listed. Spells in all lists are
presented in alphabetical order, except when a spell's name begins with “communal,” “greater,”
“lesser,” or “mass,” in which case it is alphabetized under the base spell name.
