::: paragraph statline
A thoracic nanite chamber augments the vital organs within a creature's chest, particularly its
heart, lungs, and pulmonary system. Once installed, a thoracic nanite chamber grants an enhancement
bonus to Constitution. This enhancement bonus alters the implant's price, implantation value,
install DCs, and Craft DCs as listed below.
