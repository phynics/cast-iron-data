::: paragraph statline
Cyberfiber muscles are powerful synthetic muscles that augment but do not replace existing muscle
mass in a creature. Once installed, cyberfiber muscles grant an enhancement bonus to Strength. This
enhancement bonus alters price, implantation value, install DCs, and Craft DCs as listed below.
