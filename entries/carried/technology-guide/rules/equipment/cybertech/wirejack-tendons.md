::: paragraph statline
Wirejack tendons not only strengthen and enhance existing reflexes, but also enhance the
transmission of nerve impulses to surrounding musculature. Once installed, wirejack tendons grant an
enhancement bonus to Dexterity. This enhancement bonus alters the price, implantation values,
install DCs, and Craft DCs of the implant as listed below.
