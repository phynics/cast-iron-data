::: paragraph statline
A hologram generator is a handheld device with a long, thin barrel. It manipulates photons and sound
waves to produce highly realistic images and sounds, similar in nature to those generated by figment
illusions.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Before a hologram generator can generate an image, though, it must store the image in its memory.
The maximum size and maximum number of images a generator can store vary according to its color, but
storing an image is a standard action regardless of the strength of the generator. More powerful
generators can store moving images and sounds, or even generate illusions that interact with their
environments.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Once a hologram generator's memory is full, additional images overwrite existing images as chosen by
the user at the time of their recording.
:::

::: paragraph statline
Generating a hologram is a standard action. If the hologram generator is then left alone, it
continues to project the image until it depletes its charges. Alternatively, the user can aim the
hologram at a different location as a move action.
:::

::: paragraph statline
A hologram generator is a color-coded item, and the particulars of its abilities, including its
energy capacity, the range at which it can create its holograms, and the Will save DC to disbelieve
the hologram if it is interacted with, vary as detailed below.
:::

## Brown Hologram Generator {#brown-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A brown hologram generator can create only a static, motionless image no larger than a Small
creature, and can store only one such image.
:::

## Black Hologram Generator {#black-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A black hologram generator can create a static, motionless image no larger than a Medium creature,
and can store three images.
:::

## White Hologram Generator {#white-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A white hologram generator can create a static, motionless image no larger than a Large creature,
and can store five images.
:::

## Gray Hologram Generator {#gray-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A gray hologram generator functions as a white hologram generator (and can store up to seven
images), but can also store up to 1 minute of moving imagery as well. A moving hologram can move
anywhere in range of the generator, as long as the user continues to take a move action each round
to aim and direct the motion. An unattended generator's hologram cannot leave its imagery, but it
can be mobile in that area.
:::

## Green Hologram Generator {#green-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A green hologram generator functions as a gray one, except that it can incorporate sound as well,
with all appropriate sound coming from the image. It can store up to nine images or 2 minutes of
moving imagery. It can store images equal in size to a Huge or smaller creature.
:::

## Red Hologram Generator {#red-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A red hologram generator functions as a green one, except that it can create two images within range
simultaneously. Directing these images is a free action. A red hologram generator can store up to 11
images or 3 minutes of moving imagery.
:::

## Blue Hologram Generator {#blue-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A blue hologram generator functions as a red one, except that it can create three images
simultaneously, can store up to 13 images, and can play up to 4 minutes of moving imagery.
:::

## Orange Hologram Generator {#orange-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
An orange hologram generator functions as a blue one, except that it can create up to four images
simultaneously, can store up to 15 images, and can play up to 4 minutes of moving imagery. In
addition, it can store images equal in size to a Gargantuan or smaller creature.
:::

## Prismatic Hologram Generator {#prismatic-hologram-generator}

::: paragraph statline
A prismatic hologram generator functions as an orange one. As long as only one image is created, the
image is further enhanced by graviton fields, which give it the ability to interact with the
environment. (Such images still last for only 4 minutes.) A barrier generated by a prismatic
hologram generator has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10 and 50 hit points (break DC 28). A creature
generated in this manner can be directed to attack targets. The creature can make only one attack
per round regardless of its appearance, attacking at the wielder's base attack bonus modified by the
wielder's Intelligence score. On a successful hit, the hologram deals 2d6+6 points of force damage,
regardless of the hologram's size.
:::
