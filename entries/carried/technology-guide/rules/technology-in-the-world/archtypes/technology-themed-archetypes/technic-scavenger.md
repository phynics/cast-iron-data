::: statblock
Abbrev: Rogue Archetype
:::

A technic scavenger lives for the discovery of new technological wonders scraped from the depths of
strange ruins, metal caverns, and outlandish junkyards. She is well-versed in all the ways that
technology can be used to protect sites, as well as the ways in which it can malfunction.

## Technic Training {#technic-training}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A technic scavenger adds 1/2 her rogue level to Perception skill checks to locate mechanical or
high-tech traps and to Disable Device skill checks against these traps (minimum +1). She gains
Technologist as a bonus feat. She cannot use Disable Device to disarm magical traps. This ability
replaces trapfinding.

## Lucky Glitch {#lucky-glitch}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

Starting at 4th level, whenever a technic scavenger triggers a glitch when using a timeworn
technological item, she rolls twice and can choose which of the two glitch effects actually occurs.
A technic scavenger always adds her level to rolls to determine what kind of glitch occurs, and
treats rolls of over 100 as 100. This ability replaces uncanny dodge.

## Robot Slayer {#robot-slayer}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

At 8th level, a technic scavenger knows just where to strike robots to incapacitate and disable
them. When she deals sneak attack damage to a robot, she ignores all [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
that robot possesses. This ability replaces improved uncanny dodge.
