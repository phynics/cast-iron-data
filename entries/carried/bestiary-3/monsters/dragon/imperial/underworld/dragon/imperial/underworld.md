## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Adamantine Claws {#adamantine-claws}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

The claws of an underworld dragon are made of adamantine, and have the qualities of a weapon made
from that material.

### Change Shape {#change-shape}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A young or older underworld dragon can assume any humanoid form three times per day as if using
polymorph.

### Lava Eruption {#lava-eruption}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

As a full-round action, a great wyrm underworld dragon can burrow through the ground up to twice its
burrow speed. At the end of that movement, if the underworld dragon has use of its breath weapon, it
can emerge from the ground spouting lava in a 30-foot-radius burst, dealing damage as the breath
weapon.

### Smoke Vision {#smoke-vision}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

A very young and older underworld dragon can see perfectly in smoky conditions (such as those
created by pyrotechnics).

### Underworld Burrower {#underworld-burrower}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

An adult underworld dragon gains a 10-foot bonus to its burrow speed. When the underworld dragon
becomes old and every two age categories thereafter, its burrow speed increases by an additional 10
feet.
