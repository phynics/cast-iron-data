## Special Abilities {#special-abilities}

### Change Shape {#change-shape}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A young or older sovereign dragon can assume any humanoid form three times per day as polymorph.

### Dogmatic Discordance {#dogmatic-discordance}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Good or evil creatures take a –2 penalty when making saving throws against a sovereign dragon's
spells, [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), breath weapon, and aura.

### Golden Armor {#golden-armor}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

Once per day as a standard action, an old or older sovereign dragon can cover its form in golden
armor, granting it a +4 armor bonus to AC and energy resistance 15 to one energy type, chosen at the
time the armor is summoned. This armor lasts for a number of rounds equal to the dragon's age
category. The sovereign dragon can dismiss the armor as a free action.

### Master Counterspelling {#master-counterspelling}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Supernatural
:::

A great wyrm sovereign dragon can counterspell once per round as an immediate action. It need not
know the spell it is countering, but can instead expend any spell that is one level higher to
automatically counter the spell.

### Violent Retort {#violent-retort}

::: statblock
Ability Type: Extraordinary
:::

When a young or older sovereign dragon takes damage from a melee attack critical hit, it can, as an
immediate action, make a claw or tail slap attack against the creature that made the critical hit.
