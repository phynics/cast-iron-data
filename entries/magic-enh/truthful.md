**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 239\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on a melee weapon. A truthful weapon unerringly pierces
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) provided by illusions, allowing the wielder to ignore the miss
chance provided by illusions such as [blur](ref/spell/blur), [displacement](ref/spell/displacement),
and [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility). Attacks with a truthful weapon against a creature
affected by [mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image) or a similar figment always attack the real
target. This ability allows the wielder to ignore the miss chance, not to otherwise detect the
target. When attacking an invisible creature, for instance, the wielder might still need to guess
the target's location.

In addition to its other abilities, a truthful weapon functions as a special kind of
[dispelling](ref/magic-enh/dispelling) weapon against figments and shadow conjuration effects; if
the wielder makes an attack against such an effect, the weapon automatically attempts to use the
stored [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) against the illusion. If the attempt fails, the
wielder is not aware of the attempt, and unless she disbelieves the illusion or the illusion's
duration ends, the weapon continues to impart that it is storing
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ (though she is free to cast
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ on the weapon again if she has another reason to believe
the effect was expended and failed to dispel an illusion).
