**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 48\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

Typically forged by [aeons](ref/family/aeon) or other creatures that eschew commonly held structures
of morality, unaligned weapons quell the effects of extreme alignments on the wielder. The wielder
counts her Hit Dice as 4 lower than her actual total HD when someone attempts to detect her
alignment using [detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos), [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil),
[detect good](ref/spell/detect-good), [detect law](ref/spell/detect-law), or similar spells or
effects. Furthermore, if the wielder is an outsider and is targeted by a spell or effect with an
alignment descriptor that deals additional damage to or has an additional effect on outsiders (such
as [holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite)), she adds a bonus equal to the unaligned weapon's enhancement
bonus on saving throws she attempts against that spell or effect. Finally, the wielder takes no
penalties on her Charisma-, Intelligence-, or Wisdom-based checks from a plane's mildly aligned or
strongly aligned traits, regardless of her actual alignment.
