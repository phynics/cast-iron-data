**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 48\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A planestriking weapon functions as a [planar](ref/magic-enh/planar) weapon, ignoring up to 5 points
of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) when used against an outsider. In addition, when
the wielder of a planestriking weapon is on a plane other than his home plane, his planestriking
weapon ignores up to 10 points of damage reduction when used against outsiders native to the plane.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal); **Price** -
