**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 239\
**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on a melee weapon. Upon command, an unseen weapon can be
made invisible, including any scabbard, sheath, or similar accessory used to hold the weapon. A
careful search will still reveal its presence, but purely visual examination will not. While
invisible, an unseen weapon causes its wielder to take a -1 penalty on attack rolls unless she has
the [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) feat or is able to see invisible objects. On the first
attack made with an unseen weapon during a combat, the defender is denied its Dexterity bonus to
Armor Class unless it has the Blind-Fight feat, is able to see invisible objects, or is specifically
aware that the wielder is using an unseen weapon. An unseen weapon is considered to have total
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) for attacks or effects directed at it, such as combat maneuver
checks made to [disarm](ref/rule/disarm), [steal](ref/rule/steal), or [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) it
and [disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate) or [rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp).
