## Spell Resistance

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 122, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 463\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** armor/shield quality; **Price** 13 (+2 bonus), 15 (+3 bonus), 17 (+4 bonus), 19 (+5 bonus);
**Weight** -

This special ability grants the armor or shield's wearer
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) while the armor or shield is worn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[spell resistance](ref/spell/spell-resistance); **Price** varies
