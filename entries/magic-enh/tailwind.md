**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 27\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on ranged weapons. Whenever a tailwind weapon is used to
make a ranged attack, the weapon or its projectile is pushed along by a powerful tailwind that fills
its space. This tailwind acts similarly to the _[gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind)_ spell,
except it has an instantaneous duration and doesn't affect creatures. However, it can still
extinguish unprotected flames and accomplish anything a sudden burst of wind could. The tailwind
negates range penalties to the weapon's attack until it reaches the end of the wind's range (60
feet); each whole range increment beyond 60 feet applies a penalty to the attack as normal.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind); **Price** -
