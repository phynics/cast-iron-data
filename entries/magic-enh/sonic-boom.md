**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on firearms and firearm ammunition. Attacks with a sonic
boom weapon deal an additional 1d6 points of sonic damage to the target and 1d6 points of sonic
damage to all creatures adjacent to the bullet's path within one range increment of the wielder, or
adjacent to the cone of a [scatter](ref/misc/scatter) shot. Creatures that take sonic damage from
the boom must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save or be deafened for 1 minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shout](ref/spell/shout); **Price** -
