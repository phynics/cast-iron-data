## Spell Storing

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 122\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This armor allows a spellcaster to store a single touch spell of up to 3rd level in it. Anytime a
creature hits the wearer with a melee attack or melee touch attack, the armor can cast the spell on
that creature as an immediate action if the wearer desires. Once the spell has been cast from the
armor, a spellcaster can cast any other targeted touch spell of up to 3rd level into it. The armor
magically imparts to the wielder the name of the spell currently stored within it. A randomly rolled
suit of spell storing armor has a 50% chance to have a spell stored in it already. Spell storing
armor emits a strong aura of the evocation school, plus the aura of the stored spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), creator must be
a caster of at least 12th level; **Price** +1 bonus
