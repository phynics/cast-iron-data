**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 24\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 10th\
**Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

Once per day as a standard action, the wearer of ghost spike armor can command armor spikes made of
shimmering force to erupt from the armor for up to 10 rounds. Treat these spikes as +1
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-armor) [armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes) with
which the wearer is proficient. While the spikes are active, the wearer can attempt to grapple
incorporeal creatures with a -4 penalty on his combat maneuver checks to
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple). The wearer can dismiss the spikes as a free action. The ghost spike
ability cannot be placed on spiked armor or on a shield.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon); **Price** +2 bonus
