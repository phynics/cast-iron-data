**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A dispelling burst weapon functions like a [dispelling](ref/magic-enh/dispelling) weapon but may
store [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) or
_[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater)._ In addition, these spells can be
discharged as a free action when the wielder of a dispelling burst weapon confirms a critical hit.
When the wielder does so, the bonus to the check made to dispel (including from
[dispelling strike](ref/arcana/dispelling-strike), [spell sunder](ref/ragepower/spell-sunder), or
[sunder enchantment](ref/ragepower/sunder-enchantment)) increases by an amount equal to the weapon's
enhancement bonus or the weapon's critical multiplier (whichever is higher).

If a dispelling burst weapon confirms a critical hit while it is not currently storing a dispel
magic or greater dispel magic spell, yet if the wielder has either spell prepared (or is a
spontaneous caster able to cast either spell), she may cast either spell into the weapon as a swift
action and then immediately discharge it into the target as a free action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater), creator must be a caster of at least 12th
level; **Price** -
