**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 140, Ultimate Combat pg 141\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This weapon special ability can only be applied to alchemical or metal firearm cartridges. Dry load
cartridges can be used to load guns underwater or in other airless environments, such as a vacuum.
This special ability protects the cartridge's contents as it is being loaded into a firearm and
creates a residual bubble of air that surrounds that firearm, further protecting the ammunition and
allowing the firearm with this ammunition loaded in it to be fired in an airless environment. After
the cartridge is loaded, the bubble of air lasts for 3 minutes or until the firearm is fired,
whichever occurs first.

A firearm loaded with this ammunition still takes the -2 penalty on attack rolls when fired
underwater for every 5 feet of water the bullet passes through, in addition to the normal penalties
to range. When firing a dry load cartridge underwater or in an airless environment, a misfire that
results in a firearm explosion occurs normally.

The listed price is for 50 dry load cartridges.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[air bubble](ref/spell/air-bubble); **Price** +750 gp
