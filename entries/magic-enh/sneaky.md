**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 215\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +5,000 gp; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on a melee weapon. Once per day as a free action, the
wielder of a sneaky weapon can designate a single foe within his melee reach as his prey. If the
wielder has the [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) class feature, he adds his sneak attack
damage to the next attack he makes against his prey, even if the wielder is not
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking) and the target is not denied its Dexterity bonus to AC, as long as
that attack is made before the end of the wielder's next turn. If the wielder is a rogue or slayer
with the [hunter's surprise](ref/talent/hunters-surprise) talent, he can use that talent one
additional time per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[doom](ref/spell/doom); **Price** 2,500 gp
