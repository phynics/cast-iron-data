**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A suit of withstanding armor is inscribed with eldritch runes across its surface and decorated with
the snarling countenances of specific creatures. Each suit of withstanding armor is tailored to a
specific type of creature, chosen from the same list as the options for [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane)
weapons. The wearer of this armor gains a +2 enhancement bonus on Knowledge checks related to the
chosen type of creature, and when the wearer is struck by a [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack)
or a confirmed critical hit by creatures of the chosen type, there is a 50% chance that the critical
hit or sneak attack is negated and damage is instead rolled normally. This does not stack with any
other form of critical hit negation, such as that imposed by the
[fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification) special ability. Only medium armor can be enchanted
with the withstanding special ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair); **Price** +1 bonus
