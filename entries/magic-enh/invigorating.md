**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 144\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. After an attack with this weapon drops a
living foe (kills it or drops it to negative hit points), the weapon infuses its wielder with vigor.
If its wielder is [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued), the weapon removes the fatigue. If
[exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted), the wielder becomes fatigued instead. If neither exhausted nor
fatigued, the wielder gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and a +10 foot enhancement bonus to
all modes of movement possessed until the end of the wielder's next turn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[good hope](ref/spell/good-hope); **Price** -
