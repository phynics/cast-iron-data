**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 143\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +5,000 gp; **Weight** -

Taking the form of a deity's favored weapon, a sacrosanct weapon serves as both a martial implement
and the corresponding deity's holy (or unholy) symbol. Once per day when the wielder uses the
sacrosanct weapon to [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), she can increase the radius of
the channel energy to 40 feet. They wielder must be able to channel positive or negative energy to
use this ability.

Alternatively, the wielder can expend one use of mythic power to use this ability instead of
expending its daily use. When using mythic power to activate this ability, it can be done at will.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), the crafter must be able to channel energy; **Price** -
