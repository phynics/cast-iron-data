**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 134\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This ability can only be placed on composite bows. An adaptive bow responds to the strength of its
wielder, acting as a bow with a strength rating equal to its wielder's Strength bonus. The wielder
can fire it with a lesser Strength bonus (and cause less damage) if desired.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[warp wood](ref/spell/warp-wood); **Price** +500 gp
