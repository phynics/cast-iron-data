**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 123, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 463\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** armor/shield quality; **Price** +49,000 gp; **Weight** -

Undead controlling armor and shields often have skeletal or other grisly decorations or flourishes
to their decor. They let the user control up to 26 Hit Dice of undead per day, as the
_[control undead](ref/spell/control-undead)_ spell. At dawn each day, the wearer loses control of
any undead still under his sway. Armor or a shield with this ability appears to be made of bone;
this feature is entirely decorative and has no other effect on the armor.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[control undead](ref/spell/control-undead); **Price** +24,500 gp
