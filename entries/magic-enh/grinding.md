**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 119\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor/shield quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A shield or suit of armor with this special ability is covered with jagged burrs and razor-sharp
serrations that saw and grind whenever the wearer is [grappled](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) or
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled). Any creature succeeding at a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat
maneuver check against the wearer takes damage equal to the armor or shield's enhancement bonus
(with each successful check) as the barbs and blades bite into it. This special ability counts as
[armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes) for the purpose of making attacks on the wearer's turn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Price** +1 bonus
