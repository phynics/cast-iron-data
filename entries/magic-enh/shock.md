**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

Upon command, a shock weapon is sheathed in crackling electricity that deals an extra 1d6 points of
electricity damage on a successful hit. The electricity does not harm the wielder. The effect
remains until another command is given.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[call lightning](ref/spell/call-lightning) or [lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt);
**Price** -
