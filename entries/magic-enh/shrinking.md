**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on melee weapons. When the wielder issues the command word,
a shrinking weapon shrinks to the size of a standard [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger). While in this
state, the weapon deals 1d4 points of damage but is more easily concealable. When the command word
is spoken again, the weapon returns to its original state.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Price** +500 gp
