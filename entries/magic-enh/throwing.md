**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A melee weapon crafted with this ability
gains a range increment of 10 feet and can be thrown by a wielder proficient in its normal use.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[magic stone](ref/spell/magic-stone); **Price** -
