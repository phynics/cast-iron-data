**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 210\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A shield or set of armor with this ability causes unpleasant resonant vibrations in weapons that
strike its wearer. As an immediate action up to three times per day, when the wearer is hit by a
melee attack made with a weapon, he can activate the armor or shield to send jarring vibrations back
through the weapon. The attacker must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude saving throw or become
[dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shatter](ref/spell/shatter); **Price** +2 bonus
