**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on ranged weapons or ammunition. Each time a ranged weapon
or ammunition with this ability hits a creature, its magic designates the target. Allies gain a +2
morale bonus on melee attack and melee damage rolls against the designated target for 1 round.
Multiple successful shots fired at the same target do not increase the bonuses or their duration.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[instant enemy](ref/spell/instant-enemy); **Price** -
