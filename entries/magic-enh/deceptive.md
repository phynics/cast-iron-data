**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A deceptive weapon's shape blurs and appears to bend unpredictably. When the weapon is in hand, the
wielder gains a bonus on Bluff checks to [feint](ref/rule/feint) equal to the enhancement bonus of
the weapon. In addition, when the wielder successfully confirms a critical hit with a deceptive
weapon against an opponent, she can attempt to feint against that opponent as an immediate action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dazzling blade](ref/spell/dazzling-blade); **Price** -
