## Anarchic, Axiomatic, Holy, Unholy

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg. 135-6, 143, 149, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 469\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

An anarchic weapon is infused with the power of chaos. It makes the weapon chaotically aligned and
thus bypasses the corresponding [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). It deals an extra 2d6
points of damage against all creatures of lawful alignment. It bestows one permanent
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on any lawful creature attempting to
wield it. The negative level remains as long as the weapon is in hand and disappears when the weapon
is no longer wielded. This negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including
[restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while the weapon is wielded.

An axiomatic weapon behaves like an anarchic weapon, but is law-aligned and deals its extra damage
to - or bestows a negative level on - chaotic creatures.

A holy weapon behaves like an anarchic weapon, but is good-aligned and deals its extra damage to -
or bestows a negative level on - evil creatures.

An unholy weapon behaves like an anarchic weapon, but is evil-aligned and deals its extra damage
to - or bestows a negative level on - good creatures.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor) and one of the
following: [chaos hammer](ref/spell/chaos-hammer) and the creator must be chaotic (anarchic only),
[order's wrath](ref/spell/orders-wrath) and the creator must be lawful (axiomatic only),
[holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite) and the creator must be good (holy only), or
