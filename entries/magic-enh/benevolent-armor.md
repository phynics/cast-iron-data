## Benevolent

**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This suit of armor best serves a wearer who focuses on assisting and protecting his allies.
Benevolent armor is usually decorated with motifs of prosperity, plenty, and generosity, such as
helping hands, cornucopias, and flourishing plants. When the wearer of a suit of benevolent armor
uses the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to give an ally a bonus to AC against an
opponent's next attack, he also adds the benevolent armor's enhancement bonus to the ally's AC.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[aid](ref/spell/aid); **Price** +1 bonus
