**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 26\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on heavy armor. Volcanic armor is often stony in appearance
and gives off a soft red glow. Once per day, the wearer can command the armor to erupt, causing a
cloud of smoke and hot molten liquid to shoot forth from the armor in a 5-foot radius centered on
the wearer. The smoke provides [concealment](ref/rule/concealment), and the liquid deals 1d6 points
of fire damage and ignites flammable substances. One round after the armor erupts, the smoke clears
and the liquid cools and solidifies, creating [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles).
The wearer of this armor isn't affected by the smoke, fire damage, or difficult terrain created by
this armor.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[incendiary cloud](ref/spell/incendiary-cloud); **Price** +2 bonus
