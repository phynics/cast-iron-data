**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 141, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 469\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A flaming burst weapon functions as a [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon that also explodes
with flame upon striking a successful critical hit. The fire does not harm the wielder. In addition
to the extra fire damage from the flaming ability, a flaming burst weapon deals an extra 1d10 points
of fire damage on a successful critical hit. If the weapon's critical multiplier is ×3, add an extra
2d10 points of fire damage instead, and if the multiplier is ×4, add an extra 3d10 points of fire
damage.

Even if the flaming ability is not active, the weapon still deals its extra fire damage on a
successful critical hit.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor) and
[fireball](ref/spell/fireball), [flame blade](ref/spell/flame-blade), or
[flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike); **Price** -
