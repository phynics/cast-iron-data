**Aura** strong illusion; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +16,875 gp; **Weight** -

This special ability functions as [sniping](ref/magic-enh/sniping), except the wielder gains a +15
competence bonus on Stealth checks to remain hidden after sniping with the weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [silence](ref/spell/silence); **Price** 8,437 gp
