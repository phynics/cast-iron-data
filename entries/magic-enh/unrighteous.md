## Righteous, Unrighteous, Unbound, Vigilant

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg. 121, 123, Advanced Player's Guide pg 283\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +27,000 gp; **Weight** -

Righteous armor is often engraved or enameled with religious symbols. Once per day on command, the
wearer can invoke an effect, as per the spell _[righteous might](ref/spell/righteous-might),_
lasting for 10 rounds. Righteous armor is always aligned toward good (positive energy), for the
effects of _[righteous might](ref/spell/righteous-might)._ Righteous armor bestows one permanent
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on any evil creature attempting to wear
it. The negative level remains as long as the armor is worn and disappears when it is removed. This
negative level cannot be overcome in any way (including by [restoration](ref/spell/restoration)
spells) while the armor is worn.

Unrighteous armor is often sculpted with unholy symbols, and works the same as righteous armor,
except the effect is aligned toward evil (negative energy), and bestows a negative level on good
creatures that wear it.

Unbound armor is often engraved or enameled with chaotic religious symbols, and works the same as
righteous or unrighteous armor, except the wearer gains DR 5/lawful instead of DR/evil or DR/good,
and it bestows a negative level on chaotic creatures that wear it.

Vigilant armor is often engraved or enameled with lawful religious symbols, and works as unbound
armor, except it grants DR 5/chaotic, and it bestows a negative level on lawful creatures that wear
it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[righteous might](ref/spell/righteous-might), creator must be lawful (vigilant armor only) or
chaotic (unbound armor only); **Price** +13,500 gp
