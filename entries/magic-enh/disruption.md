**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 140, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 469\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A disruption weapon is the bane of all undead. Any undead creature struck in combat must succeed at
a DC 14 Will save or be destroyed. A disruption weapon must be a bludgeoning melee weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[heal](ref/spell/heal); **Price** -
