**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A glorious weapon glows with a dazzling light equal to a _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ spell when
drawn. The wielder cannot suppress this light, though it can be temporarily suppressed by any effect
that can suppress _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)._ A glorious weapon flashes with light, and any
creature the wielder attacks in melee is automatically [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) until the
beginning of the wielder's next turn. When a glorious weapon confirms a critical hit, the target is
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) until the beginning of the wielder's next turn (DC 14 Will negates); if
the weapon's critical multiplier is greater than ×2, this blindness lasts 1 additional round per
multiple over ×2. Only a melee weapon can have the glorious ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness), [daylight](ref/spell/daylight),
[flare](ref/spell/flare); **Price** -
