**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 140\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A dispelling weapon functions like a [spell-storing](ref/magic-enh/spell-storing) weapon, but it may
only store _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic);_ however, the caster level check to dispel gains
an additional bonus equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus. This bonus also applies to a magus's
[dispelling strike](ref/arcana/dispelling-strike) arcana or a barbarian's
[spell sunder](ref/ragepower/spell-sunder) or [sunder enchantment](ref/ragepower/sunder-enchantment)
combat maneuver check.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), creator must be a caster of at least 10th level; **Price** -
