**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

When a limning weapon is wielded, a pale glow outlines it. On a successful strike against a creature
affected by a means of magical concealment, the weapon outlines the creature in
_[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire)_ (as the spell) for 1 round. Magical means of concealment
include [blur](ref/spell/blur), [displacement](ref/spell/displacement),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), and similar effects, whether from a spell,
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or supernatural means, but not those provided by
extraordinary abilities. A limning weapon has no special effect against creatures merely hiding or
concealed by nonmagical means, or those hidden by environmental conditions (even magical) such as
darkness or fog.
