**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 139, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 469\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A defending weapon allows the wielder to transfer some or all of the weapon's enhancement bonus to
his AC as a bonus that stacks with all others. As a free action, the wielder chooses how to allocate
the weapon's enhancement bonus at the start of his turn before using the weapon, and the bonus to AC
lasts until his next turn. This ability can only be placed on melee weapons.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shield](ref/spell/shield) or [shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith); **Price** -
