**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 136, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 469\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 16th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +4 bonus; **Weight** -

A brilliant energy weapon has its significant portion transformed into light, although this does not
modify the item's weight. It always gives off light as a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) (20-foot
radius). A brilliant energy weapon ignores nonliving matter. Armor and shield bonuses to AC
(including any enhancement bonuses to that armor) do not count against it because the weapon passes
through armor. (Dexterity, deflection, dodge, natural armor, and other such bonuses still apply.) A
brilliant energy weapon cannot harm undead, constructs, or objects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form), [continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame); **Price** -
