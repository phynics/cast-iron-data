**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 143\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A grounding weapon can safely touch
electrically charged surfaces without harm to its wielder. When used against a creature of the air
subtype, it deals an extra 1d6 points of damage. The wielder of a grounding weapon receives a +2
competence bonus on saving throws against air- and electricity-based effects, and the weapon itself
is immune to electricity damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shocking grasp](ref/spell/shocking-grasp); **Price** -
