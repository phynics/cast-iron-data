**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 143\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

These weapons were first created by deities and given to mortals so that they could combat mythic
foes. When the wielder confirms a critical hit against a mythic creature with a disjoining weapon,
the target loses the ability to expend its mythic power for 1d4 rounds. A mythic creature wielding a
disjoining weapon loses the ability to expend uses of mythic power for as long as he wields the
weapon. Only melee weapons can have this ability.
