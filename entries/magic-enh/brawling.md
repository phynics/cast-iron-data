## Brawling

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 116\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** -

The wearer of brawling armor gains a +2 bonus on unarmed attack and damage rolls, including combat
maneuver checks made to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple). Her unarmed strikes count as magic weapons for
the purpose of bypassing [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). These bonuses do not apply
to natural weapons. This special ability does not prevent the wearer's unarmed strikes from
provoking attacks of opportunity or make the wearer's unarmed strikes count as armed attacks. The
brawling ability can be applied only to light armor.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Price** +3 bonus
