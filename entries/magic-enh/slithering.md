**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on a melee weapon. A slithering weapon can bend and flex
when swung at a target, allowing it to fit through narrow gaps and around difficult angles with
greater ease and making the weapon difficult to strike. When a slithering weapon is used to attack a
creature with [cover](ref/rule/cover), the target's cover bonus to Armor Class is halved. In
addition, the wielder adds the weapon's enhancement bonus to her CMD against attempts to
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) or [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) her slithering weapon. If the wielder is
[squeezing](ref/misc/squeezing) in a narrow space, she takes only half the normal penalty on attack
rolls with her slithering weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[fluid form](ref/spell/fluid-form); **Price** -
