**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 26\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Price** +5,000 gp; **Weight** -

A suit of armor with this special ability alters the perceived aura of the wearer for the purpose of
divination spells that reveal auras (such as [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)) to an aura
decided when the armor is enchanted. The wearer always radiates this alternate alignment aura while
wearing a suit of deceiving armor, and cannot suppress this effect. The wearer does not count as
having this modified alignment for effects other than detection, such as smite, access to class
abilities, and spells that have alternative effects based on a target's alignment. If the wearer is
struck by an attack or effect that is more effective against evil-aligned creatures (such as smite,
[holy word](ref/spell/holy-word), etc.), the attacker perceives the strike as being far more
effective than it actually is. However, with a successful DC 20 Sense Motive check, the attacker
realizes something is amiss with the attack.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection), [unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight); **Price** +2,500
gp
