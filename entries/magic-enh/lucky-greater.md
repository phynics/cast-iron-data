**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on one-handed or two-handed firearms. A greater lucky
firearm is nearly identical to a [lucky](ref/magic-enh/lucky) firearm, but its reservoir holds 3
grit points instead of 1. A firearm cannot have both this special ability and the lucky special
ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[greater heroism](ref/spell/heroism-greater), creator must be a grit user; **Price** -
