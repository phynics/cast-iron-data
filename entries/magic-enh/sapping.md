**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 24\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed on only weapons that deal nonlethal damage. A sapping weapon
deals an additional 2d6 points of nonlethal damage. When the wielder confirms a critical hit with
the weapon, the target becomes [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for 5 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[pain strike](ref/spell/pain-strike), [touch of fatigue](ref/spell/touch-of-fatigue); **Price** -
