**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This armor special ability works only for evil creatures with the challenge ability (such as
[cavaliers](ref/class/cavalier)) or the smite good ability (such as
[antipaladins](ref/class/antipaladin), [half-fiends](ref/template/half-fiend), and creatures with
the [fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) creature template). A wearer with one of these abilities gains
a +2 profane bonus to AC against attacks from the chosen opponent.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[protection from good](ref/spell/protection-from-good); **Price** +1 bonus
