**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 177\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This ability can be placed only on a [corrosive](ref/magic-enh/corrosive) or
[corrosive burst](ref/magic-enh/corrosive-burst) weapon. When the wielder of a catalytic weapon hits
a creature, the weapon triggers a devastating chemical reaction that burns the struck creature. The
creature struck must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save, or take 1d6 points of acid damage at the
start of its turn for 1d4 rounds. Successive hits with the catalytic weapon increase the duration
but don't add to the damage. The creature can attempt to neutralize the reaction as a full-round
action as if it were [on fire](ref/rule/catching-on-fire) (including bonuses for dropping and
rolling on the ground or jumping into water). Anyone who hits a creature subject to this chemical
reaction with a natural weapon or unarmed strike takes 1d6 points of acid damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[acid arrow](ref/spell/acid-arrow); **Price** -
