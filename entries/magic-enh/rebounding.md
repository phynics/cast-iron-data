**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 21\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A rebounding shield provides an additional +2 enhancement bonus to AC against thrown weapons, and it
adds its total enhancement bonus to the wielder's touch AC against splash weapons. If an opponent
targets the wielder of a rebounding shield with a splash weapon and misses, the wielder may choose
the [misdirection](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) of the splash weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shield](ref/spell/shield); **Price** +1 bonus
