**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 26, Ultimate Equipment pg 115\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

As an immediate action, the wearer of a suit of balanced armor can reduce the enhancement bonus of
the armor to gain an equal enhancement bonus on one type of saving throw (Fortitude, Reflex, or
Will) designated when the armor is created. This effect lasts until the beginning of the wearer's
next turn. This ability can be applied only to medium armor.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance); **Price** +1 bonus
