**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 144, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 469\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This ability doubles the threat range of a weapon. Only piercing or slashing melee weapons can be
keen. If you roll this special ability randomly for an inappropriate weapon, reroll. This benefit
doesn't stack with any other effects that expand the threat range of a weapon (such as the
_[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge)_ spell or the [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical)
feat).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Price** -
