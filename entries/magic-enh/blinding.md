**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 116, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 463\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shield quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A shield with this ability flashes with a brilliant light up to twice per day upon command of the
wielder. Except for the wielder, anyone within 20 feet must make a successful DC 14 Reflex save or
be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[searing light](ref/spell/searing-light); **Price** +1 bonus
