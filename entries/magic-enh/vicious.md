**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. When a vicious weapon strikes an opponent,
it creates a flash of disruptive energy that resonates between the opponent and the wielder. This
energy deals an extra 2d6 points of damage to the opponent and 1d6 points of damage to the wielder.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[enervation](ref/spell/enervation); **Price** -
