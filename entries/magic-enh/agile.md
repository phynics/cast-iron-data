**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A character with [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) can apply her Dexterity modifier to
damage rolls with an agile weapon in place of her Strength modifier. This modifier to damage is not
increased for two-handed weapons, but is still reduced for off-hand weapons. This weapon special
ability can be placed only on melee weapons usable with Weapon Finesse.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace); **Price** -
