**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 149\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. When a [cavalier](ref/class/cavalier)
wielding a valiant weapon targets a foe with his challenge ability, the valiant weapon deals an
additional 1d6 points of damage against that foe. The wielder receives a +2 bonus on combat maneuver
checks made to [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) or [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) a challenged foe's weapon as
well as a +4 bonus to his CMD against disarm and sunder attacks from that foe.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Price** -
