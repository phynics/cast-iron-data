**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 147, Ultimate Combat pg 142\
**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on firearms. A greater reliable firearm is enchanted so as
to be less likely to jam than other firearms. The greater reliable special ability reduces the
misfire value of the affected firearm by 4 (minimum 0). This reduction occurs after any increases
are calculated for firing with the broken condition, or for any other effect that might increase the
misfire value of a firearm. A firearm with the greater reliable special ability cannot have the
[reliable](ref/magic-enh/reliable) special ability.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[make whole](ref/spell/make-whole); **Price** -
