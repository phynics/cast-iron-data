**Aura** faint abjuration and evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +5,000 gp; **Weight** -

This armor is usually decorated with engravings of storm clouds and lightning bolts. If an attack
strikes the wearer and deals at least 10 points of electricity damage, the armor becomes visibly
charged with electricity for 1 round. As a swift action on the wearer's next turn that does not
provoke an attack of opportunity, she may use _[shocking grasp](ref/spell/shocking-grasp)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), dealing 1d6 points of electricity damage for
every 10 points of damage dealt to the wearer since her last turn (maximum 5d6 for 50 or more points
of electrical damage taken). Determine the effect of the
_[shocking grasp](ref/spell/shocking-grasp)_ based on how much damage the triggering electrical
attack would have dealt the wearer before any applicable resistances or immunities.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), [shocking grasp](ref/spell/shocking-grasp); **Price**
+5,000 gp
