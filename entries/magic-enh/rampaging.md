**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 28\
**Aura** faint conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

When the wearer of a suit of rampaging armor [charges](ref/rule/charge) or attempts a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) combat maneuver check, he can ignore
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). Once per day as a swift action, when the wearer
of a suit of rampaging armor charges or performs a bull rush combat maneuver, he can turn each
square he moves through into difficult terrain as he demolishes everything in his path. This change
of terrain remains for 1 minute. The rampaging special ability can be applied only to heavy armor.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[feather step](ref/spell/feather-step), [stone call](ref/spell/stone-call); **Price** +2 bonus
