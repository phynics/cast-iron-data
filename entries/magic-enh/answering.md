**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 212\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This ability can be placed only on melee weapons. For the purpose of the
[opportune parry and riposte](ref/swashdeed/opportune-parry-and-riposte) deed, the weapon's
enhancement bonus increases by 4 (to a maximum of +5).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[good hope](ref/spell/good-hope), creator must have the opportune parry and riposte deed;
**Price** -
