**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on melee bludgeoning weapons. A smashing weapon deals an
additional 2d6 points of damage to inanimate objects, including when it is used to
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) armor and weapons.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Price** -
