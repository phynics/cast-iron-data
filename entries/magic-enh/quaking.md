**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 30\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on melee bludgeoning weapons. The wielder of a quaking
weapon can strike the ground as a standard action to perform a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver
that applies to all foes within a 5-foot-radius [spread](ref/misc/spread), a 10-foot cone, or a
20-foot line along the ground. The wielder can trip creatures of any size this way. This trip
maneuver attempt provokes attacks of opportunity unless the wielder has some feat or ability that
prevents it, and the wielder can never be knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone) as a result of failing
this special trip combat maneuver check.
