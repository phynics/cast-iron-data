**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 147\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +4 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on bows. Once per round, when a shot made with this weapon
misses its target, as a free action the weapon's wielder can cause that shot to swerve and try to
hit the target again. The arrow fired gets a second attack at the same attack bonus as the first
attack it made.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[ricochet shot](ref/spell/ricochet-shot); **Price** -
