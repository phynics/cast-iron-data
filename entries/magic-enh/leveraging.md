**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on melee weapons. The wielder of a leveraging weapon gains a
bonus equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus to her Combat Maneuver Defense against
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [drag](ref/rule/drag), [reposition](ref/rule/reposition), and
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuvers. The leveraging weapon's enhancement bonus is doubled when
applied to combat maneuver checks to attempt bull rush, drag, reposition, and trip combat maneuvers.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person); **Price** -
