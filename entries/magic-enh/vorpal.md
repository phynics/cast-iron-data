**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 149, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 469\
**Aura** strong necromancy and transmutation; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +5 bonus; **Weight** -

This potent and feared ability allows the weapon to sever the heads of those it strikes. Upon a roll
of natural 20 (followed by a successful roll to confirm the critical hit), the weapon severs the
opponent's head (if it has one) from its body. Some creatures, such as many aberrations and all
oozes, have no heads. Others, such as golems and undead creatures other than vampires, are not
affected by the loss of their heads. Most other creatures, however, die when their heads are cut
off. A vorpal weapon must be a slashing melee weapon. If you roll this special ability randomly for
an inappropriate weapon, reroll.
