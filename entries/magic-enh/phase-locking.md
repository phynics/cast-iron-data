**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 146\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A phase locking weapon interferes with dimensional travel. A creature damaged by a phase locking
weapon is affected as though by the _[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ spell for 1
round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor); **Price** -
