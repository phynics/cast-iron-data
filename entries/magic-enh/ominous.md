**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 146\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. An ominous weapon trails a shadowy haze
behind every stroke, and moans a menacing dirge in battle. An ominous weapon adds its enhancement
bonus on Intimidate checks made by the wielder. In addition, when an ominous weapon confirms a
critical hit, the target is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 minute (DC 13 Will negates); if the
weapon's critical multiplier is greater than ×2, this condition lasts 1 additional minute per
multiple over ×2. A creature that gains the shaken condition from an ominous weapon cannot gain that
condition again from the same weapon for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[doom](ref/spell/doom) or [scare](ref/spell/scare); **Price** -
