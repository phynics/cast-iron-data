**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 117, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 463\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** armor/shield quality; **Price** +18,000 gp; **Weight** -

A suit of armor or a shield with this special ability protects against one type of energy (acid,
cold, electricity, fire, or sonic) and is designed with patterns depicting the element it protects
against. The armor absorbs the first 10 points of energy damage per attack that the wearer would
normally take (similar to the _[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy)_ spell).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy); **Price** +9,000 gp
