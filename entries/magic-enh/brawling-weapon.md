## Brawling

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 212\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This weapon special ability can be applied only to [brass knuckles](ref/eq-weapon/brass-knuckles), a
[cestus](ref/eq-weapon/cestus), a [sap](ref/eq-weapon/sap), or a light bludgeoning weapon. The
wielder of this weapon gains an enhancement bonus on combat maneuver checks equal to the enhancement
bonus of the weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip), [guidance](ref/spell/guidance); **Price** -
