**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 26\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can be placed only on firearms or firearm ammunition. Whenever a dazzling
weapon is fired, it emits an explosive flash that [dazzles](ref/misc/dazzled) all foes adjacent to
the bullet's path or within or adjacent to the cone of a [scatter](ref/misc/scatter) shot. This
condition lasts a number of rounds equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[flare burst](ref/spell/flare-burst); **Price** -
