**Sources:** Quests and Campaigns pg 30\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A heretical weapon functions as a [fervent](ref/magic-enh/fervent) weapon, but when wielded against
a member of the religion to which it is attuned, the weapon's enhancement bonus is +2 better than
its actual bonus. It also deals an extra 2d6 points of damage against followers of the designated
religion. This extra damage is not multiplied on a critical hit.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[align weapon](ref/spell/align-weapon), [weapon of awe](ref/spell/weapon-of-awe), creator must be a
member of the attuned religion; **Price** -
