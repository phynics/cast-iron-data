**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 26\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

Armor and shields with the frosted ability are coated in a thin layer of frost, making the armor or
shield shimmer faintly and take on a bluish-white hue. The wearer gains a +4 bonus to CMD against
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuvers. Additionally, the armor deals 1d8 points of cold
damage every round to opponents that [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) or
[pin](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) the wearer or [swallow the wearer whole](ref/umr/swallow-whole).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[chill metal](ref/spell/chill-metal); **Price** +2 bonus
