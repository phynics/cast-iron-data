**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 115, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 463\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shield quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This shield protects the wielder as if he had the [Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows) feat.
Once per round when he would normally be struck by a ranged weapon, he can make a DC 20 Reflex save.
If the ranged weapon (or piece of ammunition) has an enhancement bonus, the DC increases by that
amount. If he succeeds, the shield deflects the weapon. He must be aware of the attack and not
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed). Attempting to deflect a ranged weapon doesn't count as an
action. Exceptional ranged weapons, such as boulders hurled by giants or
[acid arrows](ref/spell/acid-arrow), can't be deflected.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shield](ref/spell/shield); **Price** +2 bonus
