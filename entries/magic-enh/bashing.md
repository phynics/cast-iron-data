**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 115, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 463\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** shield quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A shield with this special ability is designed to perform a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash). A
bashing shield deals damage as if it were a weapon of
[two size categories larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) (a Medium light shield thus deals 1d6
points of damage and a Medium heavy shield deals 1d8 points of damage). The shield acts as a +1
weapon when used to bash. Only light and heavy shields can have this ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Price** +1 bonus
