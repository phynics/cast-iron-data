**Sources:** Monster Summoner's Handbook pg 21\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A summon bane weapon excels against summoned creatures, including those summoned by conjuration
(summoning) spells (such as _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ and
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spells), those summoned by conjuration
(calling) spells (such as [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) and
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding)), and eidolons. Against such summoned creatures, the
weapon's enhancement bonus is +2 better than its normal bonus. It also deals an extra 2d6 points of
damage against such foes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) or
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4); **Price** -
