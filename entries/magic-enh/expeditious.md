**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 118\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +4,000 gp; **Weight** -

Three times per day as a swift action, the wearer of expeditious armor can summon a burst of speed
and gain a +10-foot enhancement bonus on all modes of movement for 1 round. This ability can be
applied to any kind of armor, but not shields.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat); **Price** +2,000 gp
