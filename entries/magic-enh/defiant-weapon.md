## Defiant

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 140\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A defiant weapon helps its wielder stay
alive in desperate conditions. It stays in its wielder's hand even if she is
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked), [stunned](ref/misc/stunned), or [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious).
She adds the weapon's enhancement bonus as a bonus on checks to stabilize when
[dying](ref/misc/dying) and on saving throws to end ongoing conditions such as disease, poison, and
[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person). If the wielder possesses
[Heroic Defiance](ref/feat/heroic-defiance), [Heroic Recovery](ref/feat/heroic-recovery),
[Improved Great Fortitude](ref/feat/improved-great-fortitude),
[Improved Iron Will](ref/feat/improved-iron-will), or
[Improved Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/improved-lightning-reflexes), she gains a number of
additional daily uses equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus that can be used on any of these
feats.
