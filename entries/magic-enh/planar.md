**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 146\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A planar weapon is effective against all types of extradimensional beings, able to pierce their
resistance to physical harm. When used to attack outsiders, a planar weapon ignores 5 points of
their [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Price** -
