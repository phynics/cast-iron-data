**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This weapon responds to [channeled positive and negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy). When
the wielder spends a swift action to channel energy through the weapon, it ignites with a strange
gray flame that sheds light as a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch), increases the weapon's enhancement
bonus by +1, and deals +1d6 damage (as the divine power from [flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike))
to creatures struck by the weapon. This flame lasts for 1 round for every d6 of damage or healing
the channeling normally provides. When charged with positive energy, the flame is a silvery gray,
good creatures are immune to the weapon's extra damage, and the weapon counts as a good and silver
weapon for the purpose of bypassing [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). When charged with
negative energy, the flame is an ashen gray, evil creatures are immune to the weapon's extra damage,
and the weapon counts as an evil and cold iron weapon for the purpose of bypassing damage reduction.
This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Channel Smite](ref/feat/channel-smite), [align weapon](ref/spell/align-weapon); **Price** -
