**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This armor protects its wearer from harmful negative and positive energy, including
[channeled energy](ref/ability/channel-energy). The armor absorbs the first 10 points of positive or
negative energy damage per attack that the wearer would normally take. The wearer has a 25% chance
to ignore [negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) from any attack. Deathless
armor does not block healing of any kind and does not protect against positive or negative energy
effects that do not deal damage or bestow negative levels. The deathless ability can be applied to
armor of any sort, but not shields.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward); **Price** +1 bonus
