**Aura** strong abjuration and evocation \[good\]; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** -

A redeemed weapon was once a corrupt unholy weapon. The taint of evil has been purged from it,
however, and the weapon is now in all ways a [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) weapon, dealing an
additional 2d6 points of damage against all creatures of evil alignment. In addition, the wielder
gains a sacred bonus on saving throws equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus against the spells and
abilities of evil outsiders and a +5 competence bonus on Knowledge (planes) checks to identify evil
outsiders and their special powers or vulnerabilities. When the redeemed weapon special ability is
added to an [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) weapon, the weapon loses the unholy property and gains
the redeemed property, and the crafter must pay the price of adding a +1 enhancement bonus to the
weapon.
