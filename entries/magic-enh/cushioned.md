**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1,000 gp; **Weight** -

Armor with this special ability reduces the damage its wearer takes from falling as if the distance
were 20 feet less. The cushioned special ability can be applied only to armor made primarily from
cloth and padding, such as [padded](ref/eq-armor/padded),
[quilted cloth](ref/eq-armor/quilted-cloth), and [silken ceremonial](ref/eq-armor/silken-ceremonial)
armor.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall); **Price** 500 gp
