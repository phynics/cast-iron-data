**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 24\
**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

Three times per day, the wearer of a suit of phantasmal armor can leave an illusory afterimage of
herself in a square she occupied as part of a move action. This double is created through illusion
magic from the [shadow subschool](ref/spelldef/shadow-subschool) and is partly real. The illusory
double cannot move from its designated square but otherwise reacts and maneuvers as directed by the
wearer of a suit of phantasmal armor. The double cannot attack, but it can menace opponents and
distract them, and thus counts as an ally for the purpose of determining
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking). The illusory double has an AC equal to 12 + the enhancement bonus of
the phantasmal armor. A successful melee or ranged attack against the illusory double (or any damage
from an area effect attack) dismisses the effect. The phantasmal special ability can be applied only
to light armor.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shadow projection](ref/spell/shadow-projection); **Price** +2 bonus
