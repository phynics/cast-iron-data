**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A dueling weapon bears magical enhancements that makes it particularly effective at performing
certain combat maneuvers. When a dueling weapon is used to perform a combat maneuver that utilizes
the weapon only, it grants a luck bonus equal to twice its enhancement bonus on the CMB check made
to carry out the maneuver. The dueling weapon also grants this same luck bonus to the wielder's CMD
score against these types of combat maneuvers. These combat maneuvers include
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) and [trip](ref/rule/trip) maneuvers, but not
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [grapple](ref/rule/grapple), or [overrun](ref/rule/overrun)
maneuvers. If you're using the additional combat maneuvers, this also includes any
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) maneuvers that utilize the weapon, as well as
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuvers, but not [drag](ref/rule/drag) or
[steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuvers. Note that this luck bonus stacks with the weapon's
enhancement bonus, which in and of itself adds to CMB checks normally.
