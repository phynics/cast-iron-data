**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

The weapon channels and amplifies the wielder's ki, allowing her to use her special ki attacks
through the weapon as though they were unarmed attacks. These attacks include the
[monk's](ref/class/monk) ki strike, quivering palm, and the [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist)
feat. The save DC for ki abilities used through the weapon increases by half the enhancement bonus
of the weapon (minimum 1).

After a successful strike with a ki intensifying weapon, the wielder can spend 1 ki point as a swift
action to perform a combat maneuver to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [disarm](ref/rule/disarm),
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition), or [trip](ref/rule/trip) as a free action without provoking
attacks of opportunity. Any bonuses or penalties applicable to the weapon attack also apply to this
combat maneuver check. The wielder cannot move from her space as a part of the chosen maneuver.

Only melee weapons can have the ki intensifying ability. It cannot be placed on an
[amulet of mighty fists](ref/magic-wondrous/amulet-of-mighty-fists) or similar non-weapon item.
Weapons with the [ki focus](ref/magic-enh/ki-focus) ability can be upgraded to be ki intensifying.
