**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

A suit of trapwarding armor helps to protect the wearer against all manner of traps. The wearer of a
suit of trapwarding armor gains a luck bonus on all saving throws against traps equal to the armor's
enhancement bonus.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[find traps](ref/spell/find-traps); **Price** +1 bonus
