**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

A creature that has taken nonlethal damage from a peaceful weapon becomes [shaken](ref/misc/shaken)
for 1 round each time it deals lethal damage to another living creature. The duration stacks with
multiple attacks, but this effect can't cause the affected creature to become
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) (even if it is affected by other fear effects). If the affected
creature's nonlethal damage is completely healed, this prevents subsequent attacks from extending
the duration of the shaken condition, but doesn't end the shaken condition from attacks already
made.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions), [cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds);
**Price** -
