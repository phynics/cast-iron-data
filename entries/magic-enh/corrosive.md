**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 138, Advanced Player's Guide pg 287\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

Upon command, a corrosive weapon becomes slick with acid that deals an extra 1d6 points of acid
damage on a successful hit. The acid does not harm the wielder. The effect remains until another
command is given.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[acid arrow](ref/spell/acid-arrow); **Price** -
