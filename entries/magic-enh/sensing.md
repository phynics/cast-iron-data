**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** -

A set of sensing armor compensates when its wearer has been [blinded](ref/misc/blinded), is in total
darkness (as long as the wearer doesn't have [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) or the
[see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) ability), or is in magical darkness (as long as the
wearer doesn't have the see in darkness ability). When one of these conditions affects the wearer,
sensing armor immediately grants its wearer [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) with a 5-foot range and
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) with a 60-foot range. As soon as the wearer can see, the additional
senses end. The wearer can't gain these abilities by closing her eyes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Price** +3 bonus
