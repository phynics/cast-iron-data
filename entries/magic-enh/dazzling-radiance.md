**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 7th\
**Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special weapon ability can be placed on only melee weapons. When a character wielding a
dazzling radiance weapon uses the [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display) feat, the weapon
flashes in a burst of bright color. All creatures within 15 feet of the wielder who can see the
display must attempt a DC 17 Fortitude save. Those who fail are [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 2
rounds and [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for an additional 1d4 rounds; those who succeed are instead
dazzled for 1d4 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[color spray](ref/spell/color-spray); **Price** -
