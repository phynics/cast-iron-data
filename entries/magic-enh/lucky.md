**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 145, Ultimate Combat pg 142\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

This special ability can only be placed on one-handed or two-handed firearms. A lucky firearm has
its own magical reservoir of grit. Usually this grit is stored within the marks of an engraving or
in a trinket that hangs from the firearm. Often these marks take the form of holy symbols or
fetishes, but such a reservoir can take just about any form. This reservoir holds 1 grit point,
which is refreshed at the start of each day. Whether or not the wielder of a lucky firearm has any
deeds, she can always spend 1 grit point from the lucky firearm to reroll an attack from it that
would result in a misfire. When the wielder does so, she must take the second result, even if that
attack roll also results in a misfire.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[heroism](ref/spell/heroism), creator must be a grit user; **Price** -
