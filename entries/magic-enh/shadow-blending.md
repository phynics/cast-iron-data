**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** -

Shadow blending armor is drab and mutes any colors in its wearer's attire into dull grays. Attacks
against the wearer in [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) have a 40% miss chance instead of the
normal 20%. This ability does not grant [total concealment](ref/rule/concealment); it only increases
the miss chance. If the armor also has the [shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow),
[improved shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow-improved), or [greater shadow](ref/magic-enh/shadow-greater)
armor special ability, the miss chance increases to 50%.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk); **Price** +2 bonus
