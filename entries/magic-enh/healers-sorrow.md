**Aura** weak necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

The sickly gray wood of this arrow seems quite fragile and delicate. Created from pale necromantic
bone or casket wood, this arrow splinters when it impacts, embedding pieces of itself in the target
and making the wound resistant to magical healing. Removing the splinters requires 10 minutes and a
DC 25 Heal check. Until the splinters are removed, all magical healing effects used on the target
restore only half as many hit points as normal. This applies whether the target requires positive or
negative energy for healing.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell); **Price** -
