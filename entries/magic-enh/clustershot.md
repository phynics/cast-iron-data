**Sources:** Elves of Golarion pg 23\
**Aura** weak transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** -

These arrows are inscribed with a spiral groove down the length of the haft and an eye-glyph on the
head. They are designed to maintain accuracy when firing multiple arrows at the same time (such as
with the [Manyshot](ref/feat/manyshot) feat) by steering their paths toward the impact point of the
first arrow. For a clustershot arrow to function, all arrows fired in the attack must have this
property. If the first arrow can deal [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) damage or is a
critical hit, the remaining arrows from that shot can deal half normal sneak attack damage, and if
they critically hit deal half normal critical hit damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon); **Price** -
