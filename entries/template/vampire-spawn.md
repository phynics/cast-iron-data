**Vampire Spawn**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Source:** Monster Codex/244

The following template can be used to create unique vampire spawn with class levels.

"Vampire spawn" is an acquired template that can be added to any living creature with 4 or more Hit
Dice (referred to hereafter as the base creature). A vampire spawn uses the base creature's stats
and abilities except as noted here.

**CR:** Same as the base creature + 1.

**Alignment:** Any evil.

**Type:** The creature's type changes to [undead](ref/type/undead). Do not recalculate class Hit
Dice, BAB, or saves.

**Senses:** A vampire spawn gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to 60 feet.

**AC:** Natural armor increases to +4, unless the base creature's natural armor is already +4 or
higher.

**Hit Dice:** Change all racial Hit Dice to d8s. Class Hit Dice are unaffected. As undead, vampire
spawn use their Cha modifier to determine bonus hit points (instead of Con).

**Defensive Abilities:** A vampire spawn gains [channel resistance](ref/umr/channel-resistance) +2,
DR 5/silver, [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 against cold and electricity, and
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2.

**Weaknesses:** A vampire spawn has the same weaknesses of the vampire that created it.

> **Resurrection Vulnerability (Su):** A [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) or similar spell cast on
> a vampire spawn destroys it (Will negates). Using the spell in this way does not require a
> material component.

**Melee:** A vampire spawn gains a [slam attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) if the base creature
didn't have one. Damage for the slam depends on the vampire spawn's size.

**Special Attacks:** A vampire spawn gains a vampire's blood drain and dominate special attacks. The
vampire who created the spawn can influence a spawn's dominated creature as if she had dominated it
herself.

**Energy Drain (Su):** A creature hit by a vampire spawn's slam (or other natural weapon) gains 1
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels). This ability only triggers only once
per round.

**Special Qualities:** A vampire spawn gains the gaseous form, shadowless, and spider climb
abilities of a vampire.

**Ability Scores:** Cha +2.

**Skills:** Spawn gain a +8 racial bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) and
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks.

**Feats:** Spawn gain [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) ([Perception](ref/skill/perception)) as a
bonus feat.
