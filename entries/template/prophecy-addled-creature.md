**Prophecy-Addled Creature**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Pathfinder \#125: Tower of the Drowned Dead/50

Prophecy-addled creatures receive powerful oracular insights, but they cannot easily distinguish
between these flashes of mystical insight and reality. A prophecy-addled creature's quick and
rebuild rules are the same.

**Rebuild Rules:** **AC** increase insight bonus by +2; **Saves** +2 insight bonus on Fortitude and
Reflex saves, -4 penalty on saves against confusion effects; **Special Qualities** If the creature
has flash of brutality, flash of insight, or another special quality that allows it to reroll a d20
roll a certain number of times each day, it can use that ability two additional times each day. If
not, the creature gains the following special quality.
