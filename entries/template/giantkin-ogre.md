**Giantkin Ogre**

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Inherited:** present

**Simple:** present

[Ogres](ref/monster/ogre) sometimes interbreed with other giants. The result of such crossbreeding
is a giantkin ogre, which gains some abilities from its non-ogre parents. In areas where ogre clans
live near large populations of giants, interbreeding can be so common that entire ogre clans consist
solely of giantkin.

Blessed with the blood of greater giants, giantkin ogres are larger and stronger than their normal
ogre relatives and gain abilities from their [giant](ref/subtype/giant) progenitors.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Hit points** +10 hit points; **Saving Throws** +1 racial bonus to all saving
throws; **Ability Scores** +4 Strength, +4 Constitution; **Special Attacks**
[rock throwing](ref/umr/rock-throwing) (1d6, 90 ft.), **Special** see table.

|Crossbreed|Special Abilities|
| --- | --- |
|Fireblood|[Resist](ref/umr/resistance) fire 10|
|Frostblood|Resist cold 10|
|Hillblood|Increase natural armor by 2|
|Stoneblood|[Rock catching](ref/umr/rock-catching), rock throwing (1d8, 120 ft.)|
