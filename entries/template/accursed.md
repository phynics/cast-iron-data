**Accursed**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Horror Adventures/248

An accursed creature suffers under a powerful curse and can spread a spiteful curse of its own to
others. Choose a thematic curse that you can convey to the players, but don't change the creature's
statistics. You can use the sample curses as inspiration.

**Quick Rules:** Two claw attacks that deal 1d4 points of damage
[(for Medium creatures)](ref/umr/natural-attacks); natural attacks cause
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse) (DC = 10 + 1/2 creature's HD + creature's Cha modifier, CL =
creature's HD) on a successful hit (this can inflict any effect of
_[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ or spread the creature's personal curse).

**Rebuild Rules:** **Melee** two claw attacks that each deal 1d4 points of damage (for Medium
creatures); **Special Attacks** natural attacks cause _[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ (DC =
10 + 1/2 creature's HD + creature's Cha modifier, CL = creature's HD) on a successful hit (this can
inflict any effect of _[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ or spread the creature's personal
curse); **Special Qualities** the creature is under a particular curse.
