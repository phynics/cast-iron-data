**Calcified**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

A creature with the calcified template is preserved by the toxic fluids pumped into its body by an
[incutilis lord](ref/monster/incutilis-incutilis-lord). Though the creature's identity is lost and
its life soon fades, the incutilis lord retains use of the creature's feats, physical skills,
extraordinary abilities, [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural
abilities. This template can be applied to any living corporeal creature.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Senses** [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.; **AC** increase natural
armor by 4; **Immune** mind-affecting effects; **Ability Scores** +4 Str and Con. A calcified
creature has no Intelligence score, but can use its physical skills as directed by the incutilis
lord that created it and does not lose any skill ranks. **Skills:** +8 racial bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks in dim or no light.
