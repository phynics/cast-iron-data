**Lycanthropic Creature**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Horror Adventures/249

Lycanthropic creatures can act as simpler variants of any sort of lycanthrope in hybrid form, but
the default is for a werewolf-like creature, so if the base lycanthrope has different features,
adjust the creature accordingly (for instance, a wereshark can swim). This simple template reflects
the creature's hybrid form. The creature can't take full animal form, and when it's in its ordinary
form, remove this template with the exceptions of change shape,
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), and scent. Lycanthropic creatures don't have to be
humanoids, and at your discretion, a lycanthropic creature can afflict creatures of its own type
with lycanthropy, in addition to humanoids.

**Quick Rules:** +1 to AC; DR 5/silver; +1 on rolls based on Str, Dex, and Con; +1 hp/HD;
[bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals 1d6 points of damage (for Medium creatures) plus
curse of lycanthropy;^[1](ref/template/lycanthropic-creature#footnote-l)^ change shape (normal or
hybrid form; polymorph); lycanthropic empathy^[1](ref/template/lycanthropic-creature#footnote-l)^
(as appropriate for the lycanthrope).

**Rebuild Rules:** **Type** gain the shapechanger subtype; DR 5/silver; **Senses**
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), scent; **AC** natural armor bonus increases by 1;
**Melee** bite attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (for Medium creatures) plus curse of
lycanthropy;^[1](ref/template/lycanthropic-creature#footnote-l)^ **Special Attacks** curse of
lycanthropy;^[1](ref/template/lycanthropic-creature#footnote-l)^ **Special Qualities** change shape
(normal or hybrid form; polymorph), lycanthropic
empathy^[1](ref/template/lycanthropic-creature#footnote-l)^ (as appropriate for the lycanthrope);
**Ability Scores** +2 Str, +2 Dex, +2 Con.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-l}

This ability can be found in the [lycanthrope](ref/template/lycanthrope) template.
