**Chthonic**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +0 or +1

**Maybesummon:** present

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Monster Summoner's Handbook/19

Applying this template to a summoned creature requires the feat
[Versatile Summon Monster](ref/feat/versatile-summon-monster) or
[Versatile Summon Nature's Ally](ref/feat/versatile-summon-natures-ally).

Chthonic creatures are native denizens of the Elemental [Plane of Earth](ref/rule/plane-of-earth).
They produce acid, which they use to help them burrow quickly through the dense rock of their homes.
This template can be applied only to a non-outsider that has none of the following subtypes: air,
cold, earth, fire, or water. A chthonic creature's CR increases by 1 only if the base creature has 5
or more HD.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Type** The creature gains the [earth](ref/subtype/earth) subtype; **Senses**
gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.; **Defensive Abilities** gains DR and resistance to
acid as noted on the table below; **Speed** gains a burrow speed equal to half its highest speed
(its tunnels always collapse behind it, and never leave behind a usable passage); **Attacks** gains
bonus acid damage as noted on the table below on attacks with natural weapons.
