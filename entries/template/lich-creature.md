**Lich Creature**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Horror Adventures/249

While it might not have followed the traditional path to lichdom, which requires a strong aptitude
for magic, a lich creature's connection to an object allows it to survive as an undead indefinitely,
returning each time its foes destroy it unless they can destroy the lich creature's phylactery.

**Quick Rules:** Counts as [undead](ref/type/undead); [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.;
rejuvenation;^[1](ref/template/lich-creature#footnote-l)^ immune to cold and electricity; undead
immunities; +1 on rolls based on Int, Wis, and Cha; touch attack deals 1d8 + 1/2 HD points of damage
plus paralyzing touch;^[1](ref/template/lich-creature#footnote-l)^ fear
aura.^[1](ref/template/lich-creature#footnote-l)^

**Rebuild Rules:** **Type** change to undead; **Senses** [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.;
**Defensive Abilities** rejuvenation;^[1](ref/template/lich-creature#footnote-l)^ **Immune** cold,
electricity; **Melee** touch attack deals 1d8 + 1/2 HD points of damage plus paralyzing
touch;^[1](ref/template/lich-creature#footnote-l)^ **Special Attacks** fear
aura,^[1](ref/template/lich-creature#footnote-l)^ paralyzing
touch;^[1](ref/template/lich-creature#footnote-l)^ **Ability Scores** +2 Int, +2 Wis, +2 Cha.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-l}

This ability is described in the [lich](ref/template/lich) template.
