**Pickin Ogre**

**Challenge rating:** +0

**Inherited:** present

**Simple:** present

These tall, bone-thin ogres have thick, bark-like skin and spiked growths all over their bodies, and
their hands end in sharp, thorn-like claws.

**Quick Rules:** +2 on AC, -1 hp/HD, and -1 on Fortitude saves and Constitution checks; gains 2
[claw attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d6); +4 racial bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
checks in forests.

**Rebuild Rules:** -2 Constitution, +2 natural armor bonus; **Attacks** gains 2 claw attacks (1d6);
**Special** +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks in forests.
