**Ogrekin**

**Challenge rating:** +1 (minimum 2)

**Inherited:** present

The result of an unfortunate union between an ogre and a humanoid, the ogrekin (or
half-ogre^[1](ref/template/ogrekin#footnote-h)^) is cursed with horrific malformations due to its
tragic ancestry. Shunned by both parents, ogrekin tend to form unstable clans of their own, often
resorting to inbreeding to sustain the collective. While good-natured ogrekin are not entirely
unheard of, they are far and few between.

### Creating an Ogrekin

"Ogrekin" is an inherited template that can be added to any Medium [humanoid](ref/type/humanoid)
(referred to hereafter as the base creature). An ogrekin retains all the base creature's statistics
and special abilities except as noted here.

**CR:** Same as base creature +1 (minimum 2).

**Alignment:** Usually evil.

**Type:** The creature's subtype changes to [giant](ref/subtype/giant).

**AC:** Natural armor improves by +3.

**Ability Scores:** Str +6, Con +4, Int -2, Cha -2.

**Special Qualities and Defenses:** An ogrekin gains [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).
In addition, ogrekin receive two random deformities - one beneficial and one disadvantageous.

**Beneficial Deformities:** The ogrekin gains one of these, chosen randomly.

|d6|Deformity|
| --- | --- |
|1|**Oversized Limb:** The ogrekin can wield weapons one size category larger than normal without any penalty and gains a +2 bonus to its Strength.|
|2|**Oversized Maw:** The ogrekin gains a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d4).|
|3|**Quick Metabolism:** The ogrekin gains a +2 racial bonus on Fortitude saves.|
|4|**Thick Skin:** Improve natural armor bonus by +2.|
|5|**Vestigial Limb:** Vestigial third arm (can't be used to use items) grants a +4 racial bonus on [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) checks.|
|6|**Vestigial Twin:** A malformed twin's head juts out from the ogrekin, providing the ogrekin with [all-around vision](ref/umr/all-around-vision).|

**Disadvantageous Deformities:** The ogrekin gains one of these, chosen randomly.

|d6|Deformity|
| --- | --- |
|1|**Deformed Hand:** One hand can't wield weapons; -2 penalty on attack rolls with two-handed weapons.|
|2|**Fragile:** The ogrekin is particularly frail and gaunt. It loses its +4 racial bonus to Con.|
|3|**Light Sensitive:** The ogrekin gains [light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity).|
|4|**Obese:** The ogrekin takes a -2 penalty to Dexterity (minimum score of 1).|
|5|**Stunted Legs:** The ogrekin's base speed is reduced by 10 feet (minimum base speed of 5 feet).|
|6|**Weak Mind:** The ogrekin's head is huge and misshapen. It gains a -2 penalty on Will saving throws.|

#### Additional Deformities

**Sources:** Inner Sea Monster Codex pg 47\
All ogrekin have distinct deformities as a result of their incessant inbreeding and the partially
incompatible genetics between ogres and other humanoid races. In addition to the deformities
available above, ogrekin can have any of the new deformities presented below.

**Beneficial Deformities:** The ogrekin gains one of the following, chosen randomly.

|d10|Deformity|
| --- | --- |
|1|**Bulging Eye:** The ogrekin gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet.|
|2|**Enlarged Skull:** The ogrekin takes no penalty to Intelligence from the ogrekin template.|
|3|**Gnarled Hands:** The ogrekin gains a claw attack. This attack is considered a primary natural attack that deals 1d6 points of damage. If the ogrekin already possesses a claw attack, it instead gains [Improved Natural Attack](ref/feat/improved-natural-attack) (claw) as a bonus feat.|
|4|**Grotesque Ears:** The ogrekin gains [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) 10 feet and a +4 racial bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks.|
|5|**Lanky:** The ogrekin's natural reach increases by 5 feet.|
|6|**Snout:** The ogrekin gains the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability.|
|7|**Thick Feet:** The ogrekin gains a +4 racial bonus to its CMD against [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun), and [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuvers, as well as a +2 bonus to its AC against attacks made as part of a [charge](ref/rule/charge) action.|
|8|**Vice Grip:** The ogrekin gains a +2 racial bonus on [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) and grapple combat maneuver checks and a +4 racial bonus to CMD against disarm and grapple maneuvers.|
|9|**Warty Knuckles:** The ogrekin gains [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as a bonus feat.|
|10|**Webbed Fingers:** The ogrekin gains a swim speed equal to half its land speed.|

**Disadvantageous Deformities:** The ogrekin gains one of the following, chosen randomly.

|d10|Deformity|
| --- | --- |
|1|**Atrophied Eyes:** The ogrekin gains the [light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness) weakness.|
|2|**Bad Eyes:** The ogrekin is partially blind. Creatures targeting the ogrekin increase their attack bonuses for [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) to +4, and the ogrekin takes a -2 penalty on all ranged attack rolls against targets more than 30 feet away.|
|3|**Brittle Bones:** The ogrekin's bones are malformed and weak. Creatures gain a +4 circumstance bonus on attack rolls to confirm critical hits against the ogrekin.|
|4|**Deformed Feet:** The ogrekin can't run and takes a -2 penalty to its CMD against bull rush, overrun, and trip combat maneuvers.|
|5|**Distractible:** If the ogrekin rolls a natural 1 on an ability check, attack roll, saving throw, or skill check, it becomes [confused](ref/misc/confused) until the end of its next turn.|
|6|**Flaking Skin:** The ogrekin reduces the bonus to natural armor granted by the ogrekin template to +1 and gains [vulnerability](ref/umr/vulnerabilities) to nonlethal damage.|
|7|**Massive Girth:** The ogrekin is treated as being one size category larger when attempting [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks.|
|8|**Offensive Odor:** Increase the Charisma penalty imposed by the ogrekin template to -4. Creatures with scent can detect the ogrekin from twice the normal distance.|
|9|**Trusting:** The ogrekin takes a -2 penalty on Will saves against mind-affecting effects and a -1 penalty on initiative checks.|
|10|**Twisted Spine:** The ogrekin takes a -4 penalty on grapple and trip combat maneuver checks. Standing up from [prone](ref/rule/prone) requires the ogrekin to take a full-round action.|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-h}

A template for [half-ogres](ref/template/half-ogre) exists, for the offspring of ogres and other
giants.
