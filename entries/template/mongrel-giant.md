**Mongrel Giant**

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Inherited:** present

**Source:** Bestiary 6/192

While most [giants](ref/subtype/giant) have the physical features of the type of giant of their
immediate ancestors, occasionally a giant gives birth to a child who has physical traits associated
with one of the other types of giants. [Hill giants](ref/monster/giant-hill-giant) are the one type
of giant whose traits don't arise in other giants.

"Mongrel giant" is an inherited template that can be added to any creature with the giant subtype
(referred to hereafter as the base creature). A mongrel giant retains all the base creature's
statistics and special abilities except as noted here.

**CR:** Same as the base creature's CR + 1.

**Special Qualities:** A mongrel giant retains all the special attacks, qualities, and abilities of
the base creature and gains the following special quality.

> **Giant Ancestry (Ex):** A mongrel giant has traits and features of another variety of giant and
> gains additional abilities based on this ancestry (see Giant Ancestry Traits below).

**Abilities:** Con +2. Most mongrel giants gain an additional ability score increase as determined
by their giant ancestry; if an ancestry grants a bonus to Constitution higher than +2, that higher
bonus replaces this standard +2 bonus.

### Giant Ancestry Traits

A mongrel giant gains additional traits based on his ancestry. For
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), the mongrel giant's caster level is equal to
the base creature's HD or the caster level of the base creature's spell-like abilities, whichever is
higher.

[Ash Giant:](ref/monster/giant-ash-giant) Ash mongrels are deformed and covered in open sores and
tumors. They gain [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to disease and the ash giant's disease (ash leprosy)
ability. Constitution +4.

[Cave Giant:](ref/monster/giant-cave-giant) Cave mongrels have prominent teeth and gray-green skin.
They gain the cave giant's axe wielder ability and the [ferocity](ref/umr/ferocity) universal
monster ability. Strength +2.

[Cliff Giant:](ref/monster/giant-cliff-giant) Cliff mongrels have red-brown skin shot through with
streaks of shimmering color. They gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 30 feet when in contact
with unworked stone or natural earth, and they can use the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day:
[cure moderate wounds](ref/spell/cure-moderate-wounds),
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals), and [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape). Wisdom
+2.

[Cloud Giant:](ref/monster/giant-cloud-giant) Cloud mongrels have fine features and pale blue or
white skin. They can use the following [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per
day: [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud), [levitate](ref/spell/levitate) (self plus 2,000 pounds), and
[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist). They also gain the oversized weapon ability (the giant
can wield a weapon of one size category larger than his size would normally allow without penalty).
Wisdom +2.

[Desert Giant:](ref/monster/giant-desert-giant) Desert mongrels have roughly textured tan or orange
skin. They gain immunity to fire and
[Martial Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/martial-weapon-proficiency)
([scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar)) as a bonus feat. Dexterity +2.

[Eclipse Giant:](ref/monster/giant-eclipse-giant) Eclipse mongrels have dark gray skin and appear
somewhat overweight. They gain immunity to death effects and can use the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day: [daylight](ref/spell/daylight) or
[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) (choose one) and [heal](ref/spell/heal) or
[harm](ref/spell/harm) (choose one). Wisdom +2.

[Fire Giant:](ref/monster/giant-fire-fire-giant) Fire mongrels have orange hair and red or black
skin. They gain the [fire](ref/subtype/fire) subtype (including immunity to fire and
[vulnerability](ref/umr/vulnerabilities) to cold), and gain Martial Weapon Proficiency
([greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword)) as a bonus feat. Strength +2.

[Frost Giant:](ref/monster/giant-frost-frost-giant) Frost mongrels have light blue skin and dirty
yellow hair. They gain the [cold](ref/subtype/cold) subtype (including immunity to cold and
vulnerability to fire), and gain Martial Weapon Proficiency ([greataxe](ref/eq-weapon/greataxe)) as
a bonus feat. Constitution +4.

[Jungle Giant:](ref/monster/giant-jungle-giant) Jungle mongrels have brown and green skin that is
textured like fibrous plant material or tree bark. They gain immunity to poison and gain the jungle
giant's archery expert ability. Dexterity +2.

[Marsh Giant:](ref/monster/giant-marsh-giant) Marsh mongrels have pale green skin and hairless
bodies. They gain a swim speed of 20 feet and can use the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day: [augury](ref/spell/augury),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse), and [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud). Strength +2.

[Moon Giant:](ref/monster/giant-moon-giant) Moon giant mongrels have pale gray skin that sparkles
faintly in dim light. They gain cold [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 and fire resistance 10, and
can use the following [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day:
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance),
[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights), and [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing). Wisdom +2.

[Mountain Giant:](ref/monster/giant-mountain-giant) Mountain mongrels have warty skin. They gain
immunity to fear and can use the following [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once
per day: [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness), [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door),
and [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility). Strength +2.

[Ocean Giant:](ref/monster/giant-ocean-giant) Ocean mongrels have blue skin. They gain the
[amphibious](ref/umr/amphibious) special quality, the [aquatic](ref/subtype/aquatic) subtype, cold
resistance 10, and electricity resistance 10. Strength +2.

[Plague Giant:](ref/monster/giant-plague-giant) Plague mongrels are thin and their skin looks
diseased. They gain immunity to disease and can use the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day: [contagion](ref/spell/contagion),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), and [wither limb](ref/spell/wither-limb). Wisdom +2.

[River Giant:](ref/monster/giant-river-giant) River mongrels have green skin marked with swirling
patterns. They gain the [hold breath](ref/umr/hold-breath) universal monster ability and a +4 racial
bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks. Constitution +4.

[Rune Giant:](ref/monster/giant-rune-giant) Rune giant mongrels are among the rarest of all mongrel
giants. They have black skin through which red runes shimmer, almost like faintly glowing tattoos. A
rune mongrel's CR is the same as the base creature's CR + 2. They gain immunity to cold,
electricity, and fire; gain the runes ability that rune giants have; and can use the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day: [air walk](ref/spell/air-walk),
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person), [demand](ref/spell/demand),
[mass charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster-mass), and [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion). Strength
+6, Constitution +8, Wisdom +4, Charisma +4.

[Shadow Giant:](ref/monster/giant-shadow-giant) Shadow mongrels have dark gray skin. They gain the
shadow giant's militant special ability and [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet. Wisdom +2.

[Slag Giant:](ref/monster/giant-slag-giant) Slag mongrels have rust-colored skin. They gain fire
resistance 10 and the slag giant's shattering blow ability. Strength +2.

[Stone Giant:](ref/monster/giant-stone-giant) Stone mongrels have elongated heads and brown or gray
skin that resembles rock. They gain the stone giant's improved rock catching ability, and increase
the range of their [rock throwing](ref/umr/rock-throwing) ability by 40 feet. If the base creature
doesn't have rock throwing, it gains that ability with a range increment of 120 feet. Natural armor
bonus increases by 2.

[Storm Giant:](ref/monster/giant-storm-giant) Storm mongrels gain immunity to electricity and the
following [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) usable once per day:
[call lightning](ref/spell/call-lightning), [control weather](ref/spell/control-weather), and
[levitate](ref/spell/levitate). Strength +2.

[Sun Giant:](ref/monster/giant-sun-giant) Sun mongrels have golden skin and faintly glowing
flame-colored hair. They are immune to fire and blindness, and can use the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day: [daylight](ref/spell/daylight)
and [flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike). Wisdom +2.

[Taiga Giant:](ref/monster/giant-taiga-giant) Taiga mongrels have dark gray skin, red hair, and a
strong lower jaw. They gain a +4 deflection bonus to their Armor Class from protective spirits and
are immune to illusions. Constitution +4.

[Tomb Giant:](ref/monster/giant-tomb-giant) Tomb mongrels have milky-white, hairless flesh. They
gain immunity to death effects and paralysis, gain the
[negative energy affinity](ref/umr/negative-energy-affinity) defensive ability, and can use
[control undead](ref/spell/control-undead) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Wisdom +2.

[Wood Giant:](ref/monster/giant-wood-giant) Wood mongrels have pale pink skin and prominent brows.
They can use the following [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day:
[charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal), [enlarge person (self only)](ref/spell/enlarge-person),
[pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace), [quench](ref/spell/quench),
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals), [spike growth](ref/spell/spike-growth), and
[tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape). Dexterity +2.
