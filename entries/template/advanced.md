**Advanced**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary/294

Creatures with the advanced template are fiercer and more powerful than their ordinary cousins.

**Quick Rules:** +2 on all rolls (including damage rolls) and special ability DCs; +4 to AC and CMD;
+2 hp/HD.

**Rebuild Rules:** **AC** increase natural armor by +2; **Ability Scores** +4 to all ability scores
(except Int scores of 2 or less).
