**Giant**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary/295

Creatures with the giant template are larger and stronger than their normal-sized kin. This template
cannot be applied to creatures that are Colossal.

**Quick Rules:** +2 to all rolls based on Str or Con, +2 hp/ HD, -1 penalty on all rolls based on
Dex.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Size** increase by one category; **AC** increase natural armor by +3;
**Attacks** increase dice rolled [by one step](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments); **Ability Scores**
+4 size bonus to Str and Con, -2 Dex.
