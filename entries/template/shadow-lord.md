**Shadow Lord**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +2

"Shadow lord" is an acquired template that can be added to any [shadow](ref/template/shadow)
creature with 5 or more Hit Dice (referred to hereafter as the base creature). Most shadow lords
were originally humanoids. A shadow lord retains all the base creature's statistics and abilities
(including those granted by the shadow creature template) except as noted here.

**CR:** Same as the base creature +2.

**Alignment:** Any evil.

**Senses:** A shadow lord gains the [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) ability.

**AC:** Same as the base creature (see also the _incorporeal step_ ability).

**Defensive Abilities:** A shadow lord gains the following defensive abilities.

> **Energy Resistance and Damage Reduction (Su):** A shadow lord gains
> [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to cold and
> electricity based on its Hit Dice, as noted on the table below. This replaces the energy
> resistance and damage reduction granted by the shadow creature template.
>
> |Hit Dice|Resist Cold and Electricity|DR|
> | --- | --- | --- |
> |5-10|15|10/magic|
> |11-15|20|15/magic|
> |16+|30|20/magic|
>
> **Incorporeal Step (Su):** When a shadow lord moves, it gains the
> [incorporeal](ref/subtype/incorporeal) subtype and [incorporeal](ref/umr/incorporeal) quality,
> including a deflection bonus to AC equal to its Charisma bonus. It loses the incorporeal subtype
> and special ability when it stops moving.

**Attacks:** A shadow lord gains a melee touch attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (Fortitude
negates). The save DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 the shadow lord's Hit Dice + the shadow lord's Charisma
modifier. On a critical hit, the shadow lord's touch attack also deals 1 point of
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) (also negated by the saving throw).

**Special Attacks:** A shadow lord gains the following special attacks. Their saving throw DCs for
these attacks are equal to 10 + 1/2 the shadow lord's Hit Dice + the shadow lord's Charisma
modifier, unless otherwise noted.

> **Cloying Gloom Blast (Su):** Three times per day, the shadow lord can unleash a 30-foot cone of
> cloying gloom. On a failed Fortitude saving throw, creatures in the cone are affected by a
> [slow](ref/spell/slow) spell (caster level equal to the shadow lord's Hit Dice) and are blinded
> for the duration of the _[slow](ref/spell/slow)_ effect.

**Spell-Like Abilities:** A shadow lord gains the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), with a caster level equal to its Hit Dice: at
will-[ray of sickening](ref/spell/ray-of-sickening);
3/day-[shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration), [shadow step](ref/spell/shadow-step);
1/day-[greater shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration-greater) (if the shadow lord has 11
or more Hit Dice), [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk).

A creature created with _[shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration)_ or
_[greater shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration-greater)_ that would normally have a
celestial or fiendish template (such as a bear) instead gains the [shadow](ref/template/shadow)
creature template.

**Special Qualities:** A shadow lord gains the following special quality.

> **Planar Thinning (Su):** Once per day as a full-round action, a shadow lord can thin the barriers
> between the Material Plane and Shadow Plane, making it considerably easier for creatures to cross
> between the two. This functions like the planar travel aspect of the [gate](ref/spell/gate) spell
> (caster level equal to the shadow lord's Hit Dice). This planar thinning is immediately dispelled
> if in an area of normal or bright light.

**Ability Scores:** Dex +4, Cha +4.

**Skills:** A shadow lord gains a +8 racial bonus on all [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks.
