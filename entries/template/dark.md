**Dark**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +0 or +1

**Maybesummon:** present

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Monster Summoner's Handbook/19

Applying this template to a summoned creature requires the feat
[Versatile Summon Monster](ref/feat/versatile-summon-monster).

Dark creatures are native denizens of the [Plane of Shadow](ref/rule/shadow-plane), and generally
have a dark gray or deep purple coloration. This template can be applied only to a non-outsider that
has none of the following subtypes: air, cold, earth, fire, or water. A dark creature's CR increases
by 1 only if the base creature has 5 or more HD.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Senses** gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft. and
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision); **Defensive Abilities** gains DR and resistance to
cold and electricity based on its Hit Dice, as noted on the table below; **SR** gains SR equal to
its new CR + 5.

|Hit Dice|DR|Resist Cold and Electricity|
| --- | --- | --- |
|1-4|-|5|
|5-10|5/Magic|10|
|11+|10/magic|15|
