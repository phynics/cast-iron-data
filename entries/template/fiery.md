**Fiery**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +0 or +1

**Maybesummon:** present

**Simple:** present

Applying this template to a summoned creature requires the feat
[Versatile Summon Monster](ref/feat/versatile-summon-monster) or
[Versatile Summon Nature's Ally](ref/feat/versatile-summon-natures-ally).

Fiery creatures are native denizens of the Elemental [Plane of Fire](ref/rule/plane-of-fire), and
act as conduits to the burning energies of their home plane. This template can be applied only to a
non-outsider that has none of the following subtypes: air, cold, earth, fire, or water. Creatures
with a swim speed can't be fiery creatures. A fiery creature's CR increases by 1 only if the base
creature has 5 or more HD.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Type** gains the [fire](ref/subtype/fire) subtype; **Senses** gains
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.; **Defensive Abilities** gains DR as noted on the table
below; **Attacks** gains bonus fire damage as noted on the table below on attacks with natural
weapons and metal weapons.
