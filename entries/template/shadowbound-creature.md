**Shadowbound Creature**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Horror Adventures/250

A shadowbound creature has lost its color and capacity for joy and pleasure.

**Quick Rules:** [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 120 ft.; +1 to AC; +2 on rolls based on Con; +2
hp/HD; [light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness); can't gain morale bonuses; regretful gaze
([shaken](ref/misc/shaken) 1 round, 30 ft., Will save negates, DC = 10 + 1/2 HD + Cha modifier);
after taking 3 points of damage per HD in a single attack, gain a +2 profane bonus on attack rolls,
damage rolls, saving throws, and skill checks for 1 round.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Senses** [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 120 ft.; **AC** gain a deflection
bonus to AC equal to 1/4 CR (minimum 1); **Weaknesses** light blindness, can't gain morale bonuses;
**Special Attacks** regretful [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) (shaken 1 round, 30 ft., Will negates, DC = 10 +
1/2 HD + Cha modifier); after taking 3 points of damage per HD in a single attack, gain a +2 profane
bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, saving throws, and skill checks for 1 round; **Ability Scores**
+4 Con.
