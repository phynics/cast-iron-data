**Resolute**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +0 or +1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Bestiary 2/293

**Summonable:** present

Creatures with the resolute template live in planes where law is paramount. They can be summoned
using spells such as [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) and
[planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally). A resolute creature's CR increases by +1 only if the base
creature has 5 or more HD. A resolute creature's quick and rebuild rules are the same.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Senses** gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.; **Defensive
Abilities** gains DR and energy resistance as noted on the table; **SR** gains SR equal to new CR
+5; **Special Attacks** smite chaos 1/day as a swift action (adds Cha bonus to attack rolls and
damage bonus equal to HD against chaotic foes; smite persists until target is dead or the resolute
creature rests).
