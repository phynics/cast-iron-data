**Alchemically Invisible**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +2

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Rival Guide/47

A creature that is alchemically invisible also suffers from bouts of madness as a result of its
condition.

**Quick/Rebuild Rules:** The creature is naturally invisible, but must make a DC 15 Will save at the
start of each round of combat to avoid being [confused](ref/misc/confused) for 1 round.
