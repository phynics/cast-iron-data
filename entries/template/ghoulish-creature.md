**Ghoulish Creature**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

Ghoulish creatures have succumbed to [ghoul fever](ref/disease/ghoul-fever) and transformed into
cannibalistic undead versions of their previous selves.

**Quick Rules:** Counts as [undead](ref/type/undead); [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.;
undead immunities; +1 to AC; +1 on rolls based on Str and Cha; two
[claw attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deal each 1d4 points of damage (for Medium creatures)
plus [paralysis](ref/rule/paralysis) and one bite attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (for Medium
creatures) plus paralysis and disease; disease (_Ghoul Fever,_ Fort DC = 10 + 1/2 HD + Cha
modifier); paralysis (1d4+1 rounds, DC = 10 + 1/2 HD + Cha modifier, [elves](ref/race/elf) are
immune).

**Rebuild Rules:** **Type** change to undead; **Senses** [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.;
**AC** natural armor bonus increases by 1; **Melee** two claw attacks that each deal 1d4 points of
damage (for Medium creatures) plus paralysis and one bite attack that deals 1d6 points of damage
(for Medium creatures) plus paralysis and disease; **Special Attacks** disease
([Ghoul Fever](ref/disease/ghoul-fever): Fort DC = 10 + 1/2 HD + Cha modifier), paralysis (1d4+1
rounds, DC = 10 + 1/2 HD + Cha modifier, elves are immune); **Ability Scores** +2 Str, +2 Cha.
