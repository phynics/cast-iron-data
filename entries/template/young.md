**Young**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** -1

**Simple:** present

**Source:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary/295

Creatures with the young template are immature specimens of the base creature. You can also use this
simple template to easily create a smaller variant of a monster. This template cannot be applied to
creatures that increase in power through aging or feeding (such as [dragons](ref/family/dragon-true)
or [barghests](ref/monster/barghest)) or creatures that are Fine-sized.

**Quick Rules:** +2 to all Dex-based rolls, -2 to all other rolls, -2 hp/HD.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Size** decrease by one category; **AC** reduce natural armor by -2 (minimum
+0); **Attacks** decrease damage dice [by one step](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments); **Ability
Scores** -4 Strength, -4 Con, +4 size bonus to Dex.
