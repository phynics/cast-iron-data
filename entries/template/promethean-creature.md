**Promethean Creature**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +1

**Simple:** present

A promethean creature has had some of its body replaced by [golem](ref/family/golem) parts.

**Quick Rules:** +4 to AC; +2 on rolls based on Str; [slam attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that
deals 1d6 points of damage (for Medium creatures); +4 on saving throws vs. poison and disease;
immune to emotion effects except those that produce rage, hatred, or anger; automatically
[stabilize](ref/misc/stable) when below 0 hp.

**Rebuild Rules:** **AC** natural armor bonus increases by 4; **Defensive Abilities** +4 on saving
throws vs. poison and disease, immune to emotion effects except those that produce rage, hatred, or
anger; automatically stabilize when below 0 hp; **Melee** slam attack that deals 1d6 points of
damage (for Medium creatures); **Special Qualities** doesn't need to eat, sleep, or drink; **Ability
Scores** +4 Str.
