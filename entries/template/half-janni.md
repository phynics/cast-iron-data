**Half-Janni**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +2

**Source:** Qadira, Gateway to the East/27

Jann are the kind of genie closest to humans in interests and temperament, and are drawn to mingle
with mortals. Sometimes these associations result in the birth of half-janni children. Beautiful and
exotic, the elemental power that resides in these half-jann mixes with the unique potential of the
human parent, sometimes rivaling or even exceeding that of their genie parent.

Half-jann often seek to sequester themselves on the edge of Keleshite civilizations, capable of
observing their human kin without getting deeply involved in mortal affairs. Like their genie
ancestors, however, the half-jann are fascinated by true humans, and often take them as mates,
leading to generations of suli-jann (see below).

No matter their human ancestry, half-jann are lovely to look upon, possessing deeply tanned bronze
skin and intense eyes that sometimes seem to be swirling pools of elemental power.

Half-jann are the offspring of a janni and a mortal human. Most half-jann are born to human mothers,
but a rare few are born to janni females. An expectant janni mother always knows if her child will
be born a full genie or a half-janni, and most choose to keep their children in janni society,
though several bard stories tell of a foundling child given to a noble to raise. A half-janni is
almost always a single birth; twins are nearly unheard of, but considered a blessing to the parents,
a sign that the children are destined for greatness.

Half-jann age at the same rates as humans, reaching sexual maturity alongside their peers. Once a
half-janni is fully matured, however, her aging slows to that of a [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf)
(which is often how a foundling half-janni realizes her unusual heritage).

Unlike their full genie kin, half-jann must eat food in order to survive. While a half-janni can
rely on her genie parent's [create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water) ability for
sustenance, most consider this dependency a weakness and learn to provide for themselves as soon as
they are able - some taking this to the extreme of exiling themselves upon reaching adulthood,
avoiding their own kind for years to prove their independence.

Although physically and emotionally very similar to humans, half-jann are reminded from birth of
their superior status, either by one of their parents, or the fully human folk around them.
Half-jann are rare enough that there are no communities of any size of these beings, and most prefer
to remain hidden among normal human populations or join the wandering tribes of their genie
kinsfolk.

Most half-jann go decades without ever meeting another of their kind. Those half-jann that live
among humans often become powerful bards, fighters, or paladins; those who prefer janni society have
no tendency toward one class over others. Half-jann tend to avoid extremes in alignment, and are
usually neutral on at least one alignment axis (chaotic-lawful or good-evil). Whatever their morals,
half-jann are passionate about their chosen paths - a combination of genie arrogance and Keleshite
pride driving them to acts of renown or foolishness.

### Creating a Half-Janni

"Half-janni" is an inherited template that can be added to any human (referred to hereafter as the
"base creature").

A half-janni uses all the base creature's statistics and special abilities except as noted here.

**Size and Type:** The creature's type changes to [outsider](ref/type/outsider)
([augmented](ref/subtype/augmented) [humanoid](ref/type/humanoid), human,
[native](ref/subtype/native)). Do not recalculate the base creature's Hit Dice, base attack bonus,
or saves. Size is unchanged.

**Speed:** A half-janni gains the ability to fly, with a speed of 20 feet (good maneuverability).

**AC:** The half-janni's natural armor improves by +1 (this stacks with any natural armor bonus the
base creature has).

**Spell-Like Abilities:** A half-janni with an Intelligence or Wisdom score of 8 or higher has
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), depending on its level, as indicated on the
table below. The abilities are cumulative.

Caster level equals the creature's level, and the save DC is Charisma-based.

|Level|Abilities|
| --- | --- |
|1-2|[Speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) 3/day|
|3-6|[Enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person) or [reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person) 2/day in any combination|
|7-8|[Invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility) (self only) 3/day|
|9-14|[Create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water) 1/day|
|15-16|[Ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt) 1/day|

**Special Qualities:** A half-janni has all the special qualities of the base creature, plus the
following special qualities.

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) out to 60 feet
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to disease
- Fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10

**Abilities:** Increase from the base creature as follows: Str +2, Dex +2, Int +2, Wis +4, Cha +4

**CR:** Same as base creature +2
