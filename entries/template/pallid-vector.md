**Pallid Vector**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** +2

**Source:** Villain Codex/68

Followers of [Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa) revere all sicknesses as worldly expressions of her
divine will, but none more so than the pallid gift, which opens its victims' fevered minds to the
glory of the Pallid Princess. Creatures that die while afflicted with the disease rise as
[undead](ref/type/undead), but some creatures form a symbiotic bond with it and become pallid
vectors.

"Pallid vector" is an acquired template that can be added to a corporeal, living creature not immune
to disease. A pallid vector retains all the base creature's statistics and special abilities except
as noted.

**CR:** Base creature's CR + 2.

**Alignment:** Any evil.

**AC:** The creature's natural armor bonus increases by 3.

**Defensive Abilities:** A pallid vector is treated as undead for the purposes of negative and
positive energy, and it gains [immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to disease, exhaustion, fatigue, poison,
and stunning. Additionally, it gains the following ability.

> **Pale Rebirth (Su):** When a pallid vector dies, it rises as a
> [plague zombie](ref/template/zombie) 1 round later. Instead of zombie rot, it spreads
> [pallid gift](ref/disease/pallid-gift). Sprinkling [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water) on the
> body (a standard action) before it rises prevents this. A humanoid pallid vector that kills itself
> ritualistically or dies within a _[desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate)_ effect or other area that
> promotes undeath rises as a more powerful undead instead, as if it had died from pallid gift (see
> the disease page).

**Special Attacks:** A pallid vector gains stench (10 rounds) and disease. The DCs are
Constitution-based.

> **Disease (Su):** All of a pallid vector's melee attacks, even with manufactured weapons, carry
> the pallid gift disease.

**Ability Scores:** Str +2, Con +4, Wis +2.
