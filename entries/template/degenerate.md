**Degenerate**

**Acquired:** present

**Challenge rating:** -1

**Simple:** present

Degenerate creatures are weaker than their ordinary cousins.

**Quick Rules:** -2 on all rolls (including damage rolls) and to special ability DCs; -2 to AC and
CMD; -2 hp/HD.

**Rebuild Rules:** **Ability Scores** -4 to all ability scores (minimum 1).
