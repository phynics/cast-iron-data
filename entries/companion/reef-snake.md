**Companion Type**

**Details:** Animal

Highly venomous and an excellent swimmer, the brightly colored reef snake is at home in tidal waters
but can slither about on land if needed.

### Starting Statistics

**Size**

**Details:** Medium

**Speed**

**Details:** 10 ft., swim 40 ft.

**AC**

**Details:** +2 natural armor

**Attack**

**Details:** bite (1d4 plus poison)

**Ability Scores**

**Details:** Str 10, Dex 15, Con 12, Int 1, Wis 13, Cha 2

**Special Attacks**

**Details:** [poison](ref/umr/poison) (see below)

**Special Qualities**

**Details:** [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [hold breath](ref/umr/hold-breath),
[scent](ref/umr/scent)

**Type:** Poison

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the companion's HD + its Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1

**Cure:** 1 save

### 7th-Level Advancement

**Ability Scores**

**Details:** Str +2, Dex +2

**Special Attacks**

**Details:** poison (same as above, but damage becomes 1d2 Str and cure is now 2 saves)
