**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 178

**Companion Type**

**Details:** Animal

The anglerfish is a fish that uses a bioluminescent lure at the tip of a stalk protruding from its
head to attract prey.

### Starting Statistics

**Size**

**Details:** Small

**Speed**

**Details:** swim 40 ft.

**AC**

**Details:** +1 natural armor

**Attack**

**Details:** bite (1d8 plus grab)

**Ability Scores**

**Details:** Str 13, Dex 15, Con 12, Int 1, Wis 12, Cha 2

**Special Qualities**

**Details:** aquatic, [grab](ref/umr/grab) (Medium), [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision),
lure (sheds light as per a [candle](ref/eq-misc/candle)), [scent](ref/umr/scent)

> _There are no rules for the lure ability specifically tied to the anglerfish, but a "Lure" ability
> exists for [ceratioidi](ref/monster/ceratioidi) and
> [sea linnorms](ref/monster/linnorm-sea-linnorm) that is very anglerfish-themed._
