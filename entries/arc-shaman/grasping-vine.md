Grasping vine shamans are typically adherents of one of several scattered sects dedicated to the
Green Mother. They revere plant life in all its forms, with a special fondness for poisonous or
carnivorous plants. Unlike most shamans, a grasping vine is guided by a plant-like creature rather
than a spirit animal.

**Spirit**

**Info:** Though a grasping vine is not limited to a particular choice of spirit, common selections
are [Life](ref/shamanspirit/life), [Nature](ref/shamanspirit/nature), or
[Wood](ref/shamanspirit/wood).

**Plant Spirit (Ex)**

**Ability:** A shaman's spirit animal is made of leaves and thorny vines. This plant spirit has the
statistics and abilities as a shaman's spirit animal, but it is treated as a creature of the
[plant](ref/type/plant) type rather than a native outsider.

**Alters:** Spirit animal

**Verdant Magic**

**Info:** A grasping vine adds the following spells to the list of spells she can cast using _spirit
magic_ at the listed spell levels.

**Replaces:** Spirit magic spells granted by the shaman's spirit

**Spells:** Entangle

**Spells:** Barkskin

**Spells:** Thorny entanglement

**Spells:** Command plants

**Spells:** Wall of thorns

**Spells:** Fire seeds

**Spells:** Animate plants

**Spells:** Horrid wilting

**Spells:** Shambler

**Greentongue (Sp)**

**Ability:** In addition, while under the effects of
_[speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants),_ the grasping vine can deliver a
[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion) (as per the spell) to a single plant creature within 30 feet,
ignoring the plant's immunity to mind-affecting effects. The grasping vine can use this second
ability once per day at 2nd level.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Shaman's hex gained at 2nd level

**Standard:** A grasping vine can use [speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 shaman levels

At 6th level: The _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ can be used times 2 per day.

At 10th level: The _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ can be used times 3 per day.

At 14th level: The _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ can be used times 4 per day.

At 18th level: The _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ can be used times 5 per day.

**Flower's Form (Sp)**

**At 12th Level:** This ability functions as [plant shape II](ref/spell/plant-shape-2).

**At 16th Level:** This ability functions as [plant shape III](ref/spell/plant-shape-3).

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Shaman's hex gained at 8th level

**Standard:** A grasping vine can assume the form of a Small or Medium plant creature, as per
[plant shape I](ref/spell/plant-shape-1).

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per shaman level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments
