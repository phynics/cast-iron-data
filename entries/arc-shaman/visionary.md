The visionary is a master at divination, drawing upon her intimate relationship with the spirit
world to ferret out all manner of secrets and insights about the world around her and beyond.

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** The visionary gains [Diviner's Delving](ref/feat/diviners-delving) as a bonus feat,
even if she does not meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Discern Magical Expertise (Ex)**

**Ability:** The shaman can determine what type of spellcasting expertise a creature possesses by
studying the creature for 2 rounds with [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) or any of the
following spells: [detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos), [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil),
[detect good](ref/spell/detect-good), or [detect law](ref/spell/detect-law) (if the creature belongs
to the alignment). This ability tells the visionary what bloodlines, domains, hexes, schools, or
mysteries (if any) the creature possesses. A successful Will saving throw negates this effect (DC =
10 + 1/2 the shaman's level + the shaman's Wisdom modifier).

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Wandering spirit gained at 4th level

**Special:** A creature affected by this ability cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours. This
ability functions through magical sensors as with
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance) and [scrying](ref/spell/scrying).

**Vision Spirit Magic**

**Info:** The visionary adds the following spells to the list of spells she can cast using _spirit
magic_ at the listed spell level:

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Spirit magic spells gained from the shaman's wandering spirit

**Spells:** See alignment

**Spells:** See invisibility

**Spells:** Clairaudience/clairvoyance

**Spells:** Detect scrying

**Spells:** Prying eyes

**Spells:** Legend lore

**Spells:** Vision

**Spells:** Moment of prescience

**Spells:** Foresight

**Improved Divination (Su)**

**Ability:** The visionary becomes more adept at divination magic. When she casts the
[augury](ref/spell/augury) spell, her chance for an accurate answer is automatically the maximum of
90%. Likewise, when she casts [divination](ref/spell/divination), she has the maximum 90% chance of
an accurate answer.

**Ability2:** The visionary can prepare _[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ as a 4th-level spell, and it
requires only 1 minute to cast. The visionary also has a 10% chance per caster level to cast the
spells listed in the _[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ spell description, instead of 5% per caster
level (to a maximum of 100%). The visionary must still prepare these spells to receive these
benefits.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Wandering hex gained at 6th level

**Wandering Spirit (Su)**

**At 12th Level:** The visionary forms a temporary bond with another spirit (other than the one she
selected using her _spirit_ class feature). This is identical to the 4th-level shaman class feature.
This adds the _wandering spirit magic_ spells to the list of spells she can cast using _spirit
magic,_ along with _vision spirit magic_ and her original spirit.

**At 20th Level:** She gains the abilities listed in the _greater_ version of her wandering spirit.

**Replaces:** Greater version of wandering spirit gained at 12th level, true version of wandering
spirit gained at 20th level
