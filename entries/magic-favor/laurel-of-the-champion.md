**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** -

This favor is a garland of leaves, often presented to the winner of a joust or other competition.
For 24 hours, the recipient takes a -2 penalty on Disguise checks and can roll twice and take the
better result on Diplomacy checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), [status](ref/spell/status); **Cost** 1,000 gp
