**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 13\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** arm or wrist; **Price** 600 gp; **Weight** -

This favor is a simple checked kerchief worn around the arm or wrist. For 1 week, the recipient can
automatically call to mind a detailed recollection of the presenter's features, even if his memory
has been impaired or modified (such as by [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory)). In addition,
the recipient can take a standard action to know the presenter's _[status](ref/spell/status)_ in the
same manner that the presenter can take a standard action to know the recipient's
_[status](ref/spell/status)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), [status](ref/spell/status); **Cost** 300 gp
