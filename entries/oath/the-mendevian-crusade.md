**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 20\
The Worldwound threatens all of Golarion with the uttermost depths of depravity, and so paladins
across the world stand firm against it. This oath is identical to the
[oath against fiends](ref/oath/fiends) except as noted here.

**Deities**

**Details:** [Angradd](ref/faith/angradd), [Arqueros](ref/faith/arqueros),
[Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), [Kols](ref/faith/kols), [Ragathiel](ref/faith/ragathiel),
[Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae), [Torag](ref/faith/torag), [Vildeis](ref/faith/vildeis).

**Divine Bond (Su)**

**Ability:** The paladin adds evil outsider [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) to the list of weapon special
abilities that can be added to her weapon and removes _flaming_ from the list.

**Alters:** Divine bond

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Anchoring Aura (Su)**

**Ability:** A paladin's aura hampers extradimensional travel by evil outsiders. The aura extends 20
feet from the paladin. Evil outsiders attempting to use abilities such as
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door), [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift), or
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport) to leave or enter the aura must succeed at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2
the paladin's level + the paladin's Charisma modifier); failure means the ability does not function,
as if the outsider were affected by [dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor).

The aura functions only while the paladin is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

**Immediate:** Alternatively, the paladin can expend one use of her _smite evil_ ability to target
an evil outsider within 30 feet with _[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)._ A
targeted _[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ persists even if the paladin is
unconscious or dead.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Aura of resolve

**Holy Vessel (Su)**

**Ability:** A paladin whose _divine bond_ is with a weapon can also use her _divine bond_ to
augment her armor or shield with enhancement bonuses, or add any of the following armor or shield
properties: [bashing](ref/magic-enh/bashing), [fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification), and
[spell resistance](ref/magic-enh/spell-resistance). Adding these properties consumes an amount of
bonus equal to the property's cost (see the
[armor special abilities and shield special abilities tables](ref/rule/magic-armor)).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** 9th-level mercy

**Special:** She can divide her bonuses from the _divine bond_ ability among her weapon, armor, and
shield as she sees fit. This ability has no effect for a paladin whose _divine bond_ is with a
mount.

**Mercy (Su)**

**Ability:** The paladin can choose the following mercy.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Possessed**

**Ability:** If the subject is affected by [dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person), possessed,
or otherwise magically controlled by an evil outsider, this mercy grants the victim a new saving
throw against the effect to end the effect at once.

**Code of Conduct**

**Details:** Never suffer an evil outsider to live if it is in your power to destroy it. Banish
fiends you cannot kill. Purge the evil from those possessed by fiends.

**Oath Spells**

**Spells:** Resist energy

**Spells:** Detect thoughts

**Spells:** Invisibility purge

**Spells:** Plane shift
