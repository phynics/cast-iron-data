The [Whispering Way](ref/rule/f-gravepath) is an enemy to all life in the world, and as a result
many paladins feel called to contain its evil. (This oath is identical to the
[oath against undeath](ref/oath/undeath) except for the _aura against necromancy_ below.)

**Deities**

**Details:** [Angradd](ref/faith/angradd), [Arqueros](ref/faith/arqueros),
[Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae), [Torag](ref/faith/torag),
[Vildeis](ref/faith/vildeis).

**Detect Undead (Su)**

**Ability:** This ability works like the standard paladin ability to
_[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil),_ except as [detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead) instead
of _[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)._

**Replaces:** Detect evil

**Special:** You can regain _[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)_ through the
[Sin Seer](ref/feat/sin-seer) feat.

**Ghost Touch Aura (Su)**

**At 3rd Level:** The paladin's armor is treated as if it had the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-armor) armor property. This does not affect the cost or
effect of any other abilities of the armor.

**At 9th Level:** This ability also applies to the paladin's shield.

**Replaces:** 3rd-level and 9th-level mercies

**Aura against Necromancy (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** The paladin gains a +4 morale bonus on saves against necromancy spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Each ally within 10 feet of her gains a +2
morale bonus on these saves.

**Replaces:** Aura of resolve

**Special:** This ability functions only while the paladin is conscious, not if she is unconscious
or dead.

**Superior Channeler (Su)**

**Ability:** A paladin can channel positive energy to harm undead by expending a single usage of her
_lay on hands_ ability instead of two.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Aura of justice

**Code of Conduct**

**Details:** Destroy agents of the Whispering Way and stop the spread of their philosophy.

**Oath Spells**

**Spells:** Sanctify corpse

**Spells:** [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)

**Spells:** Searing light

**Spells:** Halt undead
