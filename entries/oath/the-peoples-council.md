The [Eagle Knights](ref/rule/aff-vangard) and the people of Andoran hold their elected officials to
extremely high standards of conduct. Paladins swearing the oath of the People's Council serve the
common good by finding and thwarting those who abuse their authority. Many seek specifically to
emulate Talmandor, the agathion patron of Andoran.

**Deities**

**Details:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae),
[Ragathiel](ref/faith/ragathiel), [Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn), [Vildeis](ref/faith/vildeis).

**Gained class skills:** ling, per, perf

**Lost class skills:** ha, ride, spc

**Stirring Monologue (Su)**

**Ability:** The paladin can deliver a _stirring monologue_ on the ideals of justice and fairness,
motivating allies and persuading others. This functions as bardic performance as per a bard of her
paladin level using Perform (oratory). All the effects are language-dependent even if they would not
normally be.

The paladin gains the following performances at the indicated levels.

**At 1st Level:** [Inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage)

**At 10th Level:** [Dirge of doom](ref/performance/dirge-of-doom)

**At 13th Level:** [Inspire greatness](ref/performance/inspire-greatness)

**At 16th Level:** [Frightening tune](ref/performance/frightening-tune)

**At 19th Level:** [Mass suggestion](ref/performance/mass-suggestion)

**At 4th Level:** [Fascinate](ref/performance/fascinate)

**At 5th Level:** [Inspire competence](ref/performance/inspire-competence)

**At 7th Level:** [Suggestion](ref/performance/suggestion)

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Smite evil

**Aura of Truth (Su)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** The paladin automatically succeeds at saving throws to disbelieve illusions of the
phantasm subschool and automatically gains a saving throw to disbelieve figment illusions when she
observes them. Allies within 30 feet gain a +4 bonus on saving throws to disbelieve illusions of the
phantasm subschool.

**Replaces:** Aura of justice

**Champion of Andoran (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever she uses her _stirring monologue,_ up to four creatures (including the paladin
herself) affected by the _monologue_ gain SR 30 against spells with the evil descriptor or
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) cast by evil outsiders or undead. Weapons
wielded by those affected by her _stirring monologue_ are treated as good, silver, and cold iron for
the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Ability2:** In addition, whenever the oathbound paladin channels positive energy or uses _lay on
hands_ to heal a creature, she heals it the maximum possible amount.

**Alters:** Holy champion

**Flavor:** An oathbound paladin becomes a champion of the people of Andoran.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Her DR increases to 10/evil.

**Code of Conduct**

**Details:** Know the laws of Andoran and do not exempt authorities from the application of the law.

**Spells:** Locate object

**Spells:** Tongues
