**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 15\
Despite the seemingly inherent contradiction, the militant [Hellknights](ref/rule/aff-lawknights) of
Cheliax count a number of paladins among their ranks, especially the
[Order of the Godclaw](ref/hkorder/order-of-the-godclaw). Typically these paladins reconcile their
allegiance to the Hellknights by dedication to the rule of law above all other things.

**Detect Chaos (Su)**

**Ability:** This ability works like the standard paladin ability to
_[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil),_ except as [detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos) instead of
_[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)._

**Replaces:** Detect evil

**Smite Chaos (Su)**

**Ability:** This ability works like the standard paladin ability to _smite evil,_ except the
paladin gains bonuses against targets with chaotic alignments instead of evil. The paladin deals
additional damage against outsiders with the chaotic subtype, chaotic-aligned dragons, and
aberrations, instead of against evil outsiders, evil dragons, and undead.

**Replaces:** Smite evil

**Order of Good (Su)**

**At 4th Level:** She gains the ability to spend one use of her _lay on hands_ ability when using
her _smite chaos_ ability to instead _smite evil,_ as the paladin ability of the same name.

**Replaces:** Channel positive energy

**Code of Conduct**

**Details:** Spread the order of law wherever you go, so long as the law is just; do not serve as a
tool of tyranny. Fight vigilantly the servants of chaos.

**Spells:** Command

**Spells:** Calm emotions

**Spells:** Suggestion
