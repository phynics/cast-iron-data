**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 21\
The dwarven people look to the legendary Sky Citadels as beacons of dwarven culture. Paladins with
the oath of the skyseeker swear to defend those citadels their people retain and to help reclaim
those the dwarves have lost, hoping to one day rebuild them on the greatest, most honorable ideals
of the dwarven gods.

**Deities**

**Details:** [Angradd](ref/faith/angradd), [Folgrit](ref/faith/folgrit), [Kols](ref/faith/kols),
[Torag](ref/faith/torag), [Trudd](ref/faith/trudd).

**Smite Evil Hordes (Su)**

**Ability:** When the paladin or an ally defeats the target of the paladin's _smite evil_ ability,
the paladin can change the target of her _smite_ to a different target within 30 feet of the
previously defeated target without using an action. The new target must be of the same creature type
and subtype as the previous target. If no applicable target is within 30 feet, then the _smite evil_
effect ends.

**Alters:** Smite evil

**Special:** The paladin's _smite_ does not deal more damage to evil outsiders, dragons, or undead
than to other evil creatures.

**Divine Bond (Su)**

**Ability:** The paladin adds [mighty cleaving](ref/magic-enh/mighty-cleaving) to the list of weapon
special abilities that can be added to her weapon and removes _flaming_ from the list.

**Alters:** Divine bond

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Mercy for the Lost (Sp)**

**Ability:** If she is on the Material Plane, the paladin can use
[find the path](ref/spell/find-the-path) once per day at her paladin level, but only to find her way
to a known Sky Citadel. If she is not on the Material Plane, she can instead use this ability to use
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift) as per the spell to travel to a known Sky Citadel.

**Flavor:** The paladin can use the Sky Citadels as a beacon leading her on the path to her deity.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Aura of justice

**Special:** If she is in a Sky Citadel, she can expend her daily use of this ability to use
_[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ to travel to the realm of her deity.

**Stalwart (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The paladin's quest has strengthened her body and mind.

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Passive:** If she succeeds at a Fortitude or Will saving throw against an effect that has a
reduced effect on a successful save, she instead avoids the effect entirely.

**Replaces:** Aura of faith

**Special:** A [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) paladin does not gain the benefit of the _stalwart_
ability.

**Code of Conduct**

**Details:** Reclaim a Sky Citadel in the name of honorable dwarven ideals. Defend Sky Citadels
already held by dwarves.
