Appearing to be an upstanding member of society, the cabalist clandestinely follows the tenets of a
secret society. Practice with blood sacrifices, necromancy, and shadow magic gives a cabalist a
reputation as a sinister and dangerous vigilante.

**Gained class skills:** ka, spc

**Lost class skills:** dd, ke, per, sur

**Skill Ranks per Level**

**Alters:** Skill ranks per level

**Info:** The cabalist gains a number of skill ranks equal to 4 + his Intelligence modifier at each
level, instead of 6 + his Intelligence modifier skill ranks.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Ability:** A cabalist is not proficient with medium armor. Like a [magus](ref/class/magus), he can
cast cabalist spells while wearing light armor without incurring the normal arcane spell failure
chance.

**Alters:** Armor and weapon proficiencies

**Spellcasting and Spellbook**

**Ability:** A cabalist casts arcane spells and cantrips as a magus. He prepares spells using a
spellbook, choosing them from the 6th-level and lower spells from the witch spell list; higher-level
witch spells are not on the cabalist's spell list.

**Replaces:** 4th-, 8th-, 10th-, 14th-, and 16th-level vigilante talents

**Special:** A cabalist has a spellbook that functions in the same way as a magus's spellbook but
uses the 6th-level and lower spells from the witch spell list.

**Spill Blood (Ex)**

**Ability:** A cabalist can cause bleeding when he deals piercing or slashing damage with a melee
attack (or a ranged attack within 30 feet) against a living foe who is unaware of his presence, who
considers him an ally, or who is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC. The amount of
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage is equal to the cabalist's level.

**Replaces:** Vigilante specialization

**Special:** A cabalist can't deal this bleed damage to a creature with total
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment).

**Cabalist Talents**

**Alters:** Vigilante talents

**Info:** A cabalist can select from the following vigilante talents, in addition to general
vigilante talents.

**Bond of Blood (Sp, Su)**

**Ability:** When the cabalist deals bleed damage or 5 or more points of piercing or slashing damage
to an adjacent living creature, he gains 1d6 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points).
These temporary hit points last for 1 hour, and the cabalist can't gain temporary hit points again
from _bond of blood_ during that hour (even if he loses all the temporary hit points from _bond of
blood_ before that time).

**Flavor:** The cabalist has a magical bond to life-giving blood.

**At 4th Level:** The cabalist can also activate [blood armor](ref/spell/blood-armor) once per day
as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as a **swift action**.

At 4th level: The number of temporary hit points gained +2d6.

At 8th level: The number of temporary hit points gained +3d6.

At 12th level: The number of temporary hit points gained +4d6.

At 16th level: The number of temporary hit points gained +5d6.

At 20th level: The number of temporary hit points gained +6d6.

**Familiar (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cabalist gains a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar), using his vigilante level as
his effective wizard level. The [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) also has a social identity as a
seemingly normal animal, though cabalists with outlandish familiars might still need to hide them.

**Living Shadow (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, the cabalist can become a living shadow as though he were using
[shadow body](ref/spell/shadow-body). This effect lasts for 1 round per vigilante level, and the
cabalist can't cast spells while in this form.

**At 14th Level:** The cabalist can remain as a living shadow for 1 minute per vigilante level
instead, and can cast spells while he is a living shadow.

**Prereq:** 12th-level vigilante

**Necromantic Focus (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cabalist gains [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (necromancy) as a bonus feat. He
also adds the following additional spells to his spellbook, provided he can cast spells of the given
level.

**Spells:** Cause fear

**Spells:** Blindness/deafness

**Spells:** Bestow curse

**Spells:** Poison

**Spells:** Feast on fear

**Spells:** Slay living

**Shadow Jump (Su)**

**Ability:** The cabalist gains the [shadowdancer's](ref/pclass/shadowdancer) _shadow jump_ ability
with an effective shadowdancer level equal to his vigilante level - 6. If he has levels in
shadowdancer, those levels stack with his vigilante levels to determine the total distance per day
he can shadow jump (to a maximum of 320 feet per day).

**At 10th Level:** _The total distance increases to 320 feet._

**At 4th Level:** _A shadowdancer gains the ability to travel between shadows as if by means of a
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) spell. The limitation is that the magical transport must
begin and end in an area with at least some dim light. A shadowdancer can jump up to a total of 40
feet each day in this way; this may be a single jump of 40 feet or four jumps of 10 feet each._

**At 6th Level:** _The total distance increases to 80 feet._

**At 8th Level:** _The total distance increases to 160 feet._

**Prereq:** 10th-level vigilante

**Special:** _This amount can be split among many jumps, but each one, no matter how small, counts
as a 10-foot increment._

**Tattoo Chamber (Su)**

**Ability:** The cabalist can magically absorb items through a tattoo on his hand or wrist and
easily retrieve them or activate their magical abilities without making them manifest. The items
seemingly disappear into the tattoo, but actually enter an extradimensional space that the cabalist
can access via the tattoo. Stowing an item in this way requires a **full-round action**, and the
space can store a number of items equal to 1 + [one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his
vigilante level. These items must be items the cabalist can hold in one hand.

**Ability2:** Retrieving a stowed item requires mentally activating the tattoo as a **swift
action**. The item appears in the cabalist's hand, so worn items must still be donned to gain their
benefits.

**Special:** The cabalist can also activate any [spell-trigger](ref/misc/spell-trigger) abilities of
a stored item as though he were wielding the item, producing the magic effect from his tattoo rather
than from the item. If the cabalist dies while items are within his tattoo chamber, those items fade
into existence within 5 feet of the corpse.

**Bloodbound Spell (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever the cabalist casts a spell against a foe that is taking bleed damage, the foe
takes a -1 penalty on its saving throw against the spell (or to its AC if the spell requires an
attack roll). This penalty increases to -2 if the spell is from the necromancy school.

**Flavor:** A cabalist gets an advantage when casting spells against bleeding foes.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Startling appearance

**Bloody Horror (Ex)**

**Free:** When a cabalist casts a spell and one of the targets (or one creature in the area for an
area spell) is taking bleed damage, the cabalist can make an Intimidate check to demoralize all
targets of the spell (or all creatures in the area for an area spell). The cabalist rolls only one
Intimidate check and applies the result to all the targets.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Frightening appearance

**Special:** Once a creature has been the target of this ability (regardless of whether or not it
was successful), it is immune to that cabalist's bloody horror for 24 hours. This is a
mind-affecting fear effect.

**Shadowy Appearance (Sp)**

**Ability:** He gains a continuous 20% miss chance (as per [blur](ref/spell/blur)). The cabalist can
deactivate or reactivate this effect as a **standard action**.

**Flavor:** A cabalist becomes cloaked in shadow while in his vigilante identity.

**Immediate:** Once per day, he can gain the benefits of
[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater) for 1d6 rounds.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Stunning appearance
