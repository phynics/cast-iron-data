Within some vigilantes are incredible innate abilities that, although normally the domain of natural
beasts, inexplicably aid them in fighting their enemies. These vigilantes have ties to the creature
whose abilities they emulate, whether from a distant lycanthrope ancestor, a divine touch, unnatural
experimentation, or something else altogether.

**Natural Course (Ex)**

**Alters:** Vigilante specialization

**At 1st Level:** When a wildsoul chooses a vigilante specialization, he must also choose a natural
course: _arachnid, falconine, feline,_ or _ursine._ This represents the type of creature that
influences his body, granting him his remarkable abilities. Once this choice is made, it can't be
changed.

At 2nd, 6th, 12th, and 18th levels, a wildsoul gains a specific ability tied to his natural course.
These abilities count as vigilante talents, as onlookers who see the wildsoul in his social identity
use them overtly might suspect him of being something more than he appears; the more obvious
animalistic abilities (like _bear form_ and _shoot webs_) impart a -10 penalty on the wildsoul's
Disguise check to appear as his social identity.

**Replaces:** Vigilante talents gained at 2nd, 6th, 12th, and 18th levels

### Arachnid

These wildsouls have the blood of spiders running through their veins.

**Heightened Senses (Ex)**

**Ability:** He gains the [stalker sense](ref/vigtalent/stalker-sense) vigilante talent, even if he
is not a stalker.

**Flavor:** An arachnid wildsoul can anticipate physical danger and react astonishingly quickly.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Shoot Webs (Ex)**

**Ability:** An arachnid wildsoul's wrists can produce thick, sticky webbing that he can shoot at
his enemies. Shooting webbing is a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. The
webbing acts as a [tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag) when it hits a creature, except the
DC of the Reflex save to avoid being glued to the floor or unable to fly is equal to 10 + 1/2 the
arachnid wildsoul's vigilante level + his Constitution modifier. Like a tanglefoot bag, the webbing
does not work underwater.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Usage:** Constitution3 modifier times/day

**Web Specialist (Ex)**

**Ability:** An arachnid wildsoul can coat his hands and feet in super-sticky webbing, even over
equipment such as gloves and boots. This gives him a climb speed of 30 feet.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Move:** An arachnid wildsoul can use his webbing to create the equivalent of a 50-foot length of
[silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft) (hp 4, break DC 24) and can shoot it at any flat,
unoccupied surface that is at least one 5-foot square in size. This provokes attacks of opportunity.
If the arachnid wildsoul succeeds at a ranged attack against AC 10, the rope sticks fast to the
surface, can only be removed if it is destroyed, and can support the weight of the arachnid wildsoul
plus one additional Medium or smaller creature. If more creatures attempt to use the webbing rope,
it is destroyed. This costs a daily use of _shoot webs._

**Web Master (Ex)**

**Ability:** An arachnid wildsoul can use _web specialist_ without spending a daily use of _shoot
webs._ Additionally, he can create numerous web-ropes as he is moving and swing from rope to rope,
essentially allowing him to move at his base speed through the air as long as he always uses at
least one hand to hold the webbing and continues to have tether points within 50 feet and above him.
The web ropes he creates in this way dissolve as soon as he is done using them to swing.

**Gained:** At 18th Level

### Falconine

These wildsouls have the blood of falcons or other large birds of prey running through their veins.

**Soft Landing (Ex)**

**Ability:** A falconine wildsoul can grow feathery wing-flaps under his arms that allow him to fall
slowly. He gains the [perfect fall](ref/vigtalent/perfect-fall) vigilante talent.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Eagle Eye (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A falconine wildsoul hones his visual senses. He gains a competence bonus equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his vigilante level on visual Perception checks.

**Take to the Air (Ex)**

**Ability:** While in his vigilante identity, he has feathery wings that grant him a fly speed of 40
feet (with good maneuverability).

**Flavor:** A falconine wildsoul can harness the flight inherent in his blood.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Deadly Dive (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A falconine wildsoul has learned how to mimic the swooping and diving of birds of prey.

**Fullround:** While he is flying at least 30 feet above the ground, a falconine wildsoul can dive
onto a creature by moving up to twice his fly speed and make a single melee attack. The movement
provokes attacks of opportunity as normal, and the attack provokes an attack of opportunity from the
attack's target. If the attack is successful, the falconine wildsoul deals the attack's damage plus
2d6 points of damage for every 10 feet he dove (to a maximum of 20d6 damage if he dove 100 feet or
more). Even if the attack misses, the target still takes the extra damage for the distance of the
dive, but it receives a Reflex save to halve the damage (DC = 10 + 1/2 the falconine wildsoul's
vigilante level + his Strength or Dexterity modifier, whichever is higher).

A falconine vigilante can dive in this way a number of times per day equal to his Strength or
Dexterity modifier, whichever is higher.

**Gained:** At 18th Level

### Ursine

These wildsouls have the blood of bears running through their veins.

**Deadly Claws (Ex)**

**Ability:** An ursine wildsoul's hands become claws in his vigilante identity, granting him two
claw attacks. These are primary [natural attacks](ref/rule/natural-attacks) that deal 1d4 points of
damage if he is Medium or [1d3 if he is Small](ref/umr/natural-attacks).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Bear Jaws (Ex)**

**Ability:** An ursine wildsoul gains a bite attack while in his vigilante identity. This is a
primary natural attack that deals 1d4 points of damage if he is Medium or 1d3 if he is Small.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Thick Hide (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** An ursine wildsoul in his vigilante identity has skin that bristles with thick hair
like that of a bear's. He gains a natural armor bonus of +1.

At 16th level: This natural armor bonus +2.

At 20th level: This natural armor bonus +3.

**Bear Form (Su)**

**Flavor:** An ursine wildsoul has learned how to take a bear's form.

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Standard:** He can change into bear form at will, and the transformation lasts until he changes
back. While transformed, he becomes a [grizzly bear](ref/monster/bear-grizzly-bear) as
[beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2). An ursine wildsoul loses his ability to speak while in
bear form and is limited to the sounds that a normal bear can make, though he can communicate
normally with other bears, as [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals).

### Feline

**Sources:** Blood of the Beast pg 7\
Feline wildsouls have the blood of great cats running through their veins.

**Feline Attributes (Ex)**

**Ability:** A feline wildsoul gains the [low-light vision](ref/umr/low-light-vision) and
[scent](ref/umr/scent) special abilities and reduces any penalty he takes on Perception checks due
to distance by an amount equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his vigilante level
(minimum 0). If he also gains [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) or scent from a feat or
racial trait, he instead gains a +2 bonus on Perception checks for each ability he already has.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Special:** He can take [Aspect of the Beast](ref/feat/aspect-of-the-beast), ignoring the feat's
prerequisites.

**Feline Grace (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** He also gains [improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge).

**At 6th Level:** A feline wildsoul gains [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge), using his
vigilante level as his effective [rogue](ref/class/rogue) level.

**Feline Pounce (Ex)**

**Ability:** He gains the [mad rush](ref/vigtalent/mad-rush) avenger vigilante talent, even if he is
not an avenger.

**Flavor:** A feline wildsoul can strike quickly and lethally at a moment's notice.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Nine Lives (Su)**

**Ability:** He gains the [defensive roll](ref/talent/defensive-roll) advanced rogue talent and can
use this ability eight times per day.

**Flavor:** A feline wildsoul can survive attacks that would kill lesser vigilantes.

**Gained:** At 18th Level
