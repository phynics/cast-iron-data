**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 24\
Since the disappearance of the Rogarvias, Brevoy draws ever closer to civil war. The rule of
King-Regent Noleski Surtova grows increasingly tenuous; to hold his position, he sends handpicked
agents into the kingdom's courts to flush out traitors to the Dragonscale Throne. When not acting as
courtiers, these agents don fearsome draconic masks, becoming anonymous protectors of the
kingregent's rule.

**Ruby Courtier (Ex)**

**Alters:** Social talents

**Flavor:** Surtova's minions pose as minor nobles studying the Aldori swordlord traditions common
throughout Brevoy.

**At 1st Level:** A Dragonscale loyalist gains
[Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
([Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword)) as a bonus feat. His social identity
must appear to be that of a noble, and he can't select [double time](ref/soctalent/double-time),
[many guises](ref/soctalent/many-guises), or any social talent inappropriate for a noble social
identity.

**Replaces:** 1st-level social talent

**Reflexive Reaction (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A Dragonscale loyalist lacks the conviction of other vigilantes, but his reactions are
keenly honed.

**At 3rd Level:** If a Dragonscale loyalist acts during a surprise round, he can take a full round's
worth of actions. If he does, he is [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) during the first full round of
combat.

**Replaces:** Unshakable

**False Allegiance (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Eventually, a loyalist begins training to infiltrate one of Brevoy's great houses.

**At 5th Level:** A Dragonscale loyalist chooses one of the seven houses of Brevoy, gaining a bonus
feat and a special ability appropriate to the chosen house. He need not meet the feat's
prerequisites.

**Replaces:** Startling appearance

**House Garess**

**Ability:** The Dragonscale loyalist gains [Sure Grasp](ref/feat/sure-grasp) and
[dwarves'](ref/race/dwarf) stonecunning racial trait.

**House Lebeda**

**Ability:** The Dragonscale loyalist gains [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Appraise). He can
also resell items for 60% of their listed value, rather than 50%. The purchase limit of the
settlement must be high enough to accommodate the increased value.

**House Lodovka**

**Ability:** The Dragonscale loyalist gains [Sea Legs](ref/feat/sea-legs). When attempting Swim
checks, he ignores the armor check penalty of light or medium armor. If the loyalist has the
[heavy training](ref/vigtalent/heavy-training) vigilante talent, this applies to heavy armor as
well.

**House Medvyed**

**Ability:** The Dragonscale loyalist gains [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) and the druid's
[resist nature's lure](ref/ability/resist-natures-lure) class feature.

**House Orlovsky**

**Ability:** The Dragonscale loyalist gains [Call Truce](ref/feat/call-truce) and a +3 bonus to his
CMD.

**House Rogarvia**

**Ability:** The Dragonscale loyalist gains Skill Focus (Knowledge \[history\]) and a +2 bonus on
Diplomacy checks to gather information. This bonus increases to +4 when gathering information
related to the Rogarvias.

**House Surtova**

**Ability:** The Dragonscale loyalist gains [Persuasive](ref/feat/persuasive) and a +4 bonus on
Sense Motive checks against creatures that profess to be his allies.

**Dragonscale Vigilance (Su)**

**Flavor:** A Dragonscale loyalist becomes supernaturally perceptive to plots against his lord.

**At 11th Level:** While in his vigilante identity, a Dragonscale loyalist is treated as constantly
being under the effects of [perceive betrayal](ref/spell/perceive-betrayal). This effect's caster
level is equal to the Dragonscale loyalist's vigilante level, and he doesn't require material
components or a focus.

**Replaces:** Frightening appearance

**Conqueror's Wrath (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Inspired by the deeds of Chora the Conqueror, a loyalist can rampage through enemy
ranks.

**Fullround:** A Dragonscale loyalist can make a full attack and move up to his speed. The loyalist
can pause at any point or points during the movement, make one or more of his attacks, and continue
moving. Creatures making attacks of opportunity against the loyalist during this movement gain a +4
bonus on their attack and damage rolls.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Stunning appearance

**Special:** Using this ability counts as using a vigilante talent.
