Teisatsu are specialized vigilantes who focus on infiltrating social scenes and high-society
gatherings on behalf of the feuding lords of Minkai. Trained as assassins and spies, they cultivate
a suite of personalities and study secret arts that hone their bodies into deadly weapons.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** Teisatsu are proficient with all simple weapons, plus the [kama](ref/eq-weapon/kama),
[katana](ref/eq-weapon/katana), [kusarigama](ref/eq-weapon/kusarigama),
[nunchaku](ref/eq-weapon/nunchaku), [sai](ref/eq-weapon/sai),
[short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword), [shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow),
[shuriken](ref/eq-weapon/shuriken), [siangham](ref/eq-weapon/siangham), and
[wakizashi](ref/eq-weapon/wakizashi). They are proficient with light and medium armor, but not with
shields.

**Replaces:** Weapon and armor proficiencies

**Infiltrator (Ex)**

**Alters:** Vigilante specialization

**Info:** A teisatsu is required to choose stalker as his specialization.

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** A teisatsu gains a ki pool, identical to the [ninja](ref/class/ninja) class feature,
using his vigilante level as his effective ninja level.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Vigilante talent gained at 2nd level

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a pool of ki points. The number of points in your ki pool is equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your ninja level + your Charisma modifier.

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the ninja class ability.

**At 10th Level:** As long as you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, you reduce the DC of
Acrobatics skill checks made to jump by 1/2 (although you still can't move farther than your speed
allows).

**Passive:** As long as you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, you treat any
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) skill check made to jump as if you had a running start.

**Special:** If the ninja has levels in another class that grants points to a _ki_ pool, all of
these levels stack to determine the total number of _ki_ points in the combined pool, but only one
ability score modifier is added to the total. The choice of which score to use is made when the
second class ability is gained, and once made, the choice is set. The ninja can now use _ki_ points
from this pool to power the abilities of every class she possesses that grants a _ki_ pool.

The ki pool is replenished each morning after 8 hours of rest or meditation; these hours do not need
to be consecutive.

**Swift:** By spending 1 point from your ki pool, you can do one of the following: 1) take one
additional attack at your highest attack bonus when making a full attack, 2) increase your speed by
20 feet for one round, or 3) give yourself a +4 insight bonus on Stealth checks for 1 round.

**Teisatsu Talents**

**Ability:** A teisatsu selects vigilante talents as normal. He can't select the
[rogue talent](ref/vigtalent/rogue-talent) vigilante talent. He can, however, select either of the
talents below, which are unique to the teisatsu.

**Alters:** Vigilante talents

**Ki Power (Su)**

**Ability:** The teisatsu gains a single ki power of his choice from the list available to the
[unchained monk](ref/class/unchained-monk) class.

**Shadow Tricks (Ex)**

**Ability:** The teisatsu gains a single ninja trick (but not a _master trick_) of his choice. He
cannot select a _rogue talent_ in place of a _ninja trick_ in this manner. If he selects a _ninja
trick_ labeled as a Primary Sneak Attack Trick, that talent applies to his _hidden strikes_ instead
of to sneak attacks, and the talent counts as a Hidden Strike Talent for the limitation of applying
only one effect to a single _hidden strike._
