**Ki Meditation (Su)**

**Fullround:** A monk with this ability can spend 2 ki points to meditate, as if using Combat
Meditation. Alternatively, he can spend 4 ki points to meditate as a **move action** instead.

**Prereq:** [Combat Meditation](ref/feat/combat-meditation)

**Special:** Using these ki points does not count as one of the monk's daily uses of Combat
Meditation, but it grants him the usual benefits he would gain from his meditation feats for doing
so.
