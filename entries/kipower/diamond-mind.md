By spending 1 point from his ki pool as a swift action, a monk with this power can suppress a fear
effect affecting him as if using _[remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear)_, using his monk level as his
caster level. He can spend 2 ki points to activate this ability even when
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked). A monk must be at least 6th
level before selecting this ki power.
