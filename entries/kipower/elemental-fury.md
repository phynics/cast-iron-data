**Elemental Fury (Su)**

**Choice:** A monk who selects this ki power must select one type of energy: acid, cold,
electricity, or fire. Once this choice is made, it cannot be changed.

**Prereq:** 6th-level monk

**Swift:** The monk can expend 1 ki point to imbue his natural attacks with the chosen energy,
causing them to deal 1d6 points of damage of the chosen type for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 his
monk level.
