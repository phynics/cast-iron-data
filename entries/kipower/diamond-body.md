By spending 1 point from his ki pool, a monk with this power can remove one toxin from his body as
if using _[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison)_, using his monk level as his caster
level. He can remove only one poison in this way per use of this ability. A monk must be at least
8th level before selecting this ki power.
