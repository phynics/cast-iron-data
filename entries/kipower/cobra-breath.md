**Cobra Breath (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a monk with this ki power uses _diamond body_ to neutralize a poison, he can
instead release that poison as a ranged touch attack against a single foe within 30 feet. If the
attack hits, the foe must succeed at a saving throw (using the poison's original DC) or suffer the
poison's effects, even if it was not originally a contact poison.

**Prereq:** 12th-level monk, [diamond body](ref/kipower/diamond-body)
