Whenever a monk with this ki power uses [diamond body](ref/kipower/diamond-body) to neutralize a
poison, he can instead release that poison as a ranged touch attack against a single foe within 30
feet. If the attack hits, the foe must succeed at a saving throw (using the poison's original DC) or
suffer the poison's effects, even if it was not originally a contact poison. A monk must be at least
12th level and must possess the [diamond body](ref/kipower/diamond-body) ki power before selecting
this ki power.
