**&L&Ki Metabolism (Su)&FN&**

**Flavor:** A monk with this ability uses his ki to control his metabolism.

**Move:** He can spend 1 ki point to enter a state of suspended animation, falling unconscious and
appearing dead to all senses. At the time he enters this state, he indicates a preset period of time
or a triggering condition, after which he awakens.

**Passive:** As long as he has at least 1 point remaining in his ki pool, the monk needs to eat and
drink only 1/4 as often as normal, needs only 2 hours of sleep each night (including to replenish
his ki pool), and can [hold his breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for up to 1 hour per point of
Constitution.
