**Furious Defense (Ex)**

**Immediate:** By spending 1 ki point, a monk with this ki power can grant himself a +4 dodge bonus
to AC until the end of his next turn.

**Prereq:** 7th-level monk

**Special:** The monk can activate this ability and the
[formless mastery](ref/kipower/formless-mastery) ki power with the same immediate action for a total
cost of 3 ki points.
