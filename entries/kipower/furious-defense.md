By spending 1 point from his ki pool as an immediate action, a monk with this ki power can grant
himself a +4 dodge bonus to AC until the end of his next turn. The monk can activate this ability
and the [formless mastery](ref/kipower/formless-mastery) ki power with the same immediate action for
a total cost of 3 ki points. A monk must be at least 7th level before selecting this ki power.
