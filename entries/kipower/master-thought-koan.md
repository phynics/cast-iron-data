**Master-Thought Koan (Su)**

**Ability:** A monk with this ability can spend an additional 2 ki points when using either the
_breaking-down koan_ or _building-up koan_ ki powers. When combined with _master-thought koan,
breaking-down koan_ affects a number of characters of your choice within 30 feet equal to the monk's
wisdom modifier. When combined with _master-thought koan, building-up koan_ can be used on an ally
within 30 feet.

**Prereq:** 12th-level monk, either [breaking-down koan](ref/kipower/breaking-down-koan) or
[building-up koan](ref/kipower/building-up-koan)
