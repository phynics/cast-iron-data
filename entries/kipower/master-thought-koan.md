A monk with this ability can spend an additional 2 ki points when using either the
[breaking-down koan](ref/kipower/breaking-down-koan) or
[building-up koan](ref/kipower/building-up-koan) ki powers. When combined with master-thought koan,
breaking-down koan affects a number of characters of your choice within 30 feet equal to the monk's
wisdom modifier. When combined with master-thought koan, building-up koan can be used on an ally
within 30 feet. A monk must be at least 12th level and have either the
[breaking-down koan](ref/kipower/breaking-down-koan) or the building-up koan ki power before
selecting this ki power.
