**&L&One Touch (Ex)&FN&**

**Prereq:** 12th-level monk

**Special:** He can spend 1 ki point to double this bonus for that attack.

**Standard:** As long as he has at least 1 point remaining in his ki pool, a monk with this power
can make an unarmed strike against a foe as a touch attack. He adds 1/2 his monk level as a bonus on
the damage roll.
