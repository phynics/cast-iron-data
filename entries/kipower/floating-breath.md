**&L&Floating Breath (Su)&FN&**

**Prereq:** 8th-level monk

**Swift:** A monk with this ki power can spend 1 ki point to balance on the air, hovering in place,
until the end of his next turn. This power does not allow the monk to fly or otherwise move into the
air; he must jump or use some other ability to reach his position. This otherwise functions as
[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk).
