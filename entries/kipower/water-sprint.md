**Water Sprint (Su)**

**Prereq:** 6th-level monk

**Swift:** A monk with this ki power can spend 1 ki point to gain the ability to walk on water, as
if under the effects of [water walk](ref/spell/water-walk). Once activated, this ability lasts for 1
minute per monk level.
