A monk with this ki power can spend 1 point from his ki pool as a swift action to gain the ability
to walk on water, as if under the effects of _[water walk](ref/spell/water-walk)_. Once activated,
this ability lasts for 1 minute per monk level. A monk must be at least 6th level before selecting
this ki power.
