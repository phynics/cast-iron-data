A monk can spend 2 points from his ki pool as a swift action to gain
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to his monk level + 10. This spell resistance
lasts for a number of rounds equal to the monk's level. A monk must be at least 12th level before
selecting this ki power.
