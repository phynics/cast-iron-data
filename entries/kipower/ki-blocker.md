**&L&Ki Blocker (Su)&FN&**

**Free:** A monk with this ability can spend 1 ki point before making an unarmed strike against a
foe. If the attack hits, the monk can interrupt the target's flow of ki.

If the monk successfully damages the target of his attack, that opponent's cost to activate
abilities with ki increases by 1 ki point for 1 hour.

**Prereq:** 10th-level monk

**Special:** If the monk spends 2 ki points, he can use this ability to affect the target's _arcane
pool, arcane reservoir, grit points, inspiration,_ or _panache points_ instead of its ki pool. The
effects of this ability do not stack, but multiple hits increase the duration by 1 hour for each
hit.
