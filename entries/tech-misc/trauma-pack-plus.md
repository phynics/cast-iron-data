**Price** 11,250 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Capacity** 5; **Usage** 1 charge (disposable)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 26; **Cost** 5,625 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[medical lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A trauma pack plus functions as a [trauma pack](ref/tech-misc/trauma-pack), except that it's
advanced chemicals and tools allow someone to restore a recently slain creature to life if the
healing provided by the trauma pack plus is administered within 1 round of the target's death, as
breath of life. A trauma pack heals 2d8+2 points of damage on a successful DC 15 Heal check. For
every 5 points by which the result exceeds the DC, the pack provides an additional 2d8+2 points of
healing.
