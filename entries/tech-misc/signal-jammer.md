**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 52\
**Price** 10,000 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Capacity** 24; **Usage** 1 charge/hour

### Construction

**Craft DC** 26; **Cost** 5,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This small device emits a low, hissing sound when activated. While active, a white noise generator
creates a 60-foot-radius [spread](ref/misc/spread) in which all [signals](ref/misc/signal) are
completely blocked.
