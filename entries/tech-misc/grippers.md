**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 47\
**Price** 100 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Capacity** -; **Usage** -

### Construction

**Craft DC** 14; **Cost** 50 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

Grippers are powerful, long-handled tools that resemble a blacksmith's tongs. Once they're set, they
can be locked into place to grip an object. Used when attempting to pry open a stuck object (in a
manner similar to the use of a [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar)), grippers grant a +2 circumstance
bonus on Strength checks. When used to attach two devices, a set of grippers holds on with an
effective Strength score of 24. Applying grippers to a creature and then locking the grippers to
crush flesh and bone is a standard action that provokes an attack of opportunity and also requires a
successful melee attack with an [improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons). Once locked, the
grippers deal 1d4+7 points of damage.
