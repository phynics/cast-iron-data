**Price** 500 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge

### Construction

**Craft DC** 23; **Cost** 250 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A detonator is a small device that can be used to trigger
[cranial bombs](ref/tech-cyber/cranial-bomb), [cylex](ref/tech-misc/cylex), and other explosives
remotely. While an explosive is being set, the user can key the explosive to a detonator with a
standard action that costs the detonator one charge. Once the explosive is keyed, as a standard
action the user can push a button to trigger the explosive. A typical detonator has a
[signal](ref/misc/signal) range of 1,000 feet, though some may have a reduced range (intentionally
or otherwise) and others might be enhanced by a [signal booster](ref/tech-misc/signal-booster).
