**Price** 90,000 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge/round

### Construction

**Craft DC** 32; **Cost** 45,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [graviton lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories),
[enervation](ref/spell/enervation), [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis)

**Aura** moderate necromancy and transmutation\
This black, wand-like object creates a narrow gravitational field that affects both body and soul.
It duplicates the effects of _[telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis),_ but only to create a sustained
force (maximum 250 lbs.) toward or away from the wielder, or to perform a bull rush, drag, or steal
[combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) (CMB +15). The grip of death can perform combat
maneuvers on incorporeal undead. If the user performs a successful combat maneuver against a living
creature using the grip of death, the target gains 1 negative level as a portion of its soul is
wrenched from its body. A living creature killed in this way becomes a
[spectre](ref/monster/spectre). Otherwise, the negative levels fade after 10 hours.
