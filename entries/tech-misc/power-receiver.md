**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 51\
**Price** 5,000 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Capacity** -; **Usage** -

### Construction

**Craft DC** 30; **Cost** 2,500 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A power receiver is a device that attaches to any technological item's battery slot. The power
receiver is then set to the same frequency as a nearby power generator capable of broadcasting
power, after which the device draws power from the generator as if it were attached via a
[power cable](ref/tech-misc/power-cable). The generator determines the range at which the device can
draw power; this [signal](ref/misc/signal) can be enhanced by a
[signal booster](ref/tech-misc/signal-booster) or blocked by a
[signal jammer](ref/tech-misc/signal-jammer).
