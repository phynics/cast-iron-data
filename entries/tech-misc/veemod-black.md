A veemod ("vision enhancement module") is a narrow crystalline strip that slots into a set of
[veemod goggles](ref/tech-misc/veemod-goggles) to enhance the goggles' use. Inserting a veemod into
a pair of goggles is a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity. Veemods are color-coded
items, and provide the following benefits.

**Brown Veemod:** This veemod darkens the goggles' lenses, providing a +1 circumstance bonus on all
saving throws against bright light effects that cause [dazzling](ref/misc/dazzled) or
[blindness](ref/misc/blinded). When worn, they also allow creatures impacted by
[light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness) or [light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity) to see
normally in areas of bright light.\
**Black Veemod:** A black veemod enhances vision, and by blinking twice rapidly, the wearer can
magnify what she's seeing. Blinking twice again restores normal vision. This grants a +2 competence
bonus on all Perception checks.\
**White Veemod:** This veemod is similar to a black veemod, except that magnification is much
stronger, granting a +5 competence bonus on Perception checks.\
**Gray Veemod:** This veemod grants [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).\
**Green Veemod:** This veemod magnifies vision even more than a white veemod, granting a +10
competence bonus on Perception checks.\
**Red Veemod:** This veemod grants [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to a range of 60 feet.\
**Blue Veemod:** This veemod dramatically extends the scope of peripheral vision, granting the
effects of [all-around vision](ref/umr/all-around-vision) - the wearer cannot be
[flanked](ref/rule/flanking).\
**Orange Veemod:** The wearer can [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) of any kind, including
magical darkness.\
**Prismatic Veemod:** This veemod allows the user to see through solid objects. Vision range is 20
feet, with the wearer seeing as if he were looking at something in normal light even if there is no
illumination. [X-ray vision](ref/umr/x-ray-vision) can penetrate 20 feet of cloth, wood, or similar
animal or vegetable material. It can see through up to 10 feet of stone and some metals. It can see
through up to 10 inches of iron, steel, copper, brass, or similar metals. It cannot see through
lead, gold, platinum, plutonium, or [skymetals](ref/eq-material/skymetal) at all. It's possible to
scan an area of up to 100 square feet in 1 round. Secret compartments are 90% likely to be located
by x-ray scanning.
