**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 45\
**Price** 30 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge/8 hours

### Construction

**Craft DC** 16; **Cost** 15 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

When activated, a flashlight creates a beam of normal light in a 60-foot cone. It also increases the
light level in the area beyond this initial cone by one step, out to a 120-foot cone. It does not
increase the light level in normal light or bright light. A flashlight has no effect in areas of
magical darkness.
