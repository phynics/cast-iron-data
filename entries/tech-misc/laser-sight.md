**Price** 8,000 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge/hour

### Construction

**Craft DC** 24; **Cost** 4,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This small, cylindrical device can be attached to the barrel of any firearm. When activated, it
emits a low-power laser beam that paints a small red dot on the target. This effect grants the user
a +1 circumstance bonus on all attack rolls made with that weapon. Further, a laser sight reduces
the penalty for firing into melee by 2.
