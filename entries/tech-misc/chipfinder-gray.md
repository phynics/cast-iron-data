A chipfinder is a handheld device fitted with a circular screen. When a chipfinder is activated, all
active [tracker chips](ref/tech-misc/tracker-chip) within a certain radius appear as glowing dots on
the screen, indicating the direction and approximate distance to each installed tracker chip, as
well identification number for each chip. The screen can filter out unwanted data to make it easier
to track a specific chip. Chipfinders are color-coded items; the higher the chipfinder's color
rating, the longer its range. This range is a [signal](ref/misc/signal), and can be enhanced by a
[signal booster](ref/tech-misc/signal-booster) or blocked by a solid enough barrier.
