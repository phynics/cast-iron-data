**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 51\
**Price** 2,000 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge/hour

### Construction

**Craft DC** 27; **Cost** 1,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This device measures [radiation](ref/rule/radiation) levels in a 120-foot-radius circle, as the
[detect radiation](ref/spell/detect-radiation) spell. When activated, the device makes a soft
clicking sound that changes in volume and frequency according to the radiation level encountered. A
small screen on the top of the handheld device indicates the approximate source and strength of
radiation in these areas, with red areas indicating severe radiation, yellow high radiation, green
medium radiation, and blue low radiation. Areas with less radiation don't register on the device's
screen.
