**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 42\
**Price** 6,000 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge/hour

### Construction

**Craft DC** 27; **Cost** 3,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This handheld device allows for communication between two commset users. A commset carries audio and
visual [signals](ref/misc/signal), and includes a built-in camera that can record all communications
being broadcast. Communication between two commsets requires both users to tune their individual
devices to the same frequency. A commset has a range of 1 mile - beyond this range, communication is
impossible without enhancing the signal strength with a
[signal booster](ref/tech-misc/signal-booster). The price and cost listed for this item are for a
single commset.
