**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 50\
**Price** 500 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Capacity** -; **Usage** disposable

### Construction

**Craft DC** 25; **Cost** 250 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[nanotech lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A nanite canister is used to power technological devices that use nanites - such items generally
don't require other sources of power, as the nanites themselves do the bulk of the work when the
object is activated. The canister contains a small, independent power source and a supply of
nanites. A single canister contains enough nanites to provide 10 charges for a nanotech item or
weapon.
