**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 40\
**Price** 2,500 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge

### Construction

**Craft DC** 25; **Cost** 1,250 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A chemalyzer is a handheld unit with an extendable wand tipped with a sampling nozzle. It grants a
+5 competence bonus on all Craft (alchemy) or Knowledge (engineering) checks to identify an unknown
pharmaceutical, poison, chemical, or substance. It cannot aid in the identification of magical
effects or items like potions.
