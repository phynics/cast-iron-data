**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 53\
**Price** 20 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge

### Construction

**Craft DC** 18; **Cost** 10 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A zipstick is a small pen-like device used to administer a small jolt of electricity. This jolt is
enough to cause 1 point of nonlethal damage with a successful touch attack. A zipstick is used most
often to activate or deactivate items that function on ion-bonding technology, such as
[ion tape](ref/tech-misc/ion-tape).
