**Price** 3,000 gp\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Capacity** 20; **Usage** 1 charge/hour

### Construction

**Craft DC** 25; **Cost** 1,500 gp\
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This handheld device can capture still photographs or record video. The device uses a charge when
activated and consumes an additional charge each hour; during that hour, individual photographs or
video recordings do not consume additional charges. Images and videos captured by a camera can be
displayed on the camera's palm-sized screen. A camera can hold up to 20 hours of video footage and
200 photographs.
