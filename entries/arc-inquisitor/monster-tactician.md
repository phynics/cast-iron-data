While most inquisitors have learned to take advantage of the movements of their opponents and allies
in combat, some instead summon creatures as a means of claiming strategic advantage, exploiting the
mystic connection between themselves and their summoned creatures to impart a bit of their own
tactical knowledge.

**Summon Monster (Sp)**

**At 2nd Level:** Beginning at **2nd level** and **each level** thereafter, the monster tactician
can add one monster from the [Expanded Summon Monster](ref/feat/expanded-summon-monster) list to her
list of available summoned creatures. She can add a new option to any
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell she can cast at the time she chooses the monster;
once made, these choices cannot be changed.

**At 1st Level:** A monster tactician can cast [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). She can cast this spell as a **standard
action**, and the creatures remain for 1 minute per level (instead of 1 round per level).

**Replaces:** Judgment

**Special:** A monster tactician cannot have more than one
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell active in this way at a time; if she uses
another, any existing _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ immediately ends. These
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells are considered to be part of the monster
tactician's spell list for the purposes of spell trigger and spell completion items. In addition,
she can expend uses of this ability to fulfill the construction requirements of any magic items she
creates, so long as she can use this ability to cast the required spells

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

At 3rd level: This now behaves as summon monster +2.

At 5th level: This now behaves as summon monster +3.

At 7th level: This now behaves as summon monster +4.

At 9th level: This now behaves as summon monster +5.

At 11th level: This now behaves as summon monster +6.

At 13th level: This now behaves as summon monster +7.

At 15th level: This now behaves as summon monster +8.

At 17th level: This now behaves as summon monster +9.

**Summon Tactics (Su)**

**Ability:** Every time a monster tactician casts a summoning spell, she can choose any one teamwork
feat she knows. All creatures summoned by the spell are treated as if they had the chosen feat for
all purposes, without needing to meet any prerequisites.

**At 16th Level:** She can choose three teamwork feats. She cannot choose the same feat twice.

**At 20th Level:** All creatures summoned by the spell are treated as if they knew all of the
monster tactician's teamwork feats.

**At 8th Level:** She can choose two different teamwork feats.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Discern lies, second judgment, third judgment, slayer, true judgment
