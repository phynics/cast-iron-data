**Sources:** Blood of the Beast pg 4\
Throughout the Mwangi Expanse, cults of [Angazhan](ref/faith/angazhan) pollute the pristine jungle
with demonic influence and wanton bloodshed. For generations, the [catfolk](ref/race/catfolk) of
Murraseth have viewed such faiths with loathing and hatred, and they believe it is their sacred duty
to hunt down the followers of the Ravener King and expel them from the Material Plane.

**Prerequisites:** Catfolk; Alignment must be nonevil

**Charged by Nature**

**Ability:** She gains one revelation from her chosen mystery. She must meet the revelation's
prerequisites, using her inquisitor level as her effective [oracle](ref/class/oracle) level to
determine the revelation's effects, and she never qualifies for the
[Extra Revelation](ref/feat/extra-revelation) feat.

**Choice:** A ravener hunter chooses an oracle [mystery](ref/ability/mysteries) from the following
list: [ancestor](ref/mystery/ancestor), [battle](ref/mystery/battle), [flame](ref/mystery/flame),
[heavens](ref/mystery/heavens), [life](ref/mystery/life), [lunar](ref/mystery/lunar),
[nature](ref/mystery/nature), [solar](ref/mystery/solar), [stone](ref/mystery/stone),
[time](ref/mystery/time), [volcano](ref/mystery/volcano), [waves](ref/mystery/waves),
[wind](ref/mystery/wind), or [wood](ref/mystery/wood).

**Flavor:** Rather than having a deity patron, a ravener hunter is charged by the spirits of the
Mwangi to eradicate evil wherever it appears.

**At 8th Level:** The ravener hunter gains a second revelation from her chosen mystery.

**Replaces:** Domain

**Holy Magic**

**Alters:** Spells

**Info:** A ravener hunter adds all spells of 6th-level and lower on the cleric spell list with the
good descriptor to her inquisitor spell list as inquisitor spells of the same level. If a spell
appears on both the cleric and inquisitor spell lists, the ravener hunter uses the lower of the two
spell levels listed for the spell. She cannot cast spells^1^ with the chaotic, evil, or lawful
descriptors, even from spell trigger or spell completion items.

**Demon Hunter**

**Ability:** A ravener hunter gains [Demon Hunter](ref/feat/demon-hunter) as a bonus feat, ignoring
its prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** She also gains a +2 bonus on Knowledge (religion) checks to recognize the worshipers of
any deity with the Demon subdomain, as well as a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and caster level
checks to overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) of creatures that she recognizes as
followers of such a deity.

**Replaces:** Teamwork feat gained at 3rd level

**Solo Tactics (Ex)**

**Info:** A ravener hunter gains this ability at 6th level instead of 3rd level.

**Gained:** At 6th Level
