**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 28\
Some inquisitors are adept at appearing harmless until they throw off their unassuming facades and
take their enemies by surprise.

**Gained class skills:** soh

**Lost class skills:** intm

**Lure Prey (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Cloaked wolves are skilled at maintaining a harmless guise even while they silently
prepare to strike against their enemies.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A cloaked wolf receives a morale bonus on all Disguise and Sleight of Hand checks equal
to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her inquisitor level (minimum +1). When a cloaked wolf
uses Sleight of Hand to draw a hidden weapon, this bonus doubles.

**Replaces:** Stern gaze

**Always Wary (Ex)**

**Alters:** Cunning initiative

**Flavor:** A cloaked wolf is always ready for betrayal and subterfuge.

**At 1st Level:** Whenever she attempts a Sense Motive check to avoid being surprised by a foe using
Bluff to conceal the attack, the cloaked wolf can always act during the surprise round, regardless
of the result of the check.

**At 2nd Level:** She adds her Wisdom modifier (in addition to her Dexterity modifier) to her
initiative checks during any such encounter.

**Replaces:** Monster lore

**Unleashed Fury (Ex)**

**Ability:** A cloaked wolf can transition between parley and combat in the blink of an eye. She
gains [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw) as a bonus feat.

**Action:** At 6th Level

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Solo tactics, teamwork feats

**Special:** She must meet the prerequisites of any bonus feat she selects.

At 9th level: The inquisitor gains a 2nd bonus feat from the list above.

At 12th level: The cloaked wolf chooses a 3rd bonus feat from the list above.

At 15th level: The inquisitor gains a 4th bonus feat from the list above.

At 18th level: The cloaked wolf chooses a 5th bonus feat from the list above.

She gains a bonus feat from the following list: [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard), [Deceitful](ref/feat/deceitful),
[Deft Hands](ref/feat/deft-hands), [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Improved Initiative](ref/feat/improved-initiative),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Improvised Weapon Mastery](ref/feat/improvised-weapon-mastery), [Lookout](ref/feat/lookout),
[Mobility](ref/feat/mobility), [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack),
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), and [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything).

As a **standard action**, a cloaked wolf can choose to learn a new bonus feat from the above list in
place of the most recent bonus feat she has already learned. In effect, the cloaked wolf loses the
bonus feat in exchange for the new one. She can only change the most recent bonus feat gained.
Whenever she gains a new bonus feat, the previous bonus feat becomes set and cannot be changed
again.

A cloaked wolf can change her most recent bonus feat a number of times per day equal to her Wisdom
modifier (minimum 1).
