**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 99\
Some inquisitors create a strong bond with an animal companion, and they hunt and punish threats to
the faith as an awe-inspiring duo. When they work together as one, there are few that dare to stand
in their way.

**Animal Companion (Ex)**

**Ability:** A sacred huntsmaster forms a bond with an
[animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion). This ability works as the
[hunter](ref/class/hunter) class feature of the same name, using her inquisitor level as her hunter
level.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Judgment 1/day

**Hunter Tactics (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A sacred huntsmaster automatically grants her teamwork feats to her animal companion.
The companion doesn't need to meet the prerequisites of these teamwork feats.

**Replaces:** Solo tactics

**Animal Focus (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Later iterations of the judgment ability

**Swift:** A sacred huntsmaster can take on the aspects of an animal. This ability works as the
hunter's [animal focus](ref/ability/animal-foci) class feature, with her inquisitor level serving as
her hunter level.

**Improved Empathic Link (Su)**

**Ability:** The sacred huntsmaster gains an empathic link with her animal companion. This functions
like an empathic link with a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar), except the sacred huntsmaster can
also see through a companion's eyes as a **swift action**, maintaining this connection as long as
she likes (as long as the companion is within 1 mile) and ending it as a **free action**.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Second judgment

**Special:** The sacred huntsmaster is [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) while maintaining this
connection.

**Raise Animal Companion (Sp)**

**Ability:** A sacred huntsmaster gains [raise animal companion](ref/spell/raise-animal-companion)
as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (not restricted to her own animal
companion). Using this ability gives the sacred huntsmaster a
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) that cannot be overcome in any way
(including by [restoration](ref/spell/restoration)), but automatically ends after 24 hours.

**At 16th Level:** This functions as [resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection) instead of
[raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead), but otherwise operates as normal.

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Replaces:** Third judgment

**Second Animal Focus (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a sacred huntsmaster uses her _animal focus_ ability, she selects two
different animal aspects for herself instead of one, and can assign two aspects to her companion
instead of one. As with the companion's previous aspect, the second one does not count against the
minutes per day a sacred huntsmaster can take on an aspect. If her animal companion is dead and the
sacred huntsmaster has applied that aspect to herself, that aspect counts toward her maximum of two
aspects at once. The sacred huntsmaster can still only apply one of her dead companion's to herself,
not both.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Slayer

**Greater Empathic Link (Su)**

**Ability:** The range of a sacred huntsmaster's empathic link with her animal companion increases
to 10 miles. If the animal companion is within 1 mile, it and the sacred huntsmaster can communicate
telepathically.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** True judgment
