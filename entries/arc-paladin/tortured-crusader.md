Though tortured crusaders may be beaten and battered, body, mind, and soul, they still somehow
continue through torment and hardship. Despite setbacks and impossible odds, they shine like candles
even in the darkest times. Somewhere in their minds, they realize that they can't win in the end,
but this only strengthens their resolve to fight for what is right, rather than giving in to
hopelessness.

**Torment**

**Alters:** Spellcasting, smite evil, lay on hands

**Flavor:** A tortured crusader's great suffering in the face of inevitable horror strengthens her
faith and resolve, even as it makes her introverted and far less personable than most paladins.

**Info:** A tortured crusader uses Wisdom instead of Charisma as her key spellcasting ability score
(to determine her spell DCs, bonus spells per day, bonus on concentration checks, and so on), and to
determine the effects of _lay on hands._

**Gained class skills:** ka, kd, kp, per, stl, sur, umd

**Lost class skills:** diplo, ha

**Self-Sufficient**

**Alters:** Class skills, skill ranks per level

**Info:** A tortured crusader gains a number of skill ranks equal to 4 + her Intelligence modifier
at each level, instead of gaining a number of skill ranks equal to 2 + her Intelligence modifier.

**All is Darkness**

**Alters:** Smite evil

**Flavor:** Everything around a tortured crusader is so bleak that detecting evil only reminds her
that the odds are always against her.

**At 1st Level:** A tortured crusader gains the _smite evil_ ability, but she doesn't add her
Charisma bonus to attack rolls or a deflection bonus equal to her Charisma bonus to her AC against
the target of her smite.

**At 2nd Level:** She adds a +4 bonus to attack rolls and a +4 deflection bonus to AC against the
target of her smite.

**Info:** A tortured crusader doesn't gain the _[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)_ or _divine
grace_ class features.

**Replaces:** Detect evil, divine grace

**Alone in the Dark**

**Alters:** Lay on hands, channel energy, divine bond, aura of courage, aura of resolve, aura of
faith, aura of righteousness

**Flavor:** Faced with endless foes, a tortured crusader can't ask others to confront the same
horrors she does.

**At 4th Level:** She can convert two uses of _lay on hands_ into an additional use of _smite evil._

**Info:** She can't use her _lay on hands_ ability to heal others or to channel energy. Her _aura of
courage, aura of resolve, aura of faith,_ and _aura of righteousness_ affect only her, not her
allies. When she selects a _divine bond,_ it must be with a weapon, not a mount.

**Second Chance (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, a tortured crusader can spend two uses of _lay on hands_ and choose an
unambiguous trigger such as &quot&when I am paralyzed&quot& or &quot&when I am about to fall
unconscious from hit point damage,&quot& as well as a _mercy_ she knows starting at 3rd level. When
the named trigger occurs, the tortured crusader gains the benefits of a _lay on hands_ with the
chosen _mercy_ without spending an action. This healing can prevent her from falling unconscious or
dying. If the effect doesn't trigger, it expires the next time the tortured crusader recovers uses
of _lay on hands._

**Alters:** Lay on hands, mercy

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Last Stand (Su)**

**Ability:** A tortured crusader can spend an additional use of _smite evil_ when declaring a smite
to make it a _last stand._ The duration of _last stand's_ benefits is only 1 minute (rather than 24
hours). However, the bonus damage from _smite evil_ is doubled on every attack (this doesn't stack
with the doubling if the smite's target is an evil outsider, undead, or evil dragon).

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Aura of justice
