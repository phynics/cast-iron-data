The first legates in the Inner Sea region were ambassadors from Holomog, a celestial-worshiping
confederation in southern Garund. Though they hold to the same strict paladin code, legates present
themselves not as armed crusaders, but as peaceful envoys and advisors, bringing wisdom garnered
from the long, stable history of their nation. They often serve as diplomats (and sometimes spies)
for good organizations. Yet woe to those who believe them defenseless, for a legate can call upon
her divine patron to provide her with armor and weapons.

**Celestial Armor (Sp)**

**Info:** If the legate has a steed bond and is riding her steed, she can include her steed when
summoning celestial armor, granting the steed barding with the same bonuses and special abilities
(see below) as her own.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Repeat:** The armor gains an additional +1 enhancement bonus., 6, 3

**Replaces:** Mercy

**Special:** These bonuses can be used to add any of the following armor special abilities:
[champion](ref/magic-enh/champion), [fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification) (heavy, light, or
medium), [invulnerability](ref/magic-enh/invulnerability), or
[spell resistance](ref/magic-enh/spell-resistance) (13, 15, 17, or 19).

In addition, the legate can grant her armor the [energy resistance](ref/magic-enh/energy-resistance)
special ability at the cost of a +3 bonus, the
[improved energy resistance](ref/magic-enh/energy-resistance-improved) special ability at the cost
of a +5 bonus, or the [righteous](ref/magic-enh/righteous) special ability at a cost of a +4 bonus.

The armor must have least a +1 enhancement bonus before any special abilities can be added. The
bonus and special abilities granted are determined when this ability is used and cannot be changed
until this ability is used again.

**Standard:** A legate can conjure armor. This ability acts as the spell
[instant armor](ref/spell/instant-armor), using her paladin level as her caster level, except as
follows.

**Usage:** A number of times per day equal to her Wisdom bonus plus
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her paladin level.
