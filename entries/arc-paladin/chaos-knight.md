**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 18\
Despite the entropic forces inherent in ganzis, some forgo a life of chaos, instead harnessing their
heritage to enforce the power of law. The chaos knight is able to tap into the nature of the
Maelstrom in order to grant or gain small, unpredictable blessings to aid on her quest for order.
The following archetype is available to ganzi characters.

**Prerequisites:** Ganzi

**Entropic Touch (Su)**

**Flavor:** A chaos knight can employ the forces of entropy with a touch to shatter a mind-affecting
spell or effect currently affecting an ally.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Lay on hands

**Special:** If the chaos knight targets himself, he can use this ability as a **swift action**.

**Standard:** He can touch a creature to grant that creature a new saving throw against one
mind-affecting effect, with a bonus on the save equal to
[one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) the chaos knight's level (minimum +0).

**Usage:** A number of times per day equal to half his paladin level + his Charisma modifier

**Usage:** A number of times per day equal to half his paladin level + his Charisma modifier

**Blessings of the Maelstrom (Su)**

**Action:** Ability

**Flavor:** A chaos knight can harness the unpredictable essence of the Maelstrom to bless a
creature.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Mercy

At 5th level: He can now maintain the blessing on 2 creatures at the same time.

At 8th level: He can now maintain the blessing on 3 creatures at the same time.

At 11th level: He can now maintain the blessing on 4 creatures at the same time.

At 14th level: He can now maintain the blessing on 5 creatures at the same time.

At 17th level: He can now maintain the blessing on 6 creatures at the same time.

At 20th level: He can now maintain the blessing on 7 creatures at the same time.

When he uses his _entropic touch_ ability, the chaos knight can grant the touched creature a
blessing from the table below that lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1 + the chaos knight's
Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round). The creature rolls 1d4 and compares the result to the table
below to determine the blessing. If the chaos knight targets himself with the blessing, he can roll
twice and choose his preferred result.

|D4|Blessing|
| --- | --- |
|1|The creature gains a sacred bonus to AC equal to 1/5 the paladin's level (minimum +1).|
|2|The creature gains a sacred bonus on weapon damage rolls equal to the chaos knight's Charisma modifier.|
|3|The creature gains [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2. This fast healing increases by 1 for every 3 levels the chaos knight has.|
|4|The creature gains an enhancement bonus to its movement speeds, adding 5 feet of movement for every 2 levels the chaos knight has (to a maximum of +40 feet).|

Only one creature can have this blessing at a time. If another creature gains the blessing, the
previous blessing ends.

**Aura of Chaos (Su)**

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Passive:** A chaos knight treats his weapons as chaos-aligned for the purposes of bypassing
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). In addition, the chaos knight treats attacks
targeting enemies within 10 feet of him as chaos-aligned for the purpose of overcoming damage
reduction.

**Replaces:** Aura of faith
