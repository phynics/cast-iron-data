**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 62\
Most paladins rush into battle, meeting evil toe-to-toe. The divine hunter prefers to engage evil
from afar, striking down her foes before they can threaten her allies.

**Precise Shot**

**At 1st Level:** A divine hunter gains [Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot) as a bonus feat, even
if she doesn't meet the prerequisites.

**Replaces:** Heavy Armor Proficiency

**Shared Precision (Su)**

**Ability:** When a divine hunter hits a creature with a ranged attack, she grants her allies within
10 feet of her the benefit of the Precise Shot feat against that target until the start of her next
turn. Her allies must remain within 10 feet of her, and must be able both to see and hear the divine
hunter to gain this benefit.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Aura of courage

**Divine Bond (Su)**

**Ability:** A divine hunter forms a bond with her deity. This functions as the paladin's _divine
bond_ ability, except the bond must always take the form of a ranged or throwing weapon (excluding
ammunition). In addition to the listed abilities, a divine hunter can add the
[distance](ref/magic-enh/distance), [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning), or
[seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking) special abilities to her weapon, but she cannot add the _defending_
or _disruption_ special abilities.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Divine bond

**Special:** Special abilities added to throwing weapons function normally when the weapon is used
in melee.

**Distant Mercy (Sp)**

**Ability:** A divine hunter can expend two uses of her _lay on hands_ abilities to use her _lay on
hands_ ability on a target within 5 feet per paladin level. _Distant mercy_ has no effect on
creatures harmed by positive energy, such as undead.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** 6th-level mercy

**Aura of Care (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** A divine hunter and her allies gain a preternatural awareness of each other's position
in battle. She and any allies within 10 feet of her no longer provide [cover](ref/rule/cover)
against each other's ranged attacks unless they wish to. Allies who cannot move or otherwise take
actions still provide cover, as do [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) allies. The divine hunter and
her allies must be able to see or hear each other to use this ability.

**Replaces:** Aura of resolve

**Hunter's Blessing (Su)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Aura of justice

**Special:** Evil creatures gain no benefit from this ability.

**Swift:** A divine hunter can expend a use of her _smite evil_ ability to grant herself and all
allies within 10 feet the [Deadly Aim](ref/feat/deadly-aim), Precise Shot, and
[Improved Precise Shot](ref/feat/improved-precise-shot) feats, even if they lack the prerequisites.
The effects last for 1 minute.

**Righteous Hunter (Su)**

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Passive:** A divine hunter's ranged weapons are treated as good-aligned for the purposes of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). Any ranged attacks made by an ally within
10 feet of her are likewise treated as good-aligned for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

**Replaces:** Aura of righteousness

**Special:** This ability functions only while the divine hunter is conscious.
