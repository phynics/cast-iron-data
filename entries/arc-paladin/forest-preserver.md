Forest preservers are sacred defenders of the woodlands and of the wild creatures and natural beauty
within them.

**Gained class skills:** climb, kna, sur

**Lost class skills:** diplo, kno, sm

**Favored Terrain (Ex)**

**Ability:** A forest preserver gains the ranger's [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain)
ability but must select either forest or jungle as her favored terrain.

**Info:** Text from the ability: _The ranger gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Knowledge
(geography), Perception, Stealth, and Survival skill checks when he is in this terrain. A ranger
traveling through his favored terrain normally leaves no trail and cannot be tracked (though he may
leave a trail if he so chooses)._

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Aura of courage

**Special:** She does not gain additional favored terrains.

At 8th level: The bonuses granted by this ability +4.

At 13th level: The bonuses granted by this ability +6.

At 18th level: The bonuses granted by this ability +8.

**Woodland Stride (Ex)**

**Ability:** A forest preserver (as well as her _divine bond_ creature, if she has one) gains
[woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride), as per the druid class feature.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Divine health

**Sacred Botany (Ex)**

**Info:** A forest preserver adds the following spells to her spell list at the listed spell level.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Channel positive energy

**Spells:** Detect animals or plants, entangle, goodberry, shillelagh

**Spells:** Barkskin, eagle eye, tree shape, warp wood, wood shape

**Spells:** Diminish plants, plant growth, snare, speak with plants, spike growth

**Spells:** Antiplant shell, awaken\|(trees only), blight, command plants, commune with nature,
thorn body, tree stride, wall of thorns

**Special:** In addition, she treats her caster level as 1 higher when casting these spells in her
favored terrain.

**Fireproof Aura (Su)**

**Immediate:** Whenever a fire spell would affect a creature within the _fireproof aura,_ the forest
preserver can expend three uses of her _lay on hands_ ability to attempt to counter the effect, as
if she had readied a [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) spell (caster level = her paladin level)
to counterspell.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** A forest preserver and creatures within 10 feet of her gain a +2 bonus on saving throws
against fire effects and gain fire resistance 5; these are doubled for plant allies, which also gain
[evasion](ref/ability/evasion) against fire effects only.

**Replaces:** Aura of resolve

**Special:** This ability functions only if the forest preserver is conscious.

**Sacred Grove (Su)**

**Ability:** A forest preserver can expend two uses of her _smite evil_ ability to consecrate a
20-foot-radius area that must contain a living tree. This fills the area with dense undergrowth,
though it does not impede her allies' movement. In addition, animals, fey, and plants allied with
the forest preserver gain a +1 sacred bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws, and
animals, fey, and plants called or summoned within this area gain 1 hit point per Hit Die as long as
the calling or summoning effect lasts or until the _sacred grove_ effect ends (whichever comes
first). If the area contains a shrine or hallowed area dedicated to a good-aligned deity (or at the
GM's discretion, a place of natural magical power, such as a natural portal to the First World),
these bonuses are doubled.

**Ability2:** When she uses this ability in her favored terrain, she can also imbue plants in the
area with limited animation, allowing them to obscure vision and impede movement and attacks as if
the area were affected by [solid fog](ref/spell/solid-fog).

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Aura of justice

**Special:** A _sacred grove_ remains enhanced in this way for a number of minutes equal to the
paladin's level.

**Aura of Preservation (Su)**

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Passive:** A forest preserver grants animals and plants within 10 feet
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 11 + her paladin level. This spell resistance
doesn't apply against harmless effects created by the forest preserver or allies within 10 feet of
her. This ability functions only if the forest preserver is conscious.

**Replaces:** Aura of faith
