**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 29\
**Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 45 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This heavy stone box measures 2 feet to a side, and the tiny compartment inside is lined with lead.
Inside the compartment is a half-ounce of deep green paste. This paste is made of animal fat mixed
with groundup [blightburn](ref/eq-material/blightburn). Once the box is opened, blightburn paste
limits teleportation within 60 feet and irradiates anyone within 60 feet, afflicting them with
[blightburn sickness](ref/disease/blightburn-sickness). Any creature that touches the paste is
afflicted with blightburn sickness (no save), and takes 2d6 points of fire damage per round until
the paste is cleaned away with a successful DC 15 Heal check. Crafting this item requires a
successful DC 30 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Blightburn

The mineral blightburn is the deadliest radioactive substance in the Darklands. The green-glowing
crystal burns anyone who touches it, dealing 2d6 points of fire damage per round of contact, and its
radiation causes blightburn sickness. The substance also limits teleportation. Anyone casting a
teleportation spell in or teleporting to a cavern with blightburn in its walls must succeed at a DC
30 caster level check.
