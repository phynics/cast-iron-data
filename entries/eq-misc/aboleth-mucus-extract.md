**Sources:** Pathfinder \#123: The Flooded Cathedral pg 30\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This jar contains 6 ounces of alchemically preserved [aboleth](ref/monster/alghollthu-aboleth)
mucus. When 1 ounce of the extract is applied to a living creature's mouth, nose, and throat (a
full-round action), the target loses its ability to breathe air but gains the ability to breathe
water for 1 hour. An unwilling target can attempt a DC 14 Fortitude save to negate the effect. If
additional doses are applied to the same creature, the duration of the effect extends by 1 hour for
each ounce used, to a maximum of 3 hours.
