**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Tools

This foot-long tube has marks for the freezing temperature of seawater, a moderate spring day in
Absalom, and the boiling temperature of melted ice. Temperatures much below the freezing point or
above the boiling point break the device.
