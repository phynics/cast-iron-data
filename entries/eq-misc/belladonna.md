**Price** 2 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Black Market

Also known as deadly nightshade, this plant, with its distinctive greenish-purple leaves and dull
black berries, has served as a cosmetic and medicine for longer than memory despite its
[toxicity](ref/eq-poison/belladonna). It can also induce vivid but usually unpleasant
hallucinations, particularly ones dealing with flight. Herbalists and others trained to use it
safely believe it has numerous applications, including pain relief.
