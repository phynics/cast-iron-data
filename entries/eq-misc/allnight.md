**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Black Market

This treated wafer dissolves into a chalky paste when placed under the tongue and then gives the
imbiber a jolt of restless energy. It eliminates the effects of [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued) for the
next 8 hours; when the drug's effect ends, the user is [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted). Allnight
makes its users jittery and unable to focus; they suffer a -2 penalty on all skill checks until its
effects wear off.
