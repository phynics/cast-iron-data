**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 5\
**Price** 1000 gp; **Weight** 44 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

The lore seeker has the equipment necessary to delve into ancient ruins searching for lost
knowledge. As this equipment package contains little by way of armor or weapons, it is most
appropriate for alchemists, bards, monks, sorcerers, and wizards. If the PC can cast spells, add a
spell component pouch and a holy symbol (if required).

**Weapons:** [Light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) with 10 bolts,
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), [silver](ref/eq-material/alchemical-silver)
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger).

**Combat Gear:** [Alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire) (3), antitoxin, oil of
[erase](ref/spell/erase), potions of [cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds) (2), potion of
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil),
[tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag), [thunderstone](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone).

**Other Gear:** [Backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), [bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll),
[belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle) (5), [chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk)
(5 pieces), [compass](ref/eq-misc/compass), [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar),
[everburning torch](ref/eq-misc/everburning-torch), [flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel),
[grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook), [ink](ref/eq-misc/ink), [inkpens](ref/eq-misc/inkpen)
(2), [journals](ref/eq-misc/journal) (2), [magnifying glass](ref/eq-misc/magnifying-glass),
[mapmaker's kit](ref/eq-misc/mapmakers-kit), [mess kit](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit),
[sack](ref/eq-misc/sack), [signal whistle](ref/eq-misc/whistle-signal),
[silk rope (50 ft.)](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft), [trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) (5
days), [traveler's any-tool](ref/magic-wondrous/travelers-any-tool),
[waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin), 8 gp.

**Total Weight:** 44 lbs. (27-3/4 lbs. for a Small character).
