## Inn stay

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 99, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159\
**Price** 5 sp (common), 2 gp (good), 2 sp (poor), 16 gp (suite, average), 32 gp+ (suite,
luxurious), 4 gp (suite, small); **Weight** - (common), - (good), - (poor), - (suite, average), -
(suite, luxurious), - (suite, small)

The listed price is for a single night's stay at an inn. Poor accommodations at an inn amount to a
place on the floor near the hearth. Common accommodations consist of a place on a raised, heated
floor and the use of a blanket and a pillow. Good accommodations consist of a small, private room
with one bed, some amenities, and a covered chamber pot in the corner.

**Inn Suite:** Only available in larger and more expensive inns, a suite is a rented bedroom and an
attached private room. A small suite includes a fireplace and [good lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-good) on
the outer door. An average suite includes a fireplace, a [superior lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-superior)
on the door, and either two bedrooms or one larger bedroom. A luxurious suite includes a fireplace,
a superior lock on the outer door, and two large or three or four smaller bedrooms, plus servants on
call to clean, cook, help with dressing, or send messages.
