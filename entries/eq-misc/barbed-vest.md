**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56, Advanced Player's Guide pg 181\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Thin leather flaps keep the hundreds of tiny, fishhook-like needles dotting the surface of this
black vest from harming you while you wear it. However, any creature that injures you with a natural
or unarmed attack must make a DC 15 Reflex save or take 1 point of damage. If a creature swallows
you, it takes 1 point of damage each round until it either spits you up, you escape, or you die (at
which point the vest has sustained enough damage that it no longer serves as a threat). The vest can
only be worn over light armor or no armor.
