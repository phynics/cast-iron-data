**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 103, Advanced Player's Guide pg 184\
**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This white, dry powder mixes with water to form a paste, which hardens over the course of an hour to
create a solid material. It can be used to make casts of footprints or carvings, fill in gaps or
cracks in walls, or (if applied over a cloth wrapping) create a cast for a broken bone. Hardened
plaster has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 1 and 5 hit points per inch of thickness. A 5-pound clay
pot of plaster can cover about 5 square feet of flat space to a depth of 1 inch, five Medium forearm
or lower-leg casts, two Medium full-arm or full-leg casts, or twice as many casts for a Small
creature.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
