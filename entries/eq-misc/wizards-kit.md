**Price** 21 gp; **Weight** 21 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit includes a [backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), a [bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll), a
[belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), a [flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel),
[ink](ref/eq-misc/ink), an [inkpen](ref/eq-misc/inkpen), an [iron pot](ref/eq-misc/pot-iron), a
[mess kit](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit), [soap](ref/eq-misc/soap), a
[spell component pouch](ref/eq-misc/spell-component-pouch), [torches](ref/eq-misc/torch) (10),
[trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) (5 days), and a [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin). The
kit does not contain a [spellbook](ref/eq-misc/spellbook) because a wizard begins play with a
spellbook and does not need to purchase one.
