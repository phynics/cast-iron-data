**Price** 24 gp; **Weight** 1,000 lbs.\
**Category** Mounts/Pets

These shaggy-haired relatives to cows are more at home in mountainous terrain where they are
frequently used as pack animals and to pull plows. Yaks have the same statistics as bison.
