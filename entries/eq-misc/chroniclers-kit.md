**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 47\
**Price** 40 gp; **Weight** 4-1/2 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This bundle contains a map case, two vials of [ink](ref/eq-misc/ink), two
[inkpens](ref/eq-misc/inkpen), 10 sheets of [paper](ref/eq-misc/paper-sheet), two blank
[journals](ref/eq-misc/journal), a pound of fine [powder](ref/eq-misc/powder) for drying ink, and a
20-foot [measuring cord](ref/eq-misc/measuring-cord-10-ft). The supplies usually suffice for
chronicling a single expedition of not more than 2 months' duration.
