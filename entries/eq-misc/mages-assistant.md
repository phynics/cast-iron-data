**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 22\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This navy blue liquid is riddled with colorful flecks of minerals. For 1 hour after drinking this
tincture, you gain a +5 alchemical bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks. The
increased focus imposes a -2 penalty on Perception and Sense Motive checks for the same duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
