**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

A formula book has 100 pages of parchment, and each alchemical extract formula takes up one page per
extract level. A newly purchased formula book contains no extract formulas. An alchemist character
begins play with a formula book containing the extract formulae he knows. This is the alchemist
equivalent of a spellbook for wizards (a [spellbook](ref/eq-misc/spellbook) and a formula book are
essentially the same item).
