**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 236\
**Price** 190 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

By drinking this clear, fizzy liquid, you gain 1d4 uses of
[alchemical inspiration](ref/misc/alchemical-inspiration) that can be used only on Sense Motive
checks. The benefits of intuition serum last for 1 hour; during this time, you must roll all Bluff
checks twice and take the worse result.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
