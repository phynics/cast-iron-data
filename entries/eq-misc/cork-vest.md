**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 203\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Clothing

This fabric vest contains pockets full of cork, providing the wearer additional buoyancy. Originally
worn by fishers and sailors, it protects against drowning. While wearing a cork vest, you take a -2
penalty on Dexterity and Swim checks, but instead of going underwater after failing the check by 5
or more, you go underwater only if you fail by 10 or more. Additionally, you gain a +4 bonus on Swim
checks to avoid damage from fatigue. The cork vest can be worn under armor.
