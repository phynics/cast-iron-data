**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This soothing elixir was created to counter the energy-draining effects of vampires, wights, and
similar horrible creatures. If you have a negative level (whether temporary or permanent), you can
drink a dose of soul stimulant, negating the negative level's penalty for 12 hours. You can only
benefit from 1 dose of soul stimulant at a time, though you can continue to take a dose every 12
hours to stave off the negative level's effects.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
