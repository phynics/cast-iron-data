**Price** 12 gp; **Weight** 22 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit consists of a [backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), a [bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll), a
[belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), a clay [mug](ref/eq-misc/mug-tankard), a
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger), two [fishhooks](ref/eq-misc/fishhook), a
[flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel), a [sewing needle](ref/eq-misc/sewing-needle), a
[signal whistle](ref/eq-misc/whistle-signal), 50 feet of
[string](ref/eq-misc/string-or-twine-50-ft), 50 feet of thread, a week's worth of
[trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations), a [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin), and a
[whetstone](ref/eq-misc/whetstone). For Small creatures, the weight of a Pathfinder's kit is 7-1/2
pounds. Leaving the bedroll and rations at camp or on a mount reduces the weight by 12 pounds for
Medium creatures and by 3 pounds for Small creatures.
