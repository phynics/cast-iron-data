**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 7\
**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This bone-white paste makes your face appear skeletal and emotionless, like a visage of death
itself. When you apply frightful war paint to yourself (or to a willing adjacent creature) as a
full-round action, attempt a Disguise or Charisma check. Living creatures within 30 feet of the
wearer must succeed at a Will save (DC equal to the result of your Disguise or Charisma check,
maximum DC 20) or become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d4 rounds. Blind creatures and creatures out
of line of sight are unaffected. A creature that succeeds at its check becomes immune to this
frightful war paint for 24 hours. Frightful war paint can be worn indefinitely, but it contains
quicksilver and realgar, which have deleterious mental effects. If you wear frightful war paint for
more than 1 minute per day (whether as a single use of it or as multiple uses over several periods
of time), you take 1 point of [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), plus 1 point of
Wisdom damage for every minute you wear the paint that day after the first.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (40 dew of lunary + 40 quicksilver +
50 realgar)/earth; **Craft DC** 30\
**Time** 10 minutes; **Tools** -; **Type** alchemical tool
