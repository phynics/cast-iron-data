## Gecko

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 81, Advanced Race Guide pg 105\
**Price** 300 gp (riding), 400 gp (riding, combat trained); **Weight** 1,200 lbs. (riding), 1,500
lbs. (riding, combat trained)

Larger than even giant geckos, these mammoth lizards have been specifically bred to be used as
mounts. Prized for their ability to run along cave ceilings and sheer walls, they require
[exotic saddles](ref/eq-misc/saddle-exotic-riding) to ride. A riding gecko has the same statistics
as a giant gecko with the [giant template](ref/template/giant).
