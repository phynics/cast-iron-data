**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When applied to the nose as a standard action, this alchemical paste traps airborne particles and
sharpens your olfactory receptors, heightening your sense of smell. You gain the
[scent](ref/umr/scent) ability, except you can detect creatures and odors at only half the distance.
If you already have the scent ability, rhinarium paste increases the range at which you can detect
creatures by 50%. Rhinarium paste's effects lasts for 1 hour. Crafting this item requires a
successful DC 20 Craft (alchemy) check.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
