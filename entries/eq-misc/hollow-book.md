**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 233\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Under regular scrutiny, a hollow book appears as a normal tome. However, it is designed to hold a
concealed item within its pages. One type of hollow book simply has a cavity cut into the interior
pages that can hold a small pouch or similar item. Flipping the cover of this type of book open
instantly reveals its true nature. Another type has a much thinner cavity cut into its unusually
thick cover. The cavity is concealed by the book's end-paper, which is affixed to the inside of the
cover by a gentle adhesive and can be peeled back and then reclosed repeatedly. A small packet of
folded documents can be carefully hidden inside the cavity, and a successful DC 20 Perception check
is required to notice the hidden space.
