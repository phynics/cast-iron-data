**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This liquid makes the eyes more receptive to light for 4 hours. The user gains
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), but also suffers from
[light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity) for the duration. Crafting duskeye requires a
successful DC 16 Craft (alchemy) check.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 16
