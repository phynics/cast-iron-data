**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 7\
**Price** 751 gp; **Weight** 17 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is designed for melee-focused warriors who hope to kill or capture a dragon. It includes 50
feet of [chain](ref/eq-misc/chain-10-ft), a [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar), an
[everburning torch](ref/eq-misc/everburning-torch), an application of
[fire](ref/eq-misc/fire-ward-gel) (or [frost](ref/eq-misc/frost-ward-gel)) ward gel,
[fire-resistant boots](ref/eq-misc/fire-resistant-boots), a potion of
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), three [sacks](ref/eq-misc/sack), a
[shield sconce](ref/eq-misc/shield-sconce), a [simple lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-simple), and a
[weapon cord](ref/eq-misc/weapon-cord).
