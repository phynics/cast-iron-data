**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 111\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This lantern does not burn oil, but instead burns [shadowcloy](ref/eq-weapon/shadowcloy-flask). When
shadowcloy is used as its fuel, this lantern creates a strange, hazy darkness that decreases the
light level for 30 feet around it by one step. Unlike when shadowcloy is thrown at a single target,
this haze does not decrease natural darkness to supernatural darkness. One flask of shadowcloy fuels
a darklight lantern for 1 minute.
