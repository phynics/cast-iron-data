**Price** 174 gp; **Weight** 32 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit holds equipment for exploring underground, consisting of a
[lantern](ref/eq-misc/hooded-lantern) with 4 flasks of [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil), a
[hammer](ref/eq-misc/hammer) and 8 [pitons](ref/eq-misc/piton), 100 feet of
[silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft), a [grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook), and a
[climber's kit](ref/eq-misc/climbers-kit).
