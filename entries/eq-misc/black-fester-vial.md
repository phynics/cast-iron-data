**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 56\
**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This black paste is often applied on orc weapons before going on raids to stymie an enemy's healing
magic. Like a poison, black fester stays on a weapon until the first time it strikes an opponent. It
remains in the target's body for 1 hour. A creature exposed to black fester resists magical healing;
a creature trying to magically heal the target must make a DC 10 caster level check to restore any
hit points to the target.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
