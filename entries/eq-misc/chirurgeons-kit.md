**Price** 400 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This sturdy leather case straps to the waist or thigh, or can be slung over a shoulder. It consists
of a [leather pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), a [healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit), a bottle of
strong [brandy](ref/eq-misc/whiskey-cup), a potion of
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), a vial of
[smelling salts](ref/eq-misc/smelling-salts), and 2 doses each of
[alchemist's kindness](ref/eq-misc/alchemists-kindness), [antiplague](ref/eq-misc/antiplague),
[antitoxin](ref/eq-misc/antitoxin), [bloodblock](ref/eq-misc/bloodblock), and
[soothe syrup](ref/eq-misc/soothe-syrup).
