**Price** 1000 gp; **Weight** 67 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This equipment package is designed to provide the most aid to divine casters, such as clerics,
druids, oracles, and inquisitors, who prefer to help their companions from behind the front lines of
a fight.

**Armor:** [Leather armor](ref/eq-armor/leather),
[light wooden shield](ref/eq-armor/light-wooden-shield).

**Weapons:** [Shortspear](ref/eq-weapon/shortspear), [sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling) with 10
[bullets](ref/eq-weapon/sling-bullets).

**Combat Gear:** [Antitoxin](ref/eq-misc/antitoxin), bead of blessing (as a
[lesser strand of prayer beads](ref/magic-wondrous/strand-of-prayer-beads-lesser) without the bead
of healing), [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water) (2), scrolls of
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds) (2), scroll of
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements).

**Other Gear:** [Backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), [bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll),
[belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle) (5), [chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk)
(5 pieces), [flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel), [healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit),
[holy symbol (silver)](ref/eq-misc/holy-symbol-silver), [ioun torch](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-torch),
[mess kit](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit), [mirror](ref/eq-misc/mirror), [sack](ref/eq-misc/sack),
[shovel](ref/eq-misc/shovel-common), [silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft) (50 ft.),
[smelling salts](ref/eq-misc/smelling-salts), [soap](ref/eq-misc/soap),
[spell component pouch](ref/eq-misc/spell-component-pouch),
[trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) (5 days), [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin), 4 sp.

**Total Weight:** 67 lbs. (39-1/4 lbs. for a Small character).
