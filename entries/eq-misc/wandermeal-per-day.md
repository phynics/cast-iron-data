**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 95, Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 1 cp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Food/Drink

This tough, dried cake is a halfling invention made from flour, water, and spices. Wandermeal keeps
for months without spoiling, travels well, and fills the belly. However, eating it for over a week
without other nutrients requires the eater to make a daily Fortitude saving throw (DC 15 + 1 for
each additional day) or be [sickened](ref/misc/sickened). The effect ends 1 day after more
nutritious food is eaten. The listed price is for a day's worth of food.
