**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 208\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When inhaled, this finely ground, alchemically treated [tobacco](ref/eq-misc/tobacco) greatly
enhances your senses, especially your sense of smell. It grants the scent ability and a +2
alchemical bonus on Perception checks for 1 hour. However, once the effect wears off your body is
wracked with terrible pain as your joints begin to stiffen and seize, dealing 1d2 points of
[Dexterity damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to you.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
