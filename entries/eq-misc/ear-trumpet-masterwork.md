## Ear trumpet

**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 20\
**Price** 25 gp (common), 100 gp (masterwork); **Weight** 2 lbs. (common), 2 lbs. (masterwork)

This foot-long brass funnel amplifies sounds entering through the broad end for anyone listening at
the narrow end. The listener gains a +2 circumstance bonus on sound-based Perception checks but must
use one hand to hold the trumpet to the ear in order to gain the benefit. A masterwork ear trumpet's
exquisite acoustic properties grant the listener a +4 circumstance bonus instead.

## Ear trumpet

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

By holding this specially crafted ear trumpet to your ear and placing the other end against a door
or wall, you reduce the DC penalty for listening through a door or wall by 5.
