**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This is a clear, viscous liquid that is typically kept in a frosted glass vial with an unassuming
label and cork. The oil is used to reduce the sound armor and weapons make while moving. Applying
this oil to a single piece of equipment takes 1 minute and grants the wearer a +2 circumstance bonus
on Stealth checks for 30 minutes. A vial of this oil is enough to coat one Medium piece of
equipment, while a Large piece of equipment requires 4 vials. Two pieces of Small equipment can be
covered with a single vial of oil. Exposure to or submersion in large amounts of water, such as
swimming, immediately ends the effect of the oil.
