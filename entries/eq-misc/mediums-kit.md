**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 249\
**Price** 40 gp; **Weight** 14 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit includes tools that aid in contacting supernatural beings. It includes an
[automatic writing planchette](ref/eq-misc/automatic-writing-planchette),
[candles](ref/eq-misc/candle) (10), [candlesticks](ref/eq-misc/candlestick) (10),
[holly and mistletoe](ref/eq-misc/holly-and-mistletoe), [incense](ref/eq-misc/incense) (10 sticks),
a [scholar's outfit](ref/eq-misc/scholars-outfit), a
[spell component pouch](ref/eq-misc/spell-component-pouch), and a
[wooden holy symbol](ref/eq-misc/wooden-holy-symbol).
