**Sources:** Dungeon Denizens Revisited pg 19\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This alchemical concoction can be spread over a Medium or Small creature's body as a full-round
action. It provides a +5 alchemical bonus on Escape Artist checks, grapple checks to avoid a
grapple, and Reflex saves to avoid an ooze's improved [grab](ref/umr/grab) or
[engulf](ref/umr/engulf) abilities, but gives a -5 penalty to disarm checks, grapple checks to start
or maintain a grapple or pin, and other checks that may be hindered by a loose grip (such as Climb).
One application lasts for up to 1 hour and can be removed with soap and water.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
