**Sources:** Undead Slayer's Handbook pg 11\
**Price** 265 gp; **Weight** 22 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit provides all the tools necessary to seal a coffin or other burial container and ensure that
it stays closed. It includes 20 feet of [chain](ref/eq-misc/chain-10-ft), a
[good lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-good), 1 dose of [alchemical cement](ref/eq-misc/alchemical-cement), a
[folding shovel](ref/eq-misc/shovel-folding), a [stubborn nail](ref/magic-wondrous/stubborn-nail),
and a leather-bound copy of the [Pharasmin](ref/faith/pharasma) holy text, The Bones Land in a
Spiral.
