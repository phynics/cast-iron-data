## Bounty hunter's kit

**Price** 105 gp (common), 380 gp (superior); **Weight** 15 lbs. (common), 24 lbs. (superior)

This kit provides useful tools for bounty hunters. It includes basic maps showing major landmarks,
[caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops), [common manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-common) with an
[average lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-average), a [net](ref/eq-weapon/net), a [sap](ref/eq-weapon/sap),
and a 10-foot [chain](ref/eq-misc/chain-10-ft). Bounty hunters are notoriously individualistic, so
variant kits are common. One such variant is the superior bounty hunter's kit, which replaces the
common manacles with [masterwork manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-masterwork) and the average lock
with a [good lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-good), and includes a
[footprint book](ref/eq-misc/footprint-book), a [tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag), and
[masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork).
