**Sources:** Heroes of the Darklands pg 8\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A filter mask is a rubber-sealed leather mask with a filtering respiration apparatus. Ordinary
filter masks have cloth filters laced with floral perfumes, while masterwork filter masks use
alchemical charcoal filters. When worn, a filter mask provides a +2 bonus on Fortitude saves against
inhaled poisons or diseases and other inhaled particles such as smoke or spores.
