**Price** 180 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

A creature with DR/silver can swallow these bitter-tasting capsules of a concentrated alchemical
silver to convert their [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) to DR/- for 2d4 rounds. When
this effect ends, the creature is [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round. A creature can only
benefit from silver drops once per 24 hours. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 30 Craft
(alchemy) check.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
