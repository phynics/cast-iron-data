**Sources:** Blood of the Sea pg 18\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When you release this vial of alchemical particles into the water as a standard action, the
particles immediately neutralize the scent of exposed blood within 20 feet, rendering the blood
undetectable by [scent](ref/umr/scent) (to a hunting shark, for example). Blood neutralizer has no
effect on any blood that enters the area after the particles have been used. Blood neutralizer can
be crafted with a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) skill check.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
