**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 233\
**Price** 12 gp; **Weight** 1/10 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A concealment coin appears in all respects like a typical coin of the realm and is often crafted
from an ordinary coin - though for soft metals like gold, the coin is typically made with an iron or
steel interior and then plated with the more valuable metal. Twisting the coin along its edge
unscrews the two halves, revealing a thin, hollow space inside. Such coins are often used to hide
small messages written on very fine paper for covert delivery. Noticing a concealment coin's true
nature requires a successful DC 25 Perception check.
