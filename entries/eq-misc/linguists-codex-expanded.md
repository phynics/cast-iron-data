## Linguist's codex

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 16\
**Price** 200 gp (expanded), 50 gp (standard); **Weight** 5 lbs. (expanded), 3 lbs. (standard)

This leather-bound reference tome contains several alphabets used in the various languages of
Golarion, along with extensively cross-referenced and indexed words, phrases, and idioms. As long as
you speak at least three languages, consulting a standard linguist's codex grants you a +2
circumstance bonus on Linguistics checks when communicating in a language you do not speak.

An expanded linguist's codex has far more phrases and guidance, including instructions to pronounce
the sounds in a correct and pleasing manner. These codices provide a +2 circumstance bonus on
Diplomacy checks when communicating in a language you do not speak.
