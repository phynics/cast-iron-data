**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs. (or 10 lbs.)\
**Category** Clothing

Outwardly, this suit of clothing appears to be an ordinary
[traveler's outfit](ref/eq-misc/travelers-outfit), albeit cut from thicker-than-normal cloth. The
suit's inner lining is made of dozens of thin, flexible sponges. When soaked with water, these
sponges can be worn against the skin to maintain the wearer's moisture while traveling out of the
water.

It takes 10 minutes to fully saturate the suit, which increases its weight from 2 pounds to 10
pounds.

When worn, a sponge suit doubles the amount of time a water-dependent creature can spend on dry land
before it begins to take damage or suffer other negative effects. When the sponges dry, wearers must
submerge themselves in water or resaturate the suit, or else they suffer the consequences of being
on dry land.
