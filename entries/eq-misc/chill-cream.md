**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 103\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This sticky light blue paste grows pleasantly cool to the touch whenever it is exposed to light.
Spreading a dose on your face and hands takes a full-round action and grants you a +2 alchemical
bonus on saves to resist [hot weather](ref/rule/heat-dangers). In areas of bright light this bonus
increases to +4, while in areas of [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darkness it is negated.
A dose of chill cream lasts for 1 hour after being applied. A newly crafted bottle of chill cream
holds 5 doses.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
