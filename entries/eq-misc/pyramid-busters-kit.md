**Price** 310 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit contains items helpful in navigating the winding corridors of pyramids, but is also useful
in other crypts and tombs. It contains three sticks of [chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk), five sheets of
[parchment](ref/eq-misc/parchment-sheet), 1 dose of [synthetic mumia](ref/eq-misc/synthetic-mumia),
a flask of [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), a
[tangleburn bag](ref/eq-weapon/tangleburn-bag), and a pair of [sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod).
