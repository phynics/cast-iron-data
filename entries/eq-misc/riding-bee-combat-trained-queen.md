## Riding bee

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 12\
**Price** 12,000 gp (combat-trained queen), 450 gp (combat-trained worker), 8,000 gp (queen), 300 gp
(worker); **Weight** 140 lbs. (combat-trained queen), 60 lbs. (combat-trained worker), 140 lbs.
(queen), 60 lbs. (worker)

[Gathlains](ref/race/gathlain) have learned to domesticate giant bees, which they use to travel long
distances. Extra queens from a hive's brood are saved and trained for hauling heavier equipment or a
retinue of gathlains. Bees require an exotic saddle, which is a sort of basket that places the rider
underneath the bee's abdomen, where it protects the rider as if he were a bee larva.
