::: statblock
Price: 70 gp
Weight: 1/10 lb.
Category: Alchemical Tools
:::

These alchemically treated [incense](ref/eq-misc/incense) sticks contain pheromones that certain
creatures find repellent. Each odor stalk is created to irritate a specific creature type. After
burning for 1 round, an odor stalk fills a 10-foot square with a clear gas that causes any living
creature of the prescribed type to become [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round, then
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for as long as it remains in the area. On a successful DC 12 Fortitude
save, the creature negates the nauseated condition but not the sickened condition. This is a poison
effect. The cloud lasts for 10 minutes, but can be dispersed like
[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist). Additionally, odor stalks can be used as an additional
spell component for the following spells to modify the spell's effects.

[Ghoul touch:](ref/spell/ghoul-touch) The stench only affects creatures of the odor stalk's
associated creature type.

[Repel vermin:](ref/spell/repel-vermin) The spell functions as written, but applies to the odor
stalk's associated creature type instead of to vermin. The Will save DC is always 12 when using this
component.

[Stinking cloud:](ref/spell/stinking-cloud) The Fortitude save DC increases by 1 for creatures of
the odor stalk's prescribed type, but decreases by 1 for all other creature types.
