**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 27\
**Price** 70 gp; **Weight** 1/10 lbs\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

These alchemically treated [incense](ref/eq-misc/incense) sticks contain pheromones that certain
creatures find repellent. Each odor stalk is created to irritate a specific creature type. After
burning for 1 round, an odor stalk fills a 10-foot square with a clear gas that causes any living
creature of the prescribed type to become [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round, then
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for as long as it remains in the area. On a successful DC 12 Fortitude
save, the creature negates the nauseated condition but not the sickened condition. This is a poison
effect. The cloud lasts for 10 minutes, but can be dispersed like
[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist).
