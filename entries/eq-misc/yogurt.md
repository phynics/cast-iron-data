**Price** 1 sp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Food/Drink

This thick, fermented milk has a tangier taste than unprocessed milk. It may be sweetened with
fruit, honey, or jam; blended with chopped herbs and oil to create a sauce; or mixed with water and
salt, sugar, fruit, or mint as a drink.
