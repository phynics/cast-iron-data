**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 26\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This glass vial contains alchemically preserved musk from a single variety of creature. Sniffing
bottled musk provides a +4 circumstance bonus on Survival checks to track creatures of the same
variety by [scent](ref/umr/scent). Additionally, bottled musk can be used as an additional spell
component for the following spells to modify the spell's effects.

> **Detect undead:** Functions as written, but detects creatures of the musk's associated creature
> type.
>
> **Locate creature:** Detects the same variety of creature as the musk (for example, using bison
> musk detects only bison, not all herd animals or animals), and provides a +2 bonus on caster level
> checks to overcome nondetection.
