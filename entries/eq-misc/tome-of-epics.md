**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 206\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

This hefty book is bound in oilskin and decorated with scenes of glorious combat between ancient
heroes and ferocious monsters. It contains several tales of valor, defeat, and victory, all with
brightly colored illustrations. After consulting the book for 1 hour, for the next 24 hours you gain
a +2 circumstance bonus on Perform (oratory) and Perform (sing) checks and a +2 circumstance bonus
on Knowledge (nobility) checks pertaining to heroic lineages.
