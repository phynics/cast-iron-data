**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This green-tinged brew has a pungent, bitter bite. It's flavored with a variety of foul-smelling
herbs and is intensely carbonated through a unique fermentation process. Sometimes given to visitors
as a prank, wyrm's breath bitter causes the drinker to become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1d6
minutes (Fortitude DC 12 negates); dwarves gain a +5 racial bonus on this saving throw. After
drinking a dose of wyrm's breath bitter, the drinker can, as a move action, unleash a thunderous and
noxious belch in either a 5-foot-radius [spread](ref/misc/spread) or a 10-foot cone. Creatures in
the area are [deafened](ref/misc/deafened) and sickened for 1 round (Fortitude DC 12 negates). The
drinker must wait 1d4 rounds before belching again, but can belch as often as desired for up to 10
minutes after drinking a wyrm's breath bitter.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (2 black powder + 10 brimstone + 20
spirit of wine)/digestion; **Craft DC** 21\
**Time** 1 week; **Tools** [brewer's kit](ref/eq-misc/brewers-kit); **Type** alcohol
