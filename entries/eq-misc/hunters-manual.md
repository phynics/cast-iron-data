**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 26\
**Price** 90 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

These books detail common behavior patterns and special abilities of a particular creature type.
When used as a reference (an action that typically requires 1d4 minutes of searching the text), a
hunter's manual grants a +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge checks to learn about special abilities
and weaknesses of the text's associated creature type for 1 hour.

If the reader successfully makes such a Knowledge check using the book, she gains a +1 circumstance
bonus on her next saving throw against any of that creature's spell-like, supernatural, or
extraordinary abilities she identified.
