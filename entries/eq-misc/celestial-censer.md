**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 87\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

This blessed thurible holds up to 10 pieces of [incense](ref/eq-misc/incense), and burns at a rate
of 1 stick per hour. If a [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick) is added to the incense in the
[censer](ref/eq-misc/censer) while it burns, creatures with the evil subtype are
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for as long as they remain within the area of the resulting smoke.
