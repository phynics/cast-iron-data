## Waffle iron

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 58\
**Price** 1 gp (common), 2,501 gp (mithral); **Weight** 5 lbs. (common), 2-1/2 lbs. (mithral)

This pair of hinged metal plates is decorated with studs to add texture to the pastry cakes you cook
with it. You pour batter on it, close it, and place it on a fire or stove to cook. Some waffle irons
have plates that imprint the waffle with a noble's coat of arms, a religious symbol, or a country's
flag. A mithral waffle iron almost always bears a specific insignia rather than the generic waffle
pattern. As with other mithral cookware, food rarely sticks to a mithral waffle iron.
