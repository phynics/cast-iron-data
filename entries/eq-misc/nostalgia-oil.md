**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 147\
**Price** 80 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

[Pathfinders](ref/rule/aff-scholars) pride themselves on their camaraderie, and among the worst
threats are those that can magically compel agents to fight among themselves. Nostalgia oil is a
vial of concentrated aromatics that evoke positive memories of the Grand Lodge and common trials
overcome during a Pathfinder's training. By dabbing this oil on her face, neck, or arms as a
standard action, a Pathfinder gains a +2 alchemical bonus on saving throws against effects with the
emotion descriptor and on secondary saving throws made to resist
[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person) and similar compulsion effects that would force her to
harm her allies or betray her mission. Applied in this way, a vial of nostalgia oil remains potent
for 8 hours or until washed off with soap and water. Nostalgia oil can also be
[thrown as a splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon), extending its benefits for 1 hour to any
Pathfinder struck with a direct hit. Alternatively, a creature can mix it with a pint of lantern
[oil](ref/eq-misc/oil) or piece of [incense](ref/eq-misc/incense), which grants half the nostalgia
oil's benefits to all Pathfinders within 20 feet of the lantern or censor for 1 hour; any strong
wind (21+ mph) dissipates the aroma for 1 minute.

Crafting a vial of nostalgia oil requires a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check. Although most
nostalgia oil is mixed to benefit only Pathfinder agents, other mixtures might be created to benefit
only members of another clan, neighborhood, or organization.
