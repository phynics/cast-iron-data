**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 206\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 500 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit for a [heavy wagon](ref/eq-misc/wagon-heavy) includes specialized boxes and pots for
growing a wide range of plants, in addition to space for a couple of animals, such as goats, and
their feed. A traveling garden provides food and healing herbs. It functions similar to a
[healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit), providing up to five uses per day, and is never exhausted.
In addition, those who ingest a daily variety of fresh herbs and vegetables from the garden gain a
+1 bonus on saving throws against disease.
