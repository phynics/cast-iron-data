**Price** 5 cp; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Category** Animal Gear

Consisting of various kinds of raw meat that have been jerked, smoked, or salted for preservation, a
day's worth of carnivore feed is adequate food for any Small or Medium meat-eating animal such as a
tiger, bear, or dog. Larger carnivores might require up to 2 to 4 days' worth of carnivore feed per
day.
