**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 37\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This 1-foot-long, silver-tipped tin rod glows dimly when struck as a standard action. It sheds dim
light in a 30-foot radius. However, the light given off by a moonrod is particularly easily to
perceive for creatures with [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision). For such creatures, a
moonrod increases the [light level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) by one step (to a maximum of normal)
for a 60-foot radius. It glows for 6 hours, after which the silver tip is burned out and worthless.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
