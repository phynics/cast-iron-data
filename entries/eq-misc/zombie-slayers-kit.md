**Sources:** Undead Slayer's Handbook pg 15\
**Price** 170 gp; **Weight** 31 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This large satchel contains the tools needed to put down potential zombie uprisings. The kit
includes a [shovel](ref/eq-misc/shovel-common), two flasks of [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil), a
[bullseye lantern](ref/eq-misc/bullseye-lantern), a flask of
[alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), two flasks of
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), 50 feet of hemp [rope](ref/eq-misc/rope-50-ft), a
[crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar), a [hammer](ref/eq-misc/hammer) and 10 nails, two vials of
[antiplague](ref/eq-misc/antiplague), and a [wooden holy symbol](ref/eq-misc/wooden-holy-symbol).
