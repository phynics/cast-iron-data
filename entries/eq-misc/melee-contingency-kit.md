**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 20\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** 14 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit contains items necessary for a melee combatant to overcome the most common forms of damage
resistance. The kit includes a [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron)
[morningstar](ref/eq-weapon/morningstar), a [silver](ref/eq-material/alchemical-silver)
[sickle](ref/eq-weapon/sickle), two flasks of [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid), two flasks of
[alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), and two flasks of
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water).
