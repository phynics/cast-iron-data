**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56\
**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This leather belt is worn over one shoulder and runs diagonally across the chest and back. It has
small loops or pouches for holding eight objects the size of a flask or small dagger. You can use
the "retrieve a stored item" action to take an item from a bandolier. You can wear up to two
bandoliers at the same time (any more than this and they get in each other's way and restrict your
movement).
