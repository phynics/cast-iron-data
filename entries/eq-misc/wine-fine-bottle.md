## Wine

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 95, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159\
**Price** 2 sp (common, pitcher), 10 gp (fine, bottle); **Weight** 6 lbs. (common, pitcher), 1-1/2
lbs. (fine, bottle)

Wine is made from fermented fruit juice, usually grapes, but also sometimes berries, apples, or even
rice (sake). The lower listed price is for unremarkable common wine and the higher is for
significantly finer wine, though wine from certain vintners (and specific years) may fetch much
higher prices. In colder climates, wine is often mulled with fruit, spices, honey, and almonds and
served as a warming beverage during the winter.
