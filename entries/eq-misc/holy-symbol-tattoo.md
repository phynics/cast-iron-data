**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 57\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

In some religions, you are allowed to tattoo or brand your deity's holy symbol onto your skin
(typically the palm, back of the hand, or forearm) in a special ceremony. Thereafter, it functions
like an actual holy symbol of your faith, and you may use it as a divine focus for spellcasting,
channeling energy, and so on. The tattoo must be fully visible to use it in this way (it cannot be
covered with a glove, gauntlet, or any other material). The listed price includes the cost of a
simple brand or non-fading black tattoo; add appropriate tattoo costs if you want something more
elaborate. Specifically damaging or erasing the tattoo or brand negates its use as an actual holy
symbol.
