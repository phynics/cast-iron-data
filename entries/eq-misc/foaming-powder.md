**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 103\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When you add this 1-pound bag of green powder to a gallon of water, the two combine to form 50 cubic
feet of thick green foam. Unless contained, in 1 round the foam fills a 5-foot-square to a depth of
2 feet. When multiple pounds of powder are used, the foam expands at a rate of one 5-foot square per
round. Ground covered with the foam is treated as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles), but is otherwise harmless. After 1 hour, the
foam hardens to form a buoyant material roughly the density of honeycomb. The cured foam is easy to
cut, with [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 0 and 5 hit points per foot of thickness. The cured foam
breaks down over a few days, and even faster if exposed to water. Casting
[transmute mud to rock](ref/spell/transmute-mud-to-rock) on uncured foam converts it into a soft,
pumice-like stone (hardness 2, 5 hit points per inch of thickness).

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
