**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 77\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

This kit is used by midwives to care for pregnant women and to deliver babies. Each kit contains
herbs to treat common conditions during pregnancy, salves for aches and distended skin, swaddling
cloths, a small but sharp knife for cutting an umbilical cord, powder for cleaning the newborn,
bandages, a needle, and thread. Using the kit gives you a +4 circumstance bonus on Heal checks when
dealing with humanoid pregnancy or birth. You may use it for other kinds pregnant or birthing
creatures (such as animals or monstrous humanoids), but it only gives a +2 bonus on such Heal
checks. A midwife's kit is exhausted after 10 uses.
