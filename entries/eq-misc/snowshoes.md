**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 89, Pathfinder \#51: The Hungry Storm pg 67, Adventurer's Armory
pg 19, Advanced Player's Guide pg 185\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Clothing

These high-tension nets of rope or sinew in wooden frames are lashed to the feet to spread your
weight across the snow, making you much less likely to break through the crust and rendering walking
much easier. Snowshoes reduce the penalty for walking through heavy snow by 50%; for example, if
moving through snow normally costs you 2 squares of movement (1 square plus a 1 square penalty) per
square traveled, snowshoes reduce this cost to 1.5 squares per square traveled. Snowshoes may
supplement another outfit.
