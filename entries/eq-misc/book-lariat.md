**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 202\
**Price** 3 sp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This braided metal cord comes with a clasp that affixes to the lock of a standard spellbook. The
other end of the cord attaches to a belt or belt loop. The cord is 10 feet long and retractable. If
you drop your spellbook while it's attached to the lariat, you can recover the spellbook as a
standard action. While attached to you, the book can never be farther than 10 feet from you.
Unclasping the book requires a move action, or the cord can be cut to free the book
([hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 5, hp 10).
