**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 50\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This pill causes the imbiber's skin to shift colors and patterns to match whatever is behind her. As
long as a significant portion of her skin is exposed (her head and forearms, for example), the
imbiber gains a +4 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks for 4 hours. This bonus doesn't stack with
that granted by [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [vanish](ref/spell/vanish), or similar
magical effects. Crafting a chameleon pill requires a successful DC 15 Craft (alchemy) check.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
