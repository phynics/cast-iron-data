**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 147\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This gritty, gray ointment weakens rocky materials. When applied to an object made of stone, it
reduces the object's [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) by 3 for 10 minutes. As a standard action, a
creature can apply the paste to affect one 5-foot-square or a Medium or smaller creature. The paste
only affects hardness, not [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) or similar defenses.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
