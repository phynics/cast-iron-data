**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 153\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

Blight tonic increases the potency of a creature's disease attack transmitted by physical contact,
such as a [dire rat's](ref/monster/rat-dire-rat) [filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever). The next
time a creature must save against the drinker's disease save DC, the DC for the initial infection
increases by +2. The tonic lasts for 10 minutes or until the next time a creature must save against
the drinker's disease DC, whichever comes first.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
