**Sources:** Undead Slayer's Handbook pg 13\
**Price** 290 gp; **Weight** 12 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This stained mahogany box contains tools that aid in contacting supernatural beings either as part
of an act or a genuine ritual. This kit includes a nonmagical
[spirit planchette set](ref/eq-misc/spirit-planchette-set), 10 [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle), 10
[candlesticks](ref/eq-misc/candlestick), one
[entertainer's outfit](ref/eq-misc/entertainers-outfit), one [harrow deck](ref/eq-misc/harrow-deck),
one [magnet](ref/eq-misc/magnet), 10 sticks of [incense](ref/eq-misc/incense), one scroll of
[augury](ref/spell/augury), and two scrolls of [prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation).
