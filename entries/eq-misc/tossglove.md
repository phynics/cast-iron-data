**Sources:** Goblins of Golarion pg 12\
**Price** 1 sp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Tools

A tossglove is a hefty leather and metal glove made for holding and throwing an insect, snake, or
other tiny creature that would otherwise bite the goblin. Those who don't use a tossglove when
attempting to sling such creatures provoke an attack of opportunity from the animal they attempt to
throw or stash. The glove has overlapping plates that cover the goblin's arm up to the elbow. It's a
full-round action to put on or take off a tossglove, and while it's worn, that hand can't be used to
perform acts of fine manipulation (such as making a Disable Device check or firing an arrow). The
gloved hand can still wield a melee weapon.
