**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 153\
**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** [Herbs](ref/rule/herbalism)\
**Gather** DC 24; **Yield** 1 dose\
**Terrain** any swamps\
**Preparation** A bone reed must be pressed and dried. This process requires a successful DC 10
Craft (alchemy) check and takes 8 hours to complete.\
**Use** A creature that sleeps for at least 8 hours in 1 day with a bone reed bound to one of its
arms or legs [heals](ref/rule/healing) double the normal ability damage and hit point damage (or
triple with full bed rest). A bone reed loses its effectiveness after use. Binding a bone reed to a
limb requires 1 minute of work.

This aquatic grass resembles translucent white horsetail.
