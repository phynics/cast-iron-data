**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 21\
**Price** 80 gp; **Weight** 35 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

This sentry mannequin is designed to seem more lifelike than its common counterpart. After 5 minutes
of assembly and coverage with the included loose hood and baggy clothing, this wire frame looks like
a vigilant humanoid figure. It has pivoted joints with counterweights that move it in ways
reminiscent of an alert guard every few rounds for 8 hours after it's set up. Creatures more than 30
feet from the mannequin must succeed at a DC 15 Perception check to realize it's not someone
standing watch.
