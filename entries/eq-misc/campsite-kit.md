**Price** 12 gp; **Weight** 80 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is actually four bundles of gear, designed so four individuals can share the load. It
consists of four [bedrolls](ref/eq-misc/bedroll), four [blankets](ref/eq-misc/blanket), a day's
worth of [firewood](ref/eq-misc/firewood-per-day), a [flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel),
a [tindertwig](ref/eq-misc/tindertwig), four [mess kits](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit), a
[cooking kit](ref/eq-misc/cooking-kit), and 8 days of [trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations)
(with the expectation that adventurers will supplement the rations with a little hunting as they
travel). Adventurers expecting inclement weather should also purchase one or more
[tents](ref/eq-misc/tent-small).
