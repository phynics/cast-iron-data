**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** [Herbs](ref/rule/herbalism)\
**Gather** DC 18; **Yield** 1 dose\
**Terrain** temperate or warm forests or plains\
**Preparation** The amaranth's flowers and stalks must be carefully ground into paste. This requires
a successful DC 12 Craft (alchemy) check and 1 hour of work.\
**Use** A dose of black amaranth paste can be applied to a dead body to slow decay. One dose used on
a Tiny or smaller creature delays the onset of decay for 3 days (as per
[gentle repose](ref/spell/gentle-repose)). Two doses are needed for Small creatures, 3 for Medium
creatures, 6 for Large creatures, 12 for Huge creatures, 24 for Gargantuan creatures, and 48 for
Colossal creatures. Each dose applied requires a full-round action that provokes attacks of
opportunity.

Black amaranth grows in small clusters from the remains of the dead.
