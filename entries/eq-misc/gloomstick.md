**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 24\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This variant [sunrod](ref/eq-misc/sunrod) was developed by [fetchling](ref/race/fetchling)
[alchemists](ref/class/alchemist). When twisted as a standard action, a gloomstick begins drawing in
light from the surrounding area, reducing the [light level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) by one step
(to a minimum of dim light) in a 20-foot radius. A gloomstick remains active for 1 minute before
crumbling to dust.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
