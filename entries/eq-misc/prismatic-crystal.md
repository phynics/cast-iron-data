**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 250\
**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This chunk of crystal shines with the colors of the rainbow. A prismatic crystal can be used as a
focus for the [read aura](ref/skill/perception) occult skill unlock to reduce the time needed to
read an aura from 10 minutes to 5 minutes. When held by psychic creatures, these crystals also emit
light in a small area, increasing the [light level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in a 10-foot radius
by one step (darkness becomes dim light, and dim light becomes normal light). A prismatic crystal
can't increase the light level above normal light.
