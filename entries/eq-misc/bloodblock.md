**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This gooey, pinkish substance helps treat wounds. Using a dose gives you a +5 alchemical bonus on
Heal checks for providing first aid, treating wounds made by [caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops) or
similar objects, or treating deadly wounds. A dose of bloodblock ends a [bleed](ref/rule/bleed)
effect as if you had made a DC 15 Heal check. When treating deadly wounds, using a dose of
bloodblock counts as one use of a [healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit) (and grants the +5 bonus
stated above).

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
