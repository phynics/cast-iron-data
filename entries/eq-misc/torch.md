**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 58, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 158\
**Price** 1 cp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A torch burns for 1 hour, shedding normal light in a 20-foot radius and increasing the
[light level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) by one step for an additional 20 feet beyond that area
(darkness becomes dim light and dim light becomes normal light). A torch does not increase the light
level in normal light or bright light. If a torch is used in combat, treat it as a one-handed
improvised weapon that deals bludgeoning damage equal to that of a gauntlet of its size, plus 1
point of fire damage.
