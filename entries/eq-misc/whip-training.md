**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Animal Gear

This short lash has a reach of only 10 feet, but is otherwise identical to a normal
[whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip). The wielder gains a +2 circumstance bonus on
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks to push a trained animal and on Intimidate checks
made to demoralize any animal as long as the animal is within the whip's reach. When using the whip
to demoralize a trained animal, the wielder may choose to [fascinate](ref/misc/fascinated) the
animal rather than causing it to become shaken for the same duration as the shaken condition, but
the fascinate effect ends if line of sight between the wielder and the animal is broken.
