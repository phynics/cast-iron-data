**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56, Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 3 gp; **Weight** 16 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit contains an [iron pot](ref/eq-misc/pot-iron), an iron [skillet](ref/eq-misc/skillet), a
ladle, a skewer, a wooden cutting board, a cutting knife, an iron tripod for the pot, a packet of
tinder, and a small selection of local or otherwise easy to find seasonings. You can attach the
skewer to the tripod for roasting small game animals. All the component pieces (except the skillet)
fit within the pot for easy storage and transport.
