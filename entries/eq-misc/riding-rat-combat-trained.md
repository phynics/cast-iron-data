## Riding rat

**Price** 75 gp (combat trained), 50 gp (common); **Weight** 50 lbs. (combat trained), 50 lbs.
(common)

Horses, ponies, and other draft animals are ill-suited to living underground for long stretches, so
the [ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk) bred a particularly strong type of dire rat to assist in pulling
their wagons and serving as mounts for caravan scouts. As rodents, riding rats respond favorably to
ratfolk's commands, and the animals' hardiness and omnivorous diet make them very easy to care for
even on long journeys through inhospitable terrain. Use the statistics for a
[dire rat](ref/monster/rat-dire-rat) with the [giant](ref/template/giant) simple template. A ratfolk
with the animal companion, mount, or similar class feature can select a riding rat as her mount.
