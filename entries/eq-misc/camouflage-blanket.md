**Price** 12 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This large, coarse blanket covers a single square and has a multi-colored design that blends in with
a specific environment type, such as underground, forests, or plains (see the ranger's
[Favored Terrains](ref/ability/favored-terrain) table). A character covered by a camouflage blanket
in that blanket's matching terrain has [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). Such characters who are
also [prone](ref/rule/prone) gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks. The bonus applies even
when the covered character attempts a Stealth check to remain hidden after sniping with a projectile
weapon.
