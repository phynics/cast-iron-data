**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 30\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

This leather pouch contains dyes, odorous balsams, colorchanging eye drops, reversible collars, and
a host of other tools to disguise one animal as another. The kit grants a +4 circumstance bonus on
Disguise checks to disguise an animal as another specific animal of the same species, and a +2
circumstance bonus on Disguise checks to disguise an animal as an animal of a different species. An
animal disguise kit is exhausted after 10 uses.
