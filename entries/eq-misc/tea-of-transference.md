**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 25\
**Price** 40 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This foul-tasting green tea allows monks to focus their ki on improving abilities they've gained
through other training. By drinking a cup of tea of transference, you can expend 1 point from your
ki pool to regain 1 point from your arcane pool, 2 rounds of barbarian rage, 2 rounds of bardic
performance, one use of channel energy, 1 grit point, one use of smite evil, one expended extract
slot at least 1 level lower than the highest-level extract you can create, or one expended spell
slot at least 2 levels lower than the highest-level spell you can cast. You must have the ki pool
class feature and the class feature you want to regain uses of in order to benefit from tea of
transference.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (5 gold + 5 realgar + 15 spirit of
wine)/sublimation; **Craft DC** 20\
**Time** 1 day; **Tools** [retort](ref/eq-misc/retort); **Type** alchemical remedy
