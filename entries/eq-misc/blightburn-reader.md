**Sources:** Heroes of the Darklands pg 8\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A blightburn reader is a small wooden or metal frame, roughly 2 inches wide and 4 inches long,
containing a specially prepared alchemical paper pressed between two thin panes of glass. Whenever a
character wearing a blightburn reader enters an area with a radiation hazard, the paper within
changes from white to black over the course of 1 minute. Once the paper turns black, it is destroyed
and must be replaced at a cost of 15 gp before the blightburn reader can be used again.
