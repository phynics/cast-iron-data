**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Drinking this cloudy tea has a soothing effect that clarifies your thoughts. For 10 minutes after
drinking the tea, you gain a +2 alchemical bonus against mind-affecting effects. If you drink
meditation tea while suffering from a mind-affecting affect, you may immediately roll another saving
throw (with the +2 bonus) against the effect; you may gain this particular benefit only once per
day.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
