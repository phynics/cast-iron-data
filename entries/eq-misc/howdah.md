**Sources:** Belkzen, Hold of the Orc Hordes pg 39\
**Price** 500 gp; **Weight** 250 lbs.\
**Category** Animal Gear

This wooden platform is tied like a saddle to a Huge creature's back. The platform is 10 feet square
and enclosed by a low wall and sometimes a roof. The howdah has room for four Medium creatures
(usually armed with bows or longspears), or two Medium creatures and a light ballista or light
catapult. Occupants of the howdah have [cover](ref/rule/cover) against attackers from below and are
considered mounted for the purposes of making attacks.
