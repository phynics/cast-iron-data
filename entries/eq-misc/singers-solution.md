**Price** 120 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When you imbibe this draught, you can accept [alchemical burn](ref/misc/alchemical-burn) to gain an
additional 4 rounds of bardic performance or raging song for the day. The effects of singer's
solution last for 1 hour; if not spent within 1 hour, these additional rounds are lost. Singer's
solution has no effect if you don't have the bardic performance or
[raging song](ref/ability/raging-song) class features.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
