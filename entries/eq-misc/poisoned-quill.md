**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 29\
**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tools

This long [pen](ref/eq-misc/inkpen) is made from a delicate feather and has a thin and unusually
long nib. The quill is typically white in color, though variations that are crimson, onyx, or some
combination of these colors also exist. A hidden compartment inside the nib allows the quill to hold
a single dose of poison that is dispensable with a small squeeze. Additionally, this provides a +2
circumstance bonus on Sleight of Hand checks to conceal poisons inside the quill. If this quill is
used with [vector ink](ref/eq-misc/vector-ink), it increases the DC of a poison mixed with the ink
by 1. This quill can be used as a small shiv to deal 1d3 points of damage and deliver its contained
poison. However, because of the fragility of the nib, using the quill in this way destroys it.
