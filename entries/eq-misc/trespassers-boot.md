**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 8 sp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

Consisting of a small wooden frame with inward-pointing barbed spikes, this trap is placed in a
shallow pit just deep enough for a Medium-sized character's lower leg. When someone steps in the
square, the boot catches and holds her leg. The target takes damage if she tries to move or if a
Disable Device attempt to free her fails by 5 or more.

**Trespasser's Boot - CR 1/2**\
**Type** mechanical; Perception DC 20; Disable Device DC 20\
**Trigger** location; Reset manual\
**Effect** pit spikes (Atk +10 melee, 2d4); spikes hold the target in place; the trap can be escaped
with a DC 20 Disable Device check, DC 20 Escape Artist check, or DC 24 Strength check
