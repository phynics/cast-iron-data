**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 244\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This 5-foot-long pole is fitted with a metal spike at its base. A trekking pole is meant to assist
with overland movement, particularly long hikes and travel through difficult terrain. If you use a
trekking pole as part of overland movement, you increase your distance traveled by 10% (so a
creature with a 30-foot base speed travels 3-1/3 miles in 1 hour of walking, instead of just 3
miles). While traveling in areas of impaired movement - such as a trackless desert or a swamp
trail - the increase to distance traveled while using a trekking pole increases to 25%.
