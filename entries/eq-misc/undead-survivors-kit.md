**Price** 900 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

Although this sturdy leather bandolier contains a few items to help combat undead foes, its primary
purpose is to supply the right tools to recover from an undead attack and escape. This kit includes
4 potions of [hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead), a potion of
[lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser), 2 flasks of
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), 2 doses of
[holy weapon balm](ref/eq-weapon/holy-weapon-balm), a vial of
[soul stimulant](ref/eq-misc/soul-stimulant), and 2 vials of
[twitch tonic](ref/eq-misc/twitch-tonic).
