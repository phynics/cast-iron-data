**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 93\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Clothing

These soft and subtle boots are constructed of silk and specially cured leather. They are specially
designed for [catfolk](ref/race/catfolk) feet. They work with the feline structure of the race's
feet to soften footfalls and to reduce the imprints of their tracks. While wearing softpaw boots, a
catfolk gains a +1 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks. Furthermore, the DC to notice or follow the
tracks of a catfolk wearing softpaw boots increases by +2.
