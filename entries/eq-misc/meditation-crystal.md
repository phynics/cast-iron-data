**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 25\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Channel Foci\
**Deity** Any

Though this focus is usually a hand-sized crystal, the exact form (such as a wooden idol, an iron
disk, and so on) may vary for some religions. A monk or paladin of the cleric's faith may
concentrate upon an activated meditation crystal, taking no actions for 1 minute, and regain 1 ki
point or one use of lay on hands. Once activated, the crystal retains its energy until a monk or
paladin uses it for this purpose or 24 hours pass.
