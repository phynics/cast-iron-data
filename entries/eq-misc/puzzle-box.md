**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 92, Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 1 gp-1,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.-5 lbs.\
**Category** Entertainment

This box features moving parts, secret panels, or unusual codes and locking mechanisms. Depending on
the box, a Disable Device, Knowledge (engineering), Linguistics, or simple Intelligence check might
open it. The DC of the check varies with the complexity of the box, but is typically 15 or 20 (retry
once per day). Multiple skill checks might be necessary for different stages of the puzzle. If the
puzzle box was constructed with the intention that one specific person should be able to open it,
that person gets a +5 circumstance bonus on the check.
