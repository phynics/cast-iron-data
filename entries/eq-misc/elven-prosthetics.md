**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 22\
**Price** 250 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Clothing

The canny [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf) sometimes needs a little something extra to look like a full
elf. Ear extensions make somewhat elongated half-elf ears look like sharp elf ears, and colored
lenses make a half-elf's human-like eyes look like elven eyes. These accessories give a half-elf a
+6 circumstance bonus on Disguise checks to resemble an elf and can be used only by a half-elf.
