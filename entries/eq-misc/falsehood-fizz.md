**Price** 190 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When imbibed, this cloudy, pink liquid grants you 1d4 uses of
[alchemical inspiration](ref/misc/alchemical-inspiration) that can be used only on Bluff checks to
lie or to deliver secret messages. Additionally, you can spend two uses of _alchemical inspiration_
to gain inspiration on a saving throw against truth-detecting or truth-forcing magic (such as
discern lies or zone of truth).

The benefits of falsehood fizz last for 1 hour. During this time, you must roll Sense Motive checks
twice and take the worse result.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
