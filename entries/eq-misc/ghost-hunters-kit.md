**Price** 670 gp; **Weight** 22 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This sturdy satchel is the quintessential toolkit for detecting [ghosts](ref/template/ghost) and
sending them to their final slumber. This kit includes 10 [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle), three
flasks of [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), one potion of
[lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser), a [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar), a
[folding shovel](ref/eq-misc/shovel-folding), four [sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod), one
[spiritbane spike](ref/eq-misc/spiritbane-spike), and a
[wooden holy symbol](ref/eq-misc/wooden-holy-symbol).
