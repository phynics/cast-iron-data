## Horse

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 81, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159, Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 200 gp (heavy), 300 gp (heavy, combat trained), 75 gp (light), 110 gp (light, combat
trained), 30 gp (pony), 45 gp (pony, combat trained); **Weight** 2,000 lbs. (heavy), 3,000 lbs.
(heavy, combat trained), 900 lbs. (light), 1,200 lbs. (light, combat trained), 800 lbs. (pony), 900
lbs. (pony, combat trained)

This Large quadruped is used as a mount and pack animal. Horses can be trained for combat but
otherwise are skittish around battle. Many cultures support breeding programs and often consider
their horse the best at whatever task they breed it to perform.

**Stat blocks:** [Horse](ref/monster/horse), [Pony](ref/monster/horse-pony).

**Heavy Horse:** These horses are often used as portage animals, pulling significant loads across
great distances.

**Heavy Horse (Combat Trained):** Heavy warhorses are bred and trained for combat. They are often
are outfitted with heavy barding, but fight just as fiercely even without the armor.

**Light Horse:** These horses can be ridden, but often serve as porters or carriage horses.

**Light Horse (Combat Trained):** These light warhorses are bred for war and are favored by fast and
light cavalry, scouts, and daredevil knights.

**Pony:** These creatures are typically used for carting goods along narrow mountain trails or as
steeds for young or Small riders.

**Pony (Combat Trained):** Ponies can be trained for combat just as effectively as their larger,
more mature brethren. Such steeds often serve Small riders that expect to face battle.

### Specific Mounts

**Sources:** Knights of the Inner Sea pg 33\
Each of the following listings details a specific breed of riding animal and provides a trait that
grants an additional boon to the mount or its rider. The trait granted to a mount counts against a
character's total maximum number of traits. If a knight replaces his mount, he effectively loses the
old trait and gains the new mount's trait.

These specific breeds do not have published prices. Consult your GM if you wish to buy one.

**Chiardmar:** Wild horses found only in the hilly countryside of northern Nidal, chiardmars are
renowned for their speed and grace, and are particularly prized by Nidalese couriers and traveling
agents of the Umbral Court. **Associated Trait:** [Fleet of foot](ref/trait/fleet-of-foot).

**Dort Charger:** Held in particularly high esteem by the crusaders of Lastwall, Dort chargers are
sturdy warhorses, and can often be found among the front lines on both sides of the battlefield.
**Associated Trait:** [Burst of Speed](ref/trait/burst-of-speed).

**Fell Pony:** Bred in the dour county of Amaans in south-central Ustalav, fell ponies have dark
brown fur and are noted especially for their surefootedness. **Associated Trait:**
[Black as Night](ref/trait/black-as-night).

**Lastwall Jasper:** Named after the famed horse Jasper of the city of Vigil - which mistakenly
thought its rider was drowning during the swimming portion of the Steeplechase and swam across the
entire Path River trying to save him - these brown-and-white horses are prized for their impressive
water-faring capabilities. **Associated Trait:** [Water-Trained](ref/trait/water-trained).

**Taldor Jennet:** Taldor jennets typically sport white or tan coats, and have a slimmer frame than
other purebreds. Despite their seemingly fragile builds, they pull their own weight when pressed
into battle and are remarkably intelligent to boot. **Associated Trait:**
[Cunning Breed](ref/trait/cunning-breed).

**Chernasardo Riding Elk:** Native to the southwestern corner of the Fangwood in Nirmathas,
Chernasardo riding elk are prized by the rangers who reside in that region. **Associated Trait:**
[Stalwart Elk](ref/trait/stalwart-elk).

Chernasardo Riding Elk use the stats of [river elk](ref/monster/herd-animal-elk) and do not have
published prices, nor any published confirmation of whether it uses the Large or Medium version of
the elk. They have been placed in the "horse" entry because of their similar nature. Consult your GM
if you wish to buy one.
