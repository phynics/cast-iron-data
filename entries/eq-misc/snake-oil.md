**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 252\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This mildly toxic liniment numbs flesh and can soothe injuries. Though the tincture is actually
poisonous, an imbiber who avoids becoming poisoned recovers from minor injuries.

### Snake Oil

**Type** poison (contact); **Save** Fortitude DC 12\
**Onset** 1 round; **Frequency** 1/minute for 5 minutes\
**Effect** On a failed save, snake oil deals 1 point of nonlethal damage. However, on a successful
save, the toxin heals 1d6 points of nonlethal damage; **Cure** 1 save

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
