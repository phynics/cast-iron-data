**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 7\
**Price** 563 gp; **Weight** 33 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

The exact location of a dragon's lair must sometimes be learned through exploration. This kit
provides tools helpful in tracking a dragon back to its lair and includes an area map, a
[climber's kit](ref/eq-misc/climbers-kit), an
[elixir of swimming](ref/magic-wondrous/elixir-of-swimming), an
[everburning torch](ref/eq-misc/everburning-torch), a [footprint book](ref/eq-misc/footprint-book),
a [grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook), a [hammer](ref/eq-misc/hammer), eight
[pitons](ref/eq-misc/piton), four [sacks](ref/eq-misc/sack), and 100 feet of
[silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft).
