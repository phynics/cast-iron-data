**Sources:** Heroes of the Wild pg 9\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This alchemically infused wax is spread over exposed skin to protect it from chapping, cold, dry
winds, heat, and the loss of moisture. Applying it takes 5 minutes; once applied, it reduces any
nonlethal damage taken from [cold](ref/rule/cold-dangers) or [hot](ref/rule/heat-dangers)
environments by 1 point for 6 hours. Crafting blain bane requires a successful DC 19 Craft (alchemy)
check.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 19
