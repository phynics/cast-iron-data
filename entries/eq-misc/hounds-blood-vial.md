**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 36\
**Price** 110 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

If you apply the thick, red paste known as hound's blood to your nostrils or upper lip, it greatly
enhances your sense of smell. For most creatures, this grants a +2 alchemical bonus on Perception
checks for 1 hour. For gnomes, it briefly grants a super-heightened sense of smell, granting the
[scent](ref/umr/scent) ability for 5 minutes, before the potency is reduced to a +2 alchemical bonus
on Perception checks for an additional 1 hour.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
