**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 17\
**Price** 900 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is designed to help adventurers deal with surprise encounters against magically concealed
enemies, and comes in a small, waterproof satchel. The kit contains 2 scrolls of
[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust), 6 1/2-pound packages of [powder](ref/eq-misc/powder) such as
flour, and one packet of [dust of appearance](ref/magic-wondrous/dust-of-appearance).
