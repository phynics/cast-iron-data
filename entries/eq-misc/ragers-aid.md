**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 206\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This packet filled with spikey-edged leaves has a pungent scent almost strong enough to make your
eyes water. While you chew the leaves, you ignore the effects of [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued). The
leaves last for 6 rounds, at which point only a wad of pulp remains. When the effect of rager's aid
wears off, you are [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) instead.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
