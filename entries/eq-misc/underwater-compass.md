**Sources:** Blood of the Sea pg 19\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This otherwise ordinary [compass](ref/eq-misc/compass) is sealed inside a glass case amid a quantity
of clear oil. The oil keeps the glass from cracking at high pressures, allowing the compass to
function at any depth up to 1,000 feet. An underwater compass grants you a +2 circumstance bonus on
Survival checks to avoid becoming lost and also indicates your depth below the surface to the
nearest increment of 20 feet. Underwater compasses are not particularly durable and cannot be
roughly handled. An underwater compass has a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 1, 2 hit points, and a
break DC of 15.
