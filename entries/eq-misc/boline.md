**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 16\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

Bolines are hook-shaped knives commonly used by herbalists and spellcasters to harvest ingredients
for their craft. A boline's blade is typically 5 inches long, and is too small and irregular to use
as a weapon. You can use a boline to finely slice the herbs in a
[healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit), so you can treat deadly wounds with only a single use from
a healer's kit rather than two uses.
