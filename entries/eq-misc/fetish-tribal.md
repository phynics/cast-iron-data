**Sources:** Goblins of Golarion pg 12\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tools

Tribal fetishes vary from tribe to tribe, but are generally small, carved wooden or stone religious
symbols, luck tokens, or curious naturally occurring objects specially carved to resemble something
of significance (such as an acorn with a wolf fangs, a dried mushroom with skull-shaped blotches, or
a stone with a lizard carved on it). A [goblin](ref/race/goblin) divine spellcaster can use a tribal
fetish as a divine focus for spellcasting.
