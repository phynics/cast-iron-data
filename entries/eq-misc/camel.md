**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 81\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** 1,500 lbs\
**Category** Mounts/Pets

Due to their incredible endurance, these Large animals are the preferred mounts of desert-dwelling
people. [Camels](ref/monster/herd-animal-camel) tend to be ill-tempered beasts.

### Specific Mounts

**Sources:** Knights of the Inner Sea pg 33, Wilderness Origins pg 23\
Each of the following listings details a specific breed of riding animal and provides a trait that
grants an additional boon to the mount or its rider. The trait granted to a mount counts against a
character's total maximum number of traits. If a knight replaces his mount, he effectively loses the
old trait and gains the new mount's trait.

These specific breeds do not have published prices. Consult your GM if you wish to buy one.

**Keleshite Trade Camel:** The camels of the Padishah Empire are larger and shaggier than their
Qadiran cousins, with two humps and long hair. They are also ill-tempered even by camel standards,
with a penchant for spitting when annoyed. **Associated Trait:**
[Nauseating Spit](ref/trait/nauseating-spit).

**Qadiran Dromedary:** These hardy beasts of burden are capable of running long distances and going
without water for days on end. **Associated Trait:**
[Relentless Fortitude](ref/trait/relentless-fortitude).
