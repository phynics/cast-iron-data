**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 15\
**Price** 625 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This serum transfers a [homunculus's](ref/monster/homunculus) loyalty from one master to another. In
order for this to work, the homunculus's current master and its master-to-be must each place a drop
of their blood into the serum. When an appropriate homunculus drinks this concoction, it thereafter
treats the master-to-be as its master. The change in ownership also causes the homunculus's
alignment to change to match that of its new owner.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (200 quicksilver + 150 realgar + 200
spirit of wine)/distillation; **Craft DC** 30\
**Time** 1 day; **Tools** [retort](ref/eq-misc/retort); **Type** alchemical remedy
