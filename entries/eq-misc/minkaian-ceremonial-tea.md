**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 28\
**Price** 900 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This tea is extremely rare and expensive, requiring several years to grow and prepare. A
[geisha](ref/arc-bard/geisha) bard who uses this tea as part of her tea ceremony to inspire courage
increases the bonuses granted by 1. A [lotus geisha](ref/arc-bard/lotus-geisha) bard who drinks this
tea increases the bonus granted by her enrapturing performance ability by 1; this effect lasts for
24 hours.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 35
