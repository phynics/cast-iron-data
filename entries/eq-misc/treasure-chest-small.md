## Treasure chest

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 12\
**Price** 7 gp (medium), 37 gp (huge), 15 gp (large), 3 gp (small); **Weight** 50 lbs. (medium), 250
lbs. (huge), 100 lbs. (large), 25 lbs. (small)

A treasure chest is a common wooden [chest](ref/eq-misc/chest) treated with resin to make the wood
water-resistant. Metal bands, usually bronze to prevent rusting, are strapped around the treasure
chest for extra reinforcement, and the lock is also typically made of bronze. A treasure chest uses
the statistics of an ordinary wooden chest, but its hit points increase by 25% and its break DC
increases by 2. Treasure chests are waterproof and withstand being buried better than ordinary
chests
