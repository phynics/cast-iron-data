**Price** 6 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Animal Gear

This small chime is designed to be used in aquatic environs. After submerging the pinger in water,
the user can lower a miniature mallet into the dry hollow of the chime from above and ring the
instrument with a successful DC 5 Perform (percussion) check. An aquatic pinger can be heard clearly
from up to a half-mile away by underwater creatures (Perception DC 0). For each half-mile beyond,
Perception checks to hear an aquatic pinger take a -2 penalty.
