**Price** 190 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When imbibed, this bitter, rust-colored concoction grants you 1d4 uses of
[alchemical inspiration](ref/misc/alchemical-inspiration) that can be used only on Intimidate
checks. The benefits of boar's bellow last for 1 hour; during this time, you must roll all Diplomacy
checks twice and take the worse result.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
