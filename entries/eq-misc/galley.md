**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 87, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159\
**Price** 30,000 gp; **Price of Passage** (per mile) 1 sp\
**Category** Transport, Sea

This three-masted ship has 70 [oars](ref/eq-misc/oar) on either side and requires a total crew
of 200. A [galley](ref/rule/wv-galley) is 130 feet long and 20 feet wide, and can carry 150 tons of
cargo or 250 soldiers. For 8,000 gp more, it can be fitted with a ram and firing platforms fore,
aft, and amidships. This ship cannot make sea voyages and sticks close to the coast. It moves about
4 miles per hour when being rowed or under sail.
