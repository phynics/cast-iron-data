**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 236\
**Price** 250 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

When imbibed, this bitter, rust-colored concoction grants you 1d4 uses of
[alchemical inspiration](ref/misc/alchemical-inspiration) that can be used only on Disable Device
checks and Sleight of Hand checks. These benefits last for 1 hour; during this time, you must roll
all Acrobatics checks and Escape Artist checks twice and take the worse result.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
