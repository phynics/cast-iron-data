**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 80, Ultimate Equipment pg 93\
**Price** 1-6 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Category** Mounts/Pets

As an agricultural animal, goats provide milk, meat, hair, and hide, and they are sometimes used to
clear fields of weeds and shrubbery. Some spellcasters bond with them as familiars.

### Specific Mounts

**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 23\
Each of the following listings details a specific breed of riding animal and provides a trait that
grants an additional boon to the mount or its rider. The trait granted to a mount counts against a
character's total maximum number of traits. If a knight replaces his mount, he effectively loses the
old trait and gains the new mount's trait.

**Mindspin Mountain Goat:** The goats of the Mindspin Mountains are known for being sure-footed
climbers, scaling vertical peaks that would give a monkey pause. **Associated Trait:**
[Clamber](ref/trait/clamber).

Mindspin Mountain Goats use the stats of [rams](ref/monster/herd-animal-ram) and do not have
published prices. They have been placed in the "goat" entry because of their similar nature. Consult
your GM if you wish to buy one.
