**Sources:** People of the Stars pg 19\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Food/Drink

Generally composed of meat, hard cheese, fruits and vegetables, nut and bean pastes, and vital
herbs, space rations are dehydrated and then kept extremely dry in order to maximize preservation
and storage efficiency. A single serving feeds a Medium character for 1 day, but unless an equal
weight of water is added to the rations, the character eating them becomes
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) until she drinks enough water.
