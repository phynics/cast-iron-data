**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 236\
**Price** 35 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

These alchemical beads function as [sentry seeds](ref/eq-misc/sentry-seeds), but are also filled
with an alchemical gas that explodes on contact with the air. This explosion is louder and flashier
than the pop of normal sentry seeds (Perception DC 0). Creatures within 5 feet of the beads when
they break must succeed at a DC 11 Fortitude save or be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
