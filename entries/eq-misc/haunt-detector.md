**Sources:** Haunted Heroes Handbook pg 12\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This unique instrument is composed of a 1-foot-long, sealed glass tube filled with a clear
alchemical solution. Eight smaller glass vessels, each containing a different uniquely colored
liquid, float within the primary tube. As temperatures change in the atmosphere surrounding the
haunt detector, the interior vessels rise and fall in proportion to the density of their respective
liquids. Observation of a haunt detector's fluctuations grants a +2 competence bonus on Perception
checks to notice haunts or the presence of creatures with the cold or fire subtypes.
