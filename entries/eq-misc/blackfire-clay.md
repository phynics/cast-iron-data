**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 103\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This pliant black clay is always pleasantly warm to the touch. Working blackfire clay in your hands
for a full round causes the clay to grow much warmer, granting you a +4 alchemical bonus on saving
throws made to resist [cold weather](ref/rule/cold-dangers). You can combine five blocks of clay
over the course of a minute to produce a source of warmth equivalent to a small campfire, allowing
you to heat a campsite and cook food.

Blackfire clay only produces heat, never light, smoke, or odor. The clay emits warmth for 1 hour
before hardening into an unusable lump. A newly created brick of blackfire clay is composed of 10
blocks.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
