**Price** 1 sp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Used by swimmers for longer dives, this bladder is similar to a [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin),
but is relatively airtight. If inflated, it holds enough air to sustain a Medium creature for 1
round or a Small creature for 2 rounds.
