**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Troll styptic is used as a field treatment for wounds and bleeding, particularly where magical
healing is not available. When applied directly to wounds, this jar of paste grants a living
creature [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2 for 2d4 rounds. While active, the styptic also
closes any open wounds the subject receives, negating any [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) effects that would
affect the target. This is a painful cure, and the target must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or
be [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for the styptic's duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
