**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 24, Seekers of Secrets pg 43\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

Pale blue when wet, ghost ink dries to near-transparency 1 minute after application. Ghost ink is
used to subtly mark trails and locations. The pigment glows red under the light shed by
[fire beetle](ref/monster/beetle-fire-beetle) glands and [sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod); otherwise, a
successful DC 25 Perception check is required to notice a mark in ghost ink. One vial of ghost ink
is sufficient for writing a page's worth of characters.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
