**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Tools

This eldritch chart describes part of the unfathomably vast shape of the physical universe and the
subtler motions of the stars. A cosmogram grants a +1 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (geography)
checks relating to stars and on Survival checks to navigate the void of space between star systems.
A cosmogram can be consulted while using an [orrery](ref/eq-misc/orrery) as a focus for
[augury](ref/spell/augury). Doing so increases the chance of a successful reading by 2%.
