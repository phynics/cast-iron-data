**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 245\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Clothing

These waterproof boots are felt lined and have a thin layer of metal around the soles. The top of
each boot ends just before the knee, with a cord for tying the top shut over clothing or exposed
skin. These boots assist in prolonged hikes through deep mud or shallow pools, granting the wearer a
+1 circumstance bonus on Profession (fisher) checks while fishing in shallow water.
