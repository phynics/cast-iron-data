**Sources:** People of the Sands pg 28\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Each application of this alchemical solution, typically compounded from substances found in desert
flowers, fruits, and roots, heals 1d4 points of nonlethal damage that the target has taken from
[heat exposure](ref/rule/heat-dangers). If you succeed at a DC 20 Heal check while applying sun
cream, you may add your Wisdom modifier to the nonlethal damage healed. A creature cannot benefit
from more than one application of sun cream in a 24-hour period.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
