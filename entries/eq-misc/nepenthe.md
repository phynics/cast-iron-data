**Price** 400 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** [Herbs](ref/rule/herbalism)\
**Gather** DC 20; **Yield** 1 dose\
**Terrain** warm forests or swamps\
**Preparation** A dose of pollen must be mixed with alchemical reagents with a successful DC 16
Craft (alchemy) check and then left to sit for 1 hour before it can be used.\
**Use** A dose of nepenthe pollen must be inhaled as a standard action, whereupon the potent vapors
act upon unpleasant or unknown thoughts and memories. This grants the creature a new saving throw to
immediately end a single mind-affecting effect currently affecting the creature (effects that allow
no saving throw cannot be removed in this way). An unwilling creature can be dosed with the pollen
as a touch attack that provokes an attack of opportunity. A creature can be affected by a dose of
nepenthe only once in a single 24-hour period. This is a poison effect.

This foot-tall blue-green pitcher plant produces a pollen that smells faintly of mint and lures
insects and even small vertebrates toward its dangerous mouth.
