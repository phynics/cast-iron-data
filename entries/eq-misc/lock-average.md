## Lock

**Price** 40 gp (average), 80 gp (good), 20 gp (simple), 150 gp (superior); **Weight** 1 lb.
(average), 1 lb. (good), 1 lb. (simple), 1 lb. (superior)

This can be a padlock, an inset lock in a door or chest, or any other type of keyed lock. The DC to
open a lock with the Disable Device skill depends on the lock's quality: simple (DC 20), average (DC
25), good (DC 30), or superior (DC 40).
