**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 202\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Entertainment

Each book contains 10 puzzles made to test the mind and stir the intellect. Solving a single puzzle
requires at least an hour and a successful DC 10 Intelligence check, though every 5 points by which
you exceed the check reduces the amount of time you need to solve the puzzle by 10 minutes (to a
minimum of 10 minutes to solve an individual puzzle). Once you solve a puzzle, for the next 24 hours
you can choose to roll twice on a single Disable Device, Knowledge, or Sense Motive skill check and
take the higher result. Once all the puzzles are solved, the book is useless, though you can
purchase another puzzle book with different puzzles.
