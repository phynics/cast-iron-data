**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 95\
**Price** 1 sp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Food/Drink

This exotic dessert is made with milk and cream, often flavored with fruit or mint. Because it
quickly melts at room temperature, it must be made fresh from snow or ice, or maintained at a low
temperature, such as in a cold cellar or with alchemy or magic. This limitation means it is
expensive and in most lands it is only available during certain seasons. The listed price is for a
large scoop (1 cup).
