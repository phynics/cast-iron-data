**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This is little more than a handle with a cup, loop, or spur to hold the butt of a
[dart](ref/eq-weapon/dart), [javelin](ref/eq-weapon/javelin), or
[shortspear](ref/eq-weapon/shortspear). Using a spear-thrower to throw such a weapon doubles the
projectile's range increment. Setting a weapon into a spear-thrower is a move action; by taking the
[Rapid Reload](ref/feat/rapid-reload) (spear-thrower) feat, you reduce this to a free action.
