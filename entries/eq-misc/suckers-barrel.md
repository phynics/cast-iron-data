**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 117\
**Price** 4 gp; **Weight** 35 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A sucker's barrel is constructed to appear to the casual eye exactly like a normal barrel. However,
an inner barrier installed near one of the barrel's ends hides a cavity containing a bladder filled
with water. This creates a false bottom such that, when the barrel is full, it weighs and feels the
same as a barrel filled completely with more valuable liquid, such as alcohol or oil. In this way, a
merchant can "fill" a barrel with a smaller quantity of a liquid (typically 75% of the barrel's
total capacity) and then sell it as if it were a full barrel's worth.
