## Dinosaurs and Megafauna

**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 14\
**Price** Varies; **Weight** -\
**Category** Mounts/Pets

The prices listed are for dinosaurs and megafauna that have been reared from birth in order to serve
as pets or mounts. Combat-trained dinosaurs and megafauna are incredibly rare and have similarly
extravagant costs (if one can even find a seller). Though not normally readily available,
combat-trained dinosaurs and megafauna typically cost an amount equal to 1-1/2 × the price of the
standard animal.
