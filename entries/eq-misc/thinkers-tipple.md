**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 23\
**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This orange liquid invigorates the mind. For 1 hour after drinking this tincture, you gain a +2
alchemical bonus on Knowledge checks and can attempt untrained Knowledge checks if the DC is 15 or
lower. This saps your energy, imposing a -4 penalty on Fortitude saving throws for the same
duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
