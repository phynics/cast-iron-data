**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 248\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

These leather goggles have dark, purple-hued lenses coated with a special alchemical substance that
reveals particular psychic spectrums. To provide any benefit, the goggles must be continuously worn
for 1 hour, during which time the wearer takes a -2 penalty on all sight-based Perception checks.
After this 1-hour period, the user can remove the goggles to gain a +2 circumstance bonus on
Perception checks for the [read aura](ref/skill/perception) occult skill unlock. This benefit fades
after 1 hour.
