**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 22\
**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

Favored by performers of all kinds, this tincture is a blend of alchemically enhanced herbs and
spices mixed into a pint of ale. For 6 hours after drinking this tincture, you gain a +2 alchemical
bonus on Bluff and Perform checks, and you treat your level as 1 higher for the purpose of
determining the effects of a bardic performance or a [raging song](ref/ability/raging-song). The
potent drink causes you take a -2 penalty on Acrobatics, Intimidate, Knowledge, Sense Motive, and
Spellcraft checks for the same duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
