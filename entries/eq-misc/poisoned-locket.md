**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 29\
**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tools

This heart-shaped locket is attached to a thin metal chain made of looped strands of gold or silver.
Inside the locket is a small, spring-loaded vial that can hold a single dose of powdered poison.
Loading the locket with poison carries the same risks as applying poison to a weapon. When the
locket is opened, the vial is shattered by a ball bearing, releasing the powder into the air and
treating the poison's type as if it were inhaled. The airborne poison affects all creatures within
10 feet of the locket.
