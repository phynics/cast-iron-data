**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 65\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Each small tin of this specially treated jam contains just enough of the gooey stuff to provide a
[halfling](ref/race/halfling) with a single serving of revitalizing nourishment. While any creature
can eat these preserves as a standard action, it only affects halflings in a beneficial way.
Halflings who eat the preserves recover from [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued). Non-halflings who eat
alchemical preserves become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
