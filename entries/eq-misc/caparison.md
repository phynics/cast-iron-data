**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 14\
**Price** 2 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Animal Gear

A caparison is a sizable cloth covering laid over a mount or other four-legged creature. It serves
many of the same purposes a cloak does for a humanoid creature, occupies the shoulder slot, and can
be enchanted in the same manner. Caparisons are sometimes made of precious materials or crafted with
delicate artistry - this does not increase their practicality, but can significantly increase their
price as art objects.
