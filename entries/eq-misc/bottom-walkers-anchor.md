**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This dense anchor is attached to a sturdy but easily detachable chain (removing the chain is a move
action). Wearing a bottomwalker's anchor all but guarantees an undersea explorer a swiftly sinking
[buoyancy](ref/rule/buoyancy), though it also increases her armor check penalty by 1 and decreases
her maximum Dexterity bonus by 1 (or sets it at +6, if the wearer didn't have a maximum Dexterity
bonus). Compared to simply carrying plenty of dense items, it's unwieldy, but the advantage is that
the undersea explorer can detach the anchor in a hurry in order to float to the surface, while
unloading a number of dense objects might be a slow process (and could deprive the explorer of
valuable gear).
