**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 13\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

These wooden braces hook on to a [gathlain's](ref/race/gathlain) wings to help in clinging to ivy,
tree limbs, or other surfaces with significant handholds. Many wing hooks are decorated with
intricate carvings. A gathlain wearing wing hooks can cling to a surface with a Climb DC of 10 or
lower without effort, and can safely sleep there. The wing hooks leave the gathlain's arms free
while attempting Climb checks on such surfaces.
