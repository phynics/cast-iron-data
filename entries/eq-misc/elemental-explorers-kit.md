**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 17\
**Price** 2,700 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This waterproof and flame-resistant case contains items useful to a spellcaster traveling the
Elemental Planes, either intentionally or due to some mishap. The kit includes durable guides to
each of the four Elemental Planes, describing a few common creatures and hazards found on each plane
and pointing out major landmarks. Each guide grants a +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (planes)
checks and Survival checks for its plane. This kit also contains 3 scrolls of
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages), 2 scrolls of
[planar adaptation](ref/spell/planar-adaptation), and a scroll of
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift) attuned to the Material Plane so the kit's owner can return
home. The price reflects a kit with divine scrolls; a kit containing arcane scrolls instead costs an
additional 2,000 gp
