## Owl

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 20 gp (common), 20 gp (Verduran); **Weight** 1-3 lbs. (common), 3 lbs. (Verduran)

These Tiny birds of prey are mainly nocturnal and are known for their keen eyes and near-silent
flight. Owls eat small animals and insects, and can be found in nearly any environment. Some farmers
create owl homes near fields and barns to control rodent populations. The smartest owls make
excellent familiars.
