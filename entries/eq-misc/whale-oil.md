**Price** 2 sp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A common source of illumination in the Lands of the Linnorm Kings, 1 pint of whale oil burns for 10
hours in a lantern or lamp. It otherwise functions as normal [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil).
