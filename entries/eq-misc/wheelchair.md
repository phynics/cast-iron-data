**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This device is a chair, typically made from wood, with large wheels attached on either side. A
wheelchair can seat a single Medium or Small creature. When seated in a wheelchair, you can propel
yourself along a reasonably flat surface at your base speed as a move action, so long as you have at
least one hand free. Moving twice in a round or taking the run action requires you to have both
hands free. Moving uphill or over rough terrain halves the speed you move in a wheelchair, and
certain terrain (such as fallen logs) may be entirely impassable. A wheelchair has
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 5 and 30 hit points.
