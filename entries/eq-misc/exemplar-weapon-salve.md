**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 20\
**Price** 350 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This alchemical salve strengthens the material of a weapon, improves its balance, gives it greater
flexibility without sacrificing resilience, and enhances its ability to hold an edge or point and
survive an impact. An application turns a typical nonmagical melee weapon into a
[masterwork weapon](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons). A double weapon requires 2 doses of exemplar
weapon salve, and 50 arrows, bolts, or similar ammunition can be enhanced by a single application.
