**Sources:** Dungeoneer's Handbook pg 7\
**Price** 353 gp; **Weight** 40 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit contains all manner of items useful for smashing down doors, creating diversions, and
blowing things up. It includes a dose of [alchemical glue](ref/eq-misc/alchemical-glue), a flask of
[alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), a [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar), a
[drill](ref/eq-misc/drill), a [fuse grenade](ref/eq-weapon/fuse-grenade), a
[glass cutter](ref/eq-misc/glass-cutter), a [jetcaster](ref/eq-misc/jetcaster), a vial of
[phosphorescent gel](ref/eq-misc/phosphorescent-gel), 4 pints of [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil), a
[portable ram](ref/eq-misc/portable-ram), a dose of [rusting powder](ref/eq-misc/rusting-powder), 5
[tindertwigs](ref/eq-misc/tindertwig), and a [wire](ref/eq-misc/wire).
