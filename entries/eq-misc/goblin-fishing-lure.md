**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 243\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

The end of this fishing lure is colored in bright greens and shaped like a comical goblin face. The
lure's ridiculous coloration and unique shape attract all sorts of fish. Using this lure grants a +2
circumstance bonus on Profession (fisher) checks, as per a masterwork tool. Each time such a check
is attempted, there is a 25% chance the lure is lost. You reduce the chance of losing the lure by 2%
for every rank of Profession (fisher) you have.
