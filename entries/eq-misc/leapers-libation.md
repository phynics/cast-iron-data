**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This bubbly drink has a zesty scent and invigorates the muscles. For 1 hour after drinking this
tincture, you gain a +4 alchemical bonus on Acrobatics checks to jump horizontally, you are always
treated as having a running start for the purpose of these checks, and your jump distance can exceed
your base speed. Due to your exaggerated movement, you take a -2 penalty on attack rolls and cannot
take 5-foot steps for the same duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
