**Price** 32 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Extracted from bioluminescent jellyfish, plankton, and other marine organisms that glow in the
ocean's depths, this ink is refined in a process that makes the ink stable and long lasting. This
colorful ink glows gently like a [candle](ref/eq-misc/candle), so it is easier to discern any
writing at a distance, especially in the dark. The ink's glow lasts for about 1 year, after which
time the writing remains colorful but no longer glows. Bioluminescent ink is typically stored in a
waterproof bladder.
