**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 24\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Animal Gear

A short [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) with a leather loop at the end, this whip can be felt through a
horse's armor but does not deal damage to the horse. A rider who uses a horse whip while using the
Ride skill to spur her mount does not deal damage to the mount, as opposed to the 1d3 points of
damage normally dealt by this action. Using a horse whip does not negate the fatigue that spurring a
mount can cause.
