**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 99\
**Price** 500 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This small, latched wooden case contains numerous tools and devices used in hunting and slaying
vampires. It contains 10 cloves of garlic, four [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons)
[wooden stakes](ref/eq-weapon/wooden-stake), a [hammer](ref/eq-misc/hammer), a silvered
[mirror](ref/eq-misc/mirror), 4 vials of [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), 1 application of
[silversheen](ref/magic-wondrous/silversheen), and a
[masterwork holy symbol](ref/eq-misc/holy-symbol). It also contains numerous regents and powders
used for treating wounds and making wards, salves, and other concoctions. In addition to its general
usefulness, when displayed, a vampire slayer's kit grants its owner a +2 bonus on all Charisma-based
skill checks made to investigate or seek out vampires.
