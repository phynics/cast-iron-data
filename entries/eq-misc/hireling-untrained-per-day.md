## Hireling

**Price** 3 sp per day (trained), 1 sp per day (untrained); **Weight** - (trained), - (untrained)

This listing is for any other sort of typical employment not covered by another service or job in
this section. An untrained hireling is a crier, laborer, maid, mourner, porter, or other menial
worker. A trained hireling is a mason, mercenary warrior, carpenter, blacksmith, cook, scribe,
painter, teamster, and so on. The listed price represents a minimum wage for an adequately skilled
worker, and an expert hireling usually requires significantly higher pay. The listed price is a
day's wages (generally 7-10 hours of work per day).

Typical equipment for a guard or mercenary warrior is
[studded leather armor](ref/eq-armor/studded-leather) and a [club](ref/eq-weapon/club),
[shortsword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword), or [shortspear](ref/eq-weapon/shortspear). Most guards are
off-duty soldiers or city watchmen, though some are unskilled laborers with a talent for fighting.
