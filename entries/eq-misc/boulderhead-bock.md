**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 13\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This malty ale has a legendarily thick, creamy head. It creates a pleasant buzz and sense of
euphoria that dull the effects of pain and concussions, granting the drinker a +1 alchemical bonus
on saving throws to avoid becoming dazed, staggered, or stunned, or against any effect with the pain
descriptor for 1 hour. In addition, a dose of boulderhead bock grants the drinker 1d6 temporary hit
points that apply exclusively against nonlethal damage. Multiple doses of boulderhead bock stack, to
a maximum alchemical bonus of +5 and a maximum of 20 temporary hit points.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (4 gold + 15 spirit of
wine)/fermentation; **Craft DC** 19\
**Time** 1 week; **Tools** [brewer's kit](ref/eq-misc/brewers-kit); **Type** alcohol
