## Lock of the saved

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 25\
**Price** +50 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Channel Foci\
**Deity** Any

This sturdy lock may be of any quality; the cost of making it a channel focus is in addition to the
normal [lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-average) cost. When activated, the lock's Disable Device DC and the
break DC for whatever it is locking (such as a door or chest) increase by 5; the lock gains +2
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) and +4 hit points. The lock retains these bonuses for 24 hours or until
it is forced open (including picking the lock).
