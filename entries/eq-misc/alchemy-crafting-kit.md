**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 77\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

An alchemist with an alchemy crafting kit is assumed to have all the material components needed for
his extracts, mutagens, and bombs, except for those components that have a specific cost. An alchemy
crafting kit provides no bonuses on Craft (alchemy) checks. (This item was called an "alchemist's
kit" in the _Advanced Player's Guide,_ and was renamed to avoid confusion with the pre-selected set
of adventuring gear in _Ultimate Equipment_ called an
["alchemist's kit."](ref/eq-misc/alchemists-kit))
