## Straitjacket

**Price** 5 gp (common), 50 gp (masterwork); **Weight** 1 lb. (common), 1 lb. (masterwork)

Fashioned of durable canvas and leather straps, this instrument of restraint prevents its wearer
from using his arms entirely. Properly binding a creature in a straitjacket requires 1 minute, and
the creature must be willing or helpless. This time can be reduced to 5 rounds, but doing so lowers
the DC to escape the restraints by 5. A creature wearing a straitjacket gains the
[grappled condition](ref/rule/grapple-conditions), except it is able to move. Escaping from the
straitjacket requires a successful DC 28 Escape Artist check (DC 30 for a masterwork straitjacket.)
