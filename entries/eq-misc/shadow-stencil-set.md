**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 211\
**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

This set of 10 elaborate metal stencils are used in shadow puppetry to cast shadows of highly
stylized figures and objects. If used to aid in the creation of any type of shadow spell or effect
(including [shadow puppet](ref/arc-bard/shadow-puppeteer) bardic performances), a
[wayang](ref/race/wayang) adds a +1 competence bonus to her saving throw DC. Using a shadow stencil
requires one hand.
