**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Lodging/Services

A footman is a house servant in good physical shape, who is dressed in his employer's livery and
capable of running ahead of his employer to make arrangements at a destination (such as an inn or a
noble's mansion). This is a position of lesser rank than a [valet](ref/eq-misc/valet), and is often
a status symbol for the employer, with taller or better-looking employees earning higher wages as
footmen. A skilled and personable footman can hope to eventually become a valet. The listed price is
per day.
