**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 101, Advanced Player's Guide pg 184\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This sweet and wholesome-tasting blue liquid creates a sense of warmth and comfort. Soothe syrup
coats your stomach and makes it much more difficult for you to succumb to queasiness. For 1 hour
after drinking soothe syrup, you gain a +5 alchemical bonus on saving throws made to resist effects
that would make you nauseated or sickened.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
