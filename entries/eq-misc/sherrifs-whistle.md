**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 37\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Used most often by [gnome](ref/race/gnome) sheriffs and rangers, a sheriff's whistle gives off a
piercing shriek that can easily be heard up to 1,000 feet away under ordinary circumstances.
However, the frequency of the shriek is such that it can only be heard by badgers, dogs, and gnomes.
