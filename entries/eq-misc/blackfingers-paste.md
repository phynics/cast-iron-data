**Sources:** Rival Guide pg 53\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

Developed by followers of Norgorber, this inky paste aids unskilled poison-users. When smeared on
the fingers, a dose of blackfingers paste prevents accidental poisoning while applying poison to a
weapon, as if the user possessed the [poison use](ref/ability/poison-use) ability. A single dose of
blackfingers paste lasts for 1d6 hours, but the black stains the stuff creates on the user's fingers
lasts for days. A single dose of this alchemical item costs 50 gp.
