## Rowboat

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 87, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159\
**Price** 50 gp; **Price of Passage** (per mile) 2 cp\
**Category** Transport, Sea

This 8- to 12-foot-long boat with two [oars](ref/eq-misc/oar) holds two or three Medium passengers,
and is either carried on the deck of a larger ship or moored to a dock onshore. A
[rowboat](ref/rule/wv-rowboat) moves about 1-1/2 miles per hour.
