**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 154\
**Price** special; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Most common among [ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk) alchemists, arcane stink ink is used to inscribe
formulae or spells into formula books and spellbooks. Like normal
[stink ink](ref/eq-misc/stink-ink-common), the arcane version can only be read by creatures with the
[scent](ref/umr/scent) ability (although [read magic](ref/spell/read-magic) works normally on spells
and arcane formulae inscribed with stink ink). Using arcane stink ink to inscribe a spell or formula
into a book costs +10% of the normal amount.
