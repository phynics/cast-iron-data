**Sources:** Lost Treasures pg 42\
**Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Black Market

These crystalline fragments are all that remains of an
[orb of dragonkind](ref/magic-artifact/orbs-of-dragonkind) that was destroyed by the great wyrm gold
dragon Mengkare at the outset of his grand experiment. The owner of a shard receives a +2
circumstance bonus on Diplomacy checks with citizens of Hermea. The individual shards are
nonmagical, but many believe that were they to be reunited into a complete sphere, the item would
regain its powers over gold dragons, including Mengkare himself.
