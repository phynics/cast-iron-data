**Sources:** The Harrow Handbook pg 30\
**Price** 80 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Entertainment

This thick leather mat is large enough to fit all the cards necessary for a standard harrow reading.
If you use a harrow mat as part of a reading, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on Bluff or Diplomacy
checks (harrower's choice) to convince the subjects of your harrowing that your words are true.
