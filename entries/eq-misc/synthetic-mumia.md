**Sources:** Undead Slayer's Handbook pg 9\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

A laboratory-created version of the expensive and infamous Osirian drug [mumia](ref/drug/mumia),
synthetic mumia is a mixture of honey, embalming fluids, and various animal organs. The murky liquid
comes enclosed in small packets made of intestine. Mumia and synthetic mumia may be taken
interchangeably to stave off the effects of addiction to either substance, and is thus used by some
mumia junkies hoping to quit their addiction.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 14
