**Sources:** Antihero's Handbook pg 28\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This alchemically concocted black ink can be attuned to a standard sheet of parchment. Anything
inscribed using the ink (anywhere on the same plane of existence) appears on the attuned parchment
in a sparkling blue script that lasts 1 hour. There is enough ink in one vial to fill one sheet of
parchment. The ink cannot duplicate any written magical effects, such as a
[symbol of death](ref/spell/symbol-of-death) (as per the spell of the same name). Crafting a vial of
ink of stolen secrets requires a successful DC 15 Craft (alchemy) check.
