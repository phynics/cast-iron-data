**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

This disguise kit includes metallic or stone-colored makeup and cloth that you can apply to appear
to be a statue; it grants a +2 bonus only on Disguise checks to resemble metal, stone, or similar
materials. You take a -5 penalty on Disguise checks when impersonating an inanimate object. A viewer
can attempt a single Perception check opposed by your Disguise check upon viewing you, and an
additional check each time a creature interacts with you. This kit is exhausted after 10 uses.
