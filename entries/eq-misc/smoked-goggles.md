**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

These spectacles have lenses made of smoked glass that help protect against creatures with
[gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attacks. You are always treated as averting your gaze when dealing with gaze
attacks, and you gain a +8 circumstance bonus on saving throws against visual-based attacks (any
attack that a [blind](ref/misc/blinded) creature would be immune to). You take a -4 penalty on
Perception checks while wearing the goggles, and all opponents are treated as having
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance).
