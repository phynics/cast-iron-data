## Raft

**Price** -; **Price of Passage** (per mile) 1 cp\
**Category** Transport, Sea

The most basic and primitive type of watercraft, a raft is a simple, flat boat with no hull, often
made of logs lashed together. It typically uses two to four [oars](ref/eq-misc/oar) for propulsion.
