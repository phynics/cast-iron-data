**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 203\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A calumet is a two-piece ceremonial smoking pipe with a bowl shaped from stone or clay and an
intricately carved wooden stem decorated with dangling fetishes. The pipe is typically carried in a
special leather pouch festooned with beads, patterns, and trinkets. The pipe is used to smoke
various herbal mixtures required for certain rituals. The communal smoking of a calumet is sometimes
incorporated into diplomatic meetings as a sign of solidarity between various parties. You gain a +1
circumstance bonus on Diplomacy checks against anyone you share your calumet with in this way.
