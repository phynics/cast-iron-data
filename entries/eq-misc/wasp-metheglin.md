**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 4\
**Price** 400 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Food/Drink

A rare breed of wasp produces the honey used to make this spiced, mead-like drink. For 1 hour after
consuming a glass of wasp metheglin (this takes 1 round), the drinker is quick to respond to harm,
gaining a +1 alchemical bonus on attack rolls against any creature that has damaged her with an
attack within the previous round. This bonus does not stack with itself. A typical bottle holds four
glasses' worth of metheglin.
