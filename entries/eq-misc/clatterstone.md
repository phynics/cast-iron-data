**Sources:** Heroes of the Darklands pg 28\
**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

Comprising several chunks of magnetized ore connected by twine, a clatterstone creates an intense
racket when it strikes a solid surface. The combination of magnetic disturbances, noise, and
vibrations interferes with [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight). A creature using
[blindsight](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) perceives a detonated clatterstone as a Medium
target until it attempts to interact with the perceived "creature" or identifies it with some other
sense, such as [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision). You can throw a clatterstone as a ranged attack
with a range increment of 20 feet, and it remains active for 1d4+1 rounds before becoming inert.
