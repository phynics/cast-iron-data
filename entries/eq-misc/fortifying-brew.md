**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 206\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This liquid causes a pleasant warming sensation when consumed. For the next hour, you gain a +2
morale bonus on saving throws against fear. Using more than 1 dose within 24 hours causes you to
become [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 hour.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
