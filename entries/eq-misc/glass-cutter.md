**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This small metal rod has a tiny sharp wheel on one end and a bulb on the other. You score the glass
with the wheel and tap it with the bulb to break it. If you succeed at a DC 15 Sleight of Hand
check, you can break the glass very quietly (DC 15 Perception check to hear). Most thieves use
[glue paper](ref/eq-misc/glue-paper) with a glass cutter to reduce noise and capture glass
fragments.
