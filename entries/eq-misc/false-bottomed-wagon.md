**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 12\
**Price** 375 gp; **Weight** 400 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This [wagon](ref/eq-misc/wagon) has a hollow below its apparent bottom. The hollow is 6 inches deep
and has a capacity of 75 cubic feet. The hollow can be noticed during a search of the wagon with a
successful DC 20 Perception check. The wagon otherwise resembles a normal medium wagon.
