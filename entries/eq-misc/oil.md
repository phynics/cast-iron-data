**Price** 1 sp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A 1-pint flask of oil burns for 6 hours in a lantern or lamp. You can also use a flask of oil as a
splash weapon. Use the rules for [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), except that it
takes a full-round action to prepare a flask with a fuse. Once it is thrown, there is a 50% chance
of the flask igniting successfully.

You can pour a pint of oil on the ground to cover an area 5 feet square, provided that the surface
is smooth. If lit, the oil burns for 2 rounds and deals 1d3 points of fire damage to each creature
in the area.
