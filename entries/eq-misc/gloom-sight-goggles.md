**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 111\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

These nonmagical goggles are set with a piece of alchemically treated, black, obsidian-like stone
found in the mountainous regions of the Shadow Plane. Gloom sight goggles interact with the unique
eyes of [fetchlings](ref/race/fetchling) in such a way that when the goggles are worn over both
eyes, they expand the range of a fetchling's [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to 90 feet, but the
fetchling also gains the [light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity) weakness. Other races cannot
see through the lenses of these goggles, and they have no affect on fetchlings whose eyes have been
modified by the [Gloom Sight](ref/feat/gloom-sight) feat. Though they are alchemical rather than
magical in nature, these goggles take up the magic item eye slot.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
