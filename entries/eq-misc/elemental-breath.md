**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 159\
**Price** 80 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This vial at first seems to be empty, but when it's opened, a rush of wind issues forth from within.
When elemental breath is inhaled (as drinking a potion) by an air-breathing creature, that creature
does not need to breathe air for 10 rounds. A vial of elemental breath must be inhaled on the round
it was opened to gain this effect. If it is not, the item is wasted.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
