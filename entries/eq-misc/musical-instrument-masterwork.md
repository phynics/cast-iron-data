## Musical instrument

**Price** 5 gp (common), 100 gp (masterwork); **Weight** 3 lbs. (common), 3 lbs. (masterwork)

If you want to perform using a Perform skill associated with a musical instrument (keyboard,
percussion, string, or wind), you must have an appropriate musical instrument; otherwise you can't
use the skill. The listed cost and weight is for a typical small instrument you could carry by hand,
such as a drum, lyre, bell, harp, or flute; larger instruments such as a cello or piano are of
course larger and more expensive.

**Masterwork Musical Instrument:** This high-quality instrument grants a +2 circumstance bonus on
Perform checks made to perform with the instrument.
