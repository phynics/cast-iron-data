**Sources:** Blood of the Elements pg 21\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This vibrant, variegated gemstone is found naturally on the Elemental Plane of Earth but can also be
artificially reproduced, albeit with a fair amount of difficulty. The catalyst is highly sought for
its remarkable ability to enhance and alter alchemical tools and weapons.

As a move action, you can apply the catalyst to one of the following items to enhance or alter the
alchemical item's effects. If the item is not used within 3 rounds, the benefit of the enhanced
alchemical item is lost and the item reverts to normal. Once applied to an item, a planar alchemical
catalyst is expended.

**[Alchemist's Fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire):** The type of energy damage changes to acid,
cold, or electricity (user's choice).

**[Flash Powder](ref/eq-weapon/flash-powder):** The DC of the Fortitude save increases to 15, and
targets are [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1d4+1 rounds whether or not they succeed at their saving
throw.

**[Holy Water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water):** The splash area becomes
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) for undead and evil outsiders, and undead or
evil outsiders that end their turn in the affected area take 2 points of damage. Both of these
effects last 3 rounds.

**[Impact Foam](ref/eq-misc/impact-foam):** The foam does not dissolve when exposed to acid or
water, and instead becomes a pliable-yet-rigid platform that can be dragged onto water and used as a
makeshift raft for up to 5 Medium creatures. The 10-minute duration still applies.

**[Liquid Blade](ref/eq-weapon/liquid-blade):** The blade deals 2 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed)
damage on a hit (this does not stack with bleed damage from other sources).

**[Liquid Ice:](ref/eq-weapon/liquid-ice)** In addition to its normal effects, the liquid ice acts
as a [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick), except it emits dense white fog rather than smoke.

**[Shard Gel](ref/eq-weapon/shard-gel):** The shard gel remains embedded in the target on a direct
hit, continuing to deal 1d3 points of damage each round after until the gel dissolves (5 rounds) or
the target takes a full round action to remove it.

**[Tanglefoot Bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag):** The DC of the Reflex saving throw increases to
17, the penalty on attack rolls is -3, and the tanglefoot bag can affect targets up to size category
Huge.

**[Thunderstone](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone):** In addition to being [deafened](ref/misc/deafened),
targets that fail their Fortitude saves are [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. Affected
targets are deafened for 1d4 rounds whether or not they succeed at their saving throw.
