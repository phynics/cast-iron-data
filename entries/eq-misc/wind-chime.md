**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 251\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Made up of a series of suspended bells, tubes, or rods, a wind chime is constructed out of metal or
wood. Often hung outside buildings by the superstitious to deter spirits, a wind chime can also act
as a makeshift alarm.
