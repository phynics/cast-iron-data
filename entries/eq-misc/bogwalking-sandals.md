**Price** 250 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Clothing

These sandals have blackwood soles and leather straps that wrap around the wearer's feet. The
sandals are waterproof and are thus popular among individuals who tread the harsh locales of the
swamps and wastelands beyond Cassomir proper - especially those who are in such environments for
long periods of time without access to the comforts of a skilled cobbler. The sandals' design also
allows the wearer to [move through shallow bogs](ref/rule/marsh-terrain) normally and to treat deep
bogs as if they were shallow bogs. The sandals do not affect the penalty on
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks while in bogs, and creatures wearing the sandals cannot
gain [cover](ref/rule/cover) from deep bogs.
