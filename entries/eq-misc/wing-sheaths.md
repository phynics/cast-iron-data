**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Wing sheaths are protective coverings created from thick fabric or canvas. The sheaths are designed
to tightly cover a pair of wings to keep them hidden beneath a cloak or loose-fitting clothing.
Noticing hidden wings bound within wing sheaths requires a successful DC 20 Perception check.
Donning or removing wing sheaths requires 1 minute, or a full-round action with assistance. Wing
sheaths can be burst with a successful DC 22 Strength check.
