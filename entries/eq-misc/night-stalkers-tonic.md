**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

Once you imbibe this metallic-tasting liquid, its effects last for 1 hour. During this time, you can
accept [alchemical burn](ref/misc/alchemical-burn) to increase your sneak attack damage by 1d6
points or your hidden strike damage by 1d4 or 1d8 points (as appropriate) for 1 round. If you don't
possess the sneak attack or hidden strike abilities, imbibing this tonic has no effect.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
