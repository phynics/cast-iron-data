**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 114\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This alchemically enhanced honey is typically transported in small, lantern-shaped containers
capable of holding up to 3 doses of honey. A single dose of lantern honey functions as normal
[antitoxin](ref/eq-misc/antitoxin), except that it also provides immunity to
[drow poison](ref/eq-poison/drow-poison) for 1 hour after consumption.
