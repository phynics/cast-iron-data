**Sources:** People of the Stars pg 19\
**Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

These devices strap onto a character's legs in order to allow for easier movement in low- or
high-gravity environments. A character wearing leg stabilizers can move and attempt Acrobatics
checks in gravity between ×1 and ×3 as if in gravity ×1. A character wearing them in gravity
stronger than ×1/3 but weaker than ×1 gains a foot circumstance bonus to his land speed. This device
has no effect on other actions. Attaching or removing leg stabilizers is a full-round action.
