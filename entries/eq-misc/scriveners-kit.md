**Price** 2 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Kits

This soft leather case contains a vial for holding [ink](ref/eq-misc/ink), an
[ink pen](ref/eq-misc/inkpen), spare pen nibs, a small container of pigment for making ink, a tiny
knife for cutting quills into pens, a blotter, and a small ruler.
