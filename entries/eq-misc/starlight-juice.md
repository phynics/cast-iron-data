**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 23\
**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This clear liquid glows with pale light. For 4 hours after drinking this tincture, you emit light as
a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch). You are constantly [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) during this time. If
any effect would cause you to become dazzled during this time, you are instead
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded). While under the effect of this tincture, you can intensify your glow as
a swift action to that of bright light, as per [daylight](ref/spell/daylight), for 1 round. After
activating the bright light effect, you emit only dim light with a range of 10 feet for the
remainder of the tincture's duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
