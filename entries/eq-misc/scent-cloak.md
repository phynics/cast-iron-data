**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This collection of coarsely ground spices, seeds, musk, and alchemical reagents are meant to be
rubbed into your skin and clothes in order to override your scent, increasing the DC of tracking you
by scent by +10 for 24 hours. Because you still have a smell, creatures with [scent](ref/umr/scent)
can still detect and pinpoint you if you are cloaked; they just can't identify your smell as
something unique. Washing for 1 full round removes the scent cloak.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
