## Air tank

**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 56\
**Price** 5 sp/ gallon; **Weight** 1/2 lb./gallon\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Tanks full of normal air don't last long as breathing devices, but a large air tank is excellent for
ensuring a character has a swiftly rising [buoyancy](ref/rule/buoyancy), and a savvy diver can
adjust her air tank just enough to give her a neutral buoyancy, making it much easier to explore
underwater. Every 6 gallons of air is enough to breathe for roughly 1 minute underwater; it takes
far less air for a Medium creature to achieve neutral buoyancy unless it's carrying a significant
amount of metal and other dense material.
