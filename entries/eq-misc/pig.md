**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 80, Ultimate Equipment pg 93\
**Price** 3-10 gp; **Weight** 100-300 lbs.\
**Category** Mounts/Pets

Domesticated pigs are the descendents of wild boars, and are raised for their meat and hide, being
among the most versatile and common farm animals.

### Specific Mounts

**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 23\
Each of the following listings details a specific breed of riding animal and provides a trait that
grants an additional boon to the mount or its rider. The trait granted to a mount counts against a
character's total maximum number of traits. If a knight replaces his mount, he effectively loses the
old trait and gains the new mount's trait.

**Warthog:** Hailing from the Mwangi Expanse, these broad-faced boars are considered unusually ugly,
with enormous tusks and large "warts" (in truth, these fleshy facial protrusions are protective
ridges). Despite that, those who have worked with them find warthogs easier to deal with than the
wild boars of more northerly climes, as warthogs are more prone to flight than aggression.
**Associated Trait:** [Running Pig](ref/trait/running-pig).

Warthogs use the stats of [boars](ref/monster/boar) and do not have published prices. They have been
placed in the "pig" entry because of their similar nature. Consult your GM if you wish to buy one.
