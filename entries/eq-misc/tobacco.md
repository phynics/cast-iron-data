**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Black Market

These crushed and shredded leaves range in color from peppery red to black; users can either smoke
or chew them. Tobacco users experience a certain level of calm and are more easily able to shrug off
hunger pangs. Tobacco is addictive (Fort DC 10 to resist), and long-term users suffer
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).
