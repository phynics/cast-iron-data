**Category** Kits

The guard issues a standard set of supplies to each of its members. This kit consists of a
[backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), a [belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), a
[flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel), [manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-common), 50 feet of
[rope](ref/eq-misc/rope-50-ft), a [signal whistle](ref/eq-misc/whistle-signal),
[soap](ref/eq-misc/soap), and 5 [torches](ref/eq-misc/torch).
