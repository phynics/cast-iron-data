**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

When used in conjunction with a [healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit), surgeon's tools raise the
kit's bonus to a +3 circumstance bonus on Heal checks to treat wounds or deadly wounds.
