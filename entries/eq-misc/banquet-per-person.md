**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 95, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Food/Drink

A banquet includes several food courses, good drinks, and servants to bring the food and take away
empty plates. The listed price is for having a banquet at a restaurant (though some restaurant
owners can be hired to serve a banquet at a private location). The price listed above is per person.
