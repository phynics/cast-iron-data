**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Anointing a creature's eyes with a dose of fury drops causes it to immediately fly into a rage for
1d6 rounds, granting it a +2 alchemical bonus to Strength and Constitution, a +1 alchemical bonus on
Will saves, and a -2 penalty to AC. The effect is otherwise identical to a
[barbarian's](ref/class/barbarian) rage, and creatures are [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) at the end
of the rage. Only creatures with an Intelligence score of 1 or 2 can be affected by fury drops.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
