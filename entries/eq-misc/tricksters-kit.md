**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 7\
**Price** 474 gp; **Weight** 23 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

For those brave enough to match wits with draconic intellects, this kit can help a dragonslayer
sneak into or out of a dragon's lair or trick a dragon should the interloper be discovered. It
includes a pot of [alchemical grease](ref/eq-misc/alchemical-grease), a
[board game](ref/eq-misc/board-game), five [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle), a
[candle lamp](ref/eq-misc/candle-lamp), a common [snorkel](ref/eq-misc/snorkel-common),
[fake footprint shoes](ref/eq-misc/fake-footprint-shoes), a
[grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook), a [mirror](ref/eq-misc/mirror), a
[periscope](ref/eq-misc/periscope), a potion of [eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), two
[sacks](ref/eq-misc/sack), a [scent cloak](ref/eq-misc/scent-cloak), 50 feet of
[silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft), a [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick), a dose of
[sneezing powder](ref/eq-weapon/sneezing-powder), five [tindertwigs](ref/eq-misc/tindertwig), and 50
feet of [twine](ref/eq-misc/string-or-twine-50-ft).
