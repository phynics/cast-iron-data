**Sources:** Blood of the Elements pg 27\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This elixir turns its imbiber fiery red, making it easier to pass as an
[efreeti](ref/monster/genie-efreeti). Unscrupulous efreet can sell fireskin elixir to guests who
have been barred from citizen-only districts and must rely on disguises. Fireskin is
[highly addictive](ref/drug/fireskin-elixir). Many once-savvy merchants have fallen into destitution
as they scrambled to maintain efreet favor and secure a steady supply of the drug.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
