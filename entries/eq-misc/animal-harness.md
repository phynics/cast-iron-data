**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 81, Advanced Player's Guide pg 185, Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 2 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Animal Gear

This leather or hemp harness allows a holder to restrain and control a domesticated animal. A
harness is standard equipment for anyone trying to teach an animal tricks with the Handle Animal
skill. Ready-made harnesses are available in most markets for common domesticated animals such as
cats, dogs, horses, and oxen, but harnesses can be made for nearly any creature.
