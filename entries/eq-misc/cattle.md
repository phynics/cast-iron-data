**Price** 10-50 gp; **Weight** 1,500 lbs.\
**Category** Mounts/Pets

Cattle are kept for milk and meat, and used as beasts of burden. A bull is used primarily for
mating. Other males are castrated and become oxen, which are used to carry loads and plow fields.
