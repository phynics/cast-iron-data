**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This ruby-red earth can be sprinkled over a 5-foot patch of ground as a standard action that
provokes attacks of opportunity. Non-creature plants and fungi in the area grow with alarming speed,
and the ground becomes weedy, [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) for non-plant
creatures. Any non-plant creature in the square must succeed at a DC 15 Reflex save or it becomes
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) by the surging plants. Any plant creature in the affected square is
healed of 1d6 points of damage.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (15 magnesium + 20 phosphorus + 10
salt)/calcination; **Craft DC** 20\
**Time** 1 hour; **Tools** [crucible](ref/eq-misc/crucible); **Type** alchemical tool
