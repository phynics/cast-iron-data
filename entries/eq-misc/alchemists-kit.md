**Price** 40 gp; **Weight** 24 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit includes an [alchemy crafting kit](ref/eq-misc/alchemy-crafting-kit), a
[backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), a [bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll), a
[belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), a [flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel),
[ink](ref/eq-misc/ink), an [inkpen](ref/eq-misc/inkpen), an [iron pot](ref/eq-misc/pot-iron), a
[mess kit](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit), [soap](ref/eq-misc/soap), [torches](ref/eq-misc/torch) (10),
[trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) (5 days), and a [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin). The
kit does not contain a formula book because an alchemist begins play with a formula book and does
not need to purchase one.
