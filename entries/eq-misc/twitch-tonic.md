**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 101\
**Price** 45 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This thick syrup is extracted from bitter herbs and enhanced by alchemy to ward against sleep,
paralysis, and the [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) condition. If you drink twitch tonic, you gain a
+2 alchemical bonus on saving throws made against these effects for the next hour. If you drink
twitch tonic while suffering from any of these effects, you may immediately roll another saving
throw against the effect (with the +2 bonus); you may gain this particular benefit only once per
day. You can administer twitch tonic to an unconscious or paralyzed creature as a full-round action,
similar to administering a potion.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
