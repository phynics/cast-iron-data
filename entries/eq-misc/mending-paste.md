**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

You can use this smelly, silvery paste to temporarily repair metal objects. It is sold in a sealed
flask and reacts with air, turning solid in 1d6+4 minutes. You must make an appropriate DC 20 Craft
check to repair a metal item with the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition; success means the object
loses the broken condition but gains the [fragile](ref/misc/fragile) condition. Increase the DC by
+5 if the broken object is a magic item or by +5 if it is a firearm (add both modifiers if the item
is a magical firearm). If the check fails, the mending paste is consumed but you may try again with
another flask or conventional repair methods.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
