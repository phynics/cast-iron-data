**Price** 45 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This volatile gray paste sticks to solid surfaces and creates a concentrated blast when exposed to
fire, making it a valuable excavation tool. You can apply 1 dose of blasting jelly to a 5-foot
square as a standard action. The paste can be ignited with another standard action by striking it
from an adjacent square with a metal weapon or object. When thus ignited or otherwise exposed to
fire, the jelly explodes, dealing 2d6 points of fire damage to creatures and unattended objects
within the treated square. If the ignited square is adjacent to another square containing blasting
jelly, that square ignites as well. Blasting jelly deals full damage to objects made of stone or
softer material. Blasting jelly decays relatively quickly when exposed to air, becoming inert after
4 hours if not ignited.
