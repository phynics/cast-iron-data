**Price** 1 sp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Weapon cords are 2-foot-long leather straps that attach your weapon to your wrist. If you drop your
weapon or are disarmed, you can recover it as a move action, and it never moves any farther away
from you than an adjacent square. However, you cannot switch to a different weapon without first
untying the cord (a full-round action) or cutting it (a move action or an attack,
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 0, 1 hit point). Unlike with a
[locked gauntlet](ref/eq-armorenh/locked-gauntlet), you can still use a hand with a weapon cord,
though a dangling weapon may interfere with finer actions.
