## Monster hunter's kit

**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 27\
**Price** 257 gp (abberation), 224 gp (fey), 232 gp (ooze), 202 gp (regular); **Weight** 15 1/2 lbs.
(abberation), 15 1/2 lbs. (fey), 17 lbs. (ooze), 15 lbs. (regular)

This kit includes a [bear trap](ref/eq-misc/bear-trap), a
[footprint book](ref/eq-misc/footprint-book), a [scent cloak](ref/eq-misc/scent-cloak), and
[monster bait](ref/eq-misc/monster-bait) and an [odor stalk](ref/eq-misc/odor-stalk) keyed to a
specific creature type. The kit also contains the following additional items if designed to hunt the
following creature types.

**Aberration:** The kit contains a dose of [soothe syrup](ref/eq-misc/soothe-syrup) and a dose of
[wismuth salix](ref/eq-misc/wismuth-salix) (+55 gp).

**Fey:** The kit's bear trap is made of [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron), and the kit includes
one dose of [cold iron weapon blanch](ref/eq-misc/weapon-blanch-cold-iron) (+22 gp).

**Ooze:** The kit contains two [alkali flasks](ref/eq-weapon/alkali-flask) (+30 gp).
