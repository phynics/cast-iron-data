**Price** 140 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is useful to [Pathfinders](ref/rule/aff-scholars) who must practice guile and deception in
order to acquire useful information, and includes a set of [caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops),
[chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk), a [disguise kit](ref/eq-misc/disguise-kit), an
[ear trumpet](ref/eq-misc/ear-trumpet), [fake footprint shoes](ref/eq-misc/fake-footprint-shoes), a
[skeleton key](ref/eq-misc/skeleton-key), and a [wrist sheath](ref/eq-misc/wrist-sheath). For Small
creatures, the weight of an infiltration kit is 9 pounds.
