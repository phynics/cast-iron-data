**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 13\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

A brewer's kit includes a mash tun, as well as copper kettles and piping for boiling and cooling
before casking it and allowing it to ferment. It also includes a variety of dried hops and spices
for flavoring.
