**Price** 40 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Clothing

Whether made of sheer gauze or thicker material, a veil is useful for hiding one's facial
expressions. While worn, a veil grants a +2 circumstance bonus on Bluff checks; however, the wearer
treats all other creatures as having [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). The wearer has a 50%
chance to avoid [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attacks, as if averting his eyes.
