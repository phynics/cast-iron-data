**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 116\
**Price** varies; **Price of Passage** (per mile) varies\
**Category** Transport, Land

A smuggler's wagon functions in most respects exactly like an ordinary wagon, except it contains
hidden compartments that allow for cargo to be transported in secret. The price of a smuggler's
wagon depends on two factors - the size or type of the original [wagon](ref/eq-misc/wagon), and the
skill with which the hidden compartments have been constructed. The table below details the prices
of such a vehicle (the DC is for a Perception check to successfully locate the hidden compartments
during a search of the wagon).

|Size|DC 15|DC 20|DC 25|
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|Light|75 gp|100 gp|150 gp|
|Medium|115 gp|150 gp|225 gp|
|Heavy|150 gp|200 gp|300 gp|

Compartments can be located throughout the wagon, in such places as a hollowed-out axle, beneath the
seats, between panels on the floor decking, and so forth. The total amount of weight the wagon and
its draft animals can accommodate remains the same as the original variety of wagon.
