**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Channel Foci\
**Deity** Any

Each carefully forged link of these [masterwork manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-masterwork) (with a
[good lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-good)) contains a parable or other quotes excerpted from sacred texts.
Activating the focus increases its Disable Device, Escape Artist, and break DCs by 5; its
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) increases by 2 and its hit points by 4. This effect lasts 24 hours or
until the creature frees itself or is released.
