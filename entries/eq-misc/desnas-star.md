**Sources:** Heroes of the Wild pg 10\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** [Herbs](ref/rule/herbalism)\
**Gather** DC 13\
**Terrain** forests or plains

Clerics of [Desna](ref/faith/desna) claim that the Great Dreamer planted the first seed of this
flower with her own hand. When the orange petals are cured and left to steep in hot water, the
leaves of Desna's star make a mild, aromatic tea that facilitates restful sleep. When taken before
sleeping, this tea grants the drinker the benefits of a full 8 hours of uninterrupted sleep in only
6 hours. One dose of Desna's star makes enough tea to serve six.
