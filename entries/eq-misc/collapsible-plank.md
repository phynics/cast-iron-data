**Price** 4 sp; **Weight** 10 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Hinged in two places, collapsible planks can hold 250 pounds before breaking. The 10-foot-long plank
folds down into a 3-foot-by-1-foot-by-6-inch bundle. Folding or unfolding it is a standard action.
