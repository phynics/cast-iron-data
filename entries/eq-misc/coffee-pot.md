**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56\
**Price** 3 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This tall, teapot-like device contains a small chamber for coffee grounds and a large chamber for
water, connected by a small tube. Heating the pot forces boiling water through the tube and into the
grounds. A glass knob at the top of the tube allows you to see the color of the brew and stop when
it is sufficiently strong. It can brew up to 4 [cups of coffee](ref/eq-misc/coffee-cup) at a time.
It can also be used to make tea, steep medicinal herbs, or just boil water.
