**Price** 1,665 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This wooden box contains the tools to interrogate a captive with ease. The kit includes
[masterwork manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-masterwork) with an
[average lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-average), a vial of [gravelly tonic](ref/eq-misc/gravelly-tonic), 2
[elixirs of truth](ref/magic-wondrous/elixir-of-truth), and a scroll of
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts). In case other methods fail, the kit also includes a
scroll of [speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead).
