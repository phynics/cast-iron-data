## Holy symbol (flask)

**Price** +10 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This version of a standard holy symbol is oversized and has a watertight compartment that can hold 1
pint (the same volume as a flask of [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water)). Adventurers normally
use the compartment to hold holy water or alcohol. A holy symbol flask is not meant for you to throw
at enemies and is not fragile like a flask or vial. Like using holy water on incorporeal opponent,
you unstopper the flask and pour the contents onto an adjacent target. Detecting the compartment
requires a successful DC 10 Perception check.
