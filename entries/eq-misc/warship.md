**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 87, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159\
**Price** 25,000 gp; **Price of Passage** (per mile) 2 sp\
**Category** Transport, Sea

This 100-foot-long ship has a single mast, although oars can also propel it. It has a crew of 60 to
80 rowers. This [ship](ref/rule/wv-warship) can carry 160 soldiers, but not for long distances,
since there isn't room for supplies to support that many people. A warship cannot make sea voyages
and sticks to the coast. It is not used for cargo. It moves about 2-1/2 miles per hour when being
rowed or under sail.
