**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 30\
**Price** 80 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

These pouch-like boots come in sets of four, and can be placed on an animal's paws and fastened with
a drawstring. The soles are sewn with treated leather shaped like human footprints that allow the
wearer to leave human tracks. A successful DC 20 Survival or Knowledge (nature) check allows an
individual to recognize the tracks as fake but gives no clue as to the identity of the real
perpetrator.
