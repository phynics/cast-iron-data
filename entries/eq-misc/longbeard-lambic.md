**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 13\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

One of the oldest known dwarven ales, longbeard lambic is fermented with wild airborne yeasts and
cultures, especially those that proliferate in the dank caves where it has been brewed since long
before the Quest for Sky began. Longbeard lambic has a bracing effect that shocks the system. If the
drinker is [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued), [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), or
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered), she can ignore these conditions for 1d4 rounds after imbibing
longbeard lambic. In addition, a dose of longbeard lambic can reduce the
[exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) condition to fatigued or the [frightened](ref/misc/frightened)
condition to shaken (drinker's choice) for the same duration. If you consume a second longbeard
lambic within 1 minute of the previous dose, you must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or be
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1d4 rounds. The DC increases by 5 for each dose imbibed within
the same time span. Dwarves gain a +5 racial bonus on this saving throw.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (3 gold + 5 quicksilver + 4 spirit of
wine)/fermentation; **Craft DC** 18\
**Time** 1 week; **Tools** [brewer's kit](ref/eq-misc/brewers-kit); **Type** alcohol
