**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A hanging tent is a [small tent](ref/eq-misc/tent-small) (able to comfortably house a single Medium
creature) with a teardrop shape, which hangs from a sturdy anchor rope. The rope can be tied to a
tree limb, grappling hook, or even a piton hammered into a cliff side. Hanging tents are used
primarily in mountainous terrain (where no flat surface may be available for camping) and swamps
(where no dry ground may be available). Climbing in or out of a hanging tent is a full-round action
that requires a DC 10 Climb check. Failure indicates another attempt must be made, while failure by
5 or more indicates the camper has fallen from the tent.
