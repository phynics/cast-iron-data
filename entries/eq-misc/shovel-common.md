## Shovel or Spade

**Price** 2 gp (common), 10 gp (folding); **Weight** 8 lbs. (common), 12 lbs. (folding)

This tool lets you dig a pit at a rate of 2 cubic feet per minute. If a shovel is used in combat,
treat it as a one-handed [improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) that deals bludgeoning
damage equal to that of a [club](ref/eq-weapon/club) of its size.

**Folding Shovel:** A folding shovel is a full-sized shovel with a sturdy sectional handle that
folds into pieces no longer than the shovel blade, allowing it to fit in a smaller space such as a
backpack. Converting the shovel from its folded state to usable (or vice versa) is 2 full-round
actions.

> _Adventurer's Armory pg. 20 lists this as costing 2cp and weighing 3lbs. This is probably an
> error._
