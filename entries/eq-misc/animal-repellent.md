**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 24\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Animal Gear

Leshys and other plant creatures have to be wary of hungry herbivorous animals. A dose of animal
repellent makes a plant creature smell unappetizing for 24 hours. Animals do not attempt to consume
a plant creature coated in animal repellent if there are other food sources available, and in combat
prefer to use natural attacks that are not bite attacks against such a creature if they can. Animal
repellent counts as a strong scent, allowing creatures with the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability to
detect it at a greater range. Animal repellent does not work on [ghorans](ref/race/ghoran) with the
delicious racial trait. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 20 Craft (alchemy) check.
