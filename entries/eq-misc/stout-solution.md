**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 23\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This highly viscous liquid resembles tree sap and is difficult to swallow. For 2 hours after
drinking this tincture, you gain a +2 alchemical bonus to your CMD and you can perform
[combat maneuvers](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) as if you were one size category larger. Your
skeleton's increased density causes you to be treated as if you were
[carrying](ref/rule/carrying-capacity) a medium load, or a heavy load if you were already encumbered
by a medium load, for the same duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
