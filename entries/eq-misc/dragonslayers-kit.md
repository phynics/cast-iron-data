**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 47\
**Price** 485 gp; **Weight** 64 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

Aimed more at hired blades than seasoned adventurers, this kit provides supplemental equipment for
outfitting hirelings to hunt dragons. It includes a potion of
[cure moderate wounds](ref/spell/cure-moderate-wounds), oil of
[bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon) (1 application), a [longspear](ref/eq-weapon/longspear), a
[backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), three large [sacks](ref/eq-misc/sack), a
[sunrod](ref/eq-misc/sunrod), a [tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield), and 5 vials of
[alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire). For hunting dragons that are immune to fire, the
alchemist's fire can be replaced with [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid) at the time of purchase, for a
savings of 50 gp.
