**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 5\
**Price** 1000 gp; **Weight** 127 1/2 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

If not descended from nobility, the questing knight certainly looks like he fits the part. This
equipment package is useful for cavaliers, fighters, and paladins. If the PC can cast spells, add a
[spell component pouch](ref/eq-misc/spell-component-pouch) and a
[holy symbol](ref/eq-misc/holy-symbol) (if required). If the PC gains a mount from a class feature,
remove the horse from the list below and replace the lance with a masterwork lance.

**Armor:** [Half-plate](ref/eq-armor/half-plate),
[heavy wooden shield](ref/eq-armor/heavy-wooden-shield).

**Weapons:** [Javelins](ref/eq-weapon/javelin) (4), [lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance),
[longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword).

**Combat Gear:** [Sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod) (2).

**Other Gear:** [Backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), [banner](ref/eq-misc/banner),
[bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll), [belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), [flask](ref/eq-misc/flask),
[flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel), [mess kit](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit),
[pole](ref/eq-misc/pole) (10 ft.), [sack](ref/eq-misc/sack), [shovel](ref/eq-misc/shovel-common),
[signet ring](ref/eq-misc/signet-ring), [silk rope (50 ft.)](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft),
[trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) (5 days), [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin),
[whetstone](ref/eq-misc/whetstone), 8 gp.

**Mount:** [Heavy horse (combat trained)](ref/eq-misc/horse-heavy-combat-trained) with
[bit and bridle](ref/eq-misc/bit-and-bridle), [military saddle](ref/eq-misc/military-saddle), and
[saddlebags](ref/eq-misc/saddlebags).

**Total Weight:** 127-1/2 lbs. (73-3/4 lbs. for a Small character).
