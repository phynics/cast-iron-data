**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 57, Ultimate Combat pg 141, Inner Sea World Guide pg 293\
**Price** 3 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Typically crafted from hollowed-out animal horn, but increasingly crafted from metal in a wide
variety of shapes, a powder horn can hold up to 10 doses of
[black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose). A powder horn protects black powder stored within
in it from exposure to fire, electricity, firearm misfires, and water, and the narrow end of the
horn is useful in funneling the expensive powder carefully into the barrel of a firearm.
