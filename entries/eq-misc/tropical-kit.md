**Sources:** Heroes of the Wild pg 9\
**Price** 120 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is intended for hot and moist environments such as jungles and warm marshes. It includes 1
dose each of [antiplague](ref/eq-misc/antiplague) and [antitoxin](ref/eq-misc/antitoxin), five
[fishhooks](ref/eq-misc/fishhook), a [hammock](ref/eq-misc/hammock), a [kukri](ref/eq-weapon/kukri),
1 dose of [vermin repellent](ref/eq-misc/vermin-repellent), and a
[water purification sponge](ref/eq-misc/water-purification-sponge).
