## Thieves' tool extenders

**Sources:** Dungeoneer's Handbook pg 25\
**Price** 70 gp (common), 820 gp (mithral); **Weight** 3 lbs. (common), 1 1/2 lbs. (mithral)

These long-reaching extenders can be attached to or detached from
[thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools) as a standard action, allowing the user to make Disable
Device checks on devices in any space he could reach with a reach weapon. High-quality mirrors on
the shafts let him see what he's doing, but he takes a -4 penalty on Disable Device checks when
working on a device more than 5 feet away.
