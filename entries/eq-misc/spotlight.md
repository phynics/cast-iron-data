**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 27\
**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** 18 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This large contraption functions like a [hooded lantern](ref/eq-misc/hooded-lantern), shedding large
amounts of light in a single direction. A spotlight also contains several mirrors which amplify and
aim the light. A spotlight creates a 5-foot-wide beam of normal light, which extends to a distance
of 90 feet and continues for another 90 feet as [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light). It does not
radiate any light outside the area of the beam. A spotlight does not increase the light level in
normal light or bright light. A lantern burns for 1 hour on 1 pint of oil, and requires two hands to
move and one hand to aim.
