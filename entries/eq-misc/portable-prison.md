**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 300 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit for a [wagon](ref/eq-misc/wagon) provides a set of metal bars with a door for transporting
imprisoned people or creatures. Though portable prisons were originally developed by traveling acts
to hold ferocious animals, city guards commonly use them to round up criminals, and some bounty
hunters hire them to transport large groups of prisoners. Most prisons come with
[locks](ref/eq-misc/lock-average); add the cost of the desired lock to the cost of the portable
prison. A cage meant for people includes benches and rails for manacles. One meant for animals
contains a trough for water and a smaller door for providing food.
