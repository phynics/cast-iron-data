**Sources:** Faiths of Balance pg 25\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Category** Channel Foci\
**Deity** [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria)

This whip is made from braids of metallic wire. When it is activated, the wielder can expend the
power within it to gain a sacred (if activated with positive energy) or profane (if activated with
negative energy) bonus on her next disarm or trip combat maneuver with the whip. This bonus is equal
to the number of dice of the activating cleric's [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy)
ability. The whip remains charged until its magic is used or 1 hour has passed.
