**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 103, Advanced Player's Guide pg 184\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This hand-sized metal plate is covered with a thin layer of light-sensitive transparent paste. If
exposed to light, the paste darkens and becomes opaque, depending on the amount of light. Bright
light causes it to fully darken in 1 round, normal light in 3 rounds, and
[dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in 10 rounds. It is mainly used by creatures with
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to determine whether creatures carrying light have recently passed
through an area. The plate is sold wrapped in a thick black cloth to prevent accidental light
exposure from ruining the plate.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 10
