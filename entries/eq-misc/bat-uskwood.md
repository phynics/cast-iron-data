## Bat

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 80, Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 5 gp, 50 gp (Uskwood); **Weight** 1 lb., 2 lbs. (Uskwood)

These Diminutive rodents are nocturnal fliers that eat insects and fruit. They rely primarily on
sound to navigate rather than sight. They can be found all over Golarion and are absent only in
desert and arctic regions. Some cultures keep bat houses near towns and villages to control insect
problems. Bats from the Uskwood in Nidal are noted for their matte black fur and large size.
