**Price** 121 gp; **Weight** 38 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is ideal for adventurers who venture into sunken ruins or half-flooded temples, and
includes an [air bladder](ref/eq-misc/air-bladder), a common [buoy](ref/eq-misc/buoy), 30 feet of
[chain](ref/eq-misc/chain-10-ft), a [fishing net](ref/eq-misc/fishing-net), a
[glass bottle](ref/eq-misc/bottle), a pound of [soap](ref/eq-misc/soap), three
[sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod), a [waterproof bag](ref/eq-misc/waterproof-bag), and a
[waterproof hooded lantern](ref/eq-misc/hooded-lantern) with 3 flasks of [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil).
