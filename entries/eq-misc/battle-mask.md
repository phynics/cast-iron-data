**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Clothing

Made from wood, bone, or similar materials, this mask covers its wearer's actual appearance and
identity by depicting a hateful, leering face instead. Because of a battle mask's excellent
craftsmanship and exquisite details, the wearer gains a +1 bonus on Intimidate checks made to
demoralize an opponent.
