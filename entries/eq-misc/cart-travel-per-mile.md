## Cart

**Price** 15 gp; **Price of Passage** (per mile) 1 cp\
**Category** Transport, Land

This two-wheeled vehicle can be drawn by a single horse or other beast of burden, and is often used
to transport goods across short distances. It comes with a [harness](ref/eq-misc/animal-harness).
