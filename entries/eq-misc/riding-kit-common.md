## Riding kit

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 57, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 47\
**Price** 16 gp (common), 36 gp (exotic mount); **Weight** 54 lbs. (common), 59 lbs. (exotic mount)

This kit includes a [bit and bridle](ref/eq-misc/bit-and-bridle), a
[saddle](ref/eq-misc/saddle-riding), a [saddle blanket](ref/eq-misc/blanket),
[saddlebags](ref/eq-misc/saddlebags), and 2 days' worth of [feed](ref/eq-misc/feed-per-day) for a
mount. The weight can be lightened 10 pounds by discarding the feed.
