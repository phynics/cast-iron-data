## Holy symbol (compartment)

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 57\
**Price** +5 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Designed and approved by a member of the appropriate religion, this modification to a holy symbol
creates a secret compartment large enough to hold a potion vial. Detecting the compartment requires
a DC 20 Perception check.
