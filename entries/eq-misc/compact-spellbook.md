**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 27\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Tools

The need to be able to record and travel with dozens or even hundreds of spells often forces elven
wizards to seek lighter spellbooks. Compact spellbooks hold only 70 pages of spells, but they weigh
significantly less than an ordinary [spellbook](ref/eq-misc/spellbook).
