**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 23\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

Gravel floats in this umber liquid. For 1 hour after drinking this tincture, you gain a +1
alchemical bonus to natural armor and a +2 alchemical bonus on Strength checks and Strength-based
skill checks. The tonic swells your musculature, decreasing your movement speed by 5 feet, to a
minimum of 5 feet, and causing you to take a -4 penalty on Reflex saving throws for the same
duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
