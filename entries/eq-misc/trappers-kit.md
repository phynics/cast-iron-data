**Sources:** Dungeoneer's Handbook pg 7\
**Price** 263 gp; **Weight** 90 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is particularly useful for dungeon explorers who specialize in trapping or taming whatever
vile quarry they find. A trapper's kit contains an [average lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-average), a
[bear trap](ref/eq-misc/bear-trap), a container of [bone paste](ref/eq-misc/bone-paste), a
[Small cage](ref/eq-misc/cage), a set of [manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-common), a
[bag of marbles](ref/eq-misc/marbles), 50 feet of [silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft), two
[tanglefoot bags](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag), 50 feet of
[twine](ref/eq-misc/string-or-twine-50-ft), and a [wire](ref/eq-misc/wire).
