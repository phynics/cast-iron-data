**Price** 1-20 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This is a banner, flag, or pennant. You tie it to a pole, lance, or polearm. Most are woven, dyed,
or painted with a pattern or symbol, such as a knight's crest or a country's flag. A simple banner
with one field color and a simple insignia such as a weapon or shield costs 1 gp. A banner with two
to four field colors and a complex insignia such as a lion or dragon costs 5 gp. A detailed banner
with four or more field colors and a very complex insignia, such as a heraldic device with eight or
more sections, costs 20 gp.
