**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 12\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 26 lbs.\
**Category** Animal Gear

This kit includes a [dandy brush](ref/eq-misc/dandy-brush),
[training harness](ref/eq-misc/training-harness), [training whip](ref/eq-misc/whip-training),
[whet bone](ref/eq-misc/whet-bone), and 2 days' worth of
[carnivore feed](ref/eq-misc/carnivore-feed-per-day). The weight of this kit can be reduced by 10
pounds by forgoing the carnivore feed.
