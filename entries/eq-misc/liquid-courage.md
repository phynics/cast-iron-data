**Price** 175 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This crimson liquid roils when held. For 1 hour after drinking this tincture, you gain immunity to
effects that would cause you to become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken); you are still susceptible to
becoming [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked). The drink clouds your
judgment, imposing a -2 penalty on Wisdom-based skill checks for the same duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
