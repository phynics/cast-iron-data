**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 242\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This vestment works just like a [cold-weather outfit](ref/eq-misc/cold-weather-outfit), granting a
+5 circumstance bonus on Fortitude saving throws against exposure to cold weather, except it is
custom made for an [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion). The added cost represents
custom tailoring to ensure that the furs used don't cause unwanted reactions from the animal wearing
the outfit.
