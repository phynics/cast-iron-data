**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 50\
**Price** varies; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Generally stored in a glass vial, an antidote is formulated to counteract a specific poison chosen
at the time of the antidote's creation. Drinking the antidote grants a +10 alchemical bonus on
saving throws against that poison for 4 hours. If the imbiber succeeds at a saving throw with this
bonus, she is automatically cured even if she would not normally be cured by successful saving
throws or the number required to cure her has not been met yet. However, the antidote has no effect
against other poisons. An antidote's price is half that of the poison it counteracts. An antidote
has no effect against a magical poison. The Craft (alchemy) DC to create an antidote is equal to the
save DC of the poison.
