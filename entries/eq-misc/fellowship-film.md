**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 236\
**Price** 190 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

By eating this thin sheet of glossy gelatin, you gain 1d4 uses of
[alchemical inspiration](ref/misc/alchemical-inspiration) that can be used only on Diplomacy checks.
The benefits of fellowship film last for 1 hour, during which you must roll all Intimidate checks
twice and take the worse result.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
