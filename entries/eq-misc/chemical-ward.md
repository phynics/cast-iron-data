**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 26\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This gray paste congeals when spread onto a surface, gradually changing color to match its
surroundings. When applied, its reagents bond with chemicals in the environment, exuding a magnified
aura that can fool [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) and [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense). This
affects only [blindsight](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) or blindsense based on smell, taste,
vibration, and similar senses; it does not affect extraordinary sight, hearing, or
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense). Applying a flask of chemical ward to the edge of a 5-foot square
takes a full-round action, creating an opaque barrier against blindsense and blindsight that lasts
for 1 hour. A creature can sense through the barrier with a successful Perception check (DC 15 for
blindsight, 20 for blindsense), although individuals on the opposite side of the barrier can attempt
Stealth checks against the creature's blindsight or blindsense as though they had
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment). The barrier does not inhibit movement.
