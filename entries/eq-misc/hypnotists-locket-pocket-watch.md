## Hypnotist's locket

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 249\
**Price** 15 gp (locket), 250 gp (pocket watch); **Weight** 1 lb. (locket), 1 lb. (pocket watch)

A small, elliptical metal case containing a small portrait or keepsake, a hypnotist's locket can be
attached to a belt or worn as a pendant. When hanging from a metal chain, a hypnotist's locket
grants a +2 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy checks for the [hypnotism](ref/skill/diplomacy) occult
skill unlock.

A hypnotist's locket can also come in the form of a [pocket watch](ref/eq-misc/pocket-watch). It can
be worn the same way, still includes a space for a portrait or keepsake, and adds the same bonus for
hypnotism, but it also functions as a timepiece.
