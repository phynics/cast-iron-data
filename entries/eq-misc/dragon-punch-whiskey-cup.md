## Dragon Punch Whiskey

**Sources:** Halflings of Golarion pg 10\
**Price** 10 gp/cup; **Weight** -\
**Category** Food/Drink

Non-halflings do not receive any bonus from this drink. According to legend, the inventor of this
dark and harsh liquor actually did attack a dragon bare handed (and presumably lived to pass on the
recipe). [Halflings](ref/race/halfling) who drink four cups of Dragon Punch Whiskey in the space of
an hour take a -4 penalty to Dexterity but gain a +4 alchemical bonus on saves against fear for the
next 10 minutes.
