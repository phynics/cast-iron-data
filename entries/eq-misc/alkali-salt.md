**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

These tiny granules can be dissolved in water, creating a slimy material used to coat metallic items
such as weapons or armor. Such a coating neutralizes the metal-eating acid of
[black puddings](ref/monster/black-pudding) and [gray oozes](ref/monster/gray-ooze), protecting the
item from 1d3 contacts with an ooze's acidic touch.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
