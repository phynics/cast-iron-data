**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Applying this herbal salve to a [bleeding](ref/rule/bleed) wound cures 1 point of damage and
prevents additional damage from bleeding for 1 hour per application. After an hour, if the bleed
effect hasn't been properly treated, the injury resumes bleeding and more salve must be applied.
Though blood-clotter salve can be applied successively to the same injury, applying multiple doses
doesn't cure additional damage.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
