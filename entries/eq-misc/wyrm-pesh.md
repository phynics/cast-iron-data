**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 19\
**Price** 500 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Dragoncraft

This rare and expensive variant of standard Garundi [pesh](ref/drug/pesh) is produced by introducing
a vial of [dragon's blood](ref/eq-misc/dragons-blood) into a dose of pesh during the refining
process, and has a noticeable reddish tint. While the drug provides a more potent high and fewer
immediate penalties, it is [highly addictive](ref/drug/wyrm-pesh), and unless a user has a steady
supply of dragon's blood, the cost to her well-being can be severe.
