**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 158\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A typical leather or cloth belt pouch has a leather cord to cinch it shut and another to tie it to
your belt. It is large enough to hold 100-200 coins or two apples.
