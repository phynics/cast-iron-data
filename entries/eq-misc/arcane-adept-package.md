**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 4\
**Price** 1000 gp; **Weight** 45 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

The arcane adept has collected useful magical gear to ensure her success on her adventures, and she
prides herself on having just the right tool for the job. This equipment package is appropriate for
an arcanist, sorcerer, witch, or wizard. Some bards and summoners might also find it attractive.
This equipment package can also work for a magus if the 1st-level scrolls and wand are replaced with
a masterwork melee weapon.

**Weapons:** [Light crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/light-crossbow) with 10
[bolts](ref/eq-weapon/crossbow-bolts), [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff).

**Combat Gear:** [Acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid) (2), scrolls of
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages) (2), scroll of
[detect secret doors](ref/spell/detect-secret-doors), scroll of
[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust), scrolls of [identify](ref/spell/identify) (2), scroll of
[mount](ref/spell/mount), scroll of [rope trick](ref/spell/rope-trick),
[thunderstone](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone), wand of [mage armor](ref/spell/mage-armor) (16 charges).

**Other Gear:** [Backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack), [bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll),
[belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch), [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle) (5), [chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk)
(5 pieces), [flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel), [ink](ref/eq-misc/ink),
[inkpen](ref/eq-misc/inkpen), [ioun torch](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-torch),
[journal](ref/eq-misc/journal), [magnifying glass](ref/eq-misc/magnifying-glass),
[mess kit](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit), [scroll box](ref/eq-misc/scroll-box),
[silk rope (50 ft.)](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft),
[spell component pouch](ref/eq-misc/spell-component-pouch),
[trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) (5 days), [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin), 3 gp.

**Total Weight:** 45 lbs. (28-3/4 lbs. for a Small character).
