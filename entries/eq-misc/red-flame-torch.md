**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 246\
**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This otherwise normal-looking [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) is encrusted with lithium salts and burns
with a dark red flame. It sheds only [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in a 10-foot radius, and
the light it casts is undetectable outside this radius by normal vision,
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), or [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision). This torch
burns for 1 hour.
