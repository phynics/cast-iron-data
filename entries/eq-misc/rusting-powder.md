**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 103\
**Price** 60 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

This flaky brown powder derived from [rust monster](ref/monster/rust-monster) fluids causes iron and
similar metals to corrode and fall apart. If you apply a dose of rusting powder to a metal lock or
trap as part of using the Disable Device skill, you gain a +5 alchemical bonus to open the lock or
disable the trap, but there is a 75% chance that the mechanism is destroyed and cannot be used
afterward. If the check fails, the mechanism is destroyed. A destroyed lock cannot be unlocked (but
still counts as locked for the purpose opening the locked object). A destroyed trap mechanism
immediately triggers the trap. If you fail the DC by 5 or more, the powder also lands on an object
in your square (including possibly your armor or weapon), dealing 5 hit points of damage to the
object.

Rusting powder is sold in a paper tube; you apply it by tearing the ends off the tube and blowing
the flakes into the target device. Rusting powder does not affect [gold](ref/eq-material/gold),
silver, copper, [bronze](ref/eq-material/bronze), brass, or [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral), but
easily affects iron, steel, and [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine).

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
