**Price** 644 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

Pits, fissures, and errant magic might unexpectedly drop a traveler into underground tunnels. This
backpack contains a variety of tools to survive an accidental subterranean expedition, including
[chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk), a [climber's kit](ref/eq-misc/climbers-kit), a
[compass](ref/eq-misc/compass), a [grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook), a
[hammer](ref/eq-misc/hammer), 5 [pitons](ref/eq-misc/piton), 100 feet of
[silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft), and an [everburning torch](ref/eq-misc/everburning-torch).
The kit also contains a potion of [darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision) in the event light would draw
unwanted attention. Since oozes are a common underground hazard, the kit also includes 2
[alkali flasks](ref/eq-weapon/alkali-flask) and a dose of [bladeguard](ref/eq-misc/bladeguard).
Finally, a guidebook provides helpful information about underground flora and fauna, granting a +2
circumstance bonus on Knowledge (dungeoneering) checks to identify the abilities and weaknesses of
creatures encountered in that terrain.
