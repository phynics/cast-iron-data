## Dire animal

**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 15\
**Price** Varies; **Weight** -\
**Category** Mounts/Pets

Dire animals are all but untamable, and are typically only sought out by violent brawlers or cruel
lords, either for brutish protection or to pit against equally vicious creatures in violent animal
fights. At the GM's discretion, PCs who acquire dire animals may be required to attempt wild empathy
or Handle Animal checks every day to keep their pets from running away or attacking them and their
allies. Dire animals are not generally suitable as mounts (with the notable exception of the
[dire bat](ref/eq-misc/dire-bat)), though the GM may make exceptions at her discretion.
