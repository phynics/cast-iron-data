**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 22\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This tincture contains powdered plant roots and comes in [cold-weather](ref/rule/cold-dangers) and
[hot-weather](ref/rule/heat-dangers) varieties. For 6 hours after drinking the tincture, you gain a
+5 alchemical bonus on Fortitude saving throws to resist the effects of the chosen temperature. The
tincture interferes with blood flow, causing you to take a -2 penalty on all other Fortitude saving
throws for the same duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
