**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 117\
**Price** 12 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Tools

This curved wicker basket is worn like a glove over one hand. The basket extends approximately 2
feet in a steady curve, allowing you to launch a thrown splash weapon at incredible speeds and over
long distances. Using a bombchucker increases the range increment of any thrown splash weapon by 10
feet. Loading a bombchucker requires a free hand, as the hand wielding the bombchucker can't be used
for any other purpose. A bombchucker doesn't get in the way of preparing and throwing a bomb created
with the bomb class feature.
