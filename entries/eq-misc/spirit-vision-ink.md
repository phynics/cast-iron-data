**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 28\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

Originally developed by kami-revering priests to write prayer scrolls, this pale ink is now often
used by ninjas to mark locations or leave messages. Once spirit-vision ink dries, it becomes
completely invisible. To invisible creatures, however, writing in spirit-vision ink shimmers with a
rich azure hue.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
