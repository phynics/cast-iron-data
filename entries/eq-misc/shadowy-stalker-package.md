**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 5\
**Price** 1000 gp; **Weight** 63 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

Skulking through a city or a dungeon, the shadowy stalker is equipped to strike quickly and fade
away. This equipment package is appropriate for stealthy characters such as rangers, rogues, and
slayers, and for some bards and investigators. If the PC can cast spells, add a spell component
pouch.

**Armor:** [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-armor) [leather armor](ref/eq-armor/leather).

**Weapons:** [Daggers](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) (3), [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons)
[sickle](ref/eq-weapon/sickle), [sap](ref/eq-weapon/sap).

**Combat Gear:** [Alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire) (3),
[smokesticks](ref/eq-misc/smokestick) (2), [sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod) (2).

**Other Gear:** [Bedroll](ref/eq-misc/bedroll), [belt pouch](ref/eq-misc/belt-pouch),
[caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops), [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle) (5), [chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk) (5
pieces), [disguise kit](ref/eq-misc/disguise-kit), [flint and steel](ref/eq-misc/flint-and-steel),
[glass cutter](ref/eq-misc/glass-cutter), [masterwork backpack](ref/eq-misc/backpack-masterwork),
[masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork), [mess kit](ref/eq-misc/mess-kit),
[sack](ref/eq-misc/sack), [silk rope (50 ft.)](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft),
[sleeves of many garments](ref/magic-wondrous/sleeves-of-many-garments),
[trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) (5 days), [waterskin](ref/eq-misc/waterskin), 1 gp.

**Total Weight:** 63 lbs. (33-1/4 lbs. for a Small character).
