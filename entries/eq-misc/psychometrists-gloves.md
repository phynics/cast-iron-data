**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Category** Tools

These layered lambskin gloves are specially designed and psychically attuned to increase the tactile
sensitivity of the wearer's hands. To provide any benefit, the gloves must be worn for 8
uninterrupted hours. All Dexterity skill checks requiring the use of the hands take a -2 penalty as
long as the gloves remain on. After this 8-hour period, the user can remove the gloves to gain a +2
circumstance bonus on Appraise checks for the [psychometry](ref/skill/appraise) occult skill unlock.
This benefit fades after 1 hour.
