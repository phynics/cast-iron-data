**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

This tablet of compressed herbs and potent alchemical reagents can purify tainted, brackish, muddy,
or alkaline water in order to make it drinkable. If used to purify water that is diseased or
poisonous, each tablet reduces the save DC by 1 (to a maximum reduction of 5) for any disease or
poison present in the water. A single purification pellet can affect up to 1 gallon of tainted
water.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
