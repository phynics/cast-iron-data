**Price** 27 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit is designed for use in cold climates, where the weather can be deadly and the terrain
treacherous. It includes 10 [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle), a [candle lamp](ref/eq-misc/candle-lamp),
[cleats](ref/eq-misc/cleats), a [cold-weather outfit](ref/eq-misc/cold-weather-outfit), a
[handaxe](ref/eq-weapon/handaxe), a [shovel](ref/eq-misc/shovel-common), a
[signal whistle](ref/eq-misc/whistle-signal), a small [iron pot](ref/eq-misc/pot-iron),
[snowshoes](ref/eq-misc/snowshoes), and five [tindertwigs](ref/eq-misc/tindertwig), all wrapped in a
winter [blanket](ref/eq-misc/blanket).
