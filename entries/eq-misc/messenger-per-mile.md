## Messenger

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 99, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 159\
**Price** 2 cp per mile; **Weight** -\
**Category** Lodging/Services

A messenger carries a written or verbal message to its intended target. Verbal messages must be
short enough for the messenger to memorize (typically about 25 words). A few messengers have
exceptional memories and charge 5 sp to relay a verbal message of up to 300 words. Most messengers
are only willing to travel to locations within the current city or outlying areas. You can pay a
messenger the return fee if you want her to wait for the message recipient to reply, just in case
the recipient is too miserly or poor to pay for a return message. Depending on the location and the
overall wealth in a settlement, a messenger may be a runner or ride a horse. If you can find someone
already traveling to a place, she may only charge you half as much to carry your message.
