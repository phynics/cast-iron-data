## Cane

**Price** 1 sp (common), 25 gp (elegant), 2 gp (simple); **Weight** 3 lbs. (common), 4 lbs.
(elegant), 3 lbs. (simple)

A cane is a walking stick fashioned from wood. A cane sports either a metal cap or a curved top
that's easy to grip. Simple canes are no more than a sturdy branch, while high quality canes are
made from fine materials and bear intricate carvings or adornments.
