**Price** 180 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

These [masterwork manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-masterwork) are designed to swiftly secure a
restrained creature. The price includes a quick-release mechanism that, when attached to a belt or
pack, allows you to retrieve the quickcatch manacles with one hand as a swift action. Quickcatch
manacles include a [good lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-good) that locks automatically when the manacles are
applied. When you take the tie up action in a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple), you can lock your
opponent in quickcatch manacles instead of using rope. The quickcatch manacles must be sized
appropriately for your opponent. Quickcatch manacles can be constructed for creatures of different
sizes using the same multipliers as ordinary manacles.
