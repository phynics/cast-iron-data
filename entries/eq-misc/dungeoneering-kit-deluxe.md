## Dungeoneering kit

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 56, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 47\
**Price** 15 gp (common), 130 gp (deluxe); **Weight** 25 lbs. (common), 15 lbs. (deluxe)

This bundle of essential supplies for exploring ruins and abandoned cities includes two
[candles](ref/eq-misc/candle), [chalk](ref/eq-misc/chalk), a [hammer](ref/eq-misc/hammer) and four
[pitons](ref/eq-misc/piton), 50 feet of hemp [rope](ref/eq-misc/rope-50-ft), a
[hooded lantern](ref/eq-misc/hooded-lantern) with 5 flasks of [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil), two
[sacks](ref/eq-misc/sack), two [torches](ref/eq-misc/torch), and four
[tindertwigs](ref/eq-misc/tindertwig). A deluxe dungeoneering kit contains two candles, chalk, a
hammer and four pitons, 50 feet of silk rope, two sacks, three [sunrods](ref/eq-misc/sunrod), four
tindertwigs, and an [everburning torch](ref/eq-misc/everburning-torch).
