**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 57\
**Price** 1-100 gp; **Weight** 1-20 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This is the religious text of a particular religion, with a title, text, and detailed rites specific
to that faith. The cheap variety is a small, light book with no illustrations, printed or written on
thin paper, and with a cover made of canvas or thin leather. A typical holy text for an experienced
adventuring priest is durable, with a leather cover, chapter illustrations, quality paper; it costs
25 gp and weighs 2 pounds. The most expensive versions have detailed illustrations, gilded initials
and border decorations, covers made of fine leather, wood, or even metal, and are often oversized
and difficult to carry around.
