**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 103\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Tools

You mix this fine gray powder with water and a cubic foot of sand or gravel to form a durable
stone-like material. Until it cures, it has the consistency of thick mud, and you must hold it in
place (typically with a frame of earth or wood) if you want it to harden in a specific shape. It
takes 1d10+10 minutes for the cement to partially cure, gaining [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 2 and 5
hit points per inch of thickness. After 1d6 hours the cement is fully cured and as hard as true
stone.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 15
