**Sources:** Pirates of the Inner Sea pg 21\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

A peg leg is a wooden stump with a socket built on one end to fit over an individual's knee. Pirates
who lose legs at sea often wear peg legs. A peg leg reduces your base speed by 5 feet and imposes a
-4 penalty on Acrobatics, Climb, and Swim checks. You take half damage from
[caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops). Pirates often carve hidden compartments into their peg legs -
these compartments can hold small items weighing up to 1 or 2 pounds (or a few swigs of grog). If
you have two peg legs, your base speed is reduced to half and you take a -10 penalty on Acrobatics,
Climb, and Swim checks. You are immune to damage from caltrops.

Peg legs presume that the knee joint is intact. If the knee joint is not intact, use the
[prosthesis](ref/eq-misc/prosthetic-leg) statistics instead.
