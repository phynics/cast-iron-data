**Sources:** The Harrow Handbook pg 30\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Entertainment

This small darkwood case contains a recessed area to hold a [harrow deck](ref/eq-misc/harrow-deck)
and a [harrow mat](ref/eq-misc/harrow-mat), as well as a compartment for
[incense](ref/eq-misc/incense). Nonmagical harrow cards are protected against rips, tears, water
damage, bending, and flames so long as they are stored in a harrow carrying case.
