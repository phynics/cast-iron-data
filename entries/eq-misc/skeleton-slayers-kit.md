**Sources:** Undead Slayer's Handbook pg 15\
**Price** 120 gp; **Weight** 32 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit contains all the tools necessary for clearing out small packs of skeletons from tombs and
crypts. It includes three flasks of [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid), a [club](ref/eq-weapon/club), a
[sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling), 10 [sling bullets](ref/eq-weapon/sling-bullets), two flasks of
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), a [tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag), 50 feet of
[hemp rope](ref/eq-misc/rope-50-ft), a [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar), and a
[wooden holy symbol](ref/eq-misc/wooden-holy-symbol).
