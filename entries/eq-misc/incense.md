**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 249, Advanced Race Guide pg 87\
**Price** 7 sp; **Weight** 0.1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This aromatic resin is imbued with fragrant oils and formed into sticks, cones, or balls. It's often
burned in religious ceremonies or during meditation. A piece of incense burns for 1 hour.

**Note:** The weight is based on 10 incense sticks weighing 1 pound in the ARG. That book also lists
10 sticks as 10 gold, instead of the 7sp cost in the OA incense entry.
