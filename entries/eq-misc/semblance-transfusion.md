**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

When combined with a drop of a humanoid's blood, this solution allows the
[homunculus](ref/monster/homunculus) that drinks it to grow to size Small and assume the likeness of
the subject whose blood was used. If the homunculus can speak, its voice changes to resemble that of
the subject. The homunculus's space and reach become 5 feet and it loses its bite attack, but
otherwise its statistics remain unchanged. This transformation lasts 24 hours. The homunculus gains
a +10 circumstance bonus on Disguise checks to appear as the subject, but unless the subject is a
halfling, a gnome, a child, or another Small humanoid, the homunculus is likely shorter than the
subject.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (110 magnesium + 200 silver + 100
spirit of wine)/distillation; **Craft DC** 25\
**Time** 1 day; **Tools** [retort](ref/eq-misc/retort); **Type** alchemical remedy
