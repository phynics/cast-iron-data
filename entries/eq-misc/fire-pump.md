**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 204\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 500 lbs.\
**Category** Kits

This kit for a [heavy wagon](ref/eq-misc/wagon-heavy) provides a water tank, platform pump, and a
rotating nozzle. If the operator succeeds at a DC 20 Strength check, the fire pump releases a stream
of water that reaches up to 30 feet away. Each person assisting with the pump lowers the DC by 5.
Operating or assisting is a full-round action. The pump extinguishes 5 square feet of nonmagical
fire per round. The water tank holds enough water for 10 rounds of pumping and takes 10 minutes to
refill from a stream, pond, lake, or other body of water.
