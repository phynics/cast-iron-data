**Sources:** Faiths of Balance pg 25\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Channel Foci\
**Deity** [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma)

This amulet is made of a dulled dagger wrapped with feathers. When activated, it protects the wearer
against undead creatures. The first time the wearer is subject to an attack from an undead creature
that requires a saving throw (such as disease, paralysis, or energy drain), the amulet gives the
wearer a sacred (if activated with positive energy) or profane (if activated with negative energy)
bonus on her saving throw against that attack. This bonus is equal to the number of dice of the
activating cleric's [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) ability. The amulet remains charged
until its magic is used or 1 hour has passed.
