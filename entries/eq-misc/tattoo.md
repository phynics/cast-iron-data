**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 58, Adventurer's Armory pg 19, Advanced Player's Guide pg 183,
Pirates of the Inner Sea pg 21\
**Price** 1 cp-20 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

The price of a tattoo depends on the quality, size, and number of colors used. A coin-sized tattoo
in blue ink that will blur over a decade costs 1 cp, a hand-sized one in black ink that won't fade
costs 1 sp, and a tattoo covering an entire back takes several sessions and costs 10 gp. Each
additional color costs as much as a single tattoo of its size. While these prices represent the
baseline, tattoos - like any other artwork - can be worth far more depending on the prestige of the
artist.
