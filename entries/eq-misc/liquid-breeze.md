**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 29\
**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Remedies

Developed in ancient times to ease travels across the harsh deserts of Kelesh, liquid breeze is a
common ware in markets catering to travelers, adventurers, and explorers. This soothing liquid
grants the benefits of [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) for 8 hours, but only against
hot environments. This effect is nonmagical and can't be dispelled.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
