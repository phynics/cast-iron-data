## Gas mask (modern)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#71: Rasputin Must Die! pg 67\
**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lbs.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Gas masks are rubberized-cloth masks fitted with thick glass eyepieces and a charcoal-dust filter in
a rectangular canister that screws in near the mouthpiece. A gas mask is worn tightly around the
head and face, allowing the user to breathe in hazardous environments. A gas mask grants immunity to
inhaled poisons and other nonmagical airborne attacks that require you to breathe them, and a +2
bonus on saving throws against magical cloud or magical gas attacks. Using a gas mask imposes a -2
penalty on hearing- and sight-based Perception checks. A gas mask's filter canister can be used for
8 hours before needing to be replaced.
