**Price** 6 sp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

Not all underwater creatures use sight as their primary means of reading text, so underwater paper
is significantly thicker than surface paper, allowing the writer to carve the letters into the
sheet. This lets creatures with [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) see the textured writing from afar
as well as allowing others to read it by touch, or sometimes from a short distance if they have
unusually precise [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense). For underwater societies where vision is more
prominent, the writer can fill the carved grooves with colorful ink (and potentially
[bioluminescent ink](ref/eq-misc/ink-bioluminescent)) and then adhere a transparent waterproof sheet
on top to prevent the ink from washing away. This transparent sheet costs an additional 4 sp.
