**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 56\
**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Tools

This specifically designed kit was put together by half-orcs to help them appear more human. It
includes skin cream to disguise green-gray skin color and other items designed to conceal or draw
attention away from typical half-orc physical characteristics. Using this kit on a half-orc grants a
+3 bonus on Disguise checks to appear human. Used on an orc, it only grants a +1 bonus on these
checks. A half-orc disguise kit is exhausted after 10 uses.
