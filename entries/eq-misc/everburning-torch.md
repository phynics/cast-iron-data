**Price** 110 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Adventuring Gear

This otherwise normal torch has a [continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame) spell cast on it.
This causes it to shed light like an ordinary torch, but it does not emit heat or deal fire damage
if used as a weapon.
