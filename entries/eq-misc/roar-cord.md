**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 66\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Tools

This thin length of rope has many oddly shaped bits of hollow metal fixed along its length. As a
standard action, a creature can swing a roar cord over its head to generate a variety of eerie
noises. For the next round, any creature within 60 feet of the roar cord takes a -2 penalty on
Perception checks that rely on sound and a -1 penalty on saving throws against fear effects. The
roar cord can be used as a bardic instrument (string) that grants the bard a +2 bonus on Perform
checks when using the [countersong](ref/performance/countersong) bardic performance.
