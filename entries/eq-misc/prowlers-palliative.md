**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 23\
**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Tincture

This pitch-black liquid slows your heart rate, putting you in an almost trance-like state. For 1
hour after drinking this tincture, you gain a +2 alchemical bonus on Stealth checks, you can use
Stealth while moving at full speed without penalty, and the DCs for Intimidate checks to demoralize
you is 2 higher. The tincture's sedative nature causes you to take a -4 penalty on Strength-based
skill checks and a -2 penalty on damage rolls for the same duration.

**Construction:** Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
