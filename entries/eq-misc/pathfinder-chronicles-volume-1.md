**Sources:** Pathfinder Society Primer pg 26\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** [Pathfinder Chronicles](ref/rule/pathfinder-chronicles)

The premier Pathfinder Chronicles volume (first published in 4317 AR) features an account of Durvin
Gest's exploration of the ruins of Azlant, details of Selmius Foster's race against a marid and an
efreeti, and Gregaro Voth's adventures in the Mwangi Expanse.

### Skill Bonuses

- Deal with geniekind more effectively (Diplomacy and Knowledge \[planes\] +2)
- Gain insight into Azlanti sentence construction and idioms (Linguistics +2)
- Navigate the dense jungles of the Mwangi Expanse (Survival +2)
- Understand the history of Azlant (Knowledge \[history\] +2)
