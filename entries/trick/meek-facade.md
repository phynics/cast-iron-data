**Meek Facade**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject misses a creature with an attack
(even if it's part of a full attack in which she hits that creature with another attack). The enemy
must attack no one other than the subject, and the subject gains a +2 dodge bonus to AC against the
triggering enemy's attacks. Both of these effects last for 1 round.

**Flavor:** The subject magically seems like a weak target, goading an enemy into attacking her.

**Special:** This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect.

At 5th level: The bonus to AC +3.

At 10th level: The bonus to AC +4.

At 15th level: The bonus to AC +5.

At 20th level: The bonus to AC +6.
