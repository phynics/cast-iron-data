The subject magically seems like a weak target, goading an enemy into attacking her. The mesmerist
can trigger this trick when the subject misses a creature with an attack (even if it's part of a
full attack in which she hits that creature with another attack). The enemy must attack no one other
than the subject, and the subject gains a +2 dodge bonus to AC against the triggering enemy's
attacks. Both of these effects last for 1 round. The bonus to AC increases by 1 for every 5 levels
the mesmerist possesses. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect.
