**&L&Unwitting Messenger&FN&**

**Ability:** The mesmerist describes a recipient to the subject while implanting this trick, then
recites a verbal message no longer than five words per mesmerist level. The subject of _unwitting
messenger_ has no memory of the message or the target recipient.

When the subject encounters the specified recipient, she repeats the message verbatim, but does not
recall speaking the message or its contents.

**Special:** The message remains buried in the subject's subconscious for 24 hours, after which time
it disappears if not delivered. The subject of _unwitting messenger_ can be fooled by mundane
disguises or illusions, causing her to deliver the message to the wrong recipient.
