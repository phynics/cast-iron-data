**Shadow Blend**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick whenever the subject attempts a Stealth check
while in an area of [dim or darker light](ref/rule/vision-and-light). The subject gains total
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from all enemies (50% miss chance) as long as it remains within
areas of dim or darker light. Against creatures with [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), the subject
gains concealment (20% miss chance) rather than total concealment.

**Special:** Once triggered, this effect lasts for 1 round per mesmerist level or until the subject
enters an area of normal or brighter light.
