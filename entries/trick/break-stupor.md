**Break Stupor**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick whenever the subject is under an effect such as
the [fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) condition or magical slumber as per the
[sleep](ref/spell/sleep) spell that would be ended by the subject taking damage or by an obvious
threat. The trick sends a momentary phantom threat and sensation of pain, ending the effect. This
ability works fast enough to prevent a sleeping creature from falling [prone](ref/rule/prone) or
dropping what it is holding, if the mesmerist uses it immediately.

The mesmerist can also trigger the trick if the subject becomes [confused](ref/misc/confused) as per
[confusion](ref/spell/confusion), but if he does so, the subject attacks the mesmerist on its next
turn, as if the mesmerist attacked the subject.
