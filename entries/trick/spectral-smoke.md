A cloud of smoke pops up around the subject, foiling attacks. The mesmerist can trigger this trick
when the subject is targeted by an attack or by a spell that requires an attack roll. A smoke cloud
appears in the subject's square and in a 10-foot radius around it. This cloud lasts for 1 round per
mesmerist level. The cloud functions as _[fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud)_, but is an illusion
(figment) effect that can't be dispersed by wind and can be used underwater. A creature that
interacts with the cloud can attempt a saving throw to disbelieve the effect. The radius of the
cloud increases by 5 feet for every 5 caster levels the mesmerist possesses, to a maximum of 30 feet
at 20th level.
