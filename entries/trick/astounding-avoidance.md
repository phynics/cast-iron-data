**Astounding Avoidance**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject attempts a saving throw against
an attack or spell that normally deals half damage on a successful save, before the subject rolls
the save. If the subject succeeds at her save, she takes no damage.

**Flavor:** The subject can avoid damage from magical and unusual attacks.

**At 12th Level:** The subject takes only half damage if she fails her save.

**Special:** The benefits of this trick don't stack with those of the _evasion_ or _stalwart_ class
features.
