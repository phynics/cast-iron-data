**Cursed Sanction**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject is attacked or becomes the target
of a spell that requires an attack roll. The attacker takes a -4 penalty on attack rolls, saves,
ability checks, and skill checks for 1 minute per mesmerist level (Will negates). This penalty
applies to the triggering attack roll.

**Flavor:** A ward curses an enemy who attacks the subject.

**Passive:** While this trick is implanted, a visible, circular symbol appears on the subject's
forehead.

**Special:** This is a curse effect.
