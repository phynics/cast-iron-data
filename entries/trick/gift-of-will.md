**&L&Gift of Will&FN&**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject attempts a Will saving throw or a
Sense Motive check, or is targeted by an Intimidate check to demoralize her. For a saving throw, the
subject uses the mesmerist's Will saving throw bonus instead of her own. For a Sense Motive check or
Intimidate check, she adds the mesmerist's Charisma bonus as a morale bonus on the check or to the
DC.

**Flavor:** A small portion of the mesmerist's vast mental power buttresses his ally's mind.

**Special:** The mesmerist can't implant this trick on himself.
