**Spatial Switch**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when an enemy moves adjacent to or attacks either
the subject or the mesmerist, provided the subject and the mesmerist are within 100 feet of one
another. The subject and the mesmerist then swap positions.

**Flavor:** The mesmerist allows himself and an ally to physically swap places.

**Special:** If this trick was triggered by an attack, the swap happens before checking to see
whether the attack hits. The mesmerist can't implant this trick on himself. This is a conjuration
(teleportation) effect.
