**&L&Linked Reaction&FN&**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when either the subject or the mesmerist is
surprised and the other isn't (even if the mesmerist would normally be unable to take actions
because of being surprised). Both the mesmerist and the subject can act in this surprise round.

**Flavor:** A hive mind allows the mesmerist and an ally to share one another's reaction times.

**Special:** The mesmerist can't implant this trick on himself.
