A hive mind allows the mesmerist and an ally to share one another's reaction times. The mesmerist
can trigger this trick when either the subject or the mesmerist is surprised and the other isn't
(even if the mesmerist would normally be unable to take actions because of being surprised). Both
the mesmerist and the subject can act in this surprise round. The mesmerist can't implant this trick
on himself.
