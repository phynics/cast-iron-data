The subject changes shape to elude an attacking enemy. The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the
subject takes damage from an enemy's attack. The subject transforms into a bird (as
_[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)_). Though the subject can appear as any Tiny bird, it
uses the statistics for a [raven](ref/monster/familiar-raven). This is a polymorph effect.
