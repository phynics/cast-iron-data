**Psychic Impression**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject touches an object that another
creature with an Intelligence score of 3 or more has touched in the last 10 minutes. The mesmerist
receives a _psychic impression_ of the emotional state of the last such creature to touch the
object. This impression contains no images or languages and can't show the mesmerist who the
creature was if the mesmerist doesn't already know.

**Flavor:** Objects retain _psychic impressions_ of those who come in contact with them, and a
powerful mind can read these impressions.
