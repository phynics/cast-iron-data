**Misdirection**

**Ability:** The mesmerist can trigger this trick when the subject makes an attack or uses a spell
that requires an attack roll. The mesmerist attempts a [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check to feint
against a single target of the subject's attack. Unlike a normal feint, this ability can be used on
non-melee attacks. If the feint succeeds, the target of the attack is denied its Dexterity bonus to
AC against the triggering attack. The target doesn't suffer any additional effects that the
mesmerist or the subject would cause on a normal feint or Bluff check.

**Flavor:** An enemy's perception of an attack by the subject shifts, making the foe unprepared to
defend against the attack.

**Special:** This is an illusion (glamer) effect.
