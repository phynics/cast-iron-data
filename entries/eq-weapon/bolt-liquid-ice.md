**Price** 40 gp; **Weight** 0.2 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

These metal crossbow bolts have a glass section in the middle filled with
[liquid ice](ref/eq-weapon/liquid-ice). On a hit, it deals 1d4 points of cold damage in addition to
its normal damage. Liquid ice bolts do not cause splash damage.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25

**Note:** Weight is based on 10 normal crossbow bolts weighing one pound and assuming these
liquid-filled bolts weigh double that. The source material lists weight as —.
