**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 30\
**Price** 60 gp; **Weight** 0.5 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

The viscous turquoise oil in this fine glass vial bursts into a pearlescent, silvery mist when
exposed to air. You can throw a vial of silvercloud oil as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. A direct hit allows
the mist to enter the body of a target with DR/silver and eat away at the victim's defenses,
reducing its DR/silver by 1 per round for 1d4+1 rounds (to a minimum of 0). Once the duration of
this effect ends, the target fully regains its [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). Each
creature with DR/silver within 5 feet of the point where the vial struck takes a -1 penalty to its
DR/silver for 1 round. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
