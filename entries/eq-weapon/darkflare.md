**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This alchemical flare explodes in black sparks 1 round after lighting. Any creature with
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) in the same square as a darkflare when it explodes must succeed at
a DC 15 Fortitude save or lose its darkvision for 1d10 rounds. Affected creatures can still use
other vision (such as normal vision and [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision)) with no
penalty.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
