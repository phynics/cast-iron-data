These small tea kettles are made with clamping lids and heavily reinforced handles, allowing them to
be swung like a club. A traveling kettle with boiling water inside deals an additional 1 point of
fire damage on a hit. Boiling water cools to a non-damaging temperature after 5 minutes.

**Cost** 5 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special**
[monk](ref/misc/monk), see text\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Simple\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Monk
