Like a [glaive](ref/eq-weapon/glaive), the fauchard is a curved blade at the end of a pole. However,
the cutting edge of a fauchard is along the concave side, like that of a
[sickle](ref/eq-weapon/sickle) or [scythe](ref/eq-weapon/scythe).

**Cost** 14 gp **Weight** 10 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** 18-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special**
[reach](ref/misc/reach), [trip](ref/misc/trip)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Polearms
