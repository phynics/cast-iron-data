**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 18, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 211

These arrows are a specialty of the Ekujae shamans, who craft the arrowheads out of the thistles of
a toxic plant that most creatures find highly caustic. They deal normal damage but then become
embedded in the wound and deal an additional 1 point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage each round
for 1d6 rounds from their irritating sap. Creatures immune to poison are immune to this extra
damage. A creature can remove an embedded thistle arrow as a move action without provoking attacks
of opportunity, but doing so deals an additional 1d3 points of damage as the thorny barbs are pulled
free. A DC 12 Heal check (made as a standard action) can pull free a thistle arrow's head without
dealing any additional damage.

**Cost** 1 gp **Weight** 0.15 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition

**Note:** Weight is based on 20 normal arrows weighing three pounds.
