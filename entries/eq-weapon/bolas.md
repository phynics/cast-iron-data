**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 33, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 143, Ultimate Equipment pg 21

A bolas is a pair of wooden, stone, or metal weights connected by a thin rope or cord. You can whirl
and throw this weapon to make a ranged trip attack against an opponent. You can't be tripped during
your own trip attempt when using a bolas.

**Cost** 5 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** B; **Special**
[nonlethal](ref/misc/nonlethal), [trip](ref/misc/trip)\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Thrown
