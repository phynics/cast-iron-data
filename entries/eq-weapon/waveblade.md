Also known as a "bich'wa" or a "scorpion's tail" due to its unique, stinger-like shape, this short,
double-curved blade has no hilt but features a knuckle guard. You gain a +2 bonus to your CMD
against disarm attempts made to disarm you of a waveblade.

**Cost** 5 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** 18-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** P or S;
**Special** [monk](ref/misc/monk), see text\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Close
