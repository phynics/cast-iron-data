**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 43, Ultimate Combat pg 140

This twin-barreled shotgun can be shot one barrel at a time, or both together as one attack. A
double shot that fires bullets is inaccurate, and takes a -4 penalty on both attacks. A double shot
that fires bullets targets only a single creature and increases the damage of each barrel to 2d6
points (Small) or 2d8 points (Medium) for a total of 4d6 or 4d8 points of damage. A doublebarreled
shotgun uses [metal cartridges](ref/eq-weapon/metal-cartridge) (loaded with either a
[bullet](ref/eq-weapon/firearm-bullet) or [pellets](ref/eq-weapon/pellets-handful)) as ammunition.
This is an advanced firearm.

**Cost** 7,000 gp **Weight** 15 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 20 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** scatter\
**Misfire** 1-2; **Capacity** 2\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
