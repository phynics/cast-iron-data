Though less powerful than other, larger firearms, this pistol is small enough to be easily concealed
in a jacket or coat. You get a +2 bonus on Sleight of Hand checks made to conceal a coat pistol on
your body. A coat pistol uses either a [bullet](ref/eq-weapon/firearm-bullet) and 1 dose of
[black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose) or a single
[alchemical cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge) as ammunition. This is an early firearm.

**Cost** 750 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 1\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
