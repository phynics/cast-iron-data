**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 17, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 142

This metal glove lets you deal lethal damage rather than nonlethal damage with unarmed strikes. A
strike with a gauntlet is otherwise considered an unarmed attack. The cost and weight given are for
a single gauntlet. Medium and heavy armors (except breastplates) come with gauntlets. Your opponent
cannot use a disarm action to disarm you of gauntlets.

**Cost** 2 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d2 (small), 1d3 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special** -\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Simple\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Close
