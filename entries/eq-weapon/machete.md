**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 19

This tool is a wide, single-edged blade about 2 feet in length. Most often used to hack through
dense vegetation, it works equally well as a weapon. A character proficient with a machete gains a
+1 circumstance bonus on Survival checks to get along in the wild.

**Cost** 10 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special** -\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Light
