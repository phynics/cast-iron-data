**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 43, Ultimate Combat pg 138

This pistol has six barrels instead of one. The entire barrel housing can be quickly rotated by hand
between shots (a free action requiring one free hand), allowing all six bullets to be fired before
the weapon must be reloaded. Each barrel of a pepperbox uses either a
[bullet](ref/eq-weapon/firearm-bullet) and 1 dose of [black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose)
or a single [alchemical cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge) as ammunition. This is an
early firearm.

**Cost** 3,000 gp **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×4; **Range** 20 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1-2 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 6\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
