**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 21, Elves of Golarion pg 23\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 0.15 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

Behind the head of this arrow is a small receptacle of alchemical material that heats up when
exposed to air and eventually combusts; barbs on the arrowhead pierce the pouch when it hits a
target. If you hit a target with a slow burn arrow, it deals damage as normal, but at the beginning
of your next turn, the arrow bursts into flames and deals 1d6 points of fire damage to the target.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (70 magnesium + 80 myrrh + 25
phosphorus)/congelation; **Craft DC** 25\
**Time** 10 minutes; **Tools** [alchemist's lab](ref/eq-misc/alchemists-lab); **Type** alchemical
weapon
