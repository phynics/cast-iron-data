**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 20, Ultimate Combat pg 132

This weapon has a single [kama](ref/eq-weapon/kama) or [sickle](ref/eq-weapon/sickle) held in the
off hand, attached by 10 feet of fine chain to a weighted metal ball. The sickle can be used to make
trip attacks, jabs, and blocks, while the ball is whipped around at high speeds and then smashed
into the opponent, or used to tangle an opponent's sword or spear, allowing the wielder to then
attack with the sickle.

**Cost** 12 gp **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d2/1d4 (small), 1d3/1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** S or B;
**Special** [double](ref/misc/double), [grapple](ref/misc/grapple), [monk](ref/misc/monk),
[reach](ref/misc/reach), [trip](ref/misc/trip)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Double; Flails; Monk
