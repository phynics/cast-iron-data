**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 20, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 209, Adventurer's Armory pg
33, Advanced Player's Guide pg 178

This heavy blade has a single sharp edge, concave at the bottom and convex at the top, with a
hook-shaped hilt. Its shape distributes the weight to give it the momentum of an axe with the
cutting edge of a sword, much like a larger version of a [kukri](ref/eq-weapon/kukri). The hooked
hilt is sometimes carved to resemble the head of a horse or bird of prey, and may have a thin chain
stretched across its opening, acting as a partial guard for the hand and fingers.

**Cost** 18 gp **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×3; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special** -\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Heavy
