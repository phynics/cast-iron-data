**Sources:** Pathfinder \#115: Trail of the Hunted pg 74\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This hollow clay container holds black powder, a slow-burning fuse, and rock salt or hard beads of
resin or rubber. Lighting the fuse is a move action; 1d3 rounds later, the sting grenade explodes,
dealing 2d6 points of nonlethal damage and 1d6 points of fire damage in a 10-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) (Reflex DC 15 half). A sting grenade is treated as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. Crafting this item
requires a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
