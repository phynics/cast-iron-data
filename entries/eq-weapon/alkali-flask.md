**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This flask of caustic liquid reacts with an ooze's natural acids. You can throw an alkali flask as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. Against non-ooze
creatures, an alkali flask functions as a normal flask of [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid). Against oozes
and other acid-based creatures, the alkali flask inflicts double damage. Crafting this item is a DC
20 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
