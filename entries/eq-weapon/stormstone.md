**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 27\
**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

A stormstone functions like a [thunderstone](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone), but deals 1d6 points of
sonic damage in addition to [deafening](ref/misc/deafened) creatures; a successful DC 15 Fortitude
save halves the damage and reduces the deafness to 1 round rather than negating it.

**Mythic:** If you expend one use of mythic power while throwing a stormstone, the item deals an
amount of electricity damage equal to your mythic tier to all affected creatures, regardless of
whether they succeed at their save. Creatures that fail their saving throws against a mythic
stormstone become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round in addition to the other effects. The
stormstone ignores non-mythic resistances, non-mythic immunity to electricity or sonic damage, and
non-mythic silencing effects.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (15 black powder + 85
phosphorus)/digestion; **Craft DC** 30\
**Time** 1 hour; **Tools** [crucible](ref/eq-misc/crucible); **Type** alchemical weapon
