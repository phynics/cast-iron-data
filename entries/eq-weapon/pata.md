**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 20, Pathfinder \#9: Escape from Old Korvosa pg 83, Pathfinder
Campaign Setting pg 209, Adventurer's Armory pg 18, Ultimate Combat pg 144

An evolution of the [punching dagger](ref/eq-weapon/punching-dagger), the pata is a
[short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword) that ends in a full, fingerless gauntlet hilt. You punch
rather than stab with the weapon, allowing you to put more force behind each strike. While wearing a
pata, you cannot use that hand for anything else. A pata provides a +10 bonus to your CMD against
disarm attempts.

**Cost** 14 gp **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[performance](ref/misc/performance)\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Axes; Blades, Light
