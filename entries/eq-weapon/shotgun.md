**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 43, Ultimate Combat pg 140

This advanced version of the [blunderbuss](ref/eq-weapon/blunderbuss) shoots in a 30-foot cone when
firing [pellets](ref/eq-weapon/pellets-handful), and has a 20-foot range increment when firing a
[bullet](ref/eq-weapon/firearm-bullet) (often called a slug). Shotguns can either be simple and
functional, or art pieces in their own right, with engravings and inlay on the stocks and breeches.
Those used by the upper class are often emblazoned with images of game animals, family crests, or
elaborate maker's marks.

A shotgun uses [metal cartridges](ref/eq-weapon/metal-cartridge) (loaded with either a bullet or
pellets) as ammunition. This is an advanced firearm.

**Cost** 5,000 gp **Weight** 12 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 20 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** scatter\
**Misfire** 1-2; **Capacity** 1\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
