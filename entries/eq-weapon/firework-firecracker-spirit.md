**Price** 30 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

These firecrackers are covered in magical runes and are mixed with
[ghost salt](ref/eq-misc/weapon-blanch-ghost-salt). One round after you light a spirit firecracker
in an adjacent square, the firework detonates and deals 1d4+1 points of damage to incorporeal
creatures in the same space. In addition, an affected incorporeal creature with
[light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity), [light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness), or
[sunlight powerlessness](ref/umr/sunlight-powerlessness) becomes [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1
round. A spirit firecracker has no effect on corporeal creatures.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (1 black powder + 6 brimstone + 4
saltpeter + 10 silver)/calcination; **Craft DC** 25\
**Time** 1 hour; **Tools** [crucible](ref/eq-misc/crucible); **Type** alchemical weapon
