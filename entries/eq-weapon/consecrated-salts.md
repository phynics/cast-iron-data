**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 63\
**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** 0.5 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

The powdered salts in this stoppered glass sphere contains reagents blessed by a cleric or another
divine authority. You can throw a sphere of consecrated salts as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. Consecrated salts
deal damage to evil outsiders, [haunts](ref/rule/haunts), and undead as if they were a flask of
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water). Additionally, if a haunt or rejuvenating undead such as a
[ghost](ref/template/ghost) is reduced to 0 hp within 1 minute of being damaged by consecrated
salts, its reset time (in the case of a haunt) or rejuvenation is delayed by 1 day. This delay can
also be achieved by sprinkling consecrated salts on the earthly remains of a ghost or the affected
area of a haunt. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 30 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
