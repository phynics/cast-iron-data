**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 19

Adapted from a garden rake, the kumade consists of a wood or bamboo pole topped with a metal claw.
The rake head doubles as a [grappling hook](ref/eq-weapon/grappling-hook). When a kumade is secured
to a surface, such as a rafter or window sill, its handle can be climbed as a 5-foot length of rope.

**Cost** 5 gp **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[grapple](ref/misc/grapple)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Simple\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Axes
