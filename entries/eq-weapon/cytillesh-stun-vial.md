**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 29\
**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** 0.5 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

Harvested from the cytillesh fungus commonly found in the Darklands, these small vials emit a bright
blue glow, and are commonly used in intertribal kobold disputes. When broken, a cytillesh stun vial
releases a flash of blue light consisting of bright light in a 10-foot radius and
[dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in a 20-foot radius. All creatures within the flash area must
make a Will save (DC 20). Creatures that fail are [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1d2 rounds if
they're in the area of bright light, or are [confused](ref/misc/confused) for 1 round if in the area
of dim light. Crafting this item requires a successful DC 30 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 30
