**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 21, Ultimate Combat pg 134

This unusual sword has a straight 2-foot-long blade with six shorter L-shaped blades protruding from
it, three on each side in a staggered pattern. The shorter blades can be used to snag opponents'
clothing or armor, or can target weapons in order to [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) them. To snag armor
or clothing, the attacker makes a [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempt. If successful, the victim doesn't
fall [prone](ref/rule/prone), but instead is snagged and stumbles forward, leaving the victim
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) for the remainder of the round.

**Cost** 50 gp **Weight** 7 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special**
[disarm](ref/misc/disarm), [monk](ref/misc/monk)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Heavy; Monk
