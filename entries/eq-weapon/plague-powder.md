**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 213\
**Price** varies; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This ash-gray powder is refined from the crushed bones of creatures that died of virulent diseases,
illegally produced by nefarious alchemists, and sold on the black market to those seeking to spread
illness and death. The application of the powder depends on the disease from which it is refined.
Someone using powder made from diseases contracted by ingestion must apply it to food (a full-round
action) for the victim to consume. Those using powder made from diseases contracted by injury must
apply the powder to weapons (a standard action, as per the [poison application](ref/rule/poison)
rules) and deal damage to victims to subject them to the contagion. Diseases contracted by
inhalation require the user to disperse the powder by throwing it into the air. Throwing a handful
of plague powder is a [splash attack](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 5
feet. Anyone standing in the square of impact must succeed at a save with a DC equal to that of the
disease from which the powder is made to avoid contracting the illness, while those in adjacent
squares must attempt the same save with a +4 bonus. Diseases spread through contact can use any of
the three manners of application.

The most common disease refined for these powders is [filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever), costing
144 gp, but refined plague powders of other diseases are available, with a price equal to the base
save DC of the disease squared. Making a plague powder always requires access to a sample of the
disease and exposes the creator to the disease in question each day of crafting. Crafting a dose of
plague powder requires a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
