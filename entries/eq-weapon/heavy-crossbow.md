You draw a heavy crossbow back by turning a small winch. Loading a heavy crossbow is a full-round
action that provokes attacks of opportunity. A heavy crossbow fires crossbow bolts.

Normally, operating a heavy crossbow requires two hands. However, you can shoot, but not load, a
heavy crossbow with one hand at a -4 penalty on attack rolls. You can shoot a heavy crossbow with
each hand, but you take a penalty on attack rolls as if attacking with two one-handed weapons. This
penalty is cumulative with the penalty for one-handed firing.

**Cost** 50 gp **Weight** 8 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×2; **Range** 120 ft.; **Type** P;
**Special** -\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Simple\
