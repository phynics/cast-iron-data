A dagger is a fighting knife. Its blade is usually less than 1 foot long, and can be curved or
straight, with one edge or two. Dagger designs vary widely, and can have many different names (such
as a thrusting dagger being called a "dirk"). You get a +2 bonus on Sleight of Hand skill checks
made to conceal a dagger on your body.

**Cost** 2 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×2; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** P or S;
**Special** -\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Simple\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Light; Thrown; Tribal
