**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 18, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 142

This variant of the [light pick](ref/eq-weapon/light-pick) has a longer handle and can be used with
one or two hands. It is a common, inexpensive weapon for mounted soldiers since it can be used
effectively from horseback.
