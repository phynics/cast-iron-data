**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 43, Ultimate Combat pg 140

The four barrels of this rifle are set into a turnable housing that can be quickly rotated by hand
(a free action) between shots. A pepperbox rifle uses
[metal cartridges](ref/eq-weapon/metal-cartridge) as ammunition. This is an advanced firearm.

**Cost** 7,000 gp **Weight** 15 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** ×4; **Range** 80 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1-2; **Capacity** 4\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
