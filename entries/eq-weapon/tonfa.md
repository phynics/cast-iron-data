These L-shaped fighting batons are good for striking and blocking. The wielder holds the handle and
either spins the stick or strikes with the stick covering the forearm.

**Cost** 1 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special**
[blocking](ref/misc/blocking), [monk](ref/misc/monk)\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Close; Monk
