**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 20\
**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

Once its fuse has been lit, this hoop-shaped firework emits colorful sparks from a series of
outward-angled wooden tubes. It can then be rolled along the ground or thrown as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 30 feet. One round after
being lit, a fire wheel firework explodes in a spectacular 5-foot-tall cylindrical blast with a
20-foot radius. All creatures in the area take 3d6 points of fire damage and are
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 minute (Reflex DC 16 halves damage, negates blindness).

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
