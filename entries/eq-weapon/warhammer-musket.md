**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 43, Ultimate Combat pg 138

This musket has a warhammer head at the end of its barrel, which allows it to be used as both a
[musket](ref/eq-weapon/musket) and a [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer). It is considered a double
weapon for the purposes of creating masterwork or magical versions of this weapon. If this firearm
gains the broken condition, both the firearm component and the warhammer are considered broken. A
warhammer musket uses either a [bullet](ref/eq-weapon/firearm-bullet) and 1 dose of
[black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose) or a single
[alchemical cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge) as ammunition. This is an early firearm.

**Cost** 1,600 gp **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×4; **Range** 30 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1-2 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 1\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
