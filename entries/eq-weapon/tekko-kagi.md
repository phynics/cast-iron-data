Also known as an iron claw, this device consists of a fan-like structure of three or more 10-inch
blades secured to a sturdy handle strapped to the forearm of the off hand. It can be used as an
offensive weapon or defensively like a buckler, or used to disarm an opponent without provoking an
attack of opportunity. It provides its owner with a +2 circumstance bonus on attempts to
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) or [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) swords or other slender-bladed weapons.

**Cost** 2 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 1d2 (small), 1d3 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[disarm](ref/misc/disarm), see text\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Close
