**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 20, Ultimate Combat pg 144

Knuckle axes, usually used in pairs, resemble axe blades mounted on brass knuckles. You may hold,
but not wield, a weapon or other object in a hand wearing a knuckle axe. Unlike when wearing
[brass knuckles](ref/eq-weapon/brass-knuckles), you cannot use a hand wearing a knuckle axe for
spellcasting.

**Cost** 9 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special**
[monk](ref/misc/monk), [performance](ref/misc/performance)\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Axes
