**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 19, Ultimate Combat pg 131

This slender, 10-foot-long polearm has an added hook protruding from the head that is used to snag
the clothing and armor of opponents. A hooked lance, unlike a lance, can't be wielded in one hand
while mounted and doesn't deal extra damage if used while mounted.

**Cost** 3 gp **Weight** 10 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×4; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[reach](ref/misc/reach), [trip](ref/misc/trip)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Polearms
