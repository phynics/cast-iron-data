**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 107\
**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This hollow metal or wooden tube contains slow-burning powder. When you light the fuse (a move
action), it creates a 3-foot-long, blazing fountain of red-hot fiery sparks. You wield this fountain
of sparks as if it were a [heavy mace](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-mace). Attacks with the fountain are
melee touch attacks. The fountain deals 1d8 points of fire damage (your Strength modifier does not
apply to the damage).

The fountain sheds light as a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) and can ignite combustible materials such
as parchment, straw, dry sticks, and cloth. Once ignited, it lasts for 1 minute. If extinguished
before this time, the remaining duration is wasted. Crafting this item is a DC 25 Craft (alchemy)
check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
