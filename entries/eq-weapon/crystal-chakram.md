Shaped and carved from quartz or stranger subterranean crystals, these circular throwing discs have
jagged razor-sharp edges. When a crystal chakram strikes a foe, the weapon shatters into tiny sharp
fragments; if it misses, there is a 50% chance the chakram shatters when it hits the ground or
another solid object; otherwise it can be retrieved and used again. A crystal chakram is treated as
ammunition for the purpose of creating magic weapons.

**Cost** 20 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** 18-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special** -\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic
