This short musket features an axe blade at the end of its barrel. It can be used as both a
[musket](ref/eq-weapon/musket) and a [battleaxe](ref/eq-weapon/battleaxe). It is considered a double
weapon for the purposes of creating masterwork or magical versions of this weapon. If this firearm
gains the broken condition, both the firearm component and the axe are considered broken. An axe
musket uses either a [bullet](ref/eq-weapon/firearm-bullet) and a single dose of
[black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose) or an
[alchemical cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge) as ammunition. This is an early firearm.

**Cost** 1,600 gp **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×4; **Range** 30 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1-2 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 1\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
