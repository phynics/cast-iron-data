**Sources:** People of the Wastes pg 14\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This sealed vial contains a highly potent indigo liquid and is an essential item for Dongun Hold
miners, who often keep such vials handy for use against hostile subterranean creatures. The vial can
be thrown as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. A
direct hit deals 2d6 points of acid damage; this damage is doubled against creatures made of
crystal, earth, or metal. Every creature within 5 feet of the impact takes 1 point of acid damage.
Crafting mineral acid requires a successful DC 20 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
