**Sources:** Pirates of the Inner Sea pg 19, Skull and Shackles Player's Guide pg 6

A boarding pike is an 8-foot-long pole topped with a foot-long tapered metal tip. Boarding pikes
look much like [longspears](ref/eq-weapon/longspear), but the metal pike is designed to flow into
the wooden haft, leaving no grooves, collars, or spikes that could trap salt water and cause rust.
Boarding pikes are mainly used defensively, to repel boarding actions. The pikes are kept in
brackets around the mast, so sailors can quickly grab pikes and brace them against the gunwale to
repel boarders.

**Cost** 8 gp **Weight** 9 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[brace](ref/misc/brace), [reach](ref/misc/reach)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Simple
