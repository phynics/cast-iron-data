**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 11

These weapons are treated sticks of wood, either left bare or covered in elaborate decorative
designs. Baston fighting sticks vary in length from 2 to 4 feet, with thicknesses varying between 1
to 3 inches. In most applications, these weapons are similar to [clubs](ref/eq-weapon/club) or
[quarterstaffs](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), but in the hands of a trained expert, they truly shine.
A creature with the [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes) feat treats the weapon as having the
[blocking](ref/misc/blocking) and [performance](ref/misc/performance) weapon special features.

**Cost** 10 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special**
[monk](ref/misc/monk), see text\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Simple
