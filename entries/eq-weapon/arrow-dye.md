**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 21, Elves of Golarion pg 22\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** 0.15 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This arrow ends in a crystalline bubble filled with a viscous alchemical dyeing agent. Firing a dye
arrow is a ranged touch attack; a creature struck by a dye arrow takes no damage but is splashed
with enough black, blue, green, or red marker dye to coat about 1 square foot. The stain caused by
marker dye cannot be washed off except with magic for the first 72 hours, but fades completely after
2 weeks.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (1 cold iron + 1 phosphorus)/earth;
**Craft DC** 25\
**Time** 10 minutes; **Tools** -; **Type** alchemical weapon

**Note:** Weight is based on 20 normal [arrows](ref/eq-weapon/arrow) weighing three pounds. The
source material lists weight as —.
