**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

The alchemists of Nex have found several uses for the toxic residue left over from their massive
ooze cultivating experiments, including a variety of items known collectively as slime grenades.
These ceramic ovoids are filled with caustic sludge and capped with a stone plug. You can throw a
slime grenade as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 5 feet. A
successful ranged touch attack coats the target with [green slime](ref/rule/slimes-molds-and-fungi),
dealing 2d6 points of acid damage to the creature. If the target is wearing wooden or metal armor or
wielding a wooden or metal shield, a slime grenade eats through the material on a hit, dealing 3d6
points of acid damage to the equipment and ignoring the items' [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).
Creatures in the splash radius of a slime grenade take no damage, but any wooden or metal armor or
shields they are wearing take 1d6 points of acid damage (ignoring hardness). Affected creatures can
attempt DC 15 Reflex saves to halve the damage dealt to their equipment.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (50 phosphorus + 75 quicksilver + 50
urea)/congelation; **Craft DC** 25\
**Time** 10 minutes; **Tools** [alchemist's lab](ref/eq-misc/alchemists-lab); **Type** alchemical
weapon
