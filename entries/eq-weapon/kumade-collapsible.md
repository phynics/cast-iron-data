**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 19

This weapon acts as a [kumade](ref/eq-weapon/kumade), but the handle consists of bamboo sections
held together by a taut rope running through the center. Releasing the rope is a standard action
that provokes attacks of opportunity and causes the weapon to collapse into a bundle that fits into
a backpack. Reassembling the collapsible kumade requires a full-round action that provokes attacks
of opportunity.

**Cost** 10 gp **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[grapple](ref/misc/grapple)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Simple\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Axes
