**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 22, Advanced Player's Guide pg 176

These arrows have light shafts and special fletching to give them greater range. A flight arrow's
range increment is 20 feet greater with [longbows](ref/eq-weapon/longbow) and 10 feet greater with
[shortbows](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow). They deal damage as if
[one size category smaller](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments).

**Cost** 0.1 gp **Weight** 0.15 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition

**Note:** Stats are an extrapolation from the original quantity of 20.
