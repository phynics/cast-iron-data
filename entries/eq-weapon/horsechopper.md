Crafted by goblins for battling horses, this weapon is essentially a long-handled
[halberd](ref/eq-weapon/halberd) with an enlarged hook on the blade. Most horsechoppers are sized
for Small creatures.

**Cost** 10 gp **Weight** 12 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** P or S; **Special**
[reach](ref/misc/reach), [trip](ref/misc/trip)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Polearms
