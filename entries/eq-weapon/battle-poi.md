This pair of arm-length chains has handles at one end and heavy fuel-soaked torch heads at the
other. The weight of the poi is insufficient to deal physical damage, but the burning fuel deals
fire damage. If you are proficient in battle poi, you are treated as if you have the
[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) feat for the purposes of making poi attacks. Poi
can be extinguished by spending a full round action smothering them in sand or submerging them in
water.

**Cost** 5 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d3 fire (small), 1d4 fire (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** fire;
**Special** -\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Flails
