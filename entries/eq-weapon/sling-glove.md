**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 5, Legacy of Fire Player's Guide pg 23

This curved, scooped-shaped, 2-foot-long wicker basket attaches to your wrist, allowing you to throw
fist-sized stones at great speed merely by whipping your arm forward. Loading a sling glove is a
free action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity so long as you have a free hand. You take
-4 penalty when throwing a stone at an adjacent target and cannot use the weapon to attack creatures
in your space.

**Cost** 5 gp **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 50 ft.; **Type** B; **Special** -\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Thrown
