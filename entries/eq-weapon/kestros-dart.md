This dart, meant to be thrown using a [kestros](ref/eq-weapon/kestros), consists of a heavy metal
point about 9 inches long attached to a shaft of wood about 12 inches long, fletched with feathers
for stability. If used as a melee weapon, it is a light
[improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) (-4 penalty on attack rolls) and deals damage as a
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) of its size (critical multiplier ×2). Kestros darts come in a quiver
that holds 10 darts.

**Cost** 0.5 gp **Weight** 0.5 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition

**Note:** Stats are an extrapolation from the original quantity of 10.
