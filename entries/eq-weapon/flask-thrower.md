This staff has a cradle at the end, designed to hold alchemical weapons such as
[acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid). A flask thrower significantly extends the range of thrown substances
that deal splash damage, such as acid, [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), or
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), as well as that of tools such as
[tanglefoot bags](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag), [thunderstones](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone), or
[caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops).
