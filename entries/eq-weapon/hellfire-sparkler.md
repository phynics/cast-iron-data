**Price** 250 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This large barrel-shaped sparkler is imbued with mysterious, hellish forces. The fuse can be lit as
a standard action; 1d4 rounds later, the sparkler crackles violently with sickly-green sparks that
take the form of an army of ferocious devils. Creatures within a 10-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) take 3d6 points of fire damage and become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 2
rounds; a target can attempt a DC 15 Will save to halve the damage and the duration of the shaken
condition. Crafting a hellfire sparkler requires a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
