This slender tube is strapped to one forearm and designed to be concealed beneath the sleeve, where
the wielder can fire a [featherweight dart](ref/eq-weapon/featherweight-dart) with a twist of the
wrist. The launcher functions by means of a tightly compressed spring, and must be reloaded and
re-cocked with a separate ramrod as a full-round action. After firing, the wearer can attempt a
Sleight of Hand check opposed by opponents' Perception checks to keep the wrist launcher concealed.
If you are proficient with [hand crossbows](ref/eq-weapon/hand-crossbow), you are also proficient
with wrist launchers.

**Cost** 200 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** - (small), - (medium); **Critical** -; **Range** 20 ft.; **Type** P; **Special** -\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic
