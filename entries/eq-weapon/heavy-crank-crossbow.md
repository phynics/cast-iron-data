This [heavy crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-crossbow) has a winch mounted below the stock. Up to 50
feet of [silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft) can be wound onto the winch as a full-round action
that provokes attacks of opportunity. The wound rope can be attached to a
[barbed bolt](ref/eq-weapon/barbed-arrow) or [grappling bolt](ref/eq-misc/arrow-grappling) as part
of a loading action. After firing such a bolt, the wielder can use the winch to retract the rope or
pull herself along the rope if the bolt is secure. When used with a barbed bolt, the winch provides
a +2 bonus on grapple combat maneuver checks to pull the target toward you. When used with a
grappling bolt, the winch provides a +2 bonus on Climb checks.

**Cost** 400 gp **Weight** 12 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×2; **Range** 120 ft.; **Type** P;
**Special** -\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic
