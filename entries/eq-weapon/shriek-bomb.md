**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 7\
**Price** 45 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This yellow ball of volatile resin has a hemp wick. Lighting the fuse is a move action; 1d3-1 rounds
later (a result of 0 rounds takes place at the end of your turn), the shriek bomb explodes, dealing
2d6 points of sonic damage to creatures in a 10-foot radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) (Fortitude DC 15
half). A shriek bomb can be thrown as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range
of 10 feet.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (20 magnesium + 30 myrrh + 5
saltpeter)/congelation; **Craft DC** 20\
**Time** 10 minutes; **Tools** [alchemist's lab](ref/eq-misc/alchemists-lab); **Type** alchemical
weapon
