**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 154\
**Price** 50 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

A pox burster is an alchemically preserved animal bladder or gourd that has been filled with toxic,
rotting materials. You can throw a pox burster as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon).
Treat this attack as a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. A direct hit forces a
target to immediately make a DC 13 Fortitude save or contract
[filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever). Every space adjacent to the target square of the pox burster
is covered in disease-causing filth. For the next minute, any creature that is injured while in one
of these spaces must also make a DC 9 Fortitude save or contract filth fever.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
