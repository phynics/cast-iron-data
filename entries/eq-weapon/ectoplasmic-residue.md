**Price** 20 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This flask is filled with the detritus of minor ectoplasmic phantoms and similar creatures. You can
throw a flask of ectoplasmic residue as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon). Treat this
attack as a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. A direct hit deals no damage, but
the target must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d4 rounds. Every
creature within 5 feet of the point where the flask hits must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be
shaken for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
