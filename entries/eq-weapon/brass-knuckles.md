These weapons fit snugly around the knuckles and allow you to deal lethal damage with an unarmed
strike. You may hold, but not wield, a weapon or other object in a hand wearing brass knuckles. You
may cast a spell with a somatic component while wearing brass knuckles if you make a successful
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC 10 + the level of the spell you're casting). Monks
are proficient with brass knuckles. Brass knuckles can't be disarmed.

**Cost** 1 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d2 (small), 1d3 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special**
[monk](ref/misc/monk), see text\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Simple\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Close; Monk
