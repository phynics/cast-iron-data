**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 20, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 209, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 143

Essentially a longer version of a [scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar), but with a thinner blade, the
elven curve blade is exceptionally rare. You receive a +2 circumstance bonus to your CMD whenever a
foe attempts to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) your elven curve blade because of its flexible metal.

You can use the [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) feat to apply your Dexterity modifier
instead of your Strength modifier on attack rolls with an elven curve blade that is sized for you,
even though it isn't a light weapon.

**Cost** 80 gp **Weight** 7 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** 18-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special** -\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Heavy

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
The Elven curved blade has the [_agile_ weapon quality](ref/rule/equipment-errata).

You can apply your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier to attack rolls when
wielding an agile weapon sized for you, even though it isn't a [finesse](ref/rule/equipment-errata)
weapon.
