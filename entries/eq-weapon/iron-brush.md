This is an iron-handled version of a scholar's brush with a sharpened handle. Though it does little
damage, it is easily concealed (you gain a +2 bonus on Sleight of Hand checks made to conceal an
iron brush on your body) and can be thrown short distances, or used to jab at key nerve clusters and
acupuncture points to surprisingly devastating effect.

**Cost** 2 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 1d2 (small), 1d3 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** P; **Special** -\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Close
