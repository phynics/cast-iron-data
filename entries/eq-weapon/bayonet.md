**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 17, Adventurer's Armory pg 2, Pirates of the Inner Sea pg 19,
Advanced Player's Guide pg 176, Skull and Shackles Player's Guide pg 6, Pathfinder \#71: Rasputin
Must Die! pg 67

Bayonets are close combat weapons designed to fit into the grooves or muzzles of crossbows and
firearms. They allow you to make melee attacks with these weapons but render them temporarily
useless as ranged weapons. Attaching or removing a bayonet is a move action.

**Bayonet, Socket:** A socket bayonet fits onto a lug mounted on the barrel of some modern firearms.
It has the same statistics as a bayonet, but a firearm fitted with a bayonet lug can be fired while
the bayonet is in place, albeit with a -2 penalty on attack rolls. Each bayonet is designed for a
specific model of firearm.
