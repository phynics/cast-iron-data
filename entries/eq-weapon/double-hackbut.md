This double-length rifle uses a pair of trunnions to mount its barrel into a swiveling mechanism
fastened to the top of a lightweight, two-wheeled carriage. It takes a full-round action to set up
the carriage. The carriage has a hind leg, allowing the wielder to wheel the device about and
immediately prop it for stability during combat. Unlike other two-handed firearms, you must fire the
double hackbut while it is mounted, or else firing it imparts a -4 penalty on attack rolls and the
recoil knocks you [prone](ref/rule/prone). A Large or larger creature can fire a double hackbut one
size smaller than itself without the gun's mounting as a normal two-handed weapon and without the
danger of being knocked prone, but takes the normal penalty for firing an
[inappropriately sized weapon](ref/misc/weapon-size). This is an early firearm.

**Cost** 4,000 gp **Weight** 18 lbs.\
**Damage** 2d10 (small), 2d12 (medium); **Critical** ×4; **Range** 50 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1-2 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 2\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
