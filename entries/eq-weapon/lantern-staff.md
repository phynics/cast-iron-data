**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 9

This long metal staff has reservoirs for lamp [oil](ref/eq-misc/oil), and a lantern-like structure
at its head. The lantern staff is fueled as and provides light as a
[hooded lantern](ref/eq-misc/hooded-lantern). While lit, attacks with the lantern staff deal 1 point
of fire damage in addition to the normal damage. Any effects that apply to a
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), except those that require it be used as a double weapon,
also apply to a lantern staff.

**Cost** 15 gp **Weight** 9 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special** see text\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Simple\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Hammers
