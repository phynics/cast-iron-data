**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 43, Ultimate Combat pg 137

This primitive firearm is merely a long tube that, when ignited, propels forth a short gout of flame
and a [javelin](ref/eq-weapon/javelin). Unlike other firearms, the fire lance is wildly imprecise,
and targets AC rather than touch AC. A fire lance is always treated as having the broken condition
for the purpose of determining the effects of a misfire. A fire lance uses a javelin and 2 doses of
[black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose) as ammunition. This is an early firearm.

**Cost** 25 gp **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×4; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** P; **Special** -\
**Misfire** 1-4 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 1\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
