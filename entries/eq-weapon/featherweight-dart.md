These extremely small and lightweight darts function like blowgun darts, except they are used to
deliver poisons surreptitiously instead of dealing damage. When you hit with a dart, the poison is
injected into the target while the dart breaks off and falls away, leaving the wound hard to spot.
Any amount of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), [hardness](ref/umr/hardness), or
similar protection prevents the featherweight dart from delivering its injected poison. Anyone
examining the target must succeed at a DC 20 Perception check to find the puncture wound, while
finding the dart itself at the location where it fell requires a successful DC 15 Perception check.
This ammunition can be used in both [blowguns](ref/eq-weapon/blowgun) and
[wrist launchers](ref/eq-weapon/wrist-launcher).

**Cost** 0.1 gp **Weight** -\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition
