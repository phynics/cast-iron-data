**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 20, Ultimate Combat pg 130

This weapon is used to augment unarmed martial techniques. It consists of an 8- to 10-inch-long,
dual-pointed steel spike set on swivels and mounted on a ring, so that it can be spun around at high
speeds when slipped over the wielder's ring finger. The ring prevents the wielder from being
disarmed and turns unarmed strikes into piercing attacks.

**Cost** 3 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 1d2 (small), 1d3 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
see text\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Exotic\
