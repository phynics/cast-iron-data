**Sources:** Pathfinder \#91: Battle of Bloodmarch Hills pg 72\
**Price** 250 gp; **Weight** 20 lbs.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This [larger version](ref/rule/greater-splash-weapon) of a
[tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag) entangles its target as normal and requires a
successful DC 20 Reflex saving throw to avoid being stuck to the floor. In addition, it can affect
Huge and smaller creatures, and has a range increment of 60 feet. A creature that is glued to the
floor can break free by succeeding at a DC 30 Strength check or by dealing 25 points of damage to
the goo with a slashing weapon.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 35
