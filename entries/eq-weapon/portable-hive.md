**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This large flask holds a swarm of angry wasps. A portable hive can be thrown as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon), and when it hits a grid intersection or a creature, a
[wasp swarm](ref/monster/wasp-wasp-swarm) appears in a 10-foot-by-10-foot square at the point of
impact. This swarm does not have the distraction or poison special abilities, and it disperses after
1d4 rounds. Crafting a portable hive requires a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
