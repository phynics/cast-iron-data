**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 29\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

These small paper or cloth cylinders or spheres are filled with a tiny charge of black powder. One
round after lighting a firecracker in an adjacent square, the firework detonates with a loud bang,
dealing 1d2+1 points of nonlethal damage to any creatures in the square and
[deafening](ref/misc/deafened) them for 1d4 rounds (Fortitude DC 11 halves damage and duration of
deafness). The bang also causes unintelligent animals within 20 feet of it to become
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for 1d3 rounds; an animal can overcome this effect with a
successful DC 11 Will save or if an adjacent ally calms the animal with a successful DC 11 Handle
Animal check (an immediate action).

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (1 black powder + 1
saltpeter)/calcination; **Craft DC** 20\
**Time** 10 minutes; **Tools** [crucible](ref/eq-misc/crucible); **Type** alchemical weapon
