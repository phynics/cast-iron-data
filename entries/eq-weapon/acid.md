**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 30, Ultimate Equipment pg 107, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 158\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

You can throw a flask of acid as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon). Treat this attack
as a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. A direct hit deals 1d6 points of acid
damage. Every creature within 5 feet of the point where the acid hits takes 1 point of acid damage
from the splash.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (10 brimstone + 8 salt + 1
saltpeter)/distilation; **Craft DC** 15\
**Time** 1 day; **Tools** [retort](ref/eq-misc/retort); **Type** alchemical weapon
