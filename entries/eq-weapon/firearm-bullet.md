The ammunition of most one-handed and two-handed firearms, firearm bullets typically take the form
of small balls of lead or some other metal. Thirty bullets weigh half a pound.

**Cost** 1 gp **Weight** 0.0166 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Exotic

**Note:** Weight is approximate, based on 30 bullets weighing half a pound; 15 bullets weigh 0.25
lb, and 3 bullets weigh 0.05 lb.
