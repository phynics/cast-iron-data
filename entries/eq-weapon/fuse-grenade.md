**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This hollow clay container holds a small charge of black powder and a slow-burning fuse. Lighting
the fuse is a move action; 1d3 rounds later the grenade explodes, dealing 2d6 points of bludgeoning
damage and 1d6 points of fire damage in a 10-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) (Reflex DC 15
halves). You throw a fuse grenade as if it were a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon).
Crafting this item is a DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
