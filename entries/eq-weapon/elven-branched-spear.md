**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 19

Behind this long pole weapon's spearhead, several short branches project from the shaft at irregular
intervals, each angled forward and tipped with a smaller leaf like blade. When you make attacks of
opportunity provoked by movement, you gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls. You can use the
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) feat to apply your Dexterity modifier instead of your
Strength modifier on attack rolls with an elven branched spear sized for you, even though it isn't a
light weapon. Elves treat elven branched spears as martial weapons.

**Cost** 20 gp **Weight** 10 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[brace](ref/misc/brace), [reach](ref/misc/reach)\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Spears
