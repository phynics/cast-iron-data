**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 23\
**Price** 15 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This volatile liquid is usually dispensed from a spray atomizer, which disperses the liquid in a
15-foot cone. Congealer spray causes the surface of an ooze creature to stiffen into a leathery
material. Ooze and plant creatures in the affected area become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for
1d4 rounds (Fortitude DC 15 negates). If an affected creature has the [split](ref/umr/split)
defensive ability, it cannot benefit from that ability until the effects of the congealer spray wear
off, regardless of whether the creature succeeded at its saving throw.

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (4 realgar + 5 salt + 5
urea)/distillation; **Craft DC** 20\
**Time** 10 minutes; **Tools** [retort](ref/eq-misc/retort); **Type** alchemical weapon
