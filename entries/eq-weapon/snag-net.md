**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 33, Advanced Race Guide pg 201, Ultimate Equipment pg 21

This short, wide net is covered in barbed loops and slipknots. It works like a typical
[net](ref/eq-weapon/net), except it has the trip weapon special feature. If you entangle an opponent
and hold the trailing rope, on your turn in place of a melee attack you may make a combat maneuver
check to trip against that opponent; if you succeed, you may trip your opponent or deal 1 point of
piercing damage to it. The [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) DC to cast while entangled in a
snag net is 17 + the spell's level. The Escape Artist DC to escape a snag net is 22.

**Cost** 30 gp **Weight** 10 lbs.\
**Damage** - (small), - (medium); **Critical** -; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** P; **Special**
[trip](ref/misc/trip), see text\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Thrown
