**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 8

This dagger is used in the off hand to block attacks. When you
[fight defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or use
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) while wielding a dueling dagger, your bonus to AC
increases by 1. This benefit applies even if you did not attack with the dueling dagger. If you
aren't proficient with a dueling dagger, you treat it as a [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) and gain
no special benefits. Any effects that apply to daggers apply to dueling daggers.

**Cost** 12 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×2; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** P or S;
**Special** see text\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Light; Thrown
