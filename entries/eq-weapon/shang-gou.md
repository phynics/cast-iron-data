**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 18, Ultimate Combat pg 134

Forged in pairs, these solid metal blades end in curved hooks, while the hand guards are hammered
into outward-facing spiked crescents. Both the crescent and the tip of the hilt are sharpened for
combat. They can be wielded as single weapons or with two-weapon fighting styles. These weapons gain
the [disarm](ref/misc/disarm) special feature when they are used individually. A pair of shang gou
can also be formed into a single one-handed weapon to gain the [trip](ref/misc/trip) special
feature.

**Cost** 6 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special** disarm
or trip, [monk](ref/misc/monk)\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Monk
