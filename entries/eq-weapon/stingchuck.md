**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 5

A stingchuck is a foul bag made of a humanoid's head with the brain removed and the skull heavily
scored so that it bursts open when thrown. Normally filled with biting vermin, it acts as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon). When it hits, the vermin bite and sting the target,
dealing 1d6 points of damage and forcing a DC 11 Fortitude save to avoid being
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1d3 rounds. Each round a creature remains nauseated by a
stingchuck, it takes 1 additional point of damage from the biting vermin. All creatures within the
splash effect take 1 point of damage from the vermin but do not risk being nauseated.

**Cost** - **Weight** 9 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** B; **Special** see
text\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Simple
