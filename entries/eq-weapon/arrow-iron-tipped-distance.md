Used tactically for long-range attacks on troops, these arrows are fitted with heavy iron heads.
While these heads help them travel great distances, they make the arrows less effective at dealing
damage. Iron-tipped distance arrows increase their bow's range increment by 10 feet but take a -1
penalty on damage dealt per range increment (minimum 1 point of damage). They are sold in leather
quivers in quantities of 20.

**Cost** 0.05 gp **Weight** 0.2 lbs.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition

**Note:** Stats are an extrapolation from the original quantity of 20.
