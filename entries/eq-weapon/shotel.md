The shotel is a downward-curving sword designed to reach over or around an opponent's shield. You
gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls with a shotel against opponents using bucklers, light shields, or
heavy shields. The curve of a shotel's blade varies from sword to sword, and some models are
double-edged, while others are single-edged, with the back edge able to be braced against a shield
for additional power. Shotels are particularly useful for hooking mounted combatants and ripping
them from their steeds.

**Cost** 30 gp **Weight** 3 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** P; **Special**
[performance](ref/misc/performance)\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Heavy
