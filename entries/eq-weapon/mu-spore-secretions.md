**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

These secretions are only one of the many spores and fluids that the mu spores of Orv produce, but
this is one of the few that surface dwellers have learned to safely use. This clear secretion can be
added to any alchemical weapon, including an alchemist's bombs, as a move action. When mixed in this
way, the affected alchemical weapon ignores half the [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of any wooden
objects it strikes. Alternatively, against creatures primarily made of wood (such as
[treants](ref/monster/treant) or [wood golems](ref/monster/golem-wood-golem)), such weapons ignore
the first 5 points of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) the creatures has.
