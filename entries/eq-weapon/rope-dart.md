This deceptively complex weapon appears to be nothing more than a 12-foot rope attached to a
6-inch-long, conical metal spike. Similar to a [meteor hammer](ref/eq-weapon/meteor-hammer), it can
be whirled at great speeds, then aimed to strike and pierce opponents with great reach. Once it
strikes, the wielder can quickly retrieve the weapon with a tug of the rope as a free action.

**Cost** 1 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 20 ft.; **Type** P; **Special**
[blocking](ref/misc/blocking), [distracting](ref/misc/distracting), [monk](ref/misc/monk)\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Monk; Thrown
