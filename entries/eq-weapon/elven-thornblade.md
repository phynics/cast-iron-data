This blade is suited for thrusts and parries, making it especially capable of inflicting deep,
penetrating wounds. A wielder with [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) can apply her Dexterity
modifier to attack rolls with an elven thornblade instead of her Strength modifier. An elven
thornblade grants no additional benefit when wielded two-handed. In addition, attacks with an elven
thornblade gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls made to confirm critical hits.

**Cost** 60 gp **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** 18-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** P or S;
**Special** see text\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
