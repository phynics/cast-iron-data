**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 7, Ultimate Equipment pg 20

This [khopesh](ref/eq-weapon/khopesh) has a silver blade polished to an almost blinding sheen and
etched with motifs of vines and leaves. Blades of life's defense can be bought in Anuli's Sword
Alley, and are becoming more popular as rumblings of an open war with Geb continue to grow ever
louder. Each blade is imbued with a shimmer of nonmagical might against undead that grants the
blade's bearer a +1 sacred bonus on saving throws against the abilities of undead. It is otherwise
treated as a standard khopesh.

**Cost** 170 gp **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** 19-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special**
[trip](ref/misc/trip)\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Heavy
