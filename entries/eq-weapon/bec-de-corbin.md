The bec de corbin is a polearm very similar to the [lucerne hammer](ref/eq-weapon/lucerne-hammer),
but the hammer head is slightly blunter and the spike is stouter and more hooked. You primarily
attack with the spike. You gain a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder)
medium or heavy armor with a bec de corbin.

**Cost** 15 gp **Weight** 12 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** -; **Type** B or P; **Special**
[brace](ref/misc/brace), [reach](ref/misc/reach), see text\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Martial\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Polearms
