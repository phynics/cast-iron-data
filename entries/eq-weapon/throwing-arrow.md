**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 19

Too long to use in a bow, the throwing arrow has no nock at the tail. Instead, it has a notch ahead
of the fletching where a [throwing arrow cord](ref/eq-weapon/throwing-arrow-cord) can be attached.
If thrown without the use of a cord, treat a throwing arrow as a [dart](ref/eq-weapon/dart).

**Cost** 0.5 gp **Weight** 0.5 lb.\
**Category** Ammunition; **Proficiency** Ammunition
