**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 57

This long-barreled musket shoots more precisely than other muskets but is slightly less damaging on
a critical hit. It can fire only [dragoon cartridges](ref/eq-weapon/dragoon-cartridge) or specially
modified [mage shot](ref/magic-weapon/mage-shot).

**Cost** 2,000 gp **Weight** 12 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d10 (small), 1d12 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** 60 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1-2 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 3\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
