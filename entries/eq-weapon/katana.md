**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 20, Ultimate Combat pg 131

Specifically constructed for [samurai](ref/class/samurai), katanas employ multiple types of steel
combined in a distinctive forging process. The result are swords noted for their wickedly sharp yet
slender, gently curved blades, designed to make graceful hacking strokes capable of severing
opponents' heads and limbs. Though finely balanced, these blades are difficult to master. Characters
can use a katana two-handed as a martial weapon, but must take the
[Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (katana) feat to use it one-handed.

**Cost** 50 gp **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Critical** 18-20/×2; **Range** -; **Type** S; **Special**
[deadly](ref/misc/deadly)\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Blades, Heavy
