**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 107\
**Price** 150 gp (adamantine), 100 gp (cold iron), 50 gp (iron), 70 gp (silver); **Weight** 1 lb.
(adamantine), 1 lb. (cold iron), 1 lb. (iron), 1 lb. (silver)

This variant of a [fuse grenade](ref/eq-weapon/fuse-grenade) has most of the black powder replaced
with metal pellets. When it explodes, it deals 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage, 1d6 points of
piercing damage, and 1d6 points of fire damage in a 10-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) (Reflex
DC 15 halves). A typical pellet grenade uses iron pellets, but they can instead be packed with
silver, cold iron, or adamantine, bypassing [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) as
appropriate to that material type. Like ammunition, the pellets are destroyed after one use.
Crafting a pellet grenade is a DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
