An alchemically fired piston in the head of this two-handed hammer strikes targets with astounding
force. Successful [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) attacks made with a piston maul deal an additional 4
points of damage. A piston maul requires a [thunderstone](ref/eq-weapon/thunderstone) to function;
otherwise, it functions as a [greatclub](ref/eq-weapon/greatclub). Inserting a thunderstone into the
weapon's compartment is a standard action, and powers the weapon for 24 hours, after which the
thunderstone is consumed.

**Cost** 70 gp **Weight** 15 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** B; **Special** see
text\
**Category** Two-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Hammers
