**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 107, Adventurer's Armory pg 19\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** -\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

_Originally known as "Desnan Candle Firework"._

When lit, this foot-long wooden tube launches a flaming pyrotechnic "candle" every round for 4
rounds. The projectiles deal 1 point of nonlethal damage and 1 point of fire damage if they hit; on
a critical hit, the target is also [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round. The projectiles shed
light as [candles](ref/eq-misc/candle) for 1 round and have a range increment of 5 feet. Attacking
with a star candle is a ranged touch attack and always has a -4 nonproficiency penalty. Crafting
this item is a DC 20 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
