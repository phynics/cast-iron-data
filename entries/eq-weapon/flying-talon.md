**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 8, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 208

This weapon consists of a three-pronged, barbed hook attached to a length of chain. Due to the
weapon's unwieldiness, you cannot make attacks of opportunity with a flying talon and do not
threaten any squares with it.

A flying talon has reach, so you can strike opponents 10 feet away with it. When using a flying
talon, you gain a +2 bonus on opposed attack rolls to disarm or trip an opponent (including the roll
to avoid being disarmed if such an attempt fails).

**Cost** 15 gp **Weight** 5 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** -; **Type** P or S; **Special**
[disarm](ref/misc/disarm), [reach](ref/misc/reach), [trip](ref/misc/trip), see text\
**Category** Light; **Proficiency** Exotic\
