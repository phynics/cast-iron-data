**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 43, Ultimate Combat pg 139

A combination weapon, this gun mixes a [coat pistol](ref/eq-weapon/coat-pistol) with a
[sword cane](ref/eq-weapon/sword-cane). The sword cane pistol is considered a double weapon for the
purpose of creating masterwork or magical versions of this weapon. The pistol attachment makes the
nature of the weapon a little more difficult to hide. An observer must succeed at a DC 15 Perception
check to realize that an undrawn sword cane pistol is a weapon rather than a simple walking stick;
the DC decreases to 5 if the observer is able to handle the weapon. A sword cane pistol uses either
a [bullet](ref/eq-weapon/firearm-bullet) and 1 dose of
[black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose) or a single
[alchemical cartridge](ref/eq-weapon/alchemical-cartridge) as ammunition. The sword part of the
weapon must be drawn in order to load the pistol part of the weapon. This is an early firearm.

**Cost** 775 gp **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Critical** ×3; **Range** 10 ft.; **Type** B and P;
**Special** -\
**Misfire** 1 (5 ft.); **Capacity** 1\
**Category** One-Handed; **Proficiency** Exotic
