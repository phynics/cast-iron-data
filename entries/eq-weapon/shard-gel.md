**Price** 25 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

This vivid green gel instantly crystallizes into needle-like shards upon contact with the air. You
can throw a flask of shard gel as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon). A direct hit
deals 1d4 points of piercing damage as the shards penetrate the target's flesh. The target's square
and each square adjacent to it are covered in shards that function like
[caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops). The caltrops last for 5 rounds before dissolving into harmless
green sludge. Any effect that deals sonic damage to an area (such as
[sound burst](ref/spell/sound-burst) or [shout](ref/spell/shout)) immediately destroys all gel
shards in that area. Crafting a flask of shard gel is a DC 20 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 20
