**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Category** Alchemical Weapons

The casing of this modified [fuse grenade](ref/eq-weapon/fuse-grenade) is partially filled with
animal dung or guano. You can throw a dung grenade as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. Any creature
directly hit by a dung grenade or in its splash radius takes 1d6 points of fire damage and is
covered with the grenade's malodorous contents, becoming [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1d4
rounds. A target can attempt a DC 15 Reflex saving throw to halve the damage and the duration of the
sickened condition. Crafting a dung grenade requires a successful DC 25 Craft (alchemy) check.

### Construction

Craft (Alchemy) DC 25
