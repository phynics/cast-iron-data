## Saint's Holy Regalia

**Sources:** Psychic Anthology pg 27\
This panoply is associated with devoted members of a good-aligned faith and the power of belief.

**Implements**

**Details:** Censer ([conjuration](ref/implement/conjuration)) and holy symbol
([abjuration](ref/implement/abjuration)).

### Resonant Power

**Font of Healing (Su)**

**Ability:** When you cast a cure spell (a spell with &quot&cure&quot& in its name), you restore 1
additional hit point for every 3 points of total mental focus invested in all of the associated
implements, to a maximum bonus equal to half the occultist's level. This doesn't apply to damage
dealt to undead with the spell.

**Flavor:** The panoply grants you mastery over healing magic.

### Base Focus Power

**Restoring Touch (Sp)**

**At 10th Level:** You can expend an additional point of mental focus to instead restore the same
amount of permanent ability drain.

**Standard:** You can expend 2 points of mental focus to cure a living creature you touch of
temporary [ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain). You cure an amount of temporary ability
damage to a single ability score equal to [one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of you
occultist level.

### Focus Powers

**Guardian Aura (Sp)**

**Fullround:** You can expend 3 points of mental focus to emit a 20-foot-radius aura of protection
that lasts for 1 minute. You and your allies within the aura gain a sacred bonus to AC equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your occultist level (minimum +1). Your speed is
reduced to 10 feet while this power is active, but you can dismiss it as a **free action**.

**Martyr's Benediction (Sp)**

**Immediate:** When you would be reduced to fewer than 0 hit points or killed (including by effects
that kill without dealing damage, such as [phantasmal killer](ref/spell/phantasmal-killer) and
[power word kill](ref/spell/power-word-kill)), you can expend 1 point of mental focus to restore a
number of hit points equal to 1d8 + your occultist level to all allies (other than you) within 30
feet.

In addition, if any healed ally is under a fear effect, she can immediately attempt a new saving
throw to end it.

**Rebuke Anathema (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can present the holy symbol used as the panoply's associated implement and expend
2 points of mental focus to keep a single creature at bay. Your alignment must be within one step of
that of the holy symbol's deity, and the creature's alignment must be more than one step away from
both your alignment and the deity's alignment. If neither is the case, this power is ineffective and
the spent mental focus is wasted. If the target creature fails a Will save, it can't move toward you
for a number of rounds equal to your occultist level.
