**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 55\
Illusion implements allow the occultist to distort the senses and cloak creatures from sight.

**Implements**

**Details:** Crystal, hat, mask, prism, ring.

### Resonant Power

**Distortion (Sp)**

**Flavor:** The implement allows its bearer to distort his form and location, protecting him from
harm.

**Special:** Creatures with [see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing), or similar abilities ignore the miss chance from this ability.

**Standard:** The bearer can gain a [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) miss chance equal to 5% for
every point of mental focus invested in the implement (to a maximum of 5%) until the next time the
bearer makes an attack. If this miss chance reaches 50%, it doesn't increase further, but the bearer
gains all the benefits of [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility).

At 2nd level: The miss chance maximum 10%.

At 4th level: The miss chance maximum 15%.

At 6th level: The miss chance maximum 20%.

At 8th level: The miss chance maximum 25%.

At 10th level: The miss chance maximum 30%.

At 12th level: The miss chance maximum 35%.

At 14th level: The miss chance maximum 40%.

At 16th level: The miss chance maximum 45%.

At 18th level: The miss chance maximum 50%.

### Base Focus Power

**Minor Figment (Sp)**

**At 7th Level:** The figment lasts for a number of minutes equal to your occultist level, and you
can cause the figment to change once during its duration when a specified condition occurs.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to create a minor figment. This can function as
either [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound) or [minor image](ref/spell/minor-image). In either case,
the effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to your occultist level.

### Focus Powers

**Cloak Image (Sp)**

**At 5th Level:** You can instead expend 2 points of mental focus to appear as a creature of a
different type, but you must remain the same size.

**At 7th Level:** You can expend 1 additional point of mental focus to target a willing creature
other than yourself with this ability.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to hide your appearance behind an illusion, as
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self). The effect lasts for 1 minute per occultist level you
possess.

**Color Beam (Sp)**

**Special:** Regardless of whether it succeeds at its save, the target is then immune to this effect
for 1 day. This is a mind-affecting illusion effect.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to unleash a beam of cascading colors at any
one target within 30 feet. Doing so requires a ranged touch attack. If the beam hits, the target is
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round if it has a number of Hit Dice equal to or lower than your
occultist level. A foe with a number of Hit Dice greater than your occultist level is instead
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 round. The foe can attempt a Will save to negate the effect.

**Masquerade (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level occultist

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to take on the appearance of another creature
that you touch. If the creature is not friendly to you, this requires a melee touch attack, but the
target doesn't receive a saving throw to negate the effect. You gain a circumstance bonus on
[Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks equal to your occultist level + 10, but only to look like the
creature touched. This bonus doesn't stack with the bonuses from polymorph spells or spells like
_[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)._

While using this ability, you can change your disguise as a **standard action** by touching a
different creature and assuming its appearance. You can end this effect at any time. This effect
lasts for 10 minutes per occultist level you possess, but you can renew the duration at any time by
expending 1 additional point of mental focus.

**Mirage (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 5th-level occultist

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to cloak an area in illusion, allowing you to
change the area's appearance. You can affect up to one 5-foot-cube area for every occultist level
you posses, changing the appearance in a way that seems natural to the surroundings, subject to GM
discretion. You could, for example, hide a door or passageway, make a table appear to be covered in
food, or even create the illusion of a pit. You can't make creatures or objects invisible with this
power, but you can change the appearance of an object or create an illusion for creatures to hide
behind.

This functions as [major image](ref/spell/major-image), and creatures that physically interact with
the illusion in any way can attempt a Will save to see through the _[mirage](ref/spell/mirage)._

**Shadow Beast (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 9th-level occultist

**Standard:** You can call forth one or more beasts made of shadow by expending 1 point of mental
focus. This functions as [shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration), but it can be used to
duplicate only the effects of _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells. Creatures created
with this spell deal 50% of the normal damage to those that disbelieve the illusion, and their
non-damaging effects have only a 50% chance of affecting disbelieving targets.

This can be used to duplicate any summon monster spell up to
[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5). Regardless of the spell duplicated, the creatures
remain for 1 round per occultist level you possess.

At 11th level: This can now duplicate summon monster +6.

At 13th level: This can now duplicate summon monster +7.

At 15th level: This can now duplicate summon monster +8.

At 17th level: This can now duplicate summon monster +9.

**Unseen (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 3rd-level occultist

**Special:** If the invisible creature makes an attack or otherwise takes an action that would cause
the invisibility to end, you can immediately expend 1 additional point of mental focus to allow the
creature to remain invisible if the creature is within 30 feet of you.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to become invisible, as _invisibility._ This
effect lasts for 1 minute per occultist level you possess.

You can expend 2 points of mental focus instead of 1 to use this power on a willing adjacent
creature instead of yourself. You can still see creatures you make invisible with this power.

### Sacred Implement

**Sources:** Occult Origins pg 15

**Deity**

**Details:** [Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber)

**Implement**

**Details:** mask

**Opposition School**

**Details:** conjuration

**Psychic Mirage (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to become invisible to a single target within 30
feet, as per _invisibility,_ and remain so for 1 minute per occultist level you have. This
invisibility doesn't affect other creatures. While within 30 feet of the target, you can expend 1
point of mental focus to read the target's surface thoughts as per
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts).
