**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 53\
Enchantment implements allow the occultist to befuddle the mind and charm his foes.

**Implements**

**Details:** Censer, crown, helm, musical instrument, necklace.

### Resonant Power

**Glorious Presence (Su)**

**Flavor:** The implement invokes the presence of those who have worn it in the past.

**Passive:** The implement's wearer gains a +1 competence bonus on all Charisma-based skill checks
and ability checks for every 2 points of mental focus invested in the implement, to a maximum bonus
of 1 + [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your occultist level.

### Base Focus Power

**Cloud Mind (Su)**

**Special:** The foe can attempt a Will saving throw to negate the effect. Whether or not it
succeeds at the save, the target is then immune to this effect for 1 day. This is a mind-affecting
effect.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to cloud the mind of one foe within 30 feet.
That foe is [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round if the number of Hit Dice it possesses is less than
or equal to or your occultist level. If it has more Hit Dice than your occultist level, it is
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round instead.

### Focus Powers

**Binding Pattern (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level occultist

**Special:** At the end of each of its turns, it can attempt another Will save to end the paralyzed
effect and instead be staggered for the remaining duration. This is a mind-affecting compulsion
effect.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to create a binding pattern of psychic energy
that causes a living creature to become [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed). The target must be within
30 feet of you, and it can attempt a Will saving throw to negate the effect. If it fails the save,
it is paralyzed for a number of rounds equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
occultist level.

**Forced Alliance (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 5th-level occultist

**Special:** This is a mind-affecting charm effect.

**Standard:** You can alter a living creature's mind, causing it to see you as a friend and ally. If
the creature is the same creature type as you, this power requires you to expend 1 point of mental
focus; if it's is not, you must expend 2 points instead. The creature can attempt a Will save to
negate this effect. If it fails the saving throw, the creature treats you as an ally and doesn't
attempt to harm you (although your allies are not protected).

You can call on the creature to aid you directly, but while doing so the creature can attempt a new
Will save at the end of each round to end the effect. This effect otherwise lasts 1 round per
occultist level you possess.

**Inspired Assault (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can inspire a living creature with a touch by expending 1 point of mental focus.
The creature receives a morale bonus on attack rolls. This bonus is equal to 1 + one-sixth of your
occultist level. This bonus also applies on saving throws against fear effects. This bonus lasts for
1 minute.

**Mental Discord (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to disrupt the mind of one living creature
within 30 feet. The creature can attempt a Will saving throw to negate the effect. If it fails the
save, the creature can't concentrate on any spell effects and must succeed at a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check to cast any spell (DC = 15 + double the spell level).
The affected creature takes a -4 penalty on this check if the spell includes a thought component.

The creature takes a -2 penalty on all Will saving throws while under this effect. This effect lasts
for 1 round per occultist level you possess.

**Mind Slumber (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 3rd-level occultist

**Special:** This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect. Regardless of whether it succeeds at the
save, the target is then immune to this effect for 24 hours.

**Standard:** By expending 1 point of mental focus, you can lull a living creature into a deep
mental slumber, forcing it to view the world as a dream. The target must be within 30 feet of you,
and it can attempt a Will saving throw to negate the effect. If it fails the save, the creature
falls asleep, although it still perceives the world around it as a dream. This doesn't cause the
creature to fall [prone](ref/rule/prone), but it does drop whatever it is holding. The creature
can't take any actions while in this state, but it isn't [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) - it
perceives danger as part of its dream.

The creature remains asleep for a number of rounds equal to your occultist level or until it takes
any amount of damage, which immediately wakes it. At the end of each of its turns, it receives
another Will saving throw to end the effect. The creature can be manipulated or examined without
waking it, but it can't be moved from its square without waking it.

**Obey (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can issue a command to one living creature by expending 1 point of mental focus.
This functions as [command](ref/spell/command). The target must be within 30 feet and capable of
understanding your order. The target can attempt a Will save to negate this effect. If the creature
is the same creature type as you, it takes a -2 penalty on this saving throw.

### Sacred Implement

**Sources:** Occult Origins pg 15

**Deity**

**Details:** [Aroden](ref/faith/aroden)

**Implement**

**Details:** necklace

**Opposition School**

**Details:** illusion

**Forgotten (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can expend 3 points of mental focus to target a single creature within 30 feet
using [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory). This ability can be used only to cause the target to
forget the last 5 minutes of interaction with you.
