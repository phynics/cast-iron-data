**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 54\
Implements focused on evocation grant the ability to create and direct energy to protect and to
destroy.

**Implements**

**Details:** Gloves, rod, staff, wand.

### Resonant Power

**Intense Focus (Su)**

**Ability:** A spellcaster who bears the implement can add the implement as an additional focus
component for any of his damaging evocation spells that have an instantaneous duration or focus
powers with an instantaneous duration. If he does so, the spell or focus power deals 1 additional
point of damage of the same type to each creature for every 2 points of mental focus invested in the
implement, to a maximum of 1 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your occultist level.

**Flavor:** The implement channels and enhances the effects of damaging evocations.

### Base Focus Power

**Energy Ray (Sp)**

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** Yes

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to unleash a ray of pure energy as a ranged
touch attack. This ray has a range of 30 feet. The ray deals an amount of energy damage equal to 1d6
points. When you unleash an _energy ray,_ you must decide what type of damage it deals (acid, cold,
electricity, or fire).

At 3rd level: The damage 2d6.

At 5th level: The damage 3d6.

At 7th level: The damage 4d6.

At 9th level: The damage 5d6.

At 11th level: The damage 6d6.

At 13th level: The damage 7d6.

At 15th level: The damage 8d6.

At 17th level: The damage 9d6.

At 19th level: The damage 10d6.

### Focus Powers

**Energy Blast (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 5th-level occultist

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** Yes

**Standard:** You can expend 2 points of mental focus to unleash a blast of energy. This blast has a
range of 100 feet, and deals 5d6 points of energy damage. The blast deals damage to each creature in
a 20-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst), but each affected creature can attempt a Reflex save to
halve the damage. When you unleash an _energy blast,_ you must decide what type of damage it deals
(acid, cold, electricity, or fire).

At 7th level: The damage 6d6.

At 9th level: The damage 7d6.

At 11th level: The damage 8d6.

At 13th level: The damage 9d6.

At 15th level: The damage 10d6.

At 17th level: The damage 11d6.

At 19th level: The damage 12d6.

**Energy Ward (Sp)**

**At 13th Level:** The resistance becomes 15.

**At 19th Level:** The resistance increases to 20.

**Prereq:** 7th-level occultist

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to surround yourself in a swirling ward of
energy. When you activate this power, you must select acid, cold, electricity, or fire. You gain
[resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 against that energy type, and whenever a creature hits you with
a melee weapon or natural weapon, that creature takes 1d6 points of energy damage of the chosen
type. Attacks made with melee weapons with reach don't cause the attacker to take this damage. The
_energy ward_ lasts for 1 round per occultist level.

**Light Matrix (Sp)**

**At 5th Level:** You can direct the _light matrix_ to make a melee touch attack against one foe
within 30 feet. If the attack hits, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save or be
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4 rounds by a flash of light. If the attack hits, regardless of
whether the target succeeds at the save, the _light matrix_ immediately ends.

**Move:** You can direct it to move up to 30 feet away from you. If the _light matrix_ is ever more
than 30 feet away from you, it returns to hover over your shoulder.

**Special:** Unless ended early, the _light matrix_ has a duration of 1 hour per occultist level you
possess. You can't have more than one _light matrix_ in existence at one time. If you use this power
again, any previous _light matrices_ immediately end.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to create a glowing orb of swirling lights that
obeys your commands. This orb illuminates the area like the [light](ref/spell/light) spell. The orb
can be commanded to hover over your shoulder, moving with you.

**Radiance (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a weapon to cause it to glow with light by expending 1 point of mental
focus. The weapon produces light like a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch). For the purpose of
[darkness](ref/spelldef/darkness) spells and abilities, this effect counts as a light spell of a
level equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your occultist level. This light lasts for
1 minute per occultist level you possess.

In addition, whenever the weapon scores a critical hit against a foe, the wielder can choose to end
the effect, causing the foe to be surrounded by the _radiance_ for 1d4 rounds. While illuminated in
this way, the target can't benefit from [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), nor can it attempt Stealth checks to avoid being seen. All
attack rolls made against an illuminated foe receive a +2 circumstance bonus.

**Shape Mastery (Su)**

**Ability:** As part of casting an evocation spell with an area of effect, you can expend a number
of points of mental focus up to your Intelligence modifier to exclude an equal number of squares
from the area.

**Wall of Power (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 9th-level occultist

**Standard:** By expending 1 point of mental focus, you can create a wall of pure energy with a
length of up to 5 feet per occultist level you possess. This wall is 10 feet high and 1 foot thick.
It doesn't block passage, line of sight, or line of effect, but does deal damage to anyone passing
through it.

The wall deals 2d6 points of energy damage + 1 point of energy damage per occultist level you
possess. You must select acid, cold, electricity, or fire when you create the wall to determine the
type of damage it deals. The wall must be straight, and can't pass through a creature's space when
created. The wall lasts for 1 round per occultist level you possess.

### Sacred Implement

**Sources:** Occult Origins pg 15

**Deity**

**Details:** [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug)

**Implement**

**Details:** figurine

**Opposition School**

**Details:** abjuration

**Footsteps of the Rough Beast (Su)**

**Move:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to cause the ground in a 5-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) centered on you to crumble and rupture, becoming
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). If you remain stationary, in subsequent rounds
you can [concentrate](ref/rule/concentration) on this area as though concentrating on a spell by
expending 1 additional mental focus each round. As long as you maintain concentration, the radius of
the difficult terrain increases by 5 feet (maximum 30-foot radius) each round.

**Special:** You immediately cease concentrating if you move from your originating location.
Difficult terrain created this way remains for 24 hours before returning to its original state.
