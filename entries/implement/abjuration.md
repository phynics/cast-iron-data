**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 50\
Abjuration implements are objects associated with protection and wards.

**Implements**

**Details:** Amulet, armor, bell, bracers, brooch, cloak, holy symbol, shield.

### Resonant Power

**Warding Talisman (Su)**

**Flavor:** The implement wards against adverse effects.

**Passive:** Whoever wears (or holds, for bells) the implement gains a +1 resistance bonus on saving
throws for every 2 points of mental focus invested in the implement, to a maximum bonus of 1 +
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your occultist level

### Base Focus Power

**Mind Barrier (Sp)**

**Special:** You can activate this ability as an **immediate action**, but doing so costs 2 points
of mental focus instead of 1.

**Swift:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to create a shield of mental energy around you
that protects you from harm. The shield prevents a total of 2 points of damage per occultist level
you possess. It lasts until the start of your next turn or until exhausted.

For example, if you are 5th level, the mind barrier protects you from 10 points of damage; if you
are hit by an attack that would deal 12 points of damage, the mind barrier is exhausted and you take
2 points of damage.

### Focus Powers

**Aegis (Su)**

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus and touch a suit of armor or a shield to grant
it an enhancement bonus. This bonus is equal to 1 + one-sixth of your occultist level. Enhancement
bonuses gained via this ability stack with those of the armor or shield, to a maximum total
enhancement bonus of +5.

You can also imbue the armor or shield with any one armor or shield special ability that has an
equivalent enhancement bonus less than or equal to your maximum bonus granted by this ability by
reducing the granted enhancement bonus by the appropriate amount. The item must have an enhancement
bonus of at least +1 (from the item itself or from _aegis_) to gain an armor or shield special
ability. In either case, these bonuses last for 1 minute.

**Energy Shield (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 3rd-level occultist

**Special:** You can activate the energy shield as an **immediate action** by expending 2 points of
mental focus instead of 1.

**Swift:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to surround yourself with a shield that protects
you from energy damage. Whenever you take acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage, the shield
absorbs the damage (as [protection from energy](ref/spell/protection-from-energy)). The energy
shield can absorb up to 5 points of energy damage per occultist level you possess.

This shield lasts for 1 minute or until its power is exhausted. Its effect doesn't stack with
itself, with _[protection from energy](ref/spell/protection-from-energy),_ or with
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy).

**Globe of Negation (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 11th-level occultist

**Standard:** You can expend 3 points of mental focus to create a stationary _globe of negation._
This _globe_ is 10 feet in diameter and cancels any spell effect that is cast into or through its
area. This functions as [globe of invulnerability](ref/spell/globe-of-invulnerability), but it
affects spells of any level.

The _globe_ can negate a total number of spell levels equal to your occultist level, after which the
_globe_ collapses. Spells that exceed the number of remaining levels remove all remaining levels,
but are weakened; any creatures targeted by such spells receive a +4 circumstance bonus on any
saving throws against the spells' effects.

The _globe_ lasts for a number of rounds equal to your occultist level. It has no effect on spells
originating within the _globe_ - only on those that enter its area as or after they are cast.

**Loci Sentry (Sp)**

**Special:** The sentry lasts for up to 1 hour per occultist level you possess, or until triggered.
When you set the sentry, you can set any number of specific creatures to be immune to the sentry and
thus avoid triggering it.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to set a ward about an area. This area has a
maximum radius of 10 feet + 5 feet per occultist level you possess. Whenever a creature enters the
area, a sentry manifested by your psyche appears and strikes out at the intruder. The affected
creature must succeed at a Will save or be [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round. You are immediately
aware of the sentry's activation.

**Planar Ward (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can expend 2 points of mental focus to surround yourself with a planar ward.
Creatures not native to the plane that you are currently on take a -4 penalty on attacks against
you, and you receive a +4 circumstance bonus on saving throws against the spells,
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural abilities of such creatures.
This ward lasts for 1 minute.

**Unraveling (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 5th-level occultist

**Special:** If the effect is created by psychic magic, you receive a +5 bonus on your caster level
check to unravel it. If the effect targets an object in your possession, you automatically succeed
at the check.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to unravel a magical effect. This functions as
a targeted [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) spell, but you must be adjacent to the effect to
unravel it.

### Sacred Implement

**Sources:** Occult Origins pg 15

**Deity**

**Details:** [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma)

**Implement**

**Details:** brooch

**Opposition School**

**Details:** necromancy

**Grandmother's Ward (Su)**

**Special:** The warded area moves with you and can be used only defensively. Any attempt to force
the ward against an undead creature immediately ends the effect.

**Standard:** You can expend 3 points of psychic focus to create a 5-foot-radius ward centered on
you for 1 round per occultist level. This ward burns undead creatures, dealing 1d4 damage when the
undead enter the ward and each round they begin their turns within this area of effect.

At 2nd level: The damage 2d4.

At 4th level: The damage 3d4.

At 6th level: The damage 4d4.

At 8th level: The damage 5d4.

At 10th level: The damage 6d4.

At 12th level: The damage 7d4.

At 14th level: The damage 8d4.

At 16th level: The damage 9d4.

At 18th level: The damage 10d4.

At 20th level: The damage 11d4.
