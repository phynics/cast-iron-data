**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 51\
Implements used in conjuration allow the occultist to perform magic that transports or calls
creatures.

**Implements**

**Details:** Bowl, brazier, compass, figurine, lantern, mirror.

### Resonant Power

**Casting Focus (Su)**

**Ability:** The bearer can add the implement as an additional focus component to any conjuration
spell he casts that has a duration measured in rounds per level. If he does so, he adds 1 to his
caster level for every 2 points of mental focus stored in the implement (to a maximum bonus equal to
your occultist level).

**Flavor:** The implement empowers the bearer's ties to the worlds beyond, allowing his spells to
maintain their power for a longer period of time.

**Special:** This increase applies only when determining the duration of the spell. Apply this
increase after other effects that adjust a spell's duration, such as
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell).

### Base Focus Power

**Servitor (Sp)**

**Special:** You can't have more than one servitor in effect at a time. At any time, you can expend
1 point of mental focus as a **free action** to extend the duration of an active servitor by 1
minute.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to summon a servitor. This ability functions as
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1), but you can use it only to summon a single creature,
and the effect lasts for 1 minute.

At 4th level: This now behaves as summon monster +2.

At 7th level: This now behaves as summon monster +3.

At 10th level: This now behaves as summon monster +4.

At 13th level: This now behaves as summon monster +5.

At 16th level: This now behaves as summon monster +6.

At 19th level: This now behaves as summon monster +7.

### Focus Powers

**Conjure Implement (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to conjure any item that qualifies as an
implement that you know how to use. This implement can be used as the item in question, functioning
as a masterwork version of that item, and can also be used to cast your psychic spells, even though
you did not select it at the beginning of the day.

**Special:** If you have more than one implement for the same school, decide which set of spells the
conjured implement will grant when you first conjure it. The implement can't be used to store mental
focus or create any effects that require mental focus. The implement lasts for 10 minutes per
occultist level you possess.

**Flesh Mend (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 3rd-level occultist

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to heal a living creature with a touch. The
creature is healed an amount of damage equal to 1d8 + your occultist level. This has no effect on
undead creatures.

At 7th level: The healing 2d8 + your occultist level.

At 11th level: The healing 3d8 + your occultist level.

At 15th level: The healing 4d8 + your occultist level.

At 19th level: The healing 5d8 + your occultist level.

**Mind Steed (Sp)**

**At 5th Level:** The mount's base land speed increases by 20 feet.

**At 9th Level:** If you expend 1 additional point of mental focus, the mount can fly at a speed of
60 feet with good maneuverability, but doing so reduces the mount's duration to 1 minute per
occultist level you possess.

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to conjure a spectral horse. This horse can be
either Medium or Large, but it otherwise functions as [mount](ref/spell/mount). The horse lasts for
10 minutes per occultist level you possess.

**Psychic Fog (Sp)**

**At 7th Level:** You can expend 1 additional point of mental focus when creating this fog, causing
it to become more tangible and function as [solid fog](ref/spell/solid-fog), except it doesn't slow
down any creature that has psychic powers or that can cast psychic spells.

When you create a solid fog in this way, its duration is reduced to 1 round per occultist level you
possess.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level occultist

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to create a cloud of fog. This fog lasts for 1
minute per occultist level you possess. It functions as [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud), except it
can't be dispersed by wind.

**Purge Corruption (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 5th-level occultist

**Standard:** You can expend 1 point of mental focus to draw out the corruption from a creature. You
must touch the target with your implement to use this power. This ability functions as either
[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison) or [remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease),
using your occultist level as the caster level. Each use of this ability can cure only one poison or
one disease.

**Side Step (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can create a temporary fissure in space by expending 1 point of mental focus. You
can use this ability as part of a **move action** taken to move. The fissure begins in any square
you designate and allows you to teleport to any other square you can see within 10 feet per
occultist level.

Stepping between these locations requires you to expend 5 feet of movement, and the movement through
the rift does not provoke attacks of opportunity. This otherwise functions as
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport).

**Prereq:** 7th-level occultist

### Sacred Implement

**Sources:** Occult Origins pg 15

**Deity**

**Details:** [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus)

**Implement**

**Details:** bowl

**Opposition School**

**Details:** transmutation

**Amber Prison (Su)**

**Ability:** You can expend 3 points of mental focus to entomb a creature within 40 feet in an
amber-like prison, rendering it [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) and
[unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious) for 1 minute per occultist level you have. The amber prison has
20 hit points; destroying the prison frees the creature.

**Special:** A successful Reflex save negates this entombment. Whether or not the saving throw is
successful, the creature can't be the target of this focus power again for 1 day.
