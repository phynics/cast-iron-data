When the sting of betrayal lingers beyond the grave, it can manifest as a disjointed mass of
confused and paranoid spiritual energy that can turn even the closest of allies against one another.

**Betrayal**

**Challenge rating:** 7

**XP** 3,200\
CE persistent haunt (30-ft. radius)\
**Caster Level** 7th

**Notice:** Perception or Sense Motive DC 20 (to see the outllines of a dozen cloaked figures begin
to form or feel a suden sense of suspicion)

**Hit points:** 31

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

As dozens of cloaked figures swarm the area, the line between friend and foe blurs, and paranoia
descends upon everyone within the haunt's area, as per [confusion](ref/spell/confusion) (Will DC 15
negates). Furthermore, creatures within the haunt's area must attempt a DC 15 Will saving throw
against the haunt's curse. Until it is removed, the curse forces its victims to attempt saving
throws against spells, including those designated harmless. Additionally, the victims cannot benefit
from any effect that requires them to treat another creature as an ally, such as
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking) or [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2).
[Remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) can remove this effect.

### Destruction

The betrayal haunt is destroyed if a victim falls unconscious or dies because of damage it took on
behalf of another creature in the area. Any spell or ability that allows for the transfer of damage,
such as [shield other](ref/spell/shield-other) or a [life](ref/mystery/life) oracle's lifelink
ability, satisfies this haunt's requirement. This doesn't work if the characters in question
colluded to manufacture the damage transfer to destroy the haunt; it works only if it was a truly
selfless act of protecting another.
