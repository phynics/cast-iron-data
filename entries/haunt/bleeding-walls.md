**Bleeding Walls**

**Challenge rating:** 5

**XP** 1,600\
CE haunt (5ft. by 20 ft. hallway)\
**Caster Level** 5th

**Notice:** Perception DC 20 (to hear the sound of soft sobbing)

**Hit points:** 10

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

When this haunt is triggered, thick rivulets of blood course down the walls accompanied by the
shrill shriek of a woman's scream. All creatures in the hallway are targeted by a
[fear](ref/spell/fear) spell (save DC 16).

### Destruction

The body of the maid entombed behind the walls must be extracted and given a proper burial.
