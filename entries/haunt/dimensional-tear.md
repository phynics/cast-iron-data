**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 133

**Dimensional Tear**

**Challenge rating:** 8

**XP** 4,800\
N persistent variant haunt (30-ft. radius)\
**Aura** moderate conjuration (calling)\
**Caster Level** 13th

**Notice:** Perception DC 18 (to see rippling, rainbow-hued distortions in the air)

**Hit points:** 27

**Weakness:** negative energy (see [echoes of the first world](ref/rule/echoes-of-the-first-world))

**Trigger:** proximity

### Effect

As soon as a Small or larger creature enters the area of a dimensional tear, it is affected as if by
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift) and must succeed at a Will save (DC 21) or be immediately
transported to [the First World](ref/rule/the-first-world). When this occurs, a creature from the
First World (determined randomly from the [summon nature's ally VI](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-6)
list) is teleported to a space adjacent to the echo, determined by the GM. The creature has a
starting attitude of hostile.

### Overcharge

Determine the First World creature called using the
[summon nature's ally VII](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-7) list instead, and then add the
[fey creature template](ref/template/fey-creature).

### Destruction

A character with _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ prepared can attempt to
[counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) the echo's effect. Successfully doing so destroys the
dimensional tear.
