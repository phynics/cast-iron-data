Overwhelming, unresolved guilt - or horrific death by thousands of tiny teeth - can lead to the
manifestation of ghostly vermin swarms. The haunted area often displays signs of ancient rodent
infestation.

**Spectral Vermin**

**Challenge rating:** 5

**XP** 1,600\
CE free-roaming persistent vaporous haunt (30-ft.-by-30-ft. room)\
**Caster Level** 5th

**Notice:** Perception DC 15 (to hear scratching in walls)

**Hit points:** 22

**Weakness:** tricked by [hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead)

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

This free-roaming haunt wanders the vacant halls of old manors and dank dungeons. When the haunt is
triggered, the skeletal remains of spectral rats burst forth from every crack and crevice of the
room, scurrying over the area and voraciously consuming trespassers, as the
[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm) spell.

### Destruction

A character must ritually kill a common cat in the area. The cat's ghost manifests and consumes the
ectoplasmic swarm over the course of 1 week, earning both the cat and the rats permanent rest.
