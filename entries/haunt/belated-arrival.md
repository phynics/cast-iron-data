**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 176\
The regret of those who arrived too late to spare others a terrible fate can manifest as a haunt. A
belated arrival haunt often arises near another haunt or undead, representing the fate that these
regretful spirits were not fast enough to stop. A belated arrival haunt is often chained to a
[ghost](ref/template/ghost) but a [necrocraft](ref/monster/necrocraft) is a truly horrifying
alternative.

**Belated Arrival**

**Challenge rating:** 5

**XP** 1,600\
N chained haunt (25-ft. radius)\
**Caster Level** 9th

**Notice:** Perception DC 20 (to hear running footsteps)

**Hit points:** 22

**Weakness:** [slow](ref/spell/slow)

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

The spectral forms of several people take faltering steps before falling to their knees and wailing
with grief. All creatures in the area are [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) (no save, as per
[waves of exhaustion](ref/spell/waves-of-exhaustion)) and are slowed for 13 minutes as per
_[slow](ref/spell/slow)_ unless they succeed at a DC 16 Will saving throw.

### Destruction

These spirits find rest only if those they could not save lead them to the afterlife.
