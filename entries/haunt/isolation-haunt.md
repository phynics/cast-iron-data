**Isolation Haunt**

**Challenge rating:** -

**XP** 0\
NE haunt (30-ft. radius)\
**Caster Level** 5th

**Notice:** Perception DC 20 (to notice ambient sound disappear and other sounds grow quiet)

**Hit points:** -

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

Each targets believes it is alone in the world; it cannot perceive, target, nor interact with anyone
it considers an ally. This is a mind-affecting effect. A creature that succeeds at a DC 12 Will save
negates this effect.

### Destruction

A boisterous celebration of friendship lasting at least 1 hour at the site of the haunt.
