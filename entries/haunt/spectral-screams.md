Some spirits take joy in terrifying the living. Spectral screams are collections of lesser spirits
who have banded together to increase the amount of terror they can spread.

**Spectral Screams**

**Challenge rating:** 2

**XP** 600\
NE haunt (10-ft. radius)\
**Caster Level** 3rd

**Notice:** Perception DC 10 (to hear the moaning spirits approaching)

**Hit points:** 4

**Weakness:** [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 hour

### Effect

Spectral forms of disembodied heads emerge from the floor and scream at everyone in the area, their
jaws distending to an impossible width. Creatures are affected by [scare](ref/spell/scare). The
spectral heads take pride in terrorizing their victims; if a creature succeeds at a DC 16 Intimidate
check to demonstrate its own frightening nature, the spirits decide the PC is the true winner of the
current contest, after which they lose interest in the PCs for 1 hour as if reduced to 0 hit points.

### Destruction

The spectral screams haunt is destroyed if a creature spends 8 consecutive hours sleeping in its
area.
