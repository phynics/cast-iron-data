**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 230\
The most common haunts are rapping spirits: unquiet dead with just enough substance to produce
disembodied knocking and bumps in the night. Characters can attempt communication with these
intelligent spirits by working out codes the spirits can use to convey cryptic messages, or by using
tools like [spirit planchettes](ref/magic-wondrous/spirit-planchette) to increase the efficiency of
communication with the spirit.

**Sources:** Occult Mysteries pg 54\
While most haunts are malevolent in nature, some are more benign and serve as a means to communicate
with spirits. The most common of these haunts are rapping spirits: unquiet dead with just enough
substance to produce cacophonous knocking and loud bumps in the night. Adventurers can attempt
communication with these intelligent spirits by working out codes to form cryptic messages at the
rate of 1d10 words per minute with a successful [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) check.

**Rapping Spirit**

**Challenge rating:** 1

**XP** 400\
Alignment varies persistent haunt (5-ft. radius)\
**Caster Level** 1st

**Notice:** Perception DC 10 (to hear faint knocking on the walls, floor, and furniture)

**Hit points:** 4

**Weakness:** tricked by [hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead)

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

A chorus of agitated raps and blows rings out from nearby hard surfaces (limited to those capable of
being physically manipulated by [mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand)) as unquiet spirits convey their
unrest to the living. All who hear the supernatural knocks are affected by a
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear) effect (Will DC 11 negates).

### Destruction

Depending on its alignment, a rapping spirit typically asks for its mortal remains to be laid to
rest or for the PCs to seek revenge for its death. Fulfilling the request dismisses the haunt.
