A murder or other violent death lingers as bloody marks that harm any creature touching the surface
on which they appear.

**Bloody Handprints**

**Challenge rating:** 1/4

**XP** 100\
NE haunt (surface with area up to 5-ft. square)\
**Caster Level** 1st

**Notice:** Perception DC 5 (to see dried bloody prints on the surface)

**Hit points:** 1

**Trigger:** touch

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

Bloody handprints slowly appear on the surface. Anyone touching the surface takes 1d6 points of
damage.

### Destruction

The bloody handprints vanish if the surface is thoroughly cleaned and scrubbed with
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water).
