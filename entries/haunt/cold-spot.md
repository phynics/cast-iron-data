Cold spots are echoes of spirits too weak to manifest as ghosts. They can occur alone or cluster
around significant areas.

**Cold Spot**

**Challenge rating:** 1

**XP** 400\
NE haunt (5-ft. radius)\
**Caster Level** 1st

**Notice:** Perception DC 20 (to feel the temperature dropping)

**Hit points:** 2

**Weakness:** fire (these effects can damage the haunt)

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

The temperature suddenly plummets below freezing. All creatures within the radius are affected as if
they had been outside in [cold weather](ref/rule/cold-dangers) for 1 hour, taking 1d6 points of
nonlethal damage unless they succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save. The cold temperature lingers for 10
minutes, but has no additional mechanical effect.

### Destruction

If the cold spot is destroyed with holy fire (such as with [flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike)),
it never returns.
