**Sources:** Pathfinder \#144: Midwives to Death pg 65

**Flytrap**

**Challenge rating:** 4

**XP** 1,200\
N persistent botanical haunt (up to 20-foot radius)\
**Caster Level** 4th

**Notice:** Perception DC 20 (to spot flytrap heads opening and closing erratically)

**Hit points:** 36

**Trigger:** proximity

**Reset:** 1 day

### Effect

This patch of sentient flytraps sprouts sharp hairs around its mouths and uses a highly concentrated
acid to digest its prey. Each round, the flytrap haunt makes melee attacks with a +4 bonus against
each creature in the affected area, dealing 2d6 points of acid damage and 1d6 points of piercing
damage on a successful hit.

### Destruction

The nest of [accuser devils](ref/monster/devil-accuser-devil-zebub) making their home nearby must be
eradicated.
