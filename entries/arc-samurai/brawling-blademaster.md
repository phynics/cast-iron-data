While some martial arts traditions focus on the strength of the body, most schools of combat and
self-defense know the importance of using weapons as well. Brawling blademaster samurai use weapon
strikes and unarmed attacks in equal measure - foes expecting to defend themselves against a dagger
or a sword are often caught off guard by a brawling blademaster's brutal kicks or punches.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Weapon and armor proficiency

**Info:** Brawling blademasters are proficient with all simple and martial weapons, plus the
[katana](ref/eq-weapon/katana) and [wakizashi](ref/eq-weapon/wakizashi). Brawling blademasters are
proficient with light armor, but not with shields.

**Empty Hand (Ex)**

**Ability:** A brawling blademaster gains
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as a bonus feat. She uses her samurai
level as her [monk](ref/class/monk) level for determining the amount of damage she deals with an
unarmed strike. These levels stack with monk levels to determine the brawling blademaster's unarmed
strike damage.

**Replaces:** Mount

**Unarmed Strike**

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the monk class ability.

**At 12th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d10 (Small), 2d6 (Medium), or 3d6 (Large) damage.

**At 16th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d6 (Small), 2d8 (Medium), or 3d8 (Large) damage.

**At 20th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d8 (Small), 2d10 (Medium), or 4d8 (Large) damage.

**At 4th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium), or 2d6 (Large) damage.

**At 8th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d8 (Small), 1d10 (Medium), or 2d8 (Large) damage.

**At 1st Level:** Your unarmed strike deals 1d4 (Small), 1d6 (Medium), or 1d8 (Large) damage.

**Nimble (Ex)**

**Ability:** A brawling blademaster gains the [gunslinger's](ref/class/gunslinger) nimble class
feature, using her samurai level as his gunslinger level.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Nimble (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Text from the gunslinger ability.

**Passive:** You gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC while wearing light or no armor. Anything that causes
you to lose your Dexterity bonus to AC also causes you to lose this dodge bonus.

At 6th level: The dodge bonus +2.

At 10th level: The dodge bonus +3.

At 14th level: The dodge bonus +4.

At 18th level: The dodge bonus +5.

**Dual Expertise (Ex)**

**Ability:** A brawling blademaster gains [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) as a
bonus feat, even if she doesn't meet the prerequisites. Furthermore, when the brawling blademaster
selects a feat with the _bonus feat_ class feature, she can ignore any Dexterity requirements for
the feat if its prerequisites include the Two-Weapon Fighting feat.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Weapon expertise

**Harmonious Flow (Ex)**

**Ability:** When attacking the target of her _challenge,_ the brawling blademaster doesn't take the
usual -2 penalty on attack rolls associated with attacking with two weapons if her main-hand weapon
is a light or one-handed manufactured weapon and her off-hand weapon is an unarmed strike (or vice
versa).

**Flavor:** The brawling blademaster becomes skilled at combining weapon attacks and unarmed strikes
into a harmonious combat style.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Mounted archer

**Special:** While she's using this ability, one of her hands must be empty even if she uses another
body part to make an unarmed strike.

**Perfect Clarity (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The brawling blademaster sees all her foes with perfect clarity even when focused on one
opponent.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** She no longer takes a penalty to her AC during a _challenge._

**Replaces:** Banner

**Superior Focus (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Passive:** The brawling blademaster gains a +2 bonus on saving throws during a _challenge._

**Replaces:** Greater banner
