Warrior poets often study calligraphy, flower arrangement, poetry, and other courtly arts, but when
called to battle, they treat combat as its own art form, fighting with beauty and grace. While these
traditions first arose among the Jininese elves and are still extremely common in Jinin, the warrior
poet's art has spread to different nations and peoples in Tian Xia over centuries.

**Dancer's Grace (Ex)**

**Passive:** When wearing no armor and not using a shield, the warrior poet gains a bonus to Armor
Class equal to her Charisma bonus (to a maximum of her samurai level). A warrior poet loses this
bonus while [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) or otherwise denied her Dexterity bonus.

**Replaces:** Proficiency with medium armor, heavy armor, and shields

**Flourish (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The warrior poet is skilled at performing elegant moves in battle.

**At 1st Level:** The warrior poet gains a flourish of her choice from the list below.

**Repeat At:** She gains another flourish., 3, 5, 9, 13, 17, 20

**Replaces:** Mount, weapon expertise, banner, greater banner

**Chrysanthemum's Blooming**

**Ability:** The warrior poet gains [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike) as a bonus feat and can
apply its benefit when using [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack).

**At 16th Level:** If the warrior poet has [Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike),
she can apply that feat's benefit instead.

**Prereq:** 11th-level samurai

**Exodus of Jinin**

**Passive:** As long as the warrior poet is wearing light or no armor and carrying no more than a
light load, her land speed increases by 10 feet.

**Special:** A warrior poet can select this flourish up to three times.

**Harmony of the Tranquil Garden**

**Move:** The warrior poet can focus her senses to gain [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) out to 5
feet for 1 round.

At 10th level: The range of her [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) 10 feet.

At 15th level: The range of her [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) 15 feet.

At 20th level: The range of her [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) 20 feet.

**Jininsiel's Guidance**

**Ability:** The warrior poet gains the rogue's [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge) class
feature.

**Prereq:** 4th-level samuari

**Special:** This flourish can be selected a second time beginning at 8th level; the warrior poet
then gains the rogue's [improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) class feature.

**Kitsune's Mystique**

**Ability:** The warrior poet gains [Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint) as a bonus feat,
ignoring its prerequisites. When the warrior poet uses Spring Attack or takes a move action to move,
she can attempt to feint against one creature she threatens during her movement as part of her
movement.

**Petals on the Wind**

**Ability:** Whenever a foe provokes an attack of opportunity from the warrior poet, she can move 5
feet before making the attack of opportunity. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
If the warrior poet takes an action to move during her next turn, she subtracts 5 feet from her
total movement for each time she has used this ability since her last turn.

**Wrath of the Heavens**

**Ability:** The warrior poet gains [Shot on the Run](ref/feat/shot-on-the-run) as a bonus feat,
ignoring its prerequisites.

**Graceful Warrior (Ex)**

**Ability:** The warrior poet gains [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) as a bonus feat and
can apply its benefits to [glaives](ref/eq-weapon/glaive), [katanas](ref/eq-weapon/katana), and
[naginatas](ref/eq-weapon/naginata) as if they were light weapons. This does not alter the weapons'
properties for the purposes of any other effects.

**Skirmisher's Challenge (Ex)**

**Ability:** The warrior poet adds her samurai level to her damage rolls only on her first
successful attack against a _challenged_ target each round.

**Alters:** Challenge

**Graceful Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** When the warrior poet uses Weapon Finesse to make a melee attack that adds her
Dexterity bonus to attack rolls and her Strength bonus to damage rolls, she also adds half her
samurai level to damage rolls.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Mounted archer, demanding challenge

**Battle Dance (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** She gains [Improved Spring Attack](ref/feat/improved-spring-attack) as a bonus
feat.

**At 18th Level:** She gains [Greater Spring Attack](ref/feat/greater-spring-attack) as a bonus
feat.

**At 6th Level:** The warrior poet gains Spring Attack as a bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Bonus combat feats

**Special:** The warrior poet does not need to meet the prerequisites for these feats.
