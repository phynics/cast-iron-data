In Tian Xia, many samurai are tutored in the ways of honor using lessons that originate from
sovereign dragons and their dedication to safeguarding harmony.

**Alignment**

**Info:** A sovereign blade's alignment must be at least partially neutral. If the sovereign blade's
alignment is not partially neutral, she is treated as if she had violated her order's edicts each
day until her alignment is again partially neutral.

**Dogmatic Denial (Ex)**

**Passive:** A sovereign blade gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against spells with any of the
following descriptors that don't match part of the character's own alignment: chaotic, evil, good,
or lawful.

**Replaces:** Mount

**Golden Armor (Su)**

**Ability:** A sovereign blade can spend one use of her _resolve_ to cause her armor to become
golden for 1 round per class level, gaining energy resistance equal to 1/2 her samurai level against
one energy type of her choice.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Mounted archer

**Sovereign Breath (Sp)**

**Ability:** The sovereign blade can issue forth an immense roar similar to the breath weapon of a
[sovereign dragon](ref/family/dragon-imperial-sovereign). This functions as
[dragon's breath](ref/spell/dragons-breath), except it deals sonic damage in a 30-foot cone and has
a caster level equal to half the sovereign blade's samurai level.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 6th, 12th, and 18th level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 6 samurai levels
