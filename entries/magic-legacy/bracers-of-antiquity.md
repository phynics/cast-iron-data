**Sources:** Chronicle of Legends pg 20\
**Slot** wrists; **CL** 13th; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Aura** strong divination

Despite the fact that they are millennia old, these polished metal armbands, inlaid with intricate
patterns in gold and platinum, are in remarkable condition. Part of a cache of Azlanti artifacts
discovered by Arustun in the Cave of Tiandra in approximately -4160 AR, the bracers became a
treasured possession of the Jistka Imperium's founder and a valuable tool in his decades-long quest
to recover and interpret remnants of Azlanti and Thassilonian culture.

Each of the bracers has one slot in which an ioun stone can be embedded. An
[ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone) embedded in this manner grants the bearer its normal
benefits as if it were orbiting her head and resonates its powers as if it were slotted in a
[wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder). Additionally, as long as at least one ioun stone is
slotted into the bracers, the wearer can understand any written language, as per
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages).

**6th Level** The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on Knowledge (arcana), Spellcraft, and Use
Magic Device checks.

**9th Level** The bracers act as [spell storing](ref/magic-enh/spell-storing-armor)
[bracers of armor](ref/magic-wondrous/bracers-of-armor).

**12th Level** While at least one ioun stone is embedded in the bracers, the wearer can speak and
understand any language, as per [tongues](ref/spell/tongues).

**15th Level** The wearer gains a +4 insight bonus on saving throws against language-dependent
spells and effects and on saving throws against magical glyphs, symbols, sigils, or similar written
traps.

**18th Level** Twice per day, the wearer can temporarily drain the magic of one of the bracers'
embedded ioun stones to cast [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift). After powering this ability, the
ioun stone becomes inert for 24 hours and impossible to remove from the bracer.

**Goal:** Recover and identify 30 named texts or artifacts that were created more than 4,000 years
before the present day. Recovering a major artifact counts as 3 artifacts toward the completion of
this goal.

**Reward:** By handling a magic item for 1 minute, the wearer can identify its properties and
command word as if she had successfully examined the item using
[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) and succeeded at a Spellcraft check. This ability does not
reveal whether the item is cursed unless the wearer's character level is equal to or greater than
the caster level of the item.
