**Sources:** Chronicle of Legends pg 21\
**Slot** ring; **CL** 15th; **Weight** -\
**Aura** strong abjuration

According to records salvaged from abandoned temples of Aroden, the high priest Narthoc was wearing
a ring of sealed souls when he trapped [the Whispering Tyrant](ref/monster/the-whispering-tyrant)
under Gallowspire in 3827 AR.

If the wearer of this braided ring is slain while wearing the ring, he cannot be raised as undead.
Additionally, the wearer can be revived with [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) or similar effects
even if he was slain by a death effect.

**8th Level:** The wearer can use [hold portal](ref/spell/hold-portal) at will as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), though he can have only one portal held by this
ability at a time. Should he use the ability again while the spell is already active, the first
casting is immediately dismissed.

**11th Level:** Weapons wielded by the wearer gain the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon property.

**14th Level:** The wearer can use [guards and wards](ref/spell/guards-and-wards) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**17th Level:** The wearer adds [binding](ref/spell/binding) to his class spell list and list of
spells known. The wearer can use _[binding](ref/spell/binding)_ once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), but he must provide the expensive material
component for the spell.

**Goal:** The wearer must defeat an undead creature or evil outsider whose CR is at least 4 greater
than his Hit Dice.

**Reward:** The wearer is constantly under the effect of
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) with a caster level equal to his character
level. Should this effect be [dispelled](ref/spell/dispel-magic), he can resume it as a free action.
