A small selection of spiritualists are connected to spirits of combat and warfare, and manifest a
weapon instead of a phantom. These spiritualists are known as phantom blades and fight with
ectoplasmic weapons. Phantom blades are often found in Mendev and the Worldwound, where
spiritualists are baptized in the blood of comrades; the last survivor of an unfortunate squad might
return bearing one of these ghostly blades.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Weapon, armor proficiency

**Info:** A phantom blade is proficient with all simple and martial weapons. She is also proficient
with light and medium armor.

**Phantom Weapon**

**Ability:** A phantom blade begins play with an ectoplasmic sentient weapon known as a _phantom
weapon_ whose weapon type is chosen by the phantom blade. The weapon type must be one with which the
phantom blade is proficient. A _phantom weapon_ functions similarly to the _black blade_ of the
[bladebound](ref/arc-magus/bladebound) magus archetype except as noted on the Phantom Weapon
Progression table below. A phantom blade with this class feature can't have a phantom of any kind,
even from another class.

**Replaces:** Phantom, phantom recall, spiritual bond, dual bond

**Spell Combat (Ex)**

**Ability:** A phantom blade can cast spells and wield her _phantom weapon_ at the same time. This
functions as the [magus's](ref/class/magus) _spell combat_ class ability.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Etheric tether, shared consciousness, fused consciousness, empowered consciousness

**Spell Combat (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Text from the magus class ability.

**Fullround:** You can make all of your attacks with your melee weapon at a -2 penalty and can also
cast any spell from the magus spell list with a casting time of 1 standard action (any attack roll
made as part of this spell also takes this penalty). If you cast this spell defensively, you can
decide to take an additional penalty on your attack rolls, up to your Intelligence bonus, and add
the same amount as a circumstance bonus on your [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check. If
the check fails, the spell is wasted, but the attacks still take the penalty. You can choose to cast
the spell first or make the weapon attacks first, but if you have more than one attack, you cannot
cast the spell between weapon attacks.

**Info:** A magus learn to cast spells and wield his weapons at the same time. This functions much
like [two-weapon fighting](ref/rule/two-weapon-fighting), but the off-hand weapon is a spell that is
being cast.

**Special:** To use this ability, you must have one hand free (even if the spell being cast does not
have somatic components), while wielding a light or one-handed melee weapon in the other hand.

**Spellstrike (Su)**

**Ability:** A phantom blade can deliver spells through her _phantom weapon._ This functions like
the magus's _spellstrike_ class ability except the only weapon the spiritualist can use to deliver
spells is her _phantom weapon._

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Bonded senses

**Spellstrike (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell with a range of &quot&touch&quot& from the magus spell list,
you can deliver the spell through any weapon you are wielding as part of a melee attack. Instead of
the free melee touch attack normally allowed to deliver the spell, you can make one free melee
attack with your weapon (at your highest base attack bonus) as part of casting this spell. If
successful, this melee attack deals its normal damage as well as the effects of the spell. If you
make this attack in concert with _spell combat,_ this melee attack takes all the penalties accrued
by spell combat melee attacks.

**Flavor:** Text from the magus class ability.

**Special:** This attack uses the weapon's critical range (20, 19-20, or 18-20 and modified by the
_keen_ weapon property or similar effects), but the spell effect only deals &times&2 damage on a
successful critical hit, while the weapon damage uses its own critical modifier.

**Spirit of War (Ex)**

**Ability:** A phantom blade gains a bonus combat feat. The spiritualist must meet the prerequisites
for these feats, but she treats her spiritualist level as her base attack bonus (in addition to base
attack bonuses gained from other classes and racial Hit Dice) for the purpose of qualifying for
these feats. The phantom blade also treats her spiritualist level as her fighter level for the
purpose of selecting feats with a minimum number of fighter levels as a prerequisite.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Repeat:** She gains another bonus feat, following the restrictions above., 8, 5

**Replaces:** Bonded manifestation

**Etheric Focus (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The phantom blade can use her _phantom weapon_ to aid her in casting spells with
[thought components](ref/rule/components-oa).

**At 12th Level:** The spiritualist can center herself as a **free action**.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Spiritual interference, greater spiritual interference

**Swift:** She can center herself (instead of as a move action).

### Phantom Weapon Ability Descriptions

A _phantom weapon_ has special abilities (or imparts abilities to its wielder) depending on the
wielder's spiritualist level. These abilities are cumulative. A _phantom weapon_ normally refuses to
use any of its abilities when wielded by anyone other than its phantom blade, and acts as an
incorporeal masterwork weapon of its type.

**Alertness (Ex)**

**Ability:** While the phantom blade is wielding or harboring her _phantom weapon,_ she gains the
[Alertness](ref/feat/alertness) feat.

**Phantom Touch (Ex)**

**Ability:** A _phantom weapon_ is always treated as a
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon, regardless of weapon type or any other
abilities modifying the weapon. If the _phantom weapon_ would ever be destroyed, it is instead
harbored in the spiritualist's consciousness for 24 hours.

**Telepathy (Su)**

**Ability:** While the phantom blade is wielding or carrying her _phantom weapon,_ she can
communicate telepathically with the weapon.

**Weapon of the Mind (Ex)**

**Ability:** The phantom blade can harbor her _phantom weapon_ in her consciousness or manifest it
in its ectoplasmic form. The phantom blade can manifest the weapon through a ritual that takes 1
minute to perform. Harboring the _phantom weapon_ requires a **full-round action**. While the weapon
is harbored, the phantom blade gains [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as
a bonus feat and treats her unarmed strike damage as a [monk](ref/class/monk) of her level - 2. If
the phantom blade already has this feat, she instead gains [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus)
(unarmed strike).

**Special:** The phantom blade's unarmed strikes gain the _phantom weapon's_ enhancement bonus and
other abilities when the weapon is harbored. The _phantom weapon_ also recovers a number of hit
points equal to its ego score every hour that it is harbored.

**Unarmed Strike**

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the monk class ability.

**At 12th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d10 (Small), 2d6 (Medium), or 3d6 (Large) damage.

**At 16th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d6 (Small), 2d8 (Medium), or 3d8 (Large) damage.

**At 4th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium), or 2d6 (Large) damage.

**At 8th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d8 (Small), 1d10 (Medium), or 2d8 (Large) damage.

**At 1st Level:** Your unarmed strike deals 1d4 (Small), 1d6 (Medium), or 1d8 (Large) damage.

**Quick Manifest (Ex)**

**Ability:** The phantom blade can manifest her _phantom weapon_ as a **swift action**.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Ectoplasmic Pool (Ex)**

**Ability:** The phantom blade gains a reservoir of excess ethereal energy and ectoplasm that she
can draw upon to improve her _phantom weapon._ This pool has a number of ectoplasmic points equal to
half her spiritualist level + her Wisdom modifier. The pool refreshes once per day when the
spiritualist refreshes her daily spells.

**Ability2:** The phantom blade can spend a number of ectoplasmic points to enhance or modify her
_phantom weapon._ As a **free action**, she can spend 1 point to manifest or harbor her weapon. The
spiritualist can also spend 2 ectoplasmic points as a **free action** to allow attacks made with her
_phantom weapon_ to resolve against touch AC for 1 round.

**At 11th Level:** She can spend 3 ectoplasmic points to grant the weapon one of the following
weapon special abilities for 1 minute: [corrosive burst](ref/magic-enh/corrosive-burst),
[disruption](ref/magic-enh/disruption), [flaming burst](ref/magic-enh/flaming-burst),
[holy](ref/magic-enh/holy), [icy burst](ref/magic-enh/icy-burst),
[shocking burst](ref/magic-enh/shocking-burst), or [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy).

**At 15th Level:** She can spend 5 ectoplasmic points to grant the weapon one of the following
weapon special abilities for 1 minute: [brilliant energy](ref/magic-enh/brilliant-energy),
[dancing](ref/magic-enh/dancing), or [speed](ref/magic-enh/speed).

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Special:** A phantom blade can spend an appropriate number of points to grant the weapon multiple
abilities as one action. A _phantom weapon_ cannot have a modified bonus higher than +10.

**Swift:** She can spend 2 ectoplasmic points to grant the weapon one of the following weapon
special abilities for 1 minute: [corrosive](ref/magic-enh/corrosive),
[defending](ref/magic-enh/defending), [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming),
[frost](ref/magic-enh/frost), [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen), [merciful](ref/magic-enh/merciful),
[shock](ref/magic-enh/shock), or [throwing](ref/magic-enh/throwing).

**Reshape (Ex)**

**Ability:** A phantom blade can change the weapon type of her _phantom weapon_ when she refreshes
her daily spells.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Phantom Tether (Su)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Swift:** A phantom blade can spend 1 ectoplasmic point to instantly recall her _phantom weapon_ to
her hand or consciousness from as far as 1 mile away.

**Quick Reshape (Ex)**

**Ability:** A phantom blade can spend 1 ectoplasmic point to reshape her _phantom weapon_ as a
**full-round action**, 2 ectoplasmic points to reshape her weapon as a **standard action**, 3
ectoplasmic points to reshape her weapon as a **move action**, and 4 ectoplasmic points to reshape
her weapon as a **swift action**.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Etheric Strikes (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Swift:** A phantom blade can spend 1 ectoplasmic point to allow her _phantom weapon_ to ignore a
number of points of [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) equal to twice her Wisdom modifier
or twice the weapon's Charisma modifier, whichever is higher.

**Soul Eater (Su)**

**Ability:** Each time she kills a living or incorporeal creature with her _phantom weapon,_ she can
pick one of the following effects: she gains a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls for 1 round, she
recovers one 1st- or 2nd-level spell slot, or she gains a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to the _phantom weapon's_ ego (these
temporary hit points last until lost or 1 minute, whichever is shorter).

**Flavor:** The phantom blade and her weapon can feast on the energy of departing souls.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Special:** The creature killed must have a number of Hit Dice equal to or greater than half the
phantom blade's spiritualist level for this to occur.
