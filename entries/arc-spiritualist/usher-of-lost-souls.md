Ushers of lost souls are spiritualists who focus on bringing the souls of the dead to their final
judgments at the end of the River of Souls. To this end, they specialize in destroying undead and in
laying haunts to rest. Skeptics who point out the apparent contradiction that ushers of lost souls
use phantoms to aid them are quickly told that their ectoplasmic allies hail from the Ethereal Plane
and are not actually undead. Ushers of lost souls are common among the clergy of Pharasma and are
seen throughout the Inner Sea region.

**Etheric Channel (Su)**

**Ability:** The spiritualist can [channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy). She
channels energy like a cleric of her spiritualist level, except the channeled energy can be used
only to harm undead or haunts. This otherwise functions as _channel energy_ for the purposes of
feats and abilities. The spiritualist does not require a holy symbol to use this ability.

**Flavor:** An usher of lost souls can focus ethereal energies to channel waves of disruption.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Channel Energy (Su)**

**Flavor:** Relevant text from the cleric class ability.

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Standard:** You cause a [burst](ref/misc/burst) that affects all creatures of one type (either
undead or living) in a 30-foot radius centered on you. This deals 1d6 damage. Creatures that take
damage from channeled energy receive a Will save to halve the damage, with a DC equal to 10 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your cleric level + your Charisma modifier.

At 3rd level: The damage 2d6.

At 5th level: The damage 3d6.

At 7th level: The damage 4d6.

At 9th level: The damage 5d6.

At 11th level: The damage 6d6.

At 13th level: The damage 7d6.

At 15th level: The damage 8d6.

At 17th level: The damage 9d6.

At 19th level: The damage 10d6.

**Spirit Senses (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** An usher of lost souls gains a bonus equal to half her spiritualist level on Perception
checks to detect haunts and incorporeal creatures and on Sense Motive checks to determine whether a
creature is possessed, under the effects of an enchantment or curse, or otherwise magically
controlled.

**Replaces:** Bonded senses

**Disrupting Phantom (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** Her phantom can deal critical hits to a haunt, and the critical threat range for
its slam attacks against haunts increases to 19-20.

**At 4th Level:** An usher of lost souls's phantom gains the ability to directly harm haunts. The
phantom can make an attack while within a haunt's area against an AC equal to 10 + the haunt's
caster level. On a hit, the phantom deals its slam damage to the haunt; the haunt is immune to
critical hits from the phantom.

**Replaces:** Spiritual interference, greater spiritual interference

**Locate Haunts (Su)**

**Flavor:** An usher of lost souls can locate inactive haunts.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** See invisibility

**Standard:** She can enhance her vision to detect haunts within 60 feet of her. A haunt's area
glows with a bright light for an untriggered haunt or dim light for a haunt that is waiting to
reset.

**Usage:** 10 minutes/day per spiritualist level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be
spent in 10-minute increments

**Disruptive Influence (Su)**

**Ability:** She can use one of her uses of _[calm spirit](ref/spell/calm-spirit)_ to instead cast
[purge spirit](ref/spell/purge-spirit) as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). She
can expend two uses of her _etheric channel_ to cast either of these spells as a spell-like ability.

**Flavor:** The usher of lost souls can put spirits to rest.

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Replaces:** Spiritual bond
