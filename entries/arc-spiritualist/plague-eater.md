Iobaria has been ravaged by plague for centuries, and many of its dead do not rest. These ghosts
often flock around specialized spiritualists called plague eaters. Such a spiritualist bears the
weight of many souls within her consciousness, spirits that remember the devastation of disease and
seek to protect their host from infections, cleanse others of plague, and use disease itself as a
weapon.

**Plague Ward**

**Ability:** A plague eater does not bond with a single spirit, but rather a group of spirits
gathered around her psychic presence. The spiritualist can meditate on an emotional focus during her
8-hour rest and bond with a spirit from her community with a matching focus. A spirit associated
with the chosen emotion links with the spiritualist and acts as her phantom until the plague eater
changes her emotional focus with another 8-hour rest. The changing roster of spirits dilutes the
bond between the plague eater and her community, and the manifesting phantom has a level 1 lower
than the spiritualist's level (minimum level 1).

**Alters:** Phantom, shared consciousness

**At 12th Level:** The saving throw bonus becomes +8.

**Passive:** While a phantom is confined in a plague eater's consciousness (but not while it's fully
manifested or banished to the Ethereal Plane), it grants a +4 bonus on saving throws against
disease.

**Special:** In addition, the phantom grants the plague eater the
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) feat in one of the skills determined by the phantom's emotional
focus. This skill is chosen by the plague eater the first time she confines the phantom tied to that
particular emotional focus. Once this choice is made for a given emotional focus, it cannot be
changed.

**Spiritual Inoculation (Su)**

**Alters:** Spiritual interference

**At 4th Level:** Whenever a plague eater is within reach of her ectoplasmic manifested phantom, she
gains a +4 circumstance bonus on saving throws against disease.

**Remove Sickness (Sp)**

**Ability:** A plague eater's understanding of disease grows to the point where she can cast
[remove sickness](ref/spell/remove-sickness) at will as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to her spiritualist
level.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Detect undead

**Disfiguring Touch (Sp)**

**Ability:** A plague eater can inflict upon others the superficial disfigurement associated with
plague by casting [disfiguring touch](ref/spell/disfiguring-touch) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Calm spirit

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 spiritualist levels beyond 7th

**Remove Disease (Sp)**

**Ability:** A plague eater gains the ability to purge disease from herself and others. She gains
the ability to cast [remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) three times per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** See invisibility

**Fused Well-Being (Su)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** A plague eater always gains the benefits of _bonded senses_ when her phantom is
manifested. She also gains the skill ranks and bonus to resist disease granted by the spirit's
emotional focus, even when her phantom is manifested.

**Replaces:** Fused consciousness

**Greater Spiritual Inoculation (Su)**

**Alters:** Greater spiritual interference

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** The plague eater becomes immune to disease, including supernatural and magical
diseases. In addition, whenever a manifested phantom is in ectoplasmic form, each of the plague
eater's allies within the phantom's reach gains a +4 circumstance bonus on saving throws against
disease.

**Withdraw Affliction (Sp)**

**Ability:** A plague eater can reach into a body, remove an affliction directly from the flesh, and
use the diseased matter as a weapon. She can cast
[withdraw affliction](ref/spell/withdraw-affliction) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to her spiritualist
level.

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Replaces:** Call spirit
