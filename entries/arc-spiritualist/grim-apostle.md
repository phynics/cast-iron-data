Some devotees of the Four Horsemen can form spiritual manifestations of the daemonic demigods.

**Grim Phantom (Su)**

**Ability:** The spirit linked to a grim apostle functions in all respects as a regular
spiritualist's phantom, but each day when the grim apostle regains her daily spell slots, she can
choose which Horseman focus (see below) is dominant within the grim phantom.

**Ability2:** When the grim phantom is banished to the Ethereal Plane, the grim apostle takes an
amount of damage equal to 1d4 &times& the grim phantom's number of Hit Dice.

**Alters:** Phantom

**Replaces:** The phantom's emotional focus

**Hardened Soul (Su)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** While a grim phantom is confined in the grim apostle's consciousness, the grim apostle
gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against death effects and effects that would remove or subsume the
grim apostle's soul (such as [possession](ref/spell/possession) or
[trap the soul](ref/spell/trap-the-soul)).

**Replaces:** Shared consciousness, fused consciousness

At 6th level: This bonus +6.

At 12th level: This bonus +8.

At 18th level: This bonus +10.

### Horseman Focus

Instead of an emotional focus, a grim phantom has one of the following Horseman foci.

The DC for a saving throw against any of the grim phantom's abilities is equal to 10 + 1/2 the grim
phantom's Hit Dice + the grim phantom's Charisma modifier. For any
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) a grim phantom receives, it uses its Hit Dice
as its caster level.

#### Death

Grim phantoms influenced by [Charon](ref/faith/charon), the Horseman of Death, are dark, almost
shapeless specters.

**Skills and Saves**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains ranks in Knowledge (history) and Stealth equal to its number of
Hit Dice. Its good saves are Fortitude and Will.

**Fleet**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains [Fleet](ref/feat/fleet) as a bonus feat.

**Marked for Death (Su)**

**Ability:** When manifested in ectoplasmic form, the grim phantom gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls
against a creature the grim apostle has hit with a melee or ranged attack since its last turn (the
phantom's &quot&marked target&quot&). Additionally, such an attack by the grim phantom deals
additional **X**d6 negative energy damage, where **X** is 1 +
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his spiritualist level.

**Fatal Aura (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Swift:** The grim phantom can create a 10-foot-radius aura that makes healing more difficult. The
DC of Heal checks to provide first aid and treat deadly wounds within the aura is increased by 5.
Any creature within the aura that casts a conjuration (healing) spell, or casts another spell or
uses an ability that restores hit points, must succeed at a Will saving throw or lose the spell or
use of the ability. Ending the aura is a **free action**.

**No Escape (Su)**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains the benefits of the [Following Step](ref/feat/following-step),
[Step Up](ref/feat/step-up), and [Step Up and Strike](ref/feat/step-up-and-strike) feats but only
against its _marked target_ (see above).

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Killing Word (Sp)**

**Ability:** The grim phantom can cast [power word kill](ref/spell/power-word-kill) once per day as
a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Gained:** At 17th Level

#### Famine

Grim phantoms influenced by [Trelmarixian](ref/faith/trelmarixian), the Horseman of Famine, are
gaunt with jackal-like features.

**Skills and Saves**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains ranks in Perception and Sense Motive equal to its number of Hit
Dice. Its good saves are Reflex and Will.

**Weapon Finesse**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) as a bonus feat.

**Ravenous Bite (Ex)**

**Ability:** The grim phantom's natural attacks take the form of bites that deal piercing damage as
a creature [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) than its current size. It
also gains [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack) as a bonus feat.

**Aura of Starvation (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Swift:** The grim phantom can create a 10-foot-radius _aura of starvation._ Enemies within the
aura take a -2 penalty on attack and damage rolls. Ending the aura is a **free action**.

**Wail of the Hungry (Sp)**

**Ability:** The grim phantom can cast [waves of fatigue](ref/spell/waves-of-fatigue) once per day
as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Distended Gullet (Su)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Special:** The grim phantom is [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) for 1 hour after using this ability
and can't use it again during that time.

**Swift:** The grim phantom in ectoplasmic form gains the [swallow whole](ref/umr/swallow-whole)
ability (4d8 negative energy damage, AC 17, one-tenth the phantom's total hit points) for a number
of rounds equal to its Charisma modifier. This ability can affect creatures no more than two size
categories larger than the phantom. Each creature the grim phantom swallows is shunted into an
extradimensional space that can hold any number of creatures. When the duration of this ability
ends, any creatures still within the extradimensional space appear adjacent to the grim phantom.

#### Pestilence

Grim phantoms influenced by [Apollyon](ref/faith/apollyon), the Horseman of Pestilence, often have
rams' skulls for heads.

**Skills and Saves**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains ranks in Acrobatics and Heal equal to its number of Hit Dice.
Its good saves are Fortitude and Will.

**Great Fortitude**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains [Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude) as a bonus feat.

**Weakening Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** A creature hit by the grim phantom's slam attack must succeed at a Fortitude saving
throw or take a -2 penalty on ability checks, attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks for 1d4
rounds.

**Special:** This is a disease effect. Penalties from multiple hits don't stack.

**Aura of Flies (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Swift:** The grim phantom can surround itself with a cloud of biting flies. Any creature that ends
its turn adjacent to the grim phantom takes 1d6 piercing damage. Ending the aura is a **free
action**.

At 11th level: This damage 2d6.

At 15th level: This damage 3d6.

At 19th level: This damage 4d6.

**Contagious Touch (Sp)**

**Ability:** The grim phantom can cast [contagion](ref/spell/contagion) three times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Virulent Attack (Su)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** Any creature under the effects of the grim phantom's _weakening strike_ becomes a
dangerous vector for disease. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to the target creature is
immediately exposed to any diseases the target creature has, no matter the normal method of
contracting that disease. The creature must immediately attempt a saving throw against that disease,
and the onset period doesn't apply.

#### War

Grim phantoms influenced by [Szuriel](ref/faith/szuriel), the Horseman of War, look like sinister,
black-winged angels.

**Skills and Saves**

**Ability:** The grim phantom gains ranks in Intimidate and Survival equal to its number of Hit
Dice. Its good saves are Fortitude and Reflex.

**Strength Focus**

**Info:** The grim phantom gains a +2 bonus to Strength and a -2 penalty to Dexterity. The phantom
gains bonuses to Strength instead of Dexterity as the grim apostle gains spiritualist levels.

**Ruthless Combatant (Su)**

**Ability:** The grim phantom threatens a critical hit with its slam attack on a roll of 19-20.

**At 11th Level:** The phantom's critical modifier with slam attacks increases to &times&3. This
doesn't stack with [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) or similar effects.

**Aura of Fury (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Swift:** The grim phantom can create a 20-foot-radius aura. Creatures within the aura gain a +2
bonus on melee attack rolls but take a -2 penalty to AC. Ending the aura is a **free action**.

**Bloodlust (Su)**

**Ability:** The grim phantom regains 1d6 hit points with each successful slam attack it makes.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Juggernaut (Su)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** The grim phantom becomes immune to any effects that would cause it to become cowering,
exhausted, fatigued, frightened, panicked, shaken, or slowed (as the [slow](ref/spell/slow) spell).
