Sometimes a spiritualist is so haunted by a past life's failure to reach enlightenment that the past
life is reborn not as a new creature but as a phantom in a spiritualist's consciousness. The
spiritualist and phantom work in tandem to reach enlightenment and atone for the past life's
failures.

**Echoes of Expertise (Ex)**

**Free:** Once per day, the seeker of enlightenment can attempt a check with any Knowledge skill,
including one in which she is not trained, and add her spiritualist level as a bonus on the check.

**At 12th Level:** The bonus to saving throws increases to +4.

**Passive:** A seeker of enlightenment gains a bonus equal to her spiritualist level on checks
attempted with the two skills determined by her phantom's emotional focus. When the phantom is
confined to the seeker of enlightenment's consciousness, it grants a +2 bonus on saving throws
against mind-affecting effects.

**Replaces:** Shared consciousness

**Words of the Past (Sp)**

**Ability:** A seeker of enlightenment's connection with her past lives becomes so strong that she
can cast [comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages) at will as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), with a caster level equal to her spiritualist
level.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Detect undead

**Karmic Insight (Sp)**

**Ability:** She can cast [augury](ref/spell/augury) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Flavor:** A seeker of enlightenment gains the ability to understand which actions will push her
toward ultimate understanding.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Calm spirit

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 spiritualist levels beyond 7th

**Pinpoint Influence (Sp)**

**Ability:** She can cast [locate object](ref/spell/locate-object) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day, with a duration of 10 minutes.

**Flavor:** A seeker of enlightenment gains the ability to track objects that are important to her
goal of enlightenment.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** See invisibility

**Echoes of Mastery (Ex)**

**Ability:** A seeker of enlightenment gains the bonus on saving throws from _echoes of expertise_
even when her phantom is manifested. Additionally, once per day, she can take 20 on any Knowledge
check, including one in which she is not trained.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** Fused consciousness

**Knowledge of the Ancestors (Sp)**

**Ability:** She can cast [legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day.

**Flavor:** A seeker of enlightenment learns how to tap into the knowledge of her past lives.

**Gained:** At 16th Level

**Replaces:** Call spirit
