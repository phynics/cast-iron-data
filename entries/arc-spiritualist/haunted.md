The haunted are a dangerous breed of spellcasters bound to temperamental and unpredictable phantoms
that leech power from their spiritualists.

**Usurp Manifestation (Su)**

**Immediate:** The phantom must activate this ability at the start of the spiritualist's turn. The
phantom can dismiss the effects of a _usurped manifestation_ as a **free action** on its turn. While
this ability is in effect, the haunted's features become sunken and withdrawn, and she gains the
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) condition until the effect ends. Conversely, the phantom surges in
size and power, becoming one size category larger (as if affected by an
[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person) spell) and increasing its natural armor bonus to AC by 2
for the effect's duration.

**Info:** The haunted's phantom begins to subtly subvert the relationship with its master, gaining
rather than granting power from the connection. When fully manifested in ectoplasmic form, the
phantom can use its _usurp manifestation_ ability to draw upon the haunted's body to strengthen its
ectoplasmic might, temporarily weakening the spiritualist in order to increase its own power.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Special:** If the spiritualist prevents or mitigates the nauseated condition, the phantom is
unable to siphon energy and doesn't gain the benefits of _usurp manifestation._

**Usage:** 1 time/day per spiritualist3 level

**Usurp Spell**

**Flavor:** While using _usurp manifestation,_ the phantom can leech spell slots from the haunted to
increase its own power.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Special:** This ability consumes two of the haunted's spell slots of the appropriate level and
otherwise follows the rules for the _share spells_ and _deliver touch spells_ abilities.

**Swift:** The phantom can reach through the etheric tether to drain the nauseated haunted's psychic
spell energy, effectively taking any touch spell or spell with a target of &quot&you&quot& that the
haunted knows and casting it on itself (assuming the haunted has not yet used up her allotment of
spells per day for the spell's level).

**Teleplasmic Surge**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** When using _usurp manifestation,_ the phantom gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls,
saving throws, and skill checks.

**Usurped Bond (Su)**

**Info:** The haunted's phantom can use _usurp manifestation_ for a number of rounds per day equal
to 3 + double the haunted's spiritualist level.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Dual bond

**Ferocious Phantom**

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Passive:** When using _usurp manifestation_ the phantom gains DR 10/slashing and
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 11 + the haunted's spiritualist level (this
spell resistance doesn't apply to spells originating from the spiritualist or from the _usurp spell_
ability).

**Replaces:** Bonded manifestation
