**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 109\
Some phantoms retain less of their personalities and memories than others. Though most phantoms
maintain some semblance of their former selves, others of their kind, known as
[geists](ref/monster/geist), came close to being lost forever before they became phantoms. Incapable
of manifesting in ectoplasmic form and devoid of human emotions, these strange entities are much
closer to ghosts than most other phantoms. The spiritualists who work with them, known as geist
channelers, are able to call on them in unique ways.

**Geistform Phantom (Su)**

**Ability:** While manifested incorporeally, a geist channeler's phantom can attack only other
incorporeal creatures. Because the phantom is incorporeal, the damage it deals to such creatures is
not reduced as a result of them being incorporeal.

**Alters:** Phantom

**At 10th Level:** The slam attack's damage increases to 2d6 (Medium). When the phantom infuses
itself, its attacks deal normal damage to corporeal creatures.

**At 15th Level:** The slam attack's damage increases to 2d8 (Medium).

**At 20th Level:** The slam attack's damage increases to 2d10 (Medium)

**At 4th Level:** The phantom does not gain the _magic attacks_ ability at 4th level.

**At 5th Level:** The slam attack's damage increases to 1d10 points (Medium). The phantom can take a
**swift action** to infuse itself with trace amounts of ectoplasm so it can attack corporeal
creatures for 1 round. During that time, it deals half damage to corporeal creatures with its slam,
and corporeal creatures with nonmagical weapons deal half damage to the phantom.

**Info:** A geist channeler's phantom can't manifest in ectoplasmic form, and is limited to
manifesting in incorporeal form. As a result, the phantom has no Strength score, and has a starting
Wisdom score of 12. Instead of gaining two slam attacks, it gains only a single slam attack that
deals 1d8 points of damage (for a Medium phantom; perform
[damage die adjustments](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) for differently-sized phantoms).

**Spiritual Manifestation (Su)**

**Ability:** Because of the nature of her phantom, a geist channeler can use the _bonded
manifestation_ ability to manifest his phantom only in incorporeal form.

**Alters:** Bonded manifestation

**Emotionless (Su)**

**Ability:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in Knowledge (arcana) and Knowledge (religion)
equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in the geist channeler's consciousness, the phantom grants the
geist channeler [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of these skills.

**Info:** A geist channeler's phantom doesn't have an emotional focus; instead, it is cold and
devoid of emotions.

**Special:** The phantom has good Reflex and Will saves, and gains [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will)
as a bonus feat, as well as the following abilities.

**Unnerving Touch (Su)**

**At 5th Level:** When the phantom gains the ability to damage corporeal creatures with its slam
attack, its slam attacks against living corporeal creatures gain this ability.

**Special:** Multiple attacks against the same creature don't cause the creature to become
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened). This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

**Standard:** A geist channeler's phantom can make a melee touch attack against a living creature,
even if that creature is corporeal. If the attack hits, the target is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for
1d4+1 rounds. A successful Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma
modifier) negates this effect.

**Poltergeist's Knack (Sp)**

**Ability:** A geist channeler's phantom can use [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). It can use only the _sustained force_ and
_violent thrust_ versions of this spell. Its caster level for this spell-like ability is equal to
the phantom's Hit Dice.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Usage:** 3 times/day + 1 per 3 phantom levels beyond 7th

**Spectral Protection (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Standard:** While a geist channeler's phantom is confined within his psyche, he can call upon its
power to project an aura of spectral energy that prevents incorporeal creatures from approaching
him. This takes the form of a 30-foot-radius [emanation](ref/misc/emanation) centered on the geist
channeler. Any incorporeal creature attempting to enter this area must succeed at a Will save (DC =
10 + 1/2 the geist channeler's spiritualist class level + the geist channeler's Wisdom modifier) or
be unable to do so. This ability can be used only defensively, not aggressively; forcing the aura
against an incorporeal creature collapses the aura.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per spiritualist level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be
spent in 1-minute increments

**Malevolent Possession (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, a geist channeler's phantom can invade the bodies of other creatures.
This functions as [possession](ref/spell/possession). To use this ability, the phantom must be
adjacent to the target. The target can resist the effect with a successful Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2
the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma modifier).

**Alters:** Phantom

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** The phantom's emotional focus
