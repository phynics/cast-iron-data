Not all spiritualists deal in the souls of creatures that refuse to pass into death. Zeitgeist
binders channel the emotional connections of a group of people into a unique phantom that
personifies a community.

**Zeitgeist (Su)**

**Alters:** Phantom

**Info:** A zeitgeist binder's phantom is called a zeitgeist. In addition to an emotion, a zeitgeist
is tied to one [aspect of its settlement](ref/rule/the-settlement-stat-block): corruption, crime,
economy, law, lore, or society. In order to select an aspect, that aspect of the settlement must
have a positive modifier (for instance, a settlement with a negative lore modifier or a modifier of
0 could not manifest a lore zeitgeist). Although a zeitgeist personifies a single chosen settlement,
a zeitgeist binder can call it anywhere, but outside the bounds of its settlement, it loses the
benefits of its _settlement aspect_ and _settlement avatar_ abilities.

**Settlement Aspect (Sp)**

**Ability:** A zeitgeist binder gains a number of
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) tied to her zeitgeist's settlement aspect.
Other than being based on Wisdom instead of Charisma, these work the same way as the
[fractured mind's](ref/arc-spiritualist/fractured-mind) emotional power ability. The
_[masterwork transformation](ref/spell/masterwork-transformation)_ spell-like ability does not
ignore the material component.

**Replaces:** Detect undead, calm spirit, see invisibility, call spirit

**Settlement Avatar (Su)**

**Flavor:** The zeitgeist becomes a living avatar of the settlement.

**At 10th Level:** The zeitgeist can project its senses to feel its settlement aspect at will.
Additionally, the zeitgeist binder gains a bonus teamwork feat as long as she is in her zeitgeist's
settlement. The zeitgeist binder must meet the teamwork feat's prerequisites, and she grants the
feat as a bonus feat to her zeitgeist, even if it doesn't meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Bonded senses, fused consciousness

**Standard:** The zeitgeist can project its senses out into the settlement, perceiving the general
pulse of its chosen aspect throughout the whole settlement as well as anything involving its aspect
that is occurring within 50 feet per spiritualist level. The sensing works like
[enter image](ref/spell/enter-image), so the zeitgeist attempts a Perception check at a - 10 penalty
to notice any particular activity within range.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per spiritualist level
