**Sources:** Blood of the Ancients pg 24\
Not all gods of Ninshabur are forgotten. Prophets of the herogod Namzaruum claim he rests in the
Hall of Slumbering Kings, but will return to lead the faithful to victory.

**Base Legend**

**Details:** [Marshal](ref/spirit/marshal)

**Gaining Favor**

**Details:** To gain Namzaruum's favor, journey to his ziggurat at the center of Ezida. There, you
must swear to help gather the descendants of Ninshabur and to tell all of Namzaruum's inevitable
return. You must also succeed at a Charisma check before Namzaruum will answer your call.

**Taboos**

**Details:** If you accept a _taboo_ while channeling Namzaruum, you must take any opportunity to
seek out Ninshaburian descendants and proselytize, even if doing so would be disadvantageous.

### Lesser Spirit Power

**Phalanx Defense (Su)**

**Ability:** This functions as the _marshal's order_ ability, except if you or an ally who
participated in your _seance_ are adjacent to another ally who participated, you and any applicable
allies can also add your _spirit surge_ to your CMD against bull rush, drag, overrun, and trip
attempts.

**Alters:** Marshal's order

### Greater Spirit Power

**Slumbering Sword (Su)**

**Immediate:** When an ally within 30 feet is attacked, you can allow Namzaruum to gain 1 point of
influence over you to provide partial [cover](ref/rule/cover) to that ally for a single attack. The
ally must be able to see and hear you for this ability to function.

If the attack against your ally misses, your ally can make an attack of opportunity against the
enemy who made the attack if that enemy is within your ally's reach.

**Replaces:** Decisive strike

**Special:** You can use this ability as a **standard action** to instead grant an ally partial
cover for 1 round, but missed attacks against the ally do not provoke attacks of opportunity.
