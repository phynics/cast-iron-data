A hierophant spirit is a being of true and pure faith.

**Spirit Bonus**

**Details:** When you channel a hierophant, your _spirit bonus_ applies on Wisdom checks,
Wisdom-based skill checks, and Will saves.

**Seance Boon**

**Details:** Your healing spells and abilities heal an additional 2 points of damage to each target.
This does not affect healing conferred by magic items, nor does it add to
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) or similar effects.

**Favored Locations**

**Details:** Altars, churches, sacred groves, shrines.

**Influence Penalty**

**Details:** Whether the spirit hopes to offer a chance for redemption or to sacrifice foes later on
a dark altar, you must strike for nonlethal damage in combat whenever possible. You take a penalty
equal to your _spirit bonus_ on all Charisma checks and Charisma-based skill checks involving
worshipers of faiths other than the spirit's, except checks to convince others to convert to the
spirit's faith.

**Taboos**

**Details:** Choose one:\~\~\~\~\~\~- You must strongly revere nature and may not wear metal armor
or shields.\~\~\~- You must follow a paladin or antipaladin code as appropriate for the spirit's
deity.\~\~\~- You may not deliberately speak any lies, including bluffing, exaggerating, stating
half-truths with intent to deceive, lies by omission, and so on, and if you know the answer to a
question that someone asks you, you must give the answer.

### Lesser Spirit Power

**Divine Surge (Su)**

**Ability:** Instead of your normal spells per day for your medium level, you use the spells per day
from the [Mesmerist](ref/class/mesmerist) class. For each level of spell you can now cast (including
level 0), each time you channel a hierophant spirit, select a single spell of that level from the
cleric/oracle spell list to add to your medium spell list and spells known until you lose contact
with the hierophant. When you cast these spells, they count as divine (though not for the purpose of
fulfilling prerequisites), and thus you must provide verbal and somatic components instead of
thought and emotion components, and you need a divine focus if the spell requires one.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Energy Font (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) a number of times per day equal to
1 + your Charisma modifier.

**Choice:** Choose whether you channel positive or negative energy each time you contact a
hierophant spirit; this choice must match the spirit's faith. If you choose positive energy, add
cure spells of each level you can cast from the cleric list to your medium spell list and spells
known. Otherwise, add inflict spells in the same way. These spells count as divine, as in the divine
surge spirit power.

### Greater Spirit Power

**Overflowing Grace (Su)**

**Ability:** When you heal a creature to full hit points or a creature already at full hit points
with your positive or negative energy, that creature gains a +1 sacred bonus on attack rolls, skill
checks, ability checks, and saving throws for 1 round. The bonus is sacred if you use positive
energy and profane if you use negative energy.

**Ability2:** If you destroy or kill one or more creatures with positive or negative energy, you
gain a +1 bonus of the same type on attack rolls, skill checks, ability checks, and saving throws
for 1 round.

### Supreme Spirit Power

**Legendary Hierophant (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can request a minor miracle. This request must be in line with the
options for [miracle](ref/spell/miracle) that don't cost powdered diamond. As usual for a
_[miracle](ref/spell/miracle),_ the deity or other being the spirit worships can choose whether or
not to grant the request at its whim.
