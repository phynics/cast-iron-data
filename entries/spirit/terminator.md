**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 59\
The Terminator is a stoic spirit of relentless death.

**Base Legend**

**Details:** [Guardian](ref/spirit/guardian)

**Gaining Favor**

**Details:** To gain the Terminator's favor, you must engage in over 1 hour (600 combat rounds) of
actual life-or-death battle in the same day, refusing to cease because of either fatigue or mercy.
In addition to the battles themselves, this requires a Constitution check. If you succeed, you must
swear an oath to never retreat from combat and to eschew all mercy, always killing unconscious,
captured, or surrendered foes, regardless of extenuating circumstances.

**Favored Locations**

**Details:** Arenas, fortifications under siege, places of violence.

### Lesser Spirit Power

**Bloodless (Su)**

**Ability:** You can allow the Terminator to gain 1 point of influence over you to automatically end
any [bleed](ref/umr/bleed) or [blood drain](ref/umr/blood-drain) effect or effect with the pain
descriptor. Doing so is not an action, so you can do this even if you are unconscious.

**Passive:** You automatically [stabilize](ref/misc/stable) when at negative hit points, and you are
killed only if your negative hit point total exceeds double your Constitution score.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Emotionless Killer (Su)**

**Passive:** You are immune to emotion effects, including fear, but you can't benefit from morale
bonuses or bardic performances.
