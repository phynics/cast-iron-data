An [agathion](ref/family/agathion) is an animal-like spirit from Nirvana, a defender of innocence
and a champion of good.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral

**Spirits**

**Details:** - **Lspirit Archmage**: [Reptial](ref/monster/agathion-reptial)\~\~\~- **Lspirit
Champion**: [Leonal](ref/monster/agathion-leonal)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Guardian**:
[Avoral](ref/monster/agathion-avoral)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Hierophant**:
[Bishop agathion](ref/monster/agathion-bishop-agathion)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Marshal**:
[Cervinal](ref/monster/agathion-cervinal)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Trickster**:
[Vulpinal](ref/monster/agathion-vulpinal)\~\~\~

**Taboos**

**Details:** Choose one of the following:\~\~\~\~\~\~- You must act aggressively to best (though not
necessarily slay) evil foes.\~\~\~- You must provide whatever service you can to promote good in the
world and help the innocent, as long as such actions do not impair or prevent you from doing good in
the future.\~\~\~- You must offer your full protection and support to nonevil innocents who are in
need of your service.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Lay on Hands (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) class feature, using your medium
level as your effective paladin level.

### Gift

**Voice of Nirvana (Su)**

**Standard:** You can speak to beasts and men alike, gaining the benefits of
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) and [tongues](ref/spell/tongues) simultaneously.

**Usage:** 10 minutes/day per medium level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments
