**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 58\
The Heretic warps the faith of a good deity.

**Base Legend**

**Details:** [Hierophant](ref/spirit/hierophant)

**Gaining Favor**

**Details:** To gain the Heretic's favor, you must first pervert the worship of a congregation of
worshipers of a good deity to evil. You must then vow to corrupt, debase, and violate the worship of
that deity and never perform a truly altruistic act, and finally succeed at a Charisma check.

**Spirit Bonus**

**Details:** The Heretic warps the faith of a good deity.

**Seance Boon**

**Details:** For the purpose of spells and effects that have an effect based on which deity you
worship, you count as worshiping the deity chosen in your vow. For the purpose of divinations, your
alignment is the same as the deity's, and you have an aura corresponding to that deity's alignment
as if you were a [cleric](ref/class/cleric) of that deity. This doesn't alter other effects based on
alignment, like _smite evil._

**Favored Locations**

**Details:** Hidden shrines, repositories of dark knowledge, sacrificial altars, temples.

### Lesser Spirit Power

**Heresy (Su)**

**Ability:** This functions as the _divine surge_ lesser spirit power but you gain the following
[inquisitor](ref/class/inquisitor) spells at the indicated spell levels, instead of selecting a set
of cleric spells each day.

**Cantrips:** Guidance

**Spells:** Divine favor

**Spells:** Castigate

**Spells:** Terrible remorse

**Spells:** Reprobation

**Spells:** Mass castigate

**Spells:** Overwhelming presence

**Special:** Whenever you use your _spirit surge_ on a skill check or ability check to convince or
trick someone into perverting his faith in the good deity you chose with your vow, the Heretic is
pleased and does not gain the usual point of influence over you.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**False Front (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the effects of the _energy font_ intermediate spirit power, but you must match
the faith you chose to pervert, meaning that you always
[channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) and add cure spells.

### Greater Spirit Power

**Stolen Grace (Su)**

**Ability:** When a member of your chosen faith who believes that you are a member of that faith's
clergy requests you to cast a cleric spell of a spell level you can cast, you can allow the Heretic
to gain 1 point of influence over you and expend a spell slot of the appropriate level to cast the
spell.
