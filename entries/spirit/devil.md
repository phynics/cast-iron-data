**Sources:** Psychic Anthology pg 15\
A [devil](ref/family/devil) is a legalistic fiend from Hell, nothing less than absolute law and
order incarnate.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral

**Spirits**

**Details:** - **Lspirit Archmage**: [Ayngavhaul](ref/monster/devil-heresy-devil-ayngavhaul)\~\~\~-
**Lspirit Champion**: [Cornugon](ref/monster/devil-horned-devil-cornugon)\~\~\~- **Lspirit
Guardian**: [Hamatula](ref/monster/devil-barbed-devil-hamatula)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Hierophant**:
[Deimavigga](ref/monster/devil-apostate-devil-deimavigga)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Trickster**:
[Osyluth](ref/monster/devil-bone-devil-osyluth)\~\~\~[Erinyes](ref/monster/devil-erinyes) (champion)

**Taboos**

**Details:** Choose one of the following:\~\~\~\~\~\~- You must abide by any contracts you sign or
verbally agree to, though you are only ever required to obey the letter of these agreements and may
twist their intent to your favor.\~\~\~- You must tempt others whenever the opportunity
arises.\~\~\~- You must destroy or despoil all holy sites and relics that you encounter, if doing so
would not put you in obvious danger.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Summon Devil (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can summon a denizen of Hell. This functions as per
[summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2), but you can summon only animals with the
[fiendish template](ref/template/fiendish) or outsiders with the [devil](ref/subtype/devil) subtype.
This ability can summon only one creature, regardless of the list used.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

At 8th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +3._

At 10th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +4._

At 12th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +5._

At 14th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +6._

At 16th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +7._

At 18th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +8._

At 20th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +9._

### Gift

**See in Darkness (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 30 feet. If you already have darkvision,
your darkvision range increases by 30 feet instead. This darkvision allows you to see perfectly in
darkness of any kind, even that created by a [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) spell.
