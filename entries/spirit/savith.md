Savith was the Azlanti hero who, during the Age of Legend, descended into the serpentfolk city of
Ilmurea and beheaded the evil creatures' god, [Ydersius](ref/faith/ydersius).

**Base Legend**

**Details:** [Champion](ref/spirit/champion)

**Gaining Favor**

**Details:** To gain Savith's favor, you must defeat an evil serpentfolk who is at least 1 level
higher than yourself. Using a magic weapon, you must decapitate the creature (which requires a
successful Strength check) and toss its head into lava or a massive fire while vowing to never
retreat from a battle involving evil serpentfolk or worshipers of Ydersius. Savith then answers your
call.

**Taboos**

**Details:** If you accept a _taboo_ while channeling Savith, you must never coat any of your
natural or manufactured weapons in poison, nor can you ever dose an enemy with any type of poison.

### Lesser Spirit Power

**Azlanti Heroics (Su)**

**Ability:** Savith grants a +1 bonus on all attack rolls when you are wielding a
[greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword) in melee combat against evil monstrous humanoids instead of
granting proficiency in an exotic weapon with _champion's prowess._

**Alters:** Champion's prowess

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Toe to Toe (Sp)**

**Swift:** When you are in melee combat with a creature that is at least one size category larger
than you, if none of your allies are [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) it, you can increase your own
size as if through [enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person), gaining all the effects of that
spell. This does not provoke attacks of opportunity, and the effect lasts as long as you are in
melee with that creature or until it is dead.

### Supreme Spirit Power

**Azlanti Heroics (Su)**

**Ability:** For the _legendary champion_ ability, Savith grants any two of the following feats:
[Disruptive](ref/feat/disruptive), [Greater Weapon Focus](ref/feat/greater-weapon-focus)
(greatsword), [Greater Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/greater-weapon-specialization) (greatsword),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (greatsword),
[Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/weapon-specialization) (greatsword), and
[Spellbreaker](ref/feat/spellbreaker).

**Alters:** Legendary champion
