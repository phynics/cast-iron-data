**Sources:** Psychic Anthology pg 14\
A [demon](ref/family/demon) is a fiendish spawn of the fecund Abyss, discord and destruction made
flesh.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral

**Spirits**

**Details:** - **Lspirit Archmage**: [Glabrezu](ref/monster/demon-glabrezu)\~\~\~- **Lspirit

**Taboos**

**Details:** Choose one of the following:\~\~\~\~\~\~- You must not ignore an opportunity to destroy
something (or someone) precious to those who actively oppose your plans or goals.\~\~\~- You must
take every opportunity to tempt or coerce others into embracing their most sinful desires, and you
must partake in such delights at every opportunity.\~\~\~- You must take every opportunity to
inflict physical or emotional pain upon others, provided such indulgences do not detract from your
personal goals or safety.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Summon Demon (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can summon a horror from the Abyss. This functions as per
[summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2), but you can summon only animals with the
[fiendish template](ref/template/fiendish) or outsiders with the [demon](ref/subtype/demon) subtype.
This ability can summon only one creature, regardless of the list used.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

At 8th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +3._

At 10th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +4._

At 12th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +5._

At 14th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +6._

At 16th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +7._

At 18th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +8._

At 20th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +9._

### Gift

**Demonic Taint (Su)**

**Passive:** Your natural weapons, as well as any weapons you wield, are treated as chaotic and evil
for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
