A [psychopomp](ref/family/psychopomp) is a steward of souls from the Boneyard, tasked with bringing
mortal souls to the afterlife.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral

**Spirits**

**Details:** - **Lspirit Archmage**: [Viduus](ref/monster/psychopomp-viduus)\~\~\~- **Lspirit
Champion**: [Memitim](ref/monster/psychopomp-memitim)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Guardian**:
[Vanth](ref/monster/psychopomp-vanth)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Hierophant**:
[Ahmuuth](ref/monster/psychopomp-ahmuuth)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Marshal**:
[Catrina](ref/monster/psychopomp-catrina)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Trickster**:
[Morrigna](ref/monster/psychopomp-morrigna)\~\~\~

**Taboos**

**Details:** Choose one of the following:\~\~\~\~\~\~- You must destroy all haunts and undead
creatures whose paths you cross and oppose the creation of such creatures.\~\~\~- You must destroy
all creatures that would seek to imprison or destroy mortal souls, and set trapped souls free at
every opportunity.\~\~\~- You must wear a mask that covers the entirety of your face, and never
allow it to be removed for as long as you channel the psychopomp.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Spirit Touch (Su)**

**Flavor:** You gain powers that assist in battling evil spirits.

**Passive:** You are always granted a saving throw to negate death spells, magical death effects,
and energy drain, even if one is not normally allowed.

**Swift:** You can grant your natural weapons, as well as any weapon you wield, the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon special ability for a number of rounds equal
to your medium level. These rounds don't need to be consecutive.

### Gift

**Spiritsense (Su)**

**Ability:** You can notice, locate, and distinguish between living and undead creatures within 30
feet, just as if you have the [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) ability. This sense does not allow
you to detect objects, but it does allow you to notice living things that are not creatures.
