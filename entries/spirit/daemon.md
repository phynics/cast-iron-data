**Sources:** Psychic Anthology pg 14\
A [daemon](ref/family/daemon) is a nihilistic fiend from Abaddon, an entity obsessed with the
eradication of mortal life.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral

**Spirits**

**Details:** - **Lspirit Archmage**: [Venedaemon](ref/monster/daemon-venedaemon)\~\~\~- **Lspirit
Champion**: [Derghodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-derghodaemon)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Guardian**:
[Ceustodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-ceustodaemon)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Hierophant**:
[Astradaemon](ref/monster/daemon-astradaemon)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Marshal**:
[Purrodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-purrodaemon)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Trickster**:
[Phasmadaemon](ref/monster/daemon-phasmadaemon)\~\~\~

**Taboos**

**Details:** Choose one of the following:\~\~\~\~\~\~- You must painfully end the life of a single
mortal creature (with no fewer than half your Hit Dice) per day, provided that doing so does not
jeopardize your own safety.\~\~\~- You must take every opportunity to manipulate and coerce others
into performing self-deprecating and self-destructive actions.\~\~\~- You must consume a bit of
every mortal creature you slay, be it flesh or soul.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Summon Daemon (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can summon a servant of Abaddon. This functions as per
[summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2), but you can summon only animals with the
[fiendish template](ref/template/fiendish) or outsiders with the [daemon](ref/subtype/daemon)
subtype. This ability can summon only one creature, regardless of the list used.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

At 8th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +3._

At 10th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +4._

At 12th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +5._

At 14th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +6._

At 16th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +7._

At 18th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +8._

At 20th level: The level of the spell increases to _summon monster +9._

### Gift

**Consume the Soul (Sp)**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to cast [death knell](ref/spell/death-knell) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) using your medium level as the spell's caster
level. Instead of gaining the normal benefits of this spell when the target fails its save, you can
instead lock the target's soul within a [soul gem](ref/spell/create-soul-gem), functioning like the
_soul lock_ ability of a [cacodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-cacodaemon).

**Flavor:** You can consume or imprison the soul of a dying creature.

**Special:** You count as an evil outsider for the purpose of determining whether you receive a
benefit when you ingest a soul gem created by the _soul lock_ ability.

**Usage:** Three times per day

**Usage:** Three times per day
