**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 52\
Eccardian Drovenge, son of [Mammon](ref/monster/archdevil-mammon), was slain atop the Arodennama
during a bid for rulership of Westcrown, in a battle that saw the collapse of the Council of
Thieves. It was Aspexia's accidental communion with his spirit during a recent _seance_ at the
monument that inspired her to rebuild the [Council of Thieves](ref/rule/aff-thieves) in its new
incarnation.

**Base Legend**

**Details:** [Trickster](ref/spirit/trickster)

**Gaining Favor**

**Details:** To gain the Prince's favor, you must climb atop the 90-foot-tall Arodennama in
Westcrown. There, you must pour oil down the statue's cheeks, then set the oil afire. You must vow
to seek political influence, and then succeed at a Charisma check (DC = 10 + half your medium
level). Tieflings gain a +5 bonus on this check.

**Favored Locations**

**Details:** Monuments, noble homes, thieves' dens, anywhere in Westcrown.

**Taboos**

**Details:** Eccardian's pride demanded that his victims know whose hand they died by. If you accept
a _taboo_ while channeling the Prince, you must not attack any foe who is completely unaware of your
presence.

### Greater Spirit Power

**Master Duelist (Su)**

**Passive:** While wielding a light or one-handed piercing weapon, you gain a +3 dodge bonus to your
AC against melee attacks. Your _surprise strike_ damage also applies to attacks against creatures
you are [flanking](ref/rule/flanking).
