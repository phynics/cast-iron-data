An [angel](ref/family/angel) is a servant of the good-aligned planes, a voice of unity and
cooperation among celestial beings.

**Alignment**

**Details:** Neutral

**Spirits**

**Details:** - **Lspirit Archmage**: [Empyrean](ref/monster/angel-empyrean)\~\~\~- **Lspirit
Champion**: [Movanic deva](ref/monster/angel-movanic-deva)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Guardian**:
[Solar](ref/monster/angel-solar)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Hierophant**:
[Balisse](ref/monster/angel-balisse)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Marshal**:
[Planetar](ref/monster/angel-planetar)\~\~\~- **Lspirit Trickster**:
[Astral deva](ref/monster/angel-astral-deva)\~\~\~

**Taboos**

**Details:** Choose one of the following:\~\~\~\~\~\~- You must not ignore an opportunity to destroy
or hinder an evil creature or organization unless doing so would put innocents in harm's way.\~\~\~-
You must always offer your foes a chance to peacefully surrender to you, and you must accept and
honor any such terms that your foes agree to.\~\~\~- You must offer whatever support and assistance
you can spare to any creature that asks such service from you, provided your actions do not further
the cause of evil.

### Intermediate Spirit Power

**Protective Aura (Su)**

**Passive:** You gain a +4 deflection bonus to your AC and a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws
against attacks made by or effects created by evil creatures. In addition, you are protected from
effects created by evil creatures that possess you or exert mental control over you, as per
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil).

**Standard:** You can project this benefit to allies within 10 feet as an aura.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per medium level

### Gift

**Truespeech (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [tongues](ref/spell/tongues) as a constant
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), with a caster level equal to your medium level.
