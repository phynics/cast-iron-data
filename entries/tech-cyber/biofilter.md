**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 35\
**Price** 16,000 gp\
**Slot** body; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Install** DC 25; **Implantation** 3

### Construction

**Craft DC** 25; **Cost** 8,000 gp\
[Craft Cybernetics](ref/feat/craft-cybernetics), [cybernetics lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A nanite matrix is installed in the kidneys and liver to help purge the body of toxins. A biofilter
provides a +4 competence bonus on saving throws against disease and poison. In addition, when the
user attempts such a saving throw, she rolls twice and takes the higher result.
