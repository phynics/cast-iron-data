**Price** 4,000 gp\
**Slot** eyes; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Install** DC 28; **Implantation** 1

### Construction

**Craft DC** 28; **Cost** 2,000 gp\
[Craft Cybernetics](ref/feat/craft-cybernetics), [cybernetics lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A cybernetic eye can be enhanced by [cyberart](ref/tech-cyber/cyberart) for no additional cost,
giving it an unusual color or pattern. When a pair of eyes is installed, the user gains a +2
circumstance bonus on Perception checks and a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws against blindness
or visual effects. The bonus on Perception checks stacks with circumstance bonuses on Perception
checks from other cybernetic sources. A pair of cybernetic eyes can be further enhanced by one
[veemod](ref/tech-misc/veemod-brown) at a price equal to twice the veemod's normal price.

The listed price, implantation value, and install DC are for having two eyes installed. A single
cybernetic eye costs half the price and half the listed implantation value, but a creature must
replace all of its eyes to receive the benefits above.
