## Cyberfiber Muscles (Mark I)

**Price** 8,000 gp (Mark I), 32,000 gp (Mark II), 72,000 gp (Mark III)\
**Slot** body; **Weight** 10 lbs. (Mark I), 10 lbs. (Mark II), 10 lbs. (Mark III)\
**Install** DC 24 (Mark I), DC 28 (Mark II), DC 36 (Mark III)\
**Implantation** 2 (Mark I), 4 (Mark II), 8 (Mark III)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 24 (Mark I), DC 28 (Mark II), DC 32 (Mark III); **Cost** 4,000 gp (Mark I), 16,000 gp
(Mark II), 36,000 gp (Mark III)\
[Craft Cybernetics](ref/feat/craft-cybernetics), [cybernetics lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

Cyberfiber muscles are powerful synthetic muscles that augment but do not replace existing muscle
mass in a creature. Once installed, cyberfiber muscles grant an enhancement bonus to Strength. This
enhancement bonus alters price, implantation value, install DCs, and Craft DCs as listed below.

|Cyberfiber|Enhancement|Implantation|Install|
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|Mark I|+2 Str|2|DC 24|
|Mark II|+4 Str|4|DC 28|
|Mark III|+6 Str|8|DC 36|
