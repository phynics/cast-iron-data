## Wirejack Tendons (Mark I)

**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 38\
**Price** 8,000 gp (Mark I), 32,000 gp (Mark II), 72,000 gp (Mark III)\
**Slot** body; **Weight** 10 lbs. (Mark I), 10 lbs. (Mark II), 10 lbs. (Mark III)\
**Install** DC 24 (Mark I), DC 28 (Mark II), DC 36 (Mark III)\
**Implantation** 2 (Mark I), 4 (Mark II), 8 (Mark III)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 24 (Mark I), DC 28 (Mark II), DC 32 (Mark III); **Cost** 4,000 gp (Mark I), 16,000 gp
(Mark II), 36,000 gp (Mark III)\
[Craft Cybernetics](ref/feat/craft-cybernetics), [cybernetics lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

Wirejack tendons not only strengthen and enhance existing reflexes, but also enhance the
transmission of nerve impulses to surrounding musculature. Once installed, wirejack tendons grant an
enhancement bonus to Dexterity. This enhancement bonus alters the price, implantation values,
install DCs, and Craft DCs of the implant as listed below.

|Wirejack|Enhancement|Implantation|Install|
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|Mark I|+2 Dex|2|DC 24|
|Mark II|+4 Dex|4|DC 28|
|Mark III|+6 Dex|8|DC 36|
