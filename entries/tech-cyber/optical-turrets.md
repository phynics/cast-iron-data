**Sources:** Pathfinder \#88: Valley of the Brain Collectors pg 75\
**Price** 6,000 gp\
**Slot** eyes; **Weight** —\
**Install** DC 30; **Implantation** 2

### Construction

**Craft DC** 30; **Cost** 3,000 gp\
[Craft Cybernetics](ref/feat/craft-cybernetics), [cybernetics lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

These cybernetic implants replace the recipient's eyes with turrets containing multiple lenses, such
as one finds on an optical microscope. In addition to providing normal vision, the implants can
rapidly adjust to varying light levels, making the wearer immune to both the
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) and [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) conditions.

If the wearer is willing or helpless, additional lenses can be added (or removed) from the optical
turrets with 1 minute's work. Each of the three turrets can hold one eyes slot magic item. These
lenses do not count against the wearer's limit of one eyes slot item, but only one function can be
active at a time. The wearer can change which of the installed lenses is active (or deactivate them
all) as a swift action.
