**Before Combat** The ranger casts longstrider and resist energy (fire), then drinks his potion of
bull's strength.\
**During Combat** The ranger uses Shield Slam to push opponents into difficult terrain, off cliffs,
or into the area of spike growth.\
**Base Statistics** Without bull's strength, longstrider, and resist energy, the ranger's statistics
are **Resist** none; **Speed** 20 ft.; **Melee** +1 warhammer +7/+2 (1d8+4/×3), mwk heavy shield
+7/+2 (1d4+3 plus bull rush); **Str** 16; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 22 (26 vs. bull rush or trip);
