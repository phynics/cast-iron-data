### Description

The fire cleric serves infernal or elemental powers and uses his magic to purge weakness from the
world.

### Aleksion Coric

Aleksion sees himself as bringing the flame of truth and order to burn away all that is impure and
rebellious. Though he serves Hell, he believes fulfilling his task is necessary to preserve human
civilization in a world beset by barbarians and demons.
