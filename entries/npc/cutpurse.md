### Description

The cutpurse is a dirty sneak who uses surprise and stealth to rob unsuspecting victims.

### Avem the Blade

A young tough trying to make a name for himself, Avem prefers robbery and burglary to outright
murder - but isn't afraid to kill.

**Combat Encounters:** Avem might target a solitary PC in a dangerous part of town, or may be the
leader of a gang of unskilled thugs.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** Avem is skilled at many kinds of theft, and can be hired to commit
petty larceny or create a distraction at reasonable rates.
