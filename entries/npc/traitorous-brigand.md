### Description

Brigands are hard as nails and always look out for themselves first.

### Rorn the Black

Rorn has made a good living as a bandit, but the same can't be said for his allies. Sometimes he
runs out on them when their luck turns bad, sometimes he's the only survivor, and sometimes he cuts
down his own allies to claim all the loot.
