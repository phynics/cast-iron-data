### Description

These academics search for historical clues and relics.

### Rindol Runehand

Orphaned in a giant attack, Rindol dedicated his life to learning about giantkind so he can aid the
dwarven people in destroying them. He specializes in magic that can confound giants' unsophisticated
minds, and has memorized grand tales and songs of dwarven glory.
