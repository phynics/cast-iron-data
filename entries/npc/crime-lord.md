### Description

Though most criminal masterminds work behind the scenes, crime lords don't mind getting their hands
dirty.

### Tontelizi Legbreaker

When Tontelizi first took to a life of crime, he was relegated to leg-breaking as a joke on his
slight stature - and collections doubled. Soon, the crime family found that Tontelizi excelled at
any task involving extortion, intimidation, or violence. When the patriarch's health failed -
assisted by Tontelizi's poison - the gnome eliminated two likely successors and cowed the rest with
threats and blackmail. He was chosen to take over the family with nearly unanimous support.
