### Tactics

**Before Combat** The druid casts shambler to create 1d4+2 advanced shambling mounds.\
**During Combat** The druid deploys shambling mounds, wild shapes into a Huge earth elemental, and
casts spells on himself. If interrupted, he earth glides and casts storm of vengeance.
