### Description

A battle mage is always ready for a fight. She knows that the one who strikes first strikes best.
Never lacking in firepower, her versatility on the battlefield is always appreciated. Battle mages
make excellent military fire support and magical bodyguards. They can be found alone, guarding a
traveling merchant (CR 7) or guide (CR 8) or adventuring with a medium or minstrel, monster hunter
or gladiator, and tomb raider (CR 9). A squad of four battle mages (CR 9) might be attached to an
army.
