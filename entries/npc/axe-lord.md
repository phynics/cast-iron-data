### Description

Axe lords guard dwarven citadels, hunting foul monsters from the deeps and threats from outside.

### Edager Graybeard

A grim and proud dwarven warrior, Edager has a hard time trusting people not of his race, but saves
his true enmity for goblins, giants, orcs, and the foul creatures of the Darklands.
