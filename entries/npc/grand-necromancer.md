### Tactics

**Before Combat** The wizard casts false life, mage armor, mind blank, resist energy (fire), see
invisibility, and spell turning.\
**During Combat** The wizard casts time stop and energy drain on the most dangerous-looking target,
then thins out the ranks of his enemies with chain lightning.\
**Base Statistics** Without false life, mage armor, mind blank, resist energy (fire), see
invisibility, and spell turning, the wizard's statistics are **AC** 19, touch 16, flat-footed 18;
**hp** 155; **Fort** +14, **Ref** +11, **Will** +17; **Defensive Abilities** none; **Resist**
cold 20.
