### Description

Filled with bluster and swagger, a recruit lacks the discipline of an experienced soldier and can
quickly lose resolve if he finds himself in over his head. A recruit works best in a large group
under a strong leader who can keep all the soldiers in line and working together.

Left to his own devices, a recruit becomes bored and looks for entertainments such as drinking and
gambling to pass the time.
