### Tactics

**Before Combat** Twice per month, the druid casts ironwood on her armor and also casts liveoak.\
**During Combat** The druid casts empowered cone of cold, then begins spontaneously summoning
creatures and casting animal growth, stoneskin, and greater magic fang on them. While they are
fighting, she casts spells to heal them and enhance her abilities. When entering melee, she casts
true seeing, death ward, freedom of movement, and barkskin on herself, and drinks her potions of
haste and displacement before wild shaping into a Huge water elemental.
