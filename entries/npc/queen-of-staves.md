### Description

A lone, lightly armored traveler carrying a walking staff, a queen of staves mixes spell power with
whirling quarterstaff attacks.

### Esmerelda Darkshadow

Those who think all elves are creatures of light and joy have not met Esmerelda. Dressing in dark
colors with a temperament to match, Esmerelda believes she is doomed to a life of pain and gloom.
Only when her enemies lie broken at her feet does she crack the slightest hint of a smile. Yet this
dour disposition is not simply part of her nature - as a young woman, Esmerelda was told by an
oracle that she would bring ruin and destruction to everything she ever loved. Two days later, her
wife-to-be was slain by orcs. Resolved to never love again, she paints herself gray with ash to
symbolize the death she carries.

**Combat Encounters:** Esmerelda travels the world alone, attacking those who trigger her mercurial
temper (such as by not keeping their distance). Any sort of romantic proposition or prophecy magic
sends her into a rage.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** All Esmerelda can talk about is the ultimate futility of life and the
inevitability of death and decay.
