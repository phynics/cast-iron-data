**Boon** A captain can arrange sea passage for PCs or cargo to any navigable destination, and can

### Description

A captain is mistress of a ship at sea. She may be the legal authority and representative of her
nation, company, faith, or faction, or she may be an independent captain owing allegiance to none
and claiming no home but the sea itself. Captains may be used as pirates, admirals, or master
merchants. A captain is usually accompanied by a first mate and a traveling merchant as
quartermaster (CR 12).
