**Before Combat** The cleric casts deathwatch.\

### Description

The vivisectionist cleric serves the god of pain and darkness, and can keep victims alive for weeks.

### Melisai Hesani

Melisai is personable despite her arrogance, and considers herself a surgeon and a sculptor. When
not adventuring, she works with poorhouse medics, embalmers preparing bodies, and torturers-anyone
who gives her the opportunity to experiment. She prefers the company of like-minded adventurers, but
if none are available, she animates zombies.

**Combat Encounters:** Melisai is useful as an enemy leader, member of a rival adventuring party, or
servant of an important NPC or villain.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** Though evil and disturbing, Melisai is easy to get along with. She is
forthright, and has no problem helping the PCs if it achieves her own ends.
