### Tactics

**Before Combat** The druid casts barkskin and cat's grace. She casts endure elements every morning.\
**During Combat** The druid keeps her distance from opponents. She casts freedom of movement, then

### Description

Typically found in arid regions, these druids use magic to supply others with water for a price.

### Sasrukutenek

Sasrukutenek sells water at a trade stop on the overland route between two desert cities. She
overcharges for this commodity, but since it's so scarce, her customers have very little choice.
