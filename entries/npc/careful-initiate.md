**Careful Initiate**

**Challenge rating:** 1/2

**XP** 200\
Human [monk](ref/class/monk) 1\
LN Medium humanoid (human)\
**Init** +3; **Senses** Perception +6

### Defense

**AC** 16, touch 16, flat-footed 12 (+3 Dex, +1 dodge, +2 Wis)\
**hp** 9 (1d8+1)\
**Fort** +2, **Ref** +5, **Will** +4

### Offense

**Speed** 30 ft.\
**Melee** unarmed strike +3 (1d6+1) or kama +3 (1d6+1) or unarmed strike flurry of blows +2/+2
(1d6+1)\
**Ranged** light crossbow +3 (1d8/19-20) or shuriken +3 (1d2+1) or shuriken flurry of blows +2/+2
(1d2+1)\
**Special Attacks** flurry of blows, stunning fist (1/day, DC 12)

### Tactics

**Before Combat** The monk uses Stealth to catch enemies off-guard, starting any surprise round with
Stunning Fist.\
**During Combat** The monk never fights multiple opponents if she can help it, and prefers to use
her shuriken with a flurry of blows before entering melee.

**Skills** Acrobatics +7, Knowledge (history, religion) +5, Perception +6, Sense Motive +6, Stealth
+7\

**Combat Gear** potions of [cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds) (2), potions of

bolts, [shuriken](ref/eq-weapon/shuriken) (20), 50 gp

### Description

Neophyte monks are often eager to prove their mettle in battle, but just as often their strict
training means they tend to act tentatively when deprived of their master's guidance.
