### Tactics

**Before Combat** The fighter applies oil of greater magic weapon to his falchion and oil of magic
vestment, then drinks his potions of barkskin, heroism, and bear's endurance and activates his boots
of flying.\
**During Combat** The fighter attacks a single enemy until he deals a pair of critical hits, then
switches to another. He usually opts to inflict stun and bleed, then blind and exhaust. He goes
after good outsiders, paladins, and clerics of good-aligned deities first.\
