### Description

Haughty avengers often serve as diplomats and bodyguards.

### Nyalil Stormstrike

Nyalil sees herself as a full elf, not a half-breed. She so thoroughly rejects her human side that
she treats humans as lesser creatures - but can be so charming that humans often love her anyway.

**Combat Encounters:** Nyalil is often in the company of elven knights, wizards, and priests, who
only halfway accept her.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** Nyalil is both charming and arrogant, and her words are often a stream
of veiled insultsand clever quips, especially when she's dealing with humans.
