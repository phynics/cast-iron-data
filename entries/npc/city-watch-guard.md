### Description

Guards of the city watch are vigilant soldiers, dedicated to keeping the peace and maintaining
order. They defend the city walls and gates against external threats, but they also stand ready to
break up fights and brawls, disarming or sundering weapons drawn in anger, forcing unruly citizens
apart, and tending the wounded.

Different types of guards may be created with lighter armor (breastplates and heavy shields) and
one-handed weapons (battle axes or longswords). Replacing guards' halberds with glaives, guisarmes,
or ranseurs gives them a reach attack. Outfitting guards with pikes makes a group of pikemen, while
giving them tower shields and short swords creates crossbowmen for a siege. Temple guards can
replace Improved Sunder with [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
(two-bladed sword). Prison guards might replace Improved Sunder with
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) and Power Attack with Exotic Weapon
Proficiency (whip). Four guards and a turnkey (CR 6) or a torturer (CR 7) can provide security for a
small prison or dungeon.

Guards may be encountered alone, in pairs (CR 3), in groups of four (CR 5), or as a patrol of six
accompanied by a guard officer (CR 7), depending on how dangerous their city or neighborhood is.
