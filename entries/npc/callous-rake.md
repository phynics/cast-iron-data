### Description

The callous rake is a young paladin exhibiting un-paladin-like behavior. If unchecked, these habits
become the first step toward disgrace.

### Tristram Reis

Tall, lean, and exceedingly handsome, Tristram Reis is the vision of a true and noble paladin-until
he opens his mouth. The son of a lesser noble, he has faith in the goddess of valor, but is a bit
arrogant and enamored with his own status as a holy warrior.
