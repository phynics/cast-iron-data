### Description

The desert stalker snares his prey and bleeds it dry.

### Arkesh

Arkesh loves the desert, its people, and their traditions, and hates those who would change or
exploit any of these. He might let a water caravan run by foreigners pass, but would kill a similar
group bringing exotic goods to tempt desert people into strange new ways.

**Combat Encounters:** Arkesh attacks the PCs for a perceived affront to his territory or
traditions.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** If PCs respect the desert, Arkesh might rescue them during a sandstorm,
guide them past a dangerous monster, or explain in detail the hazards of the wilds.
