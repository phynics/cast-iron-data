### Description

Masters at breaking into treasure vaults, many arcane tricksters are more interested in the
challenge of such break-ins than the riches they gain from them.

### Delant the Bold

A true professional, Delant is meticulous and organized, and hates it when her compatriots improvise
or otherwise fail to follow her carefully laid plans.
