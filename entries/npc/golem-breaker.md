### Description

A golem-breaker makes a ruin of constructs, clockworks, and complex devices.

### Esheyna

Esheyna has always had a knack for spotting the weak points in structures, a talent she has long put
to use to sabotage enemy siege engines. Now a legendary mercenary, she enjoys bashing apart teams of
iron golems just to see how they're supposed to fit together.

**Combat Encounters:** Esheyna might battle the PCs for control of an ancient juggernaut just so she
has bragging rights about destroying it.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** Esheyna offers advice and assistance to the PCs about ancient war
machines or clockwork invaders from another plane.
