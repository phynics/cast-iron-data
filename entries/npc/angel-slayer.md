### Description

An angel slayer decries false gods and their messengers.

### Wrathack

Wrathack doesn't tolerate preaching about goodness or the benign nature of the gods. She decorates
her clothing with bloody feathers from angel wings as a warning to any who dare speak lies about
hope, kindness, and salvation.
