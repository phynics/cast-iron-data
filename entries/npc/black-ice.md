### Description

These evasive spellcasters make frustrating enemies.

### Scal Wistalvak

Gnomes love pranks and practical jokes, but few are as dark, devious, and deadly as those of Scal
Wistalvak. The entire time he uses illusions and ice magic to bring about psychological torture and
chilling death, he stays out of sight, laughing maniacally.
