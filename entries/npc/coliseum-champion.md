### Description

The champion is mistress of the arena, leaving in her wake a trail of wrecked armaments, battered
bodies, and shattered dreams. Raising her bloodied blade, she exults in the roar of the crowd and
the terror she inspires in her foes. Champions can also be used as barbarian chieftains or mercenary
captains. A champion is usually encountered alone or in the company of a pair of lesser gladiators
(CR 10).
