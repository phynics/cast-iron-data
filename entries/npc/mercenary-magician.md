### Description

The mercenary magician serves whoever can pay her, but is not particularly loyal to her employers.

### Maron Blackthorne

Though she's fond of gambling, Maron doesn't always have the best luck. When the odds turn against
her, she leaves town in a hurry to avoid paying her debts, and picks jobs that get her quickly out
of range of collectors, even if those jobs are dangerous.
