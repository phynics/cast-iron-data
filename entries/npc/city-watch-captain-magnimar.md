### Description

Magnimarian watch captains are selected from the ranks of proven city guards and undergo more
training than standard troops. Captain Acacia Uriana demands intelligent watch captains, and many
are trained in investigative techniques to help solve ongoing crimes throughout the city, as well as
to collect valuable evidence that helps determine a suspect's guilt or innocence. Watch captains
train in deadlier weapons than their more common counterparts, and most prefer to wield two blades
at a given time; like city guards, however, they prefer to use nonlethal means to handle minor
crimes, and are adept at disarming their opponents with their swordbreaker daggers.
