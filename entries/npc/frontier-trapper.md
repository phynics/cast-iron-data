### Description

Trappers are roving hunters who wander the woods. They take any animal they can safely hunt or trap,
but they are best known as the heart of the fur trade, making a variety of handcrafted but deadly
effective traps to catch the unwary beasts of the forest. Trappers could be used as royal game
wardens, as scouts, or as hunters for a nomadic tribe.

Trappers are typically loners but will sometimes pair up with another trapper (CR 5), a monster
hunter (CR 6), or a beast master (CR 7) for companionship.
