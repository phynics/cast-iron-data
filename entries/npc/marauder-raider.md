### Description

Raiders are the children of the raging storm, wild warriors from the wrinkled hills and jagged
mountains. They descend from their aeries with moods as foul and war cries as terrifying as the wild
weather that drives them, leaping, scaling the heights, diving from cover to cover, and raining
death upon their enemies.

Raiders can be used as skirmishers or scouts, or they can represent any sort of bandits or wild men
from the hills who strike the outlying bastions of civilization without warning.

Raiders are often found alone but may travel with a monster hunter (CR 7) or in pairs with a viking
(CR 9). Six raiders might form a raiding party with a chieftain, or serve as a chieftain's honor
guard (CR 12).
