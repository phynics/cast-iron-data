**Before Combat** The sorcerer casts stoneskin.\

transformation on himself.\

### Description

The fiery dragonkin embodies all the greedy, violent, and territorial impulses of red dragons.
Heedless of the lives they crush in their rise to glory, the dragonkin sorcerers are born
conquerors, and see in their noble draconic blood an undeniable right to rule over lesser beings.
