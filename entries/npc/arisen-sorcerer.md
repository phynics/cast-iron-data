### Tactics

**Before Combat** The sorcerer casts false life and mage armor.\
**During Combat** The sorcerer casts energy drain, power word kill, and wail of the banshee. She may
deter opponents with solid fog, waves of exhaustion, or her grasp of the dead ability.\
**Base Statistics** Without false life and mage armor, the sorcerer's statistics are **AC** 26,
touch 17, flat-footed 23; **hp** 202.
