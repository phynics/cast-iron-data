### Description

Stalking through boiling and sometimes acidic mud pits, these druids have caustic personalities and
abilities.

### Kiro

Kiro, the mud shaman of a remote tribe, sees outsiders as a threat to his culture and people. He
acquired a green dragonhide breastplate years ago from a trader, but keeps it hidden under cloaks
and wraps, ashamed that he had to buy it rather than earn it through combat.

**Combat Encounters:** Kiro uses mud pits as traps to entangle his enemies.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** A stubborn xenophobe, Kiro does not like speaking with those who are
not of his tribe.
