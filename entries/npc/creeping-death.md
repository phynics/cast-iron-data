### Tactics

**Before Combat** The druid casts stoneskin, barkskin, and longstrider and drinks her potion of
haste.\
**During Combat** The druid locks down opponents with entangle, wall of stone, and transmute rock to
mud, while casting creeping doom and animate plants from a distance.\
**Base Statistics** Without stoneskin, barkskin, and longstrider, the druid's statistics are **AC**
27, touch 16, flat-footed 23; **DR** none; **Speed** 20 ft.

Creeping death druids see terrain as the ultimate weapon against those who would despoil their

This stalker of bogs and moors works tirelessly to end the encroachment of civilization on her
desolate home.
