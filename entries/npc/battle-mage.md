### Tactics

**Before Combat** The wizard uses her wand to cast mage armor.\
**During Combat** The wizard attacks with scorching ray, her longbow, and hand of the apprentice. If
forced into melee, she casts mirror image and obscuring mist.\
**Base Statistics** Without mage armor, the wizard's statistics are **AC** 12, touch 12,
flat-footed 10.
