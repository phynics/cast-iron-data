### Description

The town watcher protects a settlement from hostile creatures.

### Colin Fletcher

Colin vigilantly guards his town, and is quick to respond if any visitors start fights or cause
trouble with locals.

**Combat Encounters:** Colin fires a warning shot from a rooftop to distract unruly adventurers in
town, or uses a tanglefoot bag on a PC who's obviously drunk, raging, or out of control.

**Roleplaying Suggestions:** Colin is the eyes and ears for his town, and is a source of valuable
information about the comings and goings of strangers.
