### Description

These elves shun civilization and protect ancient groves.

### Selindril Longstrider

A century ago, Selindril abandoned civilization. Years in the wilderness made her distant,
ill-mannered, and confrontational. She keeps a regular journal devoted half to her travels and half
to rambling screeds on her personal philosophy of extreme isolationism.
