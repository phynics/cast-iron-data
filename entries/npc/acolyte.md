### Description

An acolyte has just begun to unravel the mysteries of her faith, and lacks the fervent zeal that
more indoctrinated members of her religion have. She is eager to learn, but her incomplete teachings
mean she is more easily swayed by contrary rhetoric.

Settlements that have adepts rather than clerics are often primitive or remote. Their religious
practices may be a strange or heretical offshoot of a main religion, weaker than the common form but
giving access to spells that are normally unavailable to true clerics (such as minor creation and
sleep). A person trained by a cleric who instead manifests adept abilities may be cast out as a
blasphemer or witch.
