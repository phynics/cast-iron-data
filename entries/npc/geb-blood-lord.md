### Description

Ruling Geb from the Ebon Mausoleum in Mechitar, Blood Lords ensure the steady operation of the
country. Acting as lawmakers and judges, Blood Lords are all powerful necromancers, and many of them
are undead: typically vampires and liches, though other types of abominations also fill their ranks.
The living and the dead of Geb fear the Blood Lords and take every pain to avoid their notice, for
if a citizen crosses a Blood Lord, the rest of his life, and perhaps his afterlife, can become a
nightmare.
