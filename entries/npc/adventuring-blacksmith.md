### Description

Smiths who craft weapons may also be skilled in their use.

### Vika Beliskar

Apprenticed to a blacksmith in her youth, Vika quit the trade to take up a new life as an
adventurer. After a cache of tomes taught her how to mix magic with the forging of steel, she found
herself drawn back to her old calling.
