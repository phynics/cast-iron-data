### Description

Generals are the masters of the battlefield, expert and veteran soldiers who conceive and execute
tactical plans and inspire their troops on to victory. Generals may command from a rearward vantage
point, but when needed, may dive into the fray with sword in hand to turn the tide through sheer
puissance. Generals can be used as arena champions, masters of fighting schools, or elite warriors
trained to fight with or against spellcasters. A general is usually accompanied by a knight
adjutant, with a lance of four cavalry as bodyguards and messengers (CR 12).
