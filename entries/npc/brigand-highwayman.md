### Description

Highwaymen are notorious outlaws or flamboyant criminals who flaunt the law, prey upon innocent
travellers, and revel in the discomfiture their predations have upon the local constabulary.
Highwaymen love deception and trickery and elevate taunting to an art form. For highwaymen, the
humiliation of an enemy can be more important than a successful heist. Highwaymen can be used as
expert spies or scouts, or even as agile gladiators.

Two highwaymen might be the bodyguards of a guildmaster (CR 11), while a trio of highwaymen may
serve as the henchmen of a bandit lord or bounty hunter (CR 12). A lone highwayman may lead a gang
of a dozen bandits (CR 8) or travel with a minstrel and a pair of cavalry for his gang (CR 9).
