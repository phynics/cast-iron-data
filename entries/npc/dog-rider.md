**Dog Rider**

**Challenge rating:** 3

**XP** 800\
Halfling [barbarian](ref/class/barbarian) 4\
N Small humanoid (halfling)\
**Init** +3; **Senses** Perception +10

### Defense

**AC** 18, touch 12, flat-footed 15 (+5 armor, +3 Dex, -2 rage, +1 shield, +1 size)\
**hp** 43 (4d12+12)\
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +5, **Will** +5; +3 vs. spells and spell-like or supernatural abilities, +2 vs.
fear\
**Defensive Abilities** [trap sense](ref/ability/trap-sense) +1,
[uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge)

### Offense

**Speed** 30 ft.\
**Melee** lance +9 (1d6+4/×3)\
**Ranged** +1 halfling sling staff +10 (1d6+5/×3)\
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with lance)\
**Special Attacks** rage (11 rounds/day), rage powers (superstition +3, surprise accuracy +2)

### Tactics

**Before Combat** The barbarian applies oil of magic weapon.\
**During Combat** The barbarian keeps his distance, pelting enemies with bullets. If ranged attacks
fail, he charges with his lance.\
**Base Statistics** When not _raging_ and without magic weapon, the barbarian's statistics are
**AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 17; **hp** 35; **Fort** +6, **Will** +3; **Melee** lance +7
(1d6+2/×3); **Ranged** mwk halfling sling staff +10 (1d6+2/×3); **Str** 14, **Con** 13; **CMB** +5;
**Skills** Climb +9, Swim +5.

### Statistics

**Str** 18, **Dex** 16, **Con** 17, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 10\
**Base Atk** +4; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 18\
**Feats** [Deadly Aim](ref/feat/deadly-aim), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (halfling sling
staff)\
**Skills** Acrobatics +11, Handle Animal +6, Perception +10, Ride +9, Stealth +10, Survival +5\
**SQ** fast movement\
**Combat Gear** oils of [magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon) (2), potions of
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds) (2); **Other Gear** +1
[chain shirt](ref/eq-armor/chain-shirt), masterwork [buckler](ref/eq-armor/buckler),
[lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance), masterwork [halfling sling staff](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-sling-staff)
with 20 bullets, [riding dog](ref/eq-misc/dog-riding) with
[riding saddle](ref/eq-misc/saddle-riding) and [leather](ref/eq-armor/leather)
[barding](ref/eq-armor/barding), 55 gp

### Description

Small and adept at skirmish maneuvers, a dog rider is a deadly and cautious warrior.
