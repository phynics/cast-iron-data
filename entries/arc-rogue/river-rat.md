**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 76, People of the River pg 21\
What highway robbers are to roads, river rats are to waterways. Skilled at hiding amid reeds and
roots, these rogues strike the unwary from the shallows as others would from the shadows. Despite
their strong association with banditry, river rats make excellent guides and hunters.

**Gained class skills:** kna

**Lost class skills:** kd

**Swamper (Ex)**

**Ability:** A river rat ignores [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) caused by light
undergrowth and shallow bogs, and it costs her only 2 squares of movement to enter a square of
[deep bog](ref/rule/marsh-terrain) or [heavy undergrowth](ref/rule/forest-terrain), rather than 4
squares of movement.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A river rat gains a bonus equal to half her rogue level on Swim checks (minimum +1).
She takes no penalty on Acrobatics or Stealth checks for being in bogs and undergrowth.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Special:** All of these abilities apply only when she is wearing light or no armor and carrying no
more than a light load.

**Rat's Resilience (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A river rat is inured to dangers presented by disease-carrying parasites, poisonous
fish, and other toxic river denizens.

**Passive:** A river rat gains a bonus on saving throws against disease and poison effects. This
bonus is equal to one-third of her rogue level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Camouflage, hold breath, rogue crawl, stand up,
strong stroke, survivalist

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Hide in plain sight, opportunist, skill
mastery, stealthy sniper
