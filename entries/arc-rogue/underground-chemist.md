Underground chemists are part of the rotting, fetid underbelly of the alchemical world. While
underground chemists can't hold a candle to dedicated alchemists, they're tricky and dangerous with
alchemical substances and potions.

**Chemical Weapons (Ex)**

**Ability:** An underground chemist is able to retrieve an alchemical item as if
[drawing a weapon](ref/rule/draw-or-sheathe-a-weapon).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** She adds her Intelligence modifier to damage dealt with
[splash weapons](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon), including any splash damage. She adds 1/2 her level
to Craft (alchemy) checks.

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Precise Splash Weapons (Ex)**

**Ability:** An underground chemist can deal sneak attack damage with splash weapons. The attack
must be her first attack that round, qualify for dealing [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack)
damage (such as against a [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) target), and be directed at a creature
rather than a square.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 4th level

**Discovery (Su)**

**Ability:** An underground chemist can select one of the following alchemist discoveries in place
of a rogue talent: [concentrate poison](ref/discovery/concentrate-poison),
[dilution](ref/discovery/dilution), [enhance potion](ref/discovery/enhance-potion),
[extend potion](ref/discovery/extend-potion), [mummification](ref/discovery/mummification),
[nauseating flesh](ref/discovery/nauseating-flesh),
[poison conversion](ref/discovery/poison-conversion),
[preserve organs](ref/discovery/preserve-organs),
[spontaneous healing](ref/discovery/spontaneous-healing), or
[sticky poison](ref/discovery/sticky-poison). She uses her rogue level as her alchemist level for
determining the effects of her discoveries and whether she is able to select one.

**Alters:** Advanced talents

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Black market connections, fast fingers, lasting
poison, sniper's eye, swift poison

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Deadly cocktail, thoughtful reexamining
