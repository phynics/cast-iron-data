**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 41\
Guerrillas fight against oppressive leadership and governments - often under cover of night -
including Chelish colonization in Sargava and the power structures in Cheliax proper, Nidal, and
Razmiran. Any rogue (including the unchained rogue) can select the guerrilla archetype.

**Skilled Liar (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a guerrilla uses Bluff to attempt to deceive someone, she gains a bonus on the
opposed roll equal to 1/2 her rogue level (minimum +1). A guerrilla can still use Disable Device to
disarm magic traps.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Cover of Night (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A guerrilla learns to use darkness to her advantage.

**Fullround:** She can create mundane disguises in dim light or darkness (at a faster rate than the
normal 1d3 &times& 10 minutes).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** She gains a +5 bonus on Disguise, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth checks while she is in
areas of [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darkness. Additionally, while in dim light or
darkness, if a guerrilla would have [concealment](ref/rule/concealment), she instead has total
concealment.

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Secret Messenger (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A guerrilla gains a bonus on Bluff checks to convey secret messages and on Sense Motive
checks to discern secret messages. These bonuses are equal to one-third of her rogue level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Guerrilla Sniping (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a guerrilla is in an area of dim light or darkness, she takes only a -10 penalty
on her Stealth check to maintain her obscured location while sniping.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Uncanny Dodge (Ex)**

**Ability:** A guerrilla gains [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge).

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Camouflage, fast stealth, powerful sneak, rogue
crawl, stand up, surprise attack

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Deadly sneak, master of disguise,
opportunist, stealthy sniper
