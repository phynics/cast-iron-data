A charlatan is a master of lies and deception. Whether creating simple cons or elaborately woven
hoaxes capable of swaying the masses and those in positions of power, a charlatan is often a
purveyor of snake-oil, forgeries, and rumormongering.

**Natural Born Liar (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a charlatan successfully deceives a creature with a Bluff, that creature takes a
-2 penalty on the charlatan's Bluff checks for the next 24 hours. This ability does not stack with
itself.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Grand Hoax (Ex)**

**Ability:** She gains the [rumormonger](ref/talent/rumormonger) advanced talent, even though she is
not yet 10th level.

**Flavor:** The charlatan begins to master the art of the _grand hoax_ and learns to create
deceptions designed to bestow harm upon her foes.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Black market connections, coax information,
convincing lie, honeyed words, underhanded

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Hard minded, skill mastery, unwitting
ally
