The scroll scoundrel is a rogue archetype that relies on cunning and quick wits to stay alive. He
excels in exploiting overconfidence and predictability, using knowledge he's gained from his
extensive research and field experience combined with hearsay and recent observations to react to
problems as soon as they arise with swift but unnaturally accurate guesswork. A scroll scoundrel is
often called upon by the Pathfinders to undertake missions where killing a foe isn't nearly as
important as humiliating them in public duels or similar conflicts. A scroll scoundrel has the
following class features.

**Fast Talker (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A scroll scoundrel's penalty on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks that are unlikely,
farfetched, or impossible to achieve is reduced by 5. When he makes a
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check to adjust a creature's starting attitude, he gains a +3
competence bonus on the skill check.

**Replaces:** 2nd-level rogue talent

**Adaptive Learning (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A scroll scoundrel gains a bonus on saving throws against abilities and spells he has
already successfully saved against within the past minute (even if the spells or abilities in
question were used by different creatures). This bonus is equal to one-third of his rogue level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Pay it Back (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** This bonus increases to +2.

**At 4th Level:** A scroll scoundrel receives a +1 bonus on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls
against foes who made melee attacks against him in the previous round.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Elusive Gambit (Ex)**

**Ability:** If a scroll scoundrel provokes an attack of opportunity by moving past an opponent and
that opponent then misses with the attack, that foe is considered to be
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against the scroll scoundrel's next melee attack, provided that
melee attack comes within 1 round of the missed attack of opportunity.

**Flavor:** A scroll scoundrel's movement can leave a foe off-balance.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Spot Weakness (Ex)**

**At 15th Level:** He ignores the first 15 points of DR or [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

**At 20th Level:** He ignores the first 20 points of DR or [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** 10th-level rogue talent

**Special:** If the attack deals any sneak attack damage, he ignores all of the foe's
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) or [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) for that attack. He
cannot use this ability on foes that are immune to critical hits.

**Swift:** A scroll scoundrel can discover a weakness in his enemy's defenses. His next successful
hit in the same round ignores 5 points of the foe's [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) or
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness).
