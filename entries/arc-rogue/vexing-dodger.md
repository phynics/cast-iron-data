Vexing dodgers take advantage of their smaller size to outmaneuver larger foes such as giants.
Vexing dodgers have the following class features.

**Limb-Climber (Ex)**

**Ability:** When adjacent to or in the space of a corporeal creature at least one size category
larger than herself, a vexing dodger can climb that creature's body with a successful
[Climb](ref/skill/climb) check against a DC equal to the target creature's CMD. Although the vexing
dodger is holding on to the creature, this action isn't a grapple; it doesn't provoke attacks of
opportunity from the creature, and neither the vexing dodger nor the creature she climbs gains the
grappled condition.

**Passive:** While the vexing dodger is on the climbed creature, the creature takes a penalty on
attack rolls against the vexing dodger equal to the number of sneak attack dice the dodger
possesses.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Improved Dirty Trick (Ex)**

**Ability:** A vexing dodger gains [Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick) as a bonus
feat, even if she doesn't meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 2nd level

**Underfoot Agility (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A vexing dodger gains a morale bonus on Acrobatics, Climb, and Escape Artist attempts
against creatures at least one size category larger than herself. This bonus is equal to one-third
of her rogue level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Underfoot Trickster (Ex)**

**Ability:** A vexing dodger can move through the space of a creature two size categories larger
than herself without needing to attempt an Acrobatics check. She would still need to succeed at
Acrobatics checks to avoid attacks of opportunity provoked by leaving a threatened space; this
ability covers only the ability to move.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Swift:** Additionally, whenever the vexing dodger hits a larger foe with a melee sneak attack, she
can choose to forgo the sneak attack bonus damage and instead attempt make a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver.

**Distracting Climber (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a vexing dodger attempts a dirty trick maneuver against a creature she's climbing,
she gets a bonus on the combat maneuver check equal to the number of sneak attack dice she has. If
she chooses to [sicken](ref/misc/sickened) the target with her dirty trick and succeeds at her
combat maneuver check, the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the vexing
dodger's level + her Intelligence modifier) or be [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Rogue Talents (Ex)**

**Ability:** A vexing dodger can choose [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge) as a rogue talent
starting at 4th level, and she can choose to gain
[improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) when she gains access to advanced rogue
talents.

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** befuddling strike, confounding blades, peerless
maneuver, positioning attack, slow reactions, wall scramble
