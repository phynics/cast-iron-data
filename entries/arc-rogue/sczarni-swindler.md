If life is a game, the Sczarni play to win. When skill and luck aren't enough, cheating is often the
best option.

**Let Fate Decide (Ex)**

**Ability:** The Sczarni swindler can declare two different actions that rely on different types of
rolls or checks, such as attacking a creature (an attack roll) or sneaking past the same creature (a
Stealth check). As a **standard action**, the swindler then uses a random method to choose one of
the declared activities, such as flipping a coin, rolling a die, or drawing a harrow card. The
specific method doesn't matter as long as there is an equal chance of either activity being chosen.
If the swindler then performs the chosen activity within the next round, she gains a luck bonus on
the roll type required for that activity - attack rolls with a specific weapon, a specific skill
check, a specific ability check, or a specific saving throw - equal to half her rogue level (minimum
+1) for 1 minute.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Special:** If the swindler performs any other action (whether declared or not) in the round after
using this ability, she becomes [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 minute instead.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Quicker Than the Eye (Ex)**

**Ability:** When the Sczarni swindler uses Sleight of Hand, creatures take a penalty on their
Perception checks equal to half the swindler's class level to notice the attempt. The swindler also
reduces the normal -20 penalty by an amount equal to her class level when attempting a
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check as a move action instead of as a standard action.
Lastly, the swindler can draw hidden weapons or objects from her person as a **move action** instead
of the usual standard action.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Poker Face (Ex)**

**Ability:** The Sczarni swindler can also attempt to feint against non-humanoid targets without
penalty, though she still takes a -8 penalty on feint attempts against creatures with animal
intelligence and she cannot feint against mindless creatures.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** The Sczarni swindler gains a bonus on Bluff, Profession (gambler), and Sense Motive
checks equal to [one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her rogue level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**No Fool (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** The Sczarni swindler gains a bonus on Will saving throws equal to one-fourth of her
rogue level.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Cheat Fate (Ex)**

**At 14th Level:** The swindler can use this ability twice per day.

**At 20th Level:** She can use this ability three times per day.

**At 8th Level:** Once per day, the Sczarni swindler can reroll any one d20 roll she has just made
before the GM has revealed the result. She must take the result of the second roll, even if it is
worse.

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Canny observer, fast fingers, hard to fool,
honeyed words, major magic, minor magic

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Master of disguise, skill mastery,
slippery mind
