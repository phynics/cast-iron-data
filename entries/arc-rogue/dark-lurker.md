**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 26\
Dark lurkers use darkness as an ally. They are widely feared in many major metropolises, spoken of
as "living shadows" by guards of Magnimar who patrol under the Irespan and called "the hungry night"
by citizens of Absalom who mistake them for fetchling assassins.

**Blades from the Shadows (Ex)**

**At 2nd Level:** A dark lurker gains [Shadow Strike](ref/feat/shadow-strike) as a bonus feat. (A
character using the unchained rogue class instead gains the ability to make attacks of opportunity
against foes with [cover](ref/rule/cover), though not foes with improved cover or total cover).

**At 6th Level:** The dark lurker can deal precision damage to targets with
[total concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (though any miss chance applies normally, and may prevent
an attack from hitting in the first place).

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Special:** The dark lurker can take [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) as a rogue talent.

**Blind-Fight (Ex)**

**Ability:** A dark lurker gains [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 2nd level

**Improved Blind-Fight (Ex)**

**At 8th Level:** A dark lurker gains [Improved Blind-Fight](ref/feat/improved-blind-fight) as a
bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 8th level

**Greater Blind-Fight (Ex)**

**At 14th Level:** A dark lurker gains [Greater Blind-Fight](ref/feat/greater-blind-fight) as a
bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 14th level

**Instinctual Sense (Ex)**

**At 20th Level:** A dark lurker gains [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) with a range of 30 feet.

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 20th level

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Befuddling strike, fast getaway, fast stealth,
sniper's eye, surprise attack

**This advanced rogue talent complements this archetype:** [Opportunist](ref/talent/opportunist).
