Notorious for their stealth, imagination, and elaborately planned thefts and capers, heisters
specialize in breaking into secure locations, stealing items of value, and escaping undetected.
Heisters often work as members of or independent operatives contracted to criminal organizations,
crime families, or wealthy individuals inclined to unlawful activity.

**Rum Dubber (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** Whenever a heister attempts to open a lock without a set of
[thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools), the DC increases by 2, instead of 10.

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 2nd level

**Ferret's Grace (Ex)**

**Ability:** A heister gains [Stealthy](ref/feat/stealthy) as a bonus feat. Additionally, a heister
counts as a creature one size category smaller than she actually is when determining whether or not
she is [squeezing](ref/misc/squeezing) into an enclosed space. Finally, when a heister uses the
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) skill to move through a tight space where her head fits but
her shoulders do not, the DC is 20, instead of 30.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge, rogue talent gained at 4th level

**Unseen (Ex)**

**Ability:** A heister masters hiding in underground and urban terrain. She can use the Stealth
skill to hide in underground and urban terrain, even while being observed. She also gains the
[terrain mastery](ref/talent/terrain-mastery) rogue talent twice, once each for underground and
urban terrain.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge, rogue talent gained at 8th level

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Certainty\|unchained rogue only, expert leaper,
fast getaway, fast picks, fast stealth, getaway artist, ledge walker, quick disable

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Cutting edge\|unchained rogue only, fast
tumble, getaway master, hidden mind, skill mastery
