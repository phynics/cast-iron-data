**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 23\
Deep in darkness, the snare setter constructs his deadly traps and brutal mechanisms to confound and
destroy any who try to invade his domain.

**Prerequisites:** Kobold

**Trapper (Ex)**

**Ability:** A snare setter gains [Learn Ranger Trap](ref/feat/learn-ranger-trap) as a bonus feat.
In addition to the trap granted by this feat, he learns how to construct the
[tripwire](ref/rangertrap/tripwire) trap for free. For the purposes of this feat, he's considered a
ranger of his snare setter level, and uses his Intelligence in place of Wisdom to determine uses per
day and the DC of his ranger traps.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Sneak attack (but see below)

**Special:** Whenever the snare setter could learn a new rogue trick, he may instead learn a new
ranger trap.

**Trapsmithing (Ex)**

**Ability:** A snare setter gains a bonus on Perception skill checks to detect traps and on Craft
(traps) checks equal to 1/2 his snare setter level. He can use Craft (traps) in place of Disable
Device to disarm traps, including magical traps.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Deadly Traps (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** The snare setter's traps deal an additional 1d6 points of damage on the first round
they're activated. This damage is of the same type the trap normally deals. Traps that don't deal
damage are unaffected by this ability.

At 7th level: This additional damage 2d6.

At 11th level: This additional damage 3d6.

At 15th level: This additional damage 4d6.

At 19th level: This additional damage 5d6.

**Sneak Attack (Su)**

**Ability:** The snare setter gains [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +1d6. This functions as
the rogue ability of the same name.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

At 9th level: The sneak attack damage +2d6.

At 13th level: The sneak attack damage +3d6.

At 17th level: The sneak attack damage +4d6.

**Master Snare Setter (Ex)**

**Ability:** The snare setter can attach one of a rogue's _master strike_ effects to a trap he
creates. When the trap is triggered, the subject makes an additional Fortitude save at the same DC
as the trap to avoid this effect. A snare setter can have only one trap affected by this ability
active at any time. He may remove this ability from a trap at any range as a **free action**.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Master strike

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Camouflage, cunning trigger, fast stealth,
sniper's eye
