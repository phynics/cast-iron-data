Pushed into a life of crime by the society around them, half-orcs gravitate toward criminal
activities that suit them best. Half-orc rogues leave subtle tactics and finesse to halflings and
elves, and rely on brute strength and thuggery when they go about making mischief. Skulking slayers
have turned the use of raw strength and surprise into an art form. A skulking slayer has the
following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Half-Orc

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** The skulking slayer gains proficiency with [greatclubs](ref/eq-weapon/greatclub) and
[whips](ref/eq-weapon/whip), but loses proficiency with rapiers and hand crossbows.

**Lost class skills:** dd, ling, soh

**Skill Ranks per Level**

**Info:** She gains 6 + Int modifier skill points each level.

**Pass for Human (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** When trying to conceal her half-orc heritage, a skulking slayer gains a bonus on
Disguise checks equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her level. When using disguise to
appear as a specific individual, skulking stalkers ignore the normal -2 penalty to appear as another
race.

**Underhanded Maneuvers (Ex)**

**Ability:** When she could normally make a sneak attack, a slayer may instead make a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) or [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver with a bonus on her
roll. This bonus is equal to her number of sneak attack dice for a dirty trick combat maneuver, or
1-1/2 &times& her number of sneak attack dice for a steal combat maneuver.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Bonus Feats**

**At 10th Level:** She can select the
[Improved Surprise Follow-Through](ref/feat/improved-surprise-follow-through) feat (if she already
has Surprise Follow-Through) in place of an advanced rogue talent.

**At 2nd Level:** A skulking slayer can select the
[Surprise Follow-Through](ref/feat/surprise-follow-through) feat in place of a rogue talent.

**Bold Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a skulking slayer [charges](ref/rule/charge) and makes a sneak attack with a
two-handed weapon, she rolls d8s instead of d6s for her sneak attack damage.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense +1 and +4

**Shifty (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A skulking slayer gains a bonus on Bluff checks to feint equal to half her level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense +2

**Unexpected Charge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense +3

**Swift:** A skulking slayer can make a [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check to feint before a charge.

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Combat swipe, combat trick, powerful sneak,
surprise attack, terrain mastery

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Crippling strike, deadly sneak,
unwitting ally
