An absolute master of disguise, a chameleon effortlessly blends into any environment. Whether
disappearing into crowded city streets, vanishing into desert sands, or slipping into the darkness
of subterranean tunnels, the chameleon relies upon her ability to become part of her surroundings.

**Misdirection (Ex)**

**Ability:** Every day she gains a pool of stealth points equal to her ranks in Bluff. These points
refresh at the start of each day. Before making a Stealth check, she can choose to put stealth
points into the roll, gaining a bonus on Stealth checks equal to the number of stealth points she
puts into the roll.

**Flavor:** A chameleon begins her career knowing that the secret to disappearing lies in deceiving
the senses of her observers.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Special:** If she gains a bonus on Bluff checks because of a feat (such as
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) \[Bluff\]), she adds a number of points to her stealth pool
equal to the bonus the feat grants.

**Effortless Sneak (Sp)**

**Ability:** The chameleon chooses a single terrain from the ranger's
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) class feature. While she is within that terrain, she
can take 10 on any Stealth check she can make within that terrain.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Repeat:** She chooses a new type of terrain and gains this ability with the newly picked terrain.,
6, 3

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Camouflage, fast stealth, quick disguise, terrain
mastery

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Hide in plain sight, master of disguise
