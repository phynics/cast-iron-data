Consiglieres act as trusted mediators and administrators for crime families and various criminal
organizations, and many serve as the right hand to the leaders of such groups. Other consiglieres
prefer to work as independent operators and sell their advice and expertise to the highest bidder.
Though some consiglieres insulate themselves from violence and criminal activities, many enjoy
getting their hands dirty leading strike teams or gangs of hardened thugs.

**Gained class skills:** kno

**Lost class skills:** kd

**Convincing Attitude (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** A consigliere gains [Persuasive](ref/feat/persuasive) as a bonus feat.

**At 2nd Level:** He gains the [charmer](ref/talent/charmer) rogue talent (or the
[certainty](ref/talent/certainty) \[Diplomacy\] rogue talent if he is an unchained rogue).

**At 3rd Level:** If a consigliere attempts a Diplomacy check and fails to influence a creature's
attitude by 5 or more, the creature's attitude remains unchanged instead of decreasing by one step.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding, evasion

**Combat Advisor (Ex)**

**At 4th Level:** Whenever a consigliere misses with a melee attack against an opponent, he can
designate an ally within 30 feet of the target to receive a +1 insight bonus on her next attack roll
against that opponent before the consigliere's next turn.

**At 8th Level:** The insight bonus increases to +2.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge, improved uncanny dodge

**Bonus Feats (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** The consigliere can choose to gain teamwork feats instead of rogue talents as
many times as she chooses.

**At 4th Level:** A consigliere can choose to gain a single teamwork feat instead of a single rogue
talent. (This substitution can only be used once, at either 4th, 6th, or 8th level.)

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Field Boss (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** Rogue talent normally gained at 10th level

**Standard:** A consigliere can grant a teamwork feat to all allies within 30 feet who can see and
hear her. This teamwork feat must be one the consigliere knows. Allies retain the use of this feat
for a number of rounds equal to 3 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her rogue level.
Allies do not need to meet the prerequisites of this teamwork feat.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 rogue levels beyond 10th

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** assault leader, black market connections, shades
of gray, coax information, honeyed words, certainty\|Bluff; unchained rogue only

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Hard minded, opportunist, redirect
attack, rumormonger, skill mastery, slippery mind, unwitting ally
