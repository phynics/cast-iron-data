Some combatants specialize in the study of certain weapons, but those without formal training often
learn to survive a fight by making brilliant use of whatever is lying around.

**Weapon Proficiency**

**Info:** The makeshift scrapper is proficient with only simple weapons.

**Improvised Weapons (Ex)**

**Ability:** A makeshift scrapper gains [Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard) and
[Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything) as bonus feats.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Supernatural Improvisation (Su)**

**Flavor:** A makeshift scrapper gains the ability to enhance an item, making it a more effective
weapon or changing the way it can be used.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**Special:** The makeshift scrapper can change the enhancement each time she activates the ability.

**Swift:** She can alter a single [improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) to either grant
it a +1 enhancement bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls or use it to gain a +1 shield bonus to
her AC.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per rogue level

At 7th level: The bonus +2.

At 11th level: The bonus +3.

At 15th level: The bonus +4.

At 19th level: The bonus +5.

**Improvised Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Ability:** The makeshift scrapper gains
[Improvised Weapon Mastery](ref/feat/improvised-weapon-mastery) as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** Rogue talent gained at 12th level

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** [Expert leaper](ref/talent/expert-leaper),
[fast getaway](ref/talent/fast-getaway), [ki pool](ref/talent/ki-pool),
[offensive defense](ref/talent/offensive-defense), [ninja trick](ref/talent/ninja-trick)
\[[acrobatic master](ref/ninjatrick/acrobatic-master)\],
[peerless maneuver](ref/talent/peerless-maneuver), [surprise attack](ref/talent/surprise-attack)

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Another day, defensive roll, getaway
master, opportunist
