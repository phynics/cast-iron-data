**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 13\
A Numerian scavenger lives for the discovery of new technological wonders scraped from the depths of
the strange ruins, metal caverns, and outlandish junkyards of Numeria. She is well-versed in all the
ways that technology can be used to protect sites, as well as the ways in which it can malfunction.

**Technic Training (Ex)**

**Ability:** She gains [Technologist](ref/feat/technologist) as a bonus feat.

**Passive:** A Numerian scavenger adds 1/2 her rogue level to Perception skill checks to locate
mechanical or high-tech traps and to Disable Device skill checks against these traps (minimum +1).

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Special:** She cannot use Disable Device to disarm magical traps.

**Lucky Glitch (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a Numerian scavenger triggers a [glitch](ref/rule/timeworn-technology) when
using a timeworn technological item, she rolls twice and can choose which of the two glitch effects
actually occurs. A Numerian scavenger always adds her level to rolls to determine what kind of
glitch occurs, and treats rolls of over 100 as 100.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Robot Slayer (Ex)**

**Ability:** When she deals sneak attack damage to a robot, she ignores all
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) that robot possesses.

**Flavor:** A Numerian scavenger knows just where to strike robots to incapacitate and disable them.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge
