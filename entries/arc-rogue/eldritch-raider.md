An eldritch raider is a rogue who seeks to unravel the mysteries of the destruction of the gillmen's
homeland. They explore old ruins that date back to the days of the old human empire and track down
relics and lore from its glory days. An eldritch raider has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Gillman

**Gained class skills:** ka, kh, spc

**Lost class skills:** dis, perf, soh

**Skill Ranks per Level**

**Info:** The eldritch raider gains only 6 + Int modifier skill points per level.

**Detect Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** An eldritch raider gains the ability to use [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) at
will. The eldritch raider treats her rogue level as her caster level for this ability.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Rogue talent rogues gained at 2nd level

**Special:** This ability counts as the [minor magic](ref/talent/minor-magic) rogue talent for
purposes of qualifying for other rogue talents.

**Eldritch Intuition (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** An eldritch raider gains an intuitive sense that allows her to more easily activate
sorcerer and wizard spell completion and spell trigger items. She gains a bonus on Use Magic Device
checks for this purpose. This bonus is equal to one-third of her rogue level.

**Replaces:** Trap sense

**New Talents**

**Info:** An eldritch raider has access to the following new advanced talents when selecting rogue
advanced talents.

**Minor Eldritch Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** The eldritch raider gains the ability to cast a 2nd-level spell from the
sorcerer/spells\_ spell list two times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this ability is equal to
her rogue level. The save DC for this spell is 12 + her Intelligence modifier.

**Prereq:** _Minor magic_ and [major magic](ref/talent/major-magic) rogue talents; Intelligence 12

**Major Eldritch Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** The eldritch raider gains the ability to cast a 3rd-level spell from the
sorcerer/wizard spell list two times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this ability is equal to
her rogue level. The save DC for this spell is 13 + her Intelligence modifier.

**Prereq:** Minor Eldritch Magic; Intelligence 13

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:**
[Black market connections](ref/talent/black-market-connections),
[esoteric scholar](ref/talent/esoteric-scholar), [fast picks](ref/talent/fast-picks), major magic,
minor magic, [ninja trick](ref/talent/ninja-trick)
\[[slow metabolism](ref/ninjatrick/slow-metabolism), [wall climber](ref/ninjatrick/wall-climber)\],
[quick disable](ref/talent/quick-disable), [trap spotter](ref/talent/trap-spotter)

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Dispelling attack, familiar, hard
minded, improved evasion, slippery mind, thoughtful reexamining
