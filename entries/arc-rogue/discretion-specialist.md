Often brought in to handle messy situations, discretion specialists deal with bodies, inconvenient
witnesses, and other loose ends. Many organizations employ them to cover up activities that might be
unpopular - should they become known to the general populace.

This archetype is appropriate for rogues and unchained rogues.

**Fast Talker (Ex)**

**Passive:** A discretion specialist adds half her rogue level (minimum +1) as a bonus on Bluff,
Diplomacy, and Intimidate checks.

**Replaces:** Trapfinding

**Obfuscation (Su)**

**Flavor:** A discretion specialist is highly skilled at convincing others not to trust their own
senses.

**Fullround:** The discretion specialist can convince one living creature within 30 feet that up to
5 minutes of its memories from the past hour are unreliable. If the target fails a Will saving throw
(DC = 10 + half the discretion specialist's rogue level + her Intelligence modifier), the target is
convinced that those memories didn't happen or that it's confusing the memories with a dream (or is
otherwise hazy as to the memories' reality). Each hour after the discretion specialist uses this
ability, the target can attempt a new Will saving throw to realize that its relevant memories have
been tampered with, although not necessarily that the discretion specialist was the originator of
the tampering.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Trap sense (for a core rogue), danger sense (for an unchained rogue)

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 rogue levels

At 6th level: The discretion specialist can obfuscate memories from up to 2 hours in the past.

At 9th level: The discretion specialist can obfuscate memories from up to 3 hours in the past.

At 12th level: The discretion specialist can obfuscate memories from up to 4 hours in the past.

At 15th level: The discretion specialist can obfuscate memories from up to 5 hours in the past.

At 18th level: The discretion specialist can obfuscate memories from up to 6 hours in the past.

**Evidence Disposal (Sp)**

**Flavor:** A discretion specialist can cover up a corpse's cause of a death - or dispose of it
entirely.

**At 12th Level:** She can spend two uses of this ability to disintegrate a corpse entirely as a
**full-round action**, leaving behind only a trace of fine dust (a disintegrated creature's
equipment is unaffected). The target cannot be an undead creature. A corpse disintegrated this way
cannot be brought back to life except by [resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection) or more powerful
magic, including [miracle](ref/spell/miracle) or [wish](ref/spell/wish).

**At 4th Level:** The discretion specialist gains the ability to cast
[dress corpse](ref/spell/dress-corpse) as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities),
using her rogue level as her caster level. She can use this ability a number of times per day equal
to 1 + [one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her rogue level.

**Replaces:** Rogue talents gained at 4th and 12th levels

**No Loose Ends (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The discretion specialist can prevent opponents from escaping.

**At 4th Level:** Opponents damaged by the discretion specialist's sneak attack are unable to take
the [withdraw](ref/rule/withdraw) action for 1 round, though they can still move as normal.

**At 8th Level:** A creature damaged by the discretion specialist's sneak attack is hampered in
casting spells of the teleportation subschool (such as [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door))
as well as using methods of magical transport such as [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk). A target
attempting to cast such a spell before the end of the discretion specialist's next turn must succeed
at a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (in addition to any concentration checks required
for casting while threatened or other circumstances) or lose the spell. The DC of this check is
equal to 10 + half the discretion specialist's rogue level + her Intelligence modifier.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge, improved uncanny dodge

**These rogue talents complement this archetype:** Camouflage, charmer, convincing lie, honeyed
words, quick disguise

**These advanced rogue talents complement this archetype:** Hide in plain sight, master of disguise
