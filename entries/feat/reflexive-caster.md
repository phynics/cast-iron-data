You are ready to defend yourself with a spell at a moment's notice.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes), caster level 5th.\
**Benefit:** If you fail a Perception check that results in you being unable to act in a surprise
round, you still roll initiative normally. You can act on your initiative count in the surprise
round, but can only take a standard action to cast an abjuration spell that targets only yourself.
