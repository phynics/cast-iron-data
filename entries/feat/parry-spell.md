You can throw an enemy spell back at its caster.\
**Prerequisites:** Spellcraft 15 ranks, [Improved Counterspell](ref/feat/improved-counterspell).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully [counter](ref/rule/counterspells) a spell, it returns back to
its caster. This works exactly like the [spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning) spell.
