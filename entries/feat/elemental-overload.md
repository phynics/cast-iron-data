**Sources:** Psychic Anthology pg 24

As you become suffused with power, you become more and more like an elemental.\
**Prerequisites:** Elemental overflow class feature, [kineticist](ref/class/kineticist) level 15th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you have accepted at least 4 points of burn, the chance to ignore the effects
of a critical hit or sneak attack increases by an additional 10%. This chance increases by an
additional 5% for every 2 points of burn you accept after that. This chance can't exceed 100%.\
**Normal:** Whenever you have accepted at least 3 points of burn, your chance to ignore the effects
of a critical hit or sneak attack equals 5% × your current number of points of burn.
