**Sources:** Rival Guide pg 11

With swift feet and deadly intent, you finish off your target before anyone sees your blade.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility),
[Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack), base attack bonus +13.\
**Benefit:** You can move up to half your speed instead of taking a 5-foot step when taking a
full-round action to perform a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless). Performing a _coup de grace_ does
not provoke attacks of opportunity.

You can use this feat only when wielding a light or one-handed weapon.
