You have an extra wellspring of kundalini energy that you can use to open your chakras.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to awaken the root chakra.\
**Benefit:** You gain a pool that holds 3 points of serpentfire ki, which you can use only to open
or maintain [chakras](ref/rule/activating-chakras). These serpent-fire ki points are replenished at
the same time you replenish the points in your regular ki pool.
