## Duelist of the Shrouded Lake (Combat)

You have embraced the Shrouded Lake style of Aldori swordplay, known for its circuitous footwork.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aldori Dueling Disciple](ref/feat/aldori-dueling-disciple),
[Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (Aldori dueling sword),
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (Aldori dueling
sword), base attack bonus +5.\
**Benefit:** When attempting to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or [reposition](ref/rule/reposition)
a foe, you gain a +1 insight bonus on the combat maneuver check and a +4 dodge bonus to your AC
against attacks of opportunity you provoke. You also gain a +1 insight bonus to your CMD against
bull rush and reposition attempts. When you attempt a [dueling dodge](ref/rule/dueling-dodge) during
a duel, your bonus to AC increases to +6. To gain these benefits, you must be wielding only an
[Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword) (not using a shield, an off-hand weapon,
armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons).
