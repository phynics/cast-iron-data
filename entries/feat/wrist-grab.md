## Wrist Grab (Combat)

When an enemy overextends to attack you, you quickly catch its arm and knock its weapon loose.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes), [Improved Disarm](ref/feat/improved-disarm),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** When an opponent's attack misses you by 5 or more, you can attempt a
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) combat maneuver check against that foe as an immediate action.
