**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 77

Others are often dumfounded by your luck.\
**Prerequisites:** [Defiant Luck](ref/feat/defiant-luck), [human](ref/race/human).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, as a free action before a roll is made, you gain a +8 bonus on any single
d20 roll. You can also use this ability after the roll is made, but if you do, this bonus is reduced
to +4.
