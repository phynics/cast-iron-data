You have trained yourself to move and fight in strong winds, allowing you to use their elemental
power to boost your own speed.\
**Prerequisites:** Acrobatics or Fly 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When flying within strong winds, you gain a +15 foot bonus to your fly speed. For every
[wind](ref/rule/winds) strength category above strong, you gain an additional +15 foot bonus to your
fly speed (for a total of +30 feet in severe winds, +45 feet in a windstorm, and +60 feet in
tornadoes). This feat does not give you any bonuses on Fly or Strength checks to determine if you
are potentially checked or blown away.
