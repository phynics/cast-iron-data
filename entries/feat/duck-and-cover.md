## Duck and Cover (Teamwork)

Your allies assist you in avoiding certain attacks.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, and both of you are
required to make a Reflex saving throw against a spell or effect, you may take the result of your
die roll or that of your ally (your modifiers still apply to the roll, regardless of which result
you take). If you take your ally's result, you are knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone) (or
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) on your next turn, if you are already prone or cannot be knocked
prone). In addition, you receive a +2 cover bonus to your AC against ranged attacks as long as your
ally is wielding a shield.
