Your innate grace allows you to get out of the stickiest situations.\
**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 13, [catfolk](ref/race/catfolk).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus to your CMD against [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush),
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun), [repositioning](ref/rule/reposition), and
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuvers.
