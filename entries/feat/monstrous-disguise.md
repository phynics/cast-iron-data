You can disguise yourself as a monstrous humanoid.\
**Prerequisites:** Disguise 5 ranks, Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can use the Disguise skill to emulate creatures of the monstrous humanoid type, at
a -5 penalty. Creating a monstrous humanoid disguise takes 1d3 hours.
