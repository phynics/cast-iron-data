**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 217, Seekers of Secrets pg 16, Animal Archive pg 18

Your bond with your animal companion or familiar is unusually close.\
**Prerequisites:** Animal companion class feature or familiar class feature.\
**Benefit:** The abilities of your [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) or
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) are calculated as though your class were 4 levels higher, to a
maximum effective druid level equal to your character level. If you have more than one animal
companion or familiar, choose one to receive this benefit. If you dismiss or lose an animal
companion or familiar that has received this benefit, you can apply this feat to the replacement
creature.\
**Special:** You can select this feat more than once. The effects do not stack. Each time you take
the feat, it applies to a different animal companion or familiar.
