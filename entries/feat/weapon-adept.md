## Weapon Adept (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 11

You are skilled at using customized weaponry.\
**Benefit:** Choose a weapon modification (such as [jagged hooks](ref/eq-weaponenh/jagged-hooks)).
You treat weapons with that modification as being of their normal category (simple, martial, or
exotic).\
**Normal:** A modified weapon is treated as one category more difficult to wield.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times, choosing a different weapon modification each
time.
