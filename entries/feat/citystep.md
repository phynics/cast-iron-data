You are as at home in the cities of mankind as your elven ancestors are within the forests.\
**Prerequisites:** Stealth 1 rank, [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** You do not treat [crowds](ref/rule/city-streets) as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles), and you gain a +5 circumstance bonus on Stealth
checks when using a crowd as [cover](ref/rule/cover).
