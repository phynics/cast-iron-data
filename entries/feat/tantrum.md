Your crying, sputtering, and even frothing at the mouth makes you so laughably incoherent that
opponents are apt to dismiss you as a threat.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 1 rank, rage class feature, [gnome](ref/race/gnome).\
**Benefit:** You may use the Bluff skill to [feint](ref/rule/feint) while raging. The effect of the
feint lasts only until the first time you hit the target.
