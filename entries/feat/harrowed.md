Numerous Harrow readings early in your life seem to have hit the mark precisely, increasing your
belief that you are destined for a specific purpose in life.\
**Benefit:** You get a +1 bonus on all Will saves made to resist enchantment effects. Once per day,
you may draw a card from a [Harrow deck](ref/eq-misc/harrow-deck) you own. At any one time for the
rest of that day, you may apply a +2 bonus on any d20 roll modified by the card's suit. For example,
if you drew a card from the suit of Dexterity, you could apply this +2 bonus on an Initiative check,
a Reflex save, a Dexterity-based skill check, or a ranged attack roll. You may assign this +2 bonus
after you make the roll, but you must do so before you know whether the roll was a success or not.
