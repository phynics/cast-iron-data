## Slurk Rider (Combat)

**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 129

You are skilled at riding slurks into battle.\
**Prerequisites:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** You are able to control, guide, and communicate with [slurks](ref/monster/slurk), as if
they understood Draconic. You can also spend a standard action to manipulate the nodules on the
slurk's back to activate its slime ability. Both you and the slurk can activate this ability during
the same round.
