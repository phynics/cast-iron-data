## Bushwhack (Combat)

You can subdue an unsuspecting foe quickly.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** When a flat-footed creature is unaware of your presence and you successfully grapple
it, you can attempt a second grapple combat maneuver check to pin the creature as a free action. If
you have the [Chokehold](ref/feat/chokehold) feat, you can instead use this second combat maneuver
check to initiate a chokehold.
