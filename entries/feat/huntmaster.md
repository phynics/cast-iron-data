You are an expert trainer of horses, hounds, falcons, or hunting cats.\
**Prerequisites:** Handle Animal 1 rank; either the animal companion, divine bond (mount), or mount
class feature; [human](ref/race/human).\
**Benefit:** If you have the animal companion class feature, pick one of the following types of
animal companions that this feat affects: [bird](ref/companion/bird), [dog](ref/companion/dog),
[small cat](ref/companion/cat-small-cheetah-leopard), or [horse](ref/companion/horse). If you have
the divine bond (mount) or mount class feature, this feat always affects horses.

You gain a +2 bonus on Handle Animal and Knowledge (nature) checks with creatures of that type of
animal. Furthermore, you are treated as one level higher when determining the abilities of your
animal companion or mount, as long as it is of the chosen type.
