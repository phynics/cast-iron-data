## Giant's Crush (Combat)

When you strike hard, you knock smaller foes off their feet.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), base attack bonus +6, giant subtype.\
**Benefit:** If you hit a creature at least one size category smaller than you with a melee attack
while using both Power Attack and Vital Strike, the creature that you attacked is also knocked
[prone](ref/rule/prone). A successful Reflex save negates this effect (DC = 10 + your base attack
bonus). You must choose to use this feat before making the attack roll.\
**Special:** This effect stacks with the effect of [Giant's Wallop](ref/feat/giants-wallop).
