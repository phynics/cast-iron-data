You know how to keep your head down and crawl away undetected by larger foes.\
**Benefit:** You can crawl at half your normal speed and can take a 5-foot step when crawling. When
crawling, you don't provoke attacks of opportunity from opponents that are one or more size
categories larger than you.\
**Normal:** You can crawl 5 feet as a move action, and crawling provokes attacks of opportunity.
