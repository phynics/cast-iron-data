**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (dungeoneering) 4 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +3 bonus on Perception checks in underground or mountainous environments. If
you have 10 or more ranks in Knowledge (dungeoneering), this bonus increases to +6.
