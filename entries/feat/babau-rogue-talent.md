**Sources:** Demons Revisited pg 8

Your instinct for combat is much like that of a rogue.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 17, [babau](ref/monster/demon-babau).\
**Benefit:** Choose one rogue talent that adds to sneak attacks (such as
[bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack) or [slow reactions](ref/talent/slow-reactions)).\
**Special:** You may select this feat multiple times. Each time you take the feat, you gain a new
rogue talent.
