You have the ability to perfectly emulate a human.\
**Prerequisites:** [Change shape](ref/umr/change-shape) ability, shapechanger subtype, must be able
to change shape into a human or must have a true form that appears human.\
**Benefit:** You count as both human and your race for the purpose of taking character options, such
as archetypes, feats, spells, traits, and prestige classes. In addition, you count as human for all
other effects whenever you would otherwise appear human. For instance, a +1 human
[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) arrow would deal additional damage to a [kitsune](ref/race/kitsune) with
this feat while she was in her human form (as per change shape), but not in her true form. Likewise,
such an arrow would deal additional damage to a [skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker) with this feat
while she was in her true form, but would not in her bestial form (as per change shape).
