**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 100

When [driving a chosen type of vehicle](ref/rule/driving-vehicles), you exhibit incredible control,
maneuvering the vehicle with greater ease and stopping with uncanny precision.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skilled Driver](ref/feat/skilled-driver) with the selected vehicle type.\
**Benefit:** You can make an accelerate, decelerate, or turn action as a move action instead of a
standard action. Furthermore, when stopping a vehicle, you subtract 10 feet from the roll to
determine how many feet the vehicle moves forward before it stops.
