## Restoration Mastery (Item Mastery)

You can heal more than just injuries of the flesh.\
**Prerequisites:** Heal 5 ranks, Use Magic Device 5 ranks, base Fortitude saving throw bonus +3.\
**Benefit:** You can cause an item that has a conjuration (healing) spell of 2nd level or higher
listed in its construction requirements to cast [lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser).
If you have 9 ranks in both Heal and Use Magic Device, you can instead cause an item that has a
conjuration (healing) spell of 3rd level or higher listed in its construction requirements to cast
either [remove blindness/deafness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness) or
[remove paralysis](ref/spell/remove-paralysis). You can use this feat once per day, plus an
additional time per day when your base Fortitude save bonus reaches +9 and +12.
