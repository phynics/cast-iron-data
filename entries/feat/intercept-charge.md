## Intercept Charge (Combat, Teamwork)

You can get in the way of an opponent charging your ally.\
**Benefit:** When an opponent [charges](ref/rule/charge) your ally with this feat, as an immediate
action you can move up to your speed toward any square in the path of the charge. If you end your
movement in the path of the charge, the opponent must stop when it becomes adjacent to you and then
attack you instead of your ally. Your movement from using this feat counts toward your movement on
your next turn.
