Your affinity with the Shadow Plane allows you to draw a cloak of concealing gloom around you, even
in bright light.\
**Prerequisites:** Stealth 9 ranks, shadow blending racial trait or shadow call
[shadowdancer](ref/pclass/shadowdancer) prestige class feature.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can pull a haze of shadow around you, granting yourself
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance) in bright light or normal light for 1 minute.
You can use this ability once per day, plus one additional time per day when your character level
reaches 12th, 15th, and 18th.
