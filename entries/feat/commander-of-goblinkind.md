You employ ruthlessness and malice to command other races of goblinoids to do your bidding.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [hobgoblin](ref/race/hobgoblin).\
**Benefit:** You receive a +5 competence bonus on Knowledge (local) checks and Charisma-based skill
checks regarding other goblinoids. If you have the [Leadership](ref/feat/leadership) feat, treat
your leadership score as 2 higher when taking followers or a cohort with the goblinoid subtype.
