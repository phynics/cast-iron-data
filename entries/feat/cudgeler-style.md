## Cudgeler Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 13

You have learned how to rush your foe and deliver an incapacitating blow.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bludgeoner](ref/feat/bludgeoner).\
**Benefit:** When you hit with a charge attack to deal nonlethal damage while using this style, your
weapon deals damage as if it were [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) than
it actually is. If you have the ability to make more than one attack on a charge, this increase
applies only to your first attack.
