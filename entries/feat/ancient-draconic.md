**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 24

You empower spells using the ancient language of dragons.\
**Prerequisites:** Arcane caster level 5th, speaks Draconic.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on caster level checks to overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) when casting arcane spells that have a verbal
component. If you speak Draconic when casting an arcane language-dependent spell, all intelligent
creatures with a language can understand your words.
