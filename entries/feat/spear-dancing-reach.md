## Spear Dancing Reach (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 17

You can strike from afar with double weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 17, [Spear Dancing Spiral](ref/feat/spear-dancing-spiral),
[Spear Dancing Style](ref/feat/spear-dancing-style), [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** While using Spear Dancing Style and making a full attack using the chosen weapon as a
[double](ref/misc/double) weapon, you can grant the [reach](ref/misc/reach) special weapon feature
to one or both of the weapon's ends until the end of your turn as a swift action.
