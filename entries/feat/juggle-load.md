## Juggle Load (Combat)

Your fingers are so nimble that reloading your sling is almost effortless for you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Ammo Drop](ref/feat/ammo-drop), Sleight of Hand 1 rank, proficient with
[sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling).\
**Benefit:** You can load a sling or [double sling](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-double-sling) as a free
action. This does not provoke attacks of opportunity. This feat allows you to fire your sling as
many times in a full-attack action as you could attack if you were using a bow.
