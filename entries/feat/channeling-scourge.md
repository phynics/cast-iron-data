**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 92

Your zeal for hunting your faith's enemies empowers your ability to channel divine energy, as long
as you channel that energy for harm.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature, inquisitor level 1st.\
**Benefit:** When you use channel energy to deal damage, your inquisitor levels count as cleric
levels for determining the number of damage dice and the saving throw DC.
