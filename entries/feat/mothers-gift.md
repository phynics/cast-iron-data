**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 185

You inherit a special boon from your hag parent.\
**Prerequisites:** [Changeling](ref/race/changeling).\
**Benefit:** Your dark legacy manifests in one of the following ways. You choose the manifestation
when you choose the feat, and once selected it cannot be changed.

> **Hag Claws (Ex):** You gain a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with your claws.
>
> **Surprisingly Tough (Ex):** Your natural armor bonus increases by +1.
>
> **Uncanny Resistance (Su):** You gain [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 6 +
> your character level.

**Special:** You can gain this feat up to three times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take
the feat, you must select a different manifestation.
