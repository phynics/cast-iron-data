## Grabbing Master (Combat)

You can grapple two foes as easily as one.\
**Prerequisites:** [Grabbing Drag](ref/feat/grabbing-drag),
[Grabbing Style](ref/feat/grabbing-style), [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple); base
attack bonus +12, brawler level 8th, or monk level 8th.\
**Benefit:** When you are grabbing two opponents while using Grabbing Style, you can use your
grapple to move or damage one or both opponents you are grappling, instead of just one.
