## Voracious Blade (Combat)

You can channel your daemonic energies through the vile blade you wield.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (rhoka sword), base attack bonus +4,
[urdefhan](ref/monster/urdefhan).\
**Benefit:** When wielding a [rhoka sword](ref/eq-weapon/rhoka-sword), you can channel your daemonic
energies through the blade as a swift action, causing it to deal Strength damage as your bite
attack. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your Constitution modifier
(minimum 1).
