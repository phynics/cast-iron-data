Some ghorans glow with a nurturing inner light.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [ghoran](ref/race/ghoran).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can cast [daylight](ref/spell/daylight) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to your character
level. If you have the light dependent racial trait, you can instead cast this spell without the
usual effect, but you are considered to be exposed to sunlight for that day.
