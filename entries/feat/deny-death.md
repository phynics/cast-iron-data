Your ki is so strong that it can deny death.\
**Prerequisites:** Ki pool, [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance).\
**Benefit:** As long as you have 1 ki point in your ki pool, when you fail your Constitution check
to stabilize, you do not lose 1 hit point. If you succeed at the check, you can spend 1 ki point to
heal 1d6 hit points. If you roll a natural 20 on the check to stabilize, you can spend 1 ki point to
heal 2d6 hit points of damage instead.
