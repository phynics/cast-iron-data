You take Grand Prince Beldam I's insistence upon the resilience of simple creatures to heart,
emulating them to shield yourself from complex mental effects.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will).

When you attempt a saving throw against an enchantment effect that cannot affect an animal (such as
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)), you can roll the saving throw twice and take the better
result.
