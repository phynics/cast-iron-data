Your gaseous state is more potent than that of your peers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Vampire](ref/template/vampire).\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can assume _[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)_ at will
(caster level 5th), but can remain gaseous indefinitely and have a fly speed of 20 feet with perfect
maneuverability. This is a supernatural ability.

If you already possess the _[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)_ special quality, you can instead
choose to augment your _[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)_ in one of the following ways. These
effects apply only to creatures that enter your space while you are in
_[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)._

> **Glitterdust (Su):** Creatures must succeed at a Will save or be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) and
> outlined in golden particles as though under the effects of [glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust).
>
> **Mind Fog (Su):** Creatures must succeed at a Will save or take penalties as though under the
> effects of [mind fog](ref/spell/mind-fog). This effect lasts as long as a creature shares your
> space and for 2d6 rounds after.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take this feat, choose another
swarm type.
