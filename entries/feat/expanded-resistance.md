You have expanded your natural resistance to magic to encompass different kinds of magic.\
**Prerequisites:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome), illusion resistance racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Select one school of magic other than illusion. You gain a +2 racial bonus on saving
throws against spells or effects from the selected school.\
**Special:** You may select this feat more than once. Its effects do not stack. Each time you select
it, it applies to a different school of magic.
