## Rebounding Leap (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 115

Your riding and lancing expertise allows you to enter and leave the saddle with great speed.\
**Prerequisites:** \[Leaping lance class feature\][dragoon](ref/arc-fighter/dragoon), Acrobatics 5
ranks, Ride 11 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at the Acrobatics check to jump as part of your leaping lance class
feature, you can remount your steed as a swift action.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 130\
When attempting an Acrobatics check to jump as part of your leaping lance class feature, you can
spend up to 5 stamina points to gain a bonus on that check equal to the number of stamina points you
spent.
