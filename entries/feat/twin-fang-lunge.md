## Twin Fang Lunge (Combat)

You strike with the force of a serpent's lunge.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15; [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw);
[Twin Fang Strike](ref/feat/twin-fang-strike); [Twin Fang Style](ref/feat/twin-fang-style);
Acrobatics 8 ranks; base attack bonus +8 or monk level 8th.\
**Benefit:** While using Twin Fang Style, you can spend a full-round action to move up to twice your
speed and then use Twin Fang Strike as if you were taking the [attack](ref/rule/attack) action.
