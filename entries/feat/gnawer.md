Thanks to your teeth's strength and quick growth, you are able to chew through nearly any obstacle
given sufficient time.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sharptooth](ref/feat/sharptooth), [ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk).\
**Benefit:** Damage dealt by your bite attack ignores an amount of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
equal to 3 plus half your character level. You can spend a full-round action to deal double your
bite damage to an unattended, inanimate object.\
**Special:** If you have the [Burrowing Teeth](ref/feat/burrowing-teeth) feat, you can burrow
through solid stone at a speed of 5 feet per 10 minutes.
