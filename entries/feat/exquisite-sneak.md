You know how to move silently even while wearing some forms of armor.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Careful Sneak](ref/feat/careful-sneak), Stealth 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You do not apply the armor check penalty for light and medium armor on Stealth skill
checks no matter how fast you move. You still cannot use [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) while running
or charging.
