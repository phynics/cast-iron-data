**Sources:** Chronicle of Legends pg 9

Shadows rise and fall at your whim.\
**Prerequisites:** Shadow illusion class feature.\
**Benefit:** Your _shadow illusion_ ability can encase you in shadows as the
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness) spell, using your [shadowdancer](ref/pclass/shadowdancer) level as
the caster level. As a standard action, you can raise or lower the
[illumination level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) of the effect, from darkness up to normal light.
