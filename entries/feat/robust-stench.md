Your questionable hygiene choices may offend your allies, but exposure has inured them to other,
deadlier effects.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin).\
**Benefit:** Allies within 5 feet of you receive a +2 morale bonus on Fortitude saving throws. This
bonus ends as soon as they move more than 5 feet away from you. This bonus applies only to allies
you have fought alongside at least once before.
