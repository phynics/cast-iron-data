**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 157

Your senses are especially keen in the utter darkness.\
**Prerequisites:** [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet.\
**Benefit:** Your darkvision has a range of 120 feet.\
**Normal:** Darkvision normally extends 60 or 90 feet.

### Mythic Deepsight

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 62\
Your sharp eyes can penetrate deeper into the darkness, revealing more to you.\
**Prerequisites:** Deepsight.\
**Benefit:** The range of your [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) increases by 10 feet per tier.
