Your good luck helps you and your business persevere even during the most troublesome times.\
**Prerequisites:** Own at least one building.\
**Benefit:** During the Event phase of [downtime](ref/rule/downtime), if an event occurs and the GM
rolls on a building event table or organization event table, you may, once per week, choose to roll
twice on the applicable table and choose either result. You must declare you are using this feat
before the results of the initial roll are revealed.
