## Cleaving Sweep (Combat)

**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 68

You can sweep multiple foes to the ground with a single blow of your axe.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Cleave](ref/feat/cleave), [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (greataxe), base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** When using a full-attack action with a two-handed weapon, you can give up your regular
attacks and instead attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver at your highest base attack
bonus against each opponent within reach. You must attempt a separate combat maneuver check against
each opponent.
