Your knowledge of strange life forms makes you adept at piercing their disguises and detecting their
hiding places.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Knowledge \[any\]), Knowledge (any) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can substitute a [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill for opposed Perception
checks against creatures' Disguise and Stealth checks, and for Survival checks to track creatures.
You must have 3 ranks in the Knowledge skill that corresponds to the creature's type.
