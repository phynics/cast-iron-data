## Kobold Ambusher (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 137

You are adept at moving fast and staying hidden.\
**Prerequisites:** Stealth 4 ranks, [kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** You take no penalty on Stealth checks when you move up to your full speed. You can use
the [run](ref/rule/run) or [charge](ref/rule/charge) action and remain hidden, taking a -10 penalty
on the check to do so.\
**Normal:** When moving greater than half speed up to your full speed, you take a -5 penalty on
Stealth checks. When you are able to run or charge while using Stealth, you take a -20 penalty on
the Stealth check.
