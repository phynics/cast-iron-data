You are adept at numerous disciplines.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 5th, [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf), multitalented racial trait.\
**Benefit:** All of your classes are considered [favored classes](ref/rule/favored-class). You gain
either +1 hit point or +1 skill point whenever you take a level in any class. Apply these bonuses
retroactively for all class levels that have not yet gained one of these bonuses.\
**Normal:** Half-elves with the multitalented racial trait have two favored classes.
