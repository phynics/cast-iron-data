## Coordinated Reposition (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 92

You are skilled in coordinating combat positioning with your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Step-Up](ref/feat/step-up).\
**Benefit:** Whenever an ally with this feat who is threatening a creature you are threatening takes
a 5-foot step, you can move 5 feet as an immediate action. This movement does not provoke attacks of
opportunity.
