## Hurtful (Combat)

You know how to add injury to insult.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack).\
**Benefit:** When you successfully demoralize an opponent within your melee reach with an
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check, you can make a single melee attack against that creature
as a swift action. If your attack fails to damage the target, its [shaken](ref/misc/shaken)
condition from being demoralized immediately ends.
