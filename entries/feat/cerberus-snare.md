## Cerberus Snare (Combat)

Your strikes latch onto your opponent like the jaws of a cerberus, preventing them from fleeing
across planes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Cerberus Style](ref/feat/cerberus-style), base attack bonus +9 or monk level
9th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you damage an opponent with Cerberus Style, except that its duration is 1
round. [Spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) applies; for the purpose of checks to overcome
spell resistance, your caster level is equal to your character level.
