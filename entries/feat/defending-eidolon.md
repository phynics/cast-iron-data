You have trained your eidolon to protect you.\
**Prerequisites:** Shield ally feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are adjacent to your eidolon, you can choose for the eidolon to take a -1
penalty on melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks to gain a +1 dodge bonus to your Armor
Class. When your eidolon's base attack bonus reaches +5, and for every +5 thereafter, the penalty
increases by -1 and the dodge bonus increases by +1. You must choose to use this feat when your
eidolon is making an attack or full-attack action with melee or natural weapons, and its effect
lasts until your eidolon's next turn, or until you are no longer adjacent to the eidolon, whichever
occurs first.
