You can speak with burrowing animals.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [gnome](ref/race/gnome), gnome magic racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You can use [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) at will, but only to communicate with burrowing
animals like gophers, moles, and the like. You can still use your gnome speak with animals ability
once per day to speak with any animal, as usual.
