You have an especially strong stomach.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13; [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc), or
[orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against any effect causing the
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) or [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) conditions and against all ingested
poisons (but not other poisons). In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) skill checks to find food for yourself (and only yourself).
