## Punch Through (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Demon Hunter's Handbook pg 21

You and your allies are adept at bypassing enemy defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, you may make a single attack against a target. If the attack
hits and deals damage to the target, all of your allies with this feat ignore up to 5 points of
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) against the same target for 1 round. This feat does
not apply to damage reduction without a type (such as DR 10/-).
