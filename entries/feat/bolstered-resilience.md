You can dramatically increase your [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) in exchange for its
temporary loss.\
**Prerequisites:** Damage reduction.\
**Benefit:** As an immediate action, you can double your DR against a single attack, to a maximum of
DR 20. The type of the DR remains unchanged. If the attack you are guarding against is not
successful, the increased damage reduction persists until you are hit with an attack or until the
start of your next turn, whichever happens first. At the start of your next turn, you become
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued). You cannot use this feat while you are fatigued.
