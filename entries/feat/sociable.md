You have a way of helping others to get along.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** As a move action, you grant all friendly creatures within 30 feet who can see or hear
you (including yourself) a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks for a number of rounds equal to your
Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round).
