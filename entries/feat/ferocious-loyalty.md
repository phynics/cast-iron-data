## Ferocious Loyalty (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 11

You have a powerful sense of responsibility for your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Werebear-kin](ref/race/skinwalker), werebear, or someone who associates with
these creatures\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls against any foe that currently threatens an
ally who also has this feat. Whenever an ally with this feat is rendered
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless) or killed within 30 feet of you, you gain a +2 morale bonus on attack
rolls for 1 minute or until the foe responsible is rendered helpless or killed, whichever comes
first.
