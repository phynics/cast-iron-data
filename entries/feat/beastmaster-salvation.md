## Beastmaster Salvation (Combat)

Your presence grants your companion the toughness and will to resist all manner of threats.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Beastmaster Style](ref/feat/beastmaster-style), Handle Animal 5 ranks, Sense Motive 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** If you are adjacent to your animal companion when it attempts a saving throw, you can
attempt a Handle Animal check as an immediate action. Your animal companion does not attempt the
saving throw, but instead uses your Handle Animal result as its result for the save.
