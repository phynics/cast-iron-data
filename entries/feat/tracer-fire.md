## Tracer Fire (Combat)

You highlight the path of your projectile with light, allowing your allies to better spot the
target.\
**Prerequisites:** [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot),
[Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot).\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can make a single ranged attack at your highest base attack
bonus with an illuminated throwing weapon or piece of ammunition, such as a
[flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) [shuriken](ref/eq-weapon/shuriken) or an arrow imbued with a
magical light effect. If the attack hits its target, attacks against the same target gain a +2
circumstance bonus for 1 round, so long as those attacks originate in
[dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darker areas. The target counts as being in bright light
until the projectile is removed or extinguished. Removing the projectile is a move action that
provokes an attack of opportunity.
