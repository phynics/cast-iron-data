You are able to emulate the echolocation techniques of bats.\
**Prerequisites:** [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight),
[Improved Blind-Fight](ref/feat/improved-blind-fight), Perception 10 ranks.\
**Benefit:** By spending a move action to make a sound and listen for the echoes, you gain
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) 30 feet until the end of your turn. Using this feat requires making
audible sounds that impose a -4 penalty on your Stealth checks.\
**Special:** This feat cannot be used if you are [deafened](ref/misc/deafened) or unable to make
noise, and you cannot detect anything in areas that are magically silenced.
