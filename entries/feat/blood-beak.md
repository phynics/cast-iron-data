## Blood Beak (Combat)

Your bleed attack is bloody and dangerous.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +5, natural weapon racial trait, [tengu](ref/race/tengu).\
**Benefit:** Increase the damage of your beak attack to 1d6. Furthermore, when you confirm a
critical hit with your beak attack, you also deal 1 point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage.\
**Special:** The bleed effect from this feat stacks with that of the
[Bleeding Critical](ref/feat/bleeding-critical) feat and similar effects, adding 1 point to your
bleed damage.
