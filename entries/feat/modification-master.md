## Modification Master (Combat)

You can create inconceivable bastardizations of the smith's art.\
**Prerequisites:** [Creative Armorsmith](ref/feat/creative-armorsmith),
[Armor Adept](ref/feat/armor-adept), and Craft (armor) 7 ranks, or
[Creative Weaponsmith](ref/feat/creative-weaponsmith), [Weapon Adept](ref/feat/weapon-adept), and
Craft (weapons) 7 ranks; Knowledge (engineering) 7 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can use Creative Armorsmith or Creative Weaponsmith to add a modification to a
weapon or suit of armor that already has a modification. The modification you are adding must be one
that you have selected with Armor Adept or Weapon Adept. In addition, others can use armor or
weapons with your temporary modifications.
