## Giant Vendetta (Story)

Your life has been shaped by an encounter with giants.\
**Prerequisites:** You must have been knocked unconscious in an encounter with a challenging giant
foe or have a regional background trait tied to giants, such as
[Giant Investigator](ref/trait/giant-investigator) (marsh, ocean, river, storm, taiga giants),
[Giant Dodger](ref/trait/giant-dodger) (cliff, desert, river giants),
[Enchanted by Giants](ref/trait/enchanted-by-giants) (jungle, taiga, wood giants),
[Giant Ambivalence](ref/trait/giant-ambivalence) (cloud, hill, river, stone giants),
[Chilled by Brutality](ref/trait/chilled-by-brutality) (cloud, frost, river, rune, taiga giants),
[Scrambling Servant](ref/trait/scrambling-servant) (cave, fire, river, slag, storm giants), and
[Giant-Harried](ref/trait/giant-harried) (ash, hill, river, shadow giants).\
**Benefit:** Choose one kind of giant (such as hill or stone); you must choose a kind of giant that
allows you to qualify for this feat. You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls against giants of that
kind. You also gain a +1 bonus on Knowledge (local) checks regarding giants of that kind and can
attempt those Knowledge checks untrained. In addition, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC against all
giants.\
**Goal:** Defeat a giant of the chosen kind that is a challenging foe.\
**Completion Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on initiative checks when facing a giant of any kind,
which increases to +2 against giants of the selected kind. Whenever you defeat a giant of any kind
that is a challenging foe, the benefits of this feat that apply only to a specific kind of giant now
also apply to giants of the kind you defeated.
