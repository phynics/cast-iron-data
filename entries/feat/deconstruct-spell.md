**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 14

You deconstruct a spell into its constituent glyphs, storing them to replenish your crystalline
dust.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Counterspell](ref/feat/improved-counterspell),
[aphorite](ref/race/aphorite), crystalline dust racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you successfully [counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) or
[dispel](ref/spell/dispel-magic) a spell, you can instead deconstruct the spell to regain a number
of rounds of your crystalline dust racial trait equal to half the level of the spell. You can't use
this feat to gain more than your maximum number of rounds of your crystalline dust racial trait.
