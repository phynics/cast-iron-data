**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 170

You are especially resistant to magic.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), hardy racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +4 racial bonus on saving throws against spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This replaces the normal bonus from the
dwarf's hardy racial trait.\
**Normal:** Dwarves normally receive a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against spells and
spell-like abilities.
