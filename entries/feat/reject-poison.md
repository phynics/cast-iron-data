**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 215, Faiths of Corruption pg 24

You are a careful poisoner, and know how to protect yourself against exposure to the ill effects of
most substances.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [poison use](ref/ability/poison-use) class feature, worshiper of
[Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you fail the initial saving throw against poison, you have a 20% chance of
naturally rejecting the toxin, negating its effects and wasting its use.
