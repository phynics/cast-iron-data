## Master Performer (Faction)

When using your bardic performance abilities, you grant your allies greater bonuses.\
**Prerequisites:** Bardic performance class feature,
[Extra Performance](ref/feat/extra-performance), [Kitharodian Academy](ref/rule/f-bards) 15 TPA.\
**Benefit:** When any of your bardic performance abilities grant your allies a bonus, that bonus is
increased by +1.
