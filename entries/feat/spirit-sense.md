You sense the presence of the unseen.\
**Prerequisites:** [Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) or ability to cast psychic
spells.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +5 bonus on checks to notice haunts. In addition, when you target an
incorporeal creature with a corporeal spell or effect that doesn't deal damage, you have a 75%
chance of affecting the target.\
**Normal:** Corporeal spells and effects that don't deal damage have only a 50% chance of affecting
an incorporeal creature.
