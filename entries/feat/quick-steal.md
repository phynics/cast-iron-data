## Quick Steal (Combat)

You are adept at relieving foes of their belongings even while you strike.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Steal](ref/feat/improved-steal), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** On your turn, you can perform a single [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver in place
of one of your melee attacks. You must choose the melee attack with the highest base attack bonus to
make the steal.\
**Normal:** A steal combat maneuver is a standard action.
