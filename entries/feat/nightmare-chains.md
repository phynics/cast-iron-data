Chains are a kyton's inheritance and dominion.\
**Prerequisites:** Kyton subtype, 5 or more Hit Dice.\
**Benefit:** You gain the [kyton's](ref/monster/kyton) dancing chains ability, except you can
control only up to two chains this way. This grants you two chain attacks, which deal damage as per
a [spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain) made for a creature of your size. If you already have
the dancing chains ability, you can control two additional chains.\
**Special:** At the GM's discretion, non-kyton outsiders can select this feat if they have a
thematic link to chains (such as [horned devils](ref/monster/devil-horned-devil-cornugon) or demonic
servants of [Shivaska](ref/faith/shivaska)).
