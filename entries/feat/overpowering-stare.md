## Overpowering Stare (Stare)

Your painful stare overwhelms your foe's mind.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mesmerist](ref/class/mesmerist) level 11th, painful stare class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use your painful stare, the target must succeed at a Will saving throw (DC
= 10 + half your mesmerist level + your Charisma modifier) or become [confused](ref/misc/confused)
for 1 round. Whether or not the save is successful, a creature cannot be affected by this ability
again for 24 hours.
