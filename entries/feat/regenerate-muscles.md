You use your powerful metabolism to temporarily increase the strength of your muscles, but doing so
costs you your regeneration powers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration),
[troll](ref/monster/troll).\
**Benefit:** As a free action, you can make your regeneration stop working for 1 minute and gain a
+2 enhancement bonus to your Strength. You lose your regeneration ability while you benefit from
this bonus to Strength and for 1 minute afterward. You can end this ability early as a free action.
You can activate this ability only if your regeneration is functioning.
