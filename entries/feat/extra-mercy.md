Your lay on hands ability adds an additional mercy.\
**Prerequisites:** [Lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) class feature, mercy class feature.\
**Benefit:** Select one additional mercy for which you qualify. When you use lay on hands to heal
damage to one target, it also receives the additional effects of this mercy.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take
this feat, select a new mercy.
