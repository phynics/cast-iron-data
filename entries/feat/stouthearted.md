You fight back your fear by voicing the song in your heart.\
**Prerequisites:** [Raging song](ref/ability/raging-song) class feature.\
**Benefit:** While you are maintaining a raging song, if you fail a saving throw against a fear
effect, you can expend a round of performing to reroll your saving throw as an immediate action. You
must keep this second result, even if it lower.
