Your blessings are longer lasting, and you can bless more than one item at a time.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bless Equipment](ref/feat/bless-equipment),
[Improved Bless Equipment](ref/feat/improved-bless-equipment), caster level 12th,
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** The duration of your equipment blessings doubles. In addition, as a standard action you
may bless up to two pieces of equipment within range with a single blessing, expending uses of
channeled energy as if you had only blessed one item.
