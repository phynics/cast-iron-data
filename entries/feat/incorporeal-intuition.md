You notice incorporeal creatures, even those lurking in walls.\
**Prerequisites:** [Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) or the ability to cast
psychic spells, [Spirit Sense](ref/feat/spirit-sense).\
**Benefit:** You can sense the presence and movement of incorporeal creatures though solid walls, as
long as that incorporeal creature is adjacent to you. When you sense a creature in this way, you can
attempt a Knowledge (religion) check to identify the creature and its special powers or
vulnerabilities, with a -5 penalty. When you sense an incorporeal creature with this ability and use
a [readied action](ref/rule/ready) to attack it when it attacks from inside the wall, it doesn't
gain [cover](ref/rule/cover) against your attack.\
**Normal:** Incorporeal creatures are silent unless they choose to make a sound and are thus almost
impossible to detect when inside a solid wall. When readying to attack an incorporeal creature in a
solid wall, it gains cover against your attack.
