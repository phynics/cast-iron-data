## Hinder (Blood Hex)

**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 29

You curse a bleeding opponent with uncertainty.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, Use Magic Device 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can curse an enemy you've damaged with a melee attack in the
last minute to become hesitant. The target must succeed at a Reflex save or take a -10 penalty on
any initiative check it makes in the next minute.\
**Special:** If a shaman or witch uses this [blood hex](ref/rule/feats-class), the target is unable
to take a full-attack action for 1 round. This effect can be extended with the cackle hex.
