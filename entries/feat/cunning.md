You are particularly devious, and can master more skills than others in the same amount of time.\
**Benefit:** You gain 1 additional skill point per Hit Die. When you take this feat, you gain a
number of skill points equal to your Hit Dice right away, and every time your Hit Dice increase in
the future, you will gain an additional skill point as well.
