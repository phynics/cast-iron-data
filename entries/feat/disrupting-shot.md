## Disrupting Shot (Combat)

With a well-placed shot, you make it harder for a nearby opponent to cast spells.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot), fighter level 6th.\
**Benefit:** If you [ready an action](ref/rule/ready) to shoot an opponent casting a spell within 30
feet and successfully hit that opponent with a ranged attack, the concentration DC to successfully
cast the spell is increased by +4.
