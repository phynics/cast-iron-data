**Sources:** Classic Horrors Revisited pg 32

You gain some of the skills and knowledge of a consumed foe by eating its brain.\
**Prerequisites:** [Ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul), Int 17.\
**Benefit:** If you eat a portion of the brain of a creature with Intelligence 3 or higher, you gain
a +2 insight bonus on all skill checks and Will saving throws for 1 hour. Eating a brain is a
full-round action, and the target must be dead or helpless. If the target is living, you may attempt
to eat its brain as a coup de grace attack on the target, but you gain the insight bonus only if
your attempt results in the victim's death.
