**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 30

Your time in the [Dimension of Dreams](ref/rule/dimension-of-dreams) has improved your ability to
manipulate its landscape.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 15, [Lucid Dreamer](ref/feat/lucid-dreamer), Knowledge (planes) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can reroll a failed attempt to perform an impossible action, such as
casting a spell you can't normally cast, gaining an effect of a spell as if it had been cast, or
conjuring a magic item. You also gain an additional +2 bonus on Charisma checks to determine your
initial condition when entering a dream with your lucid body.
