Your skill with foul sorcery is even more potent than that of others of your race.\
**Prerequisites:** Fiendish sorcery racial trait, [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** If you are a sorcerer and possess the [Rakshasa](ref/sorcbloodline/rakshasa) bloodline
or [Shadow](ref/sorcbloodline/shadow) bloodline, treat your Charisma score as 2 points higher for
all sorcerer class abilities.
