## Solo Maneuvers (Combat)

You fight best when you're on your own.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are the only one threatening an opponent, you gain a +1 bonus on combat
maneuvers against that creature and a +1 bonus to CMD against combat maneuvers made by that
creature.
