## Cooperative Swarmer (Combat)

You're a team player and help out those normally too large or clumsy to fight in close quarters with
you.\
**Prerequisites:** Swarming racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You can share a square with an ally your size or larger, even if your ally does not
have the swarming racial trait. Neither you nor the creature you share a space with count as
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking) your targets, but you gain a +2 shield bonus to AC while sharing a
square with a larger ally. If you perform an [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to aid
your ally's attack roll or AC, increase the bonus you provide by 1.
