In your personal interactions, you notice what others don't. It's hard to get anything past you.\
**Prerequisites:** Stern gaze class feature, Sense Motive 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you make a Sense Motive check to oppose someone's Bluff check, you can roll
two dice and take the higher result.
