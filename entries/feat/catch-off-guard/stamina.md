**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 115\
When making an attack with an improvised weapon against an armed opponent, you can spend 5 stamina
points. If you do, the target is considered flat-footed during that attack. An opponent who
recognizes you and has seen you use Catch Off-Guard in this way is immune to this effect.
