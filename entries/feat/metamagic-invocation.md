You have further refined your combination of elemental power and spellcasting with metamagic
techniques.\
**Prerequisites:** Metakinesis class feature.\
**Benefit:** Choose a metamagic feat from the following list:
[Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell), [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell),
[Intuitive Spell](ref/feat/intuitive-spell), [Logical Spell](ref/feat/logical-spell),
[Quicken Spell](ref/feat/quicken-spell), [Reach Spell](ref/feat/reach-spell), or
[Tenacious Spell](ref/feat/tenacious-spell). When casting a spell via
[Kinetic Invocation](ref/feat/kinetic-invocation), you can increase its burn cost to augment the
spell with that metamagic feat.

The burn cost increase is equal to the spell level increase applied by the feat. Augmenting a spell
increases its casting time as if it were cast spontaneously.

**Special:** You can take this feat more than once. Each time you take this feat, select another
metamagic feat; you can augment a spell with multiple feats by accepting additional points of burn
equal to the total increased spell level.
