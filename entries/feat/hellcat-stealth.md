**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Stealth), Stealth 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You may make Stealth checks in normal or bright light even when observed, but at a -10
penalty.\
**Normal:** You cannot make Stealth checks while observed.
