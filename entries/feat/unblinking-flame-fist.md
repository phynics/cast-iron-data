## Unblinking Flame Fist (Combat)

You can exploit the weaknesses in your opponents' defenses, like a flame seeking fuel to consume.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13; Wis 13; [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise);
[Perfect Style](ref/feat/perfect-style); [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist);
[Unblinking Flame Feint](ref/feat/unblinking-flame-feint); base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.\
**Benefit:** When using Perfect Style, the save DC of your stunning fist attack increases by 2
against [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) targets. When you spend 1 ki point to gain the benefits
of [Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint) for 1 round, you can attempt a [feint](ref/rule/feint)
as part of your movement when [charging](ref/rule/charge).
