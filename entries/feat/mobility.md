## Mobility (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 130

You can easily move through a dangerous melee.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge).\
**Benefit:** You get a +4 dodge bonus to Armor Class against attacks of opportunity caused when you
move out of or within a threatened area. A condition that makes you lose your Dexterity bonus to
Armor Class (if any) also makes you lose dodge bonuses.

Dodge bonuses stack with each other, unlike most types of bonuses.
