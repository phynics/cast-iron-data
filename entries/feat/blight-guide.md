Your confidence in exploring dangerous regions inspires the same in your companions.\
**Prerequisites:** [Blight Survivalist](ref/feat/blight-survivalist),
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Survival).\
**Benefit:** Your bonus on saving throws gained from the Blight Survivalist feat extends to your
allies within 30 feet. Furthermore, once per day, if you would fail a saving throw against an
environmental hazard or weather effect, you can reroll that save, but you must use the second
result, even if it is lower.
