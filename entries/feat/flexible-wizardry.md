**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 148

You have learned a trick that makes your spellcasting more flexible.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Mastery](ref/feat/spell-mastery), wizard level 1st.\
**Benefit:** When you prepare your spells each day, you can choose to keep a number of spell slots
equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) flexibly prepared. Instead of preparing a single
spell within each of these slots, you can partially prepare two spells. Anytime after you prepare
these flexible slots, you can spend a full-round action to finalize one slot, choosing one of the
two spells to be finished and fully prepared in that slot.
