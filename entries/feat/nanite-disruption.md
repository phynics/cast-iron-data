You can use your nanite surge to inflict wounds upon other mechanical beings.\
**Prerequisites:** Nanite surge racial trait.\
**Benefit:** As long as you are adjacent to an android, a robot, or a creature primarily made of
electronic components (GM's discretion), you can use your nanite surge ability as an immediate
action to short-circuit that creature's hardware. If you succeed at a melee touch attack and the
target fails a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your character level + 1/2 your Constitution modifier), it
takes a penalty equal to 1 + 1/2 your level (minimum 0) on its next d20 roll. This penalty lasts
until the beginning of your next turn.
