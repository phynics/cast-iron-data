**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 170

You can catch the sour scent of fear on the wind.\
**Prerequisites:** [Keen Scent](ref/feat/keen-scent), [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or
[orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** You receive a +4 bonus on Perception checks to detect creatures with the
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken), [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked)
conditions using [scent](ref/umr/scent). You can substitute your Perception skill for Sense Motive
skill checks if the subject has one of the previous conditions or is attempting to cover up their
fear in some way.
