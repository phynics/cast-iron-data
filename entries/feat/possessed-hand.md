You've made a peaceful and permanent bargain with a ghost, spirit, or outsider. It possesses your
hand, guiding and aiding you in exchange for help in return.\
**Benefit:** You attract a ghost, spirit, or outsider with personal motivations to possess your
hand. This usually involves some sort of mutually beneficial pact or agreement. Your possessed hand
helps you in numerous ways, but its periodic, uncontrolled antics are distracting; you take a
permanent -2 penalty on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks once you gain this feat.

Any attack you make with a one-handed weapon, light weapon, unarmed strike, or natural attack with
your possessed hand gains a +1 insight bonus on the attack roll and damage roll. You also gain a +1
insight bonus on Disable Device and Sleight of Hand checks. Once per day as a swift action, you can
retrieve any stowed object you carry on your person, provided the object weighs no more than 5
pounds.
