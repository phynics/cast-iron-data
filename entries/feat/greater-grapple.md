## Greater Grapple (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 125

Maintaining a grapple is second nature to you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +6, Dex 13.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +2 bonus on checks made to [grapple](ref/misc/grapple) a foe. This bonus
stacks with the bonus granted by Improved Grapple. Once you have grappled a creature, maintaining
the grapple is a move action. This feat allows you to make two grapple checks each round (to move,
harm, or pin your opponent), but you are not required to make two checks. You only need to succeed
at one of these checks to maintain the grapple.\
**Normal:** Maintaining a grapple is a standard action.
