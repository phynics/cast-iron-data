## Bleeding Stare (Combat, Stare)

Your stare causes your foe to bleed out of its eyes.\
**Prerequisites:** Mesmerist level 5th, painful stare class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you trigger your painful stare, the target takes an amount of
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage equal to 1/3 your mesmerist level. Bleed damage from multiple uses of
Bleeding Stare doesn't stack.
