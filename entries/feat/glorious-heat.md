**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 212, Faiths of Purity pg 25

When you cast divine fire spells, their heat empowers nearby allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast divine spells, caster level 5th, worshiper of
[Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a divine spell with the fire descriptor, choose a single ally within
30 feet that you can see. That ally heals a number of hit points equal to half your level and gains
a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
