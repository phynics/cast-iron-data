## Sun Striker (Combat, Critical)

Called by the Dawnflower to strike down the undead, your scimitar becomes an agent of her burning
will.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), character level 7th, worshiper of
[Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit against an undead creature, your attack bypasses
all DR, deals full damage to incorporeal creatures, and acts as though it has the
[flaming burst](ref/magic-enh/flaming-burst) weapon special ability.
