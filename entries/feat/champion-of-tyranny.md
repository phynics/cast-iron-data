## Champion of Tyranny (Alignment)

You must beat down the masses to have true order.\
**Prerequisites:** 10 Hit Dice, lawful evil alignment.\
**Benefit:** You can store a number of [affirmations](ref/rule/shifts-and-affirmations) up to your
Charisma bonus (minimum 1) to use at any time, not just within the next 24 hours. You can use an
affirmation to cast [hold person](ref/spell/hold-person) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as a standard action, with a caster level equal
to your Hit Dice. You gain a +2 bonus on weapon and spell damage rolls against chaotic and good
creatures (or +4 if the creature is both chaotic and good). This is an alignment-based effect.\
**Residual:** If you have this feat but you are no longer lawful evil, you continue to gain the +2
bonus on weapon and spell damage rolls against chaotic and good creatures (or a +4 bonus if the
creature is both chaotic and good).
