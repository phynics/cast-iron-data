## Selective Spell (Metamagic)

Your allies need not fear friendly fire.\
**Prerequisites:** Spellcraft 10 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When casting a selective spell with an area effect and a duration of instantaneous, you
can choose a number of targets in the area equal to the ability score modifier used to determine
bonus spells of the same type (Charisma for antipaladins, bards, bloodragers, mediums, mesmerists,
oracles, paladins, skalds, sorcerers, and summoners; Intelligence for arcanists, occultists,
psychics, witches, and wizards; Wisdom for adepts, clerics, druids, hunters, inquisitors, rangers,
shamans, spiritualists, and warpriests). These targets are excluded from the effects of your spell.
A selective spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell's actual level.

Spells that do not have an area of effect do not benefit from this feat.
