pg 218

You have trained to channel your magical energies in ways that do not harm the natural world around
you.\
**Prerequisites:** Follower of the [Green Faith](ref/faith/green-faith).\
**Benefit:** Spells you cast that deal damage,
[channel negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), or otherwise harm life do not hurt normal or
magical plants. In addition, whenever you cast a spell that utilizes, heals, or enhances normal or
magical plants (such as [entangle](ref/spell/entangle) or [plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth)),
you cast the spell at +1 caster level.
