**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 121

You learn quickly from past mistakes.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are allowed to reroll an ability check, a skill check, or a saving throw,
you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on the reroll.

### Mythic Strong Comeback

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 75\
You never make the same mistake twice.\
**Prerequisites:** Strong Comeback.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you're allowed to reroll an ability check, a skill check, or a saving throw,
roll two dice and take the higher result, before adding the bonus from Strong Comeback.
