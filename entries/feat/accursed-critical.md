## Accursed Critical (Critical)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 142

Your spells carry an embedded curse that manifests when they strike true.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), ability to cast
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse) or [major curse](ref/spell/curse-major), caster level 9th.\
**Benefit:** When you confirm a critical hit with a spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), you may cast
_[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ or _[major curse](ref/spell/curse-major)_ on that target as
an immediate action. This works even with ranged spells. You must have
_[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ or _[major curse](ref/spell/curse-major)_ prepared or
otherwise available to cast, and using this ability casts the corresponding spell.
