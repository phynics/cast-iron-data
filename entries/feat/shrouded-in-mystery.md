**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 209

Common folk have many superstitious rumors about your kind - tall tales born out of ignorance and
fear alike. Whether or not what they say about your race is true is irrelevant, as you use their
beliefs to take advantage of them whenever you can.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 1 rank; Disguise 1 rank; [kitsune](ref/race/kitsune),
[nagaji](ref/race/nagaji), [samsaran](ref/race/samsaran), [tengu](ref/race/tengu), or
[wayang](ref/race/wayang).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 insight bonus on Will saves against mind-affecting spells and effects and
a +3 insight bonus on Bluff checks. Both benefits apply only against humanoid creatures of races
other than your own.
