## Acrobatic Spellcaster (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 75

Your skillful movements prevent foes from disrupting your spells.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Casting](ref/feat/combat-casting), [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus)
(Acrobatics).\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at an Acrobatics check to move through a threatened square without
provoking attacks of opportunity or to move through an enemy's space, creatures denied attacks of
opportunity by your Acrobatics check also cannot make attacks of opportunity against you when you
cast spells for the remainder of your turn.\
**Normal:** Casting a spell within an enemy's reach provokes attacks of opportunity even after you
succeed at an Acrobatics check to move through a threatened square.
