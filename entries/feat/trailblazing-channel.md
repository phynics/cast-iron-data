Your positive energy makes trailblazers of those you heal.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 3d6, worshiper of
[Desna](ref/faith/desna).\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you channel positive energy, you can cause healed creatures to be
unimpeded by [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) for a number of minutes equal to
your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 minute).
