You're so attuned to the earth that your skin can harden to the thickness of stone.\
**Prerequisites:** [Stone giant](ref/monster/giant-stone-giant).\
**Benefit:** Your natural armor bonus increases by 1. Once per day, you can use
[stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin) (on yourself only) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Your caster level is equal to 1/2 your Hit Dice.\
**Special:** You can take this feat up to three times. Each additional time you take this feat, the
number of times you can use _[stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin)_ per day increases by one.
