## Sea Hunter (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 195

Your blows knock swimming opponents off balance.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), merfolk.\
**Benefit:** When you make a successful melee attack against a swimming target, as a free action you
can attempt to knock the target off balance. Treat this as a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver.
If you succeed, the target is considered [off-balance](ref/rule/off-balance-and-prone-underwater)
until it recovers its balance, usually by making a Swim check on its turn. This feat has no effect
on creatures with a swim speed, those using magic such as freedom of movement, and creatures that
can't be tripped.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 130\
When you successfully knock a target off balance while using this feat, you can spend up to 5
stamina points to apply a penalty on the target's Swim checks to recover its balance from the effect
equal to the number of stamina points you spent.
