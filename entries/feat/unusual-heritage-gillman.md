Your heritage is strange or difficult to trace, and people fear your otherworldly powers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Gillman](ref/race/gillman).\
**Benefit:** As a so-called "Low Azlanti," you have abilities supposedly tied to mysterious
ancestors who still watch over you and your ilk. Your divination spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) manifest at 1 caster level higher. In addition,
once per day while fully immersed in water, you can cast [augury](ref/spell/augury) as a spell-like
ability.
