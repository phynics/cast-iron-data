A derro magister's mind is particularly affected by the consumption of strange mold, granting
enhanced resistance to magic and additional [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) and
traits.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 20, [derro](ref/monster/derro).\
**Benefit:** A derro magister's [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equals the derro's Hit
Dice +10. [Heal](ref/skill/heal) is always a class skill for a derro magister, and it gains a +4
racial bonus on all Heal checks.

A derro with this feat treats its caster level for all
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as equal to his Hit Dice. In addition, as soon
as a derro with this feat achieves a total of 6 Hit Dice by gaining enough levels in any combination
of classes, it gains the use of additional spell-like abilities as detailed below.

> _At will_-[levitate](ref/spell/levitate)\
> _1/day_-[deep slumber](ref/spell/deep-slumber), [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory)
