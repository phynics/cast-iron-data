You have learned secrets of woodworking from dryads.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [gathlain](ref/race/gathlain).\
**Benefit:** You can cast [wood shape](ref/spell/wood-shape) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) at will, but only on a piece of wood no larger
than 1 pound. You gain a +4 bonus on Craft checks involving wood, and you are always treated as if
you had [masterwork artisan's woodworking tools](ref/eq-misc/artisans-tools-masterwork) when
attempting such checks.
