**Sources:** Goblins of Golarion pg 24

Embracing fear and hatred of your ancestral foes allows you to make particularly telling blows.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin), Handle Animal 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 morale bonus on all attack and damage rolls made against
[dogs](ref/monster/dog) and [horses](ref/monster/horse) (including dog-like and horse-like targets
such a [nightmares](ref/monster/nightmare) or [worgs](ref/monster/worg)). Additionally, you gain a
+2 bonus on rolls to confirm critical hits against these foes.
