Your ranger traps and land mines occupy a larger area.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to use ranger traps or [land mines](ref/arc-alchemist/trap-breaker).\
**Benefit:** You can designate any ranger trap or land mine you lay to occupy up to 4 contiguous
squares of your choice. When the trap is activated, the effect is centered on whichever square the
triggering creature first entered. If more than one square is entered simultaneously, choose at
random.
