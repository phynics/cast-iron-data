You are adept at spellcasting when threatened or distracted.\
**Benefit:** You get a +4 bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks made to cast a
spell or use a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) when casting on the defensive or
while grappled.
