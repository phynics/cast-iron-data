## Painful Collision (Combat)

You shove your opponents into each other, causing painful and damaging collisions.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack).\
**Benefit:** When you [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) an enemy into another creature, both creatures
take 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage, plus an additional 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage for every
5 feet your original target has moved as a result of your bull rush. For instance, if you
successfully bull rush a goblin 10 feet and it then collides with another goblin, both creatures
would take 3d6 points of bludgeoning damage.
