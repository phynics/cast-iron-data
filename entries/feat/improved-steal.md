## Improved Steal (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 163

You have a knack for snatching items from your opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise).\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a [steal](ref/rule/steal)
combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks made to steal an item from a foe. You
also receive a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense when an opponent tries to steal an item from
you.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a steal combat maneuver.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 124\
You can select this feat even if you don't meet the ability score prerequisite (Intelligence 13).
You gain the benefits of this feat only as long as you have at least 1 stamina point in your stamina
pool. When you are the target of a steal combat maneuver, you can spend a number of stamina points
up to your Strength or Dexterity bonus, whichever is greater. Your CMD against that steal attempt
increases by that number.

### Mythic Improved Steal

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 67\
Your light fingers can lift even the most closely held item off an enemy.\
**Prerequisites:** Improved Steal.\
**Benefit:** You gain a bonus equal to half your tier on checks to attempt a steal combat maneuver,
and to your CMD when an opponent attempts a steal combat maneuver on you. These bonuses stack with
those granted by Improved Steal.

Furthermore, you can make an attack of opportunity against any creature that attempts a steal
maneuver against, you unless it also has this feat.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat **may** be removed, replaced with the new [Deft Maneuvers](ref/rule/deft-maneuvers) feat.
