Your vines can wrap around foes you grapple.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +6,
[gathlain](ref/race/gathlain).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple), and your
wings count as a free hand for the purpose of grappling a foe. You can maintain a grapple with your
wings and still make attacks with your other limbs. You cannot fly while maintaining a grapple with
your wings.
