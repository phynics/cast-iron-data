## Titan Strike (Mythic)

Your fists can fell titanic foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mythic Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** Your unarmed strike deals damage as if you were
[one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments). You also gain a +1 bonus for each size
category that your target is larger than you on the following: [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush),
[drag](ref/rule/drag), [grapple](ref/rule/grapple), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun),
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder), and [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver checks and the DC of your
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist).
