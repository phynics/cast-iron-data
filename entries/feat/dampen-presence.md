Your habitual stillness makes you difficult to perceive for creatures that use
[blindsight](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) or blindsense.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Stealth), Stealth 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You may use the Stealth skill to hide from any creature attempting to perceive you
using [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) or [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense), even if you are clearly
in that creature's perceptual field. This feat does not confer any advantages against other forms of
perception, such as [scent](ref/umr/scent), vision, or [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense).
