**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 100

You possess some of the dominating powers of your vampire progenitor.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 17, [dhampir](ref/race/dhampir).\
**Benefit:** You can take 20 on any Charisma-based skills to charm, convince, persuade, or seduce
humanoids whose attitude is at least friendly to you. Taking 20 still requires 20 times the normal
time to perform the skill.\
**Normal:** You cannot take 20 on any check where you incur penalties for failure.
