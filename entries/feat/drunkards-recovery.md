**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 82

You need only a swallow of strong drink to bring you back from the brink of death.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13.\
**Benefit:** If you are [dying](ref/misc/dying) and a creature gives you at least a sip of alcohol
(a standard action for an adjacent creature), you immediately stabilize.
