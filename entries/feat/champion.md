## Champion (Story)

**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 67

You must prove yourself through single combat.\
**Prerequisites:** You must have defeated a single challenging foe without any aid from another, or
have the [Competition Champion](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more) major childhood event
background, or the Champion of a God (barbarian), Champion of the People (cavalier), or Gladiator
(fighter) [class background](ref/rule/class-background-tables).\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can declare a single combat challenge to one foe within 50 feet
and in line of sight. Upon doing so, you gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and a +1 dodge bonus to AC
against that foe as long as no one else threatens that opponent or until the single combat challenge
ends. If another combatant attacks you or your foe, the challenge ends and you take a -2 penalty on
attack rolls and to AC for 1 round. Though you can declare a single combat challenge at will, once
you declare it on a foe you can't declare it on the same foe for another 24 hours.\
**Goal:** Defeat an appropriate number of challenging foes in single combat. These combats must not
be interrupted by other creatures, and the foes must not have already been substantially injured or
impaired prior to combat with you.\
**Completion Benefit:** Your bonuses for single combat increase to +2. In addition, any confirmed
critical hits you make against such a foe deal an additional 1d6 points of damage.
