## Thunder and Fang (Combat)

You have mastered the ancient Shoanti Thunder and Fang fighting style, allowing you to fight with
increased effectiveness when wielding an [earth breaker](ref/eq-weapon/earth-breaker) and
[klar](ref/eq-armor/klar).\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (earth breaker), Weapon Focus (klar).\
**Benefit:** You can use an earth breaker as though it were a one-handed weapon. When using an earth
breaker in one hand and a klar in your off hand, you retain the shield bonus your klar grants to
your Armor Class even when you use it to attack. Treat your klar as a light weapon for the purposes
of determining your two-weapon fighting penalty.\
**Normal:** An earth breaker is a two-handed weapon, preventing the use of a klar in one hand
without imposing penalties for using the earth breaker one-handed. A klar can be used either as a
one-handed weapon or a shield; it does not grant a bonus to AC during rounds in which it is used as
a weapon.\
**Special:** This is a combat feat, and may be selected with class features that grant bonus combat
feats.
