**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 71

The magistrates of death can breathe death and decay. You were in the presence of a final judge once
and your soul remembers that moment still.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 5d6,
[duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker), ghost hunter racial trait.\
**Benefit:** When you channel positive energy to harm undead, you can expend your ghost hunter
racial ability as a free action. Your channeled energy takes the form of spectral beetles and other
insectile scavengers, dealing 50% more damage to the undead. You also gain an additional daily use
of ghost hunter.
