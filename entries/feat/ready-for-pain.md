**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 138

Your implanted trick hardens the body against damage.\
**Prerequisites:** Mesmerist trick class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you implant a trick, you can grant the subject DR 1/- against nonlethal damage
until the trick is triggered or its duration ends. This DR increases by 1 for every 5 mesmerist
levels you possess.
