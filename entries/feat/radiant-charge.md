When you charge, you do so with the power of faith.\
**Prerequisites:** [Lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you hit with a [charge](ref/rule/charge) attack, you can expend all of your
remaining uses of lay on hands to deal extra damage equal to 1d6 per use of lay on hands expended +
your Charisma bonus. This damage comes from holy power and is not subject to
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), energy immunities, or energy resistances.
