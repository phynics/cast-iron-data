## Stick-Fighting Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 12

You know how to use batons, clubs, and sticks in new ways.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** When fighting with a [club](ref/eq-weapon/club),
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), [dan bong](ref/eq-weapon/dan-bong),
[sap](ref/eq-weapon/sap), or [tonfa](ref/eq-weapon/tonfa) with which you are proficient, you gain a
+1 enhancement bonus on attack rolls as though it were a
[masterwork weapon](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons). If the weapon actually is a masterwork weapon, it
instead counts as having the [blocking](ref/misc/blocking), [disarm](ref/misc/disarm),
[distracting](ref/misc/distracting), [performance](ref/misc/performance), and [trip](ref/misc/trip)
special weapon qualities, even if the weapon normally doesn't have them.
