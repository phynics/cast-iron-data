You gain an additional focus power from your implements.\
**Prerequisites:** Focus power class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain one additional focus power from among those available from your chosen
[implement schools](ref/ability/implements). You must select a power for which you normally qualify.\
**Special:** You can choose this feat once for every implement school you know, up to a maximum of
seven times at 18th level. Each time you do, you must choose a different focus power. If you have
selected the same implement school more than once, you can select a focus power from that school
once for each time you selected that school.
