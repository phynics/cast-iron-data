By focusing on your knowledge of magic and spells that negate its powers, you use your bare hands to
rip magical defenses from your enemy.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus
+11, ability to cast [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), caster level 7th.\
**Benefit:** If you have _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ prepared or can cast it
spontaneously, you can cast it as a swift action after hitting an opponent with an unarmed strike.
Treat this as a targeted dispel against the opponent you hit.
