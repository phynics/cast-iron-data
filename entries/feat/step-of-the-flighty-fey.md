**Sources:** Legacy of the First World pg 8

Your [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) taps the magic and resilience of the First
World.\
**Prerequisites:** [Gathlain](ref/race/gathlain), feather step spell-like ability.\
**Benefit:** A creature under the effect of your _[feather step](ref/spell/feather-step)_ spell-like
ability gains DR 1/cold iron for the duration of the effect. If you have at least 8 Hit Dice, the
creature instead gains DR 2/cold iron, and if you have at least 16 Hit Dice, the creature instead
gains DR 3/cold iron.
