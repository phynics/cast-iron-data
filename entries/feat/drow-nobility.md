Your blood courses with power, granting you greater
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Prerequisites:** Able to use drow spell-like abilities, [drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** You may use [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) as a spell-like ability at will, and
add feather fall and levitate to the spell-like abilities that you may use once each per day. Your
caster level is equal to your character level.
