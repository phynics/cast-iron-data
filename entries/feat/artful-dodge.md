## Artful Dodge (Combat)

**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 7

You are practiced at avoiding attacks when outnumbered.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13.\
**Benefit:** If you are the only character threatening an opponent, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC
against that opponent.\
**Special:** The Artful Dodge feat acts as the [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge) feat for the purpose of
satisfying prerequisites that require Dodge. You can use Intelligence, rather than Dexterity, for
feats with a minimum Dexterity prerequisite.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 20\
You can spend 5 stamina points to gain a +1 dodge bonus for each opponent that you threaten that no
other ally threatens.
