Creatures affected by your channel energy are not affected by difficult terrain.\
**Prerequisites:** [Animal](ref/domain/animal) or [Plant](ref/domain/plant) domain;
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you expend one additional use when you channel energy, you enable affected
creatures to move freely through undergrowth as per the druid's
[woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride) class feature. This effect lasts a for number of
minutes equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Multiple uses of this feat extend the duration
of the woodland stride effect.
