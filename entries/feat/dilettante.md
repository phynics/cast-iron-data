**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 17

What some would call dabbling you call a wide range of interests. Your breadth of knowledge offers
unexpected insights in many situations.\
**Prerequisites:** 2 ranks each in 5 different Knowledge skills.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Knowledge checks if you have 1-5 ranks in that skill. This bonus
does not stack with [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus). You can make untrained Knowledge checks
with DCs up to 15.\
**Normal:** You may only make untrained Knowledge checks for common knowledge (DC 10 or lower).
