## Arc Slinger (Combat)

You can twirl your sling in a way that maximizes its effectiveness.\
**Prerequisites:** [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot), proficient with
[sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling) or [halfling sling staff](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-sling-staff).\
**Benefit:** When using a sling or sling staff, you reduce your penalty on ranged attack rolls due
to range by 2. Point-Blank Shot's damage bonus applies within the first normal range increment of
your sling (50 feet) or sling staff (80 feet).
