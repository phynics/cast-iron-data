**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 92

You can expend a large portion of your channeling power to reverse death itself.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 6d6 (positive energy).\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity, you can expend three uses
of your channel energy class feature to restore a dead creature to life as if you had cast the
[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life) spell.
