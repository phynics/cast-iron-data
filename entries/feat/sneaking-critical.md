## Sneaking Critical (Combat, Critical)

**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 11

Your critical hits are particularly deadly.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), base attack bonus +9,
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit on a sneak attack, you can roll an additional
number of sneak attack dice equal to your weapon's critical modifier.\
**Special:** You can apply the effects of only one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
possess Critical Mastery.
