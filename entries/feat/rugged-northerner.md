**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 133, Inner Sea World Guide pg 288

You live a hard life in a cold climate, and after enduring the hardships of many brutal winters,
you've gained some benefit from the frozen environs.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, Survival 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You treat [extreme cold conditions](ref/rule/cold-dangers) as severe cold, and severe
cold as cold weather conditions. You are not impacted at all by normal cold weather conditions. In
addition, you do not become fatigued by frostbite or hypothermia.
