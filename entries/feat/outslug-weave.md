## Outslug Weave (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 16

You land precise attacks from afar.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13; [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) or brawler's cunning class
feature; [Lunge](ref/feat/lunge); [Outslug Style](ref/feat/outslug-style);
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** While using Outslug Style, you don't take a -2 penalty to your AC when using the Lunge
feat. The bonuses to AC and on weapon damage rolls that you gain from Outslug Style increase to +2.
