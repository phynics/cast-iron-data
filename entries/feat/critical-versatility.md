## Critical Versatility (Combat)

An open mind and combat training grant versatility to your critical hits.\
**Prerequisites:** Fighter level 11th, [human](ref/race/human).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can spend 1 hour practicing maneuvers to gain one single critical
feat that you meet the prerequisites for. You gain the benefits of the chosen critical feat until
you choose to practice a different critical feat.
