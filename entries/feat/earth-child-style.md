## Earth Child Style (Combat, Style)

Your martial training makes you a dangerous and elusive target for giants.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf) or [gnome](ref/race/gnome), defensive training
racial trait, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Acrobatics 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While using this style, your defensive training dodge bonus to AC increases to +6.
Further, against creatures of the giant subtype, you can add your Wisdom bonus on your unarmed
strike damage rolls.
