By using lay on hands, you can bring the dead back to life.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 19, [Greater Mercy](ref/feat/greater-mercy),
[lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands), mercy class feature.\
**Benefit:** You can expend 10 uses of lay on hands to bring a single dead creature you touch back
to life as a [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) spell with a caster level equal to your paladin
level. You must provide the material component for _[raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead)_ or choose to
accept 1 temporary [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels); this level
automatically goes away after 24 hours, never becomes a permanent negative level, and cannot be
overcome in any way except by waiting for the duration to expire.
