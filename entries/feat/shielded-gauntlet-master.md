## Shielded Gauntlet Master (Combat)

Your can effectively attack and defend with your gauntlet.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shielded Gauntlet Attack](ref/feat/shielded-gauntlet-attack),
[Shield Gauntlet Style](ref/feat/shield-gauntlet-style), proficiency with bucklers and light
shields.\
**Benefit:** While using Shield Gauntlet Style, you no longer lose your shield bonus to AC when you
attack with your gauntlet (or spiked gauntlet) or use it to hold a weapon. In addition, you add your
gauntlet's enhancement bonus to the shield bonus to AC granted by this feat as if it were a shield
enhancement bonus.
