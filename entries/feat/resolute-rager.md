Fear passes quickly while you are raging.\
**Prerequisites:** [Orc](ref/race/orc), rage class feature.\
**Benefit:** While raging, when under the effect of a fear effect that allows a saving throw, you
can make a new saving throw against that fear effect at the start of each of your turns before
acting. If you make the new save, the fear effect ends.
