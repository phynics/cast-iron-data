Little can shake your peaceful temperament.\
**Prerequisites:** Bleachling racial trait, [gnome](ref/race/gnome).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 racial bonus on saving throws against spells and effects with the emotion
descriptor. Effects with the fear descriptor or that grant you a morale bonus affect you for only
half the normal duration (minimum 1 round), and such effects that you can activate on yourself (such
as a barbarian's rage) require you to expend daily uses or rounds of those abilities at twice the
normal rate.
