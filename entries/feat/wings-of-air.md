The winds lift you, carrying you where you want to go.\
**Prerequisites:** [Airy Step](ref/feat/airy-step), character level 9th, [sylph](ref/race/sylph).\
**Benefit:** Your bonus on saves against effects with the air or electricity descriptors and effects
that deal electricity damage increases to +4. In addition, you gain a supernatural fly speed equal
to your base speed (good maneuverability). You may only fly with this ability when wearing light
armor or no armor.
