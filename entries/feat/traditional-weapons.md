## Traditional Weapons (Combat)

**Sources:** Path of the Hellknight pg 45

You're a master of utilizing simple, time-trusted arms against strange or technological weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks against creatures wielding exotic
weapons, firearms, or technological weapons. You also receive a +2 bonus to your CMD whenever an
opponent attempts any combat maneuver against you while wielding such a weapon. You gain these
bonuses only if you are not wielding an exotic weapon, firearm, or technological weapon.
