## Deadhand Initiate (Combat)

**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 84

Your adulation of death not only shields you from your fears, it allows you to feed on them.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 19, [Deadhand Style](ref/feat/deadhand-style),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Knowledge (religion) 6 ranks, ki pool
class feature, nongood alignment.\
**Benefit:** While using Deadhand Style, as long as you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, your
bonus on saving throws against fear effects increases to +4, and the DC of Intimidate checks
attempted against you increases by 8.

Additionally, if an enemy targets you with a fear effect, you can spend 1 point from your ki pool as
an immediate action to gain a number of [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal
to twice your Hit Dice.
