You have a broad interest in Osirion and are something of an authority in one specialized field.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (history) 1 rank, Knowledge (local) 1 rank, must be able to speak
Osiriani and Ancient Osiriani.\
**Benefit:** Pick one Intelligence-based skill. You gain a +3 bonus on all checks made using that
skill in relation to Osirion or its people. In addition, you gain a +1 bonus on all other
Intelligence-based skill checks made in relation to Osirion or its people.
