**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 114

An opponent can do little to evade your wrathful pitching of weapons and objects.\
**Prerequisites:** Rage class feature, [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything).\
**Benefit:** While raging, you can throw a two-handed weapon as a standard action, and you double
the range increment for weapons you throw. If you also have the [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw)
feat, you can throw two-handed weapons at your full normal rate of attacks. Further, you can pick up
an unattended object that you can use as an [improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) within
your reach as part of the attack action to throw that item.
