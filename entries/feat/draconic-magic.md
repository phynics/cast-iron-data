Your draconic aspect manifests as magical might.\
**Prerequisites:** [Draconic Aspect](ref/feat/draconic-aspect), [kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** You gain a group of [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) based on the
color of your dragon aspect. Each spell-like ability can be used once per day, and the DC of any of
these spell-like abilities is 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + Charisma modifier.

> **Black:** [Corrosive touch](ref/spell/corrosive-touch)†, [darkness](ref/spell/darkness).
>
> **Blue:** [Minor image](ref/spell/minor-image), [shocking grasp](ref/spell/shocking-grasp)†.
>
> **Green:** [Entangle](ref/spell/entangle)†, [gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind).
>
> **Red:** [Burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands)†, [pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics).
>
> **White:** _Burning hands†_ (deals cold damage instead of fire), [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud).

**Special:** You can use this feat instead of [Draconic Breath](ref/feat/draconic-breath) or
[Draconic Glide](ref/feat/draconic-glide) to qualify for the
[Draconic Paragon](ref/feat/draconic-paragon) feat. If you have this feat and Draconic Paragon, you
can use the [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) marked with a dagger (†) twice per
day.
