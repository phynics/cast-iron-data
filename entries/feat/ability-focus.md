## Ability Focus (Monster)

**Sources:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary pg 314

One of this creature's special attacks is particularly difficult to resist.\
**Prerequisites:** Special attack.\
**Benefit:** Choose one of the creature's special attacks. Add +2 to the DC for all saving throws
against the special attack on which the creature focuses.\
**Special:** A creature can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time the
creature takes the feat, it applies to a different special attack.
