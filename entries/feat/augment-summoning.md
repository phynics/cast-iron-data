**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 118

Your summoned creatures are more powerful and robust.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (conjuration).\
**Benefit:** Each creature you conjure with any
_[summon](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/summon)_ spell gains a +4 enhancement
bonus to Strength and Constitution for the duration of the spell that summoned it.

### Mythic Augment Summoning

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 60\
Your summoning spells bring not mere ordinary beasts, but instead mythic creatures.\
**Prerequisites:** Augment Summoning.\
**Benefit:** Any creature you summon using a
_[summon](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/summon)_ spell is considered mythic for
the purpose of interacting with other mythic creatures. It doesn't gain any mythic abilities or
power, but is affected by mythic spells and abilities as if it were a 1st-tier mythic creature.
Additionally, if the creature summoned has [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), its damage
reduction becomes DR/epic.
