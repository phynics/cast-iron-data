## Verbose Performer (Faction)

**Sources:** Faction Guide pg 58

The range of your bardic performances increases.\
**Prerequisites:** Bardic performance class feature,
[Extra Performance](ref/feat/extra-performance), [Master Performer](ref/feat/master-performer),
[bard](ref/class/bard) level 8th, [Kitharodian Academy](ref/rule/f-bards) 15 TPA.\
**Benefit:** Double the range of any bardic performance ability that possesses a range in the
ability description.
