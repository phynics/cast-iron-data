**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 20

Half-drow tap into inner reserves, fighting even more fiercely when losing.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, darkborn [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** When reduced to half of your maximum hit points or fewer, you gain a +1 morale bonus on
attack and damage rolls. This bonus increases to +2 at 8th level and to +3 at 15th level.
