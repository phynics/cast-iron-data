**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 207

Your foul patron's influence hastens death's approach.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature, worshiper of one of
the Four Horsemen.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can expend one of your uses of channel energy to emanate a
30-foot-radius aura of succumbing for a number of rounds equal to your effective cleric level.
[Dying](ref/misc/dying) creatures in the area take 1 point of damage per die of your normal channel
negative energy damage at the start of your turn each round. A successful Will save (DC equal to the
DC of your channel energy ability) halves this damage. Whenever a living creature dies while within
your aura of succumbing, you gain a number of [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points)
equal to that creature's Hit Dice. These temporary hit points last for 1 hour.
