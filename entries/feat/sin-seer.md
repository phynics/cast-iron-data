Unlike others who have taken the [oath against undeath](ref/oath/undeath), your preoccupation with
the undead does not cloud your view of good and evil.\
**Prerequisites:** Detect undead paladin class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain the _[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)_ class feature. You may use it or
the _[detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead)_ class feature, but not at the same time.
