## Encouraging Spell (Metamagic)

Your inspiration provides your allies with greater support.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, Diplomacy 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** Any morale bonus granted by an encouraging spell is increased by 1. An encouraging
spell uses up a spell slot 1 level higher than the spell's actual level.
