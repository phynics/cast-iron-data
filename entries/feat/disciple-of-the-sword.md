**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 210

Your dedication to mastering Iomedae's favored weapon puts other to shame.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (longsword), cleric or inquisitor level
4th, worshiper of [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae).\
**Benefit:** You gain the benefits of [Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/weapon-specialization)
(longsword), and you can use your cleric or inquisitor level in place of your fighter level for the
purpose of qualifying for [Greater Weapon Focus](ref/feat/greater-weapon-focus) (longsword) and
[Greater Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/greater-weapon-specialization) (longsword). You can use
this feat instead of Weapon Specialization (longsword) to qualify for Greater Weapon Specialization
(longsword).
