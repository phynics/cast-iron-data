## Soulblade (Combat)

You can't always count on having a source of positive energy to fight haunts, and have mastered an
eerie talent for briefly focusing your own life energy into your weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (religion) 1 rank, Perception 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can make a weapon attack against a [haunt](ref/rule/haunts).
If successful, your attack deals damage equal to your weapon dice plus any enhancement bonus (but
not other bonuses, such as those granted by Strength,
[Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/weapon-specialization), or class features like sneak attack). You
can use any [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike) feat in order to enhance this attack.
