## Strangler (UC) (Combat)

Throttling the life out of enemies is second nature to you.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +1d6,
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully maintain a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) and choose to deal
damage, you can spend a swift action to deal your sneak attack damage to the creature you are
grappling.
