You have an instinct for waiting until just the right moment to spring a hazard or trap.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (traps) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you manually trigger a trap against opponents, that trap receives either a +2
circumstance bonus on melee attack rolls or a +2 circumstance bonus to its saving throw DC.
