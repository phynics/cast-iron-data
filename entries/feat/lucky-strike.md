## Lucky Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 67

Your luck increases the potency of your weapon attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +5, adaptive luck racial trait, [halfling](ref/race/halfling).\
**Benefit:** Spend a use of your adaptive luck racial trait to reroll the damage from a single
weapon attack. You deal damage equal to the new damage roll, or the original roll, whichever is
greater.
