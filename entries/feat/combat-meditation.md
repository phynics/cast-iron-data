## Combat Meditation (Combat, Meditation)

Quick momentary meditation allows you to gain insight in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Meditation Master](ref/feat/meditation-master), character level 5th.\
**Benefit:** You can meditate as a full-round action. If you do, you gain all the benefits of your
meditation feats, but they last for only 1 round per character level. Combat meditation allows you
to expend the +1 insight bonus granted from the Meditation Master feat to instead roll twice when
attempting an ability check, attack roll, saving throw, or skill check, taking the better of the two
results. You must decide to use this ability before the initial roll is made. You may only use this
alternate ability in place of bonuses granted by the Meditation Master feat. You can perform combat
meditation a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier.
