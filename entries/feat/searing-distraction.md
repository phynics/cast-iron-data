## Searing Distraction (Combat)

You harry your foes with the light and heat of your weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard).\
**Benefit:** When you make an attack using a light source such as a [sunrod](ref/eq-misc/sunrod) or
[torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) as an [improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons), the target must
succeed at a Fortitude save or be [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 round. On a critical hit, the
target is instead [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round. The DC of the effect is 10 + half your
character level + your Dexterity modifier. Blind targets are immune to this effect.
