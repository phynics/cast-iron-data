The teachings of your deity help you keep your secrets safe.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 3rd, ability to cast at least one glamer or figment, worshiper of
[Sivanah](ref/faith/sivanah).\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you create a glamer or figment effect, you can empower the effect so
that the DC to disbelieve it is 2 higher than normal.
