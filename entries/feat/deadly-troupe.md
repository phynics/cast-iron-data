## Deadly Troupe (Teamwork)

You combine elements of dance and acting with your combat style to distract your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** Perform (act) or Perform (dance) 3 ranks, [human](ref/race/human) (Varisian).\
**Benefit:** When at least one ally who also has this feat is threatening a foe, you gain a +4
circumstance bonus against that foe on Bluff checks to feint, Bluff checks to create a diversion to
hide, and Acrobatics checks to move through that foe's threatened squares. You also gain a +2
circumstance bonus on [steal](ref/rule/steal) and [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat
maneuver checks made against that foe.
