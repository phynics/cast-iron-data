## Shark Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 59

You fight like a shark, shredding your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Swim 3 ranks, base
attack bonus +3 or monk level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** You can deal piercing or bludgeoning damage with your unarmed strikes. Your piercing
unarmed strikes and bite attacks deal an additional 1d6 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage.
