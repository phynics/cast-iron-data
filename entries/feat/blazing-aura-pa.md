## Blazing Aura (PA) (Combat, Conduit)

You cover yourself in coils of flame drawn from the Plane of Fire.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can shroud yourself in fire. Until the end of your turn,
whenever a creature makes a successful melee attack against you, that creature takes a number of
points of fire damage equal to 1d6 plus half your ranks in Knowledge (planes); attacks made using
reach weapons ignore this effect. A creature can halve this fire damage with a successful Reflex
save (DC = 10 + half your level + your Constitution modifier).

You can use this feat's benefit a number of times per day equal to your ranks in Knowledge (planes).
If you have at least 9 ranks in Knowledge (planes), activating this ability is a move action. If you
have at least 15 ranks in Knowledge (planes), you can activate this ability as a move action or a
swift action.
