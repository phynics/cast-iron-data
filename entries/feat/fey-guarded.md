You have trained your mind to resist fey magic - both that cast by fey creatures and that cast by
other denizens of the First World.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (nature) or Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against enchantment and illusion spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) cast by creatures of the fey type.
