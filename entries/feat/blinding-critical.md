## Blinding Critical (Combat, Critical)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 119

Your critical hits blind your opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), base attack bonus +15.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you score a critical hit, your opponent is permanently
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded). A successful Fortitude save reduces this to [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled)
for 1d4 rounds. The DC of this Fortitude save is equal to 10 + your base attack bonus. This feat has
no effect on creatures that do not rely on eyes for sight or creatures with more than two eyes
(although multiple critical hits might cause blindness, at the GM's discretion). Blindness can be
cured by [heal](ref/spell/heal), [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration),
[remove blindness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness), or similar abilities.\
**Special:** You can only apply the effects of one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
possess [Critical Mastery](ref/feat/critical-mastery).
