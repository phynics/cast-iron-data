## Breaker of Barriers (Combat)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 208

You see it as your sacred duty to destroy all obstacles.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 17, worshiper of [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Strength checks to break down doors, walls, dams, and other
barriers and on combat maneuver checks to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) and
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) opponents. You ignore the first 5 points of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
when you deal damage to doors, walls, dams, and other barriers.
