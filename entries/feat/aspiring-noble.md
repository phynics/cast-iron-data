## Aspiring Noble (Story)

You strive to be seen as a legitimate noble.\
**Prerequisites:** Must be a member of an established family that is yet unrecognized as nobility.\
**Benefit:** You have learned to further your name among the people. Choose a settlement and either
Diplomacy or Intimidate. While in your chosen settlement, you gain a +2 bonus on checks with the
chosen skill when dealing with the commoners of that settlement. If you have 10 or more ranks in the
chosen skill, the bonus increases to +4.\
**Goal:** Have your family recognized as nobility by either three established noble families or a
ruling monarch.\
**Completion Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on checks with your chosen skill when dealing with the
rest of the citizens of your chosen settlement. Your bonus to interact with commoners increases to
+4, or it increases to +6 if you have 10 or more ranks in your chosen skill.
