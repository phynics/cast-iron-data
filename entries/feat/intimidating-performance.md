Your performance strikes fear in the hearts of foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to start a performance (or raging song) as a move action, bardic
performance or [raging song](ref/ability/raging-song) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you start a performance (or a raging song) as a move or swift action, you can use
a standard action to demoralize a foe, attempting a Perform check appropriate to your performance in
place of the Intimidate check. Your performance must be one with an audible component.

If you can start a performance as a swift action and you have the
[Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display) feat, you can gain the benefit of Dazzling Display by
succeeding at a Perform check in place of an Intimidate check.
