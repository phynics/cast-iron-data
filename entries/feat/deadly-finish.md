## Deadly Finish (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 94

Your attacks don't just fell your opponents - they kill them outright.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** When you hit with a melee attack and reduce your opponent to -1 or fewer hit points,
you can force that opponent to succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 15 + the damage your attack dealt) or
die.
