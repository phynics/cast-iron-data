When you disguise yourself as a monstrous humanoid, you can gain some of its qualities.\
**Prerequisites:** [Monstrous Disguise](ref/feat/monstrous-disguise), Disguise 9 ranks, Knowledge
(nature) 9 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use the Monstrous Disguise feat to assume the shape of a monstrous humanoid,
you can gain a rudimentary version of one of that creature's abilities selected from the following
list: climb 10 feet, fly 20 feet (clumsy maneuverability), swim 20 feet,
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 3/magic, or resistance 5 to one energy type. Preparing
a disguise in this way takes 1 additional hour and costs an extra 500 gp. The bonus ability granted
by this feat has a 25% chance to cease functioning every hour after you apply the disguise. This
disguise automatically falls apart after 8 hours.
