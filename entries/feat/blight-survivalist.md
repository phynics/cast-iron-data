You have learned to survive in dangerous environments.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Survival).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +3 bonus on saving throws against environmental hazards and weather effects.\
**Special:** If you have the [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) class feature and the
blighted land being explored matches one of those terrains, you can choose to use your favored
terrain bonus in place of the +3 bonus granted by this feat.
