## Pure Legion Assault (Combat)

The Pure Legion trained you to apply your knowledge of religions to predicting and defeating divine
servitors.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 1 rank, Knowledge (religion) 1 rank,
[Pure Legion Recruit](ref/trait/pure-legion-recruit) trait.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against divine spells and effects and abilities
from outsiders. If you must attempt a saving throw against a divine spell, you gain a +1 bonus on
attack rolls against the creature that cast the spell
