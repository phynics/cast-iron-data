## Dazing Assault (Combat)

You can daze foes with wild attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** You can choose to take a -5 penalty on all melee attack rolls and combat maneuver
checks to [daze](ref/misc/dazed) opponents you hit with your melee attacks for 1 round, in addition
to the normal damage dealt by the attack. A successful Fortitude save negates the effect. The DC of
this save is 10 + your base attack bonus. You must choose to use this feat before making the attack
roll, and its effects last until your next turn.
