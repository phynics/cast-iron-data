## Lantern Glare (Combat)

Like an archon's aura, the light of your thrown weapons can shake even the fiercest of foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Lantern Light](ref/feat/lantern-light), [Lantern Style](ref/feat/lantern-style),
[Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot), good or lawful alignment, caster level 11th or monk level
11th.\
**Benefit:** Creatures you hit with a thrown weapon as well as creatures within 20 feet of your
target must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half your character level + your Charisma modifier) or
become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) until they damage you or 24 hours have passed. A creature that
succeeds at its saving throw or that damages you after becoming shaken becomes immune to this effect
for 24 hours.
