## Bulette Rampage (Combat)

You are able to channel your enhanced momentum to deal incredible damage to multiple foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Bulette Charge Style](ref/feat/bulette-charge-style),
[Bulette Leap](ref/feat/bulette-leap), [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), proficiency with heavy armor.\
**Benefit:** While you're using Bulette Charge Style, whenever you succeed at an
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuver check against a foe, that foe takes an amount of damage
equal to 1d8 (if you're Medium) or 1d6 (if you're Small) + 1/2 your armor bonus to AC + 1-1/2 times
your Strength bonus.
