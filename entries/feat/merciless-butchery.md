## Merciless Butchery (Combat)

Your talent for slaughter lets you dispatch helpless opponents before they can recover.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dastardly Finish](ref/feat/dastardly-finish),
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +5d6, studied target class feature.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity, you can attempt a
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) against a [cowering](ref/misc/cowering),
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless), or [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) opponent that you have designated as
your studied target.
