**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 153

If an opponent knocks you down, you swiftly rebound with an attack.\
**Prerequisites:** Ki pool.\
**Benefit:** While you have at least 1 ki point in your ki pool, you can stand up as a swift action
that provokes attacks of opportunity.

You may spend 1 point of your ki pool to stand up as a swift action without provoking attacks of
opportunity.
