## Kyton Shield (Combat)

**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 88

Whether in your hand or draped around your body, the spiked chain serves as both weapon and shield.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (spiked
chain), [Kyton Style](ref/feat/kyton-style), base attack bonus +5, Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks, ki
pool class feature.\
**Benefit:** When using Kyton Style, you gain a +1 shield bonus to your Armor Class as long as you
are wielding a [spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain). If the spiked chain is magical, add 1/2
its enhancement bonus to this shield bonus. If the spiked chain has the vicious ability, instead add
its full enhancement bonus to the shield bonus.
