## Stunning Pin (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 122

You can render a pinned foe temporarily incapacitated.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you [pin](ref/rule/grapple) an opponent, you can spend a swift action to make
a Stunning Fist attempt against that opponent.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 133\
You can spend 5 stamina points to use Stunning Pin as a free action instead of a swift action.
