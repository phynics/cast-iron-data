You are so steadfast in the principles of your alignment, even magic has difficult swaying you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will),
[Unimpeachable Honor](ref/feat/unimpeachable-honor), lawful alignment.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are under the effect of a charm or compulsion effect cast by or coming
from a source with a chaotic alignment or an alignment on the good and evil axis opposite of your
alignment (evil if you are good, and good if you are evil) you gain a +2 on any saving throw or
opposed Charisma check to break free from the effect. This bonus stacks with itself and with the
bonus granted by Unimpeachable Honor.
