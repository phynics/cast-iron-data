## Pummeling Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 154

Your unarmed strikes weave together in an effortless combo, focusing on the spots you've weakened
with the last hit.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike); base attack bonus
+6, brawler's flurry class feature, or flurry of blows class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use a full-attack action or flurry of blows to make multiple attacks
against a single opponent with unarmed strikes, total the damage from all hits before applying
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). This ability works only with unarmed strikes, no
matter what other abilities you might possess.
