**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 91

You summon creatures whose very presence turns the stomach of your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (conjuration); ability to cast
[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) or [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally).\
**Benefit:** When you cast a _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ or
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell, you can choose to summon a single
creature that exudes a putrid odor or befouls the water surrounding it, granting it the
[stench](ref/umr/stench) universal monster ability. The duration of the
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) condition created by this stench is a number of rounds equal to the
spell's level. When performing a putrid summon, you summon a creature from the next lower list than
the spell you are casting. For instance, if you cast
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4), you summon a creature from the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) list.
