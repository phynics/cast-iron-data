You sup on the blood of your ancestors to fuel your fury.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15, [aasimar](ref/race/aasimar).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are adjacent to a good outsider that takes [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) or
[blood drain](ref/umr/blood-drain) damage, you gain 1
[temporary hit point](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) as you bathe in the celestial being's gore.
Furthermore, as a full-round action, you can feast on the fallen body of a good outsider that has
been dead no longer than 24 hours. When you do, you regain 1 temporary hit point per Hit Die the
outsider possessed at a rate of 1 hit point per minute. Temporary hit points gained from this feat
last 1d4 hours.
