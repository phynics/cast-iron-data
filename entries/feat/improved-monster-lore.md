You are obsessed with the abilities and weaknesses of monsters.\
**Prerequisites:** Monster lore class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain a sacred bonus on all skill checks to identify the abilities and weaknesses of
creatures equal to 1/2 your level in classes that grant you the monster lore class feature.
