You can build robots.\
**Prerequisites:**
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Technological Item](ref/feat/craft-technological-item),
[Technologist](ref/feat/technologist), Craft (mechanical) 9 ranks, Knowledge (engineering) 9 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can create [robots](ref/family/robot), provided you have access to a robotics lab
and the necessary materials. A robot's price is equal to its CR × 20,000 gp, and the cost to create
one is half this amount. Creating a robot takes 1 day for every 1,000 gp of its price. At the end of
the process you must attempt a single Craft (mechanical) skill check to finish the robot. Failing
this check means that the robot doesn't function and the materials and time are wasted. The DC for
this skill check is equal to 20 + the robot's CR (minimum 21). A newly created robot has average hit
points for its Hit Dice.\
**Special:** A [robotics lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories) is required to craft robots and uses
200 charges each day it is in operation.
