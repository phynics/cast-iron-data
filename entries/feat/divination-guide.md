You deity grants you greater guidance and divine insight.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 3rd, ability to cast [augury](ref/spell/augury) or
[divination](ref/spell/divination), must worship a deity.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you cast _[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ or _[divination](ref/spell/divination),_
you receive further guidance from your deity's agents. You gain a +1 sacred bonus if your worship a
good deity or a +1 profane bonus if you worship an evil deity (if you worship a neutral deity the
type of bonus is either determined by the type of energy you channel with the channel energy class
feature or, if you don't have that class ability, your choice upon taking this feat) on all saving
throws for 1 minute per caster level of the augury or divination spell you cast.
