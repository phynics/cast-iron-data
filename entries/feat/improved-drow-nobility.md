**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 105

Your magical heritage is more potent than that of your peers, as demonstrated by your superior
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [Drow Nobility](ref/feat/drow-nobility), able to use drow spell-like
abilities, [drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** You may use your [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights),
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire), [feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), and
[levitate](ref/spell/levitate) spell-like abilities twice per day. Your
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness) spell-like ability instead becomes
[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness), which you may use twice per day.
