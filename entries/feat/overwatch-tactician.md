## Overwatch Tactician (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 15

You quickly prepare to react to your enemies' actions.\
**Prerequisites:** [Overwatch Style](ref/feat/overwatch-style),
[Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot), [Rapid Shot](ref/feat/rapid-shot),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** While using Overwatch Style, you can ready two ranged attacks as a standard action,
rather than a full-round action.
