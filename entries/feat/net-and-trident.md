## Net and Trident (Combat)

Your skill with lighter weapons allows you to wield one alongside your net.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (net),
[Net Adept](ref/feat/net-adept), [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting).\
**Benefit:** You can treat a [net](ref/eq-weapon/net) as a one-handed ranged weapon, allowing you to
wield a light or one-handed melee weapon and still make ranged attacks with your net. When you use
your light or one-handed melee weapon to attack an entangled opponent, you gain a +2 bonus on damage
rolls and on attack rolls to confirm a critical hit.\
**Normal:** A net is a two-handed ranged weapon.
