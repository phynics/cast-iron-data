## Bulette Leap (Combat)

You can shove aside multiple opponents while leaping into the air.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Bulette Charge Style](ref/feat/bulette-charge-style),
[Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), proficiency
with heavy armor.\
**Benefit:** While using Bulette Charge Style, you gain a bonus on Acrobatics checks to jump equal
to your Strength bonus. Additionally, when you perform an [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat
maneuver, you can attempt to overrun multiple foes, but you take a cumulative -2 penalty on each
successive overrun attempt in the same round. If an overrun attempt fails, you cannot make any
further attempts until your next round.
