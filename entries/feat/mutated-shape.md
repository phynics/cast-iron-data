Your wild shape form gains an additional appendage you can use to attack your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 19, base attack bonus +6, wild shape class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you use wild shape, you grow an additional appendage of your choice that can be
used to make one of the following attacks: bite, claw, gore, slam, sting, or talons. The appendage
can be used as part of a full attack using your highest base attack bonus, and it deals damage as
described in the table below. This appendage lasts for as long as you stay in the same form with
this use of wild shape.
