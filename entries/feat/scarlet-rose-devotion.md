Through the bond of sisterhood, you can inspire your allies to overcome terrible threats.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bodyguard](ref/feat/bodyguard), [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
Perform (oratory) 3 ranks, any good alignment.\
**Benefit:** Whenever an ally within 30 feet attempts a saving throw, you can attempt a DC 10
Perform (oratory) check as an immediate action. If you are successful, the ally gains a +2 morale
bonus on her saving throw. This ability has no effect if your ally cannot hear you speak or
understand your words.
