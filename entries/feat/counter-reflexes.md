## Counter Reflexes (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 144

Your opponent's advanced training in moving around you is surpassed only by your readiness for it.\
**Prerequisites:** [Anticipate Dodge](ref/feat/anticipate-dodge), [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Mobility](ref/feat/mobility); base attack bonus +9, brawler level 6th, or monk level 6th.\
**Benefit:** Opponents with the Mobility feat do not gain the dodge bonus granted by that feat when
they provoke attacks of opportunity by moving out of or within your threatened area.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 116\
When an opponent with the Mobility feat moves out of or within your threatened area, you can spend 2
stamina points to gain a +4 bonus on your attack of opportunity.
