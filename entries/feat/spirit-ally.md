You have a close connection with a spiritual assistant capable of aiding you in small ways.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster Level 8th.\
**Benefit:** You gain the assistance of an invisible shapeless spirit. This acts as a constant
[unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant) spell, using your character level as your effective
caster level. For every 4 character levels you have, the spirit ally's effective Strength score
increases by 2 (to a maximum of 12 at 20th level). If the spirit ally dissipates or is dispelled, it
returns after 24 hours.
