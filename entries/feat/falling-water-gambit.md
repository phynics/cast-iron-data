## Falling Water Gambit (Combat)

You have integrated both the Roaring Falls and Shrouded Lake techniques of Aldori swordplay into a
seamless union.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aldori Dueling Disciple](ref/feat/aldori-dueling-disciple),
[Duelist of the Roaring Falls](ref/feat/duelist-of-the-roaring-falls),
[Duelist of the Shrouded Lake](ref/feat/duelist-of-the-shrouded-lake),
[Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (Aldori dueling sword),
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (Aldori dueling
sword), base attack bonus +8.\
**Benefit:** When attacking a creature denied its Dexterity bonus to AC because of your successful
feint, you increase the threat range of your
[Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword) by 1 (typically to 18-20/×2), and gain a
+2 bonus on attack rolls to confirm critical hits. After you make a
[dueling dodge](ref/rule/dueling-dodge) or succeed at a [dueling parry](ref/rule/dueling-parry)
during a duel, you can make an attack of opportunity against the foe who attacked you if that foe is
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken), [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked). To
gain these benefits, you must be wielding only an Aldori dueling sword (not using a shield, an
off-hand weapon, armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons).
