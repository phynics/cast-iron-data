## Cataract (Blood Hex)

**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 29

You can curse the eyes of a wounded enemy.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, Perception 5 ranks, Use Magic Device 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, you can curse a creature you've dealt damage to since the
beginning of your last turn. The creature must succeed at a Fortitude save or its eyes become milky
and its vision is obscured. For 1 round, any target the creature makes a ranged touch attack against
gains [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) for the purposes of that attack.\
**Special:** A shaman or witch with this [blood hex](ref/rule/feats-class) can use it against any
creature she has dealt damage to in the past minute. A shaman or witch can use this blood hex as a
standard action.
