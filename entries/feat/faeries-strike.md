## Faerie's Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 147

Ephemeral wisps of nature spirits dance about those you strike.\
**Prerequisites:** [Nature Magic](ref/feat/nature-magic) or the ability to cast druid or ranger
spells; [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use Vital Strike (or [Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike)
or [Greater Vital Strike](ref/feat/greater-vital-strike)), the creature you hit with the attack must
succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be illuminated
as if by [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire) for 1 minute. You can use this benefit a number of
times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1).

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 119\
When using this feat, you can spend up to 5 stamina points to increase the DC of the effect by an
amount equal to the number of stamina points you spent.
