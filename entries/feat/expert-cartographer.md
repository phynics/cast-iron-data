**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 110

You are skilled at drawing quality maps, making your cartographic works both more useful and more
valuable when sold.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (maps) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on Craft (maps) checks when creating a map, as well as 1 additional
[Discovery Point](ref/rule/discovery-and-exploration). If you succeed at the check by 5 or more, the
list price of the map increases by 20%.
