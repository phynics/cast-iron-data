Choose one [weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups) listed under the fighter's Weapon Training
class feature. **Prerequisites:** Proficiency with at least one weapon from the selected weapon
group, base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on all attack rolls you make using any weapon from the selected
group.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new weapon group.
