## Torch Bearer (Combat)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 217

You can make use of a torch as a deadly, mace-like weapon.\
**Benefit:** You treat a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) as a light weapon that deals bludgeoning damage
equal to that of a [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace) of its size plus 1 point of fire damage,
and you do not incur penalties as you would for using it as an
[improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons).\
**Normal:** A torch used in combat is treated as a one-handed improvised weapon that deals
bludgeoning damage equal to that of a gauntlet of its size plus 1 point of fire damage.
