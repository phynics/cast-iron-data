## Boar Shred (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 90

The wounds you inflict with your unarmed strikes bleed, giving you renewed vigor.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Boar Ferocity](ref/feat/boar-ferocity), [Boar Style](ref/feat/boar-style), Intimidate 9 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can make an Intimidate check to demoralize an opponent as a move action. While
using Boar Style, whenever you tear an opponent's flesh, once per round at the start of that
opponent's turn he takes 1d6 bleed damage. The bleed damage dealt while using Boar Style persist
even if you later switch to a different style.
