**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 142

You can smash traps instead of disarming them.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), Disable Device 1 rank,
[orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** You can attempt to disarm a trap by striking it with a melee weapon instead of making a
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) check. As a full-round action, make a melee attack
against an Armor Class equal to the trap's Disable Device DC. If you miss, the trap activates. If
you hit, roll damage. If this damage is at least half the trap's Disable Device DC, you disable the
trap. If this damage is less than half the trap's Disable Device DC, the trap activates. You can
only attempt this on nonmagical traps. You must be able to reach some part of the trap with your
attack in order to use this feat. At the GM's discretion, some traps may not be susceptible to this
feat.
