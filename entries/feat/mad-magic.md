## Mad Magic (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Origins pg 27

Magic is in your blood, allowing you to cast spells no matter how furious you become.\
**Prerequisites:** Bloodrage class feature or [perfect clarity](ref/ragepower/perfect-clarity) rage
power.\
**Benefit:** You can cast spells from any class that grants you spells while in a bloodrage, and you
keep your rage benefits when using [moment of clarity](ref/ragepower/moment-of-clarity) during a
rage. If you have the greater bloodrage class feature, you also gain a +1 bonus to the save DCs of
spells you cast while in a bloodrage.
