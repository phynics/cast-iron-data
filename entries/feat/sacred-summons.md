**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 155

The minions of your divine patrons stand ready to answer your call.\
**Prerequisites:** Aura class feature, ability to cast [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster).\
**Benefit:** When using _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ to summon creatures whose
alignment subtype or subtypes exactly match your aura, you may cast the spell as a standard action
instead of with a casting time of 1 round.
