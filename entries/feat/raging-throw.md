You expend some of your rage to throw one opponent at another.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, Con 13, rage class feature,
[Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base
attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** While raging, when you attempt a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) combat maneuver, you
can spend 1 additional round of your rage as a swift action to add your Constitution bonus on your
combat maneuver check to the bull rush. Further, if you bull rush an opponent into a square another
creature occupies or into a solid object, the opponent and the creature or object take bludgeoning
damage equal to your Strength modifier + your Constitution modifier.
