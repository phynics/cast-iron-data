You can call upon your connection to cloud dragons to veil your presence.\
**Prerequisites:** Mbe'ke [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can call upon your spiritual connection to
[cloud dragons](ref/family/dragon-primal-cloud) to create a thick mist as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The mist functions as
[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist), except it has a duration of only 1 round per level and
is only a 10-foot-radius spread that is 10 feet high.
