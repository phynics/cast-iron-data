## Secret Language (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Cohorts and Companions pg 23

You and your allies have worked out a complex set of codes and phrases that allow you to secretly
pass information back and forth.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 1 rank, Linguistics 1 rank, Sense Motive 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +5 circumstance bonus on Bluff checks made to pass secret messages to other
characters that have this feat, and can communicate as much information as you could normally say in
one round as a swift action.
