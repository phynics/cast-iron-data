**Sources:** Osirion, Land of the Pharaohs pg 28

You have learned the essential tricks of disguising your odor and keeping creatures from locating
you by your scent.\
**Prerequisites:** Stealth 3 ranks, Survival 3 ranks.^[1](ref/feat/conceal-scent#footnote-p)^\
**Benefit:** Creatures cannot use the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability to track you (though they can
still track you through standard means such as footprints). Creatures with scent can detect your
presence by smell at half the normal distance, but cannot pinpoint your location with scent.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-p}

This source was printed for 3.5; Pathfinder Society play reduced the skill rank prerequisites by 3.
consult your GM if you wish to use the lower prerequisites.
