You can use the movements of the sun, moon, stars, and planets to navigate and acquire eldritch
knowledge about the fundamental nature of reality.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Knowledge (geography) skill checks to learn about the stars or
planets. At night, as long as the sky is clear and you can see the stars, you cannot get lost. Once
per night when the stars are visible, you can reroll a Knowledge check you failed earlier in the
day. When you do so, you can choose to roll the appropriate Knowledge skill check again or you can
instead use your Knowledge (geography) skill to gain the same information.
