Your aura of courage becomes more potent, as your steadfast resolve is also manifested by your
allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Aura of courage class feature, caster level 8th.\
**Benefit:** Your aura of courage expands to a 20-foot-radius [emanation](ref/misc/emanation).
Allies within the aura are immune to fear effects.\
**Normal:** The aura of courage affects each ally within 10 feet, and grants a +4 morale bonus on
saving throws against fear effects.
