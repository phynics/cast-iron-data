## Loyal to the Death (Teamwork)

You're so loyal to your family or friends that you're willing to suffer for them.\
**Prerequisites:** [Human](ref/race/human) (Tian).\
**Benefit:** Whenever an opponent makes a melee or ranged attack against one of your allies who is
adjacent to you and who also has this feat, you can spend an immediate action to shield the ally
with your own body. You become the target of the attack, and the opponent gains a +4 bonus on her
attack roll. For the purpose of determining distance, [cover](ref/rule/cover) and
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment), you count as being in your ally's square when the attack is
resolved. If the attack misses you due to concealment, [blink](ref/spell/blink), or a similar
effect, it instead targets your ally as if you had not used this feat's ability.
