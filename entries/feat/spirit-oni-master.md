Through forbidden blood pacts, you have bound a [spirit oni](ref/monster/oni-spirit-oni) to your
service.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks; Lawful neutral, lawful evil, or neutral evil.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you wear a specially crafted oni mask (which costs 50 gp), you gain a primary
gore attack dealing 1d4 points of damage. If you have [poison use](ref/ability/poison-use), you can
apply poison to this gore attack as a move action.\
**Normal:** Applying poison to a weapon is a standard action.
