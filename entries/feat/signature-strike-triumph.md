## Signature Strike Triumph (Combat)

With an effortless flourish, you strike terror into your foes and inspire your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot),
[Signature Strike Style](ref/feat/signature-strike-style),
[Signature Strike Taunt](ref/feat/signature-strike-taunt), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus),
base attack bonus +11, Intimidate 11 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use Signature Strike Style to give a creature the [shaken](ref/misc/shaken)
condition, increase the duration to 1d4+1 rounds. When you confirm a critical hit against a creature
marked by your Signature Strike Style, one ally within 60 feet who can see you can attempt a new
saving throw against one ongoing effect with the emotion, fear, or mind-affecting descriptor caused
by the marked creature. If the ally succeeds at this saving throw and the effect's remaining
duration is 24 hours or less, the effect ends; if the remaining duration is more than 24 hours, the
effect is suppressed for 1 minute.
