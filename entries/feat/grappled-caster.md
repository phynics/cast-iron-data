You have trained to cast spells while wrestling an opponent.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Casting](ref/feat/combat-casting).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks to cast spells
while grappled. This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by Combat Casting.
