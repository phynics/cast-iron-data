## Untwisting Iron Skin (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 107, Inner Sea Combat pg 13

Your training has tempered your flesh to resist blows as though it were forged iron.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15; Wis 13; [Perfect Style](ref/feat/perfect-style);
[Toughness](ref/feat/toughness); [Untwisting Iron Strength](ref/feat/untwisting-iron-strength); base
attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.\
**Benefit:** When using Perfect Style, you gain an amount of DR/adamantine equal to 1/3 your
character level (maximum 6). Additionally, whenever you break an item with a
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 10 or more, you gain DR 1/- for a number of rounds equal to half the
item's hit points (maximum 10 rounds); the value of this
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) increases by 1 for every 5 points of hardness above 10
the broken item had. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift action to double either of the damage
reduction values granted by this feat for 1 round.
