## Shadows of Fear (Combat)

Your enemies' fear causes them to jump at shadows, diverting their attention.\
**Prerequisites:** Hidden strike +2d8 or [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +2d6.\
**Benefit:** The first time each round that you hit a creature suffering from a fear effect, you can
deal hidden strike or sneak attack damage as if you were [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) that creature
([improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) and other effects that prevent
flanking also prevent a hidden strike or sneak attack from this feat).
