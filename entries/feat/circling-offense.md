## Circling Offense (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Rival Guide pg 35

You are skilled at approaching large foes with an ally in a way that makes it difficult for your
foes to find an advantage against you or your ally.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility), base attack bonus +3.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +1 dodge bonus to your Armor Class against attacks of opportunity
provoked by moving through a creature's reach, provided the creature is of a larger size category
than you and provided at least one other ally with Circling Offense is within the creature's reach.
If a creature that you're receiving this dodge bonus against attacks you and misses with the attack,
it provokes attacks of opportunity from any of your allies who possess Circling Offense.
