**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 80

You have been dead for so long you have become quite difficult to kill.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sleeper](ref/feat/sleeper), [Warren Digger](ref/feat/warren-digger), creature
has been a [ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul) for at least 500 years.\
**Benefit:** When you are reduced to 0 hit points, you are not destroyed - you cannot be killed
unless your body is completely destroyed with fire or doused in
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), or you take damage from a _cure spell_ or
[channeled positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) after you've been reduced to 0 hit points.
Anytime you are reduced to 0 hit points, you remain unconscious until you are healed or until the
following midnight, at which time you regain 1 hit point per Hit Die and awaken.
