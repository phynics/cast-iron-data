**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 155

Your boundless rage grants your blows extra strength, though at the expense of accuracy.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack); rage or
[raging song](ref/ability/raging-song) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you use Power Attack while raging or while using raging song, you take an
additional -1 penalty on melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks, and you gain an additional
+2 bonus on melee damage rolls. Modify this damage bonus appropriately based on the type of weapon
you are using, as normal for Power Attack.
