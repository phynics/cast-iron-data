You can make powerful attacks that place more of your weapon's poison into a target's wounds.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack); Craft (poison) 3 ranks or
[poison use](ref/ability/poison-use) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you damage an opponent with a Power Attack while using a poisoned weapon, you can
forgo the bonus damage from Power Attack to increase the save DC of the poison by 1. When your base
attack bonus reaches +4, and every +4 thereafter, the bonus to the poison's save DC increases by an
additional 1. This can't cause the save DC to exceed 15 + 1/2 your character level.
