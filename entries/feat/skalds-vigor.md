**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 156

Your song and your enthusiasm combine to invigorate you in battle.\
**Prerequisites:** [Raging song](ref/ability/raging-song) class feature.\
**Benefit:** While maintaining a raging song, you gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) equal to
the Strength bonus your song provides, starting in the round after you begin the song. If you stop
maintaining your song, the fast healing ends, even if the effects of your song persist.
