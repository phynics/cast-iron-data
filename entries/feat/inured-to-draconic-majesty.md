**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 19

You have spent enough time around dragons to believe that much of their fearsomeness is bluster.\
**Prerequisites:** [Favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) (dragon) or Knowledge (arcana) 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against extraordinary or supernatural fear effects
(such as a dragon's [frightful presence](ref/umr/frightful-presence)). If a failed saving throw
against an extraordinary or supernatural fear effect would make you
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened), you are instead [shaken](ref/misc/shaken); and if a failed saving
throw would make you [panicked](ref/misc/panicked), you are instead frightened.
