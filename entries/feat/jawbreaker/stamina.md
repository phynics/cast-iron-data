**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 125\
When you hit with a Jawbreaker attack, you can spend 5 stamina points to increase the bleed damage
to 1d6 points, increase the DC of the Heal check to end the bleed to 20, and require the user of a
spell or ability that cures hit point damage to succeed at a DC 15 caster level check to end the
bleed effect.
