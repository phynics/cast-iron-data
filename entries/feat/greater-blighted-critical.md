## Greater Blighted Critical (Critical)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 151

Your critical hit from a spell or [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) afflicts the
target with a major spellblight.\
**Prerequisites:** [Blighted Critical](ref/feat/blighted-critical), caster level 12th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit with a touch spell, ranged touch spell, or
spell-like ability against an opponent, the victim gains a random major
[spellblight](ref/rule/spellblights).\
**Special:** You can only apply the effects of one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
possess [Critical Mastery](ref/feat/critical-mastery).
