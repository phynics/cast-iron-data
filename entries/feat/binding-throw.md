## Binding Throw (Combat)

You can strike your enemy and use the blow as an opportunity to grab and hold him.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Ki Throw](ref/feat/ki-throw).\
**Benefit:** After you successfully use the Ki Throw feat on an opponent, you can use a swift action
to attempt a grapple combat maneuver against that opponent.\
**Normal:** The grapple combat maneuver is a standard action.\
**Special:** A monk can gain Binding Throw as a bonus feat starting at 14th level.
