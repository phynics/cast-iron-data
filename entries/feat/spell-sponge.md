Your mystical connection with your spellcasting master attunes you to helpful magical effects.\
**Prerequisites:** [Animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) or
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar), share spells ability.\
**Benefit:** Whenever your master targets you with a harmless spell with a target of "you," the
spell's duration is doubled as if modified by the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) metamagic
feat. This does not affect spells with a duration of concentration, instantaneous, or permanent, or
spells already modified by Extend Spell.
