**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 137

Your phantom is a deadly foe of incorporeal adversaries.\
**Prerequisites:** Phantom class feature, phantom with the magic attacks ability.\
**Benefit:** Your [phantom's](ref/sidekick/phantom) natural weapons are treated as having the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) property. In addition, when you cast a touch spell
to be delivered by your phantom, you can modify the spell as if you possessed the
[Ectoplasmic Spell](ref/feat/ectoplasmic-spell) metamagic feat.
