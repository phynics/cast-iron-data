## Wyvern Fury Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Legacy of Dragons pg 21

You can combine sweeping attacks with a whip and quick stabs with a blade, imitating a wyvern's
powerful bite and tail tipped with a stinger.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (whip),
Acrobatics 3 ranks, brawler's flurry class feature or flurry of blows class feature.\
**Benefit:** While using this style, you can make attacks with a [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger),
[punching dagger](ref/eq-weapon/punching-dagger), [scorpion whip](ref/eq-weapon/scorpion-whip),
[spiked gauntlet](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-gauntlet), or [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) when using a flurry
of blows or brawler's flurry. Only one of these attacks can be made with a whip or scorpion whip.
