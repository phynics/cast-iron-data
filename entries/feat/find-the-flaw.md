You sense the minute imperfections in the otherwise ordered structure of your foes' weapons and
armor.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +1, Craft (armor or weapons) 5 ranks,
[aphorite](ref/race/aphorite).\
**Benefit:** When performing a [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver, you ignore a number of
points of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) equal to your Intelligence modifier.
