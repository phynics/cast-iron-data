**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 31

You can draw your weapon with such speed and finesse that your opponents don't realize they're
threatened.\
**Prerequisites:** [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw).\
**Benefit:** When you draw a light weapon, you may make a Sleight of Hand check instead of a Bluff
check to [feint](ref/rule/feint) in combat. Other feats and abilities that affect a feint still
apply to your feint.
