**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 95

When you dispel an enemy's magical defenses, those defenses crash down with debilitating effects.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) or
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater), caster level 11th.\
**Benefit:** When you successfully make a targeted dispel check against an opponent, that opponent
must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC equals the DC of the spell used to dispel) or be
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) until the start of your next turn. If the save succeeds, the opponent is
instead [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) until the start of your next turn.
