## Distracting Charge (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 144

Your ally's charge exposes an opening you can exploit.\
**Benefit:** When your ally with this feat uses the [charge](ref/rule/charge) action and hits, you
gain a +2 bonus on your next attack roll against the target of that charge. This bonus must be used
before your ally's next turn, or it is lost.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 118\
You can spend 1 stamina point when an ally with this feat charges but misses with the charge attack.
When you do, you can still gain the benefit of this feat when you charge.
