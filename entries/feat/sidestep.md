## Sidestep (Combat)

You can reposition yourself after a foe's missed swing.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility).\
**Benefit:** Whenever an opponent misses you with a melee attack, you may move 5 feet as an
immediate action so long as you remain within that opponent's threatened area. This movement does
not provoke attacks of opportunity. If you take this step, you cannot take a 5-foot step during your
next turn. If you take an action to move during your next turn, subtract 5 feet from your total
movement.
