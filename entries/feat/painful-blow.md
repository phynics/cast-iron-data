## Painful Blow (Combat)

**Sources:** Arcane Anthology pg 31

You have learned how to land blows so painful it is difficult for struck spellcasters to
concentrate.\
**Prerequisites:** [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), base attack bonus +6, Knowledge (arcana) 3
ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use Vital Strike (or [Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike)
or [Greater Vital Strike](ref/feat/greater-vital-strike)), the creature you hit with the attack must
treat the damage dealt as continuous damage until the beginning of your next turn. This forces the
target to attempt a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check if it casts a spell. You can use
this benefit twice per day, or three times per day if your base attack bonus is +12 or higher.
