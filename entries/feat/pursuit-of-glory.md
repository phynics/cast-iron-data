## Pursuit of Glory (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 208

You know that daring deeds and heroics live forever in song, and sagas will carry your deeds into
immortality.\
**Prerequisites:** [Human](ref/race/human) (Ulfen).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you start or end a charge attack in a space adjacent to an ally who also has
this feat, you gain a +2 morale bonus on one attack roll you make as part of the charge.
Furthermore, you can charge through a space containing an ally who also has this feat.
