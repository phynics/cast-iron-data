**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 134

You have mastered a small handful of spells, and can prepare these spells without referencing your
spellbooks at all.\
**Prerequisites:** 1st-level wizard\
**Benefit:** Each time you take this feat, choose a number of spells that you already know equal to
your Intelligence modifier. From that point on, you can prepare these spells without referring to a
spellbook.\
**Normal:** Without this feat, you must use a spellbook to prepare all your spells, except
[read magic](ref/spell/read-magic).

### Mythic Spell Mastery

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 74\
You're so familiar with certain spells you can prepare them with the greatest of ease.\
**Prerequisites:** Spell Mastery.\
**Benefit:** You can prepare spells you have selected with Spell Mastery as a full-round action. You
must spend the appropriate amount of time preparing other spells you have not mastered. As a
full-round action, you can expend one use of mythic power to prepare all your spells that you have
selected with Spell Mastery.
