**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 165

Your gaze is naturally drawn to valuable glittering objects.\
**Prerequisites:** [Tengu](ref/race/tengu).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks. You may determine the
most valuable item in a hoard as a standard action and gain an additional +2 bonus on the Appraise
check to do so. In addition, if you fail an Appraise check by 5 or more, you treat the check as if
you had failed by less than 5.\
**Normal:** Determining the most valuable object in a treasure hoard takes 1 full-round action.
