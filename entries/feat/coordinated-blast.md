## Coordinated Blast (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 204

With trained precision, you create safe pockets within the areas of your spells, inside which your
allies remain safe.\
**Prerequisites:** Spellcraft 5 ranks, any racial
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use a spell or ability with an area (such as
[fireball](ref/spell/fireball) or [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy)), you can exclude any
number of allies who also have this feat from that area of effect.
