## Smashing Style (Combat, Style)

Your hammer blows dent armor, hampering your opponent.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** Choose one weapon from the axes, hammers, or flails
[fighter weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups). While using this style, when you succeed at a
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver check against a suit of armor worn by an opponent, you can
immediately attempt a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver
against that target with the same combat maneuver bonus. This maneuver does not provoke attacks of
opportunity.\
**Special:** A character with the weapon training (axes, hammers, or flails) class feature can use
Smashing Style with any axe, flail, or hammer, respectively, in addition to the chosen weapon.
