## Greater Awesome Blow (Combat)

When you perform an awesome blow, you fling opponents back farther.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 25, [Awesome Blow](ref/feat/awesome-blow),
[Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), size Large
or larger.\
**Benefit:** When you make an awesome blow combat maneuver, for every 5 by which your combat
maneuver check exceeds the target's CMD, you send the target flying 5 additional feet. Furthermore,
this movement provokes attacks of opportunity from your allies.
