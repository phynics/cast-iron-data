You are able to cast a 1st-level spell as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast 4th-level spells.\
**Benefit:** Choose one 1st-level spell that you know. You may cast that spell twice per day as a
spell-like ability. The caster level for this spell-like ability is equal to your caster level in
the class from whose spell list the spell is taken. The spell-like ability's save DC is
Charisma-based. If the spell has an expensive focus or material component, it may not be chosen for
this feat. You cannot apply metamagic feats to this spell.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new spell.
