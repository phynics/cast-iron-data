You cause anyone damaged by your negative energy to become susceptible to poison.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 3d6, worshiper of
[Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber).\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you channel negative energy to damage living creatures, you can cause
any creatures that failed their save against your channel to take a -4 penalty on saving throws
against poison effects. This penalty lasts for 1 minute.
