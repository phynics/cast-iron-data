You're an expert at traveling the various realms of the Great Beyond via plane shift.\
**Prerequisites:** Wizard level 9th.\
**Benefit:** Add [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift) to your spell list and to your spellbook as a
5th-level wizard spell. The time required for you to identify a portal's destination is reduced to 1
round. In addition, you automatically identify any plane on which you arrive.\
**Normal:** Plane shift is a 7th-level wizard spell.\
**Special:** A wizard can select this as a wizard bonus feat.
