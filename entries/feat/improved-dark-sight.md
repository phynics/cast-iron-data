With further modification, your eyes see clearly in not only normal darkness, but in magical
darkness as well.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dark Sight](ref/feat/dark-sight), [Gloom Sight](ref/feat/gloom-sight),
[fetchling](ref/race/fetchling).\
**Benefit:** You gain the [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) ability and lose the
[light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity) weakness, but gain the
[light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness) weakness.
