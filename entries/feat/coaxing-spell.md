## Coaxing Spell (Metamagic)

Your charms and enchantments reach the minds of even the lowliest dungeon denizens.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (enchantment), Knowledge (dungeoneering) 6
ranks.\
**Benefit:** This feat only works with mind-affecting effects. A coaxing spell affects mindless
oozes and vermin as if they weren't mindless, but has no effect on other creature types. A coaxing
spell uses a spell slot 2 levels higher than the spell's actual level.
