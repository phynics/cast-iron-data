When you attack a creature subject to the _[blink](ref/spell/blink)_ spell, you can spend 5 stamina
points. If you do, your Greater Blind-Fight feat works against that creature until the beginning of
your next turn, either treating the _[blink](ref/spell/blink)_ miss chance as 20% and allowing a
reroll or ignoring the _[blink](ref/spell/blink)_ miss chance if you have a way to see or strike
ethereal creatures.
