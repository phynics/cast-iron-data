## Fleshwarper (Item Creation)

You can create creatures through an abominable alchemical process.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (alchemy) 5 ranks, Heal 5 ranks, evil alignment.\
**Benefit:** You can create fleshwarped creatures and fleshcraft grafts (see
[Fleshwarping](ref/rule/fleshwarping) for full details). A newly created fleshwarped creature has
average hit points for its Hit Dice.
