## Crushing Impact (Combat)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 24

You slam into your foes with brute strength as unyielding as a wall.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) an enemy and its movement is
stopped by a solid object or barrier, you deal your unarmed strike damage to the foe, as long as
it's within your threatened area. If you performed your bull rush as part of a charge, you gain a +2
bonus on your damage roll.
