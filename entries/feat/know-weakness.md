## Know Weakness (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Origins pg 27

You are well versed in the susceptibilities of your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Bardic knowledge class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you successfully identify a creature with the appropriate
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) check, you gain a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls against
that creature for 1 round.
