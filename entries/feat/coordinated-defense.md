## Coordinated Defense (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 156

You are adept at working with allies to avoid being tripped, grappled, and subjected to other
maneuvers.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, you receive a +2
competence bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense. This bonus increases to +4 if the creature
attempting the maneuver is larger than both you and your ally.
