Your death-stealing gaze creates more powerful undead.\
**Prerequisites:** [Nabasu](ref/monster/demon-nabasu), [Ability Focus](ref/feat/ability-focus)
(death-stealing gaze)\
**Benefit:** When you would normally create a ghoul with your death-stealing gaze, you instead
create a [ghast](ref/monster/ghoul). As a free action, you may also spend a number of growth points
in order to even further augment your new undead minion as it is created. If you spend 1 growth
point, you create a [wight](ref/monster/wight) instead of a ghast. If you spend 3 growth points, you
create a [wraith](ref/monster/wraith) instead of a ghast. And if you spend 5 growth points, you
transform the target into a [juju zombie](ref/template/juju-zombie) instead of a ghast. Note that
spending growth in this manner reduces your statistics as appropriate.
