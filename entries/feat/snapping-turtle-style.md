## Snapping Turtle Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 120

Your deft unarmed style allows you to shield your body from harm.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +1
or monk level 1st.\
**Benefit:** While using the Snapping Turtle Style feat with at least one hand free, you gain a +1
shield bonus to AC.
