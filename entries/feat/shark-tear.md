## Shark Tear (Combat)

You tear into bleeding foes and can smell their blood in the water.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Shark Style](ref/feat/shark-style), Swim 6 ranks, base attack bonus +6 or monk level 6th.\
**Benefit:** While in Shark Style, you gain [scent](ref/umr/scent) while in water, but only against
bleeding creatures. You also gain a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls against bleeding creatures.
