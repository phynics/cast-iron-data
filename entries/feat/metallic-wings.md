Your wing feathers are made of gleaming metal.\
**Prerequisites:** [Angelic Blood](ref/feat/angelic-blood), [Angelic Flesh](ref/feat/angelic-flesh),
[Angel Wings](ref/feat/angel-wings), [aasimar](ref/race/aasimar), character level 11th.\
**Benefit:** You gain two wing attacks. These are secondary natural attacks that deal 1d4 points of
slashing damage (or 1d3 if you are Small).
