## Smiting Reversal (Combat)

Your can withstand the onslaught of holy or unholy forces.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness).\
**Benefit:** Three times per day after being targeted by the smite attack of an enemy, you can
immediately make an attack of opportunity against the target. You gain a bonus on this attack roll
equal to your Charisma bonus. If this attack of opportunity hits, you gain a bonus on the damage
roll equal to your character level. This attack of opportunity ignores all
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) the creature possesses.
