**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 80

You are skilled at moving quickly through forbidding parts of cities.\
**Prerequisites:** [Street Smarts](ref/feat/street-smarts).\
**Benefit:** You do not treat [crowds](ref/rule/city-streets) as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). You also gain an additional +2 bonus on
Acrobatics checks to move along rooftops and on slippery sections of city streets and sewers.
