Your magical arrows produce a wider range of powers.\
**Prerequisites:** Enhance arrows class feature.\
**Benefit:** You add [corrosive](ref/magic-enh/corrosive) to the list of special properties you can
grant your nonmagical arrows with your enhance arrows ability at 3rd level. At 5th level, you can
add the [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon), [limning](ref/magic-enh/limning), or
[planar](ref/magic-enh/planar) special ability instead of [distance](ref/magic-enh/distance). At 7th
level, you can add the [corrosive burst](ref/magic-enh/corrosive-burst) special ability.
