## Neckbreaker (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 111

With a quick jerk, you snap an enemy's neck.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bonebreaker](ref/feat/bonebreaker), [Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Jawbreaker](ref/feat/jawbreaker),
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), Heal 12 ranks.\
**Benefit:** If you have an opponent your size or smaller [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) or
[pinned](ref/rule/grapple-conditions), after you initiate or maintain a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple),
you can make a Stunning Fist attempt at a -5 penalty on the attack roll. If you succeed, you wrench
that opponent's neck, dealing 2d6 Strength or Dexterity damage. If the targeted ability score is
reduced to 0, any remaining damage is dealt to that opponent's Constitution score. A creature that
is immune to critical hits or that has no discernible head and neck is immune to the effects of this
feat.
