## Cautious Fighter (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 66

You care more about survival than victory.\
**Prerequisites:** [Halfling](ref/race/halfling).\
**Benefit:** When [fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or using
[total defense](ref/misc/total-defense), your dodge bonus to AC increases by 2.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 115\
When not fighting defensively or using total defense, you can spend 5 stamina points to increase
your dodge bonus by 2. This increase lasts until the start of your next turn.
