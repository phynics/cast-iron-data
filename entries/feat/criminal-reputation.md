**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 81

Your acts of audacious larceny have endeared you to the criminal underworld.\
**Prerequisites:** Diplomacy 5 ranks, Intimidate 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks when interacting with criminals,
whether they're individual thieves and cutpurses or criminal organizations. If you have 10 or more
ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

These bonuses do not stack with those granted by [Persuasive](ref/feat/persuasive), but this feat
counts as Persuasive for the purposes of feats and other rules elements with Persuasive as a
prerequisite.
