You are a master of drow noble magic.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, Wis 13, [Drow Nobility](ref/feat/drow-nobility),
[Greater Drow Nobility](ref/feat/greater-drow-nobility),
[Improved Drow Nobility](ref/feat/improved-drow-nobility), [Umbral Scion](ref/feat/umbral-scion),
able to use drow [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), [drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** You may use [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),
[divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor), and [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion) once per day as
spell-like abilities. Your caster level is equal to your character level.
