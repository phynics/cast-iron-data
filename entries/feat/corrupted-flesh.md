**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 152

You have sickened, rotting flesh from some deformity or vestigial limb that has turned fetid and
necrotic, yet refuses to heal or fall off.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15, 6 HD, [ogre](ref/monster/ogre).\
**Benefit:** You gain the [stench](ref/umr/stench) special ability (DC 10 + 1/2 your HD + your
Constitution modifier).
