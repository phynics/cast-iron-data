You were selected by your celestial ancestor to be a guide for a chosen people, and serve as a
beacon of hope in times of despair.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar), must be taken at 1st level.\
**Benefit:** Select one of the following creature subtypes: dwarf, elf, gnome, halfling, human, or
orc. You gain a +4 racial bonus on Diplomacy checks made to influence creatures of the selected
subtype.
