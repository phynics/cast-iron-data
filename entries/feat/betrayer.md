You can charm people into lowering their defenses, allowing you to ambush them more effectively.\
**Prerequisites:** [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw), [Persuasive](ref/feat/persuasive), base attack
bonus +3.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a Diplomacy check to change a creature's attitude, you can draw a
weapon and make a single melee attack against that creature as an immediate action. If you changed
your target's attitude to friendly or better, your target is considered
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against this attack. If the target survives, it takes a -2
penalty on its initiative check for this combat.

Once you attack a creature, its attitude becomes hostile.
