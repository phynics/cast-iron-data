You gain extra fiendish resistances.\
**Prerequisites:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** Pick one of the following energy types that you do not already have resistance to:
acid, cold, electricity, or fire. You gain resistance 5 to that energy type.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, pick another energy type you
do not have resistance to. You gain resistance 5 to that energy type.
