## Fortified Armor Training (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 102

You have learned to let your armor bear the brunt of the worst attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Proficient with armor or shield.\
**Benefit:** If an opponent scores a critical hit against you, you can turn the critical hit into a
normal hit. If you do, either your armor or your shield gains the [broken](ref/misc/broken)
condition (your choice).
