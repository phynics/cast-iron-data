You bear intricate tattoos that inspire and empower your natural magic ability. These tattoos mark
you as a worker of the ancient traditions of Varisian magic. A Varisian tattoo typically consists of
a long string of complex characters from the Thassilonian alphabet.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus).\
**Benefit:** Select a school of magic (other than divination) in which you have Spell Focus - you
cast spells from this school at +1 caster level. Additionally, you gain a single
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) usable up to three times per day. The spell-like
ability gained (and its Varisian name) are as follows:

- Abjuration (avidais): [resistance](ref/spell/resistance)
- Conjuration (idolis): [acid splash](ref/spell/acid-splash)
- Enchantment (carnasia): [daze](ref/spell/daze)
- Evocation (ragario): [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)
- Illusion (vangloris): [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound)
- Necromancy (voratalo): [touch of fatigue](ref/spell/touch-of-fatigue)
- Transmutation (avaria): [mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand)
