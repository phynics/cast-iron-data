## Greater Rending Fury (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 103

When your claws latch on to an opponent, the effect is bloody and horrific.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Rending Fury](ref/feat/improved-rending-fury),
[Rending Fury](ref/feat/rending-fury), base attack bonus +12, [rend](ref/umr/rend) special attack.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you rend an opponent, you deal 1d6 [bleed](ref/umr/bleed) damage to that
opponent. This is an addition to the effects of the rend.
