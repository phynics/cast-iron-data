## Vim and Vigor (Combat)

You rebound more quickly than normal when you draw upon your inner reserve to repair your wounds.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [Combat Vigor](ref/feat/combat-vigor), [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance),
character level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** Your vigor pool gains 1 vigor point. The [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) condition that
you gain from spending vigor points (as described by the Combat Vigor feat) lasts for 3 rounds
instead of 1 minute. In addition, when you take this feat, you gain a +2 bonus on saving throws
against effects that cause fatigue or [exhaustion](ref/misc/exhausted).
