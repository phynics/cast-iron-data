## Shield Material Mastery (Combat, Shield Mastery)

Your extraordinary understanding of the special materials from which your shield is made lets you
take advantage of it more often.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus),
[Shield Material Expertise](ref/feat/shield-material-expertise), base attack bonus +11 or fighter
level 8th.\
**Benefit:** You can use the ability granted by Shield Material Expertise two additional times per
day.
