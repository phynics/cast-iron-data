## Explosive Weapon (Combat)

You can channel magical power through your weapon to devastating effect.\
**Prerequisites:** [Arcing Weapon](ref/feat/arcing-weapon).

As a standard action, you can cast a spell with an area of effect and a duration of instantaneous,
such as [fireball](ref/spell/fireball), and release it through your melee weapon as part of a melee
attack. This functions as the [magus](ref/class/magus) spellstrike ability, except the spell damage
does not multiply on a critical hit, the spell effect is centered on you, and you are excluded from
its effects.
