## Luck of Heroes (Hero Point)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 324

To others, it seems that you always have a bit of luck around you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Hero's Fortune](ref/feat/heros-fortune).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you spend a [hero point](ref/rule/hero-points) to reroll a die roll or to
grant yourself a bonus before a die roll is made, there is a chance that the hero point is not
spent. Whenever you spend a hero point, roll a d20. If the result is greater than 15, the hero point
is not spent. You cannot use this feat when you cheat death.
