## Horde Charge (Teamwork)

When you charge with an ally, you are more deadly.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** When charging during the same round as an ally with this feat, you gain a +2 bonus on
attack and damage rolls in addition to the normal bonus for charging. If you can make multiple
attacks on a [charge](ref/rule/charge), this bonus only applies to the first attack.
