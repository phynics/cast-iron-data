The goddess [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae) welcomes all who seek good in the world into her fold,
and some goblins are drawn to worship her, in part due to her incarnation as a giant ball of fire.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin), worshiper of Sarenrae.\
**Benefit:** If the sun is visible, you can spend 1 round performing a wild dance in worship of the
Dawnflower. Starting at the beginning of your next turn, allies who can see you gain a +1 morale
bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws. This effect lasts for a number of rounds
equal to half your character level + your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1 round). At 9th level, this
bonus increases to +2.
