**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 129

You can shift and change a binary mindscape to your advantage during a psychic duel.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, ability to cast
[instigate psychic duel](ref/spell/instigate-psychic-duel).\
**Benefit:** When you cast _[instigate psychic duel](ref/spell/instigate-psychic-duel)_ and create a
[binary mindscape](ref/rule/binary-mindscapes), the arena you create puts your enemy at a
disadvantage. Your opponent must spend 1 more manifestation point than normal when creating any
defensive manifestations.
