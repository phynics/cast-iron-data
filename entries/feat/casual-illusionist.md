You can use your innate magic to create minor illusions that augment your efforts at trickery and
deceit.\
**Prerequisites:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome), gnome magic racial trait.\
**Benefit:** As long as you have at least one [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
unused from your gnome magic racial trait, you gain a +2 racial bonus on all Bluff, Disguise, and
Sleight of Hand checks you make. At 10th level, these bonuses increase to +4.\
**Special:** This feat counts as the [Deceitful](ref/feat/deceitful) feat for the purposes of all
prerequisites.
