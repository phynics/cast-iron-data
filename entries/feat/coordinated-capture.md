## Coordinated Capture (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 44

You work with allies to prevent foes from escaping.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you and one or more allies with this feat threaten the same enemy, the enemy
takes a penalty on Acrobatics checks and [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks to avoid
provoking attacks of opportunity equal to the number of creatures with this feat that are
threatening him (maximum +5).
