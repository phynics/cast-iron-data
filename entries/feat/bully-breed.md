Your animal companion can terrify as well as fight.\
**Prerequisites:** Handle Animal 4 ranks, Intimidate 4 ranks, animal companion or mount class
feature.\
**Benefit:** Your [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) or mount gains
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) as a class skill. It can attempt to demoralize an opponent using
the Intimidate skill as a move action on the same round that it damages an opponent with a natural
attack.\
**Normal:** Animal companions and mounts with an Intelligence score of 2 or less must select feats
from a limited list.\
**Special:** Your animal companion can select the [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display) and
[Shatter Defenses](ref/feat/shatter-defenses) feats if it meets the prerequisites. This feat does
not allow your animal companion to use Intimidate to make a target friendly or gain their
assistance.
