## Piercing Spell (Metamagic)

Your studies have helped you develop methods to overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance).\
**Benefit:** When you cast a piercing spell against a target with spell resistance, it treats the
spell resistance of the target as 5 lower than its actual SR. A piercing spell uses up a spell slot
one level higher than the spell's actual level.
