**Sources:** Distant Realms pg 42

You can focus your concentration when maintaining pattern effects to slowly move them.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (illusion), caster level 5th.\
**Benefit:** When you are concentrating to maintain a spell from the pattern subschool, you can move
any area effects associated with that spell by up to 10 feet as a move action. Creatures within the
new area of effect are subject to the spell as if it had just been cast (attempting saves as
normal). When concentrating on a pattern spell that already allows you to move the effect, such as
[rainbow pattern](ref/spell/rainbow-pattern), increase the distance you can move the pattern by 50%.
