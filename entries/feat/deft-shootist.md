## Deft Shootist (Grit)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 95

You keep an eye out while focusing on your weapon, allowing you to avoid attacks while shooting and
reloading firearms.\
**Prerequisites:** Grit class feature or [Amateur Gunslinger](ref/feat/amateur-gunslinger) feat,
[Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility).\
**Benefit:** As long as you have at least 1 grit point, you do not provoke attacks of opportunity
when shooting or reloading a firearm.
