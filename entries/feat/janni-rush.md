## Janni Rush (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 106

When you leap to the attack, your blows are like bolts from on high.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Janni Style](ref/feat/janni-style), [Janni Tempest](ref/feat/janni-tempest), Acrobatics 8 ranks,
Perform (dance) 8 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While using Janni Style, you are always considered to have a running start when
jumping. Further, if you jump as part of a [charge](ref/rule/charge) and make an unarmed strike
against the designated opponent, a hit allows you to roll the unarmed strike's damage dice twice and
add the results together before adding modifiers (such as from Strength) or extra dice (such as
precision-based damage or dice from weapon abilities). The extra damage dice are not multiplied on a
successful critical hit.
