You possess some of the qualities of your dragon ancestors.\
**Prerequisites:** [Kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** Your scales take on the color and some of the resistances of one of the chromatic
dragons. Choose one of the following chromatic dragon types: black (acid), blue (electricity), green
(acid), red (fire), or white (cold). Your scales take on the color of that dragon, and you gain
resistance 5 to the dragon color's corresponding energy type.\
**Special:** If you have the dragon-scaled racial trait, your scale color does not change and you
gain a +1 natural armor bonus instead.
