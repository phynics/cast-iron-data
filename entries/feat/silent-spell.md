## Silent Spell (Metamagic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 133

You can cast your spells without making any sound.\
**Benefit:** A silent spell can be cast with no verbal components. Spells without verbal components
are not affected. A silent spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell's actual
level.\
**Special:** Bard and skald spells cannot be enhanced by this feat.
