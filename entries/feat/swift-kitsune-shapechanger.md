**Sources:** Dragon Empires Primer pg 5

You can change shape more quickly than most kitsune.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, base attack bonus +6, [kitsune](ref/race/kitsune).\
**Benefit:** You can assume human or kitsune form as a swift action. If you have the
[Fox Shape](ref/feat/fox-shape) feat, you can assume fox form as a swift action as well.\
**Normal:** A kitsune's change shape ability is a standard action.
