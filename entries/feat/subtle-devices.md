**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 29

You are skilled at concealing your use of magic items with guile and deft tricks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Stealthy](ref/feat/stealthy).\
**Benefit:** When triggering a wand, staff or any other magic item, you can attempt a Stealth check
(opposed by observers' Perception checks) to conceal the trigger from onlookers. If the magic item
produces an obvious effect (such as a summoned creature or visible spell effect), you take a -4
penalty on the Stealth check, and even if your check is successful, observers still see the spell
effect (though they fail to notice you are responsible for it).
