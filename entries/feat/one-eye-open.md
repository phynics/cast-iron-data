**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 116, Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 15

Your senses seek out threats even while you sleep.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness).\
**Benefit:** The DCs of your [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks don't increase when you are
asleep. If you succeed at a Perception check to notice something dangerous while asleep, you can
wake up to confront the danger.\
**Normal:** The DC for a Perception check attempted by a sleeping creature increases by 10.
