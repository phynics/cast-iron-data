## Engulf Horror (Combat)

The repulsive horror of your smothering attack nearly incapacitates most foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Engulf Revulsion](ref/feat/engulf-revulsion); [engulf](ref/umr/engulf) or
[smother](ref/umr/smother) universal monster ability.\
**Benefit:** When you successfully engulf or smother an opponent, successfully maintain a grapple
combat maneuver on a smothered opponent, the opponent must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2
your Hit Dice + your Strength modifier) or become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. This
is a mind-affecting fear effect.
