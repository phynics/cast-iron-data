In refining your palate and experiencing the food, drink, and meal rituals of myriad cultures, you
have learned to divine truths about human behavior and use intoxication to break down psychological
barriers.\
**Prerequisites:** Appraise 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can perform the [psychometry](ref/skill/appraise) occult skill unlock even if you
can't use psychic magic, though you must consume a drink of high-quality alcohol (costing at least
10 gp per drink) to do so. If you can cast psychic spells or have the
[Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) feat, you can use psychometry one additional
time per day.

If you consume a drink of high-quality alcohol, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on
[Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks for 1 minute and
can increase the caster level of the next divination spell you cast within 1 minute by 1. If you are
[addicted](ref/rule/addiction) to alcohol, these bonuses increase by 1, but so too does the save DC
for your addiction.
