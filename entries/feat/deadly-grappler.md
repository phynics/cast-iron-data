## Deadly Grappler (Combat)

**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 14

While you're grappled, you become even more dangerous.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** You have learned to leverage a grappling creature's weight against it. While you're
grappled, attacks you make against the grappler using [armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes),
a light or one-handed weapon, a natural weapon, or an unarmed strike deal damage as if the strike or
weapon were [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) than it actually is.
