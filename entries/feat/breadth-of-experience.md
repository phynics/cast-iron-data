**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 151

Although still young for your kind, you have a lifetime of knowledge and training.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), [elf](ref/race/elf), or [gnome](ref/race/gnome); 100+
years old.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on all Knowledge and Profession skill checks, and can make checks
with those skills untrained.
