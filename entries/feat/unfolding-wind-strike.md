## Unfolding Wind Strike (Combat)

The wind through which your thrown weapons fly directs them to their targets or back to your hand.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13; Wis 13; [Perfect Style](ref/feat/perfect-style);
[Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw); base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.\
**Benefit:** When using Perfect Style, you double the range increment of any thrown weapon you
wield. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift action to imbue a number of thrown weapons equal to your
Wisdom bonus with the [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) or [seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking)
weapon special ability for 1 round.
