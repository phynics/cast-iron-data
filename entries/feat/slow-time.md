## Slow Time (Combat, Meditation)

Meditation allows you to perceive time as if it were moving more slowly.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Combat Meditation](ref/feat/combat-meditation),
[Meditation Master](ref/feat/meditation-master), character level 7th.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, when you meditate as a full-round action using the Combat Meditation
feat, you gain the effects of [haste](ref/spell/haste) for 1 round per 2 character levels. These
effects do not stack with the _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_ spell, the [speed](ref/magic-enh/speed)
magic weapon special ability, or other effects granting the same result. This is an extraordinary
ability.
