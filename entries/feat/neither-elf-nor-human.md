You have removed yourself from your heritage so thoroughly that even magic does not recognize you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exile's Path](ref/feat/exiles-path),
[Seen and Unseen](ref/feat/seen-and-unseen), character level 11th, [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** You are not considered elven or human for the purpose of harmful spells or effects
based on your type, like a [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon or a ranger's
[favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) class feature.
