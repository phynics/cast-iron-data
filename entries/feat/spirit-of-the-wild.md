**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 28

Your mystic connection with one type of wilderness has grown even stronger.\
**Prerequisites:** [Attuned to the Wild](ref/feat/attuned-to-the-wild),
[Guardian of the Wild](ref/feat/guardian-of-the-wild), [elf](ref/race/elf).\
**Benefit:** When you are in a terrain type you have selected the Attuned to the Wild feat for, you
gain a +4 bonus on Perception checks made to act in the surprise round of combat. If you act in the
surprise round, you gain [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) out to a range of 30 feet during the
surprise round. If you are in an area that qualifies as more than one kind of terrain, these bonuses
do not stack; you receive the bonus for only one terrain type.
