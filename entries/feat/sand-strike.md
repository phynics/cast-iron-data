## Sand Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 9

You can incorporate sand as part of your unarmed fighting techniques.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), proficiency with the [cestus](ref/eq-weapon/cestus).\
**Benefit:** You can take 1 minute to tightly embed sand into the joints and crevices of a pair of
cestuses you wear. While they have sand embedded in them, the cestuses gain +1
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) and +5 hit points. While wearing cestuses embedded with sand, you can
perform a [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver to [blind](ref/misc/blinded) a foe in
place of one of your melee attacks without provoking an attack of opportunity. Doing so causes the
cestuses to no longer have sand embedded in them. You can embed a single cestus with sand as a
full-round action, but you take a -4 penalty on the dirty trick combat maneuver check using a single
cestus.
