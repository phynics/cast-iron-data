Your erratic movements disorient your opponent.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), Acrobatics 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** If you successfully use [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) to tumble through an
opponent's space, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls against that opponent until the
start of your next turn. If you choose to make a [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempt against that
opponent, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on your combat maneuver check. This bonus on trip also
lasts until the start of your next turn.
