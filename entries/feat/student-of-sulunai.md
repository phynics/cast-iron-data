**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 19

You have studied the life and works of the aasimar Sulunai, who led the people of Tianjing in a time
of nationwide chaos. You know that second chances are opportunities for salvation.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar).\
**Benefit:** Whenever a creature within 30 feet of you rerolls an ability check, saving throw, or
skill check, you can give the creature a +2 insight bonus on the new roll as an immediate action.
You can also cast [divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day, using your character level as your
caster level.
