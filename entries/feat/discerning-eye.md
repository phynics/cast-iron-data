You are not easily fooled by illusions and forgeries.\
**Prerequisites:** [Elf](ref/race/elf) or [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf), keen senses racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against illusion spells and effects and
a +2 bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks to detect forgeries. You can use the
Linguistics skill to detect forgeries untrained.
