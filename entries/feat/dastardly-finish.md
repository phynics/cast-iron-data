## Dastardly Finish (Combat)

You can take advantage of an enemy's debilitated state to attempt a coup de grace.\
**Prerequisites:** Sneak attack +5d6.\
**Benefit:** You can deliver a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) to [cowering](ref/misc/cowering)
or [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) targets.\
**Normal:** You can only coup de grace [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) targets.
