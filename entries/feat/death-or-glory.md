## Death or Glory (Combat)

Even when facing a larger foe, you aren't afraid to take great risks in order to finish the fight.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** Against a creature of size Large or larger, you can make a single melee attack as a
full-round action, gaining a +4 bonus on the attack roll, damage roll, and critical confirmation
roll. You gain an additional +1 on this bonus at base attack bonus +11, +16, and +20 (for a maximum
of +7 at base attack +20). After you resolve your attack, the opponent you attack can spend an
immediate action to make a single melee attack against you with the same bonuses.\
**Special:** You can combine the full-round action attack this feat allows with the benefit of
[Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), [Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike), or
[Greater Vital Strike](ref/feat/greater-vital-strike).
