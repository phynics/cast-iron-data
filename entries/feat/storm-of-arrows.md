## Storm of Arrows (Combat)

You can rain arrows upon a greater number of foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Hail of arrows class feature.\
**Benefit:** When using hail of arrows, you can target up to two targets for every
[arcane archer](ref/pclass/arcane-archer) level you have.
