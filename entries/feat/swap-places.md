## Swap Places (Combat, Teamwork)

You are skilled at changing places with your ally during a chaotic melee.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, you can move into your
ally's square as part of normal movement. At the same time, your ally moves into your previous space
as an immediate action. Both you and your ally must be willing and able to move to take advantage of
this feat. Your ally must be the same size as you to utilize this feat. Your ally does not provoke
an attack of opportunity from this movement, but you provoke as normal. This movement does not count
against your ally's movement on his next turn.
