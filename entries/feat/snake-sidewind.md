## Snake Sidewind (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 119

Your sensitive twisting movements make you difficult to anticipate combat.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Snake Style](ref/feat/snake-style), Acrobatics 3 ranks, Sense Motive 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus to CMD against [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuvers and on
Acrobatics checks and saving throws to avoid being knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone). While using the
Snake Style feat, whenever you score a critical threat with your unarmed strike, you can make a
Sense Motive check in place of the attack roll to confirm the critical hit. Whenever you score a
critical hit with your unarmed strike, you can spend an immediate action to take a 5-foot step even
if you have otherwise moved this round.\
**Normal:** You can take a 5-foot step only if you have not otherwise moved this round.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 132\
When you threaten a critical hit with your unarmed strike while using Snake Style and choose to
attempt a Sense Motive check in place of the confirmation roll, you can spend up to 5 stamina points
to gain a bonus on the Sense Motive check equal to the number of stamina points you spent.
