You can gain some insight into the mood of a crowd with just a look.\
**Prerequisites:** [Drunken Sing-Along](ref/feat/drunken-sing-along), Diplomacy 1 rank, Perform
(sing) 1 rank, Sense Motive 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** Before you use the Drunken Sing-Along feat, you can attempt a DC 20 Sense Motive check
as a free action to pick the exact right song for that crowd. If you are successful, you gain a +4
circumstance bonus on your next Diplomacy check to gather information in that tavern.
