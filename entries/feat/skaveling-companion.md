You are bonded with a terrifying undead mount created through foul daemonic rites.\
**Prerequisites:** 7th level in a class that grants an animal companion or mount,
[urdefhan](ref/monster/urdefhan).\
**Benefit:** In place of the animal companion or mount granted by your class feature, you are bonded
with a [skaveling](ref/monster/bat-skaveling). Though less intelligent than others of its kind and
lacking the ability to paralyze and infect victims with its bite, the beast serves you unerringly.

### Bat, Skaveling

**Size** Large; **Speed** 20 ft., fly 40 ft. (good); **AC** +6 natural armor; **Attack** bite +10
(2d8+6); **Ability Scores** Str 23, Dex 19, Con -, Int 2, Wis 15, Cha 14; **Special Attacks**
screech (DC 15); **Languages** none; **Special Abilities** _Screech (Su)_ Once per day as a standard
action, a skaveling can utter an ear-splitting screech that stuns non-skavelings in a 20-foot burst.
All creatures within the area must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or be
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1d3 rounds. Other skavelings, [mobats](ref/monster/bat-mobat), and
urdefhans are immune to this effect. This is a sonic mind-affecting effect. The save DC is
Charisma-based.
