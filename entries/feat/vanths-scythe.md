**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 71

You've seen the reapers many times, and have learned something of their methods.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 9th, [duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker), ghost hunter racial
trait.\
**Benefit:** When you use your ghost hunter racial trait to grant a [scythe](ref/eq-weapon/scythe),
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip), or bow the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon special ability, you also grant the
[undead-bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon special ability. You also gain an additional daily use of
ghost hunter.
