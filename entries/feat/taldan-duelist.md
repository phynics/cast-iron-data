## Taldan Duelist (Combat)

**Sources:** Inner Sea World Guide pg 289, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 137

You trained at one of Taldor's elite fighting schools and are skilled at rondelero, the art of
fighting with the falcata and buckler.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
(falcata), [Shield Proficiency](ref/feat/shield-proficiency) (buckler),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (falcata).\
**Benefit:** When fighting with the [falcata](ref/eq-weapon/falcata) and
[buckler](ref/eq-armor/buckler), your shield bonus to AC increases by +1 and you gain a +2 bonus on
Acrobatics checks.
