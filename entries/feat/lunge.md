## Lunge (Combat)

You can strike foes that would normally be out of reach.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You can increase the reach of your melee attacks by 5 feet until the end of your turn
by taking a -2 penalty to your AC until your next turn. You must decide to use this ability before
any attacks are made.
