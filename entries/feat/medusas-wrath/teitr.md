You can take advantage of your opponent's confusion, delivering multiple blows.\
**Prerequisites:** [Unarmed Combatant](ref/rule/unarmed-combatant),
[Gorgon's Fist](ref/feat/gorgons-fist), [Scorpion Stance](ref/rule/scorpion-stance), base attack
bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use the full-attack action and make at least one unarmed strike, you can
make two additional unarmed strikes at your highest base attack bonus. These bonus attacks must be
made against a [dazed](ref/misc/dazed), [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed),
[paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed), [staggered](ref/misc/staggered), [stunned](ref/misc/stunned), or
[unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious) foe.
