## Notorious Vigilante (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 87

You are widely known as dangerous and fearsome.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus), any nongood alignment,
[great renown](ref/soctalent/great-renown) social talent, proficiency with chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** Your presence can act as a fast and effective Dazzling Display. As a standard action,
you use the benefit of Dazzling Display even when you are not wielding a weapon in which you have
Weapon Focus. If you are wielding such a weapon, you gain a +2 bonus on the Intimidate check.
