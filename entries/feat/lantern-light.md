## Lantern Light (Combat)

**Sources:** Distant Realms pg 33

Your thrown weapons become pure light, overcoming enemy defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** [Lantern Style](ref/feat/lantern-style), [Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot),
good or lawful alignment, caster level 7th or monk level 7th.\
**Benefit:** When you make a single ranged attack with a thrown weapon, that weapon temporarily
transforms into pure holy light, overcoming the target's
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), if any.
