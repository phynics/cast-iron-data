## Two-Fisted Drinker (Mythic)

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 76

You can pound down drinks with amazing verve.\
**Prerequisites:** [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw).\
**Benefit:** You can draw two potions or other beverages - mundane or magical - from a pouch,
[bandolier](ref/eq-misc/bandolier), or similar holder (but not from a backpack) as a move action. As
a standard action, you can drink two potions or other beverages. You must have two free hands to use
this feat.
