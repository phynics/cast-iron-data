**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 79

By taking your time, you negate penalties while sneaking in most armor.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, Stealth 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You do not apply the armor check penalty for light and medium armor on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) skill checks as long as you move half your speed or less. You still
cannot run or charge while using Stealth in this way.
