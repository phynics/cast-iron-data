## Cloak and Dagger Subterfuge (Combat)

**Sources:** Blood of Shadows pg 25

Your increased martial skill allows you to take advantage of distracted foes to easily impose
conditions, and even steal items without being noticed.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Cloak and Dagger Style](ref/feat/cloak-and-dagger-style),
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with
the chosen weapon, base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** While you are using Cloak and Dagger Style and wielding your chosen weapon, whenever
you make an attack of opportunity against a target, you can attempt a free
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver check against the target as a free action as
well. Additionally, whenever you successfully perform a dirty trick maneuver against a target, you
can immediately attempt a steal combat maneuver check against the target as a free action. If you
succeed, the target is unaware you have stolen an item.
