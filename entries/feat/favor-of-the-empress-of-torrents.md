You can unleash powerful watery torrents to push back your foes underwater.\
**Prerequisites:** Worshiper of [Lysianassa](ref/magic-artifact/the-gasping-pearl) or
[pearl seeker](ref/arc-paladin/pearl-seeker) paladin, character level 5th.\
**Benefit:** Once per day while underwater, you can use
[hydraulic torrent](ref/spell/hydraulic-torrent) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, you
can use this ability an additional time each day.
