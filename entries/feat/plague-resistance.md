**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 68

Diseases tend to have milder effects on you.\
**Prerequisites:** Constitution 13.\
**Benefit:** When you take [damage or drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to your ability scores
as the result of a disease, roll damage and drain to each ability score twice and take the lower
result. You suffer any other effects of the disease as normal.
