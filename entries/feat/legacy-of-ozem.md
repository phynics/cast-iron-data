Your purity is so great that the blades and bows of long-forgotten heroes sing to you as if they had
been made for your righteous hands.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +5, good alignment.\
**Benefit:** When using good-aligned weapons, including those under the effects of a
[bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon) spell, you gain a +1 sacred bonus on damage rolls.
