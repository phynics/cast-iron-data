## Follow-Up Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 22

You bat away an opponent's weapon, creating an opening for a punch or kick.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Disarm](ref/feat/improved-disarm),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +6 or monk level 6th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you succeed at a combat maneuver check to [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) an
opponent, you can spend a swift action to make an unarmed strike attack at your highest base attack
bonus against the target you disarmed.
