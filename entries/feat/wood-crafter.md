**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 121

Eschewing the use of metals in your craft, you excel at crafting armor from wood.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (armor) 3 ranks, Knowledge (nature) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on Craft (armor) checks to create armor and shields from wood or
special materials containing wood (such as [darkwood](ref/eq-material/darkwood)). If you succeed at
the check by 5 or more, you decrease the cost to make the item by 10%.
