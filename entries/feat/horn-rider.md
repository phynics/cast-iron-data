## Horn Rider (Combat)

You can defend yourself using the horn or tusk of a creature you're lashed to.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 shield bonus to AC when lashed into a
[horn harness](ref/eq-misc/horn-harness).\
**Special:** A creature with this feat can take the [Mounted Archery](ref/feat/mounted-archery) feat
without possessing 1 rank in the Ride skill or the [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat) feat.
