## Eroding Strikes (Combat, Combination)

With careful, repeated blows, you weaken your foe's defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with bludgeoning weapon.\
**Benefit:** When you deal damage with a melee attack with a bludgeoning weapon to an opponent you
have already damaged this round, you can also reduce the value of that target's
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) by 1 until the beginning of your next turn. This
reduction stacks with itself.
