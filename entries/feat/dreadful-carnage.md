## Dreadful Carnage (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 158

Slaying an enemy demoralizes your other nearby foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Furious Focus](ref/feat/furious-focus), base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you reduce an enemy to 0 or fewer hit points, you can make an
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to demoralize all enemies within 30 feet as a free action.
Enemies that cannot see both you and the enemy you reduced to 0 or fewer hit points are unaffected.
