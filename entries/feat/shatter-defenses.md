## Shatter Defenses (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 133

Your skill with your chosen weapon leaves opponents unable to defend themselves if you strike them
when their defenses are already compromised.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus),
[Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display), base attack bonus +6, proficiency with weapon.\
**Benefit:** Any [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked) opponent hit by you this round is [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed)
to your attacks until the end of your next turn. This includes any additional attacks you make this
round.
