## Shatter Defenses (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 133

Your skill with your chosen weapon leaves opponents unable to defend themselves if you strike them
when their defenses are already compromised.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus),
[Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display), base attack bonus +6, proficiency with weapon.\
**Benefit:** Any [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked) opponent hit by you this round is [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed)
to your attacks until the end of your next turn. This includes any additional attacks you make this
round.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 131\
When you hit an opponent that has been made flat-footed to your attacks by this feat, you can spend
5 stamina points to make that opponent flat-footed to the attacks of one ally who is within 30 feet
of that creature until the start of your next turn.

### Mythic Shatter Defenses

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 72\
Your dazzling attacks leave your opponents flummoxed and bewildered, unable to attack you or to
defend themselves effectively.\
**Prerequisites:** Shatter Defenses.\
**Benefit:** An opponent you affect with Shatter Defenses is flat-footed to all attacks, not just
yours.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
Your skill with your chosen weapon group leaves opponents unable to defend themselves if you strike
them when their defenses are already compromised.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** Any [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked) opponent hit by you this round is [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed)
to your attacks until the end of your next turn. This includes any additional attacks you make this
round.
