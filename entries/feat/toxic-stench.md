**Sources:** Demons Revisited pg 26

Your stench is poisonous.\
**Prerequisites:** [Stench](ref/umr/stench) universal monster ability, immunity to poison,
[Improved Stench](ref/feat/improved-stench), [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness)\
**Benefit:** The first round a creature is affected by your stench, it must also make a successful
Fortitude save against the same DC to resist becoming poisoned. If you have 10 or fewer HD, this
poison is identical to [insanity mist](ref/eq-poison/insanity-mist); if you have 11 or more HD, the
poison also adds [confusion](ref/misc/confused) for 1 round to its effects.
