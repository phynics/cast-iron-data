## Octopus Thrash (Combat)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 15

Your arms flail about in a confusing pattern of strikes that trips and batters your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip),
[Octopus Focus](ref/feat/octopus-focus), [Octopus Style](ref/feat/octopus-style), Acrobatics 7
ranks, Perception 7 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While using Octopus Style, when you threaten two enemies who are adjacent to each
other, you can attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver check against both targets at once in
place of a melee attack. You attempt a single combat maneuver check at a -5 penalty and apply the
result to both targets.
