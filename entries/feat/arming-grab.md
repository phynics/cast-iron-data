## Arming Grab (Combat)

Opponents would be foolish to underestimate you, even if you appear unarmed and helpless. You are
practiced at turning the tables on armed foes and using their own weapons against them.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard),
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), [Improved Disarm](ref/feat/improved-disarm).\
**Benefit:** You take no penalty on your attack roll when attempting to [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) an
opponent while you are unarmed. If you succeed at a disarm attempt without using a weapon and choose
to pick up the disarmed weapon, you can attack with that weapon as though you were proficient with
it until the end of your next turn. If you are already proficient with that type of weapon, you gain
a +2 morale bonus to your attack rolls with that weapon until the end of your next turn.\
**Normal:** Attempting to disarm a foe while unarmed imposes a -4 penalty on the attack.
