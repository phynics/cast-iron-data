You're able to construct statements and commands that are much more convincing than their content
would otherwise suggest.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Linguistics).\
**Benefit:** You can use a [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) check in place of a
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check to tell a falsehood or conceal information, in place of a
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check to change the attitude of a creature, or in place of an
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to force a creature to cooperate. You must deliver your
attempt in a language the target understands.
