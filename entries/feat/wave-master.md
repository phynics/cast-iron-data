Gozreh has seen fit to bless you as an uncommonly skilled swimmer.\
**Prerequisites:** Worshiper of [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh).\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a Swim check, you can move your full speed as a full-round action
or half your speed as a move action. This does not grant you the benefit of having a swim speed.\
**Normal:** A successful Swim check allows you to move up to half your speed as a full-round action
or up to a quarter your speed as a move action.
