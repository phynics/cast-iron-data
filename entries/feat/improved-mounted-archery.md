**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 15

You are an expert at making ranged attacks while mounted.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mounted Archery](ref/feat/mounted-archery),
[Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat), Ride 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You take no penalty when using a ranged weapon while mounted and taking a double move
action. The penalty while your mount is running is reduced to -2.
