**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 82

You can manage multiple contingency effects.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 19th.\
**Benefit:** You can have two [contingency](ref/spell/contingency) effects active at one time. If
they would both trigger on the same round, one (chosen randomly) does not trigger until 1 round
later.\
**Normal:** You can benefit from only a single contingency active at a time.
