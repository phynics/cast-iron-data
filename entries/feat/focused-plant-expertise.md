## Focused Plant Expertise (Combat)

**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 23

You are quick to dodge and slash anything that would ensnare you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) (plant) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain a bonus equal to half your favored enemy (plant) bonus on Reflex saves to
avoid being entangled and to your CMD whenever an opponent attempts to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple)
you. Your weapons' critical hit multipliers increase by one against creatures with the plant type.
