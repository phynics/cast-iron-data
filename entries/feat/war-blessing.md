**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 159

Your fervent call to your deity imbues you with a temporary blessing.\
**Prerequisites:** Mystery or domain class feature.\
**Benefit:** Choose two warpriest [blessings](ref/ability/blessings) when you take this feat. Each
of these blessings must be tied to a domain granted by your deity or to one of the two domains that
represent your spiritual inclination and abilities. Twice per day, you can call upon the minor
blessing from one or the other of your chosen blessings. This ability otherwise acts like the
warpriest blessings class feature. Your effective warpriest level is the highest level that you have
in the class that has the mystery or domain class feature.
