Your heritage is strange or difficult to trace, and people fear your otherworldly powers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dhampir](ref/race/dhampir).\
**Benefit:** Your undead progenitor left you with more than a hint of vampiric nature. You gain a
natural bite attack that deals 1d4 points of damage. Once per day upon making a successful bite
attack, you can choose to deal an additional 1d4 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage to the
creature struck.\
**Special:** You can only select this feat at 1st level.
