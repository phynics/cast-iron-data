## Improved Weapon of the Chosen (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 151

You gain even greater favor when you use your deity's favored weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with your deity's favored weapon,
[Weapon of the Chosen](ref/feat/weapon-of-the-chosen).\
**Benefit:** This feat acts as Weapon of the Chosen, except you gain the benefits on all attacks
until the start of your next turn. Your attacks gain a single alignment component of your deity -
either chaotic, evil, good, or lawful - for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). If your deity is neutral with no other alignment
components, your attacks instead overcome damage reduction as though your weapon were both cold iron
and silver.
