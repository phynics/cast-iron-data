## Censoring Critical (Combat, Critical)

Your critical hits mute your enemies. Whenever you score a critical hit against an opponent, the
victim is unable to speak for 1d4+1 rounds. This prevents spellcasters from using spells with verbal
components. A successful Fortitude saving throw reduces this muteness to 1 round. The DC of this
Fortitude save is equal to 10 + your base attack bonus.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), base attack bonus +15.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you score a critical hit against an opponent, the victim is unable to speak
for 1d4+1 rounds. This prevents spellcasters from using spells with verbal components. A successful
Fortitude saving throw reduces this muteness to 1 round. The DC of this Fortitude save is equal to
10 + your base attack bonus.\
**Special:** You can apply the effects of only one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
have [Critical Mastery](ref/feat/critical-mastery).
