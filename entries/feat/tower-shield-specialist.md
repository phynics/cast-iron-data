## Tower Shield Specialist (Combat, Shield Mastery)

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 19

You wield tower shields with ease.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus),
[Tower Shield Proficiency](ref/feat/tower-shield-proficiency), base attack bonus +11 or fighter
level 8th.\
**Benefit:** You reduce the armor check penalty for [tower shields](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) by 3,
and if you have the armor training class feature, you modify the armor check penalty and maximum
Dexterity bonus of tower shields as if they were armor.
