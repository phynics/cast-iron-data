**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 30

You have observed the dark stalkers who roam the [Hao Jin Tapestry](ref/rule/hao-jin-tapestry) and
adopted some of their tricks.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 3rd, must have visited the Hao Jin Tapestry.\
**Benefit:** You can cast [darkness](ref/spell/darkness) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) using your character level as your caster level.
At 7th level, you can also cast [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) once per day as a
spell-like ability using your character level as your caster level.
