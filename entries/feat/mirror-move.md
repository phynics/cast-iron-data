## Mirror Move (Combat)

**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 7

By tracking an opponent's movements, you can attempt to duplicate his techniques.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), base attack bonus +4.\
**Benefit:** If you are the only character threatening an opponent and that opponent uses a combat
feat (including taking an action that benefits from a combat feat, or gaining any bonus to AC,
attack rolls, damage rolls, or saving throws from a combat feat), you can take an immediate action
to gain the benefit of that feat until the end of your next turn. You must meet all the feat's
prerequisites. If a feat has a daily use limitation, as does
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), any uses of that feat while you're using Mirror Move count
toward that feat's daily limit for you. You can use Mirror Move once per day for every 4 levels you
have attained.
