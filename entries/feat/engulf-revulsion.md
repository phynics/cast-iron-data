## Engulf Revulsion (Combat)

**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 86

The stomach-churning terror of your smothering attack leaves creatures sick and weakened.\
**Prerequisites:** [Engulf](ref/umr/engulf) or [smother](ref/umr/smother) universal monster ability.\
**Benefit:** When you successfully engulf or smother an opponent, or successfully maintain a grapple
combat maneuver on an engulfed or smothered opponent, that opponent must succeed at a Will save (DC
= 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Strength modifier) or become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round.
This is a mind-affecting fear effect.
