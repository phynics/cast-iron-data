## Throat Slicer (Combat)

Sometimes the only way to deal with those who see your illicit activities is to get rid of them.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** When using a one-handed, light, or natural weapon, you can deliver a
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) to an unconscious, bound, or
[pinned](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) target (though not other kinds of helpless targets) as a
standard action.
