## Bonebreaker (Combat)

When your opponent is unable to adequately defend against them, your precise unarmed strikes break
bone and tear tissue.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Jawbreaker](ref/feat/jawbreaker),
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), Heal 9 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you make a successful Stunning Fist attempt against an opponent that is grappled,
helpless, or stunned, you can forgo any other Stunning Fist effect to deal 1d6
[Strength or Dexterity damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to that opponent.
