## Inerrant Justice (Story)

**Sources:** Spymaster's Handbook pg 15

You will punish the guilty without letting any innocents be harmed as a result.\
**Prerequisites:** Must have witnessed or suffered a grave injustice that went unpunished, or have
the An Eye for an Eye [ranger background](ref/rule/class-background-tables) or the Raiders
[childhood event background](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more). If the offender is not a
challenging foe, it either advances to become one or allies with one or more creatures who are.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can choose to take the maximum possible weapon damage die roll rather
than rolling when you hit a foe that is unaware of you or considers you an ally. Bonus dice,
including from [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack), surprise attack, and
[Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), are rolled normally.\
**Goal:** Decisively defeat or slay the offender without killing any innocents in the process. You
can complete the goal after killing innocents if you receive an [atonement](ref/spell/atonement)
spell.\
**Completion Benefit:** You can use this feat's benefit once per combat.
