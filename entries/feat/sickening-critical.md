## Sickening Critical (Combat, Critical)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 133

Your critical hits cause opponents to become sickened.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you score a critical hit, your opponent becomes [sickened](ref/misc/sickened)
for 1 minute. The effects of this feat do not stack. Additional hits instead add to the effect's
duration.\
**Special:** You can only apply the effects of one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
possess [Critical Mastery](ref/feat/critical-mastery).
