## Servant in the House of Truth (Origin)

Your creator was a scholar and a seeker of lore, and your memories are filled with the feel of
papyrus and ink.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shabti](ref/race/shabti).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you attempt a check for a Knowledge skill in which you have ranks, you can
roll the check twice and use the better of the two results.
