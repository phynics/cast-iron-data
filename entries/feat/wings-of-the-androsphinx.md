## Wings of the Androsphinx (Combat)

You can reshape the kinetic energy of foes' charges.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Reposition](ref/feat/improved-reposition), base attack bonus +1 or monk level 1st.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus to AC against [charge](ref/rule/charge) attacks. If a creature
charges you and fails its attack roll, as an immediate action you can attempt a
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuver to reposition that foe. If you use a
[readied action](ref/rule/ready) to attack a creature charging you, you can roll to attack and then
attempt to reposition that creature as a free action before it resolves its charge attack.\
**Note:** A monk can take this feat as a bonus feat at 1st level. To benefit from this feat, monks
must have both hands free and be capable of delivering an unarmed strike.
