## Fox Style (Combat, Style)

Your martial training helps you manipulate foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13.\
**Benefit:** While using this style, you can use your base attack bonus in place of your ranks in
Bluff on Bluff checks to feint in combat and to create a distraction to hide. If your Intelligence
is at least 19, you gain a +4 bonus on such Bluff checks when adding your Charisma modifier.
