## Greater Shield Specialization (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 162

Your masterful shieldwork provides even greater protection to your vital areas.\
**Prerequisites:** Proficiency with selected shield,
[Greater Shield Focus](ref/feat/greater-shield-focus), [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus),
[Shield Specialization](ref/feat/shield-specialization) with selected shield, fighter level 12th.\
**Benefit:** Choose one type of shield (buckler, light, heavy, or tower shield) for which you
possess the Shield Specialization feat. With the selected shield, you gain a +2 bonus to your Armor
Class against critical hit confirmation rolls (this bonus stacks with that from Shield
Specialization). In addition, once per day you may negate a critical hit, and damage is instead
rolled normally.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new type of shield.
