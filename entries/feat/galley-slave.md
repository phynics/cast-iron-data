Your experience as a galley slave has given you some unique insight into the world of sailing and
piracy.\
**Prerequisites:** Profession (sailor) 1 rank, current or former galley slave.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 circumstance bonus on Profession (sailor) checks made while aboard a ship
with oars and rowers. When fighting on a ship, your first hit each battle gets a +2 damage bonus.
