## Riving Strike (Combat)

When you infuse your weapon with arcane might, your attacks make foes more susceptible to magic.\
**Prerequisites:** [Arcane Strike](ref/feat/arcane-strike), ability to cast arcane spells.\
**Benefit:** If you have a weapon that is augmented by your Arcane Strike feat, when you damage a
creature with an attack made with that weapon, that creature takes a -2 penalty on saving throws
against spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). This effect lasts for 1
round.
