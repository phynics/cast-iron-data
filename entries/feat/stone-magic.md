Knowledgeable even among stone giant elders, you have unlocked greater mastery over earth and stone.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 17, [stone giant](ref/monster/giant-stone-giant) elder (age 700 years or
older).\
**Benefit:** In addition to the usual [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of a
stone giant elder, you gain the ability to use [passwall](ref/spell/passwall),
[spike stones](ref/spell/spike-stones), [statue](ref/spell/statue),
[stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin), and [wall of stone](ref/spell/wall-of-stone) once per day as
spell-like abilities (caster level 10th, the save DCs are Charisma-based).
