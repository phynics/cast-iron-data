## Spear Dancing Style (Combat, Style)

You can use polearms and spears as double weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** Choose one weapon from the polearm or spear
[fighter weapon groups](ref/ability/weapon-groups). While using this style, you grant the chosen
weapon the [double](ref/misc/double) special weapon feature, using the weapon's normal statistics
for its main-hand end and the statistics of a light mace for its off-hand end. A weapon wielded in
this way loses the [brace](ref/misc/brace) and [reach](ref/misc/reach) special weapon features.\
**Special:** A character with the weapon training (polearms or spears) class feature can use Spear
Dancing Style with any polearm or spear, respectively, in addition to the chosen weapon.
