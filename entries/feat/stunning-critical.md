## Stunning Critical (Combat, Critical)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 135

Your critical hits cause opponents to become stunned.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus),
[Staggering Critical](ref/feat/staggering-critical), base attack bonus +17.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you score a critical hit, your opponent becomes [stunned](ref/misc/stunned)
for 1d4 rounds. A successful Fortitude save reduces this to [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d4
rounds. The DC of this Fortitude save is equal to 10 + your base attack bonus. The effects of this
feat do not stack. Additional hits instead add to the duration.\
**Special:** You can only apply the effects of one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
possess [Critical Mastery](ref/feat/critical-mastery).
