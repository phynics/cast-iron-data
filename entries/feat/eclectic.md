You have a talent for picking up different vocations.\
**Prerequisites:** [Human](ref/race/human).\
**Benefit:** Choose an additional [favored class](ref/rule/favored-class) and gain either +1 hit
point or +1 skill point whenever you take a level in that class. If you choose a class in which you
already have levels, the benefits of this feat are retroactive.
