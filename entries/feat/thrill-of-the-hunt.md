## Thrill of the Hunt (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 118

The pursuit of prey invigorates you. You revel in tracking down and slaying your quarry.\
**Prerequisites:** Survival 1 rank; base attack bonus +4 or track class feature.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, when you succeed at a Survival check to find or follow a creature's
tracks, you can designate that creature to be your prize for a number of hours equal to 4 + your
ranks in Survival. You gain a +2 morale bonus on Survival checks to follow your prize's tracks and
on weapon damage rolls against your prize. If you find and subsequently render your prize
[dead](ref/misc/dead) or [helpless](ref/misc/helpless), you gain a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls,
saves, and skill checks for a number of hours equal to your ranks in Survival.\
**Special:** An [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) can choose this feat as if it were
an animal feat.
