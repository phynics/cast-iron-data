## Lighting the Way (Story)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#131: The Reaper's Right Hand pg 79

You seek to lead others through your pious example.\
**Prerequisites:** Be a member of House Zespire, or have the
[Marked by the Gods](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more) circumstance background, or the Devoted
(cleric), Exemplar (inquisitor), or Righteous Mentor (paladin)
[class background](ref/rule/class-background-tables).\
**Benefit:** Once per day as a standard action, you can give all allies within 30 feet who can hear
you a +1 bonus on attack rolls and on saving throws against mind-affecting effects for 1 minute.\
**Goal:** Establish your religion in an existing settlement where it isn't present. You can do this
by having a settlement gain the Holy Site or Pious [quality](ref/rule/settlement-qualities) for
devotion to your deity, or change its government to a theocracy based on your religion.\
**Completion Benefit:** You can spontaneously convert any 2nd-level or higher divine spell into
[enthrall](ref/spell/enthrall) and can spontaneously convert any 3rd-level or higher divine spell
into [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion).
