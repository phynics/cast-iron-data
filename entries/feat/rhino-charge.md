## Rhino Charge (Combat)

Your charges are both violent and unpredictable.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), base attack bonus +5.\
**Benefit:** You may ready a [charge](ref/rule/charge), though you may only move up to your speed on
the charge.\
**Normal:** Charging is a full-round action and allows you to move twice your speed.
