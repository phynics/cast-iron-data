**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 215

You unlock greater secrets of divine spellcasting from the First Vault.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Casting](ref/feat/combat-casting),
[Divine Dignity](ref/feat/divine-dignity), ability to cast divine spells, worshiper of
[Abadar](ref/faith/abadar).\
**Benefit:** When you use Divine Dignity, it can affect the casting of any of your divine spells,
not just harmless and domain spells.
