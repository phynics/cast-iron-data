## Verdant Spell (Metamagic)

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 119, Heroes of the Wild pg 26

Your magical connection to nature allows you to entice, fool, and misdirect plants and fungi as
though they were people.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (enchantment), Knowledge (nature) 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** A verdant spell affects plant creatures (even mindless plant creatures) as if they
weren't immune to mind-affecting effects, but it has no effect on other types of creatures. A
verdant spell uses up a spell slot 2 levels higher than the spell's actual level. This feat works
only on mind-affecting spells.\
**Normal:** Creatures of the plant type are immune to mind-affecting effects.
