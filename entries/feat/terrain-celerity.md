You can use your surroundings to accelerate your movement, and your enemy's presence pushes you
further.\
**Prerequisites:** [Favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) class feature,
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) class feature.\
**Benefit:** While you're in any of your favored terrain types, you gain a 5-foot bonus to your
movement speed. This bonus increases to 10 feet if a creature of the same type as one of your
favored enemies is visible and within the same terrain type.
