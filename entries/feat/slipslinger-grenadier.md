## Slipslinger Grenadier (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 13

You can hurl splash weapons with your sling.\
**Prerequisites:** [Slipslinger Style](ref/feat/slipslinger-style),
[Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything), warslinger racial trait.\
**Benefit:** While using Slipslinger Style, as a standard action you can use a
[sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling) to hurl an alchemical splash weapon, treating it as a sling bullet for
the purpose of drawing and loading it into your sling. Your sling deals its normal weapon damage,
plus the loaded splash weapon's effect or damage. This extra damage is not multiplied on a critical
hit.\
**Special:** This feat cannot be used with alchemists' bombs.
