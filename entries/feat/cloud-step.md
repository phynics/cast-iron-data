Your tread is of unearthly lightness.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spider Step](ref/feat/spider-step), monk level 12th.\
**Benefit:** As a move action, you can [air walk](ref/spell/air-walk) (as the spell) up to half your
slow fall distance, maximum 50 feet. You must reach a solid, level surface by the end of your turn
or you will fall.
