**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 151

When spreading fear to your enemies, you further embolden your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to perform [dirge of doom](ref/performance/dirge-of-doom).\
**Benefit:** Allies in the area of your dirge of doom receive a +4 morale bonus on saving throws
against fear effects. At least one enemy must be in the dirge's area and become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) by the dirge for you and your allies to receive this bonus.
