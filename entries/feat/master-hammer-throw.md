## Master Hammer Throw (Combat)

**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 9

Your ability to knock down foes with thrown weapons is second to none.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Hammer Throw](ref/feat/hammer-throw), [Improved Hammer Throw](ref/feat/improved-hammer-throw),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip), [Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot), base attack bonus
+6, [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf).\
**Benefit:** You can immediately attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver against a target
that you successfully hit and damage with a thrown bludgeoning weapon with any attack, even as part
of a full-attack action.
