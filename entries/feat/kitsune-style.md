## Kitsune Style (Combat, Style)

Your quick movement and trickery allow you to catch opponents off guard.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick).\
**Benefit:** While using this style, you can attempt to perform a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) in place of an attack at the end of a charge.
