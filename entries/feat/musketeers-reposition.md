## Musketeer's Reposition (Combat, Grit)

You can knock a foe away with your two-handed firearm.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (firearms),
[gunslinger](ref/class/gunslinger) level 6th, [pistol-whip](ref/deed/pistol-whip) deed.\
**Benefit:** When you hit an opponent with a two-handed firearm while using the pistol-whip deed,
you can perform a [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuver instead of knocking the target
[prone](ref/rule/prone). However, you can't reposition the foe closer to you. This combat maneuver
does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
