**Sources:** Pathfinder \#132: The Six-Legend Soul pg 73

Inspired by tales of Cyricas the Leaping Lion vaulting from his sickbed, you refuse to allow ability
damage to hinder you.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude).\
**Benefit:** Choose one ability. You ignore penalties from ability damage to that ability until you
have taken an amount of ability damage equal to your score in that ability.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time, choose a different ability to which
its benefits apply.
