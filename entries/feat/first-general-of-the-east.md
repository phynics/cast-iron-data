## First General of the East (Origin)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 73

Your creator was a soldier and person of war, and your memories are filled with the taste of blood.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 5th, [shabti](ref/race/shabti).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can cast [greater magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon-greater) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). You gain a second daily use at 9th level, and a
third at 15th level. Only you can benefit from this spell-like ability; anyone else who wields the
enchanted weapon gains no benefit from the spell. The caster level for this ability is equal to your
character level.
