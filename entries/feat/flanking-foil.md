## Flanking Foil (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 102

Fighting multiple foes is easy for you.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you hit an adjacent opponent with a melee attack, until the start of your next
turn, that opponent does not gain any [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) bonus on attack rolls while it
is flanking you and cannot deal [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) damage to you. It can still
provide a flank for its allies.
