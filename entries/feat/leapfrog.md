## Leapfrog (Combat, Performance)

You can use an opponent as a springboard.\
**Prerequisites:** Acrobatics 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you spend a swift action to attempt a
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) check after a successful charge attack or combat
maneuver, you gain a +2 bonus on your performance combat check and gain a +4 bonus on your next
Acrobatics, Climb, Fly, Ride, or Swim check attempted before the end of your next turn.
