You have trained to steel yourself against the cold when in dark, lightless realms such as the deep
ocean.\
**Prerequisites:** [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), [gillman](ref/race/gillman).\
**Benefit:** While you are in darkness or [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light), you can take a
swift action to gain cold resistance 5 for 1 round. If you have 11 or more Hit Dice, you gain cold
resistance 10 instead.
