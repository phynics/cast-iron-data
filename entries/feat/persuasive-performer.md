**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 215

For you, performance is the heart of negotiation.\
**Prerequisites:** Versatile performance class feature, worshiper of [Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn).\
**Benefit:** You can use any of your Perform skills in place of Diplomacy, not just Perform
(keyboard, oratory, string, or wind). If you do use Perform (keyboard, oratory, string, or wind),
you gain a +2 bonus on your check.
