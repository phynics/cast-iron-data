Your ties to the First World manifest in the form of magical abilities that tap into a natural
element.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, Knowledge (nature) 3 ranks, [gnome](ref/race/gnome), gnome magic racial
trait.\
**Benefit:** Add one of the following to your gnome magic spell-like abilities:
[burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands), [corrosive touch](ref/spell/corrosive-touch),
[gentle breeze](ref/spell/gentle-breeze), [icicle dagger](ref/spell/icicle-dagger), or
[shocking grasp](ref/spell/shocking-grasp). You can use this
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day.
