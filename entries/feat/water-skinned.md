**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 178

Your touch extinguishes small flames.\
**Prerequisites:** [Undine](ref/race/undine).\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can extinguish a small nonmagical fire with a touch,
affecting anything up to the size of a large campfire. This ability does not affect fires with a
total area greater than 5 square feet. Touching the fire in this way does not harm you.
