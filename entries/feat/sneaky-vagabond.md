You have a well-practiced ability at going unnoticed.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on Stealth checks when others are actively trying to find you. In
crowds, this bonus increases to +4. Additionally, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy and
Knowledge (local) checks when trying to find a place to hide.
