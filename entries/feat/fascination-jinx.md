If you jinx an already fascinated creature, it doesn't fully recover until the jinx ends.\
**Prerequisites:** Bardic performance class feature, Halfling Jinx trait.\
**Benefit:** If you jinx a creature under the effect of a bard's
[fascinate](ref/performance/fascinate) ability, it also takes a -10 penalty on initiative checks
while affected by the jinx. A creature under the effect of your fascinate ability has a -2 penalty
on its save against your jinx.
