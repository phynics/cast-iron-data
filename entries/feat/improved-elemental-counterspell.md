You can charge your kinetic blast with disruptive elemental energy that tears apart elemental
spells.\
**Prerequisites:** Two basic blast wild talents from two different elements, expanded element class
feature, kinetic blast class feature.\
**Benefit:** You can use a readied kinetic blast to [counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) any spell
of equal or lower level, provided that the target spell has any of the following descriptors: air,
cold, darkness, earth, electricity, fire, force, light, or water.\
**Normal:** A readied kinetic blast can be used to counterspell spells that share its descriptor.
