## Hammer the Gap (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 103

You repeatedly strike the same location, causing increasing amounts of damage.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** When you take a full-attack action, each consecutive hit against the same opponent
deals extra damage equal to the number of previous consecutive hits you have made against that
opponent this turn. This damage is multiplied on a critical hit.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 122\
When you use this feat, you can spend 5 stamina points to increase the amount of extra damage dealt
by 1 point per consecutive hit you make.
