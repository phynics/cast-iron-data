## Overseer of the House of Truth (Origin)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 73

Your creator was a politician and administrator, and your memories are filled with the sound of
clinking coins.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shabti](ref/race/shabti).\
**Benefit:** You gain a second daily use of your [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). You gain a third daily use at 8th level, a
fourth at 13th level, and a fifth at 16th level.
