The suffering of others compels you to greater cruelties.\
**Prerequisites:** Worshiper of [Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you deal [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage to an opponent, cause an opponent to
suffer a fear or pain effect, or reduce an opponent's hit points to 0 or fewer, you gain a +2 morale
bonus on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls for 1 round.
