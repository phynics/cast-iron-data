## Dragonfly Wings (Combat)

You can jump off willing allies and unwilling opponents to attack creatures from above.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, Wis 15, [Dragonfly Style](ref/feat/dragonfly-style),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Acrobatics 5 ranks, Climb 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While using Dragonfly Style, the bonus on your melee attacks increases to +2 and
applies to opponents up to two size categories larger than you. In addition, you can substitute a
creature your size or larger in place of a vertical surface. You can freely use a willing creature
in such a manner, but using a hostile creature requires a successful Acrobatics check with a DC
equal to the creature's CMD. If you fail this check by 5 or more, you provoke an attack of
opportunity from the hostile creature.
