You are at home on the shifting waves of sand.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, Con 13.\
**Benefit:** When [desert terrain](ref/rule/desert-terrain) would reduce your overland movement
speed to 1/2 normal, you can move at 3/4 your normal speed. You gain a +2 bonus to Survival checks
to find food and water, and to checks to avoid getting lost in a desert.
