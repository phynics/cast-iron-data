## Improved Two-Weapon Feint (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 106

Your primary weapon keeps a foe off balance, allowing you to slip your off-hand weapon past his
defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 17, Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), base attack bonus
+6.\
**Benefit:** While using [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) to make melee attacks,
you can forgo your first primary-hand melee attack to make a Bluff check to [feint](ref/rule/feint)
an opponent. If you successfully feint, that opponent is denied his Dexterity bonus to AC until the
end of your turn.
