## Following Step (Combat)

You can repeatedly close the distance when foes try to move away, without impeding your normal
movement.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Step Up](ref/feat/step-up).\
**Benefit:** When using the Step Up feat to follow an adjacent foe, you may move up to 10 feet. You
may still take a 5-foot step during your next turn, and any movement you make using this feat does
not subtract any distance from your movement during your next turn.\
**Normal:** You can only take a 5-foot step to follow an opponent using Step Up.
