**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 119\
At the start of your turn, you can spend any number of stamina points. Until the beginning of your
next turn, for every 2 stamina points you spent this way, you can apply the effects of Feral Combat
Training to a single natural weapon attack made with a natural weapon other than the weapon chosen
with this feat.
