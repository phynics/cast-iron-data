**Sources:** Inner Sea Monster Codex pg 59

Your violent death brings woe to the enemies of your daemon masters.\
**Prerequisites:** [Urdefhan](ref/monster/urdefhan).\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at using your daemonic pact ability and allow your soul to be
consumed, the resulting blast's radius increases to 10 feet, and you gain a +2 profane bonus to the
DC of the Reflex save victims must attempt to halve this damage.\
**Normal:** A successful use of the daemonic pact ability deals negative energy damage to creatures
in a 5-foot radius.
