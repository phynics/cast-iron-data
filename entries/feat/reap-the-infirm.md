## Reap the Infirm (Combat)

**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 68

You can use a creature's diseased state against it.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +3.\
**Benefit:** When you hit a diseased creature with a weapon attack, you deal an additional 1d6
points of damage. This is precision damage. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus on combat maneuver
checks against diseased creatures.
