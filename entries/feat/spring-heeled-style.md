## Spring-Heeled Style (Combat, Style)

You cunningly dart across the battlefield from foe to foe, taking advantage of your armor's
flexibility to duck blows.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility),
[Shot on the Run](ref/feat/shot-on-the-run) or [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack), base attack
bonus +4, proficiency with light armor.\
**Benefit:** If using this style during any round in which you take a move action to move 10 or more
feet, you gain a +2 bonus on weapon attack rolls until the start of your next turn. While using this
style, you gain the AC bonus from Mobility against attacks of opportunity provoked by making a
ranged attack or loading a ranged weapon.\
**Special:** A character with the armor training class feature can use Spring-Heeled Style while
wearing any type of armor with which she is proficient.
