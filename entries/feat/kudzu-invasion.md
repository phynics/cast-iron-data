## Kudzu Invasion (Combat)

In addition to obscuring creatures' vision, your vines crush their bodies.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Kudzu Grappler](ref/feat/kudzu-grappler).\
**Benefit:** When you [blind](ref/misc/blinded) a creature using your Kudzu Grappler feat, you can
also deal your unarmed strike damage to it.
