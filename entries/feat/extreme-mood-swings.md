Your emotions sometimes get the better of you.\
**Benefit:** Increase each morale bonus you receive by 1. You take a -1 penalty on saves against
emotion and fear effects. When you are [drunk](ref/rule/drunkenness) (or worse), this penalty
doubles, but you increase each morale bonus you receive by 2 instead.
