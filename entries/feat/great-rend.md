## Great Rend (Combat)

You use your teeth and claws to rip your foes to shreds.\
**Prerequisites:** Bite attack, [rend](ref/umr/rend), [troll](ref/monster/troll).\
**Benefit:** If you are able to use your rend ability on the same turn that you also succeed at a
bite attack, your rend deals an amount of additional damage equal to half your Strength modifier.
