## Smash (Combat)

You overcome obstacles by breaking them.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc).\
**Benefit:** When you attack an inanimate, unattended object, you ignore the first 5 points of its
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness). You also receive a +5 bonus on Strength checks made to knock down or
break open doors.\
**Special:** The benefits of this feat do not apply to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) attempts or attacks
against constructs, only attacks on inanimate, unattended objects.
