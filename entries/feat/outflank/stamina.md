When you threaten but fail to confirm a critical hit against a creature you are flanking, you can
spend 5 stamina points. If you do, that creature provokes an attack of opportunity from each ally
with this feat who is flanking the creature, even though you didn't confirm the critical hit. You
still can't use this ability against targets immune to critical hits.
