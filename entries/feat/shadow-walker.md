**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 112

You can pierce the veil between the Shadow Plane and the Material Plane more often, and to greater
effect.\
**Prerequisites:** [Fetchling](ref/race/fetchling), shadow walk
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** You can expend one use of your _[shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk)_ spell-like
ability to use [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) as a spell-like ability. Your start and
end locations for this ability must be in [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darkness.
