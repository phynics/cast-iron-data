Your eyes develop keener sight in dim light and darkness.\
**Prerequisites:** [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft., [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** You gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) and your darkvision improves to
120 ft.\
**Special:** You can take this feat twice. When you take it a second time, you gain the
[see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) universal monster ability.
