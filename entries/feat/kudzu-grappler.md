**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 24

You climb all over creatures you're grappling like kudzu, blocking their vision.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple), base attack bonus +6,
[vine leshy](ref/race/vine-leshy).\
**Benefit:** You add an option to [blind](ref/misc/blinded) the grappled creature for 1 round to the
list of actions you can take when you succeed at a check to maintain a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple).
