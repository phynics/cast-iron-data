Other races have difficulty reading your wooden features.\
**Prerequisites:** [Wyrwood](ref/race/wyrwood).\
**Benefit:** Your lack of emotion make it difficult for non-wyrwoods to relate to you. When dealing
with any race other than your own, you gain a +2 bonus on Bluff checks. In addition, creatures of
other races take a -2 penalty on Diplomacy and Sense Motive checks attempted against you.
