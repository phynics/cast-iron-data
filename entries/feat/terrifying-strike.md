Your touch spreads fear.\
**Prerequisites:** Despair special ability, [mummy](ref/monster/mummy).\
**Benefit:** Any creature struck by your slam attack must succeed at a Will save at the same DC as
your despair ability or be [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d4 rounds. If the creature is already
shaken, [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked), it is
[paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for 1 round instead. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.
