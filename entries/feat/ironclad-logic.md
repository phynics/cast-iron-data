You appeal to sound reasoning and fact, rather than to emotion, when attempting to sway others.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 19, Diplomacy 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on Diplomacy checks whenever you add your Charisma modifier on
those checks. If you have at least 10 ranks in Diplomacy and an Intelligence score of 27 or more,
this bonus increases to +8. This bonus doesn't stack with the bonus from
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Diplomacy), but this feat counts as Skill Focus (Diplomacy) for
the purpose of feats and other elements with Skill Focus (Diplomacy) as a prerequisite. In
[verbal duels](ref/rule/verbal-duels), when using tactics to which you assigned an
Intelligence-based skill, you can add your Intelligence modifier instead of your Charisma modifier
to the associated skill check.
