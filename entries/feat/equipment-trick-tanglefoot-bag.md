## Equipment Trick (Tanglefoot Bag) (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 26

Choose a piece of equipment, such as an boots, a cloak, rope, a shield, or a heavy blade scabbard.
You understand how to use that piece of equipment in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You can use any [Equipment Trick](ref/feat/equipment-trick) related to the chosen item
as long as you meet the trick's prerequisites. If the item would normally be considered an
[improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons), you can treat it as either a normal weapon or an
improvised weapon, depending on which is more beneficial for you.\
**Special:** You can gain Equipment Trick multiple times. Each time you take the feat, it applies to
a new type of equipment. (These are listed as separate feats for reading convenience.)

[Tanglefoot bags](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag) can be a versatile and even deadly tool in the hands
of a skilled user. In addition to the feat, skill, or other requirements listed for each of these
tricks, you must have the Equipment Trick (tanglefoot bag) feat to use a trick. At your GM's
discretion, you may be able to use some of these tricks with a
[tangleburn bag](ref/eq-weapon/tangleburn-bag), but at the risk of dealing fire damage to connected
items or creatures.

> **Captivating Embrace;** _([Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple)):_ You get up close and
> personal, then use a tanglefoot bag to keep things that way. When you successfully grapple a
> creature, you burst a tanglefoot bag to stick yourself to your target. You must have the
> tanglefoot bag in hand to use this ability unless you have the [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw)
> feat (in which case the tanglefoot bag must be easily accessible on your person). While the
> tanglefoot bag's effect lasts, you do not take a penalty to grapple checks for not having two
> hands free, and your opponent takes a -4 penalty on combat maneuver checks and Escape Artist
> checks to break the grapple.
>
> **Sticky Bombs;** _(Quick Draw, [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything)):_ You can slap another
> item on a tanglefoot bag and toss both to ensure contact. When you throw an alchemical item, you
> can attach a tanglefoot bag to it as you throw it; this does not require an additional action. On
> a hit, the target is affected by the tanglefoot bag, with the alchemical item firmly stuck in the
> goo. The item stays in the target's square as long the tanglefoot bag's effect lasts, making this
> most useful with items that don't immediately take effect, such as
> [darkflares](ref/eq-weapon/darkflare) or [fuse grenades](ref/eq-weapon/fuse-grenade).
>
> **Thieving Shield;** _([Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus) or armor training class feature):_ As
> an immediate action, you can splatter a tanglefoot bag on your shield, making it sticky. Your
> shield gains the [grapple](ref/misc/grapple) weapon quality when used as a weapon with a
> [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash) attack. In addition, the first time each round an attack with
> a manufactured weapon misses you by 4 or less, you can make a free [disarm](ref/rule/disarm)
> attempt against that weapon. This disarm attempt does not provoke an attack of opportunity. Your
> shield remains sticky for 2d4 rounds, after which it must be cleaned (a full-round action) before
> you can use this trick again.
