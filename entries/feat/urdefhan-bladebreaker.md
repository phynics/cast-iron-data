## Urdefhan Bladebreaker (Combat)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Monster Codex pg 59

You can use your rhoka sword to destroy your foes' blades.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, proficiency with the
[rhoka sword](ref/eq-weapon/rhoka-sword), [urdefhan](ref/monster/urdefhan).\
**Benefit:** When wielding a rhoka sword, you can attempt to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) an opponent's
sword or other slender-bladed weapon without provoking an attack of opportunity. You gain a +2
circumstance bonus on the combat maneuver check to do so.
