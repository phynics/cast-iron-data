## Snapping Turtle Clutch (Combat)

Your unarmed style allows you to turn your opponent's attack into an opportunity.\
**Prerequisites:** [Snapping Turtle Style](ref/feat/snapping-turtle-style),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +3 or monk level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** While you are using the Snapping Turtle Style feat, the shield bonus the style grants
to your AC applies to your CMD and touch AC. Whenever an opponent misses you with a melee attack
while you are using the Snapping Turtle Style feat, you can use an immediate action to attempt a
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver against that opponent, but with a -2 penalty.
