## Spellcut (Weapon Mastery)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 22

You can weaken waves of harmful magic energy with weapon blows.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Cut from the Air](ref/feat/cut-from-the-air),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +9, Spellcraft 1 rank, weapon training
class feature with a melee weapon.\
**Benefit:** Once per round, you can use your base attack bonus in place of your total saving throw
bonus for a spell, [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or supernatural ability that
either allows a Reflex save or is not a melee attack and targets only you.
