**Sources:** Blood of the Ancients pg 22

You are able to treat deadly wounds with an almost supernatural skill.\
**Prerequisites:** Heal 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use the [Heal](ref/skill/heal) skill to treat deadly wounds, the target heals
a number of hit points equal to either the result of your Heal check or the normal amount, whichever
is higher. A creature can still benefit from having deadly wounds treated no more than once per day.
