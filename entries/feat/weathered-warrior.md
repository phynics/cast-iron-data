## Weathered Warrior (Combat)

You have a steady hand in any weather.\
**Prerequisites:** [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance).\
**Benefit:** In areas of strong or severe [wind](ref/rule/winds) or when affected by wind effects or
spells that penalize ranged attacks, you take only half the ranged attack penalty. You can make
ranged attacks in windstorm-level winds at a -4 penalty.\
**Normal:** Strong and severe winds impose -2 and -4 penalties on ranged attack rolls, respectively.
Making ranged attacks in windstorms is impossible.
