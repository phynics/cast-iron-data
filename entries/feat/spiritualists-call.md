Whenever you summon your phantom, it is more powerful for a brief period of time.\
**Prerequisites:** Phantom class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you summon your [phantom](ref/sidekick/phantom), you can give it a +2
enhancement bonus to its Strength, Dexterity, or Charisma. This bonus lasts for 10 minutes after the
summoning ritual is complete.
