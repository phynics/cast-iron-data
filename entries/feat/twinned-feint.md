## Twinned Feint (Combat)

You can fool two opponents with a single feint.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can attempt to [feint](ref/rule/feint) against a foe within
your reach. If you succeed at the feint, you can make an additional feint attempt against a foe that
is adjacent to the first one and also within reach. You can attempt only one additional feint per
action with this feat. When you use this feat, you take a -2 penalty to your AC until your next
turn.\
**Special:** If you have the [Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint) feat, you can use this feat
as a move action instead.
