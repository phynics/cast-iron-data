Burdened with heavy responsibility at a young age, you must mature faster than most.\
**Prerequisites:** Rainkin [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc), skilled alternate racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma when you take this feat. This choice cannot be
changed. As an immediate action a number of times per day equal to your modifier in the chosen
attribute, you can gain a +2 circumstance bonus on any skill check that uses the chosen attribute.
You can use this ability after rolling, but must use it before the results of the rolls are
declared.
