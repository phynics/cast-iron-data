You can travel between magic plants and plants with which you have an affinity.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, character level 7th, plant type.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can step into an adjacent plant with which you share affinity, then
exit from a plant of the same kind. This functions as [tree stride](ref/spell/tree-stride) with the
following changes: the duration is 1 round; the transport range is 200 feet per character level;
transporting yourself is a standard action; and a [ghoran](ref/race/ghoran) using this ability can
pass through any living tree, while a [vine leshy](ref/race/vine-leshy) can pass through any living
vine. You can also enter any magically created or enhanced plants, such as those produced by
[wall of thorns](ref/spell/wall-of-thorns) or plants affected by [entangle](ref/spell/entangle),
then exit from either another similar plant or a plant with which you have an affinity.\
**Special:** The GM determines plant affinity for non-ghoran and non-vine leshy plant creatures.
