## Seeping Darkness (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 209

Your innate magical darkness seeps through your skin and strengthens when you're near another
creature whose soul is as wicked as yours.\
**Prerequisites:** [Drow](ref/race/drow), darkness racial
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you're adjacent to a drow ally who also has this feat, you can spend a move
action to gain [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance) for 1 round.
