Your ranger traps are especially difficult to notice and avoid.\
**Prerequisites:** Trap class feature, ranger level 5th.\
**Benefit:** Add +1 to the Difficulty Class on all Perception and Disable Device skill checks to
find or disable the traps you make with your trap class feature. Add a +1 to the Difficulty Class on
all saving throws against the effects of the trap you make with your trap class feature.
