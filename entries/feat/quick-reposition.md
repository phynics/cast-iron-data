## Quick Reposition (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 114

Your opponent becomes an unwitting dance partner, following your lead while you fight.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Reposition](ref/feat/improved-reposition), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** On your turn, you can perform a single [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat
maneuver in place of one of your melee attacks. You must choose the melee attack with the highest
base attack bonus to make the reposition.\
**Normal:** A reposition combat maneuver is a standard action.
