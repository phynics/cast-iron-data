Your melee blows deal extra damage to creatures sensitive to light, making you adept at hunting foul
monsters of the night.\
**Prerequisites:** [Arcane Strike](ref/feat/arcane-strike), able to prepare or cast daylight.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully hit an enemy that is vulnerable to light (such as by the
[light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness), [light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity), or
[sunlight powerlessness](ref/umr/sunlight-powerlessness) universal monster rules) with a melee
weapon you've imbued with your Arcane Strike feat, you deal an additional 1d6 points of damage to
the target of your attack. The struck creature must also succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC
10 + your character level + your Strength modifier) or become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1
round.
