**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 147

You have dwarven blood coursing through your veins.\
**Prerequisites:** [Oread](ref/race/oread).\
**Benefit:** Your dwarven heritage manifests in two ways. First, your speed is never modified by
armor or encumbrance, as the [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf) slow and steady racial trait. Second, you gain
the stonecunning dwarf racial trait.
