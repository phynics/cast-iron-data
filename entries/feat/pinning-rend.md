## Pinning Rend (Combat)

You tear flesh when you damage an opponent that you have pinned.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.\
**Benefit:** While you have an opponent pinned, when you succeed at a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple)
combat maneuver check to deal an opponent damage using an unarmed strike or a light or one-handed
weapon, that opponent also takes [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage equal to your unarmed strike or
weapon damage dice. Any creature that is immune to critical hits is immune to the effects of this
feat.
