Torag's blessing heals the body and hardens the skin.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 7d6, worshiper of
[Torag](ref/faith/torag).\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you channel positive energy to heal living creatures, you can grant
healed creatures damage resistance. Affected creatures gain DR 2/- (or increase their DR/- by 2, if
they already have DR/-) for a number of minutes equal to your Charisma bonus (minimum 1 minute).
