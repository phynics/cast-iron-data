## Giant's Smash (Combat)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#91: Battle of Bloodmarch Hills pg 69

Obstacles do not concern you.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), giant subtype.\
**Benefit:** When you attack an inanimate, unattended object, you ignore the first 5 points of its
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness). You also receive a +5 bonus on Strength checks to knock down or break
open doors.
