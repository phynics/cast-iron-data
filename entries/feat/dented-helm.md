## Dented Helm (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 18

Your helm protects you from hard hits.\
**Prerequisites:** [Hard-Headed](ref/feat/hard-headed), base attack bonus +6,
[dwarf](ref/race/dwarf).\
**Benefit:** When wearing a helmet, you add +1 to your AC against critical hit confirmation rolls.
When a critical hit is confirmed against you, as an immediate action, you can apply half of the
damage from the attack to your helmet rather than yourself, applying [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) as
normal. If the damage destroys your helmet, any leftover damage is applied to you. After using this
feat, you are [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) until the end of your next turn. You may not use this
feat if your helmet has the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition or the attack ignores armor bonuses
to AC.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 117\
After using this feat, you can spend 5 stamina points to become sickened rather than staggered until
the end of your next turn. You can't use this ability if you are immune to being sickened.
