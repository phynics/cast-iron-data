## Kitsune Vengeance (Combat)

You cleverly hide your attacks within your devious antics.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13; [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise);
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick); [Kitsune Style](ref/feat/kitsune-style);
[Kitsune Tricks](ref/feat/kitsune-tricks); base attack bonus +6 or monk level 6th.\
**Benefit:** While using the Kitsune Style feat, when a foe provokes an attack of opportunity from
you, you can attempt to perform a [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver in place of
making a melee attack.
