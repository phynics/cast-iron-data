You spent time as a squire to a Taldan knight and understand how to fight alongside him.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, proficient in a martial weapon.\
**Benefit:** When within 50 feet of a mounted ally with the
[Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat) feat, you get a +2 bonus on initiative. If this ally is
injured, you gain a +1 dodge bonus on AC as long as you are within 10 feet of him.
