## Armor Adept (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 11

You are skilled at using customized armor.\
**Benefit:** Choose two armor modifications (such as [deflecting](ref/eq-armorenh/deflecting) or
[jarring](ref/eq-armorenh/jarring)). You no longer suffer the drawbacks of wearing armor with those
modifications.\
**Normal:** Each type of armor modification imparts a drawback on a wearer.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times, choosing two different armor modifications each
time.
