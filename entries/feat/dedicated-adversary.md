## Dedicated Adversary (Combat)

Your repeated encounters with a particular type of enemy have honed your skills against that foe.\
**Prerequisites:** No levels in a class that has the [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy)
class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you select this feat, choose a specific kind of creature, such as
[wolf](ref/monster/wolf), [frost giant](ref/monster/giant-frost-frost-giant),
[goblin](ref/monster/goblin), or [babau demon](ref/monster/demon-babau). You gain the ranger's
favored enemy class ability against this particular type of creature with a bonus of +2. This feat
does not grant the favored enemy class feature for the purposes of prerequisites. If you later gain
the favored enemy class feature, you can replace this feat with a feat for which you qualify and
whose prerequisites include the favored enemy class feature.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a different creature.
