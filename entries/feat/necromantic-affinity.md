**Sources:** Inner Sea World Guide pg 287

Long exposure to necromantic energies has granted you a small measure of resistance against them.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws made to resist spells and effects that utilize
negative energy, cause negative levels, or cause ability damage, ability drain, or ability
penalties. In addition, damage caused by inflict spells heals you as if you were an undead creature,
but you also take damage from cure spells.
