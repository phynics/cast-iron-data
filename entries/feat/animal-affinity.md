**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 118

You are skilled at working with animals and mounts.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on all Handle Animal and Ride skill checks. If you have 10 or more
ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

### Mythic Animal Affinity

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 55\
You have a greater affinity with wild animals, and can speak with them.\
**Prerequisites:** Animal Affinity.\
**Benefit:** The bonus on Handle Animal and Ride skill checks from Animal Affinity increases by 2.
In addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) as the spell. Your caster level for this effect
is equal to twice your tier.
