## Stick-Fighting Maneuver (Combat)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 12

Your attacks with sticks have become blindingly fast, and you can throw in a combat maneuver between
your attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Stick-Fighting Counter](ref/feat/stick-fighting-counter),
[Stick-Fighting Style](ref/feat/stick-fighting-style), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** When making a full attack using only [clubs](ref/eq-weapon/club), a
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), [dan bongs](ref/eq-weapon/dan-bong),
[saps](ref/eq-weapon/sap), or [tonfas](ref/eq-weapon/tonfa), you can perform a combat maneuver as an
extra attack using your full base attack bonus.
