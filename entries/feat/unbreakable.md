## Unbreakable (Combat)

As a veteran [Gray Maiden](ref/rule/aff-scarred-ladies), you can endure a staggering amount of
punishment.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness), fighter
level 4th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you gain 1 hit point as a fighter [favored class](ref/rule/favored-class)
benefit, you gain an additional hit point. Treat your effective Constitution score as being 4 higher
for the purpose of determining when you would die from hit point damage. These benefits are
retroactive.
