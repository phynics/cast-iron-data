You are deadly when you team up with allies against a single foe.\
**Prerequisites:** Bane class feature.\
**Benefit:** You can use your bane class feature to imbue a melee weapon with the
[menacing](ref/magic-enh/menacing) special weapon ability instead of [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane). You
can spend a swift action to switch between the two special weapon abilities. Doing so otherwise
works according to your bane class feature.\
**Special:** If you have the [Double Bane](ref/feat/double-bane) feat, you can imbue each weapon you
wield with either bane or menacing. No single weapon can have both.
