## Elephant Stomp (Combat)

**Sources:** Sargava, the Lost Colony pg 24

You deliver a crushing blow to downed enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** When you [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) an opponent and your maneuver check exceeds your
opponent's CMD by 5 or more, instead of moving through your opponent's space and knocking her
[prone](ref/rule/prone), you may stop in the space directly in front of the opponent (or the nearest
adjacent space) and make one attack with an unarmed strike or a natural weapon against that opponent
as an immediate action.\
**Normal:** When your overrun maneuver check exceeds your opponent's CMD by 5 or more, you move
through the target's space and she is knocked prone.
