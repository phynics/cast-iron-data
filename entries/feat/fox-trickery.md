## Fox Trickery (Combat)

Whenever a foes drops his guard, you can make him suffer with a dirty trick.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Fox Insight](ref/feat/fox-insight), [Fox Style](ref/feat/fox-style),
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick).

While using Fox Style, you can perform [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuvers as
attacks of opportunity. If your Intelligence is at least 19, you gain a +4 bonus on dirty trick
combat maneuver checks.
