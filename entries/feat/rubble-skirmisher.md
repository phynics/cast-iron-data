## Rubble Skirmisher (Combat)

You use difficult terrain to your advantage when performing combat maneuvers.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Nimble Moves](ref/feat/nimble-moves), base attack bonus +2.\
**Benefit:** When you are in a square that has naturally occurring
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) (bushes, rubble, undergrowth, and so forth), you
gain a +2 circumstance bonus on combat maneuver checks and to your CMD.
