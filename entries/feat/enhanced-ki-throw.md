## Enhanced Ki Throw (Combat)

You enhance the force of your ki throws, enabling you to devastate your opponents as you slam them
to the ground.\
**Prerequisites:** Ki pool class feature, [Ki Throw](ref/feat/ki-throw).\
**Benefit:** When using the Ki Throw feat, you can expend 1 ki point to amplify the force of your
attack. If your ki throw succeeds, when the target hits the ground, it takes damage as if you had
hit it with an unarmed strike.
