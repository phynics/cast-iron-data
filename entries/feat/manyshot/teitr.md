**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
You can fire multiple arrows at a single target.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 17, [Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot), [Rapid Shot](ref/feat/rapid-shot),
base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** When making a full-attack action with a bow, crossbow, or sling, your first attack
fires two projectiles. If the attack hits, both arrows, bolts, or bullets hit. Apply precision-based
damage (such as [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack)) and critical hit damage only once for this
attack. Strength bonuses apply to each projectile, as do other damage bonuses, such as a ranger's
[favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) bonus. [Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and
resistances apply separately to each arrow, bolt, or bullet.
