**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 137

When you discard your host, you alter or obliterate its memory of your handiwork.\
**Prerequisites:** Int or Cha 19, [Hidden Presence](ref/feat/hidden-presence), ability to cast
possession or greater possession.\
**Benefit:** Upon completion of your possession, you can alter up to 5 minutes of your host's
memories as you see fit. This functions as the [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory) spell. Your
host's awareness of the possession doesn't impact this ability, and if your control of the host
lasted less than 5 minutes, you can cause it to forget the possession entirely. The host can attempt
a Will save with a DC equal to that of the original possession effect to resist this alteration.
