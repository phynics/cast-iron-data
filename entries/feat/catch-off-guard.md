## Catch Off-Guard (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 119

Foes are surprised by your skilled use of unorthodox and
[improvised weapons](ref/misc/improvised-weapons).\
**Benefit:** You do not suffer any penalties for using an improvised melee weapon. Unarmed opponents
are [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against any attacks you make with an improvised melee
weapon.\
**Normal:** You take a -4 penalty on attack rolls made with an improvised weapon.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 115\
When making an attack with an improvised weapon against an armed opponent, you can spend 5 stamina
points. If you do, the target is considered flat-footed during that attack. An opponent who
recognizes you and has seen you use Catch Off-Guard in this way is immune to this effect.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
Foes are surprised by your skilled use of unorthodox and improvised weapons.\
