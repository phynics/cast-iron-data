**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 126

Your channeled energy is harder to resist.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** Add 2 to the DC of saving throws made to resist the effects of your channel energy
ability.

### Mythic Improved Channel

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 66\
As a divine conduit for energy, you're unmatched.\
**Prerequisites:** Improved Channel.\
**Benefit:** Non-mythic creatures that take damage from your channel energy must roll their saving
throws twice and take the lower result.
