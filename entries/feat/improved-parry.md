## Improved Parry (Combat)

A flick of the wrist binds your opponent's weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise).\
**Benefit:** When you successfully parry a foe's melee attack (with a
[dueling parry](ref/rule/dueling-parry) or the parry class feature), your next melee attack against
the target does not allow the enemy to apply its Dexterity bonus to AC (if any). This attack must be
made before the end of your next turn.
