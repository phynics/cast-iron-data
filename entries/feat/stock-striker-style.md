## Stock-Striker Style (Combat, Style)

Your fighting technique makes you equally deadly with your firearm's stock as with its bullets.\
**Prerequisites:** [Pistol-whip](ref/deed/pistol-whip) gunslinger deed.\
**Benefit:** When you perform the pistol-whip deed while using this style, you gain a +1 bonus on
the attack roll and on the combat maneuver check to knock the target [prone](ref/rule/prone); this
increases to +2 when you use a two-handed firearm. If you successfully knock the target prone, you
do not provoke attacks of opportunity from the target until the end of your next turn when you make
ranged attacks with your firearm. You can treat one-handed firearms as light weapons for the purpose
of abilities such as [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) when performing the pistol-whip deed.
