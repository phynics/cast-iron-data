## Pinpoint Jab (Combat)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 26

You strikes land exactly where you mean them to.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), and either a ki pool
or martial flexibility class feature.

As a full-round action, you can expend 1 ki point or one daily use of martial flexibility to use a
limited version of the targeted strike deed, targeting either hands or legs with an unarmed attack.
A target who succeeds at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + your base attack bonus) can negate the effect.
When your base attack bonus reaches +11, you can instead target head or torso, which the target can
negate with a Fortitude save. When your BAB reaches +16, you can target two locations; the target
must save against each effect separately.\
**Special:** A monk can substitute his class level for his base attack bonus for this feat's
effects.
