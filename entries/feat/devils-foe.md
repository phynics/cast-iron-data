## Devil's Foe (Achievement)

**Sources:** Andoran, Spirit of Liberty pg 13

Once you were just another helpless victim, but now even the deepest pits of Hell ring with the
lamentations of your adversaries.\
**Prerequisites:** Face 10 different [devils](ref/family/devil) in combat and take damage or suffer
some other sort of injury from five or more attacks made by each one without falling unconscious,
fleeing, or otherwise becoming unable to strike back at them.\
**Benefit:** Your attacks count as good-aligned for the purposes of bypassing the
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) of devils.
