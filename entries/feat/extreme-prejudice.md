## Extreme Prejudice (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 147

Your hatred of a particular type of creature has honed your killer instinct.\
**Prerequisites:** [Seething Hatred](ref/feat/seething-hatred),
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +3d6, studied target class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a sneak attack against a creature you selected as the target of
your Seething Hatred feat, you use d8s to roll sneak attack damage instead of d6s.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 119\
When you succeed at a sneak attack against a creature that is not the target of your Seething Hatred
feat, you can spend 5 stamina points to treat the creature as if it were the target of your Seething
Hatred for that attack.
