Your intimate knowledge of anatomy, deep hatred of your own kind, and delight in spilling your
people's blood make you a lethal combatant against your own race.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge 3 ranks (see special), evil alignment.\
**Benefit:** Your attacks deal an additional 1d6 points of precision damage against creatures that
have the same creature type (and subtype, if applicable) as you. This bonus damage stacks with other
sources of precision damage, such as [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack). If you have more than
one subtype, you must choose one of them - this feat applies only to targets of that subtype. Once
made, this choice can't be changed.\
**Special:** The [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill used to qualify for this feat must be
appropriate for identifying creatures of your type.
