Your eyes have grown accustomed to the half-light of dusk and dawn.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sharp Senses](ref/feat/sharp-senses), Perception 7 ranks, keen senses racial
trait, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).\
**Benefit:** Creatures within an area of [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) don't gain
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from you. You're still effectively [blinded](ref/misc/blinded)
in an area of darkness.\
**Normal:** Creatures within an area of dim light gain concealment (20% miss chance in combat) from
those without [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) or the ability to see in darkness.
