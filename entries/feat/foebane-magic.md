The spells you cast are particularly effective against your favored enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast 1st-level spells, [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy)
class feature.\
**Benefit:** Your favored enemies take a -1 penalty on saving throws against spells you cast.
Furthermore, you gain your favored enemy bonus on Spellcraft checks to identify spells cast by
favored enemies.
