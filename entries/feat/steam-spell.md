## Steam Spell (Metamagic)

**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 59

You've built off of some of the closely-guarded secrets of the undine steamcasters, allowing you to
use fire magic underwater easily.\
**Benefit:** You can apply Steam Spell only to a spell with the fire descriptor. A steam spell works
underwater without a caster level check, but it requires a caster level check to function above
water. A steam spell uses up a slot the same spell level as the spell's actual level.
