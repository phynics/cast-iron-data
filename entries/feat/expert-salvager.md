You are adept at [foraging](ref/rule/foraging) and [salvaging](ref/rule/salvaging) raw materials
from even the most seemingly sparse of environments.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (any) 2 ranks, Spellcraft 2 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on Craft checks for Craft skills in which you have at least 2 ranks
and Spellcraft checks when crafting items by foraging alchemical supplies and material components,
salvaging raw crafting materials, and salvaging raw magical item materials.
