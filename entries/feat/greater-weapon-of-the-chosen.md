## Greater Weapon of the Chosen (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 149

Your deity guides your hand when you fight with her favored weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Weapon of the Chosen](ref/feat/improved-weapon-of-the-chosen),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with deity's favored weapon,
[Weapon of the Chosen](ref/feat/weapon-of-the-chosen), worship and receive spells from a deity.\
**Benefit:** When you use your deity's favored weapon to attempt a single attack with the attack
action, you roll two dice for your attack roll and take the higher result. You do not need to use
your Weapon of the Chosen feat to gain this feat's benefit. As usual, the reroll does not apply to
any confirmation rolls.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 122\
When making an attack as part of a full attack with the weapon designated by your Weapon of the
Chosen feat, you can spend 5 stamina points to gain the benefits of this feat on that attack.
