## Orc Snarl (Combat)

Your attacks leave your opponent quivering in fear.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bullying Blow](ref/feat/bullying-blow),
[Intimidating Prowess](ref/feat/intimidating-prowess), [Orc Fury Style](ref/feat/orc-fury-style),
[Orc Rampage](ref/feat/orc-rampage), Intimidate 7 ranks, weapon familiarity racial trait.\
**Benefit:** While using Orc Fury Style, you gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against effects
created by [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) foes.
