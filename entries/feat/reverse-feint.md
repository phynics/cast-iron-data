## Reverse-Feint (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 142

You can goad an opponent into attacking you in order to make your counter attack all the more
powerful.\
**Prerequisites:** [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness), base attack bonus +1, [orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** As a move action, you can leave a gap in your defenses for one adjacent opponent to
use. If the opponent attacks you on its next turn, it gains a +4 bonus on its attack roll. Whether
or not the opponent successfully hits, you may attack it as an immediate action with a single melee
attack, gaining a +2 bonus on your attack roll.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 130\
You can spend 2 stamina points to make the attack granted by this feat as a free action rather than
an immediate action. You can still make the attack only once per use of this feat.
