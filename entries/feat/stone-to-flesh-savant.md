You can use your mercies to transform petrified victims back to flesh.\
**Prerequisites:** Paladin level 12th, [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) class feature, mercy
class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain a unique mercy, which aids victims of petrification. Whenever you use your lay
on hands ability to heal damage, you can emulate [stone to flesh](ref/spell/stone-to-flesh) on the
target, using your paladin level as your caster level. This functions only on a creature that has
been petrified, and it has no effect on other stone.
