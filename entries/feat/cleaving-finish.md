## Cleaving Finish (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 92

When you strike down an opponent, you can continue your swing into another target.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Cleave](ref/feat/cleave), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack).\
**Benefit:** If you make a melee attack, and your target drops to 0 or fewer hit points as a result
of your attack, you can make another melee attack using your highest base attack bonus against
another opponent within reach. You can make only one extra attack per round with this feat.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 115\
After making a Cleaving Finish attack, if you bring another target below 0 hit points in the same
round, you can spend 5 stamina points to make another Cleaving Finish attack.
