You are granted a small bit of divine power as a result of your veneration of a pantheon of deities.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, must worship a pantheon.\
**Benefit:** When you take this feat, select a pantheon from the list below. You gain the listed
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) for your selected pantheon, usable once per day.
If the granted spell allows a saving throw, the DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 your character level + your
Charisma modifier.\
**Special:** If you act out of line with the tenets of your pantheon's beliefs, you lose the ability
to cast the granted spell-like ability until you have received an [atonement](ref/spell/atonement)
for your transgression (as a [cleric](ref/class/cleric) attempting to regain lost class abilities).

### Juju Pantheon (Cultural)

**Deities** wendo (supernatural entities of the spirit world)

**Common Believers** juju practitioners, wendifa

**Granted Spell-like Ability** [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)

### Sar-Gorog Pantheon (Aligned and Cultural)

**Deities** [Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa) (NE), [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug) (CE), and
[Zura](ref/faith/zura) (CE)

**Common Believers** Koboto cannibal tribes of the Sodden Lands

**Granted Spell-like Ability** [decompose corpse](ref/spell/decompose-corpse); if you use this
ability on a corpse rather than on a corporeal undead, you receive nourishment as though you had
eaten a normal meal for a Medium creature.

**Note:** This feat was originally titled _Pantheistic Blessing._
