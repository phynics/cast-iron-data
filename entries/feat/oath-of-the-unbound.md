You have sworn never to be shackled.\
**Prerequisites:** Worshiper of [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to break free of a
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) or pin, on Strength checks to burst bonds or to break through doors or
walls, and on saving throws to resist spells and effects that would entangle or slow you.
