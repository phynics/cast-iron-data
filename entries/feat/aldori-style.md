## Aldori Style (Combat, Style)

You have learned the classic Aldori dueling stance.\
**Prerequisites:** [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
([Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword)),
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (Aldori dueling
sword).\
**Benefit:** While using Aldori Style and wielding only a single Aldori dueling sword in one hand
(not using a shield, an off-hand weapon, armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons), when
you [fight defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or use
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), you gain a +2 bonus on weapon damage rolls.
