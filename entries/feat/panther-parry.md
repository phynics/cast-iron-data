## Panther Parry (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 112

Your vicious strikes impair your foe's ability to attack you when you move.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Panther Claw](ref/feat/panther-claw),
[Panther Style](ref/feat/panther-style).\
**Benefit:** While using Panther Style, your retaliatory unarmed strikes are resolved before the
triggering attacks. If your retaliatory unarmed strike deals damage to an opponent, that opponent
takes a -2 penalty on attack and damage rolls with the triggering attack of opportunity.
