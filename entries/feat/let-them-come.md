## Let Them Come (Combat, Faction)

You have trained to fight onrushing enemy hordes. With a carefully timed strike you ensure that
their reckless charge will be their undoing.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), [Ninth Battalion](ref/rule/f-legion) 25 TPA.\
**Benefit:** You treat all one- or two-handed melee weapons as if they had the
[brace](ref/misc/brace) special feature.\
**Normal:** Only weapons with the brace special feature can be readied against
[charges](ref/rule/charge).
