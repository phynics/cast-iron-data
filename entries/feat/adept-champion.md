**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 89

You can alter your smite ability, channeling the power of your deity into divine inspiration that
grants you greater aptitude for performing combat maneuvers.\
**Prerequisites:** Smite evil class feature, base attack bonus +5.\
**Benefit:** While using your smite evil class feature, as a swift action at the start of your turn,
you can forgo the bonus on damage rolls and instead gain half that bonus as a bonus on combat
maneuver checks against the target of your smite. The effects of your smite evil feature return to
normal at the start of your next turn.
