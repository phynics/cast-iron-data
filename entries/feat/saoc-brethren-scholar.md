You have learned to draw additional power from celestial phenomena, harnessing their influence for
longer than their natural duration.\
**Prerequisites:** [Saoc Brethren Initiate](ref/feat/saoc-brethren-initiate), Knowledge (arcana) 5
ranks, Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks, Knowledge (planes) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You increase the duration of effects caused by
[astrological events](ref/rule/astrological-events). The duration of effects that last less than a
day is increased by 50%, while those that last 24 hours or longer last 1 additional day.
