You can call plant creatures to your aid in battle.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (nature) 1 rank, ability to cast summon nature's ally spells.\
**Benefit:** When casting a _[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell, you gain
access to the list of plant creatures below.

[Summon nature's ally I:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1)
[Leaf leshy](ref/monster/leshy-leaf-leshy), [vegepygmy](ref/monster/vegepygmy).

[Summon nature's ally II:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)
[Gourd leshy](ref/monster/leshy-gourd-leshy), [leaf ray](ref/monster/leaf-ray) (without seed
ability).

[Summon nature's ally III:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3)
[Fungus leshy](ref/monster/leshy-fungus-leshy), [weedwhip](ref/monster/weedwhip).

[Summon nature's ally IV:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4)
[Assassin vine](ref/monster/assassin-vine), [seaweed leshy](ref/monster/leshy-seaweed-leshy)
(without air cyst ability).

[Summon nature's ally V:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5) [Mandragora](ref/monster/mandragora),
[shambling mound](ref/monster/shambling-mound).

[Summon nature's ally VI:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-6) [Tendriculos](ref/monster/tendriculos),
[treant](ref/monster/treant).

[Summon nature's ally VII:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-7)
[Giant flytrap](ref/monster/giant-flytrap).

[Summon nature's ally VIII:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-8) [Jinmenju](ref/monster/jinmenju).

[Summon nature's ally IX:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-9) [Alraune](ref/monster/alraune).
