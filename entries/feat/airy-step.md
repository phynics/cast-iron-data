The air responds to your innate elemental nature, protecting you from harm and cushioning your
falls.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sylph](ref/race/sylph).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against effects with the air or electricity
descriptors and effects that deal electricity damage. You may ignore the first 30 feet of any fall
when determining [falling](ref/rule/falling) damage.
