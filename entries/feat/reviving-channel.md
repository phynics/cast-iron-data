**Sources:** Distant Realms pg 33

You can target an unconscious ally to gain additional benefit from your channeled energy.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 3d6.\
**Benefit:** When you use channel energy, you can focus on one creature within range that would be
healed by your channel energy whose hit points are below 0. When you do so, no other creatures are
healed, but the target creature is restored to 0 hit points before the normal healing takes effect.
