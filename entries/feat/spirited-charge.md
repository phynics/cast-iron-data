## Spirited Charge (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 134

Your mounted charge attacks deal a tremendous amount of damage.\
**Prerequisites:** Ride 1 rank, [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat).\
**Benefit:** When mounted and using the [charge](ref/rule/charge) action, you deal double damage
with a melee weapon (or triple damage with a lance).

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 132\
When making a mounted charge, you can spend a number of stamina points up to your Strength bonus. If
your charge attack hits, you deal an amount of extra damage equal to the number of stamina points
you spent. This additional damage is doubled by this feat (or tripled if you're using a lance).

### Mythic Spirited Charge

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 74\
Your mounted charge knocks the wind and wits from those foolish enough to stand against you.\
**Prerequisites:** Spirited Charge.\
**Benefit:** When you hit an opponent with a mounted charge, that opponent must succeed at a
Fortitude saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Str modifier) or be
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for a number of rounds equal to your tier. If you use a lance with
this attack, increase the saving throw DC by 2. As a free action before you make the attack, you can
expend one use of mythic power to [daze](ref/misc/dazed) your opponent on a failed saving throw
instead of staggering it.
