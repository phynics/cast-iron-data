Your healing spells are more potent when you have a judgment active.\
**Prerequisites:** Judgment class feature.\
**Benefit:** If you cast a _cure_ spell while you have a judgment active, each target regains 1
extra hit point from the _cure_ spell + 1 hit point per three inquisitor levels you possess.
