## Skyseeker Impact (Combat)

You've been trained to devastate larger opponents and shatter their weapons to negate their reach
advantage.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [Skyseeker Style](ref/feat/skyseeker-style),
[Skyseeker Thrash](ref/feat/skyseeker-thrash), [Step Up](ref/feat/step-up), base attack bonus +9.\
**Benefit:** While using Skyseeker Style, whenever you hit an opponent larger than you with a melee
attack, your weapon deals damage as if that weapon were
[one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments). You also gain a +4 bonus on
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver checks against targets larger than you.
