## Sorcerous Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 120

The power flowing through your veins also flows through your unarmed strike.\
**Prerequisites:** Sorcerer bloodline class feature,
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** When you gain this feat, you choose one [bloodline](ref/ability/sorcerer-bloodlines)
power that you can use to affect a single opponent. If you make a successful unarmed strike against
an opponent, in addition to dealing your unarmed strike damage, you can spend a swift action to
deliver the effects of the chosen bloodline power to that opponent. Doing so provokes no attacks of
opportunity.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you take it, you apply it to a
different qualifying bloodline power.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 132\
After hitting with an unarmed strike, you can spend 2 stamina points to apply the effects of your
chosen bloodline power as a free action instead of a swift action.
