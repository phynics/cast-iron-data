Your excellent coordination allows you to maneuver better in close quarters.\
**Prerequisites:** Escape Artist 1 rank, animal or magical beast.\
**Benefit:** You do not take penalties on your attack rolls or to your AC for
[squeezing](ref/misc/squeezing) through a narrow space that is at least half as wide as your normal
space, though each move into or through a narrow space still counts as if it were 2 squares.\
**Normal:** You take a -4 penalty on attack rolls and a -4 penalty to AC while squeezing.
