## Alien Mindpaths (Teamwork)

When you're in the company of other aliens, your thoughts become difficult to follow.\
**Prerequisites:** [Android](ref/race/android), [kasatha](ref/race/kasatha),
[lashunta](ref/race/lashunta), or [Triaxian](ref/race/triaxian).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you're adjacent to at least one other android, kasatha, lashunta, or Triaxian
ally who also has this feat, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on saves against mind-affecting
effects and divination (scrying) effects, unless the effect comes from an android, kasatha,
lashunta, or Triaxian.
