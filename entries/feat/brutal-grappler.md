## Brutal Grappler (Combat, Teamwork)

When helping an ally grapple, you can damage the target.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** When you and an ally with this feat have grappled the same creature, you may attempt to
damage the opponent on your turn (whether you were the one who first initiated the grapple or were
assisting your ally in doing so). You and the ally are treated as aiding each other in the grapple
(+2 bonus on your grapple combat maneuver) as long as you both decide to maintain the grapple.\
**Normal:** When multiple creatures grapple one target, the creature that first initiates the
grapple is the only one that makes a check to damage a grappled opponent, with a +2 bonus for each
creature that assists the grapple using the [Aid Another](ref/rule/aid-another) action.
