## Panther Claw (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 112

You unleash a rapid series of blows on foes that attempt to attack you when you move.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Panther Style](ref/feat/panther-style).\
**Benefit:** While using Panther Style, you can spend a free action, instead of spending a swift
action, to make a retaliatory unarmed strike. You can make a number of retaliatory unarmed strikes
on your turn equal to your Wisdom modifier.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 128\
When making a retaliatory strike, you can spend up to 5 stamina points to gain a bonus on the damage
roll equal to double the number of stamina points you spent.
