The Fire God rewards you for killing enemies with flame.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [orc](ref/race/orc), worshiper of the Fire God
([Sezelrian](ref/faith/sezelrian)).\
**Benefit:** When in combat, if you deal fire damage to an enemy, you heal 1 hit point. You can only
benefit from this healing once per round. Attacks that cause a target to catch on fire heal you each
round the target takes fire damage.
