## Greater Two-Weapon Fighting (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 126

You are incredibly skilled at fighting with two weapons at the same time.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 19, [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/improved-two-weapon-fighting),
[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting), base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** You get a third attack with your off-hand weapon, albeit at a -10 penalty.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
You are skilled at fighting with two weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, Two-Weapon Fighting, base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** In addition to the standard single extra attack you get with an off-hand weapon, you
get a second attack with it, albeit at a -5 penalty. When your base attack bonus reaches +11, you
also gain a third attack with your off-hand weapon, albeit at a -10 penalty.\
**Normal:** Without this feat, you can only get a single extra attack with an off-hand weapon.
