Your scrying spells last longer.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast a spell of the [scrying](ref/spelldef/scrying) subschool.\
**Benefit:** Any scrying spell you cast lasts twice as long as normal, as if it were cast as an
extended spell with the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) metamagic feat. Additionally, when you
cast a scrying spell with a casting time of 10 minutes or longer, that casting time is halved.
