## Redistributed Might (Combat)

You've gained enough control over your body to translate strength into swiftness.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, Wis 13, [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
(aldori dueling sword), [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will), base attack bonus +4.\
**Benefit:** Whenever an effect would grant you an enhancement or morale bonus to your Strength
score (such as via [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength) or a [skald's](ref/class/skald)
inspired rage performance), you can instead apply that bonus to your Dexterity score. You make this
choice when you first gain the bonus. The bonus type does not change.
