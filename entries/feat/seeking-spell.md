## Seeking Spell (Metamagic)

You can cast spells that bend around barriers to reach their intended destination.\
**Benefit:** A seeking spell's range can bend around obstacles to reach the intended target. You can
define the route yourself or unambiguously identify a target and allow the spell to determine its
own path. However, the spell fails if it would have to travel farther than its maximum range to
reach the identified target. A ranged attack roll made to deliver a seeking spell is not subject to
[cover](ref/rule/cover) or [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). In order to benefit from this feat,
the selected spell must have a range greater than touch and target one or more creatures, or it must
require the caster to make a ranged touch attack. A seeking spell uses up a spell slot 2 levels
higher than the spell's actual level.
