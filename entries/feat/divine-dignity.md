Secrets of the First Vault help you perfect your spellcasting.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Casting](ref/feat/combat-casting), ability to cast divine spells,
worshiper of [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar).\
**Benefit:** Three times per day, as a free action when you cast a harmless divine spell or a domain
spell, you can choose for the casting to not provoke attacks of opportunity.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. When you do, you can use this feat three more
times per day.
