**Sources:** Classic Monsters Revisited pg 43

Your charge sends foes reeling away, knocking them to the ground.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), powerful charge.\
**Benefit:** When you hit an opponent with a [charge](ref/rule/charge) attack, you might also
initiate a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) as a free action. This does not require you to move into
your opponent's square and does not provoke an attack of opportunity. If you successfully push your
opponent 15 feet or more, he is also knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone).
