## Medium Armor Proficiency (Combat)

You are skilled at wearing medium armor.\
**Prerequisites:** [Light Armor Proficiency](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-light).\
**Benefit:** See Light Armor Proficiency.\
**Normal:** See Light Armor Proficiency.\
**Special:** Aristocrats, barbarians, bloodragers, cavaliers, clerics, druids, fighters, hunters,
inquisitors, mediums, occultists, oracles, paladins, rangers, shamans, shifters, skalds, slayers,
vigilantes, and warriors automatically have Medium Armor Proficiency as a bonus feat. They need not
select it.
