**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 120

Your [fragile](ref/misc/fragile) weapon works to your advantage, breaking off fragments in wounds
you inflict.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, proficient with weapon, weapon made of primitive material.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use a melee or thrown weapon with the fragile weapon feature or similar
quality and hit an opponent, you can give your weapon the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition to
deal that opponent 1d4 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage.
