Wildborn who grow up together are closely attuned to the presence of others and carry this skill
throughout their lives.\
**Prerequisites:** Perception 3 ranks, wildborn [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** You are aware of the location of all allies within 60 feet, even if they are hiding,
invisible, or otherwise could not normally be detected.
