**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 110, Heroes of the Wild pg 26

You can draw raw magical energy from the ground to empower your spells.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast 1st-level spells, [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain)
class feature.\
**Benefit:** While you're in your favored terrain, your effective caster level increases by 1 for
the purpose of improving spell effects dependent on caster level. This increase in effective caster
level doesn't grant you access to more spells.
