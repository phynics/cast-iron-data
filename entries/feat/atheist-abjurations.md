Your critical attitude toward the divine allows you to determine the best ways to remove divine
influence.\
**Prerequisites:** [Divine Defiance](ref/feat/divine-defiance).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus to your caster level whenever you use an abjuration spell to dispel
or counter a divine spell, or send an extraplanar outsider summoned or called by a divine caster
back to its home plane.
