## Demon Grafter (Item Creation)

Your studies into demonic anatomy and physiology allow you to graft their immortal tissues onto
mortal flesh.\
**Prerequisites:** [Demonologist](ref/feat/demonologist), Heal 5 ranks, Knowledge (planes) 5 ranks,
Spellcraft 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Heal checks regarding evil outsiders or creatures that already
have a demonic graft. In addition, you can create demonic implants as if you had the
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item) feat, using your ranks in Heal as your caster
level. The DC to create the item still increases for any necessary spell requirements.
