You can modify your spell's superficial qualities in a greater variety of ways.\
**Prerequisites:** [Stylized Spell](ref/feat/stylized-spell), Bluff 10 ranks, Spellcraft 10 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you modify a spell with Stylized Spell, you can apply one of the stylized magic
options (see the Stylized Spell metamagic feat) without reducing the DC of the check to identify the
spell. In addition, you can apply up to two additional stylized magic options, reducing the DC of
checks to identify the spell by 5 per option as normal.
