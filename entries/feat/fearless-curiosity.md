Your desire to see and experience the world overrides healthy caution.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [human](ref/race/human).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on saving throws against effects with the emotion descriptor. In
addition, for any round in which you begin your turn affected by a fear effect, you gain a new save
at the beginning of your turn to reduce the severity of the fear effect, from
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked) to [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), frightened to
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken), and shaken to unaffected.
