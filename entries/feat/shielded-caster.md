## Shielded Caster (Teamwork)

Your allies cover you while you cast complicated spells.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, you receive a +4
competence bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks. If your ally is wielding a
buckler or a light shield, this bonus increases by +1. If your ally is wielding a heavy shield or a
tower shield, this bonus increases by +2. Finally, if an enemy threatening you and your ally has the
[Disruptive](ref/feat/disruptive) feat, or another ability that increases the DC of concentration
checks, the amount of the increase is halved.
