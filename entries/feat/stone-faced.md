Rocks show more expression than you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf).\
**Benefit:** You receive a +4 bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to lie or conceal your
feelings or true motives, but not to [feint](ref/rule/feint) in combat or deliver secret messages.
In addition, the DC for [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks to get a hunch about you is 25
rather than 20.
