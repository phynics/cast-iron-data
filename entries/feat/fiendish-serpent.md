While transforming into a serpent is most common among fiendish servants of serpentine demigods such
as Abraxas or Geryon, worshipers of any fiend might assume this shape.\
**Prerequisites:** Bipedal body shape, non-native [outsider](ref/type/outsider) with the evil
subtype.\
**Benefit:** You slither upon a serpentine tail that has replaced your legs. This grants you a climb
speed and swim speed equal to your base speed, and you can't be tripped.
