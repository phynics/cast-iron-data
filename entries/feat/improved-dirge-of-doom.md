The foreboding tone of your dirge is especially effective at unsettling your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to perform [dirge of doom](ref/performance/dirge-of-doom).\
**Benefit:** The range of your dirge of doom ability is extended to 60 feet. Additionally, if a
creature is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) from another effect, the effect of your dirge of doom is
changed to [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for that specific creature. This benefit cannot cause a
creature to become [panicked](ref/misc/panicked), even if a target is already frightened from
another effect.\
**Normal:** The range of dirge of doom is 30 feet. A creature that is already shaken cannot become
frightened by dirge of doom. Once affected by this feat, a creature cannot be affected by it again
for 24 hours.
