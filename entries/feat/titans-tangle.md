## Titan's Tangle (Combat)

Even creatures much bigger than you can lose their balance when you apply the appropriate leverage.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip), [Tangled Limbs](ref/feat/tangled-limbs),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** Choose one weapon with the [reach](ref/misc/reach) special quality. When you attempt a
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver check with the chosen weapon, you can try to trip creatures
that are up to two size categories larger than you. If you have the
[Greater Weapon Focus](ref/feat/greater-weapon-focus) feat with the chosen weapon, you can attempt
to trip creatures up to three size categories larger than you are.\
**Normal:** You can trip only an opponent who is no more than one size category larger than you.\
**Special:** In addition to the chosen weapon, a character with the weapon training class feature
can use Titan's Tangle with any melee weapon from any
[fighter weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups) that he has selected with weapon training and that
has the reach special quality.
