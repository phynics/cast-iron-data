## Jaguar Pounce (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 115, Sargava, the Lost Colony pg 24

Your ambushes are especially lethal.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +4.\
**Benefit:** When making a charge or using [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack) against an
opponent who is [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) or [helpless](ref/misc/helpless), you treat your
first melee attack against that target as if you had the
[Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) feat.
