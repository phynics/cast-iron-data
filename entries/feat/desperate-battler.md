## Desperate Battler (Combat)

Your experiences fighting against numerous foes that have already overwhelmed your allies, friends,
and family have forced you to excel at fighting on your own.\
**Benefit:** When no ally is within 10 feet of you and you are not receiving benefits from the
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action, you gain a +1 morale bonus on melee attack and damage
rolls.
