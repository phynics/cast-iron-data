Through violent and torturous conditioning, you have built up a resistance to pain.\
**Prerequisites:** Survived being tortured 10 or more times.\
**Benefit:** If you take nonlethal damage, you may attempt a Fortitude saving throw (DC equal to
10 + the nonlethal damage dealt) to reduce that damage by half. The Endurance feat grants a +4 bonus
on this saving throw.
