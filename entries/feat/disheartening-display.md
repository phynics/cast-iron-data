## Disheartening Display (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 144

Your show of prowess further demoralizes others.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus), base attack bonus +6, proficiency with chosen weapon.\
**Benefit:** When you successfully use Dazzling Display against any [shaken](ref/misc/shaken),
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked) opponents, their fear increases
by one step. An already panicked creature demoralized by this feat [cowers](ref/misc/cowering). Once
affected by this feat, a creature cannot be affected by it again (by you or anyone else) for 24
hours.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 118\
When you affect a creature with this feat, you can spend 5 stamina points to gain the ability to
affect that creature with this feat a second time during the same 24-hour period.
