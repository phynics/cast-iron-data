## Wind Stance (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 137

Your erratic movements make it difficult for enemies to pinpoint your location.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** If you move more than 5 feet this turn, you gain 20%
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) for 1 round against ranged attacks.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 135\
When using this feat, you can spend 5 stamina points to gain 20% concealment for 1 round against all
attacks (not just ranged attacks).
