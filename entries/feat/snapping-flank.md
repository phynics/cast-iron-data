## Snapping Flank (Combat, Teamwork)

You can make a swift bite attack against a flanked foe.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +9, bite attack.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) with an ally who also has this feat, as
a swift action you can make a bite attack against the opponent you and your ally are flanking.
