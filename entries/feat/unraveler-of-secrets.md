You have a knack for learning what others would like to remain hidden.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 15, Knowledge (history) 1 rank, gloomkin [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (history) checks. In addition, you can
cast [object reading](ref/spell/object-reading) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), attempting a Knowledge (history) check in place
of an Appraise check.
