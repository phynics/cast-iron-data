## Hero's Fortune (Hero Point)

Even at the start of your career, it was clear that you had a chance at greatness, and your legend
continues to grow with every adventure.\
**Benefit:** You gain a hero point. The maximum number of [hero points](ref/rule/hero-points) you
can have at any one time is increased to 5.\
**Normal:** You can have no more than 3 hero points at one time.\
**Special:** NPCs who take this feat receive 1 hero point and can have up to 3 (not 5).
