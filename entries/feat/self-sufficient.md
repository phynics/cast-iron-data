**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 133

You know how to get along in the wild and how to effectively treat wounds.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on all Heal checks and Survival checks. If you have 10 or more ranks
in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

### Mythic Self-Sufficient

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 72\
Independence comes naturally to you, and you have mastered taking care of yourself.\
**Prerequisites:** Self-Sufficient.\
**Benefit:** The bonus on Heal and Survival skill checks from Self-Sufficient increases by 2. In
addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Heal or Survival check as if you had
rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
