**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 24\
During any combat that is not a duel, you can spend 1 stamina point at the start of your turn to
treat the combat as a duel until the start of your next turn for the purpose of gaining the benefits
of the Aldori Dueling Discipline feat.
