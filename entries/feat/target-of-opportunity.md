## Target of Opportunity (Combat, Teamwork)

You and your allies pelt your enemies with a deadly barrage of missiles.\
**Prerequisites:** [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** When an ally who also has this feat makes a ranged attack and hits an opponent within
30 feet of you, you can spend an immediate action to make a single ranged attack against that
opponent. Your ranged weapon must be in hand, loaded, and ready to be fired or thrown for you to
make the ranged attack.
