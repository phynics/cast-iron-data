**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 47

Your drow blood is particularly strong.\
**Prerequisites:** Drow-blooded and drow magic racial traits, [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** You count as a drow for any effects that relate to race. Furthermore, the
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) granted to you by your drow magic racial trait
count as drow spell-like abilities for the purposes of any feat prerequisites.
