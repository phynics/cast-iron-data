Your mystic connection with the wilderness enhances your spellcasting.\
**Prerequisites:** [Attuned to the Wild](ref/feat/attuned-to-the-wild), [elf](ref/race/elf).\
**Benefit:** When you are in a terrain type you have selected the Attuned to the Wild feat for, you
gain a +2 bonus on caster level checks, [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks, and,
Knowledge (arcana) and Spellcraft checks. If you are in an area that qualifies as more than one kind
of terrain, these bonuses do not stack; you receive the bonus for only one of the terrain types.
