Your summoned minions are infused with the power of the moon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (conjuration), able to cast
[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally).\
**Benefit:** Creatures you summon shed light as a [light](ref/spell/light) spell. They are immune to
confusion and sleep effects, and their natural weapons are treated as silver for the purposes of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
