**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 158

Your quick reflexes and dexterous movements make up for your lack of stamina.\
**Prerequisites:** [Evasion](ref/ability/evasion).\
**Benefit:** While you are wearing light armor or no armor, if you are forced to attempt a Fortitude
saving throw, you can use an immediate action to instead attempt a Reflex saving throw (at the same
DC). If you succeed at this saving throw and the attack has a reduced effect on a successful save,
you avoid the effect entirely. Whether the saving throw is successful or not, you are
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) until the end of your next turn. If you are prevented from becoming
staggered, you can't use Twist Away, nor can you ignore the staggered condition from Twist Away or
remove it early.
