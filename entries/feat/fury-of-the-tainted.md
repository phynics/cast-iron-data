## Fury of the Tainted (Combat)

The unstoppable anger you feel toward do-gooders and self-proclaimed saints makes you a force to be
reckoned with.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, rage class feature, [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** While raging, you gain a +4 competence bonus on saving throws made against spells and
effects with the good descriptor.
