## Quick Study (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 89

You can learn quickly, although you do not always retain what you learn long.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, bravery +3 class feature, fighter level 10th.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can train with someone who has a combat feat you do not possess to
gain that feat. You must train with that person for 8 hours and must meet the feat's prerequisites
to gain it this way. You gain this feat until you learn another one using this feat.
