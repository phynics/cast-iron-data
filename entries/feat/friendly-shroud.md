You can help conceal your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Stealth 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are adjacent to an ally, and neither you nor that ally move for at least 1
round, and both you and that ally attempt Stealth checks, that ally can use your Stealth check
result if it is better than his own.
