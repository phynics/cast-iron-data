## Stupefying Strike (Combat)

Your batter your foes with such disorienting force that they can't exert their full abilities.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, Wis 13, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base
attack bonus +8.\
**Benefit:** When using this feat, in addition to dealing damage normally, you force a foe damaged
by your unarmed attack to attempt a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half your character level + your
Wisdom modifier). If it fails, it loses its skill ranks and activated feats, such as
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack) or [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), for 1d4 rounds.
You must declare you are using this feat before you make your attack roll; if you miss, the target
is unaffected and your attempt is wasted. You can try to use Stupefying Strike once per day for
every 4 character levels you have, but no more than once per round. Constructs, incorporeal
creatures, mindless creatures, plants, undead, and creatures immune to critical hits can't be
affected by this ability.
