## Diva Advance (Combat)

You stride forward with confidence, trusting your performance to protect you as much as your arms
and armor.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 17, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Diva Strike](ref/feat/diva-strike), [Diva Style](ref/feat/diva-style),
[Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint), base attack bonus +11 or bard level 11th.\
**Benefit:** While using Diva Style, you do not provoke attacks of opportunity from creatures you
have successfully feinted against. Additionally, you can [feint](ref/rule/feint) against a creature
using Diva Style from up to 30 feet away.
