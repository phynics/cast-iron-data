## Fire Hand (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 118

Born with a torch in your hand, you have a gift with anything that burns.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin).\
**Benefit:** You can wield a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) as a weapon without taking the nonproficient
penalty and gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls with melee weapons that deal fire damage.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 119\
When you hit with a torch attack, you can spend 1 stamina point. If you do, the fire damage dealt
with that attack increases to 1d6 points of fire damage.
