## Ace Disarm (Targeting, Weapon Mastery)

You catch objects with your ranged attacks and drag them along with you.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Deadly Aim](ref/feat/deadly-aim),
[Ranged Disarm](ref/feat/ranged-disarm) base attack bonus +6, weapon training class feature with a
ranged weapon.\
**Benefit:** You don't take the -2 penalty for attempting [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) combat maneuver
checks with a ranged weapon using Ranged Disarm, and you can attempt a [steal](ref/rule/steal)
combat maneuver check instead of a disarm combat maneuver check when using Ranged Disarm. In
addition, when you successfully disarm or steal an item from an opponent, that item is deposited in
that opponent's square. For every 5 by which your attack exceeds the target's CMD, you can deposit
the item 5 feet away from the target (to a maximum of the attack's maximum range or until your
projectile strikes an object that it cannot penetrate). If you use this ability with a thrown weapon
and have the [Ricochet Toss](ref/feat/ricochet-toss) feat, both the weapon and the item return to
you.
