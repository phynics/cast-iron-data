## Dragon Roar (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 98

The spirit of the dragon wells up inside you and bursts forth in a mighty roar.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Dragon Style](ref/feat/dragon-style), [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), Acrobatics 8 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain one additional Stunning Fist attempt per day. While using Dragon Style, as a
standard action you can expend two Stunning Fist attempts to unleash a concussive roar in a 15-foot
cone. Creatures caught in the cone take your unarmed strike damage and become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d4 rounds. A successful Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level +
your Wis modifier) reduces the damage by half and prevents a target from being shaken.\
**Special:** If you have the [Elemental Fist](ref/feat/elemental-fist) feat, you can expend a daily
use of that feat to deal your Elemental Fist damage to those caught in the cone. This damage is not
halved even on a save.
