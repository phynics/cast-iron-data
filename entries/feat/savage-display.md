## Savage Display (Combat, Performance)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 116

With your victory and a roar, you push yourself on with increased savagery.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display).\
**Benefit:** When you spend a swift action to make a
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) check, you gain a +2 bonus on your performance
combat check and gain a +1d6 bonus on damage rolls until the end of your next turn. This extra
damage is not precision damage.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 130\
When using this feat, you can spend 2 stamina points to gain a +2d6 bonus on damage rolls (instead
of +1d6).
