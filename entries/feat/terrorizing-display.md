## Terrorizing Display (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 124

Your battle prowess frightens friend and foe alike.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display),
[Demoralizing Lash](ref/feat/demoralizing-lash), [Taskmaster](ref/feat/taskmaster),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus), Intimidate 10 ranks, [hobgoblin](ref/race/hobgoblin),
proficiency with selected weapon.\
**Benefit:** When you use Dazzling Display, in addition to its normal effects, you can use it to
spur allies within 30 feet as if you had used the Taskmaster feat. Use the same Intimidate check to
determine the effects of Dazzling Display and this feat.
