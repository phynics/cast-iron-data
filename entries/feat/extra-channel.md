**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 146, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 123

You can channel divine energy more often.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You can channel energy two additional times per day.\
**Special:** If a [paladin](ref/class/paladin) with the ability to channel positive energy takes
this feat, she can use [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) four additional times per day, but
only to channel positive energy. If a [warpriest](ref/class/warpriest) with the ability to channel
energy takes this feat, he gains four additional uses of fervor per day, but can use them only to
channel energy.
