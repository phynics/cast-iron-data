## Rushing Winds (Combat)

You can fly through the air at high speed, creating a column of wind in your path that destabilizes
other fliers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Turbulent Takeoff](ref/feat/turbulent-takeoff), Fly 10 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, you can fly up to twice your Fly speed in a straight line,
creating a shockwave of rushing winds around you as you move. Each flying creature you pass adjacent
to as part of this movement must succeed at a DC 25 Fly check or be blown back 1d6×10 feet and take
2d6 points of nonlethal damage due to battering and buffeting. Creatures that fail this check by 5
or more also lose 1d4×10 feet of altitude, and creatures that fail this check by 10 or more plummet
to the ground.
