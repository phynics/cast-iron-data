## Crane Style (Combat, Style)

Your unarmed fighting techniques blend poise with graceful defense.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +2 or monk level 1st.\
**Benefit:** You take only a -2 penalty on attack rolls for
[fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively). While using this style and fighting
defensively or using the [total defense](ref/misc/total-defense) action, you gain an additional +1
dodge bonus to your Armor Class.
