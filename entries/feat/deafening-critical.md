## Deafening Critical (Combat, Critical)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 121

Your critical hits cause enemies to lose their hearing.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), base attack bonus +13.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you score a critical hit against an opponent, the victim is permanently
[deafened](ref/misc/deafened). A successful Fortitude save reduces the deafness to 1 round. The DC
of this Fortitude save is equal to 10 + your base attack bonus. This feat has no effect on deaf
creatures. This deafness can be cured by [heal](ref/spell/heal),
[regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration), [remove deafness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness), or a
similar ability.\
**Special:** You can only apply the effects of one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
possess [Critical Mastery](ref/feat/critical-mastery).

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 116\
When you confirm a critical hit and attempt to deafen an opponent with this feat, you can spend up
to 5 stamina points to increase the DC of this feat's saving throw by an amount equal to the number
of stamina points you spent.
