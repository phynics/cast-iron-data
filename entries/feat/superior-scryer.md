You are adept at seeing more when scrying.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (divination), the ability to cast at least
one spell of the scrying subschool.\
**Benefit:** When casting a scrying spell, you see through the spell's sensor with
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet, whether or not you possess darkvision normally and
whether or not the spell normally allows you to see with darkvision. If the spell sends back sensory
information only within 10 feet of the sensor (such as with [scrying](ref/spell/scrying) and
[greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater)), this feat doesn't increase that range. You receive a
+2 bonus on Perception checks to notice things through a scrying sensor, and your
[prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes) and [insect spies](ref/spell/insect-spies) also receive a +2
bonus on Perception checks.

When you use the _[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ or _[greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater)_
spell, you have a 10% increase to the chance of using spells through the sensor.
