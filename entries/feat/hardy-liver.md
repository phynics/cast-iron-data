You can hold your liquor and poisons better than most.\
**Prerequisites:** [Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Fortitude saving throws against ingested poisons. You take no
penalties when you are tipsy, and the penalties for being [drunk or soused](ref/rule/drunkenness)
are halved for you.
