## Craft Wondrous Item (Item Creation)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 120

You can create wondrous items, a type of magic item.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** You can create a wide variety of magic wondrous items. Crafting a wondrous item takes 1
day for each 1,000 gp in its price. To create a wondrous item, you must use up raw materials costing
half of its base price. See the [magic item creation rules](ref/rule/magic-item-creation) for more
information.

You can also mend a broken wondrous item if it is one that you could make. Doing so costs half the
raw materials and half the time it would take to craft that item.
