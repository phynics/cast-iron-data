## Shapeshifter Twist (Combat)

Your form flexes and contorts in unexpected ways, making it difficult for enemies to move you
against your will.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shapeshifter Style](ref/feat/shapeshifter-style), base attack bonus +5, change
shape ability, shapechanger subtype.\
**Benefit:** While you are using Shapeshifter Style, you gain a +2 bonus to your CMD versus
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [drag](ref/rule/drag), [grapple](ref/rule/grapple),
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun), [reposition](ref/rule/reposition), and [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat
maneuvers. If you begin your turn with the grappled condition, you can attempt a combat maneuver
check or an Escape Artist check as a move action to attempt to break the grapple.\
**Special:** A character with the wild shape class feature counts as having the change shape ability
and shapechanger subtype for the purpose of meeting this feat's prerequisites.
