**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 117\
Whenever you use Vital Strike, Improved Vital Strike, or Greater Vital Strike, you can spend 5
stamina points to increase the bonus damage to +4 on each extra weapon damage die. For weapons with
more than one damage die, count all of the weapon's base damage dice together as one set.
