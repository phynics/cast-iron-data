## Blood Feast (Combat)

**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 82

You become more aggressive and vicious when you taste living flesh and blood.\
**Prerequisites:** Bite attack.\
**Benefit:** When you damage a living creature with your bite attack, you gain a +1 morale bonus on
attack and damage rolls with your bite attack until the end of your next turn.
