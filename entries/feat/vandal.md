Even other goblins admire your talent for mindless destruction.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin), [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) class
feature.\
**Benefit:** You treat unattended objects as if they were vulnerable to your sneak attack, but you
only deal half as much damage as you would against an actual creature. For instance, if you rolled
12 extra damage from the sneak attack you would deal an extra 6 damage to the object. This only
applies when attacking unattended objects.
