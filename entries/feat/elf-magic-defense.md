**Sources:** Pathfinder \#115: Trail of the Hunted pg 74

You can better resist arcane trickery from your hated foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) class feature,
[hobgoblin](ref/race/hobgoblin).\
**Benefit:** In addition to the normal benefits against your favored enemies, you add half your
favored enemy bonus on saving throws made against arcane spells,
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural abilities of your favored
enemies.
