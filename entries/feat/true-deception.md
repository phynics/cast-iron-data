Time spent learning the mystic arts of disguise has given you insight into the anonymity of the
everyday.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 17, Disguise 17 ranks, [master disguise](ref/ninjatrick/master-disguise)
master ninja trick or [master of disguise](ref/talent/master-of-disguise) advanced rogue talent,
ninja level 10th or rogue level 10th.\
**Benefit:** When using the master disguise master ninja trick or master of disguise advanced rogue
talent to take on the appearance of a specific individual, you can fool divination magic and effects
designed to locate the subject of your disguise as per the vigilante's
[any guise](ref/soctalent/any-guise) social talent. In addition, you gain a +10 bonus on Bluff
checks to act like that individual.
