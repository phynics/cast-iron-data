## Dwarven Fury (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 12

You can channel your hatred against many opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarven Hatred Style](ref/feat/dwarven-hatred-style),
[Dwarven Seething](ref/feat/dwarven-seething), base attack bonus +7, defensive training and hatred
racial traits, Medium size.\
**Benefit:** When using Dwarven Seething, you can maintain bonuses from both your defensive training
racial trait and hatred racial trait's against any number of opponents.
