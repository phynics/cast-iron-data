## Technology Adept (Combat)

You utilize high-tech firearms to maximum effect.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
(firearms), [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot)\
**Benefit:** When you attack with a [technological firearm](ref/rule/tech-weapons) that consumes
charges, the save DC of any effect caused by that firearm increases by 1. If your bonuses from
Point-Blank Shot apply, it increases by 2 instead.
