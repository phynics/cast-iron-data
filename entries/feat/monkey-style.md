## Monkey Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 110

Your unarmed fighting style is nimble and unpredictable, full of ground rolls and short leaps.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Acrobatics 5
ranks, Climb 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You add your Wisdom bonus on Acrobatics checks. While using this style, you take no
penalty on melee attack rolls or to AC while [prone](ref/rule/prone). Further, you can crawl and
stand up from lying prone without provoking attacks of opportunity, and you can stand up as a swift
action if you succeed at a DC 20 Acrobatics check.\
**Normal:** You take a -4 penalty on attack rolls and AC against melee attacks while prone. Standing
up is a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity.
