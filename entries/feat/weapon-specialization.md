## Weapon Specialization (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 137

You are skilled at dealing damage with one weapon. Choose one type of weapon (including unarmed
strike or grapple) for which you have already selected the Weapon Focus feat. You deal extra damage
when using this weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** Proficiency with selected weapon, [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with
selected weapon, fighter level 4th.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on all damage rolls you make using the selected weapon.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new type of weapon.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
You are skilled at dealing damage with one type of weapon. Choose one
[weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups) listed under the fighter's Weapon Training class feature
for which you have already selected the Weapon Focus feat. You deal extra damage when using these
weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with selected weapon group, fighter level
4th.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on all damage rolls you make using the selected weapon.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new weapon group.
