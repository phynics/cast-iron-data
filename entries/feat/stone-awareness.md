**Sources:** Giants Revisited pg 49

You can sense creatures touching earth or stone.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, Perception 10 ranks, [stone giant](ref/monster/giant-stone-giant).\
**Benefit:** You gain tremorsense 15 feet when in contact with earth or stone, including worked
stone, and gain all the benefits of the [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) universal monster
ability.
