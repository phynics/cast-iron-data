## Giant's Wallop (Combat)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#91: Battle of Bloodmarch Hills pg 69

When you strike, you knock the sense out of smaller foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), base attack bonus +6, giant subtype.\
**Benefit:** If you hit a creature at least one size category smaller than you with a melee attack
while using both Power Attack and Vital Strike, the creature you attacked is also
[dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round. A successful Fortitude save negates this effect (DC = 10 + your
base attack bonus). You must choose to use this feat before making the attack roll.\
**Special:** This effect stacks with the effect of [Giant's Crush](ref/feat/giants-crush).
