When you channel energy, Iomedae grants protection against dark forces.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 7d6, worshiper of
[Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae).\
**Benefit:** When you channel positive energy to heal living creatures, you can choose to affect
healed creatures with a [protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) spell (caster level
equal to your effective cleric level).
