## Steadfast Slayer (Combat)

**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 7

Like the dragonslayers and giantslayers of legend, you stand alone against creatures far larger than
you.\
**Benefit:** If you are the only character threatening an opponent and you successfully attack the
opponent with a two-handed melee weapon, for each size category larger than you that opponent is,
you gain a +2 bonus on your melee damage roll.
