**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 71

One of the Boneyard's psychopomp bounty hunters has taught you the trick of using spider webs to
defend against the undead.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 9th, [duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker), ward against corruption
racial trait.\
**Benefit:** So long as you have a spider web wrapped around some part of your body, the bonus
provided by your ward against corruption racial trait increases to +4.
