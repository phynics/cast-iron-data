Your physical mastery grants you an impossible stride.\
**Prerequisites:** Acrobatics 6 ranks, Climb 6 ranks, monk level 6th.\
**Benefit:** As a move action, you can move up to half your slow fall distance across a wall or
ceiling or across ropes, branches, or even water or other surfaces that cannot support your weight.
You must reach a solid, level surface by the end of your turn or you will fall.
