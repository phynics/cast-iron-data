## Stick-Fighting Counter (Combat)

Using stick-fighting techniques both defensively and offensively, you can turn your parries into
counterattacks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Stick-Fighting Style](ref/feat/stick-fighting-style), base attack bonus +4.\
**Benefit:** While you are [fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively), as a free action
you can select an opponent you have attacked on that turn and prepare yourself to counter one of its
attacks. Once per round, when that opponent attempts a melee attack against you and the attack
misses, the attacker provokes an attack of opportunity from you, provided that it is within your
threatened area. You must use a [club](ref/eq-weapon/club),
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), [dan bong](ref/eq-weapon/dan-bong),
[sap](ref/eq-weapon/sap), or [tonfa](ref/eq-weapon/tonfa) to perform the attack of opportunity.
