## Improved Feint Partner (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 105

Knowledge of your companions' tricks and techniques allow you to take even greater advantage of your
allies' feints.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 1 rank, [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Feint Partner](ref/feat/feint-partner), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** Whenever an ally who also has this feat successfully [feints](ref/rule/feint) against
an opponent, that opponent provokes an attack of opportunity from you.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 123\
When you feint, you can spend 5 stamina points for each additional ally to grant an attack of
opportunity to any number of additional allies - not just those with this feat.
