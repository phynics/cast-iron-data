Effects that grant natural armor work even better for you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness), [duergar](ref/race/duergar).\
**Benefit:** When you gain an enhancement bonus to your natural armor, that bonus increases by 2.
This has no effect if your natural armor bonus is +0 or you do not have an enhancement bonus
increasing your natural armor bonus.
