**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 157

Your summoned minions shine with the power of the sun.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (conjuration), able to cast summon nature's
ally.\
**Benefit:** Creatures that you summon shed light as a [light](ref/spell/light) spell. They are
immune to blinding or dazzling effects, and their natural weapons are treated as magical for
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
