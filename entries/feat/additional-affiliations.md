You have an expansive personality and can forge more [affiliations](ref/rule/affiliations) than
normal.\
**Benefit:** Increase your affiliation slot total by 2.\
**Normal:** You have a number of affiliation slots equal to your Charisma modifier + 1 (minimum 1).\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times; each time you do so, your number of affiliation
slots increases by 2.
