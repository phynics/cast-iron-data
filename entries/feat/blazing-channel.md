Your holy power burns the undead like fire.\
**Prerequisites:** [Turn Undead](ref/feat/turn-undead),
[channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you use your Turn Undead feat, undead failing their saving throw are engulfed in
holy fire. This functions identically to [catching on fire](ref/rule/catching-on-fire), but the
damage dealt is divine (like the divine damage from [flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike)) rather
than fire. Water cannot extinguish this holy fire, but any negative energy effect that heals the
creature of at least 5 points of damage extinguishes the flames instantly.
