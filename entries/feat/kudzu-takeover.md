## Kudzu Takeover (Combat)

**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 14

Your magical nature infuses your vines, further obscuring grappled foes' vision - even for opponents
with enhanced senses.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, base attack bonus +6 or monk level 6th,
[Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple), [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Kudzu Grappler](ref/feat/kudzu-grappler), [Kudzu Invasion](ref/feat/kudzu-invasion).\
**Benefit:** Opponents [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) by your Kudzu Grappler feat increase their miss
chance against creatures they attack by 20%. If the opponent has means of ignoring blindness, such
as [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight), they instead treat other creatures as if these creatures had
normal [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance).\
**Normal:** All opponents are considered to have total concealment (50% miss chance) against blinded
creatures.
