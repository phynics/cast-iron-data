## Black Powder Spectacle (Combat, Performance)

You can sacrifice ammunition to make a grand spectacle.\
**Prerequisites:** [Amateur Gunslinger](ref/feat/amateur-gunslinger) or the grit class feature, base
attack bonus +4.\
**Benefit:** When you spend a swift action to attempt a
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) check, you can sacrifice one cartridge or an
amount of black powder required for a single shot to make either a loud sound or an impressive
flash. If you choose to make your weapon's report louder, you gain a +4 bonus on all Intimidate
checks you attempt for 1 round. If you choose to create an impressive flash, you gain a +4 bonus on
any Perform checks you attempt for 1 round.
