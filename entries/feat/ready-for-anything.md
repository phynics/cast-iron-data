## Ready for Anything (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 90

You never let down your guard.\
**Prerequisites:** [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Improved Initiative](ref/feat/improved-initiative),
[Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/lightning-reflexes), [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw), base attack
bonus +6 or [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You can always act in the surprise round even if you fail a Perception check to notice
foes, but you are still considered [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) until you take an action.
