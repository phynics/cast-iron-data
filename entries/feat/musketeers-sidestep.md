## Musketeer's Sidestep (Combat, Panache)

You're able to dodge attacks with increased panache.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dodging panache](ref/swashdeed/dodging-panache) deed,
[swashbuckler](ref/class/swashbuckler) level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** When you use the dodging panache deed, the movement granted by that deed does not
provoke attacks of opportunity from any creature, and you gain a +2 dodge bonus to your Armor Class.
