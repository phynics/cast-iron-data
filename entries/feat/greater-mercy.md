Your mercy has incredible recuperative properties.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) class feature, mercy class
feature.\
**Benefit:** When you use your lay on hands ability and the target of that ability does not have any
conditions your mercies can remove, it instead heals an additional +1d6 points of damage.
