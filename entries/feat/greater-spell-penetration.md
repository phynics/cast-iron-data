Your spells break through [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) much more easily than most.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Penetration](ref/feat/spell-penetration).\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on caster level checks (1d20 + caster level) made to overcome a
creature's spell resistance. This bonus stacks with the one from Spell Penetration.
