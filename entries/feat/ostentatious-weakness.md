## Ostentatious Weakness (Combat, Performance)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 12

You use knowledge of your hated foes to please onlookers.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +5, [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you spend a swift action to attempt a
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) check, you may add your favored enemy bonus to the
check if you're fighting a favored enemy.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 25\
You can spend 2 stamina points to select another type of favored enemy (and subtype if humanoid or
outsider is chosen). For the purposes of Ostentatious Weakness, that type counts as having been
chosen with the favored enemy class feature, with a bonus equal to your highest favored enemy bonus.
