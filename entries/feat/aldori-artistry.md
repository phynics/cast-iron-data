## Aldori Artistry (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 24

You can do more with your blade than slash and stab.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
([Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword)),
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse), base attack bonus +2.\
**Benefit:** Choose one of the following [combat maneuvers](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers): disarm,
reposition, steal, sunder, or trip. You gain a +2 bonus when performing the selected maneuver while
wielding an Aldori dueling sword. This bonus does not stack with the bonus provided by a combat
maneuver feat with "improved" in its name (such as [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip)).\
**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times, choosing a different combat maneuver each
time.
