## Wolf Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 121, Blood of the Moon pg 25

While in this style, you hamper foes that turn their backs on you.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Knowledge
(nature) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While using this style, whenever you deal at least 10 points of damage to a foe with an
attack of opportunity, that foe's base speed decreases by 5 feet until the end of its next turn. For
every 10 points of damage your attack deals beyond 10, the foe's base speed decreases by an
additional 5 feet. If the penalty meets or exceeds the total base speed of the foe, you can attempt
to [trip](ref/rule/trip) the foe as a free action after the attack of opportunity is resolved.
