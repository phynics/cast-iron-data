Your ascension is complete; you have the [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) approaching
that of a demon.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, Wis 13, [Greater Drow Nobility](ref/feat/greater-drow-nobility),
character level 13th, [drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** Your spell resistance is equal to 11 + your character level.\
**Special:** You receive a +1 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks made against any
drow.
