## Net Maneuvering (Combat)

With sweeping movements and brute force, you can use your net to put foes at a disadvantage.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (net),
[Net Adept](ref/feat/net-adept), base attack bonus +3.\
**Benefit:** In melee, you can use a [net](ref/eq-weapon/net) to [trip](ref/rule/trip) or
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) opponents instead of entangling them. You gain a +2 bonus on disarm checks
made to use a net in this way. Further, if you have an opponent entangled in your net, you can
attempt to [drag](ref/rule/drag) or [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) that opponent as long as he is
within your net's reach or you control the trailing rope on your net.
