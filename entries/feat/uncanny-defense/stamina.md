When fighting defensively or taking the total defense action, if you attempt a Reflex saving throw
or are the target of a combat maneuver, you can spend a number of stamina points up to your
Dexterity bonus to gain a bonus on the Reflex save or to your CMD equal to the number of stamina
points you spent.
