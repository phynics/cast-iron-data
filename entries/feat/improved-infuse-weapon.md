When you infuse weapons, you may add an additional magical quality.\
**Prerequisites:** Infuse weapon special ability, Cha 27\
**Benefit:** When you infuse a weapon, you can add an additional +1 worth of weapon qualities to
that weapon; this could either be used to increase a weapon infused to be a +1 weapon to be a +2
weapon, or to grant a weapon quality like [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming),
[defending](ref/magic-enh/defending), [keen](ref/magic-enh/keen), or any other +1 quality. This
effect occurs automatically as you infuse a weapon, but you may only do so to one weapon you wield
at a time.\
**Special:** You may take this feat more than once; each time you do, the limit to the number of
weapons on which you can simultaneously use Improved Infuse Weapon increases by 2.
