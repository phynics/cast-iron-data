## Exceptional Aid (Teamwork)

You're always ready to lend your partner a hand.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bonded Mind](ref/feat/bonded-mind).\
**Benefit:** When you successfully use the [Aid Another](ref/rule/aid-another) action to assist an
ally's skill check, and the ally also has this feat, the bonus is +4 instead of +2.
