## Death's Suitor (Faction)

You have died and returned so often that even Pharasma now deems it part of your fate.\
**Prerequisites:** [Risen Guard](ref/rule/f-second-life) 20 TPA.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you may reroll a save or skill check if failure has a chance of killing
you (whether from hit point damage, ability damage, or some instant effect). You must decide to use
this ability before the results are revealed. You must take the second roll, even if it is worse.
