Your judgment against a particular type of creature is particularly harsh.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, judgment class feature.\
**Benefit:** Select a favored race from the ranger's [favored enemies](ref/ability/favored-enemy)
chart. Any sacred or profane bonus you gain from a judgment is 1 higher for attacks you make against
or take from creatures that match the selected favored enemy.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a different favored enemy.
