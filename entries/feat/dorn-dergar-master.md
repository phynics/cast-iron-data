## Dorn-Dergar Master (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 11, Dwarves of Golarion pg 21

You can use a dorn-dergar with only one hand.\
**Prerequisites:** [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting), base attack bonus +4,
proficiency with the [dwarven dorn-dergar](ref/eq-weapon/dorn-dergar-dwarven).\
**Benefit:** You can use a dorn-dergar as a one-handed weapon. When using it one-handed, changing
whether it's a normal or reach weapon is a full-round action. If you have the
[Darting Viper](ref/feat/darting-viper) feat, changing its reach is a move action.\
**Normal:** A dorn-dergar requires two hands to wield.
