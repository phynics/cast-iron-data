You can manipulate the elemental nature of your spells.\
**Benefit:** Choose one energy type: acid, cold, electricity, or fire. You may replace a spell's
normal damage with that energy type or split the spell's damage, so that half is of that energy type
and half is of its normal type. An elemental spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the
spell's actual level.
