You are as comfortable dangling from a ship's rigging as you are on stable land.\
**Prerequisites:** Affiliated with the Gray Corsairs.\
**Benefit:** Acrobatics and Climb are class skills for you. You gain a +2 bonus on Acrobatics and
Climb checks to climb or swing on a rope or ship's rigging. You do not provoke attacks of
opportunity from climbing or swinging on a rope or ship's rigging.
