## Cudgeler Takedown (Combat)

Opponents you batter to the ground stay down.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bludgeoner](ref/feat/bludgeoner), [Cudgeler Style](ref/feat/cudgeler-style),
[Cudgeler Sweep](ref/feat/cudgeler-sweep), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.\
**Benefit:** When you hit with a charge attack using the Cudgeler Style feat, your weapon deals
damage as though it were [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) for 1 round or
until it leaves your hand. When you successfully [trip](ref/rule/trip) an opponent with the Cudgeler
Sweep feat, your opponent must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + your character level + your
Strength modifier) or be [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round.
