**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 118

You possess inherent physical prowess.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on Climb and Swim skill checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in one
of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

### Mythic Athletic

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 60\
You excel far beyond others at acts of physical prowess.\
**Prerequisites:** Athletic.\
**Benefit:** The bonus on Climb and Swim skill checks from Athletic increases by 2. In addition, you
can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Climb or Swim check as if you had rolled a natural 20.
You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
