## Inspired Sneak Attack (Combat)

Your brilliant insight makes you more dangerous in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Inspiration pool; [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) or studied strike
class feature.\
**Benefit:** When dealing sneak attack or studied strike damage, you can expend a use of inspiration
from your pool to reroll all of your sneak attack dice that resulted in 1s. If you have the
[powerful sneak](ref/talent/powerful-sneak) rogue talent, rather than treat all 1s on your sneak
attack damage dice as 2s, you can reroll all your sneak attack dice that resulted in 1s. If you have
the [deadly sneak](ref/talent/deadly-sneak) advanced rogue talent, rather than treat all 1s and 2s
on your sneak attack damage dice as 3s, you can reroll all your sneak attack damage dice that
resulted in 1s or 2s. In either case, you must take the result of the rerolls, even if it's worse
than the original result.
