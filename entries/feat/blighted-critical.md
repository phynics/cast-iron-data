## Blighted Critical (Critical)

With a critical hit from a spell or [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), you give the
target a minor spellblight.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 5th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit with a touch spell, ranged touch spell, or
spell-like ability against an opponent, the victim gains a random
[minor spellblight](ref/rule/minor-spellblights).\
**Special:** You can apply the effects of only one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you
possess Critical Mastery.
