## Canny Tumble (Combat)

Your acrobatic prowess distracts your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility), Acrobatics 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use Acrobatics to move through an opponent's threatened area or space without
provoking an attack of opportunity from that opponent, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your next
melee attack roll against that opponent and that opponent is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC, as
long as you make that attack before the start of your next turn.
