## Guided Star (Combat)

**Sources:** Paths of the Righteous pg 29

The grace of Desna guides your starknife to its target and back to your hand.\
**Prerequisites:** [Starry Grace](ref/feat/starry-grace), worshiper of [Desna](ref/faith/desna).\
**Benefit:** You can empower a [starknife](ref/eq-weapon/starknife) with magical power once per day
per character level as a swift action. Until the end of your turn, add your Wisdom modifier to your
damage rolls with the starknife in addition to any other modifiers you normally add. If you throw
the starknife during that time, it returns to you at the beginning of your next turn (as per the
[returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) weapon special ability).
