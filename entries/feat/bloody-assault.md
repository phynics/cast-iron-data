## Bloody Assault (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 151

Sacrificing accuracy, you can inflict bloody wounds that are slow to heal.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You can choose to take a -5 penalty on all melee attack rolls and combat maneuver
checks to inflict 1d4 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage with your weapon melee attacks, in
addition to the normal damage dealt by the weapon. A creature continues to take bleed damage every
round at the start of its turn. Bleed damage can be stopped by a DC 15 Heal check or through any
magical healing. Bleed damage from this feat does not stack with itself. You must choose to use this
feat before making the attack roll, and its effects last until your next turn (although the bleeding
lasts until healed, as normal).
