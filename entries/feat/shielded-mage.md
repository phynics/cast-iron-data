## Shielded Mage (Combat, Shield Mastery)

You have learned how to cast spells from behind the safety of a shield.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus), base attack bonus +3 or fighter level 1st.\
**Benefit:** You reduce the arcane spell failure of any shield you use by 15% (to a minimum of 0%).
Using a shield does not prevent you from completing somatic spell components with the hand wielding
the shield.
