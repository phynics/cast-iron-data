## Tail Weapon (Combat)

**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 200

You have strengthened your tail enough that you can use it to make slap attacks and augment your
balance.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, [serpentfolk](ref/monster/serpentfolk).\
**Benefit:** You can make a tail slap attack with your tail. This is a secondary
[natural attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage. You also gain
a +2 racial bonus on Acrobatics checks to balance and to your CMD against maneuvers that attempt to
move you from your square.
