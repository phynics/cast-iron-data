You have developed a resistance to the life-sapping environment of the Shadow Plane.\
**Prerequisites:** [Fetchling](ref/race/fetchling), shadowy resistance racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You gain resistance 5 against negative energy damage, do not lose hit points when you
gain a negative level, and gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against death effects, energy
drain, negative energy, and spells or [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of the
necromancy school.
