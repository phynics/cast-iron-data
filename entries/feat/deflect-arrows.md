## Deflect Arrows (Combat)

You can knock arrows and other projectiles off course, preventing them from hitting you.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** You must have at least one hand free (holding nothing) to use this feat. Once per round
when you would normally be hit with an attack from a ranged weapon, you may deflect it so that you
take no damage from it. You must be aware of the attack and not [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed).
Attempting to deflect a ranged attack doesn't count as an action. Unusually massive ranged weapons
(such as boulders or ballista bolts) and ranged attacks generated by natural attacks or spell
effects can't be deflected.
