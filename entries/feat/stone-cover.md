**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 9

You can use stone around you to help you avoid attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Stone Shroud](ref/feat/stone-shroud), Stealth 6 ranks,
[dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), stonecunning racial trait.\
**Benefit:** While in areas of rocky terrain or while underground, you can gain 10% miss chance as a
swift action. Alternatively, you can take a moment to better position yourself and gain
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) as a move action. The miss chance or concealment you gain from
this feat does not stack with similar effects and remains until the start of your next turn.
