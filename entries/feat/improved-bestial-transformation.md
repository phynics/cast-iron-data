**Sources:** Blood of the Night pg 16

You have mastered the ability to transform into a variety of beasts.\
**Prerequisites:** [Vampire](ref/template/vampire).\
**Benefit:** You can [change shape](ref/umr/change-shape) to assume the form of one of the following
beasts, as [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2): [boar](ref/monster/boar),
[dire bat](ref/monster/bat-dire-bat), [giant frog](ref/monster/frog-giant-frog),
[grizzly bear](ref/monster/bear-grizzly-bear), [hyena](ref/monster/hyena),
[leopard](ref/monster/cat-leopard), [lion](ref/monster/lion), [shark](ref/monster/shark),
[squid](ref/monster/squid), or [wolf](ref/monster/wolf). This is a supernatural ability.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take this feat, choose another
animal transformation.
