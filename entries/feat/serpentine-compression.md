**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 200

You can squeeze through small areas thanks to your slippery scales and lithe musculature.\
**Prerequisites:** [Serpentfolk](ref/monster/serpentfolk).\
**Benefit:** You gain the [compression](ref/umr/compression) universal monster ability, allowing you
to move through an area as small as one-quarter your space without [squeezing](ref/misc/squeezing),
or one-eighth your space when squeezing.
