## Craft Rod (Item Creation)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 120

You can create magic rods.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 9th.\
**Benefit:** You can create magic rods. Crafting a rod takes 1 day for each 1,000 gp in its base
price. To craft a rod, you must use up raw materials costing half of its base price. See the
[magic item creation rules](ref/rule/magic-item-creation) for more information.
