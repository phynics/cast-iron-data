You are comfortable in the driving snow and glaring ice of frigid climes, and you can survive much
longer in such harsh environments than those who are unaccustomed to the cold.\
**Prerequisites:** [Favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) (cold) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You treat [cold environments](ref/rule/cold-dangers) as though they were one step less
severe than they normally are. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus on Perception checks against
creatures that gain a racial bonus on Stealth checks in snowy conditions, and you gain a +4 bonus on
saving throws and checks to avoid becoming blinded or dazzled by ice or snow glare.
