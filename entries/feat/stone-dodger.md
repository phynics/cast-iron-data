## Stone Dodger (Teamwork)

By working with an ally to gauge the trajectory of incoming attacks, you can more easily avoid
taking damage.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are able to see and hear at least one ally with this feat, you receive a
+4 dodge bonus to AC against attacks from hurled rocks (or projectiles of similar shape, including
bludgeoning ranged attacks from siege weapons). If you are hit by such an attack while you have this
bonus, you can attempt a Reflex save to take only half damage from the attack. The DC for this save
is equal to the bonus of the ranged attack used to hurl the rock.
