You can rifle through the thoughts and memories of your host.\
**Prerequisites:** Int or Cha 21, [Hidden Presence](ref/feat/hidden-presence),
[Manipulative Presence](ref/feat/manipulative-presence), ability to cast
[possession](ref/spell/possession) or [greater possession](ref/spell/possession-greater).\
**Benefit:** You can use [detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts) at will as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) against a host creature you possess. The host
receives no saving throw, and the effect functions as if you had concentrated for 3 rounds. You can
also use [seek thoughts](ref/spell/seek-thoughts) on your host as a spell-like ability once during
the possession. The host can attempt a Will save with a DC equal to the DC of the original
possession effect. If the host succeeds, the effect fails and the host become aware of the
possession if it wasn't already.
