## Rapid Grappler (Combat)

You are a quick hand at grappling.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use Greater Grapple to successfully maintain a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple)
as a move action, you can then spend a swift action to make a grapple combat maneuver check at a -5
penalty.
