## Elven Battle Torrent (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 12

You rapidly counter enemy attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Elven Battle Focus](ref/feat/elven-battle-focus),
[Elven Battle Style](ref/feat/elven-battle-style),
[Elven Battle Training](ref/feat/elven-battle-training), [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse),
base attack bonus +10, weapon familiarity racial trait.\
**Benefit:** While using Elven Battle Style, any opponent that makes an attack against you and
misses while you are [fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or using
[total defense](ref/misc/total-defense) or the [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) feat
provokes an attack of opportunity from you. Even if you have Combat Reflexes, you can't benefit from
this feat more than once per round.
