## Brisk Spell (Metamagic)

Your movement spells make allies swifter than normal.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13+.\
**Benefit:** You can alter spells that grant a creature a movement type it doesn't normally have to
increase the speed for that movement type by 10 feet. A brisk spell uses a spell slot equal to the
spell's normal spell level.
