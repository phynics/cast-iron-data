## Wolf Trip (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 121, Blood of the Moon pg 25

You have studied the manner in which wolves bring down their prey.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Wolf Style](ref/feat/wolf-style), Knowledge (nature) 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While using Wolf Style, you gain a +2 bonus when you attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip)
combat maneuver as part of an attack of opportunity. Whenever you successfully trip a creature, as a
free action you can choose an available space that is both adjacent to you and the creature's
original space for the tripped creature to land [prone](ref/rule/prone) in.
