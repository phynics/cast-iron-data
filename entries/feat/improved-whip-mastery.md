## Improved Whip Mastery (Combat)

You are able to entangle opponents with the coils of your whip.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (whip),
[Whip Mastery](ref/feat/whip-mastery), base attack bonus +5.\
**Benefit:** While wielding a [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip), you threaten the area of your natural
reach plus 5 feet. You can also use a whip to grasp an unattended Small or Tiny object within your
whip's reach and pull that object into your square. To do so, you must hit AC 10 with a melee touch
attack. Further, you can use the whip to grasp onto an object within your whip's reach, using 5 feet
of your whip as if it were a [grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook), allowing you to use the
rest of your whip to swing on like a rope. As a free action, you can release the object your whip is
grasping, but you cannot use the whip to attack while the whip is grasping an object.
