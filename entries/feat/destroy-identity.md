## Destroy Identity (Combat, Critical)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 210, Faiths of Corruption pg 24

You can use your weapons to mar your opponents' most distinctive characteristics.\
**Prerequisites:** [Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), base attack bonus +11, worshiper of
[Lamashtu](ref/faith/lamashtu).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit against an opponent, in addition to dealing
critical damage as normal, you maim your target so much that it takes 2 points of Charisma damage
and becomes [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round.
