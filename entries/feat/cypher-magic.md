**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 60, Second Darkness Player's Guide pg 27, Inner Sea World Guide
pg 284

Your intimacy with runes and the mysteries of ancient arcana has taught you secret ways to draw
greater power from magical writings. The [Cyphermages](ref/rule/aff-i-tradition) of Riddleport
initially devised these unusual methods, but they have since spread far and wide through the Inner
Sea region.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 15, [Scribe Scroll](ref/feat/scribe-scroll).\
**Benefit:** You cast spells from scrolls at 1 caster level higher than the scroll's caster level.
In addition, you gain a +2 bonus on caster level checks to activate a scroll with a higher caster
level than your own.
