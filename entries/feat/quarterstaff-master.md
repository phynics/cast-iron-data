## Quarterstaff Master (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 154

You can wield a quarterstaff as either a two-handed or one-handed weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (quarterstaff), base attack bonus +5.\
**Benefit:** By employing a number of different stances and techniques, you can wield a
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff) as a one-handed weapon. At the start of your turn, you
decide whether or not you are going to wield the quarterstaff as a one-handed or two-handed weapon.
When you wield it as a one-handed weapon, your other hand is free, and you cannot use the staff as a
double weapon. You can take the feat [Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/weapon-specialization) in the
quarterstaff even if you have no levels in fighter.
