Your dual heritage makes you something more than merely a mix of your parent races.\
**Prerequisites:** Two subtypes, elf blood or orc blood racial trait.\
**Benefit:** For the purposes of any effect related to subtype (such as a ranger's
[favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) and [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapons), you count as
neither of your subtypes, and you no longer count as either of your parent races for the purposes of
effects related to race.
