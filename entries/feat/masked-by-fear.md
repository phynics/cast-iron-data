You embody all that you fear while wearing your mask.\
**Prerequisites:** [Nameless One](ref/feat/nameless-one); base Will save bonus +6 or bravery class
feature.\
**Benefit:** By donning a mask etched with your greatest terrors, you are able to eschew fear
completely. While benefiting from the Nameless One feat, when you suffer the
[cowering](ref/misc/cowering), [frightened](ref/misc/frightened), or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked)
condition, that condition causes you to act as if [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) instead of imposing its
normal effects. In addition, whenever you attempt a skill check (normally
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)) to demoralize an opponent, you gain a +3 bonus on the check.
