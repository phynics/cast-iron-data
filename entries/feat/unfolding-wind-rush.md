## Unfolding Wind Rush (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 106, Inner Sea Combat pg 13

Thrown weapons fly from your hands as if blown by a gale, and you leave behind a wake of wind as you
move.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13; Wis 13; [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge); [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility);
[Perfect Style](ref/feat/perfect-style); [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw);
[Unfolding Wind Strike](ref/feat/unfolding-wind-strike); base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.\
**Benefit:** When using Perfect Style and wielding thrown weapons, you can combine a full-attack
action with a single move action. You must forgo the attack at your highest bonus but can take the
remaining attacks as normal at any point during your movement. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift
action to create a path of wind along your path of movement that functions as
[wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall) for 1 round; this effect does not affect your ranged attacks.
