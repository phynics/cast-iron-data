The terrifying force of your croak disorients and debilitates your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sonic Croak](ref/feat/sonic-croak), [Throat Pouch](ref/feat/throat-pouch),
[boggard](ref/monster/boggard), terrifying croak.\
**Benefit:** When you use your terrifying croak ability, instead of imparting its normal effect you
can choose to stun one creature within 30 feet for 1 round. If the target succeeds at a Fortitude
save, it is instead [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. The DC of this Fortitude save is
the same as that of your terrifying croak ability.
