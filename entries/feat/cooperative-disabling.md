## Cooperative Disabling (Teamwork)

You are skilled at working with an ally to disable traps.\
**Prerequisites:** Disable Device 1 rank, [trapfinding](ref/ability/trapfinding) class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you use the Disable Device skill and fail the check against a trap, a single
adjacent ally with this feat who is also within reach of the trap can attempt a Disable Device check
against the same device as an immediate action. The ally must have remained adjacent throughout the
process of disabling the device and must have either aided your Disable Device check or taken no
other action. If your ally succeeds at the check, your attempt is considered to be successful. If
your ally fails, your attempt is considered to have failed by 5 or more, even if the original check
failed by 4 or less.
