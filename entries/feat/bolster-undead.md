You can fortify undead against positive energy.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 6d6, worshiper of
[Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you [channel negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) to heal undead
creatures, you grant all undead you heal +1 [channel resistance](ref/umr/channel-resistance) (or a
+1 bonus to their existing [channel resistance](ref/rule/channel-resistance)) for a number of rounds
equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round).
