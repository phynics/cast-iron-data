## Malleable Form (Conduit)

You infuse your body with protoplasm from the Maelstrom.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (planes) 7 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, you can infuse yourself with protoplasm, converting your body
and equipment to a semi-gelatinous state. You gain the [compression](ref/umr/compression) universal
monster ability, which allows you to move through an area as small as one-quarter your space without
[squeezing](ref/misc/squeezing) or one-eighth your space when squeezing. You also gain a bonus to
your CMD against [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) and [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuvers equal to
half your ranks in Knowledge (planes). Due to your form's softness and instability, your attacks
with manufactured weapons, natural weapons, and unarmed strikes deal half damage while you are in
malleable form. This effect offers no protection against critical hits or precision damage.

You can use this feat's benefit for a number of minutes per day equal to your ranks in Knowledge
(planes). These minutes need not be consecutive, but they must be spent in 1-minute increments.
