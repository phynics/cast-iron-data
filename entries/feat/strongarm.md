You are experienced at long-term intimidation of those you wish to exploit, and strike fear into the
hearts of those you bully.\
**Prerequisites:** [Intimidating Prowess](ref/feat/intimidating-prowess), member of the Lumber
Consortium.\
**Benefit:** When you use the Intimidate skill to improve a target's attitude, if your check result
exceeds the DC by 5 or more, the target's attitude is improved for 5d6 hours. During this time,
should it attack you, the target immediately becomes [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d6+1 rounds, as
though it had been demoralized.
