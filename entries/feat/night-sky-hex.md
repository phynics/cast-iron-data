**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 116

You use the mystical network of power that blazes in the night sky to empower your hexes.\
**Prerequisites:** Hex class feature; [moon](ref/patron/moon), [stars](ref/patron/stars), or
[winter](ref/patron/winter) patron.\
**Benefit:** When you gain this feat, choose one hex that you can use to affect no more than one
opponent. When you are in view of the night sky and use that hex, increase the save DC of that hex
by 2.
