## Nightmare Weaver (Combat)

You can use your ability to create magical darkness to terrorize enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Nightmare Fist](ref/feat/nightmare-fist), Intimidate 2 ranks, ability to cast
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness).\
**Benefit:** By spending a full-round action to cast _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness),_ you can also
make [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks to demoralize all foes in the spell's initial area.\
**Special:** This feat counts as [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display) for purposes of
qualifying for [Deadly Stroke](ref/feat/deadly-stroke) and
[Shatter Defenses](ref/feat/shatter-defenses).
