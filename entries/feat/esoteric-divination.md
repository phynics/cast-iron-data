## Esoteric Divination (Esoteric)

Scrying and other difficult divinations become more likely to get to the truth when you use verdant
salts.\
**Benefit:** When you cast a divination spell using verdant salts as an
[esoteric material component](ref/rule/esoteric-material-components), you're more likely to get good
information. You gain one of the following benefits, as appropriate to the spell: you increase the
likelihood of a percentage-based divination to work correctly by 5% (giving a -5 penalty on the d%
roll for [contact other plane](ref/spell/contact-other-plane), for example), you gain a +2
competence bonus on Perception checks while perceiving through a divination (scrying) spell such as
[scrying](ref/spell/scrying), or you can ask one additional question (with
_[contact other plane](ref/spell/contact-other-plane)_ or
[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead)).

When you gain verdant salts' greater component effect with a divination spell, increase the DC by an
additional 1.
