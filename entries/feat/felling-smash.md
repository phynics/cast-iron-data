## Felling Smash (Combat)

You commit all your focus to a devastating blow, trying to crush your opponent to the ground.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, Str 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus
+6.\
**Benefit:** If you use the attack action to make a single melee attack at your highest base attack
bonus while using Power Attack and you hit an opponent, you can spend a swift action to attempt a
trip combat maneuver against that opponent.
