## Improved Armor Focus (Combat)

Your mastery makes the most of your armor.\
**Prerequisites:** [Armor Focus](ref/feat/armor-focus), base attack bonus +6, proficiency with
selected armor.\
**Benefit:** The armor check penalty of the selected armor decreases by 1 (to a minimum of 0). You
also use your character level in place of your base attack bonus for the purpose of
[armor mastery feat](ref/rule/feats-mastery) prerequisites.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new type of armor.
