**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 71

The collectors of the lost dead prefer to use complex theology or blunt warnings to gather wayward
spirits, but sometimes a more direct approach is necessary. One of them shared a trade secret with
you.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast [disrupt undead](ref/spell/disrupt-undead), character level 3rd,
[duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker).\
**Benefit:** The damage dealt by your _[disrupt undead](ref/spell/disrupt-undead)_ spell increases
by 1d6. It increases by an additional 1d6 at 6th level and every 3 levels thereafter (to a maximum
of 7d6 at 18th level).
