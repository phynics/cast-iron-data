## Hellcat Pounce (Combat)

**Sources:** Cheliax, Empire of Devils pg 27

You attack unsuspecting foes twice.\
**Prerequisites:** [Hellcat Stealth](ref/feat/hellcat-stealth),
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +2d6.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you attack and damage an opponent in the surprise round, you may immediately
make a second attack against the same opponent using the same attack bonus. The target is not
considered [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against this second attack.\
**Special:** This ability only functions when carrying a light load or less.
