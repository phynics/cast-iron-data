You have learned to ignore the effects of pain through long years of exposure to it.\
**Prerequisites:** [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), character level 7th, worshiper of
[Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against pain effects. Also, you suffer no adverse
effect when your nonlethal damage equals your current hit points, and you become
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) when your nonlethal damage exceeds your current hit points. You
never fall unconscious due to nonlethal damage.\
**Normal:** When your nonlethal damage equals your current hit points, you become staggered. When
your nonlethal damage exceeds your current hit points, you fall unconscious.
