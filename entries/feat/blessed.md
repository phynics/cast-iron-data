## Blessed (Story)

**Sources:** Quests and Campaigns pg 8

The repeated notice and boons of celestials have marked you as one of their chosen agents.\
**Prerequisites:** You must have had friendly contact with a good outsider that would qualify as a
challenging foe, be a sorcerer with the celestial bloodline, or have direct celestial ancestry (such
as by being an [aasimar](ref/race/aasimar)).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Charisma-based checks involving good-aligned outsiders and a +1
bonus to the DC of spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) you use against
such creatures. You take a -2 penalty on Charisma-based checks involving evil-aligned outsiders.\
**Goal:** Thwart or decisively defeat an evil creature that qualifies as a challenging foe, but show
mercy and then convert that creature toward good.\
**Completion Benefit:** You gain the benefit of
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) as a constant spell-like ability. In
addition, you gain a +2 bonus on caster level checks against evil-aligned outsiders.

_Suggested Traits:_ [Indomitable Faith](ref/trait/indomitable-faith),
[Inspiring](ref/trait/inspiring), [Memorable](ref/trait/memorable).
