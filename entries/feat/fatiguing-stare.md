## Fatiguing Stare (Combat, Stare)

Your painful stare fatigues your opponent.\
**Prerequisites:** Mesmerist level 7th, painful stare class feature.\
**Benefit:** When a target takes damage from your painful stare, it must succeed at a Fortitude
saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 your mesmerist level + your Charisma modifier) or become
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for 1 round. This can't cause the target to become
[exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted), even if the target is already fatigued.
