## Adder Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 89

You can quickly apply poison to gloved hands, protected feet, or other protected body parts,
delivering the poison with your unarmed strikes.\
**Prerequisites:** Poison use class feature, Craft (alchemy) 1 rank,
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can apply one dose of contact or injury poison to two body parts
that you use for unarmed strikes. You must still protect yourself against exposure to contact
poisons you apply in this way.\
**Normal:** Applying poison to a weapon or single piece of ammunition is a standard action.
