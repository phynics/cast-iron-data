## Swordplay Upset (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 17

You catch opponents off guard with your attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint), [Swordplay Style](ref/feat/swordplay-style),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon, base attack bonus +5.\
**Benefit:** While using Swordplay Style, as an immediate action, you can attempt a
[feint](ref/rule/feint) against an opponent that makes a melee attack against you and misses.
