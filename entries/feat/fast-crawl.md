**Sources:** Faction Guide pg 56

You are skilled at moving while prone.\
**Benefit:** While [prone](ref/rule/prone), you can move at half speed. This movement provokes
attacks of opportunity as normal. You can take a 5-foot step while crawling. This benefit does not
stack with the [rogue crawl](ref/talent/rogue-crawl) talent.\
**Normal:** You can crawl 5 feet as a move action. You cannot take a 5-foot step while crawling.
