Your understanding of horses goes so deep that you can communicate with them as if you shared a
language.\
**Prerequisites:** [Horse Whisperer](ref/feat/horse-whisperer), Handle Animal 5 ranks, Ride 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** With a successful DC 20 Handle Animal check, you can communicate with a
[horse](ref/monster/horse) as if you were using [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)
for 1 round. The horse's ability to communicate and take actions is still limited by its
intelligence.
