## Iconoclast (Combat)

**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 7

You know how to turn a holy symbol into a weapon against her followers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Divine Defiance](ref/feat/divine-defiance),
[Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), Intimidate 3
ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver against a holy or
unholy symbol, you can attempt an Intimidate check to demoralize as a free action. This
demoralization affects all followers of the deity or philosophy whose symbol you sundered who saw
you sunder it and are within 30 feet. If you destroyed the symbol, you gain a +5 bonus on your
Intimidate check. You can use this feat when sundering any physical holy symbol - it doesn't have to
be the hand-held variety commonly used by clerics and other divine spellcasters.
