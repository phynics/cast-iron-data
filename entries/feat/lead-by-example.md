## Lead by Example (Combat)

You have been placed in positions of importance since a young age, and others naturally follow your
example.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 15, [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit in combat, all allies within 30 feet gain a +2
morale bonus on attack rolls for the next round.
