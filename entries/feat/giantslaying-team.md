## Giantslaying Team (Combat, Teamwork)

When a giant attempts to hit your ally, that giant unwittingly reveals an opening in her defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility), base attack bonus
+4, defensive training racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Whenever a foe with the giant subtype you threaten misses with an attack of opportunity
against an ally with this feat, you can make a melee attack against that giant as an immediate
action.
