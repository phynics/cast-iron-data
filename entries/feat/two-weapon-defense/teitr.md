You are skilled at defending yourself while dual-wielding.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting).\
**Benefit:** When wielding a double weapon or two weapons (not including natural weapons or unarmed
strikes), you gain a +1 shield bonus to your AC. When you are using
[defensive stance](ref/rule/combat-errata) or using the [total defense](ref/misc/total-defense)
action, this shield bonus increases to +2.
