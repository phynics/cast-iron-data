You excel at hiding your traps in the wilderness, making them more difficult for your enemies to
locate and avoid.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (traps) 4 ranks, Survival 4 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you craft and set a trap in a wilderness environment, you increase the DC of the
Perception check required to find it by 5.
