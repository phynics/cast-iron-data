**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 151

Magic is in your blood, and at your fingertips.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 10; [elf](ref/race/elf), [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf), or
[gnome](ref/race/gnome).\
**Benefit:** Choose a 0-level spell from the sorcerer/wizard spell list. You can cast this spell
three times per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level is
equal to your character level. The save DC is 10 + your Charisma modifier.
