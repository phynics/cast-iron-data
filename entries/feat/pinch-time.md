Often having one parent with a human lifespan and one with an elven lifespan, many half-elves have a
skewed perception of time, and none more so than the Spireborn, who also study the subject of time
academically.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 5th, Spireborn [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, as a standard action, you can gain the benefits of
[haste](ref/spell/haste) until the end of your next turn.
