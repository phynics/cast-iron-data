The raw magic that flows through your gnome blood is stronger than normal.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [Gnome](ref/race/gnome).\
**Benefit:** You gain an additional three uses per day of your gnome
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) ([dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights),
[ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound), [prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation)). You can use
these in any combination; for example, you can use _[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ four
times in one day (taking all three additional uses for the same spell), or you can cast
_[ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound)_ twice, _[prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation)_
twice, [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) twice, and
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ once. If you have a feat, trait, or other ability that
changes your racial 0-level spell-like abilities to other 0-level spells, this feat applies to them
instead.
