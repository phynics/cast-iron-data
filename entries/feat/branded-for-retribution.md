You brand an enemy with your bane weapon, making it more vulnerable to your allies' attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Bane class feature.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, expend 3 rounds of your bane class feature and make a melee touch
attack with the weapon affected by bane. If you hit, your target takes no damage but is branded
until the start of your next turn. While this brand remains, your allies' weapons are considered to
have the [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) ability with which your weapon is imbued when they attack the
branded creature.
