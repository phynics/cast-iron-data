Even when your innate powers over light fail you, you and your allies prove vigilant against dark
forces, fighting until the end.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar), [daylight](ref/spell/daylight)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you cast your _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ spell-like ability, you do so
as if your caster level were one higher. Whenever an enemy successfully counters or dispels your
_[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ spell-like ability, you and any allies within a 20-foot radius gain
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet for a number of rounds equal to your character level. This
is a supernatural effect.
