## Cornugon Trip (Combat)

**Sources:** Cheliax, Empire of Devils pg 26

You can throw your spiked chain to make trip attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (spiked chain).\
**Benefit:** You may throw your [spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain) (as if it were a thrown
weapon with a range increment of 10 feet) and can make trip attacks when throwing it. Failing at
this attack does not knock you prone.
