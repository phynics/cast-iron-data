Your ability to possess bodies is more powerful.\
**Prerequisites:** [Demonic Possession](ref/feat/demonic-possession) or
[shadow demon](ref/monster/demon-shadow-demon), Cha 25\
**Benefit:** The duration of your [magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) increases to 24 hours. You can choose to activate
a host body's extraordinary abilities, supernatural abilities, spell-like abilities, and spells
known or prepared.
