## Manyshot (Combat)

You can fire multiple arrows at a single target.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 17, [Rapid Shot](ref/feat/rapid-shot), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** When making a full-attack action with a bow, your first attack fires two arrows. If the
attack hits, both arrows hit. Apply precision-based damage (such as
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack)) and critical hit damage only once for this attack. Damage
bonuses from using a composite bow with a high Strength bonus apply to each arrow, as do other
damage bonuses, such as a ranger's [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) bonus.
[Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and resistances apply separately to each arrow.
