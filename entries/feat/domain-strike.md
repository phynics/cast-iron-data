## Domain Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 98

You unleash a domain power upon your enemy as part of your unarmed strike.\
**Prerequisites:** Domain class feature,
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** When you gain this feat, choose one domain-granted power that you can use to affect no
more than one opponent. If you make a successful unarmed strike against an opponent, in addition to
dealing your unarmed strike damage, you can use a swift action to deliver the effects of the chosen
granted power to that opponent. Doing so provokes no attacks of opportunity.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you take it, you apply it to a
different qualifying domain power.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 118\
You can spend 2 stamina points to apply the effects of your chosen domain power as a free action
instead of a swift action.
