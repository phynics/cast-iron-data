You can tattoo arcane runes upon your flesh or the flesh of others.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** You can create runes. Inscribing a rune takes one day for each 1,000 gp in its base
price. To inscribe a rune, you must spend 1/25 of its base price in XP and use up raw materials
costing half of its base price.
