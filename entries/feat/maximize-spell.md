## Maximize Spell (Metamagic)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 130

Your spells have the maximum possible effect.\
**Benefit:** All variable, numeric effects of a spell modified by this feat are maximized. Saving
throws and opposed rolls are not affected, nor are spells without random variables. A maximized
spell uses up a spell slot three levels higher than the spell's actual level.

An [empowered](ref/feat/empower-spell), maximized spell gains the separate benefits of each feat:
the maximum result plus half the normally rolled result.
