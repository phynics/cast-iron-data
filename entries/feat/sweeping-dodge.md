## Sweeping Dodge (Combat)

A quick sweep of your cloak deflects an incoming attack.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge).\
**Benefit:** When you make a [dueling dodge](ref/rule/dueling-dodge) while wearing a cloak, you gain
[evasion](ref/ability/evasion) (as the rogue ability). If a critical hit or
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) is scored on you, there is a 25% chance that the critical
hit or sneak attack is negated and damage is instead rolled normally. This feat only applies until
the attack that triggered the immediate action is resolved. This feat does not stack with the
[fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification) armor special ability or similar effects.
