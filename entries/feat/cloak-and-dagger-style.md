## Cloak and Dagger Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Blood of Shadows pg 24

Your attacks effortlessly inflict debilitating conditions on unaware opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick), [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon, base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** Choose one light melee weapon. While using this style, whenever you use the attack
action to attack an opponent during a surprise round with your chosen weapon, you can attempt a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver check against that opponent as a free action.
Additionally, if an adjacent target attempts to remove a condition that you inflicted upon it with
the dirty trick combat maneuver, it provokes an attack of opportunity from you.\
**Special:** In addition to the chosen weapon, a character with the weapon training class feature
can use Cloak and Dagger Style with any light melee weapon belonging to any fighter weapon group he
has selected with weapon training. A character with the swashbuckler weapon training class feature
can use Cloak and Dagger Style with light and one-handed piercing weapons.
