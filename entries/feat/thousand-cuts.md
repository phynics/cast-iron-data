## Thousand Cuts (Combat, Combination)

Your attacks are calculated to draw the most blood possible. With every swing, you open persistent,
vicious wounds that drain the life from your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with slashing weapon.\
**Benefit:** When you deal damage with a melee attack with a slashing weapon to an opponent you have
already damaged this round, you can also deal 1 point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage that lasts
for 2 rounds. This bleed damage stacks with itself, but only if dealt in the same round.
