## Earth Child Topple (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 99

Your mastery of balance and momentum allows you to bring down giants with your bare hands.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf) or [gnome](ref/race/gnome), defensive training
racial trait, [Earth Child Style](ref/feat/earth-child-style),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Acrobatics 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can [trip](ref/rule/trip) a creature of the giant subtype of up to Huge size. While
using Earth Child Style, you add your Wisdom bonus on combat maneuver checks made to trip a creature
of the giant subtype, as well as on attack rolls to confirm a critical hit against such a creature.\
**Normal:** You can trip only those opponents that are one size category larger than you.
