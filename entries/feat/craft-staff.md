## Craft Staff (Item Creation)

You can create magic staves.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 11th.\
**Benefit:** You can create any staff whose prerequisites you meet. Crafting a staff takes 1 day for
each 1,000 gp in its base price. To craft a staff, you must use up raw materials costing half of its
base price. A newly created staff has 10 charges. See the
[magic item creation rules](ref/rule/magic-item-creation) for more information.
