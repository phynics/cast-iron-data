## Spiked Destroyer (Combat)

You can attack with your armor spikes while using bull rush or overrun.\
**Prerequisites:** Proficient with [armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes).\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or [overrun](ref/rule/overrun)
combat maneuver, you may automatically make an attack roll with your armor spikes against the target
of the maneuver as a swift action, using your highest attack bonus.
