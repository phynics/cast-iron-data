## Fearsome Finish (Combat)

When you slay enemies, you frighten other opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** [Intimidating Prowess](ref/feat/intimidating-prowess), worshiper of
[Lamashtu](ref/faith/lamashtu).\
**Benefit:** Upon reducing an enemy to 0 or fewer hit points, for the remaining duration of the
combat encounter, you gain a +4 bonus on any [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks made to
demoralize opponents who can see both you and the opponent you dropped.
