Your knowledge of your enemies' vulnerable spots is especially punishing.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +6d6,
[Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), any critical feat, base attack bonus +9.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully sneak attack an opponent for a second time on your turn, you
can spend a swift action to apply the effects of one critical feat you know to that opponent.
