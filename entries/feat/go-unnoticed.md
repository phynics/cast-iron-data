Your small size lets you quickly duck out of sight.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, Small size or smaller.\
**Benefit:** During the first round of combat, [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) opponents are
considered not to have noticed you yet for the purposes of [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) skill
checks, allowing you to make a Stealth check that round to hide from them.
