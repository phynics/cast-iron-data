## Lector Priest (Origin)

Your creator was a priest and magician, and your memories are filled with the smell of incense.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shabti](ref/race/shabti).\
**Benefit:** You gain [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) as a class skill. Whenever you attempt a
Spellcraft check, you can roll twice and take the better of the two results.
