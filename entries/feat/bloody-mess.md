## Bloody Mess (Combat)

**Sources:** Antihero's Handbook pg 8

Your recklessness often puts you in harm's way, and you are terrifying to behold when covered in
your own blood.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Intimidate).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you take an amount of piercing, slashing, or bleed damage equal to or greater
than double your character level, you can attempt an [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check as an
immediate action to demoralize the creature that dealt the damage as long as it is within 30 feet of
you. If the damage resulted from a critical hit, you can cause the target creature to be
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) instead of [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) if you successfully demoralize
it. You can use this feat only once per round to attempt an Intimidate check to demoralize an
opponent.
