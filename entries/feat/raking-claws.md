Your claws cause bleeding wounds.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +8, [shifter](ref/class/shifter), wild shape class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you deal piercing or slashing damage with your primary natural weapons, your
opponent takes 1d6 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage at the start of his turn each round, in
addition to the damage dealt by the attack. Bleed damage can be stopped with a successful DC 15 Heal
skill check or any magical healing.
