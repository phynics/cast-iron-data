## Kobold Flood (Combat)

You are an expert at keeping creatures on the ground.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Kobold Groundling](ref/feat/kobold-groundling), [Kobold Style](ref/feat/kobold-style), Small size
or smaller.\
**Benefit:** You can make a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver check to mount a prone
Medium or Large foe. If you're successful, you move into the target's square and are considered to
be on top of the target. For the target to stand, it must succeed at a combat maneuver check to
break your grapple.
