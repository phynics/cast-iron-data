**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory pg 31

You are adept at increasing the range, area, and accuracy of splash weapons.\
**Benefit:** When [throwing a splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon), you act as if you had
the [Far Shot](ref/feat/far-shot) feat. When you hit with a splash weapon, select one additional
square adjacent to the splash area; creatures in this area also take splash damage. When you miss
with a splash weapon, you may adjust the miss direction on the grid by +1 or -1. This feat counts as
Far Shot for the purpose of qualifying for other feats, but only in regard to splash weapons.
