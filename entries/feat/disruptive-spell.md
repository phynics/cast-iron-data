## Disruptive Spell (Metamagic)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 157

Your magical energies cling to enemies, interfering with their spellcasting.\
**Benefit:** Targets affected by a disruptive spell must make
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks when using spells or
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (DC equals the save DC of the disruptive spell
plus the level of the spell being cast) for 1 round. Targets that avoid the spell's effects avoid
this feat's effect as well. A disruptive spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the
spell's actual level.
