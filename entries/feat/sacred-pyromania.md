**Sources:** Heroes of Golarion pg 10

Your faith in Sarenrae and the power of flame allows you to lace fire with holy power and burn
things that normally can't be burned.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin), worshiper of [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you make an attack or cast a spell that deals fire damage, the fire damage
applies to incorporeal creatures, regardless of whether the fire is magical. An incorporeal
creature's 50% reduction in damage from corporeal sources does not apply to any fire damage that you
deal.
