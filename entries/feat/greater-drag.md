## Greater Drag (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 161

Foes that you drag are thrown out of balance.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Drag](ref/feat/improved-drag),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +2 bonus on checks made to [drag](ref/rule/drag) a foe. This bonus stacks
with the bonus granted by Improved Drag. Whenever you drag a foe, his movement provokes attacks of
opportunity from all of your allies (but not you).\
**Normal:** Creatures moved by drag do not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 121\
When you succeed at a drag combat maneuver check, you can spend 5 stamina points to gain up to 10
feet of movement for your drag. You can use this movement in place of your normal movement, but only
to drag an opponent.
