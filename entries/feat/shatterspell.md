## Shatterspell (Combat)

Your mighty blows shatter your enemy's magic.\
**Prerequisites:** [Disruptive](ref/feat/disruptive), [Spellbreaker](ref/feat/spellbreaker),
[dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), 10th-level fighter.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can attempt to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) an ongoing spell
effect as if you had the [spell sunder](ref/ragepower/spell-sunder) rage power. You may use this
feat once per day, plus one additional time per day for every 5 points by which your base attack
bonus exceeds +10.
