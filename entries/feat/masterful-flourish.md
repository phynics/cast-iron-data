## Masterful Flourish (Combat, Performance)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 12

Your training with gladiatorial weapons gives you the upper hand when using them in performance
combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Weapon training class feature.\
**Benefit:** When wielding a weapon with the [performance](ref/misc/performance) weapon special
quality that belongs to a [category of weapons](ref/ability/weapon-groups) in which you have weapon
training, you may add the bonus from your weapon training to any
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) checks made as a result of using the weapon.
