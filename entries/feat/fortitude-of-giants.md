Your great mass and vigor allows you to shrug off the effects of poison and fatigue.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 14, [Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude), giant subtype.\
**Benefit:** When you attempt a saving throw against a poison effect or an effect that inflicts the
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) condition, roll twice and take the higher result.
