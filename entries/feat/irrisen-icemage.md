**Sources:** Cities of Golarion pg 62

You were born with the power to drive ice and snow as others drive their slaves.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sorcerer](ref/class/sorcerer), Irrisen affinity, may only be taken at first
level.\
**Benefit:** All of your spells with the cold descriptor are cast at +1 caster level. Any spell with
a different energy descriptor is cast at -1 caster level. Three times per day you may alter a spell
you cast to deal cold damage instead of its normal damage; your +1 caster level for this feat
applies to this variant casting.
