You are skilled at both manufacturing and using alchemical grenades.\
**Prerequisites:** [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything).\
**Benefit:** When you light an alchemical grenade (such as a
[fuse grenade](ref/eq-weapon/fuse-grenade), [pellet grenade](ref/eq-weapon/pellet-grenade), or
[sting grenade](ref/eq-weapon/sting-grenade)) you select how many rounds later the grenade explodes
(minimum 1 round, maximum 3 rounds). In addition, you gain a +4 bonus on Craft (alchemy) checks to
craft grenades.\
**Normal:** Alchemical grenades explode 1d3 rounds after the fuse is lit.
