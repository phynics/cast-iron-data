## Deathless Initiate (Combat)

For you, impending death is a call to wrath.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, Con 13, [orc](ref/race/orc) or [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc),
[Diehard](ref/feat/diehard), [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You are not [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) while using the Diehard feat, but if you
take a move and a standard action or a full-round action while you are at 0 or fewer hit points you
take 1 point of damage. Further, while using the Diehard feat, you gain a +2 bonus on melee attacks
and damage rolls.
