**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 116\
If your successful critical confirmation roll is a natural 19 or 20, you can spend 2 stamina points
to roll another confirmation roll. If this confirmation roll also succeeds, increase your critical
multiplier by 1 for this attack, and you can roll again. If you continue to roll 19 or 20, you can
continue to spend stamina points for additional rolls, and the increases to the critical multiplier
stack.
