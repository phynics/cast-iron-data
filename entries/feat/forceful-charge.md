This animal's charges move its enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion).\
**Benefit:** Once per round when this animal companion hits an opponent with a
[charge](ref/rule/charge) attack, it can initiate a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) against that
target as a free action. If it moves its full speed as part of the charge, it gains a +4 bonus on
its combat maneuver check to resolve this bull rush.
