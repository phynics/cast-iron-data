You are an exceptional shapechanger.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker).\
**Benefit:** When you change shape to your bestial form, you may choose one additional feature from
those listed in your shapechange ability and gain that benefit while in bestial form.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects stack.
