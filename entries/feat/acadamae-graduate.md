You have passed the grueling Test of Summoning and graduated from the Acadamae.\
**Prerequisites:** Specialist wizard 1st; cannot have conjuration as a forbidden school.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you cast a prepared arcane spell from the conjuration (summoning) school that
takes longer than a standard action to cast, reduce the casting time by one round (to a minimum
casting time of one standard action). Casting a spell in this way is taxing and requires a Fortitude
save (DC 15 + spell level) to resist becoming [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued).
