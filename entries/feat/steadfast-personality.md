You rely on your assuredness and sense of self to help keep your mind clear.\
**Benefit:** Add your Charisma modifier instead of your Wisdom bonus on Will saves against
mind-affecting effects. If you have a Wisdom penalty, you must apply both your Wisdom penalty and
your Charisma modifier.
