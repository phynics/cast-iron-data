Your curses are particularly tenacious.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast a spell or [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
with the curse descriptor, or a special ability with a curse effect.\
**Benefit:** Any curse effect you create is difficult to remove. When a creature makes a caster
level check to remove the curse, it must roll twice and take the worse result.
