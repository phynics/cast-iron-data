You have learned to experience emotion.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [android](ref/race/android).\
**Benefit:** You lose the emotionless special quality. You can gain morale bonuses, and can be
affected by emotion-based effects and fear effects. You lack the +4 racial bonus on saving throws
against mind-affecting effects.
