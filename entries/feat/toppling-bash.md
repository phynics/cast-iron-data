## Toppling Bash (Combat, Shield Mastery)

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 19

Your shield bash throws your enemies off balance.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Shield Bash](ref/feat/improved-shield-bash),
[Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus), [Stumbling Bash](ref/feat/stumbling-bash), base attack bonus
+11 or fighter level 8th.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action when you hit a creature with a [shield bash](ref/misc/shield-bash),
you can attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver against that creature at a -5 penalty. This
does not provoke an attack of opportunity, and if your attempt fails by 10 or more, you are not
knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone).

### Stamina

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 21\
You can spend 5 stamina points to negate the penalty on your combat maneuver check to knock a
creature prone.
