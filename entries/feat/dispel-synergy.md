By tearing away an opponent's magical defenses, you leave your enemy vulnerable, making it difficult
for him to resist your spells.\
**Prerequisites:** Spellcraft 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** If you successfully [dispel](ref/spell/dispel-magic) an ongoing magical effect on an
opponent, that opponent takes a -2 penalty on saving throws against your spells until the end of
your next turn.
