## Spear Dancer (Combat)

You incorporate your two-handed reach weapon into a dizzying martial dance.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (any two-handed reach weapon), Perform
(dance) 4 ranks.\
**Benefit:** Each time you hit a creature with a two-handed reach weapon that you have Weapon Focus
in, the creature is [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 round.
