Your summoned minions slink along under the shadows of the stars.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (conjuration), able to cast
[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally).\
**Benefit:** Creatures you summon gain the [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight) feat, a +5 bonus to
Perception and Stealth checks in [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darkness, and their
natural weapons are treated as cold iron for overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
