## Relic Breaker (Combat)

You are furious when destroying dangerous objects.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Gate Breaker](ref/feat/gate-breaker),
[Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack
bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) a held object, its bearer must succeed at a
Reflex saving throw or the bearer [catches on fire](ref/rule/catching-on-fire) (taking 1d6 points of
fire damage immediately, and an additional 1d6 points of fire damage each subsequent round the
creature fails a Reflex saving throw). When your total Hit Dice reach 5, this damage increases by
1d6, and it increases by 1d6 every 5 levels thereafter, to a maximum of 5d6 at 20th level. The
saving throw's DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 your HD + your Charisma modifier.
