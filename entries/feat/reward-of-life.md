**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 155

When you lay on hands, you are also healed.\
**Prerequisites:** [Lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) class feature.\
**Benefit:** Each time you use your lay on hands ability to heal a creature other than yourself, you
heal a number of hit points equal to your Charisma bonus. This ability has no effect if you use lay
on hands to harm undead.
