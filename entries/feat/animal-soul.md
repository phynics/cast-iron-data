Your close bond with an animal allows you to ignore harmful magic that cannot affect your wild side.\
**Prerequisites:** Animal companion or mount class feature.\
**Benefit:** You can choose not to allow spells and effects to affect you if they would not be
capable of affecting both your original creature type and the animal creature type.
