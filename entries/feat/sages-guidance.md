## Sage's Guidance (Familiar)

**Sources:** Familiar Folio pg 19

Your familiar's keen observations help you defeat foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Must have a familiar with the [sage](ref/arc-familiar/sage) archetype.\
**Benefit:** When your [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) succeeds at a Knowledge check to identify a
hazard or an opponent's weaknesses and abilities, you gain a +2 insight bonus on attacks and skill
checks against the opponent or hazard for 1 round. If your familiar is sharing a space with the
opponent or hazard, this bonus is granted to all allies who can see and understand the familiar.
