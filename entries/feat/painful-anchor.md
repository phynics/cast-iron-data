**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 153

Evil outsiders take damage when they attempt to connect to other planes.\
**Prerequisites:** Anchoring aura class feature.\
**Benefit:** When an evil outsider uses a calling, summoning, or teleportation effect, or any
ability that physically transports a creature to or from another plane (such as
[blink](ref/spell/blink) or [etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness)) within your anchoring aura, it
takes damage equal to 4d8 + your Charisma modifier. This damage comes from holy power and is not
subject to [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction), energy immunities, or energy resistances.
