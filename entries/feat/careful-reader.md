**Sources:** Occult Origins pg 24

Your training with the [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars) (or its rivals) has taught you
proper caution and wariness when deciphering the ancient scrolls and inscriptions of fallen
civilizations.\
**Prerequisites:** Linguistics 3 ranks, Spellcraft 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against written magical effects, including
[explosive runes](ref/spell/explosive-runes), [glyph of warding](ref/spell/glyph-of-warding),
[illusory script](ref/spell/illusory-script), [symbol of death](ref/spell/symbol-of-death), and
similar magic, as well as against any form of curse, madness, possession, or similar effect -
magical or not - that might occur from reading a book, inscription, scroll, or any other form of
writing.
