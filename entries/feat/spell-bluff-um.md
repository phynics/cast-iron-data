You know the principles of arcane dueling, and when fighting other spellcasters, you have learned to
hide the true nature of your spells until the last possible moment.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 5 ranks, Spellcraft 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** If another spellcaster tries to [counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) your casting,
she adds +4 to her Spellcraft DC when trying to determine your spell.

Because you have studied how to mask the recognizable elements of your spellcasting, you gain a +2
bonus on your Spellcraft checks to identify and counter an opponent's spell if it is a spell you
know or have in your spellbook.
