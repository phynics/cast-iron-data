You can regain power when you kill a creature.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, sorcerer bloodline class feature.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, as an immediate action upon reducing a creature to 0 or fewer hit points
with one of your sorcerer spells, you can regain one usage of a
[sorcerer bloodline](ref/ability/sorcerer-bloodlines) power that has a limited number of uses per
day. The slain creature must have at least half as many Hit Dice as your sorcerer level. You cannot
use this feat to gain another usage of a bloodline power that you have not yet used today.
