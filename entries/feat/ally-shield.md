## Ally Shield (Betrayal, Teamwork)

You are willing to use your allies as shields to ward off attacks aimed at you.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are the target of a melee or ranged attack and are adjacent to an ally who
also has this feat, you can initiate this feat to skillfully pull the abettor into harm's way or
dodge behind the abettor as an immediate action. You gain [cover](ref/rule/cover) against that
attack (and only that attack). If the attack misses you but would have hit you if not for the cover
bonus to your Armor Class, the abettor becomes the target of the attack and the attacker must make a
new attack roll (with all the same modifiers) against the abettor's Armor Class.
