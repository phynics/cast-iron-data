## Street Sweep (Combat)

After driving your foe back, you can quickly knock it to the ground and slow it down.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Street Style](ref/feat/street-style), base attack bonus +6 or monk level 5th.\
**Benefit:** While using Street Style, the first time each round that you deal damage with an
unarmed strike to a foe that you [bull rushed](ref/rule/bull-rush) with Street Style since the
beginning of your last turn, that foe must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + your base attack
bonus) or be knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone) and staggered for 1 round.
