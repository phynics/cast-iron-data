## Improved Dirty Trick (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 162

You are skilled at pulling dirty tricks on your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise).\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks
made to attempt a dirty trick. You also receive a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense when an
opponent tries a dirty trick on you.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a dirty trick combat maneuver.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 123\
You can select this feat even if you don't meet the ability score prerequisite (Intelligence 13).
You gain the benefits of this feat only as long as you have at least 1 stamina point in your stamina
pool. When you are the target of a dirty trick combat maneuver, you can spend a number of stamina
points up to your Strength or Dexterity bonus, whichever is greater. Your CMD against that dirty
trick attempt increases by that number.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat **may** be removed, replaced with the new [Deft Maneuvers](ref/rule/deft-maneuvers) feat.
