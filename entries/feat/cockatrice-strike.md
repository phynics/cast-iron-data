## Cockatrice Strike (Combat)

With a single strike, you transmute flesh to stone.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Gorgon's Fist](ref/feat/gorgons-fist), [Medusa's Wrath](ref/feat/medusas-wrath), base attack bonus
+16.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, you can make a single unarmed strike against a dazed,
flat-footed, paralyzed, staggered, stunned, or unconscious foe. If that attack is a critical hit,
the target is [petrified](ref/rule/petrified) unless it succeeds on a Fortitude saving throw with a
DC of 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier. This is a supernatural polymorph effect.
