The forces of nature bolster your vitality when you use wild shape.\
**Prerequisites:** Wild shape class feature.\
**Benefit:** A number of times per day equal to your Wisdom bonus (minimum 1), when you use wild
shape, you can gain a number of [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to your
base attack bonus. These temporary hit points last for the duration of the wild shape or until they
are lost.
