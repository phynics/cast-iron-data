You delight and excel in bringing down hobbled targets.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) class feature,
[kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** Your sneak attack damage increases by 1d6 against targets that are already afflicted
with any of the following conditions: [blinded](ref/misc/blinded), [dying](ref/misc/dying),
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled), [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted), [frightened](ref/misc/frightened),
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless), [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated), [panicked](ref/misc/panicked),
[paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed), or [stunned](ref/misc/stunned).
