**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 213

When you channel positive energy, you can also channel ki.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature, worshiper of
[Irori](ref/faith/irori).\
**Benefit:** When you channel positive energy to heal living creatures, any creature that could be
healed by the channeled energy can choose to regain ki from it instead. Affected creatures regain a
number of ki points equal to the number of dice healed by the channel. A creature regaining ki in
such a way must either regain ki or be healed by the channel (it cannot gain both).
