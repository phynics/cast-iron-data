**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 113\
When casting a spell while wearing armor, you can spend 1 stamina point to activate Arcane Armor
Mastery as a free action, or spend a number of stamina points equal to the spell's level (minimum 1)
to ignore your armor's arcane spell failure chance entirely for that casting.
