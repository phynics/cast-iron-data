## Cleave (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 119

You can strike two adjacent foes with a single swing.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can make a single attack at your full base attack bonus
against a foe within reach. If you hit, you deal damage normally and can make an additional attack
(using your full base attack bonus) against a foe that is adjacent to the first and also within
reach. You can only make one additional attack per round with this feat. When you use this feat, you
take a -2 penalty to your Armor Class until your next turn.
