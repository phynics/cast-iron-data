## Spell Bluff (AH) (Teamwork)

When your ally is in the area of effect of a harmful spell, your foes are caught off guard.\
**Benefit:** When an ally who also has this feat fails her saving throw (including voluntarily
failing the save) against a spell that would harm her with an area of effect that includes her,
intelligent enemies within the spell's area take a -2 penalty on their saving throws to resist the
spell's effects.
