## Martial Dominance (Combat)

Your skill at arms intimidates your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +5, Intimidate 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You can use your base attack bonus in place of your ranks in Intimidate to determine
your Intimidate skill bonus. When you confirm a critical hit against a creature, you can attempt an
Intimidate check to demoralize that creature as an immediate action.
