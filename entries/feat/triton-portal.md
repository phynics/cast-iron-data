You can channel your inner magic to summon allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 5th, hydraulic push
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), [undine](ref/race/undine).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you may expend your racial hydraulic push ability to instead cast
[summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3) as a spell-like ability with a caster
level equal to your character level. This use of the ability can only summon 1d3
[Small water elementals](ref/monster/water-elemental), 1d4+1 [dolphins](ref/monster/dolphin), a
[shark](ref/monster/shark), or an [electric eel](ref/monster/eel-electric-eel). Using this ability
is a full-round action.
