## Motivating Display (Combat)

**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 23

You can empower your allies through their fear of you.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use Dazzling Display to demoralize foes, you can motivate your allies to
better serve you. In addition to possibly demoralizing foes within 30 feet, your
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check while using Dazzling Display also applies to allies within
30 feet who can see you. The DC for this effect is the same as it would be to demoralize your
allies. Each ally so affected gains a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls and skill checks for the
duration that it would normally be shaken.
