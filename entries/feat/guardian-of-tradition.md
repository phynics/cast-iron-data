Your deity rewards your devotion to the duty of your race.\
**Prerequisites:** Domain class feature, [girtablilu](ref/monster/girtablilu).\
**Benefit:** You can replace one of the domains offered by your deity with one of the following
druid domains: [Desert](ref/druiddomain/desert), [Ruins](ref/druiddomain/ruins), or
[Vermin](ref/druiddomain/vermin). You gain a +1 bonus to your caster level when casting domain
spells from the selected domain.
