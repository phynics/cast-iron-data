## Uncanny Defense (Combat)

Your instinct for self-preservation gives you many advantages.\
**Prerequisites:** [Cautious Fighter](ref/feat/cautious-fighter), base attack bonus +3,
[halfling](ref/race/halfling).\
**Benefit:** While [fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or taking the
[total defense](ref/misc/total-defense) action, you gain a bonus on your Reflex saving throws and to
your CMD equal to 1/2 of the dodge bonus to AC you gained from taking that action.
