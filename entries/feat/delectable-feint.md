You can distract opponents with your delectable rinds.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15, delicious racial trait, [ghoran](ref/race/ghoran).\
**Benefit:** Other creatures find you so tasty that they become distracted in combat, allowing you
to exploit their momentary lapses in concentration. You gain a +2 bonus on Bluff checks to feint
living herbivorous or omnivorous creatures. In addition, whenever you successfully feint such an
opponent, in addition to the normal effects of being feinted in combat, that opponent takes a -2
penalty on Perception checks and [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks until the start of
your next turn.
