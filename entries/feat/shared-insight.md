You deftly direct others' attentions where you wish.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** As a move action, you can grant all friendly creatures within 30 feet who can see or
hear you a +2 bonus on Perception checks for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier
(minimum 1 round).
