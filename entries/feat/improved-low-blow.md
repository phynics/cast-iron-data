## Improved Low Blow (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 66

You are adept at hitting larger opponents where it hurts.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +4, [halfling](ref/race/halfling), low-blow racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Your bonus to confirm critical hits against opponents larger than yourself improves to
+2. Furthermore, once per day, after you fail to hit with a critical hit confirmation roll, you can
reroll the confirmation roll, but must take the new result even if it is worse.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 123\
You can spend 5 stamina points to reroll a second critical hit confirmation roll in the same day.
