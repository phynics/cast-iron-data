Your extravagant jewelry and accessories give you an air of importance you can use to your advantage
in many social situations.\
**Benefit:** As long as you are wearing a valuable nonmagical item in a magic item slot, you gain a
+1 bonus on skill checks with a corresponding skill. Bonuses from wearing items in multiple slots
that affect the same skill do not stack.

Each item must be worth at least 5% of your [character wealth by level](ref/rule/placing-treasure).
For example, a 6th-level PC gains a +1 bonus on Bluff checks while wearing a gem-studded tiara
(which fills the headband slot) worth at least 800 gp. When she reaches 7th level, she must wear an
accessory in the appropriate slot worth at least 1,175 gp in order to continue gaining this bonus.

|Item Slot|Skill Bonus|
| --- | --- |
|Belt, chest, shoulders|+1 Intimidate|
|Body, feet, neck|+1 Diplomacy|
|Eyes, hands, headband|+1 Bluff|
|Head, ring, wrists|+1 Perform (any)|
