## Figment's Fluidity (Familiar)

Your familiar can rework the dream-stuff it is made of.\
**Prerequisites:** Must have a [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) with the
[figment](ref/arc-familiar/figment) archetype.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, your [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) can spend a full-round action to
manually reshape itself, reassigning 1 evolution point of eidolon evolutions. The evolution it loses
can't be a prerequisite for any other evolutions it has. It can use this ability twice per day at
7th level, and three times per day at 13th level.
