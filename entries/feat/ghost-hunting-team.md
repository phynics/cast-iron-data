Your loathing of undead is so great that even your allies can feel it.\
**Prerequisites:** [Duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker), ghost hunter racial trait.\
**Benefit:** When you use your ghost hunter ability to grant your weapons the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) special ability, you can also affect the weapons of
allies within 30 feet of you. You can activate ghost hunter an additional time each day.
