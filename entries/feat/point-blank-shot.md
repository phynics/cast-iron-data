## Point-Blank Shot (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 131

You are especially accurate when making ranged attacks against close targets.\
**Benefit:** You get a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with ranged weapons at ranges of up to 30
feet.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 129\
You can spend up to 6 stamina points to increase this feat's range by 5 feet for each stamina point
you spent.

### Mythic Point-Blank Shot

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 71\
Your accuracy with ranged attacks is unmatched at pointblank range.\
**Prerequisites:** Point-Blank Shot.\
**Benefit:** The bonus on attack and damage rolls granted by Point-Blank Shot increases to +2. As a
swift action, you can expend one use of mythic power to gain an additional bonus on these rolls
equal to half your tier.
