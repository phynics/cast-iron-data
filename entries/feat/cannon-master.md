## Cannon Master (Combat)

You have learned how to keep firearm siege engines ready for battle despite harrowing circumstances.\
**Prerequisites:** [Siege Engineer](ref/feat/siege-engineer), Knowledge (engineering) or Profession
(siege engineer) 7 ranks, proficiency with at least one firearm siege engine.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 competence bonus on any Craft (siege engine) checks made to repair a
firearm siege engine. In addition, if you are the crew leader for a firearm siege engine with the
broken condition, that siege engine's misfire range is increased by only 2 instead of by 4, and the
broken siege engine must misfire twice before it explodes.
