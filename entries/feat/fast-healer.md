You benefit greatly from your healing, be it from spells or natural healing.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard), [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance).\
**Benefit:** When you regain hit points by resting or through magical healing, you recover
additional hit points equal to half your Constitution modifier (minimum +1).
