Your long pink tongue is capable of manipulating small items and even stealing objects.\
**Prerequisites:** [Grippli](ref/race/grippli).\
**Benefit:** You have a prehensile tongue with a range of 10 feet. You can pick up items weighing no
more than 5 pounds, make Sleight of Hand checks, perform the steal or disarm
[combat maneuvers](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers), or make melee touch attacks with your tongue.
