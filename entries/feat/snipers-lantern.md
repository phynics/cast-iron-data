**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 32

You use your innate magic powers to target enemies and spot their weaknesses.\
**Prerequisites:** [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot),
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ racial
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** You can use your _[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ racial spell-like ability
to create one orb of penetrating light. This light grants you a +2 insight bonus on all ranged
attacks made against targets within 5 feet of the light's location. You can direct this light to a
new location anywhere within the spell's range as a move action, otherwise the effect functions
exactly like [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights). You can use this ability once per day, plus
an additional time per day when your caster level for your racial
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ spell-like ability reaches 5th, 10th, 15th, and 20th.
