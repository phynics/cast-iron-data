You are accustomed to riding while wearing armor, and your armor doesn't hinder your riding skills.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat), Ride 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You don't take the usual armor check penalty on Ride checks. If you are knocked
unconscious while in a saddle, you always remain in the saddle.\
**Normal:** If you are knocked unconscious while riding, you have a 50% chance to stay in the saddle
(75% if you're in a military saddle).
