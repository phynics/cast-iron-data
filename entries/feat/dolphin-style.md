## Dolphin Style (Combat, Style)

You fight like a dolphin, bunching your enemies up together to make it easier to pick them off.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Swim 3 ranks, base
attack bonus +3 or monk level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on melee attack rolls against any creature adjacent to at least two
of its other allies as long as you aren't [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) that creature, and your
melee attacks ignore any bonus to AC gained by use of the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2)
action.
