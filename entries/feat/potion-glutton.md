**Prerequisites:** Worshiper of [Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa).\
**Benefit:** You can drink potions, elixirs, or other potables (but not extracts) as a move action
without provoking an attack of opportunity.\
**Normal:** Drinking potions is a standard action that provokes an attack of opportunity.
