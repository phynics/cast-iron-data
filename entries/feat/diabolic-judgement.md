## Diabolic Judgement (Combat)

You punish your foes for every opportunity they present.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes);
[Diabolic Humiliation](ref/feat/diabolic-humiliation); [Diabolic Style](ref/feat/diabolic-style);
[Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike); [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike);
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) or [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus)
(unarmed strike); base attack bonus +12 or monk level 11th.\
**Benefit:** While using Diabolic Style, the first time each round that you successfully hit with an
attack of opportunity, you can apply the effects of any Vital Strike feat you have to that attack.\
**Special:** A monk of at least 14th level with this feat can select
[Greater Vital Strike](ref/feat/greater-vital-strike) as a monk bonus feat.
