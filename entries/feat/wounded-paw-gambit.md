## Wounded Paw Gambit (Combat, Teamwork)

Your enemy sees you as an easy mark, but doesn't notice your allies poised to shoot.\
**Prerequisites:** [Broken Wing Gambit](ref/feat/broken-wing-gambit), Bluff 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use Broken Wing Gambit and an opponent attacks you as a result, each ally
who has this feat and is within 30 feet of that opponent can attempt a ranged attack against it as
an immediate action. The ally's ranged weapon must be in hand, loaded, and ready to be fired or
thrown in order to make this attack possible.

An ally who has this feat and Broken Wing Gambit can instead use Broken Wing Gambit to attempt an
attack of opportunity against the foe, but cannot attempt both that attack of opportunity and this
ranged attack.
