## Improved Overrun (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 127

You are skilled at running down your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing an
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks made to
overrrun a foe. You also receive a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense whenever an opponent
tries to overrun you. Targets of your overrun attempt may not chose to avoid you.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing an overrun combat maneuver.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 123\
When you are the target of an overrun combat maneuver, you can spend a number of stamina points up
to your Strength or Dexterity bonus, whichever is greater. Your CMD against that overrun attempt
increases by that number.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat has been removed, replaced with the new [Powerful Maneuvers](ref/rule/powerful-maneuvers)
feat.
