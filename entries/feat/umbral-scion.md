**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 106, Pathfinder \#15: The Armageddon Echo pg 59, Pathfinder
\#18: Descent into Midnight pg 14

New [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) are unlocked for you as you rise to
ascendency among the drow people.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, Wis 13, [Drow Nobility](ref/feat/drow-nobility),
[Greater Drow Nobility](ref/feat/greater-drow-nobility),
[Improved Drow Nobility](ref/feat/improved-drow-nobility), able to use drow spell-like abilities,
[drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** Select one of the following: [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),
[divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor), or [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion). You may use this
spell once per day as a spell-like ability. Your caster level is equal to your character level.
