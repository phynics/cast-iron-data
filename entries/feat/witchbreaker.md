## Witchbreaker (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 120, People of the North pg 24

You are trained to be resilient to and disrupt the magic of hags and witches.\
**Prerequisites:** [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against the hexes, spells,
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural abilities of
[hags](ref/family/hag) and [witches](ref/class/witch). In addition, whenever you confirm a critical
hit against a hag or a witch, any of your allies affected by a mind-affecting effect from that
creature can attempt a new saving throw against the effect as an immediate action.
