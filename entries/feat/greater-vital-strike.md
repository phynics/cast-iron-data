## Greater Vital Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 126

You can make a single attack that deals incredible damage.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike),
[Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), base attack bonus +16.\
**Benefit:** When you use the attack action, you can make one attack at your highest base attack
bonus that deals additional damage. Roll the weapon's damage dice for the attack four times and add
the results together before adding bonuses from Strength, weapon abilities (such as
[flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)), precision based damage, and other damage bonuses. These extra
weapon damage dice are not multiplied on a critical hit, but are added to the total.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 122\
When using the Vital Strike combat trick, you can spend 4 stamina points instead of 2 to reroll four
damage dice.
