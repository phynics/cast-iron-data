## Lingering Spell (Metamagic)

You spell clings to existence, slowly fading from the world.\
**Benefit:** You may cause an instantaneous spell that affects an area to persist until the
beginning of your next turn. Those already in the area suffer no additional harm, but other
creatures or objects entering the area are subject to its effects. A lingering spell with a visual
manifestation obscures vision, providing [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance)
beyond 5 feet and total concealment (50% miss chance) beyond 20 feet.

A lingering spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell's actual level.
