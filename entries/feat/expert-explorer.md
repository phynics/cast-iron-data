**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 110

You are trained to seek out the unknown places of the wild and can explore more efficiently than
your untrained rivals.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (nature) or Survival 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on skill checks when using the
[exploration](ref/rule/discovery-and-exploration) rules. If you succeed at such a skill check by 5
or more, you gain an additional 1d4 Discovery Points.
