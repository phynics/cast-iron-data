You can use your mask to enhance your occult powers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Nameless One](ref/feat/nameless-one), ability to cast one or more occult
rituals.\
**Benefit:** While benefiting from the Nameless One feat, your masked identity affords you a greater
spiritual connection when you act as the primary caster for an
[occult ritual](ref/rule/occult-rituals) you know. For any such occult ritual, you can attempt all
required skill checks, even if untrained in such a skill. If you are trained in such a skill, you
instead gain a +3 bonus on your skill check.

In addition, if you have the spirit class feature (as per the [shaman](ref/class/shaman) or
[medium](ref/class/medium) class), you can use the ritualistic focus afforded by this feat to
enhance your connection to spirits with which you have communed. Once per day, when you use a
supernatural ability granted to you by your spirit (such as a shaman's spirit's hex or spirit
abilities, or a medium's spirit powers), you increase your effective class level by 1 when
determining the effects of that supernatural ability.
