Choose chaos, evil, good, or law. You can channel divine energy to affect outsiders that possess
this subtype.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy).\
**Benefit:** Instead of its normal effect, you can choose to have your ability to channel energy
heal or harm outsiders of the chosen alignment subtype. You must make this choice each time you
channel energy. If you choose to heal or harm creatures of the chosen alignment subtype, your
channel energy has no effect on other creatures. The amount of damage healed or dealt and the DC to
halve the damage is otherwise unchanged.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take
this feat, it applies to a new alignment subtype. Whenever you channel energy, you must choose which
type to effect.
