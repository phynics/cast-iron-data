## Earth Child Binder (Combat)

Even the greatest giants fear your technique.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf) or [gnome](ref/race/gnome), defensive training
racial trait, [Earth Child Style](ref/feat/earth-child-style),
[Earth Child Topple](ref/feat/earth-child-topple), [Greater Trip](ref/feat/greater-trip),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), Acrobatics 9 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can trip a creature of the giant subtype no matter its size. While you are using
Earth Child Style, when a [prone](ref/rule/prone) creature of the giant subtype stands up and
provokes an attack of opportunity from you, if you make an unarmed strike, you can declare you are
making a Stunning Fist attempt after the attack hits. You gain a +4 bonus to the DC of any Stunning
Fist effect you deliver in this way.\
**Normal:** You can only trip opponents who are one size category larger than you.
