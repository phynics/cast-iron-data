## Living Fortress (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 207

Stubborn, tenacious, and proud, you're all but immovable when you have kin to back you up.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), hardy racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you [fight defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) and you're adjacent to
an ally who also has this feat, your racial bonus on saving throws against spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) increases by 2.
