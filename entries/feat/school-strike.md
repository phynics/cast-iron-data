## School Strike (Combat)

You focus the secrets of your school of wizardry into your unarmed strike.\
**Prerequisites:** Wizard school class feature,
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** When you gain this feat, choose one [arcane school](ref/ability/arcane-schools) power
that you can use to affect no more than one opponent. If you make a successful unarmed strike
against an opponent, in addition to dealing your unarmed strike damage, you can use a swift action
to deliver the effects of the chosen school power to that opponent. Doing so provokes no attacks of
opportunity.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you take it, you apply it to a
different qualifying arcane school power.
