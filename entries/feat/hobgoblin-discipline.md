The presence of other hobgoblins bolsters your resolve.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, [hobgoblin](ref/race/hobgoblin).\
**Benefit:** While you are within 30 feet of at least two other hobgoblins, you gain a +1 morale
bonus on saving throws.
