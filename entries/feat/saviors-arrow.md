Calling on the power of Erastil, you can fire an arrow that heals instead of harms.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast _cure_ spells, proficient with longbow, worshiper of
[Erastil](ref/faith/erastil).\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you cast a cure spell, you can replace the normal somatic component
with drawing a [longbow](ref/eq-weapon/longbow) to create a shaft of green glowing light. When you
fire the bow as a part of casting the spell and hit your target with a ranged touch attack, the
green shaft of light cures the target as if it were touched by the _cure_ spell.
