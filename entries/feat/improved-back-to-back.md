## Improved Back to Back (Teamwork)

After much practice, you and an ally have become adept at fighting in close proximity to one
another.\
**Prerequisites:** [Back to Back](ref/feat/back-to-back), Perception 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While you are adjacent to an ally who is [flanked](ref/rule/flanking) and also has this
feat, you can spend a swift action to gain a +2 bonus to AC against all flankers until the start of
your next turn.
