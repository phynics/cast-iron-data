## Snatch (Monster)

**Sources:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary pg 316

This creature can grab other creatures with ease.\
**Prerequisites:** Size Huge or larger.\
**Benefit:** The creature can start a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) when it hits with a claw or bite
attack, as though it had the [grab](ref/umr/grab) ability. If it grapples a creature three or more
sizes smaller, it squeezes each round for automatic bite or claw damage with a successful grapple
check. A snatched opponent held in the creature's mouth is not allowed a Reflex save against the
creature's breath weapon, if it has one.

The creature can drop a creature it has snatched as a free action or use a standard action to fling
it aside. A flung creature travels 1d6 × 10 feet, and takes 1d6 points of damage per 10 feet
traveled. If the creature flings a snatched opponent while flying, the opponent takes this amount or
[falling](ref/rule/falling) damage, whichever is greater.
