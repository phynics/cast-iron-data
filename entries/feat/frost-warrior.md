## Frost Warrior (Combat)

The frigid cold of your body makes your melee attacks more formidable.\
**Prerequisites:** Cold subtype, giant subtype.\
**Benefit:** You naturally transfer the cold of your body to any metal melee weapons you wield,
dealing 1d6 additional points of cold damage.
