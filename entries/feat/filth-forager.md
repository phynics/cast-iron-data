Your regular exposure to the filth and unfortunate residues common to sewers and subterranean urban
environs has caused you to develop a resistance to the effects of such conditions.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on all saving throws against diseases and any effect that would
cause you to become [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) or [sickened](ref/misc/sickened).
