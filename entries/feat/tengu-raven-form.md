You can shift into the form of a giant black raven.\
**Prerequisites:** [Tengu Wings](ref/feat/tengu-wings), character level 7th,
[tengu](ref/race/tengu).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can take the form of a Large black bird resembling a raven, granting
you a fly speed of 60 feet (good maneuverability), a +4 size bonus to your Strength, a -2 penalty to
your Dexterity, and a +4 natural armor bonus. This
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) otherwise functions as
[beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2) with a caster level equal to your level.
