Although shoreborn see little of their aquatic parents, they can still call on their underwater kin
for help.\
**Prerequisites:** Shoreborn [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can fill a vial with water from a natural source and the water will
turn magical, functioning as a potion of a 1st-level druid spell of your choice with a caster level
equal to your Hit Dice. This potion becomes inert after a number of hours equal to your Hit Dice.
