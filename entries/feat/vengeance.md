## Vengeance (Story)

**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 73

The need to avenge those you loved drives you to great deeds.\
**Prerequisites:** You must have a close family member or other loved one slain by a specific
challenging foe or that foe's minions, or have the
[Raiders (childhood event)](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more) or
[Vengeance (barbarian)](ref/rule/class-background-tables) background.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on saving throws, attack rolls, and weapon damage rolls against
your chosen foe and known minions of that foe.\
**Goal:** Thwart your chosen foe.\
**Completion Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on all saving throws. This bonus stacks with this
feats's bonus against your foe and its minions, should they survive.
