## Greater Serpent Lash (Combat)

Your ability to disarm and pull foes off balance with your whip is impressive.\
**Prerequisites:** [Serpent Lash](ref/feat/serpent-lash), base attack bonus +4.\
**Benefit:** When you make a successful [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) or [trip](ref/rule/trip) maneuver
using the Serpent Lash feat, you can make your additional disarm or trip attempt against any target
within your whip's reach. With a [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) maneuver, you can move the target
creature to any point within your whip's reach.
