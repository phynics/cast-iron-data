By manifesting an eerie, spiritual eye of its own, your possessed hand can "see" on your behalf.\
**Prerequisites:** [Possessed Hand](ref/feat/possessed-hand).\
**Benefit:** While your possessed hand isn't carrying anything, you gain
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet and cannot be
[flanked](ref/rule/flanking).
