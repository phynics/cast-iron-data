## Improved Bull Rush (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 126

You are skilled at pushing your foes around.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks made
to bull rush a foe. You also receive a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense whenever an opponent
tries to bull rush you.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a bull rush combat maneuver.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 122\
When you are the target of a bull rush combat maneuver, you can spend a number of stamina points up
to your Strength or Dexterity bonus, whichever is greater. Your CMD increases by that number for
that bull rush attempt.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat has been removed, replaced with the new [Powerful Maneuvers](ref/rule/powerful-maneuvers)
feat.
