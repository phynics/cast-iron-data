Your channeled positive energy is irresistibly sweet to nearby undead.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can channel positive energy to
[fascinate](ref/misc/fascinated) all undead within 30 feet for a number of rounds equal to your
Charisma modifier (minimum of 1). Undead that succeed at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your cleric
level + your Charisma modifier) are unaffected. Channeling energy for this purpose does not heal or
harm creatures.
