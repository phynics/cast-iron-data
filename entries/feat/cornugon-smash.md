## Cornugon Smash (Combat)

Your terrible attacks strike fear into your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), Intimidate 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you damage an opponent with a Power Attack, you may make an immediate Intimidate
check as a free action to attempt to demoralize your opponent.
