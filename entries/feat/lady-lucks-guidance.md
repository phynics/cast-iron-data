[Desna](ref/faith/desna) has taken a liking to you and guides you from the stars as you travel.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (geography) 5 ranks, Knowledge (planes) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** Once per day during [overland travel](ref/rule/overland-movement), you can attempt a DC
25 Knowledge (geography) check to follow the stars. On a success, you and up to eight other
creatures use Desna's mystical guidance to travel twice the usual distance during overland travel.
