## Forward from Beneath (Story)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#131: The Reaper's Right Hand pg 78

You have vowed to infiltrate your wicked kin and redeem your family's reputation.\
**Prerequisites:** Be a member of House Kastner, be a good-aligned member of a family or
organization whose secret evil has been publicly revealed, or have the Dishonored Family
(circumstance) or Betrayal (chldhood event) [backgrounds](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more).\
**Benefit:** Your experience with infiltrating your own family or organization gives you a +2 bonus
on Disguise checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in Disguise, this bonus increases to +4. If you are
not evil, as a standard action you can choose to radiate a faint aura of evil for the purposes of
spells such as [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil). You can dismiss this ersatz aura as a standard
action.\
**Goal:** Redeem your family or organization.\
**Completion Benefit:** You gain the benefit of
[undetectable alignment](ref/spell/undetectable-alignment) as a constant
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). You can suppress or resume this ability as a
standard action.
