## Orc Rampage (Combat)

You effortlessly savage fearful enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bullying Blow](ref/feat/bullying-blow),
[Intimidating Prowess](ref/feat/intimidating-prowess), [Orc Fury Style](ref/feat/orc-fury-style),
Intimidate 5 ranks, weapon familiarity racial trait.\
**Benefit:** While using Orc Fury Style, you gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls
against [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) opponents, and you don't take an attack penalty when using the
Bullying Blow feat.
