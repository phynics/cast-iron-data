## Asura Sight (Combat)

**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 8

Taking inspiration from the many faces of asuras, you have learned to split your focus to avoid
being caught off guard.\
**Prerequisites:** [Asura Style](ref/feat/asura-style), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.\
**Benefit:** When using Asura Style, you gain
[improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) as a rogue of your character level.
