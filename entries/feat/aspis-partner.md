You have earned the attention of the [Aspis Consortium](ref/rule/aff-profiteers) and can call in
favors when necessary.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are in a settlement that is the size of a small town or larger, you can
purchase (but not sell) items through your Aspis contacts. Treat your current settlement
[as though it were a metropolis](ref/rule/purchasing-magic-items) to determine its base value and
available magic items. You pay a 5% markup for such items, and orders take 3d6 days to be delivered
through Consortium channels. You can pay an additional 1,000 gp to have your item magically
delivered to you in 1d3 days instead.
