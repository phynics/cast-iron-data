Your eidolon is highly observant, and its link with you increases your own watchfulness.\
**Prerequisites:** Eidolon class feature.\
**Benefit:** While your eidolon is within your reach, you gain a +4 bonus on Perception checks. If
you have 10 or more ranks in Perception, this bonus increases to +8. This does not apply if your
eidolon is helpless or unconscious.
