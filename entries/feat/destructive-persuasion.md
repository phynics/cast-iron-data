## Destructive Persuasion (Combat)

Sometimes, you have to break things if you want people to get your point.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), Intimidate 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can attempt to smash an unattended inanimate object while
attempting an [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check. If you break the object, you gain a bonus to
the Intimidate check equal to half its [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) (minimum +1). If you destroy the
object, the bonus is equal to its hardness (minimum +1). Creatures intimidated by this feat cannot
be affected by it again for 24 hours.
