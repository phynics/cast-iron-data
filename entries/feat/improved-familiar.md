This feat allows you to acquire a powerful familiar, but only when you could normally acquire a new
familiar.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to acquire a new [familiar](ref/ability/familiars), compatible alignment,
sufficiently high level.\
**Benefit:** When choosing a familiar, [the creatures listed here](ref/ability/improved-familiars)
are also available to you. You may choose a familiar with an alignment up to one step away on each
alignment axis (lawful through chaotic, good through evil).

Improved familiars otherwise use the rules for regular familiars, with two exceptions: if the
creature's type is something other than animal, its type does not change; and improved familiars do
not gain the ability to speak with other creatures of their kind (although many of them already have
the ability to communicate).
