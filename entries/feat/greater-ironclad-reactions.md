## Greater Ironclad Reactions (Armor Mastery, Combat)

You are able to quickly react to attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Ironclad Reactions](ref/feat/ironclad-reactions), base attack bonus +11 or fighter level 8th, armor
training class feature, proficiency with medium armor.\
**Benefit:** You can use Ironclad Reactions more than once per round, but not more than once for
each act you take that provokes an attack of opportunity. Each 5-foot step you take still counts as
expending one use of an attack of opportunity.
