You were trained from youth to infiltrate and ambush aboveground settlements.\
**Prerequisites:** [Drow](ref/race/drow), [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) drow
racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You can use [fear the sun](ref/spell/fear-the-sun) and
[ignoble form](ref/spell/ignoble-form) each once per day as a spell-like ability. Your caster level
is equal to your character level.
