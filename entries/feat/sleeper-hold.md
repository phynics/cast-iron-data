## Sleeper Hold (Combat)

By maintaining a grapple hold on your opponent, you can cause it to lose consciousness.\
**Prerequisites:** [Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple), base attack bonus +8.\
**Benefit:** You must declare that you are using this feat before you make a combat maneuver check
to maintain a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) (thus a failed check to maintain the grapple ruins the
attempt). If you maintain a grapple for a number of consecutive rounds equal to your opponent's
Constitution bonus (minimum 1 round), you can attempt to knock out your opponent. The victim must
succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Str modifier) or else it falls
[unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious) for 1d4 rounds. Each successive round you attempt this, the
target takes a cumulative -1 penalty on its saving throw. When you use this feat, you take an
additional -2 penalty to your AC. Creatures that are immune to bleed damage, stunning, or critical
hits are immune to this ability.
