## Terrifying Assault (Combat)

Your foes flee when you appear suddenly.\
**Prerequisites:** [Frightening Ambush](ref/feat/frightening-ambush), Intimidate 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When using [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) to demoralize an opponent during a
surprise round, if you exceed the DC by 10 or more, you can make the target
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for 1 round instead of shaken.
