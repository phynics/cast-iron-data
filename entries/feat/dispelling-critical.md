## Dispelling Critical (Critical)

Your blows attack the physical and arcane forms of your enemies at the same time.\
**Prerequisites:** [Arcane Strike](ref/feat/arcane-strike), base attack bonus +11, ability to cast
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic).\
**Benefit:** If you have _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ prepared or can cast it
spontaneously, when you score a critical hit against an opponent, you may use a swift action to cast
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ to make a targeted dispel against that opponent.
