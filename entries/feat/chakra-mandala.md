Using scars, tattoos, or henna, you inscribe symbols over your chakras to help maintain your balance
of kundalini.\
**Prerequisites:** Ki pool class feature.\
**Benefit:** If you fail the Fortitude saving throw to maintain your awakened
[chakras](ref/rule/activating-chakras), you take only half the normal damage.
