## Weapon Evoker Mastery (Item Mastery)

You rack opponents with eldritch power coursing from your magic weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** Use Magic Device 2 ranks, base Fortitude saving throw bonus +3.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action while wielding a magic weapon with a weapon special ability that
deals extra acid, cold, fire, electricity, or sonic damage on a hit, you deal an additional 1d4
points of damage of the same type with each hit you make with that weapon for 1 round.
