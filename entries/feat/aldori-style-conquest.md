## Aldori Style Conquest (Combat)

Defense is now second nature to you, and your blade punishes those who try to attack and fail.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aldori Style](ref/feat/aldori-style),
[Aldori Style Aegis](ref/feat/aldori-style-aegis), [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency)
([Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword)),
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (Aldori dueling
sword).\
**Benefit:** When you attempt to parry an attack using Aldori Style Aegis, you do not take any
penalties on the attack roll incurred from using [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) or
[fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively).
