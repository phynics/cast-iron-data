You have two colors of scales, which mark you as special.\
**Prerequisites:** [Draconic Aspect](ref/feat/draconic-aspect) or dragon-scaled racial trait,
[kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** Choose an additional color for your Draconic Aspect feat or dragon-scaled racial trait.
You gain the benefits of both this color and the color you originally chose. (If you have both
Draconic Aspect and dragon-scaled, this benefit applies to both.) If you gain abilities that rely on
scale color, choose which color gains the benefits.
