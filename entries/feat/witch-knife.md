**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 159

You empower your witch spells by incorporating the use of a special ceremonial knife during your
castings.\
**Prerequisites:** [Witch](ref/class/witch) class.\
**Benefit:** Each day, when you prepare your spells, you can select a masterwork or magical
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger), transforming it into a _witch knife,_ which serves as an additional
focus component for witch patron spells. Add +1 to the DC of all your patron spells.

### Mythic Witch Knife

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 77\
Your _witch knife_ is as much an extension of your body as it is an extension of your power.\
**Prerequisites:** Witch Knife.\
**Benefit:** You can use your _witch knife_ as an additional focus component for all your witch
spells, not just your patron spells, adding 1 to their DC. This bonus stacks with the bonus to
patron spells granted by Witch Knife, granting your patron spells a total +2 bonus to their DC.
Additionally, the hand holding the _witch knife_ is still considered free for the purpose of casting
spells and delivering touch attacks.
