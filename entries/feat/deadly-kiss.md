**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 224

You can change your venom to poison those it touches.\
**Prerequisites:** Toxic racial trait, [vishkanya](ref/race/vishkanya).\
**Benefit:** As a swift action when applying your venom to a weapon or object, you can alter it to
become a contact poison for 1d4 rounds. In its contact form, your venom has an onset time of 1
minute, and remains indistinguishable from your saliva or blood.

In addition, you can use your toxic racial trait one additional time per day.

**Normal:** Vishkanya venom is an injury poison.
