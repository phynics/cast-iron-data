## Break Guard (Combat)

You can use one of your two weapons to occupy your opponent's defenses while attacking with the
other.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Disarm](ref/feat/improved-disarm).\
**Benefit:** While wielding two weapons, whenever you successfully use one weapon to
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) an opponent, you can spend a swift action to attack the opponent you
attempted to disarm using your other weapon.
