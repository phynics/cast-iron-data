## Grasping Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 148

The surrounding foliage lurches forth at your enemies in concert with your strikes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Nature Magic](ref/feat/nature-magic) or the ability to cast druid or ranger
spells; [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use Vital Strike (or [Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike)
or [Greater Vital Strike](ref/feat/greater-vital-strike)), you cause the foliage in the area to
reach out and [entangle](ref/rule/entangled) your foe (as the condition) if it fails a Reflex save
(DC = 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier). The effect lasts for 1 minute or until
the enemy breaks free with a successful Strength check or an Escape Artist check (DC = the effect's
saving throw DC), whichever comes first.

You can use this benefit a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). You
can choose to use this ability as a free action after you hit the enemy with the attack. This is a
supernatural ability.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 120\
When using this feat, you can spend up to 5 stamina points to increase the DC of the effect by an
amount equal to the number of stamina points you spent.
