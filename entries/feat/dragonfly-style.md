## Dragonfly Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 14

You can leap off walls and objects to strike from above.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Acrobatics 2
ranks, Climb 2 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You add your Wisdom modifier to Acrobatics checks in addition to your Dexterity
modifier. While using this style, when you are adjacent to a vertical surface or object capable of
supporting your weight, such as a wall or a tree, you gain a +1 bonus on melee attack rolls against
opponents your size or smaller, as if you were on higher ground.
