## Weapon of the Chosen (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 159

The influence of your deity guides your favored weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with deity's favored weapon, must worship
and receive spells from a deity.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can call upon your deity to guide an attack you make with your
deity's favored weapon. On your next attack in that round with that weapon, your weapon counts as
magical for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) or striking an
incorporeal creature. If your attack misses because of [concealment](ref/rule/concealment), you can
reroll your miss chance one time to see whether you actually hit.
