**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 158

You have learned to enter a deeper state when casting spells, allowing you to shrug off
distractions, damage, weather effects, and even the effects of other spells.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Casting](ref/feat/combat-casting).\
**Benefit:** You do not need to make [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks when affected by
vigorous or violent motion or by violent weather. You gain a +2 bonus on all other concentration
checks.
