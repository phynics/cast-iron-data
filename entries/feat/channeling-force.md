## Channeling Force (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 143

You distill your channeled energy into a sheen of force that surrounds your weapon for a limited
time.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast at least one [force](ref/spelldef/force) spell,
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** A swift action, you can expend one use of channel energy to grant your weapon attacks a
bonus on damage rolls equal to the number of dice of your channel energy. This extra damage is force
damage. This lasts for your next three weapon attacks or until the end of combat, whichever comes
first.
