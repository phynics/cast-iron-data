The animal's devotion to its master allows it to stand against unnatural forces.\
**Prerequisites:** [Animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion), devotion special ability.\
**Benefit:** The animal companion gains a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against spells,
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural abilities of aberrations,
outsiders, and undead. The animal companion is immune to the unnatural aura ability that some undead
creatures (such as [wraiths](ref/monster/wraith)) have.
