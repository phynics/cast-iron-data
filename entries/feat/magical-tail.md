You grow an extra tail that represents your growing magical powers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Kitsune](ref/race/kitsune).\
**Benefit:** You gain a new [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), each usable twice
per day, from the following list, in order: [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self),
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person), [misdirection](ref/spell/misdirection),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion),
[displacement](ref/spell/displacement), [confusion](ref/spell/confusion),
[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person). For example, the first time you select this feat, you
gain _[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)_ 2/day; the second time you select this feat, you
gain _[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)_ 2/day. Your caster level for these spells is equal to
your Hit Dice. The DCs for these abilities are Charisma-based.\
**Special:** You may select this feat up to eight times. Each time you take it, you gain an
additional ability as described above.
