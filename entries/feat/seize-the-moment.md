## Seize the Moment (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 118

You and your allies are poised to pounce whenever one of you scores a telling blow.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical).\
**Benefit:** When an ally who also has this feat confirms a critical hit against an opponent that
you also threaten, you can make an attack of opportunity against that opponent.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 131\
When an ally with this feat threatens a critical hit but fails to confirm it, you can spend 5
stamina points to make your attack of opportunity anyway.
