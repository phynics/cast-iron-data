**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 123\
When you are the target of a grapple combat maneuver to initiate or maintain a grapple, you can
spend a number of stamina points up to your Strength or Dexterity bonus, whichever is greater. Your
CMD against that grapple attempt increases by that number.
