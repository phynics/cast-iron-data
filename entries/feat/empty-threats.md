You have a gift for making authentic-sounding threats you never intend to follow through on.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can use the [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) skill to demoralize opponents in combat
instead of [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate). You can attempt a Bluff check in place of an
Intimidate check when using the [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display) feat and feats that
list Dazzling Display as a prerequisite. If you use the Bluff skill to demoralize, you cannot use it
to feint until the beginning of your next turn, and vice versa.
