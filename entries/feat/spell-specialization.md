Select one spell. You cast that spell with greater than normal power.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus)\
**Benefit:** Select one spell of a school for which you have taken the Spell Focus feat. Treat your
caster level as being two higher for all level-variable effects of the spell.

Every time you gain an even level in the spellcasting class you chose your spell from, you can
choose a new spell to replace the spell selected with this feat, and that spell becomes your
specialized spell.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a different spell.
