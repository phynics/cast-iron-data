Your wild shapes are mighty and muscular.\
**Prerequisites:** Wild shape class feature, druid level 8th.\
**Benefit:** When in wild shape, treat your size as one category larger for the purpose of
calculating CMB, CMD, carrying capacity, and any size-based special attacks you use or that are used
against you (such as [grab](ref/umr/grab), [swallow whole](ref/umr/swallow-whole), and
[trample](ref/umr/trample)).
