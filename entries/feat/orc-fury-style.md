## Orc Fury Style (Combat, Style)

You inspire great fear in your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bullying Blow](ref/feat/bullying-blow),
[Intimidating Prowess](ref/feat/intimidating-prowess), Intimidate 3 ranks, weapon familiarity racial
trait.\
**Benefit:** While wielding a [greataxe](ref/eq-weapon/greataxe), a
[falchion](ref/eq-weapon/falchion), or any melee weapon that has "orc" in its name, you can use the
Bullying Blow feat with the first attack of a full-attack action or as part of a standard action to
make one or more attacks (such as with the [Cleave](ref/feat/cleave) feat).
