**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 121

You have exceptional manual dexterity.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on Disable Device and Sleight of Hand skill checks. If you have 10
or more ranks in one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.

### Mythic Deft Hands

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 63\
Your hands are astonishingly nimble.\
**Prerequisites:** Deft Hands.\
**Benefit:** The bonus on Disable Device and Sleight of Hand skill checks from Deft Hands increases
by 2. In addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Disable Device or Sleight of
Hand check as if you had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the
roll.
