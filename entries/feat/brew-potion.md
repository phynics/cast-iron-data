## Brew Potion (Item Creation)

You can create magic potions.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** You can create a potion of any 3rd-level or lower spell that you know and that targets
one or more creatures or objects. Brewing a potion takes 2 hours if its base price is 250 gp or
less, otherwise brewing a potion takes 1 day for each 1,000 gp in its base price. When you create a
potion, you set the caster level, which must be sufficient to cast the spell in question and no
higher than your own level. To brew a potion, you must use up raw materials costing one half this
base price. See the [magic item creation rules](ref/rule/magic-item-creation) for more information.

When you create a potion, you make any choices that you would normally make when casting the spell.
Whoever drinks the potion is the target of the spell.
