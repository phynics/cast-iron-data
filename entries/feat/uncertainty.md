## Uncertainty (Blood Hex)

When you draw a foe's blood in combat, that creature begins to doubt its skills.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13; Bluff, Diplomacy, and Intimidate 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, you can diminish the confidence of a creature you've dealt
damage to within the last minute. That creature must succeed at a Will save or take a -6 penalty on
skill checks for one of the following skills of your choice: Bluff, Diplomacy, or Intimidate. This
ability lasts until the subject is healed of all hit point damage, up to a maximum of 1 hour.\
**Special:** A shaman or witch with this [blood hex](ref/rule/feats-class) can use it as a standard
action. Additionally, if a shaman or witch uses this blood hex, the target takes the penalty on
Bluff, Diplomacy, and Intimidate checks.
