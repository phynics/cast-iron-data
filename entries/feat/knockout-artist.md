**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 106

You can throw devastating knockout punches.\
**Prerequisites:** [Sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) class feature,
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** When you use your unarmed strike to deal nonlethal damage and sneak attack damage to an
opponent denied his Dexterity bonus to AC, you gain a +1 bonus on the damage roll per each sneak
attack damage die you roll.

### Mythic Knockout Artist

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 68\
Your knockout punches are truly mighty.\
**Prerequisites:** Knockout Artist.\
**Benefit:** When you use your unarmed strike to deal nonlethal damage and sneak attack damage to an
opponent, roll d8s for each sneak attack die instead of d6s.
