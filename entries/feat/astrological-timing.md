**Sources:** People of the Stars pg 27

You can perform more accurate auguries according to planetary movements in relation to the Cosmic
Caravan.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast [augury](ref/spell/augury) as a spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** You can use a cosmogram or star chart as an optional focus component for
_[augury](ref/spell/augury)._ When you do, the _[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ can account for
consequences up to 1 hour into the future, and your chance of a successful reading increases by 4%.
If you also consult a calculating standard orrery or calculating grand orrery, the
_[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ can see up to 1 day into the future and your chance of success
increases by 8%.
