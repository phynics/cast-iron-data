**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 163

Your sense for stonework is uncanny.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), stonecunning racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +4 bonus on Perception checks to notice unusual stonework. This bonus
replaces the stonecunning ability's normal bonus on Perception checks.

### Mythic Improved Stonecunning

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 67\
If you listen close, you can hear the secrets of stone.\
**Prerequisites:** Improved Stonecunning.\
**Benefit:** You gain [stone tell](ref/spell/stone-tell) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) usable once per day with a caster level equal to
twice your tier.
