Teleportation does not faze you.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to use the abundant step class feature or cast
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door).\
**Benefit:** After using abundant step or casting dimension door, you can take any actions you still
have remaining on your turn. You also gain a +4 bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration)
checks when casting teleportation spells.
