## Heavy Armor Proficiency (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 118

You are skilled at wearing heavy armor.\
**Prerequisites:** [Light Armor Proficiency](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-light),
[Medium Armor Proficiency](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-medium).\
**Benefit:** See Light Armor Proficiency.\
**Normal:** See Light Armor Proficiency.\
**Special:** Antipaladins, aristocrats, cavaliers, fighters, paladins, samurai, warpriests, and
warriors automatically have Heavy Armor Proficiency as a bonus feat. They need not select it.
