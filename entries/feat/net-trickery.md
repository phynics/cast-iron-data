## Net Trickery (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 111

You have become very proficient at using your net to hinder your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (net),
[Net Adept](ref/feat/net-adept), [Net Maneuvering](ref/feat/net-maneuvering), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** In place of one of your melee attacks, you can use your [net](ref/eq-weapon/net) to
attempt a [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver to [blind](ref/misc/blinded) an
opponent. If you have an opponent entangled in your net, you can attempt to [trip](ref/rule/trip)
that opponent as long as he is within your net's reach or you control the trailing rope on your net.
You also gain a +2 bonus on [drag](ref/rule/drag) and [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat
maneuver checks you make using your net.
