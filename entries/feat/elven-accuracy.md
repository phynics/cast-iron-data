## Elven Accuracy (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 159

Your sharp eyesight makes difficult shots easier.\
**Prerequisites:** [Elf](ref/race/elf).\
**Benefit:** If you miss due to [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) when making a ranged attack with
a [longbow](ref/eq-weapon/longbow) or [shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow) (including composite bows),
you can reroll your miss chance roll one time to see if you actually hit.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 119\
If you miss an attack with a longbow or shortbow due to concealment, you can spend 5 stamina points
to automatically succeed at the miss chance reroll from this feat.

### Mythic Elven Accuracy

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 64\
Your elven eyesight cuts though concealment.\
**Prerequisites:** Elven Accuracy.\
**Benefit:** When you make an attack with a longbow or short bow (including composite bows), you can
ignore concealment, but not total concealment. You still suffer a miss chance for total concealment,
but if you miss due to total concealment, you may reroll your miss chance as per Elven Accuracy.
