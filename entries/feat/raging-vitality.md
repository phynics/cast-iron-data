**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 168

While raging, you are full of vigor and health.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15, rage class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you are raging, the morale bonus to your Constitution increases by +2. Your
rage does not end if you become [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious). While unconscious you must
still expend rounds of rage per day each round.
