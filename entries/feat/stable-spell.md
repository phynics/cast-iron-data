## Stable Spell (Metamagic)

You can cast spells in areas of [primal magic](ref/rule/primal-magic) with less chance of triggering
a primal magic event.\
**Benefit:** In areas of primal magic, the chance of a stable spell triggering a primal magic event
is reduced by 25%. In cases where a primal magic event would trigger despite the reduced chances,
the caster of a stable spell receives a +2 bonus on the [concentration](ref/rule/concentration)
check to avoid the primal magic event. A stable spell uses up a spell slot 1 level higher than the
spell's actual level.
