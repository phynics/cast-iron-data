**Sources:** Demons Revisited pg 38

You gain bonuses on damage when you wield identical one-handed weapons.\
**Prerequisites:** Three or more arms, Dex 21\
**Benefit:** When you wield one-handed or light weapons in each of your arms, and all of those
weapons belong to the same [weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups) (as defined for the fighter
class), all attacks with those weapons gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls.
