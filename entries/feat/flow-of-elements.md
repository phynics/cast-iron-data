## Flow of Elements (Teamwork)

You can spontaneously channel your ally's elemental essence, be it burning fire, freezing ice,
crackling lightning, or searing acid.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast spells; [ifrit](ref/race/ifrit), [oread](ref/race/oread),
[sylph](ref/race/sylph), or [undine](ref/race/undine).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you're adjacent to an ifrit, oread, sylph, or undine ally who also has this
feat, you can spontaneously replace or split a spell's damage when casting it, as though the spell
were affected by [Elemental Spell](ref/feat/elemental-spell) (without using a higher-level spell
slot). The type of energy damage depends on your ally's race: acid for oread, electricity for sylph,
fire for ifrit, or cold for undine.
