You can refresh your [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) by expending spell energy.\
**Prerequisites:** Arcane spellcaster, caster level 4th, [serpentfolk](ref/monster/serpentfolk).\
**Benefit:** You can expend two arcane spell slots or prepared spells to cast one of your racial
spell-like abilities. The level of each expended spell must be equal to or higher than the
sorcerer/wizard spell level of the spell-like ability you want to cast.
