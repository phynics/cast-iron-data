## Goblin Gunslinger (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 118

You have learned how to fire the big guns.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin).\
**Benefit:** You can wield Medium firearms without taking the penalty for an
[inappropriately sized weapon](ref/misc/weapon-size).\
**Normal:** You take a -2 penalty when using an inappropriately sized weapon.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 120\
When wielding a firearm that deals fire damage, when you threaten but fail to confirm a critical
hit, you can spend 5 stamina points to regain 1 grit point. You still can't gain a grit point if the
target is immune to critical hits.
