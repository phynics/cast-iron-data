Your songs draw upon the savage chants and brutal ways of your people, inspiring those around you to
new heights of violence and savagery.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, bardic performance class feature, [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or
[orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** When you use bardic performance with audible components on a battlefield (any area
where a combat involving a dozen or more combatants is taking place), the range or area of your
chosen performance is doubled. In addition, the DC for saving throws against your bardic performance
is increased by +2 for creatures of the orc subtype, regardless of where the performance occurs.
