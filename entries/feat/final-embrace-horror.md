## Final Embrace Horror (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 101

Your constricting attack has become stronger and more lethal.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, Int 3; naga, [serpentfolk](ref/monster/serpentfolk), or creature that has
the [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) special attack; [Final Embrace](ref/feat/final-embrace); base
attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** A creature that takes damage from your constrict attack is also
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) until the start of your next turn.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 119\
When a creature is damaged by your constrict ability, you can spend 5 stamina points to sicken that
creature until the start of your next turn.
