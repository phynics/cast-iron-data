## Esoteric Abjuration (Esoteric)

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 154

A carefully selected set of geodes causes the defenses you create with your spells to offer even
better protection.\
**Benefit:** When you cast an abjuration spell using geodes as an
[esoteric material component](ref/rule/esoteric-material-components), you can grant a single target
of that spell a +1 competence bonus on its Fortitude, Reflex, or Will saving throws for the duration
of the spell. The spell must have a duration measured in rounds or minutes, and a creature can
benefit from no more than one bonus from this feat at a time.

When you gain geodes' greater component effect with an abjuration spell, treat your caster level as
an additional 1 higher for determining the duration.
