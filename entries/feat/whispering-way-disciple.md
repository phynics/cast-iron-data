**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 13

Your research has gained the attention of the [Whispering Way](ref/rule/f-gravepath), which rewards
you with access to its libraries.\
**Prerequisites:** [Associate](ref/feat/associate) (Whispering Way).\
**Benefit:** You can exploit lore acquired from the Whispering Way once per day to combine your own
blood and a vial of [unholy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water) to create a potion of
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds) or a potion of
[inflict light wounds](ref/spell/inflict-light-wounds) as a full-round action. The blood loss from
this transformation deals 1 point of Constitution damage to you. In addition, in any
[settlement](ref/rule/the-settlement-stat-block) that is the size of a small town or larger, you can
always find scrolls of the following spells available for purchase regardless of their legality:
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead), [control undead](ref/spell/control-undead),
[create undead](ref/spell/create-undead), [detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead),
[disrupt undead](ref/spell/disrupt-undead), [false life](ref/spell/false-life),
[greater false life](ref/spell/false-life-greater), [ghoul touch](ref/spell/ghoul-touch),
[lesser animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead-lesser),
[mass repair undead](ref/spell/repair-undead-mass), [repair undead](ref/spell/repair-undead), and
[undead anatomy I-IV](ref/spell/undead-anatomy-1).
