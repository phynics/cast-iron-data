You can infuse your elemental spells with primal magic.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, when you cast a spell that deals acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage,
you can infuse the spell with [primal magic](ref/rule/primal-magic) and transform its elemental
energy at random. After you cast the spell but before its effects are resolved, roll 1d4 to
determine the new element (1 = acid, 2 = cold, 3 = electricity, 4 = fire). If you are in an area
where primal magic is predominant, you can instead use this ability once per hour.
