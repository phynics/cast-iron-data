## Diplomatic Ruse (Teamwork)

With practiced gestures, you buy enough time to prepare yourself for an inevitable battle.\
**Prerequisites:** Diplomacy 3 ranks, [human](ref/race/human) (Keleshite).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you're adjacent to an ally who also has this feat, you can perform a quick,
seemingly courteous gesture when you make an initiative check. As part of the gesture, you can
perform a single free action, such as dropping an item or entering rage. This feat can be used only
when combat breaks out after a conversation, such as from failed negotiations.
