**Sources:** Blood of Fiends pg 25

Your fiendish visage has made you a truly terrifying spectacle to behold, and you can force others
to betray their feelings with a single look.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [Monstrous Mask](ref/feat/monstrous-mask), [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** Anytime you can make a [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) check to get a hunch or
detect whether someone is trustworthy or not, you can choose to instead make an
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check at a -2 penalty. This ability only works on creatures of
the humanoid type.
