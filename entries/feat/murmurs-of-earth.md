The earth opens up her secrets to your perceptions.\
**Prerequisites:** [Echoes of Stone](ref/feat/echoes-of-stone), character level 9th,
[oread](ref/race/oread).\
**Benefit:** You gain a limited form of [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense). As a move action, you
become aware of all creatures within 15 feet that are in contact with the ground at that moment.
