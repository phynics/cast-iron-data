## Falter (Blood Hex)

**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 29

You curse the blood of your foes, forcing them to falter in their subsequent actions.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, Spellcraft 5 ranks, Use Magic Device 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can curse an enemy you've dealt damage to since your last
turn. That target must succeed at a Will save or be unable to take a 5-foot step,
[delay](ref/rule/delay) an action, or [ready an action](ref/rule/ready) for 1 round.\
**Special:** A shaman or witch with this [blood hex](ref/rule/feats-class) can use it against any
creature she has dealt damage to in the past minute. A creature affected by this blood hex used by a
shaman or witch also has its movement rate halved for the blood hex's duration.
