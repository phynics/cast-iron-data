You have mastered the lesser [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of the drow,
demonstrating true nobility.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [Drow Nobility](ref/feat/drow-nobility),
[Improved Drow Nobility](ref/feat/improved-drow-nobility), able to use drow spell-like abilities,
[drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** Your [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) spell-like ability is now constant. You may
use your [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights), [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness),
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire), [feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), and
[levitate](ref/spell/levitate) spell-like abilities at will.
