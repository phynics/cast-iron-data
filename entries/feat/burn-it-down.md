## Burn It Down! (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 201

Burning torches and the discordant symphony of riotous singing ignite the fervor within you.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, [goblin](ref/race/goblin).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you make an attack with a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch), a flask of lamp
[oil](ref/eq-misc/oil) fitted with a fuse, [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), or a
[flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon, you gain a morale bonus on your damage roll equal to the
number of goblin allies within 30 feet who also have this feat (maximum +4). This bonus damage is
fire damage.
