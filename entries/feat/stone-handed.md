## Stone-Handed (Combat)

**Sources:** Elemental Master's Handbook pg 10

Drawing upon techniques to combat animated creatures, you bring a strike like an avalanche of stone.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist).\
**Benefit:** Before making a melee attack with an unarmed strike, you can expend one use of Stunning
Fist to declare a stone-handed strike. Resolve the attack normally, except your attack ignores an
amount of the target's [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) equal to your monk level or one-quarter your
base attack bonus (minimum 1), whichever is higher. This effect replaces other effects or conditions
that would be caused by your Stunning Fist.
