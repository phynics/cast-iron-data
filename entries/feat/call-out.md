## Call Out (Combat)

A well-crafted insult forces an opponent to duel you.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can make an Intimidate check against a hostile target within
30 feet that can clearly see and hear you. The DC of this check is equal to 10 + your opponent's Hit
Dice + its Wisdom modifier. If the target is trained in Sense Motive, the DC is instead equal to
10 + your opponent's Sense Motive bonus, if higher. If you succeed at this check, the target enters
a [duel](ref/rule/duels) with you. The target cannot withdraw from the duel for 1 round + 1 round
for every 5 by which the check beat the DC.
