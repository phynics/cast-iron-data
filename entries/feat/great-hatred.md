## Great Hatred (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 37

Your rage burns with vast intensity.\
**Prerequisites:** [Gnome](ref/race/gnome), hatred racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You gain an additional +1 bonus on melee and thrown weapon attacks against targets of
your hatred racial trait.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 120\
When you hit an opponent of a subtype subject to your hatred racial trait, you can spend up to 5
stamina points to deal an amount of extra damage equal to double the number of stamina points you
spent.
