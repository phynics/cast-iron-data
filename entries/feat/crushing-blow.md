## Crushing Blow (Combat)

Your focus allows you to smash your enemy's defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist).\
**Benefit:** You can make a Stunning Fist attempt as a full-round action. If successful, instead of
stunning your target, you reduce the target's AC by an amount equal to your Wisdom modifier for 1
minute. This penalty does not stack with other penalties applied due to Crushing Blow.
