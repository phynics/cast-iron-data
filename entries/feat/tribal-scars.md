You endured the grueling coming-of-age rituals of your tribe or following, and proudly bear the
scars that grant you the blessings of your tribe's ancestors or totem.\
**Benefit:** You gain one of the following benefits, depending on which Mammoth Lord following you
belong to. (At the GM's discretion, other options of her own design could exist for different
tribes.)

**Bearpelt:** You gain a +1 bonus on Fortitude saves and a +2 bonus on Intimidate checks.

**Greattusk:** You gain a +2 bonus on checks to perform [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuvers and a +2 bonus on Ride checks.

**Ice Chasm:** You gain a +1 bonus on Reflex saves and a +2 bonus on Climb checks.

**Night Hunt:** You gain a +2 bonus on Perception and Survival checks.

**Raptorscale:** Your base land speed increases by 5 feet, and you gain a +2 bonus on Acrobatics
checks.

**Slothjaw:** You gain a +1 bonus on Will saves and a +2 bonus on Handle Animal checks.
