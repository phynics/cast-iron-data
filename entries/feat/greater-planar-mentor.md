**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 26

Your mentor begins to look upon you as an equal and can even answer your call.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Planar Mentor](ref/feat/improved-planar-mentor),
[Planar Mentor](ref/feat/planar-mentor), character level 13th.\
**Benefit:** You can benefit from your Planar Mentor feat one additional time per day. You add the
following to the list of [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) you can cast using
Improved Planar Mentor for their respective alignments, each of which affects only a single target
within 30 feet: [blasphemy](ref/spell/blasphemy) (evil), [dictum](ref/spell/dictum) (lawful),
[holy word](ref/spell/holy-word) (good), [slay living](ref/spell/slay-living) (neutral), and
[word of chaos](ref/spell/word-of-chaos) (chaotic).

Once per week you can cast [greater planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally-greater) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), but only to conjure your planar mentor. Your
mentor requires only half the normal payment for its services.
