## Kobold Sniper (Combat)

You snipe quickly, making it harder for others to find the location of your attack.\
**Prerequisites:** Stealth 1 rank, [kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** When you are [sniping](ref/skill/stealth), you only take a -10 penalty on your Stealth
checks to stay hidden.\
**Normal:** You take a -20 penalty on your Stealth check to maintain your obscured location while
sniping.
