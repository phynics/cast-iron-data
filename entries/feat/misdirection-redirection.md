## Misdirection Redirection (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 87

After misdirecting your opponent's weapon attack, you trick it into striking someone else.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Misdirection Tactics](ref/feat/misdirection-tactics), [Deceitful](ref/feat/deceitful), Bluff 10
ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you successfully use the Misdirection Tactics feat to negate a melee weapon
attack, you redirect your foe's attack and trick your foe into striking another creature of your
choice within the foe's melee reach. To resolve this attack, your foe must make a new attack roll
against the new target.
