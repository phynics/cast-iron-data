## Tandem Trip (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 122

You know how to work together to trip your foes.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver against an enemy
threatened by an ally with this feat, you roll twice and take the better result.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 133\
When attempting to trip a target that is threatened by one of your allies, you can spend 2 stamina
points to gain this feat's benefit even if the threatening ally doesn't have this feat.
