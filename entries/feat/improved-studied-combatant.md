**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 150

Deep understanding of your foes' tactics grants you the upper hand in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Amateur Investigator](ref/feat/amateur-investigator),
[Studied Combatant](ref/feat/studied-combatant), base attack bonus +8, 1 rank in at least one
Knowledge skill, no levels in a class that has the inspiration class feature.\
**Benefit:** Your bonuses for Studied Combatant increase to a +4 insight bonus on melee attack rolls
and a +4 bonus on damage rolls. This feat otherwise works like the Studied Combatant feat.
