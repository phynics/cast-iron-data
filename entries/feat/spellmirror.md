Spells that are cast at you are sometimes deflected, courtesy of your natural resistance.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, character level 6th, [ghoran](ref/race/ghoran), spelleater racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can use the [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) granted by your spelleater racial trait as an
immediate action to attempt to [counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells). If your counterspell attempt
is successful, the spell rebounds back upon the original caster, taking effect as if the spell had
been cast upon that creature. This exhausts the daily use of
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ granted by the spelleater racial trait.
