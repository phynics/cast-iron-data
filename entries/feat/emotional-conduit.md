The deep emotional resonance you share with your phantom enables you to master the emotions of
others.\
**Prerequisites:** Shared consciousness class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain familiarity with a number of additional spells based on the
[emotional focus](ref/ability/emotional-focus) of your [phantom](ref/sidekick/phantom). These spells
are added to both your class spell list (if not already on that list) and your list of spells known;
they are in addition to the normal number of spells known for your level. The spells you gain are as
follows:

**Anger:** 1st-[burst of adrenaline](ref/spell/burst-of-adrenaline), 2nd-[rage](ref/spell/rage),
3rd-[howling agony](ref/spell/howling-agony),
4th-[telekinetic charge](ref/spell/telekinetic-charge),
5th-[vengeful outrage](ref/spell/vengeful-outrage), 6th-[transformation](ref/spell/transformation).

**Despair:** 1st-[touch of gracelessness](ref/spell/touch-of-gracelessness),
2nd-[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), 3rd-[ray of exhaustion](ref/spell/ray-of-exhaustion),
4th-[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair), 5th-[suffocation](ref/spell/suffocation),
6th-[eyebite](ref/spell/eyebite).

**Devotion:** 1st-[invisibility alarm](ref/spell/invisibility-alarm),
2nd-[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon),
3rd-[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds),
4th-[spell immunity](ref/spell/spell-immunity),
5th-[vampiric shadow shield](ref/spell/vampiric-shadow-shield),
6th-[greater heroism](ref/spell/heroism-greater).

**Fear:** 1st-[heightened awareness](ref/spell/heightened-awareness), 2nd-[scare](ref/spell/scare),
3rd-[fear](ref/spell/fear), 4th-[feast on fear](ref/spell/feast-on-fear),
5th-[nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare), 6th-[frightful aspect](ref/spell/frightful-aspect).

**Hatred:** 1st-[murderous command](ref/spell/murderous-command),
2nd-[disfiguring touch](ref/spell/disfiguring-touch), 3rd-[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse),
4th-[debilitating portent](ref/spell/debilitating-portent),
5th-[foster hatred](ref/spell/foster-hatred),
6th-[phantasmal revenge](ref/spell/phantasmal-revenge).

**Jealousy:** 1st-[compel hostility](ref/spell/compel-hostility),
2nd-[life pact](ref/spell/life-pact), 3rd-[ectoplasmic snare](ref/spell/ectoplasmic-snare),
4th-[entrap spirit](ref/spell/entrap-spirit), 5th-[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person),
6th-[unwilling shield](ref/spell/unwilling-shield).

**Kindess:** 1st-[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person),
2nd-[blessing of courage and life](ref/spell/blessing-of-courage-and-life),
3rd-[good hope](ref/spell/good-hope), 4th-[mass charm person](ref/spell/charm-person-mass),
5th-[waves of ecstasy](ref/spell/waves-of-ecstasy), 6th-[sympathy](ref/spell/sympathy). — Psychic
Anthology pg. 4

**Remorse:** 1st-[inflict light wounds](ref/spell/inflict-light-wounds),
2nd-_[disfiguring touch](ref/spell/disfiguring-touch),_
3rd-_[howling agony](ref/spell/howling-agony),_
4th-_[debilitating portent](ref/spell/debilitating-portent),_
5th-_[nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare),_ 6th-[harm](ref/spell/harm). — Occult Origins pg. 19

**Whimsey:** 1st-[color spray](ref/spell/color-spray),
2nd-[hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter), 3rd-[major image](ref/spell/major-image),
