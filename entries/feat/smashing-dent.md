## Smashing Dent (Combat)

Your hammer strikes cause traumatic injuries.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder),
[Smashing Crush](ref/feat/smashing-crush), [Smashing Style](ref/feat/smashing-style),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon, base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** While using Smashing Style, each time you damage a suit of armor with a
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver, you reduce its armor bonus to its Armor Class by 1 and
increase its armor check penalty by 1. Armor cannot have its armor bonus to AC reduced to less than
50% of its normal bonus or its armor check penalty more than doubled. These reductions and increases
both end when the damage dealt by the sunder is repaired.
