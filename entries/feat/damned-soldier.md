Your favored boons reflect your martial predilections.\
**Prerequisites:** [Fiendish Obedience](ref/feat/fiendish-obedience).\
**Benefit:** When you gain boons through the Fiendish Obedience feat, you can choose a sentinel boon
or an exalted boon, but once you select this boon, it can't be changed. You gain a +1 profane bonus
on weapon damage rolls with your god's favored weapon.
