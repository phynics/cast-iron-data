**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 173

Your fiendish traits take the form of a protective scaly skin.\
**Prerequisites:** [Tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 natural armor bonus.\
**Special:** If you have the scaled skin racial trait, you instead gain resistance 5 to two of the
following energy types that you don't have resistance to already: cold, electricity, and fire.
