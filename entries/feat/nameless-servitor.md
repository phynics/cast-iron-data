## Nameless Servitor (Faction)

The fact that you have given up your real name makes you resistant to certain types of magic.\
**Prerequisites:** [Risen Guard](ref/rule/f-second-life) rank, Risen Guard 10 TPA.\
**Benefit:** Anyone who attempts to use magic in order to learn personal information about you, such
as through [divination](ref/spell/divination), [scrying](ref/spell/scrying), or similar magic, must
make a caster level check with a DC equal to 10 plus your level to succeed. You are immune to spells
that require the use of your name in order to function (such as
[trap the soul](ref/spell/trap-the-soul)).

Most members of the Risen Guard with this feat are addressed by their rank.

**Special:** If you ever choose a real name, you lose all benefits of this feat.
