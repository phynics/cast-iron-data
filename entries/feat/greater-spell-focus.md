Choose a school of magic to which you have already applied the [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus)
feat. Any spells you cast of this school are very hard to resist.\
**Prerequisites:** Spell Focus.\
**Benefit:** Add +1 to the Difficulty Class for all saving throws against spells from the school of
magic you select. This bonus stacks with the bonus from Spell Focus.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new school to which you already have applied the Spell Focus feat.
