## Blinding Sneak Attack (Combat)

When you strike from the cover of darkness, you inject foes with a fraction of your foul magic.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +5, [darkness](ref/spell/darkness)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) class
feature, [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** When you successfully deal sneak attack damage to a foe while you are within an area of
magical darkness, you temporarily [blind](ref/misc/blinded) your opponent for 1 round. A successful
Fortitude save (DC 10 + the amount of sneak attack damage dealt) negates this effect.
