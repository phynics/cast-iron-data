## Perturbing Stare (Combat, Stare)

Your stare dulls your foe's reactions.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mesmerist](ref/class/mesmerist) level 5th, painful stare class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you trigger your painful stare, the target must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 +
half your mesmerist level + your Charisma modifier) or lose its Dexterity modifier to AC for 1
round.
