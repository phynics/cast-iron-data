## Improved Balor Whip (Combat)

**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 80

You can pull in foes with your whip and attack them with your sword.\
**Prerequisites:** [Balor Whip](ref/feat/balor-whip), [Whip Mastery](ref/feat/whip-mastery), base
attack bonus +12.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a [drag](ref/rule/drag) attempt with your
[whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) against a non-adjacent target and the target ends the drag adjacent to
you, as a free action you can make a single attack against the target at your highest attack bonus
using the melee weapon held in your other hand. You gain an additional +2 bonus on drag combat
maneuver checks when using a whip to drag, but this bonus does not stack with the bonus from
[Greater Drag](ref/feat/greater-drag).
