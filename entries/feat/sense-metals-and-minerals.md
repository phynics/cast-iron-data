You have a sixth sense for the valuable treasures of the earth\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on Perception checks to notice natural deposits of precious metals
or minerals, and a +4 bonus on Appraise checks to determine their worth. By spending 5 minutes
scrutinizing a discovered vein of metals or minerals, you can determine the worth of the full vein
to within 1,000 gp, to a maximum of 50,000 gp.
