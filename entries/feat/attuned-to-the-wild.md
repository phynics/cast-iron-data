You share a strong mystic connection with one type of wilderness terrain.\
**Prerequisites:** [Elf](ref/race/elf).\
**Benefit:** Select one type of terrain from the ranger class's
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) class feature (except urban). While you are in your
selected terrain type, your natural healing rate (the amount of hit points and ability damage you
heal from a full night's rest) doubles.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take it,
it applies to a different terrain type.
