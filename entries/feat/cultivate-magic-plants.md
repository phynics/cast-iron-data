## Cultivate Magic Plants (Item Creation)

You combine a natural green thumb and knowledge of magic in order to grow magic plants.\
**Prerequisites:** [Brew Potion](ref/feat/brew-potion),
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), Knowledge (nature) 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You can cultivate magic plants. Cultivating a magic plant takes 1 week per 1,000 gp in
its base price. When you create a magic plant, you make the same choices that you would normally
make when casting the spell. Whoever consumes the fruit of the magic plant is the target of the
spell.
