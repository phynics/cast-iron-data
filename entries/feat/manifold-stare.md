## Manifold Stare (Combat, Stare)

Your hypnotic gaze causes tremendous pain in its victims.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mesmerist](ref/class/mesmerist) level 3rd, painful stare class feature.\
**Benefit:** You can trigger your painful stare one additional time per round.\
**Special:** This feat can be taken once by a 3rd-level mesmerist, and again when he reaches 9th and
15th levels. Its effects stack. A mesmerist can take this feat in place of a bold stare improvement.
