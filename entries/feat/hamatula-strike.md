## Hamatula Strike (Combat)

You can catch your opponents on your weapon and hold them in place.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple), Strength 13, Base Attack Bonus +7.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you damage an opponent with a piercing weapon, you can immediately make a
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) check; success means the opponent is impaled on your weapon and you both
gain the grappled condition. While the opponent is impaled, as an attack action you may make a
grapple check on your turn at a -4 penalty to damage the opponent with your weapon, even if your
weapon cannot normally be used in a grapple.\
**Normal:** You can only attack with an unarmed strike, natural weapon, or light weapon against
opponents you are grappling.
