You use Thassilonian magic in a non-traditional way that preserves the ancient traditions while
lifting some of its restrictions.\
**Prerequisites:** [Thassilonian Magic](ref/arcaneschool/thassilonian-magic) specialization.\
**Benefit:** You choose one of your two opposition schools. You also gain Thassilonian as a bonus
language. If you already speak Thassilonian, you gain one of the following languages as a bonus
language instead: Aklo, Azlanti, Giant, Shoanti, or Varisian.\
**Normal:** A Thassilonian specialist cannot customize his choice for opposition schools.
