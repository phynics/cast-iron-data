The ground parts for you at the slightest touch, allowing you to dig with great speed.\
**Prerequisites:** [Stony Step](ref/feat/stony-step), character level 9th, [oread](ref/race/oread).\
**Benefit:** You gain a burrow speed equal to 1/2 your base speed. You can burrow through sand,
dirt, clay, gravel, or similar materials, but not solid stone. You do not leave a hole behind, nor
is your passage marked on the surface.
