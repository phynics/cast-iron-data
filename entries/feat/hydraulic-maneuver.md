You can use your hydraulic push to disarm or trip.\
**Prerequisites:** Hydraulic push [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities),
[undine](ref/race/undine).\
**Benefit:** You may use _[hydraulic push](ref/spell/hydraulic-push)_ to attempt a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [disarm](ref/rule/disarm), [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick), or
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver. Each time you use hydraulic push, you must decide which of
the allowed combat maneuvers you want to perform. You may use this feat with your hydraulic push
racial spell-like ability, your class-granted use of hydraulic push, or any hydraulic push spells
you cast, but not with magic items or other external sources that use that spell.\
**Normal:** Hydraulic push can only be used to make a bull rush combat maneuver.
