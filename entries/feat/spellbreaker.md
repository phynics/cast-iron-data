## Spellbreaker (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 134

You can strike at enemy spellcasters who fail to cast defensively when you threaten them.\
**Prerequisites:** [Disruptive](ref/feat/disruptive), 10th-level fighter.\
**Benefit:** Enemies in your threatened area that fail their checks to cast spells defensively
provoke attacks of opportunity from you.\
**Normal:** Enemies that fail to cast spells defensively do not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 132\
When you hit a caster with an attack of opportunity it provoked by failing to
[cast defensively](ref/rule/concentration), you can spend 5 stamina points to [daze](ref/misc/dazed)
the caster until the end of its next turn. If the caster succeeds at a Fortitude save with a DC
equal to 10 + your base attack bonus, it is [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) instead.
