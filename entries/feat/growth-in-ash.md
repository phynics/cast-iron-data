You're aware that when fire passes, growth can begin.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a Reflex save to avoid
[catching on fire](ref/rule/catching-on-fire), or when you cease taking ongoing fire damage from a
spell or effect, you can smear ash on yourself as an immediate action to gain
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1 for 3 rounds. For every Hit Die you have beyond 3, extend the
duration of this fast healing by an additional round.
