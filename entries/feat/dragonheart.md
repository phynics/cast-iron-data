Your familiarity with dragonkind makes you less susceptible to their abilities.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (arcana) 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on all saving throws against auras, breath weapons,
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), spells, supernatural abilities, and other
special attacks of creatures with the dragon type.
