Your weapons spread your sickness.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cause disease with a natural weapon via an extraordinary or
supernatural ability.\
**Benefit:** Creatures damaged by your ranged and melee weapon attacks have a chance to contract
disease, as though they were damaged with your disease-spreading natural weapon.
