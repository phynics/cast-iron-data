## Shatter Mental Mask (Combat)

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 139

You are adept at ripping away an opponent's mental mask, exposing its true visage during a psychic
duel.\
**Prerequisites:** [Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) or ability to cast psychic
spells or use psychic [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), character level 2nd.\
**Benefit:** While in a [psychic duel](ref/rule/psychic-duels), when you create an offensive
manifestation that can be negated or halved only with a successful Will saving throw, if your
opponent fails that Will saving throw, its mental mask is shattered, revealing the opponent's true
form.\
**Special:** If you possess the [Third Eye](ref/feat/third-eye) feat, your opponent's mental mask is
shattered even on a successful Will saving throw.
