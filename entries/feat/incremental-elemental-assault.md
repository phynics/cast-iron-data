**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 203

You may activate and quench your elemental assault ability multiple times per day.\
**Prerequisites:** [Suli](ref/race/suli).\
**Benefit:** You may use your elemental assault ability in 1-round increments, up to a maximum
number of rounds per day equal to your character level. These rounds do not have to be consecutive.
Activating the ability is a swift action; ending it is a free action.\
**Normal:** You can use elemental assault once per day. It lasts a number of rounds equal to your
class level.
