## Squirming Pile (Combat, Teamwork)

Your body is easily lost in the muddy chaos of fighting as a swarm, making your vital areas
difficult to target.\
**Prerequisites:** Acrobatics 5 ranks, swarming racial trait.\
**Benefit:** While sharing a square with an ally who has this feat, you gain a 25% chance of
negating the additional damage from a successful critical hit or
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack); if you negate the damage, it is instead rolled normally.
This negation chance doesn't stack with similar protection provided by any other source, such as the
[fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification) armor special ability.
