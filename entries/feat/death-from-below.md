## Death from Below (Combat)

You have been trained to fight flying creatures.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus against flying opponents when you have set a weapon
against a charge, or when you are allowed an attack of opportunity against the flying opponent.
