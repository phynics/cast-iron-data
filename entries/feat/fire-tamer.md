You know your way around even magical fire.\
**Prerequisites:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on saves against spells with the fire descriptor. Additionally,
your scars mark you as a talented fire tamer, granting you a +2 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy and
Intimidate checks when dealing with other goblins.
