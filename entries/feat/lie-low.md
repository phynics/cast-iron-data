**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 10

You're even harder to see when prone than most are.\
**Benefit:** If you have been [prone](ref/rule/prone) since the end of your last turn, the penalty
on Perception checks for others to notice you increases by 1 for every 20 feet between you and the
observer, to a maximum increase of 5 at 100 feet. This stacks with the normal modifier for distance.
