You treat the art of negotiation as devilish science.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Diplomacy), worshiper of
[Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus).\
**Benefit:** You can add your Intelligence or Wisdom modifier (whichever is higher) on Diplomacy
checks in place of your Charisma modifier. You can shift a creature's attitude more than two steps
with Diplomacy, and a creature's attitude is only decreased by one step if you roll a natural 1 and
fail the check by 5 or more.
