You are a master of the art of dispelling.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dispel Focus](ref/feat/dispel-focus), ability to cast
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you attempt a dispel check based on your caster level, you gain a +2 bonus to
the check. This stacks with the bonus from Dispel Focus.
