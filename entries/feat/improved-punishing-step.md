## Improved Punishing Step (Combat)

You know how to take advantage of a devastating charge.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Punishing Step](ref/feat/punishing-step), base
attack bonus +8.\
**Benefit:** Your bonus to AC from the Dodge feat increases to +2 against [charge](ref/rule/charge)
attacks. When a creature charges you and misses with its attack, it loses its Dexterity bonus to AC
against the next melee attack you make against it before the end of your next turn. If a creature
misses with multiple attacks (such as when using the [pounce](ref/umr/pounce) ability), you can make
one such attack against the creature's [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) AC before the end of your
next turn for each of that creature's attacks that missed you.
