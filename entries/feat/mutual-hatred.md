## Mutual Hatred (Combat)

**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 164

Others hate you, and their hatred feeds your own.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls against creatures whose hatred
racial trait or [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) class feature applies to you. For
instance, an [orc](ref/race/orc) would gain this bonus against all [dwarves](ref/race/dwarf) and
against rangers who chose humanoid (orc) as a favored enemy.
