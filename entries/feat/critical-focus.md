## Critical Focus (Combat, Critical)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 120

You are trained in the art of causing pain.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +9.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +4 circumstance bonus on attack rolls made to confirm critical hits.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 116\
If your successful critical confirmation roll is a natural 19 or 20, you can spend 2 stamina points
to roll another confirmation roll. If this confirmation roll also succeeds, increase your critical
multiplier by 1 for this attack, and you can roll again. If you continue to roll 19 or 20, you can
continue to spend stamina points for additional rolls, and the increases to the critical multiplier
stack.

### Mythic Critical Focus

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 61\
Your blows unerringly find your target's vital spots.\
**Prerequisites:** Critical Focus.\
**Benefit:** You automatically confirm critical threats against non-mythic opponents. In addition,
when you threaten a critical hit against a creature wearing armor with the
[fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification) special ability or similar effect, that creature must
roll twice and take the worse result when determining critical hit negation.
