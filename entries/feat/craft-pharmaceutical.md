You can craft pharmaceuticals.\
**Prerequisites:** [Technologist](ref/feat/technologist), Heal 9 ranks, Knowledge (nature) 9 ranks\
**Benefit:** You can create any pharmaceutical or poison. Creating a pharmaceutical takes 2 hours if
its base price is 250 gp or less; otherwise, the creation of the pharmaceutical takes 1 day for
every 1,000 gp in its price. To create the pharmaceutical or poison, you must use up raw materials
costing half of this total price. See [Crafting High-Tech Items](ref/rule/crafting-high-tech-items)
for more information.
