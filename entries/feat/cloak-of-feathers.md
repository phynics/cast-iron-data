You have learned to blend in with wingless humanoids, and as long as you are not flying, you can
fold your wings in a manner that resembles a cloak or robe.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [strix](ref/race/strix).\
**Benefit:** When you fold your wings, you gain a +2 bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks
to pose as a human, and ignore the penalties for disguising yourself as a different race while doing
so. Additionally, you can hide one small item within your wings' folds (such as a potion, a wand, a
weapon weighing 1 pound or less, or a similarly sized item at your GM's discretion). Any such hidden
item requires a successful DC 25 Perception check for others to notice. You can retrieve this item
with a swift action, but unfolding your wings causes it to drop to the ground.\
**Special:** Other creatures with natural wings can also take Cloak of Feathers at the GM's
discretion, ignoring the racial prerequisite.
