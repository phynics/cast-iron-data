## Jabbing Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 152

A cluster of quick strikes deals more damage than a single roundhouse swing.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike); base attack bonus
+6, brawler's flurry class feature, or flurry of blows class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you hit a target with an unarmed strike and you have hit that target with an
unarmed strike previously that round, you deal an extra 1d6 points of damage to that target.
