**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 12

Your natural spells are significantly increased, enabling you to feed and heal others.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15, character level 8th, [ghoran](ref/race/ghoran), natural magic racial
trait.\
**Benefit:** Once per week, you can cast [heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to your character
level. If you do so, you lose access to the spells normally granted by the natural magic racial
trait for the next 48 hours.
