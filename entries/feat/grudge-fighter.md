## Grudge Fighter (Combat)

You feel great anger at anyone who dares to attack you, and this fury makes your own attacks that
much stronger.\
**Prerequisites:** [Orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls made against any creature that
attacked you in the current combat.
