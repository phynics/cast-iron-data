Your muscles work overtime in areas with heavy gravity.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 17 or [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance).\
**Benefit:** You do not take the normal penalties for being on heavy
[gravity](ref/rule/gravity-traits) planes. At the GM's discretion, this effect may apply to certain
environments on the Material Plane, such as high-gravity planets. In addition, your Strength is
considered to be 4 higher for the purpose of determining your carrying capacity.
