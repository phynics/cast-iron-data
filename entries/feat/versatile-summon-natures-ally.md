You've learned to summon a wider array of creatures.\
**Prerequisites:** [Augment Summoning](ref/feat/augment-summoning), Knowledge (nature) 1 rank,
Knowledge (planes) 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** When you summon one or more animals, humanoids, or vermin using a
[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) spell (or an effect that mimics such a spell),
instead of granting them the benefit from Augment Summoning, you can instead apply one of the
following templates to them: [aerial](ref/template/aerial), [aqueous](ref/template/aqueous),
[chthonic](ref/template/chthonic), [fiery](ref/template/fiery), or
[primordial](ref/template/primordial). If you summon multiple creatures with one casting, they must
all have the same template.
