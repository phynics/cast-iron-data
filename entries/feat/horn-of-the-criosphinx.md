## Horn of the Criosphinx (Combat)

You end your charge in a devastating two-handed attack.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +6 or monk level 6th.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you make a successful charge attack while wielding a two-handed weapon in both
hands, add two times your Strength bonus to the damage roll.\
**Normal:** A character wielding a two-handed weapon adds 1-1/2 times her Strength bonus to damage
rolls.\
**Special:** A monk can use this feat as long as he is wielding a two-handed weapon or both his
hands are empty.\
**Note:** A monk can take this feat as a bonus feat at 6th level. To benefit from this feat, monks
must have both hands free and be capable of delivering an unarmed strike.
