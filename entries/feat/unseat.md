## Unseat (Combat)

You are skilled at unseating your mounted opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, Ride 1 rank, [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), base
attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** When charging an opponent while mounted and wielding a [lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance),
resolve the attack as normal. If it hits, you may immediately make a free
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) attempt in addition to the normal damage. If successful, the target
is knocked off his horse and lands [prone](ref/rule/prone) in a space adjacent to his mount that is
directly away from you.
