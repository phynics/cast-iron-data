## Punishing Step (Combat)

You can hurt a foe that uses a combat maneuver against you.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge).\
**Benefit:** Your bonus to AC from the Dodge feat increases to +2 when calculating your CMD. When a
creature attempts a combat maneuver against you and fails, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on
attack rolls against that creature until the end of your next turn.
