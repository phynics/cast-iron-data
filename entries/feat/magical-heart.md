## Magical Heart (Combat)

**Sources:** Heroes of Golarion pg 7

Through intense focus, your magical strikes reinforce your ioun heart.\
**Prerequisites:** [Arcane Strike](ref/feat/arcane-strike),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** While your unarmed strikes are imbued with power from the Arcane Strike feat, a
successful hit with an unarmed strike grants you a number of temporary hit points equal to the bonus
damage granted by Arcane Strike.
