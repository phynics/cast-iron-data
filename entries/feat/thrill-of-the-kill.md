**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 58, Orcs of Golarion pg 25

Killing fuels your rage.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [orc](ref/race/orc), rage class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you are raging and your attack reduces an enemy to negative hit points or kills
it, you regain 1 round of rage. You may only use this feat if the fallen enemy had at least as many
Hit Dice as you. You can only gain this benefit once per round.
