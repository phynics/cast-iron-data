You have spent significant time in the Grand Duchy of Alkenstar, and your proximity to so many
firearms has given you a familiarity with their delicate handling.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +3, proficiency with at least one firearm.\
**Benefit:** Once per day as an immediate action, you can reroll an attack roll with a firearm that
would have resulted in a misfire. You must take the results of the new roll, even if it results in a
misfire as well.
