## Simple Weapon Proficiency (Combat)

You are trained in the use of basic weapons.\
**Benefit:** You make attack rolls with simple weapons without penalty.\
**Normal:** When using a weapon with which you are not proficient, you take a -4 penalty on attack
rolls.\
**Special:** All characters except for commoners, druids, monks, unchained monks, shifters, and
wizards are automatically proficient with all simple weapons. They need not select this feat.
