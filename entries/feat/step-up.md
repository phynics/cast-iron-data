## Step Up (Combat)

You can close the distance when a foe tries to move away.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** Whenever an adjacent foe attempts to take a 5-foot step away from you, you may also
make a 5-foot step as an immediate action so long as you end up adjacent to the foe that triggered
this ability. If you take this step, you cannot take a 5-foot step during your next turn. If you
take an action to move during your next turn, subtract 5 feet from your total movement.
