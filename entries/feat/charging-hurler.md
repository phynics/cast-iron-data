## Charging Hurler (Combat)

You know how to use your momentum to enhance your thrown weapon attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot).\
**Benefit:** You can use the charge rules to make a thrown weapon attack. All the parameters of a
charge apply, except that you must only move closer to your opponent, and you must end your movement
within 30 feet of that opponent. If you do, you can make a single thrown weapon attack against that
opponent, gaining the +2 bonus on the attack roll and taking a -2 penalty to your AC until the start
of your next turn.
