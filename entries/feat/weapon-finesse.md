## Weapon Finesse (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 136

You are trained in using your agility in melee combat, as opposed to brute strength.\
**Benefit:** With a light weapon, [rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier), [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip), or
[spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain) made for a creature of your size category, you may use
your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier on attack rolls. If you carry a shield,
its armor check penalty applies to your attack rolls.\
**Special:** Natural weapons are considered light weapons.

**Note:** Further publications expanded the number of weapons that can be used with Weapon Finesse.
Sometimes there are restrictions on this behavior: check the weapon entry to be sure.

- [Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword)
- [Bladed scarf](ref/eq-weapon/bladed-scarf)
- [Elven branched spear](ref/eq-weapon/elven-branched-spear)
- [Elven curve blade](ref/eq-weapon/elven-curve-blade)
- [Elven thornblade](ref/eq-weapon/elven-thornblade)
- [Estoc](ref/eq-weapon/estoc)
- [Spiral rapier](ref/eq-weapon/spiral-rapier)
- [Sword cane](ref/eq-weapon/sword-cane)

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 135\
You can spend 2 stamina points to negate the penalty on attack rolls from your shield's armor check
penalty until the start of your next turn.

### Mythic Weapon Finesse

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 77\
You're an expert with weapons that rely on your agility.\
**Prerequisites:** Weapon Finesse.\
**Benefit:** When using Weapon Finesse, you may also use your Dexterity modifier instead of your
Strength modifier on your damage rolls. If you carry a shield, its armor check penalty doesn't apply
to either the attack rolls or the damage rolls.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
The Weapon Finesse feat has been removed, and now any character may choose to use their dexterity
bonus to hit with light melee weapons. As a consequence, the Light Melee Weapons designation has
been renamed to [Finesse](ref/rule/equipment-errata) Melee Weapons. A new special weapon feature
called [agile](ref/rule/equipment-errata) has been added for melee weapons that are one-handed or
two-handed but can still be finessed, such as the [rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier).
