Calling on the destructive aspect of Nethys, you channel pure force.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 7d6, worshiper of
[Nethys](ref/faith/nethys).\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you channel negative energy to deal damage to living creatures, you
can cause affected creatures to make Fortitude saves instead of Will saves. Affected creatures take
the amount of damage channeled in force damage (a successful save halves). Creatures that fail their
saves are also knocked prone.
