**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 58

You've trained at swimming in combat and over long distances for so long that you can move through
the water like a native.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Swim), Swim 10 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a swim speed of 30 feet or your unmodified base land speed, whichever is
slower; your racial bonus on Swim checks for having a swim speed is +2, instead of +8.
