You discern the best path through rocky terrain.\
**Prerequisites:** Stonecunning racial trait, worshiper of [Torag](ref/faith/torag).\
**Benefit:** You can move through any sort of nonmagical rocky terrain without taking damage,
becoming slowed due to [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles), or suffering any other
impairments the terrain would otherwise cause.
