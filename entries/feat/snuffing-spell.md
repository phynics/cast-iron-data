## Snuffing Spell (Metamagic)

**Sources:** Blood of Shadows pg 25

Your magic disrupts sources of light.\
**Benefit:** You can modify a spell to extinguish magical and nonmagical light sources that the
target has. The first time a creature takes damage from or fails a saving throw against a snuffing
spell, any nonmagical light sources it has are immediately extinguished and you can attempt to
dispel any active spells with the light descriptor that are affecting the target as if you had also
cast the targeted version of [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic). Spells that do not target
creatures can't be snuffing spells.

A snuffing spell uses up a spell slot 2 levels higher than the spell's actual level.
