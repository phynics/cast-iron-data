## Boar Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 90

Your sharp teeth and nails rip your foes open.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Intimidate 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can deal bludgeoning damage or slashing damage with your unarmed strikes - changing
damage type is a free action. While using this style, once per round when you hit a single foe with
two or more unarmed strikes, you can tear flesh. When you do, you deal 2d6 extra points of damage
with the attack.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 114\
While using this style, you can spend 3 stamina points when you hit a target with only one unarmed
strike during a round to tear flesh and deal 2d6 points of damage with the attack. You can still
only tear flesh once per round.
