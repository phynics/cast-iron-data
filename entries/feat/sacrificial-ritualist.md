Your magical power used to originate in yourself, but now you draw it from others.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mutilating Ritualist](ref/feat/mutilating-ritualist), Knowledge (arcana) 4
ranks, Knowledge (religion) 4 ranks, Spellcraft 4 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can improve your ability to cast an [occult ritual](ref/rule/occult-rituals) by
powering it with the blood of creatures. When you begin the occult ritual with the death of a
sentient creature (or number of sentient creatures with a total number of Hit Dice equal to twice
the effective level of the ritual), you and all secondary casters gain a +4 bonus on all skill
checks necessary to complete the occult ritual. This feat's sacrifices are in addition to any
sacrifices normally required to complete the ritual.
