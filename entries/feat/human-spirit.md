Your blood burns with the passion and unyielding quest for self-improvement displayed so prominently
by your human relatives.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** You receive 1 bonus skill rank. Whenever you gain another Hit Die, you gain an
additional skill rank. You cannot gain more than four skill ranks in this way.\
**Special:** You can only take this feat at 1st level. If you take this feat, you cannot take the
[Drow Spirit](ref/feat/drow-spirit) or [Elven Spirit](ref/feat/elven-spirit) feats.
