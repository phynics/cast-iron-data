## Wingclipper (Combat)

**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 25

Your attacks can prevent winged opponents from flying.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +9.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit against an opponent that uses wings to fly, you can
forgo the extra critical damage to instead prevent it from flying for a number of rounds equal to
twice the critical multiplier of your attack. Opponents that are flying at the time of the critical
effect must succeed at a DC 20 Fly check to avoid taking [falling](ref/rule/falling) damage.
