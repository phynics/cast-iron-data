After years spent digging up graves, you are able to move through dirt almost as easily as a fish
through water.\
**Prerequisites:** [Ghoul](ref/monster/ghoul).\
**Benefit:** You gain a burrow speed of 10 feet through earth, sand, or soil.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take it, add 10 feet to your
burrow speed, up to a maximum of your base land speed.
