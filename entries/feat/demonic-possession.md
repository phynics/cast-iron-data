You can possess creatures.\
**Prerequisites:** [Demon](ref/family/demon) or [half-demon](ref/template/half-fiend), Cha 21, Wis
17\
**Benefit:** You gain the ability to use [magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day.\
**Special:** You may take this feat more than once. Each time you select this feat, you gain the
ability to use your magic jar spell-like ability one additional time per day.
