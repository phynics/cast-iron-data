Your careful study of mental magic grants you mastery in your discipline.\
**Prerequisites:** Psychic discipline class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on caster level checks and [concentration](ref/rule/concentration)
checks when casting spells from your [psychic discipline](ref/ability/disciplines), and you gain a
+1 insight bonus on saving throws against spells from your discipline.
