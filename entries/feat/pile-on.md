## Pile On (Combat)

You can keep a foe shuddering in fear.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Hurtful](ref/feat/hurtful), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack).\
**Benefit:** Once per round when you damage a creature that is shaken, frightened, or panicked, you
can choose to deal half your normal damage in order to extend the duration of its fear condition by
1 round.
