Others listen to your words of encouragement and perform acts above and beyond as a result.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [inspire competence](ref/performance/inspire-competence) bardic
performance.\
**Benefit:** Inspire competence now affects all allies within 30 feet who can hear your performance,
as long as they are attempting the skill you've selected.\
**Normal:** Inspire competence only affects one ally.
