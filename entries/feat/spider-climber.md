You tread where only arachnids dare.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 3rd, [drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** You can cast [spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using your character level as the caster level.
Furthermore, you gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against the [web](ref/umr/web) special attacks of
spiders and the effects of [web](ref/spell/web) and other similar spells (such as the
[web cloud](ref/spell/web-cloud) spell).
