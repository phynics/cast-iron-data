## Tatzlwyrm Claw Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Legacy of Dragons pg 21

Your curled fingers can tear into opponents' flesh and snatch free grappled allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Escape Artist 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You can choose for your unarmed attacks to deal slashing damage. If you strike a target
that has another creature grappled or pinned, as a swift action you can immediately attempt a
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver check against the target's CMD. If you succeed at the
check, you can force the target to release a grappled creature or switch a pinned creature to being
merely grappled.
