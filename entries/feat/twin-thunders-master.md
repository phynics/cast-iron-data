## Twin Thunders Master (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 123

With thunderous simultaneous strikes, you can batter a mighty giant into submission.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf) or [gnome](ref/race/gnome); defensive training racial
trait; [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/improved-two-weapon-fighting) and
[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting), or flurry of blows class feature;
[Twin Thunders](ref/feat/twin-thunders); [Twin Thunders Flurry](ref/feat/twin-thunders-flurry);
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with both wielded weapons; base attack bonus +9.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you deal an opponent extra damage with the Twin Thunders feat, that opponent
is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round. You also force that opponent to succeed at a Fortitude
saving throw (DC 10 + half your level + your Str modifier) or become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered)
for 1 round. If you use this feat to render staggered an opponent that is already staggered, you
[daze](ref/misc/dazed) that opponent instead. In a similar way, you can [stun](ref/misc/stunned) an
opponent that is already dazed.
