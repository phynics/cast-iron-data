**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 165

You can cast a single low-level spell as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Prerequisites:** [Minor Spell Expertise](ref/feat/minor-spell-expertise), ability to cast
9th-level spells.\
**Benefit:** Choose one spell that you know of 5th level or lower. You may cast that spell twice per
day as a spell-like ability. The caster level for this spell-like ability is equal to your caster
level in the class from whose spell list the spell is taken. The spell-like ability's save DC is
Charisma-based. If the spell has an expensive focus or material component, it may not be chosen for
this feat. You cannot apply metamagic feats to this spell.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to a new spell.
