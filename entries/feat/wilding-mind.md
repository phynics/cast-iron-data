**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 120

You can repress your conscious mind in favor of a primal state that helps you resist mental
influence.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Wilding](ref/feat/wilding).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you fail a saving throw against a fear or mind-affecting effect, you can take
1d3 points of Intelligence damage as an immediate action and reroll the saving throw. This damage
cannot be reduced or avoided in any way. You must take the new result, even if it is worse.
