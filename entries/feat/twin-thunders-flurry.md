## Twin Thunders Flurry (Combat)

Your dual bludgeoning strikes are especially deadly when you are fighting giants.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf) or [gnome](ref/race/gnome); defensive training racial
trait; [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/improved-two-weapon-fighting) and
[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting), or flurry of blows class feature;
[Twin Thunders](ref/feat/twin-thunders); [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with both wielded
weapons; base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You can trip a creature with the giant subtype of up to Huge size, and you gain a +2
bonus on damage rolls against creatures of the giant subtype. Further, each time you hit a creature
of the giant subtype with your off-hand weapon after you hit that creature with your primary weapon,
you can deal the extra off-hand weapon damage Twin Thunders grants you.
