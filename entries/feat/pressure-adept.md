You've spent so much time at unusual pressures that You've extended your native range beyond the
norms of your species.\
**Prerequisites:** Swim 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** Add one [oceanic zone](ref/rule/oceanic-zones) to your native range when calculating
pressure (meaning that you don't take [pressure damage](ref/rule/water-dangers) in that range unless
instantaneously transported up or down, and even then you adapt to the change all at once rather
than 100 feet at a time). Surface-dwellers taking this feat must select the sunlight zone, and
aquatic creatures can select any zone either directly above or directly below their native range.
