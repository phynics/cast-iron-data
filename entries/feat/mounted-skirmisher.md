## Mounted Skirmisher (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 165

You are adept at attacking from upon a swift moving steed.\
**Prerequisites:** Ride rank 14, [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat),
[Trick Riding](ref/feat/trick-riding).\
**Benefit:** If your mount moves its speed or less, you can still take a full-attack action.\
**Normal:** If your mount moves more than 5 feet, you can only take an attack action.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 127\
When your mount moves its speed or less, you can spend 5 stamina points to spur it on 10 additional
feet. This is bonus movement, and you can still make a full attack when your mount takes this bonus
movement.
