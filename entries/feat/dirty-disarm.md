## Dirty Disarm (Combat)

You can pull dirty tricks while disarming your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick), [Improved Disarm](ref/feat/improved-disarm),
base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You can attempt to perform a [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) and a
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) maneuver together against a single target as a full-round action. You
attempt a single combat maneuver check for both maneuvers. If your CMB is different for the two
types of maneuvers, use the lower of the two bonuses. If your target's CMD is different against the
two types of maneuvers, use the higher CMD. If you succeed at the check, you successfully perform
both combat maneuvers.
