**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 135

You have enhanced physical stamina.\
**Benefit:** You gain +3 hit points. For every Hit Die you possess beyond 3, you gain an additional
+1 hit point. If you have more than 3 Hit Dice, you gain +1 hit points whenever you gain a Hit Die
(such as when you gain a level).

### Mythic Toughness

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 75\
You have legendary resilience and durability.\
**Prerequisites:** Toughness.\
**Benefit:** Toughness provides you twice as many hit points. In addition, when you're below 0 hit
points, you gain DR 10/epic. This DR stacks with any other DR/epic you might possess.
