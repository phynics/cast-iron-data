You have enhanced physical stamina.\
**Benefit:** You gain +3 hit points. For every Hit Die you possess beyond 3, you gain an additional
+1 hit point. If you have more than 3 Hit Dice, you gain +1 hit points whenever you gain a Hit Die
(such as when you gain a level).
