You have immersed yourself deeply in studies of the nature of the Abyss and its inhabitants.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on all Knowledge checks related to the Abyss or to
[demons](ref/family/demon), and you may take 10 on Knowledge checks made to identify demons.
