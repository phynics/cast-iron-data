## Ascetic Strike (Combat)

Your weapon strikes deal more damage than usual.\
**Prerequisites:** [Ascetic Form](ref/feat/ascetic-form); [Ascetic Style](ref/feat/ascetic-style);
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen weapon; base attack bonus +7 or monk level
7th.\
**Benefit:** You can use the unarmed strike damage of a monk 4 levels lower than your character
level (minimum 1st) instead of the base damage for the chosen weapon. Ascetic Strike functions in
all other ways as the [brawler's](ref/class/brawler) close weapon mastery class feature. In
addition, you ignore the still mind class feature prerequisite for the
[Monastic Legacy](ref/feat/monastic-legacy) feat.
