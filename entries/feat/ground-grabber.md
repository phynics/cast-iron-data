## Ground-Grabber (Combat)

When pressed by big opponents, you hunker down to more easily keep your feet.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 competence bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense against awesome blow,
bull rush, drag, reposition, or trip combat maneuvers from opponents larger than you.
