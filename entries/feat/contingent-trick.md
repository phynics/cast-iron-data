## Contingent Trick (Trick)

You implant a flexible mesmerist trick in the target, triggering the more beneficial trick when the
time is right.\
**Prerequisites:** Any two trick feats.\
**Benefit:** You can implant a contingent trick in a target's mind, following the same rules and
limitations for implanting mesmerist tricks. A contingent trick acts as a mesmerist trick in all
ways, except that when you are implanting a contingent trick, you choose any two mesmerist tricks
you know (including masterful tricks) and implant both in your target. You can't choose the same
trick twice. When you trigger a contingent trick, it acts only as the mesmerist trick that
corresponds to the condition you used to trigger the trick.
