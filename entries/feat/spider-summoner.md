You gain the ability to summon powerful spiders.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast \*summon monster or
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spells, [drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** When casting either a _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell or a
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell, your options increase. Depending on
the level of the spell, you can summon the spiders listed below.

> [Summon Monster:](ref/spell/summon-monster) 1st
> level-[giant crab spider](ref/monster/spider-giant-crab-spider)\*; 4th
> level-[giant black widow](ref/monster/spider-giant-black-widow)\*; 5th
> level-[ogre spider](ref/monster/spider-ogre-spider)\*; 7th
> level-[giant tarantula](ref/monster/spider-giant-tarantula)\*
>
> [Summon Nature's Ally:](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) 1st level-giant crab spider; 4th
> level-giant black widow; 5th level-ogre spider; 7th level-giant tarantula

Creatures marked with an asterisk (\*) are summoned with the [celestial](ref/template/celestial)
template if you are good, and the [fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) template if you are evil. If you
are neutral, you may choose which template to apply to the creature.

Furthermore, when you summon spiders using _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ or
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally),_ the DC of the summoned monster's poison and
web effects increases by 2.
