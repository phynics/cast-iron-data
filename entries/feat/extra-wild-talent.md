You gain an additional wild talent.\
**Prerequisites:** [Kineticist](ref/class/kineticist) level 6th.\
**Benefit:** You gain a wild talent that's at least 2 levels lower than the highest-level wild
talent you can currently use. You can select an infusion or a non-infusion wild talent, but not a
blast or defense wild talent. If you have the expanded element class feature, you can select a wild
talent from any of your elements that's at least 2 levels lower than the highest-level wild talent
from your primary element that you can currently use.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time, you must choose a different wild
talent.
