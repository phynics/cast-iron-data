## Opening Volley (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 112

Your ranged assault leaves your foe disoriented and vulnerable to your melee attack.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you deal damage with a ranged attack, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on the
next melee attack roll you make against the opponent. This attack must occur before the end of your
next turn.
