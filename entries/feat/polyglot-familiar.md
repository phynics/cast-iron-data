## Polyglot Familiar (Familiar)

**Sources:** Familiar Folio pg 19

Your familiar can speak with animals other than those of its own kind.\
**Prerequisites:** Must have a familiar.\
**Benefit:** Choose a category of creature: amphibians, birds, felines, invertebrates, reptiles,
rodents, simians, or vermin. Your [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) can speak with creatures of that
kind.\
**Special:** This feat can be taken multiple times, choosing a different category of creature each
time. The first time you take this feat, if your familiar can't already speak with creatures of its
kind, you must choose that category of creature. If your familiar later gains the ability to speak
with creatures of its kind, you can choose to apply this feat's benefits to a different category of
creature instead.
