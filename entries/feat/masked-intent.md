**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 124, Arcane Anthology pg 15

Your masked face makes your intentions nearly impossible to discern.\
**Prerequisites:** [Nameless One](ref/feat/nameless-one).\
**Benefit:** While you're benefiting from the Nameless One feat, your intentions become difficult to
read. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus on opposed Bluff checks, and increase the DC by 4 of checks
to gather information about you using Diplomacy, to answer questions about you or your abilities or
weaknesses using the appropriate [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill, or to identify any
spells or [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) that you cast using
[Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft). If you have 10 or more ranks in any of these skills, the bonus
for that skill or increase to that DC increases to +8.
