## Cudgeler Sweep (Combat)

Your powerful blows can knock down your opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** [Bludgeoner](ref/feat/bludgeoner), [Cudgeler Style](ref/feat/cudgeler-style),
base attack bonus +5 or monk level 5th.\
**Benefit:** When you hit with a charge attack using the Cudgeler Style feat, you can immediately
attempt a single [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver check against the target with a +2 competence
bonus as a free action. This provokes an attack of opportunity as normal for a trip combat maneuver.
