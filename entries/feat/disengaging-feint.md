## Disengaging Feint (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 96

You can feint to disengage from combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint).\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, use [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) to feint against an opponent.
Instead of denying that opponent his Dexterity bonus to AC, a successful feint allows you to move up
to your speed without provoking an attack of opportunity from the opponent you feinted for leaving
the square you start in.
