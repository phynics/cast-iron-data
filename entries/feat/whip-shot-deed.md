## Whip-Shot Deed (Grit)

**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 22

You can fire your weapon and strike with it at the same time.\
**Prerequisites:** Grit class feature or [Amateur Gunslinger](ref/feat/amateur-gunslinger) feat,
[pistol-whip](ref/deed/pistol-whip) deed.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use your _pistol-whip_ deed, you can fire a single bullet from your
firearm at the same time that you strike a foe with the butt of your firearm. You must spend a total
of 2 grit points to use the _pistol-whip_ deed in this way, and must still succeed at a ranged touch
attack against the target of your _pistol-whip_ deed to hit. You cannot use the
[scatter](ref/misc/scatter) weapon quality of a firearm when you use this feat.
