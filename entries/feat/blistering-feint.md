## Blistering Feint (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 130

Your foes flinch from the heat of your weapon, giving you an opportunity to slip past their
defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint), [ifrit](ref/race/ifrit).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on feint checks made while wielding a weapon that deals fire
damage. Anytime you successfully feint a creature while using such a weapon, you may deal its fire
damage to the enemy.
