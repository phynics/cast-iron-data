## Got Your Back (Combat, Teamwork)

You protect an ally from attacks he doesn't see coming.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise).\
**Benefit:** If you successfully use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to give an
ally who also has this feat a +2 bonus to AC against an opponent's next attack, the ally is also not
considered [flanked](ref/rule/flanking) or [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against that opponent
until the beginning of your next turn.\
**Normal:** You can use aid another to grant an ally a +2 bonus to AC against the next attack from
an adjacent foe.
