## Octopus Style (Combat, Style)

You anticipate the attacks of more than one enemy at once.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), Acrobatics 1 rank, Perception 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** As a move action when using this style, you can select a number of creatures you are
aware of equal to your Dexterity modifier. You can't be [flanked](ref/rule/flanking) by the selected
creatures until the start of your next turn.
