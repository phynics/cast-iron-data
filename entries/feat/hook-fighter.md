## Hook Fighter (Combat)

In your deft hands, a climbing tool becomes a deadly weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You treat a [grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook) as a one-handed weapon that
deals piercing damage equal to a [heavy pick](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-pick) of its size and that has the
[disarm](ref/misc/disarm) and [trip](ref/misc/trip) special weapon features. You do not incur
penalties as you would for using a grappling hook as an
[improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons). If you are proficient with
[whips](ref/eq-weapon/whip) and your grappling hook has at least 10 feet of rope or chain attached
to it, you can treat it as a two-handed melee weapon with 15-foot reach, though you can use it
against foes anywhere within your reach (including adjacent foes), though you don't threaten any
squares with it. Changing between using a grappling hook as a normal weapon and a reach weapon is a
move action. When performing a [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) maneuver with a grappling hook, you
can only move the target toward you from its original position.
