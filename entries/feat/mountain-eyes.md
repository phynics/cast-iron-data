Your gaze cuts through vapors with ease.\
**Prerequisites:** [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight), Perception 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You ignore [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (but not total concealment) caused by
fog, rain, smoke, wind, and other gases or weather effects (including magical effects like
[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist)), and you ignore up to -4 in penalties on Perception
checks from such effects. You treat total concealment resulting from any such gas or weather effects
within 10 feet of you as concealment (20% miss chance).
