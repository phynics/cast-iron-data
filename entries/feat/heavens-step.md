## Heaven's Step (Combat)

Like the daring Heaven's Step Offensive championed by Grand Prince Gennaris III of Taldor, you
confidently take the fight to your enemy and strike an unexpected blow.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Following Step](ref/feat/following-step), [Step Up](ref/feat/step-up),
[Step Up and Strike](ref/feat/step-up-and-strike), base attack bonus +10.\
**Benefit:** When you make an attack using Step Up and Strike, you can also make an extra attack
with your offhand weapon using your highest base attack bonus. Both attacks take the normal
penalties for [two-weapon fighting](ref/rule/two-weapon-fighting).
