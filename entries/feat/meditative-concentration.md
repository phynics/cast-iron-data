## Meditative Concentration (Meditation)

**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 19

Meditation clears your mind, allowing you to concentrate on your spells as if not distracted.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Combat Meditation](ref/feat/combat-meditation),
[Meditation Master](ref/feat/meditation-master), character level 5th.\
**Benefit:** For 1 round per character level after you perform combat meditation, whenever you
attempt a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check to avoid losing a spell or a Fortitude save
to resist a distraction effect (such as the distraction ability of a swarm), you gain a +4 bonus on
the check. If you choose to use the benefits of Combat Meditation on the check, the bonus applies to
both die rolls.
