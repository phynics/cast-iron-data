**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 7

You distrust, disrespect, or doubt the gods, which helps you fight off their power.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against divine spells and the
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) granted by levels in a divine casting class.\
**Special:** In certain circumstances, some outsiders and creatures of other types might have a
strong connection to a specific deity they work for as agents and servants. These creatures' spells
and spell-like abilities should likewise be affected by this feat, regardless of the creatures'
planar origins or sources of magical power, subject to the GM's discretion.
