You have a near-perfect sense of direction.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Survival), Survival 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to avoid becoming lost and
to determine whether you are lost. You also gain a +4 bonus on Intelligence checks to navigate
mazes, including to escape a [maze](ref/spell/maze) spell. Once per day, you can use your intuition
to determine whether traveling a particular direction from a fork in the road (or other junction)
might be productive or dangerous. This functions like the [augury](ref/spell/augury) spell, except
it provides weal or woe based on traveling in a particular direction.
