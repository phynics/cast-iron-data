## Deadly Stroke (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 121

With a well-placed strike, you can bring a swift and painful end to most foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display),
[Greater Weapon Focus](ref/feat/greater-weapon-focus),
[Shatter Defenses](ref/feat/shatter-defenses), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus), proficiency
with the selected weapon, base attack bonus +11.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, make a single attack with the weapon for which you have Greater
Weapon Focus against a [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) or [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) opponent.
If you hit, you deal double the normal damage and the target takes 1 point of Constitution
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed). The additional damage and bleed is not multiplied on a critical hit.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 116\
You can spend 5 stamina points to make a Deadly Stroke attack against a [dazed](ref/misc/dazed)
opponent, or 10 stamina points to make a Deadly Stroke against an opponent you are flanking.

### Mythic Deadly Stroke

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 62\
You can kill with a single stroke.\
**Prerequisites:** Deadly Stroke.\
**Benefit:** When making a Deadly Stroke attack, you can expend one use of mythic power to instantly
kill a non-mythic living creature. A Fortitude save (DC 10 + your base attack bonus + your tier)
negates this effect, though the attack still deals double damage and Con bleed as Deadly Stroke.
Constructs, oozes, and creatures immune to critical hits or precision-based damage can't be killed
with this feat.
