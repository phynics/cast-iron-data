## Improved Reposition (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 163

You have learned how to force your enemies to move around the battlefield.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise).\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks
made to reposition a foe. You also receive a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense when an
opponent tries to reposition you.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a reposition combat maneuver.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat **may** be removed, replaced with the new [Deft Maneuvers](ref/rule/deft-maneuvers) feat.
