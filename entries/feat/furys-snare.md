## Fury's Snare (Combat)

You entangle your foe in your weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Fury's Fall](ref/feat/furys-fall), [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip).\
**Benefit:** If you successfully use a [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) to trip a foe, you can choose to
drop your whip. Your tripped target gains the [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) condition. The target
can break free of the whip by making a DC 10 Strength check or a DC 15 Escape Artist check (add the
enhancement bonus of the whip to these DCs), or by using a full-round action to disentangle himself.
Escaping the whip by making a Strength check means the whip gains the [broken](ref/misc/broken)
condition. If the whip has a magical quality such as [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming), apply this
quality's damage to the target every round on your turn.
