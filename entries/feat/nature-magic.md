**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 152

You are able to use simple spells by drawing on nature's raw majesty.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (nature) 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You gain [know direction](ref/spell/know-direction) as a constant
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and can choose another druid orison (0-level
spell) you can cast as a spell-like ability once per day. Your caster level for both of these
spell-like abilities is equal to your character level.
