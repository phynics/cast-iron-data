You adopt a defensive stance that allows you to absorb and redirect hits.\
**Prerequisites:** [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard), [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), base attack bonus
+4.\
**Benefit:** While using the [total defense](ref/misc/total-defense) action,
[fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) action, or
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), you can forgo the dodge bonus to AC you would
normally gain to instead gain an equivalent amount of DR, to a maximum of DR 5/-, until the start of
your next turn. This [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) stacks with DR you gain from
class features, such as the [barbarian's](ref/class/barbarian), but not with DR from any other
source. If you are denied your Dexterity bonus to AC, you are also denied this DR.
