You can change into a fox in addition to your other forms.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, base attack bonus +3, [kitsune](ref/race/kitsune).\
**Benefit:** You can take the form of a fox whose appearance is static and cannot be changed each
time you assume this form. Your bite attack's damage is reduced to 1d3 points of damage on a hit,
but you gain a +10 racial bonus on Disguise checks made to appear as a fox. Changing from kitsune to
fox shape is a standard action. This ability otherwise functions as
[beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2), and your ability scores change accordingly.
