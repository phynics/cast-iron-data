Your courage is unnaturally strong.\
**Prerequisites:** Animal or magical beast.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 morale bonus on saves against fear and emotion effects. If you can serve
as a mount, your rider gains a +4 morale bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal),
[Ride](ref/skill/ride), and [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks to force you within
range of a creature with the [unnatural aura universal monster rule](ref/umr/unnatural-aura). In
addition, the DC to push you with the Handle Animal skill does not increase if you are wounded or
have taken any nonlethal damage or ability score damage.\
**Normal:** The DC to push an animal increases by 2 if the animal is wounded or has taken any
nonlethal damage or ability score damage.
