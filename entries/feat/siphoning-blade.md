## Siphoning Blade (Combat)

You can empower yourself by drawing blood through your rhoka sword.\
**Prerequisites:** [Voracious Blade](ref/feat/voracious-blade), base attack bonus +8,
[urdefhan](ref/monster/urdefhan).\
**Benefit:** When you use the Voracious Blade feat to deal Strength damage with your
[rhoka sword](ref/eq-weapon/rhoka-sword), you also drain the victim's blood. The attack deals 1
point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage in addition to its normal damage, and you gain
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2 for 3 rounds. You can use this ability only once against an
individual creature in a 24-hour period.
