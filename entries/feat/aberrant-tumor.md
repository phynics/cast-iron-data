To the surprise of others, that strange growth on you is actually your spellcasting companion.\
**Prerequisites:** Aberrant bloodline.\
**Benefit:** You gain a tumor familiar, as the [tumor familiar](ref/discovery/tumor-familiar)
alchemist discovery, with an effective alchemist level equal to the level of the class that grants
your [aberrant](ref/sorcbloodline/aberrant) bloodline for determining the tumor familiar's
abilities. If multiple classes grant you the [aberrant](ref/blrgbloodline/aberrant) bloodline, those
class levels stack for determining your effective alchemist level.
