You possess a long pedigree of ruthless generals and military tacticians, and an army under your
command is more difficult to rout as a result.\
**Prerequisites:** [Hobgoblin](ref/race/hobgoblin).\
**Benefit:** When serving as a commander using the [mass combat rules](ref/rule/mass-combat), you
can add your class level to your army's Morale checks in place of your Charisma modifier.
