Your rapid teleportation makes your combat maneuvers more difficult to avoid.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to use the abundant step class feature or cast
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door), [Dimensional Agility](ref/feat/dimensional-agility),
[Dimensional Assault](ref/feat/dimensional-assault),
[Dimensional Dervish](ref/feat/dimensional-dervish), base attack bonus +9.\
**Benefit:** While using the Dimensional Dervish feat, you gain a +4 bonus on
[combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) checks to bull rush, disarm, reposition, or trip an
opponent.
