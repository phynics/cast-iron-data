## Saving Shield (Combat)

You deflect attacks that could mean your ally's death.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shield Proficiency](ref/feat/shield-proficiency).\
**Benefit:** Whenever an adjacent ally is the target of an attack, you can, as an immediate action,
grant that adjacent ally a +2 shield bonus to AC. You must be wielding a light shield, heavy shield,
or [tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) to use this feat.
