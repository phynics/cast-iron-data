**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 216, Faiths of Corruption pg 25

Your negative energy causes despair in those you harm.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature, worshiper of
[Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa).\
**Benefit:** When you damage a creature by channeling negative energy, you fill your foe with fear.
A creature that fails its Will save against your channeled energy also becomes
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for a number of rounds equal to the number of dice you roll for your
channel energy ability.
