**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 129

You are more resistant to mental effects.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on all Will saving throws.

### Mythic Iron Will

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 68\
Your will is incorruptible.\
**Prerequisites:** Iron Will.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you roll a Will saving throw against a spell,
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or supernatural ability from a non-mythic
source, roll twice and take the higher result.
