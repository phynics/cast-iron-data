## Greater Penetrating Strike (Combat)

Your attacks penetrate the defenses of most foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Penetrating Strike](ref/feat/penetrating-strike),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus), 16th-level fighter.\
**Benefit:** Your attacks with weapons selected with Weapon Focus ignore up to 10 points of
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). This amount is reduced to 5 points for damage
reduction without a type (such as DR 10/-).
