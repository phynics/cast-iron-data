## Teleportation Mastery (Item Mastery)

You can coax teleportation from conjuration magic items.\
**Prerequisites:** Use Magic Device 4 ranks, base Fortitude save bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You can cause an item that has a conjuration spell of 3rd level or higher in its
construction requirements to cast [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door). You can use this
ability once per day, plus an additional time per day at base Fortitude save bonus +9 and +12.
