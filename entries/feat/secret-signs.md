**Sources:** Inner Sea World Guide pg 288, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 57

You are particularly adept at communicating with others via innuendo, gestures, and secret hand
signs.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus on Bluff checks made to pass secret messages. In addition, you are
adept at hiding the somatic components of spellcasting. If you cast a spell that has only somatic
components, an observer must make a Perception check opposed by your Sleight of Hand check to notice
your spellcasting. Spellcraft checks made to identify any spell you cast that has somatic components
take a -2 penalty.
