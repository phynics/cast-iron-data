**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 8

You know how to adjust the amount of time required for a poison to take effect.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (alchemy) 11 ranks, [Toxicological Timing](ref/feat/toxicological-timing).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you craft a poison with the Craft (alchemy) skill, you can add or subtract up
to 1 day from its onset time (to a minimum onset period of 1 round).
