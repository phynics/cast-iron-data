Your spiritual guardians are more powerful than those of most others.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast [spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon) or
[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally), spirit magic class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you cast _spiritual weapon, spiritual ally,_ or a similar spell that grants
you a spiritual guardian, that guardian uses your shaman level instead of your base attack bonus to
determine its base attack bonus, potentially granting it multiple attacks. Additionally, it gains a
+2 bonus on caster level checks to overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) as well as
on damage rolls.
