**Prerequisites:** Con 15, [gillman](ref/race/gillman), water dependent racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You lose the water dependent racial trait, but you are [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) if
you have not submerged yourself in water within the past 24 hours.
