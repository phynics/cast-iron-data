You fully open your mind to the spirit world, learning to perceive the hidden.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 15, [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will), [Spirit Beacon](ref/feat/spirit-beacon).\
**Benefit:** Invisible creatures of the fey, outsider, and undead types gain only
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from you, rather than total concealment (though they can still
gain total concealment from [cover](ref/rule/cover) or use of the Stealth skill). Such creatures do
not gain a bonus on attack rolls against you or ignore your Dexterity bonus to AC as normally
granted by invisibility.
