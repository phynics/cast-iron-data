When you strike true in battle, you wash away allies' fear.\
**Prerequisites:** Aura of courage.\
**Benefit:** When you confirm a critical hit against an enemy, any fear effect affecting allies
within 30 feet is reduced by one step. [Panicked](ref/misc/panicked) becomes
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened), frightened becomes [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), and shaken is
removed. In addition, any ally suffering other effects from a failed save against a fear effect,
such as the [paralysis](ref/rule/paralysis) from a [mummy's](ref/monster/mummy) despair aura,
receives a new save against the effect at the original DC.
