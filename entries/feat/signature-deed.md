## Signature Deed (Grit)

You are known for performing a particular deed, and can perform it with greater ease.\
**Prerequisites:** Grit class feature, [gunslinger](ref/class/gunslinger) level 11th.\
**Benefit:** Pick a deed that you have access to and that you must spend grit to perform. Once per
round, you can perform this deed for 1 fewer grit point (minimum 0). You can reduce the cost of a
deed in this way only if you have at least 1 grit point.
