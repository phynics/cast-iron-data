You can draw upon aid from the ocean more frequently.\
**Prerequisites:** [Triton](ref/race/triton),
[summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), character level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** You gain two additional uses of
_[summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)_ per day.\
**Special:** You may take this feat multiple times. Each time you select it, you gain two additional
uses of _[summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)._
