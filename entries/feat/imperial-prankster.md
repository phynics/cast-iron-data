**Sources:** Pathfinder \#132: The Six-Legend Soul pg 73

You model your behavior on that of the legendary trickster, Grand Prince Beldam I, to confound
proponents of law and order.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 5 ranks, any chaotic alignment.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Bluff checks and combat maneuver checks against lawful
creatures. Against creatures with the lawful subtype, this bonus increases to +4.
