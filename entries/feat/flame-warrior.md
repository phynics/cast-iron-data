## Flame Warrior (Combat)

The raging inferno of your body flows through any sword or axe you wield.\
**Prerequisites:** Fire subtype, giant subtype.\
**Benefit:** You naturally transfer the heat of your body to any metal melee weapons you wield,
dealing 1d6 additional points of fire damage.
