Your spells of a certain element are more difficult to resist.\
**Benefit:** Choose one energy type (acid, cold, electricity, or fire). Add +1 to the Difficulty
Class for all saving throws against spells that deal damage of the energy type you select.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take
this feat, it applies to a new energy type.
