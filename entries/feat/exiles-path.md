A lifetime spent shunned by others and eschewing your heritage makes you resistant to efforts to pry
inside of your mind.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, when you fail a Will save against an enchantment spell or effect, you may
reroll that saving throw, but must take the reroll result even if it's worse.
