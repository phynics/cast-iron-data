## Extended Combat Meditation (Combat, Meditation)

You are able to glean insight from your momentary meditation for longer than most can.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Combat Meditation](ref/feat/combat-meditation),
[Meditation Master](ref/feat/meditation-master), character level 9th.\
**Benefit:** Add your Wisdom bonus (if any) to the number of rounds that you gain the benefits of
your meditation feats when using Combat Meditation.
