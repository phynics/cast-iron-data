## Bodyguard (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 151

Your swift strikes ward off enemies attacking nearby allies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes).\
**Benefit:** When an adjacent ally is attacked, you may use an attack of opportunity to attempt the
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to improve your ally's AC. You may not use the aid
another action to improve your ally's attack roll with this attack.\
**Normal:** Aid another is a standard action.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 114\
When you use an attack of opportunity to use an aid another action to improve an adjacent ally's
Armor Class, you can spend 1 or 2 stamina points. Increase the bonus to the ally's AC by an amount
equal to the number of stamina points you spent.
