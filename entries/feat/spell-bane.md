While your bane weapon is active, creatures that your bane affects find it more difficult to resist
your spells.\
**Prerequisites:** Bane class feature.\
**Benefit:** While your bane class feature is affecting a creature type, the saving throw's DCs for
your spells increase by +2 for creatures of that type.
