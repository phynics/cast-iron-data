## Improved Feinting Flurry (Combat)

**Sources:** Dragon Empires Primer pg 25

Your first melee attack throws your opponent completely off guard.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 17, flurry of blows class feature,
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), [Feinting Flurry](ref/feat/feinting-flurry), base
attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** While using Feinting Flurry, if you successfully [feint](ref/rule/feint), your opponent
is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC until the end of your turn.\
**Normal:** A successful feint causes your opponent to be denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against
your next attack.
