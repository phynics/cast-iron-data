## Fabulist (Grit)

You enjoy telling tall tales and gain confidence when others believe them.\
**Prerequisites:** [Amateur Gunslinger](ref/feat/amateur-gunslinger) feat or grit class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on Perform (act, comedy, oratory, and sing) skill checks, and
Perform is a class skill for you. In addition, each time you make a successful
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check to convince someone of a far-fetched or impossible story, you regain
1 grit point. Successfully lying to a friendly or helpful creature, or a creature that has fewer Hit
Dice than half your character level, doesn't restore grit.
