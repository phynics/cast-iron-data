## Dodge (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 122

Your training and reflexes allow you to react swiftly to avoid an opponents' attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 dodge bonus to your AC. A condition that makes you lose your Dex bonus to
AC also makes you lose the benefits of this feat.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
Your training and reflexes allow you to react swiftly to avoid an opponents' attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 dodge bonus to your AC. This bonus increases to +5 against attacks of
opportunity caused when you move out of or within a threatened tile. A condition that makes you lose
your Dex bonus to AC also makes you lose the benefits of this feat.
