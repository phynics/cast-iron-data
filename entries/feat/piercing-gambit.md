## Piercing Gambit (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Antihero's Handbook pg 10

You cast a spell destined to fail in order to help your ally overcome an enemy's
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance).\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Penetration](ref/feat/spell-penetration) or Bluff 3 ranks; caster level
1st.\
**Benefit:** You can expend one prepared spell or spell slot, as if you had cast the spell, to
weaken an enemy's defenses. Your spell has no effect, but a designated ally who also has this feat
gains a bonus on her caster level check to overcome spell resistance equal to half the level of the
spell or spell slot you expended, lasting until the start of your next turn.
