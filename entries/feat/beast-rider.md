You gain the service of a monstrous companion or mount.\
**Prerequisites:** Animal companion or mount class feature, character level 7th,
[half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** Select one of the following creature types:
[elephant](ref/companion/elephant-mastodon), [pteranodon](ref/companion/pteranodon),
[rhinoceros](ref/companion/rhinoceros), [stegosaurus](ref/companion/stegosaurus), or
[triceratops](ref/companion/triceratops). Add this creature type to your list of possible animal
companions or mounts. When summoning a creature of the selected type to serve as a mount or
companion, treat your effective druid level as if it were two levels higher (to a maximum of your
character level). If the creature is large enough for you to ride, it gains the
[combat training general purpose](ref/ability/animal-tricks) at no cost.
