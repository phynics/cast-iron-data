## Ruthless Opportunist (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 208

Your timing and fierceness take the enemy by surprise.\
**Prerequisites:** [Human](ref/race/human) (Chelaxian).\
**Benefit:** When a Chelish ally with this feat hits with an attack of opportunity against an enemy
both you and that ally are threatening, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your melee attack rolls
against that enemy for 1 round.
