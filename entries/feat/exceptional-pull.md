## Exceptional Pull (Combat)

You have mastered techniques to get the most out of composite bows.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Deadly Aim](ref/feat/deadly-aim), base attack bonus +3.\
**Benefit:** When you wield a ranged weapon that you are proficient with and that has a strength
rating, add 2 to the weapon's strength rating. You don't take a penalty on attack rolls for having a
Strength modifier lower than the strength rating of a weapon, provided you're proficient with that
weapon.
