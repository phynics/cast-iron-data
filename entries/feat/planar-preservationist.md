You know how to preserve and reconstitute extraplanar monsters as well as normal animals.\
**Prerequisites:** [Preservationist](ref/arc-alchemist/preservationist) alchemist archetype.\
**Benefit:** For every [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) extract you know, you
learn the equivalent [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell as an extract. If you later
learn other _[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ extracts, you automatically
learn the equivalent _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell as an extract.
