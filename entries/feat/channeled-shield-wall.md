**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 143

You draw upon your channel energy to enhance the protective ability of your shield and those of
allies while they are adjacent to you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 3d6, proficiency with a shield.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can spend a use of your channel energy to grant yourself a +2
deflection bonus while using a shield. This bonus lasts 1 minute per cleric level or effective
cleric level. While you benefit from this bonus, allies with shields also gain a +2 deflection bonus
while they are adjacent to you.
