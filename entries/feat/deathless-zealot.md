## Deathless Zealot (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 95

Only the most serious wounds can stop you.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, Con 17, [orc](ref/race/orc) or [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc),
[Deathless Initiate](ref/feat/deathless-initiate), [Deathless Master](ref/feat/deathless-master),
[Diehard](ref/feat/diehard), [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), [Ironhide](ref/feat/ironhide), base
attack bonus +12.\
**Benefit:** Whenever a creature rolls to confirm a critical hit against you, it must roll twice and
take the lowest result.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 117\
Whenever a creature threatens a critical hit against you and neither of its confirmation rolls would
succeed, you can spend 5 stamina points to make an attack of opportunity against that creature.
