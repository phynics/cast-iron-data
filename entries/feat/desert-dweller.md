The endless sands and waterless wastes are your home, and neither the heat nor dehydration presents
as lethal of a threat to you as it does to other travelers.\
**Prerequisites:** [Favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) (desert) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You treat [hot environments](ref/rule/heat-dangers) as though they were one step less
severe; if you have a similar ability from another feat, such as
[Torrid Tolerance](ref/feat/torrid-tolerance), the benefits stack and you treat hot conditions as if
they were two steps less severe. You need to consume only half the normal amount of water for a
creature of your size, and you gain a +4 bonus on Constitution checks to resist the effects of
thirst. You also gain a +4 bonus on saving throws and checks to avoid becoming
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) or [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) by glare or being deceived by a desert
mirage.
