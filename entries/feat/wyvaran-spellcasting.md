Your unique physiology makes you particularly adept at wielding draconic magic.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (arcana) 6 ranks, [wyvaran](ref/race/wyvaran).\
**Benefit:** Select one spell you can cast that you gained through your
[draconic](ref/sorcbloodline/draconic) sorcerer bloodline, [draconic](ref/blrgbloodline/draconic)
bloodrager bloodline, the [Scalykind](ref/domain/scalykind) domain, or the
[Dragon](ref/domain/scalykind) subdomain. It must be at least 1 spell level lower than the
highest-level spell you can cast. You can cast this spell as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) twice per day.
