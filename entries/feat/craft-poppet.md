## Craft Poppet (Item Creation)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 31

Some construct builders learn their art by creating poppets.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 1st.\
**Benefit:** You can craft [poppets](ref/family/poppet) and add augmentations to existing poppets
that you control. You are treated as having both
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor) and
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item) for the purpose of fulfilling the prerequisites
for [Craft Construct](ref/feat/craft-construct).
