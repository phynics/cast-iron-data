## Felling Escape (Combat)

Through the use of fluid contortions and manipulations of leverage, you can throw your opponent to
the ground after escaping a grapple.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip).\
**Benefit:** When you break an opponent's grapple with a combat maneuver check or Escape Artist
check, you can spend a swift action to make a trip attempt against that opponent.
