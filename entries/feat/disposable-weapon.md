You ignore the limitations of your equipment, striking harder despite the damage it does to your
weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, proficient with weapon.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use a melee or thrown weapon with the [fragile](ref/misc/fragile) weapon
special quality to score a critical threat against an opponent, you can give your weapon the
[broken](ref/misc/broken) condition to automatically confirm the critical hit.
