**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 10

Dryads trust you to bear their power and their bond.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 15, [Dryad's Apprentice](ref/feat/dryads-apprentice), character level 9th,
[gathlain](ref/race/gathlain).

You can cast [meld into stone](ref/spell/meld-into-stone) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day with a caster level equal to your
character level, except that you instead meld with wood. You can remain melded with the wood for up
to 24 hours. If you meld with a [dryad's](ref/monster/dryad) bonded tree, the dryad can treat you as
her bonded tree for a number of days equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) after you leave it;
the dryad retains her bond to her tree during this time as well.
