## Disengaging Shot (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 96

You make one last attack before beating a hasty retreat.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Disengaging Feint](ref/feat/disengaging-feint), [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use Disengaging Feint or
[Disengaging Flourish](ref/feat/disengaging-flourish), you can make a single melee attack against
one opponent you succeeded at feinting against. That opponent is denied his Dexterity bonus to AC
against this attack.
