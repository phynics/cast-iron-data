## Kitsune Tricks (Combat)

Your quick wit and even quicker maneuvers leave your opponents reeling.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13; [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise);
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick); [Kitsune Style](ref/feat/kitsune-style); base
attack bonus +3 or monk level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** While you are using the Kitsune Style feat, you can apply two different conditions with
a single [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver check. Removing both conditions
imparted in this way requires only one action.
