## Greater Subjective Slam (Combat)

Your gravitational impact is meteoric, and your ability to use the act of falling devastating.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15; Wis 15; [Subjective Mobility](ref/feat/subjective-mobility);
[Subjective Slam](ref/feat/subjective-slam); base attack bonus +8 or monk level 7th.\
**Benefit:** When using Subjective Slam, you take only a -1 penalty on the combat maneuver check for
every 100 feet of falling speed, and the maximum damage for such a slam increases to 12d6.
