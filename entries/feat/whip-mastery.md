## Whip Mastery (Combat)

Your superior expertise with this weapon does not provoke attacks of opportunity from your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (whip), base attack bonus +2.\
**Benefit:** You no longer provoke attacks of opportunity when attacking with a
[whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip). You can deal lethal damage with a whip, although you can still deal
nonlethal damage when you want. Further, you can deal damage with a whip despite a creature's armor
bonus or natural armor bonus.\
**Normal:** Attacking with a whip provokes attacks of opportunity as if you used a ranged weapon. A
whip deals no damage to a creature that has an armor bonus of +1 or natural armor bonus of +3.
