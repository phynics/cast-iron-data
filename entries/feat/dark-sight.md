With further modification, your eyes can pierce the gloom of even magical darkness.\
**Prerequisites:** [Gloom Sight](ref/feat/gloom-sight), [fetchling](ref/race/fetchling).\
**Benefit:** You gain the ability to see up to 15 feet clearly in magical darkness, such as that
created by the [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) spell.
