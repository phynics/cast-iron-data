## Intimidate Animals (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 115

You are skilled at intimidating animals with your bluster and fury.\
**Prerequisites:** [Greater Wild Empathy](ref/feat/greater-wild-empathy), Intimidate 5 ranks,
Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks, [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You add your Wisdom modifier on Intimidate checks against animals in addition to your
Charisma modifier. An animal with 4 or fewer Hit Dice is [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) instead
of [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) on a successful Intimidate check to demoralize it.
