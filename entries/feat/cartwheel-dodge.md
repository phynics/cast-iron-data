You use your knack for avoiding damage to reposition yourself in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** [Evasion](ref/ability/evasion) class feature,
[improved evasion](ref/ability/improved-evasion) class feature, acrobatics 12 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you successfully use improved evasion to avoid taking damage, you can move up to
half your speed as an immediate action. This movement provokes attacks of opportunity as normal.
