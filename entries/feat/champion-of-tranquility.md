## Champion of Tranquility (Alignment)

The harmony of law is your highest ideal.\
**Prerequisites:** 10 Hit Dice, lawful neutral alignment.\
**Benefit:** You can store a number of [affirmations](ref/rule/shifts-and-affirmations) up to your
Charisma bonus (minimum 1) to use at any time, not just within the next 24 hours. You can use an
affirmation to cast [calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as a standard action, with a caster level equal
to your Hit Dice. Lastly, you gain a +2 bonus on weapon and spell damage rolls against chaotic
creatures. This is an alignment-based effect.\
**Residual:** If you have this feat but you are no longer lawful neutral, you continue to gain the
+2 bonus on weapon and spell damage rolls against chaotic creatures.
