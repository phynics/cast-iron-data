## Back to Back (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 89

Your ally's eyes are your own, and yours are his.\
**Prerequisites:** Perception 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While you are flanked and adjacent to an ally with this feat, you receive a +2
circumstance bonus to AC against attacks from opponents [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) you.
