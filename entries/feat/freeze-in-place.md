## Freeze in Place (Combat)

You become an expert of hiding in plain sight.\
**Prerequisites:** Stealth 5 ranks, [wyrwood](ref/race/wyrwood).\
**Benefit:** You gain the [freeze](ref/umr/freeze) universal monster rule, allowing you to appear as
an ordinary statue. If you attack a creature unaware of you while using this ability, you double
your critical threat range. This effect doesn't stack with any other effect that expands the threat
range of your attacks or weapons.
