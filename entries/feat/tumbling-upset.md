## Tumbling Upset (Combat)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 25

You nimbly dodge beneath your opponent, creating an opportunity to upend your foe.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip), Acrobatics 7 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at an Acrobatics check to move through an enemy's space, you can spend
a swift action to attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver check against the opponent. Even
if you fail this check by 10 or more, you do not fall prone.\
**Normal:** If you fail a trip combat maneuver check by 10 or more, you are knocked prone.
