**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 159

Using the same divine energy as your lay on hands ability, you can heal others at a distance.\
**Prerequisites:** [Lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You may use your lay on hands to heal another creature at a range of 30 feet as a
standard action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity. You must be able to speak and have a
free hand to use this ability. The target heals half the amount they would have healed if you had
touched them, but gains the benefits of your mercies as normal.
