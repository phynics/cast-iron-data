You can assume the form of various varmints.\
**Prerequisites:** [Vampire](ref/template/vampire).\
**Benefit:** Choose one of the following forms: [bat swarm](ref/monster/bat-bat-swarm),
[centipede swarm](ref/monster/centipede-centipede-swarm),
[cockroach swarm](ref/monster/cockroach-cockroach-swarm),
[leech swarm](ref/monster/leech-leech-swarm), [rat swarm](ref/monster/rat-rat-swarm), or
[spider swarm](ref/monster/spider-spider-swarm). As a standard action, you can change into this
form.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take this feat, choose another
swarm type.
