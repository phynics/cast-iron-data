**Sources:** Pathfinder Society Primer pg 8

You learned the importance of an honest day's work while training as an initiate of the
[Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars).\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to attempt Day Job checks (see the _Guide to Pathfinder Society Organized
Play_ ^[1](ref/feat/improved-day-job#footnote-x)^).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +3 bonus on Day Job checks using one skill you choose when you take this
feat. This bonus stacks with other feats that grant a bonus to the chosen skill, including
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus).

###### ^1^ {#footnote-x}

Not included in this app. The Day Job appears in version 1.1 of that document, but not all versions.
