**Sources:** Pathfinder Society Primer pg 9

Word of your pre-Pathfinder accomplishments precedes you, and you know how to make the most of your
previous reputation.\
**Benefit:** When in a settlement of 5,000 people or more, treat your
[Fame](ref/rule/reputation-and-fame) score as 2 higher for the purposes of qualifying for
[Prestige Awards](ref/rule/faction-prestige) or purchasing equipment.

This feat doesn't grant you any additional Prestige Points.

**Normal:** Your Fame score is determined by the total number of Prestige Points earned over the
course of your career.
