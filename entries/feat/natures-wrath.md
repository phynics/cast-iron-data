**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 140

Though a plant creature, you are infused with the Blight Caller's primal rage against civilization.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 1, any nongood alignment, plant type.\
**Benefit:** You are no longer immune to effects that cause morale bonuses or effects with the
emotion descriptor, and you take a -1 penalty on saves against anger or rage emotion effects. You
gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls against humanoid creatures.
