You ferocity is quick but shorter lived.\
**Prerequisites:** Ferocity racial trait, [orc](ref/race/orc).\
**Benefit:** When you fall to 0 hit points or fewer, you lose 2 hit points each round, but you are
not [staggered](ref/misc/staggered). If you are in a rage (such as that caused by the
[barbarian](ref/class/barbarian) rage class feature), you instead only lose 1 hit point per round.
