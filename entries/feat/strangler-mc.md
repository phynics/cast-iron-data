You can choke foes with a lasso.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (lasso),
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (lasso).\
**Benefit:** When you successfully entangle a creature with a [lasso](ref/eq-weapon/lasso) and beat
the target's CMD by 5 or more, the target cannot speak above a whisper and must succeed at a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC = 20 + your CMB + the spell level) to cast a spell
with a verbal component, use a command word item, or use any magic requiring speech. Additionally,
the DC of an Escape Artist check to slip free of a lasso you are using equals 15 or your CMD,
whichever is higher.
