## Legendary Teamwork (Mythic)

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 68

You and your allies fight with great coordination.\
**Prerequisites:** Any two teamwork feats.\
**Benefit:** Any fixed numeric bonuses you gain from teamwork feats are increased by 1. This doesn't
apply to variable numeric bonuses or to effects that are not numeric bonuses, such as additional
attacks of opportunity, movement, or other extra actions.
