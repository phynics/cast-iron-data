## Destroyer's Blessing (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 56, Orcs of Golarion pg 24

Breaking things adds to your power.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [orc](ref/race/orc), rage class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you are raging and you succeed at a [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver, you
regain 1 round of rage. If the sunder attempt causes the object to gain the
[broken](ref/misc/broken) condition, you heal 1 hit point. You can only gain these benefits once per
round.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 117\
You can spend 2 stamina points to regain 1 round of rage after reducing a foe whose CR is at least
half your character level to below 0 hit points, rather than upon successfully sundering a target.
