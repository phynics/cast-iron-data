## Merciful Spell (Metamagic)

Your damaging spells subdue rather than kill.\
**Benefit:** You can alter spells that inflict damage to inflict nonlethal damage instead. Spells
that inflict damage of a particular type (such as fire) inflict nonlethal damage of that same type.
A merciful spell does not use up a higher-level spell slot than the spell's actual level.
