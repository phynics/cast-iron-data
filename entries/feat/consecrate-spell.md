## Consecrate Spell (Metamagic)

**Sources:** Blood of Angels pg 25

You infuse spells with heavenly power, drastically increasing their effectiveness against would-be
heralds of evil.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar), able to prepare or cast
[consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate).\
**Benefit:** A consecrated spell is treated as a maximized spell against evil creatures and
creatures with the evil subtype. Against all other creatures, this feat does not modify the spell in
any way (for example, a consecrated [fireball](ref/spell/fireball) acts as a normal fireball against
neutral or good creatures). This feat does not stack with [Maximize Spell](ref/feat/maximize-spell).
A consecrated spell uses up a spell slot two levels higher than the spell's actual level.
