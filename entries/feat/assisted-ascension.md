## Assisted Ascension (Teamwork)

You and your allies have practiced scaling heights together.\
**Prerequisites:** Climb 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a Climb check, allies within 20 feet who also have this feat can
climb 5 feet along the same surface as an immediate action. You gain a +5 bonus on Climb checks to
catch any falling ally who also has this feat.
