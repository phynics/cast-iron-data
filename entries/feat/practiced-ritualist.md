You are an experienced leader among ritual casters.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (arcana, history, planes, and religion) 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on skill checks to perform
[occult rituals](ref/rule/occult-rituals), and on Intelligence checks to learn the method of casting
an occult ritual. If you are a ritual's primary caster, the DC for saving throws against the
ritual's effects is 2 higher. If the ritual has a minimum number of required secondary casters, that
number is decreased by 1.
