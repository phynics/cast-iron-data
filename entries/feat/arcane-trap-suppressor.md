**Sources:** Dungeoneer's Handbook pg 8

Your magic can suppress magic traps longer than usual.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) or
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater) as a spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** When you target a magic trap with _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ or
_[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater),_ if your caster level check exceeds the
Disable Device DC of the trap, the trap is disabled for 1d4 minutes.\
**Normal:** _[Dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ suppresses an item's magical properties for 1d4
rounds.
