You are good at avoiding unwanted attention and slipping out of bonds.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on all [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) and
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) skill checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in one of these skills, the
bonus increases to +4 for that skill.
