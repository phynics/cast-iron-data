**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 15, Qadira, Gateway to the East pg 9, Qadira, Jewel of the East
pg 15

You can use the momentum of your mount to carry your weapon through one foe and into another.\
**Prerequisites:** [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat), Ride 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use the [Ride-By Attack](ref/feat/ride-by-attack) feat, if your attack hits,
you can also make an attack against a target adjacent to your original target. You take a -5 penalty
on this additional attack, which does not benefit from any effects of a charge.
