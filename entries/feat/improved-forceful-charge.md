Enemies fall before this animal's charge.\
**Prerequisites:** [Forceful Charge](ref/feat/forceful-charge),
[Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), animal
companion.\
**Benefit:** This animal can bull rush a target that is up to two size categories larger than
itself. If it pushes the target at least 10 feet as a result of this bull rush, it can attempt a
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver check against the target as a free action. This trip combat
maneuver provokes attacks of opportunity as normal.
