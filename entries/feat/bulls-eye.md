## Bull's Eye (Blood Hex)

**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 29

Your ranged attacks unerringly seek those you have cursed.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, Int 13, [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility),
[Shot on the Run](ref/feat/shot-on-the-run), base attack bonus +4, Use Magic Device 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can curse a creature you've dealt damage to with a ranged
weapon attack since the beginning of your last turn. The target must succeed at a Reflex save, or
you don't take penalties on ranged weapon attacks against the creature for the first range increment
and you ignore any bonus to the creature's Armor Class from [cover](ref/rule/cover). The creature
still benefits from total cover. This effect lasts for 1 minute.\
**Special:** A shaman or witch with this [blood hex](ref/rule/feats-class) can use it against a
target she has damaged with any ranged attack since her last turn, and applies its benefits to any
ranged attack she makes against an affected creature.
