Your channeled energy is truly liberating.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 7d6, worshiper of
[Cayden Cailean](ref/faith/cayden-cailean).\
**Benefit:** When you channel positive energy, any creature healed by the channel also gains the
effect of [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) for a number of rounds equal to your
Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round). This effect has a caster level equal to your effective cleric
level.
