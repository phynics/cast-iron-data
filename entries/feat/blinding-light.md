You use your impenetrable luminescence to disorient harbingers of darkness.\
**Prerequisites:** [Inner Light (BoA)](ref/feat/inner-light-boa), [aasimar](ref/race/aasimar),
[daylight](ref/spell/daylight) [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** Whenever an enemy unsuccessfully attempts to counter or dispel your daylight spell-like
ability, it must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your
Charisma modifier) or become [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round as its mind is overwhelmed with
celestial light. This is a supernatural mind-affecting effect.
