**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 158

You can marshal vast armies of the undead to serve you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (necromancy), the ability to cast
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead) or [command undead](ref/spell/command-undead).\
**Benefit:** When you cast _[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead)_ or use the
[Command Undead](ref/feat/command-undead) feat, you are considered to be four levels higher when
determining the number of Hit Dice you animate. When you cast
_[command undead](ref/spell/command-undead),_ your duration is doubled.

### Mythic Undead Master

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 76\
Through your fearsome mythic nature, you have matchless power over the undead.\
**Prerequisites:** Undead Master.\
**Benefit:** When you cast _[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead)_ or use the Command Undead feat,
add your tier to your caster level when determining the number of Hit Dice of undead you animate.
This stacks with the increased caster level of Undead Master. When you cast
_[command undead](ref/spell/command-undead),_ your duration is tripled rather than doubled.
