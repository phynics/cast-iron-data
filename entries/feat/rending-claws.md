## Rending Claws (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 168

Your claw attacks do greater harm to your enemy.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, two claw natural weapon attacks, base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** If you hit a creature with two claw attacks in the same turn, the second claw attack
deals an additional 1d6 points of damage. This damage is precision damage and is not multiplied on a
critical hit. You can use this feat once per round.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 130\
When you make two claw attacks but hit with only one, you can spend 5 stamina points to deal the
precision damage granted by this feat anyway.
