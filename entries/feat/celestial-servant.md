**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 88

Rather than being a normal animal or beast, your companion or familiar hails from the heavenly
realms.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar), [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion),
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar), or mount class feature.\
**Benefit:** Your animal companion, familiar, or mount gains the [celestial](ref/template/celestial)
template and becomes a magical beast, though you may still treat it as an animal when using Handle
Animal, wild empathy, or any other spells or class abilities that specifically affect animals.
