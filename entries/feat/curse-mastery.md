## Curse Mastery (Item Mastery)

You can coax curses from necromantic magic items.\
**Prerequisites:** Use Magic Device 3 ranks, base Fortitude save bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You can cause an item that has a necromancy spell of 3rd level or higher in its
construction requirements to cast [bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse). You can use this feat once
per day, plus an additional time per day at base Fortitude save bonus +9 and +12.
