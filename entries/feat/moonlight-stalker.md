## Moonlight Stalker (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 110

You are adept at using shadows to conceal your attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight),
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), Bluff 3 ranks, [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) or
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) racial trait.\
**Benefit:** While you have [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from an opponent, you gain a +2
bonus on attack and damage rolls against that opponent.
