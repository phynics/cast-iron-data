Your deity infuses your channeled energy with bounty.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 7d6, worshiper of
[Erastil](ref/faith/erastil).\
**Benefit:** When you channel positive energy, you can cause plants in the affected area to grow as
though targeted by the overgrowth version of [plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth) (caster level
equal to your effective cleric level).
