## Phalanx Formation (Combat)

You are trained to use long weapons in tight formations.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** When you wield a [reach](ref/misc/reach) weapon with which you are proficient, allies
don't provide soft cover to opponents you attack with reach.\
**Normal:** Attacking a target that is beyond another creature, even an ally, can result in the
target having soft cover from you.
