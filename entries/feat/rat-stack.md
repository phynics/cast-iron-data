## Rat Stack (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Blood of the Beast pg 23

You coordinate efficiently in tight spaces.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, swarming racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You can occupy the same square with up to two other creatures that also have this feat.
If all participants also have the [Tunnel Rat](ref/feat/tunnel-rat) feat, up to four creatures can
share the same space.
