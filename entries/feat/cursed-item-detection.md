**Sources:** Dungeoneer's Handbook pg 12

Your arcane prowess is so great that even cursed items fail to befuddle you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) 5 ranks, ability to cast
[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) as a spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on checks made to identify the properties of magic items. In
addition, you need only exceed the DC of these checks by 5 in order to determine whether or not an
item is cursed.\
**Normal:** Unless the check made to identify a cursed item exceeds the DC by 10 or more, the item's
curse cannot be detected.
