## Mobile Bulwark Style (Combat, Style)

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 12

You are able to swing your tower shield around quickly.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus),
[Tower Shield Proficiency](ref/feat/tower-shield-proficiency), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** While using a [tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield), add the shield's bonus to AC
(including its enhancement bonus) to your CMD against [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) and
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuvers. While using this style, you can use a tower shield to
grant you total [cover](ref/rule/cover) along one edge of your space as a move action.
