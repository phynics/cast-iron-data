## Merciful Takedown (Combat)

You are adept at removing your defeated opponents from the fight while avoiding their deaths.\
**Benefit:** When you perform a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) and deal only nonlethal damage,
instead of attempting a Fortitude save to avoid death, the target must instead attempt a Fortitude
save to avoid falling unconscious for 1 hour. The DC of this save is the same as that of a lethal
_coup de grace._
