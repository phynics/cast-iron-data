You have honed your mind to harness psychic power.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 11, [Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity).\
**Benefit:** You gain the ability to cast a 0-level knack from the psychic class's spell list. You
can cast this spell three times per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The
caster level for this ability is equal to your character level, and the save DC is equal to 10 +
your Intelligence modifier.\
**Special:** You can choose this feat multiple times, choosing a different knack each time.
