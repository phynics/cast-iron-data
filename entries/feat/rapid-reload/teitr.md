**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
Choose the crossbows or thrown [weapon groups](ref/ability/weapon-groups) listed under the fighter's
Weapon Training class feature. You can reload such weapons quickly.\
**Prerequisites:** Proficiency with at least one weapon from the selected weapon group.\
**Benefit:** The time required for you to reload a weapon from the selected group is reduced to a
free action (for a hand crossbow, light crossbow, [sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling), or
[halfling sling staff](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-sling-staff)) or a move action (for a heavy crossbow).
Reloading no longer provokes an attack of opportunity. For all these weapons but the heavy crossbow,
you may fire that weapon as many times in a full-attack action as you could attack if you were using
a bow.

**Normal:** A character without this feat needs a move action to reload a hand crossbow, light
crossbow, sling, or halfling sling staff, or a full-round action to load a heavy crossbow.

**Special:** You can gain Rapid Reload multiple times. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a
new weapon group.
