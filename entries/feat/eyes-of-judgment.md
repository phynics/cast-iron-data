**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 150

The true motives of creatures cannot escape your discerning gaze.\
**Prerequisites:** Detect alignment class feature, caster level 6th.\
**Benefit:** When using your detect alignment class feature, you may spend 3 rounds studying a
creature within 60 feet. You cannot take any other actions while doing this. After that time has
passed, you learn the alignment of the creature.
