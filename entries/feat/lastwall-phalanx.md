## Lastwall Phalanx (Teamwork)

**Sources:** Champions of Purity pg 23

When battling the terrifying hordes of Belkzen, you find strength in your shield brothers and
sisters.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +3, good alignment.\
**Benefit:** You gain a sacred bonus to your AC against the attacks of evil creatures and a sacred
bonus to saves against the spells and abilities of evil creatures equal to the number of adjacent
allies who also have this feat.
