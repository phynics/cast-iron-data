## Improved Sunder (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 128

You are skilled at damaging your foes' weapons and armor.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a [sunder](ref/rule/sunder)
combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks made to sunder an item. You also
receive a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense whenever an opponent tries to sunder your gear.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a sunder combat maneuver.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 124\
When you are the target of a sunder combat maneuver, you can spend a number of stamina points up to
your Strength or Dexterity bonus, whichever is greater. Your CMD increases by that number for that
sunder attempt.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat has been removed, replaced with the new [Powerful Maneuvers](ref/rule/powerful-maneuvers)
feat.
