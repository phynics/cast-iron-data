You are adept at defending yourself during psychic duels.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) or the ability to
cast psychic spells or use psychic [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), character
level 3rd.\
**Benefit:** Each time you enter a psychic duel, you gain a number of temporary hit points equal to
your manifesting level. These temporary hit points last until the end of the psychic duel.
