You can make an additional ranged attack.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot).\
**Benefit:** When making a full-attack action with a ranged weapon, you can fire one additional time
this round at your highest bonus. All of your attack rolls take a -2 penalty when using Rapid Shot.
