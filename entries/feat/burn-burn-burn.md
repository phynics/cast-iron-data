You take the goblin love of arson and fire play to a whole new level.\
**Prerequisites:** Disable Device 1 rank, [goblin](ref/race/goblin).\
**Benefit:** You deal an extra 1d4 points of fire damage when you attack with fire from an
alchemical or nonmagical source (such as with [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire) or
[torches](ref/eq-misc/torch)) and gain a +4 competence bonus on Reflex saving throws made to avoid
[catching on fire](ref/rule/catching-on-fire) or to put yourself out when on fire. Additional damage
caused by this feat does not apply to magical attacks (such as an [alchemist's](ref/class/alchemist)
bomb) or to splash damage.
