**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 189

Your wounds move you into a deeper frenzy.\
**Prerequisites:** Blood frenzy ability, [sahuagin](ref/monster/sahuagin).\
**Benefit:** You no longer take a penalty to AC while in a blood frenzy. In addition, if you strike
an enemy with both of your claw attacks - or two or more claw attacks if you have extra limbs -
while in a blood frenzy, you can [rend](ref/umr/rend) (as the special attack), dealing an amount of
additional damage equal to your claw damage plus 1-1/2 times your Strength bonus (minimum 0).
