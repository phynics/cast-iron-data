Your deity blesses any strike you make with that deity's favored weapon.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature,
[Channel Smite](ref/feat/channel-smite), proficiency with your deity's favored weapon.\
**Benefit:** With your deity's favored weapon, you can use your Wisdom modifier instead of your
Strength or Dexterity modifier on attack rolls.
