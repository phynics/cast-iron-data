## Gate Breaker (Combat)

**Sources:** Path of the Hellknight pg 38

Your strikes against objects are especially vicious.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you would deal damage to an object, you deal normal damage plus an additional
amount equal to your Strength modifier. This applies to both unattended objects and those you damage
as part of a successful [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver check.
