## Cracking the Shell (Combat, Combination)

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 27

The tip of your weapon pierces both the body and the spirit.\
**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with piercing weapon.\
**Benefit:** When you deal damage with a melee attack with a piercing weapon to an opponent you have
already damaged this round, you can also reduce the value of that target's
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) or energy resistances by 1 until the start of your
next turn. This reduction stacks with itself.
