## Ambush Sense (Combat)

You live in dangerous times, and are alert for betrayal or unexpected attack.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [trap sense](ref/ability/trap-sense) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain a bonus on Perception checks made to determine awareness for the surprise
round of combat, and a dodge bonus to AC in any surprise round in which you get to act. These
bonuses are equal to the bonus you gain from trap sense.
