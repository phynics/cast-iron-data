**Sources:** Champions of Balance pg 33

You can summon creatures that embody the forces of balance.\
**Prerequisites:** Chaotic neutral, lawful neutral, or neutral alignment.\
**Benefit:** When casting a [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell, you gain access to the
list of neutral creatures on this page. You may also summon creatures from the standard
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ list and apply the
[counterpoised creature simple template](ref/template/counterpoised) to applicable creatures instead
of the [celestial](ref/template/celestial) or [fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) template. Creatures
you summon from the list on this page and creatures you summon with the counterpoised template gain
a +2 resistance bonus on Will saves.
