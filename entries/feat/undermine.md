## Undermine (Teamwork)

**Sources:** People of the Sands pg 9

You and your allies take advantage of shifting terrain to unbalance enemies.\
**Benefit:** When you use a combat maneuver to move a creature into or through a square adjacent to
an ally with this feat, your opponent treats all squares adjacent to that ally as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) during his next turn. In addition, the DC of any
of your opponent's Acrobatics checks in the affected squares increases by 2 for each of your allies
with this feat who are adjacent to him.
