## Never Conquered, Forever Feared (Story)

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#131: The Reaper's Right Hand pg 78

You are dauntless in battle, and your lineage echoes with awe-inspiring power.\
**Prerequisites:** Be a member of House Heskillar, or have the Adopted by Dragons
[adopted background](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more), or the Path of Righteous Rage
(inquisitor) or Proud Heritage (sorcerer) [class background](ref/rule/class-background-tables).\
**Benefit:** Your stern demeanor gives you a +2 bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks.
If you have 10 or more ranks in Intimidate, this bonus increases to +4. You also gain a +2 bonus on
saves against paralysis and sleep effects.\
**Goal:** You must individually slay an appropriate number of significant foes in succession,
without retreating or withdrawing from a fight.\
**Completion Benefit:** You can display your imposing presence as a free action when you take an
offensive action, such as an [attack](ref/rule/attack) or a [charge](ref/rule/charge). Opponents
within 30 feet of you must succeed at a Will save or become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 3d6
rounds. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Charisma modifier. This
ability does not cause opponents that are already shaken to become frightened, and opponents with
more Hit Dice than your character level are immune to the effect. This is a mind-affecting fear
effect.
