## Improved Swap Places (Combat, Teamwork)

When you switch places with your comrade, your sizes don't matter.\
**Prerequisites:** [Swap Places](ref/feat/swap-places).\
**Benefit:** When you and your ally use Swap Places, your ally can be up to one size larger or
smaller than you, and your movement into the ally's square does not provoke an attack of
opportunity. If your ally cannot fit into the space you had been occupying and there are no
available adjacent squares to accommodate the rest of the ally's space, the ally must
[squeeze](ref/misc/squeezing). Alternatively, as part of its movement, the ally can attempt a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) combat maneuver against a creature that occupies a space your ally
would occupy, but this bull rush cannot move the creature more than 5 feet.\
**Normal:** Using Swap Places requires you and your ally to be the same size, and your movement into
the ally's square provokes attacks of opportunity.
