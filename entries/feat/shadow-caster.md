**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 105

Your command over shadow and darkness create longer-lasting spell effects.\
**Prerequisites:** Caster level 1st, [drow](ref/race/drow).\
**Benefit:** When you cast spells of the shadow subschool or spells with the darkness or shadow
descriptor, you are considered two levels higher when determining the duration of those spells.
