## Quick Draw (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 131

You can draw weapons faster than most.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You can draw a weapon as a free action instead of as a move action. You can draw a
hidden weapon (see the [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) skill) as a move action. A
character who has selected this feat may throw weapons at his full normal rate of attacks (much like
a character with a bow). Alchemical items, potions, scrolls, and wands cannot be drawn quickly using
this feat.\
**Normal:** Without this feat, you may draw a weapon as a move action, or (if your base attack bonus
is +1 or higher) as a free action as part of movement. Without this feat, you can draw a hidden
weapon as a standard action.
