## Excoriating Stare (Combat, Stare)

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 133

Your painful stare burns through your opponents.\
**Prerequisites:** Mesmerist level 11th, painful stare class feature.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you trigger a painful stare, the target must succeed at a Will saving throw
(DC = 10 + 1/2 your mesmerist level + your Charisma modifier) or become
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) by the pain for 1 round.
