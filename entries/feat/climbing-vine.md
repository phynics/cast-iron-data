You can climb like a vine.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [vine leshy](ref/race/vine-leshy).\
**Benefit:** You gain a climb speed of 10 feet. If you already have a natural climb speed, your
climb speed increases by 10 feet. You can take this feat multiple times, but your climb speed can
never exceed your base speed.
