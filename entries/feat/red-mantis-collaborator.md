**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 13

You pass along private details to the [Red Mantis](ref/rule/aff-assassins), and they protect you in
return.\
**Prerequisites:** [Associate](ref/feat/associate) (Red Mantis).\
**Benefit:** The Red Mantis can help you hide from trouble in one of their secret safe houses.
Anyone attempting Diplomacy or Survival checks to locate you during this time takes a -10 penalty,
and anyone attempting to scry upon you or magically divine your location must first succeed at a
caster level check (DC = 15 + your character level). You can stay in a Mantis safe house for up to 1
day per character level before you are forced to vacate.
