## Lonely Death (Origin)

**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 17

Whether because you were lost in wilderness or quarantined during a plague, you died alone,
unnoticed, and unmourned. Your body might not have been discovered for weeks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker).\
**Benefit:** Twice per day, if you are reduced below 0 hit points, as an immediate action you can
move up to twice your base speed. If this brings you into [cover](ref/rule/cover) or
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment), you can also attempt a Stealth check to hide. At 11th level,
you can use this feat two additional times per day.
