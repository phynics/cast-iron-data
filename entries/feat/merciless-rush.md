## Merciless Rush (Combat)

Though hordes stand against you, they rarely stand long.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), worshiper of
[Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug).\
**Benefit:** When you [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) a creature and your check exceeds the target's
CMD by 5 or more, you deal damage equal to your Strength modifier to that target.
