## Diva Style (Combat, Style)

You employ song, dance, or some other method of performance to distract your enemies from the battle
at hand.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13; base attack bonus +1 or bard level 1st.\
**Benefit:** While using this style, you can use any Perform skill in place of
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) to feint in combat. You can attempt a Perform check to feint as a move
action when you begin a bardic performance.
