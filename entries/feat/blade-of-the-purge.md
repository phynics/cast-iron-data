Emulating the fervor of Grand Prince Stavian I, you can identify a religion you perceive to be
opposed to your own and empower your weapons to strike deadly blows against its adherents.\
**Prerequisites:** Bane class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you activate your bane class feature, you can select a deity or religion other
than your own. Your bane effect applies only to followers of that deity or religion.
