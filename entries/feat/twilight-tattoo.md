You have a special tattoo that identifies you as a Twilight Talon when you speak a designated
command word.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, affiliated with the Twilight Talons.\
**Benefit:** You gain an invisible magical tattoo that identifies you as a member of the Twilight
Talons. When you speak the command word, the tattoo becomes visible for 1 round before fading again.
The tattoo has no magical aura when not visible and is not an illusion; it can't be observed through
magical means. (The command word causes the tattoo to manifest rather than ending the magic
concealing it.) Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus on Disguise checks, on Sleight of Hand checks to
hide small objects on your person, and on Will saving throws against attempts to scry upon you or
read your mind.
