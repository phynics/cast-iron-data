## Arcane Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 118

You draw upon your arcane power to enhance your weapons with magical energy.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast arcane spells.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can imbue your weapons with a fraction of your power. For 1
round, your weapons deal +1 damage and are treated as magic for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). For every five caster levels you possess, this bonus
increases by +1, to a maximum of +5 at 20th level.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 114\
You can spend any number of stamina points to increase the duration of this feat's effects by 1
round for each stamina point spent.

### Mythic Arcane Strike

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 55\
Using your amazing arcane talents, you can deliver devastating blows infused with magical energy.\
**Prerequisites:** Arcane Strike.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you use Arcane Strike to enhance your weapons, the effect lasts for 1 minute
instead of 1 round. If you expend one use of mythic power when using Arcane Strike, you can also add
any one magic weapon special ability with a base price modifier of +1 to your weapons. This base
price modifier increases to +2 at 4th tier, +3 at 7th tier, and +4 at 10th tier.
