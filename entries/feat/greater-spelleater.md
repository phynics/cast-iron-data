Your ability to defeat magic has grown stronger with use.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, character level 5th, [ghoran](ref/race/ghoran), spelleater racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Once per week, instead of casting [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), you can cast
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater). This exhausts your daily use of
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ granted by the spelleater racial trait. In all other
respects, this ability is treated as the spelleater racial trait.
