**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 68

You move on ice and snow as if it were dry ground.\
**Prerequisites:** [Frost giant](ref/monster/giant-frost-frost-giant).\
**Benefit:** You can move across icy and snowy surfaces without penalty, and don't need to attempt
Acrobatics checks to run or charge on ice. You gain a +4 bonus on Climb checks to climb ice.
