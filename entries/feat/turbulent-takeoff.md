## Turbulent Takeoff (Combat)

When you take off, you create a burst of wind that keeps foes from attacking you.\
**Prerequisites:** Fly 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When you use your fly speed to leave the ground, you can create a blast of wind. Each
creature that threatens you cannot make attacks of opportunities as you move away unless it succeeds
at a Reflex save (DC = 10 + your skill ranks in Fly + your Dexterity modifier). A creature that fail
this Reflex save by 10 or more is knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone). You must begin your move action
standing on the ground to use this ability.
