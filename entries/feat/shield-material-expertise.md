## Shield Material Expertise (Combat, Shield Mastery)

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 19

You emphasize and exploit the potential of the special material from which your shield is made.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus), base attack bonus +6 or fighter level 4th.\
**Benefit:** When wielding a shield made of one of the materials listed below, you can use the
listed ability twice per day.

> [Darkwood:](ref/eq-material/darkwood) As a free action as part of an attack with your shield, you
> can drive splinters of your shield into your opponent, dealing an amount of bleed damage equal to
> 1 + the shield's enhancement bonus.
>
> [Dragonhide:](ref/eq-material/dragonhide) As an immediate action when you take energy damage of
> the type to which the dragonhide shield is immune, you can reduce the amount of damage you take by
> 10 hit points.
>
> [Living Steel:](ref/eq-material/living-steel) As a free action as part of an attack with your
> shield, you can attempt a sunder combat maneuver check against a metal weapon wielded by your
> target. If you succeed at the attempt, the weapon gains the broken condition; if the weapon
> already has the broken condition, it is destroyed.
