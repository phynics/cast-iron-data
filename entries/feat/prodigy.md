**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 154

You are naturally skilled at arts, professions, and the acquisition of knowledge.\
**Benefit:** Choose two Craft, Perform, or Profession skills in any combination (two Craft skills, a
Craft skill and a Perform skill, and so on). You receive a +2 bonus on checks with these skills. If
you have 10 or more ranks in any one of these skills, the bonus increases to +4 for that skill.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the
feat, it applies to two new skills.
