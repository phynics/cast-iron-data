## Wanderer's Fortune (Conduit)

**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 33

The promise of Elysium's freedom liberates you from all forms of restraint, allowing you to move as
you will and to avoid numerous kinds of restraints that would otherwise vex a traveler or explorer.\
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you gain the benefits of
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) until the end of your turn or until you benefit
from the effects to escape a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) or magical restraint, whichever comes
first.

You can use this feat's benefit once per day, plus an additional time per day for every 5 ranks you
have in Knowledge (planes).
