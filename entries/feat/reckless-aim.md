## Reckless Aim (Combat)

**Sources:** Blood of Fiends pg 25

Your lack of regard for others proves a boon when you fire projectiles into melee.\
**Prerequisites:** [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot),
[Precise Shot](ref/feat/precise-shot).\
**Benefit:** When you shoot or throw ranged weapons at an opponent engaged in melee, you can choose
to take a -1 penalty to your AC and gain a +2 competence bonus on your attack roll. However, when
you roll a natural 1 on a ranged attack roll made with this bonus, you automatically hit a random
adjacent creature that threatens your intended target.
