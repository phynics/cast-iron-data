**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 209

You can change the nature of your toxic spittle to put your enemies to sleep.\
**Prerequisites:** [Vishkanya](ref/race/vishkanya).\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you may alter the effects of your venom so the target falls
unconscious. This changes the initial and secondary effect of your venom to the following: _initial
effect_ [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d4 rounds; _secondary effect_ unconsciousness for 1
minute. You must make the decision to alter your venom before you apply it to a weapon.\
**Normal:** Vishkanya venom deals Dexterity damage.
