Unlike others of your kind, you are able to pull your prey toward yourself.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 17, sticky tongue.\
**Benefit:** Your tongue attack gains the [pull](ref/umr/pull) special ability. At the start of your
turn, if your tongue is attached to a target of your size or smaller and you succeed at a combat
maneuver check, you can pull the target 5 feet closer to you as free action.
