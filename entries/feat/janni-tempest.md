## Janni Tempest (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 106

Your gale of attacks easily throws your opponents off balance.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike),
[Janni Style](ref/feat/janni-style), Acrobatics 5 ranks, Perform (dance) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** While you are using the Janni Style feat, whenever you make an unarmed attack and hit
an opponent, you gain a +4 bonus on checks made to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or
[trip](ref/rule/trip) that opponent, as long as the combat maneuver is your next attack by the end
of your turn. You do not provoke an attack of opportunity from the target of the maneuver.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 124\
When you hit an opponent with an unarmed strike while using Janni Style, you can spend 2 stamina
points to increase this feat's bonus by 2. You can also spend 2 stamina points to apply the feat's
bonus on checks to a drag or reposition combat maneuver instead of a bull rush or trip.
