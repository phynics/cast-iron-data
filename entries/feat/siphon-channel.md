You can channel negative energy to feed off the suffering of others.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 3d6, worshiper of
[Ghlaunder](ref/faith/ghlaunder).\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you channel negative energy to damage living creatures, you gain a
number of [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to half the die result of your
channeled energy + 1 per creature affected by the channeled energy. For each affected creature that
did not succeed at its saving throw against the channeled energy, you gain an additional temporary
hit point. These temporary hit points disappear after 1 hour.
