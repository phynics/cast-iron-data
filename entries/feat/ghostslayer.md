## Ghostslayer (Combat)

Your blade is keen enough to hew incorporeal creatures.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, Knowledge (religion) 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can imbue a weapon you wield with spiritual energies for 1
round. An imbued weapon damages incorporeal creatures as though it were magical. If the imbued
weapon has an enhancement bonus of +2 or greater, it functions as a
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon. A weapon imbued in this way can deal
precision damage (such as [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) damage) to incorporeal creatures.
