## Seize Advantage (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 156

You can take advantage of your foes when they overcommit on their attacks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Opportune parry and riposte](ref/swashdeed/opportune-parry-and-riposte) deed.\
**Benefit:** When your foe is using [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack) and you successfully use
the _opportune parry and riposte_ deed to parry its attack and then respond with a riposte, you add
your foe's current bonus on damage rolls from Power Attack to your own damage roll, but you do not
take the penalty to hit from your foe's Power Attack.
