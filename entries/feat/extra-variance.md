**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 9

You can augment your channeled energy in new ways.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channeling Variance](ref/feat/channeling-variance) or
[variant channeling](ref/ability/variant-channeling) ability,
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You gain one additional variant channeling ability that matches one of your deity's
domains, subdomains, or areas of concern. Three times per day, you can apply the modifications of
this variant channel energy, reducing the damage dealt or healing performed by your channeled energy
as normal. You cannot apply more than one variant channeling ability to your channeled energy at
once.\
**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times. Each time you do, you gain another variant
channeling ability. If you have chosen Channeling Variance twice, you can use any variant channeling
ability chosen with this feat whenever you channel energy.
