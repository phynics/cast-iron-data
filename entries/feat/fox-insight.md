## Fox Insight (Combat)

Your fox-like movements protect you from your opponents' attempts to manipulate you.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Fox Style](ref/feat/fox-style).\
**Benefit:** While using Fox Style, you can use your base attack bonus in place of your ranks in
Sense Motive to determine your Sense Motive skill bonus when foes attempt to feint against you, and
creatures attempting to demoralize you don't gain a bonus for being bigger than you (though they
still take a penalty for being smaller). If your Intelligence score is at least 19, the DC to
demoralize or feint you increases by 4.
