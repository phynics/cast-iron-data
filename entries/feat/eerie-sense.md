**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 211

You have a knack for sniffing out undead.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast [detect undead](ref/spell/detect-undead) or
[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil) as a spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), worshiper of [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma).\
**Benefit:** At the beginning of your turn each round, if you are within 60 feet of an undead
creature or a haunt, you get a chill down your spine and instinctively sense that something eerie is
very near. This sense does not alert you to the direction of the undead or haunt. Anything that can
block detect undead can likewise block this sense.
