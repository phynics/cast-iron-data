**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 18

Your system recuperates from the effects of poisons with astonishing speed.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dwarf](ref/race/dwarf), hardy racial trait.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you succeed at a saving throw against poison, you heal 1 point of ability
damage of the type dealt by the poison. Whenever you heal ability damage naturally or magically, you
heal 1 additional point of ability damage. This feat has no effect on penalties to ability scores or
ability drain.
