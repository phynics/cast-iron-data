## Kraken Throttle (Combat)

**Sources:** Melee Tactics Toolbox pg 13

You tighten your grip around your target's throat.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Kraken Style](ref/feat/kraken-style),
base attack bonus +5 or monk level 5th.\
**Benefit:** The damage that you deal with the Kraken Style feat on a successful
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver check increases to an amount equal to your Wisdom
bonus + 2.

While using this style, you can choke your opponent when you successfully maintain a grapple instead
of choosing to damage, move, pin, or tie up your opponent. This suffocates your opponent. The
grappled opponent can take a breath during any round in which you do not maintain the grapple.
