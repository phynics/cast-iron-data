## Serren's Masterstroke (Combat)

**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 26

When bypassing a foe's defenses while feinting, you can deal devastating damage.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (Aldori dueling
sword), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (Aldori dueling sword), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** Once per round when you hit a foe you have successfully [feinted](ref/rule/feint)
against via a melee attack using an [Aldori dueling sword](ref/eq-weapon/aldori-dueling-sword), you
deal an extra 2d6 points of precision damage. When your base attack bonus reaches +11, this damage
bonus increases to +3d6, and at base attack bonus +16, it increases to +4d6. This precision damage
does not stack with damage from [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack), a vigilante's sudden
strike, or similar effects. Any effect which protects against sneak attack damage also protects
against this additional damage.
