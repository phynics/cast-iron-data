A familiar is more than just an animal. It's a spirit and an intelligence, and your familiar can
choose to take more than one physical form.\
**Prerequisites:** [Familiar](ref/ability/familiars) class feature, caster level 7th.\
**Benefit:** Your [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) gains the [change shape](ref/umr/change-shape)
universal monster ability. This ability functions as [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2) and
grants access to a single alternate form, which must be the same size as the familiar. Once chosen,
this form cannot be changed.\
**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take this feat, your familiar
gains an additional alternate form. If taken by a familiar that already has the change shape
universal monster ability (like an [imp](ref/monster/devil-imp)), this grants a single additional
form.
