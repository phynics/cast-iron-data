**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 13

You quickly bring down an off-balanced opponent.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun).\
**Benefit:** When attempting an [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuver check against a
flat-footed opponent, if the attempt knocks the creature prone, you can also fall
[prone](ref/rule/prone) in order to attempt a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver check
against the creature as a free action. Doing so does not provoke an attack of opportunity from the
target of your grapple.
