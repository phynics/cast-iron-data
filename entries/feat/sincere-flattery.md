Wyvarans have little skill in creating original art, but they have proven to be experts in imitating
and even improving on it.\
**Prerequisites:** [Wyvaran](ref/race/wyvaran).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +4 circumstance bonus on Craft checks to recreate an object you have seen
before. You can spend 1 week studying the object to increase the circumstance bonus to +8. This
doesn't include the time spent crafting the item.
