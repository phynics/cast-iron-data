## Haunted Gnome Shroud (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 104

Your disturbing glamer expands to make your exact location hard to pinpoint.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, gnome magic racial trait, [Haunted Gnome](ref/feat/haunted-gnome),
[Haunted Gnome Assault](ref/feat/haunted-gnome-assault), Knowledge (arcana) 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You gain another independent use of your gnome magic like that which Haunted Gnome
Assault grants. Further, while you are under the effect of
[haunted fey aspect](ref/spell/haunted-fey-aspect), you also have concealment (20% miss chance)
against an opponent until that opponent deals you damage.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 122\
When an opponent misses you due to the miss chance from Haunted Gnome Shroud, you can spend 5
stamina points to impose a -2 penalty on all that opponent's attack rolls against you until that
opponent deals damage to you. This ability doesn't stack with itself.
