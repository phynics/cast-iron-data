When you are changed by magic, your skin also toughens.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13.\
**Benefit:** Whenever a transmutation spell or [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
affects you, your natural armor bonus increases by 1. If you have no natural armor bonus to Armor
Class, treat your natural armor bonus as 0 for the purposes of this feat. The bonus to your natural
armor bonus increases by 2 if the caster level of the effect is 10th or higher.
