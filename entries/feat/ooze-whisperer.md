You have a special bond with slimes and oozes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) class feature.\
**Benefit:** You may target oozes with spells and special abilities that normally only affect
animals as if the oozes were magical beasts with Intelligence 1, though they gain a +4 bonus to
their saving throws. You may use wild empathy to influence oozes as easily as you influence magical
beasts with Intelligence 1.
