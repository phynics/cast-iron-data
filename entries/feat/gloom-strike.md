## Gloom Strike (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 111

Few creatures are as accustomed to fighting in the shadows as you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight), [fetchling](ref/race/fetchling).\
**Benefit:** When you are within an area of [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darkness, you
gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls against enemies that are also within dim light or darkness.
