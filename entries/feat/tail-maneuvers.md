You can use your tail to help you maneuver more adroitly on the battlefield.\
**Prerequisites:** Dragon creature type, tail attack.\
**Benefit:** If you don't use your tail to make an attack, you gain a +2 bonus to your CMD and on
any combat maneuver checks you attempt until the beginning of your next turn.
