## Secured Armor (Armor Mastery, Combat)

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 17

You are adept at using your armor to cover your most vulnerable areas.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +11 or fighter level 8th, armor training class feature,
proficiency with medium or heavy armor.\
**Benefit:** When you are hit by a confirmed critical hit or a
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack), there is a 25% chance that the critical hit or sneak
attack is negated and damage is instead rolled normally.\
**Special:** This chance stacks with the [light fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-light)
and [moderate fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-moderate) armor special abilities.
