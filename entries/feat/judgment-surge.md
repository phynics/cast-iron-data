Once per day, the power of your faith surges, further empowering your judgments.\
**Prerequisites:** Judgment class feature.\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can treat your class level for your judgment class feature as if it
were 3 higher than normal. If you have multiple judgments active at the same time, this benefit
applies to all of them.
