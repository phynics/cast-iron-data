**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 119

You know how to battle your favored enemies with such efficacy that any weapon you wield against
them becomes more deadly.\
**Prerequisites:** [Favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) class feature, base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** When you take this feat, choose one of your favored enemy types. Against enemies of
that type, the threat range of any weapon you wield is doubled. This effect does not stack with any
other effect that expands a weapon's threat range.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you take it, you choose a different
favored enemy type.
