Your ability to possess foes can bypass magical protections.\
**Prerequisites:** [Magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar) as a known spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), Cha 21\
**Benefit:** The first time you attempt to possess a host under the effect of
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) spell or any similar spell, you have a chance
to dispel that spell as if you had cast [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) on the spell.
