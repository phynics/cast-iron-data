## Wingover (Monster)

This creature can make turns with ease while flying.\
**Prerequisites:** Fly speed.\
**Benefit:** Once each round, a creature with this feat can turn up to 180 degrees as a free action
without making a [Fly](ref/skill/fly) skill check. This free turn does not consume any additional
movement from the creature.\
**Normal:** A flying creature can turn up to 90 degrees by making a DC 15 Fly skill check and
expending 5 feet of movement. A flying creature can turn up to 180 degrees by making a DC 20 Fly
skill check and expending 10 feet of movement.
