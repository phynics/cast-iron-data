You can channel the shackles of Hell onto your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 5d6; worshiper of an archdevil,
infernal duke, malbranche, or whore queen.\
**Benefit:** Once per day when you channel negative energy to damage living creatures, you can
attempt to affect damaged creatures with a [dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor). Those
damaged must make a successful Will save (DC equal to the DC of the channel effect) in addition to
the normal Will save to halve damage from the channeled energy. Those that fail their save against
the channeled energy take a -4 penalty on their saving throw against the
_[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ effect. Those that fail their second Will save
are affected by _[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ for a number of minutes equal
to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 minute).
