Your familiar gains greater power.\
**Prerequisites:** [Familiar Bond](ref/feat/familiar-bond), [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will).\
**Benefit:** Your familiar gains all the normal abilities available to a familiar of a wizard with a
level equal to your total Hit Dice. You also gain the special ability normally gained by a master of
your type of familiar.
