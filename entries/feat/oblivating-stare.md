## Oblivating Stare (Stare)

Your stare can cause a target to lose track of someone.\
**Prerequisites:** [Allure](ref/stare/allure) bold stare improvement, hypnotic stare class feature,
[Mesmerist](ref/class/mesmerist) level 5th.\
**Benefit:** You can cause a target of your hypnotic stare to lose track of creatures other than
yourself. If the target of your hypnotic stare is in an environment that would allow the target to
take 10 on skill checks (if not in immediate danger or distracted), creatures can attempt Stealth
checks opposed by the target's Perception check even if they lack cover or concealment. If the
target of your hypnotic stare fails an opposed Perception check by 5 or more, it forgets the
creature attempting the Stealth check was ever present, and acts as if it had failed a Will saving
throw against a [hidden presence](ref/spell/hidden-presence) spell cast by the creature using
Stealth. If your hypnotic stare ends, the target takes damage, a creature using Stealth takes an
action that would end a _[hidden presence](ref/spell/hidden-presence)_ spell, or the target's
circumstances change so it would be unable to take 10 on a skill check, all effects of Obliviating
Stare end and the target is immune to your Obliviating Stare for 24 hours.
