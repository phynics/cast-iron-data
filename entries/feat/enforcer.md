## Enforcer (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 159, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 125

You are skilled at causing fear in those you brutalize.\
**Prerequisites:** Intimidate 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you deal nonlethal damage with a melee weapon, you can make an Intimidate
check to demoralize your target as a free action. If you are successful, the target is
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for a number of rounds equal to the damage dealt. If your attack was a
critical hit, your target is frightened for 1 round with a successful Intimidate check, as well as
being shaken for a number of rounds equal to the damage dealt.
