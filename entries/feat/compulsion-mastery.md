## Compulsion Mastery (Item Mastery)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 26

You can gain influence over the minds of others using enchantment magic items.\
**Prerequisites:** Use Magic Device 3 ranks, base Fortitude save bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You can cast [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion) as per the spell by using an item that
has an enchantment spell of 3rd level or higher in its construction requirements. You can use this
feat once per day, plus an additional time per day at base Fortitude save bonus +9 and +12.
