You excel at hunting humans in the dead of night and murdering them quietly.\
**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Stealth), [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision),
[half-ogre](ref/template/half-ogre) or [ogre](ref/monster/ogre), size Large or larger.\
**Benefit:** While in areas of [dim lighting](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or natural darkness, you
ignore all size penalties on Stealth checks and gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls against flat-footed
enemies.
