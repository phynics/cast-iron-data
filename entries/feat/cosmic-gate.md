You know that the stars shining in Cynosure's pools aren't reflections, but are gateways, and you
can use this knowledge in your travels.\
**Prerequisites:** [Lady Luck's Guidance](ref/feat/lady-lucks-guidance), Knowledge (planes) 10
ranks.

Once per day, you can briefly enter [Cynosure](ref/rule/cynosure) and use the pools located there to
instantly travel to a nearby location on the Material Plane; this functions as
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport) except you are always considered very familiar with your destination.
