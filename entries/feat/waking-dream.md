You can manipulate the world of dreams, even in your physical form.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 17, [Ephemeral Tread](ref/feat/ephemeral-tread),
[Lucid Dreamer](ref/feat/lucid-dreamer), Knowledge (planes) 10 ranks.\
**Benefit:** When in the [Dimension of Dreams](ref/rule/dimension-of-dreams) in your physical body,
you gain an additional attempt at an impossible action per day, for a total of two attempts.
However, as a result of your meddling with the fabric of the realm, when you travel to the Dimension
of Dreams in your physical body, you must succeed at a DC 15 Charisma check or arrive at a
disadvantage.
