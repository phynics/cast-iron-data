## Stag Submission (Combat)

**Sources:** Heroes of the Wild pg 23

You can quickly subdue an opponent you've charged using a special submission hold.\
**Prerequisites:** [Charging Stag Style](ref/feat/charging-stag-style), [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility),
[Stag Horns](ref/feat/stag-horns).\
**Benefit:** When you are in Charging Stag Style and succeed at a combat maneuver check to
[pin](ref/rule/grapple) a foe, you can choose to also knock the foe [prone](ref/rule/prone) or to
deal your unarmed attack damage to the foe.\
**Normal:** After successfully maintaining a grapple on a target, you can choose to move, damage, or
pin the target.
