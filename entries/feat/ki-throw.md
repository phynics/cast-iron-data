## Ki Throw (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 164

Your physical control and mastery of momentum allows you to throw enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike).\
**Benefit:** On a successful unarmed [trip](ref/rule/trip) attack against a target your size or
smaller, you may throw the target [prone](ref/rule/prone) in any square you threaten rather than its
own square. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity, and you cannot throw the creature
into a space occupied by other creatures.\
**Special:** A monk may gain Ki Throw as a bonus feat at 10th level. A monk with this feat can
affect creatures larger than his own size by spending 1 ki point per size category difference.
