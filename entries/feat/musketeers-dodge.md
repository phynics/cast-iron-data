## Musketeer's Dodge (Combat, Grit)

You dodge ranged attacks with greater proficiency than most.\
**Prerequisites:** [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (firearms),
[gunslinger](ref/class/gunslinger) level 3rd, [gunslinger's dodge](ref/deed/gunslingers-dodge) deed.\
**Benefit:** When you use the gunslinger's dodge deed to move, the movement granted by that deed
does not provoke attacks of opportunity, and you gain a +4 dodge bonus to your Armor Class.
