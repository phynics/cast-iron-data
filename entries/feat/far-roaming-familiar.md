**Sources:** Familiar Folio pg 18

Distance can't sever the bond between you and your familiar.\
**Prerequisites:** Intelligence 13, must have a [familiar](ref/ability/familiars).\
**Benefit:** You retain your empathic link to your familiar regardless of distance, though lead
blocks the link at distances greater than 1 mile (similar to the way lead blocks
[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) effects).
