## Second Wind (Combat, Performance)

The roar of the crowd strengthens your resolve.\
**Prerequisites:** [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance).\
**Benefit:** When you spend a swift action to attempt a
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) check, you gain a +2 bonus on your performance
combat check, and if you are [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), [sickened](ref/misc/sickened), or
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued), these conditions are suppressed until the end of your next turn.
