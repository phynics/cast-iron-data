Feathered wings sprout from your back.\
**Prerequisites:** [Angelic Blood](ref/feat/angelic-blood), [aasimar](ref/race/aasimar), character
level 10th.\
**Benefit:** You gain a pair of gleaming feathered wings that grant a fly speed of 30 feet (average
maneuverability) if wearing light armor or unencumbered, or 20 feet (poor maneuverability) with a
medium or heavy load or medium or heavy armor. Fly is a class skill for you.
