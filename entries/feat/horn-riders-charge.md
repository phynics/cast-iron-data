## Horn Rider's Charge (Combat)

**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 164

When the animal you're lashed to charges, you use its momentum in your own attack.\
**Prerequisites:** [Horn Rider](ref/feat/horn-rider), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you're lashed into a [horn harness](ref/eq-misc/horn-harness) and you
[ready an action](ref/rule/ready) to make a melee attack against the target of a
[charge](ref/rule/charge) made by the creature you're lashed to, you gain the attack bonus for
charging and your attack deals double damage (or triple damage with a [lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance)).
This benefit does not stack with the benefit of [Spirited Charge](ref/feat/spirited-charge).
