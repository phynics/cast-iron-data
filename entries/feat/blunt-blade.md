## Blunt Blade (Blood Hex)

**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 28

With a single curse, you make your rival less fearsome in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, base attack bonus +6, Use Magic Device 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can curse the accuracy of an enemy you have dealt damage to
in the last minute. The target must succeed at a Fortitude save or it automatically fails to confirm
all critical threats. This effect lasts for 1 round.\
**Special:** If a shaman or witch uses this [blood hex](ref/rule/feats-class), the target is unable
to deal precision damage or [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) damage for the hex's duration.
