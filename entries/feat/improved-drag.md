## Improved Drag (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 162

You are skilled at dragging foes around the battlefield.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a [drag](ref/rule/drag)
combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks made to drag a foe. You also receive
a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense when an opponent tries to drag you.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a drag combat maneuver.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 123\
When you are the target of a drag combat maneuver, you can spend a number of stamina points up to
your Strength or Dexterity bonus, whichever is greater. Your CMD against that drag attempt increases
by that number.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat **may** be removed, replaced with the new
[Powerful Maneuvers](ref/rule/powerful-maneuvers) feat.
