**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 11

You have a natural understanding of the Druidic language.\
**Prerequisites:** Linguistics 1 rank, cannot be a druid.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +5 bonus on Linguistics checks made to decipher writings in the Druidic
language. If you have 10 or more ranks in Linguistics, add Druidic to the list of bonus languages
available to you.
