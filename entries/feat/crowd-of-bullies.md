## Crowd of Bullies (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 204

Your failed attempt to humiliate a foe reveals its weaknesses to your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you fail a combat maneuver check, your allies within 30 feet who also have
this feat gain a +2 circumstance bonus on combat maneuver checks of the same type against the same
foe for 1 round.
