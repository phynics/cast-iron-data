## Calm Disposition (Faction)

You always appear to be in control of your situation.\
**Prerequisites:** [Prophets of Kalistrade](ref/rule/f-profits) 10 TPA.\
**Benefit:** You gain +2 bonus on saving throws against effects that affect your composure. This
includes all mind-affecting spells, as well as any spell or ability that would cause you to behave
in an out-of-control manner, such as fear effects and nausea. This does not include physical effects
such as [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued), and does not reduce any damage taken from concurrent effects.
