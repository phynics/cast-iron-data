You know the language of the plants.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, character level 5th, [gathlain](ref/race/gathlain).\
**Benefit:** You can cast [speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants) once per day, as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, you
can cast speak with plants an additional time per day.
