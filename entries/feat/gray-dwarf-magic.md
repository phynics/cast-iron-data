You gain a new [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) from the list of duergar racial
traits.\
**Prerequisites:** [Duergar](ref/race/duergar).\
**Benefit:** Choose one spell-like ability that is usable once per day and is granted by a duergar
racial trait you don't have. You can use that spell-like ability once per day, with a caster level
equal to your character level.\
**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, you must select a spell-like
ability that's usable once per day from a different duergar racial trait.
