## Emissary's Emboldening (Familiar)

Your familiar lends you divine courage.\
**Prerequisites:** Must have a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) with the
[emissary](ref/arc-familiar/emissary) archetype.\
**Benefit:** As long as your familiar is adjacent to you, you gain a +1 morale bonus against fear
effects. This bonus increases by 1 at 4th level and every 4 levels thereafter (to a maximum of +5 at
16th level). At 20th level, you become immune to fear effects as long as your familiar is adjacent
to you.
