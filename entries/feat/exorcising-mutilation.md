Malevolent entities that seek to possess or dominate you are unaware of how far you will go to stop
them.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 15, [Clarity of Pain](ref/feat/clarity-of-pain),
[Improved Iron Will](ref/feat/improved-iron-will), [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will).\
**Benefit:** When you fail a saving throw to resist possession or a _dominate_ spell, you can deal 4
points of Constitution damage to yourself and reroll your saving throw as an immediate action. You
cannot reduce or negate this damage in any way, and the only way to heal the damage is through
natural recovery; magical methods don't work. You must take the result of the second roll, even if
it is worse. If you succeed at the second saving throw, you also become immune to that spell or
ability from that creature for 24 hours. You must be wielding a lethal piercing or slashing weapon,
or possess a claw natural attack or some way to draw such a weapon reactively without using your
immediate action, to use this ability.
