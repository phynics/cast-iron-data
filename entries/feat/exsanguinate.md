## Exsanguinate (Combat)

You drink blood from the living at a furious rate, often draining creatures dry in a matter of
seconds.\
**Prerequisites:** [Blood drain](ref/umr/blood-drain) universal monster ability,
[grab](ref/umr/grab) universal monster ability.\
**Benefit:** Once per round, when you successfully pin an opponent, that opponent takes double the
normal damage from your blood drain.
