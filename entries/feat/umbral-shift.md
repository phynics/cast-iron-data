## Umbral Shift (Teamwork)

Your shadows dance in the darkness, carrying you with them swiftly and silently so that you're able
to swap places with your kindred.\
**Prerequisites:** [Darkness](ref/spell/darkness) racial
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you and an ally with the _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_ racial spell-like
ability who also has this feat are each in an area of darkness within 20 feet of an object radiating
darkness from a _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_ spell, you can swap places with that ally as a
swift action. Your ally must be willing and must spend an immediate action to complete the swap.
