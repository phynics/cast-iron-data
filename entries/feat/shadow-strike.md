## Shadow Strike (Combat)

You accurately strike even those you cannot clearly see.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** You can deal precision damage, such as [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) damage,
against targets with [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (but not total concealment).
