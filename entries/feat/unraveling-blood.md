**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 6

Your toxic blood has the power to destroy both flesh and foul curses.\
**Prerequisites:** [Dispelling Blood](ref/feat/dispelling-blood), Knowledge (arcana) 9 ranks,
[vishkanya](ref/race/vishkanya).\
**Benefit:** As a swift action, you can alter the effects of your vishkanya venom to function as
[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment) when striking a creature, using your character
level as your caster level. This dispelling effect affects creatures even if they are immune to
poison effects.\
**Normal:** Vishkanya venom deals Dexterity damage.
