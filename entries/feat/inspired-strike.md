**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 151

You can turn your natural talent as easily to weapon-work as to skills.\
**Prerequisites:** Studied combat class feature.\
**Benefit:** When you damage a creature by using the studied combat class feature, you can expend
one use of inspiration to roll an inspiration die and increase the damage by the number rolled.
