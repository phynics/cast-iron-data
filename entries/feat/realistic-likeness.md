**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 193

When you are in human form, you can take the shape of a specific individual.\
**Prerequisites:** [Kitsune](ref/race/kitsune).\
**Benefit:** You can precisely mimic the physical features of any individual you have encountered.
When you use your racial change shape ability, you can attempt to take the form of an individual,
granting you a +10 circumstance bonus on Disguise checks made to fool others with your
impersonation.
