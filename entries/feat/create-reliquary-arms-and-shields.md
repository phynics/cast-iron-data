**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 148

Your magical creations are infused with divine power.\
**Prerequisites:** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), ability to
cast [consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate) or [desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate).\
**Benefit:** When you craft a magic weapon, magic armor, or magic shield, you may add one casting of
_[consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate)_ or _[desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate)_ as part of the item
crafting process. This increases the item's price by 250 gp. The item becomes a reliquary and can be
used as a holy (or unholy) symbol divine focus of your deity. If you cast
_[consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate)_ or _[desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate),_ your reliquary counts as
a permanent fixture for that spell while it remains in the spell's area.
