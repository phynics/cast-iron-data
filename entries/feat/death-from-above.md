## Death from Above (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 94

You allow gravity to add extra force to your charges.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you charge an opponent from higher ground, or from above while flying, you
gain a +5 bonus on attack rolls in place of the bonuses from charging and being on higher ground.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 117\
When charging an opponent from higher ground or from above while flying, you can spend 4 stamina
points to gain a +5 bonus on the damage roll, and also retain the bonuses from charging and being on
higher ground.
