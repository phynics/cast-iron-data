**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 30

You have trained with the Muckmouth lizardfolk of the Hao Jin Tapestry and learned their ways.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, must have visited the [Hao Jin Tapestry](ref/rule/hao-jin-tapestry).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Swim checks. If you have 10 or more ranks in Swim, the bonus
increases to +4. This bonus doubles while you're in marshy terrain. In addition, you can move
normally into squares with a shallow bog, and it costs you only 2 squares of movement to move into a
square with a deep bog.
