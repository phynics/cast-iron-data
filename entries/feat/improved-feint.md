## Improved Feint (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 127

You are skilled at fooling your opponents in combat.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise).\
**Benefit:** You can make a Bluff check to [feint](ref/rule/feint) in combat as a move action.\
**Normal:** Feinting in combat is a standard action.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat has been removed, replaced with the new [Deft Maneuvers](ref/rule/deft-maneuvers) feat.
