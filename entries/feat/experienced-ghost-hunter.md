You've fought enough specters that doing so has become second nature.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 7th, [duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker), ghost hunter racial
trait.\
**Benefit:** You can use your ghost hunter ability to grant your weapons the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) special ability as a swift action. You can activate
ghost hunter an additional time each day.
