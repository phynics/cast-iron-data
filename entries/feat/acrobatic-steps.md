You can easily move over and through obstacles.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Nimble Moves](ref/feat/nimble-moves).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you move, you may move through up to 15 feet of
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) each round as if it were normal terrain. The
effects of this feat stack with those provided by Nimble Moves (allowing you to move normally
through a total of 20 feet of difficult terrain each round).
