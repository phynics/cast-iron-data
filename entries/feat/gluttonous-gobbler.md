Some ogres are able to quickly choke down even the largest of meals so they can get on with other
business.\
**Prerequisites:** Both [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) and
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple) or the [grab](ref/umr/grab) ability,
[ogre](ref/monster/ogre), size Large or larger.\
**Benefit:** If you start your turn grappling another creature at least two size categories smaller
than you, you can put the grappled creature in your mouth as a move action and attempt another
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver check (as though attempting to pin the opponent). If you
succeed, you swallow the opponent as if you had the [swallow whole](ref/umr/swallow-whole) special
ability.
