## Startoss Comet (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 15

You can aim a thrown weapon so it strikes two foes.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 13, [Point-Blank Shot](ref/feat/point-blank-shot),
[Startoss Style](ref/feat/startoss-style), [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) with the chosen
weapon.\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can make a single ranged thrown weapon attack at your full
attack bonus with the chosen weapon. If you hit, you deal damage normally and can make a second
attack (at your full attack bonus) against a target within one range increment of the first. You
determine cover for this attack from the first target's space instead of your space. You can make
only one additional attack per round with this feat. If you have
[Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike), [Improved Vital Strike](ref/feat/improved-vital-strike), or
[Greater Vital Strike](ref/feat/greater-vital-strike), you can add the additional damage from those
feats to the initial ranged attack (but not the second attack).
