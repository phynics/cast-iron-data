The walking dead respond to your call.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (necromancy), ability to cast
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)._\
**Benefit:** Add "human skeleton" to the list of creatures you can summon with
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1) and "human skeletal champion" to the list of
creatures you can summon with [summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3).

Once per day, when you cast _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster),_ you may summon a skeletal
version of one of the creatures on that spell's summoning list (apply the
[skeleton](ref/template/skeleton) template to that creature to create this monster).
