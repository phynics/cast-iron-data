Your orc heritage allows you to fight on.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [half-orc](ref/race/half-orc), orc ferocity racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You gain the [ferocity](ref/umr/ferocity) universal monster ability, allowing you to
continue fighting at negative hit points. When using this ability, you gain a +2 bonus on Intimidate
checks.\
**Normal:** A half-orc with the orc ferocity racial trait can fight for 1 more round after he is
brought below 0 hit points.
