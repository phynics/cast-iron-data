## Surprise Strike (Combat)

You actually seem to do more damage when frantically trying to avoid your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Cautious Fighter](ref/feat/cautious-fighter),
[Desperate Swing](ref/feat/desperate-swing), base attack bonus +6, [halfling](ref/race/halfling).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, when [fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively) or making an
attack of opportunity while taking the [total defense](ref/misc/total-defense) action with the
Desperate Swing feat, you take no penalty on the attack roll.
