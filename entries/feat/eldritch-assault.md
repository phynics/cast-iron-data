**Sources:** Magic Tactics Toolbox pg 5

You make deadly strikes when magic cloaks your location.\
**Prerequisites:** [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight),
[Improved Initiative](ref/feat/improved-initiative).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you attack from total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) provided by a
magical source (such as the [displacement](ref/spell/displacement) or
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility) spells), the first attack roll you make each round that is a
critical threat is automatically confirmed as a critical hit.
