## Artifact Hunter (Story)

You seek an artifact - either a specific one or merely any item of legendary prowess - with dogged
tenacity.\
**Prerequisites:** You must have come within sight of a legendary artifact at some point in your
past.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Use Magic Device checks when emulating a class feature, ability
score, race, or alignment. If you have 10 or more ranks in Use Magic Device, this bonus increases to
+4. In addition, once per day, instead of attempting a Use Magic Device check normally, you can
choose to determine the result as if you had rolled a 15. If you have 10 or more ranks in Use Magic
Device, you can use this ability twice per day.\
**Goal:** Claim ownership of an artifact.\
**Completion Benefit:** You learn how to coax the most out of your magic items. Three times per day
when you activate a use-activated or command word magic item, you can increase the item's effective
caster level by +2 as a free action. You can use this ability once per round.

_Suggested Traits:_ [Dangerously Curious](ref/trait/dangerously-curious),
[Fortunate](ref/trait/fortunate), [Secret-Keeper (QaC)](ref/trait/secret-keeper-qac).
