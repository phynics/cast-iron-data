**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 155

When you are in the heat of rage, not even pain can break your concentration.\
**Prerequisites:** Blood casting class feature.\
**Benefit:** While in a bloodrage, you gain a bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration)
checks equal to the morale bonus granted to your Constitution by the bloodrage.
