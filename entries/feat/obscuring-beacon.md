## Obscuring Beacon (Combat)

You use light to disrupt your foes' darkness-adapted vision.\
**Benefit:** When you're holding a light source in an area that is otherwise dim or darker, foes
within the normal or bright light radius shed by your light source take a -1 penalty on attack rolls
against other targets that are also within the radius of your light. Blind creatures aren't affected
by this feat.
