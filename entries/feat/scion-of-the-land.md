## Scion of the Land (Teamwork)

You are strongly linked to natural terrain and to others who share your link.\
**Prerequisites:** Survival 1 rank.\
**Benefit:** When traveling through terrain in which you can leave no trail and be impossible to
track (as a result of [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain), trackless step, or a similar
class feature), you grant that ability to allies within 60 feet who also have this feat.

If an ally within 60 feet who has this feat is also able to move normally through the current
terrain without leaving tracks, you gain a +1 bonus on Knowledge (nature), Perception, and Survival
checks while in that terrain.
