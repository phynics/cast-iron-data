Only the strong thrive in your homeland, and you are no weakling, even among your kin.\
**Prerequisites:** Con 13, [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard), [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +5 bonus on all Constitution checks made to stabilize while
[dying](ref/misc/dying). Once per day, if you are struck by a critical hit or
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack), you can spend an immediate action to negate the critical
or the sneak attack damage, making the attack a normal hit.
