## Unstoppable Esotery (Esoteric)

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 154

When you cast a spell using esoteric materials, you reinforce your spell's magical structure, making
the spell difficult to stop or remove.\
**Benefit:** When you use [esoteric material components](ref/rule/esoteric-material-components) as
part of casting a spell, you gain a +2 bonus on [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks to
cast that spell, and the spell is treated as though its caster level were 2 higher against attempts
to [dispel](ref/spell/dispel-magic) it. This increase is in addition to any increases due to greater
component or limited magic benefits.
