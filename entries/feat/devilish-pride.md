Your pride allows no master but Asmodeus.\
**Prerequisites:** [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will), worshiper of [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you attempt a Will save against a charm or compulsion spell cast by anyone who
is not a worshiper of Asmodeus, you may roll twice and take the better result.
