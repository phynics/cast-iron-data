Your vocal chords are especially tough and allow you to croak more often.\
**Prerequisites:** [Boggard](ref/monster/boggard), terrifying croak.\
**Benefit:** You gain one extra use of terrifying croak per hour. If your recharge time is less than
1 hour, you instead gain an extra use each time the ability recharges.
