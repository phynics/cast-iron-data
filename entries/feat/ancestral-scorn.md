The fury you harbor for your fiendish ancestors gives evil outsiders great reason to fear you.\
**Prerequisites:** Intimidate 5 ranks, [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling).\
**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully demoralize an outsider of the evil subtype with an Intimidate
check, it becomes [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round in addition to being affected by the
normal effects of being demoralized. If you beat the DC by 5 or more, the creature is
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round instead.\
**Normal:** Demoralizing a foe with a successful Intimidate check causes it to become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round, +1 round for every 5 by which you beat the DC.
