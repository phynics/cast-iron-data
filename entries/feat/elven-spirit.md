**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 46

Although you are of mixed heritage, you are closer to your elven relatives and the magic in their
blood flows freely in your veins.\
**Prerequisites:** [Half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** You possess the elven magic racial trait of the elves, granting you a +2 racial bonus
on caster level checks made to overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). In addition,
you receive a +2 racial bonus on Spellcraft checks made to identify the properties of magic items.
Alternatively, you can instead gain any one racial trait that elves can exchange for the elven magic
racial trait.\
**Special:** You can only take this feat at 1st level. If you take this feat, you cannot take the
[Drow Spirit](ref/feat/drow-spirit) or [Human Spirit](ref/feat/human-spirit) feats.
