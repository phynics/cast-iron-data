You spent significant time as a vagabond and know your way around their society.\
**Prerequisites:** Bluff 2 ranks, Knowledge (local) 2 ranks, member of an underclass.\
**Benefit:** When dealing with criminals, vagabonds, unsavory characters, or other members of a
country's underclass, you gain a +1 bonus on Bluff, Diplomacy, Knowledge (local), and Sense Motive
checks. When traveling with at least one other person who has this feat, you gain a +2 circumstance
bonus on Perception checks to avoid being surprised at the start of combat.
