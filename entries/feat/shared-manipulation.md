You can subtly bolster allies' ability to misdirect and infuriate their enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf).\
**Benefit:** As a move action, you can grant all friendly creatures within 30 feet who can see or
hear you a +2 bonus on Bluff or Intimidate checks (choose which skill to affect each time you use
this ability) for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round).
