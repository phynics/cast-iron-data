You can assume the aspects of an animal for longer stretches of time than most of your rivals and
allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Animal focus class feature.\
**Benefit:** Add your Wisdom bonus (minimum 1) to the number of minutes per day that you can use
your [animal focus](ref/ability/animal-foci) ability to assume the aspects of an animal.
