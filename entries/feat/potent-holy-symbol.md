In your pious hands, your holy symbol is a shining light of faith.\
**Prerequisites:** Good alignment, worshiper of a good deity.\
**Benefit:** Any round in which you use your holy symbol as a divine focus for the casting of a
spell or to [channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), you count as presenting a holy
symbol, chanting prayers, or ringing a hand bell for the purpose of keeping
[vampires](ref/template/vampire) at bay.
