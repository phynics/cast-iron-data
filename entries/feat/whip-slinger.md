## Whip-Slinger (Combat)

You can use your sling as a sap to make attacks of opportunity.\
**Prerequisites:** Proficient with [sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling).\
**Benefit:** When wielding a sling, [double sling](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-double-sling), or
[halfling sling staff](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-sling-staff), you threaten areas around you as normal
and can make attacks of opportunity with the sling into these threatened areas. The sling deals 1d4
nonlethal bludgeoning damage (1d6 for a Medium wielder), threatens a critical hit on a 20, and deals
×2 damage on a critical hit. If the sling isn't loaded with ammunition when you make an attack of
opportunity, you take a -4 penalty on the attack roll.\
**Normal:** Slings and double slings are ranged weapons, do not threaten areas, and cannot make
attacks of opportunity. A halfling sling staff can be used as a simple melee weapon that deals
bludgeoning damage equal to that of a club its size; as a melee weapon, it threatens areas normally
and its wielder can make attacks of opportunity with it.
