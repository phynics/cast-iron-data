You have learned some words of the mystic language treants use to call trees to their defense.\

character level 13th, [gathlain](ref/race/gathlain).\
**Benefit:** You can cast [liveoak](ref/spell/liveoak) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), but its duration is only 10 minutes.
