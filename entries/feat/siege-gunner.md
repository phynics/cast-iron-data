## Siege Gunner (Combat)

Aiming outsized siege weapons poses little difficulty for you.\
**Prerequisites:** [Siege Engineer](ref/feat/siege-engineer), Profession (siege engineer) 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You take no size penalty for aiming a direct-fire
[siege weapon](ref/rule/siege-engine-rules) larger than yourself. If you operate an indirect-fire
siege weapon and miss, you misdirect fire by 1 square per range increment.\
**Normal:** Direct-fire weapons impose a -2 attack roll penalty per size category by which the
weapon is larger than the creature aiming it. An indirect-fire weapon that misses misdirects fire by
1d4 squares per range increment.
