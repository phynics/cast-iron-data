## Slipslinger Bombardment (Combat)

**Sources:** Weapon Master's Handbook pg 13

You can sling a rain of splash weapons at your enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw),
[Slipslinger Grenadier](ref/feat/slipslinger-grenadier),
[Slipslinger Style](ref/feat/slipslinger-style), [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything),
warslinger racial trait.\
**Benefit:** You can use a [sling](ref/eq-weapon/sling) to hurl alchemical splash weapons as
ammunition with Slipslinger Grenadier during an attack action or a full-attack action, loading your
sling with such weapons as a free action.\
**Special:** This feat can't be used with alchemists' bombs.
