Your many visits to the [Hao Jin Tapestry](ref/rule/hao-jin-tapestry) demiplane have made you an
expert in long-distance travel.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 5th.\
**Benefit:** You cast spells of the teleportation subschool as though your caster level were 2
higher, and you treat yourself as one step more familiar when teleporting to an established
Pathfinder lodge with the [teleport](ref/spell/teleport) spell. If you can teleport as a
supernatural ability, you treat your class level as if it were 2 higher when calculating the total
distance that you can travel with this ability.
