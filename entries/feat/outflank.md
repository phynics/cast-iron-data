## Outflank (Combat, Teamwork)

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 165

You look for every edge when flanking an enemy.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +4.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you and an ally who also has this feat are [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) the
same creature, your flanking bonus on attack rolls increases to +4. In addition, whenever you score
a critical hit against the flanked creature, it provokes an attack of opportunity from your ally.
