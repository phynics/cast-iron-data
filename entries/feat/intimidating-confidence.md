**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 78

You have boundless faith in your success.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 13, [Fearless Curiosity](ref/feat/fearless-curiosity), Intimidate 5 ranks,
[human](ref/race/human).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +1 bonus on saving throws against effects with the emotion descriptor; this
bonus stacks with the bonus granted by Fearless Curiosity. When you confirm a critical hit, as a
free action you can make an Intimidate check to demoralize one creature you threaten. If you have
the [Dazzling Display](ref/feat/dazzling-display) feat, you can make Intimidate checks to demoralize
all creatures you threaten instead. You gain a +2 bonus on this check if your weapon has a ×3
critical modifier, or +4 if it has a ×4 critical modifier.
