## Sword's Shadow (Combat)

You attack with a second strike before your opponent can recover from the first.\
**Prerequisites:** [Deft Hands](ref/feat/deft-hands).\
**Benefit:** When you successfully damage an enemy with the first attack of a full attack, you can
attempt a Sleight of Hand check against that enemy's CMD as an immediate action. If you succeed, you
can treat that enemy as [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) for the next attack you make as part of
the full attack action.
