## Tripping Twirl (Combat)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 158

You can make a trip attack with a quarterstaff on all adjacent enemies.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip), [Tripping Staff](ref/feat/tripping-staff), base attack
bonus +12.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, while wielding a [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff)
two-handed, you can attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver against each enemy adjacent to
you.\
**Special:** If you are a magus with the [staff magus](ref/arc-magus/staff-magus) archetype, you can
use spellstrike on any one trip combat maneuver you make with the staff.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 134\
You can select this feat even if you don't meet the ability score prerequisite (Intelligence 13).
You gain the benefits of this feat only as long as you have at least 1 stamina point in your stamina
pool.
