## Combat Patrol (Combat)

You range across the battlefield, dealing with threats wherever they arise.\
**Prerequisites:** [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility), base
attack bonus +5.\
**Benefit:** As a full-round action, you may set up a combat patrol, increasing your threatened area
by 5 feet for every 5 points of your base attack bonus. Until the beginning of your next turn, you
may make attacks of opportunity against any opponent in this threatened area that provokes attacks
of opportunity. You may move as part of these attacks, provided your total movement before your next
turn does not exceed your speed. Any movement you make provokes attacks of opportunity as normal.
