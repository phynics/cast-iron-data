Your channeled energy can deliver a warpriest's blessing.\
**Prerequisites:** Blessings class feature, [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) class
feature.\
**Benefit:** When you channel energy to heal, you can instead deliver a warpriest's
[blessing](ref/ability/blessings) to a single willing creature (including yourself) in the area that
otherwise would have been healed by your channeled energy. The blessing must be one that requires a
standard action and affects one or more creatures. If the blessing would normally affect multiple
targets, you affect only a single target. The target receives the blessing in place of the healing
and any other effects of the channeled energy. (This application doesn't count toward your uses of
blessings per day.)
