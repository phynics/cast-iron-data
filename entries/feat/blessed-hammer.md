Your god's power radiates from your warhammer.\
**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast 3rd-level divine spells, proficient with
[warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer), worshiper of [Torag](ref/faith/torag).\
**Benefit:** You can use any warhammer you wield as a holy symbol when casting spells that require a
divine focus or when you [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy).

Furthermore, as a swift action when you cast a divine touch spell, you can choose to have the
warhammer carry the charge of the spell instead of your hand. When you deliver a touch spell with
your warhammer you can do so as part of melee attack made with the warhammer. If you drop the
warhammer or the warhammer is disarmed while carrying a charge, the charge is lost.
