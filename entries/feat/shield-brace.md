## Shield Brace (Combat, Shield Mastery)

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 19

You've mastered the art of balancing a polearm's weight against a shield's stability.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus); base attack bonus +3 or fighter level 1st;
proficiency with light shields, heavy shields, or tower shields.\
**Benefit:** You can use a two-handed weapon sized appropriately for you from the polearm or spears
[weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups) while also using a light, heavy, or
[tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) with which you are proficient. The shield's armor check
penalty (if any) applies to attacks made with the weapon.

### Stamina

**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 21\
You can spend 1 stamina point to negate the penalty on attacks for using this feat for a single
round.
