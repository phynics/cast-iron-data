When you call for aid from the sea, your summoned creatures remain to serve you longer.\
**Prerequisites:** [Triton](ref/race/triton),
[summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), character level 5th.\
**Benefit:** The duration of your _[summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)_
spell-like ability is 1 minute per level.\
**Normal:** _[Summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)_ has a duration of 1 round
per level.
