## Greater Reposition (Combat)

When you reposition foes, they are left vulnerable to the attacks of your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Reposition](ref/feat/improved-reposition), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +2 bonus on checks made to [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) a foe. This
bonus stacks with the bonus granted by Improved Reposition. Whenever you reposition a foe, his
movement provokes attacks of opportunity from all of your allies (but not you).\
**Normal:** Creatures moved by reposition do not provoke attacks of opportunity.
