**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 134

Your spells break through [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) more easily than most.\
**Benefit:** You get a +2 bonus on caster level checks (1d20 + caster level) made to overcome a
creature's spell resistance.

### Mythic Spell Penetration

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 74\
Your mythic power breaches your enemies' defenses.\
**Prerequisites:** Spell Penetration.\
**Benefit:** Add half your tier to caster level checks to overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). If you have
[Greater Spell Penetration](ref/feat/greater-spell-penetration), add your full tier instead.
