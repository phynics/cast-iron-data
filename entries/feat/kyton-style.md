## Kyton Style (Combat, Style)

Your fighting style emulates the disciplined nature of the kytons, with passion and pain held
tightly in chains.\
**Prerequisites:** Wis 13, [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (spiked
chain), Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks, ki pool class feature.\
**Benefit:** When using this style, you treat [spiked chains](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain) as if they
had the [monk](ref/misc/monk) special weapon feature. Additionally, if you have
[Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), you can spend 1 point from your ki pool to make a Stunning
Fist attack with your spiked chain instead of an unarmed strike. This still costs a use of Stunning
Fist as normal.
