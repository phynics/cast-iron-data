You can extend your reach to touch foes that would normally be too far away.\
**Prerequisites:** Spellcraft 6 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You can increase the reach of your spells' melee touch attacks by 5 feet until the end
of your turn by taking a -2 penalty to your AC until your next turn. You must decide to use this
ability before you attempt any attacks on your turn.
