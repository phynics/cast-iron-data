When you and your eidolon share senses, your combined minds grant you exceptional powers of
observation.\
**Prerequisites:** Bond senses class feature.\
**Benefit:** When sharing the senses of your eidolon, you gain a +4 competence bonus on Perception
checks for the duration of your bond senses ability.
