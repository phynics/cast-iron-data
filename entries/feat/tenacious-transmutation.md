Your mastery of the magic of changing makes your transmutations more durable.\
**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (transmutation).\
**Benefit:** The DC of caster level checks to [dispel](ref/spell/dispel-magic) or remove your
transmutations increases by 2; even if the spell is negated, its effects persist for 1 additional
round before dissipating.
