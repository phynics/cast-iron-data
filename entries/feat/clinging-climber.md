## Clinging Climber (Combat)

Using leverage and pure brawn, you can cling to a cliff face, ladder, or rope with your legs to free
both of your hands to take other actions.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 13, Climb 3 ranks.\
**Benefit:** As a move action while climbing, you can cling to your climbing surface with your legs
by succeeding at a Climb check with the same DC as that of climbing the surface as a move action. If
successful, you can make attacks with a two-handed ranged weapon and reload ranged weapons until you
resume climbing. The GM can rule that this feat doesn't work on certain climbing surfaces.
