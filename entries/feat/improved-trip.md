## Improved Trip (Combat)

**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 128

You are skilled at sending your opponents to the ground.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise).\
**Benefit:** You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a [trip](ref/rule/trip)
combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks made to trip a foe. You also receive
a +2 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense whenever an opponent tries to trip you.\
**Normal:** You provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a trip combat maneuver.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
This feat has been removed, replaced with the new [Deft Maneuvers](ref/rule/deft-maneuvers) feat.
