You can concoct potent brews.\
**Prerequisites:** Craft (alchemy) 1 rank, Profession (brewer) 1 rank, [dwarf](ref/race/dwarf).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Craft (alchemy) and Profession (brewer) checks, and you add +1
to the DC of any ingested poison you create.
