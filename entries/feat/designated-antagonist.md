## Designated Antagonist (Teamwork)

When you antagonize your foes, you can redirect their enmity to your ally.\
**Prerequisites:** [Antagonize](ref/feat/antagonize).\
**Benefit:** When you succeed at a skill check using Antagonize, you can cause the target creature
to focus its efforts on a willing ally who also has this feat, as if the ally had been the one to
use the ability.
