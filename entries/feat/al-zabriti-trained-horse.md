This horse was trained by the [Al-Zabriti](ref/rule/aff-deserts) tribespeople. It knows more tricks
and is more loyal to its rider than most mounts.\
**Prerequisites:** Horse.\
**Benefit:** This horse automatically knows the [tricks](ref/ability/animal-tricks) included in the
combat riding trick (attack, come, defend, down, guard, and heel), and these tricks do not count
against the normal maximum number of tricks the horse can learn. The Handle Animal DC to get the
horse to perform a trick is reduced by 5, and the DC to teach the horse new tricks is reduced by 5.
