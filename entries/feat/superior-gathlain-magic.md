Your experience has enabled you to dramatically develop your innate magical abilities.\
**Prerequisites:** Cha 17, [Advanced Gathlain Magic](ref/feat/advanced-gathlain-magic),
[Greater Gathlain Magic](ref/feat/greater-gathlain-magic), character level 15th,
[gathlain](ref/race/gathlain).\
**Benefit:** You gain the following spells as [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities),
each usable 1/day: [liveoak](ref/spell/liveoak), [tree stride](ref/spell/tree-stride).
