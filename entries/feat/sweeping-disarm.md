## Sweeping Disarm (Combat)

When you knock a weapon from an opponent's grasp, the errant weapon may disarm another foe.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Disarm](ref/feat/improved-disarm), base attack bonus +1.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you succeed at a combat maneuver check to [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) an
opponent, you can attempt a second disarm at a -5 penalty against an opponent adjacent to the
original target. You need not be threatening the second target. You can attempt only one additional
disarm combat maneuver check per round with this feat.
