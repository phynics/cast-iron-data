You retain your senses when seeking a possession target.\
**Prerequisites:** [Magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar) as a known spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), Wis 15\
**Benefit:** When you sense life forces while seeking a host, you can observe them as you could when
you were in your own body. Attempting to possess a body is a standard action.
