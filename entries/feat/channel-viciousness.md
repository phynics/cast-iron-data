You can channel brutality into the weapons of your allies.\
**Prerequisites:** [Channel negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 4d6, worshiper of
[Gorum](ref/faith/gorum).\
**Benefit:** As a standard action, you can expend one of your uses of channel energy to charge the
melee weapons of all allies within 30 feet of you with negative energy. Affected melee weapons gain
the [vicious](ref/magic-enh/vicious) magic weapon special ability for 1 minute × your Charisma bonus
(minimum 1 minute).
