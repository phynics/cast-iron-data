## Street Carnage (Combat)

For you, the urban environment becomes another brutal weapon you can use to shred flesh and break
bones.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 15, [Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Street Style](ref/feat/street-style), [Street Sweep](ref/feat/street-sweep), base attack bonus +8
or monk level 7th.\
**Benefit:** While using Street Style, the critical multiplier of your unarmed strikes becomes ×3,
instead of ×2.
