## Chain Mastery (Combat)

**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 178

You wield spiked chains with effortless lethality.\
**Prerequisites:** Dex 15, [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (spiked
chain).\
**Benefit:** You treat a [spiked chain](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-chain) as a one-handed martial weapon
for all purposes. You can still choose to wield a spiked chain as a two-handed weapon, and if you do
so, you gain a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to disarm or trip foes with your spiked chain.
