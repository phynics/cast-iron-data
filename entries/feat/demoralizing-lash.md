## Demoralizing Lash (Combat)

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 124

You cow your enemies with the lash of a whip.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +1, [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) 1 rank,
[hobgoblin](ref/race/hobgoblin).\
**Benefit:** To use this feat, you must use a [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip) (or another weapon in the
flails fighter [weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)) to attack a foe demoralized by an
Intimidate check. If the attack hits, you extend the duration the creature is
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) by 1 round. This feat has no effect on creatures whose shaken conditions
are from sources other than Intimidate checks.
