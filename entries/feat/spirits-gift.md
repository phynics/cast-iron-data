**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 157

You commune with a spirit of your choice, and it grants your animal companion or familiar a boon.\
**Prerequisites:** Animal companion class feature or [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) class
feature.\
**Benefit:** At the start of the day, you can choose to commune with a single
[shaman spirit](ref/ability/shaman-spirits). (Although a shaman would need an hour of preparation,
this takes you no time.) Once during the next 24 hours, you can spend a standard action to grant
your animal companion or familiar that shaman spirit's spirit animal ability for 1 minute per level
in the class that grants your animal companion or familiar.
