Through both might of arms and spiritual wisdom, you have attained full understanding of your
barbaric totem.\
**Prerequisites:** Str 17; Dex 13; Wis 13; [Athletic](ref/feat/athletic);
[Totemic Disciple](ref/feat/totemic-disciple); [Totemic Initiate](ref/feat/totemic-initiate); base
attack bonus +13; no barbarian totem rage powers except those chosen with this feat, Totemic
Disciple, or Totemic Initiate; nonlawful alignment.\
**Benefit:** Choose one greater totem rage power that requires the totem rage power you chose with
Totemic Disciple. While gaining the benefits of Totemic Initiate, you also gain the benefits of this
new rage power as well as the one you chose with Totemic Disciple. If you gain the pounce ability
this way, you can make only natural attacks using your pounce ability.
