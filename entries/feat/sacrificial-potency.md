Your powers wax with the deaths of mortals.\
**Prerequisites:** One or more racial [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), outsider
with the evil subtype.\
**Benefit:** Whenever you perform a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) that kills a creature, choose
a racial spell-like ability you have that is usable a limited number of times per day (but not more
limited, such as once per week or once per month). The spell-like ability's spell level must be
equal to or less than half the victim's Hit Dice. You regain a use of that spell-like ability. This
has no effect if you haven't expended any uses of that spell-like ability.

In addition, you can gain this benefit when an ally within 30 feet performs a _coup de grace_ that
kills its victim. The ally must openly proclaim that the killing is done in your name. This
otherwise acts as if you had performed the _coup de grace_ yourself.

You can gain this feat's benefit only once every 24 hours.
