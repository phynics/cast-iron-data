You are deadly when delivering your master's magic.\
**Prerequisites:** Deliver touch spells ability, [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar).\
**Benefit:** When delivering a touch spell for your master, the critical threat range of your touch
attack is doubled. This feat does not stack with [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) or
other effects that increase critical threat range.
