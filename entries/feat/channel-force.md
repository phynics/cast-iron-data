**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 88

Your channel is bolstered by your faith, allowing you to move and damage your foes.\
**Prerequisites:** [Aasimar](ref/race/aasimar), [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) 2d6.\
**Benefit:** When you channel energy to deal damage, you may choose to affect only a single target
within 30 feet. In addition to dealing damage, if that single target fails its saving throw, you may
pull or push the target up to 5 feet for every 2d6 points of channel energy damage you are capable
of dealing.
