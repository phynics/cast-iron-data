**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 95

You've developed your skills at picking up items while on the move.\
**Prerequisites:** [Deft Hands](ref/feat/deft-hands), Sleight of Hand 5 ranks.\
**Benefit:** You no longer take the -20 penalty for attempting a
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check as a move action. You can attempt a Sleight of
Hand check as a standard action in the middle of your move action.\
**Normal:** You can attempt a Sleight of Hand check as a move action only by taking a -20 penalty on
the check.
