## Difficult Swings (Weapon Mastery)

You can force foes to move cautiously when near you.\
**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +5, weapon training class feature with a melee weapon.\
**Benefit:** When you make a full attack with an appropriate melee weapon, you can force creatures
to treat squares adjacent to you as [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) until the
beginning of your next turn. You can choose to allow any creature you are aware of to ignore the
difficult terrain you effectively create with this feat.
