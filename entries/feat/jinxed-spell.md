## Jinxed Spell (Metamagic)

You can make a creature that fails its spell save suffer your jinx as well.\
**Prerequisites:** Any two metamagic feats, Halfling Jinx trait.\
**Benefit:** Any creature that fails its saving throw against a jinxed spell also suffers the
effects of your jinx. This feat has no effect on spells that do not allow a saving throw. If the
spell affects an area or multiple creatures, you must select one creature to take the jinx, unless
you have the [Area Jinx](ref/feat/area-jinx) feat; if you have the Area Jinx feat, choose a jinx
[burst](ref/misc/burst) area within the spell's area or among its targets and apply the jinx to
those in the burst that failed their save. A jinxed spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than
the spell's actual level.
