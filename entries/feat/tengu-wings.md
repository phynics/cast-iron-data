You can grow wings that allow you to fly.\
**Prerequisites:** Character level 5th, [tengu](ref/race/tengu).\
**Benefit:** Once per day, you can sprout a pair of giant black crow's wings, granting you a fly
speed of 30 feet (average maneuverability). This [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
otherwise functions as [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1) (though you do not gain any other
benefits of that spell) with a caster level equal to your level.
