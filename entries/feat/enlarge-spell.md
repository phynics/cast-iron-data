## Enlarge Spell (Metamagic)

You can increase the range of your spells.\
**Benefit:** You can alter a spell with a range of close, medium, or long to increase its range by
100%. An enlarged spell with a range of close now has a range of 50 ft. + 5 ft./level, while
medium-range spells have a range of 200 ft. + 20 ft./level and long-range spells have a range of 800
ft. + 80 ft./level. An enlarged spell uses up a spell slot one level higher than the spell's actual
level.

Spells whose ranges are not defined by distance, as well as spells whose ranges are not close,
medium, or long, do not benefit from this feat.
