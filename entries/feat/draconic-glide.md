**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 136

You possess draconic defenses and wings that allow you to glide.\
**Prerequisites:** [Draconic Aspect](ref/feat/draconic-aspect), [kobold](ref/race/kobold).\
**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus against sleep and paralysis effects. You grow a pair of wings that
you can use to fall and glide at a safe pace. You can make a DC 15 Fly check to fall safely from any
height without taking falling damage, as if using [feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall). When
falling safely, you may make an additional DC 15 Fly check to glide, moving 5 feet laterally for
every 20 feet you fall.\
**Special:** If you have the gliding wings racial trait, you don't need to make a Fly check to
glide, and you can move 10 feet laterally for every 20 feet you fall.
