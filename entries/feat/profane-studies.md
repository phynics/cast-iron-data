Your study of fiends grants you advantages when summoning or fighting them.\
**Prerequisites:** Int 13, Knowledge (planes) 4 ranks, ability to cast a
[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell.\
**Benefit:** You gain a +5 bonus on Knowledge (planes) checks involving evil outsiders.
Additionally, when you cast a _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell to summon an evil
outsider, treat your caster level as 2 levels higher for the purpose of determining the duration.
