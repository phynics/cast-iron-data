## Giant-Killer Stance (Combat)

**Sources:** Giant Hunter's Handbook pg 23

You can prepare a crippling strike against larger foes that rush into your weapon's reach.\
**Prerequisites:** [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack).\
**Benefit:** You can treat any [reach](ref/misc/reach) weapon you wield as having the
[brace](ref/misc/brace) property against creatures that are Large or larger. If your readied attack
hits, the creature takes a -2 penalty on melee attack rolls for 1 round.
