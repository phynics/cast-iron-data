**Sources:** Bestiary 4 pg 290\
When the creature takes damage in combat, on its next turn it can fly into a rage as a free action.
It gains +2 Constitution and +2 Strength, but takes a -2 penalty to its AC. The rage lasts as long
as the battle or 1 minute, whichever is shorter. It cannot end its rage voluntarily.
