**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 8\
**Pathfinder Name:** Al-Zabriti\
**Scope:** national\
**Alignment:** NG\
**Values:** ecological integrity (both protection of existing desert and prevention of
desertification), hospitality to travelers, isolationism, resistance to assimilation,
[Sarenite faith](ref/faith/sarenrae), secrecy, tradition\
**Goals:** breed superior horses, eliminate evil outsiders, preserve traditional lifestyle, prevent
corruption of the Sarenite faith, protect business interests, shore up border with hostile
neighboring nation\
**Allies:** genies, mainline Sarenite church, various empyreal lords\
**Enemies:** cultists of [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug), demons, devils, divs, horse thieves,
qlippoth, raiders, neighboring nation\
**Rivals:** none

### Associated Concepts

**Prestige Classes:** [Asavir](ref/pclass/asavir)

**Archetypes:** [Qadiran Horselord](ref/arc-cavalier/qadiran-horselord) (Cavalier),
[Sunrider](ref/arc-druid/sunrider) (Druid)

**Equipment:** This gear is generally readily available to all buyers in the nation, or in
cosmopolitan cities throughout the region: [caparison](ref/eq-misc/caparison),
[mithral horseshoes](ref/eq-misc/mithral-horseshoes), [sunblock kohl](ref/eq-misc/sunblock-kohl),
[various spices](ref/eq-misc/black-cumin)

**Feats:** [Armored Rider](ref/feat/armored-rider), [Horse Whisperer](ref/feat/horse-whisperer),
[Improved Horse Whisperer](ref/feat/improved-horse-whisperer),
[Improved Mounted Archery](ref/feat/improved-mounted-archery),
[Mounted Blade](ref/feat/mounted-blade), [Mounted Onslaught](ref/feat/mounted-onslaught),
[Purifying Channel](ref/feat/purifying-channel)

**Animal Companion Feats:** [Al-Zabriti-Trained Horse](ref/feat/al-zabriti-trained-horse),
[Forceful Charge](ref/feat/forceful-charge),
[Improved Forceful Charge](ref/feat/improved-forceful-charge)

**Magic Items:** The tribes of the desert create and use the following items, though they rarely
share them with outsiders; they can sometimes be found in black markets in the nation, though
purchasing them there might earn the enmity of the desert tribespeople:
[Caparison of Resistance](ref/magic-wondrous/caparison-of-resistance),
[Cloak of Heavenly Fire](ref/magic-wondrous/cloak-of-heavenly-fire)

**Spells:** The following spells are closely guarded by the tribespeople:
[Blade of Light](ref/spell/blade-of-light), [Burning Sands](ref/spell/burning-sands),
[Cleansing Fire](ref/spell/cleansing-fire), [Sun's Disdain](ref/spell/suns-disdain),
[Mass Sun's Disdain](ref/spell/suns-disdain-mass), [Sunstalker](ref/spell/sunstalker)
