**Covert Action** **(Spies):** A team of spies works with any other team to hide evidence that they
or the militia were involved in an operation. All d20 rolls to resolve a militia action taken
immediately following a Covert Action gain a bonus equal to the Charisma bonus of the spies'
manager, and if the action is successful, the militia's [Notoriety](ref/rule/notoriety-mt) score
doesn't increase. Alternatively, a Covert Action can be used to place a contact or
[cache](ref/rule/caches) in a specific adventure site. Once the militia places a contact or cache
there, it remains there for only 1 week, after which it is stolen or lost.
