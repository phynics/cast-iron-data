**Progress to Complete** 16\
**Ground** very soft (DC 5) or soft (DC 10); **Maximum Advantages** 3

Swampy terrain includes bogs, marshes, and fens, as well as any other sort of wetlands. A swamp tile
with a significant number of deep areas, quicksand, or more can easily have more maximum advantages
and take more progress to complete.
