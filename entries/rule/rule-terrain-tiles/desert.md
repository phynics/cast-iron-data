**Progress to Complete** 16\
**Ground** very soft (DC 5), soft (DC 10), or firm (DC 15); **Maximum Advantages** 3

Desert terrain includes warm and sandy areas. The rules for
[environmental heat dangers](ref/rule/heat-dangers) apply in most cases, potentially affecting both
groups.
