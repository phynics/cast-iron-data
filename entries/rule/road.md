**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 211

A Road speeds travel through your kingdom and promotes trade. You can upgrade a Road to a Highway.

**Terrain:** Any land.

**Effect:** Economy +1 for every 4 hexes of Road, Stability +1 for every 8 hexes of Road; improves
[overland travel speed](ref/rule/overland-movement).

**Cost:** See the Road column of
[Table 4-6: Terrain and Terrain Improvements](ref/rule/terrain-improvements).
