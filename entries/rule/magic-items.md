From the common potion of cure light wounds to the mighty holy avenger, magic items are valuable
tools used by heroes and villains alike. This section contains a wide variety of items to enhance
any character.

Magic items are divided into categories: armor, weapons, potions, rings, rods, scrolls, staves,
wands, and wondrous items. In addition, some magic items are cursed or intelligent. Finally, a few
magic items are of such rarity and power that they are considered to belong to a category of their
own: artifacts. Artifacts are classified in turn as minor (extremely rare but not one-of-a-kind
items) or major (each one unique and incredibly potent).
