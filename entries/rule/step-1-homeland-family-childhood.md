The tables below determine the basics of your family and homeland. These establish the circumstances
of your upbringing, the composition of your family, and your family's social standing, as well as a
major event you experienced in childhood. To determine these aspects of your background, do the
following:

1. Find the sections for your race and roll on the Homeland, Parents, and Siblings tables within the
  section. If you have siblings, also consult [Relative Age of Sibling table](ref/rule/siblings) and
  the Race of Adopted Sibling table to determine the relative age of your siblings and the race of
  adopted siblings.
1. Roll on the [Circumstance of Birth table](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more), to determine the
  circumstance of your birth. Based on those circumstances, you may have to roll on the subsequent
  tables to determine details of those circumstances.
1. Roll on the [Major Childhood Event table](ref/rule/birth-circumstances-and-more) to determine a
  major childhood event.
1. Proceed to [Step 2-Adolescence and Training](ref/rule/step-2-adolescence-and-training).
