**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 47

In addition to their normal allotment of regular skill ranks, all characters gain 2 background skill
ranks each time they gain a level in a PC class. The character's Intelligence modifier doesn't
adjust this value. Background skill ranks can be used to gain ranks only in background skills, not
adventuring skills. Characters can expend their regular skill ranks on background skills if they
desire.

Even the most dedicated adventurers have other things they enjoy doing in their spare time. Some
pursue business interests during their downtime between adventures, and though these can be modeled
with the [downtime rules](ref/rule/downtime), players who aren't interested in such a robust system
still might wish to include elements of those interests on their character sheets.
