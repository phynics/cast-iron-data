**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 226

Loci spirits form when excess psychic energy becomes saturated with powerful positive emotional
resonances. Loci spirits typically arise in places with connections to previous miracles, benevolent
guardian spirits, or good-aligned deities. Loci spirits infuse a specific area; their psychic energy
heightens the positive emotions experienced there to create a feeling of peace and protection. A
loci spirit also manifests a unique ceremonial power linked specifically to the emotional energy
that birthed the spirit. Except in rare circumstances, a given area can house at most one loci
spirit.
