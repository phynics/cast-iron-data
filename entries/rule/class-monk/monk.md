**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 56

For the truly exemplary, martial skill transcends the battlefield - it is a lifestyle, a doctrine, a
state of mind. These warrior-artists search out methods of battle beyond swords and shields, finding
weapons within themselves just as capable of crippling or killing as any blade. These monks (so
called since they adhere to ancient philosophies and strict martial disciplines) elevate their
bodies to become weapons of war, from battle-minded ascetics to self-taught brawlers. Monks tread
the path of discipline, and those with the will to endure that path discover within themselves not
what they are, but what they are meant to be.

**Role:** Monks excel at overcoming even the most daunting perils, striking where it's least
expected, and taking advantage of enemy vulnerabilities. Fleet of foot and skilled in combat, monks
can navigate any battlefield with ease, aiding allies wherever they are needed most.

**Alignment:** Any lawful

**Hit Die:** d8

**Starting Wealth:** 1d6 × 10 gp (average 35 gp)

**Skill Points per Level:** 4 + Int modifier

**Class Skills (Str):** Climb, Swim

**Class Skills (Dex):** Acrobatics, Escape Artist, Ride, Stealth

**Class Skills (Int):** Craft (any), Knowledge (history), Knowledge (religion)

**Class Skills (Wis):** Perception, Profession (any), Sense Motive

**Class Skills (Cha):** Intimidate, Perform (any)

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Weapons:** club, dagger, light crossbow, heavy crossbow, handaxe, javelin, kama, nunchaku,
quarterstaff, sai, short sword, shortspear, shuriken, siangham, sling, spear, Any weapon with the
[monk](ref/misc/monk) special weapon quality.

**Armor:** No armor

**Shields:** No

**Notes:** Wearing armor or using a shield can stifle some of your abilities.

**AC Bonus (Ex)**

**At 4th Level:** You gain an additional bonus to your AC and CMD equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your monk level.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You add your Wisdom bonus (if any) to your AC and your CMD. These bonuses to AC apply
even against touch attacks or when you are [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed).

**Special:** You lose these bonuses when you are immobilized or [helpless](ref/misc/helpless), when
you wear any armor, when you carry a shield, or when you carry a medium or heavy load.

**Flurry of Blows (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**At 15th Level:** You can make three additional attacks when you use _flurry of blows,_ as if using
[Greater Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/greater-two-weapon-fighting) (even if you do not meet the
prerequisites for the feat).

**At 8th Level:** You can make two additional attacks when you use _flurry of blows,_ as if using
[Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/improved-two-weapon-fighting) (even if you do not meet the
prerequisites for the feat).

**Section:** Flurry of Blows

**Gained:** At 1st Level

You can make a _flurry of blows_ as a full-attack action. When doing so you may make one additional
attack using any combination of unarmed strikes or attacks with a special monk weapon (kama,
nunchaku, quarterstaff, sai, shuriken, and siangham) as if using the
[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) feat (even if you do not meet the prerequisites
for the feat). For the purpose of these attacks, your base attack bonus from your monk class levels
is equal to your monk level. For all other purposes, such as qualifying for a feat or a prestige
class, you use your normal base attack bonus.

You apply your full Strength bonus to your damage rolls for all successful attacks made with _flurry
of blows,_ whether the attacks are made with an off-hand or with a weapon wielded in both hands. You
may substitute [disarm](ref/rule/disarm), [sunder](ref/rule/sunder), and [trip](ref/rule/trip)
combat maneuvers for unarmed attacks as part of a _flurry of blows._ You cannot use any weapon other
than an unarmed strike or a special monk weapon as part of a _flurry of blows._ You cannot use
natural weapons (if you have any) as part of a _flurry of blows,_ nor can you make natural attacks
in addition to your _flurry of blows_ attacks.

**Unarmed Strike**

**Action:** Ability

**At 1st Level:** Your unarmed strike deals 1d4 (Small), 1d6 (Medium), or 1d8 (Large) damage.

**At 12th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d10 (Small), 2d6 (Medium), or 3d6 (Large) damage.

**At 16th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d6 (Small), 2d8 (Medium), or 3d8 (Large) damage.

**At 20th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d8 (Small), 2d10 (Medium), or 4d8 (Large) damage.

**At 4th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium), or 2d6 (Large) damage.

**At 8th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d8 (Small), 1d10 (Medium), or 2d8 (Large) damage.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** Usually your unarmed strikes deal lethal damage, but you can choose to deal nonlethal
damage instead with no penalty on your attack roll. You have the same choice to deal lethal or
nonlethal damage while grappling. Your unarmed strike is treated as both a manufactured weapon and a
natural weapon for the purpose of spells and effects that enhance or improve either manufactured
weapons or natural weapons.

You gain [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as a bonus feat. Your attacks
may be with fist, elbows, knees, and feet. This means that you may make unarmed strikes with your
hands full. There is no such thing as an off-hand attack for your unarmed strikes, so you apply your
full Strength bonus on damage rolls for all your unarmed strikes.

You also deal more damage with your unarmed strikes than a normal person would. The unarmed damage
values listed on the table above are for Medium monks only.

**Vows:** A monk can discipline his body to by upholding the strict tenets of a vow. By adhering to
his [vow's](ref/ability/vows) tenets, the monk's ki pool increases by the amount listed in the vow's
description. Every vow comes with a penalty or limitation to offset this increase in ki. A monk can
take a vow at any level, and can even take multiple vows over his career, but each vow does not add
to his ki pool until he gains a ki pool as a class feature (usually at 4th level).

A monk who takes a vow never gains the _still mind_ class feature, even if he later abandons all his
vows.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Repeat:** You gain another bonus feat from the list., 2, 4

A monk may also choose a meditation feat in place of a bonus feat, but he must meet their
prerequisites.

**Stunning Fist (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist) as a bonus feat, even if you don't
meet the prerequisites.

**At 12th Level:** You can make the target [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d6+1 rounds

**At 16th Level:** You can permanently [blind](ref/misc/blinded) or [deafen](ref/misc/deafened) the
target.

**At 20th Level:** You can [paralyze](ref/rule/paralyzed) the target for 1d6+1 rounds.

**At 4th Level:** You gain the ability to apply [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued) to the target of your
Stunning Fist. This condition replaces stunning the target for 1 round, and a successful saving
throw still negates the effect.

**At 8th Level:** You can now make the target [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 minute.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** You must choose which condition will apply before the attack roll is made. These
effects do not stack with themselves (a creature fatigued by Stunning Fist cannot become
[exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) if hit by Stunning Fist again), but additional hits do increase the
duration.

**Evasion (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** If you make a successful Reflex saving throw against an attack that normally deals half
damage on a successful save, you instead takes no damage.

**Special:** This only applies if you are wearing light armor or no armor, and are not
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless)

**Fast Movement (Ex)**

**Section:** Third-Level Abilities

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain an enhancement bonus of +10 ft. to your land speed.

**Special:** If you wear armor or carry a medium or heavy load, you lose this extra speed.

At 6th level: The bonus +20 ft.

At 9th level: The bonus +30 ft.

At 12th level: The bonus +40 ft.

At 15th level: The bonus +50 ft.

At 18th level: The bonus +60 ft.

**Maneuver Training (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You use your monk level in place of your base attack bonus when calculating your Combat
Maneuver Bonus. Base attack bonuses granted from other classes are unaffected and are added
normally.

**Still Mind (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells and effects.

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a pool of ki points. The number of points in your ki pool is equal to 1/2 your
monk level + your Wisdom modifier.

**Flavor:** A monk gains supernatural energy he can use to accomplish amazing feats.

**At 10th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are also treated as lawful weapons for the purpose of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 16th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are treated as [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) weapons
for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and bypassing
[hardness](ref/rule/breaking-things).

**At 7th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are also treated as cold iron and silver for the purpose of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** As long as you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, you can make a _ki strike,_ which
allows your unarmed attacks to be treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Special:** You gain additional powers that consume points from your ki pool as you gain levels.
The ki pool is replenished each morning after 8 hours of rest or meditation; these hours do not need
to be consecutive.

**Swift:** By spending 1 point from your ki pool, you can make one additional unarmed strike at your
highest attack bonus when making a _flurry of blows_ attack. In addition, you can spend 1 point to
increase your speed by 20 feet for 1 round. Finally, your can spend 1 point from your _ki_ pool to
give yourself a +4 dodge bonus to AC for 1 round.

**Slow Fall (Ex)**

**Ability:** If it within arm's reach, you can use a wall to slow your descent, taking damage as if
your fall was shorter. When determining damage from a fall, you treat take your fall distance and
subtract a number of feet equal to 10 &times& half your monk level. (For example, at 4th level, a 30
ft. fall is treated as a 10 ft. fall.)

**At 20th Level:** You can use a nearby wall to slow your descent and fall any distance without
harm.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**High Jump (Ex)**

**Section:** Fifth-to-Tenth-Level Abilities

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** You add your level to all Acrobatics checks made to jump, both for vertical jumps and
horizontal jumps. In addition, you always count as having a running start when making jump checks
using Acrobatics.

**Swift:** By spending 1 point from your _ki_ pool, you gain a +20 bonus on Acrobatics checks made
to jump for 1 round.

**Purity of Body (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to all diseases, including supernatural and magical diseases.

**Wholeness of Body (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Standard:** You can heal a number of hit points of damage equal to your monk level by using 2
points from your ki pool.

**Improved Evasion (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Your evasion ability improves. As long as you are not helpless, you still take no
damage on a successful Reflex saving throw against attacks, but you take only half damage on a
failed save.

**Diamond Body (Su)**

**Section:** Eleventh-to-Fourteenth-Level Abilities

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** You are immune to poisons of all kinds.

**Abundant Step (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Move:** You can slip magically between spaces, as if using the spell
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door). Using this ability consumes 2 points from your _ki_
pool. Your caster level for this effect is equal to your monk level. You cannot take other creatures
with you when you use this ability.

**Diamond Soul (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** You gain [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to your monk level + 10.

**Quivering Palm (Su)**

**Ability:** You must announce your intent before making your attack roll. Creatures immune to
critical hits cannot be affected. Otherwise, if you strike successfully and the target takes damage
from the blow, the quivering palm attack succeeds. Thereafter, you can try to slay the victim at any
later time, as long as the attempt is made within a number of days equal to your monk level.

**Flavor:** A monk can set up vibrations within the body of another creature that can thereafter be
fatal.

**Free:** You can merely will the target to die, and unless the target makes a Fortitude saving
throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your monk level + your Wis modifier), it dies.

**Section:** Higher-Level Abilities

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Special:** If the saving throw is successful, the target is no longer in danger from that
particular quivering palm attack, but it may still be affected by another one at a later time. You
can have no more than 1 quivering palm in effect at one time. If you use quivering palm while
another is still in effect, the previous effect is negated.

**Usage:** Once a day.

**Usage:** Once a day.

**Timeless Body (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** You no longer take penalties to your ability scores for aging and cannot be magically
[aged](ref/rule/age). Any such penalties that you have already taken, however, remain in place.

**Special:** Age bonuses still accrue, and you still die of old age when your time is up.

**Tongue of the Sun and Moon (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** You can speak with any living creature.

**Empty Body (Su)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Move:** You gain the ability to assume an ethereal state for 1 minute as though using the spell
[etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness). Using this ability consumes 3 points from your _ki_ pool.
This ability only affects you and cannot be used to make other creatures ethereal.

**Perfect Self**

**Flavor:** A monk becomes a magical creature.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You are forevermore treated as an [outsider](ref/type/outsider) rather than as a
humanoid (or whatever your creature type was) for the purpose of spells and magical effects.
Additionally, you gain DR 10/chaotic. Unlike other outsiders, you can still be brought back from the
dead as if you were a member of your previous creature type.

### Ex-Monks

A monk who becomes nonlawful cannot gain new levels as a monk but retains all monk abilities. (Also
see the [Sin Monk](ref/arc-monk/sin-monk) archetype for more options.)

### Alternate Capstones

**Source** Chronicle of Legends pg. 28\
When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Old Master (Ex)**

At 20th level, the monk has reached the highest levels of his martial arts school. The monk gains
one additional attack at his highest base attack bonus when using _flurry of blows,_ and his dodge
bonus to AC increases by 2.

**Also available:** Old Dog, New Tricks, Ki Sage

### Archetypes

**Source** Advanced Player's Guide pg. 72\
From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.

**Martial Artist**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Still Mind; Slow Fall; Ki Pool; Purity of Body; Diamond Body; Perfect
Self; Wholeness of Body; Timeless Body; Tongue of Sun and Moon; Abundant Step; Diamond Soul; Empty
Body

**Description:** The martial artist pursues a mastery of pure martial arts without the monastic
traditions. He is a master of form, but lacks the ability to harness his ki.

**Master of Many Styles**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bonus Feats; Flurry of Blows; Perfect Self

**Description:** The master of many styles is a collector. For every move, he seeks a counter. For
every style, he has a riposte.

**Menhir Guardian**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Alignment; Weapon/Armor Proficiencies; Unarmed Strike; Ki Pool; Flurry of
Blows; Stunning Fist; High Jump; Quivering Palm

**Description:** Menhir guardians are ascetic protectors of sacred druidic sites, less concerned
with structure and maintaining internal order than they are with preserving the natural balance of
the world.

**Monk of the Empty Hand**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Monk Weapon Proficiencies; Still Mind; Purity of Body; Diamond Body

**Description:** The monk of the empty hand eschews normal weapons in favor of whatever is lying
around - rocks, chair legs, flagons of ale, even a simple quill pen all become deadly weapons in the
hands of such a monk.

**Monk of the Four Winds**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Stunning Fist; Abundant Step; Timeless Body; Perfect Self

**Description:** The monk of the four winds is connected to the natural world in a way few other
creatures - even other monks - can hope to match.

**Monk of the Healing Hand**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Wholeness of Body; Diamond Body; Quivering Palm; Perfect Self

**Description:** Monks of the healing hand seek perfection through helping others.

**Monk of the Lotus**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Stunning Fist; Abundant Step; Quivering Palm; Tongue of the Sun and the
Moon

**Description:** While a monk of the lotus realizes that combat cannot always be avoided - and is
more than capable in a fight - he understands that all creatures are connected, and to harm another
is to harm the self.

**Monk of the Mantis**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th-level Bonus Feats; Wholeness of Body;
Diamond Body; Diamond Soul; Quivering Palm

**Description:** A body contains many points where the flesh, mind, and spirit coincide. A monk of
the mantis is skilled at manipulating these points.

**Monk of the Sacred Mountain**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Evasion; Slow Fall; High Jump; Improved Evasion; Tongue of the Sun and the
Moon

**Description:** The monk of the sacred mountain finds strength and power in the earth beneath his
feet.

**Monk of the Seven Forms**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bonus Feats; Stunning Fist; Maneuver Training; Abundant Step; Quivering
Palm

**Description:** In their place, the monks of the isolated sanctuary now teach a style featuring
lightning-fast strikes that mimic both dervish dances and the searing winds of the arid desert,
favoring mobility and endurance.

**Nimble Guardian**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Evasion; Still Mind; Purity of Body; Wholeness of Body; Improved Evasion

**Description:** (Catfolk Only) Some catfolk monks dedicate their graceful prowess to the defense of
others, especially those dedicated to a similar ethos or who prove themselves as stalwart allies of
the monk's cause.

**Nornkith**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Key Ability Score; Stunning Fist; 1st-, 10th-level Bonus Feat; Purity of
Body; Diamond Soul; Quivering Palm; Empty Body; Perfect Self

**Description:** Nornkith hone their bodies and minds to follow the branching threefold paths of
fate.

**Ouat**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Greed, Hatred, Defensive Training, Stonecunning, Weapon Familiarity
(Dwarven Racial Traits); Stunning Fist; Wholeness of Body

**Description:** (Dwarf Only) The ascetic, desert-dwelling dwarves known as the Ouat have eschewed
dwarven traditions, whose unchanging rigidity, they believe, shackles their people to the past and
inhibits self-improvement.

**Perfect Scholar**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Still Mind; Slow Fall; Tongue of the Sun and Moon; Perfect
Self

**Description:** Perfect scholars, often worshipers of Irori, hone their minds and bodies through
the accumulation of knowledge

**Qinggong Monk**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Slow Fall; High Jump; Wholeness of Body; Diamond Body; Abundant Step;
Diamond Soul; Quivering Palm; Timeless Body; Tongue of the Sun and Moon; Empty Body; Perfect Self

**Description:** The qinggong monk is a master of her ki, using it to perform superhuman stunts or
even blast opponents with supernatural energy.

**Sage Counselor**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; 1st, 2nd, 6th, 10th-level Bonus Feat; Ki Pool

**Description:** Sage counselors are ascetics and mystics who leave the confines of the monastery
walls to advise secular people about spiritual truths and to seek knowledge of the outside world.

**Scaled Fist**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bonus Feats; Still Mind; Maneuver Training; Quivering Palm

**Description:** Scaled fists eschew passive introspection in favor of unshakable confidence.
Steeped in traditions that trace their origins to the warrior-monks who trained under the tutelage
of draconic masters in Tian Xia, scaled fists eschew passive introspection in favor of unshakable
confidence. Scaled fists learn to combine brutal intimidation with the brazen ferocity of an ancient
wyrm to devastate their foes.

**Scarred Monk**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Ki Pool; High Jump; Wholeness of Body; Abundant Step; Empty Body

**Description:** For monks who seek the truth hidden in pain, the practice of mortifications of the
flesh helps to achieve enlightenment and acquire disturbing powers.

**Sensei**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Flurry of Blows; Fast Movement; Improved Evasion; Evasion; 2nd, 6th, 12th,
18th-level Bonus Feats

**Description:** The sensei is a revered teacher who imparts lessons on the oneness of mind, body,
and spirit, along with occasional correction that is subtle and swift.

**Serpent-Fire Adept**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Stunning Fist; 1st, 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th-level
Bonus Feats; Slow Fall; High Jump; Wholeness of Body

**Description:** A serpent-fire adept embraces the secrets of her chakras and mastery of their
energetic flow.

**Sin Monk**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Ki Pool; High Jump; Wholeness of Body; Abundant Step; Empty Body; Perfect
Self

**Description:** (Ex-monk archetype) Martial experts who have allowed such passions to lead them
astray may find themselves gaining powers drawn from their newly embraced sins.

**Softstrike Monk**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Weapon Proficiency; Unarmed Strike; 6th, 10th-level Bonus Feats; Wholeness
of Body; Quivering Palm; Empty Body

**Description:** Softstrike monks employ a nonlethal but effective form of combat.

**Sohei**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Weapon/Armor Proficiency; Stunning Fist; Fast Movement; Increased Unarmed
Damage; Slow Fall; Abundant Step; Purity of Body; Diamond Body; Quivering Palm; Timeless Body;
Tongue of Sun and Moon

**Description:** The sohei are masters of the horse and the hunt.

**Soul Shepherd**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Stunning Fist; Evasion; Improved Evasion; Abundant Step; Quivering Palm

**Description:** Whether studying at the feet of psychopomps or merely imitating the creatures'
philosophy and methods, soul shepherds believe in safeguarding the Cycle of Souls.

**Spirit Master**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Still Mind; Maneuver Training; Purity of Body; Wholeness of Body;
Quivering Palm; Empty Body; Perfect Self

**Description:** Spirit masters are monks who specialize in combating the undead, laying their
corpses to rest, and sending their souls to final judgment.

**Student of Stone**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Evasion; Fast Movement; Bonus Feat; High Jump; Improved Evasion; Abundant
Step

**Description:** (Oread Only) By following the path of the stone, students of stone give up much of
monks' mobility in favor of sheer resilience.

**Terra-Cotta Monk**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Evasion; High Jump; Improved Evasion; Abundant Step;
Quivering Palm; Empty Body

**Description:** Possessing a natural affinity for all things earthen, terracotta monks are experts
at turning the stone around them against their enemies.

**Tetori**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bonus Feats; Flurry of Blows; Slow Fall; High Jump; Abundant Step;
Improved Evasion; Timeless Body; Tongue of Sun and Moon; Diamond Soul; Empty Body

**Description:** The style of the tetori is that of the majestic wrestler - a warrior with a
dizzying array of grabs, holds, and locks with which to bewilder and incapacitate his foes.

**Treetop Monk**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Still Mind; Purity of Body; Abundant Step

**Description:** (Vanara Only) While many vanaras follow traditional monastic training and
traditions, others learn to blend exotic combat and the mysterious forces of ki with the natural
world, allowing them to move through trees and overgrowth to deliver devastating attacks.

**Underfoot Adept**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** 1st-level Bonus Feat; Stunning Fist; High Jump

**Description:** (Halfling Only) An underfoot adept turns his diminutive stature and unorthodox
footwork into a powerful weapon.

**Wanderer**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; 1st-level Bonus Feat; Still Mind; Slow Fall; High Jump;
Wholeness of Body; Abundant Step; Diamond Soul

**Description:** (Human Only) Some monks wander the world in humility to learn and to share wisdom
and philosophy from their teachers with those they meet, often aiding those who are in need.

**Wasteland Meditant**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Stunning Fist; Evasion; Slow Fall; Improved Evasion; Quivering Palm

**Description:** Some monks journey to remote and isolated corners of the world to find serenity and
a heightened sense of awareness.

**Water Dancer**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Flurry of Blows; Stunning Fist; Quivering Palm; 1st, 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th,
and 18th-level Bonus Feats; Unarmed Strike; Evasion; Slow Fall; Improved Evasion; Abundant Step

**Description:** Water dancers derive their martial training from ancient nereid traditions
jealously guarded by these enigmatic fey.

**Weapon Adept**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Stunning Fist; Evasion; Improved Evasion; Timeless Body; Perfect Self

**Description:** While all monks train in both unarmed combat and with weapons, the weapon adept
seeks to become one with his weapons, transforming them into perfect extensions of his own body.

**Wildcat**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Still Mind; Ki Pool; High Jump; Slow Fall; Improved Evasion; Abundant
Step; Diamond Soul; Empty Body

**Description:** A wildcat is a student of the school of hard knocks, who dedicates himself to
learning how to take down foes by any means necessary.

**Windstep Master**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Stunning Fist; Slow Fall; Ki Pool

**Description:** Most monks are agile and fleet of foot, but few can rival a windstep master's
lightness of step.

**Zen Archer**

**Class:** monk

**Modifies or Replaces:** Stunning Fist; Ki Pool; Evasion; Maneuver Training; Still Mind; Purity of
Body; Improved Evasion; Diamond Body; Tongue of the Sun and the Moon

**Description:** The zen archer takes a weapon most other monks eschew and seeks perfection in the
pull of a taut bowstring, the flex of a bow's limbs, and the flight of an arrow fired true.
