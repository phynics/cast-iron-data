If a vehicle has no driver, any creature can take control of the vehicle as long as the creature is
within the driving space of the vehicle and makes a driving check as a free action. The vehicle's
driver can always give over control to another adjacent creature that is within the driving space of
the vehicle as a free action. When a new creature becomes the driver, the vehicle moves on the new
driver's turn, but not on the new driver's first turn after taking control of the vehicle.

If a creature wants to take control of a vehicle from another forcefully, it must pull the driver
off the controls as part of a grapple and take over the driving device as part of a pin. When a
creature successfully pins a vehicle's driver, it can choose to end the grapple immediately. When it
does, the creature moves the current driver 5 feet to any unoccupied space within the vehicle (this
movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity) and becomes the vehicle's new driver.
