The time required to don armor depends on its type; see Table 6-7.

**Don:** This column tells how long it takes a character to put the armor on. (One minute is 10
rounds.) Readying (strapping on) a shield is only a move action.

**Don Hastily:** This column tells how long it takes to put the armor on in a hurry. The armor check
penalty and armor bonus for hastily donned armor are each 1 point worse than normal.

**Remove:** This column tells how long it takes to get the armor off. Removing a shield from the arm
and dropping it is only a move action.

### Table 6-7: Donning Armor

|Armor Type|Don|Don Hastily|Remove|
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|Shield (any)|1 move action|n/a|1 move action|
|Padded, leather, hide, studded leather, or chain shirt|1 minute|5 rounds|1 minute^[1](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor#footnote-1)^|
|Breastplate, scale mail, chainmail, banded mail, or splint mail|4 minutes^[1](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor#footnote-1)^|1 minute|1 minute^[1](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor#footnote-1)^|
|Half-plate or full plate|4 minutes^[2](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor#footnote-2)^|4 minutes^[1](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor#footnote-1)^|1d4+1 minutes^[1](ref/rule/getting-into-and-out-of-armor#footnote-1)^|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

If the character has some help, cut this time in half. A single character doing nothing else can
help one or two adjacent characters. Two characters can't help each other don armor at the same
time.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

The wearer must have help to don this armor. Without help, it can be donned only hastily.
