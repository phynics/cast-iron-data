**Pathfinder Name:** Eagle Knights\
**Scope:** regional\
**Alignment:** NG\
**Values:** community, equality for all, freedom, justice, personal responsibility\
**Goals:** abolish slavery in all its forms, lead the people of the region into a new era of common
rule and individual liberty, maintain vigilance against all threats to the nation's freedom via any
means that don't threaten the core values of the Vangard\
**Allies:** [Breakers of Chains](ref/rule/aff-antislavery), [Thieves' Guild](ref/rule/aff-thieves),
[Erastilians](ref/faith/erastil), [Iomedaeans](ref/faith/iomedae),
[Dwarven Animists](ref/rule/aff-animists), [Shelynites](ref/faith/shelyn),
[The Rebellion](ref/rule/aff-rebels), various empyreal lord cults\
**Enemies:** [Asmodeans](ref/faith/asmodeus), [Crimson Syndicate](ref/rule/aff-profiteers), enemies
of the nation, [Norgorberites](ref/faith/norgorber), [Cult of the Assassin](ref/rule/aff-assassins),
slavers\
**Rivals:** none\
**Also See:** [Vangard of Liberty (faction)](ref/rule/f-vangard)
