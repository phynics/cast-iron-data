Throughout much of the _Skull & Shackles_ Adventure Path, the PCs come into conflict with other
ships on the high seas - whether these conflicts arise as the PCs seek to seize plunder or are
forced to defend themselves from attack, the [ship-to-ship rules](ref/rule/ship-to-ship-combat)
presented in the _Skull & Shackles Player's Guide_ are meant only to suffice for small-scale naval
battles between just a handful of ships at most. But PCs may find themselves involved in some much
larger naval conflicts involving dozens of ships at once. Rather than playing out such large,
complex battles ship by ship, you can use the following rules for fleet battles to determine the
results of these mass conflicts. Note that while the rules presented here are specifically tailored
for individual naval battles that occur in the _Skull & Shackles_ Adventure Path, they can serve to
resolve large-scale naval battles in other campaigns as well.
