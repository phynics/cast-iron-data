Many magic items don't need to be activated. Certain magic items, however, do need to be activated,
especially potions, scrolls, wands, rods, and staves. Unless otherwise noted, activating a magic
item is a standard action.

**Spell Completion Items:** [Activating a spell completion item](ref/rule/using-items) is the
equivalent of casting a spell. It requires concentration and provokes attacks of opportunity. You
lose the spell if your concentration is broken, and you can attempt to activate the item while on
the defensive, as with casting a spell.

**Spell Trigger, Command Word, or Use-Activated Items:** Activating any of these kinds of items does
not require concentration and does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
