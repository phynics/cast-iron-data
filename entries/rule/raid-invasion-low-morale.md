**Raid:** An enemy finds the militia. Deactivate all refuges in a random settlement with at least
one active refuge. Anyone who was hiding in a refuge is taken prisoner and can be retrieved during
the following week's Activity phase using a [Rescue Character](ref/rule/rescue-character) action (DC
= 5 + the militia's rank). _Mitigate:_ A successful DC 20 Security check per person reduces his
chance of being taken prisoner by 50%.

**Invasion:** A dangerous creature or enemy invades; the GM should present the PCs with a random
combat encounter whose CR equals the PCs' Average Party Level + 1.

**Low Morale\*:** The militia takes a -2 penalty on Loyalty checks. _Twice:_ The event becomes
persistent.
