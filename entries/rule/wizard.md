**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 90

A character who chooses [wizard](ref/class/wizard) as his secondary class gains the following
secondary class features.

**School:** At 1st level, he chooses a [school of magic](ref/ability/arcane-schools) in which to
specialize. For all powers of that school, he treats his character level as his effective wizard
level.

**Familiar:** At 3rd level, he gains a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar), treating his character
level as his effective wizard level.

**School Power:** At 7th level, he gains the 1st-level powers of his chosen school. If any of those
powers grant an extra effect at 20th level, the character does not gain that extra effect.

**Cantrip:** At 11th level, if he has an Intelligence score of 10 or higher, he chooses a wizard
cantrip from his chosen school and can cast that cantrip as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) at will. He uses his character level as the
caster level and Intelligence as the cantrip's key ability score.

**Discovery:** At 15th level, he gains an [arcane discovery](ref/class/wizard) or wizard bonus feat,
treating his character level as his effective wizard level.

**Greater School Power:** At 19th level, he gains the 8th-level power of his chosen school.
