Below are example buildings and their component rooms. Note that many of these buildings can also be

  Artisan, Farm, Fort_
- [Group 3:](ref/rule/buildings3) _Garrison, Granery, Graveyard, Guildhall, Herbalist, Hospital,
  House, Inn, Jail_
- [Group 4:](ref/rule/buildings4) _Library, Luxury Store, Magic Shop, Magical Academy, Mansion,
  Menagerie, Military Academy, Mill_
- [Group 5:](ref/rule/buildings5) _Mint, Monastery, Monument, Museum, Noble Villa, Observatory,
  Orphanage, Palace, Pier_
- [Group 6:](ref/rule/buildings6) _School, Shop, Shrine, Smithy, Stable, Stockyard, Tannery, Tavern_
- [Group 7:](ref/rule/buildings7) _Temple, Tenement, Theater, Town Hall, Trade Shop, University,
  Watchtower, Waterfront_
