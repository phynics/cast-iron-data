Espionage teams help undermine and infiltrate enemies.

### Moles (Tier 1 Espionage)

**Recruitment** Secrecy DC 15\
**Granted Actions** Secure Cache\
**Size** three people\
**Upgrades To** propagandists\
A team of moles can place minor [caches](ref/rule/caches) of equipment for later use.

### Propagandists (Tier 2 Espionage)

**Cost** 250 gp\
**Granted Actions** Secure Cache, Spread Propaganda\
**Size** three people\
**Upgrades From** moles\
Propagandists can spread the militia's message to other towns and cities. They can also place
intermediate caches.

### Saboteurs (Tier 3 Espionage)

**Cost** 1,000 gp\
**Granted Actions** Sabotage, Secure Cache, Spread Propaganda\
**Size** three people\
**Upgrades From** propagandists\
Saboteurs can undermine the actions of opposing governments. They can also place major caches.

### Spies (Tier 3 Espionage)

**Cost** 1,000 gp\
**Granted Actions** Covert Action, Secure Cache, Spread Propaganda\
**Size** three people\
**Upgrades From** propagandists\
Spies can orchestrate covert actions and are capable of stashing and retrieving major caches.
