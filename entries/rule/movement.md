There are three movement scales, as follows:

- **Tactical,** for combat, measured in feet (or 5-foot squares) per round.
- **Local,** for exploring an area, measured in feet per minute.
- **Overland,** for getting from place to place, measured in miles per hour or miles per day.

### Modes of Movement

While moving at the different movement scales, creatures generally _walk, hustle,_ or _run._

**Walk:** A walk represents unhurried but purposeful movement (3 miles per hour for an unencumbered
adult human).

**Hustle:** A hustle is a jog (about 6 miles per hour for an unencumbered human). A character moving
his speed twice in a single round, or moving that speed in the same round that he or she performs a
standard action or another move action, is hustling when he or she moves.

**Run (×3):** Moving three times speed is a running pace for a character in heavy armor (about 7
miles per hour for a human in full plate).

**Run (×4):** Moving four times speed is a running pace for a character in light, medium, or no
armor (about 12 miles per hour for an unencumbered human, or 9 miles per hour for a human in
chainmail). See Table 7-6 for details.
