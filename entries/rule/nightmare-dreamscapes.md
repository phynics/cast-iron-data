**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 162

The following section provides guidelines for creating nightmare dreamscapes: realms of fear and
horror within the [Dimension of Dreams](ref/rule/dimension-of-dreams). A nightmare typically has a
goal, which the dreamer can complete within the nightmare dreamscape to win freedom from it, and
features, which represent the ways in which the dreamer's fears manifest. A nightmare's goal and
features might come entirely from the dreamer's own mind or be chosen by the creator of a
supernatural effect.
