**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 178\
**Pathfinder Name:** Storm Kindlers\
**Scope:** regional\
**Alignment:** CN\
**Values:** enlightenment, fearlessness, freedom, unity of consciousness, worship of
[Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh)\
**Goals:** merge with the consciousness of the god Gozreh, recover and reclaim lost lore from
previous generations of Storm Seekers\
**Allies:** druids of the [Green Faith](ref/faith/green-faith), [Gozrens](ref/faith/gozreh),
hermits, madmen, [The Ancient School](ref/rule/aff-i-school), vagabonds, wanderers\
**Enemies:** heretics of Gozreh, humanoid tribes (boggards, lizardfolk), pirates, scavengers/looters\
**Rivals:** none

### Associated Concepts

**Prestige Classes:** [Storm Kindler](ref/pclass/storm-kindler)

**Archetypes:** [Storm Dreamer](ref/arc-medium/storm-dreamer) (Medium),
[Tempest Druid](ref/arc-druid/tempest-druid) (Druid)

**Feats:** [Storm-Lashed](ref/feat/storm-lashed)

**Magic Items:** Many of the magic items valued by the Seekers were first created by their
predecessors; explorers of the region can sometimes find these items in creatures' lairs or ancient
ruins, but the Seekers take offense when those who aren't at least affiliated with them claim such
items as their own: [Amulet of the Storm](ref/magic-wondrous/amulet-of-the-storm),
[Goz Mask](ref/magic-wondrous/goz-mask), [Jar of Lightning](ref/magic-wondrous/jar-of-lightning),
[Storm Kindler's Rod](ref/magic-artifact/storm-kindlers-rod) (minor artifact)

**Spells:** [Storm Sight](ref/spell/storm-sight)
