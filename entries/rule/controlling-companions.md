**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 140

How a companion works depends on the campaign as well as the companion's nature, intelligence, and
abilities. In some cases, the rules do not specify whether you or the GM controls the companion. If
you're entirely in control, the companion acts like a subsidiary PC, doing exactly what you want
just like a true PC. If the GM is control, you can make suggestions or attempt to influence the
companion, but the GM determines whether the creature is willing or able to attempt what you want.
