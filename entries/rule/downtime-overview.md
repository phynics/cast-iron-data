**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 76

The key parts of the downtime rules that you'll be referencing often are the following:

- Explanation of the [downtime terminology](ref/rule/downtime-terminology) used throughout this
  section.
- [Earning downtime capital](ref/rule/gaining-capital) such as Goods, Influence, and Labor.
- The [phase sequence](ref/rule/downtime-phases) for using downtime.
- The kinds of [activities](ref/rule/downtime-activities) you can do with this system.
- Constructing [rooms](ref/rule/rooms-and-teams), the building blocks of buildings such as
  guildhalls or temples, and recruiting teams such as apprentices and guards.
- [Example](ref/rule/buildings-and-organizations) buildings constructed out of rooms, and example
  organizations built out of teams.
- Positive and negative [events](ref/rule/downtime-events) that can occur during downtime.
- Downtime tracking sheet (included in physical book only).
