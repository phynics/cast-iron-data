When the stakes of a conflict are no more than a plot device, it's okay for them to be somewhat
nebulous. Other times, the stakes are concrete, and you'll want a precise way to measure them. For
this purpose, you can use the rules for [gaining capital in the downtime system](ref/rule/downtime)
or the [influence system for organizations](ref/rule/organizational-influence).
