For some, there is only rage. In the ways of their people, in the fury of their passion, in the howl
of battle, conflict is all these brutal souls know. Savages, hired muscle, masters of vicious
martial techniques, they are not soldiers or professional warriors - they are the battle possessed,
creatures of slaughter and spirits of war. Known as barbarians, these warmongers know little of
training, preparation, or the rules of warfare; for them, only the moment exists, with the foes that
stand before them and the knowledge that the next moment might hold their death. They possess a
sixth sense in regard to danger and the endurance to weather all that might entail. These brutal
warriors might rise from all walks of life, both civilized and savage, though whole societies
embracing such philosophies roam the wild places of the world. Within barbarians storms the primal
spirit of battle, and woe to those who face their rage.

**Role:** Barbarians excel in combat, possessing the martial prowess and fortitude to take on foes
seemingly far superior to themselves. With rage granting them boldness and daring beyond that of
most other warriors, barbarians charge furiously into battle and ruin all who would stand in their
way.

**Alignment:** Any nonlawful

**Hit Die:** d12

**Starting Wealth:** 3d6 × 10 gp (average 105 gp)

**Skill Points per Level:** 4 + Int modifier

**Class Skills (Str):** Climb, Swim

**Class Skills (Dex):** Acrobatics, Ride

**Class Skills (Int):** Craft (any), Knowledge (nature)

**Class Skills (Wis):** Perception, Survival

**Class Skills (Cha):** Handle Animal, Intimidate

### Class Features {#class-features}

All of the following are class features of the barbarian.

#### Weapon and Armor Proficiency {#weapon-and-armor-proficiencies}

A barbarian is proficient with all simple and martial weapons, light armor, medium armor, and
shields (except tower shields).

#### Fast Movement (Ex) {#fast-movement}

A barbarian's base speed is faster than the norm for her race by +10 feet. This benefit applies only
when she is wearing no armor, light armor, or medium armor, and not carrying a heavy load. Apply
this bonus before modifying the barbarian's speed because of any load carried or armor worn. This
bonus stacks with any other bonuses to the barbarian's base speed.

#### Rage (Ex) {#rage}

A barbarian can call upon inner reserves of strength and ferocity, granting her additional combat
prowess. Starting at 1st level, a barbarian can rage for a number of rounds per day equal to 4 + her
Constitution modifier. At each level after 1st, she can rage for 2 additional rounds. Temporary
increases to Constitution, such as those gained from rage and spells like
_[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance),_ do not increase the total number of rounds that a
barbarian can rage per day. A barbarian can enter rage as a free action. The total number of rounds
of rage per day is renewed after resting for 8 hours, although these hours do not need to be
consecutive.

While in rage, a barbarian gains a +4 morale bonus to her Strength and Constitution, as well as a +2
morale bonus on Will saves. In addition, she takes a -2 penalty to Armor Class. The increase to
Constitution grants the barbarian 2 hit points per Hit Dice, but these disappear when the rage ends
and are not lost first like [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points). While in rage, a
barbarian cannot use any Charisma-, Dexterity-, or Intelligence-based skills (except Acrobatics,
Fly, Intimidate, and Ride) or any ability that requires patience or concentration.

A barbarian can end her rage as a free action and is [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) after rage for a
number of rounds equal to 2 times the number of rounds spent in the rage. A barbarian cannot enter a
new rage while fatigued or [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) but can otherwise enter rage multiple
times during a single encounter or combat. If a barbarian falls unconscious, her rage immediately
ends, placing her in peril of death.

#### Rage Powers (Ex) {#rage-powers}

As a barbarian gains levels, she learns to use her rage in new ways. Starting at 2nd level, a
barbarian gains a rage power. She gains another rage power for every two levels of barbarian
attained after 2nd level. A barbarian gains the benefits of rage powers only while raging, and some
of these powers require the barbarian to take an action first. Unless otherwise noted, a barbarian
cannot select an individual power more than once.

#### Uncanny Dodge (Ex) {#uncanny-dodge}

At 2nd level, a barbarian gains the ability to react to danger before her senses would normally
allow her to do so. She cannot be caught [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed), nor does she lose her
Dex bonus to AC if the attacker is invisible. She still loses her Dexterity bonus to AC if
immobilized. A barbarian with this ability can still lose her Dexterity bonus to AC if an opponent
successfully uses the feint action against her.

If a barbarian already has uncanny dodge from a different class, she automatically gains improved
uncanny dodge (see below) instead.

#### Trap Sense (Ex) {#trap-sense}

At 3rd level, a barbarian gains a +1 bonus on Reflex saves made to avoid traps and a +1 dodge bonus
to AC against attacks made by traps. These bonuses increase by +1 every three barbarian levels
thereafter (6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th level). Trap sense bonuses gained from multiple classes
stack.

#### Improved Uncanny Dodge (Ex) {#improved-uncanny-dodge}

At 5th level and higher, a barbarian can no longer be [flanked](ref/rule/flanking). This defense
denies a rogue the ability to [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) the barbarian by flanking
her, unless the attacker has at least four more rogue levels than the target has barbarian levels.

If a character already has uncanny dodge (see above) from another class, the levels from the classes
that grant uncanny dodge stack to determine the minimum rogue level required to flank the character.

#### Damage Reduction (Ex) {#damage-reduction}

At 7th level, a barbarian gains [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). Subtract 1 from the
damage the barbarian takes each time she is dealt damage from a weapon or a natural attack. At 10th
level, and every three barbarian levels thereafter (13th, 16th, and 19th level), this damage
reduction rises by 1 point. Damage reduction can reduce damage to 0 but not below 0.

#### Greater Rage (Ex) {#greater-rage}

At 11th level, when a barbarian enters rage, the morale bonus to her Strength and Constitution
increases to +6 and the morale bonus on her Will saves increases to +3.

#### Indomitable Will (Ex) {#indomitable-will}

While in rage, a barbarian of 14th level or higher gains a +4 bonus on Will saves to resist
enchantment spells. This bonus stacks with all other modifiers, including the morale bonus on Will
saves she also receives during her rage.

#### Tireless Rage (Ex) {#tireless-rage}

Starting at 17th level, a barbarian no longer becomes fatigued at the end of her rage.

#### Mighty Rage (Ex) {#mighty-rage}

At 20th level, when a barbarian enters rage, the morale bonus to her Strength and Constitution
increases to +8 and the morale bonus on her Will saves increases to +4.

### Ex-Barbarians

A barbarian who becomes lawful loses the ability to rage and cannot gain more levels in barbarian.
She retains all other benefits of the class.

### Alternate Capstones

When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Unstoppable (Ex)**

At 20th level, nothing can kill the barbarian, though not for lack of trying. The barbarian gains DR
3/- or increases the value of any existing [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) by 3. In
addition, she gains energy [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to acid, cold, electricity, and fire
equal to her DR/- value.

### Archetypes

From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; Improved Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** Some barbarians disdain the hides and leather used as armor by most of their kin.
Instead they master the heaviest of armors, even those created by more civilized people, to gain
greater protection and stability in battle.

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; 4th, 8th, 12th-level Rage Power

**Description:** While some barbarians take on bestial aspects in their _rages,_ the beastkin
berserker descends so deeply into primal fury that she actually transforms into an animal.

**Description:** Breakers find the need to destroy their surroundings.

great strength.

**Cave Dweller**

[Damage Reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)

**Dreadnought**

**Description:** Unstoppable and fearless, the dreadnought turns the terror of her enemies into
violent physical power.

**Drunken Brute**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement

powers.

**Drunken Rager**

12th-level Rage Power

**Description:** These hotheaded, hard-drinking ruffians and brawlers are as dangerous in the midst

**Elemental Kin**

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trap Sense

**Fearsome Defender**

**Flesheater**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Rage; 2nd, 8th, and 14th-level Rage Powers; Greater Rage; Mighty Rage

**Description:** A flesheater eats flesh to create a spiritual bond between herself and the consumed
creature, allowing her to take on aspects of the creature that served as the meal.

**Geminate Invoker**

**Modifies or Replaces:** Alignment; Class Skills; Rage; Trap Sense; 4th, 8th, 12th-level Rage

**Giant Stalker**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Rage; Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense

**Description:** Giant stalkers are followers of the Mammoth Lords who have trained since childhood

**Hateful Rager**

**Description:** (Half-Orc Only) From a young age, many half-orcs are treated cruelly, bullied,

they channel into a raw fury and unleash against their enemies.

**Hurler**

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement

the kill.

**Invulnerable Rager**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge;

**Description:** Some barbarians learn to take whatever comes their way, shrugging off mortal wounds

**Jungle Rager**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; Improved Uncanny Dodge;

**Description:** A jungle rager can disappear from sight, strike from hiding, and use the
environment to shield herself from harm.

**Mad Dog**

**Modifies or Replaces:** 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th, 18th-level Rage Powers; Rage; Uncanny Dodge;
Improved Uncanny Dodge; [Damage Reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction); Indomitable Will

**Description:** Though named for the wild savages who fight alongside rabid dogs, mad dogs employ
all manner of beasts as their battle brethren.

**Mooncursed**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Rage; Improved Uncanny Dodge; Greater Rage; Mighty Rage

**Description:** Some barbarians exhibit an unusual form of lycanthropy powered by their fury. Such

**Mounted Fury**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** Many barbarian tribes are masters of the horse, teaching their members how to ride
from a young age.

**Numerian Liberator**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge; Indomitable Will

**Description:** While many native Kellids oppose the Technic League and its espousal of foul alien
artifacts, the most stalwart and staunchly dedicated of these technophobic traditionalists refer to
themselves as Numerian liberators.

**Pack Hunter**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Rage Powers; Trap Sense; 6th-level Rage Power

**Description:** Pack hunter barbarians team up to hunt dangerous prey to feed, to protect their
camps, and as a rite of passage.

**Pack Rager**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th-level Rage Powers;

**Description:** Barbarian _rages_ can be a thing of savage beauty, exhibiting a lethal grace. While

**Primal Hunter**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Rage

**Description:** Rather than exploding with anger, primal hunters focus their _rage_ to strike

**Scarred Rager**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; Improved Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** The scarred rager believes each wound tells the tales of her prowess and bravery.

**Sea Reaver**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; Improved Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** Some sea reavers are no more than hunters of the open sea, while others are raiders

**Sharptooth**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; 6th and
12th-level Rage Powers

**Description:** Coastal hunters and agents of the ocean's rage, sharptooth barbarians emulate the

**Shoanti Burn Rider**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th-level Rage
Powers; Improved Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** The Shoanti tribes who live in the Cinderlands have turned the deadly emberstorms

thrill-seekers are known as burn riders, and their exploits are celebrated by all Shoanti.

**Superstitious**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trap Sense; [Damage Reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)

**Description:** The superstitious barbarian is naturally distrusting, developing keen senses to

**Titan Mauler**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; Improved Uncanny Dodge;
Indomitable Will

**Description:** In lands overrun by giants, dragons, and other hulking beasts, entire fellowships
of barbarians hone tactics and traditions with one purpose - to bring low these massive foes.

**Totem Warrior**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** None

**Description:** The totem warrior has a special totem that is the patron of her tribe.

**True Primitive**

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fast Movement; Trap Sense

**Description:** Isolated and xenophobic tribes that dwell in areas untouched by civilization often

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Uncanny Dodge; Trap Sense; Improved Uncanny Dodge;

**Urban Barbarian**

**Class:** barbarian

**Class:** barbarian

**Modifies or Replaces:** Rage; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** Rages are barely controlled, but there are those who wholly give in to their more
