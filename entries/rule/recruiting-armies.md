**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 241

If you are a leader of a kingdom, that fact is sufficient for you to recruit armies from your
citizens. If you are not the leader of a kingdom, the GM may decide that you need to achieve some
sort of in-game accomplishment to earn the respect and renown needed to raise an army.

Being able to recruit a monster army usually requires a special quest or adventure; you can't simply
recruit an army of goblins to serve you because you've found a tribe of goblins or know they live in
your kingdom.
