By definition, a tavern is a business where customers can purchase alcoholic beverages. Sounds
simple enough, right? But just as with modern bars, a fantasy tavern can be far more than just a
place to get a flagon of ale. Defining what characters can find within a particular tavern goes a
long way toward giving the place a unique feel.
