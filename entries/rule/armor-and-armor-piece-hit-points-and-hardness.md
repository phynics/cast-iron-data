To determine the hit points and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of your armor, use the hardness for the
weakest material, and to determine hit points, add the total armor bonus (including the +1 for
wearing a complete suit of armor) and multiply that value by 5. If you need to determine the
hardness and hit points for a single armor piece, it has the hardness of its main material and hit
points equal to its armor bonus (treat as +1 for armor pieces with a +0 armor bonus) × 5.
[Hardness for substances can be found in Table 7-13](ref/rule/breaking-things).
