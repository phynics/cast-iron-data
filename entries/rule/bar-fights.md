Whether or not the player characters start it, an evening of drunken antics can often culminate in a
chaotic brawl. Most times such a fight can be handled with the standard combat rules, especially
when deadly weapons or harmful spells are brought to bear. However, the following system can be used
to simulate an out-and-out slugfest where battlefield positions are essentially irrelevant and the
participants are merely trying to knock one another out, not kill each other.
