**Week of Serenity:** For the next week, the militia gains a +5 bonus on all organization checks,
and its [training](ref/rule/training-mt) score increases by double the normal amount during the next
week's Activity phase. _Twice:_ There is no additional effect.

**War Games:** A trainer surprises the militia with war games, giving it a training boost. Add the
militia's [rank](ref/rule/rank-mt) to its training score.

**Night Ops:** The weather, moon, and other factors are just right for late-night operations. This
week, the PCs gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks after dark. _Twice:_ The bonus
increases to +5.
