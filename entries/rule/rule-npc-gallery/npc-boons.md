A previous section presents [an optional system for boons](ref/rule/npc-boons): minor in-game
bonuses and benefits specific NPCs can grant to PCs who befriend them. This system of favors and
benefits encourages PCs to invest greater interest in working with NPCs and creates a way to reward
characters with something other than experience and treasure. At the end of the statistics for each
of the following NPCs is a suggestion for a minor benefit that is appropriate to the NPC and that
works within this system. GMs who wish to employ these favors or create their own boons might use
those presented here as guides for new benefits. At the same time, GMs should not feel that every
one of the following NPCs has to offer exactly these boons, or any boon at all. Those interested in
designing their own boons or customizing them to their game should feel free to modify these effects
however they feel best suits their needs.
