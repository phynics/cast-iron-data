**Knowledge Check** **(Scholars):** The scholars try to answer any question that could normally be
answered with a successful [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) check. Rather than attempting a
Knowledge check, though, the PCs attempt a Secrecy check. Add the militia's [rank](ref/rule/rank-mt)
to this result, and treat the total as the DC achieved in the desired Knowledge category. This
action can also be used to identify magic items or inquire about the abilities of an NPC or monster.
