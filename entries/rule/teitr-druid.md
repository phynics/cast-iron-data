The druid's [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) feats have been adjusted to account
for changes in the feat tree.

**Animal Feats**\
Animal companions can select from the following feats: [Acrobatic](ref/feat/acrobatic), Armor
Proficiency ([light](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-light), [medium](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-medium),
and [heavy](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-heavy)), [Athletic](ref/feat/athletic),
[Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight), [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Diehard](ref/feat/diehard), [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance),
[Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude), [Improved Initiative](ref/feat/improved-initiative),
[Improved Natural Attack](ref/feat/improved-natural-attack),
[Intimidating Prowess](ref/feat/intimidating-prowess), [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will),
[Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/lightning-reflexes),
[Powerful Maneuvers](ref/rule/powerful-maneuvers), [Run](ref/feat/run),
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus), [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack),
[Stealthy](ref/feat/stealthy), [Toughness](ref/feat/toughness), and
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus). Animal companions with an Intelligence of 3 or higher can
select any feat they are physically capable of using. GMs may expand this list to include feats from
other sources.
