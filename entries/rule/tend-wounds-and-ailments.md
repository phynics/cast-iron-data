This use of Survival functions as the [Heal core skill](ref/skill/heal).

**Action:** Providing first aid, treating a wound, or treating poison is a standard action. Treating
a disease or tending to a creature that has been wounded by a spike growth or spike stones spell
requires 10 minutes of work. Treating deadly wounds requires 1 hour of work. Providing long-term
care requires 8 hours of light activity.

**Try Again:** Varies. Generally speaking, you can't try to tend wounds and ailments without
witnessing proof of the original check's failure. You can always retry a check to provide first aid
if the target is still alive.
