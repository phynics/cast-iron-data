Roleplaying games are, at their cores, simulations, with most rules focusing on how to perform epic
feats and participate in the fantastic adventures of legendary heroes. Thus, games like the
Pathfinder RPG highlight the most common elements of fantasy stories: battle, magic, monsters, and
the like, detailing facets of the simulation that benefit from or require more detail than a GM
might comfortably arbitrate on his own.
