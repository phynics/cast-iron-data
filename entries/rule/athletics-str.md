### Athletics (Str; Armor Check Penalty)

You are skilled at deeds of physical prowess such as leaping, scaling walls, and swimming. Raw
lifting ability uses a Strength check rather than an Athletics check.

**Functions:** Catch, climb, jump, swim.

**Core Skills:** Acrobatics (jumping only), Climb, Swim.
