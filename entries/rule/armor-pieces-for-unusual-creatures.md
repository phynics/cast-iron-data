Armor pieces for unusually big creatures, unusually little creatures, and non-humanoid creatures
(such as horses) have different costs and weights from those given in Tables [5-7](ref/rule/arms),
[5-8](ref/rule/legs), and [5-9](ref/rule/torso). Refer to the appropriate line in
[Table 6-8](ref/rule/armor-for-unusual-creatures) and apply the multipliers to cost and weight. For
animals with four or more legs, the armor for half of the legs counts as the leg armor piece, and
the armor for the other half counts as the arm armor piece.
