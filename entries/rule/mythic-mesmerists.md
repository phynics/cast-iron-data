Mythic mesmerists acquire their powers because their inherent mythic potential already gives them a
knack for manipulating others. Many mythic mesmerists are believed to be godlings descended from the
likes of [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) or [Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber), although such claims
are best taken with a grain of salt. Mesmerists frequently take the path of the **trickster** (and
unlike with many mythic occult practitioners, there is no special name for mesmerists who do).
