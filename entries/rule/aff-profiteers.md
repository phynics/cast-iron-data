**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 28\
**Pathfinder Name:** Aspis Consortium\
**Scope:** global\
**Alignment:** NE\
**Values:** development of new revenue streams, elimination of competition, indirect political
power, monopolies, profit\
**Goals:** finance new ventures by securing exotic goods for customers, providing consultation
services, and taking control of rare and profitable resources; maximize earnings; secure capital\
**Allies:** aspiring tyrants; [Golden League](ref/pclass/golden-legionnaire); numerous municipal,
national, and organizational authorities throughout the region and beyond; sundry jungle tribes\
**Enemies:** competing mercantile interests, [Vangard of Liberty](ref/rule/aff-vangard), Pirate
Council, [The Ancient School](ref/rule/aff-i-school),
[Exploration Association](ref/rule/aff-scholars)\
**Rivals:** Exploration Association\
**Also See:** [Crimson Syndicate (faction)](ref/rule/f-profiteers)
