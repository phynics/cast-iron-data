A Fort is a walled encampment for military forces outside a settlement. You can upgrade a Watchtower
to a Fort (Unrest decreases when you do so, just as if you had built the Fort from scratch).

**Terrain:** Any land.

**Effect:** Stability +2, Defense +4, increase Consumption by 1 BP; Unrest decreases by 1 when
completed. If this hex becomes a settlement, this improvement counts as one Barracks and one Stables
building.

**Cost:** 24 BP.
