You can retrain skill ranks you have assigned to skills. Retraining skill ranks takes 5 days. When
the training period ends, reassign a number of skill ranks up to your Intelligence bonus (minimum
1), removing them from your existing skill (or skills) and adding them to a different skill (or
skills).

If retraining skill ranks means you no longer qualify for a feat or other ability you have, you
can't use that feat or ability until you meet the qualifications again. (Or you can retrain that
feat or other ability.)
