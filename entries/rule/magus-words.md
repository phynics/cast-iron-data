**0-Level Magus Words:** Acid Burn (Acid), Cold Snap (Cold), Decipher (Language), Flame Jet (Fire),
Force Block (Armor), Lift (Gravity), Sense Magic (Detection), Spark (Electricity)

**1st-Level Magus Words:** Burning Flash (Fire), Dash (Time), Decipher\* (Language), Fade
(Concealing), Force Block\* (Armor), Force Shield (Armor), Glide (Flight), Radiance (Illumination),
Shock Arc (Electricity)

**2nd-Level Magus Words:** Accelerate (Time), Burning Flash\* (Fire), Corrosive Bolt (Acid),
Decelerate (Time), Disappear (Concealing), Discordant Note (Sonic), Enhance Form (Body), Float
(Flight), Force Bolt (Force), Frost Fingers (Cold), Gloom (Illumination), Suppress (Dispelling)

**3rd-Level Magus Words:** Altered Form (Change), Blizzard (Weather), Dimensional Hop
(Teleportation), Fire Blast (Fire), Force Armor (Armor), Lightning Blast (Electricity), Soar
(Flight), Suppress\* (Dispelling), Wind Wall (Wall)

**4th-Level Magus Words:** Acid Wave (Acid), Altered Form\* (Change), Bestial Form (Change),
Blizzard\* (Weather), Borrow Future (Time), Disappear\* (Concealing), Fire Wall (Wall), Ice Blast
(Cold), Ice Wall (Wall), Life Leech (Death), Perfect Form (Body), Sound Blast (Sonic)

**5th-Level Magus Words:** Ball Lightning (Electricity), Bestial Form\* (Change), Cinder Storm
(Fire), Dimensional Jump (Teleportation), Force Blast (Force), Monstrous Form (Change), Stone Wall
(Wall)

**6th-Level Magus Words:** Force Ward (Armor), Monstrous Form\* (Change), Unmake (Dispelling)
