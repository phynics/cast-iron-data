The life of an adventurer has never been safe or comfortable. Terrible dangers lurk around every
corner and the threat of death is a constant companion. Despite such grim realities, far more
horrifying fates await those who find themselves facing off against true darkness: nightmares that
thirst for the tears of the innocent and hunger for the flesh of the living. Adventurers who find
themselves in a horror game must be prepared to face terror, madness, and threats to their very
souls.
