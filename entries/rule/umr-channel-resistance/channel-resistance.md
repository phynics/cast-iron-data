**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 291\
A creature with this special quality (usually an undead) is less easily affected by channel energy.
A creature with channel resistance adds its listed bonus on saves to resist the effects of channel
energy, including effects that rely on the use of channel energy (such as the
[Command Undead](ref/feat/command-undead) feat).

**Format**

**Details:** channel resistance +4

**Location**

**Details:** Defensive Abilities
