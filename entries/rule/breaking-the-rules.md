**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 147

While these guidelines stress the importance of realism, don't forget that you're designing a
fantasy world! Anything goes - feel free to have rivers flowing uphill, mountains that float on the
ocean, deserts or forests that move, or swaths of ocean where the water drops away to expose
portions of the sea bed. As long as you realize that these locations are unrealistic, you can design
for them and explain them however you wish.
