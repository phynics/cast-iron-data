**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 44

Conventions are hotbeds of gaming opportunity. While many RPG events available at game conventions
are single-session adventures, timed dungeon runs, or tournaments, others are run through organized
play groups like the Pathfinder Society which allow players to keep the same character over the
course of several games (or conventions!), accumulating experience, gear, and notoriety. Campaigns
that operate via game conventions and other public events lose a great deal of the camaraderie of a
standard campaign due to the infrequency of sessions and the variable player mix, but such events
can be perfect for players without a lot of free time or other players nearby.
