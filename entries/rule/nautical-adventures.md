**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 214

Water can also provide the setting for a different and unique game experience: the nautical
adventure. In such a scenario, the effects and dangers of underwater adventuring are replaced by
surface hazards as the PCs and their opponents use vehicles like ships and boats to navigate the
terrain. For the most part, shipboard adventures can be resolved normally, with a combat taking
place aboard a ship functioning almost identically to one that occurs on land. If the combat happens
during a storm or in heavy seas, treat the ship's deck as difficult terrain. Remember to take into
account the effects on spellcasters' [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks due to weather
or the motion of the ship's deck.
