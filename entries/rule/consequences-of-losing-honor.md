If your honor score reaches 0, you take a -2 penalty on Will saving throws and Charisma-based
checks, representing your sense of shame. If you are part of an honor-bound institution, your lack
of honor may bring shame upon the institution, and cause its leaders to punish you.

You may renounce your code of honor at any time. You lose all honor points and benefits from honor,
but do not take the penalty for having 0 honor points (not having a code is not the same as
flaunting your code). Any characters who believe in that code refuse to speak or deal with you any
more than they must. Your NPC allies avoid you. Your honorable institution declares you an enemy.
Even those who have no association with your former code may steer clear of you, fearing retribution
from your honorable institution.
