An Aqueduct brings water from alpine lakes and rivers to lowland cities where water is scarce or
insufficient for the local populace. A finished series of Aqueduct hexes must connect to a hill or
mountain hex (with a river or lake) on one end and a settlement on the other end; otherwise, you do
not gain its benefit.

**Terrain:** One end must be hill or mountain hex; can pass through any type of hex.

**Effect:** Loyalty +1, Stability +1, allows settlement to build water-dependent buildings.

**Cost:** As a Road, except the cost is not doubled for hexes with rivers (see
[Table 4-6: Terrain and Terrain Improvements](ref/rule/terrain-improvements)).
