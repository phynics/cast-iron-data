You can use downtime to rest and recover. It is assumed that you spend 8 hours resting at night,
which allows you to recover 1 hp per level per day and 1 point of ability damage for each affected
ability score. If you spend a full day of downtime resting in bed, you recover another 1 hp per
level per day and another 1 point of ability damage for each affected ability score.
