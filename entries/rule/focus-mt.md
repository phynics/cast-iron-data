**Focus:** When the PCs create a militia, the party members as a group must decide which
organization check they want their organization to concentrate on. Their bonuses for that
organization check will increase more quickly than for their organization's other two checks, which
are considered their secondary checks (see [Table 6-1](ref/rule/militia-terminology)). If they'd
like to focus on their militia's reputation and influence in various settlements, they should choose
Loyalty. If they prefer to concentrate on espionage and subverting their enemies, they should pick
Secrecy. They should choose Security if they want to prioritize martial force and keeping militia
members safe.
