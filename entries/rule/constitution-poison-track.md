1. Healthy
1. Weakened
1. Impaired
1. Disabled
1. Unconscious
1. Dead

**Weakened:** A character whose health is weakened by Constitution poison takes a -2 penalty on all
Fortitude saves and Constitution checks. Every time she attempts a Fortitude save against the
poison, whether she succeeds or fails, she takes damage as on initial exposure.

**Impaired:** A character impaired by Constitution poison takes an additional -2 penalty on the
above checks.

**Disabled:** A character disabled by Constitution poison gains the disabled condition. If she takes
a standard action, her hit points drop by 1 or to -1, whichever is worse.

**Unconscious:** A character rendered unconscious by Constitution poison enters a state of shock and
can't be woken by any means as long as she remains in this state.

**Dead:** The character's immune system is ravaged by the poison, and she expires.
