In games of pure chance, luck should rule the day. Neither the player nor the house is in control -
the dice are, and no ranks in Profession (gambler) will help a character win. But this is as it
should be. Players don't want to win at craps because their characters have high Dexterity scores -
they want to win because the dice are hot tonight.

Of course, cheating is the only way to change your luck in a game of chance. If someone at the table
wants to cheat, Sleight of Hand is the most useful skill, but Disable Device might also be allowed.
In addition, magic can be quite useful for cheating. Spells like mage hand, silent image, and modify
memory can turn bad results into good results. Getting caught, however, can turn these good results
into much, much worse outcomes.
