The Magister guides the kingdom's higher learning and magic, promoting education and knowledge among
the citizens and representing the interests of magic, science, and academia. In most kingdoms, the
Magister is a sage, a wizard, or a priest of a deity of knowledge, and oversees the governmental
bureaucracy except regarding finance.

**Benefit:** Add your Charisma modifier or Intelligence modifier to Economy.

**Vacancy Penalty:** Economy decreases by 4.
