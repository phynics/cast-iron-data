**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 132

While the [Leadership](ref/feat/leadership) feat excels at granting the widest variety of cohorts
and followers, there are a number of other ways is which you can introduce Leadership into your
campaign. The following are a few different forms of Leadership, most of which can be taken at a
lower level and then upgraded to the full Leadership feat at some later point in the campaign, if so
desired. All of the variant feats in this section are optional, and Vile Leadership is suited only
for evil characters. A player should get her GM's permission before selecting any of these feats.

- [Groom](ref/feat/groom)
- [Light Bearer](ref/feat/light-bearer)
- [Page](ref/feat/page)
- [Recruits](ref/feat/recruits)
- [Vile Leadership](ref/feat/vile-leadership)
- [Weapon Bearer](ref/feat/weapon-bearer)
