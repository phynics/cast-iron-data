**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 17

The capricious and carefree gnomes of the world are widespread and varied. They typically either
form their own gnome communities or integrate themselves into other humanoid societies.

### Gnome Homeland

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-30|**Forest:** You gain access to the [Log Roller](ref/trait/log-roller) regional trait and the [Animal Friend](ref/trait/animal-friend) race trait.|
|31-65|**Non-Gnome Town or Village:** You gain access to the Animal Friend race trait.|
|66-95|**Non-Gnome City or Metropolis:** You gain access to the [Rapscallion](ref/trait/rapscallion) race trait.|
|96-100|**Unusual Homeland:** Roll on the [Unusual Homeland table](ref/rule/unusual-homeland).|

### Gnome Parents

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-90|Both of your parents are alive.|
|91-93|Only your father is alive.|
|94-96|Only your mother is alive.|
|97-100|Both of your parents are dead. You gain access to the [Orphaned](ref/trait/orphaned) social trait.|

### Gnome Siblings

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-50|1d4 biological siblings. With two or more siblings, you gain access to the [Kin Guardian](ref/trait/kin-guardian) combat trait.|
|51-60|1d4-1 biological siblings and one adopted sibling. With two or more siblings, you gain access to the Kin Guardian combat trait. Roll on the [Race of Adopted Sibling table](ref/rule/siblings) to determine the race of any adopted siblings.|
|61-100|No siblings.|
