**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 30\
A character's class is one of his most defining features. It's the source of most of his abilities,
and gives him a specific role in any adventuring party. The following eleven classes represent the
core classes of the game.

> **Barbarian:** The [barbarian](ref/class/barbarian) is a brutal berserker from beyond the edge of
> civilized lands.
>
> **Bard:** The [bard](ref/class/bard) uses skill and spell alike to bolster his allies, confound
> his enemies, and build upon his fame.
>
> **Cleric:** A devout follower of a deity, the [cleric](ref/class/cleric) can heal wounds, raise
> the dead, and call down the wrath of the gods.
>
> **Druid:** The [druid](ref/class/druid) is a worshiper of all things natural; a spellcaster, a
> friend to animals, and a skilled shapechanger.
>
> **Fighter:** Brave and stalwart, the [fighter](ref/class/fighter) is a master of all manner of
> arms and armor.
>
> **Monk:** A student of martial arts, the [monk](ref/class/monk) trains his body to be his greatest
> weapon and defense.
>
> **Paladin:** The [paladin](ref/class/paladin) is the knight in shining armor, a devoted follower
> of law and good.
>
> **Ranger:** A tracker and hunter, the [ranger](ref/class/ranger) is a creature of the wild and of
> tracking down his favored foes.
>
> **Rogue:** The [rogue](ref/class/rogue) is a thief and a scout, an opportunist capable of
> delivering brutal strikes against unwary foes.
>
> **Sorcerer:** The spellcasting [sorcerer](ref/class/sorcerer) is born with an innate knack for
> magic and has strange, eldritch powers.
>
> **Wizard:** The [wizard](ref/class/wizard) masters magic through constant study that gives him
> incredible magical power

Over the years, more classes were developed and published. These include brand new **Base Classes**
that fit new niches, **Hybrid Classes** that merge two Core or Base classes together, **Alternate
Classes** that tweak an existing Core or Base class into something new, **Unchained Classes** that
sought to fix a broken Core or Base class, and **Occult Classes** that lean heavily into spirits,
psychic energy, and the unknown.

Much like [alternate racial traits](ref/rule/racial-traits), flavorful tweaks were introduced to
classes, as well. These are known as **Archetypes.**

Finally, there are **Prestige Classes**, which cannot be taken at first level. Instead, each class
has prerequisites that will take you several regular class levels to meet. Once you meet the
qualifications, you can then proceed to take levels in the prestige class.
