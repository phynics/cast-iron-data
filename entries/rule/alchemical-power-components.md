An alchemical power component is an alchemical item used as a material component or focus for a
spell in order to alter or augment the spell's normal effects. What is listed is a sample of these
effects; your GM may allow other combinations. Spells followed by an (M) expend the alchemical item
as a material component; those followed by an (F) use the item as a focus and do not expend it. In
both cases, the alchemical item does not have its normal effect and does not affect any other
parameters of the spell. You cannot use the same item as both a focus and a material component at
the same time.

There are two types of power components. One type can be used to enhance specific spells, while the
other type can be used on a broad range of spells.
