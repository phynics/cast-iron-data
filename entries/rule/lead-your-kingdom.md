If you are using the [kingdom-building rules](ref/rule/kingdom-building) and have a leadership role
in the kingdom, you must spend 7 days per month performing various
[leadership duties](ref/rule/leadership-roles). In the downtime system, performing leadership duties
for a day costs 1 day of downtime. You can't perform any other downtime activities on a day you
perform kingdom leadership duties.
