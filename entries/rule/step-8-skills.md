**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 240

Skill bonuses in simple monster creation are divided into two categories: good and master. A good
skill is one the monster has a significant bonus in, representing what would normally come from a
moderate ability modifier and a decent number of skill ranks. A master skill is one the monster is
incredibly talented with, representing a high ability modifier, full skill ranks, and possibly a
significant racial bonus.

Any skills that don't have a good or master rating default to the monster's appropriate ability
modifier. Monsters automatically use the good modifier for Perception without needing to spend a
good skill slot on it, though they can still increase this by taking Perception as a master skill.
