**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 437

If a city has main thoroughfares, they are lined with lanterns hanging at a height of 7 feet from
building awnings. These lanterns are spaced 60 feet apart, so their illumination is all but
continuous. Secondary streets and alleys are not lit; it is common for citizens to hire
lantern-bearers when going out after dark.

Alleys can be dark places even in daylight, thanks to the shadows of the tall buildings that
surround them. A dark alley in daylight is rarely dark enough to afford true concealment, but it can
lend a +2 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks.
