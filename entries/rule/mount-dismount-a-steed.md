**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 187

Mounting or dismounting a steed requires a move action.

**Fast Mount or Dismount:** You can mount or dismount as a free action with a DC 20 Ride check. If
you fail the check, mounting or dismounting is a move action instead. You can't attempt a fast mount
or fast dismount unless you can perform the mount or dismount as a move action in the current round.
