**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 210

A Bridge allows your Road hexes to cross rivers.

**Cost:** When you build a Road in a hex that contains a river, the doubled coast of the Road
includes the cost of bridges needed to cross the river. You don't need to build a Bridge as a
separate unit; it's listed here for reference only.
