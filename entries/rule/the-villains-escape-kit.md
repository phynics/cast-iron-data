**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 93

Sometimes a villain needs to appear and then get away. With all the versatility and options at the
hands of a capable party of adventurers, this can prove quite difficult for the villain and
dangerous to the plot should she get trapped. At the same time, PCs should never feel incapable of
opposing the villain or suspect the GM of unfairly favoring the antagonist. If it's important that a
villain escape, consider some of the following spells when planning her contingencies.

**Airborne Escape:** [Air walk](ref/spell/air-walk), [elemental body](ref/spell/elemental-body-1),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form),
[levitate](ref/spell/levitate), [overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight),
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb), [wind walk](ref/spell/wind-walk)
