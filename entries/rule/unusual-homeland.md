You grew up in a region not typically associated with the lands that your people call home. Roll on
the Unusual Homeland table to determine the homeland of your family. If the result is one that is
typical for your race, this region is one where there are few other members of your race, outside of
your family.

### Unusual Homeland

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-10|**Subterranean:** You gain access to the [Surface Stranger](ref/trait/surface-stranger) regional trait.|
|11-25|**Mountains:** You gain access to the [Highlander](ref/trait/highlander) regional trait.|
|26-40|**Plains:** You gain access to the [Savanna Child](ref/trait/savanna-child) regional trait.|
|41-50|**Town or Village:** You gain access to the [Militia Veteran](ref/trait/militia-veteran) regional trait.|
|51-60|**City or Metropolis:** You gain access to the [Civilized](ref/trait/civilized) social trait and the [Vagabond Child](ref/trait/vagabond-child) regional trait.|
|61-70|**Forest:** You gain access to the [Log Roller](ref/trait/log-roller) regional trait.|
|71-80|**River, Swamp, or Wetlands:** You gain access to the [River Rat](ref/trait/river-rat) regional trait.|
|81-85|**Desert:** You gain access to the [Desert Child](ref/trait/desert-child) regional trait.|
|86-90|**Sea:** You gain access to the [Sea-Souled](ref/trait/sea-souled) regional trait.|
|91-95|**Tundra:** You gain access to the [Tundra Child](ref/trait/tundra-child) regional trait.|
|96-100|**Another Plane:** Your GM chooses the plane. You gain access to the [Scholar of the Great Beyond](ref/trait/scholar-of-the-great-beyond) faith trait.|
