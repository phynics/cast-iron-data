**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 182\
Large land vehicle\
**Squares** 6 (10 ft. by 15 ft.; 5 feet high); **Cost** 200 gp

### Defense

**AC** 8; **[Hardness](ref/umr/hardness)** 5\
**hp** 80 (44)\
**Base Save** +1

### Offense

**Maximum Speed** twice the speed of the pulling creature(s); **Acceleration** half the speed of the
pulling creature(s)\
**CMB** +2; **CMD** 12\
**Ramming Damage** 1d8

### Description

This two-wheeled vehicle is sometimes used as a fighting platform or for transporting a light
ballista onto the battlefield.

**Propulsion** muscle (pulled; 6 Medium creatures or 2 Large creatures)\
**Driving Check** [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) or
[Profession (driver)](ref/skill/profession-any) (in the case of creatures with animal intelligence
pulling the vehicle), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) or [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) (in the
case of intelligent creatures)\
**Forward Facing** toward the creatures pulling the vehicle\
**Driving Device** reins\
**Driving Space** the two most forward squares of the chariot\
**Decks** 1\
**Weapons** light ballista
