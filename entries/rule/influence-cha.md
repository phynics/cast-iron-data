### Influence (Cha)

You can manipulate other people through negotiation, deceit, or intimidation.

**Functions:** Change others' attitudes, create diversions, demoralize, feint in combat, gather
information, intimidate, lie, make requests, pass secret messages.

**Core Skills:** Bluff, Diplomacy, Intimidate.
