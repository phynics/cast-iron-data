Lava or magma deals 2d6 points of fire damage per round of exposure, except in the case of total
immersion (such as when a character falls into the crater of an active volcano), which deals 20d6
points of fire damage per round.

Damage from lava continues for 1d3 rounds after exposure ceases, but this additional damage is only
half of that dealt during actual contact (that is, 1d6 or 10d6 points per round). Immunity or
resistance to fire serves as an immunity or resistance to lava or magma. A creature immune or
resistant to fire might still drown if completely immersed in lava (see
[Drowning](ref/rule/drowning)).
