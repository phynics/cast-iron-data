The population of a settlement limits how much help you can get on a given day. The following
numbers represent the limit of how much Goods, Influence, and Labor you can utilize in settlement
each day. Even if you have a lot of Goods and Labor at your disposal from favors and such, a tiny
settlement might have only a few hands to spare to turn that capital into finished projects.

|Settlement|Spending Limit per Day (Goods, Influence, or Labor)|
| --- | --- |
|Thorp|2|
|Hamlet|4|
|Village|10|
|Small town|15|
|Large town|25|
|Small city|35|
|Large city|50|
|Metropolis|65|
