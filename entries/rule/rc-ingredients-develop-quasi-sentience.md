**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 184\
Somehow, one or more of your item's components have developed a limited intelligence.

### Tasks

**Coax Ingredients to Greater Performance** Handle Animal DC 30

**Magically Force Ingredients into Order** Spellcraft DC 15 + item's caster level

### Results

**Critical Success** Item proceeds as normal but becomes an intelligent item of your alignment, and
it likes you.

**Success** Ingredients work with you. 1 perk if you coaxed the ingredients; -5% cost if you coerced
the ingredients.

**Failure** Ingredients rebel against you and escape or sabotage the item. +15% cost, 1 flaw.

**Critical Failure** Item proceeds as normal but becomes an intelligent item of a contrary
alignment, and it hates you.
