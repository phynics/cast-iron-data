**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 204

An adventure might be a masterpiece of terror, but if it's being played on a sunny day with people
laughing in the background, the players still might not be able to feel the mood. The surroundings
can be a GM's greatest ally in telling a truly effective horror tale, but they might also work
against her entirely. Consider the game space as a stage. This section includes ways that a GM might
manipulate that performance space to create an atmosphere perfect for horror adventures.
