**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 188

You may retrain an ability score increase gained at level 4, 8, 12, 16, or 20. This takes 5 days. At
the end of the training period, remove the +1 bonus from the original ability score and add it to a
different ability score. If this retraining means you no longer qualify for a feat, prestige class,
or other ability you have, you can't use that feat, prestige class, or ability until you meet the
qualifications again. (You can retrain that feat, prestige class, or other ability separately.)
