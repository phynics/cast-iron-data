**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 224

In addition to using a mythic simple template, there are two ways to create a new mythic monster.
The first is to take an existing monster, give it the mythic subtype, and
[add abilities](ref/rule/modify-an-existing-monster) as described in that subtype. The second method
is to [create an entirely new monster](ref/rule/create-a-new-monster) with the mythic subtype and
incorporate additional abilities into the final monster. Both methods are described in the following
sections.
