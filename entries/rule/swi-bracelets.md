**Slot** wrists; CL 4th; **Weight** -\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **Price** 480 gp; **Scaling** bauble

### Description

These bracelets are crafted from thin yet surprisingly durable silver, and they glitter and sparkle
faintly in moonlight or shadow. The wearer can activate the bracelets once per day as a free action
to gain a +4 competence bonus on a Bluff or Stealth check.

**6th Level:** The wearer can activate the bracelets to increase the DC of the next enchantment
spell she casts by 1 instead of gaining a bonus on a skill check.

**8th Level:** The bracelets can be used twice per day, but no more than once per hour.

### Construction Requirements

[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [charm person](ref/spell/charm-person),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), creator must be fey; **Cost** 825 gp
