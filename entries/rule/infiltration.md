An infiltration requires stealth and discretion to win the day. Unlike a heist, infiltration is
typically limited to a smaller set of skills, and suited for one or two PCs rather than utilizing
the whole group.

Infiltration covers both a direct infiltration with a single, set goal (similar to a heist) and
long-term espionage that requires living a double life and has a less specific goal. Infiltration
ranges from breaking and entering to using social skills to get inside a location. Infiltration
usually requires the Stealth and Disguise skills, and often social skills and Sleight of Hand as
well.
