Spellcasting villains can use magical means to taunt and provoke the PCs without exposing themselves
to any danger. Scrying and clairvoyance spells allow the villain to keep an eye on the PCs and
adjust the minions' tactics accordingly, while magic mouth, whispering wind, and similar spells let
the villain give orders and issue taunts. Various illusion spells allow the villain to appear before
the PCs without any risk.
