Incorporating occult themes into games where magic is commonplace requires careful consideration.
Think of the occult like layers of an onion. By peeling back these layers one at a time, PCs can be
at first perplexed by, then increasingly familiar with, the strange rules that govern this parallel
world. But once PCs become comfortable with one occult reality, they discover a new deeper, darker
layer.

In occult adventures, each new piece of the puzzle opens up more questions than it answers, and the
mysteries of the story and plot are carefully nested like so many matryoshka dolls. In such games,
haunts do not hesitate to reveal the causes for their spiritual contamination, and ritually marked
corpses are far more than atmospheric window dressing. The very locations adventurers investigate
may themselves be intrinsically linked by mysterious ley lines to other locations with similarly
disturbing histories.
