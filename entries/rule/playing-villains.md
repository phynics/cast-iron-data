The time and effort that goes into the creation of a major villain is wasted if the villain does not
get enough time with the player characters. Every GM knows the frustration of spending hours
crafting a great villain, only to have him cut down in a few rounds with scarcely a word exchanged.
Every villain deserves a moment to defy or mock the PC, and there are a number of ways to arrange
this without fudging dice rolls or railroading the players.
