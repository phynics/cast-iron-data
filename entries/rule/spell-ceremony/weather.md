**Song of the Tempest.** The subject can call one 10-foot-by-10-foot rain cloud in an area within 30
feet, requiring creatures in the affected area attempting to cast spells to first succeed at DC 13
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks for 1 round.\
_Augmented:_ The DC is 15 for 3 rounds.
