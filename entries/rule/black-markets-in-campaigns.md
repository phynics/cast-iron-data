Even idealists and wonder-workers are at times driven to the outlaw havens of black markets. Magical
visionaries use these unauthorized enterprises to fund and protect their work even when the populace
fears and opposes their methods (reasonably or not). Freedom fighters rely on black markets to
support their efforts to undermine oppressors. Persecuted faithful and isolated cultists hide their
practices and their relics for fear of confiscation and worse, relying on each other for safety -
and just as often on the questionable honor of the thieves with whom they must deal.
