**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 140

While running games within a published campaign setting or a favorite fictional universe is a lot of
fun, many GMs enjoy creating an entire setting from scratch - a world in which every idea, NPC, and
location, is an expression of your creativity. It's a powerful feeling, one that can give you
countless hours of enjoyment even outside of the game session.
