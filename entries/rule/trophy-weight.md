In most cases, the weight of trophy components recovered from a creature depend on the creature's
size. This weight is doubled for bones, hides, and skins, and halved for feathers, hair, and small
organs such as eyes or glands. Once components have been processed into actual trophies, the
resulting weight of the trophy is generally determined by the nature of the trophy created (in the
case of jewelry or the like) or half the weight of the components used (in the case of something
like a preserved head or limb).
