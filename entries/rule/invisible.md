**Invisible:** Invisible creatures are visually undetectable. An invisible creature gains a +2 bonus
on attack rolls against a sighted opponent, and ignores its opponent's Dexterity bonus to AC (if
any). See [Invisibility](ref/rule/invisibility), under Special Abilities.
