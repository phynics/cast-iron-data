**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 567

**Entangled:** The character is ensnared. Being entangled impedes movement, but does not entirely
prevent it unless the bonds are anchored to an immobile object or tethered by an opposing force. An
entangled creature moves at half speed, cannot run or charge, and takes a -2 penalty on all attack
rolls and a -4 penalty to Dexterity. An entangled character who attempts to cast a spell must make a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC 15 + spell level) or lose the spell.
