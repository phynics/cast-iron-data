### Performance (Cha)

You can compel an emotional reaction through a musical, oratory, or physical performance. Your
ability to act convincingly and use stagecraft also makes you better at disguising yourself. This
skill applies to performances of any type (unlike in the Core Rulebook).

**Functions:** Disguise yourself, impress audiences.

**Core Skills:** Disguise, Perform (all).
