### Perception (Wis)

The acuity of your senses allows you to notice fine details, see danger coming, and tell when people
are lying or otherwise behaving suspiciously.

**Functions:** Discern secret messages, get hunches, notice creatures and details, search locations,
sense enchantments, sense motives.

**Core Skills:** Perception, Sense Motive.
