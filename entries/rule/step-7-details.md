Once you have assigned all of the NPC's gear, all that remains is to fill out the details. Determine
the character's attack and damage bonuses, CMB, CMD, initiative modifier, and Armor Class. If the
character's magic items affect his skills or ability scores, make sure to take those changes into
account. Determine the character's total hit points by assuming the average result. Finally, fill
out any other important details, such as name, alignment, religion, and a few personality traits to
round him out.
