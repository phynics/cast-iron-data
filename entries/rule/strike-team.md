**Strike Team** **(Specialists):** Specialists can call in a group of powerful allies to help the
militia secure a safe location or evacuate the injured in an emergency. The PCs must specify the
location where the team is sent when the militia takes this action during the Activity phase. Once
during the following week - at the PCs' determination - while the party is at that location, each PC
gains a +2 competence bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws for a number of
consecutive rounds equal to half the militia's rank. After aiding the PCs, the specialists head back
home.

Alternatively, the specialists are able to rescue any incapacitated characters at the specified
location. Any characters who are [bleeding](ref/rule/bleed) automatically stabilize and any dead
characters instantly come under the effects of [gentle repose](ref/spell/gentle-repose) (CL 12th).
Regardless of how the remaining PCs escape, the specialists bring any bodies back to the militia's
current headquarters as quickly as possible.
