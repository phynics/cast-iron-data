**Sabotage** **(Saboteurs):** Saboteurs are a special team that allows the militia to react to the
[Event phase](ref/rule/event-phase-m). If an event occurs and the militia's saboteurs aren't
currently engaged in an operation, [missing, or disabled](ref/rule/team-conditions), the PCs can
activate the saboteurs and the team can try to negate the event. The DC to negate the effect is 15 +
the militia's [rank](ref/rule/rank-mt). Taking this action increases the militia's
[Notoriety](ref/rule/notoriety-mt) score by 1d6 regardless of whether it's successful.
