**1st-Level Alchemist Words:** Dash (Time), Decipher (Language), Fade (Concealing), Force Shield
(Armor), Fortify (Body), Glide (Flight), Lesser Cure (Healing)

**2nd-Level Alchemist Words:** Accelerate (Time), Decipher\* (Language), Disappear (Concealing),
Energy Resistance (Body), Enhance Form (Body), Float (Flight), Moderate Cure (Healing), Sense Hidden
(Detection), Sense Thoughts (Detection)

**3rd-Level Alchemist Words:** Altered Form (Change), Greater Cure (Healing), Soar (Flight),
Translate (Language)

**4th-Level Alchemist Words:** Altered Form\* (Change), Bestial Form (Change), Borrow Future (Time),
Disappear\* (Concealing), Elder Cure (Healing), Perfect Form (Body), Purify (Life)

**5th-Level Alchemist Words:** Bestial Form\* (Change), Monstrous Form (Change), Resist Arcana
(Dispelling)

**6th-Level Alchemist Words:** Energy Immunity (Body), Monstrous Form\* (Change), Sense Hidden\*
(Detection)
