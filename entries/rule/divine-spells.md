Clerics, druids, experienced paladins, and experienced rangers can cast divine spells. Unlike arcane
spells, divine spells draw power from a divine source. Clerics gain spell power from deities or from
divine forces. The divine force of nature powers druid and ranger spells, and the divine forces of
law and good power paladin spells. Divine spells tend to focus on healing and protection and are
less flashy, destructive, and disruptive than arcane spells.
