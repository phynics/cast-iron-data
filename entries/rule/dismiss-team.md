**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 55

**Dismiss Team** **(No Team Required):** The PCs remove a team from the militia, freeing up that
slot to recruit a new team. Attempt a DC 10 Loyalty check; on a failed check, the militia's
[Notoriety](ref/rule/notoriety-mt) score increases by 1d6 as disenfranchised team members' gossip
spreads.
