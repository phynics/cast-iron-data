Moving or manipulating an item is usually a move action. This includes retrieving or putting away a
stored item, picking up an item, moving a heavy object, and opening a door. Examples of this kind of
action, along with whether they incur an attack of opportunity, are given in
[the table here](ref/rule/move-actions).

Some spells allow you to redirect the effect to new targets or areas after you cast the spell.
**Redirecting a spell** requires a move action and does not provoke attacks of opportunity or
require concentration.

**Standing up** from a prone position requires a move action and provokes attacks of opportunity.
