**Sanguinary Cloud (CR 6):** Often found floating over campsites of unfortunate travelers drained of
all bodily fluids, these blood-red fog banks can be mistaken for colossal
[vampiric mists](ref/monster/vampiric-mist). A sanguinary cloud typically settles over a
60-foot-radius area, obscuring all sight beyond 5 feet, including [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision),
granting concealment (20% miss chance) to all creatures 5 feet away or farther. Creatures caught
within a bank of this deadly fog must succeed at a DC 18 Fortitude save each round or take 1d3
points of Constitution damage as their bodily fluids are forcibly extracted from their pores and
mucous membranes and drawn into the crimson mist. A severe or greater wind disperses a sanguinary
cloud, leaving behind a thin sheen of bloody bile.
