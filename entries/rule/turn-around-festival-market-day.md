**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 58

**Turn Around:** Any [disabled](ref/rule/team-conditions) teams automatically recover from whatever
was ailing them. If there are no disabled teams, one team has a morale bump and gains a +2 bonus on
one check during the next week's Activity phase.

**Festival:** One of the nearby towns is celebrating an important day. Choose a town out of which
the militia recently operated. The PCs gain a +2 morale bonus on all Bluff, Diplomacy, and
Intimidate checks attempted within that town during the following week. _Twice:_ The morale bonus
increases to +5.

**Market Day:** All items and services purchased in one town in which a militia team has operated
(PCs' choice) can be purchased for an additional 5% discount. _Twice:_ All such marketplaces,
including any created with a [Broker Market](ref/rule/broker-market) action, receive this discount.
