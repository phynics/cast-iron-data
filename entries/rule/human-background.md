**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 19

As diverse as they are widespread, humans tend to grow up in small or large societies of people with
similar origins and histories, though individuals' paths may run the gamut from idealized to tragic.

### Human Homeland

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-50|**Town or Village:** You gain access to the [Militia Veteran](ref/trait/militia-veteran) regional trait.|
|51-85|**City or Metropolis:** If you're a human, you gain access to the [Civilized](ref/trait/civilized) social trait and the [Vagabond Child](ref/trait/vagabond-child) regional trait. If you're a half-elf, you gain access to the Civilized social trait and the [Failed Apprentice](ref/trait/failed-apprentice) race trait. If you're a half-orc, you gain access to the [Brute](ref/trait/brute-orc) race trait and the Vagabond Child regional trait.|
|86-95|**Frontier:** You gain access to the [Frontier-Forged](ref/trait/frontier-forged) regional trait.|
|96-100|**Unusual Homeland:** Roll on the [Unusual Homeland table](ref/rule/unusual-homeland).|

### Human Parents

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-50|Both of your parents are alive.|
|51-70|Only your father is alive.|
|71-90|Only your mother is alive.|
|91-100|Both of your parents are dead. You gain access to the [Orphaned](ref/trait/orphaned) social trait.|

### Human Siblings

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-40|1d2 siblings. With two siblings, you gain access to the [Kin Guardian](ref/trait/kin-guardian) combat trait.|
|41-70|1d2 siblings and 1d2 half-siblings (roll d% to determine each one's race; 01-50: half-elf, 51-100: half-orc). You gain access to the Kin Guardian combat trait.|
|71-90|2d4 siblings. You gain access to the Kin Guardian combat trait.|
|91-100|No siblings.|
