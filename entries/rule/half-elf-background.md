**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 18

Born of two very different worlds, half-elves rarely have easy childhoods. Torn as half-elves are
between disparate peoples, the presence of their families helps ensure they do not grow up totally
alone, though orphaned half-elves have to create families of their own.

### Half-Elf Homeland

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-25|**Raised in an Elven Homeland:** Roll on the [Elf Homeland table](ref/rule/elf-background).|
|26-75|**Raised in a Human Homeland:** Roll on the [Human Homeland table](ref/rule/human-background).|
|76-95|**Forest:** You gain access to the [Log Roller](ref/trait/log-roller) regional trait.|
|96-100|**Unusual Homeland:** Roll on the [Unusual Homeland table](ref/rule/unusual-homeland).|

### Half-Elf Parents

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-20|Both of your parents are alive.|
|21-55|Only your father is alive.|
|56-90|Only your mother is alive.|
|91-100|Both of your parents are dead. You gain access to the [Orphaned](ref/trait/orphaned) social trait.|

### Half-Elf Siblings

|d%|Result|
| --- | --- |
|01-20|1d2 half-siblings (either elf or human, your choice). With two or more siblings, you gain access to the [Kin Guardian](ref/trait/kin-guardian) combat trait.|
|21-30|One half-elf sibling. You gain access to the [Kin Bond](ref/trait/kin-bond) magic trait.|
|31-100|No siblings.|
