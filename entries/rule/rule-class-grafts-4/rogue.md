Rogues always have a trick up their sleeves. Armed with numerous skills and tricks to confound their
adversaries, rogues can always stay one step ahead of the foe.

**Required Array:** Expert.

**Statistics Adjustments:** Increase Reflex saving throw bonus by 3; skills-master Perception and
Stealth; options-improved initiative, sneak attack.

**CR 1:** Options-evasion, one any.

**CR 3:** Options-evasion, uncanny dodge, one combat, one social.

**CR 9:** Options-evasion, uncanny dodge, unflankable, one combat, one social.

**CR 11:** Options-evasion, uncanny dodge, unflankable, one combat, one social, one any.

**CR 19:** Options-evasion, slaying attack (Fortitude), uncanny dodge, unflankable, two combat, one
social, one any.

**Suggested Ability Modifiers:** Dexterity, Charisma, Intelligence.

**Suggested Options:** Constant invisibility, mobility, persuasive, poison, slippery mind, trap
squares.
