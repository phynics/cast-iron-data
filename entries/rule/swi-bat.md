### Bat Cloak

**Slot** shoulders; CL 4th; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **Price** 900 gp; **Scaling** prize

### Description

Fashioned of dark brown or black cloth, this cloak resembles gigantic bat wings. It allows its
wearer to hang upside down from the ceiling like a bat.

**7th Level:** The cloak grants its wearer a +5 competence bonus on Stealth checks.

**10th Level:** The wearer can grasp the edges of the cloak to fly (as the [fly](ref/spell/fly)
spell) for up to 7 minutes three times per day, but only in dim light or lower. After each use of
this ability, the wearer must wait for at least as long as the _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_ effect lasted
until using this ability again.

**12th Level:** For each daily use of _[fly](ref/spell/fly),_ the wearer can instead choose to
transform into an ordinary bat as [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3) for the same duration.

**14th Level:** The wearer can use _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_ or
_[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)_ any number of times per day, but is still subject to
the dim light restriction and the limit on using the ability again after a previous use.

**16th Level:** The wearer can also transform into a dire bat with
_[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3),_ and he can summon a swarm of bats at will as
[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm).

**18th Level:** The wearer constantly gains the echolocation of a bat. He can hear high-pitched
sounds such as bat echolocation, and he gains [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) 20
feet. This blindsense is sound-based, and is blocked by spells such as [silence](ref/spell/silence).

**20th Level:** The wearer's [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) increases to
blindsense 60 feet, like that of a dire bat. Instead of using beast shape or [fly](ref/spell/fly),
he can transform himself into a bat swarm, gaining the abilities of a bat as
_[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)_ plus the bat swarm's distraction, immunity to weapon
damage, swarm traits, and wounding abilities.

### Construction Requirements

[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3),
[echolocation](ref/spell/echolocation), _fly, summon swarm;_ **Cost** 66,000 gp
