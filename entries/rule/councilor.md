The Councilor acts as a liaison between the citizenry and the other kingdom leaders, parsing
requests from the commonwealth and presenting the leaders' proclamations to the people in
understandable ways. It is the Councilor's responsibility to make sure the Ruler is making decisions
that benefit the kingdom's communities and its citizens.

**Benefit:** Add your Charisma modifier or Wisdom modifier to Loyalty.

**Vacancy Penalty:** Loyalty decreases by 2. The kingdom gains no benefits from the
[Holiday edict](ref/rule/holiday-edicts). During the Upkeep phase, Unrest increases by 1.
