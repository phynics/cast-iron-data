Other types of feats exist, detailed below.

### Combination Feats

**Sources:** Martial Arts Handbook pg 26\
Masters of martial arts can string one attack into the next, setting an opponent up for her allies
or wearing a foe down with inevitable attrition. You can apply the effects of only a single
combination feat (7 feats) to an attack.

### Conduit Feats

**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 22\
Conduit feats (23 feats) grant the ability to draw energies from other planes. The effects of
conduit feats are supernatural and do not function in an antimagic field or similar effect. In
addition, since these abilities require energies from other planes, you cannot use them in areas
that are warded against planar or dimensional travel (such as those under the effects of dimensional
lock or forbiddance) or when you have been denied this ability (such as via dimensional anchor).

### Monster Feats

**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 78\
Monster feats (42 feats) were introduced in _Pathfinder RPG Bestiary,_ though without a separate tag
(all feats in Bestiary are monster feats except [Craft Construct](ref/feat/craft-construct)). Most
of these feats apply specifically to monsters and might grant abilities that could be disruptive in
the hands of PCs, although with the GM's permission PCs can take one of these feats if they meet the
prerequisites.

Some monster feats allow a creature to apply metamagic feat-like effects to its
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). You can select a spell-like ability
duplicating a spell with a level less than or equal to 1/2 the monster's caster level (round down) -
1 or 2, depending on the ability. The table below summarizes these feats and what spell-like
abilities they can affect by caster level.

### Origin Feats

**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 16, Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 73\
Origin feats (9 feats) are [duskwalker](ref/race/duskwalker) or [shabti](ref/race/shabti) racial
feats that grant additional powers based on the duskwalker's original cause of death, or the nature
of the shabti's creators. Generally, a duskwalker or shabti can qualify for only a single origin
feat, but at the GM's discretion, a duskwalker whose death matches multiple origin feats may be
allowed to take more than one.

### Performance Feats

**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 78\
Performance feats (15 feats) are used when you make a
[performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat) check, typically granting a special action that
occurs when you make that check. Unless you have the [Masterful Display](ref/feat/masterful-display)
feat, you can only apply the effect of one performance feat to each performance combat check you
make.

### Versatile Brewing Feats

**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 16\
These feats allow potion brewers to concoct unusual variations in their potions, altering the
potions' effects in strange and useful ways. These feats function similarly to metamagic feats,
though they affect the creation of potions instead of the normal casting of spells. Level limits for
potions apply to their level after the application of any relevant metamagic feats (if the feat
affects the component spell's level).

### Alternate Rules Feats

Other sets of feats (21 feats) only work if your GM is using the applicable alternate rules systems,
such as [Called Shots](ref/rule/called-shots), [Hero Points](ref/rule/hero-points),
[Words of Power](ref/rule/words-of-power), and
[Esoteric Material Components for spells](ref/rule/esoteric-material-components).

### Mythic Feats

Mythic feats (144 feats) are used with the [mythic rules](ref/rule/mythic-rules), and are mostly
upgrades to other, already-existing feats.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

Bouncing, disruptive, intensified, lingering, or scarring ability.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

Fearsome, sickening, or traumatic ability.
