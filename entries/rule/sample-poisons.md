Examples of poisons that use the various tracks are given below. If a poison does not specify an
effect, it imposes only the effects for the victim's state on the pertinent track.

### Black Lotus Extract

**Type:** Poison (Poison, contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 20

**Onset:** 1 minute

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Track:** Constitution (special) (Healthy-Weakened-Disabled-Dead)

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Poison** [Black Lotus Extract](ref/eq-poison/black-lotus-extract)

### Blue Whinnis

**Type:** Poison (Poison, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Frequency:** 1/round for 2 rounds

**Track:** Constitution (special) (Healthy-Weakened-Unconscious; no end state)

**Cure:** 1 save

**Original Poison** [Blue Whinnis](ref/eq-poison/blue-whinnis)

### Deathblade

**Type:** Poison (Poison, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 20

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Track:** Constitution

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Poison** [Deathblade](ref/eq-poison/deathblade)

### Green Lotus

**Type:** Poison (Poison, contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** 1 minute

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Track:** Charisma (special) (Healthy-Weakened-Impaired-Pliable-Pliable; the second pliable is an
end state)

**Cure:** 1 save

### Id Moss

**Type:** Poison (Poison, ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Onset:** 10 minutes

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 6 minutes

**Track:** Intelligence

**Cure:** 1 save

**Original Poison** [Id Moss](ref/eq-poison/id-moss)

### Insanity Mist

**Type:** Poison (Poison, inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Track:** Wisdom

**Cure:** 1 save

**Original Poison** [Insanity Mist](ref/eq-poison/insanity-mist)

### Large Scorpion Venom

**Type:** Poison (Poison, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 17

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Track:** Strength

**Cure:** 1 save

**Original Poison** [Large Scorpion Venom](ref/eq-poison/large-scorpion-venom)

### Small Centipede Poison

**Type:** Poison (Poison, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 11

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Track:** Dexterity (Requires two failed saves to progress to each state past sluggish)

**Cure:** 1 save

**Original Poison** [Small Centipede Poison](ref/eq-poison/small-centipede-poison)

### Ungol Dust

**Type:** Poison (Poison, inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Track:** Charisma

**Effect:** Victim does not recover past weakened without heal or restoration

**Cure:** 1 save

**Original Poison** [Ungol Dust](ref/eq-poison/ungol-dust)
