These rules can also serve in battles where more than two armies clash. In such battles, when your
army attempts an Offense check, you choose which enemy army (or armies, if you have multiple armies
in the field) it is attacking and apply damage appropriately. On each phase, you may change which
army you are targeting. If your kingdom fields multiple armies in a battle, you may want to divide
responsibility for these armies among the other players to speed up play.
