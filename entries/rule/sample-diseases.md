**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 140

Examples of diseases that use the various tracks are given below. If a disease does not specify an
effect, it imposes only the effects for the victim's state on the pertinent track.

### Blinding Sickness

**Type:** Disease (Disease, ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Effect:** At the impaired state, also become permanently blind

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Blinding Sickness](ref/disease/blinding-sickness)

### Bubonic Plague

**Type:** Disease (Disease, injury or inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 17

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Bubonic Plague](ref/disease/bubonic-plague)

### Cackle Fever

**Type:** Disease (Disease, inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** mental

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Cackle Fever](ref/disease/cackle-fever)

### Dementia Dust

**Type:** Disease (Disease, inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Frequency:** 1/week

**Track:** mental

**Effect:** Even if the disease is removed with remove disease, condition does not improve without
greater restoration or heal

**Cure:** magic only

### Demon Fever

**Type:** Disease (Disease, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Effect:** At the impaired state, penalties from the weakened state become permanent until victim
receives _[heal](ref/spell/heal)_ or restoration

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Demon Fever](ref/disease/demon-fever)

### Devil Chills

**Type:** Disease (Disease, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Cure:** 3 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Devil Chills](ref/disease/devil-chills)

### Filth Fever

**Type:** Disease (Disease, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 12

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Filth Fever](ref/disease/filth-fever)

### Leprosy

**Type:** Disease (Disease, contact, inhaled, or injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 12

**Frequency:** 1/week

**Track:** physical (special)

**Effect:** Healthy-Latent/Carrier-Sluggish-Stiffened; sluggish and stiffened are as Dexterity
poison, stiffened is an end state

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Leprosy](ref/disease/leprosy)

### Mindfire

**Type:** Disease (Disease, inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 12

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** mental

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Mindfire](ref/disease/mindfire)

### Mummy Rot

**Type:** Disease (Disease, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical and mental (special)

**Effect:** No latent/carrier state; victim suffers all penalties from progressing on both the
physical and mental disease tracks

**Cure:** remove curse and remove disease within 1 minute of each other

**Original Curse** [Mummy Rot](ref/curse/mummy-rot)

### Red Ache

**Type:** Disease (Disease, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Red Ache](ref/disease/red-ache)

### Shakes

**Type:** Disease (Disease, contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 13

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Shakes](ref/disease/shakes)

### Slimy Doom

**Type:** Disease (Disease, contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Track:** physical

**Effect:** At the impaired state and beyond, penalties from the weakened state become permanent
until the victim receives heal or restoration

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Original Disease** [Slimy Doom](ref/disease/slimy-doom)
