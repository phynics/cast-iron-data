**Slot** none; CL 4th; **Weight** 1/2 lb.\
**Aura** faint (many schools); **Price** 900 gp; **Scaling** prize

### Description

This item appears to be a simple string of prayer beads until the owner casts a divine spell while
carrying the beads. Once that occurs, she learns how to activate one of the strand's special beads -
a [bead of blessing](ref/magic-wondrous/strand-of-prayer-beads-lesser) - to cast
[bless](ref/spell/bless) once per day.

**9th Level:** The owner can also activate the strand's bead of healing to cast
[cure serious wounds](ref/spell/cure-serious-wounds) once per day.

**10th Level:** The owner can also activate the strand's bead of healing to cast
[remove blindness/deafness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness) or
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease) instead of
_[cure serious wounds](ref/spell/cure-serious-wounds)._

**14th Level:** The owner can also activate the strand's bead of smiting to cast
[chaos hammer](ref/spell/chaos-hammer), [holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite),
[order's wrath](ref/spell/orders-wrath), or [unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight) (whichever was
used to craft the item) once per day.

**16th Level:** The owner can also activate the strand's bead of karma to cast her spells with a +4
caster level bonus for 10 minutes once per day.

**19th Level:** The owner can also activate the strand's bead of wind walking to cast
[wind walk](ref/spell/wind-walk) once per day.

### Construction Requirements

[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [bless](ref/spell/bless),
[cure serious wounds](ref/spell/cure-serious-wounds),
[remove blindness/deafness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness),
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease), [righteous might](ref/spell/righteous-might),
_[wind walk](ref/spell/wind-walk),_ and either _chaos hammer, holy smite, order's wrath,_ or
_[unholy blight](ref/spell/unholy-blight);_ **Cost** 51,375 gp
