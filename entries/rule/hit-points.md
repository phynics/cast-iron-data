**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 179

When your hit point total reaches 0, you're [disabled](ref/rule/disabled-0-hit-points). When it
reaches -1, you're [dying](ref/rule/dying-negative-hit-points). When it gets to a negative amount
equal to your Constitution score, you're [dead](ref/rule/dead).
