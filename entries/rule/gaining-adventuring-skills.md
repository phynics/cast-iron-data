**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 47

Adventuring skills are those skills that are most relevant for characters while they're actively
adventuring. Adventuring skills are purchased with the standard skill ranks each character class
receives, modified by a character's Intelligence (and sometimes by race or other factors). No
adjustments need to be made to these skills when using the background skills system.

Most adventuring skills are related to training and practice, and each has a clear and specific
application to the everyday challenges that face a professional adventurer. Adventuring skills get
used for the majority of skill checks, so most skill ranks should be devoted to those skills.
