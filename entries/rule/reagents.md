**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 4

The following reagents are purified through long processes from their raw states for use in
alchemical recipes. Each alchemical reagent may also be used as an
[alchemical power component](ref/rule/alchemical-power-components), augmenting the effects of
certain spells when used as an additional material component.

[Black powder](ref/eq-reagent/black-powder)

**Weapon:** [Black powder](ref/eq-weapon/black-powder-dose)\
**Price** 10 gp; **Weight** -

Black powder is a volatile explosive and is the primary component in fireworks and other explosives.

[Brimstone](ref/eq-reagent/brimstone)

**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -

Brimstone, also called sulfur, has a distinctive odor and caustic properties.

[Cold Iron](ref/eq-reagent/cold-iron)

**Special material:** [Cold Iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron)\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** -

Cold iron is often used to produce alloyed metals, catalysts, and items that interfere with magic.

[Cytillesh Spores](ref/eq-reagent/cytillesh-spores)

**Poison:** [Cytillesh Extract](ref/eq-poison/cytillesh-extract)\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** -

The spore of the cytillesh fungus is a versatile reagent, and is often combined with other poisonous
elements to create deliriums, hallucinogens, and psychotics.

[Darkwood](ref/eq-reagent/darkwood)

**Special material:** [Darkwood](ref/eq-material/darkwood)\
**Price** 2 gp; **Weight** -

The bark of the darkwood tree is useful in numerous alchemical applications, especially those that
require a light but strong foundation.

[Dew of Lunary](ref/eq-reagent/dew-of-lunary)

**Price** 4 gp; **Weight** -

Dew of lunary is derived from a potent herb. It is commonly used in the creation of medicinal,
protective, and divinatory items.

[Ginger Extract](ref/eq-reagent/ginger-extract)

**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -

In addition to serving as a spice and as an alchemical reagent, ginger extract has a wide variety of
uses in herbal remedies that treat nausea and pain.

[Gold](ref/eq-reagent/gold)

**Special material:** [Gold](ref/eq-material/gold)\
**Price** 5 gp; **Weight** -

In its purest form, gold is a dense, non-reactive metal. Its alchemical products are useful in
stains and in treating swelling, pain, and infections.

[Magnesium](ref/eq-reagent/magnesium)

**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** -

Magnesium is an extremely light and reactive metal that burns brightly when exposed to air. It is
important for all living things, especially plants.

[Mugwort Extract](ref/eq-reagent/mugwort-extract)

**Price** 2 gp; **Weight** -

Many varieties of mugwort exist; some are used in acupuncture, while others are key ingredients in
herbal remedies that ward off fatigue, cold, or even supernatural dangers.

[Myrrh](ref/eq-reagent/myrrh)

**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -

Myrrh is an aromatic gum used as a base for alchemical remedies and as a sticky bonding agent.

[Phosphorus](ref/eq-reagent/phosphorus)

**Price** 2 sp; **Weight** -

Phosphorus is a highly reactive mineral that glows faintly in air. Vital to all life, it is most
commonly used by alchemists to create fertilizers, poisons, soaps, and tindertwigs. It is obtained
by processing uric acid or bone ash.

[Quicksilver](ref/eq-reagent/quicksilver)

**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** -

Quicksilver is a liquid metal with dangerous mental effects on those who handle it. It is most often
distilled from crystalline amalgams such as cinnabar and stored in iron flasks.

[Realgar](ref/eq-reagent/realgar)

**Price** 3 gp; **Weight** -

Realgar, also called ruby of arsenic, is a red crystal useful in creating poisons and medicines. It
is commonly purified into [arsenic](ref/eq-poison/arsenic), a crystalline metal known as the "king
of poisons" for its popularity among nobles seeking to poison their rivals.

[Salt](ref/eq-reagent/salt)

**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -

Salt is a vital mineral commodity. It is most commonly used as a desiccant and as a catalyst.

[Saltpeter](ref/eq-reagent/saltpeter)

**Price** 3 gp; **Weight** -

Saltpeter is used to create fertilizers, propellants, fireworks, and preservatives.

[Silver](ref/eq-reagent/silver)

**Special material:** [Silver](ref/eq-material/alchemical-silver)\
**Price** 1 gp; **Weight** -

Silver is a lustrous precious metal used in mirrors, disinfectants, electrics, and illusion-creating
magic items.

[Spirit of Wine](ref/eq-reagent/spirit-of-wine)

**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -

Spirit of wine is purified, alchemically active alcohol commonly used as a fuel or solvent in
alchemical items, specialized inks, and perfumes. It is too pure to drink alone, but can be used as
a reagent to form tinctures and alchemical drinks.

[Urea](ref/eq-reagent/urea)

**Price** 5 sp; **Weight** -

Urea is a highly versatile reagent most commonly used in salves and as a stabilizer for particularly
volatile alchemical reactions.
