**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 301\
[Undead](ref/type/undead) are immune to death effects, disease, mind-affecting effects (charms,
compulsions, morale effects, patterns, and phantasms), paralysis, poison, sleep, stun, and any
effect that requires a Fortitude save (unless the effect also works on objects or is harmless).
Undead are not subject to ability drain, energy drain, or nonlethal damage. Undead are immune to
damage or penalties to their physical ability scores (Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution), as
well as to fatigue and exhaustion effects. Undead are not at risk of death from
[massive damage](ref/rule/loss-of-hit-points).
