These events apply to a criminal code such as that used by a thieves' guild or ninja clan.

|Event|Honor Points|
| --- | --- |
|Become a guildmaster (city population 25,000+)^[1](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-1)^|+50|
|Become a guildmaster (city population 10,000+)^[1](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-1)^|+25|
|Adopt a specific criminal code^[2](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-2)^|+4|
|End a long-running feud (1+ years) with a criminal more honorable than you^[3](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-3)^|+3|
|Party humiliates an honorable noble^[4](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-4)^|+3|
|Corrupt a high-ranking public official^[5](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-5)^|+2|
|Willingly take the fall for a higher-ranking criminal|+2|
|Minstrels willingly sing about your exploits^[5](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-5)^|+1|
|Defeat a legal challenge, be acquitted, or avoid sentencing|+1|
|Party agrees to and then commits a significant crime|+1|
|Steal a powerful magic item or valuable treasure|+1^[6](ref/rule/criminal-code#footnote-6)^|
|Be convicted of a significant crime|+1|
|Pay minstrels to sing about your exploits|-2|
|Willingly work with the authorities to prevent or solve a crime|-2|
|Party agrees to commit a crime and then reneges|-3|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

These events don't stack for the same city. If the city's population increases after you become
guildmaster, you gain the difference in honor points between the two events.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

Such as "I commit only property crimes" or "I never steal, I only commit murder"

###### ^3^ {#footnote-3}

Your honor score and the other criminal's must differ by at least 20 points.

###### ^4^ {#footnote-4}

Non-criminal honor point score of 50 or more.

###### ^5^ {#footnote-5}

You can gain honor points this way once per month.

###### ^6^ {#footnote-6}

Per 40,000 gp of the item's price.
