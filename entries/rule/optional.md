**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 163

Words of power are an optional subsystem for the Pathfinder Roleplaying Game. Before making a
wordcaster, check with your GM to make sure her campaign allows them.

**Warning:** Words of power spellcasting is a complicated subsystem that, in the hands of players
suffering from bouts of analysis paralysis, may slow down game play. But in the right hands, the
system creates a fun, flexible play experience for fans of free-form spellcasting that works either
alone or with standard Pathfinder spellcasters.
