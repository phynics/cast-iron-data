**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 106

Over time, a PC's influence over an NPC is likely to wane if the PC doesn't keep in contact with her
(and continue making influence checks every once in a while), depending on the influence's nature.
Generally, the higher the influence level, the more effort the PC must commit and thus the faster
the influence degrades with neglect. However, if the PCs achieve major sway over an NPC because she
becomes deeply indebted to them, at the GM's discretion their influence level may not degrade until
the NPC feels she has repaid that debt, making it a matter of favors rather than time.
