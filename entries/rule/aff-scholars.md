**Pathfinder Name:** Pathfinder Society\
**Scope:** global\
**Alignment:** N\
**Values:** camaraderie, cooperation, proper documentation of important findings, self-promotion
though publication, world travel\
**Goals:** understand the world's past and present wonders by exploring important sites, recovering
relics, chronicling what's found, and publishing findings\
**Allies:** few steadfast allies; countless minor alliances maintained by a region's
venture-captains and agents; [Inheritors of the Ancients](ref/rule/aff-i-tradition);
[The Ancient School](ref/rule/aff-i-school)\
**Enemies:** [Crimson Syndicate](ref/rule/aff-profiteers),
[Cult of the Assassin](ref/rule/aff-assassins)\
**Rivals:** Crimson Syndicate
