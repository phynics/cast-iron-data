**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 162

When a character gains the ability to cast spells from a class, he must decide whether to become a
wordcaster or a normal spellcaster in that class. Once made, this choice cannot be changed. A
character who elects to be a wordcaster in a spellcasting class typically cannot use the spells of
that class (though the [Experimental Spellcaster](ref/feat/experimental-spellcaster) feat allows a
spellcaster to learn and use a limited number of words of power), but he can utilize spell trigger
and spell completion items just as if he were an ordinary spellcaster of that class.
