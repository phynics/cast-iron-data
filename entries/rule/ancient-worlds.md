**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 163

In contrast to a young world, an ancient world has already hit the peak of its civilizations and is
fast approaching its end. In the twilight of this setting, some races have been lost to history or
have moved on to other shores in the multiverse. Technology may have come and gone, giving the
setting either a dystopian, post-apocalyptic theme or a pastoral, back-to-nature aesthetic. Or it
may have advanced to a truly astounding level, marrying the traditional fantasy elements to
steampunk or cyberpunk tropes. If the world is ancient, tradition might be even more dominant over
the lives of individuals. Sorcerers may no longer exist, having had their magical traits codified
into the power of wizards. Barbarians and druids might no longer have a place, or perhaps their role
is changed to that of degenerate savages.
