**Sources:** Heroes of Golarion pg 28

In much the same way that kineticists can channel elemental energy, a tiny number of them are
eventually able to master the ability to channel the strange energy that empowers mythic creatures.
Some believe that any kineticist can unlock this ability with enough dedicated work and skill, while
others believe it is a trait one must be born with. In either case, mythic kineticists tend to
gravitate toward either the path of the **champion** (generally known as _the path of the
channeler_) or the **guardian** (sometimes referred to as _the path of infinite stillness_).
