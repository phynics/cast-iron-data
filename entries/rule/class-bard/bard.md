**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 34

Untold wonders and secrets exist for those skillful enough to discover them. Through cleverness,
talent, and magic, these cunning few unravel the wiles of the world, becoming adept in the arts of
persuasion, manipulation, and inspiration. Typically masters of one or many forms of artistry, bards
possess an uncanny ability to know more than they should and use what they learn to keep themselves
and their allies ever one step ahead of danger. Bards are quick-witted and captivating, and their
skills might lead them down many paths, be they gamblers or jacks-of-all-trades, scholars or
performers, leaders or scoundrels, or even all of the above. For bards, every day brings its own
opportunities, adventures, and challenges, and only by bucking the odds, knowing the most, and being
the best might they claim the treasures of each.

**Role:** Bards capably confuse and confound their foes while inspiring their allies to ever-greater
daring. While accomplished with both weapons and magic, the true strength of bards lies outside
melee, where they can support their companions and undermine their foes without fear of
interruptions to their performances.

**Alignment:** Any

**Hit Die:** d8

**Starting Wealth:** 3d6 × 10 gp (average 105 gp)

**Skill Points per Level:** 6 + Int modifier

**Class Skills (Str):** Climb

**Class Skills (Dex):** Acrobatics, Escape Artist, Sleight of Hand, Stealth

**Class Skills (Int):** Appraise, Craft (any), Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (dungeoneering),
Knowledge (engineering), Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (history), Knowledge (local), Knowledge
(nature), Knowledge (nobility), Knowledge (planes), Knowledge (religion), Linguistics, Spellcraft

**Class Skills (Wis):** Perception, Profession (any), Sense Motive

**Class Skills (Cha):** Bluff, Diplomacy, Disguise, Intimidate, Perform (any), Use Magic Device

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Weapons:** All simple weapons, longsword, rapier, sap, short sword, shortbow, whip

**Armor:** Light armor

**Shields:** Yes, except tower shields

**Notes:** You can cast bard spells while wearing light armor and use a shield without incurring the
normal arcane spell failure chance. Like any other arcane spellcaster, wearing medium or heavy armor
incurs a chance of arcane spell failure if the spell in question has a somatic component. A
multiclass bard still incurs the normal arcane spell failure chance for arcane spells received from
other classes.

**Spells**

**Spell list:** Arcane spells from the bard list, from 1st level

**Key ability:** Charisma. Minimum score 10 + spell level; save DC 10 + spell level + Charisma
modifier; a high score grants bonus spells per day.

**Casting:** Spontaneous: cast any spell known without preparation, within the daily allotment per
spell level.

**Spells known:** Very limited selection; starts with four 0-level and two 1st-level spells and
grows by level as shown on the table. Charisma does not change the number known.

**Spell swap:** At 5th, 8th, 11th, and every 3 levels after, one known spell may be exchanged for
another of the same level that is at least one level below the highest level you can cast.

**Notes:** Every spell has a verbal component (song, recitation, or music).

**Cantrips**

**Ability:** 0-level arcane spells. Cast without using a spell slot; one placed in a higher slot
(for example through metamagic) uses that slot.

**Bardic Knowledge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You add [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your class level (minimum 1) to all
Knowledge skill checks and may make all Knowledge skill checks untrained.

**Bardic Performance**

**Action:** Standard Action

**Flavor:** You are trained to use the Perform skill to create magical effects on those around you,
including yourself if desired.

**At 13th Level:** You can begin a performance as a **swift action**.

**At 7th Level:** You can begin a performance as a **move-equivalent action**.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Usage:** 2 + twice your bard level + your Cha modifier rounds/day.

**Usage:** 2 + twice your bard level + your Cha modifier rounds/day.

Each round, you can produce any one of the types of bardic performance that you have mastered, as
indicated by your level.

Starting a bardic performance is a **standard action**, but it can be maintained each round as a
**free action**. Changing a bardic performance from one effect to another requires you to stop the
previous performance and start a new one as a standard action. A bardic performance cannot be
disrupted, but it ends immediately if you are killed, [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed),
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned), knocked [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious), or otherwise prevented
from taking a free action to maintain it each round. You cannot have more than one bardic
performance in effect at one time.

Each bardic performance has audible components, visual components, or both.

If a bardic performance has audible components, the targets must be able to hear the bard for the
performance to have any effect, and many such performances are language dependent (as noted in the
description. A deaf bard has a 20% chance to fail when attempting to use a bardic performance with
an audible component. If he fails this check, the attempt still counts against his daily limit. Deaf
creatures are immune to bardic performances with audible components.

If a bardic performance has a visual component, the targets must have line of sight to the bard for
the performance to have any effect. A blind bard has a 50% chance to fail when attempting to use a
bardic performance with a visual component. If he fails this check, the attempt still counts against
his daily limit. Blind creatures are immune to bardic performances with visual components.

Bardic performances are collected here.

**Bardic Masterpieces:** Talented bards can learn or create masterpieces, unusual applications of
the bardic performance ability requiring special training. Each masterpiece has an associated cost
to learn it. Typically, a bard must spend one of his bard spells known of a specific spell level or
select it in place of a feat.

Also, a bard may learn [saga](ref/ability/sagas), which are useable with his bardic performance
ability.

**Versatile Performance (Ex)**

**Action:** Choice

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Repeat:** You can select an additional type of Perform to substitute., 6, 4

You can choose one type of Perform skill, using your bonus in that skill in place of your bonus in
associated skills. When substituting in this way, you use your total Perform skill bonus, including
class skill bonus, in place of its associated skill's bonus, whether or not you have ranks in that
skill or if it is a class skill.

The types of Perform and their associated skills are:

- **Act:** Bluff, Disguise
- **Comedy:** Bluff, Intimidate
- **Dance:** Acrobatics, Fly
- **Keyboard Instruments:** Diplomacy, Intimidate
- **Oratory:** Diplomacy, Sense Motive
- **Percussion:** Handle Animal, Intimidate
- **Sing:** Bluff, Sense Motive
- **String:** Bluff, Diplomacy
- **Wind:** Diplomacy, Handle Animal

Beginning at 6th level, instead of selecting an additional skill with versatile performance, a bard
can choose an [advanced versatile performance](ref/ability/advanced-versatile-performances) for one
Perform skill he previously selected with versatile performance. Some advanced versatile performance
options can be selected only if the bard meets the option's prerequisites. A bard with an archetype
that replaces versatile performance cannot select advanced versatile performance options.

**Well-Versed (Ex)**

**Flavor:** You become resistant to the bardic performance of others, and to sonic effects in
general.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws made against bardic performance, sonic, and
language-dependent effects.

**Lore Master (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can take 10 on any Knowledge skill check that you have ranks in even when
threatened or distracted. You can also choose not to take 10 and can instead roll normally.

**Flavor:** You become a master of lore.

**At 11th Level:** You can Take 20 twice a day.

**At 17th Level:** You can Take 20 three times a day.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Standard:** Once per day, you can take 20 on any Knowledge skill check.

**Jack-of-All-Trades (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** You can use any skill, even if the skill normally requires you to be trained.

**At 16th Level:** You consider all skills to be class skills.

**At 19th Level:** You can take 10 on any skill check, even if it is not normally allowed.

### Alternate Capstones

**Source** Chronicle of Legends pg. 28\
When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Adoring Fan (Su)**

At 20th level, the bard can use his performance to convert even the toughest audience. To be
affected, a target must be able to see and hear the bard perform for 1 full round and be within 30
feet. The target can attempt a Will save (DC = 10 + half the bard's level + the bard's Charisma
modifier) to negate the effect. If a creature succeeds at the saving throw, the bard cannot use this
ability on that creature again for 24 hours. On a failure, the creature is dominated (as
[dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster)) for as long as the bard keeps playing and is charmed
(as [charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster)) for 1d4 hours thereafter. Adoring fan is a
mind-affecting, compulsion effect that relies on audible and visual components.

**Also available:** Deep Magics

### Archetypes

**Source** Advanced Player's Guide pg. 72\
From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Countersong; Inspire Competence; Suggestion; Mass
Suggestion; Versatile Performance; Lore Master

**Description:** The masked performer takes on the persona represented by the likeness she wears.

**Mute Musician**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Inspire Competence; Frightening Tune; Inspire Heroics;
Mass Suggestion; Versatile Performance; Well-Versed; Jack-of-All-Trades

**Description:** A mute musician forswears speech for the unnatural songs and thunderous silences of
the depths of space.

**Negotiator**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Bardic Performance; Versatile Performance; Lore Master

**Description:** Negotiators are those bards who take the skills of a performer and apply them to
the realms of business and law.

**Phrenologist**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Well-Versed; Jack-of-All-Trades; Inspire Courage;
Inspire Competence; Fascinate

**Description:** The phrenologist is an expert at reading creatures' skulls, and he learns to use
this knowledge to his benefit.

**Pitax: Academy of Grand Arts**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Versatile Performance

**Description:** Those who have successfully mastered the strict curriculum of the Academy of Grand
Arts.

**Plant Speaker**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Inspire Greatness; Bardic Knowledge; Well-Versed; Lore Master

**Description:** (Vine Leshy only) Plant speakers build upon their racial plantspeech ability and
their storytelling tendencies to create an art form full of deep metaphors and allegory rather than
specific details and facts.

**Prankster**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fascinate; Suggestion; Mass Suggestion; Lore Master

**Description:** (Gnome Only) The prankster sees humor as the highest form or art, and pranks as the
highest form of humor.

**Provocateur**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; 2nd-level Versatile Performance; Suggestion, Mass
Suggestion

**Description:** Provocateurs are bards who use their art to make controversial political
statements, undermine enemies' reputations, and upset the status quo on a large scale.

**Ringleader (AG)**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Performance; Well-Versed; Versatile Performance; Suggestion; Mass
Suggestion; Soothing Performance

**Description:** Ringleaders are specialists who oversee large operations in which trouble could
strike at any moment and any place.

**Ringleader (UI)**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Inspire Competence; Dirge of Doom; Frightening Tune; Versatile
Performance; Well-Versed; Lore Master

**Description:** Ringleaders are adept in getting the most out of their allies, and they excel at
planning for unexpected complications in advance.

**Sandman**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Inspire Courage; Suggestion; Inspire Greatness; Inspire Heroics; Mass
Suggestion; Deadly Performance; Bardic Knowledge; Versatile Performance; Inspire Competence; Lore
Master

**Description:** Combining performance with stealth, trickery, and guile, the sandman uses
cleverness to keep others off-balance.

**Savage Skald**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fascinate; Suggestion; Jack-of-All-Trades; Soothing Performance; Mass
Suggestion

**Description:** Far from civilization, furious tribes have their own war-singers, work-chanters,
and lore-keepers, savaging enemies with song and sword alike.

**Sea Singer**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Countersong; Inspire Competence; Suggestion; Mass Suggestion; Bardic
Knowledge; Versatile Performance; Well-Versed

**Description:** The sea singer calls the blue waters his home, and is much in demand among sea
captains wishing good fortune for their crew and hull as they ply the tradewinds far and wide.

**Shadow Puppeteer**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Inspire Courage; Inspire Competence

**Description:** (Wayang Only) A shadow puppeteer invokes amazing and terrifying shadow puppet
shows, producing supernatural effects by creating and manipulating shadow.

**Silver Balladeer**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Alignment; Suggestion; Inspire Greatness; Mass Suggestion; Well-Versed;
2nd-level Versatile Performance

**Description:** The bright purity of silver makes beautiful music, and its vibrations are also
anathema to unnatural creatures. Some bards use a mixture of silver-stringed instruments and
esoteric knowledge to battle the dark forces of the world.

**Solacer**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Bardic Knowledge; Lore Master; Countersong; Versatile
Performance; Jack-of-All-Trades

**Description:** Solacers are experts in the healing arts as well as creators of performances that
console the distraught, rally the stricken, raise spirits, and vanquish sorrow.

**Songhealer**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Versatile Performance; Frightening Tune; Deadly Performance

**Description:** The songhealer brings peace and surcease of pain, calming wild emotions and
providing a balm for the wounded body.

**Sorrowsoul**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Soothing Performance; Versatile Performance; Well-Versed; Lore Master

**Description:** While most bards, on occasion, sing mournful tunes or craft elegies that depict the
deepest sadness or the most profound suffering, the sorrowsoul has known tragedy and loss on a level
so intimate, he has bound it to his soul.

**Sound Striker**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Inspire Competence; Suggestion

**Description:** They say that words can cut deeper than any blade, and the sound striker proves
this true.

**Speaker of the Palatine Eye**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Spells; Countersong; Jack-of-All-Trades

**Description:** A speaker of the Palatine Eye influences others using his occult knowledge rather
than the boisterous enthusiasm of most bards.

**Stonesinger**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spellcasting; Bardic Performance; Countersong; Dirge of Doom

**Description:** Stonesingers are rare bards capable of vocalizing their bardic performances through
subsonic harmonies, allowing their performances to carry as subtle vibrations through stone, rather
than through the air.

**Street Performer**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Inspire Courage; Inspire Competence; Inspire Greatness; Inspire Heroics;
Countersong; Bardic Knowledge; Lore Master

**Description:** Whether acrobat, troubadour, or thespian, the street performer mixes with the
masses, singing for his supper.

**Studious Librarian**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Distraction; Suggestion; Jack-of-All-Trades; Dirge of Doom; Mass
Suggestion; Deadly Performance

**Description:** Studious librarians are bards that have studied long in great libraries of the
Inner Sea region.

**Thundercaller**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Inspire Competence; Suggestion; Mass Suggestion; Dirge
of Doom; Frightening Tune

**Description:** Thundercallers bear the knowledge of the ancients, wielding it like a weapon to
protect the land for which they care so deeply.

**Voice of Brigh**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fascinate; Dirge of Doom; Soothing Performance; Frightening Tune

**Description:** The Whisper in Bronze sometimes sends inspiration to her favorite followers in the
form of music

**Voice of the Wild**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Countersong; Versatile Performance; Jack-of-All-Trades;
Inspire Competence; Dirge of Doom; Inspire Heroics

**Description:** Most bards are inspired by the art of civilization, yet the voice of the wild's
muse is the grandeur and beauty of nature.

**Wasteland Chronicler**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Inspire Competence

**Description:** Wasteland chroniclers explore the farthest reaches of desolate wastelands, seeking
to unlock the mysteries found there and meet the inhabitants of such regions

**Watersinger**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Fascinate, Suggestion, Mass Suggestion, Inspire Competence, 5th-level Lore
Master

**Description:** (Undine Only) The watersinger's song reaches from the depths of his soul into the
elemental waters from which life first sprang.

**Wit**

**Class:** bard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bardic Knowledge; Inspire Competence; Dirge of Doom; Frightening Tune;
Versatile Performance; Lore Master; Jack-of-All-Trades

**Description:** The wit is a master of clever repartee and verbal dueling, moving through noble
courts like a fish through water.
