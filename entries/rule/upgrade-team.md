**Upgrade Team** **(No Team Required):** By spending the amount of gold pieces listed for an
upgraded team's cost, the PCs can upgrade a lower-tier team. Each team can be upgraded only once per
week, but the PCs can upgrade as many different teams as the militia can afford (in gold and militia
actions) for that week.
