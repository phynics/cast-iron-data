Though they're mental constructs, mindscapes can sometimes cause physiological effects through
mental feedback. A creator with exceptional psychic power might be able to create a mindscape that
is harmless for her but harmful for all other inhabitants, but most mindscapes affect everyone in
the same way.

**Harmful:** Injuries and conditions inflicted upon individuals visiting a harmful mindscape are
real. Any psychic attacks harm the body by convincing the brain that the damage is real. Binary
mindscapes are always harmful.

**Harmless:** Occupants of a harmless mindscape still feel the sensation of the environment or other
creatures harming them, but injuries and conditions are not real: they cause no physical harm to the
host's body. A creature that dies in a harmless mindscape wakes up none the worse for wear.
