Stairs are the most common means of traveling up and down within a dungeon. A character can move up
or down stairs as part of their movement without penalty, but they cannot run on them. Increase the
DC of any Acrobatics skill check made on stairs by 4. Some stairs are particularly steep and are
treated as difficult terrain.
