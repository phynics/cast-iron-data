Rings work in much the same way as wondrous items.

**Removed Items:** Remove all [rings of protection](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-protection-1), and add
the deflection bonus specified on Table 4-12: Rings to all other rings.

**Multiple Rings:** Note that deflection bonuses granted by multiple rings don't stack. It's up to
the GM to decide how to deal with this, with two main options. The first option is to follow the
rule outlined above and charge the price increase for each ring - consider the additional cost the
price of essentially getting a third ring slot. The other option is to make the deflection bonus
granted by rings (and the associated price increases) optional on a ring-by-ring basis, allowing
characters to avoid paying twice for a non-stacking bonus. Doing so adds additional bookkeeping,
however.

|32,000-49,999 gp|+4|+32,000 gp|
|50,000+ gp|+5|+50,000 gp|
