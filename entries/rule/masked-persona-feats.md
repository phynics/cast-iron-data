**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 13

The following feats help a character maintain or gain additional benefits from her masked persona. A
character must be have an established, uncompromised masked persona to gain the benefits of the
feats listed below (excluding Convincing Persona), and loses access to the feats' benefits if her
identity is compromised.

- [Convincing Persona](ref/feat/convincing-persona)
- [Masked Renown](ref/feat/masked-renown)
- [Masked Symbol](ref/feat/masked-symbol)
