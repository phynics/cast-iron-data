**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 163

Each nightmare has one or more nightmare features, which complicate the nightmare in some fashion.
For naturally occurring nightmares, roll twice on the table below or simply select which nightmare
features to use.

|d%|Nightmare Feature|
| --- | --- |
|01-20|Being chased|
|21-30|False awakening|
|31-50|Incompetent|
|51-65|No gear|
|66-80|Unable to move|
|81-95|Unable to speak|
|96-100|Other disadvantage, or roll again twice|

**Being Chased:** The dreamer is being stalked or chased by something. The thing should be difficult
(or impossible) to defeat in combat, but possible to escape. Either employ the
[chase rules](ref/rule/chases) or adjudicate the stalking creature's threat in some other way.

**False Awakening:** When the nightmare ends, the dreamer believes it is awake, but actually enters
another nightmare dreamscape. Roll one nightmare feature fewer when determining the nature of this
dreamscape.

**Incompetent:** The dreamer is suddenly unable to do something that it is normally good at. It
might lose ranks in an important skill, have its base attack bonus reduced by half, or lose a key
class feature or ability for the duration of the dream.

**No Gear:** The dreamer arrives in the dream naked, without any gear or equipment.

**Unable to Move:** The dreamer has difficulty traveling. The dreamer could literally be paralyzed,
or it might find that no matter where it goes, it always winds up in the same spot. Either way, the
inability to move shouldn't render the nightmare's goal impossible.

**Unable to Speak:** The dreamer either can't speak or can't understand anything said in the dream.
