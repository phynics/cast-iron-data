**Sources:** Heroes of Golarion pg 29

Mythic spiritualists almost always travel in the company of the phantoms of legendary heroes and
villains, although it is unclear whether it is the phantom that gives the spiritualist mythic power
or the spiritualist's mythic nature that attracts such a potent phantom. Most spiritualists are
drawn to the **hierophant** path, and spiritualists who take this path are often referred to as
_shepherds of the underworld._ **A spiritualist treats any references to an eidolon within a mythic
ability** (such as the _mythic companion_ [guardian path ability](ref/rule/guardian-1)) **as though
it also referred to phantoms.**
