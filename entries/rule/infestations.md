**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 245

Parasites such as ear seekers or rot grubs cause infestations, a type of affliction similar to
diseases. Infestations can only be cured through specific means; no matter how many saving throws
are made, the infestation continues to afflict the target. While a
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease) spell (or similar effect) instantly halts an infestation,
immunity to disease offers no protection, as the infestation itself is caused by parasites.
Infestations can afflict [androids](ref/race/android) despite their artificial nature and immunity
to disease, but they can't afflict nonliving creatures such as undead.

Infestations use the same stat blocks as other [afflictions](ref/rule/afflictions).

|Infestation|Fortitude DC|Description|

|[Ear Seekers](ref/infestation/ear-seekers)|15|Ear seekers are tiny, pale-colored worms that dwell in rotting wood or other organic detritus.|

|[Rot Grubs](ref/infestation/rot-grubs)|17|Rot grubs are nauseating parasites that feed on flesh and nest in corpses.|
|[Soldier's Peace](ref/infestation/soldiers-peace)|15|These nanites settle in the brain, where they inflict crippling pain in response to violent acts and thoughts.|
|[Thought Crawlers](ref/infestation/thought-crawlers)|16|These parasitic worms live in cerebrospinal fluid, causing an escalating progression of debilitating neurological symptoms.|
