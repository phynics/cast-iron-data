**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 114

In some cases, the PCs themselves can take on the responsibility of providing their own rewards,
using character abilities and resources gained from their adventures to create exactly the weapons,
armors, tools, and treasures they desire. While mundane items might be created using various Craft
skills, many PCs set their sights upon more extraordinary goals, such as researching and designing
new spells and crafting magic items.
