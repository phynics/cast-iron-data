###### ^1^ {#footnote-a}

Consult Table 5-15: Weapon Special Materials.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-b}

Consult [Table 5-3: Random Armor](ref/rule/random-armor-and-shields). There is a 10% chance the
armor is magical; if so, consult Table 5-8: Magic Armor and Shields.

###### ^3^ {#footnote-c}

Consult [Table 5-5: Random Shields](ref/rule/random-armor-and-shields); reroll buckler and tower
shield (which cannot have spikes). There is a 10% chance the shield is magical; if so, consult Table
5-8: Magic Armor and Shields.

###### ^4^ {#footnote-d}

For adamantine weapons, add +2700 gp; for ammunition, add +54 gp per missile.

###### ^5^ {#footnote-e}

For cold iron weapons, double the item's base cost.

###### ^6^ {#footnote-f}

For darkwood weapons, add +10 gp per pound.

###### ^7^ {#footnote-g}

For mithral weapons, add +500 gp per pound.

###### ^8^ {#footnote-h}

For silver weapons, add +2 gp for ammunition, +20 gp for light weapons, +90 gp for one-handed
weapons, and +180 gp for two-handed weapons

###### ^9^ {#footnote-i}

For ammunition, this price is for 50 arrows, bolts, or bullets.

###### ^10^ {#footnote-j}

A weapon can't have an enhancement bonus higher than +5. Use these lines to determine price when
special abilities are added in.

###### ^11^ {#footnote-k}

Consult Table 5-17: Magic Melee Weapon Special Abilities or Table 5-18: Magic Ranged Weapon Special
Abilities, as appropriate.

###### ^12^ {#footnote-l}

Add to enhancement bonus on Table 5-16: Magic Weapons to determine total market price.

###### ^13^ {#footnote-m}

Piercing or slashing weapons only (slashing only for vorpal). Reroll if randomly generated for a
bludgeoning weapon.

###### ^14^ {#footnote-n}

Bludgeoning weapons only. Reroll if randomly generated for a piercing or slashing weapon.

###### ^15^ {#footnote-o}

Reroll if you get a duplicate special ability, an ability incompatible with an ability that you've
already rolled, or if the extra ability puts you over the +10 limit. A weapon's enhancement bonus
and special ability bonus equivalents can't total more than +10.

###### ^16^ {#footnote-p}

Bows, crossbows, and slings crafted with this ability bestow this power upon their ammunition.
