Dimming the lights can go a long way in creating a moodier environment. Shadows add an air of the
unknown, and cause everyday distractions to fade away. Make sure that the players can see, though -
there's a lot of reading and page referencing in Pathfinder that GMs don't want to turn into a
chore. A room with lights on a dimmer switch works well, as does turning off overhead lighting and
moving a single-bulb lamp into the room. Candles and non-electric lamps typically prove distracting
and troublesome, if not outright hazardous. While rooms with natural lighting can create issues
during the day, at night, the dark can make them prime game spaces.
