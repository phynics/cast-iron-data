**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 496

This is a catch all category for anything that doesn't fall into the other groups. Anyone can use a
wondrous item (unless specified otherwise in the description).

**Physical Description:** Varies.

**Activation:** Usually use-activated or command word, but details vary from item to item.

**Special Qualities:** Roll d%. An 01 result indicates the wondrous item is intelligent, 02-31
indicates that something (a design, inscription, or the like) provides a clue to its function, and
32-100 indicates no special qualities. [Intelligent items](ref/rule/intelligent-items) have extra
abilities and sometimes extraordinary powers and special purposes.

Wondrous items with charges can never be intelligent.
