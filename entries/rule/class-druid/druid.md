Within the purity of the elements and the order of the wilds lingers a power beyond the marvels of
civilization. Furtive yet undeniable, these primal magics are guarded over by servants of
philosophical balance known as druids. Allies to beasts and manipulators of nature, these often
misunderstood protectors of the wild strive to shield their lands from all who would threaten them
and prove the might of the wilds to those who lock themselves behind city walls. Rewarded for their
devotion with incredible powers, druids gain unparalleled shape-shifting abilities, the
companionship of mighty beasts, and the power to call upon nature's wrath. The mightiest temper
powers akin to storms, earthquakes, and volcanoes with primeval wisdom long abandoned and forgotten
by civilization.

**Role:** While some druids might keep to the fringe of battle, allowing companions and summoned
creatures to fight while they confound foes with the powers of nature, others transform into deadly
beasts and savagely wade into combat. Druids worship personifications of elemental forces, natural
powers, or nature itself. Typically this means devotion to a nature deity, though druids are just as
likely to revere vague spirits, animalistic demigods, or even specific awe-inspiring natural
wonders.

**Alignment:** Any neutral

**Hit Die:** d8

**Starting Wealth:** 2d6 × 10 gp (average 70 gp)

**Skill Points per Level:** 4 + Int modifier

**Class Skills (Str):** Climb, Swim

**Class Skills (Dex):** Fly, Ride

**Class Skills (Int):** Craft (any), Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (nature), Spellcraft

**Class Skills (Wis):** Heal, Perception, Profession (any), Survival

**Class Skills (Cha):** Handle Animal

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Weapons:** club, dagger, dart, quarterstaff, scimitar, scythe, sickle, shortspear, sling, spear,
All natural attacks (claw, bite, and so forth) of any form the druid assumes with wild shape

**Armor:** Light armor, Medium armor

**Shields:** Yes, except tower shields

**Armor Notes:** Druids are prohibited from wearing metal armor or using metal shields, but is
allowed to wear wooden armor that has been altered by the [ironwood](ref/spell/ironwood) spell so
that it functions as though it were steel.

**Notes:** A druid who wears prohibited armor or uses a prohibited shield is unable to cast druid
spells or use any of her supernatural or spell-like class abilities while doing so and for 24 hours
thereafter.

**Spells**

**Spell list:** Divine spells from the druid list, from 1st level

**Key ability:** Wisdom. Minimum score 10 + spell level; save DC 10 + spell level + Wisdom modifier;
a high score grants bonus spells per day.

**Casting:** Prepared: choose and prepare spells in advance.

**Notes:** Alignment can bar spells opposed to it. Regains spells after 1 hour meditating on nature
each day; any spell on the list can be prepared.

**Orisons**

**Ability:** 0-level divine spells. Prepared like other spells but not expended when cast; one
placed in a higher slot (for example through metamagic) uses that slot.

**Spontaneous Casting**

**Ability:** You can &quot&lose&quot& a prepared spell in order to cast any
[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) spell of the same level or lower.

**Flavor:** A druid can channel stored spell energy into summoning spells that she hasn't prepared
ahead of time.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Chaotic, Evil, Good, and Lawful Spells**

**Section:** Aligned Spells

**Passive:** You can't cast spells of an alignment opposed to your own or your deity's (if you have
one). Spells associated with particular alignments are indicated by the chaos, evil, good, and law
descriptors in their spell descriptions.

**Bonus Languages**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Your bonus language options include Sylvan, the language of woodland creatures. This
choice is in addition to the bonus languages available to you because of your race. You also know
Druidic, a secret language known only to druids, which you learn upon becoming a 1st-level druid.

**Special:** Druidic is a free language; that is, you know it in addition to your regular allotment
of languages and it doesn't take up a language slot. Druids are forbidden to teach this language to
non-druids. Druidic has its own alphabet.

**Nature Bond (Ex)**

**Choice:** You form a bond with nature. This bond can take one of three forms: domain, companion,
or herbalism.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Domain**

**Ability:** You gain one of the following cleric domains: [Air](ref/domain/air),
[Animal](ref/domain/animal), [Earth](ref/domain/earth), [Fire](ref/domain/fire),
[Plant](ref/domain/plant), [Water](ref/domain/water), or [Weather](ref/domain/weather). Alternately,
you can choose a [druid-specific domain](ref/ability/druid-domains). When determining the powers and
bonus spells granted by this domain, your effective cleric level is equal to your druid level. A
druid that selects this option also receives additional domain spell slots, just like a
[cleric](ref/class/cleric). You must prepare the spell from your domain in this slot and this spell
cannot be used to cast a spell spontaneously.

**Companion**

**Ability:** You may begin play with any of the animals listed in Animal Companions, who becomes a
loyal companion that accompanies you on your adventures. Unlike normal animals of its kind, an
[animal companion's](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) Hit Dice, abilities, skills, and feats advance
as you advance in level.

**Special:** If you receive an animal companion from more than one source, your effective druid
levels stack for the purposes of determining the statistics and abilities of the companion. Most
animal companions increase in size when their druid reaches 4th or 7th level, depending on the
companion. If you release your companion from service, you may gain a new one by performing a
ceremony requiring 24 uninterrupted hours of prayer in the environment where the new companion
typically lives. This ceremony can also replace an animal companion that has perished.

**Herbalism**

**Ability:** You can create a free herbal concoction. Additional concoctions cost the same as
creating an equivalent potion using Brew Potion. You can sell your concoctions just as if they were
potions (though NPCs unfamiliar with druidic herbalism may need some convincing before purchasing
these wares).

**At 4th Level:** You can now make a concoction of any spell on any list that you can cast. Your
additional concoctions take [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) the time to make, at half the
cost. You can also disguise the effects of a concoction. When a creature fails a Perception or
Spellcraft check to identify one, the concoction appears to be a different herbal concoction of your
choice. You must designate this false result when creating the concoction. If a creature exceeds the
check's DC by 5 or more, it correctly identifies the concoction, though not that you tried to fool
it.

**At 7th Level:** A creature must exceed the DC by 10 to correctly identify a disguised concoction.
Also, you can create any herbal concoction in 1 minute. You can also create a special concoction of
any spell higher than 3rd level that you can cast, but you must expend a spell slot of the same
level. These special concoctions do not cost you anything to create and function like extracts
created by an [alchemist](ref/class/alchemist) with the [infusion](ref/discovery/infusion)
discovery.

**Info:** You can use combinations of nuts, berries, dried herbs, and other natural ingredients
along with appropriate containers to create herbal concoctions or magic consumables that function
like potions. This acts like the [Brew Potion](ref/feat/brew-potion) feat, but only for spells on
the druid spell list. Herbal concoctions are typically thick and sludgy, and their creation time,
caster level, spell duplication capabilities, and all other variables and properties are identical
to those of potions created using Brew Potion. Herbal concoctions created with herbs that cause
special effects when ingested retain those effects as well as the appropriate spell effect.

**Usage:** Wis modifier times/day

**Nature Sense (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on Knowledge (nature) and Survival checks.

**Wild Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** This ability functions just like a [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) check made to
improve the attitude of a person. You roll 1d20 and add your druid level and your Charisma modifier
to determine the wild empathy check result. The typical domestic animal has a starting attitude of
indifferent, while wild animals are usually unfriendly. You can also use this ability to influence a
magical beast with an Intelligence score of 1 or 2, but you take a -4 penalty on the check.

**Flavor:** A druid can improve the attitude of an animal.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** To use wild empathy, you and the animal must be within 30 feet of one another under
normal conditions. Generally, influencing an animal in this way takes 1 minute but, as with
influencing people, it might take more or less time.

**Woodland Stride (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** You may move through any sort of undergrowth (such as natural thorns, briars, overgrown
areas, and similar terrain) at your normal speed and without taking damage or suffering any other
impairment. Thorns, briars, and overgrown areas that have been magically manipulated to impede
motion, however, still affect you.

**Trackless Step (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You leave no trail in natural surroundings and cannot be tracked. You may choose to
leave a trail if so desired.

**Resist Nature's Lure (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against the spell-like and supernatural abilities
of fey. This bonus also applies to spells and effects that utilize or target plants, such as
[blight](ref/spell/blight), [entangle](ref/spell/entangle), [spike growth](ref/spell/spike-growth),
and [warp wood](ref/spell/warp-wood).

**Wild Shape (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** You can _wild shape_ 4 times/day, and can choose to change into a Small, Medium,
or Large elemental (as [elemental body III](ref/spell/elemental-body-3)) or a Small, Medium, or
Large plant creature (as [plant shape II](ref/spell/plant-shape-2)).

**At 12th Level:** You can _wild shape_ 5 times/day, and can choose to change into a Small, Medium,
Large, or Huge elemental (as [elemental body IV](ref/spell/elemental-body-4)) or a Small, Medium,
Large, or Huge plant creature (as [plant shape III](ref/spell/plant-shape-3)).

**At 14th Level:** You can _wild shape_ 6 times/day.

**At 16th Level:** You can _wild shape_ 7 times/day.

**At 18th Level:** You can _wild shape_ 8 times/day.

**At 20th Level:** You can _wild shape_ at will.

**At 6th Level:** You can _wild shape_ twice a day as [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2), and
you can now change into a Large or Tiny animal or a Small elemental (as
[elemental body I](ref/spell/elemental-body-1)).

**At 8th Level:** You can _wild shape_ 3 times/day as [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3),
and you can now change into a Huge or Diminutive animal, a Small or Medium elemental (as
[elemental body II](ref/spell/elemental-body-2)), or a Small or Medium plant creature (as
[plant shape I](ref/spell/plant-shape-1)).

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Special:** You lose your ability to speak while in animal form because you are limited to the
sounds that a normal, untrained animal can make, but you can communicate normally with other animals
of the same general grouping as your new form. (The normal sound a wild parrot makes is a squawk, so
changing to this form does not permit speech.)

**Standard:** You gain the ability to turn yourself into any Small or Medium animal and back again.
Your options for new forms include all creatures with the animal type. This ability functions like
the [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1) spell, except as noted here. The effect lasts for 1
hour per druid level, or until you change back. The form chosen must be that of an animal with which
you are familiar.

**Usage:** Once a day.

**Usage:** Once a day.

**Venom Immunity (Ex)**

**Section:** Other Abilities

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** You have immunity to all poisons.

**A Thousand Faces (Su)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Standard:** You can change your appearance at will, as if using the
[alter self](ref/spell/alter-self) spell, but only while in your normal form.

**Timeless Body (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** You no longer take ability score penalties for aging and cannot be magically
[aged](ref/rule/age). Any penalties you may have already incurred, however, remain in place. Bonuses
still accrue, and you still die of old age when your time is up.

### Ex-Druids

A druid who ceases to revere nature, changes to a prohibited alignment, or teaches the Druidic
language to a non-druid loses all spells and druid abilities (including her animal companion, but
not including weapon, armor, and shield proficiencies). She cannot thereafter gain levels as a druid
until she [atones](ref/spell/atonement). (Also see the
[Planar Extremist](ref/arc-druid/planar-extremist) archetype for options.)

### Alternate Capstones

**Source** Chronicle of Legends pg. 28\
When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Home Ground (Su)**

At 20th level, the druid knows every tree, every deer, every blade of grass, and every scuttling
beetle of her home. The druid selects a specific area relevant to the campaign, such as a prominent
forest, mountain, or even a patch of sea. The druid is constantly under the effect of
[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature) with regards to that area and can change the
facts she gleans from the spell when she prepares her spells each day. In addition, while on her
home ground, the druid gains a +4 bonus to her caster level.

**Also available:** Great Beast

### Archetypes

**Source** Advanced Player's Guide pg. 72\
From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.

**Modifies or Replaces:** A Thousand Faces; Venom Immunity

**Description:** A shaman with this totem calls upon the proud lion, imposing and majestic, the
mighty leader of deadly hunters.

**Mantella**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Wild Shape; Venom Immunity; Spontaneous Casting

**Description:** (Grippli) A mantella focuses on exploring her poisonous heritage, whether or not it
has manifested in her own personal biology.

**Menhir Savant**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Sense; Wild Empathy; Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; A Thousand
Faces

**Description:** Some druids study the paths of nature's power through the nodes and ley lines that
connect standing stones and megalithic circles, learning to tap into their energies.

**Mooncaller**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Woodland Stride; Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity; A Thousand Faces

**Description:** A mooncaller is bound to the subtle influences of the ever-changing moon and its
endless cycles from light to dark and back again.

**Mountain Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity; A
Thousand Faces

**Description:** As more and more of the soft, easy lands become cultivated and civilized, many
druids look for refuge and solitude among the eternal peaks of the highest mountains.

**Naga Aspirant**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spontaneous Casting; Resist Nature's Lure; Wild Shape; Venom Immunity; A
Thousand Faces; Timeless Body

**Description:** (Nagaji Only) The naga aspirant follows the ancient beliefs and engages in the
rituals of a druidic sect dedicated to the transcendence of her nagaji form through absolute
devotion to nagas and naga gods.

**Nature Fang**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Sense; Wild Empathy; Woodland Stride; Wild Shape; Resist Nature's
Lure; Venom Immunity

**Description:** A nature fang is a druid who stalks and slays those who despoil nature, kill scarce
animals, or introduce diseases to unprotected habitats.

**Nature Priest**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Deity; Class Skills; Weapon Proficiencies; Nature Sense; Nature Bond;
Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity

**Description:** Nature priests serve two masters: their deities and nature itself, and can call
upon the power of both to fuel their divine abilities.

**Nithveil Adept**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Sense; Nature Bond; Spontaneous Casting; Resist Nature's Lure

**Description:** Nithveil adepts learn the secret magic of the First World fey in the moving,
reality-phasing city of Nithveil

**Pack Lord**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Bond; 6th-level Wild Shape

**Description:** Some druids bond with many animal companions rather than just one, achieving a
level of communion rare even in druidic circles and leading their pack brothers and pack sisters
with total authority.

**Plains Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity; A
Thousand Faces

**Description:** Out upon the wide and rolling prairies and savannahs, plains druids stand guard
over the grasslands.

**Planar Extremist**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Alignment; Spells; Nature's Bond; Spontaneous Casting; Resist Nature's
Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** (Ex-druid archetype) Planar extremists are those druids that have lost their
neutral stance and find themselves gravitating toward one of the four most extreme alignments

**Progenitor**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spontaneous Casting; Nature Bond; Wild Shape

**Description:** Progenitors are druids who tap into the vibrant power of the First World to
propagate natural and supernatural wonders.

**Reincarnated Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Woodland Stride; Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity; Timeless Body

**Description:** Spun off into the endless circle of life, an incarnate druid is an embodiment of
nature's eternal renewal.

**Restorer**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Sense; Spontaneous Casting; Resist Nature's Lure; A Thousand Faces

**Description:** Restorers carefully tend to the health of both the environment and creatures in it.

**River Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Nature Sense; Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist
Nature's Lure; Wild Shape; Timeless Body

**Description:** These druids are the guardians of rivers and the creatures that depend on them.

**Road Keeper**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spontaneous Casting; Nature Bond; Wild Empathy; Woodland Stride; Resist
Nature's Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** Guardians of the lonely paths between cities, road keepers protect the wild from
travelers, and travelers from the wild.

**Rot Warden**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spontaneous Casting; Nature Bond; Wild Empathy; Trackless Step; Venom
Immunity; Resist Nature's Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** Harbingers of decay, rot wardens live in damp lands where insects and rot break
down the dead to feed the living.

**Saurian Shaman**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Venom Immunity

**Description:** A shaman with this focus calls upon the primeval dinosaur, the archaic terror that
lingers as a hungering, atavistic stranger at the fringes of the ecosystem, a destroyer and
despoiler whose coming other animals dread.

**Season Keeper**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Bond; Trackless Step; Venom Immunity; Timeless Body; Wild Shape

**Description:** (Triaxian Only) Imbued with the primeval potency of their planet's seasons, those
druids known as season keepers guide Triaxian communities through the stark transition from summer
to winter (and vice versa).

**Season Sage**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Wild Shape

**Description:** (Gathlain) Season sages wander as the seasons change, turning the leaves and
transforming nature as they pass and taking pleasure in the transition between seasons.

**Serpent Shaman**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** A Thousand Faces; Venom Immunity

**Description:** A shaman with this totem calls upon the cunning serpent, the stealthy deceiver who
draws the weak minded in and strikes while they are unaware.

**Shark Shaman**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** A Thousand Faces; Venom Immunity

**Description:** Some druids emulate the deadly shark, a remorseless hunter that marine dwellers
dread.

**Skinshaper**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Wild Shape; A Thousand Faces

**Description:** Using the same primal energy with which other druids assume animal forms,
skinshapers can imitate some of the most dangerous beasts of all: humans and other humanoids.

**Sky Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Weapon/Armor Proficiency; Nature Bond; Woodland Stride; Resist Nature's
Lure; Trackless Step; Wild Shape; Venom Immunity; A Thousand Faces

**Description:** (Sylph Only) Some druids develop ties not to a particular landscape, but instead to
the endless blue expanse of the skies.

**Storm Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spontaneous Casting; Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's
Lure; Venom Immunity; A Thousand Faces

**Description:** While most druids focus their attention upon the rich earth and the bounty of
nature that springs forth from it, the storm druid's eyes have ever been cast to the skies and the
endless expanse of blue, channeling the most raw and untamed aspects of nature.

**Sunrider**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Weapon and Armor Proficiencies; Nature Bond; Woodland
Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** Sunriders help their allies, be they fellow tribe members or fellow adventurers, to
survive in harsh desert terrain, while simultaneously making it even harsher for their foes.

**Supernaturalist**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Weapon/Armor Proficiencies; Nature Bond; Wild Empathy; Nature Sense;
Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** Supernaturalists wholly embrace paranormal phenomena as extensions of nature.

**Survivor**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Weapon/Armor Proficiencies; Spellcasting; Nature Bond;
Resist Nature's Lure; Wild Shape; Venom Immunity

**Description:** The survivor embodies the neutral aspect of the natural world and eschews druidic
magic in favor of more pragmatic and worldly survival methods.

**Swamp Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; A Thousand Faces

**Description:** Some druids eschew pleasant glades and groves and instead seek out dank marshes,
misty bogs and heaths, and trackless swamps as the place they call home and watch over with care,
finding beauty and life in abundance in places few others would willingly enter.

**Swarm Monger**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Bond; Woodland Stride; Nature Sense; Resist Nature's Lure;
12th-level Wild Shape options

**Description:** (Ratfolk archetype) Whereas other druids commune with nature or even the spirit of
a city, swarm mongers find beauty and strength in decay, and they draw their power from fungus,
disease, and their own singular will to survive.

**Tempest Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Alignment; Weapon and Armor Proficiencies; Nature Bond; Nature Sense;
Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity

**Description:** Some druids have come to worship the Eye of Abendego as an aspect of pure elemental
fury, channeling its mysteries into their own magic

**Tempest Tamer**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Bonus Languages; Resist Nature's Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** Druids draw upon the fiercest powers of nature, and tsunamis, typhoons, and
whirlpools are among the most powerful forces in the ocean.

**Toxicologist**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spontaneous Casting; Wild Empathy; Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist
Nature's Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** Toxicologists specialize in the stealthy application of insidious poisons to their
targets, believing word of their foes' horrifying deaths will deter others from attempting similar
desecrations of the natural world.

**Treesinger**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Bond; Wild Empathy; Wild Shape

**Description:** (Elf and Vine Leshy Only) Some elves turn to the timeless growth of nature for
solace, finding allies among the great trees themselves, and even leading the forest's plants into
combat.

**Troll Fury**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Wild Empathy; Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity

**Description:** (Troll Only) Troll furies combine a druid's love of natural balance with a
single-minded devotion to the welfare of the troll tribe.

**Undine Adept**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Domains; Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Wild
Shape; Venom Immunity

**Description:** (Undine Only) An undine adept dedicates herself to preserving the knowledge of the
first undines and ensuring her people's ancient connections to the natural world remain undisturbed.

**Urban Druid**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spontaneous Casting; Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's
Lure; Venom Immunity

**Description:** While many druids keep to the wilderness, some make their way within settlements,
communing with the animals and vermin who live there and speaking for the nature that runs rampant
in civilization's very cradle.

**Urushiol**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Nature Bond; Resist Nature's Lure

**Description:** When an urushiol druid forms a bond with nature, it takes a vastly different form
than for most druids. His body becomes increasingly toxic, allowing him to secrete a deadly poison
through his pores.

**Wild Whisperer**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Woodland Stride; Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Wild Shape

**Description:** A wild whisperer is an expert at studying, predicting, and explaining animal
behavior.

**Wolf Shaman**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** A Thousand Faces; Venom Immunity

**Description:** A shaman with this totem calls upon the clever wolf, capable of roaming alone yet
wise enough to run with a pack when facing dangers too great for one alone.

**World Walker**

**Class:** druid

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trackless Step; Resist Nature's Lure; Venom Immunity; Timeless Body

**Description:** While all druids traverse the wilderness with ease, the world walkers take it upon
themselves to travel the entire world.
