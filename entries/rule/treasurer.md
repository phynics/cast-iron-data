**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 203

The Treasurer monitors the state of the kingdom's Treasury and citizens' confidence in the value of
their money and investigates whether any businesses are taking unfair advantage of the system. The
Treasurer is in charge of the tax collectors and tracks debts and credits with guilds and other
governments.

**Benefit:** Add your Intelligence modifier or Wisdom modifier to Economy.

**Vacancy Penalty:** Economy decreases by 4. The kingdom cannot collect taxes - during the Edict
phase, when you would normally collect taxes, the kingdom does not collect taxes at all and the
taxation level is considered "none."
