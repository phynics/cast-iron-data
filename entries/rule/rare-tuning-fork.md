**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 86

A rare tuning fork is one attuned to an obscure demiplane, a demiplane that is simply difficult to
travel to due to its nature, or a demiplane that is particularly small and insignificant. Planes
that require rare tuning forks include the Dead Vault, the Harrowed Realm, and Jandelay.

Attuning a rare tuning fork is done using the same method as an uncommon fork, but the fork must be
struck against an artifact created on that plane, a creature of CR 21 or higher native to the plane,
or a unique or specific feature of the plane that you determine - such features are generally well
guarded and difficult to reach, and thus they should require relatively high-level play (15th level
or above) to use. Once so struck, the fork must be allowed to absorb the plane's energies for 1
week. After this time has passed, the untuned fork is attuned to that plane.
