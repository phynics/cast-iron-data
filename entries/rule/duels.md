**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 150

There is no form of combat more civilized than the duel. Be it with steel or spells, duels are used
to settle disputes in situations where a chaotic melee would be disruptive or even illegal. Although
duels are often considered honorable, this does not necessarily make them any less deadly. Duels
often permit combatants to engage in more even fights than the fracas of the battlefield, allowing
the true skill, power, and wit of each to determine the victor.
