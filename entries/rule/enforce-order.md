Your team keeps the black market relatively safe for customers and vendors for 1 day. Enforcing
order requires a successful Influence or Labor check against the black market's access DC. Success
raises the black market's Law modifier by 2 and reduces its Danger by 10 for 24 hours. While your
agents enforce order, merchants and patrons might approach you to resolve internal disputes or
address outside concerns (such as unaligned thieves or nosy guards).
