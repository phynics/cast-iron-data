You or your team uses 1 day of downtime to browbeat a person or team with a successful Intimidate or
Influence check (DC = 15 + target's Hit Dice + target's Wisdom modifier). If you succeed, you can
coerce the foe to perform a downtime activity on your behalf, or no activity for 1 day. Whether you
succeed or fail, the target's attitude becomes unfriendly.
