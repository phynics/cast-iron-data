If you lose or dismiss your animal companion, you can spend 1 day performing a ceremony to gain a
new one. This ceremony requires 24 uninterrupted hours of prayer in the environment where the new
companion typically lives (at the GM's discretion, traveling might add to the downtime requirement
if there's no suitable environment near the settlement).
