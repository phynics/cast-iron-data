Large land vehicle\
**Squares** 2 (5 ft. by 10 ft.; 5 feet high); **Cost** 50 gp

### Defense

**AC** 9; **[Hardness](ref/umr/hardness)** 5\
**hp** 30 (14)\
**Base Save** +0

### Offense

**Maximum Speed** twice the speed of the pulling creature(s); **Acceleration** the speed of the
pulling creature(s)\
**CMB** +1; **CMD** 11\
**Ramming Damage** 1d8

### Description

These two-wheeled vehicles are often used for racing or as the platform for a single archer racing
though the battlefield at high speeds.

**Propulsion** Muscle (pulled; 2 Medium creatures or 1 Large creature)\
**Driving Check** [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) or
[Profession (driver)](ref/skill/profession-any) (in the case of creatures with animal intelligence
pulling the vehicle), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) or [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) (in the
case of intelligent creatures pulling the vehicle)\
**Forward Facing** toward the creatures pulling the vehicle\
**Driving Device** reins\
**Driving Space** the most forward square of the chariot\
**Decks** 1
