Religion can be used to recall knowledge on the subjects of gods, ecclesiastic tradition, holy
symbols, mythic history, the planes, and planar magic. See
[Recall Knowledge](ref/rule/skill-descriptions-pu) for details. Use the following DCs for certain
tasks.

|Task|Religion DC|
| --- | --- |
|Know the names of the planes|10|
|Recognize a common deity's symbol or clergy|10|
|Know common mythology and tenets|15|
|Recognize the current plane|15|
|Identify a creature's planar origin|20|
|Recognize an obscure deity's symbol or clergy|20|
