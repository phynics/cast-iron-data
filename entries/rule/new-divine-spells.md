Divine spellcasters gain new spells as follows.

**Spells Gained at a New Level:** Characters who can cast divine spells undertake a certain amount
of study between adventures. Each time such a character receives a new level of divine spells, she
learns all of the spells from that level automatically.

**Independent Research:** A divine spellcaster can also research a spell independently, much as an
arcane spellcaster can. Only the creator of such a spell can prepare and cast it, unless she decides
to share it with others.
