**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 50

**Treasury:** The PCs' militia starts with 10 gp in its treasury. The militia requires gold to
perform certain actions, to recruit or upgrade its teams, or occasionally because a special event
requires it. In many cases, gold expenditure represents the costs to upgrade or outfit individual
teams. In addition, some teams can earn gold for the militia.

It's important to keep track of the amount of gold in the militia's treasury. The PCs can add or
remove gold at the end of the [Upkeep phase](ref/rule/upkeep-phase-m).

**Minimum Treasury:** This value is the minimum amount of gold reserves needed for a militia to
support its training needs, and is equal to the militia's [rank](ref/rule/rank-mt) × 10 gp. If the
funds in the treasury drop below the minimum required amount, the militia's members may get nervous
and slack off, reducing the militia's training score.
