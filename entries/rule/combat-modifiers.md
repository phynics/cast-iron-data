**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 195

A number of factors and conditions can influence an attack roll. Many of these situations grant a
bonus or penalty on attack rolls or to a defender's Armor Class.

### Table 8-5: Attack Roll Modifiers

|Attacker is...|Melee|Ranged|
| --- | --- | --- |
|Dazzled|-1|-1|
|Entangled|-2^[1](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-1)^|-2^[1](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-1)^|
|Flanking defender|+2|-|
|Invisible|+2^[2](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-2)^|+2^[2](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-2)^|
|On higher ground|+1|+0|
|Prone|-4|-^[3](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-3)^|
|Shaken or frightened|-2|-2|
|Squeezing through a space|-4|-4|

### Table 8-6: Armor Class Modifiers

|Defender is...|Melee|Ranged|
| --- | --- | --- |
|Behind cover|+4|+4|
|Blinded|-2^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|-2^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|
|Concealed or invisible|See Concealment|See Concealment|
|Cowering|-2^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|-2^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|
|Entangled|+0^[2](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-2)^|+0^[2](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-2)^|
|Flat-footed|+0^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|+0^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|
|Grappling (but the attacker is not)|+0|+0|
|Helpless|-4^[5](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-5)^|+0^[5](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-5)^|
|Kneeling or sitting|-2|+2|
|Pinned|-4^[5](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-5)^|+0^[5](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-5)^|
|Prone|-4|+4|
|Squeezing through a space|-4|-4|
|Stunned|-2^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|-2^[4](ref/rule/combat-modifiers#footnote-4)^|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

An entangled character also takes a -4 penalty to Dexterity, which may affect his attack roll.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

The defender loses any Dexterity bonus to AC.

###### ^3^ {#footnote-3}

Most ranged weapons can't be used while the attacker is prone, but you can use a crossbow or
shuriken while prone at no penalty.

###### ^4^ {#footnote-4}

The defender loses any Dexterity bonus to AC.

###### ^5^ {#footnote-5}

The defender is denied his Dexterity bonus to his Armor Class.
