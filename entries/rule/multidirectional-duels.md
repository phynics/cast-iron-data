In unusual circumstances, a verbal duel might involve more than two independent duelists. In this
case, the rules work the same with the following modification. First, when a duelist opens an
exchange, she selects one of the other duelists and the exchange continues between the two of them.
When that exchange's winner is determined or the exchange ends, the winner must then start an
exchange with a different duelist. This goes on until only one duelist remains.
