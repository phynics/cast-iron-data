**Hell**

**Cat:** Outer Plane (lawful evil)

**Desc:** The regimented realm of Asmodeus and devilkind, Hell is formed of nine layers, each
tailored to the whim of its ruling archdevil, where the souls of evil mortals and victims of the
devils' machinations endure unending torments.

**Sub:** The Pit

### Description

Hell is not the oldest plane of the Great Beyond, yet it may well be the most notorious, for here
Asmodeus and his legions of devils have worked since the dawn of mortality to tempt, corrupt, and
lure those who have the gift (or curse) of free will into eternal damnation.

### Hell's Layers

> **Avernus**\
> Avernus, the gateway to Hell, is ruled by [Barbatos](ref/faith/barbatos), the archdevil of
> animals, corruption, and gateways.
>
> **Dis**\
> The Infernal City of Dis is the largest city in Hell. It is ruled by
> [Dispater](ref/faith/dispater), the archdevil of cities, prisons, and rulership.
>
> **Erebus**\
> Erebus is ruled by [Mammon](ref/faith/mammon), the archdevil of avarice, watchfulness, and wealth.
> These lightless vaults serve as Hell's treasury.
>
> **Phlegethon**\
> The burning reaches of Phlegethon contain immense mines and endless toil, and they are ruled by
> [Belial](ref/faith/belial), the archdevil of adultery, deception, and desire.
>
> **Stygia**\
> Flooded and festering Stygia is a poisonous realm ruled by [Geryon](ref/faith/geryon), the
> archdevil of forbidden knowledge, heresy, and snakes.
>
> **Malebolge**\
> The grimly beautiful, ash-caked landscape of Malebolge is ruled by [Moloch](ref/faith/moloch), the
> General of Hell and the archdevil of fire, obedience, and war.
>
> **Cocytus**\
> The seventh layer of Hell is a perpetually frozen landscape where nothing is as it seems. It is
> ruled by [Baalzebul](ref/faith/baalzebul), the archdevil of arrogance, flies, and lies.
>
> **Caina**\
> Called the Bones of Hell, Caina is a realm of immense spikes and chains amid a dark and dismal
> gulf ruled by [Mephistopheles](ref/faith/mephistopheles), the archdevil of contracts, devils, and
> secrets.
>
> **Nessus**\
> The deepest layer of Hell is the sole domain of Asmodeus, the Prince of Darkness.

### Exploration

Hell is famed throughout the Great Beyond for its innumerable dangers and torments. The skies above
range from gloomy and depressing on the best of days to burning red with ash and fire, or, in realms
like Erebus, entirely absent. The exact nature of the clouds and illumination varies from each layer
of Hell to the next, but there is never anything comforting or soothing in the view.

Asmodeus's throne, the Catafalque, is Hell's "heart" and the point which functions as the strongest
navigational aid for items like [astralabes](ref/magic-wondrous/astralabe). Anything in the
direction of the Catafalque is often referred to as "Pitward" by the denizens of Hell, but Material
Plane standards of north, south, east, and west are used also, in large part due to an obscure
proviso in an ancient contract that no devil has yet felt the need or had the time to seek out and
revise.
