Rewards from this type of treasure represent the kinds of items that might be found in the vault of
an entire community of creatures or in the hoard of a mighty beast such as a dragon. While this
reward can include items of any type, it focuses on coins, gems, and larger, more valuable items.
The individual items included are determined by referring to other tables in this section. The
specific table used is specified by the reward.
