**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 163

Time isn't just about holidays, calendars, and politics. On a more cosmic level, the tone of the
campaign may depend on how old or young the world is, and what this means to the people, places, and
plots that play out upon it. While the standard fantasy world assumes such things as ancient empires
and lost races as an explanation for the many dungeons, artifacts, and legends that feature in
adventures, this is merely a default. Consider these other options.
