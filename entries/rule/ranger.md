A character who chooses [ranger](ref/class/ranger) as his secondary class gains the following
secondary class features.

**Track:** At 3rd level, he gains the track class feature, treating his character level as his
effective ranger level.

**Favored Enemy:** At 7th level, he gains the 1st [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) class
feature.

**Favored Terrain:** At 11th level, he gains the 1st [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain)
class feature.

**Expert Tracker:** At 15th level, he gains the woodland stride and swift tracker class features.

**Quarry:** At 19th level, he gains the quarry class feature.
