**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 218

Wizards, sorcerers, and bards cast arcane spells. Compared to divine spells, arcane spells are more
likely to produce dramatic results.

**Spell Slots:** The various character class tables show how many spells of each level a character
can cast per day. These openings for daily spells are called spell slots. A spellcaster always has
the option to fill a higher-level spell slot with a lower-level spell. A spellcaster who lacks a
high enough ability score to cast spells that would otherwise be his due still gets the slots but
must fill them with spells of lower levels.
