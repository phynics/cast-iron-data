**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 157

At 7th level, the character chooses one physical ability score (Strength, Dexterity, or
Constitution) to gain a permanent +2 enhancement bonus. At 12th level, this bonus increases to +4.
At 13th level, the character chooses a second physical ability score to gain a permanent +2
enhancement bonus. At 16th level, the character increases one of these enhancement bonuses by 2. At
17th level, she chooses a third physical ability score to gain a permanent +2 enhancement bonus.
