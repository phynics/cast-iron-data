The Grand Diplomat is in charge of the kingdom's foreign policy - how it interacts with other
kingdoms and similar political organizations such as tribes of intelligent monsters. The Grand
Diplomat is the head of all of the kingdom's diplomats, envoys, and ambassadors. It is the Grand
Diplomat's responsibility to represent and protect the interests of the kingdom with regard to
foreign powers.

**Benefit:** Add your Charisma modifier or Intelligence modifier to Stability.

**Vacancy Penalty:** Stability decreases by 2. The kingdom cannot issue
[Diplomatic](ref/rule/diplomatic-edicts) or [Exploration](ref/rule/exploration-edicts) edicts.
