A Society check can allow you to decipher writing in an unfamiliar language or a message written in
an incomplete or archaic form. If you succeed at your check, you understand the general content of
about one page worth of writing. If you fail, you must succeed at a DC 5 Wisdom check or draw a
false conclusion about the text. Both checks are rolled by the GM in secret, so you don't know
whether the conclusion you drew is true or false.

|Unfamiliar Message|Society DC|
| --- | --- |
|Simple message|20|
|Standard text|20|
|Intricate, exotic, or very old writing|20|

**Action:** 1 minute per page.

**Try Again:** Yes.
