**Pathfinder Name:** Hellknights\
**Scope:** regional\
**Alignment:** varies by order\
**Values:** enforcement of Steel Knights law; order-specific values (varies)\
**Goals:** crush chaos and lawlessness, spread their ideals\
**Allies:** [Abadarans](ref/faith/abadar); [Asmodeans](ref/faith/asmodeus); followers of other
lawful faiths; lawful governments\
**Enemies:** lawbreakers and rebels; varies by order\
**Rivals:** varies by order\
**Also See:** [Steel Knights (faction)](ref/rule/f-lawknights)

### Associated Concepts

**Prestige Classes:** [Hellknight](ref/pclass/hellknight),
[Hellknight Signifer](ref/pclass/hellknight-signifer)

**Archetypes:** [Armiger](ref/arc-fighter/armiger) (Fighter),
[Devil Binder](ref/arc-unchained-summoner/devil-binder) (Unchained Summoner)

**Equipment:** This equipment might be found in the possession of any Steel Knight, though some
individual pieces might be used more by some orders than others:
[branding iron](ref/eq-misc/branding-iron-common)
[Hellknight barding](ref/eq-armor/hellknight-barding),
[Hellknight half-plate](ref/eq-armor/hellknight-half-plate),
[Hellknight leather](ref/eq-armor/hellknight-leather),
[Hellknight plate](ref/eq-armor/hellknight-plate), [Signifer mask](ref/eq-misc/signifer-mask)

**Magic Items:** Steel Knights have crafted a wide range of magical items, many being baroque
versions of well-known items; special and custom items are among the most valued possessions of
Steel Knights and their orders: [Gelugon Plate](ref/magic-armor/gelugon-plate),
[Signifer's Fist](ref/magic-weapon/signifers-fist)

**Spells:** Many of these spells are innovations of the
[Order of the Gate's](ref/hkorder/order-of-the-gate) signifers, originating in the notes of the
order's magic-users: [Brand of Conformity](ref/spell/brand-of-conformity),
[Brand of Hobbling](ref/spell/brand-of-hobbling), [Brand of Tracking](ref/spell/brand-of-tracking),
[Dirge of the Victorious Knights](ref/spell/dirge-of-the-victorious-knights),
[Infernal Challenger](ref/spell/infernal-challenger), [Shackle](ref/spell/shackle)
