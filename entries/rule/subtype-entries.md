The following subtypes cover most of those presented in bestiaries, with the exception of those that
either don't grant significant abilities or have such an impact on the monster that a graft can't
cover them. Those subtypes appear in the "Other Subtypes" section below.

[Aeon](ref/subtype/aeon):\*\* Gain a deflection bonus to AC equal to 1/4 CR; options-energy
resistance 10 (electricity and fire), immunity (cold, poison, and critical hits).

[Agathion](ref/subtype/agathion):\*\* +4 racial bonus on saving throws against poison, truespeech;
options-energy resistance 10 (cold and sonic), healing touch, immunity (electricity and poison).

[Air](ref/subtype/air):\*\* Fly 60 ft. (perfect); skills-gain Fly as an additional master skill.

[Angel](ref/subtype/angel):\*\* +4 racial bonus on saving throws against poison; protective aura;
truespeech; options-energy resistance 10 (electricity and fire), immunity (acid, cold, and
petrification).

[Aquatic](ref/subtype/aquatic):\*\* Swim 30 ft.; skills-gain Swim as an additional master skill.

[Archon](ref/subtype/archon):\*\* +4 racial bonus on saving throws against poison; aura of menace,
teleport; options-immunity (electricity and petrification).

[Asura](ref/subtype/asura):\*\* +2 racial bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells; elusive
aura; options-energy resistance 10 (acid and electricity), immunity (curses, disease, and poison),
regeneration (overcome by good), [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance), summon allies,
telepathy; skills-gain Escape Artist and Perception as additional master skills.

[Azata](ref/subtype/azata):\*\* Options-energy resistance 10 (cold and fire), immunity (electricity
and petrification).

[Clockwork](ref/subtype/clockwork):\*\* Increase AC and touch AC by 2, increase Reflex saving throw
bonus by 2, vulnerable to electricity; options-improved initiative.

[Cold](ref/subtype/cold):\*\* Vulnerable to fire; options-immunity (cold).

[Daemon](ref/subtype/daemon):\*\* Options-energy resistance 10 (cold, electricity, and fire),
immunity (acid, death effects, disease, and poison), summon allies, telepathy.

[Demodand](ref/subtype/demodand):\*\* Faith-stealing strike; heretical soul; options-energy
resistance 10 (cold and fire), immunity (acid and poison), summon allies.

[Demon](ref/subtype/demon):\*\* Options-energy resistance 10 (acid, cold, and fire), immunity
(electricity and poison), summon allies, telepathy.

[Devil](ref/subtype/devil):\*\* See in darkness; options-energy resistance 10 (acid and cold),
immunity (fire and poison), summon allies, telepathy.

[Div](ref/subtype/div):\*\* See in darkness; options-energy resistance 10 (acid and electricity),
immunity (fire and poison), summon allies, telepathy.

[Dwarf](ref/subtype/dwarf):\*\* [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.; +2 racial bonus on saving
throws against poison, spells, and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

[Earth](ref/subtype/earth):\*\* Burrow 20 ft., [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) (range varies).

[Elemental](ref/subtype/elemental):\*\* Options-immunity (bleed, critical hits, flanking, paralysis,
poison, sleep effects, and stunning).

[Elf](ref/subtype/elf):\*\* [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision); skills-gain Perception or
Spellcraft as an additional master skill; options-immunity (sleep).

[Fire](ref/subtype/fire):\*\* Vulnerable to cold; options-immunity (fire).

[Giant](ref/subtype/giant):\*\* [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision); skills-gain
Intimidate as additional good skill.

[Gnome](ref/subtype/gnome):\*\* Gain the Small graft, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision),
gain one CR-appropriate spell from the illusion spell list.

[Goblinoid](ref/subtype/goblinoid):\*\* Skills-gain Stealth as an additional good skill.

[Half-Elf](ref/race/half-elf):\*\* [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision); options-immunity
(sleep); skills-gain one additional master skill.

[Half-Orc](ref/race/half-orc):\*\* [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft.; skill-gain Intimidate
as an additional good skill.

[Halfling](ref/subtype/halfling):\*\* Gain the Small graft, +2 racial bonus on saving throws against
fear.

[Human](ref/subtype/human):\*\* Options-gain one additional combat or social option.

[Incorporeal](ref/subtype/incorporeal):\*\* Set touch AC to same value as AC; options-immunity
(critical hits and precision-based damage from weapons without
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon)), incorporeal.

[Inevitable](ref/subtype/inevitable):\*\* [Low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision);
options-regeneration (overcome by chaotic).

[Kami](ref/subtype/kami):\*\* Merge with ward, ward; Options-energy resistance 10 (acid,
electricity, and fire), [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing), immunity (bleed, mind-affecting
effects, petrification, and polymorph effects), telepathy.

[Leshy](ref/subtype/leshy):\*\* Constant pass without trace; plantspeech; verdant burst;
options-change shape, immunity (electricity and sonic).

[Nightshade](ref/subtype/nightshade):\*\* Desecrating aura, darksense,
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), light aversion; options-channel energy (negative
energy).

[Oni](ref/subtype/oni):\*\* Options-change shape; regeneration (typically overcome by acid or fire).

[Orc](ref/subtype/orc):\*\* [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft., light sensitivity.

[Protean](ref/subtype/protean):\*\* [Blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) (range varies),
amorphous anatomy, constant [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement); options-change
shape, constrict, energy resistance 10 (electricity and sonic), immunity (acid), improved combat
maneuver (grab).

[Psychopomp](ref/subtype/psychopomp):\*\* Spiritsense, spirit touch; options-damage reduction
(5/adamantine), energy resistance 10 (cold and electricity), immunity (death effects, disease, and
poison).

[Qlippoth](ref/subtype/qlippoth):\*\* Horrific appearance; options-energy resistance 10 (acid,
electricity, and fire), immunity (cold, mind-affecting effects, and poison), telepathy.

[Rakshasa](ref/subtype/rakshasa):\*\* Detect thoughts, enhanced defenses; options-change shape.

[Shapechanger](ref/subtype/shapechanger):\*\* Options-change shape.

[Swarm](ref/subtype/swarm):\*\* Takes an additional 50% damage from spells or effects that affect an
area; swarm attack; if made of Tiny creatures, the monster takes half damage from slashing and
piercing weapons; if made of Fine or Diminutive creatures, the monster is immune to weapon damage;
options-distraction, immunity (critical hits, flanking, trip, grapple, bull rush, and spells that
target a specific number of creatures).

[Water](ref/subtype/water):\*\* Swim 30 ft.; skills-gain Swim as an additional master skill.

monsters, and are listed below. A monster gains all subtypes appropriate to it, even those without
rules in this section, such as the following.

### Complex Subtypes

The subtypes listed below don't appear because they're so complex that adding a subtype graft isn't
enough. Creatures of these subtypes need to be created carefully, and have detailed rules associated
with them that go beyond the scope of simple monster creation.

- [Behemoth](ref/subtype/behemoth)
- [Colossus](ref/subtype/colossus)
- [Great Old One](ref/subtype/great-old-one)
- [Kaiju](ref/subtype/kaiju)
- [Mythic](ref/subtype/mythic)
