**Grabbing Appendages (6 RP):** **Prerequisites:** Any type except humanoid, Large size;
**Benefit:** Members of this race have a small group of appendages that are useful for little more
than to aid in grappling. Members of this race gain [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple) as
a bonus feat, and can maintain a grapple and still make attacks with their main appendages.
