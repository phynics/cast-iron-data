**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 50

**Militia Actions:** Every week, the militia can take actions like drilling and sabotaging foes. Its
maximum number of actions per week depends on its rank (see
[Table 6-1](ref/rule/militia-terminology)). This number can be increased by bonuses from officers,
unique allies, and certain events, so track it carefully.

**Available Actions:** As the PCs' militia progresses and it adds and upgrades teams, it can access
more actions. See the [Activity Phase](ref/rule/activity-phase-m) section on for a complete list of
actions and which teams are needed to take those actions.
