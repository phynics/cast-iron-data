If your campaign uses alternate racial traits, you can retrain a racial trait. This takes 20
non-consecutive days and requires a trainer with the racial trait you want. The replacement trait
must be an appropriate one from your racial list. The old and new racial traits must replace the
same standard racial trait. For example, the
[magic resistant and stubborn alternate dwarven traits replace the hardy standard trait](ref/race/dwarf),
so you can retrain one of those for the other.
