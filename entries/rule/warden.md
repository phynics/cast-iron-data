The Warden is responsible for enforcing laws in larger settlements, as well as ensuring the safety
of the kingdom leaders. The Warden also works with the General to deploy forces to protect
settlements and react to internal threats.

**Benefit:** Add your Constitution modifier or Strength modifier to Loyalty.

**Vacancy Penalty:** Loyalty and Stability decrease by 2.
