**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 55

**Guarantee Event** **(No Team Required):** By increasing its [Notoriety](ref/rule/notoriety-mt)
score by 1d6 and spending an amount of gold equal to the militia's minimum
[treasury](ref/rule/treasury-mt) (representing a lowering of its guard), the PCs can guarantee that
an event occurs during the [Event phase](ref/rule/event-phase-m). If the militia performs this
action, the GM rolls twice and the PCs can choose which event they would like to happen.
