**Sources:** Occult Mysteries pg 41

Cards in the suit of keys are tied to Dexterity.

**The Dance (LG; 1 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target gains the benefits of [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement)
> for 1 round.\
> **Seed** A challenge or guardian must be overcome through grace, not force.

**The Cricket (NG; 2 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target's speed increases by 30 feet for 1 round\
> **Seed** A [grig](ref/monster/grig) must dance, but cannot dance alone.

**The Juggler (CG; 3 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target gains the [Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows) feat for the rest of the
> combat.\
> **Seed** Giants attempt to crush the PCs from afar.

**The Locksmith (LN; 4 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target gains a +10 bonus on a single Disable Device check.\
> **Seed** Thieves come for the PCs' treasures in the night.

**The Peacock (N; 5 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target is [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for 1 round.\
> **Seed** PCs must rescue a [petrified](ref/rule/petrified) victim.

**The Rabbit Prince (CN; 6 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target takes a -5 penalty to its AC, but gains a +5 bonus on melee attack rolls for
> the remainder of combat.\
> **Seed** A daring bravado challenges the PCs in an effort to build her reputation.

**The Avalanche (LE; 7 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target enters a [rage](ref/spell/rage), as the spell, for 3 rounds.\
> **Seed** A [rockslide](ref/rule/avalanches) threatens the PCs, a village, or an ally.

**The Crows (NE; 8 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target gains [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) +3d6 for 1 round.\
> **Seed** Thieves plot to loot the PCs.

**The Demon's Lantern (CE; 9 on 1d10)**

> **Plot Twist** Target takes a -10 penalty on Survival and Perception checks to notice hazards for
> the next 24 hours.\
> **Seed** A false lure guides the PCs into danger.
