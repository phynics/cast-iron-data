Combat is cyclical; everybody acts in turn in a regular cycle of rounds. Combat follows this
sequence:

1. When combat begins, all combatants roll initiative.
1. Determine which characters are aware of their opponents. These characters can act during a
  surprise round. If all the characters are aware of their opponents, proceed with normal rounds.
  See the surprise section for more information.
1. After the surprise round (if any), all combatants are ready to being the first normal round of
  combat.
1. Combatants act in initiative order (highest to lowest).
1. When everyone has had a turn, the next round begins with the combatant with the highest
  initiative, and steps 4 and 5 repeat until combat ends.
