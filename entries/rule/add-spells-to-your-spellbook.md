**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 84

If you're a magus or wizard, you can spend 1 day of downtime to copy up to eight spells from other
sources into your spellbook (see
[Spells Copied from Another's Spellbook or a Scroll](ref/rule/adding-spells-to-a-wizards-spellbook)).
If you're an alchemist, you can use this option to add new formulae to your formula book. If you're
a witch, you can use this option to add spells to your familiar. You may spend Magic toward the cost
of copying spells.
