**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 565

A creature that is taking bleed damage takes the listed amount of damage at the beginning of its
turn. Bleeding can be stopped by a DC 15 Heal check or through the application of any spell that
cures hit point damage (even if the bleed is ability damage). Some bleed effects cause
[ability damage or even ability drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain). Bleed effects do not stack
with each other unless they deal different kinds of damage. When two or more bleed effects deal the
same kind of damage, take the worse effect. In this case, ability drain is worse than ability
damage.
