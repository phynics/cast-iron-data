Many taverns, and certainly most inns, offer food to their customers. As with drinks, menus
typically consist of local fare, although more upscale restaurants, particularly those in urban
settings, may offer food from farther away, ranging from mildly unusual dishes from the barony a few
leagues away to exotic dishes from the farthest reaches of the globe. See items marked "food and
drink" for different types of common meals and their prices.

Offering exotic or bizarre food on a menu is another great way to make a tavern or inn more
memorable. The players will quickly forget the tavern that just serves mutton, but they will
probably remember the inn that serves poached drake eggs in firebrandy sauce.
