**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 62

This use of Performance functions as the [Disguise core skill](ref/skill/disguise).

**Action:** 1d3 × 10 minutes. Using magic (such as disguise self) reduces this to the time required
to cast the spell or trigger the effect.

**Try Again:** Yes, though if others know a disguise was attempted, they'll be more suspicious.
