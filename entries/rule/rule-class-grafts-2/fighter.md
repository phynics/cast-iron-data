Fighters excel in battle. Their training makes them the undisputed masters of arms and armor, and
their grit and determination see them through almost any conflict.

**Required Array:** Combatant.

**Statistics Adjustments:** Increase Fortitude and Reflex saving throw bonuses by 1; option-one
combat.

**CR 1:** Options-one combat.

**CR 3:** Options-two combat.

**CR 5:** Options-three combat.

**CR 7:** Options-four combat.

**CR 11:** Options-five combat.

**CR 15:** Options-six combat.

**Suggested Ability Modifiers:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution.

**Suggested Options:** Accuracy, combat reflexes, extra armor, extra attack, improved combat
maneuver, extra armor, power attack.
