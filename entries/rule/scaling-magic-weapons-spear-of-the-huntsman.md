**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 163

**Slot** none; CL 6th; **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Aura** moderate divination; **Price** 2,650 gp; **Scaling** prize

### Description

This +1 spear of jet-black wood has a head of polished amber. The spear's wielder can use
[know direction](ref/spell/know-direction) at will.

**8th Level:** With the spear in hand, the wielder gains a +5 competence bonus on Survival checks.

**10th Level:** A wielder with the [quarry class feature](ref/class/ranger) can use the spear to use
[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature) once per day to find his quarry. If his quarry is
within range, the amber spearhead glows brightly when pointed in the proper direction.

**12th Level:** The spear gains the [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) weapon special ability.

### Construction Requirements

[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants),
[know direction](ref/spell/know-direction), _[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature),_
[telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Cost** 10,380 gp
