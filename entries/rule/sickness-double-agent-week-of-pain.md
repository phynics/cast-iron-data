**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 58

**Sickness:** One team at random becomes [disabled](ref/rule/team-conditions). _Twice:_ That team is
instead lost unless the militia succeeds at a DC 20 Loyalty check.

**Double Agent\*:** Someone in the militia is betraying the PCs. The militia cannot take the
[Secure Cache](ref/rule/secure-cache) action in the next Activity phase. The militia takes a -2
penalty on Secrecy checks. _Twice:_ This event becomes persistent.

**Week of Pain:** For the next week, the militia takes a -1 penalty on all
[organization checks](ref/rule/organization-checks-mt), and its [training](ref/rule/training-mt)
score decreases by double the normal amount during the next week's Upkeep phase. _Twice:_ There is
no additional effect.
