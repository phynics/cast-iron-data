**Pathfinder Name:** Aldori Swordlords\
**Scope:** national\
**Alignment:** N\
**Values:** constant honing of martial skills, defense of swordmaster traditions, duels and
honorable combat, preservation of swordmaster traditions and secrets\
**Goals:** establish the swordmaster dueling style as supreme among all forms of swordplay, maintain
independence from national rule, protect and defend the interests of their local area and the
surrounding holdings\
**Allies:** surrounding country, neighboring nations\
**Enemies:** bandits and brigands, nationalists, those who would spread the secrets of the
swordmasters indiscriminately and without care to the traditions of the technique\
**Rivals:** none
