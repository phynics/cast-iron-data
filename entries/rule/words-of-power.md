**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 160

Most spellcasters spend their entire careers learning and mastering the spells that make up their
calling, but there is another way. Some turn their backs on the rote memorization and formulae that
traditional spellcasters use to create magic, and instead unlock the power behind the spells
themselves, the fundamental building blocks of magic. Such a spellcaster learns the words of power,
and through them, learns to control the very forces underlying magic, shaping and wielding them like
no other.

While they function in much the same way as every other spellcaster, words of power spellcasters (or
wordcasters, as they tend to call themselves) have a great deal of flexibility in how they prepare
and cast their spells, which they call wordspells. Each wordcaster learns a number of words of power
and, with some restrictions, can combine these words to create any effect he can dream up.
