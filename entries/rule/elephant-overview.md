Rules for how feat prerequisites and feat types function remain unchanged from the _Pathfinder RPG
Core Rulebook._

_You have toggled *Elephant*-related information off on the settings page. You will need to toggle
it back on to see the changes in the feats linked below._

### Altered Feats

These feats have been adjusted so that they work very differently from the original feats.

|Altered Feat|Description|
| --- | --- |
|Agile Combatant|Formerly titled [Agile Maneuvers](ref/feat/agile-maneuvers). Includes the hamstring combat maneuver and acknowledges the changes to Weapon Finesse.|
|[Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard)|Combines the effects of Catch Off-Guard and Throw Anything.|
|[Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows)|Characters with Shield Focus may take this feat to deflect arrows with their shield.|
|[Dodge](ref/feat/dodge)|Combines the effects of Dodge and Mobility.|
|[Greater Shield Focus](ref/feat/greater-shield-focus)|The AC bonus granted by Shield Focus now affects ranged touch attacks (including rays and similar magical effects).|
|[Greater Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/greater-two-weapon-fighting)|Combines the effects of Improved Two-Weapon Fighting and Greater Two-Weapon Fighting.|
|[Improvised Weapon Mastery](ref/feat/improvised-weapon-mastery)|Solely increases the damage dealt by improvised weapons, as Catch Off-Guard already removes all penalties to attack.|
|[Natural Spell](ref/feat/natural-spell)|Now works with the wizard's change shape class feature.|
|[Rapid Reload](ref/feat/rapid-reload)|Slings and sling staves may now be reloaded as a free action.|
|[Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus)|Grants an additional +4 bonus to AC against ranged attacks.|
|[Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting)|Verbiage changed to reflect the changes to Weapon Finesse.|
