While not strictly necessary, adding additional elements to your map can make it more attractive.
Details such as furniture (tables, chairs, beds, and chests are all good examples), light sources
(fireplaces or firepits, windows, skylights, and so on), and architectural features add a touch of
realism to inhabited dungeons. Be careful not to add too many extra details to the map, however -
not only can they clutter your map and obscure important information, but they can have a
detrimental effect on combat as well. Remember that characters and monsters need room to fight and
maneuver, and a room filled with superfluous furniture can make combat overly complex.
