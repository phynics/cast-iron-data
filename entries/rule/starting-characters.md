This section details the part of a Pathfinder game the Game Master does not directly control,
discussing the elements of an adventuring party, players' rights and responsibilities, ways to
involve newcomers and re-engage veterans, and suggestions for how to work with gamers of all types
toward the creation of more exciting stories and more enjoyable games.
