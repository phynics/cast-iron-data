**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 56

**Recruit Team** **(No Team Required):** The militia can attempt to recruit a new team, as long as
it does not already support the maximum number of [teams](ref/rule/teams-m) allowed (discounting
bonus teams). The DC and specific organization check required to recruit the team depend on the type
of team being recruited. Rolling a natural 1 on the organization check does not mean automatic
failure, but the militia's [Notoriety](ref/rule/notoriety-mt) score increases by 1d6.
