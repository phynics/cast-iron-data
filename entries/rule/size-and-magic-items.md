**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 459

When an article of magic clothing or jewelry is discovered, most of the time size shouldn't be an
issue. Many magic garments are made to be easily adjustable, or they adjust themselves magically to
the wearer. Size should not keep characters of various kinds from using magic items. There may be
rare exceptions, especially with race-specific items.

**Armor and Weapon Sizes:** Armor and weapons that are found at random have a 30% chance of being
Small (01-30), a 60% chance of being Medium (31-90), and a 10% chance of being any other size
(91-100).
