**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 182

Most of the common actions characters take, aside from movement, fall into the realm of standard
actions.

|Standard Action|Attack of Opportunity^[1](ref/rule/standard-actions#footnote-1)^|
| --- | --- |
|Attack (melee)|No|
|Attack (ranged)|Yes|
|Attack (unarmed)|Yes|
|Activate a magic item other than a potion or oil|No|
|Aid another|Maybe^[2](ref/rule/standard-actions#footnote-2)^|
|Cast a spell (1 standard action casting time)|Yes|
|Channel energy|No|
|Concentration to maintain an active spell|No|
|Dismiss a spell|No|
|Draw a hidden weapon (see [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand))|No|
|Drink a potion or apply an oil|Yes|
|Escape a grapple|No|
|Feint|No|
|Light a torch with a tindertwig|Yes|
|Lower [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance)|No|
|Read a scroll|Yes|
|Ready (triggers a standard action)|No|
|Stabilize a dying friend (see [Heal](ref/skill/heal))|Yes|
|Total defense|No|
|Use extraordinary ability|No|
|Use skill that takes 1 action|Usually|
|Use [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)|Yes|
|Use supernatural ability|No|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

Regardless of the action, if you move out of a threatened square, you usually provoke an attack of
opportunity. This column indicates whether the action itself, not moving, provokes an attack of
opportunity.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

If you aid someone performing an action that would normally provoke an attack of opportunity, then
the act of aiding another provokes an attack of opportunity as well.
