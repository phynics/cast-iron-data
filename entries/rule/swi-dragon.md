**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 168

**Slot** body; CL 12th; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **Price** 38,000 gp; **Scaling** wonder

### Description

Embroidered dragons of gold thread adorn this red silk robe. The wearer gains a +3 enhancement bonus
to natural armor and fire resistance 10. Once per day, the wearer can gain the benefit of
[darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision), as the spell. A typical dragon robe is attuned to red dragons,
though variants attuned to other types of dragons exist.

**14th Level:** The wearer can use [dragon's breath](ref/spell/dragons-breath) (red dragon) once per
day. The save DC is equal to 13 + the wearer's Constitution modifier, and
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) does not apply.

**16th Level:** The wearer's enhancement bonus to natural armor increases to +4, and his fire
resistance increases to 20.

**18th Level:** The wearer can use _[dragon's breath](ref/spell/dragons-breath)_ three times per
day. The wearer can also use [form of the dragon III](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-3) once per day,
with a save DC equal to 22 + the wearer's Constitution modifier.

**20th Level:** The wearer's enhancement bonus to natural armor increases to +5 and his fire
resistance to 30. In addition, any _[dragon's breath](ref/spell/dragons-breath)_ effect created by
this item is [maximized](ref/feat/maximize-spell).
