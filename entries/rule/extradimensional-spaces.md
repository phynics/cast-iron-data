**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 501

A number of spells and magic items utilize extradimensional spaces, such as
[rope trick](ref/spell/rope-trick), a [bag of holding](ref/magic-wondrous/bag-of-holding), a
[handy haversack](ref/magic-wondrous/handy-haversack), and a
[portable hole](ref/magic-wondrous/portable-hole). These spells and magic items create a tiny pocket
space that does not exist in any dimension. Such items do not function, however, inside another
extradimensional space. If placed inside such a space, they cease to function until removed from the
extradimensional space. For example, if a bag of holding is brought into a rope trick, the contents
of the bag of holding become inaccessible until the bag of holding is taken outside the rope trick.
The only exception to this is when a bag of holding and a portable hole interact, forming a rift to
the [Astral Plane](ref/rule/astral-plane), as noted in their descriptions.
