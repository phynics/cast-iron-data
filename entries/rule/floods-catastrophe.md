**Sources:** Pathfinder \#30: The Twice-Damned Prince pg 60\
Floods occur when the water levels in an area rise beyond their usual levels. This might be caused
by river banks overflowing into a town, a sudden and unexpected rush of water from a broken
reservoir, the effects of a powerful storm, or a similar incident. GMs contemplating a flood in
their games should consider the basic rules for [floods](ref/rule/floods), as well as those for
[aquatic terrain](ref/rule/aquatic-terrain), [underwater combat](ref/rule/underwater-combat),
[swimming](ref/skill/swim), and [drowning](ref/rule/drowning).
