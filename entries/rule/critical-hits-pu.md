When you threaten a critical hit, roll to confirm at your full bonus and apply the effects of the
critical hit to any one of your hits. If your original attack roll scored multiple hits and the
critical confirmation roll also falls within your weapon's critical threat range, you score two
critical hits and can apply them to any two hits.
