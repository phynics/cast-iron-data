**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 94

Spellblights are rare and unusual magical conditions that uniquely affect spellcasters, including
creatures that use [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Spellblights are curses,
some functioning continuously and others manifesting only when the afflicted creature attempts to
cast a spell or use a spell-like ability. A creature that lacks the ability to cast spells or use
spell-like abilities cannot usually be afflicted by a spellblight.

Unlike many magical effects, a spellblight usually persists in an antimagic field, though because
they often affect spellcasting, their effect is typically lessened in such a field.
