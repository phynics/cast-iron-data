**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 19

Your campaign need not have an overarching storyline, but having one (or more) continuing plots can
help tie your adventures together into a continuous narrative, and inspire new ones.

Don't overdo this element. Your goal isn't to script your campaign, but rather to explain how and
why things happen. Keep the story general, with an eye toward details your characters can notice and
perhaps change through their actions. Choose something that can unfold slowly so that the story can
move along even when your player characters aren't actively involved in it. Consider how the player
characters might shape or redirect the story, but also establish what happens if they don't get
involved.
