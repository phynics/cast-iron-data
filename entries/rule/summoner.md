A character who chooses [summoner](ref/class/summoner) as his secondary class gains the following
secondary class features.

**Summon Monster:** At 3rd level, he gains the summoner's [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as a summoner of his character level - 2. He can
use this ability once per day; the casting time is 1 full round, and the duration is 1 round per
effective summoner level.

**Eidolon:** At 7th level, he gains the ability to summon an eidolon, using the statistics of an
eidolon for a summoner of his character level - 4, except with half as many evolution points. This
eidolon can only be summoned using his _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Additional Summons:** At 11th level, he can use his _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) three times per day.

**Shield Ally:** At 15th level, he gains the shield ally class feature.

**Aspect:** At 19th level, he gains the aspect class feature, except he can divert only 1 evolution
point from his eidolon to himself.
