The following section details spellblights, split up based on their severity and featuring
descriptions of effects and special methods to end those effects. Note that some of the effects of
spellblights can be somewhat beneficial. Crafty spellcasters can make the best of even the worst
situations, and the beneficial effects of any spellblights represent this. If a spellblight calls
for a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check based on the level of the effect, and the caster
is using a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) that doesn't have an effective level,
use half the caster level of the spell-like ability instead.
