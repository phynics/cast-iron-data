An enemy that is reduced to 0 hit points either flees or disbands - it's generally not completely
wiped out. Defeating an enemy in this manner gives you XP and allows your caravan to continue on its
journey - successful combats do not reduce the total miles a caravan can travel in a day. Some foes
defeated in this manner may even have treasure or supplies your caravan can take.
