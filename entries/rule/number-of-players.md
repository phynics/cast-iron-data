Before a game ever begins it's up to the GM to decide how many players to invite to play. While
there is no firm minimum or maximum limit to how many players a game can accommodate, this choice is
largely a matter of how many players the GM expects he can comfortably manage and what limitations a
game's expected play space present. Although most published adventures present challenges for
parties with four characters, any encounter can easily be customized to suit the needs of any size
group.
