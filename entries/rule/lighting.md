Lighting is like music: it's easy to overdo it. But a single candle or dimmer switch can get people
to focus, or at least get their attention off their phone or laptop and onto the gaming table. The
main thing lighting can do is help minimize distractions. A single spotlight on the center of the GM
screen and battlemat usually does very well, if you can set up a table lamp to do so while still
allowing enough light for people to see their character sheets.
