**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder

You are skilled at shielding yourself from flames and magical effects.\
**Prerequisites:** [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus),
[Shield Proficiency](ref/feat/shield-proficiency), base attack bonus +6.\
**Benefit:** You gain the benefits of the rogue's [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) class ability as
long as you have a heavy shield or [tower shield](ref/eq-armor/tower-shield) in one hand. Unlike
evasion, you do not need to be wearing light or medium armor to benefit from this effect.
