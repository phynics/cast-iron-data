Performing a minor feat of legerdemain or palming a coin-sized, unattended object requires a
successful DC 10 Finesse check. If you're under close observation, the observer notices your action
if it succeeds at an opposed Perception check against your Finesse check.

**Action:** Standard, or move at a -20 penalty.

**Try Again:** Yes, but a second attempt against the same target increases the DC by 10.
