Experiences in childhood have a monumental impact on the person you become later in life. Family,
social class, region, family trade or profession, religion, culture, and major events that occur
during your life have a formative influence on your character development and the worldview that you
adopt as an adult. As you think about your early life, consider the following questions and imagine
your roots in a time long before you gained the knack for the character class you have chosen -
after all, almost nobody starts life with their future profession already laid out for them. This
information will influence your choice of skills, traits, story feats, and penalties, and help to
ground you in the campaign world.
