When you use logic, you present facts, figures, and expert testimony. While logic can still be used
to mislead your adversary or the audience, unlike most other tactics, it still requires a strong
understanding of the subject matter to do so.

**Associated Skills:** Knowledge (any pertinent); occasionally, other skills will apply instead,
such as Appraise (for a verbal duel involving barter or haggling) or Profession (for a verbal duel
involving knowledge or practice of that profession's skill set, such as Profession \[barrister\]
during a trial).

**Interaction:** You gain a +2 bonus on the associated skill check when you use logic as an opener.
You take a -2 penalty on the associated skill check when you use logic to counter baiting, emotional
appeal, mockery, red herring, or wit.

**Special:** When you win an exchange with logic, you gain 1 edge that you can only use with logic.
