As explained in the Buildings section, the Settlement entry for a building lists
[modifiers that affect skill checks in the settlement](ref/rule/settlement-modifiers). If the GM
wants these modifiers to influence the kingdom as a whole, add up the Settlement modifiers for all
settlements in your kingdom, divide them by 10, and apply the following adjustments according to
your kingdom's alignment: Chaotic: +1 Crime; Evil: +1 Corruption; Good: +1 Society; Lawful +1 Law;
Neutral: +1 Lore (apply this twice if the kingdom's alignment is simply Neutral, not Chaotic Neutral
or Lawful Neutral). Use these total modifiers everywhere in your kingdom. If a settlement has its
own settlement modifier, use the higher of the two modifiers for rolls relating to that settlement.
