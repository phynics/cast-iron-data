You can trade 3 points of Goods, Labor, Influence, or Magic for 1 point of Goods, Labor, or
Influence. Under certain circumstances, the GM may allow you to trade these resources at a 2-for-1
rate rather than the normal 3-for-1. You can trade 5 points of Goods, Labor, or Influence for 1
point of Magic.
