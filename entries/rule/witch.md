A character who chooses [witch](ref/class/witch) as her secondary class gains the following
secondary class features.

**Patron:** At 1st level, she chooses a patron. She never qualifies for the
[Extra Hex](ref/feat/extra-hex) feat.

**Witch's Familiar:** At 3rd level, she gains a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar), treating her
character level as her effective witch level.

**Hex:** At 7th level, she gains a hex of her choice as a 1st-level witch. If she chooses the
slumber hex, it can affect only creatures with Hit Dice less than or equal to her character level.

**Cantrip:** At 11th level, if she has an Intelligence score of 10 or higher, she chooses a witch
cantrip and can cast that cantrip as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) at will.
She uses her character level as the caster level and Intelligence as the cantrip's key ability
score.

**Improved Hex:** At 15th level, she gains a second hex as a 1st-level witch, and her first hex
advances as if she were an 8th-level witch.

**Major Hex:** At 19th level, she gains a major hex as a 10th-level witch. She cannot gain the ice
tomb hex.
