**Slot** neck; CL 4th; **Weight** -\
**Aura** faint evocation; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Scaling** prize

### Description

This small pendant of brass and glass is crafted in the shape of a lantern and continuously sheds
light as a [light](ref/spell/light) spell. As a move action, the wearer can open the miniature
lantern pendant and direct the tiny phantasmal fire within to float to any point within 110 feet.
This functions as [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights), but the wearer can create a number of
lights equal to 1/2 his character level, up to a maximum of four, and can create only torch-like
lights, not glowing spheres or humanoid shapes. While the
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ are active, the light of the lantern pendant itself is
extinguished.

**6th Level:** Once per day, the wearer can command the phantasmal fire to fly forth and limn all
creatures in an area as [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire). Just as when using
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights),_ the pendant's light is extinguished throughout the
duration.

**8th Level:** The wearer can choose between _[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire)_ and
[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust) (Will DC 13) when he uses the 6th-level ability.

**10th Level:** Once per day, the wearer can focus the lantern's power, causing its light to
increase from the _[light](ref/spell/light)_ spell to the [daylight](ref/spell/daylight) spell, with
a duration as _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)._ The _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ is suppressed
whenever he activates any other ability that extinguishes the pendant's light, but such time still
counts against its duration.

**13th Level:** Once per day, the wearer can release the lantern's lights in the form of
[wandering star motes](ref/spell/wandering-star-motes) (Will DC 16). During the duration, the
pendant's light is extinguished.

### Construction Requirements

[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights),
_daylight, faerie fire,_ [light](ref/spell/light),
_[wandering star motes](ref/spell/wandering-star-motes);_ **Cost** 10,500 gp
