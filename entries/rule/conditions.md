If more than one condition affects a character, apply them all. If effects can't combine, apply the
most severe effect.

> **Awareness Conditions** include Blinded, Confused, Dazed, Dazzled, Deafened, Fascinated,
> Helpless, Staggered, Stunned, and Unconscious.
>
> **Dying Conditions** comprise Disabled, Dying, Stable, and Dead.
>
> The **Exhaustion Conditions** are Fatigued and Exhausted.
>
> **Fear Conditions** include Shaken, Frightened, Panicked, and Cowering.
>
> **Grapple Conditions** are Grappled and Pinned.
>
> The **Sickened Conditions** are Sickened and Nauseated.

_Note: The condition groups above are not official terms from the rules. They were created for this
app in order to better present the underlying information._
