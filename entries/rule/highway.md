A highway is a paved and well-maintained version of a Road. You may upgrade a Road into a Highway.
You must have a kingdom of Size 26 or greater to build a Highway.

**Terrain:** Any hex with a Road.

**Effect:** Economy +1 for every 4 hexes of Highway, Stability +1 for every 8 hexes of Highway;
improves [overland travel speed](ref/rule/overland-movement).

**Cost:** Twice the cost of a Road (see
[Table 4-6: Terrain and Terrain Improvements](ref/rule/terrain-improvements)).
