If a ship has no pilot, another creature can take control of the ship as long as the creature is
adjacent to the ship's control device and makes a [sailing check](ref/rule/sailing-check) as a free
action. The ship's pilot can always give over control to another adjacent creature as a free action.
If a creature wants to take control of a ship from another forcefully, it must kill the pilot or
otherwise remove the pilot from the control device. When a new creature becomes the pilot, the ship
moves on the new pilot's turn, but not on the new pilot's first turn after taking control of the
ship.
