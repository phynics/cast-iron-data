The wilderness can be a dangerous place. Monsters roam and hunt within their territories, barbarians
protect their settlements with frightening force, and sudden shifts in the weather can overwhelm the
unprepared or unlucky explorer. But sometimes the landscape itself presents dangers that dwarf all
others. The following are just a few ways that the environment can challenge hapless adventurers in
the wild.
