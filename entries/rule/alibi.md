With a successful DC 15 Bluff or Influence check, you or a team can spend 1 day of downtime to
interfere with a current or future investigation. This applies a +2 bonus to any Bluff check to
convince others you were uninvolved in a specific event of your choice that happened in the past 48
hours or will happen within 24 hours. You can spend 1 point of Influence to increase this bonus to
+5.
