A duelist and any of her allies who have at least 10 minutes to interact with the crowd before a
verbal duel begins can attempt a check to determine an [audience bias](ref/rule/audience-biases).
Succeeding at a DC 15 Sense Motive check allows a duelist or one of her allies to learn one of the
crowd's biases. Sometimes assessing an audience can have a higher DC if the GM feels the crowd is
particularly tight-lipped or their biases are otherwise obscured. Once a character attempts a Sense
Motive check to assess an audience's biases, she can't retry that check, even if she has more time
to study the audience.
