**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder

You can stop enemies in their tracks.\
**Prerequisites:** [Powerful Maneuvers](ref/rule/powerful-maneuvers), base attack bonus +6, Str 13.\
**Benefit:** You receive a +2 bonus on checks made to [hamstring](ref/rule/combat-errata) a foe.
This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by Powerful Maneuvers. When you successfully hamstring an
opponent using an attack of opportunity, that opponent cannot move for the rest of his turn.
