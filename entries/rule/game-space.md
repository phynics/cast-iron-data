Since playing Pathfinder can take up a considerable area, the size of which is often limited by any
number of practical constraints, game space can be one of the most difficult environmental factors
to influence. If the GM has a choice, though, she can seek a quiet place where interruptions will be
few - traffic and background noise can negatively impact the atmosphere being created. If she has to
share a space, it might help for the GM to tell non-players that she's running a game and would
appreciate not being interrupted, or she might schedule a time when disturbances will be limited.
While a GM might consider running the game outside or in creepy surroundings like a crypt or cabin
in the woods, keep in mind that such a venue could be distracting in itself and is probably more
trouble than it's worth.
