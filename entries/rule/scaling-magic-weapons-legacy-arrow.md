**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 163

**Slot** none; CL 4th; **Weight** 1/10 lb.\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **Price** 305 gp; **Scaling** bauble

### Description

This ornately carved arrow always survives being fired. By concentrating as a standard action, the
last person to shoot the arrow can sense the direction in which it lies, up to a distance of 1 mile.
This effect fades 24 hours after firing. Initially, a legacy arrow acts merely as a masterwork
arrow, but it always retains its ability to survive and be recovered after use. Legacy arrows can be
found as other sorts of ammunition, including bolts, shuriken, sling bullets, and even firearm
bullets.

**6th Level:** The arrow has the [distance](ref/magic-enh/distance) special ability, normally not
available to ammunition. When fired, it loses this weapon special ability and any gained at higher
levels for 1 day.

**9th Level:** The arrow has the distance and [seeking](ref/magic-enh/seeking) special abilities.

**11th Level:** When fired, the arrow regains its special abilities after 10 minutes instead of 1
day.

**14th Level:** Once per day as a standard action, the arrow's wielder can designate it as a
[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) arrow for a category of her choice. It retains its current bane
properties until they are changed. The arrow still takes 10 minutes to regain the use of bane, as
usual.

**16th Level:** The arrow retains its bane, distance, and seeking special abilities after being
fired.

**18th Level:** Once per week, the wielder can take a standard action to fire the arrow, giving it
the properties of a [greater slaying arrow](ref/magic-weapon/slaying-arrow-greater) (keyed to the
same category as its bane ability) in addition to its other special abilities. After being fired,
the arrow loses all special abilities for 1 week.

**20th Level:** The greater slaying ability can be used once per day (after which the arrow loses
all special abilities for 1 day) rather than once per week. The arrow overcomes
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) as though adamantine.
