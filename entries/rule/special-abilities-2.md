**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 554

The following special abilities include rules commonly used by a number of creatures, spells, and
traps.

**Extraordinary Abilities (Ex):** Extraordinary abilities are nonmagical. They are, however, not
something that just anyone can do or even learn to do without extensive training. Effects or areas
that suppress or negate magic have no effect on extraordinary abilities.

**Spell-Like Abilities (Sp):** [Spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), as the name
implies, are magical abilities that are very much like spells. Spell-like abilities are subject to
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) and [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic). They do
not function in areas where magic is suppressed or negated (such as an
[antimagic field](ref/spell/antimagic-field)). Spell-like abilities can be dispelled, but they
cannot be counterspelled or used to counterspell.

**Supernatural Abilities (Su):** Supernatural abilities are magical but not spell-like. Supernatural
abilities are not subject to [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) and do not function in
areas where magic is suppressed or negated (such as an antimagic field). A supernatural ability's
effect cannot be dispelled and is not subject to counterspells. See Table 16-1 for a summary of the
types of special abilities.

### Table 16-1: Special Ability Types

|Ability|Extraordinary|Spell-Like|Supernatural|
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|Dispel|No|Yes|No|
|[Spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance)|No|Yes|No|
|Antimagic field|No|Yes|Yes|
|Attack of opportunity|No|Yes|No|

**Dispel:** Can [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) and similar spells dispel the effects of
abilities of that type?

**Spell Resistance:** Does [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) protect a creature from
these abilities?

**Antimagic Field:** Does an antimagic field or similar magic suppress the ability?

**Attack of Opportunity:** Does using the ability provoke attacks of opportunity the way that
casting a spell does?
