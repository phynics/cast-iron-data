You can create and recruit for an organization that doesn't rely on a specific building. For
example, you could may want to recruit employees (or minions) if you're a rogue and want to start
your own gang of cutpurses or a cleric who wants to start a cult of followers. You create an
organization out of component teams, so you can configure the organization exactly how you want it
(see [Rooms and Teams](ref/rule/rooms-and-teams)). How much downtime capital you can spend in a day
is limited by the size of the settlement you're in (see
[Spending Limits](ref/rule/spending-limits)). When you've spent the appropriate capital and time for
your organization, it's complete and you can put it to work immediately.
