Insanity is an affliction inflicted upon those who suffer from extraordinary physical, mental, or
spiritual anguishes and trials. Insanity can also be caused by exposure to particularly potent
sources of unhinging horror, madness, or alien natures, such that the mind simply cannot withstand
them. Insanity is a [mind-affecting](ref/spelldef/mind-affecting) effect.

### Curing Insanity

All insanities have a DC that represents the insanity's strength. An insanity's DC indicates the
Will save you need to roll in order to resist contracting the insanity when you are initially
exposed to it, but also the DC you need to make to recover. Recovering from an insanity naturally is
a lengthy process - once per week, you make a Will save against the insanity's current DC. If you
succeed on this save, the insanity's DC is reduced by a number of points equal to your Charisma
bonus (minimum of 1). You continue to suffer the full effects of the insanity until its DC is
reduced to 0, at which point you are cured and the insanity vanishes completely.

[Lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser) has no effect on insanity, but
[restoration](ref/spell/restoration) reduces the current DC of one insanity currently affecting a
target by an amount equal to the caster's level.
[Greater restoration](ref/spell/restoration-greater), [heal](ref/spell/heal),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [miracle](ref/spell/miracle), or [wish](ref/spell/wish)
immediately cures a target of all insanity.

### Types of Insanity

When a creature goes insane, roll on the following table to determine what form of insanity strikes.
Alternatively, you can assign the insanity to match the cause.

|d%|Insanity|
| --- | --- |
|01-11|[Amnesia](ref/madness/amnesia)|
|12-48|[Mania](ref/madness/mania)/[Phobia](ref/madness/phobia)|
|49-68|[Dissociated Identity](ref/madness/dissociated-identity)|
|69-78|[Paranoid](ref/madness/paranoid)|
|79-84|[Moral Insanity](ref/madness/moral-insanity)|
|85-100|[Schizophrenia](ref/madness/schizophrenia)|

**Note:** These rules point to the "original rules version" of some of the madnesses, which can be
found at the bottom of their entries. _Horror Adventures_ introduced
[different rules for madness](ref/rule/madness), which you may prefer to use.
