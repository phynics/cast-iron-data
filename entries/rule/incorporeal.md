**Incorporeal:** Creatures with the incorporeal condition do not have a physical body. Incorporeal
creatures are immune to all nonmagical attack forms. Incorporeal creatures take half damage (50%)
from magic weapons, spells, spell-like effects, and supernatural effects. Incorporeal creatures take
full damage from other incorporeal creatures and effects, as well as all force effects.
