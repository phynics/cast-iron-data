**Description:** The army regains a number of hit points equal to half its regeneration value each
Ranged or Melee phase. When an army with regeneration is reduced to 0 hp, it is defeated only if at
least one enemy army survives at the end of that phase to finish off the regenerating creatures.
Outside of battle, the army regains a number of hit points equal to half its regeneration value each
hour.
