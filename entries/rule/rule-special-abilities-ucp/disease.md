**Description:** If the army damages an enemy, the enemy becomes diseased and takes a cumulative -1
penalty to its OM and DV each day after the battle. Curing the disease requires a successful
Stability check modified by this penalty, and allows the army to reduce this penalty by 1 each day
thereafter until the penalty is gone.
