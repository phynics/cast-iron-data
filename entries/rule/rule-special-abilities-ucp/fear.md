**Description:** If the army damages an enemy army, that army must attempt a Morale check (DC = 10 +
your army's ACR). Failure means the enemy army is afraid and can't attempt an Offense check to
attack during the next phase. If an army fails a Morale check during a phase in which it is already
afraid, it routs.
