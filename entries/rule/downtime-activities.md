This section provides many examples of activities you can undertake during downtime. Some of these
are new, and others expand on options from the Core Rulebook or other sourcebooks to explain how
those activities relate to the downtime rules. In most cases, using the downtime rules doesn't
change the costs for performing the action, but it might allow you to spend capital instead of gp as
per [Table 2-1: Capital Values](ref/rule/gaining-capital). You can substitute 1 point of Goods or
Labor for 20 gp, 1 point of Influence for 30 gp, and 1 point of Magic for 100 gp where appropriate.
You can combine multiple types of capital when substituting for a gp value.

Some downtime activities allow you to spend Goods, Influence, Labor, or Magic to modify the outcome
of a check. You must decide to spend this capital before you attempt the check.

Also see: [Criminal Downtime Activities](ref/rule/criminal-downtime-activities)
