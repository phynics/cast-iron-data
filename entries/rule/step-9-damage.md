Each weapon and type of natural attack has a damage dice value associated with it. The values in the
monster arrays from Step 1 give only the total amount of damage each attack deals. Assigning the
right damage dice to each of a monster's attacks helps make it fit more naturally with the rest of
the game's rules. For damage dice for natural attacks, reference
[Table 3-1: Natural Attacks by Size](ref/umr/natural-attacks).
