**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 168

**Slot** shoulders; CL 12th; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **Price** 5,400 gp; **Scaling** bauble

### Description

When this plain gray cloak is worn with the hood drawn up around the head, the wearer gains a +5
competence bonus on Stealth checks. If the wearer spends at least 1 minute concentrating on blending
the cloak's colors to match her surroundings, she can attempt Stealth checks without cover or
concealment until she makes an attack or moves more than half her base speed on her turn. Once she
breaks this effect, she must spend another minute to reactivate it.

**14th Level:** The cloak grants its wearer the camouflage [ranger](ref/class/ranger) ability in
forested areas.

**18th Level:** At will, when the wearer envelops herself in the cloak as a standard action, she can
become invisible as [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility).

**20th Level:** If the wearer also wears the
[boots of the elven spirit](ref/rule/swi-boots-elven-spirit), she can focus the cloak's
_invisibility_ effect. As a free action, she can choose to make it so her attacks do not end the
_invisibility_ effect for 1 round. She can use this ability for 10 rounds per day, but the rounds
need not be consecutive.

### Construction Requirements

[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [blend](ref/spell/blend),
[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater), _invisibility,_ creator must be an
[elf](ref/race/elf); **Cost** 22,000 gp
