With the exception of specific movement-related skills, most move actions don't require a check.

|Move Action|Attack of Opportunity^[1](ref/rule/move-actions#footnote-1)^|
| --- | --- |
|Move|Yes|
|Control a frightened mount|Yes|
|Direct or redirect an active spell|No|
|Draw a weapon^[2](ref/rule/move-actions#footnote-2)^|No|
|Load a hand crossbow or light crossbow|Yes|
|Open or close a door|No|
|Mount/dismount a steed|No|
|Move a heavy object|Yes|
|Pick up an item|Yes|
|Sheathe a weapon|Yes|
|Stand up from prone|Yes|
|Ready or drop a shield^[2](ref/rule/move-actions#footnote-2)^|No|
|Retrieve a stored item|Yes|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

Regardless of the action, if you move out of a threatened square, you usually provoke an attack of
opportunity. This column indicates whether the action itself, not moving, provokes an attack of
opportunity.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

If you have a base attack bonus of +1 or higher, you can combine one of these actions with a regular
move. If you have the [Two-Weapon Fighting](ref/feat/two-weapon-fighting) feat, you can draw two
light or one-handed weapons in the time it would normally take you to draw one.
