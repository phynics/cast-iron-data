**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 188

In some situations, your movement may be so hampered that you don't have sufficient speed even to
move 5 feet (a single square). In such a case, you may spend a full-round action to **move 5 feet**
(1 square) in any direction, even diagonally. Even though this looks like a 5-foot step, it's not,
and thus it provokes attacks of opportunity normally.

**Using a special ability** is usually a standard action, but some may be full-round actions, as
defined by the ability.
