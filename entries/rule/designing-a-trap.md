Designing new traps is a simple process. Start by deciding what type of trap you want to create.

To calculate the Challenge Rating of a trap, add all the CR modifiers to the base CR for the trap
type.

**Mechanical Trap:** The base CR for a mechanical trap is 0. If your final CR is 0 or lower, add
features until you get a CR of 1 or higher.

**Magic Trap:** For a spell trap or magic device trap, the base CR is 1. The highest-level spell
used modifies the CR.
