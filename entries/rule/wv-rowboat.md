**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 184

Large water vehicle\
**Squares** 3 (5 ft. by 15 ft.); **Cost** 50 gp

#### Defense

**AC** 9; **[Hardness](ref/umr/hardness)** 5\
**hp** 60 (29)\
**Base Save** +0

#### Offense

**Maximum Speed** 30 ft.; **Acceleration** 10 ft.\
**CMB** +1; **CMD** 11\
**Ramming Damage** 1d8

#### Description

This small water vehicle is primarily used to disembark from larger ships or to traverse small areas
of water—perhaps ferrying a few passengers across a stream or small lake. A rowboat can carry 1,000
pounds of cargo or 2 passengers.

**Propulsion** current (water) or muscle (pushed; 1 or 2 Medium rowers; one is the driver)\
**Driving Check** [Survival](ref/skill/survival)\
**Forward Facing** boat's forward\
**Driving Device** oars\
**Driving Space** the center square of the rowboat\
**Decks** 1
