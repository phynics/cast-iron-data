**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 159

Spells that protect against or repel all sorts of wilderness threats appear in this range.

**[Antilife Shell](ref/spell/antilife-shell) and [Antiplant Shell](ref/spell/antiplant-shell):**
These are notable defensive spells because they don't allow a saving throw to resist their effects.
While [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) offers a means of defense against these spells,
such an ability is almost non-existent among plant creatures. Despite the lengthy 1-round casting
time, successfully casting _[antilife shell](ref/spell/antilife-shell)_ can buy precious rounds of
protection to cast other spells. _[Antiplant shell](ref/spell/antiplant-shell)_ has a shorter
casting time and a lower spell level, making it more useful when dealing with plant threats.

**[Repel vermin](ref/spell/repel-vermin):** _[Repel vermin](ref/spell/repel-vermin)_ is similar to
_[antilife shell](ref/spell/antilife-shell),_ but it takes up a lower-level spell slot and requires
less time to cast. While the field created by this spell can be bypassed with a successful Will
save, it still deals damage to vermin managing to traverse it - a particularly powerful effect
against large numbers of enemies with few hit points. Perhaps the most enticing use of
_[repel vermin](ref/spell/repel-vermin)_ is its ability to diminish the effectiveness of vermin
swarms.
