**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 54

During the Activity phase, the militia can take a number of actions as set by its rank and modified
further by a Strategist, other unique allies, or events. Actions can be taken in any order, but each
requires a qualified team unless otherwise stated. Once assigned, a team can't take additional
actions during the same phase.

The available actions are listed below. The teams that grant access to each action are listed in
parentheses after the action's name. If the militia doesn't have an available team of that type, the
militia can't take that action. An action that specifies "no team required" doesn't require a team.
Rather, the PCs themselves take the action during the week. Such actions still counts toward the
week's total available actions.

|Action|Team(s)|
| --- | --- |
|Activate Black Market|Black Marketeers|
|Activate Refuge|Conspirators, Scholars, or Spellcasters|
|Broker Market|Black Marketeers, Fixers, or Merchants|
|Change Officer Role|No Team Required|
|Covert Action|Spies|
|Dismiss Team|No Team Required|
|Drill Militia|No Team Required|
|Earn Gold|Black Marketeers, Fixers, Merchants, or Patrons|
|Gather Information|Conspirators, Informants, Scholars, or Spellcasters|
|Guarantee Event|No Team Required|
|Knowledge Check|Scholars|
|Lie Low|No Team Required|
|Manipulate Events|Guardians|
|Recruit Team|No Team Required|
|Reduce Danger|Defenders, Guardians, Infiltrators, or Specialists|
|Rescue Character|Guardians, Infiltrators, or Specialists|
|Restore Character|Spellcasters|
|Sabotage|Saboteurs|
|Secure Cache|Moles, Propagandists, Saboteurs, and Spies|
|Special|No Team Required|
|Special Order|Fixers|
|Spread Propaganda|Propagandists, Saboteurs, and Spies|
|Strike Team|Specialists|
|Upgrade Team|No Team Required|
