You use humor or cleverness to gain an advantage over your opponent, but the tactic can backfire if
your jokes and jibes fall flat.

**Associated Skills:** Linguistics, Perform (comedy).

**Special:** When using wit, you can choose to gain a +2 bonus on the associated skill check. If you
do so and fail the associated skill check, decrease your determination by 1. If you fail by 5 or
more, you take a -2 penalty on wit's associated skill checks for the rest of the duel.
