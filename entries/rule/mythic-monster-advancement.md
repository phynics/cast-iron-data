This section addresses how to turn a non-mythic monster into a mythic monster and how to create an
entirely new mythic creature. Just as characters' abilities depend on their mythic tier, monsters'
abilities depend on their mythic rank (MR), with a higher rank meaning a creature has additional
mythic abilities.
