**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 163

**Slot** none; CL 6th; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **Price** 2,650 gp; **Scaling** prize

### Description

This +1 light mace has bands of several different metals around its hilt, each band one inch thick.
Once per day, the mace's owner can transform the mace into a different metal as a standard action.
The multimetal mace remains that type of metal until transformed again. To begin with, it can
transform to silver or back to steel. It retains all magical abilities when it transforms, along
with any ongoing spells and effects currently affecting it.

**8th Level:** The multimetal mace can be transformed into cold iron.

**10th Level:** The multimetal mace can be transformed into adamantine.

**12th Level:** The multimetal mace is a +2 light mace.

### Construction Requirements

[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[heart of the metal](ref/spell/heart-of-the-metal); **Cost** 11,405 gp
