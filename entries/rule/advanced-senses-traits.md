**Scent (4 RP):** **Prerequisites:** None; **Benefit:** Members of this race gain the
[scent](ref/umr/scent) ability.

**See in Darkness (4 RP):** **Prerequisites:** None; **Benefit:** Members of this race can
[see perfectly in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) of any kind, including that created by spells
such as [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness).
