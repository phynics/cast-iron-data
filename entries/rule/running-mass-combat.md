Since an army's strength is represented by an ACR score, the GM can balance armies against each
other using the [guidelines for CR](ref/rule/designing-encounters). For example, two ACR 9 armies
should make for a relatively even battle, but so would an ACR 9 army against three ACR 6 armies.
This applies mostly to typical humanoid armies, as monsters with powerful abilities might be
significantly more formidable.
