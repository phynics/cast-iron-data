1. Healthy
1. Sluggish
1. Stiffened
1. Staggered
1. Immobile
1. Dead

**Sluggish:** A character rendered sluggish by Dexterity poison has dulled reactions. He takes a -2
penalty on Reflex saves and all Dexterity-based attack rolls, ability checks, and skill checks, as
well as to AC.

**Stiffened:** A character stiffened by Dexterity poison feels numb and stiff. He is considered
flat-footed and is denied his Dexterity bonus to AC, even if he has uncanny dodge. He can't make
attacks of opportunity.

**Staggered:** A character staggered by Dexterity poison is so slowed and stiffened that he gains
all the effects of the staggered condition (except that he can take a full-round action if it is
purely mental).

**Immobile:** A character rendered immobile by Dexterity poison cannot move his body at all. He is
helpless and can take only purely mental actions.

**Dead:** The character's body becomes completely incapable of movement and dies.
