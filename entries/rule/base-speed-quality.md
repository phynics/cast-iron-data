The next step is to pick the base speed quality for your race. Some racial traits can increase speed
or grant other movement types, but these traits usually require the normal speed quality as a
prerequisite. You have the following options.

**Normal Speed (0 RP):** The race has a base speed of 30 feet.

**Slow Speed (-1 RP):** The race has a base speed of 20 feet. If the race is Medium, its members'
speed is never modified by armor or encumbrance.
