**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 535

A dedicated power operates only when an intelligent item is in pursuit of its special purpose. This
determination is always made by the item. It should always be easy and straightforward to see how
the ends justify the means. Unlike its other powers, an intelligent item can refuse to use its
dedicated powers even if the owner is dominant (see
[Items Against Characters](ref/rule/items-against-characters)). The caster level for these effects
is equal to the item's caster level. Save DCs are based on the item's highest mental ability score.
See Table 15-26 for a list of dedicated powers.
