Psychic manifestations have three main types: offensive manifestations, defensive manifestations,
and thought-form creatures. For the purpose of [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks,
creating a manifestation is considered casting a spell with a spell level equal to the number of MP
spent to create the manifestation. The combatant creating the manifestation treats her manifesting
level as her caster level for such checks.
