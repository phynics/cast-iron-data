**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 201

Psychic duels are battles in which combatants' minds become intertwined within a
[binary mindscape](ref/rule/binary-mindscapes). Though psychic spellcasters wield tremendous power
in such battles, other creatures can also wield mental might with surprising potency.
