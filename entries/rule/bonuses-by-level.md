The table above presents fractional values for the base save and base attack bonuses. To determine
the total base save bonus or base attack bonus of a multiclass character, calculate the fractional
values for each of the character's classes using the table and add them together.

This rule affects only multiclass characters, and such characters will have a number of attacks
depending on their combined base attack bonuses from several classes. For this reason, the table
does not list the multiple attacks gained by characters with a BAB of +6 or greater. Just remember
that a second attack is gained when a character's total BAB reaches +6, a third at +11, and a fourth
at +16, just as normal. For a character who's an 11th-level fighter and a 9th-level rogue, adding a
BAB of +11 to a BAB of +6-3/4 yields a BAB of +17 (rounded down from +17-3/4), with additional
attacks with BABs of +12, +7, and +2, respectively.
