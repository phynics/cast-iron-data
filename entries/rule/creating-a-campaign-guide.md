**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 20

In television series and similar ongoing media, there is the concept of the "story bible," a
document setting forth the essential information about the characters, story, and setting of the
series so that later writers can produce scripts with some amount of consistency. In a similar vein,
Game Masters can create a campaign guide to help keep track of their creation and to supply players
with an overview of an ongoing campaign. This way, the players have some idea what to expect and can
create and play their characters to best fit into the overall setting. The most important function
of a campaign guide is to make your players excited to delve into the setting and play the game!
