**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 177

The following are some of the typical driving devices for vehicles, plus their usual Armor Class,
hit points, and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness). A broken driving device increases the driving check of
the vehicle by 10. When a driving device is destroyed, a vehicle cannot be driven until the driving
device is repaired.

|Device|AC|hp|Hardness|
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|Reins|14|10|0|
|Rigging|9|5 per square of sail|0|
|Steering Wheel|10|25|5|
|Throttle|12|15|5|
|Rudder|10|25|5|
|Magically Treated|-|×2|×2|
