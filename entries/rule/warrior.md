Professional [warriors](ref/npcclass/warrior) typically have a wide range of experience and useful
contacts among other career combatants, those they serve, and those they oppose.

**Favor:** Gifts a PC one non-magical weapon, piece of armor, or adventuring gear.

**Favor:** Can guard a precious object or hide it where none will find it.

**Skill:** Relates his experience patrolling the local sewers, granting a PC a +2 bonus on Knowledge
(dungeoneering) checks in the city sewers.

**Skill:** Provides information with which to blackmail a local criminal, granting a PC a +4 bonus
on Intimidate checks against local street thugs.

**Unique:** Can form a posse, bringing together a group of 2d4 low-level warriors to aid in one
specific plan.

**Unique:** Grants the secret of a specialized fighting style, providing a PC with a +1 bonus on
initiative.
