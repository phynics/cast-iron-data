###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

Reroll buckler and tower shield, which cannot have spikes.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

For adamantine armor, add +4,850 gp to light armor, +9,850 gp to medium armor, +14,850 gp to heavy
armor.

###### ^3^ {#footnote-3}

For dragonhide armor, double the item's cost.

###### ^4^ {#footnote-4}

For mithral armor, add +850 gp for light armor, +3,850 gp for medium armor, +8,850 gp for heavy
armor.

###### ^5^ {#footnote-5}

For darkwood shields, add +10 gp per pound.

###### ^6^ {#footnote-6}

For dragonhide shields, double the item's cost.

###### ^7^ {#footnote-7}

For mithral shields, add +850 gp.

###### ^8^ {#footnote-8}

Armor and shields can't have enhancement bonuses higher than +5. Use these lines to determine price
when special abilities are added in.

###### ^9^ {#footnote-9}

Reroll specific armor, specific shield, or special ability. Consult Table 5-9 or Table 5-10, as
appropriate.

###### ^10^ {#footnote-a}

Add to enhancement bonus on Table 5-8: Magic Armor and Shields to determine total market price.

###### ^11^ {#footnote-b}

If you roll a special ability twice, roll again. If you roll two versions of the same special
ability, use the better.
