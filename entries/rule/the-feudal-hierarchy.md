A sample feudal hierarchy, from top to bottom, is as follows. Note that the nobility holds the land
in trust for the crown and does not own it outright. Certain exceptions, called allodial holdings,
can be granted to anyone by the crown, but they usually quickly revert to the crown via trickery or
conquest. As a feudal society doesn't leave a lot of leeway for travel, adventurers might be freemen
out to improve their station through great deeds, or the youngest progeny of the nobility, eager to
win their own titles through force of arms.
