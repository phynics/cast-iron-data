Many wilderness-appropriate spells are available as early as 1st level. These low-level spells are
often disregarded, as many of them can be substituted with class abilities, equipment, and skills.
Still, low-level spells (those of 3rd level and lower) can remain useful at high levels, especially
because high-level characters can cast them far more frequently.
