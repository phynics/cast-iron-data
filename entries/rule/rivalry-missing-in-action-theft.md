**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 59

**Rivalry\*:** Choose two random teams; those teams develop an unhealthy but temporary rivalry.
During the next Activity phase, neither team can take actions. _Twice:_ This event becomes
persistent until an [officer](ref/rule/officers) succeeds at a DC 20 Bluff, Diplomacy, or Intimidate
check.

**Missing in Action:** A team in the field is [missing](ref/rule/team-conditions). One team that
operated this week, determined at random, is unavailable for the following week. _Twice:_ The team
returns at the end of the following week, but is disabled.

**Theft\*:** Halve the militia's current [treasury](ref/rule/treasury-mt) total. _Mitigate:_ If the
militia succeeds at a DC 20 Loyalty check, it loses only 10% of its treasury. _Twice:_ This event
becomes persistent. The militia loses half of all income added to its treasury until it takes a
successful [Reduce Danger](ref/rule/reduce-danger) action.
