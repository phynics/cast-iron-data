**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 154

Magic influences nearly everything in the Pathfinder Roleplaying Game. In an intrigue-based
campaign, the principal focus shifts from exploration and dungeon-delving - where magic is primarily
used for survival and fighting - to navigating complex and precarious social interactions. Politics,
organized crime, espionage, mercantilism, and other intrigue-based objectives require extensive use
of subtlety, subterfuge, thoughtful planning, and orchestrated tactics. As a result, characters
engaged in intrigue often utilize spells that are geared toward communication rather than combat,
spying and intelligence-gathering rather than physical defense, and winning power and influence
rather than slaying opponents outright and taking their treasure.

The following section offers advice on certain spells particularly likely to see use in an
intrigue-focused game, organized by level of play and spell school.
