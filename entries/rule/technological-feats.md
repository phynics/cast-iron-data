Several new technology-related feats interact with Numerian technology.

[Craft Cybernetics:](ref/feat/craft-cybernetics) You can build cyberware and install it in a
creature's body.

[Craft Pharmaceutical:](ref/feat/craft-pharmaceutical) You can craft pharmaceuticals.

[Craft Technological Arms and Armor:](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor) You can build
technological weapons and armor.

[Craft Technological Item:](ref/feat/craft-technological-item) You can craft technological gear and
items.

[Robot's Bane:](ref/feat/robots-bane) You have trained to avoid the attacks and effects employed by
robots and technology and to combat them effectively.

[Scavenger's Luck:](ref/feat/scavengers-luck) You coax better behavior out of timeworn technology.

[Technologist:](ref/feat/technologist) You are familiar with the basic mechanics of technology.

[Technology Adept:](ref/feat/technology-adept) (Combat) You utilize high-tech firearms to maximum
effect.

[Technophobe:](ref/feat/technophobe) Smashing technological abominations brings you joy.

[Wrest Charge:](ref/feat/wrest-charge) You can extract a charge from otherwise depleted technology.
