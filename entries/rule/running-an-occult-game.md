The word "occult" refers to that which is hidden or occluded, and it encompasses a wide range of
strange disciplines known to only a few. Ancient though they may be, the mysteries of the occult
remain obscure, protected by hermetic orders or locked within esoteric repositories of knowledge.

In an occult adventure, the characters uncover clues as they search for the meaning behind
perplexing events and supernatural phenomena. The tone is usually ominous and laden with secrecy and
veiled threats as the characters untangle each portentous thread of occult mystery. Ordinary folk
seldom recognize the patterns beneath events; only the truly initiated, undaunted by the risks to
mind, body, and soul, dare plumb the depths of the occult in order to protect their world from
unseen dangers.
