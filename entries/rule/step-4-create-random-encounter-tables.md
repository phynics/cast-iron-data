A territory's CR sets the average CR of random encounters occurring within that territory. Numerous
example encounter tables appear in [Adventures](ref/rule/adventures), but your encounter tables
don't need to be as complex. Usually, a short table of a half dozen or so potential encounters is
enough. It's generally best to check for encounters four times per day - once at dawn, once at noon,
once at dusk, and once at midnight, with a 20% chance of an encounter each time. You can even tie
encounters to exploration checks, with a result of a natural 1 on such a check indicating a random
encounter. Feel free to adjust the frequency of these checks and the chance of a random encounter
occurring as best suits your game. Keep in mind that too many random encounters and wandering
monsters can slow down the progression of your plot and can frustrate players. It's often best to
limit random encounters to one per day of in-game time.
