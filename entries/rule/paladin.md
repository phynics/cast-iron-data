A character who chooses [paladin](ref/class/paladin) as her secondary class gains the following
secondary class features.

**Code:** At 1st level, she must follow the paladin's code of conduct and gains the paladin's aura
of good.

**Detect Evil:** At 3rd level, she can [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil) as a 1st-level paladin.

**Lay on Hands:** At 7th level, she gains the ability to lay on hands a number of times per day
equal to half her character level, healing as much as a paladin of her character level - 4.

**Smite Evil:** At 11th level, she gains the ability to smite evil once per day as a paladin of her
character level - 4.

**Mercy:** At 15th level, she selects one mercy from the paladin's 3rd-level mercy list.

**Divine Bond:** At 19th level, she gains a divine bond to a weapon as a paladin of her character
level - 3.
