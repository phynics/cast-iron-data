**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 206

A settlement's alignment not only describes the community's general personality and attitude, but
also influences its modifiers. A lawful component to a settlement's alignment increases its law
modifier by 1. A good component increases its society modifier by 1. A chaotic component increases
its crime modifier by 1. An evil component increases its corruption modifier by 1. A neutral
component increases its lore modifier by 1 (a truly neutral city gains an increase of 2 to its lore
modifier). Alignment never modifies a settlement's economy modifier.
