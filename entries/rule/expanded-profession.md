A profession often encompasses many smaller areas of expertise, and these auxiliary skills can come
in handy in situations beyond just making money or answering tradespecific questions. Below are some
sample additional uses for Profession skills, and GMs are encouraged to create their own.

| --- | --- | --- |

|Prepare trail rations (takes 1 hour per day's worth of rations)|Baker or cook|10|
|Obtain a legal permit|Barrister or clerk|15|
|Get someone released from jail who has been imprisoned for a minor crime^[1](ref/rule/expanded-profession#footnote-1)^|Barrister|20|

###### ^1^ {#footnote-1}

A major crime typically requires a trial involving a series of Bluff, Diplomacy, Intimidate,
Profession (barrister), and Sense Motive skill checks, as well as appropriate Knowledge checks.

###### ^2^ {#footnote-2}

To a minimum of 25% of the original value.

###### ^3^ {#footnote-3}

Provide this benefit for yourself plus one other person for every 2 points by which your skill check
exceeds the DC.

###### ^4^ {#footnote-4}

Increase the DC by 5 if the item is from a region the character is unfamiliar with, and by another 5
if it's more than 20 years old. Exceptionally rare or ancient pieces can't usually have their origin
identified in this way.
