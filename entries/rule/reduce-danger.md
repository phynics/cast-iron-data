**Sources:** Lands of Conflict pg 56

**Reduce Danger** **(Defenders, Guardians, Infiltrators, or Specialists):** With a successful DC 15
Security check, the team attacks a secured settlement with hit-and-run tactics, temporarily
improving the militia's [reputation](ref/rule/reputation-mt) in a town by one step for the week, and
allowing militia members to walk freely on the streets of hostile and unfriendly settlements. On a
failed check, the militia's [Notoriety](ref/rule/notoriety-mt) score increases by 1d4.
