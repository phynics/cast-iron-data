When your character's current hit points drop to a negative amount equal to his Constitution score
or lower, or if he succumbs to [massive damage](ref/rule/loss-of-hit-points), he's dead. A character
can also die from taking [ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) or suffering an ability
drain that reduces his Constitution score to 0.

Certain types of powerful magic, such as [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) and
[resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection), can restore life to a dead character.
