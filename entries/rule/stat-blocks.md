One of the most complex parts of the game is the stat block. Every NPC, every monster, and every
timid little forest creature in the campaign world has its own stat block. This isn't to say, of
course, that you need to generate a stat block for every creature that appears in your adventure,
but you should certainly generate stat blocks for all of the important NPCs and monsters with whom
you expect the PCs to interact.

The _Pathfinder RPG Bestiary_ provides more than 300 pre-made monster stat blocks for use in
adventures, and that's just the beginning: you can use stat blocks from other monster bestiaries or
adventures just as easily in your game. One good trick is to copy a stat block onto a 3×5 card or
into a small document you can easily bring up on your computer during the game - you can keep these
cards and documents forever to reuse them as needed.
