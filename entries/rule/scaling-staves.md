A staff is a long shaft that stores several spells. Staves usually hold spells grouped by a theme
and sometimes possess other abilities that they grant to the wielder. A staff has 10 charges when
created but can be recharged by a spellcaster with the appropriate spells.

### Staff of the Pyre Lord

**Slot** none; CL 8th; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **Price** 8,800 gp; **Scaling** wonder

#### Description

This staff is carved entirely of polished obsidian inset with rubies. Flickering flames seem to
dance across its surface. As the wielder goes up in level, the illusory flames grow larger and
change in color, starting out a faint yellow and eventually becoming a deep blue. This staff allows
the use of the following spells.

- [Spark](ref/spell/spark) (1 charge)
- [Scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray) (2 charges)

**10th Level:** _[Scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray)_ now costs only 1 charge, and the staff
allows the use of [fireball](ref/spell/fireball) for 2 charges.

**12th Level:** The staff's _[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)_ spells become
[empowered](ref/feat/empower-spell) _[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)._ The staff counts as a +1
[flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) quarterstaff when used as a weapon.

**14th Level:** The staff allows the use of [summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6) for 2
charges to summon fire elementals only.

**16th Level:** The staff replaces _[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6)_ with
[summon monster VIII](ref/spell/summon-monster-8) for 3 charges to summon fire elementals only. It
also allows the use of [fire storm](ref/spell/fire-storm) for 2 charges.

**18th Level:** The staff allows the use of [meteor swarm](ref/spell/meteor-swarm) for 3 charges.
The cost for _[summon monster VIII](ref/spell/summon-monster-8)_ decreases to 2 charges, and
empowered _[fireball](ref/spell/fireball)_ now costs 1 charge.

**20th Level:** The cost of _[meteor swarm](ref/spell/meteor-swarm)_ and
_[fire storm](ref/spell/fire-storm)_ each decrease to 1 charge.

#### Construction Requirements

[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [Empower Spell](ref/feat/empower-spell),
_[fire storm](ref/spell/fire-storm),_ [fireball](ref/spell/fireball),
_[meteor swarm](ref/spell/meteor-swarm),_ [scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray),
[spark](ref/spell/spark), [summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6),
_[summon monster VIII](ref/spell/summon-monster-8);_ **Cost** 132,000 gp

### Staff of Ways

**Slot** none; CL 10th; **Weight** 1 lb.\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Scaling** prize

#### Description

This wooden staff is carved with intricate patterns of waves, wind, trails, and vines. As the
wielder goes up in level, the patterns begin to move, slowly at first, but gradually increasing in
speed and magnitude. At the highest levels, the staff appears to be filled with a twisting and
complex nature scene, with forking paths and raging tsunamis. This staff allows the use of the
following spells.

- [Longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider) (1 charge)
- [Expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat) (2 charges)
- [Monkey fish](ref/spell/monkey-fish) (2 charges)

**12th Level:** The staff allows the use of [spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb) for 2 charges.
The cost of _[expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat)_ and
_[monkey fish](ref/spell/monkey-fish)_ each reduce to 1 charge.

**14th Level:** The staff allows the use of [fly](ref/spell/fly) for 2 charges. The cost of
_[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb)_ decreases to 1 charge.

**16th Level:** The staff grants its owner a permanent +10-foot enhancement bonus to her base
movement speed, a swim speed of 10 feet, and a climb speed of 10 feet. The staff can no longer be
used to cast _[longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider)_ or _[monkey fish](ref/spell/monkey-fish)._ The
staff allows the use of [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) for 2 charges and
[water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing) for 3 charges. The cost of _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_
decreases to 1 charge.

**18th Level:** The staff allows the use of [find the path](ref/spell/find-the-path) for 3 charges.
The cost for _[water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing)_ decreases to 1 charge.

**20th Level:** The staff grants its owner a fly speed of 30 feet (poor maneuverability).
_[Find the path](ref/spell/find-the-path)_ now costs 2 charges, and
_[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement)_ costs only 1 charge. If the staff's wielder
breaks the staff in two as a full-round action, she can transport herself and up to 19 allies to a
safe location she knows extremely well. This ability ignores local conditions as if it were the
transport travelers effect of a [wish](ref/spell/wish).

#### Construction Requirements

[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat),
_[find the path](ref/spell/find-the-path),_ [fly](ref/spell/fly),
_[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement),_ [longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider),
[monkey fish](ref/spell/monkey-fish), [spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb),
_[water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing);_ **Cost** 66,500 gp
