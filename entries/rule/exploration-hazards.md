**Sources:** Ultimate Campaign pg 158

Hazards are dangerous obstacles or events relevant to a hex's terrain type. They represent the
natural disasters, harsh weather, and bad luck that can befall an expedition, and are included to
liven up the PCs' journey as they explore the world. In addition to these hazards, you might use an
[environmental danger that's suitable to the current terrain](ref/rule/wilderness). After the
effects of a hazard have been resolved, treat the hex as a standard example of that terrain.

### Bad Weather (Any)

Bad weather can range from minor precipitation to a [serious storm](ref/rule/storms). The weather
can include lightning strikes and cause floods, landslides, and other natural hazards.

### Blight (Any)

Whether from a lack of water, a plague, or hostile magic, the plants and wildlife in the hex are
suffering. A blight affects the hex and all adjacent unexplored hexes, and lasts for 1d4+2 weeks.
During a blight, Survival DCs to get along in the wild increase by 5.

### Deadly Gas (Desert, Marsh)

In a marsh, pockets of flammable gas can build up under the surface before violently erupting,
throwing rocks, mud, and debris in all directions with startling force. In a desert, toxic fumes
from a natural vent, old mine, or magical disaster might leak into the air, poisoning or mutating
nearby creatures.

In a marsh, PCs can attempt a DC 15 Perception check to notice the smell and swelling before it
erupts. The eruption deals 2d6 points of bludgeoning damage in a 20-foot radius, or 4d6 points of
fire damage if the area contains open flame.

In a desert, PCs can attempt a DC 15 Perception check to notice the fumes and get out of their path
before coming to harm. Otherwise, they must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or take 1d4 points of
Constitution damage and be nauseated for 10 minutes.

### Dust Devil (Desert, Hill, Plain)

A dust devil is a whirlwind not associated with a storm, particularly in a region with little or no
topsoil. Treat a dust devil as a [duststorm](ref/rule/storms),
[sandstorm, or tornado](ref/rule/severe-events).

### Grass Fire (Hill, Plain)

Grass fires are often caused by lightning or careless camp fires. A grass fire is similar to a
[forest fire](ref/rule/forest-fires), except it can be spotted at twice the normal distance, and a
PC caught in its area saves against heat damage only every 10 rounds.

### Volcanic Tremor (Hill, Mountain)

Though active volcanoes are rare, even dormant volcanoes can produce tremors. Tremors last anywhere
from 1d4 rounds to 2d6 minutes and increase Climb DCs by 2. The tremors might start an avalanche or
collapse a cave or cliff (similar to an [earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake) spell).
