This use of Spellcraft functions as the [Use Magic Device core skill](ref/skill/use-magic-device),
but uses Intelligence instead of Charisma.

**Action:** None.

**Try Again:** Yes, but if you ever roll a natural 1 while attempting to activate an item and you
fail, you can't try to activate that item again for 24 hours.
