Even the most superficial treatment of a campaign setting needs to deal with history and the passage
of time. Every ancient ruin, powerful artifact, and court intrigue is rooted in the past, and
history brings context to adventures. Your decisions on such things as the age of the world, the
nature of the calendar, and even the length of the seasons have significant impacts on the stories
that unfold.
