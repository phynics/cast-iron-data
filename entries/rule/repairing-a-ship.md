**Sources:** Skull and Shackles Player's Guide pg 16\
The fastest and easiest way to repair a ship is with spells. [Mending](ref/spell/mending) is not
powerful enough to meaningfully affect an object as large as a ship, but
[make whole](ref/spell/make-whole) affects a ship as if it were a construct, repairing 1d6 points of
damage per level. In addition, more mundane methods can also be used to repair ships. Because of
their specialized construction, ships (as well as oars and sails) usually require the
[Craft (ships)](ref/skill/craft-any) skill to repair. Depending on the nature of the damage, skills
such as Craft (carpentry) or Craft (sails), or even various [Profession](ref/skill/profession-any)
skills, can be used to repair ships with the GM's approval. In general, a day's worth of work by a
single person using the appropriate skill to repair a ship requires 10 gp of raw materials and a DC
10 skill check, and repairs 10 points of damage on a success, or 5 hit points on a failure.
[Fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate) can also be used to create the raw material needed for repairs. New
[oars](ref/eq-misc/oar) can be purchased for 2 gp each.
