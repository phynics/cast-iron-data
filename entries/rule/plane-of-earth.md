**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 190

The Plane of Earth is a solid place made of soil and stone. An unwary traveler might find himself
entombed within this vast solidity of material and crushed into nothingness, with his powdered
remains left as a warning to any foolish enough to follow. Despite its solid, unyielding nature, the
Plane of Earth is varied in its consistency, ranging from soft soil to veins of heavier and more
valuable metal.

The Plane of Earth has the following traits:

- Earth-Dominant
- Enhanced Magic: Spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with the
  [earth](ref/spelldef/earth) descriptor or that use, manipulate, or create earth or stone
  (including those of the [Earth](ref/domain/earth) domain and the
  [elemental](ref/sorcbloodline/elemental) \[earth\] bloodline) are enhanced.
- Impeded Magic: Spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with the
  [air](ref/spelldef/air) descriptor or that use or create air (including those of the
  [Air](ref/domain/air) domain, spell-like abilities of the elemental \[air\] bloodline, and spells
  that summon air elementals or outsiders with the [air](ref/subtype/air) subtype) are impeded.
