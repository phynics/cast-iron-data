**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 188

The Material Plane is the center of most cosmologies and defines what is considered normal. It is
the plane most campaign worlds occupy. The Material Plane has the following traits:

- Normal Gravity
- Normal Time
- Alterable Morphic
- No Elemental or Energy Traits: Specific locations may have these traits, however.
- Mildly Neutral-Aligned: Though it may contain high concentrations of evil or good, law or chaos in
  places.
- Normal Magic
