A fleet battle begins as a result of two fleets encountering each other on the high seas. During a
mass naval conflict, players do not draw upon their characters' abilities - instead, they use their
fleet's squadrons to make attacks against the enemy, with their characters serving as commanders on
the ships. A mass naval combat plays out over the course of multiple rounds, with each round
consisting of a battle phase and a rout phase.

At the start of a combat, place each fleet's ships on the table as representations for the attack.
For each squadron, place a number of appropriately sized ship counters on the table so that they are
adjacent to each other. Actual placement on the table is irrelevant; since these fleet battle rules
are streamlined and simplified, the allocation of damage represents the ships maneuvering and
shifting position during the battle.
