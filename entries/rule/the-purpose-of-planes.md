**Sources:** GameMastery Guide pg 166

If you're pouring all your efforts into developing a vibrant, self-contained world for your campaign
setting, why should you bother introducing planes at all? The simplest answer is that many rules
systems expect you to. Monsters like devils and angels - and in fact, all creatures of the
"outsider" type in the Pathfinder Roleplaying Game - are presumed to stem from different planes than
the PCs, often ones tied to specific alignments. In addition, it can be difficult to challenge
high-level PCs within the boundaries of the same world that housed their low-level adventures. By
having your players travel to other planes, you gain the opportunity to reinvent the natural laws of
the setting and introduce both creatures and characters powerful enough to keep the PCs on their
toes. Most importantly, however, planes fill out your world and help to answer the biggest of
spiritual questions.
