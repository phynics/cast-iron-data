**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 136

In a standard Pathfinder game, when a dragon with 800 hit points has taken 798 points of damage,
it's still fighting at full capacity, but as soon as it has taken 801 points of damage, it's knocked
completely unconscious. Though this ensures that injured characters remain useful and effective, it
can feel unrealistic, and it devalues any healing that doesn't strictly prevent a knockout. Consider
using the following variant to add tension and increase the strategic value of healing, but be aware
that it can lead to situations that punish the side that's already behind, and that the pace of
fights could drag in the late stages as two injured parties slug it out. Wound thresholds also make
it especially dangerous for a party to take on higher-CR opponents.
