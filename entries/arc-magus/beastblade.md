**Sources:** Familiar Folio pg 8\
Beastblade magi work in tandem with their familiars, using spell, steel, and claw to clear the
battlefield of foes.

**Familiar (Ex)**

**Ability:** The beastblade gains the [familiar](ref/arcana/familiar) magus arcana.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Magus arcana gained at 3rd level

**Tandem Touch (Su)**

**Ability:** While the beastblade's familiar is holding the charge for a touch spell, the beastblade
can cast another spell without discharging the familiar's held charge.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Spell recall

**Familiar Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** When a beastblade prepares his magus spells, he can expend points from his _arcane
pool_ (up to a maximum number of points equal to his Intelligence modifier) to empower his familiar.
For each point expended in this way, the familiar can cast one magus spell as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once that day. Each spell selected must be 3
spell levels lower than the highest spell level the beastblade can cast, and can't require a
material component that costs more than 1 gp.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Knowledge pool

**Familiar Spellstrike (Su)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** Whenever a beastblade's familiar successfully delivers a touch spell against a creature
the magus threatens, the target provokes an attack of opportunity from the magus.

**Replaces:** Improved spell recall
