While most magi use a one-handed weapon as their melee implement of choice, one group of magi uses
the quarterstaff instead. These lightly armored magi use staves for both defense and inflicting
their spells upon enemies. Skilled in manipulating these weapons with one hand or two, they
eventually learn how to use arcane staves as well, and are just as formidable in combat as their
sword-swinging brethren.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A staff magus is proficient with simple weapons only. He can cast magus spells while
wearing light armor without incurring the normal arcane spell failure chance. Like any other arcane
spellcaster, a magus wearing medium armor or heavy armor or using a shield incurs a chance of arcane
spell failure if the spell in question has a somatic component. A multiclass magus still incurs the
normal arcane spell failure chance for arcane spells received from other classes.

**Replaces:** Weapon and armor proficiencies

**Quarterstaff Master (Ex)**

**Ability:** The staff magus gains the [Quarterstaff Master](ref/feat/quarterstaff-master) feat as a
bonus feat, even if he does not meet the normal prerequisites. He only gains the benefit of this
feat when wearing no armor or light armor.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Quarterstaff Defense (Ex)**

**At 13th Level:** This bonus increases by +3.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** While wielding a quarterstaff, the staff magus gains a shield bonus to his Armor Class
equal to the enhancement bonus of the quarterstaff, including any enhancement bonus on that staff
from his _arcane pool_ class feature.

**Replaces:** Medium armor, heavy armor

**Staff Weapon (Su)**

**Ability:** A staff magus can restore 1 charge to a staff by expending a number of points from his
_arcane pool_ equal to the level of the highest-level spell cast in the staff, as long as at least
one spell in the staff is on the magus spell list. The magus cannot restore charges to more than one
staff per day.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** A staff magus treats any magical staff he is wielding as a magical quarterstaff with an
enhancement bonus to attack and damage equal to [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of the
staff's caster level (minimum +1). The staff must have at least 1 charge for the magus to use this
ability.

**Replaces:** Fighter training

**Magus Arcana:** The following magus arcana complement the staff magus archetype:
[critical strike](ref/arcana/critical-strike), [dispelling strike](ref/arcana/dispelling-strike),
[maneuver mastery](ref/arcana/maneuver-mastery), and [spell shield](ref/arcana/spell-shield).
