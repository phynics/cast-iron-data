Spire defenders are magi who train themselves to accompany sages and archaeologists who venture from
the Mordant Spire, acting as aids and bodyguards. Because they often operate in
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) - narrow trenches in dig sites, flooded
dungeons, and tight corridors in ancient ruins' spire defenders place mobility and agility at a
premium. As a result, they eschew armor entirely. A spire defender has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** It's exceptionally rare for a non-[elf](ref/race/elf) to be granted access to the
training required to take this archetype (non-elves need GM approval to take this archetype).

**Weapon Proficiency**

**Ability:** A spire defender is proficient with all light and one-handed simple and martial
weapons, as well as one exotic light or one-handed melee weapon that has the
[disarm](ref/misc/disarm) or [trip](ref/misc/trip) special feature.

**Replaces:** Weapon proficiencies

**Bonus Feats (Ex)**

**Ability:** The spire defender gains [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) and
[Dodge](ref/feat/dodge) as bonus feats, even if he doesn't meet the prerequisites. The spire
defender is not proficient in any kind of armor or shield. The spire defender does not have the
magus' ability to ignore arcane spell failure from armor; however, if the spire defender becomes
proficient in light armor, he automatically gains the magus's ability to ignore the arcane spell
failure chance of light armor.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Armor proficiencies

**Special:** If he becomes proficient in medium armor, at 7th level he ignores medium armor's chance
of arcane spell failure. If he becomes proficient in heavy armor, at 13th level he ignores heavy
armor's chance of arcane spell failure.

**Arcane Augmentation (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Spell recall

**Swift:** A magus can expend 1 point from his _arcane pool_ to grant himself a +5 competence bonus
for 1 minute to one of the following skills: Acrobatics, Climb, Escape Artist, Perception, Stealth,
and Swim.

At 7th level: The competence bonus +2.

At 10th level: The competence bonus +3.

At 13th level: The competence bonus +4.

At 16th level: The competence bonus +5.

At 19th level: The competence bonus +6.
