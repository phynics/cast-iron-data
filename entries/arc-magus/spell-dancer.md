The strong emphasis on wizards within elven culture influences how even non-wizard elves see
themselves. Many elven magi do not consider themselves masters of a blend of martial and magical
talents, but rather a sub-category of wizards who study the effect of physical movement and
techniques upon spellcasting ability. They believe their ability to cast spells while fighting is an
outgrowth of the concept of the "spell dance," which itself is just another kind of wizardry. A
spell dancer has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Elf

**Gained class skills:** acro, [Perform (dance)](ref/skill/perform-any) (CHA)

**Lost class skills:** intm, ride

**Spell Dance (Su)**

**At 13th Level:** The spell dancer now has the option to gain
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) for 1d4 rounds, instead of the 5th- and
9th-level options.

**At 5th Level:** Once per spelldance as a **swift action**, the spell dancer may use one of the
following on himself: [blur](ref/spell/blur), [fly](ref/spell/fly), or [haste](ref/spell/haste).
These abilities last for 1 round.

**At 9th Level:** The spell dancer gains the option to use
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities),
in lieu of the 5th-level options.

**At 1st Level:** A spell dancer gains the ability to expend 1 point from his _arcane pool_ as a
**swift action** to gain a +10 enhancement bonus to his movement rate and a +2 dodge bonus to Armor
Class against attacks of opportunity provoked by moving through threatened spaces for 1 minute.

**Replaces:** The magus's ability to expend points from his _arcane pool_ as a swift action to grant
any weapon he is holding magic bonuses for 1 minute

**Arcane Movement (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** Whenever a spell dancer casts a magus spell, he gains a competence bonus on Acrobatics,
Climb, Escape Artist, and Stealth checks equal to the spell's level until the beginning of his next
turn.

**Replaces:** 5th-level bonus feat

**Dance of Avoidance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** While wearing light armor or no armor, a spell dancer gains a +2 insight bonus to Armor
Class.

**Replaces:** Medium armor

**Greater Dance of Avoidance (Su)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** While wearing light armor or no armor, a spell dancer's insight bonus to Armor Class
increases to +4.

**Replaces:** Heavy armor

**Magus Arcana:** The following magus arcana complement the spell dancer archetype:
[arcane cloak](ref/arcana/arcane-cloak), [prescient defense](ref/arcana/prescient-defense),
[close range](ref/arcana/close-range), [concentrate](ref/arcana/concentrate),
[hasted assault](ref/arcana/hasted-assault), [spell shield](ref/arcana/spell-shield).
