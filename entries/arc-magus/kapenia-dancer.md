For some Varisians, their traditional dances pulse with the power of their ancestors. These dancers
incorporate their kapenias—scarves embroidered with the history of the bearer's clan—into their
dances, weaving deadly whirling steps with the power of their ancestors.

## Weapon and Armor Proficiency {#weapon-and-armor-proficiency}

A kapenia dancer is proficient in simple weapons and [bladed scarves](ref/eq-weapon/bladed-scarf)
(_The Inner Sea World Guide_ 290). Additionally, a kapenia dancer can wield a bladed scarf as a
one-handed melee weapon. A kapenia dancer is not proficient with armor or shields and suffers the
normal arcane spell failure chance when casting magus spells while armored.

## Diminished Spellcasting {#diminished-spellcasting}

A kapenia dancer can cast one fewer spell of each level than normal. If this reduces the number to
0, he can cast spells of that level only if his Intelligence allows
[bonus spells](ref/rule/determine-bonuses) of that level.

## Canny Defense (Ex) {#canny-defense}

At 1st level, when a kapenia dancer is wielding a bladed scarf, he gains the canny defense ability.
This is identical to the [duelist prestige class](ref/pclass/duelist) ability of the same name
(_Core Rulebook_ 382), save that his magus level (rather than his duelist level) affects his Armor
Class.

## Weapon Focus (Ex) {#weapon-focus}

At 1st level, a kapenia dancer gains [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (bladed scarf) as a bonus
feat.

## Kapenia Dancer Arcana {#kapenia-dancer-arcana}

A kapenia dancer gains access to the following magus arcana. He cannot select any arcana more than
once.

### Elasticity (Su) {#elasticity}

As a free action, the kapenia dancer can spend 1 point from his arcane pool in order to grant a
bladed scarf he's wielding reach until the end of his turn. A kapenia dancer can use this arcana in
tandem with other magus arcana.

### Kapenia Curse (Su) {#kapenia-curse}

Choose an ability score upon taking this arcana. Whenever the kapenia dancer scores a critical hit
with a bladed scarf, he may expend 1 point from his arcane pool to deal 1d4 points of
[damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to that ability score of the creature struck. This arcana
can be selected multiple times, but a different ability score must be chosen each time. The kapenia
dancer must be at least 9th level before selecting this arcana.

### Spirit Fingers (Su) {#spirit-fingers}

Whenever the kapenia dancer scores a hit with a melee weapon, he can spend 1 point from his arcane
pool as a swift action to immediately attempt a [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver.

### Spirit Step (Su) {#spirit-step}

Whenever the kapenia dancer scores a critical hit with a bladed scarf, he may expend 1 point from
his arcane pool to teleport to any point within 30 feet. The kapenia dancer's turn ends after being
teleported. The kapenia dancer must be at least 12th level before selecting this arcana.

## Magus Arcana {#magus-arcana}

The following magus arcana from Ultimate Combat (UC) and Ultimate Magic (UM) complement the kapenia
dancer archetype: [arcane cloak](ref/arcana/arcane-cloak),
[dispelling strike](ref/arcana/dispelling-strike), [hasted assault](ref/arcana/hasted-assault),
[maneuver mastery](ref/arcana/maneuver-mastery), [prescient attack](ref/arcana/prescient-attack),
[prescient defense](ref/arcana/prescient-defense).
