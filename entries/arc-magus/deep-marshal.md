**Sources:** Arcane Anthology pg 28\
According to dwarven legend, the first deep marshals were handpicked by King Taargick himself to
create massive tunnels to aid in the dwarven exodus to Golarion's surface during the Quest for Sky.
Largely unseen by visitors, the deep marshals have survived into the modern era as keepers,
protectors, and repairers of the myriad of stony passages that travel around, through, and under the
Five Kings Mountains. Although the vast majority of deep marshals are [dwarves](ref/race/dwarf), a
few non-dwarves have earned enough trust to be trained as deep marshals, or have learned the same
techniques from studying old dwarven spellbooks.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Ability:** A deep marshal is proficient with light armor, medium armor, and shields (except tower
shields). A deep marshal can cast magus spells while wearing light armor or medium armor without
incurring the normal spell failure chance.

**Alters:** Armor proficiencies, medium armor, heavy armor

**At 9th Level:** A deep marshal gains the heavy armor magus ability at 9th level.

**Deep Spellcasting**

**Ability:** A deep marshal learns numerous arcane secrets involving the shaping and warding of
earth and stone. She adds the following sorcerer/wizard to her magus spell list as magus spells of
the same level: [crafter's curse](ref/spell/crafters-curse),
[crafter's fortune](ref/spell/crafters-fortune), [erase](ref/spell/erase),
[expeditious excavation](ref/spell/expeditious-excavation), [fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate),
[greater make whole](ref/spell/make-whole-greater), [knock](ref/spell/knock),
[major creation](ref/spell/major-creation), [make whole](ref/spell/make-whole),
[mending](ref/spell/mending), [minor creation](ref/spell/minor-creation),
[move earth](ref/spell/move-earth), [passwall](ref/spell/passwall),
[stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape), [transmute mud to rock](ref/spell/transmute-mud-to-rock),
[transmute rock to mud](ref/spell/transmute-rock-to-mud), and all 0- through 6th-level
sorcerer/wizard spells of the abjuration school. She must still add such spells to her spellbook to
prepare them.

**Alters:** Spellcasting

**Info:** If a spell appears on both the magus and sorcerer/wizard spell lists, the magus uses the
lower of the two spell levels listed for the spell.

**Special:** A deep marshal cannot cast spells from the enchantment, illusion, or necromancy school
and she cannot activate spell completion or spell trigger magic items that use spells from those
schools (without making a successful [Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) check).

**Bound by Tradition**

**Alters:** Arcane pool, spell combat, spellstrike

**Info:** A deep marshal is limited in how she can use her arcane powers. Her _arcane pool_ has a
number of points equal to 1/3 her magus level (minimum 1) + her Intelligence modifier. Furthermore,
she cannot use her _arcane pool_ to grant an enhancement bonus or weapon special ability to a weapon
she is holding unless it is a [battleaxe](ref/eq-weapon/battleaxe),
[heavy pick](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-pick), [warhammer](ref/eq-weapon/warhammer), or weapon with the
word &quot&dwarven&quot& in its name. She can use her _spell combat_ or _spellstrike_ abilities only
with such weapons.

**Miner's Focus (Su/Ex)**

**Ability:** A deep marshal can use the magical properties of her weapon in order to augment her
spellcasting. Whenever she casts a magus spell while wielding a magic battleaxe, heavy pick,
warhammer, or weapon with the word &quot&dwarven&quot& in its name, she adds half the weapon's
enhancement bonus to the her caster level (minimum +1). When attempting a caster level check to
overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance), she instead adds her weapon's full
enhancement bonus.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** In addition, she is treated as having a number of ranks in Knowledge (dungeoneering)
and Profession (miner) equal to her magus level, and she attempts Profession (miner) checks using
her Intelligence modifier instead of her Wisdom modifier.

**Replaces:** Magus arcana gained at 3rd level
