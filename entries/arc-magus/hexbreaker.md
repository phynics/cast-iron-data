**Sources:** Blood of the Coven pg 27\
Hexbreakers - sometimes called witch-hammers for their proclivity toward warhammers - have borne
witness to the damage of unrestrained arcane power and hone their own arcane arts to battle such
abuse. They train themselves to shatter the curses and dark transformations that can haunt others
for a lifetime.

**Repel Curse (Su)**

**Ability:** The magus adds _[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse)_ to his spell list as a
3rd-level spell and adds [break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment) as a 4th-level spell.

**Flavor:** A hexbreaker has the chance to protect others from being cursed.

**Immediate:** When a spell with the curse descriptor or a curse-style effect (such as a witch's
[evil eye](ref/hex/evil-eye) or [misfortune](ref/hex/misfortune) hex or a
[sea hag's](ref/monster/hag-sea-hag) evil eye) targets a creature within 30 feet of him, a
hexbreaker can spend 1 point from his _arcane pool_ to attempt to dispel that effect, as if he had
cast [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse). This ability has no effect on ongoing effects, such as
a sea hag's horrific appearance or a [pugwampi's](ref/monster/gremlin-pugwampi) unluck aura.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Spell recall

**Counter Curse (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a hexbreaker successfully dispels a curse effect or spell with the curse
descriptor using his _repel curse_ ability, he can immediately spend 2 points from his _arcane pool_
to reflect the spell back on the original caster. This otherwise functions as
[spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning).

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Improved spell recall

**Hexbreaker Arcana**

**Info:** A hexbreaker can select from the following arcana in addition to those normally available.

**Improved Remove Curse (Su)**

**Ability:** The hexbreaker can spend additional points from his _arcane pool_ to improve his
ability to remove a curse. Each point spent while casting _[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse)_
or _[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment)_ grants a +2 bonus to his caster level check.
He can use this arcana after rolling the caster level check, but only before the results are
revealed. This can be used with the hexbreaker's _repel curse_ and _counter curse_ abilities.

**Wracking Dispel (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever the hexbreaker successfully dispels a spell using
_[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment),_ [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), or
_[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse),_ he can expend 1 point from his _arcane pool_ to wrack the
original caster with pain, dealing 1d4 points of damage per spell level of the effect dispelled. If
the target succeeds at a Will save (DC = 15 + the level of the spell dispelled + the hexbreaker's
Intelligence modifier) it takes only 1 point of damage per spell level. The hexbreaker cannot target
the same caster more than once per round with this ability, regardless of how many effects he
dispels.

**Magus Arcana:** The following magus arcana complement the hexbreaker:
[bane blade](ref/arcana/bane-blade), [dispelling strike](ref/arcana/dispelling-strike),
[disruptive](ref/arcana/disruptive), [lingering pain](ref/arcana/lingering-pain),
[spellbreaker](ref/arcana/spellbreaker), and [reflection](ref/arcana/reflection).
