**Sources:** Arcane Anthology pg 27\
Puppetmasters focus on using charm and illusion spells to control the senses of those for whom they
perform.

**Gained class skills:** kh, kl, kr, perf

**Lost class skills:** climb, ride, swim

**Skills**

**Alters:** Skills

**Info:** A puppetmaster gains 4 skill points per level.

**Spells**

**Ability:** A puppetmaster can cast spells from the bard and magus spell lists, and treats bard
spells as magus spells. Because he does not have a bardic performance ability, some bard spells
(which modify or require bardic performance) are poor choices for a puppetmaster. He must add bard
spells to his spellbook prior to preparing them as normal. A puppetmaster can learn bard spells from
a formula book or spellbook (if they are also alchemist extracts or wizard spells) or a scroll (as
if learning a wizard spell from a scroll), or can select them to be one or more of the two magus
spells he adds to his spellbook at each magus level.

**Replaces:** Knowledge pool, greater spell access

**Arcane Pool**

**Alters:** Arcane pool

**Flavor:** A puppetmaster cannot expend points from his _arcane pool_ to grant enhancement bonuses
or weapon special abilities to a weapon he is holding.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Swift:** Whenever the puppetmaster casts a spell of the enchantment or illusion school, he can
expend 1 point from his _arcane pool_ to increase the spell's save DC or caster level by 1.

At 7th level: The increase to the spell's save DC or caster level changes to 2.

At 13th level: The increase to the spell's save DC or caster level changes to 3.

At 19th level: The increase to the spell's save DC or caster level changes to 4.

**Puppet Combat (Ex)**

**Alters:** Spell combat, improved spell combat, greater spell combat

**Info:** A puppetmaster can use _spell combat, improved spell combat,_ and _greater spell combat_
only with spells of the enchantment and illusion schools of magic.

**Charmstrike (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** The puppetmaster can instead cast a 2nd-level enchantment spell on the foe.

**At 16th Level:** He can cast a 3rd-level enchantment spell on the foe.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Spellstrike, fighter training, counterstrike

**Special:** The prepared spell is expended, as if cast normally.

**Swift:** When a foe fails a saving throw against a spell (but not a spell-like or supernatural
ability) cast by a puppetmaster, the puppetmaster can expend 1 point from his _arcane pool_ to cast
a prepared 1st-level enchantment on the foe. The foe must be one that the spell can target, and is
the only creature affected (even if the spell can normally target multiple creatures).

**The Show Must Go On (Su)**

**Ability:** When a puppetmaster has a creature under the effects of an enchantment spell and that
creature has line of sight to an illusion spell the puppetmaster has cast with a duration of
concentration, the puppetmaster can link the illusion spell to the enchanted target. As long as the
target has line of sight to the illusion and is enchanted, and the puppetmaster has line of sight to
both the target and the illusion, the puppetmaster can maintain the illusion as a **free action**
each round. The puppetmaster can maintain only one illusion spell in this way.

**At 13th Level:** The puppetmaster can maintain two illusion spells in this way, requiring a
separate enchanted target for each illusion.

**Replaces:** Medium armor, heavy armor

**Scene Stealer (Su)**

**Ability:** If a puppetmaster successfully disbelieves an illusion, he can expend 1 point from his
_arcane pool_ to try to negate or steal control of the illusion. The puppetmaster attempts a caster
level check as if he were dispelling the effect with [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic). If he
succeeds, he can either end the effect or alter it as if he were the spell's caster. If the spell's
duration is concentration, the puppetmaster must concentrate on the new effect (or transfer it to an
enchanted target using _the show must go on_) or it ends.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** True magus
