**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 37\
A shaman who selects the battle spirit gains scars from every wound she takes, and the grit of
battle always seems to cling on her body. When she calls upon one of this spirit's abilities, she
grows in stature - becoming taller and more muscular, with a grimace of rage stretching across her
face.

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Battle spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Battle Master (Ex)**

**Ability:** The shaman makes an extra attack of opportunity each round. This ability stacks with
the attacks of opportunity granted by the [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes) feat.

**At 16th Level:** The shaman gains the [Greater Weapon Focus](ref/feat/greater-weapon-focus) feat
as a bonus feat, for the same weapon chosen for Weapon Specialization.

**At 8th Level:** The shaman gains the [Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/weapon-specialization) feat
in a weapon of her choice as a bonus feat.

**Special:** The shaman doesn't need to meet the prerequisites of these feats.

**Battle Ward (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman touches a willing creature (including herself) and grants a battle ward. The
next time a foe makes an attack roll against the target, the ward activates and grants a +3
deflection bonus to the warded creature's AC. Each subsequent time she's attacked, the defection
bonus reduces by 1 (to +2 for the second time she's attacked and +1 for the third). The ward fades
when the bonus is reduced to +0 or after 24 hours, whichever comes first.

**Special:** A creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

At 8th level: The starting bonus +4.

At 16th level: The starting bonus +5.

**Curse of Suffering (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to take more damage from
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) effects and causes its wounds to heal at a slower rate. When the cursed
creature takes bleed damage, it takes 1 additional point of bleed damage (even if the bleed is
[ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain)). Furthermore, when the target is subject to an
effect that would restore its hit points, that effect restores only half the normal amount of hit
points.

This curse lasts for a number of rounds equal to the shaman's level. A creature affected by this hex
cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

**Eyes of Battle (Su)**

**Flavor:** The shaman's senses become magically heightened in the heat of battle.

**Swift:** She can grant herself a +10 insight bonus for 1 round on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks made to notice and pinpoint invisible creatures within 30
feet.

She can instead use this ability to ignore the affects of [cover](ref/rule/cover) or partial cover
(but not total cover) on her next attack, as long as that attack is made before the end of her next
turn.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per shaman level

**Hampering Hex (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to take a -2 penalty to AC and CMD for a
number of rounds equal to the shaman's level. A successful Will saving throw reduces this to just 1
round.

**At 8th Level:** The penalty becomes -4.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, a creature affected by a _hampering hex_ cannot
be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal looks like a fiercer version of its species, with rippling
muscles and a stockier frame.

**Passive:** It gains a +2 natural armor bonus to AC. If it already has a natural armor bonus, the
bonus increases by 2 instead.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Battle spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Battle Spirit (Su)**

**Flavor:** A shaman surrounds herself with the spirit of battle. Allies within 30 feet of the
shaman (including the shaman) receive a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier rounds/day

At 8th level: This bonus +2.

At 16th level: This bonus +3.

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Battle spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Enemies' Bane (Su)**

**Swift:** The shaman imbues a single weapon she's wielding with the
[bane weapon](ref/magic-enh/bane) special ability, choosing the type of creature affected each time
she does. The effect lasts for 1 minute. If the weapon already has the _[bane](ref/spell/bane)_
weapon special ability of the type chosen, the additional damage dealt by _[bane](ref/spell/bane)_
increases to 4d6.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Battle spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Paragon of Battle (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman assumes a form that combines the effects of
[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person) and [deadly juggernaut](ref/spell/deadly-juggernaut) for
1 minute or until dismissed.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** Whenever she scores a critical hit, the attack ignores
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a spirit of battle.

**Fullround:** She can make a full attack and move up to her speed (either before or after the
attacks).

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** She gains a +4 insight bonus to AC for the purposes of confirming critical hits against
her. If she is reduced to below 0 hit points, she does not die until her negative hit point total
exceeds double her Constitution score.
