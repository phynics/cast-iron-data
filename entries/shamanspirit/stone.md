### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Stone spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Crystal Sight (Ex)**

**Ability:** The shaman sees through stone, earth, or sand as easily as if it were transparent
crystal. Her gaze can penetrate a number of feet equal to her shaman level (or if seeing through
metal, a number of inches equal to her shaman level).

**Usage:** 1 round/day per shaman level

**Lodestone (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes one creature within 30 feet to become heavy and lethargic. The
creature is treated as if it were [carrying a medium load](ref/rule/carrying-capacity). If the
creature is already carrying a medium load, it is instead treated as if it were carrying a heavy
load. If the creature is carrying a heavy load, its maximum Dexterity bonus to AC is reduced to +0,
it takes a -9 armor check penalty, and its movement is reduced to 5 feet.

**Special:** The effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to the shaman's level. A successful Will
saving throw negates this effect. Whether or not the save is successful, the creature cannot be the
target of this hex again for 24 hours.

**Metal Curse (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to become slightly magnetic until the end
of the shaman's next turn. Whenever the creature is attacked with a melee or ranged weapon
constructed primarily of metal, it takes a -2 penalty to AC.

**At 16th Level:** The penalty increases to -6 and lasts for three rounds

**At 8th Level:** The penalty becomes -4 and it lasts for two rounds.

**Special:** Once affected, the creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.

**Stone Stability (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** The shaman receives [Greater Trip](ref/feat/greater-trip) as a bonus feat.

**At 5th Level:** The shaman receives [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip) as a bonus feat.

**Passive:** The shaman receives a +4 bonus to her CMD when resisting bull rush or trip attempts as
long as she is standing on the ground.

**Special:** The shaman does not need to meet the prerequisites of these feats.

**Ward of Stone (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman touches a willing creature (including herself) and grants a _ward of stone._
The next time the warded creature is struck with a melee attack, it is treated as if it has DR
5/adamantine. This _ward_ lasts for 1 minute, after which it fades away if not already expended.

**At 16th Level:** The _ward_ now lasts through three attacks.

**At 8th Level:** The _ward_ persists through two attacks.

**Special:** A creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal looks as though it's made out of earth and stone, with tiny
gemstones embedded in its flesh.

**Passive:** The animal gains DR 5/adamantine.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Stone spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Touch of Acid (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon she wields is treated as a [corrosive](ref/magic-enh/corrosive)
weapon.

**Standard:** The shaman can make a melee touch attack that deals an amount of acid damage equal to
1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her shaman level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Stone spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Body of Earth (Su)**

**Passive:** The shaman gains DR/adamantine equal to [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding)
of her shaman level, minimum 2.

**Standard:** In addition, she can cause jagged pieces of stone to explode from her body in a
10-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst). This deals 1d6 points of piercing damage per 2 shaman levels
she possesses. A successful Reflex saving throw halves this damage.

The shaman can use this ability three times per day, but she must wait 1d4 rounds between each use.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Stone spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Elemental Form (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman assumes the form of a Huge (or smaller) earth elemental, as
[elemental body IV](ref/spell/elemental-body-4) with a duration of 1 hour per level.

**Usage:** Once per day

**Usage:** Once per day

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** She can also apply any one of the following feats to any acid or earth spell she casts
without increasing the spell's level or casting time: [Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell),
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell), [Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), or
[Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell). She doesn't need to possess these feats to use this ability.

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a being of acid and earth.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** The shaman gains acid resistance 30.
