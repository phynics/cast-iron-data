**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 24\
A shaman who selects the tribe spirit strives to protect her allies, whether they be a traditional
tribal unit or a chosen group of adventuring companions.

**Spirit Magic Spells**

**Info:** The shaman gains these spells at the listed spell levels.

**Spells:** Bless

**Spells:** Shield other

**Spells:** Create food and water

**Spells:** Spiritual ally

**Spells:** Life bubble

**Spells:** Battlemind link

**Spells:** Vision

**Spells:** Discern location

**Spells:** Mass heal

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Tribe spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Curse of Faltering (Su)**

**Immediate:** When an enemy within 30 feet threatens a critical hit, the shaman can force the
creature to reroll its original attack roll with a penalty equal to the shaman's Charisma modifier.
The target can attempt a Will save to negate this ability.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, the creature cannot be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours. This is a curse effect.

**Curse of Isolation (Ex)**

**Ability:** The shaman makes an enemy within 30 feet keenly feel its status outside the tribe. For
a number of rounds equal to the shaman's level, the target gains no benefit from
[flanking](ref/rule/flanking) or the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action, and it doesn't
benefit from morale bonuses. The target can attempt a Will save to negate this ability.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, the creature cannot be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours. This is a curse effect.

**Steadfast Example (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman touches a willing creature and bolsters its mental and emotional defenses.
The next time the target attempts a Will saving throw, the subject can use the shaman's Will saving
throw bonus instead of his own.

**Special:** The shaman can have only one creature under the effect of this hex at a time, and a
creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

**Threatening Coordination Hex (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to view the shaman's allies as obstacles.
The target treats squares adjacent to the shaman's allies as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) for a number of rounds equal to the shaman's
level, or for 1 round if the target succeeds at a Will saving throw.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, a creature affected by this hex cannot be the
target of it again for 24 hours.

**Touch of Succor (Su)**

**At 12th Level:** She also adds dazed, nauseated, and panicked.

**At 8th Level:** She adds confused and frightened to the list of conditions she can remove.

**Standard:** The shaman can touch a willing creature to remove one of the following conditions:
fatigued, shaken, or sickened.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per shaman level

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Ability:** When the spirit animal successfully performs the aid another action, the bonus it
provides increases by 1.

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal has colorations or markings that resemble a totem or
important symbol for the shaman's tribe.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Tribe spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Tribal Cooperation (Su)**

**Flavor:** The shaman gains a teamwork feat as a bonus feat. She must meet the feat's
prerequisites.

**Standard:** The shaman can grant one of her teamwork feats to all allies within 30 feet who can
see and hear her. Allies retain the use of this bonus feat for a number of rounds equal to 3 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her shaman level. Allies do not need to meet the
prerequisites of this bonus feat.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Tribe spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Tribal Bond (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day when she communes with her spirit animal to regain spells, the shaman can
select a number of creatures equal to half her shaman level to serve as her honorary tribe. These
creatures can constantly communicate with each other, as
[telepathic bond](ref/spell/telepathic-bond).

**Flavor:** The shaman and her tribe share a transcendent bond.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Tribe spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Guardian of the Tribe (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman can cast a harmless spell with a range of touch on a member of her _tribal
bond_ as long as that creature is within 30 feet.

**Passive:** In addition, she is constantly aware of the condition of all members of her _tribal
bond_ ability, as [status](ref/spell/status).

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Flavor:** The shaman embodies the strength and unity of her tribe.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** She gains a bonus on all of her saving throws equal to her Charisma modifier and
becomes immune to [compulsion](ref/spelldef/compulsion) spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Standard:** Once per day, she can attempt to revive a creature connected to her by her _tribal
bond_ ability who has died within 1 round as [breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life), except that
the spell can be cast at any range as long as the target is on the same plane, and the target
regains a number of hit points equal to 10 &times& the shaman's level (maximum 200).
