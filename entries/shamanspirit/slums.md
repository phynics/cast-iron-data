**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 21\
A shaman who selects the slums spirit gains the city's alleys and avenues as steadfast allies. The
rats in the gutter, the torches along the walls, the coins that flow through the market are all a
part of her and serve her whim.

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Slums spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Accident (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a target within 30 feet to stumble and fall. The shaman attempts a
caster level check with a DC equal to the target's CMD against [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempts. On a
successful check, the target falls [prone](ref/rule/prone) and takes 1d6 points of damage. If the
target is adjacent to a pit or similar drop-off, he must also succeed at a Reflex save (with a DC
equal to the shaman's caster level check) or fall into the pit. Observers must succeed at a
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) or [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) check with a DC equal
to the shaman's caster level check to identify her as the source of the accident.

**Bad Penny (Su)**

**At 8th Level:** The penalty becomes -4.

**Special:** If the shaman curses a new coin, the previous curse ends. This is a curse effect.

**Standard:** The shaman can curse a coin. The next bearer of the cursed coin takes a -2 penalty on
all saving throws and skill checks as long he has the coin on his person. Once the coin leaves his
person, the curse ends and the coin becomes a mundane piece of tender again.

**City Spirit (Su)**

**Swift:** The shaman channels the city's spirit through herself, gaining a +4 bonus on all
Dexterity- and Wisdom-based skill checks.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier rounds/day

**Ward of the City (Su)**

**Ability:** The spirit of the city shrouds one creature the shaman touches from the hazards of the
slums. The warded creature gains a +5 bonus on saves against disease and poison, and a +25% bonus on
percentage chances to negate critical hits and sneak attacks. (This stacks with effects such as
[fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification), or abilities that grant a creature with no chance to
negate critical hits a flat 25% chance.)

**At 16th Level:** The bonuses become +9 and the percentage chance becomes 45%.

**At 8th Level:** The bonuses increase to +7 and the percentage change increases to 35%.

**Special:** Each time the _ward_ is used (whether the roll is successful or not), the bonuses are
reduced by 1 and 5%, respectively. The _ward_ ends when the bonuses are reduced to 0, when the
shaman wards a new creature, or after 24 hours, whichever comes first. A creature affected by this
hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal looks like a leaner version of its species, with hungry eyes
and a wiry frame.

**Passive:** It gains a +4 bonus on initiative checks.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Slums spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Doors to Everywhere (Ex)**

**At 14th Level:** The shaman can use this ability as per [tree stride](ref/spell/tree-stride)
(treating all doors as generic coniferous trees).

**At 9th Level:** The shaman can use this ability as per [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door).

**Special:** Regardless of what spell this functions as, it can transport only you, and both your
departure and arrival spaces must be adjacent to a door or similar opening.

**Standard:** The shaman can step through any door and instantly exit through another distant
doorway, as per [jester's jaunt](ref/spell/jesters-jaunt).

**Usage:** 3 times/day + 1 per 8 shaman levels beyond 4th

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Slums spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**City's Shroud (Su)**

**Flavor:** When in an urban environment, the shaman blends into the streets around her, making her
difficult to pin down.

**Passive:** She gains the [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) and
[improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) class features.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Slums spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Paragon of the City (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman assumes a spirit-infused paragon form that makes her a lethal stalker of
the alleys and shadows. She gains the ability to make [sneak attacks](ref/ability/sneak-attack) as a
[rogue](ref/class/rogue) of her shaman level for 1 minute or until dismissed.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a spirit of the slums.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** She is immune to all diseases and poisons. When in an urban environment, she gains a +4
insight bonus to her AC and on Reflex saves.
