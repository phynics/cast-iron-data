**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 93, Heroes of the Wild pg 26\
A shaman who selects the wood spirit has a skin tone similar to the coloration of trees in her home
region. Her vibrant hair is fragrant and resembles leaves and blossoms.

**Spirit Magic Spells**

**Info:** The shaman gains these spells at the listed spell levels.

**Spells:** Shillelagh

**Spells:** Barkskin

**Spells:** Minor creation\|(wood items only)

**Spells:** Thorn body

**Spells:** Tree stride

**Spells:** Ironwood

**Spells:** Transmute metal to wood

**Spells:** Changestaff

**Spells:** Wooden phalanx

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Wood spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Hex of Lignification (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to turn into a twisted, tree-like shape for
2 rounds. The target gains [hardness](ref/rule/breaking-things) 5 but is
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered), and can negate the effect with a successful Fortitude saving throw.

**Special:** Whether or not the target succeeds at its save, it can't be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours.

**Nature's Gifts (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, the shaman can command trees and other plants to yield magical berries
and fruit. This ability functions as [goodberry](ref/spell/goodberry), except the maximum number of
hit points it can restore to a subject in a 24-hour period from this hex is equal to the shaman's
Charisma modifier (minimum 1 hit point per day).

**Spines and Brambles (Su)**

**Ability:** With a beckoning gesture, the shaman conjures spiny shrubs in a number of squares equal
to her Charisma modifier (minimum 1) within 30 feet. The squares become filled with
[light undergrowth](ref/rule/forest-terrain). The shaman can pass through the affected squares
without impediment.

**Special:** When the shaman uses this hex again, any previously conjured undergrowth withers away.

**Verdant Path (Sp)**

**Ability:** The shaman gains [woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride), as per the druid
ability of the same name.

**Flavor:** Even the most tangled briars make way for the shaman, and suitable roots and branches
appear to support her feet.

**At 8th Level:** She can use [air walk](ref/spell/air-walk) at will whenever she is within 10 feet
of a tree, though the effect ends instantly when she moves farther than 10 feet from a tree.

**Whispering Leaves (Sp)**

**Ability:** Whenever the shaman is within 10 feet of a tree or undergrowth, she can cast
[whispering wind](ref/spell/whispering-wind) as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
with a caster level equal to her shaman level. The targeted area must also contain trees or
undergrowth, which relay the message in a gentle, rustling voice.

**At 8th Level:** The shaman can also listen to the targeted area as though she were using
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance) for the 1 round during which the
hex is delivering the message.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Ability:** The animal gains [freeze](ref/umr/freeze) as per the universal monster rule.

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal looks like a wooden figurine or a vaguely animal-shaped tree
branch when it is motionless.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Wood spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Tree Limb (Su)**

**At 16th Level:** The shaman can transform both of her arms, gaining two slam attacks.

**At 8th Level:** The reach of this slam attack increases by 5 feet.

**Swift:** The shaman can turn one of her arms into a heavy, branch-like limb. She must drop
anything held in that hand, and she can't use this ability if she is wearing a shield on that arm.
Until the beginning of her next turn, she gains a [slam attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals
1d8 points of damage (for a Medium shaman; 1d6 if Small, 2d6 if Large).

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Wood spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Bloody Roots (Sp)**

**Standard:** The shaman can cause a field of thick roots to burrow up from the ground. This ability
functions as [black tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles) with a caster level equal to the shaman's
level. The area is centered on the shaman when she activates the ability but remains stationary if
she then moves.

The shaman is unaffected by the roots. Her allies treat the area as
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles), but the roots don't attack them. The shaman can
end the effect as a **free action**.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier rounds/day

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Wood spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Tree Form (Sp)**

**Standard:** The shaman can assume the form of a plant creature as per
[plant shape III](ref/spell/plant-shape-3) with a duration of 1 hour per level. She can use this
ability once per day.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** At will, the shaman can meld with any tree or single block of wood (as per
[meld into stone](ref/spell/meld-into-stone), except she can meld only with wood and has no limit on
how long she can remain in the wood).

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a living creature of wood.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** She is forevermore treated as a [plant creature](ref/type/plant) rather than her
original creature type for the purposes of spells and magical effects. Her skin takes on the
appearance of polished wood grain, and she gains a +4 natural armor bonus to her Armor Class and
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 10/- against wooden weapons and the natural attacks of
wooden and wood-like creatures. She gains immunity to paralysis, poison, polymorph, sleep, and stun.
