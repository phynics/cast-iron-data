A shaman who selects the flame spirit has a radiant light behind her eyes and the faint smell of
smoke about her. When she calls upon one of this spirit's abilities, a hungry spectral flame dances
around her body.

**Spirit Magic Spells**

**Info:** The shaman gains these spells at the listed spell levels.

**Spells:** Burning hands

**Spells:** Resist energy

**Spells:** Fireball

**Spells:** Wall of fire

**Spells:** Summon monster V\|(fire elementals only)

**Spells:** Fire seeds

**Spells:** Fire storm

**Spells:** Incendiary cloud

**Spells:** Fiery body

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Flame spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Cinder Dance (Ex)**

**Ability:** The shaman's base speed increases by 10 feet.

**At 10th Level:** The shaman receives [Acrobatic Steps](ref/feat/acrobatic-steps) as a bonus feat.

**At 5th Level:** The shaman receives [Nimble Moves](ref/feat/nimble-moves) as a bonus feat.

**Special:** The shaman doesn't need to meet the prerequisites of these feats.

**Fire Nimbus (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to gain a nimbus of fire. Though this
doesn't harm the creature, it does cause the creature to emit light like a
[torch](ref/eq-misc/torch), preventing it from gaining any benefit from
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility). The target also takes
a -2 penalty on saving throws against spells or effects that deal fire damage.

The fire nimbus lasts for a number of rounds equal to the shaman's level. A successful Will saving
throw negates this effect.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, the creature cannot be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours.

**Flame Curse (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes a creature within 30 feet to become
[vulnerable](ref/umr/vulnerabilities) to fire until the end of the shaman's next turn. If the
creature is already vulnerable to fire, this hex has no effect. Fire immunity and resistances apply
as normal, and any saving throw allowed by the effect that caused the damage reduces it as normal.

**Special:** A creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

At 8th level: The duration of this hex 2 rounds.

At 16th level: The duration of this hex 3 rounds.

**Gaze of Flames (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman sees through fire, fog, and smoke without penalty as long as there is enough
light to otherwise allow her to see normally.

**At 7th Level:** The shaman can gaze through any source of flame within 10 feet per shaman level,
as [clairaudience](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance). The shaman can use this ability a number
of rounds per day equal to her shaman level, but these rounds do not need to be consecutive.

**Ward of Flames (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman touches a willing creature (including herself) and grants a _ward of
flames._ The next time the warded creature is struck with a melee attack, the creature making the
attack takes an amount of fire damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her
shaman level. This _ward_ lasts for 1 minute, after which it fades away if not already expended.

**Special:** A creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

At 8th level: This hex now persists through 2 attacks.

At 16th level: This hex now persists through 3 attacks.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Passive:** The shaman's spirit animal is surrounded by a nimbus of flame that gives off light like
a [candle](ref/eq-misc/candle). This nimbus is warm to the touch, but doesn't cause any damage. The
animal is immune to fire damage, but is vulnerable to cold damage.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Flame spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Touch of Flame (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon she wields is treated as a [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon.

**Standard:** The shaman can make a melee touch attack that deals 1an amount of fire damage equal to
1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her shaman level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Flame spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Fiery Soul (Su)**

**Passive:** The shaman gains fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10.

**Standard:** In addition, she can unleash a 15-foot cone of flame from her mouth, dealing 1d4
points of fire damage per shaman level she possesses. A successful Reflex saving throw halves this
damage.

The shaman can use this ability three times per day, but she must wait 1d4 rounds between each use.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Flame spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Elemental Form (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman assumes the form of a Huge (or smaller) fire elemental, as if using
[elemental body IV](ref/spell/elemental-body-4) with a duration of 1 hour per level. The shaman can
use this ability once per day.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** She can apply any one of the following feats to any fire spell she casts without
increasing the spell's level or casting time: [Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell),
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell), [Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), or
[Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell). She doesn't need to possess these feats to use this ability.

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a spirit of flame.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** The shaman gains fire resistance 30.
