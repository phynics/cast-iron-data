**Sources:** Cohorts and Companions pg 19\
A shaman that selects the ancestors spirit has wise eyes and thick white or silver hair. Fine
wrinkles line the shaman's face, becoming more obvious when she smiles or glowers. When she calls
upon one of this spirit's abilities, her hair glows as though lit from within, rustling of its own
accord.

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Ancestors spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Ancestral Blessing (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman can grant the blessings of her ancestors to any ally within 30 feet. The
blessed creature receives a competence bonus on attack and damage rolls equal to 1 +
[one-eighth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her shaman level. This blessing lasts until the
blessed creature hits with an attack or deals damage to a target.

**Special:** The shaman can have only one ancestral blessing active at a time. If the shaman uses
this ability again, the previous blessing immediately ends.

**Ghost Blade (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman can touch a creature to grant all of her weapons the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon property for a number of rounds equal to her
Charisma bonus.

**Special:** Once a creature has been the target of this ability, it cannot be the target of this
ability again for 24 hours.

**Intercessor (Sp)**

**Ability:** The shaman can invoke an ancestor spirit into an intact, humanoid or monstrous humanoid
corpse to learn what the body knew in life. The acts as
[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead), but the shaman may ask only a single question.

**Special:** If an animated corpse or undead is targeted with this ability, the hex immediately
fails. Once a corpse has answered a single question, it cannot be targeted with this ability again.

**Might of the Fallen (Su)**

**Flavor:** The shaman can call upon the ancestral heroes of her family to bolster ailing allies.

**At 7th Level:** This increases to 1d4 points of temporary ability damage.

**Special:** Once a creature has been the target of this hex, it cannot be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours.

**Standard:** The shaman can cure 1 point of temporary
[ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) affecting the creature touched.

**Wisdom of the Ages (Su)**

**Flavor:** The shaman can call upon her ancestors for lore and guidance.

**Passive:** The shaman can use her Wisdom modifier instead of her Intelligence modifier on all
Intelligence-based skill checks.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Ability:** The spirit animal can speak and understand a number of bonus languages equal to the
shaman's Charisma bonus.

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal has streaks of gray or silver hide, hair, or fur, and long
facial hair that appears similar to a wispy mustache or bushy eyebrows.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Ancestors spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Ancestor's Council (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman can call upon her ancestors to provide advice and assistance to one ally
within 30 feet. The ally gains a +2 bonus on any attack roll, saving throw, ability check, or skill
check made before the beginning of the shaman's next turn.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Ancestors spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Ancestral Weapon (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** The weapon gains the [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon
property.

**Standard:** The shaman can summon an appropriately-sized simple or martial weapon with a +1
enhancement bonus from her family's history. She is always considered proficient with this weapon.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per shaman level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

At 15th level: The enhancement bonus +2.

At 19th level: The enhancement bonus +3.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Ancestors spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Ancestral Guardian (Sp)**

**Flavor:** The shaman can call on the ancient allies of her ancestors to physically appear and
assist her, even if they have moved on to new roles in the cosmos.

**Standard:** Once per day, the shaman can cast [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally). Although there
is no cost to use the [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), the planar ally demands
payment for services it performs as normal for the spell.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** She gains [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) out to a range of 60 feet.

**Ability2:** She can cast [astral projection](ref/spell/astral-projection) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day without requiring material
components.

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes one with the spirits of her ancestors.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** She gains a bonus on Will saving throws equal to her Charisma modifier and a +4 bonus
to her caster level for all divination spells.
