**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 132, Advanced Class Origins pg 16\
A shaman who selects the mammoth spirit is abnormally tall and stocky, with thick shaggy hair. When
she uses a special ability of this spirit, her muscles ripple and flex, and her stature seems even
greater than before. At times, particularly when she uses her most powerful abilities, a ghostly
image of a mammoth may seem to rise around her as a visible aura of ghostly power.

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Mammoth spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Burden of the Beast (Su)**

**Ability:** This ability works as the _lodestone_ ability of the [stone](ref/shamanspirit/stone)
spirit: The shaman causes one creature within 30 feet to become heavy and lethargic. The creature is
treated as if it were [carrying a medium load](ref/rule/carrying-capacity). If the creature is
already carrying a medium load, it is instead treated as if it were carrying a heavy load. If the
creature is carrying a heavy load, its maximum Dexterity bonus to AC is reduced to +0, it takes a -9
armor check penalty, and its movement is reduced to 5 feet.

**Special:** The effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to the shaman's level. A successful Will
saving throw negates this effect. Whether or not the save is successful, the creature cannot be the
target of this hex again for 24 hours.

**Mammoth's Hide (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman can touch a willing ally and cause its skin to thicken and sprout thick,
shaggy fur. The creature gains a +2 enhancement bonus to natural armor and cold
[resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 for 10 minutes.

**At 15th Level:** This enhancement bonus increases to +4 and the cold resistance to 15.

**At 9th Level:** The enhancement bonus increases to +3 and the cold resistance to 10.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Phantom Stampede (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman summons a host of ghostly herd beasts to trample a single creature. These
phantom beasts affect only the target creature, which is buffeted and pummeled by their passing. The
creature takes no damage from the ability, but takes a -4 penalty to its CMD against
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun), and [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempts.
Additionally, spellcasters under the effect of this ability take a -4 penalty on
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks.

**Special:** The target receives no saving throw to negate this effect. This effect lasts a number
of rounds equal to the shaman's level. The creature can't be the target of this hex again for 24
hours.

**Primal Speaker (Ex)**

**Ability:** The shaman can speak with [mammoths](ref/monster/elephant-mastodon) and any other
megafauna or [elephant](ref/monster/elephant) creatures as if she were under the effects of
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals).

**At 10th Level:** The shaman can affect one such animal within 30 feet as if she'd cast
[charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal) (Will negates). Whether or not the target succeeds at the
saving throw, it can't be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.

**At 5th Level:** The shaman gains a bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks when
dealing with those animals equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her shaman level.

**Thunder Foot (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The shaman's body thickens and becomes more muscular.

**At 11th Level:** The shaman gains [Greater Overrun](ref/feat/greater-overrun) as a bonus feat.

**At 7th Level:** The shaman gains [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun) as a bonus feat.

**Passive:** For the purpose of the overrun combat maneuver, she treats her shaman level as her base
attack bonus when calculating her CMB and CMD.

**Special:** The shaman doesn't need to meet the prerequisites of these feats.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Ability:** It gains a +2 inherent bonus to its Strength score. The spirit animal loses this bonus
when it manifests as a megafauna companion from the _true spirit_ ability.

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal appears more primal and prehistoric than an ordinary animal
of its kind.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Mammoth spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Powerful Smash (Ex)**

**Standard:** The shaman can attack an enemy with an unarmed strike as if she had the
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) feat. If the shaman hits a creature in
this way, that creature must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half the shaman's class level +
her Charisma modifier) or be [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Mammoth spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Strength of the Beast (Ex)**

**At 14th Level:** If this is her _spirit,_ this improves to +4.

**At 18th Level:** If this is her _wandering spirit,_ this improves to +4.

**At 20th Level:** If this is her _spirit,_ this bonus increases to +6.

**Passive:** The shaman gains a +2 enhancement bonus to her Strength score.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Mammoth spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Megafauna Companion (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman's spirit animal transforms into a megafauna
[animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion), using the shaman's shaman level as her effective
druid level. The shaman must choose an [arsinoitherium](ref/companion/arsinoitherium),
[baluchitherium](ref/companion/baluchitherium), [brontotherium](ref/companion/brontotherium),
[chalicotherium](ref/companion/chalicotherium), [deinotherium](ref/companion/deinotherium),
[elasmotherium](ref/companion/elasmotherium), [glyptodon](ref/companion/glyptodon),
[mastodon](ref/companion/elephant-mastodon), [megaloceros](ref/companion/megaloceros),
[megatherium](ref/companion/megatherium), [uintatherium](ref/companion/uintatherium), or another
mammalian megafauna (including most dire animals) that has animal companion statistics. It retains
its Intelligence score and the special abilities it gains from the _spirit animal_ class feature,
but it also has the statistics and abilities of an animal companion.

**Special:** If the animal companion is dismissed, lost, or dies, it can be replaced in the same way
as a normal spirit animal.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** The shaman can transform into any animal listed under the megafauna or elephant
heading. This ability works as per [beast shape IV](ref/spell/beast-shape-4), but the shaman can
activate and dismiss the ability as often as she likes and the duration is permanent.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
