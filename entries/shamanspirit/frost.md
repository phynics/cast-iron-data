Far to the north, Erutaki tribes have adapted to life in the bitter cold of the Crown of the World.
The frost spirit is seen by some Erutaki as a protector of their way of life, and shamans who
commune with the spirit are shown great respect in their communities.

A shaman who selects the frost spirit has coarse white hair and always feels cold to the touch.

**Info:** The shaman gains these spells at the listed spell levels.

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Frost spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Biting Frost (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman turns the air frigid around a target within 30 feet for a number of rounds
equal to the shaman's Charisma modifier (minimum 1). The target must attempt a Fortitude saving
throw at the beginning of each turn or be damaged by exposure to the extreme cold. On a failed save,
the target takes 1d6 points of nonlethal damage. On a successful save, the effect ends immediately.

**Special:** Whether or not the initial save is successful, the creature cannot be the target of
this hex again for 24 hours.

**Hypothermia (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman afflicts a creature within 30 feet with hypothermia. The target must attempt
a Fortitude saving throw. On a failed save, the target is [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for 2
rounds.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, the creature cannot be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours.

At 8th level: The duration 3 rounds.

At 16th level: The duration 4 rounds.

**Sluggish (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes the speed of a creature within 30 feet to be halved. The target can
attempt a Fortitude saving throw to negate this effect. The penalty lasts for a number of rounds
equal to the shaman's character level and does not stack with other effects that reduce speed.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, the creature can't be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours.

**Tundra Dweller (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman touches a willing creature and grants it cold
[resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 for a number of rounds equal to her Charisma modifier (minimum
1). This resistance does not stack with any other cold resistance, such as from special abilities or
magical items.

**Special:** A creature targeted by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

At 8th level: The duration 2 rounds.

At 16th level: The duration 3 rounds.

**Wilds-Attuned (Ex)**

**Passive:** The shaman receives [Animal Affinity](ref/feat/animal-affinity) as a bonus feat and
gains a +4 insight bonus on Knowledge (nature) checks when in a cold climate.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal is covered in a light layer of glimmering frost, and its
breath comes out as mist regardless of the temperature.

**Passive:** The animal has resistance 5 to cold and electricity.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Frost spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Ice Splinter (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon she wields is treated as a [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) weapon.

**Standard:** The shaman can shoot razor-sharp icicles at an enemy within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. This barrage deals an amount of piercing damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her shaman level. The shaman can use this ability a number
of times per day equal to 3 + her Charisma modifier.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Frost spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Frigid Blast (Su)**

**Passive:** The shaman gains cold resistance 10.

**Special:** The shaman can use this ability three times per day, but she must wait at least 1d4
rounds between each use.

**Standard:** In addition, she can summon an icy blast in a 20-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst)
originating from a point she can see within 30 feet. This blast deals cold damage equal to 1d6 per
shaman level she has to each creature caught in the burst. Each target can attempt a Reflex saving
throw to halve this damage.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Frost spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Guardian of the North (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman assumes the form, as [beast shape IV](ref/spell/beast-shape-4), of one of
the following animals: [dire bear](ref/monster/bear-dire-bear-cave-bear),
[dire tiger](ref/monster/tiger-dire-tiger-smilodon), [mastodon](ref/monster/elephant-mastodon), or
[woolly rhinoceros](ref/monster/rhinoceros-woolly-rhinoceros). The duration of this transformation
is 1 hour per level. The shaman can use this ability once per day.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** She can also apply any one of the following feats to any spell with the cold descriptor
that she casts without increasing the spell's level or casting time:
[Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell), [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell),
[Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), or [Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell). She doesn't need to
have these feats to use this ability.

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a being of ice and snow.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** The shaman gains immunity to cold.
