**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 44\
A shaman who selects the nature spirit takes on an appearance that reflects the aspect of the
natural world she has the closest connection to. A nature shaman from the forest has a green tinge
to her skin and hair, with eyes of sparkling emerald and the scent of green leaves and flowers about
her. A nature shaman from the tundra is typically alabaster pale, with platinum hair and crystal
blue eyes, and her skin always seems strangely cold.

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Nature spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Entangling Curse (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman [entangles](ref/spell/entangle) a creature within 30 feet for a number of
rounds equal to the shaman's Charisma modifier (minimum 1). A successful Reflex saving throw negates
this effect.

**Special:** Whether or not the save is successful, the creature cannot be the target of this hex
again for 24 hours.

**Erosion Curse (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman summons the powers of nature to erode a construct or object within 30 feet.
This erosion deals 1d6 points of damage per 2 shaman levels, ignoring [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)
and [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). If used against a construct or an object in
another creature's possession, the construct or the creature possessing the object can attempt a
Reflex saving throw to halve the damage. Once an object or a construct is damaged by this erosion,
it cannot be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.

**Friend to Animals (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman can spontaneously cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)
spells as a [druid](ref/class/druid), &quot&losing&quot& a prepared spell in order to cast any
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell of the same level or lower.

**Passive:** In addition, all animals within 30 feet of the shaman receive a sacred bonus on all
saving throws equal to the shaman's Charisma modifier.

**Speak with Animals (Ex)**

**Ability:** The shaman selects one or more specific kinds of animal (eagle, fox, dog, and so on),
up to a maximum of 1 + [one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her shaman level. The shaman
gains the ability to converse with those types of animal as if she were under the effects of
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals).

**Special:** As she increases in level, she can select more kinds of animals.

**Stormwalker (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman can move through nonmagical fog, rain, mist, snow, and other
[environmental effects](ref/rule/weather) without penalty. She is never slowed by such effects, and
she doesn't need to attempt Acrobatics skill checks to move across such surfaces. She can also move
through magical environmental effects that she created.

**At 10th Level:** The shaman can see twice as far as normal through environmental effects, whether
or not they are magical in nature.

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Ability:** The animal can move through any sort of undergrowth or natural
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) at its normal speed without taking damage or
suffering any other impairment. If the animal has a fly speed, it can ignore the penalty on
[Fly](ref/skill/fly) skill checks for winds up to windstorm strength.

**Flavor:** The shaman's spirit animal looks feral, and appears to be in peak physical form.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Nature spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Storm Burst (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon she wields is treated as a [thundering](ref/magic-enh/thundering)
weapon.

**Standard:** The shaman causes a small storm of swirling wind and rain to form around one creature
within 30 feet. This storm causes the target to treat all foes as if they had
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment), suffering a 20% miss chance for a number of rounds equal to 1 +
one-fourth of her shaman level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Nature spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Spirit of Nature (Su)**

**At 15th Level:** This increases to [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 3.

**Passive:** Whenever the shaman is reduced to below 0 hit points, she automatically stabilizes and
gains [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1 for 1d4 rounds.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Nature spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Companion Animal (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman's spirit animal takes the form of an
[animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion) of her choice, using her shaman level as her
effective druid level. The animal retains all the special abilities and the Intelligence score of
the spirit animal, but also has the statistics and abilities of an animal companion. If the animal
is dismissed, is lost, or dies, it can be replaced in the same way as a normal spirit animal.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Ability:** Once per day, she can surround herself with an organic cocoon of silk as a **full-round
action**. While enclosed in the cocoon, she's considered [helpless](ref/misc/helpless). Eight hours
later, she emerges, having changed her type to plant, animal, or humanoid, and having gained
superficial physical characteristics of the chosen type as appropriate. She must choose a type that
is different from her current type.

This effect change doesn't alter her Hit Dice, hit points, saving throws, skill ranks, class skills,
or proficiencies. The effect is permanent, until the shaman chooses to transform again.

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a spirit of nature.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Special:** Each time the transformation is made, the shaman is cleansed of all poisons or
diseases, restored to full hit points, and healed of all ability damage.
