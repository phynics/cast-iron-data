**Optional Background Skill**

**Details:** Yes

You are educated in a field of study and can answer both simple and complex questions. Like the
Craft, Perform, and Profession skills, Knowledge actually encompasses a number of different
specialties. Below are listed typical fields of study.

_Geography_ typically covers the following: lands, terrain, climate, people

### Check

Answering a question within your field of study has a DC of 10 (for really easy questions), 15 (for
basic questions), or 20 to 30 (for really tough questions).

|Task|DC|
| --- | --- |
|Identify a creature's ethnicity or accent|10|
|Recognize regional terrain features|15|
|Know location of nearest community or noteworthy site|20|
|Astronomy|Varies|

### Action

Usually none. In most cases, a Knowledge check doesn't take an action (but see "Untrained," below).

### Try Again

No. The check represents what you know, and thinking about a topic a second time doesn't let you
know something that you never learned in the first place.

### Untrained

You cannot make an untrained Knowledge check with a DC higher than 10. If you have access to an
extensive library that covers a specific skill, this limit is removed. The time to make checks using
a library, however, increases to 1d4 hours. Particularly complete libraries might even grant a bonus
on Knowledge checks in the fields that they cover.

### Researching Technology

**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 5\
When using the [technological equipment](ref/rule/technological-equipment) rules, a character can
use Knowledge (geography) for astronomy.

### Unchained Rogue Skill Unlocks

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 85\
If using [skill unlocks](ref/rule/skill-unlocks), with sufficient ranks in Knowledge, you earn the
following.

**15 Ranks**

**Details:** When you fail a Knowledge check, you can reroll the check at a -10 penalty. The
competence bonus above also applies to saving throws against exceptional, spell-like, or
supernatural abilities used by creatures you identify.

**20 Ranks**

**Details:** Whenever you attempt a Knowledge check, you can roll twice and take the better result.
