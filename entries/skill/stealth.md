**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 106

**Associated Skill**

**Details:** Dexterity

**Trained Only**

**Details:** No

**Armor Check Penalty**

**Details:** Yes

**Optional Unchained Skill**

**Details:** No

**Optional Background Skill**

**Details:** No

### Check

Your Stealth check is opposed by the [Perception](ref/skill/perception) check of anyone who might
notice you. Creatures that fail to beat your Stealth check are not aware of you and treat you as if
you had total [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). You can move up to half your normal speed and use
Stealth at no penalty. When moving at a speed greater than half but less than your normal speed, you
take a -5 penalty. It's impossible to use Stealth while attacking, running, or charging.

Creatures gain a bonus or penalty on Stealth checks based on their size: Fine +16, Diminutive +12,
Tiny +8, Small +4, Medium +0, Large -4, Huge -8, Gargantuan -12, Colossal -16.

If people are observing you using any of their senses (but typically sight), you can't use Stealth.
Against most creatures, finding [cover](ref/rule/cover) or [concealment](ref/rule/concealment)
allows you to use Stealth. If your observers are momentarily distracted (such as by a
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check), you can attempt to use Stealth. While the others turn their
attention from you, you can attempt a Stealth check if you can get to an unobserved place of some
kind. This check, however, is made at a -10 penalty because you have to move fast.

#### Breaking Stealth

When you start your turn using Stealth, you can leave cover or concealment and remain unobserved as
long as you succeed at a Stealth check and end your turn in cover or concealment. Your Stealth
immediately ends after you make an attack roll, whether or not the attack is successful (except when
_sniping_ as noted below).

#### Sniping

If you've already successfully used Stealth at least 10 feet from your target, you can make one
ranged attack and then immediately use Stealth again. You take a -20 penalty on your Stealth check
to maintain your obscured location.

#### Creating a Diversion to Hide

You can use Bluff to allow you to use Stealth. A successful Bluff check can give you the momentary
diversion you need to attempt a Stealth check while people are aware of you.

### Action

Usually none. Normally, you make a Stealth check as part of movement, so it doesn't take a separate
action. However, using Stealth immediately after a ranged attack (see Sniping, above) is a **move
action**.

### Special

If you are invisible, you gain a +40 bonus on Stealth checks if you are immobile, or a +20 bonus on
Stealth checks if you're moving.

If you have the [Stealthy](ref/feat/stealthy) feat, you get a bonus on Stealth checks.

### Unchained Rogue Skill Unlocks

**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 86\
If using [skill unlocks](ref/rule/skill-unlocks), with sufficient ranks in Stealth, you earn the
following.

**5 Ranks**

**Details:** Reduce the Stealth penalty from sniping by 10.

**10 Ranks**

**Details:** Stealth check penalties for moving quickly are halved, including the ability unlocked
at 5 ranks, moving full speed, and reaching concealment after creating a distraction.

**15 Ranks**

**Details:** If you attack after successfully using Stealth, your target is denied its Dexterity
bonus against all attacks that you make before the end of your turn.

**20 Ranks**

**Details:** If you attack after successfully using Stealth, your target is denied its Dexterity
bonus against all attacks that you make before the beginning of your next turn.
