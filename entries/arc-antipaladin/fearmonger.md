**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 20\
The fearmonger wants to do more than cause pain, misery, and confusion. He wishes to spread fear
among his foes and infect entire populations with it. The fearmonger archetype is available only to
the antipaladin alternate class.

**Feed on Fear (Su)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** The first time in a round a creature fails a saving throw against a fear effect created
by a fearmonger's spell, [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or supernatural ability
(including any ability that causes a creature to be shaken, frightened, or panicked), the fearmonger
is healed of 1 point of damage per 2 antipaladin levels. If the fearmonger is at full hit points
when this ability is activated, he instead gains an equal number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points). These temporary hit points last for 1 minute.
Temporary hit points gained through multiple successful uses of this ability do not stack; the
duration increases instead.

**Replaces:** Touch of corruption

**Usage:** A number of times per day equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his
antipaladin level plus his Charisma bonus.

antipaladin level plus his Charisma bonus.

**Frightening Cruelty (Su)**

**At 12th Level:** The fearmonger gains the _panicked_ cruelty.

**At 3rd Level:** The fearmonger must select [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) as his 3rd-level cruelty.

**At 6th Level:** He must choose [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) as his 6th-level cruelty.

**At 9th Level:** He must select [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) as his 9th-level cruelty.

**Panicked**

**Ability:** The target is [panicked](ref/misc/panicked) for 1 round per 4 class levels the
antipaladin possesses. The antipaladin must have the _shaken_ and _frightened_ cruelties before
selecting this cruelty.

**Alters:** Cruelty
