**Sources:** Armor Master's Handbook pg 4\
Covered from head to toe in blackened armor decorated with grotesque shapes and bristling with
spikes, iron tyrants make an unmistakable impression on the battlefield. These antipaladins' armor
is an outward symbol of their inner power, and they are rarely seen without it. Iron tyrants seek
the strength to rule over domains as unfettered despots, and depend on their armor as protection
against those they have not yet cowed.

Most iron tyrants are orcs or half-orcs who worship the orc deities [Lanishra](ref/faith/lanishra)
(god of slavery, subjugation, and tyranny) or [Varg](ref/faith/varg) (god of iron, siege weapons,
and warfare). Though rarer, some iron tyrants belong to other races and worship
[Urazra](ref/faith/urazra) (giant god of battle, brutality, and strength) or demon lords such as
[Aldinach](ref/faith/aldinach) (She of the Six Venoms), [Angazhan](ref/faith/angazhan) (the Ravenous
King), or [Izyagna](ref/faith/izyagna) (She of the Sevenfold Swarm).

**Iron Fist (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** An iron tyrant gains [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as a
bonus feat. In addition, whenever the iron tyrant makes a successful attack with a gauntlet, spiked
gauntlet, or armor spikes, the weapon damage is based on his level and not the weapon type, as per
the [warpriest's](ref/class/warpriest) _sacred weapon_ ability.

**Replaces:** Touch of corruption

**Bonus Feats**

**Ability:** At 3rd level and every 3 antipaladin levels thereafter (6th, 9th, 12th, and so on), an
iron tyrant gains a bonus feat in addition to those gained from normal advancement. This feat must
be a combat feat that relates to the iron tyrant's armor or shield, such as
[Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus), or one of the armor mastery or shield mastery feats.

**Replaces:** Cruelty

**Unstoppable (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** When wearing heavy armor, an iron tyrant is not slowed by terrain that halves movement
(such as dense rubble, light undergrowth, and shallow bogs). Terrain that has been magically
manipulated to impede motion or terrain that reduces movement by more than half still affects him.

**Replaces:** Channel negative energy

**Fiendish Bond (Su)**

**Flavor:** Instead of forming a _fiendish bond_ with his weapon or a servant, an iron tyrant can
form a bond with his armor.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Fiendish boon

**Special:** If a suit of bonded armor with a fiendish spirit is destroyed, the iron tyrant loses
the use of this ability for 30 days, or until he gains a level, whichever comes first. During this
30-day period, the iron tyrant takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls.

**Standard:** An iron tyrant can enhance his armor by calling upon a fiendish spirit's aid. This
bond lasts for 1 minute per antipaladin level. When called, the spirit causes the armor to shed
unholy light like a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch).

**At 5th Level**

**Action:** Ability

**At 13th Level:** The iron tyrant can use this three times a day.

**At 17th Level:** The iron tyrant can use this four times a day.

**At 9th Level:** The iron tyrant can use this twice a day.

**Special:** The bonus and special abilities granted by the spirit are determined when the spirit is
called and cannot be changed until the spirit is called again. The fiendish spirit imparts no
bonuses if the armor is worn by anyone other than the iron tyrant, but it resumes giving bonuses if
the iron tyrant dons the armor again.

**Usage:** Once per day.

**Usage:** Once per day.

The spirit grants the armor a +1 enhancement bonus. For every 3 antipaladin levels beyond 5th, the
armor gains another +1 enhancement bonus, to a maximum of +6 at 20th level. These bonuses stack with
existing armor enhancement bonuses to a maximum of +5, or they can be used to add any of the
following armor special abilities. Adding these special abilities consumes an amount of bonus equal
to the special ability's base price modifier (or +4 for _unrighteous_), as shown below. These
special abilities are added to any special abilities the armor already has, but duplicate abilities
do not stack. If the armor is not magical, at least a +1 enhancement bonus must be added before any
other special abilities can be added.

- +1: [dastard](ref/magic-enh/dastard)
- +1: [fortification (light)](ref/magic-enh/fortification)
- +2: [spell resistance 13](ref/magic-enh/spell-resistance)
- +3: [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-armor)
- +3: fortification (medium)
- +3: [invulnerability](ref/magic-enh/invulnerability)
- +3: [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) 15
- +4: [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) 17
- +4: [unrighteous](ref/magic-enh/unrighteous)
- +5: fortification (heavy)
- +5: [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) 19
