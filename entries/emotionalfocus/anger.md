**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 80\
Phantoms with this emotional focus are filled with seething anger from events in their past lives.
Phantoms with this focus often take the form of hulking brutes with furrowed brows or of frenzied
creatures that always seem ready to strike down those who come too near. Many times, these phantoms
exude a bright red aura, especially when they are engaged in combat, or they seem to breathe a red
mist in shallow pants from behind clenched, phantasmal teeth.

**Skills**

**Details:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in its spiritualist's
consciensness, it grants the spiritualist [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of those
skills.

**Good Saves**

**Details:** Fortitude and Will

**Strength Focus**

**Passive:** The phantom gains a +2 bonus to Strength and a -2 penalty to Dexterity. Instead of the
phantom gaining a bonus to Dexterity as the spiritualist gains levels, an anger-focused phantom
gains a bonus to Strength instead.

**Powerful Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** The phantom deals slam damage as a creature
[one size category](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) larger than its current size. The phantom also
gains [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack) as a bonus feat.

**Flavor:** A phantom with this focus deals more damage with its slam attacks.

**Aura of Fury (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Special:** The phantom can use this ability in either ectoplasmic or incorporeal form.

**Swift:** The phantom can emit a 20-foot-radius aura of fury. Creatures within the aura gain a +2
bonus on melee attack rolls but take a -2 penalty to AC. Ending the aura is a **free action**.

**Ferocious Mien (Su)**

**At 18th Level:** A phantom using _ferocious mien_ also gains the
[frightful presence](ref/umr/frightful-presence) extraordinary ability (range 30 feet, duration 5d6
rounds).

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Swift:** Once per day, a phantom in ectoplasmic form can grow more ferocious and frightening. It
becomes one size category larger than its current size, as affected by an
[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person) spell, and grows fiercer in combat, as if affected by a
[rage](ref/spell/rage) spell. This effect lasts for 1 round per class level of the spiritualist.

**Furious Wail (Su)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Special:** The phantom can use this ability in either ectoplasmic or incorporeal form.

**Standard:** Once per day, the phantom can emit a single angry wail that acts as
[wail of the banshee](ref/spell/wail-of-the-banshee). The phantom uses its Hit Dice as its caster
level for the effect, and the DC of the effect is 10 + 1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's
Charisma modifier.
