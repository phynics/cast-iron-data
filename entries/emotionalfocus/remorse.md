**Sources:** Occult Origins pg 19\
Spirits of creatures that died with heavy weights upon their consciences manifest with the remorse
emotional focus. These phantoms, whose forms are often darkened by shadows, force foes to experience
their regret and self-loathing. A spiritualist with the
[fated guide](ref/arc-spiritualist/fated-guide) archetype must choose remorse as her phantom's
emotional focus, but other spiritualists can also select this emotional focus for their phantoms.

**Skills**

**Details:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in [Perception](ref/skill/perception) and
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in its spiritualist's
consciensness, it grants the spiritualist [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of those
skills.

**Good Saves**

**Details:** Fortitude and Will

**Antagonistic**

**Ability:** The phantom gains [Antagonize](ref/feat/antagonize) as a bonus feat.

**Long-Suffering Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** When a phantom with this focus hits a creature with its slam attack, that creature must
succeed at a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma
modifier) or any attacks targeting the creature gain a +1 bonus on their attack rolls and damage
rolls until the beginning of the phantom's next turn. Penalties from multiple hits don't stack with
themselves.

**Aura of Regret (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Special:** The phantom can use this ability in either ectoplasmic or incorporeal form.

**Swift:** Her phantom can emit a 10-foot-radius aura of regret. Enemies within the aura take a -2
penalty on skill checks, on caster level checks, on combat maneuver checks, and to CMD. Ending the
aura is a **free action**.

**Keening (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Special:** The phantom can use this ability in either ectoplasmic or incorporeal form.

**Standard:** Her phantom can wail about its misdeeds once per day, affecting all creatures within a
30-foot cone with the [terrible remorse](ref/spell/terrible-remorse) spell. The phantom uses its Hit
Dice as its caster level for the effect, and the DC equals 10 + 1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the
phantom's Charisma modifier.

**Utter Misery (Su)**

**Ability:** Her phantom's _long-suffering strike_ also causes a creature that fails its save to
become [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) until the beginning of the phantom's next turn.

**Gained:** At 17th Level
