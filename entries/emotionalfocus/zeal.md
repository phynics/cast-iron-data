**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 83\
A phantom with this emotional focus fixates on every task given as if it were the phantom's last.
The most basic commands are treated as life-and-death situations, and the truly dangerous ones are
faced with a resolve and tenacity that sometimes defies common sense. Zeal phantoms take the form of
steadfast protectors or daring and manic creatures looking for the next challenge or opportunity to
prove itself to either its master or itself. Often very prideful creatures, these phantoms display
an orange aura upon completing their tasks. They are prone to boast about their accomplishments and
chastise those around them for not accomplishing more.

**Skills**

**Details:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in its spiritualist's
consciousness, it grants the spiritualist [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of those
skills.

**Good Saves**

**Details:** Fortitude and Reflex

**Tracking**

**Passive:** The phantom adds half its number of Hit Dice (minimum 1) to
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) skill checks to follow tracks.

**Ruthless Combatant (Su)**

**Ability:** The phantom threatens a critical hit with its slam attacks on a roll of 19-20.

**At 11th Level:** The phantom's critical modifier with slam attacks increases to &times&3. This
doesn't stack with [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) or similar effects.

**Determination Aura (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Special:** The phantom can use this ability in either ectoplasmic or incorporeal form.

**Swift:** The phantom can emit a 20-foot-radius aura that grants its zeal to nearby allies. Allies
within the aura gain a +2 competence bonus on attack rolls and saving throws.

**Steadfast Servant (Su)**

**Ability:** The phantom can stay manifested even when its spiritualist master is unconscious or
asleep.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Zeal's Resolve (Su)**

**Free:** Three times per day, when the phantom misses with an attack roll or fails a saving throw,
it can reroll the failed attack or saving throw. It must take the new result, even if that result is
worse.

**Gained:** At 17th Level
