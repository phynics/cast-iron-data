**Sources:** Occult Realms pg 14\
A phantom with this focus aches for the intoxicating pleasures it experienced during life. These
phantoms often writhe sensuously and whisper promises of unadulterated bliss to those they fight.
Lust phantoms are typically deep rose in color, and they sometimes emit musky, exotic scents when
they move.

**Skills**

**Details:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in its spiritualist's
consciensness, it grants the spiritualist [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of those
skills.

**Good Saves**

**Details:** Fortitude and Will

**Constitution Focus**

**Passive:** A phantom with this focus gains a +2 bonus to Constitution and a -2 penalty to
Dexterity.

**Special:** Instead of gaining a bonus to Dexterity as the spiritualist gains levels, a
lust-focused phantom gains a bonus to Constitution instead.

**Alluring Presence (Su)**

**Flavor:** A phantom with this focus can coerce enemies to attack it.

**Immediate:** The phantom attempts a Diplomacy check against a creature targeting the spiritualist
with an attack or harmful spell, provided the phantom is within the target creature's reach (or
range, if the creature is attacking with a ranged weapon or spell). The DC for this check equals
10 + 1/2 the attacking creature's HD + the attacking creature's Wisdom modifier. If successful, the
phantom becomes the target of all the creature's attacks for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting
compulsion emotion effect.

**Mine Alone (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** Whenever the spiritualist or the phantom is subject to a charm or compulsion effect,
the target of the effect can roll twice for its save - once using the spiritualist's Will save
modifier and once using the phantom's - and take the better result.

**Aura of Ecstasy (Su)**

**Ability:** The lust phantom emanates an aura of ecstasy. All creatures adjacent to the phantom
must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma modifier)
or be [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) and [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) each round that they remain
within the _aura of ecstasy._ Creatures that succeed at this save are immune to this phantom's _aura
of ecstasy_ for 24 hours.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Sinful Command (Su)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Standard:** Once per day, the phantom can attempt to assume control of another creature's mind as
per the [dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster) spell. The DC of the effect is equal to 10 +
1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma modifier.
