A phantom with this emotional focus was a being with a generous heart in life and continues to
remain that way well after death. The phantom's intense generosity compels it to remain a phantom
and continue assisting the living, especially relatives or those who had been its good friends
during its living years. Kindness phantoms have pleasant and gentle demeanors and speak with a
melodic cadence, putting most who see them at ease. Their auras are bright emerald green with
occasional fluctuating scarlet or golden hues.

**Skills**

**Details:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Heal](ref/skill/heal) equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in its spiritualist's consciensness, it
grants the spiritualist [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of those skills.

**Good Saves**

**Details:** Fortitude and Will

**Benevolent**

**Ability:** When the phantom uses the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action, the bonus granted
by a successful check increases by 1. If the phantom aids the spiritualist this way, the bonus
instead increases by 2.

**Opening Strike (Su)**

**Standard:** The phantom can make a melee attack against a foe. If this attack hits, the
spiritualist designates one ally threatening that enemy. This ally can make a single attack at his
full base attack bonus against that enemy as an **immediate action**.

**Etheric Healing (Su)**

**Ability:** The phantom gains the [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands) ability with an
effective paladin level equal to its Hit Dice. The phantom can use this ability on the spiritualist
as a **swift action**.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Expanded Aid (Su)**

**Ability:** The phantom can use the aid another action as a **move action**. Additionally, the
phantom selects four mercies available to a 12th-level paladin that it adds to its _lay on hands_
ability.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Exceptional Aid (Su)**

**Ability:** The phantom can use the aid another action as a **swift action**. Additionally, when
designating an ally for an _opening strike,_ that ally gains a bonus equal to the phantom's Charisma
modifier on damage rolls for the attack granted to him.

**Gained:** At 17th Level
