Some creatures die in such horrific ways, or live such pointless and senseless lives, that despair
grips their very beings. Phantoms with this focus use misery as a weapon, inflicting the living with
the gloom of the phantoms' continued existence. Despair phantoms often appear twisted or wounded,
showing the grisly circumstances of their demise. Their coloration tends to have a grayish or sickly
green cast. When they speak, they do so in terrifying whispers or high-pitched screeches.

**Skills**

**Details:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) and
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in its spiritualist's
consciensness, it grants the spiritualist [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of those
skills.

**Good Saves**

**Details:** Fortitude and Will

**Power from Despair**

**Passive:** The phantom gains a +2 bonus on attack and damage rolls against creatures that are
shaken, frightened, panicked, cowering, or subject to effects such as _aura of despair_ or
_[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair)._

**Miserable Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** If the phantom hits a creature with a slam attack, that creature must succeed at a Will
saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma modifier) or take a -2
penalty on attack and damage rolls for 1 round.

**Special:** This is a mind-affecting fear and emotion effect. Penalties from multiple hits don't
stack with themselves.

**Aura of Despair (Su)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Special:** This is a fear effect. Deactivating the aura is a **free action**.

**Swift:** The phantom can emit a 10-foot aura of despair. Enemies within the aura take a -2 penalty
on all saving throws.

**Despairing Shout (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Special:** The phantom can use this ability in either ectoplasmic or incorporeal form.

**Standard:** Three times per day, the phantom can emit a shout that acts as
[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair). The phantom uses its Hit Dice as its caster level
for the effect, and the DC of the effect equals 10 + 1/2 the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's
Charisma modifier.

**Inescapable Despair (Su)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** If the phantom hits with its slam attack, the creature hit doesn't get a save to resist
the effects of _miserable strike._
