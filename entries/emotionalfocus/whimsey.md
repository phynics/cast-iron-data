**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe pg 17\
Phantoms with this emotional focus carry a simplistic, childlike wonder and are fond of games and
playing tricks. Whimsical phantoms are often the ghosts of [gnomes](ref/race/gnome); in the case of
bleachling spiritualists, the phantom may even be a psychic vestige of the bleachling's own lost
childlike nature. Among other races, most phantoms with the whimsy focus are the spirits of those
who died as children and carried their childlike nature into the afterlife.

**Skills**

**Details:** The phantom gains a number of ranks in [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) and
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) equal to its Hit Dice. While confined in its spiritualist's
consciensness, it grants the spiritualist [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) in each of those
skills.

**Good Saves**

**Details:** Reflex and Will

**Tricky Combatant**

**Ability:** A phantom with the whimsy focus gains
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick) as a bonus feat, even if it does not meet the
prerequisites.

**Colorful Burst (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, a phantom with this focus can produce an explosion of bright colors in a
15-foot cone. This functions as the spell [color spray](ref/spell/color-spray), except that
creatures with fewer HD than the phantom are treated as if they had 2 HD or less, creatures with HD
equal to that of the phantom are treated as if they had 3 or 4 HD, and creatures with more HD than
the phantom are treated as if they had 5 or more HD. A creature can negate this effect with a
successful Will saving throw (DC = 10 + half the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma
modifier).

**Aura of Laughter (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Swift:** The phantom can emit a 10-foot aura of childish laughter. Enemies within the aura are
distracted by the laughter, taking a -2 penalty on attack and damage rolls and a -5 penalty on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks. This ability has no effect on creatures that cannot hear.

**Telekinetic Prankster (Sp)**

**Ability:** The phantom can perform combat maneuvers against creatures up to 15 feet away. When
doing so, it can use its Charisma modifier in place of its Strength modifier on its combat maneuver
checks.

**Flavor:** The phantom gains the ability to use limited telekinesis to trip up its foes.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Invoke Laughter (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Special:** The phantom can use this ability in either ectoplasmic or incorporeal form.

**Standard:** Once per day, the phantom can cause nearby creatures to fall into fits of
uncontrollable laughter. Each creature in a 30-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) centered on the
phantom must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half the phantom's Hit Dice + the phantom's Charisma
modifier) or begin laughing uncontrollably, as the spell
[hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter).
