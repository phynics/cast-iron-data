**Sources:** Inner Sea Magic pg 40\
The tattooed sorcerer has embraced the ancient traditions of Varisian spellcasting, and uses
colorful and intricate tattoos to enhance her magical powers. A tattooed sorcerer has the following
class features.

**Familiar Tattoo (Su)**

**Ability:** A tattooed sorcerer gains a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) as an _arcane bond,_ as a
[wizard](ref/class/wizard) equal to her sorcerer level. Her sorcerer levels stack with any wizard or
[witch](ref/class/witch) levels she possesses when determining the powers of her
[familiar](ref/ability/familiars) - this ability does not allow her to have both a familiar and a
bonded item.

**Ability2:** Unlike most familiars, her familiar can transform itself into a tattoo that she
carries in her flesh. Transforming into a tattoo or back to normal familiar form is a **move
action** for her familiar.

**Replaces:** 1st-level bloodline power

**Special:** In tattoo form, the familiar looks like a stylized version of itself, but does not
count as a creature separate from the tattooed sorcerer. In tattoo form it continues to grant its
special familiar ability, but otherwise has no abilities and can take no actions except to transform
from tattoo into creature. A familiar tattoo cannot be erased or dispelled.

**Varisian Tattoo (Ex)**

**Ability:** The tattooed sorcerer gains [Varisian Tattoo](ref/feat/varisian-tattoo) as a bonus
feat. If she doesn't have [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus), she may choose which school of magic
her Varisian Tattoo enhances.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Eschew Materials bonus feat

**Bloodline Tattoos (Ex)**

**Passive:** Whenever a tattooed sorcerer gains a bloodline spell, a new tattoo manifests on her
body to represent this spell. Her bloodline spells are always enhanced by her Varisian Tattoo feat,
even if they don't match the school to which her Varisian Tattoo belongs.

**Create Spell Tattoo (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** She can use this ability twice per day.

**At 15th Level:** She can use this ability three times per day.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** 7th-level bloodline feat

**Special:** If she gives the spell tattoo to herself, it does not count against the regular limit
of magic tattoos she can have. The spell tattoo must be of a spell that she knows that has no
material or focus component. She can maintain one spell tattoo created by this ability at a time -
if she uses this ability again, the previous spell tattoo she created fades away. Spell tattoos she
creates with [Inscribe Magical Tattoo](ref/feat/inscribe-magical-tattoo) do not count against this
limit.

**Standard:** A tattooed sorcerer can create a [spell tattoo](ref/magic-tattoo/spell-tattoo) once
per day with a single touch. The recipient of the spell tattoo must be willing to receive the spell
tattoo.

**Enhanced Varisian Tattoo (Su)**

**Ability:** The tattooed sorcerer can pick any one spell she knows for which she has a Varisian
Tattoo feat. This spell must be one that lacks focus components and costly material components. She
can now use that spell as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day. This
spell-like ability is not enhanced by her Varisian Tattoo, but it functions at +2 caster levels
above her sorcerer caster level.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** 9th-level bloodline power

**Special:** Whenever she gains a bloodline power at a later date, she may change this
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) to another qualifying spell.
