The blood of dragons not only flows through a dragon drinker's veins, but also empowers her spells
and magical abilities when consumed. Dragon drinkers have the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** [Draconic](ref/sorcbloodline/draconic) bloodline

**Bleeding Spells (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a dragon drinker casts a spell that deals damage, the spell deals 1 point of
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage per spell level to any creatures of the dragon type damaged.

**Replaces:** Bloodline arcana

**Blood Drinking (Su)**

**Ability:** A dragon drinker gains the ability to drink [dragon's blood](ref/eq-misc/dragons-blood)
to alter or augment her spellcasting abilities. For the purposes of harvesting dragon's blood, treat
the dragon drinker as though she has [Dragoncrafting](ref/feat/dragoncrafting) feat.

**At 11th Level:** The alchemical bonus on Fortitude and Reflex saves granted by the blood increases
to +2.

**At 5th Level:** Drinking a vial of dragon's blood is a **standard action** that provides the
dragon drinker with the normal benefits of the item, as well as a +1 bonus to her caster level for
purposes of level-dependent spell effects on a single spell cast within 1 minute.

**At 7th Level:** A consumed vial of dragon blood also heals 1d8 points of damage instead of the
standard 1d4.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** 1st-level bloodline power

**Special:** In the absence of true dragon's blood, the dragon drinker may tap into the draconic
blood flowing through her veins to gain the temporary effects of having consumed dragon's blood. As
a **free action**, she can activate this ability, healing 1d4 points of damage and gaining a +1
resistance bonus on Will and Fortitude saves for 1 round. This action may only be performed once per
round, and can be performed a number of times per day equal to 3 + her Charisma modifier.

**Energy Assimilation (Su)**

**Ability:** When the dragon drinker drinks true dragon's blood from a dragon with a breath weapon
that deals a different type of energy damage than her chosen energy type, she may adopt the energy
resistance of the blood's source dragon as if it were her selected bloodline. This effect lasts for
1 minute per dose of dragon's blood, after which the dragon drinker's energy resistance reverts back
to her normal energy type.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** 7th-level bloodline feat

**Breath Mimicry (Su)**

**Ability:** When the dragon drinker drinks true dragon's blood from a dragon with a breath weapon
of a shape or energy type different from her own, she may use the shape and energy type of the
blood's source dragon when she next uses her breath weapon.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** 13th-level bloodline feat

**Blood Siphon (Su)**

**Ability:** Additionally, when she consumes dragon's blood, it heals 3d8 points of damage and
provides a +3 resistance bonus on Fortitude and Will saves for 1 minute in place of its normal
effects.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** 19th-level bloodline feat

**Swift:** When the dragon drinker is within 60 feet of a creature of the dragon type that is
suffering bleed damage, she can draw a dose of its blood into herself as though she had consumed it
from a vial.
