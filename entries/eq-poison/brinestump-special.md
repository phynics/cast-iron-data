**Price** 400 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Onset:** 1 round

**Frequency:** No frequency was provided in the source

**Effect:** nauseated for 5 rounds

**Cure:** 1 save

Invented as part of a series of dares between feuding [goblin](ref/monster/goblin) camps, this
noxious brew fermented from a slurry of swamp flora is a favored concoction of the Licktoad tribe.
The potent intoxicant is popular among goblinoids (who are immune to its nauseating effect), but all
other races that partake of this frothy drink are wracked with nausea.
