**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** 1 round

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** impaired healing (see description)

**Cure:** 2 saves

This gritty black paste is a poison that interferes with an afflicted creature's ability to recover
from injuries. All Heal checks applied to the creature suffer a -10 penalty. In addition, anyone
using magical healing on the target must make a DC 25 caster level check to succeed.
