**Sources:** Rival Guide pg 53

**Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Contact, injury, ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 12

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** Dexterity damage 1d2

**Cure:** 1 save

A creature who takes Dexterity damage equal to its Dexterity score from this poison becomes
petrified; each day, a petrified victim can attempt a new DC 12 Fortitude save to recover from the
venom and become unpetrified with an amount of Dexterity damage equal to 1 point less than its
Dexterity score.
