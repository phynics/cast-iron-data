**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 24

**Price** 250 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** 1d2 Wis damage. Additionally, the creature has double vision, imposing a 50% miss chance
on its attacks against all opponents. Blind creatures are unaffected by this additional effect

**Cure:** 1 save

[Pei Zin herbalists](ref/rule/pei-zin-herbalism) accidentally created diplopic serum while
attempting to devise a treatment for cataracts. Derived from dew of lunary, this aromatic pink serum
temporarily affects a creature's vision systems, in addition to temporarily impairing its mental
faculties.
