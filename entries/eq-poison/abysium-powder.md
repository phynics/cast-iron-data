**Sources:** Pathfinder \#61: Shards of Sin pg 70

**Price** 900 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** 10 minutes

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 6 minutes

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d4 and nausea

**Cure:** 2 saves

[Abysium](ref/eq-material/abysium) can be powdered and alchemically distilled with other rare
catalysts and chemicals to form a much more potent toxin. It was in this form that the metal was
most traditionally used in ancient Thassilon. A pound of Abysium is enough to make 1 dose of abysium
powder.
