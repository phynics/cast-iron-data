**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 25

**Price** 750 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day for 7 days

**Effect:** 1 Wis damage. The creature looks younger by as much as half his age for a duration of 1
day. The younger appearance is purely cosmetic and has no mechanical effect

**Cure:** 1 save

Swindlers claim this green capsule restores the victim's youth. After 1 day, anyone taking juvebloom
sees the seemingly time-bending results in the mirror. However, the benefit is not only temporary
but also potentially quite damaging. Particularly daring confidence artists mix this capsule into a
solution, attempting to sell it as the much more valuable
[sun orchid elixir](ref/magic-artifact/sun-orchid-elixir).
