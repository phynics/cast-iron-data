**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 145

**Price** 2,600 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** 1d4 minutes

**Frequency:** 1/10 minutes for 60 minutes

**Effect:** Wisdom damage 1d4 and the creature loses its memories from the past 10 minutes, as
though they had been erased by the spell modify memory

**Cure:** 1 save

This milky-white sap can be harvested from a rare jungle tree known as a "weeping maiden" in the
local tongue. The tree's branches end in long, vine-like tendrils that hang low to the ground and
bear large, bitter-tasting red fruit. While the fruit is safe to eat, the sap of the tree carries
powerful neurotoxins that cause short-term memory loss in those that consume it. In some cultures,
styx sap is occasionally taken deliberately in order to allow one to forget unpleasant or tragic
events.
