**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 145, Heroes of the Wild pg 20

**Price** 800 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 13

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 6 minutes

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d2 and Constitution damage 1d2

**Cure:** 1 save

The putrefying essence of a [violet fungus](ref/monster/violet-fungus) must be harvested with great
care from a living specimen and processed quickly before it loses potency. Numerous kinds of
subterranean creatures harvest this toxin to help them in their hunts for food, and they coat their
weapons in the poison to help defend their underground settlements.
