**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 24

**Price** 300 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 17

**Onset:** 10 minutes

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 6 minutes

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d2 and 1d3 bleed

**Cure:** 2 saves

Blackfingers's salt can easily be hidden in all foods and beverages, as it lacks color, odor, and,
despite the name, flavor. Soon after consuming this dastardly poison, a creature develops painful,
bleeding ulcers throughout its stomach and esophagus. Followers of [Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber)
originally developed the salt to mimic targets suffering a medical emergency and thus avoid
suspicion of poisoning.
