**Price** 500 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** immediate

**Frequency:** 1/round for 2 rounds

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d2

**Cure:** 1 save

A skilled alchemist can collect and preserve the juices from a
[copsewight's](ref/monster/copsewight) fruit, distilling them into a contact poison. The fruit from
a single copsewight can produce up to 12 doses of poison.
