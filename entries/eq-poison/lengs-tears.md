**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 18

**Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Onset:** 1 round

**Frequency:** 1/10 minutes for 1 hour

**Effect:** Wisdom damage 1d2 and paralyzed for 10 minutes

**Cure:** 1 save

Made from the distilled tears and sweat of [denizens of Leng](ref/monster/denizen-of-leng), this
substance causes hallucinations and temporary paralysis. A creature that fails its initial saving
throw is paralyzed and experiences terrifying hallucinations of wandering through an icy, windswept
waste. The creature's paralysis ends if it is cured or once the poison runs its course, though
memories of the vivid hallucinations may haunt it for some time.
