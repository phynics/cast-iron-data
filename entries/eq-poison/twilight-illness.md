**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 51

**Price** 750 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 19

**Onset:** eating food

**Frequency:** 1/hour for 8 hours

**Effect:** nauseated for 1 hour and then sickened for 1 day

**Cure:** 1 save

This poison is used by [Twilight Talons](ref/pclass/twilight-talon) to render targets too ill to
work for a day - long enough to impersonate them.
