**Price** 200 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Frequency:** 1/round for 2 rounds

**Initial effect:** Constitution damage 1

**Secondary effect:** nausea for 1d4 rounds

**Cure:** 1 save

Black whinnis is a poison made from the same red creeper vine that produces the better-known
[blue whinnis](ref/eq-poison/blue-whinnis) poison, but the distilled sap from the blue whinnis
thorns is further refined using a slightly more volatile process.
