**Price** 600 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 18

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** uncontrollble laughter (as per hideous laughter)

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

The short and thorny jackalroot grows in desert climates, typically under the partial shade of
ridges or boulders. It is identifiable by its pale green berries, which it bears twice per year. To
followers of [Lamashtu](ref/faith/lamashtu), the gnarled, prickle-covered jackalroot is a blessing.
The roots of this hearty desert shrub contain a potent, incapacitating ichor often used by gnolls in
religious ceremonies and when capturing slaves.
