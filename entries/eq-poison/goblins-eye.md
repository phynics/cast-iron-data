**Price** 75 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 13

**Onset:** 1 minute

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 6 minutes

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1 and the creature is nauseated for 1 minute

**Cure:** 1 save

These bright red seeds feature a single black dot each, giving them a superficial resemblance to an
eye. The bright colors of the seeds make them attractive to certain animals, as well as those
unfamiliar with wilderness lore. A character who succeeds at a DC 15 Knowledge (nature) or Survival
check identifies the seed as poisonous. In some places, the seeds are used as jewelry.
