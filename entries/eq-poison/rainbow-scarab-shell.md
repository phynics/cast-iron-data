**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Onset:** 1 round

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d4 and suffocation (see text)

**Cure:** 1 save

This iridescent toxin is made from the crushed shells of a rare type of Osirian beetle. A creature
reduced to 0 Strength by the poison asphyxiates and immediately begins
[suffocating](ref/rule/suffocation). Creatures that do not breathe are immune to this suffocation,
but not the Strength damage.
