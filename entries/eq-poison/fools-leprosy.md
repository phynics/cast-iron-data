**Sources:** Curse of the Crimson Throne pg 432

**Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 20

**Onset:** 1 day

**Frequency:** 1/day for 6 days

**Effect:** Constitution drain 1d2 and Charisma damage 1d2

**Cure:** 3 consecutive saves

This cunning supernatural venom was originally engineered by [Red Mantis](ref/rule/aff-assassins)
alchemists. Fool's leprosy is a slow-acting but ultimately fatal ingested toxin that, once it takes
hold in its victim's system, closely mimics the effects of a fast-moving form of
[leprosy](ref/disease/leprosy). An attempt to divine its nature via
[detect poison](ref/spell/detect-poison) fails unless the caster succeeds at a DC 25 caster level
check, and use of a spell like [diagnose disease](ref/spell/diagnose-disease) always misidentifies
the venom as a disease unless the caster succeeds at a DC 25 caster level check. In either event,
the GM should roll for these caster level checks in secret.
[Neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison) and [delay poison](ref/spell/delay-poison) function
normally on a person suffering from fool's leprosy, but spells like
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease) do not, as the condition is a poison effect, not a
disease effect. [Heal](ref/spell/heal) can cure the effects of fool's leprosy but does nothing to
educate the caster about the nature of the effect it removes. As long as the truth about fool's
leprosy remains hidden, a successful DC 35 Heal check is required to recognize that someone
suffering from its effects is affected by poison, not a disease.
