**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 51

**Price** 150 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

**Type:** Poison (Inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 17

**Onset:** injury or affected by any fear condition or effect

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 4 minutes

**Initial effect:** shaken for 10 minutes (this can't produce a more severe fear effect)

**Secondary effect:** Wisdom damage 1d2

**Cure:** 1 save

This poison is the creation of the Guild of Wonders in Absalom, whose members hunt down and
intimidate their enemies.
