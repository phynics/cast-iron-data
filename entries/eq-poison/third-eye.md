**Price** 900 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Contact)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 17

**Onset:** 1 minute

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Wisdom damage 1d3

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Each time the victim takes Wisdom damage, it must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or become confused
for that round.

This poison is produced in the liver of a particular species of water-dwelling lizard and produces
potent hallucinations. In some places, the lizards are raised as livestock specifically for the
harvesting of the poison, which is then used for ritual ceremonies and recreational purposes. People
in some cultures voluntarily dose themselves with this poison as a way to gain oracular insights
into the world, but more often than not the revelations are nothing but subtle nightmares and lack
true insight.
