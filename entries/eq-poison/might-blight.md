**Price** 60 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 13

**Onset:** 1 round

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

The [ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk) of Diguo-Dashu, the "Empire of the Rats," developed this poison to
help their soldiers in overtaking foes. The poison produces lactic acids that slowly degrade the
target's muscles, though for full efficacy, an exceptionally high dosage, repeated application, or a
particularly feeble opponent is required.
