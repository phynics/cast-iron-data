**Price** 750 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 20

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Wisdom damage 1d4+1

**Cure:** 2 saves

The poison muddling heresy is often used by worshipers of [Geryon](ref/faith/geryon) to make others
more susceptible to being convinced of blasphemies and revelations of heretical truths. While the
poison can be used on its own, when used as part of a deific boon, the save DC, duration, or other
effects may differ from normal.
