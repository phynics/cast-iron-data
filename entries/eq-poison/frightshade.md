**Sources:** Antihero's Handbook pg 29

**Price** 100 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** This plant's pollen contains a powerful hallucinogen that causes affected creatures to
experience terribly realistic visions of death and destruction. The target is shaken until the
beginning of her next turn. When the target succeeds at her saving throw against the poison, or when
the initial duration ends, she is shaken for an additional 1d4 rounds. (This is not a fear effect.)

**Cure:** 1 save
