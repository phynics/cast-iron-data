**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 145, Heroes of the Wild pg 20

**Price** 1,200 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Ingested)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Onset:** 10 minutes

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 10 minutes

**Effect:** confusion

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

Named for the scab-red mushrooms from which it is made, this potent hallucinogen is brewed by evil
druids and witches in remote regions in order to enhance the savagery of their seasonal revels. Some
use this poison to debilitate their victims for ritual sacrifice.
