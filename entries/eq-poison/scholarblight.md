**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 22

**Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** —

**Type:** Poison (Inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 6 minutes

**Effect:** 1d3 Int damage, see text

**Cure:** 1 save

Created by the [Whispering Way](ref/rule/f-gravepath) to simultaneously protect and bind newcomers
and servants who have handled dangerous writings, scholarblight has long since become a preferred
poison for those who target arcane spellcasters for assassination. Scholarblight causes headaches
and impairs a victim's ability to form or recall memories through mild confusion and dysphoria. More
drastically, victims who fail a saving throw against scholarblight lose their ability to read or
write for 24 hours, as letters and symbols seem to twist, move, and reorient themselves. This
prevents victims from using scrolls and spellbooks, but also protects an afflicted creature from any
effect dependent on reading text, such as [explosive runes](ref/spell/explosive-runes).
