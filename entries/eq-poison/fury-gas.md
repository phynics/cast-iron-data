**Sources:** Alchemy Manual pg 7

**Price** 900 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

**Type:** Poison (Inhaled)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 16

**Frequency:** 1/minute for 4 minutes

**Effect:** Non-orcs are blinded for 1 minute. Orcs rage for 1 minute, gaining a +2 alchemical bonus
to Strength and Dexterity, a +1 alchemical bonus on Will saves, and a -2 penalty to AC. This
poison's effects are otherwise similar to a barbarian's rage, except that affected orcs are unable
to discern friend from foe - attacking the nearest creature each round - and they are not fatigued
afterward

**Cure:** 2 saves

**[Spontaneous Alchemy](ref/rule/spontaneous-alchemy) Recipe** (180 brimstone + 80 quicksilver + 60
saltpeter)/fermentation; **Craft** DC 16\
**Time** 1 day; **Tools** heat source; **Type** poison
