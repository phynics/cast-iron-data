**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 63\
**Yield** 1,000; **Fort** +2; **[Hardness](ref/umr/hardness)** 10; **HP** 1,500; **Explosion** 20d10
bludgeoning and 20d10 fire plus severe [radiation](ref/rule/radiation) (250-ft. radius; Reflex DC 20
half); **Perception Penalty** -10

A fission reactor harnesses power from nuclear energy gathered by processing and consuming
radioactive fuel. Once activated, a fission reactor can function for many years before its
radioactive fuel must be replaced. The core of a fission reactor is constantly irradiated at a high
level of radioactivity; the bulk of the reactor actually consists of shielding meant to protect
those nearby from [radiation poisoning](ref/rule/radiation). If a fission generator explodes, it
inundates its blast radius with severe radiation. These generators are relatively rare: while they
produce the highest yield of all generators, the incredible damage they can do if they do explode
often means that safety-minded operators find them suitable only for remote, unmanned operations.
