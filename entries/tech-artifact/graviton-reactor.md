**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 63\
**Yield** 800; **Fort** +14; **[Hardness](ref/umr/hardness)** 20; **HP** 2,500; **Explosion** 10d6
bludgeoning, 10d6 electricity, and 20d6 force (50-ft. radius; Reflex DC 20 half; see text);
**Perception Penalty** -2

A graviton reactor produce tremendous amounts of energy. It's fairly quiet while running, generating
little more than a low-frequency hum. Damage caused by a graviton reactor explosion consists of an
implosion rather than a classic explosion. On the round following this implosion, all objects and
creatures in the blast radius suffer the explosion damage again as they are then hurled back
outward. No radiation is generated by a graviton reactor, and creatures and objects outside the
blast radius can escape relatively unharmed (apart from possible damage resulting from objects that
are hurled beyond the blast radius).
