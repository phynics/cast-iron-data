### Lazurite Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** [Lazurite](ref/eq-material/lazurite) radiation increases the victim's susceptibility to
the attacks of undead creatures that are bolstered by the radiation. The target takes a -2 penalty
to Armor Class against the attacks of undead creatures and on saving throws against effects created
by undead. This effect lasts for 10 minutes per ranger level. A successful Will save negates this
effect.\~\~\~If this is an extraordinary trap, the ranger must supply a sliver of lazurite.
