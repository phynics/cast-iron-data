### Swarm Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The trap releases a [bat swarm](ref/monster/bat-bat-swarm),
[rat swarm](ref/monster/rat-rat-swarm), or [spider swarm](ref/monster/spider-spider-swarm) that
attacks all creatures in the area. The swarm remains in the general area for no longer than 1 round
per ranger level, after which it disperses.\~\~\~If the trap is an extraordinary trap, the ranger
must provide the creatures of the swarm (typically in a small box, cage, or crate); these creatures
may die of starvation before the trap is triggered (an extraordinary trap lasts 1 day per level).
