### Cleansing Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The trap suppresses the target's ability to use one randomly selected attack, ability,
or quality that creates a disease or poison effect; this lasts for 1 round per ranger level. If the
creature has more than one such attack, ability, or quality that creates a disease or poison effect,
the attack, ability, or quality to be suppressed when the creature is affected by this trap is
determined at random from those not already suppressed.
