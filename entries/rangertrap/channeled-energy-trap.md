### Channeled Energy Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** This trap creates a positive or negative energy effect designed to harm either undead
or living creatures, respectively. The target takes a number of points of damage equal to 1d8 + the
ranger's level from either positive energy or negative energy (Will save for half).\~\~\~If this is
an extraordinary trap, the ranger must supply a vial of [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water)
(positive energy) or unholy water (negative energy).
