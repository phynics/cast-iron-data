### Toxic Fumes Trap (Ex or Su)

**Modification:**

**Details:** A ranger can add this to a [smoke trap](ref/rangertrap/smoke-trap).\~\~\~The smoke this
trap creates is extremely noxious. Any breathing creature must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw
(in addition to the Fortitude save for the smoke trap) or be [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) every
round it's within the smoke and for 1d4+1 rounds after leaving the smoke.\~\~\~If this is an
extraordinary trap, the ranger must use a toxic material when setting the trap, such as
[noxious aromatic](ref/eq-weapon/noxious-aromatic-flask).
