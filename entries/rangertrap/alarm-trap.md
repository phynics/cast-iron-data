### Alarm Trap (Ex or Su)

::: statblock
Type: mechanical or magical
Perception: DC 10 + 1/2 Ranger level + Wisdom bonus (-2 if mechanical)
Disable Device: DC 10 + 1/2 Ranger level + Wisdom bonus (-2 if mechanical)
Trigger: location
Reset: none
:::

**Effect:** When the trap is triggered, it also creates a momentary loud noise equivalent to the
audible alarm version of [alarm](ref/spell/alarm).
