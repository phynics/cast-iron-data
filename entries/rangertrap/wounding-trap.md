### Wounding Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** This trap makes a melee attack against the target using the ranger's base attack
bonus + his Wisdom modifier for the attack roll. It deals an amount of damage equal to 1d6 + 1/2 the
ranger's level. The ranger chooses whether this damage is bludgeoning, piercing, or
slashing.\~\~\~If this is a supernatural trap, the attack counts as magical for the purposes of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
