### Selective Trigger (Ex or Su)

**Modification:**

**Details:** The ranger adds a race, type, alignment, or minimum weight restriction to the trap's
trigger.\~\~\~If this is an extraordinary trap, the ranger can choose only the minimum weight
restriction.

**Magical Component:**

**Details:** _Potion of [identify](ref/spell/identify)_
