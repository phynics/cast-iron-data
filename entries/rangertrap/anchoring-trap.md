### Anchoring Trap (Su)

**Modification:**

**Details:** This trap can be added to a supernatural
[snare trap](ref/rangertrap/snare-trap).\~\~\~The trap briefly imprisons any called creature (such
as those called by the [planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding) spells) for 1d4 rounds (Will
negates). During this time the creature may not move from the location of the trap, though it may
still use ranged effects and attack any creature within its reach.\~\~\~Additionally, anchoring
traps disable the creature's ability to travel within or between dimensions. This includes casting
[astral projection](ref/spell/astral-projection), [blink](ref/spell/blink),
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door), [ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt),
[etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness), [gate](ref/spell/gate), [maze](ref/spell/maze),
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift), [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk),
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport), [teleportation circle](ref/spell/teleportation-circle), and similar
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The trap does not prevent the creature from
using magic items that produce these effects, like [cubic gates](ref/magic-wondrous/cubic-gate),
[helms of teleportation](ref/magic-wondrous/helm-of-teleportation), or
[amulets of the planes](ref/magic-wondrous/amulet-of-the-planes).\~\~\~The trap can hold up to a
Medium creature; each extra daily use of the ranger's trap ability spent when the trap is set
increases the maximum size of creature the trap can hold by one size category.
