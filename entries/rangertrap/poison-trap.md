**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 64

### Poison Trap (Ex or Su)

::: statblock
Type: mechanical or magical
Perception: DC 10 + 1/2 Ranger level + Wisdom bonus (-2 if mechanical)
Disable Device: DC 10 + 1/2 Ranger level + Wisdom bonus (-2 if mechanical)
Trigger: location
Reset: none
:::

**Effect:** The trap poisons the creature that triggers it. If it is a supernatural trap, the poison
deals 1d2 [Con damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per round for 6 rounds. If it is an
extraordinary trap, the ranger must provide 1 dose of contact, inhaled, or injury poison when
setting the trap, and the trap uses that poison's effects and DC.
