### Poison Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The trap poisons the creature that triggers it. If it is a supernatural trap, the
poison deals 1d2 [Con damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per round for 6 rounds. If it is an
extraordinary trap, the ranger must provide 1 dose of contact, inhaled, or injury poison when
setting the trap, and the trap uses that poison's effects and DC.
