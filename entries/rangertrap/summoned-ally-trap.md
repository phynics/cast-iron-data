### Summoned Ally Trap (Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The trap summons a creature as
[summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1) with a caster level equal to the ranger's
level - 3.\~\~\~This trap functions as [summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2) at
8th level, [summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3) at 11th level, and
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4) at 14th level.\~\~\~The summoned creature
or creatures appear in a location within the trap's range designated when the trap is set; if it's
not possible for the creatures to appear in that location, they appear in the nearest unoccupied
space. The ranger can choose a command of no more than 25 words when the trap is created; the
creature or creatures follow those instructions, or otherwise attack the creature that triggered the
trap.
