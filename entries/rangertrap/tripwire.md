### Tripwire (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** A taut wire stretched between two vertical surfaces knocks the target
[prone](ref/rule/prone) unless it succeeds at a Reflex save. A [running](ref/rule/run) or
[charging](ref/rule/charge) creature takes a -6 penalty on its save.
