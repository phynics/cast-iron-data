### Penetrating Trap (Ex or Su)

**Modification:**

**Details:** A ranger can only add this to a [wounding trap](ref/rangertrap/wounding-trap).\~\~\~The
damage die of a penetrating trap increases to 1d8. Choose either
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine), [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron), or
[silver](ref/eq-material/alchemical-silver). The trap counts as that material for the purposes of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).\~\~\~If this is an extraordinary trap, the
ranger must provide 1 pound of the chosen material during the trap's construction.
