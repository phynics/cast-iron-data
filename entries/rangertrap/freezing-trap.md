### Freezing Trap (Su)

::: statblock
Type: magical
Perception: DC 10 + 1/2 Ranger level + Wisdom bonus
Disable Device: DC 10 + 1/2 Ranger level + Wisdom bonus
Trigger: location
Reset: none
:::

**Effect:** The trap creates a burst of ice that damages and encases the triggering creature. The
creature takes 1d3 points of cold damage + 1/4 the ranger's level and is
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) and stuck to the ground, similar to the effect of a
[tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag). A successful Reflex save halves the damage and means
the creature is not stuck to the floor by the ice. The ice is 1 inch thick per 2 Hit Dice of the
ranger (minimum 1), has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 0 and 3 hit points per inch of thickness, and
melts in 2d4 rounds.
