### Firework Trap (Ex or Su)

**Modification:**

**Details:** A ranger can add this augmentation to a [fire](ref/rangertrap/fire-trap) or
[smoke trap](ref/rangertrap/smoke-trap).\~\~\~The trap explodes in a flash of colored lights. All
creatures within 10 feet must succeed at a saving throw (Fortitude if an extraordinary firework
trap, Will if supernatural) or be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4+1 rounds.\~\~\~If this is an
extraordinary trap, the ranger must use an alchemical weapon when setting the trap, such as
[flash powder](ref/eq-weapon/flash-powder) or a firework.
