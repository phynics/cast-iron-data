### Pit Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** This simple pit is covered over with leaves or appropriate materials for the area. It's
5 feet deep plus 5 feet for every 4 ranger levels. A victim that succeeds at a Reflex save doesn't
fall into the pit.\~\~\~If it is an extraordinary trap, the ranger can set this trap only in terrain
with soft ground.

**Magical Component:**

**Details:** _Scroll of [create pit](ref/spell/create-pit)_
