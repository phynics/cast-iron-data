### Telepathy Trap (Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The trap resonates at a high-pitched frequency audible only to creatures that
communicate via telepathy. If the triggering creature has the ability to communicate via telepathy,
the creature becomes overwhelmed by psychic feedback. The trapped creature is
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d4 rounds (Will negates), and may not communicate
telepathically during this time.\~\~\~This is a sonic, mind-affecting effect. Creatures that lack
telepathy are not affected by this trap, though they may still trigger it.
