### Rust Monster Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The trap throws up a cloud of dust ground from the antennae of a
[rust monster](ref/monster/rust-monster), dealing 1d4 points of damage to the target's metal armor
and weapons. A ranger may increase the damage by 1 point for each additional daily use of his trap
ability he spends when creating this trap. A successful Reflex save negates this damage.\~\~\~If
this is an extraordinary trap, the ranger must supply a set of rust monster antennae.

**Magical Component:**

**Details:** _Scroll of [rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp)_
