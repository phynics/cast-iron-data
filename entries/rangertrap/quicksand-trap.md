### Quicksand Trap (Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The trap and all adjacent squares become [quicksand](ref/rule/marsh-terrain) as long as
they contain loose sand or soil. The Swim DC to move in this quicksand is equal to the trap's save
DC, and the Swim DC to tread in the quicksand is equal to the trap's DC - 5.\~\~\~The quicksand
lasts for a number of rounds equal to the ranger's level. When the effect ends, any creature still
in the quicksand is returned to the surface [prone](ref/rule/prone).
