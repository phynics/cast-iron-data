### Tar Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The target is coated in a thin layer of sticky tar unless it succeeds at a Reflex save.
It is [entangled](ref/rule/entangled), and becomes susceptible to catching fire from any source of
flame.\~\~\~If lit on fire, the tar burns intensely for 1 round; it deals 2d6 points of fire damage
and is destroyed in the process. Unless burned away, the tar remains for 1 round per ranger
level.\~\~\~If this is an extraordinary trap, the ranger must supply a dose of
[tar](ref/eq-weapon/tar-bomb).
