### Distraction Trap (Ex or Su)

**Effect:**

**Details:** The target is affected with irritation that detracts from its alertness. It takes a -2
penalty on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks, initiative checks, and Reflex saves for 10
minutes per ranger level.\~\~\~If this is a supernatural trap, this is a mind-affecting effect. If
this is an extraordinary trap, the ranger must supply a dose of
[itching powder](ref/eq-weapon/itching-powder) or another alchemical irritant.

**Magical Component:**

**Details:** _Scroll of [daze monster](ref/spell/daze-monster)_
