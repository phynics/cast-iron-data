Humans begin play speaking Common and their ethnic language. Humans whose ethnic language is Common
(or Taldane, in the case of Chelaxians or Taldans) do not receive an additional ethnic language.
Humans with high Intelligence scores can choose any languages they want (except secret languages,
such as Druidic).
