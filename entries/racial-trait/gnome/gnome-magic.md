Gnomes add 1 to the DCs of any saving throws to resist illusion spells that they cast. Gnomes with a
Charisma of 11 or higher also gain the following spell-like abilities:
1/day-[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation), and
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals). The caster level for these effects is equal to
the gnome's character level. The DC for these spells is equal to 10 + the spell's level + the
gnome's Charisma modifier.
