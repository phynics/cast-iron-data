**Type:** Infestation (nanite, injury)

**Save:** Fortitude DC 15

**Onset:** 1 week

**Frequency:** 1/day

**Effect:** 1 negative level. Such negative levels never become permanent, but as long as the victim
remains infested, they cannot be removed. A creature that dies from this sickness cannot rise as
undead.

**Cure:** 3 consecutive saves

Circuitry-like traces spread across the scalp of victims of fractal etch, as the nanites behind this
affliction scrub away the folds of the brain, causing loss of experience and memory.
