**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 7, Faction Guide pg 55\
**Cost** +1,000 gp **Weight** +10 lbs.

The artistry of a piece of armor's design lies not just in the protection it provides but also in
its ability to deflect blows, creating momentary windows of opportunity when an opponent's attack is
rebuffed. When an opponent attacks you with a bludgeoning or slashing melee weapon and misses by 5
or fewer, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on your first melee attack roll against that opponent
during your next turn.

**Drawback:** Deflecting armor reduces your speed by 5 feet.
