**Cost** +500 gp **Weight** +5 lbs.

Metal armor can be polished to reflect light in your enemies' eyes. In areas of bright light, all
enemies within 30 feet that can see you must succeed at a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + your base
attack bonus) or be [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 round. Regardless of the result of its saving
throw, a target may only be affected by this armor modification once per day.

**Drawback:** Polished armor imparts a -10 penalty to Stealth checks made in areas of bright or
normal light.
