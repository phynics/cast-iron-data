**Cost** +50 gp **Weight** -\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Critical** ×2; **Range** 20 ft.; **Type** B; **Special**
performance, trip\
**Category** Ranged; **Proficiency** Exotic\
**[Weapon Group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)** Thrown

This shield is designed for throwing and comes with specially designed straps allowing you to
unclasp and throw it as a free action. Tower shields cannot be throwing shields. Neither a shield's
enhancement bonus to AC nor its [shield spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/shield-spikes) apply to your attack
or damage rolls.
