**Sources:** Adventurer's Armory 2 pg 7, Faction Guide pg 55\
**Cost** +1,000 gp **Weight** +20 lbs.

Opponents who swing too hard at targets wearing this armor often regret it - striking jarring armor
sends a shock up the arm. Whenever an opponent makes a melee attack against you using the
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack) feat and misses by 5 or fewer, he must succeed at a Fortitude
saving throw (DC = 10 + the bonus that Power Attack would have applied to the attacker's weapon
damage roll) or be [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round.

**Drawback:** Jarring armor reduces your speed by 5 feet.
