Dwarves have long treasured [bulettes](ref/monster/bulette) for their thick hides and armor plating.
In fact, some dwarf clans require their young to travel on long pilgrimages to areas thick with land
sharks to hunt the reclusive and vicious beasts, intent on harvesting their plates for armor. A
single adult male bulette has enough armor plating and hide to produce two sets of Medium bulette
plate mail and four sets of Medium leather or studded leather armor. These armor sets are more often
than not imbued with the [delving](ref/magic-enh/delving) armor quality, and veteran dwarf bulette
hunters are rarely seen without their +1 delving bulette full plate while on the hunt. Creating
bulette armor from the creature's hide requires a skilled armorer; crafted bulette armor may be
normal or masterwork quality.

A set of bulette plate is functionally similar to metal [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate), but
is prized by wealthy collectors and military commanders the world over, and tends to sell for up to
10 times more than a normal set. Bulette full plate is slightly heavier (65 pounds) than regular
steel plate, but is more flexible and durable (max Dex bonus +2, [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 12).

A suit of bulette leather armor weighs 20 pounds, and its appearance matches the coloration of the
landshark from which it was made. It sells for 50 gp, but has the same statistics as
[studded leather](ref/eq-armor/studded-leather).
