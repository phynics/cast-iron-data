Forged by the Mendevian smiths of Nerosyan out of iron and the unique mineral nexavar, this bluish
steel strikes true against monsters vulnerable to cold iron (such as demons). Weapons made of
nexavaran steel cost 1-1/2 times as much to make as their normal counterparts. This steel is even
more delicate than cold iron regarding magical enhancements; adding enhancements to the steel
increases its price by 3,000 gp. This increase is applied the first time the item is enhanced, not
once per ability added. Nexavaran steel is otherwise identical to
[cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron).
