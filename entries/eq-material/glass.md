**Sources:** Pathfinder \#71: Rasputin Must Die! pg 89

Brittle and often strange in appearance, glass weapons mostly serve as ceremonial instruments, but
are also the best way of permanently slaying fexts. The glass parts of these weapons replace what is
typically a metal or stone component, such as an arrowhead or a dagger's blade. The remainder of the
weapon is created normally, though the fittings require some specialization.

Glass weapons cost half of what base items of their type do, and have 75% the weight of base items
of their type. Glass can be used to craft light and one-handed weapons that deal piercing or
slashing damage, as well as bullets, spear tips, and arrowheads. Glass weapons have half the
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of their base weapon and have the [fragile](ref/misc/fragile) quality.
