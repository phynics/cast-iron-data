**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 17

Heatstone plating involves placing [heatstones](ref/eq-misc/heatstone) at strategic points within
clothing or armor. A character wearing armor that has heatstone plating is constantly protected from
cold weather as per the [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) spell. Armor with heatstone
plating weighs 1 additional pound for every 5 pounds of the armor's original weight (for example, a
suit of leather armor with heatstone plating weighs 18 pounds). Heatstone plating cannot be sewn
into heavy armor.
