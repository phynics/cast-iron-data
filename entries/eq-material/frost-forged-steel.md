This material is the same substance as fire-forged steel with a subtle difference in the alignment
of the metal during crafting. Instead of channeling heat away from the wearer, it channels heat
toward the wearer. Frost-forged steel works similarly to fire-forged steel, except its effects apply
to cold damage rather than fire damage. This means frost-forged steel weapons are less useful than
their fire-forged counterparts, as there are few nonmagical sources of cold that can quickly imbue
it with enough cold energy to deal bonus damage.

Armor and weapons made from frost-forged steel are always considered masterwork, and the masterwork
costs are included in the listed prices.

Frost-forged steel has the same hit points and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) as
[steel](ref/eq-material/steel). Frost-forged steel costs the same as fire-forged steel.
