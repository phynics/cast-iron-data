**Sources:** Pathfinder \#61: Shards of Sin pg 71

This metal's structure allows it to pass through iron and steel without touching them, seemingly
shifting in and out of phase with reality. This quality earned the pale metal the nickname "ghost
iron." Inubrix is associated with necromancy magic, temperance, and gluttony. Inubrix is the softest
of the solid [skymetals](ref/eq-material/skymetal), being only slightly less malleable than lead. It
doesn't function well for crafting armor as a result, and though inubrix weapons can penetrate most
metal armors with relative ease, the weapons tend to break easily. Inubrix has 10 hit points per
inch of thickness and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 5.

An inubrix weapon deals damage as if it were
[one size category smaller](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) than its actual size, and is always
treated as if it had the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition. It ignores all armor or shield bonuses
granted by iron or steel armor or shields. Inubrix weapons cannot damage these materials at all
(and, by extension, cannot harm iron golems or similar creatures). An inubrix weapon costs +5,000
gp.
