**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 49, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 154

Armorsmiths can work with the hides of dragons to produce armor or shields of
[masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-armor) quality. One dragon produces enough hide to make a single
suit of masterwork [hide armor](ref/eq-armor/hide) for a creature one size category smaller than the
dragon. By selecting only choice scales and bits of hide, an armorsmith can produce one suit of
masterwork [banded mail](ref/eq-armor/banded-mail) for a creature two sizes smaller, one suit of
masterwork [half-plate](ref/eq-armor/half-plate) for a creature three sizes smaller, or one
masterwork [breastplate](ref/eq-armor/breastplate) or suit of [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate)
for a creature four sizes smaller. In each case, enough hide is available to produce a light or
heavy masterwork shield in addition to the armor, provided that the dragon is Large or larger. If
the dragonhide comes from a dragon that had immunity to an energy type, the armor is also immune to
that energy type, although this does not confer any protection to the wearer. If the armor or shield
is later given the ability to protect the wearer against that energy type, the cost to add such
protection is reduced by 25%.

Because dragonhide armor isn't made of metal, druids can wear it without penalty.

Dragonhide armor costs twice as much as masterwork armor of that type, but it takes no longer to
make than ordinary armor of that type (double all Craft results).

Dragonhide has 10 hit points per inch of thickness and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10. The hide of
a dragon is typically between 1/2 inch and 1 inch thick.

**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 19\
A classic status symbol for adventurers across Golarion, dragonhide armor is coveted for its
toughness. Though smiths have been making dragonhide armor for centuries without the benefit of
dragoncrafting, those with the [Dragoncrafting](ref/feat/dragoncrafting) feat are able to improve
upon an already excellent product.

Normally, if a suit of dragonhide armor has energy immunity, one may enhance such a suit with
additional energy protection for the wearer at a 25% reduced cost. However, only effects with the
same elemental type as the armor's immunity are eligible for this discount. An artisan with the
Dragoncrafting feat may add 1 additional elemental protection to a suit of dragonhide armor at the
same 25% discount, even if this energy resistance is not normally associated with the type of
dragonhide being used.
