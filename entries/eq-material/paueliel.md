Towering above most other trees, the lofty paueliel are widely held as the "first trees."
Lumberjacks superstitiously connect them to the fey races of the woodlands and, despite the strength
of the wood, often avoid harvesting them. Nature enthusiasts revere them. These silver-barked
hardwoods grow to immense heights, but never spread to more than a few feet in diameter.

**Stats:** As [darkwood](ref/eq-material/darkwood) but with [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 7 and 150%
as expensive.
