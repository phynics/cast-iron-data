**&L&Papercraft Tools (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** So long has the rogue has access to a [deck of cards](ref/eq-misc/cards), she can
destroy one card to attempt a single [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) check as if she had
a set of [thieves tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools). If she uses a
[harrow card](ref/eq-misc/harrow-deck) for this purpose, she can treat the card as a set of
[masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork) (granting her a +2 circumstance
bonus on the Disable Device check), though the card is still destroyed after use.

**Flavor:** Traditional thieves' tools might be suspicious, but no guard looks twice at a common
deck of cards.
