**Scry Slip (Su)**

**Passive:** Whenever a rogue with this talent is targeted by a scrying effect that allows a Will
save, the caster of the divination must succeed at a caster level check (DC = 15 + the rogue's class
level). This talent wards the rogue and any objects she is holding or carrying.
