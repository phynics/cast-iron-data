**Reflexive Shadow Shield (Su)**

**Immediate:** Once per day, the rogue can gain an amount of cold [resistance](ref/umr/resistance)
or electricity resistance equal to half the rogue's level. This resistance lasts for 1 round.

**Prereq:** [Resiliency](ref/talent/resiliency)

**Special:** If the rogue has the _shadowy resistance_ racial trait, the cold or electricity
resistance stacks with the resistance that trait grants; otherwise, the energy resistance does not
stack with any cold or electricity resistance the rogue has.
