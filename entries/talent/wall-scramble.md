**Wall Scramble (Ex)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent rolls twice when making [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks and
takes the better of the two rolls. If she already rolls twice while making a Climb check because of
another ability or effect, she gains a +2 insight bonus on both of those rolls instead. If the rogue
is under the effect of a spell or ability that forces her to roll two dice and take the worse
result, she only needs to roll 1d20 while making Climb checks.
