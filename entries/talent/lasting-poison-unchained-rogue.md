**&L&Lasting Poison (Unchained Rogue) (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent can apply poison to a weapon in such a way that it is
effective for a number of successful attacks equal to her Dexterity modifier (minimum two) instead
of one. This poison has a reduced effect, however, and saves made against the poison gain a +2
circumstance bonus. Applying poison in this way is a **full-round action**, or a **standard action**
if the rogue has the [swift poison](ref/talent/swift-poison) rogue talent.
