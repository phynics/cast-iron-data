**&L&Escaping Stunt (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** When she is the target of a grapple combat maneuver, a rogue with this talent can
attempt an Escape Artist check as an **immediate action**, using the result of the skill check in
place of her CMD against that combat maneuver attempt.

**Immediate:** A rogue with this talent can attempt an Escape Artist check in place of a Reflex
saving throw against any effect that would impose the [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) condition on
her.

**Prereq:** Trained in [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist)

**Usage:** 1 grapple/day + 1 per five rogue levels
