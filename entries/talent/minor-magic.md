**Minor Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent gains the ability to cast a 0-level spell from the
sorcerer/wizard spell list. This spell can be cast three times a day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this ability is equal to
the rogue's level. The save DC for this spell is 10 + the rogue's Intelligence modifier.

**Prereq:** Intelligence 10+
