**Face in the Crowd**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent can blend into groups easily. Opposed
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks take a -2
penalty when made against the rogue's [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise),
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand), or [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks if she's within
30 feet of at least two non-hostile characters of her apparent creature type. This penalty increases
to -4 if she's within 30 feet of at least eight such creatures or is currently in a square occupied
by a crowd.
