**Umbral Gear (Su)**

**Action:** Standard Action

**Special:** A rogue can select this talent more than once, gaining an additional 10 minutes of
duration each time this talent is selected. If a rogue has selected this talent at least twice, she
adds 50 feet of silk rope with a [grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook), a
[masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-armor) light melee weapon with which she is proficient, and
[masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork) to the list of things she can
create.

While in an area of [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darkness, a rogue with this talent can
coalesce wisps of shadow into a quasi-real, functional item. The rogue must choose whether to make a
[crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar), 50 feet of [silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft), a
[glass cutter](ref/eq-misc/glass-cutter), a light melee weapon with which she is proficient, a
[reversible cloak](ref/eq-misc/reversible-cloak), [thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools), or a
[wire saw](ref/eq-misc/wire-saw-common); the GM may allow other similar items. The rogue can use
such items for a number of minutes per day equal to 10 plus her rogue level. This duration does not
need to be consecutive, but it must be used in 1-minute increments.

An item created with this ability remains until the rogue is no longer touching it, or until the
rogue runs out of duration for this talent, at which time it dissolves.
