**Cloying Shades (Su)**

**Ability:** When a rogue with this talent uses [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) as a
spell or [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), including the _abundant step_ or
_shadow jump_ class feature, then all creatures adjacent to the rogue at the start and end of her
teleportation are [entangled](ref/rule/entangled) by grasping shadows for 1 round (although the
creatures are not anchored in place). A successful Reflex saving throw negates this effect. The DC
of this saving throw is equal to 10 + 1/2 the rogue's level + the rogue's Intelligence or Charisma
modifier (whichever is higher).

**Prereq:** Has _[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_ as a spell or
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (including the _abundant step_ or _shadow jump_
class feature)
