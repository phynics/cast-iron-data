**&L&Multitalented (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** Once per day, she can use a rogue talent that is normally only usable once per day one
additional time.

**Flavor:** The rogue can use her rogue talents more often.

**At 10th Level:** She can use this talent one additional time per day (for a total of 2).

**At 18th Level:** She can use this talent one additional time per day (for a total of 3).

**Special:** The uses of this talent do not have to be spent on the same rogue talent. This ability
cannot be used with an _advanced talent._
