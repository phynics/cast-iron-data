**Wild Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent gains the ability to cast a 0-level spell from the druid spell
list. This spell can be cast three times per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The caster level for this ability is equal to
the rogue's level. The save DC for this spell is 10 + the rogue's Wisdom modifier.

**Prereq:** Wisdom 10+
