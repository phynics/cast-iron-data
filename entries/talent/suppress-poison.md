**Suppress Poison (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A rogue with this talent can stave off the effects of a poison.

**Immediate:** When she fails a saving throw against a poison, the rogue can attempt the saving
throw a second time. If she succeeds at this second saving throw, the poison does not affect her for
a number of rounds equal to her Constitution modifier (minimum 1 round), but this time it counts
against the poison's duration. This talent has no effect on poisons with an onset longer than
immediate.
