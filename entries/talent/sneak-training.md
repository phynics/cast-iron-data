**Sneak Training (Ex)**

**Passive:** The rogue counts as having the sneak attack class feature of a rogue of her level for
the purpose of meeting prestige class requirements, although this talent doesn't improve the rogue's
existing sneak attack ability or grant her the sneak attack class feature if she doesn't already
have it. This talent is most useful for rogues that lack the sneak attack class feature, such as
those with the [phantom thief](ref/arc-rogue/phantom-thief) archetype.
