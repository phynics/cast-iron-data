**Accurate Poisoner (Ex)**

**Ability:** When the rogue successfully hits an opponent with a poisoned weapon and would deal
sneak attack damage, she can forgo the sneak attack damage and increase the poison's potency. If she
does, the poison's duration increases by 2 (for example,
[large scorpion venom](ref/eq-poison/large-scorpion-venom) lasts for 8 rounds instead of 6 rounds,
and [drow poison](ref/eq-poison/drow-poison) lasts for 4 minutes instead of 2 minutes).

**Flavor:** A rogue with this talent delivers poisons with deadly precision.
