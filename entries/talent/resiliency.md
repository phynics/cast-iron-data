Once per day, a rogue with this ability can gain a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to the rogue's level. Activating this
ability is an immediate action that can only be performed when she is brought to below 0 hit points.
This ability can be used to prevent her from [dying](ref/misc/dying). These temporary hit points
last for 1 minute. If the rogue's hit points drop below 0 due to the loss of these temporary hit
points, she falls [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious) and is dying as normal.
