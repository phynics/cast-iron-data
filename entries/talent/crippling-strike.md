Talents marked with an asterisk add effects to a rogue’s sneak attack. Only one of these talents can
be applied to an individual attack and the decision must be made before the attack roll is made.

A rogue with this ability can sneak attack opponents with such precision that her blows weaken and
hamper them. An opponent damaged by one of her sneak attacks also takes 2 points of
[Strength damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).
