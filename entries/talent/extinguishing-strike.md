**Extinguishing Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a rogue with this talent hits a creature with a melee attack that deals sneak
attack damage, any nonmagical light sources worn or carried by the creature (such as lit torches,
lanterns, or sunrods) are automatically extinguished.

**Ability2:** Once per day, the rogue can use this ability to also attempt a dispel check (as per
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)) against any magical sources of light a target carries, using
her rogue level as the caster level.
