**Bardic Pretender (Ex)**

**Ability:** The rogue knows enough about music and performance to survive in a bardic college and
can fake what she doesn't know. The rogue qualifies as having the
[inspire competence](ref/performance/inspire-competence) bardic performance class feature for the
purpose of meeting prestige class requirements, although this talent doesn't grant her the _bardic
performance_ ability.
