**&L&Without a Trace (Ex)&FN&**

**Immediate:** When the rogue successfully uses [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) or
[improved evasion](ref/talent/improved-evasion) to avoid taking damage, she can attempt to hide. The
rogue attempts a Stealth check with a -20 penalty, but gains a +1 bonus on her check for every die
of damage the avoided attack would have dealt. The rogue can attempt this check even while being
observed, as long as she has [cover](ref/rule/cover) or [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or an
ability that allows her to use the Stealth skill while being observed without cover or concealment.
