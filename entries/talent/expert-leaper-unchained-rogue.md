When using the Acrobatics skill to jump, a rogue with this talent is always considered to have a
running start and adds her rogue level to the check result. Whenever she deliberately falls, a
successful DC 15 Acrobatics check allows her to ignore the first 20 feet fallen. For every 5 by
which she exceeds the DC of this check, she can ignore an additional 10 feet of distance fallen.
