Talents marked with an asterisk add effects to a rogue’s sneak attack. Only one of these talents can
be applied to an individual attack and the decision must be made before the attack roll is made.

Opponents that are dealt sneak attack damage by a rogue with this ability are affected by a targeted
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_, targeting the lowest-level spell effect active on the
target. The caster level for this ability is equal to the rogue's level. A rogue must have the
[major magic](ref/talent/major-magic) rogue talent before choosing dispelling attack.
