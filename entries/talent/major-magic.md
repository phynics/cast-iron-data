**Major Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent gains the ability to cast a 1st-level spell from the
sorcerer/wizard spell list two times a day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).
The caster level for this ability is equal to the rogue's level. The save DC for this spell is 11 +
the rogue's Intelligence modifier.

**Prereq:** Intelligence 11+, [minor magic](ref/talent/minor-magic)
