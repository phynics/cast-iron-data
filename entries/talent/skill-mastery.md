The rogue becomes so confident in the use of certain skills that she can use them reliably even
under adverse conditions. Upon gaining this ability, she selects a number of skills equal to 3 + her
Intelligence modifier. When making a skill check with one of these skills, she may take 10 even if
stress and distractions would normally prevent her from doing so. A rogue may gain this special
ability multiple times, selecting additional skills for skill mastery to apply to each time.
