**&L&Skill Mastery&FN&**

**Ability:** When making a skill check with one of these skills, she may take 10 even if stress and
distractions would normally prevent her from doing so.

**Choice:** Upon gaining this ability, the rogue selects a number of skills equal to 3 + her
Intelligence modifier.

**Flavor:** The rogue becomes so confident in the use of certain skills that she can use them
reliably even under adverse conditions.

**Special:** A rogue may gain this special ability multiple times, selecting additional skills for
_skill mastery_ to apply to each time.
