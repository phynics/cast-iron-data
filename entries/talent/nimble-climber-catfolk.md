**&L&Nimble Climber (Ex)&FN&**

**Passive:** A catfolk rogue with this talent gains a +4 bonus on Climb checks.

**Prereq:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk)

**Special:** If she has the _climber racial_ trait, she can take 10 on her Climb checks even when in
immediate danger or distracted.
