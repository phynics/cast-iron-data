**&L&Sever Alignment (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** When making a sneak attack against an opponent with an alignment subtype, a rogue with
this talent can forgo her sneak attack damage to scramble the creature's metaphysical nature. If the
attack succeeds, the rogue deals weapon damage as normal, and the target must succeed at a Fortitude
save (DC = 10 + half the rogue's level + the rogue's Intelligence modifier) or lose all
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) overcome by aligned weapons (such as DR 10/good), lose
all [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) overcome by aligned sources (such as regeneration 10
\[good\]).

**Prereq:** [Aligned sneak attack](ref/talent/aligned-sneak-attack)
