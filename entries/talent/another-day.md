Once per day, when the rogue would be reduced to 0 or fewer hit points by a melee attack, she can
take a 5-foot step as an immediate action. If the movement takes her out of the reach of the attack,
she takes no damage from the attack. The rogue is [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round on her
next turn.
