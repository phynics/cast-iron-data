Once per day, a rogue with this talent can roll two dice while making an
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) check, and take the better result. She must choose to use this
talent before making the Acrobatics check. A rogue can use this ability one additional time per day
for every 5 rogue levels she possesses.
