**&L&Shadow Duplicate (Sp)&FN&**

**Immediate:** When the rogue is hit, the rogue can create a single shadow duplicate of herself, as
per [mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image). The GM randomly determines whether the attack hit the
rogue or the shadow duplicate. The shadow duplicate lasts for a number of rounds equal to the
rogue's level, or until the shadow duplicate is dispelled or destroyed. This ability does not stack
with the _[mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image)_ spell. The caster level for this ability is equal
to the rogue's level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 rogue levels
