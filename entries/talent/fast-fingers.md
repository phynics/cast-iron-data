Once per day, a rogue with this talent can roll two dice while making a
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check and take the better result. She must choose to
use this talent before making the Sleight of Hand check. A rogue can use this ability one additional
time per day for every 5 rogue levels she possesses.
