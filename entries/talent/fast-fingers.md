**&L&Fast Fingers (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent can roll two dice while making a
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check and take the better result. She must choose to
use this talent before making the Sleight of Hand check.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 rogue levels
