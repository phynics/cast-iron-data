**Demon Lantern (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day when she casts [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights), the rogue can
conjure a single _demon's lantern_ instead of the usual four lights. A _demon lantern_ acts in all
ways as a [hypnotic pattern](ref/spell/hypnotic-pattern) spell cast by a wizard, except it also
casts light as a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) and it [fascinates](ref/misc/fascinated) only a single
target whose Hit Dice do not exceed the rogue's class level. The save DC of this effect is equal to
11 + the rogue's Intelligence modifier.

**Prereq:** Intelligence 11+, [minor magic](ref/talent/minor-magic)
(_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_) or otherwise able to cast
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_
