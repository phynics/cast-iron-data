**&L&Shove Aside (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** When you and an adjacent ally are both in the area of an attack that requires a saving
throw, you can take a -4 penalty on your saving throw to grant your ally a +4 bonus on his saving
throw. You lose the [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) ability against this attack. If you have
[improved evasion](ref/talent/improved-evasion), it is reduced to evasion for this attack instead.

**Prereq:** _Evasion_
