**&L&False Attacker (Ex)&FN&**

**Immediate:** Whenever the rogue strikes a foe from hiding, she can attempt a
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check (opposed by the target's Sense Motive or Perception check, whichever
has a higher bonus) before rolling damage to convince the foe that another creature was the
attacker. This Bluff check applies the normal modifiers for convincing a creature of a falsehood,
but can be accomplished with just physical trickery (a shared language is not required).

**Special:** If the Bluff is successful, and the rogue maintains concealment or cover, the rogue's
stealth does not end.
