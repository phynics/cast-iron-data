**Disease Use**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent can apply filth from garbage, a sewer, a city street, a
polluted area, or long-unwashed clothing or fur to a weapon as a **standard action**. Doing so
causes the weapon to inflict [filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever) on its next strike. The rogue
does not risk exposure to this disease unless she is damaged by the weapon.

**Special:** After a successful attack with the weapon, the filth is dispersed and must be
reapplied.
