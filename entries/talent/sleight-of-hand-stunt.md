**Sleight of Hand Stunt (Ex)**

**Ability:** In place of an attack of opportunity, a rogue with this talent can attempt a
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) check against the CMD of an opponent that provokes an
attack of opportunity by firing a projectile weapon while threatened. If successful, the rogue
plucks the ammunition from the provoking weapon, negating the attack.

**Prereq:** Trained in [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand)

**Special:** The rogue may use this ability as many times in a round as she could make attacks of
opportunity. At the GM's discretion, certain projectile weapons may not be susceptible to this
ability, such as siege engines or firearms.
