**Hamstring Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** Anytime a rogue with this talent successfully hits an opponent with a sneak attack, she
may forgo her sneak attack damage to try to hamstring her opponent. If the attack succeeds, the
rogue deals weapon damage as normal, but instead of the rogue dealing sneak attack damage, the
target is knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone) and is unable to take move actions during its next turn. A
successful Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 the rogue's level + the rogue's Dexterity modifier) negates
this effect.
