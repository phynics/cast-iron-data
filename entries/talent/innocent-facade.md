**Innocent Facade (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, a rogue with this talent can use [innocence](ref/spell/innocence) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using her rogue level as the spell's caster
level.

**Prereq:** [Minor magic](ref/talent/minor-magic)
