**Hairpin Trick (Ex)**

**Ability:** The rogue is skilled at making do with anything she can find. She takes no penalty on
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks for using improvised tools, and can attempt such
checks without any tools at a -4 penalty. She treats all non-improvised
[thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools) as masterwork and doubles the bonus she gets from
[masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork) from +2 to +4.
