**Certainty (Ex)**

**Ability:** The rogue can reroll a skill check she just made with the selected skill and take the
better result.

**Choice:** The rogue chooses one of the skills she selected with _rogue's edge._

**Prereq:** _Rogue's edge_ class feature

**Special:** A rogue can select this talent multiple times. Each time, the rogue must choose a
different skill.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 5 rogue levels
