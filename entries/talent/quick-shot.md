**Quick Shot (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever the rogue rolls initiative, she can also make a single attack with a ranged
weapon as a **swift action**. She can use this ability only if she has a weapon in hand and it is
loaded (if applicable).

**Special:** If more than one rogue has this talent, their initiative check results determine the
order in which they make their attacks. After these attacks are resolved, the round proceeds as
normal.
