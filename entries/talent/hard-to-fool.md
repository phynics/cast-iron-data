Once per day, a rogue with this talent can roll two dice while making a
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) check, and take the better result. She must choose to use
this talent before making the Sense Motive check. A rogue can use this ability one additional time
per day for every 5 rogue levels she possesses.
