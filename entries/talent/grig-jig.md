**Grig Jig (Su)**

**Ability:** The rogue can target one humanoid within 30 feet and attempt a
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (dance) check as a **full-round action** that does not provoke an
attack of opportunity. Her target must succeed at a Will save (DC equal to the rogue's Perform
\[dance\]) or it begins to dance uncontrollably.

So long as the rogue continues to spend a full-round action dancing each round, the affected target
does so as well. If the target is attacked or otherwise in immediate danger while dancing, the
effect ends.

Each round on its turn, the target can attempt an [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) or Perform
(dance) check or a Will save (target's choice) against the rogue's Perform (dance) check to end the
effect.

**Flavor:** The rogue can weave a bit of old Varisian magic into her footwork, dancing with an
infectious passion that compels another nearby to join in.

**Prereq:** Intelligence 12+

**Special:** A target who resists the _jig_ can not be affected by the same rogue's _jig_ for 24
hours. The _grig jig_ is a mind-affecting effect.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 rogue levels
