**Deadly Sneak (Ex)**

**Passive:** Whenever a rogue with this talent uses the _powerful sneak_ rogue talent, she treats
all 1s and 2s on the sneak attack damage dice as 3s.

**Prereq:** [Powerful sneak](ref/talent/powerful-sneak)
