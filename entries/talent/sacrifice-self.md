**&L&Sacrifice Self (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** A rogue who makes a successful Reflex save against an area effect can ignore the
benefits of her [evasion](ref/ability/evasion) ability to shield an adjacent ally against the
effect, essentially halving the damage that ally would normally take.

A rogue with [improved evasion](ref/talent/improved-evasion) can attempt a secondary save against
her ally's damage. If the save is successful, neither she nor her ally takes any damage.

**Prereq:** _Evasion_
