Talents marked with an asterisk add effects to a rogue's sneak attack. Only one of these talents can
be applied to an individual attack, and the decision must be made before the attack roll is made.

Whenever the rogue inflicts a penalty against a target using the debilitating injury class feature,
she can select two penalties to inflict. The target can never be subject to more than two penalties
in this way at one time. If any additional penalties are inflicted, any previous penalties
immediately end. Any effect that heals damage to the target negates both of the penalties.
