**&L&Glib Facade (Sp)&FN&**

**Ability:** Once per day, a rogue with this advanced talent can use [glibness](ref/spell/glibness)
as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using her rogue level as the spell's caster
level.
