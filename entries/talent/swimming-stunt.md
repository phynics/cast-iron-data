**Swimming Stunt (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per round, when making an attack against an opponent underwater that qualifies for
sneak attack damage, a rogue with this talent can attempt a Swim check as a **free action** against
her opponent's CMD. If the attack and Swim check are both successful, and the attack deals sneak
attack damage, the rogue can choose to forgo one or more of her sneak attack dice to reduce the
number of total rounds her opponent can [hold its breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) by 1 round per
die.

**Prereq:** Trained in [Swim](ref/skill/swim)
