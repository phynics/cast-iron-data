**Coax Information (Ex)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent can use [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) or
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) in place of [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) to force an opponent
to act friendly toward her. When the duration of the attitude shift ends, that opponents' attitude
toward the rogue returns to its previous level, rather than dropping one level.
