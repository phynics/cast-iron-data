**One of Those Faces (Sp)**

**Ability:** Each day, you can use [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) for up to 10 minutes per character level. This
duration need not be continuous, but it must be used in 10-minute increments.

**Special:** Once you have used this ability, whenever you use it for the next 24 hours you must
take the same alternate appearance.
