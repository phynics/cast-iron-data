**Graceful Faller (Ex)**

**Ability:** A catfolk rogue with this talent lands on her feet even when she takes lethal damage
from a fall.

**Prereq:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk)

**Special:** If the catfolk rogue also has the _nimble faller_ racial trait, she takes damage from
any fall as if it were 20 feet shorter than it actually is.
