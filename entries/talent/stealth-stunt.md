**&L&Stealth Stunt (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** When benefiting from [concealment](ref/rule/concealment), a rogue with this talent can
forgo an attack of opportunity to attempt a [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) check against the provoking
opponent's CMD. Success allows the rogue to treat her opponent as
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against the rogue's first melee attack before the end of her
next turn. Using this ability does not count against the rogue's available attacks of opportunity
for the round.

**Prereq:** Trained in [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
