**Scrying Familiarity (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can roll twice and take the better result on saving throws against
[divination (scrying)](ref/spelldef/scrying) spells and effects, on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to notice scrying sensors, and on caster level checks to
overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) when you use a scrying spell or effect.

If you notice a magical sensor, you can attempt a [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) check opposed by the
caster's caster level check to avoid being detected by the sensor.

**Flavor:** You are well acquainted with scrying sensors.
