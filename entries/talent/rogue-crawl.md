**&L&Rogue Crawl (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** While [prone](ref/rule/prone), a rogue with this ability can move at half speed. This
movement provokes attacks of opportunity as normal. A rogue with this talent can take a 5-foot step
while crawling.
