**Favored Terrain (Ex)**

**Ability:** The rogue selects one terrain from the ranger's
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) list; she gains this as a _favored terrain,_ as the
ranger class feature.

**Prereq:** 5th-level rogue

**Special:** If the rogue also has or later takes the
[hide in plain sight](ref/talent/hide-in-plain-sight) advanced rogue talent, her _favored terrain_
must match a terrain she has selected for that advanced talent. If the rogue has ranger levels, her
effective ranger level is equal to her ranger level + 5 for purposes of improving the bonuses from
this _favored terrain_ and gaining new _favored terrains._
