**&L&Founders' Blessing&FN&**

**Ability:** Once per day, she can choose a skill in which she has no ranks. After she spends 10
minutes communing with these mystical spirits, they grant her a luck bonus equal to her rogue level
with that skill, and she can attempt checks with that skill as if she were trained in its use. The
effects of _founder's blessing_ last for 8 hours.

**Flavor:** The rogue can call upon the mystical legacies of the Council.
