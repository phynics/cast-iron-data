**Greater Gloom Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent gains the ability to cast
[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness) once each day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The darkness created by this ability does not
impair the rogue's vision. The caster level for this ability is equal to the rogue's level.

**Prereq:** Intelligence 13+, [gloom magic](ref/talent/gloom-magic),
[minor magic](ref/talent/minor-magic)
