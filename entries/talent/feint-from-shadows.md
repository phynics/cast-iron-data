**Feint from Shadows (Ex)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent can [feint](ref/skill/bluff) in combat using a ranged weapon
against a target within 30 feet and cause the opponent to lose his Dexterity modifier against her
next melee or ranged attack. The rogue must have [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (but not full
concealment) from the target of the feint.
