**Deadly Scratch (Ex)**

**Ability:** A catfolk rogue with this talent can apply poison to her claws without accidentally
poisoning herself.

**Prereq:** [Catfolk](ref/race/catfolk), _cat's claws_ racial trait,
[poison use](ref/ability/poison-use) class feature
