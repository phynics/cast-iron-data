**Coax Information (Ex)**

**Ability:** A rogue with this talent can use [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) or
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) in place of [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) to force an opponent
to act friendly toward her.
