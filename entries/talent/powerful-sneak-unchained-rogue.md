**Powerful Sneak (Unchained Rogue) (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a rogue with this talent takes a full attack action, she can elect to take a
-2 penalty on all attack rolls until the start of her next turn. If an attack during this time is a
sneak attack, she treats all 1s on the sneak attack damage dice as 2s. She can reroll multiple dice,
but she can't reroll any individual die more than once per attack.
