**&L&Charmer (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** The rogue can roll two dice while making a Diplomacy check, and take the better result.
She must choose to use this talent before making the Diplomacy check.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 rogue levels
