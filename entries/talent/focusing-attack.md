**Focusing Attack (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a rogue selects this talent, she must choose the [confused](ref/misc/confused),
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken), or [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) condition. When the rogue has the
selected condition and hits a creature with a melee attack that deals sneak attack damage, the rogue
no longer has that condition.

**Special:** A rogue can take this talent up to three times. Each time, she must select a different
condition that she is able to remove from herself with a melee attack that deals sneak attack
damage. Even if the rogue has taken this talent multiple times, she can remove only a single effect
on herself with each melee attack that deals sneak attack damage.
