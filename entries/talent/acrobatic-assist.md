**&L&Acrobatic Assist (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** You can expend an attack of opportunity to perform an
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action to assist an adjacent ally's Acrobatics check, so long as
he makes the check as part of movement that passes through your space or an adjacent space.

Additionally, whenever you use the aid another action to assist an ally in making an Acrobatics
check and you succeed, your ally gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity caused
when he moves out of or within a threatened area, until the end of his turn.
