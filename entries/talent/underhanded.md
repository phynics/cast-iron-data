**Underhanded (Ex)**

**Ability:** Furthermore, if she makes a sneak attack during the surprise round using a concealed
weapon that her opponent didn't know about, she does not have to roll sneak attack damage, and the
sneak attack deals maximum damage.

**Passive:** A rogue with this talent gains a +4 circumstance bonus on all
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks made to conceal a weapon.

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day
