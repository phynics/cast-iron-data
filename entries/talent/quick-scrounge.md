**Quick Scrounge**

**Ability:** The rogue can search a creature, object, or area in half as much time as normal. If her
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) check would normally require a full-round action or less, it
becomes one step shorter along the following progression: **full-round action**, **standard
action**, **move action**, **swift action**, **immediate action**.

**Special:** This talent has no effect on Perception checks that are attempted as free actions or
Perception checks that don't require an action.
