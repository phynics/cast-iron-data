A rogue with this talent can select a single [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain). Her
bonuses in that terrain increase to +4. At 13th level and 18th level, they increase by an additional
+2 (to a maximum of +8). A rogue must have the [terrain mastery](ref/talent/terrain-mastery) talent
to select this advanced talent.
