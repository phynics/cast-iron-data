**Discipline Powers**

**Description:** Your enlightened mind eases your burden and allows troubles to harmlessly wash over
you.

**Expanded Awareness (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** You can use _expanded awareness_ to gain
[see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) or [thoughtsense](ref/umr/thoughtsense) (20 feet).

**At 15th Level:** You can use it to gain [lifesense](ref/umr/lifesense).

**Move:** You can focus on your senses and gain one of the following sensory abilities:
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) (10 feet), [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) (30 feet),
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), or [scent](ref/umr/scent). This new sense lasts for 1
round per psychic level.

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day

**Patient Insight (Ex)**

**Ability:** When you attempt a [Heal](ref/skill/heal), [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all),
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), or [Survival](ref/skill/survival) check, you can spend 1
point from your phrenic pool to roll the check twice and take the higher result.

**Focused Trance (Ex)**

**At 5th Level:** You can enter a deep meditation, allowing you to concentrate on a single problem,
philosophical issue, or memory. This trance lasts 1d6 rounds, during which time you can take only
move actions and gain a bonus equal to your psychic level on all saves against sonic effects and
[gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attacks. When you come out of your trance, you can attempt a single
[Appraise](ref/skill/appraise), Knowledge, or [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) check with a +20
circumstance bonus.

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day

**Empty Mind (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can clear your mind by focusing on a sense of emptiness, purging yourself of doubt
and discomfort.

**At 13th Level:** You can spend 1 point from your phrenic pool on your turn as a **free action** to
remove the blinded, confused, dazed, deafened, staggered, or stunned condition from yourself.
