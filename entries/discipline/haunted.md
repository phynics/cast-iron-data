**Sources:** Haunted Heroes Handbook pg 18\
You attract the attention of spirits as naturally as a lodestone attracts iron filings. Testing your
power leads you to develop some control over undead forces, despite the inherent defenses such
creatures have against psychic abilities. Meanwhile, those spirits clinging to your life essence
become increasingly protective, channeling their abilities to aid you and warn of threats. Psychics
of this discipline are most common in Ustalav and Shenmen, where so many spirits are stuck in the
transition between life and death.

**Discipline Powers**

**Description:** Your powers interact with the spiritual world, attracting the attention of minor
spirits and undead.

**Lingering Spirits (Sp)**

**Ability:** Numerous minor spirits find themselves attracted to you. Your connection with these
spirits allows you to manifest any of the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as a **swift action**:
[mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[grave words](ref/spell/grave-words), or [telekinetic projectile](ref/spell/telekinetic-projectile).

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Spiritual Bulwark (Su)**

**Flavor:** You develop a deeper attachment to your lingering spirits, and learn to rely on their
presence as protection from external spiritual attacks.

**At 5th Level:** You gain a bonus equal to your Charisma modifier on saving throws against effects
generated by haunts, incorporeal undead, incorporeal outsiders, and on saving throws against
possession attempts.

**Phantasmal Assault (Su)**

**Flavor:** You learn to harness your psychic force against all forms of haunts and undead
creatures.

**At 13th Level:** Your mind-affecting spells that deal damage can now be used against haunts and
undead normally immune to mind-affecting effects (including mindless undead). These spells are
treated as though they dealt positive energy damage. This affects only spells that deal purely
psychic-based damage (such as [psychic crush](ref/spell/psychic-crush-1),
[mind thrust](ref/spell/mind-thrust-1), or [synapse overload](ref/spell/synapse-overload)); effects
based on physical effects (such as _[telekinetic projectile](ref/spell/telekinetic-projectile)_ or
[telekinetic storm](ref/spell/telekinetic-storm)) don't benefit from this ability. Additional
non-damaging effects from such spells don't affect the haunt, unless it would be normally affected.

**Special:** When you use a mind-affecting effect that deals damage against a possessed creature,
the possessing entity takes a -4 penalty on the saving throw against the mind-affecting attack.
