**Discipline Powers**

**Description:** Your powers relate to your discipline and mastery of mind over matter.

**Agitating Cognizance (Su)**

**Passive:** Whenever you are not maintaining an altered form via _metamorphosis_ (including the 24
hours normally required to assume a new form), your maximum number of points in your phrenic pool
increases by 1.

**Metamorphosis (Su)**

**Ability:** By meditating for 1 hour, you can trigger a slow, physical metamorphosis that takes 24
hours to complete (during which you do not gain the benefits of this new form). This ability cannot
change your type or subtype, grant new abilities or natural weapons, or change your size category,
but otherwise allows you to transform your appearance into anything possible for creatures of your
type and subtype.

You gain an enhancement bonus to one physical ability score of your choice (Strength, Dexterity, or
Constitution) while in this altered form. This bonus is equal to 1 +
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your psychic level. Your shape and ability score
bonus persist until you assume a new form or your current altered form ends.

When meditating to begin your _metamorphosis,_ you can spend 1 point from your phrenic pool either
to change your size category to Small or Medium, or to gain a new ability from those provided by the
[alter self](ref/spell/alter-self) spell. If you do so, your altered form lasts only 1 hour per
level.

**Spirit Channeling (Su)**

**Flavor:** As your self-awareness expands, you become increasingly attuned to the spirit world
around you.

**At 5th Level:** As part of your meditation to initiate _metamorphosis,_ you can invite a
[wandering spirit](ref/ability/shaman-spirits) to share your body as if you were a
[shaman](ref/class/shaman). You gain the associated 1st-level _spirit ability_ as long as you
maintain your altered form, but your _metamorphosis_ form also exhibits some aspect of the spirit
you host, such as vivid red hair for a [flame](ref/shamanspirit/flame) spirit or gauntness for a
[bones](ref/shamanspirit/bones) spirit.

**Greater Spirit Channeling (Su)**

**At 13th Level:** When you open your body to a wandering spirit, you also gain that wandering
spirit's _greater spirit ability_ as long as you are in a metamorphosed form.
