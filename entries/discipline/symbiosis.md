**Discipline Powers**

**Description:** Your powers allow you to connect with and manipulate natural life.

**Animal Mastery (Su)**

**At 7th Level:** The mental link becomes stronger. Whenever you share an animal's senses, you can
control it, as per _[dominate animal](ref/spell/dominate-animal),_ except the duration, Hit Dice
limit, range, and other conditions listed above apply.

**Standard:** You can project your mind into the mind of an animal you can see, establishing a
mental link that allows you to share the animal's senses - hearing, seeing, smelling, tasting, and
touching everything the animal does. You cannot use this ability on an animal if its Hit Dice exceed
your psychic level. The animal can attempt a Will save to resist the effect. If it is successful,
the animal is immune to this ability for 24 hours.

While sharing an animal's senses, your body is effectively [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious), but
you can end the effect as a **free action**. You can maintain the link for 1 hour per level, but the
link is immediately severed if the animal moves beyond a range of 1 mile per psychic level you have.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**One with Nature (Sp, Su)**

**Ability:** You can cast [detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) at will. You gain a +2 insight bonus on Knowledge
(nature) checks, and while using _[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants),_
the bonus increases to +4 to identify a matching animal or plant creature within the area of effect.

**At 7th Level:** You can speak with any animal you successfully identify, as per
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals), but this ability doesn't make the creature any
friendlier than normal.

**Bionetwork (Su)**

**Flavor:** Your mental influence over nature increases.

**At 5th Level:** You can spend 10 minutes to mentally connect with living fungi, leaves, moss,
roots, and similar plants or plant-like things. You form a temporary network that functions as an
extension of your senses. The network allows you to use one of the following two effects: you can
remotely view any familiar or obvious location within a range of 250 feet per psychic level, as per
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance); or you can attempt to locate a
creature within the area, as per [scrying](ref/spell/scrying). You don't need a focus to use this
effect, and you can't cast spells through the sensor.

This ability doesn't function in areas of sparse vegetation, such as arid deserts, barren caverns,
or frozen tundras. Buildings and urban areas are generally excluded from your network, but ruins
reclaimed by nature are connected. While you're underground, roots and fungi function as your
network, but the radius of the network is only 100 feet per psychic level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 psychic levels beyond 5th

**Animate Trees (Su)**

**At 13th Level:** You can animate a tree within 180 feet as a **standard action**. This functions
as a [treant's](ref/monster/treant) _animate trees_ ability, with the following exceptions. Each
day, you can animate a number of trees equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier, and you can control up
to one tree at a time. A tree remains animated for 10 minutes per level, or until dismissed by you
or destroyed.

At 15th level: The amount of trees you can control 2.

At 17th level: The amount of trees you can control 3.

At 19th level: The amount of trees you can control 4.
