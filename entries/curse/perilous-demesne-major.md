**Type:** Curse (Regional curse)

**Save:** Will DC 16

**Effect:** As per minor perilous demesne, except this can involve up to four hazards, each of CR 6
or less, imperil creatures in the area. If any hazards are encountered together, the encounter's
effective CR from hazards must be no more than 7 (for instance, two CR 5 hazards could be
encountered together).
