**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 138

**Type:** Curse

**Save:** Will DC 17

**Effect:** The victim is haunted by a ghost as described in minor haunting.

**Cure:** Unlike a minor haunting, a major haunting can be ended only by putting the ghost to rest,
though casting remove curse directly after the ghost is destroyed prevents it from reviving for 2d4
days.
