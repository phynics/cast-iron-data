**Type:** Curse (Regional curse)

**Save:** Will DC 13

**Effect:** Up to three hazards (see the Horror Environments section), each of CR 3 or less, imperil
creatures in the area, though never with more than one hazard at a time. If a creature places this
curse, it can choose the hazards. The hazards must be appropriate to the type of terrain (at the
GM's discretion). Any of the hazards that are rendered safe reset themselves and become dangerous
again at each midnight.
