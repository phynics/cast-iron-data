**Type:** Curse (Regional curse)

**Save:** Will DC 19

**Effect:** As per major perilous demesne, except up to six hazards, each of CR 10 or less, imperil
creatures in the area. The hazards are arranged so that if any are encountered together, the
encounter's effective CR from hazards must be no more than 11 (for instance, two CR 9 hazards could
be encountered together).

**Cure:** Remove curse removes a greater perilous demesne only if followed by a casting of hallow at
the effect's center.
