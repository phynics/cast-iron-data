**Type:** Curse

**Save:** Will DC 15

**Effect:** The target is haunted by horrible phantasmal visions that distract him at critical
moments. He takes a -4 penalty on concentration checks and on saving throws against effects that
would impose any of the following conditions on him: confused, dazed, nauseated, shaken, sickened,
or stunned. Once per day, when alone or confronted by something novel and strange, he must succeed
at a Will save or take 1d3 points of Wisdom damage (or 1d6 points of sanity damage if using the
sanity system).

**Cure:** The tormenting visions curse can be removed only by remove curse followed by banishment,
each cast within 1 minute of each another.
