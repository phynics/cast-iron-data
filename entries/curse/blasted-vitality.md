**Type:** Curse

**Save:** Fortitude DC 19

**Effect:** The victim's mind and body become unreliable, imposing a -4 penalty on all ability
scores. Whenever the victim enters combat, he must succeed at a Fortitude save against the curse's
DC or become fatigued.
