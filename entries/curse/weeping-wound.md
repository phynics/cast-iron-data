**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 140

**Type:** Curse

**Save:** Fortitude DC 14

**Effect:** The victim has a wound that cannot heal, effectively reducing her maximum hit points by
an amount equal to her level or 5, whichever is more. Magical healing that would raise a victim's
hit points above this reduced maximum is wasted.
