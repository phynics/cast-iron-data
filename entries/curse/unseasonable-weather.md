**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 145

**Type:** Curse (Regional curse)

**Save:** Will DC 23

**Effect:** The weather in the cursed region changes drastically, even bringing snow to a blazing
desert. The area is plagued by your choice of very cold conditions, very hot conditions, or frequent
powerful storms. The weather remains unpredictable, and can change over time (as determined by the
GM), but the baseline weather is altered as long as the curse remains.

**Cure:** The cursed weather can be returned to normal only by casting remove curse at the center of
the affected area, followed by a casting of control weather.
