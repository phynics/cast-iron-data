An internal alchemist studies medicine, diet, and the living body to purify the self in the hope of
gaining immortality by means of alchemical concoctions and controlling vital energy. Internal
alchemists develop unusual physical abilities from heightened knowledge of how their bodies work. An
internal alchemist has the following class features.

**Breath Mastery**

**Ability:** Without preparation, he can [hold his breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for a number
of minutes equal to his Constitution score (after this, he must begin making Constitution checks or
risk suffocation).

**Flavor:** The internal alchemist can control his breath and the flow of vital energy within his
body.

**Fullround:** By spending a **full-round action** preparing himself, he can increase this duration
to 1 hour per point of Constitution.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Move:** He can put himself into a state of suspended animation as a **move action**, and is then
unconscious and appears completely dead; he awakens at a preset time or in response to a condition
set by him when he enters this state.

**Passive:** The alchemist can survive twice as long as normal without food or water before he
starts to take penalties.

**Replaces:** Throw Anything

**Bonus Feats**

**Passive:** An internal alchemist can select [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Extra Ki](ref/feat/extra-ki), [Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude),
[Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) (unarmed strike),
[Improved Initiative](ref/feat/improved-initiative),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike), [Iron Will](ref/feat/iron-will),
[Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/lightning-reflexes), [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist), or
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus) (unarmed strike) in place of an alchemical discovery.

**Disease Resistance**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** An internal alchemist gains a bonus on all saving throws against disease equal to his
alchemist class bonus against poison.

**Replaces:** Swift alchemy

**Special:** When he gains the _poison immunity_ alchemist class ability, he becomes immune to
disease as well.

**Uncanny Dodge**

**Flavor:** An internal alchemist can react to danger before his senses would normally allow him to
do so.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** He cannot be caught [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed), nor does he lose his Dexterity
bonus to AC if the attacker is invisible. However, he still loses his Dexterity bonus to AC if he is
immobilized, or if an opponent successfully uses the feint action (see [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff))
against him.

**Replaces:** Swift poisoning

**Special:** If the internal alchemist already has _uncanny dodge_ from a different class, he
automatically gains [improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) (as a rogue)
instead.

**Discoveries:** The following discoveries complement the internal alchemist archetype:
[cognatogen](ref/discovery/cognatogen), [elixir of life](ref/discovery/elixir-of-life),
[grand cognatogen](ref/discovery/grand-cognatogen),
[greater cognatogen](ref/discovery/greater-cognatogen),
[lingering spirit](ref/discovery/lingering-spirit), [mummification](ref/discovery/mummification),
and [spontaneous healing](ref/discovery/spontaneous-healing).
