**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 19\
Some alchemists are obsessed with collecting and preserving exotic creatures. These preservationists
may use bottled animals and monsters as teaching tools, but some learn how to reanimate them for
short periods to battle on the alchemist's behalf. A preservationist has the following class
features.

**Bottled Ally I**

**Ability:** He adds [summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1) to his formula book
as a 1st-level extract. When he prepares that extract, he actually prepares a tiny, preserved
specimen in a bottle (as with a caster casting the spell, the preservationist doesn't have to choose
the creature until he uses the extract). When the alchemist opens the bottle, the specimen animates
and grows to normal size, serving the preservationist as per the spell and otherwise being treated
as a summoned creature. When the duration expires, the preserved creature decays into powder.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A preservationist adds [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) (WIS) to his list of
class skills.

**Replaces:** Poison use

**Special:** If the preservationist has the [infusion](ref/discovery/infusion) discovery, another
character can use the infused specimen. The [Augment Summoning](ref/feat/augment-summoning) feat can
be applied to these specimens.

**Bottled Ally II**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A preservationist adds [summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2) to
his formula book as a 2nd-level extract.

**Replaces:** Poison resistance +4

**Bottled Ally III**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** A preservationist adds [summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4) to
his formula book as a 3rd-level extract.

**Replaces:** Poison resistance +6

**Bottled Ally IV**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** A preservationist adds [summon nature's ally V](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5) to his
formula book as a 4th-level extract.

**Replaces:** Poison immunity

**Bottled Ally V**

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Passive:** A preservationist adds [summon nature's ally VII](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-7) to
his formula book as a 5th-level extract.

**Replaces:** Persistent mutagen

**Bottled Ally VI**

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Passive:** A preservationist adds [summon nature's ally IX](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-9) to
his formula book as a 6th-level extract.

**Replaces:** 18th-level discovery

**Discoveries:** The following discoveries complement the preservationist archetype:
[bottled ooze](ref/discovery/bottled-ooze), [preserve organs](ref/discovery/preserve-organs),
[tentacle](ref/discovery/tentacle), [vestigial arm](ref/discovery/vestigial-arm), and
[wings](ref/discovery/wings).

**Feats:** The [Planar Preservationist](ref/feat/planar-preservationist) feat may be of particular
interest to a Preservationist.
