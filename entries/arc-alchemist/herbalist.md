Vine leshys who study the physical and spiritual composition of their own bodies have discovered a
strange herbal alchemy all their own.

**Prerequisites:** Vine Leshy

**Herbalism (Su)**

**Ability:** An herbalist's studies allow him a unique method of creating alchemical discoveries. He
can attempt Profession (herbalist) checks in place of Craft (alchemy) checks for all purposes, and
he gains a competence bonus equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his alchemist level on
Profession (herbalist) checks to create alchemical items and to forage for and process herbs. An
herbalist uses his **Wisdom** score instead of his Intelligence score for all alchemist class
abilities.

**Alters:** Alchemy, the alchemist's key ability score

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Natural Magic**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** An herbalist's study of the leshy spirit grants him access to natural magic that eludes
other alchemists. Each time he gains access to a new level of extracts (including 1st level), he can
add two spells from that spell level on the druid spell list to his alchemist class spell list;
these spells must target a creature or creatures or have a range of &quot&you,&quot& and he must
still add them to his formula book as normal before he can prepare them.

**Replaces:** Throw Anything

**Special:** As always, the extracts affect the drinker as if she were the target, so offensive
touch spells are a nasty trap for the drinker.

**Seedpods (Su)**

**Ability:** An herbalist can produce and throw seedpods as ranged touch attacks, similar to the
ranged attacks of other leshys. He can create a number of seedpods each day equal to his alchemist
level + his Wisdom modifier and can throw them as a standard action with a range increment of 20
feet. A seedpod deals 1d6 points of bludgeoning damage, plus an additional 1d6 points of damage at
3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter (this additional damage isn't multiplied on a critical hit
or from effects like [Vital Strike](ref/feat/vital-strike)). Seedpods are considered weapons and can
be selected for feats like [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus), and the herbalist can take the
[Extra Bombs](ref/feat/extra-bombs) feat to gain additional daily uses of his seedpods. His seedpods
also gain one of the following additional effects unless the target succeeds at a Fortitude save (DC
= 10 + half the herbalist's alchemist level + his Wisdom modifier).

**At 1st Level:** His seedpods can [deafen](ref/misc/deafened) their target for 1 round like a
[leaf leshy](ref/monster/leshy-leaf-leshy).

**At 10th Level:** They can [blind](ref/misc/blinded) their target until the end of their next turn
like the water jets of a [seaweed leshy](ref/monster/leshy-seaweed-leshy).

**At 2nd Level:** They can reduce the target's vision to 10 feet for 1 minute (unless the target
spends a full-round action to wash the spores from its eyes early) like the spores of a
[fungus leshy](ref/monster/leshy-fungus-leshy).

**At 5th Level:** They can [entangle](ref/rule/entangled) their target for 2d4 rounds (unless the
target succeeds at a Strength check or Escape Artist check at the save DC to break the vine early)
like the seeds of a [gourd leshy](ref/monster/leshy-gourd-leshy).

**At 8th Level:** They can [sicken](ref/misc/sickened) their target for 1d4 rounds like the spittle
of a [flytrap leshy](ref/monster/leshy-flytrap-leshy).

**Replaces:** Bombs, poison use, poison resistance, poison immunity
