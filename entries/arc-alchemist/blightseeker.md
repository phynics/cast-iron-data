**Sources:** Heroes of the Darklands pg 10\
Blightseekers are alchemists devoted to the study of fungi native to the Darklands. While diverse
fungi exist on the surface world, the unique combination of environmental conditions found in the
Darklands fosters the growth of unpredictable and otherworldly fungal growths.

**Spore Bomb (Su)**

**Ability:** A blightseeker creates a special kind of alchemist bomb that delivers a payload of
alchemically engineered fungal spores. These bombs infest their target with a virulent blight (see
below). The blightseeker's bombs deal 1d4 points of damage (instead of 1d6), plus 1d4 points of
damage for every 2 alchemist levels beyond 1st.

**Alters:** Bombs

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** A creature that takes a direct hit from a spore bomb must succeed at a Fortitude save
(DC = 10 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) the blightseeker's level + his Intelligence
modifier) or suffer the associated blight effects. Creatures that take splash damage from the bomb
are not affected by the blight.

**Blights (Ex)**

**Alters:** Bombs

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A blightseeker gains the blights from the list below. When he prepares bombs at the
start of the day, a blightseeker chooses which blight to apply to each bomb. If a blight has no
duration listed, it lasts a number of rounds equal to the blightseeker's Intelligence modifier.

**Impairing**

**Passive:** The target's base land speed decreases by 10 feet, to a minimum of 10 feet. This effect
doesn't stack with itself.

**Minor Sickening**

**Passive:** The target is [sickened](ref/misc/sickened), but it takes only a -1 penalty on affected
rolls.

**Blights (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** At 6th level, a blightseeker adds the following blights to the list of those that can
be applied to his bombs.

**Replaces:** The discovery gained at 6th level

**Fatiguing**

**Passive:** The target is [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued).

**Fear-Inducing**

**Passive:** The target is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken).

**Sickening**

**Passive:** The target is sickened.

**Blights (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Passive:** At 12th level, a blightseeker adds the following blights to the list of those blights
that can be applied to his bombs.

**Replaces:** The discovery gained at 12th level

**Confusing**

**Passive:** The target is [confused](ref/misc/confused).

**Exhausting**

**Passive:** The target is [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted).

**Nauseating**

**Passive:** The target is [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round.

**Staggering**

**Passive:** The target becomes [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round.

**Blight Engineering (Ex)**

**Ability:** A blightseeker can apply two blights that he knows to each spore bomb he creates.

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Replaces:** Persistent mutagen

**Blights (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Passive:** At 18th level, a blightseeker adds the following blights to the list of those that can
be selected.

**Replaces:** The discovery gained at 18th level

**Blinding**

**Passive:** The target is [blinded](ref/misc/blinded).

**Deafening**

**Passive:** The target is [deafened](ref/misc/deafened).

**Stunning**

**Passive:** The target is [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round.
