**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 46\
Tinkerers constantly dabble in clockworking, creating special familiars that they regularly upgrade
and with which they form bizarre bonds. Many worship [Brigh](ref/faith/brigh) and can be found
throughout Golarion, especially in dwarven and gnome communities.

**Clockwork Bond (Ex)**

**Ability:** With 1 minute of work and a successful DC 15 Heal check, a tinkerer can restore 1d4 hit
points to her clockwork spy familiar.

**At 12th Level:** The tinkerer can now heal 3d6 hit points.

**At 16th Level:** The tinkerer can now heal 4d6 hit points.

**At 20th Level:** The tinkerer can now heal 5d6 hit points.

**At 3rd Level:** If the tinkerer has the [infusion](ref/discovery/infusion) discovery, a clockwork
spy familiar can deliver the tinkerer's extracts with a range of touch for her.

**At 4th Level:** The tinkerer can now restore 1d6 hit points to her familiar.

**At 7th Level:** A clockwork spy can communicate with constructs with the clockwork subtype.

**At 8th Level:** The tinkerer can now restore 2d6 hit points to her familiar.

**Info:** A tinkerer forms a bond with one of her creations, and begins play with it at no cost.
This functions as the [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) option of the wizard's arcane bond class
feature, with the tinkerer's effective wizard level equal to her alchemist level. If a tinkerer
would gain a familiar through another class, those levels stack for purposes of determining the
familiar's abilities.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A tinkerer's [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) can take any shape the tinkerer wishes,
but its statistics and abilities are identical to a
[clockwork spy](ref/monster/clockwork-clockwork-spy) with the normal adjustments and abilities for a
familiar applied, and it is a [construct](ref/type/construct) with the
[clockwork](ref/subtype/clockwork) subtype. Unlike most familiars, a clockwork spy does not grant
special abilities to its master.

**Replaces:** Mutagen

**Special:** A tinkerer cannot choose the _cognatogen_ or _mutagen_ discoveries.

**Tinkering (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**At 5th Level:** The tinkerer can choose a second option from the list. The same option cannot be
chosen twice.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Poison resistance +2, poison use, poison resistance +4

**Special:** A tinkerer can change the enhancements applied to her familiar, but doing so takes 1
hour of work per enhancement she wishes to change.

A tinkerer can spend 1 hour working on her clockwork spy familiar to enhance it with one of the
options from the following list.

- The clockwork spy familiar gains DR 2/adamantine.
- The clockwork spy familiar gains resist cold 5.
- The clockwork spy familiar gains resist fire 5.
- The clockwork spy's slam attack deals 1d3 points of damage.
- The clockwork spy gains a +5 alchemical bonus on Stealth checks.
- The clockwork spy gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 120 feet.
- The clockwork spy gains a +2 resistance bonus on Reflex saving throws.
- The clockwork spy gains a +2 resistance bonus on Will saving throws.
- The clockwork spy gains a +2 natural armor bonus.
- The clockwork familiar's maneuverability is average.

**Clockwork Familiar (Ex)**

**Ability:** The tinkerer turns her familiar into a
[clockwork familiar](ref/monster/clockwork-clockwork-familiar) that takes any form she wishes. She
must choose which type of item is installed in the clockwork familiar. She gains the clockwork
familiar at no cost, as if she were a 7th-level spellcaster with the
[Improved Familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar) feat.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Swift poisoning

**Greater Tinkering (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Poison resistance +6

A tinkerer can spend 1 hour working on her familiar to enhance it with one of the options from the
following list. A tinkerer can change the enhancement applied to her familiar, but doing so takes 1
hour.

- The clockwork familiar's bite attack deals 1d4 points of damage.
- The clockwork familiar gains DR 10/adamantine.
- The clockwork familiar gains any feat for which it qualifies as a bonus feat.
- The clockwork familiar gains a +10 alchemical bonus on Stealth checks.

**Clockwork Upgrade (Ex)**

**Ability:** A tinkerer has learned how to change the type of magic item her clockwork familiar can
carry. This requires 1 hour of uninterrupted work. A tinkerer can change the type of item her
clockwork familiar carries only once every 24 hours.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Move:** A tinkerer can remove a spent item and install a new one.

**Replaces:** Poison immunity

**Clockwork Mimicries (Ex)**

**Ability:** A tinkerer can use her mechanical mastery to create clockwork mimicries of powerful
magic items. Doing so takes 1 hour of uninterrupted work, and allows the tinkerer to create a
wondrous item that functions for a number of hours per day equal to her alchemist level. When
creating such a clockwork item, the tinkerer must use materials worth an amount equal to the
wondrous item's construction cost. She need not meet any of the item's other creation requirements.
A tinkerer cannot create a mimicry of a consumable wondrous item or a wondrous item with finite
charges, and she can have only one mimicry at a time. The clockwork item never functions as a
wondrous item for any other creature.

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Replaces:** Persistent mutagen

**Special:** The tinkerer can spend 1 hour disassembling a clockwork mimicry and reassembling it
into a mimicry of a different wondrous item. When she disassembles a clockwork mimicry, she can
salvage parts worth [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of the mimicry's creation cost. She
still must pay the full creation cost for the new mimicry. This resets the duration the mimicry
functions per day.

**Discoveries:** The following discoveries complement the tinkerer archetype:
[combine extracts](ref/discovery/combine-extracts), [dilution](ref/discovery/dilution),
[elixir of life](ref/discovery/elixir-of-life), [enhance potion](ref/discovery/enhance-potion),
[eternal potion](ref/discovery/eternal-potion), [extend potion](ref/discovery/extend-potion),
[infusion](ref/discovery/infusion), and [promethean disciple](ref/discovery/promethean-disciple).
