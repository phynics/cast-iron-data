A vivisectionist studies bodies to better understand their function. Unlike a
[chirurgeon](ref/arc-alchemist/chirurgeon), a vivisectionist's goals are not related to healing, but
rather to experimentation and knowledge that most people would consider evil. A vivisectionist has
the following class features.

**Sneak Attack**

**Ability:** A vivisectionist gains the [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) ability as a rogue
of the same level. If a character already has sneak attack from another class, the levels from the
classes that grant sneak attack stack to determine the effective rogue level for the sneak attack's
extra damage dice (so an alchemist 1/rogue 1 has a +1d6 sneak attack like a 2nd-level rogue, an
alchemist 2/rogue 1 has a +2d6 sneak attack like a 3rd-level rogue, and so on).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Bomb

**Bleeding Attack**

**Ability:** A vivisectionist may select the [bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack) rogue
talent in place of a discovery.

**Torturer's Eye**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A vivisectionist adds [deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch) to his formula book as a
1st-level extract.

**Cruel Anatomist**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A vivisectionist may use his Knowledge (nature) skill bonus in place of his Heal skill
bonus.

**Torturous Transformation**

**Ability:** A vivisectionist adds [anthropomorphic animal](ref/spell/anthropomorphic-animal) to his
formula book as a 2nd-level extract. When he uses this extract, he injects it into an animal as part
of a 2-hour surgical procedure. By using multiple doses of this extract as part of the surgery, he
multiplies the duration by the number of extracts used.

**At 15th Level:** A vivisectionist adds [regenerate](ref/spell/regenerate) to his formula book as a
5th-level extract.

**At 9th Level:** A vivisectionist adds [awaken](ref/spell/awaken) and
[baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph) to his formula book as 3rd-level extracts. When he
uses the _[awaken](ref/spell/awaken)_ or _[baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph)_ extract,
he injects it into the target (not a plant) as part of a 24-hour surgical procedure. He can make
_[anthropomorphic animal](ref/spell/anthropomorphic-animal)_ [permanent](ref/spell/permanency) on a
creature by spending 7,500 gp.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Crippling Strike**

**At 10th Level:** A vivisectionist may select the [crippling strike](ref/talent/crippling-strike)
rogue talent in place of a discovery.

**Discoveries:** The following discoveries complement the vivisectionist archetype:
[alchemical simulacrum](ref/discovery/alchemical-simulacrum),
[concentrate poison](ref/discovery/concentrate-poison),
[doppelganger simulacrum](ref/discovery/doppelganger-simulacrum),
[feral mutagen](ref/discovery/feral-mutagen), [parasitic twin](ref/discovery/parasitic-twin),
[preserve organs](ref/discovery/preserve-organs), [tentacle](ref/discovery/tentacle),
[tumor familiar](ref/discovery/tumor-familiar), [vestigial arm](ref/discovery/vestigial-arm), and
[wings](ref/discovery/wings).
