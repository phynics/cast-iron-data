**Sources:** Familiar Folio pg 8\
One of the grand goals of alchemy is the ability to create new life. A homunculist has made this
dream a reality, growing and modifying a familiar in his own laboratory.

**Diminished Poisoning**

**Passive:** A homunculist doesn't gain the _poison use, poison resistance, poison immunity,_ or
_swift poisoning_ class features.

**Homunculus Familiar (Ex)**

**Ability:** The homunculist has created a living [homunculus](ref/monster/homunculus) in the shape
of an animal or vermin. It functions in all ways as a [familiar](ref/ability/familiars), treating
the homunculist's alchemist level as his effective wizard level.

**Replaces:** Mutagen

**Experimentation (Ex)**

**Ability:** A homunculist is constantly experimenting on his [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) to
give it new and unusual features and abilities. The homunculist can grant his familiar 1 evolution
point worth of eidolon evolutions for every 4 alchemist levels he possesses, though he can't select
any evolutions that require a particular base form. Each time the homunculist gains a level, he can
change his familiar's evolutions. These evolutions stack with those from the
[Evolved Familiar](ref/feat/evolved-familiar) feat.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** The homunculist can select the Evolved Familiar feat as an alchemist discovery if he
meets its prerequisites.
