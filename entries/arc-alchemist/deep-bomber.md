Consumed with keeping hidden from the horrors below the surface, svirfneblin use their racial
proclivity for alchemy and their inherent talent for obfuscation to strike their enemies from the
darkness and retreat unseen. A deep bomber has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Svirfneblin

**Silent Bomb**

**Ability:** When the deep bomber creates a bomb, he can choose to have it explode without making
any noise, although those damaged by it may cry out.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Poison use

**Targeting Bomb**

**Ability:** When the deep bomber creates a bomb, he can choose to have its detonation include a
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire) effect that applies to all creatures within the splash radius
(including the target, if any). This cannot be used with other discoveries that modify bombs and
have an only-one-per-bomb restriction.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Instant alchemy

**Special:** The deep bomber gains swift alchemy at 18th level.

**Stonekin**

**At 6th Level:** The deep bomber automatically learns [tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape) as a
2nd-level extract, except instead of a tree, he takes the form of a stalagmite that is the same size
as his current size.

**At 7th Level:** He automatically learns [meld into stone](ref/spell/meld-into-stone) as a
3rd-level extract.

**Replaces:** Swift poisoning

**Swift Alchemy**

**Ability:** The deep bomber gains the _swift alchemy_ alchemist ability at 18th level.

**Alters:** Swift alchemy

**Gained:** At 18th Level

**Discoveries:** The following discoveries complement the deep bomber archetype:
[delayed bomb](ref/discovery/delayed-bomb), [dispelling bomb](ref/discovery/dispelling-bomb),
[fast bombs](ref/discovery/fast-bombs), [infusion](ref/discovery/infusion),
[madness bomb](ref/discovery/madness-bomb), [poison bomb](ref/discovery/poison-bomb).
