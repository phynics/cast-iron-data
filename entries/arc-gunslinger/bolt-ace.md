**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 94\
While gunslingers are full of sound and fury, there is a class of gunslingers that never soil their
hands with powder or feel the sting of gun smoke. They commit their deeds in a more hushed manner,
but with just as much flair.

**Crossbow Maven**

**Alters:** Weapon proficiencies

**Info:** A bolt ace is proficient with all crossbows instead of all firearms and begins play with a
masterwork crossbow of her choice.

**Replaces:** Gunsmith

**Grit (Ex)**

**Alters:** Grit

**Passive:** A bolt ace regains grit when she scores a critical hit or deals a killing blow with any
kind of crossbow.

**Deeds**

**Info:** A bolt ace can perform the following deeds with a crossbow instead of a firearm:
[gunslinger initiative](ref/deed/gunslinger-initiative), [pistol-whip](ref/deed/pistol-whip),
[dead shot](ref/deed/dead-shot), targeting, [bleeding wound](ref/deed/bleeding-wound),
[death's shot](ref/deed/deaths-shot), and [stunning shot](ref/deed/stunning-shot). The bolt ace
swaps the following deeds.

**Sharp Shoot (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bolt ace can resolve an attack against touch AC instead of normal AC when firing a
crossbow at a target within its first range increment. Performing this deed costs 1 grit point. This
deed's cost cannot be reduced by any ability or effect that reduces the amount of grit points a deed
costs (such as [Signature Deed](ref/feat/signature-deed)).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Deadeye

**Vigilant Loading (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** As long as a bolt ace has at least 1 grit point, she does not provoke attacks of
opportunity when loading a crossbow.

**Replaces:** Quick clear

**Shooter's Resolve (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bolt ace can spend 1 grit point when making a crossbow attack as a **standard
action** and ignore the effects of [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (though not total
concealment) and [cover](ref/rule/cover) (other than total cover) against that shot.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Utility shot

**Distracting Shot (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bolt ace can spend 1 grit point and choose to miss a target that she could normally
attack within her range with a crossbow attack. When she does, the target loses its Dexterity bonus
to AC (if any) for 1 round.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Startling shot

**Vigilant Shooter (Ex)**

**Ability:** As long as a bolt ace spends 1 grit point when she does so, she does not provoke
attacks of opportunity when firing a crossbow.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Expert loading

**Inexplicable Reload (Ex)**

**Ability:** Loading a crossbow becomes unthinking and automatic for a bolt ace. As long as she has
at least 1 grit point, she always starts each round of combat (even a surprise round) with her
crossbow loaded. Also the amount of time needed to reload a crossbow decreases by one step: a
standard action becomes a move action, a move action becomes a swift action, a swift action becomes
a free action, and a free action becomes not an action.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Lightning reload

**Pinning Shot (Ex)**

**Ability:** The bolt ace can spend 1 grit point while shooting a crossbow and attempt to pin down
its target with the bolt. If the crossbow attack hits, it pins the target to a nearby object, to a
wall, or to the ground, [entangling](ref/rule/entangled) and [staggering](ref/misc/staggered) the
target. While pinned by the bolt, the target cannot move out of its space until it takes a standard
action to free itself from the pinning bolt, except by means of teleportation.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Menacing shot

**Crossbow Training (Ex)**

**Choice:** A bolt ace can select one specific type of crossbow, such as
[hand crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/hand-crossbow) or [heavy crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/heavy-crossbow). She
gains a bonus on damage rolls equal to her Dexterity modifier with that crossbow. Furthermore, when
she scores a critical hit with that type of crossbow, her critical modifier increases by 1 (a
&times&2 becomes a &times&3, for example).

**At 13th Level:** She can pick a third crossbow.

**At 17th Level:** She can pick a fourth crossbow.

**At 9th Level:** She can pick a different type of crossbow, gaining the bonus damage and increased
critical multiplier with that type of crossbow as well.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Gun training
