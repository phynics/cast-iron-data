Pistols and other one-handed firearms are often easy to conceal and faster to load than other
firearms, and do sufficient damage for most situations. But some gunslingers want a little more
firepower and range. These are the musket masters; the best of these gunslingers can reload a musket
with dazzling speed, potentially putting even their pistol-wielding brethren to shame.

**Weapon Proficiency**

**Info:** Instead of proficiency with all firearms, a musket master only gains proficiency with
two-handed firearms. She must take the
[Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (firearm) feat to gain proficiency
with one-handed firearms and firearm siege engines.

**Gunsmith**

**Alters:** Gunsmith

**Info:** A musket master must take a [musket](ref/eq-weapon/musket) when she chooses a battered
firearm at 1st level.

**Deeds**

**Info:** A musket master swaps a pair of deeds for the following.

**Steady Aim (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Move:** As long as a musket master has at least 1 grit point, she can increase the accuracy of a
two-handed firearm. When she does, she increases the range increment of the firearm she is firing by
10 feet. This stacks with other abilities that increase her range increment.

**Replaces:** Gunslinger's dodge deed

**Fast Musket (Ex)**

**Ability:** As long as the musket master has 1 grit point, she can reload any two-handed firearm as
if it were a one-handed firearm.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Utility shot deed

**Rapid Reloader**

**Ability:** A musket master gains [Rapid Reload](ref/feat/rapid-reload) (muskets) as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Musket Training (Ex)**

**Ability:** A musket master increases her skill with two-handed firearms. She gains a bonus on
damage rolls equal to her Dexterity modifier, and when she misfires with a two-handed firearm, the
misfire value increases by 2 instead of 4.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Firearm training 1, 2, 3, and 4

At 9th level: The damage bonus +1.

At 13th level: The damage bonus +2.

At 17th level: The damage bonus +3.
