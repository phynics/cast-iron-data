**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 73\
Freebooters who cling to the convoluted codes that rule independent ships, the buccaneer is a
gunslinger of the high seas. Their exploits are fueled by the thrill of danger and often by the
temporary courage provided by grog. A buccaneer has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Human

**Deeds**

**Info:** A buccaneer swaps four of the normal gunslinger deeds for the following deeds.

**Seadog's Gait (Ex)**

**Ability:** The buccaneer gains [Sea Legs](ref/feat/sea-legs) as a bonus feat. If she spends 1 grit
point, she can ignore [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) until the end of her turn.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Quick clear deed

**Pirate's Jargon (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** The buccaneer's baffling palaver of nautical jargon and piratical cant provides a +2
bonus on Bluff and Intimidate checks.

**Replaces:** Pistol whip deed

**Swift:** She can spend 1 grit point to cause a single living creature within 30 feet to make a
Will saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 her buccaneer level + her Charisma modifier) or become
[confused](ref/misc/confused) for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting language-dependent effect.

**Rope Swing (Ex)**

**Ability:** If she spends 1 grit point, her move while climbing or swinging on a rope does not
provoke attacks of opportunity.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** As long as the buccaneer has at least 1 grit point, she gains a bonus on Acrobatics and
Climb checks equal to her gunslinger level when climbing or swinging on a rope.

**Replaces:** Dead shot deed

**Captain's Curse (Sp)**

**Ability:** A buccaneer can spend 2 grit points to use
[old salt's curse](ref/spell/old-salts-curse) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 15th Level:** She may use [black mark](ref/spell/black-mark) instead.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Lightning reload deed

**Special:** The caster level for these [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) is
equal to the buccaneer's class level, and the save DC is equal to 10 + her buccaneer level + her
Charisma modifier.

**Grit (Ex)**

**Alters:** Grit

**Flavor:** Like the sea itself, a buccaneer is a force of nature.

**Info:** Instead of using her Wisdom modifier to determine the number of grit points she gains at
the start of each day, she uses her Charisma modifier. This ability works in all other ways like the
gunslinger's _grit_ class feature.

**Liquid Courage (Ex)**

**Ability:** A buccaneer gains the ability to fortify her grit with strong drink. The act of
drinking is a **standard action** that provokes attacks of opportunity, and each drink provides 1
grog point. Grog points can be used in place of grit points to fuel deeds or grit feats (including
those requiring a minimum of 1 grit point to use).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** She gains a morale bonus on saves against fear and a dodge bonus to AC against attacks
of opportunity equal to her current grog point total.

**Replaces:** Nimble

**Special:** The buccaneer can gain a maximum number of grog points each day equal to her
Constitution modifier (minimum 1), and they last for 1 hour or until used, whichever comes first.

**Bonus Feat**

**Info:** In addition to combat and grit feats, a buccaneer can select from the following feats as
her bonus feats: [Expert Driver](ref/feat/expert-driver) (water vehicles only),
[Master Siege Engineer](ref/feat/master-siege-engineer), [Siege Engineer](ref/feat/siege-engineer),
[Siege Gunner](ref/feat/siege-gunner), [Skilled Driver](ref/feat/skilled-driver) (water vehicles
only).

**Exotic Pet (Ex)**

**Ability:** A buccaneer gains a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) as a [wizard](ref/class/wizard)
of half the buccaneer's class level (though the exotic pet never gains the ability to _deliver touch
spells_ or _share spells_). This pet is typically a [monkey](ref/monster/familiar-monkey) or parrot
(use the stats for a [raven](ref/monster/familiar-raven) familiar). Such animals are useful scouts,
even in the thick of combat.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** As long as the pet is within 30 feet of the buccaneer, the buccaneer also gains the
benefit of [evasion](ref/ability/evasion).

**Replaces:** Gun training 1

**Sword and Pistol**

**Ability:** A buccaneer gains [Sword and Pistol](ref/feat/sword-and-pistol) as a bonus feat even if
she does not meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Gun training 2

**Gun Training (Ex)**

**Choice:** The buccaneer selects one specific type of firearm (such as an
[axe musket](ref/eq-weapon/axe-musket), [blunderbuss](ref/eq-weapon/blunderbuss),
[musket](ref/eq-weapon/musket), or [pistol](ref/eq-weapon/pistol)). She gain a bonus equal to her
Dexterity modifier on damage rolls when firing that type of firearm. Furthermore, when she misfires
with that type of firearm, the misfire value of that firearm increases by 2 instead of 4.

**Info:** This is identical to the normal gunslinger ability of the same name, except the buccaneer
gains it late, and only chooses a single type of firearm.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Gun training 3

**Raider's Riposte (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** Whenever an enemy misses a buccaneer with an attack of opportunity, it provokes an
attack of opportunity from the buccaneer.

**Replaces:** Gun training 4
