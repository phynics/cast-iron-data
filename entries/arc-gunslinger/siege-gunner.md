The Grand Duchy of Alkenstar has spawned all manner of firearm specialists, wielding small and large
firearms alike. Siege gunners specialize in the very largest: [cannons](ref/eq-siegeengine/cannon),
[bombards](ref/eq-siegeengine/bombard-standard), and [firedrakes](ref/eq-siegeengine/firedrake).
These specialists are rarely seen outside of Alkenstar, however, in part because the duchy keeps
close tabs on its most powerful guns and few are found elsewhere in the Inner Sea. A few siege
gunners outside of Alkenstar make a living renting out their services and the use of their personal
collection of siege weaponry to causes in which they believe or to the highest bidder.

**Grit (Ex)**

**Info:** A siege gunner relies less on luck and intuition and more on cold calculation and wit. The
number of grit points a siege gunner gains each day and her maximum grit are based on the siege
gunner's Intelligence modifier rather than her Wisdom modifier.

**Engineer Training (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A siege gunner is trained in all aspects of siegecraft, including the workings of siege
engines and knowledge of the structures that are often the target of their assaults.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A siege gunner gains a bonus on Knowledge (engineering) checks equal to 1/2 her
gunslinger level.

**Replaces:** Nimble

**Deeds**

**Info:** A siege gunner swaps a pair of deeds of her choosing for the following.

**Targeted Blast (Ex)**

**Ability:** When using a firearm or siege engine that targets an area rather than a specific
creature, the siege gunner can spend 1 grit point to focus the brunt of the blast on specific
creatures within the target area. She can select a number of creatures equal to 1 +
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her gunslinger level. The target creatures take 1
additional point of damage for each gunslinger level the siege gunner possesses.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Deadeye deed

**Special:** This is precision damage and is not multiplied on a critical hit. Targets must be
selected before any attack rolls or saving throws are made.

**Scattershot (Ex)**

**Ability:** If the siege gunner has at least 1 grit point, she can increase the effectiveness of
[scattering shots](ref/misc/scatter) from hand-held firearms and
[blast shots](ref/eq-siegeengine/blast-shot) from siege engines. The cone radius of scattering shots
or blast shots increases by 5 feet for every three levels the siege gunner possesses (to a maximum
of 30 feet at 18th level).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Gunslinger initiative deed

**Bonus Feats**

**At 12th Level:** The siege gunner gains a bonus feat in addition to those gained by normal
advancement. This must be a combat feat or grit feat, and she must meet the prerequisites as normal.

**At 16th Level:** She gains another bonus combat or grit feat, as above.

**At 20th Level:** She gains a third bonus combat or grit feat, as above.

**At 4th Level:** A siege gunner gains [Siege Engineer](ref/feat/siege-engineer) as a bonus feat.
She does not need to meet the prerequisites for this feat.

**At 8th Level:** She gains [Master Siege Engineer](ref/feat/master-siege-engineer) as a bonus feat.
She does not need to meet the prerequisites for this feat.

**Replaces:** The gunslinger's normal bonus feats
