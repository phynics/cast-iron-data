These long-shot artists are masters at taking down dragons with sharp shooting and light siege
weaponry.

The following are class features for wyrm snipers.

**Gained class skills:** ka

**Artillery Proficiency**

**Ability:** Wyrm snipers are proficient with light [siege engines](ref/rule/siege-engines), in
addition to the normal gunslinger weapon proficiencies.

**Deeds**

**Info:** A wyrm sniper swaps a trio of deeds for the following deeds.

**Munitions Master**

**Ability:** A wyrm sniper can spend 1 grit point to reduce the number of full-round actions (or
move actions, if the sniper has the Master Siege Engineer feat) required to load a light siege
engine by 1.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Startling shot deed

**Skeleton Crew**

**Ability:** As long as a wyrm sniper has at least 1 grit point, she treats all light siege engines
as requiring a crew of one fewer than normal to operate (minimum 1).

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Lightning reload deed

**Anti-Air Targeting**

**Ability:** Whenever a wyrm sniper targets a creature's wings with the targeting deed, the wyrm
sniper completely disables one of the creature's wings on a successful hit. If the wyrm sniper hits
her target, the creature falls to the ground, regardless of its altitude, taking the appropriate
amount of falling damage as a result. As long as the target has at least one functioning wing, it
may attempt a DC 20 Fly check to gain the benefits of [feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall) instead
of plummeting to the ground. In either case, it must succeed at a Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 the wyrm
sniper's level + the wyrm sniper's Wisdom modifier) or fall [prone](ref/rule/prone) when reaching
the ground.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Menacing shot deed

**Siege Commander (Ex)**

**Ability:** A wyrm sniper may select the [Siege Commander](ref/feat/siege-commander) combat feat as
her bonus feat, even if she does not meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Heavy Gunner (Ex)**

**Alters:** Gun training

**At 13th Level:** She may again select another type of light siege engine.

**At 17th Level:** She may again select another type of light siege engine.

**At 5th Level:** A wyrm sniper may select one specific type of light siege weapon (such as a
[ballista](ref/eq-siegeengine/ballista-light) or [light bombard](ref/eq-siegeengine/bombard-light))
in place of a specific type of firearm as part of her _gun training_ class ability.

**At 9th Level:** The gunslinger may again select another type of light siege engine.

**Master Siege Engineer (Ex)**

**Ability:** A wyrm sniper may select the [Master Siege Engineer](ref/feat/master-siege-engineer)
combat feat as her bonus feat, even if she does not meet the feat's prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 12th Level
