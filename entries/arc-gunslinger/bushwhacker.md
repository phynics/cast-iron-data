**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 134\
The bushwhacker specializes in the art of the ambush. For her, gunplay works best when it comes from
a concealed position and is directed against a target that falls with the very first volley and is
dead before the smoke clears. A bushwhacker has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Kobold

**Trembling Grit (Ex)**

**Ability:** In addition to all the ways any gunslinger can regain grit, a bushwhacker who reduces a
helpless or unaware opponent to 0 hit points or fewer also regains 1 grit point. This expands the
way in which the bushwhacker gains grit points, so a bushwhacker who gains grit points in this way
only gains 1 grit point for reducing a helpless or unaware opponent, not 2 grit points. This
otherwise works like the gunslinger's _grit_ ability.

**Alters:** Grit

**Info:** At the start of each day, a bushwhacker gains grit points equal to her Wisdom modifier - 1
(minimum of 1).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Craven Deeds (Ex)**

**Alters:** Deeds

**Info:** A bushwhacker replaces some of her deeds with the following deeds. This ability otherwise
works like the gunslinger's _deed_ ability.

**Shifty Shot (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bushwhacker with at least 1 grit point deals 1d6 points of extra damage when using
her firearm to attack opponents denied their Dexterity bonus to AC (whether the target actually has
a Dexterity bonus or not).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Utility shot deed

**Special:** Except for the requirement that she must use a firearm and must have at least 1 grit
point to use it, this deed otherwise works identically to a rogue's
[sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) class feature (including the requirement that she must be
within 30 feet of her target to deal this extra damage), and stacks with the _sneak shot_ ability
(see below) and _sneak attack._

**Long Range Shifty Shot (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bushwhacker can double the range at which she deals extra damage from a _sneak
attack_ or _shifty shot_ (from 30 feet to 60 feet) with a firearm by spending 1 grit point.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Targeting deed

**Special:** When she spends this grit point, she must still have at least 1 grit point left to deal
extra damage from _shifty shot_ if she is using this deed.

**Sneak Shot (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bushwhacker deals extra damage when using a firearm to attack opponents who are
unable to properly defend themselves. The extra damage is a number of d6s equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her gunslinger level.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feats the gunslinger gains at 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 20th level

**Special:** Unlike with the _shifty shot_ ability, she deals this extra damage regardless of
whether or not she has any grit points when making the attack. Except for the requirement that she
makes a firearm attack, this works identically to a rogue's _sneak attack_ class feature. This extra
damage stacks with _sneak attack_ and the damage from _shifty shot._
