**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 50\
Gunslingers usually don't use heavy armor, but there are a few who use and modify their armor to
protect themselves from both traditional weapons and gunfire. These gun tanks trundle into battle
with barrels blazing, dealing out punishment with their firearms while knowing few can penetrate
their hard protective shells.

**Armor Proficiency**

**Ability:** A gun tank is proficient with all armors and shields, including tower shields.

**Deeds**

**Info:** A gun tank swaps a pair of deeds for the following.

**Gun Tank's Resolve (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The gun tank can shrug off the most serious of attacks.

**Immediate:** When the gun tank is wearing medium or heavy armor and is subject to a critical hit
or [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack), she can spend 1 grit point to attempt to negate the
critical hit or sneak attack damage. She has a 25% chance of doing so.

**At 10th Level:** The negation chance becomes 50%.

**At 15th Level:** The chance increases to 75%.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Gunslinger's dodge deed

**Special:** While a gun tank's resolve does not stack with the
[fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-light) armor special ability, it does work in concert
with that armor special ability or similar effects, so a gun tank can use this ability even after
the armor of fortification has failed to negate the critical hit or sneak attack damage.

**Gun Tank's Resilience (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** When the gun tank has at least 1 grit point and makes a successful Fortitude saving
throw against an attack that would deal half damage or have a partial effect, she takes no damage or
other effects from that attack. Furthermore, she gains a +2 bonus on all saving throws against fear
and mind-affecting effects.

**Replaces:** Evasive deed

**Bullet Defection (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A gun tank is adept at modifying and using her armor to stop firearm attacks.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A gun tank wearing medium or heavy armor gains half the armor's bonus plus the armor's
enhancement bonus (if any) as a deflection bonus against any non-siege firearm or splash weapon
attack (including the [alchemist's](ref/class/alchemist) bomb class ability). This ability has no
affect on spells, [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or supernatural abilities
that make a touch attack.

**Replaces:** Nimble

**Armor Training (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A gun tank learns to be more maneuverable while wearing armor.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** Whenever she is wearing armor, the armor check penalty is reduced by
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her gunslinger level (to a minimum of 0) and the
maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by her armor increases by one-fourth of her gunslinger level.

**Replaces:** All bonus combat/grit feats

**Special:** This ability stacks with the [fighter](ref/class/fighter) class ability of the same
name. If the gun tank has both class features, she takes the most advantageous benefit of the two
class features.
