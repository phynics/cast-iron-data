## Type: Ooze

**Sources:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary pg 308

An ooze is an amorphous or mutable creature, usually mindless.

An ooze has the following features.

- d8 Hit Die.
- Base attack bonus equal to 3/4 total Hit Dice (medium progression).
- No good saving throws.
- Skill points equal to 2 + Int modifier (minimum 1) per Hit Die. However, most oozes are mindless
  and gain no skill points or feats. Oozes do not have any class skills.

**Traits:** An ooze possesses the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry).

- **Mindless:** No Intelligence score, and immunity to all mind-affecting effects (charms,
  compulsions, phantasms, patterns, and morale effects). An ooze with an Intelligence score loses
  this trait.
- [Blind](ref/misc/blinded) (but have the [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) special quality), with
  immunity to gaze attacks, visual effects, illusions, and other attack forms that rely on sight.
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to poison, sleep effects, paralysis, polymorph, and stunning.
- Some oozes have the ability to deal acid damage to objects.
- Not subject to critical hits or [flanking](ref/rule/flanking). Does not take additional damage
  from precision-based attacks, such as [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack).
- Proficient with its natural weapons only.
- Proficient with no armor.
- Oozes eat and breathe, but do not sleep.
