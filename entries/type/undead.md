## Type: Undead

**Sources:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary pg 309

Undead are once-living creatures animated by spiritual or supernatural forces.

An undead creature has the following features.

- d8 Hit Die.
- Base attack bonus equal to 3/4 total Hit Dice (medium progression).
- Good Will Saves.
- Skill points equal to 4 + Int modifier (minimum 1) per Hit Die. Many undead, however, are mindless
  and gain no skill points or feats. The following are class skills for undead:
  [Climb](ref/skill/climb), [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise), [Fly](ref/skill/fly),
  [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), [Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana),
  [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion), [Perception](ref/skill/perception),
  [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft), and
  [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth).

**Traits:** An undead creature possesses the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a
creature's entry).

- No Constitution score. Undead use their Charisma score in place of their Constitution score when
  calculating hit points, Fortitude saves, and any special ability that relies on Constitution (such
  as when calculating a breath weapon's DC).
- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet.
- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects,
  patterns, and phantasms).
- Immunity to bleed, death effects, disease, paralysis, poison, sleep effects, and stunning.
- Not subject to nonlethal damage, ability drain, or energy drain. Immune to damage to its physical
  ability scores (Constitution, Dexterity, and Strength), as well as to exhaustion and fatigue
  effects.
- Cannot heal damage on its own if it has no Intelligence score, although it can be healed. Negative
  energy (such as an [inflict](ref/spell/inflict-light-wounds) spell) can heal undead creatures. The
  [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) special quality works regardless of the creature's
  Intelligence score.
- Immunity to any effect that requires a Fortitude save (unless the effect also works on objects or
  is harmless).
- Not at risk of death from massive damage, but is immediately destroyed when reduced to 0 hit
  points.
- Not affected by [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) and [reincarnate](ref/spell/reincarnate) spells
  or abilities. [Resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection) and
  [true resurrection](ref/spell/true-resurrection) can affect undead creatures. These spells turn
  undead creatures back into the living creatures they were before becoming undead.
- Proficient with its natural weapons, all simple weapons, and any weapons mentioned in its entry.
- Proficient with whatever type of armor (light, medium, or heavy) it is described as wearing, as
  well as all lighter types. Undead not indicated as wearing armor are not proficient with armor.
  Undead are proficient with shields if they are proficient with any form of armor.
- Undead do not breathe, eat, or sleep.
