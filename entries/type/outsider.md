## Type: Outsider

**Sources:** Pathfinder RPG Bestiary pg 309

An outsider is at least partially composed of the essence (but not necessarily the material) of some
plane other than the Material Plane. Some creatures start out as some other type and become
outsiders when they attain a higher (or lower) state of spiritual existence.

An outsider has the following features.

- d10 Hit Dice.
- Base attack bonus equal to total Hit Dice (fast progression).
- Two good saving throws, usually Reflex and Will.
- Skill points equal to 6 + Int modifier (minimum 1) per Hit Die. The following are class skills for
  outsiders: [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any),
  [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes), [Perception](ref/skill/perception),
  [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), and [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth). Due to their varied
  nature, outsiders also receive 4 additional class skills determined by the creature's theme.

**Traits:** An outsider possesses the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's
entry).

- [Darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet.
- Unlike most living creatures, an outsider does not have a dual nature—its soul and body form one
  unit. When an outsider is slain, no soul is set loose. Spells that restore souls to their bodies,
  such as [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead), [reincarnate](ref/spell/reincarnate), and
  [resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection), don't work on an outsider. It takes a different magical
  effect, such as [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [miracle](ref/spell/miracle),
  [true resurrection](ref/spell/true-resurrection), or [wish](ref/spell/wish) to restore it to life.
  An outsider with the [native](ref/subtype/native) subtype can be _raised, reincarnated,_ or
  _resurrected_ just as other living creatures can be.
- Proficient with all simple and martial weapons and any weapons mentioned in its entry.
- Proficient with whatever type of armor (light, medium, or heavy) it is described as wearing, as
  well as all lighter types. Outsiders not indicated as wearing armor are not proficient with armor.
  Outsiders are proficient with shields if they are proficient with any form of armor.
- Outsiders breathe, but do not need to eat or sleep (although they can do so if they wish). Native
  outsiders breathe, eat, and sleep.
