**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 168\
**Price** 32,000 gp; **Elixir Price** 1,600 gp\
**Saving Throw DC** 20; **Slot** hands; **Temporary Duration** 1 hour

**Benefit:** The subject's hands are replaced with clawed appendages that can be used as
[natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that a Medium creature can use to deal 1d8 points of
slashing damage (1d6 points if Small). The subject gains a +4 bonus on [grapple](ref/rule/grapple)
combat maneuver checks with the gauntlet.

**Penalty:** The subject can't use the clawed hand to wield a weapon or manipulate objects. It takes
a -4 penalty when performing precision-based tasks involving hands (such as opening locks) if
attempting to do so with one hand.

### Creation Requirements

**Cost** 16,000 gp\
[Fleshwarper](ref/feat/fleshwarper), Craft (alchemy) 13 ranks; Heal DC 23; **Elixir Cost** 800 gp
