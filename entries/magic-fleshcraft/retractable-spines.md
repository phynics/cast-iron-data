**Price** 24,000 gp; **Elixir Price** 650 gp\
**Saving Throw DC** 15; **Slot** wrists; **Temporary Duration** 1 hour

**Benefit:** Serrated, reptilian spines run along portions of the subject's arms. As a standard
action, the subject can extend or retract a spine in one or both of its forearms. A Medium creature
can use these spines as [natural weapons](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deal 1d6 points of piercing
damage (1d4 points if Small), though as with claws, an attack with the spines uses the corresponding
hand. The subject also gains a +2 bonus on grapple combat maneuver checks when both spines are
extended.

**Penalty:** The subject has a hard time wielding manufactured weapons and performing
precision-based tasks involving hands (such as opening locks) while the spines are extended. Attacks
with such weapons and skill or ability checks for such tasks take a -2 penalty. If the subject gains
this penalty because of a failed saving throw after drinking a
[fleshcraft elixir](ref/feat/fleshwarper), the skin of its arms calcifies and it takes this penalty
for the entire duration, even though it does not gain the use of the spines.

### Creation Requirements

**Cost** 12,000 gp\
[Fleshwarper](ref/feat/fleshwarper), Craft (alchemy) 10 ranks; Heal DC 20; **Elixir Cost** 325 gp
