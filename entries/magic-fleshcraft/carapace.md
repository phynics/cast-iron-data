**Price** 18,000 gp; **Elixir Price** 600 gp\
**Saving Throw DC** 19; **Slot** body; **Temporary Duration** 1 hour

**Benefit:** A chitinous carapace covers the subject's body, attached by a collection of fibrous
nerves. This grants a +4 enhancement bonus to the subject's natural armor bonus.

**Penalty:** The inflexibility of the plates or the stiffness of a failed transformation imposes a
-4 armor check penalty, which stacks with all other armor check penalties.

### Creation Requirements

**Cost** 9,000 gp\
[Fleshwarper](ref/feat/fleshwarper), Craft (alchemy) 14 ranks; Heal DC 24; **Elixir Cost** 300 gp
