**Analagous Order**

**Details:** [Order of the Scourge](ref/hkorder/order-of-the-scourge)

The Hellknights of the Order of the Torrent were mandated in the wake of the Chelish Civil War after
the order's founder, Reya Naulvaneti, rescued the abducted son of Kintargo's then lord-mayor. In
honor of this accomplishment, the Order of the Torrent has focused itself on rescuing the abducted.
The Order of the Torrent appeals to lawful good Hellknights - they have never knowingly had an evil
member among their ranks. The order has diminished in recent years to a historic low of 23 members.
Most of them are spread throughout Avistan, where they search for the lost and taken throughout the
Inner Sea region, but they also often serve as mercenaries aboard ships in waters sailed by slavers.
