**Symbol**

**Details:** Five-pointed iron star

**Headquarters**

**Details:** Citadel Dinyar, in the Aspodell Mountains

**Leader**

**Details:** Lictor Resarc Ountor (LN male fighter 4/cleric 2/Hellknight 6)

**Members**

**Details:** Crusaders, religious zealots

**Armor Features**

**Details:** Breastplate with claw-like vambraces, claw-like helm

**Favored Weapon**

**Details:** [Morningstar](ref/eq-weapon/morningstar)

**Reckoning**

**Benefit:** Whenever you are the target of a lawful divine caster's spell that heals hit point
damage, you heal 1 additional point per die rolled.

**Special:** Additionally, if you cast divine spells, you can use the holy symbols of Abadar,
Asmodeus, Iomedae, Irori, and Torag interchangeably.

**Reckoning:** Flagellate yourself with a five-tailed lash.

Order of the Godclaw Hellknights enforce law with a crusader's zeal, going where they're needed most
in the fight against the forces of chaos. They know no bounds or borders in the quest for absolute
order. To Hellknights of the Godclaw, the cause of order is a holy mandate for which they will
travel far to battle chaos in lands that know little of civilization. To others, these Hellknights'
faith is likely inexplicable - some believe the members of the order are experts in all five
individual faiths or even that they are heretics.

### Tenets

- I will eliminate chaos, whatever its form and wherever it exists, resting only when the world is
  right.
- I will destroy those who root lawlessness in weak hearts and minds.
- I will strive to be worthy of not one, but five.
- I will seek the great principles behind life, the truths spoken through many voices.
- I will be a paragon of order - an unassailable example that others might follow.
- I will commit what I don't know or can't understand to the judgment of the five.

### [Hellknight Obedience](ref/feat/hellknight-obedience) Boons

**1: Pentamic Zeal (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can cast [bane](ref/spell/bane), [bless](ref/spell/bless),
[tireless pursuit](ref/spell/tireless-pursuit), or [wrath](ref/spell/wrath) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) three times per day, with your effective caster
level equal to your character level.

**2: Tireless Judgment (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** You can use this ability four times per day, and the bonuses increase to +4 and
+8.

**Special:** If you already have access to [judgments](ref/ability/judgments), you can use the
_justice_ judgment three times per day (four times at 20th level) without it counting toward your
daily uses, or you can add the _justice_ judgment's effects to another judgment without it counting
as a second or third judgment.

**Swift:** Three times per day, you can gain a +3 sacred bonus on attack rolls, and a +6 sacred
bonus to confirm critical hits. Otherwise, this functions as the
[inquisitor's](ref/class/inquisitor) _justice_ judgment.

**3: Blessing of the Five (Su)**

**Special:** This ability can only revive one creature per use, and a revived creature gains a
temporary [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) that lasts for 1 day. This
ability cannot be modified using any feats or other abilities that affect channeled energy.

**Standard:** Once per day, you can brandish a holy symbol to heal all living creatures in a
30-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) centered on you. Creatures in the area heal 10d6 points of
damage. You can choose up to three creatures in the area that are not healed. Additionally, if a
creature in the area has died within 1 round and this brings its hit point total to a negative
amount less than its Constitution score, the creature returns to life and
[stabilizes](ref/misc/stable).
