**Analagous Order**

**Details:** [Order of the Scourge](ref/hkorder/order-of-the-scourge)

This small order of Hellknights operates out of the tower of Citadel Ordeial in northeastern
Cheliax, where it organizes expeditions to seek out and slay a variety of monsters. The order was
disbanded after the Chelish Civil War, but it was officially reinstated 30 years ago thanks to its
members' continued efforts to weed out monsters in the Whisperwood and beyond.
