**Details:** Hand wrapped in chains

**Details:** Citadel Gheradesca, near Corentyn

**Details:** Lictor Uro Adom (LN male human fighter 5/Hellknight 7)

**Details:** Guards, sleuths, trackers, victims of outlaws

**Armor Features**

**Details:** Lock-and-chain design, manacle-like gauntlets, helms reminiscent of iron prisoners'
masks

**Favored Weapon**

**Details:** Flail

**Reckoning**

**Benefit:** Gain a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks when attempting to [disarm](ref/rule/disarm),
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple), or [trip](ref/rule/trip) opponents.

**Reckoning:** Crush your limbs with tightened chains.

Hellknights of the Chain value social order and responsibility, seeking to root out anarchists and
fugitives. They have reputations as relentless hunters who drag lawbreakers away in chains. These
Hellknights keep meticulous count of the number of criminals they've brought to justice; they never
abandon the pursuit of a fugitive, and they believe everyone has a specific, crucial role to play in
society.

### Tenets

- I will remove those who create barriers to the people's future greatness.
- My desires are secondary to the good of my order, my nation, and my people. They will not distract
  me from my tasks.
- I will remove the foes of the people, both those who call themselves enemies and who call
  themselves friends.
- My quarry and my captives are my charges. I am responsible for their deeds, their lives, and their
  deaths.
- Those in my charge deserve neither mercy nor cruelty.
- I will warden first and judge second. I will uphold the law, no matter the cost.

### [Hellknight Obedience](ref/feat/hellknight-obedience) Boons

**1: Taken Alive (Ex)**

**Ability:** Additionally, while performing your reckoning, you can focus on understanding one
specific individual. For the next 24 hours, you gain a +4 bonus on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception), [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks that relate to noticing, seeing through the disguises of,
interrogating others about the location of, or tracking that individual, or any activity directly
related to doing so.

**Flavor:** You are an expert in hunting your quarry and capturing it alive.

**Passive:** You take no penalty on attack rolls for using a flail to deal nonlethal damage.

**2: Pierce Disguise (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Flavor:** You are suspicious of everyone, and your wariness grants you senses others do not have.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per 2 character levels; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be
spent in 1-minute increments

**3: True Identities (Su)**

**Flavor:** You are not easily fooled by those who would hide their true natures.

**Free:** Three times per day, when you successfully disbelieve an illusion that has a duration of
longer than instantaneous (normally or using _[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing)_), you can
attempt to dispel the illusion. Treat this as a targeted dispel, as per the spell
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), but targeting only illusions and with your caster level
equal to your character level.

**Passive:** You gain a +4 bonus on Will saving throws to disbelieve illusions.
