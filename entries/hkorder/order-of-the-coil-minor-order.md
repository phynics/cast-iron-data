Among the smallest Hellknight factions, the Order of the Coil maintains holdings near the Sargavan
city of Eleder, from where they viciously seek to tame the tribal natives of the country and put an
end to their backward pollution of the outside world. Seeing the efforts of scholars and
adventurers - particularly Pathfinders - as spreading a disease of savagery by carrying curios from
the jungles into the world beyond, the Coil hunts down and destroys such explorers and artifacts,
notorious for ending these perceived corruptions with poison and flames.
