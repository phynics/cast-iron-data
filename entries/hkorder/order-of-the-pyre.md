**Reckoning:** Burn yourself over an open flame.

Hellknights of the Order of the Pyre seek to combat outlandish faiths and beliefs, preventing them
from gaining purchase in the Inner Sea region. They are cult hunters first and foremost, but also
fight to repress other philosophies they deem dangerous. These Hellknights hate and vocally condemn
cultists, priests of rare religions, and followers of mystical orders.

### Tenets

- I will not tolerate the weight of unfit gods upon mortal shoulders.
- My fire will burn those thickets of philosophy that crowd the path of progress.
- Blasphemy knows no borders - land or sea. I will pursue it where I must, and where I find it, I
  will be relentless.
- Zealots walk the path of destruction, regardless of the names on their lips.
- I will not mistake clever trickery for power or power for divinity.
- Manipulators of divine magic and esoteric eldritch powers must prove their trustworthiness to me,
  for trust breeds dangerous complacency.
- I will focus my iron scrutiny on any faith I find appealing. Whatever I worship must be worthy of
  me.

### [Hellknight Obedience](ref/feat/hellknight-obedience) Boons

**1: Favored Cult (Ex)**

**Choice:** Each day while performing your obedience, choose a divine power that grants four or
fewer domains.

**Flavor:** You can hone your skills to become a bane to those who worship a divinity you hate.

**Passive:** You gain +2 bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate),
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all), [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), and
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks against those who worship that power. Additionally, you get a
+1 bonus on weapon attack rolls and weapon damage rolls against the worshipers of that deity.

**2: Immolation (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [delayed blast fireball](ref/spell/delayed-blast-fireball)
using your character level as your caster level.

**Flavor:** You can channel your wrath into power that can scorch your foes.

**3: Quell Zealotry (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can tear the divine power from the grasp of those who would use it to stand against
you.

**Standard:** Once per day, you can target a divine spellcaster within 100 feet. If your target
fails a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma bonus), it loses access to
any spellcasting and all the supernatural and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
it gains from any of its divine spellcasting classes for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your character level.

When a creature under the effects of this ability tries to use an ability to which it no longer has
access, instead of its god's favor, it feels only a profound, black emptiness, as if its deity no
longer existed.
