**Details:** [Lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance) or [halberd](ref/eq-weapon/halberd)

**Reckoning**

**Benefit:** Gain a +4 bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) checks.
Additionally, if you are trained in Knowledge (geography), you automatically succeed at attempts to
use that skill to identify a creature's ethnicity or accent, as long as you are familiar with that
ethnicity or accent.

**Reckoning:** Pierce your flesh with sharp metal.

Order of the Nail Hellknights idealize the civilized society of southern Avistan and seek to unite
all people in advancing that cultural baseline. In fact, the Order of the Nail's members preach that
anyone who doesn't embrace Chelish culture is a criminal and heathen who must be destroyed. The
order's members idealize the lifestyles and challenges of frontier settlers, no matter the
displacement, indignation, or cruelty they might visit on those who were there before.

### Tenets

- I will strike against civilization's foes, braving both thicket and darkness.
- I will rid the world of threats to civilization and make it fit for the law to flourish.
- I will fear no creature, though the enemies of order are terrible.
- I will not be merciful, though the enemies of order may bear familiar guises - and may even be
  individuals I have considered dear.
- I will seek allies and advantage, confounding luck with wisdom.
- I will be the face that monsters dread: fierce, sharp, and unflinching.

### [Hellknight Obedience](ref/feat/hellknight-obedience) Boons

**1: Domination (Ex)**

**Ability:** Select a creature type from the ranger [favored enemies](ref/ability/favored-enemy)
table. You treat that creature type as your favored enemy, as per the ranger ability. If you choose
humanoid (human), you can select one of the following ethnicities Kellid, Mwangi, Shoanti, or Ulfen
(or another clannish or tribal ethnicity, at the GM's discretion). If you choose an ethnicity, the
bonuses on skill checks, weapon attack, and weapon damage rolls increase to +4, but you do not gain
any bonuses against other humans. These bonuses stack with any favored enemy bonuses gained from
other classes.

**2: Identification (Su)**

**Special:** Though unaware of your probing, the target can attempt a Will saving throw to negate
this ability (DC = 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier). Spells such as
[undetectable alignment](ref/spell/undetectable-alignment) may prevent a portion of this information
from being gleaned, while spells such as [nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection) stymie this effect
entirely. This is a divination effect.

**Swift:** Three times per day, you can choose a living creature within sight and know its creature
type, ethnicity (if any), and alignment. Additionally, for a number of hours equal to your character
level, that creature is treated as though you had cast the spell [status](ref/spell/status) upon it.

**3: End Superstition (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [mage's disjunction](ref/spell/mages-disjunction), using
your character level as your caster level.
