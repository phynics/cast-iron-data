**Benefit:** Gain a +4 bonus on Fortitude saving throws to resist poison. This increases to +6

**Reckoning:** Endure internal burns.

Order of the Rack Hellknights hate wastefulness of both thought and deed. They crush pointless
dreams, end rebellions, and trample frivolous or dangerous inventions. They also closely observe
individuals, seeking hints of destructive ambitions and wasteful vices, and are skilled at
destroying seditious writing and strange inventions.

### Tenets

- I will seek the chaos that manifests in common words and thoughts.
- Those who lack direction will find guidance by my hand - whether the palm or the fist.
- I will take the tools from the grasp of those who would craft their own dooms, whether they would
  do so intentionally or unintentionally.
- Though the flag of no nation has my alliance, every rebel is my foe.
- My discipline is the same as my honor. I will make myself worthy of our brethren's tradition of
  greatness.
- My mere presence will represent the pinnacle of orderliness. I will save my words and deeds for
  when they are needed most.
- I will not allow the shedding of tears to rust the wheels of progress.

### [Hellknight Obedience](ref/feat/hellknight-obedience) Boons

**1: Mute (Su)**

**Special:** Each round on its turn, the target can attempt a new saving throw to end the effect.
This effect is [dismissible](ref/misc/dismissible). It does not make the target immune to sonic or
language-based attacks, spells, or effects.

**Standard:** Three times per day, you can target a single creature within 100 feet. If the creature
fails a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier), the creature
cannot make any sounds for a number of rounds equal to your character level. This prevents the
target from speaking, otherwise making noise, casting spells with verbal components, and using
similar abilities with auditory effects.

**2: Dismantle (Su)**

**Special:** You and your possessions can enter the cloud and not be affected. This effect is
dismissible.

**Standard:** Once per day, you can cause a cloud of thick, brown fog to arise from a chosen point
within 200 feet. This cloud functions as per [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud), using your character
level as your caster level, except the fog [spreads](ref/misc/spread) in a 10-foot radius, and every
creature or object within is affected as per [rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp), without any
touch attacks required.

Attended metal objects, including weapons and armor, receive a Fortitude saving throw to negate this
effect using their wielder's Fortitude saving throw bonus (DC = 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your
Charisma modifier). Creatures within the cloud are affected by the rusting effect only once, not
every round, but creatures that move back into the area are exposed again.

**3: Ruin (Sp)**

**Standard:** Once per day, you can use [greater shout](ref/spell/shout-greater) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (DC = 18 + your Charisma modifier).
