Hate-mongers are xenophobic and judgmental rabblerousers who whip up mobs against enemies real or
imagined.

**Alignment**

**Ability:** A hate-monger must be of a nongood alignment. A hate-monger who becomes good cannot
gain new levels as a mesmerist but retains all his class abilities.

**Mental Manipulator (Ex)**

**Ability:** A hate-monger masters a broad repertoire of emotional corruption. At 1st level, he adds
one spell from the 1st-level spells below to his spells known. When he would gain a new mesmerist
trick, he can learn additional spells instead, adding two of the following spells of a level he can
currently cast to his spells known. When he casts a spell he learns in this way, he increases the
duration as if his caster level were 1 higher.

**Alters:** Mesmerist tricks

**At 5th Level:** The save DC is increased by 1 for creatures of his favored enemy type (see
_xenophobe_ below).

**Replaces:** Consummate liar

**Spells:** Cause fear, compel hostility, delusional pride, demand offering, forbid action, lesser
confusion, murderous command, scare, sow thought

**Spells:** Aversion, paranoia, rage, unadulterated loathing

**Spells:** Confusion, crushing despair, curse of disgust, fear, instant enemy, malicious spite,
smug narcissism, spite

**Spells:** Envious urge, feast on fear, greater forbid action, modify memory, utter contempt

**Spells:** Foe to friend, foster hatred, song of discord, repress memory, unwilling shield,
vengeful outrage

**Spells:** Eyebite, insanity, overwhelming presence, symbol of persuasion

**Insidious Hatred (Su)**

**Ability:** He can use his _touch treatment_ only on others, but as part of the action of using
_touch treatment,_ he can cast any targeted spell from the _mental manipulator_ list that does not
have a range of &quot&personal&quot& and has a casting time of 1 standard action or less, targeting
only the creature that receives his _touch treatment._ The target creature takes a -2 penalty on its
saving throw and to its [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) against the spell.

**Alters:** Touch treatment

**Flavor:** A hate-monger can sow hatred and strife under the pretense of assisting others.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Special:** That creature doesn't realize the hatemonger has cast a spell, and considers any
manifestations to be part of the _touch treatment._ Other creatures notice as normal.

**Xenophobe (Ex)**

**Ability:** A hate-monger can choose one [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy), as per the
[ranger](ref/class/ranger) class feature. His favored enemy bonus begins at +2, but he does not gain
additional favored enemies. His favored enemy bonus also applies on Intimidate checks against his
favored enemy.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Mental potency, manifold tricks

At 9th level: The bonus +4.

At 13th level: The bonus +6.

At 17th level: The bonus +8.

**Out for Blood (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** When a creature takes extra damage from the hate-monger's _painful stare,_ it also
takes 1 point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage (or 1 point of bleed damage per 1d6 points of damage
dealt by the _painful stare_ if the hatemonger damages the target himself). This bleed damage does
not stack with itself or other sources of bleed damage.

**Replaces:** 7th-level bold stare

**Insidious Emotions (Su)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** The power of a hatemonger's psychic hold becomes more difficult to detect or to break.
The DC of Sense Motive checks to notice if a creature is under the effects of his enchantments with
the emotion descriptor increases by 5 (if he also has the
[Subtle Enchantments](ref/feat/subtle-enchantments) feat, the effects stack), as does the DC to
identify his emotion spells. Magical divinations can detect his emotion effects only if the caster
succeeds at a caster level check (DC = 11 the hate-monger's mesmerist level) as if the affected
creatures were the subject of [nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection).

**Replaces:** Glib lie
