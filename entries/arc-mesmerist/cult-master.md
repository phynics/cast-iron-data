The leaders of some cults are simply charlatans, bathing in the adulation of their followers. Others
believe themselves to be truly in touch with an unknown power, bringing their unique messages to
those willing to follow and obey them.

**Insidious Personality (Ex)**

**Passive:** A cult master adds 1/2 his class level (minimum 1) as a bonus on all Diplomacy checks.

**Replaces:** Consummate liar

**Fanatical Stare (Su)**

**Ability:** The target of the gaze gains a competence bonus on attack rolls and weapon damage
rolls. This bonus is equal to 1 + one-sixth of his mesmerist level.

**Flavor:** A cult master can turn his gaze on a creature to fill it with fervent belief in the cult
master.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** Instead of taking the _hypnotic stare's_ penalty on Will saves, the target gains a
bonus of an equal amount on Will saves. The bonuses don't apply to attacks against the cult master
or on saves against the cult master's spells or abilities. The cult master can also suppress any
penalties inflicted by his stare for the target.

**Replaces:** Painful stare

**Cult Tricks (Su)**

**Info:** The following new tricks can be selected by a cult master. These tricks can be implanted
only in a cohort or follower of the cult master.

**Double**

**Ability:** An illusion makes either the cult master's cohort or one of his followers appear to be
him. The cult master can trigger this trick when he is targeted by an attack, provided he is
adjacent to the subject, to give the attack a 50% miss chance. If the attack misses the cult master,
it targets the subject instead. This is an illusion (glamer) effect.

**Extol**

**Ability:** A mental compulsion causes a cohort or follower to extol the virtues of the cult
master. The cult master can trigger this trick whenever he attempts a Diplomacy check, as long as he
is within 60 feet of the subject. The subject takes the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action
on the cult master's Diplomacy check. If the subject is successful, the cult master gains a +4 bonus
on his check. This is an enchantment (compulsion) effect.

**Alters:** Mesmerist tricks

**False Healing (Su)**

**Flavor:** A cult master can provide temporary healing by using his psychic powers to trick the
mind.

**At 6th Level:** The cult master can either grant a number of temporary hit points equal to 2d8 +
his Charisma modifier to the target or temporarily remove 1d4 points of
[ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** 3rd- and 6th-level touch treatments

**Special:** This can be used as a **swift action** if the cult master uses it on himself. A
creature can receive the effect of the _false healing_ only once per day. The temporary hit points
or reduction in ability damage lasts a number of hours equal to the cult master's class level.

**Standard:** The cult master must be able to touch his target. The cult master grants a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to 1d8 + his Charisma modifier to the
target.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Faithful Followers (Ex)**

**Ability:** The cult master gains [Leadership](ref/feat/leadership) as a bonus feat.

**Flavor:** A cult master begins to attract followers to his espoused beliefs, thanks to his
magnetic personality.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** 7th-level bold stare

**Fanatical Devotion (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A cult master has manipulated his cohort and followers into becoming utterly devoted to
him.

**At 14th Level:** The cult master no longer takes a penalty to his Leadership score for any cohorts
or followers he has killed or caused to be killed.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** The cult master no longer takes a penalty to his Leadership score for being aloof or
cruel. Additionally, the DCs of all enchantment spells the cult master casts on his cohort and
followers increase by 4.

**Replaces:** 10th- and 14th-level touch treatments

**Masterful Cult Tricks (Su)**

**Alters:** Masterful tricks

**Info:** The following new master tricks can be selected by any cult master of 12th level or
higher. These tricks can be implanted only in a cohort or follower of the cult master.

**Prereq:** 12th-level mesmerist

**Avenge Me**

**Ability:** The cult master creates a compulsion for one of his followers to avenge him. Whenever
the cult master takes hit point damage from an attack, he can trigger this trick as long as he is
within 60 feet of the subject. The subject is immediately affected as if by the
[rage](ref/spell/rage) spell for 1 round per mesmerist level.

**Die for Me**

**Ability:** The cult master instills within either his cohort or one of his followers the desire to
give her life for him. The cult master can trigger this trick whenever he would take hit point
damage from an attack, spell, or effect and the subject is with 60 feet of him. Half the damage is
psychically transferred to the subject. This is an enchantment (compulsion) effect.

**Reborn (Su)**

**Ability:** The cult master can perform a 1-hour ceremony costing 5,000 gp in rare materials in
which he uses his psychic powers to implant a small portion of his essence into his cohort, who must
be a minimum of 17th level. When the cult master dies, in 7 days the cohort physically and mentally
transforms into the cult master as if [clone](ref/spell/clone) had been cast, except that the
cohort's body takes the place of the inert body.

**Flavor:** A cult master can ensure his own survival upon his death.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Rule minds

**Special:** The cult master can't use this ability again until he gains a new cohort of at least
17th level and performs the necessary ceremony. The cohort can't be brought back to life except
through [miracle](ref/spell/miracle) or [wish](ref/spell/wish).
