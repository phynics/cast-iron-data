Mesmerists focus their abilities through the eyes, drawing forth their power as the windows to the
mind and soul. Eyebiters take this a step further. An eyebiter's eyes become so infused with psychic
might that they can leave the eyebiter's body and move about on their own.

**Eyeball Familiar (Ex)**

**Ability:** An eyebiter imbues one of his eyes with limited sentience, so that he can pluck it out
and allow it to move on its own. The _eyeball familiar_ functions similarly to the
[tumor familiar](ref/discovery/tumor-familiar) alchemist discovery except as follows. The _eyeball
familiar_ does not have [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing). The _eyeball familiar_ doesn't
resemble an animal, and its size is Fine for a Medium or smaller eyebiter (or one size category
larger for every size category the eyebiter is larger than Medium). It has a fly speed of 20 feet
(perfect maneuverability) and no physical attacks (though it can still deliver touch spells once the
eyebiter has reached 3rd level). The eyeball has a Strength score of 1, a Dexterity score of 12, and
a Constitution score of 10. It has no set Hit Die, base saves, or skill ranks of its own, though it
uses its master's when a familiar normally would. It doesn't grant its master any familiar bonus,
nor can it ever be an _improved familiar._

**Replaces:** Consummate liar, mesmerist trick gained at 1st level

**Special:** Whenever the eyebiter's _eyeball familiar_ leaves his body, he is
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) until it returns (if it is destroyed, the eyebiter loses that eye until
he gains the effects of a [regeneration](ref/spell/regenerate)). An eyebiter must have at least one
eye to use this ability, and an eyebiter with only one eye is [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) instead of
dazzled until his familiar returns.

**Ocular Occlusion (Su)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** All instances of touch treatment

**Special:** If the eyebiter chooses to use an _ocular occlusion_ of a lower level than the
highest-level effect he has gained, he can instead use a standard action to target a creature within
30 feet with that effect as a ranged touch attack.

**Standard:** An eyebiter gains the ability to cloud the sight of others with a melee touch attack.
A successful Will save negates the effect (DC = 10 + 1/2 the eyebiter's mesmerist level + his
Charisma modifier); this is a mind-affecting ability.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Clouded Vision**

**Ability:** The eyebiter can cause the target to become dazzled for 1 minute and suffer a 20% miss
chance on all attacks the target makes against opponents within 60 feet. Opponents more than 60 feet
away have [total concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from the target.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Cause or Cure Blindness**

**Ability:** The eyebiter can blind the target for 1 minute or affect it as per the spell
[remove blindness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness).

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Eyeless Horror**

**Ability:** The eyebiter creates a powerful illusion that causes the target's eyes to appear as if
they have been ripped out, with blood pouring from the sockets. The target is blinded and
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round per the eyebiter's mesmerist level, taking 1d4 points of
nonlethal damage each round. The target can attempt a second Will save when attacked to negate the
shaken effect and the nonlethal damage.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Spellblight**

**Ability:** The eyebiter can afflict the target with the _ebon eyes_ or the _hemoculysis_
[minor spellblight](ref/rule/minor-spellblights), or the _eldritch cataracts_
[major spellblight](ref/rule/major-spellblights).

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Staring Eye (Su)**

**Ability:** An eyebiter can expend one use of his daily mesmerist tricks to either see through his
_eyeball familiar_ as if it were an [arcane eye](ref/spell/arcane-eye), or to use his _hypnotic
stare_ (including related abilities like _painful stare_ and _bold stare_), as well as any
[gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack he possesses (like [burning gaze](ref/spell/burning-gaze) and
[eyebite](ref/spell/eyebite)) through his _eyeball familiar,_ as if he were at its location.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Mental potency

**Omnivisual (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** An eyebiter's roving _eyeball familiar_ is constantly watching in all directions. As
long as his _eyeball familiar_ is outside his body and near enough to see the same things (such as
being in the same room), the eyebiter gains [all-around vision](ref/umr/all-around-vision) and
cannot be [flanked](ref/rule/flanking).

**Replaces:** Glib lie
