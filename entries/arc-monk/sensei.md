**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 60\
The sensei is a revered teacher who imparts lessons on the oneness of mind, body, and spirit, along
with occasional correction that is subtle and swift. Rather than using wisdom for his own benefit
alone, he uses it to better those around him.

**Gained class skills:** diplo, ling, ka, kd, ke, kg, kh, kl, kna, kno, kp, kr

**Advice (Ex)**

**Ability:** A sensei's _advice_ is identical to bardic performance (using oratory).

**At 1st Level:** He can [inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage).

**At 3rd Level:** He can [inspire competence](ref/performance/inspire-competence).

**At 9th Level:** He can [inspire greatness](ref/performance/inspire-greatness).

**Replaces:** Flurry of blows, fast movement, improved evasion

**Special:** These abilities are equal to a bard of the sensei's level, and are usable a total
number of rounds per day equal to his level + his Wisdom modifier (minimum 1).

**Insightful Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** A sensei may use his Wisdom bonus in lieu of his Strength or Dexterity on attack rolls
and combat maneuver checks with unarmed strikes or monk weapons.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Evasion, bonus feat gained at 2nd level

**Mystic Wisdom (Su)**

**At 14th Level:** A sensei may spend 2 points to grant one of the abilities listed above to all
allies within 30 feet, or _diamond body, diamond soul,_ or _improved evasion_ to a single ally
within 30 feet.

**At 10th Level:** A sensei may affect all allies within 30 feet rather than himself (spending
points from his _ki pool_ only once, not once for each target). Or, a sensei may instead spend 1
point from his _ki pool_ (as a **swift action**) while using _advice_ to provide a single ally
within 30 feet with _evasion, fast movement, high jump, purity of body,_ or _slow fall._

**At 6th Level:** A sensei may use his _advice_ ability when spending points from his _ki pool_ to
activate a class ability (using the normal actions required for each) in order to have that ability
affect one ally within 30 feet rather than the sensei himself.

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 6th, 10th, and 14th level

**Special:** These abilities function at the sensei's level and last 1 round.
