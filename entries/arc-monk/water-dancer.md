Water dancers derive their martial training from ancient nereid traditions jealously guarded by
these enigmatic fey.

**Elemental Focus (Su and Sp)**

**Ability:** A water dancer gains the _elemental focus_ ability of the
[kineticist](ref/class/kineticist) class, but must choose water as his
[element](ref/ability/elements). He gains the [basic hydrokinesis](ref/kinetic/basic-hydrokinesis)
wild talent as normal, and gains the [kinetic blast](ref/kinetic/water-blast) feature of the
kineticist class for the water element, using his monk level as his effective kineticist level. He
can't use his kinetic blast when armored or encumbered.

**Replaces:** Flurry of blows, stunning fist, quivering palm

**Nereid's Grace (Su)**

**Info:** He uses his Charisma score instead of his Wisdom score to determine the size of his _ki
pool_ and the DC and effects of monk class features.

**Passive:** When unarmored and unencumbered, a water dancer adds 1 point of Charisma bonus per monk
level to his Armor Class as a dodge bonus. If he is caught [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) or
otherwise denied his Dexterity bonus, he also loses this bonus.

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 1st level

**Unarmed Strike**

**Ability:** A water dancer gains the unarmed strike monk class feature but treats his unarmed
strike damage as that of a monk 4 levels lower (minimum 1st level).

**Alters:** Unarmed strike

**Burn (Ex)**

**Ability:** A water dancer gains the _burn_ class feature of the kineticist class.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Wild Talents**

**Ability:** A water dancer selects a new [utility wild talent](ref/ability/wild-utility-talents)
from the list of options available to the kineticist class, treating his monk level - 2 (minimum 1)
as his kineticist level for the purpose of fulfilling prerequisites as well as for level-dependent
effects of the utility talent. He can select only universal wild talents or those that match his
element (see _elemental focus_ above).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Repeat At:** He can replace one of his utility wild talents with another wild talent of the same
level or lower. He can't replace a wild talent that he used to qualify for another of his wild
talents., 6, 10, 16

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 2nd, 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th levels

At 6th level: He learns a 2nd wild talent, following the rules above.

At 10th level: He learns a 3rd wild talent, following the rules above.

At 14th level: He learns a 4th wild talent, following the rules above.

At 18th level: He learns a 5th wild talent, following the rules above.

**Water Dance (Su)**

**Ability:** A water dancer can move across any liquid surface as though it were solid ground up to
a maximum distance of 20 feet before falling through the liquid's surface as normal.

**At 10th Level:** The water dancer can even move vertically up water surfaces (such as a flowing
waterfall) as though under the effects of [spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb). If he runs out of
movement before reaching a solid surface when climbing a vertical water source, he immediately
falls.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Slow fall

At 6th level: The maximum distance 2 feet.

At 8th level: The maximum distance 3 feet.

At 10th level: The maximum distance 4 feet.

At 12th level: The maximum distance 5 feet.

At 14th level: The maximum distance 6 feet.

At 16th level: The maximum distance 7 feet.

At 18th level: The maximum distance 8 feet.

**Metakinesis (Su)**

**Ability:** A water dancer gains the ability to alter his kinetic blasts as the kineticist's
_metakinesis (empower)_ class feature.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Improved evasion

**Water Stride (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Move:** A water dancer can leap from one source of water to another as per
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) by expending 1 point of ki from his _ki pool._ He must
begin and end his movement in a space of water equal to his size. He cannot take other creatures
with him when he uses this ability.

**Replaces:** Abundant step
