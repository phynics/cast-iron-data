Possessing a natural affinity for all things earthen, terracotta monks are experts at turning the
stone around them against their enemies.

**Gained class skills:** kd, ke, dd

**Lost class skills:** kh, perf, ride

**Trap Intuition (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** Whenever a terra-cotta monk comes within 10 feet of a trap, he receives an immediate
Perception check to notice the trap. The GM should make this check in secret. A terra-cotta monk
gains a +2 bonus on Perception checks to notice traps. This bonus increases to +4 if the trap is
located in a stone wall or floor.

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Stone Grip (Su)**

**Ability:** He can move at his full movement rate while climbing with no increase to the check's
DC.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A terra-cotta monk adds a bonus equal to his class level on all Climb checks.

**Replaces:** High jump

**Swift:** By spending 1 point from his _ki pool,_ a monk gains the benefits of
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb) for 1 round.

**Trap Dodge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** A terra-cotta monk gains a bonus equal to his Wisdom modifier on all saving throws made
against effects produced by mechanical traps.

**Replaces:** Improved evasion

**Sudden Adit (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Move:** A terra-cotta monk can create passages through stone or dirt as if using the spell
[passwall](ref/spell/passwall) with a caster level equal to his monk level. Using this ability
consumes 2 points of the monk's _ki pool._

**Replaces:** Abundant step

**Petrifying Strike (Su)**

**Ability:** A terra-cotta monk can turn an opponent to stone with a single strike. He can use this
ability once per day, and must announce the attempt before making his attack roll. If the
terra-cotta monk's strike is successful, the creature must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 +
1/2 the monk's level + the monk's Wis modifier) or be [petrified](ref/rule/petrified) for 1 hour per
monk level. Incorporeal creatures and creatures immune to petrification are unaffected by this
ability.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Quivering palm

**Rainmaker (Su)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Empty body

**Standard:** Anytime a terra-cotta monk is underground, he may cause a section of the roof to come
down on his enemies per the spell [earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake), with a caster level equal to
his monk level. The terra-cotta monk may use this ability only while in a cave, cavern, or tunnel.
Using this ability consumes 3 points from his _ki pool._
