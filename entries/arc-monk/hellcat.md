Monks of the hellcat sect follow the teachings of Asmodeus or another infernal power. Their brutal
training strips away their physical weaknesses, and their vicious fighting style is inspired by the
predatory techniques of the hellcat.

**Alignment**

**Alters:** Alignment requirements

**Info:** A hellcat must be lawful evil or lawful neutral. A hellcat who becomes lawful good or
nonlawful cannot gain new levels as a monk but retains all monk abilities.

**Gained class skills:** kp, sur

**Lost class skills:** craft, kh, perf, prof

**Weapon Proficiency**

**Info:** A hellcat is proficient with the [club](ref/eq-weapon/club), crossbow (light or heavy),
[dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger), [kama](ref/eq-weapon/kama), [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace),
[nunchaku](ref/eq-weapon/nunchaku), [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), repeating crossbow
(light or heavy), [sai](ref/eq-weapon/sai), [shuriken](ref/eq-weapon/shuriken), and
[siangham](ref/eq-weapon/siangham). In addition to monk weapons and unarmed strikes, a hellcat can
use a [light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace) to perform a _flurry of blows_.

**Replaces:** Weapon proficiencies

**Bonus Feat**

**Alters:** Bonus feat

**At 1st Level:** A hellcat selects bonus feats from the following list:
[Aspect of the Beast](ref/feat/aspect-of-the-beast), [Blind-Fight](ref/feat/blind-fight),
[Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes), [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple), and [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack).

**At 10th Level:** The following feats are added to the list:
[Bleeding Critical](ref/feat/bleeding-critical),
[Greater Blind-Fight](ref/feat/greater-blind-fight),
[Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical), [Improved Stalwart](ref/feat/improved-stalwart),
and [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack).

**At 6th Level:** The following feats are added to the list:
[Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus), [Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple),
[Improved Blind-Fight](ref/feat/improved-blind-fight), [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility), and
[Stalwart](ref/feat/stalwart).

**Hellcat Fury (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** A hellcat can deal slashing damage with his unarmed strikes.

**At 4th Level:** Whenever a hellcat confirms a critical hit with his slashing unarmed strike, the
target of that attack also takes 1d4 points of [bleed](ref/umr/bleed) damage.

**Replaces:** Stunning fist, fast movement

**Torture Training (Ex)**

**Ability:** A hellcat who fails a save against an effect that causes him to become fatigued,
sickened, or staggered can attempt a second save against the effect at the end of his next turn.
Only one additional save is allowed.

**At 5th Level:** He can also attempt this second save against effects that would cause him to
become frightened or shaken.

**At 9th Level:** He can also attempt this second save against effects that would cause him to
become exhausted, nauseated, or dazed.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Evasion, purity of body, improved evasion

**Crucible of Pain (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A hellcat gains an amount of [DR](ref/rule/damage-reduction)/- equal to half his monk
level against nonlethal damage. In addition, a hellcat is inured to hot climate effects as if using
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements).

**Replaces:** Slow fall, still mind

**Hellcat Ki (Su)**

**Ability:** A hellcat gains a pool of ki points that he can use as per the monk class feature but
with the following changes. In addition to spending ki to gain an additional attack, increased
speed, or improved AC, a hellcat can spend 1 ki point as a **swift action** to enhance his sight
(gaining 60-foot [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)) or his sense of smell (gaining the
[scent](ref/umr/scent) ability) for 1 minute per level.

**Alters:** Ki pool

**At 10th Level:** The fire resistance increases to 10.

**At 11th Level:** A hellcat can spend 1 point of ki as a **swift action** to become silhouetted
with a ghostly flame, which makes him harder to see in light but easier to see in darkness for 1
round per level. He becomes invisible in bright light, gains [concealment](ref/rule/concealment)
(20% miss chance) in normal light, gains no benefit in dim light, and gives off light like a
[candle](ref/eq-misc/candle) in darkness, allowing other creatures to see him even without
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision). If he takes any action that would break
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility) while in bright light, his invisibility is reduced to
concealment until the end of his next turn.

**At 14th Level:** The fire resistance increases to 15.

**At 7th Level:** A hellcat can spend 1 point of ki as a **swift action** to gain fire resistance 5
for 1 round per level.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Wholeness of body, diamond body
