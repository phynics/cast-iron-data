The style of the tetori is that of the majestic wrestler - a warrior with a dizzying array of grabs,
holds, and locks with which to bewilder and incapacitate his foes.

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** A tetori gains the following bonus feats.

**At 1st Level:** [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple)

**At 10th Level:** feat/Pinning Knockout

**At 14th Level:** [Chokehold](ref/feat/chokehold)

**At 18th Level:** [Neckbreaker](ref/feat/neckbreaker).

**At 2nd Level:** [Stunning Pin](ref/feat/stunning-pin)

**At 6th Level:** [Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple)

**Replaces:** Bonus feats

**Graceful Grappler (Ex)**

**Ability:** A tetori uses his monk level in place of his base attack bonus to determine CMB and CMD
for grappling.

**At 15th Level:** A tetori gains the [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) special attack, inflicting his
unarmed strike damage on any successful [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) check.

**At 4th Level:** He suffers no penalties on attack rolls, can make attacks of opportunity while
grappling, and retains his Dexterity bonus to AC when pinning an opponent or when
[grappled](ref/rule/grapple-conditions).

**At 8th Level:** A tetori gains the [grab](ref/umr/grab) special attack when using unarmed strikes,
and can use this ability against creatures his own size or smaller by spending 1 point from his _ki
pool,_ or against larger creatures by spending 2 points from his _ki pool._

**Replaces:** Flurry of blows

**Counter-Grapple (Ex)**

**Ability:** A tetori wrestler may make an attack of opportunity against a creature attempting to
grapple him. This ability does not allow the tetori to make an attack of opportunity against a
creature with the Greater Grapple feat.

**At 10th Level:** He may _counter-grapple_ even if his attacker has exceptional reach.

**At 6th Level:** He may use _counter-grapple_ even if his attacker has
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or total concealment.

**At 8th Level:** He may _counter-grapple_ even if he is flat-footed.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Slow fall

**Break Free (Ex)**

**Immediate:** If a tetori fails a save against an effect that causes him to become
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled), [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed), [slowed](ref/spell/slow), or
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered), he may spend 1 point from his _ki pool_ to attempt a new save.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A tetori adds his monk level on combat maneuver or Escape Artist checks made to escape
a grapple.

**Replaces:** High jump

**Inescapable Grasp (Su)**

**At 13th Level:** This ability also duplicates the effect of
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor).

**At 17th Level:** The tetori's unarmed strike gains the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) special ability, and an
[incorporeal](ref/subtype/incorporeal) creature that he strikes gains the grappled condition (Reflex
negates, DC 10 + 1/2 the wrestler's level + his Wisdom modifier).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Abundant step, improved evasion, timeless body, tongue of sun and moon

**Swift:** A tetori can spend 1 point from his _ki pool_ to suppress his opponents'
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) and magical bonuses to Escape Artist or on
checks to escape a grapple. This lasts until the beginning of the wrestler's next turn.

**Form Lock (Su)**

**Ability:** A tetori can negate a polymorph effect by touch with a Wisdom check, adding a bonus
equal to his monk level, against a DC of 11 + the caster level of the effect (or HD of the creature,
for supernatural polymorph effects). This is a **standard action** requiring 2 points from the
tetori's _ki pool,_ or an **immediate action** if a creature the tetori is grappling attempts to use
a polymorph effect.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Diamond soul

**Iron Body (Su)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Move:** A tetori can make his tissues ultra-dense for 1 minute as the
[iron body](ref/spell/iron-body) spell by spending 3 points from his _ki pool._

**Replaces:** Empty body
