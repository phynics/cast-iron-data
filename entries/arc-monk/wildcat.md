A wildcat is a student of the school of hard knocks, who dedicates himself to learning how to take
down foes by any means necessary. A wildcat isn't afraid to smash a tankard over a foe's head, stomp
an opponent's foot, gouge an eye, or generally create mayhem to gain any possible advantage.

**Ready for Anything (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A wildcat gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Perception checks to act in a
surprise round.

**Replaces:** Still mind

**Brawler Maneuver Training (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A wildcat gains additional training with the dirty tricks.

**At 4th Level:** He gains a +1 bonus on combat maneuver checks when attempting a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver and a +1 bonus to his CMD when defending against
this maneuver.

**Repeat At:** A wildcat becomes further trained in another combat maneuver, gaining the above +1
bonus on combat maneuver checks and to CMD., 7, 10, 16

**Replaces:** Ki pool, high jump

**Special:** When he becomes trained in a new maneuver, the bonuses granted by previous _maneuver
training_ increase by 1 each. For example, when a wildcat reaches 7th level, he gains a +1 bonus on
one type of combat maneuver, +1 to her CMD against that combat maneuver, and the bonuses for the
dirty trick combat maneuver increase to +2.

**Improvised Weapon Mastery (Ex)**

**Ability:** A wildcat's damage with improvised weapons increases. When wielding an
[improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons), he uses the unarmed strike damage of a monk four
levels lower instead of the base damage for that weapon (minimum monk level 1). For example, a
6th-level Medium wildcat wielding a broken bottle deals 1d6 points of damage instead of the weapon's
normal 1d4. If the weapon normally deals more damage than this, its damage is unchanged. This
increase in damage does not affect any other aspect of the weapon.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** All instances of slow fall

**Special:** The wildcat can decide to use the weapon's base damage instead of his adjusted unarmed
strike damage. This must be declared before the attack roll is attempted.

**Bonus Feat**

**At 10th Level:** He adds the following feats, too:
[Quick Dirty Trick](ref/feat/quick-dirty-trick), [Quick Reposition](ref/feat/quick-reposition),
[Quick Steal](ref/feat/quick-steal).

**At 6th Level:** A wildcat adds the following feats to his list of bonus feats:
[Improved Dirty Trick](ref/feat/improved-dirty-trick),
[Improved Reposition](ref/feat/improved-reposition), [Improved Steal](ref/feat/improved-steal).

**Knockout (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day a wildcat can unleash a devastating attack that can instantly knock a
target unconscious. He must announce this intent before making his attack roll. If the wildcat's
strike is successful and the target takes damage from the blow, the target must succeed at a
Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the wildcat's level + the higher of the wildcat's Strength or
Dexterity modifier) or fall [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious) for 1d6 rounds. Each round on its
turn, the unconscious target can attempt a new saving throw to end the effect; this is a
**full-round action** that does **not** provoke attacks of opportunity.

**At 12th Level:** A wildcat can use this ability twice per day.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Improved evasion, abundant step

**Special:** Creatures immune to critical hits or nonlethal damage are immune to this ability.

**Turn the Tables (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** Opponents provoke an attack of opportunity from the wildcat whenever they fail at a
combat maneuver against the wildcat.

**Replaces:** Diamond soul

**Dirty Blow (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a wildcat succeeds at a dirty trick combat maneuver, he can deal his unarmed
strike damage to that opponent.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Empty body
