Some monks wander the world in humility to learn and to share wisdom and philosophy from their
teachers with those they meet, often aiding those who are in need. A wanderer has the following
class features.

**Prerequisites:** Human

**Gained class skills:** diplo, kg, kl, ling, sur

**Far Traveler (Ex)**

**Ability:** The wanderer gains either one additional language known or proficiency in one exotic or
martial weapon.

**At 4th Level:** The wanderer may gain an additional language known or may retrain her weapon
proficiency from this ability to a different exotic or martial weapon.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Repeat:** She may gain a language or retrain again., 4, 4

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 1st level

**Long Walk (Ex)**

**Ability:** The wanderer gains [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) as a bonus feat, and the feat bonus
doubles when he makes Constitution checks because of a forced march. In addition, a wanderer gains a
+2 bonus on saving throws against spells and effects that cause exhaustion and fatigue.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Still mind

**Light Step (Su)**

**Ability:** By spending 1 point from his _ki pool,_ he can use [ant haul](ref/spell/ant-haul),
[feather step](ref/spell/feather-step), [pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace), or
[tireless pursuit](ref/spell/tireless-pursuit) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (with a caster level equal to his monk level).

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A wanderer leaves no trail and cannot be tracked, though he can leave a trail if
desired.

**Replaces:** Slow fall

**Inscrutable (Su)**

**Ability:** By spending 1 point from his _ki pool,_ the wanderer gains
[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection) for 24 hours with a caster level equal to his monk level.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** The wanderer gains a supernatural air of mystery. The DC to gain information or insight
into the wanderer with Diplomacy, Knowledge skills, or Sense Motive increases by 5.

**Replaces:** High jump

**Wanderer's Wisdom (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The wanderer can dispense excellent advice in the form of philosophical proverbs and
parables.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Wholeness of body

**Swift:** The wanderer can [inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage) or
[inspire competence](ref/performance/inspire-competence) as a [bard](ref/class/bard) of his monk
level by spending 2 points from his _ki pool._ This affects one creature within 30 feet and lasts a
number of rounds equal to the wanderer's Wisdom modifier (minimum 1 round). This ability is
language-dependent.

**Disappear Unnoticed (Ex)**

**Ability:** The wanderer may use Stealth to hide even while being directly observed or when no
cover or concealment is available, as long as he is adjacent to at least one creature of his size or
larger, by spending 1 point from his _ki pool._ This effect lasts until the beginning of the
wanderer's next turn and may be continued in consecutive rounds by spending 1 ki point each round.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** Abundant step

**Free Step (Su)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** The wanderer gains continuous [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) as a
continuous [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Replaces:** Diamond soul
