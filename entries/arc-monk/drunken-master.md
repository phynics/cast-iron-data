Most monks lead lives of moderation and quiet contemplation. But the drunken master finds perfection
through excess. Powered by strong wine, he uses his intoxication to reach a state where his ki is
more potent, if somewhat fleeting. A drunken master has the following class features.

**Drunken Ki (Su)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Replaces:** Still mind

**Special:** The monk can gain this temporary ki even before he gains a _ki pool_ at 4th level.
These _drunken ki points_ last for 1 hour or until spent, whichever is shorter.

**Standard:** A drunken master can drink a tankard of ale or strong alcohol and gain one temporary
ki point. This does not provoke attacks of opportunity. The monk can have a maximum of 1 _drunken ki
point._

**Swift:** As long as he has at least 1 _drunken ki point,_ the monk can spend 1 ki point to move 5
feet without provoking attacks of opportunity.

At 5th level: The monk's maximum 2 _drunken ki points._

At 7th level: The monk's maximum 3 _drunken ki points._

At 9th level: The monk's maximum 4 _drunken ki points._

At 11th level: The monk's maximum 5 _drunken ki points._

At 13th level: The monk's maximum 6 _drunken ki points._

At 15th level: The monk's maximum 7 _drunken ki points._

At 17th level: The monk's maximum 8 _drunken ki points._

At 19th level: The monk's maximum 9 _drunken ki points._

**Drunken Strength (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Purity of body

**Special:** The monk must have at least 1 _drunken ki point_ to use this ability.

**Swift:** A drunken master can spend 1 point of ki to inflict 1d6 extra points of damage on a
single successful melee attack. The monk can choose to apply the damage after the attack roll is
made.

**Drunken Courage (Su)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** A drunken master is immune to fear as long as he has at least 1 point of _drunken ki._

**Replaces:** Diamond body

**Drunken Resilience (Ex)**

**At 16th Level:** The DR increases to 2/-.

**At 19th Level:** It increases to 3/-.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** A drunken master gains [DR](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 1/- as long as he has at least 1
point of _drunken ki._

**Replaces:** Diamond soul

**Firewater Breath (Su)**

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Replaces:** Empty body

**Special:** The monk must have at least 1 _drunken ki point_ to use this ability.

**Standard:** A drunken master can take a drink and expel a gout of alcohol-fueled fire in a 30-foot
cone. Creatures within the cone take 20d6 points of fire damage. A successful Reflex saving throw
(DC 10 + 1/2 the monk's level + the monk's Wis modifier) halves the damage. Using this ability
consumes 4 ki points from the monk's _ki pool._
