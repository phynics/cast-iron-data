Through discipline and training, an ironskin monk hardens his body to withstand punishing blows.
Though slow on his feet, his calloused hands and feet can shatter stone and stagger foes.

**Prerequisites:** Hobgoblin

**Iron Skin (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** An ironskin monk gains a bonus to his natural armor equal to 1 +
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his monk level. This bonus stacks with any existing
natural armor the ironskin monk already has.

**Replaces:** AC bonus, the ability to add his Wisdom bonus to his AC

**Bonus Feat**

**At 1st Level:** An ironskin monk adds [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack) to his list of bonus
feats.

**At 10th Level:** He adds [Greater Sunder](ref/feat/greater-sunder) to the list.

**At 6th Level:** He adds [Improved Sunder](ref/feat/improved-sunder) to the list.

**Replaces:** Dodge, Mobility, and Spring Attack on the monk's bonus feat list

**Resilience (Ex)**

**Ability:** If he makes a Fortitude saving throw against an attack that has a reduced effect on a
successful save, he instead avoids the effect entirely.

**Flavor:** An ironskin monk can shake off the physical effects of certain attacks.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Special:** This ability can be used only if the monk is wearing light armor or no armor. A
helpless monk does not gain the benefits of _resilience._

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** An ironskin monk can spend 1 point from his _ki pool_ to gain a damage bonus equal to
1/2 his level against objects and constructs for 1 round.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** The ability to increase speed with ki

**Staggering Blow (Ex)**

**Free:** An ironskin monk attacking with an unarmed strike can spend 1 point from his _ki pool_
after a successful critical hit to [stagger](ref/misc/staggered) the creature struck for 1 round
(Fort DC 10 + 1/2 the ironskin monk's level + the ironskin monk's Wisdom modifier negates).

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** High jump

**Tough as Nails (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** An ironskin monk gains [DR](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 1/-.

**Replaces:** Fast movement, slow fall

At 9th level: The DR 2/-.

At 12th level: The DR 3/-.

At 15th level: The DR 4/-.

At 18th level: The DR 5/-.

**Evasion (Ex)**

**Ability:** An ironskin monk gains [evasion](ref/ability/evasion).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Improved evasion

**Surefooted (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** An ironskin monk's speed is not reduced by
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles).

**Replaces:** Tongue of the sun and moon

**Unbreakable (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** An ironskin monk sets aside many of the frailties of mortal flesh. He becomes immune to
death effects and stunning. He is not subject to
[ability damage or ability drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), and has a 75% chance of ignoring
the extra damage dealt by critical hits and sneak attacks.

**Replaces:** Perfect self
