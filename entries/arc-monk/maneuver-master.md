**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 58\
The maneuver master specializes in more complicated moves than simple damage-dealing strikes.

**Bonus Feat**

**At 1st Level:** In addition to normal monk bonus feats, a maneuver master may select any Improved
combat maneuver feat (such as [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun)) as a bonus feat.

**At 10th Level:** He may select any maneuver Strike feat (such as
[Tripping Strike](ref/feat/tripping-strike)) as a bonus feat.

**At 6th Level:** He may select any Greater combat maneuver feat (such as
[Greater Grapple](ref/feat/greater-grapple)) as a bonus feat.

**Flurry of Maneuvers (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** As part of a full-attack action, a maneuver master can make one additional combat
maneuver, regardless of whether the maneuver normally replaces a melee attack or requires a standard
action. The maneuver master uses his monk level in place of his base attack bonus to determine his
CMB for the bonus maneuvers, though all combat maneuver checks suffer a -2 penalty when using a
flurry.

**At 15th Level:** A maneuver master may attempt a third additional combat maneuver, with an
additional -7 penalty on combat maneuver checks.

**At 8th Level:** A maneuver master may attempt a second additional combat maneuver, with an
additional -3 penalty on combat maneuver checks.

**Replaces:** Flurry of blows

**Special:** A maneuver master loses this ability when wearing armor, using a shield, or carrying a
medium or heavy load.

**Maneuver Defense (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** If a maneuver master has an Improved combat maneuver feat, any creature attempting that
maneuver against the maneuver master provokes an attack of opportunity, even if it would not
normally do so.

**Replaces:** Still mind

**Reliable Maneuver (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Slow fall

**Swift:** A maneuver master may spend 1 point from his _ki pool_ before attempting a combat
maneuver. He can roll his combat maneuver check for that maneuver twice and use the better result.

**Meditative Maneuver (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Purity of body

**Swift:** A maneuver master can add his Wisdom modifier on any combat maneuver check he makes
before the beginning of his next turn. He must choose which combat maneuver check to grant the bonus
to before making the combat maneuver check.

**Sweeping Maneuver (Ex)**

**Ability:** A maneuver master can make two combat maneuvers as a **standard action**, as long as
neither maneuver requires the maneuver master to move. He may perform two identical maneuvers
against two adjacent enemies, or he may perform two different combat maneuvers against the same
target.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Diamond body

**Whirlwind Maneuver (Ex)**

**Fullround:** Once per day, a maneuver master can attempt a single combat maneuver against every
opponent he threatens, as long as the combat maneuver does not require movement. He makes a single
combat maneuver check, and it applies to all targets.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Quivering palm
