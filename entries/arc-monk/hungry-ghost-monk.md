**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 110\
The hungry ghost monk looks to spirits that prey upon the living as models of perfection. He sees
the life energy of the universe as a resource to be manipulated, even stealing it from other
creatures. It is through this constant influx of energy that the hungry ghost monk reaches his
ultimate goal: power - personal, pure, and simple. A hungry ghost monk has the following class
features.

**Punishing Kick (Ex)**

**Ability:** A hungry ghost monk gains [Punishing Kick](ref/feat/punishing-kick) as a bonus feat,
even if he does not meet the prerequisites.

**At 10th Level:** The monk can push the target of his Punishing Kick an additional 5 feet (to 10
feet).

**At 15th Level:** He can push the target 15 feet away. Or, he can instead choose to push the target
5 feet and knock the target [prone](ref/rule/prone) with the same attack. The target still gets a
saving throw to avoid being knocked prone.

**At 20th Level:** He can push the target 20 feet away.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Stunning Fist

**Steal Ki (Ex)**

**Ability:** If the monk scores a confirmed critical hit against a living enemy or reduces a living
enemy to 0 or fewer hit points, he can steal some of that creature's ki. This ability replenishes 1
spent ki point to the monk's _ki pool,_ as long as the monk has at least 1 ki point in his pool. He
cannot exceed his \*ki pool's maximum.

**Flavor:** A hungry ghost monk can steal ki from other creatures, though this ability is
controversial in some circles of monks, who see it as nothing less than a form of vampirism.

**At 11th Level:** Each time the monk successfully steals ki, he can make an immediate saving throw
against one disease he is suffering from. There is no penalty for failing this saving throw. The
monk gains a bonus equal to his Wisdom modifier on the saving throw.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Purity of body

**Life Funnel (Su)**

**Ability:** If the monk has at least 1 ki point in his _ki pool_ and scores a confirmed critical
hit against a living enemy or reduces a living enemy to 0 or fewer hit points, he heals a number of
hit points equal to his monk level. As with _steal ki,_ some monks believe that _life funnel_ is an
unsavory act, no better than what the undead do to the living.

**Flavor:** A hungry ghost monk can steal a creature's life force to replenish his own.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Wholeness of body

**Special:** A monk with this ability cannot steal both ki and hit points at the same time.

**Life from a Stone (Su)**

**Ability:** If the monk has at least 1 ki point in his pool, he gains the benefit of _life funnel_
and _steal ki_ when he confirms a critical hit against any creature or reduces any creature to 0 or
fewer hit points.

**Flavor:** A hungry ghost monk can steal ki or life force from any creature, not just living
creatures.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Diamond body

**Sipping Demon (Su)**

**Ability:** A hungry ghost monk gains 1 [temporary hit point](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) each
time he hits an enemy with a melee attack. The monk gains a number of temporary hit points equal to
his Wisdom modifier when he scores a critical hit. The maximum number of temporary hit points the
monk can have is equal to his monk level. The temporary hit points disappear 1 hour later.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Diamond soul

**Special:** The monk can only use this ability when he has at least 1 ki point in his _ki pool._
This ability is a proscribed manipulation of ki considered by many good monks to be a corruption.
