**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 67\
Sage counselors are ascetics and mystics who leave the confines of the monastery walls to advise
secular people about spiritual truths and to seek knowledge of the outside world. They often find
work as mentors teaching religion and martial arts, and some of them even become counselors for
people in high places. Sage counselors often speak in metaphors, knowing that indirect persuasion is
more effective than speaking plainly, and they use indirect tactics in combat as well.

**Gained class skills:** bluff, diplo, kno

**Lost class skills:** climb, ea, per

**Cunning Fist (Ex)**

**Ability:** A sage counselor gains [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise) as a bonus feat,
and he can ignore the Intelligence prerequisite on feats that have Combat Expertise as a
prerequisite.

**At 2nd Level:** He gains [Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint).

**At 6th Level:** He gains [Greater Feint](ref/feat/greater-feint).

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 1st, 2nd, and 6th levels

**Special:** He doesn't need to meet the prerequisites of these bonus feats.

**Feinting Flurry**

**Alters:** Flurry of blows, ki pool

**At 10th Level:** He can also choose to replace his first attack during a _flurry of blows_ with a
feint check.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** The ability to spend ki to make an additional attack with a _flurry of blows_, bonus
feat gained at 10th level

**Swift:** A sage counselor can spend 1 ki point while performing a _flurry of blows_ to
[feint](ref/skill/bluff) an opponent, but he can't spend 1 ki point to make an additional attack
when making a _flurry of blows._

**Deceptive Ki (Su)**

**Alters:** Ki pool

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** The ability to spend ki to increase his speed by 20 feet for 1 round

**Swift:** The sage counselor can spend 1 point from his _ki pool_ to give himself a +4 insight
bonus on his next Bluff check.
