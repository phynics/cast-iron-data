**Sources:** Healer's Handbook pg 4\
Disciples of wholeness train to bring themselves and others closer to physical and spiritual
perfection.

**Healing Ki (Su)**

**Alters:** Ki pool

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** The ability to spend ki to gain an extra attack during a _flurry of blows_

**Swift:** A disciple of wholeness can touch a creature and spend 1 point from her _ki pool_ to
restore a number of hit points equal to 1d4 + 1/2 her monk level to that creature. If she has the
_wholeness of body_ class feature, she can spend 2 points to restore a number of hit points equal to
1d8 + her monk level to a touched creature.

**Hone Body (Su)**

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** A disciple of wholeness has immunity to supernatural and magical diseases as long as
she is undamaged (she loses this immunity when she has hit point or ability score damage).

**Replaces:** Purity of body

**Swift:** She can touch a creature and spend 1 point from her _ki pool_ to render it immune to all
diseases for 1 day. She cannot grant this immunity if the target is already afflicted with a
disease.

**Greater Hone Body (Su)**

**Ability:** A disciple of wholeness is immune to poison while undamaged and can grant immunity to
poison (lasting for 1 day). This ability otherwise functions as per _hone body._

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Diamond body

**Hone Soul (Su)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Diamond soul

**Standard:** A disciple of wholeness can dispel a spell affecting a living creature by touching it
and spending 1 point from her _ki pool._ This power functions like the targeted dispel option of
[greater dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic-greater), with the caster level equal to the disciple
of wholeness's monk level.
