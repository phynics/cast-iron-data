**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 187\
The gray disciple contemplates the inner voice of duergar magic and the silent eternity of stone,
mastering these dual mysteries and combining them to deadly effect.

**Prerequisites:** Duergar

**Fade from Sight (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Slow fall, still mind

**Special:** To use this ability, the gray disciple must already have _invisibility_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Swift:** The gray disciple can become invisible (as the [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility)
spell) for 1 round by spending 1 ki point.

**Gray Heart (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** High jump, bonus feat gained at 6th level

**Swift:** The gray disciple can enlarge himself (as the [enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)
spell) for 1 minute by spending 1 ki point. To use this ability, the gray disciple must already have
_[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_ available as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Born in Darkness (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Wholeness of body

**Standard:** The gray disciple can radiate [darkness](ref/spell/darkness) (as the spell, except
originating from the disciple's person) for 1 round per level by spending 1 ki point.

**Earth Glide (Su)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** Abundant step

**Swift:** The gray disciple can spend a ki point to walk through solid stone for 1 round. This
functions as the [earth glide](ref/umr/earth-glide) universal monster ability. The gray disciple may
continue _earth gliding_ as long as he spends 1 ki point every round as a **swift action**. If he
ceases _earth gliding_ within a solid object, he is violently ejected and takes 5d6 points of
damage. The gray disciple is not harmed by damage caused to material he is _earth gliding_ through,
but a [stone to flesh](ref/spell/stone-to-flesh) spell cast upon it causes violent ejection as
described above.

**Entomb (Su)**

**Ability:** A gray disciple can phase a foe into solid rock, killing it instantly. To use this
ability, he expends 1 ki point as part of a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuver against a creature adjacent to unworked earth or
stone. If the attempt succeeds, the gray disciple pushes his foe inside the rock using his
_[earth glide](ref/spell/earth-glide)_ ability. If the creature succeeds at a Reflex save (DC 10 +
1/2 the gray disciple's monk level + the gray disciple's Wisdom modifier), it is ejected in the
nearest open space and takes 5d6 points of damage. If it fails, it dies instantly as its body merges
with the surrounding stone.

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Replaces:** Quivering palm

**Special:** _[Entomb](ref/spell/entomb)_ is usable once per day, but a failed bull rush or
reposition attempt does not count as a use of the ability. _[Entomb](ref/spell/entomb)_ has no
effect on creatures that can _[earth glide](ref/spell/earth-glide),_ are
[incorporeal](ref/umr/incorporeal), or can otherwise can survive merging with a solid object.

**Earthen Thrall (Sp)**

**Ability:** A gray disciple can attempt to control a creature with the earth subtype once per day.
This ability is treated as [dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster) (DC 10 + 1/2 the gray
disciple's monk level + the gray disciple's Wisdom modifier), but is only effective against
creatures with the earth subtype, and the gray disciple can keep only a single creature enthralled.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Tongue of the sun and moon, empty body

**Special:** If he attempts to control a second creature with this ability, the first creature is
automatically released from domination whether or not the second attempt succeeds.
