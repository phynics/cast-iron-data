A serpent-fire adept embraces the secrets of her chakras and mastery of their energetic flow.

**Gained class skills:** app, diplo, fly, heal, ka, ling, sur

**Lost class skills:** climb, intm, kh, perf, ride, swim

**Chakra Training**

**Ability:** A serpent-fire adept gains [Chakra Initiate](ref/feat/chakra-initiate) and
[Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) as bonus feats. She need not meet these feats'
prerequisites, and can use her serpent-fire ki from Chakra Initiate to open and maintain her root
chakra, despite not being 2nd level.

**Replaces:** Stunning fist, bonus feat gained at 1st level

**Chakra Expertise (Ex)**

**At 16th Level:** This increases to 2 rounds.

**At 2nd Level:** The serpent-fire adept gains a bonus equal to 1/2 her monk level on Fortitude and
Will saves attempted to maintain awakened chakras.

**At 8th Level:** When she has opened one or more chakras, she can maintain those chakras (without
opening any new chakras) for 1 round without taking a swift action, expending ki, or attempting
Fortitude or Will saves to maintain the chakras.

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 2nd level

**Special:** After maintaining her chakras with _chakra expertise,_ the monk must open a new chakra
or maintain her open chakras normally before she can use _chakra expertise_ again.

**Light Spirit (Su)**

**Ability:** When the serpent-fire adept has her sacral chakra open and chooses to benefit from its
power, her fly speed is equal to either her base land speed or her bonus to her base speed from the
monk _fast movement_ class ability (whichever is higher). She maintains this fly speed for a number
of rounds equal to her Wisdom bonus (minimum 1 round), even if she closes her chakras.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Slow fall, high jump

**Chakra Adept (Ex)**

**Ability:** The serpent-fire adept gains [Chakra Adept](ref/feat/chakra-adept) as a bonus feat. She
need not meet the feat's prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Bonus feat gained at 6th level

**Special:** When the serpent-fire adept has used all her rounds of _chakra expertise_ to maintain
open chakras, if she does not spend additional ki to maintain her open chakras on the next round,
her highest chakra closes and she can spend a **swift action** to use any one of the abilities of
her remaining open chakras. On the round after that, all her chakras close, and if she wishes to
reopen them, she must start again with her root chakra.

**Linked Chakras (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Wholeness of body

**Standard:** Once per day, the serpent-fire adept can open multiple chakras at once. The round that
she does this, she is required to make saving throws only to resist being overwhelmed by the highest
chakra she opens, though the save DCs are increased by 5.

At 12th level: She can now do this 2 times a day.

At 17th level: She can now do this 3 times a day.

**Chakra Mastery (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** The serpent-fire adept gains [Chakra Master](ref/feat/chakra-master) as a bonus
feat. She need not meet the feat's prerequisites. When maintaining her open chakras with _chakra
expertise,_ she can spend 1 point of ki to gain the benefits of two chakras rather than one in that
round (or three chakras, if she has opened her crown chakra).

**At 14th Level:** The serpent-fire adept's pool of serpent-fire ki increases to 7 points.

**At 18th Level:** It increases to 9 points.

**Replaces:** Bonus feats gained at 10th, 14th, and 18th level
