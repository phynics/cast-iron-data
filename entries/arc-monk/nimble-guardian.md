**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 92\
Some catfolk monks dedicate their graceful prowess to the defense of others, especially those
dedicated to a similar ethos or who prove themselves as stalwart allies of the monk's cause. A
nimble guardian has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Catfolk

**Defensive Aid (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A nimble guardian can interpose herself between one adjacent ally and an attack or
damage dealt in an area of effect.

**Immediate:** If an adjacent ally is the target of the attack or is required to make a Reflex
saving throw against a damaging effect, the nimble guardian can grant that ally a +4 circumstance
bonus to AC or on the saving throw against the effect.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Special:** The nimble guardian must use this ability before the attack roll or saving throw is
made. The nimble guardian can only use this ability if he is wearing light or no armor.

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day

**Nimble Reflexes (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A nimble guardian gains a +2 bonus on all Reflex saving throws.

**Replaces:** Still mind

**Defensive Mastery (Ex)**

**Ability:** A nimble guardian gains 3 additional uses of her _defensive aid_ ability per day.
Furthermore, if an ally that gained the benefit of a use of defensive aid succeeds her Reflex saving
throw, and the effect still deals damage on a successful saving throw, the nimble guardian can spend
1 ki point to negate that damage. Doing so is not an action.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Purity of body

**Guardian Feline (Su)**

**At 9th Level:** This ability functions as [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3).

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** No

**Replaces:** Wholeness of body

**Standard:** A nimble guardian can transform himself into a feline creature by spending 2 ki
points. The effect lasts for 1 hour or until the nimble guardian changes back. The chosen form must
be some form of feline ([cheetah](ref/monster/cat-cheetah), [lion](ref/monster/lion), etc.). This
ability is otherwise identical to [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2).

**Evasion (Ex)**

**Ability:** A nimble guardian gains [evasion](ref/ability/evasion).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Improved evasion
