**Sources:** Inner Sea Magic pg 35\
Most summoners have a close connection to creatures from the farthest reaches of the planes. A few
have a primal connection to nature, and their power is tied to the First World. It is not unusual
for these summoners to associate with druids, for like druids, first worlders have a powerful tie to
the natural world. Some first worlders are driven half-mad by the strange energies and intelligences
that seep into their minds and bodies, though, and these poor souls are prone to lash out at
loggers, druids, or innocent passers-by without considering whether or not they are threats. A first
worlder's [eidolon](ref/sidekick/eidolon) usually resembles a fey creature or a plant monster,
though some are fantastic otherworldly animals with exaggerated features. A first worlder has the
following class features.

**Summon Nature's Ally (Sp)**

**Ability:** At levels where a summoner would gain a more powerful
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), he instead gains the equivalent
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell (at 19th level, he can use
[summon nature's ally IX](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-9) or [gate](ref/spell/gate)).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Summon monster

**Special:** When a first worlder gains a _[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_
spell as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), he adds it to his class spell list
(he must still select it as a spell known if he wants to cast it as an actual spell).

**Usage:** A first worlder can cast [summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally) a number
of times per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier.

of times per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier.

A first worlder adds the following creatures to the lists of what he can summon with his
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell-like ability:

_**Summon nature's ally II**_

**Details:** Gremlin ([jinkin](ref/monster/gremlin-jinkin),
[pugwampi](ref/monster/gremlin-pugwampi), or [vexgit](ref/monster/gremlin-vexgit)).

_**Summon nature's ally III**_

**Details:** Gremlin ([nuglub](ref/monster/gremlin-nuglub)).

_**Summon nature's ally IV**_

**Details:** [Unicorn](ref/monster/unicorn).

_**Summon nature's ally V**_

**Details:** [Pixie](ref/monster/pixie), [satyr](ref/monster/satyr).

_**Summon nature's ally VII**_

**Details:** [Nymph](ref/monster/nymph).

;;;ab\{title=Eidolon action=Info icon=power replace=Eidolon\}

Instead of an outsider, a first worlder's eidolon has the [fey](ref/type/fey) creature type and the
[extraplanar](ref/subtype/extraplanar) subtype. Its statistics are changed from a standard eidolon
as follows:

**Hit Dice**

**Details:** d6 Hit Die (instead of d10).

**BAB**

**Details:** Equal to 1/2 the eidolon's Hit Dice.

**Good/Bad Saves**

**Details:** The eidolon's good saves area always Reflex and Will.

**Skills**

**Details:** The eidolon's class skills are [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics),
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Climb](ref/skill/climb), [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any),
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise),
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist), [Fly](ref/skill/fly),
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography),
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature), [Perception](ref/skill/perception),
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any), [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive),
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand), [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth), [Swim](ref/skill/swim),
and [Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device).

**Senses**

**Details:** The eidolon gains [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) instead of
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision). The summoner can grant the eidolon darkvision 60 ft. as a 1-point
evolution.

**Damage Reduction**

**Details:** If the first worlder chooses [this evolution](ref/evolution/damage-reduction), he may
select DR/cold iron instead of one of the alignment-based types of
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
