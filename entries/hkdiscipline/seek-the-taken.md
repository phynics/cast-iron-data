**Seek the Taken**

**Ability:** When a member of the _Order of the Torrent_ becomes a 3rd-level Hellknight, he gains
his first discipline: _seek the taken._ This enables the Hellknight to use an
[enlarged](ref/feat/enlarge-spell) [locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to his total character
level.

**Special:** When an _Order of the Torrent_ Hellknight gains his third discipline, he cannot choose
the [summon devil](ref/hkdiscipline/summon-devil) discipline.

**Order:** [Order of the Torrent](ref/hkorder/order-of-the-torrent-minor-order)
