**&L&Summon Servant of Law (Sp)&FN&**

**Ability:** The Hellknight can summon a creature or creatures to help her, as if using a
[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell.

**At 3rd Level:** She can summon 1d3 [hell hounds](ref/monster/hell-hound).

**At 6th Level:** She can summon 1d3 [advanced](ref/template/advanced) hell hounds or one
[axiomite](ref/monster/axiomite).

**At 9th Level:** She can summon 1d4+1 hell hounds, 1d3 axiomites, or one
[zelekhut inevitable](ref/monster/inevitable-zelekhut).

**Order:** Any order
