**Greater Numerical Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, when the investigator has confirmed a critical hit with a _studied
strike,_ he can deal the strike's maximum damage instead of rolling damage as normal. This includes
the damage dealt by the successful attack as well as the additional damage from the _studied
strike._

**Prereq:** [Numerical strike](ref/invtalent/numerical-strike), 13th-level investigator

**Special:** He can expend a use of _inspiration_ to use this ability a second time per day. Using
this ability doesn't require an action.
