**Didactic Strike**

**Ability:** When the investigator activates his _studied strike,_ he can end his _studied combat_
and choose not to deal his _studied strike_ damage. He is instead able to show any allies within 30
feet who can see him a weak spot on the target.

Until the beginning of the investigator's next turn, each affected ally's first attack that hits the
target deals additional damage equal to one-third the investigator's level.

**Special:** He can end this effect as an **immediate action** to instead cause an affected ally's
first successful attack to deal the investigator's _studied strike_ damage. This additional damage
is precision damage and does not affect creatures that are immune to sneak attacks.
