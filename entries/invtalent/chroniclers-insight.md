**Chronicler's Insight (Ex)**

**Ability:** Upon studying a Pathfinder Chronicle, the investigator can expend one use of
_inspiration_ to apply the volume's benefits for a number of hours equal to his Intelligence
modifier and gain an additional benefit based on the volume's associated Knowledge skill during that
time. The investigator can have only one such ongoing benefit at a time, and activating a new
benefit ends the first one.

**Standard:** The investigator can peruse a
[Pathfinder Chronicle](ref/eq-misc/pathfinder-chronicle), applying its benefits as though he had
studied it for the full 1d4 rounds.

**Arcana, Nature, Planes, or Religion**

**Ability:** When performing a _studied strike,_ the investigator's attack overcomes
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) as if it were a weapon with the following property
based on the associated Knowledge check: adamantine (arcana), chaotic (planes), cold iron (nature),
evil (planes), good (planes), lawful (planes), silver (religion).

He also gains a +1 insight bonus on saving throws against creatures identifiable by the associated
Knowledge check (for example, outsiders for Knowledge \[planes\]).

**Dungeoneering**

**Ability:** The investigator can deal half his _studied strike_ damage to creatures immune to sneak
attacks.

**Engineering**

**Ability:** Whenever he uses his _inspiration_ on [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks
to open locks or on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to find hidden compartments, the
investigator gains a circumstance bonus on the check equal to
[one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his level.

**Geography**

**Ability:** The investigator increases any bonuses to AC and on Reflex saving throws he gains from
partial [cover](ref/rule/cover), total cover, and improved cover by 1.

**History**

**Fullround:** The investigator can study a foe using _studied combat_ a second time in 24 hours
without expending _inspiration._ Subsequent uses of _studied combat_ require _inspiration_ as
normal.

**Local**

**Ability:** Whenever he uses his _inspiration_ on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to feint,
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to gather information, or [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)
checks to improve a creature's attitude, the investigator gains a circumstance bonus on the check
equal to one-third his level.

**Nobility**

**Ability:** Whenever he uses his _inspiration_ on Bluff checks to lie, Diplomacy checks to request
favors, or Intimidate checks to demoralize, the investigator gains a circumstance bonus on the check
equal to one-third his level.
