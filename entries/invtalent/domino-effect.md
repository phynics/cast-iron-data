**Domino Effect (Ex)**

**Flavor:** When the investigator uses _studied strike,_ he uses his opponents against each other
and sets himself up for his next move.

**Free:** Whenever he successfully deals damage to an opponent with _studied strike,_ the
investigator can apply the effects of _studied combat_ to an opponent adjacent to the first.

**Prereq:** 5th-level investigator
