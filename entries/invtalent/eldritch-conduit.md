**Eldritch Conduit (Su)**

**Flavor:** Survival in the Mana Wastes between the nations of Nex and Geb can depend heavily on
locating sufficient magical equipment, which sometimes involves stealing power from a wand or scroll
to power a more necessary one.

**Fullround:** An investigator with this talent can use two potions, two wands, or two scrolls. Each
item is expended or loses a charge as if used normally. The investigator chooses to gain the magical
effect of one, but calculates its effects using the caster level of the other.

**Special:** The investigator must still succeed at a Use Magic Device check to operate scrolls or
wands unless some other ability allows him to do so, but makes only one skill check to operate both
items.
