**Greater Anathema (Ex)**

**Ability:** The ability reduction of the investigator's _anathemas_ increases to 10, and he adds
DR/- and regeneration to the list of abilities he can lower with an _anathema._

In addition, when an investigator creates an _anathema,_ he can designate a specific kind of
creature (such as [vampires](ref/template/vampire)) in addition to the creature type. When used
against this specific foe, the DC of the save against the _anathema_ is 2 higher than the component
poison.

**Prereq:** [Anathema](ref/invtalent/anathema)
