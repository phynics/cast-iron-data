Investigator talents marked with an asterisk (\*) add effects to an investigator’s studied combat or
studied strike. Only one of these talents can be applied to an individual attack, but the decision
can be made when the damage is dealt.

When the investigator deals damage with studied strike, he can perform a [trip](ref/rule/trip)
combat maneuver as a free action against the creature damaged by studied strike. This trip does not
provoke attacks of opportunity. An investigator must be at least 9th level to select this talent.
