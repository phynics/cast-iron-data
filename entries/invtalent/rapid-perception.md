**&L&Rapid Perception (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** An investigator with this talent can attempt a [Perception](ref/skill/perception) check
to intentionally search for a specific item or creature as a **swift action** instead of a move
action. If she is intentionally searching for an invisible creature, the creature's bonus on its
Stealth check from its [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility) is halved.

**Prereq:** [Expanded inspiration](ref/invtalent/expanded-inspiration)
