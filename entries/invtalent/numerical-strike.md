**Numerical Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day when the investigator has confirmed a critical hit with a _studied
strike,_ he can deal average damage instead of rolling damage as normal. This includes the damage
dealt by the successful attack as well as the additional damage from the _studied strike._

**Prereq:** 5th-level investigator

**Special:** He can expend a use of _inspiration_ to use this ability a second time per day. Using
this ability doesn't require an action.
