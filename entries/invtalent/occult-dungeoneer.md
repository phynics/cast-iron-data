**Occult Dungeoneer (Su)**

**Ability:** An investigator with this ability can use [spell trigger](ref/misc/spell-trigger) and
[spell completion](ref/misc/spell-completion) items as if the following spells were on a spell list
to which he has access: [detect secret doors](ref/spell/detect-secret-doors),
[find traps](ref/spell/find-traps), [knock](ref/spell/knock),
[locate object](ref/spell/locate-object), and [obscure object](ref/spell/obscure-object).

If the [spell-trigger](ref/misc/spell-trigger) device allows a wielder to apply his own caster level
(such as with a staff), the investigator may use his class level as his caster level.

**Flavor:** Street thieves in Absalom expect wares to be securely locked, and sometimes it takes
high-quality tools and magic training to ensure there's a meal at the end of the day.

**Special:** The investigator may also use _[knock](ref/spell/knock)_ as a supernatural ability once
per day, using his investigator level as his caster level.
