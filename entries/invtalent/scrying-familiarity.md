**Scrying Familiarity (Ex)**

**Ability:** You are well acquainted with scrying sensors. You can roll twice and take the better
result on saving throws against divination (scrying) spells and effects, on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to notice scrying sensors, and on caster level checks to
overcome [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) when you use a scrying spell or effect.

**Special:** If you notice a magical sensor, you can attempt a [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) check
opposed by the caster's caster level check to avoid being detected by the sensor.
