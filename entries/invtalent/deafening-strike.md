**&L&Deafening Strike (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** When an investigator deals damage with _studied strike,_ the opponent must succeed at a
Fortitude saving throw or be permanently [deafened](ref/misc/deafened). A successful Fortitude
saving throw reduces the duration of this effect to 1 round. The DC for this save is equal to 10 +
1/2 the investigator's level + his Intelligence modifier.

**Prereq:** 15th-level investigator

**Special:** This talent has no effect on deaf creatures. Deafness can be cured by
[heal](ref/spell/heal), [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration),
[remove blindness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness), or similar effects.
