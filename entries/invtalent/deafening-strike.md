Investigator talents marked with an asterisk (\*) add effects to an investigator’s studied combat or
studied strike. Only one of these talents can be applied to an individual attack, but the decision
can be made when the damage is dealt.

When an investigator deals damage with studied strike, the opponent must succeed at a Fortitude
saving throw or be permanently [deafened](ref/misc/deafened). A successful Fortitude saving throw
reduces the duration of this effect to 1 round. The DC for this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 the
investigator's level + his Intelligence modifier. This talent has no effect on deaf creatures.
Deafness can be cured by _[heal](ref/spell/heal)_, _[regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration)_,
_[remove blindness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness)_, or similar effects. An investigator must
be at least 15th level to select this talent.
