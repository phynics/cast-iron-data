**Applied Engineering (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The investigator can leverage his knowledge of engineering to solve tasks that normally
require brute strength or keen eyes.

**Fullround:** He can expend one use of _inspiration_ to study an object or area and attempt a
[Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering) check. On his next turn, he can use the
result of that Knowledge (engineering) check in place of a Strength check to break the object or in
place of a Perception check to locate hidden doors or compartments in that area.
