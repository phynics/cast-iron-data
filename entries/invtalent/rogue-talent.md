**&L&Rogue Talent (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** An investigator can select one of the following rogue talents in place of an
investigator talent: [acrobatic assist](ref/talent/acrobatic-assist),
[assault leader](ref/talent/assault-leader),
[black market connections](ref/talent/black-market-connections),
[camouflage](ref/talent/camouflage), [canny observer](ref/talent/canny-observer),
[charmer](ref/talent/charmer), [coax information](ref/talent/coax-information),
[combat swipe](ref/talent/combat-swipe), [convincing lie](ref/talent/convincing-lie),
[cunning trigger](ref/talent/cunning-trigger), [deft palm](ref/talent/deft-palm),
[demand attention](ref/talent/demand-attention), [expert leaper](ref/talent/expert-leaper),
[fast fingers](ref/talent/fast-fingers), [fast getaway](ref/talent/fast-getaway),
[fast picks](ref/talent/fast-picks), [fast stealth](ref/talent/fast-stealth),
[firearm training](ref/talent/firearm-training), [got your back](ref/talent/got-your-back),
[guileful polyglot](ref/talent/guileful-polyglot), [grit](ref/talent/grit),
[hard to fool](ref/talent/hard-to-fool), [hold breath](ref/talent/hold-breath),
[honeyed words](ref/talent/honeyed-words), [iron guts](ref/talent/iron-guts),
[lasting poison](ref/talent/lasting-poison), [ledge walker](ref/talent/ledge-walker),
[major magic](ref/talent/major-magic), [minor magic](ref/talent/minor-magic),
[nimble climber](ref/talent/nimble-climber), [peerless maneuver](ref/talent/peerless-maneuver),
[quick disable](ref/talent/quick-disable), [quick disguise](ref/talent/quick-disguise),
[quick trapsmith](ref/talent/quick-trapsmith), [resiliency](ref/talent/resiliency),
[rogue crawl](ref/talent/rogue-crawl), [rope master](ref/talent/rope-master),
[stand up](ref/talent/stand-up), [strong stroke](ref/talent/strong-stroke),
[terrain mastery](ref/talent/terrain-mastery), [trap spotter](ref/talent/trap-spotter), or
[wall scramble](ref/talent/wall-scramble).

Any talent effects based on rogue level use the investigator's class level. If the rogue talent has
a prerequisite (such as the _major magic_ rogue talent requiring the _minor magic_ talent), the
investigator must fulfill the prerequisite before selecting that rogue talent.

**Special:** This talent can be selected multiple times; each time, it grants the investigator a new
rogue talent.
