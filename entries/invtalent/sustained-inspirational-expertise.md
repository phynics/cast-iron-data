**Sustained Inspirational Expertise (Ex)**

**Prereq:** 11th-level investigator,
[inspirational expertise](ref/invtalent/inspirational-expertise)

**Swift:** When granting a bonus to allies via the _inspirational expertise_ investigator talent,
the investigator can spend one use of _inspiration_ the following round to extend the duration of
the granted bonus for 1 additional round. The insight bonus granted decreases by 1 each round this
ability is used.
