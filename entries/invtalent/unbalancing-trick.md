**Unbalancing Trick (Ex)**

**Ability:** The investigator gains [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip) as a bonus feat, even if
she does not meet the prerequisites.

**At 6th Level:** She is treated as if she meets all the prerequisites of
[Greater Trip](ref/feat/greater-trip) (although she must take the feat as normal to gain its
benefits).
