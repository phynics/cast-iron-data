**Alchemist Discovery (Ex)**

**Ability:** The investigator can select one of the following alchemist discoveries as an
investigator talent: [combine extracts](ref/discovery/combine-extracts),
[concentrate poison](ref/discovery/concentrate-poison), [dilution](ref/discovery/dilution),
[elixir of life](ref/discovery/elixir-of-life), [enhance potion](ref/discovery/enhance-potion),
[eternal potion](ref/discovery/eternal-potion), [extend potion](ref/discovery/extend-potion),
[infusion](ref/discovery/infusion), [mutagen](ref/discovery/mutagen), and
[poison conversion](ref/discovery/poison-conversion).

He can also choose to learn an [annointing](ref/ability/annointings), instead. When selecting an
alchemist discovery or annointing, he must be high enough level to qualify for it, using his
investigator level as his alchemist level to determine if he qualifies.

**Special:** This talent can be selected multiple times; each time grants a new alchemist discovery.
