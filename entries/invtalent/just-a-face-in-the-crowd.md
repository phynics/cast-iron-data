**Just a Face in the Crowd (Su)**

**Flavor:** The rabble-rousers of Galt can blend into a crowd and are able to hide and spot others
who appear to be hiding.

**Passive:** The investigator gains a bonus equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his
class level on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) and [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks when 10
or more creatures of his size are within 30 feet of him.
