**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 120\
A stygian slayer crawls out of the darkest shadows to strike fear into the hearts of civilized folk.
He's a merciless killer who can control a sliver of magic, allowing him to arrive unseen, commit
murder, and depart without detection.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A stygian slayer is proficient with light armor, but not with medium armor, heavy armor,
or any kind of shield (including tower shields).

**Replaces:** Armor proficiency

**Invisibility (Sp)**

**Ability:** A stygian slayer can cast [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), using his slayer
level as his caster level. The slayer uses his Intelligence modifier for
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks when using this ability.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 4th level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 4 slayer levels

**Spell Use**

**Ability:** A stygian slayer is able to use spell completion and spell trigger items as if he were
an arcane caster with these spells on his spell list: [darkness](ref/spell/darkness),
[forced quiet](ref/spell/forced-quiet), [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory),
[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection), [obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist),
[phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed), [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk), and wizard spells of
the illusion school of spell level 0 through 4th. The slayer's uses his class level as his caster
level for this ability.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Stalker

**Shadowy Mist Form (Sp)**

**Ability:** A stygian slayer can transform into an inky black cloud of mist. This functions as
[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form), except it also obscures vision as
[fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud).

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** Advanced slayer talent gained at 10th level

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per slayer level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**The following slayer talents complement the stygian slayer archetype:** poison use, rogue
talent\|[bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack), [lasting poison](ref/talent/lasting-poison),
[powerful sneak](ref/talent/powerful-sneak), [rogue crawl](ref/talent/rogue-crawl),
[slow reactions](ref/talent/slow-reactions), [surprise attack](ref/talent/surprise-attack),
[swift poison](ref/talent/swift-poison)

**The following advanced slayer talents complement the stygian slayer archetype:** advanced rogue
talent\|[hunter's surprise](ref/talent/hunters-surprise), assassinate, slayer camouflage
