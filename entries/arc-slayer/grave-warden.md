While paladins and inquisitors use their connection with the divine to fight undead hordes and other
horrors of the night, a grave warden relies on knowledge, skill with weapons, and tenacity to put an
end to these night-born terrors.

**Holy Water Sprinkler (Ex)**

**Ability:** A grave warden can draw a flask of [holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water) as if it were
a weapon (and can thus use the [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw) feat to draw flasks of holy water).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 2nd level

**Swift:** He can open a flask of holy water and pour it onto a held or adjacent melee weapon. If
the weapon successfully hits an undead creature before the end of the grave warden's next turn, the
undead takes damage as if it took a direct hit from the holy water, in addition to the damage from
the weapon, if any.

**Death Ward (Sp)**

**Ability:** A grave warden learns to perform a short ritual that grants the benefits of
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward), using his slayer level as his caster level. Performing this
ritual takes 1 minute and uses 4 flasks of holy water. The grave warden can protect only himself
with this ability.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Stalker

**Dustbringer (Ex)**

**Ability:** A grave warden can slay a _studied_ undead opponent. This functions as the
[assassinate](ref/slayertalent/assassinate) slayer talent, except it requires a successful Will
saving throw instead of a successful Fortitude save, affects only undead, and destroys the target
instead of killing it. If the target succeeds at its saving throw, it becomes immune to that grave
warden's dustbringer ability for 24 hours.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** Advanced slayer talent gained at 10th level

**The following rogue talents complement the grave warden archetype:** fast stealth, finesse rogue,
surprise attack, swift poison, terrain mastery, trap spotter, unwitting ally

**The following advanced slayer talents complement the grave warden archetype:** advanced rogue
talent\|[hunter's surprise](ref/talent/hunters-surprise), slayer camouflage
