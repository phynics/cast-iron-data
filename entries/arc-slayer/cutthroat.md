**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 119\
Street-smart and extremely resourceful, cutthroats stalk city streets and alleyways, preying on
those unfortunate enough to catch their eye. More opportunists than careful planners, they don't
care who they rob or kill, and take advantage of whatever circumstances put them near a suitable
target.

**Gained class skills:** diplo, kno, ling

**Lost class skills:** kd, kg, sur

**Street Stalker (Ex)**

**Choice:** A cutthroat selects the Acrobatics, Climb, or Knowledge (local) skill. Once selected,
this cannot be changed.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** When in urban environments, the cutthroat adds 1/2 his slayer level (minimum 1) to
checks attempted with this skill.

**Replaces:** Track

**Opportune Target (Ex)**

**Ability:** If a cutthroat can act in the surprise round, once during that surprise round he can
study a foe as a **free action** (instead of a move action) and select it as his _studied target._

**Flavor:** A cutthroat who catches a foe by surprise can quickly study that foe to find its
weaknesses.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 2nd level

**Stab and Grab (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 6th level

**Swift:** A cutthroat can attempt a [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver against a dead or
unconscious target or a _studied target_ he has just confirmed a critical hit against. A dead or
unconscious opponent takes a -10 penalty to its CMD against this maneuver.

**The following slayer talents complement the cutthroat archetype:** poison use, rogue
talent\|[fast stealth](ref/talent/fast-stealth), [surprise attack](ref/talent/surprise-attack),
[terrain mastery](ref/talent/terrain-mastery), slowing strike

**The following advanced slayer talents complement the cutthroat archetype:** advanced rogue
talent\|[knock-out blow](ref/talent/knock-out-blow), [opportunist](ref/talent/opportunist),
assassinate, slayer camouflage
