**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 66\
Most slayers focus their efforts on single targets, but family hunters try to root out every branch
of a tainted tree of life.

**Find Family (Sp)**

**Ability:** She can target any creature from that family line with
[discern next of kin](ref/spell/discern-next-of-kin) (with a caster level equal to her slayer level)
at will as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). The save DC is Charisma-based and
is modified by the slayer's _studied target_ bonus if the creature is also her _studied target._ A
creature that successfully saves against this effect is immune to this ability for 24 hours.

**Choice:** A family hunter chooses a particular family line of mortal creatures to cleanse (she
must choose an actual family line like &quot&The Montrose family,&quot& rather than something
generic that would cover all creatures of a given kind like &quot&All humans descended from the
first human created by the gods&quot&).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 2nd level

**Special:** If the family hunter's chosen family line is completely eradicated, she can choose a
new one after 1 month of studying.

**Kinslayer (Su)**

**Ability:** A family hunter's sneak attack deals an additional 1d6 points of damage to any creature
with a blood relation to the family line she seeks to cleanse.

**Alters:** Sneak attack

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Studied Target (Ex)**

**Alters:** Studied target

**Info:** A slayer can maintain more than one _studied target_ at a time only if all targets are
closely related to one another (even if they aren't part of the family line she seeks to cleanse).
This includes blood relatives; undead spawn; constructs or undead and their creator; summoned
creatures and their summoner; creatures that are charmed, dominated, or otherwise under direct
magical control and their controller; and cohorts, followers, animal companions, familiars, and
similar associates of one master.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Disrupt Teamwork (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A family hunter's _studied target_ never counts as an ally to its allies for the
purpose of [flanking](ref/rule/flanking) the family hunter or using teamwork feats or the
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action against the family hunter.

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 6th level
