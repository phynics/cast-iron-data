**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 24\
Followers of a tradition dating back to the ancient empire of Yixing, the yellow-robed agents known
as butterfly blades now operate primarily in Goka, where many serve the city's politically savvy
ruler. Butterfly blades work in the shadows, eliminating threats with their namesake weapons and
intimidating troublesome nobles.

**Studied Stalker (Ex)**

**Ability:** A butterfly blade gains the _stalker_ class feature at 1st level, rather than at 7th
level.

**Alters:** Studied target

**Replaces:** Track

**Special:** The butterfly blade doesn't add his _studied target_ bonus on Knowledge or Survival
checks.

**Bonus Feats**

**Alters:** Slayer talents

**Choice:** Whenever a butterfly blade would gain a slayer talent, he can instead select a feat from
the following list: [Cutting Humiliation](ref/feat/cutting-humiliation),
[Enforcer](ref/feat/enforcer), [Intimidating Prowess](ref/feat/intimidating-prowess),
[Nerve-Racking Negotiator](ref/feat/nerve-racking-negotiator), or
[Threatening Negotiator](ref/feat/threatening-negotiator). The butterfly blade does not need to meet
the feat's prerequisites.

**Butterfly's Kiss (Ex)**

**Ability:** A butterfly blade can deal nonlethal damage with
[butterfly swords](ref/eq-weapon/butterfly-sword) without taking a penalty on attack rolls.

**Flavor:** Butterfly blades train to inflict painful cuts rather than lethal ones.

**At 15th Level:** The bonus increases to +3 and the damage die increases another step.

**At 3rd Level:** A butterfly blade gets a +1 bonus on attack rolls with butterfly swords, and the
damage die of any butterfly sword he wields increases
[by one step](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments).

**At 6th Level:** The butterfly blade gains the _knockout_ class feature, as per a
[brawler](ref/class/brawler) of his class level - 2.

**At 9th Level:** The bonus becomes +2 and the damage die increases another step.

**Replaces:** Sneak attack

**Knockout (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Text from the brawler class ability.

**At 10th Level:** You can use this twice a day.

**At 16th Level:** You can use this three times per day.

**Special:** Each round on its turn, the unconscious target may attempt a new saving throw to end
the effect as a **full-round action** that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. Creatures immune
to critical hits or nonlethal damage are immune to this ability.

**Standard:** Once a day, you can unleash a devastating attack that can instantly knock a target
unconscious. You must announce this intent before making your attack roll. If you hit and the target
takes damage from the blow, the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your brawler level + the higher of your Strength or
Dexterity modifier) or fall unconscious for 1d6 rounds.

**Innocent Butterfly (Sp)**

**Ability:** A butterfly blade can use
[aura of the unremarkable](ref/spell/aura-of-the-unremarkable) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 11th Level:** He can use it twice per day.

**At 14th Level:** It becomes a constant [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) that he
can activate or deactivate as a **free action**.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Stalker, swift tracker, quarry

**Special:** The ability's caster level is equal to his slayer class level, and the DC is equal to
10 + the number of ranks the butterfly blade has in either Bluff or Intimidate, whichever is higher.

**Deadly Butterfly (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A butterfly blade becomes a bloody whirlwind in combat.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Passive:** When he strikes a foe with a butterfly sword, he automatically confirms critical
threats. In addition, any butterfly sword he wields is treated as if it has the
[speed](ref/magic-enh/speed) weapon special ability. If the weapon already has the _speed_ weapon
special ability, this doesn't stack.

**Replaces:** Improved quarry
