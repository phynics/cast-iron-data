Bloody jakes are cruel backwoods folk who prey upon their country cousins while terrorizing
civilized people who venture into their rural range.

**Alignment**

**Info:** A bloody jake who becomes nonevil cannot gain new levels as a slayer but retains all his
class abilities.

**Prereq:** A bloody jake must be of an evil alignment.

**Favored Terrain (Ex)**

**Choice:** A bloody jake can select a type of terrain from the
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain) table for [rangers](ref/class/ranger), though he is
restricted to cold, desert, forest, jungle, mountains, plains, and swamp.

**At 5th Level:** His bonuses in the selected terrain increase by 2.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Proficiency in medium armor and shields

**Special:** He does not gain the ability to select additional _favored terrains_ unless he takes
the [terrain mastery](ref/talent/terrain-mastery) rogue talent.

At 10th level: His bonuses in the selected terrain now increase by 4.

At 15th level: His bonuses in the selected terrain now increase by 6.

At 20th level: His bonuses in the selected terrain now increase by 8.

**Cruel Tricks**

**Ability:** He gains the _cruel tricks_ below at the indicated slayer levels.

**Flavor:** Bloody jakes use backwoods folk magic to confuse, terrify, and weaken their prey.

**Replaces:** The 1st-level studied target, slayer talents gained at 4th, 8th, 12th, and 16th level

**Usage:** slayer level + Charisma modifier times/day

**Distant Voices (Sp)**

**Ability:** A bloody jake can cause his voice to seem to emanate from somewhere else, as per
[ventriloquism](ref/spell/ventriloquism). This ability doesn't take any other action to activate and
can be used as part of speaking (as a **free action**); it costs one use of _cruel tricks_ to affect
his speech for 1 round.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Lose Your Way (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Standard:** A bloody jake can spend one use of _cruel tricks_ to cause a creature that is within
30 feet and within the bloody jake's _favored terrain_ to become disoriented, taking a penalty on
Survival checks equal to the bloody jake's slayer level and a -2 penalty on saving throws against
fear and illusions. This effect lasts for 1 hour per slayer level of the bloody jake or for as long
as the affected creature remains within the bloody jake's _favored terrain,_ whichever is shorter.

**Something's Not Right (Su)**

**At 12th Level:** He can use this ability as a **swift action**.

**At 16th Level:** A bloody jake can make any number of the affected foes
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for 1 round, though such foes receive a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2
the loody jake's slayer level + his Charisma modifier) to negate the frightened condition; even so,
such a foe is still shaken.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Move:** A bloody jake can spend one use of _cruel tricks_ to cause all foes within 30 feet to
become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round. This is a fear effect that doesn't increase in
strength by applying other effects that cause creatures to become shaken.

**The Woods Have Eyes (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Swift:** A bloody jake in his _favored terrain_ can spend one use of _cruel tricks_ to gain
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) in a 30-foot radius for 1 round; this
[blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) is sound-based.

**Poor Study (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bloody jake gains _studied target_ at 5th level rather than 1st level, with a bonus
equal to [one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his slayer level.

**Alters:** Studied target

**At 11th Level:** A bloody jake may study an opponent as a **move** or **swift action**.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Sadistic Snare (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bloody jake gains [Learn Ranger Trap](ref/feat/learn-ranger-trap) as a bonus feat,
benefiting from it as if he were a ranger. He can expend two uses of his traps ability to add his
sneak attack damage to the trap's damage; any slayer talents or rogue talents he has that modify
sneak attack damage, such as [bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack), also apply to sneak
attack damage dealt by his ranger traps.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 6th level

**Cruel Trick (Ex)**

**Ability:** A bloody jake can maim or humiliate his target with a sneak attack, substituting 1
point of [Dexterity or Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per die of sneak attack
damage forfeited. A successful Will save negates this ability damage (DC = 10 + 1/2 the bloody
jake's slayer level + his Charisma modifier).

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Swift tracker

**Woodland Shortcut (Sp)**

**Ability:** A bloody jake can move with supernatural ease among the trees once per day, as if using
[tree stride](ref/spell/tree-stride) with a caster level equal to his slayer level.

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Replaces:** Slayer's advance
