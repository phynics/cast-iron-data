Born into cultures where magic users are hated and feared, witch killers devote their lives to purge
the arcane taint from the world.

**Gained class skills:** ka, spc

**Studied Witch (Ex)**

**Ability:** A witch killer's _studied combat_ bonus applies on Spellcraft checks and saving throws
against arcane spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) and hexes. This bonus
is reduced by 1 (minimum 0) if the target is not an arcane spellcaster, or a being called or
summoned by arcane magic.

**Alters:** Studied target

**Special:** He does not gain the increases to the maximum number of _studied targets_ he can have
at a time normally attained at 5th, 10th, 15th, and 20th levels, but his bonuses still increase at
those levels.

**Superstitous (Ex)**

**Ability:** Instead of being able to select ranger combat style feats as slayer talents, a witch
killer can select any of the following barbarian rage powers as slayer talents, treating his slayer
level as his barbarian level: [clear mind](ref/ragepower/clear-mind),
[disruptive](ref/ragepower/disruptive), [eater of magic](ref/ragepower/eater-of-magic),
[spell sunder](ref/ragepower/spell-sunder), [spellbreaker](ref/ragepower/spellbreaker),
[sunder enchantment](ref/ragepower/sunder-enchantment), [superstition](ref/ragepower/superstition),
or [witch hunter](ref/ragepower/witch-hunter).

**Alters:** Slayer talents

**Special:** If the witch killer selects a rage power that can normally be used only once per rage
(_eater of magic, spell sunder,_ or _sunder enchantment_), he can use it once per day per 5 slayer
levels he possesses, though no more than once per round in any event. The other rage powers affect
the slayer constantly, though the _superstition_ and _witch hunter_ rage powers apply only against
arcane spells and [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) and the creatures that use
them.

**Lingering Thrust (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a witch killer's attack against an arcane spellcaster in the midst of casting
forces the spellcaster to attempt a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check, the witch killer
adds half his slayer level to the check's DC.

**Ability2:** When he makes a sneak attack against an arcane spellcaster, up to 1 point of sneak
attack damage per slayer level counts as ongoing damage for the purpose of forcing the spellcaster
to attempt concentration checks (the witch killer does not add half his slayer level to this DC).

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 4th level

**Scent Magic (Su)**

**Ability:** He can use [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) at will (as per a
[paladin's](ref/class/paladin) ability to _[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)_), but only against
arcane magic, and the ability is scent-based and thus defeated by effects like
[negate aroma](ref/spell/negate-aroma).

**Flavor:** A witch killer can detect arcane magic by scent.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** Even when he isn't concentrating on using his _scent magic,_ when a creature within
scent range casts an arcane spell (including arcane
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) or magic item effects), the witch killer
automatically pinpoints the caster's location.

**Burn the Witch (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day when a witch killer observes a creature within 30 feet cast an arcane
spell or use an arcane [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), he can use
[foster hatred](ref/spell/foster-hatred) on that character, or target one creature harmed by the
arcane effect with [vengeful outrage](ref/spell/vengeful-outrage), identifying the caster as the
enemy.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** Slayer talent gained at 10th level

**Special:** The save DC is Charisma-based and uses the witch killer's slayer level as his caster
level.

At 15th level: He can use this ability 2 times a day.

At 20th level: He can use this ability 3 times a day.
