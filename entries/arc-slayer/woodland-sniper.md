Woodland snipers are guardians of forest and grove, keeping vigil on their borders and hunting
bounties and trespassers from the shelter of sturdy branches.

**Tree Climber (Ex)**

**Ability:** With a successful DC 15 Acrobatics check, he can make ranged attacks with bows while
balancing on branches.

**Passive:** A woodland sniper adds half his level to Acrobatics and Climb checks to move between,
through, or up trees.

**Replaces:** Track

**Ranged Sneak Attack (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever a woodland sniper makes a ranged attack against a creature within 30 feet that
is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC (whether the target actually has a Dexterity bonus or not), he
deals additional damage. This damage is a number of d6 equal to
[one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his slayer level. Should the woodland sniper score a
critical hit with his ranged attack, this additional damage is not multiplied.

**Alters:** Sneak attack

**At 6th Level:** The range at which he can make a sneak attack increases by 10 feet.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Special:** A woodland sniper cannot use sneak attack with a melee attack.

At 9th level: The range at which he can make a sneak attack is now increased by 20 feet.

At 12th level: The range at which he can make a sneak attack is now increased by 30 feet.

At 15th level: The range at which he can make a sneak attack is now increased by 40 feet.

At 18th level: The range at which he can make a sneak attack is now increased by 50 feet.

**Still Shot (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** When he has [cover](ref/rule/cover) provided by one or more trees, the woodland sniper
reduces the penalty on Stealth checks to remain hidden while sniping by half his slayer level.

**Replaces:** Stalker

**Branchwalking (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** The woodland sniper gains a climb speed equal to his base speed.

**At 13th Level:** He can move from one tree to another via connecting branches by adding 5 to the
DC of an Acrobatics or Climb check to move within the canopy. The woodland sniper cannot end his
movement on a branch that cannot support his weight.

**Replaces:** Swift tracker, slayer's advance
