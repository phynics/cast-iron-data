**Price** 500 gp

### Construction

**Craft DC** 25; **Cost** 250 gp\
[Craft Pharmaceutical](ref/feat/craft-pharmaceutical), [medical lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

The glass receptacle of this injector contains 1 dose of a bright-yellow serum. When injected into a
creature (this is a standard action), the substance grants immunity to low
[radiation](ref/rule/radiation) for 1 hour, and a +5 alchemical bonus on all saving throws against
other radiation effects for that duration. In addition, it heals 1d4 points of
[Strength damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) caused by
[radiation poisoning](ref/rule/radiation). Vitality serum cannot cure Constitution drain caused by
radiation poisoning.
