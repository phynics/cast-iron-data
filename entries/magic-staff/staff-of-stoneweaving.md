**Sources:** Pathfinder \#34: Blood for Blood pg 67\
**Aura** strong abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 63,180 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

A staff of stoneweaving is a staff of rough-hewn obsidian with a glowing ruby floating between the
forked branches of its top end. It allows the use of the following spells:

- _[Spike stones](ref/spell/spike-stones)_ (1 charge)
- _[Statue](ref/spell/statue)_ (2 charges)
- _[Stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape)_ (1 charge)
- _[Stone to flesh](ref/spell/stone-to-flesh)_ (2 charges)
- _[Stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin)_ (2 charges)

Additionally, the staff of stoneweaving acts as a +1
[halfling sling-staff](ref/eq-weapon/halfling-sling-staff). The staff creates its own ammunition -
ruby [sling bullets](ref/eq-weapon/sling-bullets) which disappear after striking or missing their
target. These bullets are created by tapping the head of the staff against the ground (a move
action). The user cannot pick up bullets after firing them or otherwise harvest them; at the end of
the round after a bullet is created, it disappears from existence.
