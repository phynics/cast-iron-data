**Sources:** Pathfinder \#72: The Witch Queen's Revenge pg 63

**Aura** strong evocation and transmutation; **CL** 19th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 228,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This staff is made of a single icicle that tapers to a sharpened point at each end. It can be used
as a +3 [icy burst](ref/magic-enh/icy-burst) [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff) (only one
end of the staff bears this magical enhancement), even if all of its charges are drained. It allows
the use of the following spells and abilities:

- _[Frostbite](ref/spell/frostbite)_ (1 charge)
- _[Ice storm](ref/spell/ice-storm)_ (1 charge)
- _[Freezing sphere](ref/spell/freezing-sphere)_ (2 charges)
- _[Icy prison](ref/spell/icy-prison)_ (2 charges)
- _[Wall of ice](ref/spell/wall-of-ice)_ (2 charges)
- _[Cone of cold](ref/spell/cone-of-cold)_ (3 charges)
- _[Ice body](ref/spell/ice-body)_ (3 charges)

Like a frost brand, Winter's Reach extinguishes all nonmagical fires in a 20-foot radius. As a
standard action, it can also _dispel_ lasting fire spells, but not instantaneous effects. The bearer
must succeed at a _dispel_ check (1d20+19) against each spell to _dispel_ it. The DC to _dispel_
such spells is 11 + the caster level of the fire spell. The staff can also be used to
[counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) any spell with the fire descriptor as if with
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)._ Dispelling or counterspelling a fire spell in this way
uses 1 charge.
