**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 199, Advanced Player's Guide pg 297\
**Aura** moderate varies; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 52,850 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Dark and solid, this worn staff appears to be carved of rough stone, yet feels and weighs like light
wood. It allows use of the following spells:

- _[Arcane lock](ref/spell/arcane-lock)_ (1 charge)
- _[Grease](ref/spell/grease)_ (1 charge)
- _[Wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall)_ (1 charge)
- _[Wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire)_ (2 charges)
- _[Wall of ice](ref/spell/wall-of-ice)_ (2 charges)
- _[Wall of stone](ref/spell/wall-of-stone)_ (3 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [arcane lock](ref/spell/arcane-lock),
[grease](ref/spell/grease), [wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire),
[wall of ice](ref/spell/wall-of-ice), [wall of stone](ref/spell/wall-of-stone),
[wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall); **Cost** 27,050 gp
