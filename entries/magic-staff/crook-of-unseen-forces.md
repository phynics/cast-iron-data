**Aura** strong conjuration and evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 56,300 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This iron staff, silvered and inlaid with diamonds, curves at one end like a shepherd's crook. It
functions as a +1/+1 [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff) and grants an additional +2 bonus on
[drag](ref/rule/drag) and [reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuvers. However, its true
power is to summon and command invisible forces, allowing the use of the following spells.

- _[Spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon)_ (1 charge)
- _[Spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally)_ (2 charges)
- _[Summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6)_ (3 charges,
  [invisible stalker](ref/monster/invisible-stalker) only)

Both the _[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon)_ and
_[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally)_ gain a +1 enhancement bonus to attack and damage, and
any _[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon)_ or _[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally)_
created by the crook is invisible as per [greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater),
gaining the [usual benefits](ref/rule/combat-modifiers) when attacking creatures are unable to see
it.

The wielder of a crook of unseen forces can spend 1 or more charges as a standard action to create
an invisible wave of force in a 30-foot line. This wave of force attempts a drag, reposition, or
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver (wielder's choice) against one creature in this line for each
charge expended. This special combat maneuver check does not provoke attacks of opportunity and uses
the better of the wielder's Charisma, Intelligence, or Wisdom modifiers in place of his Strength
modifier to his CMB.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),

[telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Cost** 28,300 gp
