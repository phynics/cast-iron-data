**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

A ring of curved fangs circles the tip of this 7-foot-long bone staff. When
_[magic mouth](ref/spell/magic-mouth)_ is cast from the staff, the resulting illusory mouth is
filled with countless needle-like fangs. After _[magic mouth](ref/spell/magic-mouth)_ is cast, one
charge remains drained from the staff and can't be recharged until the user
[dismisses](ref/misc/dismissible) the spell or until the spell discharges.

When casting [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1) from the staff, the caster can choose only
creatures with bite attacks, and the user's bite attacks deal damage as if the assumed shape were
[two sizes larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments).

The staff allows the use of the following spells.

- _[Magic mouth](ref/spell/magic-mouth)_ (1 charge)
- _[Beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1)_ (see text; 2 charges)
- _[Hungry pit](ref/spell/hungry-pit)_ (3 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1),
[hungry pit](ref/spell/hungry-pit), [magic mouth](ref/spell/magic-mouth); **Cost** 15,500 gp
