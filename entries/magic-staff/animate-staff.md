**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 49,800 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Created from a number of iron rods, copper gears, and silver screws, this staff constantly shifts
and changes. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[Make whole](ref/spell/make-whole)_ (1 charge)
- _[Mending](ref/spell/mending)_ (1 charge)
- _[Animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)_ (2 charges)

In addition, once per day, the staff can become a Medium
[animated object](ref/monster/animated-object) with the _metal_ special ability. It can remain in
this form for up to 10 minutes. If destroyed while in this form, it reverts to being a staff, but it
loses all of its charges and this ability cannot be used again until it is fully recharged.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects),
[make whole](ref/spell/make-whole), [mending](ref/spell/mending); **Cost** 24,900 gp
