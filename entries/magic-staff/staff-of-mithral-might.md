**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 58,000 gp; **Weight** 7 lbs.

This staff is made of silver, with a shimmering sphere of [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) at
either end. One end features a golden snake wrapped around the staff and cradling the larger of
these two mithral spheres. The staff may be used as a weapon, functioning as a +2/+2
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff). It also grants a +2 enhancement bonus to Intelligence to
whoever possesses it. These two attributes continue to function after all the charges are expended.
It allows the use of the following spells:

- _[Bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength)_ (1 charge)
- _[Enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_ (1 charge)
- _[Telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis)_ (3 charges)
- _[Flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone)_ (4 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength),
[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person), [flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone),
[fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning), [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Cost** 29,300 gp
