**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 217\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Whenever the user is holding this wooden staff, 10 bulging eyes carved along the length of the staff
animate, granting the user a +5 competence bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks as
long as he is grasping the staff. As a move action, the wielder can select a creature within 100
feet and see through its eyes with no saving throw. The creature selected must be either a spider
summoned with the staff or a creature [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) by a
_[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness)_ spell cast from the staff (the wielder ignores
the _[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness)_ effect). The connection lasts as long as
the wielder and the target creature remain within 100 feet of each other, though the wielder can end
it as a free action. The effect also ends if the target was a blinded creature and regains the
ability to see. The wielder is blinded while maintaining this connection.

The staff allows the use of the following spells.

- _[Blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness)_ (blindness only; 1 charge)
- _[Summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2)_ (giant spider only; 1 charge)
- _[Prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes)_ (3 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff),
[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness), [prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes),
[summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2); **Cost** 16,000 gp
