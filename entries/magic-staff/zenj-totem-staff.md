**Aura** moderate varies; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 24,800 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

A creature that willingly grasps a zenj totem staff is affected as though it were in a
_[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth)._ This effect has no saving throw, lasts as long as the
creature remains in contact with the staff, and can simultaneously affect as many creatures as can
hold the staff at one time. This power is always on, and doesn't need to be activated.

The staff also acts as a normal magical staff and contains the following spells:

- _[Protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil)_ (1 charge)
- _[Summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)_ (animals only, 2 charges)
- _[Aura sight](ref/spell/aura-sight)_ (3 charges)
- _[Summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4)_ (animals only, 4 charges)

A [shaman](ref/class/shaman) who wields the staff can cast
_[summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2)_ and
_[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4)_ from the staff even though those spells
are not on her spell list. Additionally, once per day when a [druid](ref/class/druid) or shaman uses
the staff to cast either of those spells, she can instead summon the creature to perform a single
task for her with a duration of up to 1 hour per level. If summoned in this fashion, the summoned
creature refuses to enter combat and is immediately dismissed if it takes damage from any source.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [aura sight](ref/spell/aura-sight),
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil),
[summon nature's ally II](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-2),
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4),^[1](ref/magic-staff/zenj-totem-staff#footnote-n)^
[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth); **Cost** 12,400 gp

###### ^1^ {#footnote-n}

The original source lists _[summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1)_ and _II_ as
requirements, which feels like an error. Consult your GM.
