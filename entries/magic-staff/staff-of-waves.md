**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 63\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 90,300 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This staff is made of entwined coral and topped with a brilliant sapphire. The staff functions as a
+1 [quenching](ref/magic-enh/quenching) [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff) above the waves
and a +1 [impact](ref/magic-enh/impact) quarterstaff below, and attacks with the staff of waves take
no penalties for being [bludgeoning melee attacks underwater](ref/rule/physical-attacks-underwater).
It allows use of the following spells:

- _[Hydraulic torrent](ref/spell/hydraulic-torrent)_ (1 charge)
- _[Slipstream](ref/spell/slipstream)_ (1 charge)
- _[Wave form](ref/spell/wave-form)_ (2 charges)
- _[Ride the waves](ref/spell/ride-the-waves)_ (2 charges)
- _[Vortex](ref/spell/vortex)_ (3 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff),
[hydraulic torrent](ref/spell/hydraulic-torrent), [ride the waves](ref/spell/ride-the-waves),
[slipstream](ref/spell/slipstream), [vortex](ref/spell/vortex), [wave form](ref/spell/wave-form);
**Cost** 45,300 gp
