**Aura** strong varies; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 100,400 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Appearing to have grown naturally into its shape, this oak, ash, or yew staff allows use of the
following spells:

- _[Charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal)_ (1 charge)
- _[Speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ (1 charge)
- _[Barkskin](ref/spell/barkskin)_ (2 charges)
- _[Summon nature's ally VI](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-6)_ (3 charges)
- _[Wall of thorns](ref/spell/wall-of-thorns)_ (3 charges)
- _[Animate plants](ref/spell/animate-plants)_ (4 charges)

The staff may be used as a weapon, functioning as a +2 [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff).
The staff of the woodlands also allows its wielder to cast
_[pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace)_ at will, with no charge cost. These two
attributes continue to function after all the charges are expended.

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [animate plants](ref/spell/animate-plants),

[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),
[summon nature's ally VI](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-6),
[wall of thorns](ref/spell/wall-of-thorns); **Cost** 50,500 gp
