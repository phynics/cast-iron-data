**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 63,960 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This obsidian staff twinkles as if reflecting the star-filled sky at night. The staff allows use of
the following spells.

- _[Planar adaptation](ref/spell/planar-adaptation)_ (1 charge)
- _[Planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding)_ (2 charges)
- _[Plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ (5 charges)

If the staff of the planes is fully charged, the wielder can deliberately snap the staff, causing it
to shatter into thousands of pieces but opening a [gate](ref/spell/gate) to a plane of the wielder's
choice. This can only be used for planar travel, and the connection lasts for 1 minute, without
requiring the wielder's concentration. If used in this way, the staff cannot be repaired or
restored.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff),
[planar adaptation](ref/spell/planar-adaptation), [planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding),
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Cost** 31,980 gp
