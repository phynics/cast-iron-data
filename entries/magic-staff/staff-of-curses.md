**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 43,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This gnarled wooden staff is bedecked with broken charms and burnt fetishes that were once used to
ward against bad luck, but now are in shambles. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[Blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness)_ (1 charge)
- _[Ill omen](ref/spell/ill-omen)_ (1 charge)
- _[Baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph)_ (2 charges)
- _[Bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ (2 charges)

In addition, the staff of curses is a +1/+1 [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), and whenever
the wielder strikes a creature with the staff, he can expend one charge to cause the creature to
become cursed for a number of rounds equal to the number of charges remaining in the staff. While
this curse persists, the creature takes a -2 penalty on saving throws from spells cast from the
staff and a -2 penalty to AC against any attacks made by the staff.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse), [blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness),
[ill omen](ref/spell/ill-omen); **Cost** 22,050 gp
