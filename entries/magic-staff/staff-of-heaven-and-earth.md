**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 197, Pathfinder \#4: Fortress of the Stone Giants pg 23, Rise of
the Runelords Anniversary Edition pg 427\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 54,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Topped by a swirling cloudy stone and wrapped with black iron filigree, this staff allows use of the
following spells:

- _[Gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind)_ (1 charge)
- _[Stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape)_ (1 charge)
- _[Air walk](ref/spell/air-walk)_ (2 charges)
- _[Control winds](ref/spell/control-winds)_ (2 charges)
- _[Spike stones](ref/spell/spike-stones)_ (2 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [air walk](ref/spell/air-walk),
[control winds](ref/spell/control-winds), [gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind),
[spike stones](ref/spell/spike-stones), [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape); **Cost** 27,000 gp
