**Sources:** Heroes of the Wild pg 29\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 31,800 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This thick, 5-foot-long wooden staff is made of rough oak and has two branches growing from the top.
The branches twist together and bend back toward the body of the staff, and their leaves often
rustle as if blown by a light breeze even when the surrounding air is still. An oaken staff
functions as a +1 [called](ref/magic-enh/called) [spell storing](ref/magic-enh/spell-storing)
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), but can't be used as a [double](ref/misc/double) weapon
(only one end of the staff is usable in combat). The staff allows use of the following spells.

- _[Barkskin](ref/spell/barkskin)_ (1 charge)
- _[Plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth)_ (2 charges)
- _[Speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants)_ (2 charges)

The wielder is immune to the terrain effects of the _[plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth)_ spell
cast from the staff. In addition, [shillelagh](ref/spell/shillelagh) can be cast on an oaken staff
despite it being a magic quarterstaff, increasing its enhancement bonus by 1. The staff retains the
effects of the _[shillelagh](ref/spell/shillelagh)_ spell even if wielded by someone other than the
spell's caster. Once per day, a character wielding an oaken staff who has either
_[barkskin](ref/spell/barkskin)_ or _[plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth)_ on her class spell list
can cast _[speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants)_ from the staff without attempting a
[Use Magic Device](ref/skill/use-magic-device) check (this expends 3 charges instead of 2).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [barkskin](ref/spell/barkskin),
[plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth), [speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants),
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport), creator must be a caster of at least 12th level; **Cost** 16,050 gp
