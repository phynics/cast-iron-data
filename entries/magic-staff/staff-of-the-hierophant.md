**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 201\
**Aura** strong varies; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 220,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

The staff of the hierophant is a powerful magic item with the ability to both heal and harm, which
also gives its wielder a number of protections. The staff is crafted from platinum and topped with a
glowing blue diamond. The staff allows the use of the following spells:

- _[Bless](ref/spell/bless)_ (1 charge)
- _[Cure moderate wounds](ref/spell/cure-moderate-wounds)_ (1 charge)
- _[Freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement)_ (1 charge)
- _[Hold person](ref/spell/hold-person)_ (heightened to 5th level, 1 charge)
- _[Prayer](ref/spell/prayer)_ (1 charge)
- _[Searing light](ref/spell/searing-light)_ (1 charge)
- _[Cure critical wounds](ref/spell/cure-critical-wounds)_ (2 charges)
- _[Flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike)_ (2 charges)
- _[Righteous might](ref/spell/righteous-might)_ (2 charges)
- _[True seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing)_ (2 charges)

The wielder of a staff of the hierophant gains a +2 luck bonus to AC and on saving throws. The staff
is also a +2/+2 [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff), and its wielder may use it to smite
opponents. If 1 charge is expended (as a free action), the staff deals double damage (×3 on a
critical hit) for 1 round.

A staff of the hierophant can be used to restore the life of a dead creature by calling upon the
wielder's deity to take the staff as a blessing. This causes the staff to vanish, but restores life
to any one dead creature as if casting _[true resurrection](ref/spell/true-resurrection)._ In
addition, after using this effect, the wielder who triggered it receives a +2 luck bonus on all
ability checks, attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks for 1 day.
