**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 37,310 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This shining golden staff is topped with a radiant gemstone that emits light like a
[torch](ref/eq-misc/torch). As a swift action, the wielder of the staff can cause it to change into
a +2 [lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance), although it retains its other abilities. It can be made to revert
to its staff form as a free action. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[Bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon)_ (1 charge, cast on the staff only)
- _[Shield other](ref/spell/shield-other)_ (1 charge)
- _[Discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies)_ (2 charges)
- _[Holy sword](ref/spell/holy-sword)_ (4 charges, cast on the staff only)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon),
[discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies), [holy sword](ref/spell/holy-sword),
[shield other](ref/spell/shield-other); **Cost** 18,810 gp
