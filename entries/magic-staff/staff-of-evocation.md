**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 196, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 493\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 82,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This smooth staff carved from hickory or yew allows use of the following spells:

- _[Fireball](ref/spell/fireball)_ (1 charge)
- _[Magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile)_ (1 charge)
- _[Shatter](ref/spell/shatter)_ (1 charge)
- _[Ice storm](ref/spell/ice-storm)_ (2 charges)
- _[Wall of force](ref/spell/wall-of-force)_ (2 charges)
- _[Chain lightning](ref/spell/chain-lightning)_ (3 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [chain lightning](ref/spell/chain-lightning),
[fireball](ref/spell/fireball), [ice storm](ref/spell/ice-storm),
