**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,800 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This thin staff is made of stiff wood that has been wrapped in colorful bands of cloth and topped
with a number of feathers, bells, and other decorative baubles. The staff allows use of the
following spells:

- _[Ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound)_ (1 charge)
- _[Mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand)_ (1 charge)
- _[Prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation)_ (1 charge)
- _[Silent image](ref/spell/silent-image)_ (2 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand), [prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation),
[silent image](ref/spell/silent-image); **Cost** 4,400 gp
