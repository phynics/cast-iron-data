**Sources:** Pathfinder \#88: Valley of the Brain Collectors pg 63\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 43,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This staff, constructed of dark purple crystal, allows the use of the following spells:

- _[confusion](ref/spell/confusion)_ (2 charges)
- _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_ (1 charge)
- _fear_ (2 charges)
- _[irradiate](ref/spell/irradiate)_ (1 charge)
- _[planetary adaptation](ref/spell/planetary-adaptation)_ (2 charges)

The staff may be used as a weapon, functioning as a +1 [impact](ref/magic-enh/impact)
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff) even when its charges are expended.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength),
[confusion](ref/spell/confusion), [darkness](ref/spell/darkness), [fear](ref/spell/fear),
[irradiate](ref/spell/irradiate), [planetary adaptation](ref/spell/planetary-adaptation); **Cost**
21,900 gp
