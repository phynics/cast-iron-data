**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 196\
**Aura** moderate evocation and necromancy; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 47,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

Made from bleached bones that have been fused together, the head of this staff burns with an unholy
fire. This staff can be used as a +1 [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
[quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff) (only one end of the staff bears magical enhancement),
and it retains this property even if all its charges are drained. This staff can cast the following
spells:

- _[False life](ref/spell/false-life)_ (1 charge)
- _[Ray of enfeeblement](ref/spell/ray-of-enfeeblement)_ (1 charge)
- _[Scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray)_ (1 charge)
- _[Fireball](ref/spell/fireball)_ (2 charges)
- _[Animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead)_ (3 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead),
[false life](ref/spell/false-life), [fireball](ref/spell/fireball),
[ray of enfeeblement](ref/spell/ray-of-enfeeblement), [scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray);
**Cost** 27,133 gp
