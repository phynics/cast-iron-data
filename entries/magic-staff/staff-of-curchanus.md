**Sources:** Pathfinder \#122: Into the Shattered Continent pg 77\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 26,600 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This stout staff is made of wood and capped with steel. Elongated carvings of smiling beasts adorn
its length. The staff of Curchanus functions as a +1/+1 [quarterstaff](ref/eq-weapon/quarterstaff)
and allows the use of the following spells.

- _[Charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal)_ (1 charge)
- _[Longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider)_ (1 charge)
- _[Monkey fish](ref/spell/monkey-fish)_ (1 charge)
- _[Speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ (1 charge)
- _[Greater longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider-greater)_ (2 charges)

If the staff's wielder has the [Fleet](ref/feat/fleet) feat, castings of _greater longstrider,
longstrider,_ or _[monkey fish](ref/spell/monkey-fish)_ from the staff are automatically extended,
as the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) feat. If the wielder has the
[Animal Affinity](ref/feat/animal-affinity) feat, then _[charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal)_ or
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ spells cast from the staff are automatically
extended, as the Extend Spell feat. This does not increase the spells' level or casting time.
