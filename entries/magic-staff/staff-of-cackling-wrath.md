**Aura** moderate varies; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 56,700 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

The thorns embedded in this gnarled length of wood make it rather tricky for the unwary to grasp. It
allows use of the following spells:

- _[Blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness)_ (1 charge)
- _[Charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)_ (1 charge)
- _[Hold person](ref/spell/hold-person)_ (1 charge)
- _[Inflict moderate wounds](ref/spell/inflict-moderate-wounds)_ (1 charge)
- _[Baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph)_ (2 charges)
- _[Bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse)_ (2 charges)
- _[Vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch)_ (2 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff),
[baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph), [bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse),
[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness), [charm person](ref/spell/charm-person),
[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person), [inflict moderate wounds](ref/spell/inflict-moderate-wounds),
[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch); **Cost** 28,450 gp
