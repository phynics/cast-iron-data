**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This staff is made from high-quality wood and festooned with brass rods and keys, looking almost
like an instrument. The staff allows use of the following spells:

- _[Ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound)_ (1 charge)
- _[Piercing shriek](ref/spell/piercing-shriek)_ (1 charge)
- _[Sculpt sound](ref/spell/sculpt-sound)_ (2 charges)
- _[Wall of sound](ref/spell/wall-of-sound)_ (2 charges)

In addition, as a swift action, the wielder of the musical staff can cause it to transform into a
string or wind instrument. Any [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) skill checks made with the musical
staff gain a +5 competence bonus. The staff can still be used to cast spells while it is an
instrument, and it can revert back to a staff as a free action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[piercing shriek](ref/spell/piercing-shriek), [sculpt sound](ref/spell/sculpt-sound),
[wall of sound](ref/spell/wall-of-sound); **Cost** 16,000 gp
