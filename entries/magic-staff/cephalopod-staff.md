**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 217\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 22,800 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This dark mahogany staff is carved in the shape of a squid's tentacle and smells strongly of
seawater. When grasped, the staff animates, writhing momentarily until it regains its rigid, wooden
form. As a swift action, the wielder can transform the staff into a long, flexible weapon that
functions as a [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) [whip](ref/eq-weapon/whip). The staff
reverts to its normal form at the beginning of the wielder's next turn.

The staff allows the use of the following spells.

- [Summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2) ([octopus](ref/monster/octopus) or
  [squid](ref/monster/squid) only; 1 charge)
- _[Black tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles)_ (3 charges)
- _[Summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6)_
  ([giant octopus](ref/monster/octopus-giant-octopus) only; 5 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff), [black tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles),
[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6); **Cost** 11,400 gp
