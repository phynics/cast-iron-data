**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 196, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 493\
**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 82,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Often made from applewood and topped with a clear crystal, this staff allows use of the following
spells:

- _[Hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter)_ (1 charge)
- _[Sleep](ref/spell/sleep)_ (1 charge)
- _[Suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ (1 charge)
- _[Crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair)_ (2 charges)
- _[Mind fog](ref/spell/mind-fog)_ (2 charges)
- _[Mass suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion-mass)_ (3 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Staff](ref/feat/craft-staff),
[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair), [hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter),
[mass suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion-mass), [mind fog](ref/spell/mind-fog),
[sleep](ref/spell/sleep), [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion); **Cost** 41,000 gp
