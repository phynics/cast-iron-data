**Deities:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), [Torag](ref/faith/torag)

**Acid Strike (minor)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and enhance it with acidic potency.

**Ability:** For 1 minute, this weapon emits acrid fumes that deal an additional 1d4 points of acid
damage with each strike. This additional damage doesn't stack with the additional damage from the
[corrosive](ref/magic-enh/corrosive) weapon special ability.

**Armor of Earth (major)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Ability:** You can touch an ally and harden its armor or clothing. For 1 minute, the ally gains DR
1/-.

**At 12th level,** the ally's DR improves to 2/-.

**At 14th level,** the ally's DR increases to 3/-.

**At 16th level,** the ally's DR improves to 4/-.

**At 18th level,** the ally's DR increases to 5/-.

**Special:** This doesn't stack with any other damage resistance.
