**Deities:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), [Torag](ref/faith/torag)

**Acid Strike (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and enhance it with acidic potency.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, this weapon emits acrid fumes that deal an additional 1d4 points of
acid damage with each strike. This additional damage doesn't stack with the additional damage from
the [corrosive](ref/magic-enh/corrosive) weapon special ability.

**Armor of Earth (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can touch an ally and harden its armor or clothing. For 1 minute, the ally
gains DR 1/-.

**Special:** This doesn't stack with any other damage resistance.

At 12th level: The ally's DR improves to 2/-.

At 14th level: The ally's DR increases to 3/-.

At 16th level: The ally's DR improves to 4/-.

At 18th level: The ally's DR increases to 5/-.
