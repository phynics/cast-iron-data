**Deities:** [Cayden Cailean](ref/faith/cayden-cailean), [Desna](ref/faith/desna),
[Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae),
[Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn), [Torag](ref/faith/torag)

**Holy Strike (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and bless it with the power of purity and goodness.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, this weapon glows green, white, or yellow-gold and deals an
additional 1d6 points of damage against evil creatures. During this time, it's treated as good for
the purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). This additional damage
doesn't stack with the additional damage from the [holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) weapon special ability.

**Battle Companion (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can summon a battle companion. This ability functions as
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) with a duration of 1 minute, but for only a good
outsider or an animal with the [celestial](ref/template/celestial) creature simple template. This
ability can summon only one creature, regardless of the list used.

At 12th level: This now behaves as summon monster +5.

At 14th level: This now behaves as summon monster +6.

At 16th level: This now behaves as summon monster +7.

At 18th level: This now behaves as summon monster +8.

At 20th level: This now behaves as summon monster +9.
