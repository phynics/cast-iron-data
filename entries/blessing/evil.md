**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 65\
**Deities:** [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus), [Lamashtu](ref/faith/lamashtu),
[Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber), [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug), [Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa),
[Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon)

**Unholy Strike (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and give it an evil blessing.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, this weapon takes on a black, orange, or violet cast and deals an
additional 1d6 points of damage against good creatures. During this time, it's treated as evil for
the purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). This additional damage
doesn't stack with the additional damage from the [unholy](ref/magic-enh/unholy) weapon special
ability.

**Battle Companion (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can summon a battle companion. This ability functions as
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) with a duration of 1 minute, but for only an evil
outsider or an animal with the [fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) creature simple template. This
ability can summon only one creature, regardless of the list used.

At 12th level: This now behaves as summon monster +5.

At 14th level: This now behaves as summon monster +6.

At 16th level: This now behaves as summon monster +7.

At 18th level: This now behaves as summon monster +8.

At 20th level: This now behaves as summon monster +9.
