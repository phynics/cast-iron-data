**Deities:** [Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber), [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma),
[Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa), [Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon)

**From the Grave (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can take on a corpse-like visage for 1 minute, making you more intimidating and
giving you undead-like protection from harm.

**At 1st Level:** You gain a +4 bonus on Disguise checks to resemble an undead creature and on
Intimidate checks, as well as a +2 profane bonus on saving throws against disease, mind-affecting
effects, paralysis, poison, and stun.

**Death's Touch (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can make a melee touch attack against an opponent to deliver grim suffering.
If you succeed, you inflict 1 temporary [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels)
on the target for 1 minute. Alternatively, you can activate this ability as a **swift action** upon
hitting an opponent with a melee attack.

**Special:** These temporary negative levels stack. You gain no benefit from imposing these negative
levels (such as the temporary hit points undead gain from [enervation](ref/spell/enervation)).
