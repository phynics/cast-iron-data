**Deities:** [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh), [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug)

**Storm Strike (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and grant it a blessing of stormy weather.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, this weapon glows with blue or yellow sparks and deals an additional
1d4 points of electricity damage with each hit. This additional damage doesn't stack with the
additional damage from the [shock](ref/magic-enh/shock) or
[shocking burst](ref/magic-enh/shocking-burst) weapon special abilities.

**Wind Barrier (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can create a barrier of fast winds around yourself for 1 minute. This acts as
a [wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall) on all sides of your square, protects you with
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), and doesn't interfere with your ranged attacks.
