**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 97\
**Deities:** Evil deities that offer the [Air](ref/blessing/air) blessing or nonevil deities with
disasters in their portfolios

**Dust Devil (minor)**

**At 1st Level:** When you hit with a weapon attack, you can use a **swift action** to cause a
sudden burst of wind and debris to swirl around your target. The target is
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 minute.

**Howling Gale (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can invoke a howling windstorm around yourself as a **standard action**. For
1 minute, any Large or smaller creature takes twice as much movement to enter a space adjacent to
you due to the winds, and thus can't [take a 5-foot step](ref/rule/take-5-foot-step) to move
adjacent to you.
