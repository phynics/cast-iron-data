**Deities:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh)

**Animal Fury (minor)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Flavor:** You can touch one ally and grant it feral features.

**Ability:** The ally gains 2 claw attacks that each deal 1d6 points of damage if the ally is Medium
or 1d4 if it's Small, or 1 bite attack that deals 1d8 points of damage if the ally is Medium or 1d6
if it's Small. These are primary [natural attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that replace any similar
primary natural attacks the ally might have. This effect lasts for 1 minute.

**Battle Companion (major)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Flavor:** You can summon a battle companion.

**Ability:** This ability functions as [summon nature's ally V](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5)
with a duration of 1 minute. This ability can summon only one animal, regardless of the list used.

**At 12th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally VI](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-6).

**At 14th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally VII](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-7).

**At 16th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally VIII](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-8).

**At 18th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally IX](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-9).
