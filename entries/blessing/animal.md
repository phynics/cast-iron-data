**Deities:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh)

**Animal Fury (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one ally and grant it feral features.

**At 1st Level:** The ally gains 2 claw attacks that each deal 1d6 points of damage if the ally is
Medium or 1d4 if it's Small, or 1 bite attack that deals 1d8 points of damage if the ally is Medium
or 1d6 if it's Small. These are primary [natural attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that replace any
similar primary natural attacks the ally might have. This effect lasts for 1 minute.

**Battle Companion (major)**

**Flavor:** You can summon a battle companion.

**At 10th Level:** This ability functions as
[summon nature's ally V](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5) with a duration of 1 minute. This ability
can summon only one animal, regardless of the list used.

At 12th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +6.

At 14th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +7.

At 16th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +8.

At 18th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +9.
