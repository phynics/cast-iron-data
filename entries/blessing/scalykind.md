**Sources:** Advanced Class Origins pg 25\
**Deities:** [Apsu](ref/faith/apsu), [Dahak](ref/faith/dahak), [Ragadahn](ref/faith/ragadahn),
[Ydersius](ref/faith/ydersius)

**Scaly Touch (minor)**

**At 1st Level:** You can touch an ally as a **standard action** to grant it reptilian scales. For 1
minute, the ally gains an enhancement bonus to its natural armor equal to 1 +
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your warpriest level.

**Serpent Fang (major)**

**Action:** At 10th Level

As a **standard action** you can manifest venomous fangs for 1 minute. These fangs grant a
[bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals 1d4 points of damage (for a Medium warpriest, 1d3
if Small). A successful bite also afflicts the opponent with a debilitating venom.

**Serpent Venom**

**Type:** Poison (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your warpriest level + your Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d2

**Cure:** 1 save
