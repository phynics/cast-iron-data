**Deities:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh)

**Creeping Vines (minor)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Ability:** Upon hitting with a melee attack, as a **swift action** you cause the creature you hit
to sprout entangling vines that attempt to hold it in place, [entangling](ref/rule/entangled) it for
1 round (Reflex negates).

**Battle Companion (major)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Ability:** You can summon a battle companion. This ability functions as
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4), with a duration of 1 minute. This
ability can summon only one animal, regardless of the list used, and the creature's type changes to
[plant](ref/type/plant) instead of animal.

**At 12th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally V](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5).

**At 14th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally VI](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-6).

**At 16th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally VII](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-7).

**At 18th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally VIII](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-8).

**At 20th level,** this now behaves as [summon nature's ally IX](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-9).
