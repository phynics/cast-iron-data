**Deities:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh)

**Creeping Vines (minor)**

**At 1st Level:** Upon hitting with a melee attack, as a **swift action** you cause the creature you
hit to sprout entangling vines that attempt to hold it in place, [entangling](ref/rule/entangled) it
for 1 round (Reflex negates).

**Battle Companion (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can summon a battle companion. This ability functions as
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4), with a duration of 1 minute. This
ability can summon only one animal, regardless of the list used, and the creature's type changes to
[plant](ref/type/plant) instead of animal.

At 12th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +5.

At 14th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +6.

At 16th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +7.

At 18th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +8.

At 20th level: This now behaves as summon nature's ally +9.
