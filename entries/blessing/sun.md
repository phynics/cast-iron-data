**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 68\
**Deities:** [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae)

**Blinding Strike (minor)**

**At 1st Level:** You can create a flash of sunlight in the eyes of one of your opponents. The
target is [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round. If it succeeds at a Reflex saving throw, it's
instead [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 round. Creatures with
[light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness) or [light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity) take a
-4 penalty on this saving throw.

**Special:** This is a [light](ref/spelldef/light) effect. Sightless creatures are unaffected by
this ability.

**Cleansing Fire (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can touch a weapon and grant it either the [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
or undead-[bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon special ability for 1 minute. If you spend two uses of
your _blessing_ when activating this ability, the weapon can have both weapon special abilities.
