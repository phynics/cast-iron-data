**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 67\
**Deities:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), [Nethys](ref/faith/nethys), [Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn),
[Torag](ref/faith/torag)

**Increased Defense (minor)**

**At 1st Level:** You can gain a +1 sacred bonus on saving throws and a +1 sacred bonus to AC for 1
minute.

At 10th level: The bonus +2.

At 20th level: The bonus +3.

**Aura of Protection (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of protection for 1 minute. You and your allies
within this aura gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10 against acid, cold, electricity, fire, and
sonic.

**At 15th Level:** The energy resistance increases to 20.
