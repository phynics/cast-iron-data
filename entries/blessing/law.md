**Deities:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus),
[Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), [Irori](ref/faith/irori),
[Torag](ref/faith/torag), [Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon)

**Axiomatic Strike (minor)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and enhance it with the essence of law.

**Ability:** For 1 minute, this weapon glows blue, pale yellow, or white and deals an additional 1d6
points of damage against chaotic creatures. During this time, it's treated as lawful for the
purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). This additional damage doesn't
stack with the additional damage from the [axiomatic](ref/magic-enh/axiomatic) weapon special
ability.

**Battle Companion (major)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Ability:** You can summon a battle companion. This ability functions as
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) with a duration of 1 minute, but for only a lawful
outsider or an animal with the [resolute](ref/template/resolute) creature simple template. This
ability can summon only one creature, regardless of the list used.

**At 12th level,** this now behaves as [summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5).

**At 14th level,** this now behaves as [summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6).

**At 16th level,** this now behaves as [summon monster VII](ref/spell/summon-monster-7).

**At 18th level,** this now behaves as [summon monster VIII](ref/spell/summon-monster-8).

**At 20th level,** this now behaves as [summon monster IX](ref/spell/summon-monster-9).
