**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 66\
**Deities:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus),
[Erastil](ref/faith/erastil), [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), [Irori](ref/faith/irori),
[Torag](ref/faith/torag), [Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon)

**Axiomatic Strike (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and enhance it with the essence of law.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, this weapon glows blue, pale yellow, or white and deals an
additional 1d6 points of damage against chaotic creatures. During this time, it's treated as lawful
for the purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). This additional damage
doesn't stack with the additional damage from the [axiomatic](ref/magic-enh/axiomatic) weapon
special ability.

**Battle Companion (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can summon a battle companion. This ability functions as
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) with a duration of 1 minute, but for only a lawful
outsider or an animal with the [resolute](ref/template/resolute) creature simple template. This
ability can summon only one creature, regardless of the list used.

At 12th level: This now behaves as summon monster +5.

At 14th level: This now behaves as summon monster +6.

At 16th level: This now behaves as summon monster +7.

At 18th level: This now behaves as summon monster +8.

At 20th level: This now behaves as summon monster +9.
