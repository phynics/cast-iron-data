**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 63\
**Deities:** [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh), [Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn)

**Zephyr's Gift (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch any one ranged weapon and enhance it with the quality of air.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, any attacks made with the weapon take no penalties due to range. In
addition, making a ranged attack with this weapon doesn't provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Soaring Assault (major)**

**Flavor:** You can touch an ally and give her the gift of flight for 1 minute (as
[fly](ref/spell/fly)).

**At 10th Level:** The ally gains a fly speed of 60 feet with average maneuverability. She gains a
bonus on Fly skill checks equal to your level. Whenever the ally succeeds at a
[charge](ref/rule/charge) attack while flying, that attack deals an amount of additional electricity
damage equal to your level.
