**Deities:** [Irori](ref/faith/irori), [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma),
[Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae)

**Powerful Healer (minor)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Flavor:** You can add power to a cure spell as you cast it.

**Ability:** As a **swift action**, you can treat any _cure_ spell as if it were empowered (as the
[Empower Spell](ref/feat/empower-spell) feat), causing it to heal 50% more damage (or deal 50% more
damage if used against undead). This ability doesn't stack with itself or the Empower Spell feat.

**Fast Healing (major)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Ability:** You can touch an ally and grant it [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 3 for 1 minute.

### Restoration

**Replacement Blessing:** The following minor blessing replaces the _powerful healer_ ability of the
Healing blessing.

**Ameliorating Touch (minor)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Flavor:** Your touch is a balm to your suffering allies.

**Ability:** You can touch an ally as a **swift action** to temporarily relieve that ally of the
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued), [shaken](ref/misc/shaken), or [sickened](ref/misc/sickened)
condition. This blessing suppresses those conditions for 1 minute.

**At 2nd level,** you can spend two uses of your blessing ability whenever you heal yourself or an
ally with your _fervor_ class ability to use this minor blessing as a **free action** instead of a
swift action.

### Resurrection

**Replacement Blessing:** The following major blessing replaces the
_[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing)_ ability of the Healing blessing.

**Return of the Fallen (major)**

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Flavor:** Your touch can defy the cold claws of death itself.

**Ability:** You can temporarily revive a touched ally to continue fighting, as if you had cast
[raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) on her. The revived ally is also [staggered](ref/misc/staggered)
for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your warpriest level, after which she immediately dies. The ally
is free to act as she sees fit during this time, and you are granted no control over her.
