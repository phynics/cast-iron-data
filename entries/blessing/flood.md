**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 97\
**Deities:** Evil deities that offer the [Water](ref/blessing/water) blessing or nonevil deities
with disasters in their portfolios

**Lasting Breath (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch an ally to bless it with an extra reserve of breath.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, while the ally [holds its breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath),
standard and full-round actions don't decrease the number of rounds the ally can hold its breath.

**Tsunami Burst (major)**

**At 10th Level:** When you are hit by a melee attack, you can invoke this blessing as an
**immediate action**. A wave of water blasts out from you at your attacker, attempting a
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) combat maneuver check using your CMB but adding your Wisdom modifier
instead of your Strength modifier.
