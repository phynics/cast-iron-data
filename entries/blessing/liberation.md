**Deities:** [Desna](ref/faith/desna)

**Liberation (minor)**

**At 1st Level:** For 1 round as a **swift action**, you can ignore impediments to your mobility and
effects that cause [paralysis](ref/rule/paralysis) (as
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement)). You can activate this blessing even if you're
otherwise unable to take actions, but not if you're unconscious.

**Freedom's Shout (major)**

**At 10th Level:** As a **swift action** you can emit a 30-foot aura that affects all allies with
the _liberation_ blessing described above. This effect lasts for 1 round.

### Freedom

**Sources:** Healer's Handbook pg 13

**Replacement Blessing:** The following major blessing replaces the _freedom's shout_ ability of the
Liberation blessing.

**Greater Liberation (major)**

**At 10th Level:** When you use the _liberation_ ability of the Liberation blessing, its effects
last for 1 minute.
