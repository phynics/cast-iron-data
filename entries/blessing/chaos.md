**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 64\
**Deities:** [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria), [Cayden Cailean](ref/faith/cayden-cailean),
[Desna](ref/faith/desna), [Gorum](ref/faith/gorum), [Lamashtu](ref/faith/lamashtu),
[Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug)

**Anarchic Strike (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and grant it a chaotic blessing.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, the touched weapon glows yellow or purple and deals an additional
1d6 points of damage against lawful creatures. During this time, it's treated as chaotic for the
purposes of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Special:** This blessing's additional damage doesn't stack with the additional damage from the
[anarchic](ref/magic-enh/anarchic) weapon special ability.

**Battle Companion (major)**

**Flavor:** You can summon a battle companion.

**At 10th Level:** This ability functions as [summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) with a
duration of 1 minute, but for only a chaotic outsider or an animal with the
[entropic](ref/template/entropic) creature simple template. This ability can summon only one
creature, regardless of the list used.

At 12th level: This now behaves as summon monster +5.

At 14th level: This now behaves as summon monster +6.

At 16th level: This now behaves as summon monster +7.

At 18th level: This now behaves as summon monster +8.

At 20th level: This now behaves as summon monster +9.
