**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 97\
**Deities:** Evil deities that offer the [Earth](ref/blessing/earth) blessing or nonevil deities
with disasters in their portfolios

**Stone's Base (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch an ally to bless it with the stalwart resilience of stone.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, the creature gains a +4 bonus to its CMD against maneuvers that
would move the ally or knock it prone.

**Earth Hammer (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can touch a weapon to bless it with the force of a mighty earthquake for 1
minute. When the weapon hits a creature, the target must succeed at a Reflex save or fall
[prone](ref/rule/prone); either way, the creature is immune to this ability for 24 hours.
