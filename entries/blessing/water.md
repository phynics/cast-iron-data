**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 68\
**Deities:** [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh), [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma)

**Ice Strike (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch one weapon and enhance it with the power of water.

**At 1st Level:** For 1 minute, this weapon glows with a blue-white chill and deals an additional
1d4 points of cold damage with each strike. This additional damage doesn't stack with the additional
damage from the [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) or [icy burst](ref/magic-enh/icy-burst) weapon special
abilities.

**Armor of Ice (major)**

**At 10th Level:** You can touch any one ally and wreath it in freezing mist. This works as
[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield) (chill shield only) with a duration 1 minute.
