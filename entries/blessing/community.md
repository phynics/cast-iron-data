**Deities:** [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil)

**Communal Aid (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch an ally and grant it the blessing of community.

**At 1st Level:** For the next minute, whenever that ally uses the
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action, the bonus granted increases to +4. You can instead use
this ability on yourself as a swift action.

**Fight as One (major)**

**Flavor:** You can rally your allies to fight together.

**At 10th Level:** For 1 minute, whenever you make a successful melee or ranged attack against a
foe, allies within 10 feet of you gain a +2 insight bonus on attacks of the same type you made
against that foe - melee attacks if you made a melee attack, or ranged attacks if you made a ranged
attack.

**Special:** If you score a critical hit, this bonus increases to +4 until the start of your next
turn.

### Cooperation

**Sources:** Healer's Handbook pg 13

**Replacement Blessing:** The following minor blessing replaces the _communal aid_ ability of the
Community blessing.

**Team Effort (minor)**

**Flavor:** You can touch an ally and grant it the blessing of cooperation.

**At 1st Level:** Choose one teamwork feat that you have. The touched ally gains the benefit of the
chosen teamwork feat for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your warpriest level (minimum 1).
Alternatively, you can choose one teamwork feat that a touched ally has and gain the benefit of the
chosen feat for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 your warpriest level (minimum 1).
