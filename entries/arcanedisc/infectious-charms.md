**Infectious Charms**

**Ability:** Anytime you target and successfully affect a single creature with a charm or compulsion
spell and that creature is within 30 feet of another opponent, your spell has a chance of affecting
the second creature as well. As a **swift action** immediately after affecting a creature with a
charm or compulsion spell, you can cause the spell to carry over to the nearest creature within 30
feet. The spell behaves in all ways as though its new target were the original target of the spell.

**Flavor:** Your charms are so smooth that they're contagious.

**Prereq:** 11th-level wizard
