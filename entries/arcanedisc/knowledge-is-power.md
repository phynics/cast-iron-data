**Knowledge Is Power (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Your understanding of physical forces gives you power over them.

**Passive:** You add your Intelligence modifier on combat maneuver checks and to your CMD. You also
add your Intelligence modifier on Strength checks to break or lift objects.
