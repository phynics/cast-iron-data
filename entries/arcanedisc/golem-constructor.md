**Golem Constructor**

**Ability:** You have learned the art and craft of creating a single type of
[golem](ref/family/golem) (such as [stone golems](ref/monster/golem-stone-golem) or
[iron golems](ref/monster/golem-iron-golem)). When creating a golem of this type, you count as
having the [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Craft\_Magic\_Arms\_and\_Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), and
[Craft Construct](ref/feat/craft-construct) feats. You must meet all other construction requirements
for the golem as normal.

**Prereq:** 9th-level wizard

**Special:** You may select this discovery multiple times. Each time you select this discovery, it
applies to a different kind of golem.
