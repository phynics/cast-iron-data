You have learned the art and craft of creating a single type of [golem](ref/family/golem) (such as
[stone golems](ref/monster/golem-stone-golem) or [iron golems](ref/monster/golem-iron-golem)). When
creating a golem of this type, you count as having the
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), and
[Craft Construct](ref/feat/craft-construct) feats. You must meet all other construction requirements
for the golem as normal. You may select this discovery multiple times. Each time you select this
discovery, it applies to a different kind of golem. You must be at least a 9th-level wizard to
select this discovery.
