**Idealize (Su)**

**Flavor:** In your quest for self-perfection, you have discovered a way to further enhance yourself
and others.

**At 20th Level:** The bonus increases by 4.

**Passive:** When a transmutation spell you cast grants an enhancement bonus to an ability score,
that bonus increases by 2.

**Prereq:** 10th-level wizard
