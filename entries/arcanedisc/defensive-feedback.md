**Defensive Feedback (Su)**

**Flavor:** Rather than dissipate damaging energy, you can redirect some of it back to its source.

**Passive:** When an abjuration spell you cast prevents damage (with
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) or energy resistance), if the attacking creature is
within 30 feet of the protected creature, the foe takes 1d6 points of damage for every 10 points of
damage prevented.
