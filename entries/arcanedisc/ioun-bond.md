**Ioun Bond**

**Ability:** You can form an arcane bond with an [ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone). If you
choose this arcane discovery at 1st level, you gain a [dull gray](ref/magic-ioun/dull-gray-stone)
ioun stone as a bonded object at no cost. A bonded ioun stone must be orbiting your head to have
effect.

**At 12th Level:** You can turn a bonded dull gray ioun stone into another kind of ioun stone as if
you possessed the [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item) feat; if you die or replace a
bonded ioun stone that has been transformed in this way, the stone reverts to a dull gray ioun
stone.

**Prereq:** Has a bonded object from the _arcane bond_ class ability
