**Creative Destruction (Su)**

**Flavor:** You have learned how to use destructive energy to empower yourself.

**Passive:** When you cast an evocation spell that deals damage, you gain a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to the total number of dice used to
determine the damage caused by the spell. Temporary hit points gained from this discovery do not
stack and disappear after 1 hour.
