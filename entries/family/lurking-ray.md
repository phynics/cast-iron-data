Lurking rays are a whole family of creatures that infest the dark corners of the world, feeding on
the weak and unwary. The infant form, known as the executioner's hood, is an unnerving but
relatively mild threat for experienced adventurers, while its parents, the male lurker above and
female trapper, are far more dangerous. All three types of lurking rays are ambush hunters with
similar tactics, wrapping themselves thoroughly around their victims and choking the life from their
prey before feeding.

Though somewhat similar in appearance to stingrays or manta rays, lurking rays in fact represent
their own unique branch of subterranean evolution - or arcane experimentation. Executioner's hoods
are roughly a foot long and weigh 5 pounds, whereas adult lurkers above and trappers can grow to be
15 feet wide, with males weighing 500 pounds and females up to four times that.
