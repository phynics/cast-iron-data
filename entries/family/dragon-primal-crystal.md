### Special Abilities

**Razor Sharp (Sp)^[1](ref/family/dragon-primal-crystal#footnote-1)^** All of a crystal dragon's
natural attacks deal slashing damage.

**Ray Reflection (Ex)** An ancient crystal dragon's scales reflect ray spells back upon the ray's
source if the ray fails to overcome the dragon's [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance).

**Scintillating Aura (Su)** A great wyrm crystal dragon radiates an aura of scintillating color from
its jeweled scales to a radius of 60 feet. All within this area must make a Will save each round to
avoid being [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) (if the victim has 15 or fewer Hit Dice) or
[confused](ref/misc/confused) (if the victim has more than 15 Hit Dice) for 1 round. The save DC is
equal to the dragon's breath weapon save DC. This is a mind-affecting effect. The dragon can
activate or suppress this aura as a free action.

**Spell-Like Abilities (Sp)** A crystal dragon gains the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), usable at will (unless indicated otherwise)
upon reaching the listed age category. Very young-[color spray](ref/spell/color-spray);
Juvenile-[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust); Adult-[rainbow pattern](ref/spell/rainbow-pattern);
Old-[stone to flesh](ref/spell/stone-to-flesh) (3/day);
Ancient-[prismatic spray](ref/spell/prismatic-spray) (3/day); Great
wyrm-[imprisonment](ref/spell/imprisonment) (1/day).

**Tremorsense (Ex)** A juvenile^[2](ref/family/dragon-primal-crystal#footnote-2)^ crystal dragon
gains [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) to a range of 30 ft. (increases to 60 ft. at adult, and to
120 ft. at old).
