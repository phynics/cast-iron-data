**Blight Coils (Su)** In place of dealing damage with its constrict ability, the dragon can expose
the target to its virulence ability or cause the target to take damage from one disease it has as if
a day had passed.

**Constrictor (Ex)** An apocalypse dragon has no wing attacks, but its bite gains the
[grab](ref/umr/grab) ability and it gains the [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) ability, dealing damage
equal to that of its bite attack.

**Soul Drinker (Su)** The dragon gains the benefits of [greater heroism](ref/spell/heroism-greater)
for 1 round per Hit Die of the slain creature when a creature it has grappled or pinned dies.

**Soul Reaver (Su)** A spellcaster using [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) or similar magic on a
creature killed while grappled or pinned by the dragon must succeed at a DC 30 caster level check or
the spell fails and the material component is wasted.

**Vile Fortitude (Ex)** An apocalypse dragon is immune to cold, death effects, disease, and poison.

**Virulence (Su)** Any creature damaged by the dragon's breath weapon must succeed at a Fortitude
save at the same DC or contract [slimy doom](ref/disease/slimy-doom), with no onset time. An adult
dragon's disease deals 1d6 points of [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain); this
increases to 1d8 and 3 consecutive successful saves for an ancient dragon.
