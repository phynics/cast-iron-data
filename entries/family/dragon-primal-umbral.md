### Special Abilities

**Arcane** An umbral dragon can cast cleric spells as arcane spells.

**Breath Weapon (Su)** Although it deals negative energy damage, an umbral dragon's breath weapon
does not heal undead creatures.

**Create Shadows (Su)** Any creature slain by an ancient or older umbral dragon rises as a
[shadow](ref/monster/shadow) (if 8 HD or less) or
[greater shadow](ref/monster/shadow-greater-shadow) (if above 8 HD) under the umbral dragon's
control 1d4 rounds later.

**Energy Drain (Su)** A great wyrm umbral dragon deals 1
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) with each successful bite or claw attack
(1 level, DC 32).

**Ghost Bane (Su)** A young or older umbral dragon's physical attacks deal damage to incorporeal
creatures normally.

**Umbral Scion (Ex)** Umbral dragons have
[negative energy affinity](ref/umr/negative-energy-affinity) and are immune to energy drain and
death effects.

**Shadow Breath (Su)** Three times per day, an adult or older umbral dragon can breathe a cone of
shadows. Creatures who fail a Fortitude save are [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4 rounds and take
1 point of [Str drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per age category possessed by the dragon. A
successful save negates the blindness and reduces Str drain to 1d4 points.

**Spell-Like Abilities (Sp)** An umbral dragon gains the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), usable at will (unless indicated otherwise) on
reaching the listed age category. Young-[darkness](ref/spell/darkness);
Juvenile-[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch); Adult-[shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk);
Old-[project image](ref/spell/project-image); Ancient-[finger of death](ref/spell/finger-of-death)
(3/day), Great wyrm-[shades](ref/spell/shades).
