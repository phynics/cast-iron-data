### Special Abilities

**Desert Wind (Su)** A juvenile or older brass dragon can call up the desert wind to serve him. This
functions as [gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind), but any creature in its path must make a
Fortitude save or be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4 rounds by the sand. The save DC for this
effect is equal to the dragon's breath weapon DC.

**Fire Aura (Su)** An old or older brass dragon is surrounded by an aura of intense heat. All
creatures within 5 feet of the dragon take 1d6 points of fire damage at the beginning of the
dragon's turn. An ancient brass dragon's aura extends to 10 feet. A great wyrm's damage increases to
2d6. A brass dragon can suppress or activate this aura at will as a free action.

**Move Sand (Sp)** A young or older brass dragon can move sand to excavate ruins or hide treasures.
This functions as [move earth](ref/spell/move-earth), but it only affects sand. The dragon uses his
HD in place of his caster level for this effect. This is equivalent to a 5th-level spell.

**Sandstorm (Su)** An ancient or older brass dragon can create a powerful sandstorm once per day as
a full-round action. This storm has a radius of 1 mile and lasts for 1 minute per age category of
the dragon. This functions as a [sandstorm](ref/rule/sandstorms), except that it is also accompanied
by [windstorm](ref/rule/winds)-level winds.

**Sleep Breath (Su)** Instead of a line of fire, a brass dragon can breathe a cone of sleep gas.
Creatures within the cone must succeed on a Will save or fall asleep for 1d6 rounds plus 1 round per
age category of the dragon.

**Spell-Like Abilities (Sp)** A brass dragon gains the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), usable at will upon reaching the listed age
category. Very young-[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals);
Juvenile-[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements); Adult-[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion);
Old-[control winds](ref/spell/control-winds); Ancient-[control weather](ref/spell/control-weather);
Great wyrm-[whirlwind](ref/spell/whirlwind).

**Summon Djinni (Sp)** This ability, usable by a great wyrm brass dragon once per day, works like a
[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell, except that it summons one
[noble djinni](ref/monster/genie-djinni). This ability is the equivalent of a 9th-level spell.
