**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road

[Mortics](ref/subtype/mortic) are humanoids afflicted with the physiology and appetites of undead.
All mortics are pulled between two worlds: as living humanoids, they crave community and friendship,
but their necromantic nature forces them to indulge in inhuman hungers and commit violence against
the living. As a result, most mortics lurk at the edge of civilization or isolate themselves with
others of their own kind. Although living creatures, mortics can suspend their living processes
temporarily, becoming more like undead.
