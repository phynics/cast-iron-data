Despite their best intentions, the appropriately named havoc dragons often cause collateral damage
as they develop whimsical wonderlands of revelry and relaxation.

**Family:** [Planar Dragon](ref/family/dragon-planar)

**Change Shape (Su)** A havoc dragon can assume any humanoid or animal form as per
[polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph).

**Deadly Tail (Ex)** The dragon's tail slap critical multiplier becomes ×3.

**Delerium Breath (Su)** A creature that fails its save against the dragon's breath weapon becomes
[confused](ref/misc/confused) for 1 round.

**Lashing Tail (Ex)** A havoc dragon's tail slap attack has reach equal to its bite attack and gains
the [trip](ref/rule/trip) special attack.

**Performer (Su)** A havoc dragon treats all [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) skills as class skills
and can choose spells from the cleric and bard spell lists.

**Planar Artist (Su)** Three times per day as a standard action, a havoc dragon can reshape features
within the area of its planar infusion as if casting [create demiplane](ref/spell/create-demiplane)
(except the shape function). All additional features disappear when the planar infusion ends.

**Rapturous Revel (Su)** Once per day as a standard action, a havoc dragon can inspire creativity
and joy in each creature in a 100-foot radius for 1 minute. For each affected creature, the dragon
chooses to affect the target with [charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster),
[confusion](ref/spell/confusion), or [greater heroism](ref/spell/heroism-greater) (Will DC 32
negates). These are mind-affecting effects. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Soundproof (Ex)** A havoc dragon is immune to sonic damage as well as to the confused, nauseated,
and sickened conditions.
