**Sources:** Pathfinder \#135: Runeplague

Each siktempora is different in shape, alignment, and power, but they all share a few similar
traits.

- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to disease, mind-affecting effects, and poison, plus one additional
  condition thematic to the siktempora's emotional genesis
- [Fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing)
- [Evasion](ref/ability/evasion)
- _Temporal jaunt_ [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
- An emotion-based effect linked to their physical attacks
- _Temporal sense_ ability
