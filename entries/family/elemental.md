An elemental is a being composed entirely from one of the four classical elements:
[air](ref/monster/air-elemental), [earth](ref/monster/earth-elemental),
[fire](ref/monster/fire-elemental), or [water](ref/monster/water-elemental). An elemental has the
following features.

- [Immunity](ref/umr/immunity) to bleed, paralysis, poison, sleep effects, and stunning.
- Not subject to critical hits or [flanking](ref/rule/flanking). Does not take additional damage
  from precision-based attacks, such as [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack).
- Proficient with natural weapons only, unless generally humanoid in form, in which case proficient
  with all simple weapons and any weapons mentioned in its entry.
- Proficient with whatever type of armor (light, medium, or heavy) it is described as wearing, as
  well as all lighter types. Elementals not indicated as wearing armor are not proficient with
  armor. Elementals are proficient with shields if they are proficient with any form of armor.
- Elementals do not breathe, eat, or sleep.

**Sources:** Bestiary 2, Bestiary 5\
Other elementals also exist: [aether](ref/monster/aether-elemental),
[ice](ref/monster/ice-elemental), [lightning](ref/monster/lightning-elemental),
[magma](ref/monster/magma-elemental), and [mud](ref/monster/mud-elemental).
