**Clam Clamp**

**Type:** Mechanical

**Challenge rating:** 2

**Terrain:** any oceans

**Perception DC:** 20

**Disable Device DC:** 17

**Trigger:** location

**Effect:** The classic _clam clamp_ trap utilizes the heavy shell of a giant clam, but any
similarly large bivalve mollusk or even flotsam can work. A clam clamp is typically set with a lure,
such as a piece of shiny treasure. When a creature walks over a clam clamp or attempts to take the
treasure, the clamp snaps shut and attempts a grapple with a +15 bonus on the combat maneuver check.
The weight of the clam clamp is enough to hold a creature in place until the grappled creature can
escape. A clam clamp located in an underwater area is more difficult to craft but also more
dangerous to air-breathing creatures due to the fact that it can hold a creature underwater until it
drowns; an underwater clam clamp's CR is 5.

**Reset:** automatic
