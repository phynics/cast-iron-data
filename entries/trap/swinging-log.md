**Swinging Log**

**Type:** Mechanical

**Challenge rating:** 1

**Terrain:** any forests or jungles

**Perception DC:** 12

**Disable Device DC:** 12

**Trigger:** location

**Effect:** A large, heavy log is suspended in the canopy of a forest or jungle and attached to a
trip line. When a creature passes through a 5-foot-square that's been trapped by this trip line, the
log swings down and makes a melee attack with a +5 bonus (1d6+5 points of bludgeoning damage). A
creature damaged by this log must succeed at a DC 15 Reflex save to avoid being knocked prone.
Affixing a number of sharpened sticks to the log adds an additional 2d6 points of piercing damage
and increases its CR by 1.

**Reset:** manual
