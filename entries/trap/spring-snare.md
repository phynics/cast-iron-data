**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 172

**Spring Snare**

**Type:** Mechanical

**Challenge rating:** 1

**Terrain:** any forests, jungles, or swamps

**Perception DC:** 25

**Disable Device DC:** 15

**Trigger:** location

**Effect:** A spring snare consists of a strong sapling that's been bent down with a noose affixed
to the end. When a creature enters a square trapped by a spring snare, the noose attempts to snare
the triggering creature with a +15 on its combat maneuver check to grapple. If it succeeds, the tree
snaps upright and flings the target into the air for 1d6 damage. The creature then remains suspended
10 feet off the ground until it breaks the grapple.

**Reset:** manual
