**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 172

**Breakaway Vine**

**Type:** Mechanical

**Challenge rating:** 1

**Terrain:** any forests, jungles, or swamps

**Perception DC:** 20

**Disable Device DC:** 20

**Trigger:** touch (timed)

**Effect:** A breakaway vine has been sabotaged so that it snaps not long after someone begins using
it to climb. A breakaway vine trap can be crafted from a length of rope as well, but this ruins the
rope for other uses. When a Small or larger creature climbs a breakaway vine, the vine snaps just
before the creature reaches the top of the climb. A creature can grab a handhold and avoid the
resulting fall with a successful DC 20 Reflex save. This breakaway vine assumes a 20-foot fall for
2d6 points of damage; longer falls increase the CR of the breakaway vine by 1 per additional 20
feet.

**Reset:** repair
