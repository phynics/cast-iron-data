**Aura** moderate abjuration and necromancy \[evil\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 500 lbs.

This altar is a large sculpted table or slab for ceremonial feasts. Praying at the altar grants a +2
circumstance bonus on saving throws against death effects, disease,
[energy drain](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels), and negative energy effects; an undead
worshiper instead gains 5 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward), [false life](ref/spell/false-life),
[magic circle against good](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-good), creator must worship
[Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa); **Cost** 4,000 gp
