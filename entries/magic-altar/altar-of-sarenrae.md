**Aura** moderate abjuration and conjuration \[good, healing\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 750 lbs.

This altar consists of a large, 6-foot-tall golden statue of Sarenrae as she is represented in her
holy symbol. The altar is typically illuminated by a sacred brazier lit beneath the towering statue,
causing it to cast a looming, solemn shadow on the ceiling overhead. Praying at the altar grants a
+1 bonus on Fortitude saves and increases the benefits of any healing effects that target the
worshiper by +1 caster level.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds),
[magic circle against evil](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-evil),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance), creator must worship [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae); **Cost**
4,000 gp
