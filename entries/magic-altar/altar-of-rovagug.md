**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 249\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and transmutation \[chaotic, evil\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 150 lbs.

This altar is usually built around the lip of a pit that resembles a linked ring of claws and fangs
made from copper. Praying at the altar grants a +1 bonus on damage rolls and a +2 bonus on attack
rolls to confirm critical threats.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge), [magic circle against good](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-good),
[magic circle against law](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-law), creator must worship
[Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug); **Cost** 5,000 gp
