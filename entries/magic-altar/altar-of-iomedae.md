**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 248\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and transmutation \[lawful, good\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1,000 lbs.

This altar is a marble slab engraved with images of the Acts of Iomedae, with a
[longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword) bearing a [continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame) spell
mounted atop it. Praying at the altar grants a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls and saves against
fear effects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [bless](ref/spell/bless),
[magic circle against chaos](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-chaos),
[magic circle against evil](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-evil), creator must worship
[Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae); **Cost** 4,000 gp
