**Aura** moderate abjuration \[evil, lawful\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1,000 lbs.

This huge brazier burns with a raging fire. Any creature (not just a worshiper of Zursvaater) can
pray at the altar, though accepting the altar's blessing is both an evil and lawful act. Praying at
the altar grants a +2 circumstance bonus on Craft checks and cold resistance 20 for 24 hours. The
cold resistance is suppressed if the target of the effect leaves the temple grounds, but resumes if
the target returns.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance), [magic circle against chaos](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-chaos),
[magic circle against good](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-good),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), creator must worship [Zursvaater](ref/faith/zursvaater);
**Cost** 5,000 gp
