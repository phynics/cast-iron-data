**Aura** moderate abjuration and transmutation \[good, lawful\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 500 lbs.

This altar is a large anvil covered in intricate carvings of Torag's works. Praying at the altar
grants a +1 enhancement bonus to a suit of armor, shield, or piece of clothing (as
_[magic vestment](ref/spell/magic-vestment)_) and a +2 circumstance bonus on Craft checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance), [magic circle against chaos](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-chaos),
[magic circle against evil](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-evil),
[magic vestment](ref/spell/magic-vestment), creator must worship [Torag](ref/faith/torag); **Cost**
5,000 gp
