While any hydrokineticist or telekineticist can learn the rudiments of healing, some kineticists are
virtuosos of the curative arts.

**Kinetic Chirurgery (Su)**

**Ability:** A kinetic chirurgeon must select either aether or water as her primary
[element](ref/ability/elements). She gains [kinetic healer](ref/kinetic/kinetic-healer) as a bonus
wild talent at 1st level.

**At 3rd Level:** The kinetic chirurgeon can select any one [paladin](ref/class/paladin) mercy that
a paladin of that level could select. Each time she uses _kinetic healer,_ she can apply this mercy
to the target of the healing.

**At 5th Level:** She can apply only one of her chosen mercies when she uses _kinetic healer._

**Replaces:** Infusions

**Special:** A kinetic chirurgeon can never use infusions.

At 5th level: She can select a 3rd mercy (of an appropriate level).

At 7th level: She can pick a 4th mercy (of an appropriate level).

At 9th level: She can choose a 5th mercy (of an appropriate level).

At 11th level: She can select a 6th mercy (of an appropriate level).

At 13th level: She can pick a 7th mercy (of an appropriate level).

At 15th level: She can choose a 8th mercy (of an appropriate level).

At 17th level: She can select a 9th mercy (of an appropriate level).

At 19th level: She can pick a 10th mercy (of an appropriate level).

**Metahealer (Su)**

**Ability:** A kinetic chirurgeon can apply any one of the following _metahealer_ abilities to her
_kinetic healer_ wild talent instead of using her mercies.

**At 11th Level:** She can choose to roll two additional dice when using _kinetic healer._

**At 13th Level:** A kinetic chirurgeon can choose to use _kinetic healer_ on herself as a **swift
action**.

**At 17th Level:** She can choose to roll three additional dice when using _kinetic healer._ Or, she
can choose to heal both herself and another target with the same use of _kinetic healer,_ although
in that case, both she and her target must each accept 1 point of burn, instead of only one of them
needing to do so as normal for _kinetic healer._

**At 5th Level:** She can choose to roll one additional die when using _kinetic healer._

**At 9th Level:** She can choose to bring an ally who died within 1 round back from the dead; this
functions similarly to the [breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life) spell. The ally regains the
normal number of hit points from _kinetic healer._ An ally revived in this way takes 1 additional
point of burn, beyond the normal 1 point of burn that either the kinetic chirurgeon or the ally must
accept as a cost of the _kinetic healer_ wild talent.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Metakinesis, infusion specialization

**Special:** Regardless of her level, the kinetic chirurgeon can apply only one _metahealer_ ability
to a single use of _kinetic healer._ For instance, she could not bring back a dead ally and also
roll extra dice for healing.

**Healing Buffer (Su)**

**Ability:** A kinetic chirurgeon's _internal buffer_ has double the usual maximum size, and she can
use it only when she would accept points of burn for the _kinetic healer_ wild talent.

**Alters:** Internal buffer

**Gained:** At 6th Level
