### Granted Powers

Your divine guidance twists agreements, laws, and policies to suit the interests of the faith.

**Labyrinthine Words (Ex)**

**Passive:** You add your Wisdom modifier in addition to your Charisma modifier on
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks to lie and [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to influence
other creatures.

**Heart's Desire (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can use [commune](ref/spell/commune) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) using your inquisitor level as your caster level,
but only to ask questions about others' goals.

**Flavor:** You receive divine guidance on the goals of your political allies and rivals.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
