### Granted Powers

Your deity guides you when you deliver death to your chosen targets.

**Chosen Victim (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can designate a creature as your _chosen victim._ You gain a +2 bonus
on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks to gather information about your target, on
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks against your target, and on
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to track your target. The benefits of your _destruction,
piercing, purity,_ and _smiting judgments_ against your _chosen victim_ increase as if your
inquisitor level were 3 higher.

**Executioner's Strike (Su)**

**Flavor:** Your precision in combat has become incredible, and you can strike a lethal blow if your
target cannot defend itself properly.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** Once per day, you can make an attack against your _chosen victim._ If the attack hits
and the victim is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against you, any critical threats are
automatically confirmed.
