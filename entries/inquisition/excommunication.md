**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 23

**Associated Deities**

**Details:** Any

Marked by your deity for your impressive will, you are among the few with the wherewithal to carry
out the necessary work of removing dangerous influences from your church.

### Granted Powers

You can repulse the influence of confused divine servants.

**Touch of Silence (Su)**

**Ability:** With a melee touch attack, you render a target silent (as the spell
[silence](ref/spell/silence), but only affecting the target) for 1 round.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Excommunicate (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** Once per day, you can silence a creature within 30 feet. A Will save against DC = 10 +
1/2 your inquisitor level + your Wisdom modifier negates the effect. A creature that fails its save
gets a second save 1 round later. If it fails the second save, the target cannot speak, provide
verbal components for spells, make use of language-dependent effects, write, pantomime, or take any
other action meant to communicate anything more complex than a simple affirmative or negative for 1
day.
