**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 52

**Associated Deities**

**Details:** Any (with GM approval)

**Granted Powers**

**Ability:** You gain [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency) (firearms) and
[Gunsmithing](ref/feat/gunsmithing) as bonus feats.

When you hit an arcane spellcaster or a creature that uses
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a firearm attack, that creature must
succeed at a Fortitude saving throw. If the spellcaster fails, he takes a -4 penalty on
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks for 1 round.
