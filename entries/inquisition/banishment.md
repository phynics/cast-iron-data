### Granted Powers

Your devotion to your deity is matched only by your hatred for fiends. You know the rites to cast
evil outsiders back to the realms from which they hail.

**Righteous Awe (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you make an unarmed touch attack against an evil creature, it must succeed at
a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your inquisitor level + your Wisdom modifier) or become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d4 rounds. If the creature touched is an evil outsider, it becomes
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) instead of shaken.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Dismissive Touch (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can cast [dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal) once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (caster level equal to your inquisitor level)
upon making a successful unarmed touch attack against an evil outsider. The DC to save against this
ability increases by 2.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
