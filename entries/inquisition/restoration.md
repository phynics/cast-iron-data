[Shelyn](ref/faith/shelyn)

### Granted Powers

You are a healer, not just of people but also of sacred sites tainted by enemies of your faith.

**Mend the Broken (Su)**

**Standard:** You can touch an object or creature healing it of an amount of damage equal to 1d4 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your inquisitor level. You can use this ability only on a
broken object or a creature with fewer than half its maximum hit points.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Cleanse Impunity (Su)**

**Ability:** Add your Wisdom bonus on caster level checks to overcome the
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) of creatures with the chosen alignment subtype; dispel
effects with the chosen alignment descriptor; and remove curses, diseases, and poisons.

**Choice:** Choose an alignment (chaotic, evil, good, or lawful) that is opposite to one axis of
your own. Once chosen, this cannot be changed.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Special:** A neutral inquisitor gains only the bonus on caster level checks to remove curses,
diseases, and poisons.
