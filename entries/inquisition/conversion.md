**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 41

**Associated Deities**

**Details:** Any deity

### Granted Powers

You are a powerful persuader. A honeyed tongue empowered by divine argumentation sways the
indifferent and adversarial to your side.

**Charm of Wisdom (Ex)**

**Ability:** You use your Wisdom modifier instead of your Charisma modifier when making
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), and [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)
checks.

**Swaying Word (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day you may speak a word of divinely inspired wisdom that causes a single
creature to switch its alliance to you. The target must be within line of sight and able to hear
you. If he fails his Will save, he is affected by [dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person),
except the duration is only 1 minute.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
