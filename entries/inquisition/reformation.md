**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 23

**Associated Deities**

**Details:** Any

Moved by divine disapproval of your church's mistakes, you seek to correct them however you can.

### Granted Powers

You can speak with divine persuasiveness.

**Inspired Rhetoric (Ex)**

**Ability:** You use your Wisdom modifier in place of your Charisma modifier when making
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), and
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (oratory) checks. Perform (oratory) is an inquisitor class skill
for you.

**Blessed Correction (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** When you make a Diplomacy, Intimidate, or Perform (oratory) check, you may roll twice
and take the more favorable result.

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day

**Awaken Discontent (Su)**

**Ability:** You can deliver a stirring speech condemning the mistakes of those in power and calling
for reform, causing the target of your rhetoric to be affected by
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person) with a caster level equal to your inquisitor level and a save
DC of 10 + 1/2 your inquisitor level + your Wisdom modifier.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day
