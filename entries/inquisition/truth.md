### Granted Powers

To find truth, one has to wring honesty from others - whether in a friendly or hostile manner.

**Justice's True Path (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can touch a creature, granting an insight bonus on one attack roll, skill check,
ability check, or saving throw before your next turn; this bonus is equal to half your inquisitor
level (minimum 1) and lasts for 1 round.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Grasp of Honesty (Su)**

**Ability:** Any creature you are grappling or pinning is affected by
[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth) (Will negates). Outside of combat, you may use this ability
by touching a target with both hands.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Special:** The effect ends if you stop grappling, pinning, or touching the target.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per inquisitor level
