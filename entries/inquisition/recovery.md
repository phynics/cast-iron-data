### Granted Powers

Some hunt down secrets, but you focus on the search for more tangible items.

**Focused Search (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you may designate a person or object as your _focus._ This takes 1 minute
of concentration, during which time you must either picture this item or person if familiar to you,
or meditate on the item's or person's description if not. Until you choose a new _focus,_ you gain a
bonus equal to half your inquisitor level on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to see or
hear your _focus,_ or when using [Survival](ref/skill/survival) to track your _focus_ or a creature
with your _focus_ in its possession.

**Special:** This bonus stacks with that gained from the _track_ special ability.

**Locate Focus (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day you may use either [locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature) or
[locate object](ref/spell/locate-object) as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
using your current _focus_ as the target. The range of this effect is 1 mile per level.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
