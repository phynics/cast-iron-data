**Sources:** Ultimate Magic pg 43

**Associated Deities**

**Details:** Abadar, Iomedae, Sarenrae

### Granted Powers

Most inquisitors fight insane aberrations and demonic creatures as well as the chaos of heresy. They
use the dictum and the mantles of law in order to aid their struggle.

**Mantle against Chaos (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use [protection from chaos](ref/spell/protection-from-chaos) on yourself or
another creature as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per inquisitor level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be
spent in 1-minute increments

**Commanding Order (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use [greater command](ref/spell/command-greater), except it only affects a
single target.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day
