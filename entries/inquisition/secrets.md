**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 28

**Associated Deities**

**Details:** [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria), [Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber)

### Granted Powers

You have learned to keep your own secrets close, and your enemies' secrets closer.

**Detect Secrets (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast [detect anxieties](ref/spell/detect-anxieties),
[detect desires](ref/spell/detect-desires), or [detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts) or use
any of those spells as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), you can choose to gain
the effects of one of the other two spells instead. You are never overwhelmed by a target of these
spells that has a high ability score.

**Flavor:** You have learned how to detect any of a creature's secrets, not just its thoughts.

**Closed Book (Sp)**

**Flavor:** It becomes difficult for your foes to unveil your secrets.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You are constantly under the effect of [nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection), as if
you cast the spell on yourself.
