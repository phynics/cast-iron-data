### Granted Powers

The dead have paid their dues; there is no need, and no excuse, for them to walk. You protect their
rest personally.

**Disrupt Animation (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use [disrupt undead](ref/spell/disrupt-undead) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), adding your wisdom modifier to the damage.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Unravel Animation (Su)**

**Ability:** When you activate your _[bane](ref/spell/bane)_ ability with undead as the creature
type, you can choose for your weapon to instead gain the [disruption](ref/magic-enh/disruption)
weapon special ability. The DC to resist this effect is equal to 10 + 1/2 your inquisitor level +
your Wisdom modifier.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
