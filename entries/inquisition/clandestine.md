### Granted Powers

Secrecy is required of the faithful in lands where your deity's worshipers are hunted.

**Gained class skills:** soh

**Disappear (Sp)**

**Standard:** You can become invisible as per [the spell](ref/spell/invisibility). The invisibility
lasts for 1 round per inquisitor level or until you attack.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Blessed Secrecy (Su)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** When you attempt a [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise), Sleight of
Hand, or [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) skill check, you can roll twice and take the more favorable
result.

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day

**Delayed Spells (Su)**

**Ability:** You can delay the effects of a spell you cast by up to 1 round per inquisitor level.
You choose the duration of the delay when you cast the spell and can cause the spell to take effect
immediately during the period of delay by concentrating as a **standard action**.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 inquisitor levels beyond 8th
