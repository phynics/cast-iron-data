### Granted Powers

Some inquisitors believe that without faith there is only oblivion. But there are some divine
servants who know enough about the darkness that lies beyond faith to use it as a tool- and a
weapon.

**Life Anchor (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a 10-foot-radius aura that helps dying creatures cling to life. Within this
aura, a dying creature (including yourself) adds your Wisdom bonus to its
[stabilization checks](ref/misc/dying) (in addition to any other modifiers to stabilization checks).

**Stare of Oblivion (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use [feeblemind](ref/spell/feeblemind) as a [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack against
one creature (Will negates). This effect lasts 1d4 rounds.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 inquisitor levels beyond 8th
