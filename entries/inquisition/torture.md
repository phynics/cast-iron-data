### Granted Powers

Only through pain can truth and justice come to the surface. And if that doesn't work, at least
you've enacted divine vengeance.

**Torturer's Presence (Ex)**

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus when using the [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) skill. This is in
addition to your bonus for _Stern Gaze._

**Torturer's Touch (Sp)**

**Ability:** You may use [touch of fatigue](ref/spell/touch-of-fatigue) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Creatures that are immune to pain effects are
immune to this touch.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Critical Precision (Ex)**

**Ability:** When you roll a critical threat, you may expend one use of your _torturer's touch_
ability to add +4 on your critical confirmation roll. This does not stack with
[Critical Focus](ref/feat/critical-focus).

**Gained:** At 8th Level
