### Granted Powers

You embrace the spirit of chivalry, guarding your honor and training a noble mount.

**Mount (Ex)**

**Ability:** This ability acts as the [cavalier](ref/class/cavalier) ability of the same name.

**Mount (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** Your bond with your mount is strong, with the two of you learning to anticipate each
other's moods and moves. Should your mount die, you may find another mount to serve you after 1 week
of mourning. This new mount does not gain the _link, evasion, devotion,_ or _improved evasion_
special abilities until the next time you gain a level.

You gain the service of a loyal and trusty steed to carry you into battle. This mount functions as a
druid's [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion), using your level as your effective druid
level. The creature must be one that you are capable of riding and is suitable as a mount. This
mount may come from the following list:

**Medium Cavaliers:** [Axe Beak](ref/companion/axe-beak), [Camel](ref/companion/camel),
[Elk](ref/companion/elk), [Giraffe](ref/companion/giraffe), [Horse](ref/companion/horse),
[Giant Seahorse](ref/companion/seahorse-giant), [Giant Tortoise](ref/companion/tortoise-giant),
[Zebra](ref/companion/zebra); **Small Cavaliers:** [Antelope](ref/companion/antelope),
[Boar](ref/companion/boar) (requires 4th level), [Capybara](ref/companion/capybara),
[Dog](ref/companion/dog) (requires 4th level), [Kangaroo](ref/companion/kangaroo),
[Giant Gecko](ref/companion/lizard-giant-gecko), [Pony](ref/companion/pony),
[Ram](ref/companion/ram), Reindeer (uses elk statistics), [Stag](ref/companion/stag),
[Giant Weasel](ref/companion/weasel-giant), [Wolf](ref/companion/wolf),
[Wolfdog](ref/companion/wolfdog)

The GM might approve other animals as suitable mounts.

You do not take an armor check penalty on [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks while riding your mount. The
mount is always considered combat trained and begins play with
[Light Armor Proficiency](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-light) as a bonus feat. Your mount does not
gain the _share spells_ special ability.

**Faithful Steed (Su)**

**At 16th Level:** Your mount gains the benefits of two of your selected _judgments._

**At 8th Level:** When you use your _judgment_ ability, your mount gains the benefit of one of your
selected _judgments._
