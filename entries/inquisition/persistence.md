### Granted Powers

Your deity chose you for your persistence. You have vowed to pursue the enemies of the faith to the
world's end if necessary.

**Step Up**

**Ability:** You receive [Step Up](ref/feat/step-up) as a bonus feat.

**Relentless Footing (Ex)**

**Swift:** You can add 10 feet to your land speed. This increase counts as an enhancement bonus, and
it affects your [jumping](ref/skill/acrobatics) distance as normal for increased speed.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Inner Strength (Su)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Swift:** Once per day, you may heal yourself for 3d6 hit points. When you use this ability, you
can also remove one of the following conditions from yourself: blinded, confused, dazzled, deafened,
frightened, nauseated, shaken, sickened, or staggered.

At 8th level: The amount you heal 4d6.

At 10th level: The amount you heal 5d6.

At 12th level: The amount you heal 6d6.

At 14th level: The amount you heal 7d6.

At 16th level: The amount you heal 8d6.

At 18th level: The amount you heal 9d6.

At 20th level: The amount you heal 10d6.
