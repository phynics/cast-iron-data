### Granted Powers

Your connections to the criminal underworld run deeper than the physical, and your spiritual
criminality grants you underhanded powers.

**Criminal Minds (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can touch a creature and fill its mind with criminal impulses. This otherwise
functions as per [crime of opportunity](ref/spell/crime-of-opportunity), though a target that fails
its saving throw and acts on an impulse gets those desires out of its system and becomes immune to
the effects of your _criminal minds_ ability for 24 hours.

**Usage:** Wisdom3 modifier times/day

**Thief's Soul (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain [Improved Steal](ref/feat/improved-steal),
[Greater Steal](ref/feat/greater-steal), and [Quick Steal](ref/feat/quick-steal) as bonus feats,
even if you don't meet the prerequisites.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
