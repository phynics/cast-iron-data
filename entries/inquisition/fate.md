### Granted Powers

Fate is a powerful tool for those who dole out justice for their deity. You can read the strands of
fate, and those strands guide your endeavors.

**Augury (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can use [augury](ref/spell/augury) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Agent of Fate (Su)**

**Ability:** When you use your _[augury](ref/spell/augury)_
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), you gain a benefit that lasts until the end of
your next combat. If the result was &quot&weal,&quot& all curing effects used on you cure an
additional +1d10 hit points. If the result was &quot&woe,&quot& your melee attacks deal an
additional +1d6 points of damage. If the result is &quot&weal and woe&quot& or &quot&nothing,&quot&
you gain a +2 insight bonus to your Armor Class.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
