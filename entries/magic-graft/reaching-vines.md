**Price** 4,000 gp; **Slot** wrists\
Cytillesh spores seeded beneath the subject's skin enable the subject to extend and contract fungal
vines from its wrists and forearms at will. The subject gains two vine attacks per round, which
count as secondary [natural attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) with a reach of 10 feet. These vines
deal no damage, but the fungal-grafted creature can attempt to pull a struck target up to 5 feet
toward itself, as the [pull](ref/umr/pull) universal monster ability.
