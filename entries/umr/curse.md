## Curse (Su)

The creature can inflict a curse upon its enemies. The effects, including its save, frequency, and
cure, are included in the creature's description. If a curse allows a saving throw, it is usually a
Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 the cursing creature's racial HD + that creature's Charisma modifier; the
exact DC is given in the creature's descriptive text). Curses can be removed through
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) and similar effects.

**Format**

**Details:** Curse Name (Su) Slam-contact; _save_ Will DC 14; _frequency_ 1/day; _effect_ 1d4 Str
drain

**Details:** Special Attacks and individual attacks
