## Regeneration (Ex)

A creature with this ability is difficult to kill. Creatures with regeneration heal damage to
themselves at a fixed rate, as with [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing), but they can't die as long
as their regeneration is still functioning (although creatures with regeneration still fall
unconscious when their hit points are below 0). Certain damage types, typically acid and fire, cause
a creature's regeneration to stop functioning on the round following the attack. During this round,
the creature does not heal any damage and can die normally. The creature's descriptive text
describes the types of damage that cause the regeneration to cease functioning.

Attacks that don't deal hit point damage are not healed by regeneration. Regeneration also does not
restore hit points lost from starvation, suffocation, or thirst. Regenerating creatures can regrow
lost portions of their bodies and can reattach severed limbs or body parts if they are brought
together within 1 hour of severing. Severed parts that are not reattached wither and die normally.

A creature must have a Constitution score to have the regeneration ability.

**Format**

**Details:** regeneration 5 (acid, fire)
