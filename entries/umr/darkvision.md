## Darkvision (Ex or Su)

A creature with darkvision can see in total darkness, usually to a range of 60 feet. Within this
range, the creature can see as clearly as a sighted creature could see in an area of bright light.
Darkvision is black and white only but otherwise like normal sight.

**Format**

**Details:** darkvision 60 ft.

**Details:** Senses
