## Unnatural Aura (Su)

**Sources:** Bestiary 4 pg 301\
Animals do not willingly approach the creature unless the animal's master succeeds at a DC 25 Handle
Animal, Ride, or [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) check.

**Details:** unnatural aura (30 ft.)
