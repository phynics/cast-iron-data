## Ooze Traits (Ex)

[Oozes](ref/type/ooze) are immune to critical hits, flanking, precision damage, mind-affecting
effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns, and phantasms), paralysis, poison,
polymorph, sleep, stun, and visual effects.
