## Entrap (Ex or Su)

The creature has an ability that restricts another creature's movement, usually with a physical
attack such as ice, lava, mud, or webs. The target of an entrap attack must succeed at a Fortitude
save or become entangled for the listed duration. If a target is already entangled by this ability,
a second entrap attack means the target must succeed at a Fortitude save or become helpless for the
listed duration. The save DCs are Constitution-based. A target made helpless by this ability is
conscious but can take no physical actions (except attempting to break free) until the entrapping
material is removed. The target can use spells with only verbal components or
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) if it succeeds at a DC 20
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check. An entangled creature can attempt a Strength check
(at the same DC as the entrap saving throw DC) as a full-round action to break free; the DC for a
helpless creature is 5 greater. Destroying the entrapping material frees the creature.
