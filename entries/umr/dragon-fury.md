## Dragon Fury (Su) \[Mythic\]

If a mythic dragon confirms a critical hit with a natural weapon, it adds its dragon blood damage to
the damage dealt by the natural attack.

**Format**

**Details:** dragon fury (1d6 fire)

**Location**

**Details:** Special Attacks
