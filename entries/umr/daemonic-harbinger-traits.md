## Daemonic Harbinger Traits (Su)

A daemonic harbinger is a powerful [daemon](ref/family/daemon) that has not yet made the full
transition from unique daemon to a horseman. It possesses several traits, as summarized here.

- Immunity to acid, charm and compulsion effects, death effects, disease, and poison.
- Resistance to cold 30, electricity 30, and fire 30.
- Telepathy 300 feet.
- The harbinger's natural weapons, as well as any weapon it wields, are treated as evil and lawful
  for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
- The harbinger can grant spells to its worshipers. Granting spells does not require any specific
  action on its behalf. The specific domains and favored weapon and listed in the monster's entry.
