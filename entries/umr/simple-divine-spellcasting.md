## Simple Divine Spellcasting \[Mythic\]

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 228\
The creature gains the ability to cast spells from the cleric or druid spell list. Select a number
of spells with total spell levels equal to twice the creature's CR. No spell for this ability should
have a level higher than 1 + 1/2 the creature's CR. A 0-level spell counts as 1/2 spell level toward
this total. The creature can cast each of these spells once per day. Its caster level is equal to
its Hit Dice. It uses its Wisdom or Charisma (whichever is higher) to determine its spell DCs.
