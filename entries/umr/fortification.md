## Fortification (Ex)

The monster has a 50% chance to treat any critical hit or sneak attack as a normal hit, as if
wearing [moderate fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-moderate) armor.

**Format**

**Details:** fortification (50%)

**Location**

**Details:** Defensive Abilities
