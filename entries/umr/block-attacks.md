## Block Attacks (Ex) \[Mythic\]

Once per round, when the creature is hit by a melee or ranged attack, it can attempt a melee attack
using its highest attack bonus. If this result exceeds the result from the attack against it, the
creature is unaffected by the attack (as if the attack had missed).

**Format**

**Details:** block attacks

**Details:** Defensive Abilities
