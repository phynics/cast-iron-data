## Summon (Sp)

**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 300\
A creature with the summon ability can summon other specific creatures of its kind much as though
casting a [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell, but it usually has only a limited chance
of success (as specified in the creature's entry). Roll d%; on a failure, no creature answers the
summons. Summoned creatures automatically return to wherever they came from after 1 hour. A creature
summoned in this way cannot use any spells or [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
that require material components costing more than 1 gp unless those components are supplied, nor
can it use its own summon ability for 1 hour. An appropriate spell level is given for each summoning
ability for the purposes of caster level checks, [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks, and
Will saves. No experience points are awarded for defeating summoned monsters.

**Format**

**Details:** 1/day-summon (level 4, 1 [hezrou](ref/monster/demon-hezrou) 35%)
