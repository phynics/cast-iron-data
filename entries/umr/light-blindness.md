## Light Blindness (Ex)

Creatures with light blindness are [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round if exposed to bright
light, such as sunlight or the [daylight](ref/spell/daylight) spell. Such creatures are
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) as long as they remain in areas of bright light.
