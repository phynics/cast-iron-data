## Psychic Resilience (Ex)

**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 297\
Creatures with this ability are resistant to the effects of many psychic spells. They gain a +4
bonus on saving throws against all psychic spells.

**Format**

**Details:** +4 vs. psychic spells

**Location**

**Details:** After saves and in Defensive Abilities
