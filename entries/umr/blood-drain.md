## Blood Drain (Ex)

**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 290\
The creature drains blood at the end of its turn if it grapples a foe, dealing
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).

**Format**

**Details:** blood drain (1d2 Con)
