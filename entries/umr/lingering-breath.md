## Lingering Breath (Su) \[Mythic\]

The creature can expend one use of mythic power as a free action when it uses its breath weapon to
make the area radiate energy damage (of the same type as the breath weapon) for 1 round per mythic
rank. Any creature in, entering, or passing through the breath weapon's area during this duration
takes damage according to the creature's size. This ability has no effect on breath weapons that do
not deal energy damage.

#### Table 3-1: Lingering Breath Damage

|Creature Size|Points of Energy Damage|
| --- | --- |
|Medium or smaller|2d4|
|Large|2d6|
|Huge|2d8|
|Gargantuan|4d6|
|Colossal|4d8|

**Format**

**Details:** lingering breath (2d6 fire, 5 rounds)

**Details:** Special Attacks
