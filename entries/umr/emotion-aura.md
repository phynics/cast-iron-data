## Emotion Aura (Su)

A creature with this ability surrounds itself with an area of swirling, chaotic emotions. These
emotions make it difficult for spellcasters to cast spells with the
[emotion component](ref/rule/components-oa). Living creatures within 30 feet must succeed at a
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC = 10 + 1/2 the HD of the creature with an emotion
aura + that creature's Charisma modifier) to cast a spell with an emotion component. Failing this
check causes the spell to be lost with no effect. In addition, any spellcaster casting spells with
the emotion or fear descriptor on targets inside this aura must succeed at this check or the spell
fails to affect targets within the aura (although others outside the area are affected as normal).
Creatures with this ability are immune to its effects and can cast spells normally.
