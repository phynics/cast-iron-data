## Sand Glide (Ex) \[Mythic\]

This ability functions like the [earth elemental's](ref/monster/earth-elemental) earth glide
ability, but works only on sand, dirt, and other fine-grained solid matter. The creature's speed
using sand glide as the same as its base speed.

**Format**

**Details:** sand glide

**Location**

**Details:** Speed
