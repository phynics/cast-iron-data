## Flight (Ex or Su)

**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 293\
The creature can cease or resume flight as a free action. If the ability is supernatural, it becomes
ineffective in an antimagic field, and the creature loses its ability to fly for as long as the
antimagic effect persists.

**Details:** fly 30 ft. (average)
