## Low-Light Vision (Ex)

**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 295\
A creature with low-light vision can see twice as far as a human in starlight, moonlight,
torchlight, and similar conditions of dim light. It retains the ability to distinguish color and
detail under these conditions.
