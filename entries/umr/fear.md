## Fear (Su or Sp)

**Sources:** Bestiary 5 pg 293\
Fear attacks can have various effects.

**Fear Aura (Su):** The use of this ability is a free action. The aura can freeze an opponent (as in
the case of a [mummy's despair](ref/monster/mummy)) or function like the [fear](ref/spell/fear)
spell. Other effects are possible. A fear aura is an area effect. The descriptive text gives the
size and kind of the area.

**Fear Cone (Sp) and Ray (Su):** These effects usually work like the _fear_ spell. If a fear effect
allows a saving throw, it is a Will save (DC = 10 + 1/2 the fearsome creature's racial HD + that
creature's Charisma modifier; the exact DC is given in the creature's descriptive text). All fear
attacks are mind-affecting fear effects.

**Format**

**Details:** fear aura (30 ft., DC 17) or fear cone (50 ft., DC 19)

**Location**

**Details:** Aura or Special Attacks
