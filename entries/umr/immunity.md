## Immunity (Ex or Su)

A creature with immunities takes no damage from listed sources. Immunities can also apply to
afflictions, conditions, spells (based on level, save type, or school), and other effects. A
creature does not suffer any secondary effects that would normally be triggered by an effect it is
immune to.

**Format**

**Details:** Immune acid, fire, paralysis

**Location**

**Details:** Defensive Abilities
