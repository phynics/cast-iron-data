## Greensight (Su)

**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 227\
The monster can see through thick plant matter as though it were transparent, usually with a range
of 60 feet. Greenery, leaves, undergrowth, and vines offer no concealment to the monster's sight,
though solid wood still blocks its line of sight.
