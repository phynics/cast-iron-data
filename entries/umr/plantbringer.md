## Plantbringer (Su) \[Mythic\]

**Sources:** Bestiary 4 pg 297\
All plants within a 1-mile radius of the creature grow at double their normal rate and don't suffer
from any diseases or maladies. Allied plant creatures within 30 feet of the creature gain
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) equal to the creature's mythic rank. If the creature uses
[plant shape](ref/spell/plant-shape-1) or wild shape to take the form of a plant, it gains this fast
healing in plant form.
