## Dual Initiative (Ex) \[Mythic\]

The creature gets two turns each round, one on its initiative count and another on its initiative
count - 20. For example, if the monster's initiative is 23, on its first turn it could make a full
attack (and take a 5-foot step) at initiative 23, and on its second turn at initiative 3 it could
take a move action and cast a spell. This allows the monster to perform two actions per round that
normally take an entire round, such as using a [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell. For
the purposes of spells and effects that have a duration of a round or longer or trigger at the
beginning of the creature's round or the start of its turn (such as saving throws against ongoing
effects or taking bleed damage), only the monster's first turn each round counts toward such
durations.
