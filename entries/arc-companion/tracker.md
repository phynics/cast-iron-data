**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 189\
Some companions are expert trackers, able to use their scent ability to follow any trail.

**Keen Nose:** A tracker must be an animal companion with the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability.

**Tracker Skills**

**Passive:** Trackers treat Survival as a class skill.

**Expert Tracker (Ex)**

**At 6th Level:** It can move at its normal speed while using Survival to follow tracks without
taking the normal -5 penalty. It takes only a -10 penalty (instead of the normal -20 penalty) when
moving at up to twice its normal speed while tracking.

**Passive:** A tracker gains a competence bonus on Survival checks to track via scent equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) its total Hit Dice.
