**Sources:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook pg 26\
The heart of this companion beats with primal energy.

**Elemental Type (Ex)**

**Passive:** An elemental companion gains a subtype that matches its element: air, earth, fire, or
water.

**Lesser Elemental Manifestation (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** An elemental companion gains one or more special abilities based on its element. An
**air** companion gains a 20% miss chance against ranged attacks. An **earth** companion gains DR
1/adamantine, and this [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) increases by 1 for every 4
druid levels the master has. A **fire** companion can see through fire, fog, and smoke without
penalty, and it gains [Nimble Moves](ref/feat/nimble-moves) as a bonus feat. A **water** companion
can breathe underwater, gains a swim speed of 20 feet, and gains a +2 dodge bonus to AC against
attacks of opportunity; a water companion that already has a swim speed instead increases its swim
speed by 10 feet.

**Replaces:** Evasion, Improved Evasion

**Greater Elemental Manifestation (Su)**

**Ability:** An elemental companion gains an additional ability based on its element. An **air**
companion gains a fly speed of 20 feet (good maneuverability); an air companion that already has a
fly speed instead improves its maneuverability by one category. An **earth** companion's natural
attacks overcome [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) as though they were adamantine, cold
iron, and silver weapons. For a **fire** companion, choose one natural attack; that attack deals an
additional 1d6 points of fire damage for every 5 Hit Dice the companion has. For a **water**
companion, choose one melee natural attack; that attack gains the [push (10 feet)](ref/umr/push)
special ability.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Multiattack
