**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 20\
The mighty charger is a boon to heavily armored and armed champions, bearing them and their
ironmongery into battle without hesitation.

**Animal Feats**

**Passive:** In addition to the standard animal companion feats, a charger may select
[Charge Through](ref/feat/charge-through), [Greater Overrun](ref/feat/greater-overrun),
[Improved Great Fortitude](ref/feat/improved-great-fortitude), and
[Improved Iron Will](ref/feat/improved-iron-will).

**Gained class skills:** intm

**Mounted Challenge (Ex)**

**Passive:** When ridden by a [cavalier](ref/class/cavalier), chargers gain
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of the bonuses and penalties granted by the cavalier's
challenge class feature.

**Replaces:** Share Spells

**Barding Training (Ex)**

**At 15th Level:** The armor check penalty is now reduced by 3, and the maximum Dex bonus now
increases by 3.

**At 9th Level:** The armor check penalty is now reduced by 2, and the maximum Dex bonus now
increases by 2. In addition, a charger can move at normal speed in heavy armor or while carrying a
heavy load.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A charger's armor check penalty for wearing barding or for
[carrying a medium load](ref/rule/carrying-capacity) is reduced by 1 and the maximum Dexterity bonus
for its armor and load increases by 1. In addition, the charger's speed does not decrease when
wearing medium armor or carrying a medium load.

**Replaces:** Evasion, Multiattack

**Indefatigable (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A charger becomes immune to [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued).

**Replaces:** Devotion

**Stalwart Steed (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** Whenever the charger succeeds at a Fortitude or Will save that would normally result in
a reduced or partial effect, the charger is unaffected.

**Replaces:** Improved Evasion
