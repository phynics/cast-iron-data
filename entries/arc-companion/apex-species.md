Some animals have no serious competitors and no real predators. They are masters of their habitats,
perfectly evolved to take advantage of their native terrain. They need not be predators, of course -
some herbivores are just as lethally suited to their homes.

**Native Environment (Ex)**

**Choice:** An apex species selects a specific terrain type from the ranger's list of
[favored terrains](ref/ability/favored-terrain).

**Passive:** While in its chosen terrain, the apex species gains a bonus equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) its level on checks and saves to deal with environmental
hazards. An apex species may not select a plane as its native environment.

**Replaces:** Share Spells

**Favored Terrain (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** The apex species gains the ranger's [favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain)
ability for the terrain that matches its native environment, except that the bonus is +1 instead of
the ranger's +2.

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Special:** The animal companion does not gain additional favored terrains.

At 8th level: The bonus +2.

At 13th level: The bonus +3.

At 18th level: The bonus +4.

**Natural Selection (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** Devotion, Improved Evasion

The apex species gains an ability based on their favored terrain, as follows:

_**Cold:**_ The apex species gains cold resistance 5, increasing to 15 at 15th level.

_**Desert:**_ The apex species gains fire resistance 5, increasing to 15 at 15th level.

_**Forest, Jungle:**_ The apex species gains a +1 dodge bonus to CMD, increasing to +3 at 15th
level.

_**Mountains, Plains:**_ The apex species gains electricity resistance 5, increasing to 15 at 15th
level.

_**Water, Underground:**_ The apex species gains acid resistance 5, increasing to 15 at 15th level.

_**Swamp, Urban:**_ The apex species gains a +4 racial bonus on saves against disease and poison. At
15th level, the apex species becomes immune to disease and poison.

**Master of the Wild (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Multiattack

The apex species gains an ability based on its favored terrain, as follows.

_**Cold:**_ The apex species can see through weather conditions (including blizzards, rain, fog, and
mist) without penalty, ignoring any cover or concealment from such effects. If the effect is created
by magic, the apex species instead triples the distance it can see without penalty.

_**Desert:**_ The apex species gains a +4 morale bonus on Will saves against illusion spells and
effects.

_**Forest, Urban:**_ The apex species gains [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Stealth) as a bonus
feat.

_**Jungle, Mountains:**_ The apex species gains a climb speed equal to half its base land speed, or
increases its existing climb speed by 10 feet.

_**Plains:**_ The apex species increases its base land speed by 10 feet.

_**Swamp, Water:**_ The apex species gains a swim speed equal to half its base land speed, or
increases its existing swim speed by 10 feet.

_**Underground:**_ The apex species gains a burrow speed equal to half its base land speed, or
increases its existing burrow speed by 10 feet.
