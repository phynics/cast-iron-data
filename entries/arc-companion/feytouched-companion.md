**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 188\
Feytouched companions are strangely colored fauna native to the First World, the primal realm of the
fey. They gain strange abilities tied to the fey.

**Small:** A feytouched companion must be an animal companion that normally starts as Small or
smaller.

**Gained class skills:** perf

**Not Quite Animal**

**Passive:** The DC to use Handle Animal on a feytouched companion is 5 higher, as if it were a
nonanimal with an Intelligence score of 1 or 2.

**Small but Fierce (Ex)**

**Passive:** At 4th or 7th level, whenever the feytouched companion would normally gain the choice
of a listed advancement or a +2 bonus to its Dexterity and Constitution scores, it instead gains a
+2 bonus to its Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores.

**Replaces:** The 4th- or 7th-level ability score and size advancement

**Fey Magic (Sp)**

**Fullround:** A feytouched companion can cast [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights) at will as
a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 3rd Level:** A feytouched companion can [ready](ref/rule/ready) an action for its master's
spellcasting while adjacent to its master, to allow its master to spontaneously cast one of the
following spells by sacrificing a spell slot of equal or higher level:
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire) (1st), [glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust) (2nd),
[deep slumber](ref/spell/deep-slumber) (3rd), [major image](ref/spell/major-image) (4th),
[confusion](ref/spell/confusion) (5th), [feeblemind](ref/spell/feeblemind) (7th),
[mislead](ref/spell/mislead) (8th). A feytouched companion's master can cast each of the spells on
the list only once each day in this way.

**Replaces:** Share Spells, Multiattack

**Iron Bane (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A feytouched companion gains [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 1/cold iron.
This damage reduction increases by 1 for every 3 levels beyond 3rd.

**Replaces:** Evasion, Devotion, Improved Evasion

**Special:** Any creature holding or wearing an item made out of cold iron takes a -10 penalty on
Handle Animal checks to handle the feytouched companion.
