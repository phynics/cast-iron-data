Totem guides embody the wisdom and spirituality of the natural world, providing guidance as well as
aid in combat. Once a totem guide is chosen, a character can replace the companion if it is slain
but must choose the same kind of totem guide.

**Spiritual Guidance (Sp)**

**Ability:** A totem guide's master can spontaneously cast the following spells while adjacent to
the totem guide by sacrificing a spell slot of equal or higher level:
[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants) (1st), [augury](ref/spell/augury)
(2nd), [helping hand](ref/spell/helping-hand) (3rd), [divination](ref/spell/divination) (4th),
[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature) (5th), and
[find the path](ref/spell/find-the-path) (6th). The totem guide serves as a divine focus for these
spells, but costly material components must still be provided.

**Fullround:** A totem guide can use [guidance](ref/spell/guidance) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) at will, targeting itself or its master.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Beast Speech (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Passive:** A totem guide can speak with its master as though the two share a common language, and
it can speak with other animals of its species (or, at the GM's discretion, other creatures with
similar types).

**Replaces:** Devotion

**Eldritch Claws**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** A totem guide gains [Eldritch Claws](ref/feat/eldritch-claws) as a bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Multiattack

**Ghost Guardian (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** A totem guide's natural weapons deal full damage to incorporeal creatures, and its
natural armor bonus applies against incorporeal touch attacks. A totem guide can never be
transformed into or animated as an undead creature.

**Replaces:** Improved Evasion
