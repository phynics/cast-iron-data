**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 186\
Ambushers sneak up on unsuspecting prey, pouncing on targets when they least expect it.

**Camouflage (Ex)**

**At 15th Level:** An ambusher can use the Stealth skill to hide in its natural environment, even if
the environment doesn't grant [cover](ref/rule/cover) or [concealment](ref/rule/concealment).

**Passive:** An ambusher gains a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks in its natural environment (if
this is unclear, use the environment in its Bestiary entry).

**Replaces:** Share Spells, Improved Evasion

**Cunning Ambusher (Ex)**

**At 6th Level:** An ambusher gains a +2 racial bonus on initiative checks, and during the surprise
round, opponents are always considered [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against the ambusher's
attacks, even if they have already acted.

**At 9th Level:** An ambusher gains a +2 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls during a surprise
round.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** An ambusher gains [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge).

**Replaces:** Evasion, Devotion, Multiattack
