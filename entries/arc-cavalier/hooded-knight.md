Some cavaliers wander the lonely roads of the world - any world, including the First World -
protecting travelers and acting as their honor guard. Like the [Eldest](ref/rule/the-eldest) they
usually venerate, they wear heavy hoods and rarely show their faces. Hooded knights are usually
members of the [order of the blossom](ref/order/order-of-the-blossom), the
[order of the shield](ref/order/order-of-the-shield), or the
[order of the sword](ref/order/order-of-the-sword).

**Gained class skills:** kg, sur

**Lost class skills:** climb, swim

**Feytouched Mount**

**Alters:** The cavalier's mount

**Info:** A hooded knight's mount has the [fey-touched](ref/template/fey-touched-creature) simple
template when he is riding it, but it cannot use its
_[change shape](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/change-shape)_ ability while
being ridden.

**Hero of the Roads (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** He can now hustle for 6 hours without taking damage.

**At 15th Level:** He can now hustle for 8 hours without taking damage.

**At 17th Level:** His bonuses increase from +4 to +6. In addition, the hooded knight's mount's
speed is now increased by 20 feet, and he can give this increased speed to his allies' mounts, as
above.

**At 20th Level:** He can now hustle for 10 hours without taking damage.

**At 5th Level:** He can now hustle for 4 hours without taking damage.

**At 9th Level:** His bonuses increase from +2 to +4. In addition, the speed of any mount a hooded
knight rides is increased by 10 feet. By spending 1 minute coaxing his allies' mounts and expending
one use of his challenge ability, a hooded knight can grant the same speed increase to the mounts
ridden by all allies within 30 feet who can see and hear him. This speed increase to his allies'
mounts lasts for 1 hour.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** When on a road, a hooded knight gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Knowledge
(geography), Perception, Stealth, and Survival checks. A hooded knight and his mount can
[hustle](ref/rule/overland-movement) while traveling on a road without taking damage for 2 hours
each day.

**Replaces:** Tactician

**Champion of the Roads (Sp)**

**Ability:** A hooded knight gains [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to his cavalier level.
If the hooded knight is riding a mount, his mount does not count against the number of creatures he
brings along with this ability. If the destination is not a road, the hooded knight is shunted to
the nearest open space on a road, taking damage as described in the
_[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_ spell.

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Replaces:** Greater tactician

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 cavalier levels beyond 9th

**Paragon of the Roads (Sp)**

**Ability:** When hooded knight uses his _champion of the roads_ ability, he can treat the ability
as [greater teleport](ref/spell/teleport-greater) rather than
_[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)._ The hooded knight's mount and the mounts ridden by any
creatures he brings along do not count against the number of teleported creatures.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Master tactician
