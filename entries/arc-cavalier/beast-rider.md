**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 36\
The cavalier is defined not only by his dedication to his order or his skill on the battlefield, but
also by the special relationship he maintains with his mount. Where some cavaliers are simply
skilled with horses or well-trained knights, the beast rider spends his life in constant pursuit of
the most perfect mount, forming bonds with greater, more powerful, and more exotic creatures.

**Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A beast rider is proficient with light and medium armor, and with shields (with the
exception of tower shields).

**Exotic Mount (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Mount, expert trainer

A beast rider forms a bond with a strong, loyal companion that permits him to ride it as a mount.
This mount functions as a druid's [animal companion](ref/sidekick/animal-companion), using the beast
rider's level as his effective druid level. The animal chosen as a mount must be large enough to
carry the beast rider (Medium or Large for a Small character; Large or Huge for a Medium character).

The beast rider does not take an armor check penalty on [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks while riding
his mount. The mount is always considered combat trained, and begins play with
[Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) as a bonus feat. A beast rider's mount does not gain the _share
spells_ special ability.

Each time the beast rider increases in level, he can choose to select a new, more impressive mount
better suited to his increased power.

Anytime a feat or ability allows a mount to make a hoof attack, it can make a claw, slam, or other
analogous attack instead.

**Small Size**

**At 1st Level:** Small-sized beast riders can choose a [pony](ref/companion/pony) or
[wolf](ref/companion/wolf) mount.

**At 4th Level:** A Small beast rider can also choose an [allosaurus](ref/companion/allosaurus),
[ankylosaurus](ref/companion/ankylosaurus), [arsinoitherium](ref/companion/arsinoitherium),
[aurochs](ref/companion/aurochs), [bison](ref/companion/aurochs), [boar](ref/companion/boar),
[brachiosaurus](ref/companion/brachiosaurus), [elephant](ref/companion/elephant-mastodon),
[glyptodon](ref/companion/glyptodon), [hippopotamus](ref/companion/hippopotamus),
[mastodon](ref/companion/elephant-mastodon), [megaloceros](ref/companion/megaloceros),
[riding dog](ref/companion/dog), [giant snapping turtle](ref/companion/turtle-giant-snapping),
[triceratops](ref/companion/triceratops), or [tyrannosaurus](ref/companion/tyrannosaurus).

**At 7th Level:** He can also choose a dinosaur ([deinonychus](ref/companion/deinonychus) or
[velociraptor](ref/companion/velociraptor)).

**Medium Size**

**At 1st Level:** Medium beast riders can choose a [camel](ref/companion/camel) or
[horse](ref/companion/horse).

**At 4th Level:** A Medium beast rider can also choose an allosaurus, ankylosaurus, arsinoitherium,
aurochs, bison, brachiosaurus, elephant, glyptodon, hippopotamus, [lion](ref/companion/cat-big),
mastodon, megaloceros, giant snapping turtle, [tiger](ref/companion/cat-big), triceratops, or
tyrannosaurus as his mount. Additional mounts might be available with GM approval.

**At 7th Level:** A Medium beast rider can select any creature whose natural size is Large or Huge,
provided that creature is normally available as a Medium-sized animal companion at 7th level (like a
[bear](ref/companion/bear)). To generate statistics for such a mount, apply the following
modifications: **Size** Large; **Ability Scores** Str +2, Dex -2, Con +2. Increase the damage of
each of the mount's natural attacks [by one die size](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments).

**Special:** A beast rider cannot choose a mount that is not capable of bearing his weight, that has
fewer than four legs, or that has a fly speed (although the GM may allow mounts with a swim speed in
certain environments).
