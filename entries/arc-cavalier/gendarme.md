**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 37\
The gendarme cares less for the finer points of tactical precision than he does for the exhilaration
of the charge: the rush of wind through the visor of his helmet, the feel of his couched lance, the
satisfying shriek of armor giving way before his weapon's force as the point drives past metal into
his foes.

**Bonus Feats**

**Ability:** A gendarme trains to be a mounted terror, almost to the exclusion of all other
abilities. He gains bonus feats at 1st level, 5th level, and then every three levels thereafter, but
must select these bonus feats from the following list:
[Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), [Mounted Combat](ref/feat/mounted-combat),
[Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack), [Ride-By Attack](ref/feat/ride-by-attack),
[Spirited Charge](ref/feat/spirited-charge), [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack), and
[Unseat](ref/feat/unseat).

**Replaces:** Tactician, greater tactician, master tactician, cavalier bonus feats

**Special:** If the gendarme has already selected all of the listed feats, then he may select his
bonus feats from those feats listed as combat feats.

**Transfixing Charge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** A gendarme represents the epitome of mounted combat. Whenever he makes a
[charge](ref/rule/charge) attack while mounted, he deals triple the normal damage (quadruple if
using a [lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance)); this damage includes all increases from the Spirited Charge
feat and from the use of a lance. In addition, if the gendarme confirms a critical hit on a charge
attack while mounted, the attack deals maximum damage for the weapon wielded. Additional damage from
weapon properties, magic effects, precision-based bonuses, or other increases are rolled normally.

**Replaces:** Supreme charge
