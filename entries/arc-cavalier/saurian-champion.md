**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 45\
These primeval cavaliers idolize dinosaurs as apex predators and value them as powerful, oversized
mounts.

**Gained class skills:** acro, kna, per, sur

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Weapon and armor proficiencies

**Info:** A saurian champion does not gain proficiency with medium or heavy armor.

**Savage Combatant (Ex)**

**Info:** A saurian champion does not rely on lethal charges or firing from afar as more
&quot&civilized&quot& cavaliers do, preferring to rampage among his foes instead. Saurian champions
do not deal double damage when [charging](ref/rule/charge) with a [lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance) (or
similar weapon) while mounted. While mounted, a saurian champion cannot attack with ranged weapons
other than thrown weapons.

**Titanic Challenge (Ex)**

**Ability:** A saurian champion considers only the largest creatures a true challenge. His _titanic
challenge_ adds only [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his level on damage rolls against
Medium or smaller creatures, but he adds a +1 bonus on attack rolls against a target of his
_challenge_ that is at least two size categories larger than himself (Huge for Medium saurian
champions) and a +2 bonus on attack rolls against a Colossal target of his _challenge._

**Alters:** Challenge

**Wild Warrior (Ex)**

**Info:** A saurian champion hails from primeval lands where the civilized orders hold little sway.
He does not select an _order_ or gain any _order_-related benefits.

**Replaces:** Order

**Dinosaur Mount (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Alters:** Mount

**Replaces:** Tactician, greater tactician, master tactician

A saurian champion bonds with a dinosaur companion that willingly serves as his mount. This mount
functions as a cavalier's normal mount with the following adjustments:

- The saurian champion's mount must be a dinosaur (see druid companions for all the available
  options).
- The saurian champion can ride mounts of his size category and larger, rather than only those of a
  larger size.
- Increase the mount's Strength score by 8 for the purpose of determining its carrying capacity.
- Whenever an ability or feat allows the saurian champion's mount to make a hoof attack, the mount
  can make a claw or bite attack instead.

**Nimble Rider (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A saurian champion gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC when mounted. Anything that causes the
saurian champion to lose his Dexterity bonus to AC also causes him to lose this dodge bonus.

At 8th level: The bonus +2.

At 14th level: The bonus +3.

At 20th level: The bonus +4.

**Fierce Devotion (Ex)**

**Ability:** A saurian champion's mount gains the _devotion_ ability (instead of at 6th level), and
its effects also apply against emotion and fear effects. The mount is immune to the effects of
[unnatural aura](ref/umr/unnatural-aura).

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Banner

**Quick Rider (Ex)**

**Ability:** A saurian champion can attempt [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks to fast mount or fast
dismount his mount even if it's more than one size category larger than himself.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Titanic Mount (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** The bonus to Strength becomes +4 and his mount's natural armor bonus increases by
+1.

**At 14th Level:** The Strength bonus becomes +6, the Constitution bonus becomes +4, and the natural
armor now increases by +2.

**At 15th Level:** The mount's reach increases to 15 feet.

**At 16th Level:** The Strength bonus becomes +8, and the natural armor now increases by +3.

**At 18th Level:** The Strength bonus becomes +10, the Constitution bonus becomes +6, and the
natural armor now increases by +4.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Passive:** A saurian champion's mount increases in size by one category (even if the base creature
normally does not reach that size). The creature receives the appropriate size penalty on attack
rolls and to AC (-1 if growing to Large or -2 if growing to Huge) and a -2 penalty to Dexterity (to
a minimum Dexterity score of 1), its base damage increases by
[one size category](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments), and its reach increases to 10 feet (if it
wasn't already). It also gains a +2 size bonus to its Strength and Constitution score.

**Primeval Devotion (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 14th Level

**Passive:** The bonus on saving throws provided by the mount's _devotion_ ability increases by 2.
In addition, the mount adds half this bonus to other saving throws.

**Replaces:** Greater banner
