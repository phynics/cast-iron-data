**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 38\
Some cavaliers are entrusted by their masters with the care and use of expensive and powerful
oddities: firearms. Capable of inflicting shocking amounts of destruction without the aid of magic,
these musketeers lead the advance of their troops, firing devastating fusillades long before the
enemy can prepare for the armed charge that follows.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Info:** A musketeer does not gain proficiency in heavy armor. Musketeers are proficient with all
simple and martial weapons, and with firearms. A cavalier's levels stack with any fighter levels he
possesses for the purpose of meeting the level prerequisite for feats that specifically select a
firearm, such as [Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/weapon-specialization).

**Gifted Firearm (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** At 1st level, a musketeer is gifted by his order with a firearm (either a
[pistol](ref/eq-weapon/pistol) or a [musket](ref/eq-weapon/musket)). This weapon is both a symbol of
the cavalier's duty and a focus for much of his talent. He cannot sell this weapon. He also gains
the [Gunsmithing](ref/feat/gunsmithing) feat.

**At 11th Level:** While focused, he can reduce the misfire chance of his weapon by 1 (to a minimum
of 1). This doesn't stack with the similar benefits of [reliable](ref/magic-enh/reliable) magic
weapon special ability.

**At 17th Level:** While focused, the musketeer can double the range increment of his weapon. This
doesn't stack with the [distance](ref/magic-enh/distance) magical ability.

**At 20th Level:** During a period of such focus, the musketeer can engage in a flurry of gunfire.
The cavalier can expend one of his daily _challenges_ to make a [full attack](ref/rule/full-attack)
with his firearm. The musketeer ignores the normal load times for his weapon, but must still expend
enough doses of powder and enough bullets to complete each attack.

**At 8th Level:** When wielding his gifted weapon, as a **standard action**, the mustketeer can
focus himself to gain a number of benefits for 1 minute per cavalier level. The musketeer can use
this ability twice per day, plus one additional time per day for every four levels beyond 8th.
Starting out, the musketeer gains the benefit of the [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical)
feat.

**Replaces:** Mount

**Special:** If the musketeer's gifted firearm is destroyed, the cavalier loses this ability for 30
days while a replacement weapon is crafted for him. During this 30-day period, the musketeer takes a
-1 penalty on weapon and damage rolls.

**Swift Powder (Ex)**

**At 14th Level:** Each time the musketeer issues a _challenge,_ he may reload a wielded firearm as
a **free action**.

**At 4th Level:** The musketeer gains [Rapid Reload](ref/feat/rapid-reload) (musket) or Rapid Reload
(pistol) as a bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Expert trainer
