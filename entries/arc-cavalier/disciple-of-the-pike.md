The [Hellknight](ref/rule/aff-lawknights)
[Order of the Pike](ref/hkorder/order-of-the-pike-minor-order) are renowned monster hunters, taking
on large game using ancient weapon arts combined with modern equipment.

**Bigger They Are (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** The bonus increases to +4 against creatures four size categories larger.

**At 6th Level:** The bonus increases to +3 against a creature three size categories larger.

**Passive:** A disciple of the pike gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC against creatures larger than he
is. This bonus increases by 1 if the creature is two size categories larger.

**Replaces:** Mount

**Pike Charge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A disciple of the pike learns to make more accurate [charge](ref/rule/charge) attacks
with weapons from the polearms or spears [fighter weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups). He
receives a +4 bonus on melee attacks when charging with a polearm or spear (instead of the normal
+2). In addition, the disciple of the pike takes no penalty to his AC after making a charge attack
with a polearm or spear.

**Replaces:** Cavalier's charge

**Monster Hunter (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** A disciple of the pike has an uncanny ability to identify monsters. He can attempt
Knowledge checks untrained to identify monsters and their weaknesses, and gains a bonus equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his cavalier level on Knowledge checks to identify
monsters.

**Replaces:** Expert trainer

**Weapon Training (Ex)**

**Ability:** A disciple of the pike gains weapon training, just like a [fighter](ref/class/fighter).
He must select polearms or spears as his weapon group, and never gains another weapon group. His
bonuses with the selected group otherwise progress as though his fighter level were equal to his
cavalier level.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Replaces:** Banner, greater banner

**Weapon Training (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Text from the fighter class ability.

**At 13th Level:** Your previously selected group's bonus increases to +3.

**At 17th Level:** Your previously selected group's bonus increases to +4.

**At 9th Level:** Your previously selected group's bonus increases to +2.

**At 5th Level:** Select one [weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups). Whenever you attack with a
weapon from this group, you gain a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls. You also add this bonus to
any combat maneuver checks made with weapons from this group. This bonus also applies to your
[CMD](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) when defending against [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) and
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) attempts made against weapons from this group.

**Agile Charger (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Passive:** A disciple of the pike takes no penalties caused by
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) while charging. His movement is still hampered
by obstacles, poor visibility, and other conditions. This ability doesn't allow him to move through
impassible terrain.

**Replaces:** Mighty charge

**Deadly Charge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Whenever a disciple of the pike makes a charge attack with a polearm or spear, he deals
double the normal amount of damage. In addition, if he confirms a critical hit on a deadly charge,
the target is [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1d4 rounds. If the target succeeds at a Will save (DC
= 10 + the cavalier's base attack bonus), it is instead [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d4
rounds.

**Replaces:** Supreme charge
