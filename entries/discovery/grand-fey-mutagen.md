**Grand Fey Mutagen**

**Passive:** The alchemist's _fey mutagen_ now grants a +6 alchemical bonus to Dexterity and
Charisma, a -2 penalty to his Strength, DR 10/cold iron, and the druid's
[woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride) class feature.

**Prereq:** 6th-level alchemist, [fey mutagen](ref/discovery/fey-mutagen),
[greater fey mutagen](ref/discovery/greater-fey-mutagen)

**Special:** In addition, he can choose not to allow spells and effects to affect him if they would
not be capable of affecting both his original creature type and the fey creature type.
