**Greater Plague Bomb**

**Ability:** The effects of the smoke created by an alchemist's bomb duplicates the effects of
[greater contagion](ref/spell/contagion-greater) instead of [fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud),
filling an area equal to twice the bomb's splash radius for 1 round per level.

**Prereq:** 16th-level alchemist, [smoke bomb](ref/discovery/smoke-bomb),
[plague bomb](ref/discovery/plague-bomb)
