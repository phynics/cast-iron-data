**Subsumed Spirit**

**Ability:** The alchemist's parasitic twin can now manifest or hide within the alchemist's torso as
a **move action**.

**Flavor:** As the alchemist grows in power, his parasitic twin laps up a portion of his soul, like
a dog feeding on its master's scraps.

**Prereq:** [Parasitic twin](ref/discovery/parasitic-twin)

**Standard:** The alchemist can cause his manifested twin to babble, driving listeners insane. All
creatures without the _[insanity](ref/spell/insanity)_ special ability (although see below) that are
within 60 feet of the alchemist must succeed at a Will save or be affected by
[confusion](ref/spell/confusion) for 1 round per alchemist level. The alchemist can urge his
parasitic twin to babble a number of times per day equal to 3 + the alchemist's Intelligence
modifier.

**Special:** Creatures that successfully save can't be affected by this effect for 24 hours. The
alchemist is immune to its sibling's babbling. This is a sonic, mind-affecting compulsion effect.

**Note:** This discovery was presented in a section on the [Derro](ref/monster/derro) who have a
special ability called **Madness**, which is referenced by a separate discovery
([method to the madness](ref/discovery/method-to-the-madness)) in that same section. It is unclear
if this discovery is supposed to be referring to that ability, rather than &quot&insanity&quot&,
which is possessed by the unrelated [Great Old Ones](ref/subtype/great-old-one) and
[warped ones](ref/monster/warped-one). Consult your GM if needed.
