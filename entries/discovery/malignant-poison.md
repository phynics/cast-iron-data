**Malignant Poison**

**Fullround:** The alchemist can increase the save DC of any poison by 4 and increase its duration
by 2 frequency increments (for example, [large scorpion venom](ref/eq-poison/large-scorpion-venom)
lasts 8 rounds instead of 6 and [drow poison](ref/eq-poison/drow-poison) lasts 4 minutes instead of
2). Additionally, _malignant poisons_ take effect immediately and do not have an onset time.

**Prereq:** 10th-level alchemist

**Special:** This alchemical change lasts until the poison's extended duration ends or for a number
of minutes equal to the alchemist's level, whichever comes first. After that, the poison reverts
back to its original state.
