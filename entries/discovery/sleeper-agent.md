**&L&Sleeper Agent&FN&**

**Ability:** The alchemist can create a _sleeper agent_ in a ritual that takes 1 minute; the target
must be unconscious for the ritual's duration.

At any time during the next year, as long as he is within 1 mile of the _sleeper agent,_ the
alchemist can activate the agent's programming. When the _sleeper agent_ is activated, treat her as
if she were the target of a [dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person) spell with a caster level
equal to the alchemist's level at the time the _sleeper agent_ was created.

**Flavor:** The alchemist learns how to create a _sleeper agent,_ whom he releases back into its
original society none the wiser.

**Prereq:** 12th-level alchemist

**Special:** If the _sleeper agent_ is killed or the _[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person)_
effect's duration expires, the _sleeper agent_ is permanently released from the alchemist's control.
The alchemist can also release any _sleeper agent_ as a **free action**. The alchemist can have one
active _sleeper agent_ for every 6 alchemist levels he possesses.
