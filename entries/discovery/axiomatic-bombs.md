**Axiomatic Bombs**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it deal lawful divine damage.
Chaotic creatures that take a direct hit from an _axiomatic bomb_ must succeed at a Fortitude save
or be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) on their next turn. Against neutral creatures, _axiomatic
bombs_ deal half damage, and such targets are not affected by their staggering effect. _Axiomatic
bombs_ have no effect on lawful creatures.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist
