**Mummification**

**Ability:** After learning this discovery, the alchemist must perform a 30-day regimen of a special
diet, rigorous exercise, and drinking a mildly poisonous alchemical tea. At the end of this regimen,
he falls unconscious for 24 hours, then awakens as a &quot&living mummy.&quot& The alchemist's type
does not change, but he becomes immune to cold, nonlethal damage, paralysis, and sleep.

**Flavor:** The alchemist has mastered preserving flesh and applied this knowledge to his own body,
turning himself into an undead-like creature.

**Prereq:** 10th-level alchemist, [preserve organs](ref/discovery/preserve-organs)
