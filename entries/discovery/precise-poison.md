**&L&Precise Poison&FN&**

**Flavor:** The alchemist is adept at delivering poison to the most vulnerable areas of his targets.

**Passive:** When the alchemist confirms a critical hit with a poisoned weapon, he increases the
save DC of the poison by an amount equal to the weapon's critical multiplier.
