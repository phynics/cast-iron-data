**&L&Fey Mutagen&FN&**

**Ability:** The alchemist gains the ability to create a _fey mutagen,_ a mixture that lends him
some of the grace and power of [the First World](ref/rule/the-first-world). When imbibed, a _fey
mutagen_ grants a +2 alchemical bonus to the alchemist's Dexterity and Charisma, a -2 penalty to his
Strength, and DR 2/cold iron. This otherwise works like the standard _mutagen_ class feature and
interacts with other effects as though it were a _mutagen_.
