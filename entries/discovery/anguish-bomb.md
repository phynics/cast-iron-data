**Anguish Bomb**

**Ability:** You can throw bombs infused with psychic trauma.

**Special:** This discovery allows other alchemists to duplicate the _anguish bombs_ used by
[mnemostillers](ref/arc-alchemist/mnemostiller).

**Anguish Bomb (Su)**

**Ability:** An _anguish bomb_ is identical to a bomb except that it deals nonlethal damage, it
deals no damage to inanimate objects and creatures immune to mental effects, and creatures caught in
the _anguish bomb's_ splash can attempt a Will save for half damage. A mnemostiller uses his
Charisma score in place of his Intelligence score to determine the bonus damage of his _anguish
bomb_ and the save DC of the _anguish bomb's_ splash damage. He still uses his Intelligence score to
determine his number of bombs per day.

**Flavor:** Text from the mnemostiller archetype ability.

**Special:** If the mnemostiller has taken any hit point damage in the past 24 hours, his _anguish
bombs_ inflict additional nonlethal damage equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of his
alchemist level.
