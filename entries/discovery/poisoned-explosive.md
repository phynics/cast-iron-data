**Poisoned Explosive**

**Ability:** When an alchemist creates a bomb, he can apply a dose of contact or injury poison to
it, as if applying poison to a weapon. If the poisoned bomb hits its target, the bomb deals damage
as normal, but the target must also succeed at a saving throw against the poison or be affected by
that poison. No other creatures are affected by the poison.

**Prereq:** 4th-level alchemist, [Poison use](ref/ability/poison-use) class feature
