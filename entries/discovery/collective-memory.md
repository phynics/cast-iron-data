**Collective Memory**

**Ability:** While under the effects of a _cognatogen,_ the alchemist adds half his class level on
all Knowledge skill checks and may attempt all Knowledge skill checks untrained.

**Prereq:** [Cognatogen](ref/discovery/cognatogen)

**Special:** This bonus does not stack with the second Intelligence bonus granted by the
[mindchemist](ref/arc-alchemist/mindchemist) archetype's _perfect recall_ class feature.
