**Bitter Pill**

**Passive:** The alchemist becomes bitter in flavor. Any creature that hits the bitter alchemist
with a bite attack gains the [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) condition for 1 round unless it succeeds
at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half the alchemist's level + the alchemist's Constitution modifier).
Any creature that swallows the alchemist whole gains the [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) condition
for 1 round unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save (same DC as above).

**Special:** If the alchemist is trapped in the creature's gullet and it becomes nauseated, the
creature vomits him back out immediately as a free action at the start of the creature's turn; the
alchemist lands [prone](ref/rule/prone) adjacent to the creature.
