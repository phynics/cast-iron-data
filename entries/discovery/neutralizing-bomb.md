**Neutralizing Bomb**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to give it the potential to end
certain ongoing effects instead of dealing damage.

Each creature in the splash radius can attempt a new saving throw against any poison afflicting it
and any ongoing conditions or ongoing acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage. (If an effect
did not allow a saving throw, this bomb doesn't allow affected creatures to attempt one.) Success
counts as one successful save toward ending poison and immediately ends other conditions. Exposed
poison in the splash radius is also rendered harmless.

**Special:** A creature with poison abilities that is directly struck by the bomb finds those
abilities useless for 1d4 rounds unless it succeeds at a Fortitude saving throw.
