**Tainted Infusion**

**Ability:** When preparing an extract with the _infusion_ discovery, he can lace the extract with
one of his bombs as long as the extract has a duration greater than instantaneous. If he chooses,
the alchemist can reduce the duration of the extract to 1 round. As soon as the extract's duration
expires, it detonates, dealing 150% of the alchemist's bomb damage to the drinker. This effect does
not produce a splash radius.

**Flavor:** The alchemist can mask murder behind beneficence.

**Prereq:** [Delayed bomb](ref/discovery/delayed-bomb), [infusion](ref/discovery/infusion)

**Special:** Producing a tainted extract consumes both an infusion slot and a daily bomb use.
