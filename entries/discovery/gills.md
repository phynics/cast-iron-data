**&L&Gills&FN&**

**Ability:** An alchemist with this discovery grows a set of _gills_ that allow him to breathe water
in addition to breathing air. When on land, the alchemist risks his _gills_ drying out and must
bathe his _gills_ with 1/2 gallon of water every 24 hours he is out of the water or take 1 point of
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) every hour thereafter.

**Prereq:** 4th-level alchemist
