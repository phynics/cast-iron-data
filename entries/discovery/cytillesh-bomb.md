**Cytillesh Bomb**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can infuse it with
[cytillesh extract](ref/eq-poison/cytillesh-extract). _Cytillesh bombs_ deal 1d4 points of damage,
plus 1d4 points of damage for every odd-numbered level, instead of 1d6. Creatures that take a direct
hit from a _cytillesh bomb_ are [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round per alchemist level unless
they succeed at Fortitude saves.

**Special:** If a creature is rendered unconscious from this bomb or any other attack before it is
able to remove the sickened condition, the victim loses all memory from the last hour and cannot
form new memories for 8 hours. The lost and prevented memories might return later as dreams, and can
be restored with a [restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spell.
