When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it inflict sonic damage. Concussive bombs
deal 1d4 points of sonic damage, plus 1d4 points of sonic damage for every odd-numbered level,
instead of 1d6. Creatures that take a direct hit from a concussive bomb are
[deafened](ref/misc/deafened) for 1 minute unless they succeed at a Fortitude save. An alchemist
must be at least 6th level before selecting this discovery.
