**Grand Inspiring Cognatogen**

**Passive:** The alchemist's _inspiring cognatogen_ now grants a +4 dodge bonus to AC and a +4 bonus
to Reflex saving throws; a -6 penalty to Strength and Constitution; and the effects of having the
[item lore](ref/invtalent/item-lore), [tenacious inspiration](ref/invtalent/tenacious-inspiration),
and [underworld inspiration](ref/invtalent/underworld-inspiration) investigator talents.

**Prereq:** 16th-level alchemist,
[greater inspiring cognatogen](ref/discovery/greater-inspiring-cognatogen)
