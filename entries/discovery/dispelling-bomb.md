**Dispelling Bomb**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it dispel magic effects
instead of deal damage. Creatures that take a direct hit from a _dispelling bomb_ are subject to a
targeted [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) spell, using the alchemist's level as the caster
level. This cannot be used to target a specific spell effect.

**Prereq:** 6th-level alchemist
