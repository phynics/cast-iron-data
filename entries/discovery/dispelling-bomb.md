When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)
effects instead of deal damage. Creatures that take a direct hit from a dispelling bomb are subject
to a targeted _dispel magic_ spell, using the alchemist's level as the caster level. This cannot be
used to target a specific spell effect. The alchemist must be at least 6th level before selecting
this discovery.
