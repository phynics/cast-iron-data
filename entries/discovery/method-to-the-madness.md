**Method to the Madness**

**Ability:** The alchemist has learned to channel his madness into one of his alchemist class
abilities. When the alchemist takes this discovery, he chooses one of either bombs or extracts. If
he selects bombs, he uses his Charisma bonus in place of his Intelligence bonus when determining the
bonus to damage and the saving throw DCs of his bombs. If the alchemist selects extracts, he uses
his Charisma bonus in place of his Intelligence bonus to calculate bonus extracts per day. This
discovery can be taken twice, allowing the alchemist to select both bombs and extracts.

**Prereq:** The [madness](ref/monster/derro) extraordinary ability
