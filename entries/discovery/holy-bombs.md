**Holy Bombs**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it deal good divine damage.
Evil creatures that take a direct hit from a _holy bomb_ must succeed at a Fortitude save or be
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) on their next turn. Against neutral creatures, _holy bombs_ deal
half damage, and such targets are not affected by their staggering effect. _Holy bombs_ have no
effect on good-aligned creatures.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist
