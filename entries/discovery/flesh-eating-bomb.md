**Flesh-Eating Bomb**

**Ability:** The alchemist can create bombs that devour living matter. The alchemist's bomb deals
damage [one die step](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) higher than normal (regular bombs deal d8s,
[concussive bombs](ref/discovery/concussive-bomb) deal 1d6, and so on), but they have no effect on
inorganic matter.

In addition, a creature with at least a +1 armor bonus to AC is less likely to be harmed; such
creatures in the bomb's splash radius take no damage on a successful Reflex save, and the target of
a direct hit can attempt a Reflex save against the bomb's save DC to take only half damage.

;;;aside

**Note:** Despite what the text seems to indicate, _concussive bombs_ and _flesh-eating bombs_
cannot be combined under the published rules. Consult your GM if you wish to use these two together.
