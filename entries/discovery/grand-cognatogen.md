The alchemist's [cognatogen](ref/discovery/cognatogen) now grants a +6 natural armor bonus, a +8
alchemical bonus to one mental ability score (Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma), a +6 alchemical
bonus to a second mental ability score, and a +4 alchemical bonus to a third mental ability score.
The alchemist takes a -2 penalty to his Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution as long as the
[cognatogen](ref/discovery/cognatogen) persists, and takes 2 points of
[ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to each physical ability score when the cognatogen
ends. An alchemist must be at least 16th level and must have the
[greater cognatogen](ref/discovery/greater-cognatogen) discovery or class ability before selecting
this discovery.
