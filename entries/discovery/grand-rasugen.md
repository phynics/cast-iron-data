**Grand Rasugen**

**Passive:** The alchemist's rasugen now grants a +6 alchemical bonus on all saving throws, 4
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) per alchemist level, and immunity to disease,
mind-affecting effects, and poison. The alchemist takes a -6 penalty to Intelligence and a -2
penalty to Charisma and Wisdom as long as the _grand rasugen_ persists.

**Prereq:** 16th-level alchemist, [greater rasugen](ref/discovery/greater-rasugen)
