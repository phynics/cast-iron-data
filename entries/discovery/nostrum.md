**Nostrum**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates an infusion, he can mask a delayed poison within the
mixture. A _nostrum_ can contain only an ingested poison, and the save DC of the poison is reduced
by 2. The poison takes effect a number of hours after being imbibed equal to the alchemist's
Intelligence bonus (minimum 0) decided by the alchemist at the creation of the _nostrum._ The poison
within a _nostrum_ can be detected as normal by spells such as
[detect poison](ref/spell/detect-poison) and similar abilities.

**Prereq:** [Infusion](ref/discovery/infusion)

**Special:** Until the _nostrum_ is consumed or destroyed, it continues to occupy one of the
alchemist's daily extract slots.
