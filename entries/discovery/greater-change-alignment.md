**Greater Change Alignment (Su)**

**Ability:** The effects of the alchemist's _change alignment_ infusion become permanent and can
only be reversed by a [wish](ref/spell/wish) or [miracle](ref/spell/miracle).

**Prereq:** [Change alignment](ref/discovery/change-alignment), [infusion](ref/discovery/infusion)

**Special:** A permanent, forced change of alignment may be devastating, and some believe it is
little better than zealous slavery or mind control. Others consider a good alignment brought about
by any means but purity of heart an affront to freedom. This discovery remains controversial at
best.
