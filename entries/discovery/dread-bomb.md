**Dread Bomb**

**Ability:** Creatures who take damage from your _anguish bombs_ become
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for 1d6 rounds. A target that succeeds at a Will saving throw is
instead [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for a single round.

**Flavor:** Your _anguish bombs_ are tainted with fear and dread.

**Prereq:** 6th-level alchemist, [anguish bomb](ref/discovery/anguish-bomb)
