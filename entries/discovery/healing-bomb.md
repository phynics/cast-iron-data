**Healing Bomb**

**Ability:** Creating a _healing bomb_ requires the alchemist to expend an infused extract or potion
containing a cure spell. A creature that takes a direct hit from a _healing bomb_ is healed as if
she had imbibed the infusion or potion used to create the bomb. Creatures in the splash radius are
healed for the minimum amount of damage the _cure_ spell is capable of healing. A _healing bomb_
damages undead instead of healing them.

**Flavor:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it heal damage instead of
dealing it.
