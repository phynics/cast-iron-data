**Elemental Mutagen**

**Choice:** The alchemist chooses an element (air, earth, fire, or water).

**Passive:** Whenever the alchemist imbibes a _mutagen,_ he gains [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5
to the associated energy type and a +2 competence bonus on an associated skill check. This
resistance increases by 5 and the competence bonus by 2 for each of the following discoveries the
alchemist possesses: [grand mutagen](ref/discovery/grand-mutagen),
[greater mutagen](ref/discovery/greater-mutagen), and [true mutagen](ref/discovery/true-mutagen).

**Special:** The elements and their associated energies and skills are air (electricity, Fly), earth
(acid, Climb), fire (fire, Acrobatics), and water (cold, Swim).

An alchemist can select this discovery up to four times, but must choose a different element each
time. An alchemist can gain the benefits of only one _elemental mutagen_ at a time, selected when he
imbibes the _mutagen_.
