Once per day, the alchemist can dilute any one potion or elixir, creating two doses of the same
potion from one. Diluting a potion costs a number of gp equal to one-quarter of the potion's market
value. A potion that has been diluted cannot be diluted again. This discovery cannot be used to
dilute extracts or mutagens. An alchemist must be at least 12th level before selecting this
discovery.
