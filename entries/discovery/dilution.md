**&L&Dilution&FN&**

**Ability:** Once per day, the alchemist can dilute any one potion or elixir, creating two doses of
the same potion from one. Diluting a potion costs a number of gp equal to one-quarter of the
potion's market value. A potion that has been diluted cannot be diluted again.

**Prereq:** 12th-level alchemist

**Special:** This discovery cannot be used to dilute extracts or _mutagens_.
