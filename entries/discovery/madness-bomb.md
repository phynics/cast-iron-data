**Madness Bomb**

**Ability:** A creature that takes a direct hit from a _madness bomb_ takes damage from the bomb
plus 1d4 points of [Wisdom damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).

**Flavor:** The alchemist's bombs do more than sear flesh - they sear the mind.

**Prereq:** 12th-level alchemist

**Special:** Reduce the amount of normal damage dealt by the bomb by 2d6 (so a bomb that would
normally deal 6d6+4 points of damage deals 4d6+4 points of damage instead). The amount of Wisdom
damage dealt by a _madness bomb_ is reduced by 1 for each _madness bomb_ that hit the target in the
past 24 hours, to a minimum of 1 point of Wisdom damage.
