**Directed Bomb**

**Ability:** The alchemist can cause his bombs to splash in a 15-foot cone instead of a
5-foot-radius burst. The alchemist chooses the direction of the cone. If the attack misses, roll an
additional 1d8 to determine the direction of the blast from where the bomb lands.

**Special:** This discovery can't be combined with the
[explosive bomb](ref/discovery/explosive-bomb) discovery.
