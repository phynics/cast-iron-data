**Profane Bomb**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it deal evil divine damage.
Good creatures that take a direct hit from a _profane bomb_ must succeed at a Fortitude save or be
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) on their next turn. Against a neutral creature, a _profane bomb_
deals half damage, and the target is not affected by the bomb's staggering effect. A _profane bomb_
has no effect against evil-aligned creatures.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist
