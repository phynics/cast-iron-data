**Greater Cognatogen**

**Passive:** The alchemist's _mutagen_ now grants a +4 natural armor bonus, a +6 alchemical bonus to
one mental ability score (Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma), and a +4 alchemical bonus to a second
mental ability score. The alchemist takes a -2 penalty on both associated physical ability scores as
long as the _mutagen_ persists, and takes 2 points of
[ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to both associated physical ability scores when the
_cognatogen_ ends.

**Prereq:** 12th-level alchemist, [cognatogen](ref/discovery/cognatogen)
