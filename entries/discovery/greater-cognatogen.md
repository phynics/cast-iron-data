The alchemist's mutagen now grants a +4 natural armor bonus, a +6 alchemical bonus to one mental
ability score (Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma), and a +4 alchemical bonus to a second mental
ability score. The alchemist takes a -2 penalty on both associated physical ability scores as long
as the mutagen persists, and takes 2 points of [ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to
both associated physical ability scores when the [cognatogen](ref/discovery/cognatogen) ends. An
alchemist must be at least 12th level and must have the [cognatogen](ref/discovery/cognatogen)
discovery or class ability before selecting this discovery.
