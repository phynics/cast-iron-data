**&L&Monstrous Graft&FN&**

**Ability:** The alchemist can replace up to four of his own amputated limbs with those of a
monstrous beast of the same size category.

If the grafted limb is an arm, the alchemist gains either a climb speed of 15 feet or a claw or slam
[natural attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals 1d6 points of damage (1d4 for a Small
alchemist).

If the grafted limb is a leg, the alchemist gains one of the following benefits: a +5 bonus to land
speed, a swim speed of 15 feet, or a +5 bonus on Acrobatics checks when jumping.

**Special:** For each limb the alchemist replaces in this manner, he takes a -2 penalty to Wisdom as
he loses his sense of self. [Derros](ref/monster/derro) are immune to this penalty.
