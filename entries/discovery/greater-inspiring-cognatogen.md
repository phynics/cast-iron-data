**Greater Inspiring Cognatogen**

**Passive:** The alchemist's _inspiring cognatogen_ now grants a +2 dodge bonus to AC and a +2 bonus
to Reflex saving throws; a -4 penalty to Strength and Constitution; and the effects of having the
[amazing inspiration](ref/invtalent/amazing-inspiration),
[eidetic recollection](ref/invtalent/eidetic-recollection), and
[expanded inspiration](ref/invtalent/expanded-inspiration) investigator talents.

**Prereq:** 12th-level alchemist, [inspiring cognatogen](ref/discovery/inspiring-cognatogen)
