The alchemist's [inspiring cognatogen](ref/discovery/inspiring-cognatogen) now grants a +2 dodge
bonus to AC and a +2 bonus to Reflex saving throws; a -4 penalty to Strength and Constitution; and
the effects of having the amazing inspiration, eidetic recollection, and expanded inspiration
investigator talents. An alchemist must be at least 12th level and must have the
[inspiring cognatogen](ref/discovery/inspiring-cognatogen) discovery or class feature to select this
discovery.
