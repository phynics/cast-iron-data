**Greater Alchemical Simulacrum**

**Ability:** The alchemist gains the ability to create a simulacrum. This works like the
[simulacrum](ref/spell/simulacrum) spell, except it costs 100 gp in alchemical materials per Hit Die
of the simulacrum, requires 24 hours to grow, and decays into inert flesh rather than ice or snow if
killed. The created simulacrum is a creature, not a supernatural effect.

**Prereq:** 14th-level alchemist, [alchemical simulacrum](ref/discovery/alchemical-simulacrum)
