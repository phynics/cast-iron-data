**Melancholy Bomb**

**Ability:** Creatures who take damage from your _anguish bombs_ are also affected as per
[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair) for 1 round per alchemist level, or a single round if
the target succeeds at a Will saving throw.

**Flavor:** Your _anguish bombs_ are filled with memories of sadness and regret.

**Prereq:** 10th-level alchemist, [anguish bomb](ref/discovery/anguish-bomb)
