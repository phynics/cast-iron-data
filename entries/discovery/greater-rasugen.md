**Greater Rasugen**

**Passive:** The alchemist's rasugen now grants a +4 alchemical bonus on all saving throws, 3
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) per alchemist level, and immunity to
mind-affecting effects. The alchemist takes a -4 penalty to Intelligence as long as the _greater
rasugen_ persists.

**Prereq:** 12th-level alchemist, must be able to [brew a rasugen](ref/arc-alchemist/mnemostiller)
