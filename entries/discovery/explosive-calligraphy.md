**&L&Explosive Calligraphy&FN&**

**Ability:** The alchemist can create especially volatile pigments that detonate when agitated. This
requires expending one daily use of his bombs, but otherwise functions as
[explosive runes](ref/spell/explosive-runes) except that it deals damage as one of the alchemist's
bombs.

**Prereq:** 6th-level alchemist

**Special:** The alchemist can expend an additional daily use of his bombs to apply one discovery
that applies to his bombs, such as [acid bomb](ref/discovery/acid-bomb) or
[dispelling bomb](ref/discovery/dispelling-bomb), to the runes created with this discovery.
