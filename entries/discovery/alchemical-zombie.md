**Alchemical Zombie**

**Ability:** The alchemist gains the ability to animate a relatively complete corpse as an
alchemy-powered zombie. This process takes 1 hour and costs 100 gp in alchemical reagents per HD of
the corpse being animated; the dead creature gains the [zombie template](ref/template/zombie).

Zombies that are created in this manner count as undead created by
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead) for the purposes of determining how many undead the alchemist
can control. The created zombie is a creature, not a supernatural effect.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist
