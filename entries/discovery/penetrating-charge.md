**Penetrating Charge**

**Fullround:** The alchemist can place a _penetrating charge_ on a lock. Instead of dealing damage,
the _penetrating charge_ seeps into the mechanism and eats away at it, granting a +5 circumstance
bonus on any subsequent Disable Device checks against the mechanism.

**Info:** A _penetrating charge_ is a bomb specifically designed to disable locks and similar
mechanisms, though not other means of sealing a door, a container, or other sorts of devices.

**Special:** A creature can spend 10 minutes and attempt an appropriate Craft check (DC 15) to fix
the lock and remove this bonus.
