**Greater Divine Inks**

**Ability:** The alchemist's unique dyes allow him to create lifelike images. If he expends an
additional daily use of his bombs, the image he creates with the divine inks discovery also produces
sound, smell, and heat, as [major image](ref/spell/major-image).

**Prereq:** 6th-level alchemist, [divine inks](ref/discovery/divine-inks),
[improved divine inks](ref/discovery/improved-divine-inks)
