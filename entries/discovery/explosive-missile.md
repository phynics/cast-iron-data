**Explosive Missile**

**Prereq:** 4th-level alchemist

**Standard:** The alchemist can infuse a single arrow, crossbow bolt, or one-handed firearm bullet
with the power of his bomb, load the ammunition, and shoot the ranged weapon. He must be proficient
with the weapon in order to accomplish this.

When the infused ammunition hits its target, it deals damage normally and detonates as if the
alchemist had thrown the bomb at the target. If the _explosive missile_ misses, it does not
detonate.
