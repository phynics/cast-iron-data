**Blinding Bomb**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose for it to detonate very brightly.
Creatures that take a direct hit from a _blinding bomb_ are [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 minute
unless they succeed at a Fortitude save. Creatures in the splash area that fail their saves against
the bomb are [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 minute.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist

**Special:** This is a light effect.
