**&L&Grand Mutagen&FN&**

**Passive:** The alchemist's _mutagen_ now grants a +6 natural armor bonus, a +8 alchemical bonus to
one ability score (Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution), a +6 alchemical bonus to a second physical
ability score, and a +4 alchemical bonus to a third physical ability score. The alchemist takes a -2
penalty to his Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma as long as the _mutagen_ persists.

**Prereq:** 16th-level alchemist, [greater mutagen](ref/discovery/greater-mutagen)
