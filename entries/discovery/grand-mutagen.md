The alchemist's mutagen now grants a +6 natural armor bonus, a +8 alchemical bonus to one ability
score (Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution), a +6 alchemical bonus to a second physical ability
score, and a +4 alchemical bonus to a third physical ability score. The alchemist takes a -2 penalty
to his Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma as long as the mutagen persists (see Mutagen). An
alchemist must be at least 16th level and must possess the
[greater mutagen](ref/discovery/greater-mutagen) discovery before selecting this discovery.
