**Anarchic Bombs**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it deal chaotic divine damage.
Lawful creatures that take a direct hit from an _anarchic bomb_ must succeed at a Fortitude save or
be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) on their next turn. Against neutral creatures, _anarchic bombs_
deal half damage, and such targets are not affected by their staggering effect. _Anarchic bombs_
have no effect on chaotic-aligned creatures.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist
