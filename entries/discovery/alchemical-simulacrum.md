**Alchemical Simulacrum**

**Ability:** The alchemist gains the ability to create a
[lesser simulacrum](ref/spell/simulacrum-lesser). This works like the
_[lesser simulacrum](ref/spell/simulacrum-lesser)_ spell, except it costs 100 gp in alchemical
materials per Hit Die of the simulacrum, requires 24 hours to grow, and decays into inert flesh
rather than ice or snow if killed. The created simulacrum is a creature, not a supernatural effect.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist
