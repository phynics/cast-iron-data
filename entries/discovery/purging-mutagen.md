**Purging Mutagen**

**Ability:** The alchemist's _mutagen_ combines ipecac and other purgative components. A
non-alchemist who consumes the _mutagen_ is [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for only 1 round, during
which time her body rapidly metabolizes or expels all extant toxins, granting her an immediate
second saving throw against each ongoing disease and poison currently affecting her. Each successful
save counts as two consecutive successful saving throws for the purpose of curing the affliction.
Any failed saving throws do not result in additional damage or other effects.

**Special:** A non-alchemist who consumes a second _purging mutagen_ in a 24-hour period is instead
nauseated for 1 hour. When an alchemist drinks the _mutagen,_ he can choose whether or not to also
be affected by this discovery in addition to the _mutagen's_ other effects.
