**Scrap Bomb**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, he can choose to have it explode into shards of
shrapnel that deal piercing damage. A creature that takes a direct hit from a _scrap bomb_ takes 1
point of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage per die of bomb damage unless it succeeds at a Reflex save.

**Prereq:** [Goblin](ref/race/goblin)
