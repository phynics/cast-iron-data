A creature that takes a direct hit from a tanglefoot bomb must save against the bomb's DC or be
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) and glued to the floor as if it had failed its save against a
[tanglefoot bag](ref/eq-weapon/tanglefoot-bag) (see page 160 of the _Core Rulebook_). Creatures in
the splash area that fail their saves are entangled but not glued to the floor; those who make this
save are not entangled at all.
