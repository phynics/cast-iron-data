**Enduring Toxin**

**Ability:** The alchemist can alter the properties of a poison, causing it to persist longer in the
victim's body. Altering a dose of poison in the way takes 1 hour, requires the expenditure of raw
materials worth half the poison's market price, and exposes the alchemist to the poison, thereby
affecting him if he is not immune. This doubles the poison's maximum duration.

**Prereq:** 8th-level alchemist
