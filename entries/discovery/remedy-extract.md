**Remedy Extract**

**Ability:** When the alchemist creates an extract, he can add one nonmagical alchemical remedy
(such as an [antitoxin](ref/eq-misc/antitoxin)) to it. When the extract is consumed, both the
formula and the alchemical remedy take effect.

**Prereq:** 4th-level alchemist

**Special:** The extract has a level 1 higher than normal. This discovery cannot be combined with
other methods of combining multiple consumed items or extracts into one.
