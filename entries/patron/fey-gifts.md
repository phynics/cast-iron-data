**Fey Gifts**

**Ability:** You gain the [charm](ref/hex/charm) hex at 1st level.

**Info:** A powerful fey or other entity from the First World has offered you magic. You serve as an
anchor and scrying focus in the Material Plane for your patron, but you also provide frequent
amusement.

**Passive:** Your fey patron delights in your failure and injury and frequently jinxes you; once
each day, the GM can demand you reroll a single ability check, attack roll, saving throw, or skill
check and use the worse result.

**Spells:** Irresistible dance

**Spells:** Disguise self

**Spells:** Hideous laughter

**Available patron themes:** [Agility](ref/patron/agility), [Enchantment](ref/patron/enchantment),
[Trickery](ref/patron/trickery), [Winter](ref/patron/winter)

**Patron Spells:** This unique patron grants these alternate spells at the listed witch levels.
