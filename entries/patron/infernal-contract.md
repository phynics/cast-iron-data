**Infernal Contract**

**Ability:** You gain the [misfortune](ref/hex/misfortune) hex at 1st level.

**Info:** Your power was brokered from a powerful [daemon](ref/family/daemon),
[demon](ref/family/demon), or [devil](ref/family/devil) to speed the corruption and moral decay of
mortals. When you die, your soul goes to Abaddon, the Abyss, or Hell, and you can't be raised or
resurrected except by extraordinary means - though bold service may earn you a favored position in
damnation.

**Passive:** Your magic requires sacrifice; each day when you prepare spells, you must deal 1d6
points of damage to yourself as part of your infernal ritual, feeding the lost blood to your
familiar.

**Spells:** Planar ally

**Spells:** Greater planar ally\|(your bonus spells can be used to summon only the type of outsider
your patron represents)

**Spells:** Lesser planar ally

**Available patron themes:** [Enchantment](ref/patron/enchantment), [Strength](ref/patron/strength),
[Trickery](ref/patron/trickery), [Vengeance](ref/patron/vengeance)

**Patron Spells:** This unique patron grants these alternate spells at the listed witch levels.

At 5th level: You must deal 2d6 damage to yourself to get your spells.

At 10th level: You must deal 3d6 damage to yourself to get your spells.

At 15th level: You must deal 4d6 damage to yourself to get your spells.

At 20th level: You must deal 5d6 damage to yourself to get your spells.
