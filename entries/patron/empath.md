**Empath**

**Ability:** You gain the [tongues](ref/hex/tongues) hex at 1st level

**Info:** Your patron is a manifestation of the collective living experience - mortal desire and
purpose given direction by the beliefs of countless minds and hearts.

**Passive:** You cannot help but feel the despair, pain, and other negative emotions in those around
you, taking a -5 penalty on saving throws against emotion effects.

**Spells:** Retrocognition

**Spells:** Burst of insight

**Spells:** Analyze aura

**Available patron themes:** [Ancestors](ref/patron/ancestors), [Mind](ref/patron/mind),
[Spirits](ref/patron/spirits), [Wisdom](ref/patron/wisdom)

**Patron Spells:** This unique patron grants these alternate spells at the listed witch levels.
