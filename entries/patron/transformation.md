**Transformation**

**Info:** The Transformation patron grants the following spells at the listed witch levels.

**Spells:** Jump (2nd)

**Spells:** Bear's endurance (4th)

**Spells:** Beast shape I (6th)

**Spells:** Beast shape II (8th)

**Spells:** Beast shape III (10th)

**Spells:** Form of the dragon I (12th)

**Spells:** Form of the dragon II (14th)

**Spells:** Form of the dragon III (16th)

**Spells:** Shapechange (18th)

### Anointings

Witches with transformation patrons can gain a single [anointing](ref/ability/annointings) in place
of a major hex. In each of these cases, the character treats levels in the class used to select the
[anointing](ref/ability/annointings) as his alchemist level for all relevant anointing calculations.
