**Shadowbound**

**Ability:** You gain the [disguise](ref/hex/disguise) hex at 1st level.

**Info:** Deep exposure to entities from the Plane of Shadow - [kytons](ref/family/kyton),
[nightshades](ref/family/nightshade), [owbs](ref/monster/owb), and similar ethereal forces - has
infused you with shadow.

**Passive:** You also gain [light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness).

**Spells:** Shadow walk

**Spells:** Greater shadow evocation

**Spells:** Shadow conjuration

**Available patron themes:** [Death](ref/patron/death), [Deception](ref/patron/deception),
[Ethereal](ref/patron/ethereal), [Shadow](ref/patron/shadow)

**Patron Spells:** This unique patron grants these alternate spells at the listed witch levels.
