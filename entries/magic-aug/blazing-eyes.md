**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 42,000 gp; **Weight** —

The recipient's eyes are blazing orbs of fire. The recipient can cast spells with the fire
descriptor as if they were modified by the Silent Spell and Still Spell feats. This does not
increase their level or casting time. The recipient cannot use this ability if she is
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) or if her eyes are closed. The recipient's eyes shed light like a
[candle](ref/eq-misc/candle); this light cannot be concealed unless the recipient closes or covers
her eyes. The recipient cannot benefit from [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) from any source, even
a racial trait or class feature.

This elemental augmentation cannot be applied to a creature without eyes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), [Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell),
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire) or [scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray), creator must have
13 ranks in Knowledge (planes); **Cost** 21,000 gp
