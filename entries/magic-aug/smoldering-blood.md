**Sources:** Elemental Master's Handbook pg 25

**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** —

The recipient's blood is infused with elemental fire. Anyone within 5 feet of the recipient who
strikes him with a piercing or slashing weapon takes 1d6 points of fire damage from the recipient's
molten blood. The recipient suffers no harm from hot and cold environments as per
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),_ but he takes a -4 penalty on saving throws against
effects that cause [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued) or [exhaustion](ref/misc/exhausted).

This elemental augmentation cannot be applied to a creature without blood.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield), creator must
have 7 ranks in Knowledge (planes); **Cost** 4,000 gp
