**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 62,000 gp; **Weight** —

The recipient's blood is infused with liquid metal that is magically bound to the recipient's
system. When the recipient bleeds, the metal clings to itself and turns a bright silver color. The
recipient automatically [stabilizes](ref/misc/stable) when at negative hit points and is immune to
continuous damage from [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) attacks (including weapons with the
[wounding](ref/magic-enh/wounding) special ability) and
[ability damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) from blood drain. The recipient is considered a
ferrous creature for the purposes of spells and effects such as
[rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp) and a [rust monster's](ref/monster/rust-monster) attack.

This elemental augmentation cannot be applied to a creature without blood.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[adhesive blood](ref/spell/adhesive-blood), [lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser),
creator must have 9 ranks in Knowledge (planes); **Cost** 31,000 gp
