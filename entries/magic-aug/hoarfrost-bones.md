**Aura** strong transmutations; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 85,000 gp; **Weight** —

The recipient's bones are frozen with elemental rime. She gains [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing)
1 in cold weather (below 40° Fahrenheit) or when submerged at least knee deep in water, and she
gains a +1 bonus to her caster level when casting spells with the cold descriptor.

The recipient cannot heal naturally, regardless of the surrounding climate, other than the
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) provided by the hoarfrost bones, although she can still benefit
from magical healing as normal. Additionally, the recipient gains vulnerability to sonic damage.

The recipient must have a skeletal structure to receive this elemental augmentation.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[ice body](ref/spell/ice-body), [simulacrum](ref/spell/simulacrum), creator must have 13 ranks in
Knowledge (planes); **Cost** 42,500 gp
