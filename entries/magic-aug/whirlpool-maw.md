**Sources:** Elemental Master's Handbook pg 25

**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 19,000 gp; **Weight** —

The user's mouth and throat are linked to an extradimensional whirlpool of water. As a full-round
action, the user can swallow an adjacent unattended object up to 10 cubic feet in size. The
swallowed object causes no damage to the user and has no weight; while it is swallowed, the object
is stored within a non-dimensional space similar to that of a
[bag of holding](ref/magic-wondrous/bag-of-holding). The user can spend a full-round action to
retrieve the object, which is unharmed except for being waterlogged. The recipient can store only
one item at a time in his maw. If the whirlpool maw's effect is suppressed or
[dispelled](ref/spell/dispel-magic), the stored item appears instantly. The recipient constantly
dribbles water when his mouth is open and halves the duration he can normally
[hold his breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath).

This elemental augmentation cannot be applied to a creature without a mouth.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[aqueous orb](ref/spell/aqueous-orb), [shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item), creator must have 7
ranks in Knowledge (planes); **Cost** 9,500 gp
