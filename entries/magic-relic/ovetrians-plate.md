**Sources:** Pathfinder \#128: Songbird, Scion, Saboteur\
**Suitable For** PCs of 10th-12th level\
**Aura** strong enchantment\
**Slot** armor; **CL** 15th\
**Weight** 50 lbs.

This storied suit of +3 [rallying](ref/magic-enh/rallying) [full plate](ref/eq-armor/full-plate) was
worn by General Ovetrian over the course of many conquests. It was recorded that General Ovetrian
wore this armor when he was a part of the Fifth Army of Exploration, where he led a group into the
River Kingdoms and places east. Plated in platinum with a slight wave pattern, the armor's surface
bears a resemblance to white fur. The armor's visor is fashioned to look like a male lion's maned
head, and it is hinged so it can be either worn up or pulled down to protect the face. Stories of
the general's battles claim that entire lines of soldiers fled from the general when he rode into
battle.

Once every 10 minutes, the armor's wearer can slam the helm's visor shut as a standard action,
exposing the lion's head design. When he does, he can attempt to demoralize all enemies within 30
feet, and he gains a competence bonus on his [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check equal to the
armor's enhancement bonus to AC. Once per day, instead of attempting to demoralize enemies, he can
take this action to afflict all creatures within 30 feet with [fear](ref/spell/fear) (DC = 20 + 1
per additional triumph attained).

**First Triumph:** Ovetrian's Plate is +4 rallying full plate. A creature demoralized by the armor's
ability is [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for the first round of the effect's duration and is
then [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) as normal for the rest of the duration (if any). Three times per day,
the wearer of the armor can touch an adjacent ally as a standard action to affect that ally with
either [break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment) or
[greater heroism](ref/spell/heroism-greater). This ability can't be used more than once every 10
minutes.

**Second Triumph:** Ovetrian's Plate is +5 rallying full plate. When the wearer uses the armor's
ability to demoralize enemies, he gains a morale bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls equal to the
number of creatures that were demoralized. He also gains
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) of the same amount, which can't be overcome. These
effects last for 1 round, but if the wearer successfully hits a foe suffering from a fear effect,
the duration is extended by 1 round.
