**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** -

An animal totem tattoo is dedicated to a specific animal totem. The bearer gains the totem
transformation ability of a 5th-level [druid](ref/class/druid) with the corresponding animal shaman
archetype ([ape](ref/arc-druid/ape-shaman), [bat](ref/arc-druid/bat-shaman),
[bear](ref/arc-druid/bear-shaman), [boar](ref/arc-druid/boar-shaman),
[dragon](ref/arc-druid/dragon-shaman), [eagle](ref/arc-druid/eagle-shaman),
[lion](ref/arc-druid/lion-shaman), [saurian](ref/arc-druid/saurian-shaman),
[serpent](ref/arc-druid/serpent-shaman), [shark](ref/arc-druid/shark-shaman), or
[wolf](ref/arc-druid/wolf-shaman)). If the bearer already has the corresponding totem transformation
as a class ability, she is instead treated as a druid of 5 levels higher for the purposes of this
ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Inscribe Magical Tattoo](ref/feat/inscribe-magical-tattoo),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1); **Cost** 6,000 gp
