**Sources:** Inner Sea Magic pg 16\
**Aura** varies; **CL** varies\
**Slot** none; **Price** varies; **Weight** -

A spell tattoo is essentially a wearable [scroll](ref/rule/scrolls) inscribed on flesh instead of on
parchment or vellum. These tattoos appear as colorful and intricate patterns rather than magical
writing. The tattoo is a silent, [spell completion](ref/misc/spell-completion) item that only the
bearer can activate. It vanishes when activated. A spell tattoo must be visible to the bearer and
must be touched as part of its activation. These magical tattoos are not normally placed on the
head, neck, or back as a result, since most creatures would require mirrors to activate them. A
spell tattoo's aura and caster level varies as per the scroll it emulates. A spell tattoo has a
market price four times as much as an equivalent scroll.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Inscribe Magical Tattoo](ref/feat/inscribe-magical-tattoo), the spell to be
inscribed; **Cost** varies
