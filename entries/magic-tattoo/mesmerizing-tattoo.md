**Sources:** Blood of Shadows pg 12\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,800 gp; **Weight** -

This shadowy tattoo is drawn in a spiraling pattern down one of the bearer's arms. If the tattoo is
visible, the wearer gains a +2 circumstance bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks.

Whenever the bearer uses the [hypnotism](ref/skill/diplomacy) occult skill unlock, casts an
enchantment spell that targets a single creature, or uses the hypnotic stare class feature, her
target takes a -1 penalty on Will saving throws for the ability's duration and the DC to affect the
target with the hypnotism occult skill unlock is reduced by 1. This is a mind-affecting effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Inscribe Magical Tattoo](ref/feat/inscribe-magical-tattoo),
[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion); **Cost** 5,400 gp
