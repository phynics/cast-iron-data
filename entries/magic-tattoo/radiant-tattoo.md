**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 27\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This tattoo typically takes the form of a colorful sunburst. The bearer can activate the tattoo with
a command word, causing it to flare brilliantly. A single, adjacent foe is
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4 rounds. If it succeeds at a DC 15 Reflex save, it is
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1d4 rounds instead. If the target has
[light blindness](ref/umr/light-blindness), [light sensitivity,](ref/umr/light-sensitivity) or is
otherwise vulnerable to bright light, it is instead blinded for 2d4 rounds or dazzled for 2d4 rounds
on a successful save. If the bearer activates the tattoo while more than one foe is adjacent, she
may choose which foe is affected. The tattoo vanishes once it has been activated.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Inscribe Magical Tattoo](ref/feat/inscribe-magical-tattoo),
[daylight](ref/spell/daylight); **Cost** 750 gp
