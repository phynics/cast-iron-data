**Inspire Courage (Su)**

**At 1st Level:** A bard can use his performance to _inspire courage_ in his allies (including
himself), bolstering them against fear and improving their combat abilities. To be affected, an ally
must be able to perceive the bard's performance. An affected ally receives a +1 morale bonus on
saving throws against charm and fear effects and a +1 competence bonus on attack and weapon damage
rolls.

**Special:** _Inspire courage_ is a mind-affecting ability. _Inspire courage_ can use audible or
visual components. The bard must choose which component to use when starting his performance.

At 7th level: p!These bonuses +2.

At 13th level: p!These bonuses +3.

At 19th level: p!These bonuses +4.
