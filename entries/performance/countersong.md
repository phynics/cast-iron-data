**&L&Countersong (Su)&FN&**

**Action:** At 1st Level

**Special:** _Countersong_ does not work on effects that don't allow saves. _Countersong_ relies on
audible components.

A bard learns to counter magic effects that depend on sound (but not spells that have verbal
components). Each round of the _countersong_ he makes a [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (keyboard,
percussion, wind, string, or sing) skill check. Any creature within 30 feet of the bard (including
the bard himself) that is affected by a sonic or language-dependent magical attack may use the
bard's Perform check result in place of its saving throw if, after the saving throw is rolled, the
Perform check result proves to be higher.

If a creature within range of the _countersong_ is already under the effect of a non-instantaneous
sonic or language-dependent magical attack, it gains another saving throw against the effect each
round it hears the _countersong,_ but it must use the bard's Perform skill check result for the
save.
