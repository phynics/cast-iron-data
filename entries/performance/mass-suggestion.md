This ability functions just like _[suggestion](ref/performance/suggestion),_ but allows a bard of
18th level or higher to make a _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ simultaneously to any number of
creatures that he has already fascinated. _Mass suggestion_ is an enchantment (compulsion),
mind-affecting, language-dependent ability that relies on audible components.
