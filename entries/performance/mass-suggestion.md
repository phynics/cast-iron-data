**Mass Suggestion (Sp)**

**At 18th Level:** This ability functions just like [suggestion](ref/performance/suggestion), but
allows a bard to make a _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ simultaneously to any number of
creatures that he has already _fascinated._

**Special:** _Mass suggestion_ is an enchantment (compulsion), mind-affecting, language-dependent
ability that relies on audible components.
