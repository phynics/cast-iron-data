**Deadly Performance (Su)**

**At 20th Level:** A bard can use his performance to cause one enemy to die from joy or sorrow. To
be affected, the target must be able to see and hear the bard perform for 1 full round and be within
30 feet. The target receives a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 the bard's level + the bard's Cha modifier) to
negate the effect. If a creature's saving throw succeeds, the target is
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d4 rounds, and the bard cannot use _deadly performance_ on that
creature again for 24 hours. If a creature's saving throw fails, it dies.

**Special:** _Deadly performance_ is a mind-affecting death effect that relies on audible and visual
components.
