**Inspire Competence (Su)**

**At 1st Level:** A bard can use his performance to help an ally succeed at a task. That ally must
be within 30 feet and be able to hear the bard. The ally gets a +2 competence bonus on skill checks
with a particular skill as long as she continues to hear the bard's performance.

**Special:** Certain uses of this ability are infeasible, such as Stealth, and may be disallowed at
the GM's discretion. A bard can't _inspire competence_ in himself. _Inspire competence_ relies on
audible components.

At 7th level: This bonus +3.

At 11th level: This bonus +4.

At 15th level: This bonus +5.

At 19th level: This bonus +6.
