At 1st level, a bard can use his performance to counter magic effects that depend on sight. Each
round of the distraction, he makes a [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (act, comedy, dance, or
oratory) skill check. Any creature within 30 feet of the bard (including the bard himself) that is
affected by an illusion (pattern) or illusion (figment) magical attack may use the bard's Perform
check result in place of its saving throw if, after the saving throw is rolled, the Perform skill
check proves to be higher. If a creature within range of the distraction is already under the effect
of a noninstantaneous illusion (pattern) or illusion (figment) magical attack, it gains another
saving throw against the effect each round it sees the distraction, but it must use the bard's
Perform skill check result for the save. Distraction does not work on effects that don't allow
saves. Distraction relies on visual components.
