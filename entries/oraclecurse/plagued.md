**Plagued**

**Benefit:** You are immune to the [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) condition.

**Flavor:** You suffer from minor ailments and sicknesses. While you struggle to resist new
diseases, you have grown accustomed to the many inconveniences of sickness.

**At 10th Level:** Increase the save DC of any disease effect you create by +2, and you raise the
number of saves required to recover naturally from these diseases by 1.

**At 15th Level:** You are immune to the effects of disease and
[infestations](ref/rule/infestations), but you can still function as a carrier for sickness.

**At 5th Level:** Add [pox pustules](ref/spell/pox-pustules) to your list of 2nd-level oracle spells
known.

**Penalty:** You take a -1 penalty on all saving throws against disease or infestation effects.
