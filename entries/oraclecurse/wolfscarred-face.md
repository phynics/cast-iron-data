**Wolfscarred Face**

**Benefit:** You gain a [natural bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals 1d4 points of
damage if you are a Medium creature or 1d3 points of damage if you are Small.

**Flavor:** Your face is deformed, as though you were born with a wolf's muzzle instead of an
ordinary nose and jaw. Many mistake you for a werewolf, and in areas plagued by lycanthropes, you
must take pains to hide your face.

**At 10th Level:** The damage dealt by your bite attack increases to 1d8 if you are Medium or 1d6 if
you are Small.

**At 15th Level:** You add [greater magic fang](ref/spell/magic-fang-greater) to your list of known
spells and the damage dealt by your bite attack increases to 2d6 if you are Medium or 1d8 if you are
Small.

**At 5th Level:** You add [magic fang](ref/spell/magic-fang) to your list of known spells and your
bite damage increases to 1d6 if you are Medium or 1d4 if you are Small.

**Penalty:** You have a severe speech impediment, and any spells you cast with a verbal component
have a 20% chance of failing, wasting your action but not expending the spell.
