**Hunger**

**Benefit:** You gain a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) that deals an amount of piercing
damage appropriate for your size (1d8 for a Large creature, 1d6 for Medium, 1d4 for Small) as a
secondary natural attack.

**Flavor:** Ravenous hunger wracks your body in stressful situations.

**At 10th Level:** Your bite attack deals damage as if you were
[one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments).

**At 15th Level:** You gain the [blood drain](ref/umr/blood-drain) monster special ability with your
bite, dealing 1d2 points of [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) at the end of a
turn if you grapple a foe.

**At 5th Level:** You gain the [grab](ref/umr/grab) monster special ability on bite attacks against
creatures smaller than yourself.

**Penalty:** You gain none of the benefits from spells or magic items that provide nourishment, such
as [goodberry](ref/spell/goodberry), [heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast), or a
[ring of sustenance](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-sustenance). You begin each combat with the
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) condition until you deal damage with your bite attack (see below).
