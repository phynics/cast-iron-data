**Scourge**

**Benefit:** Your movement is unimpeded by undergrowth, as a druid's
[woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride) ability, as plants wither away in your footsteps.

**Flavor:** You are a blight on the natural world.

**At 10th Level:** Add [explosion of rot](ref/spell/explosion-of-rot) to your list of 4th-level
oracle spells known.

**At 15th Level:** Add [swarm skin](ref/spell/swarm-skin) to your list of 6th-level oracle spells
known.

**At 5th Level:** Add [feast of ashes](ref/spell/feast-of-ashes) to your list of 2nd-lvel oracle
spells known.

**Penalty:** Bizarre natural hazards develop within a 300-foot radius around you whenever you linger
in a natural area for more than 10 minutes, as
[lesser curse terrain](ref/spell/curse-terrain-lesser). You cannot choose the hazards or their
locations.
