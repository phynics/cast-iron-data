**Blackened**

**Benefit:** Add [burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands) to your list of spells known.

**Flavor:** Your hands and forearms are shriveled and blackened, as if you had plunged your arms
into a blazing fire, and your thin, papery skin is sensitive to the touch.

**At 10th Level:** Add [wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire) to your list of spells known and your
penalty on weapon attack rolls is reduced to -2.

**At 15th Level:** Add [delayed blast fireball](ref/spell/delayed-blast-fireball) to your list of
spells known.

**At 5th Level:** Add [scorching ray](ref/spell/scorching-ray) and
[flaming sphere](ref/spell/flaming-sphere) to your list of spells known.

**Penalty:** You take a -4 penalty on weapon attack rolls.
