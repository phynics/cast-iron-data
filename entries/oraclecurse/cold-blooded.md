**Cold-Blooded**

**Benefit:** You can survive without food four times longer than a typical creature of your species
before you begin to starve.

**Flavor:** Your blood turns sluggish without sufficient heat, and you must seek warmth and shelter
earlier than most.

**At 10th Level:** Whenever an action you have [readied](ref/rule/ready) is triggered, you can take
an additional move action at half your speed - along with your readied action - even if you have
already taken a move action that round.

**At 15th Level:** You can take an additional move action a number of times per day equal to your
Charisma modifier. You can take these additional move actions only on your turn, and can take only
one of these extra move actions per turn.

**At 5th Level:** Poisons affecting you have their onset time doubled and last half as long if they
end after a set time (for example, a poison with a frequency of 1/round for 6 rounds would instead
have a frequency of 1/round for 3 rounds). Poisons with no onset time gain an onset time of 1 round.

**Special:** This oracle curse is common among [lizardfolk](ref/monster/lizardfolk) and other
oracles with the reptilian subtype.

**Penalty:** You take a -4 penalty on saves against cold spells and effects, and whenever you fail
such a saving throw, you are [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round.
