**Legalistic**

**Benefit:** Once per day, you can make a vow to yourself that grants a +4 morale bonus on any one
roll you make while trying to fulfill a promise made to another individual.

**Flavor:** The shackles of Hell impose savage consequences should you violate a covenant, but also
imbue you with remarkable guile.

**At 10th Level:** You can make a new saving throw each minute to resist mind-affecting effects as
your subconscious searches for loopholes.

**At 15th Level:** Any creature that violates its freely given word to you takes a penalty to AC, to
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance), and on saving throws against your attacks and
abilities equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) for 24 hours.

**At 5th Level:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy),
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks while talking
to an individual one-on-one.

**Penalty:** Whenever you break your word (either purposefully or unintentionally), you become
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 24 hours or until you meet your obligation, whichever comes first.
