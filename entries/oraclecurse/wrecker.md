**Wrecker**

**Benefit:** [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) becomes a class skill for you and you can
make Disable Device checks to destroy nonmagical traps as a **move action** without the need to use
tools or take any action beyond simply touching it.

**Flavor:** The destructive power of [the Abyss](ref/rule/the-abyss) and its teeming hordes of
[demons](ref/family/demon) seeps from your very pores and into your belongings and surroundings.

**At 10th Level:** Any attacks you make against objects and constructs automatically bypass any
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) they may possess except epic.

**At 15th Level:** Whenever you are dealt damage by an attack with a manufactured weapon, you can
require the weapon's wielder to make a Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 your oracle level + your Charisma
modifier) to avoid having the weapon collapse into dust immediately after striking you (magical
weapons receive an additional saving throw against this effect).

**At 5th Level:** Whenever you attempt to damage an object with a melee attack, reduce its
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) by an amount equal to your oracle level before determining the damage
you deal with that attack.

**Penalty:** Held objects gain the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition when you use or equip them
but regain their actual condition if employed by anyone else. If a held item is restored to unbroken
condition, it becomes broken again the following round.
