**Reclusive**

**Benefit:** Instantaneous spells you cast only on yourself affect you as though your caster level
were 1 higher.

**Flavor:** You are reclusive and paranoid to the point that your allies cannot easily help you in
times of stress or unease.

**At 10th Level:** You are immune to charm spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 15th Level:** You gain [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) equal to 10 + your oracle
level.

**At 5th Level:** Any spells you cast only on yourself affect you as if they were modified by the
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) feat. This does not increase their level or casting time.

**Penalty:** Whenever you are in combat, your allies must succeed at a melee touch attack to affect
you with touch spells, and you must attempt saving throws to resist all spells cast by anyone other
than yourself, even those cast by allies.
