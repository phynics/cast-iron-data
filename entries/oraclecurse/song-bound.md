**Song-Bound**

**Benefit:** You can perform the [countersong](ref/performance/countersong) bardic performance
(using [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) \[sing\] only) for a number of rounds per day equal to your
oracle level. If you gain the bardic performance class feature, these rounds are in addition to any
other bardic performance rounds you gain, but they can be used only for the _countersong_ bardic
performance.

**Flavor:** Whenever you speak, you are compelled to sing loudly, and you draw out even short
utterances with vocal scales or simple melodies.

**At 10th Level:** Add 1 to the DC of saving throws against language-dependent spells you cast.

**At 15th Level:** Add [greater command](ref/spell/command-greater) and
[mass suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion-mass) to your list of spells known.

**At 5th Level:** Add [command](ref/spell/command) and [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion) to your
list of spells known.

**Prereq:** [Merfolk](ref/race/merfolk)

**Penalty:** You cannot speak in less than a loud voice, although you may choose not to speak.
