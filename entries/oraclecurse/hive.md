**Hive**

**Benefit:** Once per day as a **standard action**, you can cough up an ovoid, leathery sphere that
can be hurled as a flask of [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid), though it becomes inert after 24 hours.

**Flavor:** You were exposed at a young age (perhaps even before you were born) to the alien species
known as the [hive](ref/family/hive), but this exposure did not result in a full-blown infestation
by these otherworldly monsters.

**At 10th Level:** Your skin deformities grow numerous enough to increase your natural armor bonus
by 1.

**At 15th Level:** You gain acid [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 20.

**At 5th Level:** You gain the benefits of [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) in hot
environments and are immune to [infestations](ref/rule/infestations) (save for those from the hive
itself).

**Penalty:** Others (including animals) find the unsightly spines and rough growths that
periodically emerge from your flesh to be disturbing and distracting, and you take a -4 penalty on
Bluff, Diplomacy, Handle Animal, and Ride checks.
