**Acursed**

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 bonus to all saving throws against [curse](ref/spelldef/curse) effects.

**Flavor:** You are cursed with misfortune and sorrow.

**At 10th Level:** Add [greater brand](ref/spell/brand-greater) to your list of spells known.

**At 15th Level:** You are immune to curse effects except for your own oracle curse.

**At 5th Level:** Add [ill omen](ref/spell/ill-omen) to your list of spells known.

**Penalty:** You cannot gain benefit from morale bonuses.
