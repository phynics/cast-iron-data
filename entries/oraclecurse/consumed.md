**Consumed**

**Benefit:** You automatically [stabilize](ref/misc/stable) when brought below 0 hit points.

**Flavor:** Your connection to Abaddon and the [daemons](ref/family/daemon) that call it home has
left you diminished but still able to scorn many mortal frailties.

**At 10th Level:** You can go without food or water for a number of days equal to your oracle level
before suffering any ill effects due to [starvation](ref/rule/starvation-and-thirst) or thirst.

**At 15th Level:** Whenever a creature within 30 feet takes damage while in combat, you
automatically gain 1 temporary hit point. You can benefit from this ability a number of times per
round equal to your Charisma modifier, and you can gain a total number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) in this way equal to your oracle level.
Temporary hit points gained this way disappear after 1 hour.

**At 5th Level:** Whenever you make a saving throw against a disease or poison effect, roll twice
and use the higher roll as your result.

**Penalty:** Whenever you take lethal hit point damage, you take an additional number of points of
nonlethal damage equal to 1/2 the lethal damage you took.
