**Wrecking Mysticism**

**Benefit:** Whenever you would gain a mystery spell, you can gain
[Magical Tail](ref/feat/magical-tail) as a bonus feat instead. Once this choice has been made, it
cannot be changed. You cannot replace a bonus spell granted to you by an oracle archetype with
Magical Tail, even if it replaces a mystery spell.

**Flavor:** A divine entity has blessed you with a source of eldritch power.

**At 10th Level:** Add [magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar) to your list of spells known.

**At 15th Level:** Add [project image](ref/spell/project-image) to your list of spells known.

**At 5th Level:** Add [minor image](ref/spell/minor-image) and
[ventriloquism](ref/spell/ventriloquism).

**Prereq:** [Kitsune](ref/race/kitsune)

**Penalty:** This power erodes your fortitude, increasing by 50% the duration of any poison,
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) condition, or [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) condition affecting you.
