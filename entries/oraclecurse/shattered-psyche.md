**Shattered Psyche**

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 competence bonus on saving throws made against mind-affecting effects.

**Flavor:** Your mind is crowded with dozens of voices, fragmented snippets of your past lives.

**At 10th Level:** You're immune to compulsion effects.

**At 15th Level:** You're immune to all mind-affecting effects.

**At 5th Level:** You're [immune](ref/umr/immunity) to charm effects.

**Penalty:** You take a -2 penalty on all Intelligence-based skill checks, Wisdom-based skill
checks, and [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks.
