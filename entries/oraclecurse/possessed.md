**Possessed**

**Benefit:** Whenever a foe attempts to use an effect to possess or dominate you and the effect
allows a saving throw to negate, you may roll twice and take the better of the two results.

**Flavor:** Another mind shares your body, interfering with your control, and it's more difficult
for you to concentrate as a result.

**At 10th Level:** Add [possession](ref/spell/possession) to your list of 5th-level oracle spells
known.

**At 15th Level:** Add [greater object possession](ref/spell/object-possession-greater) to your list
of 7th-level oracle spells known.

**At 5th Level:** If you become [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) or [stunned](ref/misc/stunned), you can
choose instead to become [confused](ref/misc/confused) for twice the duration the daze or stun
effect would have normally lasted.

**Penalty:** You take a -2 penalty on all [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks, and
concentrating to maintain a spell duration provokes attacks of opportunity.
