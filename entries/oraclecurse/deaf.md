**Deaf**

**Benefit:** You cast all of your spells as if they were modified by the
[Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell) feat. This does not increase their level or casting time.

**Flavor:** You cannot hear.

**At 10th Level:** You gain [scent](ref/umr/scent) and you do not suffer any penalty on initiative
checks due to being deaf.

**At 15th Level:** You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) out to a range of 30 feet.

**At 5th Level:** You receive a +3 competence bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks
that do not rely upon hearing, and the initiative penalty for being deaf is reduced to -2.

**Penalty:** You suffer all of the usual penalties for being [deafened](ref/misc/deafened).
