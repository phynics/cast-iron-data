**Ghoul**

**Benefit:** If you feed on fresh, raw flesh (no older than 1 hour) from a creature of your own
race, you gain a +1 morale bonus on all saving throws for 24 hours.

**Flavor:** While your actual dietary needs don't change, you crave the flesh of sentient creatures.

**At 10th Level:** You become immune to paralysis and disease.

**At 15th Level:** You gain the effects of [heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast) whenever you feed
on the raw flesh of a sentient creature, and the effects of this
_[heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast)_ double if the flesh you eat comes from a member of your
own race (this effect applies no more often than once per day).

**At 5th Level:** You add [ghoul touch](ref/spell/ghoul-touch) to your list of 2nd-level oracle
spells known.

**Penalty:** You can only go without food for 12 hours before you begin to
[starve](ref/rule/starvation-and-thirst), and when you do starve, the DC of the Constitution check
to avoid taking damage increases by 5.
