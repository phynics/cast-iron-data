**Shadowbound**

**Benefit:** You gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to a range of 30 feet.

**Flavor:** Your pigmentation is oddly colorless, and your eyes are highly sensitive to light.

**At 10th Level:** Add [shadow conjuration](ref/spell/shadow-conjuration) to your list of 4th-level
oracle spells known and [shadow evocation](ref/spell/shadow-evocation) to your list of 5th-level
oracle spells known.

**At 15th Level:** Add [shadow walk](ref/spell/shadow-walk) to your list of 6th-level oracle spells
known.

**At 5th Level:** The range of your [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) increases by 30 feet.

**Penalty:** You are [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round when exposed to normal or bright light
and [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) while in such a lit area.
