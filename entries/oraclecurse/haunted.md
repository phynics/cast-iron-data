**Haunted**

**Benefit:** Add [mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand) and [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound) to your
list of spells known.

**Flavor:** Malevolent spirits follow you wherever you go, causing minor mishaps and strange
occurrences (such as unexpected breezes, small objects moving on their own, and faint noises).

**At 10th Level:** Add [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis) to your list of spells known.

**At 15th Level:** Add [reverse gravity](ref/spell/reverse-gravity) to your list of spells known.

**At 5th Level:** Add [levitate](ref/spell/levitate) and [minor image](ref/spell/minor-image) to
your list of spells known.

**Penalty:** Retrieving any stored item from your gear requires a **standard action**, unless it
would normally take longer. Any item you drop lands 10 feet away from you in a random direction.
