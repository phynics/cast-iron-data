**Demonic**

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on all [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. You gain Bluff or Intimidate (choose one) as a class
skill.

**Flavor:** Your heart is cursed with the pull of the Abyss.

**At 10th Level:** You gain immunity to poison.

**At 15th Level:** Any weapon you wield is treated as chaotic and evil for the purposes of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 5th Level:** You gain a +4 bonus to all saving throws against fear effects.

**Penalty:** You cannot cast spells with the good or lawful descriptors, nor can you summon good or
lawful creatures. Good and lawful creatures instinctively distrust you, and you take a -4 penalty on
all [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks against such creatures. Diplomacy is not a class skill
for you.
