**Tongues**

**Benefit:** You gain the selected language as a bonus language.

**Flavor:** In times of stress or unease, you speak in tongues.

**At 10th Level:** You can understand any spoken language, as if under the effects of
[tongues](ref/spell/tongues), even during combat.

**At 15th Level:** You can speak and understand any language, but your speech is still restricted
during combat.

**At 5th Level:** Pick an additional language to speak in combat and add it to your list of known
languages.

**Penalty:** Pick one of the following languages: Abyssal, Aklo, Aquan, Auran, Celestial, Ignan,
Infernal, or Terran. Whenever you are in combat, you can only speak and understand the selected
language. This does not interfere with spellcasting, but it does apply to spells that are
[language dependent](ref/spelldef/language-dependent).
