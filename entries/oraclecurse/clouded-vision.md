**Clouded Vision**

**Benefit:** You can see as if you had [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 30 ft.

**Flavor:** Your eyes are obscured, making it difficult for you to see.

**At 10th Level:** You gain [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) out to a range of 30 feet.

**At 15th Level:** You gain [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) out to a range of 15 feet.

**At 5th Level:** This distance increases to 60 feet.

**Penalty:** You cannot see anything beyond 30 feet.
