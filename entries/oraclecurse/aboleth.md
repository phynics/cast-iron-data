**Aboleth**

**Benefit:** Add [charm person](ref/spell/charm-person) and [hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism) to your
list of 1st-level oracle spells known.

**Flavor:** Your mind is marked by [aboleth](ref/monster/alghollthu-aboleth) tampering, either as a
result of a close encounter with one of these creatures while you were a child, or even through an
ancestor, close family member, or one of your sisters or brothers in faith - this associate's
interaction with the aboleths may be all that it takes to infect you.

**At 10th Level:** Add [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory) to your list of 4th-level oracle
spells known.

**At 15th Level:** Add [veil](ref/spell/veil) to your list of 6th-level oracle spells known.

**At 5th Level:** Add [minor image](ref/spell/minor-image) to your list of 2nd-level oracle spells
known.

**Penalty:** You take a -2 penalty on saving throws against mind-affecting effects.
