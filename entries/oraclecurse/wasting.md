**Wasting**

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 competence bonus on saves made against disease.

**Flavor:** Your body is slowly rotting away.

**At 10th Level:** You gain immunity to disease.

**At 15th Level:** You are immune to the [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) condition.

**At 5th Level:** You are immune to the [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) condition (but not nauseated).

**Penalty:** You take a -4 penalty on Charisma-based skill checks, except for
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate).
