**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 77

Beyond the veil of the mundane hide the secrets of absolute power. The works of beings beyond
mortals, the legends of realms where gods and spirits tread, the lore of creations both wondrous and
terrible - such mysteries call to those with the ambition and the intellect to rise above the common
folk to grasp true might. Such is the path of the wizard. These shrewd magic-users seek, collect,
and covet esoteric knowledge, drawing on cultic arts to work wonders beyond the abilities of mere
mortals. While some might choose a particular field of magical study and become masters of such
powers, others embrace versatility, reveling in the unbounded wonders of all magic. In either case,
wizards prove a cunning and potent lot, capable of smiting their foes, empowering their allies, and
shaping the world to their every desire.

**Role:** While universalist wizards might study to prepare themselves for any manner of danger,
specialist wizards research schools of magic that make them exceptionally skilled within a specific
focus. Yet no matter their specialty, all wizards are masters of the impossible and can aid their
allies in overcoming any danger.

**Alignment:** Any

**Hit Die:** d6

**Starting Wealth:** 2d6 × 10 gp (average 70 gp)

**Skill Points per Level:** 2 + Int modifier

**Class Skills (Dex):** Fly

**Class Skills (Int):** Appraise, Craft (any), Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (dungeoneering),
Knowledge (engineering), Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (history), Knowledge (local), Knowledge
(nature), Knowledge (nobility), Knowledge (planes), Knowledge (religion), Linguistics, Spellcraft

**Class Skills (Wis):** Profession (any)

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Weapons:** club, dagger, heavy crossbow, light crossbow, quarterstaff

**Armor:** No armor

**Shields:** No

**Notes:** Armor interferes with a wizard's movements, which can cause his spells with somatic
components to fail.

**Bonus Languages**

**Ability:** You may substitute Draconic for one of the bonus languages available to you because of
your race.

**Spells**

**Spell list:** Arcane spells from the wizard list, from 1st level

**Key ability:** Intelligence. Minimum score 10 + spell level; save DC 10 + spell level +
Intelligence modifier; a high score grants bonus spells per day.

**Casting:** Prepared: choose and prepare spells in advance.

**Notes:** Prepares spells after 8 hours of rest and 1 hour with the spellbook; no limit on spells
known.

**Cantrips**

**Ability:** You can prepare a number of cantrips, or 0-level arcane spells, each day. These spells
are cast like any other but they are not expended when cast and may be used again. You can prepare a
cantrip from a prohibited school, but it uses up two of your available slots.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Spellbooks**

**Ability:** You must study your spellbook each day to prepare your spells. You can't prepare any
spell not recorded in your spellbook, except for [read magic](ref/spell/read-magic) (which all
wizards can prepare from memory).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** You begin play with a spellbook containing all 0-level wizard spells (except those from
your prohibited schools, if any; see _Arcane Schools_) plus three 1st-level spells of your choice.
You also select a number of additional 1st-level spells equal to your Intelligence modifier to add
to the spellbook. At each new wizard level, you gain two new spells of any spell level or levels
that you can cast (based on your new wizard level) for your spellbook. At any time, you can also add
spells found in wizards' spellbooks to your own (see
[Arcane Magical Writing](ref/rule/arcane-magical-writing)).

**Arcane Bond (Ex or Sp)**

**Choice:** This bond can take one of two forms: a familiar or a bonded object. Once you make this
choice, it is permanent and cannot be changed.

**Flavor:** At 1st level, wizards form a powerful bond with an object or a creature.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Familiar**

**Ability:** A [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) is a magical pet that enhances your skills and
senses and can aid you in magic. The full rules for familiars can be found
[here](ref/ability/familiars).

**Bonded Object**

**Action:** Ability

If you select a bonded object, you begin play with one at no cost. Objects that are the subject of
an arcane bond must fall into one of the following categories: amulet, ring, staff, wand, or weapon.
These objects are always masterwork quality. Weapons acquired at 1st level are not made of any
special material. If the object is an amulet or ring, it must be worn to have effect, while staves,
wands, and weapons must be wielded. If you attempt to cast a spell without your bonded object worn
or in hand, you must make a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check or lose the spell. The DC
for this check is equal to 20 + the spell's level. If the object is a ring or amulet, it occupies
the [ring or neck slot](ref/rule/magic-items-on-the-body) accordingly.

A bonded object can be used once per day to cast any one spell that you have in your spellbook and
are capable of casting, even if the spell is not prepared. This spell is treated like any other
spell cast by you, including casting time, duration, and other effects dependent on your level. This
spell cannot be modified by metamagic feats or other abilities. The bonded object cannot be used to
cast spells from your opposition schools.

You can add additional magic abilities to your bonded object as if you have the required item
creation feats and if you meet the level prerequisites of the feat. For example, a wizard with a
bonded dagger must be at least 5th level to add magic abilities to the dagger (see
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor)). If the bonded object is a wand,
it loses its wand abilities when its last charge is consumed, but it is not destroyed and it retains
all of its bonded object properties and can be used to craft a new wand. The magic properties of a
bonded object, including any magic abilities added to the object, only function for the wizard who
owns it. If a bonded object's owner dies, or the item is replaced, the object reverts to being an
ordinary masterwork item of the appropriate type.

If a bonded object is damaged, it is restored to full hit points the next time you prepare your
spells. If the object of an arcane bond is lost or destroyed, it can be replaced after 1 week in a
special ritual that costs 200 gp per wizard level plus the cost of the masterwork item. This ritual
takes 8 hours to complete. Items replaced in this way do not possess any of the additional
enchantments of the previous bonded item. You can designate an existing magic item as your bonded
item. This functions in the same way as replacing a lost or destroyed item except that the new magic
item retains its abilities while gaining the benefits and drawbacks of becoming a bonded item.

**Arcane School**

**Action:** Choice

**Flavor:** A wizard can choose to specialize in one school of magic, gaining additional spells and
powers based on that school.

**Section:** Arcane School

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** This choice must be made at 1st level, and once made, it cannot be changed. If you
choose not to specialize, you receive the [universalist](ref/arcaneschool/universalist) school
instead; a universalist wizard has no opposition schools and thus can prepare spells from any school
without restriction, but he does not receive a school slot.

If you choose to specialize in one school of magic, you must select two other schools as your
**opposition schools**, representing knowledge sacrificed in one area of arcane lore to gain mastery
in another. If you prepare spells from your opposition schools, you must use two spell slots of that
level to prepare the spell. For example, a wizard with evocation as an opposition school must expend
two of his available 3rd-level spell slots to prepare a [fireball](ref/spell/fireball). In addition,
you take a -4 penalty on any skill checks made when crafting a magic item that has a spell from one
of your opposition schools as a prerequisite.

Each arcane school gives you a number of school powers. In addition, you receive an additional spell
slot of each spell level you can cast, from 1st on up. Each day, you can prepare a spell from your
specialty school in that slot. This spell must be in your spellbook. You can select a spell modified
by a metamagic feat to prepare in your school slot, but it uses up a higher-level spell slot.

Here is the [list of arcane schools](ref/ability/arcane-schools). They include other options, such
as _focused schools_ and _elemental schools._

**Scribe Scroll**

**Ability:** you gain [Scribe Scroll](ref/feat/scribe-scroll) as a bonus feat.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Bonus Feats**

**Ability:** You gain a bonus feat from the following choices:
[Spell Mastery](ref/feat/spell-mastery), [Eldritch Aid](ref/feat/eldritch-aid),
[Planned Spontaneity](ref/feat/planned-spontaneity), any metamagic feat, any item creation feat, or
any familiar feat. You must meet all prerequisites for a bonus feat, including caster level
minimums.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Repeat:** You gain another bonus feat from the given choices. You still need to meet its
prerequisites., 10, 5

**Arcane Discoveries:** Wizards spend much of their lives seeking deeper truths, hunting knowledge
as if it were life itself. The wizard's power is not necessarily the spells he wields; spells are
merely the outward, most visible manifestation of that power. A wizard's true power is in his fierce
intelligence, his dedication to his craft, and his ability to peel back the surface truths of
reality to understand the fundamental underpinnings of existence. A wizard spends much of his time
researching spells, and would rather find an undiscovered library than a room full of gold. A wizard
need not be a reclusive bookworm, but he must have a burning curiosity for the unknown. Arcane
discoveries are the results of this obsession with magic. A wizard can learn one of these arcane
discoveries in place of a regular feat or wizard bonus feat.

### Alternate Capstones

**Source** Chronicle of Legends pg. 28\
When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Well-Prepared (Su)**

At 20th level, the wizard has a spell for every occasion he can imagine - and a few he can't. The
wizard gains two additional cantrips per day, six additional 1st- and 2nd-level spell slots, four
additional 3rd- and 4th-level spell slots, two additional 5th-level spell slots, and one additional
6th-level spell slot.

**Also available:** Walking Library, Arch-Familiar

### Archetypes

**Source** Advanced Player's Guide pg. 72\
From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.

**Arcane Bomber**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Cantrips; Arcane School

**Description:** To many wizards, the experimentation of the alchemist seems quaint, if not
dangerous or frightening. A few wizards take up the secrets of the bomb, however, fusing alchemy
with their already considerable magical power.

**Arcane Physician**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane School; Scribe Scroll

**Description:** Arcane physicians use their scholarly knowledge and arcane mastery to achieve
incredible feats of medicine.

**Arcane Warden**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane School; Class Skills; Scribe Scroll; Bonus Feats; Hand of the
Apprentice; Metamagic Mastery

**Description:** Arcane wardens are wizards who specialize in the discovery or generation of places
of safety and refuge.

**Bonded Wizard**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Scribe Scroll; 5th, 10th, and 15th-level Bonus Feats

**Description:** Many wizards form an arcane bond with an item, but for some this bond becomes a
powerful mystic union.

**Chronomancer**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; 10th, 15th, 20th-level Bonus Feats

**Description:** Rare arcane scholars known as chronomancers demonstrate the ability to shift
themselves in short bursts between the past, future, and alternate presents.

**Clocksmith**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School; Scribe Scroll; Bonus Feats

**Description:** Clocksmiths are oddities among wizard communities, pursuing the study and creation
of clockwork constructs to near obsession

**Cruoromancer**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th-level Bonus Feat

**Description:** (Dhampir Only) A cruoromancer infuses his necromantic magic with the power of his
unique mixture of living blood and undead ichor.

**Elder Mythos Scholar**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; 1st, 8th-level Arcane School Ability; Spellbook; Scribe
Scroll; 5th, 10th-level Bonus feats

**Description:** Elder Mythos scholars risk their sanity to seek knowledge of alien and awful
entities from beyond the stars.

**Exploiter Wizard**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School

**Description:** Contrary to traditional wizardly study, an exploiter wizard forgoes the tried and
true methods of arcane focus and arcane schools for the exploits favored by an arcanist.

**Familiar Adept**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Scribe Scroll; 5th and 10th-level Bonus Feats; Arcane Bond; Spellbook

**Description:** Many wizards employ familiars to assist them, but only a few have unlocked the true
power of their school of magic through the familiar itself.

**First World Caller**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School; Scribe Scroll; 10th, 15th-level Bonus Feats

**Description:** Some wizards, typically First World gnomes, have mystical ties with the First
World, the primordial home of the fey

**Hallowed Necromancer**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane School; Spells; Power Over Undead; Grave Touch; Scribe Scroll; 5th,
10th, 15th-level Bonus Feat

**Description:** Many wizards study necromancy to create undead, but some study the same arts to
purge the stain of undeath.

**Instructor**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; 5th, 10th, 15th, and 20th-level Bonus Feats

**Description:** Whether serving as staff at a formal school of magic or simply acting as a
wandering teacher, an instructor has an apprentice who trades service to the instructor for lessons
in magic.

**Pact Wizard (FF)**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School

**Description:** Some wizards make bargains with beings from other realms in order to gain arcane
power. These pact wizards have unparalleled access to extraplanar allies, but these bonds never come
without strings attached.

**Pact Wizard (HHH)**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spellcasting; Scribe Scroll; 5th, 10th, 15th, and 20th level Bonus Feats

**Description:** Wizards who seek mastery of arcane power without tedious study and monotonous
research.

**Poleiheira Adherent**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School

**Description:** Poleiheira adherents are wizards who wish to emulate the explorations and
discoveries of Arustun and partake in great odysseys as they seek to imitate his achievements.

**Primalist**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond, 5th-level Bonus Feat, 10th-level Bonus Feat

**Description:** A primalist is a wizard who has spent a considerable amount of time studying the
chaos that is primal magic.

**Runesage**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School

**Description:** Runesages draw upon the mystic energies of ancient Thassilon.

**Scroll Scholar**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Diviner's Fortune (diviner) or Hand of the Apprentice (universalist),
5th-level Bonus Feat, 4th-level spell slot

**Description:** Those who trade some of their potential to better understand ancient texts and
scrolls can become learned scroll scholars.

**Scrollmaster**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; 10th-level Bonus Feat

**Description:** To some wizards, a scroll is not just a written form of a spell, it is a physical
weapon meant to be used in combat like a sword or a shield.

**Shadowcaster**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; 5th-level Bonus Feat; 10th-level Bonus Feat

**Description:** Trained in the dark mysteries of Nidal's Umbral Court, the shadowcaster harnesses
the power of shadows to bolster their spellcasting.

**Siege Mage**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Scribe Scroll; Arcane Bond; Cantrips; Arcane School

**Description:** The siege mage combines his arcane mastery with a supernatural link to siege
engines.

**Spell Sage**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School

**Description:** A spell sage has mastered spells of all types, and is able to increase the
effectiveness of his own spells and eventually even cast spells from other classes' spell lists.

**Spellbinder**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond

**Description:** (Elf Only) A spellbinder is an elven wizard who forges an arcane bond between
himself and one or more wizard spells.

**Spellslinger**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Scribe Scroll; Cantrips; Arcane School

**Description:** While few contest the seductive allure of commanding arcane and occult powers,
there are those wizards who become obsessed with the natural mysteries of black powder.

**Spirit Binder**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School; Scribe Scroll; Bonus Feats

**Description:** While most wizards learn their arts through gradual study, spirit binders have made
a sudden arcane breakthrough due to the traumatic experience of losing a loved one.

**Spirit Whisperer**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Spellbook; Arcane School; Bonus Feats; 20th-level Bonus Feat

**Description:** Spirit whisperers are a breed apart among wizards, and are often mistaken for
witches.

**Sword Binder**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Arcane Bond; Arcane School; Bonus School Spell Slots; 10th-level Bonus
Feat

**Description:** Sword binders follow a tradition of martial wizards who often worked with the
Church of Aroden and the crowns of Taldor and then Cheliax.

**Undead Master**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Alignment; Arcane School; Arcane Bond; Scribe Scroll; 5th, 10th, 15th,
20th-level Bonus Feats

**Description:** Undead masters have great power over undeath.

**Wind Listener**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Arcane School; Arcane Bond; 5th, 10th, 15th-level Bonus Feat

**Description:** (Sylph Only) The wind listener takes a sylph's natural curiosity to the extreme,
enhancing his natural skill at subterfuge and eavesdropping with potent arcane magic.

**Worldseeker**

**Class:** wizard

**Modifies or Replaces:** Scribe Scroll; 6th/8th-level School Ability; Arcane Bond; 5th, 15th-level
Bonus Feat

**Description:** Worldseekers are wizards who travel to all corners of the Great Beyond.
