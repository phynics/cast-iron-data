**Sources:** Pathfinder Unchained pg 14

Physical and mental perfection: that is the life goal of every monk. For these skilled warriors,
martial prowess and mental clarity are one and the same. Capable of pummeling foes with both fist
and weapon, monks are among the more versatile combatants on the battlefield. Monks are also able to
harness their ki, a spiritual life force that resides within, allowing them to perform amazing acts
of acrobatics and mystical skill. All of this depends on incredible discipline and focus, and monks
spend their entire lives perfecting these arts.

> **Unchained:** Much of the [monk](ref/class/monk) has been reworked, including its base attack
> bonus, Hit Die, saves, and many of its special abilities. The result is a version of the monk that
> not only is easier to play, but also possesses a great deal more versatility. Flurry of blows is
> now much simpler to use in play. Many of the higher-level abilities of the monk, such as abundant
> step and quivering palm, have been incorporated into the new system of ki powers. As a monk
> advances in level, he can select from a variety of ki powers, allowing the player to truly
> customize his character.

**Alignment:** Any lawful

**Hit Die:** d10

**Starting Wealth:** 1d6 × 10 gp (average 35 gp)

**Skill Points per Level:** 4 + Int modifier

**Class Skills (Str):** Climb, Swim

**Class Skills (Dex):** Acrobatics, Escape Artist, Ride, Stealth

**Class Skills (Int):** Craft (any), Knowledge (history), Knowledge (religion)

**Class Skills (Wis):** Perception, Profession (any), Sense Motive

**Class Skills (Cha):** Intimidate, Perform (any)

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Weapons:** club, dagger, light crossbow, heavy crossbow, handaxe, javelin, kama, nunchaku,
quarterstaff, sai, short sword, shortspear, shuriken, siangham, sling, spear, Any weapon with the
[monk](ref/misc/monk) special weapon quality.

**Armor:** No armor

**Shields:** No

**Notes:** Wearing armor or using a shield can stifle some of your abilities.

**AC Bonus (Ex)**

**At 4th Level:** You gain an additional bonus to AC and CMD equal to
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your monk level.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** When unarmored and unencumbered, you add your Wisdom bonus (if any) to your AC and CMD.

**Special:** These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks or when you're
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed). You lose these bonuses when immobilized or helpless, when you
wear any armor, when you carry a shield, or when you carry a medium or heavy load.

**Bonus Feat**

**Ability:** You gain a bonus feat from the following list:
[Catch Off-Guard](ref/feat/catch-off-guard), [Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes),
[Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows), [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge),
[Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple), [Ki Diversity](ref/feat/ki-diversity),
[Scorpion Style](ref/feat/scorpion-style), and [Throw Anything](ref/feat/throw-anything). You need
not have any of the prerequisites normally required for these feats to select them.

**At 10th Level:** You add these feats to the list: [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical),
[Ki Throw](ref/feat/ki-throw), [Medusa's Wrath](ref/feat/medusas-wrath),
[Snatch Arrows](ref/feat/snatch-arrows), and [Spring Attack](ref/feat/spring-attack).

**At 14th Level:** You add these feats to the list: [Binding Throw](ref/feat/binding-throw) and
[Improved Ki Throw](ref/feat/improved-ki-throw).

**At 6th Level:** You add these feats to the list: [Gorgon's Fist](ref/feat/gorgons-fist),
[Hamatulatsu](ref/feat/hamatulatsu), [Horn of the Criosphinx](ref/feat/horn-of-the-criosphinx),
[Improved Bull Rush](ref/feat/improved-bull-rush), [Improved Disarm](ref/feat/improved-disarm),
[Improved Feint](ref/feat/improved-feint), [Improved Trip](ref/feat/improved-trip), and
[Mobility](ref/feat/mobility).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Repeat:** You gain another bonus feat from the list., 2, 4

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
At 1st level, 2nd level, and every 4 levels thereafter, a monk can select a bonus feat. These feats

[Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes), [Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows),

not have any of the prerequisites normally required for these feats to select them.

**Flurry of Blows (Ex)**

**Fullround:** You can make a flurry of blows as a full-attack action. When doing this, you can make
one additional attack at your highest base attack bonus. This additional attack stacks with the
bonus attacks from [haste](ref/spell/haste) and other similar effects. When using this ability, you
can make these attacks with any combination of your unarmed strikes and weapons that have the monk
special weapon quality. You take no penalty for using multiple weapons when making a flurry of
blows, but you do not gain any additional attacks beyond what's already granted by the flurry for
doing so. (You can still gain additional attacks from a high base attack bonus, from this ability,
and from _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_ and similar effects).

**At 11th Level:** You can now make two additional attacks at your highest base attack bonus during
your flurry of blows. This still stacks with _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_ and similar effects.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Stunning Fist (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain [Stunning Fist](ref/feat/stunning-fist) as a bonus feat, even if you don't
meet the prerequisites.

**At 12th Level:** You can make the target [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1d6+1 rounds

**At 16th Level:** You can permanently [blind](ref/misc/blinded) or [deafen](ref/misc/deafened) the
target.

**At 20th Level:** You can [paralyze](ref/rule/paralyzed) the target for 1d6+1 rounds.

**At 4th Level:** You gain the ability to apply [fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued) to the target of your
Stunning Fist. This condition replaces stunning the target for 1 round, and a successful saving
throw still negates the effect.

**At 8th Level:** You can now make the target [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 minute.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** You must choose which condition will apply before the attack roll is made. These
effects do not stack with themselves (a creature fatigued by Stunning Fist cannot become
[exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) if hit by Stunning Fist again), but additional hits do increase the
duration.

**Unarmed Strike (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**At 1st Level:** Your unarmed strike deals 1d4 (Small), 1d6 (Medium), or 1d8 (Large) damage.

**At 12th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d10 (Small), 2d6 (Medium), or 3d6 (Large) damage.

**At 16th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d6 (Small), 2d8 (Medium), or 3d8 (Large) damage.

**At 20th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 2d8 (Small), 2d10 (Medium), or 4d8 (Large) damage.

**At 4th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d6 (Small), 1d8 (Medium), or 2d6 (Large) damage.

**At 8th Level:** Your unarmed strike now deals 1d8 (Small), 1d10 (Medium), or 2d8 (Large) damage.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** Your unarmed strike is treated as both a manufactured weapon and a natural weapon for
the purpose of spells and effects that enhance or improve either manufactured weapons or natural
weapons.

You gain [Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike) as a bonus feat. Your attacks
can be with fists, elbows, knees, and feet. This means that you can make unarmed strikes with your
hands full. There is no such thing as an off-hand attack when you're striking unarmed. You can apply
your full Strength bonus on damage rolls for all your unarmed strikes. Your unarmed strikes deal
lethal damage, although you can choose to deal nonlethal damage with no penalty on the attack roll.
You can make this choice while grappling as well.

You also deal more damage with your unarmed strikes than a normal person would. The unarmed damage
values listed on the table above are for Medium monks only.

**Sources:** The Elephant in the Room: Feat Taxes in Pathfinder\
Instead of Improved Unarmed Strike, monks gain [Unarmed Combatant](ref/rule/unarmed-combatant) as a
bonus feat.

**Evasion (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A monk can avoid damage from many area-effect attacks.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** If you succeed at a Reflex saving throw against an attack that normally deals half
damage on a successful save, you instead take no damage.

**Special:** _Evasion_ can be used only if you are wearing light armor or no armor, and are also not
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless).

**Fast Movement (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain an enhancement bonus to your land speed equal to
[one-third](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your monk level &times& 10.

**Special:** If you wear armor or are carrying a medium or heavy load, you lose this extra speed.

**Ki Pool (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a pool of ki points. The number of points in your ki pool is equal to 1/2 your
monk level + your Wisdom modifier.

**Flavor:** A monk gains supernatural energy he can use to accomplish amazing feats.

**At 10th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are also treated as lawful weapons for the purpose of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 16th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are treated as [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) weapons
for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and bypassing
[hardness](ref/rule/breaking-things).

**At 7th Level:** Your _ki strikes_ are also treated as cold iron and silver for the purpose of
overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** As long as you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, you can make a _ki strike,_ which
allows your unarmed attacks to be treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Special:** You gain additional powers that consume points from your ki pool as you gain levels.
The ki pool is replenished each morning after 8 hours of rest or meditation; these hours do not need
to be consecutive.

**Swift:** By spending 1 point from your ki pool, you can make one additional unarmed strike at your
highest attack bonus when making a flurry of blows attack. This bonus attack stacks with all bonus
attacks gained from flurry of blows, as well as those from _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_ and similar
effects.

**Ki Powers (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain one ki power of your choice. Once a ki power is selected, it cannot be
changed.

**Flavor:** A monk gains powers that allow him to perform amazing feats of mystical power and
acrobatic prowess by expending points from his ki pool.

**Section:** Ki Powers and Still Mind

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Special:** Some ki powers require you to be of a specific level or higher before they can be
chosen. Unless otherwise noted, you cannot select an individual ki power more than once.

**Still Mind (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against enchantment spells and effects.

**Purity of Body (Ex)**

**Section:** Purity of Body and Style Strikes

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to all diseases, including supernatural and magical diseases.

**Style Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** You learn one type of style strike. Whenever you make a flurry of blows, you can
designate one of your unarmed strikes as a style strike. This attack is resolved as normal, but it
has an additional effect depending on the type of strike chosen.

**At 15th Level:** You can designate up to two of your unarmed strikes each round as a style strike,
and each one can be a different type.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Repeat:** You learn an additional style strike., 9, 4

**Special:** You must choose which style strike to apply before the attack roll is made.

**Improved Evasion (Ex)**

**Section:** Other Abilities

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** Your _evasion_ improves. You still take no damage on successful Reflex saving throws
against attacks, but henceforth you take only half damage on failed saves.

**Special:** You do not gain the benefit of _improved evasion_ while helpless.

**Tongue of the Sun and Moon (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 13th Level

**Passive:** You can understand and speak with any living creature, as if under a permanent
[tongues](ref/spell/tongues) effect.

**Timeless Body (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Passive:** You no longer take penalties to your ability scores for aging and cannot be magically
[aged](ref/rule/age). Any such penalties that you have already taken, however, remain in place.

**Special:** Age bonuses still accrue, and you still die of old age when your time is up.

**Flawless Mind**

**Flavor:** A monk gains total control over his mental faculties.

**Gained:** At 19th Level

**Passive:** Whenever you attempt a Will save, you can roll twice and take the better result. If you
fail a Will saving throw against a spell or effect that has a duration longer than 1 hour, you can
attempt a new saving throw at the end of each hour to end the effect.

**Perfect Self**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to enter a state of perfect calm. During this time, you can take
no actions, but you regain ki at the rate of 1 point per 10 minutes spent at calm. You cannot use
this ability to gain an amount of ki in excess of your maximum.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You become a magical creature. You are forevermore treated as an outsider rather than a
humanoid (or whatever your original creature type was) for the purpose of spells and magical
effects. You gain DR 10/chaotic. Unlike other outsiders, you can still be brought back from the dead
as if you were a member of your previous creature type.

### Ex-Monks

A monk who becomes nonlawful cannot gain new levels as a monk but retains all monk abilities.

### Alternate Capstones

**Source** Chronicle of Legends pg. 28\
When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Old Master (Ex)**

At 20th level, the monk has reached the highest levels of his martial arts school. The monk gains
one additional attack at his highest base attack bonus when using flurry of blows, and his dodge
bonus to AC increases by 2.

**Also available:** Old Dog, New Tricks, Ki Sage

### Archetypes

**Source** Advanced Player's Guide pg. 72\
From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.
