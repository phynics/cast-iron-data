**Sources:** PRPG Core Rulebook pg 67

Life is an endless adventure for those who live by their wits. Ever just one step ahead of danger,
rogues bank on their cunning, skill, and charm to bend fate to their favor. Never knowing what to
expect, they prepare for everything, becoming masters of a wide variety of skills, training
themselves to be adept manipulators, agile acrobats, shadowy stalkers, or masters of any of dozens
of other professions or talents. Thieves and gamblers, fast talkers and diplomats, bandits and
bounty hunters, and explorers and investigators all might be considered rogues, as well as countless
other professions that rely upon wits, prowess, or luck. Although many rogues favor cities and the
innumerable opportunities of civilization, some embrace lives on the road, journeying far, meeting
exotic people, and facing fantastic danger in pursuit of equally fantastic riches. In the end, any
who desire to shape their fates and live life on their own terms might come to be called rogues.

**Role:** Rogues excel at moving about unseen and catching foes unaware, and tend to avoid
head-to-head combat. Their varied skills and abilities allow them to be highly versatile, with great
variations in expertise existing between different rogues. Most, however, excel in overcoming
hindrances of all types, from unlocking doors and disarming traps to outwitting magical hazards and
conning dull-witted opponents.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

**Weapons:** All simple weapons, hand crossbow, rapier, sap, shortbow, short sword

**Armor:** Light armor

**Shields:** No

**Sneak Attack**

**Ability:** Your attack deals extra damage any time your target would be denied a Dexterity bonus
to AC (whether the target actually has a Dexterity bonus or not), or when you
[flank](ref/rule/flanking) your target. This extra damage is a number of d6s equal to 1 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your rogue level. (1d6 at first, 2d6 at 3rd, 3d6 at 5th, and
so on.) You must be able to see the target well enough to pick out a vital spot and must be able to
reach such a spot. You cannot sneak attack while striking a creature with
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment).

**Flavor:** If a rogue can catch an opponent when he is unable to defend himself effectively from
her attack, she can strike a vital spot for extra damage.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Special:** If you score a critical hit with a sneak attack, this extra damage is not multiplied.
Ranged attacks can count as sneak attacks only if the target is within 30 feet. With a weapon that
deals nonlethal damage (like a sap, whip, or an unarmed strike), you can make a sneak attack that
deals nonlethal damage instead of lethal damage. You cannot use a weapon that deals lethal damage to
deal nonlethal damage in a sneak attack, not even with the usual -4 penalty.

**Trapfinding**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** You add 1/2 your level to Perception skill checks made to locate traps and to Disable
Device skill checks (minimum +1). You can use Disable Device to disarm magic traps.

**Evasion (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** If you make a successful Reflex saving throw against an attack that normally deals half
damage on a successful save, you instead take no damage. Evasion can be used only if the you are not
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless) and are wearing light armor or no armor.

**Rogue Talents**

**Ability:** You gain one rogue talent. You gain an additional rogue talent at every even-numbered
level (4th, 6th, 8th, and so on).

**Flavor:** As a rogue gains experience, she learns a number of talents that aid her and confound
her foes.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Special:** You cannot select an individual talent more than once. Some talents add effects to your
_sneak attack._ Only one of these talents can be applied to an individual _attack_ and the decision
must be made before the attack roll is made.

**Trap Sense (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain a bonus on Reflex saves made to avoid traps and a dodge bonus to AC against
attacks made by traps. Both of these bonuses are equal to one-third of your rogue level.

**Special:** Trap sense bonuses gained from multiple classes stack.

**Uncanny Dodge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** You cannot be caught [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed), nor do you lose your Dex
bonus to AC if the attacker is invisible. You still lose your Dexterity bonus to AC if immobilized,
or if an opponent successfully uses the [feint](ref/rule/feint) action against you.

**Special:** If you already have uncanny dodge from a different class, you automatically gain
improved uncanny dodge instead.

**Improved Uncanny Dodge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Passive:** You can no longer be [flanked](ref/rule/flanking) This defense denies another rogue the
ability to _sneak attack_ you by flanking you, unless the attacker has at least four more rogue
levels than you do.

**Special:** If you already have uncanny dodge from another class, the levels from the classes that
grant uncanny dodge stack to determine the minimum rogue level required to flank you.

**Advanced Talents**

**Ability:** You can choose an advanced rogue talent in place of a _rogue talent._

**Section:** Other Abilities

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Master Strike (Ex)**

**Ability:** Each time you deal _sneak attack_ damage, you can choose one of the following three
effects: the target can be put to sleep for 1d4 hours, [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for 2d6
rounds, or slain. Regardless of the effect chosen, the target receives a Fortitude save to negate
the additional effect. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 your rogue level + your Intelligence
modifier.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Special:** Once a creature has been the target of a master strike, regardless of whether or not
the save is made, that creature is immune to that rogue's master strike for 24 hours. Creatures that
are immune to _sneak attack_ damage are also immune to this ability.

### Alternate Capstones

**Source** Chronicle of Legends pg. 28\
When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Masterful Talent (Ex)**

At 20th level, the rogue has been a thief, an actor, a merchant, a scout, a confessor, a friend, an
assassin, and a dozen more things besides. The rogue gains a +4 bonus on all of her skills.

**Also available:** The Right Spot

### Archetypes

**Source** Advanced Player's Guide pg. 72\
From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Trap Sense

**Description:** Some say that the sniper is the worst kind of assassin: a killer who waits silently
in the shadows and then strikes from a distance without remorse.

**Snoop**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Evasion; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge; 8th-level
Rogue Talent

**Description:** Snoops act as information peddlers, specializing in gathering secrets through
investigation, subterfuge, and coercion, and selling or trading those secrets for personal gain.

**Spy**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Trap Sense

**Description:** The best spies are like chameleons, but not only can they change their appearances
to fit the situation, they can also change their personalities, allegiances, and even loves if
that's what it takes to achieve their clandestine goals.

**Survivalist**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Trap Sense

**Description:** The survivalist focuses her talents on surviving harsh and unforgiving conditions
that would kill a lesser rogue.

**Swamp Poisoner**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Racial Traits; Trapfinding; 2nd-level Rogue Talent; Trap Sense

**Description:** (Grippli) Swamp poisoners use their own toxins to defend their homes and level the
playing field

**Swashbuckler**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Trap Sense

**Description:** A paragon of mobile swordplay, the swashbuckler is a rogue who focuses almost
exclusively on honing her skill at arms and perfecting daring acrobatic moves and elaborate
flourishes that border on performance.

**Swordmaster**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Trap Sense

**Description:** (Tengu Only) A swordmaster meditates to strengthen her spiritual connection to her
blade. She strives to perfect her skills by mastering six deadly trances.

**Sylvan Trickster**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Trapfinding; Rogue Talents; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny
Dodge

**Description:** Sylvan tricksters are rogues who model themselves after the mischievous fey of
legend.

**Thug**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Trap Sense

**Description:** Some criminals steal with finesse, their victims only discovering the crime when
the rogue is long gone and the coin already spent. A thug, on the other hand, cares nothing for
finesse.

**Tidal Trickster**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Trap Sense; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge; 4th,
8th-level Rogue Talent

**Description:** Tidal tricksters roam the seas, using their skillful mastery of the currents to
move across aquatic battlefields and throw their enemies off-balance.

**Toxic Talon**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Weapon Proficiencies; Trapfinding; Trap Sense; Uncanny Dodge; Improved
Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** A toxic talon spends resources and time combining easy-to-obtain materials into
dangerous toxins to aid in the defense of Andoran.

**Trapsmith**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** Some rogues are not content with just disabling traps - they love to build them,
finding a captivating beauty in the turning of gears and the slither of ropes over pulleys.

**Underground Chemist**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Evasion; 4th-level Rogue Talent; Advanced Talents

**Description:** Underground chemists are part of the rotting, fetid underbelly of the alchemical
world.

**Vexing Dodger**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; 2nd-level Rogue Talent; Trap Sense; Uncanny Dodge; Improved
Uncanny Dodge

**Description:** Vexing dodgers take advantage of their smaller size to outmaneuver larger foes such
as giants.

**Waylayer**

**Class:** rogue

**Modifies or Replaces:** Trapfinding; Uncanny Dodge; Improved Uncanny Dodge; 12th and 20th-level
Rogue Talents

**Description:** A clever rogue knows the easiest battle to win is the one that's over before your
foe knows it has begun.
