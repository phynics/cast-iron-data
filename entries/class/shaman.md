While some heroes speak to gods or consort with otherworldly muses, shamans commune with the spirits
of the world and the energies that exist in every living thing. These divine adventurers draw upon
their power to shape the world and expand the influence of their spiritual patrons. Shamans have
strong ties to natural spirits. They form powerful bonds with particular spirits, and as their power
grows they learn to call upon other spirits in times of need.

**Role:** Shamans make for potent divine spellcasters, capable of using divine spells and the power
of their spirits to aid their allies and destroy their foes. While they aren't the healers that
clerics are, they can still fill that role when needed.

**Alignment:** Any

**Hit Die:** d8

**Parent Class(es):** [Oracle](ref/class/oracle) and [witch](ref/class/witch).

**Starting Wealth:** 3d6 × 10 gp (average 105 gp)

**Skill Points per Level:** 4 + Int modifier

**Class Skills (Dex):** Fly, Ride

**Class Skills (Int):** Craft (any), Knowledge (nature), Knowledge (planes), Knowledge (religion),
Spellcraft

**Class Skills (Wis):** Heal, Profession (any), Survival

**Class Skills (Cha):** Diplomacy, Handle Animal

#### Weapon and Armor Proficiencies {#weapon-and-armor-proficiencies}

**Weapons:** All simple weapons

**Armor:** Light armor, Medium armor

**Shields:** No

#### Spells {#spells}

**Spell list:** Divine spells from the shaman list, from 1st level

**Key ability:** Wisdom. Minimum score 10 + spell level; save DC 10 + spell level + Wisdom modifier;
a high score grants bonus spells per day.

**Casting:** Prepared: choose and prepare spells in advance.

**Notes:** Regains spells after 1 hour in contemplation or prayer each day; any spell on the list
can be prepared.

#### Orisons {#orisons}

**Ability:** 0-level divine spells. Prepared like other spells but not expended when cast; one
placed in a higher slot (for example through metamagic) uses that slot.

#### Spirit (Su) {#spirit}

**Ability:** You form a lasting bond with a single [spirit](ref/ability/shaman-spirits), which
grants a number of abilities and defines many of your other class features.

**Flavor:** A shaman forms a mystical bond with the spirits of the world.

**At 1st Level:** You gain the _spirit ability_ granted by your chosen spirit. You add the spells
granted by that spirit to the list of spells that you can cast using _spirit magic._ You also add
the hexes possessed by that spirit to the list of hexes that you can use with the _hex_ and
_wandering hex_ class features.

**At 16th Level:** You gain the abilities listed for the _true_ version of your selected spirit.

**At 8th Level:** You gain the abilities listed in the _greater_ version of your selected spirit.

**Special:** If you take levels in another class that grants a mystery (such as the oracle), the
spirit and mystery must match, even if that means one of them must change. Subject to GM discretion,
you can change your former mystery or spirit to make them conform.

#### Spirit Animal (Ex) {#spirit-animal}

**Action:** Ability

**At 1st Level:** Your spirit animal gains specific abilities, depending upon the type of spirit
selected by you using your spirit class feature. These abilities affect the animal's appearance and
grant it special abilities that can aid it in serving you and the spirit it represents and is
connected to. These abilities are described in the _spirit animal_ section of each individual
_spirit_ description.

**At 3rd Level:** Your spirit animal can deliver touch spells or hexes for you. If you and the
spirit animal are in contact at the time you cast a touch spell, you can designate your animal as
the &quot&toucher.&quot& The spirit animal can then deliver the touch spell just as you would. If
you cast another spell before the touch is delivered, the touch spell dissipates. If you activate a
_hex,_ your spirit animal can be used to make the touch; you don't have to be in contact with the
animal to use this ability with hexes.

**Section:** Spirit Animal

**Gained:** At 1st Level

You form a close bond with a spirit animal tied to your chosen _spirit._ This animal is your conduit
to the spirit world, guiding you along the path to enlightenment.

You must commune with your spirit animal each day to prepare your spells. While the spirit animal
does not store the spells like a witch's familiar does, the spirit animal serves as your conduit to
divine power. If your spirit animal is slain, you cannot prepare new spells or use your spirit magic
class feature until the spirit animal is replaced.

By communing with the incredible powers of your spirit, you forge a cherished bond with one specific
servant of that spirit - known as a _spirit animal._ A spirit animal is a creature chosen by you to
serve as a conduit, allowing you to more fully access the magic of your spirit on a daily basis.
Your spirit animal also grants you special powers. This ability uses the same rules as the wizard's
_arcane bond_ class feature and is treated as a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar), except as noted
below.

You use your level as your effective wizard level when determining the abilities of your spirit
animal. You can select [any familiar available to wizards](ref/ability/familiars) to serve as your
spirit animal, although your spirit animal is augmented by the power of your chosen spirit. Once
selected, the spirit animal cannot be changed. Although your spirit animal uses the statistics of a
specific animal, it is treated as an outsider with the native subtype for the purposes of spells and
abilities that affect it.

Levels of different classes that are entitled to familiars stack with shaman levels for the purpose
of determining any spirit animal abilities that depend on your level. If you possess such levels in
other classes, your spirit animal always uses your rules for spirit animals, not those used by
familiars of other classes (such as [witches](ref/class/witch), [wizards](ref/class/wizard), or
[sorcerers](ref/class/sorcerer) with the [arcane](ref/sorcbloodline/arcane) bloodline). If you have
witch levels, your spirit animal also serves as the conduit to your patron and stores your witch
spells. Your spirit animal is treated as a familiar for the purposes of all spells, effects, and
abilities that affect familiars.

If a spirit animal is lost or dies, it can be replaced after 24 hours through a special ritual that
consumes material components worth 500 gp per shaman level. The ritual takes 8 hours to complete.
The new spirit animal must be of the same sort of creature as the previous one.

#### Spirit Magic {#spirit-magic}

**Ability:** You can spontaneously cast a limited number of spells per day beyond those you prepared
ahead of time. You have one spell slot per day of each shaman spell level you can cast, not
including orisons. You can choose these spells from the list of spells granted by your spirits at
the time you cast them. You can enhance these spells using any metamagic feat that you know, using
up a higher-level spell slot as required by the feat and increasing the time to cast the spell.

#### Hex {#hex}

**Ability:** You learn one hex. You can select from any of these shaman hexes or from any of the
hexes listed in the description of your chosen spirit. You cannot select a hex more than once unless
noted otherwise.

**Flavor:** A shaman learns a number of magical tricks, called hexes, which grant her powers or
weaken foes.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Repeat At:** You learn a new hex., 4, 8, 10, 12, 16, 18, 20

**Special:** Using a hex is a **standard action** that doesn't provoke an attack of opportunity
unless otherwise noted. The saving throw DC to resist a hex is equal to 10 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your shaman level + your Wisdom modifier.

Shamans can take a blood hex feat in place of a regular hex.

#### Wandering Spirit (Su) {#wandering-spirit}

**Ability:** You can form a temporary bond with a spirit other than the one selected using your
_spirit_ class feature. You must make this selection each day when preparing your spells. While this
feature is active, you gain the _spirit ability_ granted by the spirit. You also add the spells
granted by that spirit to your list of spells that you can cast using _spirit magic._ You do not add
the hexes from your _wandering spirit_ to your list of hexes that you can choose from with the hex
class feature.

**At 12th Level:** You gain the abilities listed in the _greater_ version of your _wandering
spirit._

**At 20th Level:** You gain the ability listed in the _true_ version of your _wandering spirit._

**Gained:** At 4th Level

#### Wandering Hex {#wandering-hex}

**Ability:** You can temporarily gain the use of one of the hexes possessed by either one of your
spirits. You must make this selection each day when you prepare your spells. For the purposes of
this ability, you can select any hex possessed by your _spirit_ or _wandering spirit._ If you select
it from your _wandering spirit,_ you lose the hex immediately if you bond with another spirit,
although you can then select a different hex to gain using this ability, from either your _spirit_
or your new _wandering spirit._

**At 14th Level:** You can select two wandering hexes each day instead of one.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Special:** This ability otherwise functions as the hex class feature.

#### Manifestation (Su) {#manifestation}

**Ability:** You undergo a transformation as you manifest as a pinnacle of your main spirit. The
nature of this manifestation depends on your _spirit,_ and is described in its entry.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

### Alternate Capstones

**Source** Chronicle of Legends pg. 28\
When a character reaches the 20th level of a class, she gains a powerful class feature or ability,
sometimes referred to as a capstone. The following section provides new capstones for characters to
select at 20th level. A character can select one of the following capstones in place of the capstone
provided by her class. Some capstones are for specific classes, while others are for a range of
classes that qualify for them. In some cases, a capstone specifies what ability it replaces. A
character can't select a new capstone if she has previously traded away her class capstone via an
archetype. Clerics and wizards can receive a capstone at 20th level, despite not having one to begin
with. A character that gains certain abilities may be eligible for capstones other than those listed
below.

**Hex Mastery (Su)**

At 20th level, the shaman has learned ever more terrible hexes. She can select one grand hex from
the list available to witches.

**Also available:** Arch-Familiar

### Archetypes

**Source** Advanced Player's Guide pg. 72\
From the noble paladin to the skillful rogue, each class draws upon a central archetype. Yet, beyond
that basic concept exists the potential for innumerable interpretations, details, and refinements;
and some archetypes prove pervasive and exciting enough to see use in play time and time again.

To help players interested in creating iconic fantasy characters, **archetypes** were introduced.
These optional features represent a unique view of what a class deliberately designed to capture a
specific character archetype might become. While the types of options presented for each class
differ, each subsystem is customized to best serve that class, emulate the abilities and talents of
classic fantasy archetypes, and expand players' freedom to design exactly the characters they
desire.

**Animist**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Magic Spells; 2nd, 8th, 10th, 12th, and 18th-level Hexes;
Manifestation

**Description:** Even among mystical practitioners, the animist has a strange perspective and even
stranger magic. The animist perceives that all things have a spirit, including objects, constructs,
illnesses, buildings, and the environment.

**Benefactor**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spellcasting; Hexes

**Description:** A benefactor seeks to improve the lives of others by primarily offering powerful
supportive abilities and magic to her allies and others she deems worthy.

**Crystal Tender**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Animal; Wandering Spirit; 8th-level Shaman Hex; Manifestation

**Description:** A crystal tender focuses on the spirits of metal and stone, using crystals as a
means to focus the power of the spirits she venerates.

**Deep Shaman**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit; Spirit Animal; Class Skills; Spirit Abilities

**Description:** Deep shamans are tied to spirits from the depths.

**Draconic Shaman**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit; Spirit Animal; 4th, 10th-level Hexes; Spirit Magic

**Description:** Some shamans draw their powers from the might of dragons. These shamans each gain a
powerful drake as an ally, and view caring for that drake as a sacred duty

**Grasping Vine**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Animal; Spirit Magic; 2nd, 8th-level Hex

**Description:** Grasping vine shamans are typically adherents of one of several scattered sects
dedicated to the Green Mother. They revere plant life in all its forms, with a special fondness for
poisonous or carnivorous plants.

**Name-Keeper**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Spirit Animal; Spirit Magic; Wandering Spirit; Wandering Hex

**Description:** Name-keepers have delved into the storied histories etched into the Wall of Names
and communed with the departed spirits of those whose names are memorialized on the monument.

**Overseer**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Magic; Deliver Touch Spells

**Description:** While all shamans use their connection to the spirits of the world to draw upon
otherworldly magic powers, the shamans of the Lands of the Linnorm Kings have a unique tradition in
which they use the power of patron spirits to directly control their enemies.

**Possessed Shaman**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Magic; 2nd and 6th-level Hexes

**Description:** For a possessed shaman, merely communing with the spirit world is insufficient.
Instead, she invites the spirits to share her body, granting them the chance to experience corporeal
existence.

**Primal Warden**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Animal; Spirit Magic; 4th, 8th, 12th-level Hex

**Description:** A shaman who communes with spirits that linger in areas where primal magic reigns,
learning to stabilize these erratic energies and draw upon them to her benefit.

**Serendipity Shaman**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Magic spells

**Description:** The serendipitous, shamanistic rites associated with the traditional catfolk
worship of the so-called &quot&spirits of creation&quot& focus upon attracting good fortune and
banishing ill fortune in the name of these spirits.

**Speaker for the Past**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit Animal; Wandering Spirit; Wandering Hex

**Description:** A speaker for the past is a shaman who specifically serves as the voice for spirits
from her people's history.

**Spirit Warden**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Spirit Magic Spells; 2nd and 10th-level Hexes

**Description:** Not all spirits deserve reverence and respect. Some are twisted and despicable.
It's a spirit warden's duty to end these spirits' existence.

**True Silvered Throne**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Class Skills; Spirit Animal; Wandering Spirit; Wandering Hex; 8th-level
Hex

**Description:** Members in good standing who have risen to a prestigious rank within the Esoteric
Order of the Palatine Eye, true silvered thrones have managed to discover rituals and occult secrets
within the lore of their order.

**Unsworn Shaman**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Spirit; Hex; Spirit Animal; Wandering Spirit; Wandering Hex; Spirit Magic

**Description:** An unsworn shaman never binds herself to one specific spirit, always making new
deals as she deems necessary for the circumstances that she finds herself in.

**Visionary**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Wandering Spirit; Spirit Magic Spells; Wandering Hex; Greater Wandering
Spirit; True Wandering Spirit

**Description:** The visionary is a master at divination, drawing upon her intimate relationship
with the spirit world to ferret out all manner of secrets and insights about the world around her
and beyond.

**Witch Doctor**

**Class:** shaman

**Modifies or Replaces:** Alignment; 4th, 8th, 10th, and 12th-level Hexes

**Description:** The witch doctor is a healer who specializes in afflictions of the soul.
