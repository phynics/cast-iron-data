**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** -

This rope is 100 feet long and can perform all the functions of a
[rope of climbing](ref/magic-wondrous/rope-of-climbing). Furthermore, upon command the rope of knots
snakes out and knots itself into a rope structure. The rope can tie itself into any structure which
can be created out of 100 feet of rope. Possible structures include (but are not limited to) a
sturdy rope bridge up to 25 feet long, a 10-foot-square net or hammock, or a 40-foot-tall rope
ladder. The rope takes up to 10 rounds to create a complex structure or half that time to return
itself to a simple coil. The rope has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 1 and 20 hit points. It repairs
damage to itself at a rate of 2 hit points every 10 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate rope](ref/spell/animate-rope); **Cost** 3,000 gp
