**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 304\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 200 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This two-spouted funnel is used to safely mix two alchemical splash weapons into a single flask.
Using the vial requires 10 minutes and a DC 25 [Craft (alchemy)](ref/skill/craft-any) check; a
[half-elf](ref/race/half-elf) has a +5 bonus on this check. Failing the check means both splash
weapon are destroyed. Success means the two materials are safely mixed into one vial that is the
same volume as a normal splash weapon vial; when
[thrown as a splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon), the mixture has the effects of both
component substances and creatures are affected as if hit by both. The mixture becomes inert after
24 hours. Only liquids may be mixed with the funnel. The bearer can use it to mix an alchemical
splash weapon with either [holy water or unholy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water), but the Craft
(alchemy) DC increases to 30; half-elves still get the +5 bonus on this check. The funnel does not
work on potions, elixirs, extracts, or other materials. Mixing a substance with a similar or
identical substance (such as [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire) with alchemist's
fire) has no effect. A mixture cannot be combined with another mixture.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate) or [combine extracts](ref/discovery/combine-extracts) discovery;
**Cost** 100 gp
