**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 258\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Favored by the grave-wardens of sites known to be infested with the spirits of the dead, this
pendant is an iron ring strung on a necklace made from polished and discolored bones, nails, and
teeth. A faint blue light limns the wearer, protecting him from attack. Undead creatures are
repulsed by this light and take a -2 penalty on all attack rolls against the wearer of a gravewatch
pendant. Incorporeal undead creatures are further repulsed and instead take a -4 penalty on all
attack rolls against the wearer. Undead creatures can sense this light easily, however, and receive
a +4 circumstance bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks to detect a creature wearing
this pendant.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil); **Cost** 4,000 gp
