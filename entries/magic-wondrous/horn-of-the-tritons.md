**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 303, Pathfinder \#58: Island of Empty Eyes pg 60\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This device is a large, intricately inscribed and decorated conch shell that can be blown once per
day (except by a [triton](ref/race/triton), who can sound it three times per day). Blowing the horn
activates any one of the following functions:

- Calm rough waters in a 1-mile radius. Alternatively, this functions as a
  _[dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal)_ spell against a targeted
  [water elemental](ref/monster/water-elemental) (DC 19 negates).
- Summon 1d4+1 [sharks](ref/monster/shark) or 1d3 [advanced](ref/template/advanced) sharks as if
  using _[summon nature's ally V](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5)._
- Create a fear effect (DC 16 negates) in a 500-foot radius that only affects creatures with the
  aquatic or amphibious subtype which have Intelligence scores of 1 or 2.

Any sounding of a horn of the tritons can be heard by all tritons within a 3-mile radius.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[control water](ref/spell/control-water), [dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal), [fear](ref/spell/fear),
[summon nature's ally V](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5), creator must be a triton; **Cost** 7,500
gp
