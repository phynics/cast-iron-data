**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 26\
**Aura** moderate evocation \[good\]; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

These [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) horseshoes are imbued with heavenly power. They grant the
[holy](ref/magic-enh/holy) weapon special ability to the wearer's hoof attacks, and if the wearer
hits an evil-aligned target with both hooves in the same round, the target is
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round (Fortitude DC 16 negates). In addition, the wearer gains
a +2 sacred bonus on [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [drag](ref/rule/drag), and
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuvers. If the wearer is a mount and the rider uses the
[Trample](ref/feat/trample) feat, add 2 to the rider's overrun combat maneuver check. If the wearer
uses the [trample](ref/umr/trample) special ability, increase the trample DC by 2; if the target
fails its save against the trample attempt, add the damage from the holy weapon special ability to
the trample damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [holy smite](ref/spell/holy-smite), creator
must be good; **Cost** 10,000 gp
