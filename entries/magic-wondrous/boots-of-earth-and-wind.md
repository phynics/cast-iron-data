**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 147\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

These strange, heavy, leather-and-steel boots feature a pair of ever-fluttering wings on each side.
These boots function like [winged boots](ref/magic-wondrous/winged-boots), but also grant a +5 bonus
to CMD against [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun), and
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition) combat maneuvers as long as the wearer is standing on solid
ground. A mythic creature can expend one use of mythic power when commanding the boots to fly to
gain the bonus to CMD for as long as she uses the boots to fly.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [entangle](ref/spell/entangle), [fly](ref/spell/fly);
**Cost** 10,000 gp
