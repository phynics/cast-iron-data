## Belt of Mighty Constitution

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 209, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 502\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 4,000 gp (+2), 16,000 gp (+4), 36,000 gp (+6); **Weight** 1 lb.

This belt's golden buckle depicts a bear's head. The belt grants the wearer an enhancement bonus to
Constitution of +2, +4, or +6. Treat this as a
[temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance); **Cost** 2,000 gp (+2), 8,000 gp (+4), 18,000 gp (+6)
