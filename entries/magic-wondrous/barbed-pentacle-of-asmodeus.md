**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 260, Gods and Magic pg 54\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This iron disk hangs from an iron chain, and is inscribed with an inverted pentagram and decorated
around its outer edge with tiny metal barbs. When worn, it provides a +1 deflection bonus to AC.

If [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) is the wearer's patron, the pentacle counts as an unholy symbol.
By holding it and speaking a command word, the wearer can use
_[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)_ once per day. If the wearer squeezes the pentacle so its
barbs draw her blood (dealing 1 point of damage), the wearer can use that blood as ink, writing with
a quill or even a finger for up to 1 hour. The blood-ink dries instantly on writing surfaces so it
never smudges, and if it's used as part of a magical spell the wearer casts that requires or is
augmented by writing (such as [magic circle against evil](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-evil) or
[sepia snake sigil](ref/spell/sepia-snake-sigil)), the DC of that spell increases by 1.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [bleed](ref/spell/bleed),
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person), [shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith); **Cost** 1,500
gp
