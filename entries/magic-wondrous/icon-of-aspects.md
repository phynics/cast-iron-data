**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

When a cleric with an icon of aspects prepares spells, she may replace the granted powers of one of
her domains with those of a different domain she doesn't have but that is associated with her deity.
This lasts until the next time she prepares spells. The cleric must meet the level requirements for
both abilities. For example, a 6th-level cleric with the Good domain who swaps for the
[Healing](ref/domain/healing) domain can't use healer's blessing even though she meets the level
requirement, because the holy lance power of the Good domain requires her to be 8th level. The icon
affects only granted powers, not domain spells, bonus feats, or other benefits. The icon can affect
only one person at a time.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[imbue with spell ability](ref/spell/imbue-with-spell-ability), creator must be a divine
spellcaster; **Cost** 2,750 gp
