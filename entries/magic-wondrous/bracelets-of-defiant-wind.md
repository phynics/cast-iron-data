**Sources:** Pathfinder \#83: The Slave Trenches of Hakotep pg 69\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and evocation \[air\]; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** -

These azure-and-white marbled bracelets feel smooth and perpetually cool to the touch. When worn,
they activate once per day as an immediate action to safeguard against sudden airborne attacks and
effects by automatically surrounding their wearer in a cylinder of pure wind and elemental air. This
effect functions simultaneously as both _[air bubble](ref/spell/air-bubble)_ and
_[wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall),_ protecting against missile weapons, gases, and most gaseous
breath weapons. It also activates underwater if the wearer begins to [drown](ref/rule/drowning).
Once activated, the effect lasts for 10 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[air bubble](ref/spell/air-bubble), [contingency](ref/spell/contingency),
[wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall); **Cost** 4,500 gp
