**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** -

These pale, elegant-looking leather gloves have blood red stitching on the fingers - which bears a
startling resemblance to blood-encrusted fingernails. Three times per day, the wearer can use
_[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch)_ and _bleed._ If the wearer uses
_[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch)_ and holds the charge, the charge dissipates if the
gloves are removed.

Though _[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch)_ is normally a touch attack, the wearer may
instead use that ability as a ranged touch attack with a range of 30 feet, but only against a
[dying](ref/misc/dying) target or a target suffering from a [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) effect. When
using _[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch)_ in this way, the wearer may hold the charge on a
missed ranged touch attack.

If the wearer is suffering from a bleed effect, he may expend one use of
_[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch)_ to end the bleed effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [bleed](ref/spell/bleed),
[vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch); **Cost** 9,000 gp
