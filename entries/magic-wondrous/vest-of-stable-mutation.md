**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This leather vest is fitted with a number of pockets that are useful for storing the ingredients
necessary for alchemical tinkering. The main ability of this vest, however, is to lessen the
debilitating effects of an [alchemist's](ref/class/alchemist) mutagen. While under the effect of any
type of mutagen, the wearer takes no penalty to mental ability scores from that mutagen.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), [fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning),
[owl's wisdom](ref/spell/owls-wisdom), creator must be an alchemist; **Cost** 10,000 gp
