**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 263\
**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,300 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This heavy pastel candle is carved and painted to look like its outer surface is covered with
butterflies. A creature can use the candle to create a _[dream](ref/spell/dream)_ effect conveying a
message of no longer than 10 minutes. Alternatively, the candle can be used to send up to 10 shorter
messages, so long as the total time is no longer than 10 minutes. The user must light the candle,
identify the intended recipient, speak the message, then snuff the candle; the next time the
recipient sleeps, she receives the message.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [dream](ref/spell/dream);
**Cost** 1,150 gp
