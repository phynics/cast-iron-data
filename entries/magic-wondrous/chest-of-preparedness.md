**Sources:** Faction Guide pg 58\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 30 lbs.

Created by the [Prophets of Kalistrade](ref/rule/f-profits), this chest is approximately 2 feet wide
and 1 foot long. On creation, the maker chooses 10 nonmagical items worth under 100 gp that could
fit within the chest's dimensions. They can be specially created items for the chest (such as a
silvered [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger)) or a standard item (such as 50 feet of
[silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft)). Thereafter, when the box is closed, the owner need only
speak the name of one of the the items, and it appears within the chest, ready to be removed and
used. A given item only appears once per day, and disappears after 24 hours. Prophets use these
chests to keep backups of important tools, and to hide weapons in case of emergency.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[minor creation](ref/spell/minor-creation); **Cost** 2,500 gp
