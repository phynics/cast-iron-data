**Sources:** Pathfinder \#76: The Midnight Isles pg 61\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,000 gp; **Weight** -

A talisman of pure faith looks like a set of six of black and red hexagons nested within each other.
When worn about the neck, a talisman of pure faith physically transforms into a holy symbol that
matches the faith of the wearer, unless that information is blocked via a magical effect. It can be
utilized as a divine focus, and doesn't take up a neck slot when worn in this manner. If worn by
someone who doesn't worship a single deity, it remains in its hexagonal shape and cannot be used as
a divine focus. As long as it is carried or worn, a talisman of pure faith functions as a
[phylactery of faithfulness](ref/magic-wondrous/phylactery-of-faithfulness). In addition, while it
is worn, the talisman grants a +4 bonus on all [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks to
cast spells impeded by [planar traits](ref/rule/magic-traits).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos), [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil),
[detect good](ref/spell/detect-good), [detect law](ref/spell/detect-law); **Cost** 1,500 gp
