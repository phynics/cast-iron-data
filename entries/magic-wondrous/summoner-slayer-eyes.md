**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Crafted by the Arclords of Nex, these golden-white lenses rest in crystal frames that fit
comfortably over the eyes. Any creatures conjured by _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ or
a similar spell in the wearer's field of vision appear to the wearer as though they were surrounded
by a shimmering, purple glow. The wearer sees non-native creatures that have been called by other
forms of magic (such as [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or [gate](ref/spell/gate)) outlined in
a blue glow. This affects only creatures of which the wearer is already aware. It doesn't bypass
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or stealth, nor does it work if the target is under the effect
of an illusion spell that the wearer hasn't seen through.

Once per day when the wearer hits a summoned or called creature with a weapon, the wearer can
concentrate on that target, attempting to banish it back to its place of origin. This is treated as
a _[dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal)_ spell targeting the creature, which must succeed at a DC 16
Will save or be sent back to its home plane.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Cost** 5,500 gp
