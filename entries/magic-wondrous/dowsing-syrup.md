**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

The thick liquid in this ceramic jar smells sickly sweet. To activate its powers, the user it must
be unstopper the jar as a standard action while thinking deeply about a specific person or object
that she has seen at least once from up to 30 feet away. When then poured onto the ground, the syrup
flows at 15 feet per round following the most direct route to the creature or object visualized,
provided the creature or object is on the same plane, leaving behind a sticky trail. The syrup can
flow uphill or even up stairs and can pass through openings as narrow as one-sixteenth of an inch.
It cannot penetrate other liquids or porous or absorbent surfaces (such as sand or sawdust).

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
