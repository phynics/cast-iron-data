## Lycanthrope Skin (Skinwalker)

**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 24,000 gp (boar, rat, wolf), 40,000 gp (brown bear, bat, crocodile,
shark, tiger); **Weight** 2 lbs.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This animal-skin cloak comes in eight varieties (one for each kind of
[skinwalker](ref/race/skinwalker)). The wearer gains the ability to speak with animals of the
matching species and related species as per the spell
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)._ For example, a werewolf skin allows the wearer
to speak with canines. If the wearer is a skinwalker, and her skinwalker heritage matches that of
the cloak, the wearer can take the listed animal's form as if using the change shape ability,
gaining the base creature's size, form, and special abilities as per
_[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1)._ As normal for a skinwalker's transformation, her animal
form lasts until she chooses to end it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1), [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),
creator must be a skinwalker; **Cost** 12,000 gp (boar, rat, wolf), 20,000 gp (brown bear, bat,
crocodile, shark, tiger)
