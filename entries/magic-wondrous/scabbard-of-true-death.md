**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 32,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Whenever an [assassin](ref/pclass/assassin) draws a weapon from the scabbard of true death and makes
a successful death attack against her target, the DC of her true death ability increases by 4. In
addition, regardless of whether the wielder has levels in the assassin class, the wielder gains the
[bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack) rogue talent as though she had a sneak attack that
dealt 1d6 points of damage (thus, attacks that qualify as sneak attacks deal 1 point of
[bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage); if the wielder already has the bleeding attack rogue talent, the
bleed damage caused by that talent is increased by 1.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse), creator must have the bleeding attack rogue talent; **Cost**
16,000 gp
