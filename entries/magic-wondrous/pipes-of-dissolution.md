**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 315\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Carvings of winter scenes decorate this set of pan pipes. Activating the pipes requires a DC 15
[Perform (wind)](ref/skill/perform-any) check. Creatures within 30 feet gain a +2 competence bonus
on caster level checks to _dispel_ magical effects and overcome
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance). Maintaining the performance is challenging, requiring
a move action and a new Perform check each round, with the DC increasing by +1 for each round the
instrument is played. The magic ends when the player voluntarily stops or fails the Perform check,
after which the pipes cannot be used again for 1 hour.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic); **Cost** 6,000 gp
