**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This sturdy tool has a wooden haft topped with a curved pick head backed with a sharp adze blade at
one end and capped with an iron spike on the other. When used as a climbing aid, an alpine ice axe
grants a +2 circumstance bonus on [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks to scale rock or ice walls. In
addition, an alpine ice axe negates any Climb DC modifiers for climbing a slippery surface. Three
times per day as a standard action, the wielder can strike an area of ice or snow with the ice axe
to instantly create a series of sturdy handholds in the ice covering a distance of up to 40 feet. An
ice wall with these handholds has a Climb DC of 15.

An alpine ice axe is a tool, not a weapon, but it may be used as an
[improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) with the usual -4 penalty on attack rolls made with
it. An alpine ice axe wielded as a weapon functions either as a +1
[light pick](ref/eq-weapon/light-pick) when striking with the pick spike or as a +1
[hand axe](ref/eq-weapon/handaxe) when striking with the adze blade. The ice axe uses the standard
threat range and critical modifier for those weapons. An alpine ice axe deals double damage against
inanimate ice or snow, and if used as a weapon against a creature made mostly of ice (such as an
[ice elemental](ref/monster/ice-elemental) or [ice golem](ref/monster/golem-ice-golem)), an alpine
ice axe bypasses that creature's [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [shatter](ref/spell/shatter),
[snow shape](ref/spell/snow-shape), creator must have 5 ranks in the Climb skill; **Cost** 4,176 gp
