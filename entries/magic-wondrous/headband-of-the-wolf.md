**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 230\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This silver band is cast to resemble a pack of hunting wolves. On command, the wearer can gain the
[scent](ref/umr/scent) ability with a range of 10 feet (20 feet upwind, 5 feet downwind) as the
[wolf](ref/aspect/wolf) aspect of the hunter's animal focus class feature. If the wearer is a
[hunter](ref/class/hunter) with that aspect, this headband instead increases the wearer's effective
hunter level for the wolf aspect by 8 levels.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bloodhound](ref/spell/bloodhound), creator must have the animal focus class feature; **Cost** 2,500
gp
