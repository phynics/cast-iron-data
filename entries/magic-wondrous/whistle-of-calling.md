**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 237\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,300 gp; **Weight** -

This whistle emits a loud, shrill note. Once per day when the user blows this whistle his mount or
animal companion is instantly teleported to a safe space of the user's choice that's within 30 feet
of him. The creature must be within 1 mile, and any effect that bars or hampers teleportation also
impedes this effect. The whistle is useless unless attuned to a specific mount or animal companion;
attuning the whistle is a special ritual that takes 10 minutes. The whistle can be attuned to a new
mount or animal companion at will.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport); **Cost** 3,150 gp
