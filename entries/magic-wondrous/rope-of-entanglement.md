**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 318, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 529\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 21,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

A rope of entanglement looks just like any other hempen rope about 30 feet long. Upon command, the
rope lashes forward 20 feet or upward 10 feet to entangle a victim. An
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) creature can break free with a DC 20 Strength check or a DC 20
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) check.

A rope of entanglement has AC 22, 12 hit points, [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10, and
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 5/slashing. The rope repairs damage to itself at a
rate of 1 point per 5 minutes, but if a rope of entanglement is severed (all 12 hit points lost to
damage), it is destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [animate rope](ref/spell/animate-rope) or
[entangle](ref/spell/entangle); **Cost** 10,500 gp
