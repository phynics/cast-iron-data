**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 29\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 21,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This single, jade-encrusted [ironwood](ref/spell/ironwood) pauldron glistens slightly, as if it were
the reflective surface of a still pool, even in complete darkness. This pauldron increases the
virulence of all poisons used by the wearer. Once per day, when attempting to poison a target, the
wearer can speak a command word to magically enhance a single dose of poison, making it immune to
the effects of [delay poison](ref/spell/delay-poison). The poison's DC is unaffected and the poison
can still be affected by a [neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison) spell and similar
magical effects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[overwhelming poison](ref/spell/overwhelming-poison); **Cost** 10,800 gp
