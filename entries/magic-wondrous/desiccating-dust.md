**Sources:** Rival Guide pg 17\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This yellowish gray powder is composed of grave dirt and ground snakeskin. The user spreads the dust
in two 5-foot squares, typically across doorways frequented by the intended victims - this takes 1d4
rounds to accomplish.

A creature stepping into an area warded by desiccating dust must make a DC 19 Fortitude save to
avoid suddenly becoming horrifically thirsty and wracked with pain, taking 5d6 points of nonlethal
damage, and immediately becoming [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued). The nonlethal damage caused by this
effect cannot be healed until the creature can drink some water. A creature that remains in an area
warded by desiccating dust need not make additional saves to avoid the effect, but if it steps out
of the area, it must save again as soon as it reenters the warded area. A creature already taking
nonlethal damage or fatigue from desiccating dust suffers no additional ill effect from passing
through an area protected by this magical dust. Once a dose of desiccating dust is applied to an
area, that area remains warded by the effect for 24 hours.

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
