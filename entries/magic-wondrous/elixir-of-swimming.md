**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 295, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 511\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 2nd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 250 gp; **Weight** -

This elixir bestows swimming ability. An almost imperceptible magic sheath surrounds the drinker,
allowing him to glide through the water easily (+10 competence bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim)
checks for 1 hour).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must have 5 ranks in
the Swim skill; **Cost** 125 gp
