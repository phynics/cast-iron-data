**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 317\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

A racing broom of flying functions like a [broom of flying](ref/magic-wondrous/broom-of-flying),
except for 9 minutes per day it can fly faster than its standard counterpart. Activating this
increased speed is a swift action. The broom's increased fly speed is 60 feet with a load up to 200
pounds, or at a speed of 40 feet carrying up to 400 pounds. Each minute at this faster speed uses 1
hour of the broom's daily flight time. As a swift action, the rider may expend 5 hours of flight
time to increase the broom's speed to 120 feet with a load up to 200 pounds, or at a speed of 80
feet carrying up to 400 pounds. This speed boost lasts for 1 round, but when it ends there is a 50%
chance that the broom gains the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition and is powerless until repaired.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [haste](ref/spell/haste),
[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight), [permanency](ref/spell/permanency); **Cost** 15,000 gp
