**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 348

**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Weight** 1 lb.

This gray magical cloak seems to be a protective cloak, but when it is donned, the wearer must
succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or be instantly [petrified](ref/rule/petrified). If the initial
saving throw succeeds, the creature wearing the cloak must make further checks at the start of each
of its turns. On any failed saving throw the creature becomes petrified. A
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) or [stone to flesh](ref/spell/stone-to-flesh) transforms the
wearer back, at least long enough for someone to take the cloak off the wearer.

### Construction

**Requirements** any cloak; **Cost** -
