**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 318, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 529\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 16,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This black scabbard is engraved with intricate silver runes that depict all manner of bladed
weapons. A scabbard of keen edges can shrink or enlarge to accommodate any knife, dagger, sword, or
similar weapon up to and including a [greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword). Up to three times per
day on command, the scabbard casts _[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge)_ on any blade placed within it.
Runes similar to those on the outside of the scabbard appear on a blade while it is under this
_[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge)_ effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Cost** 8,000 gp
