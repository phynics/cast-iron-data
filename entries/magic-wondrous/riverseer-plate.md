**Sources:** People of the River pg 29\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This disc of thick green glass is 12 inches in diameter and has a small, stylized image of a boat
acid - etched into its center. When set against the deck of a waterborne vessel, the plate warns of
nearby obstacles on or under the water, presenting them as glowing green silhouettes on the glass in
relation to the boat etched on the plate's center. A riverseer plate warns of inanimate dangers
within a 300-foot radius beyond the vessel in which it is set, providing a +6 competence bonus on
[sailing checks](ref/rule/sailing-check) to navigate the vessel around potential dangers.

Once per day, a creature can apply a drop of blood to a riverseer plate's surface. For 5 minutes
thereafter, the plate marks the location of Small or larger animate creatures in the water around
the vessel, presenting them on the plate's surface as glowing red dots.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants), [riversight](ref/spell/riversight);
**Cost** 2,500 gp
