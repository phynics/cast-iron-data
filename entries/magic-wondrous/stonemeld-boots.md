**Sources:** Heroes of the Darklands pg 8\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The wearer of a pair of stonemeld boots gains a +4 bonus to CMD against
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [drag](ref/rule/drag), [reposition](ref/rule/reposition), and
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuvers. Additionally, once per day as a swift action, the boots can
be activated to grant the wearer a climb speed of 10 feet on any natural stone surface (but not
manufactured stone surfaces like brick walls) for 30 minutes. A creature using stonemeld boots to
climb in this fashion leaves obvious deformities in the rock surface. Anyone attempting to
[track](ref/skill/survival) a wearer of stonemeld boots moving in this fashion treats the base DC as
though the tracks were left in soft earth, and takes no penalties for time elapsed since the tracks
were made.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[transmute rock to mud](ref/spell/transmute-rock-to-mud); **Cost** 1,750 gp
