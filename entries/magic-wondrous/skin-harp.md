**Sources:** Pathfinder \#63: The Asylum Stone pg 58\
**Aura** moderate enchantment and necromancy; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 16,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This small harp is made from a [sprite's](ref/monster/sprite) twisted, leathery corpse, its arms and
legs arching up to hold the crossbar along the instrument's top. The strings are made from thin
strips of twisted flesh flayed from the fey's torso and strung between their anchor points on the
corpse and the crossbar. Though the corpse's chest cavity is clearly visible, all viscera have been
removed.

A skin harp functions as a [masterwork harp](ref/eq-misc/musical-instrument-masterwork). In
addition, once per day, it can be activated to cast either
_[command undead](ref/spell/command-undead)_ or _[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person)_ (Will
DC 19 negates). With a successful DC 20 [Perform (string)](ref/skill/perform-any) check, the save DC
for this effect increases by +2. When used in this manner, the harp animates and plays itself, the
corpse freeing one hand from the crossbar in order to strum its flesh strings.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[command undead](ref/spell/command-undead), [dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person); **Cost**
8,550 gp
