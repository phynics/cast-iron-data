**Sources:** Pathfinder \#113: What Grows Within pg 33\
**Aura** strong varied; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** body; **Weight** 1 lb.

This robe functions much as a robe of stars, granting its wearer the luck bonus on saving throws,
astral travel, and ability to create shuriken made of magically hardened gold. In addition, the
wearer gains a +4 armor bonus to his AC.

The robe's curse activates after 1 month of being worn regularly, or just as a creature whose patron
is [Hastur](ref/faith/hastur) or [Xhamen-Dor](ref/faith/xhamen-dor) affects the wearer with a spell
or special ability. The wearer loses his luck bonus on saving throws against such effects and
instead takes a -4 penalty on those saves. While the curse persists, the wearer takes 1d4 points of
[Wisdom drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) every time he uses the robe to access the Astral
Plane, and the shuriken created are +5 [anarchic](ref/magic-enh/anarchic) shuriken. After another
month of continuous use, the wearer permanently becomes chaotic evil and pursues increasingly
delusional goals of domination, enrichment, or anarchy. Once donned, this robe can be removed only
with a [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell.
