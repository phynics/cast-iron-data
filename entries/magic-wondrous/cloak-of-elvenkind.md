**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 263, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 507\
**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These cloaks of famed elven design are enhanced to reflect the wearer's surroundings, aiding him in
blending in with any terrain. When this plain gray cloak is worn with the hood drawn up around the
head, the wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), creator must be an elf; **Cost** 1,250 gp
