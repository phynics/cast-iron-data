**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 14,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Several alterations along the edges of this well-maintained leather
[saddle](ref/eq-misc/saddle-riding) allow for the movement of a mount's wings. A Sable Company elite
saddle grants a mount wearing it a +5 competence bonus on [Fly](ref/skill/fly) checks, and grants
the rider a +5 competence bonus on [Ride](ref/skill/ride) checks. If the rider is unsaddled during
flight, he is immediately subject to a [feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall) effect.

The mount additionally gains the benefits of any teamwork feats the rider possesses.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [fly](ref/spell/fly),
[mount](ref/spell/mount), creator must have 5 ranks in Ride; **Cost** 7,000 gp
