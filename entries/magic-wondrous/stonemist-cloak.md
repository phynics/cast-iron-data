**Aura** faint conjuration and illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

In rocky or mountainous areas, when this stone-gray cloak is worn with the hood drawn up around the
head, the wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. Once per day,
the wearer can create an area of fog equivalent to _[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist); **Cost** 1,750
gp
