**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 150\
**Aura** strong illusion; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This rough-spun green cloak is trimmed in wolf fur and fastened with a silver clasp. With the cloak
drawn over his shoulders and the hood pulled up, the wearer becomes one with his surroundings,
gaining a +5 bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks and on [Survival](ref/skill/survival)
skill checks made to follow tracks. The cloak also renders the wearer immune to the
[scent](ref/umr/scent) ability.

If the wearer expends one use of mythic power, the cloak turns him invisible for 1 hour, as
_[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater)._ If the wearer makes an attack, the effects
last for 1 minute after the attack, then end. A character made invisible by this cloak can't be
detected with [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) or other spells or effects that detect magical
auras. The greater invisibility can't be penetrated, revealed, or dispelled by spells of 2nd level
or lower (such as [see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility) or
[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust)), though [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) and
[dust of appearance](ref/magic-wondrous/dust-of-appearance) can reveal the wearer's presence.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater);
**Cost** 6,000 gp
