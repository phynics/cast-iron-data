**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 31\
**Aura** moderate divination and illusion; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,650 gp; **Weight** -

This intricate mechanism opens like a pocket watch to reveal a set of dials indicating the current
phase of the moon, as well as the previous and upcoming phases. A moon clock can be destroyed as a
standard action to release a powerful blast of lunar energy in a 30-foot-radius burst.
[Lycanthropes](ref/template/lycanthrope) in the area of this blast must succeed at a DC 20 Will save
or revert to their humanoid forms.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[divination](ref/spell/divination), [lunar veil](ref/spell/lunar-veil); **Cost** 1,325 gp
