**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 47\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

Favored by half-elves seeking to distill their heritage into a combat edge, an ancestral clasp
resembles a metal tube, open on both sides and covered in delicate elven calligraphy. Whenever a
[half-elf](ref/race/half-elf) fits an ancestral clasp to the pommel, grip, or haft of a
[longbow](ref/eq-weapon/longbow), [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword),
[rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier), or [shortbow](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow), the character is treated as
though she were proficient with that weapon. In addition, when an ancestral clasp is fitted to any
weapon with the word "elven" in the title, the character treats the weapon as if it were a martial
weapon, mirroring the Weapon Familiarity elven racial trait. A half-elf who already is proficient
with one of the above weapons adorned with an ancestral clasp receives a +1 insight bonus on attack
rolls with that weapon. Although this ability stems from the item's magic, the affected weapon is
treated as nonmagical for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).
Affixing and removing an ancestral clasp is a standard action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[paragon surge](ref/spell/paragon-surge), creator must be a half-elf; **Cost** 5,000 gp
