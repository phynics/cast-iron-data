**Sources:** Lost Treasures pg 17\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 37,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This sheet of charred bark is 2 feet long, 10 inches wide, and 1 inch thick. The Atavistic Splinter
imbues its owner with a primal authority over beasts, providing a +4 insight bonus on
[wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks.

Once each day, the owner can touch the Atavistic Splinter to a humanoid creature to force an
animalistic transformation. Using this ability against an unwilling target requires a melee touch
attack. A target touched by the Atavistic Splinter must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save or acquire
the [bestial creature](ref/template/bestial-creature) simple template for 24 hours. After this
period, the target must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude save or retain the template for an additional
24 hours, except the Intelligence and Charisma ability score penalties from the template each
increase to -4. Each day it retains the template, the target must attempt another Fortitude save; on
each successive day, the Fortitude save DC decreases by 2, and the Intelligence and Charisma
penalties increase by 2. These penalties can't reduce either ability score below 2. This effect
can't be dispelled, but can be removed with [break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [miracle](ref/spell/miracle),
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), or [wish](ref/spell/wish). If the target's Intelligence and
Charisma scores both reach 2 as a result of this effect, the template becomes permanent and can't be
removed except with _[miracle](ref/spell/miracle)_ or _[wish](ref/spell/wish)._ A target that
succeeds at a saving throw against this effect can't be affected by the Atavistic Splinter for 1
year.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dominate animal](ref/spell/dominate-animal), [polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph); **Cost** 18,500 gp
