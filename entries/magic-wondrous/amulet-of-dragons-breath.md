**Sources:** Pathfinder \#70: The Frozen Stars pg 62\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 4,800 gp; **Weight** -

This amulet is crafted from a dragon scale hung on a thin wire of twisted
[mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral). There are 10 different types of amulet of dragon's breath, each
corresponding to one of the 10 [dragon](ref/family/dragon-true) types. As a standard action, the
wearer of an amulet of dragon's breath can breathe out a blast of energy similar to a dragon's
breath weapon. This breath weapon deals 8d6 points of energy damage (Reflex DC 16 for half). The
breath weapon's shape and energy type depend on the type of dragon scale used to craft the amulet. A
new amulet of dragon's breath can be used three times before the dragon scale cracks and the amulet
becomes useless.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dragon's breath](ref/spell/dragons-breath); **Cost** 2,400 gp
