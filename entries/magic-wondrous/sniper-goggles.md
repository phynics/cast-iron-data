**Aura** faint divination (normal), moderate divination (greater); **CL** 5th (normal), 8th
(greater)\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 20,000 gp (normal), 50,000 gp (greater); **Weight** 1 lb. (normal), —
(greater)

The leather strap attached to these bulbous lenses allows their wearer to fit them to his head. The
wearer of these goggles can make ranged [sneak attacks](ref/ability/sneak-attack) from any distance
instead of the normal 30 feet. When making ranged sneak attacks within 30 feet, the wearer gains a
+2 circumstance bonus on each sneak attack damage die.

**Greater Sniper Goggles:** These goggles feature stubby periscope-like lenses that expand and
retract depending on the needs of the wearer. These goggles function as _sniper goggles,_ but the
wearer gains the +2 circumstance bonus on each sneak attack when making a sneak attack at any range.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Cost** 10,000 gp (normal), 25,000 gp (greater)
