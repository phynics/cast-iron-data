**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 29\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These simple wooden sandals allow their wearer to step in the way of danger to protect her allies.
Three times per day as an immediate action, the wearer can take a 5-foot step to move into the path
of a ranged attack and treat herself as the target of the ranged attack that passes through her
square for the purposes of [Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows),
[Snatch Arrows](ref/feat/snatch-arrows), [Missile Shield](ref/feat/missile-shield), and other feats
and abilities that allow her to deflect ranged attacks. If she lacks a feat or ability that would
allow her to deflect the ranged attack, the wearer is instead simply struck by the attack regardless
of the attack roll's result or her AC. The wearer can't take a 5-foot step during the turn after
using this ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), Deflect Arrows,
[deflection](ref/spell/deflection); **Cost** 4,000 gp
