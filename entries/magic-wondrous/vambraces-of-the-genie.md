## Vambraces of the Genie

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 275\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 14,400 gp (Efreeti), 18,900 gp (Djinni), 18,900 gp (Marid), 18,900 gp
(Shaitan); **Weight** 3 lbs.

These bracers are decorated with icons representing air, earth, fire, or water, and depictions of
genies appropriate to that element. The item protects the wearer against hot and cold environments
as if she were using endure elements The vambraces' materials and other powers depend on their
associated genie type.

> _Djinni:_ These [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) bracers allow the wearer to use
> [gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form), [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), and
> [wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall) each once per day.
>
> _Efreeti:_ These scorched bronze bracers allow the wearer to use _invisibility_ (self only),
> [produce flame](ref/spell/produce-flame), and [pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics) each once per
> day.
>
> _Marid:_ These coral bracers allow the wearer to use
> [hydraulic torrent](ref/spell/hydraulic-torrent), _invisibility,_ and
> [water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing) each once per day.
>
> _Shaitan:_ These [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) bracers allow the wearer to use
> [glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust), [meld into stone](ref/spell/meld-into-stone), and
> [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape) each once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), and the three spells the wearer can use; **Cost**
7,200 gp (Efreeti), 9,450 gp (Djinni), 9,450 gp (Marid), 9,450 gp (Shaitan)
