## Shoanti War Paint

**Aura** moderate (school varies^[1](ref/magic-wondrous/shoanti-war-paint-silver#footnote-s)^);
**CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 900 gp (Blue, Green, Orange, Red, Silver, and Yellow), 1,800 gp (Black and
White); **Weight** -

The Shoanti have developed numerous types of magical war paint, each providing a specific benefit to
aid their hunters, warriors, and heroes in a variety of tasks. Shoanti war paint can be applied to
any visible part of the body - typically the face, shoulders, legs, or arms. Applying a dose of war
paint is a full-round action that provokes an attack of opportunity. Shoanti war paint does not take
up a magic item slot, but you can benefit from only one color of Shoanti war paint at a time and
applying a dose of a different color replaces the effects of the previous application. Once Shoanti
war paint is applied, its effects last for 24 hours. Below are the eight most common colors and
their effects.

- **Black (1,800 gp):** The wearer becomes cloaked in shadows and smoke, gaining the effects of a
  [blur](ref/spell/blur) spell.
- **Blue (900 gp):** The wearer gains a 30-foot enhancement bonus to her base move speed.
- **Green (900 gp):** When the wearer uses bardic performance to
  [inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage), the morale bonus she grants increases by 1.
- **Orange (900 gp):** The wearer gains [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 1/-. This
  effect stacks with damage reduction gained via a character class, such as
  [barbarian](ref/class/barbarian).
- **Red (900 gp):** The wearer gains fire resistance 10.
- **Silver (900 gp):** The wearer gains a +3 deflection bonus to her AC.
- **White (1,800 gp):** The wearer gains a +4 resistance bonus on all saving throws against
  [energy drain](ref/umr/energy-drain) and negative energy. As soon as the wearer takes a
  [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) from an energy drain attack, the white
  war paint absorbs the negative level and then fades away, ending the ongoing duration of the
  paint's effect immediately.
- **Yellow (900 gp):** The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception)
  checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item); [blur](ref/spell/blur)
(black), [clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance) (yellow),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward) (white), [expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat)
(blue), [heroism](ref/spell/heroism) (green), [resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy) (red),
[shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith) (silver), [stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin) (orange);
**Cost** 900 gp (black and white paint), 450 gp (all other paints)

###### ^1^ {#footnote-s}

There are no official sources as to which school auras each color has. As a non-official suggestion
based on the spells required: black is illusion; yellow is divination; white is necromancy; blue is
transmutation; green is enchantment; and red, orange, and silver are abjuration.
