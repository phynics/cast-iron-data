**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 239\
**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** -

These black leather gloves are lined with soft black velveteen and have silver buckles that run from
the wrist to the elbow. Three times per day, the gloves allow the wearer to treat a ranged magus
spell as a spell with a range of "touch," allowing him to deliver the spell with his spellstrike
ability. The glove can only affect spells that normally affect one or more creatures at a range
greater than "touch" (such as [slow](ref/spell/slow)), not rays or other created effects. The
altered spell only affects the creature attacked (any other targets normally allowed by the spell
are lost).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be a
[magus](ref/class/magus); **Cost** 4,000 gp
