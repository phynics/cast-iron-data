**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 264\
**Aura** faint illusion and transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** head; **Price** 2,400 gp; **Weight** -

This crumpled leather hat has roughly hammered metal studs around its brim, and is often worn by
worshipers of [Droskar](ref/faith/droskar) who venerate the dwarf god for his cheating aspect. The
wearer can use it as a [hat of disguise](ref/magic-wondrous/hat-of-disguise), but only to disguise
herself as a dwarf. Three times per day as a free action, the wearer can manipulate a rolled die
within 10 feet (such as in a game of chance), altering the roll by a value of 1 in either direction.
The wearer adds 1 to the DC of any enchantment (compulsion) spells she casts.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell), [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self),
[mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand); **Cost** 1,200 gp
