**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 26,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This oily bronze helm is fashioned in the shape of a frog's head. Three times per day as a swift
action, the helm fires an unerring tongue of force at a visible target within 20 feet.

If the target is an unattended object weighing 250 pounds or less, it is pulled 20 feet toward the
wearer. If the target is a creature of the wearer's size or smaller, the wearer may make a
[combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) check as a free action to pull the target 20 feet
toward him.

If the target is a creature larger than the wearer, an unattended object weighing more than 250
pounds, or an immobile structure such as a wall, the wearer is pulled 20 feet toward the target.
This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity, and the object or creature stops if the
movement would move it into a solid object or creature.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Cost** 13,000 gp
