**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 291, Advanced Player's Guide pg 304\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 800 gp; **Weight** -

This container of sickly gray paste can coat the armor of one Medium or two Small creatures.
Affected objects produce dark gray fumes for the next 24 hours. These fumes cause natural plants to
wilt and diminish as soon as they come into contact with the affected armor. This allows the wearer
to move through terrain made [difficult](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) due to the presence of
vegetation as if it were normal terrain, though the creature leaves a clear path of ruined
vegetation. In addition, creatures belonging to the plant type that target someone protected by
defoliant polish with a natural attack or [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) take 1 point of damage with
each successful attack or each round they maintain the grapple.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[diminish plants](ref/spell/diminish-plants); **Cost** 400 gp
