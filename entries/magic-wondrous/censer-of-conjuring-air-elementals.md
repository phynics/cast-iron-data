**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 288\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 90,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This 6-inch-wide, perforated metallic vessel resembles a thurible found in a place of worship. The
censer must be filled with incense to function. On command as a full-round action, the bearer may
summon an air elemental. If the censer is filled with
[incense of meditation](ref/magic-wondrous/incense-of-meditation), it conjures a
[greater air elemental](ref/monster/air-elemental) as if using
_[summon monster VII](ref/spell/summon-monster-7)._ If filled with any other incense, it conjures a
[Large air elemental](ref/monster/air-elemental) as if using
_[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5)._ Conjuring an elemental consumes any normal incense
in the censer, or one-fourth of a block of incense of meditation. The censer cannot summon another
elemental until the previous one is dismissed, killed, or the duration of its summoning expires.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5), [summon monster VII](ref/spell/summon-monster-7);
**Cost** 45,000 gp
