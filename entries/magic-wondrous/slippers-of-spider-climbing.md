**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 233, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 530\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 4,800 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

When worn, a pair of these slippers enables movement on vertical surfaces or even upside down along
ceilings, leaving the wearer's hands free. Her climb speed is 20 feet. Severely slippery surfaces -
icy, oiled, or greased surfaces - make these slippers useless. The slippers can be used for 10
minutes per day, split up as the wearer chooses (minimum 1 minute per use).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb); **Cost** 2,400 gp
