**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 10\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** -

A common accoutrement often crafted by worshipers of [Hei Feng](ref/faith/hei-feng), these magical
tassels are usually affixed to the pommels of bladed weapons, though they can be fastened to other
weapons as well. The tassel has no effect unless attached to a weapon, and even then, it activates
only under certain conditions.

The wielder gains a +4 circumstance bonus on [performance combat](ref/rule/performance-combat)
checks. When the wielder takes a standard action to
[fight defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively), the next attack that misses her before the start
of her next round causes the attacker to take 3d6 points of electricity damage. Once the electricity
is discharged, the crackling tassel can't be used again for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[defensive shock](ref/spell/defensive-shock), [hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism); **Cost** 1,250 gp
