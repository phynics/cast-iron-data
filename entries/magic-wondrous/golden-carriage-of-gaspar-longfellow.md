**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 114,000 gp; **Weight** 1,200 lbs.

Crafted from the finest materials, the Golden Carriage of Gaspar Longfellow is a vehicle fit for
royalty. Larger than an average carriage, the Golden Carriage can accommodate six occupants in the
interior, as well as two drivers and a footman on the exterior. The interior is lavishly furnished
and magically maintained - occupants are protected from extreme heat and cold as if under the
effects of [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), and are treated as acclimated creatures for
altitudes up to 15,000 feet (see [altitude zones](ref/rule/mountain-travel)). Both doors of the
carriage are secured against intruders with [alarm](ref/spell/alarm) and
[arcane lock](ref/spell/arcane-lock) spells, and an invisible driver can control the reins as
[unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant) if no living driver is controlling the carriage.

The most prominent features of the Golden Carriage are the four spectral steeds that pull it.
Adorned in beautiful scarlet leather harnesses, these gray horses pull the carriage tirelessly over
nearly any terrain. These horses function as _[phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed)_ cast by a
12th-level caster, and grant their [air walk](ref/spell/air-walk),
[water walk](ref/spell/water-walk), and other abilities to the carriage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed), [secure shelter](ref/spell/secure-shelter); **Cost**
57,000 gp
