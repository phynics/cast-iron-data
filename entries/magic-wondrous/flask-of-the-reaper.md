**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 264, Gods and Magic pg 57\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,800 gp; **Weight** -

This metal container looks like a hip flask for carrying alcoholic drinks except it is barely larger
than a potion vial. The side of the flask may be blank or have a personalized decoration, but the
screw-on stopper usually bears a mark like a shield or perhaps a featureless mask. Once per day, the
bearer can drink from the flask to gain a +5 alchemical bonus on Fortitude saving throws against
poison and a +5 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks for 1 hour.

If [Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber) is the bearer's patron, once per day the bearer can drink from
the flask to gain a +5 competence bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception),
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand), or Stealth checks (drinker's choice) for 1 hour. In
addition, the bearer can pour [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid) from the flask (equivalent in volume to a
standard acid flask) once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[acid splash](ref/spell/acid-splash), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance), creator must have 5 ranks
in Perception, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth; **Cost** 1,900 gp
