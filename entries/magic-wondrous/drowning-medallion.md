**Sources:** People of the River pg 28\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Often crafted by the people of Riverton and sold to traveling river-goers, this small stone disk is
etched with the symbol of waves and affixed to a silver chain. If the wearer falls unconscious from
[drowning](ref/rule/drowning) while wearing a drowning medallion, she can remain unconscious for up
to 10 minutes before she must start attempting Constitution checks to avoid asphyxiation. One round
after emerging from the substance that caused the wearer to drown (usually water), the wearer
regains consciousness at 0 hit points and the medallion crumbles to dust.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[air bubble](ref/spell/air-bubble); **Cost** 500 gp
