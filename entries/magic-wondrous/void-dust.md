**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 324\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** -

Infused with the essence of destruction, a pinch of void dust unmakes force effects. If sprinkled on
or thrown at a force effect, it acts as a [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), unless the force
effect is immune to _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),_ in which case it acts like
_[disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate)._ Void dust can be sprinkled on an adjacent force effect or
thrown up to 10 feet. No attack roll is required. A single pinch affects only a single force effect.
A found container holds three pinches.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate); **Cost** 2,250 gp
