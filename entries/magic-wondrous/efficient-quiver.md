**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 294, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 511\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,800 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This appears to be a typical arrow container capable of holding about 20 arrows. It has three
distinct portions, each with a non-dimensional space allowing it to store far more than would
normally be possible. The first and smallest one can contain up to 60 objects of the same general
size and shape as an arrow. The second slightly longer compartment holds up to 18 objects of the
same general size and shape as a javelin. The third and longest portion of the case contains as many
as 6 objects of the same general size and shape as a bow (spears, staffs, or the like). Once the
owner has filled it, the quiver can quickly produce any item she wishes that is within the quiver,
as if from a regular quiver or scabbard. The efficient quiver weighs the same no matter what's
placed inside it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[secret chest](ref/spell/secret-chest); **Cost** 900 gp
