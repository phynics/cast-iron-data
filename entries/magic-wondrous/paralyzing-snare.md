**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 31\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,250 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Once the command word is spoken, this 5-foot-diameter loop of wire blends with its surroundings. To
find it, a character with the [trapfinding](ref/ability/trapfinding) ability must succeed at a DC 30
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) check. Setting the snare is a full-round action that provokes
attacks of opportunity. Any creature that steps into the snare must succeed at a DC 26 Will save or
become magically [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed), as the spell
_[hold monster](ref/spell/hold-monster)._ This effect can be ended by uttering the command word,
succeeding at a DC 30 [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) check, or succeeding at the Will
save. The snare is destroyed when the effect ends.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [snare](ref/spell/snare),
[hold monster](ref/spell/hold-monster); **Cost** 1,200 gp
