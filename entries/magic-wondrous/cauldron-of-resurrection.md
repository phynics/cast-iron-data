**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 287, Advanced Player's Guide pg 303\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 33,000 gp; **Weight** 35 lbs.

The mouth of this heavy, dark iron cauldron is shaped like a monstrous maw and is large enough to
accommodate a single Medium creature. When the cauldron is filled with pure water and certain rare
and sacred herbs, and a deceased creature is placed inside it, the cauldron can restore the creature
to life as if with a [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) or _[resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection)_
spell. The spell effect depends on the components used: _[raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead)_ costs
5,000 gp worth of materials, while _[resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection)_ costs 10,000 gp worth of
materials. The cauldron is usable once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection); **Cost** 16,500 gp
