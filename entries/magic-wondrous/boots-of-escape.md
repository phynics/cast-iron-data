**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 228, Advanced Race Guide pg 38\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These sturdy leather-soled cloth boots have straps at the ankles and calves to ensure a snug fit.
Once per day, when the wearer of the boots is [grappled, pinned](ref/rule/grapple-conditions), or
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled), she may transfer herself to any spot within 30 feet as if using a
_[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_ spell. A [gnome](ref/race/gnome) wearing these boots
may instead transfer herself up to 400 feet away.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door), creator must be a gnome or a wizard specialized in the
school of conjuration; **Cost** 4,000 gp
