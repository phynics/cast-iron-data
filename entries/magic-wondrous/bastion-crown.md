**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court pg 30\
**Aura** faint conjuration and conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Often worn by paranoid rulers, this simple iron crown is thick and heavy. The wearer is immune to
ninja, rogue, and slayer talents that add effects to a [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack),
such as [bleeding attack](ref/talent/bleeding-attack) or
[crippling strike](ref/talent/crippling-strike), though the wearer takes the extra damage from sneak
attacks as normal. In addition, the wearer gains a +4 insight bonus on saving throws against death
effects and an [assassin's](ref/pclass/assassin) death attack ability. Finally, the wearer can cast
_[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison)_ on himself once per day, using the caster level
of the item for any caster level check required.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward), [neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison),
[stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin); **Cost** 2,250 gp
