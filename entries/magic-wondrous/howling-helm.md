**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 244\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 22,600 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The wearer of this helm, crafted from a beast's skull, gains the ability to communicate with dogs,
wolves, dire wolves, foxes, jackals, coyotes, and similar canine animals as the
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ spell. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus
on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks and Charisma checks to influence wolf-like magical beasts
such as [blink dogs](ref/monster/blink-dog), [winter wolves](ref/monster/worg-winter-wolf), and
[worgs](ref/monster/worg).

On command three times per day, the wearer can unleash a terrifying howl. When he does so, he makes
a single [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) check to demoralize all opponents within 30 feet that
can hear the howl. At the start of the wearer's following turn, 1d3 [wolves](ref/monster/wolf)
appear as if summoned by the spell _[summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3)._
They act on the wearer's initiative and follow her instructions (or attacking her opponents, if she
cannot communicate with them) for 5 rounds before vanishing in a swirl of pine needles and snow.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),
[summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3); **Cost** 11,300 gp
