**Aura** faint abjuration and conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This forest-green wool cloak is thick and warm. Its hem and the edge of its hood are embroidered
with small figures of forest creatures (rabbits, squirrels, and the like) in white thread.

When worn, this cloak bestows a +1 resistance bonus on all saving throws. The wearer can remove the
cloak at any time and lay it on the ground outdoors. This ends the resistance bonus but summons a
number of animals from the cloak. The animals serve the character who summoned them for 10 minutes
(or until slain or returned to the cloak's folds). A summoned creature can follow any of the
commands described in the [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) skill. Use the following tables
to determine what animals can be called forth.

The cloak summons creatures only when laid down outdoors on naturally occurring ground (not, for
example, on a wooden deck or stone pathway). The cloak can be used to summon animals up to three
times per day.

|d%|Animal|
| --- | --- |
|01-30|1 [owl](ref/monster/familiar-owl)|
|31-60|1d3 [rats](ref/monster/familiar-rat)|
|61-75|1 [weasel](ref/monster/familiar-weasel)|
|76-90|1d6 [squirrels](ref/monster/familiar-squirrel)|
|91-100|1d4 [rabbits](ref/monster/familiar-rabbit)|

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance), [summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1);
**Cost** 2,250 gp
