**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 247\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This ostentatious hat functions only for a wearer who can cast divine spells. The wearer gains a +5
competence bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks. Once per day on command, the wearer can
ask a single question of her patron deity, as if by using _[commune](ref/spell/commune)._ Once per
week on command, the wearer can touch a creature, atoning it for past misdeeds as if using an
_[atonement](ref/spell/atonement)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[atonement](ref/spell/atonement), [commune](ref/spell/commune), [guidance](ref/spell/guidance);
**Cost** 9,000 gp
