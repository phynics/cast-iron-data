**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 215, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 532\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 3,750 gp; **Weight** -

This light garment is worn over normal clothing or armor. Most such vestments are green, embroidered
with elaborate plant or animal motifs. When this item is worn by a character with the wild shape
ability, the character can use that ability one additional time each day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph) or wild shape ability; **Cost** 1,375 gp
