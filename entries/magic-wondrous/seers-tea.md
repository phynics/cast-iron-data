**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 319\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 550 gp; **Weight** -

This rich tea can be made from any number of herbs. When the tea is brewed without a filter and
imbibed, the drinker can read the patterns in the leaves remaining at the bottom of her cup, gaining
the benefits of an _[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ spell.

Additionally, the unbrewed tea can be used as an extra material component when casting
_[augury](ref/spell/augury),_ removing the need for a focus. In this case, the caster instead
reveals information as if she had cast _[divination](ref/spell/divination)_ instead.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [augury](ref/spell/augury) or
[divination](ref/spell/divination); **Cost** 275 gp
