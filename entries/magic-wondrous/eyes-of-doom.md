**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 224, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 512\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** -

These crystal lenses fit over the user's eyes, enabling him to cast _[doom](ref/spell/doom)_ upon
those around him (one target per round) as a [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack, except that the wearer
must take a standard action, and those merely looking at the wearer are not affected. Those failing
a DC 11 Will save are affected as by the _[doom](ref/spell/doom)_ spell. The wearer also gains the
additional power of a continual _[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch)_ effect and can use _fear_ (Will
DC 16 partial) as a normal gaze attack once per week. Both lenses must be worn for the magic item to
take effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [doom](ref/spell/doom),
[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch), [fear](ref/spell/fear); **Cost** 12,500 gp
