**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 262, Gods and Magic pg 56\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** belt or head; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** -

This long strip of red Qadiran fabric is wrapped around the wearer's waist or head several times and
held in place by an ankh-shaped clasp of red gold. The wearer is protected as if by
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),_ but only in warm environments. If the wearer unwraps
the sash to its full length and holds the clasp in her hand, she can use
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ once per day, briefly leaving behind a trail of pleasant
red-gold light as bright as a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch) that lasts for 1 round.

If [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae) is the wearer's patron, the wearer can use the sash to cast
_[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds)_ once per day on command. The wearer can trigger
this ability as an immediate action if reduced to -1 hit points or fewer, though thereafter the sash
must be recharged by placing it in strong sunlight for 8 continuous hours. As a swift action, the
wearer can cause the clasp to shine as brightly as a torch or cease this illumination; the light is
warm, feels like sunlight, and is bright enough to make sun-fearing creatures slightly uncomfortable
but not so bright as to cause them harm.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [light](ref/spell/light); **Cost** 1,250 gp
