**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 317\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This round metal container is just large enough to contain the wooden, coin-sized token inside. Each
side of the token is inscribed with a different prayer. Once per day, a [cleric](ref/class/cleric)
(or other class with cleric [domains](ref/ability/domains)) may activate the pyxes and may select
any one of her prepared domain spells and replace it with the domain spell of that level from her
other domain. For example, a cleric with the [Fire](ref/domain/fire) and [Luck](ref/domain/luck)
domains could switch her 1st-level prepared [burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands) domain spell to
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike). The pyxes has no effect on creatures with no domain spells or
only one domain.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [prayer](ref/spell/prayer);
**Cost** 500 gp
