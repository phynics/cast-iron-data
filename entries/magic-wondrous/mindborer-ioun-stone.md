**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** varies; **Weight** -

This cursed item looks like any normal [ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone), and has powers
according to its color and shape. However, whenever you are in combat, the orbiting stone batters at
your face and head, dealing 1 hit point of damage each round. If you sleep or fall unconscious while
the stone is in orbit, it drills into your head, dealing 1 point of
[Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) each round until the
penalty to one of these ability scores exceeds your actual score, at which point it removes itself
from your head and hides itself on your person, near other gems. The stone can remove itself from a
[wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder) to attack you in this fashion. The attack automatically
wakes a sleeping character. The only way to end the attack is to interfere with the magic of the
stone (by using [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), entering an
[antimagic field](ref/spell/antimagic-field), and so on) or grab it and place it in a container.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), creator must be
12th level; **Cost** varies
