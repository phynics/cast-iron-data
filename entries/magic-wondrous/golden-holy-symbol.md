**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 152\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This holy symbol is always warm to the touch, and whenever it strikes metal it rings out with the
sound of a choir of angels. When used as a divine focus for a divine spell, the symbol emits a
bright light that heals its wearer of a number of points of damage equal to the spell's level.

If the wearer expends one use of mythic power when casting a divine spell that uses this holy symbol
as its divine focus, the emblem glows for a number of rounds equal to the wearer's mythic tier,
shedding light like a [torch](ref/eq-misc/torch). Instead of this item's normal effect, at the start
of each of the wearer's turns, she and all her allies within 30 feet heal a number of points of
damage equal to 1d6 + the wearer's tier. This effect lasts as long as the light persists.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [cure critical wounds](ref/spell/cure-critical-wounds);
**Cost** 5,000 gp
