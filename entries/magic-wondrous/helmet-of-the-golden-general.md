**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 357

**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 62,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This golden gem-studded helmet acts like a [crown of conquest](ref/magic-wondrous/crown-of-conquest)
and grants its wielder three performance or teamwork feats. The wearer can use each of these feats
as if it were on his feat list. Once chosen, at the time of the item's creation, these feats cannot
be changed. The helmet must be worn for 24 hours in order to gain the teamwork or performance feats.

Furthermore this helmet can speak through a central ruby that sits over the nose guard of the
helmet. It knows Common and four other languages. These helmets are always confident - even
arrogant - and give a running commentary of the battle, praising heroism, chiding tactical missteps,
and giving its wearer and its allies martial advice. It has the following other powers.

- Three times per day, it can affect its wearer or an ally within 30 feet as the
  _[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike)_ spell.
- Three times per day, it can attempt to demoralize all enemies within 30 feet as a free action, as
  if using the [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) skill. The helmet's Intimidate is bonus +17.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), the item's performance and
teamwork feat, [bless](ref/spell/bless), [eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor),
[prayer](ref/spell/prayer), [scare](ref/spell/scare), [true strike](ref/spell/true-strike);
**Price** 31,000 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** neutral good; **Ego** 14\
**Senses** 30 ft.\
**Int** 14, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 14\
**Communication** speech (Common and four other languages)
