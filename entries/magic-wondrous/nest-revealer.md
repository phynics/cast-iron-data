**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This small vial is sealed with a fine metal cap engraved with the visage of a noble hound. When a
few drops of a creature's blood are placed in the vial, it begins to fill on its own, replicating
the creature's blood over the span of 1 minute. After 1 minute, the vial shatters and a small
fist-sized orb of crystallized blood falls to the ground. This animate orb rolls in the direction of
the last location in which the source creature slept for at least 1 hour. The blood travels at a
rate of 20 feet per round and can travel up to 10 miles over the span of 5 hours. If the orb does
not reach the creature's nest within 5 hours, it collapses into a puddle containing enough blood to
activate another nest revealer.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature); **Cost** 250 gp
