**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 18,700 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

A tangled layer of green moss grown on a thin, damp sheet of sod, this heavy cloak can be wrapped
around the wearer to provide a measure of camouflage. When donned in forest or jungle terrain, a
moss cloak gives its wearer a +10 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. In any
terrain, the wearer can remove a single poison currently affecting him once per day as a standard
action. This causes the moss to turn a sickly shade of brown as it draws the toxins from the
wearer's body, negating the Stealth bonus until the cloak heals itself 24 hours later.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison),
[restoration](ref/spell/restoration); **Cost** 9,350 gp
