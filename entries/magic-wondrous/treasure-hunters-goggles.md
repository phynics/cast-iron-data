**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 227\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 6,400 gp; **Weight** -

These sturdy but plain goggles help the wearer locate secret doors and coins and identify treasure.
While wearing these goggles, the wearer can use
_[detect secret doors](ref/spell/detect-secret-doors)_ at will. The goggles do not aid in
determining how secret doors are opened.

Once per day on command, the wearer gains the ability to _[locate object](ref/spell/locate-object)_
as per the spell, but only to detect 100 or more coins in a small area, such as a backpack, chest,
pouch, or sack.

Three times per day, the wearer may use _[identify](ref/spell/identify)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect secret doors](ref/spell/detect-secret-doors), [identify](ref/spell/identify),
[locate object](ref/spell/locate-object); **Cost** 3,200 gp
