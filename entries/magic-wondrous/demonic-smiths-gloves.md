**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 58\
**Aura** moderate evocation and transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Used by the drow smiths of house Caldrana, these worked black leather gloves are stitched with red
symbols dedicated to [Flauros](ref/faith/flauros), the demon lord of fire and volcanoes. Whenever
the wearer makes a melee touch attack, she deals 1d6 points of fire damage. If the wearer wields a
weapon or makes an attack with an unarmed strike, that attack gains the
[flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon special ability.

When demonic smith's gloves are used during the construction of armor or weapons, they grant their
wearer a +4 competence bonus on the relevant [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) checks. If the wearer
rolls a natural 1 while benefiting from this bonus, she draws the ire of Flauros, causing the gloves
to become nonmagical until the wearer crafts at least five masterwork items and then destroys them
in the name of the demon lord.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate), [fireball](ref/spell/fireball); **Cost** 4,000 gp
