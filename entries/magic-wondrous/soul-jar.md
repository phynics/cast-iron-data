**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

An empty soul jar can be used to contain a soul that has been successfully trapped in a black
sapphire via _[soul bind](ref/spell/soul-bind)._ Holding the gemstone against the soul jar's lid for
1 full round transfers the soul in the gem to the soul jar (which can hold the soul of a creature of
up to 20 Hit Dice), leaving the black sapphire unharmed 50% of the time. The rest of the time,
transferring the soul from gem to jar shatters the black sapphire. A creature that can use
_[soul bind](ref/spell/soul-bind)_ as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (and thus
does not use a focus) can automatically place a captured soul in a held soul jar as the spell-like
ability is used. A soul in a soul jar cannot travel to the Boneyard to be judged, but can be
restored to life via [resurrection](ref/spell/resurrection) or more powerful magic. A soul jar can
contain only one soul at a time. Soul jars are fragile ([hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 1, hp 10); if a
soul jar is broken, the soul held within immediately escapes to the Boneyard.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[soul bind](ref/spell/soul-bind); **Cost** 2,500 gp
