**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 247, Pathfinder \#2: The Skinsaw Murders pg 40, Rise of the
Runelords Anniversary Edition pg 427\
**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Preserved sections harvested from several different human faces cover this mask. When worn, the mask
desaturates the wearer's color, making him appear shadowy and granting a +5 competence bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. Once per day as a full-round action, the wearer can cause the
mask's features to take on the appearance of any creature of the wearer's basic size and shape
within 60 feet that he observes, allowing the wearer to adopt that creature's appearance for 1 hour.
This grants him a +10 bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks made to appear as the creature.
The mask also grants the wearer a +1 bonus on attack rolls and weapon damage rolls made against the
creature he is disguised as, as the mask builds upon the wearer's rage and jealousy of the target's
appearance.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self), [rage](ref/spell/rage); **Cost** 1,750 gp
