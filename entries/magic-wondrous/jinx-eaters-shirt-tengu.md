**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 232\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 22,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This shirt is stitched with dozens of symbols meant to attract bad luck, including bloodshot eyes
and tengu skulls. Whenever an ally within 60 feet is subject to a curse, hex, jinx, or effect that
would force her to roll multiple times for the same check and take the worse result, a
[tengu](ref/race/tengu) wearer can attempt to eat the effect as an immediate action. The wearer of
the shirt attempts a caster level check with a DC of 11 + the effect originator's caster level (or
HD for an ability that has no caster level). If the attempt is successful, the wearer suffers the
effect instead of the original target, and the wearer gains a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to the effect's caster level or the
target's HD, whichever is lower. These temporary hit points last for 1 minute. A jinx eater's shirt
can successfully redirect an effect originating from a given creature only once each day, but
otherwise it has no limitation on usage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), creator must be a tengu; **Cost** 11,300 gp
