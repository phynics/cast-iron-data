**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 309\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This undistinguished metal lump has no powers in its default state. By concentrating on it for 1
minute, a creature who worships a deity may transform it into a masterwork holy symbol of that
deity. Whenever this holy symbol is used as a focus for
[channeled energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), as a free action the bearer can alter the channel
area to one of the following options:

- 10-foot [burst](ref/misc/burst) centered anywhere within 30 feet
- 60-foot line
- 30-foot cone

The symbol reverts to its nondescript lump form after being away from its bearer for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[mass cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds-mass), creator must be able to channel energy;
**Cost** 5,000 gp
