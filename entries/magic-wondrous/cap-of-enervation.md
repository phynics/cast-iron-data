**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 9\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** -

This floppy leather hat allows a [boggard](ref/monster/boggard) or other frog-like creature to drain
the strength of those it strikes with its tongue attack. If the wearer ends its turn with its tongue
attached to or grappling another creature, the grappled creature takes a -1 penalty to Strength, and
the wearer's tongue gains 5 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points). The penalty and
temporary hit points last for 1 minute. For each round that the target remains attached, the
Strength penalty increases by 1, the tongue gains an additional 5 temporary hit points (maximum 10),
and the duration resets.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[ray of enfeeblement](ref/spell/ray-of-enfeeblement), [vampiric touch](ref/spell/vampiric-touch);
**Cost** 1,750 gp
