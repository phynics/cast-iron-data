**Aura** faint conjuration and evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,400 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This lightweight iron lantern houses a citrine gemstone that shines brightly with arcane energy as
if affected by a permanent _[light](ref/spell/light)_ spell. The bearer can suppress the lantern's
light or cause it to resume with a command word. Additionally, once per day by saying a different
command word, the bearer can use the lantern to cast _[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust)_ in a
10-foot-radius [spread](ref/misc/spread). Any creature caught in the effect must succeed at a DC 15
Will saving throw or be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 3 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust), [light](ref/spell/light); **Cost** 2,700 gp
