**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 230, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 504\
**Aura** faint abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The wearer of these boots is able to travel across snow at her normal speed, leaving no tracks.
Additionally, the boots enable her to travel at normal speed across the slipperiest ice (horizontal
surfaces only, not vertical or sharply slanted ones) without falling or slipping. Finally, boots of
the winterlands warm the wearer, as if she were affected by an
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
[pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace); **Cost** 1,250 gp
