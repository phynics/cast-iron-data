**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 68\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This translucent sphere of iron-hard glass changes shape to match its bearer's holy symbol within an
hour of coming into her possession. After that transformation is complete, when this symbol is used
by a [halfling](ref/race/halfling) with the luck or adaptable luck racial trait to
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) in order to heal, it also grants those healed a +1 luck
bonus on saving throws for a number of rounds equal to the number of dice healed by the channel
energy.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor), the creator must be a halfling; **Cost** 3,000 gp
