**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 314\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 36,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This globe of varicolored material constantly shifts hue and texture, from steel-hard to spongy or
even gelatinous. The bearer of the orb is constantly protected by
_[entropic shield](ref/spell/entropic-shield)._ Three times per day, when the bearer holds the orb
and casts a spell with the polymorph subtype, chaotic descriptor, or one that gives creatures the
[confused](ref/misc/confused) condition, she may modify her caster level for that spell by 1d6-2.

Once per day the bearer may hold the orb and command it to _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)._
If the dispel check succeeds, the wielder absorbs the unraveled magical energy through the orb and
gains the benefit of an _[aid](ref/spell/aid)_ spell.

The orb bestows one permanent [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on any
lawful creature holding or carrying it. The negative level remains as long as the creature has the
orb and disappears immediately when the orb is no longer held or carried. This negative level cannot
be overcome in any way (including by [restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while the bearer
has the orb.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [aid](ref/spell/aid),
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), [entropic shield](ref/spell/entropic-shield); **Cost**
18,000 gp
