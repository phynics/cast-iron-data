**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 55\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** -

This elaborate red and black quill contains an endless reservoir of red ink that resembles fresh
blood. Once per day, when within 5 feet of a living creature with -1 or fewer hit points, the bearer
can activate this quill as a standard action by scribing a short sentence in the air with it
describing the dying creature's death. If the victim fails a DC 15 Will save, it dies, and the
bearer gains a +5 profane bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff), [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), and
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks, a +1 profane bonus to caster level for
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and a +2 enhancement bonus to Charisma for 10
minutes per Hit Die of the victim.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), [honeyed tongue](ref/spell/honeyed-tongue); **Cost** 2,250 gp
