**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 269\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 14,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Subtle, indefinable shapes ripple and flow when this deep blue cloak moves, seemingly devouring any
light falling upon it. Once per day at the wearer's command, two large gray tentacles emerge from
the cloak near the shoulders. Each tentacle can make a natural attack that deals 1d6 points of
bludgeoning damage, assuming the wear is Medium (the damage of the tentacles scales by size of the
wearer as a [tentacle natural attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks)). The tentacles have 10-foot reach
and the [grab](ref/umr/grab) monster special ability. In addition, the wearer gains a +4 competence
bonus on [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) maneuver checks with the tentacles. The tentacles last for 1
minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph); **Cost** 7,000 gp
