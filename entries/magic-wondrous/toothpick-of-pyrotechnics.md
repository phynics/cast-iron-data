**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 236\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 400 gp; **Weight** -

This simple wooden toothpick is useful for creating flashy diversions on stage or in a fight.
Breaking the toothpick causes a blinding flash and a dark cloud of smoke to appear. Creatures other
than the user within 20 feet must succeed at a DC 13 Fortitude save or be
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4+1 rounds. The cloud of smoke spreads 20 feet in all directions,
obscures all sight (including [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)), and lasts for 3 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics); **Cost** 200 gp
