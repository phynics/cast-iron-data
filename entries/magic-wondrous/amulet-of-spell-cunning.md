**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 256, Advanced Player's Guide pg 30\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This silver locket only has power when used as a wizard's bonded object. It allows a
[wizard](ref/class/wizard) to prepare an additional 3 levels of spells per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[mnemonic enhancer](ref/spell/mnemonic-enhancer); **Cost** 5,000 gp
