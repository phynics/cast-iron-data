## Bridle of Tricks

**Sources:** Knights of the Inner Sea pg 30\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 900 gp (3 tricks), 1,600 gp (4 tricks), 2,500 gp (5 tricks); **Weight** 2
lbs.

Appearing to be a finely crafted bridle of polished steel and blue-dyed leather, a bridle of tricks
can be placed on any animal or magical beast with an appropriate shape to wear a bridle (creatures
with unusual anatomies may be affected with GM discretion). While wearing the bridle, a creature is
treated as knowing three, four, or five additional [tricks](ref/ability/animal-tricks), as if it had
been taught the tricks with the [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) skill. The tricks granted
by a bridle of tricks are set when it is created, and cannot be changed. The most common tricks
found in a bridle of tricks are attack, defend, fetch, guard, and heel.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals), creator must have 5 ranks in the Handle Animal
skill; **Cost** 450 gp (3 tricks), 800 gp (4 tricks), 1,250 gp (5 tricks)
