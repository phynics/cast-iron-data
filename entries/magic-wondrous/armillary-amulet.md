**Sources:** From Shore to Sea pg 17\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** -

This thin chain of silvery-gray metal holds a series of interlocking rings that slowly turn within
themselves in an accurate replication of the celestial sphere. The amulet grants its wearer a +5
competence bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks. It also grants immunity to the
dangerous effects of the Orrery of Nal-Kashel (see area B7).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must have 5 ranks in
the Spellcraft skill; **Cost** 1,250 gp
