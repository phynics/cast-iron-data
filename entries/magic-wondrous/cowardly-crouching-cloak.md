**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 265, Advanced Player's Guide pg 303\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 1,800 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This nondescript gray cloak has a hood that can fit over the wearer's entire face. The wearer can,
as a move action, cover his face and drop to a huddling crouch. Any creature seeking to directly
attack him while he does this must make a DC 11 Will save to do so (as the
_[sanctuary](ref/spell/sanctuary)_ spell). The wearer cannot see or take any actions while
crouching. This makes him effectively [blind](ref/misc/blinded), with all the normal penalties, but
not [helpless](ref/misc/helpless). The wearer can end his crouch simply by standing up or moving.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[sanctuary](ref/spell/sanctuary); **Cost** 900 gp
