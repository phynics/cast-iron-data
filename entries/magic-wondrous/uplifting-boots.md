**Sources:** Black Markets pg 23\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These worn, leather traveling boots respond to the wearer's holy power. The wearer can sacrifice one
use of [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy), [lay on hands](ref/ability/lay-on-hands), or
[fervor](ref/class/warpriest) as a standard action to instead walk on air (as per the
_[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk)_ spell) for the next minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item);
[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk); creator must have the channel energy, lay on hands, or fervor class
ability; **Cost** 5,500 gp
