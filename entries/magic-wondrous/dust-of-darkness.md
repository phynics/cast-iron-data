**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 293\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 600 gp; **Weight** -

This shimmering black dust causes one creature to be coated in a layer of darkness for 1 minute. In
normal light, the creature gains [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance). In dim
light, the creature gains total concealment (50% miss chance). It has no effect in areas of bright
light or darkness (though in bright light, a viewer can make a DC 20
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) check to notice the target is slightly shadowed). Creatures with
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) see through the dust's darkness as if it were normal darkness. The
dust dispels and counters the outlining effects of [glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust), but doesn't
affect the spell's blinding effects. _[Glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust)_ dispels and counters the
effects of the dust of darkness.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness); **Cost** 300 gp
