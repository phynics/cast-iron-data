**Aura** moderate divination and transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 16,500 gp; **Weight** 20 lbs.

This wood-and-leather [riding saddle](ref/eq-misc/saddle-riding) is stitched with icons of warriors
on horseback. The saddle is of archaic design, with a thick girth, high cantle, and no stirrups. It
automatically resizes to fit any mount from Medium to Huge size.

When the saddle is worn by a mount, its rider can
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ at will, as the spell. Once per year as a
full-round action, the rider can target the animal wearing the saddle with the
_[awaken](ref/spell/awaken)_ spell. This doesn't predispose the animal toward the rider and doesn't
compel the animal to follow the rider's commands; in fact, if the rider has treated the animal
cruelly, the awakened animal may attempt to unseat or attack the rider.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [awaken](ref/spell/awaken),
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals); **Cost** 8,250 gp
