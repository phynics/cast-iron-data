**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 211\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

An engraving of a charging rhinoceros decorates this thick leather belt. The belt grants its wearer
a +2 enhancement bonus to Strength. Treat this as a
[temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

The belt magnifies the wearer's momentum whenever she charges, granting her a +2 bonus on
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) and [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) maneuvers. Furthermore, when the
wearer makes a [charge](ref/rule/charge) attack, her melee weapons and natural weapons deal damage
as if they were [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) than they actually are.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [lead blades](ref/spell/lead-blades); **Cost** 5,000 gp
