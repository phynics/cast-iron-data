**Aura** faint enchantment and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 40,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This plain white band makes the wearer seem more attractive. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus
on Charisma-based checks with other members of his creature type. If the wearer is of the humanoid
type, he gains a +5 competence bonus on creatures of his subtype instead. Once per day, the wearer
can attempt to affect a creature as the spell _[unnatural lust](ref/spell/unnatural-lust)_ but with
a duration of 10 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[unnatural lust](ref/spell/unnatural-lust); **Cost** 20,000 gp
