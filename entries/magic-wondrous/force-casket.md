**Sources:** Tombs of Golarion pg 49\
**Aura** moderate evocation, necromancy, and universal; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** -

A permanent globe of shimmering force encloses one corpse, provided the corpse is small enough to
fit within the 10-foot diameter of the sphere, which functions as a
_[resilient sphere](ref/spell/resilient-sphere)_ spell. The sphere is not subject to damage of any
sort except from a [rod of cancellation](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-cancellation), a
[rod of negation](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-negation), a [disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate) spell, or
a targeted [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) spell. These effects destroy the sphere without
causing harm to the corpse within. Nothing can pass through the sphere, either from the outside or
from within. The corpse within a force casket is also preserved so that it does not decay.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[gentle repose](ref/spell/gentle-repose), [resilient sphere](ref/spell/resilient-sphere); **Cost**
4,000 gp
