**Sources:** Faiths of Balance pg 27\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These 10 ebony, pearl, and ivory tiles, when arranged in a spiral pattern on the ground, provide you
with the effects of a _[guidance](ref/spell/guidance)_ spell once per day. The spell's effect lasts
until discharged.

If your patron is [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma), you can toss the spiral tiles upon the ground once
per day as a standard action and interpret the patterns they lie in as if you had cast
_[augury](ref/spell/augury)._ If Pharasma is not your patron and you attempt to use spiral tiles in
this manner, the result is either random or purposefully misleading.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [augury](ref/spell/augury),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance); **Cost** 3,000 gp
