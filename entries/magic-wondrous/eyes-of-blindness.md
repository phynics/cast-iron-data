**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** eyes; **Weight** -

[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) as if subject to the [blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness)
spell (no saving throw).

**Requirements** any glasses or lenses; **Cost** -
