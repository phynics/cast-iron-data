**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,700 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This drab gray cloak billows about as if blown by an invisible wind. Once per day, the wearer can
use _[doom](ref/spell/doom),_ which causes a deathlike shadow to fleetingly cross the wearer's face.

If [Groetus](ref/faith/groetus) is the wearer's patron, once per day the wearer can use
_[false life](ref/spell/false-life)._ If the wearer successfully delivers a
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) against a dying foe while _[false life](ref/spell/false-life)_ is
active, the wearer's [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) from the
_[false life](ref/spell/false-life)_ effect increase by 1 (to a maximum of 20 temporary hit points).
The dying creature must have at least as many Hit Dice as the wearer for this effect to occur.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), [doom](ref/spell/doom), [false life](ref/spell/false-life);
**Cost** 1,350 gp
