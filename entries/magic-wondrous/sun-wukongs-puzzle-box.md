**Sources:** Daughters of Fury pg 63\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and illusion; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,200 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The plainly lacquered exterior of this wooden puzzle box cunningly hides the devious clockwork
mechanism inside. To those unversed in its abilities, Sun Wukong's puzzle box seems to be little
more than a frustratingly impossible child's toy that is adorned with the holy symbol of
[the Monkey King](ref/faith/sun-wukong).

Once per day as a standard action, the user can unlock Sun Wukong's puzzle box to create two
simultaneous effects. First, a shower of golden sparks envelops the user, causing her to become
invisible and immediately teleporting her exactly 20 feet in a direction of her choice. Second, a
[monkey swarm](ref/monster/monkey-swarm) bursts from the same square the user left.

Although the monkey swarm isn't under the control of the user, the monkeys keep at least 20 feet
from the user at all times and attempt to surround as many of her opponents as possible. This effect
otherwise acts as _[mad monkeys](ref/spell/mad-monkeys)_ with a CMB of +12. Both the _invisibility_
effect and the monkey swarm last for 10 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door), [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility),
[mad monkeys](ref/spell/mad-monkeys), creator must be chaotic; **Cost** 7,600 gp
