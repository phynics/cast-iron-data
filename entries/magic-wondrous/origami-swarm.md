**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 314\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -

This stiff folded paper vaguely resembles a hand-sized pest such as a beetle, spider, or cockroach.
Completing two key folds in the paper causes it to multiply into hundreds of similar-sized
duplicates of itself. These duplicates fill a 10-foot space and act as a
[rat swarm](ref/monster/rat-rat-swarm), except they have fire vulnerability, the construct type, and
do not have the disease special ability. The paper beetles do not attack their creator, but are
otherwise uncontrolled and attack anything nearby. After 5 rounds, the beetles crumple and fall
inert. Once activated, an origami swarm loses all magic.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects); **Cost** 150 gp
