**Sources:** Lost Kingdoms pg 42\
**Aura** strong conjuration, enchantment, and evocation; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 70,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This book contains _earthquake, geas/quest, mage's magnificent mansion,_ and
_[wish](ref/spell/wish)._ The reader may treat her caster level as two levels higher than normal for
the purpose of crafting a [behemoth golem](ref/monster/golem-behemoth-golem).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Construct](ref/feat/craft-construct), creator must be caster level 18th,
[earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake), [geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest),
[mage's magnificent mansion](ref/spell/mages-magnificent-mansion), [wish](ref/spell/wish); **Cost**
35,000 gp
