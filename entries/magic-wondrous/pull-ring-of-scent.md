**Sources:** Animal Archive pg 26\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This dull brass ring is typically placed in the nose or muzzle, or elsewhere on the head of an
animal, and is used to attach a lead rope or bridle. The wearer (and its rider, if any) gives off no
scent and cannot be tracked or located by scent. In addition, it gains the [scent](ref/umr/scent)
ability within 30 feet; if the wearer already possesses scent, the range for this sense increases by
30 feet (to a maximum of 60 feet).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1); **Cost** 5,500 gp
