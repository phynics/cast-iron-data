**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 180 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The wearer of these simple gloves can speak a command word create a glowing disembodied hand. As a
swift action, the wearer can have the hand assist him with a task as if using the
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action. The hand uses the wearer's base attack bonus or skill
ranks when making the check to see if aid another is successful (it does not gain the benefit of the
wearer's ability scores or other bonuses). The hand remains until it has attempted one action or
until 1 minute passes, whichever comes first, at which time the gloves become nonmagical.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand), [unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant); **Cost** 90 gp
