**Sources:** Daughters of Fury pg 58\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,380 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This seemingly ordinary deck of 54 playing cards is held in an ornate wooden box with a simple
clasp, decorated with wasp-themed art. The clergy of [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria) sometimes
gifts decks of this kind to its more devout and covert spies. As a move action, the owner of a deck
of the hellwasp's sting can draw a card from the deck and speak a command word to transform that
card into a thrown weapon (treat as a +1 [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
[dart](ref/eq-weapon/dart)). Alternatively, the user can draw and activate a card covertly as a
standard action, using [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) to palm it and treat it as a
hidden weapon for a subsequent attack. A card is always destroyed after being thrown, even if it
doesn't hit the target. If the thrower uses the [Arcane Strike](ref/feat/arcane-strike) feat with
this attack, the feat's bonus damage doubles.

In addition, as long as there are at least 20 cards remaining in the deck, the entire deck can be
sacrificed by speaking a command word and tossing the cards into the air as a full-round action. The
cards transform into a swarm of burning, poisonous paper with the same statistics as a
[hellwasp swarm](ref/monster/hellwasp-swarm), except the swarm doesn't gain the inhabit ability, and
all damage dealt by its swarm attack is fire damage. The character who activated the ability can
control the swarm as if it were summoned by the _[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm)_ spell for
up to 10 rounds, at which point the swarm dissipates.

### Construction

**Requirements** Arcane Strike, [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[flame arrow](ref/spell/flame-arrow), [summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm); **Cost** 5,190 gp
