**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,100 gp; **Weight** -

This silver nail is finely engraved with hundreds of tiny runes that flash faintly blue under the
light of the moon. The nail can be used to trap evil outsiders and incorporeal undead creatures to
prevent their escape. To use a silver nail, the bearer must take a full-round action and succeed at
a touch attack against a creature.

When used against a [helpless](ref/misc/helpless) or [pinned](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) evil
outsider, the bearer can prevent that creature from escaping as per
_[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)._

The bearer can instead trap an incorporeal undead creature. If he succeeds at a touch attack, the
nail prevents the incorporeal undead creature from moving into walls or other solid objects.

The effects of the silver nail last 1 minute, during which the nail remains inside the creature.
When this duration ends, the nail falls out and is forever rendered powerless. If used against a
creature that is neither an outsider nor an incorporeal undead creature, the silver nail has no
effect, but is still expended.
