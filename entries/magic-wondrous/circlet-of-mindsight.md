**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 240, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 51\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 22,000 gp; **Weight** -

This elaborate circlet of gold-and-platinum filigree bears tiny gems in settings that look
disturbingly like human eyes. When worn, the circlet lets the wearer sense the presence of other
thinking creatures in her immediate area. The wearer gains the benefits of
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) 30 feet, but only against creatures with an Intelligence score that
are susceptible to mind-affecting effects.

Undead, constructs, and mindless creatures like most oozes and vermin cannot be perceived when using
the circlet, nor can creatures under the effects of [mind blank](ref/spell/mind-blank) or a
[ring of mind shielding](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-mind-shielding).

The circlet does not interfere with the wearer's ability to see normally. If the wearer has
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) or [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight), he is able to differentiate
creatures detected with those senses from creatures detected with the circlet of mindsight.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance),
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts); **Cost** 11,000 gp
