**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 257\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 2nd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Cast in the image of the sun and often ornamented with an open eye, this amulet defends the wearer
against the need for sleep. The wearer of this amulet does not need to sleep, but must remain at
ease for the normal amount of time to regain spells and other abilities that require rest (although
the wearer could perform light activity, such as reading or keeping watch). Once the wearer has used
the power of this amulet for a total of seven nights, the amulet loses its power for one day and the
wearer is [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) for this duration. If the wearer removes the amulet for
any reason after having used its ability, she becomes exhausted for four hours for every night of
sleep skipped.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[lesser restoration](ref/spell/restoration-lesser); **Cost** 4,000 gp
