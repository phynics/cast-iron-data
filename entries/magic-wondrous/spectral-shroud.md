**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 222\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 26,000 gp; **Weight** -

This thin, bleached cloth covers the wearer's entire torso. Some also cover the wearer's face, but
don't interfere with the wearer's vision. The morbid burial shroud grants the wearer some affinity
with the spectral dead. The wearer can discern invisible or ethereal creatures as though using
_[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility)._ Once per day, the wearer can become incorporeal
for 10 rounds and gain a fly speed equal to half his base speed with perfect maneuverability. The
wearer can't attack while incorporeal, but can dismiss the effect as a move action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead), [see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility);
**Cost** 13,000 gp
