**Aura** moderate conjuration and necromancy; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,250 gp; **Weight** -

This small bone container is filled with a thick dye. Each container holds enough dye to cover one
ornament created with the [Grisly Ornament](ref/feat/grisly-ornament) feat. When applied to an
ornament, it aids in the ornament's preservation, allowing it to remain effective for an additional
24 hours. Once per day as a free action, a dyed ornament can be commanded to attack a target
adjacent to the wearer. A ghostly image of the creature from which the ornament was harvested
appears and makes a natural attack against the target, attacking with the wearer's base attack bonus
and dealing 2d6 points of damage. This attack can be made in addition to any actions performed by
the wielder. An ornament integrated into a wondrous item with the
[Monstrous Crafter](ref/feat/monstrous-crafter) feat can gain this attack ability, but doing so
requires two applications of monstrous dye to function properly.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[gentle repose](ref/spell/gentle-repose),
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4); **Cost** 1,125 gp
