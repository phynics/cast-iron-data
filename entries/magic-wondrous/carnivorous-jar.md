**Sources:** Pathfinder \#82: Secrets of the Sphinx pg 75\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 10 lbs.

A wooden stopper depicting an animal or human head seals this limestone jar. Any creature that opens
the jar takes 3d6 points of [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) as several of its
internal organs vanish from its body and appear inside the jar. If the creature survives, the
resulting trauma deals 2d6 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage every round. This bleed damage
lasts until the victim receives at least 5 points of magical healing or benefits from a DC 20
[Heal](ref/skill/heal) check. A carnivorous jar functions only once, and can be found alone or in
sets of four.

### Construction

**Requirements** [preserving flask](ref/magic-wondrous/preserving-flask),
[restorative ointment](ref/magic-wondrous/restorative-ointment), or any item held in a jar or
similar container; **Cost** -
