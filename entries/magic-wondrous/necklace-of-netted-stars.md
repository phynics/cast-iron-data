**Aura** strong illusion and transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 42,000 gp; **Weight** -

This marvelous [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) necklace forms a broad, draping lacework of
thread-like chains set with a multitude of tiny jewels. Seven of these jewels are larger than the
rest. While wearing a necklace of netted stars, the wearer can create
_[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights)_ at will and
_[wandering star motes](ref/spell/wandering-star-motes)_ once per day. In addition, each of the
seven jewels of the necklace can recall a previously cast 1st-level or 0-level spell, as a
[pearl of power](ref/magic-wondrous/pearl-of-power).

Once per day, the wearer may use the necklace to catch a spell targeted at him, as if readying an
action to [counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) using _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),_
with a bonus on the check equal to 12 + the number of jewels not yet used to recall spells that day.
If all jewels have been used, this ability cannot be used that day. The level of the spell to be
countered must be less than or equal to the number of unused jewels. If the counterspell succeeds,
the spell is negated and the wearer can use its magical energy to recall a previously cast spell of
the same level or lower as an immediate action. Whether or not the attempt succeeds, this action
exhausts the power of all of the necklace's jewels for that day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights), [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),
[wandering star motes](ref/spell/wandering-star-motes), creator must be able to cast 7th-level
spells; **Cost** 21,000 gp
