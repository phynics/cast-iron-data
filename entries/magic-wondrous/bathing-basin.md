**Sources:** Inner Sea Monster Codex pg 29\
**Aura** faint conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,750 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This heavy bronze bowl, etched with outlines of waves, feels cool to the touch. Once per day as a
standard action, the bowl can be commanded to grow in size and fill with 25 gallons of cool, fresh
water. In its enlarged state, the basin can accommodate a Medium or smaller creature. The basin
remains enlarged for 1 hour or until the user commands it to shrink. All water in the basin
disappears when the item shrinks, and any creatures or objects that don't fit in the shrunken bowl
are expelled into an adjacent square.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[create water](ref/spell/create-water), [shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Cost** 3,375 gp
