**Sources:** Ranged Tactics Toolbox pg 28\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This wooden [atlatl](ref/eq-weapon/atlatl) resembles a handheld, lever-controlled catapult. One end
sports a shaft covered in carvings of alchemical formulae, and the other is a roughly carved bowl.
The user can place an alchemical splash weapon, such as a flask of [acid](ref/eq-weapon/acid) or
[alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), in the bowl. This is a move action that provokes
an attack of opportunity. When the splash weapon is launched from the atlatl, its range is doubled.
Magic items (including an [alchemist's](ref/class/alchemist) bombs) aren't affected by an
alchemist's atlatl.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[longshot](ref/spell/longshot); **Cost** 750 gp
