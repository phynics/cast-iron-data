**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 228, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 503\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 5,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These boots increase the wearer's base land speed by 10 feet. In addition to this striding ability
(considered an enhancement bonus), these boots allow the wearer to make great leaps. She can jump
with a +5 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider), creator must have 5 ranks in the Acrobatics skill; **Cost**
2,750 gp
