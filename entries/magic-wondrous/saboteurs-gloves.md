**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 55\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 15,240 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

These gloves of thick leather do not impair delicate work and grant a +4 competence bonus on
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks to disable magic traps. Once per day, a wearer
trained in Disable Device can command the gloves to alter an unattended magic item she touches to
incorporate a trap. The wearer attempts a caster level check using the higher of her caster level or
the gloves' caster level (DC = 11 + the target item's caster level). If the check is successful, the
target item is marked with a _[glyph of warding](ref/spell/glyph-of-warding)_ (blast glyph only)
that lasts for up to 1 day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[glyph of warding](ref/spell/glyph-of-warding); **Cost** 7,620 gp
