**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 222\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The sacred stones, graven star-patterns, and wisdom-writings of the ancients on this chestplate
increase the wearer's chance of success with [augury](ref/spell/augury),
_[divination](ref/spell/divination),_ and [contact other plane](ref/spell/contact-other-plane). The
wearer adds 1d6 to the d% roll each time she uses such a spell. When casting
[commune](ref/spell/commune) or [commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature), the wearer may
ask one additional question or gain one additional piece of information.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[divination](ref/spell/divination), [guidance](ref/spell/guidance); **Cost** 3,000 gp
