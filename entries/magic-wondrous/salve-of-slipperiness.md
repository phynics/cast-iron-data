**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This substance provides a +20 competence bonus on all [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist)
checks and combat maneuver checks made to escape from a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple). The salve also
grants a +10 competence bonus to the wearer's CMD for the purpose of avoiding grapple attempts. In
addition, such obstructions as webs (magical or otherwise) do not affect an anointed individual.
Magic ropes and the like do not avail against this salve. If it is smeared on a floor or on steps,
the area should be treated as a long-lasting _[grease](ref/spell/grease)_ spell. The salve requires
8 hours to wear off normally, or it can be wiped off with an alcohol solution (even wine).

Salve of slipperiness is needed to coat the inside of a container meant to hold
[sovereign glue](ref/magic-wondrous/sovereign-glue).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [grease](ref/spell/grease);
**Cost** 500 gp
