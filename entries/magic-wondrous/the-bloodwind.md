**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 126,900 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This [folding boat](ref/magic-wondrous/folding-boat) is made of rich, dark wood and a sail of
blood-red cloth. In its ship form, it is the size of a [longship](ref/eq-misc/longship), with 20
sets of oars and manacles to keep slave rowers in place. When in ship form, the Bloodwind can create
a gust of powerful wind behind its sails as from a
[feather token (fan)](ref/magic-wondrous/feather-token-fan). This ability lasts for only 1 hour, and
can be used once each day. The Bloodwind's [special purpose](ref/rule/special-purpose-items) is to
serve the [moon-beasts of Leng](ref/monster/moon-beast) as a slave ship. When called by the
moon-beasts' eerie piping, which the Bloodwind can hear from anywhere in the Dreamlands, it can sail
through the air as with _[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight)_ to the Dreamlands' mysterious
moon.

The Bloodwind has a sly and spiteful intelligence and is fond of cruel pranks. It has sufficient
self-mobility to operate its own sails, lock and unlock its own manacles, and trigger its powers,
although it cannot operate all of its oars at once and therefore requires rowers to move when the
wind is slack. The Bloodwind willingly follows the commands of moon-beasts and
[denizens of Leng](ref/monster/denizen-of-leng), but plays cruel jokes on others, both slaves and
slavers alike: untying knots, raising its anchor at inconvenient times, or even folding into a box
and obstinately refusing to return to ship form. The Bloodwind's favorite prank is to
surreptitiously unlock a new slave's manacles to offer the hope of freedom, and then snap them shut
again - preferably bloodying a finger or toe in the process.
