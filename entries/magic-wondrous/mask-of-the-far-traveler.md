**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 54\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 38,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This leather half-mask covers the wearer's nose and mouth, obscuring her lip movements but not her
voice. Creatures that are on their native plane and have a language understand all words spoken by
the wearer, as though her words were spoken in the listening creature's native tongue. In addition,
because the mask replicates local idioms efficiently and skillfully, the wearer gains a +5
competence bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) checks and on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks
to tell a lie. A mask of the far traveler does not function on the Material Plane.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [tongues](ref/spell/tongues);
**Cost** 19,000 gp
