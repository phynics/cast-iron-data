**Sources:** Inner Sea Temples pg 23\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

Images of a dwarf and human drinking together decorate the sides of this 1-foot-tall granite stein,
which is capped off with a rough-hewn orb of the same material. When the user speaks the command
word and places a watchful tankard on a solid surface, such as a cavern ceiling or wooden table, the
stein adheres to the surface (with the equivalent of an 18 Strength score). The last creature to
drink from the stein can attempt [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks from the stein's
location, as well as from its own, as long as it is within 60 feet of the watchful tankard. Any
permanent vision-based bonuses (such as [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision),
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), or permanent [arcane sight](ref/spell/arcane-sight))
are also usable through the watchful tankard but are limited to a range of 30 feet. It is possible
to attempt Perception checks from the stein's point of view only while it is adhered to a solid
surface in this way. Grabbing the handle and repeating the command word frees the watchful tankard
from this location.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [alarm](ref/spell/alarm); **Cost** 15,000 gp
