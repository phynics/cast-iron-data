**Sources:** Pathfinder \#69: Maiden, Mother, Crone pg 60\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** -

Crafted of stout, wax-treated leather and wrapped with warm fur reminiscent of a
[frost giant's](ref/monster/giant-frost-frost-giant) beard, these boots are sometimes given as gifts
by the frost giant tribes of the north to their smaller allies. In addition to functioning as
[boots of the winterlands](ref/magic-wondrous/boots-of-the-winterlands), the boots of the winter
jarl enable their wearer to take the form of a frost giant once per day, as with the
_[giant form I](ref/spell/giant-form-1)_ spell. While in giant form, the wearer gains a +6 size
bonus to Strength, a -2 penalty to Dexterity, a +4 size bonus to Constitution, a +4 natural armor
bonus, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [rock catching](ref/umr/rock-catching),
[rock throwing](ref/umr/rock-throwing), and resist cold 20, but also gains vulnerability to fire.
The wearer can remain in giant form for up to 10 minutes.

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace), [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
[giant form I](ref/spell/giant-form-1), [pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace); **Cost**
10,000 gp
