**Sources:** Champions of Corruption pg 30\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These glass amulets hold bubbling red liquid and often feature a religion's or deity's holy symbol,
most commonly that of [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) or [Zon-Kuthon](ref/faith/zon-kuthon). While
worn, the amulet tinges the user's healing magic with a wash of ecstasy that acts as a magical
[drug](ref/rule/drugs), making the recipient of the magical healing susceptible to mental
manipulation by the wearer. Anytime the wearer casts a healing spell or uses a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) on a single target, the recipient of the magical
healing additionally must succeed at a DC 13 Fortitude save or become addicted to the wearer's
healing spells. The wearer can suppress this effect by shielding the amulet from view with a hand or
held item as a free action. The drug has the following statistics.

> ### Euphoric Healing
>
> **Type** spell **Addiction** minor, Fortitude DC 13 **Effect** 24 hours, -2 penalty against all
> mind-affecting spells cast by the amulet's wearer **Damage** 1
> [Wis damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), [enthrall](ref/spell/enthrall); **Cost** 6,000 gp
