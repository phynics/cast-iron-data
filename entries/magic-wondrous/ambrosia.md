**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This divine food comes in many forms, from a light and airy honeyed cake to a deep, rich chocolate.
A mythic creature that eats this food is restored and replenished. Eating this food takes 1 minute,
and when the meal is done, the consumer regains all her uses of mythic power for the day. If a
non-mythic creature consumes ambrosia, that creature is subject to
[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison), [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), and
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease), each at caster level 10th. A mythic creature can choose
to either gain this effect instead of regaining mythic power.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [greater heroism](ref/spell/heroism-greater),
[heal](ref/spell/heal); **Cost** 5,000 gp
