**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This simple black cloth band focuses the wearer's ki, helping her use subtle misdirection against
opponents in combat. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks made
to feint and combat maneuver checks made to [reposition](ref/rule/reposition), and once per day she
can attempt a feint or reposition maneuver as a swift action. The wearer also gains a +2 insight
bonus on attack rolls on sneak attacks and can [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) creatures
with [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or total concealment, though miss chances apply normally.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), [see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility);
**Cost** 7,500 gp
