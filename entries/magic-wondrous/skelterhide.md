**Sources:** Pathfinder \#117: Assault on Longshadow pg 30\
**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This thick hide cloak grants its wearer the divided mind of a [skeltercat](ref/monster/skeltercat).
While worn, the cloak imparts a +1 resistance bonus on Fortitude and Reflex saves and a +2
resistance bonus on Will saves. In addition, once per round, the wearer can reroll the percentile
dice when determining her actions while [confused](ref/misc/confused) and choose the desired result.
Once the cloak is donned, the wearer cannot speak or write; she can communicate only in
unintelligible growls. The wearer still understands language as normal.

If the wearer suffers from one or more permanent mental afflictions that alter her behavior, such as
[insanity](ref/spell/insanity) or the effects of a
[helm of opposite alignment](ref/magic-wondrous/helm-of-opposite-alignment), for 15 minutes per day
she can function as if unaffected by these conditions. This time does not need to be used all at
once, but must be used in 1-minute increments.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance),
[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness); **Cost** 2,250 gp
