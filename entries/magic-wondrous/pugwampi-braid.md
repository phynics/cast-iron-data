**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 93\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This braid, crafted from strips of [pugwampi](ref/monster/gremlin-pugwampi) pelts, is 1 inch in
diameter and 12 inches long. It can be carried in the hand like a riding crop or worn as an
adornment.

Once per day with a command word, the owner of the braid can create a 5-foot-radius aura around the
braid for 1 minute. Creatures other than the owner within the aura must roll twice and use the lower
result whenever a situation calls for a d20 roll. This is a mind-affecting effect that does not work
on humanoids with the [gnoll](ref/monster/gnoll) subtype, animals, or
[gremlins](ref/family/gremlin). A creature under the effect of a luck bonus is immune to the aura.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse); **Cost** 4,500 gp
