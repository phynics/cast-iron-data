**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 35,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Two large horns jut from the sides of this iron helm, each of them carved with runes representing
storms, thunder, and lightning. The runes give the wearer the ability to control the elements and
bring forth a great storm. Once per day, the wearer can call upon the magic of the helm to call a
thunderstorm, as if casting the _[control weather](ref/spell/control-weather)_ spell. Like that
spell, it takes 10 minutes to call the weather and an additional 10 minutes for the thunderstorm to
manifest. Once it does, the wearer can _call lighting_ (as the spell) for 10 minutes or until the
wearer dismisses the thunderstorm with a standard action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[call lightning](ref/spell/call-lightning), [control weather](ref/spell/control-weather); **Cost**
17,500 gp
