**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 13\
**Aura** faint conjuration (creation); **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 700 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This squishy, gel-filled sack is the size of a fist, and its exterior is covered with countless tiny
metal studs. When set on the ground or somewhere else within line of sight, a poison popcushion can
be targeted with a melee or ranged attack ([hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 0, AC 7). A successful
attack causes the poison popcushion to explode, sending a spray of needles everywhere within a
10-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) and dealing 1d4+1 points of piercing damage to creatures
caught in the area each round for 1d3 rounds. A successful DC 13 Reflex save halves this damage.
Additionally, a poison popcushion can be loaded with an injury poison before it is placed. Fully
loading a poison popcushion takes 3 doses of poison (not included in the price). In addition to
dealing damage, a loaded poison popcushion affects struck creatures with the poison stored within,
though the Fortitude save DC to save against this poison is 4 lower than the poison's usual DC.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[arrow eruption](ref/spell/arrow-eruption); **Cost** 350 gp
