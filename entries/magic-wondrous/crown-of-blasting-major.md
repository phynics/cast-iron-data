## Crown of Blasting

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 241, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 507\
**Aura** moderate (minor) or strong (major) evocation; **CL** 6th (minor), 17th (major)\
**Slot** head; **Price** 6,480 gp (minor), 23,760 gp (major); **Weight** 1 lb.

**Minor:** On command, this simple golden crown projects a blast of
_[searing light](ref/spell/searing-light)_ (3d8 points of damage) once per day.

**Major**: On command, this elaborate golden crown projects a blast of
_[searing light](ref/spell/searing-light)_ (5d8 maximized for 40 points of damage) once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Maximize Spell](ref/feat/maximize-spell) (major only), [searing light](ref/spell/searing-light);
**Cost** 3,240 gp (minor), 11,880 gp (major)
