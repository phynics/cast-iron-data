**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 248\
**Aura** faint enchantment and abjuration; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 3,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This taut black band, clipped with a black metal skull, can be attached on the outside of a hat or
other head slot item or can be worn as a normal headband. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and increases the DC of any fear effect he creates by +1.
In addition, if this headband is attached to the outside of a hat, the wearer's hat or headgear
cannot be blown off or removed by wind, water, or environmental effects, and the wearer gains a +5
circumstance bonus to his CMD against [steal](ref/rule/steal) and [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) attempts
targeting his headgear.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [doom](ref/spell/doom),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Cost** 1,800 gp
