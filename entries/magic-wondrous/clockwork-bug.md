**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,100 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This Fine brass construct is made to look like a beetle, and is sometimes painted in green, brown,
or gray camouflage. A clockwork bug can record all nearby sounds within a 20-foot-radius
[spread](ref/misc/spread) onto a low-quality gemstone worth 10 gp, which is embedded in its body.
The bug can record up to 1 hour of sound, which must be continuous; if the recording is stopped
early, it cannot be restarted. Making the clockwork bug replay the recorded audio is a swift action.

Once a gemstone is imprinted with an audio recording, it cannot be reused, and must be replaced with
a fresh gemstone in order for the bug to make a new recording. Replacing a gemstone is a full-round
action that requires a DC 25 [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) check. Failing this check
causes the used gemstone to become cracked and erases any recorded sounds on that gemstone.

Though a clockwork bug isn't treated as a creature and cannot move or perform other actions by
itself, it can cling indefinitely to any surface onto which it is placed, including the underside of
objects. These bugs are also equipped with a timer that can delay the start of recording process by
up to 24 hours. Additionally, the bug is equipped with an optional self-destruct timer with a
maximum duration of 72 hours. If engaged, the self-destruct sequence causes the bug to erase any
information on its current gemstone and then send a surge of energy through its filaments, rendering
itself useless and irreparable.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound), creator must have 4 ranks in
[Craft (clockwork)](ref/skill/craft-any); **Cost** 550 gp
