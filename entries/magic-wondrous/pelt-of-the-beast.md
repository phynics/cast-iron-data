**Sources:** Blood of the Moon pg 31\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 15,600 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

These lustrous animal pelts are particularly treasured among Ulfen barbarians. While any humanoid
creature can don and use such pelts, their powers are vastly enhanced for
[skinwalkers](ref/race/skinwalker). All pelts of the beast grant the following powers:

- A bite attack that [deals damage](ref/umr/natural-attacks) appropriate to the wearer's size. If
  the wearer already has a bite attack, the critical threat range of this attack doubles. This does
  not stack with other magical effects that increase the threat range of a natural weapon.
- _[Speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ 1/day. If the wearer can already use
  _[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ as a
  [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), she may do so at will.
- The wearer may take the form of the animal from which the pelt came three times per day as if
  using _[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1)_. In addition, if the wearer is a skinwalker, her
  change shape ability is enhanced so that she may gain an additional bestial feature when taking
  her bestial form, selectable from the skinwalker heritage tied to the animal from which the pelt
  came. For instance, a werewolf-kin wearing a bear pelt of the beast could use her change shape
  ability to gain a climb speed from the pelt's bestial features (identical to the set listed for
  werebear-kin) in addition to [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) from her set of werewolf-kin
  bestial features. Feats or abilities that affect how many bestial features the skinwalker may gain
  at a single time stack with this ability.

Each pelt also grants a competence bonus on a check, based on its type. These bonuses increase by
50% for skinwalkers wearing a pelt that matches their lycanthropic heritage.

- **Bat:** +4 bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks in darkness
- **Bear:** +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple)
- **Boar:** +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush)
- **Crocodile:** +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [sunder](ref/rule/sunder)
- **Rat:** +2 bonus on saving throws against disease
- **Shark:** +2 bonus on Perception and [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks attempted while completely
  underwater
- **Tiger:** +2 bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks
- **Wolf:** +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [trip](ref/rule/trip)

Other pelts of the beast may be available at the GM's discretion.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1), [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals);
**Cost** 7,800 gp
