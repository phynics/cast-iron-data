**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 30\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 5,400 gp; **Weight** -

This circular amulet is formed from three elegantly curved golden rods and has a deep yellow topaz
in its center. When worn, the amulet provides a +2 resistance bonus on Fortitude saves against
poisons and ongoing effects from poisons. If at any point any of the wearer's ability scores would
be reduced to 1 due to ability score damage from poison, the amulet immediately shatters and the
wearer benefits from a casting of _[restoration](ref/spell/restoration)._ This effect does not cure
any poisons still affecting the wearer, but it destroys the amulet.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[restoration](ref/spell/restoration), [neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison); **Cost**
2,700 gp
