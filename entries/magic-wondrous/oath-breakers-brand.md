**Sources:** People of the River pg 28\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

When this iron medallion is placed between the palms of two persons willingly entering into an
agreement via handshake, the accord is enforced by the brand's enchantment. If either person breaks
the word of the agreement, a hideous mark appears on the top of the unfaithful creature's hand. The
brand marks her as an oath breaker throughout the River Kingdoms, imposing upon her a -8 penalty on
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks attempted anywhere in the
region as long as the brand is visible. This mark lasts for 1 year or until it is removed by
[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment), [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish),
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle), [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), or [wish](ref/spell/wish),
whichever comes first.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[mark of justice](ref/spell/mark-of-justice); **Cost** 1,250 gp
