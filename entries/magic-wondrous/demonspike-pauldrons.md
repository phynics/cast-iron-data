**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 14,350 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

These black iron pauldrons are studded with jagged spikes of various lengths. Demonspike pauldrons
act as +2 [armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes) and also deal 1d2 [bleed](ref/rule/bleed)
damage on a successful [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) attack or melee attack made with the pauldrons.
If these pauldrons are worn with normal armor spikes, increase the damage done by the armor spikes
to 1d6 (Small) or 1d8 (Medium), and use the greater of the two enhancement bonuses to attack and
damage - either from the armor spikes (if any) or the demonspike pauldrons.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), [bleed](ref/spell/bleed);
**Cost** 7,175 gp
