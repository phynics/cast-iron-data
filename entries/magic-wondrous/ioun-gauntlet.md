**Aura** faint divination and evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 1,600 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Much like [wayfinders](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder), an ioun gauntlet grants enhanced or altogether
new abilities depending on the type of [ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone) inserted into its
matrix. In its base form, an ioun gauntlet grants its wearer the ability to cast
_[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic)_ as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
once per day. When the _[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic)_ effect is active, the gauntlet also
sheds illumination as _[light](ref/spell/light)_ in the 60-foot cone of the
_[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic)_ effect.

An ioun gauntlet features a single matrix in the back of the hand, suitable for one ioun stone. When
an ioun stone is inserted into the matrix as a standard action, the wearer gains the associated
bonus from the ioun stone as well an additional effect based on the shape of the ioun stone
inserted, as listed below. While a creature can put on two ioun gauntlets, the wearer gains the
additional benefit from only one inserted ioun stone.

> **Cabochon:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks.
>
> **Disk:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) checks.
>
> **Ellipsoid:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) checks.
>
> **Nodule:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) checks.
>
> **Pentacle:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) checks.
>
> **Prism:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks.
>
> **Pyramid:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks.
>
> **Rhomboid:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks.
>
> **Sphere:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks.
>
> **Spindle:** Bonus language (Aboleth).
>
> **Star:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Knowledge (nobility)](ref/skill/knowledge-nobility) checks.
>
> **Trillian:** +1 enhancement bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic), [light](ref/spell/light); **Cost** 800 gp
