**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 55\
**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 5,400 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

These dark-gray leather gloves are extremely supple and are popular among the worshipers of
[Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber). They grant a +10 bonus on
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks to take something from a creature unnoticed.

Additionally, three times per day, when a creature notices the wearer's Sleight of Hand check to
take something from it, the wearer can cause the object to become invisible as an immediate action,
allowing the wearer to disavow the theft.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility); **Cost** 2,700 gp
