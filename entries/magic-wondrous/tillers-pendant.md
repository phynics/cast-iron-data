**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 45\
**Aura** faint illusion and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,400 gp; **Weight** -

This cluster of emeralds attached to a delicate golden clasp is arranged in the likeness of a
five-petaled blossom. Regardless of how it is worn, it does not take up an item slot. As long as the
pendant is visible on the wearer's person, it grants a +4 competence bonus on
[Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) and [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks to the wearer. In addition,
once per day when the command word is spoken, the emeralds take on the appearance of a living
flower, gaining the ability to magically imbue 2d4 freshly picked berries so that each can provide a
meal and cure 1 point of damage when eaten, as per _[goodberry](ref/spell/goodberry)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self), [goodberry](ref/spell/goodberry); **Cost** 3,200 gp
