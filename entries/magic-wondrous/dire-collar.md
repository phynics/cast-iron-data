**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 229\
**Aura** faint transmutation (normal), moderate transmutation (greater); **CL** 3rd (normal), 9th
(greater)\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 1,000 gp (normal), 14,000 gp (greater); **Weight** 2 lbs.

This leather collar resizes to fit almost any size of creature. Once per day on command, the
creature that fastened the dire collar to the animal can cause that animal to grow larger and more
bestial. The collar's fastener must be within 30 feet of the animal to do so. The animal gains the
benefits of an [enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person) spell (despite the normal targeting
restrictions of that spell) for 1 minute.

The **greater dire collar** collar functions as above, except that the animal wearing it gains the
benefit of an _[animal growth](ref/spell/animal-growth)_ spell for 1 minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animal growth](ref/spell/animal-growth); **Cost** 500 gp (normal), 7,000 gp (greater)
