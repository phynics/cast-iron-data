**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 212\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 23,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This belt grants its wearer a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength, and the ability to ignore
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) while [charging](ref/rule/charge) or making an
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun), [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), or [trample](ref/umr/trample) attempt.
Furthermore, once per day on command, its wearer can spew a 60-foot cone of poisonous green gas as a
breath weapon. Any creature caught in the area of the gas can attempt a DC 18 saving throw to resist
its effect, but if the creature fails the saving throw, it is [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) for
1d4 rounds. At the end of its turn, a creature petrified by this breath weapon can attempt a new
save to end the paralysis effect. Treat the enhancement bonus to Strength as a
[temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [feather step](ref/spell/feather-step),
[poison](ref/spell/poison); **Cost** 11,500 gp
