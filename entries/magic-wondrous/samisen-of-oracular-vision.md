**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 46,575 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This elegant, long-necked samisen is crafted of cat skin stretched over a wooden frame. The
samisen's three silken strings are magically keyed to the past, present, and future. A user must
succeed at a DC 20 [Perform (string)](ref/skill/perform-any) check to play the correct tune and cast
one of the following spell effects: _[legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore)_ (using the string of the
past), _[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ (using the string of the present), or
_[divination](ref/spell/divination)_ (using the string of the future). Each tune can be performed
once per day, and the user must play the samisen for the required casting time of the spell effect.
In the case of _[legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore),_ if the required casting time is 1 or more
days, the user needs to play for just 1 hour. As a
[masterwork samisen](ref/eq-misc/musical-instrument-masterwork), the samisen of oracular vision
provides a +2 circumstance bonus on all Perform checks using the instrument (including Perform
checks to use its abilities).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[divination](ref/spell/divination), [legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore),
[scrying](ref/spell/scrying), creator must have 10 ranks in the Perform (string) skill; **Cost**
24,075 gp
