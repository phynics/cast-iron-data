**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 93\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and necromancy; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** -

When wrapped around the neck and jaw, this swath of black cloth lets its wearer see normally in a
[sandstorm](ref/rule/severe-events) (her vision isn't reduced, nor does she take the -4 penalty on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks) and avoid taking nonlethal damage from the storm.

Once per day on command, the shawl's wearer can emit a strange cackling. Enemies within 30 feet of
the shawl's wearer who can hear the cackling must succeed at a DC 16 Will saving throw or become
[frightened](ref/misc/frightened) for 1 round. Those who succeed at the saving throw are
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d4 rounds instead. Both of the cackle's effects are sonic
mind-affecting fear effects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [fear](ref/spell/fear);
**Cost** 5,500 gp
