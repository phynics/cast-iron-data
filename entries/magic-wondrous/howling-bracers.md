**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 230\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 7,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These bracers are made of heavy iron and have a pattern of howling wolves engraved upon them. Once
per day on command, the wearer can clash the bracers together rhythmically. This causes the wolves
etched into the bracers to come to life and let out a chorus of howls, with the effect of
_[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair)_ (DC 16). Additionally, for every two creatures
under the effect of the wearer's [raging song](ref/ability/raging-song) when this ability is used,
the DC increases by 1, up to a maximum increase of 5.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair), creator must have the raging song class feature;
**Cost** 3,500 gp
