**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 323\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Six inches in diameter, one side of this porcelain seal shows a many-keyholed padlock, while the
other displays a lightning bolt superimposed on an open hand. The padlock side is used to _lock_ and
_[alarm](ref/spell/alarm)_ doors, chests, and other portals. The bearer may speak a command word to
activate it when holding it against a lockable item. If the padlock side of the seal is touching the
item, it bestows an _[alarm](ref/spell/alarm)_ (mental alarm) and
_[arcane lock](ref/spell/arcane-lock)_ on the target item. If the lightning bolt side of the seal is
touching the item, it casts _[fire trap](ref/spell/fire-trap)_ on the lock (the seal must be held to
the lock for 10 minutes to use this ability).

A creature carrying the seal can speak a command word to suspend its effects on an item for 1
minute. This ability is usable at will, but only works on effects created by that specific seal.

The seal can be used at will but can only maintain effects on three items at any given time. Using
it on a fourth item automatically negates the oldest effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [alarm](ref/spell/alarm),
[arcane lock](ref/spell/arcane-lock), [fire trap](ref/spell/fire-trap); **Cost** 5,000 gp
