**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 351, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 543

**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.

This dark, polished stone reduces the possessor's base land speed by half. Once picked up, the stone
cannot be disposed of by any nonmagical means - if it is thrown away or smashed, it reappears
somewhere on the possessor's person. If a [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell is cast upon
a loadstone, the item may be discarded normally and no longer haunts the individual.
