**Sources:** Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 53\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

In outer construction and manner of play, pipes of shifting tempo resemble a typical set of
unremarkable pan pipes, but the complex chambers within the pipes are modeled after
difficult-to-duplicate Azlanti instruments. Music created by these pipes sounds unusually mournful,
with a strange, almost warbling subtext to the notes. Up to three times per day, a user can attempt
to harness these pipes' power by making a DC 15 [Perform (wind)](ref/skill/perform-any) check as a
standard action. With a successful check, the possessor can play a grave melody to cast
_[slow](ref/spell/slow)_ or an allegro melody to cast _[haste](ref/spell/haste)._ The resulting
spell functions as normal for the item's caster level, affecting five creatures per casting.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [haste](ref/spell/haste),
[slow](ref/spell/slow), Perform (wind) 5 ranks; **Cost** 9,000 gp
