**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 227\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 80,000 gp; **Weight** -

This black band of silk grants its wearer spectacular senses within a limited range. Some believe
these items were made to grant lesser swordmasters the illusion of skill. When the wearer places the
blindfold over her eyes, she becomes [blinded](ref/misc/blinded), but gains
[blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) within the reach of her melee weapon, or 5 feet if the wearer is
not wielding a melee weapon. Furthermore, the wearer is under the effect of a constant
_[locate weakness](ref/spell/locate-weakness)_ spell, but only when making melee attacks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[blindness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness), [locate weakness](ref/spell/locate-weakness); **Cost**
40,000 gp
