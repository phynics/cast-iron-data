**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 4,400 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This linen hood is simply decorated with the colors of the forest and designed to cast a dramatic
shadow upon the wearer's face, obscuring identifiable features. A vagrant's hood grants the wearer a
+4 competence bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks and makes the wearer's voice sound deep
and throaty, as per _[vocal alteration](ref/spell/vocal-alteration)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self), [vocal alteration](ref/spell/vocal-alteration); **Cost**
2,200 gp
