**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 232\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 12 lbs.

These horseshoes are made of blackened steel and are typically stamped with infernal symbols or,
less subtly, horrific images of terrified and tortured steeds of the darkest hues. They typically
give off a slight smell of brimstone, as though they had been crafted in some hellish forge. On
command, the mount's rider may grant it the _smoke_ supernatural ability of a
[nightmare](ref/monster/nightmare) (see below). The horseshoes can be used for 10 rounds per day,
split up as the mount's rider chooses (minimum 1 round per use). While this power is activated, the
mount's eyes, hooves, mane, and tail burst into flame, and its hooves deal an extra 1d4 points of
fire damage.

> **Smoke (Su)** In battle, a nightmare exhales smoke that chokes and blinds foes, filling a 15-foot
> cone each round as a free action. Anyone in the cone must succeed on a DC 16 Fortitude save or
> become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) until 1d6 minutes after leaving the area. This smoke acts as
> [obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist) for the purposes of
> [concealment](ref/rule/concealment). The smoke persists for 1 round. The save DC is
> Constitution-based.
