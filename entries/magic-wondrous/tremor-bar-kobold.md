**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 235\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 70,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) [crowbar](ref/eq-misc/crowbar) can coax stone to shift aside
with alarming ease. Five times per day, when a [kobold](ref/race/kobold) wielder pries at a section
of stone and speaks a command word, the touched rock reshapes as if with
_[stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape)._

Additionally, once per day a kobold wielder can throw the crowbar like a
[thrown splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) at a spot of earth, metal, or stone to unleash
an earthquake on the spot where the crowbar lands.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake), [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape), creator must be a kobold;
**Cost** 35,000 gp
