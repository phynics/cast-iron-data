**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 16\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 8,500 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This scabbard of vigor prominently bears the symbol of [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae). Three times per
day, the scabbard can cast _[bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon)_ on any weapon sheathed within,
but does so reluctantly on any weapon other than a [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword). While the
user is in combat, the scabbard hurls threats of righteous retribution in a loud, clear voice as it
attempts to demoralize opponents ([Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) bonus of +7). The consciousness
within the crusader's scabbard is valorous and stridently condemns displays of cowardice or
dishonesty.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon), [greater magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon-greater);
**Price** 4,250 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** lawful good; **Ego** 6\
**Senses** 60 ft.\
**Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 14\
**Communication** speech (Common)
