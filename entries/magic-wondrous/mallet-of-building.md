**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This heavy builder's mallet has a wooden shaft and a head made from shiny black iron that turns
slightly red when held close to metal. The wielder of the mallet can, as a full-round action, swing
the hammer to create and immediately hammer into place a copper nail, iron spike, iron piton, or
4-foot wooden fence post. A created fence post can penetrate earth, and the other materials can
penetrate earth, wood, or stone. Removing the created item from the surface destroys it, but
otherwise the item lasts as long as a normal object of its sort. The mallet can create up to 100
items per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[minor creation](ref/spell/minor-creation); **Cost** 2,500 gp
