**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 281, Advanced Player's Guide pg 300\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** -

This ripe red apple looks fresh and delicious. Should anyone bite into it (even a small taste), he
must make an immediate DC 19 Will saving throw or fall into an
[eternal slumber](ref/hex/eternal-slumber) as though by the witch's grand hex of the same name. The
victim can only be awakened by a casting of [break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [wish](ref/spell/wish), or [miracle](ref/spell/miracle), or
by a kiss from someone of royal blood.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Brew Potion](ref/feat/brew-potion),
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be a [witch](ref/class/witch) with
the eternal slumber grand hex; **Cost** 1,250 gp
