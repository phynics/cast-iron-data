**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 231\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 39,600 gp; **Weight** 12 lbs.

These massive horseshoes sport deadly spikes that jut from their front. Once per day on command, the
rider of a mount wearing these shoes may choose to either increase the mount's size by one size
increment (maximum Huge), or change its type to magical beast, as
_[beast shape IV](ref/spell/beast-shape-4)._ In addition, while the mount is augmented in either of
these two ways, it gains DR/5 chaos, evil, good, or law (selected by the rider). These horseshoes
are part of a set and require all four to be worn by the same animal to properly function.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape IV](ref/spell/beast-shape-4),
[magic circle against chaos/evil/good/law](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-chaos-evil-good-law);
**Cost** 19,800 gp
