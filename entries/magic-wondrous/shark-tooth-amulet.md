**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 178\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** -

This amulet is crafted of a single massive petrified shark tooth suspended on kelp twine and grants
its wearer combat potency while [underwater](ref/rule/physical-attacks-underwater). While wearing
this amulet, its wearer can use melee weapons normally, taking no penalty to attack and dealing full
damage with such attacks. This amulet also allows the wearer to make thrown range weapon attacks
underwater, though such attacks take a -2 penalty on attack rolls and their range increment is
halved.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[touch of the sea](ref/spell/touch-of-the-sea), the crafter must be an undine or have the water
subtype; **Cost** 4,500 gp
