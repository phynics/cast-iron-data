**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 310, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 523\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,305 gp; **Weight** 160 lbs.

This mallet is 8 feet long. If used as a weapon, it is the equivalent of a +3
[greatclub](ref/eq-weapon/greatclub) and deals triple damage against inanimate objects. The wielder
must have a Strength of at least 18 to wield it properly. Otherwise, she takes a -4 penalty on
attack rolls.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[clenched fist](ref/spell/clenched-fist); **Cost** 12,305 gp
