**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Swirling green and blue patterns bedeck the surface of this fine oak
[wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder), whose compass and needle are made of light polished steel
engraved with themes of [Gozreh's](ref/faith/gozreh) oceanic aspect. The needle does not point north
as one would expect, but instead indicates the direction the wind is traveling in the area around
the wayfinder. A wayfinder of zephyrs grants its bearer a +5 circumstance bonus on
[Profession (sailor)](ref/skill/profession-any) checks. Once per day, the bearer of a wayfinder of
zephyrs can move the needle to change the direction the wind is traveling toward any direction on
the compass. Optionally, the wielder may also increase or decrease the wind's intensity by one step
while changing its direction. A wayfinder of zephyrs has no effect on magical wind effects other
than indicating the direction they are traveling.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[alter winds](ref/spell/alter-winds), [control winds](ref/spell/control-winds); **Cost** 7,500 gp
