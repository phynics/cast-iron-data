## Infernal Cord

**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 60\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 5,000 gp (Normal), 31,000 gp (Greater); **Weight** 1 lb.

The entirety of this thick hide belt is scarred with impressions of fiendish visages, each shifting
between a scowl and a smile. Commonly used by the servants of [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus) in
Cheliax, this belt grants its wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to Constitution. Treat this Constitution
bonus as a [temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the belt
is worn.

Whenever an enemy scores a critical hit against the wearer, the wearer gains
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1 for 1 minute. This ability doesn't trigger if the critical
hit deals only nonlethal damage. Damage caused by silver weapons, good-aligned weapons, or spells or
effects with the good descriptor doesn't trigger the fast healing, and suppresses the fast healing
for 1 round if it's already active.

The wearer of an infernal cord can be detected as though she were an evil creature, and can sense
the item's evil while wearing it. This belt imparts 1
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) to any nonevil creature that wears it
until the belt is removed.

**Greater Infernal Cord:** This item functions as an infernal cord, but it grants a +4 enhancement
bonus to Constitution, and the [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) effect increases to fast
healing 4. Treat the Constitution bonus as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt
is worn.

The wearer can be detected as though she were an evil [cleric](ref/class/cleric), not just an evil
creature.

### Construction

[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance), [infernal healing](ref/spell/infernal-healing);
