**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 263, Advanced Race Guide pg 119\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,800 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Wearing this animal-hair cloak grants a +1 resistance bonus on saving throws. Furthermore, the
wearer can force its teeth to grow rapidly up to five times per day as a swift action. The oversized
teeth last for 1 round, during which time the wearer can make a bite attack. Treat this attack as a
primary [natural attack](ref/rule/natural-attacks) that deals 1d6 points of damage (or 1d4 if the
wearer is Small). If the wearer already has a bite attack, the damage of that bite attack increases
[by one step](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[alter self](ref/spell/alter-self), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance); **Cost** 1,250 gp
