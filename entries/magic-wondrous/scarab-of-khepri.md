**Sources:** Pathfinder \#84: Pyramid of the Sky Pharaoh pg 61, Osirion, Land of the Pharaohs pg 25\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,800 gp; **Weight** -

This small, blue figurine is fashioned in the likeness of a
[scarab beetle](ref/monster/beetle-scarab-beetle), the sacred animal of [Khepri](ref/faith/khepri),
the Ancient Osirian god of freedom, the rising sun, and work. Once per day, the bearer of the scarab
can speak with vermin as if using _[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),_ though the
information obtained is very limited. If the bearer has the [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy)
class feature, she may use it to influence vermin as easily as she influences animals. Once per day,
the bearer can cast _[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm)_ to summon a swarm of scarab beetles
(treat as a [spider swarm](ref/monster/spider-spider-swarm), but instead of poison, the swarm causes
[filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever)).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Vermin Heart](ref/feat/vermin-heart), [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),
[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm); **Cost** 3,900 gp
