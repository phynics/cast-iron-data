**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 270, Gods and Magic pg 60\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck, ring, or none; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** -

This tiny [dagger](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) has a black leather hilt and a gold-inlaid pattern of
stripes and sharp angles on its steel blade. It can transform into a delicate necklace or ornate
finger-ring at the wearer's command. The wearer gains a +3 competence bonus on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks while the item is carried or worn. The wearer can also use
it to cast _[ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound)_ once per day.

If [Calistria](ref/faith/calistria) is the wearer's patron, once per day the wearer may speak a
command word to use _[fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning)._ As a standard action, the wearer can
prick her finger with the dagger and swear an oath on her own blood to avenge herself against a
particular named creature; the wearer gains a +1 competence bonus on her next attack roll within 1
minute against that creature. Speaking a new vengeance-oath removes the old one, whether or not the
wearer has achieved her vengeance.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance), creator must have 5 ranks in Perception; **Cost** 2,250 gp
