**Sources:** Haunted Heroes Handbook pg 31\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 3,200 gp; **Weight** -

This ghost-white shawl is partially transparent and flutters elegantly with every motion its wearer
makes. If the wearer has the shared consciousness class feature, she gains the
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) feat in both skills determined by her phantom's
[emotional focus](ref/ability/emotional-focus) while the [phantom](ref/sidekick/phantom) is
manifested in ectoplasmic or incorporeal form. While her phantom is confined in her consciousness,
she instead gains a +4 competence bonus on skill checks with those skills.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[borrow skill](ref/spell/borrow-skill), creator must be a spiritualist with the shared consciousness
class feature.; **Cost** 1,600 gp
