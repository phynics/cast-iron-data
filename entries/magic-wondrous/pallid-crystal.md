**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,300 gp; **Weight** -

This finger-length crystal is a pale, opaque pink color when worn by a living creature, but slowly
changes to a deep, translucent violet when carried by an undead. The wearer can consume spoiled food
or drink as if it were fresh and wholesome, tasting as good as it did before it spoiled. Any person
with the [Profession (cook)](ref/skill/profession-any) skill can use the crystal to season a meal
with the flavor of salt, sugar, cinnamon, ginger, or pepper (or the equivalent spices for the undead
palate - fear's breath, nightfog, bloodroot, thileu bark, or hatefinger).

If [Urgathoa](ref/faith/urgathoa) is the wearer's patron, the wearer can use
_[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell)_ once per day. Both cure and inflict spells heal the wearer,
whether the wearer is living or undead; the crystal flares with black or white energy when it
converts these spells into a form that does not harm the wearer. The crystal does not affect any
other uses of positive or negative energy, just _cure_ and _inflict_ spells (and effects that
duplicate those spells).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), [death knell](ref/spell/death-knell),
[inflict light wounds](ref/spell/inflict-light-wounds),
[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink); **Cost** 1,650 gp
