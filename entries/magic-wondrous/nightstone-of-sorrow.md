**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 267, Gods and Magic pg 58\
**Aura** faint abjuration \[evil\]; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,300 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This black rock, usually worn on a heavy chain, looks pitted and partially charred and melted. It
gives the impression of reckless evil - more a dangerous scent than any other kind of feeling. The
wearer gains fire resistance 1 and a +1 resistance bonus on Will saves against fear. If the wearer
takes 10 or more points of fire damage in a single round, a claw-shaped pattern glows faintly on the
surface of the stone, similar to an _[arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark)._

If the dragon god [Dahak](ref/faith/dahak) is the wearer's patron, the wearer can use
_[shatter](ref/spell/shatter)_ once per day. At will, the wearer can cause the claw-symbol on the
rock to glow; true dragons immediately recognize the symbol as an icon of Dahak.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark), [remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), [shatter](ref/spell/shatter); **Cost** 1,650 gp
