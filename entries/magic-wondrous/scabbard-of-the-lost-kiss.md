**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This aged and worn scabbard is believed to have once held one of a dozen legendary scimitar relics
named [Dawnflower's Kiss](ref/magic-artifact/dawnflowers-kiss). From its prolonged proximity to such
a divine blade, the scabbard has acquired its own unique magical abilities. Twice per day on
command, the wearer can draw forth a blazing beam of red-hot fire from the scabbard that can be
wielded as if it were a [scimitar](ref/eq-weapon/scimitar), as per
_[flame blade](ref/spell/flame-blade)._

If [Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae) is the wearer's patron, half of the flame blade's damage is fire
damage, but the other half results directly from divine power and is therefore not subject to being
reduced by resistance to fire.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[flame blade](ref/spell/flame-blade), [flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike); **Cost** 4,300 gp
