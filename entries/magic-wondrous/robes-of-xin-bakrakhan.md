**Aura** strong abjuration and conjuration; **CL** 16th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 198,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The traditional robes of the Runelord of Wrath are known as the robes of Xin-Bakrakhan. Woven of the
finest of crimson silks and accented with precious gold, the robes are surprisingly light and easy
to move in. The robes of Xin-Bakrakhan aid and accentuate their wearer's spellcasting ability. They
grant a +5 shield bonus and [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) 24, and the wearer casts
all spells at +1 caster level. Two of the robe's pockets function as
[handy haversacks](ref/magic-wondrous/handy-haversack). The wearer of these robes is immune to the
effects of _[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions)_ and can use all skills and abilities that
require patience or concentration when under the effects of a [rage](ref/spell/rage) spell or
similar effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions),
[protection from spells](ref/spell/protection-from-spells), [shield](ref/spell/shield),
[secret chest](ref/spell/secret-chest); **Cost** 99,000 gp
