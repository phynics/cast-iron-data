**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 19\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 48,000 gp; **Weight** 250 lbs.

This urn-shaped object is large enough for a full-grown human to crouch inside. The bottom half of
the pod is ringed in Druidic glyphs and layered with ever-fertile soil, while the top is crafted
from stained glass that magically cycles between depictions of rainy, starry, and sunny skies. A
[ghoran](ref/race/ghoran) can expel its seed into a propagation pod and seal the pod as a full-round
action; this does not cause the ghoran to gain a negative level. Over the next 2d6 days, the
ghoran's seed sprouts into an exact replica of his current appearance. After maturing, this new
ghoran remains in stasis indefinitely, acting in all ways as a fully grown clone of the ghoran as
per the _[clone](ref/spell/clone)_ spell. If the ghoran dies and its soul transfers to the clone as
a result, the propagation pod shatters into shards of powerless clay and glass.

While a ghoran's seed is expelled into a propagation pod, it cannot use its seed racial trait, nor
can it use any feats or abilities dependent upon its seed. If the ghoran has the
[Sproutling](ref/feat/sproutling) feat, he can allow his clone to develop after 1d6 days instead of
2d6 days, though his size is adjusted and his ability scores modified as described by the Sproutling
feat should he die and subsequently inhabit his clone.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [clone](ref/spell/clone),
[plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth); **Cost** 15,660 gp
