## Goblin Fire Drum

**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp (Normal), 4,500 gp (Greater); **Weight** 1 lb.

This small crude-looking drum is attached to a strap long enough to loop around the wearer's neck. A
successful DC 12 [Perform (percussion)](ref/skill/perform-any) check activates the drum, which
enhances any nearby natural or magical fire as long as the bearer spends a move action each round to
continue playing. All fires (including fire spells and effects) within 30 feet of the bearer deal an
additional 1 point of fire damage (or 2 points for the greater version).

All unattended flammable alchemical objects such as [tindertwigs](ref/eq-misc/tindertwig), flasks of
[oil](ref/eq-misc/oil) or [alchemist's fire](ref/eq-weapon/alchemists-fire), and fireworks within
this range have a 10% chance of spontaneously igniting or exploding (as appropriate to the item)
when the drum activates. This chance increases by 5% each round and is checked at the start of the
performer's turn as long as the playing continues.

A greater goblin fire drum has all the above abilities and can create
_[pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics)_ once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),

gp (Greater)
