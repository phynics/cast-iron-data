**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 166\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This elaborate fan is made with a dozen black feathers set into a metal handle embossed with a
stylized crest. The bearer can use it to create a _gust of wind, whispering wind,_ or
_[wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall)._ The fan's effects can be used 3 times per day, in any
combination.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind), [whispering wind](ref/spell/whispering-wind),
[wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall); **Cost** 5,000 gp
