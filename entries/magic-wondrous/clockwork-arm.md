**Sources:** Plunder and Peril pg 47\
**Aura** strong conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,400 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

The claw can be attached in place of the missing arm of any Medium humanoid to function as a
replacement arm. This requires an hour-long installation surgery that must be performed by a
character with 8 ranks in both [Craft (clockwork)](ref/skill/craft-any) and [Heal](ref/skill/heal).
The recipient must succeed at a DC 18 Fortitude save for the arm to properly attach, and takes 2
points of [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) from the surgery in any case. If the
arm is properly attached, the recipient can lift up to 1-1/2 his maximum load over his head, and
gains a claw attack as a secondary attack that deals 1d6 points of damage (if nothing is held in
that arm).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [regenerate](ref/spell/regenerate), creator must have 8
ranks in Craft (clockwork); **Cost** 3,200 gp
