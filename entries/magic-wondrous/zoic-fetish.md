**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 114, Inner Sea World Guide pg 299\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,500 gp; **Weight** -

Originally invented by shamans of the Kellid tribes of the Realms of the Mammoth Lords, zoic
fetishes have spread throughout the Inner Sea region and are much sought after in any area where
dangerous animals dwell. Each zoic fetish looks different, but is essentially a small fetish made
from bone, branches, grass, and twine crafted into one of several shapes - a mammal, a bird, a
reptile, an amphibian, or a fish.

As long as a creature carries a zoic fetish, it is somewhat protected against attacks from animals,
gaining a +1 insight bonus to AC against all animals. In addition, possession of a zoic fetish
grants a +4 insight bonus on [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks. Finally, once per day,
the wielder can present a zoic fetish to an animal of the same classification as the fetish
(amphibian, bird, fish, mammal, or reptile) to use _[dominate animal](ref/spell/dominate-animal)_ on
that creature.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dominate animal](ref/spell/dominate-animal), [hide from animals](ref/spell/hide-from-animals);
**Cost** 3,750 gp
