**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 234\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 27,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This [masterwork drum](ref/eq-misc/musical-instrument-masterwork) is made from mammoth hide. A
Kellid with ranks in [Perform (percussion)](ref/skill/perform-any) can cause the drum to activate in
one of two modes: rousing or quelling. Activation requires a successful DC 15 Perform (percussion)
check. The two modes have the following effects.

**Quelling:** Animals and humanoids that can hear the drum from within 30 feet are subject to
_[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions)_ (DC 13) for as long as they remain in the area and the
drummer continues to succeed at a DC 15 Perform (percussion) check each round.

**Rousing**: Animals and humanoids that can hear the stampede drum from within 30 feet are subject
to _rage_ (DC 13) for as long as they remain in the area and the drummer continues to succeed at a
DC 15 Perform (percussion) check each round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions), [rage](ref/spell/rage), creator must be a Kellid; **Cost**
13,500 gp
