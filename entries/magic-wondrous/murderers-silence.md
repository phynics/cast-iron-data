**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 900 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

In areas where divine magic is readily available, [speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead) has
captured many a murderer; this serum was invented by assassins to silence their victims. Murderer's
silence prevents a corpse from communicating via _[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead)_ by
dissolving the victim's vocal organs. To use this item, the thick black liquid must be slowly poured
down the throat of the corpse (a full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity). The
following round, the corpse's tongue, vocal cords, and lips deteriorate and the corpse becomes
incapable of responding to _[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead)._ Other means of
interrogating the spirit of the departed still function normally. Magic that restores the flesh to a
corpse doesn't restore any parts rotted away by murderer's silence. This item has no effect on parts
of the body other than those listed. The listed is for a single dose.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[contagion](ref/spell/contagion); **Cost** 450 gp
