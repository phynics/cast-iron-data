**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 230\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 24,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These sandals are woven from grass, lengths of pliant vine, living leaves, and supple bark. In
forest terrain, the wearer leaves no tracks or scent and gains a +2 competence bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. The wearer can move through natural undergrowth without
impediment or harm as a druid using [woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride), and once per day
she may step inside a tree and remain there for up to 9 hours, or teleport to a similar tree within
range, as _[tree stride](ref/spell/tree-stride)_ but allowing only a single teleport.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace), [tree stride](ref/spell/tree-stride), creator
must be a druid; **Cost** 12,000 gp
