**Sources:** Construct Handbook pg 19\
**Aura** moderate divination and enchantment; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 132,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A creature wearing this thick necklace is automatically aware of all constructs within 60 feet as
per the [lifesense](ref/umr/lifesense) ability but only sensing constructs. Constructs can't see,
hear, or otherwise detect the presence of the wearer, even when using extraordinary or supernatural
sensory capabilities such as [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense), [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight),
[scent](ref/umr/scent), or [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense). If the wearer touches a construct or
attacks any creature, this concealing ability ceases to function for 1 minute. Constructs with an
Intelligence score of 3 or greater can attempt a DC 18 Will save to detect the wearer.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [locate object](ref/spell/locate-object); **Cost** 66,000 gp
