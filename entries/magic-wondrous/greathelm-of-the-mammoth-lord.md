**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 133\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 24,700 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This hide helm is a powerful variant of the more common
[helm of the Mammoth Lord](ref/magic-wondrous/helm-of-the-mammoth-lord). Like its lesser
incarnation, a greathelm of the Mammoth Lord is set with plates of ivory carved with runes, with a
pair of tusks curving down either side of the wearer's face for cheek guards. The helm's tusks
provide a gore attack dealing 1d8 points of damage for a Medium wearer (or 1d6 points of damage for
a Small wearer) and count as magic and cold for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). The greathelm of the Mammoth Lord also protects the
wearer from cold environments as per _[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)._

Furthermore, it provides a +5 competence bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal),
[Ride](ref/skill/ride), and [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks with elephants,
mammoths, mastodons, and other elephant-like creatures. On command, the wearer can use detect
animals or plants or speak with animals, but only to detect or communicate with elephant-like
creatures. Once per day, the wearer can transform into a mammoth (treat as an
[mastodon](ref/monster/elephant-mastodon)) as _[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3), [charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal),
[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),
creator must have 5 ranks in Handle Animal and Ride; **Cost** 12,350 gp
