**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 26\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,350 gp; **Weight** 50 lbs.

This elegantly carved figurehead typically depicts a fantastic magical creature, divine figure, or
local hero, and it is either attached to the prow of a sailing vessel or carved into the hull of the
ship. Once per day, the figurehead can be activated by speaking its command word as a swift action,
awakening its protective power for 1 minute. While the figurehead is active, all ranged attacks
originating from anywhere other than the ship have a 20% miss chance against the vessel and anyone
aboard it. This ability has no effect on melee attacks, rays, or ranged attacks that do not require
an attack roll to hit (such as [magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile)).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[entropic shield](ref/spell/entropic-shield); **Cost** 675 gp
