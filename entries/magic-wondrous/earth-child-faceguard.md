**Sources:** Giant Hunter's Handbook pg 28\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** -

Decorated with the emblems of the Earth Child cadre, an earth child faceguard is designed to refine
the wearer's focus when fighting giants. The wearer gains the benefits of Earth Child Style,
regardless of whether she meets the prerequisites. If the wearer does not possess the defensive
training racial trait, the dodge bonus to AC against giants is reduced to +2. If the wearer already
possesses the Earth Child Style feat, she can enter the earth child stance as a free action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Earth Child Style](ref/feat/earth-child-style), [know the enemy](ref/spell/know-the-enemy);
**Cost** 5,000 gp
