**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 213\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

If this belt's wearer has at least one hand free (holding nothing), he can attempt to deflect a
successful melee attack with an immediate action. To do so, he rolls an unarmed strike against the
opponent, including any applicable modifiers such as a [favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy)
bonus or the penalty for [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack). If his attack roll is greater than
the opponent's attack roll, the attack is negated without effect. The unarmed strike itself causes
no damage and does not count as a successful attack against the opponent. The opponent does not need
to be within the wearer's reach to use this ability. Whether the attack succeeds or not, the wearer
takes a -4 penalty on attack rolls until the end of his next turn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise),
[Improved Unarmed Strike](ref/feat/improved-unarmed-strike); **Cost** 12,500 gp
