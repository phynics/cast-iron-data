**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 259\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,800 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

While a block of this sweet-scented incense burns, creatures inhaling its fumes become unusually
receptive to thought. In still air, the incense's smoke permeates a 40-foot-by-40-foot area to a
height of 20 feet. Creatures within that area can communicate telepathically with other creatures in
the area. Creatures communicating in this fashion don't need to share a language, but must have an
Intelligence of 3 or higher. The openness of mind engendered by the incense also provides a +5
insight bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks
against creatures in the area, and imposes a -5 penalty on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks against such creatures and a -2 penalty on saving throws
against mind-affecting effects.

Moderate wind halves the area of the smoke. Strong wind disperses the incense, rendering it
ineffective as long as the wind continues. A single block of incense of open thoughts burns for 1
hour. It can be extinguished and relit, but each use consumes at least 10 minutes of its remaining
duration.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[telepathic bond](ref/spell/telepathic-bond); **Cost** 1,400 gp
