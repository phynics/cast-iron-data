**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 268\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This long stole of unrelenting black is marked on one end with golden scales and the other with the
embroidered image of hooded justice. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks and can create a
_[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth)_ for up to 5 minutes per day; these rounds need not be
consecutive. When the wearer uses [detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos),
[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil), [detect good](ref/spell/detect-good), or
[detect law](ref/spell/detect-law), at the beginning of her next turn after casting she gains
information as if she had concentrated for 3 full rounds. Finally, if the wearer is an
[inquisitor](ref/class/inquisitor), when using her judgment she receives a bonus on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks and her combat maneuver defense as if she were wielding a
[jurist](ref/magic-enh/jurist) weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth), creator must have 5 ranks of Sense Motive; **Cost** 9,000
gp
