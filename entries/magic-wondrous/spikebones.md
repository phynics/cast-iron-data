**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 165\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 22,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Spikebones are a collection of bleached humanoid bones - typically the ribs, vertebrae, and finger
bones - carried in a crude pouch made of the humanoid's skin and connective tissue. Three times per
day, the spikebones can be cast upon the ground as a standard action to create an area of dangerous
spikes. Bony in color and shape, these sharp protrusions function like a
_[spike growth](ref/spell/spike-growth)_ spell. The spikes fill four contiguous 10-foot-square
areas, shaped by the user at the time of deployment. The spikes remain effective for up to 3 hours,
after which they vanish and the spikebones magically reappearing in their pouch.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead), [spike growth](ref/spell/spike-growth); **Cost** 11,000 gp
