**Sources:** Champions of Balance pg 28\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 54,200 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The cloak of moral refraction has long been a tool of the [riftwardens](ref/pclass/riftwarden) -
mystics who charge themselves with maintaining cosmic balance and closing extraplanar portals
throughout the multiverse. The shimmering fabric of this light silver-and-red cloak never dirties or
wrinkles, and when laid out flat, the cloak reflects light nearly as well as a mirror.

A fully charged cloak of moral refraction has 3 charges; charges used are automatically renewed at a
rate of 1 charge per day at dawn. The wearer may use the cloak to expend these charges and create
the following effects.

- **1 charge:** Dispel an ongoing spell with an alignment descriptor, as the third function of
  [dispel chaos/evil/good/law](ref/spell/dispel-chaos).
- **2 charges:** [Ready](ref/rule/ready) an action to [counter](ref/rule/counterspells) a spell or
  [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with an alignment descriptor. The effect is
  automatically countered, and no caster level check is required. The charges are expended only
  after countering a spell or spell-like ability. If you ready this action but it is not triggered,
  the charges are not expended.
- **3 charges:** As an immediate action, counter a spell or
  [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) cast by an outsider with an alignment subtype.
  The effect is automatically countered, and no caster level check is required. In addition, the
  outsider must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be banished back to its home plane, as
  _[banishment](ref/spell/banishment)._ An outsider with more than 26 Hit Dice is unaffected by the
  banishment effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[banishment](ref/spell/banishment), [dispel chaos](ref/spell/dispel-chaos),
[evil](ref/spell/dispel-evil), [good](ref/spell/dispel-good), and [law](ref/spell/dispel-law),
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), creator must be neutral; **Cost** 27,100 gp
