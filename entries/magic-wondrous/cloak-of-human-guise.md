**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 264, Advanced Race Guide pg 58\
**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 900 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This plain cloak only has any effect when worn by a member of a half-human race, such as a
[half-orc](ref/race/half-orc) or [half-elf](ref/race/half-elf). It alters the wearer's appearance
similarly to a [hat of disguise](ref/magic-wondrous/hat-of-disguise), but only to the extent that it
conceals or alters the wearer's non-human physical traits so the creature appears fully human. Any
feature that is plausibly human remains. For example, a half-orc wearing the cloak loses his green
or gray skin color and pointed ears, has no visible tusks, and is otherwise completely able to pass
as a human version of his normal self. Likewise, a half-elf wearing the cloak has round ears,
human-like eyes, and no other traits indicating elven ancestry. The wearer has no control over the
specific guise, and those familiar with his normal appearance can recognize him in his human guise.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self); **Cost** 450 gp
