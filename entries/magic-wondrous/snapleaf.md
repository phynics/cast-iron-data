**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 319\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 750 gp; **Weight** -

This crystalline carving looks like a hand-sized tree leaf. It is usually worn around the neck on a
strap so it is within easy reach, but can be affixed to a belt or clothing just like a badge. While
falling, the bearer can snap and destroy the snapleaf as an immediate action to gain the benefits of
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ and _invisibility._ The duration of the two effects work
independently; ending one early does not affect the other. The item cannot be activated to provide
just one of these two effects; they are always activated simultaneously.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility); **Cost** 375 gp
