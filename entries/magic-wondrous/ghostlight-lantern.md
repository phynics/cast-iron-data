**Sources:** Pathfinder \#92: The Hill Giant's Pledge pg 9\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This brass [bullseye lantern](ref/eq-misc/bullseye-lantern) is embossed with dancing orbs flitting
amid a leaf-and-holly motif. A ghostlight lantern functions as a normal bullseye lantern, but if the
remains of a dead [will-o'-wisp](ref/monster/will-o-wisp) are placed inside it, the lantern gains
additional abilities. The dead will-o'-wisp powers the lantern for a period of 1 week, during which
time the lantern sheds a bluish-white light without the need for oil as fuel. Creatures within the
60-foot cone of the lantern's normal light are outlined with a pale, bluish glow, as
_[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire)._ [Orcs](ref/monster/orc) within the cone are
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled), as if in bright sunlight, for as long as they remain within the light.
Additionally, three times per day as a standard action, the wielder of a ghostlight lantern can draw
upon the power of the deceased will-o'-wisp fueling it to cast
_[shocking grasp](ref/spell/shocking-grasp)._ Lastly, while within the bounds of Ghostlight Marsh in
the Hold of Belkzen, a ghostlight lantern unerringly emits a beacon that illuminates a direct path
to the circle of standing stones that form the entrance to the Vault of Thorns. After 1 week, the
power of the deceased aberration is exhausted, and the ghostlight lantern loses its additional
abilities, once again functioning only as a normal bullseye lantern until another dead will-o'-wisp
is placed inside.
