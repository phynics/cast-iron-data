**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 37,900 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This lute is a [masterwork instrument](ref/eq-misc/musical-instrument-masterwork) constructed of
polished ebony, with mother of pearl embellishments and gold frets. It grants any creature playing
it a +2 competence bonus on [Perform (string)](ref/skill/perform-any) checks as it gently
telepathically whispers ways the creature can improve is current performance. It also grants those
who can play the lute the following abilities.

- Three times per day on command, a creature with at least 6 ranks in Perform (string) can play a
  chord on the lute of discord to employ the _[murderous command](ref/spell/murderous-command)_
  spell.
- Three times per day on command, a creature with at least 6 ranks in Perform (string) can play a
  chord on the lute of discord to use the _[distracting cacophony](ref/spell/distracting-cacophony)_
  spell (3rd level).
- Once per day on command, a creature with at least 6 ranks in Perform (string) can play a chord to
  use the _[echolocation](ref/spell/echolocation)_ spell (4th level caster)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[distracting cacophony](ref/spell/distracting-cacophony), [echolocation](ref/spell/echolocation),
[murderous command](ref/spell/murderous-command); **Price** 18,950 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** chaotic neutral; **Ego** 14\
**Senses** 120 ft., [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision)\
**Int** 14, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 18\
**Communication** telepathy (Common and four other languages)
