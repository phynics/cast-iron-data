**Sources:** Tears at Bitter Manor pg 54\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 150 gp; **Weight** -

If a character drinks this gray liquid, the next time (within 24 hours) he falls below 0 hit points,
he exhales a sliver of his consciousness - whether he is alive or dead. If a character drinks
multiple elixirs in a 24-hour period, only one is effective.

As an immediate action, the sliver can guide the action of one ally within 30 feet of the drinker's
body. This guidance gives a +2 luck bonus on that ally's next attack roll, to the ally's AC against
the next attack made against her, or on the ally's next skill check. The drinker decides the type of
bonus and the ally. The bonus must be used within 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance); **Cost** 75 gp
