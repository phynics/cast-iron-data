**Aura** faint evocation and illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 4,800 gp; **Weight** -

This amulet allows the wearer to use the same spells many drow can cast innately. The wearer can use
each of the following spells once per day as a spell-like ability: _dancing lights, darkness,_ and
_[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire)._ In addition, while wearing this item, the wearer is granted
the ability to speak and understand Undercommon and gains proficiency with the
[hand crossbow](ref/eq-weapon/hand-crossbow), the [rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier), and the
[short sword](ref/eq-weapon/short-sword). While the target is wearing the amulet, she radiates the
aura of a chaotic evil creature for the purposes of spells such as
[detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos) and [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil). A bloodlink can
serve as the focus for a [recorporeal incarnation](ref/spell/recorporeal-incarnation) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bestow weapon proficiency](ref/spell/bestow-weapon-proficiency),
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages), [dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights),
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness), [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire),
[misdirection](ref/spell/misdirection); **Cost** 6,075 gp
