**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 147\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 27,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This small, weathered journal contains 10,000 jokes that change to be perfectly suited for
particular audiences. The book grants its owner a +5 bonus on any [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy)
check to influence the attitude of a creature. Additionally, up to three times per day the book can
be used to cast _[hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter)_ (Will DC 13 negates).

By expending one use of mythic power as a standard action, the book's reader can make a brutal
verbal tirade mocking a creature. The target creature is enraged per the _rage_ spell, and all other
enemies within 30 feet of the target begin laughing uncontrollably, as
_[hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter)_ (Will DC 14 negates either effect). The book
contains only one perfect joke for any given creature, so attempting to use the book on a creature a
second time yields no effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [hideous laughter](ref/spell/hideous-laughter),
[rage](ref/spell/rage); **Cost** 13,500 gp
