**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 29\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 750 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

These dense pebbles are sometimes used by [drow](ref/race/drow) skirmishers for both offense and
defense. If a stalagmite seed is thrown, it has a range increment of 20 feet. If it's slung, treat
it as a [sling bullet](ref/eq-weapon/sling-bullets). If the attack misses, roll for misdirection as
if it were a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon).

When a stalagmite seed strikes a natural stone surface, a stone stalagmite immediately emerges where
it landed, filling the 5-foot square and growing to 10 feet in height. Any creature occupying the
square takes 5d6 points of piercing and bludgeoning damage and is knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone).
Any creature that succeeds at a DC 14 Reflex save takes half damage and is not knocked prone.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic stone](ref/spell/magic-stone), [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape); **Cost** 375 gp
