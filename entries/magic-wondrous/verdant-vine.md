**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** -

Consisting of a vine with five green berries, a verdant vine is worn tied around the wrist. Once per
day on command, the wearer can pinch a berry off the vine, and throw it up to 30 feet away, like a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon). Viciously thorny vines erupt in a 20-foot radius from
the point of impact, even if the berry misses its target. Creatures within the area must succeed at
a DC 16 Reflex saving throw or become [entangled](ref/rule/entangled), as the spell
_[entangle](ref/spell/entangle),_ as the vines coil around the creature's legs. The vines persist,
but at the start of the affected creature's turn, it gains a new saving throw against the effect. If
the affected creature fails the saving throw, it also takes 1d4 points of damage.

A DC 20 Strength check, made as a standard action, allows an affected creature to break free and end
the effect. Failing the check causes the vines to constrict, dealing 1d4 points of damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[entangle](ref/spell/entangle), [plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth); **Cost** 3,000 gp
