**Sources:** Undead Unleashed pg 43\
**Aura** moderate varied; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

An undead wearing this golden mask is treated as both an undead and a creature of the type it was
before becoming undead (including any associated subtypes) for the purposes of determining its
eligibility as a target of illusion and transmutation spells (such as
[alter self](ref/spell/alter-self) or [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)). A corpse wearing
the mask gains the effects of [gentle repose](ref/spell/gentle-repose) and
_[sanctify corpse](ref/spell/sanctify-corpse)._ A living creature wearing the mask gains no effects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph), [sanctify corpse](ref/spell/sanctify-corpse); **Cost** 4,500 gp
