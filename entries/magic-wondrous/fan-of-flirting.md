**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,700 gp; **Weight** -

This fan, a favorite tool of courtiers and courtesans, is usually decorated with a delicate design
of flowers or geometric patterns. Once per day, the wielder can use the fan to attempt to completely
infatuate a humanoid target, causing it to stare deeply into the wielder's eyes and ignore
everything else around it. The wielder utters a command word and holds the fan in front of his face,
flourishing it and peering over its top to make eye contact with the target. The target must succeed
at a DC 11 Will save or fall under the combined effects of the _[hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism)_
and _[lock gaze](ref/spell/lock-gaze)_ spells. The effects last for 5 rounds or until the wielder
dismisses it by closing the fan. Afterward, the target is charmed by the fan's user for 1 hour (as
_[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person)_).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person), [hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism),
[lock gaze](ref/spell/lock-gaze); **Cost** 850 gp
