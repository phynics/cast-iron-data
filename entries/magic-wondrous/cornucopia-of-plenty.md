**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 35,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This large container, crafted from an animal horn or woven of wicker, overflows with fruits, nuts,
and vegetables. Up to three times per day, it can be inverted to pour forth delicious food equal to
a casting of _[create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water)_ for up to six people. Though
the cornucopia produces no water, the succulent fruits slake normal thirst.

If the user expends one use of mythic power when inverting the horn, it instead produces a
_[heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast)_ that also refreshes the partakers as though they had
rested for a full 8 hours sleep. Creatures with the [recuperation](ref/umr/recuperation) ability who
dine on this feast recover as though they had rested for the hour required by the recuperation
ability, with no need to expend additional uses of mythic power. Eating more than one such feast
within a single day provides no additional benefit.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water),
[heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast); **Cost** 17,500 gp
