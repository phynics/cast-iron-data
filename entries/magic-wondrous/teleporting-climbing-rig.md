**Sources:** Dungeoneer's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** moderate conjuration (teleportation); **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,600 gp; **Weight** 7 lbs.

This [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) [grappling hook](ref/eq-misc/grappling-hook) is decorated
with runic engravings and attached to 50 feet of [silk rope](ref/eq-misc/silk-rope-50-ft), with the
other end of the rope tied around a thick iron ring. Three times per day, the wielder of this item
may speak the item's command word while holding the iron ring. The grappling hook then teleports to
any designated location within 50 feet to which the user has both line of sight and line of effect.
The grappling hook does not teleport if the silk rope is held at any point other than the iron ring.
If the designated location does not possess a means for the rig's hook to attach, the grappling hook
falls to the ground after teleporting.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door); **Cost** 3,800 gp
