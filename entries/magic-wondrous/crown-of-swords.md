**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 242, Advanced Race Guide pg 78\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This radiant crown of steel is bedecked with miniature [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) swords. A
crown of swords can be used up to 10 times per day. When struck in combat, the wearer may spend one
use as an immediate action to create a [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword)-shaped
_[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon)_ that then attacks her attacker. On the wearer's
next turn, she may spend one additional use each round to continue attacking that target with the
_[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon);_ the
_[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon)_ cannot be redirected and disappears if the target
is killed or moves out of range. Multiple _spiritual weapons_ may be created (even attacking the
same target) if the wearer is attacked in subsequent rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[spiritual weapon](ref/spell/spiritual-weapon); **Cost** 3,000 gp
