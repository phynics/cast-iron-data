**Sources:** Dragonslayer's Handbook pg 30\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This gray incense smells of cold granite. When burned, the smoke from the incense spreads to fill a
space equal to ten 10-foot cubes. This smoke remains stationary unless moved by wind. If inhaled by
a creature with [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense), [scent](ref/umr/scent), or
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense), the creature must attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save. If it fails
the save, it does not benefit from its [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense),
tremorsense, or scent abilities for 10 minutes. After 10 minutes the smoke from the incense
dissipates and loses all magical properties.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[blindness/deafness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness); **Cost** 750 gp
