**Aura** minor illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** cloak; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Designed to meet the dual needs of the Chelish elite - glamour and subtlety - the cloak of flash and
shadow is a magic reversible cloak. When worn one way, the cloak shimmers with heavy gold and silver
embroidery against richly colored silk, and grants you a +2 enhancement bonus to Charisma.

When worn inside-out, the matte-black lining seems to absorb light, cloaking you in shadow and
giving you a +2 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. In this configuration you
can cast _[blur](ref/spell/blur)_ three times per day.

Reversing the cloak's position is a standard action and requires at least one free hand.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [blur](ref/spell/blur),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor); **Cost** 5,500 gp
