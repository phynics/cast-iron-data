**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 28,800 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

Unlike the heavy cast-brass mariner's astrolabes normally used at sea, this
[astrolabe](ref/eq-misc/astrolabe) is of the more common type found on land, made of flat discs of
brass. The famed navigator-astrologer Svingli of Morm, a drowned settlement of old Lirgen, was said
to have sacrificed her eye to the instrument to grant her knowledge of things beyond mortal sight,
thus giving the astrolabe its name.

In addition to an astrolabe's normal +2 circumstance bonus, Svingli's Eye grants a further +2
competence bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography),
[Profession (sailor)](ref/skill/profession-any), and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks when used
to navigate on land or sea. Once per day, the user can make a DC 20 Perception check while using
Svingli's Eye as a move action to catch a glimpse of how things really are for 1 round, as if with a
_[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing)_ spell. While using the astrolabe's
_[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing)_ ability, the user can also see through
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) caused by smoke or fog and gains a +10 competence bonus on
Perception checks to spot hidden or disguised creatures or objects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[know direction](ref/spell/know-direction), [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 14,600 gp
