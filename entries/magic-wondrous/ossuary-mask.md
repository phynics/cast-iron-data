**Sources:** Tombs of Golarion pg 43\
**Aura** faint abjuration and divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 3,100 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This golden funerary mask depicts the serene expression found only at the moment of a peaceful
death. When worn, it grants the wearer a +2 competence bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise),
[Perception](ref/skill/perception), and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks, and a +2
resistance bonus on Will saving throws.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance); **Cost** 1,550 gp
