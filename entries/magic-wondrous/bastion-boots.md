**Sources:** The Harrow Handbook pg 30\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 10,500 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

These metal boots are engraved with the imagery of [The Keep](ref/eq-misc/harrow-deck), and evoke
the unwavering determination represented by that card. The boots can be used as an
[immovable rod](ref/magic-rod/immovable-rod), even when worn. If the boots are moved, the wearer
moves with the boots. Even while not activated, bastion boots grant the wearer a +4 bonus to her CMD
against combat maneuvers to move her.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[levitate](ref/spell/levitate); **Cost** 5,250 gp
