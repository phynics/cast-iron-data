**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 305\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This iron gargoyle face holds an iron ring in its mouth. Once per day, when the flat back of the
gargoyle face is placed against a wall, floor, or ceiling, it fuses into place, creating a usable
door and penetrating the space beyond up to 1 foot as if using _[passwall](ref/spell/passwall)._
Speaking the command word again ends the _[passwall](ref/spell/passwall)_ effect, returns the
surface to its previous doorless shape, and releases the doorknocker from the surface.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[passwall](ref/spell/passwall); **Cost** 2,500 gp
