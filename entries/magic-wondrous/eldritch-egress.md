**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 55\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This cunningly designed bundle of wooden slats unfolds and interlocks to form a 3-foot-by-3-foot
frame. Once per day you may place the unfolded frame upon a solid surface to form a passage up to 10
feet deep, as if using a _[passwall](ref/spell/passwall)_ spell. The passageway lasts for 1 hour
unless the frame is removed earlier.

Folding or unfolding the frame is a full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity. Using
the unfolded frame to create a passage is a standard action. Medium or smaller creatures can easily
pass through the tunnel, while Large creatures require an [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist)
check. The eldritch egress can only be removed from outside the surface it was placed on, so careful
planning is required when using it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[passwall](ref/spell/passwall); **Cost** 5,000 gp
