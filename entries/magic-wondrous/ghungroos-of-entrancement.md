**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 225\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 3,750 gp; **Weight** -

Each of these musical anklets bears rows of tiny bells that accentuate the rhythmic elements of a
trained dancer's performances and enhance an accompanying musical performance, fascinating her
audiences. When using a [distraction](ref/performance/distraction) bardic performance, the wearer
gains a +5 competence bonus on [Perform (dance)](ref/skill/perform-any) checks; when using a
[fascinate](ref/performance/fascinate) bardic performance, the wearer gains a +1 competence bonus to
the saving throw DC. In addition, the wearer can use Perform (dance) with a
[countersong](ref/performance/countersong) bardic performance, and gains a +2 competence bonus on
the Perform (dance) check.

The wearer takes a -2 penalty on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks while wearing the ghungroos.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism), creator must have bardic performance and 5 ranks in Perform
(dance); **Cost** 1,875 gp
