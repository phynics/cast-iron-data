## Terra-Cotta Talisman

**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 13,000 gp (Horse), 16,000 gp (Warrior), 22,000 gp (Hound); **Weight** 1 lb.

These ceramic statuettes are molded in the likenesses of armored soldiers, horses, or dogs, and are
considered symbols of good fortune in battle. Once per day, the possessor of a terra-cotta talisman
can reroll a single attack roll as an immediate action, with a +1 luck bonus on the reroll, and keep
the most favorable result. A terra-cotta talisman also contains the vestige of a spirit of an
ancient warrior or warbeast that can be called upon once per day as a standard action to manifest
itself as a spiritual force. The powers of the talismans are as follows:

- **Terra-Cotta Horse:** _[phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed)_
- **Terra-Cotta Hound:** _[mage's faithful hound](ref/spell/mages-faithful-hound)_
- **Terra-Cotta Warrior:** _[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally)_

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[borrow fortune](ref/spell/borrow-fortune), [mage's faithful hound](ref/spell/mages-faithful-hound)
(hound), [phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed) (horse), or
[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally) (warrior); **Cost** 6,500 gp (Horse), 8,000 gp (Warrior),
11,000 gp (Hound)
