**Sources:** Cohorts and Companions pg 11\
**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 160,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This miniature cage of golden wire can be used to capture a weakened outsider once per day. The
target outsider must be called or otherwise physically present; a cage of captivity has no effect on
summoned outsiders. The target also must be unable to take actions continually throughout the entire
process, whether [cowering](ref/misc/cowering), [dazed](ref/misc/dazed), [dying](ref/misc/dying),
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless), [stunned](ref/misc/stunned), or [unconscious](ref/misc/unconscious).

The user can attempt to draw a subdued outsider into the cage of captivity by placing gemstones
within and concentrating for 1 minute. These gems must be worth at least 100 gp per Hit Dice of the
target. If the target fails a DC 20 Will save - this increases to DC 25 if the user speaks the
target's true name - the target shrinks to a height of 1 inch and is drawn into the cage, as the
minimum containment option of the _[binding](ref/spell/binding)_ spell. Any creature holding the
cage can converse with the trapped outsider. The planar being cannot use any supernatural or
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) while trapped, but it can still interact with,
lie to, and manipulate captors using appropriate skill checks.

Anyone holding the cage of captivity may attempt to open it as a standard action. If an outsider is
trapped within, doing so requires a DC 22 Will save. If a creature fails this save, they cannot open
the cage and are repulsed by it for 24 hours as per the _[antipathy](ref/spell/antipathy)_ spell.
The user who originally captured the imprisoned outsider gains a +5 morale bonus on this save.

Any creature opening a cage of captivity can compel the trapped outsider to perform a service in
exchange for its release. The trapped creature is compelled to fulfill this request as
_[geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest)_ if the creature opening the cage of captivity knows the
creature's true name. If not, this request functions as [lesser geas](ref/spell/geas-lesser) if the
trapped outsider has 7 or fewer Hit Dice, or as [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion) if it has more
than 7 Hit Dice. These requests ignore any [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) of the
trapped outsider, but a successful DC 20 Will save negates this effect. Once it performs this
service, an outsider can never again be trapped by the same cage of captivity.

Variant cages of captivity may take the form of lamps, bowls, bottles, and similar containers.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[antipathy](ref/spell/antipathy), [binding](ref/spell/binding), [geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest);
**Cost** 80,000 gp
