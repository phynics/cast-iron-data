**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 19,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This crown of laurel leaves inspires its wearer to achieve mighty deeds. She gains a +4 morale bonus
on saving throws against mind-affecting effects. If she succeeds at a saving throw against a harmful
mind-affecting effect, the surge of confidence gives her a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls, skill
checks, and weapon damage rolls for 1 minute. If she fails such a saving throw, she is instead
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 minute, though this condition doesn't stack with other fear effects
to make her [frightened](ref/misc/frightened) or [panicked](ref/misc/panicked).

A mythic creature wearing the wreath gains 2 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points)
per tier at the start of each day. These temporary hit points are restored whenever the wearer rests
8 hours or otherwise gains the equivalent of doing so.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [crushing despair](ref/spell/crushing-despair),
[good hope](ref/spell/good-hope); **Cost** 9,500 gp
