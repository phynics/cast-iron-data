## Headband of Alluring Charisma

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 248, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 516\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 4,000 gp (+2), 16,000 gp (+4), 36,000 gp (+6); **Weight** 1 lb.

This attractive silver headband is decorated with a number of small red and orange gemstones. The
headband grants the wearer an enhancement bonus to Charisma of +2, +4, or +6. Treat this as a
[temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the headband is
worn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor); **Cost** 2,000 gp (+2), 8,000 gp (+4), 18,000 gp (+6)
