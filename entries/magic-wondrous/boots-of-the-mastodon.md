**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 10,500 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These massive boots are made from [mastodon](ref/monster/elephant-mastodon) hide. The boots are so
large and cumbersome that their wearer takes a -2 penalty on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks,
but gains a +2 enhancement bonus on [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) attempts and a +2 dodge bonus to AC
while attempting an overrun combat maneuver. In addition, once per day the wearer can make a
[trample](ref/umr/trample) attack. Regardless of his actual size, the creature wearing the boots can
trample creatures of size Large or smaller in his path. The trample attack deals 2d8+18 points of
damage (Reflex DC 29 halves).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3); **Cost** 5,250 gp
