**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** neck; **Weight** -

This engraved gem appears to be of little value. If any character keeps the periapt in her
possession for more than 24 hours, she contracts a terrible rotting affliction that permanently
[drains](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) 1 point of Dexterity, Constitution, and Charisma every week.
The periapt (and the affliction) can be removed only by application of a
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell followed by a
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease) and then a [heal](ref/spell/heal),
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle), [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), or [wish](ref/spell/wish)
spell. The rotting can also be countered by crushing a periapt of health and sprinkling its dust
upon the afflicted character (a full-round action), whereupon the periapt of foul rotting likewise
crumbles to dust.

### Construction

**Requirements** [periapt of health](ref/magic-wondrous/periapt-of-health),
[periapt of proof against poison](ref/magic-wondrous/periapt-of-proof-against-poison),
[periapt of wound closure](ref/magic-wondrous/periapt-of-wound-closure); **Cost** -
