**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 287, Advanced Player's Guide pg 302\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

This enchanted iron pot can produce nourishing, simple fare sufficient to feed up to 36 people per
day upon command. Once per week it can also be commanded to create a
_[heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast)_ for up to 12 people. The cauldron needs neither fire nor
ingredients to produce food; it does so instantly upon command.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water), [heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast);
**Cost** 7,500 gp
