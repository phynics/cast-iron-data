**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 257, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 505\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This appears to be a piece of silver or gold jewelry used to fasten a cloak or cape. In addition to
this mundane task, it can absorb [magic missiles](ref/spell/magic-missile) of the sort generated by
the spell or [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). A brooch can absorb up to 101
points of damage from _magic missiles_ before it melts and becomes useless.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [shield](ref/spell/shield);
**Cost** 750 gp
