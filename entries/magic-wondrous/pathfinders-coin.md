**Sources:** Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 53\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This apparently simple coin, usually styled to resemble ancient currency, hides intricate gold and
platinum wires within. This matrix, carefully modeled after fragmentary Azlanti coins, causes the
Pathfinder's coin to rise an inch into the air and slowly spin in place if placed atop a
[wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder). In addition, when levitating above a wayfinder, the coin
can be given a message of 25 words or fewer. This message repeats in the speaker's voice the next
time the coin is floated above a wayfinder. Only one message may be stored at a time, and once it is
triggered, the message is expended and a new one may be recorded.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[identify](ref/spell/identify), [levitate](ref/spell/levitate),
[magic mouth](ref/spell/magic-mouth); **Cost** 750 gp
