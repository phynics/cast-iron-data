**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 22,000 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This heavy cloak is made from the hide of a [bulette](ref/monster/bulette), its thick gray leather
heavily abraded and seemingly always dusty, and features a plated dorsal fin that protrudes from
between the wearer's shoulders.

As a standard action, the wearer of a groundbreaker cloak can dive into the ground, gaining a burrow
speed of 20 feet through sand, soil, gravel, and loose stone. While burrowing, the wearer cannot
[run](ref/rule/run) or [charge](ref/rule/charge) and must travel along the surface, leaving the
exposed dorsal plate visible from above. As the wearer burrows, displaced dirt swells before her and
collapses behind her to create [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) in her wake. She
has [cover](ref/rule/cover) from attacks while burrowing. Unless the wearer has some other means of
breathing while submerged, she must [hold her breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) while burrowing.
Emerging from the ground is a move action that doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity.

Once per day, the wearer can perform a charge attack by diving deep into the ground, burrowing up to
40 feet in a straight line (ignoring any obstacles that don't extend at least 10 feet into the
ground) and bursting forth into a square adjacent to a target creature. This use of the cloak does
not disturb the terrain as noted above, except where the wearer enters and exits the ground. The
wearer ends this movement standing and the target of the charge is considered
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) against the attack.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3), [burrow](ref/spell/burrow); **Cost** 11,000 gp
