**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This gray stone carving of a gaunt older woman is usually painted with black hair and black clothing
with red trim. Once per day, the bearer can use it to cast _stabilize, resistance,_ and
_[virtue](ref/spell/virtue)_ - this is usually enough to ensure that a mother and newborn both live
through a difficult birth.

If [Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma) is the bearer's patron, the bearer can use the icon to cast
_[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ once per day. As a standard action, the bearer can touch a pregnant
woman and know whether she is carrying a boy or girl and whether it will be a single birth or
multiple. Once per day, the bearer can _[consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate)_ a corpse so if it is
buried within 24 hours it cannot be reanimated as an undead.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [augury](ref/spell/augury),
[consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate), [stabilize](ref/spell/stabilize),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance), [virtue](ref/spell/virtue); **Cost** 1,750 gp
