**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This 4-inch-square cloth pocket has no powers unless it is sewn onto a garment (which takes 1d4
minutes). Once sewn, it changes its appearance to match the garment. Any magic item inside it cannot
be magically detected, as if protected by _[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura)._ Hiding an item in
the pocket gives the garment's wearer a +5 competence bonus on
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks to conceal the item. The pocket can be removed
from the garment with a DC 10 [Heal](ref/skill/heal) check or an appropriate
[Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) check such as Craft (cloth) or Craft (leather); failing this roll by 5
or more gives the pocket the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura); **Cost** 500 gp
