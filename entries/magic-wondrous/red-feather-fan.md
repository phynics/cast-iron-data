**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 167\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This fan is made of copper fashioned into a dozen small feathers mounted on a ring-shaped bone
handle. Once per day, the bearer can hold it and speak a command word to
[counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) a darkness effect (as if using
_[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_) or counterspell a light effect (as if using
_[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness)_). If activated in an area of overlapping light and
darkness effects, the fan randomly counterspells one of them, leaving the other intact.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[daylight](ref/spell/daylight), [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness); **Cost** 2,500 gp
