**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 225\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This long cloak is a patchwork of beast and humanoid hides roughly stitched together. A crude,
crown-shaped patch of yellow-dyed fabric is sewn on the back, and a length of knotted hemp rope
secures the cloak around the wearer's neck. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and the ability to use
_[burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands)_ as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities)
three times per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands), [cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear); **Cost** 4,500 gp
