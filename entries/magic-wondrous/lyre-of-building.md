**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 308, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 522\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 13,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This magical instrument is usually made of gold and inlaid with numerous gems. If the proper chords
are struck, a single use of this lyre negates any attacks made against inanimate construction
(walls, roof, floor, and so on) within 300 feet. This includes the effects of a
[horn of blasting](ref/magic-wondrous/horn-of-blasting), a [disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate)
spell, or an attack from a ram or similar siege weapon. The lyre can be used in this way once per
day, with the protection lasting for 30 minutes.

The lyre is also useful with respect to building. Once a week, its strings can be strummed so as to
produce chords that magically construct buildings, mines, tunnels, ditches, etc. The effect produced
in 30 minutes of playing is equal to the work of 100 humans laboring for 3 days. Each hour after the
first, a character playing the lyre must make a DC 18 [Perform (string)](ref/skill/perform-any)
check. If she fails, she must stop and cannot play the lyre again for this purpose until a week has
passed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate); **Cost** 6,500 gp
