**Sources:** Pathfinder \#103: The Hellfire Compact pg 73, Gods and Magic pg 55, Inner Sea Gods pg
262\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,700 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This brilliant red cloak bears a golden symbol of an upright winged sword. The wearer can use
_[bless](ref/spell/bless)_ once per day.

If [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae) is the wearer's patron, the wearer gains a +1 enhancement bonus to
her natural armor. As a standard action, the wearer can create an illusory image of Iomedae's
banner, which floats 2 to 5 feet above the wearer's head and remains in place as long as the wearer
is conscious. If the wearer has the banner class feature, she can use this battle standard as her
banner.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[barkskin](ref/spell/barkskin), [bless](ref/spell/bless), [silent image](ref/spell/silent-image);
**Cost** 1,350 gp
