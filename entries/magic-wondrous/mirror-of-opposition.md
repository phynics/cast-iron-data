**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 312, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 524\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 92,000 gp; **Weight** 45 lbs.

This item resembles a normal mirror about 4 feet long and 3 feet wide. It can be hung or placed on a
surface and then activated by speaking a command word. The same command word deactivates the mirror.
If a creature sees its reflection in the mirror's surface, an exact duplicate of that creature comes
into being. This opposite immediately attacks the original. The duplicate has all the possessions
and powers of its original (including magic). Upon the defeat or destruction of either the duplicate
or the original, the duplicate and its items disappear completely. The mirror functions up to four
times per day. Destroying the mirror ([hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 1, 5 hit points) causes all of
the duplicates to immediately vanish.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [clone](ref/spell/clone);
**Cost** 46,000 gp
