**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 266\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 40,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These massive pauldrons take the shape of a pair of clenched fists made of beaten bronze. They
bestow a +4 bonus on CMD, and grant the wearer the [ferocity](ref/umr/ferocity) ability. On command,
the wearer can increase its size, as though subject to an
_[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_ spell, and can revert to regular size with another
command. Three times per day, as an immediate action after the pauldrons' wearer kills an opponent,
the wearer gains the benefit of the _[deadly juggernaut](ref/spell/deadly-juggernaut)_ spell for 1
minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[deadly juggernaut](ref/spell/deadly-juggernaut), [enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person),
[rage](ref/spell/rage); **Cost** 20,000 gp
