**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 324, Advanced Player's Guide pg 310\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 100 gp; **Weight** -

This thimble-sized clay container of thick, moist face paint has enough material to paint streaks
and whorls of any color on one creature's face. Once applied, the wearer can, as a swift action,
make the paint rearrange itself into a ghastly melange of colors, making her appearance momentarily
disturbing and hideous. The wearer chooses one creature who can see her painted face within 30 feet
to be affected by _[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear)_ (DC 11). Once the paint is used in this
fashion, it loses all magic and becomes normal face paint. Once applied, the wearer has 24 hours to
use the paint's magic; otherwise it fades away.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear); **Cost** 50 gp
