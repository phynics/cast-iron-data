**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This grapefruit-sized sphere of glossy black stone glows with faint whorls of orange fire deep
within its inky substance. When held in hand, it can be commanded to unleash a
_[dread bolt](ref/spell/dread-bolt)_ once per day. When used as an additional focus for casting a
spell with the evil descriptor, the bearer gains a +1 bonus to her caster level for these spells. It
bestows one permanent [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on any good
creature holding or carrying it. The negative level remains as long as the creature has the orb and
disappears immediately when the orb is no longer held or carried. This negative level cannot be
overcome in any way (including by [restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while the bearer has
the orb.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dread bolt](ref/spell/dread-bolt), creator must be evil; **Cost** 9,000 gp
