**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 177\
**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

While unattended, these rough leather boots appear to be in a slightly different place each time a
creature observes them. They seem to flicker around the wearer's feet for the first 24 hours she
wears them, reducing her movement speed by 5 feet and imposing a -2 penalty on Acrobatics checks.

After she has worn the boots for 24 hours, the flickering decreases, the penalties end, and she can
activate the boots. Three times per day, she can activate the boots as a swift action to avoid
provoking attacks of opportunity while moving out of a threatened square. While activated, the boots
conceal the wearer's movement in such a way that her foes see only short, blurred flashes of
movement. Any creature that has [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) or a similar nonvisual sense or is
affected by [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) can still make attacks of opportunity against the
wearer as normal.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [blur](ref/spell/blur);
**Cost** 1,000 gp
