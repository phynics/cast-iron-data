**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 259\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** -

The surface of this silver medallion is covered in angular geometric shapes that refract light
randomly. The medallion grants a continuous +2 resistance bonus on saves versus mind-affecting
spells, [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural abilities. If the
wearer fails a save to avoid becoming dominated or [confused](ref/misc/confused), the amulet
immediately grants the wearer a second saving throw to resist the effect. If this second save is
successful, the medallion's power fades away entirely and it becomes a simple piece of silver
jewelry.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil); **Cost** 1,750 gp
