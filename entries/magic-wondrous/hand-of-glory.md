**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 259, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 516\
**Aura** faint varied; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This mummified human hand hangs around a character's neck by a leather cord (taking up space as a
magic necklace would). If a magic ring is placed on one of the fingers of the hand, the wearer
benefits from the ring as if wearing it herself, and it does not count against her two ring limit.
The hand can wear only one ring at a time. Even without a ring, the hand itself allows its wearer to
use _[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ and _[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility)_ each once
per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead), [daylight](ref/spell/daylight),
[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility); **Cost** 4,000 gp
