**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,000 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

Made of a glassy, translucent substance, this orb swirls with the eldritch energy of spellcasters
long departed. When the bearer researches a spell to
[create a new spell or learn an existing one](ref/rule/adding-spells-to-a-wizards-spellbook) from
another source, this orb provides a +2 competence bonus on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft)
checks.

Additionally, when the command word is spoken, an orb of arcane research allows its bearer to gain a
+5 insight bonus on a [Craft (alchemy)](ref/skill/craft-any),
[Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana), [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature),
or Spellcraft check made to research a spell or extract formula. The orb can be used five times in
this way before the item cracks, losing all of its magical properties and reverting to a piece of
mundane glass.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Scribe Scroll](ref/feat/scribe-scroll), [read magic](ref/spell/read-magic); **Cost** 3,500 gp
