**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 165\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 8,800 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These thick, stout boots, crafted from the hide of a [gorthek](ref/monster/orc-gorthek), grant their
wearer a +2 enhancement bonus on attack rolls when [charging](ref/rule/charge). In addition, once
per day when the wearer hits a Large or smaller creature with an attack, he can send his foe flying
10 feet. The foe must be thrown in a straight line away from the wearer, and lands
[prone](ref/rule/prone) at the end of the movement. If an obstacle prevents the completion of the
opponent's move, the opponent and the obstacle each take 1d6 points of damage, and the opponent is
knocked prone in the space adjacent to the obstacle.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person); **Cost**
4,400 gp
