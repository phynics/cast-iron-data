## Lantern of the Four Elements

**Sources:** Disciple's Doctrine pg 29\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp (standard), 6,500 gp (greater); **Weight** 2 lbs.

This bronze lantern has a square base and four glass panels, each of which represents one of the
fundamental elements in the color, shape, and quality of the light that passes through it. The
lantern shines light as per the _[light](ref/spell/light)_ spell, and as long as the bearer carries
it the lantern protects her from heat and cold as per
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)._ (The bearer can turn the lantern's light off or on
as a standard action.)

In addition, while carrying the lantern, the bearer gains a +2 competence bonus on
[Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks to identify spells with the air, earth, fire, or water
descriptor and a +2 competence bonus on [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes) checks to
identify creatures that are native to the Elemental Planes.

The greater version of this item also allows the bearer to use certain
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). As a standard action, the bearer of a greater
lantern of the four elements can press one of the lantern's four panels to produce an effect:
_[levitate](ref/spell/levitate)_ for the air panel, _[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy)_ (fire
only) for the fire panel, _[air bubble](ref/spell/air-bubble)_ for the water panel, and
_[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb)_ for the earth panel. Each panel can grant its effect only
once per day.

### Construction

[levitate](ref/spell/levitate), [resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy),
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb) (greater lantern of the four elements); **Cost** 750 gp
(standard), 3,250 gp (greater)
