**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 107\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This turban consists of 30 feet of cloth held in place by a brooch. The owner can command the turban
to tie or untie itself as a move action. When the turban is tied on the wearer's head, the wearer
gains the benefits of _[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)._ Once per day when the turban
is untied, the owner can use the fabric to perform a rope trick (as per the spell
_[rope trick](ref/spell/rope-trick)_). In addition, the owner can spend 1 ki point as a swift action
to cast _[animate rope](ref/spell/animate-rope)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate rope](ref/spell/animate-rope), [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
[rope trick](ref/spell/rope-trick); **Cost** 2,000 gp
