**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 210\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This surprisingly soft belt has an absurdly large buckle, made even more absurd by the fact that
it's crafted in the shape of a smiling weasel's face. It grants its wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to
Dexterity, and the ability to move at half normal speed while [prone](ref/rule/prone). Treat the
enhancement bonus to Dexterity as a [temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for
the first 24 hours the belt is worn. The wearer does not take a penalty on melee attack rolls or to
AC against melee attacks while prone. Additionally, the wearer gains the
[compression](ref/umr/compression) ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1); **Cost** 5,000 gp
