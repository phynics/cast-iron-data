**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 2nd\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These travel-stained boots of stout brown leather enable the wearer to traverse great distances
without tiring. When making a [forced march](ref/rule/overland-movement), for each hour of marching
beyond 8 hours, the wearer needs only to make a Constitution check (DC 10, +1 per extra hour)
instead of the normal check. Furthermore, if the wearer fails the check, she only takes 1d4 points
of nonlethal damage and does not become [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) the first time she takes
nonlethal damage in this way.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Endurance](ref/feat/endurance); **Cost** 750 gp
