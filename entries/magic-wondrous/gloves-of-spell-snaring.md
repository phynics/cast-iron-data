**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** -

These thin gloves are covered in reflective glyphs and sigils, and the palms are slightly tacky.
Like [gloves of arrow snaring](ref/magic-wondrous/gloves-of-arrow-snaring), twice per day the wearer
can act as if she had the [Snatch Arrows](ref/feat/snatch-arrows) feat, even if she doesn't meet the
prerequisites for that feat. In addition, the wearer can expend one of this item's uses per day to
use the Snatch Arrows feat on a projectile targeting an ally in a space adjacent to her.

As an immediate action, the wearer can expend one use of mythic power and one of this item's uses
per day to block a spell targeting her or an adjacent ally, as the
[parry spell](ref/rule/guardian-3) guardian path ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [shield](ref/spell/shield),
[spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning), creator must have the parry spell path ability; **Cost**
4,500 gp
