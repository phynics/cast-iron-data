**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 157\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

These heavy, stone gloves feel surprisingly supple to anyone who puts them on. While a creature
wears stonefist gloves, his [unarmed strike damage](ref/rule/melee-and-ranged-attacks) is treated as
if he were [one size category larger](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments), and bypasses
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 8 or lower. When a mythic creature uses these gloves to make a
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver, he adds half his tier as a bonus on the combat maneuver
check.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin); **Cost** 5,000 gp
