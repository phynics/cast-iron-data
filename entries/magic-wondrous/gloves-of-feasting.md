**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 8,400 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

Each of these leather gloves has a large, ragged hole in the palm. Upon donning the gloves, the
wearer is [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) with pain for 1 round as the skin on his palms rips open,
revealing terrible gashes that each sprout fangs, lips, and a tongue. While the wearer's hands are
empty, he can breathe, eat, drink, and speak through the mouths on his hands (though this doesn't
allow him to drink more potions in a round, speak with more than one voice at a time, or otherwise
take more actions than normal). The wearer retains the use of his hands; the mouths remain shut
while the wearer is holding items in his hands.

The wearer receives a +2 enhancement bonus on combat maneuver checks to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple)
a foe with his hands. If he begins his turn grappling with his hands, the wearer can make a free
bite attack with one of the mouths on his hands. The bite is treated as a
[natural weapon](ref/rule/natural-attacks) and deals an amount of damage equal to 1d4 + his Strength
modifier for a Medium wearer (1d3 + his Strength modifier if he's Small).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph); **Cost** 4,200 gp
