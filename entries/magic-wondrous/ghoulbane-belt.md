**Sources:** Heroes of the Darklands pg 30\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Due to the toxic nature of many environments found within the Darklands, this belt is designed to
protect its wearer from exotic diseases, paralyzing attacks, and poisonous molds. The wearer gains a
+2 bonus on saving throws to resist disease, paralysis, and poison. This bonus increases to +4 on
saving throws to resist such effects caused by molds or slimes. Additionally, the wearer can cast a
single spell from the following list as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once
per day: _neutralize poison, remove disease,_ or _[remove paralysis](ref/spell/remove-paralysis)._
Activating this ability is a purely mental action that can be taken even while
[paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed) or otherwise [helpless](ref/misc/helpless). The chosen spell affects
only the belt's wearer.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison), [remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease),
[remove paralysis](ref/spell/remove-paralysis); **Cost** 10,000 gp
