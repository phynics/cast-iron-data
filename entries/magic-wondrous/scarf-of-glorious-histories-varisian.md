**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** -

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This light, brightly-colored scarf makes the wearer seem larger than life. He gains a +5 competence
bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) and [Perform (dance)](ref/skill/perform-any) checks.

If the wearer rightfully owns a kapenia and wears it at the same time as the scarf, he can glimpse
memories of the history traced by the Varisian people in their kapenias and folktales. He gains a +5
competence bonus on [Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history) checks and can attempt any
Knowledge (history) check untrained.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item); creator must have 5 ranks in
Intimidate, Knowledge (history), and Perform (dance); creator must be a Varisian; **Cost** 4,500 gp
