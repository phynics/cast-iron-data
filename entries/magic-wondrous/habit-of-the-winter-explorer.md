**Sources:** Pathfinder \#70: The Frozen Stars pg 62\
**Aura** faint abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** body; **Price** 13,380 gp; **Weight** 7 lbs.

This warm outfit consists of a woolen coat and pants, boots, gloves, and a heavy fur cloak with a
leather hood. The habit of the winter explorer provides a constant
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) effect in cold weather (it has no effect in warm
weather), and the wearer gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against magic cold effects and spells
with the cold descriptor.

When the hood of the habit is drawn up around the head, the wearer's eyes turn a piercing sky blue
color. The wearer becomes immune to [snow blindness](ref/rule/reflective-snow) and gains the ability
to see through natural or magical fog, sleet, snow, and other similar weather conditions with a
range of 60 feet.

The habit of the winter explorer also grants its wearer a +4 competence bonus on Stealth checks made
in cold weather (temperatures below 40 F). In addition, once per day in cold weather, the wearer may
turn invisible for 3 rounds as the _[vanish](ref/spell/vanish)_ spell.

All pieces of the habit of the winter explorer must be worn for the item to function.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision), [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), [vanish](ref/spell/vanish); **Cost** 6,690 gp
