**Sources:** Blood of Shadows pg 21\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This set of fine, [masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork) glimmer when
exposed to light. When held in hand, they glow with light visible only to the individual holding
them. This light allows the holder to work in all manners of darkness, including magical darkness,
unimpeded and without attracting the attention of other creatures. This light does not provide
enough illumination to see anything beyond a single lock or similar device on which the tools are
used. As they glow, the tools vary their color, indicating favorable or unfavorable positioning
within a device. These guiding lights grant a +5 circumstance bonus on
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [knock](ref/spell/knock);
**Cost** 3,000 gp
