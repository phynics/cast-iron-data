**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 234\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 17,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This brass [spyglass](ref/eq-misc/spyglass) never dulls or tarnishes. Three times per day, the user
can speak a command word while viewing a location with this elaborate brass spyglass to observe that
spot as if through _[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance)._ The effect
lasts for as long as the user continues to train the spyglass on the observed area.

If the user is a human, the effect's range is doubled and she can both see and hear at the selected
point.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell),
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance), creator must be a human;
**Cost** 8,750 gp
