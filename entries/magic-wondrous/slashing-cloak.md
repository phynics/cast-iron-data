**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 268\
**Aura** faint illusion and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This thick burgundy cloak is trimmed with [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) plates and cut to fit
handsomely over heavy armor. As a full-round action, the wearer can spin in his space, damaging each
creature within 5 feet. This attack uses the wearer's highest base attack bonus against each
creature, and deals 2d6 damage on a hit. The wearer is considered proficient with this attack.
Furthermore, the cloak can be magically enhanced like a melee weapon, adding the cost of the melee
weapon property to the cloak's cost.
