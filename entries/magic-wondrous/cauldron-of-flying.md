**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 40,000 gp; **Weight** 100 lbs.

This iron pot is large enough for two Medium humanoids to stand in comfortably. Upon command, the
cauldron and up to 500 pounds of additional weight can fly as if using
_[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight)._ The cauldron moves under the direction of the person
who spoke the activating command word. It can carry up to double its capacity, but doing so reduces
its speed to 30 feet. It can hover in place without the need for a [Fly](ref/skill/fly) skill check.
The cauldron provides partial [cover](ref/rule/cover) for anyone standing in it. It has a
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 10 and 60 hit points.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight); **Cost** 20,000 gp
