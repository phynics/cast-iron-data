## Spell Lattice

**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 235\
**Aura** varies (see text); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp (1st), 4,000 gp (2nd), 9,000 gp (3rd), 16,000 gp (4th), 25,000 gp
(5th), 36,000 gp (6th), 49,000 gp (7th), 64,000 gp (8th), 81,000 gp (9th); **Weight** 1 lb.

A spell lattice is a block of transparent crystal, internally etched with a three-dimensional
representation of a spell. This spell is chosen when the item is created (the contained spell
determines the school of the lattice's aura). When a spell lattice is wielded by a spontaneous
spellcaster who has the spell contained within it on her class spell list, she can use her spell
slots to cast that spell as if it were one of her spells known. When an
[arcanist](ref/class/arcanist) holds a spell lattice, as long as she has the contained spell on her
class spell list, she can use her spell slots to cast that spell as if it were one of her spells
currently prepared.

A spellcaster who uses a spellbook or formula book can copy the spell from a spell lattice
[as if it were a scroll](ref/rule/adding-spells-to-a-wizards-spellbook). Doing so expends the spell
from the spell lattice.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Scribe Scroll](ref/feat/scribe-scroll), creator must be able to cast the spell placed in the spell
lattice; **Cost** 500 gp (1st), 2,000 gp (2nd), 4,500 gp (3rd), 8,000 gp (4th), 12,500 gp (5th),
18,000 gp (6th), 24,500 gp (7th), 32,000 gp (8th), 40,500 gp (9th)
