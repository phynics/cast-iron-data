## Figurines of Wondrous Power

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 297, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 513\
**Aura** varies; **CL** varies\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,800 gp (Silver Raven), 6,000 gp (Coral Hippocampus), 7,000 gp (Emerald
Frog), 7,500 gp (Lapis Camel), 7,600 gp (Pearl Dolphin), 9,000 gp (Ruby Butterfly), 9,100 gp (Black
Jade Raven, Serpentine Eagle, Serpentine Owl), 10,000 gp (Bronze Griffon, Ebony Fly, Hematite
Courgar, Slate Spider), 13,000 gp (Tin Imp), 14,000 gp (Glass Walrus), 15,000 gp (Obsidian Raven),
15,500 gp (Onyx Dog), 16,500 gp (Golden Lions), 17,000 gp (Marble Elephant), 21,000 gp (Ivory
Goats), 28,500 gp (Obsidian Steed), 60,000 gp (Basalt Dragon); **Weight** 1 lb.

Each of the several kinds of figurines of wondrous power appears to be a miniature statuette of a
creature an inch or so high (with one exception). When the figurine is tossed down and the correct
command word spoken, it becomes a living creature of normal size (except when noted otherwise
below). The creature obeys and serves its owner. Unless stated otherwise, the creature understands
Common but does not speak.

If a figurine of wondrous power is broken or destroyed in its statuette form, it is forever ruined.
All magic is lost, its power departed. If slain in animal form, the figurine simply reverts to a
statuette that can be used again at a later time.

**Basalt Dragon:** This fist-sized, carved
dragon^[1](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-1)^ is sculpted from
volcanic rock. It becomes either a [pseudodragon](ref/monster/pseudodragon) or a
[flame drake](ref/monster/drake-flame-drake), depending on the command word used. The transformation
can take place twice per day, with a maximum duration of 2 continuous hours. The pseudodragon can
use its telepathy to communicate with its owner over any distance, but not across planes.

By expending one use of mythic power, the user of the figurine can instead transform the figurine
into a [young red dragon](ref/monster/dragon-chromatic-red-young-red-dragon). If the user is at
least 5th tier, she can instead expend two uses of mythic power to transform the figurine into an
[adult red dragon](ref/monster/dragon-chromatic-red-adult-red-dragon). If the user is at least 9th
tier, she can instead expend four uses of mythic power to transform the figurine into an
[ancient red dragon](ref/monster/dragon-chromatic-red-ancient-red-dragon). Transforming the figurine
into a true dragon renders the figurine powerless for 1 week after the transformation ends.; **CL**
20th; [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects),
[form of the dragon III](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-3),
[telepathic bond](ref/spell/telepathic-bond).

**Black Jade Raven:** This fist-sized piece of black jade is exquisitely tooled into the shape of a
raven with outstretched
wings.^[2](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-2)^ Upon command, it
transforms into a black raven (use the stats for an [eagle](ref/monster/eagle)) or a giant black
raven (use the stats for a [giant eagle](ref/monster/eagle-giant-eagle)). Both forms of the raven
have [Improved Steal](ref/feat/improved-steal) as a bonus feat. The raven understands Common but
cannot speak. It can answer questions related to its abilities or what it has seen by cawing once
for "yes" and twice for "no."

The raven can maintain its non-figurine status for only 24 hours per week. This duration need not be
continuous, but it must be used in 1-hour increments. After three transformations into its giant
raven form (though not its smaller version), the statuette loses all magical properties.; **CL**
11th; Craft Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Bronze Griffon:** When animated, a bronze
griffon^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ acts in all ways like
a normal [griffon](ref/monster/griffon) under the command of its possessor. The item can be used
twice per week for up to 6 hours per use. When 6 hours have passed or when the command word is
spoken, the bronze griffon once again becomes a tiny statuette.; **CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Coral Hippocampus:** When
animated,^[4](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-4)^ this figurine of
wondrous power acts in all ways like a normal [combat-trained](ref/skill/handle-animal)
[hippocampus](ref/eq-misc/hippocampus-common). The item can be used three times per week for up to
12 hours per use. When 12 hours have passed or when the command word is spoken, the coral
hippocampus again becomes a tiny statuette.; **CL** 19th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Ebony Fly:** When animated, an ebony
fly^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ is the size of a pony and
has all the statistics of a [pegasus](ref/monster/pegasus) but can make no attacks. The item can be
used three times per week for up to 12 hours per use. When 12 hours have passed or when the command
word is spoken, the ebony fly again becomes a tiny statuette.; **CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Emerald Frog:** This figurine of wondrous
power^[5](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-5)^ appears as a small,
innocuous frog carved of cloudy emerald. On command, it transforms into either a
[giant frog](ref/monster/frog-giant-frog) with the [advanced](ref/template/advanced) template or 3
[poisonous frogs](ref/monster/frog-poison-frog) - the user makes this choice at the time of
activation. The frog (or frogs) can be animated up to 3 times per week, for a maximum total duration
of 12 hours per week. If the advanced giant frog is slain, the emerald frog cannot be activated
again for 1 full week. Each poisonous frog that is slain in combat reduces the total duration that
the figurine can be activated for the week by 4 hours.

Either the giant frog or the poisonous frogs can be used as combatants or as small, unintelligent
servant-creatures, performing minor tasks or fetching small objects. The giant frog can be used by
Small or smaller creatures as a makeshift mount, although it is not equipped with a saddle or
similar equipment, and its slick hide imposes an additional -4 penalty on [Ride](ref/skill/ride)
attempts.; **CL** 8th; Craft Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Glass Walrus:** This figurine of wondrous power is shaped like a
walrus^[6](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-6)^ and is carved out of a
prismatic crystal. It follows all the normal rules for figurines of wondrous power. When animated, a
glass walrus acts in all ways like a normal walrus except it can glide along the surface of water as
though under the constant effects of _[water walk](ref/spell/water-walk)._ Up to two Medium
creatures or four Small creatures can fit on a glass walrus's back as the creature glides across
water (the walrus moves at half speed while carrying creatures), and the walrus can carry up to 600
pounds in this way before it becomes too encumbered to move. A glass walrus can be used twice per
week for up to 6 hours per use. When 6 hours have passed or when the command word is spoken, the
glass walrus again becomes a tiny statuette.; **CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects),_ [water walk](ref/spell/water-walk).

**Golden Lions:** These figurines come in
pairs.^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ They become normal
adult male [lions](ref/monster/lion). If slain in combat, the lions cannot be brought back from
statuette form for 1 full week. Otherwise, they can be used once per day for up to 1 hour. They
enlarge and shrink upon speaking the command word.; **CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Hematite Cougar:** This palm-sized figurine of a cat with a spiked
tail^[7](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-7)^ is made of gleaming
hematite. A hematite cougar turns into a [digmaul](ref/monster/digmaul) that is fully obedient to
the figurine's owner. Even in its animated form, the hematite cougar is made of dense metal,
granting it a +2 armor bonus to AC and a +2 bonus to its CMD. An animated hematite cougar conducts
low levels of electricity; creatures adjacent to the hematite cougar gain electricity resistance 2,
but any damage prevented by this ability is dealt to the hematite cougar instead. A hematite cougar
can be animated twice per week for up to 8 hours per use; speaking the command word before the 8
hours expires returns the hematite cougar to a figurine.; **CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects),_ [resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy).

**Ivory Goats:** These figurines come in
threes.^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ Each goat of this
trio looks slightly different from the others, and each has a different function:

- **The Goat of Traveling:** This statuette provides a speedy and enduring mount equal to that of a
  [heavy horse](ref/monster/horse) in every way except appearance. The goat can travel for a maximum
  of 1 day each week - continuously or in any combination of periods totaling 24 hours. At this
  point, or when the command word is uttered, it returns to its statuette form for no less than 1
  day before it can again be used.
- **The Goat of Travail:** This statuette becomes an enormous creature, larger than a bull, with the
  statistics of a [nightmare](ref/monster/nightmare) except for the addition of a pair of wicked
  horns of exceptional size (damage 1d8+4 for each horn). If it is [charging](ref/rule/charge) to
  attack, it may only use its horns (but add 6 points of damage to each successful attack in that
  round). It can be called to life just once per month for up to 12 hours at a time.
- **The Goat of Terror:** When called upon with the proper command word, this statuette becomes a
  destrier-like mount with the statistics of a [light horse](ref/monster/horse). However, its rider
  can employ the goat's horns as weapons (one horn as a +3 [heavy lance](ref/eq-weapon/lance), the
  other as a +5 [longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword)). When ridden in an attack against an opponent,
  the goat of terror radiates [fear](ref/spell/fear) as the spell in a 30-foot radius (Will DC 16
  partial). It can be used once every 2 weeks for up to 3 hours per use.

**CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Lapis Camel:** This palm-sized camel figurine is sculpted of lapis
lazuli.^[8](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-8)^ When you throw the
lapis camel to the ground and speak the command word, it turns into a dromedary camel. The camel
acts as a normal [camel](ref/eq-misc/camel) in all respects, except that it obeys your verbal
commands to the best of its animal-level intelligence. At your command, the camel returns to its
figurine form. The lapis camel can be used up to 3 times per week for up 8 hours each use.; **CL**
11th; Craft Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Marble Elephant:** This is the largest of the
figurines,^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ the statuette
being about the size of a human hand. Upon utterance of the command word, a marble elephant grows to
the size and specifications of a true [elephant](ref/monster/elephant). The animal created from the
statuette is fully obedient to the figurine's owner, serving as a beast of burden, a mount, or a
combatant. The statuette can be used four times per month for up to 24 hours at a time.; **CL**
11th; Craft Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Obsidian Steed:** This figurine appears to be a small, shapeless lump of black
stone.^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ Only careful
inspection reveals that it vaguely resembles some form of quadruped. On command, the near-formless
piece of obsidian becomes a fantastic mount. Treat it as a [heavy horse](ref/monster/horse) with the
following additional powers usable once per round at will:
[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight), _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift),_ and
[ethereal jaunt](ref/spell/ethereal-jaunt). The steed allows itself to be ridden, but if the rider
is of good alignment, the steed is 10% likely per use to carry him to the lower planes and then
return to its statuette form. The statuette can be used once per week for one continuous period of
up to 24 hours. Note that when an obsidian steed becomes ethereal or _plane shifts,_ its rider and
his gear follow suit. Thus, the user can travel to other planes via this means.; **CL** 15th; Craft
Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects),_
[etherealness](ref/spell/etherealness), [fly](ref/spell/fly), [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift).

**Onyx Dog:** When commanded, this
statuette^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ changes into a
creature with the same properties as a [riding dog](ref/monster/dog-riding-dog) except that it is
endowed with an Intelligence of 8, can communicate in Common, and has exceptional olfactory and
visual abilities. It has the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability and adds +4 on its
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks. It has 60-foot [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), and it
can [see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility). An onyx dog can be used once per week for up to
6 hours. It obeys only its owner.; **CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Pearl Dolphin:** This 2-inch-long pearl
figurine^[9](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-9)^ turns into a
[dolphin](ref/eq-misc/dolphin) on command (but it retains its composition, which gives it
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10). The dolphin follows the verbal commands of the creature who spoke
the command word and will follow commands as a summoned creature. With a second command word, the
dolphin will swim to an indicated location at double its swim speed, bearing a message just like a
creature affected by an _[animal messenger](ref/spell/animal-messenger)_ spell.

The pearl dolphin can maintain its non-figurine status for only 24 hours per week. The duration need
not be continuous, but it must be used in 1-hour increments.; **CL** 11th;
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), _animal messenger, animate objects_.

**Ruby Butterfly:** Upon command, this figurine of wondrous
power^[10](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-a)^ transforms into a
hand-sized red butterfly (same statistics as a [bat](ref/monster/familiar-bat)). A spellcaster can
use a ruby butterfly to deliver touch spells, as if the ruby butterfly were a
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) capable of this ability. A ruby butterfly can also be commanded to
guard its owner while he sleeps. Creatures protected this way gain a +5 luck bonus on saving throws
against any spell or effect that affects sleeping creatures' dreams (such as
[nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare) or a night hag's dream haunting ability). A ruby butterfly can be
used three times per week for up to 8 hours at a time.; **CL** 9th; Craft Wondrous Item,
_[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects),_ [dream](ref/spell/dream).

**Serpentine Owl:** This
figurine^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ becomes either a
normal-sized horned [owl](ref/monster/familiar-owl) or a [giant owl](ref/monster/owl-giant-owl) (use
the stats for the giant eagle) according to the command word used. The transformation can take place
once per day, with a maximum duration of 8 continuous hours. However, after three transformations
into giant owl form, the statuette loses all its magical properties. The owl communicates with its
owner by telepathic means, informing her of all it sees and hears.; **CL** 11th;
[Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)_.

**Silver Raven:** This silver
figurine^[3](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-3)^ turns into a
[raven](ref/monster/familiar-raven) on command (but it retains its metallic consistency, which gives
it [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10). Another command sends it off into the air, bearing a message
just like a creature affected by an _[animal messenger](ref/spell/animal-messenger)_ spell. If not
commanded to carry a message, the raven obeys the commands of its owner, although it has no special
powers or telepathic abilities. It can maintain its non-figurine status for only 24 hours per week,
but the duration need not be continuous.; **CL** 6th; Craft Wondrous Item, _animal messenger,
animate objects._

> **Obsidian Raven:** This figurine of wondrous
> power^[12](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-c)^ is similar to a

**Slate Spider:** This statuette of a spider with stubby
legs^[13](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-d)^ can be used once per
day for 1 minute. When activated, the figurine sprouts longer, segmented legs, and scampers about
the bearer, picking lint off robes, chiseling grime from armor, or otherwise grooming the bearer. If
commanded to do so (a free action), it perches on the muzzle of a one-handed or two-handed firearm
barrel and, after the firearm is fired, travels down the barrel and cleans out the firearm. Each
time the slate spider cleans a firearm, the next shot the firearm fires has no chance of misfiring.;
**CL** 11th; Craft Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)._

**Tin Imp:** These fat, smirking imp
figurines^[14](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power-tin-imp#footnote-e)^ are made of solid
tin. A tin imp turns into an [imp](ref/monster/devil-imp) on command, though it retains its metallic
consistency, which gives it a +4 armor bonus to AC. In imp form, a tin imp can turn invisible at
will as a swift action, though it retains none of an imp's other
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) and lacks a poisoned sting. Tin imps generally
avoid combat, but make excellent spies and trackers.

The tin imp's owner can, as a move action, designate a single creature in his line of sight as the
tin imp's quarry. The tin imp then follows its quarry clandestinely, and sends a whispered message
to its owner once each hour detailing the quarry's location and activities. The message is similar
to the _[whispering wind](ref/spell/whispering-wind)_ spell, though the message unerringly locates
the tin imp's owner instead of moving to a predetermined location. A tin imp can maintain its
animate status for up to 48 hours per week. This duration doesn't need to be continuous, but must be
used in 1-hour increments. If slain in combat, a tin imp is destroyed.

Additionally, a creature with the track class feature can command a tin imp he owns to study a set
of footprints (a standard action) and designate the creature that made the footprints as its quarry.
The tin imp can then attempt to track the creature as if its owner was tracking it, using the
owner's Perception and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) skill (and any tracking bonuses).; **CL**
11th; Craft Wondrous Item, _[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects)_,
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [whispering wind](ref/spell/whispering-wind).
