**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** -

This tiny, unremarkable silver key can open nearly any set of shackles. When the key is inserted as
a standard action into the keyhole of locked leg irons, manacles, or shackles, the lock unlocks
provided that its [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) DC is 30 or lower. Shackles whose lock
has a Disable Device DC of 15 or lower are instantly and silently broken into fragments.

A liberator's key can be used 10 times, after which it crumbles to dust.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [knock](ref/spell/knock),
[shatter](ref/spell/shatter); **Cost** 1,000 gp
