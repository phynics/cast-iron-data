**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 159, Agents of Evil pg 29\
**Aura** strong evocation and necromancy; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 50,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These stylized red gloves are often employed by agents of the [Red Mantis](ref/rule/aff-assassins)
for stealthy assassinations. If the wearer successfully [pins](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) an
opponent with a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver, she can perform a special
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) attack the next round with the gloves, treating them as if they
were [sawtooth sabres](ref/eq-weapon/sawtooth-sabre) (1d8 points of slashing damage, critical threat
range of 19-20) for the purpose of this attack. Should the wearer of these gloves slay her opponent
with this attack, the target's head erupts in a geyser of blood, preventing life-restoring spells
that require an intact body. A creature that survives this _coup de grace_ attack is immune to
subsequent attempts from any mantis embrace for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[explode head](ref/spell/explode-head), [suffocation](ref/spell/suffocation); **Cost** 25,000 gp
