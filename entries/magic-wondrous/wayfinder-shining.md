**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 148, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 55\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A shining wayfinder appears as a polished silver compass. In addition to the usual functions of a
[wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder) (including the ability to create light) a shining
wayfinder can be used to cast _[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil)_ - when so used, its needle
points not to the north but rather to the nearest source of evil within 60 feet. Once per day, a
shining wayfinder can be used to cast _[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil)_ upon
the wayfinder's bearer. A shining wayfinder functions only for a creature that is good-aligned. When
held by an evil creature, the shining wayfinder's needle spins in a frantic circle. When held by any
creature that is both nonevil and nongood, the needle does not point to anything in particular (not
even to the north).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil), [light](ref/spell/light),
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil); **Cost** 1,000 gp
