**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 151\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 80,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

If a [blind](ref/misc/blinded) creature holds this orb, that creature can see with
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 120 feet, but also gains
[light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity). If a sighted creature holds this orb and holds his
eyes shut for 1 minute, he gains the blinded condition until he opens his eyes (as a free action),
but can see through the orb as if he were a blind creature.

By expending one use of mythic power, a creature using the eye orb can see as though using
_[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing),_ and loses light sensitivity. These effects last for 1 minute
or until he stops using the orb to see, whichever comes first.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 40,000 gp
