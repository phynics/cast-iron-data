**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 301, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 516\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This beautiful and intricately carved harp can be held comfortably in one hand, but both hands are
required to utilize its magic. When played, a harp of charming enables the performer to work one
_[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)_ (as the spell, Will DC 14 negates) into the music for each 10
minutes of playing if she can succeed on a DC 14 [Perform (string)](ref/skill/perform-any) check. If
the check fails, the audience cannot be affected by any further performances from the harpist for 24
hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion); **Cost** 3,750 gp
