**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 220\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 48,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This short cape shimmers faintly under light. The wearer is immune to the
[energy drain](ref/umr/energy-drain) attacks made by undead creatures. She still suffers any other
effects of the attack, but ignores the energy drain. If a corpse is wearing the mantle, it can't
rise as an undead creature for any reason. This doesn't prevent creatures that were already undead
from using abilities like rejuvenation to return to unlife.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[death ward](ref/spell/death-ward); **Cost** 24,000 gp
