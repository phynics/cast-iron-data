**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 6,250 gp; **Weight** -

These apparently normal lightweight gloves are obviously made for use outdoors. They grant a +5
competence bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks and [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks. Both gloves
must be worn for the magic to be effective.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace); **Cost** 3,125 gp
