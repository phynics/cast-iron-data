**Aura** moderate evocation and transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 8,500 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Prized by evil warriors, these boots are made of inky black leather trimmed with a crimson mane of
fiendish horsehair. Some bear the hair of actual [nightmares](ref/monster/nightmare) along their
rims, causing them to occasionally flare and crackle with flames reminiscent of the foul equines.
When the wearer [runs](ref/rule/run) or [charges](ref/rule/charge), smoke issues up from the boots,
acting as [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance) when the wearer provokes attacks of
opportunity while making those actions. Secondly, three times per day, as a swift action, the wearer
can make the boots smoke, which grants her concealment until the end of her turn. Lastly, once per
day, when the wearer hits a creature with a melee attack, she can command the boots to ignite. When
the boots ignite, all creatures within a 5-foot radius take 2d6 points of fire damage. A DC 13
Reflex saving throw halves the damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands), [pyrotechnics](ref/spell/pyrotechnics); **Cost** 3,750 gp
