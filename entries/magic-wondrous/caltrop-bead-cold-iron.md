## Caltrop Bead

**Sources:** Demon Hunter's Handbook pg 28\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 40 gp (Iron), 80 gp (Cold Iron); **Weight** 2 lbs.

The wielder may throw this small, dense iron sphere as a ranged touch attack (with a 10-foot range
increment) or use it as a sling bullet. Upon striking a solid object, a caltrop bead explodes into
[caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops) in a 10-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst). A creature directly
hit by a caltrop bead takes 2d4 points of piercing damage, and adjacent creatures take 1d4 points of
damage as the bead suddenly bursts. Caltrops created by a caltrop bead remain for 1 hour before
disappearing.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[minor creation](ref/spell/minor-creation); **Cost** 20 gp (iron), 40 gp (cold iron)
