**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 14\
**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** -

Twice per day, when thrown into the air as a standard action, this palm-sized, tufted, purple seed
drifts at a rate of 60 feet per round toward a spot clearly visualized by the thrower. It can be
grabbed out of the air with a successful combat maneuver check against a CMD of 20, which ends its
flight. The seed flies for up to 12 miles (taking 2 hours) before it settles to the ground. A
[gathlain](ref/race/gathlain) thrower can choose to speak a message of up to 25 words when throwing
the seed; in this case, the seed repeats the message in the thrower's voice when its flight ends.

If the thrower speaks a command word as part of the action to throw it, the herald seed flies back
to where it was last thrown, even if the thrower can't visualize the location.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature), [message](ref/spell/message); **Cost** 4,000
gp
