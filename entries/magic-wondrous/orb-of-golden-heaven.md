**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 22,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This alabaster sphere, overlaid with golden bands set with diamonds in rune-like patterns, sheds
light like a [continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame) spell. Making a melee touch attack with
the orb has the same effect as dousing the target with a flask of
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water). A good creature may hold the orb in hand and command it to
invoke _[consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate)_ and _[searing light](ref/spell/searing-light)_ each once
per day.

The orb bestows one permanent [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on any
evil creature holding or carrying it. The negative level remains as long as the creature has the orb
and disappears immediately when the orb is no longer held or carried. This negative level cannot be
overcome in any way (including by [restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while the bearer has
the orb. If the bearer of an orb of golden heaven wears a
[crown of golden heaven](ref/magic-wondrous/crown-of-heaven), its light counts as
_[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ instead of _[continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame),_ and its
_[consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate)_ acts as if the orb were a shrine to the bearer's deity.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bless water](ref/spell/bless-water), [consecrate](ref/spell/consecrate),
[daylight](ref/spell/daylight), [searing light](ref/spell/searing-light); **Cost** 11,000 gp
