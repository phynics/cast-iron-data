**Sources:** Monster Hunter's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This whetstone is made from finely worked [obsidian](ref/eq-material/obsidian). Sharpening a blade
with this whetstone requires 15 minutes of work and grants the weapon the ability to ignore up to 3
points of natural armor for its next five attacks. If the target of the weapon's attack has a
natural armor bonus of less than +3, the weapon deals 1 additional point of damage for each point of
natural armor below 3 the target has. The process of honing the blade shatters a portion of the
whetstone's enchanted obsidian, dispelling its magic and turning it into a mundane whetstone
afterward.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[keen edge](ref/spell/keen-edge); **Cost** 1,000 gp
