**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 6,400 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This dark burgundy cloak shifts its hue and pattern to blend in against its surroundings, granting
the wearer a +5 competence bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. The cloak holds a concealed
scabbard in which a light blade (as defined in the
[fighter weapon group](ref/ability/weapon-groups)) can be hidden. The wearer can draw the blade as
if from a normal scabbard, but anyone searching the wearer for the item must succeed at a DC 20
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) check, as if he were looking for a secret door.

If a [swashbuckler](ref/class/swashbuckler) is wearing the cloak, she can spend 1 panache point to
_[vanish](ref/spell/vanish),_ as the spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item), [vanish](ref/spell/vanish); **Cost** 3,200 gp
