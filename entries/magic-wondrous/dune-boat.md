**Sources:** Pathfinder \#83: The Slave Trenches of Hakotep pg 62\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

Fashioned from bound reeds in traditional Osirian style, a dune boat is a wondrous variant of the
more common [folding boat](ref/magic-wondrous/folding-boat). Like that item, a dune boat appears as
a wooden box about 12 inches long, 6 inches wide, and 6 inches deep when inactive, save that each
surface is emblazoned with Ancient Osiriani hieroglyphs. A dune boat functions identically to a
folding boat, save that it can move across sandy terrain as if it were moving across water. In boat
form, it can hold 6 people comfortably, while the ship form carries 20 with ease.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate), [shifting sand](ref/spell/shifting-sand), creator must have 2
ranks in [Craft (ships)](ref/skill/craft-any); **Cost** 4,500 gp
