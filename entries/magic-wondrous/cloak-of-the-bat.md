**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 264, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 506\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 26,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Fashioned of dark brown or black cloth, this cloak bestows a +5 competence bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. The wearer is also able to hang upside down from the ceiling
like a bat.

By holding the edges of the garment, the wearer is able to _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_ as per the spell
(including a +7 bonus on [Fly](ref/skill/fly) skill checks). If he desires, the wearer can actually
polymorph himself into an ordinary bat and fly accordingly (as
_[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)_). All possessions worn or carried are part of the
transformation. Flying, either with the cloak or in bat form, can be accomplished only in darkness
(either under the night sky or in a lightless or near-lightless environment underground). Either of
the flying powers is usable for up to 7 minutes at a time, but after a flight of any duration the
cloak cannot bestow any flying power for a like period of time.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3), [fly](ref/spell/fly); **Cost** 13,000 gp
