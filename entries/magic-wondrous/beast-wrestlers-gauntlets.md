**Sources:** Advanced Class Origins pg 28\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 7,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These gauntlets grant a +1 enhancement bonus on [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) checks. Each pair of
gauntlets is created to fight a specific type or subtype of creature from the list of creature types
in the [bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) weapon special ability. While the wearer is grappling a creature
of the designated type, the gauntlets' enhancement bonus increases by 2 and the wearer deals an
additional 2d6 points of nonlethal damage when dealing damage as part of a successful grapple check.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Cost** 3,750 gp
