**Sources:** Monster Summoner's Handbook pg 21\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and evocation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 22,900 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Crafted from gold and lacquer, these elaborate masks resemble various outsiders. Once per day when
the wearer casts a _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell, he may choose to house the
summoned outsider within the visage of the bound rather than conjuring it onto the Material Plane.
For the spell's duration, the wearer can use of any of the bound outsider's
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as if they were his own. A visage of the bound
has no effect on outsiders already summoned or on summoning spells cast by other creatures. Removing
the mask immediately releases a bound outsider.

The wearer cannot use the bound outsider's [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) to
summon other creatures, and a [summoner](ref/class/summoner) cannot activate a visage of the bound
while his eidolon is summoned. Effects that extend the duration of a
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spell (such a the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell)
metamagic feat or the [conjurer's](ref/arcaneschool/conjuration) summoner's charm class ability)
have no effect on the duration of a spell contained by a visage of the bound. Outsiders who are
opposed to one or more axes of the wearer's alignment cannot be housed within a visage of the bound.
