**Sources:** Antihero's Handbook pg 30\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 26,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This crystalline cube has a thin plate of soft white stone on one of its faces that's inscribed with
several lines of cryptic runes. When the cube's bearer speaks a command word, the runes change shape
and spell out information regarding nearby secrets such as back alleys and hidden paths.

Once per day, the wielder of a crook's cube can cast _[find the path](ref/spell/find-the-path)._ The
cube also grants its wielder a +5 insight bonus on checks with the following skills in which the
wielder has at least 5 ranks: [Knowledge (dungeoneering)](ref/skill/knowledge-dungeoneering),
[Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering),
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography),
[Knowledge (history)](ref/skill/knowledge-history), and
[Knowledge (local)](ref/skill/knowledge-local).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item);
[find the path](ref/spell/find-the-path); at least 5 ranks in Knowledge (dungeoneering), Knowledge
(engineering), Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (history), or Knowledge (local); **Cost** 13,000 gp
