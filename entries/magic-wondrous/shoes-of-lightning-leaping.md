**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 233\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 10,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These short black shoes fit tightly around the wearer's feet and bear designs of leaping arcs of
lightning and wild winds. Once per day on command, a creature wearing these boots can transform into
lightning and move up to 50 feet in a straight line, re-materializing at the far end of the
lightning bolt. Creatures in the path of the lightning take 6d6 points of electricity damage and
objects take damage as per the _[lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt)_ spell. If the lightning
strikes an object or barrier and fails to burst through it, the creature wearing the boots appears
next to the impacted object and is [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. Movement while in
lightning form does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat), [lightning bolt](ref/spell/lightning-bolt);
**Cost** 5,250 gp
