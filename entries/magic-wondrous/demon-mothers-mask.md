**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 3,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This primitive hyena mask is usually made of leather, but may be made of soft metal like copper or
carved from an animal's skull. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) and [Heal](ref/skill/heal) checks. The wearer can smell
when nearby creatures are in heat or otherwise especially fertile (creatures such as humans that can
breed at any time of the year always smell fertile unless they are barren).

If [Lamashtu](ref/faith/lamashtu) is the wearer's patron, the mask counts as an unholy symbol and a
hole for a third eye appears in its forehead. The wearer can use
_[summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2)_ once per day to summon a
[fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) hyena, which obeys the wearer as if they shared a common language.
The wearer may interbreed with animals within one size category of the wearer's size. The offspring
of such unions are members of the mother's race with the fiendish simple template and bear monstrous
aesthetic features of the father's race.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants), [polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph),
[summon monster II](ref/spell/summon-monster-2), creator must have 5 ranks in Handle Animal and
Heal; **Cost** 1,800 gp
