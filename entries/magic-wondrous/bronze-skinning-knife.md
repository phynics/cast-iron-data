**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 262, Gods and Magic pg 55\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,300 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This bronze skinning knife bears the symbol of Old Deadeye on its blade. Designed as a hunting tool,
it cannot be used as an effective weapon. As long as the bearer carries the knife, he can find
enough food and water in the wilderness to satisfy one creature's hunger and thirst (either the
bearer himself or someone traveling with him). In addition, the bearer gains a +1 resistance bonus
on Fortitude saving throws.

If [Erastil](ref/faith/erastil) is the bearer's patron, once per day the bearer can speak a command
word to use _[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike)._ Once per day the bearer can speak a command word
to give a touched thrown weapon, arrow, or bolt the [returning](ref/magic-enh/returning) weapon
special ability for 1 minute. A magic arrow or bolt that returns does not keep its magical
properties but is still usable as [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) ammunition.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[create food and water](ref/spell/create-food-and-water), [delay poison](ref/spell/delay-poison),
[mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand), [true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Cost** 1,650 gp
