**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 230\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This quilted silk cloak is trimmed with thick white feathers. As a move action once per day, the
wearer can whirl the cloak about himself to activate a _[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield)_
effect. It activates as a warm shield if the ambient temperature is 65 degrees Fahrenheit or colder,
or as a chill shield if the ambient temperature is 66 degrees Fahrenheit or hotter.

If the wearer is a [Triaxian](ref/race/triaxian), he gains energy resistance for as long as he wears
the cloak. If he's summerborn, he gains fire resistance 10. If he's winterborn, he gains cold
resistance 10. If he is transitional, he gains resistance 5 to both cold and fire.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fire shield](ref/spell/fire-shield), [resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), creator must be a
Triaxian; **Cost** 15,000 gp
