**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 214\
**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This red and orange silk robe gives viewers the impression of a flame roiling just below the
fabric's slick surface. The robe grants the wearer fire resistance 5 and increases the wearer's
caster level for all spells with the fire descriptor by +1. Once per day on command, the wearer of
the robe can emit a 20-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) of fire. Creatures other than the wearer
within the area take 2d6 points of fire damage (Reflex DC 16 half).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell), [resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy),
[fireball](ref/spell/fireball); **Cost** 5,500 gp
