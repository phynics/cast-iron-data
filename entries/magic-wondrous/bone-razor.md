**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This jagged bone knife seems too thin and frail for combat but easily cuts flesh. If used to perform
a [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) on a helpless living creature and the creature dies from the
attack, the creature's flesh begins to peel off and its bones animate as a
[skeleton](ref/template/skeleton) under the command of the bearer of the razor. The creature's flesh
is not destroyed, but decays at a steady rate. The bearer of the razor can spend 1 minute
reattaching the flesh to the animate skeleton, which ends the necromantic magic and results in a
normal corpse (though a corpse of a person killed or mutilated by knife wounds). If the bearer uses
the razor to flense and animate another creature's bones, the previous animate skeleton is
immediately destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead); **Cost** 2,500 gp
