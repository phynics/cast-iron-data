**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 28\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 23,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This dark purple mask is contorted and twisted, and it covers the top half of the wearer's face in
swirls of thick brocade and lace. Dappled across the mask's swaths are dozens of twinkling
amethysts. This mask grants the wearer the ability to transform into a poisonous gaseous form once
per day, as per the spell _[miasmatic form](ref/spell/miasmatic-form)._ This transformation lasts
for 5 minutes, at which point the wearer immediately turns back into her original form. If the
wearer reverts to her original form in a space that would not normally accommodate her body, she is
shunted to the nearest such open space. Activating this mask is a standard action that provokes
attacks of opportunity.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[miasmatic form](ref/spell/miasmatic-form); **Cost** 11,500 gp
