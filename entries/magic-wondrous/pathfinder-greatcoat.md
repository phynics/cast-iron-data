**Sources:** Pathfinder Society Primer pg 25\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This long, double-breasted jacket is sewn from durable, dun-colored canvas with a removable liner to
keep its wearer comfortable in a wide range of temperatures. Anyone wearing the coat notes many
small cloth patches of various shapes. Only the wearer of the robe can see these patches, recognize
them for the items they become, and detach them. One patch can be detached each round. Detaching a
patch causes it to become an actual item, as indicated below. A Pathfinder greatcoat has each of the
following patches (unless noted otherwise, the coat has only one of each patch):

- 10 days of [trail rations](ref/eq-misc/trail-rations) and five full
  [waterskins](ref/eq-misc/waterskin)
- A case containing five [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine)
  [bolts](ref/eq-weapon/crossbow-bolts), five [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) bolts, and two
  silver bolts
- A large [scroll case](ref/eq-misc/scroll-case) containing a 2-foot-by-10-foot
  [roll of paper](ref/eq-misc/paper-sheet) and two sticks of [charcoal](ref/eq-misc/charcoal)
- A quiver containing five adamantine [arrows](ref/eq-weapon/arrow), five cold iron arrows, and two
  silver arrows
- A 10-square-yard roll of [canvas](ref/eq-misc/canvas-sq-yd) containing 10
  [pitons](ref/eq-misc/piton) and a small [hammer](ref/eq-misc/hammer)
- A silver [Aspis Consortium](ref/rule/aff-profiteers) badge and three bronze Aspis Consortium
  badges
- [Bullseye lanterns](ref/eq-misc/bullseye-lantern) (2; full and lit)
- [Daggers](ref/eq-weapon/dagger) (2)
- [Disguise kit](ref/eq-misc/disguise-kit)
- [Healer's kit](ref/eq-misc/healers-kit)
- [Hemp ropes](ref/eq-misc/rope-50-ft) (2; 50-foot coil)
- [Light combat-trained horse](ref/eq-misc/light-horse-combat-trained) with a
  [military saddle](ref/eq-misc/military-saddle)
- [Mirrors](ref/eq-misc/mirror) (2; highly polished 2-foot-by-4-foot steel mirrors)
- [Noble's outfit](ref/eq-misc/nobles-outfit) with jewelry worth 200 gold pieces
- Oil of [bless weapon](ref/spell/bless-weapon)
- Oil of [daylight](ref/spell/daylight)
- [Poles](ref/eq-misc/pole) (2; 10-foot length)
- Potion of [cure moderate wounds](ref/spell/cure-moderate-wounds)
- [Sacks](ref/eq-misc/sack) (2)
- [Universal solvent](ref/magic-wondrous/universal-solvent)
- Wand of [prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation) (CL 1st, 5 charges)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate); **Cost** 4,000 gp
