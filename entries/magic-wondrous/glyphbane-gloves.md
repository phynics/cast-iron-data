**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 238\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** -

These dark-brown, leather gloves are etched over their entire surface with arcane insignia. The
wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks made to
disable magical traps. Once per day, if the wearer's check to disarm a glyph, rune, or similar
writing-based magical trap exceeds the DC by 10 or more, she may change the trigger condition of the
trap instead of disabling it. The new trigger condition must be one allowed by the magical trap; for
example, a [glyph of warding](ref/spell/glyph-of-warding) cannot be set according to class, HD, or
level, even when manipulated by the gloves.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[find traps](ref/spell/find-traps), [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic); **Cost** 4,500 gp
