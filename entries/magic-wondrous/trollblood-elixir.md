**Sources:** Giant Hunter's Handbook pg 29\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,550 gp; **Weight** -

This rancid, bubbling tonic is mud-colored and reeks of sweat, gore, and decay. Upon drinking a dose
of trollblood elixir, the imbiber gains [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 5. This fast healing is
even capable of reattaching a severed limb that is pressed against its stump for 1 round, as long as
the limb has been severed for no more than 1 hour and is essentially intact. The effects of
trollblood elixir last 1 minute; during this time, the imbiber is [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated).
Each round, the imbiber can choose to prematurely end the elixir's effects by succeeding at a DC 10
Fortitude save as a full-round action. Trollblood elixir does not affect objects or nonliving
creatures such as constructs and undead.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[regenerate](ref/spell/regenerate); **Cost** 5,060 gp
