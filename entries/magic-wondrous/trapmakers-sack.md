**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 137\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This heavy leather bag bulges only slightly but upon closer inspection appears to be chocked full of
knives, gears, picks, shovels, and arrows. Once per day as a full-round action, the bearer can
command the sack to create any mechanical [trap](ref/rule/sample-traps) of CR 4 or less, which
appears at a designated spot within 50 feet. The bearer must make a
[Craft (traps)](ref/skill/craft-any) check with a DC equal to the normal DC for creating the trap.
If successful, the trap instantly merges with the chosen area, exactly as if someone had actually
built the trap in that spot. If the check fails, no trap appears. The sack can alter earth, stone,
and wood to accommodate the trap without altering nearby terrain (including doors, pits, and so on).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fabricate](ref/spell/fabricate), creator must have 5 ranks in Craft (traps); **Cost** 10,000 gp
