**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 4,200 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This impossibly hard, lumpy piece of unforged iron originates from metal that remained cool after
smelting - ore that resisted all attempts to forge it into another shape. Worshipers of
[Gorum](ref/faith/gorum) believe this metal is a reflection of their god's unrelenting
determination, and often fashion this "stubborn iron" into a pendant, though it is sometimes used as
a mace head or as a counterweight to a [greatsword](ref/eq-weapon/greatsword) or another large
weapon instead. While wearing a Shad-Gorum nugget around the neck, the wearer gains fire resistance
1 and a +1 resistance bonus on Will saving throws.

If Gorum is the wearer's patron, the wearer can use _[chill metal](ref/spell/chill-metal)_ once per
day. At will, the wearer can mend small metallic items as if using a _[mending](ref/spell/mending)_
spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[chill metal](ref/spell/chill-metal), [mending](ref/spell/mending),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance); **Cost** 2,100 gp
