**Sources:** Construct Handbook pg 16\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 9,800 gp; **Weight** -

This 1-inch steel cube bears a variety of unique, intricate carvings upon each face. On command, the
cube sprouts wings and arms and becomes a Diminutive-sized, cube-shaped mechanical construct (treat
as a [thrush](ref/monster/familiar-thrush) with [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10). The creature obeys
and serves whoever activated the cube. It understands Common but cannot speak. The chirurgeon cube
is skilled at healing constructs. As a standard action, the cube can use the magnets and small hooks
embedded on its underside to attach itself to a construct and quickly stitch together any torn and
broken pieces. This provides the construct with [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2 for as long
as the chirurgeon cube remains in contact with it. A chirurgeon cube can be activated for up to 10
minutes each day, spent in 1-minute increments. If the chirurgeon cube is broken or destroyed in
either form, it is forever ruined.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [fly](ref/spell/fly),
[make whole](ref/spell/make-whole); **Cost** 4,900 gp
