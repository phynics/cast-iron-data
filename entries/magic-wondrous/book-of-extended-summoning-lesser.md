## Book of Extended Summoning

**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 750 gp (Lesser), 2,750 gp (Normal), 6,126 gp (Greater); **Weight** 1 lb.

This leather-bound book is inscribed with runes relating to outsiders and conjuration magic. It
enhances spells that summon monsters. The user may apply the [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell)
feat to these spells as they are cast. This does not change the spell slot of the altered spell. The
book counts as a [metamagic rod](ref/rule/rods) for the purpose of combining multiple metamagic
effects on a spell. A normal book of extended summoning can be used with spells of 6th level or
lower, while a lesser book can be used with spells of 3rd level or lower, and a greater book can be
used with spells of 9th level or lower. Each book is keyed to a particular alignment (equal chances
of chaotic, evil, good, or lawful) and only works when used to summon a creature with that
alignment. Once the book is used, it crumbles into cold ash and is destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), Extend Spell,
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1); **Cost** 375 gp (Lesser), 1,375 gp (Normal), 3,063
gp (Greater)
