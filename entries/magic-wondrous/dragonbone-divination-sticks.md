**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 292\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,400 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These eight rune-engraved dragon bones may be used as a divine focus for
_[augury](ref/spell/augury)._ They add 5% to the chance to successfully cast
_[augury](ref/spell/augury)_ and [divination](ref/spell/divination). The bearer of the set gains a
+3 luck bonus on one type of saving throw (Fortitude, Reflex, or Will), determined randomly each
day. If a creature carries multiple sets of dragonbone divination sticks, all of them provide a
bonus on the same saving throw.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [augury](ref/spell/augury),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance); **Cost** 3,200 gp
