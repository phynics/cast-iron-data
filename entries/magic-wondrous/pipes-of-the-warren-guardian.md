**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 155\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** -

These finely crafted yellow-ivory pipes are bound in bronze and decorated with bits of amber. If the
possessor learns the proper tune, once per day he can use these pipes to summon 1d4+1
[dire rats](ref/monster/rat-dire-rat) as if using
_[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3)._ The summoned dire rats also have the swarming
racial trait, and count as [ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk) for the purposes of the ratfolk's swarming
racial trait.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3); **Cost** 3,000 gp
