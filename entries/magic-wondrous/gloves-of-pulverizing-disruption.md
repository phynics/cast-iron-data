**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 26\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 9,400 gp; **Weight** -

Three times per day, the wearer can use these thick leather gloves to make a melee touch attack
against an undead creature, a construct, or a nonmagical object and deal 3d4 points of damage to it.
When the wearer deals damage in this way, the gloves absorb part of the matter that makes up the
target. This creates dust that is stored within the gloves for 24 hours. The next time the wearer
attempts a [dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver, the dust flies from the gloves,
providing a +2 bonus on that attempt. If the wearer does not attempt a dirty trick combat maneuver
before 24 hours elapses, the dust disappears.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[corrosive touch](ref/spell/corrosive-touch); **Cost** 4,700 gp
