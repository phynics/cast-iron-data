**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 750 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

Drinking this elixir grants the imbiber a +10 bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks made to
convince another that her words are the truth. This bonus lasts for 10 minutes, and doesn't apply to
any other use of the Bluff skill, such as feinting in combat, creating a diversion to hide, or
communicating a secret message via innuendo.

If a magical effect is used against the honeytongue elixir's imbiber that would detect her lies or
force her to speak the truth, the user of the effect must succeed at a DC 16 caster level check to
compel the elixir's imbiber to tell the truth. Failure means the effect doesn't detect the imbiber's
lies or force her to tell the truth.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[glibness](ref/spell/glibness); **Cost** 375 gp
