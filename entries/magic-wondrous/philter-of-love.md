**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,000 gp; **Weight** -

This potent preparation causes a creature who drinks it to fall madly in love with the first
creature he or she perceives after consuming it. The drinker's [attitude](ref/skill/diplomacy)
toward that creature becomes helpful. If a romantic attraction is possible toward the first person
viewed, the drinker falls in love with that person. Otherwise, the drinker's love is a platonic
adoration. The effects of the philter are permanent unless removed by a
[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment), [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic),
[limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [miracle](ref/spell/miracle),
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), or [wish](ref/spell/wish).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster), [permanency](ref/spell/permanency); **Cost** 1,500 gp
