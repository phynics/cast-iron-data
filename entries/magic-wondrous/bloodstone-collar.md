**Sources:** Blood of the Coven pg 30\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 900 gp; **Weight** -

This leather collar is studded with red and black heliotropes and fastened with an iron clasp. It
functions only when worn by a spellcaster's familiar or spirit animal. If a
[familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) wearing a bloodstone collar is reduced to fewer than 0 hit points,
the familiar immediately stabilizes at -1 hit points, and any remaining damage is dealt to the
familiar's master. The master can refuse this damage, but the feedback of doing so immediately slays
the familiar. Each time a bloodstone collar activates, one of its heliotrope studs cracks; once a
third stud cracks, the collar becomes an ordinary nonmagical collar. A bloodstone collar is not
triggered by death effects or other damaging effects that do not deal hit point damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[life pact](ref/spell/life-pact); **Cost** 450 gp
