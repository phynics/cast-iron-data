**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 258\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This necklace is set with a bloodstone sculpted to look like a scintillating green eye. It gives the
wearer a +4 competence bonus on Charisma checks and Charisma-based checks made to interact with
[gnomes](ref/race/gnome), a +2 competence bonus on similar checks when dealing with beings of the
fey creature type, and a -2 penalty on similar checks when interacting with anyone else. The wearer
can read, speak, and understand the Gnome language. If the wearer is not a gnome, she gains
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), and may cast
[dancing lights](ref/spell/dancing-lights), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation), and
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals) each once per day as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be a gnome;
**Cost** 3,000 gp
