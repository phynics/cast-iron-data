**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 257\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,800 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This protective ward is fashioned from a thin, supple piece of wood bound in a circle, and then
woven with sinew to create a web-like pattern. Dreamcatchers are often decorated with beads and
feathers. More elaborate dreamcatchers might be made of gold or silver instead, and decorated with
dangling jewels and metal figurines.

A dreamcatcher protects its sleeping owner from malicious influences. The owner gains a +2 sacred
bonus on saving throws while asleep. Further, any
[ability damage or ability drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) he takes during this time is
reduced by 1 point (to a minimum of 0 points).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance); **Cost** 1,400 gp
