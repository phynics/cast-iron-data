**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** -

Written and signed in blood, a pact parchment ensures the utter and undeniable fulfillment of the
actions stated within. Upon a pact parchment, you can inscribe a contract, defining a certain
service or course of action by one or more creatures. If all listed creatures willingly (without
magical influence, threats, and so on) sign this pact in blood, they are bound to fulfill this
agreement. Henceforth everyone breaking the pact suffers a -4 penalty on attack rolls, saves,
ability checks, and skill checks, until they either satisfy the pact's conditions (if still
possible) or the parchment is destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[geas/quest](ref/spell/geas-quest); **Cost** 1,750 gp
