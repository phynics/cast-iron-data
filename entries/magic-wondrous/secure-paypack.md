**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This rugged backpack is identical to a [handy haversack](ref/magic-wondrous/handy-haversack) in most
regards, with numerous compartments for storing items. Any effort to identify it with a
[Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) check result ranging from 24 to 33 by someone unfamiliar with its
special properties reveals it to be a handy haversack. A result below 24 does not identify any magic
properties, while a result of 34 or higher reveals its additional secret properties. It has two
hidden compartments ([Perception](ref/skill/perception) DC 35), each able to store up to 1 cubic
foot of material or 10 pounds in weight. In addition, the entire item is reinforced with thin
[mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) fibers, giving it a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 15 and 5 hit
points. Each compartment has a concealed [superior lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-superior) (Perception DC
35, [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) DC 40) built in to prevent unauthorized access. When
a compartment is locked, an item can still be removed from it as a move action by a character who
has a key to the lock, like a normal handy haversack. The keys are not magical and can be crafted
([Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) DC 25) by a character able to disable the existing locks and who has
at least 5 ranks in Craft (locks). Each secure paypack is normally created with two keys.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[arcane lock](ref/spell/arcane-lock), [knock](ref/spell/knock),
[secret chest](ref/spell/secret-chest); **Cost** 2,000 gp
