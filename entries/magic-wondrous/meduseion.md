**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 29\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 45,000 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

Dried, lacquered, and stuffed with fragrant herbs, this mummified [medusa](ref/monster/medusa) head
still contains a measure of the power the medusa possessed in life. While held, a meduseion radiates
an unseen aura that causes all living creatures within 100 feet to take a -2 penalty on saving
throws to resist poison or petrification effects. Three times per day as a standard action, the
wielder of a meduseion can cause the eyes of the head to shoot forth a petrifying ray as a ranged
touch attack with a maximum range of 100 feet. Creatures struck must succeed a DC 19 Fortitude save
or be permanently transformed into stone.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [alarm](ref/spell/alarm),
[flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone); **Cost** 22,500 gp
