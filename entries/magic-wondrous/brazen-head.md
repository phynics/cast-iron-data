**Sources:** Pathfinder \#73: The Worldwound Incursion pg 64\
**Aura** faint divination (evil) and enchantment (evil); **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,500 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

A brazen head is an object sacred to the cult of [Baphomet](ref/faith/baphomet). Most brazen heads
appear to be the head of a minotaur, but others in the shape of humans, animals, or multi-faced
demons exist.

When mounted on a wall in an area sacred to Baphomet, the brazen head constantly breathes out
invisible vapors that fill a 60-foot spread. Any evil creature in this area gains the benefit of a
_[bless](ref/spell/bless)_ spell.

When in the area of an [unhallow](ref/spell/unhallow) spell cast by a worshiper of Baphomet, a
brazen head can be used once per month to perform an _[augury](ref/spell/augury)._ Rumors exist of
more powerful brazen heads capable of casting [divination](ref/spell/divination) or even
[commune](ref/spell/commune).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [bless](ref/spell/bless),
[augury](ref/spell/augury), must worship [Baphomet](ref/faith/baphomet); **Cost** 3,250 gp
