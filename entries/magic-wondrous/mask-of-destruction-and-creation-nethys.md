**Sources:** Faiths of Balance pg 27\
**Aura** faint evocation and illusion; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** -

This reversible silken mask is flat white on one side and a swirling mass of colors above a black
background on the other. When worn with the white side out, it allows the wearer to use
_[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic)_ once per day. When worn with the black side out, it allows
the wearer to use _[read magic](ref/spell/read-magic)_ once per day. If [Nethys](ref/faith/nethys)
is your patron, you can use the black side of the mask to cast
_[magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile)_ as a 3rd-level caster once per day. Likewise, you can use
the white side of the mask to cast _[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)_ once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic), [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self),
[magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile), [read magic](ref/spell/read-magic); **Cost** 2,500 gp
