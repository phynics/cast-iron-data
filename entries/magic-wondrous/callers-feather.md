**Sources:** Monster Summoner's Handbook pg 20\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** -

This magnificent feather, plucked from the wing of a powerful erinyes or angel, allows the wearer to
call stronger outsiders to her service. The wearer can use the feather to call 2 more HD worth of
creatures when casting a _[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding)_ or
_[planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally)_ spell (including _lesser_ and _greater_ versions) or when
casting [gate](ref/spell/gate). Alternatively, when casting one of these spells, the spellcaster can
expend this feather to summon a single outsider with up to 2 more Hit Dice than the spell would
normally allow. For example, he may summon a [trumpet archon](ref/monster/archon-trumpet-archon)
with planar ally, or a [pit fiend](ref/monster/devil-pit-fiend) with
[greater planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding-greater). The wearer gains a +2 insight bonus on
all Charisma checks and Charisma-based skill checks to influence the creature called. Once used, the
feather crumbles to mundane dust.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or [planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding); **Cost** 1,000
gp
