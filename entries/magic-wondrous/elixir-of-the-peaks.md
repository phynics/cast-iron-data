**Sources:** Rise of the Runelords Anniversary Edition pg 421, Pathfinder \#6: Spires of Xin-Shalast
pg 13\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,450 gp; **Weight** -

When imbibed, an elixir of the peaks gives the user the ability to scale and survive in mountainous
terrain with great skill. The imbiber is treated as if acclimated to all
[high altitudes](ref/rule/mountain-travel) lower than a death zone, and gains a +2 competence bonus
on all [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made at elevations of above 5,000 feet. She also gains
a +10 competence bonus on [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks and Survival checks in mountainous terrain
(these bonuses stack with the altitude-based bonus above). Finally, the imbiber gains the benefits
of an _[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ spell. The effects of this elixir wear off
after 8 hours.
