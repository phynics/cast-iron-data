**Aura** faint illusion and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 15,400 gp; **Weight** -

These gloves constantly project a very short-range _[silence](ref/spell/silence)_ spell, so that
anything touched or held by the wearer's hands makes no sound. Thus picking a locked door wouldn't
make a clicking noise audible to those on the other side, and holding a hand over a victim's mouth
would silence his scream. The silence extends only a few inches, so knocking on a door produces no
sound but hammering it down still does.

If the wearer presses a finger to her lips and speaks, no sound comes out, but the words are audible
to anyone she chooses in her line of sight (as the _[message](ref/spell/message)_ spell).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [message](ref/spell/message),
[silence](ref/spell/silence); **Cost** 7,700 gp
