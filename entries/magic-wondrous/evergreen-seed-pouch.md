**Sources:** Daughters of Fury pg 59\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 16,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This leather pouch is decorated with fresh mistletoe and sprigs of holly that are shaped to resemble
the druidic symbol of the [Green Faith](ref/faith/green-faith). The pouch contains seeds of various
small plants and grasses. When the pouch is used as a divine focus component for a spell that
affects or creates plant matter - such as [entangle](ref/spell/entangle) or
[spike growth](ref/spell/spike-growth) - its magic manifests as the pouch opens and the seeds float
out. The seeds plant themselves in the ground and instantly grow to create any vegetation or grass
that the spell requires to function inside of the spell's area of effect. The vegetation created by
the pouch grows regardless of the terrain, and lasts the full duration of the spell, allowing the
user to cast plant-altering or plant-creating spells in deserts, badlands, and other harsh terrains.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[wall of thorns](ref/spell/wall-of-thorns); **Cost** 8,000 gp
