## Monkey's Paw

**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 267\
**Aura** strong universal; **CL** 20th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This mummified monkey's hand bears the power to grant three [wishes](ref/spell/wish), but at a
terrible cost. The paw can grant any request within the power of a _[wish](ref/spell/wish)_ spell,
but the paw's curse twists the results to bring about despair. For example, a
_[wish](ref/spell/wish)_ to prevent an erupting volcano from burying a town might drain the magma
vault beneath the volcano, resulting in the town being swallowed up in a sinkhole when the vault
collapses into a caldera. Careful wording to avoid negative effects simply results in the attempt
failing without expending a _[wish](ref/spell/wish)._ When the monkey's paw grants a
_[wish](ref/spell/wish),_ it writhes in the holder's grasp.

Once the third wish of a monkey's paw is used, it loses its power for those who used the _wishes_
and their acquaintances, but regains its power once passed on to a stranger.
