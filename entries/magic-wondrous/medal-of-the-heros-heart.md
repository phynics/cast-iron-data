**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 266\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,400 gp; **Weight** -

The medal of the hero's heart is an honorary pendant frequently bestowed upon noble crusaders and
warriors of goodly churches. The church of [Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae) frequently award these
medals to heroes on the front lines of the Worldwound. Though it may take a variety of appearances
depending on its crafter and the champion it is awarded to, a medal of the hero's heart nearly
always features some depiction of a human heart. The wearer of a medal of the hero's heart gains a
+4 sacred bonus on saving throws to remove temporary
[negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels).

Alternatively, the wearer of the medal of the hero's heart can expend the medal's magic as an
immediate action to avoid gaining 1 permanent negative level. Doing so causes the medal to shatter,
destroying it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[restoration](ref/spell/restoration); **Cost** 1,700 gp
