**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 229\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 45,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This pair of supple, leather-heeled boots can be worn comfortably whether the wearer has feet or
hooves. The wearer can use _[fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud)_ once per day by clicking the boot's
heels together as a swift action. If the wearer is a [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling), he can
simultaneously teleport up to 1,000 feet as per _[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Quicken Spell](ref/feat/quicken-spell), [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door),
[fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud), creator must be a tiefling; **Cost** 22,500 gp
