**Sources:** Pathfinder \#34: Blood for Blood pg 69\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 39,550 gp; **Weight** -

This simple woolen cloak is trimmed across the shoulders with the feathers of eagles and other
raptors, and permits the user to _[fly](ref/spell/fly)_ (as the spell) for a total of 30 minutes
each day. These 30 minutes need not be consecutive, but each use of this ability that lasts for
fewer than 5 minutes reduces the daily limit by 5 minutes. While flying under the power of the
plumed mantle, the wearer has a +2 bonus on [Fly](ref/skill/fly) checks, in addition to any bonuses
from speed or maneuverability.

In addition to its powers of flight, the plumed mantle protects its wearer with a continuous
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ effect, and makes him appear as a golden eagle to viewers
on the ground. The wearer can suppress either effect by force of will as a free action.

While at a height greater than 40 feet, the wearer of the plumed mantle receives a +2 competence
bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks involving vision or sight.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [fly](ref/spell/fly), [minor image](ref/spell/minor-image);
**Cost** 19,775 gp
