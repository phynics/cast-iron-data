**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 251\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 5,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This simple white cotton headband is decorated with a stylized imperial dragon coiled above the
wearer's forehead. The headband grants the wearer a +2 enhancement bonus to her Wisdom score. Treat
this as a [temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the
headband is worn.

Whenever the wearer suffers a fear effect, she may spend an immediate action to reduce the severity
of the fear effect by one step. If the wearer would become [panicked](ref/misc/panicked), she is
instead [frightened](ref/misc/frightened). If she would become frightened, she is instead
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken). If the wearer would become shaken, the effect is negated entirely. The
wearer can use this ability up to three times per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[owl's wisdom](ref/spell/owls-wisdom), [remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear); **Cost** 2,800 gp
