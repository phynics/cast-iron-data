**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 13,800 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Not all who board Ulfen longships or travel the paths of Kellid mammoth hunters are capable
warriors, even though they may be just as brave. A cloak of the saga keeper is made from layers of
fox fur and leather cut in strips reminiscent of fluttering lengths of scroll. Seven of these strips
are dyed vibrant red. The wearer of the cloak constantly benefits from the effects of
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) while in cold conditions, and gains resist energy 2
against cold damage. Additionally, the wearer of the cloak can, as an immediate action, pluck one of
the cloak's seven dyed strips to gain a +6 bonus to a [Climb](ref/skill/climb),
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal), [Ride](ref/skill/ride), [Survival](ref/skill/survival), or
[Swim](ref/skill/swim) check made that round. These strips dissolve immediately upon being plucked.
Once all seven of the cloak's strips have been torn off, the wearer can no longer maker use of the
cloak's skill-improving effects, but continues to benefit from the endure elements and resistance
abilities.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[guidance](ref/spell/guidance), [resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy); **Cost** 6,900 gp
