## Dimensional Acid

**Sources:** Champions of Balance pg 28\
**Aura** varies; **CL** varies\
**Slot** none; **Price** 450 gp (Basic Pit), 1,000 gp (Jagged Pit), 1,750 gp (Corrosive Pit), 2,700
gp (Living Pit); **Weight** 1 lb.

The acid in this flask burns not through matter, but through space itself, creating a temporary
extradimensional pit wherever it touches. A flask of dimensional acid can be thrown as a
[splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. Alternatively, it
can be used as a special component when casting the [snare](ref/spell/snare) spell. Instead of the
spell's usual effect, the creature that triggers the modified _[snare](ref/spell/snare)_ is treated
as if it had taken a direct hit from the dimensional acid.

Dimensional acid comes in a number of varieties.

**Basic Pit:** This acid creates a pit, as the _[create pit](ref/spell/create-pit)_ spell, where it
lands. Faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd; [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[create pit](ref/spell/create-pit).

**Corrosive Pit:** This acid affects matter as well, creating an _[acid pit](ref/spell/acid-pit)_
where it lands. Faint conjuration; **CL** 7th; Craft Wondrous Item, [acid pit](ref/spell/acid-pit).

**Jagged Pit:** This acid creates a _[spiked pit](ref/spell/spiked-pit)_ spell where it lands.
Moderate conjuration; **CL** 5th; Craft Wondrous Item, [spiked pit](ref/spell/spiked-pit).

**Living Pit:** This acid burns into a living dimension, creating a
_[hungry pit](ref/spell/hungry-pit)_ where it lands. Moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th; Craft
Wondrous Item, [hungry pit](ref/spell/hungry-pit).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), additional spells; **Cost**
225 gp (Basic Pit), 500 gp (Jagged Pit), 875 gp (Corrosive Pit), 1,350 gp (Living Pit)
