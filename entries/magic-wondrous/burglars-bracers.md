**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 273\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 1,050 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

On command, these plain leather bracers can transform into a set of
[masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork). They can transform back to their
bracer form with another such command. Once per day, while using these thieves' tools or wearing
these bracers, the user can take 10 on a single [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) check,
even when in immediate danger or distracted.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Cost** 550 gp
