## Volatile Vaporizer

**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,200 gp (1st), 3,000 gp (2nd), 3,800 gp (3rd); **Weight** -

This lime green pill is inedible but turns a potion into a cloud of mist that can be shared by
multiple creatures. When placed inside a potion flask, the potion bubbles and froths itself into a
pungent 10-foot-radius cloud. Any creature within the cloud when it appears gains the full effect of
the potion. The cloud has no effect on creatures that cannot breathe. A creature may automatically
resist the effects of the potion cloud by momentarily holding its breath. Using the pill destroys it
and the potion. Each pill can only be used on potions of a particular spell level; using it on a
potion of a different spell level has no effect (neither the potion nor the pill are consumed). The
vaporizer also works on [alchemist](ref/class/alchemist) [infusions](ref/discovery/infusion), but
not mutagens, extracts, or magical oils.

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[alchemical allocation](ref/spell/alchemical-allocation); **Cost** 1,100 gp (1st), 1,500 gp (2nd),
1,900 gp (3rd)
