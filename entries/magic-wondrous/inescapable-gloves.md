**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 153\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The palms of these light doeskin gloves are covered with supple ripples to augment the wearer's
grip. The wearer gains a +4 competence bonus on combat maneuver checks to
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple). The wearer can expend one use of mythic power when a creature subject
to [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) or a similar effect attempts to escape the
wearer's grapple with a combat maneuver check or [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) check.
That creature must attempt the check as normal instead of automatically succeeding at it. If a
creature attempts use a non-mythic teleportation effect to escape the grapple, the wearer can expend
two uses of mythic power to negate that effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Cost** 4,500 gp
