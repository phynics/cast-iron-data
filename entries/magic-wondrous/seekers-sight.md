**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This sight can be attached to a single two-handed firearm. When this is done, the sight becomes part
of the weapon, but can be removed from that weapon with a full-round action. A firearm wielder using
a firearm that has this sight can choose to spend a full-round action to either locate an invisible
creature within line of sight or make a single shot that ignores the invisibility of a creature that
she knows is in the area.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 6,000 gp
