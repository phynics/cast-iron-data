**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 266\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 32,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This reversible multicolored cape marks the wearer's flamboyant audacity when turned with its bright
colors outward, granting a +5 competence bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) checks. Reversible as a
move action, the dappled camouflage of its other side grants a +5 competence bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks. Regardless of the cape's current facing, the wearer can spend
an immediate action at will to roll an [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) check against being
grappled, bound, or restrained as if the cape targeted her with
_[liberating command](ref/spell/liberating-command)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[liberating command](ref/spell/liberating-command); **Cost** 16,250 gp
