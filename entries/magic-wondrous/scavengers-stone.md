**Sources:** Goblins of Golarion pg 29\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This jagged, egg-shaped stone emits a faint red glow when not in direct sunlight. By rubbing a
scavenger's stone against a damaged (but not destroyed) construct or object as a full-round action,
you repair 1d6 points of damage to the object or construct. If this brings a broken object's hit
points above half its maximum hit points, it loses the [broken](ref/misc/broken) condition. As a
standard action, you can smash a scavenger's stone against a destroyed object or damaged construct.
Doing so immediately repairs the object to full functionality or heals a damaged construct of 10d6
points of damage. Destroyed magic items can be repaired in this manner if their caster level is no
higher than 3rd - more powerful destroyed magical items cannot be repaired by a scavenger's stone. A
newly-created scavenger's stone has 50 charges - smashing a stone against an object uses up 10
charges.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[make whole](ref/spell/make-whole); **Cost** 1,000 gp
