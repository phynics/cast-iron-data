**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 500 gp; **Weight** -

This elixir forces the drinker to say nothing but the truth for 10 minutes (Will DC 13 negates). She
must answer any questions put to her in that time, but with each question she can make a separate DC
13 Will save. If one of these secondary saves is successful, she doesn't break free of the
truth-compelling enchantment but also doesn't have to answer that particular question (if she does
answer, she must tell the truth). No more than one question can be asked each round. This is a
mind-affecting compulsion enchantment.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth); **Cost** 250 gp
