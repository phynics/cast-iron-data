**Aura** faint abjuration and illusion; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This thick leather cloak is charred and blackened around the edges and smells faintly of soot. Once
per day, when subject to an effect that deals fire damage, the wearer can spend an immediate action
while obscured by the flames to become invisible, leaving behind an illusory pile of ashes and
bones, as if he had been slain by the fire effect. The wearer's _invisibility_ and his illusory
remains last for 5 rounds or until the wearer attacks any creature. Creatures that study or interact
with the ashes can make a DC 11 Will save to disbelieve the illusion, though this does not end the
_invisibility_ effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [silent image](ref/spell/silent-image); **Cost** 1,300 gp
