**Aura** moderate divination and transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 52,000 gp; **Weight** -

This vibrant cluster of leaves constantly changes hue through the different colors of the autumn
season, from brown and red to orange and yellow. Once per day, the bearer may touch the leaves to
her tongue to assume _[tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape)_ (as the spell) for up to 8 hours,
appearing as a healthy tree that is lush with thousands of autumn leaves for the duration of the
effect.

While in tree form, a creature may release these leaves upon the wind, consciously controlling them
as a scrying sensor with the abilities of _[arcane eye](ref/spell/arcane-eye)._ The leaves have the
statistics of an [army ant swarm](ref/monster/ant-army-ant-swarm), except the swarm has
[plant](ref/type/plant) traits, [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 5, and vulnerability to fire.

The creature may shape the scrying leaves into a semblance of her face, allowing her to speak
through the leaves. The leaves' voice is a quiet, dry rustle, and they are capable of conveying most
of the creature's intended facial expressions (they can smile, frown, and so on). If the creature
has the [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) class ability or is a sorcerer with the
[fey](ref/sorcbloodline/fey) bloodline, she may have any [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals), or
[speak with plants](ref/spell/speak-with-plants) spell she casts originate from the leaves instead
of herself, and her _[tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape)_ does not prevent her from casting these
spells.

If the scrying leaves fail to return before creature returns to its normal shape, the magic item
does not function for 1 week. During this time, the leaves fade to brown and appear to have a light
coating of frost on them.

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[arcane eye](ref/spell/arcane-eye), [tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape),
[whispering wind](ref/spell/whispering-wind); **Cost** 26,000 gp
