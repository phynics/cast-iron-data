**Sources:** People of the Stars pg 30\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This thick black lens can be attached to the viewing end of a telescope. When the telescope is
pointed toward the sky at night, whoever gazes through the lens of starsight gains the benefits of
_[starsight](ref/spell/starsight)_ as well as a +2 competence bonus on
[Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography) checks relating to the stars.

Only one magical lens (such as this or a [harvesting lens](ref/magic-wondrous/harvesting-lens)) can
be attached to a telescope at a time.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[starsight](ref/spell/starsight), creator has at least 2 ranks in Knowledge (geography); **Cost**
1,300 gp
