**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 177\
**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** -

These magical baubles were originally created to aid Chelish nobles in slipping away to engage in
trysts with fellow partygoers, but the [Silver Ravens](ref/rule/aff-rebels) have seized upon them as
an excellent way to baffle foes in public areas without resorting to combat. A discretion charm
usually comes in the form of a cufflink, an earring, or another small piece of jewelry. Pressing the
face of the charm (a standard action) activates it, after which it remains active for up to 5
minutes.

Once it has been activated, the wearer can drop the charm as a free action to render herself
invisible, as per _[vanish](ref/spell/vanish)._ Simultaneously, the dropped charm generates an
illusory duplicate of the user in the square, as per
_[programmed image](ref/spell/programmed-image)._ This image repeats whatever activities the user
performed between the time the charm was activated and the time it was dropped. Unlike
_[programmed image](ref/spell/programmed-image),_ the illusory duplicate of the user can react to
external stimuli to a limited degree. It can turn to face those who speak to it and respond by
nodding and smiling, but otherwise cannot say anything that it did not record before being dropped.
If the charm was dropped less than 1 minute after being activated, anyone interacting with the
illusion gains a +5 bonus on the Will save to disbelieve the
_[programmed image](ref/spell/programmed-image)_ effect.

This illusory duplicate lasts for 10 minutes, whereas the _[vanish](ref/spell/vanish)_ effect on the
user lasts only 5 rounds. Once the illusion effect ends, the discretion charm loses its powers and
becomes a worthless, nonmagical piece of costume jewelry.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[programmed image](ref/spell/programmed-image), [vanish](ref/spell/vanish); **Cost** 1,750 gp
