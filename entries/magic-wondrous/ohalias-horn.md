**Sources:** Pathfinder \#131: The Reaper's Right Hand pg 19\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 55,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The bell of this slightly curved horn resembles the head of a roaring lioness. The horn functions in
many ways like a [horn of valhalla](ref/magic-wondrous/horn-of-valhalla), but while held in one
hand, the wielder gains the benefits of _[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch)._ Once per year when the
wielder sounds the horn, she can summon one [sleipnir](ref/monster/sleipnir) with a saddle for 1
hour in place of summoning the barbarians. The sleipnir willingly serves as a mount or combatant.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch), [summon monster VIII](ref/spell/summon-monster-8); **Cost**
22,500 gp
