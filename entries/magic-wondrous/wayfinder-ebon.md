**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 148, Seekers of Secrets pg 50\
**Aura** faint evocation and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Favored by [Pathfinders](ref/rule/aff-scholars) exploring dark and dangerous locales, the exterior
of this [wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder) is constructed of panels of
[darkwood](ref/eq-material/darkwood) with ebony inlays. The panels slide apart to reveal platinum
meshwork capable of holding one or two [ioun stones](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone). An ebon
wayfinder grants the bearer [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet, and with a
command word it can create _[darkness](ref/spell/darkness)_ (as per the spell) once per day,
centered on itself.

When one ioun stone is slotted into the device, its darkness ability no longer functions. When a
second stone is placed in it, the [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) ability no longer functions.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[darkness](ref/spell/darkness), [darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision); **Cost** 9,000 gp
