**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This crystalline amulet holds a fragment from the First World domain of one of the
[Eldest](ref/rule/the-eldest). A [gnome](ref/race/gnome) wearing the amulet does not count as
extraplanar on the First World. The wearer heals 1 point of lethal damage every minute and an equal
amount of nonlethal damage, up to a maximum each day equal to her Hit Dice or character level.
Finally, she gains a +4 insight bonus on saving throws against the Bleaching.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bleaching resistance](ref/spell/bleaching-resistance),
[planar adaptation](ref/spell/planar-adaptation), [regenerate](ref/spell/regenerate), creator must
be a gnome; **Cost** 5,000 gp
