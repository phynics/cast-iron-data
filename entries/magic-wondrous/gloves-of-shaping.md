**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** -

These soft leather gloves are constantly encrusted with clay between the fingers and allow the
reshaping of otherwise durable materials. The wearer can shape objects with
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 8 or lower (stone, wood, leather, and so on) as if it were soft clay.
While it's possible to rapidly make crude shapes (shaping roughly 1 cubic foot of material per
round), creating fine detail or moving parts requires additional time and an appropriate
[Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) check, at the GM's discretion.

Materials with more than 8 points of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) are still affected, though not so
dramatically. The wearer may treat these materials as having half as much hardness when attempting
to damage or reshape them. For instance, a steel bar normally has 10 points of hardness, but the
wearer of the gloves may treat it as having hardness 5, and shape it with woodworking tools.

The gloves only affect shaping materials with the wearer's hands or handheld tools, and only when
the wearer desires to do so.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[soften earth and stone](ref/spell/soften-earth-and-stone), [stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape),
[transmute rock to mud](ref/spell/transmute-rock-to-mud); **Cost** 5,000 gp
