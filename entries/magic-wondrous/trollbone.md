**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,400 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Runic engravings adorn this charred rib bone pulled from a slain troll. When the bone is tossed up
to 20 feet away, it can grow into a full-sized [troll](ref/monster/troll) under the user's control
on command. The troll normally remains for 2d4 rounds, but if the user is maintaining a bardic
performance when the item is activated, the duration is extended until the end of the performance.
When the troll is slain or the duration ends, it turns back into a bone that then crumbles into
dust.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4); **Cost** 1,200 gp
