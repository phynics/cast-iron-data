## Stone Horse

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 320\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp (Courser), 14,800 gp (Destrier); **Weight** 6,000 lbs.

This item is a roughly hewn statue of a horse, carved from some type of hard stone. A command word
brings the steed to life, enabling it to carry a burden and even to attack as if it were a real
horse of the appropriate kind. A stone horse is combat-trained.

A stone horse can carry 1,000 pounds tirelessly and never needs to rest or eat. Damage dealt to it
can be repaired either by feeding it gems (1 hp for every 50 gp value of the gem) or by casting
[stone to flesh](ref/spell/stone-to-flesh), then using healing spells until it reaches its maximum
hit points, at which time it automatically returns to its stone form.

There are two sorts of stone horses.

**Courser:** This item has the statistics of a [horse](ref/monster/horse) with
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10.

**Destrier:** This item has the statistics of a heavy horse with [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 10.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [flesh to stone](ref/spell/flesh-to-stone); **Cost**
5,000 gp (Courser), 7,400 gp (Destrier)
