**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

Made from bone and sun-dried sinew, this
[masterwork harp](ref/eq-misc/musical-instrument-masterwork) produces a vague sense of unease in
anyone near it. If the owner of this item has the bardic performance class feature he can use it to
perform a [dirge of doom](ref/performance/dirge-of-doom) even if he is not of sufficient level to
normally have access to that ability. A bard who can use dirge of doom and who plays the harp as
part of his performance increases the effective radius to 60 feet.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear); **Cost** 5,000 gp
