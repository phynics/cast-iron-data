**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 288\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 19th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This lustrous green gemstone is uncut and about the size of a human fist. The gem grants its bearer
the use of the following abilities.

- _[entropic shield](ref/spell/entropic-shield)_ (1 charge)
- _[lesser confusion](ref/spell/confusion-lesser)_ (1 charge)
- _[magic circle against law](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-law)_ (1 charge)
- _[chaos hammer](ref/spell/chaos-hammer)_ (2 charges)

A newly created chaos emerald has 1d6+2 charges. It can hold a maximum of 10 charges and
automatically regains 1d3-1 charges each day.

A non-chaotic creature who holds or carries a chaos emerald gains one
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels). This negative level persists as long as
the creature holds or carries the gem and disappears when the gem is dropped. This negative level
cannot be overcome in any way (including [restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while held or
carried.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[chaos hammer](ref/spell/chaos-hammer), [entropic shield](ref/spell/entropic-shield),
[lesser confusion](ref/spell/confusion-lesser),
[magic circle against law](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-law), creator must be chaotic; **Cost**
12,500 gp
