**Sources:** Sargava, the Lost Colony pg 29\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These soft leather boots grant the wearer the ability to move through normal undergrowth without
hindrance as if she had the [woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride) ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider), woodland stride ability; **Cost** 3,000 gp
