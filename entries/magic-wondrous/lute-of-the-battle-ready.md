**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 232\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

These magic items are usually crafted as lutes, though they can be fashioned as other instruments.
On command three times per day, the lute can transform into a +1 [spear](ref/eq-weapon/spear), +1
[longsword](ref/eq-weapon/longsword), or +1 [battleaxe](ref/eq-weapon/battleaxe). The type of the
weapon the instrument transforms into must be chosen at creation, and the weapon must be
appropriately sized for the user of the instrument. The instrument takes weapon form for 1 minute,
or until its wielder uses another command word to transform it back to its normal form.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[shillelagh](ref/spell/shillelagh); **Cost** 1,000 gp
