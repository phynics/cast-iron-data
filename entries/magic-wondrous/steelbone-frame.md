**Sources:** People of the River pg 29\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** armor; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 35 lbs.

Fashioned from the salvaged mechanical "bones" of the first
[gearsmen](ref/monster/robot-gearsman-robot) that issued forth from Silver Mount, this gear-jointed,
metal exoskeleton can be affixed to or removed from a suit of medium or heavy armor in 10 minutes.
While attached, the enchanted frame bears some of the armor's burden, reducing the armor's check
penalty by 2. In addition, three times per day, the wearer of a medium or heavy suit of armor
attached to a steelbone frame can ignore the armor's reduction to his speed for 1 round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength), [longstrider](ref/spell/longstrider); **Cost** 3,000 gp
