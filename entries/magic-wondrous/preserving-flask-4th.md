## Preserving Flask

**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp (1st), 4,000 gp (2nd), 9,000 gp (3rd), 16,000 gp (4th), 25,000 gp
(5th), 36,000 gp (6th); **Weight** -

This small, durable flask is designed to preserve alchemical extracts indefinitely. An extract
stored in a preserving flask remains potent until it is consumed or otherwise removed from the
flask. Preserved extracts count against an alchemist's extracts per day on the day they are
prepared, but not on subsequent days. Each flask is capable of preserving an extract of a particular
level. An extract stored in the flask retains duration, caster level, and other properties it had
when it was created. The flask works on extracts and infusions, but not mutagens.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be able to create
alchemical extracts of the extract level to be stored; **Cost** 500 gp (1st), 2,000 gp (2nd), 4,500
gp (3rd), 8,000 gp (4th), 12,500 gp (5th), 18,000 gp (6th)
