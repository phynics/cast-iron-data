**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 258\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 13,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This fist-shaped stone amulet glows with a fiery light. On command, this amulet causes the wearer's
fists to transform into red-hot flaming [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine). The wearer is
protected from the searing heat and can make savage blows with his hands. Unarmed strikes and
natural attacks the wearer make with his hands gain the [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming) weapon
special ability and are treated as adamantine for the purpose of bypassing
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) (but not [hardness](ref/umr/hardness)). The wearer's
hands become rigid while using this amulet and cannot be use to hold or manipulate objects (unless
that manipulation could be accomplished with a club and is not damaged by fire). The transformation
can be ended as a swift action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item);
[greater magic fang](ref/spell/magic-fang-greater); and [flame blade](ref/spell/flame-blade),
[flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike), or [fireball](ref/spell/fireball); **Cost** 6,500 gp
