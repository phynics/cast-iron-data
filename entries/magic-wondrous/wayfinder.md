**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A small magical device patterned on the design of ancient Azlanti relics, a wayfinder is a compact
compass typically made from silver and bearing gold accents. While it serves as a badge of office
for agents of the [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars), a wayfinder is as much a handy tool
as a status symbol. With a command word, you can cause a wayfinder to shine (as the
_[light](ref/spell/light)_ spell). A wayfinder also acts as a nonmagical (magnetic)
[compass](ref/eq-misc/compass), granting you a +2 circumstance bonus on
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to avoid becoming lost. All wayfinders feature a small
indentation designed to hold a single [ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone). An ioun stone
slotted in this manner grants you its normal benefits as if it were orbiting your head; some ioun
stones can grant additional powers once they are fitted into a wayfinder, at the GM's discretion.
(See the ioun stone entry for more information on these "resonant powers.")

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [light](ref/spell/light);
**Cost** 250 gp
