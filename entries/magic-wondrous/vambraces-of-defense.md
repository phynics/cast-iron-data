**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

These polished steel vambraces grant a +1 deflection bonus to AC. Once per day, as an immediate
action, if the wearer has at least one hand free, when he would normally be hit with an attack from
a ranged weapon, he may deflect it with the bracers, taking no damage from it, as if he had the
[Deflect Arrows](ref/feat/deflect-arrows) feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [shield](ref/spell/shield);
**Cost** 3,000 gp
