**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 7 lbs.

This black-and-gray wool coat sports over a dozen pockets on its front alone and even more line the
insides. Interdimensional magic causes anything placed in one pocket to immediately appear in
another randomly determined pocket. A character wearing this coat gains a +5 circumstance bonus on
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks to hide a small object on her body. However, if
a character wearing a coat of pockets places anything in one of the coat's pockets, she must spend a
standard action to retrieve it (rather than a move action).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[secret chest](ref/spell/secret-chest); **Cost** 1,250 gp
