**Sources:** Heroes of the Streets pg 14\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Worked brown leather straps run up the backs and around the soles of this sturdy set of footwear.
The wearer of a pair of combat sandals can [charge](ref/rule/charge) through allies' squares without
difficulty, and allies adjacent to the wearer don't provide [cover](ref/rule/cover) to targets the
wearer attacks with reach or ranged weapons.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat),
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement), [true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Cost**
9,000 gp
