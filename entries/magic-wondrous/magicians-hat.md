**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 245\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** -

Mystic symbols embroidered in gold thread decorate this conical hat. Three times per day as a free
action when casting a spell, the wearer can shift metamagic feats on prepared spells. The wearer can
either move a metamagic feat from another prepared spell to a spell being cast or from the spell
being cast to another prepared spell. In either case, the spell that gains the metamagic feat can't
exceed the spell slot of the spell that originally had the metamagic feat. For example, a wizard
with [detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts), [silent](ref/feat/silent-spell)
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), and [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door) prepared
could cast a silent _[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts)_ (and would now have
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ prepared), but could not cast silent
_[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)._ Moving metamagic feats does not increase spell casting
time, even for [quickened](ref/feat/quicken-spell) spells. The hat provides no benefit to
spontaneous casting or [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[mnemonic enhancer](ref/spell/mnemonic-enhancer); **Cost** 10,000 gp
