**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 152\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 96,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This helm is made of large, alchemically hardened snake scales, and is crafted in the shape of a
stylized serpent mouth. Its wearer stares out through the serpent's eyes, and sees all things cast
in a yellow tint. When worn, it grants the following
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) to the wearer:

- [Hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism) (3/day)
- _[Summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3)_
  ([constrictor snake](ref/monster/snake-constrictor-snake) only, 3/day)
- _[Beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)_ (snake form only, 1/day)
- [Poison](ref/spell/poison) (1/day)

A mythic creature can expend one use of mythic power when using the
_[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3)_ [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) to
instead cast it as _mythic beast shape III._ Alternatively, he can expend two uses of mythic power
to instead cast the augmented version of _mythic beast shape III._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3),
[summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3); **Cost** 48,000 gp
