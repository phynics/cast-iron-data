**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 252\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 4th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This simple headband of braided hide grants a +5 competence bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival)
checks and inures the wearer to the strain of long travel, as the
_[tireless pursuit](ref/spell/tireless-pursuit)_ spell. If the wearer is a
[ranger](ref/class/ranger), the hunter's band increases his
[favored enemy](ref/ability/favored-enemy) bonus on attack rolls by +1 for all his favored enemies.
If the ranger has the hunter's bond (companions) class feature, this increase also applies to the
attack roll bonus his allies gain from that ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[instant enemy](ref/spell/instant-enemy), [tireless pursuit](ref/spell/tireless-pursuit), creator
must have 5 ranks in Survival; **Cost** 5,500 gp
