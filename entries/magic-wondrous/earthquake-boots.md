**Sources:** Heroes of the Darklands pg 30\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 67,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These thick hide boots allow the wearer to detect unseen enemies, and they provide a final line of
defense against overwhelming foes. The wearer gains [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) with a range
of up to 60 feet as long as she is standing on solid ground. Additionally, once per day, by stomping
repeatedly on an earthen or stone surface as a full-round action that provokes attacks of
opportunity, the wearer can cast _[earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake), [see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility); **Cost** 33,750
gp
