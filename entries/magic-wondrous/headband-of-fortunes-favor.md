**Aura** faint enchantment and evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 7,700 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This multicolored headband with beaded tassels tips luck in the wearer's favor. The wearer gains a
+1 luck bonus on saving throws.

Any effect that gives the wearer a luck bonus (such as a divine favor or
_[prayer](ref/spell/prayer)_ spell) or explicitly increases the wearer's good luck (such as a
witch's [fortune](ref/hex/fortune) hex, the [Luck](ref/domain/luck) domain's bit of luck domain
power, and so on) lasts for 1 round longer than normal on the wearer. Abilities without a time-based
duration (such as carrying a [stone of good luck](ref/magic-wondrous/stone-of-good-luck-luckstone))
are unaffected by the headband.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [ill omen](ref/spell/ill-omen)
or [prayer](ref/spell/prayer); **Cost** 3,850 gp
