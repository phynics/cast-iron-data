**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 177\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This coarse-grained salt is flecked with bands of silver running through each crystal. A spellcaster
can include this salt as a material component when casting a spell, even if the spell does not
normally require material components. Any bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing damage dealt by a spell
enhanced by sterling salt is treated as good and silver for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and suppressing [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration),
in addition to its normal type. If a creature that is adversely affected by silver (such as a
[devil](ref/family/devil) or [lycanthrope](ref/template/lycanthrope)) is affected by a spell
augmented by sterling salt, that creature takes a -1 penalty on its saving throw to resist or negate
the effects of the spell (if a saving throw is allowed).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item); **Cost** 750 gp
