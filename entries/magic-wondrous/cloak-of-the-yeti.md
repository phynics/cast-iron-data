**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This heavy cloak is crafted from shaggy white [yeti](ref/monster/yeti) fur. When the cloak's hood is
pulled up over the wearer's head, it takes on a semblance of its arctic namesake's fearsome visage.
Northern warriors value its ability to ward against the chill of winter, as well as the protection
it provides in combat. The cloak of the yeti provides a constant
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ effect in cold weather (it has no effect in hot
weather). In battle, the cloak wraps around the wearer, granting a +1 natural armor bonus. In
addition, with the hood drawn over the wearer's head, the cloak grants a +2 competence bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) skill checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[barkskin](ref/spell/barkskin), [cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements); **Cost** 2,000 gp
