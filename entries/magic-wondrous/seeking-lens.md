**Sources:** Pathfinder \#131: The Reaper's Right Hand pg 33\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This telescope is adjustable as a full-round action, allowing magnifications anywhere from ×2 to
×250. The user gains a +5 competence bonus on [Knowledge (geography)](ref/skill/knowledge-geography)
and [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks when using the device to study planets, stars, and
other celestial bodies.

As a full-round action, the user can press the two lenses together, compressing the device to a
small assayer's lens small enough to occupy the eyes slot. In this form, the lens grants a +5
competence bonus on [Appraise](ref/skill/appraise) checks, [Perception](ref/skill/perception)
checks, and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to follow tracks. Once per week, after studying
the possessions or tracks of a creature, the wearer may designate that creature as his quarry. This
functions as the [ranger's](ref/class/ranger) quarry ability. If the wearer already has the quarry
ability, the lens instead allows him to designate a creature as his quarry even if it is not one of
his [favored enemies](ref/ability/favored-enemy).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[clairaudience/clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 10,000 gp
