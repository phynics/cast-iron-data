**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 243\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 300 gp; **Weight** -

This small, white pill has the consistency of a toughened almond. When consumed on its own, an
accent pill has no effect on the creature ingesting it. To function, the pill must first be coated
with the blood or saliva of a living creature. Once coated, the pill turns from white to a dull
brown. Consuming a coated pill grants the consuming creature the exact tone and speech mannerisms of
the creature whose blood or saliva was used to coat the pill. When used as part of a disguise, it
grants the consumer a +15 bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks to fool a listener into
thinking the speaker is the creature whose blood or saliva coated the pill; as with
_[vocal alteration](ref/spell/vocal-alteration),_ this bonus applies only to someone hearing the
voice without seeing the speaker. This effect lasts for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[vocal alteration](ref/spell/vocal-alteration), creator must possess 5 ranks in Craft (alchemy);
**Cost** 150 gp
