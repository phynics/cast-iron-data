**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This snakeskin belt is crafted from the hides of brightly hued serpents. When buckled, it
immediately binds tightly to the wearer's waist. Once per day, the wearer can command the
serpentform belt to transform her lower half into that of a serpent. While in this form, the wearer
gains a +4 bonus on combat maneuver checks to [grapple or pin](ref/rule/grapple) a foe. Once a foe
is grappled, the wearer can [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) the opponent with any successful
subsequent grapple checks, dealing an amount of bludgeoning damage equal to 1d8 + her Strength
modifier, if Medium (1d6 if Small). The snake form lasts until another command is given, or for a
maximum of 10 minutes, whichever is shorter.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1); **Cost** 10,000 gp
