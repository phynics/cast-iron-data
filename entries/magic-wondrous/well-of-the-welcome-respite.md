**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 122,000 gp; **Weight** -

While normally functioning as a standard
[well of many worlds](ref/magic-wondrous/well-of-many-worlds), this well possesses a command word
that supersedes its normal placement-based destination, and it instead opens up onto a single
location designated by its maker during the well's creation (often the creator's home or a private,
warded demiplane). Those finding the well are not immediately aware of this command word unless a
spell more powerful than a simple [identify](ref/spell/identify) spell is used, and the well's
secret destination, whatever it might be, may or may not still be occupied.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [gate](ref/spell/gate),
[mage's magnificent mansion](ref/spell/mages-magnificent-mansion); **Cost** 61,000 gp
