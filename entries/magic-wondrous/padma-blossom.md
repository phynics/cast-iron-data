**Aura** faint abjuration and enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** -

This perfect lotus flower formed from pink jade offers purity of mind and spiritual calm. While
grasped, the blossom grants its holder a +3 competence bonus on
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks and suppresses the following on its holder: morale
bonuses, fear effects, and the [confused](ref/misc/confused), [dazed](ref/misc/dazed), or
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) conditions. Twice per day, the bearer can cast
_[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions), [remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear); **Cost** 4,000 gp
