**Sources:** Kobolds of Golarion pg 31\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 300 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This brightly labeled bottle or waterskin could appear to contain any number of different beverages,
from absinthe to wine. However, it's actually a magical trap, and when the bottle is opened, a
[spider swarm](ref/monster/spider-spider-swarm) pours out. The swarm stays in the area for 3 rounds,
then disappears. Detecting that the bottle is trapped requires a successful DC 25 Perception check
made by a character with the [trapfinding](ref/ability/trapfinding) ability, and disabling it
requires a successful DC 25 [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) check. After being triggered,
the trapped beverage remains a mundane bottle or wineskin.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm); **Cost** 150 gp
