**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 16,200 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These masterwork iron manacles sap the will of their wearer. When they are attached to a humanoid
creature as their command word is spoken, they affect their prisoner with a
_[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person)_ spell, except that if the prisoner fails its saving
throw, the effect lasts for as long as the shackles are attached. Removing or destroying the
shackles immediately breaks the enchantment. The shackles can be so used once per day. Shackles of
durance vile can only be activated against a [helpless](ref/misc/helpless), restrained, or willing
creature; if attached to a creature still able to resist they function only as
[manacles](ref/eq-misc/manacles-masterwork), albeit superior ones. Shackles of durance vile have
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 15, 20 hit points, and a [superior lock](ref/eq-misc/lock-superior).
They have a break DC of 30 and an [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) DC of 35.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person); **Cost** 8,200 gp
