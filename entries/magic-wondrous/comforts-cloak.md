**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 265\
**Aura** faint abjuration and necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 15,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A favored cloak of rangers, this cloak is a patchwork of hides and fur. Comfort's cloak grants a
constant _[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ effect to the wearer, and grants a +4
competence bonus to saves against disease,
[energy drain](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels), effects that cause
[fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued) or [exhaustion](ref/misc/exhausted), and poisons. Furthermore, while
the wearer is resting he [regains](ref/rule/healing) twice his Hit Dice in hit points for 8 hours of
rest instead of the typical 1 hit point per Hit Die. When taking a full 24 hours of rest, the
character regains 5 times his Hit Dice in hit points instead of twice his Hit Dice in hit points.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements); **Cost** 7,800 gp
