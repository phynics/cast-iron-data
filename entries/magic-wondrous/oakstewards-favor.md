**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 6,550 gp; **Weight** -

This palm-sized wooden badge is covered in intricate Druidic script that bears the hallmark of the
Oakstewards of Sevenarches. Occasionally given as a gift to those who aid the druids, an
Oakstewards' favor gives the wearer a +4 competence bonus on
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) checks and increases the DC of any enchantment spells the
wearer casts against animals by 2.

If the token of favor is broken apart and added to boiling water, it becomes a nourishing porridge
that feeds up to eight Medium creatures. This meal cures all ongoing [sickness](ref/misc/sickened)
and [nausea](ref/misc/nauseated) effects, and provides the benefits of
_[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison)_ and _[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease)._
In addition, for 24 hours after eating the porridge, subjects gain a +2 luck bonus on Will saves
against fear.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal), [heroes' feast](ref/spell/heroes-feast),
[neutralize poison](ref/spell/neutralize-poison), [remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease);
**Cost** 3,275 gp
