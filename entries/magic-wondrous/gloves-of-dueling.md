**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 236, Advanced Player's Guide pg 305\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** -

These supple gloves grant the wearer a +4 bonus to her CMD against [disarm](ref/rule/disarm)
attacks, attempts to sunder her wielded weapons, and effects that cause her to lose her grip on her
weapons (such as [grease](ref/spell/grease)). The wearer doesn't drop held weapons when
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked) or [stunned](ref/misc/stunned). If the wearer has the weapon training
class feature and is using an appropriate weapon, her weapon training bonus increases by +2.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[greater magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon-greater); **Cost** 7,500 gp
