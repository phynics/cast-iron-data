**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Weight** 1 lb.

This figurine appears to be a
[figurine of wondrous power](ref/magic-wondrous/figurine-of-wondrous-power), but instead of having
the ability to animate and serve its owner, it curses that owner with bad luck. While in possession
of this figurine, the owner takes a -2 penalty to AC and CMD, and a -2 penalty on attack rolls,
skill checks, ability checks, and saving throws.

### Construction

**Requirements** any figurine of wondrous power; **Cost** -
