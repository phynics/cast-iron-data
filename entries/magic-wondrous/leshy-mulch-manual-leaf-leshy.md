## Leshy Mulch Manual

**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 30\
**Aura** varies; **CL** varies\
**Slot** none; **Price** 800 gp (Leaf Leshy), 1,125 gp (Gourd Leshy), 1,875 gp (Fungus Leshy), 3,600
gp (Seaweed Leshy); **Weight** 5 lbs.

A leshy mulch manual is a fragile, bark-bound book containing a dozen pages of coarse, grainy paper.
The book includes information and incantations that help in crafting a [leshy](ref/family/leshy).
The instructions grant a +3 competence bonus on skill checks made to create a leshy, reduce the
number of ranks in [Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) needed to create a leshy by 2,
and allow the user to treat her caster level as 2 higher for the purposes of creating a leshy. The
book can also be chopped up and mixed into the soil used to grow a leshy, replacing the normal
crafting cost for the leshy. When a leshy is cultivated from this soil, a spirit is automatically
lured into the body, with no percentile roll required.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth), [summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3);
**Cost** 1,250 gp (fungus lechy), 750 gp (gourd leshy), 500 gp (leaf leshy), 2,250 gp (seaweed
leshy)
