**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 15th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 120,000 gp; **Weight** -

This device usually appears to be a black circular amulet, although anyone looking closely at it
sees a dark, moving swirl of color. The amulet allows its wearer to utilize the spell
_[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)._ However, this is a difficult item to master. The user must
make a DC 15 Intelligence check in order to get the amulet to take her to the plane (and the
specific location on that plane) that she desires. If she fails, the amulet transports her and all
those traveling with her to a random location on that plane (01-60 on d%) or to a random plane
(61-100).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Cost** 60,000 gp
