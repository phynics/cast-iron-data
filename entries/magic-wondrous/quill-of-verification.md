**Sources:** Villain Codex pg 45\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This swan-feather quill helps its wielder verify signatures. The wielder can store a signature in
the quill by pressing the quill into the signature and speaking a command word. She can then tap the
quill's tip on another signature to check if it was written by the same individual. The quill
verifies the authenticity of the signature as if it had attained a total result of 20 on a
[Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics) check. If the signature detects as legitimate (including
skilled forgeries whose DC to detect them is greater than 20), the quill glows blue. A quill of
verification can validate a signature up to 3 times per day. The wielder can switch the stored
signature any number of times between uses of the verification ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[identify](ref/spell/identify); **Cost** 500 gp
