**Sources:** Agents of Evil pg 28\
**Aura** moderate divination and necromancy; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 16,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Sewn from deep purple fabric, an Anaphexis robe appears to be a plain version of
[Pharasmin](ref/faith/pharasma) holy vestments. A creature wearing this robe can speak directly into
the minds of other creatures wearing Anaphexis robes or carrying an unholy symbol of
[Norgorber](ref/faith/norgorber) within 100 feet, as long as they share a common language. The robes
grant a +10 circumstance bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks to appear as a monk of
Pharasma and a +10 circumstance bonus on [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks to
conceal weapons. Once per day, a wearer with the death attack class ability can reduce the number of
rounds required to study a target for a death attack by 1.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [message](ref/spell/message),
[slay living](ref/spell/slay-living); **Cost** 8,000 gp
