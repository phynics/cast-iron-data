**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 230\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 14th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 16,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This shining helm glints with the multicolored hues of the aurora, and features wings carved of fine
whalebone that sweep back from the temples. While it is in good shape, it shows signs of having seen
battle. Once per day the wearer can summon a giant spectral wolf. Treat this as
_[phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed)_ cast by a 14th-level caster, but with an AC of 24 (-1
size, +8 natural armor, +7 Dexterity) and 100 hit points. The wolf can persist for up to 24 hours,
as long as the creature that summoned it is still wearing the helm of the valkyrie. Even though it
takes the form of a fierce predator and is more powerful than other phantom steeds, the mount is
nothing more than mist and can't attack.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed); **Cost** 8,000 gp
