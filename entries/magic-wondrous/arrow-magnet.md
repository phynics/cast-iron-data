**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 600 gp; **Weight** -

This tiny silver cube is activated by throwing it at an intersection of squares within 50 feet. The
cube hovers 5 feet above the ground at that location for the next 5 rounds. Any small projectile or
thrown weapon (such as arrows, shuriken, vials, and bullets) that passes through the squares
adjacent to the cube automatically turn in midair to hit the cube (no attack roll needed) instead of
their intended target. The cube has 5 hit points and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 8. If the arrow
magnet is touched by a creature or reduced to 0 hit points or less, it falls to the ground and is
destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[protection from arrows](ref/spell/protection-from-arrows); **Cost** 300 gp
