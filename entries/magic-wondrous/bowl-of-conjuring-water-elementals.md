**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 90,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This large container is usually fashioned from silver with a rippled pattern. It is about 1 foot in
diameter and half that deep. The bowl must be filled with fresh or salt water to function. On
command as a full-round action, the bearer may summon a water elemental. If the bowl is filled with
salt water, it conjures a [Huge water elemental](ref/monster/water-elemental) as if using
_[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6)._ If filled with fresh water, it conjures a
[Large water elemental](ref/monster/water-elemental) as if using
_[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5)._ Conjuring an elemental empties the bowl. The bowl
cannot summon another elemental until the previous one is dismissed, killed, or the duration of its
summoning expires.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5), [summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6);
**Cost** 45,000 gp
