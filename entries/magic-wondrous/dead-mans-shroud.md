**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 55\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This dirt-stained cloak was woven in shadow from the burial shroud of a condemned murderer. It
prevents nonintelligent undead from detecting you, as per the
_[hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead)_ spell. Intelligent undead can perceive you if they
succeed at a DC 11 Will save. The warding is ended if you touch an undead creature,
[channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) against undead, or attack any creature. Once per day as
a standard action you can silently call forth the shadows bound into the shroud, rendering you
invisible for 5 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[hide from undead](ref/spell/hide-from-undead), [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility); **Cost**
3,000 gp
