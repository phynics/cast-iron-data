**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 265\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This ruffled silk cloak is incredibly durable despite its soft, delicate construction. Anytime the
wearer falls asleep (whether the sleep is natural or brought on by a magical effect), the cloak
immediately transforms into sticky strands that envelop the wearer's body, hardening into a solid
silk cocoon. While wrapped in the cocoon the wearer gains a +4 enhancement bonus to natural armor
and is protected from critical hits and sneak attacks as if subject to the
[light fortification](ref/magic-enh/fortification-light) armor ability (25% chance for normal damage
on a critical hit). The cocoon opens and transforms back into a cloak once the wearer awakens.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[vermin shape I](ref/spell/vermin-shape-1); **Cost** 5,000 gp
