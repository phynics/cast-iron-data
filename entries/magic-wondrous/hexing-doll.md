**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 302\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This malevolent-looking doll is made out of cheap rags and embroidery thread and is stuffed with
straw or sawdust. A creature holding or carrying the doll takes a -4 penalty on saving throws
against [witch](ref/class/witch) hexes. Alternatively, if the doll is stuffed with the hair of a
creature, any witch holding the doll increases the DC of her hexes by +2 when using the hexes
against that creature. A doll used in this way does not cause the witch to take a penalty on her
saving throws.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse); **Cost** 1,250 gp
