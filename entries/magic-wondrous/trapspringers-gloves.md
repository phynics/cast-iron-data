**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 239, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 54\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These studded leather gloves look similar to workman's gloves, though they are reinforced with
numerous metal strips that the user can extend or retract as she wills, turning the tip of each
finger into a customizable tool useful in examining and disarming traps. While worn, these gloves
grant a +5 competence bonus on all [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks made to disarm
traps. In addition, the gloves grant the user a +1 luck bonus on all saving throws made against
traps.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[find traps](ref/spell/find-traps); **Cost** 2,000 gp
