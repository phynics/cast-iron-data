**Sources:** Pathfinder \#96: Shadow of the Storm Tyrant pg 28\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 24,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This giant-sized silver brooch, like that used to fasten a cloak or cape, is fashioned in the shape
of a cloud inscribed inside a circle. The wearer of a cloud engineer badge can cast
_[life bubble](ref/spell/life-bubble)_ and _[reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person)_ once per day.
In addition, the wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on [Craft (alchemy)](ref/skill/craft-any) and
[Knowledge (engineering)](ref/skill/knowledge-engineering) checks. A cloud engineer badge occupies
the neck slot on Large or Huge creatures, but a Medium creature can wear the badge as a pectoral in
the chest slot.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[life bubble](ref/spell/life-bubble), [reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person); **Cost** 12,000 gp
