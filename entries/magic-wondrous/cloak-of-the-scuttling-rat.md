**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 265, Advanced Race Guide pg 155\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This unassuming cloak initially appears to be made of plain leather. When the wearer speaks the
command word, however, the cloak of the scuttling rat wraps itself around her, transforming her into
a [dire rat](ref/monster/rat-dire-rat) as if using _[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1)._ The
wearer can remain in dire rat form for up to 5 minutes per day. This duration need not be
consecutive, but it must be used in 1-minute increments.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1); **Cost** 3,000 gp
