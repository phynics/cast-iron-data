**Sources:** Pathfinder \#70: The Frozen Stars pg 63\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 17th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This glowing violet liquid is ice-cold regardless of the surrounding temperature. This elixir allows
a character to bypass the cold immunity or cold resistance of creatures she attacks with her magic.
For 6 rounds after drinking a truefrost elixir, a character's spells,
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), and supernatural abilities that deal cold
damage become supernaturally cold, and the damage caused by these effects is not subject to being
reduced by a creature's cold resistance. Creatures with cold immunity take half damage from such
attacks. Truefrost elixir has no effect on cold damage from weapons, on non-cold-based effects, or
against creatures without immunity to cold or cold resistance.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[polar midnight](ref/spell/polar-midnight); **Cost** 750 gp
