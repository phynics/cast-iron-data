**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 232, Pathfinder Society Field Guide pg 54\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

These soft leather sandals have soles that consist of a layer of purple velvet decorated with
intricate gold stitching. When slipped onto the feet, they feel as weightless as air. In any round
when the wearer has already moved at least 10 feet along the ground or another surface (not counting
travel on a mount or vehicle), these boots can be activated as a swift action to give the wearer the
ability to walk on air (as the _[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk)_ spell) until the end of the round.
If the wearer hasn't reached a solid surface by the end of the round, she immediately falls back to
the ground, taking any applicable [falling](ref/rule/falling) damage. These sandals can be used up
to 5 times per day, but no more often than once per minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk); **Cost** 2,500 gp
