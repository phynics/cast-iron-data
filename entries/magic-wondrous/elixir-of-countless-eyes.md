**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 20\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,200 gp; **Weight** -

This bubbling green liquid causes eyes to sprout all over the imbiber's body. These vigilant eyes
prevent the imbiber from being [flanked](ref/rule/flanking). In addition, the imbiber has a greater
awareness of her surroundings, which grants a +2 competence bonus on initiative checks for 1 hour.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[countless eyes](ref/spell/countless-eyes); **Cost** 600 gp
