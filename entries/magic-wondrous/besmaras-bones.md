**Sources:** Pathfinder \#56: Raiders of the Fever Sea pg 56\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** see text; **Price** 10,800 gp; **Weight** 300 lbs.

Named for the goddess of piracy, strife, and sea monsters, this figurehead must be mounted on the
prow of a ship to function. Once per day, a ship carrying Besmara's Bones can inflict a curse on an
enemy ship struck with a successful [ramming attack](ref/rule/ramming). The target ship must succeed
at a DC 14 Will save or its pilot takes a -4 penalty on
[Profession (sailor)](ref/skill/profession-any) checks to control the ship. In addition, the cursed
ship can never gain the upper hand in [ship-to-ship combat](ref/rule/ship-to-ship-combat). The curse
inflicted by the figurehead cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with a
[break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment), [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish),
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle), [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), or [wish](ref/spell/wish)
spell. In addition, once per day, Besmara's Bones can be used to cast
_[false life](ref/spell/false-life)_ (CL 5th) on the captain of the ship carrying the figurehead. A
ship may not have more than one figurehead, magical or otherwise, mounted on it at one time.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse), [false life](ref/spell/false-life); **Cost** 5,400 gp
