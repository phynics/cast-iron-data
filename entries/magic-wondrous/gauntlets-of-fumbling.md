**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 346, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 540\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** hands; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These gauntlets perform according to their appearance until the wearer finds herself under attack or
in a life and death situation. At that time, the curse is activated. The wearer becomes
fumble-fingered, with a 50% chance each round of dropping anything held in either hand. The
gauntlets also lower the wearer's Dexterity score by 2 points. Once the curse is activated, the
gloves can be removed only by means of a [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell, a
[wish](ref/spell/wish), or a [miracle](ref/spell/miracle).
