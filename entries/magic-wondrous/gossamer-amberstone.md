**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 2,700 gp; **Weight** -

This coin-sized piece of cracked amber is glued to a leather strap and contains a large, perfectly
preserved bloodsucking insect. Once per day, the wearer can use
_[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds)._

If [Ghlaunder](ref/faith/ghlaunder) is the wearer's patron, the wearer can use
_[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm)_ once per day. The swarm can be an
[advanced](ref/template/advanced) [spider swarm](ref/monster/spider-spider-swarm) or a
[young](ref/template/young) [mosquito swarm](ref/monster/mosquito-mosquito-swarm).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), [summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm); **Cost**
1,350 gp
