**Sources:** Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg 26\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,400 gp; **Weight** -

These fine, gray ashes usually come in a black leather bag covered in various symbols of bad luck.
When the contents of the bag are flung into the air, the ashes coat creatures within a 10-foot
radius of the user, causing them to take a -4 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, ability
checks, and skill checks for 2d4 rounds. The user is immune to this effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse); **Cost** 1,200 gp
