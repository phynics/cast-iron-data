**Sources:** Black Markets pg 22\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This drab brown, hooded cloak acts as a
[cloak of resistance +1](ref/magic-wondrous/cloak-of-resistance-1) but also quickly absorbs any
liquids poured onto it, drying in moments. Three times per day, the wearer can pour a vial of
[holy water](ref/eq-weapon/holy-water) onto the interior lining of an aspergillum cloak, causing its
outer surface to glisten and drip. The holy water cannot be recovered once absorbed. For 7 minutes,
the garment is soaked in holy water, dealing 2d4 points of damage to any undead or evil outsider
that strikes the wearer with an unarmed strike or a natural weapon. An aspergillum cloak can also
absorb unholy water instead, dealing damage to good outsiders instead. An aspergillum cloak gains no
special abilities from absorbing any other liquid.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bless water](ref/spell/bless-water); **Cost** 4,000 gp
