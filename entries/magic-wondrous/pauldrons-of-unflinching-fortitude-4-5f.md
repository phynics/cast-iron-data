## Pauldrons of Unflinching Fortitude

**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 1,500 gp (+1, +2F), 6,000 gp (+2, +3F), 13,500 gp (+3, +4F), 24,000 gp
(+4, +5F), 37,500 gp (+5/+6 Fortitude); **Weight** 1 lb.

The wearer of these elegant, polished steel pauldrons gains a bonus on all saving throws, as with a
[cloak of resistance](ref/magic-wondrous/cloak-of-resistance), but the resistance bonus on Fortitude
saves is one greater. Furthermore, if the wearer fails a Fortitude saving throw, he can expend one
use of mythic power as an immediate action to reroll that saving throw. If the wearer fails the
saving throw on the reroll, he can't use this ability again for 24 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance), the creator must have
the mythic saves ability; **Cost** 750 gp (+1, +2F), 3,000 gp (+2, +3F), 6,750 gp (+3, +4F), 12,000
gp (+4, +5F), 18,750 gp (+5/+6 Fortitude)
