**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,200 gp; **Weight** -

This dark, syrupy draught doubles the range of the drinker's [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) and
also enables her to see through _[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness)_ when using
darkvision. The effects last for 1 hour.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision), [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness); **Cost** 600 gp
