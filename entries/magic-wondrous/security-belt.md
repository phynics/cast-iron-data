**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 12,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A security belt increases the DC of [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks made against
the wearer and checks to locate items or weapons hidden on the wearer's person by 5. The wearer also
gets a +5 bonus to CMD against the [steal](ref/rule/steal) maneuver. Once per day on command, the
wearer can touch a single item of up to 10 cubic feet and shrink it into a tiny cloth duplicate as
the _[shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item)_ spell. With a move action, the wearer can draw one of
these items from one of the belt's pockets and return it to normal size. It is difficult to detect a
security belt and any items in the pockets as magic. They are affected as though a constant
_[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura)_ spell were making them appear nonmagical.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura), [shrink item](ref/spell/shrink-item); **Cost** 6,250 gp
