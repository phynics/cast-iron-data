**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 197, Book of the Damned - Volume 3: Horsemen of the Apocalypse pg
41\
**Aura** faint necromancy \[evil\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 5,400 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This carved [cacodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-cacodaemon) skull bears dozens of sharp teeth and is
strung on a thin iron chain adorned with vertebrae. Once per day as a full-round action, the wearer
can use the skull to draw forth the life essence of a dying or recently dead creature to cast
_[create soul gem](ref/spell/create-soul-gem)._ The wearer can ingest this soul gem as a standard
action. This frees the soul within the gem, which can be raised from the dead normally. The wearer
then gains [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2 for a number of rounds equal to the consumed
soul's Hit Dice when it was still living. Draining a soul with the necklace or consuming a soul gem
is an evil act.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[create soul gem](ref/spell/create-soul-gem); **Cost** 2,700 gp
