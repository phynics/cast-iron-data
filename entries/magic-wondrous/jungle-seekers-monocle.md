**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 20\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,400 gp; **Weight** -

The lens of this classical monocle is made of carefully shaped Shory glass. By looking through the
lens, a wearer of a jungle seeker's monocle can find hidden creatures even in the thickest of
jungles. The wearer can use [Perception](ref/skill/perception) to detect the presence of other
living creatures up to 3d6 ×10 feet away in any forest, treating it as if it were a sparse forest.
The monocle also grants a +2 competence bonus on Perception checks to notice creatures using
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth), regardless whether the wearer is in a forest or elsewhere.

### Construction

**Requirements** Create Wondrous Item, [acute senses](ref/spell/acute-senses); **Cost** 6,200 gp
