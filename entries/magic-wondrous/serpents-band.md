**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 252\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and enchantment; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The wearer of this circlet gains a +2 insight bonus on saving throws against effects with the
emotion descriptor, including fear effects. The wearer gains fluency in Draconic and may converse
with non-sentient (Intelligence 1 or 2) reptilian animals and magical beasts as if using the
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)_ spell, and gains a +4 competence bonus on
[Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal) and [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks made
when interacting with such creatures.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animal trance](ref/spell/animal-trance), [hypnotism](ref/spell/hypnotism),
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),
[summon nature's ally V](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-5); **Cost** 4,500 gp
