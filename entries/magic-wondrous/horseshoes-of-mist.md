**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 232\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 27,000 gp; **Weight** 12 lbs.

When a horse is shod with these horseshoes, they give off a puff of mist each time a hoof strikes
the earth. On the rider's command, the mount and the rider are both affected as if subject to a
_[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)_ spell. If for some reason the rider and the mount become
separated from one another while in _[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form),_ the effect
automatically ends for both. The horseshoes can be used for 10 minutes per day. The duration need
not be consecutive, but it must be spent in 1-minute increments.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form); **Cost** 13,500 gp
