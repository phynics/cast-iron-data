**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** -

These goggles' dark lenses are made of round obsidian sheets thin enough to see through. They grant
the wearer [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 120 ft., as well as a +4 competence bonus on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made to track
while underground.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision), [terrain bond](ref/spell/terrain-bond); **Cost** 10,000 gp
