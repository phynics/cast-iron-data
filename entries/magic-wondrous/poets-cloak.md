**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 233\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 9,750 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Even after cleaning and mending, this traveler's cloak appears worn and weathered. For wearers with
the bardic performance class feature, it grants the use of [raging song](ref/ability/raging-song) as
a 4th-level skald by spending rounds of bardic performance. For wearers with the raging song class
feature, it grants the use of [inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage) and
[inspire competence](ref/performance/inspire-competence) as a 4th-level bard by spending rounds of
raging song.

In addition, each poet's cloak allows its wearer to grant one specific rage power when using raging
song in place of his own rage powers (if any). The specific rage power varies by cloak. It must be
usable by a 4th-level skald, and can't have any prerequisites other than level.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[good hope](ref/spell/good-hope), [rage](ref/spell/rage), creator must have the bardic performance
or raging song class feature; **Cost** 4,875 gp
