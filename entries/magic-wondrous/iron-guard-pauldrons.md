**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 60\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 5,750 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

These simple, leather-strapped iron pauldrons are engraved with intricate images of icy winds and
howling winter wolves, and are worn by senior members of the military order that serves
[Baba Yaga's](ref/monster/baba-yaga) interests across Irrisen. Iron guard pauldrons grant their
wearer a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws against fire effects. If the wearer succeeds at a
saving throw against an enemy's fire effect, the pauldrons shimmer with blue light for 5 rounds.

The wearer also gains a +2 competence bonus on [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks. This bonus
increases to +5 while the pauldrons cast blue light.

The wearer of the Iron Guard pauldrons gains the benefits of
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),_ but only for cold temperatures.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance); **Cost** 2,875 gp
