**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 21\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 600 gp; **Weight** -

Horror dust is the compressed stuff of nightmares. It is contained in a glass vial that can be
[thrown as a splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. It
shatters on impact, creating a cloud of hallucinogenic magic in a 20-foot radius that causes victims
to suffer terrible visions and hear phantom screams. Targets in this area must succeed at a DC 13
Will save or be [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1d6 rounds. If a creature is directly struck with the
horror dust and fails its save, it is also [confused](ref/misc/confused) for 1 round. Goblinoids
actually enjoy the visions created by horror dust, and are immune to its fear effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear); **Cost** 300 gp
