**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 107\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 24,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The wearer of efreeti horns gains a +4 enhancement bonus to Wisdom. In addition, the wearer can
spend 1 ki point as a full-round action to gain the effects of
_[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person)_ (self only) or
_[reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person)_ (self only) for 1 minute. These effects apply to the
wearer regardless of her actual creature type.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person), [owl's wisdom](ref/spell/owls-wisdom),
[reduce person](ref/spell/reduce-person); **Cost** 12,000 gp
