**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 226\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 6,800 gp; **Weight** -

The thick glass lens of this golden-framed monocle is attached to the wearer by a weighty chain.
This monocle helps draw out the truth of things, and was originally created to aid in the
investigation of crimes and the questioning of witnesses. Twice per day on command, it can create a
_[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth),_ as per the spell. In addition, this monocle grants its
wearer a +5 competence bonus on [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks. Though it fits over
only one of the wearer's eyes, the item takes up the entire eyes slot.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[zone of truth](ref/spell/zone-of-truth); **Cost** 3,400 gp
