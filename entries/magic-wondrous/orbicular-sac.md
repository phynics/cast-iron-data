**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 13\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 500 gp; **Weight** -

Breaking open this magically enhanced spider egg sac (a standard action) conjures a massive spider
web in a square adjacent to the user. The web is 5 feet thick and up to 20 feet wide and high. The
spider web is nearly invisible; spotting it requires a successful DC 20
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) check in bright light. This DC increases by 4 for
[every step below bright light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in which the spider web is placed. This
effect otherwise acts as the _web_ spell (DC 13). Alternatively, the user can enhance a web created
by another orbicular sac. Each additional sac increases the duration of the web by 30 minutes and
the Reflex and [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) DCs by 2 (maximum increase of +10).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility), [web](ref/spell/web); **Cost** 250 gp
