**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This [concertina](ref/eq-misc/musical-instrument-common) was created by the notorious Chelish bard
Vidlian the Reveler. It can be used like a masterwork concertina, providing a +2 bonus on
[Perform (keyboard)](ref/skill/perform-any) checks. At any one time, Master Vidlian's squeeze-box is
either more compressed than extended or more extended than compressed, and switching between the two
is a free action (and in fact, happens regularly when the instrument is played). When the concertina
is compressed and held in a hand, the wielder gains the [compression](ref/umr/compression) ability.
When the concertina is extended and held in a hand, the wielder is immune to constriction damage (as
from [constrict](ref/umr/constrict) attacks) and damage from being buried, although the wielder
gains no protection from [suffocation](ref/rule/suffocation).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cushioning bands](ref/spell/cushioning-bands), [grease](ref/spell/grease); **Cost** 3,000 gp
