**Sources:** Aquatic Adventures pg 63\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This delicate spherical blood coral compass has a longer outer needle and a shorter inner needle.
When the holder activates it, the outer needle points in the direction of the nearest tide or
current within 1 mile, changing color from red to violet to indicate the relative distance (red
meaning within 30 feet and violet meaning almost 1 mile away) and the inner needle points in the
direction of the tide or current, changing color from red to violet based on the relative speed (red
meaning extremely turbulent, up to 200 feet per round, and violet meaning less than 5 feet per
round).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[locate object](ref/spell/locate-object), [slipstream](ref/spell/slipstream); **Cost** 1,000 gp
