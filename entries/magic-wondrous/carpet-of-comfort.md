**Sources:** People of the Sands pg 29\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 90,000 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This specialized [carpet of flying](ref/magic-wondrous/carpet-of-flying) always measures 10 feet by
10 feet. When it's in flight, its passengers are shielded from wind and extreme temperatures as if
inside a _[tiny hut](ref/spell/tiny-hut)_ (though without creating an opaque, immobile sphere). In
addition, once per day while the carpet of comfort is on the ground, a command word transforms the
carpet into an opulent silk tent that, other than its appearance, is equivalent to a
_[secure shelter](ref/spell/secure-shelter)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight), [secure shelter](ref/spell/secure-shelter),
[tiny hut](ref/spell/tiny-hut); **Cost** 45,000 gp
