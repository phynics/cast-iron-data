## Kitsune Star Gem

**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 193\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st (1st), 3rd (2nd), or 5th (3rd)\
**Slot** none; **Price** 750 gp (1st), 3,000 gp (2nd), 6,750 gp (3rd); **Weight** -

This magical jewel glows like [faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire), shedding light as a
[candle](ref/eq-misc/candle). It acts as [pearl of power](ref/magic-wondrous/pearl-of-power), except
it only allows a [kitsune](ref/race/kitsune) to recover a spent use of a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of the appropriate spell level.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must be a kitsune;
**Cost** 365 gp (1st), 1,500 gp (2nd), or 3,375 gp (3rd)
