**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 229, Pathfinder \#3: The Hook Mountain Massacre pg 33, Rise of
the Runelords Anniversary Edition pg 420\
**Aura** faint abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These soft leather boots confer several powers upon the wearer. First, he is granted the power to
walk on water in swampy environments, provided the water is no deeper than 5 feet - this effectively
lets him move through swampy terrain and mud at no cost to his speed. He leaves no tracks or other
signs of his passage as long as he's in swampy terrain, and never becomes uncomfortable or wet from
rain, fog, or other forms of non-freezing precipitation. Finally, the boots grant him a +2
resistance bonus on all Fortitude saves made against poison and disease.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance), [water walk](ref/spell/water-walk); **Cost** 1,750 gp
