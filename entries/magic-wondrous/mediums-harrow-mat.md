**Sources:** The Harrow Handbook pg 31\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This harrow mat allows a harrower to tap into the ancestral knowledge of nearby spirits. If a
harrower uses this mat as an additional focus component while casting
[harrowing](ref/spell/harrowing) or [greater harrowing](ref/spell/harrowing-greater), she treats her
caster level as 1 higher. When this mat is used to perform a harrowing (magical or not) over a grave
or other burial site, the spirit of the interned body influences the harrowing; for each card drawn
related to the past, the harrower can draw from the deck twice and choose which card to place.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead); **Cost** 1,250 gp
