**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 350, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 542

**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** body; **Weight** 1 lb.

A robe of powerlessness appears to be a magic robe of another sort. As soon as a character dons this
garment, she takes a -10 penalty to Strength, as well as to Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma,
forgetting spells and magic knowledge accordingly. If the character is a spellcaster, the robe
targets the character's primary spellcasting score, otherwise it targets Intelligence. The robe can
be removed easily, but in order to restore mind and body, the character must receive a
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell followed by [heal](ref/spell/heal).
