**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** -

This large, furry hat is sewn from the thick pelt of an animal native to the frozen north, and has
earflaps that can be pulled down or folded up over the crown of the hat. When worn, an ushanka of
the northlands grants its wearer a +2 resistance bonus on saving throws against
[cold effects](ref/rule/cold-dangers) and a +5 competence bonus on [Survival](ref/skill/survival)
checks. When the earflaps are pulled down, the wearer gains a +2 bonus on saving throws against
sonic effects, but also takes a -2 penalty on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks made to
hear. Pulling the earflaps down is a move action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance), creator must have 5 ranks in Survival; **Cost** 2,250 gp
