**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 357

**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 71,300 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This headband of vast intelligence +6 also holds a consciousness that shares its knowledge and
education with a suitable wearer. This intelligence can be of any alignment and its only
[purpose](ref/rule/special-purpose-items) is to share its vast knowledge with its wearer. The
headband also functions as a
[helm of comprehend languages and read magic](ref/magic-wondrous/helm-of-comprehend-languages-and-read-magic).
Furthermore, instead of granting the normal skill ranks of a
[headband of vast intelligence +6](ref/magic-wondrous/headband-of-vast-intelligence-6), this
headband grants 4 ranks in six skills, but all of those skills must be
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skills. It grants such intelligence by a stream of constant
whispers and arguments that the headband communicates telepathically in Common or in one of 10 other
languages it knows. Once per week on command, the wearer can also convince the headband to use
_[legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore)_ as the spell, though the casting time is only a standard
action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages), [fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning),
[legend lore](ref/spell/legend-lore), [read magic](ref/spell/read-magic); **Price** 35,650 gp

### Statistics

**Alignment** varies; **Ego** 20\
**Senses** 30 ft.\
**Int** 20, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 12\
**Communication** telepathy (Common and 10 other languages)
