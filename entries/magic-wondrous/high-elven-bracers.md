**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These finely crafted leather bracers have plates of silvered steel covering the back, and silver
buckles at the wrist and forearm. The bracers enable the wearer to use any traditional elven weapon
([longbows](ref/eq-weapon/longbow), [composite longbows](ref/eq-weapon/composite-longbow),
[longswords](ref/eq-weapon/longsword), [rapiers](ref/eq-weapon/rapier),
[shortbows](ref/eq-weapon/shortbow), [composite shortbows](ref/eq-weapon/composite-shortbow), and
any weapon with the word "elven" in its name) as if he were proficient in its use. If he uses such a
weapon and is already proficient with it, he gains a +1 competence bonus on attack and damage rolls.
Both bracers must be worn for the magic to be effective.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor), creator must be an
[elf](ref/race/elf); **Cost** 15,000 gp
