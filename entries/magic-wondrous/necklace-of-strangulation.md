**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 347, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 541

**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** neck; **Weight** -

A necklace of strangulation appears to be a wondrous piece of magical jewelry. When placed on the
neck, the necklace immediately tightens, dealing 6 points of damage per round. It cannot be removed
by any means short of a [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), [wish](ref/spell/wish), or
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle), and remains clasped around the victim's throat even after his death.
Only when he has decayed to a dry skeleton (after approximately 1 month) does the necklace loosen,
ready for another victim.
