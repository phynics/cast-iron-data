**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,150 gp; **Weight** -

This small silver cylinder is no bigger than a pinhead and is engraved with an Elven aphorism that
roughly translates to "unhindered flight." A silver nocking point can be attached to the string of
any bow or crossbow. When using a weapon equipped with a silver nocking point, a character can treat
penalties for [wind conditions](ref/rule/winds) as though they were one step less severe. For
example, a character firing a ranged projectile in severe wind conditions would fire at a ranged
penalty of -2 instead of -4. Magical wind effects that would normally deflect the projectile (such
as [fickle winds](ref/spell/fickle-winds)) instead impose only a -8 penalty on the attack roll.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike); **Cost** 1,575 gp
