**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

When a block of incense is placed inside this censer and ignited, the bearer gains the benefits of a
heightened 2nd-level _[sanctuary](ref/spell/sanctuary)_ spell for as long as the incense burns (1
hour per stick of incense) or until the bearer breaks the effect by attacking. Placing the incense
is a move action, and igniting it is a standard action. If
[incense of meditation](ref/magic-wondrous/incense-of-meditation) is burned instead of normal
incense, the sanctuary effect is heightened to 7th level.

While lighting the censer, the bearer can expend one use of mythic power to grant the benefits of
the censer's sanctuary to all allies within a 20-foot-radius [emanation](ref/misc/emanation) of the
censer for 10 minutes. Allies who attack break the sanctuary effect only for themselves. The incense
extinguishes 1 round after the bearer ceases to use. If incense of meditation is extinguished before
8 hours of burning, the remainder becomes normal incense.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell), [Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter),
[sanctuary](ref/spell/sanctuary); **Cost** 9,000 gp
