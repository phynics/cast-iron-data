**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 25\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 46,000 gp; **Weight** -

This black velvet eye patch is attached to an onyx chain that is worn around the head. The eye patch
grants its wearer [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet. Additionally, as a
standard action, the wearer can speak the eye patch's command word to see through powerful magical
darkness, such as an area of [deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness), for up to 11 minutes per
day. This duration need not be used all at once, but it must be used in 1-minute increments. Ending
this effect is a free action.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision), [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 23,000 gp
