## Belt of Physical Perfection

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 210, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 502\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 16th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 16,000 gp (+2), 64,000 gp (+4), 144,000 gp (+6); **Weight** 1 lb.

This belt has a large platinum buckle and grants the wearer an enhancement bonus to all physical
ability scores (Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution) of +2, +4, or +6. Treat this as a
[temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace); **Cost** 8,000 gp (+2), 32,000 gp (+4), 77,000 gp (+6)
