**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 147\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 60,000 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

This belt looks like nothing more than iron chains connecting large pieces of slate. It grants its
wearer a +5 inherent bonus to [combat maneuver](ref/rule/combat-maneuvers) defense against bull
rush, drag, grapple, overrun, reposition, and trip combat maneuvers and a +5 inherent bonus on
saving throws against any effect that attempts to move the wearer with physical force or energy.
Furthermore, if the wearer is a mythic creature and targeted by an effect that would teleport her,
she can choose whether or not she wishes to be teleported.

As a standard action, the wearer can expend one use of mythic power to create a 30-foot-radius aura
that affects all enemy creatures within it as though they were subject to a
_[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor)_ spell. This aura lasts for a number of rounds
equal to the wearer's mythic tier.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength),
[dimensional anchor](ref/spell/dimensional-anchor); **Cost** 30,000 gp
