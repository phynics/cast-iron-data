**Sources:** Ships of the Inner Sea pg 45\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 40,000 gp; **Weight** -

These talismans are crafted by evil spellcasters on the first steps of the path of the
[wendigo](ref/monster/wendigo). The amulet, a tiny human figure formed from unmelting ice, rests on
the wearer's chest. When worn, it freezes the wearer's heart solid, chilling body and soul. The
wearer immediately develops hypothermia, becoming permanently [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for as
long as he wears the amulet, but is otherwise protected against cold weather by a constant
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ effect. The amulet also grants 10 points of cold
resistance, immunity to [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) effects, and a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws
against spells with the emotion descriptor. Once per day as a standard action, the wearer can use
_[frigid touch](ref/spell/frigid-touch)_ as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).
Removing the amulet thaws the wearer's heart but leaves him fatigued until he spends 8 hours warming
up.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[calm emotions](ref/spell/calm-emotions), [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),
[frigid touch](ref/spell/frigid-touch); **Cost** 21,600 gp
