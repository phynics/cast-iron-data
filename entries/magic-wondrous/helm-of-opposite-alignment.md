**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 347, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 540

**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** head; **Weight** 3 lbs.

When placed upon the head, this item's curse immediately takes effect (Will DC 15 negates). On a
failed save, the alignment of the wearer is radically altered to an alignment as different as
possible from the former alignment - good to evil, chaotic to lawful, neutral to some extreme
commitment (LE, LG, CE, or CG). Alteration in alignment is mental as well as moral, and the
individual changed by the magic thoroughly enjoys his new outlook. A character who succeeds on his
save can continue to wear the helmet without suffering the effect of the curse, but if he takes it
off and later puts it on again, another save is required.

Only a [wish](ref/spell/wish) or a [miracle](ref/spell/miracle) can restore a character's former
alignment, and the affected individual does not make any attempt to return to the former alignment.
In fact, he views the prospect with horror and avoids it in any way possible. If a character of a
class with an alignment requirement is affected, an [atonement](ref/spell/atonement) spell is needed
as well if the curse is to be obliterated. When a _helm of opposite alignment_ has functioned once,
it loses its magical properties.
