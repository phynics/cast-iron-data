**Sources:** Pathfinder \#67: The Snows of Summer pg 60\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

The artistic scrawls on the dark surface of this small bronze cauldron depict an array of exotic
animals, legendary beasts, leering fiends, and hosts of elementals. Once per day, if used as the
focus or divine focus for a _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ or
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spell, the cauldron automatically conjures
1d3 additional creatures of the same kind from the next lower level list. For example, if a user
casts [summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) to summon 1d3 wolves, he could also summon
1d3 additional wolves (or other creatures from the 2nd-level list).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (conjuration), [summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4)
or [summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4); **Cost** 2,250 gp
