**Sources:** Pathfinder \#128: Songbird, Scion, Saboteur pg 36\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 750 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This thick copper bracelet bears rows of lightly engraved hearts. Once per week, when the bracelet
is worn and touched to another worn lover's bangle, the two become attuned and remain attuned until
one of the bangles is removed or attuned to a different lover's bangle, or until a bangle's wearer
is reduced below 0 hit points, is slain, or travels to a different plane. The wearer of the attuned
lover's bangle immediately knows that the attunement has ended, but doesn't receive any indication
about the event that broke the attunement.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [status](ref/spell/status);
**Cost** 375 gp
