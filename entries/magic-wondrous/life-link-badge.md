**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 308\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

When this plain leather badge is sewn onto a [summoner's](ref/class/summoner) armor or clothing, a
faded image of his summoner rune appears on its surface. The summoner can add charges to the badge
by expending summoner spells slots; the badge gains charges equal to the spell level of the slot
expended (maximum 5 charges). Adding charges to the badge is a standard action (regardless of the
spell level expended). When the summoner uses his life link ability, instead of sacrificing his own
hit points, he may expend charges from the badge to heal the eidolon 1d6 hit points per charge. The
summoner does not have to decide how many charges are spent beforehand (he may expend one charge,
roll, and decide whether or not to spend more charges for more healing). All charges in the badge
are lost when the summoner readies spells after resting.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[rejuvenate eidolon](ref/spell/rejuvenate-eidolon); **Cost** 3,000 gp
