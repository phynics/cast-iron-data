**Sources:** Merchant's Manifest pg 15\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 7,400 gp; **Weight** -

These dark blue, silken gloves are fingerless. Twice per day as a move action, the wearer can touch
the ground or any other solid surface and say the command word to visibly reveal all footprints
within 20 feet. When the gloves reveal footprints in this way, the wearer gains a +5 bonus on
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks to follow those tracks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[residual tracking](ref/spell/residual-tracking); **Cost** 3,700 gp
