**Sources:** Plunder and Peril pg 33\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,600 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This iridescent spiraling shell can be blown once per day as a standard action to activate any one
of these functions:

- Calm rough waters in a 40-foot radius. This reduces the DCs of [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks in
  the area by 5 (to a minimum of 10). This effect lasts for 5 minutes.
- Summon up to six [hippocampi](ref/monster/hippocampus). The creatures appear with bit, bridle, and
  a riding saddle crafted for aquatic use. They serve willingly and well, entering combat and
  risking attack unflinchingly if a rider directs them to do so (treat them as combat-trained
  mounts), though they don't make attacks of their own. These hippocampi serve the PCs for up to 2
  hours.
- Create a soothing effect in a 40-foot radius that affects only animals and magical beasts with
  Intelligence scores of 1 or 2 that also have the aquatic or amphibious subtype. This otherwise
  functions as _[calm animals](ref/spell/calm-animals)_ (Will DC 12 negates).

The sounding of this magical horn can be heard by all tritons within a 1-mile radius.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[calm animals](ref/spell/calm-animals), [communal mount](ref/spell/mount-communal),
[slipstream](ref/spell/slipstream), creator must be a triton; **Cost** 3,300 gp
