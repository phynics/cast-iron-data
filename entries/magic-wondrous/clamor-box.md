**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 289, Advanced Player's Guide pg 303\
**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This delicate tin music box can produce a great volume of noise at a specific time determined by its
owner. Four command words determine what sorts of sounds it is to play (battle, fire, massacre, or
riot), and a fifth determines the delay (anywhere from 1 round to 20 minutes) until the box begins
to produce the desired sound. The noise from the box is as loud as 40 people. The sound persists for
2d6 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound); **Cost** 1,000 gp
