**Sources:** Lost Treasures pg 63\
**Aura** strong enchantment (compulsion); **CL** 17th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 50,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This collection of twigs and dinosaur teeth can be strung through with a length of sinew to create a
necklace that offers limited protection against dangerous animals. The bearer of the Zoic of the
Primeval gains a +1 insight bonus to AC against all non-humanoids. Against animals and outsiders,
this AC bonus increases to +2.

Additionally, once per day the wearer can use the Zoic of the Primeval to dominate any creature of
the animal or outsider type as by _[dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster)_ (Will DC 23
negates).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster); **Cost** 25,000 gp
