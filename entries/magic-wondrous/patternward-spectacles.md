**Sources:** Daughters of Fury pg 62\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 750 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These spectacles fit firmly over eyes and are held in place by two crystalline stems. The lenses are
initially clear and don't hinder the wearer's vision. When the wearer is targeted with an illusion
spell or [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of the pattern subschool (such as
[color spray](ref/spell/color-spray)), the spectacles absorb the magic and darken, protecting the
wearer from the pattern effect. Once the spectacles absorb a spell and become opaque, the wearer
becomes [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) until the spectacles are removed (a swift action). Patternward
spectacles protect against spells or spell-like abilities of only 3rd level or lower; higher-level
effects affect the wearer as normal. Once the spectacles absorb an effect, they lose their magic.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[lesser globe of invulnerability](ref/spell/globe-of-invulnerability-lesser); **Cost** 375 gp
