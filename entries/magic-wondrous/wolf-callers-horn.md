**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 225\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The user can sound this ornate silver horn as a normal signal horn. Once per day as a standard
action, the user can make the horn emit an eerie, canine howl. The sound entices up to four
[wolves](ref/monster/wolf) within 200 feet to answer the howl. This includes
[dire wolves](ref/monster/wolf-dire-wolf), [winter wolves](ref/monster/worg-winter-wolf),
[wolves](ref/monster/wolf), [worgs](ref/monster/worg), and [dogs](ref/monster/dog), as well as any
creatures capable of turning into wolves with the change shape ability (such as
[barghests](ref/monster/barghest), [vampires](ref/template/vampire), or
[werewolves](ref/template/lycanthrope)). If there are more than four such creatures within the area,
those that are closest to the user are affected first.

The subjects can't take any actions other than to howl, making them effectively
[dazed](ref/misc/dazed) for 1 round. A successful DC 17 Will save negates this effect. The horn
affects werewolves in any form, though werewolves who are aware of their condition receive a +5
bonus on the saving throw while in humanoid form.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[mass daze](ref/spell/daze-mass), [mass suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion-mass); **Cost** 2,000 gp
