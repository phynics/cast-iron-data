**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 268, Gods and Magic pg 59\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 2,300 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This brightly colored robe is made of fine silk and cut in the Vudrani style. As a full-round
action, the wearer can shift the original hue of the garment slightly (from red to pink or crimson,
from blue to cyan or sapphire, and so on) or return it to its original color; the robe instantly
reverts to its original color if the wearer removes the robe. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus
on [Perform (dance)](ref/skill/perform-any) checks.

If [Irori](ref/faith/irori) is the wearer's patron, once per day as a free action the wearer can
touch a tiny button on the wrist of the robe, granting her the effects of _[haste](ref/spell/haste)_
for 1 round. Once per day, the wearer can also perform a healing dance that lasts 5 minutes. When
she does so, up to 7 allies within 30 feet heal 1 hit point.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds), [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self),
[haste](ref/spell/haste), creator must have 5 ranks in Perform (dance); **Cost** 1,150 gp
