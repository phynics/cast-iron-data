**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 46,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This grotesque collar is useful to any [witch](ref/class/witch) who is eager to form a coven, but
would rather avoid associating with one or more evil [hags](ref/family/hag). The collar is a crude
band of iron from which hang the fist-sized shrunken heads of three hags (an
[annis hag](ref/monster/hag-annis-hag), a [green hag](ref/monster/hag-green-hag), and a
[sea hag](ref/monster/hag-sea-hag)). The heads remain semi-animate - they occasionally twitch,
blink, coo softly, or whisper curses at the wearer, each other, or anyone else nearby. A witch with
the [coven](ref/hex/coven) hex who wears the collar gains access to the following powers.

> **Champion of the Unbound Coven:** If the wearer joins a coven, the coven can ignore the
> requirement to have at least one hag as a member. Each of the coven members must still have the
> coven hex, and one of the members must be at least a 9th-level witch.
>
> **Dreadful Gaze:** Three times per day as a swift action, the wearer can command the hags' heads
> to lock their terrible gazes on one humanoid within 30 feet. The target of this gaze attack must
> succeed at a DC 15 Will save or become paralyzed with terror (as the effect of the
> _[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person)_ spell). This is a mind-affecting fear effect.
>
> **Hag Form:** Once per day as a standard action, the wearer can assume the form of a powerfully
> built Medium crone that resembles the horrific union of an annis hag, a green hag, and a sea hag.
> This metamorphosis is identical to the _[transformation](ref/spell/transformation)_ spell,
> including the violent change to the wearer's mindset and the loss of spellcasting ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Craft (leather)](ref/skill/craft-any) 4 ranks, [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self),
[hold person](ref/spell/hold-person), [transformation](ref/spell/transformation), coven hex;
**Cost** 23,000 gp
