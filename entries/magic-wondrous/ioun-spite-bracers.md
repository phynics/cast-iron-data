**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These bracers find particular use by [Aspis](ref/rule/aff-profiteers) agents who often tangle with
the [Pathfinder Society](ref/rule/aff-scholars). As a standard action, a creature wearing these
bracers can attempt a [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver check to nab any orbiting
[ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone) within 30 feet, though the ioun stone's owner receives a
+4 circumstance bonus to her CMD. If the bracers' wearer succeeds, the stolen ioun stone embeds
itself in one of the bracers' four available slots (each bracer has two). Embedded ioun stones
provide their bonuses to the wearer and can be removed with a move action.

As a standard action, the wearer can temporarily drain the power of any ioun stone that is embedded
in the bracers and isn't [dull gray](ref/magic-ioun/dull-gray-stone), and can then channel that
power to cast _[magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), firing three missiles at a target or targets up
to 160 feet away. Drained ioun stones become dull gray for the next 2d4 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile), [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Cost** 7,500 gp
