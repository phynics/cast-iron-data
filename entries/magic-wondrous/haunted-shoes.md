**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 231\
**Aura** faint conjuration and illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 6,480 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Two times per day, the wearer of these shoes can call forth 1d4 spirit servants to aid her. The
spirit servants are invisible, mindless, shapeless beings that act as servants summoned by the
_[unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant)_ spell. The spirits return to the shoes after 3 hours
have passed. The wearer can choose to dismiss one or more of the spirit servants early.

Once per day, the wearer can command the ghostly spirits inside her haunted shoes to emerge and
swirl around her, making her difficult to target in combat. The wearer gains
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (20% miss chance) for 3 minutes. The spirits cannot be harmed,
but a [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) spell ignores the concealment effect. The concealment
effect cannot be activated while any of the spirits are currently being used as unseen servants.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [blur](ref/spell/blur),
[unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant); **Cost** 3,240 gp
