**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** -

This pewter chain necklace is formed from alternating sizes of metal links, creating a delicate but
imposing look. Short enough to be a choker, it is set in the middle with a brilliant jasper stone
that is finely cut and shines even when light is scarce. Whenever the wearer fails a saving throw
against an effect that results in paralysis, the periapt instead causes the wearer to be
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for 1d4 rounds. If an effect would paralyze the wearer without a
saving throw, he is instead fatigued until he removes the condition. If the wearer fails a saving
throw against a paralyzing effect while fatigued, he becomes [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted).
Characters with the exhausted condition gain no benefits from a periapt of proof against paralysis.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[remove paralysis](ref/spell/remove-paralysis); **Cost** 4,000 gp
