**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 265\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 4,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This heavy leather belt is reinforced with metal and detailed to resemble a gut wound that has
sprouted teeth. In addition to functioning as a
[belt of mighty constitution +2](ref/magic-wondrous/belt-of-mighty-constitution-2), the mouth on the
belt animates whenever the wearer gains the [grappled](ref/rule/grapple-conditions) condition,
damaging the wearer's opponent as if it were [armor spikes](ref/eq-armorenh/armor-spikes) (1d6
points of damage for a Medium wearer, 1d4 for Small). Similar to armor spikes, the mouth can be made
into a magic weapon in its own right; it can also be temporarily enhanced with magic such as
[magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon). The wearer can use the mouth to eat meat, gaining nutrition
from it as if he had eaten with his own mouth (the mouth refuses any food other than meat).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance);
**Cost** 2,250 gp
