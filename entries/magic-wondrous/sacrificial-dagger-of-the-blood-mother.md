**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 268, Gods and Magic pg 59\
**Aura** faint necromancy \[evil\]; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,700 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This dagger is usually made of chiseled stone, though some varieties are made of sharpened mammoth
bone. Its hilt is especially long, as if created for a Large creature, and the blade is carved with
a symbol of a woman holding meat in one hand and a stone tablet in the other. The dagger is
symbolic, and cannot be used as an actual weapon. Once per day, the wielder can use
_[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants)._

If the giant goddess [Fandarra](ref/faith/fandarra) is the wielder's patron, once per day the
wielder can use _[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell)_ if he offers the target's blood to the
goddess.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[death knell](ref/spell/death-knell),
[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants); **Cost** 1,350 gp
