**Aura** faint divination and evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 40,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

When lit, this [hooded lantern](ref/eq-misc/hooded-lantern) shines brightly in a 60-foot radius, as
_[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)._ Once per day as a swift action, if the owner worships Sarenrae or
is a good-aligned divine spellcaster, she can intensify the lantern's light for 1 hour. This
intensified light [dazzles](ref/misc/dazzled) evil outsiders and undead in the area, and evil
incorporeal creatures take 75% damage from magical attacks instead of 50%. If an illuminated
creature or object has a mark indicating evil allegiance - such as a
[demoniac's](ref/pclass/demoniac) demonic mark - that mark glow brilliantly, shining through
anything short of magical concealments.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[daylight](ref/spell/daylight), [detect evil](ref/spell/detect-evil), creator must worship
[Sarenrae](ref/faith/sarenrae); **Cost** 20,000 gp
