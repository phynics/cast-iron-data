**Sources:** Pathfinder \#74: Sword of Valor pg 63\
**Aura** moderate evocation \[evil\]; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

Shadowblood is among the most notorious of drugs - a fluid infused with a
[shadow demon's](ref/monster/demon-shadow-demon) essence. Anyone who drinks this substance must
succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or take 1d6 points of cold damage and 1d4 points of
[Wisdom damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), and become [nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1d6
rounds. Thereafter, the target gains 1d10+5 temporary hit points, cold resistance 5, and
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet. These effects persist for 1 hour, during which demons
take a -4 penalty on saving throws against the drinker's spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of the shadow subschool.

Shadowblood is exceedingly addictive and carries some significant disadvantages. Each time a
creature takes a dose of shadowblood, it must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or become addicted -
shadowblood addiction is a [severe addiction](ref/rule/addiction). A creature under the influence of
shadowblood or suffering from shadowblood addiction takes a -4 penalty on all saving throws against
possession type effects made by demons, and can never gain the benefits of
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) or similar spells against such effects.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[desecrate](ref/spell/desecrate), [shadow evocation](ref/spell/shadow-evocation), creator must be a
shadow demon or must have the cooperation of a shadow demon; **Cost** 750 gp
