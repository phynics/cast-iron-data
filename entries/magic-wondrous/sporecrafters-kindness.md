**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 29\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This brown hemp sack is filled with mold spores, soil, and chemical reagents. It can be
[thrown as a splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) with a range increment of 10 feet. Upon
breaking open, a sack of sporecrafter's kindness releases [russet mold](ref/monster/vegepygmy)
spores in a 5-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) and unleashes 1d4+1 vegepygmies in the nearest
adjacent squares. The vegepygmies are not under the control of the user, and immediately attack the
nearest creatures for 5 rounds, after which they collapse into nonmagical plant matter.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [poison](ref/spell/poison),
[summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3); **Cost** 750 gp
