## Mask of Giants (Lesser)

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg. 245-6, Advanced Player's Guide pg 307\
**Aura** moderate transmutation (lesser), strong transmutation (greater); **CL** 6th (lesser), 13th
(greater)\
**Slot** head; **Price** 30,000 gp (lesser), 90,000 gp (greater); **Weight** 1 lb.

This wooden mask depicts a leering humanoid with an oversized nose and ears. If the wearer has the
wild shape class feature, the mask allows her to use wild shape to take the form of a humanoid with
the giant subtype. The forms allowed by a lesser mask of giants are [ogre](ref/monster/ogre),
[troll](ref/monster/troll), [fire giant](ref/monster/giant-fire-fire-giant),
[frost giant](ref/monster/giant-frost-frost-giant), and
[stone giant](ref/monster/giant-stone-giant). If the form has any of the following abilities, the
wearer gains the listed ability: [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet,
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent). In giant form, the wearer
gains a +4 size bonus to Strength, a -2 penalty to Dexterity, and a +1 natural armor bonus.

### Mask of Giants (Greater)

A greater mask of giants has all the powers of a lesser mask of giants, plus it grants additional
abilities in giant form. If the form has any of the following abilities, the wearer gains the listed
ability: [rend](ref/umr/rend) (2d6 damage), [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) 5 (acid or fire),
[rock catching](ref/umr/rock-catching), [rock throwing](ref/umr/rock-throwing) (range 60 feet, 2d6
damage). If the chosen giant form has immunity or resistance to any energy type, the wearer gains
resistance 20 to that energy type when in giant form. If the giant form has vulnerability to an
energy type, the wearer gains that vulnerability when in giant form. In giant form, the wearer gains
a +6 size bonus to Strength, a -2 penalty to Dexterity, a +4 size bonus to Constitution, and a +4
natural armor bonus.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[giant form I](ref/spell/giant-form-1); **Cost** 15,000 gp (lesser), 45,000 gp (greater)
