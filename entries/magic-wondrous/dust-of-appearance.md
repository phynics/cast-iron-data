**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 293, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 510\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,800 gp; **Weight** -

This powder appears to be a very fine, very light metallic dust. A single handful of this substance
flung into the air coats objects within a 10-foot radius, making them visible even if they are
invisible. It likewise negates the effects of [blur](ref/spell/blur) and
[displacement](ref/spell/displacement). In this, it works just like the
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire) spell. The dust also reveals [figments](ref/spelldef/figment),
[mirror images](ref/spell/mirror-image), and [projected images](ref/spell/project-image) for what
they are. A creature coated with the dust takes a -30 penalty on its [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
checks. The dust's effect lasts for 5 minutes.

Dust of appearance is typically stored in small silk packets or hollow bone tubes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[glitterdust](ref/spell/glitterdust); **Cost** 900 gp
