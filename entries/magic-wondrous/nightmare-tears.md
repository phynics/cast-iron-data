**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 250 gp; **Weight** -

Nightmare tears are a toxic alchemical solution that, when applied to the eyes, allows the
individual to see past certain supernatural effects, as the spell
_[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing)._ These include reality-warping effects such as those caused
by the [Plateau of Leng](ref/rule/leng) and spells or supernatural powers that create phantasms such
as [weird](ref/spell/weird), [nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare), and
[phantasmal killer](ref/spell/phantasmal-killer). The effect of the tears lasts for 1 hour.

Nightmare tears have horrific side effects - users suffer both temporary blindness and insanity.
Immediately after applying the tears, the user goes [blind](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d4 rounds and
loses 1d4 points of Wisdom until the tears wear off. However, if the user fails a DC 11 Will save,
both effects become permanent.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[blindness](ref/spell/blindness-deafness), [true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 125 gp
