**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 285\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 90,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This device appears to be a normal container for holding burning coals. The brazier must be filled
with coals or wood and a fire lit within it to function. On command as a full-round action, the
bearer may summon a fire elemental. If 1 pound of brimstone (sulfur) is burning in the brazier, it
conjures a [Huge fire elemental](ref/monster/fire-elemental) as if using
_[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6)._ Otherwise, the brazier conjures a
[Large fire elemental](ref/monster/fire-elemental) as if using
_[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5)._ Conjuring an elemental extinguishes the fire in
the brazier. The brazier cannot summon another elemental until the previous one is dismissed,
killed, or the duration of its summoning expires.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5), [summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6);
**Cost** 45,000 gp
