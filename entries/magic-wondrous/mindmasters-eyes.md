**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 226\
**Aura** strong enchantment; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 95,000 gp; **Weight** -

This pair of thin and faceted ruby crystals fit over the wearer's eyes. On command, the wearer can
use _[charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster)_ (Will DC 19 to negate), or once per day the wearer
can use _[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person)_ (Will DC 20 to negate) instead.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[charm monster](ref/spell/charm-monster), [dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person); **Cost**
47,500 gp
