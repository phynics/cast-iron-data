**Sources:** Lost Kingdoms pg 51\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 6th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 9,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Made of interwoven wicker strands and insect-excreted resin, these horrific masks have the
appearance of demonic insects. The mask's wearer is rendered immune to swarms' distraction special
attack. Additionally, once per day the wearer can transform into an insect as per the spell
_[vermin shape I](ref/spell/vermin-shape-1)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[vermin shape I](ref/spell/vermin-shape-1); **Cost** 4,800 gp
