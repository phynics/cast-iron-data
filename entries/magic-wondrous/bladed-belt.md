**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 211\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Rather than being made of one continuous line of material, this belt consists of many bits of tanned
leather joined together by thick metal studs. On command, the belt's wearer can transform the belt
into a single [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) slashing and/or piercing melee weapon of her
choice. As long as she is holding the weapon, she can also revert it back to belt form with a
command. Furthermore, the belt can be enchanted like a piercing and slashing melee weapon, using the
cost of the belt as the cost of the masterwork item.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon); **Cost** 1,000 gp
