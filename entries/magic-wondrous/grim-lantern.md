**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 5,800 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This macabre lantern is composed of a humanoid skull dangling from a short length of chain. A small
fragment of soulstuff is trapped inside, burning slowly in a heatless flame. Its flickering light
shines from the skull's eye sockets, casting light as a
[bullseye lantern](ref/eq-misc/bullseye-lantern). On command, the lantern can consume its soulstuff
in a sudden blast of heat, as _[burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands)._ Doing so extinguishes the
lantern. Whenever a living creature with at least 1 HD is slain within 30 feet of an empty grim
lantern, the lantern absorbs a fragment of that creature's soul, restoring its glow.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[burning hands](ref/spell/burning-hands), creator cannot be good; **Cost** 2,900 gp
