**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 160\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 900 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This fizzy, sky-blue liquid leaves its drinker feeling buoyant, yet bloated. The drinker gains a +4
competence bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks made to make high or long jumps and on
[Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks for up to 1 hour after consuming the elixir. As a standard action, the
drinker can exhale a 15-foot-long _[gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind)._ The drinker can exhale
up to three gusts, after which the elixir's effect is discharged and the skill bonuses end. Unused
gusts dissipate after 1 hour.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind); **Cost** 450 gp
