**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 60,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This featureless gray stone is perfectly smooth and flawlessly polished. The bearer of an orb of
pure law gains a +4 deflection bonus to AC against attacks from chaotic creatures and a +4
resistance bonus on saving throws against polymorph effects, effects with the chaotic descriptor,
and effects that inflict the [confused](ref/misc/confused) condition.

When held in hand, the bearer of the orb may use _[detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos)_ and
_[detect law](ref/spell/detect-law)_ at will. A lawful creature may hold the orb in hand and command
it to invoke _[dispel chaos](ref/spell/dispel-chaos)_ and _[arrow of law](ref/spell/arrow-of-law)_
each once per day.

The orb bestows one permanent [negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on any
chaotic creature holding or carrying it. The negative level remains as long as the creature has the
orb and disappears immediately when the orb is no longer held or carried. This negative level cannot
be overcome in any way (including by [restoration](ref/spell/restoration) spells) while the bearer
has the orb.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[arrow of law](ref/spell/arrow-of-law), [detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos),
[detect law](ref/spell/detect-law), [dispel chaos](ref/spell/dispel-chaos); **Cost** 30,000 gp
