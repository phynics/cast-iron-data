## Thremyr's Beard

**Sources:** Pathfinder \#94: Ice Tomb of the Giant Queen pg 33\
**Aura** strong evocation; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 32,250 gp (Lesser), 42,250 gp (Greater); **Weight** 2 lbs.

This [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) helm has a faceplate resembling a
[frost giant's](ref/monster/giant-frost-frost-giant) face, complete with a beard formed from
layered, thin metal rods. While wearing a Thremyr's beard, a creature gains a +5 competence bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and a +2 circumstance bonus on
[Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks to impersonate a frost giant or
[svathurim](ref/monster/svathurim) (though the helm does nothing to increase the wearer's size).

Once per day as an immediate action after being successfully hit by a melee weapon, the wearer of a
lesser Thremyr's beard can order the helm to animate, whereupon the thin metal rods of the helm's
beard reach out to grasp the weapon. This functions as a [disarm](ref/rule/disarm) combat maneuver
that does not provoke an attack of opportunity.

A lesser Thremyr's beard has a CMB of +23; the wearer can use the helm's CMB or her own CMB,
whichever is higher.

A greater Thremyr's beard functions identically to the lesser version, but offers more protection
for the wearer's vulnerable neck. Once per day as an immediate action after a critical hit is
confirmed against the wearer with a melee weapon, the wearer can force the attacker to reroll the
confirmation roll and take the worse result.
