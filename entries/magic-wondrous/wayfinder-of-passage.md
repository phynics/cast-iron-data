**Sources:** Seekers of Secrets pg 51\
**Aura** moderate varied; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 136,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This [wayfinder](ref/magic-wondrous/wayfinder) is an artistic treasure, crafted in gold and set with
tiny opals, rubies, diamonds, and sapphires. Three slots on the inner side of the lid can each hold
one [ioun stone](ref/magic-wondrous/ioun-stone). It sheds light equivalent to the
_[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ spell on your command. It has the following abilities, each
activated by a different command word:

- _[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_ (once per day)
- _[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ (at will)
- _[knock](ref/spell/knock)_ (at will)
- _[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy)_ (once per day, energy resistance 20, lasts 24 hours)

When the first ioun stone slot is used, the _[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_ ability no
longer functions. When the second slot is used, _[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ and
_[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy)_ are unavailable. When the third slot is used,
_[daylight](ref/spell/daylight)_ and _[knock](ref/spell/knock)_ no longer function.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[daylight](ref/spell/daylight), [dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door),
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), [knock](ref/spell/knock),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy); **Cost** 68,000 gp
