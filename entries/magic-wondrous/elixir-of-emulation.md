**Sources:** Inner Sea Temples pg 62\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** -

A drinker must add hair, skin, fur, blood, or nail clippings of a single creature to this elixir
before drinking it, or it is expended uselessly. Once appropriate material has been added, the
elixir retains potency for 24 hours, after which it becomes an inert, valueless liquid. If drunk
while potent, the elixir functions as [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self), allowing the drinker to
assume the shape of the donor creature, which must be a living, corporeal, Small or Medium creature.
In addition, the elixir aids the drinker in duplicating the mannerisms of the donor creature,
granting her a +20 bonus on [Disguise](ref/skill/disguise) checks to pass herself off as that
creature and eliminating the penalty for masquerading as a different gender, race, or age. Once
during the elixir's duration, the drinker can try to learn information known by the donor creature
at the time its material was harvested, as if she had cast
[harvest knowledge](ref/spell/harvest-knowledge). An elixir of emulation lasts 2d6+12 hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts), [polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph); **Cost** 2,000 gp
