**Sources:** Pathfinder \#116: Fangs of War pg 44\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

What at first appears to be a fine gem dangling from a long, silver chain is in fact a cut and
polished bezoar. This necklace - often mistaken for a
[periapt of health](ref/magic-wondrous/periapt-of-health) or a
[periapt of proof against poison](ref/magic-wondrous/periapt-of-proof-against-poison) - inspires its
wearer with the utter conviction that he has been rendered immune to both disease and poison. While
the necklace imparts no genuine protections, the strength of belief it imparts nonetheless grants
its wearer a +2 morale bonus to all Fortitude saving throws against diseases and poisons.

Unless the person identifying a periapt of placebos succeeds at her
[Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) check by 5 or more, she mistakenly believes it to be a more
powerful protective necklace rather than discerning its true nature.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[delusional pride](ref/spell/delusional-pride); **Cost** 1,800 gp
