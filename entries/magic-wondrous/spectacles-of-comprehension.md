**Sources:** Disciple's Doctrine pg 30\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** -

These spectacles bear no markings or decoration, but they are obviously sturdy and of high quality.
The wearer of these spectacles gains the ability to better understand written material and unravel
the language of contracts. The wearer gains a +5 bonus on [Linguistics](ref/skill/linguistics)
checks to decipher writing, detect forgeries, research in a library, research an infernal contract,
or attempt similar types of examinations. Additionally, he is automatically aware if a contract he's
examining has any deliberate obfuscations or loopholes, though he might not know exactly what they
are.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[investigative mind](ref/spell/investigative-mind); **Cost** 1,250 gp
