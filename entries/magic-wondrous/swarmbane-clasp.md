**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 261\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

An ancient fossilized insect lies trapped within this ornate amber clasp, impaled by the long golden
pin that fastens the wearer's cloak. The wearer's weapons, unarmed attacks, and natural attacks deal
full damage to [swarms](ref/subtype/swarm), regardless of the swarm's immunity to weapon damage (if
any, although [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) applies as normal). If the wearer is
damaged by a swarm, she automatically succeeds on her saving throw against the swarm's distraction
ability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[repel vermin](ref/spell/repel-vermin); **Cost** 1,500 gp
