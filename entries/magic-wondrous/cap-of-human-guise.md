**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 800 gp; **Weight** -

This threadbare hat allows its wearer to alter her appearance at will as with a
_[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)_ spell, except she can only appear as a plainly dressed
Small human child, adult halfling, or adult gnome, such as a peasant, blacksmith, or shopkeeper. The
wearer can slightly alter the cap's appearance as part of the disguise, but otherwise must match the
overall unremarkable look of the illusory form.

Though invented and primarily used by Small humanoids, it can be used by any Small or Medium
humanoid creature.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self); **Cost** 400 gp
