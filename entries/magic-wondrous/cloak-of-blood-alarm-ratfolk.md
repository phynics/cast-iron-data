**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 230\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 3,200 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This leather cloak looks tattered and stained no matter how carefully it is cleaned and repaired.
Once per day, when a [ratfolk](ref/race/ratfolk) wearer bleeds upon the cloak (generally when he
takes hit point damage), the cloak mentally alerts the wearer's nearest ratfolk ally within 10
miles, telling her the approximate distance and direction to the wearer. It also alerts the wearer
to the approximate distance and direction of that ally.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature), [status](ref/spell/status), creator must be a ratfolk;
**Cost** 1,600 gp
