**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 8,500 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This hide helm is set with plates of ivory carved with primitive runes, with a pair of tusks curving
down on either side of the wearer's face for cheek guards. The tusks of the helm provide a gore
attack dealing 1d6 points of damage for a Medium wearer (or 1d4 points of damage for a Small wearer)
and count as a magic weapon for the purposes of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

The helm of the Mammoth Lord also protects the wearer from cold environments as the
_[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)_ spell. Furthermore, it provides a +5 competence bonus
on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal), [Ride](ref/skill/ride), and
[wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) checks with elephants, mammoths, mastodons, and other
elephant-like creatures.

On command, the wearer may use _[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants)_ or
_[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),_ but only to detect or communicate with
elephant-like creatures.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1), [charm animal](ref/spell/charm-animal),
[detect animals or plants](ref/spell/detect-animals-or-plants),
[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals),
creator must have 5 ranks in Handle Animal and Ride; **Cost** 4,250 gp
