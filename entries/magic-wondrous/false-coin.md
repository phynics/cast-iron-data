**Sources:** Occult Adventures pg 257\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 375 gp; **Weight** -

The possessor activates this well-worn gold coin by rubbing it between her thumb and forefinger
while willing it to function. The false coin can be activated only by creatures with the
[Psychic Sensitivity](ref/feat/psychic-sensitivity) feat or the ability to cast psychic spells. Once
activated, the coin maintains a link to the creature that activated it, and if passed along to
someone else, it shares empathic cues about its new possessor with the creature that activated it.
This empathic link gives the activator a +2 insight bonus on [Bluff](ref/skill/bluff),
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy), [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate), and
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks against the coin's current possessor. In addition, the
possessor takes a -1 penalty on saving throws against spells with the emotion, fear, or scrying
descriptors cast by the coin's activator. While activated, the coin appears nonmagical as though
protected by _[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura)_ (Will DC 11).

The link between the activator and the coin persists for 1 week, after which the coin loses all of
its powers and becomes a mundane coin.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts), [magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura); **Cost** 188 gp
