**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 208\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 21,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This large brass belt has three panels depicting scenes of battle. Once per day on command, the
wearer can summon the spirit of a hero of one of the depicted battles. This spirit acts as a
_[spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally),_ though unlike the force created by that spell, the
summoned hero is not entirely mindless. Whether or not the summoned hero is active, it attempts to
guide its wearer to victory via telepathic warning and advice, granting its user a +1 insight bonus
on all saving throws.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor), [spiritual ally](ref/spell/spiritual-ally); **Cost** 10,500
gp
