**Sources:** Pathfinder \#124: City in the Deep pg 81\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These gloves are covered in fish scales and always feel cold and wet to the touch. While wearing
these gauntlets, after a 24-hour attunement period, the wearer can throw a weapon that deals
piercing damage underwater without penalty, using the weapon's normal range increment. When the
wielder attacks a target in water from land, regardless of the weapon the wielder is using, the
target doesn't benefit from [cover](ref/rule/cover) or total cover for being in water.

> _Also see [Physical Attacks Underwater](ref/rule/physical-attacks-underwater) for optional
> alternate rules on underwater combat._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement); **Cost** 2,500 gp
