**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 197, Book of the Damned - Volume 3: Horsemen of the Apocalypse pg
41\
**Aura** moderate conjuration \[evil\]; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,300 gp; **Weight** -

This water-polished stone is carved in the shape of a frog-like head and decorated with evil runes.
It feels lighter than it should, as if hollow and partially filled with liquid. When a hydrodaemon
runestone is crushed, smashed, or broken (a standard action), a
[hydrodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-hydrodaemon) appears as if summoned by a
_[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6)_ spell. Normally, the daemon is under the control
of the creature that broke the runestone, but there is a 25% chance that the daemon is uncontrolled
and attacks its summoner.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6); **Cost** 1,650 gp
