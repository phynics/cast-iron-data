**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 265, Gods and Magic pg 57\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,800 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This cloak looks like the skin of a [goblin dog](ref/monster/goblin-dog), including legs and tail;
the clasp is a goblin dog's tooth. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on
[Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks.

If the wearer's patron is one of the goblin barghest hero gods ([Hadregash](ref/faith/hadregash),
[Venkelvore](ref/faith/venkelvore), [Zarongel](ref/faith/zarongel), or
[Zogmugot](ref/faith/zogmugot)), once per day the wearer can use polymorph to transform into a
goblin dog for up to 7 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[polymorph](ref/spell/polymorph), creator must have 5 ranks in Intimidate; **Cost** 1,400 gp
