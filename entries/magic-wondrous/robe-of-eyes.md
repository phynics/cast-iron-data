**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 217, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 527\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 120,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This valuable garment appears to be a normal robe until it is put on. Its wearer is able to see in
all directions at the same moment due to scores of visible, magical eye-like patterns that adorn the
robe. She also gains 120-foot [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision).

The robe of eyes sees all forms of invisible or ethereal creatures or objects within 120 feet. The
wearer of a robe of eyes gains a +10 competence bonus on Perception checks. She retains her
Dexterity bonus to AC even when flat-footed, and can't be [flanked](ref/rule/flanking). She is not
able to avert or close her eyes when confronted by a creature with a [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attack.

A [light](ref/spell/light) or [continual flame](ref/spell/continual-flame) spell cast directly on a
robe of eyes causes the wearer to be [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1d3 minutes. A
[daylight](ref/spell/daylight) spell blinds the wearer for 2d4 minutes.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 60,000 gp
