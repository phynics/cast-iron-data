**Aura** moderate evocation \[water\] and transmutation \[air, water\]; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 16,000 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This nacreous saddle is carved from giant mollusk shells, lashed with braided kelp, and designed to
be worn by aquatic mounts. On command, an arcing torrent of water rises up from an existing body of
water, allowing the mount to use its swim speed to move through the air. This functions as
_[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk)_ and can be used a total of 7 minutes per day, usable in 1-minute
increments. The arc of water trails behind the mount and rider and can be used by other swimming
creatures to move through the air (Swim DC 15). When the effect ends, the suspended water disappears
with a negligible amount of water falling on creatures underneath the path of the arc.

The sky-river created by the saddle does not harm creatures, though it does extinguish nonmagical
fires in areas it overlaps with them. As a standard action, the rider can expend 2 minutes of the
sky-river's duration to produce a _[hydraulic torrent](ref/spell/hydraulic-torrent)_ with a CMB of
+9.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[air walk](ref/spell/air-walk), [hydraulic torrent](ref/spell/hydraulic-torrent),
[water walk](ref/spell/water-walk); **Cost** 8,000 gp
