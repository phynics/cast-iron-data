**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 270\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 13,900 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This simple pair of leather shooting bracers are designed to protect the wearer's wrist from being
snapped by bowstrings. They appear similar to a set of
[bracers of archery](ref/magic-wondrous/bracers-of-archery-lesser), but a wearer notices the
difference as soon as she dons them.

The wearer of these bracers gains [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) 5/magic against
ranged weapons, as well as a +1 deflection bonus against ranged attacks. In addition, once per day,
as a swift action, the wearer can grant herself a +20 insight bonus on her next ranged attack roll
before the end of her next turn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Craft Magic Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-magic-arms-and-armor),
[protection from arrows](ref/spell/protection-from-arrows), [true strike](ref/spell/true-strike);
**Cost** 6,950 gp
