**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,250 gp; **Weight** -

This red elixir is always the temperature of freshly spilled blood. A creature can consume a gobbet
of flesh and drink this liquid as a single standard action.

If the drinker is an animal, it is [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round, and must succeed at a DC
17 Will save or permanently acquire the [man-eating animal](ref/template/man-eating-animal)
template. If the blood of Baphomet is consumed with humanoid flesh, the animal receives no saving
throw.

If the drinker is not an animal, it enters a frenzy, as the _rage_ spell. The drinker must succeed
at a DC 17 Will save or be [confused](ref/misc/confused) for as long as it rages. If the blood of
Baphomet is consumed with humanoid flesh (a chaotic evil act), the drinker receives no saving throw.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[beast shape III](ref/spell/beast-shape-3), [rage](ref/spell/rage); **Cost** 1,125 gp
