**Sources:** Spymaster's Handbook pg 20\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 18,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This black jacket is marked with the insignia of the
[Blackjackets](ref/arc-fighter/druman-blackjacket). Once per day, the wearer can speak a command
word to gain [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) 20 for 3 rounds. This spell resistance
ends if the wearer successfully resists a spell as a result of it failing to penetrate his spell
resistance. The first time each day that a spell has no effect on the wearer because of spell
resistance or a successful saving throw, the jacket becomes charged with abjuration magic and its
insignia glows faintly. The wearer can touch the glowing insignia as a standard action to cast
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ from the jacket, expending the charge. The charge fades
with no effect if unused for 1 hour.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), [spell resistance](ref/spell/spell-resistance); **Cost**
9,000 gp
