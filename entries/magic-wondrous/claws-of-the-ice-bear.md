**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 234, Pathfinder \#51: The Hungry Storm pg 62\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 1,300 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Strapped to the hands, these spiked climbing claws grant a +2 competence bonus on
[Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks and [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks made when using both
hands to aid in movement, and allow the wearer to ignore the normal penalties on those skills for
slippery or icy surfaces. In addition, up to 3 times per day, the wearer can activate the claws as a
swift action to gain the benefits of _[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb)_ for 1 round. The
wearer cannot hold anything else in her hands while using claws of the ice bear. If used as a
weapon, claws of the ice bear function as [spiked gauntlets](ref/eq-weapon/spiked-gauntlet).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[spider climb](ref/spell/spider-climb); **Cost** 750 gp
