**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** -

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This pair of leather bracers is marked by runes of bondage from ancient days. Each rune is
deliberately incomplete, in order to invert its intended function. Once per day, when a Shoanti
wearer becomes possessed by [magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar), [possession](ref/spell/possession), or
a similar effect, the bracers automatically expel the possessing creature. The possessing creature
does not have a chance to take any actions (such as removing the bracers) before this occurs.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cast out](ref/spell/cast-out), creator must be a Shoanti; **Cost** 4,500 gp
