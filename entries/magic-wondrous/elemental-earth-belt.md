**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 211\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** belt; **Price** 24,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This belt is constructed of flat pieces of slate connected with strong leather straps. This belt
grants its wearer a +4 enhancement bonus to Constitution, and the wearer is immune to effects that
would push, pull, or forcefully move the wearer. This offers no protection from teleportation
effects. Furthermore, once per day on command, the wearer can take the form of a
[Large earth elemental](ref/monster/earth-elemental), as though subject to the
_[elemental body III](ref/spell/elemental-body-3)_ spell.

Treat the enhancement bonus to Constitution as a
[temporary ability bonus](ref/rule/ability-score-bonuses) for the first 24 hours the belt is worn.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance), [elemental body III](ref/spell/elemental-body-3);
**Cost** 12,000 gp
