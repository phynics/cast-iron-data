**Sources:** Pathfinder \#104: Wrath of Thrune pg 36\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,300 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

Shaped in the form of a leering bearded devil clutching a glaive, this dusty yellowish figurine
stands just under a foot tall. When broken or shattered, a brimstone barbazu steadily heats up over
the course of 1 round, belching thick sulfuric smoke before bursting into flames. At the start of
the following round, a [bearded devil](ref/monster/devil-bearded-devil-barbazu) appears in the
square where the figurine was broken. This summoned devil is under the command of the creature that
broke the figurine, as per _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)._ The devil disappears when
slain or after 20 rounds. Once used, a brimstone barbazu is destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell), [summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5); **Cost**
1,650 gp
