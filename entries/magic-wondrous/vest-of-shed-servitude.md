**Sources:** Faiths and Philosophies pg 31\
**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This pale leather vest's surface is decorated with a web of vein-like ridges. Once per day as an
immediate action when you are struck by a critical hit or [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack),
your shed blood or flesh coagulates into a short-lived guardian that appears in any adjacent square
and follows your commands. This guardian has all the abilities of a
[homunculus](ref/monster/homunculus), except that its type is undead. If the triggering attack
inflicted [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage, the homunculus also gains the [giant](ref/template/giant)
simple template. This homunculus lasts for 7 rounds before melting, and it doesn't deal damage to
you when it's destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead), [mirror image](ref/spell/mirror-image); **Cost** 2,000 gp
