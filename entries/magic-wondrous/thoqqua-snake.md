**Sources:** Magical Marketplace pg 21\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 750 gp; **Weight** -

When burned, this firework produces smoke as a [smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick). It also expands
into a tube of ash that, after 1 round, takes the form of a [thoqqua](ref/monster/thoqqua). The
thoqqua attacks the user's opponents to the best of its ability. If the user speaks Ignan, she can
direct the thoqqua to not attack, to attack particular enemies, or to perform other actions. After 5
rounds or when it is reduced to 0 hit points (whichever comes first), the thoqqua collapses into a
pile of nonmagical ash.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3); **Cost** 375 gp
