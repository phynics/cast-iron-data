**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 284\
**Aura** strong conjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This metal bookplate is inscribed with mystical words in Draconic, leaving space for a single
written name. When the bookplate is glued to the inside cover of a book, the named individual may
speak the title of the book to summon it as if using _[instant summons](ref/spell/instant-summons)._
This ability functions once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[instant summons](ref/spell/instant-summons); **Cost** 500 gp
