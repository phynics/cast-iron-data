**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 250 gp; **Weight** -

This normal-seeming dust is actually a magic powder that can conceal the passage of its possessor
and his companions. Tossing a handful of this dust into the air causes a chamber of up to 100 square
feet of floor space to become as dusty, dirty, and cobweb-laden as if it had been abandoned and
disused for a decade.

A handful of dust sprinkled along a trail causes evidence of the passage of as many as a dozen
humanoids and horses to be obliterated for 250 feet back into the distance. The results of the dust
are instantaneous, and no magical aura lingers afterward from this use of the dust.
[Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks made to track a quarry across an area affected by this dust
have a DC 20 higher than normal.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace); **Cost** 125 gp
