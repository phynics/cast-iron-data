**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These supple silk gloves are skintight. As a standard action, the wearer can deliver a complex
secret message to a single target that can see the wearer as if she had succeeded at a DC 20
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) check. This message is conveyed in a series of rapid, complicated hand
gestures that the target automatically understands. Creatures that see the wearer send a message can
attempt a DC 20 [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) check to notice that the wearer is delivering
a secret message and decipher it.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [message](ref/spell/message);
**Cost** 500 gp
