**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 600 gp; **Weight** -

A golden ribbon winds around this smooth, ring-shaped piece of basalt, which was created to emulate
the pit of the Starstone. The stone can be thrown as a ranged attack with a range increment of 20
feet. When the stone strikes a target, it creates a 10-foot-wide, 20-foot-deep moat, as per
_[create pit](ref/spell/create-pit),_ leaving the target on a stone pillar equal in size to its
space. Alternatively, the user can target a specific grid intersection (or merely drop the stone,
which requires no attack roll) to create a 10-foot-wide, 20-foot-deep moat around the 5-foot square
onto which it was thrown or dropped. (If the wielder throws the stone at a target and misses, treat
the stone as a [splash weapon](ref/rule/throw-splash-weapon) to determine where it lands.) Each
creature that is not the target in the area where a pit is created in this way must succeed at a DC
13 Reflex saving throw to jump to safety in the nearest open space. The pit disappears after 6
rounds. The stone is consumed upon being thrown or dropped.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[create pit](ref/spell/create-pit); **Cost** 300 gp
