**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 220\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** chest; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This ornate sash of embroidered velvet stretches across the chest from shoulder to waist. If the
wearer is an [inquisitor](ref/class/inquisitor), she is treated as five levels higher when using her
bane and greater bane abilities. If the wearer is not an inquisitor, she gains the bane ability of a
5th-level inquisitor, but must first attune a light or one-handed melee weapon to the baldric by
hanging it from the cloth for 24 hours, and can only use the bane ability with the attuned weapon.
Attuning a new weapon to the baldric ends the attunement for the previous weapon.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1), creator must be an inquisitor; **Cost** 5,000 gp
