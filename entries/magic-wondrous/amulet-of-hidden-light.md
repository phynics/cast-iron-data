**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** -

This large amulet crafted of rough-cut glass can, on command, shed light as a
[sunrod](ref/eq-misc/sunrod). Unlike a normal light source, creatures outside this area cannot see
the amulet's light.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [light](ref/spell/light),
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility); **Cost** 4,500 gp
