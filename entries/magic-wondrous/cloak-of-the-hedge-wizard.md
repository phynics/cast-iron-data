**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 264\
**Aura** faint, school based on cloak type; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This cloak comes in eight varieties - one for each school of magic. The cloak grants its wearer the
ability to use a certain number of spells on command (caster level 1st) based on its school of
magic. It also grants the wearer the ability to use _[prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation)_
on command and at will.

- **Abjuration:** at will-[resistance](ref/spell/resistance);
  1/day-[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements), [shield](ref/spell/shield)
- **Conjuration:** at will-[acid splash](ref/spell/acid-splash);
  1/day-[mage armor](ref/spell/mage-armor), [unseen servant](ref/spell/unseen-servant)
- **Divination:** at will-[detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic);
  1/day-[detect secret doors](ref/spell/detect-secret-doors), [true strike](ref/spell/true-strike)
- **Enchantment:** at will-[daze](ref/spell/daze); 1/day-[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person),
  [sleep](ref/spell/sleep)
- **Evocation:** at will-[light](ref/spell/light); 1/day-[floating disk](ref/spell/floating-disk),
  [magic missile](ref/spell/magic-missile)
- **Illusion:** at will-[ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound);
  1/day-[color spray](ref/spell/color-spray), [silent image](ref/spell/silent-image)
- **Necromancy:** at will-[touch of fatigue](ref/spell/touch-of-fatigue);
  1/day-[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear), [ray of enfeeblement](ref/spell/ray-of-enfeeblement)
- **Transmutation:** at will-[mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand);
  1/day-[enlarge person](ref/spell/enlarge-person),
  [expeditious retreat](ref/spell/expeditious-retreat)

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation) and any spell the cloak allows the caster to use; the
creator must have [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) in the cloak's school of magic; **Cost** 1,125
gp
