**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 244\
**Aura** moderate abjuration and conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,200 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This simple leather belt pouch can carry a small amount of ordinary gear and objects, as a normal
container of its kind. In addition to this ordinary pouch, a black marketeer's bag contains three
separate extradimensional spaces, identical in size and function to the smaller side pouches in a
[handy haversack](ref/magic-wondrous/handy-haversack). Each compartment requires a separate command
word to access. A creature that knows the command words can access each hidden compartment without
disturbing the contents of the bag itself. A creature opening the black marketeer's bag without
first speaking one of the command words sees only the ordinary contents. A black marketeer's bag
does not radiate magic (as per _[magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura)_).

When a creature attempts to remove a black marketeer's bag from its bearer without permission, such
as with a [steal](ref/rule/steal) combat maneuver or [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand)
check, the would-be thief must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be compelled to instead place a
random item it is holding in its hand (if any) into the bag and then immediately forget the previous
round, as per _[memory lapse](ref/spell/memory-lapse)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [alarm](ref/spell/alarm),
[demand offering](ref/spell/demand-offering), [magic aura](ref/spell/magic-aura),
[memory lapse](ref/spell/memory-lapse), [secret chest](ref/spell/secret-chest); **Cost** 3,100 gp
