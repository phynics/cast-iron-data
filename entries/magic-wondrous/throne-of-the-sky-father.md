**Aura** moderate abjuration and transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1,000 lbs.

This altar takes the form of a throne sized for a Large humanoid. It's carved from mountain stone,
and its surface is inscribed with prayers to [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh) in Thassilonian and to
Ioz'om in Giant. The Throne of the Sky-Father projects a
_[magic circle against law](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-law)_ that affects creatures within 50
feet of the throne. A character within this area can speak a prayer to Gozreh to gain a temporary
blessing from the altar (treat this as speaking a command word to activate a magic item). Praying at
the altar grants a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws against weather effects and a +2
circumstance bonus on [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics), [Climb](ref/skill/climb), and
[Fly](ref/skill/fly) checks. This blessing ends after 24 hours (or earlier, if dispelled or ended by
the actions of the worshiper). The altar can provide its blessing only once per creature per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[control winds](ref/spell/control-winds), [guidance](ref/spell/guidance),
[magic circle against law](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-law),
[stone shape](ref/spell/stone-shape), creator must worship [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh); **Cost**
4,000 gp
