**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** belt; **Weight** 1 lb.

When this magical belt is put on, the wearer must immediately make a DC 20 Fortitude saving throw or
be transformed into a person of the opposite gender. The character's abilities, mind, and spirit
remain unaffected; only the character's sex changes. If the character's saving throw is a natural 1,
the item actually removes all gender from the wearer, giving him an androgynous, neutered
appearance. The change is permanent unless undone with curse-removing magic. Once its magic takes
effect, the belt can be removed without effort. A creature can only be affected by a particular
girdle once, though other girdles of this type can cause further transformations.

**Requirements** any belt; **Cost** -
