**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 228\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This diminutive, shiny brass construct looks like a spider made of gears and cogs. It's roughly the
size of an adult human's hand, and its legs are all picks, chisels, pliers, and other tools of fine
manipulation. Its body contains reservoirs of powders and oils it can spray 2 inches out of its
nozzle-spinnerets. A fine chain can be played out of a winch on the brass spider's back, connecting
it to a small disk with studs and levers that control the construct's actions, enabling it to walk
and crawl like a spider. It has no autonomous motivation.

Once per day, a character holding the brass spider's chain can use the
[Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) skill from 15 feet away without penalty, though he must
have line of sight to the device he is attempting to disable. In most cases, this means any mishap
caused by the skill affects the brass spider (which has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 5 and 25 hit
points) rather than the spider's controller. Additionally, the brass spider can be used without
limit as [masterwork thieves' tools](ref/eq-misc/thieves-tools-masterwork), without the advantage of
any additional range.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate objects](ref/spell/animate-objects), [cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace); **Cost** 1,250 gp
