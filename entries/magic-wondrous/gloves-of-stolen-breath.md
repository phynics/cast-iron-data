**Sources:** Healer's Handbook pg 28\
**Aura** moderate conjuration and necromancy; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 54,000 gp; **Weight** -

These black leather gloves feel like warm, living skin to the touch. When the wearer delivers a
[coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) that kills an opponent, she can target a single creature within
30 feet that has died within the previous 3 rounds as a free action. The target creature is healed
as if with a _[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life)_ spell (regaining 5d8+9 hit points), and if
the creature's hit point total is then greater than a negative amount equal to its Constitution
score, it comes back to life and stabilizes at its new hit point total. As with the
_[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life)_ spell, creatures brought back to life gain a temporary
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) that lasts for 1 day, but this effect
can save creatures slain by death effects. This effect cannot damage undead creatures. The opponent
killed by the _coup de grace_ must have a number of Hit Dice equal to or greater than the target
slain creature's Hit Dice or the gloves' ability is not triggered.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life), [death knell](ref/spell/death-knell); **Cost** 27,000 gp
