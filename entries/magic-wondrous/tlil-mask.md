**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 3,700 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This colorful, beaded mask bears floral patterns and attunes the wearer's senses to flora of all
varieties. The wearer gains a +3 competence bonus on
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) checks relating to plants, seasons, or weather. The
wearer can use a command word to gain the effects of _[greensight](ref/spell/greensight),_ except
that the wearer can freely focus her vision to see through plant matter for any distance up to 60
feet (for example, to examine the inner core of a reed) during the effect's duration. The
_[greensight](ref/spell/greensight)_ ability can be used a total of 10 times; when these uses are
expended, the mask loses its magic and becomes a normal beaded mask. Most Tlil masks are discovered
with 1d10 uses of _[greensight](ref/spell/greensight)_ remaining; reduce the price by 300 gp for
each expended use.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[greensight](ref/spell/greensight); **Cost** 1,850 gp
