**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 55\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** none; **Price** 250 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This silver, oval-shaped platter can hold up to 1 cubic foot worth of food. Once per day, the
platter's user can activate the item to transform its contents into another type or quality of food
of the user's choosing. The transformed food looks and smells just like actual food of its new type,
but retains its original texture, taste, nourishment, and level of fulfillment. Spoiled or poisoned
food remains so and is detectable as such under magical or supernatural scrutiny.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink); **Cost** 125 gp
