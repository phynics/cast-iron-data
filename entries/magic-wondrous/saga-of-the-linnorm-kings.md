**Sources:** People of the North pg 29\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 26,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This heavy book contains a collection of epic tales of mighty Ulfen heroes and the wondrous deeds
they performed, but entwined within the words is a powerful magical effect. If anyone reads this
book, which takes a total of 48 hours over a minimum of 6 days, she gains a +5 competence bonus on
[Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) checks and a +4 morale bonus
on saves against fear. This is a permanent magical effect that can be dispelled as normal. Once the
book is read, the magic disappears from the pages and it becomes a normal book.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), [remove fear](ref/spell/remove-fear), creator must be
a [bard](ref/class/bard); **Cost** 13,000 gp
