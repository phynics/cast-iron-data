**Sources:** Inner Sea Races pg 231\
**Aura** strong transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** -

The following wondrous item is generally available only to members of the indicated race or
ethnicity.

This green leather eye patch allows a half-orc wearer to use the [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate)
skill from 30 feet farther away. Whenever the wearer successfully intimidates a target into giving
aid, the duration of the change in disposition is doubled. The wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on
Intimidate checks to [generate capital](ref/rule/income-phase) and to coerce others into giving aid.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), creator must be a half-orc; **Cost** 5,000 gp
