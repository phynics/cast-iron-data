**Sources:** Inner Sea Temples pg 52\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 10th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This cylindrical felt hat, favored by [Dawnflower dervishes](ref/arc-bard/dawnflower-dervish),
grants its wearer a +2 competence bonus on one [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) or
[Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) skill, chosen when the dervish sikke is created. Most dervish
sikkes grant bonuses on [Knowledge (religion)](ref/skill/knowledge-religion) or Perform (dance)
checks.

If the wearer is a [bard](ref/class/bard), he is considered 5 class levels higher for purposes of
determining the effects of his bardic knowledge class feature. In addition, the bonuses granted by a
bard's [inspire courage](ref/performance/inspire-courage) and
[inspire competence](ref/performance/inspire-competence) class features are increased by 1. Finally,
a bard wearing a dervish sikke can take 10 on any Perform skill check he has ranks in.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[fox's cunning](ref/spell/foxs-cunning), [timely inspiration](ref/spell/timely-inspiration);
**Cost** 5,000 gp
