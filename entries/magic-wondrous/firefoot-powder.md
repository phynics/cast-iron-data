**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** -

This bright red powder contains a mixture of rare herbs and black salt. One dose of fire foot powder
is enough powder to spread over two 5-foot squares, usually around doorways. The first creature to
enter a powdered square must succeed on a DC 14 Fortitude save or immediately become overwhelmed by
waves of fiery pain as if its legs and feet were being burned to ashes. The victim takes 1d6 points
of nonlethal damage per round for 5 rounds, during which time it is also
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened).

Once a dose of firefoot powder is applied to an area, that area remains warded by the effect for 24
hours, or until a creature is affected by the dust, at which point the entire dusted area expends
its magic and becomes harmless.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[pain strike](ref/spell/pain-strike); **Cost** 500 gp
