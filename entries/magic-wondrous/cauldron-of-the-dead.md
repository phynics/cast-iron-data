**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 287, Advanced Player's Guide pg 303\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 35 lbs.

This cauldron of dark iron is large enough to accommodate a single Medium creature. When filled with
a mixture of water and rare herbs, the cauldron transforms any dead body placed in it into a
[zombie](ref/template/zombie) as if animated by an _[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead)_ spell.
Each corpse animated uses up 25 gp in materials per Hit Die, and the cauldron can animate one corpse
per round. The user of the cauldron commands the undead so created - up to 12 HD worth, unless the
user can cast the _[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead)_ spell, in which case that spell's limit
is used; animating more beyond this limit causes earlier specimens to become uncontrolled.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead); **Cost** 15,000 gp
