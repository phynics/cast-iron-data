**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 233\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 4,400 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The wearer of these slippers may walk on air (as the spell [air walk](ref/spell/air-walk)) for up to
10 minutes per day, which may be used in 1-minute increments and requires no action to activate.
However, she may only walk on air within or on top of natural or magical mist or fog, including
fog-like gases such as those created by [acid fog](ref/spell/acid-fog),
[cloudkill](ref/spell/cloudkill), [incendiary cloud](ref/spell/incendiary-cloud), or
[stinking cloud](ref/spell/stinking-cloud). Walking on top of such clouds carries no danger for the
wearer, but walking through the cloud has its normal effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[wind walk](ref/spell/wind-walk); **Cost** 2,200 gp
