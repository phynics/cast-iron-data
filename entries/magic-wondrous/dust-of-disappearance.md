**Aura** moderate illusion; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 3,500 gp; **Weight** -

This dust looks like [dust of appearance](ref/magic-wondrous/dust-of-appearance) and is typically
stored in the same manner. A creature or object touched by it becomes invisible (as
_[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater)_). Normal vision can't see dusted creatures
or objects, nor can they be detected by magical means, including
[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility) or
[invisibility purge](ref/spell/invisibility-purge). Dust of appearance, however, does reveal people
and objects made invisible by dust of disappearance. Other factors, such as sound and smell, also
allow possible detection. The _[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater)_ bestowed by
the dust lasts for 2d6 rounds. The invisible creature doesn't know when the duration will end.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater); **Cost** 1,750 gp
