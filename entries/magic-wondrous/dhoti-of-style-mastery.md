**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 107\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** body; **Price** 5,400 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

When the wearer enters a stance from a style feat while wearing a dhoti of style mastery, he gains
1d8+3 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) plus an additional 1d8 temporary hit
points for each feat he has that lists that style feat as a prerequisite (maximum 3d8+3 temporary
hit points). These temporary hit points last as long as the wearer maintains the stance during that
combat, but vanish when the combat concludes. The wearer cannot gain temporary hit points in this
way more than once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), any style feat,
[false life](ref/spell/false-life); **Cost** 2,700 gp
