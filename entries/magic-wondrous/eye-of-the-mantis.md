**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 159, Faction Guide pg 59\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** -

This 1-inch red marble statue depicts a tiny praying mantis. When the command word is uttered, the
mantis comes to life and moves as its owner instructs, as per a sensor eye from a
_[prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes)_ spell. The mantis remains animated for up to 9 hours or until
it returns to the bearer and replays what it saw, at which point it reverts to statue form for 1
day. If the mantis is destroyed while animate, it reverts to statue form.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[prying eyes](ref/spell/prying-eyes); **Cost** 1,000 gp
