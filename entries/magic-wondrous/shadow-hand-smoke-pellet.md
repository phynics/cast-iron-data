**Sources:** Ultimate Intrigue pg 251\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 800 gp; **Weight** -

This delicate metallic shell contains two sets of volatile alchemical substances, similar to those
used in [smoke pellets](ref/eq-misc/smoke-pellet). When the sphere is broken, the substances mingle
and fill a 10-foot cube with a cloud of dark gray smoke that lasts for 1 minute. The user can throw
the pellet as a ranged touch attack with an increment of 10 feet. Partially tangible hands of smoke
accost creatures in the cloud. These hands attempt to [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) anyone moving
through the smoke (CMB +7). Upon successfully grappling an opponent, the hands attempt to maintain
the grapple every round until the smoke dissipates.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[black tentacles](ref/spell/black-tentacles), [obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist); **Cost**
400 gp
