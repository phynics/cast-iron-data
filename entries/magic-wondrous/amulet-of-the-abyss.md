**Sources:** Book of the Damned pg 195, Book of the Damned - Volume 2: Lords of Chaos pg 49\
**Aura** faint conjuration \[chaotic, evil\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** -

Each amulet of the Abyss bears a demon lord's rune on its face, indicating the entity to which the
amulet is attuned. An amulet of the Abyss functions as an unholy symbol of the demon lord. For a
worshiper of that demon lord, an amulet of the Abyss also functions as a
[phylactery of faithfulness](ref/magic-wondrous/phylactery-of-faithfulness). An amulet of the Abyss
grants its wearer the ability to cast each of the three
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) granted by the associated demon lord's first
_exalted_ demonic boon once per day. Any lawful or good character who wears this amulet gains 2
[negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels). These negative levels remain as long
as the amulet is worn and cannot be overcome in any way as long as the amulet is worn (though they
never result in actual level loss).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), creator must worship a demon
lord; **Cost** 7,500 gp
