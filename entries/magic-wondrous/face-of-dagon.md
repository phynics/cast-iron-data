**Sources:** Pathfinder \#46: Wake of the Watcher pg 57\
**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 27,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This peculiar idol, carved from some unknown green stone, depicts a seven-legged octopoid figure
with a horribly grinning face. Also known as the Seasage Effigy, the Face of Dagon is a sacred relic
of the [ulat-kini](ref/monster/skum). The bearer of the idol can breathe water (as the
_[water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing)_ spell) as long as it is in her possession. In
addition, the bearer can cast _[commune](ref/spell/commune)_ once per week to contact
[Dagon](ref/faith/dagon) or one of the Great Old Ones or Outer Gods. Each time the Face of Dagon is
used in this way, however, the bearer must make a DC 20 Will save or suffer
[insanity](ref/spell/insanity) (as the spell) as her mind is opened to the horrors of beyond.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [commune](ref/spell/commune),
[water breathing](ref/spell/water-breathing); **Cost** 14,000 gp
