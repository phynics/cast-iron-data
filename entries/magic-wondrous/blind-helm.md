**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 147\
**Aura** strong divination; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 85,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This plain steel helm completely covers the face, lacking even eyeholes or a slit to peer out of.
Its wearer is [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) by the helm, but gains [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense)
with a range of 60 feet. As a swift action, the wearer can expend one use of mythic power to gain
[blindsight](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) with a range of 60 feet for 1 minute, or instead
expend two uses of mythic power to gain [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) with a range of 120 feet
for 1 minute. The wearer can expend three uses of mythic power to gain the effects of
_[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing)_ for 1 minute.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [echolocation](ref/spell/echolocation),
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing); **Cost** 42,500 gp
