**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 244, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 518\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 73,500 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

A character wearing this helm may _[teleport](ref/spell/teleport)_ three times per day, instantly
transporting himself and objects he might be carrying on his person to a designated destination,
exactly as if he had cast the spell of the same name.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport); **Cost** 36,750 gp
