**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This set of [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) gauntlets has pistons, gears, and hinges
connecting all the joints and plates. As a swift action, the wearer can command one or both of the
gauntlets to lock into position or unlock and move normally. If a gauntlet locks while holding a
weapon, it acts as a [locked gauntlet](ref/eq-armorenh/locked-gauntlet), providing the wearer a +10
bonus to his Combat Maneuver Defense against being [disarmed](ref/rule/disarm). Locking the
gauntlets also grants the wearer a +4 bonus on combat maneuver checks to pin a
[grappled](ref/rule/grapple) foe and [Climb](ref/skill/climb) checks to catch himself while falling.
They can also allow other uses at the GM's discretion, such as hanging from a ledge or similar
position without having to attempt a Climb check or becoming [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[bear's endurance](ref/spell/bears-endurance), [bull's strength](ref/spell/bulls-strength); **Cost**
6,000 gp
