**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 228\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 3,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These supple gray leather slippers enable the wearer to keep his balance in precarious positions.
The wearer retains his Dexterity bonus to AC when climbing, running, or using the
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) skill to move on narrow surfaces or uneven ground without
falling. In addition, the wearer gains a +2 competence bonus to CMD against [trip](ref/rule/trip)
maneuvers and on Reflex saves to avoid falling (including falling prone).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[cat's grace](ref/spell/cats-grace); **Cost** 1,500 gp
