**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 264, Advanced Race Guide pg 48\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

The forest-green cloak grants a +5 competence bonus on [Diplomacy](ref/skill/diplomacy) and
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive) checks. In addition, once per day, before making a Diplomacy
or Sense Motive check, the wearer can decide to call upon the powers of the cloak to roll twice and
take the better result. Finally, the wearer of this cloak can adjust a creature's attitude up to
three steps when using Diplomacy, instead of the normal limit of two steps. However, the cloak's
power does have one drawback. Should the wearer ever fail a Diplomacy checks made to adjust a
creature's attitude by 5 or more, the creature's attitude is reduced by two steps instead of one.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person), creator must have 5 ranks in Diplomacy and Sense Motive;
**Cost** 10,000 gp
