**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 3,300 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

A vurra is a carving of a giant's head, usually a god but sometimes a hero, loved one, or historical
figure. This hand-sized gray stone is carved to resemble a bearded, wise-looking man with a squarish
skull. It has a leather strap threaded through a hole in the top, and is strangely heavy for its
size. The wearer gains a +2 bonus on [Craft (sculpting)](ref/skill/craft-any) checks. Once per day,
the wearer can use _[magic stone](ref/spell/magic-stone),_ and this ability can affect even the
small boulders favored by giants. The carved face grows stern and angry when worn by a creature with
a chaotic alignment, only returning to its normal expression when removed or in the hands of a
nonchaotic creature.

If the giant god [Minderhal](ref/faith/minderhal) is the wearer's patron, once per day the wearer
may use _[owl's wisdom](ref/spell/owls-wisdom)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic stone](ref/spell/magic-stone), [owl's wisdom](ref/spell/owls-wisdom), creator must have 5
ranks in Craft (sculpting); **Cost** 1,650 gp
