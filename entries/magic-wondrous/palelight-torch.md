**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 2nd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 250 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This torch is made from a dull, gray wood that bears intricate carvings. It burns like a normal
torch, but only gives off [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) in a 40-foot radius. In areas of
normal light, the torch reduces the light level to dim. The torch has no effect in areas of bright
light.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [light](ref/spell/light);
**Cost** 125 gp
