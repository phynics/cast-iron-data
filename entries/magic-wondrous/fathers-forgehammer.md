**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 264, Gods and Magic pg 56\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 4,800 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This iron blacksmith's hammer bears designs of Torag's holy symbol; it is not designed for combat,
but the bearer can use it as a Medium light hammer. Once per day, the bearer can strike the hammer
hard against a weapon to target that weapon with _[magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon)._ The owner
gains a +2 competence bonus on all smithing (armorsmithing, blacksmithing, weaponsmithing, and so
on) skill checks made when crafting with the forgehammer.

If [Torag](ref/faith/torag) is the owner's patron, once per day the owner can strike the hammer
against another character's armor or shield to cast _[shield other](ref/spell/shield-other)_ on that
character. If the owner can craft magic weapons or armor and uses the forgehammer during the
crafting, the total time to craft the item is decreased by 25% (minimum 1 day). This does not reduce
the cost to create the item. A particular forgehammer can aid in only one item's crafting at a time.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon), [shield other](ref/spell/shield-other), creator must have 5
ranks in [Craft (armor) or Craft (weapons)](ref/skill/craft-any); **Cost** 2,400 gp
