**Sources:** Daughters of Fury pg 63\
**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** neck; **Price** 50,000 gp; **Weight** 4 lbs.

This tiny flake of dark, extraordinarily dense material is hung on a simple wire chain and seems to
absorb the light around it. When activated, streaks of alien color play across its surface. Once per
day, the wearer of a star cinder can choose one energy type (acid, cold, electricity, fire, or
sonic). Creatures within 60 feet of the wearer with resistance or immunity to the selected energy
type have that resistance or immunity suppressed for 12 rounds. Creatures with the elemental subtype
are unaffected.

An affected creature of the outsider type must succeed at a DC 17 Will save each round it remains
within the area of effect or be forced to return to its home plane (as per the
_[dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal)_ spell). Even if the outsider succeeds at its save, it is
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for as long as it remains in the area of effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[antimagic field](ref/spell/antimagic-field), [dismissal](ref/spell/dismissal),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy); **Cost** 25,000 gp
