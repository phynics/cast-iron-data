**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 159, Pathfinder \#9: Escape from Old Korvosa pg 69, Pathfinder
\#10: A History of Ashes pg 18, Pathfinder Campaign Setting pg 197, Inner Sea World Guide pg 298,
Curse of the Crimson Throne pg 434\
**Aura** faint divination; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** head; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A mask of the mantis is the traditional headgear of the [Red Mantis](ref/rule/aff-assassins)
assassin - a magical helm that evokes the eerie visage of a praying mantis. Designed both to mask
the wearer's identity while on a job and to enhance the wearer's ferocious appearance, a mask of the
mantis has 3 daily charges that can be used to gain additional bonuses. The wearer can spend a
charge to gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) to a range of 60 feet, the effects of
_[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility),_ the effects of
_[deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch),_ or a +5 competence bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception)
checks. Once a charge is spent, the effect granted persists for 30 minutes before fading. Multiple
effects can be active simultaneously. Charges expended from the mask replenish automatically in 24
hours.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[darkvision](ref/spell/darkvision), [deathwatch](ref/spell/deathwatch),
[see invisibility](ref/spell/see-invisibility); **Cost** 3,000 gp
