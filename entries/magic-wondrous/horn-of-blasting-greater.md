## Horn of Blasting

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 302, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 519\
**Aura** moderate (normal) or string (greater) evocation; **CL** 7th (normal), 16th (greater)\
**Slot** none; **Price** 20,000 gp (normal), 70,000 gp (greater); **Weight** 1 lb.

This spiraling animal horn can be sounded as a normal horn, but if the command word is spoken and
the instrument is then played, it deals 5d6 points of sonic damage to creatures within a 40-foot
cone and causes them to be [deafened](ref/misc/deafened) for 2d6 rounds (a DC 16 Fortitude save
reduces the damage by half and negates the deafening). Crystalline objects and creatures take 7d6
points of sonic damage, with no save unless they're held, worn, or carried by creatures (Fortitude
DC 16 negates).

If a horn of blasting is used magically more than once in a given day, there is a 20% cumulative
chance with each extra use that it explodes and deals 10d6 points of sonic damage to the person
sounding it.

A **greater horn of blasting** functions as a horn of blasting, except that it deals 10d6 points of
sonic damage, [stuns](ref/misc/stunned) creatures for 1 round, and deafens them for 4d6 rounds (a DC
19 Fortitude save reduces the damage by half and negates the stunning and deafening). Crystalline
objects take 16d6 points of sonic damage as described for the horn of blasting. A greater horn of
blasting also has a 20% cumulative chance of exploding for each usage beyond the first each day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [shout](ref/spell/shout)
(normal only), [greater shout](ref/spell/shout-greater) (greater only); **Cost** 10,000 gp (normal),
35,000 gp (greater)
