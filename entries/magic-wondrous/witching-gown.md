**Sources:** Pathfinder \#47: Ashes at Dawn pg 51\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 35,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Spun from combed cotton, this simple, unassuming gown bears a strong aura of witchcraft. These
magical garments are highly sought after by witches, for though anyone may wear a witching gown,
only a witch may realize its full potential. A witching gown grants its wearer a +2 resistance bonus
on all saving throws and a +2 competence bonus on all Bluff, Diplomacy, and Intimidate skill checks.
When worn by a [witch](ref/class/witch), these bonuses double.

In addition, a witch may change the appearance and style of the gown to match her whims (as the
[glamered](ref/magic-enh/glamered) armor special ability) and adopt virtually any disguise, gaining
a +10 bonus on Disguise checks that incorporate the gown's appearance. Only a
[true seeing](ref/spell/true-seeing) spell or similar magic can pierce this effect.

Lastly, a witch may cast a single touch range spell of 4th level or lower into the gown. Thereafter,
the gown holds the magic for up to 24 hours, functioning as a _[spite](ref/spell/spite)_ spell
against the next foe to strike the witch with a successful melee attack or combat maneuver. The
gown's spite spell counts as an active spite effect for the witch wearing the gown.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[charm person](ref/spell/charm-person), [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self),
[resistance](ref/spell/resistance), [spite](ref/spell/spite); **Cost** 17,750 gp
