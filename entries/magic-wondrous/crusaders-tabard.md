**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** body; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

A crusader's tabard is a sturdy cloth vestment worn over armor and other clothing. It is normally
decorated with the symbol or heraldic device of the owner, and may be trimmed with expensive and
delicate materials (such as silk, fur, or cloth of gold). Once per day, the wearer of the tabard may
say its command word (often a family motto); thereafter, the wearer can spend a standard action
(instead of a full-round action) to move up to his speed and make a single [charge](ref/rule/charge)
attack. This ability lasts until used or for 1 minute, whichever duration is shorter.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [haste](ref/spell/haste);
**Cost** 3,000 gp
