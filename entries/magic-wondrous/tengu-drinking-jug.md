**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 167\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** none; **Price** 1,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This looks like a one-gallon stoneware jug with white glaze, black birds painted around the middle,
and a cork stopper tied about the neck with a hemp cord. Any liquid placed within the jug becomes
safe to drink, as though affected by _[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink)_
(though the jug only affects liquids). Three times per day, the bearer can command the jug to alter
the temperature of its contents so that it ranges anywhere from ice cold to boiling hot. Once per
day, the bearer can command water placed into the jug to transform into plum liquor, sake, or tea. A
tengu jug holds up to 1 gallon of any liquid.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation),
[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink); **Cost** 500 gp
