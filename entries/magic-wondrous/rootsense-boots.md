**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 251\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** feet; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Twigs sprout from these tall brown boots. On command, while the wearer is on the ground, ghostly
roots grow from the boots' soles into the ground, granting their wearer
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) 30 feet for as long the wearer remains on the ground in the same
space.

While the roots are active, the boots provide two additional benefits. When the wearer is the target
of a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) or [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempt, living roots momentarily
sprout from the boots into the earth, granting the wearer a +4 bonus to CMD to resist the attempt.
The wearer also benefits from the flexibility of a living willow branch, gaining a +4 circumstance
bonus on combat maneuver or [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) checks to escape a grapple or
slip bonds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[entangle](ref/spell/entangle); **Cost** 4,500 gp
