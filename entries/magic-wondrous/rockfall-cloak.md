**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

A rockfall cloak is fashioned from rigid fabric with a stony texture, and it grants a +2
circumstance bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks in underground terrain. The wearer can
command the cloak to encapsulate her as a free action. The cloak then hardens into a spherical stone
shell resembling a smooth boulder that occupies an entire 5-foot square and weighs 200 pounds. The
shell has a [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) of 10 and 25 hit points. Any damage from falling or from
falling rocks is dealt to the stone shell. If it is destroyed, any remaining damage is transferred
to its wearer. A rockfall cloak remains in this stone shell form for 1 hour (or earlier, if the
wearer chooses) before crumbling to dust. Once it's used in its stone shell form, a rockfall cloak
is destroyed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[stone shield](ref/spell/stone-shield); **Cost** 1,250 gp
