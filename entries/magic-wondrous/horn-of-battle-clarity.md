**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

The call of this hollowed-out animal horn rallies nearby creatures to battle. When the horn is
blown, all creatures within 60 feet who have not yet acted in combat are no longer considered
[flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed). The horn has no effect on other conditions or abilities that
treat a creature as flat-footed.

A barbarian who sounds a horn of battle clarity may spend 2 rounds of rage to act as if she had the
[superstition](ref/ragepower/superstition) rage power for the remainder of that battle.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), [rage](ref/spell/rage);
**Cost** 3,000 gp
