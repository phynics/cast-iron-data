**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 245\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This simple [cold iron](ref/eq-material/cold-iron) circle has no markings or ornamentation upon it,
and is always slightly cool to the touch. The circle shields both the wearer's soul and his identity
from mystic intrusion. The wearer is immune to effects that target souls (such as
_[trap the soul](ref/spell/trap-the-soul)_) or that would be foiled by a
_[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection)_ spell (unlike _[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection),_ the
diviner's caster cannot attempt a [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) check to overcome
this effect). If the wearer is slain, he cannot be restored to life by magic (save by a
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle), [wish](ref/spell/wish), or divine intervention) until the circlet is
removed.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[nondetection](ref/spell/nondetection), [trap the soul](ref/spell/trap-the-soul); **Cost** 15,000 gp
