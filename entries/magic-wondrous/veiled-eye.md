**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** headband; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** -

This banded agate attaches itself to its wearer's forehead and then, blinking and focusing, begins
to act as an additional eye. When worn, the veiled eye has the same vision as the wearer (such as
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 ft., or the
oracle's [clouded vision](ref/oraclecurse/clouded-vision) curse). It grants the wearer no particular
advantages so long as his original eyes continue to function normally. However, should his original
eyes stop working, whether because of the wearer becoming [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) or even his
own decision to close them, he can see through the veiled eye instead. When the wearer is using the
veiled eye as his only vision source, he takes a -2 penalty on ranged attack rolls but gains a +2
insight bonus on saves against [gaze](ref/umr/gaze) attacks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[remove blindness/deafness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness); **Cost** 6,000 gp
