**Sources:** Blood of Shadows pg 27\
**Aura** faint evocation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** none; **Price** 30,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This ornately designed lantern is crafted from volcanic glass. When lit, the lantern's magic creates
an area of darkness in a 20-foot radius and lowers the
[illumination level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) by one step for an additional 20 feet beyond that
area (bright light becomes normal light, normal light becomes dim light, and dim light becomes
darkness). If used in an area of darkness, a voidlight lantern creates an area of supernatural
darkness (as per _[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness)_) in a 20-foot radius instead.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness); **Cost** 15,000 gp
