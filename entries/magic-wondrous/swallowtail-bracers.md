**Sources:** Pathfinder \#75: Demon's Heresy pg 63\
**Aura** moderate abjuration; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** wrists; **Price** 27,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These delicate sliver bracers are decorated with blue butterflies. While worn, swallowtail bracers
grant the wearer a +1 luck bonus on all saving throws, ability checks, and skill checks. If the
wearer worships [Desna](ref/faith/desna), the bracers grant an additional boon: Once per day, by
speaking a command word and the name and/or title of the intended recipient, the wearer may cast
_[dream](ref/spell/dream)._ Such a wearer is also immune to [nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare), and
when the wearer is targeted by that spell, the effects reflect back upon the caster.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[divine favor](ref/spell/divine-favor), [dream](ref/spell/dream), creator must worship
[Desna](ref/faith/desna); **Cost** 13,500 gp
