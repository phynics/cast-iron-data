**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 236\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** hands; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

These soft leather gloves are decorated with even rows of [mithral](ref/eq-material/mithral) rivets.
Only a spellcaster with the [Arcane Strike](ref/feat/arcane-strike) feat can activate their
abilities. These abilities are as follows.

- When the wearer uses the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action to improve an ally's attack
  roll, the ally adds the wearer's Arcane Strike damage bonus to its damage roll for that attack.
- When the wearer makes an attack using Arcane Strike and hits, enemies adjacent to the target take
  damage equal to the wearer's Arcane Strike damage bonus. This damage is of the same type as the
  weapon's damage (bludgeoning, piercing, and so on).
- When the wearer uses the aid another action to improve an ally's Armor Class, the ally also adds
  the wearer's Arcane Strike damage bonus to his AC against the opponent.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), Arcane Strike,
[magic weapon](ref/spell/magic-weapon); **Cost** 2,500 gp
