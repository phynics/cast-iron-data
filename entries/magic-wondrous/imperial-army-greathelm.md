**Sources:** Inner Sea Combat pg 60\
**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** head; **Price** 29,000 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

Many seasoned military commanders in Molthune wear this style of silver helmet adorned with a single
oversized red plume. If the wearer of an Imperial Army greathelm has the
[Leadership](ref/feat/leadership) feat, he gains a +3 bonus to his Leadership score, and any of his
followers or cohorts gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and on saving throws against fear effects while
they're within line of sight of him.

If the wearer possesses the tactician class feature, the range of that ability increases to 60 feet.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item), Leadership,
[eagle's splendor](ref/spell/eagles-splendor), [greater command](ref/spell/command-greater);
**Cost** 14,500 gp
