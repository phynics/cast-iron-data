**Sources:** Mythic Adventures pg 156\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 55,000 gp; **Weight** 7 lbs.

This cloak is made of tough leather, and is decorated with brilliant red and orange feathers. As a
free action, the wearer can rub her hands together, setting them alight - thus granting her natural
attacks and unarmed strikes the [burn](ref/umr/burn) special ability (1d6, Reflex DC 14). The wearer
can use this ability for up to 10 rounds per day, which don't need to be consecutive.

If the wearer takes damage that would reduce her below 0 hit points, she can expend one use of
mythic power to transform into a phoenix-like being. This doesn't require an action. When this
happens, she is immediately affected as though targeted with a
_[breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life)_ spell (CL 9th) and polymorphs into a bird-like creature
made of flames (as _[elemental body II](ref/spell/elemental-body-2),_ CL 9; fire elemental only).
While in this form, the wearer also gains a fly speed of 50 feet with good maneuverability, as well
as a +4 bonus on [Fly](ref/skill/fly) skill checks due to this maneuverability.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[Mythic Crafter](ref/feat/mythic-crafter), [breath of life](ref/spell/breath-of-life),
[elemental body II](ref/spell/elemental-body-2); **Cost** 27,500 gp
