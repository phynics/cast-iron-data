**&L&Foot Stomp&FN&**

**Ability:** If the attack hits and the monk ends his turn adjacent to that foe, the foe's movement
is restricted. Until the start of the monk's next turn, the target of this attack can move only in
such a way that the space it occupies is adjacent to the monk. Alternatively, it can attempt a
combat maneuver check (against the monk's CMD) as a **standard action** to break free.

**Flavor:** The monk stomps on a foe's foot, preventing the opponent from escaping.

**Special:** This strike does not work against foes that cannot be tripped. The monk must attack
with a kick to use this style strike.
