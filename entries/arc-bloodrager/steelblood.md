**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 85\
Most bloodragers prefer light armor, but some learn the secret of using heavy armors. These
steelbloods plod around the battlefield inspiring fear and delivering carnage from within a steel
shell.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Ability:** A steelblood gains proficiency in heavy armor. A steelblood can cast bloodrager spells
while wearing heavy armor without incurring an arcane spell failure chance.

**Replaces:** The bloodrager's armor proficiency

**Indomitable Stance (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A steelblood gains a +1 bonus on combat maneuver checks, to CMD against
[overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuvers, and on Reflex saving throws against
[trample](ref/umr/trample) attacks. He also gains a +1 bonus to his AC against
[charge](ref/rule/charge) attacks and on attack and damage rolls against charging creatures.

**Replaces:** Fast movement

**Armored Swiftness (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A steelblood moves faster in medium and heavy armor.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** When wearing medium or heavy armor, a steelblood can move 5 feet faster than normal in
that armor, to a maximum of his unencumbered speed.

**Replaces:** Uncanny dodge

**Armor Training (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A steelblood learns to be more maneuverable while wearing armor.

**At 13th Level:** The penalty is now reduced by 3, and the maximum bonus is increased by 3.

**At 19th Level:** The penalty is now reduced by 4, and the maximum bonus is increased by 4.

**At 9th Level:** The penalty is now reduced by 2, and the maximum bonus is increased by 2.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Passive:** Whenever he is wearing armor, he reduces the armor check penalty by 1 (to a maximum of
0) and increases the maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by his armor by 1.

**Replaces:** Improved uncanny dodge

**Special:** This ability stacks with the fighter class feature of the same name.

**Blood Deflection (Su)**

**Immediate:** A steelblood can sacrifice a bloodrager spell slot to gain a deflection bonus to AC
equal to the level of the spell sacrificed. The deflection bonus lasts until the start of his next
turn. This ability can be applied after an attack roll is made against the steelblood, allowing the
steelblood to convert a hit into a miss if the deflection bonus is high enough.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** [Damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)
