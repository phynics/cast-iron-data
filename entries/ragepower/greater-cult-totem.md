**&L&Greater Cult Totem (Ex)&FN&**

**Passive:** While the barbarian is _raging,_ when she takes hit point damage that would render her
[dying](ref/misc/dying) or [dead](ref/misc/dead), she remains conscious and _raging_ until the end
of her next turn after taking that damage, though she is [disabled](ref/misc/disabled) during that
turn. She becomes dying or dead at the end of her next turn as normal for her current hit point
total.

**Prereq:** 10th-level barbarian, [lesser cult totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-cult-totem),
[cult totem](ref/ragepower/cult-totem)

**Special:** This ability has no effect if the barbarian dies from a cause other than hit point
damage, such as from a death effect or if her Constitution damage exceeds her Constitution score.
