**Flight Response**

**Immediate:** When a barbarian attempts a saving throw against a fear effect, she can enter a
_rage_ (as long as she would normally be able to enter _rage_). This consumes 3 rounds of the
barbarian's daily allotment of _rage_ rounds, but the barbarian can maintain the _rage_ each round
on her turn normally. Any benefits from the barbarian's _rage_ apply immediately, so she gains her
bonus on Will saves against the effect that required the initial saving throw.

**Special:** Unlike most rage powers, this rage power's effects are useful only when the barbarian
is not _raging,_ so a skald can't grant this rage power to allies with raging song.
