The barbarian gains a +1 dodge bonus to her Armor Class against ranged attacks for a number of
rounds equal to the barbarian's current Constitution modifier (minimum 1). This bonus increases by
+1 for every 6 levels the barbarian has attained. Activating this ability is a move action that does
not provoke an attack of opportunity.
