**Spring Rage (Su)**

**Flavor:** The barbarian's _rage_ rejuvenates her physically and spiritually.

**Passive:** While _raging,_ she ignores penalties to her ability scores due to
[aging](ref/rule/age). She also ignores penalties from
[negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels), though she retains any negative levels
she has until they are removed normally.

**Special:** A barbarian can have only one season-themed rage power, choosing between spring,
[summer](ref/ragepower/summer-rage), [autumn](ref/ragepower/autumn-rage), or
[winter](ref/ragepower/winter-rage) rage.
