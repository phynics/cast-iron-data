**&L&Reckless Stance (Ex)&FN&**

**Flavor:** The barbarian can attack with abandon at the expense of her defense.

**Passive:** She gains a bonus on attack rolls, but takes a penalty to AC. This bonus and penalty
are equal to 1 + [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her barbarian level.
