**&L&Raging Whirlwind (Su)&FN&**

**Immediate:** The barbarian can create a whirlwind with a powerful spinning move after she confirms
a critical hit with a melee attack. The whirlwind circles the target of her melee attack, lifting it
in the air slightly until the end of the barbarian's turn. The creature can attempt a Fortitude save
(DC = 10 + half the barbarian's level + the barbarian's Constitution modifier) to negate this
effect. If it fails the save, the target loses its Dexterity modifier to AC for the rest of the
barbarian's turn and lands [prone](ref/rule/prone) on the ground at the end of her turn.

**Prereq:** 12th-level barbarian

**Special:** Raging whirlwind has no effect on flying creatures.
