**&L&Bloody Bite (Ex)&FN&**

**Passive:** The barbarian's bite attack deals 1d6 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage in
addition to its other effects.

**Prereq:** [Half-orc](ref/race/half-orc), either [animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury) or a
natural bite attack
