While raging, the barbarian's melee attacks are considered good-aligned weapons for the purpose of
bypassing [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction). Furthermore, the barbarian deals an
additional 1d6 points of damage when she damages an evil outsider with a melee attack.
