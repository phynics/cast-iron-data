**Greater Ground Breaker (Ex)**

**Ability:** When using the _ground breaker_ rage power, the barbarian can extend the radius of the
effect by 5 feet.

**Prereq:** 8th-level barbarian, [ground breaker](ref/ragepower/ground-breaker)

**Special:** This rage power can be selected up to three times, and its effects stack.
