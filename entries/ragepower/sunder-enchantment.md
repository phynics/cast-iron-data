While raging, whenever the barbarian succeeds at a [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver on a
magical item, that item's magical abilities are suppressed for 1 round, plus 1 round for every 5
points by which her combat maneuver check exceeded the target's CMD. A barbarian must have the
[spell sunder](ref/ragepower/spell-sunder) rage power and be at least 8th level before selecting
this rage power.
