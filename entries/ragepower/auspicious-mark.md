**Auspicious Mark (Su)**

**Flavor:** The barbarian has been marked by the spirits, as indicated by an impressive tattoo,
scar, or birthmark she possesses.

**Special:** She can call on the _auspicious_ mark after seeing the result of the d20 roll.

**Swift:** By spending 2 rounds of _rage,_ the barbarian can call upon the spirits' favor. Using
_auspicious mark_ grants her a +1d6 bonus on one d20 roll she has just made.

**Usage:** Once per _rage_
