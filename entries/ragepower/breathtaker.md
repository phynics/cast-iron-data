**Breathtaker (Ex)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ whenever the barbarian makes a successful melee attack against an
opponent that is [holding its breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath), in addition to any other effects
caused by that attack, the opponent loses a number of rounds of breath equal to the barbarian's
Strength modifier.

**Flavor:** The barbarian's blows take the air from her foes' lungs.
