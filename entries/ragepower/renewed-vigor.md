As a standard action, the barbarian heals 1d8 points of damage + her Constitution modifier. For
every four levels the barbarian has attained above 4th, this amount of damage healed increases by
1d8, to a maximum of 5d8 at 20th level. A barbarian must be at least 4th level before selecting this
power. This power can be used only once per day and only while raging.
