**Renewed Vigor (Ex)**

**Prereq:** 4th-level barbarian

**Special:** This power can be used only while _raging._

**Standard:** The barbarian heals 1d8 points of damage + her Constitution modifier. For every four
levels the barbarian has attained above 4th, this amount of damage healed increases by 1d8, to a
maximum of 5d8 at 20th level.

**Usage:** Once per day
