The barbarian does not gain any benefits or take any of the penalties from rage for 1 round.
Activating this power is a swift action. This includes the penalty to Armor Class and the
restriction on what actions can be performed. This round still counts against her total number of
rounds of rage per day. This power can only be used once per rage.
