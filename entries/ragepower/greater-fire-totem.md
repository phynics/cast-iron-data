**&L&Greater Fire Totem (Su)&FN&**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the barbarian can envelope herself in a cloud of smoke that fills a
10-foot-radius sphere and follows her as she moves. Creatures in the affected area must succeed at
Fortitude saves each round or [suffer the effects of breathing heavy smoke](ref/rule/forest-fires).
The barbarian is immune to the smoke's effects, including vision obstruction. She can end this smoke
as a **free action**, or it can be dispersed by wind in the same manner as a
[fog cloud](ref/spell/fog-cloud) spell.

**Prereq:** 10th-level barbarian, [fire totem](ref/ragepower/fire-totem)
