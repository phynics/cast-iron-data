**Flesh Wound (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per _rage,_ the barbarian can try to avoid serious harm from an attack. The
barbarian must make a Fortitude save with a DC equal to the damage that would be dealt by the
attack. The barbarian's armor check penalty applies on this saving throw. If the save succeeds, the
barbarian takes half damage from the attack and the damage is nonlethal.

**Prereq:** 10th-level barbarian

**Special:** The barbarian must elect to use this ability after the attack roll is made, but before
the damage is rolled.
