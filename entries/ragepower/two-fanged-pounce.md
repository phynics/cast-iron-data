**Two-Fanged Pounce**

**Ability:** If the barbarian is wielding a pair of [daggers](ref/eq-weapon/dagger),
[kukris](ref/eq-weapon/kukri), or [punching daggers](ref/eq-weapon/punching-dagger), she can attack
once with each of the two weapons when she [charges](ref/rule/charge). If she does, she loses the
bonus on attack rolls for charging and takes an additional -2 penalty to her AC, and she applies
precision damage or effects that occur on a hit only once, even if she hits with both attacks.
