**&L&Taunting Stance (Ex)&FN&**

**Flavor:** The barbarian can leave herself open to attacks while preparing devastating
counterattacks.

**Passive:** Enemies gain a +4 bonus on attack and damage rolls against the barbarian while she's in
this stance, but every attack against the barbarian provokes an attack of opportunity from her,
which is resolved prior to each provoking attack.

**Prereq:** 12th-level barbarian
