**Renewed Vitality (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, when a barbarian with ability penalties or damage ends her _rage,_ she
can reroll a saving throw, if any, against an effect that imposed an ability penalty or dealt her
ability damage. If successful, she removes an amount of ability penalty or damage equal to the
amount she can ignore while _raging._

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the barbarian ignores the effect of 1 point of ability penalty or
damage per two barbarian levels she has (maximum 10).

**Prereq:** 4th-level barbarian, [renewed vigor](ref/ragepower/renewed-vigor)
