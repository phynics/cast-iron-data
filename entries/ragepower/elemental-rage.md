**Elemental Rage (Su)**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ all of the barbarian's melee attacks deal an additional 1d6 points of
energy damage (acid, cold, electricity, or fire). The type is chosen when the barbarian begins her
_rage._

**Prereq:** 8th-level barbarian,[lesser elemental rage](ref/ragepower/lesser-elemental-rage)

**Special:** The barbarian can still use her _lesser elemental rage_ power while using this rage
power, but she must select a different energy type.
