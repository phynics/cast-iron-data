**&L&Calm Stance (Ex)&FN&**

**Flavor:** The barbarian can enter a state of calm.

**Passive:** While in this stance, the barbarian doesn't gain any benefits from _rage_ other than
the temporary hit points, but she doesn't take any of the penalties from _rage_ (including the
penalty to AC and the restriction on actions she can take). Rounds spent in a calm stance still
count against her total number of rounds of _rage_ per day.
