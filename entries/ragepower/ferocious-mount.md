While raging and mounted, the barbarian's mount also gains the benefits of rage (including greater
rage and mighty rage) as long as the barbarian is mounted or adjacent to it. While her mount is
raging in this way, she must spend one additional round of rage per round. She can elect not to pay
this cost, in which case her mount does not rage.
