**Ferocious Mount (Ex)**

**Passive:** While _raging_ and mounted, the barbarian's mount also gains the benefits of _rage_
(including _greater rage_ and _mighty rage_) as long as the barbarian is mounted or adjacent to it.
While her mount is _raging_ in this way, she must spend one additional round of _rage_ per round.
She can elect not to pay this cost, in which case her mount does not _rage._
