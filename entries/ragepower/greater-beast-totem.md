While raging, the barbarian gains the [pounce](ref/umr/pounce) special ability, allowing her to make
a full attack at the end of a charge. In addition, the damage from her claws increases to 1d8 (1d6
if Small) and the claws deal ×3 damage on a critical hit. A barbarian must have the
[beast totem](ref/ragepower/beast-totem) rage power to select this rage power. A barbarian must be
at least 10th level to select this rage power.
