**Greater Beast Totem (Su)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the barbarian gains the [pounce](ref/umr/pounce) special ability,
allowing her to make a full attack at the end of a charge. In addition, the damage from her claws
increases to 1d8 (1d6 if Small) and the claws deal &times&3 damage on a critical hit.

**Prereq:** [Beast totem](ref/ragepower/beast-totem), 10th-level barbarian
