**&L&Dragon Totem Resilience (Su)&FN&**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the barbarian gains resistance to the energy type that is associated
with her dragon totem - acid (black, copper, green), cold (silver, white), electricity (blue,
bronze), or fire (brass, gold, red). This resistance equals double her current DR/- from her
barbarian _[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction)_ class feature; this energy resistance
increases by 2 for each dragon totem rage power she possesses, including this one.

**Prereq:** [Dragon totem](ref/ragepower/dragon-totem), 8th-level barbarian
