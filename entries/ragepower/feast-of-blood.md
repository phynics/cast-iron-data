**&L&Feast of Blood (Su)&FN&**

**Flavor:** While _raging,_ the barbarian may feast on the organs of her foes and absorb their
power.

**Fullround:** After successfully ripping out a creature's vital organ, the barbarian may eat the
organ within 1 minute of extracting it. Once the organ is consumed, the barbarian recovers a number
of hit points equal to the Hit Dice of the organ's owner.

In addition, the barbarian gains any energy immunities or resistances the victim had, though the
effectiveness of these resistances is halved (for instance, a barbarian who eats the heart of a
creature with [resist](ref/umr/resistance) cold 10 would gain resist cold 5; energy immunities are
reduced to resist energy 20). This energy resistance lasts for a number of rounds equal to half the
Hit Dice of the creature whose organ was consumed.

**Prereq:** [Bloody fist](ref/ragepower/bloody-fist), 14th-level barbarian

**Provokes attack of opportunity:** Yes
