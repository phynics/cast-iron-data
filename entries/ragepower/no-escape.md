The barbarian can move up to double her base speed as an immediate action but she can only use this
ability when an adjacent foe uses a [withdraw](ref/rule/withdraw) action to move away from her. She
must end her movement adjacent to the enemy that used the withdraw action. The barbarian provokes
attacks of opportunity as normal during this movement. This power can only be used once per rage.
