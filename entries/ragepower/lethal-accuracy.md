**Lethal Accuracy (Ex)**

**Passive:** When using her _surprise accuracy_ rage power, the barbarian's critical multiplier for
damage increases by 1 (a ×2 multiplier becomes ×3, a 19-20/×2 multiplier becomes 19-20/×3, a ×3
multiplier becomes ×4, and a ×4 multiplier becomes ×5).

**Prereq:** 16th-level barbarian, [surprise accuracy](ref/ragepower/surprise-accuracy),
[deadly accuracy](ref/ragepower/deadly-accuracy)
