When using her [surprise accuracy](ref/ragepower/surprise-accuracy) rage power, the barbarian's
critical multiplier for damage increases by 1 (a ×2 multiplier becomes ×3, a 19–20/×2 multiplier
becomes 19–20/×3, a ×3 multiplier becomes ×4, and a ×4 multiplier becomes ×5). A barbarian must have
the [surprise accuracy](ref/ragepower/surprise-accuracy) and
[deadly accuracy](ref/ragepower/deadly-accuracy) rage powers and be 16th level before selecting this
rage power.
