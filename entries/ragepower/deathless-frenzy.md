**&L&Deathless Frenzy&FN&**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ you ignore the effects of being at 0 or fewer hit points until you have
been at 0 or fewer hit points for 1 round, at which point the appropriate consequences for your
current hit point total take effect. Even death can be postponed this way, potentially allowing you
to survive if you receive sufficient healing before the round is up.

**Prereq:** 12th-level barbarian, [wereboar-kin](ref/race/skinwalker) or associated with
wereboar-kin
