**&L&Savage Intuition&FN&**

**Ability:** When combat begins, if you have rounds of _rage_ remaining, you may automatically enter
a _rage_ without using an action at the start of the first round of combat (or at the start of the
surprise round, if there is one), even if you are not otherwise aware that combat has begun.

**Flavor:** Your bestial senses allow you to sense danger and become enraged before you even have
time to consciously react to danger.

**Prereq:** [Wereboar-kin](ref/race/skinwalker) or associated with wereboar-kin
