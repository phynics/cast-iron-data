**&L&Bloody Fist (Ex)&FN&**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the barbarian may attempt to punch through an opponent's flesh and rip
out one of its vital organs. In order to do so, the barbarian must confirm a critical hit against an
opponent with a natural attack or unarmed strike. The barbarian deals damage as normal for a
critical hit and the opponent must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 the barbarian's class
level + the barbarian's Strength modifier) or take 1d4 points of
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) as one of its vital organs is ripped free from
its body.

**Prereq:** 12th-level barbarian

**Usage:** Once per _rage_
