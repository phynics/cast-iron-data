While raging, each time the barbarian confirms a critical hit, the target must succeed at a Will
saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the barbarian's level + her Constitution modifier) or be
[confused](ref/misc/confused) for one round. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect.
