While raging, the spirits that surround the barbarian make it difficult for her enemies to see her.
The spirits grant the barbarian a 20% miss chance against ranged attacks and melee attacks made by
creatures that are not adjacent to the barbarian (typically due to reach). A barbarian must have the
[lesser spirit totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-spirit-totem) rage power to select this rage power. A
barbarian must be at least 6th level to select this rage power.
