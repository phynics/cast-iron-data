**Spirit Totem (Su)**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the spirits that surround the barbarian make it difficult for her
enemies to see her. The spirits grant the barbarian a 20% miss chance against ranged attacks and
melee attacks made by creatures that are not adjacent to the barbarian (typically due to reach).

**Prereq:** 6th-level barbarian, [lesser spirit totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-spirit-totem)
