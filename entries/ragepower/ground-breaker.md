Once per rage, the barbarian can attack the floor around her as a standard action. This attack
automatically hits and deals damage normally. If the barbarian manages to deal more damage than the
floor's [hardness](ref/umr/hardness), the space she occupies and all of the squares adjacent to her
become [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). Creatures in these squares, except the
barbarian, must make a DC 15 Reflex save or be knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone). A barbarian must be
at least 6th level to select this rage power.
