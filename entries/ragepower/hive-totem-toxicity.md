**Hive Totem Toxicity (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per _rage,_ a bite that hits can deliver an injury [toxin](ref/rule/poison) that
has a frequency of once per round for 4 rounds, deals 1d3 points of
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain), and has a cure of one save. The saving throw
has a Fortitude save DC of 10 + 1/2 the barbarian's level + the barbarian's Constitution modifier.

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the barbarian increases her bite damage die type with the
[animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury) rage power
[by one die type](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments) and decreases the penalty on attack rolls with
that bite to -2.

**Prereq:** [Hive totem](ref/ragepower/hive-totem),
[hive totem resilience](ref/ragepower/hive-totem-resilience), 8th-level barbarian
