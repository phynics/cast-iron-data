The barbarian unleashes a terrifying howl as a standard action. All [shaken](ref/misc/shaken)
enemies within 30 feet must make a Will save (DC equal to 10 + 1/2 the barbarian's level + the
barbarian's Strength modifier) or be [panicked](ref/misc/panicked) for 1d4+1 rounds. Once an enemy
has made a save versus terrifying howl (successful or not), it is immune to this power for 24 hours.
A barbarian must have the [intimidating glare](ref/ragepower/intimidating-glare) rage power to
select this rage power. A barbarian must be at least 8th level before selecting this power.
