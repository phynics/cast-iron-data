**Gearbreaker (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per round while _raging,_ whenever the barbarian makes an attack against a
construct, she can ignore an amount of that construct's [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) equal to her
barbarian level. This ability must be used before the attack roll is made.

**Prereq:** [Smasher](ref/ragepower/smasher)

**Special:** This power stacks with the _hard hitter_ ability from the
[Numerian liberator](ref/arc-barbarian/numerian-liberator) archetype.
