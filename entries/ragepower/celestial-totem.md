**&L&Celestial Totem (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the barbarian shines with a righteous light. This effect bestows upon
the barbarian a halo of gleaming light that shines as if it were [daylight](ref/spell/daylight) and
triggers an [invisibility purge](ref/spell/invisibility-purge) effect in the barbarian's square and
each adjacent square. The _[invisibility purge](ref/spell/invisibility-purge)_ only affects nongood
creatures.

**Prereq:** 8th-level barbarian, [lesser celestial totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-celestial-totem)
