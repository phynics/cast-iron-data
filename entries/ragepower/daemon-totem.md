**Daemon Totem (Su)**

**Ability:** While the barbarian is _raging,_ her melee attacks impose a temporary
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) on her opponent on a successful critical
hit. After 1 hour, these temporary negative levels disappear automatically (without a saving throw).

**Prereq:** 6th-level barbarian, [lesser daemon totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-daemon-totem)
