**Greater Daemon Totem (Su)**

**Ability:** If the barbarian kills an intelligent creature with a CR equal to at least half her
character level while _raging,_ she heals 5 hit points. If she is already at her maximum number of
hit points, she instead gains 5 [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points), which don't
stack if she kills multiple creatures.

**Prereq:** 10th-level barbarian, [lesser daemon totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-daemon-totem),
[daemon totem](ref/ragepower/daemon-totem)
