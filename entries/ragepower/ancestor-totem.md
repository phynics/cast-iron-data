**Ancestor Totem (Su)**

**Passive:** The barbarian's insight bonus to her chosen skill increases to +4. The barbarian can
now select any skill to receive this bonus, even a Charisma-, Dexterity-, or Intelligence-based
skill or a skill that requires patience or concentration; if she selects such a skill, she can use
it even while _raging._

**Prereq:** [Lesser ancestor totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-ancestor-totem), 6th-level barbarian
