**Fire Totem (Su)**

**Passive:** While the barbarian is _raging,_ any opponent that confirms a critical hit against her
with a piercing or slashing melee weapon is sprayed with liquid fire. (Creatures with reach weapons
are immune.) The attacker takes 1d6 points of fire damage per barbarian level (Reflex halves; DC
10 + 1/2 the barbarian's level + the barbarian's Con modifier).

**Prereq:** 6th-level barbarian, [lesser elemental totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-elemental-totem)
(fire)
