**Animal Fury (Ex)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the barbarian gains a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks). If used
as part of a full attack action, the bite attack is made at the barbarian's full base attack bonus
-5. If the bite hits, it deals 1d4 points of damage (assuming the barbarian is Medium; 1d3 points of
damage if Small) plus half the barbarian's Strength modifier.

**Special:** A barbarian can make a bite attack as part of the action to maintain or break free from
a grapple. This attack is resolved before the grapple check is made. If the bite attack hits, any
grapple checks made by the barbarian against the target this round are at a +2 bonus.
