**Dissipating Rage (Su)**

**Passive:** When the barbarian _rages,_ her feverish frenzy and hot breath burn or blow fog, smoke
and similar effects away from her. The barbarian and creatures adjacent to her do not gain
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from obfuscating effects like fog or precipitation.

**Special:** Magical effects that require more than a strong wind to dissipate are not affected by
this rage power.
