**Penetrating Bite (Ex)**

**Flavor:** When using the _animal fury_ rage power while _raging,_ the barbarian's bite is able to
pierce most resistances.

**At 10th Level:** Her bite is also treated as a chaotic weapon for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 16th Level:** Her bite is treated as an [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) weapon for the
purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) and bypassing
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

**At 4th Level:** Her bite is treated as a magic weapon for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 7th Level:** Her bite is also treated as cold iron and silver for the purpose of overcoming
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Prereq:** 4th-level barbarian, [animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury)
