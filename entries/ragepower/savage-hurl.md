**Savage Hurl (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** It becomes +4 at if her Strength modifier is at least +8.

**At 4th Level:** This bonus increases to +2 if the barbarian's Strength modifier is at least +4.

**At 8th Level:** It increases to +3 if her Strength modifier is at least +6.

**Passive:** The barbarian gains a +1 bonus on ranged attack rolls with thrown weapons that add her
Dexterity bonus to the attack roll.
