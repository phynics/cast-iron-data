**Raging Grappler (Ex)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the barbarian grapples her foes with the power and viciousness of a
river crocodile. As long as she is _raging,_ whenever the barbarian succeeds at a check to start a
[grapple](ref/rule/grapple), she can choose to deal damage as if she had also succeeded at a check
to maintain the grapple.

In addition, while she is _raging,_ whenever the barbarian succeeds at a combat maneuver check to
maintain a grapple, she can choose to give herself, the target of her grapple, or both the
[prone](ref/rule/prone) condition as a **free action** while continuing to maintain the grapple.
