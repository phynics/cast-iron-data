**Energy Resistance (Ex)**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the barbarian gains [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) to one energy type
(acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic) equal to 1/2 her barbarian level (minimum 1).

**Special:** The energy type is chosen when this rage power is selected and it cannot be changed.
This rage power can be selected more than once. Its effects do not stack. Each time is applies to a
different energy type.
