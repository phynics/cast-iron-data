**Greater Fiend Totem (Su)**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the barbarian is surrounded by an aura of menace. Good creatures
adjacent to the barbarian are [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) and take 2d6 points of slashing damage at
the beginning of the barbarian's turn as dozens of small cuts open across their flesh. Neutral
creatures that are adjacent to the barbarian are shaken, but do not take any damage. Evil creatures
are unaffected.

**Prereq:** 10th-level barbarian, [fiend totem](ref/ragepower/fiend-totem)
