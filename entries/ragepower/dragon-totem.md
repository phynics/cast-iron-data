The barbarian must select a single color of dragon (black, blue, brass, bronze, copper, gold, green,
red, silver, or white) as her totem. While raging, she gains a +1 bonus on Perception checks and a
+1 morale bonus on saving throws against fear, paralysis, and sleep effects. This bonus increases by
+1 for each additional dragon totem rage power (such as
[dragon totem resilience](ref/ragepower/dragon-totem-resilience) and
[dragon totem wings](ref/ragepower/dragon-totem-wings)) the barbarian possesses. A barbarian must
have the [animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury) and
[intimidating glare](ref/ragepower/intimidating-glare) rage powers and be at least 6th level before
selecting this rage power.
