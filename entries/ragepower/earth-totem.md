**Earth Totem (Ex)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the barbarian can burrow through sand, loose soil, or gravel at a speed
of 20 feet. This does not give the barbarian the ability to breathe underground. Loose material
collapses behind the target 1 round after it leaves the area.

**Prereq:** 6th-level barbarian, [lesser elemental totem](ref/ragepower/lesser-elemental-totem)
(earth)
