**Reckless Abandon (Ex)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ the barbarian can take a penalty to AC to gain a bonus on attack rolls.
The penalty and bonus are both equal to 1 + [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her
barbarian level.
