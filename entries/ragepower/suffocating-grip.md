**Suffocating Grip (Ex)**

**Ability:** While _raging,_ when the barbarian successfully maintains a grapple, she can choke her
opponent instead of dealing damage to, moving, pinning, or tying up her opponent. The grappled
opponent cannot speak or breathe and must [hold its breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) or begin
[suffocating](ref/rule/suffocation).

**Special:** The grappled opponent can take a breath during any round in which the barbarian does
not maintain the grapple. Creatures that do not breathe are not affected by this ability.
