While raging, the barbarian takes half damage from attacks by swarms of vermin, including spells and
effects that act like a swarm of vermin (like _[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm)_ used to
summon spiders or _[insect plague](ref/spell/insect-plague)_). She also gains a +1 bonus on Strength
ability checks and to CMD against bull rush, drag, and trip attacks for every four barbarian levels
she has (maximum +5). A barbarian must have the [animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury) power and
be at least 4th level before selecting this rage power.
