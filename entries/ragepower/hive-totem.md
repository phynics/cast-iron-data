**Hive Totem (Su)**

**Passive:** While _raging,_ the barbarian takes half damage from attacks by swarms of vermin,
including spells and effects that act like a swarm of vermin (like
[summon swarm](ref/spell/summon-swarm) used to summon spiders or
[insect plague](ref/spell/insect-plague)).

She also gains a bonus on Strength ability checks and to CMD against bull rush, drag, and trip
attacks equal to [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of her barbarian level.

**Prereq:** [Animal fury](ref/ragepower/animal-fury), 4th-level barbarian
