Once per round, the barbarian can make a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) attempt against one target
in place of a melee attack. If successful, the target takes damage equal to the barbarian's Strength
modifier and is moved back as normal. The barbarian does not need to move with the target if
successful. This does not provoke an attack of opportunity.
