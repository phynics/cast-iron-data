The aspect of the dolphin imparts clever mastery of the seas. Shifters with this aspect also embody
the size and ferociousness of large dolphins, such as orcas.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +4 competence bonus on Knowledge checks to identify creatures' abilities and
weaknesses.

At 8th level: The bonus +6.

At 15th level: The bonus +8.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a Large [dolphin](ref/monster/dolphin), but your space is
10 feet instead of 5 feet. While in this form, you gain a swim speed of 60 feet,
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) with a range of 10 feet,
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d8), and
the ability to [hold your breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for a number of minutes equal to 6
times your Constitution score before you risk drowning.

**At 15th Level:** The range of your [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) increases to
60 feet and your swim speed increases to 80 feet.

**At 8th Level:** The range of your [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) increases to 20
feet.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S), Slam (B)
