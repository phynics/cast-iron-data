The aspect of the wolf provides the senses and the attacks of the mighty wolf, whose instincts are
honed to track and take down its prey.

**Minor Form**

**Ability:** You gain the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability with a range of 10 feet, or, if you have
the scent ability, the range increases by 10 feet.

**At 15th Level:** It becomes 30 feet.

**At 8th Level:** The range of this scent or the increase to your scent increases to 20 feet.

**Special:** The range doubles if the opponent is upwind and is halved if the opponent is downwind.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a [dire wolf](ref/monster/wolf-dire-wolf). While in this
form, you gain a base speed of 50 feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), scent (30
feet), a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d8 damage), and you can make a
[trip](ref/rule/trip) attempt with your bite attack.

**At 15th Level:** You gain [Improved Natural Attack](ref/feat/improved-natural-attack) (bite).

**At 8th Level:** The range of your scent ability increases to 40 feet and you gain a +4 racial
bonus on Survival checks when tracking with scent.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S)
