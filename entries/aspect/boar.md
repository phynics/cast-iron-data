Those with the aspect of the boar are famously stubborn.

**Minor Form**

**Ability:** You gain [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard) as a bonus feat.

**At 8th Level:** You add your Hit Dice to your Constitution when determining the negative number of
hit points at which you die.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a [dire boar](ref/monster/boar-dire-boar-daeodon). While
in this form, you gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), the [scent](ref/umr/scent)
ability, a [gore attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d8), and a base speed of 40 feet.

**At 15th Level:** You gain 1 additional hit point per Hit Die you have, and you gain
[Awesome Blow](ref/feat/awesome-blow) as a bonus feat.

**At 8th Level:** You gain [Improved Natural Attack](ref/feat/improved-natural-attack) with your
gore attack, and you gain [powerful charge](ref/umr/powerful-charge) (+1d8 on your gore attack).

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Gore (P), hoof (B)
