**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 29\
The bull is a fierce charger and is adept at trampling its foes, pounding them to a bloody pulp.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Strength score.

At 8th level: The bonus +4.

At 15th level: The bonus +6.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a Large bull (use
[aurochs](ref/monster/herd-animal-aurochs) stats). While in this form, you gain a base speed of 40
feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent), and a
[gore attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d8 damage) with [powerful charge](ref/umr/powerful-charge)
(+1d8 on your gore attack).

**At 15th Level:** Your gore damage increases to 2d8 and you gain the
[Awesome Blow](ref/feat/awesome-blow) feat.

**At 8th Level:** You gain the [trample](ref/umr/trample) as per the universal monster rule (dealing
gore damage plus 1-1/2 times your Strength modifier).

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Gore (P), hoof (B), slam (B)
