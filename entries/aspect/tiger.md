The tiger is a powerful but graceful hunter of the wild, quietly stalking its prey and then taking
it down with ruthless efficiency.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Dexterity score.

At 8th level: This bonus +4.

At 15th level: This bonus +6.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a [dire tiger](ref/monster/tiger-dire-tiger-smilodon).
While in this form, you gain a base speed of 40 feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision),
[scent](ref/umr/scent) (30 feet), a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (2d6 damage), two claw
attacks (2d4 damage), the [grab](ref/umr/grab) ability with both your bite and claw attacks, and
[pounce](ref/umr/pounce).

**At 15th Level:** You gain the [rake](ref/umr/rake) attack with your back claw attacks (dealing
additional damage equal to that of your claw attack).

**At 8th Level:** You gain a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S)
