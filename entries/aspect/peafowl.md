Peafowl are popular ornamental birds due to their spectacular colors, and they are often symbols of
royalty or the divine.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Charisma score.

At 8th level: The bonus +4.

At 15th level: The bonus +6.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a Small [peafowl](ref/monster/familiar-peafowl). While in
this form you gain [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), a fly speed of 40 feet (clumsy),
two [talon attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d3 damage), and a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks.

A peafowl can't use its fly speed to hover and must end any flying move action by landing or
perching on a solid surface.

A peafowl can perform a captivating dance targeting all creatures within a 15-foot cone. Each
creature in the cone that can see your dance must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half your
shifter level + your Charisma modifier) or be [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round.

**At 15th Level:** The duration increases to 4 rounds.

**At 8th Level:** The stunned effect lasts for 2 rounds.

**Special:** Whether or not a creature succeeds at the initial save vs your dance, a creature cannot
be affected by it again for 24 hours.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S), tail slap (B)
