**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 4\
The crocodile lurks in the water, unseen until it lunges forth with its powerful jaws.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +2 competence bonus on Swim checks and [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat
maneuver checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +4.

At 15th level: This bonus +6.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a [crocodile](ref/monster/crocodile). While in this form,
you gain a swim speed of 30 feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), a
[bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d8) with the [grab](ref/umr/grab) ability, and a tail slap
attack (a secondary natural attack that deals 1d12 points of damage).

**At 15th Level:** You gain [Improved Natural Attack](ref/feat/improved-natural-attack) with your
bite attack, and when you successfully grapple a foe, you can knock your grappled foe
[prone](ref/rule/prone).

**At 8th Level:** Once per minute as a **free action**, you can increase your land speed by 20 feet
for 1 round.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S), tail slap (B)
