**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 5\
The octopus is a cunning hunter, able to contort its malleable body with ease.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +4 competence bonus on Escape Artist checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of an [octopus](ref/monster/octopus). While in this form,
you gain the [aquatic](ref/subtype/aquatic) subtype, a swim speed of 30 feet,
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d3), a
tentacles attack (no damage) with the [grab](ref/umr/grab) ability, and
[Multiattack](ref/feat/multiattack) as a bonus feat.

**At 15th Level:** While within water, you can emit a 10-foot-radius sphere of ink once per minute
as a **swift action**. This ink provides [total concealment](ref/rule/concealment) and persists for
1 minute. Also, you now deal an amount of bludgeoning damage with your tentacles attack equal to the
damage dealt by your _shifter claws._

**At 8th Level:** You gain the [jet](ref/umr/jet) ability (200 feet).

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S), tentacle (B)
