The snake is a sneaky and lethal foe who strikes unexpectedly with deadly effect, while avoiding
opportune strikes made in return.

**Minor Form**

**Ability:** You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls when making attacks of opportunity and a +2 dodge
bonus to your AC against attacks of opportunity.

At 8th level: p!These bonuses +4.

At 15th level: p!These bonuses +6.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of an [emperor cobra](ref/monster/snake-emperor-cobra).
While in this form, you gain a base speed of 30 feet, a climb speed of 30 feet, a swim speed of 30
feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent) (30 feet), a
[bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (2d6 damage), and the
[Combat Reflexes](ref/feat/combat-reflexes) feat. If you already have Combat Reflexes or your
Dexterity score is not high enough to gain more attacks of opportunity from Combat Reflexes, you can
instead make one additional attack of opportunity each round.

**Action:** At 15th Level

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**At 8th Level:** You gain a +4 racial bonus on Acrobatics and Stealth checks.

When you make an attack of opportunity with your bite attack, that attack also applies the following
poison.

**Poison**

**Type:** Poison (Bite-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your shifter level + your Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Constitution damage 1d3

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S), tail slap (B)
