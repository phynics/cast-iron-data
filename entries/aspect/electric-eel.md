**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 4\
The aspect of the electric eel imparts the ability to charge attacks with electricity and to slip
effortlessly out of danger.

**Minor Form**

**Ability:** Your attacks deal 1 point of electricity damage in addition to their normal damage.

**At 15th Level:** The extra damage increases to 1d6.

**At 8th Level:** The extra damage becomes 1d3.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of an [electric eel](ref/monster/eel-electric-eel). While in
this form, you gain a swim speed of 30 feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision),
electricity [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5, a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d6), and
a tail attack (a secondary natural touch attack that deals 1d6 points of electricity damage).

**At 15th Level:** On a critical hit with your tail attack, the creature struck must succeed at a
Fortitude save (DC = 10 + half your shifter level + your Constitution modifier) or be
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1d4 rounds.

**At 8th Level:** Your electricity resistance increases to 10 and you gain a +4 racial bonus on
Escape Artist checks.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S), tail slap (B)
