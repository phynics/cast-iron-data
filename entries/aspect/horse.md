**Sources:** Heroes of Golarion pg 22\
The aspect of the horse grants speed and maneuverability. A horse's alternate attack is hoof (B).

**Minor Form**

**Ability:** You gain the [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance) feat as well as an enhancement bonus of +5
feet to your base speed.

**At 15th Level:** Your enhancement bonus to your base speed increases to +15.

**At 8th Level:** You gain the [Run](ref/feat/run) feat. If you already have the Run feat, you
instead can run at 6 times your base speed when using the Run feat.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a [horse](ref/monster/horse). While in this form, you
gain a base speed of 50 feet, [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [scent](ref/umr/scent)
(30 feet), and the ability to move before and after an attack, as if you had the
[Ride-By Attack](ref/feat/ride-by-attack) feat.

**At 15th Level:** You gain [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun) as a bonus feat, even if
you do not meet the prerequisites.

**At 8th Level:** You can move through threatened squares without provoking attacks of opportunity,
and you gain [trample](ref/umr/trample), as the universal monster rule, dealing your _shifter claw_
damage.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Hoof (B)
