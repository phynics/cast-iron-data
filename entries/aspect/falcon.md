**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 29\
The falcon senses all and has the speed to hunt down even the most elusive prey. Those who take this
aspect gain enhanced abilities to perceive and reconnoiter the wilderness - little escapes this
shifter's attention.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +4 competence bonus on Perception checks.

At 8th level: The bonus +6.

At 15th level: The bonus +8.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a Small [falcon](ref/monster/familiar-hawk). While in
this form, you gain a base speed of 30 feet, a [bite attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d4 damage),
two claw attacks (1d3 damage), a fly speed of 60 feet (good),
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), and a +4 racial bonus on vision-based Perception
checks.

**At 15th Level:** You gain [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) with a range of 60 feet and your fly
speed increases to 90 feet (perfect).

**At 8th Level:** You gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 120 feet, and your
racial bonus on Perception checks increases to +6.

**Functions As:** [Beast Shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2)

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Bite (B, P, S), talon (S)
