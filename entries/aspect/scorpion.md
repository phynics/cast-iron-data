**Sources:** Wilderness Origins pg 5\
The aspect of the scorpion invokes a hidden menace with a poisonous sting.

**Minor Form**

**Passive:** You gain a +2 competence bonus on Stealth checks and [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat
maneuver checks.

At 8th level: This bonus +4.

At 15th level: This bonus +6.

**Major Form**

**Ability:** Your shape changes to that of a [giant scorpion](ref/monster/scorpion-giant-scorpion).
While in this form, you gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet,
[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) with a range of 10 feet, two primary
[claw attacks](ref/umr/natural-attacks) (1d6) with the [grab](ref/umr/grab) ability, and a secondary
sting attack (1d6).

**Action:** At 15th Level

**Functions As:** [Vermin Shape II](ref/spell/vermin-shape-2)

**At 8th Level:** The range of your [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) increases to 20 feet, and you
gain a +4 racial bonus on Climb and Perception checks.

When you make an attack of opportunity with your sting attack or maintain a grapple and deal damage
with your sting attack, that attack also applies the following poison.

**Poison**

**Type:** Poison (Sting-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 your shifter level + your Con modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d3

**Cure:** 2 consecutive saves

**Alternate Natural Attacks**

**Details:** Pincers (B), sting (P)
