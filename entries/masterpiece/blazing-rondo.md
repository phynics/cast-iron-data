## Blazing Rondo (Oratory, Percussion)

**Sources:** Elemental Master's Handbook

Thundering rhythms quicken your allies' movements, but the effort of keeping up with the beat is
wearying.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (oratory) 7 ranks or Perform (percussion) 7 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 3rd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** Up to one ally per bard level gains the benefits of [haste](ref/spell/haste) while you
maintain this masterpiece, except the bonus to AC and on attack rolls and Reflex saves is
[one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your bard level. These allies must be within 60 feet
of you to receive this benefit. When you cease performing this masterpiece, any creature that
received this benefit for at least 3 rounds must succeed at a Fortitude save at this masterpiece's
DC or be [fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued) for twice as many rounds as they were affected.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 round of bardic performance per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 standard action.
