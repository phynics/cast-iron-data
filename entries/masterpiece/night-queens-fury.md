## Night Queen's Fury (Act, Oratory)

**Sources:** Distant Realms

You lay a curse on those who have wronged you.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (act) or Perform (oratory) 7 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 3rd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** You scream out a litany of every way you have been harmed by others, from physical
abuse, to verbal insults, to subtle harassment, to any other number of contemptuous actions that
denied you something you were rightfully due. When you complete the performance, you fall
[prone](ref/rule/prone), the emotion or power behind your speech driving you to your knees. Any
creature that damaged you within the last round must succeed at a Will save or be cursed, as per the
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse) spell.\~\~\~Once per day, if you are reduced to fewer than 0
hit points, you can activate this bardic masterpiece as an **immediate action** before you become
[dying](ref/misc/dying) or [dead](ref/misc/dead) as long as you have at least 3 rounds of bardic
performance remaining.

**Use**

**Details:** 3 rounds of bardic performance.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 full-round action (see text).
