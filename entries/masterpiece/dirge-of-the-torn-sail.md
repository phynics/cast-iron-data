## Dirge of the Torn Sail (Sing, String)

**Sources:** Blood of the Sea

Your ominous refrain warns away those who would trespass in your domain.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing) or Perform (string) 7 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** Your song warns of the terrible fate awaiting anyone entering your territory. All
creatures within 30 feet who can hear your performance must succeed at a Will saving throw or be
compelled to flee from you, as per [greater command](ref/spell/command-greater). For creatures on a
ship, this effect means taking efforts to alter course and sail away. An affected creature continues
its efforts for as long as the performance continues, and it reacts with violence to anyone
attempting to prevent it from fleeing. Abilities that extend the duration of a bardic performance
(such as [Lingering Performance](ref/feat/lingering-performance)) affect this masterpiece.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 standard action.
