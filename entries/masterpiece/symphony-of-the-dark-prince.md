## Symphony of the Dark Prince (Sing)

**Sources:** Distant Realms

You contort your vocal cords to produce an eerie, melodious song about the wonders of
[Dispater's](ref/faith/dispater) court.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing) 15 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 5th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** The sinisterly seductive song bores its way into the target's flesh, levitating it as
per [reverse gravity](ref/spell/reverse-gravity) as long as the bard maintains the performance.
Although the symphony does have words - and is often sung in Infernal - this is not a
language-dependent effect.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 round of bardic performance per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 standard action.
