## Kaminari Drums (Percussion)

You beat a thunderous cacophony that strikes your foes with the fury of the [kami](ref/family/kami)
of storms.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (percussion) 10 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 4th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** You produce a thunderous, rolling clamor in imitation of a storm kami's drums,
culminating in a resounding blow akin to a massive thunderclap. At the completion of this
performance, you call down a smiting bolt of lightning and thunder to strike your enemies. This
functions as [flame strike](ref/spell/flame-strike), with a caster level equal to your bard level,
but half the damage is electricity damage and the other half is not subject to any energy
resistance.

**Use**

**Details:** 5 rounds of bardic performance.

**Action**

**Details:** 5 full rounds.
