## Rondeau of Heavenly Order (Sing)

Your carefully structured song imposes rigid order on your listeners.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing) 4 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** This song follows a set repetition of verses and refrains with a pattern of syllables
that remains constant from line to line. As you sing, listeners find their actions bound by your
song's relentless order.\~\~\~One enemy per bard level within listening range must make a successful
Will save or find its movements hampered. A creature that fails its save can move only in a straight
line each turn. The creature can choose which direction it wishes to move each turn, but it can move
only along a straight line in that direction, and cannot move around corners or otherwise deviate
from this line until its next turn.\~\~\~This effect lasts as long as you maintain the performance.
Although this song has words, it is not language-dependent.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 round.
