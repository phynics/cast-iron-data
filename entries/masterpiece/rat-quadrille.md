## Rat Quadrille (Percussion, Sing)

**Sources:** Magical Marketplace

This repetitive, distracting melody wears down foes.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (percussion) or Perform (sing) 4 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** You tap out or sing a diverting tune that sticks in the mind of the target, causing the
target to take a -2 penalty on attack rolls and a -4 penalty on
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks for 10 minutes. A successful Will save halves the
penalties and reduces the duration to 1 minute. This ability is a mind-affecting compulsion effect
and relies on audible components.

**Use**

**Details:** 3 bardic performance rounds.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 standard action
