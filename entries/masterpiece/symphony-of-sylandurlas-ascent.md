## Symphony of Sylandurla's Ascent (Percussion, Wind)

This elaborate arrangement for traditional elven instruments evokes Sylandurla's heroic climb up the
spire from the planar city of Axis to the Boneyard, where it's said that she sang 8,008 tales to win
elves their immunity to magical sleep.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (percussion or wind) 7 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 3rd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** All allies within 30 feet become immune to one of the following (your choice) while
they can hear your performance: charm effects, the [dazed](ref/misc/dazed) condition, fear effects,
magical sleep, [paralysis](ref/rule/paralysis), the [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) condition, or
the [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) condition. You must choose the condition before activating the
performance and can't change your choice during the performance.\~\~\~If any affected ally currently
has the chosen condition, she can attempt a new saving throw against it each round she can hear your
song. Success on this saving throw has its usual benefit, but failure has no effect at all. This
performance relies on audible components.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 full round.
