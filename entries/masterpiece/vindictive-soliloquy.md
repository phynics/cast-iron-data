## Vindictive Soliloquy (Act, Oratory, Sing)

Your bombastic speech creates booming reverberations of sound.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (act), Perform (oratory), or Perform (sing) 10 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 4th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** You provide an impassioned monologue from the point of view of a noble queen, voicing
her inner turmoil at being betrayed by her own sister and calling for justice to strike from the
heavens. If you perform this masterpiece with the Perform (sing) skill, it is a tempestuous aria
instead of a rousing soliloquy.\~\~\~Upon completing the performance, you may conjure bolts of
lightning as though you had cast [call lightning storm](ref/spell/call-lightning-storm), except the
bolts are made of elemental thunder and deal sonic damage rather than electricity damage. The bolts
do not increase in damage if outdoors in a stormy area. Instead, in any environment, you may attempt
a DC 25 Perform check as part of the **standard action** to call a bolt. If your Perform check is
successful, that bolt deals 5d10 points of sonic damage instead of 5d6.

**Use**

**Details:** 5 bardic performance rounds.

**Action**

**Details:** 5 full rounds.
