## The Dance of 23 Steps (Dance)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic

This complex dance makes you difficult to strike.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (dance) 4 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** The shuffling steps, bends, and leaps of this intricate dance make you a difficult
target to hit, but also make it more difficult for you to perform other actions. When using this
masterpiece, you take a -2 penalty on melee attack rolls and combat maneuver checks, and you must
make a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check to cast any spell (DC 15 + the spell's level),
but you gain a +2 dodge bonus to your Armor Class.\~\~\~When you have 8 ranks in Perform (dance),
and every 4 ranks thereafter, the penalty increases by -1 and the dodge bonus increases by
+1.\~\~\~You can combine this masterpiece with [fighting defensively](ref/misc/fighting-defensively)
and [Combat Expertise](ref/feat/combat-expertise), but not [total defense](ref/misc/total-defense).
When you use this masterpiece, it lasts until the start of your next turn. Abilities that extend the
duration of a bardic performance (such as [Lingering Performance](ref/feat/lingering-performance))
affect this masterpiece; this allows you to get multiple rounds of its benefit (and its penalties)
at the cost of only 1 round of bardic performance.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 free action.
