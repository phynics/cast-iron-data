## Wildsoul Aria (Sing, Wind)

**Sources:** Heroes from the Fringe

Channeling the mysterious power of [the First World](ref/rule/the-first-world), you grant the
listener the gift - or curse - of an animal's shape.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing) 7 ranks or Perform (wind) 7 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 4th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** When you complete the performance, one creature within 30 feet is affected by
[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1).\~\~\~If your caster level is at least 10th, you can affect
that creature with [baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph) instead; an affected creature
automatically succeeds at the spell's secondary save.\~\~\~If your caster level is at least 15th and
you spend 10 additional rounds of bardic performance, you can affect up to one creature per 3 bard
levels with [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2) instead. This performance allows a Fortitude
save instead of a Will save to resist its effects.

**Use**

**Details:** 10 rounds of bardic performance.

**Action**

**Details:** 3 full rounds.
