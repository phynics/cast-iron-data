## Spirit of the Horse (Strings)

**Sources:** Disciple's Doctrine

This mournful song, which is typically played upon a traditional instrument known as a morin khuur,
empowers animal companions.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (strings) 7 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 3rd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** You play a soulful melody inspired by the legend in which a boy's prized horse, slain
by a wicked lord, returns in spirit form and instructs the boy to construct an instrument from its
body so that neither boy nor horse would be lonely.\~\~\~Upon the completion of this performance,
you inspire the animal (or similar) companions, familiars, and mounts of allies who can hear the
performance to avoid a similar fate, granting them the benefits of
[greater magic fang](ref/spell/magic-fang-greater) with a caster level equal to your bard level.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 round of bardic performance per creature affected.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 round.
