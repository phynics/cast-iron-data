## Music Beyond the Spheres (Dance, Sing, String)

You use your own life force to create a phantasmagorical impression of eldritch vibrations with your
wild, flailing dance and erratic tones. The performance unravels and remakes the fabric of reality
around you according to your designs.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (dance, sing, or string) 13 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 5th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** When you enact this unnerving bardic performance, you take 2 points of
[Constitution drain or Wisdom drain](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) (your choice) to create an
effect similar to [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish), except that the effect is interpreted by
an alien entity of the Dark Tapestry. If you have at least 17 ranks in Perform (dance, sing, or
string), and you destroy a magic or technological item worth at least 25,000 gp as a material
component and take 4 points of Constitution drain or Wisdom drain, you can instead produce the
effects of a [wish](ref/spell/wish) with this performance. The GM interprets how precisely the
effects of this bardic performance are granted by the entity that you contact. This performance has
audible and visual components.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 round of bardic performance.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 full round action.
