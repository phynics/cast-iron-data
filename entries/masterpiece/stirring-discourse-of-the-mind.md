## Stirring Discourse of the Mind (Oratory)

You lecture your audience on the merits of studious endeavors and inspire them with new insights
into the subject matter. As a result of your discourse, those who listen become more receptive to
knowledge.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (oratory) 4 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** Upon completion of your lecture, anyone who listened to your performance gains a +4
insight bonus on Knowledge checks to perform [research](ref/rule/research) in a library, and on
Spellcraft checks to identify the properties of magic items. This bonus lasts for 10 minutes.

**Use**

**Details:** 2 rounds of bardic performance.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 minute.
