## The Bear's Jig (String, Wing)

Your music makes animals act like people.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (string) or Perform (wind) 11 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 4th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** In the Varisian musical The Bear, a lively tune plays whenever the eponymous animal
tumbles onto stage. When played with skill, the song actually has the power to grant an animal
temporary intelligence.\~\~\~ Choose a non-hostile animal within line of sight that can see and hear
you perform. This masterpiece affects the target as the [awaken](ref/spell/awaken) spell for as long
as you maintain the performance. Abilities that extend the duration of a bardic performance (such as
[Lingering Performance](ref/feat/lingering-performance)) affect this masterpiece.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 round of bardic performance per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 full round.
