## Melody of Surrender (Sing, Wind)

**Sources:** Heroes of the High Court

This gentle, relaxing tune makes its listeners calm and receptive.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing) or Perform (wind) 4 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** This lilting tune is soothing and repetitive. When you activate the performance, choose
one creature per bard level within close range. Any targeted creature must succeed at a Will save or
take a -2 penalty on saving throws against enchantment effects and on Sense Motive checks. This
effect lasts for as long as you maintain the performance.\~\~\~Although this performance does have
words, it is not a language-dependent effect.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 round.
