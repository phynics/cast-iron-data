## The Canticle of Joy (Sing)

Your haunting melody is capable of shattering the very minds of those who hear it.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing) 7 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 3rd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** This agonizing eulogy gets into a listener's blood, and bestows on him the phantom
sensation of all the agonies suffered by [Zon-Kuthon's](ref/faith/zon-kuthon) most pious worshipers.
One target within range is cursed as though affected by [bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse) as
long as the bard maintains the performance. Although this lullaby does have words, it is not a
language-dependent effect.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 round.
