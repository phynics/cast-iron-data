## Banshee's Requiem (Sing)

**Sources:** Horror Realms

By filling your performance with the mournful angst of a lost love, your song evokes the inexorable
pull of the grave upon every living thing.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing) 17 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 6th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** All living creatures you select within 30 feet at the start of your turn each round
gain 2 [negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels) unless they succeed at a
Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 your bard level + your Charisma bonus). This is a death effect
and a sonic effect. This performance has audible components.

**Use**

**Details:** 3 rounds of bardic performance per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 full round action.
