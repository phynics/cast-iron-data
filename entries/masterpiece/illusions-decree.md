## Illusion's Decree (Comedy, Oratory)

**Sources:** The Harrow Handbook

These stories of wiliness and mystery inspire listeners to see through deceptions.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (comedy) 5 ranks or Perform (oratory) 5 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** These tales include the stories of The Marriage, The Queen Mother, and The Rakshasa,
among others. While performing this masterpiece, you gain a +4 bonus on saving throws to disbelieve
illusions. You can expend 1 round of bardic performance for each ally other than yourself whom you
want to gain this bonus.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round per round, +1 round per ally affected.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 full round.
