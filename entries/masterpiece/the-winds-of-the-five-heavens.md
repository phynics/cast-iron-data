## The Winds of the Five Heavens (Act, Oratory)

**Sources:** Ultimate Magic

You control the wind by telling a tale demonstrating the hubris of mortals.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (act) or Perform (oratory) 10 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 4th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** This short monologue tells the story of a village priest who thought to command the
gods. This so annoyed the gods of the weather that they gave him exactly what he wished for, and the
priest's long-winded sermons tore apart his temple and scattered his parishioners. When you complete
the performance, you conjure a great wind as if you had cast
[control winds](ref/spell/control-winds).

**Use**

**Details:** 3 bardic performance rounds.

**Action**

**Details:** 3 full rounds.
