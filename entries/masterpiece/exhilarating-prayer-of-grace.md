## Exhilarating Prayer of Grace (Dance)

**Sources:** Divine Anthology

You lead your audience through a series of stretches and exercises designed to warm the muscles and
increase blood circulation.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (dance) 4 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** After completing the series of exercises, anyone who observed the performance
(including yourself) gains a circumstance bonus on all Acrobatics checks and to CMD equal to 1/5 of
your bard level (minimum +1). This bonus persists for 10 minutes.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 round of bardic performance.

**Action**

**Details:** 3 rounds.
