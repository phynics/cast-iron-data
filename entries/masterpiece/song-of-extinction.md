## Song of Extinction (Sing, String, Wind)

**Sources:** Artifacts and Legends

**Note:** This masterpiece can only be learned from the
[Song of Extinction (Music Box)](ref/magic-artifact/song-of-extinction-music-box) artifact.

The clarion of the end times, this masterpiece is a meticulous cacophony that ends all life.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (sing, string, or wind) 15 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 6th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** The area within 100 feet of the performer begins quaking and moaning as if the earth
itself were in pain. The affected area is treated as per the spell
[earthquake](ref/spell/earthquake). In addition, all creatures in the area except for the performer
must make DC 25 Reflex saves, as individual earthen maws open beneath each. Those who fail fall into
a 100-foot-deep pit that quickly grinds shut, trapping them as if they were in the
[bury zone of an avalanche and trapped without air](ref/rule/avalanches). If any living, sentient
creature is killed by the Song of Extinction's effects, the performer ceases to [age](ref/rule/age)
for 1 year.

**Use**

**Details:** All of the performer's uses of bardic performance for that day.

**Action**

**Details:** 5 full rounds.
