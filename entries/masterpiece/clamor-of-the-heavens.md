## Clamor of the Heavens (Percussion, Sing)

**Sources:** Champions of Purity

This confusing dirge of melody and counter-melody was composed for choirs celebrating the various
martyrs of Ragathiel. It is intended to praise and magnify the glory of the empyreal lord, but to
the uninitiated it sounds like a battle hymn. In fact, in Varisia, this is what the song has become
to many.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (percussion or sing) 10 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** 5th-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** Evil creatures that hear the performance and fail a Will save against the effect are
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) and [deafened](ref/misc/deafened) for the duration. On a successful
save, they are [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) instead. Undead or creatures with the evil subtype that
fail their saves are [stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for the duration, while those that succeed are
[staggered](ref/misc/staggered).

**Use**

**Details:** 3 bardic performance rounds, +1 round per additional round of duration.

**Action**

**Details:** 3 full rounds.
