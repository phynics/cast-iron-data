## Tales of Twisting Steel (Act, Oratory)

**Sources:** The Harrow Handbook

This medley of tales inspires listeners to new heights of bravery and self sacrifice.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (act) 3 ranks or Perform (oratory) 3 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** This collection includes the stories of The Big Sky, The Rabbit Prince, and The
Sickness, among others. When you complete the performance, choose one adjacent creature. You and
that creature are affected as if you had cast [shield other](ref/spell/shield-other).

**Use**

**Details:** 3 bardic performance rounds.

**Action**

**Details:** 3 rounds.
