## The Tragedy of False Hope (Oratory, Sing)

**Sources:** Blood of the Ancients

You remind those around you of the mystic's warning and how danger was ignored in expectation of
Aroden's return.

**Prerequisites**

**Details:** Perform (oratory) or Perform (sing) 4 ranks

**Cost**

**Details:** Feat or 2nd-level bard spell known.

**Effect**

**Details:** You create a feeling of deceptive confidence in an enemy. When you activate this
performance, one target within 30 feet becomes [flat-footed](ref/rule/flat-footed) for as long as
you maintain the performance (Will negates).\~\~\~If you target a creature that has not yet acted in
a surprise round, the creature does not get to act in the surprise round. Any target with the
[uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge) class feature or an ability with a similar effect is
immune to this performance. This is a mind-affecting emotion effect.

**Use**

**Details:** 1 bardic performance round per round.

**Action**

**Details:** 1 standard action.
