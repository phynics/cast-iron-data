**Type** greater madness; **Save DC** 20\
**Onset** immediate\
**Effect** The afflicted character becomes [blinded](ref/misc/blinded) or
[deafened](ref/misc/deafened), loses another special sense (like [scent](ref/umr/scent) or
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense)), loses the use of limbs, or loses a special movement speed (like a
fly or swim speed). The GM chooses how the psychosomatic loss manifests.\
**Dormancy Effect** None

While there is nothing physically wrong with the afflicted character, some kind of mental trauma has
forced her brain to shut down sensory or motor systems. Furthermore, magical effects that typically
remove these conditions (such as [remove blindness/deafness](ref/spell/remove-blindness-deafness))
have no effect on this madness; the madness must be cured in order to regain the senses or motor
skills.
