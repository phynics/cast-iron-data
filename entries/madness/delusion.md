**Sources:** Horror Adventures pg 183\
**Type** lesser madness; **Save DC** 16\
**Onset** 2d6 days\
**Effect** The afflicted character believes something that is not true, and no amount of evidence
can dissuade him of the belief.\
**Dormancy Effect** None

A delusion is the persistence of a belief that no amount of evidence to the contrary can dissuade
the afflicted character from having. The belief is often that another specific person either loves
or wants to harm the afflicted character, or the afflicted character may have an inflated view of
his own abilities or importance. Sometimes the deluded character believes he suffers from an
affliction or persecution that does not exist.

The exact nature of the delusion is decided by the GM. Delusions are typically roleplayed, though a
GM might apply penalties to skill and ability checks having to do with the delusion, or decide that
such checks fail outright because of the character's delusion. In some circumstances, delusions can
be debilitating, while in others they are merely quirky or annoying to those around the character.
