**Type** lesser madness; **Save DC** 14\
**Onset** 1d4 days\
**Effect** -2 penalty on initiative checks, and morale bonuses are halved (minimum +0)\
**Dormancy Effect** None

A character suffering from melancholia struggles with severe pessimism and can often be slow to
respond to threats and events going on around him. He can seem quiet and withdrawn, and his sense of
enthusiasm is dulled. In extreme cases, a character suffering from melancholia becomes entirely
introverted and utterly withdrawn, even to the point of near catatonia.
