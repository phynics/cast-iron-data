**Craft DC** 24; **Cost** 9,625 gp\

[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This padded jumpsuit is made of resilient polymers interwoven with superconducting thread. It
provides modest protection from conventional and energy weapons. The wearer applies the armor's AC
bonus (including enhancement bonus, if any) against touch and ranged touch attacks that deal energy
damage. In addition, the inssuit provides resist energy 5 against cold, acid, and electricity.
