### Construction

**Craft DC** 33; **Cost** 20,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[graviton lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A gravity suit consists of several black, flexible straps attached to a framework of delicate
servos, thin plates, and small strips of black polymer plastic - this give it an appearance of being
ineffective armor. The armor bonus provided by an unpowered gravity suit is +0, but when activated,
the suit generates a field of gravitons around the wearer that grants a +5 armor bonus (this bonus
is a force effect). This bonus is doubled when it applies to the wearer's CMD score against
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [overrun](ref/rule/overrun), and [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempts,
and slows any fall as if the wearer were under the effect of a
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall) spell.

At the cost of 1 additional charge per minute, the wearer of a gravity suit can fly at a speed of 60
ft. (average). Activating this ability is a standard action.
