**Cost** 15,000 gp\
**Type** Light; **Weight** 5 lbs.; **Armor Bonus** +2\
**Max Dex Bonus** +6; **Armor Check Penalty** 0\
**Arcane Spell Failure Chance** 10%\
**Speed (30 ft.)** 30 ft.; **Speed (20 ft.)** 20 ft.\
**Capacity** 50; **Usage** 1 charge/minute

### Construction

**Craft DC** 29; **Cost** 7,500 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This suit of light woven-fiber armor covers the wearer from ankle to wrist and includes a hooded
cowl. Its surface consists of millions of tiny, scale-like lenses and LED screens. When powered on,
a chameleon suit analyzes its environment and generates digital camouflage. The wearer gains a +10
competence bonus on Stealth checks and can hide in plain sight (as a ranger in his
[favored terrain](ref/ability/favored-terrain)).
