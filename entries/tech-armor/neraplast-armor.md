### Construction

**Craft DC** 22; **Cost** 600 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

This lightweight, form-fitting bodysuit features a variety of pockets, straps, and a built-in
interface for automatically adjusting the various colors and patterns on its fabric. Selecting a
color or pattern is a full-round action that consumes 1 charge and provokes an attack of
opportunity. Capable of cycling through dozens of hues, the armor can even be used to adopt
sophisticated camouflage patterns that grant a +3 competence bonus on Stealth checks. Whenever the
wearer enters a terrain of significantly different coloration and background patterns, the armor
must be recalibrated to its current area to maintain this bonus.
