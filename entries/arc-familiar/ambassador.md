**Sources:** Ultimate Wilderness pg 210\
An ambassador speaks on its master's behalf and sometimes on behalf of its master's patron or other
extraplanar contacts.

**Special Requirement:** An ambassador familiar must be able to speak at least one language, either
as a special familiar ability (as in the case of a [raven](ref/monster/familiar-raven) or
[thrush](ref/monster/familiar-thrush)) or by being an
[Improved Familiar](ref/feat/improved-familiar). A familiar that can't speak (or can speak only to
its master or other animals) can't be an ambassador.

**Gained class skills:** bluff, diplo, intm

**Persuasive (Ex)**

**Passive:** An ambassador gains [Persuasive](ref/feat/persuasive) as a bonus feat.

**Replaces:** Alertness

**Enhanced Personality (Ex)**

**Passive:** An ambassador gains a Charisma score equal to the typical Intelligence score of a
familiar of its level, if that would be higher than its normal Charisma score. The familiar's
Intelligence score remains 6 (or its normal starting Intelligence for an improved familiar) and
doesn't increase by level.

**Replaces:** The familiar's Intelligence score advancement
