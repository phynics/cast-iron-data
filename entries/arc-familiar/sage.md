Sages are masters of useful facts, able to recall them for their masters' benefit, though this leads
many to become haughty and proud.

**Gained class skills:** ka, kd, ke, kg, kh, kl, kna, kno, kp, kr

**Dazzling Intellect (Ex)**

**Alters:** The familiar's Intelligence score and natural armor adjustment

**Passive:** A sage's Intelligence score is always equal to 5 + its master's class level, but it
gains natural armor increases as if its master's class level were
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) what of the actual class level.

**Sage's Knowledge (Ex)**

**Passive:** A sage stores information on every topic and is happy to lecture its master on the
finer points of a subject. A sage can attempt all Knowledge checks untrained and gains a bonus on
Knowledge checks equal to half its master's class level. Additionally, a sage gains 2 skill ranks
each time its master gains a class level. Its maximum number of ranks in any given skill is equal to
its master's class level.

**Replaces:** Alertness, the familiar's ability to share its master's skill ranks
