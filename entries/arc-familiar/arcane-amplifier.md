The secret of the arcane amplifier familiar was first discovered in Irrisen, where the winter
witches used white-furred foxes or hares to strengthen their spells. More recently, the practice has
spread to Nex and Cheliax, and to haunted Ustalav where necromancers use black-eyed ravens for their
spells.

**Echo (Su)**

**At 1st Level:** Once per day when delivering a touch spell, an arcane amplifier can apply the
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell) metamagic feat to the spell.

**At 13th Level:** It can use this ability three times per day.

**At 8th Level:** It can use this ability twice per day.

**Replaces:** Alertness, improved evasion

**Reverberate (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** It can use this ability twice per day.

**At 7th Level:** Once per day when delivering a touch spell, an arcane amplifier can apply either
the [Empower Spell](ref/feat/empower-spell) metamagic feat to the spell or use the
[Heighten Spell](ref/feat/heighten-spell) metamagic feat to increase its spell level by 2.

**Replaces:** Speak with animals of its kind, [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance)

**Resonate (Su)**

**At 13th Level:** Once per day when delivering a touch spell, an arcane amplifier can apply the
[Maximize Spell](ref/feat/maximize-spell) metamagic feat to the spell.

**Replaces:** Scry on familiar

**NOTE:** An arcane amplifier can apply only **one** metamagic effect to a single casting of a spell
using its echo, reverberate, or resonate abilities.
