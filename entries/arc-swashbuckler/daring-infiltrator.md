Not known for their flashy entrances or for standing out in a crowd, a daring infiltrator uses
stealth, disguise, and ruthless guile to pursue her goals. Some of these swashbucklers work to
undermine evil organizations, while others are master thieves or mysterious assassins.

**Gained class skills:** dis, stl

**Lost class skills:** diplo, perf, prof

**Bonus Feats**

**Alters:** Bonus feats

**Info:** In addition to combat feats, a daring infiltrator's bonus feats at 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th,
and 20th level can come from the following list: [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness),
[Antagonize](ref/feat/antagonize), [Cosmopolitan](ref/feat/cosmopolitan),
[Deceitful](ref/feat/deceitful), [Deft Hands](ref/feat/deft-hands),
[Disarming Threat Deed](ref/feat/disarming-threat-deed), [Persuasive](ref/feat/persuasive),
[Prodigy](ref/feat/prodigy), and [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus).

**Quick-Tongued (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A daring infiltrator gains a +1 bonus on Bluff checks.

**Replaces:** Charmed life

At 6th level: This bonus +2.

At 10th level: This bonus +3.

At 14th level: This bonus +4.

At 18th level: This bonus +5.

**Deeds**

**Ability:** A daring infiltrator gains the following deeds.

**Clandestine Expertise (Ex)**

**Ability:** Also, as long as she has at least 1 panache point, when she successfully aids another
with a Disguise or Stealth check, she grants the subject a +4 bonus on the skill check instead of
the normal +2.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A daring infiltrator with at least 1 panache point gains a +2 bonus on Disguise and
Stealth checks.

**Replaces:** Swashbuckler initiative

**Silence Is Golden (Ex)**

**Ability:** When a daring infiltrator with at least 1 panache point succeeds at a
[trip](ref/rule/trip) or [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver check, the target is rendered
mute for 1 round. For every 5 by which the result of the combat maneuver check exceeds the
opponent's CMD, the target remains mute for an additional round.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Menacing swordplay

**Special:** A mute creature cannot speak, use language-dependent effects or verbal components, or
use command words.

**Authoritative Bluff (Ex)**

**Ability:** A daring infiltrator can spend 1 panache point to reroll a Bluff check after the roll
is made but before the results are revealed. She must take the result of the second roll, even if it
is lower.

**Ability2:** Additionally, a daring infiltrator with at least 1 panache point gains a +5 bonus on
Bluff checks to pretend to be someone's superior (socially or in the military). If she succeeds at
the check, the target obeys any reasonable orders she gives as it would those of an actual superior
in the situation.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Bleeding wound
