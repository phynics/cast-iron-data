A number of organizations and kingdoms search for warriors who are brave (or foolish) enough to
wield firearms on the battlefield. The daring nature of swashbucklers often makes them ideal
candidates for such service.

**Weapon Proficiency**

**Info:** A musketeer gains proficiency with all simple weapons and martial weapons, as well as
one-handed and two-handed firearms.

**Replaces:** Weapon proficiencies

**Musketeer Instruction (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** A musketeer gains the benefits of the [Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) feature
with the [rapier](ref/eq-weapon/rapier) (this counts as having the Weapon Finesse feat for purposes
of meeting feat prerequisites), as well as both [Rapid Reload](ref/feat/rapid-reload) (musket) and
[Gunsmithing](ref/feat/gunsmithing) feats as bonus feats.

**Replaces:** Swashbuckler finesse

**Deeds**

**Ability:** The musketeer gains the following deed.

**Quick Clear (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Dodging panache

**Standard:** The musketeer can spend 1 panache point to remove the [broken](ref/misc/broken)
condition from a single firearm he is currently wielding, as long as the firearm gained that
condition through a misfire.
