Most swashbucklers call cities their home, but some prefer deserts, marshlands, mountains, or woods,
where the rough terrain grants them an advantage, and have little interest in the comforts of an
urban lifestyle.

**Gained class skills:** kna, stl, sur

**Lost class skills:** kno, sm, soh

**Deeds**

**Ability:** A wildstrider gains the following deeds.

**Subterfuge (Ex)**

**Alters:** Derring-do, swashbuckler's edge

**At 1st Level:** A wildstrider adds Stealth to the list of skills to which the
[derring-do](ref/swashdeed/derring-do) deed applies.

**At 15th Level:** She adds Stealth to the list of skills on which she can take 10 using the
[swashbuckler's edge](ref/swashdeed/swashbucklers-edge) deed.

**Replaces:** Dodging panache

**Adroit Step (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Replaces:** Kip-up

**Swift:** A wildstrider can treat a 5-foot square of
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) as if it were normal terrain. If she also spends
a panache point, she can treat all difficult terrain as though it were normal terrain until the end
of her turn.

**Keen Gaze (Ex)**

**Ability:** While a wildstrider has at least 1 panache point, she can ignore the miss chance from
[concealment](ref/rule/concealment) (but not total concealment) caused by normal and magical fog,
smoke, and undergrowth, but not other sources of concealment or a miss chance, such as
[blur](ref/spell/blur).

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Subtle blade
