**Sources:** Familiar Folio pg 17\
Witches' familiars are often tied to their patrons, enhancing and reinforcing the spellcasters'
connections to the sources of their magical might. Just as a [sorcerer](ref/class/sorcerer) can gain
a [bloodline familiar](ref/arc-sorcerer/bloodline-familiars), a witch can gain a patron familiar by
choosing one at 1st level in place of her standard familiar. A patron familiar acts in all ways like
a standard witch's familiar, with the addition of the special ability indicated below according to
the witch's patron.

**Spells**

**Info:** The witch gains her patron spells 1 level later than she normally would - gaining the
patron spell she'd normally receive at 2nd level at 3rd level instead, and so on.

### Patron Familiars

**Supernatural Speed (Su)**

**Ability:** The familiar is incredibly fast for its type. It gains an enhancement bonus of +10 feet
to each of its movement speeds.

**Patron:** [Agility](ref/patron/agility)

At 10th level: This bonus +20 feet.

At 20th level: This bonus +30 feet.

**Animal Speaker (Su)**

**Ability:** The familiar gains the ability to speak with animals of its kind at 1st level.

**Special:** If it would normally gain this ability at 7th level, the familiar gains the ability to
speak with all animals (as though constantly under the effects of
[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)) at 7th level.

**Patron:** [Animals](ref/patron/animals)

**Distracting (Su and Sp)**

**Ability:** The familiar gains Bluff and Sense Motive as class skills.

**At 10th Level:** The familiar can throw its voice at will, as if using
[ventriloquism](ref/spell/ventriloquism).

**Patron:** [Deception](ref/patron/deception)

**Elemental Touch (Su)**

**Ability:** Choose an energy type: acid, cold, electricity, or fire. The familiar gains resistance
5 to the selected energy type.

**Special:** Whenever the familiar delivers a touch spell that deals energy damage, it can change
the type of energy damage dealt to the selected energy type.

**Patron:** [Elements](ref/patron/elements)

**Endure Afflictions (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** This bonus also applies against magical diseases and poisons.

**At 20th Level:** This bonus also applies against curses.

**Passive:** The familiar is unnaturally talented at resisting bodily corruption. The familiar and
any ally touching it gains a +2 resistance bonus on saving throws against nonmagical poisons and
diseases.

**Patron:** [Endurance](ref/patron/endurance)

**Diseased Touch (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, the familiar can inflict [filth fever](ref/disease/filth-fever) with its
natural attacks for 1 round.

**At 10th Level:** The familiar can inflict [red ache](ref/disease/red-ache) instead.

**At 20th Level:** It can inflict [demon fever](ref/disease/demon-fever) instead.

**Special:** The familiar may be able to inflict other injury diseases instead at the GM's
discretion.

**Patron:** [Plague](ref/patron/plague)

**Fearsome Shadows (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, the familiar can use [cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) as long as it is in an area of normal or
[dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light), affecting a single living creature with a number of Hit Dice
up to the familiar's Intelligence score. Thus, creatures normally immune to
_[cause fear](ref/spell/cause-fear)_ because they have 6 or more Hit Dice may not be immune to the
familiar's cause fear spell-like ability.

**Patron:** [Shadow](ref/patron/shadow)

**Strength of Mind (Su)**

**Standard:** A number of times per day equal to its master's Intelligence modifier (minimum once
per day), the familiar can swap its Strength and Intelligence scores. This effect lasts for a number
of rounds equal to 1/2 the master's caster level.

**Patron:** [Strength](ref/patron/strength)

**Shapechanging Familiar (Su)**

**Ability:** The familiar is able to transform itself. For a number of minutes per day equal to its
master's witch level, the familiar can alter its appearance so that it looks like a different
creature of its type and size.

**At 16th Level:** The duration is now tripled.

**At 8th Level:** The duration doubles.

**Special:** This duration need not be consecutive, but it must be used in 1 minute increments. For
instance, a cat familiar could appear as any Tiny animal. This change is purely cosmetic, and
doesn't alter the familiar's statistics.

**Patron:** [Transformation](ref/patron/transformation)

**Familiar's Illusions (Sp)**

**Ability:** The familiar has a mischievous predilection toward simple illusions. Once per day, the
familiar can use a 0-level illusion spell on its master's spell list as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 10th Level:** It can also use a 1st-level illusion spell on its master's spell list once per
day.

**At 20th Level:** It can also use a 2nd-level illusion spell on its master's spell list once per
day.

**Patron:** [Trickery](ref/patron/trickery)

**Amphibious Familiar (Su)**

**Ability:** The familiar can breathe water for a number of minutes per day equal to 1/2 its
master's witch level. These minutes need not be consecutive, but they must be spent in 1-minute
intervals. If the familiar can already breathe water, it can breathe air for the same duration.

**At 10th Level:** The familiar gains a swim speed of 30 feet (or a land speed of 30 feet if it
already has a swim speed) while using this ability.

**At 20th Level:** The familiar can move through water as though under the effects of
[freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement) while using this ability.

**Patron:** [Water](ref/patron/water)

**Preternatural Wisdom (Su)**

**Ability:** The familiar gains a Wisdom score of 6. This score increases by 1 point at 3rd level
and every 2 levels thereafter (at the same rate as its Intelligence score). This may cause a
familiar whose Wisdom score is typically higher than 6 to start with a lower Wisdom score than
normal.

**Patron:** [Wisdom](ref/patron/wisdom)
