> _**Note:** This is a witch archetype, and has no relation to the [medium](ref/class/medium) occult
> class._

**Sources:** Undead Slayer's Handbook pg 20\
Mediums tap into the spiritual power of those who have yet to find their final rest. Their ability
to interact with these souls allows mediums to learn about the physical world through the
experiences of those who came before them. Eventually, mediums of immeasurable power learn to walk
alongside the very souls that haunt their dreams and visions.

**Patron**

**Choice:** A medium has a limited choice of patrons, and must select one of the following themes:
[ancestors](ref/patron/ancestors), [death](ref/patron/death), [occult](ref/patron/occult), or
[spirits](ref/patron/spirits).

**Ectoplasmic Aptitude (Su)**

**Ability:** She gains the [Ectoplasmic Spell](ref/feat/ectoplasmic-spell) metamagic feat as a bonus
feat.

**Flavor:** The medium can sling spells at ghosts and spirits as surely as at corporeal foes.

**Immediate:** The medium can apply the Ectoplasmic Spell feat to any spell she is casting without
increasing its spell level.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** 2nd-level hex

**Usage:** Intelligence modifier times/day

**Shared Subconscious (Su)**

**Flavor:** A medium can temporarily link her subconscious to that of an undead creature in order to
gain preternatural knowledge.

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** 6th-level hex

**Special:** This ability does not allow a medium to attempt a previously failed Knowledge check
again.

**Swift:** As long as the medium is within 100 feet of an intelligent undead creature, she can
target said creature and attempt any trained Knowledge check. If the target has ranks in the same
Knowledge skill, it also attempts the Knowledge check (if it doesn't have ranks, this effect fails).
If the target's result is higher than the medium's, the medium may choose to use the undead
creature's Knowledge result instead of her own, learning any information the undead creature has
about the subject in question.

**Soul Walker (Ex)**

**Ability:** A medium can drift into the spirit world, becoming incorporeal. When she does so, the
medium gains the [incorporeal](ref/subtype/incorporeal) subtype, taking only half damage from
corporeal magical attacks and no damage from nonmagical weapons and objects. Her spells deal only
half damage to corporeal creatures, but spells and abilities that do not deal damage function
normally.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** 20th-level hex

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per witch level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments

**The following witch hexes complement this archetype:** Charm, healing, scar, tongues, ward

**The following major hexes complement this archetype:** Speak in dreams, vision

**The following grand hex complements this archetype:** Summon spirit
