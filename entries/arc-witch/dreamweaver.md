**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 185\
A changeling dreamweaver draws upon her hag heritage to ply the dream realms in order to touch
mortal minds and souls, for good or ill. A dreamweaver witch has the following class features.

**Prerequisites:** Changeling

**Gained class skills:** sm

**Lost class skills:** heal

**Patron**

**Info:** A dreamweaver's patron is normally [portents](ref/patron/portents) or
[stars](ref/patron/stars).

**Spells**

**Info:** A dreamweaver replaces some of her patron's spells at the following witch levels.

**Spells:** Dream

**Spells:** Cloak of dreams

**Spells:** Ethereal jaunt

**Spells:** Moment of prescience

**Spells:** Astral projection

**Spells:** Sow thought

**Spells:** Dust of twilight

**Spells:** Deep slumber

**Spells:** Modify memory

**Dream Spinner (Su)**

**Ability:** When a dreamweaver casts a mind-affecting spell on a target that is sleeping because of
her [slumber](ref/hex/slumber) hex or a spell she cast, she adds +1 to the mind-affecting spell's
DC. If the target succeeds at the saving throw against the spell, it does not wake up, nor does it
have any recollection of having resisted a spell. If appropriate, the dreamweaver may incorporate
elements of a mind-affecting spell (i.e., _[sow thought](ref/spell/sow-thought),_
[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion), and so on) into the target's subconscious so it believes the
spell's effects originated in its dreams (the details of how these elements fit into the dream is up
to the GM).

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** 2nd-level hex

**Dream Thief (Su)**

**Ability:** A dreamweaver can alter the sleeping mind of any creature that is sleeping because of
her _slumber_ hex or a spell she cast. She can reshape one of the target's memories as if using
_[modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory)._ Alternatively, she may insert herself into the dreaming
memories of the target, prompting the target's mind to show her some specific information; the
dreamer's subconscious may resist, or try to deceive her with out-of-context memories, similar to
the way a corpse can resist when questioned with [speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead).

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** 6th-level hex

**Special:** A Will save negates either effect (DC equal to that of the witch's hex). Whether or not
the save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 1 day.

**Dream Possession (Su)**

**Ability:** A dreamweaver can take control of any creature that is sleeping because of her
_slumber_ hex or a spell she cast. This effect functions as [magic jar](ref/spell/magic-jar), using
the witch's familiar acting as the soul receptacle.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** 10th-level hex

**Special:** A Will save negates either effect (DC equal to that of the witch's hex). Whether or not
the save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 1 day.

**The following hexes complement this archetype:** Charm, slumber, beast of ill-omen

**The following major hexes complement this archetype:** Nightmares, vision

**The following major hexes complement this archetype:** Eternal slumber, dire prophecy
