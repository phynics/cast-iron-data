**Sources:** Planar Adventures pg 16\
Though all witches forge bonds with mysterious powers known as patrons, a pact witch takes this bond
to an extreme by forging an inexorable pact with the Outer Planes.

**Alignment**

**Info:** A pact witch's alignment must match that of his chosen plane (see below). A pact witch
whose alignment no longer matches his bonded plane becomes an ex-pact witch and loses all the
benefits of this archetype until he atones for his transgressions. In time, likely after benefitting
from an [atonement](ref/spell/atonement) spell, an ex-pact witch can forge a bond with another plane
that matches his new alignment.

**Planar Pact (Su)**

**Action:** Info

**Replaces:** Patron

A pact witch chooses one Outer Plane with which to forge a pact. This plane determines the alignment
a pact witch must maintain and the type of improved familiar he gains at 6th level (see below). Many
pact witches worship specific deities or demigods native to the planes with which they forged their
pacts.

When a pact witch chooses his plane, his body transforms in minor, cosmetic ways associated with
that plane. A pact with Hell might give a pact witch small devil horns, while a pact with the Heaven
might cause a pact witch to have a softly glowing golden halo, and a pact with Axis might give a
pact witch's skin strange rune patterns. The nature of this cosmetic change varies from witch to
witch, but has no mechanical effect.

**Planar Pact Spells**

**Info:** Instead of learning new spells from a patron, a pact witch learns new spells from his
planar pact, starting at 2nd level and again every 2 levels thereafter, as listed below.

**Planar Familiar**

**Action:** Ability

**Alters:** Familiar

**Gained:** At 6th Level

**Replaces:** 6th-level hex

A pact witch's familiar turns into an outsider native to his chosen plane. The familiar retains its
spells known; its memories; and its Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores, but is treated as a
member of its new race in all other respects. The type of creature the familiar becomes is indicated
below; if more than one type of familiar is listed, a pact witch can choose which type his familiar
becomes.

|Plane|Familiar|
| --- | --- |
|Abbadon|[Cacodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-cacodaemon) or [doru](ref/monster/div-doru)|
|Abyss|[Cythnigot](ref/monster/qlippoth-cythnigot) or [quasit](ref/monster/demon-quasit)|
|Axis|[Arbiter](ref/monster/inevitable-arbiter)|

|Hell|[Imp](ref/monster/devil-imp) or [tripurasura](ref/monster/asura-tripurasura)|
