Some witches can bend the warp and weft of fabric and thread.

**Weapon Proficiency**

**Ability:** A tatterdemalion is proficient with [bolas](ref/eq-weapon/bolas),
[nets](ref/eq-weapon/net), and [whips](ref/eq-weapon/whip).

**Alters:** Weapon proficiencies

**Cantrips**

**Alters:** Cantrips

**Info:** A tatterdemalion must choose [mage hand](ref/spell/mage-hand),
[open/close](ref/spell/open-close), and [prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation) as her
prepared cantrips at 1st level, and they require a piece of cloth, string, rope, or other woven
material (including clothing) as a focus. Any action she takes with these cantrips must involve the
focus.

**Special:** Starting at 2nd level, she can choose any cantrip for her remaining cantrip.

**Dancing Strings (Su)**

**Alters:** Spells

**Info:** A tatterdemalion adds [animate rope](ref/spell/animate-rope) to her class spell list and
spells her familiar knows. She can control her clothing and cloth, rope, or woven fabric she wears
in a manner otherwise identical to the [prehensile hair](ref/hex/prehensile-hair) hex.

**Replaces:** 1st-level hex

**Witchweaver**

**Ability:** A tatterdemalion gains the following unique hexes, with a caster level equal to her
witch level.

**Replaces:** 4th-, 8th-, 12th-, and 16th-level hexes

**Moth-Eaten (Sp)**

**Ability:** A tatterdemalion can make a touch attack that damages leather, rope, or cloth like
[rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp) damages metal.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per witch level

**Lace Weaver (Sp)**

**Ability:** A tatterdemalion can create cloth as per [minor creation](ref/spell/minor-creation) or
create one 5-foot cube of tangled skeins of cloth per witch level, affecting the target areas as per
[web](ref/spell/web).

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 4 witch levels

**Sinister Stitching (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day for every 4 witch levels she has, a tatterdemalion can conjure enchanted
threads to hinder a creature she can see (Fortitude negates). The tatterdemalion can choose to
either sew shut the target's eyes ([blinding](ref/misc/blinded) it) or mouth (preventing speech,
bite attacks, breath weapons, verbal components, command words, and the like).

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Special:** The target can end the effect with a successful Strength check (DC = 15 + 1/2 the
tatterdemalion's witch level) as a **standard action**, or by slashing the stitches with a magic
slashing weapon as a **full-round action** that provokes attacks of opportunity. Either way, the
victim takes 1d4 points of [bleed](ref/rule/bleed) damage and either is [dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled)
(for stitched eyes) or takes a -2 penalty on bite attack and damage rolls and a 20% chance of spell
failure with verbal components (for a stitched mouth). Either residual effect lasts 1 minute per
witch level.

Any creature can safely remove the enchanted threads with a minute of work and a successful DC 25
Heal check.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 4 witch levels

**Unravel (Su)**

**Immediate:** Once per day when a tatterdemalion is hit by a melee or ranged attack, she can
unravel herself, causing her body and gear to appear to collapse into a bundle of tattered rags and
strands while she teleports to any open square within 30 feet. She takes no damage from the
triggering attack, but she must have line of effect to the target square.

**At 20th Level:** She can use this ability twice per day.

**Gained:** At 16th Level
