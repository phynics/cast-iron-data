Mountains can be sanctuaries for witches hunted by society. Here they form bonds with the spirits of
the lofty reaches.

**Spells**

**Info:** A mountain witch replaces some of her patron spells at the listed witch levels.

**Spells:** Wall of stone

**Spells:** Stone tell

**Spells:** Statue

**Spells:** Repel metal or stone

**Spells:** Clashing rocks

**Spells:** Magic stone

**Spells:** Stone call

**Spells:** Meld into stone

**Spells:** Stoneskin

**Stone Spirit Hex**

**Ability:** A mountain witch can select hexes from the shaman's
[stone spirit](ref/shamanspirit/stone) as if they were witch hexes. She uses her witch level as her
shaman level to determine the effect of the hex, and Intelligence instead of Wisdom to determine its
DC.

**Alters:** Hex

**Mountain Beast Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A mountain witch can influence the attitude of mountain-dwelling animals, as the
druid's [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy) class feature but only on animals native to
mountainous environments. The mountain witch uses her witch level as her druid level for this
ability.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Replaces:** 2nd-level hex

**The following witch hexes complement this archetype:**
[Beast of ill-omen](ref/hex/beast-of-ill-omen); and the stone spirit's _crystal sight, lodestone,
metal curse, stone stability,_ and _ward of stone_ hexes.

**The following major hexes complement this archetype:** Beast eye, hidden home, hoarfrost, weather
control

**The following grand hexes complement this archetype:** Eternal slumber, natural disaster, witch's
hut
