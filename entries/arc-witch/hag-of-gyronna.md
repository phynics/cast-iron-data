**Sources:** People of the River pg 23\
Only female witches dare dedicate themselves to the [Angry Hag](ref/faith/gyronna), and those who do
so are feared for their ability to bend minds and sow discord. Some say these witches can watch
their enemies from afar and can even corrupt the minds of the most innocent or loyal, including
newborn babes and cherished pets.

**Patron**

**Choice:** A hag of Gyronna must choose [Deception](ref/patron/deception),
[Trickery](ref/patron/trickery), [Vengeance](ref/patron/vengeance), or [Water](ref/patron/water) as
her witch patron.

**Spells**

**Info:** A hag of Gyronna replaces several of her patron spells at the following witch levels.

**Spells:** Commune

**Spells:** Repulsion

**Spells:** Shapechange

**Spells:** Doom

**Spells:** Obscure object

**Curdle Thoughts (Su)**

**Ability:** A hag of Gyronna can choose to increase the save DC of any spell with the fear
descriptor she casts by 2.

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Passive:** A hag of Gyronna can insert negative emotions into the mind of a single target. She
gains a +2 bonus on Diplomacy and Bluff checks to sow suspicion, hatred, or other forms of
animosity.

**Replaces:** 2nd-level hex

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 witch levels

**Sunder Hope (Su)**

**Flavor:** A hag of Gyronna can violently dismiss her curses to counter magical boons and impose
her own capricious whims.

**Immediate:** After the hag of Gyronna has successfully affected a creature within 30 feet with a
mind-affecting spell, [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or hex with a duration of
longer than 1 round, she can choose to dismiss the mind-affecting effect. If she dismisses this
effect in response to another creature targeting her victim with a beneficial spell or ability, the
witch's effect is dismissed and the caster of the beneficial spell or ability must succeed at a Will
save (DC = 10 + 1/2 the witch's level + her Intelligence modifier) or the spell or ability is wasted
to no effect and the targeted victim takes 1d4 points of
[Wisdom damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain).

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** 8th-level hex

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 6 witch levels beyond 8th

**Hag Crony (Su)**

**Flavor:** A hag of Gyronna gains a blessing from the Angry Hag in the form of an alliance with a
night hag.

**Fullround:** Once per week, the hag of Gyronna can summon the night hag from the Shadow Plane. The
night hag serves the hag of Gyronna to the best of its ability as long as the witch's commands are
consistent with the night hag's alignment and goals, but flees back to its native plane after a
number of rounds equal to the witch's level or upon losing half or more of its hit points. The night
hag may provide the hag of Gyronna with other favors or gifts at the GM's discretion.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** 12th-level hex

At 15th level: She can call upon her crony 2 times a week.

At 18th level: She can call upon her crony 3 times a week.
