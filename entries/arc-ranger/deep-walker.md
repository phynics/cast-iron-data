Some rangers devote their lives to the woods, becoming hunters, protectors, and wilderness guides
among its diverse terrain types. The deep walker is instead a master of the strange terrain of the
deep earth, exploring its caverns and caves, becoming a master of the dark corridors most surface
dwellers fear to explore - and perhaps the reason they fear to explore them.

**Deep Knowledge (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A deep walker gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Knowledge (dungeoneering),
Perception, Stealth, and Survival skill checks while underground (in caves and dungeons).

**Replaces:** Favored terrain

At 8th level: The bonus +5.

At 13th level: The bonus +8.

At 18th level: The bonus +11.

**Rock Hopper (Ex)**

**Ability:** The deep walker gains a +5 bonus on all Acrobatics and Climb checks made to traverse
underground terrain. Furthermore, the deep walker ignores
[difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) created by underground terrain and subterranean
flora.

**Flavor:** A deep walker instinctively grasps for the most stable surfaces and holds on tighter
while moving over underground rock and stone.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Deep Walker Camouflage (Ex)**

**Ability:** A deep walker can use the Stealth skill to hide in underground environments, even if
the terrain does not grant cover or concealment.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** Camouflage

**One with the Stone (Ex)**

**Ability:** A deep walker can use the Stealth skill while he is underground, even when being
observed.

**Gained:** At 17th Level

**Replaces:** Hide in plain sight
