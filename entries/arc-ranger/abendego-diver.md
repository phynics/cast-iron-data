Named for the vicious and unending storm in the southern Arcadian Ocean, Abendego divers have pushed
their bodies to their limits, holding their breath to expand their lung capacity and swimming until
ocean travel is second nature to them.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**

**Alters:** Weapon and armor proficiencies

**Info:** An Abendego diver is not proficient with shields but is proficient with
[harpoons](ref/eq-weapon/harpoon) and [nets](ref/eq-weapon/net).

**Ocean's Blessing**

**Ability:** An Abendego diver adds the following spells to his ranger spell list at the listed
spell level. He still must select them as spells known, as normal.

**Replaces:** Track

**Spells:** Hydraulic push, wave shield

**Spells:** Buoyancy, elemental speech\|(water only), river whip

**Spells:** Hydraulic torrent, water breathing

**Spells:** Control water, ride the waves

**Deep Diver (Ex)**

**Ability:** An Abendego diver can [hold his breath](ref/misc/hold-your-breath) for twice as long as
usual (4 rounds &times& his Constitution score instead of the normal &times&2).

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Wild empathy

**Aquatic Terrain (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** An Abendego diver gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Knowledge (geography),
Perception, Stealth, and Survival skill checks while underwater.

**Replaces:** Favored terrain

At 8th level: p!These bonuses +4.

At 13th level: p!These bonuses +6.

At 18th level: p!These bonuses +8.

**Aquatic Adaptation (Ex)**

**Ability:** An Abendego diver gains a swim speed equal to his unmodified base speed. If he already
has a swim speed, his swim speed increases by 10 feet.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Special:** Because he has a swim speed, he gains the standard +8 bonus on Swim checks to perform a
special action or evade a hazard, the ability to take 10 on a Swim check even if distracted or
threatened, and the ability to use the run action while swimming if he swims in a straight line.

**Shark Sense (Ex)**

**Ability:** An Abendego diver gains the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability while underwater.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Swift tracker

**Killer of the Deep (Ex)**

**Ability:** He can notice other creatures by scent in a 180-foot radius underwater and can detect
blood in the water up to a mile away.

**Flavor:** An Abendego diver is peerless at detecting prey underwater and delivering killing blows.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Replaces:** Master hunter

**Standard:** He can make a single attack against a _favored enemy_ at his full attack bonus while
underwater. If the attack hits, the target takes damage normally and must succeed at a Fortitude
save or die. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + half the ranger's level + the ranger's Wisdom
modifier. An Abendego diver can instead deal an additional amount of nonlethal damage equal to the
creature's current hit points. A successful save negates this damage. An Abendego diver can use this
ability once per day against each of his _favored enemy_ types but not against the same creature
more than once in a 24-hour period.
