Planar scouts are rangers specializing in traversing the planes - and surviving their journeys.

**Gained class skills:** kp

**Lost class skills:** kd

**Planar Empathy (Ex)**

**Ability:** A planar scout can use _wild empathy_ only on animals with the
[extraplanar](ref/subtype/extraplanar) subtype. He can use this ability to influence outsiders with
an Intelligence score of 1 or 2, but he takes a -4 penalty on the check.

**Alters:** Wild empathy

**Planar Terrains (Ex)**

**Ability:** A planar scout gains a planar terrain. _Planar terrains_ function in all ways like a
ranger's _favored terrain;_ he must select a new plane each time he gains a _favored terrain._

**Alters:** Favored terrain

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Planar Bond (Su)**

**Ability:** Rather than forge a bond with an animal or his allies, a planar scout forms a bond with
a specific plane. The planar scout becomes infused with the essence of his plane of choice, taking
on physical characteristics of denizens native to his chosen plane as well as the abilities listed
below.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Hunter's bond

**Aligned Plane**

**Ability:** If the planar scout selects a plane with an alignment trait, he gains a bonus on attack
and damage rolls against outsiders and creatures with the extraplanar subtype whose alignments
oppose this alignment trait. This bonus is equal to one-fourth of his ranger level.

**Astral Plane**

**At 10th Level:** He can do so as a **swift action**.

**Move:** Each day, the planar scout can teleport up to 5 feet per ranger level. This must be used
in 5-foot increments.

**Ethereal Plane**

**Ability:** The planar scout can damage incorporeal creatures with mundane weapons as if they were
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapons.

**At 10th Level:** He can inflict critical hits and deal precision damage to incorporeal creatures.

**First World**

**Swift:** A planar scout can gain [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2 for a number of rounds
equal to his ranger level.

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day

**Plane of Air**

**Swift:** The planar scout can gain a fly speed (average) equal to his base speed for a number of
minutes equal to his ranger level (minimum 1 minute). This duration need not be used consecutively.

**Plane of Earth**

**Ability:** The planar scout gains a bonus to his natural armor. This bonus is equal to one-fourth
of his ranger level.

**Plane of Fire**

**Swift:** The planar scout can wreathe his body in flames. This grants the planar scout fire
resistance 5 and causes his melee attacks to each deal 1d4 additional points of fire damage.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per ranger level

At 9th level: The scout's fire resistance 10.

At 14th level: The scout's fire resistance 15.

**Plane of Shadow**

**Ability:** The planar scout gains [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and a +4 bonus on
Stealth checks.

At 8th level: The bonus on Stealth checks +4.

At 12th level: The bonus on Stealth checks +6.

At 16th level: The bonus on Stealth checks +8.

At 20th level: The bonus on Stealth checks +10.

**Plane of Water**

**Ability:** The planar scout gains the [amphibious](ref/umr/amphibious) subtype and a swim speed
equal to base speed.

**Planar Adaptation (Su)**

**At 16th Level:** He confers this protection to all allies within a 30-foot
[spread](ref/misc/spread).

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** A planar scout gains [planar adaptation](ref/spell/planar-adaptation) when on his
bonded plane.

**Replaces:** Evasion, improved evasion

### \[Planar Terrains\](ref/rule/planar-terrains)

A staggering array of terrains await exploration in the Great Beyond. Some are akin to the forests,
mountains, oceans, and plains of Material Plane worlds, but others are unlike anything found there,
such as the ghostly reaches of the Ethereal Plane, the living and horrific reaches of the deep
Abyss, the constantly burning inferno of the Plane of Fire, and the life-draining nothingness of the
Negative Energy Plane.

Some classes, like the ranger, interact directly with terrain, and class abilities like _favored
terrain_ can become complicated in a plane-hopping campaign. In games that are largely limited to
the Material Plane, the planes terrain category works fine, but if you intend to run a game set on
another plane, you should consider breaking up that category into specific planes (thus, a ranger
would choose Heaven or Ethereal Plane, or even a demiplane like Leng as a _favored terrain_), or
simply doing away with the planes category entirely. With this latter solution, you should work with
ranger players to help them anticipate the terrains featured in your upcoming campaign, with new
categories like crystalline (for realms in the Plane of Earth or under Elysium, where immense
crystal formations are common), living (for truly sentient realms like portions of the Abyss), or
void (for empty reaches in the Astral Plane or the energy planes) potentially becoming new options
for rangers to select from.
