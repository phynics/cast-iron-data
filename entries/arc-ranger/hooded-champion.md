The hooded champion lives on the periphery of civilized lands, and is often at odds with the forces
of law and order. He is frequently a hero of oppressed peoples, lurking in the woods near their
homes and trying to right the injustices inflicted upon them by the wealthy and powerful.

**Panache (Ex)**

**Ability:** The hooded champion gains the [swashbuckler's](ref/class/swashbuckler) panache class
feature. He regains panache through critical hits and killing blows when using a bow of any kind,
rather than when using a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Favored enemy at 1st level

**Special:** If the hooded champion has this ability and the panache ability from another class or
archetype, the panache points from the two sources do not stack, but the hooded champion regains
panache in any way either class feature allows him to. For example, a character with a Charisma
score of 16 who has both the hooded champion's and the swashbuckler's panache has a base of 3
panache points, and regains panache from critical hits and killing blows from both bows and light or
one-handed piercing melee weapons.

**Panache (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Flavor:** Text from the swashbuckler ability

At the start of each day, you gain a number of panache points equal to your Charisma modifier
(minimum 1). Your panache goes up or down throughout the day, but usually cannot go higher than your
Charisma modifier (minimum 1), though feats and magic items can affect this maximum. You spend
panache to accomplish deeds, and regain panache in the following ways.

Each time you confirm a critical hit, you regain 1 panache point. Confirming a critical hit on a
[helpless](ref/misc/helpless) or unaware creature or a creature that has fewer Hit Dice than
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your character level doesn't restore panache.

When you reduce a creature to 0 or fewer hit points while in combat, you regain 1 panache point.
Destroying an unattended object, reducing a helpless or unaware creature to 0 or fewer hit points,
or reducing a creature that has fewer Hit Dice than half your character level to 0 or fewer hit
points doesn't restore any panache.

**Deeds**

**At 1st Level:** The hooded champion gains the swashbuckler's
[derring-do](ref/swashdeed/derring-do) and [dodging panache](ref/swashdeed/dodging-panache) deeds,
as well as the _dead aim_ deed, below.

**At 3rd Level:** The hooded champion gains the swashbuckler's [kip-up](ref/swashdeed/kip-up) deed,
as well as the _hooded champion's initiative_ deed below.

**Special:** For all deeds, treat the hooded champion's ranger level as his swashbuckler level.

**Dead Aim (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** The hooded champion's can spend 1 panache point when making a single ranged attack
(not a full attack) with a bow to make a ranged touch attack instead. The target must be in the
bow's first range increment.

**Hooded Champion's Initiative (Ex)**

**At 3rd Level:** While the hooded champion has at least 1 panache point, he gains a +2 bonus on
initiative checks. In addition, if he has the [Quick Draw](ref/feat/quick-draw) feat, his hands are
free and unrestrained, and his weapon isn't hidden, he can draw a single bow as part of the
initiative check.

**Combat Style**

**At 16th Level:** The hooded champion gains the
[swashbuckler's edge](ref/swashdeed/swashbucklers-edge) and [cheat death](ref/swashdeed/cheat-death)
deeds.

**At 2nd Level:** The hooded champion must select the archery
[combat style](ref/ability/combat-styles).

**At 9th Level:** The hooded champion gains the
[swashbuckler's grace](ref/swashdeed/swashbucklers-grace) and [evasive](ref/swashdeed/evasive)
deeds.

**Replaces:** Wild empathy, endurance, evasion, improved evasion
