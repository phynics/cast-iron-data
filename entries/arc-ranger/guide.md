Many rangers are loners, but some choose to use their familiarity with the land to guide others
safely through the wilderness. The guide forgoes a _favored enemy_ to focus on the task or foe at
hand, and can pass his knowledge and luck on to his charges. A guide has the following class
features.

**Ranger's Focus (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Replaces:** Favored enemy

**Swift:** The guide can focus on a single enemy within line of sight. That creature remains the
ranger's focus until it is reduced to 0 or fewer hit points or surrenders, or until the ranger
designates a new focus, whichever occurs first. The ranger gains a +2 bonus on attack and damage
rolls against the target of his focus.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 3 ranger levels beyond 1st

At 5th level: This bonus +4.

At 10th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +8.

At 20th level: This bonus +10.

**Terrain Bond (Ex)**

**Ability:** As long as they travel with him, the ranger's allies leave no trail and can't be
tracked. The ranger can choose for the group to leave a trail, or even specific members of the group
to leave a trail if he so desires.

**Flavor:** The guide forms a bond with the land itself, enabling him to direct others in such
terrain.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** When in his _favored terrain,_ the ranger grants all allies within line of sight and
that can hear him a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Perception, Stealth, and Survival skill
checks.

**Replaces:** Hunter's bond

**Ranger's Luck (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** The guide can either reroll one of his attack rolls or force an enemy who just hit
him with an attack to reroll the attack roll. The ranger must take the result of the second roll
even if it is worse.

**Replaces:** Evasion

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 ranger levels beyond 9th

**Inspired Moment (Ex)**

**Free:** The guide can have an inspired moment once per day. The ranger gains the following
benefits until the end of his next turn. His speed increases by 10 feet. He can take an extra
**move** or **swift action** on his turn. He gains a +4 bonus to AC and on attack rolls, skill
checks, or ability checks. Finally, he automatically confirms any critical threat he scores.

**At 19th Level:** He can use this ability twice a day.

**Gained:** At 11th Level

**Replaces:** Quarry, improved quarry

**Improved Ranger's Luck (Ex)**

**At 16th Level:** The ranger's luck increases. He gains a +4 bonus on his rerolls made with the
_ranger's luck_ ability, or if he forces an enemy to reroll an attack, that enemy takes a -4 penalty
on the roll. This bonus or penalty is also applied on any roll to confirm critical hits.

**Replaces:** Improved evasion
