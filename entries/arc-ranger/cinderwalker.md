Cinderwalkers traverse burning wastelands and restless mountains, thriving in lands most creatures
avoid.

**Born to the Fire (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** A cinderwalker gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Knowledge (geography),
Perception, Stealth, and Survival checks when in volcanic or geothermally active areas. Such areas
include calderas, hot springs, lava plains, and volcanically formed hills or mountains. In addition,
the cinderwalker adds this bonus on any ability checks, saving throws, or skill checks to avoid
geothermal or volcanic hazards. A cinderwalker traveling through volcanic or geothermally active
areas leaves no trail and cannot be tracked (though he can leave a trail if he chooses).

**Replaces:** 1st favored terrain, wild empathy

**Special:** The cinderwalker can choose to improve this bonus in place of improving a _favored
terrain_ bonus.

**Inured (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** The fire resistance increases to 20.

**At 16th Level:** The fire resistance increases to 30.

**At 20th Level:** He gains immunity to fire.

**At 8th Level:** The cinderwalker gains fire resistance 10.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** A cinderwalker gains the benefits of [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements)
against hot environments.

**Replaces:** Hunter's bond

**Cinderwalk (Ex)**

**Ability:** A cinderwalker can move through any sort of solid volcanic terrain (such as ash, cooled
lava, or volcanic rock) at his normal speed and without taking damage or suffering any other
impairment.

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Passive:** The cinderwalker ignores fire damage from being near heat sources and touching heated
surfaces (but not from immersion).

**Replaces:** Woodland stride
