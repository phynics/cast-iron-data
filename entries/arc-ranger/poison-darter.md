In dense jungles, foliage and tight spaces hinder all but the most deceptively humble weapons:
[blowguns](ref/eq-weapon/blowgun) and poison.

**Prerequisites:** Grippli

**Debilitating Venom (Ex)**

**Action:** Ability

**Flavor:** A poison darter can concoct potent toxins that he can apply to his weapons.

**At 10th Level:** The the poison's Dexterity damage increases to 1d4, and two successful saving
throws are needed to cure the poison.

**At 15th Level:** The poison dart can choose to deal Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution damage
with the _debilitating venom,_ selected when he creates the toxin.

**At 20th Level:** The poison's damage die increases to 1d6.

**At 5th Level:** The frequency increases to 6 rounds, and the toxin [sickens](ref/misc/sickened) a
creature for 1 round every time it fails its saving throw to resist the poison.

**Info:** If he has the _toxic skin_ alternate racial trait\*, he can expend a daily use of that
poison to employ his _debilitating venom_ ability an additional time.

**Replaces:** Favored enemy, master hunter

**Usage:** A number of times/day equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his ranger level +
his Wisdom modifier

**Usage:** A number of times/day equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) his ranger level +
his Wisdom modifier

The _debilitating venom_ functions only for the poison darter and becomes inert if not used within 1
hour. Creating a _debilitating venom_ is a **standard action**, and it can be applied to a weapon as
a **move action**.

**Debilitating Venom**

**Type:** Poison (Injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the the poison darter's level + his Wis modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 4 rounds

**Effect:** Dexterity damage 1d3

**Cure:** 1 save

**Poison Use (Ex)**

**Ability:** A poison darter is trained in the use of poison and cannot accidentally poison himself
when applying poison to a weapon.

**Replaces:** Wild empathy

**Poison Style (Ex)**

**Ability:** In place of a combat style feat, a poison darter can select any rogue talent that
modifies sneak attacks or any alchemist discovery that modifies poisons he creates and applies,
using his Wisdom modifier in place of his Intelligence modifier and his ranger level in place of his
alchemist or rogue level for the purpose of meeting prerequisites. He can use the
[sticky poison](ref/discovery/sticky-poison) discovery to apply a single dose of poison to two
blowgun darts without reducing its potency.

**Alters:** Combat style feat

**Gained:** At 2nd Level

**Precise Dart (Ex)**

**Ability:** A poison darter gains a [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) identical to the rogue
class feature, but only when attacking with a blowgun. This deals 1d6 extra points of damage.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Hunter's bond

At 6th level: The sneak attack damage 2d6 damage.

At 8th level: The sneak attack damage 3d6 damage.

At 10th level: The sneak attack damage 4d6 damage.

At 12th level: The sneak attack damage 5d6 damage.

At 14th level: The sneak attack damage 6d6 damage.

At 16th level: The sneak attack damage 7d6 damage.

At 18th level: The sneak attack damage 8d6 damage.

At 20th level: The sneak attack damage 9d6 damage.
