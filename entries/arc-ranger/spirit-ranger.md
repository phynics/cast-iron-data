Some rangers nurture a connection with the spirits that reside in all things. By communing with
these spirits, the spirit ranger can gain glimpses of things to come. A spirit ranger has the
following class features.

**Spirit Bond (Ex)**

**Ability:** Instead of forming a bond with his hunting companions or an animal companion, the
spirit ranger forms a bond with the spirits of nature themselves. Each day, as long as he is within
one of his _favored terrains,_ the ranger can cast [augury](ref/spell/augury) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) with a caster level equal to his ranger level.

**Ability2:** In addition, he can call upon these spirits to cast any one ranger spell that he is
capable of casting, without having to prepare the spell.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Replaces:** Hunter's bond

At 12th level: He can cast a 2nd additional spell in this way.

At 16th level: He can cast a 3rd additional spell in this way.

At 20th level: He can cast a 4th additional spell in this way.

**Wisdom of the Spirits (Sp)**

**Ability:** The spirit ranger can use his _[augury](ref/spell/augury)_
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) even when he is not in one of his _favored
terrains._ If he is within one of his _favored terrains,_ the ranger can cast
[divination](ref/spell/divination) instead. Like _[augury](ref/spell/augury),_ the caster level of
the divination is equal to the ranger's level.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** Camouflage
