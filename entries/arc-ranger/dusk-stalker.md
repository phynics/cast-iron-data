**Sources:** Advanced Race Guide pg 110\
Hunters and guides through the Shadow Plane, dusk stalkers are rangers that thrive in shadow. Adept
at hunting in dusk, darkness, and twilight, these rangers excel at manipulating shadows.

**Prerequisites:** Fetchling

**Gained class skills:** kp

**Lost class skills:** kna

**Shadow Guide**

**Ability:** A dusk stalker picks his primary terrain normally, but only gains a +1 bonus on those
checks while on a plane other than the Shadow Plane, and gains a +3 bonus on those checks while on
the Shadow Plane.

**Alters:** Favored terrain

**Info:** When a dusk stalker gains the _favored terrain_ ability, that ability is modified in the
following ways.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Special:** Each time he chooses to add a bonus in a _favored terrain,_ he gains a +1 bonus on
those checks while on a plane other than the Shadow Plane, and gains a +3 bonus on those checks
while on the Shadow Plane.

**Shadow Bond (Su)**

**Ability:** The dusk stalker can manipulate shadows in a 5-foot square within 30 feet. That square
must be in an area of dim light or darkness (magical or otherwise). Enemies with an Intelligence
score within or adjacent to that 5-foot square take a -2 penalty to AC and on Reflex saving throws.
The harassing shadows last for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

**Flavor:** A dusk stalker creates a mystical bond with shadows.

**Gained:** At 4th Level

**Passive:** The shadows around a dusk stalker weave and swirl, confusing his enemies. When a dusk
stalker is fighting in [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light) or darkness (magical or otherwise), he
gains a +4 insight bonus on Acrobatics checks made to move through an enemy's threatened area or
through its space.

**Replaces:** Hunter's bond

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier times/day

**Dark Sight (Su)**

**Ability:** A dusk stalker gains the [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness) ability.

**Gained:** At 12th Level

**Replaces:** Camouflage
