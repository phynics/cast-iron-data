Those plying the lightless waters of the Darklands learn to use sound to augment their compromised
vision, providing insight into the hazards lurking below the surface. A Darklands sailor has the
following class features.

**Skilled Pilot (Ex)**

**Ability:** A Darklands sailor adds half his level (minimum 1) to Profession (sailor) and Survival
checks to navigate or avoid natural hazards in subterranean waterways.

**Replaces:** Track

**Keen Ear (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A Darklands sailor learns how to use sound to compensate for poor visibility when facing
threats above and below the water.

**At 13th Level:** He gains [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) with a range of 30 feet against
creatures touching water.

**At 18th Level:** His [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense) range increases to 60 feet
or equal to the range of his [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), whichever is greater.

**At 3rd Level:** He gains the [uncanny dodge](ref/ability/uncanny-dodge) rogue ability when
underground and either swimming or aboard a boat.

**At 8th Level:** He gains [improved uncanny dodge](ref/ability/improved-uncanny-dodge) under these
conditions, treating his Darklands sailor level as his effective rogue level for the purpose of
denying an enemy's sneak attacks.

**Replaces:** Favored terrain

**Special:** If the Darklands sailor becomes [deafened](ref/misc/deafened), he loses all benefits of
this ability until his hearing is restored.

**Quick Swim (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 7th Level

**Move:** A Darklands sailor can swim at his full speed without penalty.

**Replaces:** Woodland stride

**Subsonic Warning (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** Swift tracker

**Special:** A deafened creature can't benefit from a Darklands sailor's subsonic warning.

**Swift:** A Darklands sailor can provide the benefits of his _keen ear_ ability to the subjects of
his _hunter's bond._

**Usage:** Wisdom modifier rounds/day

**Hidden Depths (Ex)**

**At 12th Level:** A Darklands sailor can use the Stealth skill to hide while underground or
underwater, even if he doesn't have cover or concealment.

**At 17th Level:** He can use the Stealth skill even while being observed when underground or
underwater.

**Replaces:** Camouflage, hide in plain sight
