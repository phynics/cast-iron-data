**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 219\
**Aura** faint conjuration \[evil\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 10,600 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is carved from bone. If the wearer can cast _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_
spells, the wearer adds [cacodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-cacodaemon) to the 3rd-level list of
monsters she can summon with those spells, adds [ceustodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-ceustodaemon) to
the 5th-level list, adds [hydrodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-hydrodaemon) to the 6th-level list, adds
[leukodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-leukodaemon) and [piscodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-piscodaemon) to
the 7th-level list, adds [derghodaemon](ref/monster/daemon-derghodaemon) and
[meladaemon](ref/monster/daemon-meladaemon) to the 8th level list, and adds
[thanadaemon](ref/monster/daemon-thanadaemon) to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command
the wearer can use this ring to summon a cacodaemon, as if by the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be neutral evil; **Cost** 5,300 gp
