**Sources:** Inner Sea Gods pg 258\
**Aura** faint necromancy and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 5,000 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is carved from the flat part of a human skull and inscribed along the edge with images of
skeletons at a banquet. Any food the wearer eats or drinks is automatically cleansed as if by
_[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink)._ Once per day after eating an extravagant
meal (typically one worth at least 10 gp), the wearer gains the benefit of
_[false life](ref/spell/false-life)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [false life](ref/spell/false-life),
[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink); **Cost** 2,500 gp
