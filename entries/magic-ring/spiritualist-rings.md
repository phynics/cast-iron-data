**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 177\
**Aura** strong necromancy; **CL** 12th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 70,000 gp; **Weight** -

These four garish and gaudy rings must be worn on one hand, though they take up only a single ring
slot. They grant the wearer the ability to commune with and affect the dead in the following ways.

- The wearer can use _[speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead),_ as the spell, three times per
  day. It takes 10 minutes to use this ability.
- The wearer can use _[spectral hand](ref/spell/spectral-hand),_ as the spell, three times per day.
- The wearer's weapons gain the [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon special
  ability while the wearer is wielding the weapons.
- The wearer gains a +4 sacred or profane bonus on saving throws against positive and negative
  energy effects. The bonus depends on the wearer's alignment: good-aligned wearers gain a sacred
  bonus, whereas evil-aligned wearers gain a profane bonus. Neutral-aligned wearers pick either a
  sacred or profane bonus when the ring is first put on, which cannot be changed later.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead),
[spectral hand](ref/spell/spectral-hand), [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift); **Cost** 45,000 gp
