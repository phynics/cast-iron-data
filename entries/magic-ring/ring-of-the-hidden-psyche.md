**Aura** strong (no school); **CL** 18th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 160,000 gp; **Weight** -

This ring resembles a [ring of psychic mastery](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-psychic-mastery), but its
flawless sapphire serves as a vessel for the wearer's soul. It possesses all the abilities of a ring
of psychic mastery in addition to the following effects.

The wearer's soul instantly enters the ring upon the wearer's death, even if her body is utterly
destroyed. This counts as an _[object possession](ref/spell/object-possession)_ spell, except that
the possessing soul cannot animate the ring and must instead wait for someone to wear it. From
inside the ring, she can attempt a _[greater possession](ref/spell/possession-greater)_ on anyone
who voluntarily wears the ring (Will DC 23 negates). A successful save against the
_[greater possession](ref/spell/possession-greater)_ effect renders the new wearer immune to further
such attempts for 24 hours. Dispelling the _[object possession](ref/spell/object-possession)_ effect
on the ring banishes the soul from the ring to its appropriate afterlife. Otherwise, a soul can
remain in the ring indefinitely. While in the ring, the soul knows when a creature has put on the
ring. The soul can't otherwise sense the area around it or take actions other than attempting the
_[greater possession](ref/spell/possession-greater)_ effect.

A ring of the hidden psyche resists attempts to discern its true nature.
[Detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) reveals only the properties of a ring of psychic mastery,
though [identify](ref/spell/identify) or stronger magic can reveal the ring's full powers if the
caster succeeds at a DC 23 Will save.

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring),

[object possession](ref/spell/object-possession), [wish](ref/spell/wish); **Cost** 80,000 gp
