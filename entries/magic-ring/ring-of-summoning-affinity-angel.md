**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 218\
**Aura** faint conjuration \[good\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 7,200 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is cut from transparent crystal. If the wearer can cast
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells, the wearer adds
[cassisian](ref/monster/angel-cassisian) to the 3rd-level list of monsters she can summon with those
spells, adds [movanic deva](ref/monster/angel-movanic-deva) to the 7th-level list, and adds
[monadic deva](ref/monster/angel-monadic-deva) to the 8th-level list. Also, once per day on command
the wearer can use this ring to summon a cassisian, as if by the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be good; **Cost** 3,600 gp
