**Aura** faint conjuration \[chaos, evil\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 9,100 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is shaped to resemble a snake consuming its own tail. If the wearer can cast
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells, the wearer adds [doru](ref/monster/div-doru) to
the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds
[aghash](ref/monster/div-aghash) to the 4th-level list, adds [pairaka](ref/monster/div-pairaka) to
the 6th-level list, adds [ghawwas](ref/monster/div-ghawwas) to the 7th-level list, adds
[shira](ref/monster/div-shira) to the 8th-level list, and adds [sepid](ref/monster/div-sepid) to the
9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the wearer can use this ring to summon a doru, as if
by the [summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be chaotic evil; **Cost** 4,550 gp
