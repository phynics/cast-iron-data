**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 11,000 gp; **Weight** -

This steel ring is inlaid with [adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) swords and shields. The
wearer gains a +5 competence bonus on [Profession (soldier)](ref/skill/profession-any) checks, and
anytime the wearer gives or receives a numeric bonus from a teamwork feat or the
[aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action, that bonus increase by +1. By wearing the ring for 1
hour, a creature may imbue it with a teamwork feat he knows for 24 hours. During this time, any
creature wearing the ring may use that feat when cooperating with the creature that imbued it, but
not with other creatures even if they possess the same teamwork feat. If the ring is imbued with a
new teamwork feat, it replaces the previous feat.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), creator must have 5 ranks of Profession
(soldier) and at least one teamwork feat; **Cost** 5,500 gp
