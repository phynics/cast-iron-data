**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 3,000 gp; **Weight** -

When the wearer of this ring is [bloodraging](ref/class/bloodrager) and casts a spell, as a swift
action he can increase the save DC of the spell by 1. The wearer can do this a maximum of three
times per day. This has no effect on spells that do not require saving throws.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus), creator
must have the bloodrage class feature; **Cost** 1,500 gp
