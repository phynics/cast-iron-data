**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** -

This tiny ring of living mistletoe coils about the wearer's finger of its own accord. The wearer
gains a +2 competence bonus on [Handle Animal](ref/skill/handle-animal),
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature), and [wild empathy](ref/ability/wild-empathy)
checks, and a wearer with the [woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride) ability can move
effortlessly, without harm or impediment, even through magically created or manipulated plants. This
does not allow the wearer to move through solid objects or creatures.

Furthermore, the wearer can use _[shillelagh](ref/spell/shillelagh)_ once per day.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring),
[pass without trace](ref/spell/pass-without-trace), [shillelagh](ref/spell/shillelagh); **Cost**
3,000 gp
