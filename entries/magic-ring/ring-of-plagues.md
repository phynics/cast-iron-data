**Aura** moderate necromancy; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** -

This silver ring is set with a black opal. Once per day, when the wearer is reduced to half or fewer
of her maximum hit point total, whoever dealt the activating blow to the wearer is immediately
subjected to a _[major curse](ref/spell/curse-major)_ with unlimited range. A successful DC 19 Will
saving throw is needed to negate the spell. Rather than one of the listed curse effects, the curse
imposes a -4 penalty on the victim's saving throws and causes the victim to contract a disease
chosen by the wearer from the _[contagion](ref/spell/contagion)_ list without a save. The cursed
disease has no onset time, but the attacker is allowed the normal saving throw to avoid its effects.
It can be cured only by successfully casting both [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) and
[remove disease](ref/spell/remove-disease) within 1 minute of each other. Those exposed to the
cursed attacker thereafter must save against the mundane version of the disease or become infected
(though they are not cursed).

Once per day, if the wearer is killed or reduced to negative hit points, a plague of curses is
unleashed. The attacker and the attacker's five nearest allies or immediate blood relatives become
the targets of the curse described above. If the wearer is restored to at least 1 hit point, these
curses end, allowing surviving victims to recover naturally.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [contagion](ref/spell/contagion),
[major curse](ref/spell/curse-major), [spite](ref/spell/spite); **Cost** 10,000 gp
