**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 217\
**Aura** moderate conjuration; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 12,880 gp; **Weight** -

This coiling band is made of jade carved into the shape of a sea drake. If the wearer can cast
_[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spells, the wearer adds
[forest drake](ref/monster/drake-forest-drake) and [shadow drake](ref/monster/drake-shadow-drake) to
the 4th-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells, adds
[flame drake](ref/monster/drake-flame-drake) to the 5th-level list, adds
[frost drake](ref/monster/drake-frost-drake) to the 6th-level list, and adds
[desert drake](ref/monster/drake-desert-drake) to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command
the wearer can use this ring to summon a shadow drake, as if by the
_[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring),
[greater magic fang](ref/spell/magic-fang-greater),
[summon nature's ally IV](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-4); **Cost** 6,440 gp
