**Aura** faint abjuration, conjuration, and transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 4,700 gp; **Weight** -

This white gold ring is engraved with the images of a mastiff and a thundercloud. It functions as a
[ring of protection +1](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-protection-1). In addition, once per day, the wearer
may use _[summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1)_ to summon a red mastiff (use statistics for
a celestial dog). Additionally, once per day the wearer may use
_[beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1)_ to transform into a Small red mastiff.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [beast shape I](ref/spell/beast-shape-1),
[shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith), [summon monster I](ref/spell/summon-monster-1),
creator must be at least 3rd level; **Cost** 2,350 gp
