**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 19,500 gp; **Weight** -

A ring of energy shroud always appears to be glass or crystal in construction, tinted in a red,
green, blue, or yellow hue. Closer inspection reveals a small band of metal within the glass or
crystal. Treasured by frontline fighters and anyone with a trusted evoker ally, rings of energy
shroud normally function as a
[minor ring of energy resistance](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-energy-resistance-minor). However, once per
day as an immediate action, the ring's wearer can negate the damage of an incoming acid, cold,
electricity, or fire energy effect for himself (but not for other creatures affected by the spell or
effect), transforming that damage into a shroud of energy that wreathes the wearer's body acting as
if under the effects of the _[elemental aura](ref/spell/elemental-aura)_ spell. The
_[elemental aura](ref/spell/elemental-aura)_ effect has the same energy type of the damage negated.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [elemental aura](ref/spell/elemental-aura),
[resist energy](ref/spell/resist-energy); **Cost** 9,750 gp
