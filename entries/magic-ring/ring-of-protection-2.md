## Ring of Protection

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 172, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 479\
**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 2,000 gp (+1), 8,000 gp (+2), 18,000 gp (+3), 32,000 gp (+4), 50,000 gp
(+5); **Weight** -

This ring offers continual magical protection in the form of a deflection bonus of +1 to +5 to AC.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [shield of faith](ref/spell/shield-of-faith),
caster must be of a level at least three times higher than the bonus of the ring; **Cost** 1,000 gp
(+1), 4,000 gp (+2), 9,000 gp (+3), 16,000 gp (+4), 25,000 gp (+5)
