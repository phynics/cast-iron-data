**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 7,800 gp; **Weight** -

This ring carved from a single piece of cherry wood bears an inscription written in Tien. If the
wearer can cast _[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spells, the wearer adds
[shikigami](ref/monster/kami-shikigami) to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those
spells, adds [kodama](ref/monster/kami-kodama) to the 5th-level list, adds
[zuishin](ref/monster/kami-zuishin) to the 7th-level list, and adds
[toshigami](ref/monster/kami-toshigami) to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the
wearer can use this ring to summon a shikigami, as if by the
_[summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring),
[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature),
[summon nature's ally III](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-3); **Cost** 3,900 gp
