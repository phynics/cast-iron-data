**Sources:** Inner Sea Intrigue pg 56\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 25,200 gp; **Weight** -

This ring's deep red stone hides the image of an eye within an eye or the symbol of a secret
society, since many secret societies create these rings to protect their most important agents and
leaders. By concentrating on the ring as a move action, the wearer learns whether he is being
observed and whether it is by one or by more than one creature. Once per day, the wearer can use
_[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature)_ on command to target creatures that are currently
observing him. In addition, the wearer can cast _[detect scrying](ref/spell/detect-scrying)_ once
per day on command.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [detect scrying](ref/spell/detect-scrying),
[locate creature](ref/spell/locate-creature); **Cost** 12,600 gp
