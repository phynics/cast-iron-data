**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 177\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** -

When this ring is worn, the wielder's skin attains a barely perceptible sheen. In practical terms,
the wearer ignores the first 5 points of an object's [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) when performing a
[sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver or when [smashing an object](ref/rule/breaking-things).
She also does not take the standard -4 penalty for making a combat maneuver check to
[disarm](ref/rule/disarm) while unarmed.

Additionally, the ring's wearer can focus all of the ring's power, possibly repelling a devastating
blow. Once per day, when a critical hit or [sneak attack](ref/ability/sneak-attack) is scored on the
wearer, there is a 50% chance that the critical hit or sneak attack is negated and damage is instead
rolled normally. Using this ability is an immediate action, and the wearer must decide to use it
before the critical hit or sneak attack damage is rolled.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [stone fist](ref/spell/stone-fist),
[stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin); **Cost** 6,000 gp
