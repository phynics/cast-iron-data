**Aura** moderate evocation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 4,000 gp; **Weight** -

Upon first examination, this ring seems to be a
[ring of spell storing](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-spell-storing). However, while it allows a single
spell of 1st through 6th level to be cast into it, that spell cannot be cast out of the ring again.
Instead, should that spell ever be cast upon the wearer, the spell is immediately countered, as a
[counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells) action, requiring no action (or even knowledge) on the
wearer's part. Once so used, the spell cast within the ring is gone. A new spell (or the same one as
before) may be placed into it again.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring),
[imbue with spell ability](ref/spell/imbue-with-spell-ability); **Cost** 2,000 gp
