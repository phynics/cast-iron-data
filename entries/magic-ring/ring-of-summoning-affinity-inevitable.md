**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 219\
**Aura** faint conjuration \[law\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 8,100 gp; **Weight** -

This ring resembles a gear or cog. If the wearer can cast
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells, the wearer adds
[arbiter](ref/monster/inevitable-arbiter) to the 3rd-level list of monsters she can summon with
those spells, adds [zelekhut](ref/monster/inevitable-zelekhut) to the 7th-level list, adds
[kolyarut](ref/monster/inevitable-kolyarut) to the 8th-level list, and adds
[marut](ref/monster/inevitable-marut) to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the
wearer can use this ring to summon an arbiter, as if by the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be lawful neutral; **Cost** 4,050 gp
