**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 12,000 gp; **Weight** -

Two times per day on command, the wearer of this ring can produce a horrific smell affecting all
living creatures other than the wearer within a 10-foot radius. Once created, the smell remains,
centered on the wearer, until she dismisses it as a free action. This is a poison effect.

Any creature affected by the disgusting aroma produced by the ring must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude
saving throw when it starts its turn in the ring's stench radius. Those that fail a saving throw
become [sickened](ref/misc/sickened) for 1 round. If they are already sickened, they become
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1 round instead.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [stinking cloud](ref/spell/stinking-cloud);
**Cost** 6,000 gp
