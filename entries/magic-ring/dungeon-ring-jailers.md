## Dungeon Ring

**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 166, Advanced Player's Guide pg 292\
**Aura** moderate divination; **CL** 8th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 250 gp (prisoner's), 16,000 gp (jailer's); **Weight** -

A jailer's dungeon ring is worked gold, set with carnelians. The ring is magically attuned to one or
more iron prisoner's dungeon rings. When the wearer of a jailer's ring places a prisoner's ring on a
subject, the prisoner cannot remove the ring without the use of a
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish),
[miracle](ref/spell/miracle), or [wish](ref/spell/wish) spell. The wearer of the jailer's ring can
also remove a linked prisoner's ring at any time.

The jailer is aware of any wearer of a linked prisoner's ring as per a constant
_[status](ref/spell/status)_ spell. Additionally, all wearers of linked prisoner's rings count as
familiar to the jailer for the purposes of spells such as _[scrying](ref/spell/scrying)_ and
[teleport](ref/spell/teleport).

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [scrying](ref/spell/scrying),
[status](ref/spell/status); **Cost** 125 gp (prisoner's), 8,000 gp (jailer's)
