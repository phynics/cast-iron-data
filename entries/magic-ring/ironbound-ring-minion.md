## Ironbound Ring

**Sources:** Adventurer's Guide pg 36\
**Aura** faint abjuration and enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 500 gp (Minion), 8,000 gp (Master); **Weight** -

While holding one or more minion's ironbound rings, the wearer of a master's ironbound ring can
impart instructions of 50 words or fewer to each minion's ironbound rings. The wearer of a minion's
ironbound ring can recall the instructions at will and is aware of any action that would cause him
to act against those instructions. The DC of any skill check attempted to force the wearer to
abandon, misinterpret, or consciously fail the instructions increases by 5, such as for Bluff checks
to trick the wearer or Intimidate checks to interrogate him. The wearer gains a +3 resistance bonus
on Will saves to resist spells and effects that would force him to act against the instructions,
such as [dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person) and _[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance),
[suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion); **Cost** 4,000 gp
