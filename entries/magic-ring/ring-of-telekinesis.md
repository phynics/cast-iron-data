**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 175, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 479\
**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 75,000 gp; **Weight** -

This ring, knotted with metallic tendrils encasing a setting of red and amber gemstones, allows the
caster to use the spell _[telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis)_ on command, lifting and manipulating
objects at a distance using only her mind.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [telekinesis](ref/spell/telekinesis); **Cost**
37,500 gp
