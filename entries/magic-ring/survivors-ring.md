**Aura** strong illusion; **CL** 18th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 56,160 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is shaped like a silver snake with an onyx in its mouth. Once per day, if the wearer would
be reduced to 0 or fewer hit points but not be dead, she is instead reduced to 0 hit points and
subject to an effect similar to _[mislead](ref/spell/mislead)._ Her illusory double appears to die
or to take on the [dying](ref/misc/dying) condition in her place, as appropriate for the negated
effect. The wearer cannot control the double. In addition, the wearer can touch the ring's stone as
a standard action once per day to use _[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door)_ but only while
the ring's _[mislead](ref/spell/mislead)_ effect is active.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [death ward](ref/spell/death-ward),
[dimension door](ref/spell/dimension-door), [mislead](ref/spell/mislead); **Cost** 25,080 gp
