**Aura** faint conjuration \[evil, law\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 7,300 gp; **Weight** -

This iron ring has several sharp barbs. If the wearer can cast
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells, the wearer adds
[augur](ref/monster/kyton-augur) to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon with those spells,
adds [sacristan](ref/monster/kyton-sacristan) to the 7th-level list, and adds
[interlocutor](ref/monster/kyton-interlocutor) to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command
the wearer can use this ring to summon an augur, as if by the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be lawful evil; **Cost** 3,650 gp
