**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 175, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 479\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 2nd\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 2,500 gp; **Weight** -

This silver ring usually has fish-like designs and motifs etched into the band. It continually
grants the wearer a +5 competence bonus on [Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), creator must have 5 ranks in Swim; **Cost**
1,250 gp
