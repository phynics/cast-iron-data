**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 1,500 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is carved from a single, thick antler and fashioned into the shape of a regal elk's head.
The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on Perception and Survival checks. Once per day, the wearer
can use _[animal messenger](ref/spell/animal-messenger)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [animal messenger](ref/spell/animal-messenger),
creator must have 2 ranks in Perception and Survival; **Cost** 750 gp
