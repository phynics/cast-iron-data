**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 1,200 gp; **Weight** -

This simple copper ring bears the image of a blue enamel hand. It grants the wearer a +2 bonus on
[Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) checks. At will, the wearer may create the sound of a
peaceful chime, small gong, or temple bell. Once per day, the wearer may also use
_[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike)._

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [ghost sound](ref/spell/ghost-sound),
[true strike](ref/spell/true-strike), creator must have 5 ranks in Escape Artist; **Cost** 600 gp
