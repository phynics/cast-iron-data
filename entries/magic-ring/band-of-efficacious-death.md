**Sources:** Potions and Poisons pg 28\
**Aura** faint necromancy; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 9,250 gp; **Weight** -

This golden ring is set with a large, almost gaudy onyx that can open to reveal a hidden compartment
with just enough room for a single dose of powdered poison. This ring provides a +2 competence bonus
on [Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) checks to conceal this poison on the wearer's body.
In addition, the ring contains four charges that recharge each day. When drawing a poison from the
band of efficacious death, the wearer can tap the onyx up to four times to increase the frequency of
the poison by 1 increment per tap. For example, if the poison's frequency were 1/round for 2 rounds,
by touching the onyx once, the frequency would become 1/round for 3 rounds.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Craft Wondrous Item](ref/feat/craft-wondrous-item),
[pernicious poison](ref/spell/pernicious-poison); **Cost** 4,625 gp
