**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 349

**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** ring; **Weight** -

This delicate ring appears to be a ring of spell storing or a ring of spell turning, but instead of
aiding casting or counterspelling, the ring hinders such efforts. Each time the wearer casts an
arcane spell, he suffers a 25% spell failure chance. Furthermore, the wearer of the ring takes a -4
penalty on [Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks to identify a spell when attempting to
[counterspell](ref/rule/counterspells). The ring can only be removed by a
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell or a similar effect.

### Construction

**Requirements** [ring of spell storing (any)](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-spell-storing) or a
[ring of spell turning](ref/magic-ring/ring-of-spell-turning); **Cost** -
