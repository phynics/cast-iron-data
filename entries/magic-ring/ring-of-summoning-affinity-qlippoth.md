**Aura** faint conjuration \[chaos, evil\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 8,600 gp; **Weight** -

This ring has an odd, disquieting texture that always reveals greater layers of complexity the more
closely it is examined. If the wearer can cast _[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells,
the wearer adds [cythnigot](ref/monster/qlippoth-cythnigot) to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can
summon with those spells, adds [shoggti](ref/monster/qlippoth-shoggti) to the 6th-level list, adds
[nyogoth](ref/monster/qlippoth-nyogoth) to the 7th-level list, adds
[chernobue](ref/monster/qlippoth-chernobue) to the 8th-level list, and adds
[augnagar](ref/monster/qlippoth-augnagar) to the 9th-level list. Also, once per day on command the
wearer can use this ring to summon a cythnigot, as if by the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be chaotic evil; **Cost** 4,300 gp
