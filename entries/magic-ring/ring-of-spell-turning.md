**Aura** strong abjuration; **CL** 13th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 100,000 gp; **Weight** -

Up to three times per day on command, this simple platinum band automatically reflects the next nine
levels of spells cast at the wearer, exactly as if _[spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning)_ had
been cast upon him.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [spell turning](ref/spell/spell-turning);
**Cost** 50,000 gp
