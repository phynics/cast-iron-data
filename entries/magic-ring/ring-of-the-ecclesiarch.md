**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 175\
**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 28,500 gp; **Weight** -

This ornate ring of sanctified silver is engraved with a golden signet that incorporates the holy
symbol of the wearer's deity after being worn for 24 hours. The ring can be used to seal parchments
or to mark any surface as the _[arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark)_ spell at will. The wearer is
marked as a scholarly leader of the faith and gains a +5 competence bonus on Diplomacy and Knowledge
(religion) checks. If the wearer has the [Leadership](ref/feat/leadership) feat, she may attract
double the normal number of followers, and her followers' zealous devotion grants them a +4 morale
bonus on Will saves against enchantment spells and effects.

Once per day, the wearer of the ring can use _[prayer](ref/spell/prayer)_ and
_[enter image](ref/spell/enter-image),_ often using the latter power to speak and observe through
portraits, statues, and even minted coinage bearing their likeness.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [arcane mark](ref/spell/arcane-mark),
[enter image](ref/spell/enter-image), [enthrall](ref/spell/enthrall), [prayer](ref/spell/prayer),
creator must have 5 ranks of Diplomacy and Knowledge (religion) and the ability to cast divine
spells; **Cost** 14,250 gp
