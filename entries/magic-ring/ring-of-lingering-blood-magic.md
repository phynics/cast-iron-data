**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 11th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** -

When the wearer of this ring is [bloodraging](ref/class/bloodrager), he can expend 1 round of
bloodrage to extend the duration of a beneficial spell affecting him with a duration of 1 round per
level or greater by 2 rounds. This is a free action the wearer can take once per round.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Endurance](ref/feat/endurance), [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell),
[Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), creator must have the bloodrage class feature; **Cost** 1,000 gp
