**Aura** faint enchantment; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** -

This platinum thimble, intricately crafted in the shape of an animal claw, is worn on one finger. A
[vampire](ref/template/vampire) wearer can use the thimble to inflict a tiny wound as part of the
use of a blood drain, energy drain, or drain chi ability; use of the thimble in this way isn't an
action. This wound deals no additional damage, but it turns the target's pain to pleasure. The
target must succeed at a DC 14 Will save or become [fascinated](ref/misc/fascinated) by the wearer
and make no attempt to resist the draining on that round and the following 2 rounds. At the end of
this time, the creature's senses return and it can react accordingly.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [enthrall](ref/spell/enthrall); **Cost** 1,000
gp
