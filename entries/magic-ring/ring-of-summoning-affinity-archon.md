**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 218\
**Aura** faint conjuration \[good, law\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 7,000 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is made of hammered steel. If the wearer can cast
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells, the wearer adds
[harbingers](ref/monster/archon-harbinger-archon) to the 3rd-level list of monsters he can summon
with those spells, adds [legion archon](ref/monster/archon-legion-archon) to the 6th-level list, and
adds [shield archon](ref/monster/archon-shield-archon) to the 7th-level list. Also, once per day on
command the wearer can use this ring to summon a harbinger, as if by the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be neutral good; **Cost** 3,500 gp
