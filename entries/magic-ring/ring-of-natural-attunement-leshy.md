**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 217\
**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 2,800 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is made of thin but strong vines braided together in an elegant weave. If the wearer can
cast _[summon nature's ally](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally)_ spells, the wearer adds
[leaf leshy](ref/monster/leshy-leaf-leshy) to the 1st-level list of monsters he can summon with
those spells, adds [gourd leshy](ref/monster/leshy-gourd-leshy) to the 2nd-level list, adds
[fungus leshy](ref/monster/leshy-fungus-leshy) the 3rd-level list, and adds
[seaweed leshy](ref/monster/leshy-seaweed-leshy) to the 4th-level list. Also, once per day on
command the wearer can use this ring to summon a _leaf leshy,_ as if by the
_[summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1)_ spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth),
[summon nature's ally I](ref/spell/summon-natures-ally-1); **Cost** 1,400 gp
