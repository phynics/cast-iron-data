**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** -

This ring continually grants the wearer the ability to avoid damage as if she had
[evasion](ref/ability/evasion). Whenever she makes a Reflex saving throw to determine whether she
takes half damage, a successful save results in no damage.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [jump](ref/spell/jump); **Cost** 12,500 gp
