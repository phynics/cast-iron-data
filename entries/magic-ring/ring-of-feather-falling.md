**Sources:** Ultimate Equipment pg 170, PRPG Core Rulebook pg 479\
**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 1st\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 2,200 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is crafted with a feather pattern all around its edge. It acts exactly like a
_[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall)_ spell, and activates immediately if the wearer falls more
than 5 feet.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall); **Cost**
1,100 gp
