## Ring of Resistance

**Aura** faint abjuration; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 1,500 gp (+1), 6,000 gp (+2), 13,500 gp (+3), 24,000 gp (+4), 37,500 gp
(+5); **Weight** -

Protective symbols adorn this magic ring. It functions as a
[cloak of resistance](ref/magic-wondrous/cloak-of-resistance) with an equivalent bonus.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [resistance](ref/spell/resistance), creator's
caster level must be at least three times the ring's bonus; **Cost** 750 gp (+1), 3,000 gp (+2),
6,750 gp (+3), 12,000 gp (+4), 17,750 gp (+5)
