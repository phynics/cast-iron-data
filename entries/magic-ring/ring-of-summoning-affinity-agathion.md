**Sources:** Advanced Class Guide pg 218\
**Aura** faint conjuration \[good\]; **CL** 5th\
**Slot** ring; **Price** 7,700 gp; **Weight** -

This ring is forged from electrum and inlaid with runes. If the wearer can cast
_[summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster)_ spells, the wearer adds
[silvanshee](ref/monster/agathion-silvanshee) to the 3rd-level list of monsters she can summon with
those spells, adds [vulpinal](ref/monster/agathion-vulpinal) to the 5th-level list, adds
[avoral](ref/monster/agathion-avoral) to the 7th-level list, and adds
[leonal](ref/monster/agathion-leonal) to the 8th-level list. Also, once per day on command the
wearer can use this ring to summon a silvanshee, as if by the
[summon monster III](ref/spell/summon-monster-3) spell.

### Construction

**Requirements** [Forge Ring](ref/feat/forge-ring), [planar ally](ref/spell/planar-ally) or
[planar binding](ref/spell/planar-binding), creator must be neutral good; **Cost** 3,850 gp
