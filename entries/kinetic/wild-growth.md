## Wild Growth (Sp)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/61, Occult Origins/9

You can charge plants with life energy, hindering your enemies or aiding local farmers. This
functions as [plant growth](ref/spell/plant-growth).
