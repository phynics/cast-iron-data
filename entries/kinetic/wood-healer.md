## Wood Healer (Sp)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 1

**Prerequisite:** [positive blast](ref/kinetic/positive-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

**Spell resistance:** yes

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/61, Psychic Anthology/23

This functions as [kinetic healer](ref/kinetic/kinetic-healer), but you must base the amount of
healing on your _positive blast,_ not _wood blast._ A phytokineticist with _positive blast_ as her
first blast qualifies for the [kinetic chirurgeon](ref/arc-kineticist/kinetic-chirurgeon) archetype
and gains this utility talent instead of _kinetic healer._

This wild talent counts as _kinetic healer_ for prerequisites for selecting other kineticist wild
talents.
