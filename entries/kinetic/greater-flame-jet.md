## Greater Flame Jet (Sp)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [flame jet](ref/kinetic/flame-jet)

You can use _flame jet_ as a **move action** and can emanate a mild jet of flame, allowing you to
hover without spending an action.
