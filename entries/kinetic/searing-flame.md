## Searing Flame (Su)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [burning infusion](ref/kinetic/burning-infusion)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/26

Over time, your _burning infusion_ sears away your foe's fire resistance. When you roll burn damage
from _burning infusion_ against a foe, decrease the foe's fire resistance by an amount equal to the
unmodified burn damage; don't apply fire resistance to this roll for the purpose of determining the
reduction. This decrease lasts for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your kineticist level. These decreases stack, to a
minimum resistance of 0.
