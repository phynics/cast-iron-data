## Kinetic Dissolution (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [wayang](ref/race/wayang), chosen racial trait

**Source:** Heroes of Golarion/26

Choose either the _dissolution's child_ alternate racial trait or the _light and dark_ racial trait.
You can use the chosen racial trait by accepting 1 point of burn, even if you have no daily uses of
that racial trait remaining.
