## Woodland Step (Su)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/61, Occult Origins/9

You gain [woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride), as per the druid ability. Due to your
ability to manipulate plants, you can attempt a caster level check (DC = 11 + the spell's caster
level) as a **standard action** to also ignore magically manipulated plants (but not creatures of
the plant type).
