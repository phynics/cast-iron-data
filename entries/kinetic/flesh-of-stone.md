## Flesh of Stone (Su)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** defense (Su)

**Level:** 0

**Burn:** 0

Your skin hardens like stone, dampening the impact of most attacks.

You gain DR/adamantine equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your kineticist level (DR
1/adamantine at 2nd level, up to DR 10/adamantine at 20th level). By accepting 1 point of burn, you
can increase the DR by 1 until the next time your burn is removed, to a maximum DR equal to your
kineticist level.

Whenever you accept burn while using an earth wild talent, the energy surging through you causes
your [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction) to change from DR/adamantine to DR/- for 1 round.
You can dismiss and restore this effect as an **immediate action**.
