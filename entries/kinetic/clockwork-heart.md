## Clockwork Heart (Su)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [metal blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast)

**Source:** Heroes of Golarion/27

You have used your power over metal to add clockwork components to your own body. So long as these
clockwork components are kept wound (per the
[winding ability of the clockwork subtype](ref/subtype/clockwork)), you gain the benefits of both
the [Improved Initiative](ref/feat/improved-initiative) and
[Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/lightning-reflexes) feats.
