## Smoke Storm (Sp)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Saving throw:** Fortitude negates; see text

**Spell resistance:** no

**Source:** Occult Adventures/27

You transform a source of open flame within 120 feet into a cloud of choking smoke, filling a
20-foot-radius [spread](ref/misc/spread) and affecting vision like a
[smokestick](ref/eq-misc/smokestick). All creatures that begin their turns inside the area become
[sickened](ref/misc/sickened) as long as they remain in the smoke and for 1d4+1 rounds thereafter
(Fortitude negates).
