**Element:** void

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 3

**Prerequisite:** [void healer](ref/kinetic/void-healer)

**Associated:** [Negative Blast](ref/kinetic/negative-blast), [Void Blast](ref/kinetic/void-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Elemental Master's Handbook/22

Your kinetic blast can drain your foes' vitality to replenish your own.

If your blast hits (or the enemy fails its saving throw against a blast without an attack roll), you
can activate the _void healer_ utility wild talent on yourself by accepting its burn cost; you don't
need to take an action to do so. The _void healer_ utility wild talent heals you when used in this
way even if you are a living creature.
