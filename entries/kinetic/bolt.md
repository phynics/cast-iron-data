**Element:** air

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 2

**Associated:** [Electric Blast](ref/kinetic/electric-blast),
[Thunderstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/thunderstorm-blast)

**Saving throw:** Reflex half

You unleash a bolt of lightning above an enemy within 30 feet and send it spiraling down with
tremendous force. Each bolt is a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long, vertical bolt, dealing your full blast
damage to creatures within the line. If all squares in the bolt's area are outdoors and in a storm,
the blast deals an additional 1 point of damage per die. The saving throw is Dexterity-based.
