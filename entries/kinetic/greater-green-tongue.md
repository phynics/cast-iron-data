## Greater Green Tongue (Su)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 7

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [green tongue](ref/kinetic/green-tongue)

You can spend 10 minutes in any outdoor location that would be valid for
[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature) in order to contact and chat with fey and leshy
spirits, allowing you to gain three pieces of information as per
_[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature)._
