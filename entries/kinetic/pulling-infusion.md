**Element:** void

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 1

**Associated:** [Gravity Blast](ref/kinetic/gravity-blast), [Void Blast](ref/kinetic/void-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Occult Origins/7

Your kinetic blast pulls foes toward you. Attempt a [drag](ref/rule/drag) combat maneuver check
against each target damaged by your infused blast (the blast always drags the foe closer to you),
using your Constitution modifier instead of your Strength modifier to determine your CMB. This
infusion can pull a foe a maximum of 5 feet. You can increase the maximum distance pulled by 5 feet
per additional point of burn accepted.
