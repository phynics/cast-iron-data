## Greater Waterdancer (Su)

**Element:** water

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [waterdancer](ref/kinetic/waterdancer)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/29

You can move across water (as [water walk](ref/spell/water-walk)) at will and breathe underwater,
and your slashing and bludgeoning attacks don't take the usual penalties when you're underwater.
