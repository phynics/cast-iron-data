## Basic Chaokinesis (Sp)

**Element:** void

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

You can create a shadow that protects a target from bright light. You can also change gravity to
increase a creature's carrying capacity by half or grant a creature a +4 bonus on
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to jump. Each benefit lasts 1 hour or until you use basic
chaokinesis again.
