## Plant Puppet (Sp)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/59, Occult Origins/9

You can control plants, causing them to attack your foes. This functions as the
[aether puppet](ref/kinetic/aether-puppet) wild talent, except it controls plants instead of
objects.

At 12th level, you can grant [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 5 to Large and larger animated plants.
