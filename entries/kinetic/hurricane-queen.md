## Hurricane Queen (Su)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 9

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [enveloping winds](ref/kinetic/enveloping-winds)

**Source:** Occult Realms/8

You are one with the hurricane. Your _enveloping winds_ defense wild talent has an additional 25%
chance of deflecting nonmagical ranged attacks, and your total deflection chance can exceed the
usual cap of 75%. All wind and weather (including creatures using the [whirlwind](ref/umr/whirlwind)
monster ability) affect you and your attacks only if you wish them to do so; for example, you could
shoot arrows directly through a tornado without penalty.

### Attunement

An aerokineticist must fly around the entire perimeter of the Eye of Abendego without stopping,
while succeeding at a series of 12 consecutive DC 40 [Fly](ref/skill/fly) checks. If successful, she
can learn the _hurricane queen_ wild talent. If she fails two or fewer checks, she takes 50d6 points
of bludgeoning damage from the hurricane's gale-force winds. If she fails three or more checks, she
must succeed at a DC 25 Reflex save or be pulled into the Eye of Abendego, never to return.
