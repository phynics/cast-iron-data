## Kinetic Cover (Sp)

**Element:** aether, earth, water, wood

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/26

You call up elemental matter to defend yourself and your allies from attacks. As a **standard
action**, you can select one face of a square within 30 feet of you and move elemental matter to
block that face, providing total [cover](ref/rule/cover) from that direction. The face you select
must be supported by the ground, and the _kinetic cover_ cannot support more than 5 pounds of
weight. Water, ice, and telekinetic force are translucent, but earth, metal, mud, and the like are
opaque and block line of sight.

A creature who strikes the _cover_ can easily destroy it. Regardless of its composition, the _cover_
has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 0, AC 5, and 2 hit points per kineticist level you possess. You can
have a number of _kinetic covers_ in existence equal to your Constitution modifier +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your kineticist level.
