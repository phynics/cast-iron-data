## Basic Pyrokinesis (Sp)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/23

You can use your inner flame to reproduce the effects of a [flare](ref/spell/flare),
[light](ref/spell/light), or [spark](ref/spell/spark) cantrip, except that the light you create with
light produces heat like a normal flame; using any of the three abilities ends any previous light
effect from this wild talent.
