**Element:** void

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 4

**Burn:** 3

**Associated:** [Gravity Blast](ref/kinetic/gravity-blast),
[Negative Blast](ref/kinetic/negative-blast), [Void Blast](ref/kinetic/void-blast)

**Saving throw:** Reflex half

You create a growing singularity. Choose a grid intersection within 30 feet. All creatures and
objects in a 5-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) centered on the intersection take
[one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your blast's normal amount of damage (or half
damage for a _negative blast_). On your next turn, the singularity deals damage in a 10-foot-radius
burst, and on your turn after that, it deals damage in a 15-foot-radius burst. The DC is
Dexterity-based.
