**Element:** air

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 2

**Associated:** [Electric Blast](ref/kinetic/electric-blast),
[Charged Water Blast](ref/kinetic/charged-water-blast),
[Thunderstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/thunderstorm-blast)

**Saving throw:** Will negates

**Source:** Psychic Anthology/21

Your blast sends strange electric signals through the target's body, scrambling its synapses and
causing it to become [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round. The target is aware it can choose
to take a **move action** to remove the staggered condition inflicted by this infusion. This
infusion is a mind-affecting effect.
