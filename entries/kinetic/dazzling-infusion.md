**Element:** fire, wood

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 1

**Associated:** [Blue Flame Blast](ref/kinetic/blue-flame-blast),
[Fire Blast](ref/kinetic/fire-blast), [Plasma Blast](ref/kinetic/plasma-blast),
[Positive Blast](ref/kinetic/positive-blast), [Verdant Blast](ref/kinetic/verdant-blast)

**Saving throw:** Will negates

**Source:** Elemental Master's Handbook/22

Your kinetic blast shines brightly, making it harder for your foes to see. If your blast hits a foe
(and penetrates SR if applicable), that foe must succeed at a Will save or be
[dazzled](ref/misc/dazzled) for 1 minute, whether or not it takes damage from the blast. You can
reduce the blast's damage by [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) to increase the DC of this save
by 2.
