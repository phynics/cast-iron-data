## Waterdancer (Sp)

**Element:** water

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/29

You call forth a current of water to increase your speed and allow you to swim with ease, as if
under the effects of [slipstream](ref/spell/slipstream) with unlimited duration.
