## Greater Tremorsense (Su)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [tremorsense](ref/kinetic/tremorsense)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/28

You can spend 10 minutes to extend your _[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense)_ outward while in a
natural underground setting, letting you learn three facts as if using
[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature), except your
_[tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense)_ can't tell if a creature is a woodland creature or a powerful
unnatural creature.
