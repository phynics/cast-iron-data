## Heat Adaptation (Sp)

**Element:** fire, water

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/25

You are constantly protected by [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) against hot
temperatures only. You gain an amount of fire resistance equal to twice your current amount of burn.
