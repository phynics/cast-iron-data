## Earth Glide (Su)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [earth climb](ref/kinetic/earth-climb)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/24

You can glide through earth, as an [earth elemental's](ref/monster/earth-elemental) \*earth glide
\*ability, with a burrow speed equal to your base land speed.
