## Primal Aether (Su)

**Element:** aether

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

You can gather strands of aether twisted and warped by your experiences in the Spellscar Desert, and
wind them so tightly that they explode, creating a [primal magic event](ref/rule/primal-magic) with
a CR equal to your kineticist level.

### Attunement

To locate the aether saturations deep within the desert, a telekineticist must first locate an
etheric storm. Afterwards, she must succeed at both a DC 20
[Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) check and a DC 20 [Survival](ref/skill/survival)
check and spend 1d4 hours navigating the wasteland from the storm's origin point to the saturation
that spawned it. She can then learn the _primal aether_ wild talent.
