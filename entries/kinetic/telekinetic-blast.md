## Telekinetic Blast (Sp)

**Element:** aether

**Type:** simple blast (Sp)

**Level:** 0

**Burn:** 0

**Damage type:** physical

**Damage:** bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing

**Source:** Occult Adventures/15

You throw a nearby unattended object at a single foe as a ranged attack. The object must weigh no
more than 5 pounds per kineticist level you possess. If the attack hits, the target and the thrown
object each take the blast's damage. Since the object is enfolded in strands of aether, even if you
use this power on a magic weapon or other unusual object, the attack doesn't use any of the magic
weapon's bonuses or effects; it simply deals your blast damage.

Alternatively, you can loosen the strands of aether in order to deal damage to both the object and
the target as though you had thrown the object yourself (instead of dealing your normal blast
damage). You substitute your Constitution modifier for your Strength modifier if throwing the object
would have added your Strength modifier on the damage roll, and you don't take the -4 penalty on the
attack roll for throwing an object that wasn't designed to be thrown. In this case, the object's
special effects apply (including effects from its materials), and if the object is a weapon, you
must be proficient with it and able to wield it with one hand; otherwise, the item deals damage as a
one-handed [improvised weapon](ref/misc/improvised-weapons) for a creature of your size.
