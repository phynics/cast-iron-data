## Thorn Flesh (Sp)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 1

**Prerequisite:** [flesh of wood](ref/kinetic/flesh-of-wood)

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/60, Occult Origins/9

You cover your body in barbed thorns that injure foes who strike you. This functions as the
[jagged flesh](ref/kinetic/jagged-flesh) wild talent.
