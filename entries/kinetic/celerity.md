## Celerity (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

You can galvanize the flow of electricity within your allies to enhance their speed. This acts as
[haste](ref/spell/haste) except it lasts for 1 round. You can accept 1 point of burn to increase the
duration to 1 round per kineticist level you possess.
