## Greater Elemental Whispers (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [elemental whispers](ref/kinetic/elemental-whispers)

**Source:** Horror Adventures/57

Your friendship with the elements has grown.

You gain your choice of a [wysp](ref/family/wysp) or Small [elemental](ref/family/elemental) of your
primary element as an [improved familiar](ref/ability/improved-familiars), without needing to meet
any of the usual prerequisites for gaining such a familiar. Unlike with _elemental whispers,_ this
improved familiar acts in all ways like a [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar): it's always manifested,
it has hit points equal to 1/2 your own, and so on. You no longer receive the familiar benefits from
the original creature you selected.

If your improved familiar dies, you can still contact its voice in your mind and gain the
[Alertness](ref/feat/alertness) feat, but you can't cause it to manifest. If you pay the usual costs
for replacing a familiar, you manifest a new form for your elemental friend.
