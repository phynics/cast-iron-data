**Element:** water

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 3

**Associated:** [Blizzard Blast](ref/kinetic/blizzard-blast), [Cold Blast](ref/kinetic/cold-blast),
[Ice Blast](ref/kinetic/ice-blast)

**Saving throw:** Fortitude negates

Your kinetic blast chills your foes to the bone, making their movements sluggish.

Whenever an infused blast deals cold damage to a foe, that foe is [staggered](ref/misc/staggered)
for 1 round.
