## Mesmerizing Elementalist (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [nagaji](ref/race/nagaji)

**Source:** Heroes of Golarion/26

You gain the _hypnotic gaze_ nagaji alternate racial trait. If you already have this trait, add +1
to the trait's save DC instead. You can use the _hypnotic gaze_ racial trait by accepting 1 point of
burn, even if you have no daily uses of that racial trait remaining.
