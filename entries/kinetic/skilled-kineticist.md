## Skilled Kineticist (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/27

You gain a bonus equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your kineticist level on skill
checks with the skills your primary element added to your class skill list, and you can use the
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) skill associated with your primary element to identify
elementals of your primary element's subtype.
