## Elemental Exile (Sp)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 8

**Burn:** 1

**Saving throw:** Will negates

**Spell resistance:** yes

**Source:** Wilderness Origins/28

You transport a single creature you touch to the plane associated with your element. Precise
accuracy regarding a particular arrival location on the intended plane is almost impossible. The
creature appears 5 to 500 miles (5d%) from your intended destination. _Elemental exile_ transports
creatures instantaneously and then ends. The creature must find other means to travel back (such as
casting [plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift) or using _elemental exile_ again).
