## Shimmering Mirage (Sp)

**Element:** water

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 1

**Prerequisite:** [shroud of water](ref/kinetic/shroud-of-water)

Your shroud bends light, creating a shimmering mirage. While your _shroud of water_ is active,
attacks against you suffer a 20% miss chance due to [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) until the
next time your burn is removed.
