## Flame Jet (Sp)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/25

You shoot a burst of flame behind you as a **standard action**, propelling you up to 60 feet in a
straight line, including into the air; any movement upward costs double. If you end your turn
mid-air, you fall on your next turn unless you use flame jet again.
