## Sky Walk (Su)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 7

**Burn:** 1

**Prerequisite:** [air cushion](ref/kinetic/air-cushion) or [air's leap](ref/kinetic/airs-leap),
[wings of air](ref/kinetic/wings-of-air)

By accepting 1 point of burn, you can enable your allies to fly, as [mass fly](ref/spell/fly-mass),
except the duration of the effect is 10 minutes.
