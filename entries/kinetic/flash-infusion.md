**Element:** fire

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 4

**Burn:** 3

**Associated:** [Blue Flame Blast](ref/kinetic/blue-flame-blast),
[Fire Blast](ref/kinetic/fire-blast), [Plasma Blast](ref/kinetic/plasma-blast)

**Saving throw:** Will negates

**Source:** Occult Adventures/19

Your kinetic blast is so bright that it blinds your foes.

Whenever an infused blast hits a foe and penetrates its
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance), that foe must succeed at a Will save or be
[blinded](ref/misc/blinded) for 1 round, regardless of whether it takes damage from the blast. You
can reduce the blast's damage by half to increase the DC of this save by 2.
