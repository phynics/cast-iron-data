## Infernal Bargain (Sp)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [tiefling](ref/race/tiefling) or [Planar Infusion](ref/feat/planar-infusion)
(Hell)

**Saving throw:** none

**Spell resistance:** yes

**Source:** Plane-Hopper's Handbook/8

As a **swift action** before using a utility talent on a willing target, you can add the effects of
[conditional favor](ref/spell/conditional-favor), except that it applies to any beneficial utility
talent instead of to spells of certain schools.
