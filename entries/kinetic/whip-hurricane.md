**Element:** universal

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 6

**Burn:** 4

**Prerequisite:** [kinetic blade](ref/kinetic/kinetic-blade),
[kinetic whip](ref/kinetic/kinetic-whip), [blade whirlwind](ref/kinetic/blade-whirlwind)

**Associated:** any

**Saving throw:** none

As _blade whirlwind,_ except it manifests a _kinetic whip,_ and the whip lasts until the beginning
of your next turn or until you use any form infusion that creates a blade or whip again, whichever
comes first.
