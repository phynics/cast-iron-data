## Telekinetic Invisibility (Sp)

**Element:** aether

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

You weave strands of aether, bending light and dampening sound; this works as
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility) except that the aetheric bending is easier to notice than
normal invisibility, so your bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks is halved (+10 while
moving and +20 while perfectly still). However, the dampened sound allows you to avoid automatic
detection via sound-based [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) and [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight), but
you do not receive the bonus on Stealth checks from this wild talent against a creature with such
abilities.
