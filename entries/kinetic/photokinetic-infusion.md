**Element:** wood

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 1

**Prerequisite:** [positive blast](ref/kinetic/positive-blast)

**Associated:** Positive Blast

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/24

Your blast damages living creatures with light. This deals normal damage to undead creatures but
also deals minimum damage (1 point of damage per d6, can't be increased by the 9th-level improvement
to metakinesis) to living creatures. Undead creatures that are particularly vulnerable to sunlight,
such as [vampires](ref/template/vampire), take 1 extra point of damage per die from the infused
blast.
