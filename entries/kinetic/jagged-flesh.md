## Jagged Flesh (Sp)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 1

**Prerequisite:** [flesh of stone](ref/kinetic/flesh-of-stone)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/25

You painfully push forth jagged chunks of rock from your flesh. Until the next time your burn is
removed, while your _flesh of stone_ is active, any creature that strikes you with an unarmed strike
or natural weapon or that grapples you takes 1d6 points of piercing damage. Weapons that strike you
also take this damage, though the damage is unlikely to penetrate the weapon's
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness).
