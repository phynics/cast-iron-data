## Cold Adaptation (Sp)

**Element:** fire, water

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/24

You are constantly protected by [endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements) against cold
temperatures only. You gain cold resistance equal to twice your current amount of burn.
