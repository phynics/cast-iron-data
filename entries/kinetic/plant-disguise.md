## Plant Disguise (Sp)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 4

**Burn:** 0

You can assume the shape of an immobile plant, as per [tree shape](ref/spell/tree-shape) but
transforming into any ordinary Small or Medium plant.
