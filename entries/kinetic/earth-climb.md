## Earth Climb (Su)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/24

You use your connection to earth to meld slightly into stone and earthen surfaces, granting you a
climb speed equal to your base land speed when climbing such surfaces.
