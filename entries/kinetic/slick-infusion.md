**Element:** water

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 2

**Prerequisite:** [slick](ref/kinetic/slick)

**Associated:** [Blizzard Blast](ref/kinetic/blizzard-blast),
[Charged Water Blast](ref/kinetic/charged-water-blast), [Cold Blast](ref/kinetic/cold-blast),
[Ice Blast](ref/kinetic/ice-blast), [Water Blast](ref/kinetic/water-blast)

**Saving throw:** Reflex negates

**Source:** Psychic Anthology/21

Your blast leaves behind slippery water or ice in its area for as long as the blast is in that area
and for 1 round thereafter, making the area [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) and
increasing the DC of [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks attempted in the area by 5. You can
use this infusion only if you also use a form infusion that has an area of effect.
