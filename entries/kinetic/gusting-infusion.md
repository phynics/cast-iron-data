**Element:** air

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 1

**Associated:** [Air Blast](ref/kinetic/air-blast), [Blizzard Blast](ref/kinetic/blizzard-blast),
[Sandstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/sandstorm-blast), [Thunderstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/thunderstorm-blast)

**Saving throw:** Fortitude negates

The wind from your infusion causes your blast to act as an instantaneous
[gust of wind](ref/spell/gust-of-wind). If your blast has a clear path, you can accept 2 additional
points of burn or reduce the damage to 0 in order to cause the gust of wind effect to persist for 1
round along that path.
