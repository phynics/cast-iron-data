**Element:** universal

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 3

**Prerequisite:** [kinetic blade](ref/kinetic/kinetic-blade)

**Associated:** any

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Psychic Anthology/20

You create a _kinetic blade_ and swing it in an arc around you, making a single attack against all
foes within reach as if with _kinetic blade._ If the result is a critical threat, roll to confirm
the critical hit against only the first target you hit. The _blade_ vanishes instantly after the
_[whirlwind](ref/spell/whirlwind)._
