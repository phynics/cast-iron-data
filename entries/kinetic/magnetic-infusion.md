**Element:** air, earth

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 2

**Associated:** [Charged Water Blast](ref/kinetic/charged-water-blast),
[Electric Blast](ref/kinetic/electric-blast), [Metal Blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast),
[Plasma Blast](ref/kinetic/plasma-blast), [Thunderstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/thunderstorm-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Occult Adventures/21

Your kinetic blast causes your foes to become mildly magnetic. Whenever an infused blast deals
damage to a foe, attacks made with metal weapons (including
[metal kinetic blasts](ref/kinetic/metal-blast)) against that foe receive a +4 bonus until the end
of your next turn, and the foe takes a -4 penalty on Reflex saving throws to avoid metallic objects.
