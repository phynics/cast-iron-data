## From the Ashes (Sp)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 9

**Burn:** 2

**Source:** Occult Adventures/25

When you are about to fall unconscious or die from hit point damage, as an **immediate action** you
can burst into flames that leave behind only ash, which cannot be appreciably affected by most
attacks or effects, though a [disintegrate](ref/spell/disintegrate) spell or similar magic can
annihilate it. At the start of your next turn, unless the ash has been completely destroyed, you
arise in the square where the most ash is located, having taken the damage from the triggering
effect but having also healed 5 points of damage per kineticist level you possess.
