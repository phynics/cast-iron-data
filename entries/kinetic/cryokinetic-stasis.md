## Cryokinetic Stasis (Sp)

**Element:** water

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 8

**Burn:** 1

**Source:** Psychic Anthology/22

You encase yourself or a willing target in an icy sheen, placing the target in a state of suspended
animation, as per [temporal stasis](ref/spell/temporal-stasis) except fire spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) of 3rd level or higher count as if the caster
used [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) against the stasis. Otherwise, no other force or effect
can harm the target.
