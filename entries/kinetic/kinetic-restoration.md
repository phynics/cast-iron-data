## Kinetic Restoration (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 1

**Source:** Healer's Handbook/15

You recover from 1d4 points of [ability score damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) to one of your
physical ability scores (Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution). This also eliminates any
[fatigue](ref/misc/fatigued) that you suffer, and improves an [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted)
condition to fatigued.

If you have the [kinetic healer](ref/kinetic/kinetic-healer) utility wild talent, you can use both
_kineticist restoration_ and _kinetic healer_ on the same target with one **standard action**.
