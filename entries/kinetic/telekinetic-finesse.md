## Telekinetic Finesse (Sp)

**Element:** aether

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

You can perform any sort of fine manipulation you choose within close range, including attempting
[Sleight of Hand](ref/skill/sleight-of-hand) and [Disable Device](ref/skill/disable-device) checks.
