**Element:** air

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 3

**Associated:** [Electric Blast](ref/kinetic/electric-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

Your electric blast leaps from target to target. When you hit a target with your infused blast, you
can attempt a ranged touch attack against an additional target that is within 30 feet of the first.
Each additional attack originates from the previous target, which could alter
[cover](ref/rule/cover) and other conditions.

Each additional target takes 1d6 fewer points of damage than the last (for example, 3d6 becomes
2d6), and you can't _chain_ the blast back to a previous target. You can continue _chaining_ your
blasts until a blast misses or fails to deal damage, or until your blast is reduced to a single
damage die.
