## Clockwork Puppet (Su)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [metal blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast)

**Source:** Heroes of Golarion/26

You can weave metal into clockwork parts, creating clockwork constructs to attack your foes. This
functions as the [aether puppet](ref/kinetic/aether-puppet) wild talent, except the object animated
gains the clockwork subtype and cannot have the _stone_ Construction Point ability.
