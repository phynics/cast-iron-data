## Wind Manipulator (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 6

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [engulfing winds](ref/kinetic/engulfing-winds)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/29

You can call forth mighty winds in an enormous area. As a **standard action**, you can alter the
wind around you as if using [control winds](ref/spell/control-winds), but the change remains only
for as long as you continue to take a standard action to concentrate on this effect. If you accept 1
point of burn, you can extend the duration to 10 minutes per kineticist level you possess without
your needing to concentrate.
