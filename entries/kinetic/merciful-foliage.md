## Merciful Foliage (Su)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/59, Occult Origins/8

The First World is a place of rampant life, and you can use some of its energy to ease the danger of
your attacks. You can deal nonlethal damage without taking a penalty on attack rolls with your wood
blasts or composite blasts that include wood.
