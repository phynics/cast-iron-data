## Basic Aerokinesis (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/23

You can create a light breeze that blows against a creature or object from a direction of your
choice that follows the target wherever it goes. The breeze grants the subject a +2 bonus on saves
against very [hot conditions](ref/rule/heat-dangers), severe heat, breath weapons, and cloud vapors
and gases (such as [cloudkill](ref/spell/cloudkill), [stinking cloud](ref/spell/stinking-cloud), and
inhaled poisons). This wild talent doesn't function without air or while underwater. You can have
only one such breeze active at any one time.

You can also use your aerokinesis to make it harder to detect you or others by
[scent](ref/umr/scent). You can designate a number of creatures or objects equal to your
Constitution bonus. These creatures and objects always count as being downwind for the purpose of
determining the distance at which they can be detected by scent. This effect lasts for 1 hour or
until you use basic aerokinesis again, whichever comes first.
