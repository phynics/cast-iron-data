## Searing Flesh (Su)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** defense (Su)

**Level:** 0

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/17

Your body becomes painfully hot.

Whenever a creature hits you with a natural attack or an unarmed strike, that creature takes a
number of points of fire damage equal to [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
kineticist level (minimum 1). A creature in a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) with you takes double this
amount of damage at the end of each of its turns. Weapons that strike you also take this damage,
though the damage is unlikely to penetrate the weapon's [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

By accepting 1 point of burn, you can increase this damage by one-fourth of your kineticist level
(minimum 0) until the next time your burn is removed. You can increase the damage in this way up to
seven times.

Whenever you accept burn while using a fire wild talent, the surging flame causes your _searing
flesh_ to deal double its current amount of damage for 1 round (a creature in a grapple with you
takes a total of four times as much damage as normal). You can dismiss or restore this effect as an
**immediate action**.
