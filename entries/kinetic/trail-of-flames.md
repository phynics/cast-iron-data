## Trail of Flames (Sp)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Saving throw:** none

**Spell resistance:** yes

When you take the [withdraw](ref/rule/withdraw) or [run](ref/rule/run) action, you can leave a
[wall of fire](ref/spell/wall-of-fire) in all squares you exit that lasts for 1 round.
