## Venom Speaker (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Heroes of Golarion/26

You gain the [investigator's](ref/class/investigator) _poison lore_ class feature, using your
kineticist level as your investigator level, and can use your _gather power_ ability even while
holding a dose of poison in one of your hands or appendages as long as you could otherwise use that
ability.

If you are at least 6th level, you can learn the [alchemist's](ref/class/alchemist) _swift
poisoning_ class feature^[1](ref/kinetic/venom-speaker#footnote-n)^ or one of the following
alchemist discoveries in place of a utility wild talent, using your kineticist level as your
alchemist level: [concentrate poison](ref/discovery/concentrate-poison),
[poison conversion](ref/discovery/poison-conversion), or
[sticky poison](ref/discovery/sticky-poison).

**Poison Lore (Ex)**

**Ability:** If you spend 1 minute physically examining the poison, you can attempt a
[Knowledge (nature)](ref/skill/knowledge-nature) check to identify any natural poison or
[Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) check to identify any magical poison (DC = the
poison's saving throw DC). Lastly, once a poison is identified, you can spend 1 minute and attempt a
[Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) (alchemy) check (DC = the poison's saving throw DC) to neutralize 1
dose of the poison. Success renders the dose harmless. You have no chance of accidentally poisoning
yourself when examining or attempting to neutralize a poison.

**Flavor:** Text from the investigator class ability.

**Passive:** You cannot accidentally poison yourself when applying poison to a weapon.

###### ^1^ {#footnote-n}

The investigator has no "Swift Poisoner" ability, but _does_ have a "Swift Alchemy" ability that
allows the investigator to apply poison to a weapon as a **move action**. Consult your GM.
