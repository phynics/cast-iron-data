**Element:** air, fire, water

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 2

**Associated:** [Blue Flame Blast](ref/kinetic/blue-flame-blast),
[Cold Blast](ref/kinetic/cold-blast), [Electric Blast](ref/kinetic/electric-blast),
[Fire Blast](ref/kinetic/fire-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Elemental Master's Handbook/22

The intensity of your blasts allows you to break through your foes' resistances. Treat your foe's
cold, electricity, or fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) as if it were 5 lower for the purpose of
determining the infused blast's damage; this doesn't stack with the reduction from
[searing flame](ref/kinetic/searing-flame).

You can increase the burn cost of this infusion to further reduce the foe's resistance against the
infused blast by 5 per additional point of burn accepted. This has no effect on creatures with
immunity.
