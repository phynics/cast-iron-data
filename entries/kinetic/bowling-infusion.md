**Element:** aether, earth

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 2

**Associated:** [Earth Blast](ref/kinetic/earth-blast), [Magma Blast](ref/kinetic/magma-blast),
[Metal Blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast), [Mud Blast](ref/kinetic/mud-blast),
[Telekinetic Blast](ref/kinetic/telekinetic-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

You bowl your foes over with the sheer mass of your kinetic blast. Attempt a [trip](ref/rule/trip)
combat maneuver check against each target damaged by your infused blast, using your Constitution
modifier instead of your Strength modifier to determine your Combat Maneuver Bonus.
