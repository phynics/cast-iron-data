## Spark of Life (Sp)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

You breathe a semblance of life into elemental matter, which takes the form of a Medium elemental of
any of your elements as if summoned by [summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4) with a caster
level equal to your kineticist level, except the elemental gains the mindless trait.

Each round on your turn, you must take a **move action** to guide the elemental or it collapses back
into its component element. By accepting 1 point of burn, you can pour a bit of your own sentience
into the elemental, removing the mindless quality and allowing it to persist for 1 round per
kineticist level without requiring any further actions.

At 12th level, you can choose to form a Large elemental as if by
[summon monster V](ref/spell/summon-monster-5).

At 14th level, you can choose to form a Huge elemental as if by
[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6)

At 16th level, you can choose to form a greater elemental as if by
[summon monster VII](ref/spell/summon-monster-7)

At 18th level, you can choose to form an elder elemental as if by
[summon monster VIII](ref/spell/summon-monster-8).
