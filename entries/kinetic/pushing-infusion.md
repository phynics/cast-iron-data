**Element:** aether, air, earth, void, water, wood

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 1

**Associated:** [Air Blast](ref/kinetic/air-blast), [Blizzard Blast](ref/kinetic/blizzard-blast),
[Charged Water Blast](ref/kinetic/charged-water-blast), [Earth Blast](ref/kinetic/earth-blast),
[Gravity Blast](ref/kinetic/gravity-blast), [Magma Blast](ref/kinetic/magma-blast),
[Metal Blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast), [Mud Blast](ref/kinetic/mud-blast),
[Sandstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/sandstorm-blast), [Steam Blast](ref/kinetic/steam-blast),
[Telekinetic Blast](ref/kinetic/telekinetic-blast), [Void Blast](ref/kinetic/void-blast),
[Water Blast](ref/kinetic/water-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

The momentum of your kinetic blast knocks foes back. Attempt a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush)
combat maneuver check against each target damaged by your infused blast, using your Constitution
modifier instead of your Strength modifier to determine your bonus. This infusion can push a foe
back by a maximum of 5 feet. You can increase the burn cost of this infusion to increase the maximum
distance pushed by 5 feet per additional point of burn accepted.

You can't use this infusion with a form infusion such as [cloud](ref/kinetic/cloud) that causes your
kinetic blast to lack a clear direction to push.
