**Element:** air, fire, void, water

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 1

**Associated:** [Blue Flame Blast](ref/kinetic/blue-flame-blast),
[Cold Blast](ref/kinetic/cold-blast), [Electric Blast](ref/kinetic/electric-blast),
[Fire Blast](ref/kinetic/fire-blast), [Negative Blast](ref/kinetic/negative-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

Choose a manufactured weapon in your hand (not an unarmed strike or natural attack); if the weapon
is a double weapon, you must choose one of its ends to receive this benefit. You imbue the chosen
weapon with your elemental energy as part of an attack action, [charge](ref/rule/charge) action, or
full-attack action to add extra damage to each of your attacks with that weapon until the beginning
of your next turn. Your attacks with the chosen weapon during that action deal 1d6 additional points
of damage.

At 7th level, this bonus damage becomes 2d6 points. At 13th level, it increases to 3d6, and at 19th
level it becomes 4d6. _Blue flame blasts_ deal double this additional damage. The additional damage
is of the same type as the infused blast's damage.

This additional damage ignores [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) and doesn't apply any
modifiers to your kinetic blast's damage (such as your Constitution modifier).
