## Expanded Defense (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 4

**Burn:** 0

Choose an element you selected with expanded element. You gain that element's defense wild talent.

**Special:** You can take this wild talent multiple times, choosing a different element each time.
