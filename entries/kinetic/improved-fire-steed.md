## Improved Fire Steed (Sp)

**Element:** fire

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [fire steed](ref/kinetic/fire-steed)

When using _fire steed,_ the mount's fire resistance increases to 20 and its speed increases by an
additional 10 feet. The mount can move over lava without difficulty or a decrease in speed.
