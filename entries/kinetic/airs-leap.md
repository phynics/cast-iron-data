## Air's Leap (Su)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/23

You are always considered to have a running start when jumping, you add your kineticist level as a
bonus on all [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to jump, you jump twice as far or high as the
results of your check indicate, and you can accept 1 point of burn when jumping to double the
distance you jump again (to a total of four times as far).
