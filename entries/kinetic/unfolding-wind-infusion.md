**Element:** air

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 3

**Prerequisite:** [kinetic fist](ref/kinetic/kinetic-fist), member of the Monastery of Unfolding
Wind

**Associated:** [Air Blast](ref/kinetic/air-blast),
[Thunderstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/thunderstorm-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

Unfolding winds carry you along.

Before your first attack and again after each unarmed strike with the infused blast, you can move 5
feet without provoking attacks of opportunity. You can use this infusion only while also using the
_kinetic fist_ form infusion.
