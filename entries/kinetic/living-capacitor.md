## Living Capacitor (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Psychic Anthology/23

You store electricity damage you take within your body, as if you constantly had
[body capacitance](ref/spell/body-capacitance) active. Once you discharge this electricity damage
via a touch attack, you must take electricity damage again before you can discharge it again.
