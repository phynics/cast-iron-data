## Aerial Adaptation (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

You are immune to [altitude sickness](ref/rule/mountain-travel), and gain an amount of electricity
[resistance](ref/umr/resistance) equal to twice your current amount of burn.
