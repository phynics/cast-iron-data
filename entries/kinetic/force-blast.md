## Force Blast (Sp)

**Element:** aether

**Type:** composite blast (Sp)

**Level:** 0

**Burn:** 2

**Damage type:** energy

**Damage:** force (see text)

**Prerequisite:** primary element (aether), expanded element (aether)

You throw a burst of force at a foe. Force blast deals damage as a simple energy blast instead of a
composite energy blast.
