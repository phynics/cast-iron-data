## Tengu Blade Lore (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [tengu](ref/race/tengu)

**Source:** Heroes of Golarion/26

Whenever you use [kinetic blade](ref/kinetic/kinetic-blade) or an infusion that lists kinetic blade
as a prerequisite, the weapon you create counts as having one of the following
[weapon special features](ref/rule/weapon-special-features) of your choice:
[blocking](ref/misc/blocking), [brace](ref/misc/brace), [deadly](ref/misc/deadly),
[disarm](ref/misc/disarm), [distracting](ref/misc/distracting), [sunder](ref/misc/sunder), or
[trip](ref/misc/trip).
