## Winter Blast (Sp)

**Element:** water, wood

**Type:** composite blast (Sp)

**Level:** 0

**Burn:** 2

**Damage type:** physical

**Damage:** half cold and half bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing

**Prerequisite:** [cold blast](ref/kinetic/cold-blast), [wood blast](ref/kinetic/wood-blast)

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/61, Occult Origins/9

You unleash a blast of frigid frost and jagged, bare branches at your target.
