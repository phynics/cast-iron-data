**Element:** universal

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 2

**Prerequisite:** [kinetic blade](ref/kinetic/kinetic-blade)

**Associated:** any

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Psychic Anthology/20

You use your element's power to instantly move 30 feet in any direction (even straight up), manifest
a _kinetic blade,_ and attack once. You gain a +2 bonus on the attack roll and take a -2 penalty to
your AC until the start of your next turn. The movement doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity,
though activating _blade rush_ does.

If you have the [kinetic whip](ref/kinetic/kinetic-whip) infusion, you can manifest a _kinetic whip_
instead of a _kinetic blade_ at the end of your movement by increasing the burn cost of this
infusion by 1. The _blade_ or _whip_ vanishes instantly after the rush.
