## Spark of Innovation (Sp)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [metal blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast)

**Source:** Heroes of Golarion/26

You shape metal into a clockwork creature, as [spark of life](ref/kinetic/spark-of-life) except you
summon a clockwork [earth elemental](ref/monster/earth-elemental). The elemental's type is changed
to construct (clockwork) and it gains [construct](ref/type/construct) traits and
[clockwork traits](ref/subtype/clockwork) instead of elemental traits. The creature loses any
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) or supernatural abilities it normally has.

If you have the [greater elemental whispers](ref/kinetic/greater-elemental-whispers) utility wild
talent, you can take a [clockwork familiar](ref/monster/clockwork-clockwork-familiar) as an improved
familiar with that ability instead of an earth elemental.
