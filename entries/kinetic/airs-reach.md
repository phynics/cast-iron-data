## Air's Reach (Su)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/23

When using air blasts, air wild talents, or composite blasts that include air, double the blast's
effective range. This effect applies after altering the range due to effects such as the
[extended range](ref/kinetic/extended-range) infusion. This doubles only the blast's effective
range, not the area of effect for infusions like [cloud](ref/kinetic/cloud) and
[cyclone](ref/kinetic/cyclone).
