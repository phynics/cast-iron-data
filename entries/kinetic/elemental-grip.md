## Elemental Grip (Sp)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Saving throw:** Will negates

**Spell resistance:** yes

**Source:** Occult Adventures/24

You use your element to halt a creature associated with your element, as
[hold monster](ref/spell/hold-monster) except it requires concentration to maintain, and it works
only on creatures with a subtype matching one of your elements (for example, a pyrokineticist could
use it on creatures of the fire subtype). At any time, you can accept 1 point of burn to remove the
need to concentrate.
