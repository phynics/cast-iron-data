## Shift Earth (Sp)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 4

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [kinetic cover](ref/kinetic/kinetic-cover)

**Saving throw:** see text

**Spell resistance:** no

**Source:** Occult Adventures/26

As a **standard action**, you can push or pull a 5-foot cube of earth or unworked stone within 30
feet, moving the cube 5 feet in any direction. You can create raised platforms, stairs up a cliff,
holes, or other useful features. This doesn't cause the earth to float in the air, although in areas
with plenty of earth, you can move a cube upward, creating a short pillar. If you move the earth
beneath a creature's feet, it can attempt a DC 20 Reflex save to leap elsewhere and avoid moving
along with the earth.
