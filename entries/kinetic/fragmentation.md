**Element:** earth, water

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 7

**Burn:** 4

**Prerequisite:** [extended range](ref/kinetic/extended-range)

**Associated:** [Earth Blast](ref/kinetic/earth-blast), [Ice Blast](ref/kinetic/ice-blast),
[Metal Blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast)

**Saving throw:** none or Reflex half; see text

You throw a volatile sphere of solid matter that shatters into countless shards. Attempt a ranged
attack roll against a target within 120 feet. If you hit, the target takes your kinetic blast damage
with no saving throw. All other creatures and objects within a 20-foot [burst](ref/misc/burst) take
half the normal amount of damage and can attempt a Reflex save to reduce that damage by half again.
If the attack misses its target, the blast has no effect. The saving throw DC is Dexterity-based.
