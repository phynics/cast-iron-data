## Angelic Protection (Sp)

**Element:** aether, water, wood

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [kinetic healer](ref/kinetic/kinetic-healer) wild talent,
[aasimar](ref/race/aasimar) or [Planar Infusion](ref/feat/planar-infusion) (Elysium, Heaven,
Nirvana)

**Saving throw:** none

**Spell resistance:** none

When you use _kinetic healer,_ the target of the healing gains the benefits of
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil) for 1 round.
