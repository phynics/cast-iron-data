**Element:** air, water

**Type:** form infusion

**Level:** 7

**Burn:** 4

**Prerequisite:** [extended range](ref/kinetic/extended-range)

**Associated:** [Blizzard Blast](ref/kinetic/blizzard-blast),
[Sandstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/sandstorm-blast), [Steam Blast](ref/kinetic/steam-blast),
[Thunderstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/thunderstorm-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Occult Adventures/18

You release your power in a diffuse cloud. You can center this 20-foot-radius
[spread](ref/misc/spread) anywhere within 120 feet of you. All creatures and objects within the
_cloud_ when you create it automatically take [one-fourth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) the
normal amount of damage from your blast with no saving throw. Any time a creature enters the
_cloud,_ it takes half the normal amount of damage from your blast, as do creatures that end their
turns in the _cloud._

Your _cloud_ obscures vision as [obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist). It lasts for a number of
rounds equal to your Constitution modifier or until you use the _cloud_ infusion again.
