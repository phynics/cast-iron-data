## Air Cushion (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/23

You cushion a fall from any height. You are constantly under the effects of
[feather fall](ref/spell/feather-fall), and you count as one size category larger for the purpose of
determining how you are affected by [wind](ref/rule/winds).
