## Telekinetic Deflection (Sp)

**Element:** aether

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 8

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/27

You can deflect your foes' attacks, as the spell [deflection](ref/spell/deflection) with a duration
of 1 round. You can accept 1 point of burn to increase the duration to 1 round per kineticist level
you possess.
