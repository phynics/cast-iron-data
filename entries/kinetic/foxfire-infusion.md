## Foxfire Infusion (Sp)

**Element:** fire, wood

**Type:** substance infusion (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 2

**Prerequisite:** [foxfire](ref/kinetic/foxfire) or [positive blast](ref/kinetic/positive-blast)

**Associated:** [Blue Flame Blast](ref/kinetic/blue-flame-blast),
[Fire Blast](ref/kinetic/fire-blast), Positive Blast

**Saving throw:** Will partial

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/58, Psychic Anthology/20

Your blast leaves behind flickering flames that reveal the target's location as per
[faerie fire](ref/spell/faerie-fire) for 1 minute (or until your next turn if the target succeeds at
its save). This effect occurs even if the foe doesn't take damage, as long as the attack overcomes
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) (if any). You can reduce the blast's damage by half to
increase the DC of this saving throw by 2. A creature can remove the revealing flames early by
taking a **full-round action** to extinguish them.
