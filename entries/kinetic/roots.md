## Roots (Su)

**Element:** wood

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Ultimate Wilderness/60, Occult Origins/9

Your body sinks roots into soft surfaces, making it hard to move you. This functions as the
[earth walk](ref/kinetic/earth-walk) wild talent, except it works only on soft surfaces (like soil
or snow) and not on stone. If you have both _roots_ and _earth walk,_ the bonuses stack while you're
on soft earth surfaces.
