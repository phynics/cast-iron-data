## Basic Geokinesis (Sp)

**Element:** earth

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

You can move up to 5 pounds per kineticist level of rocks, loose earth, sand, clay, and other
similar materials up to 15 feet as a move action. You can search earthen and stone areas from a
distance as if using the [sift](ref/spell/sift) cantrip.
