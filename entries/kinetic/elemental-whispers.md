## Elemental Whispers (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Horror Adventures/57

You form a friendship with a fragment of your element. The element speaks back to you empathically,
like a familiar.

Pick a type of creature usually allowed as a [familiar](ref/ability/familiars) when you gain this
wild talent. You gain the [Alertness](ref/feat/alertness) feat and the special abilities granted by
a familiar of that type.

As a **standard action**, you can give the voice of the element the body of such a creature, using
elemental matter of the appropriate element within 30 feet. As long as you concentrate, the familiar
can take actions as a normal animal of its type, and it can move any distance away from you, though
if it takes any damage or you cease concentrating, it returns to your mind. You lose Alertness while
the familiar is manifested in this way.

The [familiar](ref/sidekick/familiar) gains abilities as usual for a familiar of its type, though it
never gains the ability to speak with animals of its kind.
