## Greater Skilled Kineticist (Su)

**Element:** universal

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [skilled kineticist](ref/kinetic/skilled-kineticist)

By delving into study of the Ethereal and Elemental Planes, you are able to use your primary element
to assist with your skills in an unusual way. Add [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes)
to your list of class skills. Choose one of your kineticist class skills that wasn't granted by your
primary element to also receive the bonus from skilled kineticist.
