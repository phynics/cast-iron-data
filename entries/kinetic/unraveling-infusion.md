**Element:** fire

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 5

**Burn:** 3

**Associated:** [Blue Flame Blast](ref/kinetic/blue-flame-blast),
[Fire Blast](ref/kinetic/fire-blast)

**Saving throw:** none

**Source:** Occult Adventures/22

Your kinetic blast burns so hot that it melts away your foe's magical effects.

Whenever your infused blast hits a foe and penetrates its
[spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance), you can attempt a caster level check as if using a
targeted [dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic) before determining whether the foe takes damage from
the blast. You can choose to reduce the blast's damage by half to increase your bonus on the caster
level check by 2.

If you don't choose a specific spell effect to attempt to dispel, you automatically attempt to
dispel effects that would protect the target from fire damage before other spells.
