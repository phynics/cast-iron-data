## Engulfing Winds (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/24

You bend your winds into a formidable barrier. Until the start of your next turn, you lose the
benefits of _enveloping winds,_ but can create a [wind wall](ref/spell/wind-wall) as the spell. You
can extend the wall's duration to 1 round per kineticist level by accepting 1 point of burn.
