**Element:** earth, water, wood

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 2

**Associated:** [Blizzard Blast](ref/kinetic/blizzard-blast), [Cold Blast](ref/kinetic/cold-blast),
[Earth Blast](ref/kinetic/earth-blast), [Ice Blast](ref/kinetic/ice-blast),
[Magma Blast](ref/kinetic/magma-blast), [Metal Blast](ref/kinetic/metal-blast),
[Mud Blast](ref/kinetic/mud-blast), [Sandstorm Blast](ref/kinetic/sandstorm-blast)

**Saving throw:** Reflex negates

**Source:** Occult Adventures/19

Your kinetic blast surrounds your foes in elemental matter.

Whenever a blast with this infusion deals damage to a foe, that foe becomes
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) for 1 minute. The foe can remove this condition as a **standard
action** with a successful [Escape Artist](ref/skill/escape-artist) or Strength check (with the same
DC as for saves against your kinetic blast) or by dealing an amount of damage to the entangling
matter equal to double your kineticist level (the matter has [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) 0).

If the foe was already entangled by this infusion and fails its save against a second instance of
this infusion, the increased amount of elemental matter fuses to the ground, causing the foe to be
rooted in place as though anchored to an immobile object.
