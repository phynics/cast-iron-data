## Watersense (Su)

**Element:** water

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 4

**Burn:** 0

You gain [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense) with a range of 30 feet to detect anything in contact
with the same body of water as you. While you are benefiting from this tremorsense, your
[water blasts](ref/kinetic/water-blast) and composite blasts that include water don't suffer a miss
chance from [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) or total concealment against creatures you detect
with this tremorsense.
