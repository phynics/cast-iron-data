**Element:** void

**Type:** substance infusion

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 2

**Associated:** [Negative Blast](ref/kinetic/negative-blast), [Void Blast](ref/kinetic/void-blast)

**Saving throw:** Will negates

**Source:** Elemental Master's Handbook/22

Your kinetic blast sends the fear of oblivion into your foes.

Whenever an infused blast deals negative energy damage to a living foe, it is
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 1 round. This shaken condition doesn't stack with itself.
