## Steam Blast (Sp)

**Element:** fire, water

**Type:** composite blast (Sp)

**Level:** 0

**Burn:** 2

**Damage type:** physical

**Damage:** half bludgeoning, half fire

**Prerequisite:** [fire blast](ref/kinetic/fire-blast), [water blast](ref/kinetic/water-blast)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/16

You use your fire to superheat water into steam just as it strikes.
