## Weather Master (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 8

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/29

By focusing on your connection to air and seeding the air around you for 10 minutes, you can create
powerful weather effects, as [control weather](ref/spell/control-weather). You can create tornadoes
or hurricane-force winds, as appropriate for the season. If you have access to the fire element, you
can create hot weather, a heat wave, a thaw, or an early spring. If you have access to the water
element, you can create all other types of weather listed in
_[control weather](ref/spell/control-weather)._
