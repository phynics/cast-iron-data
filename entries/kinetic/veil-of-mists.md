## Veil of Mists (Su)

**Element:** water

**Type:** utility (Su)

**Level:** 2

**Burn:** 0

**Source:** Occult Adventures/28

You create a misty veil over your features, disguising yourself as
[disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self) for a number of minutes equal to your Constitution
modifier. If you accept 1 point of burn, until the next time your burn is removed, each use of this
wild talent lasts until you dismiss it.
