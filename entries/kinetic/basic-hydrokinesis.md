## Basic Hydrokinesis (Sp)

**Element:** water

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 1

**Burn:** 0

You can create water as the cantrip [create water](ref/spell/create-water), purify water as if using
[purify food and drink](ref/spell/purify-food-and-drink), and dry wet creatures and objects as if
using [prestidigitation](ref/spell/prestidigitation). While you cannot lift water into the air using
this ability, you can create mild currents in a body of water by concentrating. These currents are
strong enough to run a water mill as if the mill were being turned manually by a creature with a
Strength score equal to your Constitution score.
