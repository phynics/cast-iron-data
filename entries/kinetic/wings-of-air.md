## Wings of Air (Sp)

**Element:** air

**Type:** utility (Sp)

**Level:** 3

**Burn:** 0

**Prerequisite:** [air cushion](ref/kinetic/air-cushion) or [air's leap](ref/kinetic/airs-leap)

**Source:** Occult Adventures/29

The air bends to your will, allowing you to soar to great heights. You are constantly under the
effects of [fly](ref/spell/fly). If this effect is dispelled, you can call it forth again as a
**standard action**.
