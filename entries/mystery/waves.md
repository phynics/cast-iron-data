### Revelations

An oracle with the Waves mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Blizzard (Su)**

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Special:** The blizzard obscures sight beyond 5 feet, providing total concealment. A creature
within 5 feet has [concealment](ref/rule/concealment).

**Standard:** Once a day, you can create a blizzard of snow and ice. You can create one 10-foot-cube
of this storm per oracle level. These cubes can be arranged in any pattern you desire, but each cube
must be adjacent to another, and at least one must be adjacent to you.

Any creature caught in the blizzard takes 1d4 points of cold damage per oracle level, with a
successful Reflex save resulting in half damage.

The storm lasts for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier; the ground remains icy (+5
to [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) DCs) as long as local conditions permit

**Fluid Nature (Ex)**

**At 5th Level:** You gain [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge) as a bonus feat. You do not need to meet the
prerequisite to gain this feat.

**Passive:** You receive a +4 bonus to your Combat Maneuver Defense against
[bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush), [drag](ref/rule/drag), [grapple](ref/rule/grapple),
[reposition](ref/rule/reposition), and [trip](ref/rule/trip) attempts.

A creature trying to confirm a critical hit against you has a -4 penalty on its confirmation roll.

**Fluid Travel (Su)**

**Ability:** You can walk on liquid as if it were a solid surface. Walking on the liquid does not
harm you; you can walk on acid or even lava (as if walking on a solid temporary crust), though you
would still take fire damage from being near the lava. You can move across this surface at your
normal land speed.

**At 7th Level:** While this ability is in effect, you can instead go underwater, gaining a swim
speed of 60 feet and the ability to breathe water.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

**Freezing Spells (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a creature fails a saving throw and takes cold damage from one of your spells,
it is slowed (as the [slow](ref/spell/slow) spell) for 1 round.

**At 11th Level:** The duration increases to 1d4 rounds.

**Special:** Spells that do not allow a save do not slow creatures.

**Ice Armor (Su)**

**Ability:** You can conjure armor of ice that grants you a +4 armor bonus.

**At 13th Level:** This armor grants you DR 5/piercing.

**Special:** In cold conditions, the armor bonus (and DR bonus) increases by 2; in very hot
conditions it decreases by 2.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

At 7th level: The armor bonus +6.

At 11th level: The armor bonus +8.

At 15th level: The armor bonus +10.

At 19th level: The armor bonus +12.

**Icy Skin (Ex)**

**At 17th Level:** You gain immunity to cold.

**Passive:** You gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) cold 5.

At 7th level: This resistance 10.

**Punitive Transformation (Su)**

**Ability:** You can transform an opponent into a harmless animal as if using
[baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph). This transformation lasts 1 round per oracle
level.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Special:** Transforming another creature causes the first to immediately revert to normal.

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Water Form (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** You can assume the form of a Large water elemental, as
[elemental body III](ref/spell/elemental-body-3).

**At 13th Level:** You can assume the form of a Huge water elemental, as
[elemental body IV](ref/spell/elemental-body-4).

**At 9th Level:** You can assume the form of a Medium water elemental, as
[elemental body II](ref/spell/elemental-body-2).

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Standard:** You can assume the form of a [Small water elemental](ref/monster/water-elemental), as
[elemental body I](ref/spell/elemental-body-1).

**Usage:** Once per day, for 1 hour per oracle level.

**Usage:** Once per day, for 1 hour per oracle level.

**Water Sight (Su)**

**Ability:** You can see through fog and mist without penalty as long as there is enough light to
allow you to see normal.

**At 15th Level:** The scrying now functions like [greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater).

**At 7th Level:** You can use any calm pool of water at least 1 foot in diameter as a scrying
device, as if using the [scrying](ref/spell/scrying) spell.

**Special:** You can use the scrying abilities for a number of rounds per day equal to your oracle
level, but these rounds do not need to be consecutive.

**Wintry Touch (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon that you wield is treated as a [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) weapon.

**Standard:** You can perform a melee touch attack that deals cold damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your oracle level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You can apply any one of the following feats to any cold or water spell without
increasing the level or casting time: [Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell),
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell), [Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), or
[Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell).

**Flavor:** You become a master of cold and water.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
