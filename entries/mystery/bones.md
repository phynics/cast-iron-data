### Revelations

An oracle with the Bones mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Armor of Bones (Su)**

**Ability:** You can conjure armor made of bones that grants you a +4 armor bonus.

**At 13th Level:** This armor grants you DR 5/bludgeoning.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

At 7th level: The armor's bonus +6.

At 11th level: The armor's bonus +8.

At 15th level: The armor's bonus +10.

At 19th level: The armor's bonus +12.

**Bleeding Wounds (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a creature takes damage from one of your spells or effects that causes
negative energy damage (such as [inflict light wounds](ref/spell/inflict-light-wounds) or the
_death's touch_ revelation), it begins to [bleed](ref/rule/bleed), taking an amount of damage each
round equal to 1 + [one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level.

**Special:** The bleeding can be stopped by a DC 15 Heal check or any effect that heals damage.

**Death's Touch (Su)**

**Ability:** You can cause terrible wounds to appear on a creature with a melee touch attack. This
attack deals 1d6 points of negative energy damage plus a number of points equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level.

If used against an undead creature, it heals damage and grants a +2
[channel resistance](ref/rule/channel-resistance) for 1 minute.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Near Death (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** The bonus increases to +4.

**At 7th Level:** This bonus also applies on saves against death effects, sleep effects, and
stunning.

**Passive:** You gain a +2 insight bonus on saves against diseases, mind-affecting effects, and
poisons.

**Raise the Dead (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** You can use this ability twice a day.

**At 15th Level:** You can summon an [advanced](ref/template/advanced) skeleton or zombie.

**At 7th Level:** You can summon a _bloody skeleton_ or _fast zombie._

**Standard:** Once per day, you can summon a single [skeleton](ref/template/skeleton) or
[zombie](ref/template/zombie) to serve you. The undead creature has a number of Hit Dice equal to
your oracle level. It remains for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier.

**Resist Life (Su)**

**At 7th Level:** You receive [channel resistance](ref/rule/channel-resistance) +2.

**Passive:** You are treated as an undead creature when you are targeted by positive or negative
energy. You are not subject to [Turn Undead](ref/feat/turn-undead) or
[Command Undead](ref/feat/command-undead) (or any other effect that specifically targets undead),
unless you are actually an undead creature.

At 11th level: This bonus +4.

At 15th level: This bonus +6.

**Soul Siphon (Su)**

**Ability:** As a ranged touch attack, you can unleash a ray that causes a target to gain one
[negative level](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels). The ray has a range of 30 feet. This
negative level lasts for a number of minutes equal to your Charisma modifier.

Whenever this ability gives a target a negative level, you heal a number of hit points equal to your
oracle level.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 oracle levels beyond 7th

**Spirit Walk (Su)**

**At 15th Level:** You can do this twice a day.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Standard:** Once per day, you can become incorporeal and invisible. While in this form, you can
move in any direction and through any object (except for those made of force). You can take no
action other than to move while in this form. You remain in this form for a number of rounds equal
to your oracle level, but you can end this effect prematurely with a **standard action**.

**Undead Servitude (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain Command Undead as a bonus feat. You can
[channel negative energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) (as an evil cleric) a number of times per day
equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier, but only to use Command Undead.

**Special:** You can take other feats to add to this ability, such as
[Improved Channel](ref/feat/improved-channel), but not feats that alter this ability, such as
[Alignment Channel](ref/feat/alignment-channel).

**Voice of the Grave (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead), as per the spell.

**At 5th Level:** The dead creature you question takes -2 penalty on its Will save to resist this
effect.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per oracle level

At 10th level: The penalty -4.

At 15th level: The penalty -6.

At 20th level: The penalty -8.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability2:** You can cast _[animate dead](ref/spell/animate-dead)_ at will without paying a
material component cost (although you are still subject to the usual Hit Die control limit).

**Ability3:** Once per day, you can cast [power word kill](ref/spell/power-word-kill), but the spell
can target a creature with 150 hit points or less.

**Flavor:** You become a master of death.

**Free:** Once per round, you can cast [bleed](ref/spell/bleed) or [stabilize](ref/spell/stabilize).

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** If you are brought to below 0 hit points, you automatically stabilize.
