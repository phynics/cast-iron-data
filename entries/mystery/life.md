### Revelations

An oracle with the Life mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Channel (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [channel positive energy](ref/ability/channel-energy) like a cleric, using your
oracle level as your effective cleric level when determining the amount of damage healed (or caused
to undead) and the DC.

**Usage:** Charisma1 modifier times/day

**Combat Healer (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a cure spell (a spell with &quot&cure&quot& in its name), you can
cast it as a **swift action**, as if using the [Quicken Spell](ref/feat/quicken-spell) feat, by
expending two spell slots. This does not increase the level of the spell.

**Flavor:** This is the same as the [battle](ref/mystery/battle) mystery revelation.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 oracle levels beyond 7th

**Delay Affliction (Su)**

**Immediate:** Once per day, whenever you fail a saving throw against a disease or poison, you may
ignore its effects for 1 hour per level.

**At 15th Level:** You can use this ability three times per day.

**At 7th Level:** You can do this twice a day.

**Energy Body (Su)**

**Special:** You may remain in energy body form for a number of rounds per day equal to your oracle
level.

**Standard:** You can transform your body into pure life energy, resembling a golden-white
[fire elemental](ref/monster/fire-elemental). In this form, you gain the elemental subtype and give
off a warm, welcoming light that increases the [light level](ref/rule/vision-and-light) within 10
feet by one step, up to normal light.

Any undead creature striking you with its body or a handheld weapon deals normal damage, but at the
same time the attacker takes 1d6 points of positive energy damage + 1 point per oracle level.
Creatures wielding melee weapons with reach are not subject to this damage if they attack you.

If you grapple or attack an undead creature using unarmed strikes or natural weapons, you may deal
this damage in place of the normal damage for the attack.

Once per round, if you pass through a living allied creature's square or the ally passes through
your square, it heals 1d6 hit points + 1 per oracle level. You may use this ability to heal yourself
as a **move action**. You choose whether or not to heal a creature when it passes through your
space.

You may return to your normal form as a **free action**.

**Enhanced Cures (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a cure spell, the maximum number of hit points healed is based on
your oracle level, not the limit based on the spell. For example, an 11th-level oracle of life with
this revelation may cast [cure light wounds](ref/spell/cure-light-wounds) to heal 1d8+11 hit points
instead of the normal 1d8+5 maximum.

**Healing Hands (Ex)**

**Ability:** You may provide first aid to two people or treat two people for poison as a **standard
action** (make a separate Heal check for each creature). When using the Heal skill to treat wounds
from [caltrops](ref/eq-misc/caltrops) (and so on), treat deadly wounds, treat poison, treat disease,
or treat long-term care, you may treat double the normal number of people you may treat at the same
time. You may provide long-term care for yourself.

**Passive:** You gain a +4 bonus on [Heal](ref/skill/heal) checks.

**Life Link (Su)**

**Standard:** You may create a bond between yourself and another creature. Each round at the start
of your turn, if the bonded creature is wounded for 5 or more hit points below its maximum hit
points, it heals 5 hit points and you take 5 hit points of damage.

You may have one bond active per oracle level. This bond continues until the bonded creature dies,
you die, the distance between you and the other creature exceeds [medium range](ref/rule/range), or
you end it as an **immediate action** (if you have multiple bonds active, you may end as many as you
want as part of the same immediate action).

**Lifesense (Su)**

**Ability:** You notice and locate living creatures within 30 feet, just as if you possessed the
[blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight) ability.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Safe Curing (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that cures the target of hit point damage, you do not provoke
attacks of opportunity for spellcasting.

**Spirit Boost (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever your healing spells heal a target up to its maximum hit points, any excess
points persist for 1 round per level as [temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) (up to
a maximum number of temporary hit points equal to your oracle level).

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Flavor:** You become a perfect channel for life energy.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You become immune to bleed, death attacks, exhaustion, fatigue, nausea effects,
negative levels, and sickened effects. Ability damage and drain cannot reduce you below 1 in any
ability score. You automatically make saving throws against massive damage. When you are below 0 hit
points, you do not die until your negative total is in excess of twice your Constitution score.
