### Revelations

An oracle with the Winter mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Blizzard (Su)**

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Special:** The blizzard obscures sight beyond 5 feet, providing total concealment. A creature
within 5 feet has [concealment](ref/rule/concealment).

**Standard:** Once a day, you can create a blizzard of snow and ice. You can create one 10-foot-cube
of this storm per oracle level. These cubes can be arranged in any pattern you desire, but each cube
must be adjacent to another, and at least one must be adjacent to you.

Any creature caught in the blizzard takes 1d4 points of cold damage per oracle level, with a
successful Reflex save resulting in half damage.

The storm lasts for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier; the ground remains icy (+5
to [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) DCs) as long as local conditions permit

**Child of Winter (Ex)**

**Passive:** You gain the constant benefit of _[endure elements](ref/spell/endure-elements),_ but
only against cold temperatures.

You can move across regular snow without penalty, and heavy snow costs you only 2 squares of
movement instead of 4. You can move across icy surfaces without penalty, and never need to make
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) checks to [run](ref/rule/run) or [charge](ref/rule/charge) on
ice.

You leave no trail in ice or snow, and cannot be tracked (you may choose to leave a trail if you so
desire).

**Special:** During winter months, you gain a +2 insight bonus on Initiative checks and Reflex
saving throws.

**Cold Aura (Su)**

**Special:** In addition, a flurry of snow momentarily surrounds you, granting you concealment until
your next turn.

**Swift:** You can cause waves of cold to radiate from your body. This cold deals 1d6 points of cold
damage per 2 oracle levels to all creatures within 10 feet. A successful Fortitude save halves the
damage.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Freezing Spells (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a creature fails a saving throw and takes cold damage from one of your spells,
it is slowed (as the [slow](ref/spell/slow) spell) for 1 round.

**At 11th Level:** The duration increases to 1d4 rounds.

**Special:** Spells that do not allow a save do not slow creatures.

**Ice Armor (Su)**

**Ability:** You can conjure armor of ice that grants you a +4 armor bonus.

**At 13th Level:** This armor grants you DR 5/piercing.

**Special:** In cold conditions, the armor bonus (and DR bonus) increases by 2; in very hot
conditions it decreases by 2.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

At 7th level: The armor bonus +6.

At 11th level: The armor bonus +8.

At 15th level: The armor bonus +10.

At 19th level: The armor bonus +12.

**Ice Shape (Su)**

**Ability:** You are able to sculpt ice and snow into almost any shape. This ability functions like
stone shape, but targeting only ice and snow, not stone.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Icy Skin (Ex)**

**At 17th Level:** You gain immunity to cold.

**Passive:** You gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) cold 5.

At 7th level: This resistance 10.

**Servant of Winter (Sp)**

**Fullround:** Once per day, you can summon a single [ice elemental](ref/monster/ice-elemental) to
serve you.

**At 11th Level:** You can summon a Huge ice elemental, as
[summon monster VI](ref/spell/summon-monster-6).

**At 15th Level:** You can summon an elder ice elemental, as
[summon monster VIII](ref/spell/summon-monster-8). You can now use this ability twice per day.

**At 7th Level:** You can summon a Medium ice elemental, as
[summon monster IV](ref/spell/summon-monster-4).

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Snow Sight (Su)**

**Ability:** You can see through falling snow and sleet without taking any penalties on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks as long as there is enough light to allow you to see
normally.

**At 11th Level:** Once per day, in cold conditions or in icy or snowy terrain, you can learn about
your surroundings as if using the [commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature) spell.

**At 15th Level:** You can use the _[commune with nature](ref/spell/commune-with-nature)_ ability
twice per day.

**Wintry Touch (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon that you wield is treated as a [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) weapon.

**Standard:** You can perform a melee touch attack that deals cold damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your oracle level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** Your body permanently transforms into living ice, as the
_[ice body](ref/spell/ice-body)_ spell. In addition, your mastery of winter magic is such that any
of your attacks that deal cold damage bypass cold immunity or cold resistance.

**Flavor:** You become an avatar of winter and the North.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
