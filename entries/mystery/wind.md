### Revelations

An oracle with the Wind mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Air Barrier (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can create an invisible shell of air that grants you a +4 armor bonus. At 7th
level, and every four levels thereafter, this bonus increases by +2.

**At 13th Level:** This barrier causes incoming arrows, rays, and other ranged attacks requiring an
attack roll against you to have a 50% miss chance.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

At 7th level: The armor bonus +6.

At 11th level: The armor bonus +8.

At 15th level: The armor bonus +10.

At 19th level: The armor bonus +12.

**Gaseous Form (Su)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Standard:** You can assume [gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form) (as the spell). You can bring
other creatures with you in gaseous form, but each passenger costs an additional minute per minute
of travel.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Invisibility (Su)**

**At 9th Level:** Each time you activate this ability you can treat it as
[greater invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility-greater), though each round spent this way counts as 1
minute of your normal _invisibility_ duration.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level oracle

**Standard:** You can become invisible (as per the [invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility) spell).

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Lightning Breath (Su)**

**Standard:** You can breathe a 30-foot line of electricity. This line deals 1d4 points of
electricity damage per oracle level. A Reflex save halves this damage.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Spark Skin (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** The resistance becomes 20.

**At 17th Level:** You gain immunity to electricity.

**At 5th Level:** This resistance increases to 10.

**Passive:** You gain [resist](ref/umr/resistance) electricity 5.

**Thunderburst (Ex)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Standard:** You can create a blast of air accompanied by a loud peal of thunder. The blast has a
range of 100 feet and has a 20-foot radius. Creatures in the area take 1d6 points of bludgeoning
damage per oracle level and are [deafened](ref/misc/deafened) for 1 hour, with a Fortitude save
resulting in half damage and no deafness.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 oracle levels beyond 7th

At 11th level: The radius 25 feet.

At 15th level: The radius 30 feet.

At 19th level: The radius 35 feet.

**Touch of Electricity (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon that you wield is treated as a [shock](ref/magic-enh/shock) weapon.

**Standard:** You can perform a melee touch attack that deals electricity damage equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your oracle level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Vortex Spells (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever you score a critical hit against an opponent with an attack spell, the target
is [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for 1 round.

**At 11th Level:** The duration increases to 1d4 rounds.

**Wind Sight (Ex)**

**At 7th Level:** As a **standard action**, you can see and hear into any area (as if using
[clairaudience and clairvoyance](ref/spell/clairaudience-clairvoyance)) within range as long as
there is an unobstructed path for air to travel between you and the target area (this does not
require line of effect, meaning the path can turn corners and go through spaces no smaller than 1
inch in diameter).

You can use this ability a number of rounds per day equal to your oracle level, but these rounds do
not need to be consecutive.

**Passive:** You ignore penalties on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks based on wind and the
first 100 feet of distance.

**Wings of Air (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** Your speed increases to 90 feet and your maneuverability increases to perfect.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Swift:** You can manifest a pair of translucent, cloud-like wings that grant you a fly speed of 60
feet with good maneuverability.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You can apply any one of the following feats to any air or electricity spell without
increasing the level or casting time: [Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell),
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell), [Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), or
[Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell).

**Flavor:** You become a master of air and electricity.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
