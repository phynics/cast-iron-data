### Revelations

An oracle with the Ancestor mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Ancestral Weapon (Su)**

**Ability:** You can summon a simple or martial weapon from your family's history that is
appropriate for your current size. You are considered proficient with this weapon.

**At 11th Level:** The weapon gains the [ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) weapon
property.

**At 3rd Level:** The weapon is considered [masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons).

**At 7th Level:** The weapon gains a +1 enhancement bonus.

**Special:** The weapon disappears after 1 round if it leaves your grasp.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

At 15th level: The enhancement bonus +2.

At 19th level: The enhancement bonus +3.

**Blood of Heroes (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can call upon your ancestors to grant you extra bravery in battle.

**Move:** You gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, and Will saves against fear for
a number of rounds equal to your Charisma bonus.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

At 7th level: This bonus +2.

At 14th level: This bonus +3.

**Phantom Touch (Su)**

**Standard:** You can perform a melee touch attack that causes a living creature to become
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken). This ability lasts for a number of rounds equal to
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level (minimum 1 round).

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Sacred Council (Su)**

**Move:** You can call upon your ancestors to provide council. This advice grants you a +2 bonus on
any one d20 roll. This effect lasts for 1 round.

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Spirit of the Warrior (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution, and a +4
natural armor bonus to AC. Your base attack bonus while possessed equals your oracle level (which
may give you additional attacks), and you gain the [Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical)
feat with a weapon of your choice.

**Flavor:** You can summon the spirit of a great warrior ancestor and allow it to possess you,
becoming a mighty warrior yourself.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 round/day per 2 oracle levels; these rounds need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-round increments

**Spirit Shield (Su)**

**Ability:** You can call upon the spirits of your ancestors to form a shield around you that blocks
incoming attacks and grants you a +4 armor bonus.

**At 13th Level:** This shield causes arrows, rays, and other ranged attacks requiring an attack
roll against you to have a 50% miss chance.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

At 7th level: This bonus +6.

At 11th level: This bonus +8.

At 15th level: This bonus +10.

At 19th level: This bonus +12.

**Spirit Walk (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can become incorporeal and invisible. While in this form, you can
move in any direction and pass through solid objects. You can take no action other than to move
while in this form. You remain in this form for a number of rounds equal to your oracle level, but
you can end this effect prematurely as a **standard action**.

**At 15th Level:** You can use this ability twice a day.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Storm of Souls (Su)**

**Ability:** You can summon the spirits of your ancestors to attack in a ghostly barrage - their
fury creates physical wounds on creatures in the area. The _storm_ has a range of 100 feet and is a
20-foot-radius [burst](ref/misc/burst). Objects and creatures in the area take 3d8 hit points of
damage. Undead creatures in the area take 1d8 points of damage for every oracle level you possess. A
successful Fortitude save reduces the damage to half.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 oracle levels beyond 7th

At 8th level: Objects and non-undead creatures now take 4d6 damage.

At 10th level: Objects and non-undead creatures now take 5d6 damage.

At 12th level: Objects and non-undead creatures now take 6d6 damage.

At 14th level: Objects and non-undead creatures now take 7d6 damage.

At 16th level: Objects and non-undead creatures now take 8d6 damage.

At 18th level: Objects and non-undead creatures now take 9d6 damage.

At 20th level: Objects and non-undead creatures now take 10d6 damage.

**Voice of the Grave (Su)**

**Ability:** You can [speak with dead](ref/spell/speak-with-dead), as per the spell.

**At 5th Level:** The dead creature you question takes -2 penalty on its Will save to resist this
effect.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per oracle level

At 10th level: The penalty -4.

At 15th level: The penalty -6.

At 20th level: The penalty -8.

**Wisdom of the Ancestors (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can enter a trance in which you commune with the spirits of your
ancestors. This trance lasts for 10 minutes, which must be uninterrupted and during which you can
take no other actions. When you come out of this trance, you have gained mystical insight into the
future.

**At 1st Level:** This insight acts as an [augury](ref/spell/augury) spell with 80% effectiveness.

**At 5th Level:** The insight takes the form of a [divination](ref/spell/divination) with 90%
effectiveness.

**At 8th Level:** The knowledge you gain is equivalent to a [commune](ref/spell/commune) spell.

**Special:** None of these spell effects require material components.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You can cast _[astral projection](ref/spell/astral-projection)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) once per day without requiring material
components.

**Flavor:** You become one with the spirits of your ancestors.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain a bonus on Will saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier,
[blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) out to a range of 60 feet, and a +4 bonus on your caster level for
all divination spells.
