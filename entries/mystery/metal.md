### Revelations

An oracle with the Metal mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Armor Mastery (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can move at your normal speed in medium armor that is made of metal. This does not
grant proficiency in armor.

**Flavor:** You become more maneuverable while wearing armor.

**At 5th Level:** Whenever you are wearing metal armor, you reduce the armor check penalty by 1 (to
a minimum of 0) and increase the maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by your armor by 1.

At 10th level: These bonuses 2.

At 15th level: These bonuses 3.

**Dance of the Blades (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** As a **move action**, you can maneuver your weapon to create a shield of whirling
steel around yourself until the start of your next turn; non-incorporeal melee and ranged attacks
against you have a 20% miss chance while the shield is active. You must be wielding a metal weapon
to use this ability.

**At 7th Level:** You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls with a metal weapon in any round in which you
move at least 10 feet.

**Passive:** Your base speed increases by 10 feet.

At 11th level: The bonus on attack rolls +2.

At 15th level: The bonus on attack rolls +3.

At 19th level: The bonus on attack rolls +4.

**Iron Constitution (Su)**

**Passive:** You gain a bonus on Fortitude saves equal to 1 +
[one-seventh](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level.

**Iron Skin (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, your skin hardens and takes on the appearance of iron, granting you DR
10/adamantine. This ability functions as [stoneskin](ref/spell/stoneskin), using your oracle level
as the caster level, except it only affects you.

**At 15th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Iron Weapon (Su)**

**Ability:** You can create a melee simple or martial weapon that lasts for 1 minute for every
oracle level you possess. This weapon is appropriate for your size and entirely made of metal (even
if it would normally include non-metal parts, such as a spear's shaft) but functions as if it were a
normal weapon of its type. You are considered proficient with this weapon. The weapon disappears
after 1 round if it leaves your grasp.

**At 11th Level:** The blade is made of adamantine.

**At 3rd Level:** The blade is made of cold iron.

**At 7th Level:** The blade gains a +1 enhancement bonus.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

At 11th level: The enhancement bonus +2.

At 15th level: The enhancement bonus +3.

At 19th level: The enhancement bonus +4.

**Riddle of Steel (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you may spend 10 minutes meditating on the structure of a piece of
unworked metal or ore to gain a +5 insight bonus on your next [Craft](ref/skill/craft-any) check to
make something using that metal.

**Flavor:** Your sacred bond with metal gives you insight into its form and function.

**Rusting Grasp (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Standard:** You can perform a melee touch attack that rusts iron, as the
[rusting grasp](ref/spell/rusting-grasp) spell.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 oracle levels beyond 7th

**Skill at Arms (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain proficiency in all martial weapons and heavy armor.

**Steel Scarf (Su)**

**Swift:** You can harden a scarf, sleeve, cloak, or other piece of your clothing into something as
hard as steel that stretches out to be up to 30 feet long. You can then strike outward with it as if
it were a weapon making a melee attack. For the purpose of this ability, you are proficient with
this weapon. You can use the weapon to perform combat maneuvers.

Make a melee attack roll against a creature within 30 feet; you may use
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse) with this attack. If you hit, the weapon deals slashing
damage equal to 1d8 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level. After this
attack, the clothing returns to its normal length and [hardness](ref/umr/hardness).

You do not threaten an area with this weapon and cannot use it to make attacks of opportunity.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Vision in Iron (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can use any piece of polished metal at least the size of a dagger as a scrying
device, as if using the spell [scrying](ref/spell/scrying).

**At 15th Level:** This functions like [greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater).

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 time/day per oracle level

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You gain the benefits of [Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus),
[Greater Weapon Focus](ref/feat/greater-weapon-focus), and
[Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical) with any one metal weapon that you are proficient
with.

**Flavor:** You become a master of iron and steel.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Your armor is like a second skin to you - while wearing metal armor you are proficient
with, the armor's maximum Dexterity bonus increases by +5 and you take no armor check penalty.

**Special:** In addition, any metal you create with your magic (such as
_[wall of iron](ref/spell/wall-of-iron)_) has its [hardness](ref/umr/hardness) increased by +10.
