**Sources:** Pathfinder \#139: The Dead Road pg 66

Oracles with the reaper mystery strive to quiet restless souls, end lives, and send souls to their
final reward.

**Deities**

**Details:** [Achaekek](ref/faith/achaekek), [Groetus](ref/faith/groetus),
[Pharasma](ref/faith/pharasma)

**Gained class skills:** intm, per, stl, sur

**Bonus Spells**

**Spells:** Slay living

**Spells:** Undeath to death

**Spells:** Destruction

**Spells:** Horrid wilting

**Spells:** Wail of the banshee

**Spells:** Chill touch

**Spells:** Calm spirit

**Spells:** Sands of time

**Spells:** Purge spirit

### Revelations

An oracle with the Reaper mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Death's Embrace (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [Improved Grapple](ref/feat/improved-grapple) as a bonus feat, even if you do
not meet its prerequisites.

**Ability2:** Once per round when you succeed at a [grapple](ref/rule/grapple) combat maneuver
check, you can deal 1d6 points of negative energy damage to the target of your grapple.

At 5th level: This damage 2d6.

At 10th level: This damage 3d6.

At 15th level: This damage 4d6.

At 20th level: This damage 5d6.

**Haunt Channeler (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain the [medium's](ref/class/medium) _haunt channeler_ class feature with an
effective medium level equal to your oracle level - 2.

**Prereq:** 5th-level oracle

**Haunt Channeler (Su)**

**Ability:** Your touch funnels a portion of the haunt into yourself, dealing 1d6 points of damage
to the haunt per 2 medium levels you possess as if via positive energy and allowing you to ask the
haunt a single question. The haunt can answer the question however it chooses.

**Ability2:** Additionally, you can forgo your saving throw against a haunt's effects to funnel the
haunt entirely into yourself. If you do so, you suffer the haunt's full effect, but the haunt
doesn't affect anyone else (even if it normally would). The haunt then gains control of your body
for 24 hours. Once per minute, you can attempt to end this possession early by succeeding at a Will
save (DC = 20 + 1/2 your medium level).

**Flavor:** Text from the medium class ability.

**Passive:** You can always act on the surprise round against a haunt.

**Moral Crisis (Su)**

**Special:** The target can attempt a new saving throw at the end of each of its turns to end this
effect. This is a mind-affecting emotion effect.

**Standard:** You can target a living creature within 30 feet of you, reminding it of its moral
failings. The target must succeed at a Will save or be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered) for a number
of rounds equal to your oracle level.

**Obliterate Memory (Sp)**

**At 15th Level:** You may use this ability twice per day.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Standard:** Once per day, you can target a creature within 30 feet and eliminate all memory of an
event the target experienced, as [modify memory](ref/spell/modify-memory). A target can attempt a
Will save to negate this effect.

In addition, you can cause an account of the memory erased by this ability to appear on a blank page
of any book you are carrying.

**Pale Horse (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can summon a [phantom steed](ref/spell/phantom-steed), as the spell.

**Prereq:** 5th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

**Return to Dust (Su)**

**Ability:** You call upon the inexorable ravages of time and entropy to damage an object or undead
creature within 30 feet, dealing 1d6 points of damage per 2 oracle levels you have, ignoring
[hardness](ref/umr/hardness) and [damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**Special:** If used against an undead creature or an object in another creature's possession, the
undead creature or the creature possessing the object can attempt a Reflex saving throw to halve the
damage

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Spectral Spells (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [Ectoplasmic Spell](ref/feat/ectoplasmic-spell) as a bonus feat.

**Standard:** You can cast a spell with the Ectoplasmic Spell metamagic feat without increasing the
level of the spell.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 3 oracle levels beyond 4th

**Spirit Touch (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** You can spend two uses of this ability to grant it the ghost touch and
[undead bane](ref/magic-enh/bane) special abilities.

**Standard:** You can touch one weapon to grant it the
[ghost touch](ref/magic-enh/ghost-touch-weapon) special ability for a number of rounds equal to your
Charisma modifier.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Terminal Aura (Su)**

**Passive:** You gain an aura with a 10-foot radius; any creature within that has fewer than 0 hit
points but is stable must succeed at a Will save or be affected by the spell
[bleed](ref/spell/bleed). A dying creature within range of this aura does not receive a Constitution
check to stabilize unless it succeeds at a Will save, but it can still be healed as normal.

A creature with [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) or [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) must
also succeed at a Will save every round or that ability does not function for 1 round.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Special:** You can suppress and resume this aura as a **free action**.

**True Death (Su)**

**Ability:** Those slain by your spells or by one of your attacks that deals negative energy becomes
more difficult to bring back from the dead. Spellcasters attempting to bring such a creature back
from the dead using [raise dead](ref/spell/raise-dead) or similar magic must succeed at a caster
level check with a DC equal to 15 + your oracle level or the spell fails and the material component
is wasted.

**Special:** A successful casting of [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) (DC = 10 + your oracle
level) before attempting to bring the creature back from the dead suppresses this effect for 1
minute.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** In addition, you can notice, locate, and distinguish between living and undead
creatures within 60 feet, just as if you had the [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) ability. This
sense does not allow you to detect objects, but it does allow you to notice living things that are
not creatures.

**Flavor:** You are a true reaper of death and no soul can escape your sight.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You automatically confirm critical hits and creatures automatically fail Fortitude
saves against your [coup de grace](ref/misc/helpless) attempts.
