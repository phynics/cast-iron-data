**Sources:** Path of the Hellknight pg 26

Many Godclaw signifers are oracles who embrace the unique Godclaw mystery. Oracles with the Godclaw
mystery often take the [clouded vision](ref/oraclecurse/clouded-vision) or
[legalistic](ref/oraclecurse/legalistic) curses.

**Deities**

**Details:** [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar), [Asmodeus](ref/faith/asmodeus),
[Iomedae](ref/faith/iomedae), [Irori](ref/faith/irori), [Torag](ref/faith/torag).

**Gained class skills:** bluff, intm, kl, per

**Bonus Spells**

**Spells:** Break enchantment

**Spells:** Forceful hand

**Spells:** Dictum

**Spells:** Shield of law

**Spells:** Imprisonment

**Spells:** Cause fear

**Spells:** Daze monster

**Spells:** Hold person

**Spells:** Order's wrath

### Revelations

An oracle with the Godclaw mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Abadar's Boon (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can spend 1 minute meditating on and communing with a masterwork object of any
type. At the end of this time, you can speak with that item. This functions as per
[stone tell](ref/spell/stone-tell), except that it works with masterwork goods, and the item cannot
tell you what it covers or conceals if that question is not applicable to it.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Asmodeus's Boon (Sp)**

**Ability:** Whenever a creature fails a saving throw and takes damage from one of your spells, it
is [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for a number of rounds equal to the spell's level. Spells that do not
allow saves do not cause creatures to become shaken. This does not stack with other fear effects.

**Armored Mind (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** The bonus on Will saving throws increases to +4.

**At 7th Level:** Once per day, you can reroll a Will saving throw against a mind-affecting effect
and choose the more favorable result.

**Passive:** While you are wearing [Hellknight plate](ref/eq-armor/hellknight-plate) or a
[signifer mask](ref/eq-misc/signifer-mask), you gain a +2 bonus on Will saving throws to resist
mind-affecting effects.

**Might of the Godclaw (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [Deific Obedience](ref/feat/deific-obedience) as a bonus feat, even if you
don't meet its prerequisites. When you gain the feat, choose one Godclaw deity (one of the five
deities listed above); you can perform that deity's daily obedience to gain its benefits.

Whenever you would gain one of your deity's boons, you can instead gain the benefits of another
Godclaw deity's obedience. You can choose to gain the benefits of a maximum of four Godclaw deities'
obediences if you choose to forgo all three of your god's boons. You never need to spend more than 1
hour per day to perform your obedience.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level oracle

**Instant Armor (Su)**

**Ability:** Choose one type of heavy armor. You gain proficiency in that armor.

**At 11th Level:** You can spend 1 minute placing a suit of your chosen armor into an
extradimensional space. You can use this ability to immediately don or remove that armor, calling it
from or removing it to that extradimensional space. While in the extradimensional space, the armor
regains 1 hit point per hour when damaged.

**At 3rd Level:** Twice per day as an **immediate action**, you can instantly don or remove a suit
of your chosen armor that you touch, causing it to teleport onto or off of your body.

**Iomedae's Boon (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can call upon your deities to grant you courage.

**At 14th Level:** This bonus increases to +3.

**At 7th Level:** This bonus increases to +2.

**Move:** You gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, and Will saving throws against
fear effects for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma bonus.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Iron Order (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can issue an order as per [suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion). Any
creature of chaotic alignment has difficulty defying your command, taking a -4 penalty on its saving
throw to resist it.

**At 15th Level:** Your command function as per [mass suggestion](ref/spell/suggestion-mass). If you
are wearing Hellknight plate or a signifer mask, your command's target takes an additional -2
penalty on its saving throw to resist the order (regardless of the target's alignment; this stacks
with the penalty chaotic creatures take).

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Irori's Boon (Su)**

**Immediate:** Once per day, whenever you fail a saving throw that causes you to become blinded,
deafened, frightened, panicked, paralyzed, shaken, or stunned, you can attempt that saving throw
again with a +4 insight bonus on the roll. You must take the second result, even if it is worse.

**At 15th Level:** You can do this three times a day.

**At 7th Level:** You can use this ability twice a day.

**Resiliency (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, when you are brought below 0 hit points but not killed, you can act as if
you are [disabled](ref/misc/disabled) for 1 round. At the end of your next turn, unless you are
brought to above 0 hit points, you immediately fall unconscious and begin [dying](ref/misc/dying).

**At 15th Level:** Three times per day, if you are disabled and take a standard action (or any
otherwise strenuous action), you avoid taking 1 point of damage if you succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude
saving throw.

**At 7th Level:** You gain [Diehard](ref/feat/diehard) as a bonus feat, even if you do not meet the
prerequisites.

**Torag's Boon (Su)**

**At 19th Level:** The shield also grants you DR 2/chaos.

**Standard:** You can call upon Torag's protection to form a shield around you that blocks incoming
attacks. The shield grants a +4 deflection bonus to your Armor Class.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per 2 oracle levels; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be
spent in 1-minute increments

At 7th level: This bonus +5.

At 11th level: This bonus +6.

At 15th level: This bonus +7.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You can cast [detect chaos](ref/spell/detect-chaos),
[detect law](ref/spell/detect-law), and [discern lies](ref/spell/discern-lies) at will as
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), using your oracle level as your caster level.

**Ability2:** Once per day, you can cast [crushing hand](ref/spell/crushing-hand) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) without requiring a focus component, treating
your oracle level as your caster level. This hand manifests as a massive, spiked gauntlet.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You no longer take armor check penalties for wearing armor, and your armor's maximum
Dexterity bonus increases by 5.
