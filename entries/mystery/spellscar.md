### Revelations

An oracle with the Spellscar mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Animate Primal Forces (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** You can summon an elemental twice per day.

**At 7th Level:** You can summon a Medium elemental.

**At 9th Level:** You can summon a Large elemental.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level oracle

**Standard:** Once per day, you can summon a single Small air, earth, fire, or water
[elemental](ref/family/elemental) to serve you. It remains for a number of rounds equal to your
Charisma modifier.

**Eldritch Bolt (Su)**

**Ability:** You make a ranged touch attack against any foe within 30 feet. On a hit, you deal force
damage equal to 1d8 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level.

**Flavor:** You can damage foes with a bolt of raw magical energy.

**At 10th Level:** The bolt's range increases to 60 feet.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Eldritch Resistance (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** The resistance becomes 10.

**At 17th Level:** The resistance increases to 20.

**At 5th Level:** This resistance increases to 5.

**Passive:** You gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 2 to acid, cold, electricity, fire, and
sonic.

**Eldritch Scar (Su)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Special:** A creature can be marked by only one _eldritch scar_ at a time (a more powerful one
replaces a weaker one). An _eldritch scar_ fades automatically after 24 hours. It can be removed
before that point via any effect capable of removing a [curse](ref/rule/curses) (the curse's level
equals your caster level), but if an attempt to remove an _eldritch scar_ fails, that attempt
triggers a primal magic event - an event triggered this way does not cause the _eldritch scar_ to
fade.

**Swift:** Once per day when you damage a creature with a spell you cast, you may cause your magic
to leave an _eldritch scar_ upon that creature. An _eldritch scar_ appears as a normal scar, save
that it flickers with faint radiance of a color of your choice.

The next time that creature casts a spell, uses a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities), or activates a magic item, it triggers a
[primal magic event](ref/rule/primal-magic) of a CR equal to your caster level. This causes the
_eldritch scar_ to vanish.

**Magic Penetration (Ex)**

**At 9th Level:** The bonus increases to +4.

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on any caster level check made to dispel or remove a magic effect
(such as when using the _[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_ or
[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse) spell).

**Mystic Null (Ex)**

**At 11th Level:** The bonus increases to +4.

**At 7th Level:** This bonus also applies on saves against supernatural abilities.

**Passive:** You gain a +2 insight bonus on saves against spells and
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Primal Manipulation (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage, you may
change it to deal one of the other listed damage types.

**At 15th Level:** You can change a spell to deal sonic damage, though the damage is halved.

**At 20th Level:** You can change a spell to deal force damage, though the damage is halved.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Primal Mastery (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a spell you cast triggers a primal magic event, or whenever you use a
[rod of wonder](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-wonder), roll d% twice when generating the result. You may pick
which of the two results actually occurs.

**Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus on all saving throws made to resist primal magic events or magical
effects caused by a rod of wonder.

**Trigger Primal Magic Event (Su)**

**Immediate:** Once per day, you can cause a spellcaster (including yourself) within 30 feet to
trigger a primal magic event as the spell is being cast.

**At 13th Level:** You can cause any creature in the act of activating a magic item to trigger a
primal magic event.

**At 17th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**Prereq:** 9th-level oracle

**Special:** A spellcaster can make a [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) check (DC = 15 + twice
the spell's level) to focus the magic and avoid triggering a primal magic effect, but
non-spellcasters activating magic items have no such option.

**Spell Resistance (Ex)**

**Passive:** You gain SR equal to your oracle level + 5.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell, you can choose to trigger a primal magic event in addition
to casting the spell itself - you can use this ability once per minute.

**Ability2:** If your spellcasting causes a primal magic event to occur, your spell effect is not
replaced by the primal magic event - it takes place normally, along with the event itself.

**Flavor:** You become a master of primal magic.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
