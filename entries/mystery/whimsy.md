**Sources:** Legacy of the First World pg 10

Whimsy oracles gain powers from capricious fey tricksters.

**Deities**

**Details:** [The Green Mother](ref/faith/the-green-mother),
[The Lantern King](ref/faith/the-lantern-king), [Ng](ref/faith/ng), [Shyka](ref/faith/shyka).

### Revelations

An oracle with the Whimsy mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Assumed Form (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can change your appearance at will, as [disguise self](ref/spell/disguise-self)
with a caster level equal to your oracle level.

**At 11th Level:** This ability lasts until you dismiss it or use it again, allowing you to keep it
active while you sleep.

**At 7th Level:** You can instead physically transform, as [alter self](ref/spell/alter-self).

**Capricious Misdirection (Sp)**

**Ability:** You are constantly under the effects of a [misdirection](ref/spell/misdirection) spell,
except that you can take a **standard action** to select a creature or object within 60 feet to
serve as the target of detection spells used against you until you select a different creature or
object.

**Flavor:** Your ever-changing nature allows you to misdirect aura-reading divinations with ease.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Feywise (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Your whimsy grants you protection from fey mischief.

**Passive:** You gain the [resist nature's lure](ref/ability/resist-natures-lure) druid class
feature. In addition, you gain a +2 bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception),
[Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive), and [Survival](ref/skill/survival) checks against fey.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level oracle

**Flicker (Sp)**

**Flavor:** You can flicker from sight for a short time, allowing you to escape or set up a prank.

**Swift:** You can disappear for 1 round per oracle level. This ability functions as
[invisibility](ref/spell/invisibility).

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 2 oracle levels

**Misdirection Mastery (Su)**

**Ability:** You receive [Misdirection Tactics](ref/feat/misdirection-tactics) as a bonus feat.

**Flavor:** You gleefully evade attacks and confound foes with your capricious misdirection.

**At 10th Level:** You receive [Misdirection Redirection](ref/feat/misdirection-redirection) as a
bonus feat.

**At 15th Level:** You receive [Misdirection Attack](ref/feat/misdirection-attack) as a bonus feat.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level oracle

**Special:** You do not need to meet the prerequisites to receive these feats.

**Pure Whimsy (Su)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Special:** The DC of any required saving throw is 10 + half your oracle level + your Charisma
modifier, and no effect from this ability persists for more than 1 day.

**Standard:** Once per day, you can release a stream of errant chaos from the First World. When you
do so, identify a target within 90 feet and generate a random effect as from a
[rod of wonder](ref/magic-rod/rod-of-wonder) (or, at the GM's discretion, another random or unusual
effect of similar power).

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 oracle levels beyond 7th

**Versatile Comedy (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can use your total [Perform](ref/skill/perform-any) (comedy) bonus in place of your
[Bluff](ref/skill/bluff) and [Intimidate](ref/skill/intimidate) bonuses.

**Flavor:** The powers of whimsy infuse your comedy with the ability to awe and befuddle.

**Whimsical Prank (Su)**

**Special:** No save is allowed against this attempt, but a creature affected by this revelation
cannot be the target of this revelation again for 1 day.

**Standard:** You can call upon fey to play a prank on a creature within 30 feet as a
[dirty trick](ref/rule/dirty-trick) combat maneuver. Resolve this attempt as normal, except that it
doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity, you use your oracle level in place of your Combat Maneuver
Bonus, and you add your Charisma modifier in place of your Strength or Dexterity modifier.

**Whimsical Step (Su)**

**Move:** You can teleport up to 10 feet per oracle level.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 4 oracle levels beyond 7th

**Woodland Caprice (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [woodland stride](ref/ability/woodland-stride), as per the druid ability.

**Flavor:** Your fey powers allow you to move easily through woodland environments.

**At 7th Level:** You also gain the [trackless step](ref/ability/trackless-step) druid class feature
and can similarly conceal the tracks of any allies within 30 feet that you choose.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Flavor:** You become a fey trickster in the flesh.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Your type changes to [fey](ref/type/fey) and you gain
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), immunity to poison, and DR 10/cold iron.
