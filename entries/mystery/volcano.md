### Revelations

An oracle with the Volcano mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Ash Cloud (Su)**

**At 7th Level:** Your _ash cloud_ fills with embers. All creatures in the cloud except you take 1d6
points of fire damage per round and must succeed at Fortitude saves to avoid choking, as though they
were breathing in [heavy smoke](ref/rule/smoke-effects).

**Standard:** You can surround yourself with a 10-foot-radius cloud of ash, which functions as an
[obscuring mist](ref/spell/obscuring-mist) spell, except you can see through it clearly. The cloud
is stationary, and remains for 1 round per oracle level. Using this ability again causes your
previous cloud to fall to the ground.

**Breath of Creation (Ex)**

**Ability:** When you take [Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) from
[volcanic gases](ref/rule/volcanoes), you gain a bonus to your Charisma equal to the damage taken.
This bonus lasts for 1 hour or until the ability damage is healed. The maximum bonus to your
Charisma is +2.

**Flavor:** Volcanic vapors elevate your mind even as they ravage your body.

At 10th level: The max bonus +4.

At 15th level: The max bonus +6.

**Burning Magic (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever a creature fails a saving throw against one of your spells that deals fire
damage, that creature catches on fire, taking 1 point of fire damage per spell level at the
beginning of its turn. The fire lasts for 1d4 rounds, but it can be extinguished as a **move
action** if the creature succeeds at a Reflex save (using the original spell's DC). Dousing the
creature with water as a **standard action** grants a +2 bonus on this save, while immersing the
creature in water extinguishes the fire.

**Special:** Spells that don't grant a saving throw don't cause a creature to catch on fire.

**Cleansing Flames (Su)**

**At 15th Level:** You can do this three times per day.

**At 7th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**Swift:** Once per day, you can take 1d4 points of damage per oracle level to gain a new saving
throw against a single ongoing effect. The effect must be one for which you already failed a saving
throw. If you succeed, the effect is removed.

**Erupt (Su)**

**Swift:** You cause flaming shards of rock to erupt from your body. These shards deal 1d6 points of
damage per 2 oracle levels (minimum 1d6) to all creatures in a 10-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) (Reflex half). Half of this damage is fire damage and the other half is
piercing damage. The shards create [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles) for 1 round.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Fiery Conduit (Su)**

**Ability:** You can deliver touch spells to burning creatures within 30 feet without actually
touching them. For the purposes of this ability, burning creatures include creatures that have
caught on fire, creatures taking ongoing fire damage, and creatures with the fire subtype or that
are polymorphed into such creatures. You must succeed at a ranged touch attack to affect an
unwilling target.

**Lava Walk (Su)**

**Ability:** You can walk across lava, magma, and similarly heated stone at your normal movement
speed. You are immune to any fire damage caused by the surface while you walk above it, even if it
radiates damage at a distance.

While using _lava walk,_ you never slip or fall from poor or slippery footing.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Magma Form (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** You can assume the form a Large magma elemental, as
[elemental body III](ref/spell/elemental-body-3).

**At 13th Level:** You can assume the form of a Huge magma elemental, as
[elemental body IV](ref/spell/elemental-body-4).

**At 9th Level:** You can assume the form of a Medium magma elemental, as
[elemental body II](ref/spell/elemental-body-2).

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Special:** The duration of this form is one hour per oracle level.

**Standard:** Once per day, you can assume the form of a
[Small magma elemental](ref/monster/magma-elemental), as
[elemental body I](ref/spell/elemental-body-1). You gain the same abilities as if you had chosen an
earth elemental's form, except your _[earth glide](ref/spell/earth-glide)_ functions as the
_[earth glide](ref/spell/earth-glide)_ ability of a magma elemental.

**Pyroclastic Shove (Su)**

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Standard:** You conjure a cascade of volcanic ash to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) a creature
within 30 feet of you. The CMB of this cascade is equal to your base attack bonus + your Charisma
modifier + 4 from the cascade's Gargantuan size. If the bull rush succeeds, the creature takes 1d6
points of fire damage per oracle level.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels beyond 7th

**Touch of Flame (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** Any weapon that you wield is treated as a [flaming](ref/magic-enh/flaming)
weapon.

**Standard:** You can perform a melee touch attack that deals 1d6 points of fire damage plus an
additional amount of points equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your oracle level.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** The oracle's body takes on the consistency of rock, and her touch carries the heat of a
volcano. She gains the fire and earth subtypes and becomes [vulnerable](ref/umr/vulnerabilities) to
cold.

Any fire damage she deals ignores the first 10 points of fire resistance, but not fire immunity.

The oracle gains a +4 bonus to natural armor. Any creature that strikes the oracle with a natural
weapon or unarmed strike, or that succeeds at a grapple combat maneuver check against her, takes 1d6
points of fire damage. This damage stacks with other sources of fire damage.

Her attacks with natural weapons, unarmed strikes, and metal-hafted weapons deal an additional 1d6
points of fire damage.

**Special:** You can suppress or reestablish this heat as a **standard action**.
