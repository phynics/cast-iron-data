Oracles who draw their power from the mysterious and primal might of dragons are usually solitary
folk. They are deep thinkers and often quick of wit, but prideful and equally quick-tempered.

**Deities**

**Details:** [Dahak](ref/faith/dahak), [General Susumu](ref/faith/general-susumu),
[Nalinivati](ref/faith/nalinivati), [Ragadahn](ref/faith/ragadahn), [Shizuru](ref/faith/shizuru).

**Gained class skills:** fly, intm, per, ka

**Special:** The oracle can take ranks in Fly even if she does not have a method of flying under her
own power.

**Bonus Spells**

**Spells:** [Spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance)

**Spells:** Antimagic field

**Spells:** True seeing

**Spells:** Form of the dragon III

**Spells:** Overwhelming presence

**Spells:** Cause fear

**Spells:** Resist energy

**Spells:** Fly

**Spells:** Fear

**Energy Type**

**Choice:** Upon selecting this mystery, the oracle must select an energy type (acid, cold,
electricity, or fire) to be her associated element, which impacts several revelations.

### Revelations

An oracle with the Dragon mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Breath Weapon (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain a [breath weapon](ref/umr/breath-weapon). This breath weapon deals 1d6 points
of damage of your energy type per 2 oracle levels you have (minimum 1d6; Reflex half). The shape of
the breath weapon is either a 30-foot cone or a 60-foot line, selected when choosing this
revelation.

**Flavor:** The primal power of dragonkind seethes within you.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Draconic Resistance (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Like the great dragons, you are not easily harmed by common means of attack.

**At 15th Level:** Your energy resistance increases to 20 and your natural armor bonus increases to
+4

**At 9th Level:** Your energy resistance increases to 10 and your natural armor bonus increases to
+2.

**Passive:** You gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 against your chosen energy type and a +1
natural armor bonus.

**Dragon Magic (Sp)**

**Ability:** Select one spell from the sorcerer/wizard spell list that is 2 levels lower than the
highest-level spell you can cast, or two spells that are both at least 3 levels lower. You can cast
each of the selected spells once per day as a [spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**Flavor:** Your draconic power grants you a limited form of access to arcane magic.

**At 11th Level:** You can cast each selected spell twice per day.

**Dragon Senses (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain either [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet or
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision).

**Flavor:** Your senses take on a keen draconic edge.

**At 11th Level:** You gain [blindsense](ref/umr/blindsense) with a range of 30 feet. If you already
have [blindsense](ref/rule/blindsight-and-blindsense), you instead increase its range by 30 feet.

**At 15th Level:** You gain either the [scent](ref/umr/scent) ability or a +4 bonus on
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) checks.

**At 5th Level:** You can select [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) with a range of 60 feet or
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision) if you do not yet have both, or you can add 60 feet to
the range of your darkvision.

**Form of the Dragon (Su)**

**Flavor:** Your kinship with dragonkind allows you to take on the form of a dragon.

**At 15th Level:** You can assume the form of a Large dragon, as per
[form of the dragon II](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-2). If you choose to have this ability function
as per _[form of the dragon I](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-1),_ the duration is instead 1 hour per
oracle level.

**At 19th Level:** You can assume the form of a Huge dragon, as per
_[form of the dragon III](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-3)._

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Special:** Rather than _form of the dragon_ spells, you can choose for this revelation to act as
[form of the alien dragon](ref/spell/form-of-the-alien-dragon-1) or
[form of the exotic dragon](ref/spell/form-of-the-exotic-dragon-1). This choice must be made when
you first gain this revelation, and cannot be changed.

**Standard:** You can assume the form of a Medium dragon, as per
[form of the dragon I](ref/spell/form-of-the-dragon-1). You can use this ability once per day, but
the duration is 10 minutes per oracle level.

**Presence of Dragons (Su)**

**Flavor:** Those who would oppose you must overcome their fear of dragons or be struck with terror
at your draconic majesty.

**Special:** This is a mind-affecting fear effect.

**Swift:** You can manifest an aura of draconic might around yourself. Enemies within 30 feet who
can see you when you activate this ability must attempt a Will save. Success means that the creature
is immune to this ability for the following 24 hours. On a failed save, the opponent is
[shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for 2d6 rounds.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Scaled Toughness (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can manifest the scaly toughness of dragonkind.

**At 13th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Swift:** Once per day, you can harden your skin, giving it a scaly appearance and granting you DR
10/magic. During this time, you are also immune to paralysis and sleep effects. This effect lasts
for a number of rounds equal to your oracle level.

**Tail Swipe (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can grow a scaly tail. This tail can be used only to make attacks of opportunity,
but it allows you to make one additional attack of opportunity each round. This tail attack deals an
amount of bludgeoning damage equal to 1d8 (1d6 if you are Small) + your Strength modifier.

**Flavor:** You express your wrath through sweeps of a wicked tail.

**At 10th Level:** You can attempt a free [trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver check against any
creature damaged by your tail attack. This does not provoke an attack of opportunity.

**Talons of the Dragon (Su)**

**Flavor:** You fight with the fearsome talons of dragonkind.

**Free:** You can grow claws. These claws are treated as
[natural weapons](ref/rule/natural-attacks), allowing you to perform two claw attacks as a full
attack action using your full base attack bonus. Each of these attacks deals an amount of slashing
damage equal to 1d4 (1d3 if you are Small) + your Strength modifier.

**At 11th Level:** These claws deal an additional 1d6 points of damage of your chosen energy type on
a successful hit.

**At 5th Level:** These claws are considered magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming DR.

**At 7th Level:** The damage die increases [by one step](ref/misc/damage-die-adjustments), to deal
an amount of slashing damage equal to 1d6 (1d4 if you are Small) + your Strength modifier.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier rounds/day

**Wings of the Dragon (Su)**

**Flavor:** Like the great dragons, you can take to the skies and terrorize opponents from above.

**At 10th Level:** Your maneuverability increases to poor.

**At 11th Level:** You can use these wings for 10 minutes per day for each oracle level you have.

**At 15th Level:** You can use the wings indefinitely.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Swift:** You can manifest leathery dragon wings that grant you a fly speed of 60 feet (clumsy
maneuverability). You can use these wings for 1 minute per day for each oracle level you have. This
duration does not need to be consecutive, but it must be spent in 1-minute increments.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Flavor:** Your draconic destiny unfolds.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to paralysis, sleep, and damage of your energy type. You count as a
dragon for the purposes of spells and magical effects.

**Special:** If you have the
_[breath weapon](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/breath-weapon)_ revelation, you
can use your breath weapon an unlimited number of times per day, though no more often than once
every 1d4+1 rounds.
