**Sources:** Monster Codex pg 56

Among [fire giants](ref/monster/giant-fire-fire-giant-doombringer), the rare but blessed oracles of
the apocalypse are favored emissaries of the divine.

**Deities**

**Details:** [Gorum](ref/faith/gorum), [Rovagug](ref/faith/rovagug).

**Gained class skills:** bluff, dis, sur, stl

**Bonus Spells**

**Spells:** Insect plague

**Spells:** Circle of death

**Spells:** Vision

**Spells:** Incendiary cloud

**Spells:** Meteor swarm

**Spells:** Deathwatch

**Spells:** Summon swarm

**Spells:** Explosive runes

**Spells:** Ice storm

### Revelations

An oracle with the Apocalypse mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Defy Elements (Ex)**

**Ability:** Choose one energy type (acid, cold, fire, electricity, or sonic). You gain
[resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 5 to the selected energy type.

**Repeat:** You can choose an additional energy type for which to gain resistance 5, or you can
choose a previously chosen energy type and increase that resistance by 5., 0, 5

**Special:** You can only have a maximum resistance of 20 for any one energy type.

**Destructive Roots (Su)**

**Flavor:** You can root yourself to your current spot and cause the world around you to fall apart.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Special:** You immediately cease concentrating if you move from the square you started in when you
activated this ability. Difficult terrain created by this ability remains for 24 hours before
returning to its original state.

**Standard:** You can cause the ground in a 5-foot radius [burst](ref/misc/burst) centered on you to
crumble and rupture, becoming [difficult terrain](ref/rule/terrain-and-obstacles). As a **move
action** on subsequent rounds, you can continue to concentrate on this area as though concentrating
on a spell; doing so increases the radius of difficult terrain by 5 feet (maximum 30-foot-radius).

**Doomsayer (Su)**

**At 15th Level:** Continuing your _doomsaying_ each round is a **swift action** instead.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Special:** This ability cannot cause a creature to become frightened or panicked, even if the
target was already shaken from another effect. This is a mind-affecting fear effect that relies on
audible components.

**Standard:** You can utter a dire prophecy that strikes fear in your enemies and causes them to
become [shaken](ref/misc/shaken). To be affected, an enemy must be within 30 feet and able to hear
you. This effect persists as long as the enemy is within 30 feet and you use a **move action** each
round to continue your _doomsaying._

**Dust to Dust (Su)**

**At 10th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**At 11th Level:** This damage increases to 1d6 &times& your Charisma modifier (minimum 1d6).

**Standard:** Once per day, you can cause the weapons around you to shatter in their wielders'
hands. When you use this ability, attempt a single [sunder](ref/rule/sunder) combat maneuver, using
your caster level in place of your base attack bonus and your Charisma modifier in place of
Strength. Every creature except you in a 10-foot radius that is wielding a manufactured weapon is
targeted by the same attempt. You deal an amount of damage to each weapon affected equal to 1d4
&times& your Charisma modifier (minimum 1d4).

**Erosion Touch (Su)**

**Ability:** As a melee touch attack, you can deal 1d6 points of damage per level to objects or
constructs. If used against an object in another creature's possession, treat this attack as a
sunder combat maneuver.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 3 oracle levels

**Near Death (Su)**

**At 11th Level:** The bonus increases to +4.

**At 7th Level:** This bonus also applies on saves against death effects, sleep effects, and
stunning.

**Passive:** You gain a +2 insight bonus on saves against diseases, mind-affecting effects, and
poisons.

**Pass the Torch (Su)**

**Swift:** Once per day, you can channel the energy of the apocalypse into your body, causing you to
ignite. You take 1d4 points of fire damage when you activate this ability and again at the beginning
of your turn until you end the effect. Any creature that begins its turn adjacent to you takes 1d6
points of fire damage as the fire spreads, plus 1 additional point of fire damage for each previous
round you have had this ability active. For example, adjacent creatures take 1d6+5 points of damage
if you have had this ability active for 5 rounds. You can end this ability as a **free action**.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per 2 oracle levels

At 5th level: You can use this ability 2 times a day.

At 10th level: You can use this ability 3 times a day.

At 15th level: You can use this ability 4 times a day.

At 20th level: You can use this ability 5 times a day.

**Power of the Fallen (Sp)**

**Ability:** You can touch a dying creature and channel its life energy into another living
creature. This ability acts as [death knell](ref/spell/death-knell), except you can choose any ally
(including yourself) within 30 feet to gain the benefit of this effect, and you can apply the +2
enhancement bonus to either Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution.

**Prereq:** 5th-level oracle

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Spell Blast (Su)**

**Swift:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit against an opponent with a spell that requires an
attack roll, you can immediately attempt to [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) your opponent in any
direction. You don't provoke an attack of opportunity for this bull rush attempt.

**Unstoppable Overrun (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can attempt [overrun](ref/rule/overrun) combat maneuvers against opponents that are
up to two size categories larger than you.

**At 10th Level:** You gain [Greater Overrun](ref/feat/greater-overrun) as a bonus feat.

**At 5th Level:** You gain [Improved Overrun](ref/feat/improved-overrun) as a bonus feat.

**Special:** You don't need to meet the prerequisites to gain these feats.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** Anytime you successfully cast a spell or use an ability that bestows 1 or more
[negative levels](ref/rule/energy-drain-and-negative-levels), the effect bestows 1d4 additional
negative levels.

**Flavor:** You become a herald of the apocalypse and wield the awesome power to fulfill such
prophecy.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Swift:** Whenever you confirm a critical hit against a creature (including with a melee, ranged,
or spell attack), you can curse the target of your attack, as the
[bestow curse](ref/spell/bestow-curse) spell, except the target doesn't receive a Will saving throw
to negate the effects and [spell resistance](ref/rule/spell-resistance) does not apply against this
ability.
