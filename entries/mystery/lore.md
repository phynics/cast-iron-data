### Revelations

An oracle with the Lore mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Arcane Archivist (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast a spell from the wizard/sorcerer spell list as if it were on
your list of spells known. The spell consumes a spell slot one level higher than the level of the
spell.

**Flavor:** Your experience with lore-filled tomes has granted you the ability to cast arcane spells
as if they were on your spell list.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Special:** You must have a spellbook containing the spell to cast it in this way, and the spell is
erased when you complete the casting.

**Automatic Writing (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can spend a full hour in uninterrupted meditation. During this
period, your hands produce mysterious writing that pertains to the future.

**At 1st Level:** The prophetic writing manifests as an [augury](ref/spell/augury) spell with 90%
effectiveness.

**At 5th Level:** The writing takes the form of a [divination](ref/spell/divination) spell with 90%
effectiveness.

**At 8th Level:** The writing manifests as a casting of [commune](ref/spell/commune) with no
material component required.

**Brain Drain (Su)**

**Special:** The randomly stolen thoughts remain in your mind for a number of rounds equal to your
Charisma modifier. Treat the knowledge gained as if you had used
[detect thoughts](ref/spell/detect-thoughts). This is a mind-affecting effect.

**Standard:** You can violently probe the mind of a single intelligent enemy within 100 feet. The
target receives a Will save to negate the effect and immediately knows the source of this harmful
mental prying. Those who fail this save are wracked with pain, taking 1d4 points of damage for every
oracle level you possess.

After successfully attacking with this ability, you may use a **full-round action** to sort through
the jumble of stolen thoughts and memories to make a single [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all)
check using the victim's skill bonus.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Focused Trance (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can enter a deep meditation, blocking out visual and auditory stimuli and allowing
you to concentrate on a single problem, philosophical issue, or memory. This trance lasts 1d6
rounds, during which time you can only take move actions. During this period, you gain a bonus equal
to your level on all saves against sonic effects and gaze attacks. When you come out of your trance,
you may make a single Intelligence-based skill check with a +20 circumstance bonus.

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Lore Keeper (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Instead of encyclopedic knowledge, you learn most of your information through tales,
songs, and poems.

**Passive:** You may use your Charisma modifier instead of your Intelligence modifier on all
[Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) checks.

**Mental Acuity (Ex)**

**Flavor:** Your explorations into the secret mysteries of the world have granted you a
preternatural understanding of all things - and you just keep getting smarter.

**Passive:** You gain a +1 inherent bonus to Intelligence upon taking this revelation and another at
every third oracle level gained thereafter.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Sidestep Secret (Su)**

**Flavor:** Your innate understanding of the universe has granted you preternatural reflexes and the
uncanny ability to step out of danger at the very last second.

**Passive:** Add your Charisma modifier (instead of your Dexterity modifier) to your Armor Class and
all Reflex saving throws. Your armor's maximum Dexterity bonus applies to your Charisma instead of
your Dexterity.

**Spontaneous Symbology**

**Ability:** You can cast any &quot&symbol&quot& spell using a spell slot of the appropriate level,
even if the spell is not on your list of spells known. A symbol spell is any spell with the word
&quot&symbol&quot& in its name.

**Flavor:** Your knowledge of the secret history of the world has unveiled the mysterious symbols
that govern reality.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Think On It (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, the oracle of lore can re-attempt any previously failed Knowledge check.
On this attempt, add a +10 competence bonus on the check.

**Whirlwind Lesson (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can quickly browse through a magical tome or manual, gaining its benefits with only
a single 8-hour study session (rather than the usual 48 hours over a period of 6 days).

**At 15th Level:** You may share your _whirlwind lesson_ with a number of characters equal to your
oracle level. The benefits to you are permanent, whereas your students gain the tome or manual's
benefits for a number of days equal to your Charisma modifier but then forget what they have
learned.

**At 7th Level:** You may allow another character to join in your study; both of you gain the full
benefit of having read the book.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** You gain the ability to take 20 on all Knowledge skill checks.

**Ability2:** Your understanding of the fundamental underpinnings of reality has also become so
advanced that once per day, you can cast [wish](ref/spell/wish). This ability does not require a
material component, but the wish cannot be used to grant ability score bonuses, nor can it be used
to replicate spells with expensive material components.

**Gained:** At 20th Level
