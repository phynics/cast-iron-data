### Revelations

An oracle with the Shadow mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Army of Darkness (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell and summon a
creature that normally has the [celestial](ref/template/celestial) or
[fiendish](ref/template/fiendish) template, you can instead summon it with the
[shadow creature](ref/template/shadow) template.

**Special:** This revelation counts as having the [Spell Focus](ref/feat/spell-focus) (conjuration)
feat for the purpose of meeting the prerequisites of the
[Augment Summoning](ref/feat/augment-summoning) feat, as well as any feat that lists Augment
Summoning as a prerequisite.

**Cloak of Darkness (Su)**

**Ability:** You conjure a cloak of shadowy darkness that grants you a +4 armor bonus and a +2
circumstance bonus on [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks.

**Flavor:** This ability functions as the [dark tapestry](ref/mystery/dark-tapestry) mystery
revelation of the same name.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

**Dark Secrets (Su)**

**Ability:** You can add a number of spells from the sorcerer/wizard spell list equal to your
Charisma modifier (minimum 1, maximum equal to half your oracle level) to your spell list and your
list of spells known as divine spells. These have a spell level equal to their sorcerer/wizard spell
level.

**Flavor:** You learn the hidden secrets surrounding the casting of shadow spells.

**Special:** You can add only illusion spells from the shadow subschool or spells with the darkness
descriptor to your list of spells known in this manner.

Each time you gain an oracle level after taking this revelation, you can choose to replace one of
these spells for a new appropriate spell on the sorcerer/wizard spell list.

**Living Shadow (Sp)**

**Ability:** This ability functions as [gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form).

**Flavor:** Your body dissolves, and you become a living shadow.

**At 14th Level:** This ability functions as [shadow body](ref/spell/shadow-body) instead, except
you also gain DR 10/magic and become immune to poison, sneak attacks, and critical hits as if also
using _[gaseous form](ref/spell/gaseous-form)._

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Pierce the Shadows (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet. If you already have darkvision,
increase your existing darkvision by 60 feet instead.

**Flavor:** The shadows step aside from your baleful gaze, affording you sight in even the deepest
darkness.

**At 11th Level:** You can see perfectly in darkness of any kind, even in absolute darkness or the
darkness created by a _[deeper darkness](ref/spell/deeper-darkness)_ spell.

**Shadow Armament (Su)**

**Ability:** You can create a quasi-real simple or martial masterwork weapon appropriate for your
current size. You are considered proficient with this weapon.

The first time you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature can attempt a Will save to
disbelieve; failure means the weapon deals damage normally, while success means the creature takes
only 1 point of damage from the weapon's attacks. The weapon deals only 1 point of damage to
objects. At 3rd level, and again at 11th level and 19th level, the weapon gains a cumulative +1
enhancement bonus.

**At 15th Level:** The weapon deals minimum damage (as if it had rolled a 1 on each of its damage
dice) against targets that succeed at their saves instead of 1 point of damage.

**At 3rd Level:** The weapon gains a +1 enhancement bonus.

**At 7th Level:** The weapon gains either the [frost](ref/magic-enh/frost) or
[keen](ref/magic-enh/keen) weapon special ability, chosen when the weapon is created. These
abilities don't function against a target that succeeds at its Will save.

**Special:** The weapon disappears after 1 round if it leaves your grasp.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

At 11th level: The enhancement bonus +2.

At 19th level: The enhancement bonus +3.

**Shadow Mastery (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast an illusion spell from the shadow subschool, increase the strength of
such spells by 1% per oracle level you have.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Shadow Projection (Su)**

**Ability:** You infuse your life force and psyche into your shadow, causing it to separate from
your body and act as an independent creature. This ability functions as
[shadow projection](ref/spell/shadow-projection) except as follows.

Your shadow has the [outsider](ref/type/outsider) type and the [phantom](ref/subtype/phantom)
subtype instead of the undead type. As a result, this ability does not have the evil descriptor and
your shadow cannot be [turned](ref/feat/turn-undead) or affected as undead (though it can be
affected as an outsider). Your shadow's hit points are not replenished between uses, although your
shadow can be healed in the same manner as any other outsider. When this ability isn't in use, any
healing done to you also heals your shadow for an equal amount.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per 2 oracle levels; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

**Stealth Mastery (Ex)**

**Ability:** You gain [Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) with the [Stealth](ref/skill/stealth)
skill.

**At 16th Level:** You gain the _hide in plain sight_ [shadowdancer](ref/pclass/shadowdancer) class
feature.

**At 8th Level:** You gain [Signature Skill](ref/feat/signature-skill) with the Stealth skill, even
if you don't meet the feat's prerequisites.

**Hide in Plain Sight (Su)**

**Ability:** A shadowdancer can use the Stealth skill even while being observed. As long as she is
within 10 feet of an area of [dim light](ref/rule/vision-and-light), a shadowdancer can hide herself
from view in the open without anything to actually hide behind. She cannot, however, hide in her own
shadow.

**Flavor:** Text from the shadowdancer class ability.

**Wings of Darkness (Su)**

**Flavor:** This ability functions as the [dark tapestry](ref/mystery/dark-tapestry) mystery
revelation of the same name.

**At 11th Level:** You can use these wings to fly as if with
[overland flight](ref/spell/overland-flight) once per day. Used in this way, the ability lasts for
up to 1 hour per level, and counts as your total use of this ability for the day.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Swift:** You can manifest a set of translucent, inky wings that grant you a fly speed of 60 feet
with good maneuverability.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** Any spells you cast of the shadow subschool or with the darkness descriptor are
automatically [enlarged](ref/feat/enlarge-spell) without affecting their spell level.

**Flavor:** Your body becomes permanently suffused with the essence of the Shadow Plane.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain [regeneration](ref/umr/regeneration) 5 while in dim light or darkness and
immunity to cold, critical hits, and sneak attacks. Your regeneration is suppressed while in any
level of illumination brighter than dim light.
