### Revelations

An oracle with the Heavens mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Awesome Display (Su)**

**Flavor:** Your phantasmagoric displays accurately model the mysteries of the night sky,
dumbfounding all who behold them.

**Passive:** Each creature affected by your illusion (pattern) spells is treated as if its total
number of Hit Dice were equal to its number of Hit Dice minus your Charisma modifier (if positive).

**Coat of Many Stars (Su)**

**Ability:** You conjure a coat of starry radiance that grants you a +4 armor bonus.

**At 13th Level:** This armor grants you DR 5/slashing.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

At 7th level: This bonus +6.

At 11th level: This bonus +8.

At 15th level: This bonus +10.

At 19th level: This bonus +12.

**Dweller in Darkness (Sp)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you cast your psyche into the void of space to attract the attention of a
terrible otherworldly being. The _dweller in darkness_ behaves in all ways as if you had cast
[phantasmal killer](ref/spell/phantasmal-killer).

**At 17th Level:** The _dweller in darkness_ can be perceived by more than one creature, as if you
had cast [weird](ref/spell/weird).

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Guiding Star (Su)**

**Ability:** Whenever you can see the open sky at night, you can determine your precise location.

**Ability2:** Once per night while outdoors, you can cast one spell as if it were modified by the
[Empower Spell](ref/feat/empower-spell), [Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell),
[Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), or [Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell) feat without
increasing the spell's casting time or level.

**Passive:** When the night sky is visible to you, you may also add your Charisma modifier to your
Wisdom modifier on all Wisdom-based checks.

**Interstellar Void (Su)**

**Flavor:** You call upon the frigid depths of outer space to bring a terrible chill to your
enemies.

**At 10th Level:** You can use this ability twice a day.

**At 15th Level:** A creature that fail its save is [exhausted](ref/misc/exhausted) and
[stunned](ref/misc/stunned) for 1 round.

**Standard:** One target within 30 feet is cloaked in the void and takes 1d6 points of cold damage
for every oracle level you possess. A successful Fortitude save halves this damage.

**Usage:** Once per day.

**Usage:** Once per day.

**Lure of the Heavens (Su)**

**Flavor:** Your connection to the skies above is so strong that your feet barely touch the ground.

**At 1st Level:** You no longer leave tracks.

**At 10th Level:** You gain the ability to [fly](ref/spell/fly), as per the spell, for a number of
minutes per day equal to your oracle level. This duration does not need to be consecutive, but it
must be spent in 1-minute increments.

**At 5th Level:** You can hover up to 6 inches above the ground or even above liquid surfaces, as if
[levitating](ref/spell/levitate).

**Mantle of Moonlight (Su)**

**Ability:** As a melee touch attack, you may disrupt a [lycanthrope's](ref/template/lycanthrope)
connection to the moon. This action automatically forces the lycanthrope into its humanoid form,
which it must remain in for a number of rounds equal to your oracle level.

**At 5th Level:** You can use this ability to force others into a [rage](ref/spell/rage), as per the
spell, but only a number of times per day equal to [one-fifth](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of
your oracle level. It's still a melee touch attack.

**Passive:** Your innate understanding of the moon renders you immune to
[lycanthropy](ref/rule/lycanthropy).

**Moonlight Bridge (Su)**

**Ability:** You summon a bridge of shimmering moonlight. The 10-foot-wide span touches the ground
at a point adjacent to your position. From this point it can extend in any direction for 10 feet per
oracle level. The path persists until you have crossed over the bridge or for 24 hours, whichever is
shorter.

**Special:** Should the bridge be attacked, treat it as a [wall of force](ref/spell/wall-of-force).

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Spray of Shooting Stars (Su)**

**Special:** You can fire more than one ball at a time, but creatures caught inside more than one
simultaneous explosions only take damage once.

**Standard:** You can unleash a ball of energy that explodes in a 5-foot-radius
[burst](ref/misc/burst) dealing 1d4 points of fire damage per level. A successful Reflex save halves
this damage. This attack has a range of 60 feet.

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 5 oracle levels

**Star Chart (Ex)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you may spend 10 minutes contemplating your star chart to gain the
benefit of the spell [commune](ref/spell/commune).

**Flavor:** Your copious notes contain a working model of the night sky expressed in artistic
scribbles and arcane mathematical formulae.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Flavor:** Your rapport with the heavens grants you perfect harmony with the universe.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You receive a bonus on all saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier. You
automatically stabilize if you are below 0 hit points, are immune to fear effects, and automatically
confirm all critical hits.

**Special:** Should you die, you are reborn 3 days later in the form of a star child, who matures
over the course of 7 days (treat as the [reincarnate](ref/spell/reincarnate) spell).

There is no such creature or template called "Star Child"; the line was intended as a flavorful way
of saying "a reincarnated oracle".
