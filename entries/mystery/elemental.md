**Deities**

**Details:** Elemental Lords, [Gozreh](ref/faith/gozreh), [Nethys](ref/faith/nethys),

### Revelations

An oracle with the Elemental mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Dance of Whirling Water (Ex)**

**Ability:** Whenever you succeed at an [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) check to move through an
enemy's square, you gain a competence bonus equal to half your oracle level (minimum +1) on
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuvers against that creature until the start of your next turn.

**Flavor:** You dance through the battlefield with all the grace and power of the waves, sweeping up
enemies and tossing them away with the powers of air and water entwined.

**At 11th Level:** You gain [Whirlwind Attack](ref/feat/whirlwind-attack) as a bonus feat, and you
can make [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) combat maneuvers in place of any of the attacks granted by
Whirlwind Attack; you cannot move with enemies bull rushed this way.

**Desert Mirage (Su)**

**Flavor:** Air and fire shroud you from your enemies' vision and superheat your strikes in combat.

**At 7th Level:** While you are using this ability, your melee attacks each deal an additional 2
points of fire damage.

**Prereq:** 3rd-level oracle

**Swift:** You can surround yourself with heated air, gaining [concealment](ref/rule/concealment)
(as per the [blur](ref/spell/blur) spell).

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per oracle level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments

**Elemental Aegis (Su)**

**Ability:** You can conjure an enveloping, protective force made of this element that grants you a
+4 armor bonus to AC (this manifests as a solid rush of air over your body, rocky plates covering
your skin, and the like).

**Choice:** When you take this revelation, choose one element: air, earth, fire, or water.

**At 13th Level:** At 13th level, you receive an additional boon depending on the element you chose.
If you chose air, you gain a +2 bonus on Reflex saving throws. If you chose earth, you gain a +2
bonus to CMD. If you chose fire, you gain fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 2 (this stacks with
any other fire resistance you have). If you chose water, you gain a +4 bonus on
[Swim](ref/skill/swim) checks.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

At 7th level: The AC bonus +6.

At 11th level: The AC bonus +8.

At 15th level: The AC bonus +10.

At 19th level: The AC bonus +12.

**Elemental Allies (Su)**

**Ability:** When casting [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spells to summon elementals
with the air, earth, fire, or water subtype, you can cast the spell as a **standard action** instead
of a casting time of 1 round.

**Flavor:** The beings tied to the fundamental elements can hear your calls plainly when you seek
aid from them.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Usage:** 1 time/day per oracle level

**Elemental Channeling (Su)**

**Ability:** You gain [Elemental Channel](ref/feat/elemental-channel) (your choice of air, earth,
fire, or water) as a bonus feat. You gain the cleric's [channel energy](ref/ability/channel-energy)
ability, as a cleric of your oracle level, but only to use Elemental Channel. The DC for this effect
is 10 + half your oracle level + your Charisma modifier.

**Flavor:** You can heal and harm elemental beings.

**At 15th Level:** You gain Elemental Channel with the last remaining elemental subtype as a bonus
feat.

**Repeat At:** You gain Elemental Channel with one of the remaining elemental subtypes as a bonus
feat., 5, 10

**Special:** You can take other feats to add to this ability, such as
[Improved Channel](ref/feat/improved-channel), but not feats that alter this ability, such as
[Alignment Channel](ref/feat/alignment-channel). This revelation's effects do not stack with levels
in other classes that grant the _channel energy_ ability.

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**Flavor:** The forces of the Elemental Planes envelop and inure your flesh against hostile energy.

**At 11th Level:** The resistance becomes 10.

**At 17th Level:** The resistance increases to 20.

**At 7th Level:** This resistance increases to 5.

**Passive:** You gain [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 2 to acid, cold, electricity, and fire (this
stacks with any other resistance you have of that type).

**Flowing Step (Ex or Su)**

**Ability:** You can increase your base speed by 10 feet.

**Flavor:** Fire's hunger and water's flow grant you swiftness and elegance, and you move with
breathtaking purpose and grace.

**At 11th Level:** While using this ability, you can walk on liquid as if using
[water walk](ref/spell/water-walk), and you are immune to damage caused by proximity to (but not
immersion in) lava, magma, and similarly heated stone.

**At 7th Level:** While using this ability, you can use [Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) to move
at full speed through threatened squares or other creatures' squares without increasing the DC
by 10.

**Prereq:** Cannot have the [lame](ref/oraclecurse/lame) oracle curse.

**Usage:** 1 hour/day per oracle level; these hours need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-hour increments

**Reforged Arms (Su)**

**Flavor:** The forge's fire turns earthen ore into useful metal, and you can hone weapons in the
same way.

**At 11th Level:** You can have the weapon act as if it were made of
[adamantine](ref/eq-material/adamantine) rather than of alchemical silver or cold iron.

**At 3rd Level:** You can also have the weapon act as if it were made of alchemical silver or cold
iron (your choice when you activate this ability) for the purpose of bypassing
[damage reduction](ref/rule/damage-reduction).

**At 7th Level:** The weapon gains a +1 enhancement bonus, though this bonus does not stack with any
enhancement bonus the weapon already has.

**Special:** This effect lasts for 1 minute per oracle level.

**Standard:** You can touch a metal or stone weapon and transform it into a
[masterwork](ref/rule/masterwork-weapons) equivalent if it is not already a masterwork or magical
weapon.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

At 15th level: The enhancement bonus +2.

At 19th level: The enhancement bonus +3.

**Roiling Soil (Su)**

**Ability:** The ground within 5 feet per 2 oracle levels you have shifts and rolls unpredictably;
if you use this ability underwater, the surrounding waters churn. This area moves with you, and you
and your allies are unaffected by the roiling terrain.

Other creatures can move within the area at half speed with a successful DC 10
[Acrobatics](ref/skill/acrobatics) check. Failure means they stop moving for the round, while
failure by 5 or more causes them to fall [prone](ref/rule/prone); if underwater, creatures gain the
effects of being [off-balance](ref/rule/off-balance-and-prone-underwater) instead of falling prone.

This effect lasts a number of rounds equal to your oracle level.

**Flavor:** Combining your mastery of earth and water, you shape the battlefield in your favor,
bending the terrain to benefit you and your allies and hinder any who might oppose you.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Sweeping Impact (Ex)**

**Ability:** When performing a [bull rush](ref/rule/bull-rush) as part of a
[charge](ref/rule/charge), if you push the target at least 5 feet, you can attempt a
[trip](ref/rule/trip) combat maneuver against that creature as a **free action**. You are not
tripped if you fail this check by 10 or more.

**Flavor:** You can shake the earth and buffet foes with the roaring force of wind you draw from the
Elemental Plane of Air.

**At 11th Level:** You can bull rush or trip creatures three size categories larger than you.

**At 15th Level:** You can bull rush or trip creatures four size categories larger than you.

**At 19th Level:** You can bull rush or trip creatures five size categories larger than you.

**At 7th Level:** You can bull rush or trip creatures two size categories larger than you.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Flavor:** Your body undergoes an internal elemental shift, and you become a living conduit of the
Elemental Planes.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** Your external appearance remains the same, although you may take on certain cosmetic
elemental traits (such as smoldering hair or pebble-like skin). You become immune to critical hits
and precision damage such as from sneak attacks. You no longer need to breathe, eat, or sleep.
