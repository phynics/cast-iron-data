### Revelations

An oracle with the Juju mystery can choose from any of the following revelations.

**Beast Tongue (Ex)**

**Ability:** You can communicate with a single type of animal (birds, cats, snakes, fish, and so on)
as if you were constantly using _[speak with animals](ref/spell/speak-with-animals)._ Although this
ability grants you the ability to converse with such creatures, it grants you no special influence
over them.

**Connaissance (Su)**

**Fullround:** You can attune yourself to the spirit world, gaining the benefit of a
[moment of prescience](ref/spell/moment-of-prescience) spell with the insight bonus equal to one
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) your oracle level (minimum 1).

**Special:** You may only have one _connaissance_ effect active at a time.

**Usage:** Charisma modifier times/day

**Ensnare the Soul (Su)**

**Ability:** Add [charm person](ref/spell/charm-person) and
[dominate person](ref/spell/dominate-person) to your spell list. Creatures affected by either spell
become listless and pale, appearing dead to observers (DC 20 [Heal](ref/skill/heal) or
[Perception](ref/skill/perception) check to determine the creature is not undead). Affected
creatures are slow to take any action unless you specifically direct them to do so. They are not
mindless or helpless, however, and defend themselves if attacked.

**Flavor:** Your magic turns others into zombie-like servants.

**Juju Senses (Ex)**

**At 10th Level:** This bonus increases to +4.

**Passive:** When casting [detect magic](ref/spell/detect-magic) and [identify](ref/spell/identify),
you gain a +2 bonus on [Knowledge (arcana)](ref/skill/knowledge-arcana) and
[Spellcraft](ref/skill/spellcraft) checks to recognize auras and identify their properties. You also
gain a +2 bonus on [Perception](ref/skill/perception) and [Sense Motive](ref/skill/sense-motive)
checks made against spirit creatures, such as outsiders, fey, and incorporeal undead.

**Night Terror (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you may target a creature with the effects of a
[nightmare](ref/spell/nightmare) spell. Instead of the spell's normal damage, you make a full
attack, using your Charisma modifier in place of your Strength modifier, against the victim's touch
AC. You deal 1d10 damage for each successful hit.

**Flavor:** You send your spirit to torment another being's dreams.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Path of the Snake (Su)**

**At 15th Level:** You can use this ability twice per day.

**Prereq:** 11th-level oracle

**Special:** You remain in this form for a number of rounds equal to your oracle level, but you can
end this effect prematurely with a **standard action**.

**Standard:** Once per day, you can become incorporeal. While in this form, you gain a +10 bonus on
[Stealth](ref/skill/stealth) checks and can move in any direction and through any object (except for
those made of force). You can take no action other than to move while in this form.

**Spiritual Defense (Sp)**

**Flavor:** Your chanting grants protection against your enemies.

**At 6th Level:** You can chant aloud to create a magic circle effect (such as
[magic circle against evil](ref/spell/magic-circle-against-evil)).

**Move:** You can begin chanting to gain the benefit of a protection spell (such as
[protection from evil](ref/spell/protection-from-evil)) using your oracle level as your caster
level. This protection lasts until you stop chanting or for the spell's normal duration, whichever
comes first.

Chanting after the initial round of the effect is a **free action**, and you can incorporate verbal
components and communication into the chant; magical silence ends a chant and its protection effect.

**Usage:** Charisma3 modifier times/day

**Summon Nature's Spirits (Sp)**

**Prereq:** 5th-level oracle

**Standard:** Once per day, you may cast a [summon monster](ref/spell/summon-monster) spell of a
level up to the highest level you can cast. You may only summon elementals in this manner, but are
treated as having the [Augment Summoning](ref/feat/augment-summoning) feat when you do so.

**Unwilling Host (Su)**

**Flavor:** You command spirits to invade the bodies of your enemies.

**Prereq:** 7th-level oracle

**Standard:** Once per day you may target a number of creatures equal to your Charisma modifier
within 100 feet, no two of which can be more than 30 feet apart. Targeted creatures must make a Will
save or suffer the effects of a [confusion](ref/spell/confusion) spell as the spirits possess them
and cause them to behave erratically for a number of rounds equal to your oracle level.

### Final Revelation

**Final Revelation**

**Ability:** Once per day, you may cast [extended](ref/feat/extend-spell)
[dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). With this ability, you may dominate a number of
creatures equal to double your Charisma modifier.

**Flavor:** You become a master of the connections between the mortal world and the spirit world.
Your knowledge enables you to gain incredible insight and control over others by manipulating
spiritual energy.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain an insight bonus equal to your Charisma modifier to your AC and on all saving
throws and [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) checks.
