**&L&Poison Touch (Ex)&FN&**

**Action:** Ability

**Special:** Once a creature has benefited from this hex, it cannot benefit from it again for 24
hours.

The witch empowers herself or an ally within 30 feet with poisonous nails. The affected creature
gains a [claw attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) as a secondary attack that deals 1d3 points of damage
(1d2 for Small creatures) and has the following poison ability:

**Type:** Poison (Claw-injury)

**Save:** Fortitude 10 + 1/2 the witch's level + the witch's Int modifier

**Frequency:** 1/round for 6 rounds

**Effect:** Strength damage 1d2

**Cure:** 1 save

The creature gains this claw attack for a number of minutes equal to the witch's level. If the
target already has a claw attack, that attack gains the poison ability listed above, but the DC is 1
higher.
