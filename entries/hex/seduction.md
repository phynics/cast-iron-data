**Seduction (Su)**

**Ability:** The witch uses enthralling movements to [fascinate](ref/misc/fascinated) a single
creature within 60 feet that can see the witch. A Will save negates this effect. If the target is
(or could be) sexually attracted to the witch, the save DC is increased by 2.

The effect lasts for 1 round, but the witch can extend this effect for 1 additional round (up to a
total number of rounds equal to her class level) by taking a **standard action** to continue her
enthralling movements.

**At 8th Level:** The _fascination_ effect lasts for 2 additional rounds after the witch ceases
taking a standard action to maintain it.

**Special:** Whether or not the target succeeds at its save, it cannot be the target of this ability
again for 1 day. This is a mind-affecting charm effect.
