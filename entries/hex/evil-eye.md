The witch can cause doubt to creep into the mind of a foe within 30 feet that she can see. The
target takes a -2 penalty on one of the following (witch's choice): AC, ability checks, attack
rolls, saving throws, or skill checks. This hex lasts for a number of rounds equal to 3 + the
witch's Intelligence modifier. A Will save reduces this to just 1 round. This is a mind-affecting
effect. At 8th level the penalty increases to -4.
