**Evil Eye (Su)**

**Ability:** The target takes a -2 penalty on one of the following (witch's choice): AC, ability
checks, attack rolls, saving throws, or skill checks.

**Flavor:** The witch can cause doubt to creep into the mind of a foe within 30 feet that she can
see.

**At 8th Level:** The penalty increases to -4.

**Special:** This hex lasts for a number of rounds equal to 3 + the witch's Intelligence modifier. A
Will save reduces this to just 1 round. This is a mind-affecting effect.
