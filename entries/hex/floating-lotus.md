**Floating Lotus (Su)**

**Ability:** The witch can conjure a large lotus flower that floats by her side for a number of
minutes per day equal to her level. This duration doesn't need to be consecutive, but must be spent
in 1-minute increments.

While summoned, the witch can use the lotus to cross water as if she was affected by
[water walk](ref/spell/water-walk). She can also use the flower for short bursts of flight, gaining
a +10 enhancement bonus on Acrobatics checks when attempting high jumps and long jumps.

**At 5th Level:** This bonus increases to +20.

**At 9th Level:** This bonus becomes +30.
