**Animal Servant (Su)**

**Ability:** The witch can use this hex to turn a humanoid enemy into an animal and rob it of its
free will. The transformation works as [beast shape II](ref/spell/beast-shape-2) and is negated by a
successful Will save.

The transformed creature retains its Intelligence score and known languages, if any, but the witch
controls its mind. This effect functions as [dominate monster](ref/spell/dominate-monster), except
the creature does not receive further saving throws to resist the hex.

**Special:** The effect can be removed only with [wish](ref/spell/wish) or similar magic, although
slaying the witch also ends the effect. Whether or not the save is successful, a creature cannot be
the target of this hex again for 1 day.
