**&L&Witch's Bounty (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** The witch may bless a bush, plant, or tree that is planted in the ground, so that it
creates a plentiful harvest. Each day at dawn, this blessed bush grows a number of
[goodberries](ref/spell/goodberry) equal to twice her witch level. Berries on the tree remain until
they are picked, but the tree can never manifest a number of berries greater than twice her level at
one time.

**Special:** The witch may only have one _witch's bounty_ active at a time, but can shift her
blessing to a new plant with a ritual requiring 1 hour.
