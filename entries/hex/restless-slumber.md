**Restless Slumber (Su)**

**Ability:** The witch causes a creature within 30 feet to fall into a sleep riddled with
nightmares. This functions as the _slumber_ hex. Additionally, the restless nature of the sleep
causes the creature to violently toss and turn, dealing 1d10 points of damage to itself each turn.
This damage does not wake the creature.

When it wakes, the creature's addled state causes it to be [confused](ref/misc/confused) for a
number of rounds equal to half the witch's level.

**Prereq:** [Slumber](ref/hex/slumber)
