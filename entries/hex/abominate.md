**Abominate (Su)**

**Ability:** The witch transforms a creature within 30 feet into an
[aberration](ref/type/aberration). This hex acts as
[baleful polymorph](ref/spell/baleful-polymorph), except the target is transmuted into a Small,
Medium, or Large aberration. The target's abilities are modified as
[monstrous physique IV](ref/spell/monstrous-physique-4).

**Special:** Whether or not its save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of this hex
again for 1 day.
