**Pollute Water (Su)**

**Ability:** This functions as the [blight](ref/hex/blight) hex, except it affects only either an
area of standing water or a creature with the aquatic or water subtype. A creature of any type that
drinks water from a polluted area must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or become
[nauseated](ref/misc/nauseated) for 1d3 rounds and afflicted as if blighted with the
_[blight](ref/spell/blight)_ hex curse.

**Flavor:** The witch can corrupt bodies of water with foul energies.

**Special:** A creature that succeeds at this saving throw is immune to the effects of _polluted
water_ from this area for 24 hours. _Polluted water_ does not quench thirst.
