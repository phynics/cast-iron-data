**Peacebond (Su)**

**Ability:** A witch can use this hex on a creature to prevent it from drawing a weapon for a number
of rounds equal to the witch's level. This hex has no effect on natural weapons or weapons already
in a creature's hands, but does prevent an archer from drawing arrows.

**Special:** A Will save negates this effect, and whether or not the save is successful, a creature
cannot be the target of this hex again for 1 day.
