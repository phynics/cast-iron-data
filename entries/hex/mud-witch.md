**&L&Mud Witch (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** A witch with this hex can assume the form of viscous brown mud. The witch's type
changes to [ooze](ref/type/ooze) and she is able to squeeze through even the smallest cracks, but
she can't make attacks or cast spells with verbal, somatic, material, or focus components while in
this form, and she loses her supernatural abilities. If the witch has a touch spell ready to use,
that spell is discharged harmlessly when she uses this hex.

While the witch is in mud form, her speed becomes 10 feet and she gains a swim speed of 20 feet. The
witch gains DR 10/slashing and cold resistance 10.

**At 10th Level:** While the witch is in mud form, her speed increases to 20 feet and her swim speed
increases to 40 feet.

**Prereq:** [Swamp hag](ref/hex/swamp-hag)

**Special:** This hex affects only the witch.

**Usage:** 1 minute/day per witch level; these minutes need not be consecutive, but must be spent in
1-minute increments
