**&L&City Sight (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** On a failed Fortitude save, the subject loses the use of
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), [greensight](ref/umr/greensight),
[low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness), and other
visual abilities beyond simple sight, but not nonvisual means of perception like
[blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight), [scent](ref/umr/scent), or [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense).
This effect lasts 1 minute.

**Flavor:** The witch curses a target with the simple vision of urban mortals.

**At 8th Level:** This effect lasts for 10 minutes instead.

**Special:** Whether or not the creature succeeds at the saving throw, it can't be the target of
this hex again for 1 day.
