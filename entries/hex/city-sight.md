The witch curses a target with the simple vision of urban mortals. On a failed Fortitude save, the subject loses the use of [darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision), [greensight](ref/umr/greensight), [low-light vision](ref/rule/low-light-vision), [see in darkness](ref/umr/see-in-darkness), and other visual abilities beyond simple sight, but not nonvisual means of perception like [blindsight](ref/umr/blindsight), [scent](ref/umr/scent), or [tremorsense](ref/umr/tremorsense). This effect lasts 1 minute. At 8th level, this effect lasts for 10 minutes instead. Whether or not the creature succeeds at the saving throw, it can't be the target of this hex again for 1 day.
