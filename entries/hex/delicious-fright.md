**Delicious Fright**

**Ability:** The target of this hex becomes [shaken](ref/misc/shaken) for a number of rounds equal
to 3 + the witch's Intelligence modifier. As long as the witch remains within 30 feet of her target,
she gains a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls and a +1 morale bonus on saving throws as long as this
effect persists.

**Flavor:** The witch can feed on the fear of her victim.

**Special:** A successful Will save reduces the duration of this hex to 1 round. This is a
mind-affecting fear effect.
