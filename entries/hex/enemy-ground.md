**&L&Enemy Ground (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** The target takes a -4 penalty on Acrobatics checks to move over slippery or uneven
surfaces and to avoid attacks of opportunity while moving through threatened squares. This effect
lasts for 1 minute.

**Flavor:** The witch curses a target with clumsiness when in dangerous terrain.

**At 8th Level:** This penalty increases to -8.

**Special:** On a successful Will save, the penalty is reduced by half and the duration is only 1
round.
