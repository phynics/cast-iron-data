**Coven (Ex)**

**Ability:** The witch counts as a [hag](ref/family/hag) for the purpose of joining a hag's coven.
The coven must contain at least one hag.

**Ability2:** In addition, whenever the witch with this hex is within 30 feet of another witch with
this hex, she can use the [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another) action to grant a +1 bonus to the
other witch's caster level for 1 round. This bonus applies to the witch's spells and all of her
hexes.
