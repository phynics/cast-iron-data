**Pariah (Su)**

**Ability:** The witch can cause a creature within 60 feet to be shunned by its allies for a number
of rounds equal to the witch's Intelligence modifier. Any other creature attempting to directly
assist the target with a harmless spell or [aid another](ref/rule/aid-another-2) action must attempt
a Will save. If the save succeeds, the aiding creature is unaffected by this hex. If the save fails,
the aiding creature can't follow through, the action is lost, and the aiding creature can't directly
aid the target for the duration of this hex. This hex does not prevent the target from benefiting
from area of effect spells.
