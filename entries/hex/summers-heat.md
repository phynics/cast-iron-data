**Summer's Heat (Su)**

**Ability:** The witch surrounds her target with oppressive heat, dealing a number of points of
nonlethal damage equal to her witch level and causing the target to become
[fatigued](ref/misc/fatigued). The target can attempt a Fortitude save to reduce this nonlethal
damage by half and negate the fatigued condition.

**Special:** Whether or not the target succeeds at this save, it can't be the target of this hex
again for 1 day.
