**&L&Ameliorating (Su)&FN&**

**Ability:** The witch chooses one of the following conditions each time she uses this hex: dazzled,
fatigued, shaken, or sickened. If the target is or later becomes afflicted with the chosen
condition, that condition is suppressed for a number of minutes equal to the witch's level.

Alternatively, the witch can grant her target a +4 circumstance bonus on saving throws against
effects that cause any two of the listed conditions (which condition is always the witch's choice)
for 24 hours.

**Flavor:** The witch can touch a creature to suppress or protect it from negative conditions.

**Special:** Once a creature has benefited from this hex, it cannot benefit from the hex again for
24 hours.
