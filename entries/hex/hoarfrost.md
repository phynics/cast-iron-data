**Hoarfrost (Su)**

**Ability:** The target is rimed with a shell of frost needles that slowly work their way into its
flesh (Fortitude negates). The target turns pale and blue, and takes 1 point of
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per minute until it dies, saves (once per
minute), or is cured. [Break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment),
[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic), [remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse), and similar spells
end the effect.

**Special:** If the target saves, it is immune to this hex for 1 day. This is a cold effect.
