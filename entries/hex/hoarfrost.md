The target is rimed with a shell of frost needles that slowly work their way into its flesh
(Fortitude negates). The target turns pale and blue, and takes 1 point of
[Constitution damage](ref/rule/ability-damage-drain) per minute until it dies, saves (once per
minute), or is cured. _[Break enchantment](ref/spell/break-enchantment)_,
_[dispel magic](ref/spell/dispel-magic)_, _[remove curse](ref/spell/remove-curse)_, and similar
spells end the effect. If the target saves, it is immune to this hex for 1 day. This is a cold
effect.
