**Leshy Summoning (Su)**

**Passive:** The witch counts as a plant creature for the purpose of growing
[leshys](ref/family/leshy). She adds [leaf leshys](ref/monster/leshy-leaf-leshy),
[gourd leshys](ref/monster/leshy-gourd-leshy), [fungus leshys](ref/monster/leshy-fungus-leshy),
[seaweed leshys](ref/monster/leshy-seaweed-leshy), and [lotus leshys](ref/monster/leshy-lotus-leshy)
to the list of creatures she can summon with
[summon monster I, II, III, IV, and V](ref/spell/summon-monster), respectively.
