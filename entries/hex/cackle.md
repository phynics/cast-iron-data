**&L&Cackle (Su)&FN&**

**Move:** A witch can cackle madly. Any creature that is within 30 feet that is under the effects of
an [agony](ref/hex/agony) hex, [charm](ref/hex/charm) hex, [discord](ref/hex/discord) hex,
[disrupt connection](ref/hex/disrupt-connection) hex, [distraction](ref/hex/distraction) hex,
[evil eye](ref/hex/evil-eye) hex, [fortune](ref/hex/fortune) hex, [misfortune](ref/hex/misfortune)
hex, [protective luck](ref/hex/protective-luck) hex, or [sink](ref/hex/sink) hex caused by the witch
has the duration of that hex extended by 1 round.
