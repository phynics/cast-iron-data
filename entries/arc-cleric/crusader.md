**Sources:** Ultimate Combat pg 40\
Crusaders serve the militant arm of a church, ready to stand guard over the religion's holy places
and to be its swift, avenging arm against those who resist its truth.

**Diminished Spellcasting**

**Alters:** Domain, spellcasting

**Info:** A crusader chooses only one domain and gains one fewer spell of each level than normal. If
this reduces the number to 0, she may cast spells of that level only if they are domain spells or if
her Wisdom allows [bonus spells](ref/rule/determine-bonuses) of that level.

**Bonus Feat**

**Info:** Bonus feats marked with an asterisk (\*) must be applied to the favored weapon of the
crusader's deity.

**At 1st Level:** A crusader gains a bonus feat from the following list:
[Heavy Armor Proficiency](ref/feat/armor-proficiency-heavy),
[Improved Shield Bash](ref/feat/improved-shield-bash),
[Martial Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/martial-weapon-proficiency),
[Saving Shield](ref/feat/saving-shield), [Shield Focus](ref/feat/shield-focus),
[Tower Shield Proficiency](ref/feat/tower-shield-proficiency), and
[Weapon Focus](ref/feat/weapon-focus)\*.

**At 10th Level:** A crusader gains another bonus feat, and may also choose from the following
feats: [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](ref/feat/exotic-weapon-proficiency),
[Greater Shield Focus](ref/feat/greater-shield-focus),
[Greater Weapon Focus](ref/feat/greater-weapon-focus)\*,
[Improved Critical](ref/feat/improved-critical)\*, [Shield Slam](ref/feat/shield-slam),
[Shield Specialization](ref/feat/shield-specialization), and
[Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/weapon-specialization)\*.

**At 15th Level:** A crusader gains another bonus feat from either list.

**At 20th Level:** A crusader gets a fifth bonus feat from either of the lists above, or either
[Greater Shield Specialization](ref/feat/greater-shield-specialization) or
[Greater Weapon Specialization](ref/feat/greater-weapon-specialization)\*.

**At 5th Level:** A crusader gains another feat from the list above.

**Special:** A crusader need not meet the normal class- or level-based prerequisites for these bonus
feats.

**Legion's Blessing (Su)**

**Flavor:** A crusader gains the ability to confer beneficial spells quickly to a large group of
allies.

**Fullround:** The crusader may confer the effects of a single harmless spell with a range of touch
to a number of creatures equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her cleric level. The
spell's range remains touch, so all intended recipients must be within the crusader's reach when the
spell is cast. Using the _legion's blessing_ expends the prepared spell, but it also requires the
crusader to sacrifice another prepared spell three levels higher, as when spontaneously using a
_cure_ or _inflict_ spell. The higher-level spell is not cast but is simply lost, its magical energy
used to power the legion's blessing.

**Gained:** At 8th Level
