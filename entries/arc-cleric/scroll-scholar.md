A scroll scholar values knowledge and learning as much as her other abilities, trading a portion of
her potential in her chosen class for greater breadth of skill at deciphering old texts, piecing
together strange fragments of esoteric lore, and deciphering the hidden qualities of strange and
unusual magical items. The scroll scholar archetype can be taken by either clerics or wizards - they
give up [different class abilities](ref/arc-wizard/scroll-scholar) for the new abilities granted by
this archetype. A scroll scholar has the following class features.

**Diligent Student (Ex)**

**At 1st Level:** A scroll scholar chooses one [Knowledge](ref/skill/knowledge-all) (INT) skill.
This becomes a class skill if it is not already one. The scroll scholar adds
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her class level to all Knowledge checks of this type
(minimum 1).

**At 10th Level:** A scroll scholar chooses a third additional Knowledge skill to receive this
bonus.

**At 15th Level:** A scroll scholar chooses a fourth Knowledge skill to receive this bonus.

**At 20th Level:** A scroll scholar chooses a fifth Knowledge skill to receive this bonus.

**At 5th Level:** A scroll scholar chooses 1 additional Knowledge skill to receive this bonus.

**Replaces:** One of the 1st-level granted powers from her domains - the character gets to choose
which of her two 1st-level granted powers it replaces

**Secrets Revealed (Sp)**

**At 11th Level:** One of these [spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) (scholar's
choice) becomes a constant spell-like ability.

**At 13th Level:** The second one becomes a constant
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities).

**At 5th Level:** A scroll scholar gains the ability to cast
[comprehend languages](ref/spell/comprehend-languages) and [identify](ref/spell/identify) as
[spell-like abilities](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Her caster level is equal to her character
level in whatever class she took the scroll scholar archetype for. She can cast these spells once
per day.

**At 7th Level:** She can now cast these spells twice per day each.

**At 9th Level:** She can now cast these spells three times per day each.

**Replaces:** Channel energy damage boost at 5th level

**Special:** Her _channel energy_ damage increases to 3d6 at 7th level, and for the rest of her
career as a cleric lags 1d6 behind normal.

**Flash of Insight (Su)**

**Immediate:** Once per day, a scroll scholar can gain a +5 bonus on a single attack roll, caster
level check, or saving throw. This decision must be made before the results of the roll are known,
but can be made after the die is rolled.

**At 15th Level:** You can use this ability twice a day.

**At 20th Level:** You can do this three times a day.

**Gained:** At 10th Level

**Replaces:** The normal addition of a new 4th-level spell slot to the character's spells per day

**Special:** From this point on, the cleric has one fewer 4th-level spell slot than other clerics.
