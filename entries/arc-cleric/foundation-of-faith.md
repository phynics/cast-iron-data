**Sources:** Elemental Master's Handbook pg 8\
Whether aiding the faithful or defending against the depredations of unbelievers, a foundation of
faith is unshakable in her convictions. These clerics are most common among worshipers of deities of
earth or protection, such as [Abadar](ref/faith/abadar) or [Torag](ref/faith/torag), but any
religion might count foundations of faith among its priests.

**Channel Energy**

**Info:** A foundation of faith does not gain the _channel energy_ ability.

**Bastion (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A foundation of faith is an unbreakable obstacle, unmoved by threats and violence alike.

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Passive:** The foundation of faith adds her Constitution bonus to her CMD and to the DC of
attempts to use the Intimidate skill against her.

**Granite Focus (Ex)**

**Flavor:** A foundation of faith's focus is truly impressive.

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Move:** The foundation of faith centers herself, allowing her to add her Constitution modifier to
any [concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks attempted as part of casting cleric spells until
the end of her turn.

**Rooted Vitality (Su)**

**Flavor:** A foundation of faith draws healing from the earth.

**At 11th Level:** This becomes [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 4.

**At 13th Level:** This becomes [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 5.

**At 15th Level:** This becomes [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 6.

**At 17th Level:** This becomes [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 7.

**At 19th Level:** This becomes [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 8.

**At 7th Level:** This ability increases to [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 2.

**At 9th Level:** This becomes [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 3.

**Gained:** At 5th Level

**Standard:** Instead of gaining this [fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) herself, a foundation of
faith can expend a use of this ability to instead touch up to six other creatures to grant each
creature [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of the fast healing that the foundation of faith
would receive (minimum fast healing 1). A recipient must be standing on natural or worked stone to
receive this fast healing, and the fast healing lasts for a number of rounds equal to the foundation
of faith's Constitution modifier or until the recipient leaves contact with natural or worked stone.
If one recipient loses this fast healing early, it does not affect the duration for the other
targets.

**Swift:** While standing on natural or worked stone, the foundation of faith can gain
[fast healing](ref/umr/fast-healing) 1. This effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to the
foundation of faith's Constitution modifier or until the foundation of faith loses contact with the
natural or worked stone.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per 5 cleric levels
