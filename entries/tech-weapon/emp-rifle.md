### Construction

**Craft DC** 29; **Cost** 12,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

An EMP rifle is a larger, more powerful version of an EMP pistol. It emits a beam of electromagnetic
energy that can't harm living creatures, but deals significant damage to robots. It functions as an
[EMP pistol](ref/tech-weapon/emp-pistol) except for the differences listed here.
