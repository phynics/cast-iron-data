**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 21

**Cost** 12,000 gp\
**Type** one-handed ranged; **Proficiency** exotic (firearms); **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d10 (small), 2d6 (medium); **Damage Type** electricity; **Critical** ×2\
**Range** 50 ft.; **Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge; **Special**
[Semi-automatic](ref/misc/semi-automatic), [touch](ref/misc/touch)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 25; **Cost** 6,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

An EMP pistol emits a beam of electromagnetic energy that cannot harm living creatures, but deals
terrible damage to robots. An EMP pistol can harm androids and creatures with cybernetic implants,
but they take half damage from a hit. A creature that is critically hit by an EMP pistol and takes
damage from that hit must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or be [staggered](ref/misc/staggered)
for 1d4 rounds.
