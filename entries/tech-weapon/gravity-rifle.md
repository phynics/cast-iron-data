**Cost** 165,000 gp\
**Type** two-handed ranged; **Proficiency** exotic (firearms); **Weight** 6 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d10 (small), 2d6 (medium); **Damage Type** Force; **Critical** ×2\
**Range** 150 ft.; **Capacity** 20; **Usage** 1 charge; **Special** [Automatic](ref/misc/automatic),
[touch](ref/misc/touch)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 34; **Cost** 82,500 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[graviton lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A gravity rifle is a larger, more powerful version of the
[gravity pistol](ref/tech-weapon/gravity-pistol), and functions as such except for the differences
listed here. When a gravity rifle is on sustained setting, it can move objects weighing no more than
350 pounds up to 20 feet per round, and the wielder gains a +4 bonus on combat maneuver checks; she
still substitutes her Intelligence modifier in place of her Strength or Dexterity modifier when
calculating her CMB for these maneuvers.
