**Type** light melee; **Proficiency** simple; **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Damage Type** B; **Critical** ×2\
**Range** -; **Capacity** 10; **Usage** see text; **Special** -

### Construction

**Craft DC** 20; **Cost** 1,200 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

While powered down, this sturdy, 2-foot truncheon functions as a
[light mace](ref/eq-weapon/light-mace). Once it is activated (this is a swift action that consumes 1
charge), a high-density power core housed in the weapon's head generates a high-pitched whine that
causes momentary disorientation in a creature struck. Each time the weapon hits a target, it
consumes 1 charge and the creature struck must succeed at a DC 12 Fortitude save or suffer trauma to
its central nervous system. For 1 round, a creature that fails its saving throw takes a -1 penalty
on attack rolls, skill checks, and Will saving throws, as well as a -4 penalty on
[concentration](ref/rule/concentration) checks. The duration of this effect stacks with multiple
hits from the neural inhibitor. This is a mind-affecting effect.
