**Sources:** Technology Guide pg 21

**Cost** 13,000 gp\
**Type** one-handed ranged; **Proficiency** exotic (firearms); **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d6 (small), 1d8 (medium); **Damage Type** Sonic; **Critical** ×2\
**Range** 50 ft.; **Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge; **Special**
[Semi-automatic](ref/misc/semi-automatic), [touch](ref/misc/touch)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 23; **Cost** 6,500 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A sonic pistol emits a blast of devastating sound waves that shatter and blast flesh, bone, and
anything else they happen to strike. A creature critically hit by a sonic pistol must succeed at a
DC 15 Fortitude save to resist being permanently [deafened](ref/misc/deafened).
