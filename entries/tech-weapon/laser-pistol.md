### Construction

**Craft DC** 23; **Cost** 5,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A laser pistol emits a beam of intensely focused light capable of burning anything it strikes. A
laser attack can pass through [force fields](ref/tech-misc/force-field-brown) and force effects,
such as a [wall of force](ref/spell/wall-of-force), to strike a foe beyond without damaging that
field. Objects like glass or other transparent barriers do not provide [cover](ref/rule/cover) from
lasers - but unlike force barriers, a transparent physical barrier still takes damage from a laser
passing through it. Invisible creatures are immune to damage caused by a laser weapon. Fog, smoke,
and other clouds provide cover in addition to [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) from laser
attacks. Darkness (magical or otherwise) has no effect on lasers other than providing concealment,
and though a laser consists of highly focused light, it does not provide any illumination.
