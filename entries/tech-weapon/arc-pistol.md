### Construction

**Craft DC** 23; **Cost** 5,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[military lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

An arc pistol emits bolts of ionized particles that it then electrifies, creating a crackling beam
of electricity between it and its target. Arc pistols gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls
against targets that are metal or are wearing medium or heavy metal armor.
