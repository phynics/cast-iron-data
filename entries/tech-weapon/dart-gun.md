**Cost** 3,000 gp\
**Type** one-handed ranged; **Proficiency** exotic (firearms); **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d3 (small), 1d4 (medium); **Damage Type** P; **Critical** ×2\
**Range** 30 ft.; **Capacity** 1 nanite canister; **Usage** 1 charge; **Special** -

### Construction

**Craft DC** 24; **Cost** 1,500 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[nanotech lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A dart gun makes use of nanotechnology to craft a small pointed projectile that does minimal damage
on its own. Unlike other nanotech weapons, the nanites that make up a dart do not have any
additional effect on the target. Instead, a dart gun contains a small reservoir above its handle
into which a single dose of a pharmaceutical, drug, poison, or even potion can be poured as a
standard action. The next dart fired from the dart gun delivers that liquid into the target
immediately upon a hit.
