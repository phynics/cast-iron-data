**Cost** 6,000 gp\
**Type** one-handed melee; **Proficiency** martial; **Weight** 4 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d8 (small), 1d10 (medium); **Damage Type** fire; **Critical** ×3\
**Range** -; **Capacity** 10; **Usage** 1 charge; **Special** [Touch](ref/misc/touch)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 26; **Cost** 3,000 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[production lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

A laser torch is a handheld tool intended to cut through objects with great speed. When activated, a
laser torch emits a beam of highly focused light, cutting and burning through surfaces up to 6
inches away. Attacks from a laser torch resolve as touch attacks and deal 1d10 points of fire
damage. This damage is not modified further by Strength. When the laser torch is used as a tool or
as a weapon to sunder, its damage bypasses up to 20 points of [hardness](ref/umr/hardness), and
damage is not halved (as is normally the case for energy damage applied to objects) unless the
object is particularly fire-resistant. A laser torch's cutting beam passes through
[force fields](ref/tech-misc/force-field-brown) and force effects without damaging the field.
Invisible objects and creatures can't be harmed by a laser torch.
