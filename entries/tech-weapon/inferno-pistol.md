**Cost** 5,000 gp\
**Type** one-handed ranged; **Proficiency** exotic (firearms); **Weight** 2 lbs.\
**Damage** 1d4 (small), 1d6 (medium); **Damage Type** fire; **Critical** ×2\
**Range** 30 ft.; **Capacity** 1 nanite canister; **Usage** 1 charge; **Special**
[Touch](ref/misc/touch)

### Construction

**Craft DC** 21; **Cost** 2,500 gp\
[Craft Technological Arms and Armor](ref/feat/craft-technological-arms-and-armor),
[nanotech lab](ref/rule/crafting-laboratories)

An inferno pistol is a less refined variant of the longer-range laser pistol. Unlike a
[laser pistol](ref/tech-weapon/laser-pistol), which uses an intensely focused beam of light to burn
a target, an inferno pistol is much less elegant - it generates a blob of molten, red-hot material
and fires what appears to be a burning pellet of fire with a soft hissing sound. This material is
consumed swiftly in firing, leaving behind nothing but ash and painful burns on anything it strikes.
On a critical hit, a shot from an inferno pistol also inflicts 1 point of [burn](ref/umr/burn)
damage (Reflex DC 12 negates). An inferno pistol has a maximum range of 5 increments.
